To our readers!

The subscription for 1973 to the popular scientific and mathematical journal «Kvant» continues.
The magazine is intended primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is also useful for teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics or mathematics, as well as for all lovers of mathematics and physics.
The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school”, materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, apply these sciences to explain various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.
The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were offered at entrance exams for various sports, problems that were offered at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.
Notes describing physical instruments and experiments will help readers set up and test physical experiments.
The magazine publishes on its pages review articles telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists and stories from scientists themselves about how “science is done”, how scientific discoveries appear.
Receptions for books are constantly being placed - those already published and those just ready for publication.
The magazine is distributed only by subscription and is not sold in retail stores.
Room price 30 kopecks. When subscribing, refer to our index 70465.
