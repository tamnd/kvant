As can be seen from figure, placed on the first page of the cover, a square (and in general any rectangle) can be divided into any number of similar triangles. If we demand that all the figures of the partition be necessarily equal, then the rectangle (and any parallelogram) will be divided into only an even number of triangles (see figure on the left). The article “Finite parquets” (p. 25) talks in detail about the patterns that govern this kind of “parquets”, and provides interesting examples of figures cut into any number of equal triangles.
