Fight

When the time for preparation ends, the teams gather together in the hall and the battle begins, which consists of rounds (for each task). First of all, the jury, through easy additional questions, a captain's competition, or a drawing of lots, assigns the team numbers A, B, C (then the team's roles change in accordance with a predetermined schedule if none of the teams refuses the call).

This jury gives the right to team A to challenge team B to any problem that has been solved by team A and has not yet been told. If team A does not have such tasks, then it can refuse the call, but in this case it will lose the right to compete until the end of the battle. Therefore, sometimes the team recognizes the challenge of an unsolved problem. If this is later discovered, it is classified as an “incorrect call” and punished accordingly.

Next, there are 9 possible options, collected in Table 1.

Team B may accept the challenge or may refuse to share the solution. In case of refusal, the correctness of the call is checked: team A must tell the solution. If the team knows the solution, but cannot clearly tell it or suspects that there are errors in the solution, it is often more profitable to refuse to call.
