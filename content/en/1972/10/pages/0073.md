⟦folio 71⟧

In «Kvant» No. 5 in the information about the rally of students of physics and mathematics schools, we mentioned that a mathematical battle was held at the rally - a competition that has become popular in physics and mathematics schools. In this issue we publish the rules of mathematical combat and tasks of mathematical combat, held at the 6th All-Union Mathematical Klippad in Chelyabinsk.

Mathematical BS involves 2 or 3 teams, consisting of 8-12 members each, one of them is the captain. The preparation of the fight and judging is carried out by the jury, which selects tasks in advance (usually the same number as there are members in the team) and approves the rules and regulations of the fight, and during the fight awards points to the teams. The most interesting is the battle of three teams, so we will outline the rules of mathematical battle for three teams, and then talk about the communication of the battle of two teams.

Preparatory part

For the first 2-5 hours, the teams are in different rooms and solve problems. At the same time, none of the teams should receive information about what problems the opponents have solved.

At this stage, the team acts as a single whole: all its members solve problems together, and if the problem is solved by one of them, then the rest no longer need to solve it. The distribution of the task is led by the captain. However, in the future, one person will represent the team for each task, so the captain must distribute the solved problems between the teams (in this case, one person can represent several tasks). In addition, you need “experts” in solved problems, familiar with all the difficulties and subtleties of the task and capable of refuting the enemy’s wrong decision.
