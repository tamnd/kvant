2. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} 2\log_4x + \log_{1/8}y^3 = -1, \\ \log_4x^4 - \log_{1/8}y = 5. \end{cases}$$

3. From points $A$ and $B$, located at a distance of 50 km, 2 pedestrian codes were simultaneously embroidered towards each other. We met 5 hours later. After the meeting, the speed of the first pedestrian walking from $A$ to $B$ decreased by 1 km/h, and the speed of the second pedestrian walking from $B$ to $A$ increased by 1 km/h.

It is known that the first pedestrian arrived at point $B$ 2 hours earlier than the second one approached at point $A$. Determine the initial speed of the first pedestrian.

4. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the inequality is true
$$\frac{13}{2-x} > x + 4.$$

1. A metro train consists of several cars, with each car carrying the same number of passengers. The number of passengers in one carriage exceeds the number of carriages by 9. When at the station 10 people entered the 2nd carriage, and 10 people exited from the rest, the number of passengers in the 2nd carriage turned out to be equal to the number of passengers remaining in all other carriages. How many passengers were there initially in each carriage?
