⟦folio 62⟧

NEW BOOKS

In this issue we continue to publish summaries of books published in 1972 that are of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the third and fourth quarters of 1972 (orders can be sent through the Book by Mail stores).

MATHEMATICS

Publishing house "MIR"

1. Ebbin house G., Yakos K., Man F., Hermes G., Tyurin machines and recursive functions. (Volume 12 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 86 kopecks.)

This collective monograph of German mathematicians contains an elementary presentation of the theory of Tyurin machines and recursive functions - an important section of modern mathematical logic, which has found wide application in cybernetics. In addition to the foundations of this theory, the book contains a number of significant results, including recent discoveries (in particular, Kolmogorov’s results on the connection of Tyurin’s machines with the basic theories of probability). The presentation is strict, but accessible, and contains many examples and explanations.

The monograph will be read with interest by readers of different categories, starting with high school students of mathematical specialization and final employees and teachers of higher education.

2. Davis M. Theory of cer. (Volume 12 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 60 kopecks.)

Game theory is one of the young and rapidly developing areas of modern mathematics. Its subject is the formal study of conflict situations in the broadest sense of the word. The ideas and methods of game theory find application in economics, military affairs, biology and other branches of knowledge.

M. Davis's book is a fascinating popular study of game theory. The most striking feature of the book is that the author, starting with the basic, primary concepts of game theory, brings the reader to the scale of modern and subtle ideas, insignificantly highlighting a generally accessible style of presentation that does not require mathematical training from the reader. The book's six sections cover the correspondence of one-person games, finite zero-sum games, utility theory, two-person non-zero-sum games, and polychlus games.

The book is of interest to a wide range of readers who want to get acquainted with the basic ideas of game theory. Mathematicians will also read it with satisfaction.

3. Sawyer W. The Path to Modern Mathematics. (Volume 11 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 55 kopecks).

Professor W. Sawyer is known to Soviet readers for his book “Prelude to Mathematics” and the article “Algebra” in the collection “Matematika in the Modern World.”

In the book “The Path to Modern Mathematics,” discussing ideas for reform of mathematics education, the author makes attempts to highlight new issues of modern mathematics for inclusion in the school curriculum.

Considering individual topics in mathematics (maps, matrices, vector spaces, etc.), which are becoming important in scientific and engineering practice, the author shows how many of these ideas and understandable natural ones arise and develop from the topics of traditional mathematics.

Sawyer addresses his book to young people, but it will be read with interest not only by students and schoolchildren, but also by teachers, especially in connection with the prospect of school curricula.

Publishing house "HIGH SCHOOL"

4. Zorin V.V. Mathematics manual for university applicants. (Volume 12 l., price 59 kopecks.)

The manual explains the basic concepts of elementary mathematics. All concepts are given as applicable to the requirements for applicants to universities.

The manual is intended for individuals who are ready for competitive university exams.

5. Solomonik V. S., Milov P. N. Collection of questions and problems in mathematics. (Volume 15 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 67 kopecks.)

The book is compiled in accordance with the program of application copies for applicants to universities and secondary specialized educational institutions of higher education. Examples and problems in arithmetic, algebra and geometry are included.

Almost all sections begin with brief theoretical material. There are also questions that make it possible to understand how thoroughly the theory has been accepted.
