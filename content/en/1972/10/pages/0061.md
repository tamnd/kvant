OPTIONS FOR ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS
IN PHYSICS AT MOSCOW
PHYSICAL AND TECHNICAL INSTITUTE
IN 1972

Ticket 1

1. During a high-speed descent, the skier walked down a slope of φ = 45°, without pushing off with poles. The coefficient of friction between lying and snow is k = 0.1. Air resistance is proportional to the square of the speed: $F_c = \alpha v^2$, where $\alpha$ is a constant equal to 0.7 $\frac{H}{(m/c)^2}$.
What maximum speed could the flyer develop if its mass m = 90 ke?

2. The VT diagram depicts a closed process (cycle) performed by a certain mass of nitrogen (Fig. 1). It is known that the minimum gas pressure in this process is $P_{\min} = 3,28$ mm. Determine the mass of the gas and its pressure at point 1. The quantities $T_1, T_2, V_1$ and $V_2$ are indicated at figure.

3. Two parallel grids are connected to a battery with e. d.s. $E = 10$ in, as shown in figure 2. On the left, at an angle of 45° to the grids, a parallel beam of electrons with an initial energy $U_0 = 10$ in falls. At what angle $\alpha$ will the beam deviate after passing through the mesh?

4. Two converging lenses with identical focal lengths $F$ are located at a distance $F/2$ from each other (Fig. 3). Using this system, two images of the Sun were obtained - one formed by rays that, after refraction in the first lens, passed the second, the other by rays that produced successive images through both lenses. At what ratio of the lens diameters will the illuminance of the image be equal?

Ticket 2

1. One end of a stick of mass m rests on the corner between the wall and the floor. In the wall at a height equal to the length of the stick, a smooth hole is drilled through which a thread passes, tied to the upper end of the stick. The other end of the thread will be thrown over the block and some weight hangs on it (Fig. 4). What should be the size of the load so that the stick is always pressed against the wall from any initial position?

2. A vessel with a capacity V = 10 liters is pumped out by a pump with a capacity of $a = 100 \text{ л/mин}$. To what best vacuum can a vessel be evacuated if, due to the current in it, the current in the evacuated vessel rises by $\Delta p = 1 \text{ mm pm. cm}$. In time $t = 1 \text{ час } 40 \text{ мин}$ (with the pump stopped)? The air temperature in the vessel is assumed to be constant.

Note. The performance of vacuum pumps is usually characterized by the volume of gas that is removed from the pumped vessel in 1 second.

4. In two curtains of opaque particles of radius $r_1 = 5 \text{ мк}$ with a substance content of $\gamma = 0,04 \text{ e}$ per cubic meter of air, the visibility range is $l_1 = 50 \text{ м}$. How much substance in a cubic meter of air will be sprayed by another source of elevation, which creates particles of radius $r_2 = 10 \text{ мк}$, if visibility is reduced to $l_2 = 20 \text{ м}$?
