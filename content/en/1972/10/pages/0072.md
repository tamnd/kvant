K. Kikon and the first deputy editor-in-chief, Academician A. N. Kolmogorov.

The organizing committee of the Olympiad, the jury, scientists from Tbilisi State University, the Tbilisi Polytechnic Institute and the Pedagogical Institute, where they prepared an experimental task, and the workers of the Tbilisi Department of Public Education put a lot of effort into ensuring that the Olympiad was held clearly, in an organized manner, with great benefit from its accountants.
