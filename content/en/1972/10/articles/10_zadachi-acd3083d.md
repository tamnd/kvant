M166. a) Schoolchildren of the same class went on two hiking trips in September. In the first campaign, boys were less than $2/5$ of the total number of participants in this campaign, and in the second - also less than 2/5. Prove that in this class boys make up less than 4/7 of the total number of students, if it is known that each of the students has been on at least one trip.

6) Let in the $i$th campaign ($i=1,2,\dots,n$) the boys constituted $\alpha_i$ part of the total number of participants in this campaign. What is the largest proportion of boys at a general meeting of all tourists (everyone who has been on at least one of the $n$ trips)?

M167. Prove that in any arithmetic program $a, a+d, a+2d,\dots,a+nd,\dots,$ composed of natural numbers, there are infinitely many terms whose factorization into prime factors includes exactly the same prime numbers.

J. Polya

M168. In a regular truncated form (Fig. 1), point $K$ is the midpoint of some sides $AB$ of the upper base, $L$ is the middle of some sides $CD$ of the lower base.
