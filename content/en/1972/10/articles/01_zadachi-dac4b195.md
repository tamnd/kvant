QUANTUM
for naughty schoolchildren

1. Two tribes live on the island: aborigines and aliens. The natives always tell the truth, the newcomers always tell the truth. The traveler hired a native islander as a guide. They went and saw another islander. The traveler sent a guide to find out which tribe this native belonged to. The guide returned and said that he said he was an aborigine.

Who was the guide: an alien or an aborigine?

2. Is it possible to move a knight from the bottom left square of a chessboard to the top right square, while beating on which square once and only once?

3. Conchilas Great Patriotic War. Somehow two comrades met. We started talking.

“It’s been a while since we’ve seen you.” How old is my son now? - asked one.

“You know, a most curious game of numbers,” answered the second. My son was born in the same year, which was exactly the square of the logo age in the year of his birth. And now he is as old as the sum of the digits of the year of his birth.

How old is your mathematician father?

4. Prove that of all the people who have ever lived in the world and who are living now, the number of people who have made an odd number of handshakes throughout their lives is an even number.

5. $\overline{abcd} : \overline{dcba} = q$.
The dividend, divisor and quotient are perfect squares. Find them.
