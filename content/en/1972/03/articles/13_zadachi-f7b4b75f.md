F146. There is a battery with e. d.s. 100 V and internal resistance 2 ohm. F147 *. In the Rutherford and Bohr model of the atom, electrons spin around the nucleus in specific circular orbits. When moving from one orbit to another, closer to the nucleus, the atom emits a photon. What is the energy and frequency emitted by a hydrogen atom during the transition of an electron from an orbit of radius $r_1=2,1\cdot 10^{-8}$ cm to an orbit of radius $r_2=5,3\cdot 10^{-9}$ cm?

S. M. Kozel
