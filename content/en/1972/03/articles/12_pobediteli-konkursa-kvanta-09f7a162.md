⟦folio 36⟧

We have already reported (see «Kvant» No. 1 for 1970 and 1971) that schoolchildren, who are given especially original and complete solutions to the problems “Task «Kvant», will receive the right to participate in the field of three Vesegoznoy Olympids along with the winners of regional and city Olympids. Over the course of 1971, the editors received more than one and a half thousand letters with solutions and selected the authors of the correct and most interesting solutions.

Below we publish a list of schoolchildren in grades 7–10 who received the right to participate in the Moscow and Leningrad city Olympids, as well as in the region, regional and resource (in the Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic and co-resource without regional division) Olympids.
