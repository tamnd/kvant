Exercises

1. Find the average force acting on the plate during an absolutely inelastic collision with it of a ball of mass $m$, moving with a speed $v$ in the direction making an angle $\alpha$ with the plate. The collision time is $\tau$.

2. With what force does a light machine gun press on the shoulder when firing, if the mass of the bullet is $m=10 \text{ a}$, its speed at departure is $v=800 \text{ m/sec}$ and the speed of the machine gun is $n=600$ rounds per minute?

3. Two balls fall in a cloud of dust. How many times does the speed of the balls differ?

if the diameter of one of them is twice the diameter of the other?

4. A ball flying horizontally with a speed of $v$ hits a heavy steel plate moving towards it with a speed of $u$. At what speed will the ball move after an absolutely elastic collision? Use gravity.

5. A bullet of mass $m$, traveling horizontally with a speed $v$, hits a cube lying on a smooth floor and pierces it through. The mass of the cube is $M$. The speed of the bullet after departure is $u$. What part of the first initial energy of the bullet passed into the body?

6. When a projectile of mass $M=60 \text{ kg}$ exploded, three identical shards were formed. Their total kinetic energy is $E = 2,9 \cdot 10^7 \text{ J}$. What maximum speed can one of the fragments have if before the explosion the projectile was flying at a speed of $v = 800 \text{ m/sec}$?

7. A ball of mass $m$, moving at a speed $v$, collides at an angle $\alpha$ with a cube of mass $M$, standing on a smooth floor (Fig. 10). Find the speed of the ball after impact. The impact is considered to be absolutely elastic.

8. A ball of mass $m$, moving at a speed $v$, making an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon, falls into a positioning platform with sand of mass $M$ (Fig. 11) and gets stuck in the sand. Find the speed of the platform.
