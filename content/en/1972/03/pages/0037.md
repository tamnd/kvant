A metal disk is attached to the bulb of the lamp using soup or any glue that does not dissolve in water. The diameter of the disk is slightly larger than the diameter of the lamp; it blocks direct light from the lamp and corresponds to the dark disk of the Moon. The entire system is lowered into the aquarium, with the lamp in a horizontal position and the metal disk placed near the face stack (Fig. 2). It’s a good idea to include a rheostat in the lamp, then you can adjust the light intensity. When the current is turned on, you can see a beautiful corona caused by the scattering of the lamp's light on small private mastics suspended in water. The asymmetrical lamp filament produces non-uniform illumination, and this enhances the effect. If the glow columns are too sharply defined or too wide, this can be easily corrected by replacing the foil strips.

However, the picture of the background is not yet perfect; the color of the “sky” is too light and comparatively bright. If you add some bluish-green dye to the mixture, the “sky” takes on a tanny color and the crown appears more clearly. By adding paint, you can ensure that the “sky” is heavily colored, without changing the color of the crown in the slightest. This circumstance

So, an excellent reproduction of the circumsolar atmosphere has been obtained, a crown of pale gold color with a pearly sheen, with a distinguishable structure. Protruding hazy columns of glow are visible, fading against the bluish-green background of the “sky”.
Thanks to non-uniform lighting, not only the columns are visible, but also the dark areas. The effect is enhanced if the eyes are half-closed.
