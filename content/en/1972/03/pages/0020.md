The fate of other theorems and problems is peculiar...How to declare, for example, the exclusive attention of mathematicians and mathematics lovers to the Pythagorean theorem? Why were many of them not content with already known evidence, but found their own, bringing the five relatively obvious quantities to several hundred?

When it comes to the Pythagorean theorem, the non-educational begins already with its name. It is believed that it was not Pythagoras who first formulated it. It is also believed that he gave proof of it. If Pythagoras is a real person (some even doubt this!), then he most likely lived in the 6th-5th centuries. BC e. He himself did not write anything, called himself a philosopher, which means, in his understanding, “striving for wisdom,” and founded the Pythograean Union, whose members studied music, hypnastics, mathematics, physics and astronomy. Apparently, he was also a magnificent organ, as evidenced by the following legend relating to his stay in the city of Croton: “Pythagoras’s first appearance before the people in Croton began with a speech to the young men, in which he so strictly, but at the same time so captivatingly outlined the designations of the young men that the elders in the city asked not to leave them without honor. In this second speech he pointed to legality and purity of rights as the foundations of the family; in the next two he addressed children and women. The consequence of the last days of speech, in which he especially condemned Russia, was that thousands of precious dresses were delivered to the temple of Hera, or not a single bridegroom dared to show them on the street anymore...” However, even in the second century of our era, that is, 700 years later, real people lived and worked in the field, outstanding scientists who were clearly under the influence of the Pythogrean Union and treat with great respect to the fact that, according to legend, Pythagoras is created.

There is also no doubt that interest in the theorem is caused both by the fact that it occupies one of the central places in mathematics, and by the satisfaction of the authors of the proof, who overcame difficulties, about which the Roman poet Quite Horace Flask, who lived before our era, well said: “It is difficult to express well-known facts well.”
