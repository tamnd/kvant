The phenomenon of the correct arrangement of vortices allowing a streamlined body was first studied experimentally by the German physicist Benard at the beginning of our century. But it was only thanks to the work of Karman that soon followed that such a movement, which at first seemed so figurative, received an explanation.

After the name of this scientist, the system of periodic vortices is now called the Karman path.

However, as the speed increases, the vortices have less and less time to melt into a larger area of ​​liquid. It may seem that the Karman vortex street is just a beautiful natural phenomenon with no practical significance. But that's not true.
