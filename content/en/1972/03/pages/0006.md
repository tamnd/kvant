The movement of the body is straight and uniform. In this case, by “unperturbed motion” of a body we mean the motion of a body that is not affected by other bodies. In this state the law of inertia. It can be formed as follows: the direction of movement and speed of the body remain constant, and soon there are no external influences on the body, called forces. Galileo also discovered that on the surface of the Earth the speed of a freely falling body increases by equal amounts at equal intervals of time.

Newton posed a general question: how does the speed of a free body change under the action of an arbitrary given force? This is a much more general problem compared to the one considered by Galileo, or the current village in its size and direction can arbitrarily change over time.
