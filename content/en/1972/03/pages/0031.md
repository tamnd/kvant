Parallelograms corresponding to a given parallelogram ABCD that can be covered by a parallelogram ABCD. In fact, if the side AB of the parallelogram ABCD is equal to c, then any segment belonging to ABCD parallel to AB will not exceed c; Therefore, in preparing an ABCD and less than ABCD parallelogram, every parallel segment AB will be less than c. It follows from this that both vertices A and B of the parallelogram ABCD cannot be covered by (any!) parallelogram prepared by ABCD or smaller than it. Thus, it is established that we will have to cover each vertex of the parallelogram ABCD with another parallelogram, less than ABCD and ready ABCD; therefore, the total number of covering ABCD less than ABCD less will be at least 4 (according to the number of vertices ABCD). In the same way, it is established that the total number of triangles covering triangle ABC, preparatory to ABC and smaller than it, cannot be less than 3 (since each vertex of ABC will have to be covered with its triangle).
