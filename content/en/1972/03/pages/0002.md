On the first page of the cover there is an illustration of one of the proofs of the Pythagorean theorem. The proof is given in the article by V. N. Berezin “Pythagorean Theorem” (see page 18).

In figure, pictured on the left, all three triangles surrounded in yellow are equal. The height in one of them is highlighted in blue. Due to the fact that the selected angles are equal, they are vertical, and the red segment lies on the same straight line as the blue one. (This is the key to another way to prove the Pythagorean theorem, located on the fourth page of the cover).
