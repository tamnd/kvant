There are specimens that have both quadrangular and octagonal cells, but biologists have noticed that if there are no such “non-standard” cells (with less than five and more than seven sides), then the pentagonal cells are always exactly 12 larger than the heptagonal ones (there can be several hundred or even thousands of cells in total). Can you explain this fact?

M135*. Prove that for every natural number $n>1$ the identity
$$\sin x \sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{n} \right) \sin \left( x + \frac{2\pi}{n} \right) \times \cdots \times
\sin \left( x + \frac{(n-1)\pi}{n} \right) = c_n \sin nx,
$$ where $c_n$ is some number (depending on $n$), and find $c_n$.

Then from state 2 the gas slowly and adnabatically (without body supply) transitions to state 3 ($P_3, V_3, T_3$). It is known that during the transition 2→3 the gas performs work equal to the amount of body imparted to the gas during the transition 1→2.

Fig. 3.

Come up with a simple circuit to power the load and calculate the parameters of this circuit.

What is the energy and frequency emitted by a hydrogen atom during the transition of an electron from an orbit of radius $r_1=2,1\cdot 10^{-8}$ cm to an orbit of radius $r_2=5,3\cdot 10^{-9}$ cm?
