On the first page of the cover, on which you see a fragment of N. Renikh’s painting “Overseas Guests,” the picture of the waves is completely different. How to determine the speed of movement of a body from a photograph of the pattern of waves that arose during its movement? You will learn about this from the article “Waves on the Water” [see. p. 10], published in this issue of the magazine.
