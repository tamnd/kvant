It is convenient to combine formulas (10) and (11) into one. To do this, let's calculate
$$\int_{a}^{b} f(\alpha x + \beta) \, dx.$$
Let's put $g(x) = f(x + \beta)$. Then $f(\alpha x + \beta) = g(\alpha x)$. Hence,

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(\alpha x + \beta) \, dx = \int_{a}^{b} g(\alpha x) \, dx =$$
$$= \frac{1}{\alpha} \int_{\alpha a}^{\alpha b} g(x) \, dx = \frac{1}{\alpha} \int_{\alpha a}^{\alpha b} f(x + \beta) \, dx =$$
$$= \frac{1}{\alpha} \int_{\alpha a+\beta}^{\alpha b+\beta} f(x) \, dx.$$

Thus,

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(\alpha x + \beta) \, dx = \frac{1}{\alpha} \int_{\alpha a+\beta}^{\alpha b+\beta} f(x) \, dx.$$

When $\alpha$ is negative, this formula loses its meaning, since in this case $\alpha a + \beta > \alpha b + \beta$. Reflecting the rod symmetrically about the origin, we can obtain the formula

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(-x) \, dx = \int_{-b}^{a} f(x) \, dx.$$

Pozny, if $\alpha < 0$, then

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(\alpha x + \beta) \, dx = \int_{-b}^{a} f(-\alpha x + \beta) \, dx =$$
$$= -\frac{1}{\alpha} \int_{\alpha b+\beta}^{\alpha a+\beta} f(x) \, dx.$$

You can see how these formulas are applied by solving the examples below. Some of them will be discussed in detail at the end of the course (in the section “Answers, instructions, solutions”) «Kvant» No. 10.

Exercise 5. Calculate integrals

a) $\int_{-2}^{1} (x-1)^3 \, dx$;
b) $\int_{-1}^{\pi} |x^2-x| \, dx$;
c) $\sin x \, dx$;
d) $\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \cos 2x \, dx$;
e) $\int_{-1}^{1} (x^3-1)^2 \, dx$;
f) $\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^3 x \, dx$.

Exercise 6. a) Prove that if $f$ is an odd function, then

$$\int_{-a}^{a} f(x) \, dx = 0.$$

6) Prove that if $f$ is an even function, then

$$\int_{-a}^{a} f(x) \, dx = 2 \int_{0}^{a} f(x) \, dx.$$

Exercise 7. Prove that if $f$ is a periodic function and $l$ is its period, then

$$\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \, dx = \int_{a+l}^{b+l} f(x) \, dx.$$
