M165.* There is a set of $F$ points on a circle, consisting of 100 arcs. It is known that with any rotation $R$ of the circle, the set $R(F)$ has a common point $c$. (In other words, for any $\alpha$ from $0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$ in the set $F$ one can specify two points $\alpha$ apart from each other.) What is the smallest sum of lengths that the 100 arcs forming the set $F$ can have? What will be the answer if the arcs are not 100, but $n$?

Yu. P. Lysov

F165. Estimate the time of an elastic collision of two steel or two rubber balls with identical radii $R=1$ cm. For steel, Young's modulus is $E_c=2,1\cdot 10^{11}$ n/m², steel density is $\rho_c=7,8\cdot 10^3$ ke/m³. Young's modulus of rubber is $E_p\approx 10^6$ n/m², its density is $\rho_p\approx 10^3$ ke/m³. The balls move towards each other at speeds of $v=1$ m/s.

What is the average force between the balls?

G. L. Komkin
