⟦folio 31⟧

REPLENISHMENT TO THE TEAM

The city team needed to complete its national team function with a defender, a half-back, a left winger and a central winger. And although there were 7 approximately identical playing claims, the task was not easy, since it was necessary to take into account their teamwork and very complex interactions.

Kapalov agrees to join the team only as a central player, and only on the condition that Polyanchkov will not be a defender, and Alekseev will not be a left winger. Kolesnikov refuses to play for the national team if Zborovsky and Dymnikov are included in the team together or if Alekseev is included in the team without Komarov, and in order for Komarov to agree to play, it is necessary to refuse the invitation of Kolesnikov and Kapalov. Polyanchkov does not want to play together with Dymnikov if Kapalov is not on the team or if Alekseev is the central direction. Dymnikov agreed only in the direction and on the condition that Alekseev would not be on the team. Zborovsky agreed to join the national team together with Dymnikov, but at the same time refuses to be a defender.

Who was considered for the city's national team and by whom?

A. S. Sorokin
