COMBINATORICS PROBLEMS

1. There are 36 students in the class. How many options are there for choosing one subject and one secretary for holding a class meeting?

2. The Volga car has the number MMO-6057. How many cars can end up with the MMO index?

3. Telephone numbers in Leningrad consist of six digits. How many telephone numbers can there be in Leningrad?

4. During lunch in the dining room, the visitor chooses one tablespoon, one teaspoon and one fork. How many choices are there in total if there are 20 tablespoons, 10 teaspoons and 15 forks in the dining room?

5. Team 20 took part in football perfection. How many meetings will be held between them if the perfection is held in one circle?

6. In perfect chess, a section of 30 people takes place. How much cheese will there be between them if perfection is carried out in one circle?

7. On certain days, 50 officers and 100 soldiers are assigned to patrol. They need to be divided into groups of three people each (one officer and two soldiers). How many options are there for organizing patrols?

Kh. Sh. Shikhalev
