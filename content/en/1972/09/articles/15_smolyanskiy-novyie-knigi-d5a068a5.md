NEW BOOKS

In this issue we continue to publish summaries of books published in 1972 that are of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the third and fourth quarters of 1972 (orders can be sent through Kniga-by-mail stores).

MATHEMATICS

Publishing house "Science"

1. Dorofeev G.V., Potapov M.K., Rozov N.Kh. A manual on mathematics for university applicants. (Volume 30 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 04 k.)
The book is intended for people who want to deepen and expand their knowledge of mathematics before the entrance exam to a higher educational institution. It may be especially useful for employees of preparatory departments of universities. Secondary school teachers will find in it a wealth of material on some of the key topics of the school curriculum.
The book sets out individual theoretical issues, supported by a large number of analyzed competitive problems. The author pays special attention to the logic of the decision; typical mistakes of applicants are discussed in detail. The book is equipped with exercises taken from the experience of using exams.
2. Pogorelov A.V., Elementary geometry. (Volume 12 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 34 kopecks.)
The book is a significant revision of two previously published books by the same author, “Planetorium” (1969) and “Stereometry” (1970). First of all, the aximatics have been somewhat strengthened. The question of changing areas is presented in a form close to traditional. The principles of stereometry are presented compactly.

The paragraphs end with numerous review questions, monitoring progress, and exercises.

The book can be recommended to student teachers, teachers and secondary school students.

3. Antonov N.P., Vygodsky M.Ya., Nikitin V.V. et al., Collection of problems in elementary mathematics. (Volume 29 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 88 kopecks.)
The book contains about 1000 problems in elementary mathematics. Designed for people who are familiar with the course of elementary mathematics, but want to repeat this course and deepen their knowledge without the help of a teacher. The task is intended to help you learn how to solve mathematical problems, so it provides solutions for most problems.

4. Grzegorczyk A., Popular lozika. (Volume 6 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.)
A small book introducing the reader to the elements of mathematical logic and its applications to various branches of modern science and technology. The author does not touch on more difficult issues that require special mathematical training from the reader.

The book is intended for a wide audience, interested in mathematical logic - a branch of science that has received the most important application of significance in our days.

5. Yaglom A. M., Yaglom I. M., Probability and information. (Volume 25 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 97 kopecks.)

One of the most significant areas of mathematical thought that has emerged in recent decades is information theory. Claude Shannon, which serves as the mathematical foundation of the entire bookstore. This book, designed for a reader with a certain interest in mathematics and its application, but perhaps not with knowledge beyond a high school course, talks about the mathematical concept of “probability” and, on the basis of this concept, Shannon’s “collection of information”, as well as some applications of information theory to communication theory, and technology, to linguistics, and biology.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers, from high school students and junior university students to engineers and scientists who are not specialists in the field of bookkeeping or probability theory.

PHYSICS
Publishing house "Science"

1. Zubov V. G., Shalov V. P. Physics assignments. (Volume 16 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 55 kopecks.)
The collection contains problems in physics, mainly in the scope of the secondary school curriculum. Some tasks are beyond the scope of the school curriculum, but all of them are provided with the necessary concepts and can be solved on the basis of knowledge acquired in high school.

The assignment is intended for high school students, technical students and people preparing to enter universities.

2. a) Elementary textbook of physics, m. I. Mechanics, methods, molecular physics, ed. G. S. Landserga. (Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 11 k.)
6) Elementary textbook of physics, m. II. Electricity and magnetism, ed. G. S. Landserga. (Volume 30 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 94 kopecks.)
c) Elementary textbook of physics, m. III. Vibrations and waves, optics, structure of amol, ed. G. S. Landserga. (Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 13 k.)
The publication has gained enormous popularity, especially among teachers. It aims to raise the level of physics in high school. The advantage of the course is the depth of presentation of the physical side of various processes in nature and technology. The materials are presented in simple, clear language. The book was rich in instructions.

Designed for overcoming physics and high school students. can serve as a valuable tool for self-education.

3. What physicists are thinking about, vol. 8, solid state physics (electronic properties of solids), collection of popular science articles. (Volume 14 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 70 kopecks.)
A collection of translations of articles from American popular science magazines dedicated to the achievements of solid state physics. The stats were written by major scientists actively working in this rapidly developing field of physics. Lively language and simplicity of presentation make the collection accessible and interesting for a wide range of readers (from high school students, undergraduates and graduate students in various areas of solid state physics).

4. Yavorsky V. M., Pinsky A. A. Fundamentals of Physics, m. II, Electroidianika, oscillations and waves, fundamentals of quantum physics of atoms, molecules and solids, physics of nuclei and elementary particles. (Volume 35 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 1 rub. 08 k.)
The book examines all the most important sections of classical and modern physics. The peculiarity of the book is an attempt not to separate classical and modern physics, but to consider them as a single modern science. All physical ideas and the most important results of modern physics were obtained without the use of higher mathematics.

Designed for high school students of intersuccess physics, intermediate physics students and technical students. may be useful for first-year university students and individuals engaged in self-education.

5. Komarov V.N. New occupied astronomy. (Volume 15 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 60 kopecks.)
The book, in an engaging form, introduces the reader to the most interesting issues of modern astronomy: the necessary methods for studying space objects, the movement of artificial celestial bodies, new original hypotheses and assumptions about physical processes in space and the geometric properties of the Universe.

Particular attention is paid to the paradoxical phenomena from which scientific research occurs, and is, as it were, extended from Y. Perelman’s book “Entertaining Astronomy”, built on modern material.
