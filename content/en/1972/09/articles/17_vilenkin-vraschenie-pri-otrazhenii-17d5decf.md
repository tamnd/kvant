⟦folio 61⟧

ROTATION
BY REFLECTION

1. The tape recorder cassette rotates counterclockwise at a certain speed. It is illuminated by a light bulb and the reflected rays give an image of the rotating cassette. In what direction and at what speed does this image rotate?

2. A ray of light falls on a mirror and is reflected from it.
The mirror rotates at some speed counterclockwise. In what direction and at what speed does the reflected beam rotate?

3. The mirror is motionless; a light source (for example, a laser) rotates around it at a certain speed counterclockwise, the beam from which falls at the same point on the mirror. In what direction and at what speed does the reflected beam rotate?

4. The mirror is stationary, the light source rotates around its axis counterclockwise. How does the reflected beam rotate?

A. Vilenkin
