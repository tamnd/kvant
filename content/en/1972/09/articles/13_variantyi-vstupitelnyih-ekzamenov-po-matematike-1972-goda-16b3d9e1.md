OPTIONS FOR ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS
IN MATH 1972

Moscow State University named after
M. V. Lomonosova

Faculty of Chemistry

1. Pier $A$ is located 27 km from pier $B$ downstream of the river. The first boat departs from pier $A$ towards pier $B$, and an hour after its departure, the second boat departs towards it from pier $B$. Boats meet 18 km from the pier $B$.

If the boats had departed from piers $A$ and $B$ towards each other at the same time, they would have met in two and four hours. Find the speed of the first boat in still water if it is known that it is twice the speed of the river flow.

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_{x^2+6x+8}[\log_{2x^2+2x+3}(x^2-2x)] = 0.$$

3. In the trapezoid $ABCD$ the angles $A$ and $D$ at the base $AD$ are respectively 60° and 30°. Point $N$ lies on the base $BC$, and $BN:NC = 2$. Point $M$ lies on the base $AD$, line $MN$ is perpendicular to the base of the trapezoid and divides its area in half. Find the ratio $AM:MD$.

4. The base of the triangular rectangle $SABC$ is the right triangle $ABC$ with a right angle at the vertex $B$. Edge $SA$ is perpendicular to the plane of the triangle $ABC$. Through the midpoints $D$ and $E$, respectively, of edges $AC$ and $BC$, a plane is drawn parallel to edge $SC$ and forming a quadrangle $DEFH$ in section (point $F$ lies on edge $SB$). Find the area of ​​the quadrilateral $DEFH$ if it is known that $BC=a$, $AC=b$, $SA=h$.

5. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the expression
$(\sin 4x + \sin 2x)(3\cos^2x + 2\sin 2x - 6\cos x)$ positive.

Faculty of Geography and Soil Department of the Faculty of Biology

1. In two convoys, 28 cars each, there were 11 Zhigulyai, the rest were Moskovchi. How many Moskovchi were there in each convoy, if it is known that in the first convoy, each Zhigulyai car had twice as many Moskovchi as in the second?

2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sqrt{3}\sin x + 2\cos x = \sqrt{3} + \sin 2x,$$
satisfying the conditions 0 < x < 2.

3. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the inequality is true
$$\log_2 x - \log_x 32 < 4.$$

4. Find all numbers $x$ and $y$ for which
$$\begin{cases} 2^{x+1} = y^2 + 4, \\ 2^{x-1} \leq y. \end{cases}$$

5. Through the vertices $B$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ a circle is drawn that intersects side $AB$ at point $K$ and side $AC$ at point $E$. Find $AE$, knowing that $AK = KB = a$, $BCK = \alpha$, $CBE = \beta$.

Biological Department, Faculty of Biology

1. A car leaves from point $A$, and an hour later a motorcycle arrives, which catches up with the car to point $B$. At this moment, a second motorcycle is sent from $A$, and hits at the same speed as the first. The second motorcycle catches up with the car six hours after its departure. How long does it take for the motorcycle to travel from point $A$ to point $B$?

2. Simplify an expression
$$tg 4x \cdot tg (40^\circ - 2x) + tg 4x \cdot tg (50^\circ - 2x) + tg (50^\circ - 2x) \cdot tg (40^\circ - 2x).$$

3. Solve the equation
$$5\sqrt{\log_3 x + \log_5 9} \log_5 3 = 3^{1 \log_3 1.8}.$$

4. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sin x + \sin (\log_3 x^3 \pi x) = \cos x - \cos \left(\log_3 \frac{x^3 \pi x}{x}\right).$$

5. In triangle $ABC$, bibliotrices $AD$ and $BE$ intersect at point $O$. Find the ratio of the area of ​​triangle $ABC$ to the area of ​​quadrilateral $ODCE$, knowing that $BC = a$, $AC = b$, $AB = c$.
