TELEVISION PREPARES FOR UNIVERSITY

In October 1972, classes began on television physics and mathematics preparatory courses, organized by the Main Editorial Board of popular science and educational programs of Central Television. Classes on televises are based on the principles of consolidating, deepening, expanding and systematizing the knowledge that students acquired at school. During the academic year, viewers of the third program of Central Television are offered a series of programs on mathematics and physics, which is based on the “Program of entrance exams for applicants to higher educational institutions of the USSR.” The cycle is compiled taking into account possible full coverage of program issues that cause the most difficulties for applicants.

The work of the courses is organizational as follows. Regularly (in mathematics 1-2 times a week, in physics - 1 time a week) classes are held on the topic of introducing the Central Television program - lectures and practical exercises on solving problems. Every day, course participants are offered mandatory homework assignments, consisting of a task and examples of various difficulties. In mathematics there will be 20 such tasks, and each task consists of two parts - main tasks and additional tasks; in physics - 40 homework assignments. In addition, it is planned to periodically carry out control work. Homework and tests will be published in the “Television and Radio Broadcasting Programs” diary.

To complete homework, course participants must submit a postcard to the studio—a report on the completion of the work on a special form; to complete the test, he must present to the studio a similar postcard and the work itself. These reports are checked by course staff and scored according to a specially developed scale. The most actively and successfully working listeners will be invited for unrepresented participation in television programs related to the maintenance of control work.

In the 1972/73 academic year, it is also planned to conduct qualifying tests. Participation in them gives students the opportunity to check the level of their knowledge. Future applicants during tests will find themselves in conditions similar to those that occur during offensive exams, that is, they seem to be undergoing a kind of psychological preparation. On the other hand, teachers during clearing tests can determine the degree to which students have hidden lecture material, identify gaps in their knowledge and, accordingly, those introduced correctly into their work. Telefretels who have scored a decent number of points are called for extramural tests. In total, during the academic year it is planned to conduct 4 tests: 2 in physics - at the Moscow Engineering Physics Institute, 2 in mathematics - at the Moscow Economic and Statistical Institute (tests in mathematics will be carried out using electronic computers).

Course participants who successfully passed the qualifying tests and worked actively throughout the academic year receive certificates of completion of the Television Physics and Mathematics Courses for university applicants.

Telecourses can be attended by both graduating students of secondary general education and technical schools, as well as persons who already have secondary education. To be enrolled in courses, you need to send an application to Central Television to the Main Editorial Office of Popular Science and Educational Programs.
