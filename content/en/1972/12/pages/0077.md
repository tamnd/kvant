7. The amplitude value is butt instead of voltage $U = 127\sqrt{2}\theta$. At the initial moment the capacitor is not charged. During the first half-cycle, when a negative potential is applied to point $A$, diode $D_1$ is open and capacitor $C_1$ is charged to voltage $U$, while a charge $q = UC$ is induced on its right plate. During the second half-period, $D_1$ closes and $D_2$ opens, while part of the charge induced on $C_1$ flows onto the upper plate $C_2$. This process will be repeated every period until the upper plate $C_2$ has such a charge $q$ that when $D_2$ is open, the charge from $C_1$ to $C_2$ will not drain. The magnitude of the charge $q$ can be found by considering the circuit with $D_2$ open and $D_1$ closed, when point $A$ has a potential $+U$.

During the first half-cycle, charge $UC$ is induced on the right plate $C_1$. Voltage $U = \frac{q}{C} - \frac{UC}{C} \cdot \text{Отсюда заряд } q = 2UC$. Consequently, a potential difference $2U = 2 \cdot 127\sqrt{2}\theta$ is formed between points $M$ and $N$.
