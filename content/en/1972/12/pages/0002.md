The curve depicted at figure on the left in red paint is called trakti稼oi. A cart rolls along such a curve if it is on a spot, and the child pulling it walks along the edge of the sidewalk.

Here is its exact definition:
A path is a curve that has the property that the tangent segment from the point of tangency to the intersection with a given straight line $X'X$ (director) has a constant length independent of the point of tangency.

The body obtained by expressing the path 稼ы relative to the straight line $X'X$ is called a pseudosphere (we see it on the first page of the cover). As the Igalian geometer E. Beltrami established in 1863, the pseudosphere has such a remarkable property: Lobachevsky geometry is fulfilled on it. You can read about N. N. Lobachevsky and his discovery of non-Euclidean geometry in the status on page 6.
