sheniyu $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta t} \approx ky$

or

$$\Delta y \approx ky \cdot \Delta t,$$

(1)

where $k$ is the proportionality coefficient. This coefficient can be either a positive number (for example, during the process of population growth) or a negative number (for example, during the process of radioactive decay). The approximate relation (1) must be remembered in the sense that the relative error of this relation kills as the time interval $\Delta t$ decreases, that is, as $\Delta t$ tends to zero, the relation $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta t} \approx ky$ becomes more and more accurate.

Now let’s pose the question: what kind of functional dependence can describe a process that has property (1)?

Among the elements of a function studied at school, we find one function that has the desired property - this is the exponent of the function $y=a^t$. In fact, the change (or increment) of this function $\Delta y = a^t + \Delta t - a^t$, due to the main property, the exponent of the function $a^t + \Delta t = a^t \cdot a^{\Delta t}$ can be represented as a product of two ratios $\Delta y=a^t (a^{\Delta t} - 1)$. The first set $a^t$ coincides with the value of the function $y=a^t$, and the second set $a^{\Delta t} - 1$ depends only on the increment of the argument $\Delta t$. It can be shown that for small values ​​of $\Delta t$ the difference $a^{\Delta t} - 1$ is approximately proportional to $\Delta t$. This fact is equivalent to the fact that the graph of the exponent of the function $y=a^t$ has a tangent at the point of intersection of this graph with the ordinat axis (Fig. 1).
