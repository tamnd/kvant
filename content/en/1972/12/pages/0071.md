⟦folio 69⟧

Regarding Galya Turbina's letter *) about the formulas and terms of the theory of relativity, I will not hide that it caused surprise and some regret. It seems to me that for everyone who is at least a little interested in the exact sciences, school knowledge and school textbooks are not enough in our time. After all, there is popular science literature, there are electives, clubs (I myself learned about the theory of relativity in elective classes in the 9th grade). By the way, the new physics textbook for the ninth grade gives the concept of the theory of relativity. It seems to me that it is in vain that “the editors believe that Gali’s claims are fair.”

Regarding the problems, they can say that they really seem too difficult to me, although, however, edited, if, of course, she compares the level of complexity of the problems with the number of answers to them.

In general, I would like to note the timeliness and importance of the appearance of the magazine «Kvant», because many schoolchildren, including those from general, non-specialized schools (I am one of them), are interested in physics and mathematics much deeper and more fully than they can be presented within the framework of the school curriculum. I am extremely pleased with the high level and rigor of presentation of all materials in the journal.”

Letters also arrive at «Kvant» from the teacher. For example, teacher L.F. Toporkov writes from himself in Kochnevo, Kamyshlovsky district, Sverdlovsk region: “I’m not a schoolboy. I am a rural teacher, I recently graduated from the mathematics department of pedagogy by correspondence. The difficult years of distance learning somehow unexpectedly came to an end and there was an untrained feeling that I couldn’t study anymore, something was missing. After all, you still don’t know how much. Now one of my teachers is «Kvant». Thank you".

Many letters come from sixth and seventh graders with solutions to problems from section «Kvant» for younger students. In them they give answers to problems and... complain that they are unfairly knocked down, giving them little space on the pages of the magazine. We are pleased to inform them that next year the editors of the journal will more than double the volume of this section and will break with the task of publishing articles and notes in this section.

Letters containing critical comments are received for editing. Most often, critical letters point to individual

Rezhka is a criticism from readers who want to see special sciences in «Kvant», and not just popular science articles. For example, Sergei Cherenikov, Zhana-Semey station of the North Kazakhstan region, writes: “In all issues of the magazine «Kvant» not a single article was published about the problems of modern physics. All articles on physics were somehow related to elementary physics. We, the reader of the magazine, want to not only replenish and deepen our knowledge of elementary physics, but also get acquainted with the ideas and problems of modern physics. I, like many readers of the journal, would like to learn in detail about the discovery of non-conservation of four-truth and combined four-truth in weak interactions. Does it follow from these discoveries that anticobalt-60 nuclei, polarized with spins in the same direction as cobalt-60 nuclei, will emit positrons in the case of a simple directed spin? And to what extent? Does the open asymmetry of space follow from these, or does the asymmetry of the elementary parts follow?

Andrei Pechkovsky from Moscow echoes Chernikkov: “It seems to me that it would be good if your journal published articles on higher mathematics and the latest achievements in it.”

Very rarely do we receive letters in which schoolchildren report that they were unable to solve problems or understand articles.

Unfortunately, we receive few letters from our readers with critical comments about the formation of the journal, about the advantages and disadvantages of individual sections of the journal and specific articles published in these sections.

In order to most fully take into account the wishes of our readers, we are publishing a questionnaire addressed to our readers on the pages of this issue of the magazine. We ask you to send us answers to the survey questions. Your letters will help us improve the magazine together.

The editors are grateful to all readers who sent specific critical comments on the materials placed in «Kvant». We are looking forward to receiving letters with answers to the survey questions and thank all readers in advance for these letters.

V. N. Berezin,
M. L. Smolyansky
