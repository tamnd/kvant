⟦folio 66⟧

The traditional physics-mathematics Olympiad of the Moscow Physical Institute in 1972 was held according to a new system. Instead of two rounds, each of which offered problems in both physics and mathematics, separate physical and mathematical rounds were held.

The physics tour was also the Moscow City Physics Olympiad. About 1,500 schoolchildren from Moscow and the Moscow region took part in the Olympiad. More than 100 schoolchildren who solved at least five problems were awarded with prizes and certificates of the Olympiad. Below are the conditions of the tasks.

Math assignments

1 (8 grades). Given a square $ABCD$. On the diagonal $AC$ the point $M$ is taken. The straight line $BM$ intersects the side $AD$ at the point $E$, and the straight line passing through $M$ and parallel to $BD$ at the point $F$. Prove that the line $FC$ and the line passing through the point $E$ parallel to the diagonal $AC$ intersect on the diagonal $BD$.

2 (8 grades). There are 6 points given on the plane. Each two of them are connected by a red or blue line. What is the smallest number of triangles with vertices at given points and sides of the same color that there can be?

3 (8 grades). Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$. Prove that a quadrilateral with vertices at the centers of gravity of triangles $ABC, ABD, ACD$ is similar to the given one.

4 (8 grades). For some systems of points on the plane, it is known that for any pair of them there is a triangle of the system point and that the angle between the segments drawn from this point to the original ones is less than 55°. Prove that in this system there is no pair of points the distance between which is greater than the distance between any other pair of points in the system.

5 (9-10 grades). Solve the following equations in natural numbers:

$$3 \cdot 2^x + 1 = y^2.$$

6 (9 grades). The numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ satisfy the conditions:

$$x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \ldots + x_n^2 = 1.$$

Prove that

$$(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (x_1 - x_3)^2 + \ldots + (x_1 - x_n)^2 + (x_2 - x_3)^2 + \ldots + (x_{n-1} - x_n)^2 \leq n.$$

7 (9 grades). Dan 1972-pleaser. Consider all triangles with vertices at the vertices of this polygon. Prove that every point on the plane that does not lie on one of the sides of these triangles is covered by an even number of such triangles.

8 (9 grades). Given a square. The two vertices of an equilateral triangle are on opposite sides of the square. Find the locus of the third vertices of such triangles.

9 (9 grades). 10 workers must produce 50 products. Each one must be painted and then mounted. Painting time - 10 minutes, installation time - 20 minutes, after painting the product should be 5 minutes. save. How to divide workers into painters and installers in order to complete the task in the shortest possible time?

10 (10 grades). In the knots of the checkered paper, 5 points are chosen so that no 3 of them lie on the same straight line. Prove that you can always find two of them and that the segment connecting these points contains at least one more node.

11 (10 grades). If a terrahedron has an inscribed and circumscribed concentric sphere, then the sum of the plane angles at each vertex is 180°. Prove.

12 (10 grades). The diameter of the circle is divided into $n$ equal parts. Point $M$ is taken on the circle. Prove that the sum of the squares of the distance from the point $M$ to the division point (including the ends of the diameter) does not depend on the choice of point $M$ on the circle.

13 (10 grades). Ali Baba wants to get into the treasure rack. In front of the cave there is a barrel, in the lid of which there are four holes, forming a square. Under the holes there is a jug, each of which contains a herring with its tail up or down. If the tails of all the herrings are directed in one direction, then the door to the cave opens. Ali Baba can stick his hand into any two holes, determine the position of the herrings in the jugs located under him, and change their position in his own way.
