⟦folio 5⟧

The popular experience of the existence and development of the Soviet Union is a triumph of Lenin’s teaching on the national question.

One of the most striking illustrations of the successful solution of the national question can be the development of science in the resource areas of the Soviet Union.

In place of the backward federal and received outskirts of Tsarist Russia, where the overwhelming majority of the population was illiterate, numerous cities grew up and became major scientific centers. All allied resource centers now have scientific centers. Many scientific institutes have nuclear reactors for resource use. One of the world's best atomic reactors designed for physical research was built in the Georgian SSR. One of the best electron accelerators in the world is located in Armenia.

Research important for science and the national economy is being carried out in physical institutes of Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.

All allied resource agencies have resource academies of sciences, uniting major scientists.

A number of allied resource forces have created world slavery for themselves. Many international unions have learned
head the Soetsk scientists. According to Nyantono, all this led to the fact that Soviet science in general had a huge influence on the development of science throughout the world.

Evidence of this is, for example, the fact that more Soviet scientific journals are translated into foreign languages.

Readers «Kvant» know that all-Union optimization of schoolchildren is taking place in the cities of a number of allied resource centers. Parts of these optimizations were carried out on our own experience regarding our products of different nationalities.

We are accustomed to such situations, and we take it all for granted.

But we must remember that the path to this “should” was not easy and simple. On this path, the Soviet people and the Communist Party had to offer considerable difficulties associated with economic and cultural stability, to fight against the attempt of the counter-revolution to use the legacy of former national enmity for their own purposes. And in this struggle, the Soviet people emerged victorious.

Now the Soviet experience of creating a multinational social state has received scientific recognition and serves as an example for fighters for socialism and national liberation around the world.

That is why the 50th anniversary of the Soviet Union will be inflated not only by the people of our country, but also by all progressive humanity of the world.
