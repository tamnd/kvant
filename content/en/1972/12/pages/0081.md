Dear reader!

The editors of the magazine «Kvant» will be very grateful to you if you send answers to the following questions:

1. How long have you been reading our magazine?

2. Which of the published materials do you find most interesting?

3. Which ones are unsuccessful, uninteresting?

4. Which sections do you like and which ones do you not? What sections would you like to see in the magazine?

5. Do you like the magazine as a whole or not?

6. Do you systematically solve problems from “Task «Kvant»!
