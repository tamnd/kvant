The natural potential energy of the triangle $W$ is determined by the area of ​​the triangle $OCD$:

$$W = \frac{1}{2} Fx = \frac{1}{2} kx^2.$$

The potential energy of the load transforms into the kinetic energy of its movement, and then into the energy of deformation of the load. Since the total height from which the load falls is $(l+x)$, then

$$mg(l+x) = \frac{1}{2} kx^2.$$

Hence the extension of the nigga is equal to

$$x = \frac{mg + \sqrt{m^2g^2 + 2kmgl}}{k},$$

and the maximum tension force of the bottom (since $k = \frac{SE}{l}$)

$$F = mg + \sqrt{(mg)^2 + 2mgES}.$$

Therefore, the maximum acceleration

$$a = g \left(1 + \sqrt{1 + \frac{2ES}{mg}}\right)$$

(obviously, it is reached at the lowest point).

Thus, neither the maximum force nor the maximum acceleration depends on the length of the leg.
