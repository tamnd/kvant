F139. The football player hit the ball, giving it speed $v$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon, and hit the near bottom corner. If a football player hit the ball in the same place on the football field and the ball flew at the same angle to the horizon, but at a speed 5% greater than the speed $v$, then it would hit the top of the goal post. Find the speed with which the ball begins to move if the height of the turn is $h=2\text{ m}$ and the angle is $\alpha=30^\circ$.

I. Sh. Slobodechkiy

F140. Will other barometers give correct readings of quality if part of its tube (below the level of mercury in it) is made of very soft rubber?

F141. Three wires are removed from the “black box” containing an unknown electrical circuit. Two of them are connected to ground and then to ground, plotting graphs for the three possible options for switching on the circuit. These graphs are shown in figure 1. The current is considered positive if it goes to the circuit located in the box and negative otherwise. Come up with the simplest diagrams of the contents of the “black box” and determine their parameters.

B. B. Bukhovtsev

F142. A steel ball suspended on a thread of length $l$ was deflected so that the thread assumed a horizontal position and was released. At the moment when the thread made an angle $\alpha-30^\circ$ with the vertical, the ball hit a stationary steel plate (Fig. 2). To what height does the ball rise after hitting the plate, if the impact can be considered absolutely elastic?

G. L. Komkin
