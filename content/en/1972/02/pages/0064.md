We are at a distance $a$ from the side face $u$ at a distance $b$ from the side edge. Find the radius of the sphere.

Solution. Let $O$ be the center of the sphere described near the paramid $SABCD$ (Fig. 4), $OS = OA = R$ the radius of this sphere, $SK$ the height of the paramid. Let's do $SL \perp BC$, $OM \perp SL$, $ON \perp AS$. According to the conditions of problem $OM = a$, $ON = b$ (prove it!).

Since $\triangle SOM \sim \triangle SKL$ and $\triangle SON \sim \triangle ASK$, then

$$\frac{a}{\sqrt{R^2 - a^2}} = \frac{KL}{SK},$$

$$\frac{b}{\sqrt{R^2 - b^2}} = \frac{AK}{SK} = \frac{\sqrt{2}KL}{SK}.$$

From here we find

$$\frac{b}{\sqrt{R^2 - b^2}} = \frac{a\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{R^2 - a^2}},$$

$$R = \frac{ab}{\sqrt{2a^2 - b^2}}.$$
