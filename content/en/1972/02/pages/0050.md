The hoop not only moves horizontally, but also rotates around the center. The acceleration of the rotational movement is imparted to it by the same forces $F$ and $F_{TP}$, but in this case they act “in concert”, spinning the hoop in one direction. The acceleration of the rotational motion of the hoop in the coordinate system associated with its center*) is denoted by $a_2$. Then we can write that

$$ma_2 = F + F_{TP} \quad (2)$$

Now let's write down the equation of motion of the dock. In the horizontal direction, only one force acts on it - the force of friction of the hoop on the dock. Therefore, if the acceleration of dock is $a_3$, then

$$Ma_3 = F_{TP}. \quad (3)$$

So, we have three equations involving four unknown quantities: $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$ and $F_{TP}$. To find these unknowns, you need to create another equation.

When moving the hoop and docks, two cases arise: 1) the hoop does not slip relative to the dock and 2) the hoop slips relative to the dock.

Since the forces acting on the hoop and dock are constant, the accelerations $a_1$, $a_2$ and $a_3$ are also constant. Let the velocities $v_1$, $v_2$ and $v_3$ of the body arrive after a time $t$ after the start of their motion. Then $v_1 = a_1 t$, $v_2 = a_2 t$ and $v_3 = a_3 t$. Hence,
