tea $EL^2 = KL^2 + KE^2 = a^2 + \frac{1}{3} y^2 + z^2 - ay - az$.

Now from the right triangle $EFL$ we find $EF^2 = FL^2 + EL^2$, that is
$$x^2 = a^2 - y(a - y) - z(a - z).$$

The last correspondence in itself is quite interesting and can be used to solve other problems.

Consider the plane passing through the lines $EF$ and $CD$. This plane intersects the sphere circumscribed about the triangle along the circle, which is indicated at figure 6. For this circle, the segments $MN$ and $CD$ are chords intersecting at the point $F$. Hence,
$$CF \cdot DF = MF \cdot NF$$
(prove it!), that is
$$y(a - y) = \frac{5}{12} x^2.$$

Similarly, from the equality
$$AE \cdot BE = ME \cdot NE$$
we get
$$z(a - z) = \frac{1}{3} x^2.$$

From correspondence (4), (5) and (6) we find
$$x^2 = a^2 - \frac{5}{12} x^2 - \frac{1}{3} x^2, \quad x = \frac{2a}{\sqrt{7}}.$$
