Problem 8. A conductor of length $l = 1$ m slides along horizontal rails in a vertical magnetic field with induction $B = 1000$ sec. The ends of the rails are connected to a capacitor with a capacity of $C = 1$ uF (Fig. 6). Determine the charge on the capacitor if the speed of the conductor is $v = 100$ m/sec.

The voltage across the capacitor $U$ is equal to the potential difference at the ends of the moving conductor: $U = Blv$. This means that the charge on the capacitor is $Q = CU = BlvC = 10^{-5}$ k.

Note that when the conductor moves at a constant speed, the charge on the capacitor remains unchanged and the current does not flow through the conductor.

Let's consider a more complex problem in which el. d. c. induction is induced in two interconnected circuits.

Problem 9. A conductor of length $l = 1$ m slides along horizontal rails in a vertical magnetic field with induction $B = 10^{-2}$ ml. The ends of the rails are closed to resistances $R_1 = 1$ om and $R_2 = 2$ om (Fig. 7). Determine the current flowing through the conductor if the speed of the conductor is $v = 10$ m/s. Neglect the resistance of the rails and conductor.

The conductor, rails and resistance form two circuits. E.d.c. the inductions induced in these circuits are identical in magnitude and equal to $Blv$ (see problem 5). However, they are directed in different directions. The area of ​​the circuit with resistance $R_1$ increases as the conductor moves. A current arises in the circuit, the magnetic field of which weakens the external field. The area of ​​the circuit with resistance $R_2$ decreases; the magnetic field of the current arising in it must add to the external field.

So through a moving conductor is equal to the sum of currents flowing in individual circuits: $I = I_1 + I_2$; $I_1 = E_{\text{инд}}/R_1 = Blv/R_1$; $I_2 = E_{\text{инд}}/R_2 = Blv/R_2$. Therefore $I = Blv(R_1 + R_2)/R_1 R_2 = 0,15$ a. The direction of current $I$ is shown in figure 7. If you change the direction of movement of the conductor, the directions of all currents will also change.
