LENINGRAD ENGINEERING AND CONSTRUCTION INSTITUTE

1. $-27$.
2. $2 \sin \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$. Specified. Using the reduction formula and matching
   $2 \sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} - 1 = -\cos \alpha$.
3. $\frac{d^3}{12} \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} \text{ctg} \frac{\alpha}{2} \text{tg} \beta$. Not indicated. Consider any right triangle formed by the side of a rhombus and its diagonals. Express in terms of $d$ and $\alpha$ its area and height lowered to the hypotenuse.
4. $x = 25$. Specified. Think of each term as powers of two.
5. $(b - a)^2$. Specified. Note that $\frac{1}{\log_b a} = \log_a b$.

LENINGRAD INSTITUTE
PRECISION MECHANICS AND OPTICS

1. $\sqrt{x}$.
2. $x = -2$. Specified. One of the most beautiful methods for solving this equation is to “guess” the root $x = -2$. After this, prove that the equation has no other roots, since there is a strictly increasing function to the left of the equation.
3. $x = \frac{\pi}{8} + \frac{k\pi}{4}$ or $x = \pm \frac{\pi}{9} + \frac{2\pi n}{3}$ ($k, n$ are any integers). Specified. Notice $\cos 7x + \cos x$ and $\cos^2 2x - \sin^2 2x$ using known correspondences.

LENINGRAD
HYDROMETEOROLOGICAL INSTITUTE

1. $a^x$. Specified. Present all radical expressions sequentially in the form of squares.
2. $x = \frac{1}{12}$ or $x = \frac{9}{4}$. Specified. Go to new unknown $y = \log_3 4x$.
3. $\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = 0, \\ y = \pm \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \end{array} \right.$ or $\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = 1, \\ y = 1 \end{array} \right.$ or $\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = -1, \\ y = -1 \end{array} \right.$
Specified. Eliminate terms that do not contain unknowns and arrive at an equation for $xy$.
4. $x = \pm \frac{\pi}{3} + k\pi$ ($k$ is any integer). Specified. Move the expression to the left side of the equation to the common denominator and expand $1 - \sin^4 x$ using the difference of squares formula.

LENINGRAD FORESTRY ACADEMY NAMED AFTER S. M. KIROV

1. $a_1 = \frac{2}{3}$; $q = 2$. Specified. Go to the unknowns $a_1$ and $q$, where $q$ denotes the denominator of the progression. Divide the first equation by the second and solve the resulting equation for $q$ using polynomial factorization.
2. $x = 5$. Specified. Present the equation to a common denominator, then use the formula for the logarithm of the power and the product.
3. $x = 2k\pi$ or $x = (-1)^{k+1} \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$ ($k$ is any integer). Specified. Using the formula $\cos x = 1 - 2 \sin^2 \frac{x}{2}$.
4. $\frac{R^2 \text{tg} \alpha}{\cos \varphi} \sqrt{1 - \text{tg}^2 \alpha \text{tg}^2 \varphi}$. Specified. Draw a plane through the cones and a radius perpendicular to the chords. Examine the resulting rectangular triangles.
