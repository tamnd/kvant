The emergence of light

Sunlight and the flame of a match, the light of neon advertising and the glow of hot metal, despite every difference, have the same nature. The appearance of light in all sources is due to changes occurring in atoms.

According to Bohr's theory, the atom of any element can only be in the order of certain discrete states. Each state corresponds to a certain internal energy atoms $E_n$. Possible energy values ​​of atoms are called energy levels and are indicated on the level diagram by horizontal straight lines (see Fig. 2, a).
