Task 6. Determine the potential difference arising between the ends of the wings of an airplane flying horizontally at a speed $v = 800 \text{ km/час}$. The distance between the ends of the wings is $l = 30 \text{ m}$. Vertical component of the Earth's magnetic field induction $B = 0,5 \text{ sec}$.

Using the formula $U = Blv$ and changing to the SI system: $v = 800 \text{ km/час} \approx 222 \text{ m/c}$, $B = 0,5 \text{ sec} = 5 \cdot 10^{-5} \text{ m/l}$, we find $U = 0,33 \text{ sec}$. The horizontal component of the Earth's magnetic field can be ignored, since it lies in the plane of motion of the aircraft.

Note that the resulting potential difference cannot be changed with a voltmeter located in the air itself, connecting it to the ends of the wings. The magnetic flux through the circuit formed by the wings of the airplane and the voltmeter with the wires does not change when the airplane moves, and the current through the voltmeter is zero.

Task 7. A horizontal conductor of length $l = 1 \text{ m}$ moves in a vertical magnetic field with indication $B = 1000 \text{ sec}$. The speed of the conductor $v = 100 \text{ m/c}$ forms an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with the conductor itself (Fig. 5).

Determine the potential difference at the ends of the conductor.

We can again imagine such a conductor sliding along horizontal rails, closed to an infinitely large resistance. Let us determine the change in magnetic flux over time $\Delta t$ through the circuit formed by the conductor, resistance and rails. The contour area changes by values ​​$\Delta S = lv \cdot \Delta t \cdot \sin \alpha$, hence $\Delta \Phi = Blv \cdot \Delta t \cdot \sin \alpha$.

A change in magnetic flux causes the appearance of e in the circuit. d.s. indication $E_{\text{инд}} = \frac{\Delta \Phi}{\Delta t} = Blv \sin \alpha$, which is equal to the potential difference $U$ at the ends of the conductor. Substituting numerical data, we find $U = 8,65 \text{ sec}$.

When the magnetic flux changes through a circuit containing a capacitor, a charge appears on the capacitor.
