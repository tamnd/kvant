⟦folio 45⟧

Option 1

1. Given a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$, all edges of which are equal to $a$. A plane is drawn through the vertex $A$ of the base, parallel to the diagonal $BD$ and forming an angle $30^\circ$ with the plane of the base. Find the cross-sectional area.

2. Two points move uniformly along two circles whose radii are in the ratio 1:6. Determine the speed of movement of each point, if it is known that in 10 sectors they move in a larger circle, it has traveled 2 m more and made 5 times fewer revolutions.

3. Solve inequality

$$\frac{x+1}{x-1}+1>\frac{x-1}{x}.$$

4. Solve the equation

$$\log_{\sqrt{2}} \sin x(1+\cos x)=2.$$

Option 2

1. The base of the paramid $SABC$ is the equilateral triangle $ABC$, which has $AB=AC=b$, $BC=a$. Determine the cross-sectional area passing through the edge $SA$ and perpendicular to the plane of the base, if the side faces form equal dihedral angles $\alpha$ with the plane of the base.

2. Two machine guns left at the same time from point $A$ to point $B$. The speed of the second machine is 10 km/h greater than the speed of the first. Half an hour later, the third machine gun left from $A$ to $B$ at a speed of 60 km/h, which caught up with the first machine gun and, an hour and a half after that, caught up with the second machine gun. Find the speed of the machines.

$$\frac{x+3}{\sqrt{x-1}}=\sqrt{3x+1}.$$

4. Solve the equation:

$$\sin x(1+\cos x)=1+\cos x+\cos^2 x.$$

$$\lg[(6\alpha-5)x^2-5(\alpha-1)x+2\alpha-6]$$

3. Solve the equation:

$$\mathrm{tg} x+\frac{\cos x}{1+\sin x}=2.$$
