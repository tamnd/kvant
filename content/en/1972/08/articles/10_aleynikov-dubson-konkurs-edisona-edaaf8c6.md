B. I. Aleinikov,
M. S. Dubson

“I just can’t find a landowner,” Edison once complained to Einstein. “Young people come in every day, but not a single one is suitable.”

— How do you determine their naturalness? - Edison asked and showed him a piece of paper with questions:

“Whoever answers them will become my landowner.”

“How many miles is it from New York to Chicago?” - Edison ate and replied: “You need to look at the railway directory.”

“What do we make stainless steel from?”— “You can find out about this in the reference book on metallurgy...”

After skimming the rest of the questions, Edison concluded: “Without waiting for a refusal, I will withdraw my candidacy.”

The loss of the columnar landowner did not cool the determined American inventor, however. His name is associated with the first major school competition in the United States - a kind of all-American Olympiad in natural sciences. In 1929, Edison decided to reward the most intelligent and knowledgeable schoolchild in his country with a large degree. Special commissions headed by state governors selected one young man each as the winner in his state. (The ability of American girls in the exact sciences at that time, apparently, was not rated very highly.)

Forty-nine people were presented before the jury, established by Edison, and had to answer 57 questions in writing. This included six questions each from mathematics, physics and chemistry, as well as 39 questions to determine the “general development” of the subjects.

Edison defined the objectives of the competition as follows:

— The perfect goal is to choose the best from the best. I realized that only the future will be able to show whether our choice is correct, since the versatility of human nature is too great to change on such a scale. Life and human relationships are too diverse to be reduced to one formula...

Edison clearly preferred formulaic education and empirical knowledge and common sense, which are so necessary for inventive work. This was reflected in the preparation of questions for the competition. Try answering questions like these:

What is the difference between noise and musical sounds?

If the church order is not weakened by the sub-regulatory device, we will not cerrate it when it is cold in the church. Why?

Here is one of the questions about chimini:

What facts do you associate in your mind with the names of the following scientists (answer in one or two words): Mendleev, Dzvi, Faraday, Curie, Pristil, Gay-Lussac, Dalmon, Solvay, Ramsay, Lavouzier?
