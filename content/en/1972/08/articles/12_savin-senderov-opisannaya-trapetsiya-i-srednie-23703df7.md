DESCRIBED TRAPEZE AND MEDIUM

Take a look at figure. A circumscribed trapezoid in which the height $BH$ and the segment $HG$ perpendicular to the side $AB$ are drawn. It would seem that there is nothing special about this figure, but this drawing has an amazing property. It, together with the upper base $a$ and the lower base $b$, already contains all three means of these segments: the arthmetic mean, the geometric mean and the harmonic mean, namely:

$$AB = \frac{a+b}{2},$$

$$BH = \sqrt{ab},$$

$$BG = \frac{2}{\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}}.$$

Try to prove these statements yourself.

And now just one glance at the drawing is enough to make sure that the following are done between the averages:

$$\frac{a+b}{2} \geq \sqrt{ab} \geq \frac{2}{\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}}.$$

Indeed, $AB$ and $BH$ are oblique and perpendicular drawn to the line $AD$ from the point $B$, and $BH$ and $BG$ are oblique and perpendicular from the point $B$ to the line $HG$.

A. Savin, V. Senderov
