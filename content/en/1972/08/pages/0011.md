HIGH GAVENESS-CONSTITUTION AND DIMENSION

The properties of a substance depend on the distances between its atoms. As the mechanical distances decrease, the density of the substance increases, the electrical, optical, mechanical properties, and crystal structure change. By studying the properties of liquid substances, we learned more deeply about the nature and nature of the forces acting between atoms.

How to combine atoms in matter?
Let's take a cube of the substance and begin to compress it under pressure. As a result of compression along one axis, we will cause deformation in this cube, the distance between its atoms will decrease. However, we will not get significant deformations. Once the limit is reached, destruction will set in firmly.

How can we combine atoms in matter even more strongly? To do this, you need to compress the substance from all sides, that is, apply equal forces simultaneously to all sides of our cube, subjecting it to all-round high pressure. In this case, we can obtain atoms in a substance without destruction, change its physical properties, and force chemical processes to proceed differently.

The pressure range in nature is enormous. At the bottom of the ocean of air we live at a pressure of one atmosphere. A column of water presses on the diver's eardrums, and special diving suits are needed for deep-sea diving. According to calculations by geophysicists, the pressure in the center of the Earth is about 4 million atmospheres.

In the morning you open the water tap and water flows into the washbasin with a strong stream. The water pressure in the water supply is 1.5-2 atmospheres. Breakfast was prepared on a stove, to which gas was supplied at a pressure of 1.01 amp.

On the mismo street, trolley buses and buses rush past you. In their tires the air capacity is up to 2.5-4 amps. In the engine cylinders of the fast-moving Volga, the pressure reaches 30-50 amps. Power plant turbines are driven by steam at a pressure of 200-500 amps. At chemical industry plants, polyethylene is produced by skimming ethylene gas to a pressure of 1000 amps, and ammonia is synthesized from nitrogen and hydrogen at a pressure of 1500 amps. When turning graphig into diamond, the pressure reaches 100,000 amps and the temperature reaches 2000 degrees.

Let us define pressure as the force with which a gas or liquid enclosed in a tank acts per unit area of ​​the wall of this tank:

$$P = \frac{F}{S}.$$

Using this simple formula, you can set the units for measuring pressure. Putting the force equal to one dyne and the area equal to one square centimeter, we get the unit of pressure in the CGS system - $1 \frac{\delta \ln}{cm^2}$, which is called barney. However, this unit is too small for measuring high pressures, and the cup uses a million times more: $1 \text{ бар} = 10^8 \frac{\delta \ln}{cm^2}$, and its derivatives: kilobar, equal to $10^3 \text{ бар}$ or $10^9 \frac{\delta \ln}{cm^2}$; medabar equal to $10^6 \text{ бар}$.
