On the first cover page and on the adjacent figure there are photographs of kaleidoscon images. From these photographs you can determine at what point the shooting took place.

The fact is that rays of light are reflected in mirrors that form a certain angle between themselves, but not at the apex of this angle; therefore, a light line emanates from each vertex. If we continue these lines inside the base of the kaleido, then they will all intersect at one point, above which the camera lens was installed. Try to find this point.

Where do such lines come from, is it possible to get two images in a kaleidoscon at once in one photograph, and some other questions are discussed in the article by A. N. Vilenkin “Kaleidoscons”, located on pp. 41-49.
