To the note “Described trapezoid and among”
(see see 49)

The lateral side of the trapezoid is the average arithmetic base - this follows from the fact that in the circumscribed quadrilateral the sums of the lengths of the opposite sides are equal.

Let's express the height $BH$ through the bases
$$BH^2 = AB^2 - AH^2 = \left[ \frac{1}{2} (AD + BC) \right]^2 - \left[ \frac{1}{2} (AD - BC) \right]^2 = AD \cdot BC = a \cdot b.$$
Finally, we express the segment $BG$ through the bases of the trapezoid. To do this, note that the triangles $ABH$ and $HBG$ are similar, hence $\frac{BH}{BG} = \frac{AB}{BH}$. Hence,
$$\frac{1}{BG} = \frac{AB}{BH^2} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} (AD - BC)}{AD \cdot BC} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} \right).$$
