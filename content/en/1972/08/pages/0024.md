The devil disappeared very slowly. Apparently he was pretty tired.

Simon kissed his wife deeply. But she, with a dissatisfied grimace, looked at the face of her husband, who was hovering somewhere in the clouds, and asked:

- Darling, what else is wrong?

- No, nothing... But, you know, I would like to get acquainted with his work, to find out how close he came to the solution. I was over that problem no less...

He did not finish speaking and widened his eyes in amazement: the devil had once again appeared in the room. He looked very embarrassed.

“I forgot here...” he muttered. - I need... ah!

He bent over the scattered papers and began to carefully collect and smooth them out. “This thing is fascinating,” he said, avoiding his gaze.

The farm undoubtedly took advantage of him, and... Please, leave us alone.

The last words were addressed to Mrs. Flagg. The devil sat down next to Simon, tucked his tail under himself, and pointed to the sheets of paper covered in mathematical symbols.

Mrs. Flagg sighed. The devil, immersed in his mind, suddenly seemed very familiar to her: he was almost no different from the old Professor Atkins, her husband’s colleague at the university. Let two mathematicians delve deeper into the study of some painful and tempting problem, and they...

She obediently left the room with a coffee pot in her hand. There was undoubtedly a long, tedious discussion ahead. Mrs. Flagg was sure of that. After all, it was not for nothing that she was the wife of a famous mathematician.
