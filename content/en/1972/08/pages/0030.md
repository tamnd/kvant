On figure 1 there is a magnitude scale indicating the magnitudes of some celestial objects. From figure you can see how much surveillance capabilities are expanded when using phones.

At the astronomical observatory, located near the Zelenchukskaya station in the Stavropol Territory, the installation of the world's largest reflective telephone with a mirror diameter of 6 m (Fig. 2) is now being completed. This phone allows you to visually observe stars coming to us with $\left(\frac{6000}{6}\right)^2 = 10^6$ times (15 magnitude) less light than 6th magnitude stars visible to the naked eye, that is, to see stars of 6+15=21st magnitude.

However, 21st magnitude is not the limit for the penetrating power of a six-meter phone. Using special photo emulsins, it will be possible with this phone to photograph stars 2-3 magnitudes larger with exposures of several hours.
