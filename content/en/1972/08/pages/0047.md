For example, on figure 7 the fundamental polygon is shown in black, one sequence of reflections is in red, and another in blue. At point $X$ they overlap. The question arises: should we see two images or one at point $X$, and if one, then which one?

First, let's look at the following question: from which points in space (inside the kaleidoscope) the image $A_1$ is visible, but from others - not. But point $A_1$ is a point on the picture plane, what is visible in it from other positions? Obviously, an image of some point of the fundamental polygon, but reflected in another mirror.

For a different observation point, a different sequence of reflections and a different image may be obtained.
