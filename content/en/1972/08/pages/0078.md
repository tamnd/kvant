YEARS OF THE QUIET SUN 1964/1965
THE YEARS OF THE QUIET SUN 1964-1965

STAMPS DEDICATED TO THE INTERNATIONAL YEAR
GOOD SUN

The sun sends more than just visible light to us on Earth. It is a source of various invisible electromagnetic radiation and flows of charged particles. These radiations have a huge impact on the state of the atmosphere, causing auroras, magnetic storms, fading of radio waves and many other effects. Solar activity changes periodically at intervals of 11 years. The International Geophysical Year, the stamps in honor of which we introduced to readers in the second issue of Quat for 1972, took place during a period of exceptionally high activity. In order to compare the information obtained then about the Sun and the earth's atmosphere with similar information obtained from observations during a period of low activity, scientists decided to repeat an extensive program of international research. In 1964-1965, the International Year of the Quiet Sun (YYYS) was held. Many countries participating in this program have issued special postage stamps dedicated to it.
