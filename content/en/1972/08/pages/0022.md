“No,” Simon interrupted him harshly. — We signed half an hour ago. Return exactly in twenty-three and a half hours. “I won’t rush you,” he added ironically when the devil looked at his watch with alarm. “Have a glass of wine and before you go, meet my wife.”
“I never drink at work, and I don’t have time to get to know your wife... at least not now.”
He disappeared.
At the same moment Simon's wife entered.
- Waiting at the door again! Simon chided her gently.
“Of course,” she said in a choked voice. “And I want to know, dear, whether this question is really difficult.” Because if it's not... Simon, I'm absolutely terrified!
“Be calm, the question is difficult,” Simon answered nonchalantly. — Not all of this is immediately clear. You see,” he continued in the tone of a lecturer, “everyone can easily find two integers whose squares also add up to a square. For example, $3^2+4^2=5^2$, that is, just $9+16=25$. Clear?
- Yes.
She straightened her husband's tie.
- But I couldn’t find two cubes that, when added, would also give a cube or higher powers that would lead to a similar result - apparently, they simply don’t exist. And yet,” he concluded triumphantly, “it has not yet been proven that such numbers do not exist!”
Do you understand now?
- Certainly. — Simon's wife always understood the most sophisticated mathematical concepts. And if there was a stumbling block, her husband patiently explained everything to her several times. This left Mrs. Flagg little time for other things.
“I’ll cook ksfe,” she said and left.
Four hours later, as they sat listening to Brahms's third symphony, the devil appeared again.
— I have already learned the basics of algebra, trionometry and planimetry! - he announced triumphantly.
- Work quickly! - Simon shook it. - I am sure that spherical, analytical, projective, descriptive and non-Euclidean geometry will not present any difficulties for you.
The devil winced.
- Are there so many of them? - he asked in a fallen voice.
- Oh, that's not all. - Simon looked as if he had good news. “You’ll like the non-Euclidean one,” he cajoled. - To do this, you will not need to understand the drawings. The drawings won't say anything. And at times he is at odds with Euclid...
The devil groaned, faded like an old film, and disappeared. Simon's wife giggled.
“My dear,” she sang, “I’m beginning to think that you will prevail!”
