Faculty of Chemistry

1. An equilibrium trapezoid $ABCD$ is described around a circle of radius $r$. E and K are the points of contact of this circle with the sides of the trapezoid. The angle between the base $AB$ and the side $AD$ of the trapezoid is $60^\circ$. Prove that line $EK$ is parallel to line $AB$ and find the area of ​​trapezoid $ABEK$.

2. Solve the equation:

$$2 \log \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}(x-0,5)} + x = x^0, 5x^2 + \cos^4\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right).$$

3. Three excavators were tasked with digging a pit: the 1st and 2nd - with a capacity of 800 $m^3$, and the 3rd - with a capacity of 400 $m^3$. The 1st and 2nd excavators together remove more soil per hour; the second excavator removes more soil than the 3rd. The 1st and 3rd excavators began working at the same time, and the 2nd at the moment when the 1st removed $300 m^3$ of soil. When the 3rd excavator had completed 2/3 of its work, the 2nd removed $100 m^3$ of soil. The 3rd excavator was the first to complete its task. How much soil had the 1st excavator removed by the time the 3rd had finished digging its pit?

4. Solve inequality:

$$1 - \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} + \log_3 \sin x - \log_3 \cos x} > 0.$$

5. A regular right triangular feature $ABCA'B'C'$ is described around a ball of radius $r$. Point $M$ is the middle of the side edge $BB'$, point $N$ is the middle of the side edge $CC'$. A right circular cylinder is inscribed into a ball so that its base lies in the $AMN$ plane. Find the volume of this cylinder.

Faculty of Physics and Mathematics

1. Point $A$ is located at a distance of $s$ km from point $B$. A pedestrian left from point $A$ to point $B$, and after $T$ hours a cyclist left in the same direction. The cyclist caught up with the pedestrian $p$ km from $A$, reached $B$ and immediately turned back. Having traveled $p$ km from $B$, the cyclist again met the pedestrian and, continuing his journey, returned to $A$ later than the pedestrian arrived at $B$. To what extent is the pedestrian's arrival at $B$ equal to that of the cyclist's return at $A$?

2. Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} 2u + v + w = 6, \\ 3u + 2v + w = 9, \\ 3u^3 + 2v^3 + w^3 = 27. \end{cases}$$

3. Solve inequality:

$$\cos x \cdot \cos 3x + \cos^2 2x < -\frac{1}{4}.$$

4. A ball of radius $R$ is high as a pyramid with a square base. However, the side edges of the pyramid are perpendicular to the plane of the base, and the largest side edge forms an angle $\alpha$ with it. Determine the lateral surface of the pyramid.

Faculty of Engineering

1. The distance between two cities is 600 km; a freight train travels 8 hours slower than a passenger train. If the speed of each train is increased by 10 km/h, then the passenger train will continue the same only 5 hours faster.

commodity. Determine the speed of each train.

2. Solve the equation:

$$2\cos 2x \cdot (\text{ctg } x - 1) = 1 + \text{ctg } x.$$

3. Solve the equation:

$$|x^2 - 2x - 4| = 3x - 2.$$

4. The height of the cylinder is equal to the height of the cone. The lateral surface of the cylinder is related to the lateral surface of the cone as 3:2. In addition, it is known that the angle made by the edge of the cone with the plane of the base is equal to $\alpha$. Find the ratio of the volume of the cylinder to the volume of the cone.
