The following trivial problem can serve as an illustration. “One glass contains ten spoons of water, the other contains ten spoons of wine. The question is, what will be more: wine in the first glass or water in the second?
Obviously, in order to answer this question, we can continue with the following calculation. After a spoonful of water was poured into the second glass, there were 10 spoons of wine and 1 spoon of water, that is, a total of 11 spoons of liquid. Thus, one spoon of the mixture contains $\frac{10}{11}$ spoons of wine and $\frac{1}{11}$ spoons of water.
