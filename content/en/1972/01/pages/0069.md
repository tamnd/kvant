⟦folio 67⟧

Problem conditions
introductory work control
in 3MSh 1972

1. A motorcycle race is held on the ring road. The start and finish are in the same place. What is the smallest number of stages in this race if the length of the ring road is 330 km, and the length of each stage is 75 km? (The road is one-way.)
2. In a triangle, the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles are symmetrical to one of the sides. Find the angles of the triangle.
3. Let $x_1$ and $x_2$ be the roots of the equation $x^2+ax+bc=0$, and $x_1$ and $x_3$ the roots of the equation $x^2+cx+ab=0$. Prove that $x_2$ and $x_3$ are roots of the equation $x^2+bx+ac=0$ if $ac$ is not equal to $bc$.
4. Two people play this game. In front of him, several minutes are drawn on paper in a chain. Each person takes turns transporting one or two neighboring minutes onto the plane. The one who crosses the last minute wins. Who will win when running the game: the beginner or his partner and how much he needs for this game, if at the beginning it is drawn: a) 7 minutes; b) 8 minutes; c) n minutes?
5. In triangle ABC, bissertrix AK and median AM are drawn. What can the ratio of the sides AB and AC be equal to if it is known that one of the segments BM, MK, KC is equal to half the sum of the other two?
6. In what year was Matvey born, if in 1972 he turned that age, what is the sum of the digits of the year of his birth?
7. Is there at least one number $a$ such that both numbers $a+V\sqrt{15}$ and $\frac{1}{a}-V\sqrt{15}$ are integers?
8. $AC$ and $BD$ are two mutually perpendicular chords of a circle. A perpendicular dropped onto line $CD$ from point $A$ intersects line $BD$ at point $M$, and a perpendicular dropped onto line $CD$ from point $B$ intersects line $AC$ at point $K$. Prove that $ABKM$ is a rhombus.
9. One of the brothers' heels broke the window. Andrey said: “It’s either Vitya or Tolya.” Vitya said: “It was not Yura who did this.” Tolya said: “You both are telling lies.” Dima said: “No, one of them told the truth, and the other didn’t.” Yura said: “No, Dima, you’re wrong.” Their father, who of course can be trusted, is sure that at least three brothers told the truth. Who broke the window?
10. Among all the triangles whose sum of medians is 3 cm, find the triangle with the largest sum of heights.
11. Come up with four non-negative integers so that each number from 1 to 81 can be represented as the sum of four numbers - one from each triple.
12. Given an equilateral triangle ABC. About the point $M$ it is known that $\angle AMC = 30^\circ$, $\angle BMA = 40^\circ$. $\angle ABM > 90^\circ$. Find $\angle MBC$.
