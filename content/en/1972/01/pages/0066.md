8. The vessel contains a mixture of nitrogen and hydrogen. At a temperature T, when nitrogen has completely disintegrated into atoms, and hydrogen is still in a molecular state, the pressure is equal to P. At a temperature of 2 T, when both gases have completely disintegrated into atoms, the pressure in the vessel is equal to 3P. What is the ratio of the number of gram atoms of nitrogen and hydrogen in the mixture?

Introductory math assignment

1. The boy swims against the current of the river and meets an empty boat floating along the current. He continues to swim against the boat for another 2 minutes after the meeting point, and then turns and catches up with the boat 76 meters from the meeting point. What is the speed of the river?

2. Prove that the amount
$$n^3 + 3n^2 + 5n + 3$$
for any integer n is divisible by 3 without remainder.

3. Triangle ABC is inscribed in a circle. At point B a tangent to the circle is drawn. The distances from points A and C to this tangent are 15 cm and 21 cm. Find the height of triangle ABC dropped from vertex B.

4. The roots of the equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ are $x_1$ and $x_2$. Find p and q if $x_1 + 1$ and $x_2 + 1$ are roots of the equation
$$x^2 - p^2x + pq = 0.$$

5. Two sides of a triangle are equal to 6 cm and 8 cm, and its two medians intersect at right angles. Find the third side of the triangle.
