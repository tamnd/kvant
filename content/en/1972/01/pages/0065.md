An additional weight $m=10 \text{ e}$ is placed on the right weight and the system begins to move. When the right load passes the distance $1 \text{ m}$, the additional load is picked up by a special stop, and the main loads continue to move further at a constant speed $v=0,99 \frac{M}{\text{сек}}$.
