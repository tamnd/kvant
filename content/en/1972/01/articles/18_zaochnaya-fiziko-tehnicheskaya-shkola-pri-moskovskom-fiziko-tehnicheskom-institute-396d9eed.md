The correspondence school of physics and technology was created in 1966 with the aim of providing assistance to students in grades 9 and 10 of secondary schools. To study at 3FTSH you do not need any special abilities, the main thing is a love of science and a desire to work hard.

3FTS strives to develop students' physical intuition and logical thinking, as well as skills in using mathematical methods when studying issues of physics and technology.

Admission of students to 3FTS is limited and is carried out by competition based on the completion of the introductory task published below. In order to cover the opportunities of a large number of people wishing to study at 3FTSH, locally, in agreement with public education organizations, the organization of physics and technology circles.

Students who want to and have completed the introductory task well, but could not be accepted due to lack of space, are enrolled in these circles. Education in circles will be conducted according to the general program of 3FTS and under its methodological guidance, but with a merger of local physics and mathematics teachers.

In addition, physics and technology circles can be organized on the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. Recruitment for these circles is carried out on the spot from among the students who have solved the introductory task of 3FTS.

Enrollment in circles is carried out by the circle leader. A circle is considered organizational if the director of a local school reports to 3FTSH the names of its two leaders, a list of members of the circle and a summary sheet of grades received by members of the circle when completing the introductory task. Further work of the circle will take place within 3FTSH.

Students admitted directly to study at 3FTS, as well as heads of physics and technology circles, will receive regulated assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program. The tasks are designed in such a way as to develop students' independence, initiative, physical intuition and logical thinking. Students admitted directly to 3FTS will receive an analysis of the results of their assignments, as well as solutions to tasks recommended by 3FTS. The leaders of the circles will receive the solutions to the assignments recommended by 3FTSH, and they will analyze and evaluate each student’s work themselves.

Below are introductory tasks in physics and mathematics, each consisting of seven tasks. The tasks are tested in order from 1 to 10. Tasks 1-7 are intended for eighth grade students, tasks 4-10 for ninth grade.

Introductory math assignment

1. The boy swims against the current of the river and meets an empty boat floating along the current. He continues to swim against the boat for another 2 minutes after the meeting point, and then turns and catches up with the boat 76 meters from the meeting point. What is the speed of the river?

2. Prove that the amount
$$n^3 + 3n^2 + 5n + 3$$
for any integer n is divisible by 3 without remainder.

4. The roots of the equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ are $x_1$ and $x_2$. Find p and q if $x_1 + 1$ and $x_2 + 1$ are roots of the equation
$$x^2 - p^2x + pq = 0.$$
