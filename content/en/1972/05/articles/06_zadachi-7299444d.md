TASKS

Solutions to problems can be sent no later than one and a half months after the publication of the corresponding issue of the journal to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, publishing house "Nauka", editorial office of the journal «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending; for example, “Problem «Kvant», M137, F139. TASKS

M141. Let us choose an arbitrary point P at the height ВН of triangle ABC. Let K be the point of intersection of lines AP and BC, L be the point of intersection of lines CP and AB. Prove that the segments KH and LH make equal angles with the height ВH.
E. V. Sallinen

M142. a) Prove that it is impossible to number the edges of a cube with the numbers 1,2,...,12 so that for each vertex the sum of the numbers of the three edges leaving it is the same.
6)* Is it possible to cross out one of the numbers 1,2,...,12, 13 and use the remaining numbers to number the edges of the cube so that the same condition is satisfied?
N. N. Konstantinov,
H. B. Vasiliev

M143. Find the smallest natural number n for which the following condition holds:

if n is divisible by p - 1 and p is prime, then n is divisible by p.

M144. Find the necessary and sufficient conditions that the numbers a, b, a, β must satisfy so that the rectangle a×b can be cut into several rectangles α×β.

For example, is it possible to cut a 50×60 rectangle into rectangles with dimensions:
a) 20×15; 6) 5×8; c) 6.25×15;
d) (2−√2)×(2+√2).

A. T. Kolomov

M145. A total pay B on average √2 rubles. per day. They stipulated that on the nth day A will receive an integer number aₙ rubles (aₙ is equal to 1 or 2) so that the amount received in the first n days (a₁+a₂+...+aₙ) is as close as possible to the number n√2.

For example, a₁=1, a₂=2, a₃=1, a₄=2, a₅=1. Prove that the sequence a₁, a₂, a₃, ... is non-periodic.

A. K. Crowd

F153. Why do rivers that flow even across flat, homogeneous soil bend?
For simplicity, consider a river flowing along the screen.

F154. Estimate how many droplets of water there are in 1 m³ of fog if visibility is 10 m and the fog settles after 2 hours? Fog height 200 m.
