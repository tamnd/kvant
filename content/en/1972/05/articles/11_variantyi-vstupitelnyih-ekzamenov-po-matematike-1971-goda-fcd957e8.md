⟦folio 43⟧

Option 1

1. Three quantities left point $A$, the first for one hour of work of the others, which started at the same time. The speed of each velocities is constant. After some time, the third one caught up with the first one, and the second one caught up with the first one two hours later than the third one. Determine the ratio of the speeds of the first and third vehicles, if the ratio of the speed of the second to the speed of the third is 2/3.

2. Solve the equation:
$$tg x + tg 2x + tg 3x + tg 4x = 0.$$

3. The inscribed circle of triangle $ABC$ divides the median $BM$ into three equal parts. Find the ratio $BC:CA:AB$.

4. Find all solutions to the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} y^2 - xy + x^2 = z^2, \\ x^2 - xz + z^2 = y^2, \\ z^3 - y^3 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2. \end{cases}$$

5. Given a regular triangular paramid $SABC$ ($S$ is the vertex). The edge $SC$ of this paramid coincides with the side edge of the right triangular feature $A_1B_1CA_2B_2S$ ($A_1A_2, B_1B_2$ and $CS$ are the side edges; $A_1B_1C$ is one of the bases). The vertices $A_1$ and $B_1$ lie in the plane of the face $SAB$ of the paramid. Of the volume of the entire paramida, what should be the volume of its part lying inside the paramata, if the ratio of the length of the lateral edge of the paramida to the length of the side of the paramida base is equal to $2/\sqrt{3}$?

Option 2

1. If a two-digit number is divided by the product of its digits, then the partial will be 3, and the remainder will be 9. If the product of its digits is subtracted from the square of the sum of the digits of this number, then the given number will be obtained. Find this number.

2. Solve the equation:
$$\sin x + \sqrt{1 + \sin 2x} - \cos x = \frac{1}{2}.$$

3. In triangle $ABC$, on side $AC$, point $M$ is taken, and on side $BC$, point $N$. Segments $AN$ and $BM$ intersect at point $O$. Find the area of ​​triangle $CMN$ if the area of ​​triangles $OMA, OAB$ and $OBN$ are equal to $S_1, S_2, S_3$ respectively.

4. Find all solutions to the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} y + z = 2x, \\ y^2 + 3z^2 = 28x^2, \\ y^3 + 8z^3 = (y - 4x)(1 - 4z + 7xy). \end{cases}$$

5. At the base of the paramid $SABC$ lies a regular triangle $ABC$, and all lateral faces have equal areas. Edge $SA$ is equal to 2 cm, edge $SB$ is equal to $\sqrt{2}$ cm. A section of the paramid is drawn through vertex $B$ perpendicular to edge $SC$. Find the area of ​​this section.

Moscow Institute of Radio Engineering, Electronics and Automation

Option 1

1. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{3}x + \sqrt{3}y = 3, \\ x + y = 9. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{\log_2 x - 1}{\log_2 \frac{x}{2}} - 2 \log_2 \sqrt{x} + \log_2^2 x = 3.$$

3. Find the solution to the equation:
$$tg\left(\frac{5\pi}{2} + x\right) - 3 tg^2 x = (\cos 2x - 1) \sec^2 x,$$
satisfying conditions $x > 3$.

4. About the angles of the triangle $ABC$ it is known that $tg A : tg B : tg C = 1 : 2 : 3$. Find the ratio of the sines of these angles.

5. Prove that the polynomial $x^8 - x^5 + x^2 - x + 1$ is positive for all real $x$.
