QUANTUM
FOR JUNIOR SCHOOL CHILDREN

1. There is a hoop leaning against the wall.
At one point in the wall "inside"
a nail is driven into the hoop so that the hoop
touches him. Find geometric
place of points where it is necessary to drive
the second nail inside the hoop to
the hoop remained motionless.

2. In the proposed encryption
numbers are encrypted with signs and
signs +, -, =. Every line
encryption contains a recording of one
from arithmetic operations like:
25+184=209 or 2568=2573—5.
Try to determine which
number or sign corresponds to each
from signs.

3. Each of the given figures
try cutting it into two equal parts
figures, that is, into two also figures,
which can be combined by superimposing
one to the other.
As an example, in the first of
these numbers show the required size
res.
