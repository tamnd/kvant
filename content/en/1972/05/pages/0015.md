Here is a brief explanation of the program. 1st command - transfer of control: the machine will transfer to the 2nd command only after the person makes a move. On the 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th commands, the carriage moves to the right, and on the 8th command*) erases the mark in the section being viewed and moves on to the 9th command, on which it moves to the right and goes to command No. 10. On this command, if the section being viewed is empty, the machine executes the 11th command, and if it is offset - 8th. On the 11th command, the carriage moves to the right and moves to the 12th command. If now the section under which the carriage is located is empty, then the machine returns to the 11th command, and if it is displaced, it starts all over again, that is, it goes to the 1st command.

*) The purpose of the 6th and 7th commands is to stop after the person has taken the last mark.

1. a) Prove that the “machine strategy” described here always ensures a win-win game for the machine in cases where there are 11 or 16 marks on the tape; For how many other marks on the tape is the same program suitable (give your answer in a general form if possible)?

2. Given an array of n labels (that is, n consecutive deleted sections). Write a program that places these marks on the tape so that between each pair there is one empty section (the carriage looks at the rightmost of the offset sections).

3. (This and the next two problems were proposed by V.N. Kasatkin.) On the tape there is an array of n offset sections (the leftmost one is viewed), and to the right of it, at a distance of m sections, there is another offset section. Write a program that will assign this array to this section.

4. There are two arrays of different lengths on the tape (the extreme section of one of them is visible). Write a program that compares the length of arrays and the largest of them. (Specifically consider cases where the lengths of the arrays are equal.)

5. On the dan tape there is an array, the outermost section of which is viewed by the carriage. Write a program that places another array of the same length on the tape, located two empty sections to the right of this one.

6. The tape is given two arrays at a distance of one empty section from each other, with the carriage located under the outermost section offset by the section of the left array. Write a program that will assign these arrays to each other.

7. Make a winning program for the next game. There are 21 sections offset on the Post machine belt, the outermost section. The man and the machine take turns (the man goes first!), erasing no more than 6 marks per move. The winner (unlike the game above) is the one who erases the last mark.
