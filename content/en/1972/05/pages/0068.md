To our readers!
Subscription continues for the second half of 1972 for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant».
The magazine is intended primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is also useful for teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics or mathematics, as well as all lovers of mathematics and physics.
The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school”, materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to teach various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.
The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems offered at entrance exams for various disciplines, problems offered at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.
Notes describing physical equipment and experiments will help the reader to set up and prove a physical experiment.
The magazine publishes on its pages a review of the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists and stories from the most scientists about how “science is done”, how scientific discoveries appear.
Decisions are constantly being placed on books - those that have already been published and those that are still being prepared for publication.
The magazine is distributed only by subscription and is not sold in retail stores.
