Shan Zhori of the physics sections decided for serious, independent reports, for demonstrating deep knowledge in physics, for a scientific approach to solving the problem, to award special diplomas to the following participants of the meeting: *Vladimir Gonnarov* (10th grade 131 school, Kazan) for the report “Demonstration of wholesale with ultrasound”, *Grigory Liberman* (10th grade 116 school., Odessa) for the report “On a method for solving a number of gravitational problems”, *Nikolai Makarov* (10th grade. 239 school., Leningrad) for the report “On the issue of free fall of the storeys”, *Yuri Kalafami* (10th grade. 444 school., Moscow) for the report “Paramagnetic excitation of oscillations”, *Vladimir Gomolonov* (10 class 239 school, Leningrad) for the report “On the total optical efficiency and the role of beam confinement”, *Axelrod* (10th class 239 school, Leningrad) for the report “Calculation of normal vibrations of molecules”, *Kirill Tyntarevy* (10th class 239 school, Leningrad) for the report “Evaporation of a water drop in a stationary flow of warm air”.
Diplomas of the first degree were awarded by eight participants of the meeting, diplomas of the second degree were awarded by another eight participants. 19 schoolchildren received incentive diplomas.

On January 7, at the rally there was a history section (in the Leningrad History Museum; 7 reports were made at this section), a literature section (in the Pushkin House; 17 reports and a pozzie competition); The section of the Komsomol activists worked in the building of school No. 239. On January 7, two teams made up of participants in the meeting held a “mathematical battle” - a perfection (similar to KBH) in solving problems.

The rally included sports competitions in basketball, chess, table tennis and shooting, a photo competition “Cities and People of the Union”, which resulted in 10 works being awarded with prizes and certificates, and a show of artistic excellence.

During their stay in Leningrad, the participants of the rally visited V. I. Lenin’s room in Smolny, visited theaters, took excursions to the cruiser “Avora”, to the Peter and Paul Fortress, to the Kirov Plant Museum, to the Museum of the History of Leningrad, the Russian Museum, the Hermitage and a bus tour of the city “Leningrad - the city of Lenin”.
On January 8, the closing ceremony of the meeting took place; The participants of the meeting were awarded diplomas, certificates and souvenirs. Delegations from different cities presented memorable gifts to its organizer. The meeting undoubtedly played a huge role in establishing contacts between physicists and mathematical schools in different cities, and the memory of it will remain with all delegates.

A. N. Vilenkin, T. S. Petrova
