BRANDS DEDICATED
LEONARDO DA VINCI

Leonardo da Vinci (1452-1519) one of the greatest representatives of the Renaissance.

Among the titans generated by this era, he held one of the first places. According to Engels, he was “not only a great artist, but also a great mathematician, mechanic and engineer, who owed the most important discoveries to the most diverse branches of physics.”

Leonardo da Vinci argued that scientific research should be based on the experimental study of nature. He wrote: “The interpreter of nature is experience. He never cheats. Our drying is sometimes deceived because it expects results that are not confirmed by experience. We must carry out experiments, changing circumstances, until we extract general rules from them, because experience provides true rules... They, in turn, guide our research in nature and our work in the field of art.”

Leonardo da Vinci anticipated a number of discoveries made many centuries later. He laid the foundation for the doctrine of waves and leverage; they outlined the basic principles of aviation. Leonardo used his scientific discoveries in many inventions and in the construction of major buildings, canals, and military engineering structures.

The first stamps dedicated to Leonardo da Vinci were issued in his homeland in Italy in 1932. Then stamps dedicated to Leonardo were issued repeatedly. Above is a stamp issued in Italy in 1935. It is dedicated to the International Asronautics Salon in Milan; it depicts a portrait of Leonardo against the backdrop of flying airplanes - in honor of his achievements in researching the possibility of human flight.

A much larger number of stamps dedicated to Leonardo da Vinci were issued in 1952, the 500th anniversary of his birth. In the photo you see stamps issued for this DATE in Italy, Hungary, Poland, Romania and the GDR (the stamps of Italy, Hungary and Poland depict a self-portrait of Leonardo da Vinci).

Urban contemporaries highly value Leonardo da Vinci as an artist. We place a stamp on which one of his paintings ("Benois Madonna"), stored in the Hermitage, is reproduced.
