Option 2
1. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} y^2 - xy = -12, \\ x^2 - xy = 28. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve the equation:
$$2\log_9 x = \log_3 x \log_3(\sqrt{2x+1} - 1).$$

3. Find the solution to the equation
$$\frac{1}{2}(\cos 5x + \cos 7x) - \cos^2 2x + \sin^2 3x = 0,$$
satisfying the condition $|x| < 2$.

4. Given angles $B$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ ($\Rightarrow B \neq C$). Find the cotangent of the acute angle $x$, which is formed by the median emerging from the vertex $A$, from the side $BC$.

5. Prove that for $a > 0, b > 0, c > 0$
$$(a^3 + b^3 + c^3)\left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) \geq (a + b + c)^2.$$

Option 1
1. The angle at the apex of the axial section of a right circular cone is $2\alpha$, and the height of the cone is $H$. Find the volume of the ball inscribed in this cone, as well as the total surface area of ​​the cone.
2. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{1}{6} \mathrm{tg}^2 x - \frac{\sqrt{1-\cos^2 x}}{\cos x} + \frac{5}{6} = 0.$$
3. Solve the system of inequalities:
$$\begin{cases} \log \frac{3}{5}\frac{x^2+x-6}{x^2-4} < 1, \\ \sqrt{5-x^2} > x-1. \end{cases}$$
4. Find integers $x$ and $y$ that satisfy the condition $x^4+y^4+2=4xy$.
Option 2
1. Find the area of ​​the total surface of the cone if the radius of the ball inscribed in it is $R$, and the generatrix of the cone is inclined to the plane of its base at an angle $\alpha$.
2. Solve the equation:
$$\log \sqrt{2}\sin x (1+\cos x) = 2.$$
3. Solve the system of inequalities:
$$\begin{cases} \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)\frac{x^2+5x-14}{x-2} > 1, \\ 5-x > \sqrt{x^2-16}. \end{cases}$$

4. Find angles $B$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ ($\Rightarrow B \neq C$). Find the cotangent of the acute angle $x$, which is formed by the median emerging from the vertex $A$, from the side $BC$.

5. Prove that for $a > 0, b > 0, c > 0$
$$(a^3+b^3+c^3)\left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) \geq (a+b+c)^2.$$

$$1 - \cos 6x = \mathrm{tg} 3x.$$
