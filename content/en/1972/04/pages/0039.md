we have

$$|a_1+a_2| \leq |a_1|+|a_2|.$$ (2)

The equal sign in (2) means that the points $O, A_1$ and $A_2$ are located on the same line, and the point $O$ does not belong to the segment $A_1A_2$.

So, the model of the sum of two complete numbers is not greater than the sum of the models of the terms.

The difference $a_1-a_2$ of two complete numbers $a_1$ and $a_2$ is a number $a$ such that $a+a_2=a_1$ (the geometric construction is given in figure 3). Since $OA=A_1A_2$, then

$$|a_1-a_2|=A_1A_2.$$ (3)

Therefore, the model of the difference between two complete numbers is equal to the consideration between their corresponding points.

Next, $OA_1-OA_2 \leq A_1A_2 \leq OA_1+OA_2$. That's why

$$|a_1|-|a_2| \leq |a_1-a_2| \leq |a_1|+|a_2|.$$ (4)

If the points $O, A_1$ and $A_2$ belong to the same line, then one of the inequality signs in (4) turns into equality.

Thus, the model of the difference between two complete numbers is no more than the sum of their models and no less than their difference.

It is easy to check that (Fig.4) is true, for example, the following inequalities:

$$|a+b+c| \leq |a|+|b|+|c|.$$ (5)

Note that if the number $a$ corresponds to the point $A$ with coordinates $x, y$, then the number - $a$ corresponds to the point with coordinates - $x$, - $y$. These two points are symmetrical about the origin $O$ (Fig. 5). Sledova-
