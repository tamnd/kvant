ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS IN PHYSICS
AT THE PHYSICS FACULTY OF MSU
IN 1971

A. V. Ustinova

The examination card in physics for applicants to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University contains two questions and one task. The questions and tasks proposed in the tickets are similar so that the applicant can demonstrate the importance of the physics course within the school curriculum, understanding of physical phenomena and the ability to apply the laws of physics in a developmental manner to solve the problem. During the exam, which is administered by two examiners, the applicant is asked additional questions and examples. A complete answer to all the proposed questions and the correct solution to the problem and examples deserve an excellent mark. An unsolved problem will deduct one point from the overall score.

Below we present several problems in physics offered in the entrance exams at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

1. A small heavy ball on a thread collides in a vertical plane. Show that the ball cannot fall if the thread is not able to withstand a tension $T$ that is 6 times the weight of the ball.

2. A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ and length $l$ collides with an angular velocity $\omega$ in a horizontal plane around a vertical axis passing through its end. Determine the tension $T$ in the rod in the section located at a distance $a$ from the collision axis.

3. A ball of mass $m_1$, moving with a speed $v$, collides with a ball of mass $m_2$. An absolutely elastic central impact occurs. Find at what correspondence of masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ the balls after impact will scatter in opposite directions with speeds equal in absolute value.

4. A satellite, whose mass is $m=1$ $m$, flies at an altitude $h_1=200$ $km$ from the Earth's surface in a circular orbit. Gradually, due to the braking of the upper atmosphere, the radius of the satellite’s orbit decreases (in this case, we can assume that the orbit remains circular). How much energy does the satellite lose due to braking when moving into orbit from $h_2=180$ $km$? Calculation accuracy 5%.

5. A body whose mass is $m=20$ $kg$ is pulled along a horizontal surface with a force $F=120$ $N$. If this force is applied to the body at an angle $\alpha_1=60^\circ$ to the horizontal, then the body moves uniformly. With what acceleration $a$ will this body move if the same force is applied at an angle $\alpha_2=30^\circ$ to the horizon? Accept $g=10$ $m/sec^2$; calculation accuracy is 10%.
