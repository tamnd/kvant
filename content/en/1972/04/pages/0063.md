Faculty of Electronic Engineering

$$\frac{(a+x)^{-\frac{1}{2}}+(a-x)^{-\frac{1}{2}}}{(a+x)^{-\frac{1}{2}}-(a-x)^{-\frac{1}{2}}},$$

$$\frac{1}{\lg x} + \frac{2}{\lg 10x} + \frac{3}{\lg 100x} = 0.$$

$$(tg 30^\circ+tg 40^\circ+tg 50^\circ+tg 60^\circ) \sec 200^\circ \text{ctg} 330^\circ.$$

Electromechanical Faculty

1. Simplify the expression:
$$\left[ \frac{x+\sqrt{xy}}{x\sqrt{x}-y\sqrt{x}} + \frac{(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^3+2x^{\frac{3}{2}}+y^{\frac{3}{2}}}{3x^2+3y\sqrt{xy}} \right]^{-2} \times (x^2+xy-2y^2)^{-1}.$$

2. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} lg x+lg y = lg (x+y), \\ lg (\sqrt{8}-y)+lg (\sqrt{8}+y) = 2 lg x. \end{cases}$$

3. Reduce to a form convenient for logarithm:
$$tg \alpha - 1+\sin \alpha - \sin \alpha tg \alpha +\cos^2 \alpha.$$

4. Solve the equation:
$$4 \sin x \sin 2x \sin 3x=\sin 4x.$$

5. In a rectangular parallelipeid, the point of intersection of the diagonal lower base with the middle of the side edge by a segment of length $m$. This segment forms an angle $\alpha$ with the base of the paralleliped and an angle $2\alpha$ with the lateral boundary. Find the volume of the paralleliped.

Kuibyshev State Pedagogical Institute

Mathematics Department of Physiomatichematico-Faculty

1. A trigonal triangle with a hypotenuse $c$ and an acute angle $\alpha$ serves as the base of the paramid. Its side face, passing through the hypotenuse, is perpendicular to the base, and the other two side faces are inclined to it at an angle of $\beta$. Find the volume of the paramid.

2. The tractor driver plowed three plots. The area of ​​the first is equal to 2/5 of the area of ​​all three plots, and the area of ​​the second is related to the area of ​​the third as $1^{1/2}:1^{1/3}$. How many hectors are there in each of the sections, if the third section is 16 $2a$ less than the first?

3. Solve inequality:
$$(x-3)^{2x^2-7x}>1.$$

4. Solve the equation:
$$tg x+ctg x=4\sqrt{2} \cos 2x.$$

2. The train was delayed at the semaphore for 16 minutes and caught up with a burst of 80 kl on the stretch, running at a speed 10 km/h more than it was supposed to be according to the schedule. What is the scheduled speed of the train?

$$tg 2x+ctg x=8 \cos^2 x.$$
