PHYSICS
Publishing house "Science"

1. K. V. Lyubimov, S. M. Novikov. Let's get acquainted with electronic purposes. (Volume 5 printed sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 14 kopecks)
This book is primarily intended for teachers and schoolchildren in grades 6–8. It contains about 70 problems, the solutions of which will help schoolchildren become widely and deeply familiar with the design and composition of electronic goals.

2. F. S. Zavelsky. Time and its measurement. (Volume 12 printed sheets, circulation 40,000 copies, price 40 kopecks)
The book describes various methods of measuring time and the corresponding types of clocks: solar, fire, water, pendulum, molecular.
It is shown how in different eras and in connection with the development of science the very concept of time was measured and expanded. Descriptions of methods and instruments for measuring time are carried out along with distance for various scientific and technical problems.
They are so closely intertwined that such a description to some extent corresponds to the life of science itself. This story about ideas, methods and instruments - ancient and modern - is written in a popular and engaging way. The book is intended for a wide range of readers, starting with schoolchildren in grades 7-8.

3. V. G. Gorbachy. Cosmic explosions. (Volume 12 printed sheets, circulation 25,000 copies, price 40 kopecks)
The book is connected with the most powerful phenomenon of nature - the explosive process in space. First, the author talks about what an explosion is and how it occurs. Then he leads the reader from simpler examples—explosions in internal structure engines, etc.—to explosions on the Sun, stellar flares and explosions in galactic nuclei and quasars.

The author traces the role of explosions in the evil of stars and galaxies. Along the way, the book talks about research methods.

The book is available to a wide range of people, as well as high school students.

4. M. M. Dagev. Observations of the starry sky. (Volume 8 printed sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 27 kopecks)
The book talks about navigation in the starry sky and the basic principles of changing the visibility conditions of constellations throughout the year. The brightest objects observable through small telescopes are described. Methods for simple observations of planets, the Moon, variable stars, meteors and artificial satellites of the Earth are presented, which are quite accessible to beginning astronomy lovers. The final chapter of the book contains a list of celestial objects recommended for initial observations, as well as a list of literature useful for subsequent more complex observations and observation processing.

The book describes the method of making a simple homemade telescope, as well as the latest results of astronomy. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

5. V. L. Levshin, L. V. Levshin. Lamine carriers and their application. (Volume 9 printed sheets, circulation 25,000 copies, price 60 kopecks)
The book examines the characteristics of the properties of light-bearing substances, the production and structure of light-bearing substances, the processes of transformation and transfer of energy into light-bearing substances, leading to the emergence of light-bearing substances, the connection of light-bearing substances with other types of radiation. Numerous examples are given of the use of radium in scientific research, for lighting purposes, in electronic sensing devices, devices intended to detect radium with the invisible eye, for studying the occurrence of various chemical reactions, etc.

Methods of luminescent analysis in chemistry, biology, medical technology and many other areas are also considered.

The book is designed for people with high school knowledge of physics.

Publishing house "Mir"
6. H. Rachils. Physics vvantse. (Volume 5 printed sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 25 kopecks)
Young people choosing their path in life are increasingly turning to science. But what is science? How to become a researcher? What is the work of a scientist? These and many other questions are answered in the short book by X. Rachil “Physics of Vvantse”. Using the example of physical laws, the famous American popularizer of science introduces the young reader to an understandable world of scientific phenomena, teaches him to observe and draw conclusions.

This book will find numerous readers, both among those who write about science, and among those who teach it, and above all among those young people who study it.

7. B. M. Pontekorvo, V. N. Ponkrovsky tells about the life and work of one of the great scientists of our time - Enrico Fermi. The book traces the development of Fermi's personality, talks about the misconceptions under which he came to his most important discoveries, and presents a number of low-profile ministers from his life.

An organic part of the book is the replenishment of Fermi's students and friends.
