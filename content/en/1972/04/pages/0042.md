TASKS

In this and subsequent issues, the “Problem Book «Kvant»” includes some problems proposed this year at regional and resource Olympiads.

Solutions to the problem can be sent no later than one and a half months after the publication of the corresponding issue of the journal to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, publishing house "Nauka", editorial office of the journal «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending; for example: “Problem book «Kvant», M137, F139. At the beginning of the letter, indicate your last name, first name, patronymic, home address (as well as the class and school in which you study).

TASKS

M136. Is it possible to remove 50 stones from a quarry, the weights of which are 370 kg, 372 kg, 374 kg, ..., 468 kg (all form an arithmetic progression with a difference of 2 kg), on seven three-wheelers?

V. P. Fedomov

M137. Let $a, b, c, d$ be the lengths of four consecutive sides of the quadrilateral, $S$ its area.

a) Prove that $2S \leq ab + cd$.
b) Prove that $2S \leq ac + bd$.
b) Prove that if equality is achieved in at least one of these inequalities, then the quadrilateral can be inscribed in a circle.

M138. Prove that if $m$ and $n$ are integers and $1 \leq m < n$, then

$$\sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^k k^m C_n^k = 0,$$

where $C_n^k$ are biomp coefficients, that is, polynomial coefficients

$$(1+x)^m = \sum_{k=0}^{n} C_n^k x^k.$$

(For example, if $n=4$, then $C_4^0=1$, $C_4^1=4$, $C_4^2=6$, $C_4^3=4$, $C_4^4=1$ and the equalities are true

$$-1 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 6 - 3 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot 1 = 0,$$

$$-1^2 \cdot 4 + 2^2 \cdot 6 - 3^2 \cdot 4 + 4^2 \cdot 1 = 0,$$

$$-1^3 \cdot 4 + 2^3 \cdot 6 - 3^3 \cdot 4 + 4^3 \cdot 1 = 0.$$

M. I. Sidorov

M139. From the vertex $B$ of the parallelogram $ABCD$ its heights $BK$ and $BH$ are drawn. The segments $KH=a$ and $BD=b$ are known. Find the distance from point $B$ to the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $BKH$.

F. A. Barmenev

M140. With a natural number (written in the decimal system), you can perform the following operation:

A) add the number 4 at the end;
B) add the number 0 at the end;
B) divide by 2 (if the number is even).

For example, if you perform the operations $B$, $B$, $A$ and $B$ with the number 4, you will get the number 140.

a) How to get the number 1972 from the number 4?

6) * Prove that from the number 4 you can get any natural number.

A. K. Tolpieo
