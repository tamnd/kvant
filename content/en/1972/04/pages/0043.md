The internal energy of the nucleus is $E_1$, the internal energy of each of the sockets is $E_2$ ($E_1 > 2E_2$). F150. In a certain galaxy, a star Z with a planet P was discovered, making one revolution around the star in time $T_1$ and one revolution around its axis in time $T_2$. Satellite S of planet P makes one revolution around the planet in time $T_3$. How long does it take for the eclipse of satellite S to repeat at a given location on the planet? All bodies rotate in the same plane.

G. L. Komkin

F151. Two plane mirrors form a double angle with a raster $90^\circ$. A lens with focal length $F$ is inserted into the corner so that the main optical axis of the lens makes an angle $45^\circ$ with each mirror. The radius of a lens is equal to its focal length. Find the position of the source image located on the main optical axis of the lens at a distance of 1.5 $F$ from it.
