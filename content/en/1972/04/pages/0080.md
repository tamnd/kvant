2. The tension force $T$ in section $BB$ creates a centripetal acceleration $a = \omega^2 x$ for the mass $m_{BC}$ of the rod, where $x$ is the distance from the axis $AA$ to the center of gravity of the section $BC$ (Fig. 2). That's why

$$T = m_{BC} \omega^2 x,$$
$$m_{BC} = \frac{m}{l} (l - a), \quad x = a + \frac{l - a}{2}.$$

Hence,

$$T = \frac{m \omega^2}{2 l} (l^2 - a^2).$$

Notes. At $a \rightarrow 0$ the maximum tension is $T_A = \frac{m \omega^2 l}{2}$. If $a \rightarrow l$, then $T_C \rightarrow 0$, $m_C \rightarrow 0$.

3. Let us write down the law of conservation of momentum and the law of conservation of mechanical energy:

$$m_1 v = -m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2,$$
$$\frac{m_1 v^2}{2} = \frac{m_1 v_1^2}{2} + \frac{m_2 v_2^2}{2},$$

where $v_1$ and $v_2$ are the velocities of the pseudora masses $m_1$ and $m_2$. Let's transform these equations:

$$m_1 (v + v_1) (v - v_1) = m_2 v_2^2,$$
$$m_1 (v + v_1) = m_2 v_2.$$

Dividing the first equation by the second, we get

$$v - v_1 = v_2.$$

According to the conditions

$$|v_1| = |v_2| = v'.$$

Thus,

$$v' = \frac{v}{2}.$$

Substituting into equation (2), we get

$$\frac{m_2}{m_1} = 3.$$

4. The change in energy will occur as a result of the work of friction forces:

$$\Delta W = W_{\text{кин}} + \Delta W_{\text{пот}},$$
$$\Delta W_{\text{кин}} = \frac{1}{2} m \left(v_1^2 - v_2^2\right),$$
$$\Delta W_{\text{пот}} = m \left(g_1 h_1 - g_2 h_2\right),$$
$$\frac{mv_1^2}{R_0 + h_1} = mg_1, \quad \frac{mv_2^2}{R_0 + h_2} = mg_2,$$

where $R_0$ is the Emly radius, $g_1 \approx g_2 \approx g$.

$$\Delta W = \frac{mg}{2} \left(h_1 - h_2\right) + mg \left(h_1 - h_2\right) = \frac{3}{2} mg \left(h_1 - h_2\right),$$
$$\Delta W = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 1000 \cdot 9,8 \cdot 20000 \approx 2,9 \cdot 10^8 (\partial \kappa).$$

6. Let us draw isotherms $PV = \text{const}$ through points 1, 2, 3 and 4 (Fig. 4). From figure 4 it is clear that $T_1 < T_2 < T_3$, $T_3 > T_4 > T_1$. Consequently, tello enters the system in sections 1-2 and 2-3.

$$Q_{1-2} = \mu c_V (T_2 - T_1),$$
$$Q_{2-3} = \mu c_P (T_3 - T_2).$$

Using isochore 1-2 we determine $T_2$:

$$\frac{P_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2}{T_2}, \quad T_2 = 2T_1.$$

Using isobar 2-3 we determine $T_3$:

$$\frac{V_1}{T_2} = \frac{V_2}{T_3}, \quad T_3 = 4T_1.$$
