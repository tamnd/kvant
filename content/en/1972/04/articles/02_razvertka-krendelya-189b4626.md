"PRETDEL" SCREENING
On figure on the left is a development of the “pretzel” surface (see also Fig. 6, p. 33). The sweep represents a map of eight countries, each of which borders all the others.
To obtain a “pretzel” surface from a development, glue sections of the boundaries so that the points marked with the same numbers coincide.
For clarity, along the periphery of the scan, the colors of the countries adjacent to the corresponding segment of the border are indicated.
We remind you that the reamer is made of durable material (for example, it is a rubber film). A practical procedure is placed, does not glue the scan, but uses it to apply a map to the “pretzel”.
Show that a similar map with a larger number of countries does not exist.
