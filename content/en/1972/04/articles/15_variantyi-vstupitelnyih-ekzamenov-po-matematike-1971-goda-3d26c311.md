OPTIONS FOR ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS
IN MATH 1971

Moscow State University
named after M.V. Lomonosov

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

1. Solve the equation:
$$x^2 \log_3(x^2) - (2x^2 + 3) \log_9(2x + 3) = 3 \log_3\left(\frac{x}{2x + 3}\right).$$
2. Find all $x$ from the segment $0 \leq x \leq \pi$ satisfying the inequality
$$\sin 2x + \sin x - \sqrt{2} \cos x < \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}.$$
3. The lateral faces of a quadrangular pyramid are equal equilateral triangles, and at the base of the pyramid lies a rhombus, one diagonal of which is twice as long as the other. Find the volume $V$ of the pyramid if it is known that the area of ​​the lateral surface is 6, and the middle lateral edge has two edges that form an obtuse angle.
4. Find all values ​​of $\alpha$ for which the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + 4x + 3 \leq \alpha, \\ x^2 - 2x \leq 3 - 6\alpha \end{cases}$$
has a unique solution.
5. A circle can be inscribed in the quadrilateral $ABCD$ and a circle can be drawn around it. Diagonal $AC$ divides the area of ​​the quadrilateral in half. Find the length of the diagonal $BD$ if the radius of the inscribed circle is $r$ and the perimeter of the quadrilateral is $p$.

Department of Structural and Applied Linguistics, Faculty of Philology

1. Four linguists (each separately) are engaged in mathematical text processing. Each of them works with constant randomness. If the first three work for one hour, and the fourth for two hours, then together they will manage to process 26 pages of text. If the third one works for two hours, and the rest for an hour each, then 32 pages of text will be processed. If the second one works for two hours, and the others for an hour each, then 40 pages of text will be processed. How many pages of text will be processed if the first one runs for three hours, the second for five hours, and the third for seven hours?
2. The long side of the rectangle is $d$. A circle is drawn with a center at one of the vertices of the rectangle and a radius equal to its shorter side. The common part with the resulting circle is removed from the rectangle.
At what value of the length less than the side will the area of ​​the remaining part be maximum?
3. Determine how many integer solutions the inequality has
$$(n^2 - 2)(n^2 - 22)(n^2 - 52)(n^2 - 152) < 0.$$
4. Determine for which $a$ the inequality $\log_{a(a+1)}(|x|+4) > 1$ holds for any real $x$.
5. Average of complex numbers $z$ find all those for which
$$\log_2(1 + |z^2 - i|) + \log_{16}\frac{1}{(1 + |z^2 + i|)^4} = 0.$$
6. Explore and solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} x^7y^{31} = a, \\ x^2y^9 = b. \end{cases}$$
7. Prove that if $p$ and $q$ are integers, and $p^2 - 7q^2 = 2pq$, then $p=q=0$.
