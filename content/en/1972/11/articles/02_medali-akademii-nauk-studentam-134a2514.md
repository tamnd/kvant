MEDALS OF THE ACADEMY OF SCIENCES FOR STUDENTS

To further increase the activity of students of higher educational institutions in professional independent scientific research, the Presidium of the USSR Academy of Sciences decided to establish medals of the Academy of Sciences with prizes for the best student scientific works in the field of natural sciences, technology and the humanities. 5 medals will be awarded annually with prizes of 200 rubles each. No more than 15 works from those participating in the All-Union competition for the best scientific work of higher education students will be considered for acceptance. This competition is held annually by the Ministry of Higher and Secondary Special Education of the USSR and the Central Committee of the Komsomol. Decisions of the Presidium of the USSR Academy of Sciences on awarding medals with prizes are published in the “Bulletin of the USSR Academy of Sciences”, “Bulletin of Higher School” and “Komsomolskaya Pravda”. Medals and diplomas for awarding medals, along with prizes, are awarded at the annual General Meeting of the USSR Academy of Sciences.
