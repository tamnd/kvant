F183*. A dynamometer, which slides on a smooth horizontal table, is pulled with a constant force $F=4 \text{ n}$. What does the dynamometer show if the mass of the gun is equal to the mass of the body and the graduated diameter was in a horizontal position?

G. L. Komkin

F184. When transmitting a television image on Earth, 25 frames are transmitted in 1 second. This means that 1 frame is transmitted in $\frac{1}{25}$ seconds. At the same time, as is known, the transmission of one frame of the image of the Moon, which was improved by the “Moon” standard, lasted 25 minutes. Why is there such a big difference in the transmission time of 1 image frame in general cases?

F185. A volleyball with a mass of 200 g and a volume of 8 liters of nakaan to an overpressure of 0.2 amp. The ball was thrown to a height of 20 m and, after being thrown onto solid ground, jumped to almost the same height. Estimate the maximum air temperature in the ball at the moment it hits the ground. Outdoor air temperature $300^\circ \text{K}$, thermal specificity of air at constant volume $c_v=0,16 \text{ кал/л·срэд}$.

F186. When a bus or automated vehicle turns, passengers are thrown in the direction opposite to the direction of the turn. At the same time, the turn of the plane is not at all mistaken by its passengers. Explain this difference.

I. Sh. Slovodechkiy

F187. A thin metal plate of area $s$ is filled with a layer of liquid dielectric with density $\rho$ and dielectric constant $\varepsilon$, so that the thickness of this layer is much less than the linear dimensions of the plate. What will happen to the liquid if the plate is given an electric charge $+Q$?

V. D. Krivenkov
