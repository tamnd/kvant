If we find these two numbers $l_1$ and $l_2$ and put $l = \frac{l_1 + l_2}{2}$, then we can expect that almost all dots are shorter than $l$, and almost all dashes are shorter than $l$. To determine the numbers $l_1$ and $l_2$, the machine “looks through” some large piece of text and removes the long characters found in it. For example, in the case of the text shown at Fig. 2, the machine is found 4 times, length 2 - 3 times, length 3 - 1 time, length 4 - 3 times, length 5 - 1 time:

| Character length | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Number of characters | 4 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1 |

It turns out that the most particular short sign is a sign of length 1, and the most particular long sign has length 4. These two numbers the machine “takes” as $l_1$ and $l_2$ and calculates $l = \frac{1 + 4}{2} = 2,5$.

Now the machine will count all characters whose length is less than 2.5 as dots, and all other characters will be considered dashes. From time to time, for greater reliability, the machine may randomly redistribute the numbers $l_1$, $l_2$ and $l$.

This method of recognizing dots and dashes (with some learned properties) was tested on a specific machine and with excellent results: the machine read more than 90% of the characters correctly.

Of course, decisive success in this area is yet to come; but something has already been done, and scientists continue to work intensively in this area. The task of teaching a machine to read is attractive to many researchers as one of the most important, interesting and difficult tasks in modern cybernestics**).

*) All lengths are rounded to the nearest whole number.

**) For more details, see, for example, the book by M. M. Bongard “The Problem of Recognition” (M., Nauka publishing house, 1967).

Failed lunch

Dorofei Martyanch Dubodumov, having moved to his dacha for the summer, decided to give dinner and invite all eight of his neighbors.

“What are you saying,” they waved their hands at him. “Don’t you know that Porfiry Apollinarivich will never accept an invitation if you invite Afanasy Methodevich or Ambrose Evlamppevich, or if Avdotya Pafnutyevna and Nikodim Gavrilovich are invited at the same time.” Ambrose Evlamppevich and Afanasy Mefodevich, however, will not object to any invitations, then Avdotya Pafnutyevna will come only if Nikodim Gavrilovich is also invited, who will not accept the invitation if Afanasy Mefodevich is at dinner. Rosalia Leopoldovna applies the whipped cream only accompanied by Akaki Akakievchi. Cleopatra Ivanovna will not object to the invitation of Rosalia Leopoldovna only if Porfiry Apollinarovich is invited, but if Nikodima Gavrilovich is not invited. In order to invite Akaki Akakievchi, it is necessary to invite either Avdotya Pafnutyevna or Cleopatra Ivanovna, in addition, he will refuse the invitation if Nikodim Gavrilovich is at dinner without Porfiry Apollinarivchi, and it’s not even worth talking about the fact that Akaki Akakievchi will not come if Afanasy Mefodevichi or Ambrose Evlampevichi is invited.”
