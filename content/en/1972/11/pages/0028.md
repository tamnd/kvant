$$t = \frac{c}{2a} (\mu - \mu^{-1}).$$

$$x = \frac{c^2}{2a} (\mu + \mu^{-1} - 2)$$

$$v = c \frac{1 - \mu^{-2}}{1 + \mu^{-2}}.$$

(4)

$$\mu^2 = \frac{c + v}{c - v} \approx \frac{2}{1 - \frac{v}{c}}.$$
