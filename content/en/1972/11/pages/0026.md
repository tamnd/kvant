⟦folio 24⟧

Nowadays a lot is said and written about the problem of communication with external civilizations. Experts briefly call it CETI, after the first letters of the English words Communication with Extraterrestrial Intelligence, which translated into Russian means “connection with external intelligence.” They squeak not only in science fiction works, but also in Serbez, a wave of Serbez scientific works. Scientific seminars, meetings, and conferences have been devoted to this topic more than once. Recently in Armenia, at the Bioprakan Astrophysical General School, the First International Conference on this problem was held. Many outstanding Soviet and foreign scientists took part in its work - specialists in the field of astrophysics, physics, biochemistry, biology, history, philosophy, sociology, cybernetics, information theory and communications. For several days they discussed the possibility of detecting external civilizations, the problem of establishing contact with external intelligence *). Indeed, is it possible to establish contact with intelligent people of other worlds, separated from us by gigantic distances, which are easier to write down using numbers than to imagine the power of imagination?

How to do this?

Let's arm ourselves with a pencil and paper and think about this problem together.

*) You can read about these discussions in the magazine “Earth and Universe” No. 2 and No. 3 for 1972.

Of course, the most interesting thing would be to go visit them, see how they live, what they do, share their experiences... So, let's get into the rocket. To escape from the sphere of earthly acceptance, you need to develop a speed of 11.2 km/s. This is the so-called second cosmic speed (the question in parentheses: why the second, and what is the first cosmic speed?) And in order to leave the Solar system, it is necessary to develop a speed that exceeds the third cosmic speed (what is it equal to?).

Let our rocket rush at a speed of the order of the third of cosmic speed. How long will it take us to fly to the star closest to the Sun? (What is it called, how far is it from us?). And to the center of the Galaxy? To the Androemda Nebula?

If you have not made a mistake in the calculations, the result may cause despondency. It would take no human life to even reach the nearest star. What to do? Well, of course, it will increase the speed. For you and me, this does not seem to be any difficulty: after all, we are conducting a thought experiment, so we can use any means, even those that have not reached the technical day. We are forbidden only one thing: breaking the laws of physics. So, the speed will increase.
