WE HAVE A GUEST MAGAZINE
EARTH and UNIVERSE

In January 1965, the first issue of the new popular science magazine of the USSR Academy of Sciences, “Earth and the Universe,” was published. The journal is recognized to satisfy the enormous interest in the problems of space exploration, as well as their current connection with other fields of knowledge;
Reveal the anti-scientific existence of all kinds of sensational “hypotheses” and “theories” in the field of astronomy and geophysics, the dissemination of which discretely discriminates Soviet science;
Provide assistance to teachers and lecturers;
Promote wide participation of amateurs in mass observations and research important for astronomy, geophysics and geodesy;
Promote broad participation by amateur astronomers.

Over the years of its existence, the magazine has published many interesting materials devoted to the study of Lunya and the planets of the Solar System, quasars, pulsarms, replicat studies and other mysterious objects discovered by radio astronomers. He spoke about the study of our Galaxy and processes, origins in other galaxies, about new branches of astronomy (Reentn ​​astronomy, neutrino astronomy, etc.), about the causes of earthquakes, tsunamis, and the movement of terrestrial materials.

Materials published in the journal “Earth and Universe” have varying levels of difficulty. Some of them are intended for well-prepared readers; the other does not require any special training.

We are publishing two articles by regular authors of the magazine. The article by L. M. Gindlis is associated with a significant complex problem, and our reader will have to take some of its conclusions regarding the theory of photon rockets “on faith.” But the very problem of searching for space civilizations is of real interest, which is why we willingly publish this article. The article by V. I. Levantovsky is devoted to one interesting problem of modern cosmonautics. In its nature it is much closer to the materials published in our magazine.
