To the article “Distances between crossing lines”

The first problem is easily solved if we apply the Dirichlet principle. After the Olympics, many members of the Soviet team explored the possibility of solving the problem for fewer numbers. For 8 numbers, S. Konyagin constructed a primer for which the statement of the problem does not hold. For 9 numbers the problem was not solved.

Most students solved the second problem in the following way: they divided the given quadrilateral into a trapezoid and a quadrilateral with angles equal to the given ones. Then an equal-sided trapezoid was skeletalized from the trapezoid.
