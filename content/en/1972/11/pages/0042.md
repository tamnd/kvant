QUANTA

Solutions to problems in this issue can be sent no later than December 31 to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, Nauka publishing house, journal editorial office «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, indicate the solutions to which problems you are sending; for example: “Problem «Kvant», M146, M148”. At the beginning of the letter, indicate your last name, first name, patronymic, home address, as well as the class and school in which you study. The solution to each problem (if you are sending several at once) must be written on a separate piece of paper(s), the pages proofed. Solutions to problems in each of the subjects - mathematics and physics, as well as new problems - should be sent in separate envelopes.
