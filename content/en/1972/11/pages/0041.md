Independent research

1. Fasten two thin mirrors together, pressing their edges to each other, and place the mirrors on some color picture. The mirrors create wonderful kaleidoscopic patterns.

Develop mirrors and watch how the images change. Explain what is happening.

2. Reverse the image in the mirror in relation to the object you are looking at. If you close your left eye while looking into a mirror, it will appear as if the image has covered your right eye. But if you look into a mirror placed at right angles to another mirror, then the image is reversed again and all objects are seen as they really are.

Place two mirrors at right angles to each other and look at yourself. Strange, isn't it? I wonder if any of you will be able to comb your hair while looking at your “correction” image?

3. Try writing your name on a piece of paper while looking in the mirror and following the image of your hand. Place a book between your eye and hand to prevent direct observation of your hand. Did you manage to write? No? How can we explain this?

4. Take a glass directed by water and look through it at a pencil that is installed vertically behind the glass. Move the pencil to the left, now to the right. What is visible? Try holding the pencil close to the glass first, then move it away to arm's length. How to find commitment to what is happening?
