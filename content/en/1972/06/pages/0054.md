Specialties: automation, telemechanics and communications, transport electrification, bridges and tunnels

1. Two people have to do some work. At first, the first one did 2 hours of work, then the second one joined and they worked together for 1 hour. After that, the second one finishes the remaining work in 3 hours. How long can everyone do the whole job if the first one needs 2 hours less to complete the work than the second one?

2. Solve the system

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 = 4 + \frac{y}{2}, \\ \log_3 (x + 2y) + \log_3 (x - 2y) = 1. \end{cases}$$

3. A circle is inscribed in a rhombus whose side is 20 cm. Find the area of ​​the circle if one diagonal of the rhombus is $\frac{4}{3}$ times larger than the other.

4. Solve the equation

$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 x - 2 \sin 2x - \frac{3}{4} \sin^2 2x = 0.$$

Specialties: applied mathematics, automated control systems, electronic calculating machines

1. The three state farms are not located on the same straight line. The distance from the first to the third through the second is four times the length of the path between them. The distance from the first to the second through the third on a is the length of the straight path. The distance from the second to the third through the first is 185 km. In what interval is the value of a for which the indicated location of state farms would be possible?

2. Solve the equation

$$1 - 2x - x^2 = tg^2 (x + y) + ctg^2 (x + y).$$

3. The diagonal of a right parallelepid is equal to d and makes an angle α with the plane of the base, and β with one of the side faces. Determine the volume of the parallelepid.

4. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{1 - 2 \sin 4x} + \sqrt{6} \cos 2x = 0.$$

Matematiko-mechanical faculty

1. The number 1 + $\sqrt{2}$ is the root of the equation

$$x^3 + ax^2 + bx + 2 = 0.$$

Find the coefficients a, b of the equation and all its roots, provided that a and b are rational numbers.

2. Triangle AOB is rotated in its plane around point O by 90°, with vertex A moving to vertex A1, and B to B1. Prove that in triangle OAB1 the median dropped to side AB1 is perpendicular to A1B, and in triangle OA1B the median dropped to side A1B is perpendicular to AB1.

3. At what x and y does the inequality take place?

$$\log_3 x + \log_3 3 + 2 \cos y \leq 0?$$

4. Find all values ​​of parameters a and b for which the system

$$\begin{cases} x + y = a, \\ \lg x + \lg y + \lg x \lg y = b \end{cases}$$

has a solution.

5. Solve the equation

$$\log_3 x + \log_3 3 + 2 \cos y \leq 0?$$

6. Find all values ​​of parameters a and b for which the system

$$\begin{cases} x + y = a, \\ \lg x + \lg y + \lg x \lg y = b \end{cases}$$

has a solution.

7. On the ring railway from point A1 it goes along the route A2, A3, ..., An, again A1, A2, A3, ..., An and so on. It is known that each subsequent stage between neighboring points will travel at an average speed q times greater than the average speed on the previous stage. Determine how many times the time it takes to complete m full circles is greater than the time spent on the first circle.

8. Through a point M lying inside a circle of radius r, the diameter AB and the chord CD are drawn so that $\Rightarrow BMD = \alpha$, $\Rightarrow BDM = \beta$. Find the area of ​​triangle MBD.

3. Solve inequality:

$$\log_3 (x^3 + 1) \log (x + 1) x > 2.$$

4. For what values ​​of a does the equation

$$\frac{\sin x - 1}{\sin x - 2} + a = \frac{2 - \sin x}{3 - \sin x}$$

has a solution? Find a solution.

2. In a trapezoid whose bases are a and b, a straight line parallel to the base is drawn through the point of intersection of the diagonal. Find the length of the segment of this straight line cut off by the sides of the trapezoid.

$$\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x + 7} + 2 \sqrt{x^2 + 7x} < 35 - 2x.$$
