To ghazi Chimateyli!

The subscription continues for the second receipt in 1972 for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant».
The magazine is intended primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is also useful for teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics or mathematics, as well as all lovers of mathematics and physics.

The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school”, i.e. materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to teach various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were proposed for upcoming exams in various years, problems that were proposed at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical devices and experiments will help the reader set up and test a physical experiment.

The magazine publishes on its pages review articles telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists and stories from the scientists themselves about how “science is done” and how scientific discoveries appear.

The magazine constantly publishes results on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

The magazine is distributed by subscription only.
