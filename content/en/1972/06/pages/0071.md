TO THE AXIAL
SYMMETRY

1. In equilateral triangle ABC, the angle at vertex B is 40°. BD is perpendicular to AC. Triangle KDN has the smallest perimeter of all triangles inscribed in triangle ABC with vertex at point D. Find <KDN.

2. Given a square and a point A outside it. Draw a line through point A and the given square so that the line segment of the square has the greatest length.

3. Given a square ABCD and a circle outside it. Construct a new square, two vertices of which would lie on side AB or on its extension, the third - on the circle, the fourth - on any of the three sides (BC, CD, DA) of the square.

4. Given a line l and two lines a and b intersecting line l. Construct a square so that two of its vertices lie on the line l, and the other two on the lines a b. (Consider all cases.)

5. Given a billiard table of size a×b. A ball was placed inside it. The ball is given a certain speed, which is directed so that the ball, having bounced off three sides in succession, returns to the same point (Fig. 1). Find the angle formed by the initial velocity vector of the ball with the side of the billiard table (graphical).

6. Given three billiards lengths, but the same width (Fig. 2). Balls are simultaneously sent from the lengths of the sides with the same speed in magnitude and direction. Can Shari be returned to the same board untrustedly?

7a. Given a cube with a side equal to 1 m (Fig. 3). On the edge AA′ there is a point N such that AN=AN′. Through point K, located on edge B′C′, at a distance of 25 cm from vertex C′, a perpendicular to the base is drawn. The inner walls of the cube are completely elastic. From point N to straight line KS, an absolutely elastic ball is thrown so that, having hit the edge BB′C′C′, it bounces onto the edge DD′C′C′, from it to the edge AA′D′D′, and then falls onto the edge AB. Find the horizontal speed v at which the phenomenon described above is possible.

7b. The conditions are the same as in point a), but the position of the straight line KS is not given. At what angle to the face AA′D′D′ and at what horizontal speed must the ball be thrown so that it, reflected from three faces, hits point B?
