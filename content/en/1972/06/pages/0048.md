⟦folio 46⟧

16*. In a triangle, all sides are less than one. Prove that its area is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$.

Note. Do not apply Heron's theorem.

17*. Give an example of two similar but unequal triangles that have two corresponding equal sides.

Note. Try to find not a geometric, but an algebraic approach, working with the side lengths of these triangles.

18*. Let $l_1$ and $l_2$ be two crossing lines in space. Find the locus of points from which it is impossible to check the line intersecting $l_1$ and $l_2$.

19*. Is there a triangular paramida with at least one obtuse plane angle adjacent to each edge?

Note. Prove that such a paramda does not exist, using the fact that some of the sides of an obtuse triangle cannot be the largest.
