⟦folio 51⟧

Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov

Faculty of Physics

1. Solve the equation:
$$3tg x (7 + \cos 2x) = 2 (\cos 2x - 1).$$
2. Solve inequality
$$\log_3^2 x + 2 \log_9 (x^2) < 2.$$
3. A car travels from point A to point C. From point A to point B, located between A and C, it travels at a speed of 48 km/h. At point B, he reduces his speed by a km/h (0 < a < 48) and at this speed travels a third of the way from B to C. The rest of the way from B to C he travels at a speed that is 2a km/h higher than the initial speed of 48 km/h. At what value of a will the automatically fastest one travel from B to C?
4. A sphere is inscribed in a regular quadrangular rectangle with vertex S and base ABCD. The side of the base is equal to a, the side edge is equal to $\frac{a \sqrt{5}}{2}$. On the apothet SE of the DSC face, point M is taken so that SM = ME. Find the distance between the points at which straight line AM intersects a sphere inscribed in a rectangular one.
5. Pentagon ABCDE is inscribed in a circle. The points M, Q, N and P are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from the vertex E corresponding to the sides AB, BC, CD (or their extension) and the diagonal AD. It is known that EP = d, and the ratio of the area of ​​the triangle MQE to the area of ​​the triangle PNE is equal to k. Find the length of the segment EM.

Faculty of Psychology

1. Calculate without using a table
$$\frac{\log_2 24}{\log_9 2} - \frac{\log_2 192}{\log_{12} 2}.$$
2. Given a cube with base ABCD and side edges AA', BB', CC', DD'. On the continuation of edges AB and BB' of the corresponding segments AM and B'N of length AM = $\frac{1}{2}$ AB and B'N = 2B'B ($BM = \frac{3}{2} AB; BN = 3BB'$). Where on the edge CC' must we choose point P so that the section of the cube by the plane drawn through the points M, N and P is a quadrilateral?
3. Find all values ​​of x for which
$$\min \left\{ 1 - x^2, \frac{1-x}{2} \right\} > \frac{1}{2}.$$
4. A worker produced a certain number of parts of types A and B, and he produced more parts A than parts B. If he produced 2 times more parts A, then the total number of parts would be less than 32, and if he produced 2 times more parts B, then the total number of parts would be more than 28. How many parts did A and how many parts B made?

5. For what values ​​of a does the equation
$$\sin^2 3x - \left(a + \frac{1}{2}\right) \sin 3x + \frac{a}{2} = 0$$
has exactly three roots located on the segment $\frac{2\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \pi$?

Moscow Institute of Railway Transport Engineers

Specialties:
industrial and civil engineering, transport exemplification, transport economics, railway construction

1. The excavator must dig a pit with a volume of 120,000 m³ within the appointed time (no more than a month). If he removed 100 m³ more soil every day, he would spend 14 days more on completing all the work than the time it takes him to dig $1/3$ pit, working according to plan. How long did it take to dig the pit?

2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{24}{x^2 + 2x - 8} - \frac{15}{x^2 + 2x - 3} = 2.$$
3. A chord of a circle is 10 cm. A tangent to the circle is drawn through one end of the chord, and a secant parallel to the tangent is drawn through the other end. Determine the radius of the circle if the internal segment of the secant is 12 cm.
4. Prove accuracy
$$4 \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) = \frac{\sin 3\alpha}{\sin \alpha}.$$
