1. Fill in the cells at figure with the missing numbers.
2. Place a number near each vertex of the triangle. Add a number near each side of this triangle equal to the sum of the numbers at its ends. Add each number near the top to the number near the opposite side. Why are all three resulting sums equal?
3. Find an odd four-digit number whose two middle digits form a number that is five times the number of thousands and three times the number of units of that number.
4. Can the expression $\frac{a+9}{a+6}$ (a is an integer) be an integer? If so, at what values ​​of a?

5. There were 76 people in two rooms. When 30 people left one room and 40 people left the second, the number of people left in the rooms was equal. How many people were in each room originally?

V. M. Rosentuler

6. Imagine that you are a driver. The driver drives a train from Moscow to Vladivostok. It consists of 64 wagons, 14 wagons with furniture, 30 wagons with brown coal, 20 wagons with salt. Make 3 stops of 15 minutes each. To the first stop the train travels at a speed of 60 km/h. How old is the driver?

G. I. Kurlykit
