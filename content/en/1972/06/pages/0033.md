3. Greatest Common Divisor [GCD]

Let $a$ and $b$ be integers that are not both equal to zero. Distance all the numbers by which $a$, and $b$ are divisible, and choose the most from them. We will denote this greatest common divisor of the numbers $a$ and $b$ by gcd ($a, b$). For example, GCD ($4,12$) = 4; gcd($21,91$) = 7; GCD ($15,28$) = 1.

If GCD ($a, b$) = 1, then the numbers $a$ and $b$ are called coprime.
