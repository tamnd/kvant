ARMENIAN NATIONAL TASKS

(Letter from reader)

Dear editors!

At our school we are already in the 2nd year of the mathematical class “XYZ” (X - work, Y - knowledge, Z - folklore). The main club collects folk tasks, which in Armenia are called “khrakhchanaks”. And there are many of them...

Armenian mathematicians Anania Shirakatsii (VII century), Mkhitar Yerznikatsii (XIV century), Zakaria Sarkavarg (XVII century) and others proposed different “khrahchanaks” in their works.

...Members of the "XYZ" class collected and edited more than 15 problems - khrahchanaks from our cela. We present some of them to your attention.

The stone was pulled into the yard

During the construction of Vaanavaank *) a two-hundred-pound stone was taken from the quarry. Halfway there, four bulls took him for three days. the stone was heavy, it had to be broken, 150 pounds remained of it, and the oxen they took were half as many as there were. How long did it take for the stone to be dragged into Vaanavaanka's courtyard?

One hundred marzan of wheat

The miller divided 100 marzan of wheat into three parts to load onto the horse, the donkey and the burro. If you take away the donkey's load from the horse's load, then the donkey can transfer the remainder in six steps, and if you take away two donkey loads, then the donkey can transfer the remainder in four steps, and on the fifth - there will be a load left that is half less than in each of the previous four.

*) Vaanavak is the famous Armenian monastery of Khvek.

Find out how much cargo MelBNik loaded onto the horse, donkey and burro?

Daughters of Navasard

Navasard's daughters were carpet weavers. If Navasard had given each daughter seven skeins minus one, then he would still have the same share of skeins left. And if he had given each one seven and one more skein, then his daughter would have had no Zarenitki left at all. How many skeins did Navasard have and how many daughters?

Fox and cubs

On seven mountains there are seven trees, under each tree there are seven holes, in each hole there live seven foxes, and every fox has seven cubs. How many foxes are there in total?

Robert Ejanansky Club “XYZ” Lernadzor school memories of Kafan region of the Armenian USSR
