About systems of physical units

The editor of the magazine receives a large number of letters from our readers asking us to place tables of systems of physical units in the magazine.

In this issue we publish tables that show the units of the International System (SI), the GHS system, as well as introduced units.

When solving problems on equal terms, both the SI system and the GHS system can be used.

The article was prepared for publication by M. L. Smolyansky.

Basic units

1 Length meter m Length equal to 1,650,763.73 wavelengths in vacuum of radiation corresponding to the transition between levels $2p_{10}$ and $5d_{5}$ (orange line) of the atom of krypton-86.

2 Mass kilogram kg Unit of mass equal to the mass of the international prototype kilogram.

3 Time seconds ss Sekund - 9,192,631,770 periods of radiation corresponding to the transition between two hyperfine levels of the ground state of the cesium-133 atom.

4 Strength of electric current amper A The strength of an unchanging electric current, which, passing through two parallel conductors of infinite length and negligible steep cross-section, located at a distance of 1 m from each other in a vacuum, would cause between these conductors a force equal to $2.10^{-7}$ N for each meter of length.

5 Temperature Kelvin K Unit of thermodynamic temperature - 1/273.16 part of the thermodynamic temperature of the triple point of water.

6 Luminous intensity candela cd Unit of light emitted from the area $1/600 000 \text{ m}^2$ of the cross section of the full emitter in the direction perpendicular to this section, at the solidification temperature of platinum and a pressure of 101 325 Pa.

Additional units

7 Plank angle radian $pa\delta$ The angle between two radii of a circle, the arc between which is equal in length to the radius.

8 Solid angle steredian $cp$ Solid angle with the vertex at the center of the sphere, cut out on the surface of the sphere with an area equal to the area of ​​the square on the side equal in length to the radius of the sphere.

Some products of the unit

1 Speed ​​meter per second $m/c$ The speed of a rectilinearly and uniformly moving point at which it covers a distance of 1 m in 1 s.

2 Acceleration meters per second squared $m/c^2$ Acceleration of a rectilinearly and uniformly accelerated moving point, at which in a time of 1 s the speed of the point changes by 1 m/s.

3 Frequency hertz $\Gamma_u(\Gamma_u=1/c)$ The frequency at which one cycle of a periodic process occurs in 1 s.

4 Density kilogram per cubic meter $k_2/m^3$ Density of a homogeneous body having a volume of 1 $m^3$ mass 1 $k_2$.

5 Force newton $H=(H=(\kappa_2\cdot m)/c^2)$ A force that imparts an acceleration of 1 $m/c^2$ to a body of mass 1 $k_2$ in the direction of the force.

6 Quantity of motion (impulse) kilogram-meter per second $(\kappa_2\cdot m)/c$ Quantity of motion of a body with a mass of 1 $k_2$ moving at a speed of 1 $m/c$.

7 Pressure pascal $Pa(Pa=H/m^2)$ The pressure caused by a force 1 $H$ uniformly distributed over a surface of area 1 $m^2$.

8 Work, energy joyle $Джc(Джc=H\cdot m)$ Work done by force 1 $H$ when his body moves a distance of 1 $m$ in the direction of the force.

9 Power batt $Bm(Bm=Джc/c)$ The power at which equals 1 J is accomplished in time 1 $c$.

10 Moment of a force newton meter $H\cdot m$ The moment of a force equal to 1 $H$ about a point located at a distance of 1 $m$ from the line of action of the force.

11 Quantity of heat joil $Джc$ Quantity of heat equivalent to mechanical work 1 J.
