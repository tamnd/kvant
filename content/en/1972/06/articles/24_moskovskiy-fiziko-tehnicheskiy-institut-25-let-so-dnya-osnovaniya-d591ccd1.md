MOSCOW PHYSICAL AND TECHNICAL INSTITUTE

(25 years since foundation)

Many readers of our magazine ask us to tell you about the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. On November 25, 1971, MIPT celebrated its 25th anniversary. In this regard, we are publishing an article based on materials from the multi-print MIPT newspaper “For Science”. The article was prepared by A. P. Savin and N. A. Mints.

THE HISTORY OF THE MOSCOW ORDENA OF THE RED BANNER OF LABOR PHYSICAL AND TECHNICAL INSTITUTE BEGAN LONG BEFORE ITS FOUNDATION. Even before the war, a number of the largest scientists of our country - academicians I. V. Kurchatov, A. F. Ioffe, M. A. Lavrentiev, P. L. Kapita, N. N. Semenov, S. A. Khristanovich - approached the government with a proposal to open a new type of higher educational institution in the USSR to train personnel in the latest fields of physics and technology.

Phystech was created in November 1946.

The peculiarity of the educational system adopted at the Physico-Technical Institute is that here fundamental higher education is combined with special training in the so-called basic research institutes and design bureaus, where special departments of the institute have been created.

In the first years, students are given general scientific training.

Mathematics, general and theoretical physics, and social sciences are studied in the scope of university courses, so that future specialists have good knowledge that forms the basis of the education of a research physicist. Quick familiarization with the space by the flow of scientific and technical information contributes to in-depth study of foreign languages.

Training in a specific specialization and independent research work at the base institute begins already in the second or third year.

At the base institute, the student attends lectures in his specialty, participates in scientific seminars, and is deeply involved in the research work of the institute’s laboratories. The future specialist deals with the latest equipment; participates in solving not model problems invented specifically for his practice, but actual problems facing the laboratory. The thesis work of each MIPT student is, as a rule, included in the thematic plan of the base institute. In the creative atmosphere of a scientific team, the student undergoes invaluable school education. Working under the direct supervision of a major scientist largely determines the success of his future independence.

Secondary basic institutes of MIPT - leading scientific centers in Moscow: Physical Institute of the USSR Academy of Sciences named after. P. N. Lebedeva, Institute of Physical Problems of the USSR Academy of Sciences. S. I. Vavilova, Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova, Institute of Theoretical and Experimental Physics

The lecture is given by Academician B. M. Pontekorvo.

Over the years, the institute has trained 5,200 engineers, and 1,280 people have completed postgraduate studies. Among the graduates of the institute are 5 academicians and corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences, 78 doctors of science, 12 laureates of the Lenin Prize, 17 laureates of the State Prize and awards issued to scientists. More than 1000 graduates of the institute have academic degrees and degrees.

The success of the institute is largely due to the fact that it does a lot of work to prepare a large number of student ranks.

The search for talented and hardworking youth begins long before the entrance exams at the institute. 120 graduate students and more than 300 undergraduates teach at the correspondence school of physics and technology of the institute. Several thousand children from various regions of the country attend it. Teachers and students of the institute actively participate in conducting various physical and mathematical tests.

Currently, the institute is preparing a specialty in cybernetics.

Prepares physicist engineers for research in the field of modern radio electronics. The main specializations of the faculty are electronic computers and devices, automatic control systems, technical cybernetics, information theory, radiophysics and radio engineering. Students with a penchant for experimental research can specialize in the field of radio engineering and physical-technical measurements of record accuracy.

Faculty of General and Applied Physics

The faculty's specialties are very diverse. These are low-temperature physics and actrophysics, partial high-energy and solid-state physics, physics of quantum generators and space radio communications, as well as “traditional” physical specialties: acoustics, optics, theoretical physics.

Faculty of Aerophysics and Space Research

The main scientific areas of the faculty, in which specialists are trained, are related to space exploration, as well as the study of near-Earth space, the atmosphere, the Earth’s hydrosphere and the Earth’s own. Much attention at the faculty is paid to the training of scientists who ensure the development of space research tools.

The faculty has a wide range of specializations related to the physics and mechanics of liquids, gases and plasmas, physics and mechanics of rapid protection processes, mechanics of control and plastic media, technical cybernetics, flight control and processes in power plants. Specializations related to the thermohydridynamics of the sea and atmosphere are very popular.

Faculty of Molecular and Chemical Physics

The main areas studied at this faculty are plasma physics, molecular biophysics and chemical physics.

In the field of plasma physics, problems related to the development of methods for directly converting plasma energy into electric current energy are studied.

In the field of molecular biophysics, specialists are trained in the study of processes occurring in living cells and the study of problems of heredity.

Students of chemical physics get acquainted with polymer chemistry, chemical cybernetwork, radiation chemistry, physical methods of physico-chemical research

Faculty of Physical and Quantum Electronics

It trains specialists in solid state physics, gas discharge and plasma, semiconductor electronics, quantum and microelectronics, converters and energy sources.

The faculty has created a laboratory of physical and quantum electronics, where students master modern methods of film electrons, electron microsopia, electron and RENT genography, study quantum generators and amplifiers, microelectronics, converters of solar and thermal energy into electrical energy.

Faculty of Aeromechanics and Flight Technology

The objective of the faculty is to train highly qualified specialists in aerodynamics, flight dynamics, and strength of aircraft for various purposes. Graduates of the faculty must master modern methods of aerophysical experiment and computational and theoretical methods.

Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics

Graduates of the faculty should be able to describe various processes in formal mathematical language, create mathematical languages ​​capable of describing phenomena that science has not previously encountered, develop and implement new computational methods for solving various problems, including problems of mathematical physics. They study discrete analysis, machine languages, theoretical cybernetics, operations research, game theory, and optimal process theory.

Students are taught in a purely inductive manner, so even students with the same majors work, as a rule, in different directions, corresponding to their interests and inclinations.
