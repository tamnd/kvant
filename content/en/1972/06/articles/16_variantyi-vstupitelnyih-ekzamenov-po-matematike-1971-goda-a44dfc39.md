⟦folio 51⟧

Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov

Faculty of Physics

1. Solve the equation:
$$3tg x (7 + \cos 2x) = 2 (\cos 2x - 1).$$
2. Solve inequality
$$\log_3^2 x + 2 \log_9 (x^2) < 2.$$
3. A car travels from point A to point C. From point A to point B, located between A and C, it travels at a speed of 48 km/h. At point B, he reduces his speed by a km/h (0 < a < 48) and at this speed travels a third of the way from B to C. The rest of the way from B to C he travels at a speed that is 2a km/h higher than the initial speed of 48 km/h. At what value of a will the automatically fastest one travel from B to C?
4. A sphere is inscribed in a regular quadrangular rectangle with vertex S and base ABCD. The side of the base is equal to a, the side edge is equal to $\frac{a \sqrt{5}}{2}$. On the apothet SE of the DSC face, point M is taken so that SM = ME. Find the distance between the points at which straight line AM intersects a sphere inscribed in a rectangular one.
5. Pentagon ABCDE is inscribed in a circle. The points M, Q, N and P are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from the vertex E corresponding to the sides AB, BC, CD (or their extension) and the diagonal AD. It is known that EP = d, and the ratio of the area of ​​the triangle MQE to the area of ​​the triangle PNE is equal to k. Find the length of the segment EM.

Faculty of Psychology

1. Calculate without using a table
$$\frac{\log_2 24}{\log_9 2} - \frac{\log_2 192}{\log_{12} 2}.$$
2. Given a cube with base ABCD and side edges AA', BB', CC', DD'. On the continuation of edges AB and BB' of the corresponding segments AM and B'N of length AM = $\frac{1}{2}$ AB and B'N = 2B'B ($BM = \frac{3}{2} AB; BN = 3BB'$). Where on the edge CC' must we choose point P so that the section of the cube by the plane drawn through the points M, N and P is a quadrilateral?
3. Find all values ​​of x for which
$$\min \left\{ 1 - x^2, \frac{1-x}{2} \right\} > \frac{1}{2}.$$
4. A worker produced a certain number of parts of types A and B, and he produced more parts A than parts B. If he produced 2 times more parts A, then the total number of parts would be less than 32, and if he produced 2 times more parts B, then the total number of parts would be more than 28. How many parts did A and how many parts B made?

5. For what values ​​of a does the equation
$$\sin^2 3x - \left(a + \frac{1}{2}\right) \sin 3x + \frac{a}{2} = 0$$
has exactly three roots located on the segment $\frac{2\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \pi$?

Moscow Institute of Railway Transport Engineers

Specialties:
industrial and civil engineering, transport exemplification, transport economics, railway construction

1. The excavator must dig a pit with a volume of 120,000 m³ within the appointed time (no more than a month). If he removed 100 m³ more soil every day, he would spend 14 days more on completing all the work than the time it takes him to dig $1/3$ pit, working according to plan. How long did it take to dig the pit?

2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{24}{x^2 + 2x - 8} - \frac{15}{x^2 + 2x - 3} = 2.$$
3. A chord of a circle is 10 cm. A tangent to the circle is drawn through one end of the chord, and a secant parallel to the tangent is drawn through the other end. Determine the radius of the circle if the internal segment of the secant is 12 cm.
4. Prove accuracy
$$4 \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) = \frac{\sin 3\alpha}{\sin \alpha}.$$

⟦folio 52⟧

Specialties: automation, telemechanics and communications, transport electrification, bridges and tunnels

1. Two people have to do some work. At first, the first one did 2 hours of work, then the second one joined and they worked together for 1 hour. After that, the second one finishes the remaining work in 3 hours. How long can everyone do the whole job if the first one needs 2 hours less to complete the work than the second one?

2. Solve the system

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 = 4 + \frac{y}{2}, \\ \log_3 (x + 2y) + \log_3 (x - 2y) = 1. \end{cases}$$

3. A circle is inscribed in a rhombus whose side is 20 cm. Find the area of ​​the circle if one diagonal of the rhombus is $\frac{4}{3}$ times larger than the other.

4. Solve the equation

$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 x - 2 \sin 2x - \frac{3}{4} \sin^2 2x = 0.$$

Specialties: applied mathematics, automated control systems, electronic calculating machines

1. The three state farms are not located on the same straight line. The distance from the first to the third through the second is four times the length of the path between them. The distance from the first to the second through the third on a is the length of the straight path. The distance from the second to the third through the first is 185 km. In what interval is the value of a for which the indicated location of state farms would be possible?

2. Solve the equation

$$1 - 2x - x^2 = tg^2 (x + y) + ctg^2 (x + y).$$

3. The diagonal of a right parallelepid is equal to d and makes an angle α with the plane of the base, and β with one of the side faces. Determine the volume of the parallelepid.

4. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{1 - 2 \sin 4x} + \sqrt{6} \cos 2x = 0.$$

Ural State University

Matematiko-mechanical faculty

1. The number 1 + $\sqrt{2}$ is the root of the equation

$$x^3 + ax^2 + bx + 2 = 0.$$

Find the coefficients a, b of the equation and all its roots, provided that a and b are rational numbers.

2. Triangle AOB is rotated in its plane around point O by 90°, with vertex A moving to vertex A1, and B to B1. Prove that in triangle OAB1 the median dropped to side AB1 is perpendicular to A1B, and in triangle OA1B the median dropped to side A1B is perpendicular to AB1.

3. At what x and y does the inequality take place?

$$\log_3 x + \log_3 3 + 2 \cos y \leq 0?$$

4. Find all values ​​of parameters a and b for which the system

$$\begin{cases} x + y = a, \\ \lg x + \lg y + \lg x \lg y = b \end{cases}$$

has a solution.

5. Solve the equation

$$\log_3 x + \log_3 3 + 2 \cos y \leq 0?$$

6. Find all values ​​of parameters a and b for which the system

$$\begin{cases} x + y = a, \\ \lg x + \lg y + \lg x \lg y = b \end{cases}$$

has a solution.

7. On the ring railway from point A1 it goes along the route A2, A3, ..., An, again A1, A2, A3, ..., An and so on. It is known that each subsequent stage between neighboring points will travel at an average speed q times greater than the average speed on the previous stage. Determine how many times the time it takes to complete m full circles is greater than the time spent on the first circle.

8. Through a point M lying inside a circle of radius r, the diameter AB and the chord CD are drawn so that $\Rightarrow BMD = \alpha$, $\Rightarrow BDM = \beta$. Find the area of ​​triangle MBD.

3. Solve inequality:

$$\log_3 (x^3 + 1) \log (x + 1) x > 2.$$

4. For what values ​​of a does the equation

$$\frac{\sin x - 1}{\sin x - 2} + a = \frac{2 - \sin x}{3 - \sin x}$$

has a solution? Find a solution.

Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Option 1

1. If a two-digit number is divided by the sum of its digits, the result is quotient 4 and remainder 3. If the number is divided by the product of its digits, the result is quotient 3 and remainder 5. Find this number.

2. In a trapezoid whose bases are a and b, a straight line parallel to the base is drawn through the point of intersection of the diagonal. Find the length of the segment of this straight line cut off by the sides of the trapezoid.

3. Solve inequality

$$\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x + 7} + 2 \sqrt{x^2 + 7x} < 35 - 2x.$$

4. Solve the equation

$$\cos (\pi \sqrt{x - 4}) \cos (\pi \sqrt{x}) = 1.$$
