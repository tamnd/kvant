PHYSICS EXAM AT MOSCOW ENGINEERING PHYSICS INSTITUTE

For applicants to the Moscow Engineering Physics Institute, an oral exam in physics is held. Each exam card consists of two theory questions from various sections of the school physics course and one problem.

To give an idea of ​​the nature and degree of difficulty of the examination problems, we are publishing several papers offered in 1971.

Ticket 1
1. Converging and dispersing lenses. Lens formula. Construction of an image in lenses.
2. The work of moving a charge in an electric field. The concept of potential. Precise charge field potential (without output).
3. In a tube, closed on one side and closed at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal, there is a piston of mass $m = 1$ kg (Fig. 1). The cross-sectional area of ​​the tube is $S = 8$ cm$^2$.

Fig. 1.
The piston was moved, increasing the volume of air under it by $n = 2$ times. Find the initial acceleration of the piston after it is released. Disregard training. External air pressure $p_0 = 760$ mm rm. cm.

Ticket 2
1. The work of moving a charge in an electric field. The concept of potential. Precise charge field potential (without output).
2. Absolute temperature scale. Unified law of gas state.
3. A body weighing 0.5 kg, mounted on a bar 2 m long, rotates uniformly in a vertical plane around an axis, making 20 revolutions per minute. Determine the tension force of the barbell during rotation at the highest and highest points of the body trajectory.

Ticket 3
1. Kinetic and potential energy. Transition of potential energy into kinetic energy and vice versa. Law of conservation of energy in mechanics.
2. Dispersion of light. Spectrum. Emission and absorption spectra.

3. The receiving wire $r = 10$ cm is in a uniform magnetic field with induction $B = 0,1$. Vector B is perpendicular to the receiving plane. The center of the receiver is connected to it by two conductors (Fig. 2), one of which $AO$ is stationary, the other - $OC$ - is rotated around the point $O$ with an angular velocity of $\omega = 10$ rad/sec. The resistance unit of the length of all conductors is $p = 0,65$ ohm/m. Find the current in circuit $AOC$ at the moment when the angle π between $AO$ and $OC$ is equal to π.

Ticket 4
1. Electrical capacity. Unit of electrical capacity. Electrical capacity of a conducting sphere. Capacitors.
2. Mechanical work. Work formula. Power. Energy. The unit of their measurement.
3. A cylindrical vessel, closed at common ends, was placed on an inclined plane making an angle $\theta = 30^\circ$ with the horizon (Fig. 3). The cylinder contains a biography lens $L$ with a focal length of $f = 10$ cm; the focal plane of the lens coincides with the upper torso of the cylinder. The vessel contains a liquid with a refractive index $n = \sqrt{3}$ (see Fig. 3). A ray of light emerges vertically from point $S$ at the bottom of the vessel. How far will the trace of this ray shift when the cylinder slides without friction along an inclined plane?
