Let's count the values ​​of the body's potential energy from the Earth's surface, that is, assume that on the Earth's surface the potential energy is zero. This means that we need to raise the red curve by figure 1 to $\frac{\gamma M}{R_3} m$, and write the formula for the potential energy of the body as follows:

$$U = -\frac{\gamma M}{R} m + \frac{\gamma M}{R_3} m = \gamma M m \frac{(R - R_3)}{RR_3}.$$

If the change in the distance of the body to the center of the Earth $h = R - R_3$ is small

$$U = \frac{\gamma M m}{R_3^2} h.$$

Since in most practical problems the rotation of the Earth is usually neglected, we can assume that on the surface of the Earth a body of mass $m$ attracts with a force $mg = \frac{\gamma M m}{R_3^2}$, then $\frac{\gamma M}{R_3^2} = g$ and $U = mgh$.

We got the same formula again. It is clear that it can be used at $h \ll R_3$. In this case, we replace the dotted curve with a tangent to it at point $R = R_3$.
