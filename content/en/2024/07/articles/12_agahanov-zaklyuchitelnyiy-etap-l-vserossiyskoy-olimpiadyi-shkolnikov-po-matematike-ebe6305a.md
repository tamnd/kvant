The final stage of the L All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The final stage of the anniversary, fiftieth All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad took place from April 19 to April 25, 2024 in Nizhny Novgorod. The main organizer of the Olympiad was Minin University. The Olympiad participants were offered an interesting excursion program, as well as attending popular science lectures from mathematicians - representatives of the country's leading universities. 471 schoolchildren, representing almost all regions of Russia, took part in the Olympiad. Also, according to tradition, a team from China participated in the Olympiad: six schoolchildren from Zhejiang province wrote the Olympiad online.
