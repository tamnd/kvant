Estival
Flower

The puzzle was made for the XIX World Youth Festival and
students, held in Sochi in 2017. Unlike a puzzle
“Festival Pegs”, described in «Kvant» No. 3 for 2018, this puzzle was not mass produced. There are only a few homemade copies of it.

The puzzle consists of two identical disks in the shape of a festival daisy with windows into which double-layered petals protrude with a common rotating axis. The petal pairs in each of the five windows can be turned over to the other side around the axis. Both disks are fastened with a screw in the center, so they can be rotated relative to each other by an angle that is a multiple of 72°. If you perform several such operations one after another, the original coloring of the chamomile petals is disrupted. The player's goal is to restore this coloring. It turns out to be a kind of flat Rubik's cube.

The puzzle can be made at home. The discs and their windows are cut out with a jigsaw from a 4mm thick plate. Dimensions are shown at figure. A hole is drilled in the center of each disk; the disks superimposed on each other are secured with a screw. The fastened disks are placed in a vice and five through holes are drilled, strictly along the axis symmetrically for each window, in which the axes of the petal pairs meet.

By rotating the elements of the puzzle randomly, it is impossible to find a solution - after all, the puzzle has more than 100 thousand (10!/2^5) different states and only one is correct. To succeed, you need to understand the patterns of movement of daisy petals during the game and develop an algorithm for solving this puzzle.

Good luck!

N. Avilov
