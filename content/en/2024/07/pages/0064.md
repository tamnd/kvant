1. 72.
The number of two-digit numbers divisible by 6 coincides with the number of integer solutions to the inequality 10 ≤ 6k < 100. Then 2 ≤ k ≤ 16, which gives 15 two-digit numbers divisible by 6. Considering the inequality 10 ≤ 15k < 100 and its corollary 1 ≤ k ≤ 6, we get 6 numbers dividing by 15, and 10 ≤ 30k < 100 and 1 ≤ k ≤ 3 gives 3 numbers dividing by both 6 and 15. This means that 15 + 6 - 3 = 18 is the number of numbers dividing by 6 or 15. Subtracting this number from the number of two-digit numbers, 90 - 18 = 72, we get the answer to the problem question.
2. At 700 meters.
Let $a, b$ and $c$ be the total path to the ascent, along the horizontal section and to the descent of the corresponding one when moving from $A$ to $B$. This path will take $t_{AB} = \frac{a}{4} + \frac{b}{6} + \frac{c}{7}$ time. The return journey will take $t_{BA} = \frac{a}{4} + \frac{b}{6} + \frac{c}{4}$. By condition $t_{AB} - t_{BA} = \frac{9}{60}$, therefore,
$$a \left( \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{7} \right) + b \left( \frac{1}{7} - \frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{3}{20}.$$
Hence $a - b = \frac{7}{5}$. Since the sine of the angle is equal to $\frac{1}{2}$, then the path to ascent or descent of one unit gives an increase or decrease in height by half a unit. Therefore, the required height difference is $\frac{a - b}{2} = 0,7$ kilometers.

5. $\sqrt{2}$.
The largest of the numbers $x$ and $y$ is not less than $\frac{x + y}{2}$.
Therefore, the largest of the numbers $x$, $y$ and $\frac{4}{x + y}$ is not less than the largest of the numbers $\frac{x + y}{2}$ and $\frac{4}{x + y}$. The product of the last two numbers is 2, which means that one of these numbers is at least $\sqrt{2}$. Thus, it is proven that the largest of the numbers $x$, $y$ and $\frac{4}{x + y}$ is not less than $\sqrt{2}$. Equality is realized at $x = y = \sqrt{2}$. The latter means that $\sqrt{2}$ is the answer to the question in the problem.
