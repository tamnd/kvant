By choosing $x_1$ white segments and $x_2$ black segments, some pair of selected segments will certainly intersect. Prove that
$$(y_1 - x_1)(y_2 - x_2) < x_1x_2.$$
G. Chelnokov

8. The natural $n > 2$ is given. Masha writes $n$ natural numbers in a circle. Next, Taya performs the following operation: between each two adjacent numbers $a$ and $b$ she writes some divisor of the number $a + b$, greater than 1; Taya then erases the original numbers and gets a new set of $n$ numbers standing in a circle. Can Taya always perform operations in such a way that after a few operations all the numbers are equal?

T. Korotchenko

1. There is an infinite cylinder in space (i.e., the locus of points distant from a given straight line $l$ at a given distance $R > 0$). Can six straight lines containing the edges of some tetrahedron have exactly one common point with this cylinder?

A. Kuznetsov

2. A triple of positive numbers $(a,b,c)$ is called mysterious if
$$\sqrt{a^2 + \frac{1}{a^2}c^2} + 2ab + \sqrt{b^2 + \frac{1}{b^2}a^2} + 2bc + \sqrt{c^2 + \frac{1}{c^2}b^2} + 2ca = 2(a + b + c).$$
Prove that if the triple $(a,b,c)$ is mysterious, then the triple $(c,b,a)$ is also mysterious.

A. Kuznetsov, K. Sukhov

3. See problem M2801 “Problem book «Kvant»”.
4. The quadrilateral $ABCD$, which has no parallel sides, is inscribed in the circle $\omega$. A straight line $l_a \parallel BC$ is drawn through vertex $A$, a straight line $l_b \parallel CD$ is drawn through vertex $B$, a straight line $l_c \parallel DA$ is drawn through vertex $C$, and a straight line $l_d \parallel AB$ is drawn through vertex $D$. A quadrilateral whose successive sides lie on these four lines (in this order) is inscribed in the circle $\gamma$. Circles $\omega$ and $\gamma$ intersect at points $E$ and $F$. Prove that lines $AC$, $BD$ and $EF$ intersect at one point.

A. Kuznetsov

5. See problem 5 for 9th grade.
6. An acute unequal triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle $\omega$ with center at point $O$, its altitudes intersect at point $H$. A line perpendicular to $AH$ is drawn through points $O$, and a line perpendicular $AO$ is drawn through points $H$. Prove that the intersection points of these lines from the sides $AB$ and $AC$ lie on the same circle, which is tangent to the circle $\omega$.

A. Kuznetsov
