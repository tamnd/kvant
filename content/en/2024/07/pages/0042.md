The final stage of the L All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The final stage of the anniversary, fiftieth All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad took place from April 19 to April 25, 2024 in Nizhny Novgorod. The main organizer of the Olympiad was Minin University. The Olympiad participants were offered an interesting excursion program, as well as attending popular science lectures from mathematicians - representatives of the country's leading universities. 471 schoolchildren, representing almost all regions of Russia, took part in the Olympiad. Also, according to tradition, a team from China participated in the Olympiad: six schoolchildren from Zhejiang province wrote the Olympiad online. Problem conditions

9th grade

1. Petya and Vasya know only natural numbers not exceeding $10^{9} 4000$. Petya considers good numbers representable in the form abc ab ac bc, where a, b and c are natural numbers not less than 100. Vasya considers good numbers representable in the form xyz x y z, where x, y and z are natural numbers greater than 100. For which of them are there more good numbers? I. Bogdanov

2. A natural number has exactly 50 divisors. Is it possible that no difference of two different divisors is divisible by 100? Methodological commission based on the problem of A. Chironov

3. Two boys were given a bag of potatoes, each bag containing 150 tubers. The guys take turns transferring the potatoes, each of them, in their next move, transfers a non-zero number of tubers from their bag to someone else’s. At the same time, they must comply with the condition of the new possibility: on each move the boy must move more tubers than he had in the bag before any of his previous moves (if there were such moves).
