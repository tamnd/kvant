3. Density $\rho_2$ is found from the condition of equality of liquid pressure at the bottom in the left and right vessels:
$$\rho_2 g h + \rho_1 g \left(\frac{3}{2} H - h\right) = \rho_1 g H, \rho_2 = \rho_1 \left(1 - \frac{H}{2 h}\right).$$
The liquid levels in two positions of the vessel will be equal and become equal to $\frac{5H}{4}$, i.e. in the left vessel the level will drop to $\frac{H}{4}$, and in the right vessel to $\frac{H}{4}$. It should also be kept in mind that $h$ is always greater than $\frac{H}{2}$. Depending on the value of $h$ in the task, 2 cases are possible.
1) If $h < H$, then the lower boundary of the second liquid will not drop to the level of the tube. Then the mass of the piston is found from the condition of equality of pressure at the bottom of the vessel:
$$\frac{mg}{S} + \rho_2 g h + \rho_1 g \left(\frac{5H}{4} - h\right) = \rho_1 g \frac{5H}{4},$$
$$m = \left(\rho_1 - \rho_2\right) h S = \rho_1 \frac{H}{2} S.$$
2) In the case of $\frac{5H}{4} > h > H$, the lower level of liquid with density $\rho_2$ in the process of lowering the piston should reach the tube, the liquid begins to roll into the right vessel and will float up in it, since $\rho_2 < \rho_1$. If we count from the bottom, the liquid with density $\rho_1$ in the left vessel should reach the level $\frac{H}{4}$, and the column of liquid with density $\rho_2$ has a high $H$. From the condition of conservation of volumes it follows that the column of liquid with density $\rho_1$ in the right vessel now has a height of $\left(\frac{9H}{4} - h\right)$, and the height of the column of liquid with density $\rho_2$ is equal to $(h - H)$. From here we get
$$\frac{mg}{S} + \rho_2 g h + \rho_1 g \frac{H}{4} = \rho_1 g \left(\frac{9H}{4} - h\right) + \rho_2 g (h - H),$$
$$m = \rho_1 (2H - h) S + \rho_2 (h - 2H) S = \rho_1 \frac{H (2H - h)}{2h} S.$$
