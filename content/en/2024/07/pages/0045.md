EXAMINATION MATERIALS

For many years, the National Research University MIET has been holding olympids in mathematics, physics, information and other subjects that are very popular among schoolchildren. The online Olympiad “RHYTHM MIET” has been held since 2015. In the 2023/24 academic year, more than 800 people took part in 9 sections of this Olympiad. The MIET Physics and Mathematics Olympid has been held since 2018. In the current academic year, more than 1,400 schoolchildren took part in the competition stage of the Olympics. The final stage of the MIET Physics and Mathematics Olympiad took place at 13 sites in the Russian Federation and other countries. In the spring of 2024, MIET also hosted a subject tour of the National Technical Olympiad for schoolchildren with a profile in “Digital Touch Systems.”

Below are the physics problems proposed at these olympids.

Internet Olympiad
"RHYTHM MIET"

Final stage

1. A stone is thrown horizontally from point $A$. At what initial speed $v_0$ of the stone will it move away from point $B$, located at a distance $h = 6,4$ m under point $A$ on the same vertical with it, during the entire flight time? Neglect air resistance. Free pressure acceleration $g = 10 \text{ m/c}^2$.

2. At what altitude above the Earth's surface does the speed of a satellite in a circular orbit differ from the first escape velocity by $\delta = 10\%$? Take the radius of the Earth equal to $R = 6400 \text{ km}$.

3. On a horizontal surface there is a wedge, the inclined plane of which makes an angle $\alpha = 50^\circ$ (Fig. 1) with the horizon. The ball hits the wedge and bounces off elastically. Its speed before the impact is directed horizontally, and after the impact it is directed vertically. Determine the ratio of the mass of the wedge to the mass of the ball. Disregard training.

4. Find the pressure of a mixture of gases in volume $V = 2 \text{ л}$, if it contains $N = 10^{22}$ nitrogen molecules and $m = 1$ g of oxygen, at a temperature of the mixture $50^\circ \text{C}$. Molar mass of oxygen $M = 32 \text{ г/mоль}$, Boltzmann constant $k = 1,38 \cdot 10^{-23} \text{ Дж/K}$, Avogadro number $N_A = 6,02 \cdot 10^{23} \text{ mol}^-1$.
