Let us remove from the graph the subgraph from the vertex $A$ and all its descendants, thus we have removed a connected subgraph in which the number of vertices is at least $M$ and no more than $2M-1$. We will repeat this operation until there are at least $M$ vertices in the graph.
This operation can be performed at least $t = \left[ \frac{N+M-1}{2M-1} \right]$ times.
In each of the removed connected subgraphs, you can select a connected subgraph with exactly $M$ vertices by removing one vertex at a time. It corresponds to a $m$-polygon, and the removed higher vertices correspond to triangles. Thus, the polygon corresponding to this subgraph can be cut into one $m$-gon and triangles. Therefore, for each operation we will obtain one $m$-gon in the partition.
Let us give an example of a tree from which it is impossible to select a larger number of subgraphs of size $M$ that we need. Let's distance $t$ red vertices connected as on figure 9; the remaining vertices will be blue. On each of the chains there are no more than $M-1$ blue vertices (at figure an example is shown for $t=4$, $M-1=3$, the general case of the device is similar).
