1. Let the acceleration of free pressure be equal to $g$, and let the shot be fired at an angle $\alpha$ to the vertical with a speed of $v_0(\alpha)$. Then
$$a_n = \omega v_0(\alpha) = g_n = g \sin \alpha, \quad v_0(\alpha) = \frac{g \sin \alpha}{\omega}.$$
Let us introduce the coordinate axis $y'$, directed perpendicular to the surface of the slide. The projection of the projectile acceleration onto this axis is equal to $g_{y'} = -g \cos \varphi$. Then for the projectile flight times we have
$$t = -\frac{2v_{0y'}}{g_{y'}} = \frac{2v_{0y'}}{g \cos \varphi}.$$
The maximum possible flight time of the projectile corresponds to the maximum value $v_{0y'}$. Let's introduce a coordinate system $xy$ with the origin at the location of the gun, axis $x$ directed to the right, and axis $y$ vertically upward. Then
$$v_0 = \frac{g \sin \alpha}{\omega} = \frac{g}{\omega} \frac{v_{0x}}{v_0}, \quad v^2 = v_{0x}^2 + v_{0y}^2 = \frac{g v_{0x}}{omega},$$
where
$$\left(v_{0x} - \frac{g}{2\omega}\right)^2 + v_{0y}^2 = \left(\frac{g}{2\omega}\right)^2.$$
Thus, the locus of the end points of the velocity vector $\vec{v}_0$ is a circle (Fig. 22). From the geometry figure it follows that the maximum flight time corresponds to the maximum value $v_{0y'}$, which is achieved when the end of the velocity vector lies on a perpendicular drawn to the direction of the slide surface from the center of the circle. We get
$$v_{0y'(\max)} = \frac{g(1 - \sin \varphi)}{2\omega},$$ from where $t_{\max} = \frac{1 - \sin \varphi}{\omega \cos \varphi} \approx 1,15$ s.

2. Let us determine the speed of the drum after a collision with washer 1. Since there is no space between the washers and the drum, all of them are centrally removed, the movement of all bodies remains translational, and the projections of the bodies’ velocities onto the axis perpendicular to the center line remain constant during the collision. Let us introduce an axis $y$ along the line of centers, directed from washer 1 to the center of the drum. Let $\alpha$ be the angle between the speed vector of puck 1 and the line of centers. Let us write down the law of conservation of momentum in projection onto the $y$ axis and the law of conservation of energy:
$$mv_0 \cos \alpha = Mv_{M1} + mv_{1y},$$
$$\frac{mv_0^2 \cos^2 \alpha}{2} + \frac{mv_0^2 \sin^2 \alpha}{2} =$$
$$= \frac{mv_{1y}^2}{2} + \frac{mv_0^2 \sin^2 \alpha}{2} + \frac{Mv_{M1}^2}{2},$$

$$v_{M1} = v_0 \cos \alpha + v_{1y} = 2v_0 \cos \alpha - \frac{Mv_{M1}}{m} = \frac{2mv_0 \cos \alpha}{m + M}.$$
Let us obtain the conditions under which there will be a gap between washer 2 and the drum. If you introduce the axis $z$, directed from washer 2 to the center of the drum, then the collision conditions will be that the projection of the speed of the drum relative to washer 2 onto the axis $z$ will be negative, i.e.
$$v_{z(\text{отн})} = -v_{M1} \cos 2\alpha - v_0 \cos \alpha =$$
$$= -v_0 \cos \alpha \cdot \left(1 + \frac{2m \cos 2\alpha}{m + M}\right) < 0.$$
Since cos $\alpha > 0$, this condition can be written as follows:
$$1 + \frac{2m \cos 2\alpha}{m + M} = 1 + \frac{2m(2 \cos^2 \alpha - 1)}{m + M} > 0,$$
$$\cos \alpha = \frac{s}{2R} > \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}(1 - \frac{M}{m})} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}},$$ or $s > \frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}.$

$$u_1 = \frac{mv_0}{m + M} \frac{s_1}{R} = \frac{v_0}{3}, v_0 = 3u_1.$$
