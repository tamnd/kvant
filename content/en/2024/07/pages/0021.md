Solutions to problems M2786–M2793, F2793–F2796 M2786. There are 100 white dots in a circle. Anya and Borya take turns painting one not yet painted point red or blue, Anya begins. Anya wants to end up with as many pairs of multi-colored neighboring dots as possible, and Borya wants to end up with as few such pairs as possible. What is the largest number of pairs of multi-colored neighboring dots that Anya can guarantee for herself, regardless of Bori's game?

Answer: 50. In total, there are 100 pairs of neighboring points in a circle. It is enough to show that Anya can always ensure that among these 100 pairs of different-colored pairs there are at least 50, and Borya can ensure that among these 100 pairs of same-color pairs there are at least 50.
