Let point T be symmetrical to point A with respect to DO (Fig.2). Then DT is the tangent to the circle: symmetrical to the tangent DA with respect to DO. We have ETF FAE EGF. It follows that points E, F, T and G lie on the same circle. Next ETD DAE ACB EKD. It follows that points D, E, K and T lie on the same circle. It turns out that T is the Mikel point for the four straight lines DBC, DEF, EKG, FHG. This means that points T, K, H and G also lie on the same circle. To complete the solution, it remains to prove that DT is tangent to the circle (TKHG). Using the equality of inscribed angles, parallelism and symmetry with respect to DO, we have DTK FEK EFA TFE TGK. The resulting equality DTK TGK means that DT is tangent to the circle (TKHG).
