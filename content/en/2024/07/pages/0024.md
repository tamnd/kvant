M2789*. Given a natural number $n > 100$. Initially, the number 1 is written on the board. Let's give a strategy for Petya. To do this, imagine 1 as a sum of $2^n$ fractions with numerator 1, divide them into pairs of unequal ones, and add the numbers in each pair. Then $2^{n-1}$ of the obtained results will again be divided into pairs of unequal ones and we will add the numbers in each pair. We will continue to do this until we get one number. Since the sum of all fractions is 1, then after $n$ steps the number 1 remains.

Let us assume that the process described above occurs. In this case, Petya will expand 1 into the sum of two numbers that add at the $n$th step.
