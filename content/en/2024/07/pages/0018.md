KVANT 2024/№7

S.P. Novikov’s contribution to mathematical and theoretical physics received worldwide recognition. He is the founder of the method of finite-zone integration of nonlinear equations of mathematical physics, the algebrogeometric approach in the theory of solitons, and the creator of the theory of multivalued action functionals (Morse–Novikov theory). He discovered topological phenomena in normal metals under conditions of a strong magnetic field. For his contribution to science, S.P. Novikov was awarded the Great Gold Medal named after M.V. Lomonosov - the highest award of the Russian Academy of Sciences (2020), the Leonhard Euler Gold Medal of the Russian Academy of Sciences (2012), the Gold Medal named after N.N. Bogolyubov of the Russian Academy of Sciences (2009), the N.I. Lobachevsky Prize of the Russian Academy of Sciences (1980). He was elected an honorary member of many foreign academies and scientific societies. S.P. Novikov was the editor-in-chief of the journal “Uspekhi Matematicheskikh Nauk” (1988–2023), president of the Moscow Mathematical Society (1985–1996). S.P. Novikov is the creator of an outstanding scientific school, the author of fundamental monographs and textbooks. The problems posed by S.P. Novikov and the hypotheses put forward by him continue to have a great influence on the development of science. For more than 40 years, Sergei Petrovich's pedagogical activity was inextricably linked with the department of higher geometry and topology of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, which he headed from 1982 until the end of his life. S.P. Novikov was one of the initiators of the creation of the Independent Moscow University, which plays an important role in Russian mathematical education. S.P. Novikov also paid great attention to the problems of school education. Sergei Petrovich did a lot to attract both young and already well-known actively working mathematicians to the journal «Kvant» as authors. The memory of the great scientist, teacher and wonderful person will forever remain in our hearts.
