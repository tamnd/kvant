1. The velocity vectors of all points of the rigid rod at the considered moment of time are equal to each other. So that after a small time $\Delta t$ the velocity projections of all points on the direction of the rod still remain the same, the acceleration projections on the direction of the rod must also be the same. The absolute values ​​of the accelerations of the ends of the rod are different, but the accelerations are directed along parallel straight lines, so the accelerations can give the same projections on the direction of the rod only if the acceleration vectors are perpendicular to the rod. Let's move to the inertial system of the report, in which at a given moment in time the rod will end. The accelerations of the points of the rod will not change their value and direction. After an infinitely small period of time $\Delta t$ the velocities of the points of the rod will be perpendicular to the rod and the speed of the point $A$ will be twice the speed of the point $B$. The velocities of the remaining points of the rod will be proportional to the distance to the instantaneous center of rotation. Let this be a point $O$ on the rod, then the accelerations of the points at the beginning of the time interval $\Delta t$ should also be proportional to the distance to the point $O$. Let us draw straight lines through the beginnings of accelerations $\vec{a}_A$ and $\vec{a}_B$ and through their ends. The point of the rod that lies at the intersection of these lines has zero acceleration (point $O$). Let's find the acceleration of the center of mass of the rod. If the accelerations of points $A$ and $B$ are directed in the same direction, then the acceleration of the center of mass is equal to $\frac{3}{2} a_0$ and coincides with the direction of vectors $\vec{a}_A$ and $\vec{a}_B$. If the accelerations of points $A$ and $B$ are directed in different directions, then the acceleration of the center of mass is equal to $\frac{1}{2} a_0$ and coincides with the direction of the vector $\vec{a}_A$. In both cases, the force vector is directed in the same direction as the vector $\vec{a}_A$.
