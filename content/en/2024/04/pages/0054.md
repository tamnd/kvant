7. At zero.
Let's call the fast and slow cockroaches $B$ and $M$ respectively. If the cockroach moves in the same direction as at the moment of start, then we will say that it moves $\sigma$without, otherwise we will say that it moves $\sigma$back.

Before the first meeting, both cockroaches move forward; between the first and second meeting, $B$ moves forward, and $M$ moves back. Between the second and third meetings, both cockroaches move backwards, and between the third and fourth meetings, $B$ goes back, and $M$ goes forward. Finally, at the fourth meeting, $B$ turns around and they both begin to rush forward again.

We will monitor the movement of $M$. If between two meetings the cockroaches run in opposite directions, the same time always passes between such meetings, which means that $M$ always runs the same distance. Thus, between the first and second meetings, as well as between the third and fourth meetings, $M$ runs the same distance in opposite directions. Likewise, when between two encounters the cockroaches run in the same direction, it also always takes the same time and $M$ runs the same distance. Thus, before the first meeting, as well as between the second and third meetings, $M$ also runs the same distance in opposite directions. Therefore, at the moment of the fourth meeting, $M$ (and therefore $B$) will be at the starting point.

From now on, this situation will be repeated every 4 meetings. Consequently, there will be cockroaches at the starting point at the time of the hundredth meeting.
