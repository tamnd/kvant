1. Potential graph (6 points)
Three identical thin disks of radius $R$, uniformly charged with surface densities $\sigma$, $2\sigma$ and $-3\sigma$, are arranged so that the $x$ axis is the common axis of these disks, with the centers of the disks located at points with coordinates 0, $d$ and $2d$, where $d = \frac{R}{1000}$.
The potential in the center of a single disk, uniformly charged with surface density $\sigma$, is equal to 1000 V. The potential of the point at infinity is considered to be zero.

A. Obtain the formula $\varphi_0 = \frac{\sigma R}{2\varepsilon_0}$, which gives the potential at the center of a single uniformly charged disk. You can use this formula when completing the next step, even if you cannot derive it.

B. Draw a graph of the dependence of the potential $\varphi$ of the electric field of the disks at points lying on the $x$ axis on the dimensionless coordinate $a = \frac{x}{d}$ for values ​​$a$ belonging to the segment [0; 2].
