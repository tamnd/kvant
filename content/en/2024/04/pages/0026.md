MAURICE KRAJCZIK (21 APRIL 1882 – 19 August 1957) is generally considered a Belgian mathematician and popularizer of science. He was born in Minsk to a Jewish family (father - Boris Kraichik, mother - Sonya Kaufman). After graduating from school, he went to study mathematics in Belgium, where he entered the University of Lvezh. In 1923, he completed his dissertation on number theory. In 1922–1930 and after emigration, he wrote several books on number theory, and from 1931 to 1939 he published the journal Sphinx, dedicated to his mathematics. During World War II, he immigrated to the United States, where he taught a course on "Entertaining Mathematics" at the New School for Social Research in New York. After the war he returned to Europe.
