1. Rod on a plane (5 points)
A homogeneous rod of mass $m$ moves along a plane in such a way that at some instant of time the abscille values ​​of the accelerations of the ends $A$ and $B$ of the rod turn out to be equal to $a_A = 2a_0$ and $a_B = a_0$, while the accelerations are directed along parallel straight lines, and the velocity vectors of the ends are equal to each other. 2. Thermodynamic cycle (6 points)
The state of one mole of an ideal diatomic gas changes in a cycle consisting of processes with a constant heat capacity. figure 11 shows a graph of the work done by the gas versus the amount of heat received or given off by it when it reaches its current state.

A. Determine the efficiency of the cycle.

B. What is the minimum gas temperature in this cyclic process if the maximum temperature is 481 K?

Application. A quasi-static process in which the molar heat capacity of a gas $c$ remains constant is described by the relation $pV^{(c-c_p)/(c-c_v)} = \text{const}$, where $p$ and $V$ are the pressure and volume of the corresponding gas, $c_V$ and $c_p = c_V + R$ are the molar heat capacities at constant volume and constant pressure.

A. Bychikov

3. Monkey pulls udvava (8 points)
The boa constrictor straightened up, lying on a horizontal surface, relaxed and did not resist at all. Its mass $M$ is uniformly distributed along its length $L$. The surface is smooth, and along it, next to the daring and parallel to it, there is a path (not smooth) for walking. The monkey, being on this path, grabbed the Udvava by the tip of his tail and pulled this end of the Udvava towards his head. In this case, the boa constrictor lies entirely on the surface. The force $F$ with which the monkey pulls the tail of the udwava is horizontal and varies depending on time $t$ according to the law $F = At$, where $A$ is a known constant.

A. At what speed did the tip of the Udvava’s tail move at the moment when the distance from it to the Udvava’s head first became $\frac{L}{2}$?

B. How long does it take for the Udvava's tail to appear with the Udvava's motionless head?

Q. What will be the speed of the entire double at the moment when his head begins to move?

Note. The formula may be useful

$$\int_a^b x^n dx = \frac{1}{n+1}\left(b^{n+1} - a^{n+1}\right), \quad n \neq -1.$$

S. Varlamov
