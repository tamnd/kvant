Note that one of the three factors $a^2 + 1, b^2 + 1, c^2 + 1$ cannot be divided by the product of two of the numbers $p, q, r$, since it is less than this product. Indeed, consider, for example, $pq = (a + bc)(b + ca)$. From opening the brackets we see that

$$pq > c^2(ab) + ab \geq c^2 + 1, pq > b^2c + ab \geq b^2 + 1$$

and similarly $pq > a^2 + 1$. This means that each of the factors $a^2 + 1, b^2 + 1, c^2 + 1$ must be divisible by one of the numbers $p, q, r$. Let, for the sake of definition, $a$ be the smallest of the numbers $a, b, c$.
