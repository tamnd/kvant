3. Heterogeneous field (8 points)
Between the region of space in which there is no magnetic field and the region in which there is a uniform magnetic field with the direction of the induction vector $B$ parallel to the interface between these regions, there is a transition section of width $L$ in which the field is linearly directed. A proton flies into this transition region peredicularly to the interface at a speed of $v$. The maximum penetration depth of a proton to where the field is present is exactly equal to the thickness of the transition section. The proton turn lasted $\tau_p = 1$ s. At exactly the same speed and also peredicular to the interface, an electron flew into the same region. How long does it take for an electron to turn around? To what depth did the electron penetrate into the region where there is a magnetic field? The mass of an electron is 1838 times less than the mass of a proton.
S. Varlamov, K. Lvov
