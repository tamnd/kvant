1. Let us write down the kinematics equations for sections of the path covered in time $t_1$ and $t_1 + t_2$:
$$s = v_0 t_1 - \frac{g t_1^2}{2}, 2s = v_0 \left( t_1 + t_2 \right) - \frac{g \left( t_1 + t_2 \right)^2}{2},$$
where $s$ is the length of two identical sections, $v_0$ is the initial speed of the stone. Solving these equations, we get
$$v_0 = \frac{2g t_1^2 - g \left( t_1 + t_2 \right)^2}{2 \left( t_1 - t_2 \right)}.$$

Then the total flight time before impact with the ground is equal to
$$t_{\text{пол}} = \frac{2 t_1^2 - \left( t_1 + t_2 \right)^2}{t_1 - t_2} = 7\text{с}.$$

2. The area of ​​a regular hexagon is determined by the formula $S_{\text{ш}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} a^2$, where $a$ is the side of the hexagon. The volume of a straight prism is $V = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} a^2 h$, where $h$ is the height of the prism. The pressure exerted by the base of the prism will be determined by the ratio $p_1 = \frac{mg}{S_{\text{ш}}} = \frac{2mg}{3\sqrt{3} a^2}$. From here we find the side of the hexagon: $a = \sqrt{\frac{2mg}{3\sqrt{3} p_1}}$.

The pressure exerted by the side face of the prism is determined by the relation $p_2 = \frac{mg}{ah}$, from which we find the height of the prism: $h = \sqrt{\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} m g p_1} \frac{1}{p_2}$. Now let’s find the density of the prism materials:
$$\rho = \frac{m}{V} = \frac{m}{S_{\text{ш}} h} = \sqrt{\frac{2 p_1}{3\sqrt{3} m g}} \frac{p_2}{g} = 8160 \text{ кг/м}^3.$$
