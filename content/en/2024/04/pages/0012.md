A lack of growth hormones and an imbalance between them leads either to the synthesis of the missing ones or to inhibition of plant development. Let us give an example of restoring the balance between the production of auxin and cytokinin. If you mediate a young branch and place it in a base with water (while retaining the upper part with auxins and removing the lower part with cytokinins), then after some time root hairs appear in the area of ​​the medium. In this case, the formation of hormones is mixed towards cytokinins and leads to the growth of a new root. And vice versa, on the remaining branch with the root (the part with cytokinins is preserved, the part with auxins is removed) a new shoot appears.

An example of a closed circulatory system in animals and humans

The circulatory system of animals is considered closed and consists of two circles of blood circulation: one circle closes the heart and lungs, the other – the heart and internal organs (Fig. 3). At the same time, the idea of ​​complete closure of the circulatory system is inaccurate. Its functioning requires interaction with several open systems: respiratory, digestive and lymphatic.

The respiratory system brings oxygen into the lungs and removes carbon dioxide. This process takes place in the alveoli of the lungs, produced by the capillaries of the circulatory system.

The digestive system is the main supplier of “building materials” to the body.

The role of the lymphatic system is less known.
