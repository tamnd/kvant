Juniors
THERE ARE RECORDS

13-year-old Ivan Zemlyansky, at the tournament in Sharjah, achieved the grandmaster standard for the fourth time and became the youngest in the history of Russia to hold the highest chess title (according to the rules, three standards are enough, but at least one of them must be obtained in the tournament, we conduct it according to the Swiss system - this is exactly what was missing in the collection of the young sportsman). It is noteworthy that the previous record belonged to Anish Giri, who was born and raised in St. Petersburg and only changed his sports citizenship at the age of 15.

L. Mendonca – I. Zemlyansky Sharjah, 2024
