M2798. Given a straight road, laid out from green and red planks (a road is a segment divided into plank segments). The colors of the boards alternate; The first and last boards are greens. The grasshopper wants to burp forward along the road along these planks, stepping on each green plank at least once and without stepping on any red plank (or the border between neighboring planks).
