SIGN OF DIVISION ON A GRAPH
Let's show how, using the graph shown in figure, you can check the divisibility of a number by 7. For example, consider the number 3248. We will proceed as follows. Starting from the white top, take 3 steps along the red arrows, then one step along the blue arrow, then 2 steps along the red arrows, then again one step along the blue arrow, then 4 steps along the red ones and so on. If at the end we find ourselves at the white vertex again, then the number is divisible by 7. Try to prove this wonderful fact. Think about how to construct a graph of divisibility by 13 and, in general, by an arbitrary number. What will the divisibility graph by 7 look like if we start moving through the graph from the lowest digits?
