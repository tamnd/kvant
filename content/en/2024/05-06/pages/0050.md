2. 27 (example: $0,5 + 1,6 + 7,4 + 8,3 + 9,2 = 27$) and 18 (example: $0,9 + 1,8 + 3,7 + 5,4 + 6,2 = 18$).
The sum $J + b + P + B + \ddot{J}$ must be zero. The sum 10 can only be obtained if the five smallest digits are $(0 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10)$, but such an example cannot be created, since the field cannot appear after the decimal point (then the fraction will be an integer in the question). The maximum sum of a fifth of digits is $9 + 8 + 7 + 6 + 5 = 35$, so you can only get sums of 20 and 30. Note that all letters are different, i.e. all ten digits are recorded once. The total sum of all ten digits is 45. Therefore, if the sum of the digits after the decimal point is 20, then when summing after the decimal point we will get 0, and in the previous digit we will cross 2. This digit 2 will be added to the sum of the digits before the decimal point, which is equal to $45 - 20 = 25$, and we find the answer 27. Similarly, if the sum of the digits after the decimal point is 30, then the answer will be $45 - 30 + 3 = 18$.
Comment. There are a lot of examples in this problem - there are 86,400 ways to get the sum of 27 and 72,000 ways to get the sum of 18.
