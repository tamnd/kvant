Suppose that in some group all cells are flat. Then for each cell of this group the sum of the numbers in the row containing it is less than the sum of the numbers in the column containing it. Summing up these primaries over all cells of the group, we find that the sum of the numbers in the entire table, cleared by rows, is less than this sum, cleared by columns - a contradiction, which means that in each group there is a good cell and the number of good cells is not less than the number of groups, i.e. no less than $N$.
