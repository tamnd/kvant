F2807. Two identical exact positive electric charges $Q$ are located in a vacuum far from other bodies at a distance $L$ from each other. Naturally, they are repelled. Between them was placed a thin flat round metal plate of radius $R \gg L$, located so that the straight line on which the charges $Q$ and $Q$ are located is perpendicular to this plate and the center of the plate is in the middle between the charges. What is the charge on this plate if the forces acting on the charges are now zero? Edge effects can be neglected.

S.Varlamov

F2808. Four identical windings are symmetrically located on the goroidal core in the sequence $A-B-C-D$. Winding $A$ was connected to an alternating voltage source, and an ideal voltmeter was connected to winding $B$. With winding $C$ short-circuited and winding $D$ open, the voltmeter shows 50% of the source voltage, and with winding $D$ short-circuited and winding $C$ open, it shows 75%. What part of the source voltage will the voltmeter show if windings $C$ and $D$ are short-circuited? The resistance of the material of the windings and the source is negligible.

V. Poimanov
