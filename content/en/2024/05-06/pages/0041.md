Task 1. Elevator
The elevator starts moving from a state of rest and leaves two floors above after a time of $t_2 = 5,0$ s, and to four floors above after a time of $t_4 = 8,0$ s. The elevator, without leaving between floors, covers the required distance in the minimum possible time, while its speed and acceleration moduli do not exceed some unknown values ​​$v_0$ and $a_0$, respectively. The height of all floors is the same; the time for opening and closing doors can be neglected. Let us use without proof the fact that when climbing two floors, the door leaves the speed limit value $v_0$.

1) How long will it take $t_3$ for the elevator to rise to three floors?

2) How long will it take $t_1$ for the elevator to rise to one floor?

D.Rubtsov

In this case, the thread is not tensioned, the lever is horizontal. A piece of ice is placed in the vessel, after which its contents are heated at a constant power (tell losses, as well as the volume capacity of the sauce, can be neglected). At the same time, a graph of the thread tension force versus time is plotted (Fig. 7);

1) mass $m$ of a piece of ice;

2) power $P$ with which the contents of the vessel were heated;

3) initial temperature $t_0$ ice.

Markings on the lever divide it into 8 equal length parts. The lateral face of the vessel is parallel to the plane figure.
