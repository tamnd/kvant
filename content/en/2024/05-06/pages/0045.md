3. The sequence $x_n$ is given by the following relations: $x_1 = 1, x_n = -\frac{2}{2 + x_{n-1}}$ with $n > 1$. Find the sum of the first 34 terms of the sequence.
4. Find the most negative solution to the equation
$$2\sin \pi x = \sqrt{2 - \sqrt{3}}.$$
In your answer, indicate the product of this solution by 12.
5. Trains departed from the two cities towards each other. After traveling a third of the way, the first train stopped for 45 minutes. Resuming movement, he met a second train 6 minutes later. The trains arrived at their destinations simultaneously. How many minutes did the second train travel?
6. How many integer solutions does the inequality have?
$$\sqrt{3 - \sqrt{5 - x}} < \sqrt{1 - x}?$$
7. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2x^{\log_2 y} + y^{\log_4 x} = 3, \\ 2x - y = -1. \end{cases}$$
Write down $x + y$ in your answer.
8. A circle is inscribed in a right triangle with catatas $9\sqrt{2}$ and $12\sqrt{2}$. Find the distance from its center to the vertex of the right angle.
9. A regular quadrangular paramid has a lateral edge of length $3\sqrt{3}$. Find the most possible value of the volume of this paramid.
10. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ the equation
$$\left|2x^2 - 3x\right| = 2 + \frac{ax}{3}$$
has exactly three solutions? In your answer, indicate the sum of all such $a$.

Final round
1. How many two-digit natural numbers are not divisible by either 6 or 15 without a remainder?
2. From point $A$ to point $B$ there is a trail, which is a sequence of horizontal sections, ascents and descents with a slope of $30^\circ$. The pedestrian code involves an ascent, a horizontal section and a descent at speeds of 4 km/h, 6 km/h and 7 km/h respectively. It turned out that the journey from $A$ to $B$ takes 9 minutes longer than the return trip. How many meters above sea level is point $B$ higher than $A$?
3. How many integer solutions does the equation have?
$$\sin \frac{\pi x}{6} - \sqrt{3} \cos \frac{\pi x}{6} = \sqrt{3}$$
on the interval [0;50)?
4. The lateral faces of a regular triangular paramid are right-angled triangles. Find the side of the base of the paramid if the radius of the ball inscribed in it is $1/\sqrt{6}$.
5. What value does most of the numbers $x, y, \frac{4}{x + y}$ take when $x$ and $y$ are positive?
6. A circle inscribed in a triangle sequentially divides one of its medians into segments of length $2\sqrt{3}, 4\sqrt{3}, 2\sqrt{3}$, respectively. Find the length greater than the side of the triangle.
7. Two polygons are drawn on checkered paper: a rectangle $258 \times 3$ without two unit cells, straight to the opposite vertices, and a rectagon $258 \times 3$ without two unit cells, straight to the vertices of the furthest side. The number of ways to cut the first and second polygons into rectangles $2 \times 1$ along checkered paper lines $a$ and $b$ respectively. Find $a - b$.
8. The function $f(x)$ is defined on the real line and for any real $x$ and $y$
$$f(x + y) - (f(x))^2 = e^x (f(x) + f(y)).$$
Find all such functions.
9. Solve the equation
$$(5 - x)(6 - x)(\sqrt{x} + \sqrt{4 - x}) = 4.$$
10. At what values ​​of the parameter $a$ does the system
$$\begin{cases} y = \sqrt{x - a}, \\ x = \sqrt{y - a} \end{cases}$$
has a single solution?
