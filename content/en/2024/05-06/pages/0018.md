M2798. Given a straight road, laid out from green and red planks (a road is a segment divided into plank segments). The colors of the boards alternate; The first and last boards are greens. It is known that the lengths of all the planks are more than a centimeter and less than a meter, and also that the length of each subsequent plank is greater than the previous one. The grasshopper wants to burp forward along the road along these planks, stepping on each green plank at least once and without stepping on any red plank (or the border between neighboring planks). Prove that the grasshopper can do this in such a way that among the long jumps there are no more than 8 different values.

T. Korotsenko

M2799. Given a natural number $n$. Ilya thought of a couple of different polynomials of degree $n$ (with real coefficients), Sasha also thought of a couple of different polynomials of degree $n$. Lenya knows $n$; his goal is to find out whether Ila and Sasha have the same pairs of polynomials. Lenya chooses a set of $k$ real numbers $x_1 < x_2 < \ldots < x_k$ and reports these numbers. In response, Ilya fills out the table $2 \times k$: for each $i=1, 2, \ldots, k$ he enters in two cells of the $i$th column a pair of numbers $P(x_i)$, $Q(x_i)$ (in any of two possible orders), where $P$ and $Q$ are the polynomials he conceived. Sasha fills out a similar table. At what minimum $k$ can Lenya (looking at the tables) surely achieve her goal?

L. Shatunov

M2800. Given a parallelogram $ABCD$ (Fig. 4). Point $M$ is the midpoint of arc $ABC$ of a circle circumscribed about triangle $ABC$. On the segment $AD$ the point $E$ is offset, and on the segment $CD$ the point $F$ is offset. It is known that $ME = MD = MF$. Prove that points $B$, $M$, $E$ and $F$ lie on the same circle.

A. Tereshin

M2801. Yuri approached the great Mayan table. The table has 200 columns and $2^{200}$ rows. The wind rose and blew away some of the sheets: two sheets from each line. Could it happen that now Yuri can find out at least about 10,000 rows what is depicted in each of the columns?
