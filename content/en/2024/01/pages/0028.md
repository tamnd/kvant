Kirchhoff's rules for points 1 and 2 of the problem have the form

$$I_1 = I_2 + I_5,$$
$$I_4 + I_5 = I_3,$$

We write Kirchhoff's rules for points 3 and 4 of the problem in the form

If the temperature change occurs a short time after the circuit is closed (points 1 and 3 of the problem), then we can ignore heat loss and assume that the released amount of heat loss goes to heating the wire, so the increase $\Delta T_n$ in the temperature of resistor $R_n$ is equal to

$$\Delta T_n = k_{13} \frac{I_n^2 R_n}{L_n D_n^2},$$

where $k_{13}$ is the proportionality coefficient. Taking into account the known value of $\Delta T_5$, it is convenient to write the same result in the form

$$\frac{\Delta T_n}{\Delta T_5} = \left(\frac{I_n}{I_5}\right)^2 \frac{R_n}{R_5} \frac{L_5}{L_n} \left(\frac{D_5}{D_n}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{I_n}{I_5}\right)^2 \left(\frac{D_5}{D_n}\right)^4.$$
