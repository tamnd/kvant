CALENDAR-
PUZZLE

We present to our readers a puzzle invented by Norwegian inventors from DragonFjord. The puzzle is called “A Puzzle-A Day”.

The head is a field divided into squares in which numbers from 1 to 31 are written and called twelve months. The goal is to place the puzzle pieces, seven pentominoes and a 2 by 3 rectangle, in the field so that two squares remain uncovered, one with a number and one with a month.

Every day you can find exercise for your mind: collect a puzzle with the next date. Each data is collected in several ways. There are only seven ways to collect on October 6th and as many as 191 ways to collect on January 25th! You can study how many ways this or that data is collected on the website https://etudes.ru/models/puzzle-calendar/.
It is interesting that certain economy is noticeable between the number of assemblies and the location of the date. For example, for example, in a number of ways you can collect the 6th number and the 24th. The largest are 28th, 7th and 31st. The easiest time to collect dates is in January, and the most difficult is in October. Thus, here the puzzle also gives reason for thought.

We wish you success!
