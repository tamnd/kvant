Tasks
in mathematics and physics
We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given asterisks. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.
Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant».
Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same address.
