Projections of interstellar flight segments onto the galactic plane are shown in figure 5. The chaotic nature of the spacecraft’s movement from one star to another is clearly visible under conditions of flight over the shortest distance. Taking into account the fact that the total flight time of the starship is 6000 years, during the flight the starship visited more than 1000 stars, which is 1/10 of the total number of stars taken into account in the calculations.

The calculations presented in Figures 4 and 5 were obtained under the assumption that the starship is sent to the next star without delay. Taking into account the delay by time τ leads to a decrease in the angle of inclination of the straight line $r = Dt$, and the diffusion coefficient along with the parameter $l$ will include $l^* = l + v\tau$. Thus, the settlement time will increase by $l^*/l$.

60000, 150000 and 210000 km/s, assuming $r = \sqrt{vlt}$, are presented in figure 6. As can be seen from figure, even for relativistic speeds it will take hundreds of thousands and millions of years to reach stars that are more than 2000 light years away.

The results of calculations of the random walk of a starship in the Galaxy serve as a good illustration of the features of the motion of Brownian particles and the diffusion associated with this motion. They also indicate that the process of human exploration of the Galaxy will most likely proceed according to a diffusion scenario. Moreover, the diffusion coefficient will depend on a number of important factors that are not taken into account in our modeling. We will list only the main ones: the number of starships participating in the exploration of the Galaxy, the likelihood that flights to the stars will be successful, the time required to explore exoplanets and create on them the necessary actro-engineering technical base for organizing new exepids, and, of course, the number of exoplanets suitable for settlement. As can be seen from the above list, one of these factors can accelerate the process of people penetrating further and further into the depths of the Galaxy, while others, on the contrary, will slow it down and make it more and more complex and slow.
