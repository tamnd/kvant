5. 2986.
Let us write the initial condition for the coordinates of points $A(x_1;y_1)$ and $B(x_2;y_2)$ in the form $2x_2 + y_2 - 12 = 2x_1 + y_1$.
Since the coordinates of the points $A(x_1;y_1)$ and $B(x_2;y_2)$ are integers, the left and right sides of this equality can only belong to the integer values ​​$k$. A pair of points $A(x_1;y_1)$, $B(x_2;y_2)$ with integer coordinates satisfies the condition if and only if they lie on parallel lines $y = -2x + k$ and $y = -2x + k + 12$ respectively.
Next, we will find suitable values ​​for the parameter $k$. The sides $OP$ and $QR$ of the parallelogram lie on the lines $y = -2x$ and $y = -2x + 30$, so they are parallel to the lines $y = -2x + k$ and $y = -2x + k + 12$. These lines intersect the parallelogram at $0 \leq k \leq 30$ and $0 \leq k + 12 \leq 30$, so $k \in [0;18]$.
Let's find out the number of points with integer coordinates on each of the lines of the form $y = -2x + b$. If $b$ is even, i.e. $b = 2l$, then we get the straight line $y = 2(-x + l)$. For any integer $x$, the integer value $y$ is obtained, and for the point to be inside the parallelogram, it is necessary that $0 \leq y \leq 24 \Leftrightarrow l - 12 \leq x \leq l$. For any $l$, 13 integer values ​​of $x$ satisfy this inequality. If $b$ is odd, i.e. $b = 2l + 1$, we have $0 \leq -2x + 2l + 1 \leq 24 \Leftrightarrow l - \frac{23}{2} \leq x \leq l + \frac{1}{2}$. Considering that $x \in \mathbb{Z}$, we get $l - 11 \leq x \leq l$ – a total of 12 integer values.
If $k = 2l$ (there are 10 such values), then on each of the two lines $y = -2x + k$ and $y = -2x + k + 12$ you can select 13 points - a total of $10 \cdot 13 \cdot 13 = 1690$ ways. If $k = 2l + 1$ (there are 9 such values), then on each of the two lines $y = -2x + k$ and $y = -2x + k + 12$ you can choose 12 points - we have $9 \cdot 12 \cdot 12 = 1296$ ways. In total we get $1690 + 1296 = 2986$ ways.
