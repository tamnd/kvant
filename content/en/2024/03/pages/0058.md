The winners and runners-up of the qualifying stage were invited to participate in the final stage of the Olympiad.

7-9 grades

1. Santa Claus came to the schoolchildren for the holiday and asked them to change the density of his new gift, covered with thin gift wrapping, which after the changes turned out to be equal to $\rho \left( \frac{\mathrm{krg}}{\mathrm{m}^3} \right)$. Santa Claus told the schoolchildren that the gift consists of three parts of equal mass, the density of which is in the ratio 1:2:3. Find the density of the smallest part by volume of the new gift. Assume that there are no voids in the New Year's gift, and consider the volume and weight of the gift wrapping to be negligible. Give your answer in $\mathrm{krg}/\mathrm{m}^3$ and round to the nearest whole number.

2. A bug crawls along a straight line for nine minutes. The speed of the bug in the first three minutes of movement is $v_1 = 2$ cm/s, in the first six minutes the average speed of movement is $v_2 = 5$ cm/s, and in nine minutes the average speed is $v_3 = 8$ cm/s. Find the insect's average speed over the last $s$ travel distances. Give your answer in cm/s and round to the nearest hundredth.

3. The tourist decided to check how efficiently the water was heated in his two pots. The first of the boilers does not have an additional heat exchanger, but the second does. The tourist poured $m_1$ (g) of water into both pots and closed them with lids to reduce energy costs for evaporation. To heat both boilers, the tourist used the same burner and a cylinder of flammable gas with an initial mass of $m_2$. Using this system, the tourist brought water to a boil in the first pot, and then in the second. After the completion of heating the first boiler, the mass of the gas cylinder became $m_3$, and after heating the second one $m_4$. Find by what percentage the efficiency of the described heating system using the second boiler is greater than when using the first, if the removed heat of the gas in the cylinder is $q$, the removed heat of water is $c$, the initial temperature of water is $t_1$, the boiling point is $t_2$, and the thermal heat of the metal of the pots and lids can be neglected. Give your answer to the nearest whole number of percent.

4. In the diagram shown at figure 1, the direction on the source stripes is $U_0$ (B). Find the current flowing through an ideal ammeter if the resistances of all resistors are the same and equal to $R$ (Ohm). The resistance of underwater wires can be neglected.

5. The heating element is made of wire with a temperature coefficient of resistance $\alpha \left(^\circ\mathrm{C}^{-1}\right)$. After connecting the heating element to a constant voltage source, it was replaced that the heating time of water in the calorimeter from temperature $T$ to temperature $T + \Delta T$ differs from the heating time from temperature $T + \Delta T$ to temperature $T + 2\Delta T$ by $p(\%)$. Determine the temperature interval $\Delta T$ (rounded to the nearest tenth) if it is known that $T = 0^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Consider that in the temperature range from $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to 100°C, the resistivity of the heating element wiring depends linearly on temperature. Heat losses and the thermal capacity of the calorimeter can be neglected.

10th grade

1. A boy on a Sankh with mass $m$ slides down a hill with an angle of inclination $\alpha$ to the horizon. The surface of the slide smoothly transitions to a horizontal section, along which the canken glides for another $s$ meters until it stops. After this, the canken and the boy are inserted back into the slide by the rope. What is the minimum work $A_{\min}$ that needs to be done if the friction coefficient of the sled on the snow both on the inclined and horizontal sections is $\mu$? Take the free fall acceleration equal to $g = 10 \mathrm{m}/\mathrm{c}^2$. Express your answer in joyles, rounded to whole numbers.
