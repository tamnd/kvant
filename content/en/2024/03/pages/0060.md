Final stage

For students of all grades, the final stage was held face-to-face at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

The task for students in grades 7-9 consists of five problems on topics studied as part of the physics program for a basic secondary school. The tasks for students in grades 10 and 11 were compiled in full accordance with the 2024 Unified State Exam Codifier in Physics and covered the main sections of the Codifier. A typical task included five problems from five different sections.

7-9 grades

1. A stone is thrown vertically upward from the surface of the earth. Some initial section of the path, starting on the surface of the earth, it flew in a time of $t_1 = 1$ s, and the next section of the path of the same length it flew in a time of $t_2 = 3$ s. Find the total flight time of the stone before it hits the ground. Air resistance can be neglected. Consider that the stone is still moving upward at the indicated time intervals.

2. A metal homogeneous straight line with a mass of $m = 1$ kg has a right-hand

hexagon (Fig. 7). If you place the base on a horizontal surface, then it will exert a pressure of $p_1 = 6\sqrt{3}$ kPa, and if it is placed on the side face, then the pressure will be equal to $p_2 = 4080$ Pa. Determine the density of the materials in the sample. The acceleration of free pressure is taken equal to $g = 10$ m/s².

3. Having invited us inside for a tea party in her garden, the grandmother poured boiling water weighing $m_B = 250$ g at a temperature of $t_B = 100$ °C into her favorite porcelain bowl with polka dots with an initial temperature of $t_\phi = 35$ °C. Immediately after this, she placed a middle spoon weighing $m_c = 80$ g into a bowl of boiling water, and then added $m_3 = 50$ g of tea leaves at a temperature of $t_1 = 20$ °C. As a result, the cup reaches a final temperature of $t_k = 80$ °C. Consider that the middle spoon is completely immersed in boiling water. The specific heat of the brew is equal to the specific heat of water. Heat exchange with the environment can be neglected. The specific heats of water, porcelain and sulfur are $c_B = 4200$ J/(kg °C), $c_\phi = 800$ J/(kg °C) and $c_c = 250$ J/(kg °C), respectively. Find the mass $m_\phi$ of your grandmother’s favorite tsashka.
