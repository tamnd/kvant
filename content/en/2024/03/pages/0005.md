JULIY BORISOVICH KHARITON

Chief designer Yuliy Borisovich Khariton at the model of his first “brainchild” in the Sarov Museum

KB-11’s “possessions” included $100 км^{2}$ protected lands and another $10 км^{2}$ territories of the Gorky region. Today the closed area is twice as large. From the Shatki station (33 km from Arzamas) a narrow-gauge railway with a length of 95 km led there. Before government decisions were made, Khariton and Zernov made this journey on a railcar, which they had difficulty obtaining in Shatki, since the plant was practically no longer operating. Khariton later recalled how he “... almost stepped on the nest of some large bird when, getting off the trolley, he went deep into the forest during the first reconnaissance at the site of the future city of Arzamas-16.” Among the classified names of this point, in addition to KB-11, were “550”, and “Arzamas-16”, and “Privolzhskaya office of Glavpromstroi”, etc. The name of the village of Sarov and its designation were removed by special decree from all maps and from all official documents. The work plan of KB-11, presented by Khariton and Zernov in July 1946, provided for the opening of 8 scientific laboratories, workshops, test sites and pilot production, which soon became plants No. 1 and No. 2. During the war, Zernov proved himself to be an outstanding leader in the production of tanks and other weapons, becoming a major general of the tank service and deputy people's commissar of the tank industry. We must give justice to the chairman of the Special Committee - it was difficult to find a better organizer for KB-11. Zernov had enormous capacity for work, organizational acumen, technical literacy and ingenuity, and, finally, like Khariton, responsiveness and philanthropy. Zernov’s main task was to create and organize the normal work of research and engineering departments, as well as technical and personnel support for all work. But the organization of people’s lives and everyday life also played a significant role. During his five years as head of KB-11, more than 400 buildings and structures were built in Sarov, including residential buildings and dormitories, nurseries and kindergartens, schools, hospitals and pharmacies, a stadium and a ski lodge, even a theater. Zernov paid great attention to ensuring the Sunday rest of his employees, and showed special attention to young people and their needs. The grateful memory of his deeds is immortalized in Sarov in the name of the longest and most beautiful street and park of culture and recreation. And his main production activities were assessed with two Stars of Hero of Socialist Labor and the titles of Laureate of Lenin and State Prizes. After working at the “facility” P.M.
