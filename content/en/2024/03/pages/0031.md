F2795. The ring has a radius R, the thickness of the ring is small compared to its radius, the mass of the ring m is evenly distributed along its perimeter. The ring was untwisted around its axis of symmetry, placed on a rough, friction coefficient equal to P, inclined surface with an angle of inclination D to the horizon, and pushed up.
