Olympida "Lomonosov"

Physics
In the 2023/24 academic year, the Lomonosov Olympiad in Physics at Moscow State University was held in two stages - qualifying and final.

Qualifying stage
The qualifying stage took place in the form of correspondence testing. The qualifying stage tasks were compiled separately for students in junior (7-9) and senior (10-11) grades. These tasks were posted in the participants’ personal accounts on the website https://olymp.msu.ru.

Since the data in the task conditions for each participant were individual, the data values ​​​​conducted here are in a general form.
