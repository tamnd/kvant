PUZZLE COLLECTION
SIMETRIX
from Prapezza

Symmetriks are puzzles in which several figures are required
fold the figure symmetrically. This type of puzzle was invented by the Japanese
inventor Tado Kitazawa in 2003. They often appear at annual
meetings in the Russian club of puzzle connoisseurs “Diogenes” and similar
international meetings of puzzle lovers IPP (International
Puzzle Party).

There are symmetrics with two, three or more elements. In puzzles of this type, you need to put together a symmetrical figure from several game elements, guided by simple rules: by applying elements to each other, they can be rotated and turned over as you like, but you cannot overlap one another.

We invite you to get acquainted with the symmetric, all three elements of which can be obtained by cutting a rectangular trapezoid ABCD with AB = AD and angle A equal to 60°, as shown in figure. Here point M is the midpoint of AB, point K is such that KD = 2CK, and point N is such that MN is equal to 2/3 of the midline of the trapezoid. The trapezoid is divided into three parts by segments BK, KN and MN: right-angled triangle BCK, quadrangle BKNM and pentagon AMNKD. These are the three game elements of symmetry. They can easily be made from paper, cardboard, plastic or wood (as in the photo).

Fold these three parts into a trapezoid into a symmetrical shape.
We wish you success!

N. Avilov
