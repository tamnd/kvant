25. There are N identical tiles, each in the shape of an equilateral triangle. Is it possible to make some kind of trapezoid out of all these tiles (without holes or overlaps)? Give the answer for a) N 99; b) N 100; c) for all N. P. Kozhevnikov

26. Numbers were entered into the cells of the table 10 and 10 so that in each row all 10 numbers are different. You are allowed to rearrange the numbers in each line as you wish. Will it always be possible to do this so that no two identical numbers end up in different columns? P. Kozhevnikov

27. In one regular group of friends, for two years in a row, birthdays most often fell on Tuesday. On what day of the week could they most often occur next year? S. Tokarev

28. Cartesian coordinates are introduced on the plane. Let ABC be a triangle with vertices at integer points, isosceles and with area 1 2. Prove that it is rectangular. E. Bakaev
