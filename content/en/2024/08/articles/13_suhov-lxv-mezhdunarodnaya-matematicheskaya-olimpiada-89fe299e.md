LXV International
math olympiad

The LXV International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) was held from July 11 to July 22, 2024. Like five years ago, the IMO was hosted by the UK, with the University of Bath being the venue for the IMO. 609 young mathematicians from 109 countries took part in the main school mathematics competition. Schoolchildren from Russia participated in the Olympiad remotely - two IMO rounds were held for them in St. Petersburg at the Russian State Pedagogical University named after A.I. Herzen.

This year, after seven stages of selection held in our country during the academic year, the right to participate in the IMO was won by:

Ilya Zamotorin (10th grade, St. Petersburg),
Ramibor Koptilin (10th grade, Novosibirsk),
Egor Saprunov (11th grade, St. Petersburg),
Tseren Frantsuzov (11th grade, St. Petersburg),
Ivan Chasovskikh (10th grade, Khimki),
Mikhail Yugov (11th grade, St. Petersburg).

The guys showed confident results, despite the high complexity of this year's set of problems. Please, the most “olympiad” problem, which surprised even the strongest and most experienced participants, turned out to be combinatorial problem 5. Let us note the accepted fact: the total score for this problem for our guys turned out to be the highest among all the teams. Information about the scores received by our participants and the overall success of solving the problem is given in the tables below.

Objectives of the Olympiad

1. Find all real numbers $\alpha$ such that for any positive integer $n$ the integer
$$\lfloor \alpha \rfloor + \lfloor 2\alpha \rfloor + \ldots + \lfloor n\alpha \rfloor$$
multiple of $n$. (Here, $\lfloor z \rfloor$ denotes the most integer number not exceeding $z$. For example, $$\lfloor -\pi \rfloor = -4 \text{ и } \lfloor 2 \rfloor = \lfloor 2,9 \rfloor = 2.$$)

Colombia

2. Find all pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers for which there exist positive integers $g$ and $N$ such that
$$\text{НОД}(a^n + b, b^n + a) = g$$
for all integers $n \geq N$.

Indonesia

3. Given an infinite sequence of positive integers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ and a positive integer $N$. It is known that for any $n > N$ the number $a_n$ is equal to the number of times the number $a_{n-1}$ occurs among $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots, a_{n-1}$. Prove that at least one of the sequences $a_1, a_3, a_5, \ldots$ and $a_2, a_4, a_6, \ldots$ is ultimately periodic. (A sequence $b_1, b_2, b_3, \ldots$ is said to be ultimately periodic if there are positive integers $p$ and $M$ such that $b_{m+p} = b_m$ for all $m \geq M$.) Australia

4. Let $ABC$ be a triangle containing $AB < AC < BC$. Let $\omega$ be the inscribed circle in triangle $ABC$, and $I$ its center. Let $X$ be a point on line $BC$, different from $C$, such that the line passing through $X$ parallel to $AC$ touches $\omega$. Similarly, let $Y$ be a point on the line $BC$, different from $B$, such that the line passing through $Y$ parallel to $AB$ touches $\omega$. Let $AI$ intersect the circumscribed circle of triangle $ABC$ a second time at point $P \neq A$. Let $K$ and $L$ be the midpoints of the side $AC$ and $AB$ respectively. Prove that $\angle KIL + \angle YPX = 180^\circ$.

Polysha

5. Snail Turbo plays the following game on a board that has 2024 rows and 2023 columns. There is a monster hiding in 2022 squares of the board. Turbo initially doesn't know where any of the monsters are, but she does know that there is exactly one monster in each row except the first and last, and that there is at most one monster in each column.

Turbo makes a series of attempts to get from the first row to the last. On each attempt, she can choose any cell in the first row as a starting cell, and then makes a series of movements from a cell to an adjacent cell that has a common side. (She is allowed to return to previously visited squares.) If she visits a square with a monster, her attempt ends and she is moved back to the first row to make a new attempt. Monsters do not move, and Turbo remembers whether there is a monster in each cell he visits. If she reaches any square in the last row, her attempt is over and the game is over.

Determine the minimum value $n$ such that Turbo has a strategy that, regardless of the location of the monsters, guarantees reaching the last row in $n$ attempts or sooner.

Hong Kong

6. Let $\mathbb{Q}$ be the set of all rational numbers. A function $f : \mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}$ is called adjacent if the following conditions are satisfied: for any $x, y \in \mathbb{Q}$ we have
$$f(x + f(y)) = f(x) + y$$ or
$$f(f(x) + y) = x + f(y).$$
Prove that there exists an integer $c$ such that for any adjacent function $f$ there are at most $c$ distinct rational numbers of the form $f(r) + f(-r)$ for each rational $r$, and find the non-naming possible value $c$.

Japan
