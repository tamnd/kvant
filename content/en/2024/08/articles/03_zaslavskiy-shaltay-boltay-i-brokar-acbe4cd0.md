Humpty, Dumpty and Brocard

A. ZASLAVSKY

In the summer of 2024, at the XX Olympiad named after I.F. Sharygin, participants in grades 9 and 10, respectively, were offered the following two tasks. Problem 1. In an isosceles triangle ABC (AC BC) O is the circumcenter, H is the orthocenter, Q is a point inside the triangle such that AQH BQO S 2. Prove that QAC QBA QCB.

Problem 2. On one of the medians of triangle ABC there is a point P such that PAB PBC PCA. Prove that on another median there is a point Q such that QBA QCB QAC.

Since both problems mention a point Q such that QBA QCB QAC, we can assume a connection between them. Below we will show that there really is a connection, but for now we will say that the point Q and the point designated in the second problem as P are called the Brocard points of triangle ABC. Brocard points have many beautiful properties, which the project was dedicated to at the XXV Summer Conference of the Tournament of Cities in 2013. You can also read about them in the book by A.V.
