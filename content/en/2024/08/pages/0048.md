KVAN T 2024/No.8

Conductor Mikhail Kirchhoff and the members of the symphony orchestra of the Gustav Kirchhoff Memorial Concert fact: almost all the students who took part in it remained in the meeting room until the last report. The real highlight of the Gustav Kirchhoff memorial week in Kaliningrad was the concert of the Kaliningrad Symphony Orchestra, conducted by the Russian descendant of Gustav Kirchhoff, Mikhail Kirchhoff. The participation of Mikhail Kirchhoff in the anniversary events of the great physicist became a symbol of the historical connection between different eras and peoples, and the concert itself became a kind of bridge uniting two very different eras. In addition to the conference and concert, as part of the “Kirchhoff Festival” project, more than 70 scientific and educational lectures were given in Kaliningrad and the cities of the region on the topics “Gustav Kirchhoff - our fellow countryman” and “Gustav Kirchhoff’s contribution to the development of modern science and technology.” 5 cultural and educational events were also held

concerts in Kaliningrad and regional cities related to the life and scientific achievements of Gustav Kirchhoff. The final stage of the “Kirchhoff Festival” will be the release of the collection “According to Kirchhoff’s Laws,” which will be based on the reports of the Memorial Conference. The collection is intended for schoolchildren and students interested in physics. The Kirchhoff Festival could not have taken place in Kaliningrad if its performer, the Kaliningrad Innovation and Technology Center, had not received support from the Presidential Grants Fund, the Mathematics Department of the Russian Academy of Sciences and the Immanuel Kant Baltic Federal University.
