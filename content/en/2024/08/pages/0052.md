Task 5. Removal from ice

Two identical small heavy boxes rest on a horizontal surface of ice. Both boxes are located at the same distance $L = 0,8$ m from the straight edge of the ice field. A cleaner, armed with a long scraper (a flat, rectangular plate with handles), hung it from the first box and pressed the edge of the scraper against one of the edges of the box. To do this, he turned the scraper so that its edge made an angle $\alpha_1 = 10^\circ$ with the edge of the field. He then moved the scraper forward from rest with a constant rooting $a$ directed perpendicular to the edge of the field (Fig. 5). As a result, the box left the field in $t_1 = 2$ s. He again picked up the scraper progressively with the same root, but this time the edge of the scraper made an angle of $\alpha_2 = 45^\circ$ with the edge of the field.
