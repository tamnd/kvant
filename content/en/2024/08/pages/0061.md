1) Suppose that $f(x) = 0$ for some $x \neq 0$. Substitution into condition $y = x$ gives $f(x + f(x)) = f(x) + x$, or $f(x + 0) = x + 0$, $f(x) = x$, whence $0 = x$, i.e. $f(x) \neq 0$ at $x \neq 0$.
2) Let's assume that $f(0) \neq 0$. Substituting $x = -f(y)$ into condition, we get
$$\begin{cases} f(0) = y + f(x), \\ f(y + f(x)) = 0. \end{cases}$$
From here, substituting $y = f(0)$, we derive
$$\begin{cases} f(x) = 0, \\ f(y + f(x)) = 0. \end{cases}$$
3) In particular, in point 2) we see that $f$ exactly takes the value 0, but taking into account 1) we have $f(0) = 0$.
4) Once again, taking into account $f(0) = 0$, let’s look at the condition obtained in 2) after substituting $x = -f(y)$:
$$\begin{cases} f(0) = y + f(x), \\ f(y + f(x)) = 0. \end{cases}$$
We see that in any case $y + f(x) = 0$, opening $y = -f(x)$. The last equality is true for all $y$.
5) Let us introduce the function $h(y) = -f(y)$. In point 4) we deduced that $h(h(y)) = y$ for all $y$, i.e. $h - \text{инволюция}$. Below we will use this, for example, when moving $h(a) = b \Leftrightarrow h(b) = a$.
6) Let's rewrite the initial condition in terms of $h$, we get
$$\begin{cases} -h(x - h(y)) = -h(x) + y, \\ -h(-h(x) + y) = x - h(y). \end{cases}$$
Let us rewrite the first equality in the form $h(x) - y = h(x - h(y))$.
Let's rewrite the second equality in the form $h(-h(x) + y) = h(y) - x$, revealing since $h - \text{инволюция}$, it follows $-h(x) + y = h(h(y) - x)$, or $h(x) - y = -h(h(y) - x)$.
We have
$$\begin{cases} h(x) - y = h(x - h(y)), \\ h(x) - y = -h(h(y) - x). \end{cases}$$
We replace the thing $y$ with $h(y)$ and vice versa, we get
$$\begin{cases} h(x) - h(y) = h(x - y), \\ h(x) - h(y) = -h(-(x - y)). \end{cases}$$
