3) At what volumetric flow rate μ will the hot water temperature be equal to $t_{\text{к}} = 100 \, \text{°C}$?
The specific temperature of water will be equal to $c = 4200 \, \text{Дж}/(\text{кг} \cdot \text{°C})$, the density of water will be $\rho = 1000 \, \text{кг}/\text{м}^3$. Assume that the heater power is constant; temperatures may vary.

Let $P$ be the power of the water heater, $\tau$ be the time during which the water is in the heater, then the amount of heat transferred by the heater to the water during this time is equal to

$$Q_1 = P \tau.$$

The total amount of heat received by water during the time it is in the heater is equal to

$$Q_2 = cm \left(t_{\text{г}} - t_{\text{н}}\right).$$

Let's write the heat balance equation:

$$P \tau = cm \left(t_{\text{г}} - t_{\text{н}}\right).$$

Since the mass of water is $m = \rho V = \rho \mu \tau$, where $V$ is the volume of the heater, then

$$P \tau = c \rho V \left(t_{\text{г}} - t_{\text{н}}\right),$$

$$P = c \rho \mu \left(t_{\text{г}} - t_{\text{н}}\right).$$

On the graph given in the conditions, you can select points with coordinates that are “well” determined:

$$t_{\text{г1}} = 45 \, \text{°C}, \mu_1 = 2 \, \text{л/min} = \frac{2}{60} \, \text{л/c}$$

and $t_{\text{г2}} = 20 \, \text{°C}, \mu_2 = 7 \, \text{л/min} = \frac{7}{60} \, \text{л/c}.$

Then

$$P = c \rho \mu_1 \left(t_{\text{г1}} - t_{\text{н}}\right),$$

$$P = c \rho \mu_2 \left(t_{\text{г2}} - t_{\text{н}}\right).$$

From here we get

$$t_{\text{н}} = \frac{\mu_2 t_{\text{г2}} - \mu_1 t_{\text{г1}}}{\mu_2 - \mu_1} = 10 \, \text{°C},$$

$$P = 4200 \cdot 1000 \cdot \frac{0,002}{60} \cdot (45 - 10) \text{Вт} = 4,9 \text{ кВт}.$$

The volumetric flow rate at which the water heating temperature will be equal to 100 °C is equal to

$$\mu = \frac{4900}{4200 \cdot 1000 \cdot 90} \text{м}^3/\text{c} =$$

$$= 1,3 \cdot 10^{-5} \text{м}^3/\text{c} = 0,78 \text{ л/min}.$$
