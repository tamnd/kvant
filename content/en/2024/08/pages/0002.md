This very easy to make puzzle contains three equal pentacles.
exact figures in yellow and five equal three-cell figures in green
colors. For those familiar with polyomino terms, we can say that we are talking about three
yellow U-pentominoes and five green I-triminos.

The player's goal is to form a figure of three yellow pentominoes on a plane.
so that it can be covered with five green triminoes. At the same time, everything
the squares of the bottom layer must strictly coincide with the squares of the top
layer and vice versa. In Russia, such puzzles are called “layer pies.”
