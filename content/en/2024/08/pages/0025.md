Let us use the segment BC, pp₁ - the plane in which the displaceable matrix of the camera (or film, if we are talking about a film camera) will be located. Let the horizontal distance AB = 5 m between the camera matrix and the object, the focal length of the object f = 50 mm, and the angle α = π/4 between the linear vision and the horizontal be known. The rotation angle of the main optical axis φ is selected so that all displacement points of the segment BC are depicted in the plane of the matrix of the pp₁ photo camera with maximum sharpness. Find the angle φ, considering it small.
Note. For a small angle β (|β| ≪ 1), the correct approximate relations are sin β ≈ β, tan β ≈ β, cos β ≈ 1.

In order for the image to be presented as sharply as possible, it is necessary that the straight lines on which the object BC lies, the segment symbolizing the film plane, and the lens intersect at one point. In this case, the image presented will lie entirely in the plane of the film. Let us denote by the letter G the point at which the described lines intersect (Fig. 2). The desired angle is indicated by another on figure, a and a₁ are the lengths of the perpendiculars dropped from points A and A₁ onto the lens. The lengths a and a₁ are related by the lens formula

$$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{a_1}.$$

Moreover, since the desired angle φ is small (cos φ ≈ 1), the following relation is valid: a ≈ AO and a₁ ≈ A₁O. This implies the equality

$$a + a_1 = AA_1 = \frac{L}{\cos \alpha} = L\sqrt{2},$$

in which the designation L = AB was introduced. Solving two equations as a system, we invert the solution, which does not contradict the physical meaning:

$$a = L\sqrt{2} \frac{1 - \sqrt{1 - \frac{4\sqrt{2}f}{2L}}}{2} \approx L\sqrt{2} \frac{4\sqrt{2}f}{4 \cdot 2L} = f.$$

When writing the last chain of equalities, we used the approximate formula $\sqrt{1-x} \approx 1 - \frac{x}{2}$, which is valid for small x (|x| ≪ 1). Since ∠A₁AB = π/4, the segments AA₁ and AG are equal, and AA₁ = AG = L√2.

Thus, the required angle is equal to

$$\varphi \approx \frac{a}{L\sqrt{2}} \approx \frac{f}{L\sqrt{2}} \approx 7,1 \cdot 10^{-3} \text{rad} \approx 0,4^\circ.$$
