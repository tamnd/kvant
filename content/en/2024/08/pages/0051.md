Problem 2. Powerful task
The electrical circuit, the diagram of which is shown in figure 2, consists of an ideal constant voltage source $U_0 = 12 \text{ В}$, a resistor, an ideal diode (the opening voltage of which is zero) and three devices, hereinafter referred to as “constant power sources”. Let us denote $I$ the strength of the electric current flowing through the device. The operating principle of a constant power source is as follows.

1. At $I = I_A = I_B$ the device operates as a source of constant current $I_B = I_A$, delivering power no more than the rated one.
2. The device passes current only in the direction indicated by the arrow next to its designation on the diagram. The current strength does not exceed $I_B$.
3. At $I \leq 1 \text{ мА}$ the device operates as a constant voltage source, delivering power no more than the rated one and creating a voltage equal to $P/I_H$, where $P$ is the rated power of the device, and $I_H = 1 \text{ мА}$.
4. At $I_H < I < I_B$ the power supplied by the device is constant and equal to the rated one.
5. The rated power of the device is understood as $P = IU_V$, where the voltmeter readings are $U_V > 0$ (Fig. 3). Pay attention to the polarity of the voltmeter connection!

The power ratings of each source are $P_1 = 1 \text{ Вт}, P_2 = 2 \text{ Вт}$ and $P_3 = 3 \text{ Вт}$. Find the values ​​of the currents flowing in all branches of the circuit and the resistance $R$ of the resistor. It is known that a current of force $I_R = 0,8 \text{ A}$ flows through a resistor.

A. Zhigar
