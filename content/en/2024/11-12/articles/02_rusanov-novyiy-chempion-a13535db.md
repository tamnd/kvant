CHAMPION

18-year-old Gukesh Dommaraju from India became the eighteenth world chess champion by defeating Din Lijken. The typical match left a nasty impression on chess fans - the current champion admitted in an interview that he was currently taking on delress and therefore could not properly prepare for the match. This led to the fact that in almost every game he found himself in a time crunch and often tried to avoid complications. The young challenger must have been very nervous and several times missed the chance to punish his opponent for inaccuracies. In the end, the fate of the title was decided by an incredible blunder in the decisive game.
