M2819. 10 children have several bags of candy. Children begin to share candy among themselves. Everyone takes turns taking their share from each bag and leaving. The share is calculated as follows: divide the current number of candies in each bag by the number of remaining children (including yourself), if not divided completely, round down to the nearest whole number. Can everyone get a different amount of candy if: a) there are only 8 bags; b) there are only 9 bags? A. Glebov

M2821. A road on a plane is a straight line along which you can move only in one direction. a) Petya and Vasya take turns leading the roads, Petya begins. Only horizontal or vertical roads are allowed; The direction on the road is chosen by the one who draws the road (when creating this road). Can Vasya always act in such a way that after any of his moves it is possible to travel according to the rules from any crossroads to any other, no matter how Petya acts? b) Let there be several (finite set) roads on the plane, any two of which intersect. For what is the smallest m it is always possible to add m roads and make it so that you can drive from any intersection to any other? A.Perepechko

M2823. A parabola $p$ is drawn on the coordinate plane - the graph of the equation $y = -x^2$ and the point $A$, which does not lie on the parabola $p$, is offset. Through the point $A$ all possible parabolas $q$ of the form $y = x^2 + ax + b$ are drawn, intersecting $p$ at two points $X$ and $Y$. Prove that all possible lines $XY$ pass through a fixed point of the plane.
P. Kozhevnikov

M2824. There are 15 boys and 15 girls in the class. The first girl is friends with the 4th boys, the second with the 5th, the third with the 6th, ..., the 11th with the 14th, and each of the other four girls is friends with all the boys. It turned out that there are exactly $3 \cdot 2^{25}$ ways to divide the whole class into pairs, so that in each pair there are boys and girls who are friends. Prove that any of the other first girls is friends with all the other girls too.
G. Sharafemdinova

M2825. Along the heights of an acute-angled unequal triangle, three beetles simultaneously began crawling from its vertices at equal speeds. At some points it turned out that the first and second beetles were on a circle inscribed in the triangle. Prove that at this moment the third beetle is also on this circle.
A. Kuznetsov

F2825. A stone is thrown at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. The starting point is at a height $H$ above the horizontal plane. In flight, the altitude at which the stone is located increases, reaches a maximum, and then kills to zero. At the moment the stone falls onto the plate, the velocity vector of the stone forms an angle $\beta$ with the horizon. Find the horizontal displacement of the stone during its flight. Air resistance can be neglected.
Yu. Pavlenko

F2826. A wedge is located on a smooth horizontal plane. The smooth inclined plane of the wedge forms an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizon. The puck is held on the smooth inclined plane of the wedge and then released. The system begins to move, the initial speeds of the washer and wedge are zero-higher. Wedge weight $M = 0,6$ kg, washer weight $m = 0,3$ kg. Gravity acceleration $g = 10 \text{ m/c}^2$. The wedge and washer move progressively and in a straight line. Determine the model of the force exerted by the horizontal plane on the wedge.

V. Plis

F2827. A homogeneous flexible rope, straightened to its entire length $L = 0,8$ m, moves translationally at a speed $v_0 = 1 \text{ m/c}$, directed along the rope, along a smooth horizontal table, and then slides off the table through a tube with smooth inner walls (Fig. 1) fixed on the edge of the guide table. At what point in time $T$ will the entire rope assume a vertical position? Gravity acceleration $g = 10 \text{ m/c}^2$. At time $t = 0$ the right end of the string is added just before turning in the tube.

V. Plis

F2828. The cyclic process carried out with a monatomic ideal gas is represented on figure 2 in coordinates $(p, \rho)$. In process 1-2, the gas pressure changes according to the law $p = a + \frac{b}{\rho}$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants, $\rho$ is the gas density. The maximum internal energy of the gas during the process is $U_{\max} = 4986$ J. Find the work done by the gas per cycle.

V. Plis

F2829. In the first experiment, an ampere and a voltmeter connected in series were connected to a battery, the readings of the device were $I_1 = 0,3 \text{ A}, U_1 = 18 \text{ B}$. In the second experiment, the same devices, connected in parallel, were connected to the same battery, the device readings became $I_2 = 3,4 \text{ A}, U_2 = 4,5 \text{ B}$. Find the short circuit current of battery $I_0 = \frac{\varepsilon}{r}$, where $\varepsilon - \exists \Delta C$ batteries, $r$ is the internal resistance of the battery.

V. Plis

F2830. Charge Q is distributed uniformly over the area of ​​a non-conducting sphere of radius R. In the first experiment, a small ball with charge q was placed at a distance 2R from the center of the sphere. The force with which the field of the charged sphere acted on the ball was equal to $F_{1}$. In the second experiment, the ball was removed, and the same charge q was evenly distributed along the length of a thin non-conducting rod of length R. This rod was placed on a straight line passing through the center of the charged sphere. The point of the rod closest to the center of the sphere was at a distance of 2R from the center of the sphere. Find the force $F_{2}$ with which the field of the sphere acted on the charged rod. All experiments were carried out in a vacuum far from other bodies. Consider all forces except Coulomb forces to be negligible. V.Plis

F2831. The drive wheel of a bicycle dynamo is pressed against the side of the bicycle wheel tire. The diameter of this drive wheel is d. The armature of the dynamo, containing N turns each with an area S, rotates in a uniform magnetic field with induction B. At what speed v must a cyclist ride so that the light bulb in the bicycle headlight, designed for the effective value of the voltage U, glows with normal incandescence when the dynamo is operating? The resistances of the wires of the rotor and the light bulb (at the rated voltage across it) are the same. V.Plis

F2832. Three coherent monochromatic in-phase emitters are located in a vacuum far from other bodies at the vertices of an equilateral triangle with side a. The emitters operate at a frequency of Q 100 MHz. At what minimum length of the side of the triangle in the far zone on the continuation of any side of the triangle do the waves of three sources cancel each other? The speed of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum is c 3 $10^{8}$ m s. S. Varlamov, V. Plis
