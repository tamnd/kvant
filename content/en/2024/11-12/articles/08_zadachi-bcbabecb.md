FOR JUNIOR SCHOOL CHILDREN
Tasks

1. There are 10 people in the circle, each of them has an aquarium at home. It is known that everyone has no more than 6 fish in their aquarium, and no more than half of these people have at least three fish. What is the maximum number of fish that all members of this circle together can have?

3. Is there a natural number whose sum of digits in decimal notation is 100, and the sum of its digits in decimal notation is 110?
Olympics of Chile for juniors
2012

2. There are 12 different integers written in ascending order. The first three of them are odd, the last two are even, and the average of the odd numbers is next to the average of the even numbers. How many odd numbers are there in these 12?

4. Petya wrote down four different two-digit numbers on the board. Prove that Vasya can take some of them and, using the signs of four arithmetic operations and brackets, create an expression whose value is greater than 1 but less than 2.
K.Knop

Problems 1 and 2 were proposed at the Smart KANGARUO competition. Problems 3 and 4 were proposed at the Moscow Oral Olympiad.
