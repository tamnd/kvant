Unexpected decision
Nobel Committee

L. BELOPUKHOV

The 2024 NOBEL PRIZE IN PHYSICS is awarded “for fundamental discoveries and inventions that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks.”

This decision came as a complete surprise to physicists. After all, the terms “machine learning”, “artificial neural networks” as separate aspects of “artificial intelligence” belong not to physics, but to informatics. But there is no non-Belev nomination for informatics. And although non-Belev prizes in physics were sometimes awarded in cosmology, and for laser instruments, and for fiber optics, this is still close to physics. But informatika...

Moreover, information science, non-beleutical research and achievement have their own merits. The simplest of them is the Alan Thøring Prize. It is awarded by the Association for Computing Machinery, an international organization founded in 1966 under the auspices of several companies, including Google, which has been a full sponsor of the organization's activities since 2014. The association consists of 35 sections, which include scientists from many countries around the world. There are separate sections on artificial intelligence and machine-human communication. The Thøring Prize is equal in amount to the non-Belev Prize and is considered to be not inferior to it in terms of representativeness. Over 58 years, it has been awarded to 81 scientists. And so, in 2024, the Nobel Committee for Physics was unable to single out work worthy of a Nobel prize among physical research. And he decided to award a prize to cybernetics scientists, but to those who used physical ideas in their research. Takimi turned out to be two scientists who published their seminal works in 1982 and 1986. A member of the Nobel Committee in Physics, Professor of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences Ellen Moons, who represented this year at the premii award meeting, emphasized in her speech on October 8 that the achievements of the new laureates elected by the committee are evidence of the deep intertwining of mathematics, computer science, physics and biology in modern science. And she noted that their work is the foundation of all modern artificial intelligence.

In the first weeks after the award of premii, Google conducted a representative survey of scientists in various specialties. About 30% of respondents support the decision of the Nobel Committee, 50% are against it, and 20% cannot make an assessment. It seems that not only these 20%, but also many of those who were “against” do not have a clear idea of ​​​​the development of library research at the end of the present century. Over the past 15 years, a huge number of people on all continents of the globe have become accustomed to using the gifts of artificial intelligence, without delving into the nature of this remarkable human achievement.
