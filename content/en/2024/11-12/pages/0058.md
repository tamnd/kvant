1. 18.
The sum of the angles of a $n$-gon is $180^\circ \cdot (n - 2)$. On the other hand, according to the formula for the sum of an arithmetic progression, it is equal to
$$\frac{n}{2} \left(2 \cdot 143^\circ + (n - 1) \cdot 2^\circ\right).$$ Equating these expressions, we get $180(n - 2) = n(n + 142)$, which is equivalent to the equation $(n - 18)(n - 20) = 0$, revealing $n = 18$ or $n = 20$. It remains to note that the value $n = 20$ is not suitable, since in this case the largest angle was equal to $181^\circ$, which is impossible for a convex polygon.
