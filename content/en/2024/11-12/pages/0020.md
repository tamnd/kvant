PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. M2818. The natural number M was represented as a product of prime factors. Then each of them was increased by 1, and the product became equal to N. It turned out that N is divisible by M. Prove that if we now factor N into prime factors and each of them is increased by 1, then the resulting product will be divisible by N. A. Gribalko

M2819. 10 children have several bags of candy. Children begin to share candy among themselves. Everyone takes turns taking their share from each bag and leaving. The share is calculated as follows: divide the current number of candies in each bag by the number of remaining children (including yourself), if not divided completely, round down to the nearest whole number. Can everyone get a different amount of candy if: a) there are only 8 bags; b) there are only 9 bags? A. Glebov

M2821. A road on a plane is a straight line along which you can move only in one direction. a) Petya and Vasya take turns leading the roads, Petya begins. Only horizontal or vertical roads are allowed; The direction on the road is chosen by the one who draws the road (when creating this road). Can Vasya always act in such a way that after any of his moves it is possible to travel according to the rules from any crossroads to any other, no matter how Petya acts? b) Let there be several (finite set) roads on the plane, any two of which intersect. For what is the smallest m it is always possible to add m roads and make it so that you can drive from any intersection to any other? A.Perepechko
