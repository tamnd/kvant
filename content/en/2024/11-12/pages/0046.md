It is known that if four points out of all 12 lie in the same plane, then this plane is α. How many convex pyramids are there with vertices at given points? (Pyramids are considered different if their sets of vertices are different.)

7 (6 points). Given a regular hexagonal pyramid SABCDEF (S - vertex) from the side of the base 2 and the side edge 4. Point X lies on the line SF, point Y on the line AD, and the segment XY is parallel to the plane SAB (or lies in it). Find the smallest possible length of the XY segment.

Physics
9th grade

1. Free aircraft are used to deliver payloads. The flight duration of the device along the route A → B in calm weather is $T_0 = 400$ s. The distance AB is $s = 9,6$ km.

1) Find the speed u of the aircraft in calm air.

Let us assume that during the entire flight the wind blows at a constant speed $v = 16$ m/s at an angle α to straight line AB (Fig. 1) such that sin α = 0.6.

2) Find the duration $T_1$ of the flight along route A → B in this case. The speed of the vehicle relative to the air is constant and equal to u.

3) At what value of α is the flight duration along the route A → B → A maximum? The movement of the device is rectilinear.

4) Find the maximum duration $T_{\max}$ of the flight along the route A → B → A. The vehicle moves in a straight line.

2. A schoolboy hits a ball lying on a horizontal platform. The absolute speeds of the ball after $t_1 = 1$ s and $t_2 = 2$ s after the start are the same. During this period of time, the velocity vector rotated through an angle $2\beta = 60^\circ$. Free pressure acceleration $g = 10$ m/s².

1) Find the duration $T$ of the flight from the start to the fall on the platform.

2) Find the maximum altitude $H$ and range $L$ of the flight.

3) Find the radius $R$ of curvature of the trajectory at time $t_1 = 1$ s.

3. A wedge with apex angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ is on a horizontal surface. A homogeneous ball (Fig.2) touching the vertical wall accumulates on the inclined plane of the wedge.

The masses of the ball and the wedge are the same and equal to $m = 1$ kg. There is no friction. Gravity acceleration $g = 10$ m/s².

1) Find the horizontal force F that holds the system at rest.

Force F is removed, the ball and wedge begin to move in a linear motion with zero initial speed. After moving vertically by H = 0.8 m, the ball absolutely elastically collides with the horizontal surface.

2) Find the displacement h of the ball after the collision until the first stop.

3) Find the acceleration a of the wedge during acceleration.

4) At what value of angle α is the acceleration of the wedge maximum?

5) Find the maximum acceleration $a_{\max}$ of the wedge.

4. On the scale of a soil thermometer, the distance between the marks $t_1 = 35^\circ$С and $t_2 = 42^\circ$С is equal to L = 5 cm. There is m = 2 g of distance in the thermometer. It has been experimentally established that with increasing temperature, the volume of rutin increases according to a linear law. A graph of the dependence of the volume V of rutin on temperature t, changed in degrees Celsius, is presented at figure 3. At a temperature $t_{100} = 100^\circ$ C, the volume of rutin is β = 1.018 times greater than the volume of rutin at $t_0 = 0^\circ$ C. The density of rutin at a temperature $t_0$ is considered equal to $\rho = 13,6$ g/cm³. The thermal distribution of the stack is negligible.

1) Following the experimental data presented, write down the formula for the dependence of the volume V of the routine on the temperature t, changed in degrees Celsius. The formula must contain the quantities m, р, β, $t_0$, $t_{100}$, t.

2) Find the increment ΔV of the volume of routine as the temperature increases from $t_1 = 35^\circ$ C to $t_2 = 42^\circ$ C. In your answer, give the formula and number in mm³.
