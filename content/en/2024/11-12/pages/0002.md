PUZZLE COLLECTION

UBIK WITH FILLING

The “CUBE WITH FILLING” puzzle consists of a cube frame
4×4×4 and fillings from six elements (see photo). All elements
except one, can be divided into five individual cups. One
element is composed of seven cups.

The task of the puzzle is to go through the windows
size 2x2 in the frame of a cube, fill it with six elements
so that you get a completely filled 4x4x4 cube. Let it go
should not remain inside the cube, otherwise some cubes
There's not enough room for the fillings!

The puzzle is difficult because by entering the elements you
you will constantly have to fight to save space, and what
the more elements are placed in the frame, the tighter it is inside and
it is more difficult to manipulate the puzzle elements.

The author of this puzzle is Gennady Yarkova - Russian champion
on solving puzzles. The puzzle has a useful
feature: its frame is not only an actual element
puzzle, but also a case for its stuffing.

We wish you good luck!

N. Avilov
