KVANT 2024/No. 11-12

There is a very high level of abstraction and a huge number of enumeration of all available options (according to some estimates, more than the number of atoms in the Universe). However, in 2017, the machine program defeated the Chinese player Ke Jie, who occupied the first position in the unofficial ranking of Go players. Why do computer scientists spend a lot of time developing artificial intelligence game algorithms? One reason is financial, since man-machine matches attract many spectators and sponsors. The other is psychological. The widely publicized facts of the victory of artificial intelligence over humans contribute to greater interest in its implementation among those with power and finance. In any case, for several years now the world has been experiencing a boom in interest in the implementation of deep neural networks; this is one of the synonyms of artificial intelligence. Another synonym, completely far from the essence of the matter, is “digitization”. But so far the main costs are being invested in the development of algorithms for human-computer communication. We are already accustomed to communicating with a computer (and especially a smartphone) as with a person, asking it questions, sometimes not even formulating them very clearly, talking to it (“Eliza” and “Alice”) and waiting for a reasonable answer. And we are not surprised when we see how, for example, at a car factory, it is not a person, but a robot standing at the assembly line. He carefully takes the part and carefully fits it into place. We are little surprised when we see people with disabilities returning to full life with the help of smart robots. And perhaps, in ten to twenty years, on city streets and highways, most cars will be “unmanned” and will politely give way to cars with a person behind the wheel. Developments in recent years have been named ChatGPT, or simply GPT-3, GPT-4, GPT5. “Chat” does not need translation, and “GPT” is Generative Pre-trained

Transformer (generative pre-trained transformer). It is these developments that are used in the latest models of smartphones, the number of copies of which already amounts to billions and brings huge profits to manufacturing companies and the developers themselves. But the rest of the positions on the use of artificial intelligence from the above list are under development in various research centers (in the first place are the centers of Google and YouTube). According to some estimates, just through the introduction of AI systems in industry and transport, global GDP should increase by more than 4% by 2030. In comparison with other countries, our country is in tenth place in terms of costs for the development of work on artificial intelligence. It is ahead of not only the USA and China, but also countries such as India and Canada. However, in recent years the situation has begun to change dramatically. In 2021, the “National Center for Artificial Intelligence” was created under the Government of the Russian Federation, which coordinates all work carried out in this area. A large grant (7 billion rubles) was distributed by this center among six leading scientific organizations. These are: i Skolkovo Institute of Science and Technology (Moscow); i Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology; i Institute of System Programming RAS (Moscow); i Innopolis University (Tatarstan); i Higher School of Economics (Moscow); i ITMO University (St. Petersburg). This year, 2024, the Ministry of Finance together with Sberbank launched a pilot project on the use of AI in the budget process (calculation of the state budget for 2025-27). There is a turning point (government assignments) regarding the implementation of already developed AI systems in individual production projects.
