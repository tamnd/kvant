Option 2
(grades 10-11)

1 (10, 11). When testing free aircraft, the models turned out to be the best. With a headwind of 4 m/s, the A-1 model stayed in the air 150 seconds less than the B-2 model, but flew 500 meters further. Which model will fly the longest distance in calm weather and by how much? The speed of each model is considered constant. The time a model stays in the air is determined only by its technical parameters and does not depend on weather conditions.

2 (10). On a paper regular triangle on side 45, 2023 red dots were marked. Is it possible to place on this triangle two regular triangles from side 1, which do not have common interior points, so that there is not a single red point inside each of them?

3 (10, 11). Find $f(2024)$ if $f(x) = |3x - 2| - |3x - 4| + 5$ for $x \in [0; 2]$ and, in addition, for all integer values ​​of $x$ the inequalities hold
$$f(x + 3) - 6 \leq f(x) \text{ и } f(x + 2) - 4 \geq f(x).$$

4 (11). Solve the equation
$$36\cos(x + \cos x)\cos(x - \cos x) + 9 = \pi^2$$
and find the sum of its roots taking the segment $\frac{\pi}{3}; \frac{7\pi}{4}$.

5 (10, 11). In an acute triangle, $PVG$ denoted the point of intersection of the heights through $H$, and the center of the circumscribed circle through $O$. The areas of triangles $OHP$ and $OHV$ are 15 and 8 respectively. Find the area of ​​triangle $OHG$.

6 (11). The curve given by the equation $y = x^2 + px + q$ intersects the $Ox$ axis of the direct Cartesian coordinate system at points $A$ and $B$, and the $Oy$ axis at $C$ (all points are different). It is known that the point $D$ is equilateral to the point $A, B$ and $C$, and the sum of its coordinates is $(-2023)$. Find the minimum possible length of the segment $AB$ under the given conditions.

7 (10, 11). Find all values ​​of $a$, for each of which the equation
$$|2[\text{tg}a] - 1|^x = [\text{tg}a]^2 + 2$$
has a rational solution. Here $[t]$ is the integer part of the number $t$.

Profile exam in physics

In 2024, a specialized exam - an additional entrance exam in physics at Moscow State University - was conducted in a remote format on the portal https://exam.distant.msu.ru/. To study in the exam, the following technical requirements were imposed on the candidate’s workplace. 1) Availability of a laptop (preferably), personal computer, smartphone or tablet with a stable Internet connection (without interruption during the test and with a minimum speed of 5 Mbit/s). 2) When connecting to a video conference using a smartphone or tablet of any type, to receive a task, it is necessary to use a second device, for example, a computer or another smartphone/tablet. 3) You must also have the latest versions of the Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox browser and a scanning or photographing device to scan or photograph the examination paper and then upload it to the examination portal.
