Olympida
“Conquer the Sparrow Hills!”
Matematika

The final stage of the Olympics
Option 1
(grades 5-9)

1 (5-9). At the flower market, Masha bought 156 chrysanthemums, 312 tulips and 390 roses. What is the maximum number of identical bouquets that Masha can make from these flowers so that all the flowers are completely used up? (Bouquets are considered the same if each of them contains the same number of flowers of each name.)

2 (5-6). Anya and Tanya agreed to go to the cinema to see the new Harry Potter film. Anya is going to arrive 10 minutes before the start of the session so that she can calmly take her seats. Tanya decided to come 5 minutes after the start of the show in order to skip the advertising that shows before the film. Anya thinks that her watch is 15 minutes fast, although in fact it is 10 minutes behind. Tanya thinks that her watch is 5 minutes slow, although it is actually 15 minutes fast. Which girl will come first and how long will her friend wait?

3 (5-8). When writing the date 04/20/24, the numbers “0”, “2” and “4” were used twice. When is the closest date after 04/20/24, for which three different digits are used, each exactly 2 times? We assume that .data is written in the format DD.MM.YY, and if the number is less than 10, then add “0” to it on the left, for example, 02/04/24.

4 (5-8). We will call a palindrome a natural number that reads the same from left to right and from right to left. For example, 11, 323 and 4224 are palindromes, but 2024 is not. Are there two palindromes, one two-digit and one three-digit, that add up to a four-digit palindrome?

5 (5-9). A tournament was held in the chess club - I played one game with each of them. It turned out that for any three participants, among the results of their mutual games there will be at least one draw and at least one game in which there is a winner. What is the maximum number of players who could participate in the tournament?

6 (5-9). The Mountain Dwarf Empire consists of seven kingdoms, in each of the kingdoms the gnomes add gold and diamonds. Is it true that you can always choose four kingdoms that produce at least 50% of gold and at least 50% of diamonds (of the total production in the empire)?

7 (7-9). In a convex 2024-gon, the lengths of all diagonals do not exceed 1. What is the maximum number of sides of length 1 that this 2024-gon can have? (Recall that a polygon is called convex if all its points lie on the same side of any straight line, passing through two of its neighboring vertices.)

8 (9). How many different square trinomials $f(x) = x^2 + px + q$ are there with integer coefficients $p, q$ for which two conditions are satisfied:

• $f(2023) = 2025^{24}$;
• the equation $f(x) = 0$ has an integer root.

9 (9). The robot painter painting the wall is a mechanism of three parts (see figure, all parts are located in the plane of the wall):

• rod $AB$ of length $l$ is perpendicular to the floor and can move from point $a$ to point $b$ (the distance between $a$ and $b$ is $2l$; straight line $ab$ is the boundary of the wall and the floor);
• rod $BC$ of length $2l$ rotates freely in the plane of the wall relative to the hinge $B$;
• the ink nozzle $K$ crawls along the boom $BC$.

How much wall area can this robot paint?
