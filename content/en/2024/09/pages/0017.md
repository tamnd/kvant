De Brain chains

Definition 2. We will call a partition of a zonogon into parallelograms regular if the parallelograms in it are adjacent to each other on the same sides.

In other words, in a regular partition, any two adjacent parallelograms of the partition (that is, those that have a common boundary segment) must have a common side. For example, the partitions shown in Figures 5 and 7 are regular, but the partition on figure 4 is not, since there are parallelograms whose side is part of the sides of two other parallelograms.

If the partition is irregular, then additional cuts can be made that turn it into a regular one (see the wide lines on figure 13).

Fig. 13

In this case, only a finite number of additional cuts will be needed, since the number of problem situations, when several other sides are adjacent to one side, will decrease each time.

In the proof of Proposition 2, we considered families of parallelograms connected by sides. This construction naturally leads to the concept of zone, from which zonogons get their name.

Definition 3. If P is a partial parallelogram of a partition and a is its side, then the union of all parallelograms that can be entered by starting from P and dividing through the sides parallel to a is called a zone and is denoted by Z(a).

In a regular partition, each zone is represented as a sequence of parallelograms connected by common sides, and is called a de Bruijn chain.

On figure 13 one can see how, upon transition to a regular partition, the zone Z(a) splits into a union of two chains (zones) Z(a₁) and Z(a₂).

De Bruijn used such chains of parallelograms as a tool to study quasicrystals ([6], [7]). The journal «Kvant» talked about quasicrystals and Penrose mosics in the article [8]. At figure 14³ you can see de Brain chains on a Penrose mosaic.

Theorem 1 (properties of the de Brain chain).

1°. Two chains connecting the same pair of parallel sides do not intersect.

2°. Two chains connecting different pairs of parallel sides intersect exactly one parallelogram.

3°. Let the de Bruijn chain connect the sides of the zonogon, located at a distance h from each other, and consist of parallelograms with a common base a. Then the total area of ​​all parallelograms of the chain is equal to ah.

Proof. 1°. Each parallelogram in a chain of the chosen direction uniquely determines the entire chain. Therefore, if two chains connecting a pair of parallel sides have a common parallelogram, then they coincide.
