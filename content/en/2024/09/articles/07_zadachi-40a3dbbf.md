FOR JUNIOR SCHOOL CHILDREN
Tasks
1. In expression
54321
one of the five numbers is cut and replaced with its opposite. Which of them needs to be replaced so that the result becomes the smallest possible?
3. The side of a square piece of bathroom wall is expressed as a whole number of centimeters. At least lay it out with tiles as much as possible. If you lay out tiles measuring 20x20 cm, then an area of ​​201 cm² will remain uncovered. How much will remain if you lay out tiles measuring 30x30 cm?
Based on the St. Petersburg Optimide 2011
2. Three sailors and a cabin boy were looking for several fish, all of which had different masses. Young managed to divide the fish into four piles of equal mass. The sailors did not agree with him, gave the cabin boy the smallest fish, and divided the rest among themselves into three piles of equal weight. What is the smallest possible number of fish found?
A. Shapovalov
4. Each face of a 6x6x6 cube was divided into 36 individual cells and its surface was covered in one layer with straight strips, each of which covers six cells. Could it turn out that at least one strip is bent through each edge of the cube?
T. Korchemkina
Problem 1 was proposed at the Smart KANGARU competition. Problems 2–4 were offered at the Moscow Oral Optimide.
