Let the numbers $1^0, 2^1, 3^2, \dots, k^{k-1}$ be the Datirovian $d$ of different remainders when divided by $p$. Let's denote it by $t = [p/4]$. Then expressions of the form $f(a) = (2a)^{2a-1}/\left(a^{a-1}\right)^2 = 2^{2a-1}a$ with $1 \leq a \leq t$ give a maximum of $d^2$ different residues.

Let us call a pair of natural numbers $(a,b)$ such that $1 \leq a < b \leq t$ exceptional if $f(a) \equiv f(b)$. Let us show that for each $\delta = 1,2,...,t-1$ there is at most one exceptional pair $(a,b)$ in which $b - a = \delta$. Indeed, if $(a,b)$ is such a pair, then from $2^{2a-1}a = 2^{2b-1}b$ it follows that $a/b \equiv 2^{2\delta}$, whence $b = a + \delta \equiv 2^{2\delta}b + \delta$, or $b\left(1 - 2^{2\delta}\right) \equiv \delta$. Such remainder $b$ is no more than unique (since $\delta \neq 0$), and from it $a$ is reconstructed.

In total, there are at most $t-1$ exceptional pairs; Let's denote their number by $S$. Let the numbers $f(1), f(2), \dots, f(t)$ be Datirovian $l$ of different remainders modulo $p$, occurring $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_l$ times respectively.
