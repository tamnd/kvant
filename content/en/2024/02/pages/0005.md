JULIY BORISOVICH KHARITON

Yuliy Borisovich Khariton (1904–1996) an article about Khariton appeared in connection with his eightieth birthday. This was an exceptional event - the article was signed by nine academicians, including the President of the USSR Academy of Sciences, and three Russian Nobel laureates. The article was the first to say that in 1943 I.V. Kurchatov attracted Khariton to participate in research “on the uranium problem.” This clearly meant work on the atomic bomb. The article also outlines Khariton’s personal qualities: “He is exceptionally modest and democratic, but this democracy does not contradict the firm, purposeful and successful management of work on a huge scale.” And further: “He is infinitely kind - always ready to help those in need. He is an interesting conversationalist, an excellent storyteller and an attentive listener. Communication with Yuliy Borisovich brings extraordinary joy, discussing physical problems with him, as well as issues related to art and literature,

And what a pity that only a few readers of the journal “Uspekhi Fizicheskikh Nauk” read this article. All other newspapers and magazines ignored the anniversary date, apparently still afraid of breaking the veil of secrecy. And only 20 years later, in another state, in the Russian Federation, in connection with the centenary of Khariton’s birth, an expanded re-edition of the book appeared, consisting of several dozen articles about him and his own articles and speeches. The title of this collection is “Julius Borisovich Khariton. A century-long journey." Here are just a few excerpts from articles about Khariton, from which you can get some idea of ​​the merits and appearance of this man. I.E. In a difficult area with an expensive experiment, there are almost no failures or breakdowns. Nobility and crystal moral purity."
