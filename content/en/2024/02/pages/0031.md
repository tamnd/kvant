COMPETITION NAMED AFTER A.P. SAVINA

We continue our next competition to solve mathematical problems. The tasks are designed mainly for students starting from grades 8–9, and we advise younger students to try their hand at the magazine competition «Kvant» (see website kvantik.com). Send solutions to problems you can solve to: savin.contest@gmail.com. In addition to your first and last name, indicate the city, school and class in which you study. We welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by teams (in this case, one work is sent with a list of participants). You can participate starting from any tour. The winners will receive diplomas from the magazine «Kvant» and prizes. Assignments, solutions and results are published on sites.google.com/view/savin-contest We wish you success!

21. Racer Petya is training on a circular track, the length of which is an integer number of kilometers. He drives 1 km, stands for a minute, rides another 2 km, stands for a minute, rides another 3 km, stands for a minute, and so on until the stop coincides with the starting point, and then he finishes the workout. a) Could it happen that Petya will not be able to complete the training? b) Vasya trains according to a similar scheme on a shorter circular track, the length of which is also an integer number of kilometers. Could it happen that the racers were driving at the same speed, but Petya took less time to train than Vasya? B.Frenkin

22. Indian schoolboy Raj shaded the central part of the multiplication table from 1 u 1 to 19 u 19 as shown in figure 1 and multiplied the numbers in the shaded cells. Quantik wrote down the numbers 1 and 19 on the board once, the numbers 2 and 18 3 times, the numbers 3 and 17 5 times, the numbers 4 and 16, 7 times,..., the numbers 9 and 11 17 times, and the number 10 he wrote out 19 times, after which he multiplied all the numbers on the board and squared the result. Who got the higher number - Raj or Quantik? N. Avilov
