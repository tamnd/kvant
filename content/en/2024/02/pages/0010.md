And Khariton organized a laboratory for the study of explosion and detonation at the Institute of Chemical Physics, newly created by N.N. Semenov. These studies correspond to the name of the new institute (and new science). Explosion and detonation, Khariton is deeply convinced, should be studied not only as chemical phenomena. The most important role in them should be played by the gas-dynamic laws of shock waves. To create a theory, it was first necessary to obtain experimental data on the parameters of explosion and detonation. And for this, special devices were needed - high pressure sensors, ultra-fast film equipment, etc. Over 10 years of experiments and their analysis, Khariton became the most prominent scientist in the field of explosion and detonation not only in our country, but also in world science. The most important thing in his work is the identification of the limits of detonation, critical mass and critical size. The outstanding 20-year-old theoretical physicist Yakov Borisovich Zeldovich, a future academician and three times Hero of Socialist Labor, and Kirill Ivanovich Shchelkin, also a future three times Hero of Socialist Labor and corresponding member of the Academy of Sciences, took an active part in the theoretical consideration of these issues. In 1935, based on the totality of works, without defending a dissertation, Yu.B. Khariton was awarded the academic degree of Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences. A characteristic feature of the management of the explosives laboratory, as it was officially called, was to ensure complete safety of work with explosives and the strictest implementation of the safety regulations developed by Khariton. During the years of his leadership and for many subsequent years, when the laboratory was led by his students, there was not a single accident in it.
