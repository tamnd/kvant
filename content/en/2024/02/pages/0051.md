XLV Tournament of Cities

OBJECTIVES OF THE AUTUMN TOUR
Basic option
8-9 grades

1 (4)1. On the asphalt there is a 1x10 strip for playing hopscotch. From the center of the first square, you need to make 9 buckles along the centers of the squares (sometimes forward, sometimes backward) so that you hit each square once and end the route in the last square. Anya and Varya both passed the strip, and each of Anya’s next buckles was the same distance as Varya’s next buckle. Do they have to pierce the squares in the same order?

A.Tolpew

2 (4). The quadrilateral ABCD is convex and its sides AB and CD are parallel. It is known that angles DAC and ABD are equal, and also angles CAB and DBC are equal. Is ABCD necessarily a square?

A. Termeryan

3 (5). Eight farmers have an 8x8 checkered field, fenced around the perimeter and completely overgrown with berries (berries grow at every point of the field, except for the fence points). The farmers divided the field among themselves along grid lines into 8 plots of equal area (each plot is a polygon), but did not mark out the boundaries. Each farmer only monitors the berries inside (not on the border) of his plot, and replaces lost ones only if he has lost at least two berries. The crow knows all this, but where the boundaries between the areas are, she does not know. Can a crow steal 9 berries from a field so that no farmer is guaranteed to notice the loss?

T. Kazitsna

4 (5). Several positive integers (at least two) are written around the circle.

1 The number of points awarded for its complete solution is indicated in parentheses after the problem number. The result is summed up for those tasks for which the best results have been achieved (scores for different points of one task are summed up).

Among any two adjacent numbers, one is greater than the other by 2 times or 5 times. Could the sum of all these numbers equal 2023?

S.Dvoryaninov

5 (5). Petya and Vasya found 100 cups of the same size, 50 of them were white and 50 were black. They came up with a game. Let's call one or more cups standing on top of each other a turret. At the beginning of the game, all cups are placed one at a time, i.e. there are 100 turrets. In one year, the player must place one of the turrets on top of another (the turrets cannot be turned over), and the new turret must not contain two cups of the same color in a row. They take turns walking, Petya begins. Whoever cannot make a move loses. Who can ensure victory, no matter how his opponent plays?

N. Chernyamiev

10-11 grades

1 (3). Baron Munchausen was told about the polynomial $P(x) = a_nx^n + \ldots + a_1x + a_0$ only that the polynomial $P(x) + P(-x)$ has exactly 45 different real roots. Baron, not even knowing what $n$ is equal to, claims that he can determine one of the coefficients $a_n$, ..., $a_1$, $a_0$ (he is ready to indicate its number and value). Is the Baron mistaken?

B.Frenkin

2 (4). There are three hands on the clock, each turning in the same direction as usual, with a constant non-zero, but possibly incorrect speed. In the morning, the long and short arrows coincided. Exactly 3 hours later, the long and middle arrows coincided. Still_ROV but after 4 hours the short and middle arrows coincided. Do all three arrows ever have to coincide?

A.Yuran

3 (4). We took all 100-digit natural numbers, in decimal notation of which each digit is one of the digits 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. How many of these numbers are divisible by 2100?

P. Kozhevnikov
