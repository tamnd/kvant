Linguistic tasks
Portuguese words

The Portuguese language is derived from Latin; thus, the main
great mass of his words (the so-called native Portuguese words)
is the result of gradual historical change
corresponding Latin words. In addition, Portuguese
borrowed words from different languages ​​in the development of periods of its history.
In this problem, modern Portuguese words are written on the left
column, and the words to which they go back (Latin and other languages),
- on the right.
All words listed in the left column are divided by origin
into three classes: native Portuguese, early borrowings
and later borrowings.

chegar - plicare
praino - plaine
plátano - platinum
chão - planum
plebe - plebem
cheio - plenum
prancha - planche

EXERCISE. For each Portuguese word, indicate which one
trex classes it belongs to.

NOTE. In Portuguese, the combination ch is pronounced sh.
