a) Prove the absence of preperiod.

20 (BO). Are the sequences periodic, respectively, consisting of the last digits of the integral parts of the powers of the root of two and the root of ten?

21*. a) Each number is assigned one digit to the right, except for 9. Prove that sooner or later you will get a composite number.

6) Find all polynomials $f$ with natural coefficients that have the property: $f(p) -$ is prime for every prime $p$.

22* (LO). In the sequence 101010..., the non-zero term starting with the seventh is equal to the last digit of the sum of the six previous ones. Prove that six 010101 cannot be found in any way.

23. Given a sequence of numbers $a_k$ with a period of 100. Moreover, the sum of the first $n$ of its terms is non-negative when $n$ is odd and non-positive when $n$ is even. Prove that $|a_{99}|$ is not less than $|a_{100}|$.

24. It is known that $a_1 + \dots + a_n = 0$ and for all $k$ $a_{n+k} = a_k$. Then there is $i$ such that any partial sum of this sequence, starting from the $i$th term, is nonnegative.

25. a) Prove that the sequence of remainders of powers of the number $q$ when divided by $k$ has a period less than $k$, and the sequence of remainders of numbers of the form $q^{l^n}$ ($q$ and $l$ are fixed) has a period less than $k-1$.

6*) A sequence of integers $a_n$ such that $a_{n+1} = m^{a_n}$. Prove that the sequence of remainders of the numbers $a_n$ when divided by $k$, starting from some $n$, becomes constant.

26. A sequence $a_n$, all terms of which are equal to 0 or 1, such that if $k \leq 2^n$ then $a_k$ is not equal to $a(k+2^n)$. Prove that it is non-periodic.

27 (MMO, 1980). The sequence of natural numbers $a_n$ is such that $a_{n+1}$ is not greater than $a_n$. Prove that the infinite decimal fraction $0, a_1a_2\dots$, obtained by adding these numbers to each other, is non-periodic.

28. The sequence $a_1,a_2,\dots$ consists of natural numbers less than 1988. Moreover, for any $n$ and $m$ the numbers $a_n + a_m$ are divisible by $a_{n+m}$. Prove that this sequence is periodic.

29* (MMO). Prove that there are an infinite number of $m$ such that $2^n$ ends up on $m$.

30. Given a sequence $x_n$ of real numbers and a natural number $n$. Prove that if among the ordered sets of $n$ elements $(x_{k+1}, x_{k+2}, \dots, x_{k+n})$ there are at most $n$ different, then the sequence $x_n$ is periodic.

Periodicity in words

31 (A.Kanel). The text is written in Russian on an endless tape in both directions. It is known that in this text the number of different pieces of 15 characters is equal to the number of different pieces of 16 characters. Prove that the tape contains “periodic” text, for example:

...mom washed the frame, washed the frame...
(and if the tape were limited to the left, then the preperiod could be observed).

6*) If only pieces that occur an infinite number of times have this property, then the tape is periodic, with the possible exception of a finite piece. For example:

...amammrrrr...

32 (BO). In the infinite tenth expansion of the number $q$, all digits occur. Let $V(n) -$ be the number of different digital segments of length $n$ appearing in this decomposition. Prove that if for some $n$ the inequality $V(n) < n+9$ is true, then the number $q$ is rational.

33. a) A letter is a Russian letter, and a word is any finite sequence of letters. Prove that if $pq = qp$, then there is a word $s$ such that the words $p$ and $q$ are its powers.

6) Solve the equation in words: $\omega u = v\omega$.

34. Prove that every word of length $n^2$ either contains the $n$th degree of not the beginning of another, or contains the $n$th degree of some word.
