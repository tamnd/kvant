PROBLEM "QUANTUM"
Tasks
in mathematics and physics
We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are taken over by the star. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.
Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant».
Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses.

M2785*. On the given plane there is a finite set S of n > 2 points in general position (i.e., no three points lie on the same line). The number of non-self-intersecting closed n-link broken lines with vertices at these points will be denoted by f(S). Prove that
a) f(S) > 0 for any S;
b) f(S) = 1 if and only if all points of S lie on the convex hull of the set S;

c) if f(S) > 1, then f(S) ≥ n - 1; this value is achieved when inside the convex hull the set S lies exactly one of its points;
d) if inside a convex hull the set S is exactly two of its points, then
f(S) ≥ (n - 2)(n - 3)/2.

Let n > 2. We will denote by F(n) the most possible value of the function f(S) over the entire set S of n points of the surveyERO position.
e) Prove that F(n) ≥ 3·2^{n-8}/3.
E. Bakaev, D. Magzhanov

The rope is moved a little and it begins to slide off the crossbar. With what magnitude of force F does the rope act on the crossbar at the moment when the length of the rope L on one side is three times greater than on the other? What angle α with the vertical is formed by the eta if the ratio of the diameter D of the crossbar to the length L of the rope is equal to 10^{-2}?
