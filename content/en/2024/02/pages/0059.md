6) We will denote a number consisting of $n$ units by $R_n$ (such numbers are called relumimi). There are equalities

$$R_n = \underbrace{111\ldots11}_{n\text{ цифр}1} = \frac{1}{9} \cdot \underbrace{999\ldots99}_{n\text{ цифр}9} = \frac{10^n - 1}{9}.$$

Consider the number

$$b_n = \underbrace{333\ldots33}_{n\text{ цифр}3} 4 = 3R_{n+1} + 1 = \frac{10^{n+1} + 2}{3}.$$

Let's square it:

$$(b_n)^2 = \left(\frac{10^{n+1} + 2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{10^{2n+2} + 4 \cdot 10^{n+1} + 4}{9} = \frac{(10^{2n+2} - 1) + 4(10^{n+1} - 1) + 9}{9} = R_{2n+2} + 4R_{n+1} + R_1.$$

We got the sum of six numbers, each of which consists of only ones. The total number of units is

$$(2n + 2) + 4(n + 1) + 1 = 6n + 7.$$

This number will be equal to 2023 at $n = 336$. Therefore, in a line of 2023 units, five planes can be placed so that the resulting number is a perfect square, namely:

$$R_{674} + R_{337} + R_{337} + R_{337} + R_1 = \left(b_{336}\right)^2.$$

13. Such a progression exists.

This is an arithmetic progression of 17 terms with the first term $(-17)$ and the difference being 17. The last term of the progression is 17·15. The sum of the progression can be found as the first and last terms multiplied by the number of terms:

$$\frac{-17 + 17 \cdot 15}{2} \cdot 17 = 7 \cdot 17 \cdot 17 = 2023.$$
