20th International Junior Science Olympiad

Schoolchildren whose age does not exceed 15 years take part in this Olympiad. The selection of candidates for the Russian team is made from among the participants in the regional stages of the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics or chemistry, who have scored more than 30% of the maximum points. In June, 30–35 of the most successful schoolchildren are invited to a training camp at MIPT, where training is carried out according to the Olympiad program and experimental skills are developed.
