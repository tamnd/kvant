KVANT 2024/№2

“a thousand suns” - this figurative expression from the ancient Indian religious epic called the explosion of an atomic bomb, the scientific director of the work on its creation, Robert Oppenheimer, who was present at this test. But despite the terrible impression of the power of nuclear weapons, their American creators, represented by their leader, a few days after the successful test, agreed to drop nuclear bombs on the Japanese cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. This happened on August 6 and 9, 1945. One of the bombs was uranium, the other was plutonium. The consequences were terrible - more than 150 thousand people died. The world was faced with the fact that the United States had a monopoly on a new weapon, unprecedented in power and unprecedented in its damaging factors. The Soviet government viewed these events as a threat to the very existence of the socialist state from the rest of the capitalist world. The only measure against possible state destruction was the creation of its own nuclear weapons and ensuring strategic balance in the possession of these weapons. In the USA, both the government and scientific circles believed that the USSR, weakened by the war, would need at least 15-20 years to go the way that American science and industry took four years. And during this time, the United States will have so many nuclear bombs that they will be enough to destroy the entire Soviet industry. It was about the very existence of our state. Clearly aware of this, Soviet leaders radically changed their attitude towards the nuclear project. They finally realized that this was not a purely scientific question, but a matter of paramount national importance, and to speed up the work it was necessary to mobilize all the forces of the country. And to coordinate all the work, a special government body with broad powers is needed. And such a body was created. On August 20, 1945, by decree of the State Defense Committee, a Special Committee was created

⟦column⟧

under the State Defense Committee, headed by Deputy Chairman of the Council of People's Commissars of the USSR L.P. Beria. The Special Committee was entrusted with the organization of all activities related to the use of atomic energy in the USSR - research work, exploration of deposits and mining of uranium in the USSR and beyond, the creation of the nuclear industry, nuclear power plants, the development and manufacture of atomic bombs. The last task was key; all other tasks were subordinated to it. The composition of the Special Committee was determined to be nine people, seven people's commissars or their deputies and two academicians - P.L. Kapitsa and I.V. Kurchatov. The same resolution creates a Technical Council under the Special Committee chaired by the People's Commissar of Agricultural Engineering B.L. Vannikov to prepare all issues. The Technical Council consists of seven academicians (including Kurchatov and Kapitsa) and one professor - Yu.B. Khariton. And another important decision was made - a working body was created: the First Main Directorate under the Council of People's Commissars of the USSR, the future atomic ministry, which would later be called the Ministry of Medium Engineering. In June 1953, the Ministry of Medium Engineering became the head of the Atomic Project. The Technical Council is transferred to this ministry. And soon an Engineering and Technical Council was created in it to solve practical factory problems. The nerve center of the Project is still the Technical Council. And as before, Yu.B. Khariton, who has already become an academician, as part of this council participates twice a month in discussing all the main problems on atomic weapons and atomic energy. But the main event in Khariton’s life was recorded in the decision of the Special Committee of March 16, 1946. Here is a line from this decision: “To appoint Professor Yuli Borisovich Khariton as the chief designer of KB-11 for the design and manufacture of atomic bombs.” (To be continued)
