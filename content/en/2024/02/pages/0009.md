First scientific supervisor, future Nobel laureate Nikolai Nikolaevich Semenov

Ziko-technical and engineering-physical education.

At the age of 21, Yu.B. Khariton became a research fellow at the Petrograd Institute of Physics and Technology.

Beginning of scientific activity

Khariton’s diploma work, like his work in a scientific laboratory, was related to physics and chemistry. But, graduating from college, Khariton was not only engaged in science. In 1925, the “Physics Problem” was published, created by him together with his friends A.F. Walter and V.N. Kondratiev. This problem book became the first Soviet visa problem book in physics and was used in all technical universities of the country for almost 30 years. But its authors were still students when they created it.

This problem made Khariton’s name known to all physicists in the country. And the publication of his first scientific works brought his name to the international physical and chemical arena. A truly remarkable early sunrise! After only a year of work, he was recommended by A.F. Ioffe, P.L. Kapi-

Tsei and N.N. Semenov to undergo a scientific internship at the Cambridge Laboratory of E. Rutherford. There he is engaged in precise laboratory studies of the interaction of alpha particles with matter. But the laboratory as a whole is switching to research in the field of nuclear physics. And Khariton is not only aware of all the work being done in the laboratory, he takes part in discussions and discussions. However, his “nuclear” time has not yet come. In 1928, the 24-year-old scientist became a Doctor of Physiophy (physics) at the University of Cambridge, defending a thesis on his experimental work with alpha particles.

Khariton used his summer breaks from work to travel around England on a motorcycle and automatic, and to travel around Europe with its museums and ancient architecture. He becomes an avid and skilled photographer.

Returning to his homeland in 1928, Khariton remained in Berlin to witness physical assault marches inoculating Berelin residents with slogans about the chosenness of the German nation and anti-Semitic appeals. On the pages of newspapers there was a fight of physicalist demagogues with the slogans of socialists and communists. Familiar Berelin physicists and the intelligent circle of his materials assured that all this was just politicking and buffoonery, that no one would take the physicists seriously and they would soon be forgotten about them.

But history decided differently. After 4 years, Hitler became Reich Chancellor, and 10 years later Germany started a peaceful war. Khariton foresaw this turn of events. Returning to the Physico-Technical Institute, he decided to direct his scientific work towards strengthening the defense capability of his native country. This decision was another milestone that determined his life's destiny. The field of science that combined physics and chemistry in his solution was the science of explosions and detonation.
