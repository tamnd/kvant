FOR JUNIOR SCHOOL CHILDREN
Tasks
1. The magician has 10 cylinders, in one of which sits a rabbit. In one question, you can point to 1 or 2 cylinders and ask if a rabbit is sitting there (we will be answered “yes” or “no”).
Is it possible to be guaranteed to find a top hat with a rabbit in 5 questions?
O. Podlinsky
2. The wallet contained 100 rubles in 1 ruble coins. Each of the 50 people approached the wallet and either took a coin worth 1 ruble, or put a coin worth 2 or 5 rubles into the wallet. Could the wallet end up with exactly 201 rubles?
O. Podlinsky
3. Let / be the intersection point of the bisectors of triangle ABC. On the continuation A/beyond point / and on the continuation AB beyond point B, points N and M are chosen, respectively, so that BCNM is parallel
logram. Similarly, on the continuation C/ behind the point / and on the continuation CB behind the point B, points K and L are chosen, respectively, so that BAKL is a parallelograph. Prove that lines B/ and LM are perpendicular.
N. Agakhanov
4. The numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, ... are written on the board.
..., 99, 100 and 102. In one operation, you can select several numbers, the arithmetic mean of which is an integer, the power of these numbers, and together write their arithmetic mean on the board. In what number of operations can you leave only one number on the board?
N. Agakhanov
These problems were proposed at the municipal stage of the All-Russian Mathematics Olympiad in the Moscow region.
Hypostrations by D. Grishukova
