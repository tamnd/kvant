Prove that all avalini can be distributed among n airlines so that between any two cities it is possible to construct a route in which there would be no more than two flights from each airline.

M2783. The sum of the digits of a natural number is s. What is the most possible sum of digits: a) the square of this number; 6) the fourth power of this number, if s ≥ 4?

M2784. In triangle ABC, the bicsectrices AD and BE were shown, they intersected at point I. Then everything was erased, leaving only points D and E. Find the set of possible positions of point I.
M. Didin

M2785*. On the given plane there is a finite set S of n > 2 points in general position (i.e., no three points lie on the same line). The number of non-self-intersecting closed n-link broken lines with vertices at these points will be denoted by f(S). Prove that
a) f(S) > 0 for any S;
b) f(S) = 1 if and only if all points of S lie on the convex hull of the set S;

c) if f(S) > 1, then f(S) ≥ n - 1; this value is achieved when inside the convex hull the set S lies exactly one of its points;
d) if inside a convex hull the set S is exactly two of its points, then
f(S) ≥ (n - 2)(n - 3)/2.

Let n > 2. We will denote by F(n) the most possible value of the function f(S) over the entire set S of n points of the surveyERO position.
e) Prove that F(n) ≥ 3·2^{n-8}/3.
E. Bakaev, D. Magzhanov

F2789. A long, smooth, uniform and flexible rope with a mass M = 3.2 kg is thrown over a thin crossbar with a circular cross-section and is in equilibrium. The rope is moved a little and it begins to slide off the crossbar. With what magnitude of force F does the rope act on the crossbar at the moment when the length of the rope L on one side is three times greater than on the other? What angle α with the vertical is formed by the eta if the ratio of the diameter D of the crossbar to the length L of the rope is equal to 10^{-2}?
S. Varlamov, V. Plis

The walls of the recess are smooth, and during the recess, by any plane perpendicular to both the wall and the horizontal surface, the vertical y and horizontal x coordinates of the point of the recess section line by the vertical plane are connected by the dependences y = (x - a)²/(10a), where a is some constant.
