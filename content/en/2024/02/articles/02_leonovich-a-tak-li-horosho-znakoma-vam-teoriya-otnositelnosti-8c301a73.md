Is it so familiar to you?

RELATIVITY THEORY

A quarter of a century ago, the journal “Physical World,” published by the British Society of Physicists, conducted a massive survey among scientists. According to him, Albert Einstein won the championship among the greatest physicists in history, and his theory of relativity was recognized as the most important of physical discoveries. The creation and formation of a new physical picture of the world contained in the theory is akin to an exciting detective story or an adventure novel. However, only a few, perhaps not the most exciting, pages of this enormous work ended up in school textbooks. Although, remember, you had to come across the term “relativity” even before you started studying physics. Let's try to build a narrow, but hopefully strong bridge from the primary ideas about this deep concept to its modern appearance.
