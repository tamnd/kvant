11th grade

1. Two planes simultaneously took off from the same airfield and flew along the equator - one to the east, the other to the west. One plane covered the entire Earth and returned to the airfield after 40 hours, and the other after 60 hours. How many hours after takeoff did the planes first meet each other if they were flying at constant speeds?

2. The pipe stands vertically, closed on top by a piston at a height $H$ from the bottom. Under the piston, in addition to the ideal gas, there is saturated water vapor and some water at the bottom of the pipe. Find the pressure of saturated water vapor if, with a slow rise of the piston by $H_1$, the pressure under the piston decreased by half relative to the initial one, and not all of the water evaporated. The temperature of the gas and steam is assumed to be constant, and the volume of water is neglected. The initial gas pressure under the piston was $p$.

3. A square frame was made from homogeneous wiring. The resulting frame was hung with a thread, attaching it to the middle of one of the sides (Fig. 11). Find the angle at which the suspended frame rest will surface if you use tin snips to remove a piece of wire length $b(b < a)$ in the middle of the side of the frame.

4. On two parallel conductive buses, the plane of which is inclined to the horizon at an angle of $\alpha$, and the distance between is $L_1$, a conductive jumper of mass $m$ with a resistance of $R$ lies perpendicular to the buses. Friction coefficient between bridge and tires $\mu(\mu > tg\alpha)$. Below this jumper, at a distance of $L_2$, another jumper is fixed parallel to the first, which connects the busbars. A magnetic field is turned on, which grows linearly with time according to the law $B(t) = B_0t/\tau$. The direction of the magnetic field changes along the horizontal plane and is directed upward. The free pressure acceleration is $g$. How long does it take for the jumper to start moving? Neglect the resistance of the remaining sections of the circuit, except for the movable jumper.

5. Estimate how many more revolutions the train's wheel will make in winter than in summer, on the way from Moscow to Vladivostok, if the coefficient of thermal expansion of steel is $\beta = 12 \cdot 10^{-6} \text{ K}^{-1}$. It is assumed that you have a good understanding of the phenomenon, can set the quantities necessary to solve the problem, select their numerical values ​​and obtain a numerical result.

6. Demonstration task (video is shown). On a horizontal table there is a cylinder, on the inner surface of which a weight is placed (Fig. 13). A stationary wooden block is placed on top of the cylinder. If the block is deflected, it shows along with the cylinder, but does not fall. If you replace the weight with a lighter one, then in a similar experiment the block inevitably falls onto the table if it needs to be deflected. Explain the observed phenomenon.
