1. Model B-2, at 100 m.
In calm weather, the difference between the flight range of the first and second models is $x = v_1t_1 - v_2t_2 = l - ut$. Here $v_1$ and $v_2$ are the speeds in calm weather of these models, and $t_1$ and $t_2$ are the time (in seconds) that the first and second models respectively stayed in the air, $u$ is the wind speed. Model A-1 stayed in the air $t$ seconds less than model B-2, model A-1 flew $l$ meters further.
For $u = 4$, $t = 150$ and $l = 500$ we get $x = 500 - 600 = -100 < 0$. This means that the B-2 model will fly 100 meters further.
