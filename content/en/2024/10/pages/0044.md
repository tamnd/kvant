What $\delta\theta$ vessels does the student need to take to conduct the research? Write down the numbers of the selected vessels.

21. The $pT$-diagram (Fig. 8) shows how the pressure and absolute temperature of a certain constant amount of monatomic rarefied gas changed during its transition from the initial state 1 to state 4. How the gas volume $V$ and its internal energy $U$ changed in each of the three sections 1-2, 2-3, 3-4 (increased, decreased or remained constant)? Explain your answer, indicating what physical phenomena and laws you used to explain.

22. A homogeneous wooden ball with a mass of $m = 1,6$ kg lies in a vessel with water, touching the bottom and not touching the walls of the vessel, so that half of the ball is in the water. Determine the density of the tree if the ball presses on the dosad with a force of $F = 6$ N. Do figure indicating the forces acting on the ball.

23. Along two horizontal parallel conducting rails with negligible resistance, connected to a capacitor with an electrical capacity of $C = 100$ uF, a conductive rod slides progressively and uniformly (Fig. 9, top view). The distance between the rails is $l = 1$ m.

The rails with the rod are in a vertical uniform magnetic field with induction $B = 1$ T. The energy of the electric field of the capacitor after a sufficiently large period of time from the beginning of movement is $W = 50$ μJ. What is the speed of the rod? The rails are fixed on a dielectric substrate.

24. The vessel is divided by a thin partition into two parts, the volume ratio of which is $\frac{V_2}{V_1} = 3$. The first and second parts of the vessel contain air with relative humidity $\varphi_1 = 60\%$ and $\varphi_2 = 70\%$, respectively. What will be the relative humidity of the air in the vessel if the partition is removed? Assume that the air temperature in part of the vessel is the same and does not change before and after removing the partitions.

25. The electrical circuit consists of a current source and a rheostat. Internal source resistance $r = 2$ Ohm. The resistance of the rheostat can be changed continuously within the range from 1 to 5 Ohms. The maximum thermal current power $P_{\max}$ released at the rheostat is 4.5 W. What is the emf of the source?

26. A dock with mass $M = 0,8$ kg (Fig. 10) lies on a horizontal stationary table.

On the board there is a small block with a mass of $m = 200$ g. The block and the dock are connected with a non-stretchable thread, thrown over a block fixed to the wall (the sections of the thread that do not lie on the block are horizontal). The friction coefficient between the block and the dock is $\mu_1 = 0,5$, between the table and the dock $\mu_2 = 0,3$. The board is pulled to the right horizontally by force $\vec{F}$. What is the model of this force, if the model of acceleration of the block relative to the table is $a = 1$ m/$c^2$? Neglect friction in the block axis. Make figure indicating the 丝 acting on the bodies. Justify the applicability of the laws used to solve the problem.
