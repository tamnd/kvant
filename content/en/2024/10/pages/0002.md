Linguistic tasks

WINGED LATIN

Latin catch phrases with their translations into Russian are given.
Some Latin phrases have gaps that correspond to
words in italics in Russian translations.

(1) OMNIA VINCIT AMOR, ET NOS CEDAMUS AMORI!
– Love conquers everything, let us too submit to love!
(2) VALE ET ME AMA! - Farewell and love me!
(3) VIVAT, CRESCAT, FLOREAT! - May he live, grow, prosper!
(4) DUM VIVIMUS, VIVAMUS! - As long as we live, we will live!
(5) FACILE OMNES, CUM VALEMUS, CONSILIA AEGROTIS DAMUS.
– When we are all healthy, we all easily give advice to the sick.
(6) DI TIBI DENT QUOD OPTAS! - May the gods give you what you want!
(7) AMENUS PATRIAM! – We will love reporting!
(8) CURA, UT VALEAS! – Take care of your health!
(9) QUANTUM PLACET. - As many as you like.
(10) NE DIFFICILIA OPTEMUS! – Let’s not wish for something difficult!
(11) PLACEAT DEIS! - May he be rich!
(12) DIVIDE ET <...>! – Divide and rule!
(13) DIVIDE, UT IMPERES! – Divide to rule!
(14) VALEANT CIVES MEI! - May my agreement be healthy!
(15) EDIMUS, UT VIVAMUS, NON <...>. “We eat to live, but we don’t live to eat.”
(16) VIVAT ACADEMIA, VIVANT PROFESSORES!
– Long live the academy, long live the professors!
(17) BIS <...>, QUI CITO <...>. - He who gives quickly gives doubly.

TASK 1. Fill in the blanks.
TASK 2. Give a more literal translation of sentences 2,8, 9, 13 and 16.
TASK 3. Translate into Latin:

1) You are thriving.
2) He submits.
3) We are growing.
4) May you win!
5) They rule.
6) They sabotage to keep us healthy.

(From the book “Tasks of Linguistic Description”)
