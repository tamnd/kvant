6. Several methods of calculation are possible, leading to an explanation of the observed phenomenon. Let's look at two ways: one uses energy messages, and the other uses moments of force. Feather way. For the block to fall, it must be disconnected from the vertical at a sufficiently large angle at which the friction force will not keep it from sliding off the wizz. If the cylinder were stationary, then such a large shutdown could not occur, since when the block rolls, the middle of its lower edge moves upward, which means that the center of mass of the block also shifts upward (provided that the block is quite thin compared to the size of the cylinder). Consequently, if the cylinder remained motionless, then the block would not have enough energy to raise the center of mass and would turn off at a much larger angle. As a result, the cylinder moves, rolling on the surface the cylinder shifts relative to its position, the weight rises up. If the weight is heavy, then lifting it up will require too much energy, so in the experiment with a heavy weight, the displacement of the cylinder is not large and the block does not fall. In the case of a light weight, the increase in potential energy when lifting it upward is insignificant. The energy to lift a light weight is obtained by lowering the center of mass of the block when the cylinder is rotated. Therefore, in an experiment with a light weight, the position of the entire structure turns out to be unstable and the block falls.
Second way. When the block is tilted, it creates a moment of force acting on the cylinder, which tends to lift the cylinder from its initial position. When the cylinder is displaced, the weight creates a moment of force acting on the cylinder and opposing its displacement. If the weight is heavy enough, the cylinder will hardly move. Meanwhile, for the block to fall, a displacement of the cylinder is required, so the block will not fall in the experiment with a heavy weight. If the weight is light, then the moment of force created by it is insignificant and cannot prevent the rolling of the cylinder due to the moment of force acting from the block. As a result, the cylinder rolls, the block deflects to a much larger angle and falls.
