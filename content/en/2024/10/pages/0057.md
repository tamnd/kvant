In section 2-3 the process is isobaric ($p = \text{const}, v = \text{const}$). Since the absolute temperature of the gas increases, the volume of the gas also increases. 3) The internal energy of the gas $U = \frac{3}{2}vRT$ at $v = \text{const}$ in sections 1-2 and 2-3 increased, as the absolute temperature of the gas increased, but in section 3-4 did not change (the absolute temperature in this section is constant).
