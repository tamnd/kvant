Grades 7-9 (selected problems)

1. Snow burner (15 points)

The thermos is filled with wet snow (i.e., a mixture of liquid water and ice crystals in equilibrium at normal atmospheric pressure). Boiling water is supplied there very slowly at a constant flow rate - equal volumes of boiling water enter the thermos over equal periods of time. At first, the temperature of the contents of the thermos did not change, but after a time of $\tau_1 = 24$ s after the start of boiling water supply, it began to rise, and at that moment the thermos was filled to 70% of its volume.

How long after this will the thermos be completely filled? What temperature will the contents of the thermos be at this point in time? Consider the following data accurate: density of water $\rho_0 = 1 \text{ г/cm}^3$, density of ice $\rho = 0,9 \text{ г/cm}^3$, specific heat of water $c = 4,2 \text{ Дж/(г·°C)}$, heat of fusion of ice $\lambda = 336 \text{ Дж/г}$.

2. Flight schedule (17 points)

A spring cannon mounted on the surface of the Moon fires a small stone at an angle to the horizon. The observation system recorded the light reflected from the pebble and, based on its frequency shift, changed the radial component of the pebble's velocity $v_r$ (i.e., the projection of the velocity vector onto its radius vector relative to the starting point). The graph (Fig. 5) shows the dependence of its ratio to the value of the initial speed of the pebble on the time that has passed since the moment of the shot. At time $t_0 \approx 5,657$ s the speed $v_r$ becomes zero.

At what angle to the horizontal was the shot fired? Find the initial speed. The acceleration of free fall on the Moon is considered equal to $g = 1,62 \text{ m/c}^2$. There is no atmosphere on the Moon.
