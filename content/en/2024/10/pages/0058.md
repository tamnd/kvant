24. 1) After removing the partitions, the total mass of water vapor remains the same:
$$\rho_1V_1 + \rho_2V_2 = \rho(V_1 + V_2),$$
where $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ are the density of water vapor in volumes $V_1$ and $V_2$, respectively, before removing the partitions, $\rho$ is the density of water vapor after installing the partition. Let us divide all parts of this equation by the density of saturated water vapor $\rho_H$ at air temperature in the state:
$$\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_H}V_1 + \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_H}V_2 = \frac{\rho}{\rho_H}(V_1 + V_2).$$
2) According to the definition of relative air humidity,
$$\varphi_1 = \frac{\rho_1}{\rho_H}, \varphi_2 = \frac{\rho_2}{\rho_H}, \varphi = \frac{\rho}{\rho_H},$$
so we have
$$\varphi_1V_1 + \varphi_2V_2 = \varphi(V_1 + V_2).$$
From here
$$\varphi = \frac{\varphi_1V_1 + \varphi_2V_2}{V_1 + V_2} = \frac{\varphi_1 + \varphi_2\frac{V_2}{V_1}}{1 + \frac{V_2}{V_1}} = \frac{0,6 + 0,7 \cdot 3}{1 + 3} = 0,675 = 67,5\%.$$
