7. $a \in [-\pi/4 + \pi n; \pi n) \cup [\arctg 5 + \pi n; \arctg 6 + \pi n)$, $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
Let's put $b = [\text{tg} a]$. Then the equation becomes $(|2b - 1|)^x = b^2 + 2$, $b \in \mathbb{Z}$, and we need to find the integer values ​​of $b$ for which a rational solution $x$ exists.
At $b = 0$ there are no solutions. Let us consider the case $b > 0$, i.e. $b \in \mathbb{N}$. Since for any natural $b$
$b^2 + 2 > 2b - 1 \geq 1$,
then we can assume that in the representation $x = d/q$ the numbers $d$ and $q$ are natural. This means that the numbers $b^2 + 2$ and $2b - 1$ have the same prime factors.
Let $p$ be the common prime divisor of these numbers, then
$$\begin{cases} b^2 + 2 = pN, \\ 2b - 1 = pM, \end{cases}$$
where $N$ and $M$ are naturals. Eliminating $b$ from the left-hand sides of the equation of this system, we obtain
$$9 = 4(b^2 + 2) - (2b - 1)(2b + 1) = (4N - (2b + 1)M)p.$$
This means that $(4N - (2b + 1)M)$ is a natural number, and $p$ is a divisor of 9, i.e. $p = 3$. Pozny
$$\begin{cases} b^2 + 2 = p^m, \\ 2b - 1 = p^k, \end{cases}$$
where $m$ and $k$ are natural and $m > k$.

Because
$$9 = 4(b^2 + 2) - (2b - 1)(2b + 1) = 4 \cdot 3^m - (3^k + 2) \cdot 3^k = 3^k (4 \cdot 3^{m-k} - 3^k - 2),$$
and $4 \cdot 3^{m-k} - 3^k - 2$ is not divisible by 3, then $k = 2$ and $m = 3$, $b = 5$, $x = \frac{3}{2}$.
For $b < 0$ the solution is carried out almost similarly. Let's put $c = -b$. Then the original equation will be written in the form
$(2c + 1)^x = c^2 + 2, c \in \mathbb{N}$.
The case $c = 1$ is very important since the solution is $x = 1$. For $c \in \mathbb{N}$, $c \geq 2$ there are no natural solutions (similar to the previous one).
Pozny, all solutions are described by equations $[\text{tg} a] = -1$ and $[\text{tg} a] = 5$, solving which we arrive at the answer.
