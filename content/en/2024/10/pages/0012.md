Thus, the process of transforming the broken line $a_1...a_n$ into the broken line $\tilde{a}_n...\tilde{a}_1$ can be understood as a sequential expansion of the permutation

$$\begin{pmatrix}

into a work of transpositions. This process can also be understood as sorting the balls numbered 1 through $n$ into the $n$ baskets. The order of vectors in the broken line going from $S$ to $N$ determines the order of placement of the balls: the number of the first link of the broken line is the number of the ball in the first basket, etc. Thus, first the ball with number $k$ lies in the basket with number $n-k+1$. At the end of each, the ball must end up in the basket with its number. At each sorting step, two balls lying in adjacent baskets can be swapped, which in geometric language means rearranging adjacent links of the polyline. Proof.
