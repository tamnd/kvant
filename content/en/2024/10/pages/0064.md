α is a non-actor constant coefficient. According to the Mendeleev-Clapeyron equation, $pV = vRT$, therefore in this process $p \sim V$. The work done by the gas can be found by calculating the area under the graph of $p$ versus $V$. The area of ​​the trapezoid is
$$\frac{1}{2}(p_1 + p_2)(V_2 - V_1) = \frac{1}{2}(p_1V_2 - p_1V_1 + p_2V_2 - p_2V_1).$$
Since $p \sim V$, then $p_1V_2 = p_2V_1$ and
$$A = \frac{1}{2}(p_2V_2 - p_1V_1) = \frac{1}{2}vR\Delta T.$$
The change in internal energy is $\Delta U = \frac{3}{2}vR\Delta T$. According to the first law of thermodynamics, $Q = \Delta U + A$. In this process
$$Q = \Delta U + \frac{\Delta U}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\Delta U,$$
where
$$\Delta U = \frac{3}{4}Q = 15\text{Дж.}$$
