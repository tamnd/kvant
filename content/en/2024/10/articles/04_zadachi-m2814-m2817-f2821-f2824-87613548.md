M2814. Positive numbers a, b, c such that b + c = a². Find the meaning of the expression
$$\frac{\sqrt{a + \sqrt{b}} + \sqrt{a + \sqrt{c}}}{\sqrt{a - \sqrt{b}} + \sqrt{a - \sqrt{c}}.$$
Folklore

M2815. There is a set of 2n chips of n different colors, two chips of each color. The chips are placed randomly in a row. Prove that the probability of finding two adjacent chips of the same color in a row is greater than 1/2.
Folklore

M2816. Find out for which natural numbers m you can choose a natural number l such that the sum n + n² + n³ + ... + nⁿ will be divisible by m for any natural number n.
A. Skabelin

M2817*. Given fixed circles Ω and γ such that there is a hexagon ABCDEF inscribed in Ω and circumscribed around Ω. (Note that then, by Poncelet's theorem, there is an infinite family of such hexagons.) Prove that the value of $$\frac{S_{ABCDEF}}{AD + BE + CF}$$ does not depend on the choice of hexagon ABCDEF.
A. Zaslavsky, Tran Quang Hung (Vietnam)

F2821. The equal-arm two-link matrix is ​​held in a stationary position, shown in figure 1. At some point in time, the matrix release system begins to move. Determine the maximum tension value of the rods (after the transition process). Known are l – the length of one rod, m – the mass of the ball, g – the acceleration of free fall. Consider the rods weightless; the radius of the ball is small compared to the length of the rod. There is no friction in the weightless hinge connecting the rods.

F2822. The end of a straightened homogeneous chain lying on a horizontal surface with length L and linear density p is raised from the surface itself along a vertical line. Determine the minimum work to completely lift the chain (the purpose of use is a vertical position, its upper end is at level L) for two values ​​of the friction coefficient between the surface of the chain links and the surface of the plane μ₀ = 0 and μ = 1. What is the ratio of these works?

F2824. A thin-walled glass vessel with a hemispherical house of radius $R$ is partially filled with water (Fig. 2). The water flow rate is constant (this is how the hole is designed) and is equal to $\frac{dm}{dt} = -\mu$. The vessel is viewed from the side along its symmetry axis.
