IN SHYMKENT

Alexandra Goryachkina and Tan Zhongyi staged an exciting chase at the women's Grand Prix series tournament in Shymkent. The Russian chess player finished first thanks to an impressive series of 5 victories in a row, one of which was won over her main rival, who ended up trailing by half a point.
