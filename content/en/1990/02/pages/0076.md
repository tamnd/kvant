Once again about Pascal's law
1. Inserting a second tube into the barrel will not change anything.
2. The force of pressure on the attachment up to BC is equal to the weight of a water column in the shape of a cylinder with base BC and height h. Due to the shape of the vessel, the weight of this pillar is more than 25 N.
Therefore, under heavy weights, the attachment does not come off. When water is poured, the pressure forces from above and below are equal, and the bottom comes off under the influence of its own gravity.
3. The contradiction is resolved if we consider the mechanism of pressure transmission according to Pascal. By compressing the liquid with an external force, we increase its density, and elastic forces arise, transforming the previous compaction. They also cause pressure on the adjacent layer, which is transmitted through the liquid. The force of hydrostatic pressure is compensated by the weight of the overlying column of liquid and cannot be transmitted in a similar way.
