Tasks

1. A flock of birds sat on a tree - three birds on each branch, and one bird continued to fly around the tree. Then all the birds moved to the tree, four per branch, while one branch remained free. How many birds were there and how many branches?

2. In one of the New Year's telephone dachas, post A. Voznesensky read his poem, which stated that drivers consider lucky those car numbers in which the sum of the digits of the first half of the number is equal to the sum of the digits of the second half. Number 19-82 is lucky, since 1+9=8+2. And in one of the programs in the series “The Investigation is Conducted by Signs,” it was stated that according to driver’s belief, a number in which the sum of the numbers of the first and second half is equal to 100, for example 19-81, since 19 + 81 = 100, is lucky.

List all the numbers that are happy in the first and second sense at the same time.

3. The number BAOWAB is divisible by 101. What number is it?

4. From the sequence of all prime numbers: 2, 3, 5, 7,... two other sequences were constructed: 5=2+3, 8=3+5, 12=5+7, ... and 6=2·3, 15=3·5, 35=5·7,... In the first case, successive prime numbers are added, and in the second, they are variable. Is it possible for any member of the first sequence to be equal to some member of the second sequence?

5. Is there a spotted star with the following property: a circle can be described around any of its red quadrilaterals (see figure)?
