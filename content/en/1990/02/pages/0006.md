KINEMATICS
BASKETBALL THROWS

Fig. 1.

Candidate of Technical Sciences R. VINOKUR

This happened at the end of the century in the American city of Springfield in Massachusetts. The local college students loved to play baseball and football. However, due to bad weather, sports activities were often moved to the technical hall. To keep his pets from getting bored, physical education teacher James Neusmidt in 1891 came up with a game that did not require a large area, and called it “basketball” - from the English words “basket” - basket and “ball” - ball. This name very succinctly conveys the essence of the game, since no matter how often the rules of basketball were adjusted over its subsequent history, the main element of the game always remained throwing the ball into a basket: first into a natural one (from under peaches), and then into a metal hoop with a net suspended from it without a day.
