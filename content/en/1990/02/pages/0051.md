He stood and waited, counting the lights and sounds to himself. Five hundred and one, fifty two, fifty three... There was a soft dissonant chime, as if the computer was reprimanding a child for a mistake. Jered took a deep breath and put the game down.
- How long have you been here... looking? - the boy asked, turning on the stove.
“A few minutes,” Dan answered, feeling guilty, as if he had violated someone’s secret prayer. Jered looked up at him, and Dan was amazed at the wisdom and generosity that shone in his son's gaze. There was nothing of his child in those eyes, but the creature that looked at him somehow silently reassured Dan, assured him that everything was going as it should.
- Do you know what time it is? - Dan finally asked.
“I don’t want to sleep much now,” Jered said detachedly. “In fact, I need to sleep very little.” I feel completely rested. Do sounds bother you?
