The total implus of the system of bodies in this SO is equal to zero at any moment.
Let us use the SO associated with the center of mass to solve the following problem.

Problem 4. Two small bodies are moving along the main horizontal plane, connected by a weightless and inextricable thread of length l. At some instant of time, a body of mass $m_1$ is at rest, and a body of mass $m_2$ has a speed v directed perpendicular to the thread (Fig. 4, a). At this point, tighten the thread.

The center of mass of this system of bodies is located on the thread at a distance $R_1 = m_2 l / (m_1 + m_2)$ from the first body and moves relative to the plane with a speed $v_\pi = m_2 v / (m_1 + m_2)$. Let us choose the SO in which the center of mass is at rest. In it, bodies $m_1$ and $m_2$ perform uniform motion in a circle around a fixed center of mass (Fig. 4, b), while the speed of the first body relative to this S.O. is equal in magnitude to $v_\pi$.

According to Newton's second law, the tension force of the thread acting on the first body is equal to

$$F = m_1 v_\pi^2 / R_1.$$

From where, substituting the expressions for $R_1$ and $v_\pi$, we finally find

$$F = \frac{m_1 m_2 v^2}{(m_1 + m_2) l}.$$

In many cases, when moving to a S.O. associated with the center of mass, the solution to the present problem is simplified, which makes sense to first transfer all the given problems into this S.O. and get the result, and then return it to the original S.O. As an example, consider two problems about an absolutely elastic impact of balls.

Problem 5. Two balls with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$, moving at speeds $v_1$ and $v_2$, respectively, experienced an absolutely elastic LOB impact. What will be the speed of the balls after the impact?

In the SO associated with the center of mass, the total implus of the balls is zero both before and after the impact. It is easy to prove that both conservation laws - momentum and energy - will be correct if you simply change the directions of the balls' velocities to the opposite ones. Let's write down the corresponding formulas:

the initial velocities of the balls at the center of mass are equal to

$$u_1 = v_1 - v_\pi = v_1 - \frac{m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{m_2 (v_1 - v_\pi)}{m_1 + m_2}, u_2 = \frac{m_1 (v_2 - v_\pi)}{m_1 + m_2};$$

final velocities of the balls at the S.O. center of mass -

$$u'_1 = -u_1, u'_2 = -u_2;$$

the final velocities of the balls relative to the ground are

$$v'_1 = u'_1 + v_\pi = -\frac{m_2 (v_1 - v_\pi)}{m_1 + m_2} + \frac{m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{(m_1 - m_2) v_1 + 2 m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2}, v'_2 = u'_2 + v_\pi = \frac{(m_2 - m_1) v_2 + 2 m_1 v_1}{m_1 + m_2}.$$
