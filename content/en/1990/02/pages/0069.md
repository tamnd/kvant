Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. Solve the equation
$$4|\cos x| + 3 = 4\sin^2 x.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{2-x^2} + 2x + x - 2}{\log_3\left(\frac{5}{2} - x\right) + \log_32} \leq 0.$$

3. The sides $KN$ and $LM$ of the trapezoid $KLMN$ are parallel, with $KN=3$ and the angle $M$ equal to $120^\circ$. Techniques $LM$ and $MN$ are tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $KLN$. Find the area of ​​triangle $KLN$.

4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_x y + \log_y x = \frac{5}{2}, \\ 4\sqrt{x} - 3\sqrt{y} = 1. \end{cases}$$

5. The segment $PQ$ is parallel to the plane in which the rectangle $KLMN$ lies, and $KL=1$, $PQ=3$. All sides of the rectangle $KLMN$ and segments $KP$, $LP$, $NQ$, $MQ$, $PQ$ touch some ball. Find the volume of this ball.

6. Find the smallest of the values ​​$x$ for which there exist numbers $y$, $z$ satisfying the equation
$$x^2 + 2y^2 + z^2 + xy - xz - yz = 1.$$

Option 2
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

1. Is it possible to place an equilateral triangle with sides 3 inside a circle of radius $\sqrt{10}$?

2. Solve the equation
$$8\sqrt{12 + 16x - 16x^2} + 4x - 4x^2 = 33.$$

3. A pedestrian walked from point $A$ to point $B$. Following him, 2 hours later, the auxiliary specialist left from point $A$, and after another 30 minutes - motorikLIST. The pedestrian, motorist and motorist LIST moved evenly and without stopping. Some time after leaving motorikLIST, it turned out that all three had covered the same part of the path from $A$ to $B$. How many minutes ahead of the pedestrian code at point $B$ will the pedestrian code be closer to the value of the pedestrian code at point $B$ 1 hour later motorikLIST?

4. Solve the inequality
$$1 \leq |\cos x| \sqrt{2x - 3} \cdot \log_{|\cos x|}\left(\frac{1 + 2\sqrt{3}|\sin x|}{8(1 - 2\cos^2 x)}\right).$$

5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the equation
$$\log_{\frac{1}{\pi}}\left(\frac{a^2 + 4\pi^2 + 4}{4x - x^2 - 2(a - 2\pi)|x - 2| + 4\pi a}\right) - \sqrt{(x - 5a + 10\pi - 34) \cdot (|\pi - x| - a + \pi + 2)} = 0$$
has at least one integer solution.

6. Assume $SABC$ the base $H$ of the height $SH$ lies on the median $CM$ of the base $ABC$. Point $O$, which is the midpoint of height $SH$, is at the same distance from point $S$, point $E$ lying on edge $SA$, and point $F$ lying on edge $SB$. It is known that $SH=8$, $AB=16\sqrt{2}$, $EF=8\sqrt{\frac{2}{5}}$, the angle $SMC$ is not greater than $30^\circ$, and the distance between the midpoints of the edges $AB$ and $SC$ is $4\sqrt{13}$. Find the radius of the sphere written out in $SABC$.

Option 3
(Physics Department)

1. Solve the equation
$$\sin 5x - \sin x = \sqrt{8} \cos 3x.$$

2. In an acute triangle of area $S$ the values ​​$a$ and $b$ of angles $A$ and $B$ are known. Find the length of the height dropped on the side adjacent to angles $A$ and $B$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(x + 4) + 2\log_2\sqrt{x} = 5.$$

4. In a regular quadrangular square, take the ratio of the lateral edge to the height of the prim to be 2. Find the ratio of the radius of the ball drawn in the prim to the apothem of the prim.

5. For what values ​​of $m$ the equation
$$(2x)^2 - 4x(m3^m)^{\frac{1}{2}} + 3^{m+1} + m - 3 = 0$$
has roots and what are the signs of the roots for different values ​​of $m$?

6. Two circles of radii $R$ and $r$ intersect at points $A$ and $B$ and touch a line at points $C$ and $D$, $N$ is the point of intersection of lines $AB$ and $CD$ ($B$ between $A$ and $N$). Find:
1) radius of the circle circumscribed about the triangle $ACD$,
2) the ratio of the heights of the triangles $NAC$ and $NAD$ dropped from the vertex $N$.

Option 4
(chemistry department)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{2x}{x+1} < 1.$$

2. The number sequence $b_1, b_2, b_3, \dots$ is a geometric progression. It is known that $b_1 \cdot b_3 \cdot b_11 = 8$. Find $b_2 \cdot b_8$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(3\cos x - \sin x) + \log_2(\sin x) = 0.$$
