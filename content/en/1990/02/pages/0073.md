New admission to the correspondence department of the Small Mechanical Engineering Department

The Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics - a mathematical school at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University - announces the admission of students graduating from the same school classes to the correspondence department. Admission to the Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics (MMMF) is based on the results of solving the problems of the introductory work published below.

The main task of the Small Faculty is to acquire mathematics, deepen knowledge within the framework of the school curriculum, as well as expand the mathematical horizons of secondary school students.

Primary correspondence classes in October. Education at the Small Mechanism is free. The duration of study is three years. Students of the correspondence department who have especially successfully completed all assignments receive certificates of completion of the MIMF.

The teachers at the correspondence department of MMMF are graduate students and students of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics. The development of thematic brochures is carried out by the methodological council of the MMMF, consisting of professors and teachers of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

Those wishing to enter the Malyi Mekhat must send solutions to the problems of the introductory work no later than May 1, 1990 to the MMMF (in this case, it is not necessary for all those who completed the 9th or 10th grades to be solved, recommending for admission to the physics-mathematics integrative school at Moscow State University. Students, successfully issued). The work must be completed in the school notebook in the classroom. On the cover of the notebook, paste a sheet of notebook paper with the following information:

1) resources, region, region;
2) surname, given name of the student;
3) school and class (full name);
4) full home address (including post office code);
5) last name, first name, absence of parents, place of work and position.

In your work, come out with a piece of paper $4 \times 6$ cm², on which write your full home address (indicating the post office code) and sew it to the address: 119899 Moscow, Moscow State University, Maly Mekhat.

Introductory work assignment for the Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics in 1990

1. During the festive fireworks display, 2 packets were launched with an interval of 6 minutes. T minutes after its start, the first packet is at an altitude of $h_1(t) = 100\sqrt{2}t$ meters, and the second is at an altitude of $h_2(t) = 100\sqrt{3}t$ meters. Which packet will be higher 5 minutes after the start of the second packet?

2. Find a point M inside the convex quadrilateral ABCD such that the sum $MA + MB + MC + MD$ is minimal.

3. Find all positive integers x and y satisfying the equation $xy - 3x - 5y = 0$.

4. Construct a triangle using a compass and a ruler without divisions, if the following elements are known, that is, depicted on a sheet of paper: a segment equal to the side of this triangle, a segment equal to the height lowered to this side, and an angle equal to the angle between this height and one of the remaining sides.

5. Let non-negative numbers x, y and z be related by m $x + y + z = 1$. Prove that $xy + yz + zx \leq \frac{1}{3}$.

6. The lengths of the sides of a right angle triangle are x, $2kx$ and $3k^2x$. Find all possible k.

7. There are 1990 boxes on the table in which there are balls: in one box there is one, in another there are two, in the third there are three, and so on until 1990. The following operation is allowed: all the balls come out of any boxes, say m and n, m ≥ n, after which m - n balls are placed in one of these two boxes, and 2(m - n) in the other. Is it possible, by repeating the operation enough times, to ensure that all the boxes are empty?

8. For 2 vacant seats on the detachment council there were 4 candidates: Petya, Kolya, Sergei and Tanya. The elections were secret: at the pioneer gathering bicolletes with 4 surnames were distributed and each pioneer crossed out 2 surnames. According to the voting results, Petya received the most votes - 8, and Kolya received the least - 5. How many pioneers were at the gathering?

9. 7 beaks ran out into the arena of the cirak, which has the shape of a circle with a radius of 7 meters. Is it necessary that the distance between any of them does not exceed 7 meters?

10. For what a do the equations $x^3 + 2ax^2 + 2x + 1 = 0$ and $x^4 + (2a + 1)x^3 + (2a + 2)x^2 + 4x + 3 = 0$ have a common root?
