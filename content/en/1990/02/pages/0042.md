spectra?

The science of color began with Newton's discovery of breaking white light into its component colors and putting them back together again. It was introduced and named - spectrum. The question of the origin and nature of spectra has not attracted the proper attention of researchers for many decades. Listening to the observation of spectral lines under a wide variety of conditions, they did not draw any conclusions. This was the case until, in the middle of the 19th century, G. Kirchhoff firmly connected spectral lines with the chemical composition of the emitting or absorbing substance and developed, together with R. Bunsen, a method of spectral analysis.

The post-study years brought a lot of discoveries, the flow of which does not dry up even a second day. Many cells of the periodic table of elements are filled in thanks to spectral analysis. Astronomy cannot do without spectrosocks, which with their help has accumulated a lot of knowledge about the Universe, about the physical properties of stars, their chemical composition and movement.

Studying the spectra of the road to the worlds of atoms and galaxies, and the tasks of the current issue of Kaleidoscope are just a few steps along this fascinating path.
