Option 9
(Faculty of Economics)

1. Find the domain of functions
$$y = \frac{\sqrt{25 - x^2}}{\sqrt{x^2 - 2x - 8}}.$$

$$\frac{\log_3 42}{\log_{126} 3} - \frac{\log_3 378}{\log_{14} 3}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$|x + 1| - |x - 3| = 6 = x.$$

4. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\text{tg}(3\sin x) = -1,$$
satisfying conditions $\frac{\pi}{2} < x < \frac{3}{2}\pi$.

5. The circle passes through vertices $A$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$, intersects side $AB$ at point $D$ and side $BC$ at point $E$. Find the values ​​of angle $ACB$ if $CE = 1$, $BE = CD = 4$, $AD = BD = 4:1$.

6. Solve equation for number purposes
$$9x^2y^2 + 9xy^2 + 6x^2y + x^2 + 2y^2 + 18xy + 5x + 7y + 6 = 0.$$

Option 10
(Faculty of Psychology)

1. Solve the inequality
$$x + 3 < \frac{1}{x + 1}.$$

2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\left| \cos^2 \frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right| = 3\cos x + 1.$$

3. Find all solutions to the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2\log_x 8 + 3y = 24, \\ -2(\log_x 0,5)^3 + y = 8. \end{cases}$$

4. In the quadrilateral $MNPQ$ there are two disjoint circles so that one of them touches the sides $MN$, $NP$ and $PQ$, and the other touches the sides $MN$, $MQ$ and $PQ$. The points $B$ and $A$ lie, respectively, on the sides $MN$ and $PQ$, and the segment $AB$ touches both circles. Find the length of side $MQ$ if $NP = b$ and the perimeter of the quadrilateral $BAQM$ are greater than the perimeter of the quadrilateral $ABNP$ by the amounts $2p$.

5. For each value of the parameter $a$, find all solutions to the inequality
$$x + 2a - \sqrt{3ax + 4a^2} > 0.$$

Option 11
(department of applied sociology of the faculty)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x^2 - 5x + 4}{5 - x} > 0.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_{\frac{1}{3}}(11 + x) = 2\log_{\frac{1}{3}}(\sqrt{x} + 1 + 2).$$

3. There were still 6 meters left before the approaching Achilles, when the turtle realized that it had to get away from the chase, and it doomedly left. How far has the turtle traveled since the beginning of the chase if its speed is 17 times less than the speed of Achilles, the distance between them during the chase has decreased by 9 times and their movement was in one straight line?

4. Find the smallest value of the function
$$y(x) = \frac{3}{4}|x| - x^3$$
on the segment $[-0, 7; +0, 7]$.

5. Find all pairs of values ​​of $(\alpha, \beta)$, for each of which the system of equation
$$\begin{cases} 8x + (\alpha^2 + \alpha\beta + \beta^2)y = 4, \\ (\alpha - \beta)x + 26y = 2 \end{cases}$$
has infinitely many solutions.

Option 12
(department of structural and applied linearity of the faculty)

1. Solve the equation
$$2\cos(2x - \frac{\pi}{3}) + 1 = \cos(x + \frac{\pi}{3}).$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{2x^2 + x} > 1 + 2x.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_{2x+2}(2x^2 - 8x + 6) = 2.$$

4. In a circle with center $O$, move $AB$ intersects radius $OC$ at point $D$, with angle $CDA$ equal to $\frac{2\pi}{3}$. Find the radius of the circle tangent to the segments $AD$, $DC$ and other segments $AC$, if $OC = 2$, $OD = \sqrt{3}$.

5. Find all values ​​of $a$ such that for each of them there is a unique triple of numbers $(x, y, z)$ satisfying the equalities $x + y + z = x^2 + 4y^2$ and $x + 2y + 3z = a$.
