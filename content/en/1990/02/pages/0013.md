Real and complex numbers

As is known, real numbers are naturally located on the number axis, that is, on a straight line on which the point $O$ is offset, the scale and positive direction (Fig. 1) are chosen. Complex numbers in the column are naturally located on the complex plane, i.e., on the plane on which the coordinate system $Oxy$ is given, with the axis $Ox$ being called real, and the axis $Oy$ - minima (Fig. 2). The point lying on the real axis $Ox$ corresponds to the real numbers taught to us. The point lying on the minimum axis $Oy$ corresponds to the minimum numbers subordinated to the special set $i$.
