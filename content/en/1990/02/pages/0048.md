Dan looked up from the newspaper and looked at his son. The boy sat cross-legged on the floor and played with the “Ziferom”. It's been two weeks now and the child still hasn't gotten tired of it. This has never happened with any toy. On the contrary, Jered threw himself into the game, becoming more and more captivated by the flashing lights and bizarre harmonious sounds. “Sometimes he even prefers playing to TV, which in itself is fantastic,” Dan thought.

“Let me try,” he said, putting down the newspaper.

Jered didn't seem to hear. He was all in the game, continuing to repeat combinations of lights. Every time he made a mistake, the computer made a sharp, dissonant sound and started all over again - with one light and one note.
