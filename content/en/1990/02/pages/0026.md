When pushing the platform, based on the information received from different grids, it is determined which vector (force) needs to be obtained, then this vector is laid out along fixed coordinate axes. With this method, it turns out that each of the two hind paws needs to be given only one number - the coordinate of the force vector (positive or negative) that this paw should create along its fixed axis.

It turns out to be a very economical scheme. But life is so full of surprises!
