M1206. In the circle, two redistributions of diameters $AE$ and $BF$ are made to each other. Point $C$ is taken on arc $EF$. Chords $CA$ and $CB$ intersect diameters $BF$ and $AE$ respectively at points $P$ and $Q$ (Fig. 1). Prove that the area of ​​the quadrilateral $APQB$ is equal to the square of the radius of the circle.

A. Kostenkov

M1207. Prove that for any $x$, $y$ and any natural number $m$ the following inequality holds:
$$\left(x^2 + y^2\right)^m \geq 2^m x^m y^m + \left(x^m - y^m\right)^2.$$
Sh. Racimov

M1208. The sequence of numbers $h_n$ is given by the conditions:
$$h_1 = \frac{1}{2} \text{ и } h_{n+1} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - \sqrt{1 - h_n^2}}{2}}$$
for each $n$. Prove that the sum of any number of numbers $h_n$ does not exceed 1.03.

D. Akulich

M1209*. A number triangle, the first row of which consists of $N$ units, the second - of $N-1$ integers, is processed according to the following rule: for any four numbers $a, b, c, d$ located at the vertices of the rhombus ($a$ and $c$ are adjacent numbers in the line, Fig. 2), the equality $ac = bd + 1$ is satisfied. Prove that a) if all numbers in a triangle are different from 0, then they are all integers;
b) if all the numbers in a triangle are natural, then there are at least $N/4$ different numbers in it.

D. Fomin

M1210*. There is a pile of $M$ matches and a piece of paper with the number $M$ written on it. Two people play this game. The move consists of the player taking from the pile or returning to the pile from 1 to $k$ matches and writing down on a sheet of paper how many matches are in the pile. (At first, all available matches are in a pile - the players have no matches.) The loser is the one who cannot make a move or is forced to write down a number already on the sheet earlier. Which player will win if played correctly if a) $k=2$; 6) $k=5$?

K. Kokhas

F1213. A homogeneous, non-stretchable rope is suspended by the ends at points $A$ and $B$, located at different heights (Fig. 3). The tension in the rope at point $A$ is $T_A$. Find the tension in the rope at point $B$ if it is $h$ above point $A$. Rope weight $m$, length $l$.

E. Tagarinova

F1215. 1 kg of ice and 1 kg of a low-melting substance that does not mix with water are placed in a heat-conducting vessel with heating inside. First, the temperature in the vessel was -40 °C, then a heated one consuming constant power was turned on. The dependence of the temperature in the vessel on time is shown in figure 4. The specific heat of ice is $c_\pi = 2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K), of a solid substance - $c = 10^3$ J/(kg K). Find the specific heat of fusion of the substance and its specific heat in the molten state.

A. Buzdin

F1216. A flat loaded capacitor was brought into the region of a uniform electric field, the direction of which is directed as shown in figure 5. For this it was necessary to perform work $A_1$. Then the capacitor turned through an angle $\alpha$, thus performing work $A_2$. Field with the given value $\alpha$, determine the work ratio $A_2/A_1$. Assume that the entire intrinsic field of the capacitor is uniform and concentrated inside its volume.

V. Mozhaev

F1217*. Recently, a new design of contact lenses has become widespread. (Contact lenses are used to correct vision along with glasses. They are very thin plates that are pressed directly onto the eyeball.) The focusing of light by such a lens is based on its wave nature. To do this, conceptual rings with alternating transparency (opaque - transparent - etc.) are applied around the central circular transparent area of ​​the lens.
a) Determine the diameter of the central clearing area.
b) Determine the diameters of two transparent rings close to the center of the lens, provided that the focal length of the lens is $F = 25$ cm.
c) Knowing that the contact lens gives a clear image in the focal plane not only of distant objects, but also of an exact object located at a finite distance from the lens, find this distance.
To be clear, assume that the length of the light wave is $\lambda = 500$ nm, that the lens is flat, very thin, and the rings are very narrow.
