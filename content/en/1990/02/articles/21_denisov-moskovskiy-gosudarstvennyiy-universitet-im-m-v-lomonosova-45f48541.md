Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. Solve the equation
$$4|\cos x| + 3 = 4\sin^2 x.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{2-x^2} + 2x + x - 2}{\log_3\left(\frac{5}{2} - x\right) + \log_32} \leq 0.$$

3. The sides $KN$ and $LM$ of the trapezoid $KLMN$ are parallel, with $KN=3$ and the angle $M$ equal to $120^\circ$. Techniques $LM$ and $MN$ are tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $KLN$. Find the area of ​​triangle $KLN$.

4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_x y + \log_y x = \frac{5}{2}, \\ 4\sqrt{x} - 3\sqrt{y} = 1. \end{cases}$$

5. The segment $PQ$ is parallel to the plane in which the rectangle $KLMN$ lies, and $KL=1$, $PQ=3$. All sides of the rectangle $KLMN$ and segments $KP$, $LP$, $NQ$, $MQ$, $PQ$ touch some ball. Find the volume of this ball.

6. Find the smallest of the values ​​$x$ for which there exist numbers $y$, $z$ satisfying the equation
$$x^2 + 2y^2 + z^2 + xy - xz - yz = 1.$$

The pedestrian, motorist and motorist LIST moved evenly and without stopping. Some time after leaving motorikLIST, it turned out that all three had covered the same part of the path from $A$ to $B$. How many minutes ahead of the pedestrian code at point $B$ will the pedestrian code be closer to the value of the pedestrian code at point $B$ 1 hour later motorikLIST?

$$1 \leq |\cos x| \sqrt{2x - 3} \cdot \log_{|\cos x|}\left(\frac{1 + 2\sqrt{3}|\sin x|}{8(1 - 2\cos^2 x)}\right).$$

$$\log_{\frac{1}{\pi}}\left(\frac{a^2 + 4\pi^2 + 4}{4x - x^2 - 2(a - 2\pi)|x - 2| + 4\pi a}\right) - \sqrt{(x - 5a + 10\pi - 34) \cdot (|\pi - x| - a + \pi + 2)} = 0$$

6. Assume $SABC$ the base $H$ of the height $SH$ lies on the median $CM$ of the base $ABC$. Point $O$, which is the midpoint of height $SH$, is at the same distance from point $S$, point $E$ lying on edge $SA$, and point $F$ lying on edge $SB$. It is known that $SH=8$, $AB=16\sqrt{2}$, $EF=8\sqrt{\frac{2}{5}}$, the angle $SMC$ is not greater than $30^\circ$, and the distance between the midpoints of the edges $AB$ and $SC$ is $4\sqrt{13}$. Find the radius of the sphere written out in $SABC$.

Option 3
(Physics Department)

$$\sin 5x - \sin x = \sqrt{8} \cos 3x.$$

2. In an acute triangle of area $S$ the values ​​$a$ and $b$ of angles $A$ and $B$ are known. Find the length of the height dropped on the side adjacent to angles $A$ and $B$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(x + 4) + 2\log_2\sqrt{x} = 5.$$

$$\log_2(3\cos x - \sin x) + \log_2(\sin x) = 0.$$
