Correspondence school style assignment

When the Correspondence Mathematical School, which later became the All-Union School, was created, the tradition of testing optimization and large-scale studies had already spanned decades. This tradition, of course, greatly helped in the creation of VZMSH, but at the same time, it could not be completely transferred to the distance learning environment. The Olympiads have always been aimed at identifying talented children who do not yet have a mathematical culture. A typical optimization problem is a nut to crack problem that requires communication, but does not present technical difficulties, the solution of which, if one has already figured it out, can be written briefly and simply. Here is a successful example of problem optimization: “Prove that any triangular paramid has an edge such that all four plane angles adjacent to it are acute.” A positive rating (“flat-s-minus”) can be given for just one phrase: “Let’s take the longest rib.”

A correspondence school is precisely a school whose goal is to develop a mathematical culture. It requires tasks that are “ugly” from the point of view of optimization, presented difficulties in writing down solutions that require calculations, enumeration of cases, and a little independent research. A VZMSH student who has to complete the next task must work systematically this month. The Correspondence School also requires tasks that are inappropriate for optimization or an exam.

Below we present several tasks typical for VZMS. Some of them have already been published in the VZMSh textbooks, others were invented in their likeness.

1. a) The following are known about two triangles $ABC$ and $A_1B_1C_1$: $\angle BAC = \angle B_1A_1C_1$, $AB = A_1B_1$, $BC = B_1C_1$. Does it follow that $\triangle ABC = \triangle A_1B_1C_1$?

6) Consider a construction problem: construct a triangle $ABC$ using angle $BAC = a$ and sides $AB = 1$ and $BC = a$. On the coordinate plane with coordinates $a$ and $a$, show the regions corresponding to each possible number of solutions to this problem.

2. Draw a set of points on the coordinate plane whose coordinates satisfy the correspondence $\frac{1}{x} > \frac{1}{y}$.

3. Is it possible to choose four different points on the plane that do not lie on the same line, so that all the distances between them are integers?

4. Find four numbers if you know that adding them in pairs, we get the following six sums:

1, 2, 5, 6, 9, 10.

5. Is there a natural number $k$ such that $5k$ is the fifth power of an integer, $6k$ is the sixth power of an integer, and $7k$ is the seventh power of an integer?

6. Is there a natural number $k$ such that $6k$ is the sixth power of an integer, and $8k$ is the subbinding of an integer?

7. Fresh cucumbers contained 99% water. During storage, the vegetable base underwent shrinkage, after which the cucumbers contained 98% water. By what percentage did the weight of cucumbers decrease?

8. The newspaper report said: “The sportsmen arrived in the minibus. Among them there are more than forty-two masters of sports, but less than forty-three percent.” Can this message be true if the minibus has ten passengers together?

9. The vertices of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ lie on the sides of the quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$. Can the area of ​​quadrilateral $ABCD$ be greater than the area of ​​quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$?

10. Is there a triangle in which the lengths of all sides, the lengths of all heights, and the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles are integers?

Ninth grade

11. The military column is 1 km long. The messenger left the beginning of the column, passed the package to the end of the column and returned to the beginning. During this time the column covered 1 km. What route did the messenger plan? (The column moves uniformly.)

12. The two medians of a right triangle are 3 and 4. Find the third median.

13. Is it possible to measure two closed 100-link broken lines on a plane such that each edge of the first broken line intersects every edge of the second broken line?

14. Solve the system of equations

$$\begin{cases} x^2 = y + z + 2, \\ y^2 = x + z + 2, \\ z^2 = x + y + 2. \end{cases}$$

15. The following three statements have been made about the triangle $ABC$:

a) triangle $ABC$ - right angular,
b) triangle $ABC$ - equilateral,
b) triangle $ABC$ has an angle at $45^\circ$.

Only two of these statements are known to be true. Find the angles of triangle $ABC$.

16. For all values ​​of the parameter $a$ find the number of solutions of the system

$$\begin{cases} |x| + |y| = 1, \\ x^2 + y^2 = a. \end{cases}$$

17. Is it possible to have a geometric progression in which the first ten terms are integers, but all the following terms are not integers?

18. Construct a triangle using three medians.

19. Construct a triangle using three altitudes.

20. On the coordinate plane, draw a set of points $(x; y)$ for which there is a triangle $a$ with sides $x, y, 1$; $6)$ with medians $x, y, 1$; $b)$ with heights $x, y, 1$.

Tenth grade

21. Draw function graphs

a) $y = \sqrt{1970 \cos 1970 x}$,
b) $y = \arcsin(\cos x)$,
b) $y = \cos(\arcsin x)$.

22. Which is greater: $2^{3100}$ or $2^{2150}$?

23. For all values ​​of the parameter $a$, find the number of solutions to equation $x^3 - x - a = 0$.

24. Solve the equation

$$\sin^2 x + 2 \sin x \cos x - \cos^2 x = \frac{\pi}{2}$$.

25. One paramid is inside a paramid. Can the lateral surface area inside the paramid be greater than the lateral surface area of ​​the outer paramid?

26. Given a tetrahedral angle. Construct a plane whose intersection with a given angle is a parallelogram.

27. Is there a polyhedron that has six faces, but is not a cube, and in which all duhran angles are equal to each other?

28. Is there a tetrahedral paramid $SABCD$ whose lateral faces $SAB$ and $SCD$ are perpendicular to the plane of the base?

29. Is there a paramid whose side face is perpendicular to the plane of the base?

30. Let us define inductively the sequence of functions:

$$\begin{cases} f_1(x) = |x|, \\ f_n(x) = |f_n(x) - 2|. \end{cases}$$

For any $n$ solve the equation:

$$f_n(x) = 1.$$
