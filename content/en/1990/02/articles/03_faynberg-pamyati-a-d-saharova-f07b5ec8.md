Memories
A. D. Sakharova

The unexpected death of Andrei Dmitrievich Sakharov stunned... And now it is still difficult to fully imagine the depth of the loss that the country, the world, every thinking person on Earth suffered...

When it was especially difficult, even a short meeting with A.D. again always gave me hope for a better future; his very existence and unbending personal courage in the struggle for truth, for justice, for the “little” man - were a support in difficult moments of life.

40 years of intermittent communication with such a person is an invaluable gift of fate. Its impact on many people is difficult to fully comprehend. I can only try to sketch the contours of the image of this extraordinary personality that continues to be formed in my mind from many impressions.

From the first meetings in 1949 at the Physics Institute of the USSR Academy of Sciences, I subconsciously realized that A.D. Sakharov was a specially constructed personality. Already at that time he enjoyed great authority among leading scientists involved in the atomic problem, although he was not yet 30 years old.

He expressed his thoughts with difficulty, slowly, but every phrase was deeply thought out. This quality made it difficult for him to teach; students, as he said, did not understand him well. A. D.’s ability to explain complex physical problems from general qualitative messages gave rise to.

A particularly strong impression was made in 1950 by the article-report of A.D. Sakharov and I.E. Tamm on controlled thermonuclear fusion. Igor Evgenievich told me in this regard: “We worked then as if we were wearing blinders. We spent all our time on the atomic problem. And then one evening A.D. comes to me and... expounds his idea that one can try to keep plasma in a closed horizontal volume using a magnetic field and, in principle, heat it up to the temperature of a thermonuclear reaction.” This is how a new direction in physics was born - the theory and development of magnetic thermonuclear reactors.

From 1950 to 1969, A.D. worked at the All-Union Scientific Research Physics Institute (VNIIEF), making an outstanding contribution to the development and creation of nuclear weapons.

But since the late 50s, he has become increasingly aware of the danger of the accumulation of nuclear weapons among the opposing superpowers - the USSR and the USA, as well as the growing environmental threat posed by the consumption of these weapons. He was the only expert scientist who, in 1961, decisively spoke out against testing a 60-megaton hydrogen bomb on Novaya Zemlya. They didn't listen to him...

It was then that for the first time he apparently realized that the threat of a nuclear conflict would increase immeasurably in the presence of uncontrollable power of an individual or a narrow group of individuals at the head of the state. This was the beginning of a new way of thinking.

A turning point in the life of A.D. Sakharov was the year 1968, when he, as a herald of a new era, in his 蓦randum “Reflections on proreses, peaceful coexistence and intellectual freedom” drew attention to the main problems facing man: environmental, nuclear and the problem of authoritarian power. The article circulated from hand to hand. It was not allowed to be published in the open press. After this, A.D. was forced to leave VNIIEF. He returned to FIAN as a senior researcher.

I will not describe all the vicissitudes and collisions of his social and scientific activities from 1969 to January 1980, when he was sent to Gorky without trial and deprived of all government awards. It was during these years that the main features of his personality fully manifested themselves - a consistent and uncompromising defender of dissent, a fighter for fundamental changes towards democratization and a complete restructuring of our entire political, economic and social system. He was truly the forerunner of perestroika in our country.

It is difficult to imagine the pressure he and those who supported him were under. It went to the management and employees of the Department of Theoretical Physics named after. I. E. Tamma FIAH, where he worked. But none of them signed statements discrediting A.D.

All these years A.D. Sakharov continued to study science. His work on proton instability, published back in 1967, was further developed. Currently, experiments are being conducted all over the world to detect this unique phenomenon. A. D. showed great interest in issues of cosmology and quantum gravity, where in the last decade a deep relationship was revealed between the processes occurring at the early stage of the development of the Universe and the quark-lepton structure of elementary particles.

In the period from 1979 to 1988, A. D. wrote eight articles, including works on the baryon asymmetry of the Universe (1979, 1988; on this issue, A. D. made a review report at the International Conference in Leningrad dedicated to the 100th anniversary of A. A. Friedman), “Cosmological models of the Universe with the turn of the arrow of time” (1980), “Multidimensional models of the Universe” (1982). These works testify to the strength of his spirit, dedication to science, and unflagging interest in the fundamental issues of modern physics.

Another point is about responsibility. After returning from exile on December 23, 1986, A.D. prepared a report for the international form “For a doorless world, for human survival.” I asked him when it would be possible to see this report. He replied that the preparation of the text was difficult: “We have to be over every phrase, otherwise it will cause damage to our country.”

Time puts everything in its place. A.D. believed in perestroika and accepted it. He said that he believed in M. S. Gorbachev’s sincere desire to dismantle our command system. But he was resolutely against further delays in the adoption of basic laws on land, on property, on power, and on the implementation of 6 articles of the Constitution. He was pleased with the changes and especially the appearance of progressive young and talented politicians and deputies in the political arena.

He was against any violence against the individual. His tolerance and mercy impressed those around him. He loved life very much and believed that humanity was becoming kinder.

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
