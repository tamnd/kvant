There are many, many puzzles that require you to interlock multiple cut-out blocks into one rigid shape, often filled with a three-dimensional cross. Such puzzles are called burras. One of them was described in «Kvant» No. 6 for 1981 - its front can be found on sale in the form of a star-shaped manifold. Another burr, called familiar to many puzzle lovers, is the “cross of Admiral Makarov.” We offer you several, fun “flat burr”: cut out the bars drawn here, cut them along the black lines and try to weave them into a flat image of a three-dimensional cross, shown in the right view.

D.K.
