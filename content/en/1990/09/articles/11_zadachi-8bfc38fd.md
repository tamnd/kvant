Tasks

1. The price of apples was increased by 20%. However, in order for the seller to change the price tag, it was enough to swap the numbers for the cost of a kilogram of apples. How much did apples cost before their price went up, if the price was less than rubles?

2. The teacher assigned an intricate problem in class. As a result, the number of boys who solved this problem turned out to be equal to the number of girls who did not solve it. Who is more in the class - those who solved the problem or the girls?

3. Solve an arithmetic puzzle. Same letters correspond to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers.

4. Two different cylinders have the same lateral surface area of ​​100 cm². Show that you can cut out a parallelogram with an area of ​​100 cm² from paper, which can be pasted over the side surface of both the first and second cylinders.

5. The school is located on the same street as my house. One day, while walking to school, I began to count on my side of the street the sum of the house numbers that I passed by. When the sum of the numbers became 99, he crossed the cross street. After that, I started counting again and counted the total 117. Then I crossed one cross street. And in the next quarter I counted the sum of house numbers. There it turned out to be equal to 235, including the number of the school house, which is the last one in this block. What house number do I live in? What is the school's house number?

These tasks were suggested to us by G. Shegolikhin, 11th grade student at school No. 149 in Kharkov Sergey Lyashenko, 10th grade student at Physics School No. 1 in Yerevan Vahan Abrahamyan, V. Proizvolov, V. Kot.
