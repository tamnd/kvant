In memory of Academician Kolmogorov

Andrei Nikolaevich Kolmogorov paid great attention not only to classroom presentations, but also to informal communication between the Teacher and students. Together with the ribs, he went on hikes, where he discussed and solved interesting mathematical problems with him, held musical evenings, and gave lectures on art history. His favorite child was Physics and Mathematics Boarding School No. 18, opened in 1963, bringing together children interested in physics and mathematics. And it is no coincidence that it is on the basis of this school (and above the Specialized Educational and Basic Center) that the All-Informed Scientific and Methodological Conference of teachers and students, dedicated to the memory of A. N. Kolmogorov, begins its work.

As the experience of a conference of scientists from various fields shows, lively communication, discussion of reports, and exchange of experience play a colossal role in the further development of scientific thought. The Young Mathematicians holidays also help school students decide on their future profession, as well as begin independent research work. For mathematics teachers, such meetings provide an opportunity to take a more creative and professional approach to developing students’ mathematical abilities.

In April 1991, teachers and students of special schools and special classes, leaders and members of scientific societies of students, various circles specializing in in-depth study of mathematics, computer science, physics, chemistry and biology were invited to the first All-Union conference dedicated to the memory of A. N. Kolmogorov. Two independent sections are organized for teachers and students, which in turn are divided according to the scientific profile and level of difficulty of discussing topics.

The purpose of the teachers’ section is to exchange methodological experience and results of work on developing the creative abilities of the conference in the field of mathematics and natural sciences, and on early involvement of students in independent scientific research.

At the student section, students will hear reports from schoolchildren containing the results of their independent scientific work.

The Specialized Educational and Basic Center has established a permanent activity whose task includes establishing contacts with educational institutions, distributing invitations, organizing and holding conferences, publishing interesting materials in the magazines «Kvant» and “Mathematics at School”, publishing conference topics.

Anyone wishing to take part in the conference must submit applications by December 31, 1990 to the following address:
121357, Moscow, st. Kremenchugskaya, 11, SUSC, conference organizing committee. O. M.
