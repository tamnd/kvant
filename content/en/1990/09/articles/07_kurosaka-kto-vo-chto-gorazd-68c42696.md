“That’s what interests me,” he continued. “Is there any practical benefit from studying abstract concepts?” What I need is something completely different: guidance on how to become a productive member of society.

I concluded that the student had escaped from the philosophy department, but in the meantime his low-chested head and confident materials had already captivated the audience. Other students were waiting for me to answer. I cleared my throat.

“Mr. Barone,” I began, “what do you want to take away from the university?”

“I don’t know yet, sir.” I thought two years of college would help me choose a profession, but they didn't help. You see, I don't have to earn my living.

He stated it as simply as you and I might say, “I have a toothache.”

“Where do you get your livelihood from, Mr. Barone?”

“Oh, so,” I said. “Obviously, with the Midas touch?”
