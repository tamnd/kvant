APPLICANT – 91

Correspondence courses with original teaching methods will help you become a student of one of the leading classes in the country.

Students and graduates of schools, technical schools and retired soldiers are invited.
Qualified teachers, using an individual approach and taking into account the specifics of the degree you have chosen, will do everything possible to ensure that your admission to the university becomes a reality.
During the academic year, you will receive 5 methodological developments for each subject, which contain theoretical material, analysis of questions and tasks offered in the preliminary exams, and tests.
For applicants to Moskovskie.by, consultations are provided during the entrance exams.
The cost of a training course in one subject is 50 rubles. For disabled people and international soldiers a 50% discount is provided. Payment is made upon receipt of notification of protection.
To enroll in courses, you must send an application indicating the subject chosen by you, your middle name, the educational institution where you study or graduated, and 2 envelopes with your postal address written on them.
