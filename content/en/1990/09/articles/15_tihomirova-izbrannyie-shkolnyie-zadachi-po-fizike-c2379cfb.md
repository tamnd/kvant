⟦folio 47⟧

Selected school problems in physics

9th grade

1. The train traveled for the first half of the journey at a speed 1.5 times faster than the second half. The average speed of movement along the entire path is $v$. What are the speeds of the train at each half of the journey?

3. Drops of water fall from the roof of a building $h=27$ m high at regular intervals, with the first hitting the ground at the moment when the fourth drop leaves the roof. What are the distances between the drops when the first drop hits the ground?

3. At figure 1 there is a graph of the acceleration of a body moving in a straight line. Construct graphs of speed, displacement and distance as a function of time. The initial speed of the body is zero.

4. The rocket is launched vertically upward. Find the time from the moment of launch to the moment when the package reaches a maximum altitude of $H=74$ km (at minimum fuel consumption), if the acceleration of the package while moving is constant and equal to $a=2g$. Neglect air resistance and changes in the magnitude of gravity acceleration $g$.

5. A ball falls freely onto an equal horizontal plate moving vertically upward with a speed $v$. The distance from the starting point of the fall to the point where the ball meets the plate is $h$. To what height from this place will the ball jump after colliding with the plate? The impact is absolutely elastic, i.e. the speed after the impact is the same as before it.
