2. A drop of liquid with a diameter of $D$ and a surface tension coefficient of $σ$ is located between two smooth parallel plates. The liquid wets the plates in such a way that the drop looks like a cylinder with right angles at the base.

3. A small charged bead is placed on a long non-conducting structure, threaded along the diameter of a metal ball through two small holes in it. The ball and the bead have charges of the same sign (the bead's charge is much smaller in magnitude). The bead was told the speed required to “fly” through the ball. Draw a graph of the acceleration of the bead versus the distance to the center of the ball.
