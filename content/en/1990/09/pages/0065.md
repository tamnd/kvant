In the general case, the inequalities $H \leq G \leq A \leq Q$ are valid, but they are much more difficult to prove. We will prove the inequalities, which refer to the average values ​​of two positive numbers, geometrically. However, the proof of inequality $\frac{a+b}{2} \geq \sqrt{ab}$ is based on the fact that the height of a right angled triangle is the geometric mean of the projections of the legs onto the hypotenuse (Fig. 1).

However, using the configuration figure 1, it is not clear how to compare the geometric mean with the harmonic mean, and the arithmetic mean with the square mean. At the same time, there is a figure in which all the averages of two numbers $a$ and $b$ can be seen “live” - this is the trapezoid $ABCD$ with the base $BC=a$, $AD=b$ (for definition we will assume that $b>a$). The arithmetic mean is the length of the midline of the trapezoid. You will see the rest of the averages by solving the following exercise; The first of them is a useful lemma that helps deal with the others.

At the wife's. Let $EF$ be a segment of length $x$ with ends respectively on the lateral sides $AB$ and $CD$ of the trapezoid $ABCD$, parallel to its base (Fig. 2).

1. Find $x$ if you know that $BE/EA=\lambda$. (Answer: $x=\frac{a+\lambda b}{1+\lambda}$). Note. See figure 2: $GH \parallel AB$, and triangles $FCG$ and $FDH$ are similar.

2. Find $x$ if the segment $EF$ passes through the intersection point of the diagonal trapezoid. (Answer: $x=\frac{2ab}{a+b}$). Note. Use Exercise 1 and the fact that $BE/ED=a/b$.

3. Find $x$ if the trapezoids $AEFD$ and $EBCF$ are similar. (Answer: $x=\sqrt{ab}$).

4. Find $x$ if the trapezoids $AEFD$ and $EBCF$ are equal. (Answer: $x=\sqrt{\frac{a^2+b^2}{2}}$). Note. Let the lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at the point $P$ (Fig. 3). Then $\frac{S_2}{S_1+S_2}=\frac{a^2}{x^2}; \frac{S_2+2S_1}{S_2+S_1}=\frac{b^2}{x^2}$. Adding these equalities, we get the answer.

So, in the trapezoid $ABCD$ all the average numbers we mentioned $a$ and $b$ appeared. It remains to prove inequalities (*).
