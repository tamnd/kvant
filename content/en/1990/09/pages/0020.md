The most common imaging method is the B-scan method. A single device is used for imaging and for receiving reflected waves, which can be moved over the patient’s body. To avoid large energy losses at the body-action interface, a gel is applied between the device and the skin. The device produces short ultrasonic pulses. The delay times and amplitude of the reflected wave are measured. The delay time is used to determine the distance to the reflecting boundaries, obtained by the M-scan method.
