Selected school problems in physics
1. According to the definition of average speed,
$$v = \frac{l}{t_1 + t_2} = \frac{l}{l/(2v_1) + l/(2v_2)} = \frac{1}{1/(3v_2) + 1/(2v_2)} = \frac{6}{5} v_2 \Rightarrow v_2 = 5,6v = 36 \text{ km/ч и } v_1 = 1,5v_2 = 54 \text{ km/ч.}$$
2. In uniformly accelerated motion without an initial speed, the paths traversed by the body in successive equal intervals of time are related as a series of odd numbers. In our case (see Fig. 1) $l_{34}:l_{23}:l_{12} = 1:3:5$. Taking into account the fact that $l_{12} + l_{23} + l_{34} = 27 \text{ м},$ we get $l_{12} = 15 \text{ м}, l_{23} = 9 \text{ м}, l_{34} = 3 \text{ м.}$

4. The maximum lifting height is gained at two stages: during uniformly accelerated movement without an initial speed with acceleration $a = 2g$ and during a “free fall” with an initial speed directed vertically upward:
$$H = \frac{at_1^2}{2} + \frac{v_1^2}{2g} = \frac{2gt_1^2}{2} + \frac{(2gt_1)^2}{2g} = 3gt_1^2.$$
From here
$$t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{H}{3g}}, t_2 = \frac{v_1}{g} = \frac{2gt_1}{g} = 2t_1,$$
$$t = t_1 + t_2 = 3t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{3H}{g}} \approx 150 \text{ с} = 2,5 \text{ мин.}$$
5. After an absolute elastic impact on a moving plate, the ball will receive a speed equal to $u = \sqrt{2gh} + 2v$ and directed vertically upward, and will rise to a height
$$H = u^2/(2g) = (\sqrt{2gh} + 2v)^2/(2g).$$

8. The lifting force is equal to the difference between the buoyant force and the gravity force:
$$F_\pi = F_B - F_T = \rho_2 V g - \rho_1 V g.$$
The density $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ of air will be found from the equation of gas state:
$$\rho_1 = \frac{m_1}{V} = \frac{p M}{RT_1}, \rho_2 = \frac{m_2}{V} = \frac{p M}{RT_2}.$$
Finally
$$F_\pi = \frac{pMVg}{R} \frac{T_1 - T_2}{T_1T_2} \approx 300 \text{ H}.$$
