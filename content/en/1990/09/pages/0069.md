Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences

flexanna", published in
«Kvant» No. 7 for 1988 (hereinafter cited as FFF). A few more will be added here. All of these toys are easy to make and they all actually move.

Three reversible rings

One of the most attractive designs in FFF is a ring of ten terrahedra. The ring has amazing flexibility and the ability to turn inside out and inside out ad infinitum («Kvant», 1988, No. 7, quadruple cover page). A similar ring can be glued together from washed-out carbon prisms. This is how this toy is made. Cut out four strips measuring 3x42 cm from a batman sheet or thin cardboard. Divide each into seven rectangles 3x6 cm and make gibbs along the dividing lines (Fig. 1). Glue the outer rectangles of the strip together (Fig. 2). Make small cuts in the places indicated by arrows. Pass a thread through them and tie it (Fig. 3). The result is a flexible element consisting of two prisms having a common edge.
