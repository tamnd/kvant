When this instinct dies, people lose the desire to live. And what stimulus of life can we offer our creation? Curiosity? The desire to be useful to the people who created his body (perishable and replaceable) and raised his personality, soul? The desire to develop yourself in exploring the world, in ultra-long-distance travel, in creating receiving and transmitting stations for travel, in building circumstellar space bases?

Are these incentives convincing? Where does he get affection and love for his loved ones? How to educate him so that he does not turn out to be a monster with absurd and senseless aspirations for power, to give instructions, to educate and to be a benefactor? or, on the contrary, so that he does not turn out to be an infantile, impoverished creature, indifferent to the world, to his loved ones and to himself?

And, of course, huge technical problems.

How do we think? How to create stereotypes of our reactions, behavior, assessments, how is our individuality created? There is a later fact that our algorithms for seeing the world around us, analyzing, thinking are created each time anew and almost always in a different way, and their character is determined by family, friends and enemies, school, the structure of society, the joys, sorrows and good fortune of our childhood. In a society of work, work grows; in a society of the free, independent people grow, etc.

From this point of view, it is very dangerous to standardize methods of education, nurseries, kindergartens, schools... This is the worst thing you can do for your future. Humanity is rich in diversity, diversity, individuality. Of course, some basics should be common: do not kill, do not steal, do not covet... But to prepare a person according to a standard (even the highest) is to prepare for his own death.

How can you start creating artificial intelligence without understanding all these things? And most importantly, the tragic mistakes and failures that await us on this road.

Even a healthy rational existence is in support and protection, in recognition, in assessing the meaningfulness of one’s life, one’s worth and full rights. Errors in development ideas, in manufacturing, in education, insufficient decency of the creators can lead to the cooperation of disabled creatures, mentally disabled and, most importantly, non-participants. After all, we, people, have it all: deformed and disabled people, expressed and then thrown into an indifferent world, domestic cats and dogs, abandoned children and old people...

But thought has already entered into the creation of the most curious, sociable and enterprising people. Please, the task of creating artificial intelligence has become the most popular task of our time. We have to think that this thing will work.

More understandable difficulties will appear.

To send a person in the form of a package of information on a galactic journey, it is necessary to create receiving and transmitting stations (for example, in the radio range), and to route (for example, using automatic spacecraft) these stations to the desired destination (not far from any star to provide the stations with energy). If a personality is transmitted over a radio channel over galactic distances, then it will be necessary to create antennas with a size of the order of kliometers, transmitters with a power of the order of $10^8$ kW. The speeds of automatic spacecraft that fly now are ten kilometers per second. Speeds of the order of hundreds and even thousands of cliometers per second seem to be achievable. But this means that the time of transportation throughout the galaxy is millions and even hundreds of millions of years for earthlings. Delivery of stations even to the closest stars, located ten light years away from us, will require tens of thousands. During this time, interest in the enterprise may be lost. Nevertheless, this path is within the limits of the possible.
