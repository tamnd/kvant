What about the work on creating the Energia rocket? For what? For launching into orbit spacecraft with a mass of about 100 tons. But they don’t exist! And there are no projects!
Or our latest “epoch-making” achievement - the “Energia-Buran” system. The work was carried out with great stress. Other space and neo-space work was closed and pushed back. Spent enormous amounts of money. For what? To get our “toy” and show that we, like Americans, know how to spend money right away? Buran can be returned from orbit by spacecraft. But there are no such devices that would require return from orbit and the cost of which would be greater than the cost of Buran launches. Delivering vehicles into orbit using Buran is ten times more expensive than using long-existing carriers. So why?
It must be said that the reproach for extremely unsuccessful selection of major goals applies not only to our officials, but also to American ones (Lunar program, Shuttle). In principle, officials are all the same - they don’t manage their own money.
Reflections on flight to the stars will allow us to highlight several interesting areas of work:
— research into the possibility of creating and developing artificial intelligence;
— design of space work with the subsequent transition to the creation of a space man, whose personality can be separated from the material carrier and transmitted in the form of a package of information at the speed of light;
— development of the ideology and design of “T-shirts”, methods of searching for them;
— development and creation of ever larger radio and optical telecopes, with radio antenna sizes on the order of hundreds and thousands of meters;
— search for “alien” output communication channels from Earth, more subtle (with higher resolution) photography and study of the surface of the Moon (especially the far side) and other planets of the Solar System in search of means of communication of other civilizations.
These areas of work correlate well with modern human needs.

Man's first message to distant worlds was installed on the Pioneer 10 spacecraft (1972). In a few million years it will end up where the star Aldebaran is now. Will our descendants become its recipients?

Work on artificial intelligence in connection with solving the problem of creating sufficiently effective robots that can replace people in dangerous industries, save them from hard physical labor, and the routine of uncreative work, would help us in liberating the water area and the underwater world. Creating space robots is a naming task. When working in outer space, they will be more effective than a person in a spacesuit. And work in outer space will most likely be developed in the coming decades: the construction of economically efficient factories in orbit, where the absence of gravity will be used in production processes, factories that cannot be left on Earth because of their economic harmfulness.
The creation of large radio telecopes makes it possible to conduct effective research both at the boundaries of the Universe and in the center of the Galaxy...
This analysis can and should be continued. Only a joint consideration of immediate and future tasks allows us to correctly select goals and accept development.
