Flexman and donkey

Flextman is another one of the FFF heroes. This funny man can walk on inclined planes. The process of its creation is depicted in figure 9. A square is cut out of thin paper with a side of 15-20 cm. An upward fold is made along the diagonals, and a wizz fold is made along the dashed line (Fig. 9, a). Then the square is folded into a triangle (figure 9, b). After this, four operations are performed. The result of the first of them is at figure 9, c. The final result is at 9, v. This is how the flexman gets legs. And four more operations - at the ends of each leg you should bend a small triangle. The Flextman is ready, and if you place it on an inclined plane, cut off its slope, then the Flextman, showing up and waddling, begins to descend along it in small steps.

And I returned to flexman for this reason. Recently I was lucky: I bought a great toy - a walking donkey (Fig. 10). This donkey also walks on an inclined plane, but it can also walk on horizontal surfaces. There is a weight attached to the donkey on a string. If you throw this weight over the edge of the table, then the donkey will walk along the table. The same weight can be attached to a flexman, and it will also move around the table. Flexman and the donkey on the first look at each other with creatures of completely different breeds. Consequently, the mechanics of their behavior are completely identical.
