In a healthy body, a computer works with fewer failures, at greater speed, with more diverse and effective algorithms, and provides greater internal sensitivity to external threats and troubles. And, most importantly, clarity of thinking. But if we talk about the main thing in relation to our Self with the outside world, then this is freedom.

All this was said here to remind: our I, our individuality, our essence is not a material shell. There is nothing criminal, contrary to our rise of the world, in the idea of ​​​​the possibility of separating individuality and its material carrier.

From an engineering point of view, it is possible to construct a world where the human soul can be separated from the body. In such a world, a person can move from one place to another - say, within the solar system - practically globally. For example, for a selected “resident” it would be possible in a sufficiently large number of centers to have his basic package of information and transmit only changes reflecting his state at a given point in time, and a signal about his inclusion in this place. In the same world, one can imagine the infusion of the soul (spirit?) of a given person into someone else’s body. (What confusion one can imagine! Even we will be far from them.)
