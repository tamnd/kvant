9. The density of the gas mixture is
$$\rho = \frac{m_1 + m_2}{V}.$$
According to Dalton's law,
$$p = p_1 + p_2 = \frac{m_1RT}{M_1V} + \frac{m_2RT}{M_2V},$$
where
$$V = \frac{RT}{p} \left( \frac{m_1}{M_1} + \frac{m_2}{M_2} \right).$$
Then
$$\rho = \frac{(m_1 + m_2)p}{RT(m_1/M_1 + m_2/M_2)} \approx 2 \text{ кг/м}^3.$$

11. According to the definition, the average speed of a body over a period of oscillations is equal to
$$v_{\text{cp}} = \frac{2x_m}{T/2}.$$
The oscillation amplitude $x_m$ is related to the velocity amplitude $v_m$ by correspondence
$$v_m = \omega x_m = (2\pi/T)x_m.$$
Thus,
$$v_{\text{cp}} = 2v_m/\pi \approx 6 \text{ м/c.}$$
