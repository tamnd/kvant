“nightly work”?

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. VRONSHTEN

About three years ago I received a letter from a young astronomy enthusiast, Yan Kharitonov, from Kharkov. Here's what he wrote:

“Recently a wonderful book by P. G. Kulikovsky “M. V. Lomonosov - astronomer and astrophysicist”, as well as a new edition of the famous book by F. Yu. Siegel “Astronomical Mozanka”. In the first book he talks about the creative activity of M.V. Lomonosov. Including on p. 82 is given figure “night-visionary works” by Lomonosov. In the book by F. Yu. Siegel... there is a story about Leonardo da Vinci's tele, and on p. 22 is given figure of this work (in the reconstruction of V. A. Gurikov). If we remove the 5 crossing lines figure, we get... figure Lomonosov’s “night-visionary work.”

Figure from the book by P. G. Kulikovsky.

Having removed the books mentioned above from the shelf, I was convinced that Ya. Kharitonov was right. Figure are completely identical. I called both authors and asked them the same question: where did you get this figure? P. G. Kulikovsky answered: from the article by S. I. Vavilov “Nocturnal work of Lomonosov” in volume II of the collection “Lomonosov”, published in 1946. F. Yu. Siegel: from an article by V. A. Gurikov in the magazine “Earth and Universe”, I just don’t remember the year and number.*)

I take a collection with an article by S. I. Vavilov. The article is large and interesting, but figure this is not in it. I take volume IV of the “Complete Works of M.V. Lomonosov,” which contains an article on “night-visionary work,” and there is this figure, even reproduced twice: in the Latin text (Lomonosov wrote in Latin) and in the Russian translation. This is what Lomonosov writes in his marriage to figure:

“For this purpose, I adapted two works in the same way that we used to construct telescopes and microcops. In the lumen of the work ABCD, I placed a biconvex lens, the diameter of which was about 4 London inches [about 10 cm - V.V.]... In the lid of the work EFGH, inside which the first work was played, there was a round hole into which the collecting lenses mn were left, biconvex and having different diameters and foci.”

Note that Lomonosov's text mentions and explains the letter symbols found on figure.

However, Lomonosov did not include this piece of figure in the final text of the article; he crossed out the text and figure and replaced them with a shorter text (without figure). Therefore, in volume IV of the scientist’s “Complete Works,” the old text and the crossed out figure are given in a footnote.

So, it is clear: figure “night-visionary labor” undoubtedly belongs

*) This conversation took place shortly before the untimely death of F. Yu. Siegel, which followed on November 15, 1988.
