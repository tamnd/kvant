I should have left Baroyna. I took more air into my lungs and thundered:
- Stop it!
Suddenly the hall fell silent; everyone froze. In the directed silence, only the rustling of mouse wings and the sobbing of a naked girl could be heard. All eyes were fixed on me, even Baroyna's eyes. Well, I won’t hit you in the dirt.
I raised my eyebrows towards the lectern, and it gently lowered. A quick wave of the hand returned the girl's clothes.
I clasped my fingers and calmed down. Then, unclenching his fists, he released the falcons. They cleared the air of bats and, returning to my palms, obediently disappeared.
The audience turned into a continuous gaping mouth. It was necessary to defuse the situation.
— Any other questions?
The students shook their heads silently. Only Barone doesn't move.

This time, read the textbook pages three to seventeen inclusive. Solve all problems on page seventeen. You are free.
The audience dispersed slowly. Barone, who was the last to leave the hall, hesitated at the door. He looked over his shoulder. For a few seconds we found our eyes crossed. Then, as if he had made a decision, he nodded grimly. His teeth flashed in a minimal smile and he walked out humming a song.
I took a breath and collected my notes. As I was leaving, I looked at my watch. 11.30.
Maybe we made it to the next bus - at 1.15.
