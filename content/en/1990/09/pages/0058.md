It is possible to transport not receiving and transmitting stations, but technical tools, robots to create such stations on site.

One can also propose another way to carry out stellar travel, namely, to get in touch with other civilizations, transmit to them information about the construction of a receiving and transmitting station suitable for receiving and returning “our people”, the information necessary to create a standard material carrier, and in this way establish galactic travel (the same idea from science fiction - remember, for example, “Black Cloud”). Here the old problem appears in the discussions: how to get in touch with other civilizations?

The natural way is to create a “mage”, receive a return request and enter into contact. If we proceed from the idea of ​​​​creating a pulse beacon that receives energy from a star, with solar panels with a power of about 10⁹ kW (here the data applies to a radio beacon with a transmission frequency of only 100 Hz), then we can count on subscribers located at distances of up to fifty thousand light years and having receiving antennas with dimensions of up to 10-20 km near their star. The value of 10⁹ kW should not be alarmed. Solar batteries of such power should have dimensions of 100 × 100 km - gigantic, but generally feasible dimensions. Such a structure can be imagined as a light flat truss on which film photovoltaic cells are stretched.

The time frame for the implementation of such a connection will be thousands and ten thousand years. True, it’s not millions anymore, but it’s still a long time.

Could there be a shorter way?

If other civilizations had previously chosen this path of exploration of the Galaxy, then they could have already created their own “mages”. So, look for these beacons. Create receiving antennas capable of receiving signals from galactic beacons.

Radio telexes with antennas of the order of cliometers or more can be created in near-solar space. Where to look? Maybe in the center of the Galaxy? or along the middle lines of the spiral arms? in globular star clusters?

One way or another, this has already been decades, not thousands or millions of years.

Isn't there an even simpler way to get in touch with other civilizations?

Let's assume that representatives of other civilizations were (or are?) already on Earth or in the solar system. What could be the traces of their activities? Where can transmitting and receiving stations (including intermediate ones) be located?

There are two directions of search here. One is space boats. What should we expect them to be like: size, features of their life (maybe, for example, they do not need an atmosphere and organic matter for energy supply, vacuum is their natural habitat...)? Why didn’t they themselves come into contact or why don’t they want to come into contact with us? Another direction is the search for their means of communication, reception and transmission of travelers and information.

The purpose of such reflections on the verge of science fiction is to look ahead in order to understand our data, to determine promising directions for ultra-long-range search, to compare these directions with current gaps in ecology and economics, the arrangement of life on Earth, with today’s interesting scientific problems in the study of the Universe and the Earth. And from this analysis, identify the areas of work on which it is worth spending the total funds, energy and intelligence of people. This is necessary in order to make a balanced and reasonable choice, so as not to waste effort and money.

The 20th century saw a lot of examples of ridiculous decisions. You can, for example, take advantage of the work in our country on the creation of the H1 super rocket.
