Now he is at an early stage of development, he is regressing mentally. Motor activity is weakened; there is a general decrease in the activity of iron internal secretions; presence of accelerated loss of coordinates.

There are gray indicators of progressive amnesia.

The stimulating surgical thinking that we both underwent resulted in the intensification and rooting of all mental processes. Unforeseen phenomena, which I have taken the liberty of calling the “Algeron-Gordon Effect,” are the logical consequence of a general acceleration of thinking processes. The hypothesis proven here can be briefly formulated as follows: intelligence, increased artificially, then decreases at a rate directly proportional to the degree of its increase.

I think that in itself is an important discovery. According to all data, your own mental degradation will happen very quickly.

I have already begun to notice in myself signs of emotional instability and stubbornness - above all the symptoms of the end.

June 10. The deterioration is progressing. I was becoming distracted. Algeron died two days ago. The opening of the document is correct in my predictions. His entire brain was shrunk, and a general smoothing of the cerebral convolutions was detected, as well as deepening and widening of the sulci.

I believe that the same thing has happened to me or will soon happen.

I put Algeron's trip in a cheese box and buried him in the backyard. I cried.

June 15. Dr. Strauss came to see me again. I did not want to open the door and asked him to leave. I want to be left alone. I became offensive and aggressive. I feel the darkness fading. It is very difficult to get the thought of selfishness out of your head. I keep reminding myself how important this introspective journal will become later.

Why this strange sensation when you pick up a book that you read with pleasure just a month ago, and discover that you have completely stuffed it. I remembered what a great man John Milton seemed to me, but when I tried to read “The Lost Pai” today, I understood absolutely nothing. I got so angry that I threw the book across the room.

I have to try to save at least something. Anything that I have learned during this time. Oh my God, without taking everything away from me...

June 19. Sometimes in the evenings I go out for a walk. Last night I couldn't remember where I live. A policeman brought me home. I feel like it happened to me once, a long time ago. I continue to convince myself that I am the only person in the world who can describe what happened to me.

June 21. Why am I losing my memory? I have to fight. All day long I lie in bed, not knowing who I am or where I am. Then all of a sudden it all comes back. Quirks of amnesia. A symptom of old age - I fall into childhood. How relentlessly logical this is! I learned so much so quickly. And now my intellect is understood at tremendous speed. I won't allow this to happen. I will fight this. I am unable to drive away the memory of the boy from the restaurant, that expression on his face, the stupid smile, the people who laughed at him... No... I beg you... not this... again...

June 22. I forget what I recently learned. It seems that everything is going according to classical laws - what was supposedly the last is clogged first. However, is this the law? Sorry, I'd better read it again...

I re-read my report on the Algeron-Gordon Effect and it seemed as if someone else had written it. I don't even understand the non-trivial sections.

I keep tripping over things and it's getting more and more difficult for me to type on the machine.

June 23. I completely abandoned the typewriter. I have poor coordination of movements. I feel myself marveling slower and slower. My family and I were in a terrible shock. I took Krugar's article "Uber psichische Ganzheit" *) - I used it for some research to see if it would help me understand the essence of the work I had done. At first it seemed to me that there was something wrong with my vision. Then I realized that I could no longer read German. I tried other languages. Everything has disappeared.

June 30. A week passed before I decided to write again. Everything gradually flows away like sand through your fingers. Most of my books are now too difficult for me. They piss me off because I know that...

* “On mental perfection” (German). (Editor's note)
