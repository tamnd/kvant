I told her that I don't like her anymore. I said that I don't care about being smart anymore. This is not true. I still love her and I still want to be smart but I had to say so for her to leave. She paid Mrs. Flynn for my room. I don't mind it. I have to find a job.

Please... make sure I never forget how to read and write.

July 27. Mr. Donnegan was very kind when I came to the factory and asked him to hire me again as a janitor. At first he looked at me with gray confidence, but I told him what happened to me and he was very upset, put his hand on my shoulder and said Charlie Gordon, you are a courageous man.

Everyone looked at me when I went downstairs and began to clean the restroom as before. I told myself, Charlie, if they are over you, they’re not going to squeeze you, you remember, they’re not as smart as you once thought. And then they were your friends before, and if they laughed at you, it was nothing because they loved you too.

One of the workers who took after mine made a nasty joke, he said hey Charlie, I heard you are a very smart guy, a real professor. Well, say something smart to chotinbudu.

I felt bad, but then Joe Kerp came up and grabbed him by the shirt and said leave him alone, you're a lousy joker, or else I'll screw you up. I didn't expect Joe to take my side and I thought he was my true friend.

Later, Frank Reilly came up to me and told Charlie if anyone bothers you or wants to deceive you, call me or Joe and we’ll give him a light.

I said thank you to Frank and choked up and I had to go to the warehouse so that he wouldn’t see me cry. It's good to have friends.

July 28. Today I did something stupid: I forgot that I couldn’t wait to go to Miss Kinnian’s class at the adult school like before. I went into class and sat down in my old place at the end of the room and she looked at me strangely and said Charles.

I don’t remember her ever calling me that, she just said Charlie and I said hi Miss Kinnian, I prepared my pimping lesson, only I lost the reading book we use to study. She cried and ran out of the room and everyone looked at me, then I saw that these were completely different people and not those who had previously been in the same class with me.

Then I suddenly remembered what about appration and how I became smart, I said more of my dad, I knocked down Charlie Gordon. I snapped before she returned to class.

That's why I always left New York. I don't feel like doing anything like this again. I don't feel like Miss Kinnian feels sorry for me. Everyone at the factory wants me and I don’t feel that way either, so I’ll go to some place where no one knows that Charlie Gordon used to be a genius and now he can’t even read books or write well.

I take a couple of books with me and even if I can’t read them, I will exercise a lot and maybe I won’t forget everything I learned. If I try really hard, maybe I'll be a little smarter than before the appration. I have a rabbit's foot and happy singing and maybe they will help.

Miss Kinnian, if you ever read this, don't wish for me, I'm very glad that I took another chance to become smart because I learned a lot of different things that I had never even realized that they existed in the world and I'm grateful that I even saw it for a minute.

I don’t know why I became stupid again and what I did wrong, maybe it’s because I didn’t try very hard. But maybe if I put my mind to it and practice a lot, I’ll become a little smarter and know what all the words mean. I remember how pleased I was when I read a blue book with a torn cover. Therefore, I will definitely strive to become smart all the time so that I can feel so good again. It's nice to know things and be smart. I would like to be like this right now if I sat down and read all the time. And I am always probably the first stupid person in the whole world who clearly understands what is important for science. I remember what I did, but I just don’t remember what. It seems like I did something for all the stupid people like me.

Please Miss Kinnian and Dr. Strauss and everyone and R.S. please tell Dr. Nemur not to grumble so much when he is boldly overpowered and he will have more than others. It’s not at all difficult to have friends if we allow people to bully us. Where I go I will have many friends.
