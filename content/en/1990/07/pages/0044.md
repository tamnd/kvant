F1222. An inductor with L=10 Gi is connected to a 220 V network, 50 Gi in series with a diode (Fig. 1). Draw a graph of the current in the coil as a function of time. What is the maximum current value? How does the type of graph depend on the moment the circuit is connected to the network?

Indeed, the appearance of the current graph in the coil significantly depends on the moment the circuit is connected to the network.

So, if the voltage in the network is negative at this moment, the diode connected to the circuit will be closed until the voltage changes sign. Then the diode will open and will remain open until the current in the coil drops to zero - as long as the coil has energy, it will not allow the diode to close (creating the necessary voltage - emf of self-induction).
