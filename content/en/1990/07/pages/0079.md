First physics assignment
Since the task contains problems of varying degrees of complexity, to build a 3Sh physics department, it may be enough to correctly solve one or two problems.

However, after analyzing the problems of your class, it is useful (and we recommend that you) familiarize yourself with the problems for other classes, and try to solve the problems you like (the more, the better).

The experimental task is one for construction in all classes.

Measure the density of wood by placing a wooden object in a pan of water and using only a ruler to measure. The density of water is known.

Theoretical problems

9th grade
1. Since the day of the war, they have been trying to lift a sunken steel anchor weighing 780 kg using a foam ball, attaching it to the anchor with a light cable. At what minimum volume of the sphere is this possible? The densities of steel, water and foam plastic are 7800, 1000 and 150 kg/m³, respectively.

2. A wire with a resistance of 100 Ohms was cut into several equal parts and connected in parallel. After this, the total resistance became 1 ohm. How many pieces did you cut the wire into?

3. How long will it take for the tape winding radius to increase from $r$ to $R$? Belt thickness $d$, speed $v$.

4. There is a 100 Ohm resistor, rated for a power of no more than 4 W, and a 200 Ohm resistor, rated for a power of no more than 2 W. What is the maximum voltage that can be applied to a system of these resistors connected in series without the risk of damaging them?

10th grade
1. Two athletes run the same time. One attacks the first half of the time with acceleration $a$, the second with acceleration $2a$. Another athlete does the opposite, i.e. the first half of the time he beats with acceleration $2a$, the second with $a$. Which of them will run the longest distance?

2. In what minimum time can one automatically reach a mountain whose surface forms an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon? The initial speed automatically brings you to the mountain $v$, the height of the mountain $H$, the friction coefficient of the wheels on the road $\mu$ ($\mu < tg \alpha$).

3. A cone with an opening angle of $2\alpha$ at its apex stands on a horizontal table. A small body is attached to the top of the cone using a thread of length $L$. At what minimum speed can a body rotate around a cone while remaining in contact with its surface? Gravity acceleration $g$, there is no friction between the concession surface and the body.

4. The acceleration of free fall on the surface of a certain planet is $g_1$, and at a height $H$ above the surface it is $g_2$. Find the radius of this planet.

5. A body of some mass lies on a horizontal floor. It is tied to the ceiling, located at a height of $H$ from the floor, hanging freely with a weightless rubber cord of length $L$ ($L > H$). Cord stiffness $k$. What minimum horizontal speed must be imparted to the body in order for it to leave the floor? Under what conditions is such a speed significant? Gravity acceleration $g$, no friction.

The atmosphere on Venus consists of carbon dioxide, the pressure on its surface turns the air pressure on the Earth's surface 100 times, the absolute temperature on Venus is 7/3 of the temperature on Earth.

4. A sphere of radius $R$ lies on an infinite plane. The plane and the sphere have the same surface charge $c$. Determine the maximum electric field voltage of such a system.
