Problem solutions
M1206—M1210, F1218—F1222

One of the simplest solutions is obtained by a simple calculation using the formula $S_{APQB} = AQ \cdot BP/2$. If $\angle CAE = \alpha$, and $\angle CBF = \beta$, then $AQ = AO + OQ = R(1 + tg \beta)$, $BP = R(1 + tg \alpha)$, where $O$ is the center, and $R$ is the radius of the given circle, and

$$S_{APQB} = \frac{1}{2} R^2 (1 + tg \alpha + tg \beta + tg \alpha tg \beta).$$

Now we need to prove that the value in brackets is equal to 2, i.e. that $(tg \alpha + tg \beta)/(1 - tg \alpha tg \beta) = 1$. Left side.
