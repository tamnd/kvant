Prospects for searching for habitable planets

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
L. XANFOMALITI

The problems of habitability and life in the Universe are among the deepest questions of philosophy. The idea of ​​existing on distant worlds by reason leaves almost no one indifferent. Thinkers from various historical eras have always addressed this topic. Man has always dreamed of meeting his own kind and with his mind populated them first with unknown lands, then with unknown celestial bodies.

When science and technology reached modern levels, the problem of searching for life in the Universe - in one form or another - was included in the scientific research plans of many countries.

Are there planets in other star systems?

Without talking about all sorts of fantasies, you can always look for life - in the only form known to us - only on planets. Now scientists know only one planetary system - ours, and there are no further results of searching for planets around other stars. Therefore, we have to use some indirect information about how far a system like the Solar one extends.

The generally accepted hypothesis of the origin of a planetary system involves the formation of planets through the condensation of a protoplanetary gas and dust cloud captured by the protocol or remaining after its formation. This hypothesis was proposed in our country by academician O. Yu. Shmidt and was developed in detail first by himself, and subsequently by his students. Regardless of how many assumptions were made by foreign scientists, in particular K. Weizsäcker in Germany.

Let us note that the transition from qualitative reasoning to a more or less rigorous theory revealed the extreme complexity of the processes involved in the formation of planets. Many authors believe that after the formation of the Sun it is more likely that the nebula's remains will condense into a second, dwarf star than into a planetary system. In other words, the probability of the formation of a double star is much higher than the formation of a star with planets. And indeed, it is double and multiple stars that make up the overwhelming majority of the “population” of the Galaxy. Therefore, the very fact of the existence of our planet requires explanation.
