Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol operates a Correspondence School (3Sh) for students in grades 9-11 in general education schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students’ interest in the natural sciences.

There are five departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic. The mathematics, physics and chemistry departments accept students in grades 9-11, the biology department accepts only students in grades 10, and the economics department admits only students in grades 11.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also include mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives that work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. The leaders of the circles will recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the relevant subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader informs 3SH of his last name, first name, patronymic and sends a posted list of members of the circle (indicating the results of grades for the first assignment), signed by the school director and sealed. After this, the members of the circle are considered to be 3Sh students.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional materials. The work of animal education students is carried out in 3SH, and the work of circle members is carried out by its leader (at the request of the leader, some of the work of circle members can be checked in 3SH).

To become a student at the Correspondence School at NSU, you must send an application written on a postcard to the director of 3Sh, asking to send the first assignment. The deadline for sending the application is no later than September 20 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). The application must also provide some information about yourself:

Last name, first name, patronymic (in full, stamped letters)

The class in which you are a teacher in your school

Department 3Sh where you want to study (you can specify two)

Detailed home address with mandatory indication of post office code

Enrollment in 3Sh is made based on the results of the first task. In cases where the number of people wishing to study in 3Sh exceeds the capacity of our school, pre-option can be given by a student from the cela or from small towns and villages.

The head of the circle must send a letter to the director of 3Sh with a request to send the first assignment and additional materials for it.

An application for admission to the mathematics or physics department of 3Sh can be sent along with the solutions to the corresponding first assignment, published below, but no later than October 10.

Write down the solutions to the problems in a simple student space in a square, leaving the fields for a substitute teacher. On the cover of the application, please indicate the same information about yourself as in the application. Send your work along with the application only a simple parcel (do not fold the third one or roll it into a tube). In your notebook with solutions, place a piece of paper measuring 6x10 cm with your address written on it (it will be pasted on the envelope when the answers are given).
