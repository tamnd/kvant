When the core of a star is sufficiently enriched in heavy elements, the star explodes with a sharp increase in luminosity (turns into a supernova) and “dumps” most of its mass. From this “dust” a new generation of stars is formed, even richer in heavy elements, etc. Cosmologists have established that our Sun belongs to the fourth generation of stars. Since the age of the Universe is ~15 billion years, it would seem to follow from this that the average lifespan of a stellar generation is ~3 billion years. But this time is not enough for bioevolution (the age of the Earth is ~5 billion years). This means that our Sun belongs to a rather rare group of relatively long-lived stars. Most stars live shorter lives (the lifespan of a star depends on its mass); the reason for their existence is to provide solar-type stars with material from heavy elements.

The ancients spoke of harmonicepheres: the course of cosmic processes, which appears complex and meaningful to the philosophizing mind, seems to sound to it like an orchestra. And the life of a star is similar to the concept of this orchestra, where the role of the orchestra is played by the forces of nature, each of which has its own instrument and its own part. The cello - nuclear forces - and the violin - electromagnetic - lead the entire concert; the flute squeaks from time to time - weak internal forces; gravity - the double bass - remains as if in the background, there is no tact. The concert ends with “weak” interactions - putting aside the flute and taking up the French horn, they play soya - this is a rapid stream of neutrinos from the core of the exploding supernova that carries away the outer shell of the star.

So, the periodic table is “done”. The further process of the condition occurs through the formation of molecules, and the unlimited nature of this process is ensured by the presence of the amazing element carbon. On its basis, chains of molecules of enormous length can arise. Carbon is ideally suited for this*). It is tetravalent and the angles between its valence bonds are ~90°; carbon atoms, clinging to each other, form a line, as it were, and other atoms and molecules sit on the remaining two valences - something like an “inscription” appears. The entire cosmic process before the formation of carbon chains was, as it were, a process of forming a “letter”; With the formed organic molecules, “letters” - atoms - begin to unite into “words” - molecules. But words are not speech. There is a void between the jumble of “words”-molecules and the meaningful “speech” of a living organism. However, before touching on the question of life, I must say a few words about inorganic evolution.

The further the process of conditioning the structures goes, the greater the demands on the conditions for its occurrence. Nuclei do not decay up to temperatures of billions of degrees, atoms cannot withstand even several thousand, and for molecules we are talking about hundreds of degrees. Temperature is a measure of chaos, and chaos is inimical to structure.
