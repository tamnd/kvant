Is a flat mirror simple?

There seems to be nothing easier than capturing an image of an accurate light source using a flat mirror. On the other hand, it is believed that a flat mirror is the only optical instrument that gives an image of an object that is completely symmetrical to the object itself.

All of the above is true, however, only for special metal mirrors that are used in various types of optical instruments. It is known, for example, that almost completely reflect luminous silver films falling on them. Other highly conductive metals also have high reflectivity, close to ideal.
