M1231. What is the maximum number of parts that the plane $Oxy$ can be divided into by the graphs $n$ of square triangles of the form $y=ax^2+bx+c$ ($n=1, 2, 3, ...$)?

N. Vasiliev

M1232. The hostess baked a pie for the guests. There can be either $p$ people or $q$ people at the table. What is the minimum number of pieces (not necessarily equal) that the pie must be cut into in advance so that it can be distributed equally in any case? Consider the following case:
a) $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime numbers;
b) $p$ and $q$ have the most common divisor $d$.

D. Fomin

M1233. In the trapezoid $ABCD$ the diagonal $AC$ is equal to the side $BC$, $H$ is the middle of the base $AB$. Let $l$ be the line passing through $H$, and $P$ and $Q$ the intersection points of line $l$ with lines $AD$ and $BD$, respectively. Prove that angles $ACP$ and $QCB$ are equal or add up to $180^\circ$.

I. Sharitsin

M1234. Can any triangle be divided a) into 7, 6)* into 5 similar triangles?

A. Soefer (USA)

M1235*. Let $p=2 \cdot q+1$ be a prime number. Prove that the number $2^{3q}!$ - $(-1)^{q}(2q-1)!!$ is divisible by a) $p$; 6) $p^2$; c) $p^3$ (at $p>3$). (Here $q!=1 \cdot 2 \cdot \dots \cdot q$; $(2q-1)!!=1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot \dots \cdot (2q-1)$).

I. Vayshtein

F1238. In the middle of the long cylindrical tube containing glycerin is a small air bubble. If you place the tube vertically, then the bubblerek will move at a constant speed of $v_0=1$ cm/s.

The tube was placed horizontally and accelerated along its side length to a speed of $v=20$ m/s. Where will bubblerek stop? Where will it move if the speed of the swim is increased to 30 m/s? Where does he end up after the tube slows down?

A. Andrianov

F1239. In a vertical heat-conducting cylindrical vessel under a massive piston there is 1 mole of an ideal monatomic gas at a temperature of $T_0$. Let's begin to compress the gas, lowering the piston. After work $A$ was done, the piston was released and it remained in the new equilibrium position. Find the temperature $T_x$ in this state.

V. Uzdin
