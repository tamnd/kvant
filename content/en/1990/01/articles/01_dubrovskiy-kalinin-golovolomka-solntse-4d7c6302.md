By cutting this card along the lines into squares, you will get an interesting puzzle. It is similar to the game “15”: by moving the squares, you need to move the image of a face to an empty space. Don’t rush to criticize us for such a childish task. It is by no means so simple. To solve it, you need to swap places with the squares with the face and the square located under it. But in any book that says anything, it is impossible to change the places of just two chips in it. But the “sun” puzzle is solvable. What is her secret? The answer is in the next issue of the magazine.

D.K.
