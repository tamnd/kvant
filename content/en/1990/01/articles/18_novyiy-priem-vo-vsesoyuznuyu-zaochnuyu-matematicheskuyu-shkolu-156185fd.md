New admission to the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School

To the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosov are accepted for individual training in upper grades*) of secondary schools and vocational schools, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

The goal of the VZMS is to tell your students about many exciting things related to the school mathematics course, to teach them how to solve interesting and varied problems, to teach them how to work independently with a book and how to competently, clearly and concisely express their thoughts on paper. All successful graduates of the VZMS (including branches and “Collective Student” groups) are issued appropriate certificates.

To enter the VZMSH, you must complete the test given below. Preferably used in designing rural areas, workers' towns and small towns.

To be accepted into the VZMSH, it is not necessary to solve all the problems. Solutions to the problems must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. This notebook is sent as a simple parcel; don't make it difficult. Place a piece of paper on the cover of the notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the sample given at the bottom of the page.

In the notebook you need to put two sheets of paper measuring 6x14 cm with a clearly written postal address (including zip code), the student's last name and first name, as well as an envelope with your address written on it.

The tasks in the work should go in the same order as they set us: first the conditions, then the solution.

The deadline for sending the work is no later than March 15, 1990 (by postmark).

If you select a specific item, then, starting from September 1990, you will receive our assignments, which contain theoretical material and tasks for independent solution, as well as control tasks. All test papers will be checked and decided in detail by VZMSH teachers - students, graduate schools and teachers of Moscow State University and other universities in which there are branches of VZMSH. The branches operate under the same programs and benefits as the Moscow VZMSH group.

Each such group is a mathematical circle, working under the guidance of a mathematics teacher according to the VZMS program and its manuals. Admission to these groups is carried out until October 1, 1990 in two streams: for those who will learn to study in the 9th grade from September 1990, and for those who will learn to study in the 10th grade (for first and second year students of SPTU, respectively).

Admission to groups is carried out without competition. To enroll, please submit an application from the mathematics teacher in charge of the circle, indicating the list of students and the class in which they will study in the 1990/91 academic year.

The application must be signed by the school director (SPTU) and certified by a seal. The work of the head of the “Collective Student” group of the VZMS can be paid for by schools upon submission of the VZMS as elective classes. The application should be sent to VZMSH.

Without a test, only upon application, students of the All-Union and Resource Sciences, as well as the winners of regional and regional mathematics Olympiads for schoolchildren and vocational school students, are accepted for individual training.

Objectives of the introductory test at VZMSH 1990

1. Two quantities left points A and B at the same time, following each other, and met an hour later. Having nailed to points B and A, respectively, the values ​​immediately turned back and met again. How long after the first meeting did this happen?

2. Given a square table of size $4 \times 4$ cells. Is it possible to mark some cells with crosses so that in each column, in each row and on each major diagonal there are: a) two crosses; b) three crosses?

3. A natural number divisible by 37 must have 3980 decimal digits, the first 1990 digits must be the same and the last 1990 digits must be the same. What could these numbers be?

4. a) Prove that the sum of the legs of a right triangle is equal to the sum of the diameters of the circle inscribed in it and the circle circumscribed around it.

5. The height $h$ of a right triangle divides it into two smaller triangles. Circles are inscribed in all three triangles. Find the sum of the radii of these circles.

5. Two pedestrians walked towards each other simultaneously from points A and B. Two hours later they met and continued their journey in the same direction, with the first walking to B 3 hours after the second walked to A. How long did each walk?

6. At points A and B in the middle of the ocean there are two ships. The distance $AB$ is 50 km. The ships simultaneously begin to move in a straight line in unknown directions at constant speeds of 15 km/h and 20 km/h until they meet at point C. What is the most possible time for them to move before meeting?

7. In the square $ABCD$ on the side $a$ there are four circles with center $A$. The segment $MN$ is tangent to this curve (points $M$ and $N$ lie on sides $BC$ and $CD$ respectively). Prove that $MC + CN > a$.

8. Among all fractions $\frac{p}{q}$ such that $\frac{1}{1990} < \frac{p}{q} < \frac{1}{1989}$, find the fraction with the smallest possible value $q$ ($p$ and $q$ are natural numbers).

9. In the city of Voula, some of the inhabitants are liars, and they always lie, but everyone else always tells the truth. However, in one room there are several residents of this city, and one of them said the following:

— There are no more than three of us here. We are all liars.

— There are no more than four of us here. We are not all liars.

- There are five of us here. Three of us are liars. Determine how many people are in the room and how many of them are liars.

10. Find the numbers $x$, $y$ from the equation

$$2x^4 + 2y^4 = 4xy - 1.$$

11. Find all pairs of natural numbers ($a; b$) such that $3a + 2$ is divisible by $b + 1$ and $3b + 2$ is divisible by $a + 1$.

12. Let's start building a sequence of numbers 1, 2, 5, 3, ... as follows. The first digit is equal to 1, then - if a number of the first terms of the sequence are written, then the next term is calculated as follows: the last of the written digits is added to the sum of all written digits and the last digit of the sum thus obtained is taken - it is the next member of the sequence. (Check that the numbers we wrote were obtained using this rule.) What number would be in 1990th place?

13. For each four values, the sum of the digits of the number to the number itself. For every four number value, is this fraction the most important?
