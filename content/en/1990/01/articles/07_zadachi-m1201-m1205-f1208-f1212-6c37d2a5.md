Our magazine holds a daily competition for the best solution to the problem from “Problem «Kvant». The results of the competition are announced in December. The winners receive the right to participate immediately in the resource tours of the All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics and physics.

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to the problem from this issue should be sent no later than March 15, 1990 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in advanced subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 1 - 90” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1201” or “F1208”. In the column “...sender’s address”, please write your first and last name separately. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the result of checking the solution).

Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in two copies along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter

M1201. In the parliament of Anchuria, one division must be elected from each of the 999 constituencies with the same number of voters. Three parties A, B, C have been created in Anchuria, nominate your candidates. Party A is supported by only 15% of voters, B - 30%, C - 55%. If in the first.type of elections in the district none of the candidates receives 50% of the votes, then the two who received the largest number of votes advance to the second.type. In the second type, parties A and B agree to support each other, and parties C vote for the candidate of party A. What is the largest and what is the smallest number of candidates from each party that can get into parliament?

N. Vasiliev

M1202. From vertex A of square ABCD, two loops are drawn inside the square, onto which perpendiculars BK, BL, DM, DN are dropped from vertices B and D. Prove that the segments KL and MN are equal and perpendicular to each other.

D. Nyamsuren (Mongolia)

M1203. Is it possible to cut a square from a side of 1 km into a) 31 squares; 6)* 30 square so that one of them has a side of no more than 1 m?

S. Eliseev

M1204*. Points A, B, C are given on the plane - the centers of three circles. Each circle is evenly divided (the radius increases at the same rate for all circles). As soon as two circles touch each other, they “pop” - their radius increases to 0 - and begin spreading again. Is it true that if the distances AB, BC, CA are integers, then this process will be periodic?

Study how this process can develop if triangle ABC is a) equilateral; 6) isosceles; c)* rectangular on the side 3, 4, 5. The initial state can be arbitrary (not only “zero”).

M. Khovanov

M1205. A boy and a girl play the following game: the boy draws non-overlapping polygons on a plane, and the girl paints them. If two polygons have a common side segment, then it should be painted in different colors. What is the smallest number of colors that a girl will definitely need to follow this rule if the boy draws only a) equilateral triangles;
b) isosceles right triangles;
c) identical squares?

G. Kondakov

F1210. To study the properties of a nonlinear resistor, a number of experiments were carried out. First, the dependence of the resistor resistance on temperature was investigated. When the temperature increased to a value of $t_1=100$ °C, a resistance jump instantly occurred from a value of $R_1=50$ Ohm to a value of $R_2=100$ Ohm; upon cooling, a reverse jump occurred at a temperature of $t_2=99$ °C (Fig. 1).
In the second experiment, a constant voltage of $U_1=60$ V was applied to the resistor, at which its temperature turned out to be equal to $t_3=80$ °C.
Finally, when a constant voltage $U_2=80$ V was applied to the resistor, spontaneous current oscillations appeared in the circuit. Determine the period of these oscillations, as well as the maximum current value. The air temperature in the LAB is constant and equal to $t_0=20$ °C.
The heat transfer from the resistor is proportional to the temperature difference between the resistor and the surrounding air. Thermal capacity of the resistor is $c=3$ J/K.

A. Vuzdin
