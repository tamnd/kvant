Computer tournament of the XXIII National Olympiad in Mathematics

In 1989, at the All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad, the jury decided to test a computer tournament for the first time. This decision has been brewing for a long time; For several years now, computer games have been held for the participants of the Olympiad, and the jury had the opportunity to see firsthand how the children’s attraction to the computer makes a difference. Of course, it is still too early to prepare for the inclusion of a computer tournament in the official competition of the Olympiad; in the future, the All-round Mathematical Olympiad may get its own practical round. This would be very desirable, since mathematics is dangerous from the life-giving sources of its applications - having developed a thread connecting them, we risk bleeding both sides.

Computer tour tasks

1. With a pair of numbers $(a, b)$, the following transformations are allowed: add another to one of these numbers and turn the existing pair into either a pair $(a+b, b)$ or a pair $(a, a+b)$. Write and program an algorithm that calculates the smallest number of such transformations required to turn the pair $(1, 1)$ into a pair containing the number $N$ ($1 \leq N \leq 2000$).

V. Rozhdestvensky

2. The sequence $\{a_n\}$ is defined by its first term $a_1=1/e$ and the recurrence relation $a_n=1-na_{n-1}$. Calculate the number $a_n$ for $n \leq 50$ to four decimal places. Consider it known that

$$\frac{1}{e} = \frac{1}{2!} - \frac{1}{3!} + \frac{1}{4!} - \frac{1}{5!} + \dots$$

E. Tyrtyshnikov

3. The sequence $\{x_n\}$ is called periodic if for some natural numbers $M$ and $T$ the equality $x_n=x_{n+T}$ holds for any $n>M$. The smallest number $M$ and $T$ with this property is called the dilium of the lane part and the dilium of the period of the corresponding one.

It is known that for some restrictions of functions of the natural argument $f(x)$ for all $i \geq 1$ the equality $x_{i+1}=f(x_i)$ is true, so the sequence $\{x_n\}$ is periodic.

a) Specify an algorithm that, given such a sequence, will determine $M$ and $T$, using a comparison number that is linear in the maximum of these two numbers;

b) Program this algorithm for calculating the length of the period and the length of the lane part of the sequence $\{x_n\}$ given its first term $x_1$ for functions $f(x)$, the value of which at point $x$ is the remainder of division by $10^8$ of the number $g(x)$, which determines the formula

$$g(x) = \begin{cases} x/3 & \text{при } x=3k \\ 2(x-1)/3 & \text{при } x=3k+1 \\ 13(x-2)/3 & \text{при } x=3k+2. \end{cases}$$

Y. Nesterenko, A. Slinko

When checking the given task, the jury encountered a number of difficulties, which, apparently, are typical for all computer competitions. Here are just a few of them: the inability to check the correctness of the program and the correctness of the algorithms due to limited time and the number of jury members; heterogeneity in the use of computer technology and programming languages; the need to take into account the time characteristics of the program in connection with the specified heterogeneity.
