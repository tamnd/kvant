Here is the Quantum that Isaac built,
And here is the student,
Who occasionally likes to boast,
That everything is understood on the whole page
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

Here is the author of the article - a famous scientist,
Who wrote it so passionately
For this excessively Serbez girl,
Who occasionally likes to boast,
That everything is understood on the whole page
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

But the reviewer is a long-time member of the editorial team,
Which other essay is this,
Imagining myself as a pretty girl,
Which vainly purrs the page,
Trying to understand what's being said
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

And this is the editor who edited this article,
Only the author's name was left in place,
That even the dumbest girl in the world
I was able to boast at least once a year,
That everything is understood on the whole page
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

And this is an artist in deep space
Tries to think about those inklostrations
Which will charm a girl so much,
That she will immediately read the page
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

Here is the editor-in-chief - big
academician,
He rules (without demanding money for it),
So that everything is done only this way and this way,
Since he is this same Isaac,
Who fell deeply in love with a girl,
Who pokes her finger at the page,
Trying to understand what's being said
In the Quantum that Isaac built.

This poem was published in a special issue of the magazine «Kvant», published in a single copy in 1983 for the 75th anniversary of Academician I.K. Kikon. The author of the poem is A.P. Savin, a member of the editorial collection of our magazine.
