Regularly meeting with our readers, looking through letters and responses to questionnaires, we see many problems that the magazine needs to solve. Many people ask to talk more often about the history of science, to include more occupied tasks and other materials for younger students.

Of course, we would like to give space to science fiction on the pages of our magazine, we would like to revive the section “Reviews, Bibliography”, presenting new popular science books on mathematics, physics, astronomy, cosmonautics, cybernetics, and resume publication of notes under the headings “Problems for Research”, “Ask and We Answer”. We also hope to talk more often about applications of physics and mathematics, and related sciences.

Unfortunately, the years bring inevitable losses. The academicians - the founders of the magazine, one of the most active employees of the first staff of the editorial collection I. Sh. Slobodetsky, the assistant editor-in-chief L. G. Aslamazov, the founder of the "Laboratory «Kvant» G. I. Kosurov, an active participant in the "Problem Book «Kvant»" B. B. Bukhovtsev, the head of the section "The Art of Programming" A. P. Ershov have passed away... But much of those who started work in «Kvant» from the first issue continue to successfully collaborate with the journal - Academician of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR V. A. Fabrikant, Corresponding Member of the Academy of Sciences of the USSR V. G. Boltyansky, Doctors of Physical and Mathematical Sciences M. I. Bashmakov, I. F. Ginzburg, N. Kh. Rozov and Ya. A. Smorodinsky, Candidates of Physical and Mathematical Sciences N. B. Vasilyev and A. P. Savin, V. A. Leshkovtsev. And we extend words of sincere gratitude to them.

This anniversary issue is in basic condition from articles that have already been published in the magazine. Of course, we would like to recall other articles from previous years, and we suggest returning to them from time to time.
