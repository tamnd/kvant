New admission to the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School

To the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosov are accepted for individual training in upper grades*) of secondary schools and vocational schools, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

The goal of the VZMS is to tell your students about many exciting things related to the school mathematics course, to teach them how to solve interesting and varied problems, to teach them how to work independently with a book and how to competently, clearly and concisely express their thoughts on paper. All successful graduates of the VZMS (including branches and “Collective Student” groups) are issued appropriate certificates.

To enter the VZMSH, you must complete the test given below. Preferably used in designing rural areas, workers' towns and small towns.

To be accepted into the VZMSH, it is not necessary to solve all the problems. Solutions to the problems must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. This notebook is sent as a simple parcel; don't make it difficult. Place a piece of paper on the cover of the notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the sample given at the bottom of the page.

In the notebook you need to put two sheets of paper measuring 6x14 cm with a clearly written postal address (including zip code), the student's last name and first name, as well as an envelope with your address written on it.

The tasks in the work should go in the same order as they set us: first the conditions, then the solution.

The deadline for sending the work is no later than March 15, 1990 (by postmark).

If you select a specific item, then, starting from September 1990, you will receive our assignments, which contain theoretical material and tasks for independent solution, as well as control tasks. All test papers will be checked and decided in detail by VZMSH teachers - students, graduate schools and teachers of Moscow State University and other universities in which there are branches of VZMSH. The branches operate under the same programs and benefits as the Moscow VZMSH group.
