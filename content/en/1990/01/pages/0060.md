The thing is that with total internal reflection, the surface of the water becomes a good mirror, since the light reflection coefficient increases sharply. For angles of incidence close to zero, the reflection coefficient of water is approximately 2%, and for angles greater than the limit, it is almost 100%. figure shows that the mirror image of the fish is seen from a different viewing angle than the fish itself.
