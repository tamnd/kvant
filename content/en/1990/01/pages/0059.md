A group of students from Physics School No. 165 in Novosibirsk designed a device for taking photographs with a viewing angle close to 180°. They spoke about this experiment at a scientific conference of students of physical education boarding schools, which was held in March 1974 in 2. Tbilisi, and then wrote an article in «Kvant». This is what we bring to your attention.

Let's imagine a swimmer who dives under the water without a mask. What picture is open to him if he looks up across the capable surface of a body of water?

Provided that the body of water is relatively small, the swimmer will see the following. On the dark ceiling (the surface of the water) there is a bright round window, into which the entire sky is “sandwiched” from horizon to horizon. All objects surrounding the pond stand at the edges of this window, with their “tops” directed towards its center. Moreover, it will be possible to see even part of the coastal bottom.

All this can be easily explained on the basis of the phenomenon of total internal reflection of light. It is known that when light passes from an optically denser medium to an optically less dense medium, there is a limiting angle of incidence (angle α₀ on figure 1), starting from which light will not exit from the first medium to the second. It is said that in this case the light experiences total internal reflection. For example, for the water-air transition α₀=49°.
