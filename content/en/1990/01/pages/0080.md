$$\frac{(24 - 16a)x^2 + (18 - 12a)x + (3 - 2a) = 0}{(3 - 2a)(8x^2 + 6x + 1)} = 0.$$

So, if $a$ is the desired value, then either $a = \frac{3}{2}$ or the common roots of the two given equations are the roots of the quadratic equation $8x^2 + 6x + 1 = 0$, i.e., some of the numbers $x_1 = -\frac{1}{2}, x_2 = -\frac{1}{4}$.

Substituting each of the numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ into the general equations, we obtain two desired values ​​$a$, corresponding to $a_1 = \frac{3}{16}$ and $a_2 = \frac{297}{128}$.

Substituting $a = \frac{3}{2}$ into these equations, we arrive at the following system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} 22x^4 + 33x^3 - 24x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0, \\ 11x^4 + 33x^3 + 21x^2 - 3x - 2 = 0. \end{cases}$$

Subtracting the second from the first equation, we obtain the biquadratic equation $11x^4 - 45x^2 + 4 = 0$, solving which we find four possible values ​​for $x$: $-2, 2, -\frac{1}{\sqrt{11}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{11}}$.

The check shows that the value $x = 2$ is a construction and the other three values ​​satisfy the conditions.
