Physics
Oral exam assignment
1. At the ends of the thread, thrown over a stationary block, two weights weighing $m = 300$ g each are suspended. What additional weight must be placed on one of the weights so that each of them travels in time $t = 0,5$ s along a path $l = 50$ cm? Will the time it takes to travel this path change if an additional weight is placed on another weight? Find the pressure on the block axis. The mass of the block and thread is considered to be $g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

2. A body with a mass of $m = 5$ kg falls from a certain height, having an initial speed equal to $v_0 = 2$ m/s and directed vertically downwards. Calculate the work done against the resistance forces during $t = 10$ s, if it is known that at the end of this period of time the body had a speed of $v = 50$ m/s. Consider the resistance force constant.

3. The rubber bladder was inflated on shore to normal pressure ($p_0 = 10^5$ Pa). To what depth should the chamber be lowered into water to reduce its volume by half? Air temperature on the shore $t_1 = 27$ °C, water temperature $t_2 = 4$ °C, water density $\rho = 10^3$ kg/m$^3$. Determine the mass of air in the chamber if its initial volume is $V_1 = 2$ l, the molar mass of air is $M = 29 \cdot 10^{-3}$ kg/mol, the universal gas constant is $R = 8,31$ J/(mol K).

4. In a cylindrical vessel under a light movable piston there is $m = 3$ kg of water at $t_0 = 20$ °C. When heating water, the amount of heat $Q = 1017$ kJ is reported. How much steam does this produce? How high will the piston rise? Atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 10^5$ Pa, piston cross-sectional area $S = 3$ dm$^2$, specific heat of water $c = 4,19$ kJ/(kg K), specific heat of vaporization $r = 2,26 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg, molar mass of water $M = 18 \cdot 10^{-3}$ kg/mol, universal gas constant $R = 8,31$ J/(mol K). Neglect the thermal distribution of water.

5. A paraffin plate fills the entire space between the plates of a flat-plate capacitor. Dielectric capacity of paraffin $\varepsilon = 2,0$. The capacitance of the capacitor with paraffin is $C = 4,0$ mKF, its charge is $q = 0,2$ mKL. How much work must be done to remove the plate from the capacitor?

6. Two identical light bulbs with a power of $P_1 = 50$ W each, rated at a voltage of $U = 10$ V, are connected in parallel and connected to a battery with an internal resistance of $r = 0,5$ Ohm. However, one of the bulbs burned out, and it was replaced with another, designed for the same voltage, but with a power of $P_2 = 25$ W. How many times does the battery efficiency change?

7. The power lines of homogeneous power plant and magnetic fields were mutually redistributed. Charged power plant field $E = 1$ kV/m, magnetic field induction $B = 1$ mT. What should be the direction and model of the speed of the electron so that its movement in these fields is rectilinear?

8. The lens gives a magnification of $\Gamma = 3,0$ to an object located at a distance of $d = 40$ cm from it. Find the formula distance of the lens. Show the path of the rays.

9. Monochromatic light with a wavelength of $\lambda = 4,13 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m falls on a metal plate. The flux of photoelectrons emitted by this light from the surface of the metal completely delays the potential difference $U = 1$ V. Determine the work function of electrons from the metal and the wavelength of light corresponding to the red boundary of the photoelectric effect. Planck's constant $h = 6,62 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s, electron charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, electron mass $m = 9 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, speed of light $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s. Will the photoelectric effect be observed if the wavelength of the incident light is $\lambda_1 = 7 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m?

10. As a result of the thermonuclear reaction $^2\text{H} + ^3\text{H} \rightarrow ^2\text{He} + ^1\text{n}$ energy is released. What part of the released energy is carried away by the neutron? The kinetic energy of action nuclei and tritium cannot be combined. Let us distinguish between the neutron and proton masses.
