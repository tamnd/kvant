Leningrad State University

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics, Faculty of Psychology)
1. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}(x^2 - 4x + 14 - 4\sqrt{6}) < 2.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos x = \cos \frac{1}{x}.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$(x^2 + 3x - 2)^2 + 3(x^2 + 3x - 2) - 2 = x.$$
4. Two circles with equal radii $r$ touch each other externally and touch a third circle with radius $R$ internally. Find the radius of the circle simultaneously touching these three circles (of the two possible cases, the one in which the center of the 4th circle and the center of the circle with radius $R$ lie on opposite sides of the point of contact of the circles with radius $r$ is considered).
5. In the triangular paramid $ABCD$, the edge $AB$ is perpendicular to the edge $DC$, the length of $AB$ is $a$, the length of $DC$ is $b$. Note that the angles formed by $DC$ with faces $ACB$ and $ABD$ are equal to $\alpha$. Find the volume of the paramid.
Option 2
(Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes, Department of Economic Cybernetics of the Faculty of Economics, Department of Mathematical License of the Faculty of Physics)
1. Find all $x$ and $y$ that match
$$4\log_2^2 x + 1 = 2\log_2 y,$$
$$\log_2 x^2 \geq \log_2 y.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos 3x \sin 2x + \sin x = 0.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$9 + \sqrt{5}x^3 + 5x + \frac{\sqrt{5}}{x^3} = 3\sqrt{5}x^2 + 3x + \frac{3\sqrt{5} - 1}{x} + \frac{3}{x^2}.$$
4. In triangle $ABC$ angle $B$ is a right angle. The points $D$ and $E$ on leg $CB$ are located so that the segments $AD$ and $AE$ divide the angle $A$ into three equal parts. $AD = a$, $AE = b$. Find the ratio of the areas of triangles $ADB$ and $AEB.$
5. The base of a triangular paramid is a regular triangle, and the two angles at the base are $\alpha$, $\alpha$ and $\frac{\pi}{2}$. Find the volume of the paramid if it is known that its height is $H$.
Option 3
(biology and physics faculties)
1. Find tan 2$\alpha$ without tables if sin $\alpha = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\alpha \in [0; \frac{\pi}{2}]$.
2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{4-y}} \geq \sqrt{y-1}.$$
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} xy = 27, \\ 2(2\log_y x + \log \frac{1}{x}) = 3. \end{cases}$$
4. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which inequality $ax^2 + x - 4a + 2 < 0$ implies inequality $2 < x < 0$.
5. A regular quadrangular paramid is described around the ball, the height of which is four times the diameter of the ball. Find the ratio of the volume of the sphere to the volume of the paramid.
Option 4
(faculties of geography and chemistry)
1. Solve the inequality
$$x - 1 \geq \sqrt{x^3 - 2x + 1}.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$3\sin 2x - 4\cos 2x = 5.$$
3. On the plane, find the set of points $(p, q)$ for which the equation $x^2 - 2px + q = 0$ has two real roots $x_1, x_2$ such that $x_1^2 + x_2^2 = 2$.
4. An equilateral right angle triangle with leg $a$ is rotated around the vertex of the right angle by angle $30^\circ$. Find the area of ​​the portion of the original and rotated triangles.
5. Two paramids with volumes $U$ and $V$ ($U < V$) are cut off by parallel planes in a trihedral angle. Find the volume of the trailei of a paramid if its base coincides with the base of the smaller one, and the vertex lies on the base of the larger paramid.
Option 5

$$\cos \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{x}{4}\right) = 5\sin \left(\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3}\right).$$
2. Graph the Function
$$f(x) = |x^2 + x| - x - 1.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_x - 2(1 - 5x^3 + x^5) < 0.$$
