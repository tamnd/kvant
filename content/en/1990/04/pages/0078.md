4. $\frac{b + \sqrt{b^2 + 8a^2}}{2b}$ Solution. Let $\angle EAB = \angle DAE = \alpha$ (Fig. 3). Then from $AB = a \cos 2\alpha = b \cos \alpha$ we obtain the equation $a(2\cos^2\alpha - 1) = b \cos \alpha$ to determine $\cos \alpha$. Considering that $\alpha$ is an acute angle, we write
$$\cos \alpha = \frac{b + \sqrt{b^2 + 8a^2}}{4a}.$$
The ratio of the area $S_1$ of triangle $ADB$ to the area $S_2$ of triangle $AEB$ is
$$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \frac{2a}{b} \cos \alpha = \frac{b + \sqrt{b^2 + 8a^2}}{2b}.$$
