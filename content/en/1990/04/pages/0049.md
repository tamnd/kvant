Green fog
V. MAYER

Have you ever seen green fog? No? Don't be upset, this crying can be fixed. Now you will see for yourself and show your commodity not only green, but also blue, and red, and in general - multi-colored fog.

Demonstration experience. It is most convenient to conduct the experiment in a school physics room on a demonstration table. For the experiment, you will first need a light source. This could be, for example, an automatic light bulb designed for an operating voltage of 6 V and producing light with a power of 21 kD, or a school illuminator for shadow projection (you just need to attach its light bulb close to the collecting lens of the illuminator or remove the lens completely). Any other light source will do, as long as it is small in size and does not illuminate nearby objects too brightly. The easiest way to achieve this is to place the source behind the response on a big black screen.

So, install light bulb 1 (Fig. 1) and place glass bottle 2 with a capacity of 20 liters at a distance of 20-40 cm from it. (Such bottles with a diameter of approximately 25 cm and a height of about 45 cm with a neck having a hole with a diameter of 3.5-4.5 cm, continue in hardware stores.) The bottle must be thoroughly washed, immediately before the experiment, rinsed with cold water and the outer surface wiped dry. There should be no drops of water left on the walls of the bottle, either from the equipment or from the inside.
