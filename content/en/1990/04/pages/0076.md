a) Show that
$$S = \pi(1 + 3r^2 - 2r(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)).$$

2. A rubber ball contains $V = 2$ liters of oxygen at a temperature of $t = 20^\circ C$ and an atmospheric pressure of $p = 100$ kPa. Determine the mass of oxygen in the ball. The pressure inside the ball is assumed to be equal to the pressure in the ball.
3. How many times must the source voltage be increased in order for the losses in the power line to decrease by $n$ times? The power supplied by the source is considered constant.
