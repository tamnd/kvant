Let the distance between the axes of the coil be $R$. At the beginning, the distance between the coils is $(R - \frac{D + d}{2})$ (see Fig. 4). Let the length of the rewound film be equal to $x$. Let's calculate the distance between the coils now. The area of ​​the large coil decreased by $xl$ ($l$ is the thickness of the film) and became equal to $\frac{\pi D^2}{4} - xl$, hence the radius of this coil becomes $r_1 = \sqrt{\frac{D^2}{4} - \frac{xl}{\pi}}$. Matching the radius of the second coil will increase, its area becomes $\frac{\pi d^2}{4} + xl$ and its radius $r_2 = \sqrt{\frac{d^2}{4} + \frac{xl}{\pi}}$. Now the distance between the coils will be equal

$$R - \left( \sqrt{\frac{D^2}{4} - \frac{xl}{\pi}} + \sqrt{\frac{d^2}{4} + \frac{xl}{\pi}} \right).$$
