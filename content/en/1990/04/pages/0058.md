4*). There is an unlimited number of black and white cups. *) Problems 4, 7 are taken from the book by V. A. Ufnarovsky “Mathematical Aquarium” (Chisinau, Shtinitsa, 1987).

5*) (for research). An endless shelf of checkered paper in both directions consists of black and white cells. Every second a cell that has an even number of black neighbors is white, and one that has an odd number of black neighbors is black. Prove that:

a) if after 2\textsuperscript{n} seconds the original coloring is repeated, then it is periodic with a period of 3·2\textsuperscript{n};

b) the original coloring is periodic if and only if it is itself periodic (periodicity in time is balanced by periodicity in space).

b) What can be said about the position of the width? About the entire checkered plane?

6. Each number is assigned one digit to the right, except for 9. Prove that sooner or later you will get a composite number.

7*) Given a rectangle with aspect ratio equal to $\sqrt{7}$. A square is cut from it, and we make a rectangle and perform the same procedure. Prove that the sequence of side relations of the constructed rectangles is periodic.
