Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. At what minimum angle to the horizon should the ball be thrown so that it passes through the basketball hoop from above without hitting it? The radius of the ball is $r$, the radius of the ring is $R = 2r$, its height above the floor is $H = 3$ m. A basketball player throws the ball from a height of $h = 2$ m, located at a distance of $l = 5$ m from the ring, counting horizontally. Neglect the change in the speed of the ball during its flight through the well.

2. In a vessel filled with water of density $q$, with a root $a$, an air bubble of volume $V$ floats up. Find the force of pressure exerted by the vessel on the support. The mass of the vessel including water is $M$.

3. Find the center of gravity of a thin homogeneous wire, according to the form of a semicircle with radius $r$.

4. How much work must be done to climb the downward moving metro escalator in time $t$? The lift height is $h$, the speed of the escalator is constant and equal to $v$, the angle of inclination of the escalator to the horizon is $a$.

5. A cylindrical thin-walled glass, half immersed in the liquid, floats on the surface of a liquid with density $q$. How much will the top edge of the glass drop if it is placed upside down on the surface of the liquid? Glass height $h$, air pressure $p_0$.

6. The end of the capillary tube is lowered into water. How much heat is released when a liquid rises through a capillary? The surface coefficient of the new water tension is $0$, the density of water is $q$.

7. A solid metal cylinder of radius $R$ rotates with a constant angular velocity $ω$. Find the dependence of the electric field strength on the distance $r$ to the cylinder axis and the potential difference between the surface of the cylinder and the axis.

8. Three identical identically loaded bodies, the charge of each of which is $q$ and the mass $m$, are connected by weightless, inextensible and non-conducting threads of length 1 so that the threads form an equilateral triangle. One of the threads is burned out. Find the maximum speed of the bodies.

9. A circle with a radius of $r_0 = 0,2$ m is drawn on a plane. The glass cone rests its vertex on the center of the circle so that its axis is perpendicular to the plane. What is the apparent radius of the circle when viewed from a great distance along the axis of the cone? The angle at the apex of the cone is $2\alpha = 60^\circ$, the radius of the base is $r = r_0 = 0,2$ m, the refractive index of the stack is $n = \sqrt{2} \approx 1,4$.

10. The cross section of a glass prism has the shape of an equilateral triangle. One of the edges is silver plated. A ray of light falls normally on the other, non-silvered face and, after two reflections, exits through the base of the prism perpendicular to it. Find the angles of the prism.
