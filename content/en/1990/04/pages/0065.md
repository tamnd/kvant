Introductory task

Those entering the 10th grade of 3FS need to solve problems 1-4, and those entering the 11th grade need to solve problems 4-7.

1. "Bickford's shur." A rod of length $l$ is made from an explosive substance. The detonation speed (the speed of movement in the explosive areas of the explosive) is $v$, and the speed of dispersion of the explosion products is $u < v$. How does the area occupied by explosion products change over time if the rod is undermined from one of the ends? Do figure.

2. "Cumulative explosion." From the same explosive (see problem 1) it is necessary to make a thin-walled final shell, such that when it is detonated from the top, the explosion products will simultaneously be removed along the front plate. What angle between the axis of the cone and the mold should be chosen for this?

3. "Ramp". Along the wooden ramps, forming an angle $a$ with the horizon, a box is inserted behind the rope. The coefficient of friction between the box and the tool is $u$. At what angle to the horizontal should the rope be pulled to rotate the box with the smallest number?

4. "Soft landing." Estimate the average force developed by a normal lift when landing after jumping from a second floor window. To determine this, consider that the mass of a person is 70 kg, the height of the jump is 5 m, and the shock absorption of the jump occurs over a length of 1 m.

5. "Missile Defense". At what speed must a projectile fly out of a cannon at the moment the rocket launches in order to hit a rocket flying vertically with acceleration $a$? The distance from the cannon to the missile launch site is $L$, the cannon fires at an angle $45^\circ$ to the horizon.

6. "Water pillow" Liquid is poured into a semispherical bell, the edges of which fit tightly to the surface of the table, through a hole at the top. When the liquid reaches the hole, it lifts the bell and begins to flow out from under it. Find the mass of the bell if its inner radius is $R$ and the density of the liquid is $q$.

7. "Linear accelerator". A particle of mass $m$ with charge $q$ flies into a flat capacitor through its upper plate - the plates are made in the form of grids - and accelerates in it. The field strength in the capacitor is $E$, the distance between the grids is $d$. The initial velocity $v$ of the particle makes an angle $a$ with the plane of the first grid. At what speed and at what angle to the plane of the second grid will the particle fly out of the capacitor?
