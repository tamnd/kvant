Congo language
We see that writing dates in the Congo language has the form

kilumbu ya ...ya ngonda...
where in place of each gap there is one word (tatu, mosi, etc.) or a phrase starting with kumi na. A comparison of the lines shows that the word after kilumbu ya denotes a number, and the word after the last place is the ordinal number of the place, i.e. “January” literally means “1st month,” “March” means “3rd month,” etc. The numbers 11 and 18 are rendered (respectively) as “10 and 1” and “10 and 8” (apparently denoting “and”). The literal meaning of the words kilumbu ya and ya ngonda remains unclear, but it is clear that the general meaning of the construction is

date... month number...
