Quests
1. What can be the last digit of the square of an integer if the penultimate digit is an odd number?

2. Two schoolchildren took part in the student chess tournament. Together they scored 6.5 points, and all students scored equally. How many students participated in the tournament?
(In the tournament, each participant plays with each other once, 1 point is given for a win, 0.5 points for a draw, 0 points for a loss.)

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown at figure. Identical letters correspond to identical numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers.

4. Prove the test for divisibility by 13: a number is divisible by 13 if and only if the sum of the number obtained by discarding the last digit and quadruple the last digit is divisible by 13.

5. Squares (“Pythagorean pants”) are built on the sides of a right triangle. Their vertices are connected as shown in figure. Show that the triangles formed have equal areas.

These tasks were suggested to us by R. Rustamov, A. Markosyan, Kh. Zhalolov, B. Goncharenko, N. Avilov.
