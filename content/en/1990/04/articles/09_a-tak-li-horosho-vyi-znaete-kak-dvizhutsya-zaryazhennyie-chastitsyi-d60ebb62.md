Do you know so well

How do charged particles move?

In experiments related to the discovery of the electron, electric and magnetic fields, already well known to scientists and practitioners, arose in response to its movement. And the peculiarities of the interaction of charged particles with this field have opened up new areas of their wide application: in electronics, the unrecognizably transformed world of information and communication, in existing and planned power plants, for example, MHD generators and tokamaks, in mass electrometry, which allows “weighing” atoms with electric and magnetic fields. The study of the behavior of charged particles in fields has led scientists to explain phenomena on a cosmic scale - from polar forces to processes inducing in stars, and the opportunity to look into the secrets of the microcosm with the help of giant “microscope” - a particle accelerator.

This issue of Kaleidoscope is an attempt to clarify the picture of the sometimes intricate movements of charged particles, relying only on school knowledge.
