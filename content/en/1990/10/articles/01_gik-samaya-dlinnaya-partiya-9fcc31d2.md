LONGEST
PARTY

From time to time in practice there are games for which there are more than 100 moves, and sometimes it goes up to almost 200. An interesting purely theoretical question is: what is the length of the game that can be played on the chessboard?

Until recently, the record was 5898 moves, and the 50-move rule was used to create such a fantastic game (see «Kvant», 1978, No. 12). White and Black can play as they please, but every 50 moves one of the sides must either capture the enemy piece or advance their own pawn. And so on until all resources run out.

The additions made to the latest chess code (see «Kvant», 1990, No. 7) allow in some cases the extension of the “maneuvering” of pieces to 75 moves. I. Vereshchagin used this circumstance of Moscow to establish a unique record. The number of moves in the longest game he constructed is 6074.

The reader need not be alarmed: we are not going to present the entire game for proof. It is enough to indicate the key moments when one of the parties must perform some actions in order to avoid a draw.

And this is how the game turns out.
