Quests

1. Find all prime numbers p such that p+2 and p+4 are also prime numbers.

2. There are three switches in one room, and three light bulbs in the other. Each switch serves one of the lamps. How do you know which switch is connected to which light bulb if you can only enter the room with the light bulbs once?

3. One four-digit number is made up of consecutive digits arranged in ascending order, the second number is made up of the same digits but in descending order, and the third four-digit number is also made up of these four digits. What are these numbers if their sum is 12,300?

4. Inside the square is a replacement square. The vertices of the squares are connected as shown in figure. Prove that the sum of the areas of the blue quadrilaterals is equal to the sum of the areas of the red quadrilaterals.

5. The cooperative bought a batch of goods and sold them with a profit of m rubles. With the proceeds, another batch of the same goods was purchased and sold at the same prices; In this case, the income amounted to n rubles. How much did you pay for the first shipment of goods?

These tasks were suggested to us by I. Klumova, A. Zilberman, A. Abdurakhmanov, V. Proizvolov, the last task was taken from the book by N. A. Shaposhnikov and N. K. Valtsev “Collection of problems in mathematics” (M., Uchredtsz, 1931)
