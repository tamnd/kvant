Figures 1-4 show four 10x10 squares, in which some cells have been removed - “forbidden stops” (crossed out with a red cross) and “turns” (how a turn is carried out in such cells is shown in figure at the top left).
For each square on figure there is a closed non-self-intersecting polyline, following along which it can be bypassed by moving a chess rook, raising in each free cell, without entering any of the “forbidden stops” cells and turning, according to the rules, in each “turn” cell.
L. Mochalov
