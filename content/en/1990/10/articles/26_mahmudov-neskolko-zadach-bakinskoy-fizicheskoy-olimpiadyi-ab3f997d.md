⟦folio 72⟧

Several tasks of the Baku Physics Olympiad

1. A ball freely falls from a height of $H = 120$ m on a horizontal plane and is reflected from it many times. With each bounce, the speed of the ball decreases by $n = 2$ times. Plot a graph of speed versus time and find the distance traveled by the ball from the beginning of its fall to its stop. (9 grades)

2. A piece of ice with a mass of $m_2 = 5$ kg and a temperature of $t_2 = -40^\circ$ C is placed in a calorimeter containing $m_1 = 2$ kg of water at a temperature of $t_1 = +5^\circ$ C. Determine the temperature and volume of the contents of the calorimeter after establishing thermal equilibrium. Heat capacity calorimeter and heat exchange with the external environment. Specific heat of water $c_1 = 4,2$ kJ/(kg K), ice $c_2 = 2,1$ kJ/(kg K), specific heat of ice $\lambda = 0,33$ MJ/kg, density of water $\rho_1 = 10^3$ kg/m³, ice $\rho_2 = 0,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m³. (10 grades)

3. The light source is placed at a distance of $a = 5$ m from the screen, on which, with the help of a collecting lens, a selection of the source increased by $\Gamma = 4$ times is obtained. Then the screen is moved to a distance of $b = 4$ m. The clarity of the enlarged selection can be restored in two ways: 1) only the lens is moved; 2) only the source is moved. What will be the increase in election in general cases? (11th grade)
