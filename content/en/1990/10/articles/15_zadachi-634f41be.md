⟦folio 43⟧

Tasks
1. From all ten digits 0, 1, 2, ..., 9, make a tithe number such that the number from its first two digits is divisible by 2, from the first three digits - by 3, and so on until the number itself is divisible by 10.
A. Shvetsov
2. Prove that a) the number $1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdots \cdot 1987 \cdot 1989 + 2 \cdot 4 \times 6 \cdots \cdot 1988 \cdot 1990$ is divisible by 1991;
6) the number $2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 \cdots \cdots \cdot 1990 \cdot 1992 - 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \times 7 \cdots \cdot 1989 \cdot 1991$ is divisible by 1993.
B. Provozvolov
3. 1990 books were brought to three matazins. In the first three, Matazin sold 1/37, 1/11 and 1/2 of the books received, the second Matazin - 1/57, 1/9 and 1/3 of the books received, the third Matazin - 1/25, 1/30 and 1/10 of the books received. How many books did each matazin receive?
L. Kurlyandchik

4. Find the smallest natural number that ends in 56, is divisible by 56, and has a sum of digits equal to 56.
C. Lip
5. The pieces are arranged on the chessboard so that there are at least two pieces on each horizontal and vertical line. Is it always possible to remove several pieces from the dock so that there is exactly one piece left on each horizontal and vertical line? Investigate the same question in the case where there are initially exactly two figures on each horizontal and vertical line.
B. Provozvolov
6. There are 9 numbers in the cells of the square $3 \times 3$. Such a square is called magic if the sum of the numbers on each vertical and on each of the two diagonals is the same. Prove that in a magic square, the sum of the squares of the numbers in the top row is equal to the sum of the squares of the numbers in the bottom row.
A. Shvetsov
