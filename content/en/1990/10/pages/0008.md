Regular structures observed in “laboratory” seaweeds.

Law (1) practically exactly coincides with the law of dispersion of parametric waves. The experimenter takes his hat off to Theory!

From the proposed qualitative theory of cellular wave structures it follows that when changing experimental conditions, one-dimensional, square and hexagonal cells should be obtained successively. Experiments exactly confirmed this theoretical conclusion.

And I would like to mention one more coincidence (?). Let's look carefully at some facts from the described strong earthquakes.*)

“The wooden frames from the wells flew up like corks, the water followed them - up to three sahens in height...; water was thrown out of the ice holes...; The ice on the lakes was cracked, and water with silt and jackdaws flew up from the cracks.” (1862, Tsagan earthquake.)

“From the well, water sprayed out like a fountain...; The employees of the steamship, which was 30 miles from the shore, saw a column of water in the sea.” (1895, Red Road earthquake.)

Many earthquakes were accompanied by the appearance of high fountains (up to 5-8 m) of water with sand and silt, splashing out of water from pools and barrels, ink from inkwells, oil from lamps, and even the dead from graves. Apart from the last, exciting fact (1868, Peru), the remaining events clearly demonstrate a parametric increase in fluid fluctuations during certain seismic fluctuations of the day.

If you pay attention to the descriptions of sea movements, which mention “unusual bouncing waves”, “countries of movement of many waves”, “pushing waves”, “ripples”, “columns of water” or the appearance of three (!) large waves around the ship (1868, California; 1878, Chile), then you can see a fairly clear analogy with the cells of the wave structure in the experiment and even if you want to see signs of hexagonal symmetry.
