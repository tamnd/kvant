V Scientific and Technical Conference of SCHOOLCHILDREN
The fifth weighty Scientific and Technical Conference of SCHOOLCHILDREN was held from May 4 to 8, 1990 at the international youth base of the Komsomol Central Committee “Olympets”. As before, the conference was organized by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, and the sponsors were the Children's Fund named after. V.I. Lenina, All-Union Youth Aerospace Society "Soyuz" and the editorial board of the magazine "Young Technical". About 150 schoolchildren from all over the Soviet Union came to the conference. They spoke in 6 sections: mathematics, physics, biophysics and molecular physics, aerospace physics, programming, computer science and computational mathematics. The seventh section - young inventors - was held by the editors of the magazine "Young Technical". The conference participants made interesting excursions around Moscow, to the Mission Control Center, and to the basic departments of MIPT. In the evenings, schoolchildren met with the editorial boards of the magazines «Kvant» and "Young Technical", and creative teams from MIPT and gifted children invited by the Children's Fund performed in front of them. All sections of the conference were awarded diplomas and various recognitions.

Now, as usual, we are waiting for your works at the VI Scientific and Technical Conference of SCHOOLCHILDREN, which will be held in April - May 1991. Any schoolchild or group of schoolchildren can take part in the conference. To do this, it is necessary to consider any question in physics, mathematics, computer science, or conducting experiments of interest, or create a computer program. The organizing committee encourages independent research among schoolchildren.

The work is drawn up in the form of an abstract (7-25, typewritten pages if possible), one copy of which must be sent no later than February 1, 1991 to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, Organizing Committee of the VI Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren. On the first page of the abstract you must indicate the author's last name, first name, patronymic, class and school, as well as your full home address. The abstract text will not be returned, so be sure to keep a copy for yourself.

All received abstracts are decided by specialists, the authors of the best works receive invitations to speak at the HTKSh.

Additional information can be obtained by calling ZFTSH at MIPT: (095) 408-51-45.

Member of the organizing committee of HTKSH
