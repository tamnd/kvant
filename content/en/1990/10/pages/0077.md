5. Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be the written numbers, $S=a_1+\dots+a_n$. From the conditions of the problem it follows that $2=a_1(S-a_1)+a_2(S-a_2)+\dots+a_n(S-a_n)$.

Suppose $S-a_k \geqslant \sqrt{2}$ for all $k=1, 2, \dots, n$. Then $2 \geqslant a_1 \cdot \sqrt{2}+a_2 \cdot \sqrt{2}+\dots+a_n \cdot \sqrt{2}=\sqrt{2} \cdot S$, i.e. $\sqrt{2} \geqslant S$. On the other hand, $S>S-a_1 \geqslant \sqrt{2}$. Contradiction.
