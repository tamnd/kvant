List of topics recommended for development by participants in the scientific stage of the International Competition “Together to Mars” (“KVM-91”)

Direction No. 1. Scientific research program.
1. Projects of scientific experiments carried out using automatic and manned spacecraft in order to obtain new data in the field of astronomy, planetary science, physics, chemistry, space biology, medicine and other sciences.
2. Equipment for conducting scientific experiments, modes of operation of the equipment and the role of the astronaut-researcher when conducting experiments.

Direction No. 2. Concepts for solving the problem of flights to Mars, flight plans.
1. Methods and means for carrying out the flight to Mars of automatic spacecraft and a rafted complex.
2. Methods for forming the Mars Expeditionary Complex (MEC) in low-Earth orbit.
3. IEC flight plans.
4. Choosing a rational MEC propulsion system is the key to solving the problem of human flight to Mars.
5. Programs for preparing and conducting a rafted experiment on Mars.
6. The Moon as a testing ground and launching pad for MEC.

Direction No. 3. Structural and layout solutions.
1. The Martian experimental complex as a whole.
2. Residential module.
3. Landing apparatus.
4. Energy, scientific and other models and blocks included in the IEC.
5. Acceleration-deceleration rocket stages MEC.
6. Devices that provide MEC braking in the atmospheres of Earth and Mars.
7. Assembly and installation orbital center.
8. Mars rover and other means of transportation on the surface of Mars.
9. Automatic spacecraft for exploring Mars and its satellites.

Direction No. 4. Onboard system services.
1. Power plant of the interplane spacecraft and landing apparatus.
2. Crew life support system.
3. Temperature maintenance system.

4. Radio and television systems operating on board the MEC and on the surface of Mars.
5. Means providing radiation protection for the crew.
6. Means of anti-meteor protection for structures.
7. Systems for protecting astronauts from chemical and other contamination on the surface of Mars and Earth are microorganisms from another world.

Direction No. 5. Artificial softness on board the spacecraft.
1. Structural and layout solutions of the IEC when creating artificial heaviness in the compartments of a residential model.
2. Methods and means to ensure the prevention of undesirable consequences of a person’s prolonged stay in conditions of non-suction.
3. Ensuring the operation of the main propulsion system and onboard MEC systems when creating artificial gravity in the residential compartments of the complex.

Direction No. 6. Ernonomics (man in space).
1. Preparing the crew for a long flight.
