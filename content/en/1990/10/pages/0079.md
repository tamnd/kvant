Let us agree to number the rows of the table and the elements of the columns from top to bottom, and the places in the rows - from left to right. Let's select the row with the smallest sum of elements and put it in first place.

After applying this operation, the sum of the elements of the first row will remain, very small, among the sums of elements by row, and the product $P$ to obtain the table will not be notified (proof!).

In this case, the amount $S_1$ will remain the smallest, and the number $P$ will not be notified. We will do the same with a table made up of the remaining rows, and so on. As a result, we will arrive at the required table, for which the number $P$ will not exceed the first initial value.
