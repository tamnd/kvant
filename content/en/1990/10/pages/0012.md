6) At what $x$ is the minimum of a similar function with four numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{2k}$ achieved?

Discrete Signal Processing

Consider a sequence of numbers that is infinite in both directions
$...x_{-2}, x_{-1}, x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$

Let us fix the natural number $k$. We will say that the new sequence $...y_{-2}, y_{-1}, y_0, y_1, y_2, \dots$ is obtained from the old median filtering if
$y_n = m(x_{n-k}, x_{n-k+1}, \dots, x_n, \dots, x_{n+k})$. The number $2k+1$ is called the width of the filter window.

The terms “filtering”, “useful signal”, “noise” came to statistics from radio engineering, where one of the most important tasks in the pre-war years was the creation of filters that evaluate sound signals from constructed noise.

In the example we considered of measuring the temperature in a window, the “noise” will be abnormally large values. Applying a median filter with a minimum window width of 3 results in the following series of numbers:
