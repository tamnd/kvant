We have patented these turns and can prove to anyone that when the winding is energized, the weight of the object in the immediate vicinity of these turns becomes less...
- But only by a very small fraction less. And we don't know what causes it. Nobody shows the slightest interest in this issue - after all, we are talking about a small reduction in an increasingly clumsy model, clearly not enough to lift a current generator into the air. All these scientists, immersed in huge problems, didn’t give a damn about the discovery of some crazy person who managed to find a small error in Newton’s law.
- Do you think they'll do it now? - asked the young man, nervously wringing his fingers.
- Of course they will. The tensile strength of the string is selected so that it will be equal every time you try to lift the full weight of the model on it. But it holds up the model with that slight reduction in weight caused by the action of the coils. This will challenge them. No one is forcing them to deal with this problem or solve it. However, the very existence of such a discrepancy will constantly torment scientists, or they know that this effect is opposed by all law and simply cannot exist. They will immediately understand that our magnetic theory is complete nonsense. Or maybe it's not nonsense? We don't know. But they will constantly think about it and rack their brains. Some people start experimenting in their basement—just a hobby, nothing more—to find an explanation. And someday one of them will find what causes the action of these coils, and maybe they will be able to improve them!
— And all the patents are in our hands...
- Exactly. They will engage in research that will drive the problems of jet propulsion with its monstrous energy costs out of their heads and open up the horizons of real space flights before them.
“And then we will become rich - as soon as it comes to industrial production,” said the young man with a cynical smile.
“We’ll all get rich, son,” the elderly man patted him on the shoulder. “Believe me, in ten years you won’t recognize this old world.”
