Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. TAVACHNIKOV

My daughter Masha is seven and a half years old and is in second grade. Masha loves to talk about mathematics. Usually, she and I solve problems like those published in «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren.” A few days ago, she and I had a rather serious conversation. We went to a sports school (Masha plays tennis), and she told me that they were studying the Russian language.
- Guess what is more: words or endings? - asked Masha.
“Of course, words, because different words have the same endings,” I answered (let me remind you that words also have “empty” endings).
And then it seemed to me that it was time to talk about mathematics.
