17(7). A square (Fig. 9) is soldered from a wire $l=2$ m long, with a resistance $R=4$ Ohm. Sources with EMF $E_1=10$ V and $E_2=8$ V are connected to two sides of the square. The circuit is placed in a uniform magnetic field, perpendicular to the plane square and directed beyond the drawing. The magnetic field induction uniformly increases by $\Delta B=16$ T every second (i.e., it changes according to the law $B=kt$). Determine the size of the resulting rods located at the same level, there is a conductive jumper with mass $m$ (Fig. 10). Friction coefficient between rods and lintel $μ$. The rods are connected to a resistor with a resistance of $R$. The resistances of the rods and jumpers are negligible. The system is in a magnetic field, the induction of which is directed vertically upward and varies uniformly over time according to the law $B=At$ (A has the dimension T/c). Determine the direction of the induction current. Find the magnitude of the resulting induced emf at the moments of time when the jumper accumulated. At what point does the jumper begin to move along the rod? The geometric dimensions $l$ and $h$ are known.

Oscillations and waves

1(9). Two mathematical matrices with periods of oscillations $T_1=6$ s and $T_2=5$ s, respectively, simultaneously with the initial oscillation in the same phases. After what is the shortest time, the phases of their oscillations will be the same again?

2(15). The hanging outer body had a period of vertical oscillations $T_1$. An increase in its mass changed the oscillation period to a value of $T_2$. Calculate the mixed equilibrium positions of the end of the spring under the action of the body after increasing its mass.

3(3). In the elevator cabin, a ball hangs on the length of a thread. When the cabin is stationary, the period of its oscillations is $T=1$ s. In a cabin moving with constant acceleration, the period of oscillation is $T'=1,2$ s. Determine the magnitude and direction of cabin acceleration.

4(10). The spiral spring, under the action of a load suspended from it, was stretched by $x=6,5$ cm. If the load is pulled down and then lowered, then it begins to oscillate along the vertical axis. Determine the period of such oscillations.

5(3). The stand performs a harmonious oscillation in the vertical direction with an amplitude of $A=0,5$ m (Fig. 11). What should be the shortest period of oscillation so that the object lying on the stand does not get rid of it?

6(9). A $C_1=200$ µF capacitor is connected to an AC target with a frequency of $v_1=60$ Hz, and a $C_2=300$ µF capacitor is connected to an AC target with a frequency of $v_2=50$ Hz. Find the ratio of the capacitance of the first capacitor to the capacitance of the second capacitor.

7(10). The electrical capacity of the counter is $C=300$ pF. What must be the inductance of the counter so that it resonates at the frequency of electromagnetic oscillations $v=10^6$ Hz?

8(3). What is the wavelength of the electromagnetic radiation of an oscillating contraption having a capacitor with a capacity of $C=2$ pF, if an induced emf of $E=0,04$ V appears in it? The rate of change of current in the inductor is $\Delta I/\Delta t=4$ A/c.
