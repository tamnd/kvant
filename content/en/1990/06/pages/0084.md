Today we offer you tasks on cutting star polygons. In general, in puzzles of this kind, you need to cut a given figure into pieces, from which you can put together another specified figure. (This is always possible for two equal-sized polygons.) However, we have already done the first part of the work - cutting. But those who like especially difficult tasks can look for the necessary cutting on their own, copying only the contours of these figures.
1. Convert a 12-pointed star made of 24 elements (Fig. 1) into 3 smaller stars of the same size and shape.

2. From 5 pieces of a 6-pointed star (Fig. 2) you need to form a triangle. Apparently, it is impossible to cut a star into a smaller number of parts so that they form a triangle.

3. A regular 10-pointed star (Fig. 3) needs to be turned into a regular 10-pointed star of a different shape.

4. The star, consisting of 7 parts (Fig. 4), must be rebuilt into a Latin cross. (It is formed if the same squares are placed on those sides of a 1×1 square, and a rectangle 1×2 is placed on the fourth side.)
