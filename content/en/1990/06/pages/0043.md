9. In one of the vessels with water there is a piece of ice floating with an air bubble, in the other there is a frozen steel ball. Do water levels in vessels change when the ice melts?
10. One of the bottles is filled with water, the other with rue. Will the first bottle sink if placed in water? Will the second one sink if it is lowered into the river?
11. What will happen to the water level in a pool if a stone is thrown into it from a boat floating in it?
12. A wooden cube lies at the bottom of a dry vessel. Will it float if there is water in the vessel?
