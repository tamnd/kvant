(fantasy story)
D. KEEZ (USA)

Apr 20 I feel very bad. It’s not that I need a doctor, but my chest is somehow empty, as if my insides have been knocked out, and besides, I also have heartburn.

I wasn't going to write about this, but I think it should be done anyway, because it's important. Today for the first time I did not go to work and stayed at home.

Last night Joe Kerp and Frank Reilly invited me to a party. There were a lot of girls there and a few guys from the factory. I remembered how bad I felt the last time I drank too much, so I told Joe I didn't want to drink anything. Instead of alcohol, he gave me straight Coca-Cola. It tasted strange, but I thought it was just a bad taste in my mouth.

At first we had a lot of fun. Joe said I should dance with Ellyn and she would show me different steps. I fell several times and could not understand why, because besides me, Ellinnik no longer danced. And every now and then I would trip because someone would always pull out their leg.

When I stood up, I saw such an expression on Joe's face that I felt something strange in my stomach.

“He can just make things difficult,” said one of the girls.

Everyone burst out laughing.

“I haven’t laughed so hard since that evening at Muggsy’s when we sent him for the newspaper and ran away,” said Frank.

- No, just look at him. What a red horn he has.

- He's blushing. Charlie blushes.

- Hey, Ellin, what did you do to Charlie? I've never seen him like this.

I didn’t know what to do, where to put myself. Everyone was looking at me and laughing, and I felt like I was standing naked. I wanted to hide somewhere. I ran outside and vomited. Then I went home. It's strange how I never noticed that Joe, Frank and the others always liked to tag along with me in order to get rid of me. TV, I understand what it means when they say “take down Charlie Gordon”!

I can eat it.

10. Provenance report

April 21. I still haven't gone to work. I asked Mrs. Flynn, my landlady, to call the factory and tell Mr. Donnegan that I was sick. Lately Mrs. Flynn has been looking at me very strangely, as if she were afraid of me.

I think it’s good that I realized how everyone would laugh at me. I've thought about this a lot. This is because I’m so underweight and I don’t even notice when I do stupid things. People think it's funny when a stupid person can't do everything the way they do.

In any case, now I already understand that every day I became smarter. I know punctuation marks and can write correctly. I like to look up difficult words in the dictionary and remember them. I read a lot on TV, and Miss Kinnen says that I read very well. Sometimes I even understand what I read about, and it remains in my memory.

Miss Kinnen said that in addition to history, geography and arguments, I will learn foreign languages. Dr. Strauss gave me some new tapes to put on before I went to bed.

Today I feel much better, but I still seem to be a little angry at people for always making fun of me and making fun of me because I was stupid.
