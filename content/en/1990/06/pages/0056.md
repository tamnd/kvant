National Mathematical Union to create a premium fund in mathematics.
It was decided to award the new prize and gold medal to young mathematicians who were under 40 years old at the time of loading, for results that had the greatest impact on the development of mathematics. Unlike the Novel Prizes, which are awarded annually, the Field Prizes are awarded every four years at international mathematical congresses. The size of the prize is small - 1,500 Canadian dollars, but its prestige in the mathematical world is enormous. The first presentation of the Fields Prizes took place in 1936 at the Mathematics
