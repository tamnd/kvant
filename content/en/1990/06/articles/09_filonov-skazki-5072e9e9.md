Point of view on the plane
There were many lines in the plane - straight, curved, and broken. And everyone had their own points of view on the world.
“Everything in the world is divided into the sublime and the humiliated,” the truth was blunt.
“No,” the other one cut off, “everything in the world is either right or wrong.”
“Don’t fight, girls,” bend smoothly,

interfering with the curve - everything in the world is dialectal: here you are right, but here you are humiliated.
The broken ones were embarrassed to express their opinions to me, but the circle formulated it like this:
- The whole world - either internal or the rest. Well, I’m not interested in talking about the rest, then the inner world is very rich. Only those as comprehensive as me...
“Flat lines chatter so much,” the spiral interrupted her, “and as for the world, it is just a layer between the turns that they don’t get caught up in.”
And he could not cope with the change in a small, imperceptible point - the only common point of the plane and the line that passed outside this plane.

Universal gravity
Previously, the Earth did not have satellites. And when the satellite appeared, it turned out to be a small, defenseless Moon.
And every meteor, every speck of cosmic dust hurt her - after all, the Moon does not have an atmosphere that could protect her.
