Problem 2. Points A and B are located on the river, with B downstream at a distance of 20 km from A. The boat goes from A to B, then immediately returns to A and again goes to B. At the same time, a raft is sent by boat from A. While returning from B, the boat met a raft 4 km from A. At what distance from A did the boat find a raft heading to B?

Solution. Note that the boat moves away from the raft or approaches it at the same speed - its speed relative to the water. Therefore, the time that the boat swam from A to B, moving away from the raft, is equal to the time that the boat swam from B before meeting the raft. This means that the ratio of the paths taken by the boat from A to B and from B to the raft is equal to the ratio of its speed along and against the current, i.e. the ratio of the speed is 20/16 = 5/4. The same messages will refer to the path taken by the boat from A to the second meeting with the raft and from the first meeting to A. Thus, the boat will catch up with the raft 5 km from A.

Problem 3. Points A and B are located on the river. At the same time, two identical boats pass each other from these points, meet at some point, exchange almost and return back. A boat leaving A returns back 1 hour after leaving. If the boat departing from A were to leave for 15 minutes of the work of the boat departing from B, then the meeting would take place at an equal distance from the common points. How long does it take for the boat to return from point B?

Solution. Note that the moment the boat returns to A is completely determined only by the moment the boat leaves B, just as the return of the boat to B is determined by the moment the boat leaves A. To understand this, it is enough to imagine that at the meeting point they almost do not exchange, but continue moving to the opposite point. Consequently, the second time the boat leaving from A would return back 1 hour 15 minutes after leaving, i.e. halfway from A to B and back it needs 1 hour 15 minutes, and 2 hours 30 minutes the whole way. Thus, the boat leaving B returns back after 1 hour 30 minutes.

Problem 5. Five noble fish were fishing. At the end of the first one's fishing, it seemed that he had appeared more than the others, and he divided equally 1/3 of his addition between them. Problem 6. There are three pipelines in the port for loading tankers. The first of them pumps 300 tons of oil per hour, the second - 400 tons, and the third - 500 tons. We need to load two tankers. If loading is carried out first using two pipelines, connecting the first pipeline to one of the tanks, and the second pipeline to the other tanker, then loading of common tankers with the fastest of two possible connection methods will take 12 hours. In this case, one of the tankers may be filled early, and then the pipeline connected to it is turned off and will not be used for further loading. If the smaller tanker's totality were larger than it actually is, and loading was carried out by the second and third pipelines, then with the fast connection method, loading would take 14 hours. Determine how many tons of oil each tanker contains.

Solution. Obviously, a more productive pipeline should be connected to a tanker with greater integrity. Since one of the two tankers was filled exactly in 12 hours, then either the time is less than 12·300=3600 tons of oil, or more than 12·400=4800 tons. The first case is impossible, since when doubling the capacity of a smaller tanker, we get 7200 tons, and to fill such a tanker even with a third pipeline requires more than 14 hours. Consequently, the larger tanker totals 4800 tons and is filled with the second and, moreover, the third pipeline in less than 14 hours. This means that the tanker is less than $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 14 \cdot 500 = 3500$ tons.

Problem 7. The message about the flat cross-country says that the percentage of the number of group members willing to participate in the cross-country ranges from 96.8% to 97.2%. Determine the minimum possible number of group members.

Solution. The percentage of those who did not participate in the cross ranged from 2.8% to 3.2%. If 1 person did not participate in the cross-country (it is no longer possible to have less), then the number of group members would range from $1 \cdot \frac{100}{2,8} = 35,7$... to $1 \cdot \frac{100}{3,2} = 31,2$... i.e. the minimum number of group members would be 32 people. It is clear that with a smaller number of group members, 3.2% of this number will be less than 1, and according to the condition, at least one person did not participate in the cross.

1. The vegetable base contained winged mushrooms, the humidity of which was 99%. During storage, its humidity decreased by 1% (it became 98%). By what percentage has the storage mass of the fender base decreased?

2. A car travels from $A$ to $B$ in 1 hour. The car left $A$ and at the same time a pedestrian exited $B$. The car met the pedestrian, took it to $A$ and then landed at $B$, spending 2 hours 40 minutes the whole way. How long does it take for a pedestrian to go all the way from $B$ to $A$?

3. The motor ship travels from $A$ to $B$ in 3 hours, and returns back in 4 hours. How long will it take the raft to travel from $A$ to $B$ at the speed of the current?

4. A train traveling from point $A$ to point $B$ makes several stops along the way. At the first stop, 5 passengers board the train, and at each subsequent stop, 10 more passengers. At each stop, 5 passengers get off the train. Is it possible that point $B$ brings in less than 336 passengers if point $A$ leaves 462 of them?

5. The report on flat cross-country said that the percentage of the number of cross-country participants that did not catch

6. The bus on the way from $A$ to $B$ makes 5 stops of 10 minutes every 16 km (the distance from $A$ to $B$ is 96 km), the speed of the bus is 65 km/h. At the same time as the bus, a bicycle comes out from $B$ towards it at a speed of 21 km/h. At what distance from $A$ will the bus meet the bicycle?

7. Passenger train in 6 seconds. How long will it take the fast and passenger trains to pass each other if the speed of the fast train is 3/2 times the speed of the passenger train, and the speed of the passenger train is 4/3 times the length of the fast train?

8. The work began between 9 and 10 a.m. and ended between 3 and 4 p.m. the same day. Determine the duration of the work if the minute and hour hands were perpendicular at the start and end of the work.

9. One worker can produce a batch of parts in 12 hours. One worker began the work, after 1 hour a second one joined him, after another hour - a third, etc., until the work was completed. How long did the first worker work? (The labor productivity of all workers is the same.)

10. There are three slabs weighing 2, 3 and 5 kg with different copper contents. Each strand is divided into three parts, and from the ninth pieces obtained, three strands weighing 2, 3 and 5 kg with comparable copper content are obtained. When rolling parts, should the original slabs be separated to ensure an equal percentage of copper in the resulting ingots, regardless of its content in the original slabs?

11. Three schoolchildren share nuts among themselves. First, the first student divides each of the other two one-quarter of the nuts he has and another half of a nut. Then the second to each of the other two, one quarter of the nuts he had and another half of a nut. Then a third student also followed. As a result, each of them ended up with 30 nuts. How many nuts did each elementary school student have?

12. There are two vessels. One contains 3 liters of 100% acid, and the other 2 liters of water. One glass of acid was poured from the first vessel into the second, and then one glass of the mixture was poured from the second into the first. This operation was repeated three more times. As a result, the second vessel contained acid with a strength of 42%. What percentage of acid is now contained in the first vessel?

At regular intervals, the rower separated for 10 minutes (at this time the boat is moving with the current), and in total there were 8 such breaks. After how long did the rower return to $A$?
