⟦folio 67⟧

Moscow Aviation Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze

Mathematics
Written exam

Option 1

1. The sixth term of an arithmetic progression is 10, and the sum of the first sixteen terms of this progression is 200. Find the twelfth term of the progression.

2. Solve the equation

$$\text{tg} x + \text{tg} \left( x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = 1.$$

3. Find the most and most possible values ​​of the area of ​​a parallelogram, the product of the length of two unequal heights of which is equal to 9, and the values ​​of the axial angle of the parallelogram are not less than 30° and not more than 45°.

4. Solve the inequality

$$\frac{\log_3(5x+1)}{\log_3(7x-1)} \leq 1.$$

5. Find all rational values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the functions

$$\sin \left( \frac{2ax}{a^2+\sqrt{12}} \right) \text{ и } \text{tg} \left( \frac{2x}{1-2a+\sqrt{108}} \right)$$

have the same periods.

6. The vertex of the cone lies in the plane of the base ABCD equal to the quadrangular area SABCD, and the circumference of the base of the cone is a quadrilateral, resulting from the section of the area by a plane passing through the middle sides AD and BC of the base of the area and dividing the edge SC in the ratio 3:1, counting from the vertex S. Find the ratio of the volume of the cone to the volume of the area.

Option 2

1. On the segment [-2; 2] find the most value of an arbitrary function

$$y = -\frac{1}{3} x^3 + x + 1.$$

2. The number $a$ is given. Find $\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha \right) \times \sin \left( \frac{3\pi}{4} - \alpha \right)$ if you know that $\sin \alpha \left( 1 - 2\sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) = a.$

3. Solve the inequality

$$\frac{\log_2 \left( \frac{x}{2} \right)}{\log_2 x} - \frac{\log_2 x^2}{\log_2 x - 1} \leq 1.$$

4. The lateral sides AB and CD of the trapezoid are extended until they intersect at point E. Point O is the center of the circumscribed circle of triangle ADE. Find the values ​​of the acute angle A of the trapezoid if it is known that points A, B, C, D, O lie on a circle whose radius is $\sqrt{3}$ times less than the radius of the circle circumscribed about triangle ADE.

5. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{35-2\sqrt{45-2x}} = x-5.$$

6. In the regular triangular paramid SABC is a vissana sphere. The ratio of the base area of ​​the ABC paramid to the surface area of ​​the sphere is $q$. A plane parallel to the base of the paramid touches the sphere and cuts off the paramid $SA_1B_1C_1$ from the paramid SABC. Find the ratio of the surface areas of paramides $SABC$ and $SA_1B_1C_1$.

Physics

Written exam

Option 1

1. A material point on a plane makes a movement that would go in the form

$$x = a \cos \omega t,$$

$$y = b \cos (\omega t + \varphi_0),$$

where $x$ and $y$ are the coordinates of the point at time $t$, $a=4$ m, $b=8$ m, $\varphi_0=\pi$, $\omega=\pi c^{-1}$. Determine the trajectory of the point.

2. On an inclined plane forming an angle $a$ with the horizontal plane, there lies a block of mass $M$, which is kept from sliding by a stop. A bullet of mass $m$, LET, moving at a speed $v$ and entering on an inclined plane, hits the block and gets stuck in it. How long will it take for the block to return to its original position? Friction coefficient between the block and the inclined plane $\mu$.

3. An ideal gas slowly transfers from a state with a volume of $V_1=32$ l and a pressure of $p_1=4,1 \cdot 10^5$ Pa to a state with a volume of $V_2=9$ l and a pressure of $p_2=15,5 \cdot 10^5$ Pa so that during rolling the pressure changes depending on the volume according to the linear law $p=aV+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. At what volume will the temperature of the gas in this process be greatest?
