Novosibirsk State University named after. Lenin Komsomol

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics, Mathematics and Economics)

1. A fast train left from point A to point B. At the same time, a freight train left B to A to meet him. After 5 hours and 20 minutes they met. At point B, the fast train arrived 8 hours earlier than the freight train at A. How long was the journey for each train?

2. Solve the equation
$$2tg\left(\frac{\pi}{4} - x\right) = 3tg2x + 5.$$

3. The lengths of the bases AD and BC of the trapezoid ABCD are 9 and 3, respectively. Point E is the midpoint of the lateral side AB, point F is the midpoint of CD. The bissertis of angle BAD intersects the midline EF at point P, and the bissertis of angle ADC intersects at point Q. The lengths of the segments EQ, PQ and PF are equal. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

4. Find all values ​​of the parameter a for which the equation
$$|a - 2x| + 1 = |x + 3|$$
has a unique solution.

5. Given a cube with base ABCD and side edges AA', BB', CC', DD'. The length of an edge of a cube is equal to one. A plane is drawn through line B'C, intersecting edge AB and making an angle of 60° with line A'B. In what ratio does this plane divide edge AB?

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)

1. Find all five-digit numbers of the form 67m 1n (m and n are digits) that are divisible by 36.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2}sinx = \sqrt{5cosx - 1}.$$

3. One of the angles of the triangle is 60°, the radius of the circumscribed circle is 7/√3, and the radius of the inscribed circle is √3. Find the area of ​​the triangle.

4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{|\sin x|}(x^2 - 14x + 73) > \frac{2}{\log_5|\sin x|}.$$

5. Given a cube with base ABCD and side edges AA', BB', CC', DD'. The lengths of all edges of the cube are equal to one. Points M and N are the midpoints of CD and CC', respectively. Find the distance between lines AN and BM.

Option 3
(faculties of natural sciences and geological-geocalculus)

1. There are two alloys, one containing 20% ​​and the other 30% tin. How much do you need to take the first and second alloy to make 10 kg of a new alloy containing 27% tin?

2. Solve the equation
$$4tgx + 2ctgx = \frac{5}{\cos x}.$$

3. In an triangle ABC, the lengths of the medians BM, CN and the heights AH are 4, 5 and 6, respectively. Find the area of ​​the triangle.

4. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{21x + 16} - \sqrt{x - 4} < 20.$$

5. At the base of the quadrilateral line SABCD lies the rectangle ABCD on the side AB=4, BC=2. The lengths of all side edges are equal to 3, point M is the middle of AS. A plane is drawn through straight line BM parallel to diagonal AC. Determine the angle between this plane and the SAC plane.

Physics
Written exam
Faculty of Physics

Each one makes a fortune out of three types of problems.

The first three tasks are computational, of varying difficulty: from standard ones to relatively complex ones, requiring ingenuity, deep knowledge, and the ability to understand an inappropriate or conditional physical situation.

The fourth task is an assessment task. To solve it, you need to understand the physical phenomenon under consideration, which forms a simple (since only an assessment is needed) physical model of this phenomenon, select reasonable numerical values ​​of physical quantities, and finally obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. The text of the problem emphasized that the applicant could choose the quantities necessary to solve the problem and their numerical values.

The fifth task is a demonstration task in which you need to explain a physical phenomenon demonstrated in the classroom. Here it is important to understand the essential phenomena and highlight the main one among the various factors.

Five hours were given to solve the problem, starting with the completion of the demonstration.

After the text of each problem, the process of those who solved it is indicated in parentheses.
