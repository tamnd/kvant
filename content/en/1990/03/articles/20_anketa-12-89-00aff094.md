This page shows how to make a model of a “superbolic parabolic”, the general view of which is presented on the 1st page. covers. This mudflow-like surface is formed, surprisingly, by two intersecting families of straight lines. Moreover, the lattice of generatrices of the straight lines is movable, and it is possible to hinge the straight lines to each other at their points of intersection so that at these points they can heal, but not slide, and the lattice will still remain bendable. This wonderful property is clearly visible on our model: it can be folded and unfolded, and if you include it inside the cardboard cover, you will get a beautiful folding toy. More information about the hyperbolic parabolic can be read in the book by D. Hilbert and S. Kohn-Vossen, “Visible Geometry” (M., “Nauka”, 1981, p. 23).
