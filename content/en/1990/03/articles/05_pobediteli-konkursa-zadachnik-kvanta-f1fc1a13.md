We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The tasks published in it are standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time.

Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent no later than May 15, 1990 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant».
Solutions from different issues of the journal or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 3 - 90” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1211” or “F1218”. In the column “...sender’s address” please write your first and last name legibly. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of the decision verification).

The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you are a teacher.

Problems M1211 - M1215 were proposed last fall at the Mathematical Olympiad “Tournament of Cities”.
