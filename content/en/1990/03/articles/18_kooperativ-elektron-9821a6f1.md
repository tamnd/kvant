⟦folio 71⟧

COOPERATIVE "ELECTRON"

offers owners and users of PCs of the types DVK, UKNTs, IVM XT/AT, Sinclair Spectum, Atari, Commodore, Agat, Specialist, PK-86 32K, PK-86 64K, Mikrosha, BK-0010.01, Lvov, Partner, Vector, "Pravets-8D" has a wide selection of system, applied, game, and educational programs.

Offers training programs for classes UKNTs, KUVT-86, "Yamaxa".

Concludes an agreement with the authors for the replication of the developed immisoftware with the payment of a percentage of the sale, exchange of programs is possible.

Sells software and hardware kits and game libraries based on computers "Sinclair Spectum", "PK-86", "Specialist", DVK, electronic disks (64K-256K, with an operating system, for all types of household computers).

Address for inquiries and catalog requests: 103489, Moscow, Zelenograd, bldg. 705, cooperative "Electron". Phone: 536-12-81.
