The last word remained for “ChristIANYA” - a turn on parallel lines. Gradually improving, it firmly took the main place in the technical arsenal of a miner. At first, “ChristIANYA” was performed by sliding (Fig. 7). The inner track is unloaded, the body of the track is moved slightly forward, so the toe “holds” the snow more strongly. During the movement, the backs slide a little, and a turn is obtained. This method was faster and more reliable than the previous ones.

And then, by itself, simply and naturally, the idea of ​​a “cut” turn (Fig. 8) is directed, at which the resistance to movement is minimal. Speeds have increased, and the requirements for equipment have changed to the exact opposite. Now they make hard and high plastic boots that transfer the sleep directly from the shin to the cheek and allow it to be well edged. And the lick itself is soft, it bends in an arc and makes a turn, leaving a narrow “cut” mark. In order for the lick to easily take the shape of an arc, in the middle, where the boot presses, it is made a little narrower than at the ends (Fig. 9). When turning, the grip on the snow is maximum at the ends, and the pressure in the middle will drive the lick as desired.

It's not easy to give a good lick, because you have to kill at least two birds with one stone. Firstly, the lizard should bend gently in an arc in a continuous direction. Secondly, in order not to slip, it must be rigid “to the propeller,” i.e., resist twisting. Not to mention the fact that the spoon must maintain its direction and not bend in the plane of the slope (“saber-shaped bend”). Finding a successful combination of at least these qualities is a complex task, and the choice of a specific solution is ambiguous. For slam, where the expressions are steeper and the speeds are less, sportsstems take shorter and more pliable licks than for giant slam. The weather conditions and the quality of the snow have their own requirements. Therefore, literally before the start, the future champion decides which of the licks prepared the day before will serve him today.
