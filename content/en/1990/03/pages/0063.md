Problem 2. Points A and B are located on the river, with B downstream at a distance of 20 km from A. The boat goes from A to B, then immediately returns to A and again goes to B. At the same time, a raft is sent by boat from A. While returning from B, the boat met a raft 4 km from A. At what distance from A did the boat find a raft heading to B?

Solution. Note that the boat moves away from the raft or approaches it at the same speed - its speed relative to the water. Therefore, the time that the boat swam from A to B, moving away from the raft, is equal to the time that the boat swam from B before meeting the raft. This means that the ratio of the paths taken by the boat from A to B and from B to the raft is equal to the ratio of its speed along and against the current, i.e. the ratio of the speed is 20/16 = 5/4. The same messages will refer to the path taken by the boat from A to the second meeting with the raft and from the first meeting to A. Thus, the boat will catch up with the raft 5 km from A.

Problem 3. Points A and B are located on the river. At the same time, two identical boats pass each other from these points, meet at some point, exchange almost and return back. A boat leaving A returns back 1 hour after leaving. If the boat departing from A were to leave for 15 minutes of the work of the boat departing from B, then the meeting would take place at an equal distance from the common points. How long does it take for the boat to return from point B?

Solution. Note that the moment the boat returns to A is completely determined only by the moment the boat leaves B, just as the return of the boat to B is determined by the moment the boat leaves A. To understand this, it is enough to imagine that at the meeting point they almost do not exchange, but continue moving to the opposite point. Consequently, the second time the boat leaving from A would return back 1 hour 15 minutes after leaving, i.e. halfway from A to B and back it needs 1 hour 15 minutes, and 2 hours 30 minutes the whole way. Thus, the boat leaving B returns back after 1 hour 30 minutes.
