V. Geodjaev

F1200. There are two identical glasses on the table, one of which is filled with hot tea. Tea temperature $t_0$. The tea needs to be cooled to a temperature of $t_k$. This can be done in two ways:

1) immediately pour the tea into a second glass and wait until it cools to a temperature of $t_k$;
2) cool until the tea cools to a certain temperature $t_1$ such that after pouring into the second glass the temperature immediately turns out to be $t_k$.

Which way is the fastest? It is known that the heat transfer of a glass is proportional to the temperature difference between the glass and the environment; heat transfer between the tea and the glass occurs very quickly. The heat capacity of a glass is $C_0$, the heat capacity of tea is $C$.

Let us denote the temperature difference between tea and the environment by $T$:

$$T = t_{\text{чай}} - t_{\text{окр.ср.}}$$

and for brevity we will call $T$ temperature. Then the equation for the change in temperature $T$ of tea with time $\tau$, according to the conditions of the problem, can be written in the form

$$dT/d\tau = -\alpha T,$$

(*)

where $\alpha > 0$ is a constant proportionality coefficient. It also follows from the conditions of the problem that if before pouring tea from one glass to another the temperature was $T$, then after pouring it will become $CT/(C+C_0) = \beta T$, where $\beta$ is a constant coefficient.

Now consider processes 1) and 2). For the first process, we write the initial condition:

$$T^{(1)}(0) = \beta T_0$$ (immediately after transfusion)

and the equation for temperature change over time:

$$dT^{(1)}/d\tau = -\alpha T^{(1)}$$.

For the second process, we introduce a new variable $T' = \beta T^{(2)}$ and write the corresponding equalities:

$$T'(0) = \beta T_0,$$

$$dT'/d\tau = \beta dT^{(2)}/d\tau = \beta (-\alpha T^{(2)}) = -\alpha T'$$.

The same equation and the same initial conditions correspond to the same solutions. Therefore, at an arbitrary moment of time $T$

$$T'(\tau) = T^{(1)}(\tau).$$

Let at some moment of time $T_k$ the tea temperature in the first process reach $T_k$, i.e.

$$T^{(1)}(\tau_k) = T_k.$$

Let's find what the temperature will be in the second process if at time $T_k$ we pour tea from one glass to another:

$$T^{(2)}(\tau_k)_{\text{после}} = \beta T^{(2)}(\tau_k)_{\text{до}} = \beta T'(\tau_k)/\beta = T'(\tau_k) = T^{(1)}(\tau_k) = T_k.$$

So, we have found that the general methods of cooling tea require the same time. Therefore, you can pour tea from one glass to another at any time in the range from 0 to $T_k$ (see figure).
