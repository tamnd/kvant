Last year, we introduced readers to several models of multi-fold toys, which were made according to the classical law of the Japanese art of origami - that is, from one square sheet of paper without scissors and glue (see page 4 of the cover «Kvant» No. 7, 8, 9). If you relax these restrictions, you can model the horizon, more noticeable figures and even covered surfaces. This page shows how to make a model of a “superbolic parabolic”, the general view of which is presented on the 1st page. covers. This mudflow-like surface is formed, surprisingly, by two intersecting families of straight lines. Moreover, the lattice of generatrices of the straight lines is movable, and it is possible to hinge the straight lines to each other at their points of intersection so that at these points they can heal, but not slide, and the lattice will still remain bendable. This wonderful property is clearly visible on our model: it can be folded and unfolded, and if you include it inside the cardboard cover, you will get a beautiful folding toy. More information about the hyperbolic parabolic can be read in the book by D. Hilbert and S. Kohn-Vossen, “Visible Geometry” (M., “Nauka”, 1981, p. 23). Our model is assembled from 7 pairs of rectangular grapecias of 4 types with cuts parallel to the bases; In two paired grapecia, opposite sides are cut. You can come up with many similar folding structures, and we are announcing a competition among readers for an interesting amount. The winners will receive «Kvant» features and original puzzles. Send your work by August 1, 1990. Write on the envelope: “Folding design.”
