And we advise you: after reading the article, return to its beginning, take a closer look at the splash screen, and maybe you will see it with new eyes.

Much of the reader's wish is that the magazine arrives very late; last year, regular subscribers did not receive all the issues. This upsets us, but, unfortunately, we cannot help you in this matter; such problems will occur through no fault of ours. The heavy workload with which the Chekhov Printing Plant, which prints our magazine, works leads to a delay in the release schedule; The Ministry of Communications does not always ensure timely delivery of the magazine to subscribers. We have repeatedly called for the appropriate instructions; reading once does not change. We can only hope that it will improve over time.

Judging by the questionnaires, the range of issues that interest our readers needs to be widely copied. Publish more articles telling about modern scientific achievements, about today's problems of physics, mathematics and information scientists; wider coverage of the work of WACO; science fiction printing; provide more materials for students in grades 5-8 (in particular, in the “School in «Kvant» and in «Kvant» for younger students)); place crosswords, puzzles, and borrowed materials in the journal; talk about scientific public students;... - this is not a complete list of wishes expressed by readers.

It must be said that we have already taken many of these proposals into account. So, we plan to introduce a section from the new school year, which for now we tentatively call “For the youngest schoolchildren.” It will contain challenging tasks that require communication, ingenuity and “everyday” logic to be solved, small tasks that tell about interesting questions in mathematics and physics in the most accessible form.
