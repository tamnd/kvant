6) Let us show how, for a parallelogram with sides $a$, $b$ ($a > b$) and angle $\gamma$ between them ($\gamma \leq 90^\circ$), construct a suitable rectangle $KLMN$ (of the type shown in figure 2) with diagonal $KM = a$.

Let us describe the segment $KM$ as the diameter of a circle. From its center $Q$ we draw a segment $QP = b/2$ that makes an angle $\angle PQK = \gamma$ with the segment $KM$, and through the point $P$ we draw a line parallel to $KM$. Let $L$ be the point of intersection of this line with the circle (closest to $K$), $LE$ be a segment equal and parallel to $PQ$, $NF$ be a segment symmetrical to $LE$ relative to $Q$ (Fig. 3). By cutting off the triangles $KEL, LEM, MFN$ and $NFK$ relative to the side $KL, LM, ML$ and $NK$, we will obtain parallel sides $KE + KF = ME + MF = a$, $2LE = 2NF = b$ and an angle $\gamma$, suitable for the cardboard rectangle $KLMN$.
