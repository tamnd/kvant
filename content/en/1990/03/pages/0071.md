Mathematics
Written exam

Option 1
1. Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} 3\sqrt{(x-1)y} = y(2 + \sqrt{\frac{y}{x-1}}), \\ y^2 + xy - 5x + 7 = 0. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\sin 2x + \sin x - 1 \geq \sqrt{2} \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - x \right).$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2 \left( \frac{x-1}{4x+3} \right) = 3 + \log_{16} x^4 - \log_4 (x-3)^2.$$

4. In triangle ABC, point E belongs to median BD, and $|BE| = 3|ED|$. Line AE intersects side BC at point M. Find the ratio of the areas of triangles AMC and ABC.

5. At what positive values ​​of parameters a, b does the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_9 x^2 = b - \log_3 y, \\ (y+x)^2 = a^2 \end{cases} \text{ и } \begin{cases} \sin (x+y) = 0, \\ x^2 + y^2 = 2x + 2y + a^2 \end{cases}
$$have the same number of solutions?

Option 2
1. Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} 2x+y=7, \\ 2|x-1|+3|y+2|=17. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve the equation
$$1 + \sin 4x - \cos 4x = 2 \sin 5x \cdot \cos x.$$

3. Two circles of radii R and r touch each other externally at point A. The common tangents AD and BC to the circles intersect at point D. Prove that $|AD|^2 = R \cdot r.$

4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{x+2}(4-x) + \log_{(8+2x-x^2)}(x+2)^2 \leq 2.$$

5. Determine for what values ​​of the parameter a are all valid solutions of the equation
$$x^4 + (x+1) \left[ (3a-2)x^2 + (2a^2-a-3)(x+1) \right] = 0
$$belong to the segment $[-3; 0]$.

Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. A small body slides without initial velocity from a springboard of height H, the lower part of which is horizontal and located at a height h above the ground. Neglecting resistance forces, determine the further flight of the body. At what value of h, considering H=const, will the further be maximum and what will it be equal to?

2. An artificial satellite is launched into low-Earth orbit from a cosmodrome located at the equator. What speed relative to the Earth's surface must be imparted to the satellite when launching in the direction of the landing? in a westerly direction? in the east?

3. A body of mass m, moving with a speed v, collides with a body at rest and, after an elastic collision, rebounds from it at an angle of 90° to the original direction of motion with a speed of 2/3 v. Determine the mass of the second body.

4. From a load of mass M, the highest external rigidity k, its part with mass m was separated. To what maximum height will the remaining part of the load rise after this?

5. The ball is suspended by a length of thread. The first time it is raised to the point of suspension and released, the second time it is tilted at a small angle and also released. In what case and how many times faster will the ball return to its initial position?

6. The balloon shell with a volume of V=1000 m³ is filled with gel at an ambient temperature of t=27 °C. The total mass of the shell, cables and basket with aeronates m=500 kg. Determine the force of air resistance if the ball rises to the top with acceleration a=2.2 m/s². The relative molecular masses of helium and air are 4 and 29, respectively. The pressure of helium is equal to the atmospheric pressure p=10⁵ Pa.

7. A vessel with a volume of V = 20 liters, divided into two parts by a thin movable partition, maintains a constant temperature of t = 100 °C. On the left side of the vessel there is m_B=18 g of water, on the right - m_a=14 g of nitrogen. Determine the volume of the right side of the vessel if the pressure of saturated water vapor is p_H=10⁵ Pa. What volume would the water occupy if it evaporated completely?

8. A point source of light located at the bottom of a vessel with a liquid whose refractive index is n=1.8. How many times is the maximum time spent by light passing through a layer of liquid and then emerging into the air greater than the minimum time?

9. One of the plates of a flat air capacitor is illuminated with light with a wavelength of λ=0.5 μm. The electrons knocked out by light fall onto another capacitor. Determine the maximum amount of charge that can be obtained in this way if the area of ​​the plates is S=1000 cm², the distance between the plates is d=2 cm, the work function of the electron from the metal is A=3·10⁻¹⁹ J, the charge of the electron is e=1.6·10⁻¹⁹ C.
