9. An electron with a mass of $m=9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg and a charge of $e=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ K flies into a uniform magnetic field with an induction of $B=0,015$ T at a speed of $v=10^3$ km/s at an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the direction of the magnetic field induction vector. Determine the pitch of the helix along which the electron moves. Give your answer in centimeters.

10. Find the absolute refractive index of the medium in which light with a phonan energy of $E=3 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J has a wavelength of $\lambda=0,44 \cdot 10^{-6}$ m. Planck's constant is $h=6,6 \times 10^{-34}$ J s.

Option 2

1. The projectile flew out of the gun at a speed of $v_0=200$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha=60^\circ$ to the horizon. After what minimum time will the projectile velocity vector make an angle $\beta=45^\circ$ with the horizon? Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s².

2. A body of mass $m=5$ kg is acted upon by two forces equal to $F_1=3$ N and $F_2=4$ N and directed at an angle $\alpha=90^\circ$ to each other. Find the acceleration values ​​of the body.

3. A stone falls from a height of $h=20$ m with no initial velocity. What will be the speed of the stone at the moment when its potential energy decreases by $n=2$ times compared to its original value? Air resistance is not taken into account. Gravity acceleration $g=9,8$ m/s².

4. What should be the largest volume of ice floating in water if it is known that an almominine block with a volume of $V_a=0,1$ m³, approximately the size of a gray ice floe, causes it to sink? The density of ice is $\rho_\pi=0,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m³, the density of aluminum is $\rho_a=2,7 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m³, the density of water is $\rho_b=10^3$ kg/m³.

5. Two cylinders connected by a thin tube, the volumes of which are $V_1=7$ dm³ and $V_2=12$ dm³, contain a certain amount of gas.

The first cylinder has a constant temperature of $t_1=0^\circ$ C. To what temperature must the second cylinder be heated so that $n=1/3$ amount of gas remains in it?

6. At what speed should a lead pellet fly out from a gun when fired vertically down from a height of $h=100$ m so that when it hits an inelastic body the pellet melts? The initial temperature of the pellet is $T=500$ K, the melting temperature is $T_{\pi\pi}=600$ K, the specific temperature of lead is $c=0,13$ kJ/(kg K), the specific melting temperature is $\lambda=25$ kJ/kg. Assume that upon impact the entire body melted the pellet.

7. Two identical flat air capacitors are connected in series in a beam. How many times will the capacitance of the beam increase if one of the capacitors is filled with a dielectric with a dielectric substance $\varepsilon=2$?

8. An electric kettle has two windings. When one of them is turned on, the kettle boils in $t_1=10$ minutes, and when only the second one is turned on, in $t_2=15$ minutes. How long will it take (in minutes) for the kettle to boil if you boil it on the windings, connecting them in parallel?

9. A short-circuited coil consisting of $n=1000$ turns is placed in a magnetic field, the induction line of which is directed along the axis of the coil. The magnetic field induction changes at a rate of $\Delta B/\Delta t=5 \times 10^{-3}$ T/s. The cross-sectional area of ​​the coil is $S=40$ cm², the resistance of the coil is $R=160$ Ohm. Find the heat loss power. Give your answer in µW (1 µW=10⁻⁶ W).

10. The plane mirror is rotated through an angle $\alpha=35^\circ$. At what angle will the beam reflected from the mirror rotate? Give your answer in degrees.
