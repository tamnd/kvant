Apparently, there is no person who has not heard the statements “Mathematics is the language of science”, “Mathematics is the language of human culture”. Indeed, mathematics has some linguistic features. At the same time, the science of languages ​​- linguistics - uses mathematical methods in its research. Of course, linguistics is not related to mathematics: it has its own subject of research, and approaches to research are in many ways different from mathematical ones, nevertheless, one can begin to get acquainted with various linguistic phenomena and methods of linguistic analysis almost from scratch.

Try to solve three problems in linguistics. Problems of this type are offered at optimization tests in linguistics and mathematics, conducted by Moscow University since 1965.

Solutions to the problems are in one of the following issues.
