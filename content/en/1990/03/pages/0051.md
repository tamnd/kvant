Question: What is it - big, green, lives three meters underground and eats stones?
Answer: Big Green Stone Eater.

From MIPT folklore
How will the force of gravity change as the body plunges into an imaginary shaft, digging through the Earth along its diameter?

What gravitational force would act inside a “hollow” planet?
Would it be possible to walk on its inner surface? Let's try to answer these and similar questions. And not, of course, in order to develop instructions for drillers of ultra-deep wells or for astronauts, but in order to better understand the law of universal gravitation.

The law of universal gravitation, discovered by Newton, states that any two material particles (exact masses) are attracted to each other with a force proportional to the masses of these particles $m_1$ and $m_2$ and inversely proportional to the square of the distance $r$ between them:

$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2},$$

(1)

where $G = 6,67 \cdot 10^{-11} \text{ H} \cdot \text{m}^2 / \text{kr}^2$ is gravity
