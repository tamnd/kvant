Once upon a time, in the good old days, text algebraic problems were loved in school. The methods of solving them, often very sophisticated, were taught long and carefully, and these skills were retained for the rest of my life. At the same time, the school not only taught methods, but also cultivated taste - an algebraic solution was considered more beautiful than an algebraic one. However, even today, for any little mathematically educated person, algebraic solutions to algebraic problems, as well as geometric solutions to geometry problems, look much more attractive than algebraic solutions.
