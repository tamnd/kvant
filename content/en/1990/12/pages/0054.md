XXXI
International Mathematical Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. SLINKO

The next XXXI International Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Beijing from July 7 to July 19, 1990. 308 schoolchildren from 54 countries took part in the Olympiad. For the first time this year, teams from Algeria, Bahrain, Japan, Macao, and the DPRK took part in it.

Our country was represented by Malinnikova Evgeniya (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Stoyanovsky Alexaンドr (Moscow, s. sh. No. 57), Perlin Alexa ンドr (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Abramov Georgy (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Soloviev Ivan (p. Chernogolovka, Moscow region, village no. 82), Baranovsky Vladimir (Omsk, village no. 115). The scientific leaders of the team at the Olympiad were the authors of this article - associate professor of Moscow State University V. V. Vavilov and researcher at the Scientific Research Institute of SI of the USSR Academy of Sciences A. M. Slinko.

The Olympiad, as always, was held in two rounds (3 problems in each, 4.5 hours were allotted for solving them in each type). The solution to each problem was worth 7 points.

The Olympiad has long become a real international holiday of young mathematicians, the program of which includes not only scientific competitions, but also an introduction to the sculptural sights of the country where the Olympiad is being held, its culture, and history.

The next International Mathematical Olympiads will take place in 1991 in Sweden, and in 1992 in the USSR. Taking this opportunity, we appeal to individual readers and organizations to send their proposals on various aspects of the XXXIII International Mathematical Olympiad
