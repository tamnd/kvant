At the beginning of the 20th century, a general form of writing was introduced in China - from left to right. In this entry, the number 47 looks like this:

47: 四十七

Hieroglyphs are used to write numbers not only in China, but also in other countries such as Japan and Korea. However, the traditional vertical recording system was preserved only on the island of Taiwan.

On stamps, coins, and paper banknotes of many countries, national numbers are adjacent to “international” ones. This juxtaposition can be seen on Libyan postage stamps. Side by side at figure are two sides of a Japanese 5 yen coin. The first character introduces the number 5, and the second character introduces the word “yen”.
