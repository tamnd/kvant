Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 9, 10 and 11 for the 1991/92 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students interested in the Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is recruiting students from secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in the 9th, 10th and 11th grades for the 1991/92 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students interested in the Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is recruiting students from secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in the 9th, 10th and 11th grades for the 1991/92 academic year.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology courses and electives, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics.

The leaders of the cereal or elective class recruit and enroll students (at least 8-10 people) who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A group is accepted into the 3FTS if the school director informs the 3FTS of their names, having identified its leader and a posted list of students (indicating the class in the 1991/92 academic year and the completed grades for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). All these materials and an envelope for a response to admission to 3FTS with a return address addressed to one of the managers should be sent to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTS, indicating “Kruok” or “Optional” before May 25, 1991. (Notebooks with students’ work are not sent to 3FTS.) The work of the head of correspondence physics and technology classes and, in fact, the school (3FTS) at MIPT can pay for cultivative classes to the school at the request of 3FTS at MIPT as elective classes.

Students of 3FTS, heads of physics and technology classes and electives will receive assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program (6-7 tasks in each subject during the academic year), as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The tasks contain theoretical material and the development of typical tasks and examples on the relevant topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). 3FTS assignments are prepared by teachers from the departments of general physics and higher mathematics at MIPT. These tasks are intended for curious students who want to learn and who want to develop the skills of systematic and productive independent work. Student works

1. Region (region or ASSR)
2. Last name, first name, patronymic
3. The class you are in
4. Number, address and telephone number of the school (regular school, special school, what specialization)
5. Last name, first name, patronymic of your physics teacher
6. Place of work and position of parents father
mother
7. Detailed home address
8. Your favorite subjects and hobbies
9. Purpose of admission to 3FTS at MIPT

Correspondence courses are conducted by teachers, graduate students and students of MIPT, Leningrad State University and KrSU (often by graduates of 3FTS). The work of members of the physics and technology department and electives is assessed by their supervisors.

Moscow students receive classes in physics and mathematics twice a week according to the 3FTS program in evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out either based on the results of completing the 3FTS introductory task, or based on the results of an interview in physics and mathematics. Inquiries by phone 408-51-45.

Each student completes the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently. Do the work in Russian and carefully copy it into one school notebook. Keep the order of tasks the same as in the assignment. Send the notebook in a large envelope with a simple parcel (just don’t weld it together). Along with the decision to comply, embroider a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of your notebook.
