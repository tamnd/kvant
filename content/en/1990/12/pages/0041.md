Of course, you couldn’t help but notice that in the center of the geometric shadow there is a noticeable bright spot, known to physicists as the Arago-Poisson spot. The history of this spot is quite interesting and interesting.

The Paris Academy of Sciences proposed diffraction of light as a topic for its 1818 edition. Arrange a competition Most of them were supporters of the corpuscular theory of light and hoped that the competition would bring the final victory of their theory. However, Fresnel presented a work in which all optical phenomena known at that time were explained from a wave point of view. Reviewing this work, Poisson; who was a member of the showjumping commission, drew attention to the fact that Fresnel’s theory leads to an “inconsistent” conclusion: in the center of the shadow cast by a small round disk there should be a light spot. Arago immediately performed an experiment and discovered that such a spot actually existed. This fact certainly contributed to the general acceptance of the wave theory of light.*)

*) We advise you to read the article by V. Vainin and G. Gorelik “Poisson’s Spot and Sherlock Holmes” in «Kvant» No. 4 for 1990. (Editor's note)
