In the first experiment, we obtained a diffraction pattern on a polished metal cylinder with a diameter of 2 mm. The cylinder axis was directed perpendicular to the plane $XY$, the distance $SO$ was 1 m, $OP_0$ - 2.5 m, $P_0P$ - 35 mm (see Fig. 1). A 0.05 mm wide spool was located in front of the photomultiplier, parallel to the cylinder axis.

Figures 2 and 3, $a$ show the results of this experiment. As can be clearly seen, two diffraction patterns with different distortions are observed in the experiment. At the edges of the shadow of the cylinder and in the outer region, there is no general edge diffraction, i.e., alternating dark and light areas. In addition, in the area of ​​​​the geometric shadow and outside it, positions that are equidistant from each other, parallel to the axis of the cylinder, are visible. Both types of diffraction can be obtained and analyzed separately.
