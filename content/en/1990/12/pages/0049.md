Formation of IEC in satellite orbit
Let us give an example of the formation of an IEC in the installation orbit of an artificial Earth satellite for the service of organizing a rafted expedition according to a two-ship flight scheme.

The start date for transport operations to launch cargo into space is mid-2010. At this time, at the end of the orbit, two manned orbital stations are in orbit - the new generation Mir (USSR) and Freedom (USA), which in this case, in addition to traditional scientific and national economic tasks, are used as assembly and installation orbital complexes.
The first to be formed into the final orbit is an automatic interplanetary spacecraft, which will deliver the landing complex and a rocket unit with fuel for the return flight of the crew or only a tank with fuel into the orbit of the artificial satellite of Mars (ISM). The launch of ALS launch vehicles launches into space the landing complex (developed by the USA), the reentry rocket unit and the EYARDU (developed by the USSR).
