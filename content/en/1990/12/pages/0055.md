Tasks

1 (India). The chords AB and CD intersect at point E inside a given circle. Let M be an interior point of the segment BE. The tangent at point E to the circle passing through points D, E and M intersects lines BC and AC at points F and G, respectively. Let AM/AB=t. Find EG/EF as a function of t.

2 (CSFR). On the circle of a given set E there are (2n-1) different points (n≥3), of which k points are shown in black, and all the rest are shown in white. A coloring of points is called good if there are two black points, strictly between which one of the other circles contains robno n points from the set E. Find the smallest value of k for which each coloring of the points of the set E is good.

3 (Romania). Find all the integers

$n>1$ such that $\frac{2^n+1}{n^2}$ is an integer.

4 (Türkiye). Let $Q^+$ be the set of all positive rational numbers. Give an example of a function $f: Q^+ \rightarrow Q^+$ such that

$$f(xf(y)) = \frac{f(x)}{y}$$

for everyone $x, y \in Q^+$.

5 (Germany). Given a natural number $n_0>1$. Players A and B take turns choosing natural numbers $n_1, n_2, \dots$ according to the following inductive rule. Player A, knowing the number $n_{2k}$, can choose any number $n_{2k+1}$ such that

$$n_{2k} \leq n_{2k+1} \leq n_{2k}^2.$$

Player B then chooses any number $n_{2k+2}$ such that $n_{2k+1}/n_{2k+2}$ is a positive prime power. Player A wins when he chooses the number 1990, and Player B wins when he chooses 1.

Find all values ​​of $n_0$ for which

a) A has a winning strategy;
b) B has a winning strategy;
b) neither A nor B has a winning strategy.

6 (Inderlands). Prove that there exists a graduation polygon with 1990 sides such that

a) all its angles are equal;
b) the side lengths of the polygon are equal to the number $1^2, 2^2, \dots, 1989^2, 1990^2$ in some order.
