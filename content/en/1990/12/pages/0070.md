$$f(x) = f(p_1)^{n_1} f(p_2)^{n_2} \dots f(p_k)^{n_k},$$

where $f(p_j)$ is defined by the equality

$$f(p_j) = \begin{cases} p_{j+1}, & \text{ если } j \text{ нечетно}, \\ 1/p_{j-1}, & \text{ если } j \text{ четно.} \end{cases}$$

It is easy to check that the function defined in this way, for all $x, t \in Q^+$, satisfies the conditions
a) $f(xt) = f(x)f(t)$ (due to (1)),
b) $f(f(x)) = 1/x$ (due to (2)).

It immediately follows that $f$ is a solution to the given functional equation.
