Quests

1. Several students answered in class, and all received answers of at least a C. Anya received an answer that changed the sum of the others’ answers by 10; Borya received an answer that changed the sum of the other answers by 8; Vera - an answer that changes the sum of the others' answers by 6. How many people answered during the lesson and what answers did they receive?

2. Are you somewhat strong in arithmetic? Replace the letters in this rebuce (see figure) with numbers so that all equalities are satisfied. Identical letters correspond to identical numbers, different numbers correspond to different ones.

3. A major military band demonstrated its art in the square. First, the musicians performed in a square, and then changed into a rectangle, and the number of rows increased by 5. How many musicians were in the orchestra?

4. Can a number that is a perfect square be written using only the numbers 0 and 6?

5. The height of some Egyptian examples, expressed in meters, is greater than the product of two odd two-digit numbers, but is less than the square of them. For which pharaoh was it built?

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Toom, L. Motsalov, S. Dvoryaninov, S. Sefibekov and I. Akulich.
