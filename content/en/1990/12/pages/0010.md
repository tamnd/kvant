In modern physics there are constants that are called Fundamentals. Among them are the speed of light $c$, electron charge $e$, electron mass $m$, gravitational constant $G$, Planck constant $h$. These constants are included in formulas that describe physical processes in the microcosm and in outer space. The fundamental nature of these constants is that their values ​​do not change either with time or with place in space.
Wherever we change these constants, we will always get the same values. Physicists know that these constants have not changed in any significant way over the billions of years of evolution of the Universe.
