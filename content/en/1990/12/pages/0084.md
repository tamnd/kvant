45 kopecks
Index 70465

This table was compiled by the famous American specialist in “server” and entertaining mathematics, J. Convem. Shown here are 30 six-group colorings of the border of the cube (the colors of the top and four side borders are visible, the color of the bottom border is the one that is missing) - all “geometrically different” colorings, i.e., colorings of the cube that are not compatible with each other. The Convem table has a remarkable property: if you take all the cubes from the row Y and column x (except for the cube Yx, which stands at the intersection of these rows), then from them you can add a cube $2 \times 2 \times 2$, which will first be colored exactly like the cube Xy, and any two “inner” borders of the cubes adjacent to each other will be colored the same. Thus, the McMason problem, shown on the cover of the previous issue «Kvant», is instantly solved (more details about McMason cubes are discussed in this issue of the magazine in the note on p. 00). In the table you can also find simpler, but also interesting patterns: the colorings Hu and Yx are mirror images of each other, in any row - row or column - there is exactly once each of the 15 possible colors of pairs of opposite boundaries and each of the 40 “geometrically different” colors of the triborder of the corners of a cube, etc. Try to try the general purpose of compiling a table and prove, without going through all the options, that it really gives a solution to the McMason problem for any cube-image.
