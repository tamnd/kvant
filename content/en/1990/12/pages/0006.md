In Plato's understanding, Escher's world and the world of mathematics are close neighbors. I will try to illustrate this thesis.

Look at the literature “Belvedere” (see 2nd page), “Waterfall” and “Rising and Falling”. There is something wrong in each of them... Is the staircase the young man is climbing up inside or outside the gazebo? The water flows down all the time - and moves endlessly in a circle. And the monks walk along a closed staircase: they are always going up, while others are going down (by the way, the Golovtsy call meaningless work “monastic work”). Each small fragment of these works flawlessly depicts reality, but overall these fragments add up to impossible objects.

But something similar happens in mathematics!

"The Moebusch Tape" (1963).

Ornament “Steep limit” III (1959).

For example, a circle and a straight line: “in the small”, or as they say in mathematics, locally, are structured the same (if bending is allowed), but “in the whole” - globally - they are completely different. Throw a point out of a straight line and it will break into two pieces; the circle remains connected. Or it will be replaced by a Mobius strip: in the slightest it is indistinguishable from a paper ring, but after going around you find yourself on its opposite side. One of the main subjects of research in topology is objects that are locally structured the same, but globally differently.

Note the frame of the cube in the lower left corner of the Belvedere. Of course, this is an impossible object! The English mathematician Roger Penrose published in 1958 in a psychological journal an image of another impossible object - the “Penrose triangle”. This figure Escher used when working on “Waterfall”. (You will see a whole “constellation” of impossible objects on the cover of Quit No. 2, 1979, and how to get them is explained in No. 5, 1971.)

In order to discuss other general mathematical ideas with which I associate Escher's work, I present this problem to you. There is a map of the city on the table, and on it (strictly inside) is another map of this city, but on a smaller scale. You need to prove that there is a place in the city whose image on both maps matches.
