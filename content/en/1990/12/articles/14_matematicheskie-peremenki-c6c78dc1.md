⟦folio 45⟧

Mathematical changes

1. Solve the short puzzle: $?^2$.

2. A mathematician received an invitation to a dinner party. He replied with a note: S a: What did the mathematician want to say?

3. Making a sly face, the mathematician said to the nine-year-old boy: “Name the largest number.” The mathematician's face fell when he heard the answer - he himself could not have named a large number.

What number did the boy say?

4. Complete the number sequence:
1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, ?.

5. Complete another number sequence:
1, 3, 5, 10, 25, 50, ?.

6. I don’t know which noun ends up with three e. It turned out to be “long-necked”! In revenge, I came up with my own question for my friend: “Tell me which noun ends with three e”!

7. Natasha uttered a true statement. Lesha repeated it verbatim, and it became false. What did Natasha say?

8. The Pythagorean theorem, according to one author, has 114 different proofs. What does this mean?

9. As you know, “Pythagorean pants are equal in all directions.” Does it follow that Pythagoras wore pants?

10. Why does the binding of a book read 2-3 times skew to the right?

V. Proizvolov
