ABOUT FROST PATTERNS
AND SCRATCHES ON THE GLASS

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. MITROFANOV

In winter, when there is severe frost, beautiful ice patterns can be seen on the windows of houses and in vehicles. This frosty “painting” is an amazing creation of winter! Bizarre branches and flowers, feathers of unusual birds, rainbow-colored stars-snowflakes, the finest geometric ornament... - frosty patterns are varied and unique. And sometimes fluffy snow “fur” covers the glass - this is a layer of small ice crystals, the matte surface of which serves as an example of an ideal scattering screen.

Have you ever observed how ice figure forms on window glass? It turns out that very often the very first ice crystals appear on window glass not derivatively, not just anywhere, but along scratches, microcracks and other defects that are always present on glass. Such ice crystals seem to visualize these invisible or almost invisible defects.

The phenomenon in question is observed not only in winter when window glass freezes, but also in many cases during condensation of vapor or during the deposition of any substance on the surface of an amorphous or crystalline solid with a disturbed surface structure. This phenomenon is called decoration (from the Latin word decoro - I decorate).

Microdefects, such as microretina, precise defects, dislocations (lines along which the correct arrangement of atoms planes in the crystal is disrupted), etc., lead to the appearance of local force fields acting on deposited particles of matter in the vicinity of these defects.
