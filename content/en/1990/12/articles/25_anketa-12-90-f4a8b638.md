Graduate College of Sciences
about materials
at Moscow State University and the USSR Academy of Sciences
announces the first intake of students

The college is created as a faculty at Moscow State University and trains world-class legal specialists to carry out materials science programs at institutes of the USSR Academy of Sciences, in research groups of foreign companies and joint ventures.

Studying at the College allows you to receive fundamental training in natural sciences, acquire knowledge and skills in the field of management and marketing, and will ensure fluency in foreign languages ​​and a universal liberal arts education.

Over the course of equal semesters, one of which is for a foreign internship, the leading specialty, taking into account your individual abilities and interests, will introduce you to the latest achievements of modern science and technology of modern science and technical directed synthesis and diagnostics of materials of the future - ceramics, high-temperature superconductors, composites, polymers. Participation in scientific research, work on the latest foreign equipment and computer systems gives you the opportunity to specialize in your chosen field of materials science.

College students receive an increased scholarship, and College graduates receive a diploma from Moscow State University.

Nonresidents are provided with a dormitory for the duration of their studies.

Having decided to test your strength with us, you do not risk anything - selection for the first year is carried out based on the results of the correspondence winter competition and the spring.ccssiya internal competition. Please send scientific applications for the competition, indicating brief information about yourself, no later than December 31, 1990 to the address:
