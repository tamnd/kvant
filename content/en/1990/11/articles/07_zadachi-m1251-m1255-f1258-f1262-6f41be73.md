Tasks

M1251 — M1255, F1258 — F1262

M1251. On the data plane, the angle is (smaller to rotate). Draw two segments PM and QM with a given sum of length s, a quadrilateral of the largest area cut from the angle (P and Q are points on the sides of the angle, M is inside the angle).

V. Senderov

M1252. Let a and n be natural numbers, a>1. Prove that the number of proper irreducible fractions with exponent a^n - 1 is divisible by n.

K. Kokhas

M1253. A convex polygon M is drawn on a plane, divided into several convex polygons - a “map” of several “countries”. We will say that such a map is realized in space if there is a convex polygon in which one of the faces is M, and the projections of the remaining faces onto the plane of the face M are the countries of this map (and all of them lie inside M).

a) Construct an example of a map of triangles that cannot be realized in space.

Prove that the map admits a convex realization in each of the following cases:

6) all countries are acute triangles;

c) each country is an inscribed polygon containing within itself the center of a circumscribed circle.

S. Orevkov

M1254. A rectangle of m×n cells, m≥n>1, is drawn on checkered paper. Prove that it can be cut into figures of four cells in the shape of the letter Γ if and only if mn is divisible by 8.

V. Ginzburg, D. Fomin

M1255. Let h be the smallest height of the triangle, d the smallest of the distances between its two crossing edges. Prove herabinities 1/2<d/h<3/2.

A. Skopenkov

F1258. There is a coil of radius R on a horizontal table. A thin weightless thread is wound on the coil, the radius of the winding is equal to r. The thread is passed through a small hole at a height h from the table surface (h>R). At the initial moment, the coil is motionless and the thread is vertical (Fig. 1). The thread begins to be pulled with a constant force F, and the spool rolls along the table without piercing. Find the maximum speed of the coil. The mass of the coil is M. Assume that half of the total mass is concentrated on the axis of the coil, and the second half is distributed along the outer rim of radius R. Assume the thread is smooth.

A. HodULEV

F1259. The whistle produces a sound with a frequency of $f_0=2000$ Hz. How will the frequency of sound change if the air temperature rises from 20 to 40 degrees Celsius? What tone will a whistle produce in an air bell for underwater work, in which a breathing mixture of gel and oxygen is supplied at a pressure of 1.5 atm? The partial pressure of oxygen is taken to be 0.3 atm. How will the tone of a person’s speech change in this bell? Will the guitar need to be rebuilt to make it sound the same inside the bell as it does outside?

A. Zilberman

F1260. The well-known infinite chain of resistors (Fig. 2) contains resistors $r$ and $R$. What is the equal resistance changed between two adjacent nodes (separated by exactly one resistor $r$)? What is the change in resistance between two nodes that are very far apart? Find this resistance in the general case when there are $n$ resistors $r$ between the interconnections between us points.

P. Rodin

F1261. Two capacitors with capacities of $C_1=1$ µF and $C_2=3$ µF are connected in series to a battery with a voltage of $U_0=4$ V. An inductance coil $L=1$ H is connected in parallel with a capacitor $C_1$. Find the amplitude of the current in the coil. By the time the coil is connected, the voltage on the capacitors is considered to be steady. Claim losses in the chain.

R. Aleksaidrov

F1262. An electric fan with an asynchronous motor, powered by 220 V, develops at 1800 rpm. To prevent it from humming so loudly, it is connected via an autotransformer to a voltage of 127 V. At what speed will it rotate? Consider that the load on the fan blades is determined by the overcurrent air flow. We have a claim in the bearing. Consider the current strength in the stat windings to depend only on the applied voltage.

O. Fatyanov
