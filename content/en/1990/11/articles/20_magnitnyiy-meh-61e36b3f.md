Magnetic fur
Architects dealing with the problems of thermal ash coating of buildings should rest in nature. The thermal insulation layer should not be on the inside of the walls or in the walls, but on the outside. In this case, the brickwork, which has high thermal inertia, will smooth out daily temperature fluctuations inside the room. Ideally, the outer wall covering should also have water-repellent, sound-proofing, decorative and protective properties. Constructing a feasible building, however, is not so simple. The glass wool coating is too inelegant, and the electrostatic method,

used in the manufacture of artificial fur is too complex. Biologically, the company NIGHTMARE*) is looking for lawn grass that could be grown on the walls as a kind of plant “fur”. But there are fears that grass, like plush, will destroy the brickwork over time.

According to Daedalus, the new magnetic paint provides a solution to the problems. It consists of suspended sawdust in nitro varnish. The paint is applied to the surface and held over a layer of fresh paint with a powerful magnet. The sawdust is attached with a magnet and pulls out a thread of varnish. The thread hardens very quickly, and the wall is covered with a long verse. Such a coating, which can be given any color, not only has the advantage of
