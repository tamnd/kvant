XXIV All-round
Olympics
in mathematics

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. REZNICHENKO

From April 18 to 25, the final stage of the XXIV All-Union Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Ashgabat. 165 students took part in the Olympics, representing teams from 14 allied resource centers (Lithuanian teams did not participate), the cities of Moscow, Leningrad, Ashgabat and physics and mathematics schools at the universities of Moscow, Leningrad, Novosibirsk, Kiev, Yerevan, Almaty, Tashkent. It should be noted that the AzSSR team was formed from two schoolchildren based on the results of participating in a competition for solving problems in the magazine «Kvant».

As usual, Olympida participants were given four tasks in two rounds. Each given olympid was assessed in points so that the total number of points in the round for each class was equal to thirty (see Table 1).

Based on the results of the Olympiad, diplomas of I, II and III degrees and certificates were awarded to 101 schoolchildren. Table 2 provides information on the number of characteristics for each class, indicating the number of points scored based on the results of training. In addition, 26 schoolchildren were awarded special recognition from scientific and public organizations.

In the Olympics program, in violation of the main competitions, for the second time a computer tournament was held for everyone, in whom

105 schoolchildren took part, and a traditional “mathematical battle” took place between the Olympiad participants and the jury.

As part of the rich cultural program, as always, a meeting with members of the editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» was interesting and exciting.

I would like to thank the owners of the Olympics, who spared no time and effort in organizing it. Olympida was held at a high level. We congratulate all its participants and wish them new success.

10th grade

1. Is it possible to color the cells of a 1990×1990 table in black and white so that the cells that are symmetrical relative to the center of the table are painted in different colors, and in any row and in any column of the table there are equal numbers of black and white cells?

N. Adakhanov

2. Prove that for positive numbers $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$ whose sum is 1, the following inequality holds:

$$\frac{a_1^2}{a_1 + a_2} + \frac{a_2^2}{a_2 + a_3} + ... + \frac{a_n^2}{a_n + a_1} \geq \frac{1}{2}.$$

D. Tereshin

3. On the side $AB$ of the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, a point $E$ is taken, different from the points $A$ and $B$. Segments $AC$ and $DE$ intersect at point $F$. Prove that the circles circumscribed about triangles $ABC$, $CDF$ and $BDE$ have a common point.

L. Kuptsov

4. At the ends of the segment [0; 1] two blacksmiths are sitting. Several points are marked inside the segment. Grasshoppers have to yoke along a segment through marked points: the positions of the grasshopper before the buckle and after the buckle are symmetrical relative to the marked point through which the grasshopper yokes, and only buckles that do not lead out of the segment [0; 1]. The move consists in the fact that each of the grasshoppers, independently of the other, either yokes or remains in place. In what minimum number of moves can the grasshoppers always end up together on one of the segments on which they split the segment [0; 1] marked points?

S. Konyazin

5. Solve the equations in integers

$$\left[ \frac{x}{1!} \right] + \left[ \frac{x}{2!} \right] + ... + \left[ \frac{x}{10!} \right] = 1001.$$

S. Reznitsenko

6. On the sides $A_1 A_2$ and $A_2 A_3$ of the regular $2n$-gon $A_1 A_2...A_2n$ the points $K$ and $N$ are taken respectively so that $\angle KA_n + 2N = \pi/(2n)$. Prove that $NA_n + 2N$ is faster than angle $KNA_n$.

N. Adakhanov, D. Tereshin, D. Fomin

7. In a convex polygon, all diagonals are drawn. Each side and each diagonal of the polygon is colored in one of the colors so that there is no closed broken line with vertices at the vertices of the polygon and all its links of the same color. For what maximum number of vertices is this possible?

A. Anjans, D. Flaas

11th grade

1. Given two intersecting circles inscribed in an angle with vertex $A$, $B$ is one of the points of intersection of these circles, and $C$ and $D$ are the points of their tangency on one side of the angle. Prove that the line $AB$ is tangent to the circle passing through the points $B, C, D$.

2*. There are 1990 piles of 1, 2, ..., 1990 stones. In one step, it is cut to throw out piles of the same number of stones from the front set. What is the minimum number of steps in which you can throw away all the stones?

N. Adakhanov

3. A square triangle has $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ all positive coefficients and $a+b+c=1$. Prove that for any positive numbers $x_1, \dots, x_n$ satisfying the equality $x_1 \cdot x_2 \cdot \dots \cdot x_n=1$ the following inequality holds:

$$f(x_1) \cdot \dots \cdot f(x_n) \geq 1.$$

D. Fomin

4. A cube with an edge of 100 is composed of a million individual cups, the edges of which form a frame. Any triple of mutually redistributed unit edges with one common vertex is called a frame. Is it possible to divide the weight of the frame into frames that do not have common edges?

A. Berzins

Can the first one always ensure that the better equation has three different integer roots?
