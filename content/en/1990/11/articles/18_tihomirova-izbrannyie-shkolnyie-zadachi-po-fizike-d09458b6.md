Selected school problems in physics

9th grade

1. A load of mass $m$ is suspended at one end of the rope, thrown over a fixed block. With what force must the other end of the rope be pulled so that the load is lifted by acceleration $a$? It is ideal to consider the rope (you can run around with the mass and stretch of the rope). What load must be brought to the free end of the rope so that the first load (mass $m$) rises with the same acceleration $a$?

2. A ball of mass $m$ attached to a horizontal rubber cord performs rotational motion in a horizontal plane with an angular velocity $\omega$. The length of the non-stretchable rubber cord is $l_0$, the tension force of the cord increases in proportion to its stretching, the rigidity of the cord is $k$. Find the radius of the circle along which the ball moves and the tension in the cord.

3. Two satellites move in the same direction along circular trajectories lying in the same plane, with speeds $v_1$ and $v_2$. Determine the minimum distance between satellites. The radius of the Earth is $R$.

4. A block of mass $m$ is located on a horizontal surface. The sliding friction coefficient of the block is $μ$. Draw graphical dependences of the fusion on the traction force applied to the block along the sliding plane.

5. What kind of drops should the product be - large or small? Why?

10th grade

8. How much will it cost to prepare 1 kg of ice in a home refrigerator, assuming that it operates according to an ideal cycle? The room temperature is 20 °C, the freon is cooled to a temperature of -10 °C. The cost of 1 kWh of electric energy is 4 kopecks.

9. Humid air, the mass of which is equal to m, occupies a volume V at temperature T and pressure p. The saturated vapor pressure at this temperature is pₙ. Determine the relative humidity.

10. In a calorimeter containing m₁=200 g of water at a temperature t₁=8 °C, m₂=300 g of ice having a temperature t₂=-20 °C are immersed. Determine the contents of the calorimeter after setting the temperature. Specific heat of water c=4.2·10³ J/(kg·K), ice c₂=2.1·10³ J/(kg·K), specific heat of melting of ice λ=3.4·10⁶ J/kg. Heat losses are not taken into account.

11th grade
11. Determine graphically at what positions of the eye the observer can see in a flat mirror of finite dimensions the simultaneous image of point A and object BC, located relative to the mirror as shown in figure 2.

12. A point light source is placed between two mutually perpendicular flat mirrors. How many images do you get? Build them.

13. A plane-parallel plate is placed between the luminous point and the eye. Find the apparent position of the point by plotting.

14. Using a biconvex lens, an image of a candle is obtained on the screen. How will this image change if the lens is covered halfway with cardboard (Fig. 3)?

15. figure 4 (a and b) shows a luminous point S and its image S', given by a lens whose optical axis is OO'. Find the positions of the lens and its foci.
