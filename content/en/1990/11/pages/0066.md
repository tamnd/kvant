By connecting such a circuit in series with a milliammeter to the source, it would be possible to achieve a minimum current in the circuit. To do this, you should wind a coil around a ferrite rod, connect it in parallel with the capacitor and note the number of turns at resonance. The same can be repeated with others, taking into account that when using a long rod with high magnetic permeability and a coil, the turns of which are located next to each other (but not “turn to turn”, but “in bulk”), the power field lines of all turns are arbitrary to each other and the magnetic flux turns out to be proportional to the square of the number of turns of the coil. The resonance in this circuit was recorded quite accurately - flat-minus one turn was already detected and accurate measurements were obtained well. The advantage of this method is that the milliammeter is used only as an indicator of resonance at a minimum current and the error of its scale does not increase measurement errors.

The work was checked in a general way: theoretical work was “encrypted”, while the inspectors did not know whose work they were evaluating; Each work was fully checked at least twice, by different jury members - for greater objectivity. In addition, the participant is told in detail about the criteria for evaluating the tasks (specifically, after the round), and they can, if necessary, appeal to the jury if they considered the assessments they received to be unfair. It was also possible to appeal based on the results of the test of the experimental round.

The Olympians were awarded diplomas and various specialists. A special prize from Academician I.K. Kikon - a microcomputer MK-85 - was awarded for the best experimental work to a ninth-grader from

School 145 in Kyiv Nikolay Ivchenko. I would like to specially note an extremely pleasant event: for the first time in many years, a first-degree diploma was awarded to a representative of the fair sex - Anya Lempert from the village of Chernogolovka near Moscow, who not only solved theoretical problems well, but also performed excellently in the experimental type.

The hosts of the Olympics tried to properly entertain the participants. The children attended department evenings at city schools, took part in an author's tour of historical places in the region, visited the theatre, watched a performance concert at the opening, and also had as much fun as they could. She met with representatives of the editorial board of the journal «Kvant», at which numerous strict requirements were presented to the journal (fortunately, they turned out to be mutually exclusive, so there is no need to fear for «Kvant»). The students who came to the Olympiad, themselves former participants and winners of various Olympiads, actively interacted with the guys, giving them a lot of useful advice and recommendations. At the same time, each participant could see with his own eyes what wonderful results can be achieved (of course, if one behaves well).

The next All-Union Olympiad in Physics was invited by Euravan.

We wish all Olympiad participants great success!
