The magazine «Kvant» together with the Bulgarian youth magazine “Mathematics” continues a competition to solve mathematical problems for students in grades 6-8. The competition consists of 27 problems (3 in each issue) and will end in May next year. The winners will be awarded prizes from the magazines «Kvant» and Mathematics.
Send solutions to problems from this issue no later than January 15, 1991 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant» (marked “Competition “Mathematics”

Tasks
7. On the face of the checkered paper there are 100 nodes, the vertices of the cells that form a 9x9 square. Two players take turns connecting vertical or horizontal segments of two neighbors of the offset nodes. The player whose move creates a square paints it with his color. The one who paints the most squares wins. Is there a winning strategy for the first player? The second player? If yes, which one?

S. Savchev

8. The line contains 1990 numbers equal to 1 or -1. Below, between each two numbers, we write their product; you will get a new string consisting of 1989 numbers. We will continue this operation until there is a line of one number left. Show that if at least one number in the first line is equal to -1, then in the resulting number triangle the number of such numbers is at least 1990.

S. Savchev

9. A point on the plane with coordinates (a; b) is connected by segments from the point (a—b; a) and (a; b—a). Is it possible to use such segments to connect with continuous broken points?
a) (19; 90) and (1990; 3383);
b) (234; 1001) and (611; 7007)?

K. Bankov, S. Savchev
