11th grade

1. Given two intersecting circles inscribed in an angle with vertex $A$, $B$ is one of the points of intersection of these circles, and $C$ and $D$ are the points of their tangency on one side of the angle. Prove that the line $AB$ is tangent to the circle passing through the points $B, C, D$.

N. Sharyzin

2*. There are 1990 piles of 1, 2, ..., 1990 stones. In one step, it is cut to throw out piles of the same number of stones from the front set. What is the minimum number of steps in which you can throw away all the stones?

N. Adakhanov

3. A square triangle has $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ all positive coefficients and $a+b+c=1$. Prove that for any positive numbers $x_1, \dots, x_n$ satisfying the equality $x_1 \cdot x_2 \cdot \dots \cdot x_n=1$ the following inequality holds:

$$f(x_1) \cdot \dots \cdot f(x_n) \geq 1.$$

D. Fomin

4. A cube with an edge of 100 is composed of a million individual cups, the edges of which form a frame. Any triple of mutually redistributed unit edges with one common vertex is called a frame. Is it possible to divide the weight of the frame into frames that do not have common edges?

A. Berzins

7*. The equation is written on the board

$$x^3 + \dots x^2 + \dots = 0.$$

Two people play the following game: the first calls a front number, and the second puts it on the front of three empty spaces; then again calls the front number, and the second one puts it on the front of the other two empty places; finally, the first one puts any number in last place. 8. You are given 4 coins, only one of which is counterfeit. Real coins weigh the same, fake ones too, but they are lighter than real ones. How can all counterfeit coins be determined by weighing no more than 3 m on a pan scale without a grill?

XXIV National Olympiad in Physics

A. ZILBERMAN

The final round of the All-Union Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren this year took place in Vologda. Actually, the Olympics were supposed to be held in Dushanbe, but the organizers encountered difficulties, and the proposal of the Vologda residents turned out to be very timely. Holding the All-Olympiad is always associated with numerous efforts for local organization, and the Organizing Committee is very grateful to the representative of Vologda - if not for them, the Olympiad could have been in jeopardy.

Teams from all resource groups came to Vologda, except for Azerbaijan and Georgia, where for various reasons the resource group rounds of the Olympiad were not held (schoolchildren who came from Tbilisi and some other participants performed at the Olympiad in Vologda outside the competition).

The grand opening of the Olympiad took place on April 19, and on April 20 the participants had already solved the problems of the theoretical round. As usual, in the 9th grade (formerly the eighth) four problems were given and the round lasted four hours, and in the 10th and 11th grades there were five problems for five hours. Here are the conditions of these problems (often of them, according to tradition, have already been published in the section “Problem Book «Kvant»”).
