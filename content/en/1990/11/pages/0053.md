Technical means

It is advisable to build the Mars Expeditionary Complex (MEC) according to a modular principle, according to which the entire complex is divided into separate functional blocks, independently and sequentially delivered from Earth to low-Earth orbit. These include a habitable module, a scientific module, an energy module, a lander, etc.

When creating the IEC and implementing the expedition, four fundamental problems must be solved, namely:

— launching cargo of large mass and dimensions from the Earth into low-Earth orbit and assembling them in orbit;

— interorbital transportation of an interplanetary spacecraft with astronauts on board from Earth to Mars and back;

— ensuring the safety of the crew at all stages of the flight of the expeditionary complex;

— creation of artificial gravity on board an interplanetary spacecraft or the development of special measures to prevent the undesirable consequences of a long stay in weightlessness.
