Introductory task

1. A parachutist jumps out of a balloon basket at an altitude of $H=2$ km and falls for $t_1=30$ s without opening the parachute at a vertical speed of $v_1=40$ m s. The rest of the way it descended at a vertical speed of $v_2=8$ m/s. In addition, throughout the entire flight, the crosswind carried its speed $v_3=4$ m/s. Find the length of the parachutist's descent trajectory.

2. Two electricians, each $L=200$ m long, are moving towards each other. The speed of one of them is less than the other and is equal to $v_1=40$ km/h. The distance between the meeting point of the first varons and the placement of the last varons is $d=40$ m. Determine the speed of the second electrician.

3. A boiler is lowered into two vessels, each containing $M=300$ g of water at a temperature of $t=80^\circ\text{C}$. The power of the first boiler is twice that of the second. How much water will boil in the first vessel after it starts boiling in the second? Specific heat of vaporization of water $L=2,3 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg, specific heat $c=4200$ J/(kg °C).

4. The plastic cube has a cavity on the $d=6$ cm side. When completely immersed in water, the cube is in balance, that is, it does not sink or burst into flames. Determine the volume of the cavity if the following relationship between the density of water and plastic is known: $\rho_b=7/8\rho_p$.

5. A ring of copper wire has a radius of $R=30$ cm. The ends of the wire are soldered. Determine the electrical resistance between diametrically opposite points of the ring. The cross-sectional area is shown (with an index), telephone number (if available).
