Selected final type problems for grades 9-11

9th grade
19. The natural numbers $a$ and $b$ are such that $a^2 + ab + 1$ is divisible by $b^2 + ba + 1$. Prove that $a = b$.

20. On the side BC of the square ABCD, take an arbitrary point P, draw a circle through A, B and P, intersecting the diagonal BD again at point Q. Through C, P and Q, draw a circle that intersects BD again at point R. Prove that points A, R and P lie on the same straight line.

21. Let us consider all possible sets of numbers from the set $\{1, 2, ..., N\}$ that do not contain two adjacent numbers. Prove that the sum of the squares of any numbers in these sets is equal to $(N+1)! - 1$. (Example: $N=3$. Then $1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + (1 \cdot 3)^2 = 23 = 4! - 1$.)

22. The hostess baked a pie for the guests. There can be either P person or Q at the table, where P and Q are relatively prime. What is the minimum number of pieces (not necessarily equal) that the pie must be cut into in advance so that it can be distributed equally in any case?

10th grade
23. On the shelf in a free place there is a 100-volume collected works of L. N. Tolstoy. Decide to pick up any two volumes with numbers of different parities and place them in places. In what minimum number of permutations can you always put the volumes in order?

24. In the segment [0; 1] 22 points are marked. It is allowed to replace any two of these points with the middle of the segment connecting them. Prove that by performing 20 such operations, it is possible to ensure that the two remaining points are at a distance not exceeding 0.001.

11th grade
25. The checkered doka $100 \times 100$ had the top edge glued to the bottom, as well as the right to the left, after which the doka took on the appearance of a “donut”. Is it possible to place 50 rooks in its cells - red, blue and green - so that each red rook “beats” at least two blue ones, each blue one beats at least two green ones, and each green one beats at least two red ones?

26. The continuous function $f: R \rightarrow R$ is such that for any real $x$ the equality $f(x + f(x)) = f(x)$ holds. Prove that the function $f(x)$ is constant.

"Town of Discovery and Creativity"
(the country's first children's scientific and technical center at VDNKh USSR)

To open a “window” to the world around children, to awaken their interest in scientific knowledge, to stimulate children’s creative abilities—this is the goal of the children’s scientific and technical center called “Town of Discovery and Creativity” (abbreviated GOT) being created at VDNKh of the USSR. Its organizers are the USSR State Committee for Science and Technology and the USSR Exhibition of Economic Achievements.

In November of this year, the following sections of the Town are scheduled to open in the Central Pavilion: “Man in the Real World”, “The Wonderful World of Physics and Technology”, “In the World of Personal Computers”. Vivid and non-educational in form, the examination sections on the most extraordinary, difficult to explain natural phenomena will lead children into the depths of processes and phenomena, and open access to the management of the examination will make viewers participants in the production.

A computer gaming complex will offer its services to the little ones, where, along with games, subscription training is provided for the development of memory and logical thinking of the little ones. French gaming experience skills of collective children. In the spring of 1991, as part of the City of Discovery and Creativity, there were aerospace kits in which visitors, in the form of a game, will prepare for and carry out a “flight into space.”

Within the framework of the GOT, the name LAB is the “Children Create” laboratory in a wide range of physics and technical areas, hold meetings of schoolchildren with leading scientists and specialists, optimization, exhibitions of self-made creativity. The most important task of the LAB laboratory is talented children who have proven themselves in the field of physics and technology from other cities of the country. They will be invited to an internship at the Gorodok LAB, where, together with adults, they will work on scientific problems and look for practical solutions to theoretical developments.

Establishing the country's first children's science and technology center is not an easy task. An Association of Scientists, Inventors and Children “Union of Creators”, a fund for scientific and technical internships, financed by the USSR Academy of Sciences, the State Committee for Science and Technology of the USSR, the Head of Space and other organizations is being created under the GOT, but the organizers of the GOT are waiting for proposals for cooperation, advice and recommendations from interested organizations and individuals, as well as from the children themselves and their parents.
