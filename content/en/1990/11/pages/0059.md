Falling drop
and air bubble

The photographs you see here were taken and added
to our editor, a student from secondary school No. 542 at
Moscow Engineering Physics Institute Konstantin Yufryakov (now he is already a student).
The pictures show the process of air formation
bubble when a drop falls into water. Home
problem when photographing such fast-
processes - turn on the flash camera in time.
The author successfully completed this task using
a special installation he developed.
