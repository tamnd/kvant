2. Let's put 50 coins on the scales. If the scales are in balance, then the remaining coin is counterfeit, and in the next weighing we determine whether it is lighter or heavier than the real one. Let the scales not be in balance. Take a heavier pile of 50 coins from them, and put the remaining 25 in each cup. If the scales are in balance, then there is no counterfeit coin among the coins, which means that it is among a heavier pile of 50 coins, i.e. the counterfeit coin is heavier than the real one. Otherwise, the counterfeit coin is lighter.

8. 29. It is clear that more than 29 chairs cannot be occupied. We'll show you how to occupy 29 chairs. If the $k$ left chairs ($k \leq 28$) are occupied, then of the two new guests, the first one can eat on the chair numbered $k+2$, and then get up when the $(k+1)$ chair is the second one. Thus, $k+1$ chair is already occupied. If $k+1 \leq 28$, then the reasoning can be continued.
