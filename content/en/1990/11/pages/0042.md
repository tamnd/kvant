The harmonic mean $n$ of positive numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ is defined as follows:
$$M = \frac{n}{\frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{a_n}}.$$
If we compare it with the more well-known averages - arithmetic averages
$$A = \frac{a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n}{n}$$
and geometric mean
$$G = \sqrt[n]{a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdot \dots \cdot a_n}.$$
then it turns out to be less than each of them. More precisely, $A \geq G \geq M$. Equality is achieved only if all numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ are equal.

This correspondence for $n=2$ is beautiful or can be constructed geometrically. Consider an equilateral trapezoid with bases $a_1$ and $a_2$ circumscribed around a circle. (Such a tra-

Indeed, the term $1/n$ is adjacent to the term $1/(n-1)$ and $1/(n+1)$, and their harmonic mean is equal to
$$\frac{2}{\frac{1}{1} + \frac{1}{n-1}} = \frac{2}{n-1+n+1} = \frac{1}{n}.$$
The sum of its terms is called the harmonic series $1+1/2+1/3+1/4+\dots$
