BORLAND will help you get to the forefront of modern information and be fully prepared for any surprises!

In today's world, software production has become an independent industry with a huge turnover. The software industry employs hundreds of thousands of highly qualified specialists. However, one of the most famous corporations in the field of software creation is Borland International Inc. (hereinafter simply Borland). It was founded in the USA by a young and talented scientist-entrepreneur Philip Kahn in 1983. The only software product presented by the company at that time was a complator for the Pascal programming language, which received the commercial name Turbo Pascal.

The first proprietary version of one of the most popular programming languages ​​was based on simple principles that distinguish any highly cultural product: efficiency, reliability, ease of use. In addition, the news for Borland's competitors is the extremely low price of the product, which was no more than the average salary of the average programmer in the United States.

“Turbo” is the integration in a single product of the most important tools and tools for a programmer, executed at the same high level, in a single style, moreover, with beautifully written, simple and clear documentation, and this has made Turbo Pascal the most common programming language for PCs shared with the IBM PC. In a short time, more than a million users began to own the Turbo Pascal software package, and in many cases this professional software tool was purchased with personal funds, and many companies began to hastily reproduce in their software products the principles of organizing the user and programmer’s workplace, which were first proposed by Borland.

Constantly investing huge amounts of money in scientific research and development, in 1984-1988 the Borland development team dramatically expanded the range of supplies and products.

Today, Borland is a world-renowned supplier of a whole family of programs that are distinguished by conceptual innovation and high technical excellence. The company's activities can be divided into two areas:

— production of tools (“production tools”) for programmers: complators, tools for debugging programs, other tools for developers;

— production of software for end users: spreadsheets, database management systems, office automation tools, text processors.

The most popular Borland products today include:

For software

— Turbo Assembler/Debugger;
— Turbo C/Professional (2.0 and C++);
— Turbo Pascal/Professional (5.5);
— Turbo Pascal Toolboxes;
— Paradox Engine and much more;

For non-programmer users

— system for maintaining spreadsheets and graphical data display Quattro 1.0/Quattro Pro;

— database management systems Paradox 3.5, Paradox 386 (v. 2.0), Paradox OS/2 (v. 2.0);

— a tool for automating work stations for personnel management Sidekick Plus;

— Sprint word processor;

— Turbo Lightning control tools (with a software interface) and Lightning Word Wizard;

— a tool for scientific, engineering and financial calculations Eureka: The Solver;

— Reflex 2.0 flat file management system.
