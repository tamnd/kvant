F1240. In the circuit shown in figure 1, the capacitance of the capacitor is $C$, the resistance of the resistors is $R$ and $2R$. What charge will flow through jumper $AB$ after connecting a battery with voltage $U_0$? What if resistor $R$ is connected between points $A$ and $B$? All elements are considered ideal.

First, let's look at the case with a jumper (its resistance is zero). Each of the capacitors will eventually be charged to voltage $U_0$, i.e. charge will pass through the resistors

$$q = 2CU_0.$$

However, the current flowing through $R$ is at any moment twice that through $2R$. Thus, a charge will pass through the jumper

$$Q = q_R - q_C = \frac{2}{3} q - \frac{1}{2} q = \frac{CU_0}{3}.$$

Now let's discuss the general case - the resistance of the jumper is not zero. Let's consider a certain moment in time when the battery has already been connected, but equilibrium has not yet been established (Fig. 2). The current flowing through resistor $R$ (jumper) is

$$I = \frac{\varphi_A - \varphi_B}{R} = \frac{q_1 - q_2}{RC}.$$

$$\frac{U_0 - \varphi_A}{R_1} \Delta t = I \Delta t + \Delta q_1,$$

$$\frac{U_0 - \varphi_B}{R_2} \Delta t + I \Delta t = \Delta q_2.$$
