$$\frac{a_1^2}{a_1 + a_2} + \frac{a_2^2}{a_2 + a_3} + ... + \frac{a_n^2}{a_n + a_1} \geq \frac{1}{2}.$$

4. At the ends of the segment [0; 1] two blacksmiths are sitting. Several points are marked inside the segment. The move consists in the fact that each of the grasshoppers, independently of the other, either yokes or remains in place. In what minimum number of moves can the grasshoppers always end up together on one of the segments on which they split the segment [0; 1] marked points?

S. Konyazin
