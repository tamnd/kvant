After that, let the middle brother also keep his share, and give the rest to me and the elder brother equally. And then let the eldest brother keep a portion of the apples for himself, and divide the rest equally between my and the middle brother.

The brothers, not suspecting the younger quarry, agreed. As a result, everyone had the same amount of apples. How many apples did the brothers have at the very beginning?

Typically, such problems are usually solved “from the end.” We are moving ahead, sequentially determining the number of apples for the youngest, middle and oldest. It is clear that the younger brother is less than 8 apples long. On the other hand, the number of apples he has is divisible by 4 (since he managed to split half of them in half). This means that the youngest was given 4 apples. Then the average one was given less than half of the remaining apples, i.e. less than 10, therefore - from 5 to 9 pieces. By adding one apple that he received from his younger brother, we should get a number, also dividing by 4. This number should lie in the range from 6 to 10, so it is equal to 8. So, the middle one was given 8 - 1 = 7 apples, and the eldest was given everything else, i.e. 13 apples. (By the way, we note that after such a redistribution, only the elder brother remained in the ukabka. Therefore, perhaps the middle one suspected the quarsty of the proposal of the younger one, but in his own interests he remained silent.)

As you can see, fortune-telling is a very useful thing, and in any case not worthy of full consideration. Moreover, its effectiveness is higher, the more complex and intricate the condition of the problem, since when telling fortunes we do not need to worry about subtleties. Well, for better conditions and the necessary practice, try to guess the answer in the given problems.

Exercise
7. The cheerful tourist went on foot to the rally. On the first day he walked 1/3 of the way, on the second - 1/3 of the remainder, on the third - 1/3 of the new remainder. As a result, the tourist had 32 km to go. How many kilometers from the tourist’s house to the meeting place?

8. Someone, being asked how far he started, answered: “When I live another half, a third, or a quarter of my years, then I will be 100 years old.” How old is this person?

9. “Tell me, famous Pythogoras, how many students attend your school?”

“That’s how much,” answered the philosopher. - Polzovna studies mathematics, a quarter studies music, the seventh part remains silent and, in addition, there are three women.

How many students did Pythogoras have?

10. A fifth of the peshino swarm sits on the kadamba flower, one third on the slinda flowers. The triple difference of the last two numbers went to the flowers of the kutai. And there remained one more bee, flying back and forth, attracted by the scent of jasmine and pandanus. How many bees are there in total?

11. Of the four donors, the second gave twice as many coins as the first, the third - the second - more than the second, the fourth - the fourth - more than the third, and all together they gave 132 coins. How many coins for the first donor?

12. The captain, when asked how many people were in his team, replied: “2/5 of my team is on guard, 2/7 are at work, 1/4 are in the infirmary and 27 are present.” How many people are on the team?

13. (It must be the most difficult one.) Now you and I are 86 years old together. The number of my years is 15/16 of the age that you will have when my age is 9/16 of the number of years that you would have if you had to reach an age that is twice the number of my years at the moment when I can be twice your age. How old am I?
