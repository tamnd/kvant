Problem 2. Through the vertex of a convex quadrilateral, draw a straight line that divides the quadrilateral into two equal parts.

First way. Let us draw the diagonals AC and BD of the quadrilateral (Fig. 3). Through point E—the middle of segment BD—we draw a line parallel to line AC and intersecting CD at point X. Then AX is the required line. Valid, $S_{ABCE} = S_{AECD}$, since $S_{ABE} = S_{AED}$ and $S_{BCE} = S_{CED}$. But $S_{APE} = S_{PCX}$ therefore $S_{ABCX} = S_{AXD}$.

Second way. Through vertex C of the quadrilateral we draw a line parallel to the diagonal BD (Fig. 4) and intersecting the line AB at point P. Note that $S_{APD} = S_{ABCD}$, since $S_{BPN} = S_{NCD}$. Mediana AE divides triangle APD into two equal triangles. Through point E we draw a line parallel to AD and intersecting CD at point X. Then AX is the required line, since $S_{AEF} = S_{FXD}$.
