The interesting role of gravity in the evolution of life forms. If a planet with a large mass happens to be in the comfort zone, as happened, for example, in one of the model experiments carried out using a computer, the physical conditions on it were different from those on Earth. The large mass will cause the retention of light gases in the atmosphere; therefore, the composition of the latter will differ from that of the earth. The increased pressure will push the boiling point of the liquid to higher temperatures. The functioning of living organisms under conditions of increased gravity would require greater energy expenditure for movement, stronger muscles, etc. A good illustration of what has been said is a change in the nature of a person’s walking under conditions of low gravity. Let us recall that on the Moon the astronauts switched to a “fluttering” step, which turned out to be more convenient there.

It is difficult to give any quantitative estimates of the optimal mass of the planet, but everyone knows well that the Earth with its mass is suitable for life.

Dear readers!

It’s a good idea to make the April numbers either funny or with some kind of practical jokes. When we received letters from you with errors noted in No. 4 for 1990, we really wanted to blame everything on tradition - our mistakes turned out to be so incredible.

The first mistake refers to the article by V. Nikolaev “Space flight - is it that simple!?” On page 54, as an example of the longest flight, the flight of Yu. Romanenko is given. Without detracting from the merits of this cosmonaut, we must agree that today Rekord belongs to V. Titov and M. Makarov, whose expedition lasted a whole year (from 12/21/87 to 12/21/88).

The second mistake is even more incredible. Due to the editor’s ineptitude, a simple puzzle for younger schoolchildren QUANT + QUANT + QUANT = PROBLEM “turned” into the unsolvable problem QUANT + QUANT + QUANT = JOURNAL.

The editors apologize to the readers and the author of the problem.

We thank everyone who reads our magazine so carefully and took the time to point out mistakes.
