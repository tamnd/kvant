Evening physical school at Moscow State University
The Evening Physics School (VPS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces enrollment of students in grades 9, 10 and 11 for the next academic year.
The main goal of the VFS is to help students study physics in depth within the scope of the school curriculum. Classes are held in the evening in the form of lectures given every two weeks and daily seminars. In addition, students will be able to visit the faculty’s scientific laboratories and get acquainted with the main directions of modern physics at lectures by leading scientists. For those interested, elective classes in mathematics and the basics of computer science are organized.
Admission to the VFS is based on the results of an interview, which will be held starting from September 26.

School of Future Engineers
Dear guys! If you love to invent and design, that is, you are not indifferent to engineering creativity, if you want to learn to think logically, to deeply learn, understand and love physics, mathematics, and master electronic computing technology, we invite you to the Correspondence Engineering Physics and Mathematics School. You don’t have to go to classes at this school - we will send you by mail both explanations of the new material and test assignments, and you will send us the answers. You will not learn diaries and grades, the main thing in our school is Knowledge and Creativity!
You will study not just one subject in itself, but a complex of sciences that a modern engineer should know and be able to use. No wonder Kozma Prutkov wrote:

“A one-sided specialty has been likened to gumboil!” There are hundreds of mathematicians and physicists, and millions of engineers! And one more thing: a modern engineer must not only know and be able to, he must Create every day, every minute of his work. This is what our school will teach for three years.
Of course, we will communicate not only by mail. There will be rallies, student competitions, excursions to computer centers, a museum and much more. Schools like ours exist in many countries and we offer group exchanges with them.
So, the Correspondence Engineering Physics and Mathematics School is announcing enrollment! In order to be enrolled in the 9th grade, you need to complete the competition task, carefully copy it into a thin notebook and send it to the address: 190031, Leningrad, Moskovsky Prospekt, 9, September. To enroll in the school, you must personally fill out an application at the Komsomol committee of a physicist at Moscow State University (and attach two photographs measuring 3x4 cm). Applications can be submitted from September 6 to September 22, every day, except Sunday, from 16 to 18 hours. Working youth are enrolled without competition.
Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of the VFS.

ZIFSH competition task
1. Two trains are traveling towards each other, one at a speed of 36 km/h, the other at a speed of 54 km/h. A passenger in the 1st train replaces the 2nd train and mismos him for 6 seconds. What is the length of the second train?
2. In metal children, when distinguished, shells are often formed - areas filled with air. They are not visible from the outside, but can significantly affect the strength of the part. How to check whether there are such shells in a part if you have a part made of the same metal of a different shape and volume, set up without shells, change your hand with water in which any of the parts can fit, and a scale?
