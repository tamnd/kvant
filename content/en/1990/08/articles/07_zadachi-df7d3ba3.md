Exercise
1. There is a domino in the box (see figure). How are the bones located in its top row?
2. It was recently reported that due to the drop in the level of the Aral Sea, its water area (area) decreased by 40%, and the volume of water decreased by 60%. Can such a statement be true? Could it happen that the water level in the reservoir will drop and its water area will decrease by 60%, and the volume of water will decrease by 40%?
3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown at figure. Same letters correspond to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers.
4. In the quadrilateral ABCD (see figure), the angles at vertices B and D are right, AB=BC, and the height BH is 1. Find its area.
5. The drawing table for the previous world championship in football in the subgroup where the Soviet team participated looks like this:

| | IN | N | P | balls | glasses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| USSR | 2 | 1 | 0 | 9—1 | 5 |
| FRANCE | 2 | 1 | 0 | 5—1 | 5 |
| HUNGARY | 1 | 0 | 2 | 2—9 | 2 |
| CANADA | 0 | 0 | 3 | 0—5 | 0 |

What was the score between the teams if it is known that only two matches ended the same?
