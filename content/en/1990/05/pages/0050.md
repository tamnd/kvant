9. incident report - April 3
Dr. Strauss showed me how to turn down the sound on the TV and now I can sleep. I can't hear anything. And I still don’t understand what he’s saying there. Sometimes in the morning I turn it back on to see what I learned while I was sleeping and I think it’s nothing. Miss Kinnen may be saying it in another language or something. But almost always it looks like the American one. The TV speaks so fast even faster than Miss Gold who was my teacher in the 6th grade and I remember she spoke so fast that I couldn’t understand anything.

I told Dr. Strauss that it's good to become smart in your sleep. I want to be smart when I'm sick. He says the same thing.

But my tongue hurts from the party. My friends from the factory, Joe Kerp and Frank Reilly, invited me to join him at Muggsy Chinabud's Saloon for a drink. I don't like drinking but they said we'd have a lot of fun. I had a good time.

Joe Kerp said that I should show the girls how I wash the floor in the restroom at the factory and he brought me a tripe. I showed and everyone froze when I said that Mr. Donghegan says that I am the best cleaner of all the cavos he had in all the time, I love my job and I do it well, I am not afraid in any way and did not miss a single day, only when I was appraised.

I said Miss Kinnen always told Charlie to be proud of your job, you did a good job.

Everyone laughed, we were having fun and they gave me a lot to drink and Joe said what a guy Charlie is when he gets drunk. I don’t know what this means but everyone loves me and we have fun. I'm a bit of a Joda until I become as smart as my best friends Joe Kerp and Frank Reilly.

I don’t remember how the party ended, but every day I went out to buy a newspaper and coffee for Joe and Frank and when I returned there was no way they were there. I looked for them everywhere. What then I remember was not so good for me, every time I wanted to sleep or got sick. Some kind policeman brought me home. So says my painting owner Miss Flynn.

But my tongue hurts and there are more marks and severe bruises on it. I think maybe I fell but Joe Kerp says it's police work they get drunk sometimes. I didn't think so. Miss Kinnen says police officers should help people. And everyone is like this, it hurts me a lot, they talk to me and everything hurts. I think I won't drink anymore.

April 6. I defeated Algeron! I didn’t even know that I had defeated him until he said that he fell out of the chair when he had not yet finished. But then I beat him 3 more times. I must have gotten smart if I beat a smart mouse like Algeron. But I don't feel smarter.

I wanted to do more with Algeron, but Bert said that was enough for one day. I was allowed to support him for a minute. It's not that flat. He is soft like a cotton ball. He blinks and when he opens his eyes they are black and pink at the edges.

I said that I could conquer him, but I felt bad that I had defeated him and I want to be kind to everyone else. But Bert said that Algeron couldn’t allow a very special mouse with the same apracia as me, and he is the first of all animals to remain smart for so long. He said Algeron is so smart that every day he has to solve a problem to get him and goes inside to eat, so he has to learn something new every time to get his food. I began to want to knock him out if he couldn’t, the teacher would be the head.

I think it's wrong to force cavonibuds to pass a test for food. How would Dr. Nemur like it if he had to undergo a test every time he wanted to eat? I think Algeron and I will be friends.
