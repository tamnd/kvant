5. $-1/4 \leq k \leq 1/4$. Note. Since $y \geq 0, x = y^2$, we must calculate for which $k$ the equation $2ky^2 - 2y + 4k + 1 = 0$ (*) has at least one non-negative root. At $k=0$ $y=1/2$. If $k=0$, then equation (*) has roots at $-1/2 \leq k \leq -1/4$, and at $\frac{4k+1}{2k} \leq 0$, i.e. at $-\frac{1}{4} \leq k < 0$, the product of roots is non-negative and therefore one of the roots is non-negative. At $-\frac{1}{2} \leq k < \frac{1}{4}$ the roots are negative. Finally, at $0 < k \leq \frac{1}{4}$ the roots are positive.
