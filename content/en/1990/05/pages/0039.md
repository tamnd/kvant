Quests

1. Anton went to the dairy store. He didn’t have any money, but he took some milk bottles - 6 liters (20 kopecks each) and 6 half-liter bottles (15 kopecks each). The store only had bottled milk at a price of 22 kopecks per liter. Anton decided to hand over some of the bottles and pour the milk purchased with the money he received into the remaining bottles. What is the largest amount of milk he can bring home?

2. The plane took off from city A at noon and landed in city B at 2 p.m. local time. He flew back fully and arrived in city A when it was 6 o’clock in the morning. How long does it take to fly between these cities on such an airplane?

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown at figure. Same letters correspond to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers. (In German the words eins and fünf represent one and five.)

4. Represent the number 1990 in three different ways as the sum of several (two or more) consecutive natural numbers.

5. In the square ABCD there are two mutually perpendicular segments MN and PQ (see figure). Show that the sum of the perimeters of the quadrilaterals APON and CQOM is equal to the sum of the perimeters of the quadrilaterals BNOQ and DMOP.
