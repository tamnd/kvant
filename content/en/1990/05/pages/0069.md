Below are published the materials of entrance exams for various types in 1989.

Moscow State Technical University named after. N. E. Bauman

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. A team of tractor drivers plowed 300 g of soil. If the team had 3 more tractors, it would have finished the job 6 days earlier. How many tractors were there in the brigade if one tractor produced 15 g per day?
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos x - \sin(5x + 3\pi/2) = \sqrt{3} \cos(3x + \pi).$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2\left(\frac{3}{8}x + 1\right) = 1 - \log_2x.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{6+x-x^2}}{x-2} < 0.$$
5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $k$ for which the system of equation
$$\begin{cases} y - \frac{1}{2} = k(x+2), \\ y = \sqrt{x} \end{cases}$$ has a solution.
6. In the cube $ABCD_A B_1 C_1 D_1$, in which $AA_1$ is one of the edges, a plane is drawn through the vertex $A$, the middle of the edge $A_1 D_1$ and the center of the face $D_1 DCC_1$. Of all the sections of the cube parallel to this plane, find the section with the most plane; determine its area, considering the length of the edge equal to $a$.

Option 2
1. The distance between points $A$ and $B$ is 60 km, with 2/3 of the road being highway and the rest being dirt road. Find the speed of a car on a dirt road and on a highway, if its speed on the highway is 20 km/h higher than the speed on the dirt road, and it took only 2 hours for the entire journey.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x - 3 \cos^2 x = 4 \sin x.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\lg(3x^2 - 12x + 13)}{\lg(x - 1)} = 2.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2^x + 3} < \frac{1}{2^{x+2} - 1}.$$
5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the system of equation
$$\begin{cases} \lg(4+y) = \lg x, \\ a - y = \frac{1}{2}(x+a)^2 \end{cases}$$ has a solution.
6. A regular triangular paramid is projected into a sphere of radius $R$, the apothem of which is equal to the diameter of the circle circumscribed around the base. Between the sphere and the paramid there is a regular quadrilateral, one of the bases of which lies in the plane of the lateral face of the paramid, and the vertices of the other base belong to the sphere. What is the most likely sign?

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. A puck with a mass of $m = 50$ g slides without initial speed along an inclined plane making an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizon, and, having traveled a distance of $l = 50$ cm along the horizontal plane, is left behind. Find the work done by the friction force along the entire path, taking into account the friction coefficient $\mu = 0,15$.
2. A sled moving on ice at a speed of $v_0 = 6$ m/s causes it to become asphalt. The length of the sled runners is $L = 2$ m, the friction coefficient of the sled on the asphalt is $\mu = 1$. How far will the sled travel before it comes to a complete stop?
3. A light spring with a stiffness of $k$ and a length of $l$ stands vertically on the table. From a height $H$ above the table, a small ball of mass $m$ falls onto it. What is the maximum speed the ball will have as it moves downwards? Neglect training.
4. A smooth, lightweight horizontal rod $AB$ can be rotated without friction around a vertical axis passing through its end $A$. On the rod there is a small coupling with mass $m$, connected never by a spring of length $l_0$ to end $A$. Spring stiffness $k$. How much work must be done to slowly spin up these systems to an angular velocity of $\omega$?
5. Carbon gas flows through a gas pipe at a pressure of $p = 4 \cdot 10^5$ Pa and a temperature of $t = 7^\circ C$. What is the average speed of gas movement in the pipe if a gas mass of $m = 2$ kg flows in a time of $t = 10$ min? The cross-sectional area of ​​the pipe is $S = 5$ cm².
6. In an evacuated vessel with a volume of $V = 1$ dm³ there is $m = 1$ g of uranium hydride ($UH_3$). When heated to a temperature of $t = 400^\circ C$, uranium hydride completely decomposes into uranium and hydrogen. Find the pressure of hydrogen in the vessel at this temperature. The atomic mass of uranium is $A = 238$.
