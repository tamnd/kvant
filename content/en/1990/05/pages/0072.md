Option 1

1. A stone thrown from the ground at an angle of $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the horizontal plane had a vertical velocity component of $v_B = 12$ m/s $t = 0,8$ s after the start of movement. What is the distance between the point of throwing and the place where the stone falls? The acceleration of the gravity force is $g = 10$ m/s².

2. A ball with a mass of $m = 4$ kg and a speed of $v = 5$ m/s collides with a ball at rest of the same mass. Assuming the impact to be inelastic, find the amount of heat released.

3. How many times will the radius of the circular orbit of an artificial Earth satellite increase if its orbital period increases by $n = 27$ times?

4. How much greater is the density of a certain body than the density of the liquid $\rho = 800$ kg/m³, if the weight*) of the body in this liquid is $n = 3$ times less than in air?

5. When heating a gas at a constant volume by one degree, the pressure increased by a relative fraction $n = 0,004$ of the initial value. What (in kelvins) is the initial temperature of the gas?

6. Gas flows through a gas pipeline at a pressure of $p = 0,83$ MPa and a temperature of $T = 300$ K. What is the speed of gas movement in the pipe if τα $t = 2,5$ min through a cross-section of the pipe with an area of ​​$S = 5$ cm² $m = 20$ kg of gas flows? Universal gas constant $R = 8,3$ J/(mol K), average molar mass $M = 40$ kg/kmol.

7. The electric field strength of a flat impact capacitor with a capacity of $C = 4$ µF is equal to $E = 10$ V/cm. The distance between the plates is $d = 1$ mm. Determine the energy of the electric field of the capacitor.

8. A resistor with a resistance of $R = 9$ Ohm connected to a current source with an emf of $\varepsilon = 3,1$ V produces a power of $P = 1$ W. Determine the internal resistance of the current source.

9. Having passed through the acceleration potential difference $U = 3520$ V, the electron entered a uniform magnetic field with an indication of $B = 0,002$ T, perpendicular to the electron velocity. Find the radius of the circle around which the electron will travel. The ratio of the charge of an electron to its mass is $\gamma = 1,76 \cdot 10^{11}$ C/kg.

10. The actual image of an object obtained with the help of a converging lens is located at a distance of $f_1 = 80$ cm from it. If the converging lens is replaced by a diverging lens with the same focal length, the actual image of the object will be $f_2 = 20$ cm away from the lens. Find the absolute value of the focal length of the lenses.
