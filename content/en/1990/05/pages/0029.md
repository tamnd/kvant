The inductance of one of them is 1 mH, the other is 2 mH, and the modified inductance of the coils connected in series is 3.6 mH. Without changing the relative position of the coils, they are switched to parallel (Fig. 2). What inductance will we change now?

F1232. At a great distance from the Earth there is the planet DVK-1, around which the BK-0010SH satellite is orbiting in a circular orbit. A radio station is installed on the satellite, emitting a constant frequency signal. This signal is received on Earth, however, interruptions occur - for 45 minutes there is a signal, then for 45 minutes it is absent, then again for 45 minutes there is, etc. The matter is not limited to this - the frequency of the received signal changes relative to the average value $f_0 = 1,5 \cdot 10^9$ Hz according to the law shown at figure 3 ($\Delta f = 3 \cdot 10^4$ Hz). Assuming that the Earth-planet segment lies in the orbital plane, find the mass of DVK-1.
M. Gavrilov

Problem solutions
M1196—M1200, F1208—F1212

Let's see how the sum of the squares of all numbers in the set changes during these operations. Because
$$\left( a + \frac{b}{2} \right)^2 + \left( b - \frac{a}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{5}{4} \left( a^2 + b^2 \right) \geq a^2 + b^2,$$
each time this sum increases (or does not change - and only in the case of $a = b = 0$). According to the condition, at the very beginning the numbers are not equal to 0, so already at the first step the sum of squares will increase and in the future cannot take on the original value.
D. Fomin
