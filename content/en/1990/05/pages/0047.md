(fantasy story)

D. KEEZ (USA)

1. laments about the dead - March 5, 1956
Dr. Strauss says that from now on I should write down everything that I think and what happens to me. I don’t know why this is needed, but he says it’s very important for the tabo to see if they use me or not. I hope they use me. Miss Kinnen says maybe they can make me smart. I want to be smart. My name is Charlie Gordon. I am 37 years old and two weeks ago was my separation day. Now I have nothing more to write and I’m finished.

2. laments about the dead - March 6
Today I had a test. I think I was wrong and I think maybe now they won’t use me. And there was a kind young man sitting in the room and he had a few white cards and they were all covered in ink. He told Charlie what do you see on this card.
I said that I would eat an ink blot. He said it right. I thought that was all, but when I got up to leave he stopped me. He said sit down Charlie, we haven't finished yet. I don’t remember so well what happened then, he seemed to want me to say that I was writing in an ink blot. I didn’t see anything in it, but he said that there were pictures there, that other people saw some kind of pictures. But I couldn't see any pictures. I really wanted to see. I held the card close to my eyes and then far away. I said if I had glasses I would see better. I only wore glasses in the movies or when I watched TV, but I said that they were in the closet in the front room. I brought them. Then I said let me look at this card again, I will definitely find the picture now.

The author of this story is an American writer and teacher Dzinel Keyes, who at one time edited the science fiction magazine Marvelzl. Rereads from the publication “Vibloteka of modern fiction in 15 volumes. Volume 10" (M., Young Guard, 1967). Translation from English by S. Vasilyeva.

I tried very hard but still couldn’t find some pictures. I only saw a black blot. I told him maybe I need new glasses. What did he write on the paper? I was afraid that I hadn’t seen the test. I told him this is a very beautiful blot with raspberry dots. He became very sad, which means I was mistaken.

3. laments about the dead - March 7
Dr. Strauss and Dr. Nemur say that the black spots don't mean anything. I told them I had put the black on the cards wrong and I couldn’t see anything in the blots. They said that maybe they were using me after all. I said Miss Kinnen had never given me such tests, just correcting writing and reading. They said Miss Kinnen says that I am her best teacher in the evening school for adults because I try harder than anyone and I really want a teacher. They asked how it happened Charlie that you yourself came to the evening school for adults. How did you find her? I replied that I asked people and who told me where I should go to learn to read and write well. They asked why you wanted it. I said all my life I would like to be smart and not stupid. But his cleverness may be temporary. I said yes. Miss Kinnen told me. It always hurts me.

Today later I had some more crazy experiences. This test seemed easy to me because I could see the pictures. Only this time the lady who was working with me didn’t want me to tell her about the pictures. This confused me. I said that yesterday’s mucin asked me to tell him what I saw in the treasure, she said that it didn’t mean anything. She told inventive stories about the people in the cards. I said how can you talk about people you’ve never seen. Why should I make up lies. Now I don’t tell lies anymore because I always fall.
