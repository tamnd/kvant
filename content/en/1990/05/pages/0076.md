MIPT is the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The history of the creation of the institute is a source of special pride for every physicist. After all, about half a century ago, not just another species was created, but a sublime type. Its students were given a unique opportunity to study with such prominent scientists as academicians P. L. Kapitsa, I. V. Kurchatov, S. I. Vavilov, N. N. Semenov, S. A. Khristanovich, M. V. Keldysh, L. D. Landdu. The training was organized according to a new system, which is below known to the whole world as the “physics system”.
The “physics system” is based on the following principles.

1. Careful selection of the most gifted and creative youth.

Applicants will take entrance exams in mathematics (written and oral), physics (written and oral) and write an essay. The tasks offered to examinees do not go beyond the scope of the school curriculum, but require communication, the ability to think outside the box, and find ingenious solutions. Those who receive positive marks in the exams are interviewed by a commission, which consists of the country's leading scientists working in the basic institutes and laboratories of MIPT. The interview, according to the rector of physics N.V. Karlov, is the most accurate of the exams.

A prospective applicant should keep the following three points in mind:

a) the system of passing points, acceptance at other universities, is not applied at MIPT, although the sum of points is taken into account;

b) the institute does not have evening, correspondence or preparatory departments;

b) students who graduate from school with a gold medal enter MIPT on a general basis.

2. Direct participation of leading scientists from organizing research institutes (bases) in student training.

Close contacts with leading physicists and mathematicians stimulate the development of students' creative abilities and instill skills for independent scientific work.

3. Fundamental education.

Our graduates, as a rule, work at the intersection of sciences, and here the breadth and depth of general training in combination with specific training in a very specific, perhaps even narrow, area of ​​research is important. Therefore, the training of scientific personnel at MIPT combines the breadth of classical university education and the specificity of modern scientific and technical knowledge.

In the first three years, students from all faculties of physics and mathematics at the university level. The high level of teaching of these disciplines is evidenced by the fact that for many years physicists have been leaders in all-basic student optimization in physics and mathematics.

To develop technical skills (the ability to design and calculate an experimental setup, improve an instrument, conduct a delicate experiment), laboratory work is widely practiced, requiring maximum independence from the student.

A modern scientist is unthinkable without knowledge of foreign languages. Therefore, during the first three years of study it ensures deep mastery of the English language. The intensive training system allows you to freely read scientific literature, make reports on your specialty, and also conduct conversations at various levels.
