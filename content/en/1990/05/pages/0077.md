4. Special education for senior students is carried out on an experimental basis at leading research institutes of the USSR Academy of Sciences and industry research institutes and design bureaus.

Such training naturally leads to the fact that the range of MIPT specialization is constantly updated. Essentially, physics is a kind of “tracking system” that automatically adjusts the state of science and technology to the current level.

Each physics specialty has its own base institute. In each of them a corresponding basic department is organized. After introductory practice in 2-3 years, the student chooses a scientific group and a specific topic of his future work, which he performs in 4-6 years under the guidance of an experienced researcher. The results of the last two years of work will form the content of the thesis.

By participating in scientific seminars, conferences, carrying out specific sections of the research plan of the base institute, students gain experience in collective work.

Such preparation makes it possible for physics graduates to enter into life as already established specialists, immediately after graduating from the institute, ready for independent responsible activities in the latest areas of science and technology.

Most physicists who graduate from college complete their Ph.D. dissertations within the first three to five years.
