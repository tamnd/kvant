Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University - the path to big science

Physical science plays a leading role in the development of the modern natural picture of the world and the establishment of scientific and technological progress. All-round information about elementary parts, useful ones and their interactions, about the structure of materials and physical laws that manifest themselves at various levels of organization of materials from the microcosm to the Universe, various experimental and mathematical research methods - all this constitutes that wealth of universal human values ​​and the source of new ideas, without which modern science cannot successfully and purposefully develop.

Scientific achievements in various fields of physics directly determine new directions in the transformation of technology and lead to the creation of modern technologies.

The decisive role in the development of physical science belongs to highly qualified personnel. The training of such personnel is carried out at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M. V. Lomonosov.

It covers the entire spectrum of physical specialization and deservedly enjoys worldwide recognition.

The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University is rightfully one of the leading scientific centers in the country. His modern laboratories of laser physics, high-temperature superconductivity, cosmic rays, accelerators, solid state physics, plasma physics, biophysics, geophysical research, radiophysics and electronics, departments of theoretical physics and mathematics, Research Institute of Nuclear Physics and Astronomical Institute. P.K. Sternberg is the basis of many outstanding discoveries. They also serve as a first-class foundation for guiding students into the expanding world of scientific research.

Students of the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University have the opportunity to work at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research (Dubna), the Physics Institute named after. P. N. Lebedev, Mathematical Institute named after. V. A. Steklova, Institute of Biological Physics (Serpusov), etc.

The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University includes 172 doctors and 491 candidates of physical and mathematical sciences. Academicians N. N. Bogolyubov, A. A. Logunov, E. P. Velikov, G. T. Zatsepin, L. V. Keldysh, V. A. Magnitsky, B. M. Pontecorvo, I. M. Frank, A. E. Chudakov work at the faculty.

The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University consists of about 3,000 undergraduates and 500 graduate students.

The duration of study at the faculty is 5.5 years. Graduates are expected to work at research institutes of the USSR Academy of Sciences, industry research institutes, design bureaus and higher educational institutions.

In 1990, entrance exams for the Faculty of Physics were held, scientific from July 4, in mathematics (written), Russian language and literature (written), physics (oral, using a 10-point system). Acceptance of documents from June 20 to July 3, 1990.

Any additional information about the Faculty of Physics can be obtained at the address: 119899, ​​GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, Admissions Committee.
