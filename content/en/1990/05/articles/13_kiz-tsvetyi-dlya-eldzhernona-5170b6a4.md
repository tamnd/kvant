1. laments about the dead - March 5, 1956
Dr. Strauss says that from now on I should write down everything that I think and what happens to me. I don’t know why this is needed, but he says it’s very important for the tabo to see if they use me or not. I hope they use me. Miss Kinnen says maybe they can make me smart. I want to be smart. My name is Charlie Gordon. I am 37 years old and two weeks ago was my separation day. Now I have nothing more to write and I’m finished.

2. laments about the dead - March 6
Today I had a test. I think I was wrong and I think maybe now they won’t use me. And there was a kind young man sitting in the room and he had a few white cards and they were all covered in ink. He told Charlie what do you see on this card.
I said that I would eat an ink blot. He said it right. I thought that was all, but when I got up to leave he stopped me. He said sit down Charlie, we haven't finished yet. I don’t remember so well what happened then, he seemed to want me to say that I was writing in an ink blot. I didn’t see anything in it, but he said that there were pictures there, that other people saw some kind of pictures. But I couldn't see any pictures. I really wanted to see. I held the card close to my eyes and then far away. I said if I had glasses I would see better. I only wore glasses in the movies or when I watched TV, but I said that they were in the closet in the front room. I brought them. Then I said let me look at this card again, I will definitely find the picture now.
