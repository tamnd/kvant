Moscow Aviation Technical Institute named after. K. E. Tsiolkovsky

Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$6 + 4\sqrt{3} - x = 5x.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{4}{\lg 10x} - \frac{5}{\lg 100x} \ge 0.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin 3x - 4 \sin x \cos x = 0.$$

4. The pool with a volume of 1200 m³ has two pipes: supply and discharge. If both pipes are opened at the same time, the pool will fill in 60 hours. If only one pipe is open, then filling the pool with water takes two hours less than emptying it from water. How much water does each pipe carry per hour?

5. Determine the area of ​​a triangle if two of its sides are 1 and $\sqrt{13}$, and the median of the third side is 2.

Option 2

1. Solve the inequality
$$x^2 + 2x - 4 \leq x + 2.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$x + \log_2(9 - 2^x) = 3.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x = \cos 2x.$$

4. The journey from $A$ to $B$ takes 3 minutes for a passenger train and 12 minutes for a freight train. During the time it takes a freight train to travel from $A$ to $B$, a passenger train travels 288 km more. If the speed of each train is increased by 10 km/h, then the passenger train will cover the distance from $A$ to $B$ in 2 and 24 minutes for the freight train. Determine the distance from $A$ to $B$.

5. The median of a right triangle drawn to the hypotenuse divides it into two triangles with perimeters $p_1$ and $p_2$. Find the sides of the triangle.

Option 3

1. Solve the equation
$$|x - 2| = 3x - 4.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{3^x + 1} + \frac{1}{3^x - 3} \leq 0.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$1 + \sin 2x = \sin x + \cos x.$$

4. One tourist left at 6 o’clock, and the second one met him at 7 o’clock. They met at 8 o’clock and, without stopping, continued their journey. How much time did each of them spend on the entire journey if the first arrived at the place from which the second left, 28 minutes later than the second arrived at the place from which the first left? Every pole stops at a constant speed.

5. The point of intersection of the medians of a right triangle is removed from the legs at distances corresponding to 3 and 4. Find the distance from this point to the hypotenuse.

2. A frog sits on the end of a dock $l=0,8$ m long. The board floats on the surface of the pond. At what minimum speed must the frog jump in order to end up at the other end of the dock after the jump? The masses of the frog and the dock are the same. Let's make a claim about the water.

3. A car with a running engine, having an initial speed of $v=72$ km/h, is driving uphill, the surface of which is inclined at an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon. What height can a mountain be overcome automatically if the coefficient of friction on the road surface is $\mu=0,4$?

4. The chamber is filled with a mixture of hydrogen and oxygen at a temperature of $t=27^\circ\text{C}$. The partial pressures of the gases in the chamber are the same. The chamber is sealed and an explosion occurs. Immediately after the completion of the reaction of combining hydrogen with oxygen, the pressure in the chamber turns out to be twice the original. What is the temperature in the chamber at this moment?

5. What mass of water rises through a capitary tube of radius $r=0,25$ mm that is wetted by it? Surface tension coefficient of water $\sigma=0,073$ N/m.

6. Charged balls, located at a distance of $r=60$ cm from each other in a vacuum, are attracted with a force of $F=0,3$ N. The total charge of the balls is $Q=4$ µC. Determine the charge of each ball. Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0=8,85 \cdot 10^{-12}$ F/m.

7. The electric motor is powered by a battery with an emf of $\varepsilon=12$ V. What power does the motor develop when current $I_1=2$ A flows through its winding, if when the armature is completely braked, current $I_2=3$ A flows through the circuit?

8. Determine the resonant frequency of the circuit if the maximum charge of the capacitor is $q_m=1$ µC, and the maximum current in the circuit is $I_m=10$ A.
