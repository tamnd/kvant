## Condition

Let $P$ and $Q$ be the midpoints of the side $AB$ and $CD$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$, and let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$. Prove that if the lines $MN$ and $PQ$ are perpendicular, then $|BC| = |AD|$.

B. B. Prasolov
