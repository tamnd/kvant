## Condition

In the center of the square is a policeman, and at one of the vertices is a gangster. The policeman can be all over the square, but the gangster can only be on its sides. From-

⟦folio 47⟧

all that the maximum speed of a policeman is $u$, and that of a gangster is $v$. The goal of the policeman is to be on the same side of the square with the gangster. Prove that

a) if $\frac{u}{v} > \frac{1}{3}$, then he can achieve his goal;
b) if $\frac{u}{v} < \frac{1}{3}$, then the gangster can let him do it.

A. Belkin
