## Condition

In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ the diagonals intersect at the point $O$. Prove that the equality
$$AB^2 + BC^2 + CD^2 + DA^2 = 2(AO^2 + BO^2 + CO^2 + DO^2)$$
is satisfied if and only if either the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpediculars, or one of them is bisected by the point $O$.

A. P. Savin

## Solution

In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ the dizonals intersect at point O. Prove that the equality $AB^2+BC^2+CD^2+DA^2=2(AO^2+BO^2+CO^2+DO^2)$ holds and bisects at point O.

Let us write the cosine theorem for triangles $AOB, BOC, COD$ and $DOA$, assuming $\angle AOB=\alpha$ (and thus $\angle BOC=\pi-\alpha$; see figure):

$$AB^2=AO^2+BO^2-2AO \cdot BO \cos \alpha,$$
$$BC^2=BO^2+CO^2+2BO \cdot CO \cos \alpha,$$
$$CD^2=CO^2+DO^2-2CO \cdot DO \cos \alpha,$$
$$DA^2=DO^2+AO^2+2DO \cdot AO \cos \alpha.$$

Let's add these equalities and intersect the squares of the diazonal segments to the left side:

$$AB^2+BC^2+CD^2+DA^2-2(AO^2+BO^2+CO^2+DO^2)=2 \cos \alpha(-AO \cdot BO+BO \cdot CO-CO \cdot DO+DO \cdot AO)=2 \cos \alpha(BO-DO)(CO-AO).$$
