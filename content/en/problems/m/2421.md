## Condition

The company recorded its expenses in rubles for 100 budget items, receiving a list of 100 numbers (each number has no more than two decimal places). Each accountant takes a copy of the list and finds an approximate amount of expenses, proceeding as follows. First, he selects any two numbers from the list, adds them, discards the decimal places from the sum (if any) and writes the result instead of the selected two numbers. With the resulting list of 99 numbers, he does the same, and so on, until there is only one integer left in the list. It turned out that in the end all accountants got different results. What is the largest number of accountants that could work in the company? M. Evdokimov
