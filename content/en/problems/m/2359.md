## Condition

A regular 1001-gon is drawn on the board, the vertices of which are numbered 1, 2, ......, 1001. The same regular 1001-gon is cut out of cardboard. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, ..., 1001 (each number once) at the vertices of a cardboard 1001-gon so that for any overlap of 1001-gons, equal numbers appear at some vertex (the cardboard polygon can be turned over)? V. Senderov
