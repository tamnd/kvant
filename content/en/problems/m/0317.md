## Condition

On some planets, each country borders no more than 7 others. Every country has a reserve of gold. It is required to distribute gold so that each two countries bordering each other differ in the amount of gold by no more than 13 times. Prove that the distribution of gold can be organized so that each country loses no more than half of its gold.

G. V. Egorov
