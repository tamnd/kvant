## Condition

Pasha and Vova play the game, taking turns crossing out cells on the board 3 u 101. Initially, only the central cell is crossed out on the board. In one move, the player must choose a diagonal (a diagonal can have 1, 2 or 3 cells) and cross out all the uncrossed cells in it. Each move must cross out at least one new cell. The one who cannot make a move loses. Pasha begins. Which player can win regardless of the opponent's moves? I. Efremov
