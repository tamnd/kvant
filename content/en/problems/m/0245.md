## Condition

It is proposed to construct $N$ points on the plane so that all pairwise distances between them are equal to a predetermined number:
for each two points $M_i, M_j$ it is known what the distance $|M_i M_j| = r_{ij}$ should be ($i$ and $j$ are any numbers from 1 to $N$).
a) Is it possible to construct if the distances $r_{ij}$ are given in such a way that any $N$ points can be constructed?
b) Is it enough to require that all $N$ points can be constructed?
b) What will change if you build points not on a plane, but in space? What then is the smallest $K$ for which it is possible to construct any $K$ points from the given $N$ points and ensure the construction of all $N$ points?
