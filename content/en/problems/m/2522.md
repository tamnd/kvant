## Condition

Find all natural numbers n for which there is a permutation $a_{1}$, … $, a_{n}$ of the numbers 1, …, n such that all numbers $a_{1} - 1, a_{2} - 2$, …, $a_{n} - n$ are pairwise distinct. Folklore
