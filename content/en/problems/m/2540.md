## Condition

In an acute triangle ABC the altitudes $AH_{a}, BH_{b}, CH_{c}$ are drawn.

Fig. 1

⟦column⟧

Let $H_{ab}$ be the projection of the point $H_{a}$ onto the line AC (Fig.1). Let us similarly define the points $H_{ac}, H_{ba}, H_{bc}, H_{ca}, H_{cb}$. Prove that six points $H_{ab}, H_{ac}, H_{ba}, H_{bc}, H_{ca}, H_{cb}$ lie on one circle, the center of which coincides with the center of mass of a triangular contour $H_{a} H_{b} H_{c}$ made of uniform wire. I. Weinstein

$M2541^{*}$. The plane is divided into convex heptagons of unit diameter. Prove that any circle of radius 200 intersects at least a billion of these heptagons. (The diameter of a polygon is the maximum among the distances between pairs of its vertices.) A. Kanel-Belov
