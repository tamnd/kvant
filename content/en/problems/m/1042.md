## Condition

The class is limited to touring on a re-geared rope. All kinds of teams that can be made up of students of this class (of one, two, etc. people, except for the team of the entire class) should participate in the tour once at a time. Prove what each team will do with a team consisting of all the other students in the class.

I. I. Grinichuk
