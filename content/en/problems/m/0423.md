## Condition

Prove that for any real $x, y$ and $z$ the following inequality holds:
$(x^2 + y^2 - z^2)(x^2 + z^2 - y^2)(y^2 + z^2 - x^2) \leqslant (x + y - z)^2(x + z - y)^2(y + z - x)^2$.
