## Condition

a) Trapezoid ABCD with bases AB and CD is inscribed in a circle Ω. Circle $\omega$ passes through points C, D and intersects segments CA, CB at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, respectively. Points $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ are symmetrical to points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ relative to the midpoints of segments CA and CB, respectively. Prove that points $A, B, A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ lie on the same circle. b) Sphere $\omega$ passes through the vertex S of the pyramid

SABC and intersects edges SA, SB and SC secondarily at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, respectively. The sphere Ω circumscribed about the pyramid SABC intersects $\omega$ along a circle lying in a plane parallel to the plane ABC. Points $A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ are symmetrical to points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ with respect to the midpoints of the edges SA, SB and SC, respectively. Prove that points $A, B, C, A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ lie on the same sphere. I. Bogdanov
