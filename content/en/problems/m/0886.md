## Condition

Is it possible to arrange 4n-4 consecutive integers (not necessarily positive) in 4n-4 cells located along the perimeter of a square of n×n cells so that the sum of the numbers at the vertices of each rectangle, the sides of which are parallel to the diagonals of the square, as well as the sum of the numbers at the ends of each diagonal equals the same number s? Solve the problem for n equal to: a) 3, 6) 4, c) 5, d) 1985. If possible, find the acceptable values ​​of s.

V. G. Boltyansky
