## Condition

Lyuda, Marina and Natasha drew the acute triangle LMN. Then Luda constructed her own triangle, in which the lengths of two sides are equal to $|LM|$ and $|LN|$, and the angle between them is 60° greater than the angle $L$ of the triangle LMN. In the same way, Marina constructed her triangle from the sides of length $|ML|$ and $|MN|$, the angle between them is 60° greater than $\hat{M}$, and Natasha built hers, whose angle between the sides $|NL|$ and $|NM|$ is $\hat{N} + 60^\circ$. Prove that the third (new) sides of the triangles are the same for all three girls.

A. Kaplan
