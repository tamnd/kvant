## Condition

Let's put $a_n = \sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}$ and $b_n = \sqrt{4n+2}$. Prove that for any natural number n
a) $[a_n] = [b_n]$, where $[x]$ is the integer part of the number x;
6) $0 < b_n - a_n < \frac{1}{16n\sqrt{n}}$.
