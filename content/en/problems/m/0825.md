Straight. It is known that any segment of length no more than 1 can be placed on a line so that its ends belong to the set M. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments that make up M is not less than 1/k. (10)

E. I. Khukhro

## Solution

The set M consists of k pairwise disjoint segments lying on the same line. It is known that any segment of length no more than 1 can be placed on a straight line so that its ends belong to the set M. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments that make up M is not less than 1/k.
