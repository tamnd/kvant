## Condition

In one of the second geometry assignments, the following task was placed: calculate the length of the side of a right triangle placed in parabolic $y = x^2$. The instructions for the problem said that one of the vertices of the triangle coincides with the vertex of the parabolic. Is this indication correct? can the side length of a right triangle inserted into this parabola be equal to a) 3; b) 1988?
