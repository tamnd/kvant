## Condition

a) A rectangle with sides a and b is inscribed in a triangle so that all its vertices lie on the sides of the triangle. Let a₁ and b₁ be the lengths of the projections of the triangle onto lines parallel to sides a and b of the rectangle. Prove equality
$$\frac{a}{a_1} + \frac{b}{b_1} = 1.$$

6) An arbitrary tetrahedron is inscribed with rectangular parallel edges a, b and c so that all its vertices lie on the surface of the tetrahedron. Let a₁, b₁ and c₁ be the lengths of the projections of the tetrahedron onto straight lines parallel to the edges a, b and c. Prove equality
$$\frac{a}{a_1} + \frac{b}{b_1} + \frac{c}{c_1} = 1.$$
