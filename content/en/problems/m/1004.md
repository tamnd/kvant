## Condition

Through vertex A of triangle ABC, in which AB≠AC all possible straight lines are drawn. Prove that a) on each of them there is more than one point M, different from the vertices of the triangle, for which ∠ABM=∠ACM;
b) there are at most one fifth of these lines on which there is no such point M.

O. P. Musin
