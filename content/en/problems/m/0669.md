## Condition

The quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. Prove that

a) the segment connecting the midpoints of the angles $AB$ and $CD$ is perpendicular to the segment connecting the angles $BC$ and $AD$;

b) the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles $ABC, BCD, CDA$ and $DAB$ are the vertices of the rectangle.

K. Malkhasyan, I. German

## Solution

Quadrilateral ABCD is inscribed in a circle. Prove that a) the segment connecting two terms AB and CD is perpendicular to the segment connecting two terms BC and AD; b) the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles ABC, BCD, CDA and DAB are the vertices of the straight line.**

a) Since $NMP=\frac{1}{4}(BC+CD)=\frac{1}{2}\hat{A}, MNQ=\frac{1}{4}(AB+AD)=\frac{1}{2}\hat{C}$ (Fig. 1), $NMP+MNQ=\frac{1}{2}(\hat{A}+\hat{C})=90^\circ$, the inscription of the corresponding triangle ABC, BCD, CDA and DAB (Fig. 2) is revealed.

The triangles BMN and MO$_1N$ are congruent, so $|BN|=|NO_1|$. Likewise, $|CN|=|NO_2|$. But $|BN|=|CN|$; therefore, the triangle $O_1NO_2$ is equilateral, in which $[NQ]$ is a bissertrix. Therefore $[NQ] \perp [O_1O_2]$; means $[O_1O_2] \parallel [MP]$.
