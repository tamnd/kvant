## Condition

Points M and N are taken on the diagonals AC and CE of the regular hexagon ABCDEF, respectively, such that

$$\frac{|AM|}{|AC|} = \frac{|CN|}{|CE|} = \lambda.$$

It is known that points B, M and N lie on the same line. Find λ.
