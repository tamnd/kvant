## Condition

Two straight lines drawn through one and the other intersection point of the extensions of opposite sides of a convex quadrilateral cut it into four smaller quadrilaterals. Prove that if circles can be written into two of them that do not have a common side, then a circle can be written into the original quadrilateral.

I. F. Sharyzin
