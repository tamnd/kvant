## Condition

The outline of a house 60 m long and 15 m wide is copied from the photograph (Fig. 2), with the longer wall in the photograph on the left (the remaining parts of the outline in the photograph are blocked by a tree branch). Required:
a) complete the outline;
b) complete the drawing of an accurate map (projection on a horizontal plane), on which indicate the outline of the house and the shooting point;
b) determine the height of the house and the height from which the shooting is arbitrary.

D. B. Fuchs
