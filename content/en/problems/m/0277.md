## Condition

Several red and several blue dots are specified. Some of them are connected by segments. Let's call a point “special” if more than half of the points connected to it have a color different from its color. If there is at least one special point, then any special point is selected and repainted in a different color. Prove that after a finite number of steps there will be no singular point left. (10 grades)

A. M. Steinberg
