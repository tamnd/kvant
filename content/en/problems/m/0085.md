## Condition

Prove that if $a_1, a_2, ..., a_m$ are pairwise distinct natural numbers, none of which is divisible by the square of an integer greater than one, and $b_1, b_2, ..., b_m$ are integers other than zero, then

$$b_1 \sqrt{a_1} + b_2 \sqrt{a_2} + \ldots + b_m \sqrt{a_m} \neq 0.$$

L. N. Vaserstein
