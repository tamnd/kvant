## Condition

Let A c, B c, C c be the midpoints of sides BC, CA, AB, respectively, in regular triangle ABC (Fig. 1). On the continuation of the segment A c B c, point D is taken as B c, and on the continuation of the segment A c C c, point E is taken as C c so that the segment DE passes through point A. The segments B c E and C c D intersect at point X.

⟦column⟧

a) Prove that points X, A c, B c, C c lie on the same circle. b) Prove that the segments A c X, CE, BD and B c C c intersect at one point. K. Bayrak, 11th grade student
