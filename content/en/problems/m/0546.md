## Condition

From an arbitrary point $M$ of a circle circumscribed about a rectangle, we dropped perpendiculars $MP$ and $MQ$ onto its two opposite sides, and perpendiculars $MR$ and $MT$ onto the extensions of the other two sides. Prove that lines $PR$ and $QT$ are perpendicular to each other, and their intersection point belongs to the diagonal of the rectangle.

Vvede Shafaryan, 10th grade student (Yerevan)

## Solution

From an arbitrary point M of the circle circumscribed about the rectangle, we dropped perpendiculars MP and MQ to its two opposite sides and perpendiculars MR and MT to the extensions of the other two sides. Prove that the perpendiculars PR and QT are straight and others others others. And their intersection point belongs to the diagonals of the rectilinear.
