## Condition

The incircle touches sides AB, BC and AC of triangle ABC at points N, K and M respectively (Fig.1).

Lines MN and MK intersect the bisector of the external angle B at points R and S, respectively. Prove that the lines RK and SN intersect on the incircle of triangle ABC. M. Evdokimov
