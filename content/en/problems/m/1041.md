## Condition

A) four, b) three vertices are given on the plane. Using a double-sided ruler, restore the remaining vertices. (With a double-sided ruler you can do the same as a regular ruler without divisions, and also draw a straight line parallel to this one at a distance equal to the width of the ruler.)

## Solution

The following are given on the plane: a) four, b) three vertices of a right pentagon. Using a double-sided ruler, restore its remaining vertices. (With a double-sided line you can do the same as a regular line without division, and also draw a straight line parallel to the given one at a distance equal to the width of the line.)
