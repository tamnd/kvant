## Condition

On the segment $0 \leq x \leq 1$ the function $f$ is given. It is known that this function is non-negative and $f(1) = 1$. In addition, for any two numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ such that $x_1 \geq 0$, $x_2 \geq 0$ and $x_1 + x_2 \leq 1$, the inequality

$$f(x_1 + x_2) \geq f(x_1) + f(x_2).$$

a) Prove that whatever function $f$ is that satisfies the above conditions, the inequality $f(x) \leq 2x$ will be satisfied for all $x$.

6) Is it true that for everyone $x$

$$f(x) \leq 1,9 \ x ?$$

(10 grades)

A. V. Popov

$$f(x_1 + x_2) \geq f(x_1) + f(x_2).$$
