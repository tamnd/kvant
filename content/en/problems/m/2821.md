## Condition

A road on a plane is a straight line along which you can move only in one direction. a) Petya and Vasya take turns leading the roads, Petya begins. Only horizontal or vertical roads are allowed; The direction on the road is chosen by the one who draws the road (when creating this road). Can Vasya always act in such a way that after any of his moves it is possible to travel according to the rules from any crossroads to any other, no matter how Petya acts? b) Let there be several (finite set) roads on the plane, any two of which intersect. For what is the smallest m it is always possible to add m roads and make it so that you can drive from any intersection to any other? A.Perepechko
