## Condition

Given an angle $AOB$ (A and B are points on the sides of the angle). Construct a line $l$ passing through the vertex $O$ so that the areas of triangles $AOC$ and $BOD$, where $C$ and $D$ are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from the points $A$ and $B$ to the line $l$, are equal.
