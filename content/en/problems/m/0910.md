## Condition

The points $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_6$ (Fig. 1) are taken on the sides of a regular hexagon. It is known that three pairwise non-funny sides of the hexagon $A_1 \dots A_6$ ($A_1A_2, A_3A_4, A_5A_6$) define the triangle KLM, the vertices of which lie on the extensions of the diagonals of a regular hexagon. Prove that this is also true for the other three sides of the hexagon $A_1 \dots A_6$.

S. Yu. Orevkov
