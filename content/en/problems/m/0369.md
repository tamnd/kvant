## Condition

Given an acute triangle ABC. H is the point of intersection of its heights, γ is a circle with center H lying inside this triangle. Construct a triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ circumscribed about the circle γ and inscribed in triangle ABC (so that

$$A_1 \in [BC], B_1 \in [AC], C_1 \in [AB].$$

## Solution
