## Condition

Each vertex of a regular pentagon is assigned an integer so that the sum of all five numbers is positive. The following operation is allowed: if trem consecutive vertices are assigned the numbers x, y, z and y<0, then these numbers are replaced by x+y, -y and z+y, respectively. Such operations are performed as long as at least one of the five numbers is negative. Does this process necessarily end in a finite number of steps?
