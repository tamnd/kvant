## Condition

Given a prime number p. There are p integers around the circle. In one move, you are allowed to choose some integer k and replace the existing set $a_{0},^{…}, a_{p} 1$ with a set of numbers $a_{0} a_{k}, a_{1} a_{1} k,^{…}, a_{p} 1 a_{p} 1_{k}$ (indices are taken modulo p, i.e. a i a i p ). a) Prove that after performing p such moves, all numbers on the circle will be multiples of p. b*) Find all pairs of natural numbers m, n, n! 1, for which the following statement is true: for any initial set of p integers, after performing any m of the described moves, a set of p numbers that are multiples of n will be obtained. P. Kozhevnikov
