## Condition

Prove that for any triangle the projection of the diameter of the circumscribed circle perpendicular to one side of the triangle onto the line containing the second side is equal to the length of the triangle.
S. Ovchinnikov

## Solution

Prove that for any triangle, the projection of the diameter of the circumscribed circle perpendicular to one side of the triangle onto the line containing the second side is equal in length to the triangle.
