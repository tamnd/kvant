## Condition

Let $h_a, h_b, h_c$ be the altitudes, $m_a, m_b, m_c$ the medians of the acute triangle (drawn to the sides $a, b, c$), $r$ and $R$ the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circle. Prove that

$$\frac{m_a}{h_a} + \frac{m_b}{h_b} + \frac{m_c}{h_c} \leq 1 + \frac{R}{r}.$$

D. M. Milosevic
