## Condition

When the hockey tournament ended (in one round), it turned out that for any group of teams you can find a team (maybe from the same group) that scored an odd number of points in games with teams from this group. Prove that the number of teams participated in the tournament. (Loss - 0 points, draw - 1 point, win - 2 points.) (10 grades).

G. A. Kasparov
