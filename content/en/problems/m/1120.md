## Condition

a) The sequence $a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots$ is given by the relations $a_0=0, a_n=P(a_{n-1}), n=1, 2, \dots$, where $P(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, $P(x)>0$ for $x\geq 0$. Prove that for any natural numbers $m$ and $k$

$$\text{НОД}(a_m, a_k)=a_{\text{НОД}(m, k)}$$

b) Prove a similar statement for the Fibonacci sequence $a_0=0, a_1=1, a_2=1, a_3=2, \dots$ given by condition $a_{n+1}=a_n+a_{n-1}, n=1, 2, \dots$.

V. F. Lev
