## Condition

Two points A and B are given on the plane. Find the locus of the third vertex C of triangle ABC, which has:
a) height AA' is equal to side BC;
b) the median AA₁ is equal to the side AC;
b) median AA₁ is equal to side BC;
d) the height CC' is equal to the median BB₁;
e) the height BB' is equal to the median CC₁.
