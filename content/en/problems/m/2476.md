## Condition

The graphs $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ of the quadratic functions $f_{1} x p_{1} x^{2} q_{1} x r_{1}$ and $f_{2} x p_{2} x^{2} q_{2} x r_{2}$, and $p_{1} 0 p_{2}$, are plotted on the coordinate plane. The graphs $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ intersect at two different points A and B. The four tangents to $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ drawn at points A and B form a convex quadrilateral into which a circle can be inscribed. Prove that graphs $G_{1}$ and $G_{2}$ have a common axis of symmetry. A. Zaslavsky
