## Condition

The numbers 1 or -1 are written in cells of a 3x3 table. Then the number in each cell is written down by the product of the numbers in all neighboring cells (cells that have a common side are considered adjacent). Prove that after several repetitions of this operation there will be units in all cells.

I. K. Zhuk, I. V. Voronovich
