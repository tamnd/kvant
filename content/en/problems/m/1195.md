## Condition

The sequence $x_1, x_2, \dots$ is such that for any natural numbers $m$ and $n$
$$|x_{m+n} - x_m - x_n| < 1/(m+n).$$
Prove that this sequence is an arithmetic progression.
O. T. Izhboldin
