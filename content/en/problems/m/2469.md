## Condition

There are n positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2},^{…} a_{n}$ written out in a row on the board. Vasya wants to write down under each number $a_{i}$ the number $b_{i} \ge a_{i}$ so that for any two of the numbers $b_{1}, b_{2},^{…}, b_{n}$ the ratio of one of them to the other is an integer. Prove that Vasya can write down the required numbers so that the inequality n - 1 holds

$b_{1} b_{2}^{…} b_{n}$ £ $2^{2} a_{1} a_{2}^{…} a_{n}$. F.Petrov
