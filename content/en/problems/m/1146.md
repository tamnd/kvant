## Condition

Point $K$ is the midpoint of side $AB$ of equilateral triangle $ABC$. On the sides $AC$ and $BC$ the points $M$ and $N$ are taken so that $\angle MKN=60^\circ$. Prove that the perimeter of triangle $MCN$ is equal to half the perimeter of triangle $ABC$.

E. G. Gotman
