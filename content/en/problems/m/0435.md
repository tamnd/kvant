## Condition

A table of size $m \times n$ contains real numbers, with a number in each cell. In each column the k most numbers ($k \leq m$) are underlined, in each line - the l most numbers ($l \leq n$). Prove that at least kl numbers are underlined twice. First consider the case a) $k=l=2$; 6) $k=l=3$.
C. Konyatiya

## Solution

In the table of sizes $m \times n$ real numbers are written, in each cell there is a number. The $k$ largest numbers ($k \leq m$) are underlined in each column, and the $l$ largest numbers ($l \leq n$) are underlined in each row. Let us prove that at least $kl$ numbers are underlined twice.

$$k = 1; \quad l = 2.$$
