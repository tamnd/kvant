## Condition

Petya paints each cell of the 2 m u 2 n board black or white so that the cells of each color form a polygon. Then Vasya cuts the board into dominoes (rectangles of two squares). Petya strives to ensure that in the end there are as many two-color dominoes as possible, and Vasya strives to ensure that there are as few of them as possible. What is the largest number of two-color dominoes that Petya can guarantee, no matter how Vasya acts? A. Gribalko
