## Condition

A virus was placed in a cell on a checkered plane. The virus lives for one minute, after a minute it disappears, but leaves behind offspring - in each of the four cells adjacent to its cell, a new virus appears. figure shows the stages of virus reproduction (number and location) at time points t = 0, 1, 2, 3 min. Calculate the number of viruses in the initial field at t = 10 min. V. Rastorguev
