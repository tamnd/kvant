## Condition

In the card game, each card is assigned a numerical value from 1 to 100, with each card beating the smaller one, with one exception: 1 beats 100. The player knows that there are 100 cards with different values ​​lying face down in front of him. The dealer, who knows the order of these cards, can tell the player about any pair of cards which one beats which one. Prove that the dealer can make one hundred such messages, so that the player can then know the exact value of each card. I. Bogdanov, K. Knop, Y. Kuzmenko
