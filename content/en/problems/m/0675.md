## Condition

A system of weights is a set of natural numbers from which two different sets with the same sum can be extracted (for example, the numbers 24, 23, 22, 20, 17, 11 form a system of weights, but the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8 do not form: $2+3+4=1+8$). Prove that from numbers less than 1000 it is possible to select a system of weights consisting of: a) 10 numbers, b) 11 numbers.

⟦folio 25⟧

c) Prove that it is impossible to choose 14 numbers from them.
d) Prove that if the numbers form a system of weights, then the sum of their reciprocals does not exceed 5/2.
e) Choose a weighting system of 11 numbers from numbers less than 700.

G. Gurevich, A. Kolotov
