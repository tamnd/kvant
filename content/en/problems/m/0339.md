## Condition

Given a horizontal strip on a plane, the edges of which are parallel lines, and n straight lines intersecting this strip. Which two of these n lines intersect internally?

⟦folio 50⟧

three stripes and no three pass through the same point. The distance is all the paths starting at the lower edge of the strip, going along these straight lines, and ending at the upper edge, having the following property: walking along this path, we always go up: having reached the point of intersection of the straight lines, we must move to another straight line. Prove that among such paths

a) there is a path consisting of at least $n$ segments;
b) there is a path passing along at most $\frac{n}{2} + 1$ straight lines;
b) there is a path passing along all $n$ straight lines. (8-9 grades)

A. V. Karzanov
