## Condition

A point X is selected inside a convex 100-gon that does not lie on any of its sides or diagonals. Initially, the vertices of the polygon are not marked. Petya and Vasya take turns marking the yet unmarked vertices of the 100-gon, and Petya starts and marks two vertices at once with his first move, and then each marks one vertex with his next move. The loser is the one after whose move point X will lie inside the polygon with marked vertices. Prove that Petya can win no matter how Vasya moves. S. Berlov, F. Petrov
