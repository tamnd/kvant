## Condition

Circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ of the same radius intersect at points $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ (Fig. 1). Circle $\omega$ touches circle $\omega_{1}$ externally at point $T_{1}$ and circle $\omega_{2}$ internally at point $T_{2}$. Prove that the lines $X_{1} T_{1}$ and $X_{2} T_{2}$ intersect on the circle $\omega$. Ch. Latham (Luxembourg)
