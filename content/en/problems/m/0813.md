## Condition

Given segments $OA, OB$ and $OC$ of the same length (point $B$ lies inside angle $AOC$). Circles are built on them as on diameters. Prove that the area of ​​a curved triangle bounded by two of these circles and not containing the point $O$ (see Fig. 1, p. 44) is equal to half the area (of the oblique triangle $ABC$).
