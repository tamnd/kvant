## Condition

Let p and q be relatively prime natural numbers. The frog jumps along the number line, starting at point 0. Each time it jumps either p to the right or q to the left. One day the frog returned to 0. Prove that for any natural number d p q there are two numbers visited by the frog that differ exactly by d. N. Belukhov
