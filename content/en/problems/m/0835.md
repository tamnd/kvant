## Condition

$n$ chess players from country $A$ and $n$ chess players from country $B$ came to the one-round chess tournament. It turned out that no matter how the chess players are divided into pairs (so that the chess differences of countries play against each other), there will be at least one pair of chess players who have already met each other before. Prove that it is possible to select $a$ chess players from country $A$ and $b$ chess players from country $B$ such that each of the chosen $a$
