## Condition

Prove that for any natural number $n$ the equality

$$[\sqrt{n}] + [\sqrt[3]{n}] + [\sqrt[4]{n}] + \ldots + [\sqrt[n]{n}] = [\log_2 n] + [\log_3 n] + [\log_4 n] + \ldots + [\log_n n]$$

([$x$] means the integer part of the number $x$).
