## Condition

Given an unequal triangle $A_1A_2A_3$. Let $a_i$ be its side lying opposite the vertex $A_i$ ($i=1, 2, 3$), $M_i$ the midpoint of side $a_i$, $T_i$ the point of tangency of the side with the circle inscribed in the given triangle, and $S_i$ the point symmetrical to $T_i$ with respect to the bissertrix of the angle $A_i$ triangle. Prove that the lines $M_1S_1$, $M_2S_2$ and $M_3S_3$ have a common point.

## Solution

Given an unequal triangle $A_1A_2A_3$. Let $a_i$ be its side lying opposite the vertex $A_i$ ($i=1, 2, 3$), $M_i$ the midpoint of side $a_i$, $T_i$ the point of tangency of the side with the circle inscribed in the given triangle, and $S_i$ the point symmetrical to $T_i$ with respect to the bisector of the angle $A_i$ triangle.

Prove that the lines $M_1S_1$, $M_2S_2$ and $M_3S_3$ have a common point.

The sides of triangle $M_1M_2M_3$ are respectively parallel to the sides of triangle $A_1A_2A_3$. We will prove that the sides of the triangle $S_1S_2S_3$ are parallel to the sides $A_1A_2A_3$. It follows that $\triangle S_1S_2S_3$ is prepared into it by parallel transfer. The second case disappears, or the circumcircle of triangle $M_1M_2M_3$ is larger than the circumcircle of triangle $S_1S_2S_3$. Consequently, the lines connecting the corresponding vertices of triangles $S_1S_2S_3$ and $M_1M_2M_3$ must intersect at one point - the center is being prepared.

Let us show, for example, that the lines $S_1S_2$ and $A_1A_2$ are parallel (see figure). If the relative bisector of the angle $A_1$ is symmetrical, the point $S_1$ will go to $T_1$, and $T_3$ to $T_2$,

the positional arcs $S_1T_3$ and $T_1T_2$ of the incircle of triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ are equal. Similarly, if the relative bisector of the angle $A_2$ is symmetrical, the arc $T_1T_2$ will transform into the arc $T_3S_2$. Consequently, the arcs $S_1T_3$ and $T_3S_2$ are equal, and the position of the points $S_1$ and $S_2$ is at the same distance from the straight line $A_1A_2$, that is, $S_1S_2 \parallel A_1A_2$. Similarly, it is proved that the other two sides of the triangle $S_1S_2S_3$ are parallel to the corresponding sides of the triangle $A_1A_2A_3$.
