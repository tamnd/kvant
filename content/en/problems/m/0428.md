## Condition

$(m - 1)n + 1$ people are participating in the Olympiad. Prove that among them there are either $m$ participants who do not know each other in pairs, or there is one participant who is familiar with at least $n$ Olympiad participants. Will the statement of the problem remain true if the number of participants in the Olympiad is reduced by one?
