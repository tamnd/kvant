## Condition

Anya and Maxim play a game on a checkered board 100 and 100. First, Anya fills the squares of the board with integers from 1 to 10,000 so that each number occurs once. Maxim then places a chip on one of the cells of the leftmost column of his choice. Then, in a few moves, he moves the chip to the rightmost column. In one move, he can move a piece from a cell to any cell adjacent to it along the top or side. For each cell visited (including the initial one), Maxim pays Anya the number of coins that is written on this cell. Maxim wants to pay as little as possible, but Anya wants to get as much as possible. How many coins
