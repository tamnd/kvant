## Condition

13 knights from k different clans (1<k<13) are sitting at a round table. Everyone holds a gold or silver cup, and there are exactly k gold cups. King Arthur ordered the knights to simultaneously pass the cups to their neighbors on the right, then do the same again, etc. Prove that at some points there are no knights from the same clan holding golden cups.

A. A. Bolotov

## Solution
