## Condition

What is the smallest number of numbers that must be removed from the sequence 1, 2, 3, ... 1982 so that none of the remaining numbers is equal to the product of the other two remaining numbers? (9)

L. Kurlyandchik

## Solution

What is the smallest number of cesels that must be removed from the sequence 1, 2, 3, ..., 1982 so that none of the remaining cesels is equal to the product of the other two remaining cesels?

Answer: 43 cels; You can cross out cels 2, 3, ..., 44.

If you cross out the indicated 43 cels, then the condition of the problem will be met, or the product of any two of the remaining cels (excluding 1) will be greater than $45^2 = 2025 > 1982$. Let us now show that if we cross out less than 43 cels, then 3 cels remain, the product of two of which is equal to the third.

Consider 43 triples of cels
$(2, 87, 2 \cdot 87), (3, 86, 3 \cdot 86), ..., (44, 45, 44 \cdot 45)$.

Since the function $x \cdot (89 - x)$ on the interval [2; 44] increases, all calculated cels are different and do not exceed 44 · 45 = 1980 < 1982. If you cross out less than 43 cels, then at least one of the calculated triples will remain, and the condition of the problem will be violated.

L. Kurlyandchik
