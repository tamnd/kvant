## Condition

Given a parallelogram ABCD with an obtuse angle B, in which AD > AB (Fig. 1).

the other can be reached by making an even number of flights. For what smallest natural number d can one be guaranteed to fly from any city to any other by making an even number of flights not exceeding d? (It is allowed to visit the same city or take the same flight more than once.) I. Bogdanov
