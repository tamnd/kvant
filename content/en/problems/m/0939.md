## Condition

In the cells of a 10×10 table, the numbers 0,1,...,9 are written in some way so that each digit appears 10 times.
a) Is it possible to do this so that there are no more than 4 different digits in each row and each column?
b)* Prove that there is a row or column that contains at least 4 different digits.
L. D. Kurlyandchik
