## Condition

Prove (for any numbers $a, b, c, x, y, z$) the inequality
$$ax + by + cz + \sqrt{(a^2 + b^2 + c^2)(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)} \geq \frac{2}{3}(a + b + c)(x + y + z).$$
V. Curtoaje (Romania)
