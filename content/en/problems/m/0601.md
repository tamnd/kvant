## Condition

Prove that in any triangle $ABC$ the midpoint of side $BC$ lies on the segment connecting the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ with the point of the circle circumscribed about this triangle, diametrically opposite to the vertex $A$, and bisects this segment.
