## Condition

Let natural numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}$, … $, a_{n}$ be given, satisfying the condition: for any 1 £ i < j £ n, the number j – i is divisible by gcd $a_{i}, a_{j}$. Prove that there is a positive integer N such that N + i is divisible by $a_{i}$ for all i = 1, 2, …, n. P. Kozhevnikov, A. Tolpygo
