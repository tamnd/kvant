## Condition

Prove that, no matter how you color the cells of an infinite sheet of checkered paper in $N$ colors, there is
a) a rectangle, the vertices of which lie in the centers of cells of the same color (and the sides run parallel to the lines of the grid - along vertical and horizontal straight lines - Fig. 3, a);
b) $l$ horizontal and $m$ vertical lines that intersect at the centers of $lm$ cells of the same color ($l$ and $m$ are any natural numbers - Fig. 3, b);
c) an isosceles right triangle, the vertices of which are the centers of cells of the same color, at $N=2$ (Fig. 3, θ);
d)* the same for $N=3$.
