## Condition

Let A and B be adjacent vertices of a regular n-gon with center O. Triangle XYZ, equal to triangle OAB, first coincides with it, and then moves in the plane of the n-gon so that points Y and Z remain on the contour, and X remains inside the n-gon. What figure will point X describe when Y and Z make a full revolution along the boundary of the n-gon?

## Solution

Let $A$ and $B$ be neighboring vertices of a regular $n$-gon with center $O$. Triangle $XYZ$, equal to triangle $OAB$, first coincides with it, and then moves in the plane of the $n$-gon so that points $Y$ and $Z$ remain on the contour, and $X$ - inside the $n$-gon. Each figure will be described by the point $X$ when $Y$ and $Z$ make a complete revolution along the boundary of the $n$-gon?

Answer. The required set is a regular “star” composed of $n$ equal segments of length $kR$, where $k = \frac{1}{\cos(\pi/n)} - 1$ and $R$ is the circumradius of the given $n$-gon (see Fig. 1 for $n=5$); these segments are obtained from the segments connecting the center $O$ of the polygon with its vertices, with tomotonie with the coefficient - $k$ and the center $O$.

Consider two consecutive sides $AB$ and $BC$ of a given polygon (Fig. 2); let $Y$ lie on $AB$, $Z$ on $BC$. When turning with center $X$ by angle $2\pi/n$ (equal to angle $YXZ$), point $Y$ will go into $Z$, and straight line $AB$ into a straight line passing through $Z$ and making angle $2\pi/n$ with $AB$, i.e. i.e. straight line $BC$.
