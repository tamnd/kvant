## Condition

Let's call a word a line of 10 digits 0 and 1. We will consider two words synonyms if one can be obtained from the other through several operations of the following type: several numbers in a row are crossed out from the word, the sum of which is even, and the same numbers are written in their place, but in reverse order. What is the maximum number of words that do not have synonyms?
