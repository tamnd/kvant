## Condition

Let $0 \leq i_1 < i_2 < \dots < i_n$ be integers. Prove that the number of odd coefficients of a polynomial

$$(1+x)^{i_1}+(1+x)^{i_2}+\dots+(1+x)^{i_n}$$

no less than that of the polynomial $(1+x)^{i_1}$.
