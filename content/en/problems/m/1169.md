## Condition

The tonearm of the turntable is a light straight rod of length L (Fig. 2), at one end of which (at point B) a sound speaker with a stylus is fixed, and the other end is fixed in a hinge, which can rotate without friction about the vertical axis A, passing at a distance R (R>L) from the axis of rotation O of the turntable disk. The needle is placed on the flat, single-water surface of a uniformly rotating disk. Find the steady-state angle α between the tonearm and linear AO.

S. F. Kim, A. I. Latynin

## Solution

Let $M$ be a point lying inside the line ABCD, $S$ its area. Prove the inequality $S \leq AM \cdot CM + BM \cdot DM$.**

It is clear that the area of ​​the rectangle is twice the sum of the areas of triangles AMD and BMC. (To prove it, it is enough to check through $M$ the line parallel to $AD$, and then that the area of ​​each of the two formed straight lines/Lliners.) Apply to the side $AD$ the triangle AND equal to CMB so that $AN=CM$, $DN=BM$ (see Fig.ok). Then

$$S = 2(S_{AMD} + S_{BMC}) = 2S_{AMDN} = 2S_{AMN} + 2S_{DMN} \leq AM \cdot AN + DN \cdot DM = AM \cdot CM + BM \cdot DM,$$

Q.E.D.

I. Ya. Goldsheid
