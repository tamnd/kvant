## Condition

The sides, diagonals and extensions of the main trapezoid intersect straight line l at six points, i.e., cut out five segments on straight l.
a) Prove that if the outermost (1st and 5th) segments are equal, then the adjacent ones (2nd and 4th) are also equal.
b) For what ratio of the main trapezoid can the straight line l be checked so that all five segments are equal?

E. G. Gotman
