## Condition

The piece stands in the corner of the chessboard with dimensions of $n \times n$ cells. Each of the two players takes turns moving it to an adjacent field (which has a common side with the one on which the chip stands). You cannot go to the field a second time where the chip has already been beaten. The one who has nowhere to go loses.
a) Prove that if $n$ is even, then the one who starts the game can achieve a win, and if $n$ is odd, then the second one wins.
b) Who wins if initially the chip is not on the corner square, but on the one adjacent to it? (8 grades)
