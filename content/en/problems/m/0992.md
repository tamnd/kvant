## Condition

Among 90 graduates of one mathematical hypnasium, each of them has at least 10 friends. Prove that any graduate can invite three friends to visit, so that among the four gathered, each will have at least two friends.

## Solution

Among 90 graduates of one mathematical summation, each has at least 10 others. Prove that a graduate can visit three others so that among the four there are at least two others.
