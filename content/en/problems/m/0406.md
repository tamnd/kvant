## Condition

A circle of radius $R$ is divided by points $A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4$ into four equal arcs. Prove that the sum of four powers of distances from an arbitrary point on the circle $M$ to the point $A_k$ does not depend on the position of the point $M$, and
$$|A_1M|^4 + |A_2M|^4 + |A_3M|^4 + |A_4M|^4 = 24R^4.$$
Yu. Babenko
