## Condition

On the white cells of an infinite chessboard filled with a half-plane (Fig. 1) at the top, some numbers are written so that for each black cell the sum of the numbers in the two cells adjacent to it - to the right and to the left - is equal to the sum of the other two numbers located in the cells adjacent to it - above and below. You know the number in one cell of the $n$th line (blue cross on figure 1), and you need to find out the number above it in the $(n+2)$th line (red question mark on figure). How many more numbers in the bottom two lines (blue dots on figure) do you need to know?

M. L. Gerver
