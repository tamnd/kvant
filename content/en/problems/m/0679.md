## Condition

a) Four circles are located on a plane so that the first touches the second at point $A$, the second touches the third at point $B$, the third touches the fourth
at point $C$ and the fourth - the first at point $D$ (Fig. 2). Prove that through the four named points you can check a circle or a straight line.
b) *Four balls are located in space so that the first touches the second at point $A$, the second touches the third at point $B$, the third touches the fourth
at point $C$ and the fourth - the first at point $D$. Prove that through the four named points you can check a circle or a straight line.
b) *Four balls are located in space so that each touches the other three. Prove that six points of tangency belong to one sphere
or one plane.
