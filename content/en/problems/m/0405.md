## Condition

On the chessboard of size $99 \times 99$ the figure F is marked (this figure will be different in points a), b) and c)). In each cell of the F figure there is a beetle. At each moment the beetles flew up and landed again in the cells of the same figure F; Moreover, several beetles could eat in one cage. After the flight, any two beetles that occupied adjacent cells ended up back in adjacent cells or ended up on the same cell. (Neighbouring are cells that have a common side or a common vertex.)
