## Condition

Given six numbers a₁, a₂, ..., a₆. To calculate “in any case” the sum of their pairwise products a₁a₂+a₁a₃+...+a₅a₆, you need to spend 15 multiplications and 14 additions. Prove how you can immediately find the sum of these numbers, the sum of their products of two, three, four and five, using only 15 additions and 14 multiplications.

N. V. Vasiliev
