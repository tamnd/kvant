## Condition

At a dance lesson, 17 boys and 17 girls lined up in two parallel rows to form 17 pairs. Moreover, in each pair, the height of the boy differed from the height of the girls by no more than ten centimeters. Prove that if in each row the boys and girls are overgrown in height, then in each pair the boy and girl will still differ in height by no more than ten centimeters.

A. G. Pechkovsky
