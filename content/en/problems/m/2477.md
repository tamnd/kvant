## Condition

There are 25 masks, each in its own color. k wise men play a game: they are shown all the masks, then they agree among themselves, after which they are put on masks in such a way that each of them sees the masks of everyone else (but does not know who is wearing them) and does not see his own. No forms of interaction are allowed in this case. All sages simultaneously name one color, trying to guess the color of their mask. At what minimum k can they agree in advance so that at least one of them is sure to guess right? S. Berlov
