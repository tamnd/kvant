## Condition

There are jugs of juices in a circle, not necessarily of the same size. From any jug you are allowed to pour any part of the juice (perhaps none or all the juice) into the adjacent jug on the right, so that it does not overflow and the sweetness of the mixture in it becomes equal to 10%. It is known that at the initial moment such a transfusion could be done from any jug. Prove that it is possible to make several such transfusions in some order (no more than one from each jug), so that the sweetness of the mixture in all non-empty jugs becomes equal to 10%. A. Shapovalov
