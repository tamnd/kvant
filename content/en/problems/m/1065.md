## Condition

* We will consider vectors $(x; y)$ with integer non-negative coordinates, and at least one of the coordinates is different from 0. Let us isose such a generator vector if $|x - y| = 1$.

a) Prove that the vector $(x; y)$ under consideration can be represented as a sum of generators (or it itself is a generator) if and only if the quantity $k(x, y) = x + y - (x - y)^2$ is non-negative.

6) Prove that the number $n(x, y)$ different (up to order of magnitude) is represented by the vector $(x; y)$ as the sum of the generators of the table so that the ball hits the floor as far as possible from the starting point? What is this maximum distance?

A. V. Khelsis
