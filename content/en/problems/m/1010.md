## Condition

The sequence $r_1, r_2, r_3, \dots$ is determined by the conditions
$$r_1 = 2, r_{n+1} = r_1 r_2 \dots r_n + 1,$$
so $r_2 = 3, r_3 = 7, r_4 = 43, \dots$
a) Prove that for any $n$
$$\frac{1}{r_1} + \frac{1}{r_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{r_n} < 1.$$

6)* Let there be $n$ natural numbers such that the sum of their reciprocals is less than 1. Prove that this sum is not more than
$$\frac{1}{r_1} + \frac{1}{r_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{r_n}.$$

The proofs known to us are based on such a lemma, which we propose to prove to the reader.

b)* Among all sets $(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n)$ of $n$ real numbers satisfying the conditions:
$$\alpha_1 \geqslant \alpha_2 \geqslant \dots \geqslant \alpha_n \geqslant 0, \alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + \dots + \alpha_n = 1,$$
$$\alpha_1 \alpha_2 \dots \alpha_k \leqslant \alpha_{k+1} + \alpha_{k+2} + \dots + \alpha_n (k = 1, 2, \dots, n-1),$$
