## Condition

A piece of cheese must be cut into pieces in compliance with the following rules: 1) first we cut the cheese into 2 pieces, then we cut one of them into 2 more pieces, then we cut one of the three pieces into 2 pieces again, etc.; 2) after each cutting, the parts can be different in weight, but the ratio of the weight of any part to the weight of any other must be strictly greater than a given number R. a) Prove that with R = 0.5 it is possible to cut cheese so that the process never stops (after any number of cuts it will be possible to cut another piece). b) Prove that if R > 0.5, then the cutting process will someday stop.

⟦column⟧

c) What is the largest number of pieces that the cheese can be cut into if R = 0.6? d) Prove that if for a given R the cheese can be cut into 11 pieces, then it can also be cut into 12 pieces. A.Tolpygo
