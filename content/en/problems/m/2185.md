## Condition

Given a natural number $n \ge 5$. It is known about the pyramid $SA_{1} A_{2}$ … $A_{n}$ that its base $A_{1} A_{2}$ … $A_{n}$ is a regular n-gon, and all the side faces are isosceles triangles (not necessarily with vertex S). Does this pyramid have to be correct? G. Galperin
