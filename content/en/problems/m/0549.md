## Condition

Given a natural number $N$. Let's write down all its divisors $d_1, d_2, \dots, d_n$ and for each of them we will find how many divisors it has. Prove that the resulting numbers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$ satisfy the equality

$$(a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n)^2 = a_1^3 + a_2^3 + \dots + a_n^3.$$

⟦folio 33⟧

For example, the number $N=6$ has even divisors: 1, 2, 3, 6; here $a_1=1, a_2=2, a_3=2, a_4=4$ and $(1+2+2+4)^2=1^3+2^3+2^3+4^3$.
