## Condition

There is N+1 place in the book theater. First, N is an integer, having tickets indicating the place (including Igor), if for arbitrary N places, without looking at their tickets. The last (N+1)th spectator to arrive wants to take his place; if it is occupied, the person sitting there groans, he does the same, and so on, until it is necessary, according to the place, to be free. What is the probability that Igor will have to change seats? (In other words, which of all the possible audience placements are unimportant for Igor?)

I. V. Alekseev-Astafev

## Solution

In the cinema $N+1$ place. At first, $N$ people with tickets indicating a seat (including Igor) sat down in random $N$ seats, not eating their tickets. The $(N+1)$ spectator who came last wants to take his place; if it is occupied - on the one hand there, he does the same and so on until the place needed by consciousness is free. What is the probability that Igor will have to change seats? (In other words, what proportion of the environments of all possible placements of spectators are invisible to Igor?)

Answer: 1/2. Let us prove that there are as many beneficial placements for Igor as there are unprofitable ones, and a mutual correspondence has been established between the two.

Suppose that with some initial placement of $P$ $N$ spectators in $N+1$ seats, Igor moved to a free seat. then you get a new placement $P'$. Thus, all possible placements are divided into pairs $(P, P')$ and it is enough to show that in each pair one placement is beneficial for Igor (it is not worth it), and the other is unprofitable.

If $P$ is an advantageous placement, then the last spectator participating in transfers (that is, having a ticket for an empty seat) at placement $P'$ is worth Igor. This means that $P'$ is an invisible placement. If $P$ is a non-visual placement, then the spectator who is worth Igor will have no one worth it at placement $P'$, so the transfers will end there, that is, $P'$ is a placement beneficial for Igor.

I. V. Alekseev-Astafev
