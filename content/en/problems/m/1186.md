## Condition

We will say that two quadrangles - paper and cardboard - fit together if the cardboard one can be placed on the paper one so that all its vertices fall on the sides of the paper one (one on each) and at the same time, if you bend the four small paper triangles formed onto the cardboard quadrangle, they will cover it all in one layer.

a) Prove that if the quadrilaterals fit together, then the paper has either two opposite sides parallel or the diagonals are perpendicular.

b) Prove that if a paper quadrilateral is a parallelogram, then you can make a suitable cardboard quadrilateral.

N. V. Vasiliev
