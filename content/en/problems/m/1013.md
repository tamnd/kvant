## Condition

On sides $AB$ and $BC$ of triangle $ABC$ two points $M$ and $N$ are taken. When parallel lines passing through points $M$, $B$ and $N$ intersect the base $AC$ at points $K$, $D$ and $L$. Prove that the area of ​​the trapezoid (or parallelogram) $KMNL$ is not greater than the area of ​​one of the triangles $ABD$ and $DBC$ (Fig. 1).
