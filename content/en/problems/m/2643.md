Let us denote by O the center of circle Z (Fig.2). Let us draw a common point through point A

tangent to our circles; let it intersect the line CD at point P. Since PA PB, point P lies on the perpendicular bisector of segment AB, which also passes through points M and O. Since AM is the altitude in right triangle PAO, we have PM $PO PA^{2}$. On the other hand, by the tangent and secant property we have $PA^{2} PC$ PD. So PM PO PC PD.
