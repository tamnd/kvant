## Condition

In the infinite sequence $a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3},^{K}$, the number $a_{1}$ is equal to 1, and each subsequent number $a_{n}$ is constructed from the previous $a_{n} -_{1}$ according to the rule: if the largest odd divisor of the number n has a remainder of 1 when divided by 4, then $a_{n} = a_{n} -_{1} + 1$, if the remainder is 3, then $a_{n} = a_{n} -_{1} - 1$. Prove that all numbers in this sequence are positive, and each natural number appears in it an infinite number of times. A. Zaslavsky
