## Condition

Consider a sequence of words consisting of the letters A and B. The first word in the sequence is “A”; The k-e word is obtained from the (k-1)-th word using the following operation: each A is replaced by AAB, each B by A (A, AAB, AABAABA, ...).
a) Prove that each word is the beginning of the next (thereby defining an infinite sequence of letters: AABAABAAABAABA...).
b) *Where does the 1988th letter A appear in this sequence?
b) *Prove that this sequence is non-periodic.

V. M. Galperin
