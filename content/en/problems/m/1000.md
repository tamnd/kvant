## Condition

In arc $AB$ a broken line $AMB$ is inscribed from two segments ($AM > MB$). Prove that the base of the perpendicular $KN$, dropped from the middle $K$ of the arc $AB$ onto the segment $AM$, divides the broken line in half: $AH = HM + MB$ (Fig. 1, p. 42).

Archimedes (Syracuse)
