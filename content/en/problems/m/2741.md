## Condition

Given a natural number k. There are n pillars along the road at regular intervals. Misha painted them in k colors and for each pair of pillars of the same color, between which there are no other pillars of the same color, he calculated the distance between them. All these distances turned out to be different. For what largest n could this happen? M. Tikhomirov, F. Petrov
