## Condition

Gleb thought of natural numbers N and a, where a < N. He wrote the number a on the board. Then Gleb began to perform the following operation: divide N with a remainder by the last number written on the board and write the resulting remainder from the division also on the board. When the number 0 appeared on the board, he stopped. Could Gleb initially choose N and a such that the sum of the numbers written on the board would be greater than 100N? I. Mitrofanov
