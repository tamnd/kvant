## Condition

In an acute triangle, the points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$ are taken on the sides BC, CA, AB, respectively, so that the segments $AA_{1}, BB_{1}, CC_{1}$ intersect at one point. It is known that BC $AA_{1} CA$ $BB_{1} AB$ $CC_{1}$. Prove that $AA_{1}, BB_{1}, CC_{1}$ are the altitudes of the triangle. P. Kozhevnikov
