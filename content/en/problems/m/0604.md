## Condition

a) Andrey, Victor, Sergey, swimming underwater, simultaneously emerged at points $A_0, B_0, C_0$ and immediately dived again, and Andrey decided to swim a third of the way to Victor in a minute, Victor - a third of the way to Sergey, Sergey - a third of the way to Andrey. A minute later they emerged again (points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ - see Fig. 2) and decided to repeat this maneuver - already in half a minute; then - in a quarter of a minute, etc.
Where and when will they meet?
b) Inside a sphere of radius 1 km there are a million points numbered from 1 to a million. Every second, simultaneously, each point moves to the next one by number at $1/3$ distance to this point; the last point moves towards the first in the same way. Prove that after some time all the points will converge inside a certain sphere of radius 1 mm.

F. Shleifer
