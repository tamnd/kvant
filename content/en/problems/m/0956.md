## Condition

Four circles of the same radius are drawn on the plane so that three of them pass through point A and three through point B (Fig. 1). Prove that the four points of their pairwise intersection, other than A and B, are the vertices of a parallelogram.

V. and I. Kapovits, 10th grade students

## Solution

Four circles of the same radius are drawn on the plane so that three of them pass through one point A, and three through point B. Prove that the four points of their pairwise intersection, different from A and B, are the vertices of a parallelogram.
