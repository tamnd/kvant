## Condition

Two piles of matches are given. Initially, there are m matches in one pile, n matches in the other, m > n. Two players take turns taking matches from the pile. In one move, the player takes from one pile any (non-zero) number of matches that is a multiple of the number of matches in

⟦folio 34⟧

another bunch. The player who takes the last match in one of the piles wins.
a) Prove that if $m > 2n$, then the player who makes the first move can ensure a win.
b) For what α is the statement true: if $m > \alpha n$, then the player who makes the first move can ensure a win? (9 grades)
