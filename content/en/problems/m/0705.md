## Condition

On a rectangular sheet of checkered paper there are several rectangular cards, the sides of which lie on linear grids. The cards cover the sheet in two layers (that is, each cell of the sheet is covered with exactly two cards). a) Let each card have dimensions $1 \times 2$ cells. Prove that you can select some of the cards so that they cover the sheet in one layer. (You cannot move the cards.) Will this remain true if the cards 6) can have arbitrary sizes, c*) have dimensions of $1 \times 3$ cells?

G. Galperin, V. Proizvolov
