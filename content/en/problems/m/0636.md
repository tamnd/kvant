## Condition

The set $A$ consists of integers, its smallest element is 1 and its largest element is 100. Each element of $A$ except 1 is equal to the sum of two (possibly equal) numbers belonging to $A$. Among all the sets $A$ that satisfy these conditions, indicate the set with the minimum number of elements. (10)
