## Condition

Some cities only allow paired volumes of apartments (if two families exchange apartments, they do not participate in other exchanges on the same day). Prove that any complex project involving apartments of several families can be completed in two days. (It is assumed that both before and after each exchange, the family lives in a separate apartment.)

N. N. Konstantinov, A. I. Shirelman
