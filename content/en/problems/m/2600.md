## Condition

Given a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD. Circles with diameters AB and CD intersect at two points X 1 and Y 1. Circles with diameters BC and AD intersect at two points X 2 and Y 2. Circles with diameters AC and BD intersect at two points $X_{3}$ and $Y_{3}$. Prove that the lines $X 1 Y 1, X 2 Y 2, X_{3} Y_{3}$ intersect at one point. M. Didin
