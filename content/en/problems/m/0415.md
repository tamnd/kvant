## Condition

What is the maximum number of kings that can be placed on a horizontal board $n \times n$ without them being next to each other? A toric chessboard is obtained from a general size $n \times n$, in which the upper and lower horizontal lines, as well as the left and right verticals, are considered glued. On a horizontal board, from each square the king can be pinned to eight adjacent squares (Fig. 2).
