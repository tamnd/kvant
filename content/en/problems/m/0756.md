## Condition

In the country, besides the capital, there are more than 100 cities. The capital of the country is connected by air lines with 100 cities; Each of the remaining cities is connected by air line to 10 cities worldwide. It is known that from any city you can (perhaps with transfers) fly to any other. Prove that it is possible to close half of the airlines flying from the capital, so that it is possible to get from any city to any other.
