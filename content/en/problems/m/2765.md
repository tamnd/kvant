## Condition

Given 101 coins and two-pan scales. In one weighing you can compare the weights of two coins. Let's call a majority a subset of 51 coins of the same weight. For what is the least number of weighings it is possible to determine with a guarantee whether there is a majority among the given coins? K. Knop
