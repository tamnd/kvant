## Condition

When straight lines parallel to the sides of triangle ABC and passing through one point are cut off from $\triangle ABC$ by the trapezoid. When these trapezoids are diagonal and do not have common ends, they divide the triangle into seven parts, four of which are triangles (Fig. 1). Prove that the sum of the areas of these three triangles adjacent to the sides $\triangle ABC$ is equal to the area of ​​the four.

B. Kosyanchuk
