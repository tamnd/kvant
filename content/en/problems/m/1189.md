## Condition

There are $n$ lines on the plane, no three of which pass through the same point and no two of which are parallel. Prove that in each of the parts into which these lines partition the plane, you can put an integer different from 0 and not exceeding modulo $n$, so that on any side of any of these lines the sum of the numbers is equal to 0.

D. V. Fomin
