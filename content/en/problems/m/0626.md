## Condition

Each side of a convex quadrilateral is divided into 8 equal parts. Matching division points on opposite sides are connected to each other, and the resulting cells are colored in a checkerboard pattern. Prove that the sum of the areas of the cells is equal to the sum of the areas of the white cells.

V. Proizvolov
