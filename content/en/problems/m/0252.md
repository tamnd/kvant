## Condition

a) On the plane there is a regular smudge-head on the side $a$. It is allowed to “roll” along the plane, turning over (symmetrically reflecting) relative to any side. Prove that for any point $M$ of the plane and any $\varepsilon > 0$ it is possible to roll the scourer into such a position that its center will be located from the point $M$ at a distance less than $\varepsilon$ (Fig. 1).

6) Solve a similar problem for a regular pentagon.

c *) For which regular $n$-gons is a similar statement true?
