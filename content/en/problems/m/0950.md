## Condition

Twenty-five shorties share garden plots in the Flower City. Each plot is a square $1 \times 1$ and all plots together make up a square $5 \times 5$. Each shorty is at odds with no more than the other three shorties. Prove that it is possible to distribute the plots in such a way that the plots of two short people who quarreled would not be adjacent. (Adjacent are areas that have a common side.)
S. V. Kongazin
