## Condition

The distance is triangle ABC, point M in the plane of this triangle and the projections A₁, B₁, C₁ of point M to the heights drawn from vertices A, B, C, respectively. Prove that a) there is one and only one point M for which the segments AA₁, BB₁ and CC₁ are equal;
b) for such a point M, the lengths of the segments AA₁, BB₁, CC₁ are equal to the diameter of the inscribed circle in triangle ABC.

A. Kh. Jafarov
