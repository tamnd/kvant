## Condition

In some countries, cities have gray roads. The length of any road is less than 500 km and you can get from any city to any other by driving along roads less than 500 km. When one road was closed for repairs, they found out that you can drive from any city to any other along the remaining roads. Prove that this can be done by driving no more than 1500 km.

S. L. Eliseev
