## Condition

In a country with more than two cities, some pairs of cities are connected by intersecting roads. It is known that for any three cities A, B, C, it is possible to travel along this network of roads from A to B without stopping at C. Prove that one-way traffic can be installed on all roads so that from each city one can drive to any other, following the established directions.

V. E. Kolosov
