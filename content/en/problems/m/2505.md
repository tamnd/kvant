## Condition

There are 100 children in the company, some of the children are friends (friendship is always mutual). It is known that when any child is identified, the remaining 99 children can be divided into 33 groups of three so that in each group all three are friends in pairs. Find the smallest possible number of pairs of friendly children. S. Berlov, N. Vlasova, D. Khramtsov
