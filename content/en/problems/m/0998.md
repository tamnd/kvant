## Condition

Let us consider all tetrahedra $AXBY$ described near a given sphere. Prove that, given fixed points $A$ and $B$, the sum of the angles of the quadrilateral $AXBY$, that is, the sum

$$\angle AXB + \angle XBY + \angle BYA + \angle YAX,$$

does not depend on the choice of points $X$ and $Y$.

I. F. Sharyzin

## Solution

Distance of all terrahedra AXBY described near Danya's sphere. Prove that for fixed points A and B, the sum of the angles of the quadrilateral is AXBY, that is, the sum

$$\angle AXB + \angle XBY + \angle BYA + \angle YAX,$$

does not depend on the choice of points X and Y.
