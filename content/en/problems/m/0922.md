## Condition

Prove that the equation
$$\sin^p x+\cos^q x=1,$$
where $p$ and $q$ are positive numbers, has a solution on the interval $0<x<\pi/2$ if and only if $(p-2)(q-2)<0$ or $p=q=2$.

A. M. Sedletsky
