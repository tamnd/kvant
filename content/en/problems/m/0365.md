## Condition

a) The sum of several numbers is equal to 1. Can the sum of their cubes be greater than one?
b) Is it possible that the series $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots$ converges, but the series $a_1^3 + a_2^3 + a_3^3 \dots$ does not?
(Recall that the series $x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + \dots$ is said to be convergent if the sequence of numbers $S_n = x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_n$ has a limit.)
