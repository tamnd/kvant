## Condition

Given a natural number n! 100. Initially, the number 1 is written on the board. Every minute Petya represents the number written on the board as the sum of two unequal positive irreducible fractions, and Vasya leaves only one of these two fractions on the board. Prove that Petya can ensure that the denominator of the remaining fraction does not exceed $2^{n} 50$ after n minutes, regardless of Vasya’s actions. M. Didin
