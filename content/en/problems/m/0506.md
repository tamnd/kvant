## Condition

Prove that for positive $a, b, c$ and $d$ the following inequality holds:
$$a^4 + b^4 + c^4 + d^4 + 2 abcd \geq a^2 b^2 + a^2 c^2 + a^2 d^2 + b^2 c^2 + b^2 d^2 + c^2 d^2.$$
E. Turkewitz
