## Condition

The incircle of triangle ABC touches its sides AB, BC and AC at points M, N and P respectively. It is known that
$$\overrightarrow{AN} + \overrightarrow{BP} + \overrightarrow{CM} = \overrightarrow{0}.$$
Prove that triangle ABC is regular.
P. Gusyatnikov, S. Reznitsenko
