## Condition

A complete set of dominoes is laid out on the table in a closed chain, and for all pairs of neighboring dominoes the modulus of the difference in points on the squares with which they touch is calculated. Let us denote by S the sum of all such modules of differences. It is clear that the smallest value of S is 0 (this occurs when the closed chain is laid out according to domino rules). What is the greatest value that the sum S can take? A. Gribalko

## Solution

A complete set of dominoes is laid out on the table in a closed chain, and for all pairs of neighboring dominoes the modulus of the difference in points on the squares with which they touch is calculated. Let us denote by S the sum of all such modules of differences. It is clear that the smallest value of S is 0 (this occurs when the closed chain is laid out according to domino rules). What is the greatest value that the sum S can take?
