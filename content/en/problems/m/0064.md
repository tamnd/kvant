## Condition

On the data plane there is a straight line $l$ and two points $P$ and $Q$ lying on one side of it. Find on the line $l$ also the points $M$ for which the distance between the bases of the altitudes of the triangle $PQM$, dropped to the sides $PM$ and $QM$, is called.

G. A. Palatnik
