## Condition

Prove that the least common multiple $n$ of the natural numbers $a_1 < a_2 < \ldots < a_n$ is at least $na_1$.

A. Razborov

## Solution

Prove that the least common multiple of $a_1 < a_2 < ... < a_n$ is $A$. Then, obviously, $\frac{A}{a_1} > \frac{A}{a_2} > ... > \frac{A}{a_n}$ are natural numbers. Therefore $\frac{A}{a_1} \geq n$, hence $A \geq n a_1$.

This estimate is accurate. Indeed, if $a_1 = \frac{n!}{n-1}$, $a_2 = \frac{n!}{n-1}$, ..., $a_n = n!$, then $A = n! = n \cdot a_1$.

A. Razborov
