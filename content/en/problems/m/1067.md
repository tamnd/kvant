## Condition

Prove that for non-negative numbers $x, y, z$ satisfying the condition $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ the following inequality holds:

$$\frac{x}{1-x^2} + \frac{y}{1-y^2} + \frac{z}{1-z^2} \geq \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

V. E. Matizen
