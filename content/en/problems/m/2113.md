## Condition

A polynomial of degree n >1 has various real roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, K, x_{n}$, and its derivative has roots $y_{1}, y_{2}, K, y_{n} -_{1}$. Prove that the arithmetic mean of the numbers $x_{1}^{2}, x_{2}^{2}, K, x_{n}^{2}$ is greater than the arithmetic mean of the numbers $y_{1}^{2}, y_{2}^{2}, K, y_{n}^{2} -_{1}$.

M. Murashkin
