## Condition

An infinite number of participants gathered for the intergalactic Olympiad, who were registered under numbers 1, 2, 3,... It turned out that for each n = 1, 2, ..., the participant with number n has at least n friends among the remaining participants (friendship is mutual). There is a hotel for participants to stay with an endless number of double rooms. Prove that participants can be accommodated in double rooms so that each room contains a pair of friends. V. Bragin, P. Kozhevnikov
