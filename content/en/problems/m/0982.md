## Condition

On sides AB, BC and CA of triangle ABC, squares ABB₁A₂, BCB₁C₂, CAA₁C₂ are constructed on the outer side. Prove that the perpendiculars to the segments A₁A₂, B₁B₂, C₁C₂, restored at their midpoints, intersect at one point.

N. Azamov (8th grade student)

## Solution

On sides AB, BC and CA of triangle ABC, squares ABB₁A₂, BCC₁B₂, CAA₁C₂ are constructed on the outer side. Prove that the redistributions to the segments A₁A₂, B₁B₂, C₁C₂, reconstructed at their midpoints, intersect at one point.
