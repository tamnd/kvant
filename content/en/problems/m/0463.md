## Condition

* Given natural numbers $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_m, y_1, y_2, \dots, y_n$. The sums $x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_m$ and $y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_n$ are equal to each other and less than mn. Prove that in the equality $x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_m = y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_n$ you can cross out some of the terms so that the equality is true again.

K. Sibirkov
