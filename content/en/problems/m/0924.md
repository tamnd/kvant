## Condition

Each two of the $n$ points (no three of which lie on the same line) are connected by a segment, and all segments have arrows. A triangle ABC with vertices at these points is called oriented if the arrows are placed in the directions AB, BC, CA or AC, CB, BA (for example, on figure 1 there are only three oriented triangles out of 10).
a) Explain how to arrange the arrows so that no oriented triangles appear.
b)* What is the maximum possible number of oriented triangles (for each $n$)? (Draw the corresponding example for $n=4$, 5 and 6.)
I. Tsalenchuk, 10th grade student.
