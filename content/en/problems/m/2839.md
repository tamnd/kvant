## Condition

Given a natural number k. In a country whose currency is the Tugrik, only banknotes of two integer denominations are circulated. Both the buyer and the seller have quite a lot of both bills, but for each payment they can use no more than k bills together (including change). It is known that this way you can make a payment for any integer amount from 1 to n tugriks. What is the largest possible value of n? A. Shapovalov
