## Condition

Prove that for any positive numbers a, b, c, d the inequality is true

$$\frac{a}{b+c} + \frac{b}{c+d} + \frac{c}{d+a} + \frac{d}{a+b} \geq 2.$$

L. D. Kurlyandchik
