## Condition

The alphabet consists of three letters: a, b, c. Let us use a word to create a sequence of any length consisting of these letters. When forming words, certain letter combinations (of two or more letters) are considered prohibited. It is known that in the list of prohibited letter combinations all words have different lengths.

Prove that there are words of any length that do not contain forbidden letter combinations.

A. M. Stepin
