## Condition

Are all natural divisors of the number 100 possible? (including 1 and the number itself) divide: a) into two groups; b) into 100 groups so that the groups have the same number of numbers and the product of the numbers in the first group is equal to the product of the numbers in the second group? c) Find all natural n for which it is possible to divide all divisors of the number n into two groups so that the groups have the same number of numbers and the product of the numbers of the first group is equal to the product of the numbers of the second group. M. Malkin
