## Condition

a) Prove that any natural number n greater than 17 can be decomposed into the sum of three natural pairwise coprime terms, each of which is greater than 1. b*) Find out whether there is a finite or infinite set of natural numbers that cannot be represented as the sum of three coprime natural terms in a set, any two of which are not coprime. V. Letsko
