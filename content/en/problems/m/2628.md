## Condition

There are N externally identical scales. It is known that exactly one of them is flawed - they show anything, and their weight is different from the weight of the others, which weigh the same and always show the correct result. During one weighing, you can put several scales on each of the bowls of some other scales. At what maximum N in three weighings can one be guaranteed to find damaged scales? A. Gribalko, O. Mangina
