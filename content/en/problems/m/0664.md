## Condition

Given a quadrilateral ABCD of area S. Let us denote the points of intersection of the altitudes of triangles ABC, BCD, CDA, DAB by H, K, L, M, respectively. Prove that the area of ​​the quadrilateral HKLM is also equal to S.
