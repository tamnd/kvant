## Condition

It is easy to check that the volume of a regular octahedron described around a sphere of radius 1 is equal to 4 3. Find out whether the following statement is true: “the volume of any octahedron described around a sphere of radius 1 is not less than 4 3.” A. Merkulova

$M2413^{*}$. The line consists of N guys of different heights in pairs. It was divided into the smallest possible number of groups of guys standing in a row, in each of which the guys stand in ascending height from left to right (groups of one person are possible). Then in each group the children were rearranged in descending order of height from left to right. Prove that after N – 1 such operation the children will stand in descending order of height from left to right. N. Gladkov
