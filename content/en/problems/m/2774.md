## Condition

In a checkered square 50 u 50, each cell is painted in one of the 100 given colors so that all colors are present and it is impossible to cut a single-color domino from the square (i.e. a rectangle 1 u 2). Galia wants to recolor all the cells of one color into another color (out of the given 100 colors) so that it is still impossible to cut out a single-color domino. Is it true that Galia will definitely be able to do this? G. Sharafetdinova
