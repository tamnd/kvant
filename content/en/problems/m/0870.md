## Condition

On one side of the endless corridor there is an infinite number of rooms, numbered in order by integers, and on each side there is a piano. A small number of panists live in these rooms. (Several panists can live in one room.) Every day, some two panists living in neighboring rooms - the kth and (k+1)th - come to the conclusion that they are interfering with each other, and move to the (k−1)th and (k+2)th rooms, respectively. Prove that after a finite number of days these migrations will stop.

V. G. Iltsev
