## Condition

A semicircle with diameter $AB$ is cut by a segment $CD$, perpendicular to $AB$, into two curved triangles $ACD$ and $BCD$, in which circles tangent to $AB$ are inscribed at points $E$ and $F$ (Fig. 1). Prove that $|AD| = |AF|$, $|DF|$ are bissertis of angle $BDC$, the value of angle $EDF$ does not depend on the choice of point $C$ on $AB$.

B. Senderov
