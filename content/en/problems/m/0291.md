## Condition

On sides $A_2A_3$, $A_3A_1$, $A_1A_2$ of triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ squares with centers $O_1$, $O_2$, $O_3$ lying outside the triangle are constructed. Prove that:

a) segments $O_1O_2$ and $A_3O_3$ are equal in length and mutually perpendicular;

b) the midpoints of the segments $A_3A_1$, $O_1O_2$, $A_3A_2$ and $A_3O_3$ are the vertices of the square;

b) the area of ​​this square is 8 times less than the area of ​​the square with center $O_3$.

V. M. Fishman
