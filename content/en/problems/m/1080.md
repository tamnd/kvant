## Condition

Let $q$ be a natural number, $q \geq 3$. Prove that if $k^2 + k + q$ is a prime number for all integers $k$, where $0 \leq k \leq \sqrt{q}/3$, then $k^2 + k + q$ is prime for all integers $k$, where $0 \leq k \leq q - 2$.

V. F. Lev
