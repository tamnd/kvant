## Condition

The increasing sequence of natural numbers $a_{1} a_{2}$ ... is such that for every n! 100 number $a_{n}$ is equal to the smallest natural number greater than $a_{n} 1$ and not divisible by any of the numbers $a_{1}, a_{2}$, … $, a_{n} 1$. Prove that there is only a finite number of composite numbers in this sequence. P. Kozhevnikov
