## Condition

K numbers were removed from integers from 1 to 100. Is it necessary to choose k different numbers with a sum of 100 among the remaining numbers if: a) k = 9; b) k = 8? A. Shapovalov

## Solution

K numbers were removed from integers from 1 to 100. Is it necessary to choose k different numbers with a sum of 100 among the remaining numbers if: a) k = 9; b) k = 8?

a) Answer. Optional. Let's remove the numbers 1, 2,..., 9. Then the sum of even the smallest nine of the remaining numbers (10 + 11 +... + 18 = 126) is greater than 100. b) Answer. Necessarily. Consider 12 pairs of numbers that add up to 25: (1, 24), (2, 23),..., (12, 13). After removing 8 numbers, at least four intact pairs will remain. They will give a total of 100. A. Shapovalov
