## Condition

Prove that for any $n$ real numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ there is a natural number $k \leqslant n$ such that each of the $k$ numbers

$$a_k, \frac{a_{k-1}+a_k}{2}, \frac{a_{k-2}+a_{k-1}+a_k}{3}, \dots, \frac{a_1+a_2+\dots+a_k}{k}$$ does not exceed

$$\frac{a_1+a_2+\dots+a_n}{n}.$$
