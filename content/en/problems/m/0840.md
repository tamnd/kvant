## Condition

a) Prove that if $a, b, c$ is the length of the side of the triangle, then the inequality holds

$$a^2b(a - b) + b^2c(b - c) + c^2a(c - a) \geq 0.$$

Find out in what cases it turns into equality.

b) Prove that for any positive numbers $a, b, c$ the following inequality holds:

$$a^3b + b^3c + c^3a \geq a^2bc + b^2ca + c^2ab.$$
