## Condition

Find all natural numbers $a$ for which the number $a - 1$ is the sum of two $a$; $6$)* three divisors of the number $a$ (not necessarily different; the number of divisors includes 1).
C)* Prove that for any $n$ there is only a finite number of natural $a$ such that $a - 1$ is the sum of $n$ natural divisors of $a$ (not necessarily different).
