## Condition

The test consists of 30 questions, each with 2 possible answers (one is correct, the other is incorrect). In one attempt, Vitya answers all the questions, after which he is told how many questions he answered correctly. Will Vitya be able to act in such a way as to be guaranteed to find out all the correct answers no later than after the 24th attempt (and answer all questions correctly on the 25th attempt)? V.
