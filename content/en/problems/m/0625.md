## Condition

* On the coordinate plane, four points with rational coordinates are given that do not lie at the vertices of the parallelogram, and no three of them belong to the same straight line. It is allowed to draw a line through any two already obtained points and mark the intersection point of any two drawn lines. Prove that the set of points that can be obtained in this way is the set of all points in the plane.
