## Condition

What is the maximum angle that the segments $OA$ and $OB$ can make between themselves, coming from the origin $O$ of a rectangular coordinate system in space, if the point $A$ has coordinates $(x, y, z)$, and the point $B$ has coordinates $(y, z, x)$?

S. N. Vyshkov

## Solution

What is the most convenient way for the segments AO and OB to come from the origin O of a rectangular coordinate system in space, if point A has coordinates (x, y, z), and point B has coordinates (y, z, x)?

$$\sin \frac{\angle AOB}{2} = \frac{AB}{2OA} \leq \frac{AB}{2AP} = \sin 60^\circ$$
