## Condition

Several zeros, ones and twos are written on the board. It is allowed to erase two unequal numbers and write one number together, different from the erased ones (2 together 0 and 1, 1 together 0 and 2, 0 together 1 and 2). Prove that if, as a result of several such operations, only one number remains on the board, then it does not depend on the order in which the erases were made. (8th and 10th grades)

S. V. Fomin
