## Condition

Find the necessary and sufficient conditions that the numbers a, b, a, β must satisfy so that the rectangle a×b can be cut into several rectangles α×β.

For example, is it possible to cut a 50×60 rectangle into rectangles with dimensions:
a) 20×15; 6) 5×8; c) 6.25×15;
d) (2−√2)×(2+√2).

A. T. Kolomov
