## Condition

In the non-isosceles triangle ABC the medians $AA_{0}, BB_{0}, CC_{0}$ and altitudes $AA_{1}, BB_{1}, CC_{1}$ are drawn. Let $A_{2}$ be the intersection point of the circles circumscribed about triangles $BA_{1} B_{0}$ and $CA_{1} C_{0}$, different from the point $A_{1}$. Let us similarly define the points $B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$. Prove that the lines $AA_{2}, BB_{2}, CC_{2}$ intersect at one point. T. Kovalev, A. Lvov (9th grade students)
