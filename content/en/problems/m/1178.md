## Condition

a) Prove that for a non-obtuse triangle ABC with sides a, b, c, inscribed circle radius r and circumcircle radius R, the following inequality holds:
$$2(R+r) \leq \sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}.$$
6) Under what conditions does this inequality turn into equality?
