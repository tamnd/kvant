## Condition

There are $n$ points on the plane. It is necessary to place $n$ projectors at these points, each of which illuminates an angle of magnitude $360^\circ/n$ so as to illuminate the entire plane. Prove that this is possible for any location of these points if

a) $n=3; 6)$ $n=4$; c) $n$ — any natural number.

⟦folio 29⟧

d) Let now the projectors illuminate the corners $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \dots, \alpha_n$ ($\alpha_1 + \alpha_2 + \dots + \alpha_n = 360^\circ$) and are arranged at one point so that they illuminate the plane. Prove that you can transfer one of the $n$ projectors to each of these points so that the entire plane is still illuminated.

G. and V. Galperin
