## Condition

In outer space around plans $O$ three satellites rotate uniformly in three circular orbits with center $O$. The angular velocities of the satellites are $\omega_1, \omega_2$ and $\omega_3$, respectively, and their initial positions can be arbitrary. Is there necessarily a moment in time when all three satellites and point $O$ lie in the same plane if

⟦folio 31⟧

a) $\omega_1=\omega_2=\omega_3=1$; 6) $\omega_1=\omega_2=1, \omega_3=2$;
b) $\omega_1=2, \omega_2=3, \omega_3=4$?

Try to find out what the answer to this question will be for other ratios of angular velocities.
