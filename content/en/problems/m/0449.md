## Condition

a) $n$ identical balls move along one straight line. What is the maximum number of collisions between them that can occur?

b) * The same question for three balls of masses $m_1$, $m_2$ and $m_3$.

c) * Try to prove that if $n$ different balls move along the same straight line, then the total number of balls between them is finite.

(In these problems, the balls are considered as material points that collide with each other absolutely urgo, i.e., with the conservation of the total momentum and energy, and it is assumed that all occurring collisions are only pairs of e: three or more balls do not end up at the same point at the same time.)
