## Condition

On a circle tangent to the sides of an angle with vertex O, two diametrically opposite points A and B (different from the points of tangency) are selected. The tangent to the circle at point B intersects the sides of the angle at points C and D, and the line OA at point E (Fig. 1). Prove that the lengths of the segments BC and DE are equal.

A. S. Merkuryev
