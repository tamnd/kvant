## Condition

a) Prove that in any convex polyhedron the sum of the lengths of all edges is greater than triple the diameter. (The diameter of a polyhedron is the longest of the lengths of all possible segments with ends at the vertices of the polyhedron.)

6) Prove that for any two vertices $A$ and $B$ the convex polyhedron will find three broken lines, each of which goes along the edges of the polyhedron from $A$ to $B$ and no two go along the same edge.

b) Prove that if two edges are cut in a convex polyhedron, then for any two of its vertices $A$ and $B$ there is a broken line going from $A$ to $B$ along the remaining edges.

d) Prove that in problem 6) it is possible to choose three broken lines that pairwise do not have common vertices, with the exception of the ends $A$ and $B$.

A. G. Kushinirenko
