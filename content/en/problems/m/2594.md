## Condition

It is known that for some x and y the sums sin x + cos y and sin y + cos x are positive rational numbers. Prove that there are natural numbers m and n such that m sin x + n cos x is a natural number. N. Agakhanov

## Solution

It is known that for some x and y the sums sin x + cos y and sin y + cos x are positive rational numbers. Prove that there are natural numbers m and n such that m sin x + n cos x is a natural number. Let sin x cos y a and sin y cos x b. Then cos y a sin x and sin y b cos x. Let's square these two equalities and add them. By virtue of the main trigonometric identity, we obtain $1 a^{2} b^{2} 2 a$ sin x 2 b cos x 1, i.e. 2 a sin x 2 b cos $x a^{2} b^{2}$. Let N be the LCM of the denominators of numbers a and b; then, multiplying the resulting equality by $N^{2}$, we obtain the required one. N. Agakhanov
