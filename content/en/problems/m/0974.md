## Condition

Two people play chess with a clock. After both had made 40 moves, their clocks showed 2 hours 30 minutes.
a) Prove that there was a moment in the game when one’s clock was ahead of the other’s by more than 1 minute 50 seconds.
b) Is it possible to say that at some points the difference between the clock readings was at least 2 minutes?

S. V. Fomin

## Solution

Two people are playing chess with a clock. After both had made 40 moves, both clocks showed 2 hours 30 minutes.
a) Prove that there was a moment in the game when one zone’s clock marked the others’ clocks for more than 1 minute 50 seconds.
b) Is it possible to say that at some points the time difference was at least 2 minutes?

a) Let the absolute value of the difference between the clock readings at no moment exceed t=1 minutes 50 s.
