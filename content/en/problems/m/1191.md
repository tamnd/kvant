## Condition

Let $A_1, A_2, A_3, \dots$ be a certain sequence of points on the plane. Starting from the neokota point $T_0$, we construct the sequence $T_1, T_2, T_3, \dots$, where $T_n$ is a point symmetrical to $T_{n-1}$ relative to $A_n$ ($n=1, 2, 3, \dots$).

What necessary and sufficient conditions must the sequence $A_n$ satisfy in order for the sequence $T_n$ to be periodic for any choice of point $T_0$ (i.e. $T_{n+p}=T_n$ for some $p$ for all $n$)?

I. F. Akulich
