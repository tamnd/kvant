## Condition

A small ball moves inside the billiard, which has the shape of an ellipse with focuses $A$ and $B$, elastically reflected from its sides along the broken line $P_1P_2P_3P_4 \dots (P_1, P_2, \dots$ - points of the ellipse). Prove that if the link

$P_1P_2$ does not intersect the segment $AB$, then a) all subsequent links $P_2P_3, P_3P_4, \dots$ do not intersect the segment $AB$; b) all these links touch the same ellipse. (Think about how to construct this ellipse.)

A. N. Zemlyakov
