## Condition

How many different integers are there that can be represented as the difference $a - \bar{a}$, where $a - n$ is a natural number ($10^{n-1} \leq a < 10^n$), $\bar{a}$ is the number obtained by writing the digits $a$ in reverse order? For example, if $a = 1917$, then $a - \bar{a} = 1917 - 7191 = -5724$. Provide the answer for: a) $n = 4$; b) $n = 5$; b) any natural $n$.

G. O. Elstin
