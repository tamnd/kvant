## Condition

Cards with numbers 1, 2, ..., 32 are stacked in order. It is allowed to remove an excess number of cards and place them between some of the remaining ones or under them, without changing the order of both, but otherwise arbitrarily.

This operation is called moving. Prove that in 5 movements it is possible
a) rearrange the cards in reverse order;
b) arrange the cards in any order;
b) prove that not every card order can be obtained in 4 moves.

V. Turchaninov
