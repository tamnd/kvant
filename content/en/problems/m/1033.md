## Condition

The circle cuts four linear triangles from the square (the border of each consists of two circles and two segments). Let's paint two of them, adjacent to the opposite corners of the square, blue, the other two red. Prove that a) the sums of red and blue are equal;
b) the sums of the perimeters of the red and blue triangles are equal.

B. B. Prozovolyov

## Solution

The circle of a square quadrilateral (the boundary of each side is two circles and two segments). Let's paint two of them, adjacent to the opposite corners of the square, in black, the other two - in red. Prove that: a) the sums of the red and blue arcs are equal; b) the sums of the perimeters of the red and blue triangles are equal.
