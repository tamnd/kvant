## Condition

Inside the triangle $\triangle$ you need to place the triangle $\triangle_1$ so that each of the three squares built on the sides of the triangle $\triangle_1$ has two vertices lying on different sides of the triangle $\triangle$ (Fig. 1).
a) Prove that the medians of triangle $\triangle$ are perpendicular to the sides of triangle $\triangle_1$.
b) Is such a construction possible for any acute triangle $\triangle$?
