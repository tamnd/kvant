## Condition

a) Prove the equality (where $n! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot \dots \cdot n$):

$$\frac{1 \cdot 2!}{2} + \frac{2 \cdot 3!}{2^2} + \dots + \frac{n(n+1)!}{2^n} = \frac{(n+2)!}{2^n} - 2.$$

6) Find the amount

$$\frac{1 \cdot 3!}{3} + \frac{2 \cdot 4!}{3^2} + \dots + \frac{n(n+2)!}{3^n}.$$
