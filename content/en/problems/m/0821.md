## Condition

Solve the equation $x^3+x^2+x=-\frac{1}{3}$.

Yu. I. Ionin

## Solution

Solve the equation
$$x^3 + x^2 + x = -\frac{1}{3}.$$

Let's rewrite the equation in the form $3x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1 = 0$; we get the sum of cubes: $(x+1)^3 + 2x^3 = 0$. Let's write it in the form $(x+1)^3 = -2x^3$ and take the cube root from both sides: $x+1 = -x\sqrt[3]{2}$; hence $x = -\frac{1}{1 + \sqrt[3]{2}}$.

Yu. I. Ionin
