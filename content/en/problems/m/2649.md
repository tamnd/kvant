## Condition

Initially, point particles A, B, C are respectively located at points 0;0, 1;0, 0;1 of the coordinate plane. Every minute, some two particles repel each other along a straight line connecting their current positions, passing the same (positive) Fig. 2 distance (Fig.2). Can particle A end up at point a) 3;3; b) 2;3? c) Can B and C simultaneously be at points 0;100 and 100;0, respectively? K. Krivosheev
