## Condition

The bottom of the rectangular box was lined with tiles of sizes $2 \times 2$ and $1 \times 4$. The tiles spilled out of the box and in the process lost one tile $2 \times 2$. Instead, we managed to get tile $1 \times 4$. Prove that it is now impossible to tile up to the box.

L. G. Limanov
