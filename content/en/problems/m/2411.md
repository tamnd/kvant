## Condition

Petya saw several different numbers on the board and decided to create an expression whose values ​​include all these numbers, but not others. When composing an expression, Petya can use any numbers, the special sign “$\pm$”, as well as the usual signs “+”, “–”, “¥” and brackets. The values ​​of the compiled expression

⟦column⟧

It calculates it by choosing for each sign “$\pm$” either “+” or “–” in all possible combinations. For example, if there were numbers 4 and 6 on the board, the expression $5 \pm 1$ would be suitable, and if there were numbers 1, 2 and 3 on the board, then the expression $( 2 \pm 0,5 ) \pm 0,5$ would be suitable. Is it possible to compose the necessary expression if the following were written on the board: a) the numbers 1, 2, 4; b) any 100 different real numbers? Ko Bong Gyun (South Korea)
