## Condition

Given a cubic polynomial f(x). Let's call a cycle a triple of distinct numbers ( a, b, c ) such that f ( a ) = b, f ( b ) = c and f ( c ) = a. It is known that they were found

⟦column⟧

eight cycles $( a_{i}, b_{i}, c_{i} ), i = 1, 2,$…, 8, which involve 24 different numbers. Prove that among eight numbers of the form $a_{i} + b_{i} + c_{i}$ there are at least: a) three different ones; b) four different ones. A. Golovanov, F. Petrov
