## Condition

In each cell of a strip of length 100 there is a chip. For 1 ruble you can swap the places of any two adjacent chips, and you can also swap for free any two chips between which there are a) exactly three chips; b) exactly four chips. For what is the least amount of rubles you can rearrange the chips in reverse order? E. Bakaev
