## Condition

Sets of stones are considered, the mass of each of which is no more than 2 kg, and the total mass of the set is 50 kg. From such a set, several stones are selected, the total mass of which differs from 10 kg by the smallest possible number D for a given set. What is the largest value that the number D can take for all possible sets of stones?

A. Waitrob, A. Pechkovsky
