## Condition

Let k be a natural number and $1 d_{0} d_{1}$ … $d_{m} 4 k$ be all positive divisors of 4k. Prove that there is i ^ 1, …, m ` such that $d_{i} d_{i} 1 2$. I. Mitrofanov

## Solution

Let k be a natural number and $1 d_{0} d_{1}$ … $d_{m} 4k$ be all positive divisors of 4k. Prove that there is i {1,...,m} such that $d_{i} d_{i} 1 2$.

Let's assume the opposite - the required i was not found. Then if d and d + 2 are divisors of the number 4k, then so is d + 1. Note that if a is a divisor of the number 4k, and a is not divisible by 4, then 2a is also a divisor of the number 4k. Using the indicated properties, starting with the pair 1, 2, we will find the number 4k
