## Condition

There are 100 white dots in a circle. Anya and Borya take turns painting one not yet painted point red or blue, Anya begins. Anya wants to end up with as many pairs of different-colored neighboring dots as possible, and Borya wants to end up with as few such pairs as possible. What is the largest number of pairs of multi-colored neighboring dots that Anya can guarantee for herself, regardless of Bori's game? P. Kozhevnikov
