## Condition

Let $A$ be one of the points of intersection of two circles with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$, $P_1P_2$ and $Q_1Q_2$ the common tangents, $M_1$ and $M_2$ the midpoints of the chords $P_1Q_1$ and $P_2Q_2$ of these circles (Fig. 1). Prove that the angles $O_1AO_2$ and $M_1AM_2$ are equal.
