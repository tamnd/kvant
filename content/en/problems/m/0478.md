## Condition

In the volleyball tournament, each two teams played one match.

a) Prove that if for any two teams there is a third that won against these two, then the number of teams is at least seven.

6) Construct an example of such a seven-team tournament.

b)* Prove that if for any three teams there is a third that won against these three, then the number of teams is at least 15.

## Solution

In the volleyball tournament, each two teams played one match.
a) Prove that if for any two teams there is a triangle that wins over these two, then the number of teams is at least seven.
b) Prove that if for any three teams there is a triangle that wins against these three, then the number of teams is at least seven.
6) Construct an example of a seven-team tournament.
b) Prove that if for any three teams there is a triangle that wins against these three, then the number of teams is at least seven.
