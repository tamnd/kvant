## Condition

An equilateral triangle ABC is inscribed in a circle Ω and circumscribed around

Fig. 1

circle $\omega$. On sides AC and AB, points P and Q are chosen respectively so that the segment PQ touches $\omega$ (Fig.1). Circle $Ω_{b}$ with center P passes through B, and circle $Ω_{c}$ with center Q passes through C. Prove that circles Ω $, Ω_{b}$ and $Ω_{c}$ have a common point. A. Akopyan, P. Kozhevnikov
