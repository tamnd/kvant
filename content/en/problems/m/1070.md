## Condition

The tetrahedron is intersected by three planes, each of which is parallel to its two opposite edges and equally distant from them. Prove that the sum of the squared areas of these three sections is 4 times less than the sum of the squared areas of the faces of the tetrahedron.

V. N. Dubrovsky
