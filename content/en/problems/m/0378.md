## Condition

Prove that there are an infinite number of natural numbers that cannot be represented in the form
a) $x^3 + y^3 + z^3$, where x, y, z are integers;
b) $x_1^n + x_2^n + \ldots + x_n^n$, where $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ are integers.
b)* Prove that any rational number can be represented as $x^3 + y^3 + z^3$, where x, y, z are rational numbers.
B. Kolosov
