## Condition

One of the two players (“beginner”) places a knight on a certain square of the chessboard. Then the players take turns moving the knight according to the general rules (letter “Γ”), but you cannot place the knight on the field where it has already been beaten. The one who has nowhere to go loses. Who can achieve victory (regardless of the opponent’s actions) - the beginner or his partner a) on an 8x8 board; 6) on the board m×n, where m≥n≥3?

V. Zudilin, 10th grade student, Veltsy
