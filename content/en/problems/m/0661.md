## Condition

On the shore of the round lake there are four scriptures K, L, P, Q. From the scripture K a boat sails, from L a boat. If the boat goes straight to P and the boat goes straight to Q, then they will collide at some point X of the lake. Prove that if the star sails to Q and the boat to P, then they will reach these scriptures at the same time.

N. Vasiliev
