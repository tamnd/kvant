## Condition

A spherical planet moves in a circle around a star and rotates around an axis, and the axis of condensed rotation is inclined to the orbital plane at an angle α (for our Earth α = 66.5°). Find the dependence of the duration T of the shortest day of the year at a given point on the surface of the planet on the geographic latitude φ of this point. (The angular velocity of the planet’s rotation in its orbit is much less than the angular velocity of the planet’s rotation around its axis). Write a formula for the function T = T(φ) and draw an example graph.

A. P. Savin
