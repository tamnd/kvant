## Condition

There are 30 teams in the National Basketball Association, each of which plays 82 regular season games against other teams per year. Will the Association management be able to divide the teams (not necessarily equally) into the Eastern and Western conferences and schedule games so that matches between teams from different conferences account for exactly half of the total number of matches?
