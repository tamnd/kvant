## Condition

For the sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ it is known that $|a_1| = 1$ and $|a_{k+1}| = |a_k| + 1$ for each $k = 1, 2, \dots$. Find the smallest possible value of the sum $|a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n|$ if:
a) $n = 1975$;
b) $n = 1976$.

V. P. Golubyatnikov
