## Condition

Inside an acute triangle ABC, a point P is given such that $APB = \widehat{ABC} + 60^\circ$, $\widehat{BPC} = \widehat{BAC} + 60^\circ$, $\widehat{CPA} = \widehat{CBA} + 60^\circ$. Prove that the intersection points of the continuations of the segments AP, BP, CP (beyond point P) with the circumcircle $\Delta ABC$ lie at the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

A. Yagubyants

## Solution

Inside an acute triangle ABC, a point $P$ is given, which is equal to $\widehat{APB} = \widehat{ABC} + 60^\circ, \widehat{BPC} = \widehat{BAC} + 60^\circ, \widehat{CPA} = \widehat{CBA} + 60^\circ$. Prove that the intersection points of the continuations of the segments $AP, BP, CP$ (beyond the point $P$) with the circumcircle $\triangle ABC$ lie at the vertices of an equilateral triangle.**
