## Condition

From the point $P$ inside a given triangle $ABC$ perpendiculars $PA_1, PB_1, PC_1$ are dropped onto lines $BC, CA$ and $AB$. For what points is $P$ inside $\triangle ABC$ large?

$$\frac{|BC|}{|PA_1|} + \frac{|CA|}{|PB_1|} + \frac{|AB|}{|PC_1|}$$

takes the smallest value?
