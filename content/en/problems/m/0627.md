## Condition

Each cell of an infinite sheet of checkered paper contains a natural number.
a) Let each of these numbers appear exactly once. (Give examples of such arrangement of numbers!)
Prove that for any given m there are two neighboring (sharing a common side) cells whose difference in numbers is at least m.
6) * Let each number n ∈ N occur exactly n times (that is, 1 - once, 2 - twice, etc.).
Indicate the most number k such that there are sure to be two neighboring cells whose difference in numbers is not less than k. (Give an example of such an arrangement in which the difference in numbers in any pair of neighboring cells is no more than k+1.)

A. Tolyvo
