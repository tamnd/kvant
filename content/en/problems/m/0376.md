## Condition

a) There are 30 cells in a row. On the very right square there is a white chip, on the very left there is a black one. Each of the two players takes turns moving their piece one space - forward or backward. (You cannot skip a move.) The loser is the one who does not have a move. Who wins: the beginner or his partner?
6) Solve the problem by replacing 30 with N in the conditions.
