## Condition

A circle is inscribed in triangle ABC and touches sides AB, BC and CA at points C₁, A₁ and B₁ respectively. It is known that the lengths of the segments AA₁, BB₁ and CC₁ are equal. Prove that triangle ABC is regular.

A. N. Dranshinkov
