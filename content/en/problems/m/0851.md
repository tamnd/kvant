## Condition

The length of the side of the square $ABCD$ is equal to 1. On the sides $AB$ and $AD$ the points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen so that the perimeter of the triangle $APQ$ is equal to 2. Prove that $PCQ=45^\circ$.

A. B. Khodulyev
