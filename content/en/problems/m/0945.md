## Condition

Given a strictly recurrent unbounded sequence of positive numbers $a_1, a_2, ...$. Prove that for all sufficiently large $k$ the following inequality holds:

a) $\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\frac{a_2}{a_3}+\...+\frac{a_k}{a_{k+1}}<k-1$;

b) $\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\frac{a_2}{a_3}+\...+\frac{a_k}{a_{k+1}}<k-1985$.
