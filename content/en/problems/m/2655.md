## Condition

Given an infinite checkered plane. A teacher and a class of 30 students play a game, taking turns taking turns - first the teacher, then all the students in turn, then the teacher again, etc. In one move you can paint a single segment, which is the border between two adjacent cells. You cannot paint sections twice. The teacher wins if, after the move of one of the 31 players, there is a checkered rectangle 1 u 2 or 2 u 1 such that its entire border is colored, but the unit segment inside it is not colored. Prove that the teacher can win. M. Didin, A. Kuznetsov
