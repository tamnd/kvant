## Condition

Three numbers were written on the board. Then one of them was erased and the sum of two other numbers was written together, reduced by one. This operation was repeated several times and as a result the numbers 17, 1967, 1983 were obtained. Could the numbers a) 2, 2, 2 be originally written? 6) 3, 3, 3? (8)

A. A. Berzins
