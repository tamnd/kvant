## Condition

Let $A$ and $E$ be two opposite vertices of a regular octagon. At vertex $A$ there is a frog. From any vertex of the octagon, except for vertex $E$, the frog can jump to any of the two neighboring vertices. Once at vertex $E$, the frog stops and remains there. Let $a_n$ be the number of ways a frog can get from vertex $A$ to vertex $E$ in exactly $n$ jumps. Prove that
$$a_{2n-1} = 0, a_{2n} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (x^{n-1} - y^{n-1}),$$
$n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$, where $x = 2 + \sqrt{2}; y = 2 - \sqrt{2}$.
