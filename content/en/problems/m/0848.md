$$f_0(x) = \left| x - 1 \right| - 2 \left| x \right| - 3$$

b) figure 1 shows the graphs of three “piecewise linear” functions $f_1, f_2, f_3$. Write the formulas for them in the form
$$y = kx + b + c_1 |x - a_1| + \dots + c_m |x - a_m|,$$
where $k, b, c_i, a_i$ are some numbers, $m$ is the number of break points of the graph ($a_i$ are the abscissas of break points, $i = 1, 2, \dots, m$).

c) Write down the function $f_0$ from point a) in the same form.

d) Answer: If in the function record $f$, we consider in the problem, the sign of the model is included $n$ times, then the number of breaks $m(f)$ her schedule does not exceed $2^n-1$.

We will prove this statement by induction on $n$. For $n=0$ it is obvious (the linear function has no kinks). Пусть оно верно, если число модели меньше $n$, а в запись функции $f$ входит $n$ модели. Тогда эту функцию можно представить в виде $f=|g|+h$, причем функция $g$ записьается с $n_1$ модулями, а $h$ — с $n_2$ модулями, где $n_1+n_2=n-1$. By virtue of statement (1) and (2) to point a) and the assumption of induction

$$m(f) \leq m(|g|)+m(h) \leq 2m(g)+1+m(h) \leq 2 \cdot (2^{n_1}-1)+1+2^{n_2}-1=2^{n_1+1}+2^{n_2}-2 \leq 2^{n_1+n_2+1}-1=2^{n}-1,$$

since $2^{n_1+n_2+1}-2^{n_1+1}-2^{n_2}+1=(2^{n_1+1}-1)(2^{n_2}-1) \geq 0$.

Graph of a function $y=|...||x|-2^{n-2}|-2^{n-3}|...-1|$ (cm. Fig. 3 for $n=3$) has exactly $2^n-1$ break (at $n$ modules in the function notation), so the estimate in the problem is exact.
