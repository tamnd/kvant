## Condition

a) Prove that if $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 0$, then
$$\sin \alpha + \sin \beta + \sin \gamma = -4 \sin \frac{\alpha}{2} \sin \frac{\beta}{2} \sin \frac{\gamma}{2}.$$
6) The values ​​of $\hat{A}, \hat{B}$ and $\hat{C}$ angles of the triangle satisfy the conditions
$$\frac{\sin \hat{A} + \sin \hat{B} + \sin \hat{C}}{\cos \hat{A} + \cos \hat{B} + \cos \hat{C}} = \sqrt{3}.$$
Prove that at least one of them is equal to $60^\circ$.
