## Condition

* On a given plane there are 1000 squares on the side, parallel to the coordinate axes. Let $M$ be the set of centers of these squares. Prove that it is possible to mark a part of the squares so that each point of the set $M$ falls into at least one and at most four marked squares.

A. Plotkin
