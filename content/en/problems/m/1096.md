## Condition

The diameter $d$ of the circle is divided into $k$ equal parts, and through each division point a chord is drawn, redistributing the diameter. Prove that the sum of the lengths of all chords is at least 0.5 $kd$ and less than 0.8 $kd$.

P. Kharitonov, A. Chazirov (10th grade students)
