## Condition

On the segment [0; 1] a set $M$ is given, which is the union of several segments, such that the distance between any two points from $M$ is not equal to 0.1. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments making up $M$ is less than a) 0.55; b) 0.5.

A. Kach, 10th grade student
