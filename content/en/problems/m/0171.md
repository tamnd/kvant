## Condition

A regular hexagon is drawn on the plane, the side length of which is 1. Using only a ruler, construct a segment of length 1/7.

A. V. Alyaev

## Solution

A regular six-dimensional plank is drawn on the plane, the side length of which is equal to 1. Using a ruler, construct a segment of length $\sqrt{7}$.

You can easily prove that the length of the red segments on figure 2 is equal to $\sqrt{7}$.
