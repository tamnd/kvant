## Condition

On one of the two given intersecting spheres points A and B are taken, on the other - C and D. The segment AC passes through the common point of the sphere. The segment BD passes through another common point of the sphere and is parallel to the line containing the centers of the sphere. Prove that the projections of segments AB and CD onto line AC are equal.

I. F. Sharytsin
