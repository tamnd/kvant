## Condition

Mark $4k$ points on the circle and color them alternately red and blue; then $2k$ red points were randomly connected in pairs $k$ by red segments, and $2k$ blue ones by $k$ blue segments (three segments do not intersect at one point). Prove that there are at least $k$ points of intersection of the red segments with the blue ones.

S. V. Fomin
