## Condition

Let it be a $A - 2n$-digit number (the first digit is not zero). We will call a number $A$ special if it itself is a perfect square and the numbers formed by its first $n$ digits and its last $n$ digits are also perfect squares; in this case, the second number begins with the number 0, but should not be equal to zero.

a) Find all two-digit and four-digit special numbers.

b) Prove that there is at least one 20-digit special number.

b) Prove that there are at most 10 special 100-digit numbers.

d) Prove that there is at least one 30-digit special number.

A. Levin, I. Bernitsy
