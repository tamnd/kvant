## Condition

Find the smallest value of the expression $m^2+n^2$ for all possible pairs of $(m; n)$ natural numbers such that $1 \leqslant m \leqslant 1981$, $1 \leqslant n \leqslant 1981$ and $|n^2-mn-m^2|=1$.
