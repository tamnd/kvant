## Condition

The plane intersects the lateral edges of a regular quadrangular rectangle at points spaced from the vertex at distances $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ (Fig. 1). Prove that

$$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{d}.$$

K. Shvartsman
