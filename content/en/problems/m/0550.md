## Condition

From point $A$ to point $B$, the distance between which is $d$ km, there should be added $n$ hairpeds, which have $m$ hairpeds. Everyone can walk at a speed of $u$ km/h or ride a bike at a speed of $v$ km/h. In what shortest time can all $n$ hairpeds get from $A$ to $B$? (The time is calculated according to the last arrival. The bicycle can be left on the road without support.) Distance special case: $m=2, n=3$.

S. Kromov
