## Condition

The square is divided into $n^{2} \ge 4$ rectangles $2 ( n - 1 )$ by straight lines, of which n – 1 are parallel to one side of the square, and the remaining n – 1 are parallel to the other. Prove that it is possible to choose 2n partition rectangles in such a way that for any two selected rectangles, one of them can be placed inside the other (possibly rotated first). S. Berlov
