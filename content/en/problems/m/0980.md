In a short period of time $\Delta t$, the hockey player, moving at a constant speed $v$ ($\Delta t$ means that the change in speed during this time can be neglected compared to the value of the speed itself), travels a distance $\Delta x=v \cdot \Delta t$. The work done by the friction force is equal to
$$A=F_{\text{тр}} \cdot \Delta x=F_{\text{тр}} v \cdot \Delta t.$$
The mass of ice growing under the ridge during this time is equal to $\Delta m=\rho h d v \cdot \Delta t$. The amount of heat required to melt this mass of ice is -
$$\Delta Q=\lambda \cdot \Delta m=\lambda \rho h d v \cdot \Delta t.$$
Thus, $A=\Delta Q$, or
$$F_{\text{тр}} v \cdot \Delta t=\lambda \rho h d v \cdot \Delta t,$$
where
$$F_{\text{тр}}=\lambda \rho h d=3,3 \cdot 10^5 \cdot 0,9 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 10^{-5} \cdot 2 \times 10^{-3} (\text{H}) \approx 17,8 \text{ H}.$$
