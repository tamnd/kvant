## Condition

Let $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ be real numbers, $0 \leqslant x_i \leqslant 1$. Prove that the quantity
$$x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_{n-1} + x_n = x_1x_2 \cdots x_nx_1$$
does not exceed
a) 1 at $n=3$;
b) 2 at $n=4$;
b) $^* [n/2]$ for any $n \geqslant 3$.

V. Batyshev, 10th grade student
