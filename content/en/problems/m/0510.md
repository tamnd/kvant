## Condition

In the book “Hungarian Mathematical Olympiads” the following problem is given (No. 148): “Prove that for all positive $\alpha < \pi$ the inequality holds
$$\sin \alpha + \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\alpha + \frac{1}{3} \sin 3\alpha > 0.$$
Prove the following generalization of this inequality: for all $0 < \alpha < \pi$ and natural $n$
$$\sin \alpha + \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\alpha + \frac{1}{3} \sin 3\alpha + \dots$$
$$\dots + \frac{1}{n} \sin n\alpha > 0.$$
I. Birder, R. Ushakov
