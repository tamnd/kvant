## Condition

On the table there are scales and $n$ weights of various masses. The grips are placed on the scales one by one (at each step, any weight is taken from the table and added to one or the other scale).

a) Prove that the grip can be placed in such an order that first the left cup is moved, then the right one, then the left one again, the right one again, and so on.

This sequence of weighing results is comparable to a word from the letters $L$ and $R$: LRLRLR... Here the letter $L$ means to translate the left cup, and the letter $R$ means to translate the right cup.

6)* Prove that for any word of length $n$ from the letters $L$ and $R$ you can put the grip on the scales in such an order that this word corresponds to the sequence of weighing results.
