## Condition

In parliament, each member has no more than three enemies. Prove that parliament can be divided into two chambers so that each parlamentaria in the same chamber with it will have no more than one enemy. (It is considered that if $A$ is the enemy of $B$, then $B$ is the enemy of $A$.) (8-10)

O. Borodin

## Solution

In parliament, each member has no more than three enemies. Prove that parliament can be divided into two chambers so that each parliamentarian in the same chamber with him will have no more than one enemy. (It is considered that if $A$ is the enemy of $B$, then $B$ is the enemy of $A$)

Let's carry out the partition in an arbitrary way. If in any of the chambers some parlamentaria $A$ has $\geqslant 2$ enemies, then the number of his enemies in the other chamber is $\leqslant 1$. Therefore, after moving $A$ to another chamber, the total number of pairs of enemies inside the chambers will decrease. We will continue such movements until the required condition is met.
