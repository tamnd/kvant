## Condition

A natural number is given. You are allowed to place pluses between the digits of the number in an arbitrary manner and calculate the sum (for example, from the number 123456789 you can get 12345 + 6 + 789 = 13140). With the resulting number, a similar operation is again allowed, and so on. Prove that from any number you can get a single digit by performing no more than 10 such operations. A.Tolpygo
