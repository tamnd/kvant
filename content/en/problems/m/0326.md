## Condition

The chord of the circle is removed from the center by a distance $h$. A square is inscribed in each of the segments subtended by a chord so that two adjacent vertices of the square lie on the arc, and the other two on the chord (Fig. 1). What is the difference in the lengths of the sides of these squares?

*E. G. Gotman*
