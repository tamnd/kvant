## Condition

The centers of all 64 squares are marked on the chessboard. Is it possible to draw 13 lines on a board so that in each of the parts into which these lines divide the board there is no more than one marked point? (Straight lines must not pass through the centers of the fields, Fig. 1).

A. N. Pechkovsky

## Solution

The chains of all 64 squares are laid out on the chessboard. Is it possible to check 13 lines on the board so that in each of the parts in which these lines divide the board there is no more than one offset point? (Straight lines must not pass through field chains.)
