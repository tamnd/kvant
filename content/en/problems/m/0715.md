## Condition

The primo corner is divided into cells (Fig. 1). On some cells there are chips, and the arrangement of the chips can be transformed as follows: if for some chips the cell adjacent to the top and the cell adjacent to the right are free, then a chip is placed in these cells, and the old chip is removed. First, one chip is placed in the corner cell. Is it possible to use the indicated operations to clear the corners of a) three, 6) six, b) ten cells, shown in Figures 2, a, 6, b, from chips?
