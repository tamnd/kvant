## Condition

Given natural numbers $n$ and $r$, $1 \leqslant r \leqslant n$. Consider all possible subsets of the set {1, 2, ..., $n$} consisting of $r$ numbers, and in each choice the smallest number. Prove that the arithmetic mean of all chosen numbers is $(n+1) / (r+1)$. (For example, for $n=3$, $r=2$ we get three subsets {1, 2}, {1, 3}, {2, 3} and the arithmetic mean is $(1+1+2)/3 = 4/3$).
