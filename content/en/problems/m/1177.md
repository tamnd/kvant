## Condition

Prove that for positive numbers $x_1, x_2, ..., x_n$ not exceeding 1, the following inequality holds:
$$(1+x_1)^{1/x_2}(1+x_2)^{1/x_3}(1+x_3)^{1/x_4}...(1+x_n)^{1/x_1} \geq 2^n.$$
K. P. Kokhas, V. M. Televka
