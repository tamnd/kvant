## Condition

There are $n$ points $A_1, \dots, A_n$ on the plane, no three of which lie on the same straight line. What is the maximum number of segments with ends at these points that can be checked so that no triangles with vertices at these points are obtained?

A. Grigoryan, M. Primak, S. Fishbein
