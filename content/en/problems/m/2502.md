## Condition

Petya and Vasya take turns writing down natural numbers on the board that do not exceed 2018 (writing down an existing number is prohibited); Petya begins. If after a player's move there are three numbers on the board forming an arithmetic progression, that player wins. Which player has a strategy that is guaranteed to win? M. Didin, P. Kozhevnikov
