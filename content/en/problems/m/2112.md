## Condition

Let H be the point of intersection of the altitudes of an acute triangle ABC. A circle with center in the middle of side BC, passing through point H, intersects line BC at points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$. Similarly, a circle centered at the midpoint of side CA passing through point H intersects line CA at points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, and a circle centered at the midpoint of side AB passing through point H intersects line AB at points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$. Prove that points $A_{1}, A_{2}, B_{1}, B_{2}, C_{1}, C_{2}$ lie on the same circle. A. Gavrilyuk
