## Condition

On the data plane there is a straight line l and two points A and B on one side of it. On straight line l, point M is selected, the sum of the distances from which to points A and B is the smallest, and point N, for which the distances from A and B are equal: AN=BN. Prove that the point of the inverses of all the numbers written out is equal to n/2.
(For example, when n=3 $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 4} + \frac{1}{3 \cdot 4} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4} = \frac{3}{2}$).
