## Condition

In triangle ABC, sides AB and BC are equal. Point D inside the triangle is such that $\angle$ ADC $= 2 \angle$ ABC. Prove that twice the distance from point B to the outer bisector of angle ADC is equal to AD + DC. S. Berlov
