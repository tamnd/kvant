## Condition

The sequence $a_1, a_2, ...$ is given by the rule: $a_{2n}=a_n$ for $n \geqslant 1$ and $a_{4n+1}=1$, $a_{4n+3}=0$ for $n \geqslant 0$. Prove that this sequence has no period.

Yu. V. Nesterenko
