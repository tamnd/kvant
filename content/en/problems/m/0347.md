## Condition

Two people play this game. The first one guesses two numbers from 1 to 25, and the second one must guess them. He can name any two numbers from 1 to 25 and find out from the first how many of the numbers he named - 0.1 or 2 - coincide with the mystery ones. In what minimum number of questions can he definitely determine the hidden numbers?

A. A. Grizoryan

## Solution

Two cerams in a dipping cerra. The first one is given two numbers from 1 to 25, and the second one must eat them up. He can name any two numbers from 1 to 25 and find out from the first how many of the numbers he named - 0.1 or 2 - coincide with the given ones. In what minimum number of questions can he definitely determine the given numbers?

| Answer | Given numbers |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1,1 | 2i,25 |
| 1,0 | 2i-1,23 |
| 0,1 | 2i-1,24 |
| 0,0 | 2i-1,25 |
