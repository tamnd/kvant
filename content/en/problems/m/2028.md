## Condition

It is known that liars always lie, truthful people always tell the truth, and cunning people can both lie and tell the truth. You can ask questions that have a “yes” or “no” answer (for example: “is it true that this person is cunning?”). a) There are three in front of you - a liar, a truthful one and a cunning one, who know which one is which. How can you find out? b) There are four people in front of you - a liar, a truthful one and two cunning ones (all four know which one is which). Prove that cunning people can agree to answer in such a way that you, asking these four, will not know for sure who he is. B. Ginzburg, M. Gerver
