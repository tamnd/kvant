## Condition

Tangent circles to the triangle ABC drawn at points A and B intersect at point P. Prove that line PC a) intersects side AB at point K, dividing it in the ratio $AC^2:BC^2$;
b) is symmetric to the median drawn from C relative to the bissertrix of angle C of the triangle.

S. Litovchenko, 10th grade student.
