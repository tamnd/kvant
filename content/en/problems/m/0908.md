## Condition

On side AB of triangle ABC, point P is selected, and lines parallel to BC and AC are drawn through it until they intersect with sides AC and BC, respectively, at points M and N. For which choice of point P does segment MN have the shortest length?
Solve this problem a) for a triangle with right angle C; 6)* for an arbitrary triangle ABC.

E. G. Gotman
