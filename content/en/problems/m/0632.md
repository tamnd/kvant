## Condition

The cargo, packed in containers, must be delivered to the Salyut orbital space station. The number of containers is at least 35, the total cargo weight is 18 tons. There are seven Progress transport ships, each of which can deliver 3 tons of cargo into orbit. It is known that these ships can simultaneously deliver any 35 of the available containers. Prove that they can deliver all the available cargo into orbit at once. (8-9)

A. Kolotov
