## Condition

Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be distinct pairwise coprime natural numbers. Prove that there are infinitely many natural numbers $b$ such that the numbers $b + a_1, b + a_2, \dots, b + a_n$ are also pairwise relatively prime.

V. F. Lev
