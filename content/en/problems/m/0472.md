## Condition

Inside the cube there is a convex polyhedron, the projection of which onto each of the faces coincides with this face. Prove that the volume of a polyhedron is at least 1/3 the volume of a cube.

V. Prasolov

## Solution

Inside the cube there is a convex manifold, the projection of which onto each of the boundaries of the cube coincides with this boundary. Prove that the volume of the manifold is at least 1/3 of the volume of the cube.

Fig. 2.
