## Condition

The opposite sides of a convex hexagon ABCDEF are parallel. Let's call it “heights” - vectors with ends on straight lines containing opposite sides, perpendicular to them and directed from AB to DE, from EF to BC and from CD to AF. Prove that a circle can be drawn around this hexagon if and only if the sum of its “heights” is equal to the zero vector. A. Zaslavsky
