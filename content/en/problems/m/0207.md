## Condition

Given two triangles $A_1A_2A_3$ and $B_1B_2B_3$. Describe the vector of triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ triangle $M_1M_2M_3$ with the largest area, similar to triangle $B_1B_2B_3$ (in this case, vertex $A_1$ must lie on line $M_2M_3$, vertex $A_2$ on line $M_3M_1$, vertex $A_3$ on line $M_1M_2$).
