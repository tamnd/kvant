## Condition

One of the cells of the endless checkered strip is colored. Initially, the chip is at a distance of n cells from the colored one. A die is thrown, and if k points are rolled ( k O { 1,2,3,4,5,6 }), the chip moves k squares towards the colored square. The process continues until the chip enters a colored square (win) or until it passes a colored square (lose). At what natural number n is the probability of winning $p_{n}$ greatest? Find this highest probability value. V. Letsko
