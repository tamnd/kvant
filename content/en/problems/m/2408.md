## Condition

In triangle ABC, the medians $AA_{0}, BB_{0}, CC_{0}$ intersect at point M. Prove that the circumcenters of triangles $MA_{0} B_{0}, MCB_{0}, MA_{0} C_{0}, MBC_{0}$ and the point M lie on the same circle. P. Kozhevnikov

## Solution

In triangle ABC, the medians $AA_{0}, BB_{0}, CC_{0}$ intersect at point M. Prove that the circumcenters of triangles $MA_{0} B_{0}, MCB_{0}, MA_{0} C_{0}, MBC_{0}$ and the point M lie on the same circle.

Let $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}, O_{4}$ be the circumcenters of triangles $MA_{0} B_{0}, MCB_{0}, MA_{0} C_{0}, MBC_{0}$, respectively. Let us carry out the reasoning for the location of the points shown in figure (for other cases the location of the centers the reasoning is similar; to avoid analyzing the cases, we can consider all angles to be oriented).

"QUANTA"

⟦column⟧

Let us prove that $O_{4}$ lies on the circle $( MO_{1} O_{3} ).$ Line $O_{1} O_{3}$ is the perpendicular bisector to $MA_{0}$. Angle $MO_{1} O_{3}$ is equal to half the central angle $MO_{1} A_{0}$ of circle $( MA_{0} B_{0} ),$ i.e. angle $MB_{0} A_{0}$ inscribed in it. Line $O_{4} O_{3}$ is the perpendicular bisector to segment $MC_{0}$, therefore the angle between lines $MO_{4}$ and $MO_{3}$ is equal to half the central angle $MO_{4} C_{0}$ of circle $( MBC_{0} ),$ i.e. angle $MBC_{0}$ inscribed in it. And the angles $MB_{0} A_{0}$ and $MBC_{0}$ are equal due to the parallelism $B_{0} A_{0}$ and $BC_{0}$. Therefore – $MO_{1} O_{3} +$ – $MO_{4} O_{3} = 180 \infty$, i.e. $O_{4}$ lies on the circle $( MO_{1} O_{3} ).$ Similarly, $O_{2}$ lies on the circle $( MO_{1} O_{3} ).$ P. Kozhevnikov
