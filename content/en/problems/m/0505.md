## Condition

a) Let $n$ materials of points of equal mass be placed on a straight line. Let us consider an arbitrary segment of length $2r$ containing at least one of these points, and find the center of gravity $O_1$ of all points falling within it. Let's consider a segment of length $2r$ with a middle $O_1$ and find the center of gravity $O_2$ of all points that fall into this segment. Then we find the center of gravity $O_3$ of all points falling in a segment of length $2r$ with a midpoint $O_2$, etc. Prove that, starting from a certain number, all points of the sequence $O_1, O_2, O_3, \dots$ coincide.

b) Let $n$ material points be placed on the plane. Consider an arbitrary circle of radius $r$ containing at least one of these points; let us denote by $O_1$ the center of gravity of the points falling within it and construct the sequence $O_1, O_2, O_3, \dots$, where $O_{n+1}$ is the center of gravity of the points falling within a circle of radius $r$ with center $O_n$. Is it true that, starting from a certain number, all points in this sequence coincide?

P. Bleser, G. Galperin, M. Kelbert
