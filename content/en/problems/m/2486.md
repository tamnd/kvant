## Condition

The analyst made a forecast of changes in the dollar exchange rate for each of the next 12 months: by what percentage (a number greater than 0% and less than 100%) will the exchange rate change in October, by how much in November,..., by how much in September. It turned out that for each month he correctly predicted by what percentage the exchange rate would change, but was wrong with the direction of the change (i.e. if he predicted that the exchange rate would increase by x%, then the exchange rate would fall by x%, and vice versa). Moreover, after 12 months the course coincided with the forecast. In what direction did the course change in the end? A. Zaslavsky
