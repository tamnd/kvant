## Condition

Let us denote the sum $1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \dots + \frac{1}{n}$ by $h_n$. Prove (for every natural number $n$) the inequality
$$\frac{1}{h_1^2} + \frac{1}{2h_2^2} + \frac{1}{3h_3^2} + \dots + \frac{1}{nh_n^2} < 2.$$
L. D. Kurlyandchik
