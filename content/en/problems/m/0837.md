## Condition

Let $a, b, c$ be positive integers, each two of which are coprime. Prove that the largest integer that cannot be represented in the form

$$xbc + yca + zab$$

(where $x, y, z$ are non-negative integers), equals

$$2abc - ab - bc - ca.$$
