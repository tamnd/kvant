## Condition

Three machines print pairs of natural numbers on cards. The machines work as follows. The first machine, having read the card (a; b), issues a new card (a+1; b+1); the second, having read card (a; b), issues card $\left(\frac{a}{2}; \frac{b}{2}\right)$ (it only works when a and b are even); the third, given two cards (a; b) and (b; c), gives card (a; c). In addition, the machines return all read cards.

Let there initially be one card with a pair of numbers (5; 19). Can I use it in any order to get the card: a) (1;50)? 6) (1;100)?

c) Let there initially be one card (a; b), a <b>, and we want to get card (1; n). For what n can this be done? (8-10 grades)

V. Gutenmacher
