## Condition

In some kingdom, some state, the territory of which is shaped like a square with a side of 2 km, the king decides to invite all residents to his palace for a ball by 7 pm. To do this, at noon he sends a messenger with an order, who can convey any instruction to any resident, who in turn can convey any instruction to any other resident, etc. Before receiving the instruction, each resident is at home (in a known place) and can transmit at a speed of 3 km/h in any direction. Prove that the king can limit the notification so that all residents have time to arrive at the beginning of the ball.
