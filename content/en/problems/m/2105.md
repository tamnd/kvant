## Condition

A circle $\omega$ with center O is inscribed in the angle BAC and touches its sides at points B and C. A point Q is chosen inside the angle BAC. A point P is found on the segment AQ such that $AQ \perp OP$. The straight line OP intersects the circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ circumscribed about triangles BPQ and CPQ, secondarily at points M and N. Prove that OM = ON. A. Hakobyan
