## Condition

When flies crawl along the sides of the triangle $ABC$ so that the center of gravity of the triangle remains in one place. Prove that it coincides with the center of gravity of the triangle $ABC$ if it is known that one of the flies crawled along the entire boundary of the triangle. (The center of gravity of a triangle is the point of intersection of its medians.) (8 cells)

S. V. Fomin

## Solution

Three men crawl along the sides of triangle ABC so that the center of the men formed by the triangle remains in one place. Prove that it coincides with the center of the triangle ABC if it is known that one of the men has crawled along the entire boundary of the triangle. (The center of a husband is called the point of intersection of its medians.)
