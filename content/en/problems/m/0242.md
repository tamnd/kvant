## Condition

Let $A_i H_i$ be the height and $A_i M_i$ the median drawn from the vertex $A_i$ of the acute triangle $A_1 A_2 A_3$ ($i = 1, 2, 3$). Prove that one of the three products
$|H_1 M_1| \cdot |A_2 A_3|, |H_2 M_2| \cdot |A_3 A_1|, |H_3 M_3| \cdot |A_1 A_2|$
equal to the sum of the other two $^*$). Is this statement true for right and obtuse triangles?
S. Salnikov, 10th grade student (2. Mary)

## Solution

Let $A_i H_i$ be the height and $A_i M_i$ the median drawn from the vertex $A_i$ of the island triangle $A_1 A_2 A_3$ (i = 1, 2, 3). Let us prove that one of the three products $|H_1 M_1| \cdot |A_2 A_3| \cdot |H_2 M_2| \cdot |A_3 A_1| \cdot |H_3 M_3| \cdot |A_1 A_2|$ is equal to the sum of the other two *). Let us prove that this statement is true for any triangle. Let us express the product $|H_1 M_1| \cdot |A_2 A_3|$ in terms of the side lengths $|A_2 A_3| = a_1$, $|A_3 A_1| = a_2$, $|A_1 A_2| = a_3$.

From the Pythagorean theorem applied to $\triangle A_1 H_1 A_2$ and $\triangle A_1 H_1 A_3$ it follows that (Fig. 1) $|A_1 H_1|^2 = |A_1 A_2|^2 - |A_2 H_1|^2 = |A_1 A_3|^2 - |A_3 H_1|^2$, whence
$|A_2 H_1|^2 - |A_3 H_1|^2 = a_3^2 - a_2^2$.

Let us prove that, modulo, the difference (1) is equal to 2 $|H_1 M_1| \cdot |A_2 A_3|$.
