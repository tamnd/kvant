## Condition

A sheet of checkered paper measuring $n \times n$ cells was colored in $n$ colors (each cell was covered in one of these colors or not covered at all). A correct coloring is one in which each row and column does not contain two cells of the same color. Is it always possible to “repaint” the entire sheet in the correct way if they were originally covered correctly?

a) $n^2$ – 1 cell;
b) $n^2$ – 2 cells;
b) $n$ cells?

D. Logachev
