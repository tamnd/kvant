## Condition

Prove that for any positive $a_1, a_2, a_3, ..., a_n$ the following inequality holds:

$$\frac{(a_1+a_2+...+a_n)^2}{2(a_1^2+a_2^2+...+a_n^2)} \leq \frac{a_1}{a_2+a_3} + \frac{a_2}{a_3+a_4} +...+\frac{a_n}{a_1+a_2}.$$

E. G. Moiseev, 10th grade student
