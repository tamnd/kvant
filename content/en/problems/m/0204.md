## Condition

A natural number is considered good if the digits 1973 appear in a row in its decimal notation, and bad otherwise. (For example, the number 197 639 917 is bad, 116 519 732 is good.) Prove that there is a natural number $n$ such that among all $n$-digit numbers (from $10^{n-1}$ to $10^n$ there is 1)

⟦folio 29⟧

more good than bad. Try to find an opportunity less like $n$.
