## Condition

A five-word recognition with bases $A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5$ and $B_1B_2B_3B_4B_5$ is given. All edges of the bases and all segments $A_iB_j(i, j=1, 2, 3, 4, 5)$ are colored either red or green so that in each triangle with vertices at the vertices of the prism, the sides of which are colored, there are two sides of different colors. Prove that all ten edges of the bases are colored the same.

XXI International Olympiad in Mathematics, 1979

## Solution

Given is a five-pointed prism with bases $A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5$ and $B_1B_2B_3B_4B_5$. All edges of the bases and all segments $A_iB_j$ ($i, j = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$) are surrounded by either red or blue so that in every triangle with vertices at the vertices of a prism whose sides are surrounded there are two sides of different colors. Prove that all ten edges of the bases are surrounded equally.
