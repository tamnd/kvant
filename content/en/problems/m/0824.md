## Condition

In the grid shown in figure 3, each cell has size $1 \times 1$. Can this grid be represented as a union?
a) 8 broken lines of length 5;
b) 5 broken lines of length 8? (8)

N. I. Avilov

## Solution

In the grid shown at figure 1, each cell is $1 \times 1$ in size. Can this grid be represented as a union of $a)$ 8 broken lines of length 5; 6) 5 broken lines of length 8?

◆ a) It is possible. Let us denote some nodes of the mesh as shown in figure 2. Then our mesh splits into a union of eight broken lines of length 5: $AR_1S_1$, $R_1R_2S_2$, $R_2R_3S_3$, $R_3BC$, $AP_1Q_1$, $P_1P_2Q_2$, $P_2P_3Q_3$, $P_3DC$. There are other ways.
