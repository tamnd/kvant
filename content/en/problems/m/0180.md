## Condition

Two people play this game. One thinks of the natural number $n$, and the other asks questions like “is it true that $n \geq x$” (he can choose x at his discretion) and receives “yes” or “no” answers. What possible strategy $T$ of the second game is promoted by the function $f_T(n)$, equal to the number of questions (before debugging), if the number $n$ was conceived.
