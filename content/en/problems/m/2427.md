## Condition

There are N numbers written on the board, they are all different, and one of them is equal to 0. You can take any polynomial, each coefficient of which is equal to one of the written numbers (some of the coefficients can be equal), and add all the roots of this polynomial to the board. After several such operations, all the integers from –2016 to 2016 (and possibly some other numbers) ended up on the board. Find the smallest possible value of N. G. Zhukov
