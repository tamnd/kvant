## Condition

Several numbers are written in a circle. If for some four consecutive numbers $a, b, c, d$ it turns out that $(a-d)(b-c) < 0$, then the numbers $b$ and $c$ can be swapped. Prove that this operation can be performed only a finite number of times.
V. B. Alekseev
