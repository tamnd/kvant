## Condition

Given an isosceles trapezoid ABCD with bases BC and AD (see figure). The circle passes through vertices B and C and secondarily intersects side AB and diagonal BD at points X and Y, respectively. The tangent drawn to the circle at point C intersects the ray AD at point Z. Prove that points X, Y and Z lie on the same straight line. A. Kuznetsov
