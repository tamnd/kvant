## Condition

The lines containing the medians of triangle ABC intersect its circumcircle $\omega$ a second time at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Lines passing through A, B, C and parallel to opposite sides intersect $\omega$ a second time at points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$. Prove that the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}, C_{1} C_{2}$ intersect at one point. A. Zaslavsky
