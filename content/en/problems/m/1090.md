## Condition

Prove that a) for any positive numbers a, b and c, it is true that your address is not on it (in this envelope you will receive the result of checking the solution).

The conditions of each original problem proposed for publication are assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Quata problem”, a new problem in physics or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you are a teacher.

## Solution

Prove that a) for any positive numbers a, b and c the following inequality is true:
$$\sqrt{a^2 - ab + b^2} + \sqrt{b^2 - bc + c^2} \geq \sqrt{a^2 + ac + c^2};$$
b) inequality from n. a) becomes equality if and only if $1/a+1/c=1/b$.
