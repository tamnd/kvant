## Condition

The castle is surrounded by a circular wall with nine towers manned by knights. At the end of each hour, they all move to neighboring towers, with each knight moving all the time either clockwise or counterclockwise. During the night, each knight manages to be on duty on each tower. It is known that there was an hour when at least two knights were on duty on each tower, and there was an hour when exactly one knight was on duty on exactly five towers. Prove that there was an hour when there were no knights on one of the towers at all. M. Murashkin
