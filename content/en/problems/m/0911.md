## Condition

On sides AB and CD of a convex quadrilateral ABCD, arbitrary points E and F are selected, respectively. Prove that the midpoints of the segments AF, BF, CE and DE are the vertices of a quadrilateral, and its area does not depend on the choice of points E and F.
