## Condition

a) Prove that there is an arithmetic progression of 4 different terms containing only powers of natural numbers $n^k (k \geqslant 2)$. Does such a progression exist from 6) any finite number, c) an infinite number of terms?
Is there an infinite (non-constant) arithmetic progression that does not contain d) any power of a natural number, e) not a single number composed of identical digits?
