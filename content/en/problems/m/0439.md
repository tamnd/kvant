## Condition

a) Prove that the equation
$$ax^k + bx^l + cx^m = 1$$
(where $a, b, c$ are real numbers, $k, l$ and $m$ are natural numbers) has at most three positive roots.

b) Prove that the equation
$$a_1x^{k_1} + a_2x^{k_2} + \dots + a_nx^{k_n} = 1$$
(where $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ are real numbers, $k_1, k_2, \dots, k_n$ are natural numbers) has at most $n$ positive roots.

b) Prove that the equation
$$ax^k(x+1)^p + bx^l(x+1)^q + cx^m(x+1)^r = 1$$
(where $a, b, c$ are real numbers, $k, l, m, p, q, r$ are natural numbers) has no more than 14 positive roots.

A. Kushirenko
