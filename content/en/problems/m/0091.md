## Condition

Two people play tic-tac-toe on an endless sheet of checkered paper. The beginner puts a cross in any cell. Each next move, he must place a cross in any free cell; any cell adjacent to one is considered to be any cell that has a common side or a common vertex with it. The second player, with each turn, can place three polykkas at once in any three free cells (not necessarily next to each other). Prove that, no matter how the first one plays, the second one can “lock him up”: make sure that the first one has nowhere else to put a cross.
