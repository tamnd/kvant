## Condition

10 children have several bags of candy. Children begin to share candy among themselves. Everyone takes turns taking their share from each bag and leaving. The share is calculated as follows: divide the current number of candies in each bag by the number of remaining children (including yourself), if not divided completely, round down to the nearest whole number. Can everyone get a different amount of candy if: a) there are only 8 bags; b) there are only 9 bags? A. Glebov
