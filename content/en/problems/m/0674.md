## Condition

The points $A_1, B_1$ and $C_1$ are taken on the sides $BC, AC$ and $AB$ of the acute triangle $ABC$, respectively. It is known that the center of the circle circumscribed about triangle $ABC$ coincides with the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$. Prove that triangles $ABC$ and $A_1B_1C_1$ are similar.

D. Izaak
