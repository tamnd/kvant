## Condition

Let O be the circumcenter of triangle ABC. Let us plot on the rays AC and BC, respectively, the segments $AC_{a}$ and $BC_{b}$, equal in length to AB (Fig. 1). Let $O_{c}$ be the center of circle $( CC_{a} C_{b} ).$ Similarly, we define points $O_{a}$ and $O_{b}$. Prove that point O is the orthocenter of triangle $O_{a} O_{b} O_{c}$. A. Zaslavsky
