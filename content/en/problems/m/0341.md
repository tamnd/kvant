## Condition

20 teams are participating in the world championship. Among them are $k$ European teams, the results of the meeting between them at the World Championship count toward the European Championship. The championship is held in one round. In which direction $k$ can it turn out that the European team that scores strictly the most points in the European Championship will score strictly the least number of points in the World Championship, if it is a) a hockey championship (ties are allowed),
b) * volleyball championship (there are no draws)? What will be the answers to these questions if there are not 20 teams, but $n$?

Yu. A. Schrader
