## Condition

For five integers a, b, c, d, e, it is known that the sums a+b+c+d+e and a²+b²+c²+d²+e² are divisible by an odd number p. Prove that the number a⁵+b⁵+c⁵+d⁵+e⁵ - 5abcde is also divisible by p.

S. V. Fomin
