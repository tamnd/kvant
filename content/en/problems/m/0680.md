## Condition

Two signalmen play this game. There are $n$ telephone nodes, and signalmen take turns connecting

⟦folio 23⟧

hire two of them as kavels of their choice.
The winner will be the one after whose move it will be possible to reach any other node from any node (perhaps after several intermediate ones; the beginning of the game is shown at figure 3).
a) Find out who wins at $n=4, 5, 6, 7, 8$ - the beginner or his partner?
b) What is the answer for an arbitrary $n$?
Consider also two modified versions of the game:
c) Let the player who tied all the nodes lose. Answer questions a) and b) for this new game;
d) First, let all the nodes be connected in pairs by kavel, and the signalmen remove one connection at a time. The player who breaks the connection in the scheme loses. The question is the same: who wins with the correct strategy for $n=4, 5, 6, 7, 8$? for arbitrary $n$?
