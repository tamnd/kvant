## Condition

The 2016 red and 2016 blue cards have positive numbers written on them, they are all different. It is known that pairwise sums of some 64 numbers are written on cards of one color, and pairwise products of the same 64 numbers are written on cards of a different color. Is it always possible to determine what color cards have paired sums on them? B.Frenkin
