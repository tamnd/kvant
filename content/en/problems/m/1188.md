## Condition

a) Given 101 rectangles with integer sides not exceeding 100. Prove that among them there are three rectangles $A, B, C$ that can be placed inside each other: $A \subseteq B \subseteq C$.

b) Prove that among 1989 rectangles with integer sides not exceeding 100, there are 40 rectangles such that the first can be placed in the second, the second in the third, ..., the 39th in the 40th.

N. M. Sedrakin
