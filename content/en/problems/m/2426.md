## Condition

There are 64 cities in the country, some pairs of them are connected by road, but we do not know which ones. We can choose any pair of cities and get an answer to the question whether there is a road between them. We want to find out whether in this country it is possible to get from any city to any other by driving along the roads. Prove that there is no algorithm that can do this in less than 2016 questions. K. Knop
