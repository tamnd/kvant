## Condition

There are 6,783 cosmonauts in the squad preparing for a flight to Mars, and it is known that among any four of them, three can be selected to form a complex mission for the landing module. Prove that it is possible to select 5 astronauts, any three of which form a complex experiment.

N. N. Silkin, M. V. Volkov
