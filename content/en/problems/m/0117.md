## Condition

Several people observe the snail for $t$ minutes. Each one is observed for approximately 1 minute and then, during this minute, the snail crawls approximately 1 meter. At no point in time was the snail left unobserved. What kind of path could she have crawled in these $t$ minutes?

Try this problem first for small values ​​of $t$, such as $t=2,5$.
