## Condition

A point moves in a triangle, reflecting from its sides according to the law “the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.” Periodic is also the name given to the trajectory of this point, which is a closed broken line and does not pass through the vertices of the triangle. (Examples of periodic trajectories with 3 and 6 links in a triangle are given in figure 8 on p. 12. a) Prove that no triangle with different sides contains a four-link periodic trajectory.

Is there an acute triangle with a periodic trajectory 6) of 5 links? c) from 7 links?

G. A. Galperin, A. M. Stgenin
