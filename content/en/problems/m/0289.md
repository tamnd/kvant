## Condition

$N$ weights, each weighing an integer number of grams, are arranged into $K$ heaps of equal weight. Prove that it is possible to remove one of the weights in at least $K$ different ways so that the remaining $(N-1)$ weights can no longer be divided into $K$ heaps of equal weight.
