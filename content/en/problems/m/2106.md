## Condition

For what natural numbers n > 1 are there natural numbers $b_{1}, K, b_{n}$ (not all of which are equal) such that for all natural numbers k the number $( b_{1} + k )( b_{2} + k ) K ) K ( b_{n} + k )$ is a power of the natural number? (The exponent may depend on k, but must always be greater than 1.) V. Proizvolov, V. Senderov
