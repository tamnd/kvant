## Condition

Given a triangle $C_1C_2O$. It carries bissertis $C_2C_3$, then in triangle $C_2C_3O$ - bissertis $C_3C_4$ and so on. Prove that the sequence of angle values ​​$\gamma_n = \frac{1}{2}C_{n+1}C_nO$ tends to a limit, and find this limit if $\frac{1}{2}C_1OC_2 = \alpha$.

A. Vernard, M. Felshtin and I. Tkachev, 10th grade students
