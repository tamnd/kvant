## Condition

There are 12 points given on the plane, which are the vertices of four squares $A_1B_1A_2C_1$, $A_2C_2A_3B_2$, $A_3B_3A_4C_3$ and $A_4C_4A_1B_4$ (the vertices of each square are listed clockwise). Prove that $B_1B_2B_3B_4$ and $C_1C_2C_3C_4$ are congruent parallelograms, one of which is obtained from the other by rotating by $90^\circ$ (these parallelograms can be degenerate: the four vertices of each of them in this case lie on the same straight line).
