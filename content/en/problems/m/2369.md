## Condition

Grisha wrote down 100 numbers on the board. He then increased each number by 1 and noticed that the product of all 100 numbers did not change. He again increased each number by 1, and again the product of all numbers did not change, and so on. In total, Grisha repeated this procedure k times, and all k times the product of numbers did not change. Find the largest possible value of k. I. Bogdanov, G. Galperin
