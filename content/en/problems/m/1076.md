## Condition

The bissertis of the angle $A$ of an acute triangle $ABC$ intersects the side $BC$ at the point $L$, and the circumcircle of the triangle at the point $N$ (different from $A$); $K$ and $M$ are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from $L$ to sides $AB$ and $AC$ (Fig. 1). Prove that the quadrilateral $AKNM$ is equal to the triangle $ABC$.

I. A. Kushinr
