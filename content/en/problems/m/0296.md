## Condition

The table $n \times n$ contains $n^2$ numbers whose sum is non-negative. Prove that it is possible to rearrange the columns of the table so that the sum of $n$ numbers along the diagonal, looking from the lower left corner to the upper right corner, is non-negative.

A. Tupanov

## Solution

** An n × n table contains n² numbers whose sum is non-negative. Prove that you can rearrange the columns of the table so that the sum of n numbers along the diagonal going from the lower left to the upper right is non-negative.
