## Condition

In the Republic of Mathematicians, they chose the number $\alpha > 2$ and issued coins in denominations of 1 ruble, as well as in $\alpha^{k}$ rubles for each natural number k. Moreover, $\alpha$ was chosen so that the denominations of all coins, except the smallest one, are irrational. Could it turn out that any amount of natural rubles can be collected with these coins, using coins of each denomination no more than 6 times? I. Bogdanov, S. Berlov
