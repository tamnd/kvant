## Condition

Let $AB$ and $CD$ be two chords of a circle, and let the points $K$ and $H$ be constructed so that all four angles $KAB, KCD, HBA$ and $HDC$ are straight lines. Prove that line $KH$ passes through the center of the circle and through the intersection point of lines $AD$ and $BC$.
I. F. Sharytsin, A. I. Yanovsky
