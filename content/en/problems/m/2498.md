## Condition

There are 100 pairwise distinct positive numbers written on the board. For each pair of written numbers, their sum was calculated. What is the largest number of pairs that can have this sum among the numbers written on the board? Folklore (based on the Olympiads of the Czech Republic and Slovakia)
