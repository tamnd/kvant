## Condition

The polynomial $p$ has the following property: for some number $a$

$$p(x) = p(a-x).$$

Prove that $p(x)$ can be represented as a polynomial in $\left(x - \frac{a}{2}\right)^2$. For example, if $p(x) = x^5 + (1-x)^5$, then obviously $p(x) = p(1-x)$ and, as is not difficult to check,

$$p(x) = 5y^2 + \frac{5}{2}y + \frac{1}{16},$$

where $y = \left(x - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2$.
