## Condition

The radius of a circle with center O rotates uniformly, turning through an angle of 360°/n in one second (where n is a natural number greater than 3). At the initial moment it occupied position OM₀, after a second - position OM₁, after another 2 seconds - position OM₂, another 3 seconds after that - position OM₃, etc., after another n-1 second - position OMₙ−1.
a) Prove that if n is a power of 2, then the radius OM₁, ..., OMₙ−1 divides the circle into n equal sectors.
b) Is this possible for other values ​​of n?
V. V. Proizvolov
