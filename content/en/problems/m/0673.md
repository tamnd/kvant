## Condition

Three washers $A, B, C$ lie on the plane at the vertices of the triangle. The hockey player chooses one of them and walks along it so that it passes between the other two and stops at some point.
a) Show how, after heel strikes, puck $C$ can return to its place, and pucks $A$ and $B$ can be swapped.
b) Can all three pucks $A, B, C$ return to their original positions after 25 hits?

A. Razborov
