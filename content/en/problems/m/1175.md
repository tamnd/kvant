## Condition

For what natural numbers $n$ is the following statement true: no matter how several disjoint regular $n$-gons are laid out on a plane, one of them can be twisted in a certain direction without defining the others? (You cannot rotate a $n$-gon, that is, all rays emanating from the points of the selected $n$-gon in the desired direction should not define the remaining $n$-gons.)

D. A. Tereshin

## Solution

For what natural $n$ is the following statement true: as if

If several non-intersecting regular n-pleasers were laid out on a plane, one of them can be moved in a certain direction without affecting the others?
