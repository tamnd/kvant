## Condition

Circle $\omega$ is inscribed in triangle ABC, in which AB < AC. The excircle of this triangle touches side BC at $A '$. Point X is chosen on the segment $A ' A$ such that the segment $A ' X$ does not intersect $\omega$. The tangents drawn from X to $\omega$ intersect the segment BC at points Y and Z. Prove that the sum XY + XZ does not depend on the choice of point X. I. Mitrofanov
