## Condition

The table $m \times n$ is filled with $mn$ numbers so that in each row and in each column these numbers form an arithmetic progression. The sum of the four numbers in the corners of the table is $s$. What is the sum of all the numbers in the table?

## Solution

Table $m \times n$ filling mn numbers so that in each row and in each column these numbers form an arithmetic progression. The sum of the four numbers at the nodes of the table is equal to s. What is the sum of all the numbers in the table?

Answer: $smn/4$.

Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_m$ be the numbers in the top line, $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_m$ the numbers in the bottom. Then $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_m = (a_1 + a_m)m/2, b_1 + b_2 + \dots + b_m = (b_1 + b_m)m/2$.

The sums of the numbers in the columns are $(a_1 + b_1)n/2, (a_2 + b_2)n/2, \dots, (a_m + b_m)n/2$. Adding these sums, we get the sum of all the numbers in the table:
$$(a_1 + b_1 + a_2 + b_2 + \dots + a_m + b_m)n/2 = (a_1 + a_m + b_1 + b_m)mn/4 = smn/4.$$
