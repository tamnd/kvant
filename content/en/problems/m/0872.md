## Condition

There are three circles $C_1, C_2, C_3$ of radii $r_1, r_2, r_3$ on the plane, each outside the other two, $r_1>r_2$ and $r_1>r_3$. From the point of intersection of external tangents to the circle $C_1$ and $C_2$, tangents to the circle $C_3$ are drawn, and from the point of intersection of external tangents to $C_1$ and $C_3$, tangents to $C_2$. Prove that the last two pairs of tangents form a quadrilateral in which a circle can be inscribed and its radius found.

L. P. Kuptsov
