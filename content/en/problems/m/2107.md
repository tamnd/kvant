## Condition

In an isosceles triangle ABC, points H and M are the intersection points of altitudes and medians, respectively. Through vertices A, B and C, lines are drawn perpendicular to lines AM, BM, CM, respectively. Prove that the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle formed by the drawn lines lies on the line MH. L. Emelyanov
