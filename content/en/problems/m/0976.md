## Condition

From the vertex $A$ of the square $ABCD$ two loops are drawn, forming an angle $45^\circ$ between them. One intersects side $BC$ at point $E$, diagonal $BD$ at point $P$, the other intersects side $CD$ at point $F$, diagonal $BD$ at point $O$. Prove that the area of ​​triangle $AEF$ is twice the area of ​​triangle $APO$.

E. G. Gotman
