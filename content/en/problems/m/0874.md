## Condition

For which integers $m$ and $n$ does the equality a) $(5+3\sqrt{2})^m=(3+5\sqrt{2})^n$ hold? 6) $(a+b\sqrt{d})^m=(b+a\sqrt{d})^n$, where $a$ and $b$ ($a\neq b$) are coprime natural numbers, and $d>1$ is a natural number whose divisors do not have prime squares?
