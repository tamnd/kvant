## Condition

2N guests came to Ivan’s birthday party. Ivan has N black and N white cylinders. He wants to organize a ball: put top hats on the guests and line them up in round dances (one or several) so that in each round dance there are at least two people and people in top hats of the same color do not stand next to each other in the round dance. Prove that Ivan can throw a ball exactly (2N)! in various ways. (Cylinders of the same color are indistinguishable; all guests are distinguishable.) G. Pogudin
