## Condition

From one bacterium, 1000 were obtained in the following way: first, the bacterium was divided into two, then one of the two resulting bacteria was divided into two, then one of the three resulting bacteria was divided into two, and so on. Prove that at some moments there existed such a bacterium, the number of flows of which among the 1000 bacteria obtained at the end is between 334 and 667.

D. Yu. Grigoriev

## Solution

** *) From one bacterium, 1000 were obtained in the following way: first, the bacterium was divided into two, then one of the two resulting bacteria was divided into two, then one of the three resulting bacteria was divided into two, and so on. Let us prove that at some points there existed such a bacterium, the number of offspring of which in the environment of 1000 bacteria, resulting in the end, is between 334 and 667.
