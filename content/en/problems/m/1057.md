## Condition

Two players alternately write down natural numbers not exceeding p on the board. The rules of the game prohibit writing divisors of already written numbers on the board. The player who cannot make another move loses.
a) Find out which player has a winning strategy for p=10 and indicate it.
b) Find out which player has a winning strategy for p=1000.

D. V. Fomin

## Solution

Two cerroks alternately write down natural numbers on the board that do not exceed p. The rules of the cerrok prohibit writing divisors of already written numbers on the board. Produces a cerrok that cannot make another move.
a) Find out which of the cerroks has a winning strategy for p=10, and indicate it.
b) Find out which of the cerroks has a winning strategy for p=1000.
