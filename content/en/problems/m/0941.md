## Condition

Given a regular (4k+2)-gon $A_0A_1$ ... $A_{4k+1}$ with center $O$. Prove that the sum of the segments cut by the angle $A_kOA_{k+1}$ on the lines $A_1A_2k$, $A_2A_2k-1$, ..., $A_kA_{k+1}$ (see figure 1 for $k=2$) is equal to the radius $OA_0$ of the circumcircle of a (4k+2)-gon, if $k=2$, 6) $k$ — any natural number.
