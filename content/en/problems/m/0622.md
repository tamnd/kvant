## Condition

Prove that the number of solutions to the equation
$$x^3 + y^2 = z^3 + t^2 + 1$$
in natural numbers, not exceeding $10^6$, less than the number of solutions to the equation
$$x^3 + y^2 = z^3 + t^2$$
in natural numbers, not exceeding $10^6$.
