## Condition

On a spherical planet with equator length 1, they plan to lay N ring roads, each of which will go along a circle of length 1. Then several trains will be launched along each road. All trains will travel along the roads at the same positive constant speed, never stopping or colliding. (Consider the trains as arcs of zero thickness, from which the end points are ejected.) Under such conditions, what is the maximum possible total length of all trains: a) for N = 3; b) at N = 4? c*) Can the total length of trains be greater than 1000 for some N? A. Berdnikov
