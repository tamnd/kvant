## Condition

Given an acute-angled isosceles triangle ABC, in which point H is the point of intersection of the altitudes, points I and O are the centers of the inscribed and circumscribed circles. It is known that points A, O, I, H lie on the same circle $\omega$. Prove that the circle $\omega$ passes through one of the vertices B and C. P. Kozhevnikov
