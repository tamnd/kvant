## Condition

For a convex polyhedron M, let us denote by S(M) the sum of the areas of its faces, and by P(M) the sum of the products of the lengths of all its edges and the corresponding external angles of the polyhedron (the external angle for a given edge is the angle between the perpendiculars to the edges adjacent to the edge and directed to the external region of the polyhedron; it is equal to 180° minus the value of the corresponding edge angle). Prove that the polyhedron M₁ lies inside the polyhedron M₂, then
a) S(M₁) ≤ S(M₂);
b) P(M₁) ≤ P(M₂).
A. V. Goncharov
