## Condition

In the tetrahedron there are $ABCD$ ($AC \perp BC$) and $(AD) \perp (BD)$. Prove that the cosine of the angle between lines $AC$ and $BD$ is less than $|CD|/|AB|$. (10)

Yu. Nesterenko

## Solution

In a tetrahedron ABCD (AC) ⊥ (BC) and (AD) ⊥ (BD). Prove that the cosine of the angle between lines AC and BD is less than |CD|/|AB|.
