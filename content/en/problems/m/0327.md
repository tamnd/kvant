## Condition

There are $N$ people in the company. Each of them likes exactly $k$ people from this company. At what minimum $k$ can we say that there will definitely be two people from this company who like each other?

*A. Kolodin, 10th grade student*
*(Kolomaia)*

## Solution

There are N people in the company. Each of them is edited by exactly k people from this company. For every smallest k, we can say that there will definitely be two people from this company who are different?
