$$\frac{|BL|}{|NC|} = \frac{|LM|}{|CM|}.$$
Let us express the lengths of the segments in this equality through $\lambda$, considering the length of the diagonal AC to be equal to 1.
From the similarity of triangles ABL and CFL it follows that $|BL|:|LF| = |AB|:|CF| = 1:2$. Therefore, $|BL| = 1/3$, since $|BF| = |AC| = 1$. Likewise, $|AL| = 1/3$. It is clear that $|CN| = |AM| = \lambda$, $|CM| = 1 - \lambda$, and $|LM| = |AM| - |AL| = \lambda - \frac{1}{3}$. Thus,
$$\frac{1/3}{\lambda} = \frac{\lambda - 1/3}{1 - \lambda},$$
that is, $\lambda^2 = 1/3$ and $\lambda = 1/\sqrt{3}$.

♦ a) Let us denote the left side of the equation by $P(x, y)$. It can be rewritten like this:
$$P(x, y) = x^3 - 3xy^2 + y^3 = (y - x)^3 + 3(y - x)x^2 - x^3 = -y^3 + 3y(x - y)^2 + (x - y)^3.$$
Therefore, $P(x, y) = P(y - x, -x) = P(-y, x - y)$. Therefore, if the pair of integers $(x, y)$ is a solution to our equation, then two more pairs $(y - x, -x)$ and $(-y, x - y)$ also satisfy it. All these pairs are distinct, or otherwise $x = y = 0$, which is impossible with $n \neq 0$.

b) To make sure that the equation $x^3 - 3xy^2 + y^3 = 2891$ has no integer solutions, it is enough to prove that the left-hand side does not, for any integer $x$ and $y$, give remainder 2 when divided by 9, since $2891 = 9 \cdot 321 + 2$.
