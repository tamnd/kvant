## Condition

Given $x$, the value $x^8$ can be found in three arithmetic operations: $x^2 = x \cdot x, x^4 = x^2 \cdot x^2, x^8 = x^4 \cdot x^4$, and $x^{15}$ in five operations: the first three are the same, then $x^8 \cdot x^8 = x^{16}$ and $x^{16} : x = x^{15}$. Prove that
a) $x^{1000}$ can be found in 12 operations (multiplication and division);
b)* for any natural $n$ value $x^n$ can be found in more than $\frac{3}{2} \log_2 n + 1$ steps.

E. G. Belaza
