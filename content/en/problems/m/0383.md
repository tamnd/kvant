## Condition

Let $a$ and $b$ be two natural numbers. Prove that if $ab$ is even, then it will also find the natural numbers $c$ and $d$, which are $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = d^2$, and if $ab$ is odd, then such $c$ and $d$ cannot be found.

M. Gerver

## Solution

Let $a$ and $b$ be two natural numbers. Let us prove that if $ab$ is integer, then we can also find natural numbers $c$ and $d$, that $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = d^2$, and if $ab$ is not integer, then the maxims $c$ and $d$ cannot be found.
