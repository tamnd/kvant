## Condition

The first n volumes of the encyclopedia are on the shelf. You can carry out the following operation with it: take any three adjacent volumes and place them between any two volumes, as well as at the beginning or end of a row, without changing the order of these three volumes. Is it possible, after applying the specified operation several times, to arrange them in ascending order of volume numbers (regardless of the initial arrangement of volumes)?

A. Savin
