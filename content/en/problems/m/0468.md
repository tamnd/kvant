## Condition

Points A, B, C and D of the plane are such that for any point M of this plane the scalar product of the vectors $\vec{MA} \cdot \vec{MB}$ and $\vec{MC} \cdot \vec{MD}$ are not equal to each other. Prove that $\vec{AC} = \vec{DB}$. Is the opposite statement true?

Yu. Ionin
