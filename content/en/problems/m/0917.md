## Solution

a) What is the length of the maximum series of consecutive bad tickets? b) How many such series of maximum length are there? (Ticket numbers are considered to range from 000000 to 999999 inclusive; a ticket is called lucky if the sum of the first three digits of its number is equal to the sum of the last three digits.) a) Answer: 1000. Any ticket number is between two lucky numbers equal to $1000N + N$ and $1000(N + 1) + (N + 1)$ (N and N + 1 are three-digit numbers), in which the first three digits coincide, taking into account the order, from the tray last (for a number equal to 1000A + B, where A and B are three-digit numbers, you need to take N = A if A < B, and N = A - 1 if A ≥ B). Since the difference between these lucky numbers is 1001, the length of the series of unlucky tickets cannot be more than 1000. At the same time, two series of length 1000 - from 000001 to 001000 and from 998999 to 999998, obviously consists of unlucky numbers.
