## Condition

2023 natural numbers are written in a row. The first number is 12, and each number, starting from the third, is either equal to the product of the previous two numbers, or equal to the previous number increased by 4. What is the greatest number of perfect squares among the given 2023 numbers? Based on the British Mathematical Olympiad
