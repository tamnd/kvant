## Condition

The polynomial $x^{10} + x^9 + x^8 + \dots + x^2 + x + 1$ is written. Two people play this game. First, the first one replaces any of the stars with a certain number, then the second one replaces any of the remaining stars with a number, then again replaces one of the stars with a number, and so on (9 moves in total). If the resulting polynomial does not have real roots, then the first player wins, and if there is at least one root, the second player wins. Is it possible for the second player to win with any game of the first?
