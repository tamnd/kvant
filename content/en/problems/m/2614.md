## Condition

In a n u n table, rows can be swapped and columns can also be swapped. Some k cells of the table are marked with asterisks. At what maximum k can it always be ensured that all the asterisks are located on one side of the main diagonal (at the same time, there should be no asterisks on the main diagonal itself)? P. Kozhevnikov
