## Condition

The square $ABCD$ rotates around its stationary center. Find the set that describes the midpoint of the segment $PQ$, where $P$ is the base of the perpendicular dropped from the point $D$ to the fixed line $l$, and $Q$ is the midpoint of the side $AB$.
