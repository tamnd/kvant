## Condition

Let $P$ be a given point inside a given sphere and $A, B, C$ be arbitrary three points of this sphere and let the segments $PA, PB, PC$ be mutually perpendicular. Let $Q$ be the vertex of the parallelepid defined by the segments $PA, PB$ and $PC$, diagonally opposite to $P$. Determine the geometric location of points $Q$.
