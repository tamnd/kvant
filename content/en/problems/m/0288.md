## Condition

At the congress, bring together scientists, among whom there are friends. It turned out that no two scientists who had an equal number of friends at the congress had mutual friends. Prove that there is a scientist who has exactly one friend.

S. Konyazin

## Solution

** *Scientists, including friends, gathered at the congress. It turned out that no two scientists who have an equal number of friends at the congress have mutual friends. Prove that there is a scientist who has exactly one friend.*
