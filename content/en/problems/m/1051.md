## Condition

Two pieces are placed in the lower left corner of a 8x8 square chessboard in the shape of a 3x3 square. A chip can jump over any other chip, reflecting symmetrically from it, if the corresponding field is free. Is it possible to collect all the chips in the form of a 3×3 square with several such moves:
a) in the upper left corner?
b) in the upper right corner of the board?

A. E. Briskin
