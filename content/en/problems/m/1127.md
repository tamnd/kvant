## Condition

The “Cheburashka” microcalculator has an adder that allows you to subtract and find the reciprocal number 1/x from a given number x. Is it possible to use this microcalculator to get a unit if the original number is a) √19 + 88; b) √19 + √88? (It is prohibited to enter numbers into the microcalculator that differ from the original one or from those obtained as a result of calculations on it.)

A. B. Voomolnaya

## Solution

The "Cheburashka" microcalculator can add, subtract and find the reciprocal number $1/X$ from a given number $X$. Is it possible to use this microcalculator to obtain a unit, given the initial number $a)$ $\sqrt{19}+88$; b) $\sqrt{19}+88$; c) $\sqrt{19}+88$? (It is prohibited to enter numbers into the microcalculator that differ from the original ones or those obtained as a result of calculations on it.)

a) Answer: yes. First let's calculate

$$a = \frac{1}{\sqrt{19}+88} = \frac{88-\sqrt{19}}{88^2-19},$$

then we obtain the sequential numbers $88-\sqrt{19}$ (as the sum of $88^2-19$ terms $a$), $176=(88+\sqrt{19})+(88-\sqrt{19})$, $1/176$ and $1$ (the sum of $176$ terms $1/176$).

b) Answer: yes. Let us use the identity

$$\frac{1}{x} - \frac{2}{\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{1}{1/x-y}} = xy^2.$$

Its left side can be calculated as "Cheburashka". Therefore, from the number $x$ one can obtain $x^3$ (with $y=x$), $x^5=x^3 \cdot x^2$, $x^7=x^5 \cdot x^2$, ..., $x^{19}$, i.e. from $\sqrt{19}88$ one can obtain $88$, then $1/88$ and $1$.

b) Answer: no. Applying the permitted operations to numbers of the form $a\sqrt{19}+b\sqrt{88}$ with dissimilar numbers $a$ and $b$, we will again obtain numbers of the same type (in particular, $1/(a\sqrt{19}+b\sqrt{88})=(a\sqrt{19}-b\sqrt{88})/(19a^2-88b^2))$. Therefore, it is impossible to obtain one from the number $\sqrt{19}+88$.
