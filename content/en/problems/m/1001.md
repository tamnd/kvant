## Condition

There are 1001 stones in the pile. She randomly divides herself into two piles, counts the number of stones in them and writes down the product of these two numbers. Then with one of these heaps (which has more than one stone) the same operation is performed: it is divided into two and writes down the product of the numbers of stones in two new educational heaps. Then the same operation is repeated with one of the three resulting heaps, and so on, until all heaps contain one stone. What is the sum of 1000 recorded works?

A. S. Merkuryev
