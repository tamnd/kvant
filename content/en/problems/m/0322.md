## Condition

a) A figure consisting of more than one point is the intersection of N circles. Prove that the boundaries of this figure can be represented as a union of 2N - 2 circular arcs.

b) There are N letters in the alphabet. Several letters are written around a circle so that no letter appears twice in a row and for any two different letters a, b one can carry out

straight line so that all the letters a will be one from the straight line, and the letters b will be on the other (Fig. 1). Prove that no more than 2N - 2 letters are written.

S. V. Fomin

## Solution

a) A figure consisting of more than one point is the intersection of $N$ circles. Prove that the boundary of this figure can be represented as the union of at most $2N-2$ arcs of a circle.

b) In the alphabet $N$ is the letter. Several letters are written in a circle, so that the lower letter does not appear twice in a row, and for any two different letters a, b, a straight line can be drawn, so that all the letters a will be on one side of the straight line, and the letters b will be on the other (Fig. 2). Prove that no more than $2N-2$ letters are written.

Let us show how problem a) is derived from problem b).
