## Condition

Let $C_{n}^{k}$ denote the number of ways to select k items from n different items (ways that differ only in the order of selection of items are considered the same). Prove that if the natural numbers k and l are less than n, then the numbers $C_{n}^{k}$ and $C_{n}^{l}$ have a common divisor greater than 1. Folklore
