## Condition

The city is a checkered rectangle, each cell contains a five-story building. The renovation law allows you to select two adjacent cells on the side in which houses are located, and demolish the house with fewer floors (or the same number). At the same time, as many floors are built over the second house as there were in the demolished house. What is the smallest number of houses that can be left in the city, using the law on renovation, if the city has dimensions: a) 20 s 20 cells; b) 50 s 90 cells? M. Murashkin
