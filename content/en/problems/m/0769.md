## Condition

The bisectors of the triangle $ABC$ intersect at the point $L$, their continuations intersect the circumcircle of the triangle at the points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ (Fig. 2). Let $R$ be the circumradius and $r$ the circumradius of triangle $ABC$. Prove the equalities

a) $\frac{|LA_1| \cdot |LC_1|}{|LB|} = R$; b) $\frac{|LA| \cdot |LC|}{|LB_1|} = r$;

b) $\frac{S_{ABC}}{S_{A_1B_1C_1}} = \frac{2r}{R}$.

R. Mazov
