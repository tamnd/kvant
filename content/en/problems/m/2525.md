## Condition

Given natural numbers m, n, k, a, b, c. The rectangular parallelepiped m ¥ n ¥ k was cut into several “bricks” of three types: a ¥ 1 ¥ 1, 1 ¥ b ¥ 1 and 1 ¥ 1 ¥ c. (The dimensions are given in a given order - length, width, height; for example, 1 ¥ 1 ¥ 2 and 1 ¥ 2 ¥ 1 are different types of bricks.) Prove that at least one of the following statements is true: m is divisible by a; n is divisible by b; k is divisible by c. T. Zimanov (Kazakhstan) through C and perpendicular to line AC intersects the line passing through A and parallel to line BC at point P. Prove that points B, M and P lie on the same line. D. Pashutkin
