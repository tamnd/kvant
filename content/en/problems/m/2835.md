## Condition

In each square of the board 2 u 200 there is a ruble coin. Dasha and Sonya play, taking turns making moves, Dasha starts. In one move, you can select any coin and move it: Dasha moves the coin to the diagonally adjacent cell, Sonya - to the adjacent one on the side. If two coins end up in the same cell, one of them is immediately removed from the board and goes to Sonya. Sonya can stop the game at any time and take all the coins received. What is the biggest win she can get, no matter how Dasha plays? A. Kuznetsov
