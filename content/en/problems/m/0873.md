## Condition

The teacher wrote the square trinomial $x^2+10x+20$ on the board. Then each student in turn increased or decreased by one one of their choice of one of the lower coefficients (the coefficient of $x$ or the free term), but not both at once. The result is the trinomial $x^2+20x+10$. Is it true that at some points a quadratic trinomial with whole roots was written on the board?

A. A. Berzins
