## Condition

Let's call a number balanced if in its decimal notation a certain beginning coincides with a certain end (for example, the numbers 1971, 19219 are balanced, but the numbers 1415145 are not.) Prove that there is a number that, after adding any of the 10 digits to its right, becomes balanced.

G. Gurewitz
