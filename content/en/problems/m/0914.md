## Condition

On the island of Seroburomalin there are 13 gray, 15 brown and 17 crimson chameleons. If two chameleons of different colors meet, they simultaneously change their color to a third (gray and brown both become crimson, etc.). Is it possible to obey in such a way that after a while all the chameleons will be the same color?

V. G. Ilchev
