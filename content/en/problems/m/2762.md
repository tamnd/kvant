## Condition

The sum of n 100 non-zero real numbers (not necessarily distinct) is equal to zero. For each of the ways to select several (at least one) of these numbers, the sum of the selected numbers was calculated and all the resulting sums were written down in a line in non-increasing order. The first number in the line is S. Find the smallest possible value of the second number in the line. A. Golovanov
