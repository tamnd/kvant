## Condition

Prove that if $a, b, c, d, x, y, u, v$ are real numbers and $abcd > 0$, then $(ax+bu)(av+by)(cx+dv)(cu+dy) \geqslant (acuvx+bcuxy+advxy+bduvy) \times (acx+bcu+adv+bdy)$.

V. P. Fedomov

## Solution

Prove that if $a, b, c, d, x, y, u, v$ are real numbers $u abcd > 0$, then $(ax + bu)(av + by)(cx + dv) \times (cu + dy) \geq (acuvx + bcuxy + advxy + bduvy) \times (acx + bcu + adv + bdy)$.
