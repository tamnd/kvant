## Condition

Two quantities travel along two intersecting circles. Each one travels around its own circle at a constant speed. Having simultaneously left one of their intersection points and made one revolution each, the quantities met again at this point. Prove that on the plane in which the circles lie, there is a fixed point such that the distances from which to the magnitude met are the same all the time if they travel:
a) in one direction (counterclockwise);
b) in different directions.

N. Vasiliev, I. Sharien

$^*) a^{bc}$ means $a^{(bc)}$
