## Condition

The two play the following version of “sea battle”. One player places on the board $n \times n$ a certain number of non-overlapping “ships” $n \times 1$ (perhaps none). The second player simultaneously makes a series of blows on the board squares and receives an answer from the opponent for each square - hit or miss. What minimum number of fields should be struck so that the location of all his ships can be unambiguously determined from the enemy’s responses? Consider three cases: a) $n=4, 6)$ $n=10$, c) $n$ is any natural number.
