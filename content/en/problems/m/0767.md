## Condition

a) The line $l$ divides the area of ​​a convex polygon in half. Prove that the ratio in which this line divides the projection of the polygon into the line perpendicular to it does not exceed $1 + \sqrt{2}$ (Fig. 1, ​​a). b) Each of the three straight lines divides the area of ​​this figure in half. Prove that the area of ​​the part of the figure enclosed in a triangle between the lines does not exceed $1/4$ all plane figures (Fig. 1, b).
