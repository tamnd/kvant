## Condition

Given a stack of 2n+1 cards, the following two operations are allowed:
(A) remove some cards from the top and place them down, maintaining order;
(B) the top n cards are laid out in the n spaces between the bottom n+1 cards, maintaining the order.
Prove that using the indicated operations it is impossible to obtain more than 2n(2n+1) different arrangements of cards from the initial arrangement of cards in the stack.

D. V. Fomin
