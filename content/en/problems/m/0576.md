## Condition

There are several points on the given plane. For some pairs (A; B) of these points, vectors $\vec{AB}$ are taken, and in such a way that the same number of vectors begin at each point as end at it. Prove that the sum of all chosen vectors is equal to $\vec{0}$. (8)

V. Proizvolov

## Solution

On a plane at a given point. For some pairs ($A; B$) of these points, vectors $AB$ are taken, and in such a way that at each point there are as many vectors beginning as there are endings at it. Prove that the sum of all chosen vectors is equal to $\vec{0}$.**

This problem can be solved in multiimplicit ways. Let's give two of them.

First decision. Let us apply induction by the number of vectors. For $n=2$ we have vectors $\vec{AB}$ and $\vec{BC}$, the sum of which is $\vec{0}$. Let the statement be true for $n$ vectors. Distance system of $n+1$ vectors. We will replace it in this system

This means that the entire sum is 0.
