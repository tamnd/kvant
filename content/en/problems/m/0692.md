## Condition

The points $C_1, A_1, B_1$ are taken on the sides, respectively, $AB, BC, CA$ of the triangle $ABC$ so that $|AC_1| : |C_1B| = |BA_1| : |A_1C| = |CB_1| : |B_1A| = 1:3.$

Prove that the perimeter $P$ of triangle $ABC$ and the perimeter $P_1$ of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ are related by the inequalities a) $P_1 < \frac{3}{4}$ P; 6) $P_1 > \frac{1}{2}$ P. (8)
