## Condition

The set of $2n+1$ numbers $a_0, a_1, a_2, ..., a_{2n}$ is such that $a_h \geq \frac{a_{k-1}+a_{k+1}}{2}$ for all $k=1,2, ...$.
..., $2n-1$. Prove the inequality
$$\frac{a_1+a_3+a_5+\dots+a_{2n-1}}{n} \geq \frac{a_0+a_2+a_4+\dots+a_{2n}}{n+1}$$

and find out for which sets it turns into equality.
