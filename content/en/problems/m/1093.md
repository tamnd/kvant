## Condition

On the circle, the numbers 0, 1, 2 are placed at n points. Then, simultaneously at all points, perform the following transformation: each number 2 is replaced by 0, and then 1 is added to the number following it clockwise. First, let the number of twos be equal to k ≥ 1.
a) After how many transformations are completed, not a single two is left?
b) Let, in addition, at the n−k remaining points there were initially ones. Prove that in the end there are k ones and n−k zeros (Fig. 1).
H. Alexandru (Romania)
