## Condition

The coordinates of the vertices of an equilateral triangle are integers. Prove that the square of the base is an even number.

B. V. Prozovolov

## Solution

The coordinates of the vertices of an equilateral triangle are integers. Prove that the square of the base is an even number.

In parallel, intersect the given triangle $ABC$, in which $AB = BC$, so that the vertex $A$ falls at the origin. then the coordinates $(b_1, b_2)$ and $(c_1, c_2)$ of the vertices $B$ and $C$ will remain intact. From the equality $AB^2 = BC^2$ written in coordinates:

$$b_1^2 + b_2^2 = (b_1 - c_1)^2 + (b_2 - c_2)^2,$$

it follows that $AC^2 = c_1^2 + c_2^2 = 2(b_1c_1 + b_2c_2)$ is an even number.

V. V. Proizvolov
