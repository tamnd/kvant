## Condition

On the graph of the function $y x^{2}$ we marked 1000 different points, the abscissas of which are integers from the segment [0; 100000]. Prove that it is possible to choose six different marked points $A, B, C, A c, B c,^{C} c$ such that the areas of triangles ABC and A c B c C c are equal. A. Tereshin
