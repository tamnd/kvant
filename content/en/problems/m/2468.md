## Condition

Each square of the ¥100 board is colored either black or white, with all squares adjacent to the board border being black. It turned out that nowhere on the board there is a single-color checkered square 2 ¥ 2. Prove that there is a checkered square 2 ¥ 2 on the board, the cells of which are colored in a checkerboard pattern. M. Antipov
