## Condition

Initially, several (more than one) natural numbers are written on the board. Then, every minute, a number equal to the sum of the squares of all numbers already written on it is added to the board (so, if the numbers 1, 2, 2 were initially written on the board, then in the first minute the number $1^{2} + 2^{2} + 2^{2} ).$ would be added Prove that the hundredth number added has at least 100 different prime factors. I. Bogdanov, P. Kozhevnikov
