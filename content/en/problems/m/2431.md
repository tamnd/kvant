## Condition

Circle $\omega$ touches the sides of angle BAC at points B and C. Line l intersects segments AB and AC at points K and L, respectively. Circle $\omega$ intersects l at points P and Q. Points S and T are chosen on the segment BC so that KS P AC and LT P AB. Prove that points P, Q, S and T lie on the same circle. I. Bogdanov, P. Kozhevnikov
