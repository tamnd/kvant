## Condition

a) Prove that if there are $3n$ stars in a table of $2n \times 2n$ cells, then you can cross out $n$ rows and $n$ columns so that all the stars are crossed out. b) Prove that in a table of $2n \times 2n$ cells it is possible to place a $3n+1$ asterisk so that after crossing out any $n$ rows and $n$ columns, at least one asterisk remains.

K. P. Kokhas
