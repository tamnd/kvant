## Condition

From a sheet of checkered paper measuring 29 × 29 squares, 99 squares of 2 × 2 each were cut out. Prove that you can cut another square from it.

S. V. Fomin

## Solution

From a sheet of dumpling paper measuring 29 × 29 squares, 99 squares measuring 2 × 2 each were cut out. Prove that you can cut another square from it.

On figure there are 100 2×2 squares shaded in grey. By cutting out any 2x2 square from the original 29x29 square, we “touch” exactly one square. Therefore, after 99 such operations, one of the gray squares will remain untouched.

S. V. Fomin
