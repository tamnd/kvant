## Condition

Let $a$ be a natural number such that $2^a-2$ is divisible by $a$ (for example, $a=3$). Let us define the sequence $(x_n)$ by the conditions
$$x_1=a, x_{k+1}=2^{x_k}-1.$$
Prove that $2^{x_k}-2$ is divisible by $x_k$ for any $k$.
Walter Janous (Jisbruck, Austria)
