## Condition

* a) Let O and R be the center and radius of the circumcircle of triangle ABC, Z and r the center and radius of its incircle, K the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle with the vertices at the points of tangency of the incircle on the side of triangle ABC. Prove that point Z lies on the segment OK, and |OZ|:|ZK|=3R/r.

6) Let a, b, c be the side lengths of triangle ABC, $\vec{n}_a, \vec{n}_b, \vec{n}_c$ be unit vectors perpendicular to the corresponding sides of the triangle and directed outward (Fig. 1). Prove that

$$a^3\vec{n}_a + b^3\vec{n}_b + c^3\vec{n}_c = 12S \cdot M\vec{O},$$

where S is the area of ​​triangle ABC, M is the point of intersection of the medians. O is the center of the circumscribed circle.
