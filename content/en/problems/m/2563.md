## Condition

Pasha and Vova play the next game, taking turns making moves. Pasha begins. Initially, the boys have a large piece of plasticine in front of them. In one move, Pasha can cut any of the available pieces of plasticine into three parts. Vova, on his own, chooses any two pieces and molds them together. Pasha wins if at some point among the available pieces of plasticine there are 100 pieces of the same mass. Can Vova stop Pasha from winning? R. Efremov, D. Belov
