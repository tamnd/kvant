## Condition

From the vertex $B$ of the parallelogram $ABCD$ its heights $BK$ and $BH$ are drawn. The segments $KH=a$ and $BD=b$ are known. Find the distance from point $B$ to the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $BKH$.
