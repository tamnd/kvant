## Condition

Knights, liars and singers live on the island; everyone knows about everyone, which one is which. They lined up all 2018 inhabitants of the island and asked everyone to answer “Yes” or “No” to the question: “On the island there are more knights than liars?” The residents answered in turn and so that others could hear them. The knights answered the truth, the liars lied. Each singer answered in the same way as most of those who answered before him, and if there were equal numbers of “Yes” and “No” answers, he gave any of these answers. It turned out that there were exactly 1009 “Yes” answers. What was the largest number of people who sang along among the island’s inhabitants? M.Kuznetsov
