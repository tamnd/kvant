## Condition

Prove that for any polynomial P(x) with integer coefficients it is impossible to find such distinct integers x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ (n ≥ 3) for which the equalities P(x₁)=x₂, P(x₂)=x₃, ..., P(xₙ-1)=xₙ, P(xₙ)=x₁ would hold.
