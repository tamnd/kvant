## Condition

There are several children and a pile of 1000 candies in the room. Children take turns approaching the pile. Each person who comes up divides the number of candies in the pile by the number of children in the room, rounds up (if it is not a whole number), takes the resulting number of candies and leaves the room. In this case, boys round up, and girls round down. Prove that the total amount of candy the boys have when everyone leaves the room does not depend on the order of the children in the queue. M. Didin
