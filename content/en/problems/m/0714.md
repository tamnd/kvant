## Condition

N friends learned N news at the same time, and each learned one piece of news. They began calling each other and exchanging news. You can convey as much news as you like in one conversation. What is the minimum number of calls required for everyone to know all the news? Consider three cases: a) N=64, 6) N=55, b) N=100.
