## Condition

For every natural number n ≥ 3, indicate the smallest k such that any n points in the plane, no three of which lie on the same line, can be separated by k straight lines. (I will divide these points by lines if for any two of these points there is a line from which they lie on opposite sides.)

N. Vasiliev, A. Egorov

## Solution

For every natural number n ≥ 3 indicates the smallest k such that any n points in the plane, no three of which lie on a given line, can be separated by k lines.

Many of those who sent us a solution to this problem remembered this fact (it is not difficult to prove using induction):

k straight lines divide the plane into no more than $\frac{k(k+1)}{2} + 1$ parts (for k straight lines in general position there will be exactly $\frac{k(k+1)}{2} + 1$).
