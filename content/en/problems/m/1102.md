## Condition

Prove that there are n different natural numbers whose sum of cubes is equal to the cube of a natural number if a) n=3, 6) n=4, c) n is any natural number greater than 2.

L. D. Kurlyandchik

## Solution

Prove that there are puzzle natural numbers whose sum of cubes is equal to the cube of a natural number if a) n=3, b) n=4, c) n is any natural number greater than 2.

Here are examples of sets of three and four cubes of various natural numbers, the sum of which is a cube:
a) $3^3+4^3+5^3=6^3$,
b) $1^3+5^3+7^3+12^3=13^3$.

We can build sets of a large number of cubes by “building up”: by multiplying all the cubes in equality a) by $2^3$ (or in equality b) by $6^3$), we obtain an equality of cubes, the smallest of which is $6^3$; it can be replaced with $3^3+4^3+5^3$. Thus, from a) we obtain the equalities
$$3^3+4^3+5^3+8^3+10^3=12^3 \quad (n=5),$$
$$3^3+4^3+5^3+8^3+10^3+16^3+20^3=24^3 \quad (n=7)$$
etc. for all odd n, and from b) -
$$3^3+4^3+5^3+30^3+42^3+72^3=78^3 \quad (n=6),$$
$$3^3+4^3+5^3+8^3+10^3+60^3+84^3+144^3=156^3 \quad (n=8)$$
etc. for all even n.

L. D. Kurlyandchik
