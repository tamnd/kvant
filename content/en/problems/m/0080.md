## Condition

The table $m \times n$ contains arbitrary numbers. It is allowed to simultaneously change the sign of all numbers in one column or all numbers in one row. Prove that by repeating this operation several times, you can obtain a table in which the sum of the numbers in any column and the sum of the numbers in any row are non-negative.

A. S. Schwartz
