## Condition

a) Kolya and Dima play a game on the 8 ¥ 8 board, taking turns making moves, Dima starts. Kolya draws crosses in the cells, and Dima covers pairs of adjacent board cells with 1 ¥ 2 rectangles (dominoes). During his turn, Kolya must place one cross in any empty cell (that is, in a cell in which a cross has not yet been drawn and which has not yet been covered with a domino). During his turn, Dima must cover with a domino two adjacent cells (not yet covered by other dominoes), in which the total number of crosses is even (0 or 2). The one who cannot make a move loses. Which player has a winning strategy? b) The same game and the same question, but, unlike point a), Kolya makes the first move. M. Didin
