## Condition

Three natural numbers x, y, z are written on the board. Petya writes down the product of any two of these numbers on a piece of paper, and on the board he reduces the third number by 1. With the new three numbers on the board, he again performs the same operation, etc. until one of the numbers on the board becomes zero. What will be the sum of the numbers on the sheet at this moment? E. Gorsky, S. Dorichenko
