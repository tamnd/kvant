## Condition

Let us denote by $S_n$ the sum of the first $n$ primes: $S_1 = 2, S_2 = 2 + 3 = 5, S_3 = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10, S_4 = 17$, etc. Prove that for any $n$ between $S_n$ and $S_{n+1}$ an exact square occurs.

I. Zhuk
