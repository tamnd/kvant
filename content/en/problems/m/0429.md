## Condition

a) How many solutions does the equation have?
$$|x| - 1977 \{x\} = 1978?$$
(Here $|x|$ is the integer part of $x$, and $\{x\} = x - |x|$.)

b) Prove that for any $p \neq 0$ and $q$ the equation
$$|x| + p\{x\} = q$$
has a $||p||$ or $||p|| + 1$ solution.

Zh. Satarov
