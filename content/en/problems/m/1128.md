## Condition

Several pieces are placed on the chessboard. In one move, one of the chips moves to an adjacent (horizontally or vertically) free field. After several moves, it turned out that each chip hit all the fields exactly once and returned to the original field. Prove that there was a moment when not a single piece was on its original square.

E. B. Abakumov
