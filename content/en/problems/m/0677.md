## Condition

Inside the acute triangle $ABC$, point $M$ is chosen, which is
a) the point of intersection of the medians;
b) the point of intersection of the bisectors;
b) the point of intersection of heights.
Prove that if the radii of the circles written in triangle $AMB, BMC, AMC$ are equal,
then the triangle $ABC$ is right.

E. Turkevich
