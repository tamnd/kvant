## Condition

a) Triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ was obtained from triangle $ABC$ by rotating around the center of the circumcircle by some angle less than 180°. Prove that the intersection points of the corresponding lines: $(AB)$ and $(A_1B_1)$, $(BC)$ and $(B_1C_1)$, $(CA)$ and $(C_1A_1)$, are the vertices of a triangle similar to triangle $ABC$.

6) The quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ was obtained from the quadrilateral $ABCD$ circumscribed in a circle with center $O$, rotated around the center $O$ by some angle less than 180°. Prove that the intersection points of the corresponding lines: $(AB)$ and $(A_1B_1)$, $(BC)$ and $(B_1C_1)$, $(CD)$ and $(C_1D_1)$, $(DA)$ and $(D_1A_1)$ are the vertices of the parallelogram. Many readers have added solutions to these problems based on considering inscribed angles. Such decisions, as a rule, did not contain an analysis of the case in which it is not the corresponding parties that intersect, but their continuations. Let us present a simpler and more general solution that uses geometric transformations.
