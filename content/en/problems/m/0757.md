## Condition

From the sequence 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, ... it is not difficult to isolate an arithmetic progression of length three: 1/2, 1/3, 1/6. Is it possible to select an arithmetic progression from this sequence?
a) length 4?
b) length 5?
c) length k, where k is any natural number?

G. Galperin
