## Condition

a)*The scatterbrain mathematician, having entered the three-digit code of his entrance, presses the buttons (with the numbers 0,1,...,9) one per second. The door opens if three digits of the code are dialed in a row in the required order. The mathematician is confident that even in the event of “extreme bad luck” (if the desired combination is found last), he will be able to enter the entrance no later than in 16 minutes. 42 sec (1002 sec). Is he right? How should he act to get home in time?

Answer the last question if 6) only buttons with numbers 1, 2, 3 are working (other numbers are not included in the code);
c) *all buttons are working, but the mathematician remembers that all three digits of the code are different.
