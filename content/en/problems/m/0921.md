## Condition

In a convex quadrilateral ABCD, the values ​​of two angles $\angle A=\alpha$ and $\angle B=\beta$ are known, and its double area is $AB\cdot CD+BC\cdot AD$. Find the ratio of the lengths of all its sides $AB:BC:CD:DA$ if
a) $\alpha=5\pi/12$, $\beta=7\pi/12$; 6) $\alpha=\pi/2$, $\beta=\pi/3$*;
b) Find out for which $\alpha$ and $\beta$ such a quadrilateral exists, and express the ratio of its side in terms of $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
