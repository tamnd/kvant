## Condition

a) From the point $M$ inside the rectangle $ABCD$ of area $S$, bisectors $ME, MF, MG, MH$ of triangles $AMB, BMC, CMD, DMA$ are drawn. Prove that the area $S_0$ of the quadrilateral $EFGH$ satisfies the following inequalities:
$$\frac{3}{8}S < S_0 \leq \frac{1}{2}S.$$
b) For such points $M$ does the equality $S_0 = S/2$ hold?
