## Condition

The owner took out a piece of meat from the refrigerator, and the kittens gathered around her. Once a minute, the housewife cuts off a piece of meat and feeds it to one of the kittens (of her choice), and each piece should be the same proportion of the piece from which it is cut. After some time, the housewife puts the rest of the meat in the refrigerator. Can the housewife feed the kittens equal amounts of meat if there are a) two kittens in total; b) three; c*) four? A. Kushnir, V. Retinsky
