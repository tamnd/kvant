## Condition

Find the sequence $(a_n)$ defined by the conditions $a_1 = 1$,
$$1 + \sum_{d} (-1)^{n/d} a_d = 0,$$
where the sum $\sum$ is taken over all divisors $d$ of $n$ (including $d = 1$ and $d = n$).

For example, if $n = p$ is a prime number, then (*) takes the form $1 + (-1)^{p/1} a_1 + (-1)^{p/p} a_p = 0$, hence $a_p = 2$ if $p = 2$, and $a_p = 0$ if $p > 2$.

V. Abramovich
