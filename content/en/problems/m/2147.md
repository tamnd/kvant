## Condition

Each cell of an infinite checkered plane contains a real number in such a way that all horizontal and vertical sequences of numbers that are infinite in both directions are periodic. Prove that there are infinitely many horizontal sequences with different shortest periods if and only if there are infinitely many vertical sequences with different shortest periods. E.Sign
