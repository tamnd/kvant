## Condition

A $n$-gon is circumscribed around a circle. An arbitrary point $P$ inside the circle is connected to its entire vertices and points of tangency. The resulting $2n$ triangles are colored alternately red and blue. Prove that the product of the areas of the triangles is equal to the product of the areas of the blue triangles.

U. Alla

## Solution

A p-wall is described around the circle. An arbitrary point $P$ inside a circle is connected to all its vertices and points of tangency. The resulting 2n triangles are surrounded by a variable in red and blue. Prove that the product of the areas of the red triangles is equal to the product of the areas of the blue triangles.
