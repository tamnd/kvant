## Condition

Given are three parallel segments $AA_1, BB_1, CC_1$ that do not lie in the same plane. Let $M$ be the intersection point of planes $ABC_1, BCA_1$ and $CAB_1$, and $M_1$ be the intersection point of planes $A_1B_1C, B_1C_1A$ and $C_1A_1B$. Prove that the segment $MM_1$ is parallel to three vertices.

Z. Skopets
