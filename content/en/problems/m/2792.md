## Condition

There are 9 vertical columns in a row. In some places, horizontal sticks are inserted between adjacent posts; no two are at the same height. The beetle crawls from bottom to top; meeting a stick, he crawls along it to the next post and continues to crawl up. It is known that if a beetle starts at the bottom of the first (leftmost) column, then it will finish its path at the ninth (rightmost) column. Is it always possible to remove one stick so that the beetle at the end of the path ends up at the top of the fifth column?
