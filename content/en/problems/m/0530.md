## Condition

On a rectangular sheet of checkered paper, some of the squares are painted black. Then comes the simultaneous repainting of all the cells of the sheet according to the following rule: a cell with an even number of black neighbors becomes white, and a cell with an odd number of black neighbors becomes black. (Neighbors are cells that have a common side.)
a) Prove that if the set $B$ of black cells does not change during repainting (Fig. 2, a) then $B$ has an even number of cells.
b) Suppose that during recoloring the set $B_1$ of black cells goes into $B_2$, $B_2 \leftarrow B_3$, ..., $B_{r-1} \leftarrow B_r$, and $B_r \leftarrow$ again into $B_1$. Prove that the total number of black cells in the sets $B_1$, $B_2$, ..., $B_r$ is even.

R. Izmailov, 10th grade student
