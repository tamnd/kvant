## Condition

Given a prime number $p>2$. For each $k$ from 1 to $p-1$ we denote by $a_k$ the remainder when dividing the number $k^p$ by $p^2$. Prove that
$$a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots + a_{p-1} = (p^3 - p^2)/2.$$
