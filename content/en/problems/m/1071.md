## Condition

A field for playing “numbers” is drawn on the board: (((((((((__*_)*_)*_)*_)*_)*_)*_)*_)*_)*

Two players take turns. The first player, with the initial move, writes down some number in place of the first (self-made) space (___). Each further move consists of writing a number in place of the next space and replacing the asterisk (*) on the left with an addition or multiplication sign; however, no number should appear twice. At the end of the game, the value of the resulting expression is calculated. If it is an even number, then the first player wins, if it is an odd number, the second player wins. Who wins the seasoned game?

S. A. Genkin
