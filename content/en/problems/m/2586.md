## Condition

Given a polygon in which every two adjacent sides are perpendicular. Let's call two of its vertices not friendly if the bisectors of the polygon emerging from these vertices are perpendicular. Prove that for any vertex the number of vertices that are not friendly with it is even. M. Skopenkov, A. Zaslavsky
