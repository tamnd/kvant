## Condition

Let $k$ and $n$ be natural numbers, $k \leqslant n$. Let us say that the set $k$ of positive numbers $a_1,a_2,...,a_k$ is less than 1 only if for any partition $n=n_1+n_2+\dots+n_k$ of the number $n$ into non-negative integer terms at least one of the numbers $a_i n_i$ is an integer ($i=1,2,...,n$).

a) For which $k$ and $n$ are there exclusive sets?

b) What are these sets?

E. A. Gorin
