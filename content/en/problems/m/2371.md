## Condition

a) Let us call the center of a three-cell corner the point belonging to all three of its cells. The chessboard $99 \times 99$ was completely covered with three-square corners without overlaps, after which each corner was rotated around its center by $90^\circ$ (in either direction). Prove that in this case some square of the board turned out to be uncovered by a single corner. b) Solve the same problem if each corner can be rotated around its center by $90^\circ$ or $180^\circ$. A. Gribalko
