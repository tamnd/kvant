## Condition

Given a triple of numbers $2, \sqrt{2}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. It is allowed to replace any two of them with two of these: their sum divided by $\sqrt{2}$, and their difference, also divided by $\sqrt{2}$. Is it possible, after doing this procedure several times, to obtain a triple of numbers $1, \sqrt{2}, 1+\sqrt{2}$? (9)
