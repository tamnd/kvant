## Condition

The numbers 1, 2, ..., 2n—1, 2n are divided into two groups of $n$ numbers in each. Let $a_1<a_2<...<a_n$ be the numbers of the first group in ascending order, $b_1>b_2>...>b_n$ be the numbers of the second group in descending order. Prove that $|a_1-b_1|+|a_2-b_2|+...+|a_n-b_n|=n^2$.

V. V. Proizvolov
