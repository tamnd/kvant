## Condition

Hollow elastic $шарик^{А}$ has wall mass m (the walls are the same thickness everywhere). A thin pad of polyurethane foam (a material of very low density) separates the inner walls of the cavity $шарика^{А}$ from the second $шарика^{В}$ located inside this cavity with mass m 2. This “combined” ball falls from a height h (without initial speed) onto a rigid horizontal surface. The impact of one $шарика^{А}$ with this surface is absolutely elastic. To what height will the “combined” ball fly up after the impact?
