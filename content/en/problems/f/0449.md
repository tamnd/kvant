## Condition

The duration of contact between two colliding balls can be determined by varying the decrease in the charge of the capacitor when the resistance connected to the balls is instantly shorted. What precautions should be taken when performing this experiment? Let $C=0,1$ μF, $R=10\,000$ ohm, the galvanometer rejection after an instantaneous leak is 10.11 cm, without leakage - 11.94 cm. Find time for so long.
