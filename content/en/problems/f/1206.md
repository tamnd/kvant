## Condition

The two long and wide bands are uniformly charged with charge densities of +σ (upper) and –σ (lower). Find the magnitude and direction of the electric field at point M, which is located at a height h above the edge of the stripes on an axis lying in their plane of symmetry (Fig. 3). The distance between the stripes d is small compared to h.

A. I. Semenov
