## Condition

On a smooth horizontal table there is a stack of paper containing 500 sheets. The hundredth leaf (counting gray) is larger than the others. This sheet is carefully pulled in a horizontal direction. What maximum acceleration can be imparted to the pack so that it moves without falling apart? What is the maximum acceleration with which the center of mass of the pack can move? With what acceleration should the hundredth sheet move? The friction coefficient of paper on paper is $\mu=0,2$.

Z. Rafailov
