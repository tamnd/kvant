## Condition

Two packages with identical initial speeds, directed horizontally, simultaneously launch from the south and north poles of the Earth. After a time τ = 3 hours 20 minutes, the packets were at their maximum distance from each other. Determine the maximum distance between packages. The acceleration of free fall on Earth is considered known. The radius of the Earth is $R_3 = 6400$ km. (10)
