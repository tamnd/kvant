## Condition

The drive wheel of a bicycle dynamo is pressed against the side of the bicycle wheel tire. The diameter of this drive wheel is d. The armature of the dynamo, containing N turns each with an area S, rotates in a uniform magnetic field with induction B. At what speed v must a cyclist ride so that the light bulb in the bicycle headlight, designed for the effective value of the voltage U, glows with normal incandescence when the dynamo is operating? The resistances of the wires of the rotor and the light bulb (at the rated voltage across it) are the same. V.Plis
