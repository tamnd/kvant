## Condition

A ball of radius R can rotate freely on a fixed horizontal axis OO′. Under the ball, pressing against it, a rough, rigid tape moves, bent in the form of a half-cylinder with radius R (Fig. 1). The belt speed v is directed horizontally and perpendicular to the OO′ axis. What is the set rotation speed of the ball?

L. G. Markovich
