## Condition

An Earth satellite moves in a circular orbit at an altitude of $h = 760$ km above the Earth's surface. They want to transfer it to an elliptical orbit with a maximum distance from the Earth’s surface of $H = 40 000$ km and a minimum distance from the surface of $h = 760$ km. How much do you need to change the satellite's speed for this? What will be the orbital period of the satellite in its new elliptical orbit?
A. Borzhievsky, 10th grade student (Orishinskaya s.sh., Khmelnitsky region)
