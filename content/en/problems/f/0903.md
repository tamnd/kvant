## Condition

On the upper generatrix of the main cylinder of radius $R$, the axis of which is inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon, a flexible weightless rope of length $l$ (Fig. 1) is fixed. A small weight is attached to the other end of the rope. Define:
1) the length of the hanging part of the rope in the load equilibrium position;
2) the period of small vibrations of the load in a vertical plane parallel to the cylinder axis.

S. S. Krotov
