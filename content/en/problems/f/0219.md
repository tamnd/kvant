## Condition

A vertical cylinder contains $n$ moles of an ideal monatomic gas. The cylinder is closed on top by a piston of mass $M$ and area $S$. Initially, the piston is held motionless, the gas in the cylinder occupies a volume of $V_0$ and has a temperature of $T_0$. The piston was then released, and after a few vibrations it came to rest. Neglecting in the calculations all forces of friction, as well as the heat capacity of the piston and cylinder, find the temperature and volume of the gas at the new position of the piston.

The entire system is thermally ash-lined. Atmospheric pressure is $P_a$.
