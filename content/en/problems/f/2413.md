## Condition

On New Year's Eve in calm weather, an airplane takes off from the airfield of St. Petersburg $( 60\infty$ northern latitude and flies at a constant altitude h = 5 km with a constant speed v = 1000 km/h, always heading northeast (according to the stars). With what acceleration relative to the Earth (Ptolemaic system) does the plane move exactly after T = 4 hours of flight? The Earth can be considered a ball with radius R = 6400 km. V. Chkalov
