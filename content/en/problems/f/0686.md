## Condition

A thin hoop of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is placed on a horizontal plane. Along a smooth channel passing inside the hoop, a small body of mass $m$ comes together from the top point without an initial velocity. Determine the speed of the center of the hoop at the moment when the body is at the hoop point $A$, the radius vector of which forms an angle φ with vertical. At the initial moment the hoop is at rest. There is no friction between the hoop and the plane.

S. Krotov
