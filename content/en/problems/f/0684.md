## Condition

The circuit has $N$ input terminals, one output terminal and one common (“ground”). At each of the input terminals, potential grounds range from +50 to +200 V.
1) Draw a variation of the circuit that will provide the maximum potential applied to the inputs at the output terminal.
2) Draw another variant that provides the minimum applied potential at the output terminal.
Try to do without the use of additional power sources.

A. Zilberman
