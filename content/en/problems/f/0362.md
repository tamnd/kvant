## Condition

A layer capacitor consists of three metal parallel plates of area $S$. The spaces between the plates are filled with dielectrics with dielectric constants $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ and resistivities $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$. The thickness of the dielectric layer is $d_1$ and $d_2$. The capacitor is under constant voltage $U$. Determine the charge of the middle plate at a steady current in the circuit.
