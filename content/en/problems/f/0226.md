## Condition

An independently excited DC electric motor (with a permanent magnet) lifts the load at a speed v₁ using a thread wound around the motor axis. In the absence of a load, the weightless thread rises with speed v₀. At what speed will the same load be lowered if a short circuit occurs in the armature circuit, as a result of which the armature winding becomes short-circuited? We have a complaint about the bearings. (10 grades)
