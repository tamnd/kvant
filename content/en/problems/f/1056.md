## Condition

In the circuit shown in figure 3, the key K₃ is first closed, and the keys K₁ and K₂ are open. At some point in time the key K₁ is closed, and the empty time t₁=0.1 s is closed 吻 K₂. After another time t₂=0.2 s, 吻 K₃ is opened. Find: 1) maximum voltage across the capacitor; 2) current through the coil L₁ after time t₃=1 s after closing 吻 K₁. Neglect the resistance of the wires, consider the diode ideal; L₁=1 H, L₂=0.5 H, C=10 µF, U₀=10 V.

A. R. Zilberman

## Solution

In the circuit shown in figure 1, the key K₃ is first closed, and the key K₁ and K₂ are open. At some point in time the key K₁ is closed, and after a time t₁ = 0.1 s the key K₂ is closed. After another time t₂ = 0.2 s, the key K₃ is closed. Starts: 1) maximum voltage on the capacitor; 2) current through the coil L₁ after a time t₃ = 1 s after the switch K₁ is closed. As for the resistance of the wires, the diode is considered ideal; L₁ = 1 H, L₂ = 0.5 H, C = 10 µF, U₀ = 10 V.
