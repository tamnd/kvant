## Condition

The rigid rectangular structure $AOB$ ($AOB = 90^\circ$) rotates around the vertical axis $OO'$, so that $AOO' = \alpha$ (Fig. 2). Using wheels that can slide freely on the sides $AO$ and $OB$, a lightweight rod $KE$ of length $2a$ is placed on the structure. A small massive ball is attached to the middle of the rod. At what angular speed of rotation of the structure will the rod take a horizontal position?

S. Krotov
