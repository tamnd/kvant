## Condition

Two thin positive lenses with focal lengths $F_1$ and $F_2$ are located on the same axis. Using this lens system, an image of an object was obtained, and it turned out that the size of the image does not depend on the distance from the object to the lens system. Find the distance $l$ between the lenses.

E. P. Kuznetsov
