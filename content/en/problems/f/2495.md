The tube is turned to $90\infty$ with the open end up, the liquid does not flow down and the temperature is maintained constant. Atmospheric pressure $p = R gH_{0}$, where $H_{0} > L$. 1) Find the length of the air column in the horizontal tube. 2) How many gas molecules are in the tube? 3) Find the air pressure in the tube in a vertical position. 4) Find the length of the air column in the vertical tube. 5) Find the maximum length of the liquid column that can be obtained after adding liquid to the vertical tube.
