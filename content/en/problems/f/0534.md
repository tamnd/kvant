## Condition

Skylab astronauts, using a special radar altimeter, discovered that the ocean surface in the Bermuda Triangle area was 25 meters below the normal level (see, for example, the journal Science and Life, 1976, No. 9). Assuming that this “deflection” can be explained by the presence of a spherical cavity filled with water under the ocean house, estimate the radius of this cavity. The depth of the ocean is $h = 6$ km, the average density of terrestrial rocks is $\rho = 3 \times 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

A. Stasenko
