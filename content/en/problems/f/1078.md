## Condition

A long thin thread with a weight at the end is thrown over a pulley and tied to the bow of a model boat that can drink down a long straight channel of water (Fig. 1). The boat and load are released, and the speed of the boat (in cm/s) is recorded every second. Unfortunately, only the end of this entry has survived:

5,65; 6,44; 6,96; 7,31; 7,54; 7,70; 7,80; 7,86; 7,91.

Using these data, determine the speed of the boat 0.1 seconds after it was released.

A. A. Sheronov
