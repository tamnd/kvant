## Condition

A wheel of radius $R$, located at a height $H$ above the ground, rotates with an angular velocity $\omega$. A drop falls from the wheel and falls to the ground at point $B$ under the center of the wheel (Fig. 2.). Find the time the drop falls and the point $A$ of the wheel at which the drop breaks off.
