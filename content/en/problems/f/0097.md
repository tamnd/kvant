## Condition

A coil of insulated wire is bent in the shape of a figure eight, the bulb of which has a radius of $r_1 = 1$ cm and $r_2 = 3$ cm. The coil is in a magnetic field with induction $B = 10^4$ ec, perpendicular to the plane of the coil.

The wire insulation is designed for a voltage of 10 e. Will insulation breakdown occur if the magnetic field is suddenly turned off? Field switch-off time $10^{-3}$ sec.

L. G. Aslamaov
