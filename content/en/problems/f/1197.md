## Condition

When studying the electrical properties of a thin plate of the new compound, it was discovered that its conductivity is significantly anisotropic: along one direction ($X$ axis) it is maximum, and along the perpendicular direction ($Y$ axis) it is minimal. A sample was cut out of the plate in the form of a strip of length $L$ and width $d$ ($L \gg d$) at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the axes $X$ and $Y$ and a source with voltage $U_0$ (Fig. 4) was connected to the ends of the strip. By measuring the voltage between the edges of the strip in the transverse direction, we obtained the value $U$ ($U \ll U_0$). Determine the conductivity ratio along the $X$ and $Y$ axes.

A. I. Buzdin
