## Condition

To compare the thermal conductivity of different materials, it was proposed to use the following methods. Two identical cylinders made of the materials under study (Fig. 2) are placed on a hot plate. A piece of water is placed on the cylinders. Where the wax begins to melt most quickly is where the cylinder has the best thermal conductivity. Is this method correct?
