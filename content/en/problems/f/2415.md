## Condition

A long, L = 1 m, rectangular block of square cross-section a ¥ a = 10 ¥ 10 cm has a density of $\rho = 500$ kg $м^{3}$. The block was lowered into the water of the lake and held in such an unstable equilibrium position that one of the long edges of the block was dry and horizontal, while half the volume of the block was immersed in water. The block was released, and it took a stable position, rotating around the axis of symmetry at an angle $\alpha = 45 \infty$. How much has the potential energy of the “water-block” system decreased? S. Dmitriev
