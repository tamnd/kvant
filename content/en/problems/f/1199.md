## Condition

It is known from experience that the speed of waves on the ocean surface, the length of which is $\lambda = 10$ m, is equal to $v = 4$ m/s. Suppose that in the ocean at great depth there is an interface, above which there is less salty water, and below which there is more salty water, so that the difference in the density of water is $\Delta \rho = 1$ kg/m$^3$. There may be waves along this boundary (so-called internal waves). Find the speed of such waves with a length of $\lambda = 10$ m. Consider the amplitude of the waves to be small.
