## Condition

The side wall of a cubic thin-walled aquarium (a u a u a) was broken. They decided to press the new wall (a u a) with spring clips. The first spring with stiffness 2k was fixed at the middle of the upper rib of the new wall, and the second spring with stiffness k was fixed at the middle of the lower rib. After this, the aquarium was moved towards the vertical wall (Fig.2). All surfaces where the new wall comes into contact with the aquarium are flat and smooth. The new wall is rigid, completely covers the adjacent side walls and bottom and is parallel to the plane of the wall. The friction force at the point of contact of the wall with the table is negligible.
