## Condition

On top of a smooth horizontal log of radius $R$, place a “little book” made up of two homogeneous thin square plates from side $l=4R$. The plates are fastened together using a weightless hinge rod. What angle do the plates form in the equilibrium position (Fig. 2)? Will this equilibrium position be stable?

S. S. Krotov

## Solution

On top of a smooth horizontal beam of radius R is placed a “little book” made up of two homogeneous thin square planes on the side l=4R. The plates are fastened together using a weightless hinged rod. What angle do the plates form at equilibrium? Will this equilibrium be stable?
