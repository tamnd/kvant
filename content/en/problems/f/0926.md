## Condition

In space there are 1985 non-contacting metal balls, the charges of which are equal, respectively, $q, -2q, 3q, -4q, ..., -1984q, 1985q$ ($q > 0$). Prove that among them there is a ball whose surface charge density is non-negative. The distances between the balls are finite.

V. A. Yasinsky
