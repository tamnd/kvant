## Condition

To study the cyclone, which was moving north at an average speed of 20 km/h, two unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) with different flight missions were sent from the same airfield. Each device was supposed to remain at a distance of 20 km horizontally from the center of the cyclone and move at a constant speed relative to the surrounding air of 20 m s at a certain altitude (the same for both devices), to fly around the center of the cyclone. One device is clockwise (when viewed from above), and the second is counterclockwise. At the moment of launch, the distance from the airfield to the center of the cyclone was exactly 20 km. After 15 hours of flight, both UAVs almost simultaneously landed at the same airfield, located north of the airfield from which they took off, and throughout the flight, each strictly followed the flight program stored in the computer memory. How many circles around the center of the cyclone did one of the UAVs make if the second always moved in a straight line relative to the ground and did not make a single circle?

How did the temperature in the vessel on the left change during the same time?
