## Condition

A car of weight $P$, both axles of which are driven, starts moving. The automatic engine operates with constant power $W$, the coefficient of friction between the wheels and the road is $k$. Find the dependence of the automatic speed on time and draw a graph of this dependence. Neglect air resistance and friction in the mechanisms.

A. T. Drozdov
