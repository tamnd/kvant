## Condition

A man “height” $h=20$ cm is drawn on a vertical wall, the man’s head is at a height of $H=2$ m above the floor. Using a lens with a focal length of $F=0,1$ m, an image of a person on the floor is obtained. Find the size of the clearest image.

A. R. Zilberman
