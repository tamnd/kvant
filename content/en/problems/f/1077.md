## Condition

** According to one hypothesis, stars are formed from the interstellar medium (cosmic dust) by rolling under the influence of gravitational force. Estimate the time of formation of a star from a giant spherical cloud of cosmic dust with a density of $\rho=2\cdot10^{-20}$ g/cm$^3$. We can assume that when rolling, the particles do not overtake each other. Gravitational constant $G=6,67\cdot10^{-11}$ N m$^2$/kg$^2$.

V. E. Skorovarov
