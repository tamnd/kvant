## Condition

From point A, located on one bank of a river of width l, a small motor boat (Fig. 2) is transported to the opposite bank. River banks

⟦figure⟧

Fig. 2

parallel to each other. The speed of the river flow is the same everywhere and is equal to u. On the opposite bank of the river, on the same perpendicular to the banks with point A, point B is located. The speed of the boat relative to the water is constant in value and equal to 2u, and its vector during the entire crossing time is perpendicular to straight line BC, where C is the center of the boat. At some point in time $t_{1}$ the speed of the boat relative to the shore is perpendicular to the shore line. 1) In what direction did the boat start moving from point A: with the current or against the current? 2) Find angle ABC at time $t_{1}$. 3) At what distance from point B will the boat be when it reaches the opposite bank of the river? 4) At what distance from point B will the boat be at time $t_{1}$? 5) Find the radius of curvature of the boat’s trajectory in the shore reference system for two cases: a) at the moment of start from point A; b) at time $t_{1}$. A. Shishkina
