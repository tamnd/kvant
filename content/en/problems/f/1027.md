## Condition

The light source is located at a distance of $l=1$ m from the screen. A hole with a diameter of $d=1$ cm is made in the screen by directing the source. How will the flux of light through the hole change if a transparent cylinder made of materials with a refractive index of $n=1,5$ is placed between the screen and the source as shown in figure 4 (cylinder length $l=1$ m, diameter $d=1$ cm; the source is located on the axis of the cylinder)?
