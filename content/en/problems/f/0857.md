## Condition

There is a flat area on the shiny can, from which a beam of light is reflected onto the screen. Cooled liquid is poured into the jar. The reflected signal is noticeably weakened. Explain the phenomenon.

V. G. Dudnikov

## Solution

The shiny can has a flat dent from which a beam of light is reflected onto the screen. Cooled liquid is poured into the jar. The reflected scene is noticeably weakened. Explain the phenomenon.

When cooled liquid was poured into the jar, the temperature of the jar became below the dew point, and small drops of condensed moisture from the surrounding air appeared on the surface of the jar. Now the light is scattered by these droplets, and therefore the reflected signal is noticeably attenuated.

V. G. Dudnikov
