## Condition

Figure 3 is taken with a photograph of the tracks of the part in the cloud chamber. The decay of gas nuclei filling the cloud chamber is caused in this case by the action of fast neutrons on them. The cloud chamber was filled with a mixture of hydrogen (H₂), alcohol vapor (C₂H₅OH) and water (H₂O) and placed in a magnetic field with an induction of 1.3 ml. The magnetic induction vector is directed perpendicular to the plane figure.

1) Determine the energy of the proton that appears at point A. The trajectory of this proton is curve AA′. Why does the curvature of the proton trajectory change? Determine the energy of the proton at point C of its trajectory.

The mass of a proton is 1.67·10⁻²⁷ kcal.

2) Determine which element's nucleus decayed at point A if the tracks of the part starting at this point are identified as tracks of two protons and two α particles. (10 grades)
