## Condition

The model of the ship was pushed and reported a speed of v₀ = 10 m/s. When the model moves, it is acted upon by a resistance force proportional to the speed: F = -kv. a) Find the distance traveled by the model during the time during which its speed was halved. b) Find the path covered by the model before it comes to a complete stop. Consider k = 0.5 ke/s. Model mass m = 0.5 ke. (8 grades)
