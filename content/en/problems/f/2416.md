## Condition

Vasya blows a soap bubble through a long tube. Having inflated the bubble, he releases the tube from his mouth, while the air from the bubble escapes through the tube to the outside. The bubble finally disappears without bursting after time $\tau$. How long will it take for a soap bubble of twice the radius inflated in the same way to deflate? Assume that the air moves through the tube quite slowly, and the properties of the soap film are the same for both bubbles. V. Puzyrev
