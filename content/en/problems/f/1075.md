The center of mass of each component of the cap remains in place (on the axis), therefore the center of mass of the entire system remains in place. But, as is known, the center of mass of a triangular plate is at the intersection point of the medians. Consequently, the center of mass of the cap is located on its axis at a distance $2/3$ $H$ from the top.
