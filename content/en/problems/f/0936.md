## Condition

A current $I_0=0,1$ A flows through a long superconducting solenode with inductance $L_0=0,1$ H, containing $N=200$ turns. From a distance, a closed conducting coil of the same radius as the turns of the solenoid is brought to the solenode; coil inductance $L_1=1\cdot 10^{-3}$ H. The coils are placed between the coils of Solenodia pine with them. How will the current flowing through the solenoid change? How will the energy of the system change?
