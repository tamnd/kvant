## Condition

The rectangular dump truck body is filled with sand. The height of the body is $h=1$ m, its width is $d=3$ m, its length is $l=6$ m. What force acts on the rear side of the dump truck during uniform automatic movement and when it moves with acceleration $a=3$ m/s$^2$? Sand density $\rho=1,5 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, friction coefficient between sand grains $\mu=0,6$.

I. Slovodetsky
