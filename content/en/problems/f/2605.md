## Condition

Two small balls - a light ping-pong ball and a heavy rubber one - are thrown together onto the floor from a height h (the ping-pong ball is on top, the rubber ball is on the bottom; Fig.1). The balls fall to the ground and the rubber ball is reflected from the ground. Assuming that all collisions between the balls are elastic, that there is a small gap between the balls, and that the ping pong ball is much lighter than the $каучуково-_{Рис.} 1$ ball, find to what height the ping pong ball will jump. The dimensions of the balls are much smaller than the height h. Neglect air resistance.
