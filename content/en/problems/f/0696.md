## Condition

A capacitive voltmeter is a flat air capacitor, one of the plates of which is fixed motionless, and the second can move translationally in a direction perpendicular to the plane of the plates. A stiffness spring k (Fig. 3) is attached to the movable plate. A measure of the applied voltage is the change in the gap between the plates. What is the maximum voltage that can be changed with such a device? Area of ​​the plates S, gap between the plates at zero stress d.
