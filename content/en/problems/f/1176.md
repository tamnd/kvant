## Condition

On a weightless rocker arm of length 2L, which can rotate without friction around a vertical axis, charges +Q and –Q of mass M each are attached. Under the rocker arm, along the continuation of the rotation axis, there is a small diopole - charges +q and -q at a distance of 2a from each other (a≤L). At the initial moment, the rocker is in a state of stable equilibrium (Fig. 4).

a) The dipole causes rotation with angular velocity ω. At what ω will the rocker “accompany” the rotation of the diopole?

b) The dipole is motionless. Find the period of the small rocker arms.

A. V. Andrianov

## Solution

Charges +Q and –Q of mass M each are attached to a weightless beam of length 2L, which can rotate without friction around a vertical axis. Under the yoke, along the continuation of the rotation axis, there is a small diopole - charges +q and -q at a distance of 2a from each other (a≪L). At the initial moment, the yoke is in a state of stable equilibrium (Fig. 1).
a) The diopole causes rotation with angular velocity ω. At what ω will the beam “accompany” the rotation of the diopole?
