## Condition

A small particle of mass m, carrying a positive charge q, approaches the capacitor from a very large distance in a direction perpendicular to its plates and flies through the capacitor through small holes in the middle of the plates. Assuming that at a large distance from the capacitor the speed of the particle is equal to v, find its speed: 1) at point A inside the capacitor (Fig. 3); 2) at point B immediately after leaving the condenser. The capacitor is charged to a potential difference U, the distance between the plates is much less than the dimensions of the plates; The capacitor charge is much greater than q.
