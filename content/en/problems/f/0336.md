## Condition

A cylindrical cup with a handle rotates around a vertical axis with an angular velocity $\omega$. In this case, the surface of rutini forms a parabolic mirror. Determine the focal length of this mirror. Rutini density $\rho$, free fall acceleration $g$.

## Solution

The cylindrical cup with mercury in the opposite vessel takes the shape of a parabolic of rotation. The distance on the surface of the liquid is a non-tortic small element of mass $m$, located at a distance $x$ from the axis of rotation (Fig. 12). This element is acted upon by gravity forces $mg$ and reaction forces $N$ from the rest of the liquid. The force $N$ is directed perpendicular to the free surface of the liquid. This follows from the fact that the free surface of a liquid is a surface of constant pressure.

Since the selected element is uniformly impacted along a circle of radius $x$, the resulting force acting on it is directed horizontally to the impact axis and is different $m\omega^2 x$. From the parallelogram of forces at figure 12 we find:
