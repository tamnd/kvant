## Condition

Due to the presence of a space charge in the interelectronic space of a flat diode, the potential distribution $\varphi(x)$ between the cathode and anomer has the form: $\varphi(x) = x^2 - 2x$ (x is in mm, and $\varphi$ is in volts). Distance between cathode and anomal $d=10$ mm. The coordinate $x$ coincides with the distance to the cathode. Determine at what minimum kinetic energy an electron from the cathode surface can reach the anode. What will be the maximum acceleration of the electric currents that reach the anode?
