## Condition

To produce gases at ultra-high temperatures and pressures, an installation is sometimes used, consisting of a cylinder-barrel closed at one end and a piston-pool that flies into the cylinder from the open side. With good processing of the barrel and bullet, it is possible to achieve a small leakage of gas through the gap. Due to the very high temperatures, strongly rolled gases can still be considered ideal under these conditions. Estimate the upper limit of the temperature of argon subjected to rolling in such an installation, if a bullet of mass $m = 100$ e flies into a barrel having a volume $V_0 = 200\text{ cm}^3$,

with initial speed $v = 250\text{ m/c}$. The initial temperature and pressure of the gas are equal to the correspondence of $T_0 = 300^\circ\text{K}$ and $p_0 = 1\text{ atm}$.

## Solution

** *To produce gases at ultra-high temperatures and pressures, an installation is sometimes used, consisting of a cylinder-barrel closed at one end and a piston-bullet flying into the cylinder from the open side. With good processing of the bullet and barrel, it is possible to achieve low gas leakage through the gap. Due to the very high temperatures, highly compressed gases can still be considered ideal under these conditions. Estimate the upper limit of the temperature of argon subjected to compression in such a setup if a bullet of mass $m = 100$ g flies into a barrel having a volume of $V_0 = 200$ cm$^3$ with an initial speed of $v = 250$ m/s. The initial temperature and pressure of the gas are $T_0 = 300^\circ$ K and $p_0 = 1$ atm, respectively.*
