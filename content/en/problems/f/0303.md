## Condition

A point source of light is located at some distance under a thin collecting lens (Fig. 1). Where and how should a plane mirror be installed so that a parallel beam of light emerges from the lens in the direction shown by the arrow? Construct the course of the rays.

## Solution

** *A point source of light is located at some distance under a thin collecting lens (Fig. 3). Where and how should a plane mirror be installed so that a parallel beam of light emerges from the lens in the direction shown by the arrow? Construct the course of rays.*

⟦column⟧

Let $F'F''$ be the focal plane of the lens. Let us draw a secondary optical axis $AA'$ parallel to the direction specified by the arrow (see Fig. 3). If the light source is located in the focal plane of the lens at the point $S'$ of its intersection with the $AA'$ axis, then the lens collects its rays into a parallel beam directed along the $AA'$ axis, that is, a parallel beam of light emerges from the lens in the direction indicated by the arrow.

In our case, the source for the lens will be the image of the source $S$ in a plane mirror. To this image
