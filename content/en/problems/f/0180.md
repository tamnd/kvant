## Condition

A closed cubic vessel with an edge of 1 cm contains $n$ gas molecules. The stacks of the cube are such that gas molecules, having hit the wall, remain on it for $10^{-2}$ s. Estimate how many gas molecules are on the walls.

The vessel is at room temperature.
