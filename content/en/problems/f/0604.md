## Condition

The free surface of the liquid had the shape of a sphere with a radius of $R_0$. The same liquid placed in a glass spherical vessel of radius $R_2$ took the shape shown in figure 2. The free surface of the liquid was flat. Determine the height of the liquid level in a spherical vessel. What shape will the liquid have in a spherical vessel if the radius of the vessel is greater than $R_2$? less than $R_2$?
