## Condition

A monatomic ideal gas expands so that its molar temperature $c_x$ remains constant; in this case, the gas does work $A = 165$ J. Then the gas is isochorically heated to the initial temperature, for which an amount of heat $Q = 125$ J is supplied to it. Determine $c_x$.
