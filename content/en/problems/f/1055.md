## Condition

Electrical device P is connected to an alternating current network with a voltage of 220 V through a capacitor em with C = 0.5 μF (Fig. 2). The ammeter shows current I=0.01 A, the voltmeter reading is U=180 V. Find the power consumed by the device from the network. Consider the ampere and voltmeter ideal.

A. R. Zilberman

## Solution

Electrical device P is connected to an alternating current network with a voltage of 220 V through a capacitor with a capacity of $C = 0,5$ uF (Fig. 1). The ammeter shows the current $I = 0,01$ A, the voltmeter reading is $U = 180$ V. Find the power consumed from the network by the device. Consider the ammeter and voltmeter to be ideal.

Fig. 1. Fig. 2.

Let us first find the effective value of the voltage across the capacitor:

$$U_C = IX_C = I \frac{1}{2\pi C} = 63,7 \text{ В}$$

(here $v = 50$ Hz is the frequency of alternating current oscillations in the network). Now let's draw a vector diagram*) of currents and voltages for this circuit (Fig. 2). From the diagram it is easy to find the dwg of phases $\varphi$ between current $I$ and voltage $U_0$:

$$\varphi = 90^\circ - \arccos \alpha = 90^\circ - \arccos \frac{U_0^2 + U_C^2 - U^2}{2U_0 U_C} \approx 46^\circ.$$

The active power consumed by the device P is equal to the active power that is released in the entire circuit (no heat is released in the capacitor), i.e.

$$P = U_0 I \cos \varphi \approx 1,53 \text{ Вт.}$$

A. R. Zilberman

*) You can read about what vector diagrams are and how they are constructed, for example, in the article “AC Circuits” («Kvant», 1986, No. 9).
