## Condition

The lightning rod is connected to the ground using a thin-walled tube with a diameter of 2 cm and a wall thickness of 2 mm. After the udram, the tube instantly turned into a round rod. Explain this phenomenon and estimate the strength of the discharge current if it is known that when rolling a cylindrical sample with a diameter of 3 mm, made of the same materials as the tube, it is destroyed at a force of 140,000 N.

**P. L. Kapsha**
