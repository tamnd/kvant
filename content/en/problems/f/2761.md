## Condition

Maxwell's pendulum is a structure consisting of a U-shaped rigidly attached to the base of a tripod, two rigidly connected coaxial cylinders of different diameters and different lengths, as well as two threads of the same length, with one end of each thread fixed to a cylinder of a smaller diameter, and the other to a horizontal crossbar of the tripod (Fig. 1). Both cylinders are made of the same homogeneous material with density U 10 g $см^{3}$. The ratio of the diameters of the cylinders is $D_{1} D_{2} 10$, the ratio of their lengths (along the axis of symmetry) is $l_{2} l_{1} 3$. The length of each straightened thread is $10,5 D_{1}$.
