## Condition

A directed flow of electricity emerges from a thin wide gap at a speed of $v = 10^5$ m/s (Fig. 3). The concentration of electrons in the stream is $n = 10^{10}$ particles/m$^3$. At what distance from the slit will the beam thickness double? Electron mass $m = 9 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, electron charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, dielectric constant $\varepsilon_0 = 8,85 \cdot 10^{-12}$ F/m. (9)
