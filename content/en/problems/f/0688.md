## Condition

A thin hoop of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is rigidly fixed using light knitting needles on a light thin axis passing through the center of the hoop, so that the plane of the hoop makes an angle $\alpha$ (Fig. 4) with the axis. Determine what work needs to be done to spin the axis to angular velocity $\omega$.
