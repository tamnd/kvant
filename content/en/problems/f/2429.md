## Condition

The cowboy tied a baseball, $m_{м} = = 150$ g and D = 7.3 cm, to a stiff (inextensible and light) thread of length L = 1 m, the other end of which was secured to a round horizontal crossbar with a diameter of d = 6 cm. Then he walked 10 steps in a direction perpendicular to the crossbar and fired a Colt at the ball. The ball began to move quickly, winding the thread around the crossbar, and then came off. Upon examination, it turned out that the bullet was stuck in the ball, and the thread wrapped around the crossbar n = 2 times. Estimate what the speed of the bullet was before colliding with the ball if the mass of the bullet is $m_{п} = 10$ g, and the thread can support a load weighing no more than $m_{гр} = 10$ kg. D. Bograchev
