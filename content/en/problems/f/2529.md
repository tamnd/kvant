## Condition

A cone is “fitted” into a cube-shaped aquarium with an edge length of 2R (the width of the aquarium is equal to the diameter of the base of the cone, the height of the cone is equal to the height of the aquarium). The aquarium with the cone in it is completely filled with water. Determine the minimum amount of work that must be done to “drain” the cone (remove it from the water). The density of water is $U_{0}$, the density of the cone material is U.
