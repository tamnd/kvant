## Condition

figure 3 shows the fractional cross-section of the device using length as a standard. The central rod $C$ and the outer shell $A$ of the device are made of materials with a coefficient of thermal expansion $\alpha_1$; their lengths at $t_1=20$ °C are the same and equal to $l$. The internal weight $B$ is made of materials with a coefficient of thermal expansion $\alpha_2$. What should be the length of the inner pipe at $t_1=20$ °C so that the full standard $|CD|$ does not change when the temperature changes?

A. P. Ershov
