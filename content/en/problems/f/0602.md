## Condition

Capacitors with capacitances $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected to a circuit consisting of a resistor with resistance $R$ and a source with EMF $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance $r=R/3$, closing terminals 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 (Fig. 3). After the circuit, the voltage on the capacitor $C_1$ turns out to be equal to $\mathcal{E}/2$, and the potential of terminal 3 is higher than the potential of terminal 2. Determine what charge $p_0$ was on the capacitor $C_2$ before the circuit. (9).
