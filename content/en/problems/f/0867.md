## Condition

Imagine that you are standing in front of a large sheet of thick clear glass (for example, in front of a glass display case). How to determine the thickness of glass if there is no access to its edges (say, the edges of the glass are walled up in the walls), you cannot enter the room behind the glass, and you cannot break the glass? You have at your disposal a ruler, a square, paper, a pencil and a pocket calculator for making calculations.

M. V. Semenov
