## Condition

The high-precision multi-limit ampere contains a modified separate shutt for each limit. The ampere switches on at the 10 mA limit, and it shows a current of $I_1 = 2,95$ mA; when it was switched to the 3 mA limit it showed $I_2 = 2,90$ mA. What was the current strength in the circuit before the amplifier was connected? (9)

A. Zilberman
