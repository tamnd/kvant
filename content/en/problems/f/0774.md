## Condition

For multi-tails, there are values ​​of temperature $T_{\text{tr}}$ and pressure $p_{\text{tr}}$ at which all three phases of the substance - gaseous, liquid and solid - are in equilibrium with each other - the so-called triple point. For water $T_{\text{tr}} = 0,0075^{\circ}\text{C}$, $p_{\text{tr}} = 4,58$ mm Hg. st; the specific vaporization temperature at the triple point is $r = 595,8$ cal/g, the specific melting temperature is $\lambda = 79,7$ cal/g. Find the specific temperament (primary transition from solid to gaseous state) of water near the triple point.

V. Davydov

## Solution

For multi-tails, there are such values ​​of temperature $T_{\text{tp}}$ and pressure $p_{\text{tp}}$ at which all three phases of this can be attempted, for example, to depict the letters of a chain of touching circles. The number of circles in such chains should be at least 5-6 so that the letters differ from each other. Considering that the radius of these circles on the surface of the Moon should be $R = 40\text{ m}$, we obtain an estimate of the exchange letter: $l \approx 400-500\text{ m}$.

When photographing, the illumination of any point in the image of the Moon in the focal plane of the object is created due to the radiation of all elements of the Moon's surface lying within a circle of radius $R = 40\text{ m}$, that is, area $S = \pi R^2$. If part of the area of ​​this circle is covered by a black (non-reflective) disk of area $s = \pi r^2$, then the illumination of the image of the application point will be proportional to $(S-s)$, while at other points it is proportional to $S$. Therefore, the image contrast will be equal to

$$k = \frac{S - (S - s)}{S} = \frac{s}{S} = \left( \frac{r}{R} \right)^2 = 10^{-2}.$$

This value is less than the value specified under the conditions of a reliable difference in image contrast $k_{\text{min}} = 5 \cdot 10^{-2}$. Consequently, it is impossible to detect an intercaptive corpus.
