## Condition

A motor boat, located at point $A$ at a distance $R$ from the shore of the lake, begins to turn, moving at a speed of $v=18$ km/h along a circle of radius $r=R/2$; at the initial moment the speed of the boat is directed towards the shore (Fig. 2). A wave from the boat to the shore $t=3$ minutes after the start of the turn. The speed of propagation of waves from a boat along the surface of the water is $u=9$ km/h. Find the distance $R$.

A. I. Buzdin

## Solution
