## Condition

A square wire frame with perimeter $4a$ and mass $m$ imparts a certain initial velocity in the horizontal direction. The frame moves in a vertical plane, all the time being in a magnetic field perpendicular to the plane of the frame (Fig. 2). The field induction changes according to the law $\cdot B(z) = B(0) + kz$, where $k = const$. The frame resistance is $R$. After some time, the speed of the frame becomes constant and equal to $v$. Find the initial speed imparted to the frame. Gravity acceleration $g$.
