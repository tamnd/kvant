## Condition

A mass of water $M$ is poured every second into a cylindrical vessel of height $H$ with base area $S$ from a horizontal pipe. There is a small hole at the base of the vessel. A thin light sponge (Fig. 1) lies in the vessel on the surface of the water. From a certain moment, the height of the water level in the vessel becomes constant and equal to $h_0$. Determine the speed at which water flows out of the hole.

I. Omelyan

## Solution

In a cylindrical connection of height $H$ with base area $S$, a mass of water $M$ flows every second from a horizontal pipe. There is a small hole at the base. On the surface of the water in the vessel lies a thin lekka clove. From a certain moment, the height of the water level in the vessel becomes constant and equal to $h_0$. Determine the speed at which water flows out of the hole.

In the steady state, the dreams acting on the sponge are balanced. A force is exerted on the sponge by the falling water

$$F = \frac{M \cdot \Delta t \cdot \sqrt{2g(H - h_0)}}{\Delta t} = M \sqrt{2g(H - h_0)}.$$

This force does work in time $\Delta t$

$$\Delta A = F \cdot \Delta x = F \frac{\Delta V}{S} = F \frac{M \cdot \Delta t}{\rho S}.$$

According to the law of conservation of energy

$$\Delta A + \Delta \Pi = \Delta K,$$

or

$$\Delta A + M \cdot \Delta t \cdot g \cdot h_0 = M \cdot \Delta t \cdot \frac{v_1^2 - v_2^2}{2},$$

where $v$ is the speed of water falling on the sponge, $v_1$ is the speed of water flowing out of the hole. Since the hole is small, $v^2 \ll v_1^2$. Finally we get

$$\frac{F}{\rho S} + g h_0 = \frac{1}{2} v_1^2,$$

where

$$v_1 = \sqrt{2 g h_0 + \frac{2 M}{\rho S} \sqrt{2 g(H - h_0)}}.$$

I. Omelyan
