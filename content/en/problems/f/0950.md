## Condition

In a sealed capillary tube, there are two columns of rutin, separated by a drop of an aqueous solution of the HgI₂ electrolyte. Inner diameter of the tube d=0.3 mm. The tube is connected in series with a resistor with resistance R=390 kOhm to a battery with emf E=10 V (Fig. 2). After what time will the droplet move one scale division (see figure)?

E. N. Yunosov, I. V. Yaminsky
