## Condition

In the toy bow (Fig. 2), energy is stored not in the curved arms of the bow, but in the stretching string. The distance between the ends of a light string attached to the bow is L. String stiffness k. Young Robin Hood, when shooting, pulls the bowstring so tight that the tension force of the bowstring doubles, and its ends and the place in the middle of the bowstring, which is touched by the end of the arrow with feathers, are located Fig. 2 in the corners of an equilateral triangle with sides L. When the arrow is launched vertically upward, it flies up to a height of h 20L. What is the mass of the arrow m? Air resistance can be neglected.
