## Condition

To clear the impact of dust, which under normal conditions settles very slowly, you can use the fact that the dust is charged.

In the first experiment, a glass cylinder (Fig. 3) exposed to dust is placed in an electric field of intensity $E_1=1 \cdot 10^4$ B/m, directed along the axis of the cylinder. After a time of $t_1=2$ min, the dust always contained in the cylinder settled to the bottom. In the second experiment, a thin wire is pulled along the axis of the cylinder and connected to a high voltage source. It is known that in this case the stress of all the dust on the walls of the cylinder in the second test. There is little dust involved, so the space charge can be neglected.
