## Solution

A skydiver with a mass of $m=90$ kg makes a long jump. Before the parachute opened, its speed was $v_0=60$ m/s; after the parachute opened, the speed was $v=6$ m/s. What would be the maximum tension on the parachute lines if it deployed in a multi-fold fashion? Assume that the force of air resistance against a moving parachute is proportional to the square of the speed. The mass of the parachute and lines should be considered small compared to the mass of the parachute.
