## Condition

The UFO flies over the Earth at a constant, very high speed $v$. What speed will the station’s ground instruments record at that moment?

## Solution

The UFO flies over the Earth at a constant, very high speed $v$. What speed will be recorded by ground samples of the tracking station at the moment when the direction to the object makes an angle $\varphi$ with the vertical (Fig. 1)?

Let time $t$ pass from the moment the UFO flew close to the observed point $A$ (Fig. 2) to this moment (to point $B$), and this time was absent according to the fixed clock, we are at point $B$.
