## Condition

The ends of a uniform rod of constant cross-section are maintained at temperatures $T_1$ and $T_2$ ($T_2 > T_1$). The temperature coefficient of linear expansion of the rod materials is $\alpha$. What is the length of the rod if at $t = 0^\circ C$ it was $l_0$?
