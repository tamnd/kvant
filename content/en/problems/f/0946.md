## Condition

Two identical exact charges $q>0$ are located at a distance $l$ from each other. What is the minimum distance to which the line coming out of the left charge at an angle $\alpha$ to the straight line connecting the charges will “approach” the symmetry plane $\Pi$ (Fig. 2)? At what angle to the plane of symmetry will this line be located when removed to a large distance from the charges?

S. S. Krotov
