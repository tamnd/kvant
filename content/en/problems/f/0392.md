## Condition

The mass board $M$ is positioned horizontally and rests on two rotating cylinders (Fig. 5). Distance between cylinder axes $l$. Friction coefficient between dock and cylinders $k$. Prove that if the dock is in a horizontal direction, it will perform a harmonious oscillation, and find the period of these oscillations. What will be the movement of the dock if the direction of rotation of the cylinders is reversed?
