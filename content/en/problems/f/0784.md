## Condition

A constant force $F$ begins to act on a body moving at a constant speed $v$. After time $t$ the speed of the body became equal to $v/2$. Over the next similar time interval, the speed decreased by another half. Determine what the speed of the body will be after a time interval $3t$ from the beginning of the force $F$.
