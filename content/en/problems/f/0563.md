## Condition

The middle barometric tubes have so much air (Fig. 1). The cross-sectional area of ​​the tube is $s = 0,3 \text{ cm}^2$. At temperature $t = 0^\circ\text{C}$ the length of the table is $l_0 = 10 \text{ cm}$. What will be the length of this table at $t = 20^\circ\text{C}$?

## Solution

In the middle of the barometric tube there is a column of air. At a temperature of $t_0 = 0^\circ C$ the length of the column is $t_0 = 10$ cm. What will be the length of the column at $t = 20^\circ C$?
