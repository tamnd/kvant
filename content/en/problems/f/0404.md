## Condition

Secondary hydrogen consumption 24 kcal/sec. The speed of gas flow from the reaction nozzle is $4,2 \cdot 10^3$ m/sec. The calorific value of hydrogen is $1,1 \cdot 10^8$ J/kk. (9 grades)
