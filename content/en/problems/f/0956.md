## Condition

In one of the variants of the classical experiment carried out by Academician I.K. Kikon, a single-point short-circuited inductor $L=3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ Gn made of thin superconducting wire is suspended on pruguroidinites in a magnetic field directed vertically upward along the axis of the coil (figure 4). The suspension thread in the initial state is not twisted, the current strength in the coil is zero. The induction $B$ of the magnetic field slowly increases from zero to a value $B_0=0,1$ T, at which superconductivity abruptly disappeared and the wire returned to the normal state. At the same time, the coil is twisted. Determine the maximum angle of twist of the coil, assuming that the elastic moment of the thread is proportional to the angle of twist (proportionality coefficient $G=10^{-7}$ N m/rad). The number of turns of the coil is $n=100$, the radius of the turns is $R=1$ cm, the mass of the coil is $M=10$ g. The ratio of the electron charge to its mass is $e/m=1,76 \cdot 10^{11}$ C/kg.

S. M. Kozel
