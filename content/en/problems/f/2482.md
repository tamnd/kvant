Let the initial distances between the racers be l. Let's consider their movement from the point of view of Egor, who was driving slowest. There are two possible options for the initial position of the riders. 1) The distance (in the direction of travel) from Vova to Egor is l, and from Sasha to Egor is 2l. Then, before meeting with Yegor, Vova must travel a distance l at a speed $v_{2} - v_{1}$, and Sasha during the same time, having a speed $v - v_{1}$, must cover distances 2l, 5l, 8l... depending on his speed. Since by condition $v_{2} - v_{1} = 10$ km h, then $v - v_{1} = 20, 50, 80$ ... km h. This means v = 30 or 60 km h. A higher speed contradicts the conditions of the problem. 2) The distance from Vova to Egor is 2l, and from Sasha to Egor l. Then, before meeting with Yegor, Vova must travel a distance of 2l at a speed of $v_{2} - v_{1}$, and Sasha during the same time, having a speed of $v - v_{1}$, must cover distances of 4l, 7l, 10l, 13l... depending on the speed of movement. Since $v_{2} - v_{1} = 10$ km h, then $v - v_{1} = 20, 35, 50, 65$ ... km h. Therefore, v = 30, 45, 60 or 75 km h. A higher speed contradicts the conditions of the problem. Combining the sets of obtained values ​​gives the following values ​​of Sasha’s possible speeds:
