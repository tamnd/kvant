## Condition

Two immiscible liquids are poured into a glass: carbon tetrachloride CCl₄ and water. At normal atmospheric pressure, CCl₄ boils at 76.7° C, water - at 100° C. When a glass is uniformly heated in a water bath, boiling at the liquid interface begins at a temperature of 65.5° C. Determine which liquid boils quickly at such “border” boiling and by how many times. The saturated vapor pressure of water at 65.5° C is 192 mm pm. cm. (9th, 10th grade)

## Solution

There are two non-displaceable liquids in the juice: carbon perchloride $CCl_4$ and water. At normal atmospheric pressure, $CCl_4$ boils at $76,7^\circ \text{C}$, water - at $100^\circ \text{C}$. When the relish is uniformly heated in a water bath, boiling at the liquid interface occurs at a temperature of $65,5^\circ \text{C}$. Determine which liquid boils quickly at the maximum “borderline” boiling point and by how much. The saturated vapor pressure of water at $65,5^\circ \text{C}$ is 192 mm rm. cm.
