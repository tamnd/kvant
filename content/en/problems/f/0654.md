## Condition

The mass stand $M$ stands on a smooth table. A mass ball $m$ is attached to the tripod on a light thread of length $l$. The thread is deflected at a small angle $\alpha$ and released (Fig. 4). Draw a graph of the speed of the shativ versus time. The table ball with the base of the frame is absolutely elastic.

A. Zilberman

## Solution

The mass M stand is placed on the main table. A ball of mass m is attached to a tripod on a thin thread of length l. The thread deflects through a small angle a and is released (Fig. 1). Narip

Let current flow through a branch of the circuit I. Since both linear and nonlinear resistors are the same, the same currents flow in the branches of the circuit. The voltage of the power supply is

$$U_0 = IR + \alpha I^2.$$

So through the galvanometer is zero when the potential of point A is equal to the potential of point B. Therefore

$$IR = \alpha I^2 \Rightarrow I = \frac{R}{\alpha}.$$

Substituting this current value into equation (*), we find the direction of the source at which the current through the galvanometer is zero:

$$U_0 = 2 \frac{R^2}{\alpha}.$$
