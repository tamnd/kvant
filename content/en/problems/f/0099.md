## Condition

figure 18a shows V-T diagrams of two circular processes. In which of them does the gas do more work: in the process 1-2-3-1 or in the process 1-3-4-1?

Let us redraw the diagrams of the processes shown on figure in coordinates P→V.

Points 1 and 3 lie on the same isotherm, and point 1 corresponds to a smaller volume than point 3. Therefore, having drawn the isotherm, we select two points on it - points 1 and 3, as shown in figure 18, 6. Now it is not difficult to construct cycle diagrams. Line 1-2 is an isobar, and the volume at point 2 is greater than the volume at point 1. Line 2-3 is an isochore. On a P-V diagram it should be vertical. The diagram of the second process is constructed in exactly the same way.

We know that the work done by the gas per cycle is equal to the area of ​​the figure limited by the cycle graph. But from figure it is clear that the area of ​​the “red” process is larger than the area of ​​the “gray” process. This means that more work is done by the gas during the 1-2-3-1 process.
