## Condition

The pole on the mast of the ship forms an angle $60^\circ$ with the course of the ship at its speed of 20 km/h. Without changing course, the ship increased its speed by 2 times, and the angle became equal to $30^\circ$. Find the speed from these data

wind (considering it unchanged). At what speed of the ship will the angle become 90°?

## Solution

The bow on the ship's main body forms an angle $60^\circ$ with the ship's heading at its speed of 20 km/h. Without changing course, the ship's speed doubled and the angle became 30°. Find the wind speed from these data (assuming it constant). At what speed of the ship will the angle become 90°?**

**The pennant on the ship’s masthead shows the direction of vector $\vec{u} + \vec{v}$, where $\vec{v}$ is the ship’s speed vector (it determines the ship’s course), $\vec{u}$ is the wind speed vector.**

Let's project the vector $\vec{u}$ on the direction of the ship's course and on the direction perpendicular to the course. Let $u_1$ and $u_2$ be the absolute values ​​of the corresponding projects. Since the direction of the wind is

⟦folio 52⟧

unknown, in the general case we can write the following relations:

at ship speed $|\vec{v}| = 20$ km/h —
$$\frac{\pm u_2}{v \pm u_1} = \text{tg} 60^\circ = \sqrt{3},$$

(1)

at ship speed $2|\vec{v}| = 40$ km/h —
$$\frac{\pm u_2}{2v \pm u_1} = \text{tg} 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

(2)

Having solved equations (1) and (2) together, we find
$$u_1 = \frac{1}{2} v, \quad u_2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} v.$$

From here you can find a model of the wind speed vector:
$$|\vec{u}| = \sqrt{u_1^2 + u_2^2} = |\vec{v}| = 20 \text{ km/ч}.$$

The direction of the vector $\vec{u}$ cannot be found from these data, but we can say that the angle $\alpha$, which the wind speed vector forms with the ship’s heading, is determined, up to sign, by the condition
$$\text{tg} \alpha = \pm \frac{u_2}{u_1} = \pm \sqrt{3}.$$

In the case when the pennant forms an angle $90^\circ$ with the heading of the ship, the speed $v_x$ of the ship and the speed $u$ of the wind are related by the relation
$$|\vec{u}|^2 - |\vec{v}_x|^2 = u_2^2,$$ or $u_1^2 + u_2^2 - |\vec{v}_x|^2 = u_2^2,$
where
$$|\vec{v}_x| = u_1 = \frac{1}{2} v = 10 \text{ km/ч}.$$

So, the pennant will make an angle $90^\circ$ with the ship’s heading at a ship speed of $|\vec{v}_x| = 10 \text{ km/ч}.$
