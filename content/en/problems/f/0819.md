## Condition

When small bodies, whose masses are in the ratio 3:4:5 (the mass of the lightest body is $m$), are held at three different points on the inner surface of a smooth hemispherical part of radius $R$, which is attached to the horizontal surface at the lowest point. At some points they let go and imagine themselves. What is the maximum amount of heat that can be released in this system? At what initial position of the bodies will this happen? All collisions between bodies are completely inelastic.

S. K. Strokov

## Solution

Three small bodies, the masses of which are in the ratio 3:4:5 (the mass of the body itself is equal to m), are held at three different points on the inner surface of a smooth hemispherical part of radius R, which is fixed at the lowest point on a horizontal surface. At some point the bodies are released and presented to themselves. What is the maximum amount of body that can be released in such a system? At what initial position of the bodies will this happen? All collisions of bodies are absolutely inelastic.
