## Condition

The vertical length of the brick pipe is filled with cast iron. The lower end of the pipe is maintained at a temperature of $T_1 > T_{nл}$ ($T_{nл}$ is the melting point of cast iron), the upper end is maintained at a temperature of $T_2 < T_{nл}$. The thermal conductivity of molten cast iron is $k$ times greater than that of solid iron. What part of the metal is in the molten state?

A. I. Buzdin
