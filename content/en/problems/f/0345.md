## Condition

Estimate the engine power required to maintain a helicopter of mass $M = 500 \text{ kg}$ in the air with blades of length $l = 3 \text{ m}$.

Assume that the weight of air under the flapping blades moves in a uniform downward flow. (8-9 grades)
