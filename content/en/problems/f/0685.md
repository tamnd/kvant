## Condition

Between the wall and a cube of mass $M=10$ kg, an elastic ball of mass $m=0,1$ g flies on a smooth table. Its speed at the beginning, when the cube was at rest, was $v_0=100$ m/s. Find the speed of the cube at the moment when it is 2 times farther from the wall than at the beginning.
