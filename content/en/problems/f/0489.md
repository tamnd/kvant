## Condition

Electrons are accelerated in an electron gun by an electric field, passing through a gap whose direction is $U = 10^3$ V. Having flown out of the gun at point $T$, the electrons then move along a straight line $TT'$ (Fig. 1). At point $M$ at a distance of $a = 5,0$ cm from point $T$ there is a target, and straight line $TM$ forms an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with straight line $TT'$.

1) What should the induction $\vec{B}$ of a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the drawing plane be for electrons emitted from the gun to hit the target?

2) What should the induction $\vec{B}_1$ of a uniform magnetic field parallel to the straight line $MT$ be for electrons to hit the target?

Assume that $|\vec{B}|$ and $|\vec{B}_1|$ do not exceed 0.030 T.
