## Condition

On the question of a spherical surface of radius $R$ from a height $H = R/8$ near the axis of symmetry small balls fall (with zero initial velocity). Assuming the impacts of balls on the surface to be absolutely elastic, show that after the first impact each ball hits the lowest point of the surface. Mutual collisions of balls are not taken into account.

I. V. Krivenkov
