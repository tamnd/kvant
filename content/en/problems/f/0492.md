## Condition

Two round cylinders with diameters $D$ and $d$ (Fig. 2) lie on a rough plane. A cord is wound around a large cylinder, to the end of which a horizontal force $\vec{F}$ is applied. Determine at what friction coefficient $\mu$, identical for all contacting surfaces, can a large cylinder be dragged through a soap bar. What should the absolute value of the force $\vec{F}$ be in order for this to be possible?
