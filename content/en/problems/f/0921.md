## Condition

A parallel-plate capacitor is connected to a voltage source $U$. The capacitor plates have an area of ​​$S$ each, and the distance between the plates is $d_1$. A metal plate of area $S$ and thickness $d_2$ (Fig. 4), having a mass $m$, is pressed against one plate. The plastic is coming off. At what speed will it hit the other plate of the capacitor? Neglect the influence of gravity.
