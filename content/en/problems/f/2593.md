## Condition

Two paths of equal length in the park are covered with snow. Two janitors, Vasya and Petya, must clear the paths of snow. They started cleaning the first path from both ends, and when it was cleared, it turned out that Vasya had cleared 20 meters in length. They also started cleaning the second track from both ends, but now Vasya increased the speed 3 times, and Petya maintained the same pace. When both tracks were cleared, it turned out that Vasya's share was only 50 meters. How many meters of paths did Petya clear?
