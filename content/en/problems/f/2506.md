## Condition

A small cockroach runs along the surface of a stationary small school globe with a radius R = 9 cm at a constant speed v = 3 cm/s. In relation to the markings of the globe, its speed is always directed to the northeast. What is the magnitude of the acceleration of the cockroach at the moment when it steps on the circle corresponding to the position of St. Petersburg $( M = 60 \infty$ northern latitude)? T. Globusov
