## Condition

Ten ants decided to remove a straw from the table that was lying on it. How should they build if the force with which each of the ants can pull away the straw is slightly less than $1/10$ the frictional force acting on the straw when it is shuffled around the table? An ant cannot lift a straw either.
G. L. Komkin
