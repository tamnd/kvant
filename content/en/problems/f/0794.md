## Condition

Two identical body-solided calometers of height $h = 75$ cm are filled one third with ice, the other with water at a temperature of $t = 10^\circ\text{C}$. Water from the second calometer is poured into the first, and the calometer is two-thirds full. After the temperature in the calometer is established, its filling level will increase by $\Delta h = 0,5$ cm. What was the initial temperature of the ice in the calometer? Density of ice - $\varrho_{\text{л}} = 9 \cdot 10^2 \text{ кг/m}^3$, water - $\varrho_{\text{в}} = 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$; specific heat of melting of ice - $\lambda = 3,4 \times 10^5 \text{ Дж/kg}$; The specific heat of ice is $c_{\text{л}} = 2,1 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$, of water - $c_{\text{в}} = 4,2 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$.

A. I Buzdin
