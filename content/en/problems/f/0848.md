## Condition

A ball rolls along a smooth horizontal circular groove of length $l$. The speed $v$ of the ball is known with an accuracy of $\varepsilon = \pm 5\%$. For what values ​​of $v$ can we say with certainty that after time $t$ after the ball is at point $A$ (Fig. 2), it is not at point $B$?
