## Condition

In a thin smooth pipeline a flexible homogeneous cord (Fig. 1) slides (in a field of force and gravity). Sections AB and BC of the pipeline are semicircles of radius R, points A, B and C lie on the same vertical; cord length l=2πR. Find all points of the cord at which the tension is zero at the moment when the lower end of the cord is at point C.

I. Yu. Poteryako

## Solution

In the thin main coarse pipeline there is scolcite (in the field there is heavy weight) and a homogeneous cord (see figure). Section AB and BC of the rough pipeline are semicircles with radius R, points A, B, C lie on the same vertical; cord length l = 2πR. Find all the points on the cord at which the tension is zero at the moment when the lower end of the cord is at point C.

Let at that moment of time, which is imprinted on figure, the cord has acceleration a. Let's find him.

Over a short period of time Δt, the cord moved so that its end, located at point C, swept away by Δx=v·Δt, where v is the value of the instantaneous speed of the cord. The kinetic energy of the cord during this time increased by

$$\Delta K = \frac{m(v + \Delta v)^2}{2} - \frac{mv^2}{2} \approx mv \cdot \Delta v$$

(m is the mass of the cord; we neglected the term ~(Δv)²). The potential energy of the cord changed to

$$\Delta \Pi = \Delta m \cdot gh = m \frac{\Delta x}{l} gh = m \frac{v}{l} gh \cdot \Delta t,$$

where Δm is the mass of a piece of cord of length Δx, which “moved” from point A to point C, i.e., fell from a height h=4R. According to the law of conservation of energy ΔK=ΔΠ, i.e.

$$mv \cdot \Delta v = m \frac{v}{l} gh \cdot \Delta t.$$
