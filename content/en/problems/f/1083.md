## Condition

A homogeneous brick $ABCDA'B'C'D'$ (Fig. 1) has the following property: if it is cut by an arbitrary plane passing through the edge $AA'$, then each of the two parts of the brick placed on a new face $AEE'A'$ (Fig. 2) does not fall. Find correspondences between brick sizes at which this is possible.

S. V. Shlosman
