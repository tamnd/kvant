## Condition

On the bank of the river, the flow speed of which is $\omega$, at point $A$ there is a boy. He can be along the shore at a speed of $v$ and swim along the river at a speed of $u$ (relative to the water), and $u < \omega$. Determine at what distance $x$ from point $A$ there is that point $C$ of the shore from where the boy should start swimming in order to get to the buoy $B$ in the shortest time. The distance $BD$ from the buoy to the shore is $h$, the distance $AD$ is $l$ (Fig. 4).
