## Condition

A metal ball of radius $r$, removed from other objects, then through resistance $R$. A beam of electrons, whose speed far from the ball was $v$, hits the ball. $n$ electrons hit the ball per second. What is the amount of thermal conductivity on the ball per second? What is the riddle?

I. F. Ginzburg
