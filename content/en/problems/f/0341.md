## Condition

In the simplest circuit of a magnetic hydrodynamic generator, a flat capacitor with plate area $S$ and distance between it $d$ is placed in a flow of conducting fluid with specific conductivity σ. The fluid moves at a constant speed $v$ parallel to the plates. The capacitor is in a magnetic field with induction $B$, directed perpendicular to the fluid velocity and parallel to the plane of the plates. What power is released in an external circuit having resistance $R$?
