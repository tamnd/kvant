## Condition

The lamp is located at a distance of $l_1$ from the ceiling at a height of $l_2$ from the floor. When it explodes, the debris scatters in all directions with the same speed $v$. Find the radius of the circle on the floor where the rocks will fall. Assume that the impacts of the shards on the ceiling are absiltonically elastic, and on the floor - inelastic; The salts should not reach the walls.
