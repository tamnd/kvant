## Condition

A gas mixture consisting of $m_1=100$ g of nitrogen and an unknown amount of oxygen is subjected to isothermal rolling at a temperature of $T=74,4$ K. A graph of the pressure of the gas mixture versus its volume is shown in figure 3 (in units of conditions). Determine the mass of oxygen. Calculate the saturated oxygen vapor pressure at this temperature.

Note: $T=74,4$ K is the boiling point of liquid nitrogen at normal pressure; oxygen boils at a higher temperature.

A. I. Buzdin
