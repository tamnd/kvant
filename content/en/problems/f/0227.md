## Condition

Two coils are wound on a horopod ferrite core with magnetic permeability μ = 2000: a primary one containing n₁ = 2000 turns, and a secondary one with n₂ = 4000 turns. When the voltage U₁ = 100.0 V was applied to the primary coil, on the open secondary coil, if the core is replaced with a core of the same size, but made of ferrite with μ' = 20. Magnetic flux leakage and losses in the core are not taken into account. (10 grades)
