## Condition

The power solution is poured into a cylindrical vessel placed in a vertical magnetic field. Between the electrode running along the cylinder axis and the second electrode, which is the side surface of the vessel, a potential difference (Fig. 4) is maintained all the time. What can you say about the behavior of the raster in the described situation?

A. I. Vuzdin

Problem solutions
M1171—M1175, F1183—F1187

Note that for k ≥ 2
$$\frac{1}{h_{k-1}} - \frac{1}{h_k} = \frac{1}{kh_{k-1}h_k} > \frac{1}{kh_k^2}.$$
Summing these inequalities over k=2, 3, ..., n, we obtain the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2h_2^2} + \frac{1}{3h_3^2} + \dots + \frac{1}{nh_n^2} < \frac{1}{h_1} - \frac{1}{h_n} < 1$$
($h_1 = 1$). It remains to add 1/h₁² = 1 to both sides.
L. D. Kurlyandchik
