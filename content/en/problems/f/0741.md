## Condition

In an alternating current with a voltage of 220 V and a frequency of 50 Hz, two capacitors with a capacity of 1 μF each are connected in series. A resistor with a resistance of $R = 100$ kOhm (Fig. 2) is connected in parallel to one of the capacitors. Find the thermal power.

A. Zilberman

## Solution

We connect two capacitors with a capacity of 1 μF each in series to an alternating current with a direction of 220 V and a frequency of 50 Hz. A resistor with resistance $R = 100 \text{ кОм}$ is connected in parallel to one of the capacitors (see Fig.ok). Find the thermal power.

◆ Capacitance resistance at a given frequency $f = 50$ Hz is equal to

$$Z_C = \frac{1}{2\pi f C} \approx 3 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Ом}.$$

Since the resistance of the resistor is $R = 10^6 \text{ Ом}$, that is

$$R \gg Z_C, \text{ можно считать, что } U_R \approx U/2 = 110 \text{ В (U — напряжение сети)}. \text{Takим образом, тепловая мощность равна}$$
$$P = U_R^2/R \approx 0,12 \text{ Вт.}$$

A. Zilberman
