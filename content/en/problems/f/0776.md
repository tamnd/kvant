## Condition

A small kettle is filled to the top with warm water ($t_1 = 30^{\circ}\text{C}$). The kettle remains at 1 degree for $\tau = 5$ min. To prevent the kettle from cooling down, hot water is dripped into it ($t_2 = 45^{\circ}\text{C}$). The mass of one drop is $m_k = 0,2$ g. How many drops per minute must be dripped into the kettle so that the temperature is maintained at $30^{\circ}\text{C}$? How many degrees does the water remain in one minute if you start dripping into the second cup?

Assume that the temperature of the water in the kettle equalizes very quickly. Excess water pours out of the spout. The kettle contains 0.3 liters of water. Ambient air temperature $t_0 = 20^{\circ}\text{C}$. (8)

A. Zilberman
