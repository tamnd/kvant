## Condition

A horizontal cylinder on one side of a fixed piston contains 1 mol of ideal gas. There is a vacuum in the other part of the cylinder. The spring located between the piston and the cylinder wall is in an undeformed state (Fig. 2). The cylinder is thermally conductive from the environment. The piston is released and after equilibrium is established, the volume occupied by the gas doubles. How will the temperature of the gas and its pressure change? The heat capacity of the cylinder, piston and spring is negligible. (9 grades)

E. I. Butikov, A. A. Bykov, A. S. Kondratev
