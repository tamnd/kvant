## Condition

A heavy hoop with weightless knitting needles is located in a vertical plane and can rotate around a horizontal axis passing through the center. A material point is fixed in the thickness of its rim, having the same mass as the hoop itself. What will be the period of oscillation of the hoop around its axis? How will it change if: a) the motherboard is moved to the Moon, b) placed in a liquid in which it will move without friction?
