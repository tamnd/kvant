## Condition

On a rough horizontal surface of the table there is a system consisting of a prism of mass m, equipped with a light block, and a block of mass M = 2m (Fig. 4). Lateral surface of the prism

is smooth, and the angle between the face on which the block is located and the table surface is equal to D (sin D 3 5 ). A light inextensible thread is tied to the block, thrown over a block, to the opposite end of which a horizontal force F is applied. It is known that the acceleration of the prism does not depend on the magnitude of this force. 1) Determine the possible values ​​of the friction coefficient P between the prism and the table surface. 2) For each of the values ​​of P obtained in step 1, find the acceleration of the prism a. The acceleration due to gravity is g. The inclined section of the thread is parallel to the left side face of the prism. There is no friction in the block axis. I. Yudin
