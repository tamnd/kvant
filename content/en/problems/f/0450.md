## Condition

A nucleus of mass $m$, flying at a speed $v_0$, disintegrates in flight into two identical shards. Determine the maximum possible angle between the velocity vector of one of the sedges and the vector $v_0$, if during the decay of the sedge nuclei at rest they have a speed of $v<v_0$.

## Solution

A nucleus of mass m, light with a speed of $v_0$, decays into two identical fragments. Let us determine the maximum possible angle between the velocity vector of one of the debris and the vector $v_0$, if during the decay of the resting nucleus of the debris we have a speed of $|\vec{v}| < |\vec{v}_0|$.
