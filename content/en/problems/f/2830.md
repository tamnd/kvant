## Condition

Charge Q is distributed uniformly over the area of ​​a non-conducting sphere of radius R. In the first experiment, a small ball with charge q was placed at a distance 2R from the center of the sphere. The force with which the field of the charged sphere acted on the ball was equal to $F_{1}$. In the second experiment, the ball was removed, and the same charge q was evenly distributed along the length of a thin non-conducting rod of length R. This rod was placed on a straight line passing through the center of the charged sphere. The point of the rod closest to the center of the sphere was at a distance of 2R from the center of the sphere. Find the force $F_{2}$ with which the field of the sphere acted on the charged rod. All experiments were carried out in a vacuum far from other bodies. Consider all forces except Coulomb forces to be negligible. V.Plis
