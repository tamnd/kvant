## Condition

A wheel of mass $m$ can slide along a rod of length $L$. The friction force between them is $F$. Determine what minimum speed $v_0$ must be imparted to the rod in order for it to fly through the ring if the ring first collapses.

The experiment is carried out in zero gravity.

## Solution

A ring of mass $m$ can slide along a rod of length $L$. The strength of the claim between him is $F$. Determine what minimum speed $v_0$ the rod needs to communicate so that it crashes through the flask if the flask breaks first.

The experiment is carried out in zero gravity.

The solution problem is exactly the same as problem F228 (see «Kvant», 1974, No. 5). That's why

$$v_0 = \sqrt{2LF \frac{M + m}{Mm}},$$

where $M$ is the mass of the rod.
