## Condition

A boy slowly climbs a snowy hill and drags a sled behind him on a rope. The rope is always parallel to the tangent to the slide at the point where the boy is. The top of the slide is located at a height H and at a distance l from its base (Fig. 3). How much work will the boy do when lifting the sled up the hill? The mass of the sled is m, the friction coefficient of the sled on the snow is μ.

A. I. Buzdin
