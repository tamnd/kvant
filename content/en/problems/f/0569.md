## Condition

Using an electric boiler with a power of $W=500$ W, water is heated in a saucepan. In two minutes, the water temperature increased from $85^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ to $90^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$. Then the boiler was turned off and in one minute the water temperature dropped by one degree. How much water is in the pan? The specific heat capacity of water is $4,19 \cdot 10^{3}$ J/(kg K).

E. Surkov
