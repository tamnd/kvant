## Condition

A car with a mass of $m = 1200$ kg, the brake with the gear off, rolls down at a constant speed along an inclined section of the highway with an inclination angle of $\alpha$ ($\sin \alpha = \frac{1}{14}$). Each of the four wheels automatically with equal force $N$ brake pads $A$ and $A'$ (Fig. 2). Find $N$ if the sliding friction coefficient between the barbacon and the pads is $k = 0,4$. There is no slippage between the tires and the highway.

V. I. Chuvilev

## Solution

A car weighing m=1200 kg, braking with the gear off, rolls at a constant speed along an inclined section of the highway with an inclination angle α (sin α = $\frac{1}{14}$). Each of the four wheels automatically has an outer radius R and is rigidly attached to a brake drum of radius r = $\frac{5}{12}$ R, to which brake pads A and A' are pressed with equal force N (see figure). Start N if the sliding friction coefficient between the drum and the pads is k=0.4. There is no slippage between the tires and the highway.
