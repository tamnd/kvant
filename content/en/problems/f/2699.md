## Condition

Four identical rods AE, ED, DB and DC are connected as shown in $рисунке^{4.}$ At the connection points, thermal contact between the rods is ensured. The temperatures of points A, B and C are maintained at $t_{A} t, t_{B}^{2} t, t_{C}^{3} t$ respectively. Find the temperature of point D. It is known that the heat flow through the rod depends on its length, cross-sectional area and material and is proportional to the temperature difference between its ends. The heat flow through the side surfaces of the rods can be neglected.
