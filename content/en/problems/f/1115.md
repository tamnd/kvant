## Condition

A closed vessel filled with gas is divided into two parts by an impenetrable horizontal piston; the mass of the vessel is $m$, the mass of the piston is $M$. First, the vessel will end on the stand. Then the stand is only knocked out from under the vessel. With what acceleration does the vessel begin to move? Friction between the walls of the vessel and the piston claims.

S. F. Kim, A. I. Latvinin

## Solution

A closed vessel filled with gas is divided into two parts by an impenetrable horizontal piston; the mass of the vessel is $m$, the mass of the piston is $M$. First, the vessel will end on the stand. We fly and knock the stand out from under the vessel. With what acceleration does the vessel begin to move? Neglect the friction between the walls of the vessel and the piston.
