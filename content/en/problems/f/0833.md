## Condition

On the conveyor belt, receiving at a speed of $v_0=1$ m/s, a box is placed on the side. The speed of the box immediately after hitting the tape is $u_0=2$ m/s and is perpendicular to the speed of the tape. What is the minimum speed relative to the Earth that the box will have while moving? The treming force is strong enough and the box does not slip off the belt. (8)

S. S. Krotov
