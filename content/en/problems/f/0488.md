## Condition

A rectangular wire frame with side dimensions $a = 0,020$ m and $b = 0,030$ m is immersed in soapy water, due to which a soap film is formed on it. When observed in reflected light, the angle of incidence of which is $\alpha = 30^\circ$, the film appears green ($\lambda = 5 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m).

1) Is it possible to determine the mass of this film using a balance that is accurate to 0.1 mg? The density of the soap solution is $\rho = 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, the refractive index of the film is $n = 1,33$.

2) What color will the thinnest film that satisfies the conditions of the problem appear if light is incident on it and then reflected perpendicular to the film?

Note. Take into account that when light is reflected from a denser medium, the phase of the wave changes abruptly by l.
