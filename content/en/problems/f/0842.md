## Condition

In the circuit shown at figure, at some points the keys $K$ are closed. Find the set value of the point flowing through the coils with inductance $L$ and active resistance $r$. What charge will flow through the galvanometer $\Gamma$ after the switch is closed? The parameters of the elements indicated on figure are considered given. Neglect the internal resistance of the source and the resistance of the galvanometer.

In steady state, when the circuit is running a constant current and the inductor has only active resistance $r$, the circuit will be symmetrical. In this case, the current through the galvanometer is zero, and the points in the right and left part of the branched section of the circuit will be equal to each other and equal to $\frac{\varepsilon}{(R+r)}$. Therefore, in steady state, the current through the coil will be equal.

$$I_L(\infty) = \frac{\varepsilon}{(R+r)}$$

Let now, at some arbitrary moment, points also flow through the elements of the circuit, as shown in figure. Since the resistance of the galvanometer is zero, for any finite values ​​of the point through the galvanometer is zero, for any finite values ​​of the point through the galvanometer is zero, and therefore, at any instant of time, the points $I_1$ and $I_2$ will be equal.

According to the law of conservation of charge,

$$I_1 = I_\Gamma + I_L,$$

$$I = I_1 + I_2 = 2I_1 = I_L + I_3$$

According to Ohm's law for the circuit abcd,

$$I_L r - I_3 r = -L \frac{dI_L}{dt}$$

From equation (3) and (2) we obtain:

$$I_1 - I_L = \frac{L}{2r} \frac{dI_L}{dt}$$

Using equation (1), we find the connection between point $I_\Gamma$ through the galvanometer and current $I_L$ through the inductors:

The charge flowing through the galvanometer is very convenient

$$Q = \int_0^\infty I_\Gamma dt = \int_0^\infty \frac{L}{2r} \frac{dI_L}{dt} dt = \frac{L}{2r} \int_0^\infty dI_L =$$

$$= \frac{L}{2r} \left[ I_L(\infty) - I_L(0) \right] = \frac{L}{2r(R+r)}.$$

Here $I_L(0)$ is the current through the coil at the initial time. Before the key was closed, the current $I_L$ was zero; therefore, after closure it will also be equal to zero: $I_L(0) = 0$.
