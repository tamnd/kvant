## Condition

The luminous point is located on the main optical axis at a distance of $d = 40$ cm from the diverging lens with a focal length of $F = 10$ cm. The point is shifted to a distance of $h = 5$ cm in a plane perpendicular to the main optical axis. How much and where should the lens be moved so that the image of the luminous point returns to its old position?

Leningrad City Olympiad in Physics, 1979
