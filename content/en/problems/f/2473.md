## Condition

On the horizontal surface of the table lies an elongated chain with a uniform distribution of mass along its length. The chain is located perpendicular to the edge of the table. The tabletop has a rounded edge with a radius of curvature that is significantly smaller than the length of the chain, but significantly larger than the dimensions of the chain links. Attached to the end of the chain closest to the edge of the table is a weightless thread, holding the chain very tightly
