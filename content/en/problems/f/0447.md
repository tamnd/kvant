## Condition

A platinum wire is stretched along an axis passing through the center of two rigidly connected concentric cylinders and covered with a layer of segebra (Fig. 5). When current passes through the wiring, it heats up and the segebro layer evaporates. Through a narrow slot in the inner cylinder, part of the segebral atoms flies into the outer cylinder and is deposited on its inner surface.

The radius of the outer cylinder is $R=30$ cm, the radius of the inner cylinder is negligible compared to $R$; the width of the slot in a small cylinder is $\delta=1$ mm. Vapor temperature segebra $T=1000^\circ\text{K}$, vapor density $\rho=10^{-5}\text{e/cm}^3$.

The cylinders rotate at $n=10$ revolutions per second. Find the distribution of the surface of the segebra plane in the layer deposited on the inner surface of the large cylinder in a time of $t=1$ sec.
