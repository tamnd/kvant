## Solution

The charged part is released into the environment, where it is acted upon by a resistance force proportional to the speed. Before a complete stop, the part travels a distance of $L = 10$ cm. If the medium has some magnetic field perendicular to the velocity of the particle, at the same initial speed it will stop at a distance of $l_1 = 6$ cm from the entry point in the medium.

In the presence of a magnetic field, a particle in a medium is acted upon by two mutually perpendicular and proportional velocities of the particle. This is the friction force $\vec{F}_{\text{тр}}$ —

$$\vec{F}_{\text{тр}} = kv = k\Delta s/\Delta t$$

and Lorentz force $\vec{F}_{\text{л}} = qBv = qB\Delta s/\Delta t.$
