## Condition

There is an oven in which the temperature is constantly maintained at $T_1$, and a refrigerator in which the temperature is $T_0 < T_1$. A reel with a length of wire wound on it is installed in the refrigerator. The end of the wire comes out of the refrigerator, enters the oven and is attached to a reel installed there. The coils are rotated so that the wire is washed from the cold coil to the hot one, moving at a speed of $v$. Wire cross-section $s$, volumetric heat $c$. To keep the temperatures $T_1$ and $T_0$ unchanged, it was necessary to increase the oven power by $\Delta E_x$ and reduce the cooling capacity of the refrigerator by the same amount. Find $\Delta E_x$. Assume that the heat exchange between the wire and the environment does not depend on the speed of the wire.

D. V. Pavlov
