## Condition

After the football player hit, the ball flew towards the goal, located at a distance of $L=32$ m, at a speed of $v=25$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha$ to the horizon ($\cos \alpha=0,8$). Due to the side wind blowing along the goal perpendicular to the speed $\vec{v}$, the horizontal displacement of the ball in the plane of the goal turned out to be equal to $s=2$ m. Find the time of flight of the ball to the plane of the goal. The ball does not spin; wind speed $u=10$ m/s.

V. I. Chivilev

## Solution

After the soccer player hit, the ball flew in the direction of the goal, located at a distance of $L = 32$ m, at a speed of $v = 25$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha$ to the horizon ($\cos \alpha = 0,8$). Due to the side wind, which is along about perpendicular to the speed $\vec{v}$, the horizontal displacement of the ball in the plane of the goal turned out to be equal to $s = 2$ m. Find the time of flight of the ball to the plane of the goal. The ball cannot be repaired; wind speed $u = 10$ m/s.

♦ Consider the motion of a ball in the reporting system $K$, moving with speed $\vec{u}$. Let us associate with $K$ a rectangular coordinate system $XYZ$ with the origin at the impact point $A$ such that the plane $XY$ is horizontal and the axis $X$ is parallel to the plane of the goal $P$ (see figure).

The initial speed of the ball in this system

$$\vec{v}_1 = \vec{v} - \vec{u}.$$

In the system $K$ the air is motionless, and the trajectory of the ball $ABC$ will be in the vertical plane in which the vector $\vec{v}_1$ lies, and the trajectory will not be a parabola, since in addition to the gravitational force, the ball is also affected by the resistance force from the air, directed against the instantaneous speed of the ball. Let us immediately note that the nature of the dependence of the resistance force on the speed is not given in the problem and is not required for the solution.

During the flight time $t$ to the goal plane, the ball will move in the system $K$ along the axis $Y$ to a distance $L$, and along the axis $X$ to a distance $s_1$, and (see figure; point $C$ lies on the plane $P$)

$$\frac{s_1}{L} = \frac{u}{v \cos \alpha},$$

where

$$s_1 = \frac{Lu}{v \cos \alpha}.$$

(1)

During the same time $t$ the system $K$ will shift along the axis $X$ in the opposite direction by a distance

$$s_2 = ut.$$

(2)

The actual horizontal displacement of the ball (in

⟦folio 50⟧

visual reporting system) occurs in the negative direction of the $X$ axis and is equal to
$$s = s_2 - s_1.$$
Taking into account (1) and (2), we obtain the equation for finding $t$:
$$s = ut - \frac{Lu}{v \cos \alpha},$$
where
$$t = \frac{L}{v \cos \alpha} + \frac{s}{u}.$$
Substituting the numerical values ​​gives $t = 1,8$s.
