## Condition

The two charged particles were initially identical in size and direction. After a uniform electrostatic field was turned on for some time, the velocity vector of one of the particles turned to $60^\circ$, and the numerical value of the velocity decreased by half. the velocity vector of the other particle has rotated by $90^\circ$. How many times did the numerical value of the speed of the second particle change? Determine the charge-to-mass ratio for the second particle if it is $k_1$ for the first particle. (9th grade)
