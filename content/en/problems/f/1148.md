## Condition

A cylinder with a thread wound around it, the second end of which is fixed, is placed on a smooth inclined plane making an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon, as shown in figure 1. At the moment when the thread was vertical, the angular velocity of rotation of the cylinder was equal to $\omega$. Determine what is equal at this moment: a) the speed of the cylinder axis; b) the speed of a point on the cylinder touching the inclined plane. The radius of the cylinder is $R$.

S. S. Krotov
