## Condition

Two loaded dielectric threads of infinite length are located in space like two crossing perpendicular straight lines. Linear density of bobbins on threads σ. Find the force of interaction between the threads. Assume that the threads are very thin and redistribution of the bobbins will not occur.

I. V. Shutovsky, 10th grade student.

$$F = \sum f = f \frac{a}{\Delta x} = f \frac{l}{\Delta x} = fl \frac{\rho}{\sigma}.$$

$$f = 2\pi k \sigma^2 = 2\pi \frac{1}{4\pi \varepsilon_0} \sigma^2 = \frac{\sigma^2}{2\varepsilon_0}.$$
