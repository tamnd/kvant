## Condition

Zadchaa N. E. Zhukovsky. The beetle crawls along a hard (impermeable) straw resting on a smooth floor and a smooth vertical wall (Fig. 1). The straw is homogeneous, its length is $l$, its weight is $m$; beetle mass $M$ ($M \gg m$). The straw makes an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon. The initial speed of the beetle at point $B$ was equal to $v_0$. How should the beetle move so that the straw remains motionless? How does the beetle's acceleration depend on the distance it travels along the straw? How long does it take the beetle to reach the bottom point? Will the beetle be able to climb the straw from point $A$ to point $B$?
