## Condition

Initially, an uncharged capacitor embone $C$ will connect to a series-connected battery with voltage $U_0$ and a coil with inductance $L$. The current through the coils first increases and then decreases. At the moment when the current becomes zero, the capacitor is disconnected from the circuit and connected again, swapping its outputs. What is the maximum current that will flow through the coils after this? How will this current change if the switching process is repeated 1984 times? Neglect circuit resistance.

A. R. Zilberman
