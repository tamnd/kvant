## Condition

In a magnetic field, the induction $\vec{B}$ of which is vertical, the metal rod rotates about the vertical axis as shown in figure 4. The angular velocity of rotation is $\omega$, $R$ and $\alpha$ given. Find the potential difference at the ends of the rod.

L. G. Markovich

## Solution

In a magnetic field, the induction $\vec{B}$ of which is vertical, a metal rod is drawn near the vertical axis as shown in figure. The conditional speed of the doctor is $\omega$, $R$ and $\alpha$ are given. Find the potential difference at the ends of the rod.

To solve, we use the expression for the EMF in the form

$$\mathcal{E} = -\frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}.$$

First of all, we note that the emf will be constant at any time, since the rod is always in the same conditions. Therefore, we have the right to consider its full turn:

$$-\Delta\Phi = \pi R^2 B,$$

$$\Delta t = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$$

$$\mathcal{E} = \frac{\pi R^2 B}{2\pi} \omega = \frac{1}{2} BR^2 \omega,$$

that is, it depends only on $R$ and not on $\alpha$.
