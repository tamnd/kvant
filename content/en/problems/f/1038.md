## Condition

When identical inextensible threads are attached at equal distances from each other to a ring of radius r₀ and similarly to a ring of radius 2r₀. The threads are passed through a triangle of radius r₀ (Fig. 3). Ring 1 is fixed in a horizontal plane, and all systems are located. Find the distance between the centers of rings 2 and 3. All rings are made of the same wire. Neglect training.
V. P. Borodin
