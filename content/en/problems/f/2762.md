## Condition

The teacher inflated the rubber shell of the balloon with air from his lungs and tied the neck of the shell with a knot. The ball contained 10.0 liters of air at a temperature of 20 °C and a pressure that was approximately 4% greater than atmospheric pressure. Then they began to carefully pour liquid nitrogen from a thermos onto the ball. The liquid well wetted the entire surface of the ball, and as it cooled, it significantly decreased in volume. Estimate, as accurately as possible, the minimum volume of the ball when watering continues. Information about the composition of the gas mixture in the ball can be found on the Internet.
