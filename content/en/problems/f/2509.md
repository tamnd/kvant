## Condition

An open car without a roof (railway workers call them gondola cars) with a height H = 4.5 m moves at a constant speed u = 6 m/s. As soon as the carriage passed the loader, he immediately tried to throw a small bag over the back wall of the carriage. What minimum speed v (in m/s) must be imparted to the bag? It is known that when throwing a bag, the loader “releases” it at a height h = 1 m from the ground level. Gravity acceleration g = 9.8 m $с^{2}$. Neglect air resistance. A. Kipriyanov
