## Condition

A chain of mass $m$ and length $l$ is placed on the main circular cone with an apex angle $2\alpha$. The cone together with the chain rotates with an angular velocity $\omega$ around a vertical axis coinciding with the symmetry axis of the cone. The plane of the chain is horizontal. Find the tension of the chain.
