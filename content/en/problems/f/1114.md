## Condition

The stuntman falls from a height of $H=50$ m. A rubber rope is attached to him, the second end of which is fixed at the starting point. The length and rigidity of the shura are selected so that the speed is reduced to zero near the ground. After the vibrations have died down, the stuntman hangs at a height of $h=10$ m above the ground. What was the stuntman's maximum speed during the fall? Ignore air compliance.

D. V. Pavlov
