## Condition

There are two conductors $A$ and $B$ of arbitrary shape. Initially, conductor $A$ has a charge $Q$, but conductor $B$ is not charged. The conductors come into contact (Fig. 3), and charge $q$ flows onto the conductor $B$. An additional charge $q_x$ was imparted to the contacting conductor, and as a result, a charge $q$ appeared on the conductor $A$. Determine the charge $q_x$.
