## Condition

The wooden raft was pushed away from the river bank so that at the initial moment its speed was equal to v and directed perpendicular to the bank. The trajectory of the raft is shown at figure 2. The cross on the trajectory marks the place where the raft is located for time T after the start of movement. Assuming the river speed to be constant and equal to u, find the graphical points of the trajectory at which the raft is located at times 2T, 3T, 4T.

I. Yu. Poteryayko
