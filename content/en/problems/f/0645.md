## Condition

The loudspeaker has a diffuser with a cross-sectional plane $S = 300$ cm$^2$ and a mass $m = 5$ g. The resonant part of the diffuser is $v_0 = 50$ Hz. What is its resonant part if you place the loudspeaker on the wall of a closed box with a volume of $V = 40$ l (Fig. 2). The calculation is carried out under the assumption that the air temperature inside the box does not change when the diffuser oscillates. (10)

A. Zilberman
