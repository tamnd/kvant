## Condition

A smooth L-shaped rod is rotated in a horizontal plane around a vertical axis passing through the end of the rod $O$. A small mass coupling $m$ is attached to the rod at point $A$ by a spring of stiffness $k$ (Fig. 4). The length of the spring is 1.2 times its length in the unstretched state. At what angular velocity does the rod rotate?
