## Condition

A conductor whose resistance depends on temperature is connected to a constant voltage source $U$. The resistance of the conductor changes depending on the temperature according to the law $R = R_0(1 - \mu t)$, where $R_0$ is the resistance at $t = 0^\circ C$, $\mu > 0$. Determine the steady-state temperature of the conductor if the ambient temperature is $0^\circ C$, and the thermal power released by the conductor into the environment is $W = b \cdot \Delta t$, where $\Delta t$ is the temperature difference between the conductor and the environment. Neglect the change in conductor dimensions due to thermal expansion.

R. Yu. Vinokur
