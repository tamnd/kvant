## Condition

When the outside temperature is $t_0 = -20^\circ C$, a working battery maintains the room temperature $t_1 = 16^\circ C$. When, in addition to the battery, the power supply with a power of $P_1 = 1$ kW was turned on, the temperature in the room was equal to $t_2 = 22^\circ C$. Determine the thermal power of the battery.

When marking e. Body transfer from one body to another is proportional to the temperature difference between these bodies ($\Delta P = k\Delta t$, $k = \text{const}$).

## Solution

When the temperature is $t_0 = -20^\circ C$, the battery operates to maintain the room temperature $t_1 = 16^\circ C$. When, in addition to the battery, a power supply of $P_1 = 1$ kW was turned on, the temperature in the room was set at $t_2 = 22^\circ C$. Determine the thermal power of the battery.

When.mechanie. Heat transfer from one body to another is proportional to the temperature difference of these bodies ($\Delta P = k\Delta t, k = const$).

At the set temperature in the room, the thermal energy released by the devices is equal to the heat losses (heat loss losses). Therefore, the thermal power of the battery is equal to

$$P = k(t_1 - t_0).$$

With power on and battery running

$$P + P_1 = k(t_2 - t_0).$$

From (1) and (2) we find the warm power of the battery:

$$P = P_1 \frac{t_1 - t_0}{t_2 - t_1} = 6 \text{ кВт}.$$
