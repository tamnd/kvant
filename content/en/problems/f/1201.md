## Condition

There is a nonlinear electronic device $\tilde{R}$. Fig. 2 shows a graph of the dependence of the current through the device on the voltage across it (in sections 1-2 and 3-4 the overlap of the graph is very large). The states are assembled from $\tilde{R}$, a coil with inductance $L$ and a battery with an emf equal to $U_0$ (the device is turned on with the “correct” polarity corresponding to the graph). Plot a graph of the current in the circuit versus time and find the period of current oscillations.

K. S. Vedoye
