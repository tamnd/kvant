## Condition

There is a square metal frame $ABCD$ (Fig. 2) in a magnetic field of direct rectilinear current. The frame is moved to a new position, shown in figure by the dotted line. This can be done in two ways: an even repeat around the $CD$ side, or an even parallel carry along the $AD$ side. Which method will produce more tell if the time it takes to move the frame to a new position is the same in both cases?

L. E. Shmits
