## Condition

Currently, the power of all energy sources on Earth used by mankind is $\Delta P = 10^{13}$ W, and the power of solar energy entering the Earth is $P_0 = 10^{17}$ W. What overheating $\Delta T$ of the Earth's surface does earthly energy sources lead to? What is the maximum permissible value $\Delta P_{\max}$, if the maximum superheat from environmental messages should not exceed the value $\Delta T_{\max} = 0,1$ K? It is known that the energy emitted per unit time by a heated body increases 16 times with an increase in the absolute temperature of the body by 2 times. (10)

S. Kozel

## Solution

Currently, the power of all energy sources on Earth that use humans is ΔP = 10^{13} W, and the power of solar energy entering the Earth is P0 = i\theta^{17} W. To what overheating ΔT

Let us assume that the power P emitted by a heated body is related to the absolute temperature T of the degree of dependence P = σT^n, where σ are some proportionality coefficients. Then for two temperatures of the radiating body T1 and T2 we obtain:

$$P_1 = \sigma T_1^n, \quad P_2 = \sigma T_2^n, \quad \frac{P_1}{P_2} = \left(\frac{T_1}{T_2}\right)^n.$$

Are the surfaces of the Earth driven by terrestrial energy sources? What is the maximum permissible value $\Delta P_{\text{max}}$ if the maximum superheat from environmental messages should not exceed the value $\Delta T_{\text{max}} = 0,1$ K? It is known that the energy emitted per unit time by a body increases 16 times with an increase in the absolute temperature of the body by 2 times.

From here it is easy to obtain, using the data of the problem, that $n = 4$.

So, $P = \sigma T^4$. Now you can write:

$$P_0 + \Delta P = \sigma (T_0 + \Delta T)^4 \approx \sigma T_0^4 (1 + 4 \frac{\Delta T}{T_0} + \dots).$$

Since $\Delta T/T_0 \ll 1$, terms of the second and higher orders can be discarded in the last expression. Then we get:

$$\Delta P = 4P_0 \frac{\Delta T}{T_0}.$$

From here is:

$$\Delta P = \frac{\Delta P}{4P_0} T_0 \approx 10^{-2} \text{ K},$$

$$\Delta P_{\text{max}} = 4P_0 \frac{\Delta T_{\text{max}}}{T_0} \approx 10^{14} \text{ Bt}.$$

Thus, the power of all energy sources on Earth used by humans can be increased by no more than 10 times. (This, of course, does not apply to sources using solar energy.)
