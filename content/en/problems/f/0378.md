## Condition

Airplane passengers did not experience any nuisance unless their weight more than doubled in flight. What is the maximum acceleration in level flight allowed by this condition?

V. Smirnitsky

## Solution

Airplane passengers did not experience indirect assessments unless their weight more than doubled during the flight. What is the maximum acceleration in level flight allowed by this condition?**

An airplane passenger flying with horizontal acceleration $a$ is acted upon by two forces from the side of the plane: horizontal force $ma$ and vertical force $mg$, compensating for the force of gravity. Forces of the same absolute magnitude, but directed in opposite directions, act (according to Newton’s third law) from the passenger side to the vehicle. The absolute value of the resulting force acting on the support, i.e., the weight of the passenger, is

$$P = \sqrt{(ma)^2 + (mg)^2}.$$

According to the conditions of the problem

$$P \leq 2mg.$$

From here

$$a \leq \sqrt{3}g.$$

S. Kozel
