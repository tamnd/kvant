## Condition

In some galaxies, a system of planets similar to our solar system has been discovered. The average densities of the planets and the Sun in this system are two times less than the average densities of the planets and the Sun in our system, and the sizes of all celestial bodies are three times less than the sizes of the corresponding bodies in our system. How many Earth days does a year last on the discovered analogue of the Earth?
