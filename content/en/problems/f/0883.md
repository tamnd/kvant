## Condition

When a stationary anti-aircraft shell ruptures, it disintegrates into a very large number of fragments flying uniformly in all directions at a speed of $v_0$. Such a projectile, flying down at a speed of $u$, explodes at a height of $H$ above the ground. When will fragments fall to the ground most often?

I. S. Solodovnikov
