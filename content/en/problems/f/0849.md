## Condition

A dry thermometer in the ischrometer room shows a temperature of $t_1 = 18,0^\circ\text{C}$, and a wet thermometer shows a temperature of $t_2 = t_1 - \Delta t = 15,0^\circ\text{C}$. Do not use a table; determine the relative humidity in the room. The pressure of saturated water vapor at the indicated temperatures is, respectively, $p_1 = 15,5$ mm Hg. Art. and $p_2 = 12,8$ mmHg. Art. The molar temperature of water evaporation is $l = 4,07 \times 10^4$ J/mol; The specific heat of the air is $c = 20,8$ J/(mol • deg) (at constant volume).

I. S. Solodovnikov
