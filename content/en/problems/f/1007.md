## Condition

A ray of light falls on a ball composed of two hemispheres, which are made of glasses with refractive indices $n_1$ and $n_2$ ($n_1 > n_2$). The ball begins to rotate with angular velocity $\omega$ around an axis perpendicular to the direction of the beam (Fig. 4). Find the maximum size of the light spot on the screen mounted on the ball.

L. G. Markovich
