## Condition

The insulated vessel is divided into two parts by a light piston. On the left side of the vessel there is $m_1=3$ g of hydrogen at a temperature of $T_1=300$ K, on ​​the right side - $m_2=16$ g of oxygen at a temperature of $T_2=400$ K. The piston conducts the body weakly, and the temperature in the vessel gradually equalizes. What is the amount of heat from the classord by the time the piston stops moving?
