## Condition

At a certain point in space, it is necessary to create the maximum possible directional field (gravitational acceleration), having at disposal a given mass of matter of constant density.

a) What form should the substance be given?
b) What should be the relative position of the body and the point?

E. N. Yunosov, I. V. Yaminsky
