## Condition

The capillary is made of two thin glass tubes with internal diameters $d_1$ and $d_2$ (Fig. 2). Introduce a large drop of water with a mass of $M$ into it. When the capillary was placed horizontally, the entire drop “crept” into the thin part, and when it was placed vertically, all the water flowed out of it. At what angles between the axis of the capillary and the vertical will the drop be located partly in a thick and partly in a thin tube? Water surface tension coefficient $\sigma$, water density $\rho$. Wetting is considered complete.

R. A. Tillo
