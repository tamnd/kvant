## Condition

In a cylinder under a weightless piston of area $S$ there is air at atmospheric pressure $p_0$ and temperature $T_0$. The internal volume of the cylinder is divided into two equal parts by a fixed horizontal partition with a small response. A load of mass $M$ is placed on the piston, under the influence of which the piston reaches the partition. Find the temperature of the air inside the cylinder if the cylinder walls and the piston do not conduct heat.

B. A. Lugovtsovo

## Solution

In the cylinder under the weightless piston with planes $S$ there is air at atmospheric pressure $p_0$ and temperature $T_0$. The internal volume of the cylinder is divided into two equal parts by a fixed horizontal partition with a small deviation. We place a mass weight $M$ on the piston, under the action of which the piston reaches the partition. The least $T_1$ air in the cylinder, if the cylinder walls and the piston do not conduct menlo.

◆

Since the vessel is heat-plated, the change in the internal energy of the gas is equal to the work of external forces acting on the piston—gravity force $Mg$ and atmospheric pressure force $p_0S$:

$$\Delta U = (Mg + p_0S) h$$

(h is the piston stroke).

Since the internal energy of a gas depends only on temperature, then in any process the change in internal energy is the same as in a process occurring at constant volume, that is

$$\Delta U = C_V z \left( T_1 - T_0 \right).$$

Here $C_V$ is the heat content of one mole of gas at constant volume, $z$ is the number of moles of gas. Therefore in our case

$$\left( Mg + p_0S \right) h = C_V z \left( T_1 - T_0 \right).$$

From here

$$T_1 = T_0 + \frac{\left( Mg + p_0S \right) h}{C_V z}.$$

The number of moles of air in the cylinder can be found from the equation of state of the gas before the stage:

$$p_0V = zRT_0,$$

where

$$z = \frac{p_0V}{RT_0}.$$

Air consists mainly of diatomic gases - nitrogen and oxygen. Therefore we can count $C_V = \frac{5}{2} R$. Substituting the values ​​$z$, $C_V$ and $h = V/2S$ into (*), we finally find:

$$T_1 = T_0 \left( 1 + \frac{Mg + p_0S}{5p_0S} \right).$$
