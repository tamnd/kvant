## Condition

Rings $O$ and $O'$ are put on vertically fixed rods $AB$ and $A'B'$. An inextensible thread is tied to ring $O$, passed through ring $O'$ and secured at point $A'$ (Fig. 2). At the moment $\angle AOO' = \alpha$, ring $O'$ moves downward at speed $v$. Find the speed of the ring $O$ at this moment.
