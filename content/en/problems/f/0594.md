## Condition

Imagine what you find in a hot filled bathhouse, and there is frost outside the window. Where will the steam go if you open the window?

A. Varlamov

Fig. 2.

## Solution

Imagine that you are in a hot bathhouse, and there is frost outside the window. Where will the steam go if you open the window?

Steam will pour out both onto the street and inside the bathhouse. Indeed, in a bathhouse the content of water vapor in the air can be considered saturated. The dew point at high temperatures is higher than at low temperatures. If you open the window, the warm air flows out into the street, and the water vapor that it carries outside the window will condense in the cold. An observer from the street will see clouds of steam coming out through the window and rising upward. Cold air will penetrate through the window at the top of the bathhouse. Water vapor entering its jets from neighboring, warm layers of air will condense. This cold air is heavier than the surrounding layers of hot air, and watch upward as clouds of steam from the vent “fall” to the floor.

A. Varlamov
