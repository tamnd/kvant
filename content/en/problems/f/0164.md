## Condition

A foam cube of mass $M=100$ e lies on a horizontal stand. The height of the cube is $a=10$ cm. From below the cube is pierced by a vertical flying bullet with a mass of $m=10$ e (see Fig. 1). The speed of the bullet when entering the cube is $v_1=100$ m/s, when leaving it is $v_2=95$ m/s. Will the cube bounce? (8th, 9th grade)

A. R. Zilberman
