## Condition

In the simplest model, a star is considered as a gas ball in equilibrium in its own gravitational field. Assuming that the gas consists of completely infused hydrogen atoms and gel, estimate the temperature of the star. Star mass M, radius r; the relative content of hydrogen in the gas is n.
