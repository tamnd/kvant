## Condition

A voltmeter is connected to an ideal battery through a variable resistor. It shows voltage U = 3V. When the resistance of the variable resistor was reduced by three times, the voltmeter showed a voltage of 2U = 6V. What will the voltmeter show if the resistance of the resistor is doubled?
