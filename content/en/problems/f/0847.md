## Condition

An electron flies into a volumetric beam of positive noses, which has a circular cross-section, at point $A$, the speed of which is parallel to the symmetry axis of the beam (Fig. 3). The volume density of charges in the beam is equal to $\rho$, the length of the beam is $l$ (the length of the beam is much greater than its radius). At what values ​​of electron speed will it fly out of the beam at point $B$? Do not take into account the collision of the electron with nomi.
