## Condition

The screen is illuminated by a parallel point of rays perpendicular to the plane of the screen. How does the illumination of the screen change if in the path of light we place a face $ABC$ with a small angle $\alpha$ and a refractive index $n$, and the face $AB$ is parallel to the screen (Fig. 1)? Neglect the reflection of light from the sign.
