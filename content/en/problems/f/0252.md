## Condition

A rope is wound around a cylindrical pole. One of the ends of the rope is pulled with a force $F$. To prevent the rope from sliding along the table, when only one turn of the rope is wound on the pole, the second end of the rope must be held with a force of $f$. With what force should this end of the rope be held if $n$ of its turns are wound around the pole? How will the force $f$ change if we take a column twice its radius? (The force $f$ is independent of the thickness of the rope.)
