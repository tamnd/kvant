## Condition

An all-terrain vehicle can use either front or rear wheels as drive wheels. The driver wants to tow a three-lane load. What is the maximum traction force $T$ (without jerk) that an automobile can exert if the friction coefficient of the wheels on the road is $\mu=0,4$, the mass of the automobile is $M=2$ t, the distance between the centers of the wheels is $l=4$ m, the radius of the wheels is $R=0,3$ m? The front and rear axles are located in the same horizontal plane; the center of mass of the automobiles lies in this plane at an equal distance from the axes; the cable lies in the same plane. Which wheels should be driving? Does $T$ depend on this?

A. I. Buzdin
