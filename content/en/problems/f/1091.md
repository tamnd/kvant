## Condition

** A ball with mass $m$ and charge $q$ suspended on a drawn thread of length $l$ is located in the center of a hoop along which charge $Q$ (Fig. 3) is evenly distributed. The radius of the hoop is $R$, the charges $q$ and $Q$ are the same. Determine the frequency of small vibrations of the ball using the formula $(1+x)^n \approx 1 + \alpha x$ ($x \ll 1$).
