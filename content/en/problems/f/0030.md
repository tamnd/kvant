## Condition

The film camera and film projector produce 8 frames per second. An automatic vehicle with wheels, the actual diameter of which is 1 m, moves on the screen. The images of the wheels make 2 revolutions per second. What is the speed of the automatic?

G. L. Komkin

## Solution

The film camera and film projector produce 8 frames per second. On the movement screen there is an automatic vehicle with wheels, the actual diameter of which is 1 m. The images of the wheels make 2 revolutions per second. What is the speed of the automatic?

Since the images of the wheels are rotated by 2 turns during the time during which 8 frames pass through the projector, then on each frame the wheel must be rotated by $\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}$ turns compared to the previous one. Moreover, the images of the automatic vehicle wheels on the screen can rotate both “forward” and “backward,” although the automatic vehicle itself moves in both the first and the second, serving in the same direction (Fig. 5). The wheels on the screen rotate “forward” if the speed of movement of the automatic vehicle is such that during the time between frames $\tau = \frac{1}{8} c$ the wheels of the automatic vehicle make $n$ full and another $\frac{1}{4}$ revolutions around the axis. If in $t$ the wheels make $n$ full and $\frac{3}{4}$ revolutions around the axis, then the images of the wheels on the screen will rotate “backwards”. Thus, the angular speed of the automaton is either $\omega_{\text{вп}} = \frac{\left(n + \frac{1}{4}\right) 2\pi}{\frac{1}{8}} \frac{pa\partial}{c} = 16 \left(n + \frac{1}{4}\right) \pi \frac{pa\partial}{c}$ or $\omega_{\text{н}} = 16 \left(n + \frac{3}{4}\right) \pi \frac{pa\partial}{c}$. This means that the wheel axles, and with them the automatic vehicle, moves at a speed of $v = 16 \left(n + \frac{1}{4}\right) \pi R \frac{m}{c}$ (in which case the wheel images rotate “forward”) or at a speed of $u = 16 \left(n + \frac{3}{4}\right) \pi R \frac{m}{c}$ (in this case the wheel images rotate “backwards”). Substituting $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ into these formulas, we will get the answers

$$v_1 \approx 12,6 \frac{m}{c} = 45 \frac{km}{час}, \quad v_2 \approx 223 \frac{km}{час}, \quad u_1 \approx 136 \frac{km}{час}, \quad u_2 \approx 316 \frac{km}{час}, \dots$$

Moreover, since the automatic speed cannot be more than 140 $\frac{\kappa M}{\mu ac}$, it is equal to 45 $\frac{\kappa M}{\mu ac}$ if the wheel images are rotated “forward”, or 136 $\frac{\kappa M}{\mu ac}$ if the wheels on the screen are not rotated “backward”.
