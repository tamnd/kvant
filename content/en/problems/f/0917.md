## Condition

The object is located between the lens and a flat mirror, perpendicular to the main optical axis of the lens. The mirror, lens and object are enclosed in a housing made of translucent matte plastic (Fig. 2). Such a system creates two images of an object and an image of a lens. Both images of an object have the same dimensions, regardless of the distance between the lens and the object. What notification does the lens appear with?

E. P. Kuznetsov
