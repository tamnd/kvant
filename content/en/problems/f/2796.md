## Solution

A very long, thin-walled, fixed box made of non-conductive material. The cross section of the box is a square with side a. On the axis of symmetry of the box there is a very long thin rod with an electric charge evenly distributed along its length. Linear charge density V. Attached to the edge of the box is one end of a thin, non-conducting, weightless and inextensible thread, which is wound around the box, forming N identical turns. The planes of the turns are perpendicular to the axis of symmetry of the box. A point object (ball) with mass m and electric charge q is attached to the other end of the thread. At the initial moment, this object is located next to the place where the end of the thread is secured to the box and its speed at this moment is zero. This design is in a vacuum and gravity can be neglected. As an object moves, at some point it reaches its maximum speed. What is the magnitude of this speed and what is the tension in the thread at this moment?
