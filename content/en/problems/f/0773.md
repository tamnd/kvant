## Condition

*) On a rough inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\alpha$ lies a body to which a light, loose thread is tied. The free end of the thread is passed through a small hole in the plane. At the initial moment, the body lies on a plane so that the thread is horizontal (Fig. 3). The thread begins to be pulled out slowly; in this case, by the time it reaches the hole, the body describes half a circle. Find the coefficient of friction of the body on the plane. (10)

## Solution

On a rough inclined plane with an angle of inclination $a$ lies a body to which a lying inextensible thread is attached. The free end of the thread is passed through a small hole in the plane. At the initial moment, the body lies on a plane so that the thread is horizontal (Fig. 1). The thread begins to be pulled out slowly; in this case, by the time it reaches the hole, the body describes the irrigation circle. Find the friction coefficient of the body on the plane.
