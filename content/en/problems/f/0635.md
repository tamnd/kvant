## Condition

Two identical automatics with a mass of $m = 10^3$ kg are moving up the same mountain road. The dependence of the height $h$ of the road on the distance $x$ before the start of the climb is shown in figure 3. Each automatic vehicle constantly develops a power of $P = 12$ kW, and the force of resistance to the movement of the automatic vehicle is proportional to the square of its speed: $|\vec{F}| = \alpha|\vec{v}|^2$, where $\alpha = 1,4$ kg/m. On the last horizontal section of the road, the distance between the machines is 100 m. What was the minimum distance between the machines when they were moving?
