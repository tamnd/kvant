## Condition

When flying from the Mir station to the Salyut-7 station, which were in the same orbit, our cosmonauts slowed down their ship, moved to a lower orbit and, in a time of $t=30$ hours, landed on Salyut-7, which was flying ahead of Mir at a distance of $L=3000$ km. Assuming the orbits are circular, determine how many kilometers the intermediate orbit is below the main one? The orbital altitude is much less than the earth $R_3 \approx 6400$ km.

M. V. Semenov
