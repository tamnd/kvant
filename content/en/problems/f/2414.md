## Condition

A small block of mass m is located on a smooth horizontal surface at a distance $L_{0}$ from a vertical column on which a small weightless block with a fixed horizontal axis is fixed at a height h. A weightless, inextensible long thread is attached at one end to a block, thrown over a block and tensioned with a constant force F > mg. There is no friction in the block axis. At the initial moment, the block slides along the surface and has a speed $v_{0}$ directed away from the pillar. What will be the distance from the pillar to the block at the moment when the block stops for a moment? What will be the speed of the block at the moment when the block stops pressing on the surface? A. Starov
