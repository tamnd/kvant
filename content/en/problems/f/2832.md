## Condition

Three coherent monochromatic in-phase emitters are located in a vacuum far from other bodies at the vertices of an equilateral triangle with side a. The emitters operate at a frequency of Q 100 MHz. At what minimum length of the side of the triangle in the far zone on the continuation of any side of the triangle do the waves of three sources cancel each other? The speed of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum is c 3 $10^{8}$ m s. S. Varlamov, V. Plis
