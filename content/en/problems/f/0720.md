## Condition

A homogeneous beam of mass $M$ of length $l$ begins to move down an inclined plane making an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon. The initial section of length $l$ of the inclined plane is occupied by closely spaced rollers in the form of tubes of mass $m$ and radius $r \ll l$ (Fig. 5), which rotate without friction in the bearings. The rest of the inclined plane is smooth. Find the dependence of the acceleration of the load on its movement along the plane.

A. Stasenko

## Solution

A homogeneous block of mass M of length l begins to move down an inclined plane making an angle α with the horizon. The initial section of length l of the inclined plane is occupied by closely spaced rollers in the form of tubes of mass m and radius r << l (Fig. 1), which are repaired without friction in the bearings. The rest of the inclined plane of the boat. Find the relationship between the acceleration of the block and its displacement along the plane.

Let us write the equation of motion of the block for the moment of time when the movement of the block along the inclined plane is equal to x (x < l):

$$Ma_x = Mg \sin \alpha - f_{\tau p} N (1 - x/l),$$

(1)

where N = l/2r is the total number of rollers in a section of length l, N(1 - x/l) is the number of rollers with which the block is in contact at a given moment, f_{\tau p} is the absolute value of the friction force acting on the block from each roller.

To each roller in contact with the block, the friction force f_{\tau p} imparts tangential acceleration at a given moment
