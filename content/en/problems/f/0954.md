## Condition

A decorative square curtain measuring 1.5x1.5 m hangs on a cornice along a vertical wall. The lower edge of the curtain is raised flush with the top, so that the curtain is folded in half, and released. Find the dependence of the force acting on the cornice on time. The curtain is thin, smooth and weighs 3 kg.

S. S. Krotov
