## Condition

A block of mass $m_1$ lies on a board of mass $m_2$, which is on a smooth horizontal surface. The friction coefficient between the block and the board is $\mu$. A force $\vec{F}$ acts on the board, changing with time according to the law $\vec{F} |=bt$, where $b$ is a constant value. Draw graphs of the acceleration of the block and the board versus time $t$.
