## Condition

figure 2 shows the position of two points $A$ and $B$ and their images $A'$ and $B'$, which are given by a thin line. Find by constructing the position of the line and its foci.

XXIV Physical Olympiad for schoolchildren of the People's Republic of Poland

## Solution

On figure b the position of two points A and B and their images A' and B' are shown, along which the line is drawn. The optimal position of the line and its foci.

Fig. 6.

Rays passing without refraction from point A to point A' and from point B to point B' pass through the optical center of the line. Therefore, point O is the optical center of the line (see Fig. 6).

The ray passing through points A and B, after refraction in the face, passes through points A' and B'. Consequently, point M—the intersection point of rays AB and A'B'—lies in the plane of the line.

So, we have determined the position of the line. The main optical axis of the line is perpendicular to the plane of the line. Rays Aa and Bb, parallel to the main optical axis, pass through the focus of the line after refraction. Consequently, F and F' are the focus of the line, OO' is the main optical axis of the line.
