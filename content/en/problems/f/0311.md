## Condition

A homogeneous magnetic field changes in absolute value with a constant speed $k(B=kt)$. There is a piece of copper whose density is $d$ and resistivity $ρ$. The mass of the piece is $m$. From this piece you can draw a homogeneous wire, from which, in turn, you can make a closed loop. What is the maximum possible current in this circuit?

V. D. Kerzhantsev
