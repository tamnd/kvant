## Condition

At a distance $2F$ from the collecting line $\varLambda_1$ with focal length

⟦folio 27⟧

standing $F$ there is a luminous object. The illumination of a clear image of an object on the screen is equal to $E_0$. A diverging lens $\Pi_2$ with a focal length of -2$F$ was placed between the screen and lens $\Pi_1$. To obtain a clear image of the object, the screen had to be viewed at a distance equal to $F$. Determine the illumination of the image of the object in the second layer.

V. E. Belokukin

## Solution

Looking at 2F from the collecting line L1 with focal consideration F we find a luminous object. The illumination of the image of the object on the screen is equal to $E_0$. Between the screen and line L1, a scattering line L2 was placed with a focal length of 2F. To obtain an image of an object, the screen had to be switched to a consideration equal to F. Determine the illuminance of the image of the object in the second case.

From the conditions of the problem it follows that the image of the object created by lens L1 is located at a distance of 2F from it (this is easy to check using the lens formula). Let the diffuse lens L2 be placed at a distance x from the line L1 (Fig. 7). Obviously, to get a clear image, the screen had to move the data away from the line. The previous image created by lens L1 can be considered as a minimal object for line L2. Then the distance from the “object” to line L2 will be 2F - x, and the distance from the image to this line will be 3F - x. From the lens formula

$$-\frac{1}{2F-x} + \frac{1}{3F-x} = -\frac{1}{2F}$$

x can be found. Calculations give x = F.

Now let’s find the linear magnification of lens L2:

$$\Gamma = \frac{H}{h} = \frac{3F-x}{2F-x} = 2.$$
