## Condition

A car of mass M, mounted on springs with stiffness $k$, rolls along a horizontal section of track with a speed $v_0$. After passing a straight inclined section of track with a height difference H, making an angle α with the horizon (Fig. 2), the car acquires a speed of $v_x$. Determine the value of $v_x$, transforming by friction in the wheels. Consider that the period of oscillation of the varon on springs is much less than the time it takes to travel the inclined section of the track and much longer than the time it takes to travel the rounded section connecting the inclined and horizontal sections of the track; the springs “spring” only in the direction perpendicular to the plane of the rails.

I. P. Krylov
