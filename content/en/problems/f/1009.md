## Condition

A flat disk of radius R, located in a horizontal plane, begins to move between two vertical threads, the distance between which is R; the length of each thread is 2l, stiffness k, the ends of the threads are fixed (Fig. 3). Find the force acting on the disk from the side of one thread after time t, if at the initial moment the threads were unfastened and touched the disk, the disk is moving at a constant speed v. Neglect the thickness of the disk; do not take into account the training.

A. S. Rosenberg, 9th grade student.

## Solution

projecting onto the disk from the side of one thread after time t, if at the initial moment the threads were undistributed and touched the disk; The disk is moving at a constant speed v. Specify the thickness of the disc; do not take trenze into account.

Suppose that the right thread touches the disk at point $B_2$, which lies below point $B_1$ (Fig. 2, 6). It is obvious that the elasticity force $T$ of the thread is directed along the segment $BB_2$. This force has projections onto the tangent to the disk at point $B_2$ and onto the radius $OB_2$. From the disk side the thread is affected by

$$\text{В треугольнике } OB'B |OB'| = R, \quad |BB'| = vt,$$
$$\text{Так что } r = R - \sqrt{R^2 + v^2 t^2} - Rvt \sqrt{3}.$$

the reaction force $N$ is directed along the radius $OB_2$. Along the tangent, low forces from the disk do not act on the thread (there is no friction). Consequently, as a result of the action of force $T$ directed along $BB_2$, point $B_2$ of the thread will slide along the edge of the disk to point $B_1$. We would come to a similar conclusion if we assumed that the point of contact of the thread with the disk lies above point $B_1$. (Similar differences are also true for the left thread.)

Consequently, at any moment the threads touch the disk at points that lie on the radii passing through the points $A$ and $B$. Let us find the force $F$ acting on the disk from the side of the left thread at the moment when $|BB'| = vt$ (see Fig. 2, a).

Let the elongation of the thread at this moment be equal to $\Delta l$. Then, from each half of the thread, a force $T = k \cdot \Delta l$ acts on the disk at point $B_1$, and the total force with which the thread acts on the disk is equal to $F = 2T \sin \alpha = 2k \cdot \Delta l \cdot \sin \alpha$,

where $\alpha$ is the angle that the halves of the thread make with the vertical (Fig. 3). As can be seen from figure 3,

$$\sin \alpha = \frac{|bB_1|}{|BB_1|} = \frac{r}{l + \Delta l},$$
$$l + \Delta l = \sqrt{l^2 + r^2}, \quad \Delta l = \sqrt{l^2 + r^2} - l,$$

So

$$F = 2kr \frac{\sqrt{l^2 + r^2} - l}{\sqrt{l^2 + r^2}} = 2kr \left(1 - \frac{l}{\sqrt{l^2 + r^2}}\right),$$

where $r = R - \sqrt{R^2 - Rvt \sqrt{3} + v^2 t^2}$ (see Fig. 2, a).
