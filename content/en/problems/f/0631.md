## Condition

A wire spiral connected to a city network is charged with electric current. Half of the spiral begins to be cooled (for example, by a body of water). How does this affect the number of calves seen by this half of the spiral? the other half? Is everything a spiral?

Let us denote the resistance of each half of the spiral before the start of cooling as $R$. Since the resistance of metals depends on temperature (increases when heated and decreases when cooled), cooling one of the halves of the spiral leads to a decrease in the resistance of this half to a certain value $R_1 < R$. Since the mains voltage $U$ is unchanged, this will cause an increase in current in the circuit and, therefore, additional heating of the other half of the coil. The resistance of this half will increase and become equal to $R_2 > R$.

The new current value in circuit $I = \frac{U}{R_1 + R_2}$ will be greater than the previous value $I_0 = \frac{U}{2R}$. Indeed, suppose that $I \leq I_0$. Then the resistance of the uncooled half of the spiral remains the same or decreases ($R_2 \leq R$), and the resistance of the cooled half $R_1 < R$. Therefore, the total resistance is $R_1 + R_2 < 2R$ and the new current value is $I > I_0$, which contradicts our assumption.

So, $I > I_0$, and therefore the power released in the entire chain (that is, the number of calves per unit time) $P = IU$ will increase.

Since $I > I_0$ and $R_2 > R$, the amount of calf $P_2 = I^2R_2$ allocated to the uncooled half will also increase.

The power $P_1 = I^2R_1 = \frac{U^2R_1}{(R_1 + R_2)^2}$ released in the cooled half, on the contrary, will decrease compared to the initial power $P_0 = I^2R = \frac{U^2}{4R}$. Let's show it.

Let's find the ratio $P_0/P_1$:

$$\frac{P_0}{P_1} = \frac{U^2}{4R} : \frac{U^2R_1}{(R_1 + R_2)^2} = \frac{(R_1 + R_2)^2}{4R_1R}$$

It's obvious that

$$\frac{(R_1 + R_2)^2}{4R_1R} > \frac{(R_1 + R_2)^2}{4R_1R}$$

and the last expression is greater than or equal to 1, since the geometric mean of two quantities $(\sqrt{R_1R_2})$ does not exceed their arithmetic mean $(\frac{R_1 + R_2}{2})$.

Thus, indeed $P_1 < P_0$, that is, the power released in the cooled half of the spiral, will decrease.
