## Condition

Two solid balls of different sizes from one chemically pure substance are located in space far from other massive bodies and interact with each other only by gravitational means. They rotate around a common center of mass, making one revolution during one lesson T = 45 minutes. What is the minimum density of the material from which the balls are made? What metal does this density correspond to? A. Burov, E. Shalimova
