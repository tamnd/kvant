## Condition

Two bars with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ lie on a horizontal surface, connected by an undeformed spring. What is the smallest horizontal force $F$ that must be applied to one of the bars in order to move the second block? The friction coefficient of the bars on the surface is $k$.

G. Kotkin
