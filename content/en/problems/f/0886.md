## Condition

Along two parallel metal guides, inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon and located at a distance $b$ from each other, the mass bridge $m$ can slide without metal friction. The guides are closed to an uncharged capacitor emkosti $C$, and the entire structure is in a magnetic field, the induction $\vec{B}$ of which is directed vertically (Fig. 2). At the initial moment, the jumper is held at a distance $l$ from the base of the “slide”. How long does it take for the jumper to reach the base of the “slide” after it is released? What speed will it have at the base? The resistance of the conductors is a problem.

A. N. Buzdin
