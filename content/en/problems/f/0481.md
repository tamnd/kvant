## Condition

In an old battery consisting of n batteries connected in series, the internal resistance of one of the batteries increased sharply and became equal to $\rho = 10 \, r$, where r is the internal resistance of a normal battery. Considering e. d.s. all batteries are the same, determine at what load resistance R the power released at the load will not change if the damaged battery is short-circuited.
