## Condition

A stone is thrown with a speed of $v_{0} = 17$ m s at an angle of $A = 60 \infty$ to the horizontal. At what angular velocity does the velocity vector of the stone rotate t = 1 s after the throw? Take g = 10 m $с^{2}$, ignore air resistance. Express the result in radians per second and round to the nearest tenth.
