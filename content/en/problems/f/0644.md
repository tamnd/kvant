## Condition

A circuit consisting of two capacitors with capacitances $C_1$ and $C_2$ ($C_2 > C_1$) and two ideal diodes $\varDelta_1$ and $\varDelta_2$ (Fig. 1) is connected to an alternating voltage source $u = U_0$ cos $\omega t$. Determine the dependence of the voltage on the capacitors on time in the set mode. Draw the resulting dependencies on a graph. The resistance of an ideal diode in the forward direction is zero, in the reverse direction it is infinity. (10)
