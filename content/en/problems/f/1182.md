## Condition

When carrying out radar detection of the Moon, which rose above the horizon, to ensure the correct direction, the emitter of high-frequency radio signals was paired with an optical telephone. However, even though there was an optical image of the Moon, there was no reflected radio signal. At the same time, when it was possible to obtain a reflected radio signal, there was no optical image. Explain this phenomenon.

A. R. Zilberman
