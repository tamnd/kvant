## Condition

Dunno decided to make an “innovative vanka-stand-up”. To do this, he took a lot of balls of the same density, the radii of which differ by half. Dunno fastened the balls so that all their centers lie on the same straight line, and the radius of each subsequent one is 2 times less than the radius of the previous one. Dunno decided that due to the large mass of the lowest ball, such a structure, placed on a large ball, would be stable. But the “Vanka-Vstanka” was not stable. Explain why. Znayka advised Dunno to attach a point massive body to the lowest point of the large ball (Fig. 2; the massive body is shown as a colored bold dot).
