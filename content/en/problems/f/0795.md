## Condition

Water flows through a long channel with a rectangular cross-section, inclined towards the horizon. We can assume that the friction force of water against the bottom and banks of the channel is proportional to the average flow velocity and inversely proportional to its depth. During a Flood, the amount of water flowing through the channel cross-section in one second doubles. How does the average flow speed change?

G. L. Kotkin
