## Condition

A moving piston divides the cylinder into two equal parts of volume $V_0 = 10^{-3} \text{ m}^3$. One part contains dry air, the other contains water vapor and $m = 4$ g of water. As the cylinder slowly heats up, the piston begins to move. After the piston moved 1/4 of the length of the cylinder, the movement stopped. What mass of water vapor was in the vessel before heating? What is the mass of air in the vessel and its initial temperature? At what temperature did the piston stop moving?
The dependence of saturated water vapor pressure on temperature is given in the table.

A. I. Buzdin

## Solution

A moving piston divides the cylinder into two equal parts with a volume of $V_0=10^{-3}$ m³. One part contains dry air, the other contains water vapor and $m=4$ g of water. As the cylinder slowly heats up, the piston begins to move. After the piston moved 1/4 of the length of the cylinder, the movement stopped. What mass of water vapor was in the vessel before heating? What is the mass of air in the container and its initial temperature? At what temperature did the piston stop moving? The dependence of saturated water vapor pressure on temperature is given in the table.

| $t, \text{°C}$ | $p_H \cdot 10^{-5}$, Pa |
| :--- | :--- |
| 100 | 1 |
| 120 | 2 |
| 133 | 3 |
| 152 | 5 |
| 180 | 10 |
