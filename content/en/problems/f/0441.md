## Condition

* Between the plates of a closed flat capacitor there is a charge $q$. The area of ​​the infinite plates is large, the distance between them is $d$. Initially the charge is located at a distance $\frac{1}{3}d$ from the left plate. What charge will pass through the conductor closing the capacitor plates when the charge $q$ moves to a new position at which it will be at a distance $\frac{1}{3}d$ from the right plate?
