## Condition

There are 10 identical bottles of liquid. They are stored inside a box from which heat does not escape outside. For better storage, each bottle should be heated for a while to a temperature of $+ 90 \infty C$. To do this, we took the first bottle and heated it to the required temperature, spending an amount of heat Q = 30 kJ. Then they put it inside the box and waited until the temperatures of all the bottles were equalized due to heat exchange. Then they took the second bottle and did the same procedure with it, including subsequent equalization of temperatures, and so on. What amount of heat will be required to warm up the tenth bottle? V. Borovkov
