## Condition

The boy swims at half the speed of the river flow. In what direction should he swim to the other shore so that he is carried away by the current as little as possible?

O. Savchenko

## Solution

The boy swims at half the speed of tagging the river. In what direction should he swim to the other shore so that he is carried away by tagging as little as possible?
