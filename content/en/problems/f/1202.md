## Condition

During an electrical discharge in rarefied neon (Ne) at room temperature, a very small fraction of neon atoms decays into electrons and ions (the mass of $M$ neon atoms is $4 \cdot 10^4$ times the mass of $m$ electrons). The free path length of electrons (i.e., the average distance that an electron travels without collision) is $l = 0,1$ mm. The gas is in an electric field with a voltage of $E = 10$ V/cm. Estimate the “temperature” of electrons corresponding to their average kinetic energy. Large constant $k = 1,38 \cdot 10^{-23}$ J/K, electron charge $e = -1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ Cl.

D. A. Kuptsov
