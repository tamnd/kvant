## Condition

In a Konchek hole with an apex angle of 2α, a ball jumps, elastically reflecting from the wall of the hole. The points of collision of the ball with the wall lie at the same height (Fig. 2); the time from impact to impact is constant and equal to G. What is the average angular velocity of the ball around the axis of the hole if the maximum speed of the ball v lies in the range from $\frac{1}{2}gT$ to $gT/2$ sin α?

O. Ya. Savchenko
