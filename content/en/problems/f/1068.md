## Condition

A projectile flying vertically breaks into three equal fragments at the top point of its trajectory. One of the shards, moving vertically, flew away after time T₁ after the shot, the other two flew away simultaneously after time T₂ (T₁<T₂). Find the height H at which the shell exploded.
