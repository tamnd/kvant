## Condition

A rope, attached at one end to the side surface of the cylinder at its base of radius $r$, is wound around the cylinder $k$ times ($k$ is an integer). A weight is attached to the free end of the rope. The load is given a speed $v$, directed along the radius of the cylinder (Fig. 2). How long will it take for the entire rope to wrap around the cylinder again? The cylinder is fixed on a smooth surface.

B. Mukushev
