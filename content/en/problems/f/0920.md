## Condition

Air is blown through a variable cross-section tube. The inlet hole of the tube has an area of ​​$S_1$, and the outlet has an area of ​​$S_2$. At the inlet air speed $v_1$, temperature - $T_1$, pressure - $p_1$; at the outlet the air temperature is $T_2$, the pressure is $p_2$. What is the exit air speed?
