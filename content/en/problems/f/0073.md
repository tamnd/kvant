## Condition

Lint the sugar solution inside the glass tube. The lower end of the tube is covered with a film that is permeable to water but impermeable to sugar molecules. The tube is immersed in clean water until the sugar dissolves and secured in this position. At the same time, the liquid level in the tube begins to rise and, after a sufficient period of time, is established at a certain height above the water level (Fig. 3).

What explains this accumulation of water inside the cylinder? Where does the energy to lift liquid come from?
