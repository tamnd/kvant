## Condition

A massive cylinder of radius $R$ rests on two supports of equal height (Fig. 3). One stand is stationary, and the other emerges from under the cylinder at a speed of $v$. With what force does the cylinder press on the stationary stand at the moment when the distance between the points of support is $R\sqrt{2}$? Consider that at the initial moment the stands are located very close to each other; there is no friction between the cylinder and the supports. (9)

S. Krotov

## Solution

A massive cylinder of radius $R$ rests on two supports of equal height (see figure). One stand is stationary, and the other emerges from under the cylinder at a speed of $v$. With what force does the cylinder press on the stationary stand at the moment when the distance between the points of support is $R\sqrt{2}$? Assume that at the initial moment the stand is located very close to each other; There is no friction between the cylinder and the supports.

Until the cylinder comes off the supports, the axle will always be exactly in the middle between the supports. Therefore, the horizontal projection of the velocity of the cylinder axis (points $O$ on figure) is equal to $v/2$. Since all axis points move in a circle centered at point $A$, the total speed $u_t$ of each axis point is directed at any instant perpedicular to the radius $OA$. Consequently, all points of the axis move with centripetal acceleration $a_t = u_t^2/R$.

Let us write the equation of motion of the point $O$ in projection onto the axis $Y$ (see figure):

$$Mg \cos \alpha - N = Ma_t = M \frac{u_t^2}{R},$$

where $N$ is the absolute value of the reaction force from the fixed support. According to Newton's III law, with a force of the same absolute value, the cylinder presses on a stationary

$$N = Mg \cos \alpha - M \frac{u^2}{R}.$$
At the moment when the distance between the supports is $|AB| = R\sqrt{2}$,

$$\cos \alpha = \frac{R\sqrt{2}}{2R} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}.$$
The horizontal projection of the velocity of point $O$ is equal to $u_i$ cos $\alpha = v$, hence $u = v/\sqrt{2}$. Thus, at $|AB| = R\sqrt{2}$

$$N = M \frac{g}{\sqrt{2}} - M \frac{v^2}{2R}.$$
In order for the cylinder not to come off the supports before $|AB|$ becomes $R\sqrt{2}$, the condition $\frac{g}{\sqrt{2}} > \frac{v^2}{2R}$ must be satisfied, that is, $v < \sqrt{gR\sqrt{2}}$.
