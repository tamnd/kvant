## Condition

The horizontal plane on which the block lies oscillates according to the harmonic law with a frequency of $f=10$ Hz in the direction making an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ with the vertical (Fig. 3). The friction coefficient of the block on the platform is $\mu=0,5$. At what minimum amplitude of the beams will the block “crawl” along the site?

E. I. Butikov
