## Condition

The plane flies at a constant speed $v$ along a horizontal straight line passing overhead while watching. What angle $\alpha$ with the vertical is the direction along which the sound of mora reaches the observed person at the moment when the observed sees the plane in the direction making an angle $\varphi$ with the vertical? Speed ​​of sound $c$. Consider the case of $v > c$ and $v < c$.
