## Condition

A mathematical mat is a weightless rod of length $l$, to the free end of which a small massive weight is attached. A bead of the same array is put on the rod, which can slide freely along a horizontal guide passing to the level of the middle of the rod (Fig. 3). Find the period of small oscillations of such a matnik. There is no training.

L. G. Markovich
