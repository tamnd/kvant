## Condition

A cosmic particle moving at a speed close to the speed of light enters a reservoir in an experimental setup filled with a liquid with a refractive index of $n=1,6$. The passage of a particle through a liquid is accompanied by a glow. After a short period of time after the particle hit the tank, the device located at point $C$ was turned on, which at the moment of turning on recorded two luminous points $A$ and $B$ (the experimental diagram on a certain scale is shown at figure 4; a top view is given, points $C$, $A$ and $B$ lie in the horizontal plane). Explain the observed phenomenon and use figure to find the speed of the particle. The braking of particles in liquid is a claim.

A. I. Vuzdin
