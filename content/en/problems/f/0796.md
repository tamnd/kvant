## Condition

On the main optical axis $AB$ of a collecting lens with focal length $F$ there is a flat mirror rotating with an angular velocity $\omega$ around an axis perpendicular to the axis $AB$ (see Fig.ok). A parallel beam of rays falls on the mirror, which, after reflection, is focused on a screen located in the focal plane of the lens. Find the speed of the light spot on the screen at the moment when it passes the focus of the lens.

E. I. Palchikov
