## Condition

A rod $AB$ of length $l$ and mass $m$ rests on the wall with its lower end and is held in an inclined position (Fig. 2) with the help of a thread.
The thread is tied to the wall at point $C$, and to the rod at point $D$ such that $|AD| = 1/3|AB|$. The angles made by the thread and the rod with the wall are $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively. Find the possible value of the friction coefficient between the rod and the wall.
