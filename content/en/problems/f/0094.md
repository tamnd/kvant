## Condition

A cycle is performed over one gram-molecule of an ideal gas (a closed process consisting of two isochores and two isobars (Fig. 2)). The temperatures at points 1 and 3 are $T_1$ and $T_3$, respectively. Determine the work done by the gas per cycle if it is known that points 2 and 4 lie on the same isotherm.
