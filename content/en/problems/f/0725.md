## Condition

It is necessary to make a thin-walled end shell from an explosive so that during an explosion starting from the top of the cone and “sliding” down, the products of the explosion hit the horizontal plate on which the cone stands at the same time. The detonation speed (the speed at which new sections of the explosive are involved in the explosion) is $v$, and the speed of decomposition of the explosion products is $u$. What should be the angle φ between the base of the cone and its generatrix?
