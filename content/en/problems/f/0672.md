## Condition

A load with a resistance of $R_h=10$ Ohm is connected to the secondary winding of the included step-down transformer with a transformation coefficient of $K=220/127$. Network voltage $U=220$ V, resistance of the primary winding of the transformer $R_1=3,6$ Ohm, resistance of the secondary winding $R_2=1,2$ Ohm. Determine the voltage across the load.
