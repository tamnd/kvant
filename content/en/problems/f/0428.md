## Condition

A sphere of radius $R = 0,5$ m rotates around its vertical diameter with a constant angular velocity $\omega = 5 \text{rad/сек}$ (Fig. 1). Together with the sphere, a small body rotates on its inner surface, located at a height equal to half the radius.
1) Determine the minimum value of the friction coefficient at which this condition is possible.
2) Determine the minimum value of the friction coefficient if the angular velocity of the sphere is equal to $\omega = 8 \text{rad/сек}$.
3) Investigate the stability of the state in the case of the above found values ​​of the friction coefficient with: a) small changes in body position; b) small changes in the angular velocity of the sphere.
