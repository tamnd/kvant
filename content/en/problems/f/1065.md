## Condition

A saucepan in which 1 liter of water is poured cannot be brought to a boil by heating

## Solution

A saucepan containing 1 liter of water cannot be brought to a boil using a heated power of 100 W. Determine how long it will take for the water to cool by one degree if you turn off the heated one.

This is a very simple task - from the condition it is clear that the power of the heater is equal to the power leaving the pan of water in the surrounding space (the temperature of the water does not change over time). This means that if the heated one is turned off, the power output is 100 W and the water cools by one degree during the time

$$\tau = \frac{cm \cdot \Delta T}{P} = 4,2 \cdot 10^3 \frac{\text{Дж}}{\text{кг} \cdot \text{К}} \cdot 1 \text{ кг} \cdot \frac{1\text{K}}{100\text{Вт}} = 42\text{с}.$$

A. R. Zilberman
