## Condition

The descent vehicle of a spacecraft approaches the surface of a certain planet at a constant speed, transmitting data on external pressure to the spacecraft. A graph of the dependence of pressure $p$ (in terms of units) on time $t$ is shown in figure 4. Having descended on the surface of the planet, the device changed and transmitted on board data on temperature and free fall acceleration: $T=700$ K, $g=10$ m/$c^2$. Determine the descent speed of the vehicle if it is known that the planet’s atmosphere consists of carbon dioxide ($CO_2$). Also determine the temperature at an altitude of $h=15$ km above the surface of the planet. (9)

A. I. Buzdin
