## Condition

A heavy piston with mass $M$ can move freely inside a vertical heat-conducting cylinder with cross-section $S$, the upper end of which is closed and the lower end is open to the atmosphere (Fig. 2). Inside the cylinder there is a horizontal partition with a small hole that cuts off one mole of air from the atmosphere, occupying a volume of $V$ and having an atmospheric pressure of $p_a$. The piston, which initially pressed the force against the partition, is released. Assuming that the internal energy of the gas is $cT$, find how much the piston will lower.

G. L. Kotkin
