## Condition

At the top of an equilateral right triangle, the leg of which is equal to a, there are identical metal balls of radii r (r≤a). The balls are each charged with charge q. Balls 1 and 2 are connected with a conductor, then the conductor is removed. Then they do the same with balls 2 and 3 and, finally, with balls 3 and 1. What charges are installed on the balls?
