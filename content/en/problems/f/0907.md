## Condition

Two metal balls of mass $m$ each with radii $r$ and $2r$ are placed in an electric field, the direction of which is directed from the large ball to the smaller one and is equal to $E$. The distance between the centers of the balls is $R_0 = 4r$. The large ball carries a charge $q\left(k \frac{q}{r^2} \ll E\right)$, the small ball is not charged. The balls are released. The time between the first and second colliding balls is $t$. Find the time between the $n$th and $(n+1)$th collisions and the distances the balls will travel between these collisions. What is the average acceleration of the balls over a sufficiently long period of time? The collisions are considered to be absolutely elastic.
