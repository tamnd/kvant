## Condition

Two identical massive pipes roll down slides of the same height, but with different profiles (Fig. 2). Along the first slide, the pipe moves throughout the entire section without slipping; on the second slide there is an absolutely smooth section (section 1-2), but at the end of the path (at the foot of the slide) the pipe moves again without slipping. Which pipe at the end of the slide has the fastest speed?
