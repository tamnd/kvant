## Condition

If the cold water tap is fully open and the hot water tap is closed (Fig. 2), then the bathtub is filled in $t_1 = 8$ min; If you attach a hose with a shower at the end to the outlet, the filling time will increase to $t_2 = 14$ min. When the cold water tap is closed and the hot water tap is fully open, the filling time is $t_3 = 12$ min; under the same conditions, but with a shower at the end - $t_4 = 18$ min. How long will it take to fill the bathtub if both taps are fully open? And if at the same time a hose with a shower is attached?

B. E. Skorovarov
