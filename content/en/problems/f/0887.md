## Condition

A beam of parallel light rays, having a circular cross-section with a diameter of $d$ (Fig. 3), falls on a glass body, which consists of two cones connected by their bases. Body measurements are indicated at figure 3; stack refractive index $n$. What picture will be observed on a screen installed at a distance $l$ from the body?

A. V. Helvas

## Solution

A beam of parallel light rays having a circular cross-section with a diameter of $d$ (Fig. 1,a) falls on a transparent glass body, which represents two connected bases of a cone. Body dimensions are indicated at figure 1,a; glass refractive index n. What picture will be observed on a screen installed at a distance of $l$ from the body?

◆ The screen image will be different depending on the correspondence between $a$, $d$ and $l$.

1) Let us consider the case when the ray $I(I')$, after refraction on the face $AB(AD)$, hits the point $C$ (see Fig. 1,a). In this case, after double refraction of the rays (on faces $AB$ and $DC$, $AD$ and $BC$), the beam remains parallel $AC$ and a uniformly illuminated circle (Fig. 1.6) with a diameter $d$ is obtained on the screen. The receiving picture does not depend on $l$.

varnit, we determine from figure 1,a:
$$\frac{d_1}{2} = |KA| \sin \alpha = (a - |BK|) \sin \alpha = (1 - tg \alpha) \sin \alpha.$$
Substituting
$$\sin \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \sin \beta = \frac{\sin \alpha}{n}, \text{tg} \beta = \frac{\sin \beta}{\cos \beta} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2 - 1}}.$$
we get:
$$d_1 = a\sqrt{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2 - 1}}\right).$$

2) Let $0 < d_2 < a\sqrt{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2-1}}\right)$. In this case, each ray after double refraction will be new parallel to $AC$ (Fig. 2, a). The screen will produce a uniformly illuminated ring (Fig. 2, b). Radii inside and outer circle of the ring -

$$r_1 = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2-1}}\right) + \frac{d_2}{2\sqrt{2}},$$

$$r_2 = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2-1}}\right) + \frac{d_2}{2\sqrt{2}}(1 + 2\sqrt{2}).$$

The resulting picture does not depend on $l$.

3) Let $a\sqrt{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2n^2-1}}\right) < d_2 < a\sqrt{2}$. In this case (Fig. 3, a) the picture obtained on the screen depends on the distance from the point $C$ the screen is located. figure 3, b shows the pictures obtained at different screen positions: $\exists_1, \exists_2, \exists_3$.
