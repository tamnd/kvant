## Condition

Why is the face of a fencer wearing a wire mask not visible to the public, but the sport sees everything just as well as without a mask?

* *

The author of the third law of thermodynamics, Walter Nerist, raised carps during access hours. Someone once remarked thoughtfully:
— A strange choice. Raising chickens is even more interesting.
Nerist calmly replied:
— I breed animals that are in thermodynamic equilibrium with the environment. Breeding warm-blooded animals means heating the world space at your own expense.
