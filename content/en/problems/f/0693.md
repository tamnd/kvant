## Condition

On a fixed wedge forming an angle α with the horizon, there lies a non-stretchable rope (Fig. 2). One end of the rope is fixed at point A. A small weight is attached to the lower end of the rope (at point B). At some point in time, the wedge begins to move to the right with constant acceleration a. With what acceleration does the weight move while it is on the wedge?
