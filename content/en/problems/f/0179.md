## Condition

The suspended boat, angling vertically, emits short sound pulses from the radar radar with a diameter of $\tau_0$ in the direction of the day. The diminution of reflected scenes modified by zdrolocustim on the boat is $\tau$. What is the boat's sinking speed?

The speed of sound in water is $V$. The bottom is horizontal.

Let's look at what is causing the change in the duration of the disappearance. To do this, it is convenient to move from the duration of the disappearance to its length. Let the signal emitted by the sonar on the boat have the form shown in figure 8, that is, its intensity changes abruptly from zero to a certain value at time $t$, remains constant during time $\tau_0$ and again abruptly drops to zero at time $t + \tau_0$. If the boat is motionless, then the length of the emitted wave (the distance between the leading and trailing edges) is equal to $l_0 = V \tau_0$. If the boat goes down, the length of the line becomes smaller. Indeed, let

$$\tau = \frac{l_1}{V + u} = \frac{(V - u) \tau_0}{(V + u)}.$$

From here

$$u = V \frac{\tau_0 - \tau}{\tau_0 + \tau}.$$
