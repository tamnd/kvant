## Condition

Two cylinders of different radii collide in opposite directions around horizontal parallel axes with an angular velocity ω₁=ω₂=ω=2 rad/s. The horizontal distance between the axes is L=4 m. At time t=0, a beam is placed on the cylinders perpendicular to the axes so that it is in a horizontal position and simultaneously touches both surfaces of rotation, and its center of mass is located exactly above the cylinder axis less than the radius (r=0.25 m), as indicated at figure 2. Calculate and illustrate the graphical dependence of the horizontal displacement of the beam on time, starting from moment t=0. Friction coefficient μ=0.05, gravitational acceleration equal to g=10.0 m/s². (10)
