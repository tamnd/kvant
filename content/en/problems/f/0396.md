## Condition

A household vessel of varying diameter is immersed upward in a vessel directed by water, motionlessly attached to a large vessel and partially filled with water (Fig. 4). A piece of ice floats on the surface of the water inside the changing vessel. What happens to the water levels in the vessels when the ice melts? How will the answer change if the changing vessel is not attached to the larger one and floats on the surface of the water?
