## Condition

In the side wall of a vessel filled with a liquid with a refractive index $n$, a hole of small radius $r$ continues. A horizontal thin beam of light is directed along the axis of the hole from the vessel. To what level $h$ must the liquid flow above the hole so that the light ray emerges from the jet without ever experiencing internal reflection?

Neglect the change in the cross section of the jet, and assume the refractive index of the liquid to be sufficiently large.

I. F. Ginzburg
