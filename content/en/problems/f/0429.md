## Condition

The cylinder stacks, piston and internal partition with area $1 \text{dm}^2$ are made of body-insulating materials (Fig. 2). The valve in the partition opens if the pressure on the right is greater than the pressure on the left. In the initial state, in the left part of a cylinder of length $11,2 \text{dm}$ there is $12 \text{гелия}, в правой части, имеющей ту же длину, — $2 \text{helium}, on both sides the temperature is $0^{\circ}\text{C}$. The external pressure is $10^5 \text{н/м}^2$. The specific temperature of a body at constant volume is $c_V = 3,15 \cdot 10^3 \text{дж/(кг·эрад)}$, and at constant pressure $c_p = 5,25 \cdot 10^3 \text{дж/(кг·эрад)}$.
Slowly move the piston towards the partition (with a short stop at the moment the valve opens) and carefully bring the piston to the partition. What is the work done by us?
