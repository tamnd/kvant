## Condition

A cylindrical vessel, closed by a weightless piston with cross-sectional area $S$, contains gas at atmospheric pressure $p_0$. The volume of gas is equal to $V_0$. The vessel is immersed in water (density $\rho_0$) as shown in figure 4. Find the dependence of the distance $x$ between the piston and the surface of the water on the model $F$ of the force holding the thread, which is tied to the piston.
