## Condition

Two beads with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ can slide along a smooth horizontal conducting ring without friction. At first, the beads were connected by a thread and there was a rolled spring between them. The thread is burned out. After the beads begin to move, the spring is removed. At what point in the rings will the beads collide at the same time? The beads become absolutely elastic.
