## Condition

There are known cases of observing the world of the sea in the empty. At what distance from the observer does such a mirage appear? Assume that the speed of light in the surface layer in empty spaces changes according to the law $c(z)=c_0(1-az)$, where $c_0$ is the speed of light at the surface of the earth, $z$ is the height above the surface.

V. I. Klyachin
