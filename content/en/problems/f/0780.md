## Condition

A cylindrical vessel with a height of $2h$, equally divided by a partition, contains water in the upper part (its density is $\rho$) and air at an atmospheric pressure of $p_0$ (Fig. 4) in the lower part. A small hole opens in the partition so that water begins to flow into the lower part of the vessel. How thick will the layer of water be at the bottom of the vessel when air begins to flow upward from the hole? The temperature is constant. The acceleration of the rigid force is $g$.

V. Borodin

## Solution

A cylindrical vessel of height 2h, equally divided by a partition, contains water in the upper part (its density Q) and air at atmospheric pressure $p_0$ in the lower part. A small hole opens in the partition so that water begins to flow into the lower part of the vessel. How thick will the layer of water be at the bottom of the vessel when air begins to flow upward from the hole? The temperature is constant. The acceleration of the force of the weight is g.

Air from the bottom of the vessel begins to pass upward through the hole when its pressure exceeds the hydraulic pressure of water at the level of the hole, that is, at pressure

$$p = p_0 + \varrho g (h - x),$$

where x is the lowering of the water level in the upper part of the vessel and, accordingly, the thickness of the water layer in the lower part of the vessel. Since the air temperature does not change, according to the Boyle-Mariotte law

$$p_0 h S = p (h - x) S,$$

where S is the area of ​​the bottom of the vessel.

Thus,

$$p_0 (h - x) + \varrho g (h - x)^2 = p_0 h,$$

where

$$\varrho g x^2 - (2 \varrho g h + p_0) x + \varrho g h^2 = 0.$$

Solving this equation, we find x:

$$x = h + \frac{p_0}{2 \varrho g} \left( 1 - \sqrt{1 + \frac{4 \varrho g h}{p_0}} \right).$$

(The solution x = h + \frac{p_0}{2 \varrho g} \left( 1 + \sqrt{1 + \frac{4 \varrho g h}{p_0}} \right) is not suitable, since x < h is completed.)
