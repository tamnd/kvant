## Condition

A gymnast falls from a height of $H=12$ m onto a horizontal stretched elastic net, which is deflected by $h=1$ m. Estimate how many times the maximum force acting on the hypnast from the net is greater than the force of gravity if the size of the net is much larger than $h$ and the mass of the net is small compared to the mass of the person.

Leningrad City Olympics, 1979
