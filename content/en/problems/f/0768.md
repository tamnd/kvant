## Condition

A top shaped like a cone (Fig. 2) moves along a smooth table, quickly rotating around its axis. At what speed $v$ of translational motion will the top not hit the edge of the table and drain from it? The axis of the top remains vertical. The dimensions of the top are indicated at figure 2. (8)

A. Zilberman

## Solution

A cone-shaped drag moves along the main table, quickly grinding its axes (see figure). At what speed v of translational motion will the die not hit the edge of the table, oozing from it? The axis of the drag remains vertical. The dimensions of the wolf are indicated at figure.

Having jumped off the table, the wolf will move in a parabola (rotation only stabilizes the vertical position of the wolf's axis). The horizontal projection $v_x$ of the wolf's speed will be equal to the speed $v$ of its translational movement on the table; the vertical projection will change over time according to the law $v_y = gt^2/2$. The fiber will not hit the edge of the table if during the time $t$, during which it moves vertically by $H$, its horizontal displacement is greater than or equal to $r$ (see figure). Let's write this condition:

$$v_x\tau = v\tau \geq r, \quad \tau = \sqrt{\frac{2H}{g}}.$$

Thus, the speed of the wolf’s translational movement must satisfy the condition:

$$v \geq \sqrt{\frac{r^2g}{2H}}.$$

A. Zilberman
