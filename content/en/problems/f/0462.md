## Condition

If the thermostat of an electric iron is set to the “nylon” position, then the iron periodically turns on for 10 seconds and turns off for 40 seconds. The surface of the iron is heated to a temperature of 100°C. If the thermostat is set to the “clap” position, the heater periodically turns on for 20 seconds and turns off for 30 seconds. Determine the established temperature of the iron surface in this position of the thermostat. Find the temperature to which the switched-on stove will heat up if the thermostat fails.

Assume that heat transfer is proportional to the temperature difference between the iron and the surrounding air. The temperature in the room is 20 °C.

V. Skordarov

Problem solutions

M403, M405 — M409; F413 — F415, F417 — F422

## Solution

If the electronic thermostat is set to the “nylon” position, my utility will periodically turn on at $\tau_1 = 10$ s and turn off at $\tau_2 = 40$ s. In this case, the surface of the utility is heated to a temperature of $t_1 = 100^\circ C$. If the thermostat is set to the “clap” position, my utility periodically turns on at $\tau_1 = 20$ s and turns off at $\tau_2 = 30$ s. Determine the established temperature $t_2$ of the utility surface in this position of the thermostat. Namely, to what temperature $t_3$ the switched on utility boils up if the thermostat fails.
Assume that chalk removal is proportional to the temperature and ambient air. Room temperature $t_0 = 20^\circ C$.
