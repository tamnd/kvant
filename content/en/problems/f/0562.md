## Condition

Capacitors $C_1=2,00$ µF and $C_2=3,00$ µF are connected in series and connected to a battery with an emf $\varepsilon=120$ V, the middle point of which is grounded (Fig. 2). The wire connecting the capacitors can be grounded using the key $K$. Find the charges $q_1, q_2$ and $q_3$ that will pass through sections $I-I$, $II-I$ and $III-I$ after closing the key in the directions indicated at figure.

## Solution

The impotence capacitors $C_1 = 2.00$ µF and $C_2 = 3.00$ µF are connected in series and connected to a battery with an emf $E = 120$ V. the middle point of which is grounded. The wire connecting the capacitors can be grounded using key K. Find the charges $q_1, q_2$ and $q_3$ that will pass through sections I-I, II-II and III-III after closing the key in the directions indicated at figure.

Before the switch is closed, the charges $q$ of both capacitors are the same (since the capacitors are connected in series) and equal

$$q = \frac{C_1 C_2}{C_1 + C_2}$$

After closing the key, the directions on the capacitors will become equal to $\frac{1}{2} E$, and the charges of the capacitors will be equal

$$q' = \frac{1}{2} C_1 E \quad \text{and} \quad q'' = \frac{1}{2} C_2 E.$$

Consequently, charges will pass through sections I-I and II-II in given directions

$$q_1 = q' - q = \frac{C_1 (C_1 - C_2)}{2 (C_1 + C_2)} E = -2.4 \cdot 10^{-5} \text{ KJ}.$$

And

$$q_2 = q - q'' = \frac{C_2 (C_1 - C_2)}{2 (C_1 + C_2)} E = -3.6 \cdot 10^{-5} \text{ KJ}.$$

The total charge of the capacitor plates connected to each other before the switch was closed was equal to zero, and after the switch was closed it became equal to $q'' - q'$. Such a charge is very/no, and more advanced than the III-III section, that is

$$q_3 = q'' - q' = \frac{1}{2} (C_2 - C_1) E = 6 \cdot 10^{-5} \text{ KJ}.$$

I. Slobodetsky
