## Condition

The mass of the balloon together with the rope trailing behind it is equal to $M$ (Fig. 3). The Archimedean buoyant force acting on the ball is $F$, the friction coefficient of the rope on the ground is $\mu$. The air resistance force acting on the balloon is proportional to the relative air speed of the balloon $F_c = -\alpha v$. Find the speed of the ball relative to the ground if a horizontal wind blows at a speed of $u$.

A. Trubachev

The pulling force acting on the ball is equal to $\vec{F}$, the coefficient of the rope’s friction with the Earth is μ. The force of air resistance acting on a balloon is proportional to the speed of the balloon relative to the air: $\vec{F}_c = -\alpha \vec{v}$.
