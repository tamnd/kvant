## Condition

The rigid workpiece is clamped between two parallel guides moving in the horizontal direction at speeds $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$ (Fig. 4). At some point in time, the points of contact of the workpiece with the guides lie on a straight line perpendicular to the vectors $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$. Which points of the workpiece have velocities at this moment equal in absolute value to $v_1$ and $v_2$?
S. Krotov

## Solution

A rigid workpiece is clamped between two parallel guides moving in the horizontal direction at speeds $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$. At some point in time, the points of contact of the workpiece with the guides lie on a straight line perpendicular to the vectors $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$. Which points of the workpiece have velocities at this moment equal in absolute value to $v_1$ and $v_2$?

The velocities of the workpiece points currently lying on the segment $AB$ uniformly change from $\vec{v}_1$ at point $A$ to $\vec{v}_2$ at point $B$. Consequently, the speed of point $O$ (see figure) at this moment is zero. Point $O$ is the instantaneous center of rotation. (Since the workpiece is volumetric, point $O$ lies on the instantaneous rotation axis, which is perpendicular to the drawing plane.) It is clear that speed $|\vec{v}_1|$ at a given moment has workpiece points lying on a circle of radius $OA$, and speed $|\vec{v}_2|$ has points lying on a circle of radius $OB$. (In a volumetric workpiece, points with such a speed lie on cylindrical surfaces whose radii are equal to $|OA|$ and $|OB|$, respectively.)

S. Krotov
