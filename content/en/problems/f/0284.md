## Condition

Find the acceleration $a$ with which a round metal plate falls in a uniform magnetic field parallel to the surface of the Earth. The plate falls vertically downwards and its plane is oriented parallel to the magnetic field and perpendicular to the surface of the Earth. The plate thickness $d$ is much smaller than its radius $R$, mass $m$, magnetic field indication $B$, free fall acceleration $g$. (10 grades)
