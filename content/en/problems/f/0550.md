## Condition

Two washers are put on a rough copper cone (covered with a fine notch, like a file): an aluminum one with a radius of $r=1$ cm and an iron one with a radius of $R=3$ cm. The distance between the washers is $a=6$ cm. How much will this distance change if the cone and washers are heated by $\Delta t=200$ K? The thermal coefficients of linear expansion of copper, aluminum and iron are $\beta_1=2,5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$, $\beta_2=1,7 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$ and $\beta_3=10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$, respectively. The cone is located vertically, with the top apex.

G. Kotkin
