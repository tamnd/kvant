## Condition

A small spring block of mass m can move without friction inside a rectangular box of the same mass. The box is on a table covered with a thin layer of mass (Fig. 2). The friction force of the box on the table depends only on the speed u of the box moving on the table and is equal to F=-γu. At the initial moment of time, the box is finished, and the block is located at its left wall and has a speed v₀ directed to the right. How many impacts will the block make on the box if the length of the box L is much greater than the size of the block?

D. Yu. Grigoriev
