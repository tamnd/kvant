## Condition

In the pumped state, the container $V = 1 \text{ л}$ contains 1 $\text{е}$ uranium hydride $\mathrm{UH}_3$. When heated to a temperature of $t = 400^\circ \mathrm{C}$, the hydride completely decomposes into uranium (atomic weight $A = 238$) and hydrogen. Find the pressure of hydrogen in the vessel at this temperature.
