## Condition

For horizontal movement of loads over a distance of $L=20$ m, a self-propelled trolley is used, which moves along horizontal rails. On a cable of length $l=5$ m, the transported load (Fig. 5) is suspended from the trolley. The cart rises half with equal acceleration, and half with equal deceleration. Determine the possibility of trolley acceleration values ​​at which the load will remain motionless after the trolley stops at the end of the journey. (10)
