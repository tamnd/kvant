## Condition

A cube of mass m is attached to two springs with stiffnesses k₁ and k₂ and lengths in the undeformed state l₁ and l₂, respectively. The springs are fixed at the other ends (Fig. 3) so that the cube can move along a horizontal plane. The friction coefficient between the cube and the plane is μ, the distance between the points of attachment of the springs is L, the size of the cube is small and can be neglected. Find the area in which the cube can be in equilibrium.
