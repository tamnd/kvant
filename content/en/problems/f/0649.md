## Condition

The bucket contains a mixture of water and ice. The mass of the mixture is 10 kg. The bucket was brought into the room and they immediately began to change the temperature of the mixture. The resulting graph of the dependence $t$ (τ) is shown in figure 2. The specific temperature of water $c_B = 4200$ J/(kg K) and the heat of fusion of ice $\lambda = 3,4 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg are known. Determine how much ice was in the bucket when it was brought into the room. The heat capacity of the bucket is a problem. (8)

A. Buzdin

## Solution

There is a mixture of water and ice in the bucket. The mass of the mixture is 10 kg. The bucket was brought into the room and they immediately began to change the temperature of the mixture. The resulting plot of $t(\tau)$ is depicted in figure. The specific temperature of water $c_B = 4200$ J/(kg K) and the temperature of ice $\lambda = 3,4 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg are known.
