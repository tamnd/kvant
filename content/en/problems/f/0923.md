## Condition

On a smooth horizontal plane lie two disks touching each other, fastened with a non-stretchable thread (Fig. 1). A disk of radius R begins to rotate around a fixed vertical axis passing through its center, and the linear rotation speed increases with time. At some moments, a disk smaller than radius r breaks away from the large disk (stops touching it). At what angle will the large disk rotate by this moment? There is no friction between the discs.

⟦folio 26⟧

Assume that all the mass of the disk is concentrated at its center.

L. G. Markovich
