## Condition

A rope closed in a ring covers the pulleys of two blocks with horizontal and parallel axes of symmetry. From the side, the rope looks symmetrical, rotated 90q in a figure eight (f). The axles are located at a distance of 2 meters from each other on the same horizontal level and the radii of the pulleys are the same. A long, 3-meter long, uniform straight rod of round cross-section is placed in the grooves of the pulleys on top (above a thin rope). When the rope moves slowly (with a constant speed at each point in each place on the rope), the rod moves from one position to another, and the rod slips along one of the pulleys, and moves without slipping along the other pulley. At the moment of stopping, the distances from the ends of the rod to the nearest points of contact between the rod and the pulley differ by one and a half times. After stopping, the rod changes direction of speed, and the slip and non-slip situation with respect to the pulleys is reversed. At some point, the speed of the rope increased, and now the rod, moving forward, performs harmonic oscillations with a period of 3 seconds. What is the coefficient of friction when the rod slides along the pulley? What is the maximum coefficient of static friction?
