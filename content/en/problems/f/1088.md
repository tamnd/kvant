## Condition

The weightless ladder is fixed in a weightless suspension rotating at a speed of $f=10$ rpm so that it can freely swing relative to the upper step (Fig. 2). A monkey of mass $m=30$ kg begins to walk down the stairs. At what distance $l$ from the suspension will it be when the vertical position of the ladder ceases to be stable?

A. V. Andrianov
