## Condition

The cart moves along a horizontal table under the action of an inextensible thread tied to it. The thread is thrown through a small block fixed at a height H above the table plane. The free end of the thread is pulled out at a constant speed $v_{0}$, directed horizontally. Find the speed of the cart and its acceleration at the moment when the angle between the thread and the horizon is $45^\circ$. M. Uchitelev
