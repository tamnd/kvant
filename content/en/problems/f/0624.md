## Condition

In the process of the vessel, closed by a flat piston with a diameter of $D=5$ cm, there is a small amount of water (Fig. 2). The diameter of the process is $d=2$ mm. If at a constant temperature $t=20^\circ\text{C}$ the piston is lowered by $H=10$ cm, then the water level in the process increases by $h=1$ mm. Find the saturated vapor pressure of water at temperature $t=20^\circ\text{C}$.
