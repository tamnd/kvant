## Condition

Vasya decided to weigh a small piece of the Chelyabinsk meteorite he found near Lake Chebarkul using iron weights. To do this, he used symmetrical equal-arm scales made of iron. In air, weighing gave the result M = 2.1 kg. When the scales were completely immersed in the water of the lake, the result was different - to balance the scales, it was necessary to put weights on them, the total mass of which turned out to be equal to m = 1.8 kg. In this case, both the weighed substance and the weights were also completely immersed in water. Why

Is the density of the meteorite material equal? The density of iron is $\rho_{ж} = 7,9$ g $см^{3}$, the density of water is $\rho_{в} = 1,0$ g $см^{3}$. V.Statikov
