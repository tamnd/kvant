## Condition

A metal plate with a charge $Q$ rises into the space between the plates of an uncharged flat capacitor. In this case, gaps $l_1$ and $l_2$ remain between the plate and the plates of the capacitor. The areas of all plates are the same and equal to $S$. Determine the potential difference between the capacitor plates.

## Solution

In the space between the plates of an independently flat capacitor, a metal plate having a charge Q is remembered. Gaps $l_1$ and $l_2$ will remain between the plate and the plates of the capacitor. The area of ​​all plates is the same and equal to S. Determine the potential difference between the plates of the capacitor.
