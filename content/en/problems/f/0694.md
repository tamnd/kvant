## Condition

N identical balls move along a long straight trough inclined at an angle α to the horizontal without friction. What is the maximum number of collisions that can occur in the system at arbitrary initial positions and speeds of the balls? The collisions of the balls are considered to be absolutely elastic.

E. Surkov
