## Condition

Two athletes stand at points A and B and hold a rubber screw. At the signal, athlete A begins to move east at a speed of $v_0=1$ m/s, and athlete B begins to move south with constant acceleration. Find this acceleration if it is known that knot B, tied on the cord, passed through the point Γ (Fig. 1; scale is indicated at figure) during movement.
