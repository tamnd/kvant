## Condition

The left supports of the container, unlike the right ones, are made on rollers (Fig. 2), providing negligible tremie (the dimensions of the supports are small). To move the container to the left, a force $F_1$ must be applied to the center of its right wall perpendicular to it. To move the container to the right, it needs to apply a force $F_2$ to the center of its left wall perpendicularly. Determine the mass of the container, considering it a homogeneous cube.
