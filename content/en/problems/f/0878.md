## Condition

Two smooth irons forming angles α with the horizon are located in the same vertical plane (Fig. 1). From point A of one iron and from point B of another iron, two balls are simultaneously produced without initial velocities of initial forging. The time of movement to the horizontal plane of the first ball (from point A) is $t_1$, the second - $t_2$. How long after the balls started moving was the distance between them the smallest?
