## Condition

On an extended smooth horizontal surface along a vertical

⟦folio 16⟧

KVANT 2017/№11

a small absolutely elastic smooth ball is jumping along the axis. Its maximum speed is v. On the same horizontal surface, at a distance from the point of impact of this ball, equal to the maximum height of its flight, a second similar ball is located. He is given a horizontal speed v in the direction of the first. The balls collided in such a way that at the moment of impact the line passing through their centers was horizontal and coincided in direction with the speed of the second ball before the impact. 1) Find the maximum flight altitude of the first ball. 2) Find the period of motion of the first ball before hitting the second. 3) At what height was the first ball at the moment the second one started? 4) Find the speed of the first ball immediately after hitting the second. 5) At what distance from the point of impact can a vertical wall be placed so that, after being reflected from it, the first ball hits the second again? The impact time can be neglected.
