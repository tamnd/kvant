## Condition

On a smooth horizontal pillar, place a dumbbell vertically, consisting of a weightless rod with two identical small balls at the ends (Fig. 3). The upper ball is hit with a speed $v$ in the horizontal direction. At what maximum length of the dumbbell $l$ will the bottom ball immediately come off the table? (8th grade, 9th grade)

A. R. Zilberman

## Solution

On the main horizontal table there is a vertical nocturnal loop, consisting of a weightless rod with two identical small balls at the ends (Fig. 4). The impact imparts velocity v to the upper ball in the horizontal direction. At what maximum length of the ball l will the bottom ball immediately come off the table?

When the top ball is given a horizontal speed v, the balls begin

$$\frac{v^2}{2l} > g,$$

that is

$$l < \frac{v^2}{2g}$$

And

$$l_{\max} = \frac{v^2}{2g}.$$

I. Sh. Slobodetsky
