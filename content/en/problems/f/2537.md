## Condition

Two identical bars are coupled by a rigid weightless rod and move along a smooth horizontal surface of the table (see figure). At some point, the bars begin to drive onto the rough area of ​​the table. Find the coefficient of friction P of the bars on a rough surface if they had a speed v at the beginning of their movement, and after passing through the rough section of the table they move with a speed u. The length of each block is l, the rod is s, and the rough section of the table is l + s. A.Ivanov
