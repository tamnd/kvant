## Condition

Two rods from different metals with linear expansion coefficients $a_1$ and $a_2$ at a temperature $0^\circ C$ have slightly different lengths $l_1$ and $l_2$ and cross sections $s_1$ and $s_2$. At what temperature will the rods be the same a) length? b) cross sections? b) volumes?
