## Condition

Photographing the ants at close range, the experimenter used an extension ring, which increased the distance from the film to the object by $\Delta l=7,5$ mm. A sharp image of the ant was obtained in the case when a distance of $b=1,05$ m was set on the object frame (the distance values ​​from the object to the object are indicated on the frame without the use of extension rings). The focal length of the object is $F=50$ mm. How far was the ant from the object? Consider the lens to be a thin lens.

A. P. Ershov
