## Condition

A thin single-pod rod, which can rotate freely in a vertical plane around a horizontal axis passing through the end of the rod, hangs motionless above the water (Fig. 2); length l, material density ρ (ρ is less than the density of water). Slowly lowering the axle, the stubble is immersed in water. Find the relationship between the angle of deflection of the stubble from the vertical and the distance from the axis to the surface of the water; plot this relationship.

D. V. Belov
