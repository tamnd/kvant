## Condition

In a closed container with an edge of 1 cm there are $n$ gas molecules. The walls of the cube are poppy, so that gas molecules, once on the wall, remain on it $10^{-2}$ s. Estimate how many gas molecules are on the wall by the time $t$ of its flight from one wall to another:

$$\frac{n_1}{n_2} = \frac{\tau}{t}.$$

Since $n_1 + n_2 = n$, then

$$\frac{n_1}{n} = \frac{\tau}{t + \tau}.$$

From here

$$n_1 = n \frac{\tau}{t + \tau} = n \frac{1}{1 + \frac{t}{\tau}}.$$

Let us estimate the values ​​of $t$. The root mean square speed of gas molecules with molecular weight $\mu$ at a temperature $T^\circ$ K is equal to

$$\bar{v} = \sqrt{\frac{3RT}{\mu}}.$$

To determine this, we will assume that all molecules move at this speed perpendicular to the walls of the vessel. Then the time it takes for the molecule to fly the distance $l$ between the walls is equal to

$$t = \frac{l}{\bar{v}} = \frac{l}{\sqrt{\frac{3RT}{\mu}}},$$

And

$$n_1 = n \frac{1}{1 + \frac{l}{\tau} \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{3RT}}}.$$

Let there be a possibility ($\mu = 29 \text{ кД/кмоль}$) in the vessel at temperature $T = 300^\circ \text{K}$. Substituting the values ​​of all quantities into the formula for $n_1$, we obtain

$$n_1 = n \frac{1}{1 + \frac{10^{-2}}{10^{-2}} \sqrt{\frac{29}{3 \cdot 8 \cdot 3 \cdot 10^3 \cdot 300}}} \approx n \frac{1}{1 + 0.02} \approx 0.98 n.$$
