## Condition

In a long horizontal heat-conducting pipe between two pistons of mass $m$ each there is 1 mole of an ideal monatomic gas at a temperature $T_0$. There is a vacuum outside the pistons. At the initial moment, the piston velocities are directed in one direction and are equal to $v$ and $3v$. To what maximum temperature will the gas be heated? Tello pistons do not conduct. The universal gas constant is $R$.

V. Borodin
