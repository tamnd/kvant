## Condition

A hockey player skates across the ice on one skate. It is known that ice melts under the ridge to a depth of $h=0,03$ mm. Ridge width $d=2$ mm. Find the friction force between the skate and the skate. The specific heat of melting of ice is $\lambda=3,3 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg, the density of ice is $\rho=0,9$ g/cm$^3$. Consider the thermal conductivity of ice to be small.

M. G. Gavrilov (For the end see p. 34)
