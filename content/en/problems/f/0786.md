## Condition

In a neutral monatomic gas, attractive forces act between the molecules. Considering that the potential energy of molecular interaction is proportional to the density of the gas, that is, $U = \alpha \frac{N}{V}$, where $N$ is the number of gas molecules, $V$ is the volume occupied by the gas. $\alpha$ - proportionality coefficient ($\alpha < 0$), determine the difference in the molar heat capacity of the gas at constant pressure ($c_p$) and at constant volume ($c_V$).

V. Tugushchev
