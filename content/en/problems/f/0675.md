## Condition

To change large currents in circuit $CC$ (Fig. 1), a shunt Ш is used, in parallel with which a changing device Г with an internal resistance $r = 10$ Ohm is connected through resistances $R_1 = 2$ Ohm and $R_2$. In position $A$ of switch P, all the voltage of the device corresponds to the current in circuit $I_1 = 10$ A. What kind of resistance $R_2$ should be taken so that in position $B$ of switch P, all the voltage of the device corresponds to current $I_2 = 100$ A?

The shunt resistance is considered to be much less than the internal resistance of the device.
