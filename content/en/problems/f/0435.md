## Condition

In the setup shown at figure 3, the coupling $M$ is attached to two identical springs whose spring constants are $k=10 \text{ н/м}$. The coupling can slide along the horizontal rod $AB$ without friction. The installation rotates with a constant angular velocity $\omega=4,4 \text{ rad/сек}$ around a vertical axis passing through the middle of the rod. Find the period of small oscillations of the coupling. Coupling weight $m=0,2 \text{ ке}$.

At what value $\omega$ will there be no vibrations of the coupling?
