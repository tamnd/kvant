## Solution

For the high-tech lamp, a nonlinear capacitor was used (it is filled with a dielectric whose dielectric value depends on the field voltage). The graph of voltage $U$ versus charge $Q$ of the capacitor is shown in figure 1. The capacitor is charged from a battery $c$ $U_0 = 300$ V through a resistor with a resistance of $R = 10$ kOhm. Find the maximum energy of vysshiki. Estimate the maximum number of towers in 1 minute. Assume that at high voltage the capacitor is completely discharged. The minimum initial voltage is $U_1 = 250$ V.

The maximum search energy is numerically equal to the area under the graph of the curve $U(Q)$, limited by the value $U = U_0 = 300$ V (in this case $Q \approx 0,3$ K; see Fig. 1). To calculate this area, we divide the curve $U(Q)$ into sections on which the dependence $U(Q)$ can be approximately considered linear. Under each such area the area is numerically equal to $U_i \cdot \Delta Q_i$. Thus, the maximum search energy is (see Fig. 1)

$$W \approx \left( \frac{140}{2} \cdot 0,05 + \frac{140 + 200}{2} \cdot 0,075 + \frac{200 + 300}{2} \cdot 0,175 \right) \text{ Дж} = 60 \text{ Дж}.$$
