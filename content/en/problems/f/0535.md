## Condition

The potential energy of interaction between two identical loaded small balls with charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ is equal to $W_1 = \frac{q_1 q_2}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0 l}$, where $l$ is the distance between the centers of the balls. If the balls are connected with a wire, the charges of the balls will become equal, and the potential energy of interaction of the balls will become equal to $W_2 = \frac{(q_1 + q_2)^2}{16 \pi \varepsilon_0 l}$. Since $(q_1 + q_2)^2 > 4 q_1 q_2$ (at $q_1 \neq q_2$), then $W_2 > W_1$, that is, the potential energy of interaction between the particles increases! Where does the extra energy come from?
