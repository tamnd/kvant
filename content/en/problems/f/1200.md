## Condition

There are two identical glasses on the table, one of which contains hot tea. Tea temperature $t_0$. The tea needs to be cooled to temperature $t_k$. This can be done in two ways:
1) immediately pour the tea into a second glass and wait until it cools to a temperature of $t_k$;
2) wait for the tea to cool to a certain temperature $t_1$ such that after pouring into the second glass the temperature of the tea will immediately be equal to $t_k$.

Which way is the fastest? It is known that the heat transfer of a glass with something is proportional to the temperature difference between the glass and the environment; heat exchange between the glass and the glass occurs very quickly. The heat capacity of a glass is $C_0$, the heat capacity of tea is $C$.
