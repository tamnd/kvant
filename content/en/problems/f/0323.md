## Condition

A U-shaped capillary tube with elbow length $l = 10\ см$ and diameters $d_1 = 0{,}1\ мм$ and $d_2 = 0{,}2\ мм$ is lowered vertically with open ends into water and immersed so much that the water level in the narrower elbow is level with the water level in the vessel. Find the position of the water level in the wide elbow. Neglect the volume of the horizontal tube. Atmospheric pressure is normal. Surface tension coefficient of water $\sigma = 70\ дн/см$.

B. B. Bukhovtsev
