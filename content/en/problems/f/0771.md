## Condition

In the circuit shown in figure 4, subsection D and a coil with inductance $L$ at time $t=0$ are connected to an alternating voltage source $u = U_M \cos \omega t$ using a key $K$. Determine the current in the coil as a function of time; graph this function. The diode and coil are considered ideal. The internal resistance of the source is to be claimed. (10)

## Solution

In the circuit shown in figure 1, diode D and coil with inductance $L$ at time $t=0$ are connected to an alternating voltage source $u = U_M \cos \omega t$ using key K. Determine the current in the coil as a function of time; graph this function. The diode and coil are considered ideal. The internal resistance of the source is pretenbr.

Fig. 2.

The voltage across the coil will change over time according to the law

$$u = U_M \cos \left( \omega t + \frac{3}{2} \pi \right) = U_M \sin \omega t.$$

So the coil will change according to the law

$$i = \frac{U_M}{\omega L} (1 - \cos \omega t)$$

(here it is taken into account that at $t = t_2$ $I = 0$).

figure 2 shows graphs $u(t)$ and $i(t)$.
