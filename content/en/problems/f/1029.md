## Condition

When a scab of water falls stationary on a flat saucer, the following picture can be observed: at a certain radius $r$ from the place where the scab falls, the water level is very low, and at a distance $r$ the level experiences a jump (Fig. 2). Estimate the radius $r$ if the water flow is $q$, the fall height is $H$, the height of the water step is $h$. Assume that the initial speed of water flowing from the tap is $v_0 \ll \sqrt{2gH}$.

A. A. Alabuzin, A. V. Umansky
