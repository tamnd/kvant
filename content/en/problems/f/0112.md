## Condition

There is a stack of $k$ plane-parallel plates whose refractive indices are equal to $n_1, n_2, \dots, n_k$. The thickness of each plate is $d$. How much will a ray incident on a plate with a refractive index of $n_1$ at an angle of $\alpha$ to the surface of the plate be displaced after passing through a stack of plates?
