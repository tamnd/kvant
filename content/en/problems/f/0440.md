## Condition

* Estimate the approximation at what minimum radius of a planet it can retain an atmosphere consisting mainly of oxygen and nitrogen if the planet's surface temperature is $T = 300^\circ\text{K}$. The average density of the planet's substance is taken equal to $\rho = 4 \cdot 10^3 \text{ kg}/\text{m}^3$.
