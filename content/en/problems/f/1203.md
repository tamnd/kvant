## Condition

On a rough railway platform there is a uniformly filled container of height H and width L, which has small wheels on one side (Fig. 1). When the train accelerates, the container begins to slide to the right along the platform if the acceleration acceleration exceeds a₀. With what minimum acceleration must the train brake for the container to start sliding downwards? Ignore rolling motion.
