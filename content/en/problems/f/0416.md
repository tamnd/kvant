## Condition

An airplane, having flown a distance l₁ along a straight line AB, gets from point A to point B in time t₁. Then he travels the distance l₂ along the straight line BC from point B to point C in time t₂ and returns to point A along the straight line CA, covering the distance l₃ in the time t₃. During the flight, the wind blows, the speed of which does not change throughout the flight. Determine the wind speed if the speed of the aircraft relative to the air is constant in absolute value throughout the entire flight.
