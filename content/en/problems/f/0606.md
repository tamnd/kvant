## Condition

The puck slides without initial speed along an inclined plane with an angle $\alpha$. The friction coefficient between the washer and the surface of the inclined plane changes with the distance $l$ from the top according to the law $\mu=kl$ ($k=\cos\theta$). At what distance from the top should the stop be placed so that after one elastic collision with the stop the puck stops as high as possible?
