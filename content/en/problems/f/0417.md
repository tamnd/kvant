## Condition

The heat-conducting cylinder contains 5 e of hydrogen and 12 e of oxygen. The mixture is set on fire. Determine the pressure and temperature in the vessel if it is known that when 1 mole of water is formed, 2.4·10⁵ ds are released. The volume of the vessel is 100 liters. The initial temperature of the mixture of oxygen and hydrogen is 20 °C. The heat capacity of hydrogen at constant volume is 14.3 kJ/kg deg, and that of water vapor is 2.1 kJ/kg deg.
