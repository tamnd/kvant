## Condition

When observing a droplet in the region beyond the limit, which increases in size, absorbing the smallest droplets encountered on its path, it was found that the droplet moves all the time with constant acceleration. Determine this acceleration, considering the initial droplet size to be small. Neglect air resistance during drop movement.

A. Stasenko
