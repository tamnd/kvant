## Condition

The electrical circuit was assembled from pieces of copper wire of different diameters d and 2d and different lengths L and 2L (Fig. 2). All sections of the circuit are in air at room temperature 20 q C. The terminals of the circuit A and B were connected to a powerful current source for a very short time and turned off. Immediately after switching off, a short section of wire with a larger diameter had a temperature 1 q C higher than room temperature. What were the temperatures of two other pieces of wire with the same diameter but different lengths at the same moment?
