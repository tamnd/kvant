## Condition

A vertical heat-conducting vessel containing a monatomic gas is closed by a piston of mass $M$. A heater with power $N$ is turned on in the vessel, and the piston begins to slowly move upward. How long will it take it to rise to a height $H$ relative to the initial position? The heat capacity of the piston, friction and atmospheric pressure are pretenbrets.
