## Condition

A train travels at a constant speed $v = 72 \text{ km/ч}$ along a horizontal section of track. How much should the power developed by the locomotive change so that the train continues to move at the same speed during a strong vertical rain? Consider that every second $m = 100 \text{ кг}$ of water falls on the composition, which then flows to the ground along the walls of the varons. Neglect the change in friction forces.

**WITH. M. Kozel**
