## Condition

A powerful transistor that generates heat is mounted on a heat-sinking plate, blown with air whose temperature is 30 °C. figure 2 shows the temperature distribution on the plate. Determine the power dissipation. It is known that a plate uniformly heated to 70 °C dissipates power $P=10$ W at an air temperature of 20 °C. Heat transfer is proportional to the temperature difference between the plate and air.

A. R. Zilberman
