## Condition

Two well cleaned plates, platinum and tungsten, are placed in a vacuum to form a parallel plate capacitor. The space between them is filled with carbon monoxide CO. Determine the resulting potential difference between the plates. Consider that CO molecules are deposited on the surface of a metal with a carbon-metal chemical bond formed; the CO molecule is a dipole (Fig. 5), $|\Delta q| \cdot l = 3,3 \cdot 10^{-31}$ C m.

E. N. Yunosov, I. V. Yaminsky
