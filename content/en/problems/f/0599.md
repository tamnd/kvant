## Condition

A rope is thrown through a block fixed in the ceiling, on which a load of mass $M$ is balanced by a ladder with a person of mass $m$. According to what law should a person move relative to the stairs so that the reaction of the block to the ceiling is zero? The block is weightless, the rope is completely flexible, inextensible and weightless. (8)
