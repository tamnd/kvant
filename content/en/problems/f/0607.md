## Condition

The plane flies along a closed route $ABC$. Points $A, B$ and $C$ lie at the vertices of a regular triangle. In which case will the time spent on the flight be less: if the wind blows in the direction of vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$ or if the wind blows in the direction of vector $\overrightarrow{BA}$?
