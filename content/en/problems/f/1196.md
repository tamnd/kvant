## Condition

The operating voltage of the light bulb, the current-voltage characteristic of which is shown in figure 3, is equal to 3.5 V (the curve reverses at a voltage of 4 V - the light bulb burns out). Two such light bulbs are connected in series and connected to a source with a voltage of 5 V. How much current will the circuit consume? What resistor should be connected in parallel with one of the bulbs so that the voltage on the other is 3.5 V? Will any of the light bulbs burn out if this resistor is replaced with another one of the same light bulb?

A. R. Zilberman
