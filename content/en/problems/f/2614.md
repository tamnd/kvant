## Condition

Cubic vessels are filled with sea water, and the initial temperature of the water 0 q C in both vessels is the same. The lengths of the edges of the cubes are in the ratio 1:3. The side walls and bottom of each vessel have very small heat capacities and do not conduct current - with the exception of the right and left walls. And these left and right walls are made of thin copper sheets. For a short time, the key in the circuit (Fig.3) is closed and opened again. In the vessel on the right, the temperature of the water rose by 1 degree during a short time of current flow.
