## Condition

A vertical pipe with a height of $H=1$ m and a cross-sectional area of ​​$S=50$ cm$^2$ is open at both ends. A heating element with a power of $N=100$ W is installed in the lower part of the pipe. What speed of the upward flow will be established in the pipe? Consider that the heater does not contaminate the cross-section of the pipe. Atmospheric pressure $p_0=1$ atm, outside temperature is room temperature. The molar heat capacity of air at a constant volume is $C_V=2,5$ R, where $R$ is the universal gas constant.

M. M. Tsypin
