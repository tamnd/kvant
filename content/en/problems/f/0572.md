## Condition

Peren studied the dependence on height of the number of spherical parts of a special resin - gummigut - in suspension of these parts in water. For a part with a radius of $r_1=0,212$ µm, he obtained the dependences, the graph of which is shown in figure 4 (n is the concentration of the part at a height of $h$, $n_0$ is their concentration at the bottom of the cuvette). The same graph is obtained for a part with a radius of $r_2=0,106$ µm, only the picture is stretched in height by 8 times. At the same time, it is known that the oxygen density in the earth’s atmosphere kills with height, as shown in figure 5 (ρ is the oxygen density at an altitude $H$, $\rho_0$ is at the surface of the cuvette). Determine the mass of an oxygen molecule.

The density of gummigut is $\rho_r=1,194 \text{ г/cm}^3$.
