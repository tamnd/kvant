## Condition

The two parallel-plate capacitors are arranged as shown in figure. The distance $L$ between the capacitors is much greater than the size of their plates and the distance d between the plates. The charge of one capacitor is $q_1$, the charge of the other is $q_2$. With what force are these capacitors hidden?

The field voltage created by a capacitor with a charge $q_1$ at points lying near the $O_1 O_2$ axis at a distance $x$ from this capacitor is equal to

$$E_x = \frac{q_1}{4\pi \varepsilon_0} \left( \frac{1}{x^2} - \frac{1}{(x+d)^2} \right) \approx \frac{q_1 d}{2\pi \varepsilon_0 x^3},$$

(x many larger sizes of capacitor plates).

Therefore, the force acting on a capacitor with charge $q_2$ is equal to

$$F = q_2 \left( E_L - E_{L+d} \right) = \frac{q_1 q_2 d}{2\pi \varepsilon_0} \left( \frac{1}{L^3} - \frac{1}{(L+d)^3} \right) \approx \frac{3 q_1 q_2 d^2}{2\pi \varepsilon_0 L^4}.$$

(We considered separately the case when adjacent capacitor plates have charges of different signs.)
