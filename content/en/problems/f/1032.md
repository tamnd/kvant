## Condition

On automatic vehicles, the rear view mirror is used to view the ongoing task. This mirror can be flat or convex. It is widely believed that one of the disadvantages of a convex mirror is the uneven growth of the image of objects uniformly approaching it, which is why the driver of an automatic vehicle with such a mirror pays attention to the automatic vehicle catching up in the flow of traffic only when it leaves the lane to overtake. Is this idea correct?

Find the relationship between the speed of uniformly moving water of the main optical axis of a spherical mirror of an object and the rate of change in the size of its image in the mirror. Compare a convex rear view mirror with a flat one. What are its advantages and disadvantages?

V. A. Kotov
