## Condition

In the system shown in figure 2, there is no friction, the spring is elastic and

⟦figure⟧

Fig. 2

weightless, small balls are identical and elastic. The right side of the rod smoothly transitions into a circular arc of radius R located in the vertical plane, and at the transition point there is a right ball. The distance between the balls is equal to R. If, without removing the balls or holding them by external forces, the system is positioned so that the straight part of the rod takes a vertical position, then in a state of equilibrium the spring will compress by the amount R. From the position shown at figure, the right ball is deflected so that its angular displacement along the arc is small and equal to B ( B ≪ 1).

⟦column⟧

1) Find the time during which the right ball first returns to the initial position indicated on figure. 2) Find the magnitude of the velocity of the right ball on the horizontal section. 3) Find the maximum amount of compression of the spring after the balls hit. 4) Find the time of movement of the left ball between the first two hits of the balls. 5) Find the period of oscillation of this system.
