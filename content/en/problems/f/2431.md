## Condition

Three capacitors with electrical capacitances C, 2C and 3C are connected in a closed circuit in series one after another. Using a special device connected to the three connection points of the capacitors, electric charges +Q, +Q and –2Q were communicated (transferred) to these same places. The device was turned off. What is the maximum (minimum) energy that can be stored in a system of charged capacitors? S.Varlamov
