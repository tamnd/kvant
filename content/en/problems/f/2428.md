## Condition

A group of three tourists must move from point A to point B along a road of length s = 45 km. Everyone starts at the same time. There are only two bicycles for the entire group of tourists, and if two people are riding a bicycle, then their speed is 3v, and if one person is riding a bicycle, then their speed is 4v. If a tourist walks, then his speed is v = 5 km/h. In what minimum time can all tourists reach their destination? V. Sergeev
