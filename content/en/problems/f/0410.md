## Condition

In an electron-based device, a pure tungsten cathode is located in a large flask containing residual oxygen at a pressure of $p = 10^{-7}$ atm and a temperature of $T = 300^\circ\text{K}$. Assuming that every molecule that hits the cathode sticks to it, estimate the time for the formation of a monomolecular layer. Molecules can be considered balls with a diameter of $d \approx 3 \cdot 10^{-8}$ cm. (9 cells)
