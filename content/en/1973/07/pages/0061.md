To the article “Motion of charged parts in an electric field”

1. $T_0 = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g}}; T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{a}}$,

where $a = g + \frac{qE}{m}$, if the vector $E$ is vertical,

and $a = \sqrt{g^2 + \left(\frac{qE}{m}\right)^2}$ if the vector $E$ is much larger; $\frac{T}{T_0} = \sqrt{\frac{g}{a}}$.

2. $v = |e| \sqrt{\frac{l_1 - l_2}{ml_1 l_2}}$.

3. Electrons will not hit the plate when the charge on the capacitor becomes such that $\frac{mv^2}{2} = -eU$, where $U = \frac{Q}{c} = \frac{Qd}{\varepsilon_0 S}$,

that is, at $\frac{mv^2}{2} = -\frac{Qed}{\varepsilon_0 S}$. Hence $Q = -\frac{mv^2 S \varepsilon_0}{2ed} = -netS$ and $t = \frac{\varepsilon_0 mv^2}{2e^2 \pi d}$.

4. From the law of conservation of energy it follows that $\frac{mv^2}{2} + \frac{Qq}{\varepsilon_0 l} = \frac{mv'^2}{2} + \frac{Qq}{\varepsilon_0 R}$.

Hence $v' = \sqrt{v^2 - \frac{Qq}{m} \frac{(l - R)}{\varepsilon_0 lR}}$.

1. We are only interested in the ratio of the voluntariness of labor, so we can take the total amount of work as 1, the time required for factories to complete it (before increasing voluntariness) also as 1, and denote the voluntariness by $v_1$ and $v_2$. According to the conditions of the task

$v_1 + v_2 = 1$,

$(v_1 + 2v_2) \left(1 - \frac{2}{15} \frac{1}{v_1}\right) = 1$.

What is the attitude of $k = v_2 : v_1$? Let's substitute $v_2 = kv_1$ into the first equation. We get $v_1 + kv_1 = 1$, whence $v_1 = \frac{1}{1 + k}$.

Substituting $v_2 = \frac{k}{1 + k}$ and $v_1 = \frac{1}{1 + k}$ into the second equation, we find:

$\left(\frac{1}{1 + k} + 2 \frac{k}{1 + k}\right) \left(1 - \frac{2(1 + k)}{15}\right) = 1$,

whence $4k^2 - 9k + 2 = 0$, $k_1 = 2$, $k_2 = \frac{1}{4}$.
