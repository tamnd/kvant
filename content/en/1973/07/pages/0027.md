M214. The square triangle f(x) = ax² + bx + c is such that the equation f(x) = x has no real roots. Prove that the equation f(f(x)) = x also has no real roots. (10 grades)

Yu. I. Ionin

M215.* On an infinite checkered sheet of white paper, n cells are colored black. At time t = 1, 2..., a simultaneous recoloring of all cells of the sheet will occur according to the following rule: each cell k acquires the color that most of the three cells had at the previous moment: the cell k itself and its neighbors to the right and above (if two or three of these cells were white, then k becomes white, if two or three of them were black, then black).

a) Prove that after a finite time there will be no black cells left on the sheet.

6) Prove that black cells will disappear no later than at time t = n. (10 grades)

A. L. Toom

F223. The model of the ship was pushed and reported a speed of v₀ = 10 m/s. When the model moves, it is acted upon by a resistance force proportional to the speed: F = -kv. a) Find the distance traveled by the model during the time during which its speed was halved. b) Find the path covered by the model before it comes to a complete stop. Consider k = 0.5 ke/s. Model mass m = 0.5 ke. (8 grades)

F224. Along wooden ramps forming an angle α with the horizon, a box is inserted behind the rope. Coefficient of friction of the box about the convergence μ. At what angle to the horizon should the rope be directed in order to insert the box with the smallest number: a) evenly, 6) with a given acceleration a? (8, 10 grades)

F225. Two immiscible liquids are poured into a glass: carbon tetrachloride CCl₄ and water. At normal atmospheric pressure, CCl₄ boils at 76.7° C, water - at 100° C. When a glass is uniformly heated in a water bath, boiling at the liquid interface begins at a temperature of 65.5° C. Determine which liquid boils quickly at such “border” boiling and by how many times. The saturated vapor pressure of water at 65.5° C is 192 mm pm. cm. (9th, 10th grade)

F226. An independently excited DC electric motor (with a permanent magnet) lifts the load at a speed v₁ using a thread wound around the motor axis. In the absence of a load, the weightless thread rises with speed v₀. At what speed will the same load be lowered if a short circuit occurs in the armature circuit, as a result of which the armature winding becomes short-circuited? We have a complaint about the bearings. (10 grades)

F227. Two coils are wound on a horopod ferrite core with magnetic permeability μ = 2000: a primary one containing n₁ = 2000 turns, and a secondary one with n₂ = 4000 turns. When the voltage U₁ = 100.0 V was applied to the primary coil, on the open secondary coil, if the core is replaced with a core of the same size, but made of ferrite with μ' = 20. Magnetic flux leakage and losses in the core are not taken into account. (10 grades)
