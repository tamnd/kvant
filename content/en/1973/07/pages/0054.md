The Department of Physics of Magnetic Phenomena trains specialists in the field of magnetic phenomena in metals and alloys, which is of great scientific and industrial importance. With the use of magnetic communication materials, the development of electrical engineering, radio engineering, automation, etc.

The Department of Semiconductor Optics and Radiospectroscopy trains specialists to work in the field of radiospectroscopy, atomic and molecular specialization and semiconductor physics.

The departments have laboratories equipped with modern apparatus and instruments, where senior students gain experience in research work and participate directly in the implementation of the scientific program of the departments.

The Department of Astronomy and Geodesy trains astronomers of a wide profile. There are observatories and various laboratories: astronomy, astrometry, time services and others.

In the department of astronomy, specialization begins from the first year, and in other departments of the faculty - from the third.

In the first years, students receive good mathematical training, which is then deepened by specialization in mathematical disciplines in the senior years. All students become familiar with the methods of modern computing technology and with working on electronic computers.

Graduates of the Faculty of Physics work in research institutes, design bureaus, in factory laboratories, and also teach physics in universities, technical and secondary schools.

Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics

Mathematics

1. An Automobile Taxi left the airport for the city center, and at the same time an AutoRus Express left the city center for the airport. When the taxi taxi drove half the way, the taxi had 19.2 km left to the end of the route, and when the taxi had driven half the way, the taxi had 12 km left to the end of the route. Find the ratio of the speed of a taxi and a car if these speeds are the same throughout the journey.

2. The diameter of the ball is the height of a regular tetrahedron with edge a. What fraction of the surface area of ​​the tetrahedron is contained within the sphere?

3. Solve inequality
$$\lg|x^2 - x - 1| \geq \lg|x^2 + x - 2|.$$
4. At what values ​​of α does the system
$$\begin{cases} 8 \cos x \cos y (x - y) + 1 = 0, \\ x + y = \alpha \end{cases}$$
has a solution?

Physics

Ticket 1

1. Uniform movement in a circle. Linear and angular velocities. Centrifugal force and centripetal acceleration (examples).

2. Electric current in gases. Ionization of gases through collisions. Various types of discharges in gases.

3. The light source is located at a double focal distance from the collecting line on its optical axis. A flat mirror is placed behind the lens perpendicular to the optical axis. Rays of light passing through the lens are reflected from the mirror, pass through the lens a second time and exit from it in a parallel path. Determine the distance from the lens to the mirror.

Ticket 2

1. Evaporation and condensation. Saturation and unsaturation of a pair, their properties. Dependence of vapor pressure on temperature.

2. Lens formula. Construction of an image in lenses.

3. A rubber ball of mass m and radius R is immersed in water to a depth h and released. How high will the ball bounce from the surface of the water? Ignore water and air resistance.

Ticket 3

1. Newton's first law. Examples.

2. Transmission of electricity over a distance. Transformer, principles of its design and operation.
