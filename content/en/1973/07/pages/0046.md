Introductory
university exams

In this issue we continue to publish information about a number of institutes and universities and invite the attention of readers of entrance exams in physics and mathematics to these universities.

Moscow
energy institute

The Moscow Order of Lenin Energy Institute (MPEI) has nine faculties that train specialists to work in all branches of modern energy.

Students study various methods of generating energy, including nuclear power plants and MHD generators; technically energy, including cybernetics of large energy systems covering the entire country or even a number of countries.

No less important are the problems of energy use in industry, transport and other areas of the national economy. This is the creation of all types of electric motors (from micromotors to heavy-duty motors), obtaining high and low temperatures, the use of superconductivity, etc. Non-energy specializations include radio engineering, automatic equipment, computer technology, industrial electronics, electronic devices, and lighting engineering.

A number of specialties are of an engineering-physical nature: engineering electrophysics, engineering thermal physics, radiophysics and optical-electronic devices (at the department of physics).

A characteristic feature of MPEI is the widespread involvement of students in research work at the departments. To prepare for this work, for many years the curriculum has included educational and research work carried out under the guidance of a teacher.

Every year the student organizes scientific conferences and competitions for the best work.

Below we provide the tickets for the 1972 Physics Oral Entrance Examination.

Ticket 1
1. Derivation of the equation of uniform motion.
2. Magnetic interaction of conductors with current. The force acting on a current-carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field.
3. A cylinder containing 1 ke of nitrogen exploded at a temperature of 350°C during use. What amount of hydrogen (in grams) can be stored in this cylinder at 20°C, having a safety factor of five?
The density of nitrogen under normal conditions is 0.00125 e/cm³, the density of hydrogen under normal conditions is 0.00009 e/cm³.

Ticket 2
1. Series connection of conductors in a DC circuit. Series connection of current sources. Ohm's law for a complete circuit.
2. Laws of light refraction. The virtual image given by lenses. Constructing an image. Application of the lens formula. Magnifier.
