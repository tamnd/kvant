Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering

The Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering (MIEM) is one of the youngest universities in Moscow; it was created in 1962 and trains highly qualified specialists for the electronics and radio industries. Its creation is a consequence of the rapid development of radio-electronic technology, which required a significant number of specialists combining deep engineering knowledge with advanced physics and mathematics training.

MIEM has the faculties of semiconductor and electric vacuum engineering, automation and computer engineering, radio engineering, applied mathematics, evening and two special faculties of applied mathematics and automated control systems.

The Faculty of Semiconductor and Electro-Vacuum Engineering prepares mechanical engineers to work on the creation and operation of new technological equipment for the electronics industry, as well as to develop advanced methods for manufacturing devices for automating production processes.
