Modern computers are a complex complex: they are able to implement in a short time not only the most complex computational, but also logical operations, organize arrays of information and classify information. But to effectively use the capabilities of a computer, it is necessary to give the machine a program that ensures automatic planning of the work of its individual devices and the organization of its work as a whole. To do this, it is necessary to develop computer software, which represents modern transport, operating systems, libraries of standard subroutines and other software elements.

A characteristic feature of the training plan for mathematical engineers and systems engineers is that from the very first days of study at the institute, the student, along with the classical sections of mathematics, studies computer programming, mathematical logic, methods for solving problems on a computer, and a number of special mathematical disciplines. This plan makes it possible, already in the first year, to carry out a semester-long practice on numerical methods and algorithms, which included solving problems on the use of computational methods of linear algebra, interpolating functions, calculating the roots of algebraic and transcendental equations. At the same time, computational methods and algorithms are discussed from the point of view of their implementation on a computer.

The existing system of mathematical education of mathematical engineers and systems engineers at MIEM is a factor determining the mathematical training of students of all specialties of the institute.

Naturally, this circumstance is taken into account when selecting applicants for entrance exams. But one should not think that options for written or oral exams go beyond the scope of the school curriculum. For example, any applicant who has firmly agreed on the school curriculum can successfully cope with it.

Below are the options offered in the written mathematics entrance examination in 1972.

Option 1

1. Two factories must jointly process a certain amount of raw materials. If the randomness of labor in the second factory were doubled, then the time required for the factories to do the work would be reduced by $2/15$ parts of the time required for the first factory alone to do the work. In which of the factories is the randomness higher and by how many times, if it is known that each factory processed at least one third of the raw materials.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x = a (\sin^8 x - \cos^8 x).$$

3. Find the domains and ranges of a function
$$y = \lg \left( \frac{3x+1}{x-2} - 2 \right).$$

4. Solve inequality
$$(9^x - 2 \cdot 3^x - 3) \log_2^2 \left( x - \frac{1}{2} \right) \leq 0.$$

5. A plane is drawn through the vertex of a regular triangular paramid and the middle of the two sides of the base. Determine the cross-sectional area, knowing the side $a$ of the base of the paramid and the angle $\alpha$ formed by the section with the plane of the base.

Option 2

1. Find natural solutions to the system
$$\begin{cases} m = n + 2, \\ mn \leq 17, \\ \frac{n+1}{m+2} < \frac{1}{2}. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve inequality
$$(4^2x - 2 \cdot 4^x - 3) (4 \cdot 3^x - 9)^2 < 0.$$

3. Find the values ​​of φ for which the sum of the squares of the roots of the equation
$$x^2 + (\sin \varphi - 1) x - \frac{1}{2} \cos^2 \varphi = 0$$

4. Solve the equation
