The entrance exam in mathematics at all faculties of MIIT consists of a written work, the completion of which is allotted four astronomical hours, and an oral exam. The exception is the specialty "accounting", where only an oral exam in mathematics is conducted.

As experience shows, it is the written exam that is most difficult for applicants, despite the fact that the examination committee includes tasks in the tickets that require only solid and solid knowledge of the school mathematics course.

Oral exam tickets consist of two theoretical questions in algebra, geometry or trigonometry and one problem on solving an algebraic equation, inequality or system of equation, trigonometric equation or identity, investigation of functions or graphing.

Options for written work and oral exam tasks are divided into three concepts according to complexity, depending on the requirements for knowledge of the basics of mathematics in different specialties. The greatest challenges are presented in the specialty “applied mathematics”. This is quite understandable; future engineers are required to have higher knowledge of the basics of mathematics.

The next most difficult options are presented in the specialty: automated control systems, electronic computers, automation and telemechanics in industry.

Variants of the third concept, and there were most of them, are represented by knowledge of other specialties.

Below are the 1972 options.

Option 1
(specialties: industrial and civil engineering, transport expertise, construction economics, transport economics, railway construction, places and tunnels).

1. Two workers began to do some work at the same time. After 2 hours they stopped working and calculated that one of them could complete the rest of the work, working separately, in 3.2 hours, and the other in 4.8 hours. How long will it take each of them to complete this work separately?

2. Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases}
\frac{x-y}{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}} = 10, \\
\sqrt{xy} = 16.
\end{cases}$$

3. Determine the volume of a regular quadrangular straight line if the area of ​​its diagonal section is S and the side edge forms an angle α with the plane of the base.

4. Solve the equation:

$$\sqrt{2}\cos x \cdot \text{ctg}x + \sqrt{6}\cos x - \text{ctg}x = \sqrt{3}.$$

Option 2
(specialties: automation, telemechanics and communications, electronics of transport, locomotive engineering, automated control systems).

1. Two cylinders roll along an inclined plane of some length, one of which has a circumference of 2 cm, the other 3 cm. The first cylinder made 10 more revolutions than the second, rolling down the inclined plane. The circumferences of both cylinders were adjusted by the same number of centimeters, after which one of them made 3 revolutions more than the second. What is the length of the inclined plane and how long were the circumferences of the cylinders notified?

2. Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases}
x^2 + y^2 = 4 + \frac{y}{2}, \\
\log_3(x+2y) + \log_3(x-2y) = 1.
\end{cases}$$

3. In a right triangle, given the acute angle α and the distance a from the vertex of the other acute angle to the center of the inscribed circle. Determine the area of ​​the triangle.

4. Find cos (70° + α) if sin (40° + α) = b and 0° < α < 45°.
