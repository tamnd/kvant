Moscow Institute of Railway Transport Engineers
The Moscow Institute of Railway Transport Engineers is one of the oldest universities in the Soviet Union, and now one of the leading technical universities in the country.
The scientific and technological revolution, taking place throughout the country and in part in railway transport, determined a qualitative step in the development of MINTA and opened up new broad prospects for graduates of the institute, who undertake important both engineering and scientific tasks: an increase in the volume of transportation, a sharp increase in traffic speed, the development and implementation of new modern designs of cars, diesel locomotives, electric locomotives, the introduction of modern methods and means of constructing railway tracks, engineering structures, places and tunnels, a change in the nature of managing the process of transporting goods, the widespread introduction of automation, telemechanics, electronics, computer-aided technical methods for planning and optimizing transportation. Due to the wide profile of the graduates, MINT essentially turned into the largest polytechnic institute.

The institute has 10 faculties that train specialists in 19 specialties: automated control systems; automation and teleautomatics (in industry); automation, teleautomatics and communications in railway transport; accounting; carriage and carriage farming; locomotive; places and tunnels; organization of mechanized processing of economic information; applied mathematics; industrial telloenergy; industrial and civil construction; construction of railways, track and track management; construction and expensive machinery and equipment; tellow trucks and tellow transport facilities; economics and organization of construction (on railways); operation of railways; electrification of railway transport; electronics computers.

Applicants will take the following exams: mathematics (written and oral), physics (oral), Russian language and literature (written).
