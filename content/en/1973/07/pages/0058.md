⟦folio 56⟧

«Kvant» for younger students

Artist E. V. Nazarov

1. Two fishermen were asked:
“How many fish are in your baskets?”
“My basket contains half the number of fish that are in his basket, plus 10 more,” answered the first. “And I have as many fish in my basket as he has, and 20 more,” said the second one. How many fish do obonhas have?

2. The wind blows the balloon in a northerly direction. In what direction does the flag attached to the top of the gondola deviate?

3. An equilateral triangle was attached to the triangle at figure so that a new triangle was obtained. How many ways can this be done?

4. Given two perfectly identical long iron rods. One of them is magnetized. How to determine which of the two rods is magnetized without using any other objects?

5. In the closet I found a piece of rectangular plywood, laid out into 64 squares (see figure). “It would be nice to make a chessboard out of it,” I thought. But how? Help me cut this piece of plywood into 2 pieces so that they can be used to glue a chessboard together.

6. It is easy to make snowballs from such snow, but when the snow temperature is much below 0°C, snowballs are very difficult to make. Why?
