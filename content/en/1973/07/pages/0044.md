Television physics and mathematics courses for university applicants

In May of this year, tests in physics and mathematics were held for course employees.

In this issue we publish some of the problems proposed during the tests.

Physics
1. Determine the current flowing through source $E_2$, in the circuit indicated at figure 1, if
$$E_1 = 10 \text{ } \theta; r_1 = 2,5 \text{ } \omega;$$
$$E_2 = 8,0 \text{ } \theta; r_2 = 10 \text{ } \omega; R = 10 \text{ } \omega.$$
2. A conductor with length $L = 80 \text{ } \omega$ and resistance $R = 3,5 \text{ } \omega$ moves with a constant speed $V$ perpendicular to the confluent lines of a uniform magnetic field, the induction of which is $B = 0,45 \text{ } \text{m} \text{ } \text{l}$ (Fig. 2). The conductor is connected to the current source by flexible wires, the resistance of which can be changed. Current $I = 4,0 \text{ } \text{a}$ flows in the circuit. Define $V$.
3. An electron, accelerating the potential difference $U$, flies into a uniform magnetic field, the induction of which is $B = 0,80 \text{ } \text{m} \text{ } \text{l}$ (the induction lines are perpendicular to the direction of motion of the electron), and describes a circle of radius $R = 1,5 \text{ } \text{cm}$ in it. Find $U$.
4. Calculate the position of the image of the point $A$ in the system of two intrinsic and homogeneously scattering thin lines. The main optical axes of the lines coincide.
