Solution. The charge $q$ is acted upon by two forces - the force $F_1$ interaction with the charge $Q$: $F_1 = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Qq}{x^2}$ (x is the distance between the charges), and the force $F_2$ interaction with the field $E$:

$$F_2 = Eq.$$

If at the initial moment $F_1 > F_2$, that is $\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{qQ}{l^2} > Eq$, then after the charge $q$ is released, it will move to the right, and if $F_1 < F_2$ - to the left. To determine the case, let us consider the case $F_1 > F_2$. The multi-acting force $F_1$ and $F_2$ imparts to the charge $q$ the acceleration $a = \frac{F_1 + F_2}{m}$, directed at the beginning of the movement along the line of action of the force $F_1$ ($a = \frac{F_1 - F_2}{2}$). The $F_2$ force does not depend on the position of the charge, but the $F_1$ force kills as the distance between the charges increases. When the magnitude is $F_2$, the multi-acting force $F_1$ and $F_2$ will be directed towards the charge $Q$ and will cause a reduced speed of the charge $q$. Obviously, the charge speed $q$ is maximum when $F_1 = F_2$, that is, when

$$\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Qq}{x^2} = Eq.$$

The distance between charges $q$ and $Q$ at this moment is

$$x_1 = \sqrt{\frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 E}}.$$

To find the charge speed $q$ at this moment in time, we use the law of conservation of energy:

$$-\Delta W_{\text{пот}} = \Delta W_{\text{кини}}$$

or

$$(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2)q = \frac{mv^2}{2},$$

where $\varphi_1$, $\varphi_2$ are the potentials of points $x = l$ and $x = x_1$. But

$$\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = -E(x_1 - l) +$$

1. How many times will the period of oscillation of the mathematical matrix change if its ball of mass $m$ is charged with charge $q$ and the magnet is placed in a uniform electric field with intensity $E$ (field vertical or horizontal)?
2. At what minimum relative speed can protons located at a distance of $l_1 = 5$ cm from each other approach each other to a distance of $l_2 = 10^{-4}$ cm?
3. One of the plates of a flat capacitor with a distance between the plates of $d = 10$ mm is “illuminated” by RENT rays, tearing out photoelectrons from it with a speed of $v = 10^6$ m/s. The electrons are collected on the second plate. How long will it take for the photoflow to stop if $n = 10^{13}$ electrons per second are ejected from each square centimeter of the plate?
4. In a charged fixed sphere of radius $R$ there is a small hole through which a charge $q$ flies into it, moving along the radius of the sphere. What speed will this charge have at the center of the sphere if at a distance $l$ from the sphere its speed is $v$ and the charge of the sphere is $Q$?
