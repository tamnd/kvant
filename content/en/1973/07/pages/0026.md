M211. Given $n$ points, $n > 4$. Prove that you can connect them with arrows so that from each point you can get to each point by following either one arrow or two (each two points can be connected by an arrow in only one direction; you can follow the arrow only in the direction indicated on it). (8 grades)

G. Sh. Friedman

M212. At the trial, 14 coins were presented as evidence. The expert discovered that seven of them were fake, the weakened ones were real, and he found out which ones were fake and which ones were real. The court only knows that counterfeit coins have the same value, real coins have the same value, and counterfeit coins are lighter than real ones. The expert wants to prove to the court by weighing on cup scales without grime that all the counterfeit coins he discovered are indeed counterfeit, and the rest are real. Will he be able to do it? (8th, 9th grade)

R. V. Freivald
