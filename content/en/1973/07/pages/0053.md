Ural State University named after A. M. Gorky

Ural State University named after A. M. Gorky is one of the largest educational and scientific centers in the Urals; it was opened in October 1920 by decree signed by Vladimir Ilch Lenin.

Currently, the Ural University has 8 faculties, including mathematics and mechanics and physics.

Matematiko - Faculty of Mechanics prepares highly qualified mathematicians and computer scientists who are capable of solving applied problems, as well as specialists who can conduct independent scientific research, developing and solving purely mathematical problems.

In the first two years, all students of the faculty master general theoretical disciplines; from the third year, specialization begins; in the fifth year, students of the faculty undergo long-term voluntary practice in large computer centers and research institutes of the country, in laboratories and design bureaus of factories.

The Matematiko-Mechanical Faculty provides its students with the opportunity to study theory and its applications. Under the guidance of leading scientists, students acquire independent research skills. During the learning process, they actively participate in the scientific life of all six departments of the faculty.

The Faculty has a Computing Center. Students use modern computers to solve applied problems.

Graduates of the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics apply to work in computer centers, research institutes, laboratories and design bureaus of large factories, teaching work in universities and technical fields.

The Faculty of Physics of the University, which has the departments of theoretical physics, solid body physics, physics of magnetic phenomena, optics of semiconductors and radio-systems, as well as the department of astronomy and geodesy, produces a special wide-ranging profile.

The Department of Theoretical Physics prepares theoretical physicists, mainly familiar with the problems of solid body physics. During their studies, students become familiar with the use of modern mathematical methods to solve physical and technical problems in the specified area of ​​physics. Some theoretical students undergo voluntary practice and complete coursework and diploma work directly in the theoretical department and laboratories of the Institute of Metal Physics of the USSR Academy of Sciences.
