The Faculty of Automation and Computer Engineering graduates electrical engineers specializing in the design of computers, telecontrol and telecontrol systems, information and measuring automatic devices using modern discrete and continuous computers. Engineers who graduate from MIEM in this specialization can calculate and design computer elements, including creating and using machine methods for calculating elements. They can also conduct research into technologies for manufacturing processes of elements in order to find optimal modes.

The Faculty of Radio Engineering trains radio equipment design engineers. Graduates of this faculty can work as design and technological engineers in the fields of the radio-electronic industry, develop and organize the production of radio-measuring equipment in design bureaus, instructions and factories, work as technological engineers at the college, within which the Faculty of Applied Mathematics was organized in 1967 with day and evening departments. Following MIEM, departments of applied mathematics were organized at MIPT, MAI, MEPhI and many universities.
