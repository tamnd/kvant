Mathematics
Test No. 1
Solve equations and systems of equations:

1. $\log_{37} x^5 + \log_{\frac{3}{3}} \sqrt[3]{x} = 3$;
2. $2^{2x+1} \cdot 5^x - 2^{2x-1} \cdot 5^{x+1} + 4000 = 0$;
3. $\begin{cases} x^3 - x^2 y - 4xy^2 + 4y^3 = 0, \\ 2x^2 + y^2 = 12; \end{cases}$
4. $\begin{cases} \frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{5} \log_1(x+y) = 2, \\ 2 \log_3(x+y) = \log_1(x^2-y^2) = 2. \end{cases}$
5. Find a six-digit number using the following conditions: if you put the number 1130 instead of the first three digits, you will get a number that is 9 times larger than the one you are looking for; The logarithm to base 5 of a number resulting from discarding the last three digits is one more than the logarithm to base 25 of a number remaining when discarding the first three digits.
6. The areas of the lateral faces of a trihedral paramid are equal to $F_1, F_2, F_3$; the area of ​​the base is $F_0$. The lengths of the perpendiculars dropped from the top of the paramid to the sides of the base are equal to each other. Find the volume of the paramid.
7. Control work No. 2
1. A truncated cone is described around the ball, the bases of which are large circles of the other two balls. Determine the total surface area of ​​the truncated cone if the sum of the surface areas of the three balls is equal to $S$.
2. Determine the volume of a body of revolution obtained by rotating a triangle into a circle with a straight line lying in the plane of this triangle, but not intersecting it, if the area of ​​the triangle is $S$ and the distance from the point of intersection of its medians to the axis of rotation is $r$.
3. A ball is inscribed in a cone. The radius of the circle along which the lateral surface of the cone touches the ball is $r$. The radius of the ball drawn to a point on this circle is inclined to its plane at an angle $\alpha$. Find the volume of the cone.
4. The straight line connecting the midpoints of the crossing edges of a regular tetrahedron serves as the axis of a cylinder, the circles of the bases of which touch the faces of the tetrahedron at their centers. Find the ratio of the volume of the cylinder to the volume of the tetrahedron.
5. The ball touches all the edges of the cube. Find the ratio of the volume of the part of the cube that is outside the cube to the volume of the cube.
