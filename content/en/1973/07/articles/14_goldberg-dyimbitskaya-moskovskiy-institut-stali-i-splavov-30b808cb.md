Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys

The Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys is the country's leading university in training engineering and scientific personnel and conducting research in the field of metallurgy and automation of the production of ferrous, non-ferrous, rare and radioactive metals, processing of metals and alloys and physics of metals.

The institute has 5,800 students, including 4,300 full-time students. During their studies, students receive extensive physical, chemical and mathematical training necessary to master the science of metals and alloys and semi-conductor materials, the foundation of the theory and practice of modern production methods for processing ferrous, non-ferrous and rare metals, and automation of control of metallurgical processes.

The Institute also trains personnel through graduate school, which is one of the largest in the country - 548 people study there.

The institute has the following faculties: metallurgy of ferrous metals and alloys (MCHMiS), metallurgy of non-ferrous and rare metals and alloys (MCRMiS), physical and chemical (FC), semi-conductor materials and devices (PMP) and an evening faculty. The institute has a daytime preparatory department and evening preparatory courses in Moscow and in the heels of industrial centers: Bekabad, Zaporozhye, Orsk, Ust-Kamenogorsk, Cherepovets. The institute has a physics and mathematics school and a Sunday lecture.

Applicants to the faculties of PH and PMP take mathematics (written and oral), physics (oral), Russian language and literature (written); to the faculties of MCHMIS, MCRMiS and evening - mathematics (written), physics (oral), chemistry (oral), Russian language and literature (written).
