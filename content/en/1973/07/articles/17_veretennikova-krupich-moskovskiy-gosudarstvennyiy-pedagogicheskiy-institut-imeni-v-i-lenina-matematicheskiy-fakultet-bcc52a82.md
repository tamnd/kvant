Moscow State Pedagogical Institute named after V. I. Lenin
(faculty of mathematics)

The Moscow Order of Lenin and the Order of Labor Red Replacement State Pedagogical Institute named after V.I. Lenin is a

⟦folio 54⟧

the highest pedagogical educational institution in the country. The Institute was created on the basis of the Higher Women's Courses, organized in Moscow in 1872.

The faculty trains secondary school teachers in two specialties: a mathematics teacher and a mathematics teacher with the right to teach in a foreign language (English, French).

The faculty has an evening department that trains secondary school mathematics teachers. The duration of study in full-time and evening departments is 5 years.

The Faculty of Mathematics includes the following departments: mathematical analysis, algebra, geometry, number theory, computational mathematics and programming, methods of teaching mathematics and physics for non-physical specialties.

The “mathematics” department trains mathematics teachers for grades IV-X in secondary schools; The department of “mathematics in a foreign language” trains mathematics teachers for special secondary schools with teaching in English and French.

Students of these two departments undergo teaching practice in Moscow secondary schools and free practice on electronic computers.

Special offices and laboratories have been created in all departments of the faculty. The Department of Computational Mathematics and Programming has an electronic design kit.

During the years of study at the faculty, students study differential and integral calculus, algebra, geometry (analytical, differential, spatial, foundations of geometry, etc.), number theory, mathematical logic, theory of algorithms, theory of functions of a real and complex variable, probability theory, computers and programming, equations of mathematical physics, methods of teaching mathematics, general physics, theoretical mechanics, etc. Students also study social, political and psychological disciplines.

Students of the Faculty of Mathematics participate in special and random special courses in various sections of general algebra, linear programming, manifold functioning, group theory, Galois theory, associative algebra, algebraic number theory, general and algebraic topology, multidimensional geometry, relativity theory, quantum field theory, theory of random processes, etc.

At the faculty of student work scientific society. Faculty students are involved in scientific work in mathematics and conduct research work on teaching mathematics in secondary schools. The best students who have demonstrated an ability for research work are, as a rule, recommended for graduate school. Postgraduate training is carried out in all departments of the faculty.

The entrance examination in mathematics at the Faculty of Mathematics consists of a written paper and an oral examination.

The written work includes tasks that require a good and conscious knowledge of the school curriculum and, of course, certain abilities in mathematics. Oral exam tickets include two theoretical questions in accordance with mathematics programming for high school.

$$\log_2(x - 1) < 2.$$

2. Solve the equation:
$$\text{ctg}(\pi + x) + 2 \cdot \cos x = 2 + \frac{1}{\sin x}.$$
