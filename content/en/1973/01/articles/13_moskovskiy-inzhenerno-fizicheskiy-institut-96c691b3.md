⟦folio 40⟧

The Moscow Engineering Physics Institute was founded in 1942 and is currently one of the leading universities in the country. Now MEPhI has faculties of experimental and theoretical physics, physics and energy, automation and electronics, cybernetics, a special faculty of physics and a faculty of advanced training in physics at the country's enterprise universities.

The faculties of experimental and theoretical physics and physical-energetics train physics and mathematical engineers to work in the field of theoretical and experimental physics, design and experimental physical installations, apparatus and equipment.

The Faculty of Automation and Electronics produces physics engineers specializing in the field of creation and experimentation of electronic devices of modern physical devices, development and creation of automatic control systems for technical and physical processes of modern production.

The Faculty of Cybernetics trains engineers in the design and experimental design of modern high-speed electronic computers, in automated control systems and in applied mathematics. Graduates of this faculty are awarded the qualification of electrical engineer or mathematical engineer.

The Special Faculty of Physics trains specialists in the latest areas of modern physics. A graduate of the faculty is awarded the qualification of engineer-physicist. Training is carried out according to individual plans. Recruitment is carried out for the 3rd year from among excellent students of universities and other universities by visiting commissions in September-October. Classes start in February.

The wide profile of graduates provides engineering, physics and mathematics training that is uniform for all faculties during the first 2.5 years of study.

Naturally, the in-depth physics and mathematics education at the institute, as well as the wide profile of graduates, require special care when selecting applicants for entrance exams. This does not mean that the problems, examples and questions offered in the entrance exams are overly difficult or beyond the scope of the school curriculum. For example, as will be seen from the material below, successful solution of the problem and examples in the entrance exams is available to any applicant, strictly in accordance with the school curriculum, but only to graduates of physics and mathematics schools or to schoolchildren who have undergone special training in preparatory courses, in mathematics courses, etc.

⟦folio 41⟧

The entrance exam in mathematics at MEPhI consists of a written paper, which will take four astronomical hours to complete, and an oral exam.

The written work contains four tasks:

1) task of composing and solving an equation, system of equations or inequalities;
2) stereometric or planometric problem using trigonometry;
3) algebraic equations, a system of equations or inequalities;
4) trigonometric equations or inequalities.

A special entrance exam in 1972 is the fact that trigonometric inequalities (according to the program) were not included in both the written work and the oral exam.

In physics there is only an oral exam. To give an idea of ​​the nature of the written work and oral examination, we present the 1972 versions. Analysis of the solution and the nature of the construction error in the section “Answers, instructions, solutions.”

Mathematica

Option 1

1. Find all two-digit integer numbers that satisfy the following conditions: the sum of the digits of the number is at least seven; the sum of the squares of the numbers is not more than thirty; a number written in the same digits, but in reverse order, is no less than half the size of the first alphabetic.

2. In the triangle KLM there are six sectors KN and LP intersecting at point Q. The segment PN has a length of 1 cm, and the vertex M lies on the circle passing through the points N, P, Q. Find the sides and angles of the triangle PNQ.

3. Solve inequality
$$\log_2 \frac{x-1}{x+1} < \log_{\frac{1}{2}} \frac{x+1}{x-1}.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$2 \sin^2 \left( \frac{\pi}{2} \cos^2 x \right) = 1 - \cos (\pi \sin 2x).$$

Option 2

1. Within what limits does the speed of a point moving uniformly in a straight line change, if it is known that with an increase in speed by 3 m/sec, this point, when covering a distance of 630 m, gains time of no less than 1 second and no more than 280 seconds?

2. Find the complete surface of a regular triangular line given its volume V and the angle α between the side edge and the plane of the base.

3. Solve the equation:
$$\sqrt{\log_x \sqrt{3x}} \cdot \log_3 x = -1.$$

4. Solve the equation:
$$\sqrt{\lg x - 2a} - \sqrt{\lg x - 2b} = 2.$$

Option 3.

1. In a vessel with a capacity of 6 liters per litho, 4 liters of 70% sulfuric acid solution; into a second vessel of the same capacity for litho 3 liters of 90% sulfuric acid solution (available as a percentage by volume). How many liters of solution must be poured from the second vessel into the first so that a p-percent solution of sulfuric acid is obtained in the first vessel?

2. At the base of a triangular straight line is a primangular triangle with category a and acute angle α, adjacent to this category. The lateral ribs are inclined to the base plane at the same angle β. Find the radius of a sphere circumscribed about a straight line.

3. Solve the equation
$$|2^x - 1| + |2^x - 2| = 1.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$a \sin 2x + b \cos 2x + \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \cdot \sin 6x = 0.$$
