Correspondence School of Physics and Technology
T. A. Chugunova

at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology
announces enrollment of students
for the 1973-74 academic year

The correspondence school of physics and technology was created in 1966 with the goal of assisting students in grades 9 and 10 of secondary schools in independent studies in physics and mathematics at an advanced level.

The school enrolls students in grades 8, 9 and 10 mainly from rural and remote areas. ZFTSH does not accept students from Moscow.

Admission is limited, but physics and technology clubs can work locally under the ZFTSH program.

According to the “Regulations” on ZFTSH, clubs can be organized on site at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have completed the introductory task of the ZFTS. A circle is considered organizational if the school director informs the ZFTSH of the name of its leader and the rotation list of members of the circle.

Students admitted to the ZFTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the ZFTS program, as well as the solutions recommended by the ZFTS. The work of school students is checked and evaluated by the ZFTSH, and the members of the circle are assessed by its leaders.

The student completes the introductory task independently. Collective decisions are not allowed and will not be considered.

The work must be written in Russian. The order of tasks should be the same as in the assignment. Send the notebook by simple parcel post. Along with your decision, send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the notebook (solutions will not be considered without the certificate), and on the outside, stick a piece of paper on which you write the answers to the following questions.

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 10, 1973 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Solutions posted
