⟦folio 34⟧

In 1972, on the pages «Kvant» we will continue the publication of mathematics under the heading “Practice of imbiruent”. Letters received by the editor show that this section is of keen interest to many readers of the magazine. We are grateful to everyone who gave us their comments and suggestions.

Of course, there is no way to cover all the questions included in the program of entrance exams in mathematics and physics. Yes, there is no need for this - these issues are studied at school and discussed in detail in school textbooks. Therefore, the editors only hired topics that, in one form or another, are often used in solving specific problems and cause difficulties for applicants.

The theoretical issues discussed in the articles are accompanied by an analysis of specific examples, taken, as a rule, from the versions of entrance exams in recent years.

At the end of each stat, we will place tasks for independent solution, helping readers to check how well they have understood what they read. These tasks are also mainly taken from the entrance exams of recent years.

Taking into account the numerous wishes of readers, in order to provide information, we will continue to publish

written tasks offered in written and oral examinations in mathematics and physics in various countries in 1972, as well as brief information about these institutes of interest to our readers. Familiarity with this mathematics allows future applicants to specifically imagine what a written and oral admissions exam is, and to try their hand at solving a set of problems in advance and organizing time (usually 4 hours). For example, you can recommend that future applicants eliminate their own “exam” by solving the entire option in 4 hours and writing it down, and then checking it independently (or using Movaritz). Such a claim, which is as close as possible to the conditions of the exam, turns out to be especially effective. Let us only note that those attending it need a sufficiently good study of the theoretical sections.

As the editorial mail shows, programs on physics and mathematics from the series “For university applicants”, which are conducted in the subject (curriculum) program of the Central Meteorology, are very popular among young people. Taking into account the readers' requests, «Kvant» we will systematically post information and mathematics of these meteorological preparatory courses.
