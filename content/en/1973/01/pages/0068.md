EQUALLY COMPOSITED FIGURES

The lower pictures show images of a duck, a samovar, a chiclops crustacean, a sailing yacht, a dog and a goose. Prove that the areas occupied by these figures are equal.

To make the proof easier, the diagram on the right shows the pieces from which the figures can be assembled. Please note that scales figure and diagrams are different.

To solve the problem, it is enough to show that any figures are equally composed, that is, they are composed of identical pieces of the plane.
