1. Let $x_1, x_2$ and $x_3$ be the number of vouchers purchased at 21, 40 and 60 rubles for each voucher, respectively. First, we must answer the question of how only glory is needed, given in the conditions of the problem: “it is supposed to use 2000 rubles.” Does this mean that exactly 2000 rubles must be spent or the total expenditure should not exceed the specified amount? Since the second option is more general, it should be accepted. So, we need to find the most value of the expression
$$15x_1 + 27x_2 + 45x_3$$
given that
$$21x_1 + 40x_2 + 60x_3 \leq 2000.$$
Let's try to address economic considerations. Since the benefit for us is the most quantitatively per day, the vouchers need to be compared by the average cost of one day. We will receive $\frac{21}{15}$, $\frac{40}{27}$ and $\frac{60}{45}$ rubles per day, or $\frac{189}{135}, \frac{200}{135}$ and $\frac{180}{135}$.
Thus, from our point of view, 45-day vouchers will be the most available, 15-day vouchers will be second, and 27-day vouchers will be in last place. If a voucher could be obtained, it would be in the amount of 2000 rubles. It would be possible to pay for $\frac{2000}{60} \cdot 45 = 1500$ days separately. However, the vouchers are sold only as a whole. Therefore, for the available amount of 2000 rubles. you can buy 33 of the most profitable tours, which will provide $33 \times 45 = 1485$ days separately. At the same time, 20 rubles. will be left unused. Nothing will be achieved with these 20 rubles and they can be considered as a direct loss compared to the conditional case when all available debts are spent on paying for the busiest days separately. If he refuses one of the sixty-ruble vouchers, then another 60 rubles will be added to the 20 rubles and for that day you can purchase two forty-ruble vouchers:
$$2000 = 32 \times 60 + 2 \times 40.$$
The result will be
$$32 \times 45 + 2 \times 27 = 1494$$
days separately. Thus, the found option is more profitable than the previous one.
Let's prove that it is the best of all possible ones. For this purpose, let us estimate the tot conditional “loss” that we incur in comparison with the most suitable distribution of all 2000 rubles. This damage amounts to $\frac{200 - 180}{135} \cdot 54$ rub. per day, and since there will be 54 such days, we will get $\frac{20}{135} \cdot 54$ rubles in total. = 8 rub.
Compared to the found option, the best option can be only the one that uses vouchers priced at 21 rubles, which are more profitable than vouchers for 40 rubles.
Let $2000 - 21x_1 - 40x_2 - 60x_3 = a$. Then
$$21x_1 + a = 2000 - 40x_2 - 60x_3$$
that is, the number $21x_1 + a$ is divisible by 20. If $x_1 = 1$, then $a \geq 19$; if $x_1 = 2$, then $a \geq 18$. Since the remainder $a$ is a “loss”, it is not advisable to consider the case when $a > 8$. Therefore, $x_1$ cannot be less than 12. But if $x_1 \geq 12$, then the loss as a result of replacing more profitable trips with less profitable ones is no less
$$\frac{189 - 180}{135} \cdot 12 \cdot 15 = 12 \cdot руб.$$
Thus, it is most profitable to purchase 32 vouchers for 45 days each and 2 vouchers for 27 days each.
