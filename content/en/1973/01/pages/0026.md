⟦folio 24⟧

Problem «Kvant»

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. In it, there are also relatively easy problems to solve that are not easy for specialists - mathematicians and physicists. The most difficult tasks are marked with a star. They are not marked if they are unable to quickly complete the task; try to return to it again - in a day, in a week, in a month. But a solution has been found. If a task occupies you, then do not put it off right away - think about how you can write down the solution in the most rational and short way, generalize the problem, simplify or simplify its result, what similar task it allows you to solve.

In the second part of the sections, we place the solution to the given questions (approximately half a month after the publication of their conditions) taking into account the readers sent in therefore. We advise those who did not insert «Kvant» last year or did not solve the problem to get acquainted with the solutions.

In «Kvant» No. 2,4,6,8,10,12 we will give the names of the reader who sent us the correct solutions to this section. Schoolchildren who during the year decide to submit interesting and complete solutions will receive the right to participate in the regional rounds of the All-Olympiad along with the winner of the regional Olympiad. In addition, for the winners of our ongoing competition, the editorial service has introduced several special awards.

After creating tasks, we usually indicate who suggested this task to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time.

Coming up with a new original problem is usually more difficult than solving it. More difficult, but please, even more interesting. If you succeed, assign us the task along with the solution. We will publish the most beautiful and interesting assignments in the “Kvant Problem Book” or in other sections of the magazine.

Our address: 117071, Moscow B-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, editorial office of the magazine «Kvant». On the envelope, after the address of the journal, write the solutions to each task you are sending (for example: “Problem book «Kvant», M181, M184”; or “F201” or “new problem”). Solutions are given for each of the subjects (if you are sending both mathematics and physics), and also send new tasks in separate envelopes. At the beginning of the letter, be sure to write your last name, first name, patronymic, home address (as well as the class and school in which you study). We will be able to consider and take into account milk from the reader if they are written in Russian. The required solutions should be sent no later than a month and a half after the corresponding issue is published. In particular, the deadline for sending answers to assignments from this issue is no later than March 1, 1973.
