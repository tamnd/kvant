⟦folio 48⟧

VZMSH accepts only seventh grade students. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not admitted to VZMSH. Classes at the school begin on September 1.

There are three courses at VZMSH. Students who successfully complete school will receive a certificate of completion. School education is free.

This issue «Kvant» offers problems that serve as an introductory test for correspondence mathematics schools at Moscow State University and Leningrad State University. Those who want to enter the VZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 10, 1973. After the performance review (around July 1973), you will be notified whether you have been accepted into the VZMSH.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from regular school ones, no additional knowledge of mathematics is required to solve them. In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to solve every problem without exception. When evaluating work, not only the number of problems solved will be taken into account, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without any explanation may not be read. If the task is possible, it will not be possible to say!:

Work must be completed in Russian in a student’s notepad in Klatek. Entry papers are not processed. Please do not fold the terradi when sending. In the envelope along with the terradi you need to fill out a piece of paper measuring 14x6 cm with your postal address written in it (we will stick it on the envelope when we send the answer). Glue a sheet of checkered paper onto the cover of the terradi, draw it and fill it in according to the following sample (the initial work will not be checked!):

Vologda Last name, first name, year of birth: Petr Ivanov, 1959
School (full name): School No. 2, Totmyts
Class: 7th grade "B"
Last name, first name, absent mathematics teacher: Nikanorov Nikolay Alekseevich
Place of work and position of the parent: father is a driver at motor depot No. 3, mother is a housewife.
Full postal address: Totmaa, st. Lenina, 3, apt. 8

Inspection results (to be completed by the inspector).

Schoolchildren living in the Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod, Pskov regions, Komi and Karelian ASSR, Belarus, Latvian, Lithuanian and Estonian SSR must send work to the address: Leningrad, P-228, st. Savushkina, 61, Special Board at Leningrad State University, correspondence school, for a competition.

Schoolchildren living in the Kursk, Belgorod, Voronezh and Tambov regions should send their work to the address: Voronezh, University, FZMSH, for the competition.

Schoolchildren living in other regions and resource SSRs must send their work to the address: Moscow, V-234, Moscow State University, Mechanics and Mathematics School, ZMSH, for the competition.
