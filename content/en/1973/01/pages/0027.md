M181–M185; F193–F197.

M181. What is the shortest length a piece of wire must have so that it can be used to bend the frame of a cube with an edge of 10 cm? (The wire can pass along one edge twice, bend 90° and 180°, but it cannot be broken.)

M182. Prove that if a) $a > 0$, $b > 0$ and $c > 0$, then
$$\frac{a}{b+c} + \frac{b}{c+a} + \frac{c}{a+b} \geq \frac{3}{2}.$$
6) $a > 0$, $b > 0$, $c > 0$ and $d > 0$, then
$$\frac{a}{b+c+d} + \frac{b}{a+c+d} + \frac{c}{a+b+d} + \frac{d}{a+b+c} \geq \frac{4}{3}.$$
c) $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$ are positive numbers ($n \geq 2$), then
$$\frac{a_1}{a_2+a_3+\dots+a_n} + \frac{a_2}{a_1+a_3+\dots+a_n} + \dots + \frac{a_n}{a_1+a_2+\dots+a_{n-1}} \geq \frac{n}{n-1}.$$

M183. Find the height of a trapezoid whose bases are equal to a and b ($a < b$), the angle between the diagonals is 90°, and the angle between the extensions of the sides is 45°.

M184 *. Prove the exact
$$\frac{C_n^0}{x} - \frac{C_n^1}{x+1} + \dots + (-1)^n \frac{C_n^n}{x+n} = \frac{n!}{x(x+1)(x+2)\dots(x+n)}.$$
G. E. Esinenko

M185 *. On a caften with area 1, 5 patches can be placed, the area of ​​each of which is not less than $1/2$. Prove that you find two patches, area

the general part of which is not less than $1/5$.
E. B. Dykin

F193. There are two lens systems with the same focal lengths. The optical axes of the lenses coincide (Fig. 1). The first lens system consists of converging lenses; in the second, converging lenses are interconnected with diverging ones. Find the trajectories of the rays in each of the systems if the distance between the lenses is much less than the focal one.

F194. A charge is placed between the plates of a flat capacitor. How will it move if a sinusoidal voltage with an initial phase $\varphi_0 = 0$ is applied to the capacitor?

F195. Between two cubes of mass $m_1$ and $m_2$ there is a rolled spring of stiffness k. The cubes are tied with a thread, the distance between them is l (Fig. 2). To what height does the center of mass of the system rise if the thread is burned? The spring is considered incredible. Eelina in the normal state is $l_0$.

F196. A very thin beam of protons moves in a circle of radius R in the accelerator chamber. The size of the point at the initial moment is I, the total number of the part in the chamber is n. The magnetic flux through the beam, which does not change the orbit, changes with speed f. What will be the size of the point after the particles make one revolution? The speed of the part remains much less than the speed of light.

F197. On a body of mass m lying on a horizontal rough surface with a friction coefficient k, at the moment of time t=0 a force proportional to time began to act at an angle α to the horizon. Determine the speed v of the body's movement after t seconds.

A. V. Ustinova
