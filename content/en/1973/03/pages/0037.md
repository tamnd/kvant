⟦folio 35⟧

Problem «Kvant»
Tasks
M191—M195, F203—F207

Solutions to the problem from the embodied number can be sent no later than April 30 to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, Nauka Publishing House, magazine «Kvant». After the address on the envelope is the Nazi “Science”, magazine «Kvant». After the address on the envelope is the Nazi solution to which problem you are sending, for example: “Problem «Kvant» M191, M192” or “... F203”.
Solutions to problems in each of the subjects (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems sent in separate envelopes. Organizational problems proposed for publication, sent along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope there is a room “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).
Problems of increased difficulty are marked with a star.

M191. On the plane, given are two points A and B and a line l passing through point A but not through point B. An arbitrary circle is drawn through points A and B. Let O be its center, C its point of intersection with line l, different from A. Find the locus of the midpoint of the segments OC.
P. Paramonov, 9th grade student

M192. Given numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 1000. Find the most m that have the following property: no matter which m numbers are crossed out, among the remaining 1000 - m numbers there will be two, of which one is divisible by the other.

M193*. Prove that the sum of the areas of the heel of the triangles formed by the parameter and the diagonals of a convex pentagon (see Fig. 1) is greater than the area of ​​the entire pentagon.
N. B. Vasiliev

M194. Given two relatively prime natural numbers a and b. It is known that any integer can be represented as ax + by, where x and y are integers. Consider a set M of integers that can be represented in the form ax + by, where x and y are non-negative integers.
a) What is the largest number c that does not belong to the set M?
b) Prove that of two numbers n and c - n (where n is any integer), one belongs to M and the other does not.
