Competition winners «Kvant»

We have already reported (see «Kvant» No. 1 for 1972) that schoolchildren who regularly sent particularly oriental and complete solutions to the problem “Problem Book «Kvant»” will receive the right to participate in the regional rounds of the All-World Olympiad along with the winner of regional and city Olympiads. Over the past year, 1972, the editors received more than six thousand letters with solutions to the problem. We have selected the authors of the correct and most interesting solutions.

Below is a list of schoolchildren, the winners of the competition «Kvant», who received the right to participate in regional, regional and republican (in the Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic and constituency republics without regional division) Olympiads.
