Moscow State University

(Faculty of Economics)

The task of economic science is to generalize new phenomena in the economic life of society, to develop national economic problems, the solution of which contributes to the successful construction of communications. The attention of economists should be directed to identifying ways for the most efficient use of material and labor resources in the national economy, existing methods of planning and organizing industrial and agricultural production, developing the fundamental placement of productive forces and technical economic problems of building communications. These tasks set for economic science at the XXIV Congress of the CPSU are determined by the importance and profile of the training of economists at Moscow University.

The Faculty of Economics trains personnel in the specialty: “political economy” and “national economic planning” - in full-time and evening departments; "Economics" - full-time.

Graduates of the “Political Economy” department receive the qualification “economist, teacher of political economy” and are sent to work as teachers in higher and secondary specialized educational institutions, employees in economic research institutes, economists in enterprises, state farms, and planning organizations.

As a rule, persons with at least two years of practical work experience are accepted for full-time study in the Department of Political Economy. Some teacher appointments are for high school graduates who do not have work experience.

The department of “Planning and Economics” provides training in two specialties: “national economic planning” and “economic economics”. Students, along with thorough and comprehensive economic training, receive in-depth knowledge of mathematics and mathematical methods of economic analysis. Here, mathematical analysis, linear algebra, probability theory, mathematical programming, economic information system, mass theory are studied on a large scale.

After graduating with a degree in “national economic planning”, a graduate is awarded the qualification “economist, synthetic planner”, and a graduate with a degree in “economic economics” is awarded the qualification “economist-mathematician”, and they are sent to work in planning and statistical bodies, computer centers, research organizations, and large enterprises.

Below are the options for the 1972 mathematics entrance exams for these departments.

Department of Economic Cybernetics

1. A room in the shape of a rectangular parallelepid is being built for the design bureau, one of the walls of which should be made of glass, and the rest of the general material. The height of the room should be 4 meters and the area should be 80 square meters. A square meter of glass wall costs 75 rubles, and a total meter costs 50 rubles. What should be the length and width of the room so that the total cost of all walls is minimal? What is this lowest cost?

2. Find tg x if
$$2y \sin x + 5 \cos x = 2y, \quad -5 \sin x + y \cos x = 1.$$

3. Find and depict points on the coordinate plane whose coordinates satisfy the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} (3x + 1)(x - 3y - 1) = 0, \\ (3x + 1)(6y - 2x + 1)(x + y) = 0. \end{cases}$$

4. In the final quadrilateral ABCD, angles A and C are opposite. The perpendiculars BE and DF are dropped onto the diagonal AC. Prove that CE = FA.
