⟦folio 76⟧

«Kvant» for younger students

Quests

1. In the addition example, the numbers were replaced by letters, and this is what happened:
KICK + KICK = FIGHT.
Try to decipher this example.

2. Semiklassik Petya moved to a new five-story building, whose 1st and 2nd floors in the 2nd and 3rd entrances are occupied by a store. All staircases of the house are arranged in the same way, each of them contains no more than four apartments. Petya's apartment number is 31. What floor does Petya live on?

3. On a rope loop in a horizontal position there is a stick, one end of which is much thicker than the other. The stick was cut in the place where the loop was. Are the resulting parts the same weight?

4. Why does burning wood crackle?

5. If the engineer cannot immediately move a heavy train, he reverses from the agal and then slowly moves the train from a stop. What's the matter?

Artist E. V. Nazarov
