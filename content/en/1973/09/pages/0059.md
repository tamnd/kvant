On the morning of April 16, the participants of the Olympiad gathered in Peterhof at the Faculty of Physics. Here they had an interesting meeting with scientists - physicists from Leningrad University. At the end of this meeting, the children were offered a physical quiz. The experimenter performed the experiment in front of the audience, and the Quiz participants were asked to give an explanation of the demonstrated phenomenon. The answers were very interesting and sometimes funny. The winners of the Quiz received prizes, including numbers «Kvant» for 1973 with the author of Academician I.K. Kikon.

On the evening of April 16 in Leningrad, at the Young Spectator Theater, the closing ceremony of the VII All-Union Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics took place, at which the jury’s decision on awarding the Olympiad participants was announced. Here are the names of the winner.

First degree diplomas
The following Olympiad participants received:

in 8 grades - Vadim Kaplinovsky (Physical Mechanics School at Leningrad State University), Yuri Plievovko (Bryansk, rural school No. 51);

in 9 grades - Vladimir Brown (Physical School at Leningrad State University), Anamoliy Kureanov (Zhitomir, school No. 21), Dmitry Toptytsein (Moscow, school No. 2);

in 10 grades - Karol Andrei (Leningrad, school No. 30), Kushin Vladimir (Astrakhan, school No. 10), Milyasevich Igor (Rovno, school No. 12), Victor Soloduv (Physical School at Moscow State University), Uraltsev Nikolay (Physical School at Leningrad State University).

II degree diplomas
The following Olympiad participants received:

“Veterans” - winners of three All-Union Physics Olympiads I. Korepanov, T. Teokharov, A. Ushakov.
