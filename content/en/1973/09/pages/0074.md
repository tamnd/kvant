⟦folio 72⟧

VMS is an evening mathematics school. All interested students in grades 6–8 can attend this school. There are no entrance exams to the Higher Medical School.

The High School Council assigns the value “high school student” to a student who performs well, issues a certificate, and at the end of the year is recommended for admission to a mathematics school.

During the ten years of operation of the Higher School of Music, its former students competed many times in national and international mathematical olympiads and published ten scientific papers. But the VMS does not set as its sole goal the preparation and selection of future professional mathematicians.

The ability to “think mathematically” is useful (and partly necessary) for any person. After all, mathematics can be used in everything! For example, from February to May 1973, at a seminar in which I participate, mathematical models used in economics, sociology, medicine, psychology, geology, genetics, demography, and so on were considered. But, unfortunately, when discussing these issues, scientists and engineers sometimes use theorems and formulas of mathematics without clearly understanding their meaning.

This naturally leads to errors. To avoid such mistakes, you need to start training as early as possible in understanding the meaning of mathematical theorems, for example, in a mathematical circle or in evening school.

The main focus of VMS classes is problem solving; The main responsibility of VMS students is not to be shy and not afraid to ask a question when something is not entirely clear. The work program of each group (club) of the VMS is determined by the leaders of this group; At the same time, the interests of schoolchildren participating in the group are studied.

The VMS operates from October to April. The academic year begins with a meeting in the Assembly Hall of Moscow University. Classes are held once a week at school or at Moscow State University. In addition, a common problem-solving competition for all groups is regularly held.

Those interested in studying at the Higher School of Music can write to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 14, bldg. 7, CEMI, Laboratory of Theory of Vermatical Statistics, Higher Medical School, or call in the second half of September by phone: 291-85-72, 139-35-29.

Rozentl, A. K. Tolpygo “Mathematical problems” (M., “Nauka”, 1971); E. B. Dynkin, S. A. Molchanov, A. L. Rosent’l “Mathematical modernities. Arithmetic and algebra" (Moscow, "Nauka", 1970). “Meetings with the Unknown” in the magazine “Pioneer” are based on materials from the Higher School of Music.
