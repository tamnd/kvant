The following are excerpts from two original works by Thomas Young:
I - from a lecture he gave on November 24, 1803 at the Royal Society; II - from the 39th lecture of the Course of Lectures on Natural Philosophy, published in 1807.
In these works, based on an analysis of the phenomena of interference and diffraction, the validity of the wave theory of light is rigorously and surprisingly logically proven.
Translation and preparation of the publication were carried out by S. R. Filonovich.

I. “... I discovered such a simple and easily demonstrable proof of the general law of interference of two streams of light, which I had already tried to establish earlier, that I decided to submit to the Royal Society a brief report on the fact, which seems to me to be conclusive. The statement that I am now going to make is simple: color stripes appear as a result of the interference of two streams of light. I think that the fact that it is possible to test this assertion by the simple experiments which I am about to relate, and which can easily be repeated on any sunny day with the help of objects at hand, will not be answered even by prejudiced people.

I continued the small answer in the window shutter and covered it with a piece of thick paper, which I pierced with a thin needle. For greater ease of observation, I placed a small glass at the window so that it reflected sunlight horizontally onto the opposite wall. At the same time, the diverging light flux passed over the table, on which there were several small screens made of paper cards. I introduced a strip of card 1/3 inch wide* into the light stream and observed its shadow both on the opposite wall and on other cards placed at different distances from the first. At the same time, colored stripes formed along the edges of the shadow, and the shadow itself was divided into a number of parallel stripes, while its middle remained light. These stripes in the shadow itself are a consequence of the combination of two portions of light coming from different sides of the card strip and diffusing in the shadow area. If a small screen is placed a few inches from the strip so that the edges of its shadow and the strip coincide, all the streaks previously seen in the shadow on the wall will immediately disappear, although the light passing on the other side of the strip will travel the same way.
