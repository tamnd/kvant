Mathematical Olympiad
L. M. Pashkova

From April 11 to April 17, 1973, the final stage of the VII All-round Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Chisinau. The seventh all-Union competitions in “mind and knowledge” started in December 1972 at the school Olympiads. In March, Olympiads in the region and resources were held. In Chisinau, the winners of regional and resource Olympiads and 26 prize-winners of the VI All-Russian Olympiad (awarded with diplomas of I and II degrees) came closer.

In total, 227 tenth graders, 202 ninth graders, and 159 eighth graders took part in the final type Olympiad.

On April 11, the Olympiad opened at the Professional Palace. Leading scientists Moldavin, representatives of party and Soviet organizations of the republic, winner of the XIV International Mathematical Olympiad, student of school No. 19 in Saratov Sergei Konyagin addressed the guests with welcoming words.
