⟦folio 62⟧

The new school year has begun at school. It also begins in the “Enrollee's Workshop”. As evidenced by the editorial mail, many readers of the magazine have become very interested in the materials of the entrance exams - primarily, of course, the tenth and ninth grades.
This section of our magazine is primarily intended for them.
Here, as in previous years, statistics will be regulated on individual important and complex practical issues of the program in mathematics and physics, which, as practice shows, in admission exams make it most difficult for applicants.
The consideration of these issues is accompanied by an analysis of specific examples and tasks, taken, as a rule, from the entrance exam options, and an analysis of frequently encountered mistakes of applicants.
The key to success in the entrance exam is systematic work throughout the academic year. Here it is not enough to read only those articles that will be published this year in the “Applicant's Workshop”.
These articles cannot (and do not aim to) cover all the issues that applicants should understand well. It is necessary to regulate independent studies, repeat and actively reinforce textbook material, and use manuals for applicants. We recommend that our readers (primarily tenth-graders) turn to the set «Kvant» for past years, where articles for applicants have already been placed.
Let us draw the attention of nine-class students to the fact that not all articles published in the “Enrollee's Workshop” will be accessible or completely understandable to them - after all, some sections of the program are studied only in the 10th grade. However, it is very useful to analyze those parts of articles that use material that has already been consumed.
We advise students in grades 7-9 to assemble a set «Kvant» at home (including those from previous years); It will be useful in the future when preparing for exams.
Taking into account the numerous wishes of readers, the “Applicant's Workshop” will regulate samples of options proposed in 1973 at the entrance exams in physics and mathematics at universities, pedagogical and technical universities in the country. The tasks of these options, together with exercises for theoretical articles (and these exercises are also selected mainly from the entrance exam options), provide a wealth of specific material for independent work. We remember that versions of the entrance exams of past years were also systematically published in «Kvant», and the reader of interest can easily find them by looking through the complete set of the magazine.
In the current academic year, Central Television is conducting a traditional series of programs on mathematics and physics for applicants to universities. «Kvant» recommends that its readers, tenth-class students, listen to this cycle and proposes to publish materials from television preparatory courses.
