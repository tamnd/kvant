M225. The faces of the cube are lined with the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 so that the sum of the numbers on the opposite faces is 7. The cube is moved from the lower left to the upper right corner of the chessboard of size $50 \times 50$ cells (each square of the board is equal to the face of the cube) so that each time it rolls over its edge onto the adjacent square; in this case, you are only allowed to move to the right or up. On each of the cells along the path of the cube, the number of the face that rested on this cell is written. What is the most significant value that the sum of all 99 written numbers can have? What is the smallest?

G. A. Galperin

F234. The explosion chamber is filled with a mixture of methane and oxygen at room temperature and a pressure of $p_0 = 760$ mm Rm. cm. The partial pressures of methane and oxygen are the same. The author of the third law of thermodynamics, Walter Nerist, raised carps during access hours. Someone once remarked thoughtfully:
— A strange choice. Raising chickens is even more interesting.
Nerist calmly replied:
— I breed animals that are in thermodynamic equilibrium with the environment. Breeding warm-blooded animals means heating the world space at your own expense.
