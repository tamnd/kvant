Mathematical Olympiad
L. M. Pashkova

From April 11 to April 17, 1973, the final stage of the VII All-round Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Chisinau. The seventh all-Union competitions in “mind and knowledge” started in December 1972 at the school Olympiads. In March, Olympiads in the region and resources were held. In Chisinau, the winners of regional and resource Olympiads and 26 prize-winners of the VI All-Russian Olympiad (awarded with diplomas of I and II degrees) came closer.

In total, 227 tenth graders, 202 ninth graders, and 159 eighth graders took part in the final type Olympiad.

On April 11, the Olympiad opened at the Professional Palace. Leading scientists Moldavin, representatives of party and Soviet organizations of the republic, winner of the XIV International Mathematical Olympiad, student of school No. 19 in Saratov Sergei Konyagin addressed the guests with welcoming words. At the opening of the Olympiad, a greeting was read out to the young mathematicians by Academician Andrei Nikolaevich Kolmogorov:

“I wish the participants of the Olympiad success both at the Olympiad and in their future lives. The same ones who are at the Olympics. If you don't succeed, don't give up on mathematics. If, after testing yourself, you find that it is exciting for you, continue to engage it -

Hang on and try to achieve the opportunity to always choose it as your specialty.”

On April 12, schoolchildren sat down at their desks to determine the strongest in the two-day modern final stage. On the first day, the eighth-classics and tenth-classics students were each given three problems, and the ninth-classics students were given four, and on April 14, everyone was offered three more problems. Each day was allocated 4 hours to solve the problem.

The jury, chaired by the Vice-President of the Academy of Sciences of the Moldavian USSR, Academician V. A. Andrunakevich, not only carefully checked the work, but also checked with the student the analysis of solutions to the problem and typical errors.

During the Olympics, participants not only practiced “intelligence and knowledge”, but also listened to lectures by the country's leading scientists. The meetings of young mathematicians with scientists from the Academy of Sciences of the Moldavian USSR and university teachers were exciting. The participants of the Olympade greeted the representatives of the editorial staff of the magazine «Kvant» with great interest: most of the “Olympians” are active readers of this magazine.

In their free time, the Olympada participants met with schoolchildren from Chisinau. Schoolchildren from Tiraspol, Bender, villages of the Dubossary and Kotovsky districts warmly greeted the envoys of the region and resource officers and accompanied them on excursions to places of military glory, to the homeland of the legendary G.I. Kotovsky, to the Pushkin places.

The Olympada went very smoothly and brought a great role to its participants thanks to the good work of the organizing committee and jury of the Olympada, the help of scientists from Moldova and party and Soviet organizations of Moldova and the editors of the magazine «Kvant». Young mathematicians and members of the jury sent greetings and congratulations to A. N. Kolmogorov on his glorious anniversary.

Olympada results:

348 participants successfully completed the Olympics.
