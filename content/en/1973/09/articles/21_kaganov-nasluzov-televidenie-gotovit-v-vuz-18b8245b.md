⟦folio 74⟧

TV
PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

On October 1, 1973, the Main Editorial Board of popular science and educational programs of Central Television began broadcasting programs for applicants to universities according to the third program.

Televised classes will continue throughout the school year. Every week the viewer is offered the following programs: on physics - on Monday evening and Friday morning; in mathematics - on Tuesday and Wednesday morning. In the same week, these programs are repeated: in physics - on Wednesday and Sunday mornings; in mathematics - on Thursday and Saturday evenings.

The program in physics and mathematics is based on the “Program of entrance examinations for applicants to higher educational institutions of the USSR.” Telesantry helps to consolidate, deepen, expand and systematize students’ school knowledge; The lesson is designed taking into account those program issues that most often cause difficulties for applicants.

Telecourse listeners are regularly offered homework (they will be published in the Moscow Speaks and Shows diary), consisting of a task and examples of various difficulties. Periodic checks are carried out on the sections eaten. In addition, control polls will be conducted during the telecast.

Complete homework, course participants must present a postcard to the TV show - a report on the completion of work. Those who wrote the test will send a report and the work itself. These reports are checked by telecourse staff and scored according to a specially developed scale.

In the 1973/74 academic year, as in previous years, telecourses will conduct tests on the topics covered (twice in mathematics and twice in physics). As the experience of the courses shows, the teachers meet with a significant increase in learning: future applicants, being in conditions similar to those that occur during the entrance exams, undergo a kind of psychological training. On the other hand, teachers, as a result of direct contact with television viewers, can determine how listeners perceived the material presented, and, if necessary, quickly introduce appropriate adjustments to their work. Along with taking tests, periodic face-to-face consultations are also expected.

These tests and consultations are requested by students who are actively and systematically engaged in telecourses. They accept tests and provide consultations to teachers from capital.byzov. Students who successfully pass the tests receive certificates of completion of prepared television physics and mathematics courses.

Telecourses can be attended by both graduating students of secondary general education schools and technical schools, as well as persons who already have secondary education. Admission to telecourses is carried out without competition.

Those wishing to take part in preparatory television courses must send an “Application Form” and a “Registration Card” to the Central Television to the Main Editorial Office of Popular Science and Educational Programs.

They are formatted as follows.

Take two general postcards and on the front side of each write your home address, last name, first name, and patronymic in full and legible. One of these cards will be the “Application Form”, the other will be the “Registration Card”.

Fill out the reverse (blank) sides of the cards as indicated on the images, placing the cards horizontally with the short side.

Here is an example of filling out the “Application Questionnaire” and “Registration Card” when enrolling in the mathematics department of telecourses.

Don’t forget to stick your photo to the “Registration card” (in the upper left corner), and draw the bottom of the card into 50 squares (5 horizontal rows of 10 squares each; square size 10x10 mm).

Upon admission to the physical department of telecourses, the “Application Questionnaire” and “Registration Card” are also completed; Naturally, on the back of the cards you need to put the letters “M” and the letter “F” together in the upper right corner, in the “Registration card” in the third paragraph you need to put the last annual grade in physics, in the “Application Questionnaire” indicate that you want to enroll in the physics department of telecourses, and on the envelope that you send to the telecourse, make a note “F”.

If you wish to work in the department, you must fill out two “Application Questionnaires” and two “Registration Cards” (separately for the physical and mathematical departments); however, send both applications and both registration cards to the editorial office in one envelope (with two marks “F” and “M” on it).

Reports on the completion of homework are prepared on regular postcards (on each card there is a report on the completion of only one homework) as follows.

On the front side of the card, in the “where” and “to” columns, write your home address, last name, first name, patronymic. In the column “sender's address” - the editorial office address: 113162, Moscow, Shabolovka, 53, Moscow preparatory telecourses, Mathematics (or “Physics”).

On the back (blank) side of the card, write down the answers to the given questions and an example of homework.

Physics homework consists of five problems. Answers to them must be given in analytical form (formulas) and in numerical form (in the SI system). Each correctly solved problem is worth two points (1 point for the correct analytical formula and 1 point for the correct numerical answer).

Mathematics homework also consists of five problems (or examples) of varying difficulty.

Under the answers to homework assignments in both physics and mathematics, the teacher must write a fraction, the numerator of which means the sum of points received by the student for all previous homework (indicated by the student himself), and the denominator is the sum of points for this task (this figure is indicated by the course teacher).

On the homework report card, each time you must write down the homework number and your code (your code will be indicated in the “Extract on enrollment in telecourses” sent to you).

At the bottom of the card (after the answers) write questions, comments, suggestions.

Place the completed card in an envelope and send it to the editor. The envelope must include the homework number, your code and the letter “M” (or “F”).

After verification, the card is sent back to the dryer. Grade for completed homework in the “Extract on enrollment in telecourses” (in the box corresponding to this task).

The deadline for completing homework is two weeks from the date of their publication in the weekly magazine “Moscow Speaks and Shows.”
