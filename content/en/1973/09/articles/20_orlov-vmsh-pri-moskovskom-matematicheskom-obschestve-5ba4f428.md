⟦folio 72⟧

VMS is an evening mathematics school. All interested students in grades 6–8 can attend this school. There are no entrance exams to the Higher Medical School.

The High School Council assigns the value “high school student” to a student who performs well, issues a certificate, and at the end of the year is recommended for admission to a mathematics school.

During the ten years of operation of the Higher School of Music, its former students competed many times in national and international mathematical olympiads and published ten scientific papers. But the VMS does not set as its sole goal the preparation and selection of future professional mathematicians.

The ability to “think mathematically” is useful (and partly necessary) for any person. After all, mathematics can be used in everything! For example, from February to May 1973, at a seminar in which I participate, mathematical models used in economics, sociology, medicine, psychology, geology, genetics, demography, and so on were considered. But, unfortunately, when discussing these issues, scientists and engineers sometimes use theorems and formulas of mathematics without clearly understanding their meaning.

This naturally leads to errors. To avoid such mistakes, you need to start training as early as possible in understanding the meaning of mathematical theorems, for example, in a mathematical circle or in evening school.

The main focus of VMS classes is problem solving; The main responsibility of VMS students is not to be shy and not afraid to ask a question when something is not entirely clear. The work program of each group (club) of the VMS is determined by the leaders of this group; At the same time, the interests of schoolchildren participating in the group are studied.

The VMS operates from October to April. The academic year begins with a meeting in the Assembly Hall of Moscow University. Classes are held once a week at school or at Moscow State University. In addition, a common problem-solving competition for all groups is regularly held.

Those interested in studying at the Higher School of Music can write to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 14, bldg. 7, CEMI, Laboratory of Theory of Vermatical Statistics, Higher Medical School, or call in the second half of September by phone: 291-85-72, 139-35-29.

In conclusion, we will present several problems from those discussed during the VMS classes. Note that about 400 VMS problems are collected in two books: E. B. Dynkin, S. A. Molchanov, A. L. Rozentl, A. K. Tolpygo “Mathematical problems” (M., “Nauka”, 1971); E. B. Dynkin, S. A. Molchanov, A. L. Rosent’l “Mathematical modernities. Arithmetic and algebra" (Moscow, "Nauka", 1970). “Meetings with the Unknown” in the magazine “Pioneer” are based on materials from the Higher School of Music.

Exercise
1. There are 6 houses along the straight highway. Where to dig a well so that the sum of the distances from it to the houses is the smallest? The same question if there are 7 houses.
2. The gold medalist of the championship scored 7 points, the middle one - 5, the bronze - 3. How many points did the team that took last place score? (For a win in a match, 2 points are given, a draw - 1, a loss - 0.)
3. Solve the following equations in natural numbers:
   a) $xy^2 + 3y^2 - x = 108$;
   b) $x! + 12 = y^2$
   (x! is the product of all natural numbers from 1 to x, for example, $1! = 1$, $2! = 1 \cdot 2$, $3! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 = 6$, $4! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 = 24$).
4. The numbers p and p^4 - 6 are prime. Find p.
5. Given triangle ABC. Through point A a straight line is drawn parallel to BC, at which point D is taken. The perpendicular BE is dropped onto CD. Prove that the area of ​​triangle ABC is $\frac{1}{2} CD \cdot BE$.
6. Identical numbers are protected by the same letters, and different numbers are protected by different ones. Decipher the equality:
   $MЯУ \times MЯУ = MЯУЯK + УЯЯ$.
7. The projector illuminates the inside of the corner at $\alpha^0$. The fortress wall is located along the perimeter of a convex polygon, which is not a parallelogram. Prove that tray projectors can be illuminated from the outside. (Point A is considered illuminated by projector B if its beam reaches all points of some walls around A.)
8. The first one thinks of n digits $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$. The second one names n natural numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$. The first one responds with the amount $a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \dots + a_nx_n$. How many questions must be asked to the second person to guess the numbers $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$? How to act when $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ are not necessarily numbers, but natural numbers less than 1000? What if all we know about $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ is that they are natural numbers?
9. There are exactly 57 ways to give x rubles in tickets of 3 and 5 rubles. What does x equal, indicating all possibilities?
10. Find all functions f and for any numbers x, y c the following equality is true:
    $f(x)f(y) - xy = f(x) + f(y) - 1$.
11. It is known about points A, B, C that for any fourth point M the segment AM is less than at least one of the segments BM and CM. Prove that point A lies on segment BC.
12. Choose the numbers a, b, c, d, e so that the equation
    $\sqrt{ax + b} + \sqrt{cx + d} - e$
I have roots x = 3 and x = 4.
13. The natural number n is divisible by 12 and has 1 different natural divisors. Find n.
