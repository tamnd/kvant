⟦folio 24⟧

COMPETITION WINNERS
"QUANTA"

The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» established special prizes for the most interesting solutions to problems included in the “Kvant Problem Book”.
The editors of the magazine received for 1972-1973. about 4000 letters with solutions to problems placed in the “Quantum Problem Book” section. Among the authors of these letters, the editorial collection selected schoolchildren who regularly sent especially original and successful solutions. They are loaded with an annual subscription to «Kvant» magazine for 1974.
In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the All-Union Olympiad, these schoolchildren were admitted to the region round of the 1974 All-Union Olympiad along with the winner of the regional mathematical and physical Olympiad.

1. VALKOV ALEXEY (Tashkent, village no. 110, 9th class)
2. VOYNOVA OLGA (Kyrgyz SSR, village Kosh-Tegirmen, village highway No. 1, 9th class)
3. ALEXANDER GRIGORYAN (Baku, village no. 211, 9th class)
4. ZASLAVSKY ALEXANDER (Kalinin, village no. 20, 9th class)
5. KOGAN YAN (Glazov, village no. 3, 8th class)
6. LUTINGER HERMAN (Chernivtsi, rural highway No. 14, 9th class)
7. MAKARICHEV ALEXANDER (Lvov, village no. 14, 9th class)
