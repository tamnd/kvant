⟦folio 77⟧

Housewarming party at the Kvaenta Zoo

In Kvanten No. 1 for 1972, an article by A. Yu. Soifer “Our Zoo” was published, in which the youngest reader of our magazine proposed a new idea: from 12 triangular sex figures, put together the first inhabitants of the Kvaenta zoos - two cheerful geese and a camel. I also propose to come up with new inhabitants of the zoo. Today we will introduce you to them.

Our zoo has been replenished with new residents. They are close to us from Moscow and Kazan, the city of Elektrougli in the Moscow region and Norilsk, the city of Navon in the Bukhara region and Tobolsk in the Tomen region. There were a lot of new settlers, we will introduce you to the most interesting ones.

Olya Sh. (unfortunately, Olya did not give her last name) from the town of Elektrougli gave us a charming kitten (Fig. 1) and a turtle that watches to see if she is walking too fast (Fig. 2). Nine-year-old Vova and twelve-year-old Tolya Shustovskikh from Tobolsk added chicken (Fig. 3) ​​and many dogs, one of which we offer to your attention (Fig. 4).
