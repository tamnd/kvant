To our readers

From September 1, a subscription is made to the popular scientific journal «Kvant» for 1974.

The main content of the magazine is materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to explain various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were offered in entrance exams for various types, olympic problems, and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical instruments will help the reader set up and test a physical experiment.

The journal publishes review articles telling about the achievements of science, about problems that are still waiting to be solved; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are born.

The magazine constantly contains receptions for books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

The magazine is designed primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is useful for teachers, especially those who run classes or teach elective classes in physics and mathematics, as well as everyone who loves mathematics and physics.

The magazine is distributed only by subscription, which is made without restrictions.
