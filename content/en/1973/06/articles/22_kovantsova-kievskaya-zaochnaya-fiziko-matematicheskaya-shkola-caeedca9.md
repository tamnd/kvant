Kyiv correspondence school of physics and mathematics

Everyone knows how great the importance of mathematics is for the sciences that study the world around us. Her development of the theorem with theoretical polychloride mathematicians; this constantly developing science is attracting more and more interest, which does not wane over the years, but becomes stronger. It is especially joyful to see with what importance and love schoolchildren treat mathematics, how subtly they feel and understand it. This is a great merit of teachers, who reveal to students the mysterious world of mathematics, the world of formulas and theorems.

Recently, a lot of attention has been paid to the issue of improving the forms of school education - maintaining appropriate methods, using aids in the learning process. Studying at a correspondence school of physics and mathematics is one of the new forms of studying mathematics.

The first correspondence mathematics school was opened in 1963 at Moscow University. Then the same school organizations were at Leningrad and Novosibirsk universities, and in 1968 - at Kiev University. Currently, the Kyiv school has more than 2.5 thousand students.

Specialized correspondence schools work independently of the plans according to which trained secondary schools operate.

The tasks given in specialized correspondence schools of physics and mathematics must also contain elements that create an active stock of mathematical knowledge. It is necessary that the student, when solving a problem, can think for himself so that the number of data entered into it is neither excessive nor insufficient.

What topics are being developed by students of the Kyiv Correspondence School of Physics and Mathematics?

This is, first of all, the method of coordinates, functions and their graphs, elements of combinatorics, previous, derivative, equations and inequalities, trionometric equations, geometric transformations, non-Euclidean geometrics.

These questions cover the full course of schooling, and at the same time they are much more complex and voluminous. Thus, the coordinate method in our assignments is used much more widely than in high school. Students become familiar with such subtle issues of higher mathematics as the axiomatic method and non-Euclidean geometrics. In addition to the elements of formulas for geometric transformations considered in high school, students are provided with information about more complex forms - projective, affine, that is, students become familiar with group-theoretic foundations in geometry.

We consider it necessary to give students in a specialized school elements of combinatorics, even if they are included in the programs of a regular school. This becomes all the more necessary when these elements are excluded from the program. For students in specialized schools, elements of combinatorics are needed not as an end in themselves, but as a starting point for developing a theoretical-probabilistic approach to the elements of real action.

Great assistance in the work of the Kyiv Correspondence School is provided by Ukrainian Television, in technical unity with which the school has been working since the first days of its existence. Every Monday, programs are broadcast for students of our schools, prepared and read by professors and teachers of Kyiv University, scientists of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR.

Starting this year, the Vishcha School publishing house is releasing a whole series of brochures*) for students interested in mathematics. Here are the “Coordinate Method”, and “Geometric Transformations”, and “Derivative”, and much more. All brochures will be published in Ukrainian.

The purpose of such literature is to consolidate school knowledge, develop the student’s mathematical thinking skills, and in some cases take the reader beyond the school curriculum and provide him with new information, new methods of mathematical reasoning.

*) See «Kvant» No. 4, 1973, p. 66.
