BRANDS DEDICATED
TO THE FIRST WOMAN COSMONAUT

Ten years ago, on June 16, 1963, the first female cosmonaut, Valentina Vladimirovna Tereshkova, launched from Earth on the Vostok-6 spacecraft. Her flight program was designed for one day. However, excellent preliminary preparation and excellent health of Valentina Tereshkova made it possible to extend this period a second time. In a paired flight with “Yastreb” - cosmonaut-5 Valery Fedorovich Bykovsky - “Chaika” - cosmonaut-6 Tereshkova made more than 48 revolutions around the Earth and covered about two million kilometers. The astronauts successfully completed an extensive scientific research program. One of the main tasks of the joint flight of Bykovsky and Tereshkova is to study and compare the reaction of male and female organisms to the difficult conditions of space flight. Indeed, in future space experiments, along with men, women will undoubtedly also participate, whose bodies have many peculiarities.

Paying deep respect to the first female cosmonaut, many countries issued postage stamps dedicated to this flight. We will reproduce here stamps from the USSR, Bulgaria, East Germany, Cuba, Poland and Romania.

V. A. Leshkovtsev
