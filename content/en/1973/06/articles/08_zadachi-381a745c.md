Exercise

1. A triangle $ABC$ is drawn on the plane and two intersecting lines are drawn. Points $P$ and $Q$ move uniformly along these lines, and they do not pass the intersection point of the lines simultaneously. At each instant of time, a point $R$ is constructed such that the triangle $PQR$ is similar to the triangle $ABC$, and the similarity transformation preserves the orientation of the triangle.

Find the locus of points $R$.

2. Let us denote by $\varphi(n)$ the number of natural numbers less than $n$ and subtracted from the primes $n$.

Express in terms of $\varphi(n)$ the number of star polygons (that is, a self-intersecting polygon with all sides equal and all angles equal).

3. The point $P$ lies on a circle circumscribed around a regular $(2n+1)$-gon, with $P$ lying between the vertices $A_1$ and $A_{2n+1}$.

Prove that the sum of the distances from the point $P$ to a vertex with odd indices is equal to the sum of the distances from it to a vertex with even indices.

4. In triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ it is given that $AB = DE, AC = DF$ and $BAC > EDF$. Prove that $\angle ABC$ is less than $\angle DEF$ depending on whether the sum of $\angle ACB + \angle DFE$ is less than, equal to, or greater than $180^\circ$.

5. The diagonals of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ intersect at the point $O$. Prove that if $BO = OD$ and $AB + BC = AD + DC$, then this quadrilateral is either a parallelogram or symmetric with respect to the diagonal $AC$.
