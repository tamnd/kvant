⟦folio 74⟧

TASKS

1. In the circles of the triangle at figure on the right were placed all the numbers from 1 to 7 (each once), and the sum of the numbers along each line segment was the same.

Determine what number was written at the vertex of the triangle and what the indicated amounts were equal to.

2. Three communicating vessels contain water. The left vessel is open. Is the pressure at points A, B and C the same if they lie on the same horizontal line?

3. figure on the right shows a figure in the form of the letter $T$ with four squares offset. Divide this figure along the grid lines into four identical parts, so that each part has one offset cell.

4. It is safer to jump from the sand into the sand than onto solid ground. Why?

5. How to explain the following equations:
   $$09 = 0 \times 9 + (0 + 9),$$
   $$19 = 1 \times 9 + (1 + 9),$$
   $$29 = 2 \times 9 + (2 + 9),$$
   $$\cdots$$
   $$99 = 9 \times 9 + (9 + 9),$$
   $$109 = 10 \times 9 + (10 + 9),$$
   $$\cdots$$
   $$1239 = 123 \times 9 + (123 + 9)$$
   and so on?

6. Wooden and cast iron blocks are separately balanced in the air with a 10 kg weight. What will happen if the bars are placed on different cups of scales, and the scales are placed under a glass cover and the air is pumped out?
