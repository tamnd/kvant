On March 1, 1973, a member of the editorial collection of the journal «Kvant», an outstanding physicist, a major organizer of science and social activities, a member of the Presidium of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician-Secretary of the Department of General Physics and Astronomy of the USSR Academy of Sciences, head of the scientific department of the Institute of Atomic Energy named after A.V. I. V. Kurchatova, Chairman of the National Committee of Soviet Physicists, Chairman of the Scientific Council on the complex problem “Plasma Physics”, Professor at Moscow University, Hero of Socialist Labor, laureate of the Lenin and State Prizes of the USSR, Academician Lev Andreevich Artsimovich.
