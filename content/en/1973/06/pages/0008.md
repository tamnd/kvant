The center of gravity of an arbitrary $n$-gon is constructed as follows. Let us divide the $n$-gon into triangles, in each of which we find the center of gravity. Then at each center of gravity of the triangle we place a mass numerically equal to the area of ​​this triangle. The center of gravity of these masses will be the center of gravity of the original polygon (Fig. 2).

At the yarn. Construct the center of gravity of the trapezoid and the center of gravity of its contour; the same in the case of a divisor (these are two equilateral triangles having a common base, the pins of which lie on opposite sides of it) of an arbitrary irregular pentagon.

But it’s one thing to construct a point - the center of gravity, and another thing to be able to find the distance from it to the axis of rotation. In cases where you succeed (and in school and exam problems this can usually be done), verification using Gylden's theorem will be possible.

The solution to most of the problems included in the geometry problem by N. A. Rybkin (Part 2, §24) can be verified using Gulden’s theorem.

Let us now return to the problem with which we began the article and show its solution (or better yet, verification of the solution) using Gulden’s theorems. Let us first find $R$ - the distance from the axis of rotation to the center of gravity (see Fig. 1):

$$OM = R = OK + KM = \frac{a \sqrt{3}}{3};$$

$$S = P \cdot 2\pi R = 2\pi a^2 \sqrt{3};$$

$$V = S \cdot 2\pi R = \frac{1}{2} \pi a^3.$$

Using Gulden's theorem, check the answers to problems No. 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10 (§24 of the same problem).

Here is a typical task of medium difficulty.

Problem 2*). A regular triangle, side $a$, is drawn around an axis passing through the entire end of its side at an acute angle $a$ to this side. Determine the surface area of ​​the medical body.

Answer: $2\pi a^2 \sqrt{3} \sin (30^\circ + \alpha)$.

Let's get it using Gylden's theorem. From $\Delta AOM$ (Fig. 3)

$$R = AO \cdot \sin (30^\circ + \alpha) = \frac{a \sqrt{3}}{3} \times \sin (30^\circ + \alpha),$$

$$S = P \cdot 2\pi R = 2\pi a^2 \sqrt{3} \sin (30^\circ + \alpha).$$
