Task 5 (MSU, Faculty of Economics, 1970). Indicate the powers of the plane corresponding to those complied numbers that satisfy the condition $|z| + i + z = 2 + i$.

Solution. Let $z = a + bi$; then this condition will take the form

$$i\sqrt{a^2 + b^2} + a + bi = 2 + i.$$

By defining the equality of complex numbers, from here we obtain a system of equations

$$\begin{cases} a = 2, \\ \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} + b = 1, \end{cases}$$

which is easily solved. As a result, we find the number $z = 2 - \frac{3}{2}i$ that satisfies the assignment conditions.
