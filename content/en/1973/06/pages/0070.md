Physics Brochures

The publishing house “Znanie” has been publishing a series of brochures under the general title “Physics” for about 20 years, expanded to a wide range of readers. Most of them talk about modern problems of theoretical and experimental physics. The authors of these brochures are well-known scientists who actively participate in the development of the problems they cover. They usually strive to keep the reader at a distance from the basic physical ideas underlying the research, and therefore almost never use complex mathematical apparatus. Nevertheless, to fully understand such brochures, it is usually necessary to have a preliminary acquaintance with the Suzov course of general physics. But high school students who are passionate about physics will learn a lot of interesting information on the pages of these brochures.

Almost every year, among the brochures of this series there are meetings and also that are quite accessible to high school students. Let's give a few examples.

The series “Physics” produces binary outstanding scientists who created new sections of this science. Typically, the publication of such brochures is associated with anniversaries. Thus, in the work of Academician B. M. Pontecore in “Enrico Fermi” (No. 8, 1971) talks about the life and main scientific achievements of the remarkable integrated scientist. The brochure is all the more interesting because its author collaborated with Fermi for many years and was one of his most beloved students.

Brochure. released for the 100th anniversary of the birth of Ernesta Rutherford, called "On the Physics of the 20th Century" (No. 6, 1971) and contains three lectures by Rutherford, given by him in 1909, 1923 and 1936. These popular lectures are devoted to the atomic structure of electricity, the structure of atoms and the artificial transformation of elements. The lectures were proposed by Academician P. L. Kapits, who worked for Rutherford in Cambridge for almost 14 years.

The exceptional role that physics plays in the scientific and technological revolution arouses every possible interest in the history of this science. Some prominent scientists have expressed the opinion that we cannot count on success in predicting the path of development of physics in the future unless we have a sufficient understanding of its past. In this regard, the idea arose to create an anthology of prefaces to the outstanding works of scientists of the 16th–20th centuries. Such a collection, “The Formation of Physics” (No. 8, 1972), was compiled by Professor S.P. Kapitsa. Each preface offers a short biography of its author. The brochure contains proposals in whole or in expressions to the most significant works on physics of Russian and foreign scientists: Hilbert, Newton,

Lomonosov, Helmholtz, Mendeleev, Einstein, Peren, Planck, Dirac.

Some brochures in the Physics series help orient readers to facts and events related to the development of physics and its achievements. For example, A.I. Bazhenov’s brochure “Physicists-Laureates” (No. 11, 1971) talks about the Nobel Prize-winning physicist. Six famous Soviet physicists - academicians I. E. Tamp, I. M. Frank, P. A. Cherekkov, L. D. Landdu, N. G. Basov and A. M. Prosorov - were awarded Nobel Prizes. The main section of the brochure is dedicated to the Soviet physicist laureates and the works for which they were awarded these prizes. The brochure also provides a brief introduction to the Nobel Prizes and provides a complete list of Nobel laureates in physics by the time the prize was founded.

It is also worth mentioning the brochure of Academician P. L. Kapits, which stands somewhat apart from the main theme of the “Physics” series. This refers to his collection “Physical Problems” (No. 6, 1972). It was released in response to the wishes of numerous readers, especially young ones. This collection also includes several articles by P. L. Kapits on the topic of education in physics.
