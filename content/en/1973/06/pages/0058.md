Problem 7. Complex numbers $z$ satisfy the conditions

$$|z+2+i| = |z-1-4i|.$$ (4)

Where are the dots representing these numbers located?

Solution. As is known, $|z_1-z_2|$ is the distance between the points representing complex numbers $z_1$ and $z_2$. Therefore the number

$$|z+2+i| = |z-(-2-i)|$$

 is the distance from the point representing the number $z$ to the point $M(-2, -1)$, and the number

$$|z-1-4i| = |z-(1+4i)|$$

 is the distance of the point representing the number $z$ to the point $N(1, 4)$. Condition (4) then means that the required points are equidistant from the points $M$ and $N$. Consequently, the required points lie on a line perpendicular to the segment $MN$ and passing through its middle.

Problem 8. Indicate the set of points representing numbers, representing complex numbers that simultaneously satisfy two conditions

$$|z-2i| \leqslant \frac{3}{2}, \frac{\pi}{3} < \arg z < \frac{2\pi}{3}.$$ (5)

Solution. The dots representing those complex numbers that satisfy the first of conditions (5) fill a circle (including a circle) of radius $3/2$ centered at the point $(0,2)$ (Fig. 4). The second condition is satisfied by complex numbers representing the points that are located between the rays emanating from the origin and the composition

$$0 < |z|^2 - |z| + 1 < 6 + 3 |z|,$$ (6)

Solution. Inequality (6), according to the properties of logarithms, is equivalent to the double inequality

$$0 < \frac{|z|^2 - |z| + 1}{2 + |z|} < 3,$$

which can be rewritten (since $2 + |z| > 0$ for any $z$) in the form

$$0 < |z|^2 - |z| + 1 < 6 + 3 |z|.$$

But the inequality $|z|^2 - |z| + 1 > 0$ is valid for any $z$ (since the discriminant of the square trinomial $x^2 - x + 1$ is negative), and the inequality $|z|^2 - |z| + 1 < 6 + 3 |z|$, that is, $|z|^2 - 4 |z| - 5 < 0$ is satisfied for $|z| < 5$ (the inequality $x^2 - 4x - 5 < 0$ is satisfied for $-1 < x < 5$, but $|z| \geqslant 0$ for any $z$). Therefore, conditions (6) are satisfied by complex numbers $z$ satisfying conditions $|z| < 5$; these numbers fill the interior of a circle of radius 5 centered at the origin.
