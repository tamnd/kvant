4. A thin flat piclar lens of diameter $2r$ with a radius of curvature $R$ and a refractive index $n_0$ is placed in such a position that there is air $(n_1=1)$ on the left, and a transparent medium with a refractive index $n_2 \neq 1$ on the right (the convex side faces the air). In the air, at a distance of $p_1$ from the lens, an accurate source of monochromatic light is installed on the main optical axis.

1) Prove the following relationship between the position of the image, located at a distance $p_2$ from the lens, and the position of the source $p_1$ in the approximation of parastrong points: $\frac{f_1}{p_1} + \frac{f_2}{p_2} = 1$, where $f_1$ and $f_2$ are the focal lengths in air in a medium with a refractive index $n_2$ of the corresponding one.

2) The lens is cut perpendicular to the flat face into two equal parts, which are then moved apart by a distance $\delta \ll r$ (Billet bilism). On the symmetry axis of this system at a distance $p_1$ ($p_1 > f_1$) from the axis (see Fig. 2) an exact light source is installed. To the right of the system on the screen $E$, we install it parallel to the lens at a distance $d$, $N$ interference fringes are formed if there is also air on the right.
