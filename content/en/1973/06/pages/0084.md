Subscription continues for the second half of 1973 for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant».

The magazine is intended primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is also useful for teachers, especially those who teach courses or elective classes in physics or mathematics, as well as all lovers of mathematics and physics.

The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school,” that is, materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to explain various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems offered at entrance exams for various disciplines, problems offered at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical instruments will help the reader to set up and prove a physical experiment.

The magazine publishes review articles on its pages, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries will be ignited.

The magazine constantly publishes results on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

The magazine is distributed by subscription only.
