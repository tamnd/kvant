⟦folio 59⟧

A typical function of a mathematical engineer: in a team, he, together with general engineers of the relevant profiles, for example, ACS developers, solves specific technical or management problems. At the same time, the main creative contribution of a mathematical engineer is the development of acceptable computational analytical algorithms.

A more complex and difficult function of a mathematical engineer is to build mathematical models, such as technological processes. By “playing out” various variants of a mathematical model on a computer, a mathematical engineer can choose the optimal one, that is, the best one under the given conditions. Here, knowledge of applied mathematics alone is no longer enough. Some engineering knowledge is required, which is not provided by universities that produce “pure” mathematicians.

And, of course, a mathematical engineer must be able to program on a computer in modern times.

V. K. Saulev

Moscow Aviation Institute

MAI has seven faculties. These are the Faculty of Aircraft, the Faculty of Aircraft Installations, the Faculty of Aircraft and Helicopter Engineering (specialties: aircraft engineering, helicopter engineering, hydroerosive dynamics), the faculty of aircraft engines (specialty: aviation engines), the faculty of automatic control systems of aircraft (specialty: aircraft instrument making, hydroerosive dynamics), the Faculty of Devices, Automated Systems controls, electronic computers, applied mathematics), Faculty of Radio Engineering (specialties: radio engineering, design and production of radio equipment), Faculty of Economics and Organization of Aircraft Production (specialties: automatic control systems, economics and organization of the mechanical engineering industry, organization of mechanized processing of economic information).

There is a preparatory department at the MAI.

Below we present typical options for entrance exams to the Moscow Aviation Institute in mathematics and physics in 1972.

Mathematica

Option 1

1. Solve equation $3^{x-1} \cdot x^2 + x (3^x - 2^x) = 2 (2^x - 3^{x-1})$.

2. Prove that in any triangle $ABC$

$$h_a = 2p \frac{\sin B/2 \cdot \sin C/2}{\cos A/2},$$

where $h_a$ is the height omitted from the vertex $A$, $2p$ is the perimeter of the triangle.

3. Solve the equation

$$\log_{\sin x} \frac{1}{4} (4\sin x - \cos x) = 3.$$

4. The distance between cities $A$ and $B$ is 60 km. Two trains leave at the same time: one from $A$ to $B$, the other from $B$ to $A$. A train going from $A$ to $B$, having traveled 20 km, stops for half an hour, then goes further and after 4 minutes meets a train going from $B$ to $A$. Both trains approach their destination at the same time. Determine train speeds.

5. The vertex of a regular quadrangular paramid lies in the center of the face of the cube, and its base lies in the plane of the opposite face, and the sides of the base of the paramid, equal to $2\sqrt{2}$ cm, are parallel to the diagonals of the base of the cube. Find the volume of the part of the paramid located in the cube if the edge of the cube is 1 cm.

Option 2

1. Construct a quadratic equation with real coefficients if one of the roots $z = a + bi (b \geq 0)$ satisfies the equation

$$(z + 2i)^2 + 4z - 4i\bar{z} + 17 = 0,$$

Where

$$\bar{z} = a - bi.$$

2. At what real values ​​$b$ of the system

$$\begin{cases} x^2 - 2x + y^2 = 0, \\ y - ax + ab = 0 \end{cases}$$

has a real solution for any real $a$?

3. Prove that $tg(\alpha + \beta) = 4tg\beta$ if $5\sin \alpha = 3\sin (\alpha + 2\beta)$.

4. In triangle $ABC$, side $AB$ is equal to $c$, and the bisserris inside it of angle $A$ intersects sides $BC$ at point $D$ such that $DA = DB$. Find the length of side $BC$.

5. The sphere touches all the lateral edges of the regularly quadrangular prisms and its base. Find the ratio of the surface area of ​​the sphere lying in the prisms to the area of ​​the total surface.prisms.
