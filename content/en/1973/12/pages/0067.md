FIRST ROCKET TO MARS

Mars is one of the most interesting planets in the solar system. It was with him that a person on DEXDU was connected to a close meeting with other intelligent beings. For a long time, Mars asked scientists riddle after riddle. Here are the Martian “channels”, which were first noticed by the Ilyan astronomer Schipapelli in 1877, and the hypothesis of the Soviet astrophysicist I. S. Shklovsky that one of the satellites of Mars [Phobos] is artificial and much more. However, careful optical studies and even radioastronomical measurement methods did not provide accurate answers to numerous questions related to the nature of this mysterious planet.

The first and very important step towards the exploration of Mars with the help of spacecraft capable of approaching it at relatively short distances, becoming its satellites and delivering scientific equipment, and, in the future, people, to its surface, was carried out in the Soviet Union. Eleven years ago, on November 1, 1962, the world's first interplanetary astronomical station, Mars-1, set off on its journey. Since then, scientists have acquired a huge amount of valuable scientific data about the nature of Mars. Excellent photographs of a number of areas of the surface of Mars were obtained by the American Mariner astronomical stations. The Soviet interplanetary stations Mars-2 and Mars-3 turned into artificial satellites of Mars. A soft landing of an astronomical Martian station on the surface of the planet was successfully carried out. Now the astronomical station “Mars-4” and “Mars-7” are approaching Mars.
In memory of the launch of the first interplanetary astronomical station, special postage stamps were issued on a number of pages. We will reproduce here the stamps of the USSR, Poland, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Mongolia and Cuba, which depict the Mars-1 station.
V. A. Leshkovce
