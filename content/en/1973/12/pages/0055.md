Many of our readers thank «Kvant» for the articles and tasks from the “Applicant’s Practicum” section. The middle ones are tenth-grader Viktor Krygin from the city of Semipalatinsk, Kazakh SSR, mathematics teacher M. We know from letters from readers that many consider the scientific level of the materials published in the journal to be so high that it is not easy to reach it. Is it good? Here’s how ninth-grader Vasily Vasiliev from Voronezh answered this question for himself: “I believe that the level of material included corresponds to the purpose of the magazine. You can return to an article that is unavailable now after some time.” Tenth-grader Sergei Princes from Kemerovo, who dreams of becoming a mathematician, has already confirmed some success: “At first the articles were a bit difficult, but then I got stronger. Now all the materials are available to me.” Voronezh State University physics student Anatoly German, who worked on «Kvant» as a schoolboy and continues to be friends with him, adheres to me: “The level of presentation «Kvant» is quite accessible for those who want to understand the material that interests them.” As if counting on this, Moscow’s eighth-classicist Leonid Aganov writes: “I believe that I am still growing and growing to the level of a magazine. I'm leaving!"

The activity of readers can be seen in the fact that more and more often they are sending their own problems and small studies to the editorial office. In mathematics, for example, in the first five months of the new year there are about 240 such letters.

This year, many readers have given us their opinions on the design of «Kvant». The overwhelming majority responded to the questions - what are your comments and suggestions regarding the design of the magazine? - expressed satisfaction. Docent V.E. Stepan from the Krasnoyarsk Polytechnic Institute writes: “The magazine has its own appearance, which is different from other popular science magazines. In my opinion, the example should be presented in this form in the future.” Moscow State University student Valery Grobovsky believes: “The magazine is designed with taste (it does not turn into either a series of reports or decorated paintings) and with some, one might say, flavor and fantasy.” Here is the opinion of two schoolchildren. Ninth-grader Vladimir Khasanshin writes: “The magazine is designed in an original way. It would be nice if there were funny ones, but they didn’t take up too much space.” Ninth-grader Lydia Bashkatava from Voronezh believes: “Only positive things can be said about the design of the magazine. All statements are clear and understandable. It is believed that multicolor plays a big role in this.” Opinion of mathematics teachers N. Ya. Bushmina from the village. Vastan of the Mikhailovsky district of the Altai Territory: “The design is bright and red. Let it remain that way.” Non-columnar eighth-grader Igor Apalkov from the city of Saransk, Mordovian Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic: “In my opinion, the magazine is designed quite well. Although somewhat unambiguous. The bastards should add some romance.” The opposite point of view is shared by tenth-grader Grigory Beygeldrud from the city of Artemovsk, Donetsk region of the Ukrainian SSR: “Excessive oblige of flowers is wrong. I would like to see more drawings with four, preferably loosened, in accordance with the meaning, personal. It would be preferable for it to look like a scientific piece, rather than a popular piece.” Tenth-grader Anatoly Kutyrev from the Stupinsky district of the Moscow region believes: “The magazine as a whole is designed very well and colorfully. But I wanted it to have more drawings for the most difficult tasks.”

Out of more than four questionnaires from readers of questionnaires, only two contain dissatisfaction with the design. Thus, physics teacher V. A. Ganoshkin believes: “There is little invention in the design. Dull, unattractive headlines. There are few good portraits of scientists whose assessments are included in this magazine.”

The letters and answers to questions contain many wishes, which are edited to take into account and for which the reader is very grateful.

Unfortunately, very few of the schoolchildren participating in the “Problem Book” competition «Kvant» sent us an answer to the questionnaire. We ask you, dear participants of the competition, to answer the questions this time.
