Instruction 2a. The original inequality contained two parameters: $R$ and $r$. Using these problems, we replaced it with the inequality

$$\frac{2 + \text{tg}^2 \alpha}{2 \text{tg} \alpha \cdot \text{tg} \frac{\alpha}{2}} \geq \sqrt{2} + 1,$$

in which we can go to tg $\frac{\alpha}{2}$, that is, to only one parameter, which means it will be easier to prove.

Note 26. Transforming the ley part of the last inequality, we obtain

$$\frac{1 + \text{tg}^4 \frac{\alpha}{2}}{2 \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} \cdot \left(1 - \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)} \geq \sqrt{2} + 1.$$

We denote $\text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} = x$, the inequality to be proved takes the form

$$\frac{1 + x^2}{2x(1-x)} \geq \sqrt{2} + 1.$$

Instruction 2a. To prove the inequality it is enough to show that

$$A = \frac{1 + x^2}{2x(1-x)} - \left(\sqrt{2} + 1\right) \geq 0.$$

Instruction 26.

$$A = \frac{1 + x^2 - 2x\sqrt{2} - 2x^2 + 2\sqrt{2}x^2 + 2x^2}{2(1-x)} = \frac{(3 + 2\sqrt{2})x^2 - 2(1 + \sqrt{2})x + 1}{2x(1-x)}$$

The discriminant of a quadratic trinomial in the numerator is zero (check) and $3 + 2\sqrt{2} > 0$. Therefore, the trinomial is non-negative.

Instruction 2a. If it can be shown that the denominator of the fraction $2x \times (1-x)$ is positive, then the problem will be solved.

Instruction 26. $x = \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}$, but $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$, $0 < \frac{\alpha}{2} < \frac{\pi}{4}$, whence $0 < \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} < 1$, that is, $0 < x < 1$, $1 - x > 0$. This means $A \geq 0$ and inequality $\frac{R}{r} \geq \sqrt{2} + 1$ are proven.

Exercises

1. In the parallelogram $ABCD$, the point $M$ is the midpoint of $AD$, $BC = 2AB$, $E$ - the base of the perpendicular dropped from the vertex $C$ to $AB$. Prove that $\Rightarrow EMD = 3 \Rightarrow AEM$.

2. A circle is circumscribed around the triangle $ABC$. Prove that the angle between the diameter of this circle $CM$ and the height of the triangle $CK$ is equal to $| \Rightarrow A - \Rightarrow B|$.

3. Prove that $\frac{n}{12} + \frac{n^2}{8} + \frac{n^3}{24}$ is an integer for any even $n$.

4. The sides of the parallelogram are $a$ and $b$. Calculate the diagonals of the quadrilateral formed by the intersection of the bisectors of the internal angles of this parallelogram.

5. Construct a triangle $ABC$ along side $b$, radius $R$ of the circumscribed circle and median $m_c$.

6. The two faces of a triangular rectangle are equilateral right angled triangles with a common hypotenuse $AB$. The dihedral angle at $AB$ is $a$. Find a dihedral angle whose edge has a leg.

7. Find the volume of a rectiline, at the base of which lies a regular triangle from side $a$, if the dihedral angles between the plane of the base and the lateral faces are equal to $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$.

8. Find all five-digit numbers of the form $\frac{34x5y}{34x5y}$ divisible by 36.

9. Prove that $(\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2})^{243}$ can be represented as $a\sqrt{3} - b\sqrt{2}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers, and $3a^2 - 2b^2 = 1$.
