Note 1. Note that when we change the places of numbers that we perform, the sum of the products strictly increases. It follows that the sum of the products is equal to the maximum if and only if the table is obtained from a table with ordered rows by rearranging the columns.

V. M. Makarevich

**M198. Given a parallelogram ABCD. On the lines AB and BC, points of the corresponding H and K are chosen so that the triangles KAB and HCB are equilateral (KA = AB and HC = CB (Fig. 8)). Prove that the triangle KDH is equilateral.**

This is a very simple task. Let's look at triangles AKD and CDH. They are equal since AD ​​= CH, AK = DC and DAK = DCH (see Fig. 9, a, b, c). Therefore, the lengths of the segments KD and DH are the same. It is also easy to verify that the triangles KDH, AKB and BHC are similar (do this).
