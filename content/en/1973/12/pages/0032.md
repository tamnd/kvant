M239. There are two points A and B on the plane. Let C be some point equally distant from A and B. Let us construct a sequence of points $C_1 = C, C_2, C_3, \dots, C_n, C_{n+1}$, where $C_{n+1}$ is the center of the circle circumscribed about the triangle $AC_nB$. At what position of point C
a) point $C_n$ will fall in the middle of segment AB (in this case $C_{n+1}$ and further terms of the sequence are not defined)?
b) does the point $C_n$ coincide with C?
N. Chernov, 10th grade student (Krivoi Rohe)

M240. Given $x$, the values ​​of $x^8$ can be found in three arithmetic operations: $x^2 = x \cdot x, x^4 = x^2 \cdot x^2, x^8 = x^4 \cdot x^4$, and $x^{15}$ in five operations: the first three are the same, then $x^8 \cdot x^8 = x^{16}$ and $x^{16} : x = x^{15}$. Prove that
a) $x^{1000}$ can be found in 12 operations (multiplication and division);
b)* for any natural $n$ value $x^n$ can be found in more than $\frac{3}{2} \log_2 n + 1$ steps.

E. G. Belaza
