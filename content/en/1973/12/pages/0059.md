15. $\frac{1}{2} \frac{a + b}{a - b} ab \text{tg} \alpha$. Solution. Let's construct a segment $CK$ (Fig. 2) parallel to side $AD$. Then $\Rightarrow BCK = \alpha$. Let $S$ be the area of ​​the trapezoid, $S_1$ the area of ​​$\triangle BCK$, $h$ the height of the trapezoid. Then

$$S = \frac{1}{2} (a + b) h = \frac{a + b}{a - b} S_1,$$

$$S_1 = \frac{CK \cdot BC}{2} \sin \alpha.$$

According to the cosine theorem:

$$BK^2 = CK^2 + BC^2 - 2CK \cdot BC \cos \alpha.$$

Considering that $CK = AD$ and that the triangle $AOD$ and $BOC$ are right-angled, we get

$$CK \cdot BC = \frac{AD^2 + BC^2 - BK^2}{2 \cos \alpha} =$$

$$= \frac{AO^2 + OD^2 + OC^2 + BO^2 - BK^2}{2 \cos \alpha} =$$

$$= \frac{a^2 + b^2 - (a - b)^2}{2 \cos \alpha} = \frac{ab \cos \alpha}{2}$$
