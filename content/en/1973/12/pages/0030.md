Task 10. The set M consists of eights lying on the plane and not intersecting each other (an eight is a pair of tangent circles, each of which can be of any size). Prove that M is finite or countable.

It is easy to see that by placing a non-intersecting letter “G” or “O” on a plane, one can obtain an odd set. Is there any other letter that has the same properties as the number eight?

Task 11. The set M consists of the letter “T” lying on a plane and not intersecting the letter. Prove that M is finite or countable.

Having solved the previous problem, you can easily say about each letter of the Russian alphabet whether this letter can be on the plane in an odd number, or whether in any arrangement it forms no more than a countable set.
