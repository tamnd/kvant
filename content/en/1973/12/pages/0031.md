M236. a) There are 51 two-digit numbers. Prove that from these numbers it is possible to choose at least 6 numbers so that no two of the selected numbers have the same digit in any digit.

6) Given natural numbers $k$ and $n$, $1 < k < n$. For every smallest $m$ the following statement is true: for any arrangement of $m$ rooks on a board of size $n \times n$ can one choose $k$ rooks from these $m$ such that no two of the chosen rooks are each other?

M237. The angles of an acute triangle are $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$. What masses must be placed at its vertices so that the center of gravity of these three masses falls

a) at the point where the heights intersect?

6) to the center of the circumscribed circle?

b) at the point of intersection of the segments connecting the vertices and the points of tangency of the opposite sides with the inscribed circle?

d) to the center of the circumscribed circle?

B. D. Ginzbure

M238 * Prove that the sum $C_n^1 + C_n^3 \cdot 1973 + C_n^5 \cdot (1973)^2 + C_n^7 (1973)^3 + \ldots$ is divisible by $2^{n-1}$.

(Here $C_n^k$ are the coefficients of the polynomial)

$(a+b)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} C_n^k a^k b^{n-k}$.

F. G. Shleifer
