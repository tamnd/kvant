⟦folio 51⟧

To the theory of hunting
(How to catch a lion in the wilderness)

1. We cut through it with a deserted line running from the south to the south. The lion is located either in the eastern part of the village or in the western part. Let's assume for clarity that it is located in the western part. We cut it with a line going from west to east. Leo is either in the northern part or in the southern part. Let us assume for the sake of definition that it is located in the southern part, we cut it with a line going from the north to the south. Let us assume this process ad infinitum, impacting after each step a strong lattice along the dividing line. The area of ​​successively obtained regions tends to zero, so that the lion eventually finds itself surrounded by a lattice of an arbitrarily small perimeter.

2. We note that wild lions in the Sahara desert are unobservable majesties. Consequently, all observed lions in the Sahara desert are tame. We leave the capture of a tame lion to the reader as an independent exercise.

3. In any case, there is a positive, non-zero probability that the lion himself will end up in the cage. Sit and wait.

4. Through the void we will stretch a semi-permeable membrane, which will let everything through except the lion.

5. Let's irradiate the wasteland with slow neutrons. Radioactivity will be induced inside the lion and it will begin to disintegrate. If you wait long enough, the lion will not be able to offer any resistance.
