Mathematics

1. Let us denote the lengths of the diagonals of the parallelogram by $2d_1$ and $2d_2$. Then the area of ​​the parallelogram is $S = 2d_1d_2 \sin \beta$. According to the cosine theorem:

$$(3a)^2 = d_1^2 + d_2^2 + 2d_1d_2 \cos \beta,$$
$$a^2 = d_1^2 + d_2^2 - 2d_1d_2 \cos \beta,$$
whence $2a^2 = d_1d_2 \cos \beta$. Hence,
$$S = 2 \frac{2a^2}{\cos \beta} \sin \beta = 4a^2 \mathrm{tg} \beta.$$

2. The area of ​​triangle $ABC$ is equal to $\frac{1}{2} AB \cdot BC \cdot \sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2} AB \cdot BD \cdot \sin 15^\circ + \frac{1}{2} BC \cdot BD \cdot \sin 15^\circ$.

Hence $BD = \frac{6}{5 \sin 15^\circ}$. The area of ​​triangle $ABD$ is equal to $\frac{1}{2} AB \cdot BD \cdot \sin 15^\circ = \frac{12}{5} \cos^2 \theta$.
