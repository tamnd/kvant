The lower pictures show three projections of the model made from a piece of fine wire. These models do not have overlapping [double] sections or fastened knots. Using the given projections, construct visual images of the figures (all these figures fit into a cube). For example, on figure on the right [above], one of the figures is constructed in an axistric projection.
