ХV mathematical

N. S. Petrakov V. A. Skovortsov

Another International Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren has ended. This time it took place in Moscow from July 5 to 16. Teams from 16 countries took part in the Olympiad: Austria, Bulgaria, Great Britain, Hungary, East Germany, Cuba, Mongolia, the Netherlands, Poland, Romania, the Soviet Union, Finland, France, Czechoslovakia, Sweden, Yugoslavia.

It was the most representative Olympiad in the entire history of international mathematical Olympiads.

Each country's team included 8 participants (with the exception of the Cuban team, which consisted of 5 participants) - winners of national Olympiads.

The USSR team included 8 tenth grade students - first and second prize-winners of the seventh All-Union Olympiad. Among the second prize-winners, those who had the best Olympic biography were included.

Let us now present the problems of the XV International Mathematical Olympiad and their short solutions.

Tasks

1 (Czechoslovakia. The complete solution was scored with 6 points). Point O lies on straight line l; OP₁, OP₂, ..., OPₙ are unit vectors and all points P₁, P₂, ..., Pₙ lie in the same plane containing l, and all in the same line om l. Prove that if n is odd, then |OP₁| + |OP₂| + ... + |OPₙ| ≥ 1, where |OM| denotes the length of the OM vector.

2 (Poland, 6 points). Find out whether there is a finite set M in a space that does not lie in the same plane, such that for any two points A, B belonging to M, there are two other points C, D belonging to M, also that AB and CD are parallel and do not coincide.

3 (Schvenice, 8 points). The minimum value is a² + b², where a and b are real numbers for which the equation x¹ + ax³ + bx² + ax + 1 = 0 has at least one real root.

4 (Yugoslavia, 6 points). The soldier must check for mines in an area shaped like an equilateral triangle, including the border. The radius of action of its detector is equal to the position of the height of the triangle. The soldier emerges from one vertex of the triangle. Which path should he align so that the name can be considered and the tasks completed?

5 (Poland, 6 points). A non-empty set G of not equal to the last function of the real argument x of the form f(x) = ax + b, where a, b are real numbers, we satisfy the following conditions:

a) if f, g ∈ G, then g f ∈ G, where (g f)(x) = g(f(x)), that is, the set G is closed under superposition;

b) if f ∈ G, then f(x) = ax + b, then the inverse function f⁻¹ ∈ G, where f⁻¹(x) = x - b/a;

b) for any f ∈ G there exist x₁, ..., aₙ - given n positive numbers u q - given real numbers, and 0 < q < 1. At the most there are also n real numbers b₁, ..., bₙ, such that

a) aₙ > bₙ for all k from 1 to n;

b) q < (bₙ+1)/bₙ < 1/q for all k from 1 to n-1;

b) b₁ + ... + bₙ < (1+q)/(1-q)(a₁ + ... aₙ).

3. Noticing that $x = 0$ is not a root of this equation, we transform it to the form $x^2 + ax + b + \frac{a}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2} = 0$ or $(x + \frac{1}{x})^2 + a(x + \frac{1}{x}) + b - 2 = 0$.

It is easy to verify that for any real $x$ the inequality $x + \frac{1}{x} \geq 2$ is valid. To fix $x_0$, the set of pairs of numbers $(a, b)$ for which the original equation has a root equal to $x_0$ is determined by the linear equation $y_0^2 + ay_0 + b - 2 = 0$, where $y_0 = x_0 + \frac{1}{x_0}$. This set of pairs is represented by a prim on the coordinate plane $(a, b)$. Since in this plane the expression $a^2 + b^2$ can be used geometrically, the pineapple value $a^2 + b^2$ for points $(a, b)$ on our line will be equal to the square of the length of the perpendicular dropped from the origin to the line in question.

It is easy to calculate that it is equal to $\rho = \frac{(y_0^2 - 2)^2}{1 + y_0^2}; \rho \geq \frac{4}{5}$ at $y_0^2 = \left(x_0 + \frac{1}{x_0}\right)^2 \geq 4$. It is not difficult to prove that $\frac{(t - 2)^2}{1 + t} \geq \frac{4}{5}$ for $t \geq 4$, and for $t = 4$ equality is achieved. Thus, the value of $a^2 + b^2$ for all possible real roots $x_0$ is not less than $\frac{4}{5}$. Noting that $x_0 = 1$ is the root of the original equation at $a = -\frac{4}{5}, b = -\frac{2}{5}$, we find that $\frac{4}{5}$ is the smallest original value for $a^2 + b^2$.
