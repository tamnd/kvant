⟦folio 51⟧

Calendar task

It is not always easy to determine the day of the anniversary. Take, for example, the birthday in the line of astronomer Nicolaus Copernicus.

In the 15th century they used the Julian calendar. According to this calendar (as they now say, according to the old style), Copernicus was born on February 19, 1473. Nowadays they use the Greek calendar. According to this calendar (new style), Copernicus' birthday should be considered February 28, 1473. The difference between the same dates according to these calendars in the 15th century was 9 days. So the 500th anniversary of the birth of Copernicus, according to the new style, should be celebrated on February 28, 1973. But one can argue otherwise. According to the old style, this day should be celebrated on February 19. Therefore, on February 19, 1973, according to the old style, the 500th anniversary of the birth of Copernicus expired. But in the 20th century, the difference between the calendars became 13 days, and the birthday therefore falls on March 4, 1473.

Which day is calculated correctly? Why was there any difference at all?
