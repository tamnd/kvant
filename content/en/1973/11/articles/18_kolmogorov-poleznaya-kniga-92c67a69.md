The book “Beginnings of Mathematical Analysis” *) arose from lectures given at the evening department of the Geological Faculty of Moscow State University, where about a hundred hours were devoted to the entire course of higher mathematics (lectures and exercises). In accordance with this task, 24 pages of four chapters are devoted to an introduction to the theory of differential equations, which is rarely done in mathematical analysis courses of such length (the entire book contains 158 pages).

Book
about international mathematical olympiads

Two years ago, the third edition of the book by E. A. Morozova and I. S. Petrakov “International Mathematical Olympiads” * was published. Book contents_

lives the problems of all International Mathematical Olympids up to and including date one; it contains the most interesting problems from the materials of the International Jury of the Olympiads, which were not included in the competition only because of the generality of the proposed problems; The problems of national olympids of a number of countries participating in the International Mathematical Olympids are presented.

Who is able to cope with the tasks of the International Olympiad? According to the status of the International Olympiads, the participant is required to have completed secondary school training in mathematics. But to solve these problems, it is often enough to be able to reason competently, and therefore, most of the tasks are completely within the capabilities of 7th-8th grade students. The value of the book lies in the fact that it teaches how to mouse using elementary mathematical material.

Who reads this book, who draws from it material to test their strengths and capabilities in mathematics? All the winners of the National and International Olympiads (except, of course, the very first ones, who did not have such an opportunity) actively used it in preparation for the competition. But not only them. The book is also very useful for those schoolchildren who are preparing for regional mathematical Olympiads. It is interesting to everyone who loves elementary mathematics, who wants to learn how to independently solve Olympid problems, to reason mathematically competently, to substantiate their thoughts, who wants to experience what kind of “insight” with the help of which problems are solved with a “twist”.

The book “International Mathematical Olympids” not only offers readers problems related to the Olympids. It contains solutions to all problems given by the authors of the manual or the participants of the Olympiad.

The author's solutions are well thought out and well edited. This, in particular, is evidenced by the following fact: if for the first and second editions of this book the authors received letters in which shortcomings were noted and typos were pointed out, then after the publication of the third edition the authors receive only new solutions (sometimes more elegant) to problems found by readers.

Unfortunately, from the third edition of the book the use of photographs of the first signs of Olympidas was excluded almost completely. It seems that in subsequent editions this deficiency will be eliminated.

And the need for a new edition is of real importance. After the one-date Olympiad, four more International Mathematical Olympids were held. There is no doubt that the authors have accumulated rich and interesting material, which is eagerly awaited by beloved mathematicians. In addition, the first three editions were embroidered in small editions, it is almost impossible to find the book, and many current senior students know about it mainly from the stories of senior students.

It would be good if the authors in subsequent editions talked about the fates of those who competed in the Olympids. Many of them are prominent educators and renowned scientists. Obviously, participation in the Olympics played a significant role in their fate.
