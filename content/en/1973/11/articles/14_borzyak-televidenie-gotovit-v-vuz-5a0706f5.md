TV
PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

In «Kvant» No. 9 for this year we placed information on admission to television physics and mathematics courses for applicants to universities. Lectures on telecourses are given from October 1 and continue throughout the academic year. Each stage of application the following programs are offered: in physics - on Monday evening and Friday morning; in mathematics - on Tuesday and Wednesday morning. In the same week, these programs are repeated: in physics - on Wednesday and Sunday mornings; in mathematics - on Thursday and Saturday evenings.

The program in physics and mathematics is based on the “Program of entrance examinations for applicants to higher educational institutions of the USSR.” Telesantry helps to consolidate, deepen, expand and systematize students’ school knowledge.

Homework assignments are regularly offered by telecourse providers (they are published in the Moscow Speaks and Shows diary). Our magazine will publish information about the work of telecourses and assignments for independent solutions on the covered topics.

In this issue we talk about classes held in October and November.

Physics

At the department of physics of television preparatory courses in October November the first program “Mechanics” is studied. We offer readers of the magazine several tasks on this topic.

1. At the bottom of a cylindrical vertical vessel, the internal diameter of which is $D = 10$ cm, there are four metal balls with a diameter of $d = 3,65$ cm. Determine the pressure force of the balls on the side surface of the vessel if the density of the metal is $\rho = 7,7 \text{ e/cm}^3$. Let's make a claim.

2. A homogeneous workpiece moves along the rollers of a horizontal conveyor at a speed of $v = 10 \text{ m/c}$ and jumps out onto a metal platform located at the same level as the transport. Determine what maximum length the workpiece can be in order to be entirely on the platform if the friction coefficient between the workpiece and the platform is $k = 0,80$. The resistance of the conveyor rollers when moving is very small.

3. On a moving cart, a small heavy ball hangs on an inextensible and weightless thread of length $l = 80$ cm. The cart first moved by acceleration $a_1 = 2,0 \text{ m/c}^2$, and then by acceleration $a_2 = -1,0 \text{ m/c}^2$. Determine the distance between the equilibrium positions of the ball in general cases.
