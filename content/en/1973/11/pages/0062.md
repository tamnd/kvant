1. Two trains travel along two parallel tracks in one direction: a passenger train, 200 m long, at a speed of 72 km/h, and a freight train, 400 m long, at a speed of 45 km/h. How long will it take a passenger to burn out a freight one?

2. The trailing column of troops, spreading over 2.5 km and showering at a speed of 5 km/h, sent a motorcyclist with the knowledge of the commander at the head of the column. The commander receives the notice and writes a response while standing on the side of the road within three minutes. Determine the average speed of the motorcyclist if he returned to the leader after 9 minutes 27 seconds.

3. Two quantities are moving towards each other: one of them, having a speed of 7.2 km/h, descends from the mountain with an acceleration of 0.30 m/s²; the other, having a speed of 36 km/h, rises with an acceleration of -0.20 m/s². What was the distance between the velocipedes at the initial moment if they met after 0.5 minutes? At what longest mountain length does the problem have a solution?
