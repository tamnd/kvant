Superconducting states of matter

Discovery of superconductivity. The phenomenon of superconductivity was discovered a little more than
60 years ago, in 1911. In the laboratory of the Dutch physicist Kamerlingh Onnes, the electrical resistance of metals at ultra-low temperatures was studied.

Kamerlingh Onnes showed how the ability of pure metals to conduct electric current changes with changing temperatures. Resistance to the movement of electrons in a pure metal arises due to thermal vibrations of ions located at the nodes of the crystal lattice. When the temperature rises, these fluctuations become less intense, which leads to a decrease in resistance.
