BEGINNINGS OF MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS
O. S. IVASHEV - MUSATOV

The book “Beginnings of Mathematical Analysis” *) arose from lectures given at the evening department of the Geological Faculty of Moscow State University, where about a hundred hours were devoted to the entire course of higher mathematics (lectures and exercises). The author has set himself the task of introducing readers to the beginning of analysis in size, enough to understand the main ways of their application in natural science. In accordance with this task, 24 pages of four chapters are devoted to an introduction to the theory of differential equations, which is rarely done in mathematical analysis courses of such length (the entire book contains 158 pages).

On the other hand, the author did not consider it possible to sacrifice the logical clarity of presentation common among mathematicians, which is sometimes neglected in books aimed specifically at future physicists and technicians. Some
