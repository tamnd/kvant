⟦folio 66⟧

Grand opening of the XV International Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren. The chairman of the organizing committee of the Olympiad, vice-president of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR, Professor A. I. Markushevich, speaks.

On July 7, at a meeting of the International Jury, out of 14 pre-selected problems sent by the participating countries, 6 were selected, which were offered to the participants of the Olympiad, and approved the number of points awarded for solving each problem. The competition was held over two days, July 9 and 10, at school No. 68 in the Kuibyshev district of Moscow, and on each day 4 hours were allocated to solve three problems. Then the team leaders, together with their marking experts, checked the work and gave it a chairman's assessment. The unity of criteria for evaluating the work of all participants in the Olympiad was ensured by coordinates selected from among Moscow mathematicians. Each task was supervised by 3 coordinators, who checked its solution for all participants.

Discussion of schoolchildren's work. From left to right: V. Gutenacher (coordinator), head of the French team Professor M. Glaser, V. Dorofeev (coordinator).
