⟦folio 68⟧

The chairman of the organizing committee of the Olympiad, Professor A. I. Markushevich, presents a II degree diploma to Pavel Kröger (GDR), who also received the magazine prize «Kvant».

Laureate of the magazine «Kvant» Luis Sastre (Cuba).

Deputy The editor-in-chief of the magazine «Kvant» M. L. Smolyansky presents the magazine's prize to Jean-Christophe Ekkots (France).

On July 13, the International Journal shared the results of excellence and awarded the winners. It was decided to award the first degree dilomas to participants who scored from 35 to 40 points. They were received by:

Konyagin Sergey (USSR), Grozman Pavel (USSR), Egorov Georgy (USSR), Kollar Janos (Hungary), Gotto David (Great Britain).

It was decided to award II degree dilomas to participants who scored from 27 to 34 points. They were received by:

Okneanu Adrian (Romania), Budaev Victor (USSR), Ekkoc Jean-Christophe (France), Andrejczak Grzegosz (Poland), Steiner Michael (Sweden), Wegert Elias (GDR), Verman Piotr (Poland), Vonnet Michel (France), Palfi Peter Pal (Hungary), First Paul (Czechoslovakia), Kiss Emil (Hungary), Bottcher Albrext (GDR), Leschiner Dmitry (USSR), Kröger Paul (GDR), Leblond Denis (France).
