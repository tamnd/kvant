Rings
Saturn

The middle planet of the solar system, Saturn, is the only planet surrounded by pegs. These rings are four. The latter, almost invisible, was discovered just two years ago.

Recently, American scientists Thomas McDonough and Neil Brice put forward a hypothesis about the existence of a fifth ring on Saturn. In their opinion, the “culprit” for the occurrence of this ring is the largest of Saturn’s 10 satellites - Titan. Titan is unique in the solar system. It is the only satellite of the planet that has an atmosphere. The air density of Titan's atmosphere is almost the same as that of Earth (Titan's air consists mainly of hydrogen and methane). Titan's dense atmosphere is constantly dissipating: air particles in the upper layers of the atmosphere, the speed of which is quite high, escape the sphere of gravity of the satellite. Particles “escaped” from Titan’s sphere of gravity fall into Saturn’s powerful gravitational field and are “put” into orbit around the planet. This, according to American scientists, is the mechanism for the formation of a new ring of Saturn.
