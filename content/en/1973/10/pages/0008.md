In Evariste's soul, the death and funeral of his father left a deep imprint.

In February 1830, the eighteenth Evariste Galya was enrolled in the Preparatory School - an insignificant and pale shadow of the former Normal School, abolished by the Bourbons in 1826. It was easy for him to enter there - after all, this school was, as it were, a continuation of the face of Louis the Great. It trained candidates for the title of teacher.

At the Preparatory School, Evariste will test for the bachelor's degree in literature and mathematics. Only after the second attempt he was awarded this title, having passed the tests in literature poorly, but very well in mathematics and physics. During her studies, Galya published three more scientific works, which in January 1830 were submitted to a competition at the Academy of Sciences. Now his fate is in the hands of the permanent secretary of the Academy - Fourier. Fourier begins to read the manuscript, but soon dies. The second manuscript, like the first, disappears.

In the summer of 1830, when Galya was finishing his first year of study at the Preparatory School, the July Revolution deprived King Charles X of power. Students of the Polytechnic School, the very one Galya was so eager to get to, were fighting on the barricades in the front rows.

The preparatory school at that time was led by something called Guignot. Trying to prevent his students from taking part in the unfolding events, he made them promise not to leave the school and ordered the doors to be locked. Only two students refused to give the word required by the director - Galya and his cousin Benard.

On the night of July 28-29, Galya tries to break free and join the rebellious people. For this, Guigno subjects the offender to his home address. Galya is full of anger and contempt for Guignot, and the enthronement of Louis-Philippe along with the establishment of resources is, in his eyes, a betrayal of the ideal for which the barricade fighters were fighting.

During the holidays of 1830, Galya actively participated in the work of revolutionary circles and joined the Society of Other Nations. After the start of classes at the Normal School (the revolution returned it to its former name), the young man begins a struggle with the universal director. At the end of November, the rebellious Galya is prohibited from leaving school.
