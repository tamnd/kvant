To our readers

The subscription to the popular scientific physicist and mathematical journal «Kvant» continues for 1974.

The main content of the magazine is materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to explain various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.

The reader will find many tasks in the magazine. Among them are problems offered at entrance exams in various types, optimization and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical devices will help the reader to set up and prove a physical experiment.

The magazine publishes review articles telling about the achievements of science, about problems that are still waiting to be solved; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are distributed.

The magazine constantly publishes decisions on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

The magazine is intended primarily for students in grades 7-10, but starting in 1974, materials intended for schoolchildren in grades 5-6 will be published. It is useful for teachers, especially those who run classes or teach elective classes in physics and mathematics, as well as everyone who loves mathematics and physics.

The magazine is distributed only by subscription, which is made without restrictions.
