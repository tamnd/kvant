This was an armed force of resolution, and Louis Philippe's government tried to dissolve it as quickly as possible. After this, Galya took part in all the waves that shook Paris throughout 1831. Mathematics was abandoned.

On May 9, 1831, the Society of Friends of the People organized a banquet at the Vanges-de-Bourgogne restaurant. Among the guests in a place of honor is Alexandre Dumas. Many participants donned National Guard artillery uniforms in protest. Toasts to the resolution of 1793, to Robespierre, to the Montaglars are met with applause; toasts to the resolution of 1830 - shikab and laughter. And suddenly... a toast to Louis Philippe... Who proclaimed such a toast? Is it really the frantic republican Evarist Galya? In response, the whistles undressed, but suddenly everyone fell silent - in her hand Galya had not only a glass of wine, but also an opening knife! The cautious Dumas chose to leave the banquet. But the majority remained. The banquet participants, imitating Galya, raise their hands threateningly and repeat: “For Louis Philippe! For Louis Philippe!

The next day Galya is addressed. He is charged with "attempting to induce a purchase of the life and person of the King of the French by a statement made in a public place during a public meeting." The trial, held on June 15, acquitted Galya - the defense was able to convince the jury that the restaurant was not a public place. Galya calmly stood up, took the evidence knife from the table, closed it, put it in his pocket and left without saying a word.
