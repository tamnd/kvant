Pyramid height
M. L. Kreizman

What can be said about the height of an arbitrary pyramid? Quite a bit. Should we just repeat, essentially, the definition: the height of the pyramid passes through the top of the pyramid and is perpendicular to its base.

However, if some additional information is known about the pyramid, then the position of the height can be characterized in more detail. Thus, everyone is well aware of the fact that in a regular pyramid the height passes through the center of the regular polygon lying at the base. The experience of admissions exams shows that applicants usually clearly know the position of the height of a pyramid in which all the side edges are equal, but they experienced more difficulty if they need to determine the position of the height of a pyramid in which, for example, only adjacent side edges are equal to each other.

We will now examine which features of the pyramid provide additional information about the height of the pyramid. The statements below establish a connection between the properties of the pyramid and the position of its height. These statements should not, of course, be memorized. But it is useful to realize them and learn to “see” when solving one or another problem about pyramids.

Statement 1. If the lateral boundary of the pyramid is perpendicular to the plane of the base, then the height of the pyramid lies in the plane of this face, and the base of the height lies on this base (or on its extension), along which this face intersects with the plane of the base.

In fact, if the lateral boundary of ASB is perpendicular to the plane of the base of the pyramid (Fig. 1), then, according to the theorem about a perpendicular dropped onto one of two mutually perpendicular planes and having a common point with the other of these planes, the height SO of the pyramid belongs to the plane of the lateral face of ASB. In this case, the base O of the height of the pyramid is on the side AB of the base (or on its extension). In other words, the height of the pyramid is the height of triangle ASB.
