Maximum?

Sometimes it is interesting not only to solve an unsolved problem, but also to find a better solution to one that seemed to have already been solved. Here is one such task:

Let's take the numbers from 1 to 9 and divide them into four groups: the first group has three numbers, and the rest have two. Having fixed the order of the numbers in these groups, we get four numbers. A number variable for one of two values ​​and the remaining two two-digit numbers between each other. For example:

158 79
23 46
474 474
316 316
3634 3634

In either case, the product is equal to 3634.

So, you need to distribute the same 9 digits according to the same scheme (with equal products), but get the most possible product 584 × 12 = 96 × 73 = 7008.

This result lasted until 1971, when a new record solution to this problem was found in Japan. Apparently, it will be the last, although this has not yet been proven. Try to find what this last record product is equal to or increase it.
