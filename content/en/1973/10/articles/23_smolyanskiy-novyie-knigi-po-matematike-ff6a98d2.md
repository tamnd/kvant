⟦folio 68⟧

New books

In this issue we continue to publish English on books published in 1973 and of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the fourth quarter of 1973 (orders can be sent through Kniga-by-mail stores).

MATHEMATICS
Publishing house "Science"
1. Dorofeev G.V., Potapov M.I., Rozov N.Kh. A manual on mathematics for university applicants (selected questions of elementary mathematics). 3rd edition, revised and expanded (volume 30 sheets, circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 04 k.).
The book presents individual important sections of elementary mathematics in an accessible and popular way. Analyzing solutions to problems in university entrance exams, the authors pay a lot of attention to analyzing typical mistakes made by applicants.

The book will be useful not only to applicants, but also to high school teachers.

When preparing the 3rd edition, the authors took into account the experience of entrance exams in recent years.

2. Zaitsev V.V., Rozhkov V.V., Skanavi M.M. Elementary mathematics. 2nd edition, revised and expanded (volume 25 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 1 rub. 08 k.).
The book is a systematic presentation of elementary mathematics. The presentation of theoretical questions is accompanied by an analysis of the tasks offered at university entrance exams.

The book is intended for secondary school teachers, 10th grade students and individuals

preparing for university entrance exams.

3. Rosenfeld B. A., Sergeeva I. D. Steoreographic projection (volume 4 sheets, circulation 30,000 copies, price 15 kopecks).
The book is devoted to the presentation of the method of projecting a sphere onto a plane and the application of this type of design in structure and geography.

The last section of the book is related to the presentation of Lobachevsky geometry and, in particular, talks about the projection of the Lobachevsky plane onto a common plane.

The book is intended for secondary school teachers and schoolchildren in grades 9 and 10.

4. Rosenfeld B. A., Rozhanskaya M. M., Sokolovskaya Z. K. Abu-r-Raikhan al.-Beruki (volume 15 sheets, circulation 10,000 copies, price 95 kopecks).
The book is related to the greatest scientist of the Middle Ages - Beruki *).

The book is intended for a wide range of readers, including classic classes.

Publishing house "Mir"
5. Firemark D.S. The problem came from a painting (volume 10 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 60 kopecks).
This unique book is based on an interesting plot. Starting from the famous painting by N. L. Bordanov-Belsky “Oral Reckoning,” the author talks about the life of the artist and his painting. Turning to the mathematical problem, the conditions of which are written in the picture, the author talks about many problems related to numbers, the history of these problems and about major Russian, Soviet and foreign scientists who have made a great contribution to number theory.

Written in vivid empirical language, the book will certainly be of interest to a wide range of readers.

6. Gass S. An entertaining journey through the country of line programming (volume 12 sheets, circulation 75,000 copies, price 62 kopecks).
The book presents linear programming issues in a semi-character and clear way. The author, without delving into details and omitting complex calculations, talks very profitably about the transition to their mathematical models, how this model corresponds to reality. Talking about the difficulties that arose in this case, the author shows ways to overcome them.

In a special mathematical appendix (which can be omitted during the first reading), the author introduces the reader in more detail to the mathematical issues of linear programming.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
7. Kolyagin Yu. M., Lukakin G. L. Basic concepts in the school mathematics course (volume 20 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 85 kopecks).
The book outlines the basic concepts of mathematics (set theory, elements of mathematical logic, exciomatika, equalities, equations, geometric transformations of vectors, and so on) in relation to the school mathematics course.

The book will be useful for secondary mathematics teachers
