⟦folio 61⟧

The Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology trains research physicists in the latest areas of physics and technology.

The peculiarity of the educational system adopted at the Physico-Technical Institute is that here fundamental higher education is combined with special training in the so-called basic research institutes and design bureaus, where special departments of the institute have been created.

In the first year, the student is given general training.

Mathematics, general and theoretical physics, and social sciences are studied in university courses so that future specialists have a good command of the knowledge that forms the basis of the education of research physicists. Bystrow is familiar with the increasing flow of scientific and technical information, which contributes to the in-depth study of foreign languages.

Training in a specific specialization and independent research work at the base institute begins in the second or third year.

At the base institute, the student serves lectures on specialization, participates in scientific seminars, and is deeply involved in the research work of the laboratory instituta. The future specialist deals with the latest instruments; participates in solving not model problems invented specifically for his practice, but actual problems facing the laboratory. The thesis work of each MIPT student is, as a rule, included in the thematic plan of the base institute. In the creative field of the scientific team, the student undergoes an invaluable educational school. Working under the direct supervision of a prominent scientist largely determines the success of his future independent activities.

Among the basic institutes of MIPT is the leading scientific center of Moscow (see «Kvant» No. 6, 1972).

The Institute trains specialists in seven faculties: Faculty of Radio Engineering and Cybernetics, Faculty of General and Applied Physics, Faculty of Aerophysics and Space Research, Faculty of Molecular and Chemical Physics, Faculty of Physical and Quantum Electronics, Faculty of Aeromechanics and LET Engineering, Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics.

At each of the faculties, training is carried out in a wide range of specializations, covering most areas of modern physics, technology, and mathematics.
