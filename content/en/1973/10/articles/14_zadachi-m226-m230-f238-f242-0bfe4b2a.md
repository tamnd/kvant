Tasks

M226—M230; F238—F242

M226. At the three vertices of the square there are three grasshoppers. They play leapfrog. Moreover, if grasshopper A yokes through grasshopper B, then after the yoke he finds himself at the same distance from B (but, naturally, on the other side and on the same straight line; Fig. 1). Is it possible, after several buckles, for one of the smiths to hit the fourth vertex of the original square?

Yu. I. Yuonin

Fig. 1.

M227. A point is taken on each side of the parallelgram. The area of ​​a quadrilateral with vertices at these points is equal to half the area of ​​the parallelgram. Prove that at least one of the diagonals of the quadrilateral is parallel to one of the sides of the parallelgram.

E. V. Sallinen

M228. A sheet of checkered paper measuring $n \times n$ cells was colored in $n$ colors (each cell was covered in one of these colors or not covered at all). A correct coloring is one in which each row and column does not contain two cells of the same color. Is it always possible to “repaint” the entire sheet in the correct way if they were originally covered correctly?

a) $n^2$ – 1 cell;
b) $n^2$ – 2 cells;
b) $n$ cells?

D. Logachev

b) if $\frac{u}{v} < \frac{1}{3}$, then the gangster can let him do it.

A. Belkin

M230. Prove that from any convex equilateral (but not necessarily regular) pentagon one can cut a regular triangle, one of the sides of which coincides with the side of the pentagon (Fig. 2).

S. Konyashin.

F238. There are two square wires made of the same material. The cross-sectional side of one wire is 1 mm, and the other is 4 mm. In order to melt the first wire, a current of 10 A must be passed through it. How much current must be passed through the second wire to melt it?

Consider that the amount of heat lost to the environment in 1 second is proportional to the difference

F239. A wheel of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal plane. The speed of the wheel center is constant.

As is known, the trajectory of a curved linear motion of a point over a short period of time can always be considered an arc of a circle. Determine the radius of the circles along which the wheel points $A$ and $B$ move at the moment of time when the radius vector of the point $A$ is horizontal, and the radius vector of the point $B$ makes an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical (Fig. 3).

F240. The probe ball, which has a non-stretchable shell, has risen to its maximum height and makes small oscillations around the equilibrium level. Find the period of these oscillations, assuming that at such a height the air density $\rho$ decreases with height evenly by values ​​$\delta = 1,2 \cdot 10^{-2}$ $\rho$ every $h = 100$ m.

M. V. Kazarnovsky

F241. A mass wheel $m$ can slide along a rod of length $L$. The friction force between them is $F$. Determine what minimum speed $v_0$ must be imparted to the rod in order for it to fly through the ring if the ring first collapses.

The experiment is carried out in zero gravity.

F242. A camera obscura is a rectangular box with a round hole in one of the walls. The illumination of the image of the Sun, which is obtained on the opposite wall of the camera - the screen, drops by half as it moves away from the center of the image to the edge by 0.9 of the image radius. How many times is the illumination on the front wall of the camera greater than the illumination in the center of the image?

G. L. Komkin
