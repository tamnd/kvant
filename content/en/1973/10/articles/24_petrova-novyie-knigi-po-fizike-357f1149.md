PHYSICS

Publishing house "Science"
1. With cold and low Ya. A., Shvartsburg A. B. Laws of mechanics (volume 15 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 62 kopecks).
The book, based only on the material of the school physics course, breaks down in detail the problems of the movement of materials of a point on a plane, vibrations, the movement of liquid and gas, and the laws of celestial mechanics.
The book is intended for high school students, but it will also be useful for physics teachers teaching elective classes.
2. Physics. Part 11. Optics and waves. Edited by A. S. Akhmatov, 2nd edition (volume 20 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 ruble 29 kopecks).
This book is a translation of an American high school textbook on general physics. The full course consists of four parts: “The Universe”, “Optics and Waves”, “Mechanics”, “Electricity and Structure.atoma”.
The published second part of the course “Optics and Waves” is a useful addition to existing physics textbooks.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers related to existing physics course textbooks.
3. What physicists were thinking about. Issue 10. Atomic, core (volume 12 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 60 kopecks).
A collection of popular science articles translated from the American magazine “Scientific American”, it is dedicated to the achievements of atomic nucleus physics and their applications.
The stats were written by major physicists. Both high school teachers and inquisitive high school students will read them with interest.

Publishing house "Atomizdat"
4. Gladkov K. A. Atom from A to Z. 2nd edition, revised and expanded (volume 12 sheets, circulation 150,000 copies, price 60 kopecks).
This is a small encyclopedia of nuclear physics, giving a simple but fairly complete idea of ​​nuclear physics.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
5. Balash V. A. Problems in physics and methods for solving them. 3rd edition, revised and expanded (volume 25 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 85 kopecks).
This book, intended primarily for people preparing for competitive exams at the institute, contains problems of increased difficulty throughout the physics course. These tasks are presented in the entrance exams for a number of Moscow universities (MSU, MEPhI, MIPT, and so on). All problems are given with detailed solutions. Brief theoretical introductions before each section help you remember the laws underlying the solution to the problem.
The book is useful for schoolchildren in grades 9-10.
6. Katz Ts. B. Biophysics in physics lessons (volume 10 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 40 kopecks).
The book outlines the elements of biophysics and bionics.
It is of interest to high school physics teachers and high school students.
7. Prokofiev O. N. Surprising nearby (volume 6 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 20 kopecks).
This book discusses the new science of bionics.
The book is of interest for grades 8-10.

SCIENCE FICTION
Publishing house "Young Guard"
1. Altov G. Tremya thinking (volume 12 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 62 kopecks).
A science fiction novel telling about long-distance space travel of people, about meetings with the inhabitants and contacts with them.
The novel is based on an adventure plot.
2. Zhemai tiss O. Baer star (volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 50 kopecks).
A science fiction tale about the first Soviet expedition to Mars. The astronauts reveal many secrets of the tanistic planet and, most importantly, find traces of a great civilization that existed hundreds of thousands of years ago.
3. Zhurv Levva V. Golden horses of woodworking (volume 15 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 70 kopecks).
A book of science fiction stories about the life and discovery of psychology by Kirill Safray. Science is approaching great discoveries in psychology and is approaching deciphering the secrets of creativity. To show a person who will do for psychology what Einstein and Bordle did for physicists, to show the path to discovery, drama and ideas - this is the goal of the author of this collection. This is the originality, appeal and meaning of the book.

T. S. Petrova
