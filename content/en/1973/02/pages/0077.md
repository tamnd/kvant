1. The difficulty of the task lies in the fact that it is necessary to find not the unknown quantities themselves, but their ratio.

Let the automatic speeds be $v_1$, $v_2$ and $v_3$, respectively. Then
$$\frac{2}{3} v_1 = 2v_1 - v_3,$$
$$\frac{1}{2} v_2 = 2v_2 - v_3.$$

We have a system of two equations with three unknowns.
Subtract the second from the first equation:
$$\frac{2}{3} v_1 - \frac{1}{2} v_2 = 2v_1 - 2v_2,$$
and divide it by $v_2$:
$$\frac{2}{3} \left( \frac{v_1}{v_2} \right) - \frac{1}{2} = 2 \left( \frac{v_1}{v_2} \right) - 2,$$
$$\frac{4}{3} \left( \frac{v_1}{v_2} \right) = \frac{3}{2}, \quad \frac{v_1}{v_2} = \frac{9}{8}.$$
