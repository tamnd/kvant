The blind spot in the system of knowledge of the ancient naturalists was that they did not have the slightest idea about diamnik. The reason for the movement of the bodies was inaccessible to them. When it came to the planets, there could be no talk of any reasons for the movement - the planets moved on their own, simply because movement is in their nature. Almost the entire science of motion is described by Copernicus. Thus: “Aristotle says that a single and simple body also has simple motion; Of the simple movements, one is rectilinear, the other is circular; Of the straight ones, one goes up, the other goes down. Therefore, every simple movement goes either towards the middle downwards, or from the middle upwards, or around the middle, and this movement is circular. Only earth and water, which are considered heavy, should move downwards, that is, strive towards the middle; air and fire, which are light, must move upward and move away from the middle. And it seems quite reasonable to attribute rectilinear motion to these four elements, and to imagine the celestial bodies rotating around the middle. Aristotle says so."
