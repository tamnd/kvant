⟦folio 65⟧

Different faculties have different requirements for mathematics, so options for the written exam were offered of unequal difficulty. To this end, all problems and options as a whole were rated in “points” for difficulty. The tasks were divided into three groups: easy (1, 2, 3 points), medium (4, 5, 6 points) and difficult (7, 8, 9 points).

Options with a score above 25 were offered to the Faculty of Economic Cybernetics, Trade of Industrial Goods, Industrial Economics and Trade and Economics.

Faculty of General Economics (24)
1. From point A, two automatic miles leave simultaneously along the same highway, and an hour later a third follows. Another hour later, the distance between the third and first automatic miles decreased by 1.5 times, and between the third and second - by half.
How many times is the speed of the first car greater than the speed of the second, if it is known that the third did not overtake the first and second?
2. A ball is inscribed in a regular quadrangular primid. The distance from the center of the ball to the top of the primid is equal to a, and the angle of inclination of the side face of the primid to the plane of the base is equal to α.
Determine the complete surface of the primid.
3. Solve the equation
$$\cos 7x + \sin^2 2x = \cos^2 2x - \cos x.$$
4. Solve equation $$\lg^2 1 00x - \lg^2 10x + \lg^2 x = 6.$$

Faculty of Economic Cybernetics (27)
1. The first nester left point A, and the second one left point B at the same time. After the meeting, both invested in point B, and the second one arrived at B 2 hours earlier than the first.
Find the speeds of the nested ones if it is known that the distance AB is equal to 100 km and that the second nested one covers the entire distance AB 3 hours 20 minutes faster than the first.
2. The diagonals of a straight quadrangular addition make angles α and β with the base. The height of the pier is equal to H. Determine the volume of the pier if the diagonals of the base intersect at an angle γ.
3. Solve the equation
$$\left( x + 1 \right) \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2x-2} + 11 \left( x + 1 \right) \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^x - 3x - 3 = 0.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$8 \cos^2 x + 2 \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 x} = 3,$$
if x is enclosed between $\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\frac{3}{2}$ π.

Faculty of Finance (25)
1. Determine the sides of a triangle if the median and height drawn from one angle divide this angle into three equal parts, and the median is 10 cm.
2. Solve the equation
$$\lg^2 2x + \lg^2 3x = \lg^2 2 + \lg^2 3.$$
3. Two authors embroidered simultaneously towards each other, the first from city A, and the second from city B, the distance between them is 280 km, and after 4 hours they met at point D. After the meeting, the first automile arrived in city B $2^{1/3}$ hours later than the second one arrived in city A. Determine the average speed of each automile.
4. Solve the equation
$$\sin x \cdot \sin 2x \cdot \sin 3x = \frac{1}{4} \sin 4x.$$

$$\frac{1}{2} \log \frac{1}{3} \left( x^2 - 9x + 16 \right)^2 > \log \frac{1}{3}.$$
