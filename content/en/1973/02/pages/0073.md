⟦folio 71⟧

Assignment from the options for the entrance exam in mathematics at the EMS

9th grade

1. More than a hundred identical boxes with blanks were stored in the factory warehouse. Due to the consumption of hoarding goods, some time has decreased. When there were only seven blanks left in each box, another 14 full boxes were brought to the warehouse, after which the total number of blanks was three less than before consumption. How many boxes of blanks were stored in the initial warehouse?

2. In a square, on the side $a$, the middle of two adjacent sides connects to each other and to the opposite vertex of the square. Calculate the area of ​​the resulting triangle.

3. Find $k$ and $l$ if they are roots of the equation $x^2 + kx + l = 0$.

4. Thirty students from five years came up with forty problems for the Olympiad, with classmates coming up with the same number of problems, and students from different courses coming up with a different number. How many students came up with one problem?

5. In the morning, six cans of milk were brought to the store, containing 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 31 liters. Before the lunch break, milk from three cans was completely sold, and by the closing of the store, milk from two more cans was sold entirely. It turned out that twice as much milk was sold in the morning as in the afternoon. Determine from which cans milk was sold before the lunch break.

6. The height of an acute triangle is 25 cm. At what distance from the vertex should one check a line perpendicular to this height in order to divide the area of ​​the triangle in half?

7. Define $a$ so that from the roots of the equation

$$x^2 - \frac{15}{4}x + a = 0$$

was the square of another.

8. Seven mushroom pickers collected 100 mushrooms together, and all seven collected a different number of mushrooms. Prove that there are three mushroom pickers who have collected at least 50 mushrooms together.

9. Five integers are written on the board. Adding them in pairs, we got the following ten numbers: 0, 2, 4, 4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 15. What five numbers were written on the board?

10. On the basis of an equilateral triangle, a circle is constructed as a chord, tangent to the equal sides of the triangle. Find the radius of the circle if the base of the triangle is $a$ and the height is $b$.

11. Define $a$ so that the equation

$$x^2 + x + a = 0$$

had a common root.

10th grade

1. There are $n$ cities. Each city is connected to every other avaline, and not all lines allow movement in both directions. Prove that it is possible to choose such a route to travel through all cities.

2. Muggli asked the Sebeyans to bring him nuts. On the way back, the monkeys passed and each threw one nut at each. They brought 26 nuts. How many monkeys were there and how many of them picked nuts (all the monkeys picked nuts equally)?

3. The boy bought black pencils for 3 kopecks, red pencils for 5 kopecks, blue pencils for 7 kopecks. - only 8 pencils for 42 kopecks. It is known that the number of red pencils minus the number of black pencils is a complete square. How many pencils of each type were purchased?

4. Solve for the number $3^x + 4^x = 5^x$.

5. Three fishermen caught fish and went to bed. The first one woke up, saw that the number of fish was not divisible by three, threw one out, took a third of the remaining ones and left. The second one woke up, saw that the number of fish was not divisible by three, threw one out, took a third of the remaining ones and left. The third fisherman did the same, not knowing that his friend had left. Determine the minimum number of fish that they could understand.

6. Given a number that is prime to 30. Prove that the remainder when divided by 30 is a prime number.

7. The zoo is home to elephants, lions and rhinos, 30 animals in total. There are three types of cells for them, 7 in total. Each cage contains 2 rhinos, or 4 lions, or 7 elephants. How many cells of each type?

8. Given a convex quadrilateral with perimeter $P$. Its sides are divided by one outward, then continues until they intersect. Prove that the area of ​​the new quadrilateral is greater than $P + 3$.

9. How many numbers are there from 1 to 1000 that are divisible by either 5 or 7 or both?

10. In a right triangle with integer sides, the length of the hypotenuse is divisible by 3. Prove that the length of each category is divisible by 3.

11. A five-digit number is given. If we add a unit in front of it, we get a six-digit number, which is second less than the six-digit number obtained by adding a unit at the end. Find this number.

12. Given a circle and a point $A$ outside it. From point $A$ secants $AB$ and $AC$ are drawn, intersecting the circle at points $M$ and $K$ of the corresponding one. Prove that triangles $AMK$ and $ABC$ are similar.
