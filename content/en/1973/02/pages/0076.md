⟦folio 74⟧

New books

Starting with this issue, we will provide short summaries of books published in 1973 that are of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the first quarter of 1973 (orders can be sent through Book by Mail stores).

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Gel fand I.M., Glagoleva E.G., Kirillov A.A., Metsd coordinates. (Volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 13 kopecks.)
The book is devoted to the presentation of one of the basic concepts of mathematics - the coordinate method. Using various, extremely well-chosen examples, the authors show how this concept “works” in mathematics.

The book is intended for schoolchildren in grades 8–10 who are interested in mathematics.
2. Gradshtein I.S., Direct and inverse theorem. (Volume 7 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 23 kopecks.)
The book examines individual issues of mathematical logic related to the school mathematics course (necessary and sufficient conditions, positive and converse theorems, proof by contradiction, etc.).
By analyzing a number of specific examples, the author shows how rather subtle concepts of mathematical logic and set theory can be used in the study of school mathematics.

3. Wachman F., Schmidt E., retired workers. (Volume 10 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 74 kopecks.)
The book is popular, based on materials from elementary mathematics, and sets out a theory that often establishes a completely unexpected connection between geometry and modern algebra.
The large number of problems included in the book greatly helps in mastering the material presented.
The book is accessible and of great interest to a wide range of readers, including schoolchildren in grades 8-10.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"
4. What was the physicist thinking about? Issue 9. (Volume 14 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 70 kopecks)
This collection of translated articles is devoted to issues of elementary particle physics. The collection is composed of popular science articles, the authors of which are prominent scientists working creatively in the field of theoretical and experimental physics.
The collection is intended for a wide range of readers, including high school students.
5. Grebennikov E. A., Nikolai Konernik. (Volume 6 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 22 kopecks.)
The book presents in a popular form the events, facts and main ideas associated with the emergence of Konernik's theory.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
6. Shklovsky I. S., Universe, copy.

intelligence. (Volume 22 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 ruble 20 kopecks)
The book deals with a fascinating problem - the existence of introduced civilizations. It also presents the main issues of modern astronomy in a comprehensive and popular manner.
The book is intended for high school students.
7. Gurevich V.Z., Energy of invisible light. (Volume 8 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.)
This book is about infrared rays. About how they help control rockets and satellites, make a map of the planet, how they can be used to decipher the composition of an unknown substance, and many other examples of using these rays.
The book is intended for schoolchildren and readers.
8. Ostrovsky Yu. I., Holodraffy and its application. (Volume 8 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 60 kopecks.)
Holodraffy is a new rapidly developing method for recording and restoring light and acoustic rad waves. The book outlines the basics of the theory and experimental techniques of using the voice.
The book is intended for schoolchildren and readers.

Publishing house "Atomizat"
9. Kedrov V., Irene and Frederic Jollu-Curie. (Volume 9 l., circulation 90,000 copies, price 29 kopecks.)
The book, in a lively and popular form, introduces the life and scientific work of the remarkable French scientists Irène and Frédéric Jollus-Curie.
The book is intended for the most schoolchildren of readers.
