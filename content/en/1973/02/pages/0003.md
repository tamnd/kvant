Collector's Corner (3rd cover page)
A. V. Altykis. Stamps linking Copernicus
Blend (pages 21, 38, 44, 66, 73)

The portrait of Nicolaus Copernicus, which we show on the first page of the cover, was painted by an unknown artist in 1575.

On the second page of the cover is the geometry room of the University of Krakow, where Copernicus has been serving since 1491. Since there were not enough textbooks, the main theorems were drawn on the walls.

On page four you see another portrait of Copernicus. It is believed that this is a self-portrait of a scientist.
