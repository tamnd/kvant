Moscow Institute of National Economy named after G. V. Plexanov

MINCH them. G. V. Plexanova is one of the largest universities in the country. More than 14 thousand students study at its 10 academic and one correspondence faculties, and 500 graduate students work in the departments.

The institute has the following faculties (with day and evening classes): general economics, economic cybernetics, industrial economics, economics and logistics organization, financial, trade and economics, commodity management of industrial goods, technical and mechanical. There is also a correspondence technical faculty. The duration of study in full-time departments is 4 years, and in evening departments - 5 years (except for the faculties of economic cybernetics and mechanics, where the duration of study is 5 and 6 years).

Nanoscale mathematical training is provided at the Faculty of Economic Cybernetics. Students, along with general economic disciplines, study higher mathematics and programming on electronic computers, as well as a cycle of new economic and mathematical disciplines. Students learn the use of computers in economic research and planning in special laboratories, founded by appropriate machines for preparing and processing information and the Ural 11-B universal electronic computer. Department staff and students are accepted to participate in scientific research in the content of

Main Computer Center of the USSR State Planning Committee and other organizations.

FEC graduates are provided with work in the State Planning Committee and the Central Statistical Office of the USSR and the RSFSR, in research institutes, computer centers, economics and mathematical laboratories and in planning organizations of ministries.

Students of economic specialties also receive greater mathematical training. They study higher mathematics, programming, statistics courses and special economic disciplines. These faculties train economists of a wide profile, mainly for work in the planning system of economic organizations, scientific research institutes, and large enterprises.

In their multifaceted activities, the departments of the Ministry of Ecology and Chemistry, together with the State Planning Committee of the USSR and the RSFSR and other government organizations, help fraternal socialist countries in training and advanced training of national personnel, share experience and provide assistance to related departments of multi-chemical types of the country. Original textbooks and teaching aids have been published.

Students who participate in the development of economic topics and other research successfully participate in republican competitions of student scientific works are widely involved in conducting scientific research.
