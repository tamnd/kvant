⟦folio 68⟧

«Kvant» for younger students

TASKS

1. The mouse has 20 steps to the hole. A cat has 5 jumps to a mouse. While the cat makes one jump, the mouse takes 3 steps, and one cat jump is equal in length to 10 mouse steps.

Will the cat catch the mouse?

2. How can you change the height of a telephone pole with only a small ruler to guide you? (You cannot climb on the pole).

3. Find the smallest natural numbers $a$, $b$ ($b > 1$) satisfying the equality

$$\sqrt{a \sqrt{a \sqrt{a}}} = b.$$

4. The channel is an iron installed on a pile. Does the pressure on the piles change when a barge is pulled along the canal?

5. 10 points were placed at equal intervals on the straight line; they occupied a segment of length $l$. On another line, 100 points were placed at the same intervals; they occupied a segment of length $L$. How many times is $L$ greater than $l$?

6. A car is moving along a highway at a speed of 100 km/h. At the same time, the distance between cars following each other is about 15 m. Is it possible to demand that in a narrower area “to ensure safety” the speed of cars is reduced to 15 km/h?
