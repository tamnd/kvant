⟦folio 54⟧

"Alpha" is a mathematics magazine for schoolchildren, which has been published in the German Democratic Republic for the seventh year.
The magazine is published 6 times a year. In it, as in «Kvant», it talks about the problems that mathematics poses and solves, publishes intriguing problems, curiosities, reports about mathematical Olympids in the GDR and in some other countries, about international mathematical Olympids.
Most of the materials are accessible even to schoolchildren in grades 5-6.
— For a more in-depth study of school materials, “Alpha” prints a number of problems from issue to issue. Schoolchildren who solve at least seven problems out of sixteen assigned for the corresponding class during the school year receive a diploma and a badge, says the editor-in-chief of the magazine, honored resource teacher Johannes Lehmann. “And those who win diplomas three years in a row are awarded a gold badge.”
The magazine is subscribed to and read by schoolchildren not only in the GDR. This magazine is read in Poland and Austria, in Bulgaria and in the Soviet Union, and not only read, but also actively participate in its work. Thus, ninth-grader Cornelia Tanggausser from Linz (Austria) wrote in Alpha that a dispute broke out at their school about various concepts of set theory, and magazine No. 5 for 1972 published a detailed explanation of the scientist.
And Vitya Khasansky from Dzerzhinsk, Gorky region, not only regularly reads the magazine, but also composes tasks himself; Several of his problems were published in No. 1, 1973.
The magazine can be issued at any post office in the Soviet Union. Its index is 31059, subscription price is 60 kopecks. per year.
In this issue «Kvant» we publish articles and tasks kindly provided to us by the editors of the Alpha magazine.
