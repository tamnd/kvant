⟦folio 70⟧

Quests

1. Given two consecutive natural numbers $a$ and $b$, as well as their product $c$. Prove that the number $x = a^2 + b^2 + c^2$ is always the square of an odd number.

2. The electrician, installer and engineer, whose last names were Baumann, Eichler and Haan, were on a flight from Prague to Cairo. From the conversation they actually had, they found out that:
a) Baumann and the engineer were going to work on construction;
b) Electrician and Haan live permanently in Berlin;
b) Eichler is younger than the installer;
d) Haan is older than the engineer.
Give the names of the engineer and electrician (the answer must be justified).

3. The train passes through a place 171 m long in 27 s (counting from the moment the locomotive leaves the place until the last car leaves), and the mismo of a pedestrian walking towards the train at a speed of 1 m/s takes 9 s.
Find the speed of the train and its length.

4. The boat travels the path $AB$ upstream in 4 and 30 minutes, and the path $BA$ (downstream) in 3 hours.
How long will it take the raft to float from $B$ to $A$?

5. During the layover between two voyages, the sailor turned 20 years old. On this occasion, all 6 team members gather in the wardroom.
“I’m twice as old as Yugi and 6 years older than the driver,” said the helmsman.
“And I’m as much older than Yuga as I am younger than the driver,” the bossman remarked. - Besides, I am 4 years older than the sailor.
“The average age of the crew is 28 years,” the captain gave information.
How old is the captain?
