⟦folio 18⟧

I find mathematics the most sublime occupation for the mind, but at the same time I know that it is very useful, that I make little difference between a person who is only a geometer and an art artisan. That's why I called it the most beautiful craft in the world, but in the end it is just a craft. And I have often said that it is good for testing one’s strength, but not for applying this strength...” And finally, lines that speak about Pascal’s physical condition: “I am so weak that I cannot walk without a cane or ride a horse. I can’t even ride in a carriage for more than two or three years...” In December 1660, Huygens visited Pascal and found him a very old man (Pascal was 37 years old) who was unable to carry on a conversation.

Pascal devoted the last years of his life to the “study of man.” He never managed to complete his main book. The remaining materials were published set by set in different versions under different titles. Most often this book is simply called "Mice".

Blaise Pascal died on August 19, 1662.

Literature

Klyaus E. M., Pogrebysky I. B., Frankfurt U. I. Pascal. M., “Science”, 1971.

Looking through the crack

In this task you have to explain the result of an experiment that you yourself must perform. figure 1 shows a grid of vertical and horizontal lines. Take a piece of cardboard, run a razor blade along it and look at figure with one eye through a thin slit in the cardboard. Hold the slit next to the eye and place it horizontally. The distance from the eye to figure should be 30-40 cm. You will find that only vertical lines have been preserved in the lattice, and the horizontal ones have disappeared. Where did they go?

Fig. 1.

Did your experiment fail? Can you see the whole grille? Or, on the contrary, don’t you see anything? In the first case, your gap is too wide, in the second, it is too narrow. You need to tinker a little: try bending the cardboard slightly so that the width of the gap changes (optimum is about 1-10 microns). The experiment works better if the grid is well illuminated, and the sides of the cardboard facing the eye are not illuminated at all.

Well, you've finally done it. A somewhat strange maturation, isn't it?
