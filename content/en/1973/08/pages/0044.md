§ 5. What did the conductor say?

Or thought that I thought, What he thought: I’m dreaming!

S. Marshak. From Coventry Palmore.

Let the wise men themselves - without a guide - know that there is no water in the train, and that after the tunnels there will be long stops where you can wash. Besides, let more than one person get dirty in the dirty tunnel. Then, in the conductor’s announcement, there seems to be nothing new at all for any of the sages!

So what - if the conductor had not come at all - would n dirty people go to wash at the nth stop?

The temptation is great to answer “Yes”: since nothing has changed in the conditions of the problem, then, it would seem, the answer should not change either! And yet, common sense dictates that without the conductor’s announcement, you won’t be able to wash yourself, sorry! And then - what does it mean: “nothing has changed in the conditions”? Still, in one version the conductor did not come at all, but in the other, he came and said something!!!

What did he finally say?

This issue, apparently, cannot be resolved with exclamations alone. Therefore, let us calmly and scrupulously analyze the simplest possible case: n = 2 (we cannot put n = 1, since it is given that more than one person got dirty).

Case n = 2. The faces of the sages M₁ and M₂ are dirty. They see each other, and therefore each of them actually knows that someone has gotten dirty.

Putting ourselves in the place of M₂, let’s repeat the reasoning of § 4.

“There are 2 possible cases:
1) I have a clean face;
2) my face is dirty.

In the 1st case, M₁ sees only clean faces, but we know that someone has gotten dirty, but M₂ is openly aware of this? How, exactly, does M₂ know that M₁ knows that someone has gotten dirty?

... Finally, we seem to have found the key to the solution.

If the conductor passed by, then his announcement was indeed not news to M₁ and M₂. But M₂ saw that M₁ served this advertisement. That's why, if the conductor came, then M₂ knows that M₁ knows that someone got dirty.

If the conductor did not come, then there is no sign of M₂ about this. In fact, we know that M₂ has a dirty face, and therefore we know that M₁ knows that someone (maybe M₂, or maybe he too) has gotten dirty, but M₂ himself admits the possibility that he has a clean face, and that M₁ sees, therefore, only clean faces. But if M₁ does not see dirty faces and if the guide did not come, then M₁ has no way to know that someone has gotten dirty. This means that if the conductor did not come, then M₂ has no way of knowing that M₁ knows that someone got dirty.
