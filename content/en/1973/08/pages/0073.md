⟦folio 71⟧

L. Flade
Little words with big meaning

After receiving the graded math paper, Klaus was very surprised:
“How nagging our teacher is,” he said to his friends. “I wrote everything correctly, except for a few small words, but still I didn’t get a decent grade for the first four assignments!”
These are the tasks:
1. Under what conditions did division arise in the field of natural numbers?
2. How much power does one natural number contribute to?
3. Is the number 374111 divisible by 3? Justify your answer.
4. a) Is it true that all natural numbers have one preceding number?
6) If this statement is incorrect, explain why.
Klaus wrote:
1. Division a: b in the field of natural numbers occurs if the dividend a is a multiple of the divisor b OR if b is not zero *).
2. Each natural number corresponds to a power on the number axis.
3. The number 374111 is divisible by 3. Rationale: if it is divisible by 3, then the sum of the digits of this number is divisible by 3.

L. Flade «Kleine Worte—Große Wirkung».
“Alpha” No. 5, 6, 1972. Translation and processing by A. Ya. Halamizer.
*) Zero is also considered a natural number. (Approx. Transl.)
