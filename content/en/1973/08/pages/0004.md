On May 27, 1973, a member of the editorial collection of the magazine «Kvant», one of the largest Soviet scientists, an outstanding organizer of science and social activities, Chairman of the Supreme Council of the RSFSR, Vice-President of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Deputy Director of the Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova, Deputy Chairman of the Council for Coordinating the Activities of the Academies of Sciences of the Union Republics, Editor-in-Chief of the journal “Bulletin of the USSR Academy of Sciences”, Chairman of the Scientific Council on the complex problem “Methods of direct conversion of thermal energy into electrical energy”, Hero of Socialist Labor, laureate of the Lenin and State Prizes, Academician Mikhail Dmitrievich Millionshchikov.
