⟦folio 19⟧

Fluid Equilibrium Tract

We place here the first chapter from Pascal's treatise, in which he formed his landmark law on the conditions of fluid equilibrium, substantiated the principle of operation of hydraulic machines and unleashed the so-called hydrostatic paradox (all these problems are discussed in the physics course for the sixth grade). The full title of this work is as follows: “Treats on the equilibrium of liquids and all masses of air, the content of the explanation of the principles of various natural phenomena that have not yet been well known and, in particular, those that are attributed to the richness of the void.” It was published in 1663, that is, a year after the death of the author. Pascal wrote it in 1651-1653.

In Russian, this treatise, translated by A. N. Dolgov, was published in the book “Principles of Hydrostatics; Archimedes, Stavin, Galileo, Pascal,” published in 1933 by the State Technical Publishing House. The figure given in the article are taken from the treatise. The publication was prepared by V. A. Leshkovtsev.
