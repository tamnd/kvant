IF A, TO B and IF B, TO A

When Klaus argued in his work that the number 374111 is divisible by 3, he wrote:

“If a number is divisible by 3, then the sum of its digits is also divisible by 3.

Hans wrote the opposite:

“Since the sum of the digits of a number is divisible by 3, then the sum is also divisible by 3,” and received the highest mark.

Klaus believed that both statements expressed the same meaning. Is this true?

First of all, we note that the above expressions are again combinations of statements formed using the expression “IF A, TO B”. This relationship of statements is also called implication.
