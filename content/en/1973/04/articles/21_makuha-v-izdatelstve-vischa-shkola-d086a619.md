In the publishing house "Vishcha School"
Since 1972, the Ukrainian resource publishing house “Vishcha School” has published a new series “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School”.
This series is intended primarily for students of physics and mathematics schools. But the books in this series will also be understandable to high school students.
Books in the series can be ordered at the resource store “Book by mail” - 252117, Kyiv - 117, st. Popudrenko, 30, as well as in the publishing house “Vishcha Shkola” - 252054, Kyiv - 54, st. Gogolevskaya, 7.
In 1972, the following books were published (in Ukrainian):
Vyshensky V. A. “Attitude and function.”

Using a large number of well-chosen examples, the author similarly examines such basic concepts of modern mathematics as relation, graph, relation, inverse relation, function.
Dorogovtsev A. Ya., Yadrenko M. I. “Coordinate method.”
The book talks about the coordinate method, its applications to solving geometric and theoretical problems, the properties of second-order curves, and linear programming problems.
Ezhov I. I., Skorodod A. V., Yadrenko M. I. “Elements of combinatorics.”
Based on theoretical principles, the authors present the basic concepts and methods of combinatorics in a lively and popular manner. A large number of problems given in the book help you master the material.
Kovantsov N. I. “Geometric transformations.”
The book examines geometric transformation both in Euclidean space and in some non-Euclidean spaces.
Ryzhov Yu. M. “Limits”.
The book outlines the basic concepts of the theory of limits of numerical sequences, functions and non-ctoral important applications of the theory of limits.
Korsak K.V. “Electromatics”.
The author examines such functional physical concepts as “charge”, “field”, “capacity” and so on, as well as the phenomena of the electrical network and their application in technology. The book is written in a lively and understandable language.
A. Makukha.
