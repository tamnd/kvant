⟦folio 64⟧

The University of Other Nations is classified as a first category higher education institution. Both foreign and Soviet citizens study there.

The university is a member of the International Association of Universities. He collaborates with many Soviet and foreign higher educational institutions, takes part in international meetings and conferences on issues of science and education.

The University has the following faculties:

— engineer with the specialty: “mechanical engineering”, “power engineering”, “construction”, “geology and exploration of mineral deposits”, “development of mineral deposits”;

— physical, mathematical and natural sciences with the specialty: “mathematics”, “physics”, “chemistry”;

— medical with a specialty in general medicine;

— agricultural with a specialty in agronomy;

— economics and law with the specialty: “economics and national economic planning” and “international law”;

— historical and philological with a specialty: “history” and “Russian language and literature.”

Applicants will take entrance exams in the following subjects:

in mathematics - those who have chosen the specialties “mechanical engineering”, “power engineering”, “construction”, “geology and exploration of mineral deposits”, “development of mineral deposits”, “mathematics”, “physics”, “chemistry”;

in history, geography, literature - those who have chosen the specialties “history”, “Russian language and literature”, “economics and national economic planning”, “international law”. Those who have chosen the specialty “Economics and National Economy Planning” will take an exam in mathematics along with the literature exam.

Below we present typical options for the 1972 mathematics exam.

1. Faculty of Physics, Mathematics and Natural Sciences

1. Three teams compete. The first and second teams produced twice as many parts as the third, and the first and third - three times as many as the second. Who won the competition?

2. Solve the equation
$$\sin^3 x + \cos^3 x = \sin 2x + \sin x + \cos x.$$

3. At the base of the base SABCD there is an equilateral trapezoid ABCD.

$$AB = CD = 1/2, \quad AD = \sqrt{2},$$
$$BC = \sqrt{2}/2, \quad SA = \sqrt{2}.$$

Determine the height of the pier if it is known that the faces SAB and SCD are perpendicular to the base.

4. Solve the equation
$$2^{2x} + 3^{2x} - 2 \cdot 6^x + \sqrt{\log_3(x+3)} = 0.$$

2. Faculty of Engineering

1. Three workers, working together, complete the job in 6 hours. The first and second workers would complete this work in 9 hours, and the first and third workers together would do it in 12 hours. How many times is the productivity of the second worker greater than the productivity of the third?

2. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + 2^x - 2y = 1, \\ \log(2^x - y) (x + y + 1) = 1. \end{cases}$$

3. At the end there is a wissan ball. The radius of the circle along which the lateral surface of the cone touches the surface of the ball is equal to r. The radius of the circle and the radius of the ball are drawn to a point on this circle. The angle between these radii is 30°. Find the volume of the cone.

4. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{1 - \text{ctg} 3x}{1 - \text{tg} 3x} = 4 \cos^2 \frac{3x}{2} - 2.$$

3. Faculty of Economics and Law

1. Two workers do some work. The second of them was present at work 1 hour later than the first. 3 hours after the first one started work, they still had 45% of the work left to do. At the end of the work, it turned out that everyone had completed half of all the work. How long will it take each of them separately to complete all the work?

2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 - 4} (x^2 - 2x - 15) \geq 0.$$

3. The perimeter of an equilateral trapezoid of input is greater than the length of the hanging circle. Find the angle at the base of the trapezoid.

4. Solve the equation:
$$2 \cos^2 x + \cos 5x - 1 = 0.$$
