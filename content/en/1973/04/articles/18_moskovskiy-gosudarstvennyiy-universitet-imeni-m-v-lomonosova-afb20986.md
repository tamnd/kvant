Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov

(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics, Faculty of Physics)

Fans of mathematics and physics, of course, are interested in the faculties of Moscow University that train specialists in physics and mathematics. These are the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, the Faculty of Physics, the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics.

The Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics has two departments - mathematics and mechanics. Specialists are trained here in the development areas of theoretical and applied mathematics and modern mechanics.

The Faculty of Physics has six departments: experimental and theoretical physics, physics of the creative body, nuclear physics, radiophysics and electronics, geophysics, and astronomy. The faculty prepares broad-spectrum research physicists to work in research institutes and various sectors of the national economy.

The young Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics trains specialists who are able to effectively apply mathematical methods and modern computing in technical institutes and various sectors of the national economy.

The faculty has specialized departments where leading scientists of the country work. Starting from the third year, students are assigned (by their choice) to departments for in-depth study of their chosen narrower section of physics or mathematics. All faculties have the necessary material base and specialized laboratories, which makes it possible to combine theoretical education with practical training.

To help applicants, there are preparatory departments for work and rural youth, evening and correspondence preparatory courses.

Below are examples of written application exams in mathematics for the indicated faculties and tickets for oral exams in physics at the physics department.

1. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the inequality is true

$$\frac{\log_8 x}{\log_2 (1 + 2x)} \leq \frac{\log_2 \sqrt[3]{1 + 2x}}{\log_2 x}.$$

During this time, the pedestrian traveled once from $B$ to $A$, and the cyclist traveled twice in the same direction along the circular route formed by the two named roads. Find the speeds of the pedestrian and the cyclist if it is known that their second meeting will occur at a distance of 3.5 km from point $B$. (Speeds are constant.)
