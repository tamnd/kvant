Signs of divisibility

On 61. Let's cross out the last digit of this number and add the six-digit crossed out digit from the resulting number. Let's do this operation several times, and we will get smaller and smaller numbers. If at some stage it turns out to be zero, then the original number is divided by 61, and if it doesn’t work out, then it is not divided.

Example. Is 752886 divisible by 61?

752886—6×4=752862
75286—6×2=75274
7527—6×4=7503
750—6×3=732
73—6×2=61
6—6×1=0.

Answer: shares.

At 59. Cross out the last digit of this number and add the six-digit crossed out digit to the resulting number. Let's do this operation several times, again the numbers will sequentially decrease. But now, if at some stage it turns out to be 59, then the original number is divided by 59, and if it doesn’t work out, then it is not divided.

Example. Is 728355 divisible by 59?

72835+6×5=72865
7286+6×5=7316
731+6×6=767
76+6×7=118
11+6×8=59.

Answer: shares.

Try to prove these signs of divisibility and generalize them to other processes of two-digit (and maybe multi-digit) numbers.

V. M. Rosentuler
