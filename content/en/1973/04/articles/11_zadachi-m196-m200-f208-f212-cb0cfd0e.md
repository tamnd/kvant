PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than May 31, 1973 to the address: 117071, Moscow V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, Nauka Publishing House, magazine «Kvant».
After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: “Problem «Kvant», M196, M198 or “...F210”.
Solutions to the problem for each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems, should be sent in separate envelopes.
Oprizinal problems proposed for publication are assigned along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope there is a room: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). Problems from different issues of the magazine are assigned in different envelopes.
Tasks of increased difficulty are marked with an asterisk.
After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

F211. A closed process (cycle) is carried out with an ideal monatomic gas, shown in figure 7. At point $C$ the gas had a volume $V_C$ and a pressure $P_C$, and at point $B - V_B = \frac{1}{2} V_C$ and $P_B = 2P_C$. Find the efficiency of this cycle and compare it with the maximum theoretical efficiency of a cycle in which the temperatures of the heater and refrigerator are equal to the maximum and minimum temperatures of the cycle being considered, respectively.
