BRANDS,
DEDICATED
YURI GAGARIN

COLLECTOR'S CORNER

12 years ago, on April 12, 1961, an event occurred that amazed the whole world - man first ascended into outer space. The first cosmonaut was the Soviet communist Yuri Alekseevich Gagarin.

Many stamps issued in various countries of the world are dedicated to the feat of the first cosmonaut. Some of them are shown in our photographs.

The first stamp with a portrait of Gagarin was issued in the USSR in 1961. The date of the flight is indicated on it, and around the portrait there is the inscription “The world’s first cosmonaut Yu. A. Gagarin.” You see this stamp on the left for the first time in the same series, a second stamp was issued, on which Gagarin is depicted in a space suit to the left of the package taking off against the backdrop of the Kremlin. In 1964, for Cosmonautics Day in the USSR, a stamp was issued depicting Gagarin and the cabin of the Vostok-1 spacecraft.

A series of two stamps issued in Poland in 1961 is dedicated to the launch of the first cosmonaut. On one of them you see a portrait of Gagarin in the form of a parachute. He is depicted in the same form on a Cuban stamp of 1963 and a Bulgarian stamp of 1966.

Czechoslovakia issued a series of three stamps in 1961, on which Gagarin is symbolically depicted rapidly flying in space. In the same year, in connection with Yuri Gagarin's visit to Prague, a stamp was issued with his portrait in a spacesuit.

A 1967 Bulgarian stamp shows Gagarin with Tereshkova and Leonov.
