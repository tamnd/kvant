⟦folio 45⟧

TASK

Four urns contain balls. The two players take turns taking balls from the urns. For each turn, the player chooses two urns and takes any number of balls from them, and at least one ball must be taken. The one who takes the last ball wins. For each number of balls in the urns, will the beginner win and how should he play to win? Try to summarize this game.

G. I. Podolny
