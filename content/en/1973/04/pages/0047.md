F210. Two identical open vessels are connected by two identical tubes and filled to the top with water. The tubes are closed with taps $K_1$ and $K_2$ (Fig. 6). The temperature of the water in the vessels is maintained constant, and $t_1^\circ > t_2^\circ > 4^\circ$ C. What will happen to the water in the vessels if you first open the tap $K_2$, and then (with the tap $K_2$ open) open the tap $K_1$?

F211. A closed process (cycle) is carried out with an ideal monatomic gas, shown in figure 7. At point $C$ the gas had a volume $V_C$ and a pressure $P_C$, and at point $B - V_B = \frac{1}{2} V_C$ and $P_B = 2P_C$. Find the efficiency of this cycle and compare it with the maximum theoretical efficiency of a cycle in which the temperatures of the heater and refrigerator are equal to the maximum and minimum temperatures of the cycle being considered, respectively.

M. E. Marinchik

F212. From a glacier sliding into the ocean along a circular slope at a depth of 1 km, a block of ice opens - an iceberg (its height is less than 1 km). What part of the iceberg can melt when floating? The temperature of ice and water is 0°C.

For each number of balls in the urns, will the beginner win and how should he play to win? Try to summarize this game.
