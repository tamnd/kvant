More than 100 different methods have been proposed for graphically solving the Potenot problem *). They are based on the orientation of the scale tablet. Let the three points $a, b$ and $c$ (Fig. 3), which make up the triangle $abc$ on the scale tablet $Q$, correspond to the threes $A, B$ and $C$ on the terrain. A tablet $Q$ is oriented if it is positioned so that $ab \parallel AB, ac \parallel AC$ and $bc \parallel BC$.

In this case, the directions passed through $a, b$ and $c$ when sighting at the corresponding terrain points will intersect at one point. To prove this, we construct the point $m$ as the intersection point of the lines $Aa$ and $Bb$. Similarly, by reducing $\Delta ABC$ so that $AB$ goes into $ab$, we get $\Delta abc'$, but since $ac \parallel AC \parallel ac'$ and $bc \parallel BC \parallel bc'$, the points $c'$ and $c$ must coincide, and the lines $Aa, Bb$ and $Cc$ will all intersect in point $m$, corresponding on the tablet to point $M$, from which we sight at points $A, B, C$.

If the scale tablet located at point $M$ is only approximately oriented, then the same three directions $aA, bB$ and $cC$ will not intersect at one point, but form a small triangle (Fig. 4), called the error triangle. And the more accurately the orientation is made

The mensula is installed above the desired point $M$, the upper plane of the tablet is brought to a horizontal position and the tablet is oriented as accurately as possible *) $Q$ so that the lines $ab, bc$ and $ac$ on the tablet are parallel to the lines $AB, BC$ and $AC$ on the ground. After this, through three given points on the tablet, they sight at the corresponding points of the terrain and follow the line along the edge of the ruler of the sighting device (kppregel, alidade) (as in figure 3). If the orientation of the tablet is carried out accurately, then in the direction, the other point $a, b$ and $c$, when sighting at the corresponding points of the terrain, will intersect at the desired point $m$.

If the tablet $Q$, located at point $M$ of the terrain, is not oriented accurately, then the other points $a, cC$ and $bB$ will not intersect on the tablet at one point, but form an error triangle.

*) For more precise orientation of the tablet, it is recommended to use a compass.
