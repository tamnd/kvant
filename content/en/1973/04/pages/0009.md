At the age of nine, as a result of complications after scaratina, K. E. Tsilkovsky lost his hearing. Over the years, it progressed more, and Konstantin Eduardovich was forced to use his own made service pipe. When receiving a visitor, he asked to speak without raising his voice, since this megaphone amplified the sound well.
This photo shows Konstantin Eduardovich Tsilkovsky while receiving a visitor.
On my lap is a tablet with paper. During a conversation, Tsilkovsky liked to encroach on what was said with drawings, drawings, and calculations. In the background, on the floor, you can see bundles of Tsilkovsky’s brochures received from the printing house.
Here are the titles of some of his works:
"Mechanics of the Animal Organism" (1885),
“The jet device as a means of flight in emptiness and atmosphere” (1910),
"The Cause of Space" (1925),
"Air Resistance and the Fast Train" (1927),
“Pressure on a plane during normal movement in the air” (1929),
"Stratoplane semi-jet" (1932).
