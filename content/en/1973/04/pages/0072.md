⟦folio 70⟧

1. Find the smallest natural numbers $a$ and $d$ satisfying the equality
$$500a - 7d = 1.$$

2. When you pour juice from a tin can through the responses in the lid, you make two responses. Only then does a good flow come. Why?

3. My grandfather is 32 years older than my father, and my father is the same amount older than me. How old is each of us now, if three years ago we weren’t all together?

4. There is a stack of books on the table. What is easier: to pull out the bottom book, holding (but not lifting!) the rest, or to move the entire stack by pulling the bottom book?

5. Restore the example for multiplication:
$$38,? \times ?6 = ???$$
(that is, replace the ? signs with numbers that are not equal to zero).

6. In a circuit containing an electric light bulb, the resistance of the supply wires and the lamp filament are the same, but the filament glows white hot, and the wires hardly heat up. Why?
