The forces acting on the frames in the field are: $T$ - tension, $F_A$ - Ampere force, $mg$ - gravitational force (Fig. 8).

When the tilted frame is in equilibrium, the sum of the moments of all forces acting on three sides of the square relative to the $OO$ axis is equal to zero.

The moments of gravity are equal

$$M_{OD} = M_{OC} = \rho l Sg \frac{l}{2} \sin \alpha,$$

where $l$ is the length of the side of the frame, $\alpha$ is the angle of deviation of the frame from the vertical, $g$ is the acceleration of free fall.

The moments of the Ampere forces acting on the sides of the frame are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. Ticket number 5

A magnetic field that changes over time, an arbitrary turn, excites e.g. in it. d.s. induction equal in magnitude

$$E = \frac{\Delta B}{\Delta t} S,$$

where $S$ is the area of ​​the turn perpendicular to the confluent field lines. When turning the figure eight, the normal to the surface of one loop changed its direction to the opposite. Full area of ​​the figure eight

$$S = S_2 - S_1 = \pi \left(r_2^2 - r_1^2\right),$$

where $r_1$ and $r_2$ are the radii of the figure eight loops. By condition $r_2 = 2r_1$. Then $l = 2\pi \cdot 3r_1$. From here

$$r_1 = \frac{l}{6\pi}, \quad r_2 = \frac{l}{3\pi}, \quad S = \frac{l^2}{12\pi}.$$

So, flowing through the conductor is equal to

$$I = \frac{E}{R} = \frac{\Delta B}{\Delta t} \cdot \frac{l^2}{12\pi R} = \frac{1,44 \cdot 10^{-4} \cdot 10^2}{12 \cdot 3 \cdot 14 \cdot 1} a \approx 0,4 \text{ мa}.$$
