In 1887, K. E. Tsiolkovsky made a report on the basic principles of the creation and flight of a whole-metal esrostat at the Moscow Society of Natural Scientists. Among the speakers of the report were professors N. E. Zhukovsky and A. G. Stoletov.

The photo shows K. E. Tsiolkovsky with a model of a metal airship - one of his first major inventions. This photograph was taken in the summer of 1914 in the garden of Tsiolkovsky’s house by Konstantin Eduardovich himself.

K. E. Tsiolkovsky maintained an extensive correspondence. His correspondents included young schoolchildren, masterful scientists, and famous writers. In the photograph taken in May 1932, K. E. Tsiolkovsky is looking at the magazine “Red Arrow”, which was sent to him by Mayakovsky’s friend, hence the artist D. Burliuk. This progressive magazine, published in the 30s in the USA by D. Burliuk, correctly covered the life of Soviet Russia. The sent issue of the magazine contained materials about Tsiolkovsky.
