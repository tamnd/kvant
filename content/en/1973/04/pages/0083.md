A stamp with a portrait of Gagarin was issued to the International Astronautical Congress.
In 1963, a stamp (diamond-shaped) with a portrait of the first cosmonaut was issued in Romania. You can also see his image on another blush stamp, issued in 1967 in honor of the tenth anniversary of the launch of the first artificial Earth satellite.
A 1967 Bulgarian stamp shows Gagarin with Tereshkova and Leonov.
The photo also shows a stamp of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam (1965) and a stamp of the Cuban People's Republic, on which Gagarin is depicted next to a globe surrounded by space flight trajectories.
