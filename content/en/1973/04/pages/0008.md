Konstantin Eduardovich liked to work, sitting in an old comfortable cross, using a small board along the table. He wrote with a simple black pencil, usually on school notebooks. On the wall is K. E. Tsiolkovsky’s favorite pocket watch, which served as his chronometer during his experiments.

K. E. Tsiolkovsky in his workshop, next to him is K. N. Altaisky, secretary of the editorial office of the Kaluga provincial newspaper “Commune”. The scientist's workshop is located in his house, next to his office. There was a foot-operated lathe by the window; K. E. Tsiolkovsky was working on the color.
