The work of prospecting and geological experiments is shrouded in romance: bonfires and songs with a guitar, thickets and swamps, manifestations of the best and worst human traits, emerging against the backdrop of difficulties... And there is no beginning for the collaboration of a “sober” mathematician.

But this is only at first glance. Let's take a closer look, forget about romance for a while and find out why geologists organize various experiments, what is their goal, how do they achieve it? It is clear that geologists undertake experiments not only for their own satisfaction. Their goal is to search for minerals.

What are they doing? They are looking... And when they find it, it is a thrill, a joy: their hard work was not in vain. Because there are times when work goes in vain. Of course, geologists do not act in the universe; there is vast experience of predecessors, geological science, new and even newer methods of geophysics, geochemistry, geomorphology and other geo...sciences. And it still happens that the activities of search parties fail: as a result of a lot of work, nothing is found.
