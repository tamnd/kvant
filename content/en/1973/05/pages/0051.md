Problem 6. (3) A magnetic suspension point of length $l$ instantly sets in motion in the horizontal direction with a constant speed $v$, then after moving a distance $a$ it instantly stops. Before the movement began, the magnets accumulated. At what speed does the magnetic suspension, which appeared when the movement began, stop immediately after stopping? The angle of deviation of the magnet from the vertical is considered small.

Problem 7. (3) There is a parallel beam of identical nuclei moving with speed $v$. The nuclei in the beam spontaneously divide into two parts of equal mass. The maximum speed of sedges moving in the direction of the beam turns out to be equal to $u$. Find the speed of sedges moving in the direction perpendicular to the beam (for a stationary observable).

Problem 8. (3) A beetle is photographed at two scales, bringing the camera to a distance $l$, equal first to triple and then to five times the focal length of the lens. How many times must the diameter of the lens's diffraction be changed so that the illumination of the image on the film is the same in both cases? Assume that the diameter of the object is much less than $l$.

Then everywhere the magnetic field uniformly decreases to zero. What speed does the ring reach after the magnetic field disappears?
