⟦folio none⟧

1. Prove that for any natural number $n$
$$6^{2(n+1)} - 2^{n+3} \cdot 3^{n+2} + 36$$ is divisible by 900.

2. There are two large opaque vessels. One of them contains kerosene, the other contains kerosene and water. How can you distinguish these vessels, having at your disposal a dynamometer and a weight on a string?

3. All natural numbers from 1 to 100 are divided into two groups: even and odd numbers. Determine in which group the sum of all digits used to write numbers is greater and by how much.

4. The first automated one travels at a speed of 60 km/h. At what speed should the second automation go so that each kilometer is covered 2 minutes faster? 1 minute faster? In general, how much faster can the second automated 1 km be completed?

5. On figure in the formula, the numbers are replaced with colored asterisks (each number has its own asterisk). Restore the formula.

6. Why are door handles attached to the “free” edge?
