⟦folio 50⟧

New books

In this issue we continue *) to publish English books published in 1973 that are of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the second quarter of 1973 (orders can be sent through the Book - Postal store).

Mathematics
Publishing house "Nuuka"

1. Lidsky V. B., Ovsyannikov L. V., Tulaikov A. N. and others. Assignment on elementary mathematics (volume 22 pages, circulation 200,000 copies, price 72 kopecks).
This book is a collection of problems of increased difficulty in elementary mathematics. All tasks are provided with solutions or detailed instructions. When compiling this publication, the experience of entrance exams in recent years was taken into account.

The book is intended for 10th grade students and can be used in self-preparation for university entrance exams.

2. Kalinn R. A., Alebra and elementary functions (volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 13 kopecks).
The book contains materials on algebra and elementary functions in the scope of a technical course. It can also be used in self-preparation for university entrance exams.

3. Gelfand I. M., Glagoleva E. G., Shnol E. E., Functions and graphs (volume 5 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 13 kopecks).

*) See «Kvant» No. 2, p. 74, 1973.

This book is included in the “library of the physics and mathematics school”. It outlines the most important mathematical concepts - the concepts of functions, graphs of functions, and so on. Simple examples of constructing function graphs are given.

The book is intended for schoolchildren in grades 8–10 who are interested in mathematics. It can also be used in math circles.

4. Leathwood J., Mathematical mixture (volume 8 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 45 kopecks).
The book consists of a number of essays, although the scales are different in composition, but outlined by mathematical topics. They contain small studies on the theory of mathematics, and the resulting presentation of a number of mathematical questions, and interesting problems, and finally, just mathematical things.

The lively and fascinating language makes this book interesting for a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Mir"

5. Carroll L., The Story of Knots (volume 23 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 1 rub. 36 kopecks).
The name of Lewis Carroll - the author of the famous fairy tales "Alice in Wonderland" and "Alice in Wonderland" - is well known to Soviet readers. However, L. Carroll, a mathematician by training, was also the author of famous mathematical puzzles and problems that studied logical paradoxes.

This book collects interesting things that have been published from this genre by L. Carroll. The book will bring great convenience to a wide range of readers.

Physics
Publishing house "Nuuka"

6. Gursky I.P., Elementary physics (volume 25 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 80 kopecks).
The book is a guide to physics for university applicants. In it, all the questions included in the entrance exam program are discussed step by step and sequentially. The presentation of mathematics is accompanied by the solution of typical problems.

The book is intended for secondary school students, students of preparatory departments and people engaged in self-education.

7. Grigoriev V.I., Myakishev G.Ya., Sili in nature (volume 20 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 73 kopecks).
The book introduces the reader to the world of physical concepts using examples of the main types of interaction of forces in nature.

Written in good language, with a large number of uses, this book will be useful for high school students and teachers teaching extracurricular classes.

8. Ivanov B. N., Prizzi of more perfect physics (volume 8 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 50 kopecks).
The book sets out in a relatively simple and accessible form the basic concepts and principles of advanced physics and gives an idea of ​​the current state of physics as a whole.

The book is of interest to secondary school teachers, and will also be available to trained high school students,
