F2470. Three small icebergs 1, 2 and 3 float in the salty ocean, as shown in figure (not to scale everywhere). Each of them has the shape of a rectangular parallelepiped. There is a person standing on iceberg 1. The surface areas of the upper faces of icebergs are the same, and the heights of the surface parts are equal to $h_{1} = 5$ cm, $, h_{2} = 10$ cm and $h_{3} = 12$ cm, respectively. When a person moved from

iceberg 1 onto iceberg 2, then in equilibrium the surfaces of icebergs 1 and 2 were at the same height above the water level. After this, the person moved from iceberg 2 to iceberg 3. Determine what the heights of the surface parts of each iceberg became when equilibrium was restored.

For each iceberg, the force of gravity acting on it, plus the weight of a person (if a person is standing on the iceberg) is balanced by the Archimedes force acting on the underwater part of the iceberg. The Archimedes force is equal to $F_{Арх} = R gV$, where R is the density of water, g is the acceleration of gravity, V is the volume of the underwater part of the iceberg. This volume of the underwater part can be expressed by the formula V = SH, where S is the area of ​​the upper edge of the iceberg, H is the height of its underwater part. According to the conditions of the problem, the areas of the upper faces of all icebergs are the same. It follows that the mass of each iceberg plus the mass of a person (if a person is standing on the iceberg) is proportional to the height of the underwater part of the iceberg. Therefore, when a person moves from the first iceberg to the second, the second iceberg sinks into the water as much as the first one floats up. When a person moved from the first iceberg to the second, the surfaces of these two icebergs were at the same level. This means that the first iceberg floated 2.5 cm, and the second sank 2.5 cm. The heights of the surface parts of the icebergs above sea level became 7.5 cm, 7.5 cm and 12 cm, respectively. When the person moved from the second iceberg to the third, the height of the surface part of the second iceberg returned to its original value of 10 cm, and the third iceberg was submerged.
