There are exactly two common points with the parabola: K and M. It turned out that the tangents to the circle and the parabola at point M coincide. Do the tangents to the circle and the parabola at point K also necessarily coincide? Answer: not necessarily. Note. Consider two antiparallels to a parabola, one antiparallel m touches the parabola at point M, and the other passes through M and intersects the parabola a second time at point K (Fig.10). Then the circle passing through K and touching line m at point M is required.

Generalized antiparallelism criterion. Let a curve A from class K, which is not a circle, be given and different points K, L, M, N on this curve such that A does not entirely contain either the line KM or the line LN. Points K, L, M, N lie on the same circle if and only if KM and LN are antiparallel (Fig.12).
