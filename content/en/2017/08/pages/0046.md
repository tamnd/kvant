Theoretical tour

SENIOR LEAGUE

Problem 1. Boat and barge A boat, uniformly pulling a barge on a long cable, moves with it A times slower than without it, with the same propeller thrust. If, while moving with a barge, the boat turns off the engine, then the barge will travel B times greater distance than the boat before it comes to a complete stop. How many times is the mass of the barge greater than the mass of the boat? The force of water resistance is directly proportional to speed. V.Efimov

Problem 2. A drop in a tube between vessels Two identical rigid vessels are filled with different ideal gases having temperatures $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$ (and $T_{1} T_{2} ),$) and are connected by a thin horizontal cylindrical tube of length L, in the middle of which a drop of mercury is in equilibrium, not wetting the walls of the tube. The volume of gas in the tube is a very small fraction of the volume of one vessel. How will the position of the drop change if the temperature in both vessels is increased by the same amount T?

Problem 4. Wooden core Many turns of a thin wire were wound tightly in one layer on the cylindrical part of a long, sharply sharpened axisymmetric wooden pencil, and its ends were closed together. A pencil with a wire can be considered a uniform cylinder of mass m and cross-section S. The pencil was carefully placed with the lead down on a horizontal rough surface, an external uniform vertical magnetic field was turned on by induction B, then the wire was cooled, transferring it to a superconducting state, after which the pencil left the unstable equilibrium position. 1) Find the angle $J_{0}$ of the pencil’s deviation from the vertical in a stable equilibrium position. 2) Find the period of small oscillations of a pencil in a vertical plane passing through the axis of symmetry of the pencil in a stable equilibrium position, if it is known that these oscillations are possible. A. Chudnovsky
