The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics and higher mathematics at MIPT, as well as MIPT graduates and other specialists who have extensive experience working with gifted students. The assignments contain theoretical material, analysis of typical examples and problems on the relevant topic, and 8–12 test questions and problems for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones. Examples of tasks can be viewed on the ZFTSH website. The work of part-time students is checked by students, graduate students and graduates of MIPT (80% of them are graduates of our school). The student completes the introductory assignment in selected subjects independently in one school notebook in Russian, maintaining the same order of tasks as in the assignment. The notebook must be sent in an envelope with a simple parcel or a simple letter. More than 5 thousand introductory works are submitted to the competition every year. Please pay attention to filling out the form correctly! Be careful! On the inside cover of your notebook, stick a certificate from the school you attend, indicating your class. To receive an answer to the introductory task and to send you the first tasks, be sure to include two identical envelopes measuring 160 x 230 mm in your notebook. There is no need to stick stamps. Write your home address clearly on the envelopes. Send your notebook with completed assignments to ZFTSH no later than March 1, 2018. Checked entrance papers will not be sent back. All works sent to ZFTSH are registered. Information on receiving work can be found on the ZFTSH website in the “Introductory assignments” section. The decision of the admissions committee will be sent in July 2018. If a student is already studying at ZFTS and wants to add another subject for the next year, he must complete and send to ZFTS an introductory assignment for this subject. Be sure to indicate your personal number on the form. In this case, the decision of the admissions committee is not sent, and the additional subject becomes available to the student in the Personal Account in July, if the decision of the admissions committee is positive.
