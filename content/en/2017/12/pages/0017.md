Let's start with the proof of the following lemma. Lemma. Let players from N teams be lined up, each of which has at least N + 1 players. Then you can leave 2N players in the line, 2 from each team, so that any two players from the same team will stand next to each other. Proof. Induction on N. Base: for N = 1 the statement is obvious. Let's make the transition from N – 1 to N. Let's number the players in each team with the numbers 1, 2, ... from left to right. Among all the players with number 2, we will choose the one on the left. Let this be player $K_{2}$ playing for team K (here $K_{1}, K_{2}$, ... are all players of team K, numbered from left to right). Now let’s remove from the line all the players standing to the left of $K_{2}$, and the entire team K completely. Then from each remaining team we removed no more than one player (we could only remove the player with number 1, since the players with higher numbers were to the right $K_{2} ).$ By the induction assumption, for the remaining row we can choose 2N – 2 players in the required way. It remains to add to them a pair of players $K_{1}$ and $K_{2}$. Let's move on to solving the problem. Let's divide the football players into N teams as follows: into the first team we will include the N + 1 tallest players, the next N + 1 in height, etc. According to the lemma, we can leave two players from each team so that they stand next to each other. This is what is required in the problem, since the players from the i-th team are the ( 2 i - 1 )-th and 2i-th players in height among those left.
