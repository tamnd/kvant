A group (at least 7 people) is admitted to ZFTS upon the application of the director, written on the form of a general education institution (a sample can be viewed in the “part-time department” section of the ZFTS website), which must indicate the surnames, first names, patronymics of the leaders of the elective group in subjects and a named alphabetical list of students (full name in alphabetical order, indicating the class of the current academic year and final grades for the entrance exam assignment in selected subjects), address, telephone, fax and e-mail of the school. The application can be sent by regular mail, enclosing an envelope for a response to admission to the ZFTSH with the return address of one of the managers to the ZFTSH address (marked “Optional”), or sent in scanned form (with signatures and seal) to e-mail: fakultativ@mipt.ru until May 25, 2018 to the address of the ZFTSH (marked “Optional”). Notebooks with students' work are checked by teachers of physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry and are not sent to ZFTSH. The work of the heads of elective classes can be paid by the educational institution as the management of specialized elective classes by providing the ZFTSH with the relevant information. Leaders working with students will receive educational and methodological materials during the academic year (programs in physics, mathematics, chemistry and computer science, assignments on program topics, solutions to assignments with brief recommendations for assessing student work); be invited to advanced training courses for physics and mathematics teachers, conducted at MIPT. Students’ work is checked and assessed by the leaders of optional groups, and they send statements with final grades for each to the ZFTSH
