Let's summarize. We have seen that the following properties of triangle ABC are equivalent: i AB = ( AC + BC ) 3; i – AIM $= 90 \infty$, where M – the middle of AC; i – BIN $= 90 \infty$, where N is the middle of BC; i the middle line parallel to AB touches the inscribed circle; i I – midpoint of $CI_{c} ; i CI =$ 2IW, where W – midpoint of arc AB; i, the midpoints of arcs ABC, BAC and point I lie on the same straight line. After this, it no longer seems surprising that problems about such triangles appear multiple times at Olympiads. Perhaps readers will be able to discover new properties of them or find a new, no less interesting class of triangles. P.S. When the article was already written, S.
