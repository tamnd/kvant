SCHOOL AT KVANT

Thermodynamics and motorcycle cycle

A.STASENKO

“Hello, noble vectors! I am the good wizard Adi Aba Ata...” He led the Adi Aba Ata brothers to the geodetic line, pointed the way to Divgrad... How three vectors turned one determinant to zero (from the book “Physicists Continue Joking”)

ONE day at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology there was a conference of schoolchildren as part of the “Start into Science” program. The jury members listened attentively to the ninth-grader's report on motorcycles. Familiar thermodynamic images appeared in the heads of experienced listeners, starting with the simplest gas processes. For example, on figure 1 three of them are shown in the coordinates pressure - volume (p, V), pressure - temperature (p, T), as well as in three-dimensional space (p, T, V). Thermodynamics began with these particular situations about two hundred years ago. Who now does not know the laws of Gay-Lussac, Boyle-Mariotte, Charles, Mendeleev-Clapeyron! But the process is more complicated (to describe) - adiabatic, i.e. occurring without exchange of heat with surrounding bodies. On figure 2 it is marked with the number 4. Let us compare it with the isothermal process 1. It can be seen that when the working fluid (gas) expands from volume $V_{min}$ to $V_{max}$, to maintain a constant temperature in process 1 it is necessary to supply energy from the outside, but in the adiabatic process 4 there is no heat exchange and the gas has to spend part of its own energy on the same increase in volume. Therefore, its temperature and pressure decrease. It is clear to anyone who understands that in this case the parameters of the state are related by the relations
