In the wonderful article by V. Dubrovsky and V. Senderova “Trap for the triangle” («Kvant» No. 3 for 1999) the trajectories of the orthocenter, center of mass (the point of intersection of medians) and the center of the circle of nine points of the Poncelet triangles were indicated. In the article by A. Zaslavsky, D. Kosov and M. Muzafarov “Trajectories of remarkable points of the Poncelet triangle” («Kvant» No. 2 for 2003), in addition, the trajectories of the Nagel and Gergonne points were found. Recall that the vertices of the triangle and the points of tangency of opposite sides with excircles $(рис.2,б).^{1}$ All trajectories of the mentioned remarkable points are circles whose centers lie on the line of the centers of circles Ω and $\omega$. There is some analogy here with the geocentric system of Ptolemy (all planets rotate in circles whose centers are in in turn lie on the circle), this analogy gave the name “Ptolemaic axis”. In this article we will again focus on the Gergonne and Nagel points of the Poncelet triangles and give a description of the circles along which they move. But unlike the article by A. Zaslavsky, D. Kosov and M. Muzafarov, where the main tool was algebraic transformations and barycentric coordinates, we will give geometric proof.
