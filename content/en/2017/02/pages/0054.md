2. (4) There are four points marked on the line and one more point is marked outside the line. There are a total of six triangles with vertices at these points. What is the largest number of them that can be isosceles? E. Bakaev

3. There are 100 points marked on a circle. These points are numbered from 1 to 100 in some order. a) (2) Prove that, for any numbering, the points can be divided into pairs so that the segments connecting the points in the pairs do not intersect, and all sums in the pairs are odd. b) (2) Is it true that for any numbering it is possible to divide the points into pairs so that the segments connecting the points in the pairs do not intersect, and all the sums in the pairs are even? P. Kozhevnikov
