Municipal stage of the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics

In the Moscow region, the municipal stage of the Olympiad took place on December 18, 2016. 4,270 schoolchildren in grades 7–11 took part in it.

Objectives of the Olympiad

7th grade

Task 1. Astrophysics Two space objects are observed through a telescope. It is known that one of them is located at a distance of 18.0 thousand light years from Earth, and the other is located at a distance of 5.50 kiloparsecs. The angle between the visible directions to the objects is 60 degrees, and the distance between them is 1.14 billion astronomical units (AU). How many light years are there in 1 parsec? It is known that a distance of 1 AU light travels in 500 seconds and 1 light year is the distance light travels in 1 year.

Problem 3. Time is relative The train passed a bridge of length l = 450 m in $t_{1} = 45$ s. A guard standing on the bridge noticed that the train moved past him for $t_{2} = 30$ s. How long did a passenger sitting in a train carriage travel across the bridge? Find the length of the train and its speed and determine how many times the length of the train is greater than the length of the bridge.
