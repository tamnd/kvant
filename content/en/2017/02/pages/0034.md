Inverters

In the 19th century, mechanical engineers were faced with an important task: to create a straight line - a mechanism that converts circular motion into straight motion. It was assumed that the solution would be a flat hinge mechanism - a system of flat links of different lengths connected at the ends by hinges. The great Russian mathematician Pafnuty Lvovich Chebyshev was able to come up with only an approximate straight line, transforming the circular motion of one hinge into the motion of another hinge near the straight line segment, but not exactly along it (Fig.1).

The deviation of the connecting rod curve from a straight line can be reduced by changing the parameters of the mechanism. In this case, the stroke length of the driven joint will also decrease, but this occurs more slowly than the decrease in deviation from the straight line. Therefore, for practical purposes it is possible to select satisfactory parameters, but from a theoretical point of view the problem remains unsolved.
