"QUANTA"

use a regular wooden ruler 30 centimeters long. Such a ruler is also capable of autorotating - spinning independently under the influence of the oncoming air flow. You will need to make a hole at one end of the ruler, thread a strong thread through it and tie it, and then untwist the ruler in front of you. The ruler will go into autorotation mode and begin to rotate around its axis - this can be seen along the path of the LED attached to the end of the ruler. This means that the Magnus force will act on the ruler, which will take it out of the vertical plane and force it to move along the surface of a certain cone. When the direction of autorotation changes to the opposite, the Magnus force will also change its direction, and the cone will be located on the other side of the vertical plane. These two cones are clearly visible on figure 7. The reversal of autorotation occurs by itself - due to the periodically increasing twist of the thread. When rotated, the ruler produces a low vibrating sound, and many archaic musical instruments belonging to different eras and peoples are built on this principle.
