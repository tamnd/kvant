Exercise 5: Prove it. Note. Cut the scale horizontally into pieces, rearranging them to form a rectangle $t \times s$.

During a parabolic rotation, any vertical line is shifted by k horizontally, which means that our flake will transform into a new flake of the same width t, sandwiched between the same parabolas. As we can see, the area of ​​the new flake is also equal to ts. So, the areas of the scales sandwiched between vertical lines and parabolas of the form $y = x^{2} + c$ are preserved.
