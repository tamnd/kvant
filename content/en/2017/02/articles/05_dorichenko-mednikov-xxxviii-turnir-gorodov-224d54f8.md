4. (8) A square box of chocolates is divided into 49 equal square cells. Each cell contains a chocolate candy - either black or white. In one sitting, Sasha can eat two candies if they are the same color and lie in cells adjacent to the side or corner. What is the largest number of candies that Sasha is guaranteed to eat, no matter how the candies are placed in the box? A. Kuznetsov

5. (8) Three red and three blue cards have six positive numbers written on them, all of them different. It is known that pairwise sums of some three numbers are written on cards of one color, and pairwise products of the same three numbers are written on cards of a different color. Is it always possible to determine these three numbers with certainty? B.Frenkin
