Municipal stage of the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics

In the Moscow region, the municipal stage of the Olympiad took place on December 18, 2016. 4,270 schoolchildren in grades 7–11 took part in it.

Objectives of the Olympiad

7th grade

Task 1. Astrophysics Two space objects are observed through a telescope. It is known that one of them is located at a distance of 18.0 thousand light years from Earth, and the other is located at a distance of 5.50 kiloparsecs. The angle between the visible directions to the objects is 60 degrees, and the distance between them is 1.14 billion astronomical units (AU). How many light years are there in 1 parsec? It is known that a distance of 1 AU light travels in 500 seconds and 1 light year is the distance light travels in 1 year.

9th grade

Problem 1. Second half The cart, moving from rest with constant acceleration, covers a distance s and acquires a speed v = 10 m/s. Then, continuing to move uniformly with speed v, it covers the same distance s. Determine the average speed of the cart for the second half of the entire travel time.

Task 2. Flask A metal flask has the shape of a parallelepiped. The mass of a flask filled with water is equal to M. If the flask is placed on the table with its largest face, then it will exert a pressure $p_{1}$. If you put it on the middle face, then the pressure will be equal to $p_{2}$. If the flask is placed on the smallest edge, then the pressure will be equal to $p_{3}$. What are the mass m of the empty flask and the lengths of its edges a, b and c? The thickness of the walls of the flask is negligibly small compared to the lengths of its ribs. The density of water is $\rho$. Note. To be specific, designate the length of the short edge of the flask as a, the length of the middle edge as b, and the length of the long edge as c.

Problem 3. Liquid equilibrium Two cylinders of equal dimensions are balanced on a light lever. At the first weighing, the fulcrum divides the lever in a ratio of 1: 2, and the cylinders are immersed in a liquid of density $\rho$ by a third of the volume (Fig. 4,a). If the depth of immersion is doubled, then to maintain balance you will have to move the fulcrum of the lever to the left so that it divides the lever in a ratio of 1: 3 (Fig.4,b). Determine the densities of cylinders $\rho_{1}$ and $\rho_{2}$.
