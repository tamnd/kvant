The final stage of the XLIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The final stage of the All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in the city of Kaliningrad from April 24 to April 30, 2017. 364 young mathematicians from more than 60 regions of Russia took part in the Olympiad. In addition, traditionally the guest teams from China and Bulgaria took part in the Olympiad. The rounds of the Olympiad, as well as the opening and closing ceremonies, were held in the building of the very large, but at the same time cozy and hospitable Gymnasium No. 40 named after Yu.A. Gagarin. A large team of volunteers worked at the Olympiad, who, together with the organizing committee, organized the participants’ acquaintance with both the memorable places of Kaliningrad and its picturesque surroundings. Despite the fact that the tasks of this year’s Olympiad were not easy, in grades 9 and 11 there were participants who managed to solve all the problems: ninth-grader Petr Mishura (St. Petersburg, formerly Novosibirsk), who received a special prize for the absolute result, and eleventh-grader Nikita Dobronravov (Novosibirsk), who received a special prize for solving the most difficult problem of the Olympiad. After the rounds of the Olympiad, a vote was held in which the participants indicated their favorite tasks. In 9th grade, the top three problems according to schoolchildren are problems 8 (I place), 3 (II place), 6 (III place); in 10th grade – 7 (I place), 4 (II place), 3 (III place); in 11th grade – 4 (I place), 7 (II place), 3 (III place). At the end of the All-Russian Olympiad, the Russian team was determined for the 2017 International Mathematical Olympiad, which will be held in Rio de Janeiro (Brazil). Our team included five graduates: Nikita Dobronravov (Novosibirsk),

Georgy Veprev (Yaroslavl), Timofey Zaitsev (Moscow), Mikhail Ivanov (St. Petersburg) and Kirill Tyshchuk (St. Petersburg), as well as tenth grader Vadim Retinsky (Moscow, formerly the city of Livny, Oryol region). Below we publish the conditions of the tasks and the list of winners of the final stage of the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.

Problem conditions

Grade 9 1. In the country, some pairs of cities are connected by one-way direct flights (there is at most one flight between any two cities). Let's say that city A is accessible to city B if it is possible to fly from B to A, possibly with transfers. It is known that for any two cities P and Q there is a city R for which both P and Q are accessible. Prove that there is a city for which all cities in the country are accessible. (It is believed that you can fly from the city to the city itself.) V. Dolnikov

2. See problem M2466 “Problem book «Kvant»”.

3. One hundred dwarves, whose weights are 1, 2, 3, ..., 100 pounds, gathered on the left bank of the river. They cannot swim, but on the same shore there is a rowing boat with a carrying capacity of 100 pounds. Because of the current, it is difficult to swim back, so each gnome has enough strength to row from the right bank to the left bank no more than once (it is enough for any of the gnomes to row in a boat; the rower does not change during one trip). Can all the dwarves cross to the right bank? A. Shapovalov, S. Usov
