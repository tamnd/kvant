⟦folio 36⟧

KVANT 2017/№7

⟦column⟧

15. Given a trapezoid ABCD with base AD. The circumcenter of triangle ABC lies on line BD. Prove that the circumcenter of triangle ABD lies on line AC. XIII-З-8–9, E. Bakaev

16. Several points are marked on the plane, and not all of these points lie on the same straight line. A circle is circumscribed around each triangle with vertices at the marked points. Could the centers of all these circles be marked by dots? XIII-Z-8–11, A. Tolesnikov every evening. Could it happen that after some time the police will again occupy points A, B and C (it is known that point X never fell on the side of the triangle)? XII-F-8, V. Protasov

11. In triangle ABC, O is the center of the circumcircle, I is the center of the inscribed circle. A line passing through I and perpendicular to OI intersects AB at point X, and the outer bisector of angle C at point Y. In what ratio does I divide the segment XY? XII-F-9, V. Kalashnikov

12. From the heights of an acute triangle you can form a triangle. Prove that it is also possible to form a triangle from its bisectors. XII-F-9, A. Zaslavsky

13. The Devil invites Man to play the following game. First, the Man pays a certain amount s and names 97 triples [i, j, k], where i, j, k are natural numbers not exceeding 100. Then the Devil draws a convex 100-gon $A_{1} A_{2}$ ... $A_{100}$ with an area equal to 100, and pays the Man a payoff equal to the sum of the areas of 97 triangles $A_{i} A_{j} A_{k}$. At what maximum s is it beneficial for a person to agree? XII-F-10, N. Belukhov (Bulgaria)

14. The circle cuts off four right triangles from rectangle ABCD, the midpoints of the hypotenuses of which are $A_{0}, B_{0}, C_{0}$ and $D_{0}$, respectively. Prove that the segments $A_{0} C_{0}$ and $B_{0} D_{0}$ are equal. XIII-Z-8, L. Shteingarts

17. In triangle ABC, we draw Chevians AA a, BB a and CC a, which intersect at point P. The circumscribed circle of the triangle PA a B a intersects lines AC and BC at points M and N, respectively, and the circumscribed circles of triangles PC a B a and PA a C a re-intersect AC and BC, respectively, at points K and L. Let us draw a straight line c through the midpoints of segments MN and KL. The lines a and b are defined similarly. Prove that lines a, b and c intersect at one point. XIII-З-10–11, D. Prokopenko

18. Given a right triangle ABC and two mutually perpendicular lines x and y passing through the vertex of a right angle A. For a point X moving along a line x, we define $y_{b}$ as the image of the line y with symmetry about XB, and $y_{c}$ as the image of the line y with symmetry about XC. Let $y_{b}$ and $y_{c}$ intersect at point Y. Find the locus of points Y (for non-coinciding $y_{b}$ and $y_{c} ).$ XIII-З-10–11, V.Luchkin, M.Fadin

19. On the diagonal AC of the cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, take an arbitrary point P and from it drop the perpendiculars PK, PL, PM, PN, PO to AB, BC, CD, DA, BD, respectively. Prove that the distance from P to KN is equal to the distance from O to ML. XIII-З-10–11, M. Panov
