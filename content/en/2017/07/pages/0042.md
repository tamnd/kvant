To correct this situation, the Central Subject Methodology Commission (CSMC) in Physics, together with the Talent and Success Foundation, in March 2017 organized preparation for the final stage of the Olympiad for students from “non-top” regions (200 people) at the Sirius Educational Center. Below are the tasks of the theoretical round and the list of winners of the final stage of the Olympiad.

The final stage of the All-Russian Physics Olympiad was held from April 7 to April 13, 2017 in the city of Kazan (Tatarstan) on the basis of the Volga Federal University. 273 schoolchildren took part in it. As for the level of schoolchildren’s preparation for the Physics Olympiad, it remains consistently high. This is evidenced by the fact that with the ever-increasing level of complexity of the final stage tasks, the majority of children successfully cope with them. However, it is worth noting that in the country as a whole there is a sharp differentiation in the level of preparation of students in physics. Thus, a third of the participants in the final stage are Moscow schoolchildren.
