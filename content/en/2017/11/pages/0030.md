2. We use an aircraft cannon

Let's install a 23 mm caliber cannon on an aircraft that has a static ceiling of 15.5 km, a maximum flight speed of 1050 km/h, a cannon projectile mass of 0.174 kg, and an takeoff speed of 700 m/s. And here two scenarios are possible. • The plane begins to fire after exhausting its speed in climb. The simulation results are shown at figure 5. Upward shooting is carried out from a height of 15.5 km. The dashed line shows the calculation results without taking into account aerodynamic drag. As you can see, in order for the projectile to reach the target with zero speed, you can start firing from 12 km. • The aircraft reaches the target, accelerating to a maximum speed of 300 m/s at an altitude of 15 km, and firing is carried out from a half-loop (Fig.6).
