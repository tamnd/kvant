1. Brothers Yoksel and Pixel came up with a highly intellectual game. First, the random number generator produced two integer values ​​from the interval from 0 to 255, let's call them bytes A and B. Next, Yoksel chose one of three bitwise operations - AND, OR, XOR - and performed it on numbers A and B. The result was number C. Then Pixel took number C and rearranged the bits in it as he saw fit. The result of this operation was the number D. And then the brothers performed a bitwise exclusive OR operation on the numbers C and D, let's call the result of this action the number Z. And Pixel wrote down for himself as many points as there are ones in the number Z, and Yoksel - as many as zeros. Due to the brothers' high intelligence, both of them always played optimally. And soon, barely looking at the numbers that fell out, they immediately wrote down the points on the table. How many points will Yoksel write down for himself if the numbers are 129 and 188? 2.
