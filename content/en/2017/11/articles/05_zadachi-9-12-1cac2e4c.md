9. Several guys went into the forest to pick berries. It turned out that everyone picked the same amount of berries. Alyosha found 1/9 of all collected blueberries and 1/11 of all collected lingonberries. The guys did not pick berries of other types. Prove that Alyosha picked as many lingonberries as blueberries. E. Bakaev

10. There are two convex polygons. The first polygon has twice as many acute angles as the second has obtuse angles, and the second has three times as many acute angles as the first has obtuse angles. It is also known that each of them has at least one acute angle and that these polygons (one or both) also have right angles. a) Give an example of how this could be.

b) How many right angles does each of the polygons have? (You need to find all options and prove that there are no others.) I. Akulich

11. It turned out that in a French language group for any two girls, there is exactly one boy whom they both like, and each boy is liked by at least three girls. Give an example of a group in which there is more than one boy. F.Nilov

12. The 12¥12 board is cut into three square corners. Is it possible to say that there is a horizontal or vertical row of cells that intersects with at least: a) 8 corners; b) with 9 corners; c) with 10 corners? S. Kostin
