"QUANT" FOR JUNIOR SCHOOLCHILDREN

Tasks

Young mathematicians Anton, Borya, Vasya, Grisha, whose last names were Antonov, Borisov, Vasiliev and Grigoriev, drank tea at the round table twice. The first time Anton sat opposite Antonov, and Borya sat next to Vasiliev. The second time Grisha sat opposite Borisov, and Vasiliev sat next to Anton. Who has what surname, if it is known that no two young mathematicians have ever sat opposite each other twice? Explain how you got the answer. N. Chernyatyev

2.

The alien met the boy and tried to guess his name. He went through the following options for the name: Bullet, Mouse, Disk, Melon, Linden, Morse, but did not guess. But it turned out that in each of the options he guessed at least one letter (and this letter is in the same place as in the boy’s name, which also consists of 4 letters).

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8. These problems were proposed at the Kostroma regional tournament of young mathematicians.

⟦column⟧

What could this boy's name be? (It is enough to give one suitable name.) E. Bakaev, N. Chernyatyev

3.

Pasha placed 15 pluses and 15 minuses in a circle in some order. Vitya wants to replace some of the signs with opposite ones so that there are no two identical signs next to each other. Prove that in any case he can achieve this by making no more than 14 corrections. N. Chernyatyev

4.

Vera has eight gold balls: four of 1 gram and four of 2 gram. All balls look the same and are indistinguishable to the touch. Vera doesn't remember which one is which. She needs to divide the balls into two piles of equal mass. Vera has at her disposal electronic scales that show the weight of the load that was placed on them. But the battery in the scale is almost dead, and it can only last for 2 weighings. Help Vera cope with the task. N. Chernyatyev
