PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2366 – M2369 were offered at the final stage of the XLIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.

Problem M2466–M2469, F2473–F2476

M2466. Given an isosceles trapezoid ABCD with bases BC and AD (see figure). The circle passes through vertices B and C and secondarily intersects side AB and diagonal BD at points X and Y, respectively. The tangent drawn to the circle at point C intersects the ray AD at point Z. Prove that points X, Y and Z lie on the same straight line. A. Kuznetsov

M2467. a) Let P ( x ) be a polynomial of degree $n \ge 2$ with non-negative coefficients, and a, b and c be the lengths of the sides of some triangle. Prove that the numbers n P

$( a ),^{n} P ( b ) и^{n} P ( c )$ are also the lengths of the sides of some triangle. b) The same problem, where “triangle” is replaced by “acute triangle”. N. Agakhanov

M2468. Each square of the ¥100 board is colored either black or white, with all squares adjacent to the board border being black. It turned out that nowhere on the board there is a single-color checkered square 2 ¥ 2. Prove that there is a checkered square 2 ¥ 2 on the board, the cells of which are colored in a checkerboard pattern. M. Antipov

M2469*. There are n positive numbers $a_{1}, a_{2},^{…} a_{n}$ written out in a row on the board. Vasya wants to write down under each number $a_{i}$ the number $b_{i} \ge a_{i}$ so that for any two of the numbers $b_{1}, b_{2},^{…}, b_{n}$ the ratio of one of them to the other is an integer. Prove that Vasya can write down the required numbers so that the inequality n - 1 holds

$b_{1} b_{2}^{…} b_{n}$ £ $2^{2} a_{1} a_{2}^{…} a_{n}$. F.Petrov

F2473. On the horizontal surface of the table lies an elongated chain with a uniform distribution of mass along its length. The chain is located perpendicular to the edge of the table. The tabletop has a rounded edge with a radius of curvature that is significantly smaller than the length of the chain, but significantly larger than the dimensions of the chain links. Attached to the end of the chain closest to the edge of the table is a weightless thread, holding the chain very tightly
