The first wise man, whether he is a liar or a truthful one, cannot know the answer to the king's question. This means that he told the truth and therefore is not a liar. Thus, three situations are possible: PPL, PLP, PLL (“P” means someone telling the truth, “L” means a liar; it is known that there is at least one liar). From the point of view of the second sage, the second and third situations are indistinguishable from each other (and distinguishable from the first).
