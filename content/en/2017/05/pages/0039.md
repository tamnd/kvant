Sharygin problem

Back in 1985, participants in the XXVI International Mathematical Olympiad were offered the following problem, the author of which was the outstanding geometer Igor Fedorovich Sharygin (1937–2004). Task. Let the circumcircles of the triangles PAD, PBC intersect a second time at the point M, as shown in figure 5. Then $\angle$ PMO $= 90 ^\circ$, where O is zom, it is enough for us to prove that the quadrilateral PMYX is cyclic. Note that $\angle$ MDP $= \angle$ MAP as inscribed angles subtended by one arc MP, and similarly $\angle$ MCP $= \angle$ MBP (Fig.7).
