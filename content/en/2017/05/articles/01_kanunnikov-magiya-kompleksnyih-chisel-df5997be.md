The magic of complex numbers

A.KANUNIKOV

AN incredible number of popular science articles and textbooks have been WRITTEN IN COMPLEX NUMBERS; there have been publications in [4, 5, 6] and in the journal «Kvant». What is the secret of such popularity and why was another article written? The main aspect is scientific: complex numbers are ubiquitous, they are firmly established in various areas of mathematics and physics. The power of the apparatus of complex numbers, the beauty and richness of their applications can already be appreciated by a schoolchild using the example of problems in elementary mathematics. It is also useful and fascinating to study the history of the issue: how complex numbers appeared and developed, how even great minds did not believe in them at first, but over time they got used to them, admiring their amazing power. Very often, the conclusions that scientists came to were ahead of rigorous evidence, sometimes by centuries... This made complex numbers only confirm their reputation as mysterious and mystical, but at the same time it accelerated the construction of a mathematical foundation and the development of new scientific theories. The main purpose of the article is revealed by its title. We want to amaze you, dear reader, by showing several beautiful stories about complex numbers, which at the same time have important scientific significance. The less you know about complex numbers, the more surprising the results will be for you, the stronger the surprise effect will be. Imagine that you have come to a magician's show, and sit back - the rabbits hidden in the hats are eagerly awaiting their appearance and your applause!
