1. At Sheremetyevo Airport, in the long passage between two terminals, there are two travelators parallel to each other - horizontally located escalators of the same length. Schoolboy Vasya, going to the International Physics Olympiad, noticed that the first travelator was moving and the second was turned off, and while waiting for his flight, he decided to have a little fun. He began to run back and forth along the travelators, each time returning to the starting point. For the first time, Vasya ran there on a moving travelator, and back on a stationary one. The second time he ran there on a stationary travelator, and back on a moving one. The difference in Vasya’s movement times in the first and second runs was $\Delta t_{1} = 25$ s. For the third time, Vasya ran in both directions along the moving travelator, and for the fourth time, in both directions along the stationary one. The difference in Vasya’s movement times in the third and fourth runs turned out to be equal to $\Delta t_{2} = 5$ s. Vasya develops a constant speed relative to the travelator, the speed of which is also constant and equal to u = 1 m/s. Find Vasya’s speed v relative to the travelator and determine the length of the travelator. Consider that Vasya spends negligible time on acceleration, braking and turning. M. Zamyatnin

We can assume that children sitting on dad's neck, as well as acceleration and braking occur quickly. A. Bychkov
