PHYSICS

Profile exam

In 2017, the profile exam (additional entrance test) in physics at Moscow State University and its Sevastopol branch was conducted in written form. A typical assignment for an applicant covered all the main sections of the physics program for applicants to Moscow State University: 1) mechanics, 2) molecular physics and thermodynamics, 3) electrodynamics, 4) optics. For each section of the program, the applicant was asked a short theory question and a complementary task. Four astronomical hours were allotted to complete the entire task. Below are examples of tasks for the 2017 profile exam.
