How Busenka lost a culinary competition

K.KOKHAS

-T

SO, THE PARTICIPANT, STRICTLY following the recipe, must “just” cook the porridge,” the Ogryza mouse looked carefully at the audience. – This is what true culinary skill consists of! It seems like everyone is doing the same thing, but for some reason some are doing it very well, while others are just translating products. And now I will introduce you to the participants of the competition: extra-culinary Busenka! - there was friendly applause, - the attorney woodpecker Spyatel!! – the applause turned out to be a little thinner, – and the megaboss chef... Zlobnopotam!!! – the spectators fidgeted restlessly and began to look around, so that if something happened they would know where the shortest path to the exit was. – A leading culinary expert is invited to read the recipe... Ushaya! - Attention, recipe! – said the leading expert, looking at the audience. – To make porridge you need 100 grams

oatmeal, a pinch of salt, a teaspoon of granulated sugar and a little water. Place the oatmeal in a saucepan, add sugar, salt and water, then cook for 15 minutes. “Oinky-kuksy-buksy,” Ushasya suddenly said, turning to the participants for no reason at all. He always said this mantra when he was trying to hypnotize someone. – Try to use as little water as possible. Oatmeal that is too thin tastes disgusting. “I ask the participants to come to the kitchen tables,” Ogryza announced, “you have 20 minutes at your disposal.” Start! The mystery of cooking porridge has begun. Zlobnopotam was fussing the most. He meticulously examined the bowls with the ingredients, sniffed nervously, carefully measured the level of water in the glass, and at the same time continuously clicked the thorns and released rings of pink smoke from his ears. Busenka was also absorbed in the process. It is unknown where she obtained a portable hydraulic press, some pumps and retorts, more appropriate in a chemical laboratory than in a kitchen. The complete opposite of these participants was the woodpecker Spyatel; he seemed to be doing nothing at all, sitting serenely in a chair. The time for the competition was inexorably coming to an end. - Stop! – Ogryza announced. – The creative phase of the competition is over. Let's move on to the tasting part. We have invited discerning connoisseurs of taste to join the jury, I ask them to come to the tables, this is the monster Gargoyle, the cockroach Kuzka and the already familiar Ushasya. Now the participants will take turns presenting to us the results of their activities.
