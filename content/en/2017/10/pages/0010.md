Let's return to the mystery of the meaning of a fairly rapid renewal of various structures of the body. Why is this necessary? It turns out that in cells that “on average” maintain a constant composition, processes of both the creation of new structures and their destruction occur all the time. Otherwise, how can a cell get rid of waste products? Or synthesize new molecules of proteins, fats and carbohydrates? Or transport substances inside the cell? Or even protect the cell from destruction? After all, the processes of creation and destruction of structures do not proceed entirely accurately. Thus, the book by K.P. Ivanov provides data that the synthesis of proteins from amino acids occurs with errors and failures (on average, the error frequency is 3 ◊ $10^{-} 4 ).$ The background of cosmic and terrestrial radiation also leads to failures in the construction of structures. Finally, the thermal movement of molecules also leads to the slow destruction of weak bonds in the molecules that keep them in an unchanged state. The characteristic energy level of these weak bonds is easy to estimate.
