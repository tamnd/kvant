– It seems that the experts at Kashb Delicacy are very fond of baked muesli. But nevertheless, we all think that this porridge is inedible. However, given the extenuating circumstances - the spirit of the task and all that - we give this dish a generous and very good rating: 1 point! Woodpecker Spyatel looked at the jury with dissatisfaction, but did not argue. “Now the jury will evaluate the work of the last participant,” Ogryza announced. - Busenka! The jury was already standing near Busenka’s table and looking at the result. The result of the culinary creativity was a charred brown disk consisting of some kind of stinking baked powder. It didn't look like an edible dish from any point of view. If, when assessing the work of Zlobnopotam, the dominant emotions of the jury were suspicion and disgust, and near the woodpecker Spyatl’s table - bewilderment and disgust, now the dominant emotion was a feeling of cognitive dissonance, and indeed disgust too. “I think one of us is crazy,” muttered Kuzka. “We, domestic insects, often have to deal with crumbs and other dried pieces of food, but this... this...” “I strictly followed the instructions,” Busenka objected. – The fact is that although the cereal presented seemed completely dry, with the help of this wonderful press and this no less wonderful evaporator, I managed to squeeze almost 8 milliliters of water out of it. Thus, I did not add water to the porridge, but on the contrary, I extracted excess water from it, i.e., figuratively speaking, I managed to add a negative amount of water to this cereal! Brilliant, isn't it? “But this is completely inedible,” said Gargoyles sadly, “absolutely, in general, under no circumstances!” “But it fits the formula absolutely perfectly.” The value that your function takes at W = 200 is just a local maximum, it
