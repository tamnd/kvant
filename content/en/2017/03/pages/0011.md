Something about the role of diffusion in life

We did not discuss the role of electrical conductivity and thermal conductivity in life - because it is, at least partially, obvious. The current flows - the light bulb lights up, the elevator moves, the monitor shows pictures. Heat spreads - we become cold or warm. With diffusion, the situation is more complicated - the spread of the smell of perfume and the color of liquid in a glass are incorrect examples, and correct ones are not obvious. Diffusion plays an important role in various fields of science and technology, in many processes occurring in living and inanimate nature. For example, it affects the course of many chemical reactions and processes, evaporation, condensation, crystallization, dissolution, swelling, drying, and combustion. Diffusion affects the operation of nuclear reactors, vacuum tubes and semiconductor devices, batteries and accumulators. And even the work of structures - the creep of metals, changes in the size of parts under load - this is also diffusion. Many technological operations are based on diffusion - sintering of powders, many metal processing processes (nitriding and carburization of steels, homogenization of alloys, welding, removal of certain impurities), leather tanning, dyeing of fibers and fabrics. It is the diffusion of hydrogen through palladium that is used to obtain especially pure hydrogen. Diffusion of gases in metals and dielectrics is important for the operation of vacuum devices for two reasons. Firstly, during operation of the device, gases dissolved in materials diffuse to the surface of the parts, are desorbed from it, enter the vacuum volume and begin to behave badly there: interact with the materials of other parts, and at high concentrations, simply dissipate the electron flow. Secondly, the diffusion of gases from the atmosphere, primarily the leakage of helium through glass and hydrogen through metals, is quite capable of affecting the operation of the device. Diffusion, which in many cases determines the service life of a material and product, is accelerated when the material is exposed to particle flows. Therefore, the interest in it on the part of developers of nuclear and thermonuclear reactors is understandable. Irradiation by particle flows is also used as a tool by chemists - in this case, the question immediately arises about the impact of this irradiation on the materials from which the equipment is made. Everywhere you turn there is either a wedge or diffusion (and the latter is more common). The laws of diffusion govern the processes of migration of elements in the interiors of planets and stars (not to mention the Earth), as well as inside you, dear reader. Many thousands of diffusion processes take place inside you, without some we would not live even a few minutes. Why do you think?
