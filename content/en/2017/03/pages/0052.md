OLYMPICS

Regional stage of the XLIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The regional stage of the XLIII All-Russian Olympiad was held simultaneously in all regions of our country on January 30 and 31, 2017.

Objectives of the Olympiad

9th grade

1. In the product of three natural numbers, each factor was reduced by 3. Could the product increase by exactly 2016? N. Agakhanov, I. Bogdanov

2. Vasya conceived 8 squares of a chessboard, no two of which lie in the same row or in the same column. During a move, Petya places 8 rooks on the board that do not attack each other, and then Vasya points out all the rooks standing on the planned squares. If the number of rooks indicated by Vasya on this move is even (i.e. 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8), then Petya wins; otherwise, all the pieces are removed from the board and Petya makes the next move. In what minimum number of moves can Petya be guaranteed to win? I. Bogdanov

3. See problem M2457 “Problem book «Kvant»”.

4. See problem M2455 “Problem book «Kvant»”.

5. Oleg drew an empty table $50 \times 50$ and wrote a number on top of each column and to the left of each line. It turned out that all 100 written numbers are different, and 50 of them are rational, and the remaining 50 are irrational. Then in each cell of the table he wrote down the sum of the numbers written next to its row and its column (“addition table”). What is the largest number of sums in this table that could be rational numbers? O. Podlipsky

⟦column⟧

6. In an acute triangle ABC, the median AM and height BH are drawn. The perpendicular restored at the point M to the straight line AM intersects the ray HB at the point K. Prove that if $\angle$ MAC $= 30 ^\circ$, then AK = = BC. B.Obukhov

7. See problem M2456 “Problem book «Kvant»”.

8. Initially, 100 cards were placed on the table, each with a natural number written on it; there were exactly 43 cards with odd numbers. Then the next procedure was carried out every minute. For every three cards lying on the table, the product of the numbers written on them was calculated, all these products were added up and the resulting number was written on a new card, which was added to those lying on the table. A year after the start of the process, it turned out that there was a card on the table with a number divisible by $2^{10000}$. Prove that a number divisible by $2^{10000}$ was on one of the cards just one day after the start. I. Bogdanov

10th grade

1. In the product of five natural numbers, each factor was reduced by 3. Could the product increase exactly 15 times? N. Agakhanov, I. Bogdanov

2. A circle with center at point I is inscribed in a quadrilateral ABCD. Rays BA and CD intersect at point P, and rays AD and BC intersect at point Q. It is known that point P lies on the circle $\omega$ circumscribed about triangle AIC. Prove that the point Q also lies on the circle $\omega$. A. Kuznetsov
