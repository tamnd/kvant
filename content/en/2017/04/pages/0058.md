Final stage

The final stage of the Olympiad was scheduled for February 24, 2017. For students of all grades, this stage was conducted face-to-face at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University and at four regional sites in the cities of Astana, Belgorod, Vladivostok and Volgograd. The assignment for students in grades 7–8, as well as grades 9, consisted of four problems on topics studied as part of the physics program for a basic secondary school. The tasks for students in grades 10–11 were compiled in full accordance with the Unified State Examination Codifier 2017
