$2^\circ$ (lemma about the fourth vertex of a parallelogram). If three vertices of a parallelogram are nodes of some lattice, then the fourth vertex is also a node of this lattice. $3^\circ$. If we draw a straight line through arbitrary two lattice nodes Q and R, then this straight line will pass through an infinite number of nodes (see Fig.1). In this case, all distances between neighboring nodes lying on a straight line will be equal. $4^\circ$. If a parallelogram with vertices at the nodes of a certain lattice does not contain other nodes on the sides and inside itself, then it generates this lattice.
