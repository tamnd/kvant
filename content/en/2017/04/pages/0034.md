physics+flora

What we owe to the plant world, i.e. flora: both saturating the air with oxygen and providing shelter and food to the animal world for thousands of years. Humanity has always sought and improved ways of increasingly intensive mastery of nature to satisfy ever-increasing pressing needs. However, not only that, the legendary Newtonian apple became a fruit from the tree of knowledge, a plant symbol of the desire to penetrate into the secrets of the structure and functioning of the world around us. Of course, physics - both on its own and in collaboration with other natural sciences - has contributed to unraveling the mysteries of nature hidden in the inhabitants of forests, fields, rivers and seas. Scientists were driven by both curiosity and an ever-increasing concern for the preservation of the conditions of our existence, including flora. Perhaps you will find these problems reflected in today’s selection of “plant” stories.
