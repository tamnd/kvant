Oral round for 11th grade

1. On the first day $2^{n}$ schoolchildren played ping-pong to death: first two played, then the winner played with the third, the winner of this pair played with the fourth, etc., until the last student played (there are no draws in ping-pong). On the second day, the same schoolchildren played for the cup: first, they randomly split into pairs and played in pairs, the losers dropped out, and the winners again randomly split into pairs and played in pairs, etc. It turned out that the sets of pairs playing on the first and second days were the same (perhaps the winners were different). Find the largest possible value of n. B.Frenkin

2. The sphere touches 99 edges of some convex 50-gonal pyramid. Does it then necessarily touch the 100th edge of this pyramid? M. Evdokimov

3. See problem M2459 “Problem book «Kvant»”.

⟦column⟧

4. The squares of the board $100 \times 100$ are colored black and white in a checkerboard pattern. Is it possible to recolor exactly 2018 different squares of this board in the opposite color so that each row and column have the same number of black squares? Yu. Chekanov

5. Given triangle XBC. The distinct points $A_{H}, A_{I}, A_{M}$ are such that X is the orthocenter of triangle $A_{H} BC$, the incircle center of triangle $A_{I} BC$, and the intersection point of the medians of triangle $A_{M} BC$. Prove that if $A_{H} A_{M}$ and BC are parallel, then $A_{I}$ is the midpoint of $A_{H} A_{M}$. E. Bakaev

6. For what natural numbers n is the following statement true: for an arbitrary polynomial P of degree n with integer coefficients, there are distinct natural numbers a and b such that P ( a ) + P ( b ) is divisible by a + b? G. Zhukov

XXV International Olympiad “Intellectual Marathon”

International Intelligence Club (IIC) “Gluon” within the framework of the international program “Children. Intelligence. Creativity" with the participation of M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University, the Institute of Pedagogical Research of Giftedness of the Russian Academy of Education (Novosibirsk) and with the support of the International Association "Pedagogy of Giftedness and Talent", the 1C Foundation and the magazines «Kvant», "Potential" and "Physics for Schoolchildren" held the XXV International Test-Rating Olympiad "Intellectual marathon." The Olympics took place from October 2 to October 9, 2016 in the city of Protvino, Moscow Region, at the Institute of High Energy Physics. Participants from different cities of Russia came to the Olympiad. Gifted schoolchildren in grades 9–11 who showed interest in fundamental sciences competed in team and individual rounds in mathematics, physics, and the history of scientific ideas and discoveries. Teachers and psychologists gathered for their scientific session for the eighth time. This time, students from Lyceum 2 from the city of Almetyevsk (Tatarstan) won the individual competition. The absolute winner of the Olympiad was Eduard Sabirov, he was awarded a large gold medal, a gold medal in physics and a silver medal in mathematics. The second prize-winner in the overall standings was Ruslan Kamalov, who received a large silver medal and a small gold medal in mathematics. Azat Yamanaev won a large bronze medal; he also received a small bronze medal in physics.
