In the final position, all the rooks do not attack each other, and this happens if and only if s = 20. Let us prove that for s < 20 it is always possible to make a move that increases s. It will follow from this that in no more than 20 – 7 = 13 moves we will obtain a rook arrangement that satisfies the condition. Let's look at all the rooks that don't beat anyone. We cross out the rows containing such rooks. Let's say that there is an uncrossed row with exactly one rook, let it be a row. There are at least two rooks in the column in which this rook is located, which means there is an empty column. Then the rook can be moved into it. In this case, s increases. It remains to consider the option when there is no such series. Then all uncrossed lines are either empty or have at least two rooks, and both lines are present. Consider an empty line and a line containing at least two rooks, such that there are no uncrossed lines between them. Then let's move the rooks from one of these rows to another. It is easy to understand that such a move can be made and s increases.
