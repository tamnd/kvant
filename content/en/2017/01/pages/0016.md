4. Prove analogues of Theorems 1 and 2 for parallelepipeds with vertices at the nodes of the three-dimensional lattice $Z^{3}$. (In fact, they are also true for the n-dimensional lattice $Z^{n}$; to prove it, you need to construct a theory of systems of linear equations and determinants, which is what they do in the first year of mathematics departments. But it is useful to analyze the case n = 3 independently during school years.) Note that a three-dimensional analogue of Pick’s formula does not exist; see [2, p.58].
