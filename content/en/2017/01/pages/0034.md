COMPETITION NAMED AFTER A.P. SAVINA

Tasks

We continue the competition for solving mathematical problems. They are aimed primarily at students in grades 7–9, but we welcome students of all ages to participate. Send solutions to the problems you can solve by email to: savin.contest@gmail.com or by regular mail to: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant» (marked “A.P. Savin Competition”). In addition to your first and last name, indicate the city, school and class in which you study, as well as your return postal address. We welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by teams (in this case, one work is sent with a list of participants). You can participate starting from any tour. The winners will receive diplomas from the magazine «Kvant» and prizes. We wish you success!

14. In a convex quadrilateral ABCD – $A = 30 \infty$, the perimeter of triangle BCD is equal to the length of the diagonal AC. Find – S. E. Bakaev, V. Rastorguev

15. Petya drew a table of 10 ¥ 10 and filled all its cells with pluses. Then he did a few things according to the following rule. With each action, he randomly selected a row (row or column) and changed each of its 10 signs to the opposite (plus for minus, minus for plus). When Petya left after this, the bully Vasya erased some of the signs - only N signs remained. For what smallest N can it turn out that Petya’s resulting table can be uniquely reconstructed from the remaining data? E. Bakaev
