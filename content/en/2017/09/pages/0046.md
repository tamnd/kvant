In conclusion, let's try to solve another rather difficult problem. Problem 4 (Iranian Geometry Olympiad, 2014). Let H be the base of the altitude from vertex A of triangle ABC and H a ​​be the point symmetrical to point H relative to the middle of side BC (Fig. 19,a). Tangents to the circumcircle of triangle ABC, drawn at points B and C, intersect at point X. A perpendicular to XH a, drawn at point H a, intersects lines AB and AC at points Y and Z, respectively. Prove that YXB ZXC. Solution. Let's consider the case of an acute triangle (the reader can analyze other cases in a similar way). Since BX and CX are tangents, we have CBX BCX A, whence BXC 180 o 2 A. Let XYZ J, XZY Q. To solve it, it is enough to prove that YXZ 2 A or, equivalently, J Q 2 A.
