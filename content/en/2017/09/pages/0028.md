1. Smart Rabbit has an 8¥8 square plot of 64 identical 1¥1 beds. He grows cabbage in some of the beds and carrots in the rest (there are no empty beds). It is known that next to each cabbage bed there are exactly two cabbage beds, and next to each carrot bed there are exactly two carrot beds (the beds are adjacent if they are adjacent on the side). Can the share of cabbage beds be: a) exactly half, b) more than 60% of the total number of beds? M. Evdokimov

4. A million numbers are written out in a row: the first number is 123456789, and 999999 numbers are zeros. During a move, you are allowed to choose a number if it is at least 10 less than the one adjacent to the left, and increase it by 1, and reduce all numbers to the left by 9.
