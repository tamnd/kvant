Total pedal circumference

Let's return to studying the properties of angle isogonals. Let AP and AQ is an isothermal angle BAC. Property 3. The projections of the points P and Q onto the sides of the angle AB and AC lie on the same circle (Fig.10), and the center of this circle is the midpoint of the segment PQ.

Exercise 8. Prove this property.

We are now ready to prove the main theorem of this section.

Theorem 2. The pedal circles of isogonally conjugate points P and Q coincide. Proof. By property 3, the projections of the points P and Q onto the lines AB and BC lie on the same circle (Fig.11). The same is true for the projections of points P and Q onto lines AB and AC. But these two circles have in common
