Unified State Exam in Physics

The structure of the Unified State Exam options in physics in 2017 was significantly different from the options of the last two years. Each option contained 26 tasks with only the answer checked and 5 tasks with the solution checked (with a detailed answer). Tasks with a choice of one answer out of four have completely disappeared. For 17 tasks out of 26, you could get a maximum of 1 primary point, for 9 tasks out of 26, a maximum of 2 points, and for the last 5 tasks with a detailed answer, a maximum of 3 points. Below are one complete version and several selected problems of the open segment of the Unified State Exam in Physics 2017.

Option 1

Instructions for completing the work To complete the examination work in physics, 3 hours 55 minutes (235 minutes) are allotted. The work consists of 2 parts, including 31 tasks. In problems 1–4, 8–10, 14, 15, 20, 24–26, the answer is a whole number or a finite decimal fraction. Write the number in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the example below into answer form No. 1. There is no need to write units of measurement of physical quantities.

The answer to tasks 19 and 22 are two numbers. Write your answer in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the example below, without separating the numbers with a space, into answer form No. 1.

The answer to tasks 27–31 includes a detailed description of the entire progress of the task. In answer form No. 2, indicate the task number and write down its complete solution. When making calculations, it is allowed to use a non-programmable calculator. All Unified State Exam forms are filled out in bright black ink. You can use gel, capillary or fountain pens. When completing assignments, you can use a draft. Entries in the draft are not taken into account when grading work. The points you receive for completed tasks are summed up. Try to complete as many tasks as possible and score the most points. We wish you success!

Below is reference information that you may need when performing the work.
