Tasks

M756—M760; F768—F772

M756. In the country, besides the capital, there are more than 100 cities. The capital of the country is connected by air lines with 100 cities; Each of the remaining cities is connected by air line to 10 cities worldwide. It is known that from any city you can (perhaps with transfers) fly to any other. Prove that it is possible to close half of the airlines flying from the capital, so that it is possible to get from any city to any other.

M757. From the sequence 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, ... it is not difficult to isolate an arithmetic progression of length three: 1/2, 1/3, 1/6. Is it possible to select an arithmetic progression from this sequence?
a) length 4?
b) length 5?
c) length k, where k is any natural number?

G. Galperin

M758*. What is the smallest number of numbers that must be removed from the sequence 1, 2, 3, ... 1982 so that none of the remaining numbers is equal to the product of the other two remaining numbers? (9)

L. Kurlyandchik

M759. Inside a convex quadrilateral for which the sum of six pairwise distances between the vertices (that is, the sum of the lengths of all sides and diagonals) is equal to $S_1$, there is another for which this sum is equal to $S_2$.
a) Can the value $S_2$ be greater than $S_1$?
b) Prove that $S_2 < 4S_1/3$.
c) Prove that if inside an arbitrary tetrahedron with the sum of edge lengths $S_1$ there is another one for which this sum is equal to $S_2$, then $S_2 < 4S_1/3$. (10)

P. Gusyatnikov
