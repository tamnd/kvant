F772. The interplanetary spacecraft made a soft landing on the Moon. The ship has the shape of a disk of radius $r=4$ m; its surface is covered with black (non-reflective) paint. Is it possible to detect the application of a spacecraft using the world's largest Soviet television scale, the BTA, with an object diameter of $D=6$ m, if a photoplastic is installed in the focal plane and the area of ​​the lunar surface in which the expected application site is located is formatted?

Please note that the reliably distinguishable structure of an image on photographic plastic (that is, the minimum relative difference in illumination of the light and dark parts of the image) is equal to $k=0,05$.

Distance from Earth to Moon $L=4 \cdot 10^5$ km; photography is carried out in light with a wavelength of $\lambda=0,6$ µm. To estimate at what size the letters laid out by astronauts on the surface of the Moon can be read when observed from Earth using the BTA teleweight. (10)

S. Kozel

M756. There are more than 100 cities in a certain country, not counting the capital. The latter is joined by direct flights to 100 cities; each of the remaining cities is joined by direct flights to exactly 10 cities. It is known that one can fly from any city to any other (eventually, with transfers). Prove that half the airlines flying from the capital can be closed down so that it will still be possible to fly from any city to any other.

M757. It is easy to see that the sequence 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, ... contains a three-term subsequence which is an arithmetical progression: 1/2, 1/3, 1/6. Is it possible to find another subsequence, which is also an arithmetical progression,

a) of length 4?
b) of length 5?
c) of length $k$, where $k$ is any natural number?

G. Galperin
