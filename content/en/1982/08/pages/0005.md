The field of mathematical calculations is included, for example, in modern medicine and linguistics in full. How can it be included in human culture!

I would like to dwell on the following question: how does mathematics affect the development of human personality? This question is relevant because mathematics is an academic subject that is given extremely great importance at all levels of general education. In both algebra and geometry, students are taught what I might call technical human thinking. Well, you can simply say: I’m thinking. Because nowhere, perhaps, does human thinking appear with such strength and such brightness as in mathematics. This has always been recognized by all major thinkers. It is not for nothing that Plato, at the entrance to his Academy, which was supposed to unite all forms of human culture, human creativity, wrote: “Let no one who does not know geometry enter here.” Plato believed that geometry is the main step on the path to philosophical thinking. Now we don’t talk about geometry so categorically. There are probably many different paths that lead the thinking man into the temple. But that mathematics undoubtedly has one of the most important places in the development of the human mind and human creativity seems completely indisputable.

But this is only one side of the influence of mathematics on the formation of a person of personality. There is another side.

It is believed that mathematical statements have never been and are not

are controversial. In this state, mathematics has a very special position among other theoretical sciences. It never occurs to anyone to ask whether such and such a mathematical theorem is true.

I say that this occurs to mathematicians themselves, because the concept of mathematical truth is not as simple and not as unambiguous as we are accustomed to believe, based on school mathematics. When we come to the highest chapters of mathematics, we see that there are also a number of certain truths that seem completely indisputable in other areas of human knowledge. For example, there is the law of excluded middle: of statements A and (not A), one and only one is true: either statement A or its negation; nothing third is given. So in mathematics, this seemingly basic foundation of all knowledge is called into question. In mathematics, a lot can be said about the law of excluded middle, and the question of the applicability of this law is one of the big mathematical problems.

So you see: all this horizon is more complex and, I hope, more interesting than it seems at first glance. In mathematics there is a lot that is indisputable, and a lot that is controversial. In other words, mathematics is not, so to speak, a concluded formula for human thinking. This is human thinking, which is still in the stage of searching, and searching related to the most fundamental, the most important and the most difficult aspects of mental activity.
