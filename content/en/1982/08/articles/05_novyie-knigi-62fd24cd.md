Proof of Theorem 3. It is enough to build a child on our island such that its vertices are at the junction points of the boundaries, of which there is exactly one on each boundary (the boundaries with the sea can be considered inside the island). This can be done this way: place the sides of the tower in the shape of the Eiffel Tower at the points where the boundaries meet; build fortification walls on the borders, lowering towards the middle of each border and gently sloping towards each of the two countries that will divide this border; finally, dig a basin in each country, and the inclined basins should open gently to the fortress walls. An example also for the map figure 13 is shown in figure 14. Now it remains to use Theorem 1, and Theorem 3 is proven.

Consequence. If a political map with at least 3 countries is drawn on the sphere, satisfying conditions 6) and c) of Theorem 3, then for it

C+B—G=2.

Proof. You can consider one of the countries to be a sea, and the rest to be an island. then Theorem 3 applies, immediately leading to the required result.

Proof of Theorem 2 (for any convex polyhedron). Let's take point O inside the polyhedron under consideration and draw some sphere of large radius with the center at point O. Now we project our polyhedron from point O onto the sphere. then we will obtain a political map on the sphere (the faces of the polyhedron are projected into countries, and the edges into borders), to which the previous corollary is applicable.

New books

The Ukrainian publishing house “Vishcha Shkola” publishes in the 1st quarter of 1983 the book “Mathematics Today” (a popular science collection in Russian, edited by Prof. A. Ya. Dorogovtsev).

The collection is intended for university students and teachers, but will undoubtedly be interesting and useful for high school students and secondary school teachers. It includes articles on current trends in mathematics, a section of unsolved problems, articles on student olympids in mathematics, and new problems.

The collection is released in limited quantities, so we recommend that you place an order for it in a timely manner. Orders must be sent to the “Book - by mail” store at the address: 252117, Kyiv, st. Popudrenko, 26.
