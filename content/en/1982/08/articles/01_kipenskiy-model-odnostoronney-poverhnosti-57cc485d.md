The middle figure shows a model of a one-sided surface, which differs from the well-known (see, for example, «Kvant», 1978, no. 6 and 1979, no. 1) Möbius strip. It is made from three cylindrical strips of paper glued together in series (see top and middle figure). The fact that the surface is one-sided can be seen from the middle figure: traversing from the red point along the blue line

returns to this point on the other side of the sheet of paper, although the line does not go through the edges. Interestingly, if the surface is cut along the three red lines, it is divided into two mirror-symmetrical parts; one of them is shown at the bottom figure. We advise you to make these models and tinker with them (in particular, cut them along the blue lines).
