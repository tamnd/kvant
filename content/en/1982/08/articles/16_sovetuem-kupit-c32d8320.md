Why is this so?

It is known that the area of ​​a sphere is taken to be an arbitrary function $V(R)$ over $R$ (“Geometry 9–10”, § 69):

$$S = \left(\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3\right)' = 4\pi R^2.$$

A similar definition can be given for the area of ​​the lateral surface of a cylinder:

$$S = (\pi R^2 H)' = 2\pi RH.$$

However, if a similar definition is given for the area of ​​the lateral surface of a cone, the result will be incorrect:

$$\left(\frac{1}{3}\pi R^2 H\right)' = \frac{2}{3}\pi RH \neq \pi Rl.$$
