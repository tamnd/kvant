⟦folio 53⟧

6. Prove that for any natural number $n$ and any real number $a$ the following inequality holds:

$$|a| \cdot |a-1| \cdot \ldots \cdot |a-n| \geqslant \frac{n!}{2^n},$$

where $\langle a \rangle$ is the distance from the number $a$ to the nearest integer, $n! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot \ldots \cdot n$.

(Yu. Nesterenko, A. Gelfond, Moscow)

7. a) Are there polynomials $P = P(x, y, z)$, $Q = Q(x, y, z)$, $R = R(x, y, z)$ in the variables $x, y, z$ such that then there is

$$\begin{align*}
(x-y+1)^3 \cdot P + (y-z-1)^3 \cdot Q + \\
(z-2x+1)^3 \cdot R = 1
\end{align*}$$

6) Same question for then there is

$$\begin{align*}
(x-y+1)^3 \cdot P + (y-z-1)^3 \cdot Q + \\
(z-x+1)^3 \cdot R = 1.
\end{align*}$$

(P. Gusyatnikov, Y. Nesterenko, Moscow)

8. The vertices of a terzer KLMN lie inside, on the faces or on the edges of another terzer ABCD. Prove that the sum of the lengths of all edges of the terzer KLMN is less than 4/3 of the sum of the lengths of all edges of the terzer ABCD.

(P. Gusyatnikov, Y. Nesterenko, Moscow)

I would like to note those tenth-graders who have become winners for the third time. These are G. Perelman (received diplomas of II, I and I degrees in grades 8, 9 and 10), A. Spiak (diplomas III, II and I), S. Matyupov (I, III, II), K. Matveev (II, II, II), S. Samborsky (III, II, II), V. Titenko (I, II, II), A. Savkin (II, II, III), V. Shevchishin (II, I, III). These guys were among the candidates for the USSR team at the XXIII International Schoolchildren Olympiad (about this Olympiad, held in Hungary in July 1982, see «Kvant» No. 12).

Along with the main prizes, the honorees also received special prizes - for original solutions to problems, for the best results of the first day, for the will to win. A special prize was awarded to the best group of girls - eighth-grader A. Bogomolnaya from school No. 534 in Leningrad, who received a 2nd degree diploma. Prize «Kvant» - a file of numbers for 1981 - was awarded to eighth-grader Bulat Orazbekov from the school named after. Lunacharsky Kirov district of Taldy-Kurgan region of the Kazakh SSR. A subscription to «Kvant» for 1983 will be received by Sardon Gulomnabev from the school village. Shutnana, Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region, Tajik SSR (8th grade) and Taras Kitsmet from the school village of Gorokholno, Ivano-Frankovsk Region, Ukrainian SSR (9th grade).

The hero city of Odeska warmly welcomed its guests - the participants of the Olympiad. During the tours, in the evenings the guys went to the famous Odessa theaters - opera and ballet, musical comedy. Traditional suppers of other peoples of the USSR took place in various schools of Odessa, excursions around the city “Odeska - a hero city”, “Odeska - a feat”, “Literature Odessa”, an excursion by sea to the port of Ilchevsk. Like everyone else in our country, on April 17, Olympiad participants worked at the All-Union Leninist Communist Community Cleanup Day - planting trees in the beautiful park of the Young Guard pioneer camp.

On April 19, a “mathematical battle” took place between a team of schoolchildren and a jury team. The battle was stubborn and honorable - suffice it to say that before the final competition for captains the score was equal. The last match decided everything: the captain of the schoolchildren, Grisha Perelman, nevertheless lost to the captain of the team of jury members S.V. Konyagin (two-time winner of the national mathematical Olympiads, now a teacher at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, the author of many problems from the Problem Book «Kvant»). As the chief judge, I assure you that I did not help the jury members at all - rather, on the contrary!

On April 20, the closing ceremony of the Olympiad took place at the Pioneer Palace of the Young Guard pioneer camp. Professor M. I. Yadrenko addressed the participants of the Olympiad with a welcoming speech. Academician A. N. Kolmogorov gave parting words to both the winners of the Olympiad and those who did not receive the prize. The heads of the jury presented prizes, diplomas and certificates to the participants. The symbol of the Olympiad was handed over to a representative of the city of Kshinev, where the final round of the next, XVII Olympiad will take place.
