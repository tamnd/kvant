⟦folio 34⟧

M749*. a) Prove that if $x_1, x_2, x_3$ are positive numbers, then
$$\frac{x_1}{x_2+x_3} + \frac{x_2}{x_3+x_1} + \frac{x_3}{x_1+x_2} \geq \frac{3}{2};$$
Under what conditions does this inequality turn into equality?
b) Prove that if $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n (n \geq 4)$ are positive numbers, then
$$\frac{x_1}{x_2+x_n} + \frac{x_2}{x_3+x_1} + \dots + \frac{x_{n-1}}{x_n+x_{n-2}} + \frac{x_n}{x_1+x_{n-1}} \geq 2,$$
Moreover, equality is possible only for $n=4$.
c) Prove that for $n>4$ the inequality in part b) is exact in the sense that for no $n$ the number 2 on the right side cannot be replaced by a larger one.

a) Let $a=x_2+x_3, b=x_3+x_1, c=x_1+x_2$. Then $x_1 = \frac{b+c-a}{2}, x_2 = \frac{a+c-b}{2}, x_3 = \frac{a+b-c}{2}$, and the left side of the inequality will be rewritten as follows:
$$\frac{b+c-a}{2a} + \frac{a+c-b}{2b} + \frac{a+b-c}{2c} =$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{b}{a} + \frac{a}{b} \right) + \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{c}{a} + \frac{a}{c} \right) + \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{b}{c} + \frac{c}{b} \right) - \frac{3}{2}.$$
Each of the brackets in this expression is not less than 2 due to the well-known inequality $x + \frac{1}{x} \geq 2$ for $x>0$. Therefore, the entire left side is at least 3—$\frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$. And since $x + \frac{1}{x} = 2$ only for $x=1$, the proven inequality turns into equality only for $a=b=c$.
b) Let us prove the induction inequality on $n$. For $n=4$ it is obvious:
$$\frac{x_1}{x_2+x_4} + \frac{x_2}{x_3+x_1} + \frac{x_3}{x_4+x_2} + \frac{x_4}{x_1+x_3} =$$
$$= \frac{x_1+x_3}{x_2+x_4} + \frac{x_2+x_4}{x_1+x_3} \geq 2;$$
equality is possible if and only if $x_1+x_3=x_2+x_4$.
Let us now prove the inequality for arbitrary positive numbers $x_1, \dots, x_{n+1}$, assuming that it is valid for any $n (n \geq 4)$ positive numbers. Let's choose the smallest number $x_1, \dots, x_{n+1}$. Since they enter into the inequality symmetrically, we can, without loss of generality, assume that this is $x_{n+1}$. Then $x_{n+1}>0$, $x_{n+1} \leq x_n$ and $x_{n+1} \leq x_1$, and therefore
$$\frac{x_1}{x_2+x_{n+1}} + \frac{x_2}{x_3+x_{n+1}} + \dots + \frac{x_n}{x_{n+1}+x_{n-1}} + \frac{x_{n+1}}{x_1+x_{n}} > \frac{x_1}{x_2+x_n} + \frac{x_2}{x_3+x_1} + \dots + \frac{x_n}{x_1+x_{n-1}} \geq 2$$
(the last inequality is satisfied by the induction hypothesis). Along the way, we find that for $n>4$ equality is inequality.
