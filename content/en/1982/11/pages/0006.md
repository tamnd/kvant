Science, including mathematics, was able to solve a number of pressing problems of the country’s defense: theories were created that made it possible to develop actionable measures to combat the phenomena of “shimmy” and “corkscrew” in aviation, statistical methods for quality control of industrial products and statistical methods for quality management were proposed, methods for obtaining greater accuracy of the boom on fast moving objects, and much more. By the end of the war, it was necessary to urgently develop the theory of shaped charges, ballistic missiles, nuclear weapons, and modern computing facilities. Mathematicians took an active part in solving all these problems.

The end of the war raised urgent problems of restoring the national economy and its further development on a new production base. The practical requirements for mathematics grew literally every day, especially due to the fact that computer technology required the creation and development of new branches of mathematical science - programming, logic, automata theory.

Soviet mathematics has a large number of examples of the creation of general theories that entered the active circles of science and transformed the face of large scientific trends. It is enough to recall the theory of random processes, which was born from the needs of statistical physics, telephony, meteorology and biology. Now this theory is the main direction in the development of probability theory and a powerful tool in solving problems of natural science, technology, communication theory, radio engineering and many other areas of knowledge. Let us also point out the theory of optimal control, which is turning into a large branch of modern mathematics and at the same time being a powerful tool for applied research. It is appropriate to remember that a new direction of research, a better name for linear programming, was born in our country.

How many of us imagined in the pre-war period that in just fifteen years mankind would invent electronic computers? Soviet science added that in a short period of time new computer programming methods were proposed, and many thousands of specialists were convinced of their power of calculations and for producing a logical analysis of complex situations. Currently, all allied resources have not only powerful computers, but also a cadre of well-trained specialists capable of creatively examining them.

We can be proud that in the 65 years that have passed since the Great October Socialist Revolution, and in the 60 years of the existence of the Soviet Union, all resource-based mathematical scientific schools have been created.

Now the Soviet Union faces another task - to increase the pace of scientific progress and educate the younger generation in the spirit of creative quest, in the spirit of the ability and unlimited power of human development. But for this, we need to teach schoolchildren to learn not to memorize, but to delve into the meaning of what they are studying and use what they have learned for active application. Next, you should get used to independently searching for solutions to complex problems, so that the creature gets used to independent thinking and a critical approach to the problem and proposed solutions. Only creative search in studies and at the first independent steps in science allows the young generation of mathematicians to contribute to increasing the pace of scientific progress in our country.
