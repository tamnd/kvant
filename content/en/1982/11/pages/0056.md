Physics Olympiad

The final stage of the 16th All-Scholastic Olympiad in Physics for schoolchildren, dedicated to the 60th anniversary of the formation of the USSR, took place from April 14 to 21 in the capital of Soviet Azerbaijan, order-bearing Baku.

143 representatives of all allied resource workers took part in a very serious contemporary event. Among them are the winners of this year’s Resource Olympids and those recognized by the previous All-Olympics, who received diplomas of I and II degrees.

On the morning of April 15, Olympiad participants laid wreaths at the monument to V.I. Lenin and the memorial of the 26 Baku Commissars, after which in the assembly hall of the Azerbaijan Institute of Petroleum and Chemistry named after. M. Azizbekov, the grand opening of the Olympids took place. The Minister of Education of the Az. SSR E. M. Kafarov, the representative of the Jorni of the All-Oplympids, the corresponding member of the Academy of Sciences of the Az. SSR A. I. Mukhtarov and the deputy representative of the Jorni, Professor S. M. Kozel, addressed the audience with attractive words. Rector of the Institute of Petroleum and Chemistry, Academician of the Academy of Sciences of Az. SSR I. A. Ibragimov spoke about the weak history of the institute, the first higher educational institution in Europe and Asia, which began training engineers for all branches of the oil industry. It was said with pride that the outstanding physicist, Lenin Prize laureate Academician I.V. Kurchatov, Academician L.S. Leibenzon and Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences K.A. Kraussky worked within the walls of this institute; Among the graduates of the institute are Lenin Prize laureate, representative of the State Planning Committee of the USSR N.K. Baibakov, Hero of Socialist Labor, Lenin Prize laureate, Minister of Gas Industry S.A. Orudzhev and Hero of the Soviet Union, pilot-cosmonaut of the USSR V.M. Zholobov.

In the evening of the same day, the guests attended a performance at the Opera and Ballet Theater. M. F. Akhundova.

On April 16, the theoretical round of the Olympids was held. Eighth-class students were asked to solve four problems (4 hours were allotted for work), ninth-class and tenth-class students were asked to solve five problems each (time was given 5 hours). Let us present the conditions of the problem (most of them are included in the “Quenta” Problem Book in issues 7 and 8 of the magazine for this year).
