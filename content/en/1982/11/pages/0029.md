A. Anjan

F783. A horizontally located weightless rod of length $3l$ with weights with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ attached to it is held in an equilibrium position by means of two vertical threads (Fig. 1). S. Krotov

F784. A constant force $F$ begins to act on a body moving at a constant speed $v$. After time $t$ the speed of the body became equal to $v/2$. Over the next similar time interval, the speed decreased by another half. Determine what the speed of the body will be after a time interval $3t$ from the beginning of the force $F$.

K. Serdeev

F786*. In a neutral monatomic gas, attractive forces act between the molecules. V. Tugushchev
