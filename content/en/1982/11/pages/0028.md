M771 — M775; F783 — F787

M771. In triangle ABC, a bisector AK is drawn. It is known that the centers of the circles inscribed in triangle ABK and circumscribed about triangle ABC coincide. Find the angles of triangle ABC.

M772. The workshop has five different machines. Training one worker to operate one machine costs 1,000 rubles. With each minimum cost, can 8 workers be trained so that, in the absence of any three of them, all the machines can be used simultaneously? Each worker can only work on one machine at a time.

M773. The incircle of triangle ABC touches its sides AB, BC and AC at points M, N and P respectively. It is known that
$$\overrightarrow{AN} + \overrightarrow{BP} + \overrightarrow{CM} = \overrightarrow{0}.$$
Prove that triangle ABC is regular.
P. Gusyatnikov, S. Reznitsenko
