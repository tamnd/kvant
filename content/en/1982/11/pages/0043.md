⟦folio 41⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Quests

1. In each horizontal row, rearrange one match so that all six equalities are true.

2. Two knots are tied on a stretchable rope tied to two opposite walls of the room. Is it possible to swap knots without untying or tearing the rope?

3. Recreate the example for addition (different square figures correspond to different numbers, identical figures correspond to the same ones).

4. Vitya told his friend Kolya: “I came up with an example of division in which the dividend, divisor, quotient and remainder end in 1, 3, 5 and 7, respectively.” After thinking, Kolya replied: “You’re confusing something.” Is Kolya right?

These tasks were offered to us
N. Aktonovich, A. Lexis, D. Plakhta,
S. Savastok
