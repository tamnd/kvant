⟦folio none⟧

On this figure our artist managed to turn the chessboard inside out. To do this, he used the inversion transformation of the relative red circle. (The relative circle inversion (O; R) is a transformation of the plane that takes the point $M \neq O$ to the point $M'$ on the ray $OM$, satisfying the relation $|OM| \cdot |OM'| = R^2$. You can read more about this remarkable transformation in «Kvant», 1977, No. 6, p. 38.)

The attentive reader will notice that the chessboard depicted here consists of 49 squares (instead of 64) - this results in a configuration with large axes of symmetry.
