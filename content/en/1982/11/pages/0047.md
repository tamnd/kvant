Take your finger off the answer and thereby allow the air to escape, so that the water quickly rises in the drain to the level of the surrounding water.

This experience should explain to you how people can stay and work under water in a diving bell or inside those wide pipes called caissons. Water does not penetrate inside a diving bell or caisson for the same reason that it does not flow under the glass in our experiment.

Heavy newspaper. “Pick up the newspaper,” my older brother once told me in those years of my youth, when physics was still completely unfamiliar to me. — The newspaper is very light, isn’t it? And you thought, of course, that you could always lift it with at least one finger. But now you will see that the same newspaper can make it very, very heavy. Give me that drawing ruler over there.

“It’s chopped up, it’s no good.”
- So much the better: it won’t be a shame if it breaks.
The brother put the ruler on the table so that part of it was high over the edge.
- Touch me on the high end. Easy to tilt, right? Well, then...

try to tilt it when I cover the other half with newspaper.

He spread the newspaper onto the table, carefully straightening its folds and covering his ruler.
- Take a stick and hit the protruding part of the ruler hard. Strike with all your might.
“I’ll hit you so hard that the ruler will hit the ceiling!” - I exclaimed, waving.
- The main thing is to spare no effort...
