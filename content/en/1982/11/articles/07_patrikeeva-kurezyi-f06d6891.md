To explain this phenomenon, we will cite an excerpt from Ya. E. Geguzin’s book “The Drop” (M., “Nauka”, 1973), which, by the way, is highly recommended reading:

“The following will happen to the drop. Water evaporates evenly from its entire surface. It is easy to understand that as the moisture evaporates, the concentration of dissolved salt will increase and, first of all, crystals will begin to fall out where the excess salt concentration is greatest. This will be in the thinnest part of the drop, that is, along its perimeter. It is along the perimeter that the crystals will fall out. The liquid drop, wetting the falling crystals, seems to stick to them. Therefore, a drop, losing liquid, must change its shape, become flatter - after all, its volume decreases, but its perimeter remains unchanged.

At the beginning of the drying process, the shape of the drop lying on the glass was in equilibrium. This means that of all possible forms, the one in which the energy of the surface bordering the impact and the glass is the smallest for a given volume of the drop is realized...

The more moisture evaporates, the more the droplet shape evaporates, and at some stage the droplet will break away from the crystals at the perimeter and take on an equilibrium shape. And then - all over again... until the drop evaporates, leaving behind a spot, a state of concentric wheels of salt crystals.”

Curiosities

The following sayings were successful from students in grades 9-10.

1. A vacuum diode is a diode placed in a vacuum.
2. A voltmeter serves as a device to regulate the current strength in a circuit.
3. Uniformly accelerated motion is a motion in which a body increases by equal amounts over equal periods of time.
4. With the photoelectric effect, if the electric charges are negatively charged, then they escape from the metal; if they are positively charged, then the field holds them.
5. The Sun is a hot planet, located 100 light years from Earth.
6. The period of oscillations of the mathematical matrix $T = 2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$ when moving from Moscow to the Northern lane will change because the number of l will change.
7. Infrared rays are invisible red rays.
8. The receiving antenna begins to oscillate when exposed to an electromagnetic field.
9. The alternating electric field generates electrons in the antenna.
10. Cold air turns into water droplets.
