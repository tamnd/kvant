Tasks

M771 — M775; F783 — F787

M771. In triangle ABC, a bisector AK is drawn. It is known that the centers of the circles inscribed in triangle ABK and circumscribed about triangle ABC coincide. Find the angles of triangle ABC.

M772. The workshop has five different machines. Training one worker to operate one machine costs 1,000 rubles. With each minimum cost, can 8 workers be trained so that, in the absence of any three of them, all the machines can be used simultaneously? Each worker can only work on one machine at a time.

M773. The incircle of triangle ABC touches its sides AB, BC and AC at points M, N and P respectively. It is known that
$$\overrightarrow{AN} + \overrightarrow{BP} + \overrightarrow{CM} = \overrightarrow{0}.$$
Prove that triangle ABC is regular.
P. Gusyatnikov, S. Reznitsenko

M775. For what natural numbers $n \geqslant 3$ are there distinct natural numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ such that $1 \leqslant a_k \leqslant n+1$ for any $k=1, 2, \dots, n$ and all $n$ numbers $|a_1-a_2|, |a_2-a_3|, \dots, |a_{n-1}-a_n|, |a_n-a_1|$ are distinct?

A. Anjan

F783. A horizontally located weightless rod of length $3l$ with weights with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ attached to it is held in an equilibrium position by means of two vertical threads (Fig. 1). Determine the tension force of the left thread at the first moment of time if the right thread is rethreaded.

S. Krotov

F784. A constant force $F$ begins to act on a body moving at a constant speed $v$. After time $t$ the speed of the body became equal to $v/2$. Over the next similar time interval, the speed decreased by another half. Determine what the speed of the body will be after a time interval $3t$ from the beginning of the force $F$.

K. Serdeev

F785. A piece of ice floats in a glass of water. There is a layer of oil on the surface of the water. How will the level of liquid in the glass change when the ice melts? Where will the interface between water and oil shift?

S. Kazakov

F786*. In a neutral monatomic gas, attractive forces act between the molecules. Considering that the potential energy of molecular interaction is proportional to the density of the gas, that is, $U = \alpha \frac{N}{V}$, where $N$ is the number of gas molecules, $V$ is the volume occupied by the gas. $\alpha$ - proportionality coefficient ($\alpha < 0$), determine the difference in the molar heat capacity of the gas at constant pressure ($c_p$) and at constant volume ($c_V$).

V. Tugushchev

F787. The source voltage varies linearly with time. At the initial moment the direction was equal to zero. Using the key $K$, the source can be connected to the circuit shown in figure 2. At what point should the key be closed so that the current in the circuit is constant in magnitude? Resistor resistance $R$, capacitance $C$.

A. Zilberman
