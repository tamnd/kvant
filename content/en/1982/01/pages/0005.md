⟦folio 3⟧

Happy New Year!

The new year 1982 has arrived, the second year of the Eleventh Five-Year Plan. The Soviet people have great hopes for him. There is a huge amount of work ahead to improve all parts of our national economy and transfer it to the path of western intensification. It is necessary to expand and deepen the scientific and technological revolution and effectively use its greatest achievements. Science is now the main source of various changes in industry and construction, transport, communications technology and agriculture. It is not for nothing that in the report to the XXVI Congress of the CPSU, the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee L. I. Brezhnev said the following words: “The Communist Party proceeds from the fact that the construction of a new society without science is simply unthinkable.”
Our country pays great attention to successful science and training of scientific personnel. «Kvant» strives to contribute to the solution of this important national task - helping to benefit the perception of talented young researchers.
In general, the appearance of our magazine remains the same this year. However, some sections will look slightly different.
In 1982 you will find in «Kvant» more articles on various branches of modern physics and mathematics, which usually open every issue of the journal. A large article will also be published on the history of science. We intend to tell readers about the outstanding Russian scientists M. V. Ostrogadsky, A. M. Lyapunov and K. E. Tsiolkovsky, as well as about Gaspard Monge, Daniil Bernoulli and Simeon Denis Poisson.
The section “Options for university entrance exams in 1981” will look different. First of all, there will be significantly more ages in it than in the previous year. In each of the issues of the first half of the year you will see options for entrance exams for universities of different profiles - universities, pedagogical institutes, technical challenges. Meeting the numerous wishes of our readers, we will also talk about exams in economics, food, textiles and some other challenges where physics and mathematics are not the main academic disciplines.
For three years, the magazine «Kvant» ran a section “The Art of Programming,” which mainly contained materials from school programs organized by Novosibirsk scientists. She found her enthusiasts in the most separate corners of our country and abroad. The last lessons of this school will be published in the first half of this year. Then, in September, the magazine begins publishing materials for a new one-year school for young programs.
In issue No. 11 of 1981, a short questionnaire addressed to the reader was published. We received many interesting answers. We thank our readers and hope that they will contact us with their suggestions and wishes, report on scientific problems that interest them, send their questions and tasks, and actively participate in our competitions.
We wish you great success in your studies and life, our dear readers!
