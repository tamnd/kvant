This article is devoted to the latest achievements in the rapidly developing field of modern science - astrophysics.
The author, the famous Soviet theoretical physicist Academician A. B. Migdal, takes the reader to the very forefront of research, to the border between the known and the unknown, and shows how many interesting and important problems are still waiting to be solved.

A. Migdal

The fate of neutron stars

Ignorance of nature is the greatest ingratitude
Planned Efforts

I tried to talk about super-powerful explosions of stars and how stars arise from neutrons. The theory predicts that in such stars a type of nuclear transformation that has not yet been discovered in experiment can occur - the formation of nuclear matter with a density much greater than the density of atomic nuclei (and the density of atomic nuclei is of the order of $10^{14}$ g/cm$^3$).

In order to understand these phenomena, we will have to turn to many areas of physics. Here astronomy and the theory of gravity are intertwined with particle physics and nuclear physics. We will need to find out what “vacuum” is and how the properties of space of large physical bodies change.

Is it possible to talk about this in one article so that the reader can understand everything? The author did not set himself such a task. The main goal of the article is to show, at least in part, the inner beauty of the Universe, which manifests itself in the richness of the connection between heterogeneous phenomena.

I foresee that not everything will be clear, but it is believed that there is no harm in this. After all, the word “to drink” has many meanings - from complete clarity to a vague sensation. Be patient and read on; The most interesting part is at the end.
