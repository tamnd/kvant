⟦folio 2⟧

DECREE OF THE PRESIDIUM OF THE SUPREME COUNCIL OF THE USSR

On awarding the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee,
Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR,
Marshal of the Soviet Union
Comrade Brezhnev Leonid Ilyich
Order of Lenin and Gold Star medal
Hero of the Soviet Union

For outstanding services to the Communist Party and the Soviet state in strengthening the economic and defense power of the Soviet Union, great personal contribution to achieving victory over the Nazi invaders during the Great Patriotic War, the restoration and further development of the national economy of the USSR in a successive period, tireless activity in the struggle for peace, for the fruitful leadership of communist construction and in connection with semi-identity, award the General Secretary of the Central Committee from his birthday CPSU, Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR, Marshal of the Soviet Union, Comrade Brezhnev Leonid Ilyich, the Order of Lenin and the Gold Star medal
Hero of the Soviet Union.

First Deputy Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR
V. KUZNETSOV.

Secretary of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR
M. GEORGADZE.

Moscow, Kremlin. December 18, 1981
