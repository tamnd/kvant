F693. On a fixed wedge forming an angle a with the horizon, there lies a non-stretchable, weightless rope (Fig. 1). One of the ends of the rope is fixed at point A. A small group is attached to the lower end of the rope (at point B). At some point in time, the wedge begins to move to the right with constant acceleration a. With what acceleration does the group move while it is on the wedge?

By time t from the start of movement, the wedge will have covered a distance $S = at^2/2$ and acquired a speed $\vec{v}_{\text{кл}} = \vec{dt}$. By this moment, the weight will have moved along the wedge to the same distance S, its speed $v_{\text{отн}}$ relative to the wedge will be equal to $|\vec{v}_{\text{отн}}| = |\vec{a}| t$ and directed upward along the wedge. The speed of $\vec{v}_{\text{гр}}$ relative to the ground is $\vec{v}_{\text{гр}} = \vec{v}_{\text{отн}} + \vec{v}_{\text{кл}}$, that is (Fig. 2)

$$|\vec{v}_{\text{гр}}| = 2|\vec{v}_{\text{кл}}| \sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = (2|\vec{a}| \sin \frac{\alpha}{2}) t,$$

and the angle that makes $\vec{v}_{\text{гр}}$ with the horizon is $\beta = (\pi - \alpha)/2 = \text{const.}$
