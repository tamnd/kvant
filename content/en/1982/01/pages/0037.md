⟦folio 35⟧

Exercise

1. Tolya and Sasha, having played a game of dominoes, laid out all the bones. At the same time, they got a rectangular frame. Points are replaced in this frame by letters (empty cells are “zero” points). figure shows how the bones are located at the vertices of the frame (they are shaded). The positions of the remaining tiles are unknown, but it is known that the sum of points on the horizontal and vertical sides of the frame is the same. Restore the location of the bone.

2. In 1982, the square of Matleb's age coincided with the number formed by the first three digits of his year of birth. In what year was Matleb born?

3. In 1981, January 1 and December 31 were the same day of the week. Will this be the case this year? And in general, when will January 1 be the same day of the week as December 31?

4. An equality was laid out on a piece of paper made from matches (see figure; action signs are not made from matches). What is the minimum number of matches that must be moved for the equality to be true?

5. The girl approached the street crossing at the moment when the yellow light turned on and looked at the traffic light. She noticed from her watch that the red light lasted one and a half times less time than the green light, and the yellow light lasted four times less time than the red light. After the yellow light was on for the eighteenth time, the green light turned on, and the girl, after standing for 17 minutes, began to cross the street. How long does the yellow light stay on?

These tasks were suggested to us by: M. Adaev (8th grade student of the Kazvinobinsk rural school in the Masalli district of the Az. USSR), M. Varqa, N. Rozov, A. Shvetsov.
