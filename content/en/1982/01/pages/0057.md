Assignment for the introductory test at the MIMF in 1982
1. Solve inequality
$$\frac{x^2}{x-1} \ge 0.$$
2. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 11, 12 on a circle in such a way that any two adjacent numbers are different from each other?
a) no more than one;
b) no more than two?
3. How many zeros are there in the number 25? = 1 • 2 • 3 • ... • 24 • 25?
4. For 16 lemons you need to plan as many rubles as you can buy lemons for one ruble. How much does one lemon cost?

5. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the diagonals of a convex quadrilateral is always less than the perimeter of this quadrilateral.
6. The seven dwarves decided that each of them would give Snow White at least seven snowdrops for her birthday. When all the flowers were presented, Snow White said that their number did not exceed 50. Hearing this, the smallest of the gnomes immediately declared that he could accurately name the number of snowdrops presented. How many flowers did each dwarf give to Snow White?
7. Find the sum of all coefficients of the polynomial that is obtained after opening the parentheses in the expression
$$(x+2)(x+3)(x-5)(x-1)+4.$$
