⟦folio 2⟧

Red balls from the reserve at the top roll down to form a curve called normal or Gaussian (its equation is $y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-x^2}$). This curve characterizes the distribution of men and women by height, peas by size, newborns by weight, gas particles by speed of movement and many other phenomena and properties of the physical and biological world.
