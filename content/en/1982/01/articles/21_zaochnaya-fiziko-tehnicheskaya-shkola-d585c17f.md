The correspondence physics and technology school at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling students from eight-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1982/83 academic year.

The goal of this school is to help students study physics and mathematics independently. That is why, when admitting to ZFTSH, preference is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

ZFTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom subsequently become students at leading universities in our country.

In addition to individual students, ZFTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized on site at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the introductory task of the ZFTS. A club is accepted into the ZFTSH if the school director informs the ZFTSH of the names of the circle leader and a list of members of the circle by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory assignment).

Students admitted to ZFTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the ZFTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by ZFTS. ZFTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical tasks and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitive tasks at MIPT). The work of individual students is checked by the ZFTSH or its filps, and the work of members of the circle is checked by its leaders.

Physics and mathematics classes according to the ZFTSH program were tested twice with a Moscow student. Classes are held at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), recruitment to which is carried out either based on the results of completing the introductory assignment of the ZFTS, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics. The interview is usually held in the second half of September (for information, call 216-00-05, ext. 2-59).

Each student must complete the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently in Russian and carefully copy it into one school journal. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment.

Send the notebook in a large envelope with a simple parcel (just do not roll the notebook into a tube). Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the terradi. Admission to the school is decided by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1982.

Introductory task

Physics

1. A metro escalator takes a person running down it in $t_1 = 1$ minutes. How long does it take a person standing on an ecalator to descend if he runs up the ecalator while looking down in $t_2 = 4$ minutes?

2. How much ballast must an aerostat with a volume of $V = 300$ m³ emit to rise to a height of 1 km? At the Earth's surface, the air density is $q = 1,29$ kg/m³, the pressure is $p = 0,1$ MPa. When solving the problem, assume that air density is proportional to pressure.

3. An ice floe with an area of ​​$S = 2$ m² and a thickness of $h = 30$ cm floats in water. How much work must be done to completely submerge the ice floe into the water?

4. Find the gas consumption of the Zhiguli automatically at a speed of $v = 100$ km/h. Motor power $N = 60$ l. s., motor efficiency $\eta = 30\%$. The combustion temperature of gasoline is $q = 45 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg. Gasoline consumption is about $l = 100$ km.

5. The electrical circuit consists of two batteries with voltages $U_1 = 6$ V and $U_2 = 12$ V, a rheostat with a sliding contact whose total resistance is $R = 1800$ Ohm, a conductor with a resistance of $r = 200$ Ohm and an ammeter,

included as indicated at figure. Which strip of the second battery should the sliding contact be connected to and what position should it be closed so that the current through the ammeter is zero?

6. In the last second, the freely serving body has covered 3/4 of the entire path. How long did it take for the body to fall?

7. A ball with mass $m = 2$ kg moves along the equator of the inner surface of a spherical shell with a mass of $M = 10$ kg, making a full revolution in a time of $T = 2$ s. Assuming that there are no external forces and there is no tension, determine with what force the ball presses on the sphere. The distance between the centers of gravity of the ball and the shell is $a = 2$ m.

8. The football player will hit the ball with an average force of $F = 5 \cdot 10^2$ N. The ball, after being hit, flies away at an angle of $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the horizon and lands at a distance of $L = 40$ m. Determine the time

hitting the ball. Air resistance can be neglected. The mass of the ball is $m = 0,5$ kg.

9. A vessel filled with a mixture of hydrogen ($\mu H_2 = 2$ g/mol) and gel ($\mu He = 4$ g/mol) is separated from an empty vessel of equal volume by a semi-permeable partition that freely allows gel molecules to pass through and does not allow hydrogen molecules to pass through. After equilibrium is established, the pressure in the first vessel is 10%. Determine the ratio of the masses of gel and hydrogen. The temperature was kept constant.

10. With the same change in temperature, once at constant pressure, and another time at constant volume, the amount of heat supplied to the same portion of an ideal gas differs by $\Delta Q = 7$ J. Determine the change in the internal energy of the gas in these processes. The proportionality coefficient between temperature and internal energy for a given gas is $C_V = 20,75$ J/(mol K).

11. The space between the capacitor plates is filled with liquid resistance $\rho = 52$ Ohm m²/m. Find the force of electrical interaction between the plates when a current of force $I = 1$ A flows through the capacitor. The area of ​​the capacitor plates is $S = 1$ cm².

12. A capacitor with a capacity of $C = 1$ uF, charged to a voltage of $U = 10$ V, is connected to an EMF battery of $\delta = 2$ V. How much heat will be released in the circuit when the capacitor is connected?

Mathematics

1. There are 37 students in the class; of these, 25 students met the GTO standards, 8 students had sports categories, and 9 students did not meet the GTO standards and did not have sports categories. How many students in the class have sports ranks, but have not met the GTO standards?

2. There are three points on the bottom plane. How many parallelograms can be constructed with vertices at these points?

3. Prove or define the following statements:

a) in order for the number $n^2 - 2$ to be divisible by 7 ($n \ge 3$), the number $n - 3$ must be a multiple of 7;

b) in order for the number $n^2 - 2$ to be divisible by 7 ($n \ge 3$), the number $n - 3$ must be a multiple of 7.

4. In the trigon ABC, point D lies on the segment AB. Prove that if $\widehat{BCD} = \widehat{ACD}$ then $\widehat{CDB} = 90^\circ + \frac{1}{2} (\widehat{CAD} - \widehat CBD)$. Form and prove the converse theorem.

5. Are there any integer values ​​of $m$ for which the fraction $\frac{12m + 3}{18m + 4}$ cancels?
