Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\cos^2 2x + \sin \left(2x - \frac{3}{2} \pi\right)} + \frac{1}{4} = \cos \frac{20}{12} \pi.$$

2. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt[3]{x} + \sqrt[3]{y} = 3 \\ \sqrt[3]{x^2} - \sqrt[3]{xy} + \sqrt[3]{y^2} = 3. \end{cases}$$

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{9x - 15}{19} < \int_0^x \frac{dy}{(y+1)^2} - 1.$$

4. From two points, the distance between which is 2400 km, I will direct each other to depart from a simultaneous passenger and fast train. Each of them moves at a constant speed, and at some point in time they meet. If she had traveled at the speed of an express train, she would have met them three hours earlier than the actual moment of meeting. If she had traveled at the speed of a passenger train, she would have met them five hours later than the actual moment of meeting. Find the train speeds.

5. When reoce $m$, triangle $ABC$ is mapped to triangle $A_1B_1C_1$. Prove that if $A_0, B_0, C_0$ is the midpoint of BC, CA and AB, then the lines $A_0A_1, B_0B_1$ and $C_0C_1$ intersect at one point.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\left(\frac{5}{8} \sqrt{x \cdot \sqrt[3]{x^11}} - \frac{10}{13} \sqrt[3]{x \cdot \sqrt{x^11}}\right)' = 56.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\left| \mathrm{ctg} \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{2}\right) \right| = \frac{1}{\cos^2 2x} - 1.$$

3. A tangent parallel to the base is drawn to a circle inscribed in an equilibrium triangle with a base of 12 cm and a height of 8 cm. Find the length of this tangent segment between the sides of the triangle.

4. Find integer values ​​of $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log_{0.3} (\sqrt{x+5} - x+1) > 0.$$

5. If a passenger goes from $A$ to $B$ by train, then he will arrive at $B$ in 20 hours. If he has to wait for the plane for more than 5 hours, then he will be at point $B$ in 10 hours. Airplane train contract $\frac{8}{9}$ hours from the moment of departure. How many times is the speed of a plane greater than the speed of a train?

Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. What initial speed must be imparted to a signal packet released at an angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$ to the horizon so that it flares up at the most point of its trajectory, if the burning time of the fuse packets is $\tau = 6$ s?

2. A puff of air has entered the cup-shaped barometer, as a result of which the barometer shows pressure less than the true one. At a pressure of $p_1 = 768$ mmHg. Art. it shows $h_1 = 748$ mm, and the length of the launching part of the tube is $l = 80$ mm. What is the atmospheric pressure $p_2$ if the root in this barometer is at a height of $h_2 = 734$ mm? The density of the root is $\rho = 13.6 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m³.

3. In the field of a horizontal capacitor, the distance between the plates of which is $d = 2$ cm, an electron is released at a speed of $v_0 = 10^7$ m/s, directed parallel to the plates. Find the potential difference between the plates if the electron deflection along the path $l = 5$ cm along the plates was $h = 2,5$ mm. Electron mass $m = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ Cl.

4. There are 25-watt and 100-watt light bulbs connected in series and connected to a network with a voltage at which each individual light bulb is rated. Which one will release more heat?

5. The capacitance of a variable capacitor varies from $C_1 = 56$ pF to $C_2 = 667$ pF. What set of inductors do you need to have so that the oscillating circuit can be tuned to radio stations in the length range $l_1 = 40$ m to $l_2 = 2600$ m?

6. An accurate light source is placed at the bottom of a vessel with water $h = 10$ cm deep. What must be the minimum radius of an opaque disk floating on water so that no ray escapes from the water? Water assumption indicator $n = 1,33$.

7. The image of an object at a distance of $d = 0,4$ m is $\Gamma = 5$ times larger than the object. Find the possible value of the optical power of the lens.

What do you think

Once I proposed the following problem to a friend: “Find on a bounded plane a figure $a^*$, which a non-circular rotation $R$ (by a non-zero angle) and a non-circular parallel translation $F$ (different from the identical one) translate into the same figure: $R(a) = F(a)$. “This is impossible,” the acquaintance immediately answered. “No matter how many times you rotate a limited figure around a given center, it will not go beyond a certain circle. And with repeated transfers, this happens continuously.” What do you think?
