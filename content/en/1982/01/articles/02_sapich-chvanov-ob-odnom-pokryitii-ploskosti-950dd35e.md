⟦folio none⟧

The pattern drawn here is obtained as follows. The plane is divided by parallel lines into equal squares. Each square is divided into two congested rectangles, and the directions of the “long” sides are reversed: if in a given square the rectangles are vertical, then in the four neighboring squares they are horizontal, and vice versa. From the resulting network of rectangles, a covering of the octagonal plane is constructed using the following construction. The center of each rectangle is connected to the centers of seven neighbors by segments - their midpoints form the vertices of heptagons and triangles, which are circled to form octagons. Find these heptagons, triangles and octagons in our pattern. On figure diamond-shaped “clips” have been added for decorative purposes. Think about the shapes you get if you start from a different network of rectangles.

A. Sapits, V. Chvanov
