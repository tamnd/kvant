New note
in total correspondence
math school

The All-Island Correspondence Mathematics School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes and vocational school students, with the exception of those living in Moscow, Leningrad and their immediate surroundings.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1982. Training is free. Students admitted to school will regularly receive manuals that contain learning theoretical questions and problems to solve. After studying the material in the manual, VZMSH students assign tests for testing, which are carefully solved by VZMSH teachers - students, graduate students and teachers of Moscow State University and other adults. The VZMS program is closely related to the school one and is aimed at in-depth study of the main issues of the school mathematics course.

The duration of training is three years. Everyone who successfully completes the control tasks will receive a certificate of completion of the VZMSH.

The tasks of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 25. After checking the works (approximately in July), the VZMSH will inform all those who took part in the competition of the decisions of the Application Commission. Schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements will receive preference for admission.

Although some of the introductory tasks differ in appearance from regular school ones, they do not require additional knowledge of mathematics. You don't have to solve all the problems to get into school. When evaluating work, not only the number of problems solved will be taken into account, but also the quality of the solution. An unfounded answer may not be read; If there are several possible answers to a task, you must indicate them all.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. It is not sent back and no decision is issued regarding it.

In the envelope along with the notebook, you must put a piece of paper measuring 14 cm × 6 cm with the full postal address of the applicant (this sheet will be pasted on the envelope with the notice of the commission about the results of checking the entrance work).
