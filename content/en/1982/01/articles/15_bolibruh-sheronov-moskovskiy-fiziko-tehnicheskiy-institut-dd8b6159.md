Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$2 \log_4 (4-x) = 4 - \log_2 (-2-x).$$
2. In a triangle with an angle of 120°, the lengths of the sides form an arithmetic progression with a difference of 1 cm. Find the lengths of the sides of the triangle.
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{7x+y} + \sqrt{x+y} = 6 \\ \sqrt{x+y} - y+x = 2. \end{cases}$$
4. Point $D$ lies on side $AC$ of triangle $ABC$. A circle of radius $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$ inscribed in triangle $ABD$ swings sides $AB$ at point $M$, and a circle of radius $\sqrt{3}$ inscribed in triangle $BCD$ swings sides $BC$ at point $N$. It is known that $|BM| = 6$, $|BN| = 5$. Find the lengths of the sides of triangle $ABC$.
5. In a regular triangular primordium $ABCA_1B_1C_1$, point $D$ is the midpoint of edge $A_1C_1$. A regular triangular paramida is located so that the plane of its base coincides with the plane $ABC$, one side edge passes through the vertex $B$, another through the point $D$, and the third intersects the edge $CC_1$. Find the ratio of the volume of the paramida to the volume of the prim.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg} x \cdot \left( \text{ctg} x + \frac{1}{\sin x} \right) = 1$$
2. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2 \log_4 (y+1) + \log_2 y = \log_2 \left( \frac{x}{y} - 2 \right) \\ 5 + \log_2 \frac{y}{x} = \frac{6}{\log_2 \frac{x}{y}}. \end{cases}$$
3. On the continuation of the side $AB$ of the rhombus $ABCD$ the point $B$ is taken to be the point $M$, with $|MD| = |MC|$ and $\widehat{MDC} = \arctg \frac{8}{5}$. Find the ratio of the lengths of the segments $MA$ and $MB$.
4. The length of the cube edge $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is equal to $a$. Points $E$ and $F$ are the midpoints of edges $BC$ and $CC_1$, respectively. Consider triangles whose vertices are the points of intersection of planes parallel to the bases of the cube with straight lines $A_1E, DF, AD_1$. Find
a) the area of ​​the triangle whose plane passes through the middle of the edge $AA_1$;
b) the smallest possible value of the area of ​​the triangles under consideration.
5. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 6x+y^2-z^2=6 \\ x^2-y-4z=-4 \\ 21x^2-2y^2+3y=22z^2. \end{cases}$$

$$\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1+x}} + \sqrt{\frac{1+x}{x}} = \frac{5}{2}.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$4x+\log_2 9 > \log_2 (9 \cdot 2^{2x+1}-5).$$
3. In the right angle of a triangle $ABC$ with a hypotenuse $AC$ of length 2 cm, the medians $AM$ and $CN$ are drawn. A circle can be described around the quadrilateral $ANMC$. Find the radius of this circle.
4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \cos 2y+\frac{1}{2} = \left( \cos y-\frac{1}{2} \right) (1+2 \sin 2x) \\ \sin y \cdot (\text{tg}^3 x+\text{ctg}^3 x) = 3 \text{ctg} y. \end{cases}$$

a) the largest value of the volume of such cylinders with a given height $h$;

Physics

Option 1

1. A stationary, unexcited hydrogen atom is attacked by another unexcited hydrogen atom. What must be the minimum kinetic energy of the incoming atom for a photon to be emitted as a result of the collision? The ionization energy of hydrogen atom is $E_n=13,6$ eV. The frequencies of hydrogen radiation atom are determined by the formula
$$v = R \left( \frac{1}{n^2} - \frac{1}{m^2} \right),$$ where $R$ is a constant, $n$ and $m$ are integers.
2. Amounts of hydrogen ($\mu_H=2$ g/mol) and helium ($\mu_{He}=4$ g/mol) of equal mass were placed in a vessel with a volume of $V_1$, which is separated from an empty vessel with a volume of $V_2$ by a half-partition that freely allows hydrogen molecules to pass through and does not allow helium to pass through. After equilibrium was established, the pressure in the first vessel dropped by 2 times. Identify $V_2/V_1$. The temperature is constant.
3. In a flat capacitor, between the plates of which a constant potential difference is maintained, a dielectric plate with a dielectric constant $\varepsilon=3$ is introduced. How many times will the force change?

Behind the lens there is an ideal flat mirror so that 25% of the light flux that passed through the lens and reflected from the mirror hits the lens again. Option 2

1. A bullet flying horizontally at a speed of $v_0=750$ m/s hits the rear wall of a tank traveling at a speed of $v_T=72$ km/h and elastically bounces off it (Fig. 2). At what speed does the bullet hit and bounce off? The step is inclined to the vertical at an angle $\varphi=30^\circ$.

2. A sealed balloon made of non-reactivating materials should lift equipment weighing $M=10$ kg to a height of about 5.5 km, where the density of air ($\mu_B=29$ g/mol) is half that of the Earth's surface. The ball is filled with gel ($\mu_{\text{He}}=4$ g/mol) under external conditions (temperature $T=300$ K, pressure $p=1$ atm). The volume of the ball is $V=100$ m³. Determine the mass per square meter of the shell materials of the ball. The universal gas constant is $R=8,3$ J/(mol K).

3. Determine the potential difference between the electrodes of a gas-discharge tube filled with gel, at which the process of concentrating atoms by electron impact begins. It is known that the distribution of potential (in relative units) along the length of the tube at this moment has the form shown in figure 3; electron free run length $l=10^{-4}$ m; potential of helium atoms $\varphi_h=24,5$ V.

4. A point light source $S$ moves uniformly parallel to a screen in which there are two small points at a distance $d$ from each other; the distance from the source to the screen is $h$ (Fig. 4). Light receiver $A$, located on the axis of the system, registers periodic changes in illumination. Determine the speed of movement of the source $v$, if the frequency of intensity oscillations is $f=15$ Hz; light wavelength $\lambda=6 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m; $d=2$ mm; $h=1$ m. During the change, the source of motion is near the axis of the system.

Option 3

1. A weightless rod of length $L$ is fixed at one end in an ideal hinge, and the other rests on the outer rigidity $k$ (Fig. 5). Determine the period of small oscillations of the bar depending on the position $l$ of a load with mass $m$ on it.

2. What is the maximum force with which a jar (a waiting room in medical practice for laying down) is pressed against a person’s body if the diameter of its hole is $D=4$ cm? At the moment the jar is applied to the body, the impact is $t=80^\circ\text{C}$, and the ambient temperature is $t_0=20^\circ\text{C}$. Atmospheric pressure $p_0=10^3$ Pa. Changes in the volume of air in the jar (due to retraction of the skin) can be avoided.

3. Two self-induction coils with inductances $L_1$ and $L_2$ are connected through switches $K_1$ and $K_2$ to a source with a constant EMF $\delta$ and internal resistance $r$ (Fig. 6). At the initial moment of time, both keys are different. After the keys $K_1$ are closed and the current through the coils $L_1$ reaches a certain value $I_0$, the keys $K_2$ are closed. Determine the steady-state value of the currents through the coils after closing the key $K_2$. You can rely on the active resistance of the coils.

4. The position of the star as seen from Earth is slightly different from the true one due to the refraction of rays by the atmosphere. Determine the error when fixing the angular position of a star visible from the Earth at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the vertical. The average value of the refractive index of rays by the atmosphere is considered equal to $n=1,0003$.
