◆ Since the system is isolated, its total energy remains the same.

The center of mass of the system moves at a speed of 2v. In a system without a center of mass, the pistons at the initial moment of motion towards each other with equal speeds v, and the piston energy of the system is equal to the sum of the kinetic energies of the pistons and the internal energy of the gas:

E = 2 \frac{mv^2}{2} + \frac{3}{2} RT_0.

The maximum gas temperature will be at the moment when the piston speeds (in the center of mass system) are equal to zero. The energy of the system at this moment is equal to

E = \frac{3}{2} RT_{max} = 2 \frac{mv^2}{2} + \frac{3}{2} RT_0.

From here we find T_{max}:

T_{max} = T_0 + \frac{2}{3} \frac{mv^2}{R}
