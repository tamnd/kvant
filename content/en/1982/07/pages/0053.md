F729. A conveyor belt of length l moves with speed v₀. At what speed must a cube of mass m be pushed against the movement of the conveyor in order for the amount of body released due to the work of friction between the cube and the conveyor belt to be maximum? What is this maximum amount of body if the friction coefficient is μ and the condition v₀² < 2μlg is satisfied?

Fig. 1.

The amount of released body will be maximum provided that the cube travels the maximum distance relative to the transporter. To do this, it is necessary that at roller A the speed of the cube relative to the ground becomes equal to zero. The initial speed (ground ratio) that the cube should have is determined by the conditions

$$-v + at = 0, \quad l = vt - \frac{at^2}{2},$$

where a = μg is the acceleration imparted to the cube by the friction force. From here

$$v = \sqrt{2\mu g l};$$

time of movement of the cube along the conveyor belt to roller A -

$$t = \sqrt{2l/\mu g}.$$

Before stopping, the cube will follow the path of the tape

$$s_1 = l + v_0 t = l + v_0 \sqrt{2l/\mu g}.$$

Next, the cube begins to move uniformly accelerated to the right. The time after which the slippage will stop is τ = v₀/a = v₀/μg. During this time, relative to the ground, the cube will move a distance

$$s = a\tau^2/2 = v₀^2/2\mu g.$$

Since, according to the conditions of the problem, v₀ < √2μgl, then during the time τ the cube will not slide off the conveyor, that is, s < l.

The path that the cube will travel relative to the conveyor belt during this time is equal to

$$s_2 = \left| \frac{v_0^2}{2a} - v_0 \tau \right| = \frac{v_0^2}{2\mu g}.$$

The full path of the cube relative to the tape is

$$S = s_1 + s_2 = l + v_0 \sqrt{\frac{2l}{\mu g}} + \frac{v_0^2}{2\mu g}.$$

The amount of tellote released due to the work of friction is equal to

$$Q = \mu mgS = \frac{m}{2}(v_0 + \sqrt{2l\mu g})^2.$$

G. Myakshev
