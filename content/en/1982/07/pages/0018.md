They are determined not by radiation pressure, but by electromagnetic forces.

The interplanetary space in which comets move is filled with the so-called solar wind, that is, plasma continuously flowing from the Sun - protons and electrons, carrying with them (due to their high electrical conductivity) part of the solar magnetic field. The high accelerations and jet structure of type I tails are caused by the interaction of N₂⁺, CO⁺ ions and the electrons they consist of with the solar wind.

Not to mention the long-standing unresolved functional problems, such as the distortion of the Solar system, life in it, etc., which comets and research can shed light on, even in the physics of the phenomena observed in comets, there is a lot of uncertainty. The list of such incomprehensible phenomena is long, and we will not give it here.

Thus, expeditions to comets, in addition to the exciting psychological spectrum, also have enormous scientific significance. They are needed by all those who are interested in the physics of many incomprehensible phenomena observed in comets, and those who are interested in the functional problems of the distortion of our Solar system and systems similar to it in our Galaxy, the problem of the distortion of life on Earth and in the Universe in general.
