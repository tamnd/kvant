2°. Let $2^{k-1} < N < 2^k$, $N$ be an odd number. Then $\Gamma \log_2 N = k$, $N = 2^{k-1} + r$, where $0 < r < 2^{k-1}$. We'll show you how all your friends can find out all the news in $k+1$ hours. Let's divide the $N$ friends into two groups: the first, consisting of $2^{k-1}$ friends, and the second, consisting of $r$ friends. During the first hour, each friend from the second group talks to some friend from the first group. In the next $k-1$ hours $2^{k-1}$ friends of the first group exchange news among themselves as described in paragraph 1°; as a result, each of them will know all the news. Finally, in the last hour of each, a friend from the second group talks to some friend from the first group again, causing all the friends from the second group to know all the news too.

3°. Let $2^{k-1} < N \leq 2^k$, $N = 2m$ be an even number. Then $\Gamma \log_2 N = k$ and $2^{k-2} < m \leq 2^{k-1}$. We'll show you how all your friends can find out all the news in $k$ hours.

Consider two regular $m$-gons placed by a rectangular friend (see figure 2, where $m=6$). We will assume that each vertex is one of the friends (different vertices are different friends). Two friends at some moment of time $t$ will be called neighboring if their image at this moment of the vertices of the largest $m$-gons is a rectangular friend (for example, as at $t=0$ the vertices $A$ and $B$ on figure 2). Let $a$ be some news. (We will color the vertices representing friends who know news $a$ at time $t$.) Let all pairs of neighboring friends at time $t=0$ talk to each other in the first hour (see figure 2 at $t=1$). In the future, before the start of the $t$th hour ($t=2, 3, ..., k$), we will rotate the top $m$-gon clockwise to the angle $2^{t-1} \cdot \frac{180^\circ}{m}$ and assume that at the $t$th hour all pairs of friends who turn out to be neighboring at this moment are talking (at figure 2 the dissemination of news is shown $a$ after the first, second and third hours). The result of such conversations will be that after the $t$ hour the $2^{t-1}$ vertices of each of the largest $m$-gons turn out to be colored. Since $m \leq 2^{k-1}$, after $k$ hours all the naikhm will be painted. Thus, after $k$ hours, all $N$ friends will know any news $a$.

Our statement has been fully proven.
