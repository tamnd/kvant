F723. Athletes run at the same speeds $v$ in a column of length $l_0$. There is a trainer coming towards you with a speed of $u$ ($u < v$). An athlete hitting with a coach beats back at the same speed $v$. What will be the length of the column when all the athletes turn around?

The time during which all athletes, starting from the first and ending with the last in the column, repeats with the coach is equal to
$$t = l_0 / (v + u).$$

At the moment when the last athlete appears with the coach, the first will be at a distance from him
$$l = (v - u)t = l_0 \frac{(v - u)}{(v + u)}.$$

This will be the length of the column when all the athletes turn around.

(Now explain why, when light is reflected from a mirror moving away from the source, the wavelength of the light is shortened:
$$\lambda = \lambda_0 \frac{c - u}{c + u},$$
where $c$ is the speed of light, $u$ is the speed of the mirror.)

F724. How much will the end move across the movable block (point $A$ to figure) if a force $F$ is applied to it? The spring stiffnesses are the same and equal to $k$. Springs, thread and block are considered weightless.

Let the lower spring, under the action of force $F$, be stretched by amounts $x = F/k$. A force equal to $2F$ acts on the upper spring in the equilibrium position. Under the influence of this force, the spring increases by $x_1 = 2F/k = 2x$.

Thus, as a result of the action of the force $F$ by the absence block at a distance $2x$, the thread on the left will be shortened by $x$ and, consequently, the point $A$ will drop by a distance
$$l = 5x = 5F/k.$$

G. Meledin
