F763. On a horizontal surface there is a hoop of radius $R$. The same hoop (Fig. 1) moves past him at a speed of $v$. Find the dependence of the speed of the upper point of “intersection” of the hoops on the distance between their centers. The hoops are thin; the second hoop “passes” close to the first. (9)

S. Krotov

F764. The shock wave is a region of high pressure propagating at high speed in the direction of the $X$ (Fig. 2) axis; at the moment the wave arrives, the pressure rises sharply from the value $p_0$ to $2p_0$. There is a wedge in the path of wave propagation, the dimensions of the wedge are indicated at figure 3, the mass of the wedge is $m$. What speed will the wedge attain immediately after the shock wave front passes through it? Assume that the speed acquired by the wedge is much less than the speed of wave propagation; the third is negligible. (8)

A. Buzdin

F765. Currently, the power of all energy sources on Earth used by mankind is $\Delta P = 10^{13}$ W, and the power of solar energy entering the Earth is $P_0 = 10^{17}$ W. What overheating $\Delta T$ of the Earth's surface does terrestrial energy sources lead to? What is the maximum permissible value $\Delta P_{\max}$, if the maximum superheat from environmental messages should not exceed the value $\Delta T_{\max} = 0,1$ K? It is known that the energy emitted per unit time by a heated body increases 16 times with an increase in the absolute temperature of the body by 2 times. (10)

S. Kozel

F766. For the drying lamp, a nonlinear capacitor was used (it is filled with a dielectric whose dielectric constant depends on the field strength). The graph of voltage $U$ versus charge $Q$ of the capacitor is shown in figure 4. The capacitor is charged from a battery with $U_0 = 300$ V through a resistor with a resistance of $R = 10$ kOhm. Find the maximum number of searches in 1 minute. Assume that during discharge the capacitor is completely discharged. Minimum initial search voltage $U_1 = 250$ V. (9)

A. Zilberman
