Astroporia and the ancient chronicle

It is probably difficult to consider what I observe 2000 years ago as news; however, this particular news became known only recently. However, when an astronomer first discovered a new galaxy, he rightfully speaks about the discovery, although the galaxy existed long before this scientifically remarkable event.

The discovery that will be discussed was made on the pages of the ancient Chinese chronicle Shi Ji (“Historical Records”) *). It was written in 95 BC by Sima Qian. The Shi Ji contains information about the history, agriculture, and science of China. There is also a description of astronomical observations, in particular, the color of some stars is indicated. Indeed, if you look closely at the stars and compare them with each other, you can see that they differ in color.

In the Shi Chi it is written that the color of Sirrius (α Canis Major) is white, Antares (α Scorpiona) is red, Betelgeuse (α Orion) is

It is now well known that this is a variable star, namely a red light giant, and that its color is not yellow, but red. But, apparently, the ancient stars were not mistaken here either.

According to modern ideas, Betelgeuse is currently in a stage of rapid expansion.

Over 2000 years, its radius has almost doubled, and now it is approximately 300-400 times the radius of the Sun. As the star expands, it cools; and if now the temperature of its surface is about 3000 degrees, then at the time when Shi Ji was written, it should have been 1000-2000 degrees more. Compliance, and its color should be more yellow.

Thus, in the ancient chronicle there was visible confirmation of the wickedness of the star, a phenomenon that would seem to lie beyond the scope of human observation. It once again proves that any consequence of theoretical constructions will someday be subjected to experimental testing.

Recently, another interesting piece of evidence from ancient scholars was discovered in old Chinese books. In the summer of 365 BC, Jan De saw a bright point near Jupiter. maybe it was one of Jupiter’s satellites, for example, Ganymede or Callisto? At least, this is how historians explain this place.
