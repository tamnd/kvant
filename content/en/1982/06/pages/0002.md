In April of this year in Moscow, in the Central House of Artists, the performance “Scientists Draw” cooled down. Among the works presented there, the graphics of Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. T. Fomenko, a mathematician known for his research on differential geometry and topology, aroused great interest. Here we place a reproduction of A. T. Fomenko’s work, called “Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram”, and on the fourth page of the cover - a reproduction of the work “LENS SPACES”.

On the first page of the cover there is a photograph of the differential pattern from the round hole (photo by A. Shuki). A helium-neon laser was used as a light source, producing monochromatic red light.

Some differentiation experiments that you can perform on your own in your home laboratory are described in this issue of the magazine in the article by Y. Amstislavsky “Non-teaching phenomena around ordinary light sources.”
