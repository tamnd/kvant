⟦folio 44⟧

Kharkov State University named after. A. M. Gorky

Physics

Assigned oral exam

1. A sled is rolled down from a mountain whose height and base are $h=2$ m and $l=5$ m, respectively, and stops after covering a horizontal distance of $s=35$ m from the base of the mountain. Determine the friction coefficient, assuming it is constant throughout the entire path.

2. A cyclist must ride along a track shaped like a “loop”, the radius of which is $R=8$ m. From what height should he start accelerating so as not to fall at the top point of the loop?

3. The staircase rests on a vertical wall and a horizontal floor. The friction coefficient between the stairs and the wall is $\mu_1=0,5$, and between the floor and the stairs $\mu_2=0,4$. Determine the smallest angle of inclination of the ladder at which it can still remain in balance.

4. From what height must a body whose density is $\rho=0,4$ g/cm$^3$ fall in order to plunge into water to a depth of $H=6$ cm? The resistance of water and air can be neglected.

5. At what speed must a lead pellet be fired from a gun so that it melts when it hits an inelastic wall? The temperature of the pellet before impact is $t_1=27^\circ\text{C}$. Assume that the kinetic energy of the pellet is completely used to change its internal energy. Lead pellet temperature $t_2=327^\circ\text{C}$, specific temperature $c=140$ J/(kg K), specific temperature $λ=0,25 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg.

6. A cylinder with a capacity of $V=0,04$ m$^3$ is filled with rolled air at a temperature of $t=20^\circ\text{C}$ to a pressure of $p=4,63 \cdot 10^7$ N/m$^2$. What volume of water can be displaced by this air from a submarine tank into the sea at a depth of $H=30$ m, if the water temperature is $t_B=5^\circ\text{C}$ and the density of sea water is $q=1030$ kg/m$^3$?

7. The boiler heater consists of two sections with a resistance of $R=4$ Ohm each. The boiler is powered by a battery, the EMF of which is $\mathcal{E}=12$ V. The water in the boiler boils at the same time with both series and parallel connecting elements of the heater. Determine the current passing through the battery during a short circuit.

8. Voltage on the busbars of the power plant is $U_0=100$ kV. Distance to consumer $l=500$ km. The station must transmit power $P=100$ kW to the consumer. Consumption should not exceed 5% ($k=0,05$). Calculate the current in the wires, the cross-section of the wires and the mass of copper required for the wiring. Copper resistivity $\rho=0,17 \cdot 10^{-7}$ Ohm m, copper density $q_0=8960$ kg/m$^3$.

9. What is the true depth of the river if, when determined by eye in the vertical direction, its depth appears to be $h=2$ m? Water assumption indicator $n=1,33$.

10. A copper ball, distant from other bodies, is irradiated with monoxpromatic radiation, the wavelength of which is $\lambda=2 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m. To what maximum potential will the ball be charged when emitting photoelectrons? The work done by an electron on the copper surface is $A=4,5$ eV. Planck's constant $h=6,6 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s; electron charge $e=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ cl.

Preset oral eczema

1. Compare the average piston speed of the Izh-Jupiter-3 motorcycle engine in maximum power mode ($n=5300$ rpm) with the maximum speed of the motorcycle ($v_{\text{max}}=125$ km/h). Piston stroke $s=58$ mm.

2. The force $F$ acting on the body in the horizontal direction changes as shown in figure. Its maximum value is twice the sliding friction force. Construct a graph of the change in the speed of the body over time if the initial speed is $v_0=0$.

3. An ideal gas is transferred isothermally from state 1 to state 2 ($V_2<V_1$), then it is transferred isobarically to state 3 and returned isochorically to state 1. Does the gas perform positive or negative work in each of the processes and throughout the entire cycle? Does body exchange take place between the gas and the environment?

4. How many degrees will a stabilizing plate of size $5 \times 5$ cm heat up when drilling a hole in it with a feed of $l=0,5$ mm/rev, if the torque is $M=8$ N·m. Assume that the heating of the platinum is due to the amount of heat released during drilling. Specific temperament of steel $c=460$ J/(kg K), density $\rho=7,8 \times 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

5. Two constant and one variable resistor are connected to a source with voltage $U=6$ Once in parallel with each other - in series. The resistance of each constant resistor is $r=2$ Ohm, the variable resistance is $R=1-3$ Ohm. Which circuit allows you to change the current strength over a larger range by changing the resistance of a variable resistor?
