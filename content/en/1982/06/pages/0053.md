Matematika
Written exam
Option

1. Simplify and evaluate at $x=3$ the expression
$$\frac{3\sqrt{x} \cdot \sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x} \cdot 3\sqrt{x}}{1 + 6\sqrt{x}} \cdot \sqrt{x}.$$
2. Determine the value of the first term of an arithmetic progression if its difference is 25% of the value of the last term, which is the nineteenth. The progression difference value is 12.
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{x^2 - 3,5x + 1,5}{x^2 - x - 6} = 0.$$
Write your answer as a decimal fraction.
4. Find the most integer $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\frac{2x+1}{3} - \frac{3x-1}{2} > 1.$$
5. Solve the equation
$$x-1 = \sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}.$$
6. Find the most integer positive $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 + 16x + 64} > 20.$$
7. A pedestrian left from $A$ to $B$ at a speed of 3.6 km/h; After 1 hour 15 minutes, another pedestrian came out of $B$ at a speed of 4.8 km/h. What is the distance $AB$ if the second pedestrian approached at $A$ simultaneously with the arrival of the first at $B$?
8. During the pioneering period, 65 books were collected for the library. The first link collected 10 books less than the second, and the third - 30% of the number of books that brought the first and second links together. How many books did the second link collect?
9. 1600 kg of apples were brought to the store. On the first day, 30% of all apples were sold. On the second day - 40% of the number of apples remaining on the second day. On the third day, 25% of the number of apples remaining on the third day was sold. How many apples are left in the store after three days of sale?
10. Solve the equation
$$5^{2x} \cdot 6^{2x} = 900.$$
11. Solve the equation
$$\log_4 2^{4x} = 2^{\log_2 4}.$$
12. Find an integer that satisfies the inequality
$$\log_{0.3}(4x+1) < \log_{0.3}(x^2+4).$$
13. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{\sin x + \sqrt{3 \cos x}}{\sin(x + 60^\circ)}$$
to the numerical answer to two decimal places, taking into account rounding rules.

14. Calculate without tables with three decimal places
$$\cos 20^\circ \cdot \cos 40^\circ \cdot \cos 80^\circ$$
15. Find the root of the equation
$$3 \mathrm{tg}^2 2x + \sqrt{3} \mathrm{ctg}(270^\circ + 2x) = 0,$$
belonging to the interval $]0^\circ; 90^\circ[$; calculate it in degrees.
16. Find the smallest root of the equation
$$\tg 2x + \tg 4x = 0,$$
belonging to the interval $]0^\circ; 90^\circ[$; Write your answer in degrees.
17. Determine the period of translation with a base ratio of 8:3, if the tangents of the angles at the larger base are 4/3 and 3/4, and the height of the trapezoid is 12 units.
18. Round regular triangle and square. Find, to the nearest triangle after the decimal point, using rounding rules, the ratio of the area of ​​the triangle to the area of ​​the square, taking cos $30^\circ = 0,866$.
19. Find the lateral surface area of ​​a regular quadrangular rectangle if its volume is 36 and its height is 4.
20. Determine the volume of a sphere written into a cylinder whose height is equal to the diameter of the base. The cylinder volume is 128π. Accept $\pi = 3,14$. Write your answer as a decimal fraction with two decimal places, using rounding rules.
