Physics
Written exam

Option

1. From a height of $h=100$ m above the surface of the Moon, a body is thrown vertically down with an initial speed of $v_0=2$ m/s. How long will it take the body to reach the surface of the Moon? The acceleration of free fall on the Moon is $g_{\perp}=1,6$ m/s².

2. To prevent a body with a mass of $m=5$ kg from sliding off a vertical wall, it should be pressed against it with a minimum force of $F=500$ N, directed perpendicular to the plane of the wall. Find the coefficient of friction between the body and the wall. Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s².

3. At one end of a stationary length of a cart with mass $m_T=20$ kg there is a person with mass $m_y=60$ kg. At what speed will the cart move if a person appears at a speed of $v=2$ m/s relative to the cart?

4. At what depth in water is the pressure 5 times greater than atmospheric pressure, which is $p=10^5$ Pa? The density of water is $\rho=10^3$ kg/m³, the acceleration of free fall is $g=10$ m/s².

5. How much heat must be expended in order to bring $m=0,1$ kg of water taken at a temperature $T=273$ K to a boil and turn it into steam? Boiling point of water $T_k=373$ K, specific temperature $c=4200$ J/(kg K), specific temperature $r=22,6 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg. Give your answer in kJ ($1 \cdot 10^3$ J).

6. When heating a gas, the dependence of pressure on absolute temperature was obtained, shown in figure. How does the volume of gas change? Indicate the number of the correct answer: 1) increased, 2) did not change, 3) decreased, 4) there is not enough data to solve the problem.

7. The distance from a point charge has increased 7 times. How many times has the direction of the electric field decreased at a given point?

8. Two conductors are connected in parallel in a circuit; The resistance of the first conductor is 10 times greater than the second. Find the ratio of the power consumed by the first conductor to the power consumed by the second.

9. Determine the wavelength of light whose quantum energy is $W=3,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J. Planck's constant is $h=6,6 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s, the speed of light in vacuum is $c=3 \cdot 10^8$ m/s. Give your answer in nanometers ($1 \text{ nm}=10^{-9}$ m).

10. In front of a biconvex lens with a focal length of $F=1$ m there is an object with a height of $h=2$ m at a distance of $d=3$ m. Find the height of the image of the object.
