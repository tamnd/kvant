Option 1

1. Archimedean force for liquids and gases.

3. $m_2 = 50$ g of wet snow was dropped into a vessel containing $m_1 = 500$ g of water at a temperature of $t = 15^\circ C$. The temperature of the water in the vessel dropped by $\Delta t = 5^\circ C$. How much water was in the snow? Take care of heat loss. The specific heat of melting of ice is $\lambda = 330$ kJ/kg, the specific heat capacity of water is $c = 4,2$ kJ/(kg K).

4. What cross-section should a copper wire be used to install a line from a power plant to a consumer located at a distance of $l = 1$ km in order to transmit a power consumption of $P = 8$ kW? The voltage at the station is $U = 130$ V, the permissible demand line is $k = 8\%$. Copper resistivity $q = 1,7 \cdot 10^{-8}$ Ohm m.

Option 2

1. Addition of forces. Moment of power. Condition of equilibrium of a body with a fixed axis of rotation.

2. The phenomenon of thermoelectron emission. Electric current in a vacuum. Electronic tubes - diode and triode. Using a diode to rectify AC current. Electronuculation tube.

3. A full sphere with a hard shell, the mass of which is $m = 10$ g, is filled with hydrogen. Hydrogen volume $V = 10$ l. The temperature of hydrogen and the air surrounding the ball is $t = 0^\circ C$. Find the pressure of hydrogen in the ball if the resulting lifting force of the ball is zero. Atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 10^5$ Pa, molar mass of air $\mu_B = 29 \cdot 10^{-3}$ kg/mol, universal gas constant $R = 8,3$ J/(mol K).

4. The optical system consists of two proprietary lenses with focal lengths $F_1 = 20$ cm and $F_2 = 10$ cm. Distance between lenses $l = 30$ cm. A luminous point at a distance of $d = 30$ cm from the first lens. At what distance from the second lens will the image be obtained?

Option 3

1. Absolute and relative humidity.

2. Dispersion of light. Spectrocopl. Infrared and ultraviolet part of the spectrum. Emission spectra. Absorption spectra. The concept of spectral analysis.

3. A bullet with a mass of $m = 10$ g, flying horizontally at a speed of $v = 500$ m/s, hit a freely suspended wooden block with a mass of $M = 10$ kg and got stuck in it, going deeper by $l = 20$ cm. Find the average force of resistance of the tree to the movement of the bullet.

4. A single copper ball with a diameter of $d = 1$ cm is placed in oil. Oil density $\varrho_0 = 800$ kg/m³. What is the amount of contamination in a uniform electric field when a ball is suspended in oil? The charged electric field is directed vertically upward and is equal to $E = 36000$ V/m. Copper density $\varrho = 8600$ kg/m³.

1. Tell motors. Physical basis of their work. Heat engine efficiency and its maximum value.

2. Electrolysis. Faraday's laws for electrolysis.

3. An inclined plane makes an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizon. Forces are sent upward along it, which travels a distance of $l = 16$ m during $t_1 = 2$ s, after which it slides up into the oil? What is the coefficient of friction between the surface and the body?

4. The height of the flame of the burner is $h = 2,5$ cm. The lens gives on the screen an image of this flame with a height of $H_1 = 7,5$ cm. Without touching the lens, the burner was moved $l = 2$ cm further from the lens and moving the screen, we again received a sharp image of the flame, but now with a height of $H_2 = 5$ cm. Find the shape distance of the lens.

Calculate them, knowing that the masses of the balls are $m_1 = 2$ kg and $m_2 = 4$ kg, and the energy of the coiled spring is $E = 6$ J. The mass of the spring is not taken into account.

4. Two resistors with a resistance of $R = 200$ Ohm each are connected to a current source, first in series and then in parallel. The power dissipated by each resistor appears to be the same in general cases. Find the emf of the source if, with a series connection of friction, the current in the circuit is $I = 0,5$ A.
