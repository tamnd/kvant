⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. The number $\bar{ab}$ ($a>b, b\neq0$) is such that the difference $\bar{ab}-\bar{ba}$ is a perfect square. Find all two-digit numbers that have this property.

Are there three-digit numbers with a similar property: $abc-cba$ ($a>c$) - a perfect square?

2. In the edges depicted on figure (in English two is two, six is ​​six, twelve is twelve), identical letters correspond to the same numbers, and different numbers correspond to different ones. The asterisks can be any numbers. Decipher these ribs.

3. Tanya and Natasha, taken together, have 40 more hairs on their heads than Petya and Vasya, and Tanya and Petya have 20 more hairs than Natasha and Vasya. “If I,” thought Ivan Ivanovich, their neighbor, “grew as much hair as Tanya has, and then three more times as much as Petya, then I would have as much hair as Vasya and three Natashas combined.” What color is Ivan Ivanovich's hair?

4. Each cell of square $5\times5$ contains either a kitten or a cat (at figure kittens are black and cats are white). If there is a cat on a straight line between two kittens, then the kittens do not see each other. What is the maximum number of kittens that can be placed in the cells of a square so that not a single kitten sees the other kittens?
