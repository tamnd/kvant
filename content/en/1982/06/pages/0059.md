6. agccos $\frac{3\sqrt{330}}{55}$. Solution. The task, very clearly, implies a dihedral angle with an edge (MA), the faces of which are the half-plane of the MAB plane containing point B, and the half-plane of the MAC plane containing point C (Fig. 1). To find the desired angle, we project the vector MB onto MA:
$$\vec{MB}_1 = \frac{|\vec{MB}| \cos(\vec{MB}, \vec{MA})}{|\vec{MA}|}$$

(*)

Multiplying the numerator and denominator by |MA|, we obtain $\vec{MB}_1 = \frac{\vec{MB} \cdot \vec{MA}}{|\vec{MA}|^2}$ $\vec{MA} = \frac{-6}{6}$ $\vec{MA} = -\vec{MA} = (-2; -1; 1)$ (thus, figure 1 turns out to be odd in some sense: in fact, the vectors $\vec{MB}_2 = \vec{MB} - \vec{MB}_1 = (0; 4; 4)$; then $\vec{MB}_2 \perp \vec{MA}$ and the point $B_2$ lies on the face of the dihedral angle under consideration. Similarly, we find the vector $\vec{MC} = \vec{MC} \cdot \vec{MA}$ $\vec{MA} = \frac{(10; 3; \frac{5}{3}; -\frac{5}{3})}{|\vec{MA}|^2}$ (thus, on figure 1 there is another oddity: in fact, $\vec{MC} \cdot \vec{MA} > |\vec{MA}|$; this again does not affect the correctness of our calculations; if you have a need, make the correct one figure) and $\vec{MC}_2 = \vec{MC} - \vec{MC}_1 = \frac{(-1; \frac{1}{3}; \frac{10}{3}; \frac{8}{3})}{|\vec{MA}|^2}$ The angle between the vectors $\vec{MB}_2$ and. $\vec{MC}_2$ - exclude $\cos(\vec{MB}_2, \vec{MC}_2) = \frac{\vec{MB}_2 \cdot \vec{MC}_2}{|\vec{MB}_2| \cdot |\vec{MC}_2|} = \frac{3\sqrt{330}}{55}$.
