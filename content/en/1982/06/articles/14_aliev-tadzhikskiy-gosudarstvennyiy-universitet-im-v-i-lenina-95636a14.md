Matematika
Written exam

Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. A section of a cone parallel to its base passes through the center of a circumscribed ball around the cone. The volume of the cut off cone is equal to half the volume of the cone. Find the angle of inclination of the generatrix of the cone to the plane of its base.

2. Explore and graph the function $y=3x^2-x^3$.

3. Find the denominator and the sum of the seven terms of the geometric progression if $a_1=36, a_7=\frac{4}{81}$.

4. Solve the equation
$$25\sqrt{x}-124 \cdot 5\sqrt{x}=125.$$

5. Solve the inequality
$$\log_3 x + \log_{\sqrt{3}} x + \log_{\frac{1}{3}} x < 6.$$

Option 2
(Physics Department)

1. In an equilateral trapezoid $ABCD$ $|AB|=|CD|=a$ cm. The diagonal divides the angle at the lower base, the magnitude of which is $a$, in half. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

2. Find the area of ​​the figure bounded by the parabola $y=x^2$, tangent to it at the point with abscissa 2 and x-axis.

3. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} \log_y x + \log_x y = \frac{5}{2} \\ x \cdot y = 27. \end{cases}$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{\sin^2 x} - \frac{3}{\cos^2 x} = 1.$$

5. Solve the inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{\sqrt{x+2}} < 3^{-x}.$$

Option 3
(Faculty of Geology)

1. Given a terraed $ABCD$ whose edge is equal to $a$. A plane is drawn through the vertex $B$ and the middle of the edges $AD$ and $DC$. Find the perimeter of the shapes obtained in the section.

2. Examine the function $y=(5+\cos x) \times (12-\cos x)$ for an extremum.

3. Find the values ​​of $x$ if
$$\left( 6 \frac{3}{7} - \frac{3}{4} \cdot x - 2 \right) \cdot 2,8 - 1 \frac{3}{4} : \frac{1}{20} = 35.$$

4. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} 3^x \cdot 2^y = 972 \\ \log_{\sqrt{3}} (x-y) = 2. \end{cases}$$

5. Solve the inequality
$$\sin x + \sin 2x < 0.$$

Option 4
(Faculty of Planning and Economics)

1. In triangle $ABC$, draw a line $A_1B_1$ parallel to the base $AB$, so that the area of ​​the right angle $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ is the largest ($C_1$ and $D_1$ lie on $AB$).

2. Find the area of ​​the figure bounded by the parabola $y=-x^2+2x+8$ and the x-axis.

3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x^2+2xy+y^2-x-y = 6 \\ x-2y = 3. \end{cases}$$

4. Solve the equation
$$2 \sin^2 x - 3 \cos x = 3.$$

5. Solve the equation
$$x^{1-\frac{1}{3}} \lg x^2 = 10^{-\frac{2}{3}}.$$
