Problem 9 (MEPhI, 1981). The equation of the plane $\alpha$ and $\beta$ is $2x + 3y + 4z - 8 = 0$ and $4x + y + 3z - 6 = 0$, $p = \alpha \cap \beta$. a) Determine the coordinates of the point of intersection of straight line $p$ with planes $xOy$ and $yOz$. b) Determine the quantities between straight line $p$ and plane $xOz$.

The coordinates of the point $A = p \cap xOy$ are the solution to the system
$$\begin{cases}
2x + 3y + 4z - 8 = 0 \\
4x + y + 3z - 6 = 0 \\
z = 0.
\end{cases}$$

Coordinates of point $B = p \cap yOz$ - solution of the system
$$\begin{cases}
2x + 3y + 4z - 8 = 0 \\
4x + y + 3z - 6 = 0 \\
x = 0.
\end{cases}$$

Now, if $\alpha$ is the desired angle and $\beta$ is the angle between the vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{n} = (1; 0; 1)$, then $\sin \alpha = |\cos \beta|$. Answer. a) $A(1; 2; 0)$, $B(0; 0; 2)$. b) arc $\sin \frac{2}{3}$.
