⟦folio 48⟧

Moscow Architectural Institute

Mathematics

Oral exam assignment
1. Prove identity
$$\sin^2 \alpha \cdot \cos^4 \alpha = \frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{32} \cos 2\alpha - \frac{1}{16} \cos 4\alpha - \frac{1}{32} \cos 6\alpha.$$
2. Expression
$$\log_m A \cdot \log_n A + \log_p A \cdot \log_m A$$
present it as a work.
3. Construct a graph of the function $y = \frac{|x|}{x}$.
4. Solve the equation
a) $\lg(2^x + x - 41) = x(1 - \lg 5)$;
b) $\cos x + \cos 2x + \cos 3x + \cos 4x + \cos 5x = 0$.
5. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x - \sqrt{x+y} = 2\sqrt{3} \\ (x+y) \cdot 2^{y-x} = 3. \end{cases}$$
6. Solve inequality
a) $0,11^{\log_3 \frac{4x-1}{3x+2}} > 1$;
b) $3\sqrt{6+x-x^2} > 4x-2$.
7. Find
a) $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^2}{1-\cos x}$;
b) $\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{\cos 2-\cos 2x}{x^2-|x|}$.
8. Find the maximum volume of the triangular straight line $MABC$, at the base of which lies the isosceles right triangle $ABC$ ($|AB| = |BC|$), if $[MB] \perp (ABC)$ and $|MA| = \sqrt{3}$.
9. Calculate the volume of the function obtained by rotating around the x-axis of a plane function bounded by the curve $y = \cos x$ and the x-axis, $x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}; \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$.

Physics
Oral exam assignment
1. A load of mass $m = 50$ kg moves along a horizontal plane under the action of a force $F = 500$ N, directed at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizon. Treyagur coefficient on the plane $\mu = 0,5$. Determine the acceleration of the load.
2. A ball, tied by a thread to a suspension, describes a circle in the horizontal plane, having a constant speed. Determine the speed of the ball and the period of its rotation in a circle if the length of the thread is $l = 1$ m, and its angle with the vertical is $\alpha = 45^\circ$.
3. A wooden block lies on an inclined plane. With what force must the block be pressed against the inclined plane so that it remains at rest on it? The mass of the block is $m = 4$ kg, the length of the inclined plane is $l = 1$ m, the height is $h = 0,6$ m. The treyagur coefficient about the plane is $\mu = 0,4$.
4. Two balls of mass $m_1 = 4$ kg and $m_2 = 6$ kg are held together by a rod whose mass is $m_3 = 2$ kg.

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Worker $A$ can finish some work 5 days later than worker $B$, and 9 days later than worker $C$. Workers $A$ and $B$ working together can finish this work in as many days as worker $C$ can finish it. In how many days can each job individually complete this job?
2. Solve the equation
$$\sin 2x - \sin x = \cos x - \frac{1}{2}.$$
3. Find the most and most values ​​of the function $y = \ln(2x) - x^2 + x$ on the interval $\left[\frac{1}{2}; 2\right]$.
4. Calculate (by making figure) the area of ​​the function bounded by the lines $y = x - \frac{x^2}{2}$.
