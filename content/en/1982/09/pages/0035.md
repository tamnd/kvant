Tasks

M761 — M765; F773 — F777

M761. Through an arbitrary point $P$ on side $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, parallel to its medians $AK$ and $CL$, straight lines are drawn intersecting sides $BC$ and $AB$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively (Fig. 1). Prove that the medians $AK$ and $CL$ divide the segment $EF$ into three equal parts.

E. Gotman

M762. Prove that for any positive numbers $a, b, c$ the following inequalities hold:

$$a + b + c \leq \frac{a^2 + b^2}{2c} + \frac{b^2 + c^2}{2a} + \frac{c^2 + a^2}{2b} \leq \frac{a^3}{bc} + \frac{b^3}{ca} + \frac{c^3}{ab}.$$

M763*. Given a parallelogram $ABCD$, different from a rhombus. A straight line symmetrical to straight line $AB$ relative to diagonal $AC$ intersects at point $Q$ a straight line symmetrical to straight line $DC$ relative to diagonal $DB$ (Fig. 2). Find the relation $|QA|:|QD|$ if the relation $|AC|:|BD| = k$ is known.

V. Dubrovsky

M764*. Prove that each of the equations a) $x^2 + y^3 = z^5$; b) $x^2 + y^3 + z^5 = t^7$ has infinitely many solutions in natural numbers.

O. Mazurov

M765. Let $B$ be a finite set of points on the plane that do not belong to the same line. a) Prove that there are three points in the set $B$ and that the circle passing through them does not contain other sets $B$.

b) * By triangulation of the set $B$ we define a family of triangles with a set of vertices $B$ that do not overlap each other and form a convex polygon (the triangulation of the set $B$ can be obtained by connecting its points with disjoint segments, as long as this is possible). Prove that for any $B$ there is a triangulation such that the circle circumscribed about any triangle of this triangulation does not contain the sets $B$ within itself. Specify construction method
