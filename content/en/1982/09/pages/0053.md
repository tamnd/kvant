⟦folio 51⟧

Yes, I know, this is when they write to work for someone who is guilty, but who was not convicted. But I also remembered: our class teacher said that each student in the school garden needs to be assigned an area of ​​work... So, in this case, should we allocate something?

And I remember my dad talking about how he started working at the factory and his salary was set at 120 rubles. Here “determined” means established? Appointed? Strange word! There are so many meanings, and all are different! WHAT did the teacher want from you?

Tanya said:

—Have you forgotten that a word can have many meanings? By the way, Petyunya, among all the meanings of the word “definition,” you did not name one more, namely: “A definition is a sentence that tells what a given concept means.” But this is exactly what the teacher wanted from you. He wanted you to say that he would introduce the concept of “fedodalism”! And in order for you to know how to do this, you first need to know what a “concept” is. Do you know this?

“I have no idea about the concept,” Petya composed.

- Jokes aside. The fact is that the word “concept” in Russian also has different meanings, which is clear even from your wit. So what is a “concept”?

Both Sharikovs began to think. The younger one sniffled, the older one sighed, but no one dared to say anything.

“Look,” Tanya said and quickly drew three different triangles.

- What is this?
- Triangle!
- And this?
- And this is a triangle.
- And this?
- And this is a triangle.

— Question, boys: why do you use the same word “triangle” to call such different things? Their beds and sizes are different. And the color is different. And in general they bear little resemblance to each other!

- Like “why”! - both shouted out loud. - It’s clear! Because they are all triangles!

— It’s not clear. Why are they triangles?

- Yes, because they have three corners! - said Kolya.

“Formed by a closed broken line of three segments,” added Petya.

- Young! Let's follow your thoughts. You have mentally discarded all the unimportant attributes of many objects and left, in your opinion, the most essential! This is how CONCEPT arises! For a whole class of objects, perhaps even an infinite number of them, unimportant attributes are discarded, and one attribute is left - the most general and most essential! And pay attention. In this case, the concept of a triangle “in general” arises, although in nature there is no “triangle in general”: each drawn triangle is unique! So, my friends, fill it out once and for all: any science begins precisely when concepts arise in it, and not specific objects.

- Why do we really need “concepts” so much? I don't understand.

- Don't you understand? Just imagine for a moment that there is no concept in geometry, and we are dealing with specific objects. Do you know that the sum of the angles in a triangle is 180°?

- Who doesn’t know this!

“So,” said Tanya. “You have a specific triangle - wooden or metal.” You apply a protractor to its corners and make sure that it is so, the sum of the angles is 180°. But this is yours, a specific one. And the other one, of a different shape?

- I'll measure again!

- And the new one... the thousandth? The millionth? You will spend your whole life making these changes and still won’t prove anything. Can you be sure that for the first million this amount will be the same? No, until your assumption is proven. When you proved this theorem in class during a geometry lesson, you are in your proof.
