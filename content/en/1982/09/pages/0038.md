M731. Two people answer this question: the first one names a natural number from 2 to 9; the second multiplies this number by an arbitrary natural number from 2 to 9, etc.; the one who first produces a product of large a) thousand wins; b) million. Who evokes with the correct cerra - the beginner or his partner?

a) Problem solved by analysis from the end (Fig. 1). If eventually one of the players $I$ offers the other player - call him $B$ - any of the numbers from 112 to 999, then player $B$ wins (by multiplying by 9). Therefore, if $B$ offers his partner $I$ any of the numbers from 56 to 111, then $I$ will get at least 112 (he will then multiply by only 2) and no more than 999 (he will then multiply by 9), and player $B$ will be guaranteed a win. Now it is clear that if $I$ names any of the numbers from 7 to 55, then $B$ can fall into the interval that is favorable to him [56; 111]. Finally, so that $I$ must fall into this fatal interval for him [7; 55], player $B$ just needs to name any of the numbers 4, 5, 6 at the first step. So, the beginner wins with the correct number.
