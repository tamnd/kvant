If we go through all the data along the chain of definitions, we will move on to more and more general concepts, each of which will be expressed by some word in the language. If we suggest that this chain of definitions continues endlessly, it turns out that there are many words in the language of the infinite! But that's not true. After all, we have touched so far only on two links in the chain of definitions. But you can go further. What is a “parallelogram”?
