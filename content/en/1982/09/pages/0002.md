On this page we reproduce figure by K. E. Tsiolkovsky from his “Album of Space Travel”. The “Album” was conceived by the scientist as the first manual for future conquerors of international spaces. Tsiolkovsky was the first to show, on the basis of a strictly scientific analysis, that in space flights, immediately after the engine stops working, a state of weightlessness will arise. Particularly creative in the scientist’s work was the organizational combination of precise calculation and figurative clarity, and the “Album of Space Travel” is a vivid example of this.
