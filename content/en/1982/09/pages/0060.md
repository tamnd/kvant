3. "Capillary". Suggest ways to measure the diameter of a capillary, which is a drying in the middle part of a long thick-walled glass tube. Tube diameter is 3 mm, tube length is 3 cm, capillary diameter is about 1 µm, capillary length is about 1 mm.

4. "Drop". Two molten drops of tin and zinc, slowly leaving, solidified. It turned out that the tin drop has a spherical shape, while the zinc drop has flat edges. How to explain this?

5. "Evaporation". Determine experimentally the rate of evaporation (in kg/s) of water from a half-filled cylindrical vessel (glass). Make numerical estimates taking into account indoor air humidity. Consider the following case:

a) The container is open.

b) The vessel is covered with foil, in which a round hole is made with an area of ​​20% of the cross-sectional area of ​​the vessel.

b) The vessel is covered with foil, in which many randomly located holes with a diameter of about 100 microns are made. The total area of ​​the holes is 10% of the cross-sectional area of ​​the vessel.

6. "Wool". Wool thread is woven from many individual fibers. Investigate the tensile strength of such a thread depending on its length. Explain the results of the experiment.
