ON THE STAMPS - K. E. TSIOLKOVSKY

It is 125 years since the birth of Konstantin Eduardovich Tsiolkovsky, a remarkable Russian scientist, founder of the theory of space flight.
Tsiolkovsky was the first to create the structure of the mathematical theory of motion of single-stage and multi-stage rockets, substantiate the possibility of flights to the planets of the solar system, develop and scientifically substantiate the design of an all-metal airship, and give the foundations of the theory of a liquid jet engine.
Tsiolkovsky's creative legacy is rich in original ideas, many of which have been implemented quite recently, while others are still awaiting their implementation.
The name of K. E. Tsiolkovsky is extremely popular all over the world, which is naturally reflected in the philately. The first Soviet stamp dedicated to Tsiolkovsky was issued in 1951; the second - in 1957, on the centenary of the scientist’s birth. The 1957 stamp with the corresponding overprint turned out to be the world's first stamp dedicated to the first artificial satellite of the Earth. Since then, many stamps and postal blocks dedicated to K. E. Tsiolkovsky have appeared in the Soviet Union and abroad.

V. Rudov
