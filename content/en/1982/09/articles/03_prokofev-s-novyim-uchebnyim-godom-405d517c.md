Happy new school year!

Dear guys!

The year 1982 was rich in events of important political significance, each of which had a direct bearing on our school affairs.

The 60th anniversary of the formation of the USSR is celebrated. How far our country has come over the years! It was the first country in the world to pass rubles to complete secondary education for the younger generation. She can be proud that this new amount of rubles provides a deep scientific level of training for young people. Graduates of the Soviet school are characterized by a deep penetration into both the understanding of social laws and the material world. The harmonious development of a person is achieved through the knowledge of scientific truths, continuous feasible participation in socially useful labor affairs, and the acquisition of skills of high moral behavior. The school has become the most important state educational institution in which young builders of a communist society are formed.

The May (1982) plenum of the CPSU Central Committee put forward the USSR Food Program. It's up to you to work on turning it into reality. We have more work ahead of us. All the achievements of human thought are quickly translated into concrete deeds, new machines, effective technologies. Everything perfect becomes labor, the foundation of an enterprise. And you need to be well prepared. A modern worker is a person who deeply understands the processes occurring in material production and has the ability to use them with maximum finishing. That is why it is very important in schools to develop a system of interest groups, elective courses, school scientific societies, and various sections. Depending on the interests of students, additional knowledge should be added to the foundation of basic school courses. The best teachers not only enrich your memory with knowledge. They develop your abilities in every possible way and try to give you elements of a creative approach to the analysis of phenomena. Modern pedagogy is unthinkable without the use of techniques that develop and excite thoughts.

The initiative to create the magazine that you are holding in your hands belongs to the country's outstanding scientists and teachers. This is a journal of the USSR Academy of Sciences and the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences. It belongs to the genre of publication that is by no means easy reading. Nevertheless, its circulation exceeds 175 thousand copies. For many young people, «Kvant» becomes the first landmark on the path to modern science. Isn’t this an indicator of the great desire of young people in the world of mathematics, theoretical physics and other related sciences?

Let me wish you new success in mastering the riches of knowledge of nature and society. Try to delve into the existing phenomenon. Remember that not only the number of materials you completed is determined by the success of the study, but also by the depth of knowledge of this material and the ability to actively apply it in practice.

Minister of Education of the USSR
Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences
M. A. PROKOFIEV
