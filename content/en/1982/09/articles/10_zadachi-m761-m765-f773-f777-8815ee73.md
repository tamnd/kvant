Tasks

M761 — M765; F773 — F777

M761. Through an arbitrary point $P$ on side $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, parallel to its medians $AK$ and $CL$, straight lines are drawn intersecting sides $BC$ and $AB$ at points $E$ and $F$, respectively (Fig. 1). Prove that the medians $AK$ and $CL$ divide the segment $EF$ into three equal parts.

E. Gotman

M762. Prove that for any positive numbers $a, b, c$ the following inequalities hold:

$$a + b + c \leq \frac{a^2 + b^2}{2c} + \frac{b^2 + c^2}{2a} + \frac{c^2 + a^2}{2b} \leq \frac{a^3}{bc} + \frac{b^3}{ca} + \frac{c^3}{ab}.$$

M763*. Given a parallelogram $ABCD$, different from a rhombus. A straight line symmetrical to straight line $AB$ relative to diagonal $AC$ intersects at point $Q$ a straight line symmetrical to straight line $DC$ relative to diagonal $DB$ (Fig. 2). Find the relation $|QA|:|QD|$ if the relation $|AC|:|BD| = k$ is known.

V. Dubrovsky

M764*. Prove that each of the equations a) $x^2 + y^3 = z^5$; b) $x^2 + y^3 + z^5 = t^7$ has infinitely many solutions in natural numbers.

O. Mazurov

M765. Let $B$ be a finite set of points on the plane that do not belong to the same line. a) Prove that there are three points in the set $B$ and that the circle passing through them does not contain other sets $B$.

b) * By triangulation of the set $B$ we define a family of triangles with a set of vertices $B$ that do not overlap each other and form a convex polygon (the triangulation of the set $B$ can be obtained by connecting its points with disjoint segments, as long as this is possible). Prove that for any $B$ there is a triangulation such that the circle circumscribed about any triangle of this triangulation does not contain the sets $B$ within itself. Specify construction method

c) * Prove that if no points of the set $B$ lie on the same circle, then the triangulation described in paragraph 6) is unique.

F. Vayshtein

F773.*) On a rough inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\alpha$ lies a body to which a light, loose thread is tied. The free end of the thread is passed through a small hole in the plane. At the initial moment, the body lies on a plane so that the thread is horizontal (Fig. 3). The thread begins to be pulled out slowly; in this case, by the time it reaches the hole, the body describes half a circle. Find the coefficient of friction of the body on the plane. (10)

F774. For multi-tails, there are values ​​of temperature $T_{\text{tr}}$ and pressure $p_{\text{tr}}$ at which all three phases of the substance - gaseous, liquid and solid - are in equilibrium with each other - the so-called triple point. For water $T_{\text{tr}} = 0,0075^{\circ}\text{C}$, $p_{\text{tr}} = 4,58$ mmHg. st; the specific vaporization temperature at the triple point is $r = 595,8$ cal/g, the specific melting temperature is $\lambda = 79,7$ cal/g. Find the specific temperament (primary transition from solid to gaseous state) of water near the triple point.

V. Davydov

F775. The high-precision multi-limit ampere contains a modified separate shutt for each limit. The ampere switches on at the 10 mA limit, and it shows a current of $I_1 = 2,95$ mA; when it was switched to the 3 mA limit it showed $I_2 = 2,90$ mA. What was the current strength in the circuit before the amplifier was connected? (9)

A. Zilberman

F776. A small kettle is filled to the top with warm water ($t_1 = 30^{\circ}\text{C}$). The kettle remains at 1 degree for $\tau = 5$ minutes. To prevent the kettle from cooling down, hot water is dripped into it ($t_2 = 45^{\circ}\text{C}$). The mass of one drop is $m_k = 0,2$ g. How many drops per minute must be dripped into the kettle so that the temperature is maintained at $30^{\circ}\text{C}$? How many degrees does the water remain in one minute if you start dripping into the second cup?

Assume that the temperature of the water in the kettle equalizes very quickly. Excess water pours out of the spout. The kettle contains 0.3 liters of water. Ambient air temperature $t_0 = 20^{\circ}\text{C}$. (8)

A. Zilberman
