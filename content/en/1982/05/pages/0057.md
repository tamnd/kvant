Optical systems
1. A parallel beam of rays emerges from the system (telecoppic system).
2. The image is located at a distance from the line
$$f = \frac{Fd}{2d - F}.$$
The image will be valid at $d > F/2$ and minimum at $d < F/2$.

3. $F = \frac{n_1R}{2(n_1 - n)}$; a plot of $F$ versus $n$ is shown in figure 1. If $n < n_1$, the focal length of the lens is positive (converging lens); if $n > n_1$, the focal length is negative (divergent lens); if $n = n_1$, the lens turns into a plane-parallel plane with an infinitely large focal length.

4. Since the outer surface of the eye is flat, the parallel beam of rays does not experience refraction on this surface, regardless of the refractive index of the external environment. Therefore, even if part of the eye is in the water and part is in the air, a sufficiently distant floating addition (partly it is in the water and partly in the air) will be clearly visible to the crocodile.
