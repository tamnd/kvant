How long is the gamma quantum itself invisible in a cloud chamber?

There are two mandatory conditions for the birth of a couple. First, the energy of the gamma quantum must exceed the sum of the rest energies of the components of the pair: $E_\gamma > 2m_e c^2 = 1,02$ MeV. And secondly, there must be a nucleus or an electron, which will take on excess energy and momentum. Otherwise, the conservation of energy and momentum $^*$) is violated.

The birth of $e^+e^-$ pairs was due to one incomprehensible effect that researchers encountered back in 1930. Experiments have shown that hard gamma rays are absorbed in matter much more strongly than predicted by theory. As it turned out, the “anomalous” absorption was associated with the formation of pairs. And it is quite likely that, if Anderson and Blackett and Occalini had hesitated, part of the discovery of the positron would have belonged to physicists dealing with the absorption of gamma rays.

"The melody turns into a violin"

These are the words of Academician S.I. Vavilov. At one of his lectures, he talks about the reverse process of the angulation reaction, when a flying gamma quantum produces an electron + positron pair:

$$\gamma \rightarrow e^+ + e^-.$$

This process of transforming an electromagnetic field into matter was discovered almost simultaneously in 1933 by four groups of physicists - in France, Germany, England and the USA.
