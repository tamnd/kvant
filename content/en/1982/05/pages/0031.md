⟦folio 29⟧

Exercise

1. Is it possible to place crosses on 24 squares of a squared chessboard so that when any three horizontal and three vertical lines are crossed out, at least six crosses remain uncrossed?

2. Let $A$ be a natural number. Let us denote by $b$ the number formed by the last two digits of the number $A$, by $a$ the number formed by the remaining digits of the number $A$ (for example, for $A=2906$ we get $a=29$, $b=06=6$). Prove that the number $A$ is divisible by 7 if and only if $a$) $2a+b$ is divisible by 7; 6) $5a-b$ is divisible by 7.

3. Is there a year in which the thirteenth is never a day of prayer? And what is the maximum number of times it can be a supporter?

4. On each side of the triangle $ABC$ a rectangle is constructed so that their vertices touch in pairs (see figure). Prove that the lines connecting the vertices of triangle $ABC$ with the corresponding vertices of the resulting triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ intersect at one point.

5. Is it possible to cut out one cell from a square of size $5\times5$ cells so that the resulting figure can be cut along a linear grid into eight rectangular strips of size $1\times3$ cells?

These tasks were suggested to us by F. Bartenev.
A. Kartuzov, A. Savin.
