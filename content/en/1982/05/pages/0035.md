2. People stand on a downward escalator at a distance of $L = 1$ m from each other. Each flow of passengers will be prescribed:
a) the escalator duty officer standing at the bottom; b) watching while standing on another escalator moving up? The speed of movement of the escalators is taken equal to $v = 2$ m/s.

We have already said that the flow depends on the reporting system, in this case, on the observer. Relative to the person on duty on the escalator, passengers descend at a speed equal to the speed of the escalator, therefore

$$\Phi_1 = nv = \frac{v}{L} = 2 \text{ } 1/\text{c}.$$

From the point of view of an observer located on an escalator moving up with speed $v$, the flow of passengers will be twice as large, since relative to it the flow speed is $v_{\text{отн}} = 2v$, and the flow

$$\Phi_2 = nv_{\text{отн}} = 2nv = 4 \text{ } 1/\text{c}.$$

3. A truck with a carrying capacity of $M = 2$ t is loaded with arbuzami using transport. The average weight of an arbuz is $m = 5$ kg, the speed of transport is $v = 0,5$ m/s, the distance between the arbuz on the belt is $L = 0,75$ m. Find the loading time.

In this case we are dealing with mass flow. By definition it is equal to $\Phi_m = mnv = mv/L$. Then the required loading time

$$t = \frac{M}{\Phi_m} = \frac{ML}{mv} = 600 \quad c = 10 \text{ мин.}$$

4. Estimate the speed of movement of electrons in a metal conductor when electric current flows through it.

We will proceed from the fact that according to the right safety precautions, it is allowed to pass electric current with a density of up to $10 \text{ A}/\text{mm}^2 = 10^7 \text{ A}/\text{m}^2$ through wires with the natural effect of cooling.
