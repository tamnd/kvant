6) Given a sequence of three numbers $(a_n, b_n, c_n)$ such that $|a_n| \leq 1$, $|b_n| \leq 1$, $|c_n| \leq 1$ and $a_n + b_n + c_n = 0$ ($n=1, 2, \dots$). Based on it, a new sequence of three $(x_n, y_n, z_n)$ is constructed, in which $x_0 = y_0 = z_0 = 0$, and each triple $(x_n, y_n, z_n)$ with $n \geq 1$ is obtained from the previous $(x_{n-1}, y_{n-1}, z_{n-1})$ by adding to $x_{n-1}$ one of the numbers $a_n, b_n, c_n$ of our choice, to $y_{n-1}$ - another, to $z_{n-1}$ - a third. Is it possible to assume that all numbers $x_n, y_n, z_n$ will be no more than 1 in abscillot value or at least limited by some constant? C) Find out similar questions for sequences of four numbers.

N. Rozov
