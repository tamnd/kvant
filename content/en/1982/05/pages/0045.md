⟦folio 43⟧

10. How much will the focus of a thin collecting lens shift if a plane-parallel glass plate with a thickness of $d=6$ mm and a refractive index of $n=1,5$ is placed close to the lens on the path of the rays passing through it by redistribution to the main optical axis? (The focal length of the lens is much larger than its diameter, so the tangents of the angles of incidence and refraction can be replaced by their sines.)

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

1. A small block lies on the edge of a horizontal board $l=2$ m long (Fig. 5). After what time will the block slide off if the doxa begins to move horizontally to the right with an acceleration of $a=3$ m/s? The coefficient of friction between the block and the board is $\mu=0,2$.

2. Two bodies with masses $m_1=0,2$ kg and $m_2=0,1$ kg are connected by a rubber band and are located on the main horizontal surface. The system moves under the action of a constant horizontal force $F=6$ N applied to the first body. Find the potential energy of deformation of the rope if it is known that under the action of a force $F_0=1$ N it stretches by $x_0=5$ cm. The movement is considered steady; neglect the mass of the rubber band; friction can be ignored.

3. In a vertical cylinder with a volume of $V=200$ cm$^3$ under a heavy piston there is a gas at a temperature of $T=300$ K. The mass of the piston is $m=50$ kg, its area is $S=50$ cm$^2$. To increase the temperature of the gas by $\Delta T=100$, an amount of heat $Q=46,5$ J was added to it. Find the change in the internal energy of the gas. Atmospheric pressure $p_0=10^5$ Pa. Free pressure acceleration $g=10$ m/s$^2$. Friction can be ignored.

4. The cylinder contains air with a relative humidity of $r_1=60\%$ and a temperature of $t_1=10^\circ$C. What will be the relative humidity if the temperature is increased to $t_2=100^\circ$C and the volume is reduced by a piston by $k=4$ times? The pressure of saturated water vapor at a temperature of $t_1$ is $p_1=9$ mmHg. Art. Normal atmospheric pressure is $p_0=760$ mmHg. Art. $=10^5$Pa. Steam can be considered an ideal gas. Ignore the mass of the piston.

5. The projection apparatus gives a sharp image of the range on the screen, at a distance of $f=4$ m from the lens. A converging lens with an optical power of $D=0,25$ ditr was placed close to the lens. How far do you need to move the screen to keep the image sharp? The distance from the range to the lens is constant.

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. The dependence of the velocity model $v_1$ of the first body on time $t$ is drawn to obtain AMB (Fig. 6). In time $t_1$ this body has covered the same path as the second body moving at a constant speed $v_2=50$ m/s. Find the initial speed $v_0$ of the first body.

2. In the circuit shown at figure 7, $R_1=10$ Ohm and $R_2=20$ Ohm. Find the ratio of the voltage across the capacitor condensates $C_1$ and $C_2$.

3. Two constant voltage batteries with emf $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ are connected in series and connected to an external resistor. If the polarity of the second battery is reversed, then the amount of heat released in the resistor in $t=1$ s will decrease by $n=9$ times. Find the values ​​of $\varepsilon_2$ if $\varepsilon_1=6$ V. The internal resistance of the batteries can be considered.

4. The luminous points are located on a segment of length $l$ along the main optical axis of a thin collecting lens with a focal length of $F=20$ cm. The middle of the segment is at a distance of $d=30$ cm from the lens, and the lens gives a real image of all points, magnifying the segment by $F=5,33$ times. Determine the length $l$ of the segment.

5. At a distance of $d=12$ cm from the pole of the internal spherical mirror, the radius of which is $R=16$ cm, a point light source $S$ was placed. The mirror was cut into two equal parts (the cut line passes through the main optical axis of the mirror) and they were moved apart at a distance of $2r=1$ cm symmetrically relative to the optical axis (Fig. 8). Find the distance between the source images.
