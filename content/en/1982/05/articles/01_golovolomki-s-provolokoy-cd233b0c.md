In these puzzles you need to free the wire ducklings from the blue strings in which they are entangled. However, in the lower right puzzle you can free only one duckling - the other two (held by the legs) will not be able to escape from the girl. These puzzles are easy to make using scraps of wire and just some thread or string. Any wire will do, but it is better to take single-ash copper wire with colored insulation. Be especially careful when tying the figures with laces, follow figure exactly. Keep in mind that sometimes it is enough to pass the thread on the wrong side of the loop and the puzzle will not work. It is necessary to maintain the correct, exactly according to figure, proportions of figures and loops, and the length of the laces. For example, the flower should be larger than the duckling's foot, but smaller than the loop at the end of the lace.
