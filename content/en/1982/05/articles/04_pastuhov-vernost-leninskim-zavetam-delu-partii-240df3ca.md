⟦folio 2⟧

Loyalty to Lenin's covenants,
cause of the party

The 19th Congress of the All-Russian Leninist Communist Youth Union is an exciting and important event in the life of the young generation of our country. The idea and the government of adults come to their forum. Among them there is a larger group of schoolchildren, students of vocational schools, and those to whom the magazine «Kvant» is addressed.

The words of the General Secretary of the Central Committee of the CPSU, Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR Leonid Ilyich Brezhnev are addressed to you, inquisitive, thoughtful and proactive ones: “May the pure fire of patriotism continue to burn in your hearts, may a noble sense of personal responsibility live for the assigned work, for everything that happens in the country.”

The Communist Party and the Soviet state provided the younger generation with exceptional opportunities for mastering natural scientific and social political knowledge. The decisions of the 26th Congress of the CPSU determined ways to further solve the problem of developing the creative abilities of students in secondary schools, instilling in them the ability to independently expand their knowledge and navigate the rapid flow of scientific information. V. I. Lenin’s behest - to study - was and remains the most important, the most relevant in the activities of the Komsomol.

The entire Leninist Communist Youth Union considers its most important task to assist schools in every possible way in improving the quality of the educational process, to more closely connect the educational process with the preparation of young people for work, especially in the field of material production. Life has convincingly shown in relation to education that it is associated with the teaching of schoolchildren in productive work. This contributes to the development of high leadership qualities and an active life position in students.

Today, at the stage of mature socialism, when it is especially important to combine the achievements of the scientific and technological revolution with the application of socialism, the task of training a new type of specialists comes to the fore - not only highly educated, but also creative thinkers, capable of solving the most important problems of modern theory and practice. Studying communism today means the ability to look far ahead, to be ready for independent creative work, for active learning in the socio-political life of society.

To prepare oneself to participate in communist construction means to learn collectivism, discipline, to be able to realistically see life’s problems, to independently understand various phenomena of the reality around us, to be prepared to, as V.I. Lenin said, be able to “finish and entrust what we have started”).

The youth of our Motherland demonstrates their loyalty to Leninist traditions and the great cause of the Communist Party; they gathered for their 19th Congress. The decisions of the congress, the wise message of the party to its helper and reserve - the Lenin Komsomol, and all Soviet youth should become for the readers of «Kvant», as well as for all youth, a guiding star in their lives, studies, and work.

B. N. Pastukhov,
Secretary of the Komsomol Central Committee

*) V.I. Lenin. Full collection cit., vol. 41, p. 301.
