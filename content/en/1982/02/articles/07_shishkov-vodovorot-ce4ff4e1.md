The circle associated with vortex motion is extremely diverse, and there is no way to cover it completely within the framework of one article. In the listed articles, special attention was paid to the input - cyclomas, smerts, vortex rings. Currently, we are talking about almost one of the types of vortex motion in water - the whirlpool.

About the Cyclopes and... the carousel

On children's playgrounds you can often see a carousel - a freely rotating horizontal platform with a diameter of four meters and a spadial railing. Playing on this carousel requires some skill. The participants, using their own strength, cause the platform to rapidly rotate and jump onto it along the edges. Then, on command, everyone rushes to the center of the platform, moving their hands along the railing. At the same time, the carousel begins to rotate faster and faster, and the replaced participant can practically no longer follow the others - great services are required from him to stay in his place.

The increase in angular velocity can be explained in different ways. A reader familiar with the law of conservation of angular momentum will immediately understand what is going on. Anyone who has the ability to describe movement in a non-physical reporting system can explain what is happening in the language of inertial forces. But a qualitative explanation is available to every reader. At the edge of the platform, the linear (tangential) speed of its points and the location of the player there is greater. The closer to the center, the lower this speed, so when moving from the edge to the center, the participant must reduce his speed in order not to fall. This is accessible through interaction with the platform. It is clear what kind of interaction this is – training. But the occurrence of an error caused by friction acting on the platform imparts additional acceleration to it, increasing the linear velocities of all its points - and the angular velocity of rotation increases.

It turns out that there is some analogy between a child's game on a carousel and such natural phenomena as the occurrence of atmospheric cycles and the formation of a funnel in a drain. Therefore, by observing the movements of water in a bank, we will talk about a carousel and draw conclusions about the behavior of cycles and whirlpools at sea.

Watching the whirlpool

Whirlwinds and whirlpools have always attracted attention. In the 30s, the remarkable popularizer of physics Ya. I. Perelman worked with young people with the idea of ​​​​investigating the occurrence of whirlpools when water flows out of a bath and, in particular, the direction of rotation of these whirlpools. Young natural calls, a huge number of observations have been carried out.

It should be noted, however, that the bath as an “experimental equipment” has a number of disadvantages.

For example, she is “little” symmetrine, directing her each time takes a lot of time. The funnel does not form immediately after the water begins to flow out, and it is almost completely impossible to control the experimental conditions. In this regard, a cylindrical “compact” installation made from scrap material is more convenient, for example, such as in figure 1. Vessel - turn over a glass jar with a cut-off house; The bottom of the vessel (former neck) is tightly closed (for example, with a polystylene lid). The answer has a drain hose inserted in the center of the bottom; You can open and close the drain using the lockxima. The inlet hose should be secured to the inner wall so that water flows out of it horizontally and not along the radius of the vessel; then, when directing the vessel, rotation of the water will occur from the very beginning.

Fill the unit with water through the inlet hose and open the drain. After some time, a deflection of the free surface of the water will become noticeable, and finally a funnel will appear.

This phenomenon differs from the children's game on the Lisht carousel in that the role of the participants in the game is played by liquid particles; only them to the center of the pressure difference between the periphery (atmospheric + hydrostatic pressure) and the response in the bottom (atmospheric pressure).

In a uniformly rotating bucket, the free surface of the water also bends, but at the same time all particles of the liquid (and on the surface) rotate with the same angular velocity. And in the funnel that we just observed, the angular velocity returns as it approaches the center of rotation.

It is convenient to examine an established whirlpool; To obtain such a whirlpool, with the drain open, you must constantly compensate for the water flow by continuously adding water from the inlet hose.

The whirlpool can be observed not only when water flows out, but also when the vessel is filled (with the drain closed). If a thin drain stream is directed at the center of the rotating liquid (remember that the filling hose is attached so that when the container is filled, water rotates), a whirlpool will appear - the so-called “secondary” funnel. Think about how the conditions in such a funnel differ from the conditions in the whirlpool that forms during drainage.

About fluid consumption

Adjust the installation so that a steady whirlpool is formed. Place a piece of thick paper vertically into such a whirlpool so that it blocks the flow of rotating water, but does not prevent water from flowing out through the drain. And you will see that the water level quickly increases by 1-2 cm. This is due to the fact that in the presence of a vortex, the flow rate

The liquid decreases. This is easy to verify. Lower the inlet hose so that the water flows vertically into the container. Then, with the same pressure, the water level will drop.

It is clear what analogue of this phenomenon can be demonstrated on a children's cart: with the same effort expended, the participants in the game get to the center of the rotating platform much more slowly than on a stationary platform.

There is also an inverse relationship between the whirlpool and the liquid flow rate - the shape of the free surface of the liquid in the whirlpool depends on the flow rate.

Fill the container with water (with the drain closed) and disconnect the inlet hose. Now open the drain. When a funnel begins to form, lift the free end of the hose from which the water is flowing. In this case, it is easy to notice that the deflection of the free surface of the liquid increases with increasing height of the end of the hose.

By raising the end of the hose, we reduce the pressure drop at the outlet (Fig. 2); in connection with this, the speed of water flow and its consumption decreases. Why does the funnel smooth out? To understand this, let's turn to carzuela. What will happen if the participants in the game move very slowly towards the center of the rotating carzuel? It is clear that due to friction the platform will stop before anyone gets to the center. The smoothing of the funnel occurs due to the presence of three between the liquid liquids, the bottom and walls of the vessel. The slower the water decreases from the vessel, the more time the three forces have to smooth out the funnel.

Be careful - whirlpool!

They consist of the following: take a lot of air into your lungs and try to stay on the surface. The value of this advice should not be determined by swimming into a real whirlpool. Some

"Whiskers" of the whirlpool
Another interesting phenomenon observed in the whirlpool is “whiskers”. Unfortunately, the author was unable to get a good photograph with the mustache. But you see them missing if you take care of the whirlpool in the bathtub drain. They will surely attract your attention with their necessary behavior. Initially, the “whisker” is perceived as an established ruler. As the water flows out, they begin to rotate, as if winding up on the invisible axis of a whirlpool. And at the last moment you will discover that the rotation of the water is not at all in the direction of “winding the mustache.”
How does this “mustache” appear? What is the reason for their deceptive behavior?
Perhaps the “whiskers” are capillary-gravitational waves on the surface of the waterway, similar to an “accordion” on a structure with an obstacle (this “accordion” was discussed in the article by S. Soskin “Capillary waves in a jet” in the 10th issue «Kvant» for 1976). If external processes are in the water, then it appears on the surface of 1-3 characteristics of the “whisker”.
Or maybe, after all, there is some analogy between the “whiskers” of the whirlpool and ... the arms of spiral galaxies? After all, nature gives birth to the unity of phenomena that are incommensurable in scale.
To date, the question of the “mustache” remains open. Perhaps you, the reader, can express your opinion on this issue?
