Tasks

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the problem, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than April 30, 1982 to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, “Fizmatlit”, «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 2 - 82” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M726, M727” or “F738”. Solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope).

The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

M726—M730; F738—F742

M726. A point inside a regular 2n-gon is connected to the vertices. The resulting 2n triangles are colored alternately in head and red. Prove that the sum of the areas of the head triangles is equal to the sum of the areas of the red ones a) for n=4, 6) for n=3, c) for an arbitrary natural number n.

V. Prasolov

M727. Prove the inequality
a²+b²+c²+2abc<2,
where a, b, c are the side lengths of the triangle perimeter 2.

I. Zharov

M728. Let A, B, C be the vertices of the parallelepid, connected to its vertex P, and Q be the vertex opposite to P. Prove that a) the distance from the point A, B, C to the straight line PQ can serve as the length of the side of a certain triangle; 6) the area S of this triangle, the volume V of the parallelepid and the length d of its diagonal PQ are related by the relation V = 2dS.

I. Sharytsin

M729. Find a natural number that has the following property: if you write its square and its cube side by side, and then rearrange the written digits in reverse order, you get six of this number.

N. Vasiliev

M730*. The sequence (aₙ) is determined by the conditions
a₁=0, a₂n+1=a₂n=n-aₙ.
(For example, a₁₀=5-a₅=5-a₄=5-(2-a₂)=3+(1-a₁)=4.)
a) Write down the first 20 terms of the sequence and find a₁₉₂.
b) Prove that each natural number appears in the sequence 2 or 4 times. How many times will the number 2ᵉ appear in it (for each k=1, 2, 3,...)?

F738. The particles move in single file at constant speeds. Particle velocities increase uniformly from value $v_1$ at the head of the column to value $v_2$ at the tail of the column. At some point, particles occupy a segment of length $l_0$ and at the same time $n_0$ particles come per unit length. How many particles after time $t$ will arrive per unit length at the tail of the column? at the head of the column?

I. Vorobiev

F739. A mass of water $M$ is poured every second into a cylindrical vessel of height $H$ with base area $S$ from a horizontal pipe. There is a small hole at the base of the vessel. A thin light sponge (Fig. 1) lies in the vessel on the surface of the water. From a certain moment, the height of the water level in the vessel becomes constant and equal to $h_0$. Determine the speed at which water flows out of the hole.

I. Omelyan

F740. The vessel contains a mixture of gases - helium and oxygen. At a temperature of $t = -2^\circ$ C and a pressure of $p = 0,9$ atm, the density of this mixture is $\rho = 0,44$ kg/m$^3$. What will the pressure in the vessel become if half of the oxygen molecules are removed from it?

F741. In an alternating current with a voltage of 220 V and a frequency of 50 Hz, two capacitors with a capacity of 1 μF each are connected in series. A resistor with a resistance of $R = 100$ kOhm (Fig. 2) is connected in parallel to one of the capacitors. Find the thermal power.

A. Zilberman

F742. A glass vessel of rectangular cross-section is installed between two collecting lenses with focal lengths $F$ perpendicular to the optical axis of the lenses (Fig. 3). A point light source $S$ is located in the focal plane of the lens $\varLambda_1$. When the vessel is empty, the source image is observed on the screen $\varDelta$, located in the focal plane of the lens $\varLambda_2$. The vessel is filled with a transparent liquid, the refractive index of which changes with height according to the law $n(y) = n_0 + \alpha y$. The thickness of the liquid layer is $L$. How much will the source image on the screen shift vertically? The change in the refractive index with the depth of the vessel is considered small within the diameter of the light beam.

V. Mozhaev
