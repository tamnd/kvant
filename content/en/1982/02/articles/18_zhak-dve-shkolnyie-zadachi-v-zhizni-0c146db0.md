Physics

Oral exam assignment

1. A stone is thrown horizontally from a base $h=25$ m high with a speed of $v_0=15$ m/s. Find: a) how long will the stone be in motion? b) at what distance from the base of the tower will it land on the ground? c) at what speed will he settle?

2. A thread is wound on a light pulley with a radius of $R=10$ cm, to the end of which a weight is attached. The load begins to descend at a level of $a=2$ cm/$c^2$. What will be the angular velocity of the pulley at the moment when the load drops $h=100$ cm?

3. In the cylinder of a diesel engine at the beginning of the ramp stroke the air temperature was $T_1=310$ K. Find the air temperature at the end of the ramp stroke if its volume decreased by $a=12$ times and the air pressure by $b=36$ times.

4. Find the gas consumption of the Zaporozhets submachine gunner on the route $s=1$ km at a speed of $v=60$ km/h. Meter power $N=17$ kW, meter efficiency $\eta=30\%$. Heat of fuel storage $q=45 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg.

5. According to Bohr's ideas, an electron in a hydrogen atom moves around the nucleus in a circular orbit. Calculate the speed of the electron if the orbital radius is $r=0,5 \cdot 10^{-8}$ cm. The mass of the electron is $m=9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, its charge is $e=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ Cl.

6. A voltmeter connected to a source with emf $\delta=120$ V and internal resistance $r=50$ ohm reads $U=118$ V. What is the internal resistance of the voltmeter?

7. Two conductors with the same resistance $R$, connected first in parallel and then in series, are connected to a network with voltage $U$. In what case is the largest amount of power consumed from the network?

8. How many turns of the bottom wire must be threaded onto a porcelain cylinder with a diameter of $d_1=1,5$ cm to make a boiler in which $V=1,2$ liters of water taken at a temperature of $t=10^\circ C$ boils in $t=10$ minutes? Installation efficiency $\eta=60\%$; wire diameter $d_2=0,2$ mm; mains voltage $U=100$ V. Specific heat of water $c=4,2$ kJ/(kg K); resistivity below $Q=0,42 \cdot 10^{-6}$ Ohm m.

9. How much electrical energy must be expended in order to electrify a distance of $m=500$ mg of silver? Voltage on the electrodes $U=4$V. The electrical equivalent of silver is $k=1,08 \cdot 10^{-6}$ kg/Kl.

10. A ray of light falls on a plane-par plate with refractive index $n=1,5$ at an angle $\alpha=60^\circ$. What is the thickness of the plate if the beam moves by $a=2$ cm when leaving it?

About cutting timber
greatest strength

Until relatively recently, wooden beams of rectangular cross-section were widely used as truss structures (columns in wooden houses, supports in interfloor ceilings, posts in wooden places). They are still widely used in our time, especially in rural rafters.

It has been established that the larger the cross-sectional area of ​​the beam loaded centrally (that is, at the center of the gravity of the section), the more it is.

Consequently, when cutting a straight beam from round log, no more than a number of objects are used, which have expansion vessels at the highest points (in the attic of one of the houses). - containers in which excess water flows when heated.

These containers are often in the form of cylinders with a removable lid (for periodic cleaning of the section) and are made of sheet steel - a relatively expensive material. Therefore, the following practical task becomes important: given the capacity of the expansion vessel, also find its dimensions so that the least amount of sheet steel is consumed for its manufacture (the consumption of materials for seams is not taken into account, and the thickness of the walls, bottom and lid is considered the same). This problem is similar to the mathematical problem from the cyclic textbook: from all cyclidra of a given volume, find the cyclidr with the smallest total surface (Algebra 9-10, problem 420).

It is easy to show (do the calculation) that the axial section of the required cylinder is a square.

If you know that on the scale of our country, expansion vessels are manufactured with high-quality production, with the consumption of sheet metal, and therefore with significant costs.
