Mathematics manual for university applicants

This is the name of the book, which was published in large quantities by the Nauka publishing house this year.
When preparing for entrance exams, the applicant will face a difficult task: he needs to repeat 103 points of the program for university applicants and solve an even larger number of problems. One could again look through all the school textbooks from the 5th to the 10th grades and remember the solutions to the problems contained in them. However, in our opinion, this path is far from the best based on many principles (including purely psychological ones). A more effective and natural way is to use this second way when preparing for the coefficient.
The book consists of 17 chapters. Each chapter contains theoretical material and tasks. The presentation of the theory is accompanied by an analysis of the control and problems of various solutions to problems I independently. If this fails, you can study the reduction in the solution book, and then use the analytical problems of Section II to monitor your ability to solve problems of this type. The book contains more than 2000 exercises and problems. Of course, this does not mean that in order to successfully prepare for exams, all of them must be calculated.

In addition to standard problems of various difficulties, the book also breaks down those in which the solutions must demonstrate elements of logical skills. Let's give two examples.

Problem 1. Given two points A (0; 9), B (3; 6) and a system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} 2x - y + a \leq 0, \\ 6x + 3y + 5a \geq 0. \end{cases}$$

At what values ​​of the parameter a will the solution of the system be coordinates
a) at least one point of the segment AB?
b) how to the point of the segment AB?
Task 2. Pupils of the tenth “B” class were distinguished by the fact that they were taller than the students of the tenth “A”. To the math teacher’s question, “What does it actually mean that you are taller?” Tenth “B” students must do the following:
1. Any of us is higher than any of them.
2. The tallest of us is taller than the tallest of them.
3. For every student in our class, there is a class A student who is shorter in height.
4. Every student in class "A" is lower than at least one student in our class.
5. The average height of students in our class is greater than the average height of students in class “A”.
Are there any equivalent answers among these? If so, which ones?

It is known that learning is more successful the more interest the student shows in the subject being studied. The next two tasks show that the authors sought to include material in the book that was relevant to the student.

But, of course, there are few such problems in the book: Most of the problems are general problems, many of which were offered in university application exams. At the end of the book, the authors have placed some options for entrance exams that were offered at universities in Moscow, Leningrad, Novosibirsk, Kyiv and other cities.
In addition to the main ones, the book also covers topics that are not included in the school curriculum, such as “Combinatorics” and “Complex numbers”. Their study will contribute to further successful studies at universities.
The book reflects the experience of the Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTS), which is well known to many students and teachers in our country. The tasks of this school have already helped several generations of schoolchildren enter the leading challenge. 3FTSH materials have been used for a long time in teaching students in grades 8-10.
Not only school graduates, but also teachers, teachers of technical and vocational colleges and professunillos, heads of mathematical circles, students of pedagogical requests for dates in the book/materials that are convenient to have at hand.
