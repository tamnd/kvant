⟦folio 8⟧

On this cover page you see four machine-made ornaments created by 4th grade students of boarding school No. 32 in the city. Moscow by Tanya Vlasova, Tanya Debryanskaya and Lyuda Barabanova using the Algraf computer graphics system.
Figure were encoded in a simplified language that allowed geometric elements, coordinate transformations, etc. to be specified in various ways.
The authors of figure mastered coordinate systems, negative numbers, and the operations of parallel translation and rotation a little ahead of schedule. On excursions to the computer center, they were required to prepare punch cards with tasks while observing the work of the builder. Then one of the guys came up with the idea of ​​making the builder draw on glass. This is done in two stages: first, the contour line is automatically applied with black ink, then parts of the bruch image are painted over with oil red in different colors. Figure looks “from the other side” and turns out shiny and beautiful.

Yu. Kotov
