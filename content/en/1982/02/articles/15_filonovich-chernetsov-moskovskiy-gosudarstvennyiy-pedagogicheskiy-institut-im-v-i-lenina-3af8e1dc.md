Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1

1. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the graphs of the functions

$y = \frac{1}{9}x^2$ and $y = -\frac{1}{3}x + 2$.

2. Solve the equation

$tg^2\left(\frac{7}{2}\pi-x\right) - 8\sin^2(x-9\pi) = 1$.

3. $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$—parallelepiped. $A(2; -1; 1), B(1; 3; 4), D(6; 0; 1), A_1(2; 0; 0)$. Find the length of diagonal $AC_1$.

4. The base of the parameters $SABC$ is the equilateral triangle $ABC$. The side face $SAB$ is perpendicular to the base, and the other two are inclined to the base at an angle $\alpha$. Find the angle between the base plane and the side edge $SC$.

5. One tractor team must plow 240 hectares, and the other is 35% larger than the first. Plowing 3 g less every day than the second team, the first still finished the work 2 days earlier than the second team. How many hectors did each brigade plow every day?

Option 2

1. Explore the feature

$y = \frac{1}{3}x^3 - x$

and plot it.

2. Solve the inequality

$2x^2 - 20x \leq \frac{1}{2}$.

3. Solve the equation

$\sin 4x = \cos^4 x - \sin^4 x$.

4. Prove that the triangle with vertices $A(6; -4; 2), B(3; 2; 3), C(3; -5; -1)$ is right-angled.

5. The height of the cone makes an angle $\alpha$ with the shape. A plane is drawn through the top of the cone at an angle $\beta$ to the plane of the base. Find the cross-sectional area if the height of the cone is $h$.
