⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. In the triangles and squares shown on figure, write out the first two natural numbers, with odd numbers in the triangles and even numbers in the squares so that all twelve “more-less” correspondences are correct.

2. Is it possible to make a cube frame from twelve identical bars with a square cross-section?

3. When shooting at a sports target, I hit the top ten several times, scored 8 points the same number of times, and hit the top five several times. In total he scored 99 points. How many shots did the sport take?

4. There are several non-zero numbers written on the board, each of which is equal to the others. How many numbers are written?

5. Of the seven regular hexagons, one is colored with six colors (see figure). Color the remaining hexagons with the same colors so that in the “flower” shown in figure, all the triangular areas bordering the sides of the hexagons are colored the same.

6. By moving a horizontal ruler placed edgewise on the cylinder, we will roll (without slipping) the cylinder on the table (see figure). At the initial moment, the zero division of the ruler was above the point of contact between the cylinder and the table. Where will the zero division of the ruler be after the cylinder rolls 5 cm?

These tasks were offered to us
N. Avilov, S. Dvoryannikov, L. Kuptsov,
A. Savin, A. Khalamizer, A. Shvetsov.
