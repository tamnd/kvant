⟦folio 57⟧

On March 26, 11 teams met in four subgroups (in four Moscow schools) in the physical quarterfinals, which were held according to the given correspondence round using the “speaker-opponent-decision” system*). Based on the results of the physical quarterfinals, teams from schools No. 7, 18, 57, 91, 842 advanced to the semifinals of the Tournament. The right to participate in the semifinals also received teams from school No. 2 - as the winner of the Tournament's challenge prize - and a team from schools No. 179 - as the winner of the collective correspondence competition.

On April 9, physicists took part in the semifinals in three subgroups. They were carried out according to an additional list of tasks that the regatta received 10 days before the semi-finals. Below we present the conditions of the most interesting problems from this list.

Bus task. It is known that on a bus there are more “trassets” in the back seats than in the front ones. Why?

Task "Visual labor". If you look visually through a window glass, the image will be blurry, even if the glass is cleanly washed. Why?

Teams from schools No. 57, 91 and 179 won in the semifinals and advanced to the semifinals of the Tournament.

The second round was held in a sharp and exciting manner. The guys argued a lot, expressed their opinions, demonstrated a good knowledge of physics and acquired a wealth of experience in collective creativity. The physical training was organized very well by the children of the schools where the composite subgroup took place; Of particular note is the school team No. 47, in which amateur artistic performances are even prepared for fiziblada.

We believe that physical competition between individual schools or classes is possible and useful outside the framework of the general Tournament.

The third round of the Tournament - the final - was held on April 26 at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

The opening speech to the guys was made by the Chairman of the Tournament Organizing Committee, Professor of the Faculty of Physics Yu. M. Loskutov. He told the children about the Physics Faculty of Moscow State University and the main directions of scientific research carried out by the faculty's scientists. Then the professor of the Faculty of Physics, laureate of the Lomonosov Prize in Physics V.L. Bonch-Bruevich presented to the jury of the Tournament, and the team of schools No. 57, 91 and 179 began to complete the given final physioyod.

All participants were shown four experiments from different areas of physics. Teams must be given explanations of the physical phenomena observed in the experiments. An hour was allotted for this. In addition, all teams were presented with four experiments, which were given 30 minutes to solve. We present the conditions of these problems.

Problem "Spectrum of the Sun". Why is the spectrum of the Sun continuous?

The "Lightning" task. For solar matter, the equation of state $p = \frac{\theta}{\mu} RT$ is valid. What is the significance for solar matter as well? Composition of the Sun: hydrogen - 73%, green - 25%, heavy elements - 2%.

Problem "Interstellar medium". How to characterize the state of matter in interstellar space - vacuum or gas? Can sound waves travel through the interstellar medium?

Problem "Photocell". How is the classic formula for current density $j = env$ created with the photocell saturation mode?

The final physical was carried out according to the speaker-opponent-decider system and each team acted as a speaker twice. During the physical exercise, a captain competition was held - they had to answer 8 questions in a short time. The jury awarded the victory to the captain of the team of school No. 57 D. Grigoriev. The Bigs also received a task - they were asked to come up with captions for the paintings presented to the finalist teams, and very nerdy signatures, at the request of the Bigs, were awarded to one of the teams. Sympathetic Bolshoi residents are inclined to use school No. 179.

After summing up the results of the Physioid, the Tournament was closed. The chairman of the organizing committee, academician E.P. Velikhov, presented the challenge prize of the Tournament of Young Physicists and a certificate of honor for the victory in the III Moscow Tournament of Young Physicists to the team of school No. 179 (captain A. Panfilov). Certificates of honor were awarded to the team of school No. 91 (captain A. Polyakov) - for second place, the team of school No. 57 (captain D. Grigoriev) - for third place, the team of school No. 842 (captain V. Eliseev) - as the best debutant of the Tournament, D. Grigoriev - the winner of the captains' competition, D. Svirida - for the best experimental work. The jury awarded another 17 schoolchildren with certificates for their active participation in the Tournament and for the best solutions to individual problems.

E. Yunosov

*) The system “speaker - opponent - decider” is described in the information about the II Tournament of Young Physicists in the 8th issue of “Kwan” for 1980.
