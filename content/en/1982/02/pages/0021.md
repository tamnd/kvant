One of the problems he solved in this area concerned the calculation of the vibration frequencies of small metal or glass plates rigidly fixed at one point. Experiments with such plates were continued by the German Ernst Chladni, and the first information about them dates back to 1787. In 1809, Chladni demonstrated these experiments to members of the French National Institute (Academy of Sciences).
