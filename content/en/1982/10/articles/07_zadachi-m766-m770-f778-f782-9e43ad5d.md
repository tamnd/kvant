Tasks

M766 — M770; F778 — F782

M766. Prove that the sum of the squares of three consecutive integers cannot be the cube of a natural number.

Yu. Ionin

M767. a) Line $l$ divides the area of ​​a convex polygon in half. Prove that the ratio in which this line divides the projection of the polygon into the line perpendicular to it does not exceed $1 + \sqrt{2}$ (Fig. 1, a). b) Each of the three straight lines divides the area of ​​this figure in half. Prove that the area of ​​the part of the figure enclosed in a triangle between the lines does not exceed $1/4$ all plane figures (Fig. 1, b).

V. Prasolov

M768*. The sum of $n$ numbers, each of which does not exceed modulo 1, is equal to $s$. Prove that you can choose several numbers from them so that the sum of the selected numbers differs from $s/3$ by no more than $1/3$.

V. Grinberg

M769*. The bisectors of the triangle $ABC$ intersect at the point $L$, their continuations intersect the circumcircle of the triangle at the points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ (Fig. 2). Let $R$ be the circumradius and $r$ the circumradius of triangle $ABC$. Prove the equalities

a) $\frac{|LA_1| \cdot |LC_1|}{|LB|} = R$; b) $\frac{|LA| \cdot |LC|}{|LB_1|} = r$;

b) $\frac{S_{ABC}}{S_{A_1B_1C_1}} = \frac{2r}{R}$.

R. Mazov

M770*. At the base of the triangular paramid $PABC$ lies a regular triangle $ABC$. Prove that if the angles $PAB, PBC, PCA$ are congruent, then the paramida $PABC$ is regular.

⟦folio 27⟧

F778. The ring of mass $m$, loosely fastening two thin hoops of mass $M$, begins to slide down; the hoops move in different directions along a rough horizontal surface. Determine the acceleration of the ring at the initial moment of time if the angle $AO_1O_2$ (Fig. 3) is equal to $\alpha$. There is no friction between the ring and the hoops.

S. Krotov

F779. A square consisting of two halves whose mass ratio is equal to two is located on a rough surface. At what angle $\alpha$ to the interface between the halves must the rope be pulled in the horizontal plane so that the square moves forward? The point of application of the force lies at the intersection of the side of the square and the interface.

E. Aliyev

F780. A cylindrical vessel with a height of $2h$, equally divided by a partition, contains water in the upper part (its density is $\rho$) and air at an atmospheric pressure of $p_0$ (Fig. 4) in the lower part. A small hole opens in the partition so that water begins to flow into the lower part of the vessel. How thick will the layer of water be at the bottom of the vessel when air begins to flow upward from the hole? The temperature is constant. The acceleration of the rigid force is $g$.

V. Borodin

F781. Between two independent metal concentric spheres of radii $a$ and $b$ there is an exact charge $q$ at a distance $c$ from the center of the sphere. What charge will flow through a thin conductor if the spheres are closed by it?

V. Komov

F782. The capacitor was charged to a voltage of $U_0=100$ V and a resistor was connected to it. At the same time, over a certain interval of time, energy $W_1=1$ J was released in the form of heat, and during the same interval, energy $W_2=0,3$ J was released. Determine the capacitance of the capacitor.

A. Zilberman
