All-Russian Olympiad for SCHOOLCHILDREN

According to tradition, the final stage of the VIII All-Russian Schoolchildren Olympiad in Mathematics and Physics took place during the spring school holidays.

The All-Russian Olympiad is held in four stages: the first stage is school Olympiads, the second is district (city) Olympiads, the third is resource (ASSR), regional and regional Olympiads, the fourth is zonal Olympiads.

This year, the final, fourth stage took place simultaneously in four cities of the RSFSR: Kostroma, Stavropol, Ulyanovsk and Chita. Teams of autonomous resources, territories and regions, composed of the winners of the third stage, took part in it. In addition, the winners of the previous All-Russian Olympiad, who received 1st and 2nd degree diplomas, and the winners of the Quiet magazine competition took part in the zonal Olympiads. Teams from physics and mathematics boarding schools at Leningrad, Moscow and Novosibirsk universities also participated in this stage.

The competition task for the fourth stage in mathematics consisted of five problems, the solution of which was given four hours. The greatest difficulties for the Olympiad participants were caused by problems in geometry, combinatorics and mathematical analysis.

The fourth stage assignment in physics included four theoretical problems and two experimental ones. Four hours were allotted for solving theoretical problems, and three hours for experimental ones. The fourth problem for tenth graders turned out to be the most difficult of the theoretical problems. All participants successfully completed the experimental tasks.

By decision of Jorri, the winners of the All-Russian Olympiad were awarded diplomas, memorable recognitions and valuable gifts. The Ministry of Education of the RSFSR awarded certificates to teachers who trained the winners of the Olympiad.

Below are the problems in mathematics and physics of the final stage and the names of the winners of the VIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren.

Mathematics

8th grade

1. In triangle ABC the bisketris [AK] is drawn. It is known that the centers of the circles - inscribed in triangle ABK and circumscribed about triangle ABC - coincide. Find the angles of triangle ABC.

2. The workshop has five different machines. Training one worker to operate one machine costs 1,000 rubles. At every minimum cost, can 8 workers be trained so that, in the absence of any three of them, all the machines can be used at the same time? Each worker can only work on one machine at a time.

3. In some places there are six cities. The Ministry of Transport received an order from the king to establish one of three types of communication between each two cities: rail, highway or air. At the same time, the king demanded that the three cities below should not be connected by the same type of communication and that for any division of six cities into three pairs, not all types of communication in the pairs should be different. Will the Ministry of Transport be able to carry out the king's order?

4. What is the maximum number of points that can be selected on the segment [0; 1] so that on any segment [a; b] contained in the segment [0; 1], there were no more than 1 + 100(b-a)² selected points?

5. The incircle of triangle ABC touches its sides [AB], [BC] and [AC] at points M, N and P respectively. It is known that $\vec{AN} + \vec{BP} + \vec{CM} = \vec{0}$. Prove that triangle ABC is regular.

$$\begin{cases} x^3 - y^3 - z^3 = 3xyz \\ x^2 = 2(y + z) \end{cases}$$

3. In an acute triangle ABC, the altitudes intersect at point H. Let us denote it by $|AB| = c, |BC| = a, |CA| = b, |AH| = x, |BH| = y, |CH| = z$. Prove the equality $abc = ayz + xbz + xyc$.

4. Function $f(x)$ defined on the interval [0; 1], such that $f(0) = f(1) = 0$ and for any $a, b \in [0; 1]$ the inequality holds
$$f\left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right) \leq f(a) + f(b).$$

Prove that the equation $f(x) = 0$ has infinitely many solutions. Find out whether there is a function that satisfies the specified conditions and is not exactly equal to zero.

The first is filled with ice obtained by freezing water in the same cube, the second is filled with water at a temperature tₘ=10°C. Water is poured onto the ice and the level is immediately stirred. After thermal equilibrium was established, it turned out that it increased by Δh = 0.5 cm. What was the initial temperature of the ice? Ice density ρₙ=900 kg/m³. Specific heat of melting of ice λ=340 kJ/kg, specific heat of ice cₙ=2100 J/(kg K).
