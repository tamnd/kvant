⟦folio 43⟧

New books

In this issue we publish short annotations on books on mathematics and physics, accessible and interesting to our readers, published in 1983 by the Nauka publishing house. Most bookstores distributing literature on this topic, as well as Book by Mail departments, accepted unlimited pre-orders (postcards) for these books until the end of 1982. We ask all interested readers to place such orders. This will help the publishing house more accurately plan the circulation of published books. In parentheses we indicate the quarter in which the book is expected to be published.

Mathematics
1. A. Dalma. Evariste Galois - revolutionary and mathematician (translation from French). 2nd edition (I quarter). Price 35 k.
This book is about the French mathematician Évariste Galois, who died in a doozle at the age of 20.
Galois published his first work while he was still a student; he put his last work in order on the eve of the fatal doozle. These works led to a genuine revolution in mathematics - a rare example of a scientific fate that befell a person who was not 21 years old.
The author devotes much space in the book to a description of Galois’s revolutionary struggle and his directed scientific work. “This book,” writes the author, “is a tribute to Galois for all that he accomplished in mathematics and politics.”
2. L. S. Pontryagin. Mathematical analysis for schoolchildren. 2nd edition (I quarter). Price 10 k.
This brochure is intended for a first-time introduction to mathematical analysis and contains materials covering all sections of mathematical analysis studied in high school.
The presentation of the material is not formal: the author does not strive for complete rigor of presentation, but relies on clarity and practical meaning, which is very important when initially getting to know the subject. Thus, the concept of arbitrary is introduced based on the intuitive idea of ​​objects, the concept of integral is defined in the book as the area of ​​a curved linear trapezoid, etc.

"Popular lectures on mathematics"
1. P. P. Korovkin. Inequalities. 5th edition (II quarter). Price 15 k.
The brochure contains a large number of different inequalities, including the significant inequality about the arithmetic mean and geometric mean, Bernoulli and Young's inequalities and much more.
Much attention is paid to solving maximum and minimum problems using the inequalities studied in the book.
2. N. I. Vorobev. Fibonacci numbers. 5th edition (I quarter). Price 20 kopecks.
The brochure is dedicated to a detailed study of the properties of the sequence of natural numbers 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13,... (each number, starting from the third, is equal to the sum of the previous two). These numbers often arise in a variety of problems in analysis, algebra, and combinatorics. The brochure examines in detail the divisibility properties of Fibonacci numbers.
The last paragraph talks about how Fibonacci numbers “work” in the theory of finding optimal solutions.
