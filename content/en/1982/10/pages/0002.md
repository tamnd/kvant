The Soviet Union is consistently implementing an extensive program for the worldwide use of space research. Our cosmonauts are actively involved in solving important problems of the national economy. They monitor the condition of crops, warn about forest fires, help geologically search for minerals, and help create accurate maps of hard-to-reach areas.

The drawings presented here reproduce the results of surveying the same mountainous region of the earth's surface, simultaneously filming the city with a block of four synchronously operating cameras. The first of them received general cameras (Fig. 1). The remaining image registrations are in different areas of the length of visible and infrared study (500-600 nm, 600-700 nm and 700-840 nm). The resulting black and white images served as the basis for the synthesis of color images shown in Figures 2 and 3. Each of them makes it possible to clearly highlight some details of the plant solution being studied, and the third - the soil in mountain valleys. The fourth figure is also not 摄影師 - it is obtained on the basis of the previous 摄影師 using special processing equipment. Such synthesized images make it possible to more clearly determine the boundaries of various formations.

The initial ones were received on June 27, 1975 by cosmonauts P. Klimk and V. Sevastyanov at the long-term orbital station “Salyut-4” from an altitude of 340 km. These materials are intended for a comprehensive study of the natural conditions and resources of our country and the creation of accurate maps of hard-to-reach areas.
