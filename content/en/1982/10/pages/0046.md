⟦folio 44⟧

Exercise

1. In kindergarten, the teacher places in front of the child a piece of paper with several circles drawn on it.
   - How many circles are there? she asks.
   “Seven,” the child answers.
   - Right! - says the teacher. Then she places this sheet in front of another child and asks the same question.
   “Five,” the child answers.
   - Right! - the teacher says again.
   How many circles are drawn on paper?
2. Drinking tea, a sunbeam is reflected on the wall. What shape will the teng hang on the bunny if we bring a teaspoon from above to the center of the surface of the tea?
3. Two five-digit numbers are composed so that each digit appears once in any of these numbers. For example, 46781 and 50239. For each of these numbers, their product will be the smallest, and for each, the largest?
4. The watermelon was divided into 7 parts by three cuts of a knife, and when I ate it, there were 8 rinds on the table. The second watermelon was divided into 10 parts with four cuts of a knife. When I ate it, there were 12 crusts left on the table. The third watermelon was divided into 15 parts with four cuts of a knife. How were the watermelons cut and how many rinds were left from the last one?

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Kalinin, G. Kotkin, V. Proizvolov, A. Savin.
