Empty glass

It turns out that various musical sounds can be extracted from a thin-walled glass goblet, and in a unique way. However, judge for yourself.

You can use hot water on your hands to remove grease from your fingers. Dip your finger in the water and carefully move it along the edge of the glass, constantly wetting your finger with water. At first, the glass will make an unpleasant creaking sound, but then, when the edges are well wiped, the sounds will become more melodic. By changing the pressure of your finger, you can change the tone of the sound extracted from the glass. In addition, the pitch also depends on the size of the glass, the thickness of its walls and the amount of contents in it.

Please note that not every glass is capable of producing pleasant drinking sounds, so searching for a suitable glass can be long and troublesome. It is best to drink (and not creak) very thin glasses, shaped like a rotating parabolide, with a long and thin stem. The tone of the sound can be changed by adding water to the glass - the more water there is in the glass, the lower it will sound.

(And one more note to the observation. When the water level rises to the middle of the glass, waves will appear on its surface due to the cooperation of the walls of the glass. The strongest disturbance will be in the place where your finger is currently located.)
