Tasks

M766 — M770; F778 — F782

M766. Prove that the sum of the squares of three consecutive integers cannot be the cube of a natural number.

Yu. Ionin

M767. a) Line $l$ divides the area of ​​a convex polygon in half. Prove that the ratio in which this line divides the projection of the polygon into the line perpendicular to it does not exceed $1 + \sqrt{2}$ (Fig. 1, a). b) Each of the three straight lines divides the area of ​​this figure in half. Prove that the area of ​​the part of the figure enclosed in a triangle between the lines does not exceed $1/4$ all plane figures (Fig. 1, b).

V. Prasolov

M768*. The sum of $n$ numbers, each of which does not exceed modulo 1, is equal to $s$. Prove that you can choose several numbers from them so that the sum of the selected numbers differs from $s/3$ by no more than $1/3$.

V. Grinberg

M769*. The bisectors of the triangle $ABC$ intersect at the point $L$, their continuations intersect the circumcircle of the triangle at the points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ (Fig. 2). Let $R$ be the circumradius and $r$ the circumradius of triangle $ABC$. Prove the equalities

a) $\frac{|LA_1| \cdot |LC_1|}{|LB|} = R$; b) $\frac{|LA| \cdot |LC|}{|LB_1|} = r$;

b) $\frac{S_{ABC}}{S_{A_1B_1C_1}} = \frac{2r}{R}$.

R. Mazov

M770*. At the base of the triangular paramid $PABC$ lies a regular triangle $ABC$. Prove that if the angles $PAB, PBC, PCA$ are congruent, then the paramida $PABC$ is regular.
