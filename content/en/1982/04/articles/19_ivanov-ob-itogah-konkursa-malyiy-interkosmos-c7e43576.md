About the results of the “Small Intercosmos” competition

The Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Academy of Sciences of the USSR, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the USSR State Committee for Vocational Technical Education, the All-Union Society "Znanie" from February to November 1981 held the first All-Union competition of school students, professionalism for the best project of a space experiment, dedicated to the 20th anniversary of the first manned space flight, citizen of the USSR Yu. A. Gagarin *). Boys and girls from Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Gorky, Kaluga, Moscow, Rostov, Saratov, Tashkent regions took an active part in the competition. Moscow, Tbilisi. In total, the competition organizing committee received over 2 thousand projects of scientific, technical, applied and technological experiments.

From November 3 to 6, the final stage of the competition took place in Moscow, in which 400 heads of scientific and technical associations, young adventure and astronautics enthusiasts from all resource-rich countries took part. The average of them are 94 members of VOIR and NOU, 44 participants of VDNH of the USSR, 26 laureates of the HTTM. By age, the vast majority were students in grades 8-10. The youngest participant in the final is Ira Durmanova, a 3rd grade student from. w. No. 6 Kherson. The following delegations of students from the People's Republic of Vietnam, the People's Republic of Vietnam, the German Democratic Republic, the Lao PDR, the Mongolia, the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, and Czechoslovakia are brought to the final stage of the competition.

Reading of proposed projects for 8 sections. They were led by the authors.

*) The conditions of the competition were published in «Kvant», 1981, No. 4, 5.
