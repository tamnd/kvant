Physics
Oral exam tasks

1. The ordium flies out with a speed of $v_0=600$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon. Determine the highest altitude and flight time outside. Claim air resistance.

2. A car with mass $m$ moves at a constant speed $v$: 1) along a horizontal flat place; 2) at a convex place; 3) along a concave place. The radius of curvature of the place in the last two cases is $R$. With what force does the car press on the spot, driving through its middle, in each of the cases?

3. A skater with a mass of $M=70$ kg, standing on skates on ice, throws a mass of $m=3$ kg in a horizontal direction with a speed of $v=8$ m/s. How far will the skate roll during this speed skating if the friction coefficient of the skates on the ice is $\mu=0,02$?

4. Cylinders with a volume of $V_1=20$ l and $V_2=44$ l contain gas. The pressure in the first cylinder is $p_1=2,4$ mPa, in the second - $p_2=1,6$ mPa. Determine the total pressure after connecting the cylinders if the gas temperature remains the same.

5. In the air, at a distance of $r=10$ cm, there are two exact electric charges $q_1=8 \cdot 10^{-9}$ C and $q_2=-6 \cdot 10^{-9}$ C. Find the direction of the electric field at a point located in the middle between the charges.

6. Determine the internal resistance of the battery if it is known that when it is shorted to an external resistance $R_1=1$ Ohm, the voltage at the battery terminals is $U_1=2$ V, and when it is shorted to a resistance $R_2=2$ Ohm, the voltage at the terminals is $U_2=2,4$ V. The resistance of the supply wires can be claimed.

7. Find the power of an electric heater if it can be used to boil $V=2$ liters of water in $\tau=20$ minutes. Initial water temperature $t=20^\circ\text{C}$; Heater efficiency $\eta=70\%$; specific heat of water $c=4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K).

8. A straight conductor carrying current is placed in a single-pod magnetic field with induction $B=0,2$ T. Determine the force acting on the conductor if the length of the conductor is $l=10$ cm, the current strength is $I=3$ A, and the direction of the current makes an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ with the direction of the field induction.

9. A tie driven into the bottom of the lake is $H=4$ m, protruding from the water by $h=1$ m. Find the length of the shadow of the tie at the bottom of the lake if the rays of the Sun fall on the surface of the water at an angle of $\alpha=45^\circ$. Water assumption indicator $n=4/3$.

10. How to obtain a reduced-scale image of an object using a concave spherical mirror?

Written exam

Option 1
(first exam at the Faculty of Mechanics)

1. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{a^{\frac{3}{2}}+b^{\frac{3}{2}}}{(a^2-ab)^{\frac{2}{3}}}:\frac{a^{-\frac{2}{3}}\cdot\sqrt{a-b}}{a\sqrt{a}-b\sqrt{b}}$$
and evaluate at $a=1, 2$ and $b=3/5$.

2. The roots of the quadratic equation $x^2-12x+c=0$ are both integers, with one root being twice the size of the other. What is the free term $c$?

3. Compute $x$ if
$$\log_2 \log_3 \log_4 x=0.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\log_6 (5+6^{-x})=x+1.$$
5. Find the most integer value of $x$ that satisfies the inequality
$$3 \cdot 5^{2x-1}-2 \cdot 5^{x-1}<0,2.$$
6. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3-x}+\frac{6}{\sqrt{3-x}}=\sqrt{9-5x}.$$
7. Find the maximum value of the function $y=x^4-2x^2+5$ on the segment $[-2; 2]$.

8. What angle $\phi$ does the tangent to the curve $y=x-x^2$ form with the abscissa axis at the point with the abscissa $x=1$?

9. Graph the function $y=\frac{x-1}{x+1}$. How much error does a graph with axis $Ox$ have?

10. Find the sum of the geometric progression if it is known that the second term is $a_2=-5/9$, the value of the progression is $q=-1/9$. Option 2
(second exam at the Faculty of Mechanics)

1. The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 32 cm, the base is in relation to the side as 6:5. Determine the area of ​​the triangle.

2. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by lines $y=9-x^2$, $y=0$.

3. Compute $\frac{87}{3+4\cos 2\alpha}$ if $tg \alpha=0,2$.

4. Calculate $\cos 45^\circ \cdot \cos 15^\circ-\sin 45^\circ \cdot \sin 15^\circ$.

5. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{tg(\alpha+\beta)-tg(\alpha-\beta)}{tg(\alpha+\beta)} \cdot ctg \beta.$$
6. Find the most integer positive root of the equation
$$tg(x+20^\circ)+tg(70^\circ-x)=2.$$
Write your answer in degrees.

7. Find the length of the median of the triangle drawn from vertex $B$ if $A(2;-1)$, $B(0;-3)$ and $C(4;3)$ are the vertices of the triangle.
