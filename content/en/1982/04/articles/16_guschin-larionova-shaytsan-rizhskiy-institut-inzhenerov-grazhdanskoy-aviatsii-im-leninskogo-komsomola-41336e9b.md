⟦folio 48⟧

Option 2
1. Simplify the expression
$$(-\sqrt[3]{x}^{0,4})^5 + \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-1}\sqrt[3]{x^2} - x^{-1}\left(\frac{-3x^{\frac{2}{3}}}{\sqrt{x}^{-0,5}}\right)^2.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\log_5(x^2 - 11x + 43) = 2.$$
3. Solve the inequality $f'(x) \geq g'(x)$ if $f(x) = 5 - 3x + \frac{5}{2}x^2 - \frac{x^3}{3}$, $g(x) = 3x - 7$.
4. Solve the equation
$$\sin x - \cos x - 4\cos^2 x \cdot \sin x = 4\sin^3 x.$$
5. Find the volume of a cone whose height is 12 dm and whose cross-sectional area is 48 dm$^2$.

Oral exam objectives
1. Graph the function
a) $y = 2^{\log_2 x}$;
b) $y = \log_1 - \cos^2 x \sin^2 x$.
2. Find the sign of the number log x if $x^3 < x^2$.
3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{(x - 1)^2 x}{x + 1} \leq 0.$$
4. To what set does the product of the function $y = \sqrt{x^3}$ map the interval $\left[\frac{1}{16}; 81\right]$?
5. Find all values ​​of a for which the inequality holds
$$\int_0^a x dx \leq a + 4.$$
6. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the lines $y = \sqrt{x}$, $y = 2$, $x = 9$.
7. Find the angle between disjoint edges of a right triangular straight line.

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. Two ships are moving relative to the shore at speeds $v_1 = 9$ kt and $v_2 = 12$ kt, directed at angles $a_1 = 30^\circ$ and $a_2 = 60^\circ$ to the meridian. At what speed is the second ship moving relative to the first? (1 knot = 0.514 m/s - note ped.).
2. A merchant ship, having taken the cargo to the port of Murmansk ($\varphi = 70^\circ$ northern ship), transported it to a port located on the equator. How much did the weight of the cargo at the equator decrease compared to the weight of the cargo at the port of Murmansk? (When solving the problem, take into account the effect created by the rotation of the Earth; consider the Earth to be a ball.)
3. While lifting the ship, a large ice floe hit the dock. What will happen to the water level in the dock when the ice floe melts?
4. How many molecules are contained: 1) in 1 cm$^3$ of fresh water; 2) in 1 cm$^3$ of air (under normal conditions)?
5. Three identical charges $q = 10^{-6}$ C are located at the vertices of an equilateral triangle on the side $r = 20$ cm. How much work must be done to arrange these charges in one line with a distance of 10 cm between neighboring ones? The charges are in a vacuum.
6. A charged ball with a radius of $r_1 = 1$ cm is connected by a thin long conductor to an uncharged ball whose radius is $r_2 = 2$ cm. After the balls are separated, the energy of the second ball turns out to be $W = 0,3$ J. What charge was on the first ball before the connection? Neglect the electrical properties of the connecting conductor.
7. An elevator with a mass of $m = 0,5$ t rises to a height of $h = 25$ m in $t = 30$ s. Electric motor operating voltage $U = 127$ V, morat efficiency $\eta = 90\%$. Determine the power consumed by the motor and the current in the motor windings.
8. The ship's radio circuit is set to a frequency of $f = 9$ MHz. How should the capacitance of the variable capacitor of the oscillating circuit of the application be changed so that it is present at a wavelength of $\lambda = 50$ m?
9. The left side light bulb emits electromagnetic waves with a length of $\lambda = 7 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m, which corresponds to the color red. What is the length of these rays in water ($n = 1,33$)?
10. Which color of sail, black or white, will the light exert more pressure on?

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. A section is drawn through the top of a right triangular base and the middle of two sides of the base. Find the cross-sectional area and volume of the base, knowing the side of the base a and the angle a between the section and the base.
2. Solve the equation
$$tg 3x - tg x = 4 \sin x.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\lg (35 - x^3)}{\lg (5 - x)} = 3.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{x + 8 + 2\sqrt{x + 7}} + \sqrt{x + 1 - \sqrt{x + 7}} = 4.$$
5. Calculate Limit
$$\lim_{x \to 5} \frac{\sqrt{x - 1} - 2}{x - 5}.$$
