Physics
Oral exam assignment

1. A load with a mass of $m = 100$ kg is suspended from the ceiling of an elevator. The force $F = 1500$ N causes the elevator to move upward. The load is located at a distance of $l = 1$ m from the elevator field. The thread suddenly breaks. How long will it take from the moment the sinus ruptures until the load hits the floor?

2. A neutron (its mass $m_1$) hits the stationary nucleus of a carbon atom ($m_2 = 12m_1$). Assuming the impact to be central and elastic, find how many times the kinetic energy of the neutron decreases as it moves away.

3. A cylinder with a volume of $V = 10$ l contains helium under a pressure of $p_1 = 10$ atm and at a temperature of $t_1 = 27^\circ\text{C}$. After $m = 10$ g of gel was taken from the balloon, the temperature in the balloon dropped to $t_2 = 17^\circ\text{C}$. Determine the pressure of the gel remaining in the balloon.

4. A ball with a diameter of $d = 1$ cm and a charge of $q = 10^{-6}$ C is placed in oil with a density of $\rho_m = 0,8$ g/cm$^3$. The density of the ball materials is $\rho = 8,6$ g/cm$^3$. In what electric field, directed vertically upward, must the ball be placed so that it floats in oil?

5. A potential difference of $U = 300$ V is applied between the plates of a flat capacitor located at a distance of $d = 1$ cm from each other. A plane-parallel plate of stack with a thickness of $d_1 = 0,3$ cm and a plane-parallel plate of paraffin with a thickness of $d_2 = 0,7$ cm are placed in the space between the plates. Find the directions on each layer. Dielectric food power is equal, corresponding to $\varepsilon_1 = 6$ and $\varepsilon_2 = 2,6$.

6. Find the amount of heat released every second per unit volume of copper wire at a current density of $j = 30$ A/cm$^2$. Copper resistivity $q = 1,71 \times 10^{-8}$ Ohm m.

7. An electron enters a flat horizontal capacitor, parallel to its plates, with a speed of $v_0 = 2 \cdot 10^7$ m/s. The length of the capacitor is $l = 10$ cm, the direction of the electric field of the capacitor is $E = 200$ V/cm. When an electron leaves a capacitor, it enters a magnetic field whose lines are perpendicular to the confluent lines of the electric field. Magnetic field induction $B = 2 \times 10^{-2}$ T. Find the radius of the electron's helical trajectory in a magnetic field.

8. A coil, the inductance of which is $L = 3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ H, is connected to a flat capacitor with an area of ​​plates of $S = 100$ cm$^2$ and a distance between them of $d = 0,1$ mm. What is the dielectric value of the food medium filling the space between the plates if the circuit is infused with a wave length of $\lambda = 750$ m?

9. Light falling from air onto a plane-parallel glass plate is reflected (from the plate) at an angle $\gamma = 60^\circ$ and refracted (in the plate) at an angle $\beta = 30^\circ$. Determine the speed of light in plastic.

10. A charged body with mass $m = 0,02$ kg and charge $q_1 = 5 \cdot 10^{-6}$ K moves from infinity with a speed $v_0 = 3$ m/s along the main optical axis of a concave mirror with a radius of curvature $R = 1$ m to its pole, where another charge $q_2$ is located. Determine the value of charge $q_2$ at which the charged body remained with itself.
