IN ORBIT - NEW MUSEUM

You can start with the words “first”, “for the first time”. But these words largely refer to the museum itself.

For the first time in museum practice, sound, lighting, optical effects, projection equipment, and the latest designs are used in such a comprehensive form. The goal that is being pursued is that a person who comes here must experience the maximum possible “immersion” into space on earth.

Already in the display cases filling the spacesuits, visitors encounter documents, commemorative medals and other relics marking the main stages in the development of Soviet cosmonautics. Then, through the original "gateway", visitors enter the main hall, which fills the interior of the spaceship. In the depths of the halls there is a monumental composition, which consists of a stained glass window emitting a “cosmic” blue-blue glow, a semi-open sphere “Zodiac Signs”, which embodies our past ideas about the Universe, and a sculpture of an astronaut (part of this composition is reproduced on the first page of our cover).

There are few display cases in the museum. Walking along the hall, visitors see the exhibits as if through the windows of a spaceship. Mirror openings made of blackened steel create the illusion of “weightlessness” in which the displayed samples of space technology are located. A blue luminous ribbon separates the exhibits from the audience. Acoustic and television equipment allows you to tell a colorful story about the everyday life and holidays of our astronautics.

The tour of the museum ends in the cinema hall. A seven-meter model of a planetary plane with a solar battery floats above the translucent mirror. The surface of the hall walls is made in the form of a relief with a color-dynamic lighting system.

In this hall, with the help of the latest laser, electronic and split-screen technology, the audience is shown an artistic composition reflecting the humanistic ideals of man in the exploration of outer space. For 20 minutes, visitors are taken on a journey into the past, present and future of space exploration.

Artists, sculptors, architects, and designers participated in the creation of the museum's exhibition: O. Lomako (chief artist), G. Schultz, N. Lomako, Zh. Kankayan, N. Rudko, I. Kazansky. The scientific leadership for the creation of the museum was carried out by Z. Kostrikina.

V. Koleychuk
