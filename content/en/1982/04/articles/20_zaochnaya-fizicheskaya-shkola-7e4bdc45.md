⟦folio 57⟧

Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov announces enrollment of students in grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher education, primarily for the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out on the basis of the results of the entrance task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 15 to the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, Correspondence School of Physics. The decision of the 3FS admissions committee on enrollment will be communicated by October 15. Verified introductory assignments will not be sent back.

During the year, members of the 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on various sections of physics studied in high school. Solved assignments are assessed, solved and returned to students. At the end of the year, 9th grade students are transferred to 10th grade based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task

Those entering the 9th grade need to solve problems 1-4, those entering the 10th grade need to solve problems 3-6.

1. A body of mass m slides without initial velocity down a hill of height H (Fig. 1). Friction coefficient between the body and the slide μ. The slide can move on a horizontal surface without friction; slide mass M; angle of inclination to the horizon α. Find the distance the slide moves while the body moves along the slide.

2. Two plate balls with masses m and M move perpendicular to each other with velocities v and V, respectively. Their collision occurs, as a result of which the balls stick together and then move as a single whole. Determine the energy released during the collision in the form of a body.

3. A homogeneous cube of mass m with edge length a ends on a horizontal surface; friction coefficient between the surface and the cube is μ=0.8. A load of mass M is attached to the cube using an insoluble thread and is thrown over a stationary block (Fig. 2). At what minimum height h of fastening the thread will the base of the cube be rejected, and the cube will move forward? Neglect the friction of the thread on the block.

4. It is known that the acceleration of gravity on the surface of the Moon is approximately 6 times less than on the surface of the Earth. From this, estimate what results the world record holders in the height and length of the buckles, the javelin throw, and the 100-meter race would show on the Moon.

5. The cylinder, made of body-insulating material, is divided into three parts with volumes V1, V2 and V3 by non-body-conducting flaps. All three parts contain the same gas, and its pressure and temperature in the first part are p1 and T1, in the second - p2 and T2, and in the third - p3 and T3. What pressure will be established in the cylinder if the valves are open?

6. A metal cube of mass m and resistance R begins to fall under the influence of friction from a height H in a magnetic field with induction changing with height x according to the law B(x)=B0(1-x/H) (Fig. 3). Determine the steady speed of the fall of the ring if its radius is l and the plane of the ring remains perpendicular to the lines of magnetic induction.
