Option 1

1. Laws of Boyle - Mariotte, Gay-Lusac, Charles. Charts of these laws. The concept of abscyltom of temperature zeros. Absolute temperature scale. Equation of state of an ideal gas.

2. A straight permanent magnet falls through a metal ring. Will the magnet fall due to the acceleration of free fall? Air resistance can be claimed. Explain the answer.

3. The plane describes a “dead loop” in the vertical plane. Determine the lowest speed of the airplane at which the pilot will not break away from the airplane at the top of the loop. Loop radius $R = 180$ m. Gravity acceleration $g = 9,8$ m/$^2$.

4. Determine the maximum values ​​of the kinetic energy and speed of electrons emitted from the metal under the influence of gamma rays with a wavelength of $\lambda = 0,03$ nm. Planck's constant $h = 6,62 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s. The speed of light is $c = 3 \cdot 10^8$ m/s. The mass of an electron is $m = 9,1 \times 10^{-31}$ kg.

Option 2

1. Laws of light reflection. Construction of images in spherical mirrors. Focus mirror.

2. How will the period of oscillation of a magnet, which is a small-mass vessel suspended by a length of thread and filled with water, change if water gradually flows out of a hole in the bottom of the vessel?

3. The pig bullet penetrates the docky, and the bullet's speed drops from $v_0 = 400$ m/s to $v = 300$ m/s. What part of the bullet melted? Heated docks can be claimed. Initial temperature $t_0 = 27^\circ C$. The melting point of lead is $t = 327^\circ C$. The specific temperature of lead is $c = 0,126$ kJ/(kg K). The specific melting point of lead is $\lambda = 0,25 \times 10^5$ J/kg.

4. An electron (charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, mass $m = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg) flies at a speed $v = 1,5 \cdot 10^7$ m/s into a uniform magnetic field with an indication $B = 6,28 \cdot 10^{-4}$ T perpendicular to the field line. Find the radius of the circle that the electron circles in this field.

Option 3

1. AC. Alternator. Period and frequency of alternating current. Effective value of voltage and current. Transformer. Energy transmission and distribution.

2. Beams of red and green light pass from water to air. Show the path of the rays. Explain your answer.

3. A projectile with a mass of $m_1 = 50$ kg, flying at an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the vertical with a speed of $v_0 = 800$ m/s, hits a platform loaded with sand and gets stuck in it. Find the speed of the platform after the projectile falls if its mass is $m_2 = 16$ t.

4. An electron in an unexcited hydrogen atom received an energy of $E = 12$ eV. What energy level has he moved to? How many lines can be seen in the radiation spectrum when electrons move to lower energy levels? The ground state energy of the hydrogen atom is $E_1 = -13,5$ eV.
