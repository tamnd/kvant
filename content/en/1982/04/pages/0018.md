Here is what D.V. Skobeltsyn wrote:

“...from time to time, the photographs revealed straight paths of particles of unknown origin, almost not deflected by the magnetic field, but in their ionizing effect not distinguishable in any way from fast β-rays, photographs of which are available in the same photographs. Further observations, the results of which were published in 1925, made it possible to establish that the appearance of the paths of such “ultra β-rays” is relative to the passage through the cloud chamber of γ-rays, which is the presence of a stream of particles producing the atmosphere.”

Particles of such high energy that they practically did not deflect in the relatively weak field of the chamber.

Skobeltsyn proved that the initiation created by space exploration can be explained by “ultra β-particles.”

In a number of Skobeltsyn’s photographs, a solid stream of fast particles was visible. They were called cosmic showers.

So, the links in the chain leading to positrow have been assembled:

1. Cosmic rays are discovered.
2. A device was created - a cloud chamber placed in a magnetic field.
3. This device allows it to be part of space exploration. charged particles enter.

But the field was still small and did not deflect “ultra β-rays.”

Discovery of the positron

We have already mentioned the name Roberta by Andris Milliken. He was a distinguished physicist who received the Nobel Prize in 1923 “for his work on the study of the elementary charge of electricity and the photoelectric effect.” According to the recollections of friends, he combined (in addition to many other tempting traits) two qualities that were difficult not to notice: talkativeness, many times higher than the average norm, and deep physical intuition. Whether the former played any role in the discovery of the positron remains unknown. But the second one turned out to be decisive. Having learned about Skobeltsyn's experiments, Millikan immediately instructed one of his employees, Karl Anderson, to begin research into space exploration using a cloud chamber, but already placed in a much stronger magnetic field than in Skobeltsyn's experiments. This required both experimental art and technical means that were exceptional for that time.
