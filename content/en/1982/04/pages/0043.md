⟦folio 41⟧

some inequality is equivalent to one system
$$\begin{cases} f(x) < [g(x)]^2 \\ f(x) \geq 0 \\ g(x) > 0. \end{cases}$$

The inequality $\sqrt{x^3-x-1} > \sqrt{x^3-1}$ is equivalent to the system
$$\begin{cases} x^3-x-1 > x^3-1 \\ x^3-1 \geq 0. \end{cases}$$

Any inequality of the form $\sqrt{f(x)} > \sqrt{g(x)}$ is equivalent to the system
$$\begin{cases} f(x) > g(x) \\ g(x) \geq 0. \end{cases}$$
