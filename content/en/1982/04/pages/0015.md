Rutherforad's prediction that the neutron becomes an excellent research tool penetrating deep into nuclei has also come true. Many laboratories around the world began bombarding matter with neutrons and obtained interesting results.

About “accidental” discoveries

It is often said that the positron was discovered by accident. Writing like this has become almost traditional. Indeed, Carl Anderson, who made this discovery, conducted experiments “the purpose of which is to measure the energy spectrum of electrons produced in the atmosphere and in materials due to radiation coming from space.” There was no talk of any positrons.

However, any physical discovery, no matter how original and unexpected it may be, is always only a link in an endless chain of successes and mistakes of science. And an example of this is the discovery of the positron. We will only talk about some of the links in the chain that led to the goal.
