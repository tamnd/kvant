M736—M740; F748—F752

M736. The median BC and the bisketris CL of triangle ABC intersect at point P. Prove the equality
$$\frac{|PC|}{|PL|} - \frac{|AC|}{|BC|} = 1.$$
Z. Andzhaparidze

M737. Let's denote by $d_k$ the number of such houses in your city in which at least $k$ residents live ($d_1 \geq d_2 \geq d_3 \geq \dots$), and by $c_m$ the number of residents in the $m$ largest house population ($c_1 \geq c_2 \geq c_3 \geq \dots$). Prove the equalities
a) $c_1 + c_2 + c_3 + \dots = d_1 + d_2 + d_3 + \dots$;
b) $c_1^2 + c_2^2 + c_3^2 + \dots = d_1 + 3d_2 + 5d_3 + \dots + (2k-1)d_k + \dots$;
c) $d_1^2 + d_2^2 + d_3^2 + \dots = c_1 + 3c_2 + 5c_3 + \dots$;
... + $(2m-1)c_m + \dots$,
A. Zelevinsky

M738*. Prove that a) the number of straight lines of different directions onto which a given $n$-gon produces projections of equal magnitude does not exceed $2n$; b) the maximum number of such lines for any polygon is even; c) for a triangle this number is greater than 3 if and only if it is acute.

B. Prasolov

M739. Prove that for any value of $x$ for which the right part makes sense, the equalities hold
$$\frac{tg x + tg \left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + tg \left(x + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right)}{tg x \cdot tg \left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \cdot tg \left(x + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right)} = 3;$$
$$\frac{tg x + tg \left(x + \frac{\pi}{n}\right) + \dots + tg \left(x + \frac{(n-1)\pi}{n}\right)}{tg x \cdot tg \left(x + \frac{\pi}{n}\right) \cdot \dots \cdot tg \left(x + \frac{(n-1)\pi}{n}\right)} = c_n,$$
where $n$ is an odd number, $c_n$ is a constant (depending on $n$).
b) $^*$ Find $c_n$ for every odd $n=5, 7, \dots$.
