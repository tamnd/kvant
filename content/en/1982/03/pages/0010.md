The brothers were actively involved in the work of the academy. The minutes of the meeting dated January 14, 1726 record: “Daniel Bernoulli, professor of physiology, began mathematical studies on the Theory of Medical Needs, and decency will teach them physiologically: before lunch from 7 to 8. Nicholas Bernoulli, professor of mathematics, will read about those parts of mathematics that are tied to physics, and will read especially about mechanics from 8 to 9.”

The activities of Nikolai Bernoulli at the St. Petersburg Academy of Science did not last long. The climate of the northern capital turned out to be too harsh for him. Eight months after his arrival in St. Petersburg, Nikolai Bernoulli died.

Daniel Bernoulli remained in St. Petersburg until the summer of 1733, and then returned to Basel. Here he received the chair of medicine at the university, but was mainly engaged in experimental physics. In 1750, he headed the department of physics, which he held until the last days of his life.

After leaving Russia, Daniil Bernoulli maintained technical contact with the St. Petersburg Academy of Science and sent his works for publication. Of the 75 scientific works of Daniil Bernoulli, 47 were published in the proceedings of the St. Petersburg Academy of Science.

Daniel Bernoulli's contribution to science can hardly be overestimated. He studied the Ricatti equations $y = ax^n + by^2$, flasks of scabs and rods, probability theories, series, the limit of the expression $(1 + \frac{1}{n})^n$... Together with M.V. Lomonosov, he stood at the origins of the kinetic theory of gases. In his works one can find an anticipation of the laws of Gay-Lussac, Clapeyron and Charles...

Daniel Bernoulli was the first to suggest that gas pressure is caused by the thermal motion of molecules. In his “Hydrodynamics”, bringing him worldwide fame, he writes:

"Hydrodynamics" was written in St. Petersburg and published in Strasbourg in 1738. In the preface to it, Daniel Bernoulli writes: “...I gladly announce that the main part of this work is due to the leadership, plans and support of the St. Petersburg Academy of Science... My present work represents the only goal: to bring use to the point of supporting the growth and social benefits of science.”

Daniil Bernoulli repeatedly emphasized that he was formed as a scientist at the St. Petersburg Academy of Science, that he made his discoveries in science thanks exclusively to the conditions that were created in Russia for the life and work of scientists. In hydrodynamics, Daniel Bernoulli gave the equation of the established motion of an ideal incompressible fluid. This equation is now called Bernoulli's equation.
