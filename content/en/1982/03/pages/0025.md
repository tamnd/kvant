A piece of nylon of the required size can be obtained by melting old stockings, pieces of fishing line, etc. at a temperature of 120° in an aluminum or tin jar. Nylon is easily cut with a knife, and a rough cross is easy to make. To give each arm of the cross an exact cylindrical shape, the following device is convenient: drill 4-5 holes in the metal plate; the narrowest one is of the desired diameter, each next one is 0.2-0.3 mm wider. When we push the nylon arm of the cross through the hole, the sharp edge of the hole removes shavings from the nylon. After pressing through the holes one by one, we will get a shoulder with an almost perfect cylindrical shape. When processing each next shoulder, you need to ensure that it turns out to be strictly perpendicular to those already processed shoulders, to which it should be perpendicular, and to the co-stems with the opposite shoulder. Perpendicularity is checked with a square for clearance. Violations are corrected with a knife. The cores are checked by eye and trimmed with a knife.

Thin cross axes are best made from steel knitting needles with a diameter of 2.5 mm, cut into pieces of the required length. The thick arms of the cross are cut to size (Fig. 11), and then axial holes with a diameter of 2 mm are drilled into them (Fig. 12). The ends of the thin axles (~7 mm) are roughly notched with a saw. These ends are heated and pressed into high-drilled holes. In order not to damage the cross, this operation is best performed by inserting the operated shoulder into the hole that was used for cutting to size. The remaining end of the thin axle is threaded. Since the start of the thread is always not very precise, it is better to make the thin axle a little longer. When the nut is screwed onto the thread, the excess piece is sawed off with a saw.

To tighten the nut, you need to make a slot (Fig. 13: the same figure shows the end of the hole for tightening such a nut).

The final fit of the cross will be checked when the puzzle is assembled, so the cross should not be perfected immediately.
