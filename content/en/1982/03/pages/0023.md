⟦folio 21⟧

Fig. 4. Central cube on the cross (1 - lid; 2 - central cube; 3 - nut; 4 - spring; 5 - washer; 6 - thin axis of the cross; 7 - thick arm of the cross).

Fig. 5. The inner side of the cube face, from the cross (also remove one corner cube).

Fig. 6. View of a cube from which one side and one middle cube have been removed.

Figures 1, 2 and 3 show the correspondence between the inner cross, the middle cube and the corner cube. figure 4 shows the mounting of the central cube on the inner cross.

Figure 5 depicts the inner side of the face from the cross.

Figure 6 depicts a “magic cube”, with one side and one of the middle cubes.

For greater clarity, in Figures 5 and 6, the central cubes, middle cubes, corner cubes and the inner cross are shown in different colors. This coloring is not related to the colored stickers on the outer edges of the cubes.

Figures 5 and 6 show how the protrusions on the middle and corner cubes fold into an almost cylindrical protrusion on the inside of the face of the large cube, and a cylindrical ring-shaped depression is formed on the middle layer. The rotation of the layer (face) corresponds to the rotation of the cylindrical protrusion in the cylindrical recess.

This is the whole secret of E. Rubik's puzzle!

The role of spring 4 (see Fig. 4) is to be able to slightly pull back the rotated layer when turning.

From figure 4 you can see, in part, how to disassemble the “magic cube”. To do this, you need to remove the colored sticker from any one central cube, pull out the cap 1, hooking it by the edge with a needle or knife, and release the spring 4. After this, the central cube is removed from the axis of the cross, and the puzzle can be easily solved.

***

Let's move on to the second topic of the article - how to make a homemade “magic cube”. This is not about some inferior imitation of an industrial prototype. A homemade puzzle should be both more beautiful and more reliable than an industrial one. As one wise proverb says, if something is worth doing at least somehow, then it is worth doing properly.
