Why do we keep looking at the square all the time?”

Instead of answering, the teacher drew an equilateral triangle on the board, drew straight lines through its vertices, cutting off the $\frac{1}{3}$ of the opposite side (Fig. 5), and asked: “What figure do the drawn straight lines form?”

“It’s probably an equilateral triangle,” said Petya, “but I don’t know how this proves it. Maybe we should use self-alignment of an equilateral triangle here?”

“And what self-alignments and how many does an equilateral triangle have?” - asked the teacher.

After some thought, the guys came to this: “When an equilateral triangle is self-aligned, its vertices transform into themselves. This means that an equilateral triangle has as many self-combinations as the number of permutations that can be made from three letters. There are six such permutations: this means that there are no more than six self-combinations. Here they are: identity, rotations $R_0^{120^\circ}$, $R_0^{-120^\circ}$ and axial symmetries $S_{l_1}, S_{l_2}, S_{l_3}$ (O is the center of the triangle, $l_1, l_2, l_3$ are its medians).”

The guys reported that in the last problem we need to consider a rotation around the center of the triangle by $120^\circ$. With this rotation, the protected triangle will turn into itself. It follows that it is equilateral (why?). Solution problem.

In conclusion, the teacher invited the children to solve the following problems at home on their own:

1. Prove that the “inside” of a regular pentagonal star (Fig. 6) is a regular pentagonal star.
2. Two external and two internal tangents are drawn to two circles. Prove that the intersection point of the external tangents and the intersection point of the internal tangents lie on a straight line passing through the centers of the given circles.
3. Prove that a parallelogram inscribed in a circle is a straight line.
4*. Construct a square if there is a point on each side.

He also recommended reading in «Kvant» the article “Self-alignment of the square and secret writing” (1980, No. 12).
