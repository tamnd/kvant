⟦folio 10⟧

A. Varlamov, A. Shapiro

In the blue expanse

Looking around, we most often see only what we are already familiar with, what we are used to. Meanwhile, the process of cognition almost always begins with new observations.

Artists have a keen, professional observation, which is why the world reflected through the eyes of realist artists in their landscapes is especially bright and multi-colored, and individual natural phenomena appear. And at the same time, even a very good artist does not need concepts, sometimes the complex essence of phenomena that originate in nature, which he realistically displays on his canvases. But you can study the world around you from the paintings of a good landscape artist even better than in real life, or in a landscape the author seems to freeze a moment, intuitively enhancing the essential and omitting the unnecessary, the accidental.

Take a closer look at the painting “In the Blue Expanse” by Arkady Aleksandrovich Rylov. “White birds, like clouds, soar easily, bathing in the blue azure. And as smoothly as a white bird, a ship covered with sails glides over the rippling blue waves of the ocean,” this is how the famous art critic A. Fedorov-Davydov describes this picture. Admiring this wonderful painting in the Tretyakov Gallery, you forget that you are groaning in the hall of a museum, and you feel like you are a participant in this festival of nature.

And now let’s answer from painting and looking around through the eyes of an art critic. Where did we end up?

This question is not difficult to answer - the rocky island visible in the distance is covered with snow, and the sea is not at all wintery, so we are probably in polar latitudes.

Where did the artist paint the landscape from - from a rocky ledge on the shore? or from the ship?
