Winners of the Kvaenta competition

Every year our magazine holds a competition among schoolchildren to solve problems from the Quaenta Problem Book. In accordance with the decision of the organizing committee of the All-Union Olympiad, schoolchildren who won this competition receive the right to participate immediately in the fourth (republican) round of the All-Union Olympiad.

Below is a list of winners of the 1981 Quaenta competition who received the right to participate in the 1982 Republican Olympics.
