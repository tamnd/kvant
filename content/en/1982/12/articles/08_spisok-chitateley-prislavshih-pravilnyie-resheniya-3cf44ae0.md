How many degrees will the water rise in one minute if you start dripping the second cup?
Assume that the temperature of the water in the kettle equalizes very quickly. Only water pours out of the spout. The kettle contains 0.3 liters of water. Ambient air temperature $t_0 = 20^\circ C$.

F777. A block is suspended from a weightless neural thread. The distance between the suspension points is equal to the diameter of the block; the length of the vertical sections of the thread is $l$ (Fig. 1). Determine the period of small oscillations of the system in the vertical plane in which it lies.

Fig. 1. Fig. 2.

$cm \cdot \Delta t_1 = nm_k c (t_2 - t_1)$.

From here we find $n$:

$$n = \frac{m}{m_k} \frac{\Delta t_1}{t_2 - t_1} = 20 \text{ капель в минуту.}$$

If you drip not $n$, but $3n$ drops per minute, then the water in the kettle will heat up in one minute

$$\Delta t_2 = \frac{3\Delta Q_2 - \Delta Q_1}{cm} = 2\Delta t_1 = 0,4 \text{ градуса.}$$

A. Zilberman

Let us show that at small deflection angles the block moves translationally, that is, at any moment in time all points of the block have the same speed.

Let's deflect the threads at a small angle $a$ from the vertical. Let $C$ and $D$ be the extreme points of contact of the thread with the block (Fig. 2). Let's see what angle the radius vectors of the points $C$ and $B$ ($D$ and $A$) make. It is clear that the lengths of each $AD$ and $CB$ are equal

$$\Delta l = \frac{1}{2} |FE| = \frac{1}{2} d\alpha$$

(since $a$ is small, sin $\alpha \approx \alpha$).

On the other side,

$$\Delta l = \frac{1}{2} d\varphi.$$

Therefore, $\varphi = \alpha$. This means that the segment $AB$ still lies on the horizontal, that is, the block moves translationally.

Thus, the kinetic energy of the block at any moment of time is equal to $mv_i^2/2$, where $m$ is the mass of the block, $v_i$ is the speed of its center at a given moment. Therefore, at any moment of time, the total energy of the block is equal to

$$\frac{mv_i^2}{2} + mgl(1 - \cos \alpha_i),$$

that is, the same as that of a mathematical matrix of length $l$. Consequently, the oscillations of the block are identical to the oscillations of the mathematical matrix; period of oscillation

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{l/g}.$$

V. Ilyin
