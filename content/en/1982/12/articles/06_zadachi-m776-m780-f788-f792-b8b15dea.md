⟦folio 18⟧

Tasks

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time. The mathematics problems from this issue were presented this summer at the XXIII International Mathematical Olympiad. Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than February 28, 1983 to the address: 103006, Moscow, K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 12 - 82” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M776, M777” or “F788”. Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. In the letter, include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the result of checking the solution). Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “... new problem in mathematics”).

M776 — M780; F788 — F792

M776. Points M and N are taken on the diagonals AC and CE of the regular hexagon ABCDEF, respectively, such that

$$\frac{|AM|}{|AC|} = \frac{|CN|}{|CE|} = \lambda.$$

It is known that points B, M and N lie on the same line. Find λ.

M777. The equation $x^3 - 3xy^2 + y^3 = n$ is given. Prove that a) if a natural number n is such that a given equation has an integer solution, then it has at least three integer solutions;
6) with n=2891 this equation has no integer solutions.

M778*. Given an unequal triangle $A_1A_2A_3$. Let $a_i$ be its side lying opposite the vertex $A_i$ ($i=1, 2, 3$), $M_i$ the midpoint of side $a_i$, $T_i$ the point of tangency of the side with the circle inscribed in the given triangle, and $S_i$ the point symmetrical to $T_i$ with respect to the bissertrix of the angle $A_i$ triangle. Prove that the lines $M_1S_1$, $M_2S_2$ and $M_3S_3$ have a common point.

M779*. Consider sequences $\{x_n\}$ of positive numbers satisfying the conditions

$$1 = x_0 \geqslant x_1 \geqslant x_2 \geqslant \dots \geqslant x_n \geqslant \dots$$

a) Prove that for any such sequence $\{x_n\}$ there exists n such that

$$\frac{x_0^2}{x_1} + \frac{x_1^2}{x_2} + \dots + \frac{x_{n-1}^2}{x_n} \geqslant 3,999.$$

6) Find a sequence $\{x_n\}$ satisfying the specified conditions such that for any n

$$\frac{x_0^2}{x_1} + \frac{x_1^2}{x_2} + \dots + \frac{x_{n-1}^2}{x_n} < 4.$$

A. Grishkov
