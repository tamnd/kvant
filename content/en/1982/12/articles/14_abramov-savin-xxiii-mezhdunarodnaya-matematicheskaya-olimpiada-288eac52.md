XXIII International Mathematical Olympiad

The good tradition of ending the season of mathematical competitions for schoolchildren with the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) in the middle of summer was continued in 1982: from July 5 to 14, the Hungarian cities of Budapest and Cegléd took part in the XXIII Olympiad.

The guys who won the right to participate in this Olympiad had a more difficult time than their older comrades who competed earlier. Due to the rapid growth in the number of participating countries (in Hungary there were already 30 - this is an Olympiad record), the size of the team was reduced from 8 to 4 participants, and naturally, the level of requirements for selection in teams increased; increased responsibly, and consequently, so did the load. Therefore, the result of each of the members of our team, who managed to overcome the frill barriers and then perform well in Budapest (all four were loaded with medals, in an unofficial sense the team defeated many strong teams and took second place), deserves high praise. But first things first.

A new week-long gathering, at which, after a series of test tasks from 12 winners of the All-World Olympiads of recent years, 6 candidates for the USSR team were selected. The final squad was determined at the end of a month-long training camp, also held in Chernogolovka in June. Members of the USSR national team were K. Matveev (Novosibirsk, Physics School No. 165 at NSU), G. Perelman (Leningrad, school No. 239), A. Spiak (Moscow, Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University) and V. Titenko (village of Bluzha, Minsk region). The reserve participants S. Matyushov and S. Samborsky (Moscow, Physics and Mathematics School No. 18 at Moscow State University) were also well prepared - they helped the team, they themselves were ready to participate in the IMO and, by rights, became students of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University before the start of the entrance exams. (Since the timing of training camps and IMOs coincides with the timing of university entrance exams, both team members and reserve participants are enrolled in the selected universities without exams. Readers will probably agree that this route of admission is not the easiest.)

At the Yuon training camp, future MMO participants prepared according to the program developed in recent years - they solved problems, regularly practiced sports; The cultural program was also a community gathering. A. Zemlyakov, B. Ivlev, S. Konyagin, V. Prasolov actively participated in the preparation of the team at all stages. Interesting and vibrant classes were conducted by O. Lyashko and A. Razborov. The team leaders were A. M. Abramov and T. A. Sarycheva.

The team arrived in Budapest on July 7. And from July 6 to 8 in the city of Cegled, the international jury, chaired by Academician A. Csasar (VNR), completed the last preparatory stage - selected problems for the Olympiad, approved the translation texts, and evaluated the problems in points.

The high professionalism of the organization of the Olympiad once again testifies to both the level of the Hungarian mathematical school and the extensive experience of working with young mathematicians - let us note that Hungary was one of the first countries in the world to begin holding mathematical Olympids back in the last century.

Hospitable hostesses helped the participants become more familiar with Hungary, its history and culture. I spent a long time in my memory and excursion around Budapest, which rightly ranks among the most beautiful cities in the world. There was a trip with swimming to Lake Balaton, a boat trip along the Danube, which has become less blue since the time of Johann Strauss, but remains beautiful. And the presented souvenirs - the famous “snake”, “domino” and, of course, the “magic cube” - will remind Olympiad participants of their meeting with the creator of these toys, Ernyo Rubik.

As always, the atmosphere at the Olympics was the warmest and most friendly. Participants from all continents (only Antarctica was not yet represented) constantly made promises, told each other about their countries, exchanged tasks and sovereignties.

The closing ceremony of the Olympiad took place on July 13 in the assembly hall of the Horticultural University of Budapest. The XXIII Olympid is over—for many Kwan readers, a new Olympic cycle has begun.
