⟦folio 33⟧

Quantum
for younger students

Tasks

1. Replace the asterisks with numbers in the expression 52★2★ so that the resulting number is divisible by 36.

2. 8 colored square napkins are placed on the table one after another (see figure). In this case, the next napkin covers part of at least one of the previous ones. Determine which napkin was placed first and which of the two napkins - brown or white - was placed first?

3. When the second hand on the watch has passed 1 second, the minute hand has passed 6 minutes, but the watch is still working. How can I explain this?

4. For which two-digit numbers does the sum of the cube of the number of ones and the square of the number of tens equal the number itself?

5. What is the maximum number of numbers that can be written in a string such that the sum of any 17 consecutive numbers is even, and the sum of any 18 consecutive numbers is odd?

These tasks were offered to us
K. Ashurbekov, F. Bartenev,
V. Proizvolov, A. Savin, A. Shvetsov
