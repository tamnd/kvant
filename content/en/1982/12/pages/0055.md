The correspondence physics-technical school (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics-Technical Institute (MIPT) is enrolling eight-year-old and secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1983/84 academic year.

The goal of this school is to help students study physics and mathematics independently. That is why, when admitting to 3FTS, pre-option is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

3FTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom subsequently become students at leading universities in our country.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technical circles, which can be organized locally at the initiative of two subjects - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the names of the circle leader and a list of circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS and leaders of physics and technical circles will receive assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The assignments contain theoretical material and an analysis of the nature of the assignment and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 assignments for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitions, tasks at MIPT). The work of individual students is carried out in 3FTSH or its branches, and the work of members of the circle is carried out by its leader.

Moscow students are given two extra-week classes in physics and mathematics according to the 3FTS program. Classes are held in evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out either based on the results of the introductory task 3FTSH, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics (for information by phone 216-00-05, ext. 2-59).
