Performer 350 years since the founding of the University of Tartu. This is one of the oldest and most famous universities in the Soviet Union.

Substitute mathematicians also worked in Tartu, such as M. Bartels - teacher of N. I. Lobachevsky, T. Clausen, F. G. Minding. The works of academician V. Ya. Struve and his students glorified the University of Tartu as a world astronomical center. The famous physicist E. H. Lenz (Lenz rule, Joule-Lenz law) was a graduate of the university. Professor of the University of Tartu B. S. Jacobi, a physicist and electrical engineer, invented galvanoplasty; he designed the world's first electric motor. Professor B.B. Golitsyn, one of the founders of seismology, worked here. A graduate of the University of Tartu was the famous natural center K. M. Baer (he discovered the law of erosion of river banks). The creator of the “Explanatory Dictionary of the Living Great Russian Language” V. I. Dal studied here. Outstanding surgeons N.I. Pirogov and N.N. Burdenko, the famous historian academician E.V. Tarle worked at the university.

Nowadays, the University of Tartu is a prominent center of education, culture and science of the Soviet Union, a source of personnel for the Estonian Soviet Socialist Republic.
