Such an analysis can be carried out at any research reactor, but at the Institute of Nuclear Physics in Almaty this method has been developed to a good standard. The results obtained by the institute's scientists are very important when searching for deposits of rare metals.

The same institute has a cyclotron, an installation no less expensive than a research reactor.

The cyclotron, as is known, is used to accelerate charged particles, in particular α-particles. By irradiating a light metal, such as beryllium, with alpha particles, beams of neutrons can be obtained. True, the number of neutrons in this case is noticeably less than that obtained from a nuclear reactor, but, using a cyclotron, it is possible to regulate with great accuracy the energy of accelerated α-particles and, accordingly, the energy of “knocked out” neutrons. For a number of studies this advantage is invaluable.
