Panasyuk (he solved the second problem best of all).

The State Secretary of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Federal Republic of Germany, E. Kuchlewein, spoke at the closing of the Olympiad. He noted that the Olympics are not just about excellence; it helps to popularize physics among students. After all, schoolchildren attending the international Olympiad represent national Olympids, which in the early stages cover a huge number of participants. Kuchlewein wished all Olympiad students success in their future scientific work and also said that all members of the German team will receive a stipendio to continue their education.

Then the Deputy Minister of Education of Schleswig-Holstein (where the Olympics took place) J. Schafer spoke. He spoke about the social responsibility of highly gifted people to society and expressed the conviction that the Olympiad has made its contribution to the cause of mutual understanding between peoples. In memory of the northernmost land of the Federal Republic of Germany - the land of Schleswig-Holstein - J. Schafer gave memorial books to all Olympiad students.

The Chairman of the International Commission, Professor V. Kuhn, summed up the results of the Olympiad. Of the 84 students, 14 received the 1st prize, 19 received the 2nd prize, 23 students received the 3rd prize; 10 students who scored more than 50% of the maximum points received certificates of encouragement. Kuhn noted that not only winning the Olympics is important, but also self-learning in it. Those who did not receive awards this time should not be upset. This does not mean, the professor joked, that they cannot become Nobel laureates. It was especially difficult for the teams that competed at the Olympics.
