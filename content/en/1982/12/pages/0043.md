Exercises

1. How will the body move if the force acting on it changes with time as shown in figure 5?

2. Three blocks of equal mass $M = 5$ kg lie on a horizontal table. The bars are connected by threads that rotate under tension $T = 20$ N. The friction coefficients of the bars are equal, respectively, $\mu_1 = 0,3$, $\mu_2 = 0,2$ and $\mu_3 = 0,1$. Bar 3 is tapped with a force $F$, which gradually increases. Which of the threads holding the bars together will twist, and at what minimum force $F$ will this happen? How will the answer change if the force $F$ is applied to block 1?

3. A load suspended on a thread of length $L$ moves uniformly around a circle in a horizontal plane. Find the period of rotation of the load if the thread is deflected from the vertical by an angle $a$.

4. A box of mass $M$ stands on a horizontal table (Fig. 6). The friction coefficient between the table and the box is $μ$. Inside the box lies a body of mass $m$, which can move along the bottom of the box without friction. It is attached to the wall of the box with a spring of stiffness $k$. At what amplitude of body oscillations does the box begin to move on the table?
