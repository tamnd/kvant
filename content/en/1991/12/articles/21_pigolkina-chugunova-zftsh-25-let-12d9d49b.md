ZFTSH - 25 years

You are readers «Kvant». This means you know all the best buses in the country, and among them is Moscow Phystech. But only the very, very, very best are accepted there. And they are internally verified that you can study there, and you really want it, and are ready to make every effort to achieve the desired goal.

Everyone solves this problem in their own way: special class, face, tutor. But if there are no such opportunities (or the special class or tutor is qualitatively deficient), information about ZFTSH will be especially useful for you.

Zoological physical and technical school is a teacher and assistant. A strict teacher and a reliable assistant, if your greatest desire is to enter one of the most representative and best universities in the country, training specialists in the field of electronics, astronautics, laser technology, mathematical modeling, and biophysics.

Let's say right away that ZFTSH is not for every dreaming student. It is for the energetic, gifted, hardworking and purposeful. It is for such a student that ZFTS exists and is always ready to help him. To help in reaching the level of a person-class applicant and, above all, the Physics and Technology Institute, where this school was born and has been operating for 25 years.

A university with in-depth study of mathematics and physics, especially one that operates and teaches according to its own unique system, cannot accept any high school graduate. To study at a university you need a high level of knowledge, skills and abilities. In 3 years of active study at ZFTSH, this level can be achieved. The school deepens and expands the physical and mathematical knowledge of its students, teaches them to work independently and carefully, and prepares them for passing entrance exams and further education in higher education.

The ZFTSH program and methodology have been refined for many years, have proven themselves well and justified themselves. Over the years of ZFTS's operation, 44,000 schoolchildren have passed through it, 7,000 of them have become physicists, and now every second MIPT student is a graduate of ZFTS.

ZFTSH is already a whole story. Next, one might say, physicality. If you are a student, this will become clear to you. It’s not for nothing that one ZFTS graduate said (already as a student) that MIPT is one big ZFTS.

T. Piozolkina,
methodologist-consultant in mathematics ZFTSH,
T. Chudunova, director of ZFTSH
