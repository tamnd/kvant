Quests

1. Akshin returns to the village from the city with shopping for his classmates. He spent exactly 500 rubles and bought exactly 100 items: briefcases, pens and microcalculators. How many fountain pens were purchased if a fountain pen costs 1 ruble, a briefcase costs 10 rubles, and a microcalculator costs 50 rubles?

2. Arrange the numbers in the empty cells of the table so that the sum of any three adjacent numbers is the same, and the sum of all numbers is 200.

3. A neighbor brought a basket of apples for the housewife and her two sons. When the youngest son came home from school, he took $1/3$ apples, returned one apple to the basket for his mother and went to class. Then my eldest son returned from school. Not knowing about the act of taking, he also took $1/3$ of the remaining apples, and put one apple in the basket for his mother and went to practice. When the housewife returned home from work, she was unable to divide the apples in the basket into three equal parts, and there were fewer than ten of them. How many apples were there in the first basket?

4. Cut one of the shapes into four equal parts so that you can fold the second shape out of them.

5. Solve an arithmetic puzzle. Identical letters correspond to identical numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers.

These tasks were suggested to us by 11th grade students from Baku A. Kerimov, N. Antonovich, S. Lyashenko, eighth grader from Moscow S. Kostin and S. Bazhenov.
