Back before Christmas

Just before Christmas, you know, miracles will certainly happen. Either Santa Claus will bring gifts, or the devil will steal the moon.

And even those who don’t know how to guess and find out very interesting things about themselves. Here Svetlana (remember, at Zhukovsky’s?) was throwing a turn-up shoe. And Tatyana (this is already from Pushkin) asked the name of the first person she met. “He looks and answers: “Agathon.”

Fig. 1.

Remember? Who puts the wedding in front of the mirror... Who drips wax... But you never know the ways to find out your future from otherworldly forces?

So it is in the present time: they gather in the evening at the Christmas tree, eliminate the atmosphere of potanisticness - and begin to perform sacred acts. Each lover of fortune telling has his own method. For example, one of my good friends*) prefers to call upon spirits.

This is done very simply. No special equipment is required. It is enough to find four teaspoons and a sheet of paper in the household.

Don’t ask why spoons are teaspoons and a sheet of paper.

Don’t ask why spoons are teaspoons and a sheet of paper.

Don’t ask why spoons are teaspoons and a sheet of paper.

The technology for causing two currents is as follows. You take a spoon in one hand, and in the other a narrow strip of paper folded in half (see Fig. 1). Now you put the handle of the spoon into a paper loop and, holding the paper by the ends folded together, wrap it tightly around the spoon (Fig. 2). Ready? Place the spoon on the table. Now we repeat the same operation with the remaining spoons. We need to arrange them in a cross. Why, I can’t tell you. Perhaps then the Christian spirits are more willing to respond to your call.

Well, the spoons are laid out. May ask: “Nikolai Vasilyevich Gogol, are you here?” (You understand, I use Gogol as an example. But in fact, I would like to know - did he himself see the devil with the moon under his arm or did he believe Solokha’s testimony?) Now we unwind the pieces of paper, holding both ends of the strip with one hand. If the respectable two are at SEL and ready to talk, a miracle happens: at least one of the spoons... comes out of the paper loop, as if you had never threaded it!

Then you can ask about anything. You wrap your spoons again, ask the invisibly present authority a question, unfold the pieces of paper. Did you listen?

the described miracle is “yes”, if it didn’t happen – “no”.

This fortune telling made a strong impression on my neighbor. He doesn’t believe in twos—he’s such a complete materialist. In addition, Leo Tolstoy promised him general's shoulder straps in the near future (and he is not even a major).

But if you don’t rely on otherworldly forces, you have to look for a scientific explanation. My neighbor, after fortune-telling, began to conduct experiments. It turned out that a ballpoint pen, an apartment key, a rusty nail, nothing
