This law on the release of heat in a conductor during the passage of electric current is an example of an independent discovery by scientists, a discovery of “dual citizenship,” a breakthrough in the history of science. Scientists were motivated by the desire to find connections and quantitative relationships between “forces of different natures leading to the release of heat. And although the Joule-Lenz law is not as general as the fundamental law of conservation of energy, the scope of its application has not diminished to this day. You cannot do without it when calculating electrical circuits and electronic circuits, designing and examining lighting and electric heating devices.
