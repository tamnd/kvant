By this condition, the sequence $b_i$, given at $1 \leq i \leq n$, is determined for all goals $i$ - positive and negative (a typical graph of such a sequence $B = (b_i)$ is shown in figure 2). It is convenient to imagine that the axis on which the values ​​of the index $i$ are marked is wound around a circle of length $n$; Moreover, each point $K$ of the circle, $1 \leq k \leq n$, corresponds not to one number $b_k$, but to an entire progression with a zero term $b_k$ and a difference $s$.
