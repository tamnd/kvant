“Great,” he said. “Come with me, Richard.”

He led Dickie to the corner of the room, where a single wooden chair with arms stood opposite the computer. A microphone was mounted on the left armrest, and when the boy sat down in the chair, the microphone was just at the level of his mouth.

“Now relax, Richard.” You will be asked various questions, and you will think carefully about them. Then answer into the microphone. The machine will take care of the rest.

- Okay, sir.

- Now I will leave you alone. When you feel ready to start, say one word into the microphone - ready.

- Listen, sir.

The official squeezed his shoulder and left. Dickie said:
- Ready.

The light in the car came on, followed by the whirring of the mechanism. Then the voice said:
- Complete the proposed series of numbers: one, four, seven, ten.

The Jordan couple sat in the living room, did not say a word: they were rich in making any assumptions.

The phone rang around four o'clock in the afternoon. The woman reached for the pipe, but her husband was quicker.

— Mr. Jordan?

The receiver distorted the voice, the tone of the speaker was harsh, official.

- Yes, I listened.

- They say from the Government Education Service. Your son, Richard M. Jordan, classification number 600-115, has completed his government examination. We are forced to inform you with regret that his intellectual development has exceeded the level established by the government, provided for in Article 84, Section 5 of the New Code...

In the opposite corner of the room came the muffled cry of the woman, who still knew nothing, but had agreed on everything by the expression on her husband’s face.

“You can tell by phone,” the voice continued to boom, “whether you want his body to be buried by the government, or whether you prefer to bury him in a separate grave.” If the government handles the funeral, the cost is dollars.
