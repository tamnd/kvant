1. Find all numbers $x$ and $y$ for which $5x^2 + 2y^2 - 6xy + 10x - 6y + 5 = 0$.

2. From a natural number I will calculate its digits, and then cross out one digit from the resulting difference. Which digit was crossed out if the sum of the remaining digits is 1991?

3. Which fraction is greater:
$$\frac{a+b}{b+d} \text{ или } \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} \right),$$
if $a/b < c/d$ and $b < d$?

4. A circle centered at the midpoint of side $AD$ of convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is tangent to sides $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$. Find the length of side $AD$ if $AB=a$, $CD=b$.

5. Three weights, each weighing $m=1$ kg, are connected through a system of weightless blocks by weightless and weightless threads (Fig. 1). Determine the force exerted by the system on the ceiling. Let's make a claim.

6. An underwater vehicle with mass $m$ has the form of a cylinder with a base area $S$, composed of two hollow halves. The apparatus is in a stable equilibrium position at a depth $H$, equal to the distance from the surface of the water to the center line of the cylinder. Find the force acting on one half of the cylinder from the other half.

7. Find the weight of a body of mass $m$ at the equator. The radius of the Earth at the equator is $R=6350$ km.

8. Two athletes throw a ball. A ball thrown at an angle to the horizontal is in flight $\tau=2$ s. What is the maximum height the ball can reach?

10th grade

1. A student, while solving a quadratic equation, made a mistake when rewriting, rearranging the leading coefficient and the free term of the equation. It turned out that one root he found was the root of the original equation, but the second root, equal to the number 3, was not. Find the roots of the original equation.

2. Find all numbers $x$ and $y$ for which $y^4 + 4y^2x - 11y^2 + 4xy - 8y + 8x^2 - 40x + 52 = 0$.

3. In an acute triangle $ABC$, angle $B$ is equal to $30^\circ$, $H$ is the point of intersection of the altitudes, $A_1$ is the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $HBC$, $C_1$ is the center of the circle inscribed in triangle $HBA$. Find the angle between lines $AA_1$ and $CC_1$.

4. Which fraction is greater:
$$\frac{a_1+a_2+a_3}{b_1+b_2+b_3} \text{ или } \frac{1}{3} \left( \frac{a_1}{b_1} + \frac{a_2}{b_2} + \frac{a_3}{b_3} \right)$$
if $a_1/b_1 < a_2/b_2 < a_3/b_3$ and $b_1 < b_2 < b_3$?

5. In the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the intersection point of the bissertris angles $ABC$ and $BCD$ lies on the side $AD$. Find the length of side $AD$ if it is known that around the quadrilateral $ABCD$ one can describe a circle and $AB=a$, $CD=b$.

6. Two loads, each weighing $m=1$ kg, are connected through a system of weightless blocks by weightless and weightless threads (Fig. 2). Determine the force acting on one half of the cylinder from the other half.

7. Find the charge that will flow through the key after it is closed (Fig. 3). The voltage of the source, the resistance of the resistors and the capacitance of the capacitors are known.

8. A proton with mass $m$ moves in uniform electric and magnetic fields. The direction vectors of the electric field $E$ and magnetic induction $B$ are directed along the axis $Z$. At time $\tau=0$ the proton is at the origin of coordinates, its initial velocity was directed along the $X$ axis. Find the coordinates of the points of intersection of the proton trajectory with the $Z$ axis.

9. A test tube of length $l$ containing air with relative humidity $\varphi=100\%$ is completely immersed in water. At what level is the surface of the water in the test tube? The temperature in the initial and final states is the same. Saturated vapor pressure $p_H < p_{atm}$.
