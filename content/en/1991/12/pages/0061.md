3. (China) Let $S = \{1, 2, 3, \dots, 280\}$. Find the smallest natural number $n$ such that any $n$-element subset of the set $S$ contains 5 pairwise coprime numbers.

4. (USA) Given a connected graph $G$ with $k$ edges. Prove that it is possible to assign edges to all numbers 1, 2, ..., $k$ such that for each vertex of the graph that is connected by edges to at least two other vertices, the set of numbers with which these edges are labeled does not have a common divisor greater than 1.

(The graph $G$ consists of a set of points called its vertices), together with a set of edges connecting some pairs of different vertices. Each pair of distinct vertices $u, v$ belongs to at most one edge. A graph $G$ is called connected if for each pair of vertices $\{x, y\}$ there is a sequence of vertices $x = v_0, v_1, v_2, \dots, v_m = y$ such that every $v_i, v_{i+1}$ ($0 \leq i < m$) is connected by an edge $G$.

5. (France) Let $P$ be the interior point of triangle $ABC$. Prove that at least one of the angles $PAB, PBC, PCA$ is not more than 30°.

6. (Netherlands) A sleepless sequence of real numbers $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$ is said to be bounded if there is a constant $C$ such that $|x_i| \leq C$ for every $i \geq 0$.

Given a real number $a > 1$. Construct a bounded infinite sequence $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$ such that the inequality

$$|x_i - x_j| \cdot |i - j|^a \geq 1$$

performed for each pair of distinct numbers $i$ and $j$.
