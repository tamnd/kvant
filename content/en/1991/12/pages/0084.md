Our regular reader and author I. Akulich sent us the “Pyramid” cerra, which he bought in the store, and a problem related to this cerra. The author of the cerra is V. Dmitriev, and the proposed new design belongs to the editorial artist. Try to crawl into this cell, and then solve I. Akulich’s problem.

Cerra Rules

The game consists of a main board and 12 chips of 2 colors, 6 of each color. Let's play.

The chips are placed on the cells of the first row of the center field. One is on the left, the other is on the right.

They take turns walking. The player has the right to make one move (vertical or horizontal and additional moves).

Vertical stroke. Only the bottom row chip has the right to perform it. At the same time, it moves to one of the two adjacent cells of the next row.

Horizontal stroke. A chip can move along its row to any number of free cells.

Additional promotion. During the process of cerra, three pieces can take a triangle position (two below, one on top). The owner of the top chip after the next move or together with it can advance it to the cells of the next row. The first one to reach the top of the pyramid wins.
