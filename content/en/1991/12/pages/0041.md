Competition "Mathematics 6-8"

The magazine «Kvant» continues a competition to solve mathematical problems for students in grades 6–8. The competition consists of 24 tasks (3 in each room) and takes place in April of next year. Send solutions to the problem from this issue no later than March 1, 1992 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant» (marked “Competition “Mathematics 6-8”). Don't forget to include your last name, first name, school and class.

Quests

10. 50 gangsters shoot at the same time. Everyone shoots at the gangster closest to him (or one of the closest ones, if several people are at the same distance from him) and kills him outright. Find the smallest possible number of killed. (Gangsters are different points on the plane.)

N. Vasiliev

11. Find the smallest value for the side of a straight line if the sides of all the squares on which it is divided are integers.

S. Aftelyuk

12. Replace the letters with numbers so that the match is correct (same letters correspond to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different ones):

CRUNCH ROUGH=
=RRRRRRRRRR

V. Kabire

CRUNCH
BOLT
RRRRRRRRRR
