GRANDMASTERS
AGAINST "DIP SOT"

The success of Deep Cell, a unique American program, has left many chess players despondent. The timing of the duel is almost always discussed, in which there are two equal champions in the martial arts - “Deep Cell” and G. Kasparov. Even the chess king himself does not rule out such a match in the near future.

However, to confirm its ambitions, the car needed to successfully perform in a full-fledged round-robin tournament with the participation of prominent grandmasters. And such an opportunity presented itself: in the spring of this year in Hannover there was a tournament, where seven German grandmasters decided to immediately fight with each other, and at the same time test their electronic colleague from the USA (communication with “Deep Cell” took place, as usual, over a computer communication network). A new version of the Deep Cell program entered the fight against the “great server”, excellent in speed - it scanned 10 million positions per second, compared to 720 thousand in the first version (the developers, by the way, dream of a billion).

Alas, the first attempt failed - in Hannover "Deep Cell" was a fiasco, which greatly upset its fans. The robot scored 2.5 points out of seven and took the subject place. This grandmaster state, thanks to the participation of the computer in it, caused a considerable shareholder in Germany. All publications spoke in detail about the tour and cited the Deep Cell party.

In the first type, a man attacked the car with such energy, as if he wanted to tear it to pieces. Probably, meeting someone like himself, G. Grionberg would not have allowed himself to become nervous. Deep Cell easily figured out the tactical complications and gained the upper hand.
