According to the conditions of the problem
$F \cos \alpha \cdot v \tau = cm \Delta t$.

Since the body moves at a constant speed, the sum of all forces acting on it is zero:
$\vec{F} + m \vec{g} + \vec{N} + \vec{F}_{\text{tp}} = 0$,
or, in projections on the horizontal and vertical coordinate axes:
$F \cos \alpha - F_{\text{tp}} = 0$,
$mg - F \sin \alpha - N = 0$.

The sliding friction force is known to be equal to
$F_{\text{tp}} = \mu N$.

Finally we get
$\alpha = \arctg \left( \frac{g v \tau}{c \Delta t} - \frac{1}{\mu} \right)$.
