Exercise

1. Show that in the ornament (see figure) the area of ​​the figure surrounded in red is equal to the area of ​​the figure surrounded in blue.

2. At the royal modern French fencing competition, the first four places were shared by Atoc, Porthos, Armis and d'Artagnan. The sum of the places occupied by Atoc, Porthos and d'Artagnn is equal to 6, the sum of the places of Porthos and Armis is also equal to 6. What place did each of the musketeers take if Porthos took a higher place than Atoc?

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown at figure. Identical letters correspond to the same numbers, different ones - different numbers. (In Azerbaijani words “BIR” and “DIRD” are used to introduce “one” and “four”.)

4. Kolya paid 12 kopecks for one notebook, two pencils and an eraser. Sasha - 27 kopecks for two notebooks, three pencils and three erasers. How much did Anton pay for two notebooks, five pencils and one eraser?

5. In a quadrilateral ABCD, the sum of the angles ABD and BDC is 180°, and the sides AD and BC are equal (see figure). Prove that the angles at vertices A and C of this quadrilateral are equal.
