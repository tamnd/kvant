About two methods of interaction

It is known that the motion of a body changes when other bodies act on it. The characteristic, or, as they say, measure of interaction is force. It is included in the equation of motion, expressing Newton's second law. The more intense the interaction of a tag, the greater its result - the acceleration of each of them.

But force is not the only measure of interaction. There are other, no less important characteristics. This is the potential energy of interaction. Force and potential energy seem to have little resemblance to one another. One difference between them immediately catches the eye: force is a vector quantity, potential energy is scalar. Nevertheless, between these two quantities, characterizing the same interaction, there should and do exist connections. This is almost what this article is about.
