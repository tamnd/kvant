Mathematics
Written exam

Option 1
1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x}{x+1} \geq 2.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$x^2+2x-9=\sqrt{6+4x+2x^2}.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\cos 2x}{1-\sin 2x}=\text{ctg } 3x.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{x+1}(-6x-2x^2) \geq \log_{x+1}(1-3x).$$
5. In triangle ABC, the area is $30\sqrt{3}$ and the angle is $A=60^\circ$. A circle is drawn with center BC and touching sides AB and AC. The radius of this circle is $\frac{60\sqrt{3}}{23}$. Find the sides of triangle ABC.
6. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ is there a unique pair of numbers $(x; y)$ that satisfies the correspondence
$$ax^2+(3a+2)y^2+4axy-2ax+(4-6a)y+2=0.$$
Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$4x^3\sqrt{x}+3^3\sqrt{x^2}-1=0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x+1}{x^2-2x+2-|x|} \leq 0.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg } 2x-\text{tg } 2x+2 \text{ctg } x=\sin 5x.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{17}(3x^2-21x+18)-2\log_{289}x \leq 1+\frac{1}{\log_217}.$$
5. In triangle ABC, the tangent to the incircle, parallel to BC, intersects AB and AC at points M and N, with MN = 13/14. Find the segments into which side BC is divided by the incircle if BC=13 and $\angle A=120^\circ$.
6. The angle on the plane is formed by the lines $l_1$ and $l_2$, having the equations
$$y=0 \text{ и } y=-x+1.$$ There are balls at points $B_1(0;\frac{1}{2})$ and $B_2(\frac{2}{3};\frac{1}{6})$. When moving away, balls $B_1$, reflected from $l_2$, then from $l_1$ and then again from $l_2$, hit the ball $B_2$. Find the coordinates of the point of the second reflection of the ball from the straight line $l_2$.
Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. A board of mass $M$ can move without friction along an inclined plane forming an angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal. With what acceleration must a dog of mass $m$ travel along the board so that the board does not slide off the inclined plane? What must be the coefficient of friction between the dog's paws and the board for the problem to have a solution?
2. A boat with length $l$ and mass $M$ is standing in calm water. At the bow and stern of the boat there are two fishermen whose masses are $M_1$ and $M_2$. How much will the boat move if the fishermen walk across the boat and change places? Neglect water resistance.
3. The puck, sliding along a horizontal surface with a speed of $v_0=20$ m/s, rises onto a springboard with a height of $h=10$ m, the upper part of which is horizontal, and slides off it into the air (see figure). What is the distance of the puck in the air?
4. The air is in a vertical cylindrical vessel open at the top under a piston with a mass of $m=20$ kg and a cross-section of $S=20$ cm². After the vessels were pushed upward with an acceleration of $a=5$ m/s², the height of the column of influence by the piston and the bottom of the vessel decreased by 20%. Assuming the temperature is constant, find the atmospheric pressure from these data. Neglect the friction between the piston and the walls of the vessel.
5. We mixed $V_1=1$ m³ of air with humidity $\varphi_1=20\%$ and $V_2=2$ m³ of air with humidity $\varphi_2=30\%$, having the same temperature. Determine the moisture content of the resulting mixture if it occupies a volume of $V=3$ m.
6. A proton flying in the direction of the nucleus of the atom of the gel has a speed of $v=2\cdot 10^4$ m/s at a very large distance from it. How far can a proton approach the nucleus? Proton mass $m=1,67\times 10^{-27}$ kg, mass of the atom gel nucleus $M=6,68\cdot 10^{-27}$ kg, proton charge $e=1,6\times 10^{-19}$ Cl.
7. During the electronization of a solution $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4$ with a resistance of $r=0,4$ Ohm, $V=3,3$ liters of hydrogen were released in a time of $t=30$ minutes
