3. Let the original number be of the form $k k$. Then this number is $11 k$, and its square is $121 k^2$. Let the square of the number $k k$ be of the form $a a b b$. Let's write it as follows: $1100 a + 11 b$. So, $121 k^2 = 1100 a + 11 b$. Reducing by 11 we get $11 k^2 = 100 a + b$ or $11 k^2 = 99 a + a + b$. Hence $a + b = 11$, and $k^2 = 9 a + 1$. The expression on the right is a square only for $a = 7$ ($1 \leq a \leq 9$), hence $b = 4$ and $k = 8$.

With the second weighing, we divide 4.4 kg of cool in half and, using a weight, with the third weighing we answer from the resulting 0.2 kg.
