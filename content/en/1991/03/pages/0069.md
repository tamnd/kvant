1. A body of mass $m = 150$ kg moves uniformly along a horizontal plane under the action of a constant force directed at an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ to the horizontal. The coefficient of friction between the body and the plane is $\mu = 0,33$. Determine the work done to move the body over a distance of $l = 71,5$ m.
2. A wooden block with a height of $h = 10$ cm and a base area of ​​$S = 400$ cm² floats in water. The block was lightly immersed in water and released. Find the frequency of vibration of the block. Ignore the friction force. Wood density $\rho = 0,8$ g/cm³.
3. What path will the sled travel on a horizontal surface after descending from a mountain $h = 25$ m high that has an inclination $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizon? The friction coefficient does not change along the entire path and is equal to $\mu = 0,25$.
4. When a rotating part of a machine is destroyed, the fragments can be distinguished at an angle of $\alpha = 60^\circ$ to the horizon with a speed of $v = 3$ m/s. What minimum pavement should be placed as a limit at a distance of $l = 0,5$ m from the car so that the fragments do not fly beyond the limit?
5. Air at a temperature of $t_1 = 20^\circ$ C has a humidity of 100%. In this case, the saturated vapor pressure is $p_{11} = 2330$ Pa. What amount of dew (in grams) will fall from $V = 1$ m³ of air if it is cooled to a temperature of $t_2 = 17^\circ$ C, at which the saturated vapor pressure is $p_{22} = 1962$ Pa? The cooling of water vapor is considered to be Izkhoroch.
6. Determine the density of a mixture consisting of $m_1 = 8$ g hydrogen and $m_2 = 64$ g oxygen, at a temperature of $t = 27^\circ$ C and a pressure of $p = 101$ kPa.
