Selected school problems in physics
1. It is obvious that the model of the balancing force $\vec{F}$ is equal to the difference between the models of forces acting on the rod:
$$F = F_2 - F_1 = 15 \text{ Н}.$$
It is also clear that the point of application of the balancing force $\vec{F}$ lies on the straight line connecting the points of application of forces $\vec{F}_1$ and $\vec{F}_2$, to the right of the larger force (Fig. 1). Let us denote the required distance by $x$. Then we get the right moment
$$F_1(l+x) - F_2x = 0,$$
And
$$x = F_1l/(F_2 - F_1) = 1 \text{ м}.$$
Note that in the case of $F_1 = F_2$, i.e. when a so-called pair of forces acts on the body, there is no balancing force in the general sense of the word. Under the influence of a pair of forces, the body begins to rotate around the center of gravity.
