7. What is the internal resistance of one current source, if when water from such sources is turned on in two parallel groups of four sources, connected in series in each group, the same power is released at the load resistance $R=3$ Ohm as in the case of a series connection of all water elements?

8. The charged balls are at a distance of $r=0,5$ m from each other and repel with a force of $F=3,24$ N. The total charge of the balls is $q=5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ Cl. Find the difference between the larger and smaller charges of the balls.

9. The capacitors are connected according to the circuit indicated on figure 1: $C_1=C_2=2$ µF, $C_3=3$ µF, $C_4=1$ µF. The voltage on the plates of a capacitor with a capacity of $C_4$ is $U=200$ V. Determine the voltage across the entire bank of capacitors.

10. Determine the angle between the reflected and refracted rays if the angle of incidence $\alpha$ of the ray on the boundary of two media with refractive indices $n_1$ and $n_2$ satisfies the condition $tg\alpha=n_2/n_1$.

Option 2

1. Body $A$ with mass $m_1=1$ kg and body $B$ with mass $m_2=4,1$ kg are connected to each other by a spring, as shown in figure 2. Body $A$ performs free harmonic oscillations in the vertical direction with an amplitude $x_m=1,6$ cm and a cyclic frequency $\omega=2,5$ s$^{-1}$. Neglecting the mass of the spring, find the strongest force of these two bodies on the supporting horizontal plane.

2. A stone with a mass of $m=0,2$ kg is thrown horizontally from a base with a height of $h=25$ m at a speed of $v_0=15$ m/s. Find the kinetic energy of the stone $t=1$ s after the start of motion.

3. Two loads, the masses of which are $m_1=1$ kg and $m_2=2$ kg, are connected by a thread thrown over a stationary block. At the initial moment of time, both loads were motionless and the second load was $h=1$ m higher than the first. How long after the start of movement will the weights be at the same height?

4. What would be the period of rotation of the Earth around its axis for bodies at the equator to be in a state of weightlessness?

5. How many molecules are contained in a volume of $V=1$ m$^3$ gas at a temperature of $t=27$ °C and a pressure of $p=10^{-11}$ mm Hg. Art.?

6. One molecule at a temperature of $t=0$ °C is heated at constant pressure. How much heat must be added to the gas for its volume to double? The specific heat of molecules under the specified conditions is equal to $c_p=0,92$ kJ/(K·kg).

7. How many times will the potential difference on the plates of a capacitor with a capacitance of $C_3$ (Fig. 3) increase when testing a capacitor with a capacitance of $C_2$, if $C_1=200$ pF, $C_2=600$ pF, $C_3=800$ pF?

8. A pocket background battery has an emf of $I=4$ V and an internal resistance of $r=2$ ohm. How many of these batteries must be connected in series to power a lamp with a power of $P=60$ W, designed for a voltage of $U=120$ V?

9. Determine the absolute value of the induction emf arising in a straight conductor with a length of $l=0,4$ m, crossing a magnetic field with an induction of $B=1$ T at an angle of $\alpha=60°$ to the lines of magnetic induction with a speed of $v=0,6$ m/s.

10. The cross section of a glass prism has the shape of an equilateral triangle. A ray of light falls from the air onto one of the faces of the prism perpendicular to it. Find the angle between the ray emerging from the prism and the continuation of the ray incident on the prism. The refractive index of glass is $n=1,5$.
