Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1

1. The sum of the second, third and fourth terms of a decreasing arithmetic progression is three times the square of the difference of this progression. The sum of its third and sixth terms is equal to two. Find the sum of the first six terms of this progression.

2. Solve the inequality

$$\log \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{x-2}{2x-3} \right) \geq -1.$$

3. Solve the equation

$$tg x - \sin x = 2 \sin^2 \frac{x}{2}.$$

4. The area of ​​the lateral surface of the right triangular rectangle is equal to the cosine of the angle of inclination of the lateral face to the plane of the base, and the area of ​​the base is equal to the sine of the same angle. Find the volume of the straight line.

5. From the vertices $B$ and $C$ of the triangle $ABC$ to its sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively, perpendiculars are drawn, intersecting at the point $D$. Find the length of side $AC$ if it is known that angle $ABC$ is acute, angle $BAD$ is $\alpha$, and the distance from point $A$ to straight line $BC$ is $a$.

6. Solve the inequality $|66f(x)-90| \leq 24$ if you know that the equation $3f(x)+x=4$ has two roots whose sum is $-\frac{2}{11}$, and the function $f(x)$ is $f(x) = \frac{1}{2^c} \left( |x|^a + |x|^b \right)^c$, where $a, b, c$ are some positive numbers.
