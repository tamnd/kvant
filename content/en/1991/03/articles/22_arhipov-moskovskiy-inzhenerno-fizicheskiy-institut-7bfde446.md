Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2} (1 - \cos x)} = -\sin x + 6 \cos x.$$

2. When dividing the natural three-digit number $A$ by the product of the hundreds and tens digits, the quotient was 25 and the remainder was 4, and when dividing by the product of the tens and ones digits, the quotient was 12 and the remainder was 14. In addition, it is known that the number $(A-6)$ is divisible by 31 without a remainder, and the quotient of such a division is equal to the product of the hundreds and ones digits of the number $A$. Find the number $A$.

3. Find all values ​​of $a \in R$, for each of which the inequality
$$1 - \log \frac{1}{7} (x^2 + 1) \geq \log_7 (ax^2 + 4x + a)$$
holds for any real $x$.

4. The regular triangular paramid $SABC$ is intersected by a plane passing through the center of the ball circumscribed about this paramid, parallel to the edges $SA$ and $BC$. Find the area of ​​the resulting section if it is known that the length of the height $SH$ of the paramides of the ball $h$, and the ratio of the distance from the center of the ball to the plane of the base $ABC$ of the paramides of the ball to the vertex $S$ is equal to $q$.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$(\cos 4x)(\sin x + 2 \cos x)^2 = 5 \cos^3 4x.$

2. Three jobs must produce a certain number of parts. At first, only the first worker worked, and after a while the second joined him. When 1/6 of the work was completed, the third worker began work. They finished their work at the same time. It is known that to complete half of the entire work of the first and second workers, workers working together need 2.5 hours less than one third to complete the entire work. How much time does the first worker work if the first and second workers processed the same number of parts, and the third worked 2 times more of each of them, completing 1 hour less than the second worker?

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{4^x - 6 \cdot 2^x + 1} = 2^x - a, a \in R.$$

4. A triangular paramid $SABC$ is inscribed in a right circular cone with vertex $S$, all of whose lateral faces have the same area. Find the volume of the paramides $SABC$ if it is known that the lengths of the generatrix of the cone are $l$ and $\angle ASB = 2\alpha$.

Physics
Oral examination
In 1990, for the first time, a number of theoretical questions were excluded from the tickets of the city Exam and together with them three relatively simple tasks were proposed. To solve them, you need both the understanding of the fundamentals of the theory and the ability to quickly and correctly use standard methods for solving the problem.

The fourth task of the ticket, as before, was complisory.

The preparation time for the answer remained the same (30-45 minutes).

Ticket 1
1. During isobaric heating of the gas, an amount of heat of $Q=800$ J was transferred to it. At the same time, its internal energy increased by $\Delta U=700$ J. How much did the volume of the gas increase? Gas pressure $p=10^5$ Pa.

2. Determine the work function of a metal electrode if, to stop the photocurrent between the plates of this material, a retarding voltage $U$ must be created. The plates are irradiated with light with wavelength $\lambda$.

3. figure 1 shows a flat contour of thin wires located in a uniform magnetic field, which is directed beyond the plane of the drawing (the jumper of the contour coincides with the diameter of the bulb). The field induction began to decrease. Indicate the direction of induced currents in the circuit.

4. The body is pulled along a horizontal surface with a constant speed $v$ for a time $t$. At the same time, body temperature increases by $\Delta t$. Assuming that all the work of external forces went to increase the internal energy of the body, and using heat transfer, find the angle between the force with which the body is pulled and the horizon. Specific heat of the body $c$, coefficient of friction between the body and the surface $\mu$.

Ticket 2

1. Using the graph of the velocity projection versus time (Fig. 2), construct a graph of the point coordinate versus time. At the initial moment $t=0$ the point coordinate is $x=0$.

2. The $V$, $T$ diagram (Fig. 3) ​​shows the closed process of an ideal gas. Draw this process in coordinates $p$, $V$.

3. figure 4 shows: 1) the area of ​​complete visibility in a flat mirror of some direct object $AB$ (shaded with straight lines); 2) the area of ​​partial visibility of the object in the mirror (shaded with wavy lines). Determine the location of the object $AB$.

4. A liquid air capacitor with capacity $C_0=5 \cdot 10^{-9} \text{ Ф}$ is charged to voltage $U=E=2 \text{ В}$. What work needs to be done in order to expand the plates and increase the distance between them by $n=2$ times? After charging, the capacitor is disconnected from the source.

Ticket 3.

1. A body of mass $m$ lies motionless on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\alpha$ to the horizon. List all the forces acting on the body.

2. Determine the pressure at which $V=1 \text{ m}^3$ of the gas contains $N=2,4 \cdot 10^{26} \text{ молекул. Температура газа } t=60^\circ \text{ C}$.

3. figure 5 shows an image $A'B'$ of some straight continuous object $AB$ in a coincident lens. The image turned out to consist of two semi-infinite parts, one of which is real and the other is minimal. Restore the location of the item $AB$.

Sources with EMF $E_1=4 \text{ В}$ and $E_2=1 \text{ В}$ were connected to points $C$ and $B$, as well as $D$ and $B$ using conductors with low resistance. Determine the potential of point $A$. Ignore the internal resistance of the sources.
