Selected school problems in physics

9th grade

1. The rod is acted upon by two parallel forces equal to $F_1 = 10$ N and $F_2 = 25$ N and directed in opposite directions. Determine the point of application and magnitude of the force balancing the forces $\vec{F}_1$ and $\vec{F}_2$, if their points of application are located at a distance of $l = 1,5$ m from each other.

2. A thread wound on a spool is tied to a nail driven into the wall. The coil hangs touching the wall, as shown in figure 1. The radius of the coil axis is $r = 0,5$ cm, the radius of its cheeks is $R = 10$ cm. The friction coefficient between the wall and the coil is $\mu = 0,1$. At what angle $\alpha$ between the thread and the wall does the coil hang motionless?

3. Balls of mass $m$ each are placed at two vertices of an equilateral triangle. At the third vertex there is a ball of mass $2m$. Find the position of the center of mass of this system.

4. A homogeneous thin plate has the shape of a circle with radius $R$, in which a round hole is cut out with half the radius touching the edge of the plate. Where is its center of gravity?

5. A vessel with water is balanced on a scale. Will balance be maintained if you dip your finger in water without touching the bottom of the vessel?

7. A current source with EMF $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance $r$ is closed to a rheostat. Plot graphs of changes in current, direction, useful power, total power and efficiency when changing the resistance of the rheostat $R$.

8. Two identical batteries with emf $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance $r$ are connected as shown in figure 3. Find the potential difference between points $A$ and $B$. Neglect the resistance of the connected wires.

9. From a source with a voltage of $U=750$ V it is necessary to supply a power of $P=5$ kW to the consumer. What resistance can a transmission line have so that the power loss in it does not exceed 10% of the power that should reach the consumer?

10. The electric motor is powered by a battery with an emf of $\mathcal{E}=12$ V. What mechanical work does the motor do in one second during
flow through its winding current $I_1=2$ A, if when the armature is completely braked in the circuit the current flows $I_2=3$ A?

11th grade

11. What percentage of the speed of light must be the speed of the proton moving in the accelerator in order for the increase in its mass to be 25%?

12. When aluminum nuclei $^{27}$Al interact with $x$-particles, magnesium isotope nuclei $^{27}$Mg and $y$-particles are formed. When the $y$-part interacts with aluminum nuclei $^{27}$Al, they form magnesium isotope nuclei $^{24}$Mg and $z$-particles. What widely known $x, y, z$ particles are involved in these nuclear reactions?

13. When a π-meson decays into two phonases, it is recorded that these phonans fly apart at angles $\alpha=30^\circ$ and $\beta=60^\circ$ to the direction of motion of the meson. At what speed did mezоn move before decay?

14. A proton, traveling at a speed of $v_0=7,5 \cdot 10^4$ m/s, collides with the rest of an uncombined hydrogen atom. After the collision, the proton flies in the same direction, but with a speed of $v_1=1,5 \cdot 10^4$ m/s; atom goes into a state with higher internal energy. What is the length of the phonan that an atom can emit when returning to a newly excited state?

15. What energy is released during a thermonuclear reaction?
