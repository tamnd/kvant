Novosibirsk State University named after. Lenin Komsomol

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics, Mathematics and Economics)
1. Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} 2x \cdot 7^y = 112, \\ 2x - 7y = 1. \end{cases}$$
2. Find the smallest positive root of the equation
$$6 \sin^2 x = 5 - 13 \cos x + 3 \cos 2x$$
3. In a triangle $ABC$ with sides $AB=14$, $AC=15$, $BC=13$, through the base of height $CH$, lines are drawn parallel to lines $AC$ and $BC$, which intersect the sides $BC$ and $AC$ of the triangle at points $M$ and $N$. Line $MN$ intersects the extension of side $AB$ at point $D$. Find the length of segment $BD$.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x - 1}{(x + 1) \log_3 (x^2 + x + 1/2)} \geq 0.$$
5. The edge of the cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$ is equal to $2a$. The segment $PQ$, the ends of which lie on the lines $AB'$ and $BC'$, intersects the line $CD'$ at the point $O$ and is bisected by this point. Find the length of segment $PQ$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Solve the equation
$$1 - 8 \cos^2 x = 9 \sin 2x + 3 \cos 2x$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$|x^2 + 2x - 24| > 6x + 8$$
3. Around an equilateral triangle $ABC$ ($AB=BC$) with an angle $B$ equal to $30^\circ$, a circle $S$ is circumscribed with a radius $R$, $AD$ is its diameter drawn from the point $A$. Find the radius of the circle tangent to the side $AC$ of the given triangle, the diameter $AD$ and the circle $S$.
4. On the coordinate plane, a parabola $y = x^2/2 + x + 13/2$ and a tangent to it parallel to the line $y = 6x$ are given.

Find the area of ​​a triangle whose vertices are: the point of tangency, the point of intersection of the tangent with the x-axis, and the vertex of the given parabola.
5. In a regular quadrangular straight line $SABCD$, through the vertex $A$ and the middle of the side edge $SC$, a section is drawn to a plane parallel to the diagonal $BD$ of the base $ABCD$. It is known that the sectional area is 9, and the sectional plane forms an angle $60^\circ$ with the plane of the base. Find the volume of this straight line.

Option 3
(Faculties of Natural Sciences and Geology-Geophysical)
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_2 x + \log_2 x = \log_8 x + 16$$
2. Find the smallest positive root of the equation
$$2 \cos^2 x = 1 - 7 \sin x - 2 \cos 2x$$
3. The line $l_1$ touches the circles $O_1$ and $O_2$ externally (i.e., the circles lie on the same side of the line $l_1$), the distance between the points of tangency is 24. The line $l_2$ is an internal common tangent (i.e., the circles lie on opposite sides of the line $l_2$), the distance between the points of tangency is in this case it is equal to 7. The smallest distance between circles $O_1$ and $O_2$ is 1. Find the radius circles.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{3x + 22}}{x + 4} < 1$$
5. At the base of the triangular straight line $ABC'A'B'C'$ lies a regular triangle $ABC$ with side $\sqrt{3}$, the side edges have length 4. Let $K$ be the intersection point of the diagonal $BC'$ and $B'C$ side face $BB'C'C$. A sphere whose center belongs to a line tangent to faces $ABC$, $AA'B'B$, $AA'C'C$ and line $A'K$. Find the radius of the sphere.

The fourth task is an assessment task. To solve it, you need to understand the physical phenomenon under consideration, form a simple (since only an estimate is needed) physical model of this phenomenon, select dimensional numerical values ​​of physical quantities and, finally, obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. In the text of the problem I assume that the applicant can himself choose the quantities necessary to solve the problem and their numerical values.

The fifth task is a demonstration task, in which you need to explain a physical phenomenon demonstrated in the classroom. Here it is important to understand the essential phenomena and highlight the main one among the various factors.

Five hours were given to solve the problems, starting with the completion of the demonstration.

After the text of each problem, the average percentage of those who solved it is indicated in parentheses.

Option 1
1. Two identical uniformly charged thin flasks are first positioned so that they are made at right angles at point $O$ (Fig. 1). Then the flasks are combined. How many times will the direction of the electric field change at point $O$, which is generally located at a radius distance from the centers of the rings? (68%)
2. The plane moves along the response wall. At what angle to the direction of the plane's movement will the pilot catch the echo reflected from this wall? The speed of sound is $v$, the speed of the aircraft is $u$. (78%).
3. The barrel of the spring gun is directed at an angle of $45^\circ$ to the smooth floor. The mass of the projectile is equal to the mass of the gun. Determine the ratio between the heights of projectile rise when fired from an unattached and fixed cannon. (32%)
4. Estimate what the maximum weight will be indicated by the spring scales when weighing a body weighing one kilogram in a Kkaley ship moving in a near-Earth circular orbit. (13%)
5. The laser beam is aimed at the end of an Ortec glass block, while behind the block a bright spot of light is observed on the screen (Fig. 2). After the block is touched in some place with a wet cloth, the spot of light on the screen disappears. Applying with a dry cloth does not have this effect. Explain the phenomenon. (64%)

3. If a ball falls from a height $h$ onto a hard floor, then it bounces to a height $h/3$. Two such balls are hung side by side on threads of length $l$. The thread with one ball deflects at an angle $90^\circ$ from the vertical, and the ball is released. At what angles will the threads deflect after the balls collide? (28%)
4. A children's balloon is inflated with hydrogen and hangs freely in the air.
