To do this, you need fluoroplastic or lavsan film with a thickness of 10-30 microns. These films are now widely distributed. Fluoroplastic films are used in the manufacture of high-capacitors, where they serve as an insulating gasket together with waxed paper. And lavsan film is used in everyday life for wrapping flowers or boxes of chocolates. You also need household aluminum foil. If you don't have one on hand, you can use a chocolate wrapper.

You need two more clean, dry woolen slippers, a small piece of sandpaper and two thin wires.

The electret is easy to make. Cut out a rectangular piece of film of the same size, say, 10x5 cm. Spread the film on a piece of fabric. And on top, rub the film with a second piece of cloth for three or four minutes, preferably in one direction. You don't need to press hard. Under the influence of a static charge in captivity, complex polarization processes and the formation of an electret will occur.

Make straight electrodes from foil. The width of the rectangle should be slightly less than the width of the foil, for example 4 cm, and the length - 11 cm. Place the foil on the sandpaper and use the edge of your palm to rub it across the surface of the foil with little force so that the sandpaper makes the foil rough. The same must be done with the second foil.

The electret is placed on the foil so that its edges extend beyond the edges of the foil on three sides (Fig. 5). A second foil is placed on the electret. The result was a three-layer scribble tape, the long edges of which must be secured with adhesive tape or adhesive tape. The ribbon electret receiver is ready. Wires must be attached to the protruding electrode edges. In the simplest version, this can be done using common arrows.

Connect this extremely simple system to the radio network and press it to your ear, you will hear the clear sound of a melody or the voice of an announcer.

If you carefully make two receivers, securing a three-layer tape within or between two thin plates of dielectric, for example plexiglass, in which you carefully drill small holes, like a telephone handset, and fasten them with a fungus arm, you get headphones with which you can listen, without disturbing your loved ones, to a radio network broadcast, a television program or a tape recorder.
