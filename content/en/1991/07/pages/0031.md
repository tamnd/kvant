ABC is true equality
$pqr = uvw.$

From equalities (*) and $\Pi = pqruvw$ it follows that
$\Pi = (pqr + uvw)^2/4,$
and hence the required formula for $S$.

To prove formula (*), you can also use areas. The ratio of the areas of triangles AXB and CXB with a common base XB is equal to the ratio of their heights, i.e. $w/r$ (Fig. 2). Variable of three such relations, we get

$$\frac{p}{u} \cdot \frac{q}{v} \cdot \frac{r}{w} = \frac{S_{BXC}}{S_{CXA}} \cdot \frac{S_{CXA}}{S_{AXB}} \cdot \frac{S_{AXB}}{S_{BXC}} = 1.$$

N. Vasiliev, V. Prasolov
