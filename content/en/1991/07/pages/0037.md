Quests

1. At the last European Football Championship, Van Basten scored half as many goals as Gullit. Mikhailichenko scored one goal less than Van Basten. Fehler scored three more goals than Mikhailichenko. Schilacci scored three more goals than Föhler. It is known that two of these five players scored the same number of goals and no one scored more goals than Gullit. How many goals did each of them score?

2. Finish filling the square with the letters CH, U, K, E, G so that all these letters are present once in each horizontal row, in each vertical row and on each diagonal.

3. The hotel administrator works either from 8 am to 8 pm, or from 8 pm to 8 am, or 24 hours from 8 am (morning or evening). In the first case, it is no less than a day, in the second - no less than a day and a half days. What is the smallest number of administrators that should work in a hotel?

4. In the rectangle ABCD (see figure), point M is the midpoint of side BC, point N is the midpoint of side CD, P is the intersection point of segments DM and BN. Prove that angle MAN is equal to angle BPN.

5. 40 schoolchildren are engaged in the “Skillful Hands” circle, each of them has screws, bolts and nails in their pockets. Exactly 10 of them have the number of nails equal to the number of screws, and_ROV but for 15 schoolchildren the number of nails is not equal to the number of bolts. Prove that at least 15 Krupkovites have the same number of cogs as the number of bolts.

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Sarsembayev, L. Nikonov, I. Sergeev, V. Proizvol and K. Kokhas.
