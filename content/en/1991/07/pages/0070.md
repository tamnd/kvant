Numerical test

We publish a numerical test on numerous prime numerators. We took it from the book of English psychology G. Eysenck “Test Your Abilities” (M.; Mir, 1972). We carried out one of his tests in our magazine in the ninth issue for 1989. The numerical test allows you to calculate your IQ according to the following rule: if 7 tasks are completed correctly, KN = 90, then for every 4 tasks you receive another 10 points. You have 30 minutes to solve the test.
Don’t shoot if you only solved the changing part of the problem correctly: 100 points is a normal result, 125 is good, and more than 130 is great!
We wish you good luck!
