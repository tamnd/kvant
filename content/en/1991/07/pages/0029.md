F1299. A black ball with a radius of $r=1$ mm is suspended on a thin thread $l=1$ m long. The entire system is placed in an evacuated glass tube with argon at a pressure of $p_0=0,1$ Pa. The ball is illuminated with a horizontal beam of light, the energy flux density of which is equal to $w_0=100$ J/$\mathrm{m}^2$ s. Estimate the deviation of the ball from its equilibrium position under the influence of light. Neglect the thermal conductivity of the ball. Consider that an absolute black body, heated to an absolute temperature of $T$, emits energy equal to $\sigma T^4$ per unit surface area per unit time, where $\sigma=5,67 \cdot 10^{-8}$ W/$\mathrm{m}^2$·K$^4$. The gas temperature in the tube is constant and equal to $T_0=293$ K. The molar mass of argon is $M=0,04$ kg/mol, the density of the ball is $\rho=1$ g/cm$^3$.

A. Semenov

F1300. It is known that when an uncharged conducting sphere is introduced into a uniform electric field of intense $E_0$, the field strength near the point $A$ (see figure) turns out to be equal to $3E_0$, and near the point $B$ - zero. Determine the field strength near point $B$. Find the total charge induced on the hemisphere $BAB$. The radius of the sphere is $R$.

V. Mozhaev

F1301. Three identical windings are symmetrically located on the torid core, forming a transformer. One of the windings was connected to an alternating voltage source, the second was left open, and a voltmeter was connected to the third. It turned out that the voltmeter in this case shows the source voltage. What will it show if the second winding is short-circuited? Consider the resistance of the windings to be negligible, the voltmeter and the source to be ideal, and the magnetic permeability of the core to be unchanged at different values ​​of magnetic induction.

A. Andrianov

F1302. A body of mass $M$ leaning against the wall is subjected to a force perpendicular to the wall that varies according to the harmonic law $F=F_0\cos \omega t$. A weightless spring is placed between the body and the wall. What should its rigidity be for the amplitude of the force acting on the wall to differ from $F_0$ by a factor of 3?

I. Vorobiev
