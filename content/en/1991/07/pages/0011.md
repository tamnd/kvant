ABOUT THE WATER BEAST AND
ACOUSTIC RESONANCE

Candidate of Technical Sciences
R. VINOKUR

- This happened in the 5th century AD on the shores of a deep lake in the north of England. The wandering monk Father Benedict came here to convert local residents to Christianity. However, it turned out that they already had their own god - a powerful inhabitant of the lake. The monk was taken to a low cliff that dropped roundly into the water. Hearing the voices of the boats, a huge shiny black fish emerged from the abyss, the head of which was shaped like a seal and was decorated with a white horn. The natives looked very frightened, although they assured that the aquatic god does not eat meat, but eats only plant matter. The beast silently looked at the boat, its head almost reaching the edge of the cliff. Local residents fell on their faces. Then the monk raised the cross and with his name commanded the miracle to go back to the underworld, but this had no effect. At that time, guides possessed not only words, but also weapons. Having picked up a spear from the ground, dropped by one of the local residents, Father Benedict accurately threw it and hit the beast in the eye. The monster groaned, moved back and disappeared forever into the depths of the lake...

The narrator, a young man named John Styron, paused and, looking at Sherlock Holmes, added: “That’s the legend.” However, many people believe that a similar animal lives in our lake, and some say that they have seen it.
