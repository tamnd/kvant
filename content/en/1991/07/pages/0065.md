probty. It wasn’t even commands that suddenly appeared in my thoughts, but rather memories of commands. We were forbidden to leave the apartment: apparently, those who were in charge of us understood the undesirability of such actions, although the concept itself probably seemed completely abstract to them. We were forbidden to come into contact with others of our own kind. At least for a while, we will be allowed to eat and drink tap water.

When the temperature subsided, the transformation took place quickly and decisively. Almost simultaneously, Gail and I were made to freeze. She was sitting at the table at that moment, and I knelt down and barely saw her out of the corner of my eye.

Ridges were already starting to form on Gail's arm.

They had learned a lot while living inside Virgil, and now they were using completely different tactics. For about two hours my whole body was unbearably human and itched—for two hours, but then they finally broke through to my brain and found me. Centuries-old attempts in their closet were crowned with success, and now they had the opportunity to promise with the clumsy, slow mind that once owned their universe.

They weren't cruel at all. When the concept of the inconvenience caused by their actions and its undesirability became clear to this little creature, they immediately set to work to eliminate the unpleasant errors. And regret it, we got over it. For another hour I remained in a state of absolute bliss, without any possibility of contact.

The next morning we were allowed to move again. Mainly to satisfy physiological needs: they could not get rid of some products of childhood life themselves. I obeyed - the motza turned out to be purple, and Gale followed my example. We looked at each other with empty eyes for a long time, then she forced a smile.

-Are they talking to you too? - Gail asked.

I nodded.
- So I'm not crazy.

In the next few hours, control relaxed, and I managed to draft a significant part of this manuscript. I suspect that at this time another war was going on in my body. Gail could move a little, but nothing more.

When they regained full control, we were ordered to embrace, and we obeyed without hesitation.

“Eddie...” said Gail, and mine was the last sound that reached me from outside.

In this position, standing, we grew together. After a few hours, our warmers became a massive support, which spread across the floor in all directions at once. Individual shoots crawled towards the window, towards the sunlight, and into the kitchen - towards the source of drinking water. Soon filaments were added to all ends of the room, containing paint and plaster from the walls, then upholstery and filler from upholstered furniture.

By the next morning the transformation was complete.

Now I don’t see very well, and it’s hard for me to judge what we’re doing. Apparently, into two huge flat cells, with shoots of films spreading out in all directions and spreading throughout the apartment. The great imitates the small.

I was instructed to continue to write down my impressions, but this would soon be impossible. Day by day, as we are absorbed by the thinkers within, both our minds become unstable. Every day our personality characteristics are lost. We really are huge, lumbering dinosaurs. Now our memories are stored in billions of small creatures, found individuals dispersed throughout the volume of transformed blood.

Soon the need for centralization will disappear completely.

I was informed that the water supply and sewerage is under their control. Many people on other floors of the building have already undergone transformation.

In a few weeks, according to the old time scale, we will reach lakes, rivers and seas in huge numbers.

I can't even guess what the consequences will be. Every square inch of the surface of the plans will seethe with intelligence. And years later - perhaps even later - all the boats will merge, discarding the personal.

New beings will appear, and their future thinking abilities are simply impossible to imagine.

The hatred and fear have now completely left me. I—we—are concerned now with only one question.

How many times has something like this been heard somewhere else? The Earth has never been visited by aliens from outer space. And why would they do this?

After all, in every grain of sand you can find velvet.
