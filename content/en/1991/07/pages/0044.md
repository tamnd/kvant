about teacher
Volkov

Once “Pionerskaya Pravda” asked a reader to name his favorite fairy tale. Most of the guys answered: “The Wizard of the Emerald City.”

This fairy tale, as well as its sequels - “Oorfene Deuce and His Wooden Soldiers”, “Seven Underground Queens”, “Fiery God of the Marans” and others - is loved not only by children, but also by adults: fathers and mothers, grandparents. However, not everyone remembers who wrote them.

The author of these significant books, Alexander Melentevich Volkov, was born 100 years ago, on June 15, 1891. For many years he was a teacher in the then small town of Ust-Kamenogorsk (Teler is the center of the Eastern Kazakhstan region). I had to teach almost all subjects - Russian language and literature, natural sciences and geography, history, mathematics and physics.

In his “bear corner,” teacher Volkov was most afraid of falling behind the latest achievements of science. Revolutions took place, wars raged, life was hard and hungry. Teacher Volkov, like everyone else, had to endure hardships, but in any circumstances he continued to study - he studied himself in order to have the right to teach others.

In 1931, forty-year-old Volkov, the father of two children, passed all the exams “excellently” for a five-year course at the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics of Moscow University and became an assistant professor in the department of higher mathematics at the capital’s Institute of Non-Ferrous Metals and Gold.

He created interesting mathematical works and, probably, could have been both a professor and an academician. But Alexander Melentevich became interested in writing books for children. Having written them, he once again felt like a teacher, only now his lessons were held in a huge class, bringing together thousands and thousands of students - the reader. Among his works

The book “The Great Count” (essays on the history of mathematics) was not published and was preserved only in manuscript. We are publishing a chapter from it, dedicated to the first arithmetic textbook in Rus'.

A. Rozanov
