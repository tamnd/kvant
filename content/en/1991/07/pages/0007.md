Electrets in technology

Previously it was believed that water (or other liquid) completely screens the electret field. It was found, however, that the field is completely shielded by water only in the case when the liquid layer is not moving. When the liquid moves along the surface of the electret, the field is not completely screened, the liquid is polarized, and charges are separated. It is important that the layer of flowing liquid is within the TP-salt electret. And if the liquid is sprayed, then shielded droplets of liquid are formed. The electret field converts the energy of a liquid jet into the energy of charged drops, just as in a microphone the vibration energy of a membrane is converted into an electrical signal.

The electret injector (Fig. 3) was created on this principle. Without external power sources, water, sprayed by a nozzle, produces a torch with a large number of charged droplets. The photo shows an electret on the inner surface of the nozzle.

If you make a nozzle without an electret, then the torch will contain 3-5% charged droplets. Electret nozzle gives 10-15 times more. Charged water droplets energetically capture dust from the air. Therefore, electret nozzles are used in coal mines where there is a lot of dust. Irrigation of dusty air near coal combines suppresses coal dust and prevents it from spreading throughout the entire fence. At the same time, the working conditions of miners are significantly improved. In addition, by suppressing coal dust, they reduce the risk of its explosion.

If you change the sign of the electret charge on the nozzle, you can make it work in reverse mode, i.e., extinguish the charges of droplets of sprayed liquid. When spraying, for example, gasoline or other explosive fuel, it is possible in this way to prevent spontaneous combustion or explosion of fuel from static electricity.

Small closed volumes of air can be completely cleared of dust particles using electret films with a discrete surface charge, which are glued to the walls of the volume. If you roll the electret into a roll with a gap equal to the TP-sleeve and equip it with a miniature fan for air circulation, it will be able to provide significantly larger volumes.
