A flow of elastic balls is directed from below onto the cube, with a speed of $v_0 = 20$ m/s at the height of the first initial position of the base of the cube. The cube begins to oscillate, moving progressively. Find the period of these oscillations. Estimate the time during which the vibration amplitude will decrease by a factor of 2.

$$P = 2m(v_0 - v).$$
