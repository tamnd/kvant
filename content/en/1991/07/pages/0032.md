F1278. A projectile flying vertically exploded at maximum height. Projectile fragments fall to the ground during a period of time t. Find the speed of the fragments at the moment of explosion.

The speed of a projectile launched upward at the top point is zero. We will assume that the sedges fly in all directions with the same speed v. Then the maximum difference in the falling time - it corresponds to the time t of the sedges falling onto the ground - will be for the sedges flying vertically. A fragment flying upward after an explosion with a speed v, after a time 2v/g ends up at the point of explosion, and its speed will be equal to v and directed downward. This means that the time difference is exactly equal to 2v/g=t, whence
$$v = gt/2.$$
Note that if the speeds of the fragments are different, then nothing definite can be said.
