Let us note the following factor: the sum of the length of the large base and one of the lateral sides of the trapezoid is greater than the sum of the length of the smaller base and the second lateral side. Let's prove this factor. Let us denote the lengths of the bases by a and b ($a>b$), and the lengths of the sides by c and d ($c\geq d$). Let us draw segment BK parallel to side CD (Fig. 2), then in three
