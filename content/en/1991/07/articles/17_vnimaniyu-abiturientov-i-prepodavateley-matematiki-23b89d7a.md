⟦folio 58⟧

Attention
applicants
and mathematics teachers!

The collection “514 PROBLEMS WITH PARAMETERS” opens the series “MATHEMATICAL KLONDIKE”

The 160-page book contains 514 problems in elementary mathematics. The tasks are distributed across 9 chapters and 5 difficulty groups. Answers, detailed instructions or solutions are provided for all problems.
This is the first time a special collection of problems with parameters of this volume has been published in our country.
It is intended for applicants preparing for entrance examinations to higher education.
