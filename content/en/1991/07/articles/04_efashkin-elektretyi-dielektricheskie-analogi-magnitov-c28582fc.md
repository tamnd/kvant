About bioelectrets

At the beginning of the article, we said that electrets do not occur in nature. However, this is not entirely true. It turns out that a living human or animal organism uses the electretium effect. Research by scientists has shown that bioelectrets exist. The inner walls of blood vessels carry an associated negative charge. The potential created by bioelectrity is called desatopotential by doctors. Blood elements - red blood cells, platelets, leukocytes - are also negatively charged. Perhaps this explains the fact that blood, despite its enormous molecular weight, easily passes through the thinnest capillaries.

As soon as the disatoppotential disappears (due to illness, damage to a vessel), the blood clots and a blood clot forms. This is how the body's defense works. (The soluble filrinogen in the blood becomes insoluble, forms many threads in which the blood form elements become entangled, the wound heals, and a plug is formed that prevents further blood from flowing out of the vessel.)

On the other hand, the formation of blood clots inside the vessels often leads to a serious disease - thrombophlebitis, the vein becomes clogged with a blood clot, the vessels swell and swollen, clearly diseased veins are visible under the skin.

Artificial vessels—prosthetic blood vessels—have been made for a long time (Fig. 1). They are woven like stockings and made corrugated. In the case of serbez disease, the affected vessels are replaced with prostheses.

But since the artificial vessel is a foreign body and does not carry disatoppotential, blood clots form on its walls.

It turns out that with the help of electrets it is possible to promote disatoppotential. At the Institute of Transplantology of Artificial Organs and Tissues, a series of experiments was carried out in the body (as doctors say, in vitro), and then on experimental dogs (in vivo).

Instead of a vessel, in one case there was a tube sewn in, on the inner side of which the desatopotential was modeled with a negative electretin charge, in the second case a tube without a charge, in the third case with a positive charge. A tube (artificial vessel) with a positive charge became completely clogged with a blood clot after three days; without a charge, it did not clog for ten days. In a tube with a simulated disatoppotential, a thrombus did not form.

Electrets in technology

Previously it was believed that water (or other liquid) completely screens the electret field. It was found, however, that the field is completely shielded by water only in the case when the liquid layer is not moving. When the liquid moves along the surface of the electret, the field is not completely screened, the liquid is polarized, and charges are separated. It is important that the layer of flowing liquid is within the TP-salt electret. And if the liquid is sprayed, then shielded droplets of liquid are formed. The electret field converts the energy of a liquid jet into the energy of charged drops, just as in a microphone the vibration energy of a membrane is converted into an electrical signal.

The electret injector (Fig. 3) was created on this principle. Without external power sources, water, sprayed by a nozzle, produces a torch with a large number of charged droplets. The photo shows an electret on the inner surface of the nozzle.

If you make a nozzle without an electret, then the torch will contain 3-5% charged droplets. Electret nozzle gives 10-15 times more. Charged water droplets energetically capture dust from the air. Therefore, electret nozzles are used in coal mines where there is a lot of dust. Irrigation of dusty air near coal combines suppresses coal dust and prevents it from spreading throughout the entire fence. At the same time, the working conditions of miners are significantly improved. In addition, by suppressing coal dust, they reduce the risk of its explosion.

If you change the sign of the electret charge on the nozzle, you can make it work in reverse mode, i.e., extinguish the charges of droplets of sprayed liquid. When spraying, for example, gasoline or other explosive fuel, it is possible in this way to prevent spontaneous combustion or explosion of fuel from static electricity.

Small closed volumes of air can be completely cleared of dust particles using electret films with a discrete surface charge, which are glued to the walls of the volume. If you roll the electret into a roll with a gap equal to the TP-sleeve and equip it with a miniature fan for air circulation, it will be able to provide significantly larger volumes. The problem of obtaining a super-dust atmosphere is important for industrial micro-forest production, where one speck of dust a fraction of a micron in size can damage a very large or large integrated circuit (VIC or VIC). Therefore, huge amounts of money are spent on creating clean rooms. The air is filtered through a multi-stage air purification system, which occupies entire floors (the so-called technology floors).

And the requirements for cleanliness are increasing all the time. If previously it was possible to work at classistin 1000 (1000 dust particles per cubic foot, or 30 dust particles per liter) (compare, in rural areas, where we say: “Oh, what clean air!”, There are about 100,000 dust particles per liter), then now the number of dust particles should not exceed 3 per liter (class 100) or even 0.3 (class 10). And dust particles should not be larger than 0.3 microns.

Can a multi-stage air purification system cope with such a task? In principle, yes, but for a very short period of time. The fact is that the purified air quickly becomes dusty again, since in the workshop there are working mechanisms, and most importantly, people who “generate” dust (a person “generates” one and a half million dust particles per hour). Employees are passed through vestibules where dust particles are blown off, dressed in the cleanest clothes made from special fabrics, and sometimes even in spacesuits, like astronauts, but this does not solve the problem.

What is the solution? One option is to capture dust at the places where it is generated using local electret dust collectors and resistors. Such influence, purified in technological floors, will pollute the hot water more slowly.

A clean atmosphere isn't just needed in the electronics industry. Radioactive dust at coal mines and nuclear power plants is very harmful. Absolutely clean air is needed during surgical operations, in optical laboratories, and on spaceships. Electret local dust collectors without external power sources will help solve these important problems.

Electret injectors have many other uses. By spraying agricultural crops with charged water, they increase their productivity and reduce the consumption of dangerous pesticides. By atomizing fuel in engines, they reduce fuel consumption, increase engine power, and reduce the toxicity of exhaust gases...

The reader probably has a question: why are magnets known to everyone, but electrets are talked about only in a narrow circle of specialists? I think that, firstly, electrets have been studied relatively recently, and therefore even in the specialized literature there are relatively few publications about electrets; secondly, in the school textbook and even in textbooks for colleges, practically nothing is said about electrets.

And the prospects for electrets are enormous.

by friction. From tribozlektret you can make a simple earphone, which, without any converter and in time, will reproduce a local radio program.

To do this, you need fluoroplastic or lavsan film with a thickness of 10-30 microns. These films are now widely distributed. Fluoroplastic films are used in the manufacture of high-capacitors, where they serve as an insulating gasket together with waxed paper. And lavsan film is used in everyday life for wrapping flowers or boxes of chocolates. You also need household aluminum foil. If you don't have one on hand, you can use a chocolate wrapper.

You need two more clean, dry woolen slippers, a small piece of sandpaper and two thin wires.

The electret is easy to make. Cut out a rectangular piece of film of the same size, say, 10x5 cm. Spread the film on a piece of fabric. And on top, rub the film with a second piece of cloth for three or four minutes, preferably in one direction. You don't need to press hard. Under the influence of a static charge in captivity, complex polarization processes and the formation of an electret will occur.

Make straight electrodes from foil. The width of the rectangle should be slightly less than the width of the foil, for example 4 cm, and the length - 11 cm. Place the foil on the sandpaper and use the edge of your palm to rub it across the surface of the foil with little force so that the sandpaper makes the foil rough. The same must be done with the second foil.

The electret is placed on the foil so that its edges extend beyond the edges of the foil on three sides (Fig. 5). A second foil is placed on the electret. The result was a three-layer scribble tape, the long edges of which must be secured with adhesive tape or adhesive tape. The ribbon electret receiver is ready. Wires must be attached to the protruding electrode edges. In the simplest version, this can be done using common arrows.

Connect this extremely simple system to the radio network and press it to your ear, you will hear the clear sound of a melody or the voice of an announcer.

If you carefully make two receivers, securing a three-layer tape within or between two thin plates of dielectric, for example plexiglass, in which you carefully drill small holes, like a telephone handset, and fasten them with a fungus arm, you get headphones with which you can listen, without disturbing your loved ones, to a radio network broadcast, a television program or a tape recorder.
