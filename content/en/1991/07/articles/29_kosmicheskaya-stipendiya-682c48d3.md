Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk State University there is a Correspondence School (3Sh) for students in grades 9-11 of secondary schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

There are 5 departments in 3Sh: mathematical physical, chemical, biological and economic. The mathematical physics and chemistry departments include students in grades 9-11, the biological department includes only students in grades 10, and the economic department includes only students in grades 11.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also include mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives that work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. The head of the circle recruits and enrolls students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the relevant subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader informs 3SH of his last name, first name, patronymic and sends to the premises a list of members of the circle (indicating the final grades for the first assignment), signed by the school director and certified by a seal. After this, the members of the circle are counted by students 3Ш.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional materials. The work of the undergraduate students is checked in 3SH, and the work of the circle members will be checked by the leader (at the request of the leader, some of the work of the circle members can be checked in 3SH).

Every year, some students in grades 9-10 of 3Sh are invited to the Summer School at NSU. Here they, together with the winners of the Vesibirsk Olympiad, listen to lectures by prominent scientists, solve interesting problems at seminars, get acquainted with the university and research institutes of Academgorodok, and have fun separately. During the winter holidays, 3Sh students from nearby regions are invited to the Winter School at NSU.

To become a student at the Correspondence School at NSU, you must send an application written on a postcard to the director of 3Sh by September 30 with a request to send the first assignment. The application must be completed in the following format:

Last name, first name, patronymic (in full, stamped letters)
The class in which you are a teacher in your school
Department 3Sh where you want to study (you can specify two departments)
Detailed home address with mandatory indication of post office code

The head of the circle must send a letter to the director of 3Sh with a request to send the first assignment and additional materials for it.
An application for admission to the mathematics or physics department of 3Sh can be sent along with the solutions to the corresponding first assignment, published below, no later than October 15.

Write down the solutions to the problems in a simple student notebook in a square, leaving the fields for the teacher to replace. On the cover they bore the same terrifying information about themselves as in the application. Send the work along with the application, and only a simple parcel (do not bend or roll the third one). In the notebook with solutions, put a piece of paper measuring 6x10 cm with your address written on it (stick it on the envelope when you send the answer).

Training for one year in one department costs 5 rubles. Adult education in 3SH is preserved for schoolchildren studying in boarding schools, for orphans and children from large families (five or more children under 18 years of age who are sponsored by their parents).
