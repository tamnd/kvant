Another puzzle has appeared in Japan. It consists of 6 wooden plates, on which colored stripes are painted in different places (in the Japanese version, this is a set of 6 rosettes for jam).
You need to place the rosettes on top of each other so that, looking at the stack from above, you can see a caudrate made up of 9 differently sensitive caudrates.

The puzzle is very difficult, so we present its solution right away. Further, looking at the card and rearranging the plates, it is impossible to find the correct sequence of their arrangement.
The easiest way to make this tserrushka is from pieces of polyethylene film, gluing strips of colored paper onto it in the right places.
