“Genious” sayings of students

1. If the mass of the bullet is greater than the mass of the gun, it will not fire.
2. Once frozen, it is difficult to straighten the laundry because the molecules there are frozen.
3. The power of perseverance.
4. The power of gravity.
5. Puddles dry out faster in cool weather because then their speed increases.
6. In winter, after work, the horse is covered with a blanket so that it does not catch a cold. If a horse catches a cold, it is very difficult to treat it, because it does not know how to swallow pills.
7. To enlarge the image of an object produced by a lens, you need to move the lens away from the focal length.
8. The connection of conductors can be serial or transverse.
9. The air is pumped out of the lamp and a vacuum is introduced into it.

10. The southern strip of the magnet, according to the left-hand rule, will be facing us.
11. In operating rooms, ultraviolet lamps are turned on to prevent fainting.
12. On board the spaceship there were dogs Bee, Mushka and other small insects.
Collected by A. Khomenko
