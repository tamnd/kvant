⟦folio 68⟧

Akhov tests
M. Gardner

The police turned to the famous puzzle solver, psychology professor Ahu, for help. He called his extraordinary solutions “Ax phenomena” and proposed many tests to identify “Ax phenomena” in subjects.

Prof. Oh. The task is to tie the loose ends of the ropes and not use anything other than scissors.
Can you pass this test?

One of his tests is carried out using two ropes tied from the ceiling in an empty room.

Prof. Oh. Here's another test that I also really like. I place an open bottle of beer in the center of a small oriental rug. You need to get it out and remove it from the carpet.

Prof. Oh. The bottle must not be touched with your hand, foot, or any other part of the body or any object. Of course, it is also not allowed to use beer on the carpet. If you don't do well on this test, you may find the next test easier.
