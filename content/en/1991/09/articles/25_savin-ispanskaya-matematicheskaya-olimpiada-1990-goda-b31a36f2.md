Spanish mathematical optimization 1990

First day
1. Let $a, b$ and $c$ be the sides of a right triangle. Prove that if these numbers are integers, then their product $abc$ is divisible by 30.
2. The hemisphere lies on a flat part of the table, its radius is 1. Six spheres of radius $r$ are located so that each of them touches the hemisphere, the table and two neighboring spheres. Find $r$.
3. The equation $x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, $c \neq 0$, has three different roots that make up an arithmetic progression. Find $b$ and $c$ as functions of $a$.
4. The sequence of $\{a_n\}$, $n \geqslant 1$, complex numbers is determined by the correspondence
$$a_n = (1 + i)(1 + \frac{i}{\sqrt{2}}) \dots (1 + \frac{i}{\sqrt{n}}).$$
Is there a natural number $m$ such that
$$\sum_{n=1}^{m} |a_n - a_{n+1}| = 1990?$$
Second day
5. Regular pentagons, hexagons and decagons are inscribed in a circle. Show that a triangle that has the sides of these polygons as its sides is rectangular.
6. Let's define the numbers $A_n$ as the product of all natural numbers that are multiples of $n$ and not greater than 1000. (For example, $A_3 = 3 \cdot 6 \cdot 9 \dots 999$). Find the most common divisor of the numbers $A_2, A_3, \dots, A_{31}, A_{32}$.
7. Let's consider the function
$$f(x) = \cos a_1x + \cos a_2x,$$
where $a_1$ and $a_2$ are non-zero real numbers. Show that there is at least one number $x_0$ such that $f(x_0) < 0$.
8. Let $BM$ be the median, $BD$ the bissertina of the triangle $ABC$, and $BN$ the line symmetric to the median with respect to the bissertina (points $M$, $D$ and $N$ lie on the segment $AC$). Show that
$$\frac{AN}{NC} = \frac{c^2}{a^2},$$
where $a = BC$, $c = AB$ are the side lengths of triangle $ABC$.
