Newspaper test. You will be able to solve the test if you agree that two people standing on a newspaper face and separated by a door cannot touch each other. Slide a sheet of newspaper under the door, stand on it on one side of the door, and let your friend stand on it on the other side of the door. The door does not allow you to touch each other until you read from a sheet of newspaper.
Tennis ball test. The test solution is hampered by the implicit assumption that the ball must be thrown horizontally. There is really nothing stopping you from throwing the ball vertically. Then, having risen to a certain height, it will remain, after which it will begin to move back.
Another solution is to throw the ball so that it rolls up the hill. Such a solution could be ruled out in advance by requiring that the ball be in the air, but since this is not specified in the problem conditions, the second solution is carried out.
