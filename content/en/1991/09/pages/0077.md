9. The final pressure $p'$ is equal to the sum of the partial pressures $p_1$ and $p_2$ created in the volume of the vessel by monatomic and diatomic gases. Let us find these pressures, as well as the initial pressure $p_0$, from the equation of state
$$p_0V = \frac{m}{M_2}RT, p_1V = \frac{m_1}{M_1}RT, p_2V = \frac{m_2}{M_2}RT,$$

$$p' = p_1 + p_2 = 1,5p_0.$$

10. When the vessels are heated unevenly, gas flows through connecting tubes from one vessel to another, thereby equalizing the pressure. Let us write down the equations of state for gases in vessels before and after heating:
$$pV = \frac{m}{M}RT, p'V = \frac{m_1}{M}RT_1,$$
$$p'V = \frac{m_2}{M}RT_2, p'V = \frac{m_3}{M}RT,$$
where $p'$ is the final pressure in the system. Expressing the masses of gases and substituting them into the equation of conservation of total mass
$$m_1 + m_2 + m_3 = 3m,$$
we get
$$\frac{p'}{p} = \frac{3}{T} \left( \frac{1}{T_1} + \frac{1}{T_2} + \frac{1}{T} \right)^{-1} = 2.$$

$$A = \left( x_0^2 + \frac{v_0^2}{\omega^2} \right)^{1/2} = 5 \text{ cm}.$$
