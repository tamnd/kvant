The village was left behind, and soon the large loops of the highway brought us to the edge of the slow stack.

I had never been to such farms, and at first they seemed creepy to me, and my imagination and circumstances further strengthened this impression. The turbo engine of our car worked smoothly and silently, without disturbing the silent damp air, and we rushed along the serpentine highway, amid supernatural silence. To the right, along the mountain slopes that formed an unimaginably beautiful valley, in the dark green of mighty pines, absorbing the light, stood huge frames with sheets of slow stacking. The rays of the evening sun flashed brightly on the stretches, and it seemed as if someone was walking there. But in reality there was complete desertion around. Rows of these windows stood for years on the slopes above the valley, and people came against them only occasionally in the dead of night, when the unsaturated glass could not seal their presence.

The ripeness was fascinating, but neither I nor Selina said anything about it. It seems to me that we hated each other with such fury that we did not want to spoil new impressions by throwing them into the whirlpool of our emotions. I became more and more acutely aware that we had started this affair in the right way. Before, I thought that we would have enough rest and everything would fall into place. And so we set off to travel. But this did not change anything in Selina’s situation, and (what was even worse) the pregnancy continued to unnerve her.

Trying to find an excuse for the fact that her pregnancy had upset us so much, we said everything that the community says in such cases: we, of course, really want to have children, but only later, at a more appropriate time. After all, because of this, Selina had to leave her well-paid job, and along with her earnings, we also lost a new house, which we were about to buy - it was, of course, impossible to buy it with what I received for my poetry. However, in reality
In our life, the separation was explained by the fact that we, against our will, had to realize the following unpleasant truth: the one who says that he wants to have children, but only later, in fact does not want to have them at all - neither now nor later. And we are saddened by the knowledge that we have fallen into an eternal biological trap, although we have always considered ourselves special and unique.

The highway continued to wind its way along the southern slopes of Bencrachen, and from time to time the distant gray expanse of the Atlantic Ocean opened up for a moment ahead. I slowed down to be able to admire this picture, and then I saw a board nailed to a post. The inscription on it read: “Slow glass. High quality, low prices. J.R. Hagen." Obeying a sudden impulse, I stopped the car at the side of the road. Hard flakes of grass scratched the door, and I winced angrily.

- Why did you stop? - Selina asked, turning her platinum haired face towards me in surprise.

- Look at this ad. Let's go there and take a look. It is unlikely that in such a glaze they are asking particularly high prices for glass.

Selina returned it mockingly and angrily, but I was so captivated by this thought that I did not begin the service. I had a stupid mistake that we needed to do something reckless and unexpected. And then everything will settle down by itself.

“Come on,” I said. “It’s good for us to stretch our legs.” We sat in the car too long.

Selina shrugged her shoulders in such a way that my soul immediately felt bad, and got out of the car. The three of us began to climb in a circle, along the steps marked in the slope, which were reinforced with pegs. For some time the troika grew between the trees, and then we saw a clear stone house.
