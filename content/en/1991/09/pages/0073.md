For many years at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M. The Moscow City Olympiad in Physics is held in Lomonosov for schoolchildren in grades 8-11. The Moscow team for the All-Union Olympics is formed from its winners.

The Moscow City Olympiad in Physics is held in four stages:
1. Regional tour. Up to 4 thousand schoolchildren participate in it annually.
2. I city round. About a quarter of the participants in regional rounds who showed the highest results are invited to this round.
3. II city round. This is the most difficult stage. The tasks offered on this.type require the ability to find complex non-standard physical situations.
4. And finally, the last stage is the experimental round. Applicants for 1st and 2nd degree diplomas (about 40 people) are invited to attend.
All problems for the Olympics are prepared by researchers, teachers, graduate students and students of the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M. V Lomonosov, included in the zhora olimpida. Researchers from other branches and academic institutes of Moscow (MIREA, FIAN, etc.) also participate in the work of Zhora.

The Olympida winners are awarded at the “Open Day” at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. In the last two, the winners are also awarded special recognition from the Physical Society of the USSR. Recently, on the initiative of the Physical Society of the USSR and the Moscow Olympids, it was decided to conduct advanced physics tests between the winners of the Moscow Olympids and foreign schoolchildren. Unlike traditional International Olympics, in which schoolchildren from many countries of the world take part simultaneously, there will also be pairs: Soviet-Finnish, Soviet-Hungarian, etc. This allows a significantly larger number of Moscow schoolchildren to take part in international competitions. The first such tour (Soviet-Finnish) took place in October-November 1991 in Moscow, and the return one took place in April 1992 in Finland.

Below we invite you to try your hand at solving some of the problems offered at the Olympics this year.
