9th grade
1. A body of mass $m$ is located at the bottom of a vessel filled with a very strong liquid. He was told the speed $v_0$, directed vertically upward. Find the time after which the body will sink to the bottom if the drag force acting on it from the liquid is equal to $F = -\alpha v$, where $v$ is the speed of the body, $\alpha \gg mg/v_0$. When a body moves, it is always in liquid. Archimedes' power is a challenger. (I round)
2. During a heavy snowfall, a snowflake running across a field at a speed of $v = 20$ km/h noticed that $N_1 = 50$ snowflakes fell per minute. Turning back, he discovered that there were $N_2 = 30$ snowflakes. Estimate the line-of-sight range in snowfall, if the area of ​​this sports wall is $S = 24$ cm$^2$, and the size of the snowflake is $l = 1$ cm. (I round)
3. A homogeneous square log of size $a \times a$ and length $L \gg a$ in the initial state is held parallel to the surface of the water so that it touches the water of its face (Fig. 1). The density of a log is half the density of water. The log is released. Find the amount of heat that will be released until the system reaches equilibrium. (II round)
