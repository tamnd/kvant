The magnetic field acts on a force that creates a moment about the horizontal axis. The frame is deflected until this moment is balanced by the moment of the gravity force.

Microexperience

Rotation around the axes corresponding to the maximum and minimum moments of inertia of the body, as both experience and calculations show, is stable. Rotation around the axis corresponding to the intermediate value of the moment of inertia is unstable, and any small disturbance in this case leads to disordered movement.
