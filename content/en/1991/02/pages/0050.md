Exploration and exploration of the solar system

This area of ​​work includes
- exploration of the Sun,
— research of astroids,
- exploration of the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, etc.,
— research into the possibility and feasibility of space settlements.

It is unlikely that these studies will bring us fundamentally new information, but it would be simply unreasonable not to consider what is under our noses. Research can be carried out using automatic spacecraft. And only in the case of complete failure with the delivery of probe and atmosphere from Mars and, at the same time, when information appears indicating the possibility of detecting living organisms on Mars, should we always consider organizing a rafted expedition to Mars. (It is understood that the sample and atmosphere will be delivered to Earth to study them in order to determine whether there are living organisms in them, and if they are, to find out their genetic code or their reproductive mechanism and thereby obtain information in favor of one or another hypothesis about the origin of life: the “spontaneous generation” or “seeding” hypothesis.

Exploring the Universe

These are the most interesting, one might say, intriguing areas of research. They can provide us with the most valuable and necessary information. These works include
— research using space radiotelexes launched into near-solar and near-Earth orbits, working together in the radionferometry mode,
— study of the surrounding world using modern orbital astrophysical instruments (such as the Hubble Space Telescope) in various spectral ranges,
— research using optical telescopes with a spaced base, which can be located, for example, on the Moon,
— research into the problems of flight to the stars.

All of these are expensive undertakings, but if many countries combine the efforts and distribute the work wisely, they can be accomplished. In any case, the costs of their implementation will be significantly less than the military expenditures of states.

Technical means

To implement such a work program, it is necessary to improve existing space technology and create completely new ones, conducting theoretical and experimental research.
