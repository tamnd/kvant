4. 0, 2 sin 1. Note. The left side of the equation is an even function of $x$. Therefore, if $x_0$ is the root of this equation, then $-x_0$ is also its root, so the only unit root can be $x_0 = 0$. Substituting $x_0 = 0$, we obtain $a^2 - 2a \sin 1 = 0$, i.e., either $a = 0$ or $a = 2$ sin 1. It remains to verify that for these values ​​of $a$ the equation is real and has a single root.
5. $x = 5, y = 4, z = 4$. Note. The sum of the expressions under the logarithm signs is equal to 3, from which it follows that they are all equal to 1.
