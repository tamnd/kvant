Upcoming tasks in space

Doctor of Technical Sciences
K. FEOKTISTOV

At present, there is still no established consensus on the most important directions for the development of human activity in space. A wide variety of programs are offered.

For example, the program, which is associated with the name of the American astronaut Sally Ride, envisages as its main goals for the next 50 years the research and creation of bases on the Moon, astronauts and planets, the creation of means of travel in the solar system, and the creation of space settlements.

Soviet scientists working in the field of fundamental sciences consider it necessary in the next decade to concentrate efforts on the study of near-Earth space, the Earth's magnetosphere, on studies of solar terrestrial communications, the Sun, the solar corona, Mars, and on astrophysical research using automatic spacecraft.

There are also more practical proposals, which are called the development of satellite communication and television systems, systems of meteorological satellites, the creation of satellite systems for environmental monitoring and research of the Earth's natural resources, the creation of economically feasible and efficient production in orbit.
