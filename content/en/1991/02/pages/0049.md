The most pressing problems facing people today include

- environmentally, natural resources,
- political instability in the world, disunity of peoples, division of states into opposing blocs, distrust between them, etc.,
- increasing overpopulation of the Earth.

In the study of the Universe, as it seems to me today, the most interesting tasks include

— research of the solar system,
— research of stars, galaxies, celestial objects on the screens of the Universe using astrophysical instruments,
— research into the possibilities of flights to the stars.

With this approach, the following main directions of space work could be proposed.

Activity
in the interests of satisfying

We are talking about work of an applied nature that could bring specific benefits to people and, if possible, be economically viable. Three groups of work can be distinguished here.

- Group A: already defined, almost traditional, work such as economic control of the land surface, seas and oceans using spacecraft, satellite meteorological service, satellite navigation for ships, aircraft, ground transport, satellite systems for receiving signals from those in distress, communications and television.

— Group B: work related to the deployment of economically profitable production in orbit, with the transfer into orbit of necessary, but dangerous production on the surface of the Earth. To organize this work, it will be necessary to expand ground-based and, most importantly, space experiments to search for reliable and efficient technological processes in orbit.

This group of works also includes Japanese work on the emergence and feasibility of creating solar orbital power plants to supply the Earth with cheap and economically clean electricity.

This also includes work at orbital stations to determine the area of ​​the most effective activity of people directly in orbit, since until now this area has not been found and it remains unclear where, when performing what kind of work, it is more efficient for a person to be automatically employed.

— Group B: work in the interests of maintaining peace on Earth, maintaining stability and strengthening trust between states, preventing aggression. This refers to the creation of an international, open to public, satellite system for observing and monitoring the earth's surface, airspace, and underwater conditions. It is proposed to create systems of satellites that would give everyone the opportunity to see what is happening on Earth, day and night, to monitor the movements of military equipment and military equipment, the construction of suspicious (possibly military) objects, and to monitor the implementation of international agreements. Now practically only the Soviet Union and the United States of America have satellite reconnaissance systems, which are not yet year-round and do not provide a sufficiently high-quality picture at dusk and at night.
