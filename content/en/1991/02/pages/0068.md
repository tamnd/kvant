Option 5
(blues department)
1. Find all solutions to the equation
$$3 \cos 2x = 4 - 11 \cos x.$$
2. Find all solutions to the inequality
$$9^{2x+0.5} - 10 \cdot 27^{\frac{2}{3}x} > \frac{11}{3}.$$
3. From points A and B, the distance between them is 300 km, a truck and a passenger car simultaneously drove towards each other. 4 hours after the start of the movement they met. After the meeting, the car driving from A to B increased its speed by 15 km/h, and the truck increased its speed by 30 km/h. Determine the initial speed of the car if it is known that it arrived at point B 1 hour earlier than the truck arrived at point A.
4. Three corridors are drawn from point M on the circle: MN=1, MP=6, MQ=2. In this case, the angles NMP and PMQ are equal.
Find the radius of the circle.
5. Find all values ​​of a, b, x, y, z for which the matches are true
$$\begin{cases} 1 + \mathrm{tg}(bz) \sin^2(xy) + \cos(2xy) \leqslant (\cos x + \sin(ay)) \sin(2xy) |, \\ 2 + 2\sqrt{\mathrm{tg}(bz)} \cos(b(y+x)) + \cos(2b(y+x)) = 0. \end{cases}$$
Option 6
(faculty of postal science)
1. Solve the equation
$$2 - 3^{x-2} = 3^{x-1}.$$
2. Tractor drivers A and B plowed the field. On the first day they plowed 1/3 of the field, with A working 2 hours and B working 1 hour more. They plowed the rest of the field the next day, with A working for 5 hours and B for 4.5 hours. In how many hours could tractor driver B plow this field alone?
3. In triangle ABC, side AB is 4, angle A is 30°, and angle B is 130°. A circle is constructed on side AB as a diameter. Find the area of ​​the part of the circle lying inside the triangle.
4. Find the smallest value of the function
$$y = 1 + 4 \sin x - 2x$$
on the interval [0; π].

5. Find all values ​​of x for which the largest of 2x+1 and x+2 is greater than -1.
6. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2(2 - 3x) > 4x + 1.$$
Option 7
(faculty of geology)
1. Solve the equation
$$-\frac{|x|}{x} - x = \frac{x^2}{2} + 1.$$
2. Find the area of ​​triangle OMN, indicated on the plane by line segments OM, ON, MN, where O is the origin of coordinates, M is the point of intersection of lines y=x, y=-2x+2, N is the point of intersection of line y=-2x+2 and the OX axis.
3. Solve the equation
$$1 - (2 \cos x + \sqrt{3}) \text{ctg } x = 2 \sin x.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log \frac{1}{5} \left( \frac{1}{x^{15}} \right) - 2 \leqslant \log_{125} x^{12} \leqslant 4 - \frac{7}{\log_x 5}.$$
5. Find all pairs of real numbers m and n for which the equation
$$(3x^2 - 2m^2 + mn)^2 + (3m^2 - mn + 2n^2 - 12x)^2 + 4 = 4x - x^2$$
has at least one solution.
6. Point E is chosen on side BC of triangle BCD, and point F is chosen on side BD so that angle BEF is equal to angle BDC. The area of ​​the circle circumscribed around triangle CFD is 5 times smaller than the area of ​​the circle circumscribed around triangle BEF. The ratio of the area of ​​the quadrilateral CEFD to the area of ​​the triangle BEF is 9/16. The angle FDE is 45°. Find the angle CED.
Option 8
(faculty of geology)
1. Solve the equation
$$\cos^2 6x - \sin^2 3x - 1 = 0.$$
2. The product of the first and fifth terms of a geometric progression is 12. The frequency of dividing the second term by the fourth is 3. Find the second term of the progression.
3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{2^2 + \sqrt{x-1} - 24}{2^1 + \sqrt{x-1}} > 1.$$
4. On sides AB, BC and AD of the parallel ABCD, points K, M and L are taken respectively so that AK:KB=2:1, BM:MC=1:1, AL:LD=1:3. Find the ratio of the areas of triangles KBL and BML.
5. Find all values ​​of the parameter a for which the equation
$$(a+1)x^2 + (|a+2| - |a+10|)x + a = 5$$
has two different positive roots.
