Radiotelector and placement of jointly working radiotelectors in Earth orbit (base D≈250 mil. km). 1 — instrument compartment; 2 - receiver; 3 - a body-insulating design that ensures temperature stability. With the help of radiotelexes placed in near-healing orbit, you can obtain a resolution of the order of ten millionths of an arcsecond and look at the very windows of our Universe.

In addition, large radio telecommunications with placements of the order of a cliometer will allow a person to begin a search for signals from external civilizations.

9. Organic astrophysical observatories operating in various spectral ranges.

10. Optical telescopes with placed mirrors located on the Moon.

The idea of ​​such telescopes is the same as in radiopterfometry - to increase the observation base. But the base must be held and known with an accuracy of small fractions of the wavelength of electromagnetic radiation at which the observation is being made, i.e., in this case, with an accuracy of fractions of a micron. That is why the idea of ​​placing them on the Moon appears.

11. A research base on the Moon, which may require the establishment of astrophysical observatories on the lunar surface and to study the possibility of using ray minerals in human space activities.

12. Automatic devices for delivering samples of soil and atmosphere of Mars to Earth.

13. If, as a result of this work, it turns out to be necessary to carry out an experiment on Mars, then it will be necessary to develop and create the appropriate means for a manned experiment on Mars: a Martian orbital ship, a Martian experimental ship, a Martian car and the corresponding equipment for life and research on Mars.

14. Automatic means for studying Venus: an orbital base at Venus, atmospheric sounding balloons, radar surface mapping tools, landing laboratories.

15. Solar observatories with perihelion inside the orbit of Mercury, intended for regular study of the star closest to us - our Sun.

16. Automatic devices for studying astrophids.

17. Automatic spacecraft for exploring distant planets.
