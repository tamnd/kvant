The next two problems are not similar in form, but their solution is largely similar. Task 4. “Horizontal gutter”
During construction, a problem arose in determining the horizontal iron in the concrete body of the dam. In the community, this problem is solved with the help of a spirit level - a device, the main part of which is a tube of water containing an air bubble. If the tube is horizontal, then the bubble is located after the line; if it is not horizontal, then the bubble goes to one of the ends. But in this case, direct use of the device is difficult. It is possible to place a spirit level in an iron, but the iron has a bend, and therefore the device is not visible and it is not possible to determine the horizontal iron behind the bend. Help the builders. And those who solve this problem, let them think about the question: suppose the iron is not horizontal; how to determine its slope?

20. Space robots.
We should expect an expansion of work in open space in the orbits of Earth satellites. This work will be associated with the creation of orbital factories, large radio telecoms, maintenance of orbital vehicles, and possibly with the construction of orbital power plants. The constraint of a person dressed in space armor and the danger of working in open space will force us to create space robots.
