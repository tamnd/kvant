Contest
"Mathematics
6 — 8»
The magazine «Kvant», together with the large youth magazine “Mathematics,” continues a competition to solve mathematical problems for students in grades 6–8. The competition consists of 27 problems (3 in each issue) and will end in May of this year. The winners will be awarded prizes from the magazine «Kvant» and “Mathematics”.
The solution to the problem from this issue is calculated no later than April 15, 1991 at the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant» (with the mark “Competition “Mathematics 6-8”). Don't forget to include your last name, first name, school and class.

Tasks
16. The word ROUTE can be read on figure 1 in different ways. How many such ways are there? Which letter must be removed so that the number of ways is 145?
V. Radunsky

Fig. 1.
17. Several tractors plow a field of 300 hectares in a whole number of days, and each tractor plows 15 hectares per day. How many additional tractors will be needed to complete the job 6 days earlier?
I. Akulich

Fig. 2.
18. In rhombus ABCD, angle A is 60°. Prove that if one of the angles of the triangle BMN (Fig. 2) is equal to 60°, then the others are also equal to 60°.
V. Proizvolov
