I AM THAT'S ANOTHER THING
(fantasy story)

F. PAUL (USA)

I'm sitting on the edge of a metal bed. The mattress is a second blanket placed on its steel springs. It’s not very convenient, but even more trouble awaits me.

The hour is approaching when I will be transferred to the county jail, and some time after that I will find myself on death row. Of course, a judicial procedure will take place first, but this is a simple formality. Not only was I captured at the scene of the performance, but I also confessed to everything.

I deliberately killed Lawrence Connaught, my friend who saved my life. Of course, I could cite some mitigating circumstances in my defense, but will the court take them into account?

Connaught and I have been friends for many years. The war separated us. A few years after it ended, we met again in Washington, but there was some estrangement in our relationship. During this time, he, as they say, found his recognition. He worked long and hard on something, but hid from me what it was. Naturally, I had my worries, but after I failed miserably in anatomy, they no longer had anything to do with science. I must say that I have lost interest in medicine a long time ago, from the very day when I first entered the anatomical theater; I wasn’t afraid of work, there was just nothing attractive about it.

So I didn’t receive any academic title, and why would a security guard need it?

Career not very internal? Of course not. But I'm not ashamed of her. Nothing in life should be cold at all. And I should even like moi. Senators are usually quite frank in the presence of us guards, they treat us well, and we often learn interesting things about what goes on behind the scenes of government. For our part, we can be useful to a considerable number of people - newspaper reporters hunting for some story; a proper official who can sometimes use a single careless remark for an entire political campaign; as well as to all those who would like to beat during important claims in the visitors' gallery.

This is how it happened, for example, with Larry Connaught. We ran into him on the street one day, chatted a little, then he asked if I could get him a ticket for upcoming claims on foreign policy. The next day I told him by phone that everything was fine with the pass.

He arrived at the beginning of the Secretary of State's speech, and his small, moist eyes sparkled with pleasure. And then suddenly a loud scream was heard... This story, of course, is still fresh in everyone’s memory. There were three of them, these fans from Central America, who tried to influence our politics with the help of firearms. Two had pistols, the third had a hand grenade. Two senators and one guard carried pistols. Connaught and I stood very close. I rushed at the little guy, I was already swinging the grenade launcher, I knocked him down; the grenade opened to the side, I wanted to grab it and, seeing that it was cocked and about to explode, I froze for a moment, and at that same moment Larry was on it...
