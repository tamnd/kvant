What should be the diameter of each of the copper wires in the cord so that the power dissipated on it does not exceed $\eta=0,1\%$ of the power of the wastage? Copper resistivity $\rho=1,75 \times 10^{-8}$ Ohm m.

1. On Mars, the time a body released without an initial velocity falls from a certain height on the surface falls from the same height on Earth. How many times does the oscillation period of the mathematical matrix on Mars differ from its oscillation period on Earth? Atmospheric resistance can be claimed.

2. The iron consists of two boards forming two other angles, the edge of which is horizontal, and the planes make equal angles $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the horizon. A cylinder of mass $m=4$ kg lies in the gutter, the generatrix of which is parallel to the edge of the gutter. What force must be applied in the horizontal direction to the base of the cylinder so that it moves uniformly along the groove? Friction coefficient between the surfaces of the groove and the cylinder $\mu=0,1$.

3. A foam cube with an edge $a=0,5$ m, floating on the water, turned out to be sandwiched under the docampi of a low horizontal place (Fig. 16). What horizontal force must be applied to the cube to move it translationally along the space? The density of the foam is $\rho_{\pi}=60$ kg/$\mathrm{m}^3$, the density of water is $\rho_{\mathrm{B}}=10^3$ kg/$\mathrm{m}^3$, the height of the place above the water level is $h=20$ cm, the friction coefficient between the surface of the cube and the docampi of the place is $\mu=0,3$.

4. There is a piston in a horizontal tube open at both ends with a cross-sectional area of ​​$S=10$ cm² at a distance of $l=10$ cm from one of its ends. They stand up from the same end and begin to push another piston into the tube. At what distance between the pistons does the first piston move out of place? The sliding friction force acting on the piston from the side of the tube walls is $F=100$ N, the atmosphere pressure is $p_0=10^5$ Pa. The temperature is considered constant. You can complain about the thickness of the pistons.

5. In a cylinder under a smooth piston with an area of ​​$S=5 \cdot 10^{-4}$ m² there is air at a temperature of $T_0=273$ K and a pressure of $p_0=10^5$ Pa, equal to atmospheric pressure. The cylinder lies on a horizontal plane, resting against the vertical wall (Fig. 17). On the same plane lies a body of mass $m=5$ kg, connected to the piston by a horizontal rod. To what temperature must the air in the cylinder be heated for the body to move? The friction coefficient between the body and the plane is $\mu=0,3$.

6. A small ball with a mass of $m=1$ g and a charge of $q=10^{-7}$ C is suspended on a weightless thread, the other end of which is fixed. When this system was placed in a uniform vertical electric field, the tension force of the thread in the new equilibrium position remained the same as it was in the absence of the field. Find the field strength values.

7. The electron moves in a region of space where there is a uniform electric field with a strength of $E=0,5 \cdot 10^3$ V/m and a uniform magnetic field with an indication of $B=10^{-2}$ T, and the vectors $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$ have the same direction. Find the acceleration of the electron at the moment of time when its speed is equal to $v=10^5$ m/s and makes an angle $\alpha=60^\circ$ with the direction of the vectors $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$. The ratio of the charge of an electron to its mass is $e/m=1,76 \times 10^{11}$ C/kg.

9. A ball of radius $R$ from the globe lies on a round flat grain, touching the center of the grain with a silver stripe. What focal length must coincide with the lens so that its diverging lens pleases the circle on the screen does not change?
