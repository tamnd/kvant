Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. A small ball falls from a height of $h=50$ cm onto an inclined plane making an angle of $\alpha=45^\circ$ with the horizontal. Find the distance between the points of the first and second collision of the ball with the plane. The impact is considered to be elastic, and air resistance is claimed.

2. A small ball suspended on threads $l=1$ m long is deflected from the equilibrium position so that the thread makes a vertical angle $\alpha=60^\circ$, and is released without an initial speed. At the moment when the ball passes the equilibrium position, the thread turns. What angle is formed by the vertical velocity of the ball at the moment of its fall to the floor, if the distance from the point of suspension of the thread to the floor is $h=2,5$ m?

3. Two bodies, which initially rested on a smooth horizontal plane, are stretched, clamped between a spring, and begin to move translationally at a speed of $v_1=3$ m/s and $v_2=1$ m/s. Calculate how much energy was stored in the spring if it is known that the total mass of both bodies is $m=8$ kg, the spring is weightless, and there is no friction.

4. Two vessels with volumes $V_1=2$ l and $V_2=4$ l, filled with the same gas, are connected to each other by a tube with a stopcock and kept at a constant temperature. With the tap closed, the gas pressure in the first vessel is $p_1=400$ kPa, and in the second $p_2=200$ kPa. By what amount does the mass of gas in the first vessel change after opening the tap, if the initial mass of gas in this vessel is $m_1=18$ g? Do not take into account the volume of the tube and tap.

5. Find the amount of heat transferred to a monatomic gas when it is transferred from state 1 to state 2 (Fig. 12). When calculating, use $p_1=500$ kPa, $V_1=2$ l, $V_2=4$ l.

6. Two identical flat capacitors, one of which is air, and the other is filled with a dielectric with a dielectric substance $\varepsilon$, are connected, as shown in figure 13, and charged to a voltage $U_0$. How much work must be done to subtract the dielectric dam from the capacitor? Condenser air capacity $C$.

7. A capacitor with a capacity $C$, charged to a voltage $U$, is discharged through a load resistance onto a battery with an emf $\varepsilon$ and a very low internal resistance (Fig. 14). How much heat will be released in the load resistance after the switch is closed?

8. The battery is shorted to some resistance. If another identical resistance is connected in parallel to this resistance, the power released in the external circuit will not change. How much will the power released in the external circuit change if the resistance is connected to the battery in series?
