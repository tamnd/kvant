Let's turn now to the tasks.

Problem 1. The tangent to the graph of the function $y=x^2+2x-3$ is inclined to the abscissa axis at an angle $\pi/4$. Find the coordinates of the point of contact.

Solution. It follows from the conditions of the problem that $f'(x_0)=\mathrm{tg} \frac{\pi}{4}=1$. $f'(x)=2x+2$ means $2x_0+2=1$; $x_0=-\frac{1}{2}$, $y_0=-3\frac{3}{4}$.

Problem 2. What angle does the tangent to the parabola $y=x^2-5x+7$ form with the x-axis at point (2; 1)?

Solution. First of all, make sure that point (2; 1) belongs to the graph of the function. Since the value of the derivative of the function $y=x^2-5x+7$ (i.e. $y'=2x-5$) is equal to the tangent of the tangent to the graph of this function at a given point, then $tg\alpha=2x_0-5=-1$. This means that the tangent forms an angle $\alpha=135^\circ$ with the abscissa axis.
