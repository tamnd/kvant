1. Do the function range points have common points?
$$y = \sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{2}x - 2x^2$$
and the interval $[\log_3 15; +\infty)$? Justify your answer.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{25} + |16x^2 - 25| = 4 + 4|x + 1|.$$
3. On the hypotenuse $KM$ of the right triangle $KLM$ there is the center $O$ of the circle, which touches the sides $LK$ and $LM$ at points $A$ and $B$ respectively. Find the length of the segment $AK$ if it is known that $BM = \frac{23}{16}$, $AK: AC = 5:23$ (where $C$ is the point of intersection of the circle with $KM$, lying between the points $O$ and $M)$.
4. The natural numbers $a, b, c$, taken in the given order, form an increasing geometric progression, the denominator of which is an integer. The numbers 2240 and 4312 are divisible by $b$ and $c$, respectively. Find the numbers $a, b$ and $c$ if it is known that under the specified conditions the sum $a + b + c$ is maximum.
5. Find all the roots of the equation
$$\sqrt{1 - \text{ctg}^2(2\pi x)} \cos(\pi x) + \sin(\pi x) = \sqrt{2},$$
located on the segment $[-3; 1]$.
6. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the system
$$\begin{cases} (3 - 2\sqrt{2})^y + (3 + 2\sqrt{2})^y - 3a = x^2 + 6x + 5, \\ y^2 - (a^2 - 5a + 6)x^2 = 0, \\ -6 \leq x \leq 0 \end{cases}$$
has a unique solution.

Option 10
(Faculty of Philology)
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{10} \cos x - \sqrt{4 \cos x - \cos 2x} = 0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{x-2} (3x - x^2) \leq 2.$$
3. In a right triangle $ABC$, from the vertex of the right angle $C$ the median $CM$ and the height $CH$ are drawn. Find the relation $AH: AM$ if $CM: CH = 5:4$ and the point $H$ is between points $A$ and $M$.
4. A piece of the same mass was cut from two alloys with masses of 7 kg and 3 kg with different percentages of magnesium. Then the piece cut from the first alloy was fused with the remainder of the second alloy, and the piece cut from the second alloy was fused with the remainder of the first alloy. Determine the mass of each of the cut pieces if the new alloys were obtained with the same percentage of magnesium.
5. The base of the quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ is the square $ABCD$, and the height of the paramid coincides with the edge $SA$. Find the height of the paramid if the radius of the ball inscribed in the paramid is 3 and the side of the square $ABCD$ is 15.

Option 11
(Faculty of Psychology)
1. Solve the equation
$$4 \sin^2 \left(2(x + \frac{\pi}{2})\right) - 2(\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{3}) \cos(2x - \pi) + \sqrt{15} - 4 = 0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{2}{x}} - 1 + \frac{1}{3} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{x}} < \frac{1}{3}.$$
3. Find all solutions to the inequality
$$\left(-\frac{1}{2}x + \frac{5}{8} - \frac{15}{88} - \frac{32x}{2}\right)^2 \leq 1.$$
4. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, the base $AD$ is 16, the sum of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ is 36, and the angle $CAD$ is 60°. The ratio of the areas of triangles $AOD$ and $BOC$, where $O$ is the point of intersection of the diagonals, is 4. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.
5. Assuming it is known that for any $a > 0$ the equation
$$2x^3 + x^2 - x - a - 1 = 0$$
has a single positive root $x_0$ (depending on $a$), find all $a > 0$ for which
$$12x_0^3 - 7x_0 > 6a + 1.$$

Faculty of Physics
1. Two identical balls, between which a spring is sandwiched, are connected by a thread. The balls are thrown without an initial speed from a certain height so that when they fall, the centers of the balls move along one vertical line. After a time $t$ after the start of the fall, the thread breaks, the spring unclenches, and the balls spread out. At what height did the thread break if the first ball fell to the ground after time $t_1$ after the thread broke, and the second after time $t_2 > t_1$? The time of action of the spring on the balls after breaking the thread is considered small. The dimensions of the balls and springs are not taken into account, air resistance is not a concern.
2. Inside the spherical surface there is a washer (Fig. 1). One surface of the washer is a sphere of the same radius. The sliding friction coefficient between the washer and the sphere is $\mu$, the radius of the sphere is $R$. With what angular velocity must the sphere and the stationary relative non-puck rotate around the vertical axis $OO'$ so that the washer is at a height $h(h < R)$ from the bottom point of the sphere? Gravity acceleration $g$. The maximum rest time is considered equal to the sliding friction force.
3. A single-length wooden board floats in water, submerged 1/2 of its volume. A weight was placed on one end of the doxy, causing that end to sink into the water to its upper edge. What fraction of the doxy volume will be immersed in water?
