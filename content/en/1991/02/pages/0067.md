Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. In triangle ABC, side BC is 6, side AC is 5, and vertex angle B is 30°. Find the area of ​​the triangle if the distance from vertex A to line BC is less than $1/\sqrt{2}$.

2. Solve the equation
$$2^{|x-2|}\sin x = (\sqrt{2})^{|x|\sin x|}.$$

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{1-x^3}-1}{1+x} \leq x.$$

4. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the equation
$$x^2-2a\sin(\cos x)+a^2=0$$ has a unique solution.

5. Find all triples of integers $x, y, z$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log_2(2x+3y-6z+3)+\log_2(3x-5y+2z-2)+\log_2(2y+4z-5x+2) > z^2-9z+17.$$

6. At the base of the SABC priming lies a regular triangle ABC with sides $2\sqrt{3}$; SA=SB=SC=$\sqrt{7}$. The sphere $S_1$ is inscribed into the triangular angle at vertex C. Sphere $S_2$, whose radius is second greater than that of sphere $S_1$, touches sphere $S_1$, planes SAC and ABC. In this case, the line segment SB enclosed inside the sphere $S_2$ has length $6/\sqrt{7}$. Find the radius of the sphere $S_2$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{x^2+4}8 < 1.$$

2. The numbers $a_1, a_2, ..., a_{21}$ form an argument progression. It is known that the sum of the terms of this progression with odd numbers is 15 greater than the sum of the terms with even numbers. Find $a_{12}$ if $a_{20}=3a_9$.

3. Solve the equation
$$3 \cdot 64^{2\sin^2(x+\frac{\pi}{4})}-392 \cdot 8^{\sin 2x}+16=0.$$

4. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{9v^2-48v-21}+\sqrt{9v^2-51v-15} \leq |3v-6|.$$

5. The radius of the inscribed circle in triangle ABC is $\sqrt{15}/3$. A circle of radius $5\sqrt{5}/(3\sqrt{3})$ is tangent to the rays forming angle ABC and the circle inscribed in triangle ABC. Find the tangent of angle ABC if the area of ​​triangle ABC is $3\sqrt{15}$ and its longest side is side AC.

6. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ such that for any values ​​of the parameter $b$ the inequality
$$\log_6\left(\frac{x}{36}\right)+\left(\frac{10a+3b+31}{5}\right)x^2-9b^2-9b-1 \leq \log_6\left(\frac{36}{x}\right)+\left(\frac{10a+3b+41}{5}\right)x^2-9b+2x+9b^2+15b+3$$ has at least one solution.

Option 3
(Faculty of Physics)

1. Solve the equation
$$x^2-4|x|-1=0.$$

2. In an equilateral trapezoid, the diagonal of length $d$ makes an angle $\alpha$ with the base. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos x \cdot \cos 3x=\cos 2x.$$

4. In triangle ABC $\angle BAC=\alpha$, $\angle ABC=\beta$, BC=a, AD=height. Point $P$ is taken on side AB so that AP/PB=1/2. A circle is drawn through point $P$, tangent to side BC at point $D$. Find the radius of this circle.

5. Determine for which $a$ the equation $\log_{\sqrt{2-x}}2x+a=2$ has a solution, and find these solutions.

6. In a regular triangular triangle, take SABC as segment AD - the height of the base ABC. A cone with vertex $A$ and generatrix AD touches its lateral surface of the base ABC and the lateral faces ASC and ASB of the base. It is known that AD/SD=m. Find

1) the ratio of the area of ​​the lateral surface of the cone to the area of ​​the base of the prim;
2) within what limits can this ratio change when $m$ changes;
3) for which $m$ the cone does not have points located outside the base.

Option 4
(chemistry department)

1. Solve the equation
$$4^x+3 \cdot 2^{x+2}=64.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$2\sin^2x+\sin^22x=\frac{5}{4}-2\cos 2x.$$

3. Calculate the area and perimeter of the triangle formed by the coordinate axes and the tangent drawn to the graph of the function $y=a\sin x$ ($a>0$) at point $x_0=-\frac{4\pi}{3}$.
