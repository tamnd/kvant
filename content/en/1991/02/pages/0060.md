The music suddenly fell silent, the receiver disappeared. And then he reappeared in the same place; The end of the connecting cord that had jumped out of the socket fell onto the carpet with a slight thud.
“It was about the height of Everest,” said Larry, clearly trying to maintain a casual appearance. “What can you say about such a thing...
The cord lying on the floor rose, and its plug rushed to the socket, froze in the air for a second and fell back onto the floor.
“No,” Larry changed his mind, “now I’ll show you something really cool.” Keep an eye on the sampler, Dick. I made it work without current. To enhance electromagnetic oscillations,...
His intense gaze was again focused on the device. A moment, another. The light bulb flickered on, illuminating the scale; The first hissing sounds came from the speaker. I stood up from my chair and was right behind Larry.
I used the phone that was on the table next to my chair. The blow landed on the back of his head, near his ear; he went limp and fell to the floor. I hit him twice more so that he would probably not be able to come to his senses for the next hour, and threw the telephone receiver back into place.
Then he began the search. I found what interested me in his desk: notes and calculations. Everything I needed to know to be able to do what he could do. I’ll put this together in two or three lines; I burned everything else.
I picked up the phone again and called the police. Hearing their siren, I pulled out my service pistol and shot him in the throat. He was already dead when they burst into the room.

My joint is clean. At the trial, I will try to explain the motives for my action, although I am not sure that the jury will recognize them as valid.
Those two or three lines told him how to do what he, Lawrence Connaught, could do. Anyone who can read could do this too. Connaught's formula is available to all literate people - clean, dishonest, scoundrels, representatives, the mentally ill.
Lawrence Connaught was a pure idealist, that's true. We were friends since childhood, I knew his soul inside and out and, as they say, if necessary, I could trust him with my life. All this is true. But we are talking about much more!
Not only about his life! Not just about mine!
Who can vouch for a person who suddenly feels rich? Suppose that some person has become the only owner of a secret that gives him the ability to penetrate through any walls, into any closed room, into any bank account now. Assume that this person is not afraid of any weapon.
They say that power corrupts. That absolute power corrupts the absolute. Is it possible to imagine a more absolute power than that possessed by Connaught? A man who, without fear of punishment, could do whatever he wanted? Larry was my friend, but I killed him in complete cold blood, knowing that a man who possessed a secret that could make him the ruler of the world could not be left alive.
I am another matter.
