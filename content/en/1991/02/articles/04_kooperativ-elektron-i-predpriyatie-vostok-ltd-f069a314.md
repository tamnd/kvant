Cooperative "Electron"
and the enterprise "Vostok Ltd"

They offer owners and users of PCs of the types “Vector-06Ts”, “Lvov PK-01”, UK-NTs (“Elektronka MC0511”), “BK0010-01”, “BK0011”, “IBM XT/AT”, “Ponsk”, “Specialist”, “Sinclair ZX Spectrom”, “Pravets8D”, DVK-3,4, “RK-8632K”, “Mikrosha”, “Partner”, “Apogee”, “Agat-7”, “Corvette”, “Atari XE/XL/ST”, “Commodore +4.16”, “Commodore 64.128” a wide selection of system, applied, gaming, educational programs, the latest developments first-hand at reasonable prices, as well as educational programs for UK-NC classes (“Electronka MC0202"), KUVT-86.

They will enter into an agreement with the authors to sell the software developed by them with the payment of interest on sales.

They will buy programs for PCs “Vector-06Ts”, “Lvov PK-01”, UK-NTs, “Ponsk”, it is possible to exchange programs.

They will sell computers “Sinclair ZX Spectrum” without a disk drive and with a disk drive, “Specialist”, DVK of all modifications, UK-NTs (“Electronka MC0511”), UK-NTs classes (“Electronka MC0202”) with software, “Corvette”, “Epos” classes based on the Corvette PC (32 workstations), IBM-compatible computers, telefax, copying equipment, floppy disks.

These PC models such as DVK-2, DVK-3 will be modified to bring their capabilities closer to the new models DVK-3,4, and they will be equipped with additional controllers and peripheral devices.

The computers of UK-NC (Electronka MC0511) will be based on Winchester-type hard drives and EPROM cassettes with BASIC.

They will provide all enterprises and organizations interested in purchasing various imported consumer goods for Soviet rubles with the latest services: assistance in concluding contracts for your enterprise with information companies and joint ventures. Lists of goods offered for purchase will be sent upon your written request.

The above enterprise is invited to become a full participant in the free economic zone of Zelenograd. The cooperative "Electron" and the enterprise "Vostok Ltd" take over the registration of the enterprise, provide an office, telephone, manager, and accountant. The proximity of the enterprises created is guaranteed. All that is required from your company is to conclude an agreement with the Vostok Ltd company, which determines the share of participants in the established capital and the percentage and cost of payment for our services.

Will send cash on delivery:

— a set of working documentation for the manufacture of a small-sized inexpensive machine for the production of wall slags;

— books and brochures on software and hardware of the Sinclair ZX Spectrom PC;

— a set of documents on the creation of limited liability companies, joint-stock companies, small enterprises;

— a directory of addresses and telephone numbers of foreign companies accredited in Moscow.

They will provide all enterprises with marketing services in the most effective way: they will send advertisements of your products to 40,000 addresses of enterprises and institutions.

Dealers are invited to cooperate to participate in the sale of products and services of the Electron cooperative and the Vostok Ltd enterprise with the payment of interest on sales.

Postal address for inquiries, catalog requests and applications: 103489, Moscow, Zelenograd, building 705, Electron cooperative.

At the request of customers, catalogs, programs, computers, documentation are sent by mail.

Phone for inquiries: 536-12-81 (from 12 to 18 o'clock).
