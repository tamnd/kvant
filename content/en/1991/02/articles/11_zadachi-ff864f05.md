Tasks

1. They say that the founder of the Czech state, Princess Libusha, offered a riddle to three contenders for her hand. “If I gave the first groom half of the plums from this basket and another plum, the second half of the remainder and another plum, and the third half of what was left and three more plums, then the basket would be empty. How many plums are there in the basket? What do you think?

2. Prove that the sum $n$ of consecutive odd numbers is divisible by $n$.

3. When you square a certain two-digit number consisting of identical digits, you get a number whose first and second digits are the same, and also the third and fourth are the same. Find this two-digit number.

4. figure depicts an arithmetic rebus. Same letters correspond to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers. What is the smallest number of fir trees that a FOREST can consist of? (The letters E and E correspond to the same number.)

5. Straight lines are drawn through a point inside the square, parallel to its sides and diagonals (see figure). Prove that the sum of areas colored red is equal to the sum of areas colored blue.

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Savin, S. Sefibekov, O. Povalyukhina, E. Gorbunov and V. Proizvolov.
