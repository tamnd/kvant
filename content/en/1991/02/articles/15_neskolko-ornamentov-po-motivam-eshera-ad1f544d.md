ornaments

The works of the famous artist Maurice Escher have some kind of magical power. «Kvant» repeatedly turned to his amazing, and sometimes country-like, paintings (see, for example, the article by S. Tabachnikov “The World of Escher”, No. 12, 1990). Escher's work reflects many mathematical ideas. Thus, its ornaments may remind an experienced mathematician of crystallographic groups. We offer several variations on Escher’s theme, owned by our reader O. Parnasskaya. Try to satisfy the idea of ​​symmetry of the sixth order with these ornaments.
