We are coping -
they answer us
(based on materials from “Questionnaire - 90”)

As our regular readers have probably already noticed, once a quarter we publish a questionnaire in the magazine. It is important for us to know what you like about the magazine and what you don’t, what you would like to read about on its pages, how we can help a schoolchild, teacher, applicant, and whatever else «Kvant» is just a lover of physics or mathematics, astronautics or computer science.

We thank everyone who took the time to answer our questions.

Unfortunately, significantly fewer readers switched off to Questionnaire 90 than in previous years, and therefore it is more difficult to talk about a pattern. And yet...

This is the picture that emerged from the results of Questionnaire 90.

Most of our readers are, of course, high school students. Although among those who filled out the questionnaire there are teachers and students, workers and engineers. There are completely new subscriptions and those who subscribe «Kvant» since the birth of the magazine.

About the popularity of rubrics. The reader invariably gets the most attention from the sections “Problem book «Kvant»”, “School in «Kvant»”, “Kaleidoscope”, a little less - “Practice for applicants”, “Options for university entrance exams”, “Laboratory «Kvant»”, “Math circle” and «Kvant» for younger students (by the way, I like this section and “senior”). By the end of the year, the “P for Package” and “Fiction” sections became very popular.

Most liked articles:
M. Berkinblit, E. Glagoleva. "Matematika in living organisms".
P. Capizza. "On the superfluidity of liquid gel II".
A. Stasenko. “Waves on the water and “Overseas Guests” by N. Roerich.”
A. Abrikovov. “What a skier doesn’t think about.”
A. Savin. “Pebbles and a chessboard”, D. Fuchs. "Alexader's Horned Sphere."
M. Kaganov. "Letters on Physics".
K. Bogdanov. "Large and small throughout."
L. Kscanfomality. "Prospects for the search for common planets."
K. Foktistov. "Flight to the stars."
V. Levin. "Seaquake".
E. Narimanov. "56 million kilometers to the Red Planet."
Ya. Smorodinsky. "The Law of Universal Gravitation."
S. Tabachnikov. "Variations on a Theme of Escher".
S. Tabachnikov. "Matematika and ours."
Let us note that among the best articles, the so-called stats, reprinted from issues «Kvant» years ago, both we and you noted their high level.

Favorite covers:
covers of numbers 3 and 9 (to the article “Ultrasound in Medicine” and to the material “Hyperbolic Parabolide”).

As always, assessments and wishes are varied, diametrically opposed (sometimes categorical!), we are asked, demanded of us:
“Let «Kvant» be as it was before this time,”
“I hoped for the best, I wrote it out in vain!”, “Please refer to old publications and editions”, “More historicism and activity”, “Do not give the cover to abstractionists!”, “Each cover is good and corresponds to the number”, “More experiments for independent work”, “Place at least one test to find out your level of development”, “All illustrations are A”, “Stupid figure", "I like all the articles, except the science fiction stories. Don’t litter the magazine!”, “Fantastics!”, “Isn’t it possible to create «Kvant» for chemists and biologists?”, “The magazine should get thicker,” “Programming! Programming!"...

They wish us: “As soon as they read the conditions of the problem from the Problem Book, they immediately get a headache.”
We are fulfilling much of what we promised our invaders. Articles are constantly published about today's achievements in physics and mathematics, about outstanding scientists, interviews with modern scientists. “Space” materials and science fiction appear regularly in the magazine. Almost every room has materials for game and puzzle lovers. And you can’t complain about the lack of tasks and specifics! There are only up to 100 tasks in each issue, and as many as 6 specific ones were announced in 1990.

Yes... We have fulfilled some promises, but we are still thinking about and discussing some questions (so, unfortunately, the section that has not yet emerged, the conditions are called “For the youngest schoolchildren,” until we have received material with answers to your questions from the editor-in-chief of the journal, Academician Yu. A. Osipyan).

In the future, we intend to maintain the appearance of the magazine. But life moves forward, and, undoubtedly, our magazine will change too. Based on numerous requests from readers, we want to revive the “Information and Programming” section and expand «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren. We plan to publish materials on the topics “physics and biology”, “mathematics, computer science and biology” in the journal.

We hope that every reader will find “their” material in «Kvant».
Our questionnaire will still appear once a quarter. And we still receive letters from you!
