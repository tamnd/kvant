Tasks

1. A milkmaid at the market was selling milk from two barrels, one of which mixed in more milk than the other. When there were 21 liters of milk left in the small barrel and 39 liters in the large barrel, the thrush filled the small barrel with milk from the large one. As a result, the large barrel turned out to be exactly half full. How much milk did she pour and what volume were the barrels?

2. A diameter and a chord perpendicular to it are drawn in a circle. Prove that one of the segments on which the chord divides the diameter will be more than half the chord, and the other will be less.

3. A stone was placed in a glass of water, as a result of which some of the water flowed out. Is the glass lighter or heavier?

4. Solve an arithmetic puzzle. The same letter corresponds to the same numbers, and different numbers correspond to different numbers.

5. The peas are arranged in the form of a square. Show that in this case they can be arranged in the form of two equilateral triangles such that the side of one of them would be equal to the side of the square, and the other - one less.

These problems were suggested to us by: N. Antonovich, A. Khanyan, V. Vyon, G. Galperin and N. Avilov.
