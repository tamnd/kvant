Selected school problems in physics

9th grade
1. Two light carts with masses $m_1$ and $m_2=3m_1$ are connected by a spring (Fig. 1). The spring is compressed and tied by a thread. The thread is burned out, the spring straightens, and the carts move apart in opposite directions. Find the ratio of the initial speeds of the carts and the ratio of the distances covered by the carts before stopping. The friction coefficient for both carts is assumed to be the same.

2. A body with mass $m_1$ inelastically impacts a body at rest with mass $m_2$. Find the fraction of kinetic energy lost in this case.

3. A ball with a mass of $m_1=10$ g and a speed of $v_1=10$ m/s collides with another ball of mass $m_2=20$ g flying towards it. After an absolute elastic impact, the first ball moves in the opposite direction with the same absolute velocity. Determine the speed of the second ball after the collision.

4. A person standing on a smooth ice surface throws a stone in a horizontal direction from a height of $H=1,8$ m. The stone falls on the ice at a distance of $l=9$ m from the point of throwing. Determine the work done by the person throwing the stone.

5. A small body slides externally from a height $H$ along an inclined chute, turning into a “dead loop” of radius $R$ (Fig. 2). At what height will the body fall out of the “loop”? Neglect training.

10th grade
6. A metal circle with radius $R$ rotates around its axis with an angular velocity $\omega$. Determine the reading of a voltmeter connected to contacts, one of which touches the circle in the center, and the other touches the edge. The ratio of an electron's charge to its mass is $\gamma$. Consider the resistance of the voltmeter to be very high.

7. Two fixed conducting parallel rods, located in a horizontal plane at a distance of $l=1$ m from each other, are placed in a uniform magnetic field, the induction of which is equal to $B=0,1$ T and is directed vertically. A metal rod with a mass of $m=0,5$ kg rests on the rods perpendicular to them. Determine the acceleration of this rod if the current flowing through it is $I=50$ A, and the coefficient of friction between the rods is $\mu=0,2$.

8. A conductor with a mass of $m=50$ g and a length of $l=20$ cm is suspended horizontally using two non-web threads tied to its ends. The conductor is in a uniform vertical magnetic field, the induction of which is $B=0,75$ T. What current must be passed through the conductor so that it is disconnected from the equilibrium position and the threads make a vertical angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ each?

9. A uniform magnetic field, the induction of which is $B=10$ mT, is directed perpendicular to a uniform electric field with a direction of $E=17$ kV/m. The ion, passing through an accelerating potential difference $U=15$ kV and to the left in the region occupied by the fields, with a speed perpendicular to both fields, moves uniformly and rectilinearly. Determine the charge to mass ratio for this ion.

10. An electron, accelerated in an electric field with a direction of $U=20$ kV, flies into a uniform magnetic field with an induction of $B=0,1$ T. The electron velocity vector forms an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ with the direction of the magnetic induction vector. Determine the pitch of the helix described by the electron.

11th grade (repetition)
11. On the threads, they can exert a tension of $T=40$ N, and uniformly rotate a stone with a mass of $m=1$ kg in a vertical plane. At what angular speed must the stone be rotated for the thread to break? Thread length $l=1$ m.

12. An object with a mass of $m=200$ g was thrown from the ground onto a balcony located at a height of $H=6$ m. During the flight, the object reached a high temperature $H'=8$ m from the surface of the earth. Determine the work done by the force of gravity when an object flies up, down, and all the way.

13. The volume of gas, under pressure $p=8$ MPa, during the isobaric process increased by $\Delta V=0,5$ m³. In this case, the gases were given an amount of heat of $Q=6$ MJ. How much has the internal energy of the gas changed? Did the gas heat up or cool down?

14. The charges $q=10$ ncl, identical in magnitude but different in sign, are located at two vertices of an equilateral triangle on the side $a=1$ m. Find the direction of the electric field at the third vertex of the triangle.

15. Three resistors with resistances $R_1=R_2=10$ Ohm and $R_3=3$ Ohm are connected to a source with an emf of $\mathcal{E}=10$ V (Fig. 3). The current in the circuit is $I=1$ A. Find the internal resistance of the source and the short circuit current.
