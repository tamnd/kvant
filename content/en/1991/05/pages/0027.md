F1290. Capacitors $C_1$ and $C_2$ (Fig. 2) are connected between two parallel buses. The wiring of the jumper $L$ with the capacitor $C_3$ concerns the busbars. A uniform magnetic field of induction $B$ is directed perpendicular to the plane of the tires. The jumper moves at a constant speed $v$. Find the charge on capacitor $C_3$.

G. Fedotovich

F1291. A circuit of two identical resistors and a capacitor is connected to an alternating voltage network of 36 V, 50 Hz (Fig. 3). The readings of the first ampere are 0.3 A, the second - 0.2 A. Assuming the devices are ideal, find the resistance of the resistors and the capacitance of the capacitor.

A. Zilberman

F1292. A collecting lens with a diameter of 1 cm and a focal length of 10 cm is placed at a distance of 20 cm from the exact light source, and a collecting lens with a diameter of 10 cm and a focal length of 20 cm is placed at a distance of 50 cm from the source. The main optical axes of the lens coincide, the source is located on the axis. At what distance behind the large lens should the screen be placed so that the light spot on it has a minimum outer diameter? Find the diameter of this spot. Will the illumination of the spot change if you remove the small lens?

A. Sashin
