Option 1
1. Three stars with mass $m$ each, distant from other celestial bodies, maintain in their motion the configuration of an equilateral triangle on the side $L$. Find the rotation period of this triangle. The gravity constant $G$ is known.
2. The end of the capitular tube is lowered into water. What is the amount of heat in the capitol? Density of water $q=1000$ kg/m³, surface tension coefficient of water $\sigma=0,072$ N/m, acceleration of free pressure $g=10$ m/s².
3. The two sides of a right triangle are uniformly loaded sticks. In this case, in the center of the triangle - at point $O$ - the direction of the electric field is equal to $E_0$. What will be the magnitude and direction of the field at point $O$ if one of the sticks is removed?
4. The lengths of the sides of a square wire coil increase at a speed of $\Delta a/\Delta t=2$ cm/s. The coil is in a uniform magnetic field with an induction equal to $B=1$ T and perpendicular to the plane of the coil. At $t=0$ the sides of the square were equal to $a=10$ cm. Find the induced emf at the moment $T=2$ s.
5. A point light source is located on the main optical axis of a collecting line with a focal length of $F=8$ cm at a distance of $d=12$ cm from the lens. The lens begins to shift in a direction perpendicular to its main optical axis at a speed of $v_0=1$ cm/s. At what speed will the image of the source move if the source itself remains stationary?
6. In a microcalorie with a heat capacity of $C=1000$ J/K, $m=100$ mg of colablate preparation (atomic mass $A=61$) is placed. When the $^{61}$Co nucleus is distributed, energy $Q=2 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J is released. After a time of $t=50$ min, the temperature of the calorimeter increased by $\Delta t=0,06$ K. Find the half-life of the colablate preparation. Avogadro's constant $N_A=6 \cdot 10^{23}$ m/mol.
