ZIFMSH announces admission

The Correspondence Engineering Physics and Mathematics School (ZIFMSH) announces the admission of students to grades 9, 10 and 11 for the 1991/92 academic year. The main goal of the school is to develop an engineering mindset, help students study mathematics and physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, teach how to correctly formulate solutions, and prepare for entrance exams to higher educational institutions that train engineers.

Those enrolled in ZIFMS are sent methodological developments and test assignments throughout the year; solved assignments evaluate and solved studies. Training is provided in grades 9, 10 and 11. Those who successfully complete the ZIFMS receive a certificate and have an advantage when entering the Leningrad Institute of Railway Engineers named after. academic V. N. Obraztsova (LIIZHT).
