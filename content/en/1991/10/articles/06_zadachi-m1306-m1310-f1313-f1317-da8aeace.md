Tasks
M1306—M1310, F1313—F1317

M1306. Let's call the ratio of the larger side to the smaller side an elongated rectangle. Prove that the elongation of a rectangle B written into another rectangle Π (so that the vertices of B lie one at a time on the sides of Π) is not less than the elongation of Π.

I. Akulits

M1307. Prove that for any natural number n the number $2^{2n} + 2^{2n-1} + 1$ has at least n different prime factors.

N. Vasiliev, V. Senderov

M1308. Three straight lines are given on a plane. Find the set of centers of regular triangles whose vertices lie on the given lines (one on each of the three lines). Explore all cases of the relative position of these lines.

A. Savin

M1309. A triangle is given on a plane. For an arbitrary point M of the plane, the 1st “volley” is determined: the set $H_1(M)$ of three points - the midpoints of the segments connecting M with the vertices of the triangle, then the 2nd salvo: the set $H_2(M)$ of 9 points - the midpoints of the segments connecting the points $H_1(M)$ with the vertices of the triangle, and so on) $k$th salvo $H_k(M)$ consists of $3^k$ points - the midpoints of the segments connecting points $H_{k-1}(M)$ with the vertices of the triangle). Prove that for any ε>0 for a given triangle it is possible to indicate a figure of area ε such that for any point M there is a number $k=k(M, \varepsilon)$, starting from which the weight “salute” $H_k(M)$, $H_{k+1}(M)$, ... will not go beyond the boundaries of this figure.

A. Belov

M1310*. 64 boxers participate in the modern ones. There are 32 pairs of boxers meeting each day (so each one fights one). All boxers have different strengths, and in every fight the strongest one wins. Prove that in 16 days it is possible to determine the place of each boxer. (The schedule for each day is drawn up the evening before and does not change on the day before.)

A. Anjans

F1313. A bumblebee can fly vertically upward at a maximum speed of $v_1$, and downward at a maximum speed of $v_2$. Considering the “traction force” of the bumblebee does not depend on the direction of flight, and the force of air resistance is proportional to the speed of the bumblebee, determine the maximum flight speed of the bumblebee at an angle α to the horizon.

B. Korsunsky

F1314. A model of an airship is blown in a wind tunnel with an air flow at a speed of $v=300$ m/s. At point $A$ (exactly along the axis) the flow velocity becomes zero (Fig. 1). Find the air temperature near this point. The outside temperature is $T=300$ K.

F1315. To measure the intensity ($E$), the electrostatic field in the experiment uses a flat capacitor ($S=100$ cm$^2$, $d=1$ mm), one of the plates of which is motionless (Fig. 2). The other plate is periodically removed to the side using a special mechanical device, sharply reducing the capacitance of the capacitor. Assuming the elements of the measuring circuit (diodes and galvanometer) to be ideal, find the galvanometer readings at $E=1000$ V/cm and the period of mechanical readings $T=0,01$ s. Think about how much it depends on the possible imperfection of the diodes and galvanometer.

Z. Rafailov

F1316. A semiconductor thermistor has a resistance versus temperature dependence of the form $R=R_0(1-\alpha t)$. When a thermistor is heated to a temperature of $t$, it dissipates $P=B(t-t_{\text{окр}})$ power into the environment. What current will flow in the circuit if a source with voltage $U$ is connected to the thermistor?

A. Zilberman

F1317. We connect a loudspeaker and a microphone to the low-frequency amplifier and install them at a distance of 0.5 m from each other. By gradually increasing what is successful, we will ensure that the system “wins.” Estimate the frequency of the sound. What will change if the speaker terminals are swapped? (Note: this experiment can be easily done using a general magnetic background.)

R. Aleksaidrov
