Physics

The description of physics takes into account the teaching of almost three hundred students in grades 9–11 - winners of regional, regional, resource (republics that are part of the Russian Federation) Olympiad, competition «Kvant» and winners of the previous All-Russian Olympiad.

According to established tradition, the Olympics included two rounds. The theoretical round took place on March 26. In each class, clear tasks were proposed, for which clear hours were allotted.*)

On the same day, March 26, the participants’ works were protected, and Jorny began checking them. According to tradition, the zhorni in each city are mainly made up of representatives of local universities, institutes, research institutes, and schools. Representatives of the Central All-Russian Organizing Committee, i.e. those who annually compile sets of problems for the final stage of the Olympics, also worked with them in the zhorny. It should be noted that the work of participants in various olympids has been very good over the years, and now - students at the university. We take this opportunity to invite this year’s graduates to take part in it.

The works were carefully checked by members of the Zhorny at least twice. At the same time, the same task was checked for all participants by the same brigade (usually 2-3 people). In this way, maximum uniformity of scoring criteria is achieved. A correctly solved problem, regardless of the method of solution, was scored 10 points.

During the audit, it was revealed that the first two tasks were relatively simple for the activity. In the third, only a part of the participants agreed that it would be more advisable for the car to first move away from the crossing. Even fewer schoolchildren expressed the correct idea for solving the fourth problem, and only a few people were able to correctly analyze the second part of the problem using the principles of mirror reflection.

It's sad, but there were arithmetic errors in many works. There were some oddities too. Thus, in several (unfortunately, incorrect) solutions to the second problem, “the person showering on the board” was recommended to wear salogas (in one case, galoshes). And in one of the solutions to the third problem, the doctor was asked to immediately get out of the car and, without waiting for the crossing to open, take the white devices to the patient: “Not only is the crossing for some reason closed in front of the ambulance and it’s almost too late to wait for it to open, but the car can also stall because the driver switches the switch!” The decision, however, does not indicate how a doctor should “overcome” a moving pose.

The theoretical round turned out to be the most difficult for tenth graders. Surprisingly, no one completely solved the fourth problem, and only three people received an approximate answer. The remaining three problems were solved by 15 to 60 percent of all schoolchildren.

For students of the same grade, the simplest task is the second and half of the first. At the same time, the second, purely kinematic, part of the first problem - about the waiting time for the start from Mars on the way back - caused great difficulties. The third task, which by its nature was a typical Olympic task, also caused considerable difficulties. The fourth problem, the conditions called by the schoolchildren “about a hole in the focal plane,” in their opinion, is the best problem of the Olympics, although it was not solved much. And one of them even expressed gratitude in his work to the compiler of the tasks: “Now,” he wrote at the end, “I will be much more careful with the Foto camera in sunny weather.”

The next day there is an experimental tour. The preparation of the organizer brought him a lot of trouble - during practical work “on the ground” there were difficulties with the equipment. It wasn’t so easy that ours contained 20-30 sets of necessary equipment. But, to the credit of the organizing committees, all the problems were resolved, and on March 27, the schoolchildren began to carry out the experiment. They were given four hours to solve two problems.
