Tasks

1. In a large and other family, all men have the same surname, and the age difference between any father and son is 22 years. Great-grandson's name is Igor Petrovich. His name is Mitrofan Timofeevich. What was the name of the head of the family in childhood and how old was he, if Seryozha, Igor’s son, was 3 years old?

2. Find all numbers equal to the morning sum of their digits.

3. There are 10 coins: 2 x 2 kopecks, 2 x 3 kopecks, two nickels, two kopecks, one nickel and one nickel. Place them in the circles of the star (see figure) so that the sum of the denominations on each of the fifths is the same.

4. For many years now, Baron Munchausen has been going to the lake every day to hunt ducks. Since August 1, 1991, he tells his cook every day: “Today I shot more ducks than two days ago, but less than a week ago.” What is the maximum number of days the baron can pronounce this phrase? (Don't forget that Munchausen never lies.)

5. Two rays, released from the vertices at the base of the equilateral triangle, cut it into four parts (see figure). It turned out that the area of ​​the red triangle is equal to the area of ​​the blue quadrilateral. Find the angle between these rays.

These tasks were suggested to us by V. Tarakanov, V. Popov, F. Nazarov, A. Kimartsev and V. Proizvolov.
