For what reason
draw water in the well,
if you leave it overnight
in the air of the same well,
getting deeper

Participants of the conversation: visiting guest
and Plugarh

Among the guests was a visitor who was a big fan of well drinking, and our work prepared specially chilled water for him: having lifted a bucket of water from the well, they left this bucket suspended in the same well overnight so that it did not touch the surface of the water, and the water served from this bucket for dinner was better than freshly scooped well. The newcomer was a man with a philological background. He said that he borrowed this from Aristotle, who also gives a corresponding explanation. It consists in the fact that water, previously heated, after cooling turns out to be deeper than before.

The preparation of water for kings is based on this: it is heated until it boils, and then a vessel containing this water is covered with a large amount of snow, and the water is cooled. Likewise, after a hot bath, our body cools down greatly: when hot, it loosens, becomes multi-supported and takes in large amounts of external air, which produces a sharp change in it. Likewise, when water is heated in a well, it cools quickly.

We approved of the guest who remembered everything so carelessly, but we doubted the correctness of the discrepancy given. If there is a block of air surrounding the suspended bucket, how does it heat the water? If it was warmer, then how does the water, on the contrary, cool? It is impossible for the same thing to experience opposite effects from the same thing, without some change occurring.

Since the guest was silent, not knowing what to answer, I said that one should not doubt about the air: the mistake is that it is cold, and most of all - in the depths of the well. Therefore, it is impossible to assume that cold air will harmonize the water.

Rather, this cold air cannot cool the entire thickness of water in the well, but produces such an effect in a small amount of blackened water.

For what reason are guests given dough at the beginning of dinner, and spacious later?

Conversation participants: ded Lampriy
and others

After this speech *) the question arose as to why at the beginning of dinner the guests were given dough, and later - spacious, whereas, taking into account the volume of food eaten, one would expect the opposite. Here, some of us pointed out the method of influence at the table** as the reason for this: during dinner, we occupy such a position so that it is convenient for everyone to stretch out their right hand to the table; and having finished eating, we turn sideways, the calf gives a pointed figure, and we occupy a place that changes, so to speak, linearly, and not in area. Just as the dice, having fallen upright, take up less space than lying down, so each of us at the beginning of dinner leans forward with his face towards the table, and then takes a different position, heading more upward than sideways. Others said that the bedding on the beds gradually compacts and frees up more space, just as leaking shoes gradually wear out and, dividing their pores, provide the norte with more water for movement. And our grandfather, jokingly, said that at each coming there are two dissimilar bosses and managers: first comes the head, who has completed all the prescriptions of tactics, and later Dunis***), who is generally recognized as the best of all strategists. “And so, just as Epaminondas****), when the inexperienced strategists brought the phalanx into a technical position, where it had come to replace it, took command and restored the military formation, so the god, bearing the names Lysias and Horea****), restores our cheerful order and other social relations, after we, driven by a dog’s head, pounced on food in front of each other.”

*) The question was discussed whether it was necessary to invite many guests to dinner.
**) As you know, the Greeks and Romans did not sit at the table, like us, but reclined.
***) God of wine.
****) Veotian commander, founder of the Theban League.
*****) Lizya - “permitter”, “liberator”; Trochee - “round dance”, “dancing”.
