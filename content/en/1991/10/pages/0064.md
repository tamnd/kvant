The second task is space for improvisation. There are many methods for measuring the refractive index of a liquid, many of which were tested by participants. The best method was considered to be lowering a metal ruler into a liquid and simultaneously observing refracted and reflected rays.

As for the assessment of experimental tasks in general, then, as always at the Russian Olympiads, the main components of their assessment were the correct and justified choice of the method of implementation, the high-quality design of the experimental setup, competent execution of the experiment, conducting a series of experiments to obtain more reliable results, correct processing of the results, assessment of the error (the “older” the class, the more it was valued), and an explanation of the results obtained.

The next day, after deciphering the works, each participant (and not only those who wanted to appeal) could familiarize themselves with the evaluation of their work, talk directly with the jury members checking the problems, and sometimes even with the authors of these problems.
