Apparently his brain was working hard. This is what I told him:

— Beyond all expectations, your experiment was a success. Your tissues have the ability to recover completely without any changes. They have become invariant.

Photographs and precise changes show this with full appreciation, although many years have passed, centuries have passed. You are exactly the same as you were years ago.

During this time, accidents happened to you. But any wounds - both minor and deep - are healed on your body without leaving the slightest trace. Your tissues are invariant, and your brain is also invariant, or rather, its cellular structures are invariant. The brain can be compared to an electrical network. Memory is a network, coils, capacitors, and their connections. Consciousness - the process of thinking - is nothing more than the distribution of voltage in this network and the points flowing in it. This process is complex, but it is temporary. In electrical engineering terms, this is a transient process. Memory changes the very structure of the brain network, influencing all subsequent thoughts, that is, the distribution of currents and voltages in the network. The network in your brain doesn't change at all. She is also invariant.

In other words, you can check the analogy between mental processes and the operation of relays and switching devices in your 20th century, compare memory with the connection diagram of individual elements. In the brains of all other people, the connection patterns of elements change over time, elements connect and develop, new connections appear, corresponding to changes in memory. The wiring in your brain doesn't change at all. She is invariant.

Other people can adapt to a new environment, find out where the necessary things are, study the layout of rooms, adapt to the external environment, but you cannot do this, because your brain is invariant. You are bound by habits to this house, it remains exactly the same as the day you tried your remedy. Your house has been kept in perfect order for two hundred years now, renovated so that you can live in it without experiencing any inconvenience. You live here constantly, from the very day your brain became invariant.

Don't think that you don't respond to someone caring for you. You may be the greatest asset in the world. Morning, afternoon and evening - three times a day - you are allowed to visit those few lucky ones who deserve this part or need your help.

I study history. I have come to see the twentieth century through the eyes of an intelligent man of this century. You are an unusually smart, brilliant person. Your mind is the study of man, surpassing you in the power of thought. I would like your mighty brain, connected to a huge observable, to help me in the study of the 20th century. I have come to worship your brain - a fresh source, not blocked, not changed by the passing years, remaining exactly the same as it was in nineteen forty-three.

But this is not about me. Outstanding research is coming to you. They ask you questions, then repeat them with slight changes, and carefully observe your reactions. Moreover, each subsequent experiment is not distorted by your memories of the previous one. When the purpose of your thought is interrupted, there is nothing left in your memory. Your brain is still invariant.
