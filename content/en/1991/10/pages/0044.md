Winners of the competition "Mathematics 6-8"
The first competition “Mathematics 6-8”, held by the magazine «Kvant» together with the Bulgarian magazine for schoolchildren “Mathematics”, has ended. The competition problem was published from No. 9, 1990 to No. 5, 1991. The authors of the problems were both Soviet and Bulgarian mathematicians. The editors summed up the results of the competition based on letters received by our magazine.
