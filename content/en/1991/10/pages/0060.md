8. Convention delegates must select a commission. Each delegate selects 10 people from a list of candidates. Let's call a commission “good” for a delegate if at least one person chosen by the delegate is on the commission. It turned out that for any six delegates there is a commission of two people that is “good”. Prove that it is possible to choose a commission of 10 people that is “good” for all delegates. (12)

2. The altitudes AD, BE, CF of an acute triangle ABC intersect at point H. It is known that the areas of the quadrilaterals AEHF and HECD are equal. Prove that triangle ABC is equilateral. (8)

3. Let $a, b, c$ be positive numbers whose sum is 1. Prove that

7. Three lines $a, b$ and $c$ are given in space. On the lines $a, b, c, a, b, c, a$ the points $T_0, T_1, T_2, T_3, T_4, T_5, T_6$ are taken respectively so that $T_0T_1 \perp b, T_1T_2 \perp c, T_2T_3 \perp a, T_3T_4 \perp b, T_4T_5 \perp c$ and $T_5T_6 \perp a$. It turned out that points $T_0$ and $T_6$ coincide. Prove that the points $T_0$ and $T_3$ also coincide. (9)

8. There is a $n^2 + n$ angle, each of which is composed of two metal rods of length 1, soldered at right angles. From these corners a flat square lattice is processed, consisting of $n^2$ cages $1 \times 1$. Prove that the number of angles whose sides are directed from the vertex of the angle upward and to the right is equal to the number of angles whose sides are directed from the vertex of the angle downward and to the left.
