2. This condition is equivalent to the fact that the harmonic mean of $a$ and $b$ is greater than 2:
$$\left(\frac{a-1+b-1}{2}\right)^{-1} = \frac{2ab}{a+b} > 2.$$
But the arithmetic mean is not less than the harmonic mean:
$$\frac{a+b}{2} \geqslant \frac{2ab}{a+b},$$ because $(a-b)^2 \geqslant 0.$
Hence $a+b > 4$.
3. Dividing these conditions by $a$ and $b$, we obtain
$$a > \frac{a}{b} + 1, \quad b > \frac{b}{a} + 1,$$
where
$$a+b > \frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a} + 2 \geqslant 4.$$
4. Continuing the condition above with the obvious inequality

M1282. Prove that there are no two (other than parallelograms) trapezoids such that the sides of each of them are respectively equal to the base of the other.

In any trapezoid, the difference between the sides is less than the difference between the bases. Indeed, if $E$ is a point on the larger base $CD$ of the trapezoid $ABCD$ such that
$$ABCE — параллелограмм (см. рисунок), то
CD-AB=DE>|AD-AE|.$$
(The last inequality—the fact that each side of a triangle is greater than the difference of the other two—follows from the “triangle inequality,” which states that each side is less than the other two.)
This implies the statement of the problem.
