Aerospace Correspondence School
The correspondence aerospace school (ZAKSH), organized by the All-Union Youth Aerospace Society (VAKO) "Soyuz" and the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT), is conducting a competitive enrollment of students in grades 10 and 11 for the 1992-93 academic year.
ZAKSH manuals were prepared by MIPT employees and researchers and are recognized to help everyone who is going to enter universities, especially aerospace. The school program covers the following sections of science and technology:
— Movement of spacecraft
— Rocketry
— Movement in the atmosphere
— Exploring the Earth from space
— Space Research
— Advanced cosmonautics.
During the year, ZAKS students receive:
- six school tasks, including

theoretical material, a list of references for further acquaintance with the areas of aerospace physics and problems for independent solution;
— “Space chronicle of ZAKSH”, a state from the diary of the most significant events in cosmonautics of the current year and information materials on the history and current state of space research.
The full course of study at ZAKSH is 2 years. Students entering 11th grade have the opportunity to graduate from school using a shortened program.
Competitive enrollment at the school is carried out with the aim of enrolling students in a free form of education. Those who do not pass the competition can become students through a paid form of education. In addition, the school distributes the ZAKSH Space Chronicle of the current year to those interested for a small fee. All necessary information on this issue will be provided subject to the admission committee receiving a decision on the admissions task.
The introductory task for participants in the competition (solving problems in physics) must be completed in Russian

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The tasks published in it are standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we promise to indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to the problem from this issue should be sent no later than January 15, 1992 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». Solutions to problems from different issues of the journal or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 11–91” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1311” or “F1318”. In the column “...sender’s address” please write your first and last name legibly. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope).

The conditions of each original problem proposed for publication are assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “... new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you study. Problems M1311 - M1315 were proposed this year at the Leningrad City Mathematics Option, problem F1320 at the UK Physics Option, and F1322 at the All-Russian Physics Option.
