DENIS' CUBE

Reader A. Tsukar from Novosibirsk asked for a description of the puzzle, which he called “Denis’s cube.” It consists of 64 partially opaque cups, from which you need to fold a completely opaque cube $4 \times 4 \times 4$. The puzzle uses 8 different colored cubes (all shown at the top figure). Each color type is repeated in 8 cups.
Try to solve the following problems of this puzzle:
1. Fold a cube, opaque on the front side.
2. Assemble the cube so that all four colors are equal on the front side.

3. Assemble an opaque cube, by looking at it from one side, you can see all 4 colors in the front table of 4 cups.
4. Prove that with the chosen 8 types of coloring in 4 colors it is impossible to construct a cube all of whose faces would be unambiguous.
Making 64 cubes yourself is quite difficult, but if you number the cubes and colors, you can solve all the problems on a piece of paper. Even more interesting is to write a program and build a cube on the display screen. Finally, try to form and solve similar problems for a 4-dimensional cube. Then, whoever copes with this will receive a special magazine prize «Kvant».
