Introductory task
1. A ball of mass $M$ floats on the surface of a liquid, half immersed in it. There is a cavity inside the ball. Determine the mass of sand that must be poured into the cavity so that the ball floats inside the liquid.

2. A ball is thrown vertically at the top with an initial speed $v_1=25$ m/s. After $t_1=1$ c the second ball is thrown so that both balls reach the ground at the same time. Assuming that the motion occurs along one straight line, find the relative speed of the balls $t_2=1$ s before falling.

3. In a section of the circuit consisting of two series connecting resistors, the resistances of which differ by a factor of 2, power $P$ is released. Determine how the current in a section of the circuit should be changed so that when these resistors are connected in parallel, the power released increases by 2 times.

4. A copper ball weighing $m=200$ g at a temperature of $t_0=300$ °C was lowered into a vessel with water at $t=25$ °C. At the moment when the temperatures of the water and the ball were equal, another similar ball was lowered into the vessel. To what temperature will the water heat up? The specific heat of copper is $c_M=380$ J/(kg K), water $c_B=4200$ J/(kg K). Water mass $M=1$ kg.

5. The gun is mounted on a platform, moving at a constant speed $v$. The speed of the projectile barks relative to the gun $v_0=5v$. Determine the angle at which you need to shoot in the direction the platform is moving so that the projectile reaches the maximum distance. The mass of the projectile is significantly less than the mass of the platform.

6. At rest, a ball hits a ball with twice its mass. As a result of the central inelastic collision, the balls fly apart in opposite directions, and the speed of the impacted ball turns out to be 3 times less than the initial one. Determine what fraction of the kinetic energy transferred to the body.

7. The steam does $A=15$ kJ of work to move a piston with an area of ​​$S=200$ cm². In this case, steam pressure kills from $p_1=2$ MPa to $p_2=0,1$ MPa. Assuming the dependence of pressure on volume to be linear, determine the distance over which the piston will move.

8. Determine how a frozen metal ball of radius $R$ will change when an exact charge $q$ is moved between points located at distances 2R and 4R from the center of the ball.
