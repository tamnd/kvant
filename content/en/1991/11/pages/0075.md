$$\sin \alpha / \sin \beta = 1 / n,$$

The most favorable conditions for exiting through the side face are for a ray running along the cone pattern. The condition for its total reflection from the side face has the form
$$\sin \gamma > \sin \alpha_{\text{np}}.$$
Since $\sin \gamma = \cos \alpha_{\text{np}}$, we find that none of the rays exits through the side face under the condition $\cos \alpha_{\text{np}} > \sin \alpha_{\text{np}}$, i.e., for $\alpha_{\text{np}} < \pi / 4$. From here is
$$n = 1 / \arcsin \alpha_{\text{np}} > \sqrt{2}.$$
