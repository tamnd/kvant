In general, the level of difficulty of theoretical problems was approximately the same as in previous years. None of the problems remained unsolved.

The tasks of the experimental tour were prepared, according to established tradition, by the hosts of the Olympiad - Tashkent University scientists and school teachers. They had to offer a lot of difficulties (everyone who tried to buy, for example, a battery in April, understands well what we are talking about), but in the end everything worked out.

Let us briefly describe the tasks of the experimental round.

In ninth grade, the first task was in optics. It was necessary to determine the refractive index of an unknown liquid (a solution of salt in water, slightly tinted, but the areas did not know this), using a light bulb on a stand, a battery, a thin-walled cylindrical Sta-

a clear glass string, black paper, an elastic band for fastening, a ruler and graph paper. The authors of the problem proposed a beautiful solution - wrapping a glass of black paper and leaving a thin vertical slit, illuminate it with a lamp and observe from above. In this case, two bows are clearly visible - one in the air, it is not refracted, and the other in the water. Next - a little geometry and trigonometry, and the answer is ready. The participants came up with many ways to solve the problem, some of which were very beautiful - for example, using a glass of water as a cylindrical line and positioning this line, the source and the screen so that the line was in the middle, while the geometry turned out to be very simple.

The second task was about training - it was necessary to change the coefficient of the third small piece of plywood on a horizontal surface (an album with a paper cover). Two wooden rulers were also given. There was a rather clear hint: a hole was made on a piece of plywood with a drill, where it was convenient to push the corner of the ruler and only the plywood, tilting the ruler at different angles. It was quite easy to find the angle at which the plywood would no longer slide (jamming) and change it using a second ruler. However, almost no one did this, although many solutions were proposed online, including some quite interesting ones.
