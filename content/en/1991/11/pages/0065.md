1992. It included 14 winners of the All-Union Olympiad in ten classes and two ninth-classics - A. Olkhovets (who greatly outperformed the rest of the ninth-classics) and O. Shpirko (who performed among the ninth-classics).

As always, academician I.K. Kioin was recognized among the special prizes. It was received by Anatoly Olkhovets for the best solution to problems in the experimental round.

After discussion with the team leader, it was decided to publish a physics program for students in the final stage of the All-Union Physics

Czech Olympiad (perhaps this will be done on the pages «Kvant»). It will be somewhat broader than the general school curriculum (from which more and more new sections are gradually being excluded), but will not go beyond the scope of the physics elective.

At the moment, it is still impossible to name with certainty the location of the next All-National Physics Olympiad, but we really hope that it will take place.

We wish success to all Olympiad participants.
