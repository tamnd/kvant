Exercise

1. Some three-digit numbers consist of different numbers in ascending order, and in its name all words begin with the same letter. Another three-digit number, on the contrary, consists of the same digits, but in its name all the words begin with different letters. Find these numbers.

2. There are 6 numbers written on the sides of the cube. Those numbers that are not visible on figure are prime numbers. What numbers are these if it is known that the sums of the numbers on each panel of opposite faces are equal?

3. Several chess players played chess in the park all day long. Since they had only one set of chess, they established the following order of play: the winner of the next game skips the next two games, and the loser skips the next four. How many chess players were there if this event rule succeeded? (In case of a draw, the loser was the one who played with the white pieces.)

4. If in some words you replace the letters with the numbers of these letters in the alphabet, you get the number 222122111121. What word is this?

5. In a convex pentagon ABCDE, angles ABC and CDE are equal to 90°, sides BC, CD and AE are equal to 1, and the sum of sides AB and DE is equal to 1. Prove that the area of ​​the pentagon is equal to 1.

These tasks were suggested to us by I. Akulich, N. Antonovich, S. Tokarev, Kh. Akhmedov and V. Proizvolov.
