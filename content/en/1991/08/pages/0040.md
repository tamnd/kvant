Paper dancers

Now we will set up a theater in which electrified paper dancers will dance.

Take a piece of glass 35-40 cm long and 25 cm wide. Dry the good glass by the stove - it should be completely dry. Place it between the pages of two thick books, as shown in figure, so that the glass lies above the table at approximately a height of 3 cm. From thin tissue paper, cut out figures of the same size as those drawn in our upper part figure. Cut out whoever you want: people, dogs, frogs. These figures should be no more than 2 cm in height.
