You don’t have to have a lot of observability to notice that this problem is part, or rather a limiting case, of Pascal’s theorem.

Professional mathematicians, who are also involved in the organization of mathematical Olympiads, first draw beautiful and interesting problems from their scientific activities, transferring special cases of serious mathematical theorems to school soil, adapting to it certain lemmas, which in large numbers arose in the proof of almost any serious mathematical theorem. True, geometric examples are not too frequent; Serbian mathematics is still very far from school geometry. (This phrase should in no way be considered as a reproach to geometry.) Therefore, I will limit myself to one example, perhaps not very bright and characteristic.

There is such a theorem “about the zigzag”. Given two circles (possibly in space). It is known that there is a set of 2n points $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_{2n}$ such that the odd-numbered points are located on one circle, the even-numbered points on the other, and $A_1A_2 = A_2A_3 = \dots = A_{2n}A_1$. Then there are infinitely many such sets of 2n points, and any point of the first circle can be taken as the point $A$, and the distance between successive points will be the same as that of the original set of points.

I don't know an elementary proof of this theorem. However, its partial cases can be used as elementary problems. For example:

Problem 12. Two circles with radii $R$ and $r$ and distance between centers $a$ are given on the plane. Find the side of a rhombus whose two opposite vertices lie on one circle, and the remaining two on the other.

This problem is quite simple, so I will limit myself to just indicating the answer: $\sqrt{R^2 + r^2} - a^2$.

Evening physical school
at Moscow State University

The Evening Physics School (VPS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces enrollment of students in grades 9, 10 and 11 for the next academic year.

The main goal of the VFS is to help students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum. Classes are held in the evening in the form of lectures given once a day of the week and daily seminars. In addition, students will be able to visit the faculty’s scientific laboratories and lectures by leading scientists to become familiar with the main directions of modern physics.

For those interested, organizational elective classes in mathematics and the basics of computer science.

Admission to the VFS is based on the results of the owner, which will be held starting from September 26. To enroll in the school, you must personally fill out an application at the Komsomol committee of a physicist at Moscow State University (and attach two factors measuring $3 \times 4$ cm). Applications can be submitted from September 6 to September 22, every day, except Sunday, from 16 to 18 hours. Working youth are enrolled in the competition.

Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of the VFS.
