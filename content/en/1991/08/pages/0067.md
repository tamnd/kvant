L. TASHNET

I am a practical person, not like my sons, although they are smart guys. But they have enough intelligence, you can’t say anything. If they had not been born twins and had this intelligence been acquired by one and not two, then all the scientists in the world, taken together, would not hold a candle to this one. Well, even now they don’t have to wish for it - both are excellent engineers and have the best account in their company. A large company, engaged in electric peaking and something else of the same kind. I won’t name it, because the kids won’t like it. I know them well. Who would know if not me? I expressed them myself, and this, let me tell you, was not at all so easy: their mother died when they were not yet nine, and I did not marry a second time. Bot had to take care of business and make sure that everything in the house was going as it should be and there was a real eye on the guys. Well, yes, they were always good boys...

Larry has his own hobby - lasers. Well, this is a way of sending light. I don’t know how they work, because I didn’t study in colleges, I didn’t have time for that. And Leo is an amateur magician, and, I must say, he does it cleverly. Well, together they performed many different tricks and performances. Our basement is chock full of all sorts of equipment. This is what I wanted to talk about.

Larry came up with a device for Leo to create optical illusions. Well, you know: it’s like you’re seeing something that’s actually not there at all. Somehow the mirrors adapt. And Larry adapted lasers and began to make what seemed to be paintings, but not paintings at all, but holograms - that’s what he called them. The negative shows a jumble of dots and all sorts of curlicues, but if you project it onto a screen, it looks as if you could walk around this object. Volumetric image. Well, it’s hard to explain, you have to see it with your own eyes. A typical picture is flat and looks the same no matter which side you look at it from, but
The logo looks as if it is not a painting, but the whole real thing, and if you go to the right or left, what you see is completely different from what is in front.

So, it means that Larry made our Leo a device for hologram illusions. He projected the image not onto the screen, but directly into the air. Using mirrors. They called me and showed me. It's simply impossible to believe! There's a real box floating in the air, or a waza with fruit, or a bouquet - just whatever you want. Even a bunch of small coins. And that's what gave me the idea.

“The Primos are like real ones,” I say. “It’s a pity that you can’t preserve them forever!” They should spray it with plexiglass, or something - well, like preserving flowers.

I remembered about the creatures that are sold in shops for tourists - all sorts of things in fancy cubes, like glass.

The guys rolled off like that.

“Dad,” they say in unison (they always speak in unison), “this is just an illusion.” This is not real money. They're not really here.

- Real - not real... And what is “real”, may I ask you? “I chew them, and you see them,” I say. “We could at least testify in court under oath that we saw a handful of Primo change in the air.” Isn't it?

And then I say as a joke... Well, not really as a joke, because fun is fun, but since you've already had the opportunity to earn a dollar or two, why would you miss it? Bot, that's what I'm saying:

- You guys are so smart, so why don’t you come up with a way so that this illusion does not disappear even when you turn off your laser? Even if it’s a small thing, it’s still money.

Well, then they began to explain to me that light waves have no mass and all sorts of wisdom that would make the devil himself break his leg. But I still understood one thing:

- Since light waves, which, in your opinion, are intangible, can paint such pictures that it seems as if there is something here, then you need it to really be here

The story will be reprinted from the collection of science fiction stories “Practical Image” (M., Mir, 1974).
