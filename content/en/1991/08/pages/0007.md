Magnetoelastic interaction

An important place among metals is occupied by those that have magnetic properties. In them, as in ordinary metals, induction and deformation interactions operate. The matter is complicated, however, by the fact that in magnets, in addition to the reaction of electrons and ions to the electric field of an electromagnetic wave, it is also necessary to take into account the effect of the magnetic field on elementary atoms as well. As a result of the interaction of electrons, ions and elementary currents with an electromagnetic wave and with each other, quite a few different EMAT mechanisms are realized in magnets. These include, for example, sound generation mechanisms associated with the mixing of domain boundaries and rotation of the magnetization vector in domains. It also turned out that the transition of a metal from one magnetic state to another is accompanied by a gigantic (gain gain compared to inductive interaction!) return of EMAT efficiency. The qualitative effect of the giant transformation can be explained by the fact that the position of the elementary atoms of currents during the transition of metal from one device to another is not determined in any way, and therefore the electromagnetic wave is able to strongly swing these currents, and they, in turn, make them oscillate.
