Stearic motor

To make this motor, we do not need steam, electricity, rolled air, or gasoline. For this we only need... a candle.

Heated two pins and stuck their heads into the candle on both sides, in the middle, perpendicular to the wick. This will be the axis of our engine; Place the candle with the ends of the pins on the edges of two glasses and get better balance. If you now cut the candle at both ends, a drop of stearin will fall into one of the plates placed under the ends of the candle. The balance will be disrupted, the other end of the candle will overflow and fall; at the same time, a few drops of stearin will drain from it, and it will become lighter than the first end; it will rise up, the first end will drop, drop a drop, it will become lighter, and... our motor begins to work with all its might; gradually the candle's vibrations will increase more and more.

soap bubbles and films

Candle, go out!

Very large and beautiful bubbles can be blown from a glass or tin funnel.

Huge bubbles, up to 30 cm in diameter!

The funnel should be lowered into a wide vessel in order to thoroughly moisten its edges in the soap solution.

Carefully, holding the funnel vertically, lift it and blow, taking breaks, each time pinching the narrow end of the funnel with your finger. Otherwise, the tension force of the soap film will expel the air from the ball. And the film removes this air with considerable force. This is very easy to verify. Bring the narrow end of the funnel to the hot candle and say:

- Candle, go out!

The flame will fade, fade, then go out.
