XIV Moscow
economic-mathematical
Olympics

In March 1991, at the Faculty of Economics of Moscow State University, with the help of employees, graduate students and students of the Department of Mathematical Methods of Economic Analysis, the XIV Moscow Economic and Mathematical Olympada was held for schoolchildren in grades 9-11.

The Olympada consists of two rounds. However, their best would be called two attempts: any student could take part in both the first and second.type. Thus, the guys had the opportunity to “shoot in” and train.
In each class, the Olympada condition contained 4-5 questions and tasks. Among them were both purely economic and purely mathematical - however, most of them lay at the intersection of the two sciences. Accordingly, the evaluation criteria were somewhat different from those that were promised to be accepted at the math competitions.
