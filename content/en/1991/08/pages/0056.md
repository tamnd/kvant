national stage of the international competition “Together to Mars!” completed.*) From June 17 to 21, the final was held in Moscow, the winners of the composition were determined and awards were given. Before reporting on the most interesting works, we will tell you how the competitive selection took place.

In total, the Organizing Committee received about 350 works (not as many as we expected!). Depending on the topic, they were examined at the Moscow Aviation Institute, Moscow State Technical University. N. E. Bauman, at the State Astronomical Institute named after. P.K. Sternberg. Each work submitted for consideration was accompanied by a scorecard in which three specialists entered their grades. As a result, the Organizing Committee was presented with lists of authors recommended for participation in the final part of the competition. After discussing all the candidates at a joint meeting of the jury and the Organizing Committee, which was held under the presentation of Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences M. Ya. Marov, taking into account the experts, the list of participants in the final was approved.

The financiers gathered near Moscow at the Olymppets Center for International Cooperation. Here new challenges awaited them. On the first day, all final participants defended their projects in sections. 19 of the best were identified, who the next day made presentations to experts at the Moscow Aviation Institute and Moscow State Technical University. On the third day in the evening, the general jury, headed by the representative of the Scientific and Technical Council of the USSR Space Science Federation, Professor V.P. Senkevich, conducted an interview with the authors of the most protected stage: Ira Litvyakova from Vitebsk, Sergey Stenyuk from Donetsk and Maxim Shtanev from Kharkov. Their work was sent to the Planetary Society of the USA. Now, based on an analysis of all submitted works, the international jury will determine 20 participants in the final stage in Washington.

Participants in the finals in Moscow were loaded with diplomas; the authors of the nine best works were presented with valuable gifts and honorary diplomas from the USSR Space Federation. The financiers met with scientists and designers of space technology,

The result of such activity could be a multi-variant posture project for the exploration and development of Mars.

A few words about the works that have received recognition from the site.

A simple and clear model was presented by Ira Litvyakova. When flying to Mars, a situation may arise when a comet of interest for scientific research eats up not far from the ship. In such a case, it is necessary to have on board the ship an automatic device capable of merging with a comet and landing on the surface of its nucleus. According to Ira’s calculations, a very low speed is needed to take off from the surface of the nucleus of a non-sick comet. This speed can be imparted to the device by a compressed spring. The work provides a list of comets passing pergels in 1994-2000, and analyzes the probability of encountering a comet. To continue this work, it is necessary to more closely analyze the trajectories of comets and the possibility of merging with it a probe sent from an international ship.

The work of Pavel Zaychuk (Krasnoirsk) showed that approximate calculation of trajectories can be carried out not only by specialists. Pavel gives a calculation of various flight options to Mars and comes to the conclusion that it is better to assemble the Mars expeditionary complex in a high near-Earth orbit.
