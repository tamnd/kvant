Individual interaction

No matter how well a metal reflects an electromagnetic wave, there is always some, even a very small, part of the wave energy that penetrates its surface layer. This is precisely what all mechanisms of non-contact sound excitation are based on. The simplest of these mechanisms, called induction interaction, is comparator, when the substance under study is in a strong magnetic field. In the absence of a magnetic field, electrons and ions in the surface layer of the metal, under the influence of the field of an electromagnetic wave, move, as shown in figure 3, and in opposite directions, losing from time to time their selective kinetic energy during collisions. In this case, part of the energy of the electromagnetic wave penetrating into the conductor turns into a body, and no sound generation occurs.
