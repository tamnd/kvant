1. Three sociological surveys were conducted in Moscow:
   — 1000 people were surveyed from 8 to 9 am near the Universitet metro station;
   — 2,000 people were surveyed in the evening near the Sportnaya metro station;
   — 800 numbers were chosen at random from the phone book and asked questions over the phone.
Which survey results do you think most accurately reflect Moscow's opinion?
