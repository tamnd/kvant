“Don’t be upset, guys,” I tell them. “I have you.” So what else do I need? I have provided for my old age well.
Here I even cried a little - with joy.

Well, of course, there was no more talk about a patent. And the guys immediately dismantled the device. We don't talk about this invention anymore. But when there is a lot of snow, the guys smile at me, and I smiled back at them. Because all my neighbors envy me: the paths in my yard are always cleared, and none of them have ever seen me take up a shovel. We calculated that after a normal snowfall, two coins are not enough, but ten is a lot. But three will be just right. Place coins at regular intervals and shoot with a blowgun almost without missing. What good is an invention if you can’t get anything out of it, right? I'm a practical person.
