Quests

1. There are 101 candies in a bag. The Kid and Carlson play this game: in turns - the first Kid and the second Carlson - they take from 1 to 10 candies from the bag. When all the candies have been taken, the players count the number of candies taken. If these numbers are relatively prime, Malysh wins, if not, Carlson wins. Who will win this game and how should they play?

2. Solve an arithmetic puzzle. Identical letters correspond to the same numbers, different ones - different numbers.

3. The sums of numbers in rhombuses follow a certain pattern. Find it and prove the validity of your hypothesis.

4. In triangle $ABC$, segment $AM$ is the median, point $N$ is its midpoint, line $BN$ intersects base $AC$ at point $K$. In what ratio does the point $K$ divide the base of the triangle?

5. In ten days the pirate Erem is able to drink a barrel of rum,
And the pirate at Emelya
It would take two weeks.
In how many days will the Pirates finish the rum, acting together?

The first four problems were proposed to us by K. Kokhas, L. Mochalov, V. Proizvolov and V. Chuchin. We took Serdey Satin’s poem from the magazine “Crocodile” (No. 34, 1990).

We continue to publish popular science articles by the famous Soviet writer Aleksadr Volkov. You can read a story about this writer in the previous issue of the magazine.
