We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After forming a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to the problem from this issue should be sent no later than October 15, 1991 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». Solutions to problems from different issues of the journal or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 8—91” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1296” or “F1303”. In the column “...sender’s address”, please write your first and last name separately. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope).

The conditions of each original problem proposed for publication are assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you are a teacher. Problems M1296—M1300 and F1303—F1307 were proposed at the final stage of the XXV All-Union Olympiad in Mathematics and Physics.
