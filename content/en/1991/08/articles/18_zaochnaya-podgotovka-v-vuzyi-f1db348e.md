CORRESPONDENCE PREPARATION FOR UNIVERSITIES

The Association of Teachers of Capital Universities “MOSCOW LYCEUM”, which has extensive experience in educational, methodological and tutoring work, offers original methods of preparation for entrance exams to ALL HIGHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS OF THE COUNTRY. The effectiveness of our programs is confirmed by many years of practice.

MATHEMATICS, PHYSICS, LITERATURE, RUSSIAN LANGUAGE, CHEMISTRY, BIOLOGY, HISTORY, SOCIAL SCIENCE, GEOGRAPHY, COMPUTER SCIENCE, ENGLISH AND GERMAN LANGUAGES

— You can choose one or several subjects, for each of which you will be offered a cycle of 10 educational and methodological developments with test tasks.
If you doubt the correctness of your choice of future profession, the system of PSYCHOLOGICAL TESTS of the MOSCOW LYCEUM cooperative will help you. Based on the test results, psychologists will send you individual recommendations for your profession.

Detailed information about educational programs, procedures and conditions for distance learning is sent FREE OF CHARGE. Applications indicating the items you need are sent to:

125190, Moscow, PO Box 140, MOSCOW LYCEUM cooperative.

Don't forget to include two envelopes with YOUR address in your letter.
