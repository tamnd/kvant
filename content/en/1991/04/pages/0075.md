Part 2
(5 points for each task, total - 20 points)

1. At figure 11 there is a graph of the height of the rocket $y$ versus time. Rocket movements are turned on at moment $t=0$ and turned off at moment $t=t_0$. Draw graphs of the speed and acceleration of the rocket versus time.

2. Two bodies, whose masses are $M_1$ and $M_2$, are connected by a light thread thrown over a block, rotating without friction (Fig. 12). a) Draw the forces acting on the first body. b) Draw the forces acting on the second body. c) Our equations that relate the forces acting on bodies with their accelerations. d) Solve these equations and find the total acceleration of the bodies.

3. A body of mass 5 kg moving in the positive direction of the $X$ axis begins to be acted upon by a force directed in the same direction, and its magnitude changes as shown in figure 13. a) How much work will this force do to move the body from a point with coordinate $x=0$ to a point with coordinate $x=8$ m? b) If at the point with coordinate $x=0$ the speed of the body was 4 m/s, then what will the speed be at the point with coordinate $x=8$ m?

4. A body of mass $M$, tied to the end of a weightless thread, moves in a circle of radius $R$ on a smooth horizontal table. It has moment momentum $L_0$ and kinetic energy $K_0$. Suppose we slowly pull the thread through a small hole in the center of the table until the radius of the circle decreases to $R/2$. a) If at $r=R/2$ the angular momentum of the body is equal to $L$, then what is the ratio $L/L_0$? Explain your answer. b) If at $r=R/2$ the kinetic energy of the body is $K$, then what is the ratio $K/K_0$? Explain your answer.
