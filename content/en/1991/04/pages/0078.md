(4 points for each correct choice answer, total - 60 points)
1. A string of length $L$, fixed at the ends, cannot vibrate with the wavelength: a) $L$; b) $2L$; c) $L/2$; d) $2/3L$; e) $4L$.
2. The wave in the string propagates at a speed of 4.4 m/s; wavelength 2 m. The wave crosses the boundary and propagates in another string; the frequency and wavelength remain the same. In this case, the wave speed in the second string is equal to: a) 1.46 m/s; b) 2.2 m/s; c) 2.9 m/s; d) 4.4 m/s; e) 6.6 m/s.
3. The organ pipe $x$, open on both sides, is 2 times longer than the limited pipe $y$, which is closed at one end. The ratio of the main frequencies of air vibrations in pipes $x$ and $y$ is equal to: a) 1:1; b) 1:2; c) 2:1; d) 1:4; e) 4:1.
4. Determine which combination of $l$ and $m_l$ is not allowed for the hydrogen atom in the state with $n=2$: a) 0.0; b) 1.0; c) 1, -1; d) 1, +1; e) 2.2.
5. In a silicon lattice, one atom of silicon must be replaced with an atom of another element so as to obtain a $n$-type impurity semiconductor. The number of valence electrons of the impurity must be equal to: a) 0; b) 1; c) 3; d) 4; e) 5.
6. The radioactive isotope of carbon $^{14}$С has a half-life of 5730 years. Carbon dating of the bird indicates that 75% of the original carbon in its bones has already been destroyed. Since the death of the bird: a) 1433; b) 2865; c) 5730; d) 11.460; e) 22.920 years.
7. The maximum thermal radiation at a temperature of 3K occurs at a wavelength equal to: a) 1 m; b) 1 cm; c) 1 mm; d) 1 µm; e) 1 nm.
8. If you double the width of a single slit, then the intensity and size of the central maximum changes as follows: a) the intensity will double, the width of the maximum will not change; b) the intensity will double, the width of the maximum will be halved; c) the intensity will increase by 4 times, the width will decrease by 2 times; d) the intensity will increase by 4 times, the width will increase by 2 times; e) the intensity will not change, the width will decrease by 2 times.
9. Let an arbitrary container in the shape of a thin lens (Fig. 23) be filled with air ($n=1$), water ($n=1,3$) or carbon disulfide ($n=1,6$). If only the walls of the container are touched, then it can turn off the parallel beam of light, provided that: a) it is filled with air and immersed in air; b) it is filled with air and immersed in water; c) it is filled with water and immersed in carbon disulfide; d) It is filled with carbon disulfide and immersed in carbon disulfide.
10. The space method has a half-life of 2 μs at rest. If it approaches the Earth at a speed equal to 0.8 the speed of light, its half-life, as modified by that observed from Earth, is: a) 0.2 μs; b) 0.87 µs; c) 2 µs; d) 4.6 µs; e) 20 µs.
11. In relativistic motion, the total energy of the particle is equal to twice the rest energy. The momentum of such a particle is equal to: a) $mc$; b) 1/2 $mc$; c) $\sqrt{2} mc$; d) $1/\sqrt{3} mc$; e) $\sqrt{3} mc$.
12. The magnitudes of the momenta in the ground state and in the first excited state of a particle moving in a potential well (Fig. 24) are equal to: a) $h/L$ and $2h/L$; b) $h/2L$ and $2h/3L$; c) $h/2L$ and $h/L$; d) $2h/L$ and $4h/L$; e) $2h/L$ and $3h/L$.
13. A particle with mass $m$ moves in a potential well of length $L$, as in the previous problem. The probability of finding a particle in the range from $x=0$ to $x=L/2$ in the first excited state, compared with the probability of its being in the same interval in the ground state: a) less; b) the same; c) more.
14. The primary quarks that form a proton are: a) $udd$; b) $uds$; c) $uus$; d) $uud$; e) usu.
15. The electromagnetic field can be considered as a result of the symmetry of the physics equation under the following transformations of the wave function: a) displacement; b) rotation; c) reflection; d) phase; e) vertical.
