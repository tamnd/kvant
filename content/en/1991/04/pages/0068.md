Option 1

8. The hypotenuse of a right angled triangle is 4 greater on one side and 2 greater on the other side. Determine the area of ​​the circle circumscribed about this triangle.

9. In a regular quadrilateral primid, the plane angle at the vertex is $\alpha$. Find the angle of inclination of the side face to the base of the primid.

1. Between what connecting integers does the number $\sqrt{(3-\sqrt{26})^2}$ lie?
