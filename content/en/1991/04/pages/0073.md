20. The satellite is in orbit above the Earth's atmosphere. Which statement is true: a) the resulting force acting on the satellite is zero; b) the velocity and acceleration vectors are parallel to each other; c) the satellite speed is constant; d) the satellite accelerates towards the center of the Earth; e) without a source of energy, the satellite will fall to Earth.

21. The work required to keep a body moving is proportional to: a) the speed of the body; b) acceleration; c) square of speed; d) square of acceleration; e) potential energy of the body.

22. A weightlifter applies a force of 1500 N to lift a barbell to a height of 2 m from the floor in a time of 5 s. The second time he lifts the same barbell in 10 seconds. The completed work in the second case, compared to the first: a) 4 times less; b) 2 times less; c) the same; d) 2 times more; e) 4 times more.

23. A body at rest can do work if: a) its potential energy is positive; b) its potential energy is negative; c) it can move so that its kinetic energy decreases; d) it can move so that its potential energy decreases; e) none of the above is true.

24. A projectile weighing 2.5 kg is launched with an initial speed of 20 m/s at an angle α to the horizontal. The maximum height is: a) ≈10 m; b) ≈20 m; c) ≈40 m; d) ≈80 m; e) cannot be said - depends on the angle α.

25. Which of the graphs (see Fig. 8) correctly reflects the dependence of the potential energy U of the ball on its y coordinate?

26. Which of the graphs (see Fig. 9) correctly reflects the dependence of the potential energy U of a spring on its extension x?

27. The shell explodes in the air into several pieces. The total impulse immediately after the explosion: a) is less than the impulse immediately before the explosion, since part of the explosion energy was converted into sedge impulse; c) unknown if the mass and speed of each sedge are unknown; d) there can be either more or less impulse before the explosion - depending on the speed of the sedges; e) none of the above is true.

28. The unit of squared momentum divided by the unit of mass is: a) newton; b) joule; c) watt; d) meter; e) meters per second.
