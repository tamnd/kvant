- Well, I understand your arguments. And let me repeat them, so to speak, in general terms. You are considering two theories: $P$ and $Q$. Theory $P$ (arithmetic was taken as it) is beyond doubt and is considered independent and infallible. Theory $Q$ is a new theory defined by a list of axioms. We need to obtain a guarantee of the consistency of the theory $Q$. For this purpose, the following technique is used: from the understandable theory $P$, as from “building material”, an attempt is made to construct a model of theory $Q$, i.e., a scheme in which all the axioms of theory $Q$ are satisfied; if this can be done, then the consistency of the theory $Q$ is proven.
