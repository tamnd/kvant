The closing of the Festival with the presentation of prizes (including a subscription to «Kvant» and Yunost) ended with the performance of the Krasnodar variety show “Teatra Premiere”.
The kindest words are heard by the organizers of the Krasnodar Festival and, first of all, by associate professors of the Faculty of Mathematics of Kuban State University I.V. Fedorenko and S.L. Krupetsky (both of them are people's representatives) and the dean of the Faculty of Advanced Studies V.A. Lazarev. Having started work on the organization literally a month before the opening of the Festival, they managed to hold it at, unfortunately, the highest possible level, having thought through everything, from such a small thing as a souvenir cup with the Festival symbol for each of more than 200 guests, to placing participants in the best hotels in the city. The Festival participants were greeted by the Chairman of the Regional Council N.I. Kondratenko, the Rector of the Kuban University, Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences V.A. Babeshko, and the heads of other regional and city organizations. A special role in the financing and holding of the Festival was played by the Krasnodar city and its chairman V. A. Samoilenko, who at the closing invited all participants to the second Festival in 1991.
The team leaders, discussing the results of the Festival, were unanimous: this is a good start, which should be followed by an extension. And it already follows - below we print a promise about the next Krasnodar Festival.
V. Dubrovsky

II Festival of Young Mathematicians in Krasnodar

The Association “Mathematical Education of Kuban” and the magazine «Kvant» plan to hold the II Festival of Young Mathematicians at the end of October 1991 in the Krasnodar Territory. In the Festival program:
— scientific conference for schoolchildren,
- tournament of mathematical battles,
— lectures for schoolchildren on non-standard sections of elementary mathematics,
- KVN,

— lectures and debates on current problems of modern science,
- computer competitions,
- extensive cultural program.
Teams (10 people, including the leader) of mathematical schools, classes, groups, sections, pioneer houses, etc. are invited to participate in the Festival. The composition of participants will be formed according to preliminary reservations.
appearances, which must be sent before May 1, 1991 to the address:
350640, Krasnodar, GSP, st. K. Libknesta, 149, Kuban State University, FPPC ONO, Festival organizing committee.
Inquiries by phone in Krasnodar: 33-85-38, Secretary of the Organizing Committee - Yulia Petrovna Korneeva.
The time of the Festival in Kuban is the time of golden autumn, good weather, rich harvest, fruits, fairs. More foreign teams are expected to participate in the work of the Festival. Participants of the Festival will receive memorable souvenirs and prizes.
