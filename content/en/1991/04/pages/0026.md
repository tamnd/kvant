Tasks
M1276 — M1280, F1283 — F1287

M1276. For a given chord MN of the circle, triangles ABC are considered, the bases of which are the diameters AB of this circle, not intersecting MN, and the sides AC and BC pass through the ends M and N of the chord MN. Prove that the altitudes of all such triangles ABC, dropped from vertex C to side AB, intersect at one point.

E. Kulyanin

M1277. Prove that for any positive numbers a₁, a₂, ..., aₙ the following inequality holds:
$$\sqrt{\frac{a_1+a_2}{a_3}} + \sqrt{\frac{a_2+a_3}{a_4}} + \dots + \sqrt{\frac{a_{n-1}+a_n}{a_1}} + \sqrt{\frac{a_n+a_1}{a_2}} \geq n\sqrt{2}.$$
L. Kurlyandchik

M1278. There are n real numbers x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ, satisfying the conditions x₁+x+...+xₙ=0 and x₁²+x₂²+...+xₙ²=1. Prove that xᵢxⱼ≤-1/n for unknown i and j.

E. Tables

M1279. On the Oxy plane there are n non-intersecting squares on side 1, the sides of which are parallel to the axes. It is known that any two of them can be intersected by a straight line parallel to one of the axes. Prove that it is possible to intersect some n-2 squares with one straight line parallel to the axis.

A. Anjans

M1280*. Prove that during the period of the decimal fraction 1/3¹⁰⁰ there were a) at least 20 identical digits in a row;
b) sequence of numbers 123456789.

A. Korobov

F1283. A man with a heavy cart stands in front of a closed barrier. He needs to get to the store as quickly as possible, located at a distance of 300 m from the barrier. The maximum force with which a person can act on the cart is 500 N, the maximum speed of the cart is 5 m/s, the mass of the loaded cart is 2000 kg. It is known that the barrier will open in exactly 30 seconds. In what minimum time can a person reach the load to the store?

A. Korshkov, N. Korshkova

F1284. A large vessel of mass m is filled with water through a small hole in the bottom of the vessel (Fig. 1). In order to pump water of mass M into a vessel, work A had to be done. The hole opens and water begins to flow out. However, they raise the vessel so that the upper boundary of the water in it remains at the same height relative to the ground. How much work will have to be done before the container is empty?

H. Kuzma
