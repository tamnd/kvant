1. A parallel beam of light entering the human eye, accommodated to infinity, creates an actual inverted image of the object on the retina. The focal length of the eye is 2.5 cm. In order to be able to read a book while holding it at a distance of 30 cm from the eye, the focal length of the eye must change so that the image of the book on the retina is clear.
a) Find this focal length.
b) Will the radius of curvature of the ocular line increase or decrease? c) The person needs glasses only for reading. Is he nearsighted or farsighted? d) What lines are needed to correct human vision in case c)? Draw the corresponding path of the rays.
2. The sun moves in a circular orbit around the center of our galaxy. The orbital speed of the Sun is 220 km/s and the orbital radius is $R=2,7 \cdot 10^{20}$ m. a) Assuming that the mass of the galaxy is distributed spherically symmetrically, estimate the mass contained within the solar orbit. Express your answer in solar masses $(2 \cdot 10^{30}$ kg).
b) If the Sun were twice as far from the center of the galaxy and rotating at the same speed, how much mass would be contained within its orbit with a radius of 2R?
