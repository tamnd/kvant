2. A piece of ice weighing $m=2$ kg at a temperature $t=-20^\circ\text{C}$ was heated, giving it an amount of heat $Q=1$ MJ. Specific heat capacity of ice $c_\pi=2,1$ kJ/(kg K), specific heat of melting of ice $\lambda=330$ kJ/kg, specific heat capacity of water $c_\beta=4,2$ kJ/(kg K), specific heat of vaporization of water $L=2,3$ MJ/kg. a) Determine the set temperature. 6) Can ice, water and water vapor coexist at the same time at the same temperature?

3. An ammeter with an internal resistance of $R_A=2$ Ohm connected to a current source showed a current of $I=5$ A; a voltmeter with an internal resistance of $R_V=15$ Ohm connected to the same source showed $U=12$ V. a) Determine the short circuit current of the source. 6) Approximately what resistance needs to be connected to this source to create a short circuit?

4. Homogeneous magnetic and electric fields are perpendicular to each other. Electric field voltage $E=0,5$ kV/m, inductive magnetic field $B=1$ mT. Electron charge $e=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, mass $m=9 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg. a) At what speed and in what direction must an electron fly in order to move in a straight line? 6) can an electron move rectilinearly only in one electric or one magnetic field?

5. A beam of parallel rays falls from air onto the surface of water at an angle $\alpha=60^\circ$. The width of the beam in the air is $d=10$ cm. The indicator of water filling is $n=4/3$. a) Determine the width of the beam in the water. 6) Will light also be reflected?
