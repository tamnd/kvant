29. A rectangular block moves along a smooth horizontal surface, turning into a “dead loop” (Fig. 10). The block passes positions 1, 2, 3, 4, then 1 again and returns to the horizontal plane. In position 3: a) its total mechanical energy is minimal; b) the resulting force is zero; c) the block stops instantly; d) the resulting force is directed upward; e) neither answer is correct.

30. Two balls of different masses, made of elastic materials, are suspended at one point on light threads of the same length. The lighter ball is moved to the side, released, and it ends up with the second ball. Determine which quantities will be conserved throughout the motion of the balls: a) kinetic energy; b) impulse; c) angular momentum; d) all of the above; e) none of the above.

31. Add up which linear quantity has the same dimensions as the angular quantity: a) displacement; b) speed; c) acceleration; d) kinetic energy; e) none of the above.

32. Homogeneous solid bodies, one spherical in shape and the other cylindrical, having the same masses and radii, are rolled down an inclined plane. Both bodies roll without slipping, and we see that: a) the ball reaches the base of the inclined plane first, since it has less area in contact with the surface; b) the ball reaches the base first, since it “captures” less rotational energy; c) the cylinder reaches the base first, flat rotational energy; d) they reach the base at the same time; e) neither answer is correct.

33. A person whose arms are pressed to his sides rotates on a light disk (friction in the axis is low). When he extends his arms perpendicular to his body: a) his moment of inertia decreases, and therefore decreases; b) its moment of inertia decreases and therefore decreases; d) its moment of inertia decreases and therefore decreases; e) neither answer is correct.

34. The vector of the angular momentum (angular momentum) of the Earth, due to its daily rotation, is directed: a) tangentially to the equator to the east; b) tangentially to the equator to the west; c) due south; d) due north; e) always towards the Sun.

35. Determine where the body weight will be lowest: a) at the equator; b) at the North Pole; c) in the center of the Earth; d) 2000 millih above the Earth's surface; e) the cocked body weight is the same.

36. The body is raised above the Earth's surface to a height equal to two Earth radii. Then: a) its mass will decrease, but everything will remain unchanged; b) the mass will decrease and everything will decrease; c) the mass remains the same, but everything decreases; d) mass and everything remain the same; e) neither answer is correct.

37. A bomb is dropped from an artificial Earth satellite. Neglecting air resistance, determine at what point the bomb falls on the Earth: a) under the satellite at the moment of dropping; b) under the satellite at the moment of fall; c) ahead of the satellite, as it gains speed when firing; d) behind the satellite, as it moves along a curved linear trajectory; e) it will not fall to Earth.
