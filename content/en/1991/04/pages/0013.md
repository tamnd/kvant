- You want the “straight line” to pass through points A and B. In addition, since it is a “straight line”, it must also pass through point O. This means that you need to check the circle through three points A, B, O (Fig. 4). Any student can do this.

- But such a “straight line” exists, and only one. Do you agree?

- Yes, because three points uniquely define a circle.

- This means that in this model the axiom is fulfilled: a straight line passes through two different points, and only one.
