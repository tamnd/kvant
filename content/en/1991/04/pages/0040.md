1,2,3 — 2,3

Having substituted the found letters into the remaining words of the cryptograph, they proceed further according to the same method, i.e., they pay attention first of all to those words that either have the most known letters, or their characteristic placement is obtained. At the same time, having grasped the size of the verse, you can use the rules of versification, adjusting the number of words into words (and, consequently, vowels).
