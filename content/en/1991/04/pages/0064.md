For almost 20 years, every year at the beginning of November, schoolchildren, passionate about mathematics, their teachers, and with them many members of the editorial board «Kvant», like migratory birds, were drawn to warmer lands: during the autumn holidays, mathematical holidays took place in Batuti. (Our magazine has written about them more than once.) Unfortunately, in recent years the tradition of Batumi holidays has changed. And so, last year, horses and planes again took horse mathematicians to the south, now to Krasnodar, where the Horse Mathematicians Festival was held for the first time from November 3 to 10. The organizers managed to give it a wide dimension without any hesitation: the Festival was attended by guests from Moscow, Leningrad, Kyiv, Odessa, Chelyabinsk, Kirov, Izhevsk, Belorets, the Bulgarian city of Burgas and 13 teams from the Krasnodar Territory, for a total of 25 teams. And this despite the fact that not all the invitees - and the invitations were sent out based on the results of the All-City Tourism of the city - were able to come this time.

As at the holidays in Batuti, the program of the Krasnodar Festival included a scientific conference, KBH, lectures on mathematics for schoolchildren and teachers. There were also innovations in it, for example, a selection test for the Physics and Music School at Moscow State University. However, the main events unfolded, perhaps, at the team mathematical tournament. How was it built with such a large number of command units? The first stage was carried out on the model of the well-known northernization of television signs “brain ring”: teams received problems (such as problems «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren]) simultaneously and solved them against time. As a result, 8 quarter of the finalists were determined, who continued the battles according to the Optimian system with elimination. Kirov and Leningraders reached the finals. The Kirov team won somewhat unexpectedly, the main contribution to the success of which was made by the captain, Ivan Izmestev. The winners received one of two large samovars—the main award of the Festival. And the second samovar was increased by the Kievians, who won the meeting with KBH (captain - Ira Shevchenko).

If the mathematical tourist knew a total of 4 days, then the conference was satisfied in one. However, many interesting reports were made. The bottom line among them is very informative, although, frankly speaking, not very understandable for most

listeners, a report by Misha Temkin (57 school, Moscow) “Algorithms for finding a group of classes of divisors of a square field”, a report by Alexey Dolgorukov (Physical School at Moscow State University), who studied recently invented discrete models of soliites, speeches by the captain of the Leningrad team Vsevolod Zhukhovitsky, who distinguished himself with four presentations, on the Pick formula in the plane and multidimensional case and Lena Filippova from Odessa on the now fashionable topic “Knots, links and their polynomials”, report by Victor Paskhover from Kyiv “Elements of ergodic theory”. Let those whom we did not name not be squeezed - the organizers of the Festival intend to publish the materials of the conference.

The sports element introduced by the Matommi gave the Festival an originality that is worth preserving in the future. True, some teams that dropped out in the first stages felt out of place, at least compared to those who continued to fight. They found a way out by organizing, on their own initiative, a more intimate one. It is believed that the drawing system needs to be mastered.

Simultaneously with the Festival, a school teacher seminar was held in Krasnodar. At one of the meetings, the teachers met with the team leaders, who talked about the organization and methods of work; A representative of the editorial board «Kvant» also spoke at this meeting. In general, all guests of the Festival received an excellent opportunity to receive a lot of useful things for themselves.

The Festival participants were satisfied with the cultural program. They went on an excursion around Krasnodar and to the hero city of Novorossky, visited the acrobatics school, which prepared many champions, where they had the opportunity to jump on a trampoline, and the concentration of the Kuban Cossack choir.
