In position 3: a) its total mechanical energy is minimal; b) the resulting force is zero; c) the block stops instantly; d) the resulting force is directed upward; e) neither answer is correct.

30. Two balls of different masses, made of elastic materials, are suspended at one point on light threads of the same length.
