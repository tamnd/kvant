Correspondence Physics School at Moscow State University

The Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces admission of students to grades 10 and 11 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to enable secondary school students to study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as to better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher educational institutions and, first of all, to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University (when entering the Faculty of Physics, the certificate of completion of 3FS is taken into account by the admissions committee).

The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University prepares theoretical physicists and experimental physicists in all physics specialties; a fundamental university education allows graduates of the Faculty of Physics to quickly master the specifications of any scientific or technical field, as well as to successfully work in areas of science and technology where two or more scientific fields meet. These are biophysics and geophysics, radiotamma-infrared astronomy and the creation of artificial intelligence, chemical physics and astrophysics, etc.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of solving the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 1 to the address: 119899, ​​Moscow, GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3FSh.

Introductory task

Those entering the 10th grade of 3FS need to solve problems 1-5, and those entering the 11th grade need to solve problems 4-8.

1. "Chalk Line" A black board moves along the surface of a table at a constant speed. A piece of chalk moves along the board, pushed along it in such a way that at the initial moment the speed of the chalk relative to the table is perpendicular to the speed of the board. What shape should the chalk leave as it moves?

2. "Overload". With what force does an astronaut weighing 60 kg press on the support during vertical takeoff on bags with a rooting force of 9 g?

3. "Lift". The belt lift makes an angle α with the horizontal. What is the maximum rooting with which a box can be lifted on such a lift if the friction coefficient is μ? The tape does not bend.

4. "Hungry." In a caked area, the coefficient of friction between the wheels and the road is ten times less than in a non-caked area. How many times do you need to reduce the speed of the car so that the braking distance on a frozen section of the highway remains the same?
