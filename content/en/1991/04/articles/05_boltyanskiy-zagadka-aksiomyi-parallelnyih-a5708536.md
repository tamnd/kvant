Unusual association

In one city, an association of collectors was established, uniting branch dealers, numismatists, eye of needle collectors, etc. It was decided that the board of the association should consist of an odd number of people (this is convenient for voting). It was also decided that the board would be located in one building, and each person (i.e., board member) would have his own intercom telephone. And in order to avoid the need for an internal communication device, they decided not to install a switch, but to directly connect each person via telephone lines with some third party.

“Well,” said one of the founders of the association, “I think this organization of the work of the board is quite convenient.”

“I especially like,” said the second (the famous Collector of mathematical curiosities), “that the work of the board can be described by an abstract aksiomatic scheme.” Judge for yourself, we have three initial concepts: “person”, “line”, “connects”, and we want the following axioms to be fulfilled:

1) the number of persons is odd;
2) each line connects exactly two persons;
3) each person is connected in a line with the third other persons.
