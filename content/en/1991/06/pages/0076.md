8 (8). Two light bulbs connected in parallel are connected to a current source. The resistance of the first bulb is $R_1 = 360$ Ohm, the second - $R_2 = 240$ Ohm. How many times is the power released by the second lamp greater than the power released by the first?

9 (1). The wire ring is connected to the battery. Kontakts divide the length of the ring in the ratio $m:n$, while the thermal power $P_1$ is released in the ring. What power will be released in the ring if the contacts divide its length in the ratio $(m-1):(n+1)?$. The internal resistance of the battery can be neglected.

10 (5). Two lamps connected in series are connected to a city network with a voltage of 220 V. What is the power of each lamp in this connection if the cycle of one of them is 50 W and 220 V, and the cycle of the other is 100 W and 220 V? Assume that lamp resistance does not depend on power consumption.

11 (1). The electric heater is connected to two identical current sources connected in series. Show that the efficiency of the heater increases if it is connected to only one source. Consider the heater resistance constant.

Magnetic lines are perpendicular conductors.

The frame is located so that its plane is perpendicular to the line of induction of the magnetic field, and is closed to the galvanometer. When the frame is rotated at a certain angle, a charge $q = 7,5 \cdot 10^{-3}$ Cl flows through the galvanometer. At what angle (in degrees) should the frame be rotated?
