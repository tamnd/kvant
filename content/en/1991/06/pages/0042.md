AMOUNT OF MOVEMENT?

Descartes, judging by his statement, understands the fundamental meaning of the concept of momentum - or momentum of a body - introduced by him in the 17th century - as the product of the mass of a body by the magnitude of its speed. And although he made the mistake of not considering momentum as a vector quantity, the law of conservation of momentum that he formulated stood out with the pure test of time. At the beginning of the 18th century, the error was corrected, and the triumphal march of this law at the beginning and technically continues to this day.

As one of the fundamental laws of physics, it gave an incomparable ordine of research to scientists, putting a ban on one process and opening the way for others. Explosion, jet motion, atomic and nuclear transformations - this law works excellently here. And today, we hope you will see for yourself in how many common situations the concept of impulse helps to understand.

Questions and tasks

1. A body of mass $m$ is thrown with an initial speed $v_0$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. What is the increment in the modulus of the body's momentum and the modulus of the increment in momentum during the flight? Ignore impact resistance.

2. Water flows along a tread curved at a right angle. Does water affect trecha? In what direction?

3. A ball flying with a speed of $v$ hits someone blowing towards it with a speed of $u$ automatically. What will be the speed of the ball after the elastic impact?

4. Is it possible to accelerate a sailboat by directing a stream of air onto the sails from a powerful fan while in the boat? What happens if you blow past the sail?

5. The “slide” $A$ with the bodies $B$ and $C$ attached to it rests on a smooth horizontal surface. First, body $B$ slides down the “slide”, after which body $C$ slides. In what direction will the “slide” ultimately go? The masses of bodies $A$, $B$ and $C$ are the same. Neglect the motion of all three bodies.

6. A boy can throw a stone at a built-up barge or a light inflatable rubber boat. In what case will the stone move further?

7. A rocket launched vertically explodes at the highest point of its rise. The explosion produces three sedges. Prove that the natural velocities of all three sedges lie in the same plane.

8. When does a ball at rest acquire greater speed from the impact of another similar ball: with an elastic or inelastic central impact?
