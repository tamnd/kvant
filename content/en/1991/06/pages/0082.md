Let the rook standing on the 1st rank stand on the file with number $a_1$, the rook standing on the 2nd rank stand on the file with number $a_2$, etc., the rook standing on the 8th rank stand on the file with number $a_8$. The sum of the products of the horizontal and vertical numbers is $1a_1+2a_2+\dots+8a_8$, and for a centrally symmetric arrangement of rooks the sum is also $8(9-a_1)+7(9-a_2)+\dots+1(9-a_8)$. The difference between these numbers is $9(8+7+\dots+1)+9(a_1+a_2+\dots+a_8)$. But the sum of the vertical numbers is equal to the sum of the integers from 1 to 8. Therefore, this difference is equal to zero.
