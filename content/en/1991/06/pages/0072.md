14 (11). Outside a circle of radius $R$, a point $A$ is taken, from which two secants are drawn: one passes through the center, and the other at a distance $1/2 R$ from the center. Find the area of ​​the part of the circle enclosed between the secants.

15 (12). Two circles whose radii are $R$ and $r$ are externally tangent. Find the distance from the point of contact of the circles to their common external tangent.

16(3). Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. Through the point $A$ the chords $AC$ and $AD$ are drawn, tangent to the given circles. Prove that
$$AC^2 \cdot BD = AD^2 \cdot BC.$$

17 (9). The area of ​​the triangle $ABC$ is 60. On the side $AC$ the point $D$ is taken so that $AD:DC = 2:3$. The length of the perpendicular $DE$ dropped to side $BC$ is 9. Find $BC$.

18 (9). In an equilateral triangle, the altitudes drawn to the base and side of the corresponding side are 10 and 12. Find the length of the base.

19 (9). An equilateral triangle with an acute angle at the vertex and an angle $\alpha$ at the base is inscribed in a circle of radius $R$. Find the area of ​​this triangle.

20 (9). A circle is inscribed in a trapezoid with an acute angle $\alpha$ and a perimeter $p$. Find the height of the trapezoid if one of its angles is $90^\circ$.

21 (12). A triangular paramid has two faces - equilateral triangles on the side $a$, lying in perpendicular planes. Calculate the volume of the paramid.

22 (9). Find the volume of a paraline triangular paramid whose side edge is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\alpha$ and is removed from the middle of the opposite side by a distance $l$.

23(9). A plane is drawn through the edge of the base of a regular quadrangular paramid, which cuts off from the opposite face a triangle whose area is 16. Find the lateral surface of the paramid, cut off by the drawn plane from this paramid, if the lateral surface of the entire paramid is 100.

24(2). Given a regular triangular paramid $SABC$ with vertex $S$ and base side $a$. The lateral faces of the paramid are inclined to the plane of the base at an angle of $\alpha$. On the base sides $AC$ and $AB$ points $E$ and $F$ are taken so that
$$AE = \frac{1}{2} AC, FB = \frac{1}{2} AB.$$ Through points $E$ and $F$ a plane is drawn at an angle $\alpha$ to the base of the paramid, intersecting the faces $SBC$. Find the secant area.

25(2). Given a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ with vertex $S$ and base side $a$. On the sides of the base $AD$ and $BC$ the points $E$ and $F$ are taken so that $AE = \frac{1}{2} AD, FC = \frac{1}{2} BC$. Through points $E$ and $F$ a plane parallel to the face $SAB$ of the paramid is drawn. Find the area of ​​the resulting secant if the side face of the paramid is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle of $\alpha$.

26(2). Given a regular quadrangular paramid $SABC$, the lateral faces of which are inclined to the base plane $ABC$ at an angle $\alpha$. From the point $D$, lying at the height $AE$ of the base of the paramid and such that $AD:AE = 1:3$, a perpendicular of length $p$ is dropped onto the lateral edge $AS$. Find the volume of the paramid.

27 (12). In a regular quadrangular paramid, the lateral edge is equal to $b$ and forms an angle $\alpha$ with the plane of the base. A plane is drawn through the diagonal of the base parallel to the side edge. Determine the secant area, as well as the volume of the triangular paramid, cut off by the secant plane.

28 (6). The base of the paramid $SABCD$ is the parallelgram $ABCD$. Construct secant paramidas passing through the vertex $A$ and the midpoints $M$ and $P$ of edges $SB$ and $SD$. Determine in what ratio the secants are divided by the edge $SC$.

29(6). The base of the truncated paramid $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is the trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $AB$ and $CD$. Points $M$ and $P$ lie on the side edges $BB_1$ and $DD_1$, respectively. It is known that $B_1M:MB = 1:2, D_1P:PD = 1:2, AB:A_1B_1 = 3:1, CD:AB = 1:2$. Construct secant paramidas passing through vertex $A$ and points $M$ and $P$. Determine in what ratio the secants are divided by the edge $CC_1$.

30 (11). The diagonal of a right parallelped is 13, and the diagonals of its lateral faces are $4\sqrt{10}$ and $3\sqrt{17}$. Determine the volume of the parallelped.
