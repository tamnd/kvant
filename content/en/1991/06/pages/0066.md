Algebra

1 (3). Find a two-digit number if it is second greater than the sum of its digits, and the square of this sum is second greater than the desired number.

2 (3). If a two-digit number is divided by the product of its digits, then the quotient will be 4, and the remainder will be 1. If this number is divided by the sum of its digits, then the quotient will be 3, and the remainder will be 1. Find this number.

3 (9). A 364 cm long plank was cut into two parts so that the first of them was 18% shorter than the second. Find the length of each part.

4 (7). There are two alloys, one containing 40% and the other 20% silver. How many kilograms of the second alloy must be added to 20 kg of the first to obtain an alloy containing 32% silver?

5 (9). An alloy of copper and zinc weighing 12.5 kg contains 40% copper. What mass of copper must be added to this piece so that the resulting new alloy contains equal amounts of copper and zinc?

6 (7). Two pumps, working together, pumped water out of the pool in 12 hours. If first one of them pumped out half of the water, and then the other pumped out the remaining half, then it would take them 25 hours to complete the entire job. How many hours will it take each pump separately to pump out all the water from the pool?

7 (7). Two crews working together can complete the job in 8 hours. The first crew, with only one job, can complete the job 12 hours faster than the second crew. How many hours would it take the first crew to complete this job?

8 (4). The first worker spends 7 minutes less time processing one part than the second. How many parts will each of them process in 4 hours, if the first one processes 28 parts more than the second during this time?

9 (6). A truck left from point A to point B. After 1 hour, a passenger car left from point A to point B, which arrived at point B at the same time as the truck. If a truck and a passenger car had simultaneously left points A and B towards each other, they would have met 1 hour 12 minutes after departure. How long did the truck travel from A to B?

10 (2). The second courier arrived at A 30 minutes earlier than the first one arrived at B. What is the speed of the second courierERA if the speed of the first is 30 km/h?

11(2). From point A to point B, the distance between which is 50 km, a fisherman set out on a motor boat across a strong sea. After 1.5 hours, a second fisherman left A on a scooter in pursuit of the first, who, having caught up with the first, moved back to A at the same speed. When the second one nailed at A, the first fisherman continued three-quarters of the way to B, remaining after meeting the second one. What is the speed of the second fisherman if the speed of the first is 10 km/h?

12 (9). The store sells three types of candy. The cost of purchasing 0.8 kg of the first, 0.2 kg of the second and 0.3 kg of the third type is 10 rubles. The cost of purchasing 0.5 kg of the first, 0.3 kg of the second and 0.2 kg of the third type is 8 rubles. How much will you need to budget for the purchase of 1.1 kg of the first, 0.1 kg of the second and 0.4 kg of the third type?

13(9). Find the sixth term of an arithmetic progression if the sum of any first n terms is equal to 4 $n^2$.
