The two puzzles shown in our figures are combinatorial puzzles with variable orientation.
In the first of them, 4 spoken plates need to be stacked so that the individual squares on the plates cover the entire larger square. By rotating the heads by 90° or 180°, you can get 4³=64 options for their relative position (up to the rotations of the entire stack). If the plastics are also allowed to be turned over, then the number of significantly different options is reduced by 2³ = 8, but the complexity of the solution increases more. Because in this case, you don’t know whether it’s even possible to find a solution for a given stack.
In the second puzzle you need to cover all sectors of the circle with orange slices. The number of options for their relative arrangement is 12³ = 1728, but only one of them gives the correct solution.
Plastics can be cut out of thick air-conditioned film and pieces of colored paper can be glued onto it.
