We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to the problem from this issue should be sent no later than August 15, 1991 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the journal or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 6 - 91” and the numbers of the problem whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1286” or “F1293”. In the column “... sender’s address,” please write your first and last name legibly. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope).

Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you will study.

Problems M1286–M1289 were proposed at competitions and matbois in Leningrad in 1968–1971.

Tasks

M1286—M1290, F1293—F1297

M1286. There are 100 delegates at the congress, each of whom knows several foreign languages. It is known that any three can talk to each other without the help of others. Prove that delegates can be accommodated in 50 double hotel rooms so that those living in the same room can talk to each other.

M1287. In parallelogram ABCD, the diagonal AC is greater than the diagonal BD. Point M on the diagonal AC also means that a circle can be circumscribed around the quadrilateral BCDM. Prove that BD is the common tangent of the circumcircles of triangles ABM and ADM.

M1288*. Prove that the number $235^2 + 972^2$ is composite.

M1289*. The integers $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$ are such that their sum is 1. For each $k$ from 1 to $n$ we denote by $N_k$ the number of positive numbers $n$ sums: $a_k, a_k + a_{k+1}, ..., a_k + a_{k+1} + ... + a_n + a_1 + ... + a_{k-1}$. Prove that all $N_k$ are distinct.

M1290*. A square sheet of paper with dimensions $8 \times 8$ is lined with 14 straight lines into 64 square cells $1 \times 1$ and randomly folded along these lines into a book $1 \times 1$ (of 64 sheets). The sheets of the book are numbered in numerical order 们 $1, 2, ..., 64$, and then it opens up again. Let $p$ be the greatest difference in the numbers of neighboring (bordering on the side) cells. What is the smallest possible value of $p$ and what folding achieves it?

M. Gusarov

F1293. A cylindrical vessel with a light and thin attachment, tightly adjacent to the wall of the vessel, is lowered into water so that the bottom is at a depth of $H=4$ cm, and is held motionless (Fig. 1). What is the minimum mass of the weight and where should it be placed on the bottom so that it falls off? The diameter of the day is $D=10$ cm, the dimensions of the weight are considered small compared to the diameter of the vessel.

F1294. One side of a thin metal plate is illuminated by the sun. At an air temperature of $T_0$, the illuminated side has a temperature of $T_1$, and the opposite side has a temperature of $T_2$. What will the temperature be if we take a plate of double thickness?

E. Ponomarev

F1295. A multi-link resistor circuit (Fig. 2) is connected to points $A$ and $B$. Each link contains two identical resistors, the resistance of the resistors of each next link is twice as large as the previous one. What will be the resistance between points $A$ and $B$ with a very large number of links? The resistors in the first link have a resistance of $R$.

A. Zilberman
