⟦folio 16⟧

Attention to our readers!

We receive many letters from our readers asking why the magazine in some cities arrives to subscribers very late. Is this due to the lack of paper?

We hasten to conclude for the reader that this is different.

According to the agreement between the editorial office, the printing house and the Soyuzpechat CRPA, the entire circulation «Kvant» must arrive at the CRPA on the first day of the subscription period. The editors carefully fulfill their obligations to the printing house. The printing house has a small task (5-10 days). Everything else is the responsibility of the Ministry of Communications, which, having notified the delivery abruptly, considered, very indo, its functions to be exhausted.

We express our regret to our subscribers and hope that this phenomenon is still temporary. In any case, we have identified the reason for the delay and the subscriber can demand that the Ministry of Communications fulfill its obligations...
