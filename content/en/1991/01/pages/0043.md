The so-called “hyperbolic spiral”, which is given by the equation $\rho = \frac{a}{\varphi}$, where $\rho$ is the distance of a point on the spiral from the center, when the angle φ changes on the segment [0; 1] makes an infinite number of turns and the length of the resulting arc turns out to be infinitely large.

The “logarithmic spiral”, given by the equation $\rho = e^{\varphi}$, when changing the angle φ on the interval (-∞; 0), also makes an infinite number of turns, and their total length turns out to be equal to 3.
