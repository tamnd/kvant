5. $7/3 \sqrt{21/2}$. Solution. Let $\psi$ be the angle between the plane $ABC$ and the plane $\beta$ on which the paramid is projected. Let's put
$$\varphi = \begin{cases} \psi, \text{ если } \beta \text{ пересекает ребро } SC; \\ \pi - \psi \text{ в противном случае.} \end{cases}$$

Then $\varphi$ changes from 0 to $\pi$. Let us denote $S_0$ as the area of the triangle $ABC$, $\tilde{S}$ as the area of the projection of the paramid, $SO$ as the heights of the paramid (according to the condition, the point $O$ belongs to the segment $AB$), $S'$ and $C'$ as the projections onto the plane $\beta$ of points $S$ and $C$, $\varphi_1$ is the value of $\varphi$, at which $S'$ belongs to the segment $AC'$.

Let $SO = H$, $AB = c$, $BO = kc$ ($0 < k < 1$), $BC = h$ (Fig. 10). Then $S_0 = \frac{1}{2} hc$,

$$\tilde{S} = \begin{cases} S_1(\varphi), \text{ если } 0 \leq \varphi \leq \varphi_1, \\ S_2(\varphi), \text{ если } \varphi_1 < \varphi \leq \frac{\pi}{2}, \\ S_3(\varphi), \text{ если } \frac{\pi}{2} < \varphi \leq \pi, \end{cases}$$

where $S_1(\varphi) = 1/2 hc \cdot \cos \varphi$, $S_2(\varphi) = 1/2 khc \cdot \cos \varphi + 1/2 Hc \cdot \sin \varphi$, $S_3(\varphi) = -1/2 hc \cdot \cos \varphi + 1/2 Hc \cdot \sin \varphi.$

From the condition $S_1(\varphi_1) = S_2(\varphi_1)$ we obtain tg $\varphi_1 = (1 - k)h/H$. We use functions $S_i(\varphi)$. Function $S_1(\varphi)$ kills. Since an arbitrary $S_2'(\varphi)$ vanishes at the unique point $\varphi = \varphi^* \in (0; \frac{\pi}{2})$, then either the function $S_2(\varphi)$ is monotonic on the interval $(\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$ (if $\varphi^* \in (\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$), or it has a maximum at $\varphi = \varphi^*$ (if $\varphi^* \in (\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$). The function $S_3(\varphi)$ has a maximum at $\varphi = \varphi^* \in (\frac{\pi}{2}; \pi)$. But $S_3(\varphi^*) = (1/2)c\sqrt{H^2 + h^2} > S_2(\varphi^*) = (1/2)c\sqrt{H^2 + kh^2}$ means $\tilde{S}_{\max} = S_3(\varphi^*)$. Next, $\tilde{S}_{\min} = \{ \tilde{S}(\varphi_1), \tilde{S}(\frac{\pi}{2}) \} = \tilde{S}(\varphi_1)$, since $\tilde{S}(\pi/2) = 1/2 Hc = \sqrt{S^2(\varphi^*)} - S_0^2$, and from the data of the problem we obtain that $\tilde{S}_{\min} < \sqrt{S^2(\varphi^*)} - S_0^2$. So, $hc = 2S_0 = 14; \sqrt{(Hc)^2 + (hc)^2} = 2\tilde{S}_{\max} = 28$, from where $Hc = 14\sqrt{3}; hc \times Hc / \sqrt{(Hc)^2 + (hc)^2} \cdot (1 - k)^2 = 2S_{\min} = 8\sqrt{3}$, from where $k = 1/4$. Therefore, $H = \sqrt{AO \cdot BO} = (\sqrt{3}/4)c$, and therefore $c = 2\sqrt{14}$, $H = \sqrt{21/2}$, and the required volume is $V = 1/3 HS_0 = 7/3 \cdot \sqrt{21/2}$.
