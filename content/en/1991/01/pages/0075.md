Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. Two bodies were thrown vertically upward with the same speed $v_0 = 20$ m/s after a time $\tau = 1$ s one after the other. Determine where and when (how long after throwing the first body) they will meet. Learn similar and graphic ones. Gravity acceleration $g \approx 10$ m/s².
2. The projectile breaks into two parts, the masses of which are in the ratio 1:2. The explosion will occur at a height of $H = 320$ m. After a time of $\tau = 4$ s after the explosion, a larger sedge falls under the place where the explosion will occur. Determine the distance to where the light sedge falls. The speed of the projectile before the explosion is horizontal and equal to $v_0 = 150$ m/s. Avoid air resistance.
3. Determine the position of the center of gravity of a uniform square plate on the side $a = 12$ cm, in which a circular hole of radius $r = 3$ cm is cut, touching two adjacent sides.
4. The balloon has an easily stretchable closed heat-conducting shell with a mass of $m_1 = 130$ kg. The shell is filled with air weighing $m_2 = 260$ kg at the pressure and temperature of the surrounding atmosphere ($T = 273$ K). How many degrees must the air inside the shell be heated for the balloon to take off?
5. Dry atmospheric air contains oxygen, nitrogen and argon (the content of other gases can be neglected). Determine the number of molecules of these gases and their mass in a volume of $V = 1$ m³ under normal conditions: $p = 101$ kPa, $T = 273$ K. It is known that the number of nitrogen molecules is 4 times greater, and argon 20 times less than oxygen. Molar masses: oxygen - $M_1 = 32$ g/mol, nitrogen - $M_2 = 28$ g/mol, argon - $M_3 = 40$ g/mol. Universal gas constant $R = 8,3$ J/(mol·K), Avogardro number $N_A = 6 \cdot 10^{23}$ mol⁻¹.
6. Determine the efficiency of a cycle consisting of two adiabats and two isochores performed by a monatomic ideal gas (Fig. 1). It is known that in the process of adiabatic distribution the temperature $T_2 = 0,75T_1$ is established, and in the process of adiabatic reduction - $T_3 = 0,75T_4$.
7. A positively charged ball of mass $M = 30$ g performs a harmonious oscillation above a positively charged infinite horizontal plane (Fig. 2). The force of the electric interaction between the ball and the plane is $F = 0,1$ N, and the period of oscillation of the ball is $T = 2$ s. The ball is recharged so that its charge becomes negative, but modulo equal to the original one. Determine the period of harmonious oscillations of the ball in its new state.
8. Determine the charge of the $C = 2$ mF capacitor connected to the target shown at figure 3 if $C = 24$ V, $R_1 = R_2 = 5$ Ohm, $R_3 = R_4 = 10$ Ohm, $r = 1$ Ohm.
9. The electron enters a uniform magnetic field with a speed $v = 2 \cdot 10^5$ m/s, which makes an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with the direction of the induction vector $\vec{B}$. At what minimum value of magnetic field induction can the electron be able to find itself at a point lying on the same magnetic field line at a distance of $L = 2$ cm from the starting point? Specific electron charge $e/m = 1,76 \cdot 10^{11}$ C/kg.
10. The actual image of an object obtained using a converging lens is located at a distance of $f_1 = 80$ cm from it. The converging lens is replaced with a diverging lens with the same focal length. The image of the object in this case is at a distance of $f_2 = 20$ cm. Determine the focal length of the lenses and magnifications $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$.
