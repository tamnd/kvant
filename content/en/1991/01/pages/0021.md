F1271. Two identical wire rings with radius $R$ and mass $m$ each are in a uniform magnetic field, the inductive field of which is equal to $B_0$ and is directed perpendicular to the plane of the tracks (Fig. 2). At the contact points $A$ and $C$ the pegs have good electrical contact. Angle $\alpha=\pi/3$. What speed will each of the tracks acquire if the magnetic field is turned off? The electrical resistance of the piece of wire from which the ring is made is $r$. The inductance of the tracks is not taken into account. Neglect the mixing of tracks during the field shutdown. There is no friction.

V. Mozhaev

A. Zilberman

Problem solutions

M1236 — M1240, F1248 — F1252

The condition that a circle with center $C$ and radius $r$ intersects the sides of the square at 8 points is equivalent to the fact that the distance from $C$ to all vertices is greater than $r$, and to all sides is less than $r$.

It is clear that the desired set has the same 4 axes of symmetry as the square. Therefore, it is enough to find all the points of this set in one of the 8 angles into which these axes divide the plane. The easiest way to do this is using the coordinate method.

Let our square be given by the conditions $0 \leq x \leq 1$, $0 \leq y \leq 1$, and the center $C$ of a circle of radius $r$ have coordinates $(x; y)$ and lie in the triangle $0 \leq y \leq x \leq 1/2$
