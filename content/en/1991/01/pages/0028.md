Determine the density of the plate material. Coefficient of surface tension of water σ=0.073 H/m.

Task, Control

the plate is blue and on top, F is the force of surface tension. Let's look at each force separately.

The gravitational force is related to the desired densityρ of the plate material, respectively

$$mg = \rho(\pi d^2/4)ag.$$

The surface tension force acting on the plate from the side of water is equal to

$$F = \sigma \pi d.$$

The difference in pressure forces on the plate is due to the “drop” of the water level under the plate and is equal to

$$\Delta pS = \rho_B g(H + a)(\pi d^2/4),$$

where ρ_B is the density of water, H is the immersion depth of the upper edge of the plate. We will determine this value from the equilibrium condition of the volume of water allocated to figure 2 with “width” Δy (Δy << d), writing it in projections on the horizontal Y axis:

$$\sigma \Delta y = p_{cp} \Delta S = \rho_B g(H/2)H \Delta y,$$

where

$$H = \sqrt{2\sigma/(\rho_B g)}$$.

So, let us rewrite the equilibrium conditions of the plate in the form

$$\rho \frac{\pi d^2}{4} ag - \rho_B g\left(\sqrt{\frac{2\sigma}{\rho_B g}} + a\right)\frac{\pi d^2}{4} - \sigma \pi d = 0$$

and find the densityρ of the plate:

$$\rho = \rho_B + \frac{1}{a}\sqrt{\frac{2\sigma \rho_B}{g}} + \frac{4\sigma}{adg} \approx 10^4 \text{ кг/m}^3.$$
