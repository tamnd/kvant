Category problem in physics

"Bicycle Challenge"

“Look,” one said to the other, “what a wheel!” What do you think, should that wheel, if it happened, arrive in Moscow or not?

N.V. Gogol

A typical two-wheeled bicycle weighs over one hundred and sixty kilograms. This is exactly the total weight of the author and his two younger friends, who conducted an extremely complex - almost circus - experiment, the second of whom rode six kilometers on one bicycle. The author's bicycle.

However, if you pull the spokes - these thin steel rods - from the rim, you can bend them with two fingers, which is what the author’s friends did after the experimental production *). How does a bicycle produce such enormous weight, why don’t the spokes bend while riding?

Don’t rush to answer that a bicycle “stands” not on one, but on several lower spokes of the wheel. Further, all the spokes of the wheel, folded into one bundle, are not worth the effort.

So why don't the spokes of a wheel bend?

E. Gurovich
