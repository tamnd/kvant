Moscow Pedagogical State University named after. V. I. Lenina
Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of mathematics)

1. Two machines working on both sides of the tunnel should complete the excavation in 60 days. If the first machine does 30% of its work, and the second - 26 2/3% of its work, then both of them will pass 60 m of the tunnel. If the first machine did 2/3 of the entire work of the second machine in digging this tunnel, and the second machine did 0.3 of the entire work of the first machine, then the first machine would have needed 6 more days to do this than the second one. Determine how many meters each car travels per day.

2. Solve the equation
$$5^2 \cdot 5^4 \cdot 5^6 \cdots 5^{2x} = 0,04^{-28}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^8 x + \cos^8 x = \frac{17}{32}.$$

4. Find the most and most values ​​of the function $y = \frac{1}{3}x^3 - \frac{3}{2}x^2 + 1$ on the interval [-1; 1].

5. In a rectangular parallelipeid $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ there are edges $AB=a$, $BC=a$, $AA_1=b$. Find the area of ​​the section passing through the vertex $A$ and perpendicular to the diagonal $BD_1$.

Option 2
(Physics Department)

1. To the right of the triangular shape, through the sides of the lower base and the middle of the opposite edge, a plane is drawn, forming an angle of 60° with the plane of the base. The cross-sectional area is $S=8\sqrt{3}$. Find the volume and total surface area of ​​the feature.

2. Find the intervals of increasing and decreasing function
$$y=3x^3-6x^2+7.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^4 \frac{x}{2} - \cos^4 \frac{x}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.$$

4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x^2-4}{x^2-3x-4} \geq 0.$$

5. Solve the equation
$$\lg(10x^2) \cdot \lg x=1.$$

Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. A body thrown vertically upward passes through a point at height $h$ twice. The time interval between these passages is $\Delta t$. Find the initial speed of the body.

2. A man standing on skates on the smooth ice of a river throws a stone with a mass of $m=0,5$ kg. After $t=2$ s the stone reaches the shore, having traveled a distance of $l=20$ m. At what speed does the skater begin to slide if its mass is $M=60$ kg? Let's neglect the training.

3. Motorcycle LIST is driving along a horizontal road, turning into a loop with a radius of $R=4$ m. What speed must it develop in order to turn off the motor and drive through the loop?

4. A body $m=1$ kg slides off an inclined plane of length $l=12$ m, forming an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the horizon. The initial speed of the body is zero, the speed at the base of the plane is $v=4$ m/s. How much heat is released when a body rubs against a plane?

5. The vessel with the gas is divided by a movable partition into two parts, the ratio of the volumes of which is $V_1/V_2=2/3$. The gas temperatures in the smaller and larger volumes are $t_1=177^\circ\text{C}$ and $t_2=267^\circ\text{C}$, respectively; the pressure in them is the same. What will be the volume ratio if the temperatures are equal? Heat exchange is possible only through the partition.

6. In an electric kettle with a power of $P=600$ W, you can boil $V=1,5$ liters of water in $t=20$ minutes at an initial water temperature of $t=20^\circ\text{C}$. Find the efficiency of the kettle. Specific heat production of water $c=4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K).

7. Two series-connected capacitors, whose capacitances are $C=1$ µF and $C_2=3$ µF, are connected to a current source with a direction of $U=220$ V. Find the direction on each capacitor.

8. The heating coil of an electric device for evaporating water at a temperature of $t=100^\circ\text{C}$ has a resistance of $R=10$ Ohm. What current must be passed through these spirals for the apparatus to evaporate a mass of water $m=100$ g in a time $t=1$ min? Specific heat of vaporization of water $\lambda=2,3$ MJ/kg.

9. At what speed must the conductor of the perpendicular line of induction of a uniform magnetic field move in order for a potential difference $U=0,01$ V to arise between the conductor capacitors? Magnetic field induction $B=0,2$ T, conductor length $l=10$ cm.

10. Checking his glasses, the man received on the floor of the room a real image of a lamp, highest at a height of $H=3$ m, holding the glass glass under the lamp at a distance of $f=1$ m from the floor. What is the optical power of the glasses?
