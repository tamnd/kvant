The greatest noise comes from the first cascades of the receiver, since it is absorbed by all subsequent cascades. The antenna receives noise caused by the thermal movement of part of the Earth's atmosphere and noise associated with radio emission from celestial bodies. Noise is observed at all frequencies, its total power depends on the position of the frequencies and increases with increasing receiver bandwidth. Therefore, the bandwidth is not taken to be the minimum bandwidth required for signal transmission. As a result, the noise power of the receiver itself is affected only by its circuitry and the design of the input cascades.

With a frequency model, the power of the adjacent signal should be 15-20 times greater than the noise power, with an amplitude model - several times more (for this reason, frequency modeling is most often chosen in satellite systems). Experience shows that the sensitivity of the receiving device should be 2 times higher to reduce the influence of the jedr. Reception reliability will be ensured approximately 99% of the time if you use receivers whose noise power is 30-40 times less than the signal power.

Reception features

For satellite reception, a home telephone can usually only be used using a special set-top box. The set-top box is necessary to re-equip a signal from the centimeter range to the detmmeter range, to increase the sensitivity of the TV and to convert a frequency-modulated signal into an amplitude-modulated one. With this design, the noise level is kept to a minimum; there is no need for a long waveguide. In addition to satellite reception equipment, the owner of the TV may have antennas in the meter and desimeter ranges for receiving local TV.

Satellites designed for individual TV reception serve “small” zones in order to obtain an acceptable signal level and, accordingly, a moderately priced receiving device with an antenna with a diameter of about 1 m. Five such zones will be created on the territory of the Soviet Union, from the fifth position in orbit. The program for the zones will be formed with cdw in time relative to Moscow.

For collective reception, large-diameter antennas and a highly sensitive amplifier are used. Thanks to the ability to receive weak signals, satellites can serve large areas. For example, the Soviet Ekran-M system transmits Central Television programs to a network of terrestrial transmitting stations in areas of Siberia and the Far East. The Moscow Global system ensures reception of Soviet television in the postal areas of all countries of the world. The antenna operating on centimeter waves has a diameter of 4 m!

Reception from Western satellites on the territory of the Soviet Union is difficult. This is not only due to low signal levels. These satellites use a different TV standard and some signals are encoded to allow free viewing. An interesting press report: by agreement with foreign companies, collective reception of Western TV satellites will be organized in Moscow via the existing cable network.

Satellite television is rapidly developing in all countries. She is just at the start and, figuratively speaking, at the threshold of our house.
