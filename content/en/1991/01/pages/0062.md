Now insert two sheets of paper measuring 6x14 cm with your address clearly written (including postcode), last name and first name, as well as an addressed envelope.

Tasks in work should go in the same order as ours; Write down the conditions first, then the solution.

The deadline for sending the introductory work is no later than March 15, 1991 (by postmark).

If you pass the competition, then starting from September 1991 you will receive questions that contain theoretical material and problems for independent solution, as well as test problems. All test papers will be checked and decided in detail by VZMSH teachers - students, graduate students and teachers of Moscow State University and other universities in which there are VZMSH filpals. Filpals work under the same programs and benefits as the Moscow VZMSH group.

It is assumed that some of the tasks will be given according to the magazine «Kvant», so we recommend that you subscribe to it (this can be done without restrictions from anywhere in any post office; subscription index 70 465; the magazine is distributed only by subscription).

For applicants to the Mathematics department
The entry work must be sent to the address: 119823, Moscow, GSP, Moscow State University, VZMSH (for admission), department of “Mathematics” or to the address of the corresponding school.

VZMSH philipals at universities are available in the cities: Voronezh, Gomel, Donetsk, Dushanbe, Zaporozhye, Ivanovo, Izhevsk, Kazan, Krasnodar, Krasnoyarsk, Kuibyshev, Makhachkala, Odeska, Rostov-on-Don, Sverdlovsk, Tashkent, Frunze, Cheboksary, Chelyabinsk, Chernivtsi, Elite, Yaroslavl; filpals at pedagogical institutes - in the cities: Abakan, Birsk, Blagoveshchensk, Bryansk, Vitebsk, Kirov, Leninabad, Lutsk, Magadan, Pavlodar, Smolensk, Ternovol, Ulyanovsk, Uralsk, Tselinograd, Cherpovets, Chita, Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk; Filpals also work in Dubna at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research, and in Mogilev at the regional Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren.

Students living in the north-west of the RSFSR (in Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod, Pskov regions, Karabsk and Komi Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republics), in the Baltic resource areas and in Belarus (except for Vitebsk, Gomel and Mogilev regions), assign work to the address: 197136, Leningrad, Chkalovsky Ave., 25ª, S-3 ZMSH (for reception).

Schoolchildren and students of vocational technical schools who did not have time or were unable to enter VZMSH for individual training have the opportunity to take jobs under the same program in “Collective student” groups.

Each such group is a mathematical circle, working under the guidance of a mathematics accountant according to the VZMSH program and on its own.

“Collective student” groups are also accepted under the guidance of a teacher of Russian language and literature, native language or foreign language.

Introductory work (“Mathematics”)

1. The distance from the village of Vorobyovo to the village of Grachevo is an integer number of kilometers. What could this distance be equal to if it is known that from Vorobyovo to the village of Drozdovo is 2.5 kilometers, and from Grachev to Drozdov is 1.3 kilometers?

2. In an equilibrium trapezoid, the lateral side is equal to the smaller base, and the diagonal is equal to the larger one. Find the angles of the trapezoid.

3. The library contains no more than 5,000 books. If you connect them 5, 6 or 7 books per pack, then each time there is one line of books left, and if you connect 11 books, then there is no extra book left. How many books are there in the library?

4. Find all pairs of positive integers (x, y) such that
xy = x + y + 1990.

5. From the two ends A and B of the rural road, two volospedents simultaneously start towards each other. One travels the entire route AB in 21 minutes, the other in 19 minutes. Having reached A or B, each of them instantly breaks up and goes in the opposite direction. How many times will they meet face to face before the second tax is asked?

6. Two circles whose radii are R and r touch the same line at points A and B, respectively, and also touch each other.
