In conclusion, we offer several tasks similar to those we met.

Exercises
1. What is the probability that two dice rolls will result in
   a) two numbers with a sum of at least 10?
   b) two numbers, of which the first is divisible by the second?
2. A passenger arrives at a stop at a random time and is automatically assigned one of two routes, searching at intervals of 10 and 15 minutes. Find the probability $p = p(t)$ that he will have to wait at least $t$ minutes.
3. The segment is divided into three equal parts. What is the probability that three points randomly thrown onto a segment will fall into three equal pieces?
4. Four points $A, B, C, D$ are randomly selected on the circle. What is the probability that the segments $AC$ and $BD$ intersect?
5. a) A diameter is drawn in a circle. A point is randomly selected on it and a chord perpendicular to the diameter is drawn through it. What is the probability that the length of the chord is greater than the radius of the circle?
6) Two points are randomly selected on the circle. What is the probability that the length of the connector of their chord is greater than the radius?

Competition "Mathematics 6-8"
The magazine «Kvant» together with the Bolshoi youth magazine “Mathematika” continues a competition on solving mathematical problems for students in grades 6-8. The competition consists of 27 problems (no 3 in each issue) and will end in May of this year. The winners will be awarded prizes from the magazine «Kvant» and Mathematika.
Send the solution to the problem from this issue no later than March 15, 1991 to the address: 103006, Moscow K-6, st. Gorkozo, 32/1, «Kvant» (with the help of “Competition “Mathematics 6-8”). Don't forget to include your last name, first name, school and class.

13. Grandma was handing out apples inside. She gave the first one inside 1 apple and 1/10 of the remaining, the second - 2 apples and 1/10 of the remaining, the third - 3 apples and 1/10 of the remaining, etc. until the apples ran out. It turned out that everything inside was an equal number of apples. How many were inside and how many apples did they order?
A. Kyuchukov, X. Hristov

14. Telephone numbers in the city $N$ consist of five digits, with the first digit not being a zero. A lucky number is one in which all the digits are different and arranged in descending or ascending order.
(For example, 12345 is a lucky number, but 11234 or 10234 is not.) Find the number of all lucky numbers in the city $N$.

P. Koshlukov

15. Find all natural numbers $n$ that have the following properties: if $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ satisfy the equality $x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_n = 0$, then $x_1^n + x_2^n + \dots + x_n^n = n \cdot x_1 x_2 \dots x_n = 0$.

P. Koshlukov
