In each broken line, we leave all the figures one after another along the path around the broken line (Fig. 13).
6. The magic square $3 \times 3$ is uniquely determined by the ternary $a$, $b$ and $n$:

| $a$ | $b$ | $3n-a-b$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $4n-2a-b$ | $n$ | $2a+b-2n$ |
| $a+b-n$ | $2n-b$ | $2n-a$ |

It is easy to check that the sum of the verticals, horizontals and both diagonals is the same and equal to $3n$. It is also easy to check that $a^2+b^2+(3n-a-b)^2=(a-b-n)^2+(2n-b)^2+(2n-a)^2$. The same equality is true, very indo, for the first and last columns.
