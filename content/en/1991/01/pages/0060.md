A new example for the correspondence department of the Small Mechanism

The Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics - a mathematical school at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University - announces an example of students completing the eighth grade of the same general education school in the correspondence department. Admission to the Malyi Mekhanimat is made based on the results of solving the problem of the introductory work published below.

The main task of the Small Mechanics and Mathematics Faculty (MMMF) is to bring closer to mathematics, deepen knowledge within the framework of the school curriculum, as well as expand the mathematical horizons of secondary school students.

Classes at the correspondence department of primary education at the Small Mechanical Mathematics are free. The duration of study is three years. Part-time students who successfully complete all assignments receive certificates of completion of the MMMF. Students who have successfully completed the 9th or 10th grades are recommended for admission to the Physics and Mathematics School Integriat at Moscow State University.

The teachers at the correspondence department of the MMMF are graduate students and students of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University. The development of thematic brochures is carried out by the methodological council of the MMMF, consisting of professors and teachers of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

Those wishing to take a post-drink to the Maly Mehanimat must send solutions to the introductory work problem to the MMMF no later than May 1, 1991 (it is not necessary to solve all the proposed problems). The work must be completed in a checkered school notebook. On the cover of the notebook, paste a sheet of notebook paper with the following information:

1) resources, region, region;
2) surname, given name uchasheres;
3) school and class;
4) full home address (including post office code);
5) last name, first name, absent parents, place of work and position.

The work includes a piece of paper $4 \times 6$ cm, on which write the full home address (indicating the post office index) and embroider it at the address: 119899, ​​Moscow, Moscow State University, Maly Mekhanimat.

Acceptance

For schoolchildren in grades 8-11 in Moscow and the surrounding Moscow region there is an evening department of the MMMF. Inquiries by phone. 939-39-43.
