Basically, you can “do it” too. True, this is a very difficult task, because matter and antimatter, atoms and antiatoms, particles and antiparticles cannot coexist peacefully. When they are in close proximity, they are said to antigylate and disappear. But... not without a trace - during anti-giltism, enormous energy is released. Per unit mass, it is approximately 1000 times more energy released during normal nuclear reactions. Who knows if the “matter-antimatter” anti-ghost will someday become a controllable and safe form of energy? For now, this is pure fantasy, but much of what we now use was once fantasy too.

A. Kikopin
