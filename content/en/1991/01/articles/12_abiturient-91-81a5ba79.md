APPLICANT-91

Correspondence courses with original teaching methods will help you become a student of one of the leading classes in the country.

Students and graduates of schools, technical schools and retired soldiers are invited.

Qualified teachers, using an individual approach and taking into account the level of your initial preparation, will do everything possible to ensure that your admission to university becomes a reality.

Within 4-5 months you will receive 5 methodological developments for each subject, which contain theoretical material, analysis of questions and tasks, proposed preliminary exams, and tests.

For applicants to Moscow University, face-to-face consultations are held during entrance exams.

The cost of a training course in one subject is 73 rubles. For disabled people, orphans and internationalist soldiers a 50% discount is provided. Payment will be made upon receipt of notification of enrollment in courses.

To enroll in courses, you must send an application addressed to the head. courses, where the subject chosen will be indicated, your last name, first name and patronymic, the educational institution where you study or graduated, and 2 envelopes with your postal address written on them.
