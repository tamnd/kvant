Tasks

1. Solve the arithmetic puzzle you see at figure. One letter corresponds to the same numbers, and different letters correspond to different numbers; all vowels correspond to numbers of one parity, and consonants to another.

2. Show that if the side lengths of a right triangle are expressed as integers, then at least one of them is divisible by 2 and at least one of them is divisible by 3.

3. On the heel islands, 30 storks had breakfast. On each island, the storks divided the frogs equally, and each stork from the first island ate more than each stork from the second, from the second - more than from the third, etc. How many frogs were eaten on each of the islands, if a total of 40 frogs were eaten and each stork ate at least one frog?

4. Unscramble the word written in multi-colored numbers (see figure).

5. Five friends met on the first floor of a building larger than the elevator. Zhenya said: “If you count from here, then I live higher than you, Vova, twice, higher than Petya three times, higher than Andrey four times higher and higher than Tanya six times.” “You noticed this very well,” Andrey responded, “and you, Petya, knock your dumbbells over my head more quietly.” What floor does Andrey live on?

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Domashenko, A. Savin, G. Galperin, A. Shvetsov, M. Rollova.
