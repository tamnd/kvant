Problems of introductory work at the Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics in 1991
1. Which number is greater: $\sqrt{28}$ or $\sqrt{85}$?
2. Does the equation
$$x^2 + \frac{p_1 + p_2}{2}x + \frac{q_1 + q_2}{2} = 0$$
at least one solution if each of the equations $x^2 + p_1x + q_1 = 0$ and $x^2 + p_2x + q_2 = 0$ has no solutions?
3. Given seven numbers. The sum of any three of them is 0. What are these numbers?
4. Prove that the sum of the distances from any point of a regular hexagon to its vertices is not less than its perimeter.
5. Prove that for any integers $n_1, \dots, n_{1991}$ the number $|n_1 - n_2| + |n_2 - n_3| + \dots + |n_{1990} - n_{1991}| + |n_{1991} - n_1|$ is even.
6. Prove that any natural number whose decimal notation consists of 5$n$ ones is divisible by 41.
7. At the same time, two submachine gunners left two points and directed each other. After what time will they meet, if it is known that if the speed of the first was doubled, they would meet a quarter of an hour earlier, and if the initial speed of the first remained unchanged and the second was doubled, they would have met 24 minutes earlier?
8. Is it possible to place 7 points on a plane and connect neoketors from them with segments so that the segments do not intersect and only 3 segments come out of each point?
9. Find all pairs of natural numbers $m$ and $n$ satisfying the equation $19m + 91n = 1991$.

10. Teams $A$ And $B$ play volleyball. The perfectionist has established a special prize, which will be received by the team that makes sure that when submitting, the team $A$ The 26th touch of the ball will be on the other team (we assume that after the serve the ball is in play for quite a long time). Can $A$ play this way to be sure to win a special prize? We nominate that when serving, one touch is made; on the side of one team, from 1 to 3 touches are made before passing to the other.

A new example in the All-Union correspondence multi-subject scale for the departments “Matematika” and “Languages ​​and Literature”

All-Union correspondence multi-subject school of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosova *) accepts eighth-grade students of general education schools and vocational schools for individual training, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

Introductory work (“Mathematics”)

1. The distance from the village of Vorobyovo to the village of Grachevo is an integer number of kilometers. What could this distance be equal to if it is known that from Vorobyovo to the village of Drozdovo is 2.5 kilometers, and from Grachev to Drozdov is 1.3 kilometers?

2. In an equilibrium trapezoid, the lateral side is equal to the smaller base, and the diagonal is equal to the larger one. Find the angles of the trapezoid.

3. The library contains no more than 5,000 books. If you connect them 5, 6 or 7 books per pack, then each time there is one line of books left, and if you connect 11 books, then there is no extra book left. How many books are there in the library?

4. Find all pairs of positive integers (x, y) such that
xy = x + y + 1990.

5. From the two ends A and B of the rural road, two volospedents simultaneously start towards each other. One travels the entire route AB in 21 minutes, the other in 19 minutes. Having reached A or B, each of them instantly breaks up and goes in the opposite direction. How many times will they meet face to face before the second tax is asked?

6. Two circles whose radii are R and r touch the same line at points A and B, respectively, and also touch each other.
