Dear readers!

In front of you is number 253 «Kvant». Over the two decades of its existence, the magazine undoubtedly changed - we abandoned some things, new sections appeared... We were getting older and trying to keep up with life, science, and school. But the magazine has acquired its own appearance, and we intend to maintain it this year.
What awaits you in «Kvant»?
Stories about the history and achievements of science, about the problems that physicists and mathematicians are working on, about modern discoveries, interviews with major scientists, their thoughts about science and the path of its development - these materials are placed on the first pages of the magazine.
Our “Problem Book” is a section that is continued from issue to issue. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. Every day we hold a competition for the best solution to these problems. The winners receive the right to participate in the national rounds of the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren.
Materials related to the school curriculum, clarifying the most difficult issues of the school course - the content of the heading “School in «Kvant».
Those who are planning to enter universities will find useful materials in the sections “Applicant's Workshop” and “Options for Entrance Exams”.
«Kvant» for younger schoolchildren is a section in which tasks are published that require not only specific knowledge, but also communication skills and the ability to think logically. We hope that the statistics placed in this section are accessible and interesting to all schoolchildren - lovers of mathematics and physics.
For those who love physical experiments, see the materials in the “Laboratory «Kvant»” section. They will help you develop observation skills, learn how to conduct experiments, and make simple instruments.
“Mathematical circle” - for participants in mathematical circles, today and tomorrow.
For those for whom “P means package”, materials are intended on the theory of space flight, as well as on the development and problems of astronautics, on space exploration, and on the work of the All-Union Youth Aerospace Society.
What else? “Kaleidoscope”, “Fiction”, “Games and Puzzles”, “Chess Page”, “Olympiads”...
Will you like the magazine? What will be the most interesting and useful for you? Your opinion is very important to us. That’s why we publish our “Questionnaire” once a quarter.
We are waiting for your letters!
