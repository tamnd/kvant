Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology
Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{6(\text{tg} x + \text{ctg} 2x)} = -\frac{2}{\sin x}.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\log \frac{16}{x^2} - 1 (\log_3 x) < 0.$$
3. The bisquetrixes of angles $B$ and $C$ of parallelogram $ABCD$ intersect at point $O$. Find the area of ​​the parallelogram if
$$\angle A = 2 \arcsin \frac{2}{\sqrt{13}}, OA = 2\sqrt{10}, OD = 5$$
(find all solutions).
4. On the coordinate plane $xOy$, a triangle with vertices at points $A(0; 0)$, $B\left(\frac{2}{3}; 0\right)$, $C(0; -2)$ is given. To the function graph
$$y = \frac{(3x-1)^2}{3x}, x > 0$$, a tangent is drawn that cuts off from the triangle $ABC$ a quadrilateral around which a circle can be described. Find the distance from the origin to this tangent.
5. Given a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ and a cone, the center of which lies on the straight line $SO$ (SO is the height of the paramid). Point $E$ is the middle of edge $SD$, point $F$ lies on edge $AD$, and $AF = \frac{3}{2} FD$. The triangle, which is one of the axial sections of the cone, is located so that two of its vertices lie on the line $CD$, and the third - on the line $EF$. Find the volume of the cone if $AB=4$, $SO=3$.
Option 2
1. Solve the inequality
$$\log_9 x + 4 \leq 4 \log_x 3 - 1.$$
2. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \text{ctg}^4 x + 32 \sin^2 y = 55, \\ \frac{1}{\sin^2 x} - 4 \cos y = 5. \end{cases}$$
3. The function $y = \frac{1}{6} x^2 + 6x + 56$ is the difference of the cubes of two linear functions. Find those functions.
4. In the triangle $ABC$ a bissertic $AP$ is drawn. It is known that $BP=16$, $PC=20$ and that the center of the circle described by rounder $ABP$ lies on the segment $AC$. Find the length of side $AB$.
5. In the triangular paramid $SABC$, the area of ​​the base $ABC$ is 7, and the angles $ABC$, $ASB$ and the dihedral angle at the edge $AB$ are right. Consider the projections of the paramid $SABC$ onto all possible planes passing through the line $AB$. The largest of the areas of such projections is 14, and the smallest is $4\sqrt{3}$. Find the volume of the paramid.

Atmospheric pressure is assumed to be equal to the pressure created by a layer of water $h_0=10$ m high. The pressure of saturated vapor of water at the temperature of the experiment is prebeche.
3. Two identical conductive rings, each with a radius $R$, move translationally in one plane, pushing water against each other by a process passing through their centers (Fig. 2). A uniform magnetic field with induction $B$ acts perpendicular to the plane of the rings.
