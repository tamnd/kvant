Selected school problems in physics

9th grade

1. A block of mass $M$ lies on a smooth horizontal plane along which it can move without friction (Fig. 1). A body of mass $m$ lies on a block. The friction coefficient between the body and the beam is $μ$. At what value of force $F$, applied to the block in the horizontal direction, does the body begin to slide along the block?

2. Find the relative change in body weight caused by the daily rotation of the Earth around its axis when the weight changes at the equator and at the pole. The earth can be considered a sphere with a radius of $R=6400$ km.

3. Find the accelerations of bodies whose masses are $m_1$ and $m_2$, and the tension forces of the threads in the system depicted in figure 2. The mass of the blocks and threads, as well as the friction in the blocks, can be neglected.

4. A light cart with a ball suspended on a thread approaches an inclined plane (Fig. 3). In what direction from the vertical will the thread with the ball deviate when the cart begins to roll onto the inclined plane?

5. Two identical weightless blocks with parallel axes are installed at the same height. Will the system remain in equilibrium?

10th grade

6. At the vertices of the square on the side $a$ there are four charges: two of them are equal to $+q$ and two are equal to $-q$. Determine the direction of the electric field at the point of intersection of the diagonals of the square. Distance all possible mergers.

7. Two identical exact charges are located at a distance of $r_1=1$ m from each other. The magnitude of each charge is $q=10^{-10}$ Cl. How much work must be done to lick the charges to a distance of $r_2=10$ cm?

8. A picture of the location of equivalent surfaces of a certain electrostatic field (Fig. 5) is given. It is also known that $\varphi_1>\varphi_2$. Based on this picture, reconstruct an approximate picture of the lines of directions of this field and indicating their directions. In which area is the field direction greater?

9. Three identical charged particles with a mass of $m=2$ g and a charge of $q=10^{-8}$ C were each placed at the vertices of an equilateral triangle on the side $a=10$ cm. Then the particles were simultaneously released, after which they began to scatter symmetrically under the influence of Coulomb repulsive forces. Find the maximum particle speed.

10. The capacitor is connected to the battery. By moving the capacitor plates apart, we overcome the forces of electrostatic attraction between its plates and, therefore, perform positive work. What is this work for? What happens to the energy of the capacitor?
