5. The vessel with the gas is divided by a movable partition into two parts, the ratio of the volumes of which is $V_1/V_2=2/3$. The gas temperatures in the smaller and larger volumes are $t_1=177^\circ\text{C}$ and $t_2=267^\circ\text{C}$, respectively; the pressure in them is the same. What will be the volume ratio if the temperatures are equal? Heat exchange is possible only through the partition.

6. In an electric kettle with a power of $P=600$ W, you can boil $V=1,5$ liters of water in $t=20$ minutes at an initial water temperature of $t=20^\circ\text{C}$. Find the efficiency of the kettle. Specific heat production of water $c=4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K).

7. Two series-connected capacitors, whose capacitances are $C=1$ µF and $C_2=3$ µF, are connected to a current source with a direction of $U=220$ V. Find the direction on each capacitor.

8. The heating coil of an electric device for evaporating water at a temperature of $t=100^\circ\text{C}$ has a resistance of $R=10$ Ohm. What current must be passed through these spirals for the apparatus to evaporate a mass of water $m=100$ g in a time $t=1$ min? Specific heat of vaporization of water $\lambda=2,3$ MJ/kg.

9. At what speed must the conductor of the perpendicular line of induction of a uniform magnetic field move in order for a potential difference $U=0,01$ V to arise between the conductor capacitors? Magnetic field induction $B=0,2$ T, conductor length $l=10$ cm.

10. Checking his glasses, the man received on the floor of the room a real image of a lamp, highest at a height of $H=3$ m, holding the glass glass under the lamp at a distance of $f=1$ m from the floor. What is the optical power of the glasses?
