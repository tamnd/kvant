Fourth international tournament in mathematics "Friendship - 90"

From May 26 to May 31, 1990, the IV mathematics tournament “Friendship - 90” was held at the Starozagora Mineral Springs resort, in which 65 students in grades 8–11 from Bulgaria (Stara Zagora, Kazanlak, Haskovo, Shumen, Kardzhali, Gabrovo and Veliko Tarnovo), Poland (Legnica), the USSR (Sosnovy Bor, Belorets, Evpatria, Yerevan, Almaty) and Romania (Brasov). The participants received the right to compete in the tournament based on the results of two correspondence rounds of the “Friendship - 90” competition. The organizer of the tour is the school center for mathematics and information in the city of Stara Zagora.

The tour took place over two days, separately for grades 8-9 and 10-11. On the first day, Ivan Daykov (8th grade, Kazanlak), Nicoleta Voicu (9th grade, Brasov), Kazimira Georgieva (11th grade, Haskovo), Olga Dubova (11th grade, Belorets) won.

The winners of the second day were Nadezhda Hristova (8th grade, Haskovo), Bogdan Socea (8th grade, Brasov), Kamen Lozev (9th grade, Haskovo), Evgeniy Tsybanov (9th grade, Belorets), Mihai Pascu (11th grade, Brasov) and Stefan Stanciu (11th grade, Brasov).

The overall winners were: Mihai Pascu, 41 windows out of 42, Stefan Stanciu - 40, Evgeniy Tsybanov - 39 and Kamen Lozev - 39.

The next tour is expected to take place in the Polish city of Legnica.

We offer you several tasks of the IV tour.

1 (8 grades). Point M is the midpoint of the side of the square ABCD, and P is the base of the perpendicular drawn from the vertex C to the line BM. Prove that DP = DC.

2 (8 grades). The numbers a, b, c are coprime integers and

$$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{c}.$$

Prove that a-b, a-c, b-c are squares of integers.

3 (8 grades). Prove that there are no natural numbers x, y for which

$$7^x - 1 = 12(2y + 1)^2.$$

4 (9 grades). Find the most natural number n for which n² + 1990n is the square of the integer.

5 (9 grades). A circle is drawn into a corner with vertex O and two diametrically opposite points A and B are selected on it, different from the points of contact on the side of the corner. A tangent to the circle is drawn through B, which intersects the sides of the angle at points C and D. Taking OA intersects CD at point E. Prove that BC = DE.

6 (9 grades). Prove that there are no positive numbers x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ (n ≥ 3) for which

$$x_1 + \frac{x_2}{2^2} + \frac{x_3}{3^2} + ... + \frac{x_n}{n^2} = \frac{n-2}{n+1}$$

$$\frac{1}{2^2x_1} + \frac{1}{3^2x_2} + \frac{1}{4^2x_3} + ...$$

$$... + \frac{1}{(n+1)^2x_n} = \frac{n+2}{n+1}.$$

7 (10 grades). The numbers α₁, α₂, ..., αₙ form an arithmetic progression. The numbers cos α₁, cos α₂, ..., cos αₙ also form an arithmetic progression. It is known that cos α₁ = \frac{1}{2} and cos αₙ = -\frac{1}{2}.

Find n.

8 (10 grades). What is the most suitable triangle, one of whose sides is d, and its two medians intersect at right angles?

9 (10 grades). Find all integers a, b, c for which a² + b² + c² = 1990abc.

L. Lyubenov (Bulgaria)
