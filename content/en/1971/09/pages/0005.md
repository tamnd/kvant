The advantages of the dual system for computers do not end with the fact that it allows the direct use of elements with two sources. To build a computer, the total number of different sources of all elements is important. And in this respect, the double numerical system turns out to be more advantageous than the decimal one. Indeed, to write, for example, integers in the interval 1≤N≤999 in the decimal system, three elements (one for each digit), with ten sources for each, are sufficient. In total we will get 30 possible sources. The binary system will require ten elements, since $2^9=512<999$ and $2^{10}=1024>999$, but there are only two sources each, that is, a total of 20 possible sources.

Another advantage of the dual system is associated with the peculiarities of arithmetic operations. In all positional systems, numerical arithmetic operations on multi-digit numbers are related to actions on single-digit numbers, so to perform them it is enough to know the tables of addition and multiplication of single-digit numbers. In the dual system the tables are also extremely simple:

$$0+0=0 \quad 0 \times 0=0$$
$$0+1=1+0=1 \quad 0 \times 1=1 \times 0=0$$
$$1+1=10 \quad 1 \times 1=1$$

Since there are no other digits here except zero and ones, then multiplying multi-digit numbers only involves shifting numbers and addition. Thus, the basis of the arithmetic device of a computer operating in a double number system is a multi-digit double adder that adds two multi-digit double numbers. With its help, all other arithmetic operations are performed. In turn, a multi-bit adder can be composed of single-digit double adders, one of which is designed to add elements of the same bit.

Such a single-valued double adder must have three inputs: two of them receive the values ​​of the corresponding bits of the two terms, and the third is the ability to transfer to this bit from the least significant one. This adder should have two outputs: one of them is the amount that should be received in a given digit, and the second is a possible transfer from this digit to the highest one.
