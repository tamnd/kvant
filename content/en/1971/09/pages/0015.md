Ostrogradsky's works on mathematical analysis are in most cases caused by his research in mathematical physics and mechanics: they provide solutions to mathematical questions posed by the theoretical natural sciences of that time. Thus, in connection with his studies of the propagation of a body in a solid, he obtained a significant formula, which is now included in all textbooks of mathematical analysis under the name of the Ostrogradsky-Green formula. Currently, this formula plays a huge role in mathematical physics, vector analysis and other branches of mathematics and its applications. It would not be an exaggeration to say that Ostrogradsky made outstanding contributions in the field of mathematical analysis. His results have entered modern mathematics as an essential and integral part of it and represent that necessary weapon without which mathematics can no longer do.

Ostrogradsky's interests also included algebra, number theory, and probability theory. According to N. E. Zhukovsky, “in the works of M. V. Ostrogradsky we will be attracted by the society of analysis, the main idea, as broad as the expanse of his native fields.”

 Ostrogradsky rendered an invaluable service to Russian science by educating a whole galaxy of talented students who later became outstanding representatives of Russian science. Among his students were: I. A. Vyshnegradsky - the founder of the theory of automatic regulation; N.P. Petrov is the creator of the hydrodynamic theory of lubrication and the author of classical studies on the theory of mechanisms; A. N. Tikhomandrikiy, E. I. Beyer, D. M. Delarue, E. F. Sabinin are famous professors of mathematics, and many other mathematicians and outstanding engineers.

The remarkable thing about the leading representatives of the Russian natural science community and mathematicians in particular was that they were not only innovators in the sciences, bold in their thoughts, but also teachers in the broad sense of the word; They did not confine themselves to the circle of their scientific interests, but devoted a significant part of their time and energy to the cause of education.

The progressive significance of the pedagogical activities of Russian mathematicians. It lies not only in their personal teaching, but in the fact that they took an active part in the general pedagogical movement and had a significant influence on the development of mathematical education and culture in Russia. The pedagogical activity of M. V. Ostrogradsky is especially characteristic in this regard.

A man of his time, a brave thinker and a brilliant lecturer, he worked a lot and fruitfully in the pedagogical field.
