M61. Two wise men play a new game, which consists of the following. The numbers 0, 1, 2,..., 1024 are written out. The first sage crosses out 512 numbers of his choice, the second crosses out 256 of the remaining numbers, then again the first crosses out another 128, then the second – another 64 numbers, etc. With his last fifth move, the second crosses out one number. There will be two numbers left, and the second one pays the first one the difference between these numbers. How should you play the first game to get as much as possible? How can the second one lose as little as possible? How much will the second one pay the first one if she plays better?
