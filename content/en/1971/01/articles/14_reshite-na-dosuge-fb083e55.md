⟦folio 61⟧

Decide on access

1. The globe was pulled along the equator with a steel hoop, then the diameter of the hoop was increased so that the length of the hoop increased by 1 m. Will a cat be able to crawl into the gap formed between the earth and the hoop (we consider the Earth to be a ball with a radius of 6400 km) (Fig. 1)?

Fig. 1.

2. Here, two numbers are divided in the ternary number system. Decipher!

$$\frac{1}{2} \frac{1}{2} \frac{1}{2}$$

3. There are five bowls of different weights and a lever scale, the structure of which is shown in figure 2. Each pan of the scale is identical together.

Using no more than seven weighings, arrange the bulzhniks in descending order of their weights.

Fig. 2.
