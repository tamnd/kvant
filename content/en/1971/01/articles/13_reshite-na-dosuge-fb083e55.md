3. $l/e$ electrons fly through the capacitor per unit time. Since the field of the capacitor is electric, then far from the capacitor, before and after the passage of electrons, they must have the same energy and, therefore, the same momentum $mv$. This means that each electron passing through the capacitor transfers momentum $mv \sin \alpha$ to it in the vertical direction. Per unit time, the capacitor is supplied with a pulse $\frac{l}{e} mv \sin \alpha$. If we neglect the vertical deflection of the capacitor and use Hooke's law, we find that the additional distance of the spring is $bmv \frac{l}{eP} \sin \alpha$.

4. Let the fixed point be at a distance $x$ from the lower end of the roof when it is heated and at a distance $y$ from it when it is cooled. Since the point is motionless, the sum of the forces applied to it is equal to zero:

$$\left(l' - x\right) P \sin \varphi + \left(l' - x\right) kP \cos \varphi =
= xP \sin \varphi - kP \cos \varphi.$$

Hence $x = l' \frac{\sin \varphi + k \cos \varphi}{2k \cos \varphi}$.

Same as $y = l' \frac{\sin \varphi - k \cos \varphi}{2k \cos \varphi}$.

If $l' = l \left[ 1 + \alpha \left(t_2 - t_1\right) \right]$, then in $N$ days the leaf will slide a distance

$$\Delta L = N \alpha l \left(t_2 - t_1\right) \frac{\mathrm{tg} \varphi}{k}.$$
