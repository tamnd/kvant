⟦folio 37⟧

From the examiner's notebook

An X-ray tube consists of a glass balloon filled with vacuum.

Light sources are divided into two camps: natural and artificial.

The unit of frequency, the hertz, is named after the scientist who discovered this unit.

Inductance is measured in hens.

The further the object is from the lens, the more real the image will be; the closer, the smaller.

Lenses can be double-convex or single-convex.

The atom is complex and it fooled scientists for a long time.

All the objects around us: tables, chairs, etc. press on us with a force equal to their weight.

Normal people have good vision, both near and at a distance.

Gamma rays are highly penetrating.

Saturated vapors differ from unsaturated ones in that they have already been saturated.

The red beam is the longest, and the violet beam is the shortest.

Angular velocity is expressed in omegas.
