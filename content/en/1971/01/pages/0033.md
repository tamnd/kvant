Fig. 6.

It is advisable to use the construction of an auxiliary circle when solving some construction problems.

Let's consider another difficult problem, in which the most difficult thing to solve is to guess how to describe a circle around the squares.

Example 6. On sides AC and BC of triangle ABC, squares ACA₁A₂ and BCB₁B₂ are constructed outside it. Prove that lines AB₁, A₁B and A₂B₂ intersect at one point, and determine the angles between these lines.

Solution. If ∠ACB = 90°, then the validity of the theorem is obvious. Lines AB₁ and A₁B are redefined and each of them intersects line A₂B₂ at point C at an angle of 45° (Fig. 6).

If ∠ACB ≠ 90°, then the circles circumscribed about the squares have, in addition to the point C, another common point C₁, which turns out to be the intersection point of the lines AB₁, A₁B and A₂B₂ (Fig. 7). Indeed, ∠A₂C₁C = 90°, like a hanging one, resting on a semicircle. Similarly ∠CC₁B₂ = 90°. Therefore, ∠A₂C₁C + ∠CC₁B₂ = 180°, i.e. rays C₁A₂ and C₁B₂ form a straight line.

In the same way, it is proved that the point C₁ lies on the lines AB₁ and A₁B.

Considering the obtained inscribed angles, we find that the straight lines AB₁ and A₁B are mutually redefined and each of these straight lines makes an angle of 45° with the straight line A₂B₂.

The next task has a lot in common with the previous one.

8. On the sides AB, BC and CA of an arbitrary triangle ABC, equilateral triangles ABC₁, BCA₁ and CAB₁ are constructed outside it. Prove that lines AA₁, BB₁, CC₁ intersect at one point and each of them forms an angle of 60° with the other.

9. Prove that if two opposite angles of a quadrilateral are obtuse, then the diagonal connecting the vertices of these angles is shorter than the second diagonal.

Let us present two more problems involving maximum and minimum, for solving which it is also advisable to construct an auxiliary circle in a certain way.

10. On one side of the angle with the vertex at point S are the given points A and B. Find on the other side the point C from which the segment AB is visible at the most angle.

11. From an arbitrary point M lying on side AB of triangle ABC (or on its extension), perpendiculars MP and MQ are drawn to sides AC and BC. Under what conditions will the segment PQ be the longest?

Thus, various geometric problems are solved using the same technique. The construction of an auxiliary circle allows you to increase the number of theorems that can be used when solving a problem, and thanks to this, you can find dependencies between the elements of the figure.
