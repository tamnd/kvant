⟦folio 56⟧

ATTENTION
EIGHTH-GRADER STUDENTS!
CORRESPONDENCE MATH SCHOOL
ANNOUNCES ADMISSION OF STUDENTS

Only students of the highest grades are admitted to ZMSH. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into ZMSH. Classes at the school will begin on September 1.

ZMSH has two courses. School education is free.

This issue «Kvant» offers problems that serve as an introductory test for correspondence mathematics schools at Moscow State University and Leningrad State University. Those who want to enter the ZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 10, 1971. After a performance review (around July 1971), you will be notified whether you have been accepted into the ZMSH.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from regular school ones, no additional knowledge of mathematics is required to solve them.

In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to solve every problem without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. The answer without any further explanation may not be read. If there are several possible divisions in a problem, then all of them must be indicated.

Work must be completed in Russian in a student’s notepad in Klatek. Introductory works will not be sent out.

We ask you not to fold the terradi when sending it. In the envelope along with the terradi you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14x6 cm with your postal address written on it (we will paste it on the envelope when we send the answer).

Paste a sheet of checkered paper onto the cover of the terradi, draw it and fill it out according to the following sample (your work will not be checked!):

Region:
Last name, first name, year of birth:
School (full name):
Class:

Parent position:
