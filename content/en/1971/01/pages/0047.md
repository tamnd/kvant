ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS
IN MATHEMATICS IN MATHEMATICS
FACULTY OF MSPI
them. V. I. LENIN IN 1970
E. A. Shegolkov

The written examination in mathematics at our department in 1969 (the report on it can be read in «Kvant» No. 3 for 1970) was somewhat unusual. The unusualness of the state is in some imitation of competitive exams at Moscow State University. As a result, out of 475 applicants, 224, that is, about half, wrote the paper with a bad mark and dropped out of the further “struggle.” This method of exchanging applicants does not seem suitable for a pedagogical institute; too many candidates ended up without personal acquaintance with the examiners.

In 1970, the selection committee of the Faculty of Mathematics decided on almost different paths. The written work included four tasks to get to know the student well.
1. Stereometric problem with application of trigonometry.
2. The task of composing a quadratic equation with physical or other content.
3. Algebraic equations or inequality.
4. Trigonometric equation.

All tasks did not require any special knowledge or training. But at the same time, they assumed a thorough knowledge of school mathematics and known abilities of applicants. 4 hours were allotted to complete the written work.

When assessing written work, “five” was given only if all tasks were completed; “four” - if all tasks were completed, but with minor shortcomings. “Two” was given, for example, if the first tasks were not solved due to a group of errors. At the same time, the rating of “three” acquired a greater share and did not exclude the applicant from participation in the competition.

In 1970, 508 people entered the Faculty of Mathematics, and there were 175 places; the competition was about three people per place.

Of the 508 applicants for the written work, 120 wrote it as “four” and “five”, 233 as “three” and 155 as “two”.
As an example, below we will analyze the written work option and point out the typical mistakes made by applicants when solving it.

Option

1. The length of the generatrix of the cone is equal to l, the angle of the generatrix with the plane of the base is equal to α. Find the volume of a primid circumscribed around a cone whose base is a rhombus with an acute angle β.
2. In n hours, the tractor plows p hectors more than the horse. How many hectors can a horse plow in n hours and how many hectors can a tractor plow in n hours, if a tractor plows one hector in t hours faster than a horse.
3. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{1+2x}-\sqrt{x+4}<1.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{3(\cos 2x+\mathrm{ctg} 2x)}{\mathrm{ctg} 2x-\cos 2x}-2(\sin 2x+1)=0.$$
