Kepler's third law
If now in the formula (*) for the period of revolution of the planet together the energy $E$ substitute her expression with a larger one $\left( E = \gamma \frac{mM}{2a} \right)$, then we get
$$T = 2\pi a^{3/2} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\gamma M}}.$$
Therefore, $T^2$ is proportional to $a^3$, that is, the square of the planet's orbital period is proportional to the cube of the large half-ellipse. And this is Kepler's third law.
