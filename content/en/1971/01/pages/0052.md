⟦folio 50⟧

Those who entered with uncertainty solved problems involving construction both on a plane and in space. Especially noticeable among the overwhelming majority of applicants is a shallow and incomplete familiarity with the concept of function. Many had difficulty giving examples of functional dependencies; few showed a good understanding and use of the properties of monotonicity and periodicity of functions.

And finally, about the concept of limit. This most important concept is captured in most cases on the surface. And it doesn’t even come to talk about applying the limit. Thus, the sections of the school mathematics course related to the concept of limit are not sufficiently mastered.

At the same time, it is worth noting the positive aspects of schoolchildren’s knowledge of mathematics.

The mastery of formal computing apparatus and trigonometry became better, they were more confident in constructing graphs of various functions, they were better able to understand logarithms, and they were more fluent (compared to simple years) with trigonometric skills.

It should be noted that the number of excellent written papers and excellent answers in the oral examination in mathematics has increased compared to previous years. It is also encouraging that among the honors students there are many young people from rural areas.

In conclusion, we offer readers for independent exercise one genuine option, and one combined version, compiled from problems of various options offered in the exams.

Option 1

1. At the base of the primada lies an isosceles triangle with a perimeter $2p$ and an angle $\alpha$ at the base. Determine the volume of the base if its side faces are inclined to the plane of the base at an angle of $\beta$.

2. Two cars are moving uniformly in one direction along a circle of radius $R$. One of them makes a full circle $t$ seconds faster than the other. The time between two consecutive meetings is automatically equal to $a$. Determine the speed of the vehicle automatically.

3. For what real non-negative values ​​of $a$ the equation
$$a + x - x^2 = \sqrt{a + x} + x$$
has real non-negative roots? Find these roots.

4. Solve the equation
$$(1 + \sin 2x)(\cos x - \sin x) = 1 - 2\sin^2 x.$$
Option 2 (prefabricated)

1. The ball is inscribed in a right triangle. In this triangle, the perpendicular dropped from the vertex of the right angle to the hypotenuse has length $h$ and makes an angle $\alpha$ with one of the legs. Find the volume of the primed.

2. A piece of equal weight was cut from two pieces of the alloy with different percentages of copper, weighing $m$ kg and $n$ kg. Each of the cut pieces was fused with the remainder of the other piece, after which the percentage of copper in both alloys became the same. How much did each of the cut pieces weigh?

3. Solve inequality
$$\log_{0.5}x + \sqrt{1 - 4(\log_{0.5}x)^2} < 1.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\cos x}{\text{ctg}^2 \frac{x}{2} - \text{tg}^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{1}{8} \left(1 - \frac{2 \text{ctg} x}{1 + \text{ctg}^2 x}\right).$$
