Can you create an equation based on the conditions of the problem?

Others, having correctly composed the equation and solved it, did not examine the resulting roots from the point of view of their suitability as a solution to the problem.

Still others, although they examined the obtained roots of the equation, were unable to choose the desired solution.

When solving algebraic and trigonometric equations, they were often reduced to expressions containing unknowns. As a result, the roots of the equation were rubbed. In other cases, they did not pay attention to the ODZ and because of this, the roots were built.

A few words about the oral exam. The exam papers contained two questions. The first is in geometry, the second is in algebra or trigonometry. The wording of the exam questions almost verbatim repeated the wording of the 1970 university entrance exam program.

When answering the questions on the ticket, it was necessary to give an accurate proof of a theorem or derivation of a formula, and to accurately formulate the necessary definitions.

In addition to the questions on the exam paper, the applicant was given additional tasks or asked questions similar to these.

1. Graph the function:

a) $y = \lg \frac{1}{2}x$;
b) $y = -x^2 - x$;
c) $y = \frac{2}{x} - 3$;
d) $y = \sin 2x$;
e) $y = \cos \frac{x}{2}$.

2. Solve the equation:

a) $2^x = -1$;
b) $|2x - 3| = 1$;
c) $\cos 2x = -\frac{1}{2}$;

d) $\arcsin x = x$.

3. Solve inequality:

a) $\lg \frac{1}{2}x > 0$; 6) $x^2 > 5$;
b) $\frac{6}{x - 2} < 0$;
g) $|x - 2| \ge 1$.

4. Which sign has the numbers $\sin 3,14$; $\cos \frac{9\pi}{10}$; $\sin 2$?

5. Find $\sin \left(2\arcsin \frac{1}{3}\right)$.

6. Find the period of the function $y = \sin 3x$.

7. Construct a circle of given radius passing through two given points.

8. Give an example of two incommensurable segments.

9. Can the sides of a triangle have a ratio of 1:2:3?

10. Prove that in a right triangle, the median drawn from the vertex of the right angle is equal to half the hypotenuse.

11. Under what conditions can a ball be inscribed in a right circular cylinder?

12. Find the angle between the diagonal of the cube and the plane of its base.

Applicants showed much weaker knowledge on the following issues: generalization of the concept of exponents, complex numbers, number sequences, infinite geometric progression, method of mathematical induction, volumes and surfaces of bodies. Not all applicants gave clear definitions of trigonometric functions, circumference, area of ​​a circle, volume and surface of a cylinder and cone.

The following concepts are not well understood: irrational and real numbers, absolute magnitudes of numbers, consistent and incommensurable segments, radian measure of angles.

In geometrics, construction problems are solved incorrectly. Many applicants have an underdeveloped imagination.
