⟦folio 11⟧

Quantum for junior schoolchildren

1. Two numbers were multiplied on the board. Then some of the numbers were erased and replaced with asterisks. Try to restore the erased numbers.

$$\begin{array}{c}
*2 \\
*7 \\
*** \\
*** \\
\end{array}$$

2. On the chessboard there is a checker in the lower left corner. Two players take turns moving the checker to the adjacent field. Only the driving directions indicated on figure 1 are allowed.

The winner is the one who, using his own move, places a checker on the upper right field. How should a beginner play to win?

3. After some replacement of numbers with letters, it turned out that

$$mри + mри + mри = дыра$$

and ($и + ii$): $ii = ii$. What numbers correspond to the letters used in these equations? (Different numbers correspond to different letters.)

4. Try to go around the figure at figure 2 without lifting your pencil from the paper or going over the same line twice.
