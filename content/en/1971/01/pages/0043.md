SUBSTITUTION METHOD FOR SOLVING EQUATIONS AND SYSTEMS OF EQUATIONS

M. I. Scanavi

The substitution method involves moving from the original value to some other value. It allows you to reduce solutions to equations to more simply constructed ones or to equations of studied types. There is a great variety of cases when one has to use this method. It is impossible to cover them with any general recommendation. Therefore, success in choosing an appropriate substitution significantly depends on such advantages of the decision as his experience, ingenuity, intuition... The examples under consideration demonstrate the substitution method in action.

Example 1: Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{x(x+2)} - \frac{1}{(x+1)^2} = \frac{1}{12}.$$

Solution. The difference of the fractions located on the left side of this equation is a fraction whose denominator is a polynomial of four degrees with respect to $x$. In this case, template processing of the equation will lead it to an equation of four degrees, which the solver cannot do. After thinking about it, we will begin to understand that it is useful to choose a substitution
$$x^2 + 2x = z,$$
(*)

because it transforms the given equation into a much simpler one:
$$\frac{1}{z} - \frac{1}{z+1} = \frac{1}{12}$$

With respect to $z$ we obtain a quadratic equation with roots $z_1=3$ and $z_2=-4$. Substitution (*) results in two quadratic equations for $x$:
$$x^2 + 2x = 3$$
And
$$x^2 + 2x = -4.$$

From them we find all four roots of this equation:
$$x_1 = 1, x_2 = -3, x_3 = -1 + \sqrt{3}i,$$
$$x_4 = -1 - \sqrt{3}i$$

and by direct testing we are convinced of the suitability of each of them.

Example 2: Solve the equation
$$4x^4 + 12x^3 - 47x^2 + 12x + 4 = 0.$$

Solution. A so-called reciprocal equation has been proposed for solution - the coefficients of the terms equally spaced from the ends of its left side are equal. For reciprocal equations, a standard method has been developed for reducing them to an equation of lower degree. An idea of ​​this method can be gained by observing how it is applied to the equation that is being solved. Noticing that $x \neq 0$, we divide the equation by $x^2$; we get
$$4x^2 + 12x - 47 + \frac{12}{x} + \frac{4}{x^2} = 0.$$

$$4\left(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}\right) + 12\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right) - 47 = 0.$$
