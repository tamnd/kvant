⟦folio 57⟧

1. A semicircle of diameter 2 contains two circles that touch each other. Each one touches a diameter and a different circle. Find the diameter of one of them if the diameter of the other is 1.

2. Find the most common divisor of the numbers 987654321 and 123456789.

3. The bases of the trapezoid $ABCD$ are equal: $AB=a$, $CD=b$. O is the point of intersection of the diagonals. Determine the ratio of the area of ​​the triangle $OAB$ to the area of ​​the trapezoid.

4. It is known that $x+\frac{1}{x}$ is an integer.

Prove that then the number $x^8+\frac{1}{x^8}$ is also an integer.

5. The following 4 statements have been made about the triangle $ABC$:

1) Triangle $ABC$ is rectangular;
2) $\Rightarrow A=30^\circ$;
3) $AB=2BC$;
4) $AC=2BC$.

We know that two of these statements are true and the other two are false. Find the perimeter of triangle $ABC$ if $BC=1$.

6. It is known that $a+\theta+c<0$ and that the equation $ax^2+\theta x+c=0$ has no real roots. Determine what sign the number $c$ has?

7. The following is known about the points $A, B$ and $C$:

for any point $M$ on the plane, the segment $AM$ is less than at least one of the segments $BM$ and $CM$. Prove that the point $A$ lies on the segment $BC$.

8. What is the divisor of the number 1971$^{1971}$? (That is, how many different positive integers are there that this number is divisible by?)

9. Is it possible to wrap a cube with edge 1 in a square piece of paper with edge 3?

10. Is it possible to write down nine numbers 1, 2, 3 ..., 9 in a circle in such an order that the sum of any two adjacent numbers is not divisible by 3, 5, or 7?

11. Residents of the city of Pravdin always tell the truth, and residents of the city of Liars always lie. In one of these cities, a football match is arranged between the teams of these cities. After the match, next to the stadium where he was lost, the following conversation took place:

$A$ (addressing $B$ and $B$): I wasn't at the match. Tell me who won?

$B$: $\Gamma$ told me that their team lost.

$B$: Our team won.

$\Gamma$ (getting into a bus, going to another city and turning to $A$): Let’s serve together, $A$, because we are from the same city.

$A$: You're wrong, $\Gamma$. I live here. This is $B$ from your city.

Determine who from each city, where the match took place and which team won.

12. Trucks travel along the highway in one direction at a constant speed at regular intervals without stopping. However, the man walked 4 km along the highway, and during this time he was overtaken by 6 trucks. Another time he walked 7 km, and during this time he was overtaken by 8 auto carriers. The third time he walked 17 km. How many cars did he overtake? (All three times the person walked at the same speed).

13. On the base $AB$ of the equilateral triangle $ABC$, point $E$ is taken, and circles are inscribed in the triangle $ACE$ and $ECB$, which touch the segment $CE$ at points $K$ and $H$. Find the length of the segment $KH$ if $AE=a, EB=b$.
