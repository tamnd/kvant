What is “machine translation” and how is it different from machine translation? This is a smart distribution of responsibilities between man and machine. The machine is entrusted with doing what it can do well and quickly. The man does the rest. In practice this is done as follows. The translator reads the text he must translate and marks the words he does not understand. They are brought into the machine. She gives their translations. All this is simple for her. The remaining operations, which, as we have seen, are not easy to teach a machine, are performed by humans. The translation quality is excellent. The cost is less than not only “good”, but also “decent” translations.
