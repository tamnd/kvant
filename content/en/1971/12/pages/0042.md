Let us now present several problems from the exam papers.

1. A small ball of mass $m$, carrying a charge $q$, is suspended on a thread of length $l$. The ball moves along a circle lying in a horizontal plane, and a second, stationary ball carrying the same charge is fixed in the center of the circle. Find the angular velocity of the ball.

2. The air pressure inside the bottle, tightly sealed with a stopper, at a temperature of $7^\circ$ C was equal to 1 amm. To what temperature must the air in the bottle be heated in order for the cork to fly out of it, if it is known that in order to remove the cork from a cold bottle at $7^\circ$ C, it is necessary to do 1 J of work. The length of the plug is $l = 4$ amm, the section of the plug is $s = 4$ amm².

3. When manufacturing pressure increased by 0.5 mm, the gas density changed by 0.5 kD/m³. What was the initial density of the gas if the initial pressure was 1 amm?

4. A metal ball with a volume of 1 cm³ has a mass of 8 g and contains 8.6 x 10²² of free electricians. What part of these electrics must be removed from each of two such balls so that the layer of electrostatic repulsion completely compensates for the force of gravitational attraction between them ($\gamma = 6,67·10^{-11} \cdot \text{н} \cdot \text{m}^2/\text{к}^2$)?

5. The winding of the electric motor of the constructed current is made of wire with a total resistance of $R = 0,2$ ohm. When switched on with voltage $U = 120$v, current $I = 30$a flows through the winding of a running motor. Find the mechanical power, develop the electric motor.

6. Change of current in the oscillatory counterretor of the electrinite wave.

$I = 0,3 \sin 15,7 t$.

Find the length of the electrinite wave emitted by the circuit.

7. The rounder consists of two identical thin biconvex lenses having a focal length of 5 cm and located at a distance of 2.5 cm from each other. Find the distance between the focus of the eyepiece and the lens close to it.

8. Find the force acting at the point of attachment of the movable block to the ceiling if a load of mass 60 kg suspended from the block shown at figure is supported by the weight of a person who pulls a vertical downward rope.

9. How much work must be done to place three identical, located at infinity in the rest frame, exact charges of magnitude $q = 5 \cdot 10^{-8}$ k at the vertices of an equilateral triangle on a side of 5 cm ($\varepsilon = 1$)?

10. The potential difference at the battery terminals is 8.5 V, while a current of 3 A flows through the battery. If you change the direction of the current to the opposite and the magnitude of the current is reduced by 1 A, then the absolute value of the potential difference will increase by 2.5 V. Find Ed. 11. Two electric heaters, labeled 600 W, 220 W and 400 W, 220 W, are connected in series and connected to a 220 W network. How much electrical power is consumed by each heater?

12. At a distance of 15 cm from the center of the silvered Christmas tree ball there is a Christmas tree tree 1 cm long. The diameter of the ball is 8 cm, and the tree is located perpendicular to the symmetry axis of the ball passing through the middle of the tree. Find at what distance from the center of the ball the image of the tree is located and determine the size of the image.
