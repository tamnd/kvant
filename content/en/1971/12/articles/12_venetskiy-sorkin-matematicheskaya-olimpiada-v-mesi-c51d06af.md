Mathematical Olympiad at MESI

In 1932, the Moscow Economic and Statistical Institute was created. Now it occupies a leading position in the country in training specialists related to machine data processing necessary for managing complex systems.
This academic year will see the first graduation from the Faculty of Economic Cybernetics. The faculty trains mathematical engineers in computer software, as well as specialists in the use of mathematical methods in economics.
The use of modern computers is unthinkable without mathematics. That is why, when entering college, applicants will take two exams in mathematics. For the first time this year, exams in physics were taken using a computer.
This year, MESI is holding the 2nd Mathematical Olympiad for graduating high school students. The Olympiad is held in two types: I - correspondence, II - full-time.
Kuchasia in the second round is accepted by schoolchildren who have successfully completed the task of the first round and sent solutions no later than January 15, 1972 (by postmark). The full-time round will be conducted taking into account the computer (see «Kvant» No. 10, 1971). Each Olympiad participant admitted to the second round will be notified at the address indicated by him. Success in the Olympiad will be taken into account when entering the institute.
Solving problems must be completed in Russian in a student’s notebook. Our address: Moscow, G-435, V. Savinsky lane, 14, “Olympiad”.
