4. Compound $A$ is a slightly colored gas that easily dissolves in water without releasing any gaseous products, forming an acidic solution $B$. If $32,5$ mega $A$ is dissolved in water, then 10 ml of 0.1 M sodium hydroxide solution will be required to neutralize the resulting solution. Solution $B$ does not react with solutions of silver nitrate and potassium iodide, but gives a white precipitate with solution of potassium nitrate. When the solution $B$ interacts with a solution of ferrous sulfate acidified with concentrated sulfuric acid, the solution acquires a brown color. When the solution $B$ reacts with aluminum and excess sodium hydroxide, gas $B$ is released, which changes the color of the wet indicator paper. The solution $B$, when left in a glass flask for a long time, corrodes it. Determine the content of $A$ and write the equations for all the described reactions.
