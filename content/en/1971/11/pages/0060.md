Correspondence Olympiad
Aznat part of the USSR

We publish texts of correspondence Olympiad problems in mathematics, physics and chemistry for students of schools and other secondary educational institutions in Siberia, Kazakhstan, the Far East and Central Asia.

Those who successfully cope with the tasks of the correspondence Olympiad will be invited to regional, regional, resource Olympiads on an equal basis with the winner of the regional Olympiads. Invitations will be sent to public education organizations at home addresses. The best of the winners of regional and resource olympids will take part in the final type of the All-Oplympids, and will also be invited to the summer school of the Siberian branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences.

After each problem, the classes for whose students it is intended are indicated in parentheses, but you can also try your hand at problems for older classes. To become an Olympiad winner, you don’t have to solve many problems. Another ingenious solution alone is enough. But solving a problem doesn’t just mean giving an answer; it is necessary to justify it, to give precise evidence. The tasks do not require any knowledge beyond the high school curriculum. Solutions to the problem for each prerequisite must be sent in a separate standard format envelope. Write the name of the item on the envelope. The letter must be enclosed in an envelope with a return home address and a stamp; In this envelope you will be sent a notice of the results of the work. Solutions must be written very legibly. The conditions of the tasks are not rewritten, but their numbers are indicated.

At the beginning of the letter, do not forget to indicate (legibly):

1. Your last name, first name and patronymic.
2. Postal home address (detailed).
3. Class, school, college, technical, etc., address.
4. Last name, first name and patronymic of your teacher for this subject.

The last day you can still send a decision is February 1, 1972, postmarked. Letters sent later will not be considered.

Olympida participants send decisions to the address: Novosibirsk, 90, Olympida.

We wish all Olympiad participants the greatest success.

Olympics Committee
Siberian branch
Academy of Sciences of the USSR
