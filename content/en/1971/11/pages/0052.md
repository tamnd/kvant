Determine how many hours it will take us to finish one crane less power.

Of course, this problem can be solved in the usual way: introduce unknowns and create a system of two equations, and then solve it *). But the use of graphs greatly facilitates the rational choice of unknowns and finding connections between given quantities and the desired ones. In this case, the solution turns out to be shorter and more clear, since a perfect law appears and it becomes possible to use geometric properties, for example, the similarity of triangles.

Solution. Two low-power cranes complete the entire job (OA) in OF hours; Let's find the length of this segment. To do this, consider two pairs of similar triangles:

$$\triangle ODE \sim \triangle OMN,$$
from where $$\frac{DE}{MN} = \frac{5}{4,5};$$

$$\triangle CDE \sim \triangle FMN,$$
from where $$\frac{DE}{MN} = \frac{EC}{NF},$$

or $$\frac{5}{4,5} = \frac{OF + 2 - 5}{OF - 4,5}.$$ Hence the finding OF = 18. Since 18 hours is the operating time of two low-power cranes, one such crane will complete the movement in 36 hours.
