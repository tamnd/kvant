Fig. 8. Graphs of the movement of racers starting from one point.

line II and point 3 x on the Ox axis. Straight 1 B - graph of racer B's movement (every overtaking when moving around a circle and when overtaking took one lap more than the one being overtaken).

Similarly, at the point B₁ of the intersection of lines 1B and IV, we find point C on line III, determine its abscissa 2(3x-1)+1 and construct a graph—line 2C—of the movements of racer C.

The length of the ring highway can be taken as one and the speeds of the racers can be found (tangents of the slope angles of the graphs, determined from the corresponding right triangles):

$$v_A = \frac{1}{x}, \quad v_B = \frac{2}{3x-1}, \quad v_C = \frac{1}{2x-1}.$$

According to the conditions of the problem, at the moment 3x+3, the second overtaking of driver A by driver C will occur, therefore, the difference in the distances traveled by them (segment A'C') is equal to 2. We create the equation:

$$v_A(3x+3) - v_C(3x+1) = 2,$$

or

$$\frac{3x+3}{x} - \frac{3x+1}{2x-1} = 2.$$

Of its two roots, only x=3 (min.) satisfies the condition.

Exercises

1. The watermelons brought to the base are intended for two matagazins. The first matagazin immediately began transporting watermelons and transported them daily in equal weight portions. The second store arrived at the transportation of watermelons a days later and also transported them in identical, but different weight portions than the first store. Under these conditions, after b days from the start of transportation operations, half of the first initial quantity of watermelons remained at the base. How many days did it take for all the watermelons to be transported from the base if the stores finished transporting at the same time and the weight of the watermelons received by the first store is equal to the weight of the watermelons received by the second store?

2. (MSU, Faculty of Biology, 1968). The tank is filled with water using several pumps. First, three pumps of the same capacity were turned on, and 2.5 hours after the start of their operation, two more pumps of a different, but also identical capacity, were connected. As a result, 1 hour after connecting the two pumps, the water in the tank was 15 m³ short of its full volume, and the tank was still full. One of the two subclavian pumps could fill the tank in 40 hours. Find the volume of the tank.

3. (MSU, Faculty of Chemistry, 1970). An automatic vehicle went from point A to point B, and at the same time a velocipedict left from point B to point A. After the meeting they continued on their way. The car, having reached point B, immediately turned back and caught up with the vehicle two hours after the first meeting. How much time after the first meeting did the egal bicycle travel to point A, if it is known that by the time of the second meeting he had covered 2/3 of the entire path from B to A?

4. Two athletes run out simultaneously (the first from A to B, the second from B to A) and meet at a distance of a m from A. Having run the path AB to the end, each of them turns back, and they meet again at a distance of b m from B. Find the length of the path *).

5. (MSU, mechanics, 1970). Two runners start from one point on the standard ring road, and the third runner starts simultaneously with him in the same direction from a diametrically opposite point. After running 3 laps, the third runner for the first time since the start of the road. 2.5 minutes after this first, the runner reached third for the first time. How many laps per minute does the second runner run if the first one overtakes him once every 6 minutes?

*) This is problem No. 190 from N. S. book 3 of the algorithm “Competition problems in mathematics”. Kyiv, 1964. The use of graphs will suggest the complete condition for the existence of a solution to this problem: 3a>2b, which eludes the external author of the book (he only indicates the condition 3a>b, which is not sufficient).
