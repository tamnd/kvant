Fig. 4. Surface hardening of steel occurs as a result of the specific diffusion of carbon atoms into it. The middle part figure shows a micrograph, or structuring, of the distribution of carbon in a piece of steel, the surface of which is in contact with hydrocarbon. The top curve gives the percentage of carbon as a function of the distance to the surface of the piece of steel. The lower curve characterizes the strength of steel in different parts of the piece.
