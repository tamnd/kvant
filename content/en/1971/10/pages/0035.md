TASKS

In this issue, “Problem Book «Kvant»” is compiled from problems proposed at the final exams of the first All-Union Olympiad in Mathematics and Physics.

M106. Prove that if the numbers $p_1, p_2, q_1, q_2$ satisfy the inequality
$(q_1 - q_2)^2 + (p_1 - p_2)(p_1q_2 - p_2q_1) < 0,$
then square trinomials
$x^2 + p_1x + q_1$ and $x^2 + p_2x + q_2$
have real roots and between two roots of each of them lies the root of the other.

I. F. Sharyn

M107. a) Given a convex polygon $A_1A_2 \dots A_n$. (Fig. 1). On side $A_1A_2$ points $B_1$ and $D_2$ are taken, on side $A_2A_3$ - points $B_2$ and $D_3$, ..., on side $A_nA_1$ points $B_n$ and $D_1$ so that if we construct parallelograms $A_1B_1C_1D_1, A_2B_2C_2D_2, \dots, A_nB_nC_nD_n,$ then straight $A_1C_1, A_2C_2, \dots, A_nC_n$ will intersect at one point. Prove that
$A_1B_1 \cdot A_2B_2 \cdot \dots \cdot A_nB_n = A_1D_1 \cdot A_2D_2 \cdot \dots \cdot A_nD_n$.

6) Prove that the converse statement is also true for a triangle: let points $B_1$ and $D_2$ be chosen on side $A_1A_2$, points $B_2$ and $D_3$ on side $A_2A_3$, points $B_3$ and $D_1$ on side $A_3A_1$ so that if we construct parallelograms $A_1B_1C_1D_1, A_2B_2C_2D_2, \dots, A_nB_nC_nD_n,$ then the lines $A_1C_1, A_2C_2, \dots, A_nC_n$ will intersect at one point.

V. L. Gutemacher

M108. a) Prove that the line dividing the given triangle into two polygons of equal area and perimeter passes through the center of the circle inscribed in the triangle.

6) Prove a similar statement for an arbitrary polygon in which a circle can be inscribed.

Yu. I. Ionin
