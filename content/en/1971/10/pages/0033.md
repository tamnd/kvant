Perhaps slate (roofing slate) slats would be very good, as they can withstand quite a lot of heat and can be made flat and smooth.

The plates only need to be sprinkled with a layer of sand to establish reflection from the surface. The surface of the sand should appear completely robust. To successfully carry out the experiment, you need a completely flat desert; so don't feel like you've paid too much attention to adjusting the records. At one end of the desert we need to create an artificial sky. It is best to use a large mirror mounted on a window and reflecting the sky.

Between the sky and the desert you need to install a small chain of mountains cut out of cardboard. Individual peaks should have a height of 1 to 2 cm, and the valleys between them should rise only slightly above the desert level. The general arrangement is shown at figure 5.

The plates are then heated using gas burners, which need to be moved from time to time to ensure even heating.
