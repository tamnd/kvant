load: elastic force not only balances the Earth's gravity, but also gives the body acceleration $a$. If the elevator falls freely, then the weight will be zero - all bodies fall with the same acceleration, our movement will be exactly the same as the movement of the spring, and the spring will not be aware of it. Taking an apple, we will not feel its weight; it does not press on our hand, but falls freely along with our hand.

So, the weight $P=m(a+g)$, if $a$ is directed in the same direction as the acceleration of free fall $g$ and $|a|=|g|$ (free fall), a state of weightlessness occurs. This condition is experienced by astronauts when flying with their engines turned off, or when they are moving with the acceleration of gravity.

It is important that weight is due to both acceleration and gravitational attraction. It became possible to compensate one thing for another. It is possible to create weightlessness because the acceleration of gravity does not depend on either the mass or the substance of the falling bodies. This property was discovered by Galileo. But even now, researchers sometimes cannot resist the temptation to test this property. In 1964, Roll, Krotkov and Dicke established the equality of acceleration for gold and aluminum with an accuracy of 0.00000000001!

We will meet weightlessness in the cabin of a free-fall elevator. There are no overloads. Let us take a massive body created by the acceleration of gravity $a$, equal in magnitude to the desired acceleration of travel. Let's install an engine on it that can provide just this acceleration. The traveler is accommodated with all comforts in a separate cabin, freely falling onto this body. The cabin falls with acceleration $a$, but the engine is running and the body leaves with the same acceleration.

At zero initial speed, the distance between the cabin and the massive body will remain unchanged. In a freely falling cabin there is weightlessness, but at the same time it rushes with the necessary acceleration.

But before the halfway point, you need to start slowing down. The coordination of the acceleration of the body and the free fall acceleration of the cabin will be disrupted, and the cabin will crash into a massive body. In addition, there is a danger of standing up or falling on the body with any change in the acceleration of the ship (massive body). This means that we need to better think through the design of the ship.

Let's drill a shaft in the direction of acceleration through the center of a massive body, which has been shaped into a ball. The acceleration of gravity will vary depending on the distance to the center of the ball. Let us denote the acceleration on the surface of the ball by $a_0$ and the radius of the ball by $r_0$.
