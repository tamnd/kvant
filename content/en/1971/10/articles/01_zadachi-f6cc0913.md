1. The number given is 123456789101112...9899100. Cross out 150 digits from it so that the remaining number is the largest.

2. A mirror hangs vertically on the wall. Its lower edge is at a height of 1 meter from the floor. How far away from the wall do you need to be to see your shoes in the mirror? We assume that a person’s eyes are at a height of 1.5 meters from the floor.

3. There are 77 telephones. Is it possible to connect them in pairs so that each is connected exactly to 13?

4. Prove that the product of four consecutive integers increased by 1 is a perfect square.

A. P. Savin
