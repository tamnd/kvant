Brief information about the scientists mentioned in N. E. Zhukovsky’s lecture.

Euler is the greatest mathematician of the 18th century, a St. Petersburg academician, one of the creators of analytical mechanics. Laplace called Euler the father of modern mathematical analysis.

Poinsot is a French scientist, mathematician and mechanic. In 1834 he obtained an elegant geometric solution to some problems in the dynamics of rotation.

Helmholtz was an outstanding German physicist and mathematician of the 19th century. He became famous, in particular, for his work on acoustics (Helmholtz resonators) and thermodynamics. He is also the founder of the vortex theory.

Cauchy is a French scientist of the late 18th - first half of the 19th century, who was rightfully considered one of the creators of modern mathematics. He left over 800 memoirs on various branches of mathematics.

Shvedov V.F. is a professor at Odessa University, a well-known specialist in the hydrodynamics of viscous fluids (second half of the 19th century).

Tag is a professor of physics in Edinburgh (England, second half of the 19th century).

Reynolds is a professor at Owen College in Manchester (England, second half of the 19th century). He became famous for working on hydrodynamics.

PROBLEMS ABOUT THE WHEEL

1. The wheel rolls down slightly on an inclined plane. Since the movement of the wheel is accompanied by energy losses at the point of contact with the plane, even in the absence of air resistance, the wheel, starting from a certain moment, will roll evenly. It follows that the force R with which the plane acts on the wheel must be directed upward and equal to the gravitational force P. But then the force R will be created by a turning moment relative to the center O, and therefore the wheel will not rotate uniformly, but rather accelerated.
