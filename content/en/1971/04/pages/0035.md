M76. In a company of $n$ people, every two strangers had exactly two mutual acquaintances, and every two acquaintances had no more common acquaintances. Prove that everyone in this company knows the same number of people.

M77. The lengths of the two sides of the triangle are 10 and 15. Prove that the bicsectiras of the angle between them is not greater than 12.

N. B. Vasiliev

M78. Prove that every non-negative integer can be represented as $\frac{(x+y)^2+3x+y}{2}$, where $x$ and $y$ are non-negative integers, and what is the singular representation.

M79. Two points $P$ and $Q$ move along two intersecting straight lines with the same constant speed $v$. Prove that there is a fixed point $A$ on the plane, the distances from which to the points $P$ and $Q$ at any time are equal.

I. F. Sharyein

M80. Table $m \times n$ contains arbitrary numbers. It is allowed to simultaneously change the sign of all numbers in one column or all numbers in one row. Prove that by repeating this operation several times, you can obtain a table in which the sum of the numbers in any column and the sum of the numbers in any row are non-negative.

A. S. Schwartz

F88. A vessel, partially filled with a structure, moves with horizontal acceleration, resulting in the surface of the path being inclined to the horizon at a certain constant angle.

The top of the vessel is closed with a lid.

How will this angle change if the vessel is filled to the top with water?

F89. The electric field voltage in an electromagnetic wave of frequency $\omega=2 \cdot 10^{16} \text{ сек}^{-1}$, amplitude modulated with frequency $\Omega = 2 \cdot 10^{15} \text{ сек}^{-1}$, changes with time according to the law $E=a (1+\cos \Omega t) \cos \omega t$, where $a$ is a constant. Determine the energy of the electrons knocked out by this wave from the atoms of gaseous hydrogen with an induction energy of $W=13,5 \text{ эв}$.

A. D. Gladun, L. G. Aslamaozov

F90. An ideal gas of mass $m$, located at a temperature $T$, is cooled isochorically so that the pressure drops $n$ times. The gas then expands at constant pressure. In the final state its temperature is equal to the initial one. Determine the work done by the gas. The molecular weight of the gas is $\mu$.
