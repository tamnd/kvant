In the last section of the Optics program, as a rule, problems of constructing an image in lenses were not solved in cases where it was necessary to use a clear focal plane and secondary focus. Let us give examples of such problems.

Task 9 (varnant 78). Find by construction the path of the beam $AB$ after the collecting line (Fig. 11).

Let us draw a secondary optical axis parallel to $AB$ and intersecting the focal plane. The point of intersection of the secondary axis with the focal plane ($F'$) is the secondary focus at which all rays parallel to this axis are collected. Consequently, the ray $AB$ after the refraction of the line must pass through the point $F'$ (Fig. 12).
