To the article “Conversion of electrical circuits”

1. Let's transform the “star” $r_1 = r_2 = r_3$ into a triangle (see Fig.). It is not difficult to find points passing through resistances $R_{12}$, $R_{13}$ and $R_{23}: I_{12} = \frac{1\theta + 2\theta}{R_{12}} = 1a; I_{13} = \frac{1}{3}a; I_{23} = \frac{2}{3}a$. This means that battery $E_1$ carries current $I_1 = \frac{4}{3}a$, and battery $E_2$ carries current $I_2 = \frac{5}{3}a$. This means that current $I = \frac{1}{3}a$ flows through resistance $r_3$ in the original circuit.

2. When the switch is open, the total capacitance of the capacitor system is $C_1 = \frac{C}{2} + \frac{C \cdot 2C}{3C} = \frac{7}{6}C$. When the key is closed, it is equal to $C_2 = \frac{13}{11}C$. Pozotomu

$$\Delta Q = E(C_2 - C_1) = EC \left( \frac{13}{11} - \frac{7}{6} \right) = \frac{1}{66}EC.$$
