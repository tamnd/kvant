Thus, in order to find the vertex through which a reference line of a given direction passes, you need to compare the slope angle of the lines in a given direction with the slope angles of the lines on which the sides of the set of admissible points lie, and select two adjacent sides between whose slope angles the angle β lies. The intersection point of these sides will be the desired vertex. If the angle β coincides with the angle of inclination of one of the sides, then the desired reference line coincides with the line on which this side lies. Instead of comparing slope angles, you can compare their tangents, which is much more convenient, since the tangent of the slope angle is simply expressed through the coefficients of the equation of the straight line.

Numerical examples

1. Among the solutions to the system of linear inequalities $2x+y \geq 10$, $x \geq 0, y \geq 0$, $x+y \geq 8$, $3x+7y \geq 42$, $x+5y \geq 20$, find those for which the linear function $4x+3y$ reaches a minimum*).

Table 1

| Equation of a line | A point on the coordinate axis through which a straight line passes | Tangent of the angle of inclination of a straight line |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $y=0$ | $(0,4), (20,0)$ | $\frac{1}{5}$ |
| $x+5y=20$ | $(0,6), (14,0)$ | $\frac{3}{7}$ |
| $3x+7y=42$ | $(0,8), (8,0)$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ |
| $x+y=8$ | $(0,10), (5,0)$ | $\infty$ |
| $2x+y=10$ | $(0, \frac{3}{c}), (\frac{c}{4}, 0)$ | $\frac{4}{3}$ |

it is clear that the sides of this set are the rays of the coordinate axes and the segments of all four straight lines: $x+5y=20$, $3x+7y=42$, $x+y=8$, $2x+y=10$.

A supporting straight line with a slope angle $\frac{4}{3}$ must pass through a vertex lying at the intersection of a pair of neighboring sides, one of which has a slope angle less than $\frac{4}{3}$, and the other greater than $\frac{4}{3}$. Such a pair is formed by sides lying on straight lines

$x+y=8$ and $2x+y=10$,

with slope angles of 1 and 2, respectively. Thus, the coordinates of the desired vertex are determined from the system

$$\begin{cases} x+y=8, \\ 2x+y=10, \end{cases}$$

whence $x=2$, $y=6$. The value of the function $4x+3y$ at this point is $4 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 6 = 26$, and the reference line is determined by the equation $4x+3y=26$.
