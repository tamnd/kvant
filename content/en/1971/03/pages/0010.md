The set of admissible points is depicted in figure 11, from which it is clear that the straight line $x + 2y = 12$ lies entirely outside this set. Thus, the sides of this set are the rays of the coordinate axes and the segments.

The solutions to the problem are all points on the side lying on this line. The ends of this side are the vertices lying at the points of intersection of the line $2x + 8y = 32$ with the neighboring lines $y = 0$ and $3x + 4y = 36$ (recall that we excluded the line $x + 2y = 12$ from consideration).

So, the coordinates $(x_1, y_1)$ of the first vertex $M_1$ are determined from the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2x_1 + 8y_1 = 32, \\ y_1 = 0, \end{cases}$$
