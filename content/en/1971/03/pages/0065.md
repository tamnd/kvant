To the article “Kinematika and connections” («Kvant» No. 2) *

1. At point A, the projections of the velocities of the cylinder $v_{A\Upsilon}$ and rod $v_{AC}$ onto the direction $AO'$ ($AO' \perp OA$) coincide. The meaning is that the instantaneous axis of rotation of the cylinder passes through point $B$. Therefore, $v_{A\Upsilon}$ is directed perpendicular to $AB$ and is equal to $\Omega \cdot AB$, where $\Omega$ is the angular velocity of rotation of the cylinder. Since

$$\Omega = \frac{v}{r} \text{ и } AB = 2r \cos \frac{\alpha}{2}, \text{ получим}$$

$$v_{A\Upsilon} = \frac{v}{r} \cdot 2r \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = 2v \cos \frac{\alpha}{2}$$.

From the condition of equality of projections we have

$$v_{AC} = v_{A\Upsilon} \cos \left(\pi - \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)$$

or

$$\omega r \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = 2v \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} \sin \frac{\alpha}{2},$$

where

$$\frac{\omega r}{2 \sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\omega r}{1 - \cos \alpha}$$.

2. Since the speeds of the ends of the shatur are in the direction $AB$, we obtain ($v_A = \omega r$):

$$v \cos \alpha = \omega r \cos (\pi - \alpha - \beta),$$

or

$$v = \omega r \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cos \alpha$$.

Using the theorem of sines

$$\sin \alpha = \sin \beta / l$$,

can be found

$$v = \omega r \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{l}{r} \sin \alpha\right)^2} + \frac{l}{r} \sin \alpha = \omega l \cdot tg \alpha \times \left(\sqrt{\left(\frac{r}{l}\right)^2 - \sin^2 \alpha} + \cos \alpha\right)$$.
