3. Problem about chicken feed. The poultry farmer is engaged in open-casting of chickens. Let us assume for simplicity that two products can serve as food - wheat and peas. In order for a chicken to grow normally, its daily diet must contain at least a certain amount of calories, as well as proteins, fats and carbohydrates. These quantities are established by esophageal specialists, and we should consider them as given. Let it be known how much fat, proteins, fats and carbohydrates are contained in one gram of wheat and one gram of peas, and the price of each type of food is also known. How to create the cheapest but most complete diet?

In each of the above, it is necessary to make the optimal choice, although in each problem it is understood differently.

In order to see what mathematical questions arose when solving such problems, let us analyze in detail the problem of chicken feed.

To analyze this problem, you will receive all the information that is included in the school course, and all calculations can be easily checked using pencil and paper.

Let one gram of wheat contain $a_1$ calories, one gram of peas contain $b_1$ calories, and the minimum required number of calories in the daily diet is $c_1$. We will depict menus containing $x$ grams of wheat and $y$ grams of peas by point $(x, y)$ on the plane. Let's try to highlight those points on the plane that correspond to diets containing at least $c_1$ calories. Since a menu consisting of $x$ grams of wheat and $y$ grams of peas contains $a_1x+b_1y$ calories, the inequality $a_1x+b_1y \geq c_1$ holds for such points.

From your school algebra course you know that the points for which $a_1x+b_1y=c_1$ lie on the line $l_1$ passing through the points $(0, \frac{c_1}{b_1})$ and $(\frac{c_1}{a_1}, 0)$. This straight line divides the plane into two parts. For the point $(x, y)$, lying on one side of the line $l_1$, the inequality $a_1x+b_1y > c_1$ is satisfied, and for the point lying on the other side, the inequality $a_1x+b_1y < c_1$ is satisfied. Each of these parts, together with the straight line $l_1$, is called a half-plane. Thus, one half-plane consists of all points $(x, y)$ for which $a_1x+b_1y \geq c_1$,

and the other from the point for which $a_1x+b_1y \leq c_1$.
