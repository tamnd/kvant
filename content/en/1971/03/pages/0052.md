Option 2

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_2(1 + \cos 4x) \leq 1 + \log_{\sqrt{2}} \sin x.$$

2. Find all valid solutions of the system
$$\begin{cases} |xy - 2| = 6 - x^2, \\ 2 + 3y^2 = 2xy. \end{cases}$$

3. Three racers ($A$, then $B$ and then $C$) start with an interval of 1 minute from one point on the track highway and move in the same direction at constant speeds. Each racer spends more than two minutes on a lap. Having made three laps, the racer $A$ will finish $B$ for the first time at the starting point, and after another 3 minutes he will overtake $C$ for the second time. Racer $B$ agreed for the first time $C$ also at the starting point, having completed 4 laps. How many minutes does a racer $A$ spend on a lap?

4. A convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ contains two circles of the same radius $r$, externally touching each other. The center of the first circle is on the segment connecting vertex $A$ with the midpoint $F$ of side $CD$, and the center of the second circle is on the segment connecting vertex $C$ with the midpoint $E$ of side $AB$. The first circle touches sides $AB$, $AD$ and $CD$; the second circle touches sides $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$. Find $AC$.

5. The length of each edge of the triangular pyramid $SABC$ is equal to 1. $BD$ is the height of triangle $ABC$. The equilateral triangle $BDE$ lies in the plane forming an angle φ with the edge $AC$, and the points $S$ and $E$ lie on the same side of the plane $ABC$. Find the distance between points $S$ and $E$.
