WRITTEN EXAM
IN MATHEMATICS
AT MSU MEKHMAT IN 1970

N. N. Kolesnikov

In «Kvant» No. 2 for 1970, an article by N. S. Bakhvalov and N. N. Kuznetsov about the written exam in mathematics at the mexmate of Moscow State University in 1969 was published. Without repeating the comments made there about the nature of this exam, I would like to emphasize some of its features in 1970.

The tasks proposed to applicants once again confirm the position that their successful solution was achieved by any applicant, according to the conditions of the school program, and not just by a graduate of mathematics schools. You will see that these problems do not contain “pitfalls” or “traps”, but require a clear understanding of the main sections of the mathematics program for applicants to universities and certain skills in technical calculations and solving simple equations, inequalities and geometric problems.

Naturally, such “standardization” of problems implies increased requirements for the evaluation of their solutions, requirements for the ability to bring a solution to the end, and not limit oneself to hints at a solution. A satisfactory mark (tri) was given in 1970 for completing the correct solution of any two or three problems, good (four) for any four problems, excellent (five) for five problems. 4 astronomical hours were allotted to solve the problem (a set of five problems - variants).

Let's move on to analyzing one of the options for the written exam in mathematics, briefly focusing on the most typical errors encountered in the works of those admitted to the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

Option 1

1. Solve inequality
$$-\log_2(1 + \cos 3x) \leq 2 + \log_2\left(\frac{1}{4} - \cos x\right).$$

2. Find all valid solutions of the system
$$\begin{cases} y^2 - |xy| + 2 = 0, \\ 8 - x^2 = (x + 2y)^2. \end{cases}$$

3. Three racers A, B and C, starting at the same time, move at constant speeds in one direction along a ring road. At the start, racer B is in front of racer A at a distance of $1/3$ the length of the highway, and racer C is in front of racer B at the same distance. Racer A first caught up with B at the moment when B finished his first lap, and 10 minutes later A caught up with racer C for the first time. Racer B spends 2.5 minutes less per lap than C. How much time does racer A spend on a lap?

4. On the diagonal AC of the convex plane ABCD there is the center of a circle of radius r tangent to sides AB, AD and BC. On the diagonal BD is the center of a circle of the same radius r, touching sides BC, CD and AD. Find the area of ​​four (circle ABCD), knowing that the indicated circles touch each other externally.
