QUARTZ HAIRY

There is a stone known in nature that is called “hairy quartz” in Russia. This is a rich quartz crystal produced with shiny gold threads (see figure on the cover).

Pliny called the threads "Hairs of Venus", and in the east they are called "Hairs of Mohammed" and the stone was considered a binding. Currently, these “Hairs”, or whiskers as they are now called, are grown in laboratory conditions. Studies using an electron microscope showed that the “Hair of Venus” is nothing more than very thin and long crystals of titanium oxide $(\text{TiO}_2 - \text{рубин})$, which are tubes of rectangular cross-section with a wall thickness of 1–2 µm (see photograph).

Whiskers have a number of very interesting properties, one of which—high strength—makes it possible to create new, so-called composite materials based on them.

M. A. Golosova
