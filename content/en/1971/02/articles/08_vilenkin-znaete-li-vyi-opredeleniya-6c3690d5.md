⟦folio 43⟧

DO YOU KNOW THE DEFINITIONS?

1. Which of the following formulas are correct and which are false?
   a) $1 > 1$; 6) $1 \geq 1$;
   b) $1 \leq 1$; 7) $2 > 1$;
   d) $2 \geq 1$; e) $-2 \leq -1$.

2. The teacher asked the student a question: “In what cases, when drawing a redistribution curve to a line passing through a given point, do we say that we lower the redistribution curve to the straight line, and when do we say that we restore the redistribution curve to the straight line?”

The student answered: “If the point is above the straight line or on the straight line and we move the limit down, then we lower the limit, and if the point is below the line or we move up, then we restore it.”

The follower immediately asks: “What if the point is on the side of the line or the line is vertical?”

Help the student, answer the question: in these drawings do we restore the overshoot or omit it?
