Reader
This is very difficult to understand. I have never heard of anything that someone held in their hands or saw a body with a negative temperature.

Author
This is not surprising. In a stable, equilibrium state, negative temperature is impossible. A state with a negative temperature is, as they say, a metastable state. Once it has arisen, it usually does not exist for long (although there are also very long-lasting metastable states, for example glass, which in the equilibrium state is an opaque crystal). For example, when a negative temperature is created in the working substance of a laser, it very quickly transforms into a state with a positive temperature: particles from high energy levels move, as if “falling” to lower levels, and the energy lost in this case is released in the form of a laser beam.

Reader

On the other hand, temperature is related to the average clinical energy of the particles that make up the body. Finally, in quantum mechanics it acts as a quantity that determines the distribution of particles by energy state.
