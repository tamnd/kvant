Tasks proposed on the first day

1. Given a triangle ABC, M is the interior point of side AB. Let $r_1, r_2, r$ be the radii of circles inscribed in triangles AMC, BMC, ABC, respectively; $\rho_1, \rho_2, \rho$ are the radii of circles that lie inside the angle ACB, and is external for triangles AMC, BMC, ABC, respectively.

Prove that $\frac{r_1 r_2}{\rho_1 \rho_2} = \frac{r}{\rho}$ (Poland, 5 points).

2. Let $a, b$ and $n$ be natural numbers greater than ones. The numbers $a$ and $b$ are the main two number systems. The numbers $A_n, B_n$ have the same representation $\frac{x_n x_{n-1}}{x_1 x_0}$ in number systems with the basic $a$ and $b$ being $x_n \neq 0, x_{n-1} \neq 0$. The numbers obtained after crossing out the first digit $x_n$ will be called $A_{n-1}, B_{n-1}$.

Prove that $a > b$ if and only if $\frac{A_{n-1}}{A_n} < \frac{B_{n-1}}{B_n}$ (Romania, 7 points).

3. The sequence of real numbers $a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n, \dots$ satisfies the condition

$$1 = a_0 \leqslant a_1 \leqslant \dots \leqslant a_n \leqslant \dots$$

(1)

The sequence $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_n, \dots$ is defined as follows:

$$b_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \left(1 - \frac{a_k-1}{a_k}\right) \frac{1}{\sqrt{a_k}}.$$

a) Prove that $0 \leqslant b_n < 2$ for all $n$.

b) Prove that for a given $c$ such that $0 \leqslant c < 2$, there exists a sequence $a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n, \dots$ satisfying (1) also that $b_n > c$ for an infinite set of indices $n$ (Sweden, 8 points).

Problems proposed on the second day

4. Find all positive numbers $n$ that have the following properties: divide the set into two sets so that the product of all elements of one of them is equal to the product of all elements of the other (Czechoslovaks, 6 points).

5. In the terrahedron ABCD DB ⊥ DC and the main perpendicular dropped from D onto the plane of triangle ABC coincides with the orthocenter of this triangle.

Prove that $(AB + BC + CA)^2 \leqslant 6(AD^2 + BD^2 + CD^2)$. For which terrahedra is there a tie (Bulgaria 6 points)?

6. There are 100 points given on a plane, no three of which lie on the same line. All possible triangles with vertices at these points are considered.

Prove that among them there will be no more than 70% acute triangles (Soviet Union, 8 points).
