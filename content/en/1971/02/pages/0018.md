Author

We have so far determined only the minimum energy of an atom or molecule. However, this does not yet determine the kinetic energy of the substance. Something to keep in mind. According to the laws of quantum mechanics, the energy of a particle cannot be any. It can only take on completely defined values, or, as we say, particles can be at certain energy levels. They are usually depicted in the form of horizontal lines located one above the other (Fig. 1). The energy level system is something like a bookcase with shelves. Books can be on shelves, but not between them.
