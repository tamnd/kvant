Soviet team. From left to right: I. Bulzhenkov, I. Lyuksyutov, S. Gorbachevsky, V. Kravtsov, V. Petrov, M. Voloshin. Photo by M. V. Alpert (APN).

Russian Olympics in two rounds. It was the same this time. On the first type, the children were asked to solve four problems, on the second - only one, but experimentally. The international commission set the highest score for solving each problem of the first type - 10 points, the maximum number of points for experimental work equal to 20. Theoretical and experimental rounds were held on different days.

But that's all behind us. On July 13, the results were summed up. Before talking about them, we note that there was no official transfer to the Olympics. Speaking about the goals of the Olympiad, introduce its organizing committee, the vice-president of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR V. G. Zubov said that the Olympiad is, first of all, a friendly state of good friends, and its goal is to compare the knowledge of schoolchildren, compare it with the knowledge of peers from other countries. And the head of the Yugoskov delegation, Dr. Bozhidar Milich, speaking at the closing of the Olympics on behalf of the International Commission, at the suggestion of Soviet scientists, sought to preserve the tradition of the Olympic Games - “not the countries, but the participants.”

To receive the first prize at the Olympiad, it was necessary to score at least 50 points (out of 60 possible), the second - from 45 to 49 points, the third - from 39 to 44. Those who received more than half of all points were awarded with praise.

Let us present the results of the Soviet team: the first prizes were received by M. Voloshin, S. Gorbachevsky and B. Petrov, the second prizes by I. Lyuksyutov and I. Bulzhenkov, the third by V. Kravtsov. M. Voloshin scored the maximum number of points (57). And here is how the prizes and half of those recalled were distributed among the delegation:

Let's name here a few more successful Olympiad participants. Marek Zyukkowski from Poland received the first prize together with our guys. Ingree Reimann, a girl from the GDR, received a special prize for successful participation in the Olympics, and her teammate Manfred Fischer received the second prize and, in addition, a special prize for the best performance of experimental work. He received the maximum number of points for the experiment.
