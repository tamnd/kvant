§ 4. Proof of the main theorem

We will prove a stronger proposition than the main theorem.

Theorem 3. For any natural numbers N, k there are at least k distinct fractions $\frac{p}{q}$ maxims that

a) $q \leqslant Nk$,

b) $q \left| \alpha - \frac{p}{q} \right| < \frac{1}{N}$.

(The fact that Theorem 1 follows from Theorem 3 is obvious: k can be arbitrarily large, so there is an infinite number of approximations with a quality coefficient less than 1/N).
