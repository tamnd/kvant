⟦folio 25⟧

Solutions to the problems of the introductory test in the ZMS

A. L. Toom

In the first issue «Kvant» the problems of the introductory test for the Correspondence Mathematical Scale were published. ZMSH received about 6,000 letters from eighth-graders. About 2,500 of those who sent decisions were accepted into ZMSH and its branches.

In this issue we publish solutions to the most difficult problems.

Problem conditions

5. Four statements have been made about triangle ABC:
1) triangle ABC is right-angled;
2) ∠A = 30°; 3) AB = 2BC; 4) AC = 2BC.
We know that two of these statements are true and the other two are false. Find the perimeter of triangle ABC if BC = 1.

6. It is known that a+b+c<0 and that the equation ax²+bx+c=0 has no real roots. Determine what sign the number c has.

7. The following is known about points A, B, C: for any point M on the plane, the segment AM is less than at least one of the segments BM and CM. Prove that point A lies on segment BC.

9. Is it possible to wrap a cube with edge 1 in a square piece of paper with edge 3?

10. Is it possible to write down nine numbers 1,2,3,..., 9 in a circle in such an order that the sum of no two adjacent numbers is divisible by 3, 5, or 7?

11. Residents of the city of Pravdin always tell the truth, and residents of the city of Liars always tell lies.

In one of these cities, a football match took place between the teams of these cities. After the match, near the stadium where it was played, the following happened:

A (to B and C): “I wasn’t at the match. Tell me, who won?
B: “G told me that their team lost.”
B: “Our team won.”
Γ (getting into a bus going to another city, and addressing A): “Let’s go together, A, because we are from the same city.”
A: “You are mistaken, G. I live here. This is B from your city."
Determine who is from each city, where the match takes place and which team won.

12. On the highway in one direction at a constant speed at regular intervals, buses travel without stopping. One person walked 4 km along the highway, and during this time he was overtaken by 6 cars. Another time he walked 7 km, and during this time he was overtaken by 8 autovosu. The third time he walked 17 km. How many cars did he overtake? (All three times the person walked at the same speed.)
