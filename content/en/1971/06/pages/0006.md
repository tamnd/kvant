§ 3. Relationship between lattices and rational approximations

Let us now turn to our original goal: finding approximations for the irrational number $\alpha$. First, we associate some special lattice with the number $\alpha$.

Let us introduce a rectangular coordinate system on the plane. As straight lines $m_0, m_{\pm 1}, m_{\pm 2}, \dots$ we take straight lines parallel to the axis $Ox$ and passing through points $(0,0), (0,\pm 1), (0,\pm 2), \dots$. (equation of straight line $m_q: y=q$). For the line $l_0$ we take the line passing through the points $(0,0)$ and $(\alpha, 1)$; its equation is $x=\alpha y$. Finally, as lines $l_{\pm 1}, l_{\pm 2}, \dots$ we take lines parallel to line $l_0$ and passing through points $(\pm 1,0), (\pm 2,0), \dots$. (line equation $l_p: x=\alpha y-p$).
