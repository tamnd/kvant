⟦folio 47⟧

This inequality is equivalent to the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - 6 > 0, \\ 0 < \log_3(x^2 - 6) < 1 \end{cases}$$

or
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - 6 > 0, \\ 1 < x^2 - 6 < 3. \end{cases}$$

The second inequality includes the first, from which we find $7 < x^2 < 9$, $\sqrt{7} < |x| < 3$.

Answer:
$$\sqrt{7} < x < 3$$ or $-3 < x < -\sqrt{7}$.

In this problem, many applicants did not take into account that the base of the external logarithm is less than 1, and some forgot about the modulus sign and lost the second part of the answer.
