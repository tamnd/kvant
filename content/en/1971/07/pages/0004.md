Why does the Earth attract the Moon thousands of times weaker than an apple? Robert Hooke, using the spring balance he invented, tried to change the change in the force of gravity. But at the top of the mountain the load stretched the spring in the same way as on the seashore. Yet Newton suggested that gravity is weakened by stretching. The height of the mountains is small compared to the size of the Earth, and even more so compared to the stretch from the Earth to the Moon.

After studying the laws of planetary motion established by Johannes Kepler *), Newoton managed to find a simple rule for the decrease in gravitational force with stretching. This rule states: if the centers of two balls, the mass of which is uniformly distributed over their volumes, are located at a distance $r$, then the force of attraction $F$ between the balls is directed along the line connecting the centers of the balls, is proportional to the product of their masses $m_1, m_2$ and inversely proportional to the square of the distance $r$:

$$F = \gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2};$$

γ in this formula is a constant value; it can only be determined experimentally.

Let's return again to the attraction of the Moon. The Moon is located at a distance of 60 Earth radii, and the apple is only one Earth radius from the center of the Earth. Therefore, the force, and therefore the acceleration, in lunar orbit is $60^2$ times less than at the Earth’s surface:

$$a = \gamma \frac{M_3}{(60R)^2},$$

$$g = \gamma \frac{M_3}{R^2}.$$

Let's divide the first of these expressions by the second:

$$\frac{a}{g} = \frac{1}{3600},$$ or $a = 0,0027 \, m/cck^*)$.

The obtained value surprisingly exactly coincides with the previously calculated value of the centripetal acceleration of the Moon.

This is how Newton managed to test the law of universal gravitation. True, there was not such good agreement in his calculations: work in Wulsthorpe, Newton did not have the exact value of the earth's radius. Only a few years later this value was changed with sufficient accuracy.

Despite the obtained coincidence of the magnitude of the accelerations, Newoton was not satisfied with the verification. It was still necessary to solve the problem of the attraction of an apple lying on the Earth. Is it possible to consider that the globe attracts the apple as if all the mass of the Earth were concentrated in the center?

*) The gravitational constant γ cancels out - you don’t need to know it at all to be convinced of the correctness of the law of gravitation discovered by Newoton.
