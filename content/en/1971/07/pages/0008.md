The attraction of the Sun is many times stronger than the lunar one, but the heterogeneity of the solar attraction is not great. Therefore, the solar tidal force $F_C = 2\gamma \frac{M_C}{r^3_{C3}} R$ is less than the lunar one. The mass of the Sun is 27 million times greater than the mass of the Moon, this Sun-Earth distance $r_{C3}$ is 389 times greater than $r$ - the Earth-Moon distance. So $F_C = 2\gamma \frac{27.10^6 M_\pi}{(389 r)^3} R \approx 0.45 F_\pi$, that is, “solar” tides are weaker than lunar tides. Of course, we do not distinguish between these tides. Twice a month the Moon and the Sun are in line with the Earth, the solar and lunar tidal forces add up - a large tide is observed. If the Earth, Sun and Moon are located so that the Earth-Sun direction leaves an angle of 90° with the Earth-Moon direction, then the tidal forces of the Sun and Moon weaken each other and a small tide is observed. On islands lost in the ocean, where tidal phenomena are not distorted by the influence of the shores, large tides rise by 1.30 m, and small tides by only 0.65 m. If you make calculations, you can estimate the ratio of the solar and lunar masses by the magnitude of the large and small tides.

Thus, the untrained “satellite” of the Moon, the ocean tide, helped Newoton calculate the mass of the Moon. This calculation was very inaccurate. The task was complicated by the friction of water masses against the ocean and other circumstances that were difficult to take into account. The exact mass value was found after the launch of the lunar satellites. The measured periods of revolution and characteristics of the trajectory of the Lunars make it possible to determine with great accuracy the mass calculated by Newoton only approximately. Based on the movement of the artificial satellites of the Moon, scientists have determined that the lunar mass is distributed unevenly throughout its volume - Kepler's laws are satisfied for the approaching Moon satellites.

Before Newton's actronomers believed that comets passed through the solar system only once. However, Newton showed that comets can move in closed elliptical orbits. Especially their orbits are very elongated. Therefore, comets travel great distances from the Sun and their convergence period is very long. Edmond Halley managed to calculate the time of return of one of the most significant comets, records of the appearance of which have been preserved since ancient times. Calculations confirmed that the comet is returning within the timeframe predicted by Halley. It can be observed for 76 years. In 1986, this comet will again be visible from Earth. Only one actor, Johann Halle, who lived for almost a hundred years, observed Halley's comet twice. The return of comets is another confirmation of the law of universal gravitation.

Solutions to many remarkable problems in which the law of universal gravitation is applied were contained in the "Mathematical Principles": this includes the calculation of the precession of the earth's axis, and the proof that any stone thrown on the earth moves in an ellipse, one of the foci of which is the center of the earth, and, finally, a study of the influence of the mutual gravitation of the planets on their motion. The influence of the planets is small compared to solar gravity, but a planet like Jupiter changes the nature of the orbit of neighboring Saturn, or, as actronomers say, disturbs its movement.

For the third century now, Newton's laws have been used in calculations of machine tools and artillery pieces, trajectories of Earth satellites and packages. Mathematical formulas written more than two centuries ago are contained in calculating machines of the latest design, irrefutably confirming the properties of these laws.
