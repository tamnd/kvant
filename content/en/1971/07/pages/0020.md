⟦folio 18⟧

Fig. 1.

In cell 0 we will “put” everyone who has not made a single mistake, in cell 1 - those who have one mistake, in cell 2 - two, ... and so on until cell 13, where only Sasha Ivanov ended up.

We apply the Dirichlet principle (note, this is a very important point).

Let us prove the statement of the problem by contradiction. Let's assume that no three students made the same number of mistakes, that is, less than three students got into each of the cells 0, 1, ..., 12. Then in each of them there are two people or less, and in total there are no more than 26 people in these 13 cells. Having added Sasha Ivanov, we still won’t get 30 guys. Contradiction.

Can we say that exactly three people made an equal number of mistakes? Of course not. Perhaps all the guys, except Sasha, wrote the dictation without a single mistake, that is, everyone made 0 mistakes. Can we assume that at least four are in the same “cage”? No, you can't. A class in which 3 people each made 0, 1, 2 errors, 2 people each made 3, 4, ..., 12 errors, and one person made 13 errors satisfies the conditions of the problem.

2. Let there be 41 people in the class, not 30. Prove that there are four who made the same number of mistakes. (The remaining conditions are as in task 1.)

3. There are about 7.1 million residents of Moscow, each with no more than 100,000 hairs on their heads. Prove that in Moscow there are at least 70 people with the same number of hairs on their heads.

Acquaintance

Let us assume that acquaintance is a “symmetrical” relationship between people: if Komarov is familiar with Zhukov, then Zhukov is familiar with Komarov.

4. Let's choose 5 people in any way. Prove that at least of them have the same number of acquaintances among those selected.
