How to arrange in one step?

There are three empty glasses and three with milk in a row on the table. They need to be arranged so that empty glasses alternate with filled ones. To do this, take only one glass. How to do this?

How much money does Kolya have?

If Kolya buys one candy, then he will have one kopeck left, and if he wants to buy 2 candies, then he will not have enough one kopeck. How much money does Kolya have?
