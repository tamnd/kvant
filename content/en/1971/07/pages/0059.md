ABOUT MATHEMATICAL PREPARATION OF APPLICANTS

On December 22, 1970, at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys, the rector's office, together with the department of higher mathematics, held a meeting of mathematics teachers from secondary schools in Moscow to discuss the mathematical training of technical school applicants.

The joint report “On the required level of mathematical training of applicants” was made by the head. Department of Higher Mathematics, Professor I. E. Bazilevich and Professor M. A. Akivis. The report noted that when studying a higher mathematics course at a college, the following sections of the school mathematics course are especially often used: transformations of algebraic and trigonometric expressions, properties of the basic elements of functions and their graphics, solutions of various types of equations, equalities and systems of equations or inequalities, the concept of the absolute value of a real number and an arithmetic root, basic theorems and formulas of geometry, etc.

However, it is precisely these examples of sections that many applicants and first-year students have poor command of. The speakers emphasized that many applicants do not know how to correctly and competently format their work, which is associated with the inability to think clearly and consistently.

At the end of the report, some provisions were formulated, the implementation of which will help improve the level of mathematical training of applicants:

a) throughout the entire teaching of mathematics in secondary school, attention should be paid to the logical validity and coherence of the course; students must be able to think correctly, reason and correctly formulate their thoughts;

b) when solving various kinds of equations and inequalities, the concept of a region of acceptable values ​​should be used and students should be taught to monitor the change in this region during certain transformations;

b) special attention should be paid to the technical requirements of nalgebraic and trigonometric expressions; you should be required to fill out the basic formulas of algebra, trigonometrics and geometry;

d) solid values ​​of properties and graphs of the main elements of functions should be added; these properties should be used when solving equations and inequalities;

d) when solving geometric problems, students must be able to make a correct drawing and carry out a clear justification for all constructions and calculations;

e) the current school curriculum in mathematics does not contain some topics, knowledge of which is desirable when studying a mathematics course at a university. These include connection theory, Newton's biome, Bezout's theorem and solutions of two-valued equations. These topics should be included in elective mathematics classes. In addition, in these classes you should study complex numbers and inverse trigonometric functions more deeply than provided by the program.

The report of Associate Professor V.V. Goldberg highlighted the most common mistakes made by applicants during the entrance exams.
