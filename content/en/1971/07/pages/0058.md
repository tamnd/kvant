Faculty of Economics (evening department)
Option

1. Two people come out from different ends of the carriage of a moving train towards each other. The first one moves towards the head carriage of the train and has a relative speed of 0.5 m/sec. The second has a relative ground speed of 63 km/h. 5 seconds after the start of movement, the distance between them is reduced to one-fifth of the length of the carriage and is one fortieth of the distance covered by the train in 5 seconds. Find the speed of the train.

2. In the rectangle ABCD, points M and N are taken on sides AB=6 and BC=8 so that the segment MN is parallel to the segment AC. It is known that the perimeter of the polygon AMNCD is related to the perimeter of the triangle MBN as 7 : 3. Find the length of the segment MN.

3. Solve the equation sin x+sin 4x+sin 7x=0.
4. Solve the inequality 2^{2x-1}>1-2^{x-1}.

Problem solutions

1. Answer: train speed $\frac{170}{9}$ m/sec ≈ 18.9 m/sec.

2. According to the conditions of the problem MN || AC, so △MBN∼△ABC. In similar triangles, similar sides are proportional: $\frac{MB}{BN} = \frac{AB}{BC}$. But $\frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{3}{4}$, so it is convenient to take MB = 3x, then BN = $\frac{4}{3}$ MB = 4x. Expressing the perimeters of AMNCD and MBN in terms of x, we obtain the equation $\frac{28-2x}{12x} = \frac{7}{3}$, from which MN = 5x = $\frac{14}{3}$. Answer: MN = $\frac{14}{3}$.

3. Answer: $x = \frac{\pi n}{4} (n=0, \pm 1, \dots)$, $x = \frac{\pi}{3} \pm \frac{\pi}{9} + \frac{2\pi k}{3} (k=0, \pm 1, \dots)$.

4. Answer: $x > 0$.
