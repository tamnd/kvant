⟦folio 53⟧

Faculty of Economics (full-time)

Option

1. The volume of a block in the shape of a rectangular paralleliped is 150 cubic meters. cm, the total surface area is 280 cubic meters. cm, the perimeter of the base is 40 cm. Find the dimensions of the block.

2. In the square $ABCD$ of area $J$ the side $AD$ is taken as the point $D$ and on the continuation the point $O$ is taken at a distance of 3 from the point $D$. Two rays are drawn from point $O$. The first ray intersects the segment $CD$ at the point $M$ and the segment $AB$ at the point $N$, and the length of the segment $ON$ is equal to $a$. The second ray intersects the segment $CD$ at point $L$ and the segment $BC$ at point $K$, and at $\Rightarrow BKL = \alpha$. Find the area of ​​the polygon $BKLMN$.

3. Solve equation $\log_2\log_2 x = \log_4\log_4 2x$.

4. Solve equation $|z| \cdot i - z = 1 + 2i$.

Problem solutions

1. Let us denote the sides of the base of the bar by $x$ ($cm$) and $y$ ($cm$), and the height of the bar by $z$ ($cm$). Let for definition $x \geq y$. Then

$$\begin{cases} xyz = 150, \\ 2(xy + yz + xz) = 280, \\ 2(x + y) = 40, \\ x \geq y. \end{cases}$$

(*)

From the first and third equations $xy = \frac{150}{z}$ and $x + y = 20$. Substituting these expressions into the second equation, we obtain the equation for $z$:

$$\frac{15}{z} + 2z = 14,$$

whence $z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{19}}{2}$. Now we know the sum $x + y$ and the product $xy = \frac{150}{z}$ of the numbers $x$ and $y$, which means that $x$ and $y$ are the roots of the quadratic equation $t^2 - 20t + \frac{300}{7 \pm \sqrt{19}} = 0$. Solving it, we find

$$t_{1,2} = 10 \pm \sqrt{100 - \frac{300}{7 \pm \sqrt{19}}}.$$

For $z = \frac{7 - \sqrt{19}}{2}$ the discriminant of this equation is negative: 100 – $\frac{300}{7 - \sqrt{19}} < 100 - \frac{300}{7 - 4} = 0$, and for $z = \frac{7 + \sqrt{19}}{2}$ the discriminant is positive and less than 100. In this case, the values ​​$z$ $t_1 > 0$, $t_2 > 0$, that is, $x > 0$, $y > 0$. It is easy to see that the numbers $z = \frac{7 + \sqrt{19}}{2}$, $x = 10 + \sqrt{100 - \frac{300}{7 + \sqrt{19}}}$, $y = 10 - \sqrt{100 - \frac{300}{7 + \sqrt{19}}}$ are indeed a solution to system (*). Answer: the sides of the base are $10 \pm \sqrt{100 - \frac{300}{7 + \sqrt{19}}}$, the height is $\frac{7 + \sqrt{19}}{2}$.

Almost none of the applicants had any difficulty in composing the correct system of equations for this problem. However, the solution to the resulting system turned out to be beyond our power. Some applicants, having found two values ​​of $z$, proceeded to find the corresponding value $x$ and $y$, without caring that in one of the answers they provided, $x$ and $y$ are not real numbers.
