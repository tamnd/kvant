Entrance exams to MIT are conducted in full accordance with the mathematics program for applicants to universities. As in all technical schools, mathematics is a professional subject for admission to MIT.

At the faculties of energy-mechanical and technical engineering, applicants will take two exams in mathematics: written and oral. At the mechanical-technological, chemical-technological and engineering-economic faculties there is one exam in mathematics - an oral one. In all faculties, medalists will take an exam in mathematics.

Let's look at one of the options offered in the written exam.

Option

1. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg} x - 1 = \frac{\cos 2x}{\text{tg} x + 1}.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_5^4 (x - 1)^2 + \log_5^2 (x - 1)^3 = 25.$$

3. Simplify with $0 < a \leq 1$:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \left[ 4 (a + 1) + \left( \sqrt[3]{a \sqrt{a}} - 1 \right)^2 - \left( \frac{\sqrt[6]{ab^2} + \sqrt{a}}{\sqrt[3]{a} + \sqrt[3]{b}} \right)^3 \right]^{\frac{1}{2}}.$$

Analysis of varnat problems

1. We find the ODZ of the equation $x \neq \frac{\pi}{2} l, x \neq \frac{\pi}{4} (4m - 1) (l, m = 0, \pm 1, \dots)$. Now let's transform the left side: $(\text{ctg} x - 1) (\text{tg} x + 1) = \cos 2x,$
$$\frac{\cos x}{\sin x} - \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} = \cos 2x,$$
$$\cos 2x = \frac{1}{2} \cos 2x \sin 2x,$$
$$\cos 2x \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} \sin 2x \right) = 0.$$ (*)

Since the second factor in (*) is nonzero ($\sin 2x \neq 2$), then $\cos 2x = 0$ and
$$x = \frac{\pi}{4} (2k + 1) \quad (k = 0, \pm 1, \dots).$$

Taking into account the ODZ, we exclude the value $x$, which is obtained for odd $k$.

Answer:
$$x = \frac{\pi}{4} (4n + 1) \quad (n = 0, \pm 1, \dots).$$

Solving this example did not cause any particular difficulties, but a lot of extra roots came up, since the ODZ was not taken into account.

$$16 \log_5^4 (x - 1) + 9 \log_5^2 (x - 1) = 25.$$
