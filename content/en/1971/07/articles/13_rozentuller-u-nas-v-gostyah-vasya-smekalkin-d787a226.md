QUANTUM
FOR JUNIOR SCHOOL CHILDREN

OUR GUEST IS VASYA SMEKALKIN

For four years now, the Leningrad children's newspaper "Leninskie Iskuses" has been holding a mathematical competition for first, second and third grade students in schools in Leningrad and the Leningrad region. The problems of the Olympiad, their solutions and the names of the winners are published regularly in the newspaper on the page “For the Octobrist Guys”.
The Olympiad is headed by Vasya Smekalkin. Try to solve several problems of Vasya Smekalkin.

How many medals?

At the Olympic Games, our athletes won 96 medals, of which 65 were gold and bronze, and 61 were gold and silver.

How many gold, silver and bronze medals did they receive individually?

Try to split

Is it possible to divide three apples between two fathers and two sons so that each gets exactly one apple?

How many candies did Sasha have?

On his birthday, Sasha brought a bag of sweets to class.
- How many candies do you have? - the guys asked.
“I remember,” said Sasha, “when I laid them out in parameti, threes and fours, each time there was one extra left, and when I laid them out in fives, there was no extra.”
How many candies did Sasha bring?

60 steps to Nina. What about Tanya?

Nina lives on the fourth floor, and Tanya lives on the second. Nina climbs 60 steps. How many steps does Tanya climb?

Where are the balls?

Three boxes contain one ball each: white, black and green. On the first box there is an inscription: “white”, on the second - “black”, and on the third - “white or green”. But not a single inscription corresponds to reality.

How much money does Kolya have?

If Kolya buys one candy, then he will have one kopeck left, and if he wants to buy 2 candies, then he will not have enough one kopeck. How much money does Kolya have?
