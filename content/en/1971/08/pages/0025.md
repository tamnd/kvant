HOW TO CATCH A LION IN THE DESERT?

Observer in empty presence in a round cage. Lion is in a cage. An inversion is performed. Then the lion will be in the cage, and the presence will be outside it.

From graduate folklore
Moscow State University of the 20s

Geometry translated from Greek means land surveying. It owes its birth to ancient surveyors, architects, and builders. They do not have the sophisticated instruments that their descendants are armed with; they made do with the simplest means: a compass, a ruler and a measuring tape. Naturally, the ancient mathematicians used precisely these means for geometric constructions, rejecting only the measuring tape as an imprecise tool.

Sometimes you are amazed at how many different construction problems they could solve using only these simple tools.

It seemed that there were no construction problems that could not be solved with the help of a compass and a ruler.

But such problems were found. Many mathematicians puzzled over the problems of “trisection of an angle” and “doubling of a cube”. Only at the end of the last century was their unsolvability proven *). However, even now there are still people trying to trisection an angle using only a compass and a ruler.

Of the problems that can be solved with the help of a compass and a ruler, one of the most interesting is the problem of the outstanding ancient Greek geologist

*) Problem on trisection of an angle: “Indicate a method for dividing any angle into three equal parts”; cube doubling problem: “Find an edge of a cube whose volume is twice the volume of a given cube.” The proof of the unsolvability of these problems can be found in the book “Encyclopedia of Elementary Mathematics”, vol. IV, “Geometry”, M., Fizmatiz, 1963, pp. 220-223.
