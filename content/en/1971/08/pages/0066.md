4. Iversor Poselye. Let us draw the segments $OM'$ and $AB$, let $N$ be their intersection point (Fig. 2). Let us denote by $x$ the segment $MN$. Since $AM'BM$ is a rhombus, then by the Pythagorean theorem we find

$$AN^2 = AM^2 - x^2,$$

from here

$$ON^2 = OA^2 - AN^2 = OA^2 - AM^2 + x^2.$$

The segment $OM$ is equal to

$$ON - x = \sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} + x^2 - x,$$

and the segment $OM'$ is equal to

$$ON + x = \sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} + x.$$

Their work

$$OM \cdot OM' = (\sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} + x^2) \times (\sqrt{OA^2 - AM^2} + x^2) = OA^2 - AM^2,$$
