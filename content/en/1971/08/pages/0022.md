⟦folio 20⟧

made more of an impression on me. The main characteristic of a teacher should be generosity. Undoubtedly, Rutherford knew how to be generous, this, apparently, is the main secret of the fact that so many great scientists came out of his laboratory, it was always free and good to work in his laboratory, there was a good business atmosphere...

...Rutherford showed exceptional concern for his students. His view of the students was, schematically, like this - he said:

“If a young scientist works for me and after two years of work comes to me and asks what I should do next, I advise him to quit working in the field of science, or if a person after two years of work does not know what to do next, he cannot become a scientist.”

Essentially, he never posed the question so sharply and, under one proposal or another, he always found the opportunity to provide his unsuccessful students with a place either somewhere in an industrial laboratory, or a teaching position in some school or university...

...From this image of Rutherford that I have drawn for you, you will immediately see a man with a large, strong temperament. This temperament was expressed in everything, especially in cases. Rutherford could not stand dishonesty in work and mutual relations between people. If any student showed even the slightest dishonesty in anything - whether it was that he misrepresented the results of his work, or that he did not mention the source of his ideas and tried to present the work he produced as his own, when in fact he got the idea for it elsewhere - such a person lost all interest for Rutherford ...
