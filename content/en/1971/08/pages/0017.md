But the THERMAL EFFECT RESULTING FROM A GIVEN AMOUNT OF Radium cannot EXIST INFINITELY. Radium IS TRANSFORMED INTO OTHER SUBSTANCES, AND IT SEEMED THAT AFTER 20,000 YEARS ONLY A SMALL PART OF IT SHOULD REMAIN. HOWEVER THIS IS NOT SO. IT IS NOW DEFINITELY ESTABLISHED THAT RADIUM IS FORMED FROM URANIUM AND THAT IN OLD MINERALS THE AMOUNT OF RADium IS ALWAYS PROPORTIONAL TO THE AMOUNT OF URANIUM.

THE TRANSFORMATION OF URANIUM IS CONSIDERABLY SLOWER THAN THE TRANSFORMATION OF RADium, AND IT WILL LIKELY TAKE A PERIOD OF AT LEAST A THOUSAND MILLION YEARS WHILE THE URANIUM IS DISTRIBUTED. Consequently, DUE TO THE FORMATION OF RADIA DURING THE DISTRIBUTION OF RANIUM, THE AMOUNT OF RADIA IN THE EARTH WILL BE MAINTAINED AT A CONSTANT LEVEL FOR A PERIOD OF HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS OF YEARS.

Now WILL BRIEFLY DISTRIBUTE THE THEORY OF THE ORIGIN OF THE INNER BODY, THE AGE OF THE EARTH. IT IS KNOWN THAT THE TEMPERATURE OF THE EARTH GRADUALLY INCREASES AS IT ROTATES DEPTH - ON AN AVERAGE BY 1 ° C FOR EVERY THIRTY METERS IN THE DEPTH. ACCORDING TO DATA ABOUT VOLCANIC ACTIVITY, IT IS KNOWN THAT THE EARTH'S INSIDE HAS A HIGH TEMPERATURE.

WHEN SOLVING THE PROBLEM ABOUT THE AGE OF THE EARTH, KELVIN PREPOL stated that the EARTH ORIGINALLY HAD THE TEMPERATURE OF ROCK MELTING AND WAS GRADUALLY COOLED, RADIATING SOLID FROM THE SURFACE INTO SPACE. SOME QUANTITIES REQUIRED FOR DISTRIBUTION THROUGHOUT

THIS BODY WERE INSUFFICIENTLY KNOWN, BUT KELVIN CONCLUDED THAT, UNDOUBTEDLY, NO MORE THAN 100 MILLION YEARS HAVE PASSED SINCE THE EARTH'S SURFACE BECAME TEMPERATURALLY SUITABLE FOR PLANTS AND ANIMALS.

THERE IS NO DOUBT AS TO THE GENERAL CORRECTNESS OF THE CONCLUSIONS BASED ON THE ASSUMPTIONS MADE BY KELVIN. LET'S HOWEVER LET'S CONSIDER THESE PRE-HAULBS. IT IS ASSUMED THAT THE EARTH IS AN ISOLATED COOLING IN THE SPACE OF THE BODY, HAVING NO SOURCES OF THE BODY FROM THE MOMENT OF COOLING. HOWEVER, THE DETECTION OF SUBSTANCES SIMILAR TO RADIUM IN THE EARTH, WHICH EMMIT TELLO AT HIGH SPEED, IMMEDIATELY LEADS TO THE THOUGHT THAT THE EARTH IS NOT JUST A COOLING TELLO, AN ATTACK IN WHICH TELLO IS CONSTANTLY BORN.

IN 1902 I MADE SOME CALCULATIONS TO DETERMINE WHAT AMOUNT OF RADIAUM (OR OTHER RADIOACTIVE SUBSTANCE), HOMOGENEOUSLY DISTRIBUTED IN THE EARTH, WOULD BE NECESSARY TO CHANGE THE TEMPERATURE OF THE LAYERS OF THE EARTH TO REACH THE SURFACE WITH THE DEPTH REMAINED CONSTANT. IN SUCH A CASE, THE BODY COMING FROM RADIUM MUST ACCURATELY COMPENSATE THE CONSUMPTION OF THE BODY RADIATING INTO SPACE. THE CALCULATIONS ARE SIMPLE AND THEY SHOW THAT A UNIFORM DISTRIBUTION OF ONLY 1.7·10^{-13} e per 1 cm^{3} WOULD BE SUFFICIENT.
