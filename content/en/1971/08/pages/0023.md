...He treats people extremely caringly, especially his students. Arriving to work in his laboratory, I was immediately struck by this care. Rutherford does not allow you to work longer than six hours in the laboratory, and on weekends he does not allow you at all. I protested, but he said:

— It’s completely enough to work until 6 o’clock, the rest of the time we need to think. Bad people are those who work too much and think too little...

...I later saw that the time spent on separate tasks was completely repaid by the acquired energy...

...Once Rutherford called me into his office, and I found him reading a letter and roaring with his open and infectious laughter. It turned out that the letter was from students of some Ukrainian high school. They informed him that they had organized a physics circle and were going to continue his fundamental work on the study of the atom nucleus, asking him to become an honorary member and send reprints of his scientific works. When describing Rutherford's achievements and discoveries made in the field of nuclear physics, they used physiological terminology instead of physical one. Thus, the structure of atoma in the description of the students acquired the properties of a living organism, which made Rutherford laugh. I explained to Rutherford how this distortion could have occurred. Apparently, the schoolchildren themselves translated the letter and used a dictionary, and in Russian, unlike English, the word “kernel” has two meanings. Rutherford said that he assumed so, and responded to the guys with a letter in which he thanks them for the high part of their election and sends reprints of his works...

...The death of Professor Rutherford is a very heavy blow for scientists all over the world. In it, science lost the greatest pioneer of physical research since Faraday. Throughout his life, fruitful in scientific discoveries, he worked on the most fundamental problems of modern atom theory.

He can be considered not only as the creator of a new chapter in science, but also as the creator of a whole new science - nuclear physics...》.
