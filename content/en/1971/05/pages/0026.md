4. Finding matches. Using the concept of the center of gravity, problems of the following type can be easily solved. On the sides of the triangle $ABC$ there are points $K$ and $L$ (Fig. 5). Given that $\frac{AK}{KB} = \frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{BL}{LC} = \frac{3}{5}$, find the ratios of $\frac{AS}{SL}$ and $\frac{CS}{SK}$.

Solution. Let us place material points at the vertices $A, B, C$ so that the correspondence holds
$$\frac{m_B}{m_A} = \frac{AB}{KB} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \frac{m_C}{m_B} = \frac{BL}{LC} = \frac{3}{5},
$$
for example, let's put
$$m_A = 10m, \quad m_B = 5m, \quad m_C = 3m.$$
Then the center of gravity of the system of points $A$ and $B$ will lie at point $K$, and the center of gravity of the system of points $B$ and $C$ will lie at point $L$. Consequently, the center of gravity of the system of points $A, B$ and $C$ lies on the straight line $CK$ and at the same time on the straight line $AL$, that is, it is located at the intersection point $S$. From here it is easy to find that
$$\frac{AS}{SL} = \frac{m_B + m_C}{m_A} = \frac{5m + 3m}{10m} = \frac{4}{5},
$$
$$\frac{CS}{SK} = \frac{m_A + m_B}{m_C} = \frac{10m + 5m}{3m} = \frac{5}{1}.$$
