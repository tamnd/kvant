How to find out whether body movement is limited and to what extent? Do equilibrium positions exist, and if so, are they stable or unstable?

In this article we will show that such questions are easy to answer if the potential energy of the body is known. We'll discuss when it makes sense to talk about potential energy and how to calculate its magnitude at each point.

If we talk about potential energy, it means that we want to study the motion of a body, more precisely, of material points *), based on the law of conservation of mechanical energy.

This law states that for a closed system of material points (bodies) the quantities

$$E = T + U.$$

(1)

Sum

$$T = \frac{m_1 v_1^2}{2} + \frac{m_2 v_2^2}{2} + \dots + \frac{m_n v_n^2}{2}$$

is called the kinetic energy of the system, and $U$ is the potential energy of the interaction of the body. Kinetic energy in formula (1) reflects the body’s own motion, and potential energy, which depends on the distances between bodies, their mutual influence on each other.
