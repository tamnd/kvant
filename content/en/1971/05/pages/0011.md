Thus, $E \geq U$; we have obtained a criterion for determining the region in which movement with total energy $E$ is possible. At points where $E = U$, kinetic energy, and therefore speed, vanishes.

Let us consider as an example the movement of a body along a straight line (axis $x$). Then the potential energy depends only on the position on this line: $U = U(x)$. In this case, our criterion is very convenient to apply graphically. Let us draw a straight line $U(x) = E$ on the horizontal potential energy graph.
