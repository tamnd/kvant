TO OUR READERS

Subscriptions continue for the second half of 1971 to the popular scientific journal of physics and mathematics «Kvant».

The magazine is designed primarily for students in grades 7–10. It is also useful for teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics or mathematics, as well as all lovers of mathematics and physics.

The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school,” that is, materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to teach various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.

The reader will find many tasks in the magazine. Among them are problems offered at entrance exams for various disciplines, problems offered at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical instruments and experiments will help the reader set up and test a physical experiment.

The magazine publishes on its pages articles of a review nature, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists and stories from scientists themselves about how “science is done”, how scientific discoveries appear.

The magazine constantly publishes results on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
