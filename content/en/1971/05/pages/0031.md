The result was an amazing effect: when the viewer looks at people walking along this room, he observes unusual changes in their sizes. Depending on where a person goes, he seems to be either growing or faltering.

A small model of such a room allows you to see a person’s hands or face through the windows of the back wall. As in a large room, hands or faces appear large or small depending on whether they appear in the windows to the right or to the left. We are so accustomed to rooms of a general shape that in the Ames room we perceive even highly distorted objects as real. Perception of an image is a psychological process. In this case, the attitude of the abuser to the subject of abuse and even his condition at the moment is important. It is interesting that familiar faces are perceived as completely normal in Ames’s room, while the geometry of the room always becomes noticeable to the observer.

Of course, building a large Ames room yourself is quite difficult. But it is apparently possible to make a small model. By placing interior items, you can get some pretty effective photos if you want. Why not try it?
