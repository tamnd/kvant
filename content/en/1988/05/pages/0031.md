⟦folio 29⟧

Tasks

M1101—M1105, F1113—F1117

M1101. On the lateral sides AB and AC of equilateral triangle ABC, there were also points D and E, respectively, that AD=BC=EC and triangle ADE is equilateral. What could be the angle at vertex A?

V. Chiriac (Romania)

M1102. Prove that there are n different natural numbers whose sum of cubes is equal to the cube of a natural number if a) n=3, 6) n=4, c) n is any natural number greater than 2.

L. D. Kurlyandchik

M1103. a) On an infinite plane divided into square cells, there is a certain - perhaps infinite - number of rectangles of size 1x2 closed in black so that no two black rectangles have common points (hereinafter vertices). Prove that the rest of the plane can be replaced by these rectangles.

6)* Let there be several closed rectangles of size m×n on the checkered plane that have no common points. Prove that if mn is even, then the remaining part of the plane can be replaced by rectangles of size 1x2, but if mn is odd, then this is not always possible.

M. Khovanov, 10th grade student

M1104. In the ABCD tetrahedron, the faces ABC and BCD are perpendicular, ∠BAC=90°. Prove that from segments whose lengths are equal to the product of the lengths of the opposite edges of the tetrahedron, a right triangle can be formed.

V. N. Dubrovsky

M1105. After several rectangular cuts, the surfaces of the convex polygon were turned onto a plane. The result is a polygon for which it is known which points of its boundary are “glued together,” that is, they correspond to the same point on the surface of the polygon. What was the original polygon like if, when cut, the result is a) a rectangle with sides 1 and √3,
6) an equilateral triangle with an angle of 120°, and points on each side that are symmetrical about its middle are glued together.

N. P. Dolbili, M. I. Shtogrin

F1113. A ball of radius R can rotate freely on a fixed horizontal axis OO′. Under the ball, pressing against it, a rough rigid tape moves, bent in the form of a half-cylinder with radius R (Fig. 1). The belt speed v is directed horizontally and perpendicular to the OO′ axis. What is the set rotation speed of the ball?

L. G. Markovich
