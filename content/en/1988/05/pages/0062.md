Written exam
Option 1

1. The pedestrian crossed the road at a speed of $v=4,2$ km/h in a straight line making an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the direction of the road for one minute. Determine the width of the road.

2. A man with a mass of $M=60$ kg, standing on skates, throws a horizontal load with a mass of $m=2$ kg in front of him at a speed of $v=3$ m/s, and rolls back. How many seconds after the throw will the person stop if the friction coefficient of the skates is $\mu=0,01$? Take the acceleration of gravity equal to $g=10$ m/s$^2$.

3. How many times is the period of revolution of a satellite moving at a distance of $h_1=21$ 600 km from the surface of the Earth greater than the period of revolution of a satellite moving at a distance of $h_2=600$ km from the surface of the Earth, taking equal to $R=6400$ km.

4. Two balls with a diameter of $d=60$ cm each are fastened at the point of contact of their surface. At what distance from the point of contact is the mass of the system if the mass of one ball is 3 times the mass of the other?

5. The absolute temperature of the gas located in the cylinder under the piston was increased 3 times. In order for the piston to remain in position mode, a load with a mass of $m=10$ kg had to be placed on it. Piston area $S=0,001$ m$^2$. Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s$^2$. Find the initial gas pressure.

6. What pressure will occur for rutin pairs in a cylinder of a rutin lamp with a volume of $V=3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ m$^3$ at $T=300$ K, if it contains $N=10^{18}$ rutin molecules? Bigman's constant $k=1,38 \cdot 10^{-23}$ J/K.

7. The electronic field voltage of a flat impact capacitor with a capacity of $C=4$ µF is equal to $E=1000$ V/m. The distance between the capacitor plates is $d=1$ mm. Determine the energy of the electron field of the capacitor.

8. A voltmeter with a scale reading $U=100$ V has an internal resistance of $r=10$ kOhm. What potential difference can be changed by this device if a series additional resistance $R=90$ kOhm is connected to it?

9. In a uniform magnetic field there is a flat coil with an area of ​​$S=10$ cm$^2$, located perpendicular to the linear field. What amount of current will be consumed per turn if the field indication decreases at a constant rate of $\Delta B/\Delta t'=0,01$ T/s? Turn resistance $R=1$ Ohm.

10. Monchromatic light falls perpendicular to the plane of the transparent diffraction grating. At what angle will the first main maximum of the green line of the spectrum of a traditional lamp (with a wavelength of $\lambda=546$ nm) be observed if the period of the diffraction grating is $d=10,92 \times 10^{-7}$ m?
