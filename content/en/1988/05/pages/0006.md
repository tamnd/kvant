— Recently, as an expert mathematician, you participated in the creation of a project for the disposal of the emergency unit of the Chernovl NPP. What can you tell us about this?

— Apparently, a person has some hidden capabilities that science is not yet able to explain. The magician hypothesizes a person, and he hangs steadily by his fingers and toes between two chairs. This shows the absolutely extraordinary potential inherent in a person. I have always been amazed at how great the potential of our people and our scientists turned out to be during the war. Despite the huge losses, the country after the war turned out to be stronger than before it. Apparently, here too there is a moment of mass hypnosis: colossal tension strengthened the people.

We had the same situation when we were working on the problem of the Chernovl Nuclear Power Plant after the explosion. It was really intense and productive work, which I still believe - such work can be done in two, three years, but not in the two months in which we completed it. Moreover, not only we, mathematicians, but also everyone who was involved in this, all the scientists of other specialties who came, were swept up in this massive upsurge. Where did the strength come from - the work went incredibly quickly. There are no biorkathic barriers, which require the scientist’s main strength to overcome in everyday life. When we thought through and proposed any experiment, in the morning, say, I turned to organizations, and on the same day the experiment was carried out at the Chernovl NPP. We, of course, knew very well how expensive each experiment is, how many X-ray hours per person are spent on this or that test. This created extraordinary tension. Another example is that my workmate V.P. Myasnikov called me at three o’clock in the morning to inform me that the “hole” in the proof that I discovered was given - the “holds” option. Or my young employees lingered with me in the evenings after the last time of the real installation, that they could no longer work at all, they went on vacation (it was vacation time, and I detained them all), they were replaced by others. Never in my life have I experienced such intense tension. When service issues related to the carcophagus were being resolved and a responsible decision had to be made, everyone imagined how important the consequences of a wrong choice could be. The deadlines were tight, because the carcophagus was created at lightning speed, and sometimes our mathematical modeling couldn’t keep up. And when nature teaches and forces you to redo it, it is a bitter and expensive experience. Therefore, the work was absolutely extraordinary, tense and intense. And in the final case, not only calculations relating to this station directly were obtained, although we carried out calculations only for it. But nevertheless, as a result of mathematical modeling, we obtained descriptions of effects unexpected for mechanics, sometimes contradicting the assumptions of our physicist colleagues.

— In applied tasks, such as modeling the behavior of a complex system (such as an out of control nuclear power plant), a computer experiment is used. Have these techniques been used?

— The computer experiment should actually have been applied before this sad event occurred. As they say, a Russian person is strong in hindsight - such a computer experiment has just been carried out; he directly showed that an explosion was about to happen. After the explosion occurred, a computer experiment showing one of the possible variants of the reactor's behavior would no longer actually be achieved, so we had to look at all the options. After all, our goal is to give certain recommendations. For example, our calculations showed that it was not necessary to place a concrete casting (pillow) under the carcophagus.
