Physics
Written exam objectives
1. A bullet flying horizontally hits a ball suspended on a light rigid rod and gets stuck in it. The mass of the bullet is $m=5$ g, the mass of the ball is $M=0,5$ kg. Bullet speed $v=500$ m/s. At what maximum length of the rod (distance from the point of suspension to the center of the ball) will the ball rise to the top point of the circle when hit by a bullet?
2. What is the work done to lift a chain, taken by one end and lying on a plane, to a height at which the lower end moves away from the plane at a distance equal to the length of the chain? Chain length $l=2$ m, weight $m=5$ kg.
3. Some mass $m$ of an ideal gas with molar mass $M$ is heated under a piston so that the temperature changes proportionally to the square of the pressure from the first value $T_1$ to $T_2$. Determine the work done by the gas.
4. Determine the density of a mixture containing $m_1=4$ g of hydrogen and $m_2=32$ g of oxygen at a temperature of $t=7$ °C and a volume of pressure of $p=10^5$ Pa.
5. Two electrons are at an infinitely large distance from each other, with the first electron expiring first, and the second having a velocity $v_0$ directed towards the first. Determine the shortest distance at which they merge.
6. How much heat will be released in the circuit when switching key $K$ from position 1 to position 2 (Fig. 1)?
