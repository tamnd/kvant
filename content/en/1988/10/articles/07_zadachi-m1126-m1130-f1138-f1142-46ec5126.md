M1127. The “Cheburashka” microcalculator has an adder that allows you to subtract and find the reciprocal number 1/x from a given number x. Is it possible to use this microcalculator to get a unit if the original number is a) √19 + 88; b) √19 + √88? (It is prohibited to enter numbers into the microcalculator that differ from the original one or from those obtained as a result of calculations on it.)

A. B. Voomolnaya

M1128. Several pieces are placed on the chessboard. In one move, one of the chips moves to an adjacent (horizontally or vertically) free field. After several moves, it turned out that each chip hit all the fields exactly once and returned to the original field. Prove that there was a moment when not a single piece was on its original field.

E. B. Abakumov

M1129*. Fir trees and birches grow in Baron Monhausen's forest. The baron claims that at a distance of exactly 1 km from each fir tree there are exactly 10 birch trees, and there are more fir trees in his forest than birches. could this be?

F. L. Nazarov

M1130. On the plane dan there is a convex n-gon whose length of the kth side is equal to a_k, and the length of the projection of the polygon onto the line containing this side is equal to d_k (k=1, 2, ..., n). Prove the inequality

$$2 \leq \frac{a_1}{d_1} + \dots + \frac{a_n}{d_n} \leq 4.$$

D. V. Fomin

F1138. Accelerating at the maximum possible speed on a straight section of the highway, the racing car increases speed from 10.0 m/s to 10.5 m/s in 0.1 s. How long would it take him to do the same thing on a stretch of highway with a radius of 30 m? At what radius of the ring would he generally not be able to increase the speed above 10 m/s? The highway plane is horizontal.

Determine the steady-state difference in water levels in the tube bends. Reference: at a height of 23 floors (70 m above the ground), the boiling point of water is 0.25 degrees lower than at ground level. Thermal distribution of the stack when calculating pretenbr.

F1141. Currently, in connection with the discovery of the phenomenon of high-temperature superconductivity, the question of creating direct current transmission lines without energy losses on a Joule body is being studied. It is proposed to use a coaxial cable for direct current transmission, consisting of an inner cylindrical liquid and an outer cylindrical shell made of a superconductor. The electric and magnetic fields in such a system are depicted at figure 2. It is known that the magnetic field induction at the surface of a superconductor cannot exceed a certain value $B_{\max}$, otherwise superconductivity is destroyed, and the direction of the electric field should not exceed $E_{\max}$, otherwise an electrical breakdown of the insulating layer of the cable occurs. Determine how many times the maximum direct current power transmitted through such a cable will change if the diameters of the inner and outer sheath are doubled. What is the maximum power that can be transmitted through a cable with sheath diameters $D=8$ cm, $d=3$ cm, if $E_{\max}=20$ kV/cm and $B_{\max}=5 \cdot 10^{-2}$ T? Note: the magnetic field induction in the space between the cylindrical conductors coincides with the field of a straight conductor with current $I$:

$$B = \frac{\mu_0}{2\pi} \frac{I}{r}$$

($\mu_0=4\pi \cdot 10^{-7} \text{ H/A}^2$ is the magnetic constant).

S. M. Kozel

F1142. The formation of the Jupterian cometary family is described by the following scheme. The comet falls from a great distance without initial speed onto the Sun and flies not far from Jupter (Fig. 3). After the noticeable influence of Jupter’s gravitational field ceases, the comet again moves in the field of the Sun, and its speed turns out to be directed opposite to the speed of Jupter, and the apheal new orbit of the comet is located close to Jupter, i.e. at a distance of $R=5,2$ a. e. from the Sun. At what distance from the Sun will the perihelion orbit of such a comet be located?

V. E. Belonuchkin
