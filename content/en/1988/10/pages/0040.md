⟦folio 38⟧

Since $\beta_0 \approx 90^\circ$, we get

$$\sin \alpha_0 = \frac{c(h)}{c_0} = 1 - ah.$$

Next, we learn that the change in the speed of light with height is very small and therefore the bending of rays is also very small. So to calculate the distance $H$ the ray can be considered straight. From figure it is clear that $H = h \cdot tg \alpha_0$. From here, using the law of refraction, we get

$$H = \frac{h(1 - ah)}{\sqrt{ah(2 - ah)}}.$$

V. I. Klyachin

Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. The numbers $p$ and $q$ are prime. Find them if you know that the equation $x^4 - px^3 + q = 0$ has an integer root.
2. Prove that if $xyz = 1$, then

$$\frac{1}{1+x+xy} + \frac{1}{1+y+yz} + \frac{1}{1+z+zx} = 1.$$

3. Two circles of different radii touch each other internally at point $P$. The chord $BC$ of the larger circle touches less at the point $A$. Prove that $PA$ is the bisector of angle $BPC$.
4. In the rectangle $ABCD$ the perpendicular $BK$ is dropped onto the diagonal $AC$. Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of segments $AK$ and $CD$, respectively. Prove that angle $BMN$ is right.
5. Inside the given triangle $ABC$, construct a point $M$ such that the area of ​​the triangles $ABM, BCM, CAM$ will be in the ratio $k:l:m$, where $k, l, m$ are the given segments.
6. Prove that the equation

$$x^{1987} + y^{1987} = x^{1988} + y^{1988}$$

has an infinite number of solutions in radion numbers.
7. The sequence $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ ... satisfies the correspondence $x_{n+1} = x_n^3 - 3x_n^2 + 3x_n$ ($n = 1, 2, \dots$). What should $x_1$ be so that the sequences $x_{100}$ and $x_{1000}$ are equal?
8. An infinite geometric progression with positive terms and denominator $q$ has the property that each of its terms, starting from the second, is greater than the sum of all previous ones. What values ​​can $q$ take?

9. From each vertex of the pentagon, vectors are drawn to the midpoints of three sides not adjacent to this vertex. Prove that the sum of all 15 vectors constructed in this way is equal to 0.
10. In triangle $ABC$ the bisector $AF$ of angle $A$ is drawn. Let $D, E$ be the points of intersection of $AF$ with the inscribed circle in the triangle. Prove that $AD > EF$.

Tenth class
11. Solve the inequality

$$|x^9 - x| + |x^8 - x^7| \leq |x^9 - x^8 + x^7 - x|.$$

12. Let $M$ be an arbitrary point of the base $ABC$ tetrahedra $SABC$. Through point $M$ segments parallel to the side edges are drawn until they intersect with the side edges. Let the lengths of the side edges be equal to $a, b, c$, and the lengths of parallel and segments, respectively, equal to $x, y, z$. Prove that $\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b} + \frac{z}{c} = 1$.
13. Dan tetrahedр $ABCD$. One ball touches edges $AB$ and $CD$ at points $A$ and $C$, and the other ball touches edges $B$ and $D$. Prove that the projections of the edges $AC$ and $BD$ onto the line passing through the centers of these balls are equal.
14. Solve the equation $tg x - 2[tg x] - 3 = 0$ (the square brackets indicate the integer part of the number).
15. Prove that for any $a$ the equation

$$10ax^4 - 4ax^3 + a^2x^2 + 6x - 2 = 0$$

has at least one root in the interval [0; 1].
