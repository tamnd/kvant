⟦folio 55⟧

All works proposed at the conference can be divided into two large groups. The first, larger one, includes observations and research conducted by schoolchildren independently. Such works evaluate the most important statements, even if the author made minor mistakes. Thus, the jury of the physics section-1 noted the report of D. Samborsky (Kiev) “This is why labor organizations sound.” During the demonstration, a children's flute was used, which, in accordance with physical laws, sounded either higher or lower. Ninth-class students from the Physics School No. 18 in Moscow V. Kravchenko and N. Koblyakov demonstrated an application of infrared radiation created by them. An excellent installation for demonstrating the movement of stake exits in the air was constructed by Klassoyar schoolchildren D. Koval, A. Kiselev, I. Noskov, D. Rashevsky.

Another group includes purely abstract works. Among the best works noted by Jorri are the abstracts of ninth-graders V. Suvorov (Tolyatti) “Lasers” and E. Ryzhikova (Poltava) “Superfluid liquids and their properties.” Some abstracts are a section copied from a popular science book or textbook. Such abstracts are not highly rated. The best works are planned to be published in «Kvant» or “Physics at School”. Two papers from the conference have already been published in «Kvant», these are “Optimal Zip Code Encoding” (1987, No. 11) and “Observation and Photo Volume of Rapid Protection Processes” (1988, No. 2).

We will present new works in physics, mathematics and computer science at the IV Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren, which takes place in April 1989. Everyone is welcome to attend the conference. To do this, you need to write an abstract (7-25 pages, if possible, print by machine) and send one copy no later than February 1, 1989 to the address: 141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, Organizing Committee of the IV Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren. The abstract will not be returned. Additional information can be obtained by calling ZFTSH at MIPT: 408-51-45.

A. V. Sapozhnikov, member of the conference organizing committee
