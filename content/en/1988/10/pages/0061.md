8. $q \geq 2$. Solution. Our condition is equality $q^n > q^{n-1} + q^{n-2} + \dots + 1$, i.e.
$$q^n > \frac{q^n - 1}{q - 1}.$$

Since, obviously, $q > 1$, this equality can be rewritten as $q^{n+1} - 2q^n + 1 > 0$, i.e. $q^n(q - 2) + 1 > 0$. If $q \geq 2$, then the last equality holds for any $n \in \mathbb{N}$; if $1 < q < 2$, then there is $n$ for which $q^n \geq \frac{1}{2 - q}$, and for this $n$ the equality does not hold.
