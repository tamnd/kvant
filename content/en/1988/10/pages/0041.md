⟦folio 39⟧

Tasks

1. Ira, Vitya and Kolya each took a portion of all types of ice cream: fruit, cream and chocolate. However, three servings were not enough for each person, and Ira took another portion of fruit ice cream, Vitya took creamy ice cream, and Kolya took chocolate ice cream. When leaving, they paid: Ira - 70 kopecks, Vitya - 80 kopecks, Kolya - 90 kopecks. How much does each ice cream cost?

2. Arrange the numbers $a=2^{45}, b=3^{36}, c=4^{27}, d=5^{18}$ in ascending order.

3. The epitet “sparkling” is often used with the word “snow”. What causes this?

4. Unscramble the arithmetic puzzle to figure. (Identical letters correspond to identical numbers, different ones correspond to different ones.)

5. The diagonal of a convex quadrilateral bisects the segment connecting the midpoints of two opposite sides of this quadrilateral. Show that this diagonal also bisects the area of ​​the quadrilateral.

These tasks were proposed to us by: V. D. Vyon, A. P. Savin, A. A. Panov, S. Madrimov, N. Yu. Nechevgaev.
