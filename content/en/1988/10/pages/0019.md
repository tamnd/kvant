Sergey Lvovich Sobolev

(on the occasion of his 80th birthday)

October 6, 1988 marked the 80th anniversary of the birth of Tsergei Lvovich Sobolev.

The works of S. L. Sobolev had a significant influence on the development of mathematics of the 20th century; he made a fundamental contribution to the theory of partial arbitrary equations, functional analysis, function theory, mathematical physics, and computational mathematics.

Having independently founded the high school program (Sergei Lvovich studied at school for only one year - in the last grade), Sobolev in 1925 entered the Faculty of Physiomatics and Mathematics of Leningrad University. His mathematical talent manifested itself unusually early. Suffice it to say that at the age of 25 he was elected a corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences, and at the age of 31 - an academician.

The years after graduation were very fruitful for Sergei Lvovich. Together with V.I. Smirnov, he created what has become a classical method for solving the wave equation that describes oscillations in an elastic medium. The study of “Sobolev spaces” of functions introduced by S. L. Sobolev in the 30s now constitutes an entire section of functional analysis. The concept of a generalized function proposed by Sergei Lvovich turned out to be especially important; developed later in the works of the French mathematician Laurent Schwartz and the Soviet mathematician Academician I.M. Gelfand, it became one of the central concepts of mathematics.

Having moved to Moscow, S. L. Sobolev soon began teaching at Moscow University, where he worked for more than twenty years. From 1952 to 1957, he headed the first department of computational mathematics in our country. The contribution of S. L. Sobolev to this science is reflected in his monograph “Introduction to the theory of cubatrinic formulas.”

In 1957, S. L. Sobolev moved to Novosibirsk, becoming one of the organizers of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences. From 1960 to 1978 he was also a professor at Novosibirsk University.

S. L. Sobolev has many students and followers. He was elected a member of the French Academy of Sciences, the Academia Nazionale dell'Affaires Lynchenca in Rome, as well as many other scientific societies. He is the Chairman of the National Committee of Soviet Mathematicians.

The editors of the magazine «Kvant» cordially congratulate the member of the editorial board «Kvant» Sergei Lvovich Sobolev on his anniversary and wish him health and new creative success.
