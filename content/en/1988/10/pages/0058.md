XIV All-Russian Olympiad of SCHOOLCHILDREN

According to established tradition, the final, zonal stage of the All-Russian physico-mathematical and chemical optimization of schoolchildren in grades 8-10 took place during the spring school holidays.
Below are the conditions of the problems in mathematics and physics of the final stage of optimization.

Matematika
8th grade
1. Find some natural numbers $x$ and $y$ that satisfy the equation $x^2 - 51y^2 = 1$.
2. A common tangent is drawn to two circles intersecting at points $M$ and $K$. Prove that if $A$ and $B$ are points of tangency, then $\angle AMB + \angle AKB = 180^\circ$.
3. It is known that $a^5 - a^3 + a = 2$. Prove that $3 < a^6 < 4$.
4. On the face of checkered paper with squares of size $1 \times 1$, a circle of radius $R$ is drawn, centered at the grid node. Prove that if there are exactly 1988 grid points on it, then either $R$ or $R \sqrt{2}$ is an integer.
5. In a volleyball tournament, each room plays with each other room exactly once (there are no two). We will say that team $A$ will transform team $B$ if $A$ beat $B$ or if $A$ beat some team $C$, which beat $B$. At the end of the tournament, any team that outperforms all other teams is declared a champion. Can a tournament have exactly two champions?
6. Class
1. For some natural $n$, the numbers $2^n$ and $5^n$ start with the same digit. What is this number?
2. See problem 3 for 8th grade.
3. On the coordinate plane $xOy$, all possible equilateral triangles $OAB (OA = AB)$ are considered, in which the vertex $A$ lies on the graph of the function $y = \frac{2x^2}{x^2 - 1}$ ($x > 1$), and the vertex $B$ lies on the axis $Ox$. Prove that the lines $AB$ are tangent to the same circle.
4. Vectors $\overrightarrow{AP} = \overrightarrow{CR}$ are plotted from the vertices $A$ and $C$ of the convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, and vectors $\overrightarrow{BQ} = \overrightarrow{DT}$ from the vertices $B$ and $D$. It turned out that the quadrilateral $PQRT$ is convex. Prove that the quadrilateral $ABCD$ and $PQRT$ are of equal size.
5. Given a sequence of $n$ numbers: $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$. In one step, replace with the sequence $|a_1 - a|, |a_2 - a|, ..., |a_n - a|$, where $a$ is an arbitrary real number (the numbers $a$ can be different at different steps).
a) Prove that in a few steps you can obtain a sequence consisting of only zeros.
6) In what is the least number of steps this can be achieved?
10th grade
1. Let
$$\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{1 + \dots}}} = \frac{m}{n},$$
where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime natural numbers, and the left side of the equality contains 1988 fractional traits. Calculate the value of the expression $m^2 + mn - n^2$.
2. Given a triangle $ABC$. The circle passes through vertices $A, B$ and intersects sides $AC$ and $CB$ at points $P$ and $Q$ of the corresponding one. On the side $AB$ the points $R$ and $S$ are taken so that $QR \parallel CA, PS \parallel CB$. Prove that the points $P, Q, R, S$ lie on the same circle.
3. All natural numbers from 1 to 1988 inclusive are written on the board. The operations $A$ and $B$ are performed with this number: first - $A$, then - $B$, again - $A$, after which - again $B$, and so on. Operation $A$: the same natural number is subtracted from each number written on the board (the subtracted numbers may be different for different applications of the operation $A$). Operation $B$: two of the numbers on the board are drawn and their sum is written. Operations $A$ and $B$ were carried out until after the next execution of operation $B$ there was only one number left on the board. It turned out that this number is non-negative. What is it equal to?
4. There are several points in a given space, no four of which lie in the same plane. These points have the property that if the sphere passes through any four in the devil's color, and the second - any cell in the white color. At first all the cells were white. Can the second player ensure that every square note of his move is colored white?
a) at least one corner cell,
b) at least two corner cells
any square of size $5 \times 5$? (Cells are called adjacent if they have a common side; the same cell can be recolored several times during the game.)
