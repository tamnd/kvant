Physics
Theoretical tour

8th grade

1. A spiral with a constant pitch is wound from a thin wire of length $L$ and secured so that its axis slides along the spiral without friction. How long will it take for a bead released from the top point without an initial speed to reach the end of the spiral if the height of the spiral is $H$?

2. The flashlight emits a beam of light, converging at a distance of $R_0=1$ m from it into a small spot. Two square flat mirrors were placed along the path of the beam so that the line of their contact is on the beam axis at a distance of $r=70$ cm from the flashlight and perpendicular to the beam axis. The planes of the mirrors are overlapping with each other, and one of the mirrors makes an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the beam axis. At what distance from the flashlight will the teler beam converge?

3. When the switches $K_1$ and $K_2$ are open, the circuit shown at figure 1 consumes power $P_0$. With one closed key $K_1$ the power consumption is $P_1$, and with $K_2—P_2$. How much power will be consumed by the circuit if both switches are closed? The resistances of the resistors are not known.

4. In a curved tube of constant cross-section there are two different liquids, the density ratio of which is 2:1. While the tube is motionless, the liquid interface passes along the symmetry axis of the tube (Fig. 2). Where and by how much will the interface shift if the tube is rotated around a vertical axis with an angular velocity of $\omega=5$ s$^{-1}$? Distance $R=20$ cm.
