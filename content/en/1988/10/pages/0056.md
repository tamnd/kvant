III Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren at MIPT

The third Shkolnikov Scientific and Technical Conference was held on April 2-3, 1988 by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The work of the oriental conference intensified a lot earlier: since January, MIPT began receiving works from schoolchildren from all over the Soviet Union. The conference organizing committee deliberately did not offer even an approximate list of abstract topics. This caused, however, a large number of people to ask either to suggest a topic or to ask whether such and such a topic was suitable. The most difficult thing in solving a problem is posing the question. The school, unfortunately, does not teach this, and we often cannot recommend any topic because we do not know the Shkolnik’s personal interests or his ability to carry out a complex experiment. The answer to the second question was always: any topic is suitable for an essay, if you approach it seriously enough, any task is both difficult and interesting. As a result, the submitted works of more than 400 schoolchildren in grades 6–10 from 10 allied resource breeds have an algorithm with a variety of topics.

All submitted abstracts were given special consideration. Reputable subject-specific juries selected the best works from the submitted works to invite the author to speak at MIPT.

And on April 2, he received physical guests. Passions have not yet calmed down after MIPT students celebrated the first of April, and the LAB and auditoriums were filled with schoolchildren. Before the opening of the conference, there were excursions to the scientific laboratories of MIPT, and students talked about the specializations of the physics faculties.

At the opening of the conference, the rector of MIPT, corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences N.V. Karlov spoke. After this, a plenary session took place, at which leading scientists gave lectures to the children. The most interesting, sorry, was the lecture by Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences L. V. Okun, dedicated to the theory of the relative side?

Schoolchildren reported the results of their research on the second day of the conference. Meetings were held simultaneously in six sections: physics-1, physics-2, molecular physics, aerospace physics, mathematics and computer science. On the same day, the conference was closed and its participants were awarded.

Speaking at the closing of the conference, Academician-Secretary of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR V. G. Razumovsky expressed satisfaction with the level of work and the hope that in the future the same names would appear in collections of scientific works, among the authors of discoveries and inventions.
