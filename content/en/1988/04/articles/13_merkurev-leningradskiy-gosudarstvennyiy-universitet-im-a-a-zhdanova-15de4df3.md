⟦folio 62⟧

Leningrad State University named after. A. A. Zhdanava

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculties of mathematics and mechanics, physics, applied mathematics - control processes)

1. The sum of the digits of a certain three-digit number is 11. If you subtract 594 from a number written with the same digits, but in reverse order, you get the desired number. Find this three-digit number if you know that the sum of three pairwise products of the digits of this number is 31.

2. Solve the inequality $x - \sqrt{1-x} \leq 0$.

3.1. (faculty of mathematics and mechanics) The numbers $x, y, a$ are such that
$$\begin{cases} x + y = a - 1, \\ x^2 + y^2 = 5a^2 - 3a + 0,5. \end{cases}$$

At what product $a$ does $xy$ take on the greatest value?

3.2 (department of physics)
Solve the equation
$$\log_{3x+7}(9+12x+4x^2) + \log_{2x+3}(6x^2+23x+21) = 4.$$

3.3. (Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)
Solve the equation
$$8\cos x = \frac{1}{\cos x} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sin x}.$$

4. Find the area of ​​a square circumscribed by a right triangle with squares $a$ and $b$ (the side of the square lies on the hypotenuse, and the two vertices lie on the square of the triangle).

5. All four faces of the triangular triangle are equal equilateral triangles, the length of the sides of which is equal to $\sqrt{3}$. Find the length of the bases of these triangles if it is known that the volume of the base is $\frac{2}{3}$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Libraries and Soil Sciences)

1. Two skiers started one after the other with an interval of 2 minutes. The second ski slope of the agreement is located 1 km from the starting point. Having reached the turn at the 5 km mark, the second skier turned back and met the first skier. This meeting took place 20 minutes after the start of the first ski race. Find the speed of the first skier.

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_3(3^x-8) = 2-x.$$

3.1. (soil department)
Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{10-18\cos x} = 6\cos x - 2.$$

3.2 For what $a$ does the equation $\cos(\sqrt{a-x^2}) = 1$ have 8 solutions?

4. An equilateral trapezoid with an acute angle $a$ at the base and height $h$ is drawn into a circle of radius $r$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

5. The volume of a regular triangular polygon is $V$, the angle between the diagonals of two faces drawn from the same vertex is $a$. Find the side of the base of the prim.

Option 3
(geodramatic and geological faculties)

1. Two motorcyclists, leaving point $A$ at the same time, travel at different but constant speeds to point $B$ and, when it is accessible, immediately turn back. The first motiklist, having overtaken the second, meets him on the way back at a distance of 13 km from $B$, then, reaching $A$ and again turning back $B$, he meets the second motiklist, having covered $\frac{1}{7}$ distances from $A$ to $B$. Find the distance from $A$ to $B$.

2.1. (Faculty of Geography).
Solve the equation
$$\log_3\left(\frac{3}{x}\right) - \log_3\left(\frac{x^3}{\sqrt{3}}\right) = \frac{1}{2} + \log_2\sqrt{x}.$$

2.2. (Faculty of Geography)
Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{1+4x-x^2} = x-1.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin 7x - \sin x + 2\cos^2 2x = 1.$$

4. Find the angles of a right triangle if you know that the radius of the circle is 2 and the hypotenuse is 13.

5.1. (Faculty of Geography)
The angle between the side edge and the base of a regular quadrilateral is equal to $60^\circ$, the side edge is equal to $a$. A plane is drawn through the middle of one of the lateral ribs, peredicular to it. Find the cross-sectional area.

5.2. (Faculty of Geography)
Solve the equation
$$\log_{\frac{x+1}{5}}(x^2-6x+9) \geq 0.$$

Option 4
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. From a flask containing 80 g of a 10% solution of table salt, pour a neotropic part of the solution into a test tube and evaporate until the percentage of salt in the test tube increases twice. After this, the boiled solution is poured back into the flask. As a result, the salt content in the flask increases by 2%. How much solution was poured from the flask into the test tube?

2. Solve equation $(x^2-4)\sqrt{x+1} = 0.$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x = \sqrt{\sin^2 3x - \sin^2 2x}.$$
