Introductory task

1. "Record". Can a jet-skiing athlete move faster than a jet skier?

2. "Tower of Babel". Determine the highest height of a concrete tower that can collapse under the influence of its own gravity if the permissible pressure on the concrete is 5 MPa.

3. "Gradius". Why does the temperature change with a medical thermometer take a long time, but you can “shake off” the thermometer almost instantly?

4. "Rain". There is a vertical movement on the street. Drop velocity u. A ball slides along the asphalt with speed v. How many times more drops fall on a moving ball (in seconds) than on an equally stationary ball? Will the answer change if the ball is not round?

5. "Bath". Determine the shape of the surface of a liquid in a bath that has slid off an inclined plane without friction.

Youth

Clubs, olympiads, competitions, conferences - this is, of course, a good thing. But we felt that there is something unique in Omsk that distinguishes the Omsk non-governmental educational institution from similar ones in Moscow and Novosibirsk, Chelyabinsk and Krasnoyarsk, Leningrad and Chisinau. The discussion of the school reform project made us think: is this the direction we are going?

The first question that arose in all non-governmental educational institutions: wide or deep? Indeed, in 99% of cases we are engaged in self-deception, talking about “school science”, “small academies”, etc. Very rarely, high school students are able to have their say in mathematics or its applications. There is, of course, an element of play in conversations about “school science,” but the name is obligatory. If we call a society scientific, then the forms of its work should be aimed at quality. And for this we need to reduce the number of our shares by tens, make the company contain a few “promising” ones. What about the rest? They also benefit, and a lot. They learn to work with popular literature, learn about the use of mathematics in various professions, ovat sections about who to be. If society concentrates the already limited resources of curators on the best students, what will be left for others? In general, there is the same gap as in sports - training for Olympic champions, but it is also impossible to stock up on mass physical education.

What is the main task of our society? We consider - in vocational guidance, first of all - professions that require higher education. In particular, the role of mathematical sections is to familiarize the student with a wide range of mathematical professions (scientist, applied scientist, programmer, teacher of mathematics and computer science), as well as the opportunity to apply mathematics in various fields.

When we defined this task of ours, specific answers emerged. Proformance needs to start early. Therefore, for example, the correspondence mathematics school of Omsk State University today works not only with high school students, but also with students in grades 6-8. Her assignments involve breaking materials and writing an abstract, developing and checking a circle lesson, writing and implementing a program on a suitable computing device. The methodology for conducting the Olympiad team for grades 4-7 has been adjusted; topics for clubs and assignments for search work are being developed.

As a rule, a new product begins with study in a “laboratory” - the Student Scientific and Pedagogical Society of the Faculty of Mathematics of Omsk State University (by the way, winner of the Omsk Komsomol Prize). Dozens of coursework and diploma papers are devoted to such developments. Everything that “happened” is passed on to city schools and vocational schools. Pedagogy of mathematics

Vse, having received data on applicants to the Faculty of Mathematics of Omsk State University in 1987, processed them, calculating the chances of admission depending on participation in professional events (he divided the number of applicants by the number of applicants from the same group). Here's what he got:

| Type of activity | Total | Enrolled | Odds (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Classes at ZMSH | 21 | 16 | 76,2 |
| Presentations at the conference | 17 | 13 | 76,5 |
| NPOU "Ponsk" | 30 | 23 | 76,7 |
| Prize-winning places in regional competitions | 14 | 12 | 85,7 |
| Classes at the Thursday Math School | 8 | 7 | 87,5 |
| Participated in at least one event | 68 | 50 | 73,5 |
| Did not participate in this activity | 77 | 19 | 24,7 |

Everyone was surprised: it is clear that the chances of someone participating in “at least one” type of activity should be at least no lower than in the most “unreliable” of the types. But here it’s the other way around!

- study this phenomenon and understand its causes;

— present a mathematical explanation of the model.
