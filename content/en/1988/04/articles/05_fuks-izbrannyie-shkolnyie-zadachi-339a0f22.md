⟦folio 36⟧

F1092. Find the position of the image of the exact source located at a distance L from the front surface of a thick plane glass plate, in the middle on the back side (see figure). The image is viewed transversely to the surface of the plate. Plate thickness d, stack refractive index n.

At figure the image is constructed in the plate. As can be seen from figure, the image is behind the plate at a distance $x = \frac{l}{\varphi} - L = L + \frac{2d}{n}$ from the front surface.

V. K. Peterson

Selected school assignments

Eighth grade
1. Solve the system of equations
   $x_1 + x_1x_2 = 1,$
   $x_2 + x_2x_3 = 1,$
   $x_{99} + x_{99}x_{100} = 1,$
   $x_{100} + x_{100}x_1 = 1$.

2. Prove that there is a natural number whose cube ends in 1987.

3. Given 3 positive integers. Their most common divisor is 1, the sum of any two divisors by the third. Find these numbers.

4. Three positive numbers $x, y, z$ are given. Under what condition does there exist a triangle whose altitudes have lengths $x, y, z$?

5. One book describes such a case. A rubber ball hits the door of a room, reflects off the wall, then off another wall and, without hitting a single object, hits the door exactly where it flew in. Could this be?

Ninth grade
6. Solve the system of equations
   $\sin x + \sin y = \sqrt{2} \sin a,$
   $\cos x + \cos y = \sqrt{2} \cos a$.

7. The quadratic equation $x^2 + px - q = 0$ has a solution. The numbers $p$ and $q$ are changed, each by no more than 1% of its absolute value. It is known that a better equation would be:

has a solution. Is it possible to guarantee that the largest of the solutions of the new equation differs from the largest of the solutions of the old equation by no more than, say, 10% of the absolute value of the latter?

8. What is the most possible radius of a circle that can accommodate four non-overlapping circles of radius 1?

9. Given four segments of length $a, b, c, d$. Under what condition can one construct from them a closed self-intersecting broken line, which three vertices of which do not lie on the same straight line?

10. Draw a graph of the function
    $y = |1 - |1 - |1 - |1 - |x|$.

Tenth grade
11. Solve the equation $x^y = 48 261 724 457$ in positive integers.

12. Find the general domain of the function
    $f_n(x) = \sqrt{x + \sqrt{x + \ldots + \sqrt{x + 1}}}$.

13. Given a polyhedron, no plane section of which has more than 4 sides. Prove that this is a tetrad.

14. A primo circular cylinder and a straight circular cone have a common base. The volume of each of them is equal to 1. Find the volume of their common part.

15. For what $a$ and $b$ does the formula $\sqrt{x} \approx ax + b$ guarantee the most accuracy for $4 \leq x \leq 9$. In other words, at which $a$ and $b$ is the most accurate value of the expression $|\sqrt{x} - (ax + b)|$ at $4 \leq x \leq 9$?
