The Universe as a Heat Engine

(For the beginning, see p. 2)

must write down

$$\varrho_* l_{\max}^2 = \text{const}'''.$$ (4)

We have already said that the volume of a closed world is $V \approx l^3$. In order of magnitude, this volume at the maximum expansion is equal to $l_{\max}^3$, and the total mass of matter M in the Universe is

$$\varrho_* l_{\max}^3 = M.$$ (5)

Dividing (5) by (4), we get:

$$l_{\max} = \frac{M}{\text{const}'''}$$.

So, $l_{\max}$ is proportional to the total mass of matter in the Universe M. But the mass M consists of the sum of the masses of particles, the energy of their motion and the energy of photons. Thermal energy increases all the time (according to the second law of thermodynamics). This means that M will also increase, and hence the proportional value $l_{\max}$, which characterizes the amplitude of the pulsations of the Universe. Thus, it is not the damping of the pulsations that takes place, but their buildup! This conclusion was reached

in 1934 by the American physicist P. Tolman. Where does the energy for pumping come from? Obviously, the buildup occurs due to the negative energy of the gravitational field (so the sum of positive and negative energy does not change, the law of conservation of energy is fulfilled).

Is it possible that our real Universe describes the model considered? This is unlikely to be the case. The fact is that although no semblance of the old picture of “heat death” exists, during the pulsations heat and entropy accumulate more and more. If there were an infinite number of pulsations, then the entropy would accumulate infinitely. But this is not there. This means that there was not an infinite number of pulsations. But then the main attractive feature of the model disappears - its endless existence in the past. And we return again to the problem of the beginning. The reader can learn how this problem of modern science is solved from the already mentioned book “How did the Universe explode?”

Having dispelled one myth - the myth of the “thermal death of the Universe” - science has come to a new, no less mysterious problem - the problem of the beginning of the expansion of the Universe. The attention of specialist cosmologists is now focused on solving this problem. This is the eternal path of knowledge, an adventure into the secrets of nature, when something learned gives rise to even more profound questions.
