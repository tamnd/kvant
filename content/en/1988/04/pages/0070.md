6. Form a definition of parallelism between a line and a plane. Form a sign of parallelism between a line and a plane (without proof).

Physics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Electric current. Current strength. Electric target. Electrical resistivity. Ohm's law for a section of an electrical circuit. Parallel and series connection of conductors.
2. When an electric lamp is turned on, the magnitude of the current at the first moment differs from the magnitude of the current, which is established when the lamp begins to glow. How to explain this phenomenon?
3. The sound of a shot and a bullet simultaneously reach a height of $h=960$ m. The shot is fired vertically upward. What is the initial speed of the bullet? The average speed of sound in air is $v=330$ m/s.
4. An ice floe of constant thickness floats, protruding $h=3$ cm above the water level. Find the mass of the floe if the area of ​​its base is $S=250$ cm$^2$. The density of ice is $\rho_1=0,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, the density of water is $\rho_2=1,0 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.
5. An electron moves with speed $v$ in a uniform magnetic field with induction $B$ in a plane perpendicular to the magnetic induction line. Determine the radius of the electron trajectory. The ratio of the electron's charge to its mass $e/m$ is known.

Option 2
1. Electromagnetic waves and the speed of their propagation. Properties of electromagnetic waves. Electromagnetic wave energy. Invention of radio by A. S. Popov.
2. Is it possible to tension a rope of mass $m$ so that it does not sag?
3. At figure a graph of changes in the state of indel gas is given in coordinates $p, V$. Represent this process on graphs in coordinates $p, T$ and $V, T$. Indicate in which of the processes the gas gives off heat and in which it receives.
4. A stone with a mass of $m=0,5$ kg is thrown at a height of $h=30$ m with an initial speed of $v_0=25$ m/s. Before hitting the ground, the speed of the stone is $v=30$ m/s. Determine the work done by air resistance forces when the stone moves.
5. Two current sources with emf $\varepsilon_1=4$ V and $\varepsilon_2=6$ V and internal resistances $r_1=0,1$ Ohm and $r_2=0,4$ Ohm are connected in series. At what resistance of the external circuit will the potential difference between the terminals of one of the sources be equal to zero?
