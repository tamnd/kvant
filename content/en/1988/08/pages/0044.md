⟦folio 42⟧

F1112. In the circuit shown in figure, with switch K open, the capacitor is charged to a certain voltage $U_0$. The key is closed, and after some time the current in the circuit stops. What should the value of $U_0$ be so that the direction to the capacitor is set equal to $U_y=1$ V when the polarity of the plates has changed, if the emf of the battery in the circuit is $E=1,5$ V? Diodes are considered ideal.

As can be seen from figure, current can flow through diode $D_1$ only from left to right, and through diode $D_2$ - only from right to left. Thus, this circuit is an oscillatory circuit containing a current source with a constant EMF, switched on every time opposite the current.

Suppose that at some instant of time, with switch K closed, there is no current in the circuit, the direction to the capacitor is $U_n$, and the charge is $q_n=CU_n$ (the upper plate of the capacitor is positively charged). During the next half of the period, the capacitor will be recharged - first discharged, then charged with charges of opposite signs. In this case, a current of constant direction will flow through the diode $D_1$, doing work against the side of the forces in the source. Through the polariod, the charge of the capacitor will become equal to $q_{n+1}$, so that charge $q_n+q_{n+1}$ will flow through the source (the sign of the charge of the plates changes).

According to the law of conservation of energy, the energy of the electric field of the capacitor is equal to the work against the side of the forces:

$$\frac{q_n^2}{2C} - \frac{q_{n+1}^2}{2C} = (q_n + q_{n+1}) \mathcal{E},$$

where

$$\frac{q_n}{2C} - \frac{q_{n+1}}{2C} = \mathcal{E},$$

or $U_n - U_{n+1} = 2\mathcal{E}$.

Thus, through the polariod, the direction to the capacitor will decrease by $2\mathcal{E}=3$ V. This will happen until the direction (at a current strength of zero) is less than $E=1,5$ V. Since, according to the conditions of the problem, the final direction is equal to 1 V when the polarity of the plates has changed, the initial direction to the capacitor (measured in volts) can be equal to

$$U_0=4+6n,$$ where $n=0,1,2,...

При получении этой серии решения предполагалось, что в каждую половину периода колебаний, включая последнюю, знак зарядов пластин изменяется. Однако возникен случай, когда в последнюю половину периода заряд изменяется от $q_{N-1}$ до $q_N$ без изменений знака. Тогда через источник протекает заряд $q_{N-1}-q_N$, и закон сохранения энергии записывается в виде:

$$\frac{q_n^2}{2C} - \frac{q_n^2}{2C} = (q_{N-1}-q_N) \mathcal{E}.$$

Отсюда получаем

$$U_{N-1}+U_N=2\mathcal{E}.$$

Таким образом, $U_{N-1}=2\mathcal{E}-U_N=2$ В (верхняя пластина конденсатора имеет отрицательный заряд), $U_{N-2}=U_{N-1}+2\mathcal{E}=5$ В (верхняя пластина конденсатора положительно) и т. д. Начальное направление в этом случае может быть равно

$$U_0=5+6n,$$ где $n=0,1,2,...

So, there are two series:

$$U_0=\begin{cases} 4+6n, & n=0,1,2,.. \\ 5+6n, & n=0,1,2,.. \end{cases}$$

A. I. Kirkinsky
