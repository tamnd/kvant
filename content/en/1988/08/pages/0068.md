Rubik's Snake can be found in almost any toy store. Less known is another “snake”, consisting of identical cups. Leningraders V. S. Genel and S. M. Genel managed to combine the advantages of both of these toys and give the “snake” new qualities through the use of two types of elements - regular terrahedra and regular four-sided pyramids. The new “snake” consists of 24 terrahedron-pyramid pairs; their connection diagram is shown in figure. The choice of named such elements is explained by several messages. In order for the “snake” to take on different shapes, the edges of neighboring elements - here these are regular triangles - must allow rotations; if, in addition, each element has several such boundaries, it becomes possible to transfer from one face of an adjacent element to another. And due to the fact that the terrahedron and “semi-octahedron”, others presented to the group, form an inclined triangular feature (check!) and such a feature can be seen without gaps and blocked space, the new “snake” fits together well.
