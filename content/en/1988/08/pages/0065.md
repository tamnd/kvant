How is the physical body organized? Physical examination is most often carried out according to the “Speaker - Opponent - Decider” scheme in 3 or 6 days. Each team takes on the role of speaker, opponent, or decider.
Rules for one action:
a) presentation by the speaker - 5 minutes;
b) controversy on the report, individual speeches in the controversy - 1 min;
b) opponent’s speech – 3 minutes;
d) speech by the solver - 2 minutes;

e) final debate, individual speeches - 1 min;
e) words of the jury (summarizing) - 4 min.
Call for a report. The opponent calls the speaker for any task from those proposed for this physical person. The presenter can turn off the call (unnecessarily accept it, for example from current messages) - then a new call will be made. In total, a team can disconnect the call twice.
Team performances. The speaker (one or more team members) outlines the solution to the problem, focusing the attention of the audience on the main physical ideas and conclusions. In this case, it is advisable to use pre-prepared figure, posters, slides, photographs, and also demonstrate experiments if the task is experimental.
In the debate on the report, the solution presented by the speaker is discussed. In the order of performances, the opposing team has priority. The speaker answers questions from those attending.
In his speech, the opponent expresses critical comments on the report, reveals impurities and errors in understanding the problem and methods for solving it. The opponent’s speech should not coincide with the presentation of his own solution to the problem.
The reviewer evaluates the performances of the speaker and opponent.
In the final debate, the speeches of the opponent and the decider can be discussed. The opponent and the solver can, by default, present their solutions to the problem to the jury.
Limitations on the number of appearances. Each team member can act as a speaker, opponent or decision maker for no more than

twice. Additional speeches in the debate - without restrictions.

Physical judging. The physical leader monitors compliance with the regulations, gives the accountant the floor (or deprives him of the floor) and coordinates the actions of the teams.
The jury personally evaluates the speeches of the speaker, the opponent and the solver and all additional speeches, in the final speech it sums up the discussion of the problem, notes the strengths and weaknesses of the solution.
