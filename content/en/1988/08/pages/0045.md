⟦folio 43⟧

Quests

1. In this encrypted division example, all nine digits are distinct. Decipher it.

2. Is it possible to connect several ends of the segments shown in figure with segments so that one non-self-intersecting broken line is obtained?

3. Place the numbers from 1 to 8 in circles so that the numbers in the horizontal rows are squares, and the sum of the numbers located in the centrally symmetrical circles is the same.

4. Find the smallest natural number that ends in 56, is divisible by 56, and has a sum of digits equal to 56.

5. If the books that are in Professor Ivanov’s office are torn into separate pages (mentally, of course!!!), can these pages cover a football field? Godsky Park?

These tasks were suggested to us by A.V. Shvetsov, A.P. Savin, A.M. Domashenko, S.G. Guba, D.B. Fuks.
