Tasks

M1116—M1120, F1128—F1132

M1116. What is the maximum number of knots of checkered paper that can be contained in a rectangle with area a) 36; b) S whose sides follow the grid lines? (The nodes lying inside and on the border of the rectangle are counted. The area of ​​the cell is taken as 1.)

M1117. An arbitrary triangle is given. Prove that a) it is possible to construct three circles with centers at its vertices, touching each other in pairs (at points K, L, M - see Fig. 1);
b) if through the middle of each arc KL, LM, MK lying inside the triangle, draw a tangent to it, then four triangles are formed, the area of ​​one of which (the central one) is equal to the sum of the areas of the other three.

A. A. Gorbachev

M1118. a) Prove that the equation $(x+y+z)^2=x^2+y^2+z^2$ has an infinite number of integer solutions.
b) How many such solutions are there for which z=1988?

S. G. Mamikonyan

M1119. Let us define a $k$-star as a figure on the plane, consisting of $k$ rays with a common origin, dividing the plane into $k$ equal angles (along $360^\circ/k$). For every $k>2$ the following statement is true: for any $k$ points of the plane in general position (three of which do not lie on the same line) there exists a $k$-star, each of the $k$ angles of which contains exactly one of these $k$ points?

M. Khovanov, 10th grade student, Moscow

M1120. a) The sequence $a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots$ is given by the relations $a_0=0, a_n=P(a_{n-1}), n=1, 2, \dots$, where $P(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients, $P(x)>0$ for $x\geq 0$. Prove that for any natural numbers $m$ and $k$

$$\text{НОД}(a_m, a_k)=a_{\text{НОД}(m, k)}$$

b) Prove a similar statement for the Fibonacci sequence $a_0=0, a_1=1, a_2=1, a_3=2, \dots$ given by condition $a_{n+1}=a_n+a_{n-1}, n=1, 2, \dots$.

V. F. Lev

F1128. A thin homogeneous hoop of mass $M$ and radius $R$ ends on a horizontal surface. The horizontal diameter of the hoop is a light, smooth tube in which a ball of mass $m$ is placed, attached to the hoop by two springs with a stiffness of $k$ each (Fig. 2). Keeping the hoop motionless, the ball was deflected to the left by an amount of $x$, after which the system was imagined. Find the acceleration of the center of the hoop at the initial moment of time. There is no slipping of the hoop.

S. S. Krotov
