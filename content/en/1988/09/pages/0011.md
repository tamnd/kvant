FLIGHT OF A BIRD
AND THE FLIGHT OF MAN

A. A. BORIN

The example of birds, flying everywhere, always beckoned a person to take him into the air.
O. K. Antonov

A dispute that lasted centuries

The dream of flight—of flapping flight—is as old, if not as old as the world, then, in any case, as old as the history of developed human consciousness. The ancients, having before their eyes the example of birds, could not imagine any other way of flight. Such is the legend of Icarus.
This is also the first attempt preserved in history to comprehend flight from the point of view of the scientific knowledge of that time. Leonardo da Vinci did it. It took hundreds of more years of observations and experiments to come to that very important idea for us that it is possible to fly on a fixed wing. Now we have gone so far along this path that we have left behind the feathered flyers in almost all respects, although not a single flying machine “controls its body” perfectly, like a bird.
