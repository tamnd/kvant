F1134. Exploring the newly discovered planet, which has the shape of a ball with a radius of $R=6400$ km and is covered over its entire surface by an ocean with a depth of $H=10$ km of physical water, scientists have established that the acceleration of gravity remains unchanged with a high degree of accuracy when immersed in the ocean to different depths. Using these data, determine the acceleration of free fall on the planet. Gravitational constant $G=6,67 \cdot 10^{-11} \text{H} \text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.

I. I. Vorobiev

F1135. A horizontal plane with a coin lying on it makes a circular translational motion in the horizontal plane so that all its points describe circles of radius $R$ with angular velocity $\omega$. Friction coefficient between coin and flat $\mu$. What will be the steady-state movement of the coin? What trace does she “draw” on the plane?

E. I. Butikov

F1136. In the circuit shown in figure 2, the amperators show points 0.2 A and 0.3 A. After the two resistors in the circuit were swapped, the ampere readings did not change. Which one runs through the battery? Assume the battery voltage remains constant. The resistance of amperators is negligible.

A. P. Zilberman

F1137. The electrostatic generator (Fig. 3) consists of a non-conducting cylinder on which foil strips $\Phi_1$ and $\Phi_2$ are glued, outer plates $O_1$ and $O_2$, fixed current collectors $T_1$ and $T_2$ on the outside and a fixed jumper inside with current collectors $T_3$ and $T_4$. In the initial position, the foil strips are located along the plates and form capacitors with a capacity of $C_1$ each. A capacitor with a capacity $C_0$, pre-charged to a voltage $U_0$, is connected to the outer plates. What is the voltage across this capacitor after $N$ revolutions of the cylinder clockwise? The capacity between the plates and the foil strips in the expanded state is negligible.
