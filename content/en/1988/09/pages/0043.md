⟦folio 41⟧

It is generally accepted that the next pair of other numbers, 17,296 and 18,416, were discovered in 1636 by Fermat (1601–1665). But recently in one of the treatises of the Moroccan scientist Ibn al-Wanna (1256-1321) the following lines were found: “The numbers 17,296 and 18,416 are other numbers.” Allah is the Knower."

Long before Ibn al-Wanna, another Arab mathematician Ibn Qurra (836-901) formulated the following successive other mathematical values ​​of certain other numbers: if three numbers $p=3, 2^{n-1}, q=3, 2^{n-1}$ and $r=9, 2^{2n-1}-1$ are prime, then the numbers $A=2^n pq$ and $B=2^n r$ will be other distinct ones.

Many authors after Ibn Qurra studied other mathematical numbers, but did not discover anything significant. In their writings there is also a solution: “To achieve reciprocity in love, you need to write the numbers 220 and 284 on something, give less to the object of love, and eat more yourself.”
