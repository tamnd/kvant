7. From the condition we have $x_1 - x_2 = \frac{x_2 - x_3}{x_2 x_3}$, $x_2 - x_3 = \frac{x_3 - x_4}{x_3 x_4}$, ..., $x_n - x_1 = \frac{x_1 - x_2}{x_1 x_2}$. If $x_1 = x_2$, then $x_2 = x_3 = ... = x_n$. If $x_1 \neq x_2$, then $x_2 \neq x_3$, ..., $x_n \neq x_1$. In this case, the variables are all direct and multiple on $(x_1 - x_2) \times (x_2 - x_3) \dots (x_n - x_1)$, we get $(x_1 x_2 \dots x_n)^2 = 1$.

8. Let $K$, $L$ be the points of intersection of lines $NA$ and $NB$ with line $CD$ (Fig. 6). It is clear that $\frac{KD}{AF} = \frac{DC}{FE} = \frac{CL}{EB}$. From the similarity of triangles $AKD$ and $CLB$ it follows that $\frac{KD}{BC} = \frac{AD}{CL}$. Therefore $2KD \cdot CL = 2BC \cdot AD = CD^2$, which means $2AF \cdot EB = EF^2$. Next, $AE + FB = AB + EF$. Therefore, $AE^2 + FB^2 + 2AE \cdot FB = AB^2 + EF^2 + 2AB \cdot EF = AB^2 + 2(AF \cdot EB + AB \cdot EF)$. It remains to prove that $AE \cdot FB = AF \cdot EB + AB \cdot EF$. But this equality follows from the point $(AF + FE)(FE + EB) = AF \cdot EB + (AF + FE + EB)FE = AF \cdot EB + AB \cdot EF$.

10. Firstly, among the selected subsets there cannot be an empty one. Secondly, if the set $A$ does not belong to the chosen population, then the complement $A$ does. Indeed, $A$ and the complement $A$ cannot both belong to the selected population since they do not intersect. But all subsets $2^n$ and exactly half of them belong to the selected population. This means that for each set $A$ either $A$ itself or the complement $A$ belongs to the chosen population. Finally, thirdly, if $A$ and $B$ belong to the selected population, then the intersection $A \cap B$ belongs to it. Indeed, otherwise the complement $C$ of $A \cap B$ would belong to our set, but the intersection of the set $A, B, C$ is empty. This means that the intersection of all sets belongs to our collection, and this intersection is not empty, since an empty set does not belong to the collection.
