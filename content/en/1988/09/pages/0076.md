Weighty correspondence physico-mathematical Optimkadma MBTU named after. N. E. Buumana

Moscow Higher Technical School named after. N. E. Buumana conducts in the 1988/89 academic year a weighty correspondence physico-mathematical optimization course (with a technical application) for students in secondary schools, vocational schools and technical schools.

The Olympics are held in two rounds. The conditions for problems of the first type are published below. The conditions of problems of the second type will be sent to optimization participants admitted to the second trial based on the results of the first.

Solutions to problems must be written in one school terradi. On the outside cover of the terradi, paste a piece of paper indicating your detailed home address (with postal code), last name, first name, middle name and place of study. Paste a certificate from your place of study indicating your class on the inside cover.

Send the notebook with solutions in a large envelope with a simple parcel post to the address: 107005 Moscow, 2nd Bumanskaya st., 5, MBTU named after. N. E. Buumana, “Olympida - 88/89”.

Problems of the first type

1. A homogeneous beam (length L=6.0 m) of one part (l=1.0 m) lies on a horizontal platform, the rest of the beam hangs from the platform (Fig. 1). A vertical force F is applied to the end of the overhanging part of the beam. The beam is held in a horizontal position if the value of the force is from a certain minimum value F_min to a maximum F_max. Find the ratio F_max/F_min, unless the beams are significantly shorter in length.

2. Two identical point weights C and D of mass m each are connected by an inextricable thread of length l and can slide without friction along a smooth weightless horizontal rod (Fig. 2). At the initial moment of time, when the load C is on the vertical axis OZ, the system was informed of the angular velocity ω₀. Find the tension in the thread at the moment when the load C moves away from the axis to a distance of 2l.
