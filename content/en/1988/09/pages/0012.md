Safety issues aside, flapping flight still attracts a huge number of enthusiasts today. Its apparent ease encourages amateurs to build a model and submit ingenious projects, in particular the confidence that they know the secret that finally allows a person to make flapping flight.

No less impressive is the fact that in terms of load per unit of power (see note 1), birds are many times greater than airplanes. But, let’s not forget, “other things being equal”! What is their equality?
