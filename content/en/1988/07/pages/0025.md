⟦folio 23⟧

The greatest mathematicians in human history. Much attention is paid to historical events, against the backdrop of which the difficult life of the scientist took place.

9. Weil G. Mathematical thinking: Transl. from English and German 2 r.

The collection includes works by the outstanding mathematician of our time Hermann Weyl (1885-1955), devoted to value-theoretic problems of mathematics, its interaction with the sciences of nature, the role in the study of the external world and the work of replacement scientists D. Hilbert, F. Klein, E. Noether, A. Poincaré and W. Pauli.

18. Klein F. Lectures on the development of mathematics in the 19th century. In 2 volumes. T. I: Per. with him. 2 r. 50 k.

A new translation of the first volume of the classic work on the history of mathematics of the nineteenth century, written by the outstanding German mathematician, teacher and leader of mathematical education F. Klein (1849-1925).

19. Klein F. Lectures on inklocation and solving fifth degree equations: Transl. with him. 1 rub. 90 k.

It is dedicated to one of the brightest branches of mathematics - the geometric theory of inklocation and is unique in the breadth of material covered and the skill of its presentation. The second part outlines the basic geometric properties of inklocation theory of fifth-degree equations. It is published in Russian for the first time.

36. Gashkov S. B., Oleksnik S. N., Sergeev I. N. Let’s apply mathematics. 60 k.

They consider the issues of constructing and measuring limited means, finding the optimal solution in a given situation, methods of quick calculation, problems of cutting, pouring, weighing, etc.

37. Kostovsky A. N. Geometric constructions with one compass on the plane and only one spherograph in space. 30 k.

The book is written on the basis of lectures that the author read over a number of years to schoolchildren who took part in the description of the city of Lvov.

38. Prasolov V.V., Sharygin I.F. Problems in theorem. 80 k.

Contains about 600 problems, first with detailed solutions, and 70 problems for independent work. Most of the tasks on fresh topics are close to the school curriculum.

40. Math problems. Start of analysis. 1 rub. 70 k.

The methodological construction of the reference book allows you to repeat this section of mathematics in depth and independently prepare for building a university with an advanced mathematical framework. The book was created based on the experience of teaching mathematics at the preparatory department of Moscow State University (Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics).

42. Tsyshkin A. G., Pinsky A. I. Handbook on methods for solving mathematics problems for secondary school. 1 rub. 90 k.

Contains basic methods for solving problems in a school mathematics course. The presentation of methods is accompanied by an analysis of typical problems.

56. Arnold V. I. Huygens and Barruu, Newton and

Hooke - the first steps of mathematical analysis and catastrophe theory from evolvents to quasicrystals. 80 k.

The book, written on the basis of a lecture for students, tells the story of the emergence of modern mathematics and theoretical physics in the works of the great scientists of the 17th century.

57. Nikolsky S. M. Elements of mathematical analysis. 40 k.

Mathematical analysis in the first edition of the book is based on the works of the author, who is a graduate student in the field of mathematics and a professor of mathematics. The book is written in Russian, and the text is in English.
