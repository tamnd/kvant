⟦folio 45⟧

Quests

1. Check the validity of the tejdestivities in the numerical paramid shown at figure, and try to prove the general statement:
$$1+2+\dots+(k-1)+k+(k-1)+\dots+2+1=k^2.$$
The clue is next to the paramid.

2. Four friends came to the skating rink, each with her brother. They split into pairs and started skating. It turned out that in every pair the “gentleman” is higher than the “lady” and no one skates with his sister. The tallest of the company is Yura Vorobyov, the next tallest is Andrei Egorov, then Lyusya Egorova, Seryozha Petrov, Olya Petrova, Dima Krymov, Inna Krymov and Anya Vorobyova.
Who rode with whom?

3. A glass was lowered into a large saucepan filled with hot water. After some time, air bubbles began to come out of the glass. Why?

4. Show that the equation
$$x^5y=xy^5+1987$$
has no solutions in integers.

5. A rectangular sheet of paper was cut into three triangular pieces. The area of ​​one of them turned out to be equal to half the sum of the areas of the other two pieces. What is the area of ​​the resulting pieces?

These tasks were suggested to us by N. I. Avilov, M. V. Ulanovsky, A. G. Samosvat, A. P. Tonkikh, V. V. Proizvolov.
