Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol operates a correspondence school (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 in general education schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students’ interest in the natural sciences.

There are 5 departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic. On mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic integral sciences.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also include mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives that work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. Club leaders recruit and enroll students who are interested in 3SH, and circle leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional materials. The work of individual students is carried out in 3SH, and the work of circles is carried out by its leaders (optional)
