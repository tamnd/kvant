$$[x+y]=[x-y].$$

First physics assignment
To build a 3Sh physical department, it may be sufficient to solve one or two problems. However, after completing the problems of your class, it is useful (and we recommend that you) familiarize yourself with the problems for other classes, and try to solve the problems you like (the more, the better). When you build in 3SH, you will be sent solutions to all problems. Get to know them, even if you've solved them all.

An experimental task designed for students of all grades.
1. Pour some water at room temperature into a saucer and carefully place a thin-walled glass, rinsed on the outside with very hot water, upside down. After the glass has cooled, some (or all of the water) from the saucer will be drawn into it. Measure the relative pressure difference inside the glass $\Delta p/p_0=(p_a-p)/p_a$, where $p_a$ is the atmospheric air pressure in the room. Make changes several times, consider accepting the possibility of slight discrepancies in measurement results.

3. Body weight in water is two times less than in oil, and three times less than in air. Determine the density of the oil.

4. From the floating platform, a batal of volume $V=4\ m^3$ is lifted on a tred. The horizontal cross-sectional area of ​​the platform at the water surface level is $S=100\ m^2$. How far will the platform sink when the bath is completely out of the water?

5. One of the four power supplies connected as shown in figure 1 burns out. How many times will the power of each casting change after this?

6. In the scales for changing the mass of bulk solids, a cup and a counterweight are closed at the ends of a light ruler 2L long; their masses are the same and add up to $m_0$. The rounded force of the support can move relative to the ruler until the weight with the load is in equilibrium with the ruler in a horizontal position. Find the mass of the load if the center of the support moves at a distance $l$ from the center of the ruler.
