First math assignment

8th grade

1. The tourist rode a bicycle 80% of the way, walked 40% of the remaining route and rowed 12 km on a raft. Find the entire tourist path.

2. Draw a graph of the function
$$y = |x - 1| + |x + 1|$$

3. Prove that a nine-digit number, all of whose digits are the same, is divisible by 37.

4. The heights of the triangle are 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. Which triangle is it: acute, rectangular or obtuse?

5. Find the sum of all three digit numbers in which all digits are odd.

6. How to use a compass and ruler to construct a triangle using two sides and a height lowered to the third side? How many different triangles can you get?

9th grade

1. The train covers the most length of 800 meters in 1 minute, and the mismo is observed in 15 seconds. What is the length of the train and its speed?

2. On the coordinate plane, draw a set of points (x, y) whose coordinates satisfy the conditions
$$y^2 + y \geq x + |x| - 1.$$

3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x + y + xy = 11, \\ x^2 + y^2 + xy = 19. \end{cases}$$

4. In triangle ABC, the medians intersect at point M. Express the vector $\vec{AM}$ in terms of the vectors $\vec{AB}$ and $\vec{AC}$.

5. Prove that a twelve-digit number, all of whose digits are the same, is divisible by 13.

6. There are two back points on the plane. How to use a compass and a ruler to construct a square in which these points would be the midpoints of adjacent sides?
