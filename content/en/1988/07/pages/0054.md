⟦folio 52⟧

- Because you say incredible things. It can't be that simple.
Alma was taken aback:
— WHERO can’t be?
- The heels are captured, having a common border. This contradicts the meaning of the four color problem.
- Contradicts what?
“The problem of four colors,” I repeated. “There is such a problem in topology.” Although it has not been proven or disproved in any way, no one doubts that it is true.
I began to draw on the tablecloth with the end of a spoon, trying to explain to Alma what was going on here.
Alma quickly grasped the general idea.
— Maybe the islanders are captivated by a different kind of mathematics? - she suggested, rustling from the smoke of the sigret.
I shook my head.
— Mathematics, my dear, is the same for all cultures. Twice two is always four, even in Africa. If your island is really divided as you say - into five regions, each of which has a common border with four other regions - then I will teach you to believe in the mathematical abilities of your islanders. Do you have a map of the island?
Alma shook her head.
- Maybe you should come with us? — she suggested, insuring the ashes. “I’ll stay on the island of meat.” I need to check some data before I publish it, and in the meantime you can make a map of the island. If what I told you is not confirmed, I will reimburse your expenses.
What did I have to lose? The summer holidays were beginning, and this trip promised to be pleasant and unusual. And I agreed.

* * *

On the second day of our stay on the island, Alma introduced me to one of the islanders named Aguz. Aguz was a captive of the Khiyniku, who made up the intellectual elite of the island. Alma agreed with Aguz to make a second hiking trip across the island. Fortunately, the island was small: its area did not exceed 25 square miles. Having set off early in the morning, we could have covered the entire island by evening. I took a notepad and a box of pencils with me to sketch out at least a rough map of the heel of the area.

Our first visit was to a captive village, on whose territory our camp was located.

On the top piece of my notebook I drew a circle and colored it in blue. The exact configuration of the territory occupied by the Hiinku was unknown, but for my purposes, to take advantage of the territory occupied by the Wolfesi captivity, I placed a squiggle next to the blue circle and painted it green.

With difficulty making our way through the thickets of some bushes, we came to the bank of a narrow, quiet river. A small raft of logs barely protruded from the light, greasy mud that covered the river. Aguz pushed the raft into the water, climbed onto it and began to push it with a long bamboo pole. We crossed what seemed like a narrow shelf of muddy mud near the shore and squatted down to it. Using his pole, Aguz directed the raft down the winding river.

After some time, Aguz announced that we would meet Gesells in the territory of captivity. She was disposed to svegur from wolfesi.
