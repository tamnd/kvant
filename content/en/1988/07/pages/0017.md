⟦folio 15⟧

SPACE ILLUSIONS
AND MIRAGES
Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. D. CHERNIN

Cosmic sources of study, called quasars, were discovered a quarter of a century ago, in 1963. But their nature still remains not fully understood. There is no end in sight to the surprises they present to physicists and astronomers. Amazing phenomena have recently been discovered that have never before been observed in the sky: doubling of images and movement at superluminal speeds.

In this article we will not touch upon the history of the discovery of quasars, hypotheses and theories put forward to explain their properties.*) We will provide only the most general information about quasars, and then discuss in detail what mirages and illusions are in the world of quasars and how they should be understood.

*) You can read about this in A.D. Chernin’s book “Stars and Physics”, published in 1984 by the Nauka publishing house in the “Viblotechka «Kvant»” series (issue 38). (Editor's note)
