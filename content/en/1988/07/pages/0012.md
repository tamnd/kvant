FLEXAGONS, FLEXORS, FLEXMANS

A. A. PANOV

We invite you to a short excursion into the mysterious world of flexagons and flexes - paper toys with the amazing ability to suddenly change their shape and color. You can put together any of these toys in the shortest possible time.

But first of all, about the title of the article. English to flex familiar to fold, bend, bend.

Now prepare a sheet of paper or thick landscape paper, pencils, scissors, glue - and start dividing.

Hexaflexaon

Flexaon is a polygon glued together from paper, which, by bending and folding, can transform into more and more new states.

Hexaflexaon is a flexaon shaped like a regular hexagon. Let's cut out a strip of paper 2.5 cm wide, consisting of ten regular triangles, and cut out both sides of it - straight and reverse, as in figure 1.

We fold the strip several times along the boundaries of the triangles and continue making the flexaon, folding the strip as in figure 2.

Now all that remains is to bend the wizz blue triangle, glue two unpainted triangular surfaces together - and the flexaon is ready. However, his one is red, the other is blue. To bring to light its third, green surface, you need to act as follows. First, the flexaon is placed on the plane of the table so that it rests on the three lower points. These points need to be pressed closer to each other, and at the moment they are completely pressed together, the flexaon itself will turn inside out, exposing its green surface. Now all that remains is to move the upper points of the flexaon to the sides, and the flexaon is ready for a new transformation. Thus, sequentially moving from one state to another, the flexaon will alternately present to us all its three surfaces. Figure 3 gives some insight into the manufacture of flexaon.

The model we made is the simplest of the extensive family of hexaflexaons, described in Martin Gardner’s book “Mathematical Puzzles and Entertainment” (M., Mir, 1971). This interesting book outlines the history of the discovery of hexaflexaons and describes in detail how to build models with a large number of surfaces.

Double swivel joint

The double joint is a design element that serves as the basis for another range of flexo models. Let's cut out of paper two rectangles measuring 2.5 × 5 cm and two strips measuring 1 × 7 cm.
