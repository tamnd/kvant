Our cover

1 Can any map on a plane be correctly colored with four colors - as was done, for example, for the island depicted on our cover? (“Correct” means that no two neighboring countries are the same color.)
In 1976, Appel and Haken proved a theorem that gives an affirmative answer to this question.
But in M. Garder’s article “The Island of the Heel of Colors” you will come across a fantastic story that “refutes” this important theorem.

2 It’s hard to believe, but 250 years ago Newton’s theory of gravity caused mistrust, controversy and even ridicule. Evidence of this is the caricature that appeared during Newton’s lifetime.
And in our time, phenomena of a truly cosmic scale were discovered, associated with the law of universal gravitation. Read about this in the article by A. D. Chernin.
