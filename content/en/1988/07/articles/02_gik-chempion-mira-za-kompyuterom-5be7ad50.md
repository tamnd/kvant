WORLD CHAMPION
AT THE COMPUTER

Several years ago, in the computer system of the USSR Academy of Sciences, two young employees A. Dudoladov and G. Senin developed on a computer an information retrieval system (IRS), which has many valuable qualities: compact storage of party texts, a convenient way to enter, accumulate and retrieve them for various requests, fast printing, the ability to analyze a party with immediate return to the desired position, etc. Unfortunately, the work did not find sufficient support. Soon, a more powerful Chess Base information retrieval system was created in Germany, implemented on an Atari personal computer. This system can be created with a chess microcomputer, which allows you to enter the texts of the game either from an Atari keyboard or by playing them on the board.

The issues of support, updating and maintenance of Chess Base are resolved wittily. Once every two months, a “magazine”-floppy disk is published with improvements to the program, instructions for their inclusion in the system, and texts of a batch of the most important conditions. Up to 6 thousand games can be placed on one floppy disk, but 2-3 thousand are recorded in Chess Base - space is left for competencies, variations, etc.

Last year, the creator of Chess Base, M. Wulenweber, came to Moscow and demonstrated his game at the Central Chess Club. “Can the system be used to release information watchers?” - ask him at the meeting. “No problem,” the author of the system smiled, “let’s take the game, which is all wrinkled.” A recording of the 24th, decisive match between Kasparov and Karpov in Seville appeared on the screen in full. “Do you want to receive the text in English or German, or in the form of pictograms?” Those who were approaching decided to use the figures; After pressing a pair of keys, images of figures appeared on the display along with the letters.

“Which srinira text should I choose? - and he limited the table with the help of a device called a “mouse”. As a result, the recording automatically switched to the desired screenshot.

“Here,” Wulenweber pointed with an arrow with the mouse, “you need to add a comment. We make a gap in the entry and fill it with the necessary comments. And in this place,” the arrow faded again, “we will mark the diagram...” The recording was torn again, and in front of the assembled appearance a diagram appeared with the position after the corresponding move. As a result, a page of chess text was typed, and twenty seconds later it appeared in printed form. If we are talking about a Turkish press bulletin, then you can make the required number of copies from it.

World champion G. Kasparov talks about this high-response system. Preparation for tournaments and matches, in his opinion, with the help of Chess Base takes much less time and can be carried out more deeply and thoroughly. Kasparov himself used this system several times.

In 1986, in Hamburg, the world champion gave a session of simultaneous play with chaas in everything by the strongest German master and the program was 3.5: 4.5. The next year he decided to take the decision from the same “team”. But first he asked that batches of contents for the last two years be prepared for him. Kasparov asked for about three hours for “Atari”, studying with the help of “Chess Base” the uniqueness of the partners, their inclinations and style of play. The rematch brought victory to the world champion with a score of 7:1. “Each of the contents is now stored as its own articles.”

many acquaintances, knew their strengths and weaknesses,” this is how Kasparov commented on his success.

It is curious that a month later Kasparov gave a similar session to the Swiss national team in Basel. So far, he has spent about 6 hours on the computer and quickly reviewed 700 games of future opponents. The session ended with a score of 5.5:0.5 in his crawl. And once again the world champion attributed his stellar result to excellent preparation. “This is a revolution in the game of chess!” said Kasparov when he appreciated all the advantages of Chess Base.

Note that the world champion is generally a personal computer enthusiast. Not listening, he is the president of the first computer club in Moscow on Rozhdestvensky Boulevard. And most recently, Kasparov held a unique simultaneous game using communication satellites and kits. The sessioner itself was located in France, in Cannes, and its ten contents were in different countries, even on different materials. In Moscow, in a computer club, a Soviet participant, the national champion among youth, M. Ulybbin, immediately had a session with the world champion. He made the only draw in this international performance. Kasparov lost one game, the overall result of the session was 8.5:1.5 in his crawl. A brilliant result, considering that all participants in the session are masters, and two are even international.
