Before you is a ring consisting of ten regular tetrahedra. It is glued together from thick paper and can bend and twist indefinitely, all the time changing its shape. Here are depicted four successive phases of his healing. Please note that each pair of neighboring tetrahedra has a common edge, and when the chain is twisted, the tetrahedra oscillate around this edge. And the fifth LOB star, which periodically appears inside the chain mail, turns out to be rotated by 36° with each subsequent appearance. You can read about how to make such a colcho in the article “Flexaons, flexors, flexms.” The pattern of colchua is represented there at figure 15. The recommended size of the edges of tetrahedra is 3.5 cm. Take your time, carefully mark the pattern and paint it in two of your favorite colors. After that, cut it out and, following the instructions given in the article, carefully glue it together. Having done all this, you will become the owner of a toy that, when placed in your hands, instantly comes to life.
