Approaches to learning vary. In one of the courses, the principles of programming were examined using the example of a ready-made program playing “tic-tac-toe.”

In another course, on the contrary, they began studying by drawing up simple programs, drawings, and then began to use these programs, introducing the concepts of procedures, variables, cycles, transition conditions, and so on. Theoretical classes alternated with practical work on a computer.

We did not notice that computers were widely used in lessons in other subjects. They are mainly used when working with texts: typing and editing. The structure of the physics course at Phillips Academy is close to being adopted in our country, studying starting from mechanics and building up to the basics of atomic physics in three terms. There are two levels of study: one for those who wish to gain a general introduction to physics, and the second for those who wish to gain in-depth knowledge. With an outwardly identical structure, these levels differ in the use of mathematical apparatus, and therefore, to choose a deep physics course, you must first study, at a minimum, Calculus.
