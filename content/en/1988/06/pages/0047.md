⟦folio 45⟧

Quests

1. Place the numbers from 1 to 8 in the circles of the figure shown at figure, so that the sum of the three numbers at the vertices of each green triangle is 13, and at the vertices of each brown triangle is 14.

2. Solve the number puzzle shown at figure. Same numbers correspond to the same letters, different numbers correspond to different letters.

3. In the chess tournament, Zhenya and Sasha played the same number of games, fell ill and dropped out of the tournament. The remaining sections of tourism must be completed. All 23 games were played in the tournament. Did Zhenya and Sasha play tournaments with each other?

4. The computer printed two numbers: $2^{1987}$ and $5^{1987}$. How many numbers did she type?

5. All the sides and bottom of the cardboard box (without the lid) are squares of area 1. Cut the box into three pieces so that they can be folded into a square of area 5.

These tasks were offered to us by: N.I. Abilov, 6th grade student from Leningrad N. Yakovleva, 10th grade student from Gaagrin V. Bliznekov, 10th grade student from Kharkov V. Pushchiya, V.V. Proizvolov.
