3 (4). In A. S. Griboyedov’s comedy “Woe from Wit” there are the lines:

I am building the bzantrell to this gentlemen
Drive up to the capitals for the shot.
Express this distance in meters for the case when the shot is fired from a cannon at an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon, and the top point of the projectile’s flight path is at a height of $H=3125$ m. Neglect air resistance, take the free fall acceleration equal to $g=10$ m/s².

4 (5). The kinematic equations of body motion are given: $x=R \sin \omega t$, $y=R \cos \omega t$. Find its trajectory and acceleration (modulus and direction). What is the meaning of the built-in $R$ and $\omega$?

5 (6). A car with a mass of $m=16$ t, moving by inertia at a speed of $v=36$ km/h, stops after traveling a distance of $l=0,5$ km. Calculate the friction force.

6 (10). A car with a mass of $m=2 \cdot 10^3$ kg, moving from rest along a horizontal path, reaches a speed of $v=20$ m/s after $t=10$ s from the start of movement. Friction coefficient $\mu=0,1$. Determine the force of the automatic propulsion system. Present the result in kilonewtons (kN). Take $g=10$ m/s².

7 (5). Find the friction force if the friction coefficient is $\mu=0,5$, the body mass is $m=0,6$ kg, and the force applied at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon is $F=2$ N (Fig. 1).
