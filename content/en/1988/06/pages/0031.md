M1088. Prove that if the numbers p, q, r are rational and pq+qr+pr=1, then (1+p²)(1+q²)(1+r²) is the square of a rational number.

M1089. In a convex quadrilateral ABCD of area S, the diagonals intersect at point O. Let K, L, M, N be the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles AOB, BOC, COD and DOA. Prove that the product of the perimeter of quadrilaterals ABCD and KLMN is not less than 4S.

Let k, l, m, n be the radii of circles inscribed in triangles AOB, BOC, COD, DOA. Then perimeter KLMN is not less than 2(k+l+m+n) (KL≥k+l, LM≥l+m, etc.; Fig. 1). But k=2S_{AOB}/P_{AOB}, where the letters S and P denote area and perimeter, and P_{AOB}≤P_{ABCD}. The quantities l, m, n are estimated similarly. Hence,

$$P_{KLMN} \geq 4 \frac{S_{AOB}+S_{BOC}+S_{COD}+S_{DOA}}{P_{ABCD}} = \frac{4S}{P_{ABCD}},$$

which is what was required.
