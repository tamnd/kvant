Mathematics

Algebra

1(2). Find a two-digit number, knowing that the number of its tens is 4 greater than the number of its units, and the product of this number by the resulting number of its digits is 153.

2(11). MotoshikLIST went from point A to point B, located 120 km from A. He drove back at the same speed, but an hour after leaving he had to leave for 10 minutes. After this stop, he continued to A, increasing his speed by 6 km/h. What was the initial speed of motoshikLIST, if it is known that he spent the same amount of time on the way back as on the way from A to B?

3(7). Along a circle whose length is 60 m, two points move uniformly and in the same direction. One makes a full revolution 5 seconds faster than the other and at the same time catches up with the second point every minute. Find the speeds of the points.

4(7). Find the speed and length of the train if it passed past a stationary observer at a constant speed for 7 s and took 25 s to travel at the same speed along a platform 378 m long.

5(7). Two pieces of brass have (in total) a mass of 30 kg. The first piece contains 5 kg of pure copper, and the second piece contains 4 kg. What percentage of copper does the first piece of brass contain if the second one contains 15% more than the first?

6(6). To transport 60 tons of cargo, several trucks must be used. Due to the large ascent of the route, each truck had to be loaded with 0.5 tons less than expected, and therefore 4 more vehicles were required. How many trucks do you plan to use initially?

7(6). The numerator of a fraction is 2 greater than its value. If you add this fraction to its inverse, you get 74/35. Find the original fraction.

8(6). Find the sum of all two-digit numbers that are multiples of 5.
