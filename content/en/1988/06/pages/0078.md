Electrodynamics

1 (3). At the vertices of the square on the side $a$ there are four charges: two of them are equal to $+q$ and two to $-q$. Determine the direction of the electric field at the point of intersection of the diagonal square. The distance varies from case to case.

2 (5). Two identical metal balls of radius $a$ each form a capacitor. The distance $r$ between the balls is so large that the distribution of charges on their surfaces can be assumed to be uniform. Calculate the capacitance of such a capacitor, distance limiting case $r \rightarrow \infty$.

3 (11). Charges $q_1=40$ and $q_2=-10$ ikl are located at a distance of $l=10$ cm from each other. What third charge should be taken and where should it be placed in order for the system to be in equilibrium? Will the equilibrium be stable or unstable?

4 (10). A ball having a mass of $m=0,4$ g and a charge of $q=4,9 \cdot 10^{-7}$ C is suspended on a thread in a uniform electric field, the forces of which are linear and horizontal. At what angle does the thread deviate from the vertical if the direction of the field is $E=8,0 \cdot 10^3$ V/m? Present the result in degrees. Accept $g=9,8$ m/c$^2$.

5 (6). Lead wires are connected to the wire ring. In what ratio do the connection points divide the circumference if the total resistance of the resulting circuit is 6.25 times less than the resistance of the ring?

6 (8). An amperor and a voltmeter are connected in series to a battery with an emf $\mathcal{E}=6$ V. When a resistor was connected in parallel with the voltmeter, the ampere reading doubled and the voltmeter reading was halved. Determine the voltmeter reading before connecting the resistor. The resistances of the resistor and devices, as well as the internal resistance of the source, are not known.

7 (10). With a current strength of $I_1=4$ A, a power of $P_1=18$ W is released in the external circuit of the battery, and with a current strength of $I_2=2$ A - respectively, $P_2=10$ W. Determine the emf of the battery. Present the result in SI units.

8 (1). When a charge $q=20$ C moves through a conductor with a resistance of $R=0,5$ Ohm, $A=100$ J of work is done. Find the time during which the current flowed through the conductor, considering it constant.

9 (11). A trolleybus with a mass of $m=11$ t moves uniformly at a speed of $v=36$ km/h. Find the current in the motor winding if the direction is $U=550$ V and the efficiency is $\eta=80\%$. Motion resistance coefficient $\mu=0,02$.

10 (4). The electric samovar heater consists of two elements. When the first element is connected to the network, the water in the samovar boils in $t_1=15$ minutes, when only the second element is connected - in $t_2=20$ minutes. How long will it take for water to boil in the samovar if both elements are connected to the network: a) in series, b) in parallel?

11(3). A battery having an EMF of $\mathcal{E}=60$ V and an internal resistance of $r=4$ Ohm is closed to an external circuit that consumes power of $P=200$ W. Determine the current strength in the circuit, the direction drop in the external circuit, the resistance of the external circuit.

12 (6). A coil with a diameter of $d=0,4$ m is in an alternating magnetic field. When the magnetic field induction changes by $\Delta B=127,4$ T for $\Delta t=2$ s, an induction emf of $\mathcal{E}=200$ V is excited in the coil winding. How many turns does the coil have?

13 (11). The speed of an aircraft with a jet engine is $v=950$ km/h. Find the induction emf arising at the ends of the wings of such an aircraft if the vertical component of the induction of the earth's magnetic field is $B=5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ T and the wingspan of the aircraft is $l=12,5$ m.
