Tasks

M1081 — M1085, F1093 — F1097

M1081. Prove that the penultimate digit of the number $3^n$ is even for any natural number $n > 2$.

V. I. Plachko

M1082. In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ the diagonals intersect at the point $O$. Prove that the equality
$$AB^2 + BC^2 + CD^2 + DA^2 = 2(AO^2 + BO^2 + CO^2 + DO^2)$$
is satisfied if and only if either the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpediculars, or one of them is bisected by the point $O$.

A. P. Savin

M1083. The largest non-negative number $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ is $a$.
a) Prove the inequality
$$\frac{a_1^2 + a_2^2 + \dots + a_n^2}{n} \leq \left(\frac{a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n}{n}\right)^2 + \frac{a^2}{4}.$$

b) When is equality achieved?

L. G. Khanin

M1084. Two circles on a plane intersect at points $A$ and $B$. Prove that it is possible to choose a point $C$ such that any circle with chord $AC$ will intersect the given circle (for the second time) at points equally distant from $C$ (and $C \neq B$).

V. Protasov, 10th grade student (Moscow)

M1085*. Several pairs of intersecting straight lines located in space are projected on a horizontal plane. Their projections are depicted so that at the points of intersection it is clear which point is located above and which below. can the projection shown on figure $1(a - \beta)$ be obtained?

S. L. Tabachnikov

F1093. A car with a mass of $m = 1200$ kg, the brake with the gear off, rolls down at a constant speed along an inclined section of the highway with an inclination angle of $\alpha$ ($\sin \alpha = \frac{1}{14}$). Each of the four wheels automatically with equal force $N$ brake pads $A$ and $A'$ (Fig. 2). Find $N$ if the sliding friction coefficient between the barbacon and the pads is $k = 0,4$. There is no slippage between the tires and the highway.

V. I. Chuvilev
