$$\frac{x}{\sin \alpha} = \frac{R}{\sin(180^\circ - \beta)},$$ from where $x = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin \beta} R$.

Standing on a horizontal surface, when the tumbler mass increases by the mass of plasticine $m$, the toy will lose stability (fall over) if the center of mass of the tumbler - plasticine system reaches point $O$, i.e., provided
$$Mx = m(h - R)$$.
Hence, taking into account the obtained expression for $x$, it is located on the axis $X$, directed vertically upward),
$$F = F_A - pS$$.
Here $F_A = \rho gV$ is the Archimedean (buoyant) force that would act on part of the support $ABC$ if this part was surrounded on all sides by water, $p$ is the pressure in liquids at a depth $h$, which is the sum of atmospheric pressure $p_0 \approx 10^5$ Pa and additional pressure $\rho gh$ from the water side: $p = p_0 + \rho gh$. Thus,
$$F = \rho g(V - hS) - p_0S \approx -9 \cdot 10^4 \text{ H}.$$
The minus sign of the projection of force on the axis $X$ means that the force acting from the water on the axis, acting from the water on the axis, is directed downward.
