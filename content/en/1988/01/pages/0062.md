A few words about the institute itself. The Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys trains engineering and scientific personnel for the metallurgical and machine-building industries and a number of new branches of science and technology. In many departments of the institute, training is structured in such a way that students will become capable of independent scientific work from the very first year.

By entering MISiS, you will be able to study theories and experiments in the field of solid state physics, study the properties of matter at low temperatures, study high-temperature processes in liquids and solids, create alloys with new properties and new semi-conductor devices.

8 (MIPT, 1986). Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{align*}
\sin x &= \sin 2y, \\
2 \sin (4x+8y) &= \times \sin (3x+10y) + 5\cos (7x+2y) = 4.
\end{align*}$$

9 (MIPT, 1982). Find all solutions to the system of equations
$$\begin{align*}
tg x \cdot \sin y + & + 2 \text{ctg } x \cdot \cos y = \sqrt{5/2}, \\
2 \text{ctg } x \cdot \sin y - & -tg x \cdot \cos y = \sqrt{5/2},
\end{align*}$$

satisfying conditions $\pi/2 < x < 3\pi/2$, $0 < y < \pi$.
