1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{x+9} - \sqrt{\frac{x^2+8x}{x+9}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{x+9}}.$$
2. Find all values ​​of $a$ for which the distance between the vertices of the parabolas $y=x^2+ax+\frac{2}{3}$ and $y=3x^2+5ax+\frac{19}{12}a^2$ is greater than $\frac{\sqrt{29}}{3}$.
3. A circle centered at the intersection of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of the isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ is tangent to the smaller base $BC$ and the side $AB$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid $ABCD$ if you know that its height is 16 and the radius of the circle is 3.
4. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\frac{4\cos 4\pi x}{1+3\mathrm{ctg}^2\pi x} = -1,$$

$$\log_7 \frac{11}{16} \left( \frac{11}{32} - \frac{13}{8} x - x^2 \right) < 0.$$
5. At the base of the paramid $SABCD$ lies the isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$, in which $AD=2$, $BC=1$, the height of the trapezoid is 3. The height of the paramid passes through the point $O$ of intersection of the diagonals of the trapezoid, $SO=\frac{4}{\sqrt{5}}$. Point $F$ lies on the segment $SO$, and $SF:FO=1:3$. A cylinder, the axis of which is parallel to the apothem of face $SAD$, is positioned so that point $F$ is the center of its upper base, and point $O$ lies on the circle of its lower base. Find the area of ​​the part of the upper base of the cylinder lying inside the paramid.

Find the volume of the paramid $SABC$ if it is known that the radius of the sphere is $\frac{m}{\sqrt{3}}$.
