If we consider estimates, then we would eventually find (even without calculation) the set $(2\pi)^{-5}$ - after all, its appearance is by no means an accident...

And here is another - the opposite - example. I.M. Lifshts and A.V. Pogorelov (you know A.V. Pogorelov as the author of a textbook and books on geometry) investigated the laws of fission of heavy nuclei. The analogy with drops of a common liquid and the messages of dimension helped to solve the problem relatively simply and obtain the answer accurate to a dimensionless factor. The authors wanted to think that the unknown dimensionless factor was close to unity (as far as I remember, I.M. Lifshts was sure of this). A.V. Pogorelov designed a special device that makes it possible to measure the value that was estimated theoretically using a “bulk” liquid. It turned out that the required factor was 1.1 (!).

What follows from these two examples? The only thing is that caution must be exercised when making estimates based on reported dimensions. Exercise caution, but never abandon methods based on these messages.

When we are talking about the area of ​​physics that deals with such or phenomena, for the understanding of which it is enough to use the known laws of nature (that is, they are described with the necessary accuracy by known equations), dimensional methods serve as guiding messages, support for intuition. With more or less difficulty, you can make the appropriate calculation and get an accurate answer. Messages of dimension acquire a special role when a physicist enters an unvalued area, when there are no structured equations to rely on, and one can only estimate, estimate and guess. This activity of theoretical physicists requires a special instinct, based on a deep knowledge of the entire structure of physics, an understanding of what can be

question (revise), and what is unshakable, the refusal of which destroys (literally) all the data of physics. Science is much more conservative than it seems to those who look at it from the outside, admire its successes, who think that science can do everything, that everything is accessible to it. Science describes the realmente existing World, governed by really existing laws. Science is gradually erasing these laws in painful pains. Constructing logical consistency of the picture of the World is a fundamental task that requires me to treat every detail of this picture with reverent caution, constantly asking myself the question of whether I have violated something in the whole picture if I assume something new, necessary (as it seems to me) to explain some fact?...

I realize that the last paragraph seems completely abstract. And yet I don’t want to give examples - mainly because I don’t feel like I am a specialist in the physics from which these examples should be drawn: from elementary particle physics, from cosmology. I just want to draw your attention to the following. The same dimension has completely different values: the distance between Moscow and New York and the size of a hydrogen atom, the time of Neptune’s revolution around the Sun and the period of vibration of atoms in a hydrogen molecule, the mass of a proton and the mass of an electron, etc., etc. - there are many examples. Dividing a dimensional value by a value of the same dimension, we get a dimensionless number. You can ask the question: why did this particular number come out and not some other? It is clear that since the number of relations is infinite, the number of questions is also infinite. Should I ask them all? And is it necessary to answer them? - Only experience gives the answer.
