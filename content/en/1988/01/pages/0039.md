⟦folio 37⟧

The above scene is very typical. And not only at school. From year to year, many applicants give the wrong answer to the question posed - the same answer that our student gave. Students also often make mistakes when taking a mechanics exam.

The persistence and prevalence of the error in question is explained not only by a poor knowledge of physics. There appears to be a principle of a psychological nature here. Firstly, at the level of everyday situations, we are accustomed to the fact that in order to maintain the movement of the body, it is necessary to act on it with some kind of force. At the same time, it is forgotten that force is needed only to compensate for friction. Secondly, we live in a fairly fast pace and often, oh so often, we want to get results immediately. At the same time, we simply do not want to take into account... inertia, inertia.

- In whatever direction the force acts at a given moment in time, in that direction the stone should fly at that moment. If the direction of the force changes, then the direction of movement must also change. If only the force of gravity acted on the stone, it should have flown down. But he doesn’t fly down, which means that in addition to the force of gravity, there is also force. “Perhaps something like these thoughts flash through our heads, and that same error arises.”

We love to criticize inertia; we equate it with laziness and unwillingness to act. However, it takes the point of view of physics and tries to imagine what a world would look like in which inertia suddenly disappeared. In order for a body to move in such a world, a force would have to be applied to it all the time. The impact stopped - the movement immediately stopped. The force is directed to the right - the body moves to the right. the direction of the force has changed to the opposite - and at the same moment the body is moving in the opposite direction. We can say that here everything is determined only by a given moment in time (only by those acting at a given moment), only siminite influences, siminite instructions are important. There is no “reminder” about the past; the past does not affect the present at all. And accordingly, the present does not affect the future at all. A world without inertia is a world where there are no cause-and-effect relationships.

But let's return to our stone. When they threw him, of course, some kind of force was exerted on him. Now he is in flight, but “remembers” the past: the nature of his movement (in particular, the height and range of flight) is determined not only by the force of gravity, but also by the force that acted on the stone. This is why the stone does not fly down, but follows a parabolic trajectory. Speaking in the language of science, the movement of a stone at a given moment is determined both by the force acting on it at this moment, and by the initial conditions - the direction and speed patterns that it had at the initial moment of flight. It is not for nothing that problems of this type usually begin with the words: “The body was thrown with such and such a speed at such and such an angle to the horizon...” These very initial conditions express the results of the action of forces in the past. The influence on the present passed through them.

Obviously, a change in the force applied to the body leads to a change in the movement of the body. It is important, however, that the change in the movement of the body takes into account how it moved in the same way. Force directly determines not the speed of a body, but the acceleration, i.e., the change in speed. It turns out that Newton’s famous second law - the same one where mass (a measure of the inertia of a body) appears for the first time in physics - is, day by day, a law that expresses the causes of inertia in mechanics.

These are the profound conclusions one can come to when discussing the movement of a stone thrown at an angle to the horizon.

L. V. Tarasov
