⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. To my nephew in $x^2$ will be one year old $x$ years. What year was he born?

2. The log was placed with one end on one scale and the other end on the other. The first scale showed 200 kg, and the second - 100 kg. How much does a log weigh? Where is its center of gravity?

3. Koshay the Immortal buried the klad to a depth of 1 m. This seemed not enough to him, he dug the klad, deepened the well to 2 m and buried it again. This again seemed not enough to him, he dug up the well, deepened the well to 3 m and buried it. Then he did the same, deepening the well to 4 m, then to 5 m, 6 m, etc. It is known that a well $n$ meters deep is dug by Kosha in $n^2$ days. It is also known that on the 1001st day Koshey died from overwork. At what depth did the klad remain? (Neglect the time required to bury the well.)

4. Two numbers are called mirror numbers if one number is obtained from the other by rearranging the digits in reverse order. For example, 123 and 321. The product of two mirror numbers is 92,565. What numbers are these?

5. In a convex quadrilateral, mark the midpoints of the side and connect them to the vertices as shown in figure. Show that the area of ​​the red quadrilateral is equal to the sum of the areas of the blue triangles.

These tasks were suggested to us by L. D. Kurlyandchik, V. D. Vyon, D. B. Fuks, A. V. Vasin, V. V. Proizvolov.
