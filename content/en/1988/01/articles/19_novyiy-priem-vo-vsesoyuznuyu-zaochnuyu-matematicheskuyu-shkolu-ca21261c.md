Moscow State Pedagogical Institute named after. V. I. Lenina

Physics

Oral exam assignment
1. A skater with a mass of $M=60$ kg throws a stone of mass $m=2$ kg in a horizontal direction with a speed of $v=15$ m/s. How far will the skater roll back if it is known that the coefficient of friction on the ice is $\mu=0,02$?
2. An ice floe with a cross-sectional area $S=1$ m$^2$ and a height $h=0,4$ m floats in water. How much work must be done to completely submerge the ice floe into the water?
3. The rubber chamber contains air at a temperature of $t_1=27$ °C and normal atmospheric pressure. To what depth should the chamber be lowered into water to reduce its volume by half? Water temperature $t_2=4$ °C.
4. In a room with a volume of $V=75$ m$^3$ the stove was fired and the temperature rose from $t_1=16$ °C to $t_2=21$ °C. The air pressure in the room did not change and remained equal to $p_0=1$ atm. What mass of air left the room?
5. A piece of steel weighing $m_2=10$ kg, heated to $t_2=500$ °C, is dropped into a vessel containing $V_1=4,6$ liters of water at $t_1=20$ °C. The water is heated to $t_3=100$ °C, and part of it is formed into steam. Find the mass of steam produced. The specific heat capacity of water is $c_1=4200$ J/(kg K), the specific heat of vaporization of water is $r=2,3\cdot10^6$ J/kg, the specific heat capacity of steel is $c_2=460$ J/(kg K).
6. A lamp with a power of $P=500$ W is designed for a voltage of $U_1=110$ V. Determine the value of additional resistance that allows you to connect it to a network with a voltage of $U_2=220$ V without changing its power.
7. A voltmeter connected to each current source showed $U_1=6$ V. When light bulbs were connected to the same terminals, the voltmeter began to show $U_2=3$ V. What will the voltmeter show if two of the same light bulbs connected in series are connected together?
8. The electric motor of a crane operates under a voltage of $U=380$ V and consumes a current of $I=20$ A. What is the efficiency of the installation if the crane lifts a mass of $m=1$ t uniformly to a height of $h=19$ m in $t=50$ s?
9. A ray of light falls on the surface of water at an angle $a_1=40^\circ$. At what angle must the beam fall on the surface of the stack for the angle of refraction to be the same? $(n_B=1,3; n_c=1,6.)$
10. What wavelength of light falls on the surface of a metal plate with a working output of $A=10^{-18}$ J, if the speed of the photocleton is $v=3\cdot10^6$ m/s? Electron mass $m=9,1\cdot10^{-31}$ kg, Planck's constant $h=6,62\cdot10^{-34}$ J s, speed of light $c=3\cdot10^8$ m/s.

Individual

A new example at the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School

At the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosov accepts seventh grade students from general education schools and vocational schools for individual training, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

Pel VZMSH - tell your students about many interesting things related to the school mathematics course, teach them to solve a wide variety of problems, teach them to work independently with a book and competently, clearly and concisely express their thoughts on paper. We will all successfully graduate from VZMSH

(including its branches and “Collective Student” groups) are issued appropriate certificates.

To enter VZMSH, you must successfully complete the first task. Children living in rural areas, working-class villages and small towns enjoy the benefits.

Task 1. Study the article by S. M. Lvovsky and A. L. Tom “Let’s look at all the options” in this issue of the magazine and are given to solve problems 1-12 for independent solution. It is not necessary to solve all 12 problems to be accepted into the VZMSH.

Solutions to the problems should be completed in Russian in a student's squared notebook. This notebook is called a simple parcel post; no need to put it in the tube. Glue a piece of paper onto the cover of the notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the following pattern:

Regions
Last name, first name of the student
Year of birth
Class and school
Full name mathematics teacher
Place of work and position of parents
