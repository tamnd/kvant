⟦folio 90⟧

Have you noticed that on the globe shown on the 1st p. covers, Antaritida “moved” to Africa? And if we wanted, we could send it to Asia or America... This non-teachable globe was depicted by none other than Erne Rubik, always behind his famous magic cube. Here he proposes to mix up 30 square pieces of the surface of the globe, moving them in wholes of 12 pieces along the equator and two meridians, and then restore the original order. In the course of the year, almost all the last pages of the covers of «Kvant» were devoted to stories about permutational globules and the principles of their solution. In order not to repeat ourselves, we will now focus only on some of the distinctive features of the Rubik's globe.
