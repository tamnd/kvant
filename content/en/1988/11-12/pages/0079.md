6. At what minimum value $n$ does the system
$$\begin{cases} \sin x_1 + \sin x_2 + \ldots + \sin x_n = 0, \\ \sin x_1 + 2 \sin x_2 + \ldots + n \sin x_n = 100 \end{cases}$$
has a solution?

7.* The sequence $\{a_n\}$ is given matching
$$a_0 = 0, a_n = P(a_{n-1}), n = 1, 2, \ldots,$$
where $P(x)$ is a polynomial with natural coefficients. Prove that for any natural numbers $m$ and $k$ with the greatest common divisor $d$, the greatest common divisor of the numbers $a_m$ and $a_k$ is $a_d$.

V. Lev

8.* Prove that for any tetrahedron the inequality holds
$$r < \frac{ab}{2(a+b)},$$
where $a, b$ are the lengths of two crossing edges, and $r$ is the radius of the inscribed ball.

I. Sharyn
