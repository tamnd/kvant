In the first problem for grade 10, we had to find the mass of two identical weights at the ends of a light rod that can rotate around a horizontal axis. The second task will be to determine the capacitance of the capacitor and the inductance of the coil (with a known resistance $R=7,5$ Ohm). The sections have at their disposal a pointer galvanometer (microammeter), capacitors (2 µF and 4 µF), a diode, a switch and two batteries.

The capacitance of an unknown capacitor could be determined by comparing the galvanometer waste when discharging a capacitor of unknown and known capacitance. To determine the inductance of the coil, the diagram shown in figure 10 was followed. First, the switch is set to position 1, the charge of the capacitor is zero. Then, when the switch is opened, the energy $LI^2/2$ stored in the coil is converted into the energy of the charged capacitor $Q^2/(2C)$ (the diode reverses the discharge of the capacitor through the coil). Moving the switch to position 2, the charge of the capacitor is determined by the galvanometer rejection.

Please, the best solution to this problem was to use a diode in order to prevent the capacitor from discharging.
