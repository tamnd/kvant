⟦folio 47⟧

Tasks

1. Find all representations of 1988 as a sum of consecutive natural numbers.

2. Solve an arithmetic puzzle by replacing letters with numbers. Identical letters must have identical numbers, different letters must have different numbers.

3. Two wheels rolled towards each other with the same angular velocity. When they collided, they touched each other with the same points that touched the ground at the beginning of the movement. Can the radiuses of these wheels not be the same?

4. There is a bunch of 1001 stones on the table. A stone is thrown out of it and the kutsu is divided into two. Then, from any pile containing more than one stone, a stone is again thrown out, and one of the piles is again divided into two. And so on. Is it possible to leave only piles of three stones on the table after a few moves?

5. There are ten cards with the numbers 1, 2, ..., 9, 0. Make up three numbers from them that relate to each other as a) 1: 3: 5; b) 2:3:4. (All 10 cards must be used.)

These tasks were proposed to us by: N.K. Antonovich, A.M. Domashenko, N.Yu. Nechevgaev, S.V. Rukshin, A.V. Shvetsov.
