⟦folio 73⟧

Conferences in Stavropol

On one of the first days of May this year, the controllers of Moscow's Vnukovo airport were attracted by a young man trying to design a suspicious unit on board an aircraft. Fortunately, the flight did not have to be delayed: the unit turned out to be a home-made instrumentation equipment, and the young man was a student of Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University. Together with his school team, he went to Stavropol, where a physics and mathematics conference for schoolchildren was held at the Pedagogical Institute and the city Palace of the Plein Airs.

Children from other leading physics and mathematics schools in the country also came to the conference: boarding schools at Leningrad and Novosibirsk universities, Moscow School No. 542 at MEPhI. And even though there were not so many of them, as they say, there were no “tourists” in the teams - everyone came with reports, and some even two.

It is impossible to talk about all three ten reports here. Let us mention those that, unfortunately, aroused the greatest interest among ministers - pedagogy students and city school students. This is a “Study of the decay of the structure and movement of water droplets” by Leningrad residents Vadim Moroz and Sergei Belousov, who demonstrated their experimental setup in action, a presentation by Anton Kuzminn from the school at MEPhI, who proposed an original way to improve the quality of color television images, a report by the youngest participants - seventh-graders from Stavropol Nikolay Onezhko and Vitaly Samonov “The Rings of Maragonni” about the effectiveness, formed when drops fall onto a thin layer of liquid. In the display class, the conference hosts showed a number of educational and gaming programs in action.

Of course, the meeting program was not limited to reports. The guests remembered both the evening of jba and the three-way physical fight. And in response to their request, the conference participants received a wonderful gift - a most interesting trip along the roads of Stavropol and Karachay-Cherkessia in the foothills of the Caucasus, to where the RATAN-600 radio telescope and the VTA optical telescope were built.

The organizers of the Stavropol conference, among whom the first to be named is the true entheticist of this matter, the head of the department of theoretical physics, pedagogist V. S. Igropul, consider its main tasks to be the revival of interest in physics and mathematics, the search and education of young talents, the promotion of modern forms of work with schoolchildren (by the way, regional television helps them with this). Following the example of the festival of young mathematicians in Batumi, which has gained a high reputation, they plan to make the conference traditional and expand the number of participants. Let's wish them good luck and hope that the ethos of Batumia and Stavropol will be picked up by other cities in our country.

V. N. Dubrovsky
