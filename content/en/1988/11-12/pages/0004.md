On this day, a small group of his students and employees gathered in his house near Moscow in Komarovka, mostly the same ones who had invited Andrei Nikolaevich to his birthday 200 years earlier. A. N. Kolmogorov (among his students there were seven academicians!), and mathematicians were helped by those who in the last years of his life collaborated with him. We publish memories of A.N.’s close students about how they met him, worked with him, or simply communicated with him. In these memoirs, not only a vivid image of a great scientist emerges, but also an image of a unique personality.
