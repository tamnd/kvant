It turned out that if a variable is described as an integer, but during the calculation process its value exceeds the permissible previous values, then Robotron (and, by the way, most other compulators) transfer it to a real value, losing its signs, and the sequence is distorted. And it also turned out that the sequence spoiled in this way leads to the same (correct) answer: 259, found by many Olympida participants, but not all “with a guarantee.” When solving this problem, it was necessary to create a special program for working with large numbers and control the values ​​of the numbers placed in the calculation process (see also «Kvant», 1988, No. 7, p. 59).
