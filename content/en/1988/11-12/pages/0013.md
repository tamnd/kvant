At the same time, it is necessary that all types of activities that a person chooses for himself are truly notified. And A.N. managed to build his life in this way: his creative achievement was extraordinary, he knew how to appreciate a lot - he loved human communication, nature, music, literature...

Outwardly rational, dimensional, his life is filled with range, filled with both interest in everything that exists and deep experiences. However, A.N. noticed that in his opinion, every person, starting from a certain moment, continues to remain, as it were, at the age for which the attitude of that person is most characteristic.
