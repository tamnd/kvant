⟦folio 31⟧

Lightning in crystal

(For the beginning, see p. 12)

Heterolasers are manufactured in millions of units per year; their use has revolutionized our established ideas in many technical areas. A laser fiber-optic line connects in one second to transmit such an amount of digital information that all 30 volumes of BSE can be encoded! The laser disk filling is stable, the volume of a desk bedside table, and can “hold” more than a million books. Optical integrated circuits used by heterolasers are being developed with an eye to supercomputers, which will be thousands of times more powerful than computers.

Well, why “lightning in a crystal”? Yes, because the current density in LEDs and lasers can be several, and sometimes tens of times, higher than the current density in a lightning discharge. This means that dozens of “microlightning” sparkle in crystals, only they are controlled by the will of man and do not serve destruction: their flashes breathe life into optoelectronics.

Anyone who today or tomorrow devotes himself to LEDs and lasers will be lucky enough to work on the subject of solid-state physical and technical influences. Equipment has already been created that makes it possible to grow semiconductor structures in which the composition and properties change through each monoatomic layer. The creator of such superlattices, Popinet, has the “power of boron” - he can control the fate of each electron at his discretion: “put” one in a “quantum well”, “wall up” another in a “quantum box”, and let the third continuously “walk” throughout the crystal. But... it’s more pleasant to do this than to write about it. Trust me. Better yet, grow up and check for yourself.
