⟦folio 89⟧

There were enough magazine pages, and many topics had to be sacrificed. Solving this problem, the editors took measures to increase the number of magazine pages in each issue to 80 in the new year. Much attention will be paid to the issue of space exploration and astronomy. The “Art of Programming” section will be expanded, in which the main attention will be paid to the general issue of programming (formalization of tasks, preparing them for installation on a computer, etc.). Experience shows that those who have access to a programmable computing device (from a microcalculator to a personal computer) very quickly master technical programming, that is, the path from an algorithm to a program. The path from the informal formulation of the problem to the algorithm turns out to be more difficult.

We hope that our readers will be even more active in answering the questions in the questionnaire published below. At the same time, we offer you a small experiment to check. Take a coin and toss it 9 times (exactly nine!), while writing down what comes up on top - the coat of arms or the number. If there is a coat of arms at the top all 9 times, then be sure to send us a questionnaire (at least briefly answering all the questions). Thus, the “zero” question of the questionnaire will be the following: how many times did you get a coat of arms in 9 throws?

Note: Each reader of this issue of the magazine can perform a cycle of 9 throws once.

A. N. Vilenkin

Our profile

Dear readers! In order for the editors to take into account your interests, level of training and tastes when preparing materials for publication in «Kvant», we ask you to answer the questions in our traditional questionnaire.

Send your answers on a separate sheet of paper, keep the numbering of questions until January 10, 1989. Our address: 103006 Moscow K-6, st. Gorkogo, 32/1, «Kvant». Make a note on the envelope: “Questionnaire—88.”

1. Last name, first name (optional). Place of study (city, village; educational institution, class, course) or work (profession, specialty). Do you subscribe to a magazine or borrow it from friends or the library?

2. Range of interests (mathematics, physics, computer science, astronomy). Since what year have you been reading our magazine? How many people read your copies «Kvant» (for subscribers)?

3. The most and least interesting mathematics papers published in 1988.

4. The most and least interesting physics papers published in 1988.

5. The best and worst magazine covers of 1988; your opinion about the design of the magazine.

6. Which sections of the magazine do you like and which sections do you not? What new sections should be introduced?

7. What materials published in «Kvant» in 1988 helped with your work or accounting that you looked for in «Kvant» but did not find?

8. General opinion about the magazine. What would you like to see covered in the pages of our magazine in 1989-1990?
