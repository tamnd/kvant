XXII All-Russian Olympiad in Physics

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. I. BUZDIN,
M. M. TSYPIN

The final stage of the All-Scholastic Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held this year from April 13 to 20 in the capital of Georgia, Tbilisi. 46 eighth-graders, 51 ninth-graders and 60 tenth-graders came to the final competition - winners of the previous stages of this year’s Olympiad and recognized by last year’s All-Grade Olympiad.

The theoretical round was held on April 15 at the Physics and Mathematics Boarding School named after. Komarov (where, by the way, the Olympiad participants lived). Traditionally, the 8th classics were offered 4 problems (4 hours were allotted to solve them), and the 9th and 10th classics were each given 5 problems (5 hours). Let us present their conditions (some problems were published in “Problem «Kvant»” in issues 9 and 10 of the journal).

Objectives of the theoretical round
8th grade
1. Accelerating with the maximum possible acceleration on a straight section of the highway, the racing car increases speed from 10.0 to 10.5 m/s in 0.1 s. How long would it take him to do the same on a ring-marked section of the highway with a radius of 30 m? At what radius of the stake would he generally not be able to increase the speed above 10 m/s? The plane of the highway is horizontal.
2. Push the raft away from the river bank, giving it a speed of 0.3 m/s in the direction perpedicular to the bank. figure 1 shows the initial section of the raft’s trajectory. How far from the shore will the raft eventually float? The river flow speed is 0.3 m/s. The resistance force of the raft's motion is proportional to its relative water speed.
3. In the meter there is slowly a melt of the test substance. The specific heat of fusion of this substance (it was determined in previous experiments) is 200 kJ/kg. Using the graph of the temperature of a substance versus time (Fig. 2), determine the specific heat capacities of the substance in the solid and liquid states. The heat capacity of the meter is a claim.
4. In the circuit shown in figure 3, the amperators show points 0.2 A and 0.3 A. After the two resistors in the circuit were swapped, the ampere readings did not change. What current does the battery carry? Assume the battery voltage remains constant. The resistance of the amperators is pretentiously small.
9th grade
1. Investigating the new discovery, which has the shape of a ball with a radius of 6400 km and is covered over the entire surface by an ocean with a depth of 10 km (of total water), scientists have established that the acceleration of gravity with a high degree of accuracy (no worse than 10⁻⁵) remains unchanged when immersed in the ocean to different depths. Using these data, determine the acceleration of gravity on this planet. Gravitational constant G = 6.67·10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg².
2. A horizontal platform with a coin lying on it makes a circular translational motion in the horizontal plane so that all points of the platform describe circles of radius R with angular velocity ω. The friction coefficient between the pad and the coin is μ. What will be the establishing movement of the coin? What trace does she “draw” on the site?

3. The U-shaped glass tube is partially filled with water (Fig. 4). The upper ends of the tube close and heat the right elbow of the tube to a temperature of +100 °C, and the left one to +99.5 °C. Determine the steady-state difference in water levels in the tube bends. Reference: at an altitude of 23 floors (70 m above the ground), the boiling point of water is 0.25 degrees lower than on the ground. The thermal distribution of glass in the calculations is pretenbrets. Conditions on the first floor level are normal.

4. The electrophore machine model (Fig. 5) consists of a non-conducting cylinder on which foil planes Φ₁ and Φ₂ are glued, outer plates O₁ and O₂, fixed current collectors T₁ and T₂ on the outside and a fixed jumper inside with current collectors T₃ and T₄. In the initial position, the foil strips are directed towards the outer plates and form capacitors with a capacitance of C₁ each. A capacitor with a capacity C₀, pre-charged to a voltage U₀, is connected to the outer plates. What will this direction be after N revolutions of the cylinder clockwise? The capacity between the plates and the foil strips in the separated state is negligible.

5. At some distances from coil 1, through which the current I₁ flows, a short-circuited coil 2 is fixed, made of materials that are in a superconducting state at room temperature. At this moment, there is no current flowing through coil 2. When the current through coil 1 doubled, a current I₂=0.2I₁ appeared in coil 2. What will this current become if the current of coil 1 is doubled? The experiment is carried out at room temperature.

10th grade

1. The formation of the Jupterian cometary family is described by the following scheme. The comet falls from a great distance without initial speed onto the Sun and flies not far from Jupter (Fig. 6). After the noticeable influence of Jupter’s gravitational field ceases, the comet again moves in the field of the Sun, and its speed turns out to be directed opposite to the speed of Jupter, and the apheal new orbit of the comet is located near the orbit of Jupter, i.e. at a distance of 5.2 AU. e. from the Sun. At what distance from the Sun will the perihelion of the orbit of such a comet be located?

2. The charged part enters a medium where it is acted upon by a resistance force proportional to the speed. Before a particle comes to a complete stop, it travels a distance of 10 cm. If there is a certain magnetic field in the medium perpendicular to the velocity of the particle, at the same initial speed it stops at a distance of 6 cm from the point of entry into the medium. At what distance from the point of entry into the medium would the particle stop if the magnetic field induction were half as strong?

3. In a closed cavity with a volume of 3 cm³ there are drops of water in equilibrium with steam. At what droplet radius will this equilibrium be stable? The walls of the cavity are considered non-wettable; there are no other droplets or condensation centers. The temperature is constant (293 K). The graph of the excess pressure of saturated vapor over a convex surface of water versus the radius of curvature of this surface Δp=p_{us}(r)-p_{us}(\infty) is shown on Fig. 7. Saturated steam is considered an ideal gas.

4. A flat-plate air capacitor, the area of ​​which is $S$, and the distance between the areas is $d_0$, is connected to a battery with a voltage of $U_0$ in series with a resistor of resistance $R$. One of the areas of the capacitor begins to oscillate, remaining parallel to the other area, so that the distance between the areas changes with time according to the law $d=d_0 (1+m \sin \omega t)$, where $m \ll 1$. Determine the average power $P$ released on the resistor at low frequency of the bulb

$$\left( \omega \ll \frac{1}{RC} \right) \text{ и при высокой частоте } \left( \omega \gg \frac{1}{RC} \right).$$

Based on these results, construct a qualitative graph of the dependence of $P$ on $\omega$.

5. Currently, in connection with the discovery of the phenomenon of high-temperature superconductivity, the question of creating direct current transfer lines without energy losses due to Joule heat is being studied. It is proposed to use a coaxial cable for direct current transmission, consisting of an inner cylindrical core and an outer cylindrical shell made of a superconductor. The electric and magnetic fields in such a system are depicted at figure 8. It is known that the inductive magnetic field at the surface of a superconductor cannot exceed a certain value $B_{\max}$, otherwise superconductivity is destroyed, and the electric field strength should not exceed $E_{\max}$, otherwise an electrical breakdown of the insulating layer of the cable will occur. Determine how many times the maximum direct current power transmitted through such a cable changes if the diameters of the inner and outer sheaths are doubled. What is the maximum power that can be transmitted through a cable with sheath diameters $D=8$ cm and $d=3$ cm, if $E_{\max}=20$ kV/cm and $B_{\max}=5 \cdot 10^{-2}$ T? Note: the inductive magnetic field in the space between the cylindrical conductors coincides with the field of a straight conductor with current $I$ and is equal to $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$, where $\mu_0=4\pi \cdot 10^{-7} \text{ H/A}^2$ is the magnetic constant.

On the same day, the jury began checking the work of a theoretical type, which continued until late in the evening. The jury included representatives of Tbilisi and Moscow, Dolgoprudny and Leningrad, Yerevan and Novosibirsk. Everyone worked at least twice, and special attention was paid to ensuring that the marks for the same solutions (and the same mistakes) were the same.

According to the test results, the most difficult problems were: in the 8th grade - problem 2, in the 9th grade - problems 2 and 3, in the 10th grade - 1 and 2. Answering the question about which problems they improved the most, the schoolchildren named just these problems (with the exception of problem 3 in the 9th grade) plane problem 4 in the 9th grade and problem 3 in the 10th grade.

On April 16, the participants optimized the site in the All-Union Communist Community Cleanup Day, and on Sunday, April 17, they worked on completing a given experimental type (2 tasks for 4 hours), prepared by scientists from the Institute of Physics of the Academy of Sciences of the GrSSR.

Let's talk a little about these tasks.

In the first task for 8th grade, it was necessary to determine the density of the plastic from which the pattern was made. There was a vessel with water on the table, but it was too small to completely immerse the pattern in it. It was possible to change the reduction in the weight of the pattern when it is partially immersed in water, using homemade lever scales from a ruler suspended on a thread for this.

It’s interesting that writing air bubbles on the pattern could change the answer by one and a half to two times. You should pay attention to this and get rid of the bubbles.

In the first task for grade 9, it was necessary to determine the charge of an electrode by electrolyzing a NaCl solution. For some areas, the absence of chlorine release on ande was unexpected (the fact is that chlorine immediately reacts with copper anome).

The second task required: a) determine the correspondence of the black box pins to the circuit shown in figure 9; b) find the values ​​$R_1$ and $R_2$; c) applying a voltage of 9 V to the AC terminals (from a battery), plot the dependence of the output voltage $u_{b1x}=u_{DC}$ on the input voltage $u_{bx}=u_{BC}$ and determine the gain $k=\Delta u_{b1x}/\Delta u_{bx}$. The sites had two batteries, amperators and a rheostat at their disposal.

In the first problem for grade 10, we had to find the mass of two identical weights at the ends of a light rod that can rotate around a horizontal axis. The set of equipment included: a piece of plasticine (weighing 20 g), a ruler and a mathematical matrix. It was necessary to change the period of the flask rod without plasticine and with plasticine attached to one of the weights. A mathematical matrix should be used as a clock.

The second task will be to determine the capacitance of the capacitor and the inductance of the coil (with a known resistance $R=7,5$ Ohm). The sections have at their disposal a pointer galvanometer (microammeter), capacitors (2 µF and 4 µF), a diode, a switch and two batteries.

The capacitance of an unknown capacitor could be determined by comparing the galvanometer waste when discharging a capacitor of unknown and known capacitance. To determine the inductance of the coil, the diagram shown in figure 10 was followed. First, the switch is set to position 1, the charge of the capacitor is zero. Then, when the switch is opened, the energy $LI^2/2$ stored in the coil is converted into the energy of the charged capacitor $Q^2/(2C)$ (the diode reverses the discharge of the capacitor through the coil). Moving the switch to position 2, the charge of the capacitor is determined by the galvanometer rejection.

Please, the best solution to this problem was to use a diode in order to prevent the capacitor from discharging.
