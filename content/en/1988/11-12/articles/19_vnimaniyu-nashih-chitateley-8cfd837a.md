As in any auto-recuperation system, the following parts can be distinguished:

1) constant source of energy - battery;
2) oscillating system - spring;
3) a device that regulates the flow of energy into the oscillating system - a spring and contacts 2 and 3, closing the circuit;
4) Feedback is a mechanism driven by an external force and acting on a source of current. In this case, the feedback is carried out as follows: an electric current (external force) generates a magnetic field, which causes slope springs and opens the circuit. The elastic force (internal force) that appears in this case closes the circuit again.

It should be noted that the gravity force of the spring does not play a significant role, so such a system will function improbably. The energy input into the oscillatory system is equal to the energy loss due to friction, therefore such a system will perform undamped oscillations until the energy source runs out.
