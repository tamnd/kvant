Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. V. VAVILOV,
PhD in Physics and Mathematics
S. V. REZNICHENKO

The final stage of the XXII All-Union Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren took place in April 1988 in Donetsk. 29 teams took part in it: 14 allied resource teams, one team from each of the four zones of the RSFSR, teams from Moscow, Leningrad, Donetsk and 8 teams from specialized boarding schools at the country's leading universities - a total of 172 schoolchildren (51 eighth-graders, 56 ninth-graders and 65 tenth-graders).

As usual, the Olympiad participants were given four problems in two rounds, each of which was given five hours to solve.

Each task of the Olympiad was assessed in points so that the total number of points in the round for each class was equal to thirty (see Table 1).

Please note that, apparently, problems 7 and 8 in the ninth grade were too difficult, and problems 1 and 5 in the tenth grade were too easy for the Olympiad students. In the eighth and tenth grades, the first was awarded for solving 7-8 problems, and in the ninth grade - for solving 6-7 problems. The third was awarded to an eighth-grade student who scored at least 29 points in both rounds, a ninth-grade student who scored at least 21 points, and a tenth-grade student who scored at least 34 points. Table 3 shows the number of prize winners for each class.

The organizing committee for the final stage of the All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad was headed by the head of the Donetsk regional department of public education, I. M. Sakhno. The honorary representative of the Zhorni was Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR, Professor of Moscow State University V.V. Gnedenko, the representative of the Zhorni was the Rector of Donetsk State University, Professor V.P. Shevchenko, and his deputy was the Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics of the DSU, Associate Professor P.M. Veliko and Associate Professor of the Moscow State University V.V. Vavilov. The Olympics were held on the basis of sanatorium boarding school No. 8 and secondary school No. 96 in Donetsk. The hosts of the Olympiad treated the Olympiad participants and members of the school with great warmth and care. Many thanks to them all.

In their free time, schoolchildren got acquainted with the history of Donetsk, its sights, and attended the Donetsk Opera and Ballet Theater and the circus. The meeting with members of the editorial board of the journal «Kvant» was interesting.
A traditional mathematical battle between teams of schoolchildren and the jury also took place. Three teams took part in this fascinating, essentially theatrical performance: two teams of schoolchildren and a jury team. The problems this time were quite simple (about the level of section «Kvant» for junior schoolchildren]), but there were quite a lot of them (about 30) and it was easy to answer. The team of schoolchildren, led by Vitaly Volgodsky (Omsk, s.sh. No. 99), won, and the jury team took second place.
Here are some of the challenges for this competition.
1. Construct an octagonal closed broken line that intersects each of its links once.
2. What is greater: the area of ​​a circle written in a square of area 1, or the area of ​​a square written in a circle of area 1?
3. One little boy didn’t have 12 kopecks to buy ice cream, and the other one didn’t have enough to buy ice cream, 1 kopeck. When they added up their money, it turned out that they still didn’t have enough money to buy one ice cream. How much does ice cream cost?
4. Which city is located further east: Vladivostok or Khabarovsk? Which city is located further north: Moscow or Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky?
5. Continue the sequence:
   111, 213, 141, 516, 171,...
6. The clock chimes 6 times in 30 seconds. How long does it take them to hit 12 times?
7. Construct 7 points so that the environments of any three points are two, the distance between which is 1 cm.
