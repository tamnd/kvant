⟦folio 46⟧

How many angles does a triangle have?

- Of course three. It's called a TRIANGLE.

- Well, in general, there are three internal angles. And if you count all the angles of a triangle, including external ones, then there will be ten of them.

- What do external corners have to do with it?

- Despite the fact that he asks not “how many internal angles?”, but “how many angles does a triangle have?” An external angle of a triangle is a specific concept in relation to the generic concept “angle of a triangle”, so an external angle is an angle of a triangle.

- I don’t agree. Adding an adjective to a noun does not always highlight a special case. Otherwise we would have to look for the angles, solid angles, etc. of a triangle with two boundaries.

- Besides, when a triangle is drawn, then the image

only affect internal angles.

- You are both wrong. A triangle has six angles!
- Why is this?

- Because there are three corners from the inside, and three more from the outside. They are depicted!

- In this case, each vertex has 4, for a total of twelve angles.

- And this depends on how you count: at each vertex you can see up to 12, for a total of thirty-six angles.

- This is too much!
- Not at all. If we take into account that each ray defines two angles of magnitude 0° and 360°, then there are 60 of them. Sixty-seven!

— When they say “angle of a triangle,” its sides are mentally extended to the ray.

- Maybe this is not enough for you?
- Yes! The angles of a triangle are overwhelmingly dark, infinitely many.
