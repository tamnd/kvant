Who reads it and how «Kvant»

In this issue, as before at the end of the year, we publish “Questionnaire «Kvant». Your answers help us choose topics, find authors, and find new ways to present materials.

Not too many responses were received to the questionnaires published in recent years, but it turned out that the results of statistical processing of questionnaires from different years were very close. And although we cannot assume that responses were sent evenly by all categories of readers, the analysis of the management of the active part of readers is of great interest.

What is the average reader «Kvant» like with this data? The magazine is read from 5th graders to retirees. Great texx who the magazine is aimed at - high school students. Among the readers: students in grades 5-7 - 11%, grade 8 - 25%, grade 9 - 23%, grade 10 - 21%, technical students and students - 5%, teachers - 11%, others ("adults" lovers of mathematics and physics) - 4%. 84% of the magazine's circulation is distributed in cities, 16% in rural areas. One copy of the magazine is read by an average of 5 people, and in the city - 3 people, and in rural areas, like the magazine subscribed by the teacher, - a whole class.

Schoolchildren have been signing up for the magazine mainly since the 8th grade; many teachers have had «Kvant» grades since its inception.

84% of readers are interested in mathematics, 64% - in physics, which would make it both - 48%. An analysis of the distribution of reader interest in the magazine's sections showed that each section has its own reader. Questionnaires sent by the winner of the “Problem Book «Kvant»” competition (they were surveyed separately) were allocated to a separate group; The data relating to them is given in parentheses.

The most popular headings: “School in «Kvant», its interest is 54% (46%) of readers, with 36% of 8th graders, 54% of 9th graders (71%) and 67% of 10th graders (39%); "Kaleidoscope" - 50% (29%); “Problem book «Kvant» - 45% (78%); “Practicum for applicants” - 41% (52%); “The art of programming” - 39% (19%); "Olympias" - 38% (41%); “Laboratory «Kvant» - 36% (28%); “Math circle” - 34% (37%). The remaining sections are of interest to between 35 and 20% of readers (each). The most active readers of «Kvant» are 9th graders, they are interested in the content of more than 7 main sections of the magazine, 10th graders - 6 sections, 8th graders - only 5 sections. Interest in the «Kvant» rubric for younger schoolchildren drops from 83% among 5th-7th graders to 8% among 10th graders, while interest in the “Applicant's Practicum” and “Entrance Exam Options” rubrics grows from 20% among 8th-graders to 60% among 10th-graders. “The art of programming” is of interest mainly to 9th graders - 62% (41%), and fewer 10th graders - 25% (13%) and causes a lot of criticism: the materials are too simple.

The best articles of 1987 are most often named in physics: “Encounter with Halley’s comet”, “Optical electronics in fresh conditions”; in mathematics: “Ramanujan’s Riddle”, “Best Pairs for Simpletons”, “Finite Groups” (the latter also caused a lot of negative feedback).

As for the covers, everyone liked cover No. 10, most liked cover No. 2 and No. 5, and disliked cover No. 6 and No. 4.

Among the topics proposed for articles for future years, most sections of mathematics and physics, as well as space research and programming, were represented. Readers ask for advance information about upcoming popular science literature and to write more about modern discoveries in mathematics, physics and astronomy. The late timing of the magazine's publication caused a lot of criticism.

We were able to satisfy some requests, but not others. The timing of the publication of the magazine is related to the printing house, which also produces many other magazines. Hold in your hands the journal number: 11-12 (numbers 2, 4, 6, 9 were also enlarged). It turned out that this is the easiest way to coordinate the magazine issue with the month of its publication. Telef No. 1 for 1989 will confirm the schedule of the former No. 12 and will be released in early January.
