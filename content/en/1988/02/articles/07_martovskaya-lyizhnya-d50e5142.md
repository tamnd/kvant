In conclusion, we note the fundamental significance of the fact that the source of the electric field is an alternating, and not a constant, magnetic field.

In fact, if a constant magnetic field were the source of an electric field, then a “perpetual motion machine” would be possible. In wires located in such a field, in this case a current would flow, which, in turn, would create a magnetic field, which, in turn, would create

there would be current in new conductors, etc. As a result, a single wire with current would become an infinite reservoir of energy. Therefore, the fact that a constant magnetic field does not have an electrical effect necessarily follows from the necessity of “perpetual motion.”

Maxwell's theory not only united the disparate scientific knowledge about electrical and magnetic phenomena accumulated by the middle of the 19th century.
