⟦folio 37⟧

Quests

1. The calendar sheet is partially covered by the previous sheet (see figure). Which part of it should open or close?

2. Some have cubed the integer $A$. Show that at least one of the numbers: $A^3-A$ or $A^3+A$ is divisible by 10.

3. Why do birds embroider their feathers in cold weather?

4. A three-digit number begins with the number 7. From it, other three-digit numbers were obtained by moving this number to the end of the number. The resulting number was 117 less than the previous one. What number were you looking at?

5. Write the numbers 1, 2, ..., 9 (each one once) in the boxes so that the product turns out to be the largest.

These tasks were offered to us by: A. M. Domashenko, G. A. Galperin, A. P. Savin, N. K. Antonovich, L. P. Motsalov.
