Computer club "SPRITE"

Four years ago, when a school computer was as rare as a foreign vegetable - potatoes in the time of Peter I, an elective program in programming began at our school. The rare vegetable did not grow here: in order to work on a real computer, we went to the computer center of the Sverdlovsk Pedagogical Institute. A year later, the school computer ceased to be a curiosity, and our first computer appeared at school - “Electronics D3-28”. First one, and then the other. A year later, a whole selection of twelve new “Robots” appeared at the school. A display classroom had to be urgently set up for the hatched babies. However, now it is no more surprising than fried potatoes for dinner.

If you come to the display class of Sverdlovsk school No. 121 for a meeting of the SPRITE computer club, you can get into a lecture on “Computers in Medicine” or a debate about modern music, a meeting with a computer graphics specialist or a conversation about the creativity of French impression programs, or an evening of positions.

Every year new guys come to our club. They come to learn how to program and master new technology. The training of the youngest is carried out according to the “Novosibirsk” method of A.P. Ershov - G.A. Zvenigorodsky. To do this, we transferred to our school machines the performers “Ant” and “Sword”, described in the book by G. A. Zvenigorodsky “First Lessons in Programming” (M.: Nauka, 1986. Series: Library «Kvant»). Lessons for seniors and more

experienced participants undergoing a seminar form, when everyone takes turns studying a topic and explaining the rest. The older members of the club teach the younger ones. Our graduate Kolya Mokhov, now a university student, being a tenth-grader, studied with a group of fifth-graders. Nowadays it has become a tradition.

Group portrait with Robotrom.

Over the course of two or three years, the guys involved in the club successfully worked in several programming languages ​​and worked on various machines. Many of them began to create programs themselves and can competently carry out the entire set of work - from setting a task together with the customer to preparing documentation. However, one of the main areas of our work is the development of training programs in various subjects. Over the course of three years, programs were created for lessons in mathematics, chemistry, computer science, Russian and English. Many of them have found application in schools and colleges.

Members of our club are regular participants and winners of programming competitions, scientific and practical conferences for schoolchildren and professional competitions.

professional skills that are held in the city. They also took part in the Leningrad conference “School Informatics”.

During the summer holidays, our high school students worked as programmers in computer centers. At summer schools for young programmers in Novosibirsk, we found many friends and like-minded people. We made especially friends with members of the “Virtual” computer club from the city of Kursk.
