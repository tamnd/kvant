Reflection and refraction

These concepts, so simple at first glance, are deep and integral representations of one of the most important branches of physics—optics. There are few areas in physics in which, from the beginning of their development, only major scientific results have been obtained that have retained their significance to this day. That is why research in optics had a very great influence on the formation of other branches of the natural sciences, forced the fundamental principles of classical physics to change, and played a significant role in the emergence of the theory of relativity and quantum theory.
