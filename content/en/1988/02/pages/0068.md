2. Two balls touch the plane $P$ at points $A$ and $B$ and are located on opposite sides of this plane. The distance between the centers of the balls is 10. The third ball externally touches two given balls, and its center $O$ lies in the plane $P$. It is known that $AO = BO = 2\sqrt{10}$, $AB = 8$. Determine the radius of the triangular ball.

3. A straight circular cylinder of radius $R$ lies on the plane and, without intersecting with it, a ball of radius $r$ lies. The distance from the cylinder axis to the ball center is $\rho$. Find the minimum possible radius of the ball that would simultaneously touch the cylinder, the plane and the given ball.

4. Given a cube with bases $ABCD$ and $A_1B_1C_1D_1 (AA_1\parallel BB_1\parallel CC_1\parallel DD_1)$. A ball of radius $R = 0,5$ is inscribed into the corner $A$ of the cube. Find the radius of a ball inscribed in the angle $C_1$ of a cube and tangent to the given ball if the edge of the cube is $a = 1,5$.

5. Five equal balls are placed in a cone. Four of them lie on the base of the cone, and each of these four balls touches the other two lying on the base and the lateral surface of the cone. The fifth ball touches the side surface of the cone and the other four balls. Find the volume of the cone if the radius of each ball is $r$.

6. Two balls of radius $R$ are inscribed in two boundaries at an angle of magnitude $60^\circ$, touching each other. Find the radius of a ball inscribed at the same angle and tangent to both given balls.

7. In a right circular cone there are two identical balls of radius $r$ touching the base of the cone at points symmetrical to the relative center of the base. Each ball touches the side surface of the cone and another ball. The height of the cone is 4/3 times the base. Find the volume of the cone.

3. The radius of the circle inscribed in triangle $ABC$ is 4, and $AC = BC$. On the line $AB$, a point $D$ is taken, removed from the lines $AC$ and $BC$ at distances of 11 and 3, respectively. Find the cosine of angle $DBC$.

4. Two trains left simultaneously in the same direction from the cities $A$ and $B$, located at a distance of 60 km from each other, and simultaneously approached station $C$. If one of them increased its speed by 25 km/h, and the other by 20 km/h, then they would also approach station $C$ at the same time, but 2 hours earlier. Find the speed of the train.

5. Find all pairs of values ​​$a$ and $b$ for which the systems
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - y^2 + a(x+y) = x - y + a, \\ x^2 + y^2 + bxy - 1 = 0 \end{cases}$$
has at least five solutions $(x, y)$.

6. The sphere touches the edges $AS, BS, BC$ and $AC$ of the triangular paramid $SABC$ at points $K, L, M$ and $N$ respectively. Find the length of segment $KL$ if $MN = 7$ s, $NK = 5$ s, $LN = 2\sqrt{29}$ s and $KL = LM$.
