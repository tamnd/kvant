6. 9. Solution. Let us first note that the points $K, L, M, N$ lie in the same plane. Indeed, let the plane $\alpha$ (Fig. 7) be drawn through the point $S$, parallel to the plane $LMN$ and intersecting the lines $AC$ and $BC$ at points $E$ and $F$. Then, due to the equality of the corresponding tangent segments and the parallelism of the corresponding lines, we have $AK = AN$ and $KS = SL = SB - LB = FB - MB = FM = CM - CF = CN - CE = NE$. Therefore, $NK \parallel \alpha$ and point $K$ belong to the plane $LMN$. So, the quadrilateral $KLMN$ is cyclic and $KL = LM$, whence $\angle LNK = \angle LNM = \varphi$. Using the cosine theorem for triangles $LNK$ and $LNM$ we obtain
$$KL^2 = 116 + 25 - 4\sqrt{29} \cos \varphi \cdot 5 = 116 + 49 - 4\sqrt{29} \cos \varphi \cdot 7,$$
follow,
$$4\sqrt{29} \cos \varphi = 12, KL^2 = 81.$$

5. 20 res. Solution. It is enough to consider 3 types of loading machine blocks:
1) 0 large and 44 small blocks, 2) 1 large block and 30 small; 3) 2 large and 14 small blocks. If $m_1, m_2, m_3$ is the number of decisions corresponding to 1, 2 and 3 methods of loading the machine, then $44m_1 + 30m_2 + 14m_3 \geq 510, m_1 + 2m_2 \geq 24$. We need to find the set with the highest value $m_1 + m_2 + m_3$. If the required set contains $m_1 \geq 1$ and $m_3 \geq 1$, then a pair of resolves with loads of types 1 and 3 can be replaced with a pair of resolves with a second load and at the same time 2 small blocks will increase. Therefore, we can assume that $m_1 = 0$ or $m_3 = 0$. If $m_3 = 0$, then $m_2 \geq 24$. If $m_1 = 0$, then
$$\begin{cases} 15m_1 + 7m_2 \geq 255, \\ m_1 + 2m_2 \geq 24. \end{cases}$$
Multiplying the second inequality by 8 and adding it to the first, we get $m_1 + m_2 \geq 20$. For $m_2 = 16, m_3 = 4$ we get $m_1 + m_2 + m_3 = 20$.
