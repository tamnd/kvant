1. $k = 1$ ($A = B$), or $k = 100$ ($B = 0$). Solution. Let $k = (100A + B)/(100B + A)$ be an integer. The obvious answer is $k = 1$, $A = B$. Let $k \neq 1$, $A \neq B$. Then $A/B = (100k - 1)/(100 - k)$; count $k \neq 100$. Is it possible to reduce the fraction $(100k - 1)/(100 - k)$ so that both the numerator and the denominator are less than 100? If possible, then the fraction $(100k - 1)/(100 - k) - 1 = 101(k - 1)/(100 - k)$ can also be reduced in a similar way. But 100-k and 101 do not have common divisors, since 101 is prime, and 100-k < 100. Therefore, after reduction we obtain an irreducible fraction of the form $101 \cdot a/b$, the numerator of which is greater than 100 - a contradiction. This leaves the case $k = 100$, for which $B = 0$.

2. $104^2 + 72^2 = 120^2 + 40^2 = 16000$. Solution. The square of an even number is divisible by 4, the square of an odd number gives a remainder of 1 when divided by 4. Therefore, if $x^2 + y^2 = 16000$, then $x = 2m, y = 2n$. Then $m^2 + n^2 = 4000$, and similarly $m = 2m_1$, $n = 2n_1$, $m_1^2 + n_1^2 = 1000$, $m_1 = 2m_2$, $n_1 = 2n_2$, $m_2^2 + n_2^2 = 250 = 4 \cdot 62 + 2$. This means that $m_2$ and $n_2$ are both odd, and if $m_2 \geq n_2$ then $125 \leq m_2^2 < 250$. However, in this gap there are exactly two squares of odd numbers: $m_2 = 13$ and $m_2 = 15$. This gives an easy answer.

Denoting $t = xyz$, we obtain the cubic equation $t^3 + 24t^2 - 1080 = 0$, which has the root $t_1 = 6$. Equation $t^3 + 24t^2 - 1080 = (t - 6)(t^2 + 30t + 180) = 0$ has two more roots: $t_2, t_3 = -15 \pm 3\sqrt{5}$, which are not suitable for us, since we are looking for positive solutions to the system. By substitutions $xy = \frac{6}{z}$, $yz = \frac{6}{x}$, $zx = \frac{6}{y}$ the original system is reduced to a linear one, from which $y = x/2$, $z = 3x/2$.
