5. $$\left[ \frac{2}{3} - \sqrt{2}; \frac{2}{3} + \sqrt{2} \right].$$
Solution. The system is reduced to the form
$$\begin{cases} (3x - y + 2)^2 = 3(2x + 3y + \frac{1}{3}), \\ (3x - y + 2)^2 + (2x + 3y + 1 - a)^2 = 2, \end{cases}$$
and after introducing the notations $u = 3x - y + 2$, $v = 2x + 3y + \frac{1}{3}$, $b = a - \frac{2}{3} - k$ in the form
$$\begin{cases} u^2 = 3v, \\ u^2 + (v - b)^2 = 2. \end{cases}$$

For the existence of a solution to the last system, it is necessary and sufficient that the equation
$$3v + (v - b)^2 = 2$$
had at least one non-negative root, that is, so that the value of the quadratic trinomial $v^2 + (3 - 2b)v + b^2 - 2$ at $v = 0$ was non-positive. Thus, we obtain condition $b^2 \leq 2$, or $-\sqrt{2} \leq a - \frac{2}{3} \leq \sqrt{2}$.
