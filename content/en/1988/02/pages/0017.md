⟦folio 15⟧

There is much evidence of how art inspires the man of science. What's more surprising is that science can be a source of artistic inspiration. Flipping through a collection of poems by Russian poses from the beginning of our century, I came across deep and, probably, not always conscious connections between mathematical images and pose thinking.

For any person, the entrance to mathematics is the concept of number. The magic of numbers is widely reflected in art. But listen to the following lines of Nikolai Gumilyov:

And for the low life there were numbers,
Like livestock,
Because all shades of meaning
Smart number conveys.
