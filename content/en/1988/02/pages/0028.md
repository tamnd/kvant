⟦folio 26⟧

Tasks

M1086—M1090, F1098—F1102

M1086. With a number, two operations are allowed: “increase by 2 times” and “increase by 1”. For what is the smallest number from the number 0 to get: a) the number 100? b) number n?

M1087. The distance is triangle ABC, point M in the plane of this triangle and the projections A₁, B₁, C₁ of point M to the heights drawn from vertices A, B, C, respectively. Prove that a) there is one and only one point M for which the segments AA₁, BB₁ and CC₁ are equal;
b) for such a point M, the lengths of the segments AA₁, BB₁, CC₁ are equal to the diameter of the inscribed circle in triangle ABC.

A. Kh. Jafarov

M1088. Prove that if the numbers p, q, r are rational and pq+qr+pr=1, then (1+p²)(1+q²)(1+r²) is the square of a rational number.

Istvan Varga (Romania)

M1089. In a convex quadrilateral ABCD of area S, the diagonals intersect at point O. Let K, L, M, N be the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles AOB, BOC, COD and DOA. Prove that the product of the perimeters of quadrilaterals ABCD and KLMN is not less than 4S.

D. Yu. Burago, F. L. Nazarov

M1090. Prove that a) for any positive numbers a, b and c, it is true that your address is not on it (in this envelope you will receive the result of checking the solution).

The conditions of each original problem proposed for publication are assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Quata problem”, a new problem in physics or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you are a teacher.

F1098. A children's pistol, which can be thought of as a finite-mass weapon attached to a stationary wall, fires a ball, giving it a velocity v. If you shoot a ball with twice its mass, its speed will be v√2/3. What will be the speed of a ball with three times its mass?

P. I. Zubkov

F1099. There are two pistons (Fig. 1) in a horizontally fixed pipe with a cross-section S open at the ends. In the initial state, the left piston is connected to a stationary wall with an undeformed spring of stiffness k. The gas pressure p₀ between the pistons is equal to the external pressure, the distance H from the right piston to the edge of the pipe is equal to the distance between the pistons. The right piston is slowly pulled towards the edge of the pipe. What force must be applied to the piston to hold it in its extreme position? The morning demand is small, the temperature is constant.

V. P. Borodin
