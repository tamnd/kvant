⟦folio 67⟧

Option 2
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

1. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{x} + 3y = 9, \\ x - 1 = (\sqrt{x} + 1)y. \end{cases}$$

2. Are there real values ​​of $a$ for which
$$a^2 - 4a + \sqrt{3} = -a\sqrt{2}$$
If such values ​​exist, how many are there?

3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{(x+1)^2} 8 + 3 \log_4(x+1) \geq 9 \frac{1}{4}.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$(2 + 3 \cos 2x)(\sqrt{2 \cos 2x + 3 \sin x} + 3) - 2 \sin x + 1) = 0.$$

5. 24 large and 510 small concrete blocks need to be transferred from the factory to the construction site. The blocks are delivered automatically, each of which contains 44 small blocks and has a group capacity of 10 tons. The mass of a small block is 0.2 tons, a large one is 3.6 tons, a large block takes the place of 14 small ones. Find the minimum number of resources sufficient to transport all blocks.

6. In ABCD, a section KMLN is drawn so that point K lies on edge AD, point M on edge DC, point N on edge AB, point L on edge BC, O is the intersection point of diagonals KL and MN of the quadrilateral KMLN. The KMLN section divides the primida into two parts. Find the ratio of the volumes of these parts if the following correspondences between the lengths of the segments are known:
$$4 \cdot OL = 3 \cdot OK, 25 \cdot ON = 24 \cdot OM,$$
$$DK \cdot NA = KA \cdot BN = KA \cdot NA.$$

Option 3
(actual faculty)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2x} \geq \frac{1}{1-x}.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$4^{\sin x} + 2^{5-2\sin x} = 18.$$

3. It is known that $\sin \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$, $\pi < \alpha < \frac{4\pi}{3}$. Find $\cos \alpha$ and $\mathrm{tg} \alpha$.

4. Find the domain of functions
$$y = \sqrt{\log \frac{1}{2}(3x^2 - 2x)}$$.

5. Inside the right triangle ABC (angle B is a straight line), point D is taken so that the areas of triangles ABD and BDC are respectively three and four times less than the area of ​​triangle ABC. The lengths of the segments AD and DC are equal to $a$ and $c$, respectively. Find the length of segment BD.

6. A ball of radius 2 is inscribed in a regular quadrangular paramid SABCD with vertex S. The second ball of radius 1 touches the first ball, the base of the paramid and the lateral boundaries BSC and CSD. Find the volume of the paramid and the dihedral angle at the lateral edge SC.

Option 4
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. Solve the equation
$$(1 + 2 \sin x) \sin x = \sin 2x + \cos x.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$4\log_2 x + \log_2 \left(\frac{x^2}{8(x-1)}\right) \leq 4 - \log_2 (x-1) - \log_2^2 x.$$

3. The sides of the triangle lie on the coordinate axes and on the tangent to the graph of the function $y = x^2 + 2x + 1$ at the point whose abscissa $a$ satisfies the condition $-\frac{1}{2} \leq a \leq 0$. Find the value of $a$ at which the area of ​​the triangle is greatest.

4. The base of the quadrangular paramid FABCD is the square ABCD. A point E is taken on edge AF such that segment CE is perpendicular to edge AF. The projection O of point E to the base of the paramid lies on the segment AC and divides it in the ratio AO:OC=γ. Find the difference between the volumes of the paramides FABCD and EABD if it is known that $\angle ADF = 90^\circ$ and AB=a.

5. Find all values ​​of $p$ for each of which there are possible solutions to the inequality
$$(p - x^2)(p + x - 2) < 0$$
does not contain any solution to the inequality
$$x^2 \leq 1.$$

Option 5
(faculty of biology)

1. Solve the equation
$$7\sin(2x - \frac{5}{2}\pi) + 9\cos x + 1 = 0.$$

2. A raft sets off from point A along the river. At the same time, a ship is called to him from point B, located downstream relative to point A. Having met the raft, the ship immediately turns and goes downstream. Find what part of the path from A to B the raft will have completed by the time the terat returns to point B, if the tert's speed at rest is high and the river flows at high speeds.

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{3 \log_0.5 x}{2 - \log_0.5 x} \geq 2 \log_0.5 x + 1.$$

4. The area of ​​the trapezoid ABCD is 30. Point P is the midpoint of side AB. Point R on the side of the CD is chosen such that 2CD=3RD. Lines AR and PD intersect at point Q. Find the area of ​​triangle APQ if AD=2BC.

5. Find all values ​​of $a$ for which the system
$$\begin{cases} 9x^2 - 6xy + y^2 + 6x - 13y + 3 = 0, \\ 13x^2 + 6xy + 10y^2 + 16x + 2y - 4ax - 6ay + a^2 - 2a + 3 = 0 \end{cases}$$
has at least one solution.
