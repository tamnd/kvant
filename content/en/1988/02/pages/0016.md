⟦folio 14⟧

Mathematics and position, physicists and lyricists... No, «Kvant» do not intend to fan the smoldering flame of this polemic. For example, we believe that the deep natural principle inherent in the existence of the mathematical sciences brings them closer to both music and position. Proof of this is the articles published below. Their authors - well-known research mathematicians - act in an unexpected role of “researcher position”. The first article describes what unexpected works arise from poses when they turn to scientific topics in their creativity. And the second demonstrates the originality of the formal-mathematical approach to the theory of versification. In it, the author explores the structure of the rule for determining the size of the Russian style.

MATHEMATICAL IMAGERY IN POETRY
