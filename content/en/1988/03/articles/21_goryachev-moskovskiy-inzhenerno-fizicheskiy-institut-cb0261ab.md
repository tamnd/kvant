Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$\log_3(2 \sin x - 1 + 18 \sin^2 x) = -\log_3(1 - 7 \sin x).$$

2. Two workers, working at the same time, do the same job in 15 minutes. How long will it take the second worker to complete this work alone, if it is known that the first worker will complete it m hours faster than the second?

3. Solve the inequality
$$(c+1) < (c+2) \cdot 3^{\sqrt{x-1}}.$$

4. A regular quadrangular paramid SPQRT is intersected by a plane equidistant from the points S, P, Q, R, T. Determine the cross-sectional area of ​​a ball inscribed in the paramid SPQRT by this plane if the height of the paramid SO has length h and the angle SPQ is equal to α.

Option 2

1. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 - 7,5x + 14} \log_2|x - 3| \leq 0.$$

2. Two vessels with a capacity of 5 liters each contain a solution of silk. The first vessel contains 3 liters of p-percentage (by volume) solution of sulfur, the second - 4 liters of 2p-percentage solution of the same sulfur. How many liters from the second vessel must be poured into the first to obtain a 10% solution of silage in it?

3. Solve the system of equations

$$\begin{cases}
(a^2 - a) \sin \left(\frac{x}{2}\right) + 2 \cos y = a + 5, \\
3 \sin \left(\frac{x}{2}\right) + \cos y = 4, \\
a \in \mathbb{R}.
\end{cases}$$

4. In a regular quadrangular paramid SABCD, the angle BSD formed by two opposite lateral edges SB and SD is equal to 2α. Through point A, parallel to straight line BD, a plane π is drawn, intersecting the common line of planes ASC and BSD at point P, distant from straight line SB at a distance d>0. Determine the radius of the sphere circumscribed about the paramide SABCD if the plane π forms an angle of magnitude α with the plane of the base ABCD.

Oral exam objectives

At some moment it appears, and the ball begins to move with acceleration a = 2 m/s². Find the maximum speed that the ball acquires during its movement if the distance between the points of fastening of the springs does not exceed twice the length of the undeformed spring. To shave with the force of gravity.

2. The washer lies in the end part at a distance r = 20 cm from the vertical axis of the cone (Fig. 2). The angle between the generatrix and the axis of the cone is α = 60°, the friction coefficient between the washer and the bowl is μ = 0.8. At what angular speed should the clock be rotated around its axis for the puck to fly out of the bowl?

3. An alpha particle with kinetic energy W hits the initially forged nucleus of the atoma gel. At what energy will the particles coalesce to a distance r = 1·10⁻¹³ m? Keep in mind that at the moment of maximum convergence the velocities of both particles are the same.

4. A cylinder with a base area S = 100 cm² contains gas at a temperature t = 27 °C. At a height h = 30 cm from the base of the cylinder there is a mass m = 60 kg. How much work will the gas do during expansion if its temperature is slowly increased by Δt = 50 °C? Atmospheric pressure p₀ = 1.0·10⁵ Pa.

5. Along two parallel conductors located at a distance of l = 0.5 m from each other, a jumper conductor (Fig. 3) is moved at a constant speed v = 10 m/s. Between the left ends of the conductors, two capacitors are connected in series, and the capacitance C₂ is n = 1.5 times greater than the capacitance C₁. The entire system is in a uniform magnetic field directed perpendicular to the plane in which the conductors lie. Find the magnetic field indication if the capacitance on the capacitor C₂ is in the direction of U₂ = 0.5 V. The resistance of the conductors is negligible.
