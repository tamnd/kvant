Black must move pawns b7 and c7, connecting the rook to protect the gray rank. But at the same time the rook is rummaged along other ranks, which decides the matter. 1...b6 2. Ke7! (knight comes back) and 3. Nc6×; 1...b5 2. Rs3! and 3. Rlc5×, 1...c6 2. Ce7! and 3. Cd6×, 1...c5 2. Re8! and 3. Jd5×.
