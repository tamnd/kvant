How to connect three bars crosswise? Our ancestors had to solve this problem back in ancient times, when they began to build the first dwellings. Since then, hundreds of different methods have been invented: bars are tied, hooked, knocked together, screwed, glued, welded. Some connections, invented a long time ago, are so original and ingenious that they turned into entertaining puzzles. We suggest that you can easily make two of them yourself from square-section wooden blocks. The cuts in the bars must be made accurately and carefully - only in this case the assembled unit will be strong. In the notes about puzzles that we have been publishing since the end, we mainly talked about the structure of puzzles and solution algorithms. We can say that problems from “puzzle algebra” were discussed.
Now we offer the reader a task from the reader. In the first version, the cross is assembled from three bars with different configurations of cutouts; on figure they are shown on the left. The secret is that one of the bars can rotate around the longitudinal axis. Due to this, it is possible to assemble and disassemble the unit. In the second version, the cross is made up of three identical bars (picture on the upper right). But no matter how we add two of them, it is impossible to add a third to them. Therefore, it is necessary to simultaneously assemble all three bars to the center of the knot. Elements of "geometry puzzles" puzzles. Try to come up with a way to crosswise connect three round bars with cutouts. The cutout designs proposed above are not suitable for this. The assembly must be collapsible, consist of three elements and have the appearance shown in figure on the right, without additional visible cut lines. Of the various solutions, it is better to consider the one in which there is less empty space inside the node. We admit that we do not know whether this problem has been solved.
