Tasks

1. How to transfer a goat, a cabbage, two wolves and a dog in a boat from one berg to another, if it is known that the wolf cannot be left unattended with the goat and the dog, the dog is “in a quarrel” with the goat, and the goat is “not indifferent” to the cabbage? There are only three seats in the boat, so you can take no more than two animals or one animal and cabbage with you at a time.

2. 30 people took part in modern shooting events. The first shooter scored 80 points, the second shot 60 points, the third shot the arithmetic average of the first two points, the fourth - the arithmetic mean of the points scored by the previous shooters. How many points did the last shooter score?

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown on figure by substituting four digits together with the letters H, and odd numbers together with the letters H.

4. Why do ice skaters and speed skaters throw a coat or blanket over themselves after the finish, even though they were very hot during the race?

5. The plane is divided into squares with an area of ​​100 cm². How to use one ruler (without divisions) to obtain a square with an area of ​​80 cm²?

These tasks were proposed to us by: E. V. Chernyshov, N. Ya. Antonovich, A. P. Savin, V. V. Proizvolov.
