⟦folio 6⟧

Additional entrance exams, which end on August 29... The girl advised me to write an application not for enrollment as a student, but for permission to take the entrance exams again. I also wrote a statement. She herself responded to his proposal to the selection committee and after a while brought my application with a positive resolution. Looking at me, she doubtfully asked whether I would really be able to pass all the exams in the remaining few hours - there were five of them!

...I headed to the first of the indicated audiences. There was a math exam going on. A respectable man in a naval uniform sat at the table. Later I found out that this was the famous professor R. O. Kuzmin, who lectured on higher mathematics at the Faculty of Karabakh (he wore a naval uniform). On the table in front of him was Bychkov's problem book in mathematics. The professor opened a scientific problem book and suggested solving two quadratic equations with two unknowns. I looked at the problem and immediately, without solving it, told him the answer. He checked the answer and asked how I solved the problem so quickly. I told him that the problem was simple and could be solved in a simple manner. I must admit to you that this was not true. The explanation was that I have a good memory, and I completed all the problems in this textbook... and memorized the answers.

Then Professor Kuzmin offered me another problem - three quadratic equations with three unknowns. I again gave him the answers, without solving this system, I said that such a problem could easily be solved in ume. This, of course, was also untrue. Then he closed the problem book and asked me to calculate the logarithm of a number, which I did successfully, in the normal way. After asking me a few oral questions on geometry, to which I answered correctly, he asked where I had studied and then gave me a grade of “V” (very satisfactory)... The math exam lasted about fifteen minutes. From there I ran to the classroom where the physics exam was taking place. This exam took 15-20 minutes and I also received a "B" grade.

The exams in chemistry and political education were also successful.

The last exam in literature remains. For this exam, we gathered all those who took the exam into one auditorium. The exam was taken by two school teachers (there was no such subject at the Polytechnic Institute). They wrote on the board topics for essays to choose from for the exam.
