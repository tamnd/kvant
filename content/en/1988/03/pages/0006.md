...I graduated from school at the age of fifteen. By this time, I had already decided exactly in which higher educational institution I would continue my education. The fact is that shortly before this, an article by the outstanding physicist Academician Abram Fedorovich Ioffe appeared in one of the central newspapers about his main department of physics and mechanics at the Leningrad Polytechnic Institute. M.I. Kalinina. After reading this article, I firmly decided to enroll only in this faculty. But... youth were accepted into the vocational schools starting from the age of seventeen. Therefore, I decided to enter the Pskov Digging School - one of the fifth schools that existed in the country. It was the best of the secondary technical institutions in Pskov. Unfortunately, the wonderful environment in which the school was located is no longer there. I easily passed the exams and was accepted into the third year of the school (later it became known as the Land Management Technical School). However, the reason why I chose this school was that, given the relatively significant unemployment that then existed in the country, there were not enough land surveyors.

I will not dwell on the years spent at this school, but will limit myself to pointing out that my good physics and mathematics training helped me graduate from school with the highest grades.

...I was already 17 years old then, and I had the right to enter the Polytechnic Institute of my choice, the Faculty of Physics and Mechanics. ...Perhaps you will be interested to know how my entrance exams for this faculty went. In those days, the rules for passing exams for admission to universities changed frequently. In particular, in 1925, for those entering Moscow and Leningrad universities, the rules stipulated that persons who graduated from secondary schools or secondary technical educational institutions that year and wished to enter universities in the same year took exams in their region or provincial cities. Persons who graduated from secondary educational institutions before 1925 were examined at the admissions committees of colleges in the same way as now).

Thus, I have to take exams at home in Pskov. For the Pskov province, two places were allocated for the Faculty of Physics and Mechanics that I had chosen.
