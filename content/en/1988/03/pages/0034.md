Wonderful numbers.
Fibonacci numbers

Fibonacci numbers are the elements of the number sequence 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ..., in which each number, starting from the third, is the sum of the two previous ones: $f_n = f_{n-1} + f_{n-2}$

These numbers were introduced by the Taliban mathematician Leonardo of Pisa (Fibonacci) in the “Book of Abacus” (1202). He obtained them as the number of the family of cutters descending from one pair, provided that each pair of cutters produces a new pair every month.

It turns out that Fibonacci numbers arise in a wide variety of areas of life. For example, if you walk along a path divided into $n$ squares, each time moving to the next square or through one, then the number of ways to pass such a path is $f_n$.

Fibonacci numbers appear in a spiral, and the number of spirals going in one direction and the other are different - they are successive Fibonacci numbers (for example, there can be 34 and 55 spirals). The same is observed in pineapple fruits, where there are generally 8 and 14 spirals.
