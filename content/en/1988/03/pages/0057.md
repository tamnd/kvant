Option 1

1. In the middle of a pipe of length 2L and cross-section $S$, closed at the ends, there is a piston. To the left and right of the piston there are different gases at the same pressure $p$. How much will the piston move if it becomes permeable to one of the gases? The friction force of the piston on the pipe is $F$. The temperature of the gases is assumed to be constant. (51%)

2. A narrow tube of constant cross-section forms a square on the $l$ side, fixed in the vertical plane (Fig. 1). The tube is filled with equal volumes of two liquids with densities $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$ that do not penetrate into each other. Initially, a denser liquid filled the upper part of the tube. At some point the liquids began to move. Find their maximum speed. There is no friction. Free pressure acceleration $g$. (55%).

3. A wire ring of radius $R$ has a conductive jumper located along the diameter. The left and right received included capacitors $C_1$ and $C_2$. The ring is placed in a magnetic field with induction $B(t) = B_0 t / T$ that increases linearly with time, perepidicular to its plane. At some point, the jumper is removed and then the field is stopped changing. Find the charges installed on the capacitors. (29%)

4. Estimate how fast an astronaut in a light, comfortable spacesuit can walk on the Moon. (37%)

5. Two identical bottles are filled with water. A tube is inserted into one of them, the counter tubes of which are closed with a finger. The buds are simultaneously digested and at the same moment the response tubes open. Water flows out noticeably faster from a bottle with a tube. Explain the phenomenon. (91%)

Option 2

1. A washer of mass $m$ (Fig. 2) lies on a plane forming an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon. What is the minimum force that must be applied to the puck in the horizontal direction along the plane for it to move? The coefficient of friction is $\mu$. (49%)

2. Two parallel plate capacitors of capacitance $C$ of each connector are parallel and charged to voltage $U$. The plates of one of the capacitors can move freely towards each other. Find their speed at the moment when the gap between the parallel capacitors is halved. The mass of each parallel is $M$. Force gravity is not taken into account. (55%)

3. In a cylindrical pipe at distances $L$ and $2L$ from the closed end to the right of it there are two pistons that can over-

flutter without friction. The left compartment contains water vapor at a pressure of $p_0$, and the right compartment contains air at the same pressure. The saturated vapor pressure of water is $2p_0$. The right piston was slowly screwed in to a distance $a$. How far will the left piston move? The temperature of water vapor and air is considered constant. (39%)

4. The lumberjack hit the log with his ax. The ax got stuck, going halfway into the block (Fig. 3). Estimate the force with which the ax blade is removed. (36%)

5. The laser beam falls on a transparent plane-parallel plate, one surface of which is painted so that it is capable of scattering light in all directions. The following picture is visible on the plate: a light dot in the center, a dark circle with a sharply defined border, and a light halo behind the circle. Explain the phenomenon. (27%)
