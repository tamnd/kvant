Novosibirsk State University named after. Lenin Komsomol

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics, Mathematics and Economics)

1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{3 - 5 \sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) - 6 \cos 2x}{5} = x - \frac{\pi}{3}.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\log_2 \frac{4}{x}} > \frac{1}{\log_2 \frac{x}{2}} - 1.$$
3. From the point $A$ to a circle of radius $R$ a tangent $AM$ is drawn ($M$ is the point of tangency). A secant passing through the point $A$ intersects the circle at points $K$ and $L$, and $L$ is the midpoint of the segment $AK$, the angle $AMK$ is equal to $60^\circ$. Find the area of ​​triangle $AMK$.
4. At what values ​​of the parameter $a$ can the system of inequalities be solved
$$x^2 + (a+4)x + 4a \leq y,$$
$$3x + y - (2a+4) \leq 0$$
contains the segment $[-2; -1]$ of axis $Ox$?
5. Point $M$ is the middle of edge $AD$ of a single cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$. A plane is drawn through the middle of the segment $B'M$ redistributed to it. Find the distance from the center of the cube to this plane.
Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Find all solutions of the equation $\sin x + \cos 3x = 0$ that satisfy the inequality
$$\sin \frac{x}{2} + \cos \frac{x}{2} > 0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\log_2 \left(x^2 - x + 1\right)} + 1 \geq \frac{\log_2 \left(x + 3\right)}{\log_2 \left(x^2 - x + 1\right)}.$$
3. Two circles of radii 3 and 4, the distance between their centers being 5, intersect at points $A$ and $B$. Through the point $B$ a line is drawn intersecting the circles at points $C$ and $D$ so that $CD = 8$ and $B$ lies between $C$ and $D$. Find the area of ​​triangle $ACD$.
4. Prinayaka touches the parabolas $y = -x^2 + 2x + 2$ at point $A$ and intersects the $Ox$ axis at $B$ and the $Oy$ axis at $C$. It is known that the point $A$ lies in the 1st quarter of the coordinate plane and $2AB = AC$. Find the tangent equation.

5. In the triangular paramid $SABC$, the edges $AB, BC$ and $BS$ have length 2 and are mutually perpendicular. A plane is drawn through the midpoints of edges $AC$ and $SB$, intersecting edge $AB$ and forming equal angles with the planes of faces $ABS$ and $ABC$. Find the magnitudes of these angles.

Option 3
(Faculty of Natural Sciences and Geology-Geophysical Faculty)

1. Solve the equation
$$(13x^2 + 2x - 14) \cdot \arccos x = 0.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$2^2 \sin 2x - 2 \cos 2x + 3 - \left(\frac{1}{6}\right) \cos 2x - \sin 2x - \log_6 14 + 3^2 \sin 2x - 2 \cos 2x + 1 = 0.$$
3. At what value of the height of a straight line does a trapezoid with an acute angle $30^\circ$ and perimeter 6 have the most plane?
4. There are three pieces of copper alloy with lower aluminum in the ratios of 2:1, 3:1 and 5:1 by weight. Of these, a piece weighing 12 kg was fused with a copper ratio of 4:1. Find the mass of each original piece if the mass of the first was twice the mass of the second.
5. Paramida $ABCD$ is given. The edge $DC$ is redistributed to the plane of the base $ABC, AB = 3\sqrt{3}, BC = 3$, the angle $ACB$ is equal to $\pi/3, DC = \sqrt{13}$. A sphere of radius 5 with center at vertex $D$ is drawn. Find the length of the line of intersection of the sphere with the base $ABC$.

Physics
Written exam
Faculty of Physics
Each variant is a state of five tasks of three types.
The first three tasks are computational, of varying difficulty: from standard ones to relatively complex ones, requiring ingenuity, deep knowledge, and the ability to understand an unusual or conventional physical situation.
The fourth task is an assessment task. To solve it, it is necessary to obtain the physical phenomenon under consideration, formulate a simple (since only an estimate is needed) physical model of this phenomenon, select reasonable numerical values ​​of physical quantities and, finally, obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. The text of the problem supports that the applicant can choose the quantities necessary to solve the problem and their numerical values.
The fifth task is a demonstration task, in which you need to explain a physical phenomenon demonstrated in the classroom. Here it is important to understand the essential phenomena and environment of various factors to highlight the main one.
Five hours were given to solve the problem, starting from the end of the demonstration.
After the text of each problem, the percentage of those who solved it is indicated in parentheses.
