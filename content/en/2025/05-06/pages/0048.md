4. The chess king was placed on a square of the board 8 and 8 and made 64 moves so that he visited all the squares and returned to the original square. At each moment of time, the distance from the center of the square in which the king was located to the center of the entire board was calculated. Let us call a move made pleasant if, as a result of the move, this distance became less than it was before the move. Find the greatest possible number of pleasant moves. (The chess king moves in one move to a square adjacent to the side or corner.) S. Tokarev
