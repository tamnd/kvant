7. The numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 60 are written on the line (in exactly that order). Igor and Ruslan take turns putting “+”, “–” and “u” signs between them, Igor begins; Each player places one sign per turn. When a sign is placed between each two adjacent numbers, the value of the resulting expression is calculated. If it is divisible by 3, then the victory is awarded to Igor, otherwise - to Ruslan. Which player can win, regardless of the opponent's actions? I. Efremov

5. Given a natural number n. The natural numbers 1, 2, ..., n are written on the board on a line in some order. For every two adjacent numbers, their GCD (greatest common divisor) is calculated and this GCD is written down on a piece of paper. What is the largest number of different numbers that can be among all n – 1 numbers written on the piece of paper? I. Bogdanov

An expert is recognized as competent if he correctly guesses the number of gold medals for at least one athlete. For what is the largest k that experts can make predictions such that at least k of them will be recognized as competent, regardless of the outcome of the competition? M. Fedotova
