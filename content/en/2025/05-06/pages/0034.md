BIG AND SMALL in PHYSICS

Reflections on the microscopically small and the infinitely large - from atoms to the Universe - have excited the minds of not only scientists since ancient times. The integral elements of the picture of the world attracted mystery and intricacy, and still not everyone manages to make friends with them, which sometimes leads to incidents and paradoxes. However, science not only “saddled” them, but also made them a working tool, including for schoolchildren. Without them, you cannot pave the way to continue your studies - so comprehend the wisdom of zero and infinity, because where only they do not make themselves felt when mastering exact disciplines!
