Irina Aleksandrovna Tyulina volunteered for the front in 1941 as a second year student at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University. After completing nursing courses, she took part in the Battle of Moscow and fought on the 2nd Belorussian Front in Belarus and Poland. The war ended in Germany on the Elbe River. She was awarded orders and medals. After the war, Irina Aleksandrovna returned to the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics and successfully graduated. She became a mechanical scientist, a specialist in the history and methodology of mechanics.
