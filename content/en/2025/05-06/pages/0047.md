The final stage of the LI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The final stage of the LI All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad took place from April 16 to April 22, 2025 on the Sirius Federal Territory. The participants noted that the organizing committee, with the support of traditional partners of the All-Russian Olympiad, achieved a very high quality of holding the Olympiad. “Sirius” brought together about 500 of the country’s best young mathematicians; six schoolchildren from China also took part in the Olympiad online, this year from Sichuan province. Representatives of 29 regions of Russia, a total of 221 Olympiad participants, were awarded winner or prize-winner diplomas.
