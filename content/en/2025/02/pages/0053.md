And this means that we need to understand what the mechanism of elasticity is and what is the reason for such a possibility of rubber as huge (hundreds of percent) elastic relative deformations, and at the same time (as experiment shows) the volume occupied by rubber in space is preserved with good accuracy. By definition, rubber is a highly elastic polymer material obtained by vulcanization of rubber, i.e. by mixing it with a vulcanizing agent, usually sulfur, and then heating it. As is known, rubber is a substance consisting of very long molecules that are not straightened, but “crumpled”, while the ends of such long molecular chains, consisting of a large number of atoms linked to each other, are connected to each other in some places by “bridges” of sulfur atoms. Relative deformations of rubber in selected directions can be characterized by hundreds of percent precisely because the distance between the points of attachment of the ends of a long chain in a curled ball is much less than the length of this chain if it is straightened.
