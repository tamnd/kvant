If white tin is a plastic substance, then gray tin is a powder. And solder seams can be destroyed at low temperatures if the soldering contains more tin than lead. our days - on January 28, 1986, the American space shuttle Challenger crashed a few seconds after launch. Seven astronauts (including two women) were killed. The cause of the accident was the leakage of combustion products of solid rocket fuel through the sealing rings, the material of which changed its mechanical properties due to the unusually low air temperature at Cape Canaveral (8 q C, but usually it was above zero). The Challenger rocket stood at the launch site for several days at this frosty temperature, and this was enough to change the shape of the rings. It was known that many polymers change their properties at temperatures of liquid nitrogen (196 q C). For example, rubber becomes brittle and rubber parts break into pieces as if they were made of glass. But no one expected that something similar could happen to plastic at slightly frosty temperatures. For a long time, the government commission could not find the cause of the accident. It was only when the famous physicist Richard Feynman (1918–1988) was included in the commission at the insistence of President Ronald Reagan that he found this reason and demonstrated at a press conference the loss of the shape of the ring when lowered into ice water. In order to discover this cause of the accident, Feynman had to carefully analyze the manufacturing technology of all rocket engine parts and their technical testing. He carried out this gigantic work in between several oncological operations. “I worked without knowing anything,” Feynman later said, “and I had only a little time to correct it.” Some biographers of Feynman believe that the main role in the fact that he completed this research was played not even by the president’s personal request, but by Feynman’s loving wife, who believed that hard work was the best way to fight the disease, and persuaded her husband to take on this work.
