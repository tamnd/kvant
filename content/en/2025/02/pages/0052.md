Let the tangents to the circumcircle of triangle ABC, drawn at the vertices, intersect at points A c, B c, C c. Then, by fact 9, the radical center U of the semicircles of the triangle $^{A}$ $c^{B} c^{C} c$ lies on the Euler line of triangle ABC. When inverted with respect to the circumcircle, the semi-circles $треугольника^{A} c^{B} c^{C} c$ turn into Why-circles of triangle ABC. By Lemma 2, the radical center of U, the inversion center, and the radical center of Why-circles lie on the same straight line. However, the first two of the listed points lie on the Euler line, so the third one also lies.
