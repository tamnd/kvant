Theoretical tour

7th grade

1. Cable car On a horizontal cable car there are sections (track) where the car moves at a speed of $v_{тр}$ relative to the ground, and stations with platforms along which the car moves more slowly at a speed of $v_{cт} v_{тр}$

⟦figure⟧

Fig. 1

(Fig. 1, top view). Switching gears occurs instantly when the trailer is exactly above the edge of the platform. At the station, the speed of two carriages always changes simultaneously and at this moment there are 5 more carriages between them. An equal distance l = 6 m is maintained between adjacent trailers at the station. The time of slow movement of the trailer along the station from one edge of the platform to the other is W = 2 minutes. The distance between the trailers on the route is s = 40 m; you can neglect the dimensions of the trailers themselves. 1) Find the length L of the platform. 2) What is the speed $v_{cт}$ of the movement of the trailers at the station? 3) What is the speed $v_{тр}$ of the movement of the trailers on the highway? S. Karmazin
