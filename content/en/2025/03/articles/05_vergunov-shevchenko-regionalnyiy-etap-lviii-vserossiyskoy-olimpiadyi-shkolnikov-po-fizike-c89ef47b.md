7th grade Task 1. An incident on an escalator Experimenter Gluck and theorist Bug simultaneously stepped on parallel escalators in the subway. Glitch goes from top to bottom, Bug goes from bottom to top. Both travel at constant speeds as they move. By the time the friends caught up, Gluck counted $N_{1} = 144$ steps, and Bug counted $N_{2} = 48$ steps. 1) Who moves faster relative to the escalator, Glitch or Bug, and by how much? 2) How many steps $N_{4}$ will Bug count during the entire movement, if Gluck counted $N_{3} = 216$ steps during the movement? 3) How many N steps can you count if you walk along a standing escalator? Escalators are the same length and move at the same speed. A. Evseev
