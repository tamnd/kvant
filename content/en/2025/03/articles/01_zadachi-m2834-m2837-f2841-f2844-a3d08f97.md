PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2834–M2837 were offered at the regional stage of the LI All-Russian Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren.

Problem M2834–M2837, F2841–2844

M2834. Let's call a set of numbers lucky if it cannot be divided into two non-empty groups so that the product of the sum of the numbers in one group and the sum of the numbers in the other is positive. The teacher wrote several integers on the board. Prove that the children can add one more integer to the existing ones so that the resulting set is successful. A. Kuznetsov

M2835. In each square of the board 2 u 200 there is a ruble coin. Dasha and Sonya play, taking turns making moves, Dasha starts. In one move, you can select any coin and move it: Dasha moves the coin to the diagonally adjacent cell, Sonya - to the adjacent one on the side. If two coins end up in the same cell, one of them is immediately removed from the board and goes to Sonya. Sonya can stop the game at any time and take all the coins received. What is the biggest win she can get, no matter how Dasha plays? A. Kuznetsov
