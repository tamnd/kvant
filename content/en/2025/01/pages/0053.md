In the first part of this problem, we will look at the ion trapping technique known as the Paul trap. For this work, Wolfgang Paul and Hans Demelt received half of the 1989 Nobel Prize in Physics. Next, we explore the Doppler cooling technique, one of the works mentioned in the press release for the 1997 Nobel Prize in Physics, awarded to Steven Chu, Claude Cohen-Tannoudji, and William Phillips “for their development of methods for cooling and confining atoms using laser radiation.”
