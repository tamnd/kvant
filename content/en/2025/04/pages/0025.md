F2847. A cylindrical vessel installed vertically in a gravity field contains a monatomic ideal gas under a massive horizontal piston of mass m = 100 kg. There is a vacuum above the piston. The piston is in equilibrium and can move along the walls of the vessel with virtually no friction. The piston is fixed in this equilibrium position, after which a weight of mass M is placed on it. Then the piston is released. With further movement of the piston with the weight, its minimum distance $h_{min}$ to the bottom of the vessel is half the initial distance $h_{0} = 1,0$ m. Determine: 1) the mass of the weight M; 2) maximum acceleration module $a_{max}$ of the piston with a weight during movement; 3) maximum speed module $v_{max}$ of the piston with a weight during movement. After a long time, the piston stopped. 4) Find the distance h to the bottom of the vessel where the piston will be in a state of new thermodynamic equilibrium. The heat capacity of the vessel and piston is negligible, and there is no heat transfer to the environment. When the piston moves from its initial position to its first stop, the processes in the gas can be considered equilibrium. The mass of the gas is much less than the mass of the piston.
