2 (8). A paper triangle with sides a, b, c was bent in a straight line so that the vertex opposite side c fell on this side. It is known that in the resulting quadrilateral two angles adjacent to the fold line are equal. Find the lengths of the segments into which side c is divided by the vertex that falls there. A. Shapovalov

3 (7–8). A polygon was painted on checkered paper, the perimeter of which runs along the grid lines. Is it always possible to cut a rectangle of the same perimeter containing it along the grid lines? V. Senderov

4 (7–9). An abstract artist wants to paint the cells of a board 8 ѣ 8 in m > 1 colors so that the condition is satisfied: if identically colored cells A and B border on cell C, then two more cells border on C, painted identically, and in a color different from the colors of A and B. (Bordering cells are those that have a common side.) For what m can he do this? E. Barabanov, I. Akulich

5 (7–8). All integer points are marked on the number line. Points x and y are connected by an arc if |x – y| prime number. What is the smallest number of colors that can be used to paint all the whole points so that any two connected points are of different colors? V. Hurowitz

6 (7–8). Find all prime triples satisfying the equality

$x^{3} + y^{3} = 2 z^{3}$.

V. Senderov

7 (7–9). 20 teams participated in the one-round football tournament. It turned out that if any two teams played a draw with each other, then at least one of them ended in a draw of no more than three games. What is the greatest possible number of draws in such a tournament? I. Akulich

8 (8). Some of the sides and diagonals of a convex n triangle (n > 3), no three diagonals of which intersect at one point, are colored red or blue. It is known that the blue segments do not intersect and there is a single blue path between any vertices. The same is true for the red segments.
