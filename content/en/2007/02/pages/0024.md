KVANT 2007/№2

The final stage of the competition named after A.P. Savin

"Math 6–8"

The recreation center "Berendeevy Polyany" near Sudislavl, Kostroma region, has already become a traditional venue for the final competitions of the A.P. Savin competition. In the summer of 2006, the XII final summer tournament of mathematical battles took place here, which once again broke the record for the number of participating teams: thirty-six. Schoolchildren from fifteen cities attended the tournament. Separate teams arrived from Ivanovo, Kirov, Kostroma, Magnitogorsk, Perm, Troitsk, Kharkov. And, of course, Moscow presented the most teams. A notable feature of the latest tournaments is that the competitions have become attractive to ninth-graders. In addition to the magazine «Kvant», the organization of the tournament was attended by the Moscow City Palace of Children's (Youth) Creativity (chairman of the tournament organizing committee G. Kondakov), the educational program “Big Change” (program director, deputy of the regional Duma and chairman of the Federation of Trade Unions of the Kostroma Region M.A. Batin), the Department of General and Professional Education of the Administration of the Kostroma Region, the Foundation for Mathematical Education and Enlightenment. Several local organizations also provided sponsorship to the teams. Thus, Chernogolovka schoolchildren express their gratitude to the United Trade Union Organization of the Chernogolovka Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences (deputy chairman L.A. Kovaleva). The work of the methodological commission was headed by A. Shapovalov. The commission included experienced jury members who had repeatedly taken part in such events: A. Akopyan, M. Bershtein, A. Blinkov (deputy chairman of the organizing committee), Y. Blinkov, A. Gorskaya, V. Gurovits, A. Zhukov, D. Kalinin, T. Karavaeva, I. Raskina, V. Senderov, A. Skopenkov, A. Spivak, S. Tokarev (organizer of the first summer tournaments), V. Trushkov, B. Frenkin, E. Chernysheva, V. Sharich. Team leaders also took an active part in the work of the jury:
