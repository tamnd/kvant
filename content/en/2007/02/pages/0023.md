Three people with a washing machine want to cross the river. The boat can accommodate either two people and a washing machine, or three people. The trouble is that the washing machine is heavy, so only three people can load it into or out of the boat. Will they be able to cross? A. Shapovalov

2.

“...Senior military doctor Bauze from aabbb citizens caught abccc malingerers and would have caught the last one if this lucky guy had not had a stroke at the very moment when the doctor yelled at him: “All around!” ... In this excerpt from “The Adventures of the Good Soldier Schweik” we replaced all the numbers with Latin letters (same - identical, different - different). Can you determine how many malingerers the vigilant Doctor Bauze exposed? I. Akulich

King Arthur's shield has the shape of an octagon, which was formed from a square after cutting off identical isosceles triangles. Red, a symbol of courage, occupies the same area on the shield as blue, a symbol of justice. How to show this without resorting to calculations? V. Proizvolov

There are 27 students in class 8A. They were invited to attend singing, whistling and poetry reading groups. Everyone wants to attend one or more of these clubs. It turned out that more than a third of the class wanted to go to each circle. Is it possible to create such lists of clubs that everyone will go to exactly one club they want to join, and there will be an equal number of students in all clubs? D. Kalinin
