“Of the blue sea, the Old Man was catching fish with a net, the Old Woman...” Yes, she was spinning, as everyone knows, her own yarn. But not only that. It was on the shores of the Baikal Sea, there were a lot of omul fish there, so the Old Woman dried the fish, dried it, and salted it. And then I sold it to passing merchants. This is how they lived. Their hut was exactly in the place where the city of Slyudyanka now stands. There were rich deposits of amazing mica in those mountains. The region became especially famous during the time of Empress Anna Ioannovna. The Empress decided to surprise the whole world and build an unusual palace for her balls and parties. The palace was made of ice - there was ice there, chopped up on the Neva in winter. But in addition to ice, a lot of mica was also brought from the shores of Lake Baikal for its construction. Both in summer, on carts, and in winter, by sleigh, according to the Tsar's decree, convoys with mica were pulled to St. Petersburg. Mica slabs were used in construction secretly from foreigners. Ice house and ice house - that's what everyone was told. Only this house stood even in the summer and did not melt. Even then, rumors spread across Europe about Russian ice, which didn’t care about the hot sun! That mica was painfully transparent and clean, you couldn’t tell it from ice. The real eighth wonder of the world was created. After the death of the empress, the palace fell into disrepair, then it was dismantled, mica slabs and bricks were sawn into pieces, merchants sold them, and then many mica windows appeared in Rus'. But let's return to the Old Man and his Old Woman. Over the years, it became increasingly difficult for the Old Man to leave the shores on his boat. There was no longer enough strength to pull a net full of fish from the depths of Baikal. And most importantly, it became increasingly difficult for him to endure the cold coming from the icy Baikal water. There was nothing to do, the Old Man had to settle down with his fishing rod on the shore. It’s good for him to sit on the granite bank heated by the sun! Only the Old Woman began to scold: the Old Man began to bring home less fish. It is clear, of course, why. The fish is looking for something deeper... But the Old Woman does not let up: give her, Old Man, the same amount of fish as you caught before! The Old Man groaned and sighed, but there was nothing to do. We must fulfill the Starukhin's will.
