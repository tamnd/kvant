Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology (FZFTSH) at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is recruiting students from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation for the 2008/09 academic year. FZFTSH at MIPT has been operating as a state educational institution for specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 80 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its graduate. The school is financed by the Federal Education Agency. Education for students living in the Russian Federation, within the framework of the approved admission plan, is free. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (state university), which trains highly qualified specialists in modern areas of science and technology. Leading industry and academic research institutes and scientific and production associations of the country (base organizations of MIPT) take part in their preparation. Teaching at MIPT is taught by famous teachers and scientists, among whom there are about 100 members of the Russian Academy of Sciences. A Physics and Technology education allows you not only to work successfully in science, but also to navigate life well. The goal of our school is to help students interested in physics and mathematics to deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, as well as to contribute to the professional self-determination of students. Enrollment in grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2008/09 academic year is carried out for part-time, part-time and full-time courses.

Correspondence department (individual training) Tel./fax: (495) 408-51-45

Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics given below. The full training program lasts 4 years, i.e. for grades 8–11, but you can enroll in any of these classes. During the school year, in accordance with the FZFTS program, the student will receive assignments in physics and mathematics for each topic (4 assignments in each subject for grade 8, 6 - 7 assignments in each subject for grades 9, 10 and 11), and then - recommended author's solutions to these assignments along with tested work. The assignments contain theoretical material, analysis of typical examples and problems on the relevant topic, and 8–12 test questions and problems for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics and higher mathematics at MIPT. The work of part-time students is checked by students, graduate students and graduates of MIPT (80% of them are former graduates of our school). The deadline for sending the solution to the introductory task is no later than March 1, 2008. Entry papers will not be sent back. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 2008. Outside of competition, the FZFTS accepts the winners of regional, regional, republican, district and all-Russian Olympiads in physics and mathematics for the 2007/08 academic year. Before May 15, 2008, they must send to the FZFTSH the completed introductory work in physics and mathematics, along with copies of diplomas confirming participation in the above-mentioned Olympiads.
