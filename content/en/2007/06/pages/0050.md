Department of Economics

The department was founded in 1993. Training is carried out in two main programs: “Applied Economics” and “Economics and Geography”. The Applied Economics program includes the study of the fundamentals of economic theory, as well as an introduction to business practices in the business game of correspondence. Students of the “Economics and Geography” program, in addition to studying the fundamentals of economic theory, become familiar with physical and economic geography, and participate in absentia in exciting travels around the world. Those who complete one of the main programs are offered an elective specialization: “Fundamentals of Entrepreneurship and Management”, “Accounting and Financial Analysis”, “World Economy”, “Russian Economy: Past, Present and Future”. Anyone with an education of at least 7 years of secondary school is accepted into the department. Training is carried out either individually or in small groups (2 – 4 people). There is no “Collective student” form of education in the economics department. Students in grades 10–11 who want to prepare simultaneously for the entrance exams to the Faculty of Economics of Moscow State University and to pass the Unified State Exam for successful admission to other economic universities are offered a special program “Economics PLUS”, which includes, along with economic disciplines, an in-depth study of several additional subjects: mathematics, social studies, Russian language and literature. For schoolchildren who are interested in geographical science and intend to enroll in the geography department of Moscow State University or another university, there is the “Geography PLUS” program, created by teachers from the OL VZMSH and the geography department of Moscow State University based on the experience of preparatory courses in geography at Moscow University.
