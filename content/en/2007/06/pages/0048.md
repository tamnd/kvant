Department of Biology

The recruitment is announced for the 35th time. Admission is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of the introductory work. Schoolchildren who are studying in 8th or 9th grade this academic year, regardless of their place of residence, can take part in the competition. Eighth grade students need to solve problems 1–3 and one of problems 4, 5 below the introductory work, ninth graders need to solve problems 2, 3 and two of problems 4–6. Tasks

1. Despite the diversity of animals and birds (biologists count thousands of species of both), only a few of them have been domesticated by humans. What prevented the domestication of other species? Try to include as many reasons as possible. 2. In some cases, when growing a certain plant (on a large scale or on personal plots), a small number of plants of another species are planted along with it in the same area. For what purposes is this done? Offer as much explanation as possible. 3. Typically, the incubation period of the disease - the time between the entry of the pathogen into the body and the appearance of symptoms of the disease, accompanied by a clear deterioration in well-being - is several days. This situation seems quite natural - once in favorable conditions, the pathogen begins to multiply rapidly and soon reaches such a number that its impact on the physiological processes in the host organism becomes significant. However, there are also human infectious diseases for which the incubation period may be significantly longer. What diseases do you know about this? What reasons could such a delay be associated with for these diseases? 4. Not all applicants are accepted into sports sections and sports schools - many applicants are “rejected” by coaches immediately or after several classes. What considerations do coaches use in doing this? Of course, the answer to this question will depend on the sport. Therefore, consider several sports sections and give an answer for each of them. 5. At your disposal are medical and statistical data for the last few years on the frequency and causes of mortality, as well as various diseases among residents of a certain region. Based on this information, how can we determine the places of residence that are most hazardous to health? It is clear that you cannot simply choose the area with the maximum mortality rate - such a choice in some cases will turn out to be wrong. (By the way, why?) Which areas in your city or region (region, republic) are most dangerous to the health of their residents? Why do you think so? 6.
