when the voltage across the capacitor is maximum, its capacitance is abruptly reduced by a fraction $\varepsilon^{=} \Delta C C$. After a quarter of the period $\pi LC 2$ the capacitance is abruptly increased to its previous value, after another quarter of the period the capacitance is again abruptly reduced by a fraction $\varepsilon$ and so on. Under certain conditions, continuous electrical oscillations can be excited in the circuit. The circuit includes a nonlinear element (incandescent light bulb L), the current-voltage characteristic of which is presented in figure 12. Find the minimum value $\varepsilon_{min}$ at which undamped oscillations are excited in the circuit if L = 0.1 H, C $= 10^{-} 7$ F. Also find the amplitude $U_{0}$ of steady-state voltage oscillations on the light bulb if $\varepsilon^{=}$ 3%. Note. The necessary constructions should be performed directly on this figure. S.
