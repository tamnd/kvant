1.

In Sunny City, three batches of shorties were formed: screws, tongues and bolts. It turned out that for any three party members there is a party that includes at least two of them. Is it true that all members of one of the parties are members of one of the other two parties? D. Kalinin

4.

The column of recruit soldiers lined up in several identical ranks forming a rectangle. At the command “at attention”, some of the soldiers made a frightened turn to the right, some - to the left, others - around, and some were completely dumbfounded and remained motionless. Then, every second, the following happens: each recruit who finds himself face to face with another soldier makes a right turn. a) Prove that sooner or later such turns will stop. b) Will the turns stop if not both soldiers who find themselves face to face turn, but only one of them? I. Akulich
