Catalan conjecture

V. SENDEROV, B. FRANKIN are powers (greater than the first) of natural numbers. The resulting sequence is: 1, 4, 8, 9, 16, 25, 27, 32, 36, 49, 64, 81, ... The numbers 8 and 9 were not chosen by chance - these are neighboring natural numbers. The question arises: do neighboring powers of natural numbers still exist? This question was first posed by the Belgian mathematician E. Catalan: in 1844 he suggested that the equation

$x^{y} - z^{t} = 1$

(1) has a unique solution x = 3, y = 2, z = 2, t = 3 in natural numbers greater than one. This hypothesis lasted for almost 160 years, although many famous mathematicians tried to find approaches to its proof. In 2003, the Romanian mathematician P.
