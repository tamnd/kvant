Selected problems of the Moscow Physics Olympiad

First theoretical round

7th grade

1. It is most convenient to study Mars during opposition, when the Earth is between Mars and the Sun. Determine after what period of time the oppositions of Earth and Mars are repeated. Mars orbits the Sun in 687 Earth days, and Earth in 365 days. M.Romashka

2. There is a layer of snow with a thickness of h = 70 cm on the ground. The pressure of the snow on the ground (excluding atmospheric pressure) is p = 630 Pa. The weather is frosty and the snow is made of air and ice. Determine what percentage of the snow volume is occupied by ice and how much by air. Ice density $\rho_{л} = 0,9$ g $см^{3}$. Gravity acceleration g = 10 m $с^{2}$. M.Romashka

3. In a factory, a system of four pulleys and one cable attached to the ceiling is used to lift heavy workpieces, as shown in figure 1. With what force F must the end of the cable be pulled down to hold or slowly and evenly lift a workpiece whose weight is P? The sections of the cable that do not lie on blocks are horizontal or vertical; the weight of the blocks, cable and friction can be neglected. M. Semenov

4. A solid ball is suspended in a vessel by two light threads, as shown in figure 2. The free ends of the threads are fixed at the same height. After the vessel was filled with water and the ball was completely immersed in water, the tension of the threads did not change. Determine the density $\rho$ of the material from which the ball is made. The density of water is $\rho_{в} = 1000$ kg $м^{3}$. I.Gorbaty
