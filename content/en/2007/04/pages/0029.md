Competition named after A.P. Savin

"Math 6–8"

We are starting another competition to solve mathematical problems for students in grades 6–8. In this issue we have collected tasks from authors from around the world: the USA, Israel, Romania, Russia, Belarus. Send solutions to problems within a month after receiving this issue of the journal to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant» (marked “Competition “Mathematics 6–8”). Be sure to include your name, class, and home address. As before, we welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by mathematical clubs. We ask circle leaders to provide an email address or contact phone number. According to tradition, the winning clubs of the correspondence competition are invited to the final in-person tournament.

1. A numismatist laid out 100 coins from different countries in one line. It turned out that any two neighboring coins weigh almost the same, namely, the difference in their masses is strictly less than 0.01 grams. Will it always be possible to rearrange these coins in such a way and then arrange them in a circle so that the masses of any two adjacent coins on the circle differ by less than 0.02 grams? G.
