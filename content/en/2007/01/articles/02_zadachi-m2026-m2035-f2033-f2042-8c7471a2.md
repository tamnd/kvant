PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent no later than April 1, 2007 to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 1–2007” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2026” or “F2033”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2027–M2030, M2033 was offered at the XXVIII Tournament of Cities, problem M2031 was offered at the II All-Russian Olympiad in Geometry named after I.F. Sharygin.

Problem M2026 – M2035, F2033–F2042

M2026. On sides AB, BC, CD and DA of square ABCD, points P, M, N, Q are chosen, respectively, so that P MAN $= 45 ^\circ, PM P AN, AM P NQ$. Line segment PQ intersects AM and AN at points F and G, respectively. Prove that the area of ​​triangle AFG is equal to the sum of the areas of triangles FMP and GNQ. V.Proizvolov

M2027. Three natural numbers x, y, z are written on the board. Petya writes down the product of any two of these numbers on a piece of paper, and on the board he reduces the third number by 1. With the new three numbers on the board, he again performs the same operation, etc. until one of the numbers on the board becomes zero. What will be the sum of the numbers on the sheet at this moment? E. Gorsky, S. Dorichenko

M2028. It is known that liars always lie, truthful people always tell the truth, and cunning people can both lie and tell the truth. You can ask questions that have a “yes” or “no” answer (for example: “is it true that this person is cunning?”). a) There are three in front of you - a liar, a truthful one and a cunning one, who know which one is which. How can you find out? b) There are four people in front of you - a liar, a truthful one and two cunning ones (all four know which one is which). Prove that cunning people can agree to answer in such a way that you, asking these four, will not know for sure who he is. B. Ginzburg, M. Gerver

M2030. Is it possible to inscribe a regular octahedron into a cube so that the vertices of the octahedron are on the edges of the cube? L. Radzivilovsky

M2031. The lines containing the medians of triangle ABC intersect its circumcircle $\omega$ for the second time at points $A_{1}, B_{1}, C_{1}$. Lines passing through A, B, C and parallel to opposite sides intersect $\omega$ a second time at points $A_{2}, B_{2}, C_{2}$. Prove that the lines $A_{1} A_{2}, B_{1} B_{2}, C_{1} C_{2}$ intersect at one point. A. Zaslavsky

M2032. a) Is there a natural number n such that for any natural number k at least one of the numbers $n^{k} - 1, n^{k} + 1$ has the form $a^{b}$ for some natural numbers a and b > 1? b*) Let us call a natural number antiprime if it is divisible by the square of any of its prime divisors. We call two natural numbers twins if they differ by 2. Are there finite or infinitely many pairs of antiprime twin numbers? V. Senderov
