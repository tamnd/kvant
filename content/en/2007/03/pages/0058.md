The most famous international radio-controlled balloon competition is held annually in Brigachtal, a small village in south-eastern Germany. At the Royal Aeronautical Fiesta in Switzerland, radio-controlled balloons are included in an independent program. Up to forty or more models take part in such competitions, which attract hundreds of spectators. But most often, 5-7 models participate in small friendly meetings of balloonists, of which several dozen are held in Europe every year. For the first time in Russia, competitions of radio-controlled models of hot air balloons were held on January 30, 2007 at the Moscow boarding school “Intellectual” (school 1128) for the School Cup. The name of the competition “Christmas Fiesta” corresponded to the scheduled date of January 12, but due to weather conditions the fiesta was postponed several times. The idea of ​​holding the fiesta belonged to the director of the school E.V. Markelov, and was brought to life thanks to the efforts of the director of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky V.D. Boksha and the leaders of aeromodeling circles of Moscow schools V.I. Belyakov-Bodin (school 1071) and A.V. Efimov (school 1128). The first step towards the plan was a two-hour lesson held at the Intellectual school by the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky for high school students. An ordinary physics lesson devoted to Archimedes' law has turned into an interesting, educational lesson that goes far beyond the scope of the school curriculum, with an illustration of the main provisions of the law using the example of the flight of a hot air balloon. Three teams took part in the competition: teams from Moscow schools “Intellectual” and “School of Health” (school 1071), as well as a team of schoolchildren from Sergiev Posad. The delight of the participants and numerous spectators knew no bounds. It is a pity that for technical reasons teams from Syktyvkar and gymnasium 33 in the city of Udelnaya could not participate in the fiesta. The financial problems of the fiesta were resolved thanks to the sponsorship of the Dynasty Charitable Foundation.

Captains (from left to right) Egor Maksimov, Mikhail Kondratyev, Sergey Kuzin

During the competition, the teams performed two exercises: “Circle” and “Stillness and Aim.” The task in the “Circle” exercise is to land the balloon no later than five minutes after launch as close as possible to the center of a circle with a radius of 10 meters, into which the pilot has no right to enter. The pilot controls the flight altitude using a burner via a radio channel, and the position of the balloon using a 15-meter-long halyard tied to the gondola. In the “Still and Target” exercise, you need to hold the balloon for as long as possible in the altitude range from 0 to 2 meters, and then land it as close to the target as possible. Each exercise was given two attempts. The winners were determined by the sum of points in two exercises. According to the results of the competition, the team of the Intellectual school, captain Mikhail Kondratyev, took first place, 492 points; second place – “School of Health”, captain Egor Maksimov, 392 points; third place – team from Sergiev Posad, captain Sergei Kuzin, 340 points. After the end of the competition, the pilots of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky arranged for participants and spectators to ride on large balloons, which were raised on a leash to a height, albeit small, but causing delight among young passengers. More information about the Christmas aeromodeling fiesta can be found on the website of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky (http://www.a-club.airshow.ru) and on the website of mass aeromodeling projects (http:// aerostat4all.narod.ru).
