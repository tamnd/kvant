All-Russian Student Olympiad in Physics

According to the results of the next All-Russian Olympiad among students of technical universities, which took place in November 2006, in the team competition the first place was taken by the team of the St. Petersburg State Polytechnic University (106 points), second place by the team of the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys (78 points), third place by the team of the Russian State University of Oil and Gas named after. I.M.Gubkina (73 points). In the individual competition, first place was won by N. Bogoslovsky (SPGPU, 47 points), K. Kornishin (RSU of Oil and Gas named after I.M. Gubkin, 44 points) was in second place, third place was shared by I. Kovtunov (MISiS, 43 points) and Yu. Kropotina (SPGPU, 43 points).

Objectives of the Olympiad

1. Determine the angle of inclination of the low orbit plane of a spacecraft moving around the Earth to the equatorial plane if it ended up above the same point, located at the latitude of Moscow, with an interval of less than 2 hours. It is known that the period of rotation of the apparatus is 90 minutes, and the latitude of Moscow is $57 ^\circ. 2.$ A flywheel in the form of a crankshaft of mass m, the axis of which is horizontal, is at rest, resting with its cylindrical ends of radius R each on two smooth horizontal ribs. The center of mass of the flywheel is displaced by a distance R from the geometric axis of the flywheel and at the initial moment occupies the highest position. Determine the dependence of the angular acceleration of the flywheel in the event of its loss of equilibrium on time, if the moment of inertia about the horizontal axis passing through the center of mass is equal to $mR^{2}. 3.$ A spacecraft approaches a planet of radius R with acceleration on the surface g, having a satellite with an orbital radius of 81R, from a great distance with a speed v = ( 1 9 ) 7 Rg. Estimate whether it is possible for a spacecraft to be captured by the gravitational field of a planet if it is running out of fuel and the angle of rotation of the vehicle’s velocity vector when flying around a satellite in the satellite’s reference frame is equal to $120 ^\circ$.

4. A puck of radius R slides on ice with a speed of $v_{0}$, rotating around its own axis with an angular velocity of $\omega_{0} ( v_{0} = R\omega_{0} ).$ Determine the law of change in the acceleration of the puck over time if the coefficient of friction of the puck on the ice is $\mu. 5.$ The air conditioner, operating in the “heat pump” mode, pumped an amount of heat Q into the room. Air temperature outside $T_{0}$, indoors $T_{п}. 6.$ A sphere of radius R made of dielectric is divided by a diametrical plane into two identical parts, charged with uniformly opposite charges Q and –q. The work done to move the hemispheres apart to infinity is equal to A. Determine the energy of the charge q uniformly distributed over the surface of a hemisphere of radius r. 7. A very long solenoid of radius r with winding density n is placed in an infinitely long superconductor tube with radius R > r. Determine the magnetic field inside and outside the solenoid if a current I flows through its winding. 8. The most famous international radio-controlled balloon competition is held annually in Brigachtal, a small village in south-eastern Germany. At the Royal Aeronautical Fiesta in Switzerland, radio-controlled balloons are included in an independent program. Up to forty or more models take part in such competitions, which attract hundreds of spectators. But most often, 5-7 models participate in small friendly meetings of balloonists, of which several dozen are held in Europe every year. For the first time in Russia, competitions of radio-controlled models of hot air balloons were held on January 30, 2007 at the Moscow boarding school “Intellectual” (school 1128) for the School Cup. The name of the competition “Christmas Fiesta” corresponded to the scheduled date of January 12, but due to weather conditions the fiesta was postponed several times. The idea of ​​holding the fiesta belonged to the director of the school E.V. Markelov, and was brought to life thanks to the efforts of the director of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky V.D. Boksha and the leaders of aeromodeling circles of Moscow schools V.I. Belyakov-Bodin (school 1071) and A.V. Efimov (school 1128). The first step towards the plan was a two-hour lesson held at the Intellectual school by the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky for high school students. An ordinary physics lesson devoted to Archimedes' law has turned into an interesting, educational lesson that goes far beyond the scope of the school curriculum, with an illustration of the main provisions of the law using the example of the flight of a hot air balloon. Three teams took part in the competition: teams from Moscow schools “Intellectual” and “School of Health” (school 1071), as well as a team of schoolchildren from Sergiev Posad. The delight of the participants and numerous spectators knew no bounds. It is a pity that for technical reasons teams from Syktyvkar and gymnasium 33 in the city of Udelnaya could not participate in the fiesta. The financial problems of the fiesta were resolved thanks to the sponsorship of the Dynasty Charitable Foundation.

During the competition, the teams performed two exercises: “Circle” and “Stillness and Aim.” The task in the “Circle” exercise is to land the balloon no later than five minutes after launch as close as possible to the center of a circle with a radius of 10 meters, into which the pilot has no right to enter. The pilot controls the flight altitude using a burner via a radio channel, and the position of the balloon using a 15-meter-long halyard tied to the gondola. In the “Still and Target” exercise, you need to hold the balloon for as long as possible in the altitude range from 0 to 2 meters, and then land it as close to the target as possible. Each exercise was given two attempts. The winners were determined by the sum of points in two exercises. According to the results of the competition, the team of the Intellectual school, captain Mikhail Kondratyev, took first place, 492 points; second place – “School of Health”, captain Egor Maksimov, 392 points; third place – team from Sergiev Posad, captain Sergei Kuzin, 340 points. After the end of the competition, the pilots of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky arranged for participants and spectators to ride on large balloons, which were raised on a leash to a height, albeit small, but causing delight among young passengers. More information about the Christmas aeromodeling fiesta can be found on the website of the Aeronautics Club of the city of Zhukovsky (http://www.a-club.airshow.ru) and on the website of mass aeromodeling projects (http:// aerostat4all.narod.ru).
