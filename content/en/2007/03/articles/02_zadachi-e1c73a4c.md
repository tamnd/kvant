Tasks

1.

Professor Mumbum-Plumboom suspected that some students were sleeping during his lecture. To establish the number of outrageous people, shortly before the start of the lecture, he secretly installed snoremeters in all corners of the audience, which was a regular hexagon with side length a. Each snoremeter detects the number of people sleeping at a distance not exceeding a. After the lecture, it turned out that all snorometers recorded a total of 7 sleepers. How many students slept during the lecture? K. Kaibhanov

4.

One day, a physicist, having bought sugar and sausage, decided to check their weight on a cup scale, accidentally discovered in a store. He balanced the sugar on the left pan of the scale with an 8 kg weight on the right pan, and he balanced the sausage on the right pan with a 2 kg weight on the left pan. Imagine his surprise when, upon coming home and checking the weight of each product on high-precision electronic scales, he discovered that the total weight of his purchase was more than 10 kilograms. At the same time, the sausage turned out to be heavier than sugar. “Yeah, the scales were unequal!” - the physicist decided. How much do sausage and sugar weigh in this case, if the weight of each product is expressed in whole kilograms? G. Galperin

2.

The diagonals cut the quadrilateral into triangles, which are alternately colored red and blue. It is known that the sum of the areas of red triangles is equal to the sum of the areas of blue ones. Prove that some diagonal of a quadrilateral divides its area in half. V. Proizvolov
