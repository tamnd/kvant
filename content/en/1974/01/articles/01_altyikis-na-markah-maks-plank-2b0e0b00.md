On the stamps - Max Planck

Our magazine is called «Kvant», and on the first page of the cover there is a symbol of a constant introduced into physics by the creator of quantum theory, the outstanding German theoretical physicist Max Planck (1858-1947). This constant is called "Planck's constant".

Planck's important works relate to the theory of thermal radiation. In 1900, in his work “On the Theory of the Law of Energy Distribution in the Normal Spectrum,” he established a formula for the energy distribution in the radiation spectrum of an absolute black body. This formula was found by Planck semi-empirically. Subsequently, trying to theoretically substantiate this formula, Planck, for the first time in physics, came up with the idea of ​​a discrete character of energy exchange in the process of radiation and established the foundations of the theory of quantum

Copper's Corner

Comrade He showed that the magnitude of the energy quantum is proportional to the frequency of the emitted electromagnetic wave. The proportionality coefficient $h$ was called by Planck the “quantum of action”. Now this constant is called Planck’s constant, and the quantum of action is most often named from the quantity

$$h = \frac{h}{2\pi}.$$

Planck's theory was contrary to the law of classical physics and was highly unlikely, but it was well confirmed experimentally. In our country, quantum mechanics, which grew out of Planck’s discovery, has become one of the most important branches of physics. For his discovery of the laws of light emission, Planck was awarded the Nobel Prize in 1918.

Several postage stamps are dedicated to Max Planck (they are shown in the photo). The first of them with a portrait of Planck was released in 1950 in the German Democratic Republic in a series dedicated to the 250th anniversary of the Berlin Academy of Sciences. Then a stamp dedicated to Planck was issued in 1953 by the West Berlin post office.

Celebrating the 100th anniversary of the birth of the outstanding physicist, the German Democratic Republic issued two postage stamps on April 23, 1958. On one of them is a portrait of Max Planck, and on the other is the symbol of “Planck’s constant” $h$ and a facsimile of the scientist
