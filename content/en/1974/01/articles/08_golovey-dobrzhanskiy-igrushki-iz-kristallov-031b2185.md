Crystal toys

M. P. Head,
G. F. Dobryansky

Crystals are used to make jewelry and decorations. They attract our attention with their intricate shapes, sparkling edges, play of colors and richness of shades. On the pages «Kvant» we have already discussed how to grow a very large homogeneous single crystal *). We want to teach readers how to make original and red products from polycrystals, which are not difficult to grow. With some skill and accuracy, you can become, for example, the owner of an amazing tree consisting of small crystals sparkling and shimmering with a greenish light, or a green New Year's tree covered with a cap of white crystals, like snow. Once you get acquainted with the method and gain some experience, you yourself will be able to invent and make various jewelry and sovereigns from polycrystals.

The method for producing such products is based on a widely used method for obtaining single crystals - crystallization of rasters from water. When cooling a saturated solution, as well as during evaporation of the solvent and under other conditions,

when supersaturation of the solution is created, the substance dissolved in it begins to precipitate. If small crystals of the original substance (seeds) or some foreign insoluble particles, the structure of which is close to the structure of the crystals, are placed in a vessel with a solution (crystallizer), then at a very slowly reduced temperature we can ensure that the substance is deposited primarily on the seeds.

Obtaining quite large (several centimeters or more in size) homogeneous artificial single crystals requires complex equipment with precise automatic temperature control, stirring of solutions, regulation of the chemical composition of the medium, and so on. Small crystals and their terms (polycrystals) can be easily obtained without resorting to complex designs and automation.

If you lower an object containing a large number of seeds into the crystallizer, then, using the method of reducing the temperature or evaporating the solvent, you can turn it into crystals with a clearly defined cut. There is no need to stir the solution or precisely regulate the rate of temperature change. Even without this, the crystals grow to be red-faceted. To get a large number of seeds on the object being painted, you need to first wrap it with regular cotton threads No. 10 (not necessarily tightly, turn to turn, you can do it with an interval of 1-3 mm), dip it in the solution, immediately take it out and dry it properly at room temperature. Since the threads are saturated with a solution, when they dry, they form tiny crystals, which will subsequently serve as seeds.
