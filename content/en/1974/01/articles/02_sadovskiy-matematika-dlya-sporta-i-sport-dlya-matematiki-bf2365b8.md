L.E. Sagovsky MATHEMATICS FOR SPORTS
AND SPORTS FOR MATHEMATICS

In our country, a lot of attention is paid to the physical education of young people.
Without sport, harmonious personal development is impossible. Sport strengthens a person not only physically; it also forms high spiritual qualities, creates good work and assistance.
The editors of the magazine «Kvant» receive many letters, the authors of which ask whether it is possible to combine sports with a passion for physics and mathematics, how fruitful such a union is. The questions of our readers are answered by Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Professor, Head of the Department of Applied Mathematics of the Moscow Institute of Transport Engineers (MIIT) L. E. Sagovsky.

Mathematics and sports, physics and sports.
For many boys and girls who love mathematics and physics, these classes seem incompatible only due to a misunderstanding. This happens due to a lack of experience or due to the fact that in a number of schools with well-organized teaching of exact sciences, the organization of physical education lessons is less successful. In any case, among the guys whom we are accustomed to consider capable and smart, we unfortunately encounter such disdainful

attitude towards physical education, sports games, a kind of bragging about staying away from them. Such guys have no idea that they themselves are impoverishing their lives, reducing their productivity and depriving themselves of unknown mistakes.

At the same time, many mathematicians and physicists of the older generation (alas, I belong to them) pay great attention to their sports activities. And this despite the fact that in our school years, sports education is in its infancy. At that time, one could only dream of children's sports schools, as well as schools of physics and mathematics, large universities, mathematical Olympiads and a magazine like «Kvant».

During the years of study of people of the next generation, the amount of knowledge imparted at school was significantly less. Classes in groups were organized according to the so-called laborator-team method: everyone learns lessons according to their abilities and desires, and one person is responsible for the entire team - the most successful student in a given subject.

Several decades have passed, significantly changing the conditions of our life; made a qualitative leap in education, especially in the field of exact sciences. The increased flow of information has increased psychological stress, and school activities have become more focused. New living and studying conditions required youth (and even adults) to have a certain psychological and physical stability. I am convinced, based on many years of observations and my own experience, that such stability is especially necessary for those who study mathematics and physics. In fact, both those who are still just solving difficult problems and those who are already thinking about complex problems are well aware of the special mental effort required for such activity. People who know the specifics of service classes in mathematics, physics, and their applications know the joy of discovery (one of the amazing joys of life, not familiar to everyone), and hard work, and the bitterness of refusing some entertainment, and the established tension, and the established period of intense work. Norbert Wiener wrote: “Intensive research work changes to the limit. If a scientist loses the opportunity to devote himself as fully as he devotes himself to his work, this will immediately affect the quality of his articles.”*).

I have many friends who are mathematicians and physicists, and I have noticed in all of them a desire for “mental relaxation.” However, different people find release in different ways. Some are fond of bridge (isn't this a game for mathematicians?), others are into chess (the art of chess is worthy of the wise!), and others are into occasional walks. And, alas, very little - physical education. I am deeply convinced that neither bridge, nor chess, nor other games, although they appealed to me, but require mental effort, do not bring real relaxation. In addition, service classes in mathematics and chess at the same time are apparently impossible. Examples of this can be used at the time of life, a gap from mathematics, in which I previously worked actively.

Some of the mathematicians and physicists I knew well said that strong tobacco and drinks bring clarity of thought and some creative ideas. I did not argue with this point of view, but suggested trying another means - going for a run, for example; regularly engage in last-minute physical exercise

I referred to Wiener, who said that he wrote best when mental work was combined with simple, non-intellectual satisfaction - walking, swimming. A fan of the game of intellectuals explained that in sports and sports games, intelligence, education, and calculation are far from superfluous things. It’s not without reason that they say that you play tennis with your hands, but win with your head. A good tennis player must have varied and subtle technical strokes. Entering the court, a tennis player meets an opponent who, as a rule, is not inferior to him in technique. And here everything is decided by tactics, judgment, calculation and foresight. It is not without reason that the overwhelming majority of good tennis players are educated people; it is not without reason that tennis is a widely spread game among intellectual workers. But tennis is not excluded, and similar messages could be made regarding other sports games. According to major authors, modern sport in general has become more and more intellectual in recent years. It should also be borne in mind that not only checkers, chess, card games or billiards served as a source of many interesting mathematical problems. They can be found in sports everywhere. Think, for example, how many unsolved problems arise when considering the interaction of a ball with a group or grass. Open up Kemeny and Snell's book Finite Markov Chains and you'll find an interesting treatment of the tennis system with a stochastic process position.

Mathematical methods are increasingly used in sports. So, for example, using the methods of mathematical statistics, we establish the personal characteristics of athletes, the conditions most favorable for training, their effectiveness, and process the readings of sensors that control the athletes’ loads. Information theory allows us to assess the degree of workload of the visual apparatus when practicing various sports. Mathematics and physics help to find the most convenient forms of rowing vessels and oars. There is an opinion that the ideas of P. L. Chebyshev regarding the cutting of fabric were used in the design of the cloth braid of a tennis ball *).

At the same time, playing sports has a beneficial effect on a person’s mental activity and psyche, strengthening his will. This fact is indisputable for many scientists involved in swimming, tennis, running, climbing, and mountaineering. Their enthusiasm infected me. In turn, I was able to bring many others into the loop.

Let us remember the post-war years, when the family lived in a dilapidated house in Sokolniki on Bolshaya Olenei. Every Sunday in the winter we had an “opening day” for skiing friends. One sign, others narrowed. Sometimes up to twenty pairs of skis were stored in the storage room. Everyone could get tea, cookies, cheese. Everyone was warming themselves by the liver, and the pleasant fatigue after a good ski trip and hot tea was conducive to endless skies. In those years in Sokolniki, in the Yauzsky and Losinoostrovsky forestries there were few skiers, there were almost no bases, and our “ski base” was very popular. I have not the slightest doubt that the little that I managed to achieve in mathematics and teaching became possible thanks to constant sports activities. I can’t imagine my life without math or sports.

I picked up a tennis racket for the first time at the age of forty and was so carried away that five years later I added the first category. This passion has captured almost all the children of our houses on V. Olenei and my children as well. Fortunately, the hospitality courts of the Shakhtar sports club were located for use. Oh, if only all sports clubs, stadiums, and sports schools were so accessible to those looking for sports! Then many problems of youth sports would be solved without many years of discussions.

Together with me, professors P. A. Kitkin, V. M. Fokeev, V. A. Lurie, associate professor S. V. Boriskin and others “fell out” into tennis. P. A. Kitkin was the director of one of the institutes in Katseveli (in Crimea, near Siemens). There, together with other enthusiasts, he built a court, surrounded it with a fishing net and skipped training. We were his guests in the summer and slept under the trees near the court.

Years have passed. We all learned to play tennis decently, and that requires a lot of stamina. Olympic beta champion Peter Snegle wrote that, to his surprise, the load on this game turned out to be greater than the load on the beta. What helped us, no longer young people, to cope with these difficulties? Physical fitness and endurance developed in previous years by walking on flats, regular jogging, and, especially, swimming.

For several years now, a swimming section has been operating at the Moscow House of Scientists. Corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences L. A. Lyusternik, academician S. L. Sobolev and others worked in it. Once a week, starting at seven in the morning, we successfully trained for an hour. Renewed and full of strength, they went to work. Further, many years later, S. L. Sobolev said that it was a wonderful time, when wonderful thinking and writing occurred. L.A. Lyusternik was surprised to discover that he had forgotten about the runny nose, but learned about the existence of such a time of day as six o’clock in the morning. P. A. Kitkin and V. M. Fokeev wrote their doctoral dissertations during these years, and A. I. Felzenbaum wrote his candidate’s theses. I myself must admit that for a number of years I was in fits and starts looking for a solution to some problems in general algebra. Hopes gave way to disappointments. When I got down to business in earnest, I created a strict regime: the first half of the day - work, lunch, then a three-hour game of tennis in the summer or lying down and jogging in the winter. And so on every year. In the evening - an absolutely fresh head, a sense of the canvas of life, joyful anticipation of work, assistance in thinking about the paths and details of evidence. I managed to bring the work to its logical conclusion, maintain efficiency and persistence in the face of failures. Like the highest honor I remember the words of Professor P. G. If we compare children who received physical education with children who were not into sports, you will notice that the former are more likely to prolong difficulties in life and school, and are more successful in fighting illnesses.

I agree with the remark that the enormous positive influence of sport on a person’s creative influence could have been written more professionally. The purpose of this article is to share with the reader conviction based on personal experience. And it is no coincidence that the magazine «Kvant» deals with this topic. There are different routes into amateur sports. It's not too late to take advantage of these routes.

School gives you a lot: physical education lessons, ice walks, visiting swimming pools, passing the standard for the GTO badge. In addition, children's sports schools (DSS) operate under the departments of public education, the Püner Palace, and sports societies, where elementary school students are especially willing to be invited.

If you become a student, the departments of physical education and sports are at your service. In addition to (and sometimes together) compulsory classes, you can attend specialized sections: swimming, wrestling, gymnastics, track and field or weightlifting, basketball, volleyball and others. Many major games have their own sports facilities and skating fields. For example, our MIIT, in addition to the bases on Klyaziem, Alush and Europoria, has a wonderful sports complex: a swimming pool, an athletics arena, halls for sports games, gymnastics, wrestling and so on. If you plan to work, then remember about sports societies that have their own sections everywhere.

Sport will bring you a lot of health, joy, self-confidence and success in serving the Motherland, sciences, and society.
