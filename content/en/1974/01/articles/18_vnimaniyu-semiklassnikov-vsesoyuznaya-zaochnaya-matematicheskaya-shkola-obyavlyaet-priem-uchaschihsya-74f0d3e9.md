Attention seventh graders!
A weighty correspondence mathematics school announces admission of students

The All-Island Correspondence Mathematics School (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not admitted to VZMSH.

Classes will begin on September 1st. School education is free.
Most often, at school, they will regularly (about once a month) receive assignments that contain theoretical questions and problems to solve.
The Total Correspondence Mathematics School has three courses of study.

Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must send solutions to the problems no later than March 20, 1974. After the work review (around July 1974), you will be advised whether you will be accepted into the VZMSH. Schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements will have priority for admission.

Although some of the introductory problems look different from regular school problems, they do not require any additional knowledge of mathematics to solve.

In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to complete all tasks without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. The answer may not be read without any training. If the task may not contain answers, then you must indicate all of them.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student notebook in Klatek. Entry papers will not be sent back.

In the envelope along with your notebook you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14 × 6 cm with your postal address (we will stick it on the envelope when we send you a response).

Paste a sheet of checkered paper onto the cover of your notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the following sample (your work will not be checked otherwise):

Last name, first name, patronymic Nikanorov Vladimir
mathematics teacher ..... Alekseevi
Place of work and position Report - driver automatic machine No. 3
parent ..... Mother is a housewife
Full postal address ..... Tomma, st. Lenina, 3, apt. 23.

Test results
(to be completed by the inspector)

Schoolchildren living in the Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions, Komi and Karal ASSR, Belarus, Latvian, Lithuanian and Estonian SSR must accept work at the address: Leningrad, P-228, st. Savushika, 61. Specification at Leningrad State University. Correspondence mathematics school. To the competition.

Students living in the Voronezh, Belgorod, Tambov, Kursk and Lipetsk regions must send their work to the following address: Voronezh, University, FZMSH. To the competition.

Schoolchildren living in other regions of the RSFSR and other co-resource regions must accept work at the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, Moscow State University, Mechanics and Mathematics, VZMSH. To the competition.

⟦folio 73⟧

Introductory test assignment at VZMSH in 1974

1. Three friends played several games of chess, and each two played the same number of games against each other. Then they began to decide who the winner was. The first one said: “I have more than each of you who won.” The second said: “I have less than each of you losers.” But when the points were counted, it turned out that the third one scored the most okonven (Win - 1 point; draw - 1/2 point; lose - 0.) Could this be? If not, prove it; if yes, give an example.

2. Are there three positive integers a, b, c such that a is less than 1974, b is 1575 less than c and a² + b² = c²?

3. On the road connecting villages A and B, off-road areas. The bus always travels uphill at a speed of 15 km/h, downhill at 30 km/h. Find the distance between A and B if it takes 4 hours to get from A to B and back (without stops).

4. The bases of the trapezoid are 15 cm and 11 cm, the sides are 6 cm and 9 cm. Construct such a trapezoid and prove that it can be cut into three congruent trapezoids.

5. The lady will make a backpack, a suitcase, a travel bag and a basket into her luggage. The suitcase weighs more than the backpack. The bag and the backpack together weigh more than the other two things: the basket and the suitcase, and the basket and the bag together weigh the same as the suitcase and the backpack. Which thing is the heaviest and which is the lightest?

6. Point D lies on the bisector of angle ACB. We chose points A₁ and A₂ on ray AC, and points B₁ and B₂ on ray CB so that four points A₁, C, B₁, D lie on the same circle and four points A₂, C, B₂, D also lie on the same circle. Prove that A₁A₂ = B₁B₂.

7. A total of 1,974 figures were issued for the construction project. Each two-digit number, written with two adjacent digits (in the order in which they are written), is divisible by 17 or 23.
a) Last digit 1. What is the first?
b) The first digit is 9. What is the last one?

8. The perimeter of a convex quadrilateral ABCD is 10, its sides AB and CD are parallel. Find the lengths of all sides if it is known that the bisectors of angles A and B of the quadrilateral divide side CD into three equal parts, and the bisectors of angles C and D divide side AB into three equal parts.

9. a) List all possible rectangles whose side length is greater than 10 and which can be cut into 28 3 × 5 rectangles.

6) Is it possible to fill a rectangular box a × b × c with 28 blocks of size 3 × 5 × 10, where a ≥ b ≥ c ≥ 10?

10. Is there a nine-digit number, written as 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, from which it is impossible to cross out five digits so that the remaining four are in descending order?

11. Is it possible to indicate a point inside a triangle on sides 3, 4, 5, the distance from which to each of the sides of the triangle
a) less than 2;
b) less than 1?
