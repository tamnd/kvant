⟦folio 69⟧

The correspondence school of physics and technology at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology announces enrollment of students for the 1974-75 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students of secondary schools, located mainly on the territory of the RSFSR, in independent studies in physics and mathematics at an advanced level.

The school enrolls students in grades 8, 9 and 10 primarily from rural and remote areas. Students from Moscow are not accepted into 3FTSH. For them the organization is very busy.

Admission to the school is limited, but physics and technology clubs can operate locally according to the 3FTSH program. They enroll schoolchildren who are not accepted into 3FTSH due to lack of places.

In addition, according to the “Regulations” on 3FTS, clubs can be organized on site at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have completed the 3FTS introductory task. A circle is considered organizational if the school director reports to 3FTSH the names of its leader and the posted list of members of the circle.

Students admitted to 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as the solutions recommended by 3FTS. The work of school students is checked and evaluated by the 3FTSH, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

Below are introductory tasks in physics and mathematics. In the physics assignment, problems 1-5 are for 7th grade students, problems 2-8 for 8th grade students, and problems 3-11 for 9th grade students. In the introductory task in mathematics: 1-5 for rural classes, 4-10 for high-income classes, 7-13 for ninth grades.

The student completes the introductory task independently. Collective decisions are not allowed and will not be considered.

The work must be done in Russian and carefully copied into one school terrace. The order of tasks should be the same as in the assignment. Send the terrada by simple parcel post. Along with your decision, send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the terradi. Decisions without a certificate will not be considered. On the outside, stick a sheet of paper filled in according to the following pattern:

1. Region (region or ASSR)
   Kuibyshev region (Altai Territory, Mari Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic)
2. Last name, first name, patronymic
   Voronin Anatoly Dmitrievich eighth
3. The class you are in
   school No. 7, st. Sovetskaya, 5.
4. Number and address of schools
   Russian
5. Nationality
   mechanic, foreman
   sewing machine operator
6. Profession of parent and position held
   Father
   Mother
7. Detailed home address
   327840, Chaaevsk, st. Kalinina, 38, apt. 6.

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 10, 1974 (according to the postmark of the place of departure).

Solutions sent after this deadline will not be considered. Entry papers will not be sent back.

Admission to the school is arbitrary by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology and by order of the director of 3FTSH. The selection committee will communicate its decision no later than August 1, 1974.
