⟦folio 72⟧

Attention seventh graders!
A weighty correspondence mathematics school announces admission of students

The All-Island Correspondence Mathematics School (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not admitted to VZMSH.

Classes will begin on September 1st. School education is free.
Most often, at school, they will regularly (about once a month) receive assignments that contain theoretical questions and problems to solve.
The Total Correspondence Mathematics School has three courses of study.

Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must send solutions to the problems no later than March 20, 1974. After the work review (around July 1974), you will be advised whether you will be accepted into the VZMSH. Schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements will have priority for admission.

Although some of the introductory problems look different from regular school problems, they do not require any additional knowledge of mathematics to solve.

In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to complete all tasks without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. The answer may not be read without any training. If the task may not contain answers, then you must indicate all of them.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student notebook in Klatek. Entry papers will not be sent back.

In the envelope along with your notebook you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14 × 6 cm with your postal address (we will stick it on the envelope when we send you a response).

Paste a sheet of checkered paper onto the cover of your notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the following sample (your work will not be checked otherwise):

Schoolchildren living in other regions of the RSFSR and other co-resource regions must accept work at the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, Moscow State University, Mechanics and Mathematics, VZMSH. To the competition.
