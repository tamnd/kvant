Introductory assignment in physics

1. It is proposed to change the average density of the water from which sand is composed; describe in detail the method and results of your measurements.

2. A speed skater running a distance of 500 m ran the first 100 m at a speed of 10 m/s, the next 300 m at a speed of 11 m/s, and the last 100 m at a speed of 13 m/s. At what average speed did the speed skater cover the entire distance?

3. A certain amount of water, olive oil and kerosene are poured into a U-shaped vessel so that the free surfaces in the arms of the vessel are at the same level, as shown in figure 1. Is figure done correctly? Here, the densities of water, oil and kerosene are respectively 1.0 e/cm³, 0.90 e/cm³ and 0.80 e/cm³. Justify the answer.

4. On burners providing equal amounts of heat at equal intervals of time, equal masses of water and turpentine were heated. The results of changes for each of these substances in the form of a graph of changes in temperature ΔT in °C versus time t in minutes are presented on figure 2. Based on these data, determine the specific heat capacity of turpentine, taking into account the value of the specific heat capacity of water equal to 4.2·10³ dm³/kg·erad.

5. An example of a solid is olovo or ice, a liquid is mercury or water, and a gas is oxygen or water vapor. Are these statements correct? Justify the answer.

6. A gymnast in a circus jumps from a springboard and after a time t = 1.2 s lands at a distance l = 6 m from the springboard. Determine his speed at the moment of the jump.

7. There are three electric heaters designed to operate at 110 V and with powers of 500, 500 and 1000 W. How should these devices be connected to a voltage of 220 V so that each of them consumes the specified power?

8. A small massive weight suspended on a length of thread and performing small oscillations around the equilibrium position is called a mathematical matrix. Oscillations are small if the maximum deviation of the matrix thread from the vertical does not exceed 10-15°. The time of one oscillation, that is, the time during which the matrix returns to its original deviation position, is called period T.

It is proposed to study experimentally the dependence of the period of a mathematical matrix T on the length of its thread l and mass m. In the absence, describe in detail the installation used, explaining the purpose of each of its elements, the method of measuring the period of oscillation T, the length of the thread l and the mass of the weight m. We recommend the results of measuring the period T and its square T² from the length of the matrix thread l and its mass m. Explain the obtained dependencies and discuss the sources of errors.
