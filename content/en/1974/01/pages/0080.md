4. The vapor pressure at the surface of the water in the vessels is different. They differ by values ​​$\Delta p = \rho g \Delta h$, where $\Delta h$ is the difference in water levels in the vessels, $\rho$ is the density of water vapor. Therefore, when the surface with a lower level becomes saturated, the surface with a higher level will not have saturated vapor.
