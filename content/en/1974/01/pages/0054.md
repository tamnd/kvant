⟦folio 52⟧

This year, the editors will continue to publish materials in the traditional section “Applicant's Workshop”. Statistics on individual theoretical issues of school courses in mathematics and physics, analysis of competitive problems and analysis of typical mistakes of applicants, options for meeting exams at various universities and institutes of the country, various information - all this, we hope, will be interesting and useful to the reader of the magazine preparing to enter a university.

There are many letters in the editorial mail, the authors of which ask to indicate articles published in past years, which should first of all be consulted when preparing for the meeting exams. In this issue we fulfill this request. For brevity, we do not give the title of the article, but, the conditions for their development by topic, indicate only the year, the journal number and the page from which the article begins.
