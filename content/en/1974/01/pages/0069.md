Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Engineering and Physics Institute was organized in 1942. At first it was called the Moscow Mechanical Institute (MMI), and in 1953 it was changed to the Moscow Engineering and Physics Institute.

MEPhI trains broad-spectrum research engineers in a number of the latest areas of science. The duration of study at MEPhI is 5 years and 6 months.

MEPhI consists of five faculties, one faculty of advanced training and a number of educational divisions.

The Faculty of Experimental and Theoretical Physics and the Faculty of Physics and Energy prepares physicist engineers for research work in the field of theoretical and experimental physics, design and experimental physics, modern physical installations, apparatus and equipment.

The Faculty of Automation and Electronics produces physics engineers specializing in the field of creation and experimental electronic devices of modern physical installations, development and experimental systems for automatic control of technical and physical production processes.

The Faculty of Cybernetics trains electrical and mathematical engineers in the development and mathematical support of modern high-speed electronic computers and automated control systems.

The Special Faculty of Physics (SFF) is organized at MEPhI and the Order of Lenin Physics Institute named after. P. N. Lebedev Academy of Sciences of the USSR in febrero 1972. He trains physicist engineers in the latest areas of physics. The faculty enrolls students from non-Moscow higher educational institutions who have an education of two and a half courses in physics and physics-technical faculties and who have shown a penchant for scientific work. Training at SFF is carried out according to inductive plans. Currently, students from fifteen universities of the Soviet Union are studying at SFF.

The MEPhI branch in Obninsk trains electrical and mathematical engineers in the development and mathematical support of automated control systems.

The preparatory department works as a faculty and, together with the educational department and the admissions committee of the institute, carries out a set of activities to prepare young people for admission to MEPhI.

MEPhI has several forms of training for applicants. The main form of training for work and rural youth is a full-time preparatory department with separation from production, organized at MEPhI in 1970. Workers, collective farmers and demonized wars during their studies acquire knowledge in mathematics, physics, Russian language and literature and receive stylization as junior students. After successfully passing the final exams, the services of this department will be enrolled in the first year of MEPhI without additional entrance exams.
