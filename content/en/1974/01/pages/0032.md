⟦folio 30⟧

The section “Laboratory «Kvant»” belongs to the systematic sections of our magazine. In it we strive to give our readers the opportunity to carry out some kind of interesting experiment that does not require special equipment, so to speak, with improvised means that are practically available in any home. These processes of using experiments help to gain a deeper understanding of various physical phenomena and develop intelligence and observation.

Many laws and natural phenomena are not so complex at their core, and their characteristic features can be traced even in relatively simple experiments. Such experiments are the first step to independent research.

From the letters of our readers, we see that many of them willingly carry out the experiments we propose and even strive to creatively experiment with them (vary the conditions - change the temperature, for example, or other physical quantities involved in the experiment, select other substances and materials, etc.).

When reproducing the experiments we describe, it is necessary to pay special attention to the technical features and strict implementation of the recommendations in the articles on precautionary measures.

We hope that the “Laboratory «Kvant»” section will help our readers understand their abilities and develop the skills of future server nature researchers.
