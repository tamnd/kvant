Science fiction literature
Publishing house "Mir"
1. Brown F., Tenn W., Star Carsel (volume 16 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 86 kopecks).
The collection of science fiction works consists of stories by American science fiction writers F. Brown and W. Tenn, published for the first time in our country.
The rich imagination of the authors, their inquisitive interest in scientific and technical problems will undoubtedly appeal to a wide readership.
Publishing house "Young Guard"
2. Let's talk about the future (volume 12 liters, circulation 100,000 copies, price 60 kopecks).
The collection consists of articles by prominent scientists talking about the future of science.
Academician N. Semenov will show how he will increase the energy supply by the beginning of the 21st century compared to today. Academician I. Petryanov-Sokolov will speak about the problems and tasks of environmental protection. In the article “Enterprises of the Future,” Doctors of Science A. Kobrinsky and N. Kobrinsky discuss the factories and factories of the future, the degree of their automation, and the working conditions in them. Professor A. Kitaigorodsky will talk about new substances of the two centuries. Readers learned what transport and communications would be like at that time from the articles of doctors of science V. Molyarchuk and N. Petrovich.
3. Bardin V., Hospitable Antiarctic (volume 11 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 70 kopecks).
The author, a participant in six Soviet Antarctic experiments, talks about the work of Soviet Antarctic research at Molodzhnaya station, about meetings with Japanese and Belgian polar explorers, about the work of a geologist in the mountains of Queen Maud Land, about life in the Antarctic oasis at the Soviet Novolaazrevskaya station.
4. Fiction - 74 (volume 26 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 10 kopecks).
A traditional collection of science fiction stories by a Soviet author.
Publishing House "Children's Literature"
5. Anfilov G., The bestiality of astonishment (volume 18 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 61 kopecks).
The author shows how amazing natural phenomena were revealed. The way to solve complex problems in physics is the theme of this book.
6. Belousov V., Entertaining standardization (volume 9 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 54 kopecks).
The book consists of individual essays dedicated to the young, rapidly developing science of standardization. Schoolchildren in grades 8-10 will read it with satisfaction.
