1. A regular quadrangular paramid whose plane angle at the vertex is equal to $\alpha$ is inscribed in a ball of radius $R$. Find the volume of the paramid.

2. Solve system equation

$$\begin{cases}
x^2 + xy + y^2 = a^2, \\
\log_x \sqrt{a} + \log_y \sqrt{b} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{3}}.
\end{cases}$$

3. Find the domain of a function

$$y = \sqrt{\frac{x^2 - 1}{(x + 3)(x - 4)} - 1} + \frac{1}{\log_8(x - 4)}$$

$$|\cos x|^{\sin^2 x - \frac{3}{2}\sin x + \frac{1}{2}} = 1.$$

Fiizka

Entrance exams to YAPI are conducted in full accordance with the physics program for applicants to universities. Applicants will take an oral examination. During the exams, each applicant is asked two theoretical questions and one problem. Here are the problems offered at the entrance exams in 1973.

1. The car traveled the second half of the journey at a speed 1.5 times faster than the first. His average speed along the entire journey is $v_{cp} = 43,2$ km/h. What are the auto-breaking speeds in the first and second half of the journey?

2. Determine the work $A$ of lifting a load with a mass of $m = 100$ kg with an acceleration of $a = 1$ m/s² on an inclined plane $l = 2$ m long. The angle of inclination of the plane to the horizon is $\alpha = 30^\circ$. The friction coefficient of the load on the plane is $\mu = 0,1$.

3. A load of mass $m = 100$ kg is moved uniformly along a horizontal plane, applying a force $F$ at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Determine the magnitude of this force if the friction coefficient between the load and the plane $\mu = 0,3$.

4. A tram with a mass of $m = 19,6$ m moves along a convex bridge at a speed of $v = 32,4$ km/h. The radius of curvature of the bridge is $R = 30$ m. With what force does $F$ press the tram on places at a distance of $s = 15,7$ m from its middle?

5. The skater, standing on the ice, threw forward a mass of $m_1 = 5$ kg and, as a result of the recoil, it appeared at $u_2 = 1$ m/s. The mass of the skater is $m_2 = 60$ kg. Determine the work $A$ performed by the skater when throwing the grip.

6. The rocket, launched vertically, rose to a height of $h = 630$ km and began to fall. What distance $s$ will the rocket travel during the first second of free fall? Radius $R = 6370$ km. Gravity acceleration at the Earth's surface is $g_0 = 9,8$ m/s².

7. Determine the mass $m$ and the number $n$ of hydrogen molecules contained in the vessels 体重ju $V = 20$ l at a pressure $p = 2,46$ amm and temperature $t = 27^\circ C$.

8. In the water at a depth of $h_1 = 1$ m there is an air bubble. At what depth $h_2$ will this bubble of air have a second smaller volume? Atmospheric air pressure $p_0 = 760$ mm rm. cm, density of water $\rho = 10^3$ kg/m³, acceleration of free fall $g = 9,8$ m/s².

9. A metal ball in the air ($\epsilon = 1$) was given a charge $q = 1$ nc. The radius of the ball is $R = 15$ cm. Determine the voltage $E$ and the potential f of the field: a) in the ball at a distance of $r = 10$ cm from the surface of the ball, b) on the surface of the ball, c) at the center of the ball.

10. At two opposite vertices of the square from the side $a = 30$ cm there are charges $q = 2 \cdot 10^{-7}$ к. Find the voltage $E$ and the potential f of the electric field at the other two vertices of the square.

11. What work $A$ is required to be done in order for a charge of magnitude $q = 3 \cdot 10^{-6}$ to each be in the air ($\epsilon = 1$) at a distance of $r_2 = 60$ cm from each other, bringing them closer to a distance of $r_1 = 20$ cm?

12. One ball was given a charge of 13 nk, the second 18 nk. The balls were then connected with wiring. Find the final distribution of charges on the balls. The radius of the first ball is $R_1 = 8$ cm, the second $R_2 = 18$ cm. Neglect the capacitance of the connecting conductor.

13. The voltmeter has a resistance of $R = 2000$ ohm and measures voltage up to $U_1 = 100$ ohm. What additional resistance $R_x$ should be installed to change the voltage to $U = 220$ ohm?

14. Find the main focal length $F$ and optical power $D$ of a biconcave line, if the distance from the line to the object is $d = 36$ cm, and to the image $f = 9$ cm.

15. Calculate the principal focal length $F$ of a flat line with a radius of curvature $R = 20$ cm when the lines are in water. Absolute indicator of stack penetration $n_1 = 1,5$, water $n_2 = 1,33$.
