⟦folio 66⟧

From November 4 to November 9, 1973, a celebration of young mathematicians of Transcaucasia was held in Batumi. Here you see two SIM cards made during the holiday. On the top there is “Archimedes’ bath”, on the bottom there is a scene from the play “The Ball of Princess Arithmetic”. You can read about the holiday and the performance on pages 62 and 66.

Take a magazine with circles depicted on the first page of the cover in your hand and make several uniform rotational movements with your hand in the plane of the pages with the image. The circles will begin to “rotate”. You can read about how this illusion is created in the note “Rotation that is not” on p. 12 of this issue of the magazine.
