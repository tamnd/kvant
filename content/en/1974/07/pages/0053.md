1. Two rivers flow into the lake, the flow rates of which are the same; there is no current on the lake. The steamer leaves port B, located on the first river, and sails to port A, located at the mouth of the river, spending $2^{3/4}$ hours on this journey. Then the steamer sails further from port A across the lake to port C on the second river, spending 3.5 hours on this journey. It takes the ship 3 hours to travel from C to A. How long will it take a steamer to travel up the first river from port A to port B, if it is known that port A is located midway between ports B and C?
