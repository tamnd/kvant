Academician P. L. Kapitse

Dear Pyotr Leonidovich!
The editorial collection of the magazine «Kvant», of which you are a member, orgenyu congratulates you on Vashchenko’s eightieth day.
You are one of the greatest Soviet physicists of our time.
Your work in the field of physics of ultra-strong magnetic fields, low temperature physics and technology, and physical electronics will enjoy wide international recognition. Since its founding, under the leadership of the Institute of Physical Problems of the USSR Academy of Sciences, many talented scientists have grown up and received an excellent physics education, who have defined our science with their research. We, like our readers, who managed to get acquainted with several of your articles in the short period of existence of the magazine «Kvant», are especially pleased to note your constant display of love for young people and your interest in educational problems.
As we celebrate your glorious anniversary, we hope that you will again and again provide our readers with original ideas and excellent articles.
We wish you, dear Pyotr Leonidovich, many years of life in our unusually interesting century, a life that serves as an excellent example for young people embarking on the difficult roads of science.
