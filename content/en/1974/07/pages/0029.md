What does language experience consist of and how is it acquired?

If we turn to the explanatory dictionary for the meaning of the word “experience”, we will get a specific one, which will undoubtedly help us answer the question posed. According to the explanatory dictionary, experience is a set of practical conditions of knowledge, skills, and abilities.

This means that in order to accumulate linguistic experience, it is necessary, first of all, to practical application of the acquired knowledge. In other words, in order to learn to speak and understand, write and read in a foreign language, you should do this as often as possible, using all the information available for this, therefore, when choosing books in a foreign language, it is advisable to combine business with pleasure and read what particularly interests you - be it classics, adventure literature or detective stories. In addition, high school students are recommended to read articles that interest them from foreign popular science magazines, as this prepares them for reading foreign literature on their chosen specialization.

It is also important to follow the following rules: read systematically, read at least one page of foreign text every day.
