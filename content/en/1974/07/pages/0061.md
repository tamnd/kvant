topics: automatic transport, automatic roads, road construction machines and equipment, places and tunnels, economics and organization of automatic transport.

Since the founding of the institute, a full-time preparatory department has been functioning.

Below is a version of the written entrance exams in mathematics and physics in 1973.

Mathematica

Option 1

1. Simplify the expression:
$$\frac{\sqrt[3]{x + \sqrt{2 - x^2}} \cdot \sqrt[6]{1 - x \cdot \sqrt{2 - x^2}}}{\sqrt[3]{1 - x^2}}.$$
2. Solve the equation:
$$x (\lg 5 - 1) = \lg (2^x + 1) - \lg 6.$$
3. Prove access points:
$$\frac{\text{ctg}^2 2\alpha - 1}{2 \text{ctg} 2\alpha} - \cos 8\alpha \cdot \text{ctg} 4\alpha = \sin 8\alpha.$$
4. In a regular quadrangular paramid, the height equal to $H$ forms an angle $\alpha$ from the lateral boundary. The section drawn through the diagonal of the base is inclined to the base at an angle $\phi$. Determine the cross-sectional area.
Option 2

1. Simplify the expression:
$(\sqrt[3]{m^2} + n^3 \sqrt{m} + n^2) \times \frac{\sqrt[3]{m^4} - n^3 + n^2 \sqrt[3]{m} - mn}{mn^{-1} + n - n^4 m^{-1} - n^2}$.
2. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} x^3 + y^3 = 7, \\ x^2 y + xy^2 = -2. \end{cases}$$
3. Solve the equation:
$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 x = \frac{5}{8}.$$
4. The diagonals of the central cross section of a truncated cone are mutually perpendicular. The angle between the generatrix and the plane of the large base is $\alpha$. The generator is equal to $l$. Determine the total surface of the truncated cone.

Physics

Option 1

1. Volume and pitch.
2. Amount of heat. The extra-system unit of heat quantity is caloria. Formula for calculating the amount of heat required to heat a body.
3. Series connection of conductors. Rheostats.
4. Energy binding atomic nuclei.
5. A car is moving along a convex place with a radius of curvature of $R = 40$ m. What is the maximum horizontal acceleration that the car can develop at the highest point of the place, if its speed at this point is $v = 50,4$ km/h, and the friction coefficient of the wheels of the car on the place is $k = 0,6$?

Option 2

1. Centripetal acceleration.
2. The efficiency coefficient of a heat engine.
3. Joule-Lenz law.
4. Methods for observing particle elements.

5. The ice mountain makes an angle of $\alpha = 10^\circ$ with the horizon. A stone is thrown up along it, which travels a distance of $s = 12$ m in a time of $t = 3$ s, after which it slides down. What is the time it takes for the stone to move downwards? What is the coefficient of friction between the mountain and the rock?

Option 3

1. Condition for equilibrium of a body on an inclined plane.
2. Current sources. Electromotive force.
3. Open oscillatory circuit. Emission and reception of electromagnetic waves.
4. Converging lens. Construction of the depicted person.
5. In a room with a volume of 120 $m^3$ at a temperature of 15 °C, the relative humidity is 60%. Determine the mass of water vapor in the air of the room. The elasticity of saturated water vapor at 15 °C is 12.79 mm rm. cm.

Option 4

1. Variable movement. Average and instantaneous speed. Acceleration. Unit of acceleration.
2. Melting. Specific heat of fusion.
3. Electrical capacity. Unit of electrical capacity.
4. Lenses. Lens formula.
5. With an electric pulse of water through a time of $t = 25$ min, the current is $I = 20$ a. What is the temperature of the released oxygen if it is in a volume of $V = 1$ liters under a pressure of $p = 2$ amm? The electrochemical equivalent of oxygen is $k = 8,29 \cdot 10^{-8}$ kJ/kmol·rad.
