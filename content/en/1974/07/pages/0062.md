⟦folio 60⟧

New books
In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the 5th quarter of 1974. Orders for books must be placed through specialized materials or the Book by Mail store.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Pogorelov A.V., Elementary geometry (volume 12 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 32 kopecks. Edition 2-e).
The book is a systematic course in elementary geometry. The theoretical material presented in the book is devoted to the most difficult issues of the school curriculum in geometry. A large number of tasks makes the materials easier to understand. The book is intended for teachers, as well as schoolchildren in grades 8-10.
2. Perelman Ya. I., Zanitamelnaya algebra (volume 9 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 27 kopecks. Edition 13th).
The book was written by the famous popularizer of science Yakov Isidorovich Perelman. The main purpose of the book is to develop a teacher's taste for mathematics. The book is written in an interesting and captivating way. It is intended for a wide range of readers.
Publishing house "Mir"
3. Gardner M., Mathematical Novels (volume 4 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 33 kopecks).
Mathematical Novels is the third book by the famous American popularizer of science Martin Gardner, translated into Russian. The book contains short stories on a wide variety of mathematical topics, exciting problems and games. The book is of interest to all mathematics lovers.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Collection of problems and questions in physics (edited by L. S. Zhdanov; volume 21 pages, circulation 300,000 copies, price 67 kopecks. Edition 2-e).
The collection consists of problems and questions on all topics of the school physics curriculum. Most problems are given with solutions. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
2. Shasoklskaya M. P., Eltsin I. A., Collection of illustrated problems in physics (volume 12 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 44 kopecks. Edition 4-e).
The book is a collection of problems, most of which were presented at the Moscow State University Physics Olympiads. Detailed solutions and instructions are provided for all problems. The book is intended for teachers in grades 8-10, as well as for people preparing to enter college.
3. Yavorsky B. M., Pinsky A. A., Fundamentals of Physics (volume 30 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 94 kopecks. Volume 1).
The book addresses the following issues: motion and forces; conservation laws; molecular kinetic theory of gas; molecular forces and states of aggregation; electrodynamics.
4. Yavorsky B. M., Pinsky A. A., Fundamentals of Physics (volume 30 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 94 kopecks. Volume 2).

The book addresses the following issues: the quantity of structure and development of the Greater Universe. The main focus is on the role of hypotheses in the study of stars, galaxies and the Universe as a whole. The purpose of the book is to tell how scientific ideas are created, tested or confirmed.
