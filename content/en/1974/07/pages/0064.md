⟦folio 62⟧

Fifth holiday
junior mathematicians
Transcaucasia

During the autumn holidays, from November 4 to November 9, 1973, the traditional holiday of junior mathematicians of Transcaucasia was held in Batutia. On this holiday, schoolchildren from Tbilisi (Physical School No. 2 named after Komarov and the mathematical club of the Palace of Pinoyers), Baku (school No. 173), Yerevan (school No. 62), as well as members of the editorial board and authors of the magazine «Kvant», came to visit the students of Batuta Secondary School No. 7. The organizers of the holiday were the Council of Professionals of Adjara, the Education Department of the Batuta City Executive Committee and the Batuta School No. 7.

The organizing committee, headed by the mathematics teacher at school No. 7 M.I. Zhgentti, did a great job of preparing the holiday and drawing up its program; Associate Professor of Tbilisi State University A.D. Bendukidze took an active part in this work.

On November 4, the grand opening of the holiday and a meeting with representatives of the magazine «Kvant» took place. They talked about the work of the editorial board on the issues of the magazine and plans for the near future. The festival participants listened to this information with interest and expressed many wishes to the editors.

A scientific conference was held on November 5; it was mainly devoted to the analysis of articles published in «Kvant». Narrimer, several reports were read on the articles by V. N. Vaguten “Numbers $C_n^k$, polynomials, sequences” («Kvant», 1973, No. 2) and A. I. Shirshov “On a property of biominal coefficients” («Kvant», 1971, No. 10).

The holiday's gold medal for the best report (according to Vaguten's article) was awarded to Medea Toronjadze, a 9th grade student at the Tbilisi Physics School. Nanla Allahverdnova and Aftal Ismanlov from Baku, Lena Bekaya, Misha Kovalev, Albert Ovakimyan, Marina Petrosyan, Nadya Popova, Asya Sahakyan and Vladimir Khorushkin from Batuti, Ara Ordyan, Yura Timofeev and Tanya Chernysheva from Yerevan, Vakhtang received «Kvant» awards for active participation in the holiday. Maskhulya and Julieta Gagloeva from the Tbilisi Palace of Pinoyers, Misha Gabeskiria, Misha Gidalin, Veriko Kataria, Dato Poshkhshvili and Medko Toronjadze from the Tbilis FMS.

On November 6, an expert meeting was organized at the Significance Botanical Garden on Cape Verde.
