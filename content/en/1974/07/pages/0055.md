⟦folio 53⟧

Throughout the year, students participating in the courses are sent assignments and special preparatory materials. In July, course participants are called to Moscow for a very busy day.

Below are options for a written exam in mathematics and tasks for an oral exam in physics for the mathematical and physics faculties of Moscow State Pedagogical Institute. V.I. Lenin in 1973.

Faculty of Mathematics

Option 1

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_{x^2-3}8 < -3.$$
2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sqrt{1-\sin 2x} = \sin 3x + \cos 3x$$
satisfaction of condition $\frac{3}{2}\pi \leq x \leq 2\pi$.
3. Through the vertex of the base of a regular triangular paramid, at an angle $\alpha$, a section is drawn to it, parallel to the opposite side of the base and perpendicular to the opposite side face. Determine the volume of the paramid, knowing its apothem $a$.
4. From the pristina $A$ a boat and a raft set off downstream at the same time. The boat went downstream 96 km, then turned back and returned to A 14 hours later. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the current if it is known that the boat met the raft on its way back at a distance of 24 km from A.

Option 2

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_{5}\frac{x+7}{16-2x} > \log_{5}x.$$
2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\text{ctg}x - \text{tg}x = \frac{2\sqrt{3}\cos 2x}{1+\cos 2x},$$
satisfaction of condition $|x| < \pi$.
3. The base of the paramid is an equilateral triangle, the equal sides of which have length $a$. Corresponding to them, the side faces are perpendicular to the plane of the base and form an angle $\alpha$ with each other. The angle between the side face triangle and the base plane is also equal to $\alpha$. Determine the volume of the paramid.
4. Two trains depart simultaneously from points $A$ and $B$ to meet each other. The speed of the first train is 10 km/h greater than the speed of the second. Both trains meet at a distance of 28 km from the middle of $AB$. If the first train departed from point $A$ 45 minutes later than the second, then both trains would meet in the middle of $AB$. Find the distance $AB$ and the speeds of both trains.

Faculty of Physics

Option 1

1. At the base of a right triangular feature lies an equilateral triangle $ABC$, whose sides $AB = BC = a$ and angle $A$ are equal to $\alpha$. A plane is drawn through side $AC$ at an angle $\varphi\left(\varphi < \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ to the base. Find the area of ​​the section if it is known that the section forms a triangle.
2. Find the even numbers that make up a geometric progression such that the sum of the extreme terms is 35 and the sum of the middle terms is 30.
3. Solve the equation:
$$\sin^2x - \sqrt{3}\sin x \cos x = 0.$$
4. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{2^{2x+1}-9}{4} + \frac{2}{4^{x+\frac{1}{2}}} = 0.$$
Option 2

1. The base of the paramid is an equilateral triangle, the side of which is equal to $a$, and the angle at the apex is equal to $\alpha$. All side ribs are inclined to the base plane at an angle $\beta$. Find the volume of the paramid.
2. Calculate:
$$\sqrt{3\sqrt{5\sqrt{3}\sqrt{5}}} \dots$$
3. Solve the equation:
$$\cos 2x + \cos x + 1 = 0.$$
4. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{\log_5(2x+3)}{1-\log_5(2x+3)} + \frac{3}{5-\log_5(2x+3)} = 0.$$
Option 3

1. Determine the volume of a paramid whose base is a right triangle with an acute angle $\alpha$. The ribs are inclined to the base plane at an angle $\beta$ and equal to $b$.
2. Write an arithmetic progression whose first term is equal to 1, and the sum of the first five terms is equal to $1/4$ the sum of the next five terms.
3. Solve the equation:
$$4\sin^2x + 3\cos^2x = 6,5\sin 2x.$$
4. Solve the equation:
$$\frac{\log_2(3x-1)}{1+\log_2(3x-1)} = \frac{2}{3+\log_2(3x-1)}$$.
