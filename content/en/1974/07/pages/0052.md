In this issue we provide samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and samples of tickets for an oral exam in physics at the Faculty of Mathematics, Mechanics and Physics in 1973.

Written exam in mathematics

Option 1
1. The vessel has several identical taps, which open one after another at regular intervals. 8 hours after the last tap was turned on, the vessel was filled; it turned out that the first crane worked 5 times longer than the last one. How many hours will it take to fill the vessel if all the taps are opened at the same time?
2. A cube with edge $a$ is inscribed in a cone so that one of its edges lies on the diameter of the base of the cone; the vertices of the cube that do not belong to this edge lie on the lateral surface of the cone, and the center of the cube lies at the height of the cone. Determine the volume of the cone.
3. Solve the equation
$$\cos^2 x \cdot \cos 2x + \cos 4x + \cos 3x \cdot \cos x + 2\cos^4 x = \frac{1}{2} \cos \frac{x}{2}$$
4. Given the values ​​$\lg 24 \approx 1,38021$ and $\lg 25 \approx 1,39794$. Determine without the help of a table how many zeros are contained after the decimal point before the first value of the digit in the decimal notation of the number $\sin^{1000}\left(\frac{5\pi}{3}\right)$.

Option 2
1. Two workers of different qualifications completed some work in 3 hours. If the first worker had started an hour's work and the second one half an hour later, they would have finished with 18 more minutes of work than they actually did. If the second worker started work an hour later, and the first one half an hour later, then the first worker would earn 5 rubles. 60 kopecks less than it actually is. How much did each worker actually earn?
2. In the triangular paramid $SABC$, edge $AB$ is equal to $a$, and all plane angles of trihedral angles with vertices at points $A$ and $B$ are equal to $a$. Determine the volume of the paramid.
3. Find solutions to the equation
$$\sqrt{1 - \cos x} + \sqrt{1 + \cos x} = 4\sin x$$
lying in the integral $(0, 2\pi)$.
4. Solve inequality
$$2^{-|x-2|}\log_2(4x-x^2-2) \ge 1$$

Additional questions offered in the oral examination in mathematics
1. Find the domain of definition of the function $y = \tg \sqrt{6-x-x^2}$.
2. Construct a graph of the function $y = \sqrt{\cos^2 x} \cdot \sin x + \sqrt{\sin^2 x} \cdot \cos x$.
3. Solve equation $\sqrt{x+1} - \sqrt{x} = a$.
4. Which number is greater: $(\sqrt{2})^{v_3}$ or $(\sqrt{3})^{v_2}$?
5. Solve equation $2^{\sin 3x} = \frac{\sin x}{2}$.
6. Prove that the function $y = \cos x + \sin(x\sqrt{2})$ is non-periodic.
