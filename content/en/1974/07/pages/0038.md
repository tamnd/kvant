Tasks

M271 — M275; F283 — F287

M271. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3,..., n in such an order that for no two numbers they are equal to any of the numbers placed between them? (9th, 10th grade)

A. I. Plotkin

M272. Given two circles of radii $R$ and $r$, externally touching. Various trapezoids $ABCD$ are constructed so that each of the circles touches both sides and one of the bases of the trapezoid. Find the smallest possible side length $AB$. (9 grades)

E. V. Sallinen

M273. On the segment $0 \leq x \leq 1$ the function $f$ is given. It is known that this function is non-negative and $f(1) = 1$. In addition, for any two numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ such that $x_1 \geq 0$, $x_2 \geq 0$ and $x_1 + x_2 \leq 1$, the inequality

$$f(x_1 + x_2) \geq f(x_1) + f(x_2).$$

a) Prove that whatever function $f$ is that satisfies the above conditions, the inequality $f(x) \leq 2x$ will be satisfied for all $x$.

6) Is it true that for everyone $x$

$$f(x) \leq 1,9 \ x ?$$

(10 grades)

A. V. Popov

M274. Find the smallest number of the form

a) $|11^k - 5^l|$;

b) $|53^k - 37^l|$,

where $k$ and $l$ are natural numbers.

F. G. Shleifer
