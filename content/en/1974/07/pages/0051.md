⟦folio 49⟧

Belarusian State University named after. V. I. Lenina

Below we present options for the entrance written exam in mathematics, which were offered to those entering the faculties of physics and mathematics at the Belarusian State University in 1973.

Faculty of Mathematics
Option 1

1. Inside a circle of radius $R$ there are four equal circles smaller than the radius. Each of them touches the larger circle and two adjacent ones equal to it. Calculate the area of ​​a figure bounded by all equal circles.

2. Varithmetic progression $a_m = n$, $a_n = m (m \neq n)$. Find $a_p$.

3. Solve inequality
$$|x|^{x^2-x-2} < 1.$$

4. Find the values ​​of $x$ in the interval $[-2\pi, \pi]$ for which the function $f(x) = \sin x - \cos x$ has the smallest value. Option 2

1. In a triangular paramid, all six edges are identical. Find the distance from the center of the base to the side faces if the radius of the hanging ball is $R$.

2. Simplify an expression
$$\frac{\sqrt{(a+x)(x+b)} + \sqrt{(a-x)(x-b)}}{\sqrt{(a+x)(x+b)} - \sqrt{(a-x)(x-b)}},$$
if $x = \sqrt{ab}$, $a > b > 0$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x + \sin^2 2x = 1.$$

4. The triangle $ABC$ contains the bicsetrice $AD$. Prove that if $AB + BD = AC + CD$, then the triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

Faculty of Applied Mathematics
Option 3

1. The ratio of the perimeter of a parallelogram to its larger diagonal is $k$, $k > 2$. Find the angles of the parallelogram if it is known that the major diagonal divides the angle of the parallelogram in a ratio of 1:2.

2. Find two natural numbers whose difference is 66 and whose least common multiple is 360.

3. Determine at what values ​​of $a$ the equation
$$2 \lg (x+3) = \lg (ax)$$
has a single root. Find this root.

4. Reduce to a form convenient for logarithmization,
$$\sin 2\alpha + \cos 4\alpha - \sin 6\alpha.$$

Option 4

1. The ratio of the volume of a ball inscribed in a cone to the volume of a ball circumscribed about this cone is equal to $k$, where $0 < k < 1/8$. Find the angle between the generatrix of the cone and the plane of its axis.

2. Two motoricles depart simultaneously towards each other from points $A$ and $B$, the distance between which is 660 km. While the first travels 250 km, the second travels 200 km. Find the speed of movement of motoricles, considering their movement uniform if the first motoricles arrives at $B$ 3 hours earlier than the second at $A$.

3. Solve the equation
$$-2 + 4 \cos^2 x = \cos x + \sqrt{3} \sin x.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$\frac{|x+2| - x}{\sqrt{4-x^3}} > 0.$$
