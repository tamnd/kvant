figure 5 shows the apparent path of a star in the constellation Eridanus. It is an orange-yellow star similar to our Sun, but with less mass (its mass is 0.7 solar masses) and less luminosity (30% of the Sun's luminosity). It is absent from us at a distance of 10.7 light years. Based on the deviation of the visible path of the star from a straight line, it is possible to determine the characteristics of the planet: the period of revolution is 25 years, the average distance from the star is 8 astronomical units (1 AU = 149,000,000 km - the distance from the Sun to the Earth). Similar artificial trajectories were recently discovered near the star Lalande 21185 (distance from Earth - 8 light years); perhaps it also has planets.
