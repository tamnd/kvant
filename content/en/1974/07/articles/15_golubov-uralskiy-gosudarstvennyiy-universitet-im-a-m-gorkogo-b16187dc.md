In «Kvant» No. 7 for 1973, we already talked about the Ural Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after A. M. Gorky. In this issue we provide samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and samples of tickets for an oral exam in physics at the Faculty of Mathematics, Mechanics and Physics in 1973.

Mathematics
Matematiko-mechanical faculty
Written exam in mathematics

Option 1
1. The vessel has several identical taps, which open one after another at regular intervals. 8 hours after the last tap was turned on, the vessel was filled; it turned out that the first crane worked 5 times longer than the last one. How many hours will it take to fill the vessel if all the taps are opened at the same time?
2. A cube with edge $a$ is inscribed in a cone so that one of its edges lies on the diameter of the base of the cone; the vertices of the cube that do not belong to this edge lie on the lateral surface of the cone, and the center of the cube lies at the height of the cone. Determine the volume of the cone.
3. Solve the equation
$$\cos^2 x \cdot \cos 2x + \cos 4x + \cos 3x \cdot \cos x + 2\cos^4 x = \frac{1}{2} \cos \frac{x}{2}$$
4. Given the values ​​$\lg 24 \approx 1,38021$ and $\lg 25 \approx 1,39794$. Determine without the help of a table how many zeros are contained after the decimal point before the first value of the digit in the decimal notation of the number $\sin^{1000}\left(\frac{5\pi}{3}\right)$.

Option 2
1. Two workers of different qualifications completed some work in 3 hours. If the first worker had started an hour's work and the second one half an hour later, they would have finished with 18 more minutes of work than they actually did. If the second worker started work an hour later, and the first one half an hour later, then the first worker would earn 5 rubles. 60 kopecks less than it actually is. How much did each worker actually earn?
2. In the triangular paramid $SABC$, edge $AB$ is equal to $a$, and all plane angles of trihedral angles with vertices at points $A$ and $B$ are equal to $a$. Determine the volume of the paramid.
3. Find solutions to the equation
$$\sqrt{1 - \cos x} + \sqrt{1 + \cos x} = 4\sin x$$
lying in the integral $(0, 2\pi)$.
4. Solve inequality
$$2^{-|x-2|}\log_2(4x-x^2-2) \ge 1$$

3. Solve equation $\sqrt{x+1} - \sqrt{x} = a$.
4. Which number is greater: $(\sqrt{2})^{v_3}$ or $(\sqrt{3})^{v_2}$?
5. Solve equation $2^{\sin 3x} = \frac{\sin x}{2}$.
6. Prove that the function $y = \cos x + \sin(x\sqrt{2})$ is non-periodic.

Faculty of Physics
Written exam in mathematics

Option 3

1. Two rivers flow into the lake, the flow rates of which are the same; there is no current on the lake. The steamer leaves port B, located on the first river, and sails to port A, located at the mouth of the river, spending $2^{3/4}$ hours on this journey. Then the steamer sails further from port A across the lake to port C on the second river, spending 3.5 hours on this journey. It takes the ship 3 hours to travel from C to A. How long will it take a steamer to travel up the first river from port A to port B, if it is known that port A is located midway between ports B and C?

2. In an equilateral triangle with a base angle equal to $\alpha$, the height lowered to the base is greater than the radius of the inscribed circle by $m$. Determine the radius of the circumscribed circle.

3. Solve the equation
$(\cos 2x)^2 \cos 3x + 4 \cos x - 1 = \sec 2x$.

4. For every real $a$ solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{2ax - x^2} \geq a - x.$$

Option 4

1. One tug needs to move two pontona visas along the river for $l$ km in the least amount of time. It was decided that one pontoon would be sent along the river on its own, and the other would be transported by a tug for some time, after which the tug would leave it, return for the first pontoon and tow it to its final destination. How long will it take to transport the second pontoon by tug if both pontoons arrived at the final destination at the same time and if the tug’s own speed is $v$ km/h, and the speed of the river is $u$ km/h?

2. The height of a right triangular paramid is $h$, and the distance from the base of the height to the side edge is $a$. Determine the dihedral angle between the lateral edges of the paramid.

3. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases}
\frac{1}{x+y} + \frac{1}{x+z} = \frac{7}{12}, \\
\frac{1}{x+y} + \frac{1}{y+z} = \frac{8}{15}, \\
\frac{1}{y+z} + \frac{1}{x+z} = \frac{9}{20}.
\end{cases}$$

4. Prove that for all values ​​of $x$ for which the left-hand side makes sense, the following inequality holds:
$(\text{ctg}^2 x - 1) (3 \text{ctg}^2 x - 1) \times (\text{ctg} 3x \cdot \text{tg} 2x - 1) \leq -1.$

Below are options for a written exam in mathematics and tasks for an oral exam in physics for the mathematical and physics faculties of Moscow State Pedagogical Institute. V.I. Lenin in 1973.

Faculty of Mathematics

Option 1

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_{x^2-3}8 < -3.$$
2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sqrt{1-\sin 2x} = \sin 3x + \cos 3x$$
satisfaction of condition $\frac{3}{2}\pi \leq x \leq 2\pi$.
3. Through the vertex of the base of a regular triangular paramid, at an angle $\alpha$, a section is drawn to it, parallel to the opposite side of the base and perpendicular to the opposite side face. Determine the volume of the paramid, knowing its apothem $a$.
4. From the pristina $A$ a boat and a raft set off downstream at the same time. The boat went downstream 96 km, then turned back and returned to A 14 hours later. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the current if it is known that the boat met the raft on its way back at a distance of 24 km from A.

Option 2

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_{5}\frac{x+7}{16-2x} > \log_{5}x.$$
2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\text{ctg}x - \text{tg}x = \frac{2\sqrt{3}\cos 2x}{1+\cos 2x},$$
satisfaction of condition $|x| < \pi$.
3. The base of the paramid is an equilateral triangle, the equal sides of which have length $a$. Corresponding to them, the side faces are perpendicular to the plane of the base and form an angle $\alpha$ with each other. The angle between the side face triangle and the base plane is also equal to $\alpha$. Determine the volume of the paramid.
4. Two trains depart simultaneously from points $A$ and $B$ to meet each other. The speed of the first train is 10 km/h greater than the speed of the second. Both trains meet at a distance of 28 km from the middle of $AB$. If the first train departed from point $A$ 45 minutes later than the second, then both trains would meet in the middle of $AB$. Find the distance $AB$ and the speeds of both trains.
