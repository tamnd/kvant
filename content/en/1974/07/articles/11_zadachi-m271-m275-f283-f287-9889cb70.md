Tasks

M271 — M275; F283 — F287

M271. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3,..., n in such an order that for no two numbers they are equal to any of the numbers placed between them? (9th, 10th grade)

A. I. Plotkin

M272. Given two circles of radii $R$ and $r$, externally touching. Various trapezoids $ABCD$ are constructed so that each of the circles touches both sides and one of the bases of the trapezoid. Find the smallest possible side length $AB$. (9 grades)

E. V. Sallinen

M273. On the segment $0 \leq x \leq 1$ the function $f$ is given. It is known that this function is non-negative and $f(1) = 1$. In addition, for any two numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ such that $x_1 \geq 0$, $x_2 \geq 0$ and $x_1 + x_2 \leq 1$, the inequality

$$f(x_1 + x_2) \geq f(x_1) + f(x_2).$$

a) Prove that whatever function $f$ is that satisfies the above conditions, the inequality $f(x) \leq 2x$ will be satisfied for all $x$.

6) Is it true that for everyone $x$

$$f(x) \leq 1,9 \ x ?$$

(10 grades)

A. V. Popov

M274. Find the smallest number of the form

a) $|11^k - 5^l|$;

b) $|53^k - 37^l|$,

where $k$ and $l$ are natural numbers.

F. G. Shleifer

M275* a) On the plane there are $n$ vectors, the length of each of which is 1. The sum of all $n$ vectors is equal to zero the left vector. Prove that the vectors can be numbered so that for all $k = 1, 2, \dots, n$ the following condition is satisfied: the sum of the first $k$ vectors has a length of at most 3.

6) Prove a similar statement for $n$ vectors with sum 0, each of whose length does not exceed 1.

c) Can the number 3 in problem a) be replaced by less? Try to incriminate the assessment also in task 6). (9th, 10th grade)

M. L. Gerver

F283. Charged balls with the same mass, located at a distance $l$ from each other, were released (without initial speed). After $t$ seconds the distance between them doubled. After what time will the distance between the balls double if they are released from the initial distance $3l$? (9 grades)

F284. Find the acceleration $a$ with which a round metal plate falls in a uniform magnetic field parallel to the surface of the Earth. The plate falls vertically downwards and its plane is oriented parallel to the magnetic field and perpendicular to the surface of the Earth. The plate thickness $d$ is much smaller than its radius $R$, mass $m$, magnetic field indication $B$, free fall acceleration $g$. (10 grades)

F285. In an unheated room there is a refrigerator with a thermostat. When the refrigerator was connected to the network, the temperature outside, indoors and in the refrigerator was the same. Assuming the temperature outside is constant, draw an approximation on the graphs of how the temperature in the room changed
