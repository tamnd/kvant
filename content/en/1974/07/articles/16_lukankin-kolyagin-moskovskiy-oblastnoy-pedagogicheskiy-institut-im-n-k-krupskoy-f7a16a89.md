Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute named after. N. K. Krupskaya

MOPI was organized in 1931 on the basis of the pedagogical technical center named after Profintiran, who started his work in 1922. The institute has 8 faculties: mathematics, physics, literature, natural geography, history, physical education, Romance-Germanic languages, English, as well as the faculty of advanced training for teachers of the RSFSR, a preparatory department as a faculty, graduate school and other educational units. The Institute trains specialists of a wide and narrow profile to work in secondary schools under new programs.

The Faculty of Mathematics prepares mathematics teachers for secondary schools. The departments of the faculty train postgraduate students in the following specialties: theory of functions and functioning of analysis, methods of teaching mathematics, geometry and monoidology, algebra and number theory.

The Faculty of Physics trains physics teachers, physics teachers in French or English, and teachers of general chemical disciplines (with a second specialty - teacher of Cherchen). The faculty has postgraduate courses in the following specialties: experimental physics, theoretical physics and methods of teaching physics.

The preparatory department of the institute, operating as a faculty, conducts training without arbitrariness in the following specialties: mathematics (mathematics department), physics (physics department), history (history department), biology and chemistry (science department), English language (infak), Russian language and literature (literature department). Advanced workers and collective farm workers are admitted to the preparatory department (based on the results of the interview).
