New books
In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the 5th quarter of 1974. Orders for books must be placed through specialized materials or the Book by Mail store.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Pogorelov A.V., Elementary geometry (volume 12 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 32 kopecks. Edition 2-e).
The book is a systematic course in elementary geometry. The theoretical material presented in the book is devoted to the most difficult issues of the school curriculum in geometry. A large number of tasks makes the materials easier to understand. The book is intended for teachers, as well as schoolchildren in grades 8-10.
2. Perelman Ya. I., Zanitamelnaya algebra (volume 9 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 27 kopecks. Edition 13th).
The book was written by the famous popularizer of science Yakov Isidorovich Perelman. The main purpose of the book is to develop a teacher's taste for mathematics. The book is written in an interesting and captivating way. It is intended for a wide range of readers.
Publishing house "Mir"
3. Gardner M., Mathematical Novels (volume 4 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 33 kopecks).
Mathematical Novels is the third book by the famous American popularizer of science Martin Gardner, translated into Russian. The book contains short stories on a wide variety of mathematical topics, exciting problems and games. The book is of interest to all mathematics lovers.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Collection of problems and questions in physics (edited by L. S. Zhdanov; volume 21 pages, circulation 300,000 copies, price 67 kopecks. Edition 2-e).
The collection consists of problems and questions on all topics of the school physics curriculum. Most problems are given with solutions. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
2. Shasoklskaya M. P., Eltsin I. A., Collection of illustrated problems in physics (volume 12 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 44 kopecks. Edition 4-e).
The book is a collection of problems, most of which were presented at the Moscow State University Physics Olympiads. Detailed solutions and instructions are provided for all problems. The book is intended for teachers in grades 8-10, as well as for people preparing to enter college.
3. Yavorsky B. M., Pinsky A. A., Fundamentals of Physics (volume 30 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 94 kopecks. Volume 1).
The book addresses the following issues: motion and forces; conservation laws; molecular kinetic theory of gas; molecular forces and states of aggregation; electrodynamics.
4. Yavorsky B. M., Pinsky A. A., Fundamentals of Physics (volume 30 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 94 kopecks. Volume 2).

The book addresses the following issues: the quantity of structure and development of the Greater Universe. The main focus is on the role of hypotheses in the study of stars, galaxies and the Universe as a whole. The purpose of the book is to tell how scientific ideas are created, tested or confirmed.

Publishing house "Zannye"
7. Lishchevsky V.P., Physics around us (volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 17 kopecks).
The book examines issues of so-called everyday practical physics. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
8. Physicists about physics (volume 3 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 10 kopecks).
The collection contains the most interesting popular science articles by major foreign scientists about current problems in theoretical and experimental physics. The material presented is available to high school students.

Science fiction literature
Publishing house "Mir"
1. Brown F., Tenn W., Star Carsel (volume 16 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 86 kopecks).
The collection of science fiction works consists of stories by American science fiction writers F. Brown and W. Tenn, published for the first time in our country.
The rich imagination of the authors, their inquisitive interest in scientific and technical problems will undoubtedly appeal to a wide readership.
Publishing house "Young Guard"
2. Let's talk about the future (volume 12 liters, circulation 100,000 copies, price 60 kopecks).
The collection consists of articles by prominent scientists talking about the future of science.
Academician N. Semenov will show how he will increase the energy supply by the beginning of the 21st century compared to today. Academician I. Petryanov-Sokolov will speak about the problems and tasks of environmental protection. In the article “Enterprises of the Future,” Doctors of Science A. Kobrinsky and N. Kobrinsky discuss the factories and factories of the future, the degree of their automation, and the working conditions in them. Professor A. Kitaigorodsky will talk about new substances of the two centuries. Readers learned what transport and communications would be like at that time from the articles of doctors of science V. Molyarchuk and N. Petrovich.
3. Bardin V., Hospitable Antiarctic (volume 11 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 70 kopecks).
The author, a participant in six Soviet Antarctic experiments, talks about the work of Soviet Antarctic research at Molodzhnaya station, about meetings with Japanese and Belgian polar explorers, about the work of a geologist in the mountains of Queen Maud Land, about life in the Antarctic oasis at the Soviet Novolaazrevskaya station.
4. Fiction - 74 (volume 26 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 10 kopecks).
A traditional collection of science fiction stories by a Soviet author.
Publishing House "Children's Literature"
5. Anfilov G., The bestiality of astonishment (volume 18 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 61 kopecks).
The author shows how amazing natural phenomena were revealed. The way to solve complex problems in physics is the theme of this book.
6. Belousov V., Entertaining standardization (volume 9 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 54 kopecks).
The book consists of individual essays dedicated to the young, rapidly developing science of standardization. Schoolchildren in grades 8-10 will read it with satisfaction.
