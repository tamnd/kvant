The Moscow Aviation Technical Institute named after K. E. Tsiolkovsky trains process engineers in new technology for aviation and other industries.

MATI has the following faculties: innovation-technological, engineering-mechanical, radio-electronic equipment and evening faculty. The Faculty of Aviation Technology trains mechanical engineers in aircraft construction, aircraft propulsion, and technical metal processing; literary production of ferrous and non-ferrous metals; metal forming; metallurgy and technical welding production. The Faculty of Aviation Mechanics trains mechanical engineers in aviation instrumentation and radio engineers-technologists in the design and production of radio equipment.
