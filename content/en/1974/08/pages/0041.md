He was about 60 years old at that time...

At first glance, the increase in sports scientists seems strange. Many people believe that successful scientific work requires that all the scientist’s time be spent on it. Where can you find time to travel through the mountains? But it’s not for nothing that the eastern latter says that finishing belongs to labor, like night to day. Rest is necessary for everyone who works. But to separate means to open up from what one is doing during labor. But it is very difficult for a scientist to open up to science. And the best way to discover it is through sports. Moreover, one that requires great and prolonged physical activity and is associated with strong desires. This is exactly the kind of mountaineering: moving upward, with a heavy backpack, in conditions of constant danger, in the best way makes you forget about what the brain has been busy with all year... It is possible that this is why many physicists and mathematicians prefer mountaineering. But, as we have seen, other sports are not alien to them.

Sports, any kind of sport, is the best recreation for those involved in science. And for those who are still learning it!
