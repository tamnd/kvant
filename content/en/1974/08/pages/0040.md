Academy of Sciences of the USSR Boris Nikolaevich Delone not only made many climbs to the peaks himself, but was also the organizer of the first mountaineering camp in our country. This he laid the foundation for the system of training and sports improvement of climbers, which made accomplished climbers the strongest in the world.

Another mathematician, academician Aleksadr Danilovich Aleksadrov, is also known not only as the author of outstanding works on geometry, but also as a master of sports of the USSR in mountaineering, who made a number of difficult ascents on

Caucasus and other regions of the country. An excellent alpine skier, he, being the rector of Leningrad State University, more than once won excellence in slanting from his young students - university students.

Many other physicists and mathematicians both in our country and abroad, including the most famous of them, are passionate about alpine sports. Such luminaries of physics as Niels Bohr and Werner Heisenberg have climbed to the tops of mountains more than once. Enrico Fermi loved to make mountain hikes, on foot and even by bicycle. The famous physicist John Tyndall (the phenomenon of light scattering in turbid media is called the Tyndall effect) was the first to climb one of the most difficult mountaineering peaks - Mattergorin.

But mountaineering is not the only sport that has fascinated physicists and mathematics.
