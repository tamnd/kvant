⟦folio 60⟧

The decision, in part, notes that extracurricular work in mathematics with schoolchildren in the country has been carried out since the early thirties. It has become especially widespread in the last decade and currently covers hundreds of thousands of students. This work allows for constant supervision of schoolchildren’s mathematics classes, providing effective assistance to teachers (especially teachers from rural areas, working-class villages and small towns) in organizing extracurricular activities, clubs and electives.

The conference noted that the participation of Komsomol members of universities and research institutions in working with schoolchildren is a real contribution to the implementation of party decisions and praktiviteliya on increasing the level of training of secondary school students, to the approach pursued by the Komsomol Central Committee under the motto “Komsomol to rural schools.” Only a multi-state army of students and graduate students can help organize education, involve the masses of schoolchildren in extracurricular work, and develop the individual abilities of each student.

The conference addressed a request to the Press Committee under the Council of the Ministry of the USSR to increase the production of special publications related to out-of-class work with schoolchildren.

At the final plenary meeting, an appeal was made to students, postgraduate students and teachers of higher educational institutions and to employees of scientific research institutions of the Spree to expand work with schoolchildren in all subjects and disciplines. The work of the conference showed that there are all proposals for further improvement and expansion of this work, both among mathematicians and among workers in other fields of science and technology.

Problems of the mathematical battle between school No. 30 and boarding school No. 45

1. Construct a triangle whose two altitudes lie on the given lines, and the center of the circumscribed circle is at a given point.

2. Square $6 \times 6$ needs to be filled with 12 tiles, $k$ of which have the shape

$$\begin{array}{c c c}
a & (12 - k) & \text{форму}
\end{array}$$

At what $k$ is this possible?

3. On the edges $A'D'$ and $C'D'$ of the cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$ two points $K$ and $M$ are chosen so that the plane $KDM$ touches the ball written in the cube. Prove that the value of the dihedral angle at the edge $B'D$ of the tetrahedron $B'DKM$ does not depend on the choice of points $K$ and $M$.

4. On the plane lies a regular octagon and a circle. The octagon can be rotated and reflected symmetrically relative to any of its sides. Prove that with the help of such operations it is possible to ensure that the center of the octagonal grid falls into a given circle.

5. Construct a triangle at the centers of the inscribed, circumscribed, and one of the outer circles.

6. Each vertex of a regular $k$-gon contains the letter $A$ or $B$. It is known that for each vertex one can specify a number $p$, less than $k/2$, such that a word of $p$ consecutive letters starting from this vertex coincides with a word of $p$ consecutive letters ending in front of this vertex (the words are read clockwise). Prove that the $k$-gon can be rotated so that each letter is aligned with the same letter.

7. A circle of radius $R$, externally touching a circle of radius $r < R$, rolls without sliding along this circle, and a circle of radius $R + r$ touches internally a circle of radius $r$ and also rolls along it without sliding. A point is selected on each of the katic circles. Prove that the trajectory described by these points is congruent.

8. What is the greatest number of sets on the chessboard $8 \times 8$ such that each of them is attacked by no more than the others?

9. In a rectangular parallelepiped, three diagonals of faces are drawn from one vertex. Prove that the sum of the angles between these diagonals is equal to $180^\circ$.

10. Given a convex polygon. Prove that if a circle is drawn through each triangle of successive vertices, then most of these circles contains a polygon.

11. The function $f$ is defined on the set $R \setminus \{0, 1\}$. For each $x$ the equality $f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{1-x}\right) = x$ holds. Find all such functions $f$.

12. Does the inequalities $abc > c^2 + ad$ and $a_1b_1c_1 > c^2 + a_1d_1$ follow from the inequalities $(a + a_1)(b + b_1)(c + c_1) > (c + c_1)^2 + (a + a_1)(d + d_1)$ if $a, b, c, d, a_1, b_1, c_1, d_1 > 0$?

13. Isozem in a square, four cells on checkered paper, the centers of which lie at the vertices of the straight line from the side, parallel to the grid lines. What is the maximum number of cells that can be placed in the square $25 \times 25$?
