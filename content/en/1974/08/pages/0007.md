The entire program outlined then was fully implemented during the winter of 1921/22, and by the spring of 1922 the entire theory of dimension was ready. It turns out that we can naturally assign in correspondence to each set $E$ (each metric space) some non-negative integer $E$ — the dimension of this set. The new definition turned out to be fruitful; it was the source of establishing a number of new properties of exact sets, which in turn led to the formulation of new mathematical problems.
