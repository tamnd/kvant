Conferences on working with schoolchildren

This conference took place in February 1974 in Leningrad. It is described in detail on p. 59-60.

● The Mathematics building of Leningrad State University, where the conference was held.
● The solution to the mathematical battle problem is told by S. Fomin, a 10th grade student at the Physics and Mathematics School at Leningrad State University. A picky opponent is M. Guarov (school No. 30 in Leningrad).
● The Physics and Music School team at Leningrad State University receives the highest score!
● Magazine discussion «Kvant». “This is a wonderful magazine,” say conference participants. “We want it to become even better, so that «Kvant» is read in every school.”
● The audience of mathematics at Leningrad State University found it difficult to join the conference participants who wanted to attend the discussion of the journal «Kvant».
