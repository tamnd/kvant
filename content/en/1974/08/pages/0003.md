Mixture (p. 27, 35)

On the 1st s. the cover contains figure, showing how the “Sierpinski carpet” is constructed (Fig. by S. F. Lukhin). If in the middle of a square we cut out a 3 times smaller square, in the remaining squares (at the edges) we again cut out 3 times smaller squares and mak to infinity, then the remaining set of lobes, which is also a set (on the plane) of dimension 1. The definition of the concept of the dimension of lobes x and set, proposed by P. S. Uryson, is described in detail in the paper of Academician P. S. Aleksandrov (see p. 3).
