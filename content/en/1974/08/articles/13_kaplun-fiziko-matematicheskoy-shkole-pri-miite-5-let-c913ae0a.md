Physics and mathematics school
at MIIT - 5 years

On February 27, 1974, a methodological conference dedicated to the 5th anniversary of the work of the MIIT Physics and Mathematics School * was held at the Moscow Institute of Railway Engineers (MIIT) of the Order of Lenin and the Order of the Red Banner of Labor (MIIT). The conference was attended by employees of FISH MIIT - students and teachers of the institute - and guests - representatives of the physics and mathematics school of MBTU, MPEI, the Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova, Evening Mathematical School at the Moscow Mathematical Society, editorial staff of the magazine «Kvant», Faculty of “Young Railway Worker” of the Leningrad Institute of Railway Transport Engineers.

“Evening physics and mathematics schools, created on a voluntary basis at the leading universities of our country, are an exceptional phenomenon in world pedagogy,” said the scientific director of the school, head of the Department of Applied Mathematics at MIIT, Professor L. E. Sadovsky. “I was celebrated abroad, which is especially typical for the landscape of Moscow and other Soviet cities. And I tasted - school, a lot of school, educational institutions. Socialism generates a huge thirst for knowledge, a general interest in the achievements of modern science, and evening FISH play a big role in educating the younger generation.” Secretary of the Komsomol Committee of MIIT V. I. Chekov presented certificates to the best student teachers of the school V. Tsvetkov, V. Zalkind, A. Marinbakh and friends, and wished the school staff further success. Representatives of the oldest Moscow Physics and Mathematics School - FISH

*) The official birthday of FISH MIIT is considered to be January 17, 1969, when the rector of the institute, Professor F. P. Kochnev, approved the “Temporary Regulations on FISH” (in fact, the school began work in 1968 - see the article by A. L. Sadovsky “Physics and Mathematics School at MIIT”, «Kvant», 1972, No. 1).

MBTU - presented a welcome address to FISH MIIT.

Over the five years, FISH MIIT has achieved significant success. I have developed my own style and accumulated more pedagogical and methodological experience.

Therefore, the reports of our teachers caused a huge amount of teaching about the overview course of physics and the special course of elementary mathematics, which are taught at our school for 10th graders. The discussion of teaching methods lasted more than 5 hours. Then the guests attended lectures and classes in FISH groups and talked with schoolchildren.

The main goal that our FISH team sets for itself is to reveal to schoolchildren the beauty of mathematics and physics, to help them understand these sciences, and determine their recognition. The gap between the requirement in high school and the university entrance exams, which is so much discussed, is not that the university student is required to have some additional knowledge. It’s just that, in addition to the knowledge provided by the general school curriculum, the applicant must have a sufficiently high culture of thinking.
