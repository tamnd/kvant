In conclusion, we present several problems, and the reader is invited to enter all the initial data necessary to solve these problems.

Exercises

1. On the shelf in the carriage lay a weight, fastened to the wall with a spring. When the train began to accelerate, the weight on the spring began to oscillate. Find the period of its oscillations.

2. This time the weight landed on the string. As the train accelerated, he began to hesitate. Find the oscillation period and equilibrium position of this mathematical matrix.

3. A tube attached to the cart and bent at a right angle contains water filled with a horizontal bend. To what height does the water rise in the vertical bend when the cart begins to move at an accelerated rate?

4. The suitcase lying on the shelf was tied with a rope. At what acceleration of the train will the rope break? How does rubbing a suitcase on a shelf affect it?

5. The spring and weight are rotated with angular velocity $\omega$. How will the weight oscillate?

6. There is oxygen in a cylindrical vessel. The vessel rotates around its axis. How does the gas pressure change along the radius of the vessel?
