Conclusion

We have proven the possibility of an oblique parallel projection of a $n$-dimensional cube onto a plane. Note that the drawings of cubes shown in Figures 4, a, b, have one more property: the vertices *odinakovoeo beca* lie on the same straight line, parallel to the first coordinate direction. This property of the drawing is not necessary for parallel projection - it, for example, does not hold on figure 1. But often it is this property that contributes to a better understanding of the internal structure of such an algebraic object as the $n$-dimensional cube.

Meeting with our readers

The editors of the magazine visited schoolchildren in the town of Zagorsk near Moscow, in the library of the professional educationist of the Zagorsk Optical-Mechanical Plant. This library is one of the largest in the city; among its readers there are many schoolchildren who subscribe to our magazine.

The meeting was chaired by the deputy editor-in-chief of the magazine V. A. Leshkovtsev. He spoke about the tasks that «Kvant» sets for himself, about the articles that will appear in the magazine in the near future. The schoolchildren, in turn, expressed a number of wishes to the editors, indicated sections of mathematics and physics that particularly interested them, and suggested a number of topics for the article. Then the editorial staff answered the reader's questions.

At the end, a small competition was held to solve the problem, the winners of which were loaded with memorable books.
