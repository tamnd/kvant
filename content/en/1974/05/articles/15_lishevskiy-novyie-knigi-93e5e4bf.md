⟦folio 72⟧

New books

The Academy of Sciences has always paid great attention to the promotion of scientific achievements. This goal, in particular, is served by the “Popular Science Literature” series, published since 1931. Over forty-odd years, about 1,300 books have been published. Among them are the works of S. I. Vavilov, V. A. Obruev, A. E. Fersman, E. V. Tarle and other scientists, which are included in the classics of world literature. The publications of collective works giving an overview of the current state and problems of science in its leading fields have been successful, for example: “Through the Eyes of a Scientist” edited by A. N. Nesmeyanov, “Science and Youth”, “Chemistry of Large Molecules” edited by A. V. Topchev and others. Recently, the editorial office of popular scientific literature of the publishing house “Nauka” has been publishing 50-60 books daily popular science books.

In 1974, the popular science literature editorial office of the Nauka publishing house will publish the following popular science books on mathematics and physics.

1. Vilenkin N. Ya. Popularity of combinatorics. Volume 12 l. Circulation 25,000 copies. Price 40 kopecks. (IV quarter).

Combinatorial problems come to the attention of mathematicians, physicists, chemists, biologists, linguists, code specialists and many others. Combinatorial methods underlie the solution of many problems in probability theory and its applications. The book talks in a popular form about interesting combinatorial problems and methods for solving them.

2. Farmer D.S. Asked from the picture. Volume 8.2 l. Circulation 90,000 copies. Price 54 kopecks. (I quarter).

The book is a unique attempt to create a work of scientific fiction on a mathematical basis. Stories about the signs in N. P. Bogdanov-Belsky’s painting “Oral Account”, about the life of the artist and his school mentor - the famous teacher and educator S. A. Rachinsky, depicted in the picture among peasant children, the author turns to the problem, the conditions of which are on the blackboard. Next, the reader will learn about many interesting problems, about Russian, Soviet and foreign mathematicians who made a great contribution to number theory. The book contains many interesting mathematical facts, observations, theorems, and will undoubtedly be useful to the reader «Kvant».

3. Geguzin Ya. E. Essays on diffusion in crystals. Second edition, expanded. Volume 12 l. Circulation 30,000 copies. Price 40 kopecks. (III quarter).

The book contains about seventy essay chapters, each of which talks in a popular way about some diffusion phenomenon, process or mechanism. The topics of the essays are chosen so that the book covers practically all theoretical diffusion movements of atoms that are of scientific interest or important for practice. Each of the chapters is an independent story, which is connected with the others only by the unity of the theme. 4. Gubkin A. N. Electrens. Volume 10 l. Circulation 20,000 copies. Price 65 kopecks. (III quarter).

An electrnet is a constantly electrified dielectric, carrying a positive charge on one side and a negative charge on the other and capable of creating an electric field in the space surrounding it. The book describes methods for making electrets from various dielectrics, their properties, the theory of electret effect, and the principles of operation of various electrets of devices. Discussion of new areas of technical application of electrets.

5. Zhdanov G.V. Multiple generation of particles. Volume 8 l. Circulation 12,000 copies. Price 50 kopecks. (II quarter).

New astronomical discoveries have brought us to a world of ultra-high temperatures and pressures. Under these conditions, particles of atomic nuclei - nucleons - can be accelerated to energies that are equivalent to heating matter up to trillions of degrees. During collisions of such particles, unimaginably atomic energy is concentrated, which leads to the simultaneous birth (generation) of many new particles - mesons, excited nucleons and antiparticles. This book talks about the methods of experimental study of the processes of multiple generation of particles using cosmic rays and powerful accelerators of charged particles.
