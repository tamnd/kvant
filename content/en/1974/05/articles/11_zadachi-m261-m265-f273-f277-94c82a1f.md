M261. A hoop of radius $R$, placed on a stationary circle of radius $r < R$, begins to roll along this circle. Prove that the point of the hoop describes the same trajectory that describes the outside along the same circle of radius $r$ (Fig. 1, a, b). (Rolling occurs without sliding - so that the lengths of the arcs rolling over each other are equal.)
