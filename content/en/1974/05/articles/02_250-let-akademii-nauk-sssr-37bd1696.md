250 YEARS OF THE USSR ACADEMY OF SCIENCES

Performer 250 years since the formation of the highest scientific institution of our country - the USSR Academy of Sciences. The whole country solemnly celebrates this outstanding event in the history of Russian science. It is also celebrated in many other state affairs outside our homeland. The anniversary of the Academy of Sciences is a show of accomplished national science.

We talked about the history of the Academy of Sciences from the moment of its inception until the Great October Revolution in the previous issue of the magazine in articles by Academician S. I. Vavilov and Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B. V. Gnedenko. Now we will tell you about the fate of the Academy of Sciences over the past 56 years.

The Great October Revolution caused enormous changes in the activities of the Academy of Sciences. The Soviet government, headed by V.I. Lenin, immediately revealed to Russian scientists completely unprecedented possibilities for scientific work. For the victorious revolution, every scientific incident had a price; every scientist, old and young, outstanding and novice, was precious. From the very first months of the existence of Soviet power, despite the extremely difficult situation in the country, the government provided great assistance to scientific institutions and individual scientists.

V.I. Lenin understood perfectly well that science must take an active part in the construction of socialization. Therefore, he set the following task: “... everything that science has conquered for man, technical science for man, technical science for man, all the conditions for specialization, everything must go to the service of the united worker” *).

The October Revolution changed the place and role of science in the life of society. Teler science was supposed to serve the interests of the entire people. Taking this into account, V.I. Lenin said in one of his speeches in 1918: “Previously, the entire human mind, all of its genius, created only in order to give some all the benefits of technology and culture, while depriving others of the very necessity - enlightenment and development. Telery will remain technical all the time, all cultural achievements will become the property of the whole people...” **).

She was entrusted with the leadership of All-Union conferences and meetings on various problems of science and technology.

In 1934, the Academy of Sciences moved from Leningrad to Moscow. Here it began to quickly turn into the headquarters of Soviet science, technically connected with other government bodies.

On November 23, 1935, the Council of People's Commissars of the USSR approved a new accounting of the Academy of Sciences. In the first paragraph of this statement: “The Academy of Sciences of the USSR is the highest scientific institution of the USSR, uniting the most outstanding scientists of the country.”

During the difficult years of the Great Patriotic War, the Academy made a great contribution to the victory of our people over fascism. The Heroic page in the history of the Academy was written by its Leningrad institutions during the siege of the hero city.

After the end of the war, scientists from the Academy of Sciences solved a number of important problems in science and technology. Under the leadership of Academician I.V. Kurchatov, Soviet atomic and thermonuclear weapons were created in a very short time. The problems of the peaceful use of atomic energy were also successfully resolved. It is known that the world's first nuclear power plant was built in the USSR. Academician S.P. Korolev led the work on creating a package-space complex for the research and use of outer space. Our country has created the most sophisticated computer technology. The work of the Academy has acquired particular importance in connection with the current scientific and technological revolution, in which science plays a decisive role.

The Academy of Sciences today consists of hundreds of research institutes and institutions and ten thousand scientists. In 1928, the entire staff of the Institute of Physics and Mathematics of the Academy of Sciences consisted of seven people: a director, two heads of departments and four researchers. Today, the Physics Institute of the Academy of Sciences named after P. N. Lebedev alone employs several thousand employees. According to data at the beginning of 1973, the Academy consisted of 244 academicians, 449 corresponding members and 69 foreign members. All activities of the Academy are directed by the Presidium, headed by the chairman of the institution during the siege of the hero city.

Numerous academic institutes and institutions are united into departments in various branches of science: mathematics - in the department of mathematics, physics - in the department of physics and astronomy and the department of nuclear physics, mechanics - in the department of mechanics and control processes. In total, the Academy has 16 departments. In addition, the USSR Academy of Sciences has a very large Siberian branch, which includes more than 30 institutes, as well as the Ural and Far Eastern scientific centers. The Academy also includes 6 films: Kazan, Bashkir, Dagestan, Karal, Kola and Komi branch.

Each union republic has its own Academy of Sciences, technically connected with the Academy of Sciences of the USSR.

The Academy of Sciences made a huge contribution to the development of Soviet physics and mathematics. We will talk about it in the articles published in this issue.
