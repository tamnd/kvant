M261. A hoop of radius $R$, placed on a stationary circle of radius $r < R$, begins to roll along this circle. Prove that the point of the hoop describes the same trajectory that describes the outside along the same circle of radius $r$ (Fig. 1, a, b). At what ratio of the length of sides $AB$ and $AD$ will the angle $PAQ$ have the greatest magnitude? What is this largest value in the special case $p = 2$, $q = \frac{3}{2}$ (Fig. 2)?

E. G. Gotman
