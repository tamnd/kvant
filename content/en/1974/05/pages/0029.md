LEONARD EULER

B. N. Delaunay

During the existence of the Academy of Sciences in Russia, apparently its most significant member was the mathematician Leonhard Euler. In this article we talk about his life and some of his mathematical works:

The 17th century was an unusual century for mathematics. Descartes and Fermat created analytical geometry, and Newton and Leibniz created differential and integral calculus. These two magnitudes of achievement in mathematics raised man to a significant new scientific level. The opportunity has opened up to solve problems that were not possible in previous eras. Methods developed in integral and differential calculus made it possible to solve the problem of the tangent, the maxima and minima of the variable being studied, the curvature of a line at its different points, and after Newton and Leibniz managed to prove a significant theorem of analysis connecting differential and integral calculus, it turned out to be possible to calculate areas, volumes, and find the centers of gravity of such figures for which it was previously impossible to even dream of doing this. After all these achievements, the most profound further results in the field of analysis *) belong to Jacob Bernoulli, his younger brother Johann and Johann's sons - Nikolai and Daniel - a friendship arose that played a very important role in Euler's life.
