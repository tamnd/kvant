Faculty of Psychology

Option 1

1. Two workers were tasked with processing a batch of 72 identical parts. The first worker processed part of this batch in 3 hours, after which he was replaced by a second worker, who processed the rest of the batch in 4 hours. How many parts will the second worker process in an hour if it takes him $1/2$ hours more time to process 18 parts than it takes the first worker to process 18 parts?
2. Find all roots of the equation
$$2 \log_2 \sin x + \log_2 (4 \text{ ctg}^2 x) = \log_2 3.$$
3. In a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ (S is the vertex), the side of the base is $a$, the side edge of the paramid is $5a/\sqrt{2}$. Let $A$ and $C$ be opposite vertices of the base of the paramid, $E$ the middle of edge $SC$. Find the distance from the center of the ball inscribed in the paramid $SABCD$ to the plane drawn through the points $A$ and $E$ parallel to the diagonal $BD$ of the base $ABCD$.
4. Find all integers $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$2^{\frac{3}{2}} \log_2 (21 - 5x) < 32 + 2^{\log_3 (8x - 7)}$$
Option 2

1. Two automatic machines process plywood. The first machine began processing a batch of $64 \cdot m^2$ plywood and processed part of this batch in 4 hours, after which the rest of the batch was processed in 2 hours by the second machine. How much plywood will the second machine process in an hour if it processes $24 \cdot m^2$ in one hour more than it takes to process $24 \cdot m^2$ the first machine?
2. Find all integers $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log_3 (2 \text{ ctg}^2 x) = \log_3 2 - 2 \log_3 (2 \cos x).$$
3. The sides of the base of a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ (S is the vertex) are equal to $a$, the height of the paramid is $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} a$. Let $B$ and $D$ be opposite vertices of the base of the paramid, $E$ the middle of edge $SC$. Find the distance from the center of the ball described near the paramide $SABCD$ to the plane drawn through the points $A$, $D$, $E$.
4. Find all integers $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$3^{\frac{5}{2}} \log_3 (13 - 4x) - 3^{\log_2 (3x - 2)} < 47.$$

Department of Structure and Applied Linguistics of the Faculty of Philology

Option 1

1. The amount equal to 53 kopecks is made up of three-kopeck and five-kopeck coins, the total number of which is less than 15. If in this set of coins the three-kopeck coins are replaced by five-kopeck coins, and the five-kopeck coins by three-kopeck coins, then the resulting amount will decrease compared to the original, but not more than 1.5 times. How many three-point coins were in the set?
2. A plane that does not contain the vertices of a given right $n$-gonal paramid divides the collection of these vertices into two parts. How many ways can such a division be carried out?
3. Find the minimum value of the following expression:
$$\cos 2x - 8 \cos x.$$
4. Is it possible to use $ABCD$ to describe a circle if $\Rightarrow ADC = 30^\circ$, $AB = 3$, $BC = 4$ and $AC = 6$?
5. Solve the equation
$$\log_x - 1 \sqrt{2x^2 - 8x + 9} = \frac{1}{2}.$$
Option 2

1. In a class of less than 27 students, a test was written. Among the grades given for it there are only grades “3”, “4” and “5”. The same number of students received grades “3” and “5,” and more than 23 received grades higher than “3.” An odd number of students received grades “4.” How many ratings of “3” and “4” are given?
2. The faces of the trihedral sign, the numbered numbers from 1 to 5, are painted with different colors so that the faces that have a common edge are painted with different colors. How many different methods of such coloring exist that are identical only if, in each of them, edges with the same numbers are edged in the same color?
3. Find the minimum value of the following:
$$\frac{1}{\cos^4 x} - \text{tg}^2 x.$$
4. A circle can be drawn around the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Also, $AB=3$, $BC=4$, $CD=5$ and $AD=2$. What is the length of the segment $AC$?
5. Solve the equation
$$\log_x \sqrt{2x^2 - 3x + 2} = 1.$$
