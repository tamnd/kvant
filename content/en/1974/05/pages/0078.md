ME: Having solved these problems, I will be able to force the coefficient to “eat up” the sector as well.

Mathematician. - Of course, because a sector is the intersection of a triangle with a circle, and you can get both of these figures. By the way, here's another task for you.

Problem 3. How to make the coefficient semicircle?

Me. - I’ll think about your tasks. I wonder if you completely came up with the “Hungry Goat Theory” yourself or did you use some mathematical concepts?

Mathematician. — I have already talked about such things as the distance from a point to a segment, the intersection of figures. But, in my opinion, the most necessary mathematical concept that was used is the concept of “neighborhood of a figure of radius R.”

Definition. The neighborhood of the radius R of the figure Φ is called the polyline of all those points; the distance from the component points of Φ is not transformed by R (here R is a non-negative number).

J. - What is “the distance from a point to a figure”?

Mathematician. - This is a natural generalization of the concept of “distance from a point to a segment.”

Definition. The distance from a point to a segment, the intersection of figures. But, in my opinion, the most necessary mathematical concept that was used is the concept of “neighborhood of a figure of radius R.”

Definition. The neighborhood of the radius R of the figure Φ is called the polyline of all those points; the distance from the component points of Φ is not transformed by R (here R is a non-negative number).
