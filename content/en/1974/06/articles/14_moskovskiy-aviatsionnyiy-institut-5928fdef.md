Moscow Aviation Institute

We talked about MAI in detail in «Kvant» No. 6 for 1973. Now at all faculties of MAI, except for the Faculty of Applied Mathematics, all entrance exams are conducted in written form, and in mathematics there are two exams: in algebra and in geometry with trigonometry. The options for mathematics and physics with applications and tasks include theoretical questions. Applicants entering the Faculty of Applied Mathematics will take oral and written exams in mathematics, so for them the options for the written exam in mathematics do not contain a theoretical question. The materials below will introduce readers to the version of the tasks offered at the entrance exams to the MAI in 1973.

Mathematics
Faculty of Applied Mathematics

Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3-x} - \sqrt{\frac{1-x}{2-x}} = 1.$$

2. Buses and taxis left the two cities towards each other at the same time. When the taxi driver has covered half the distance, the taxi has 19.2 km left to the end of the route, and when the taxi driver has covered half the distance, the taxi has 12 km left to the end of the route. Find the ratio of the speed of such and the buses, if the speeds of such and the buses are plotted along the entire route, and each must arrive at the city from which the other left.

3. For what real values ​​of $a$ is the sum of squared roots of the equation
$$2\log_4(2x^2-x+2a-4a^2) + \log_{0,5}(x^2+ax-2a^2) = 0$$
more than one?

4. Two circles of radii $R$ and $\frac{R}{2}$ touch each other externally. One of the ends of a segment of length 2R, forming an angle of 30° with the line of centers, coincides with the center of a circle of smaller radius. What part of the segment lies outside the circle?

5. The vertex of a regular quadrilateral is parallel to the corresponding sides of the base of the cube. The sides of the base ABCD are parallel to the corresponding sides of the base of the cube. Find the volume of a cube lying inside the paramid if $\Rightarrow ASB = \text{arctg } \frac{4}{3}$ and the edge of the cube is equal to one.

Algebra written exam

Option 2
1. Solving a quadratic equation. Vieta's formulas.
2. Solve the equation
$$2^{x-1}(2^x+3^{x-1})=9^{x-1}.$$
3. Find the sum of the first seven terms of a geometric progression, the sum of the first and fourth terms of which is 9, and the sum of the second and third is 6.
4. A quick meal will cover the distance between stations 3.5 hours faster than a commodity one. If each train took the same amount of time as another train travels between stations, then an express train would travel 350 km longer than a freight train. If the speed of each train was increased by 10 km/h, then the fast train would cover the distance between stations 2.4 hours faster than the freight train. Calculate the speed of travel and the distance between stations.
5. At what values ​​of $a$ is the sum of squared roots of the equation
$$2\log_4(2x^2-x+2a-4a^2) + \log_{0,5}(x^2+ax-2a^2) = 0$$
more than one?

Written exam in geometry

Option 3
1. Prove the theorem of sines.
2. Compute $(\sin \alpha - \sin \beta) \sin \alpha + (\cos \alpha - \cos \beta) \cos \alpha$ if $\sin \frac{\alpha-\beta}{4} = a$.
3. Solve the equation:
$$(1-tg x)(1+\sin 2x)=1+tg x.$$
4. In the trapezoid ABCD, diagonals AC and BD are drawn, intersecting at point F. From vertex C, a straight line CK is drawn, parallel to the side AD, which intersects the continuation of BD at point L so that DF = BL. Find the AB:CD ratio.
5. A circle of radius R and an equilateral triangle on side $R\sqrt{3}$ lie in mutually perpendicular planes. However, the sides of the triangle lie in the plane of the circle. A segment connecting the centers of a circle and a triangle forms angles equal to 30° with their planes. Find the length of the part of the side of the triangle lying inside the circle.

Physics

Ticket 1

1. Insulators and conductors. Law of conservation of electric charges.
2. What amount of heat will be released when 20 e of water vapor condenses at 100° C and cools the resulting water to 20° C? Specific heat of water $c = 4200 \text{ } \partial x/\partial z$. Specific heat of vaporization of water at 100° C $r = 539 \text{ } \kappa a/\kappa c$.
3. A mass weighing 100 N lies on an absolutely smooth inclined plane forming an angle of 30° with the horizontal. What force must be applied to the mass parallel to the plane to keep it in balance? Find the reaction force of the plane.
4. Two balls with masses $m_1 = 2 e$ and $m_2 = 3 e$ move in a horizontal plane with a speed corresponding to an angle $\alpha = 90^\circ$ with each other. The balls collide inelastically. What is the total momentum of these balls after the impact?
5. At a distance $d = 0,14 m$ from the top of the concave mirror there is an object whose height is $l = 0,06 m$. Mirror focal length $F = 0,11 m$. Find the height $L$ of the image of the object.

Ticket 2

1. Capacitors. Capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor.
2. A pilot fires a cannon from an airplane. The speed of the aircraft relative to the Earth is 900 km/h. The speed of the projectile relative to the aircraft is 750 m/s. Determine the initial speed of the projectile relative to the Earth if: a) the shot is fired in the direction of flight, b) in the opposite direction.
3. What array can drive an electric locomotive uniformly up a slope of 0.004 if the traction force of the electric locomotive is 250 kN and the friction coefficient is $k = 0,06$?
4. 2 liters of water at 40°C were automatically poured into the radiator, and then 4 liters were added at 85°C. Determine the temperature of the mixture.
5. What force acts on a conductor 10 cm long in a uniform magnetic field with a magnetic induction of 2.6 ml, if the current in the conductor is 12 A, and the angle between the direction of the current and the induction lines is: a) 90°; b) 30°?

Ticket 3

1. Laws of light refraction. Complete reflection of light.
2. The speed of wave propagation in the medium is 200 m/s. Calculate the period of oscillation if the closest distance between the two oscillations in opposite phases is 20 cm.
3. Magnetic flux, an arbitrary contour of a conductor, uniformly changes by 0.6 bb so that e. d. c induction turns out to be equal to 1.2 b. Find the time of change of the magnetic flux and the strength of the induction current if the conductor resistance is 0.24 ohms.
4. On a horizontal section of the road, the car moved at a speed of 72 km/h for 10 minutes, and climbed at a speed of 36 km/h for 20 minutes. What is the average speed along the entire journey?
5. The passenger jet aircraft has 4 engines, developing a thrust force of 10 T each, and flies at a speed of 900 km/h. How much kerosene will an engine use for a flight of 4000 km? K.p.d. engine 0.25. Heat of combustion of kerosene $q = 46,2 \cdot 10^6 \text{ } \partial x/\partial z$.

Ticket 4

1. Photoelectric effect. Photocells and their application.
2. A person weighing 60 kg runs at a speed of 8 km/h. Having caught up with a cart moving at a speed of 2.9 km/h, he jumps on it. What will be the speed of the cart after this if its mass is 80 kg?
3. A glass contains 100 s of water at a temperature of 10 ° C. 40 s of ice are dropped into it at a temperature of -10 ° C. What remains in the glass after the temperatures are equalized?
4. Specific heat of water $c_1 = 4200 \text{ } \partial x/\partial z$. Specific heat of ice $c_2 = 2100 \text{ } \partial x/\partial z$. Specific heat of ice $\lambda = 3,3 \cdot 10^5 \text{ } \partial x/\partial z$.
4. A body falls freely from a height of 80 m. What path does it take at the last second of the fall?
5. At a current of 10 A, the power released in the external circuit is 200 W, and at a current of 15 A, the power in the external circuit is 240 W. What is the internal resistance, e.g. d. With. and the generator short circuit current?
Ticket 5

1. Boyle-Marint and Geylosskak laws.
2. A person of height $h = 1,75 m$ is at a distance $r = 6 m$ from the pillar. At what distance from himself should a person place a flat mirror horizontally on the ground in order to see through it the top of a pillar of height $h = 7 m$?
3. In what wavelength range does a radio transmitter operate if the capacitance of its oscillating circuit can vary from 60 nf to 240 nf, and the inductance is 50 mken?
4. What is the height of the television tower in Ostank if a ball, falling from the tower without an initial speed, covers the last 185 mp in 2 s? Air resistance is not taken into account.
5. When taking a turn, the aircraft has a maximum speed of $v = 75 \text{ } \text{m}/\text{s}$, length of the turn before takeoff $S = 500 \text{ } \text{m}$, aircraft weight $m = 19 \text{ } \text{m}$, friction coefficient $k = 0,02$. The movement during the turn-off is considered equal to the speed. What should be the minimum propulsion power for an airplane to take off?
