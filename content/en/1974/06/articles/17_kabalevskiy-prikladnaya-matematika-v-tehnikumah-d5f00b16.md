"Applied Mathematics"
in technical schools

There is no doubt that the reader of the magazine «Kvant» will be interested in getting acquainted with one of the youngest and at the same time very exciting specialties related to mathematics. This specialty is called “applied mathematics.”

This specialty is in very short supply, primarily due to the great development of electronic computer technology and the associated significant expansion of the scope of application of mathematical methods. Nowadays, a whole army of applied mathematicians is needed in the most diverse sectors of the national economy. They must be able to apply mathematical methods, participating in solving a variety of applied problems, starting from the formulation of the problem and ending with the last stage - compiled software solutions to the problem on an electronic computer. These solutions must be able, with the help of a mathematical engineer or independently, to create algorithms for solving a problem and write them in the form of a program in the language of a specific machine or in one of the algorithmic languages.

Secondary specialized educational institutions accept boys and girls who have completed the 8th or 10th grade of a general secondary school for the specialty “applied mathematics”. In the first case, the course of study lasts 2 years 10 months, in the second - only 1 year 10 months, since those who complete 10 classes are accepted immediately into the second year.

Recently, the USSR Ministry of Higher and Secondary Special Education approved a new curriculum. According to this plan, all subjects of the course are combined into a general education cycle, a general mathematical cycle (mathematical analysis, linear algebra and analytical geometry, mathematical logic, probability theory and mathematical statistics) and a specialized cycle, consisting of two parts:

a) general specialized subjects (algorithmic languages, programming and computational mathematics; mathematical programming; fundamentals of computer technology and organization of computational work; operations research);

b) professional subjects (fundamentals of economics and the application of mathematical methods in solving its problems; the application of mathematical methods in automation and control systems, as well as in natural scientific research).

During the entire course of study, students undergo 3 types of practice. In the 2nd year, students, in the process of educational practice, create programs for solving small educational problems on electronic computers. In the 3rd year, as part of technology practice, students, under the guidance of experienced mathematical engineers, write coursework, solving problems of practical importance. This practice is followed by pre-graduation practice, which is direct preparation for work on the diploma project.

To enter secondary specialized educational institutions with a specialization in “applied mathematics”, you must take 2 exams: an oral exam in mathematics and a written exam in literature.
