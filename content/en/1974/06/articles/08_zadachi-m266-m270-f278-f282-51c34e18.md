Also put assignments from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with the address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the solution). Please send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in duplicate along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope put: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

M266—M270; F278—F282

M266. Given a convex $n$-gon.
a) Prove that if for each three successive vertices of a $n$-gon one constructs a circle passing through these three vertices, and from the $n$ obtained circles selects the one with the largest radius, then this circle contains within itself the entire given $n$-gon;
6) Prove that if for each three consecutive sides of a $n$-gon one constructs a circle tangent to these sides, and from the $n$ obtained circles choose the one with the largest radius, then it will contain inside the given $n$-gon.

A. B. Karzanov

M267. In the sequence of triplets of integers (2, 3, 5) (6, 15, 10)... each triple is obtained from the previous one in this way: the first is multiplied by the second, the second by the third, and the third by the first, and the resulting products give a new triple.

Prove that none of the numbers obtained in this way will be powers of an integer: square, cube, etc.

F. A. Bartenev

M268. There is a piece in the corner of the chessboard. The first player can move it twice in a row as a common knight (two squares in one direction and one in a perpendicular direction), and the second player can move it once with a knight with an extended move (three squares in one direction and one in a perpendicular direction). So they take turns. The first one strives to place the figure in the opposite corner, and the second one tries to place it. Which of them will win (board size is $n \times n$, where $n \geq 4$)?

P. Katsylo, 10th grade student (Moscow)

M269. Let us denote by $T_k(n)$ the sum of the products of $k$ numbers from 1 to $n$. For example,
$$T_2(4) = 1 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 4 = 35.$$
a) Find the general formula for $T_2(n)$ and $T_3(n)$.
b) Prove that $T_k(n)$ is expressed as a polynomial in $n$ of degree $2k$ (for every natural number $k \geqslant 2$).
b) State a method for finding the polynomials $T_k(n)$ ($k = 2, 3, 4, 5, \dots$) and apply it to find the polynomials $T_4(n)$ and $T_5(n)$.
E. A. Yasinovy

M270. Let $AB$ and $CD$ be two chords of a circle, and let the points $K$ and $H$ be constructed so that all four angles $KAB, KCD, HBA$ and $HDC$ are straight lines. Prove that line $KH$ passes through the center of the circle and through the intersection point of lines $AD$ and $BC$.
I. F. Sharytsin, A. I. Yanovsky

F278. An Earth satellite moves in a circular orbit at an altitude of $h = 760$ km above the Earth's surface. They want to transfer it to an elliptical orbit with a maximum distance from the Earth’s surface of $H = 40 000$ km and a minimum distance from the surface of $h = 760$ km. How much do you need to change the satellite's speed for this? What will be the orbital period of the satellite in its new elliptical orbit?
A. Borzhievsky, 10th grade student (Orishinskaya s.sh., Khmelnitsky region)

F279. A cylinder made of non-teleconducting material is divided by a non-teleconducting partition into two parts, the volumes of which are $V_1$ and $V_2$. The first part contains gas at a temperature of $T_1$ under a pressure of $p_1$. In the second - the same gas, but at a temperature of $T_2$ and under a pressure of $p_2$. What temperature will be established in the cylinder if the partition is removed?

F280. A light source at a distance $3a$ from a lens with focal length $a$. How many times will the average illumination of a spot on the screen, which is located at a distance $1,5a$ from the lens, change if a plane-parallel glass plate of thickness $0,5a$ is placed between the source and the lens? The refractive index of glass is $n$.
