PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

At the Department of Mathematics of Moscow Technologists in May, the topics “inscribed and circumscribed spheres”, “shield and cone”, “solving problems from various sections of stereometry”, “about proofs in mathematics”, “proof of inequalities” were discussed. On the topic of stereometry, we offer a number of typical homework problems for you to solve on your own.

Mathematica

1. (MEPhI, 1973). There are two bodies moving along a circle in opposite directions, the first one uniformly with speed v, and the second one uniformly accelerated with linear acceleration a. At the initial moment, they were at the same point of circle A, and the speed of the second body was equal to zero. After what time will the first meeting of these bodies occur if the second meeting occurs at point A?

2. (MEPhI, 1973). At the base of a quadrangular paramid lies a square on side a. Since through the vertex of the paramida and the diagonals of the base, they are inclined to the plane of the base, respectively, at angles φ and ψ. Find the volume of the paramid if its height intersects one of the sides of the base of the paramid.

3. (MEPhI, 1973). The height of a straight steep cone is equal to H. Two mutually perpendicular generatrices divide the base circle into two arcs, one of which is half as long as the other. Find the volume of the cone.

4. A sphere is built at the height of the cone as on the diameter. The area of ​​the part of the surface of the sphere lying in the cone is $1/2$ the area of ​​the base of the cone. Find the angle between the height and the generatrix of the cone.

5. A right triangular limb is inscribed in a ball of radius R. The base of the sign is a right triangle with an acute angle α, and its largest side face is a square. Find the volume of recognition.

6. (MEPhI, 1972). In the triangular paramid SABC the plane angles at the vertex S are known: $\Rightarrow BSC = 90^\circ$, $\Rightarrow ASC = \Rightarrow ASB = 60^\circ$. The vertices A, S and the midpoints of the edges SB, SC, AB, AC lie on the surface of a ball with a radius of 3 m. Prove that the edge SA is the diameter of this ball and find the volume of the paramid.

7. (MSU, 1972). When the balls touch each other in pairs and some plane. The points of contact of the balls with the plane form a right triangle with a leg equal to 3 cm and an opposite angle of 30°. Determine the radii of the given balls.

At the end of May, the topics “about proofs in mathematics” and “proof of inequalities” were studied on phones. We offer you a series of problems on these topics.

8. Prove that if a + b = 1, where a and b are real numbers, then

a) $a^2 + b^2 \geq \frac{1}{2}$; 6) $a^4 + b^4 \geq \frac{1}{8}$.

9. Prove that if a, b, c, d > 0, then $\frac{a + b + c + d}{4} \geq \sqrt[4]{abcd}$, and the equality sign holds only for a = b = c = d.

10. Prove that if $a_1 > 0, a_2 > 0, \dots, a_n > 0, a_1a_2...a_n = 1, n \geq 2$ then $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n \geq n$.

in this case, the equal sign occurs only at $a_1 = a_2 = \dots = a_n$.

11. Prove that if $a_1 > 0, a_2 > 0, \dots, a_n > 0$, then $\frac{a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n}{n} \geq \sqrt[n]{a_1a_2...a_n}$, and the equality sign holds only for $a_1 = a_2 = \dots = a_n$.

12. Prove that if positive numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ form an arithmetic progression, then

$$\sqrt{a_1a_n} \leq \sqrt[n]{a_1a_2...a_n} \leq \frac{a_1 + a_n}{2}$$

13. Prove that for n ≥ 2 the inequality $n^n \leq (n!)^2$ holds.

14. Prove that if $\frac{\pi}{4} < \varphi < \frac{\pi}{2}$ then $\frac{\sin^2 \varphi}{\cos \varphi (\sin \varphi - \cos \varphi)} \geq 4$.

15. Prove that if $\frac{\pi}{4} < \varphi < \frac{\pi}{2}$ then $\frac{\sin \varphi}{\cos^2 \varphi (\sin \varphi - \cos \varphi)} > 8$.
