$$F_n^{(k)} = \frac{1}{2} n [(k - 2) n + 4 - k].$$

Multi-duple, or, as they are often called, figured numbers, have been known since ancient times. It is believed that they first appeared in the 6th century BC - in the school of Pythagoras. Subsequently, many mathematicians were interested in these numbers. Many important and difficult theorems have been proven about them. Let us give one of them: every natural number is either the sum of at most three triangular numbers, or the sum of at most four square numbers, or the sum of at most five-fold numbers, etc.

This theorem was formulated without proof by one of the greatest mathematicians of the 17th century, Pierre Fermat. It attracted the attention of many outstanding mathematicians - Euler, Lagrange, Lejandard, Gauss. Each of them contributed to its proof, but the theorem was completely proven only in the 19th century by the French mathematician Coche.
