Conclusion

Spectral representations are universal; they objectively characterize the practical important properties of any oscillations and waves. As we have seen, the spectra are intimately connected with the form and duration of the corresponding processes and signals. Spectral representations make it possible to compare various information (music, speech, interference, images, etc.) from a single perspective, determine the width of the spectrum of the signals encoding them, and based on this judge the possibility of transmitting these signals over certain communication lines.
