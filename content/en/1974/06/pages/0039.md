The period of such oscillations

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g'}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{g \cos \alpha}}.$$

Task 6. The softener consists of a metal ball suspended on a loop thread. How will the period of its oscillations change if the ball is given a positive charge, and another negatively charged ball is placed below, on the same vertical with the magnet (Fig. 9, a)?

In addition to the force of gravity and the tension force of the thread, the charged magnetic ball is also subject to an electronic force of attraction to the oppositely charged ball located below. This force, like the force of gravity, will give a component to the direction of movement (Fig. 9, b), which will increase the returning force:

$$F_B = F'_B + F''_B.$$

In this case, the average value of the magnetic acceleration will increase, and the corresponding period will decrease. The equilibrium position of the magnet remains vertical.

Exercises

1. A mathematical magnet, consisting of a thread 243 cm long and a steel ball with a diameter of 4 cm, makes harmonious vibrations with an amplitude of 10 cm. Determine the speed of the ball when passing through the equilibrium position and the greatest value of the restoring force. The density of steel is 7.8 e/cm³.

2. A body of mass M, attached to a spring, oscillates with amplitude $x_0$ on a smooth horizontal table. At the moment when the body passes the equilibrium position, a piece of plate mass m falls on it from above and floats to it. How will the amplitude of the oscillations change?

3. A ball of mass $m_1 = 0,2$ ke lies on an absolutely smooth surface. Using a thread, throw it over the block; a weight of mass $m_2 = 0,1$ ke (Fig. 10) is attached to it. The block is a thin-walled cylinder with a mass of $m_3 = 0,3$ke. When this system is in equilibrium, the light spring is stretched by $\Delta l = 5$ cm. Determine the cyclic part of the free oscillations of the ball if the load is pulled down a little and released without a push.

4. A load of mass 0.5 ke, suspended on a weightless spring, oscillates with a frequency of 0.4 ke. What will be the frequencies of oscillation of the same load on two such springs, if once they are connected in series and the other time in parallel (Fig. 11)?

5. Ptruth is poured into the U-shaped tube. Find the period of oscillations of ptr if the cross-sectional area of ​​the tube is S, the mass of ptr is m and the density of ptr is p.

6. The horizontal stand performs a harmonic oscillation in the vertical direction with amplitude $x_1$. What can be the maximum frequency of these vibrations if the object lying on the stand is not separated from it?

7. The elevator rises upward by acceleration $a_1$ for time $t_1$, then with replacement $a_2$ for time $t_2$. In the elevator there is a math magnet $l$ long. How many oscillations will it make during its movement?

8. How will the period of oscillation of the magnet change (see problem 6 in the article) if a negatively charged ball is placed at the suspension point?
