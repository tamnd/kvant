2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{\frac{1+x}{1-x}} + \frac{\sqrt{1-x}}{2\sqrt{1+x}-1} \geq 0.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_{g} x (2+4\cos^2 x) = 2.$$

4. To a circle inscribed in a triangle with a perimeter of 18 cm, a tangent is drawn parallel to the base of the triangle. The tangent segment between the sides is 2 cm. Find the base of the triangle.

5. In a regular triangular rectangle, the side of the base is equal to $a$, and the side edge is equal to $l$. Through the center of the base, a plane is parallel to one of the sides of the base and parallel to the opposite side edge. Find the sectional area.

1. A lead bullet of mass $m$, flying at a speed of $v$, hits the initial position of a lead ball of mass $M$ and gets stuck in it (love strike). At what ratio of the masses of the bullet and the ball will they heat up to the highest temperature?

2. The lower ends of a very light step-ladder leaf are connected with a rope. Find its tension $T$ when the electrician with weight $P$ climbed the ladder to its middle. The floor is perfectly smooth. Each side of the leaf makes an angle $\alpha$ with the floor.

3. In a box covered with a partition (Fig. 1), there is one mole of a monatomic ideal gas under normal conditions. Immediately behind the partition there is a clamp with a piston in a free state, located in the void. Spring elasticity coefficient $k$. The partition is removed. How will pressure, temperature and volume be established in the system if there is no thermal interaction with the external environment? In the new equilibrium position, the clamp is not compressed to failure. Piston area $S$. The heat capacity of the piston can be claimed.

4. $m \cdot e$ water is poured into the saucer, and a heating glass with thin walls, turned upside down, is placed on top. To what absolute temperature $T_1$ (lowest) must the glass be heated together with the influence in it, so that after cooling to the temperature $T_0$ of the surrounding air, all the water would be drawn into it? Atmospheric pressure $p_a$, cross-sectional area of ​​the glass $S$, height $h$, specific gravity of water $d$. The volume of water poured is less than the volume of the glass. The phenomena of evaporation, surface tension and expansion of the glass are considered. The saucer is considered wide, so the height of the water poured into it is small.

5. Find the mechanical work done by electrical forces when turning the adjustment knob of a variable capacitor connected to a battery with e. d.s. $E = 300$ c. In this case, the capacity changes from $C_1 = 10$ mf to $C_2 = 100$ mf.

6. By irradiating a metal ball with light, we observe a photo effect. How will the red border of the photoelectric effect change if a positive charge $q$ is placed on the ball? The radius of the ball is $R_0$. Launch the registration electrons at a distance of $R \gg R_0$ from the center of the ball.
