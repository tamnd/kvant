M269. Let us denote by $T_k(n)$ the sum of the products of $k$ numbers from 1 to $n$. For example,
$$T_2(4) = 1 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 4 = 35.$$
a) Find the general formula for $T_2(n)$ and $T_3(n)$.
b) Prove that $T_k(n)$ is expressed as a polynomial in $n$ of degree $2k$ (for every natural number $k \geqslant 2$).
b) State a method for finding the polynomials $T_k(n)$ ($k = 2, 3, 4, 5, \dots$) and apply it to find the polynomials $T_4(n)$ and $T_5(n)$.
E. A. Yasinovy

M270. Let $AB$ and $CD$ be two chords of a circle, and let the points $K$ and $H$ be constructed so that all four angles $KAB, KCD, HBA$ and $HDC$ are straight lines. Prove that line $KH$ passes through the center of the circle and through the intersection point of lines $AD$ and $BC$.
I. F. Sharytsin, A. I. Yanovsky

How much do you need to change the satellite's speed for this? What will be the orbital period of the satellite in its new elliptical orbit?
