⟦folio 47⟧

Leningrad State University

Leningrad Order of Lenin and Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after. A. A. Zhdanova trains specialists in mathematics and physics at three faculties.

The Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics has specialties: mathematics, mechanics, astronomy and hydropower engineering.

Students of the Faculty of Physics receive specialties: physics, geophysics, radiophysics and electronics.

The Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes produces specialists in applied mathematics.

Entrance exams for the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and the Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes in August, and for the Faculty of Physics - in July. Below we present options for the written entrance exam in mathematics and the tasks for the oral exam in physics at Leningrad State University in 1973.

Mathematics

Option 1
(Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)

1. The machine and tractor station must send a certain number of tractors to the state farm to perform a certain job, and it is known that 10 tractors can complete this work in 12 working days. In addition, it is known that the state farm pays the repair team 30 rubles during the entire period of work on the state farm. for 1 day and each tractor 4 rubles. 80 kop. for 1 day of work at the state farm and back (during the period of work, the tractors are at the state farm). With what number of tractors will the total payment to workers for completing all work be the least? What is the minimum wage for workers?

2. Solve the equation
$(2 + a) \cos^2 x + \sin x \cos x = (1 - a) \sin^2 x$ for all values ​​of parameter $a$.

3. Choose the number $b$ so that the maximum value of the function
$|-2x^2+x+b|$
on the interval $0 \leq x \leq 1$ was the smallest.

4. In the rectangular $ABCD$ we have: $AB = a$, $AD = b$. Find on the side $AB$ the point $E$ for which $\overrightarrow{CED} = \overrightarrow{AED}$.

5. At the end the height is equal to the diameter of the base. A plane tangent to the lateral surface of the cone is drawn through some generatrix of the cone, and in this plane a straight line is drawn through the vertex of the cone at an angle $\alpha$ to the generatrix. Find the angle that the drawn straight line makes with the plane of the base.

Option 2
(Faculty of Chemistry and Psychology and Faculty of Economics (except for the Department of Political Economy))

1. From a container of $v$ liters filled with $p$ percent acid solution, some amount of solution was poured and the same amount of $q$ percent solution ($q < p$) was added. The strength of the resulting rastorov turned out to be $k$ times less than the strength of the rastorov, which will be obtained if, when pouring together a $q$-percent rastorov, you use a rasta of 2 times the strength. How much of the $q$-percent raster was spent?

2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{x+2\sqrt{x-1}}+\sqrt{x-2\sqrt{x-1}} > \frac{x}{2}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$(\cos 2x+\cos x+1)^2 = 2(2\cos x+1)(\cos 2x-\cos x).$

4. In the equilateral triangle $ABC$ ($AB=BC$) the median $AD$ is drawn. Find angle $BAD$ if the angle at vertex $B$ is equal to $\alpha$.

5. In a regular triangular primina, $r$ of the circle described near the base and the angle $\alpha$ of the inclination of the lateral face to the plane of the base are extracted. Find the radius of the circumscribed sphere.

Option 3
(Faculty of Geology and Department of Political Economy, Faculty of Economics)

1. The work plan is to produce 260 parts in a few days. For the first 2 days he worked according to plan, and in the subsequent days of work he exceeded the plan, producing 10 more parts daily than was provided for by the plan. Therefore, 268 parts were already manufactured 2 days before the deadline. How many parts should be produced according to plan in one day?

2. Solve the equation
$$2\log_2x \mathrm{tg} x + \log_t x \sin x = 3.$$

3. On the plane, find the locus of a point whose coordinates $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equation
$$x = |x-y|^2 + y.$$

4. Determine the area of ​​the trapezoid if its bases are 6 cm and 11 cm, one of the sides is 4 cm, and the sum of the angles at the bottom base is $\pi/2$.
