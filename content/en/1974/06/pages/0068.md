The signature continues for the second receipt in 1974 for the popular scientific journal of physics and mathematics «Kvant».
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to take part in optimids, and are eager to be seriously involved in the exact sciences in the future.
Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet become interested in physics or mathematics, and whose interest in the exact sciences is still dormant. «Kvant» in general is a magazine that teaches you to think and work, and not a magazine for “easy reading”.
Our magazine is also useful for teachers. Nowadays, a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics is taking place. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work. He systematically publishes articles on new programs and explains material not yet included in the textbook.
In 1974, «Kvant» not only included individual problems for younger students, but also published notes for them in the section «Kvant» for younger students.

TO OUR READERS • TO OUR READERS

The main content of the journal is a “physics and mathematics school”; that is, materials that help to better understand physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to teach various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems.
The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were offered at entrance exams for various types, problems that were offered at optimids, and simply interesting problems.
Notes describing physical instruments will help the reader to set up and prove a physical experiment.
The magazine publishes on its pages a review of scientific achievements and problems that are still waiting to be solved; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are born.
The magazine constantly publishes solutions to books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
