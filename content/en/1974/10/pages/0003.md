On the first page of the cover you see a photograph of a gilded ready-made table, made in the 16th century. It includes several different compasses, protractors, a ruler, etc. However, when solving geometric problems, most of its tools are not needed: all construction problems that can be solved with their help can be solved with one compass. However, this became known only 200 years later, when the Italian mathematician Mascheroni proved that all constructions made with a compass and a ruler can be done with one compass (see article on p. 2).
