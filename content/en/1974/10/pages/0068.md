To our readers

The subscription for 1975 to the popular scientific and mathematical journal «Kvant» continues.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to take part in optimids.
Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet become interested in physics or mathematics, and whose interest in exact sciences is still dormant.
Our magazine is also useful for the reader. Now comes a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work. He systematically publishes articles on new programs and unpacks material not yet included in the textbook.
«Kvant» pays great attention to ten-cash coins. We constantly publish articles for applicants to universities with an analysis of the tasks offered in the entrance exams to the country's leading universities and options for entrance exams.
«Kvant» publishes notes for younger students. In 1975, this section will be significantly expanded.
Let's talk about the articles that will be published «Kvant» in 1975.
In the article by U. Rams, “How discoveries are made,” many interesting examples show the progress of the development of science from the initial simple ideas and hypotheses to the discovery of the most important laws.
In a series of articles, A.G. Mordkovich talks in an accessible and interesting way about the derivative, one of the basic concepts of mathematical analysis.
A series of articles by S. G. Gindikin is devoted to the topic, the border between mathematics and mechanics. The reader will learn how mathematical problems can be solved using the apparatus of mechanics.
The article by G.V. Strotsky “Camera Obscura” will talk about the construction of an image in a camera obscura and the conditions for obtaining the existing image.
Two more articles will be related to optics. This is an article by the famous foreign scientist V. Weiskoff, “How light interacts with matter,” and an article about golophy.
The article by E. G. Belga “Knots on a Mathematician’s Desk” will tell you what a knot is (from a mathematician’s point of view, of course), and what operations can be performed on knots.
The article by M. B. Balk and N. A. Grigoriev will talk about the need for barycentric coordinates, which make scales useful in solving many practical problems.
In the article, I. F. Sharygin talks in half-charine form about the world of geometry with a number of dimensions greater than three.
In a series of articles, V. Nilme talks about what problems can be solved with a “compass and a ruler” and gives problems that cannot be solved with the help of these tools.
Thus, in the article by O. N. Karpukhna “Physics of Chemical Interaction” he talks about the complex process of chemical interaction.
A. Hatzet will talk about modeling so-called “transport problems” using electronic circuits.
In 1975, in the section “Practicum for applicants” a number of articles on mathematics and physics will be published to help schoolchildren prepare for college and options for university entrance exams will be given.
For the fifth year now, the Zadaniyaik magazine section has been teaching schoolchildren to solve difficult problems in mathematics and physics. Schoolchildren from the city, villages and villages participate in the “Zadaniiik” «Kvant» competition. The winners of the competition receive the right to participate in the “Information” section.
The magazine constantly publishes results on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
Readers will be able to read news about the work of physics and mathematics schools, about physics and mathematics for modern schoolchildren, about holding optimids and about work.byzov with schoolchildren in the “Information” section.
