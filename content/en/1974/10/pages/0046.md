$$A = 10^2 a + 10 b + c$$
where $a, b$ and $c$ are the digits of the number $A$. They need to find $A$ such that $3 A = (10^2 + 10 + 1)(a + b + c)$.
From here
$$37 (a + b + c) = 10^2 a + 10 b + c.$$
Hence,
$$63 a = 27 b + 36 c$$
or
$$7 a = 3 b + 4 c.$$
Let's assume that $b \geq c$. If $7 a = 3 b + 4 c$, then $7 (a - c) = 3 (b - c)$. From here
$$b - c = a - c = 0$$
or
$$b - c = 7, a - c = 3.$$
If $b < c$, then $7 (a - b) = 4 (c - b)$, whence
$$c - b = 7, a - b = 4.$$
(Remember that $a, b$ and $c$ are numbers). We finally obtain the following solutions: numbers $A$, in which all digits are the same (there are nine such numbers); the numbers 370, 481, 592 and the numbers 407, 518, 629 - fifteen numbers in total.
