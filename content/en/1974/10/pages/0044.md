⟦folio 42⟧

9th grade
First day
1. Find the smallest number of the form $|36^k - 5^l|$, where $k$ and $l$ are natural numbers.
2. Given two circles of radii $R$ and $r$, externally tangent. Various trapezoids $ABCD$ are constructed so that each of the circles touches both sides and one of the bases of the trapezoid. Find the smallest possible side length $AB$.
3. There are 50 numbers written around the circle, each of which is equal to +1 or -1. You need to find out the product of all numbers. In one question you can find out the product of three sides of numbers. What is the smallest number of questions to ask?
4. There are $n$ vectors on the plane, the length of each of which is 1. The sum of all $n$ vectors is equal to the zero vector. Prove that the vectors can be enclosed so that for all $k = 1, 2, \dots, n$ the following condition is satisfied: the sum of the vectors has length at most 2.
Second day
5. Find all three-digit numbers $A$ that have the following properties: the arithmetic mean of all numbers obtained from the number $A$ by various permutations of its digits is equal to $A$.
6. Given a convex polygon in which no triangle of area 1 can be placed. Prove that this polygon can be placed in a triangle of area 4.
7. Is it possible to arrange 1, 2, 3, ..., 100 in such an order that for no two numbers their half-uma is equal to any of the numbers placed between them?
10th grade
First day
1. For what real $a, b, c$ is the equality
$$|ax + by + cz| + |bx + cy + az| + |cx + ay + bz| = |x| + |y| + |z|$$
true for all real $x, y, z$?
2. Given a square $ABCD$. Points $P$ and $Q$ lie respectively on sides $AB$ and $BC$, with $BP = BQ$. Let $H$ be the base of the perpendicular dropped from the point $B$ onto the segment $PC$. Prove that the angle $DHQ$ is a right angle.
3. Several red and several sinus points are specified. Some of them are connected by segments. Let's call a point “special” if more than half of the points connected to it have a color different from its color. If there is at least one special point, then any special point is selected and repainted in a different color. Prove that after a finite number of steps there will be no singular point left.
4. There are $n$ vectors on the plane, the length of each of which is 1. The sum of all vectors is equal to the zero vector. Prove that the vectors can be enclosed so that for all $k = 1, 2, \dots, n$ the following condition is satisfied: the sum of the vectors has length at most 2.
Second day
5. On the segment $0 \leq x \leq 1$ the function $f$ is given. It is known that this function is non-negative and $f(1) = 1$. In addition, for any two numbers $x_1$ and $x_2$ such that $x_1 \geq 0, x_2 \geq 0$ and $x_1 + x_2 \leq 1$, the following condition holds: the sum of the vectors has length at most 2.
6) Is it true that for everyone $x$
$$f(x) \leq 1, 9 x$$
6. Given a triangle $ABC$ of area 1. Let $A_1, B_1$ and $C_1$ be the midpoints of the side $BC$, $AC$ and $AB$ respectively. What is the minimum area that the common part of triangles $A_1B_1C_1$ and $KLM$ can have if the points $K, L$ and $M$ lie on the segments $AB_1, CA_1$ and $BC_1$, respectively?
7. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 100 in such an order that for no numbers their half-uma is equal to any of the numbers placed between them?
