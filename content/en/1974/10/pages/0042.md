Olympics
SCHOOLCHILDREN

L. G. Limanov,
M. L. Smolyansky

Olympiad in mathematics

From April 11 to April 17, 1974, the final stage of the VIII All-Russian Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in the capital of the Armenian Republic, Eurasia.

Having started in December 1973, the Olympiad successfully reached its finish line. The schoolchildren had to do a lot of work during this time. They participated in modern ones in schools, districts, regions or resource areas. And now the schoolchildren, having successfully completed three stages, sat down at their desks again to take part in the fourth, most important stage of the All-Olympiad.

In addition to the winners of the region and Resurikan Olympiads, the winners of the VII All-Russian Olympiad were also allowed to participate in the final.type. In total, the following people took part in the final.type: 8 classes - 169 people, 9 classes - 212 people and 10 classes - 220 people.

On April 11, in the building of the Eurasian University, the grand opening of the final.type VIII All-Olympiad took place. The Minister of Education of Armenia S. T. Akhumyan, the Chairman of the Olympiad jury, Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences S. Mergelyan and others greeted the schoolchildren. A greeting from the Minister of Education of the USSR M.A. Prokofiev was also read:

“Dear guys! Today, in the cities of Donetsk, Gorky and Eurasia, the best representatives of the region, co-operative and autonomous resources of our country, meet - young physicists, chemists and mathematicians who have shown interest in science, the ability to work hard, have original thoughts and work independently with a book.

These qualities will help you cope with the questions posed. It is known that the strongest win. But above all, participation in this Olympiad allows you to hope that it will enrich you and lead to the achievement of the goal that you set for yourself.

I wish you more success in mastering knowledge! Let your passion for science and your creative attitude to work never weaken!”

On April 12 and 14, the 1st and 2nd rounds of the final type Olympiad took place. In total, schoolchildren from each class were given 7 problems. It took 4 hours to solve each type of problem.

This year, along with the typical “Olympic” problems, equally accessible to schoolchildren in grades 8, 9 and 10, the solution of which requires only wit and the ability to reason logically, problems were also given in which the solution required some technical difficulties.
