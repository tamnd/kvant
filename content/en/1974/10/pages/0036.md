Lemma 2. The point of contact of the ball with the plane $P$ is located in the center of a regular triangle formed by the centers of the circles of the bases of the cones (the ball, cones and plane $P$ from the problem statement).

Now figure 4 will allow us to solve the problem. We have

$$AO_1 = \frac{2r\sqrt{3}}{3}, BO_1 = r, SO_1 = \frac{4}{3}r.$$

The required radius $OA$ is easy to find from the right triangle $AOB$, in which the legs $AB$ and $\angle OBA = \frac{1}{2} (\angle ABS)$ are known, and all trionometric functions $\angle ABS$ are easily calculated.
