Physics Olympiad

The final round of the VIII All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics took place from April 10 to 16 in the ancient Russian city of Gorky, which arose seven and a half centuries ago at the confluence of the Oka and Volga rivers.

234 students of the 10th grade, 191 students of the 9th grade, 151 students of the 8th grade and 3 students of the 7th grade, carry out the work proposed to the students of the 8th grade. Participants of the VIII All-Union Olympid are the winners of the Resursan and regional Olympids in 1974, the winners of the VII All-Union Olympid, held in 1973 in Leningrad; The winners of the competition held by the magazine «Kvant»* were also here.

On April 10, the grand opening of the Olympiad took place at the Palace of Culture of the Automobile Plant. The representative of the Olympiad jury, Academician A.V. Gaponov-Grekov, addressed the participants with words of greetings and good wishes.

On the same day, representatives of the Allied Resursan delegations laid wreaths at the monuments to V.I. Lenin and A.M. Gorky.
