Experimental tour

On the experimental.type, all parts by class performed the same work.

8th grade

External load vibrations

1. The period of oscillations $T$ of the external load depends on the mass $m$ of the load (Fig. 9). Study this relationship using a spring, a stopwatch, and a set of weights with known masses. Draw a graph based on the results of the experiment. Build a formula that describes the resulting dependence of $T$ on $m$.

2. For certain load masses, it is easy to notice that oscillations along the springs are accompanied by magnetic-like oscillations. Study this phenomenon and describe its patterns.

9th grade

"Black box"
Study the "black box" (electrical quadrupole), which is only known to consist of four two-terminal circuits connected in a star (Fig. 10). For experiments, you can use a battery, a voltmeter and a milliampere.

On the task sheet that each experimenter received, the following sentence was made: “Attention! Do not short-circuit the battery through milliamperes! The device will not read this to you and will deprive you of any chance of getting a recognized place in the Optimide!”

10th grade

Lens with shutter
What happens to the image of an object obtained using a converging lens if half of the lens is covered with an opaque screen?

Answer this question, and then check your answer experimentally by using a lens to obtain an image of the arrow of an object on a white screen at two different magnifications $u: 1)$ $u > 3$; $2)$ $u < 1/3$.

Describe and report your experimental results. To check these explanations, come up with and carry out qualitative and quantitative control experiments, using any installation details at your discretion: lens, arrow, object, flashlight, white screen, black paper, rulers, scissors.

Carry out similar studies for the action image observed

Fig. 9. Fig. 10.

without a screen, and for a minimal image. It is convenient to use a light bulb filament as an object in these experiments.

When observing without a screen, be sure to place a light attenuator between the light source and the eye!

When assessing experimental work, I take into account the ability to handle instruments, clear and consistent execution of the experimental stages, correct theoretical interpretation of the results obtained, assessment of their accuracy, clarity of graphs, drawings, forms and style of the report.
