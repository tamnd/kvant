⟦folio 64⟧

To the article “Plant-technical college at the Moscow Automobile Plant named after. I.A. Likhachev"

Mathematics

Option 1

1. $\sqrt{\frac{8S \cos \beta}{\sin \alpha}}$. Note. Denote the side of the rhombus by $x$ and express the cross-sectional area $S$ through it.

2. $x_1 = \pi + 2k\pi$, $x_2 = (-1)^k \frac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$. Note. Convert the equation to the form $2\cos^2 \frac{x}{2} (2\sin x - 1) = 0$.

3. $x = 0$. Note. Denote $5^x$ by $y$ and reduce the equation to the form $2y^2 - y - 1 = 0$.

Option 2

1. $\frac{1}{3} \pi \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} \sin \alpha ab (a + b)$. Note. The body of revolution is a truncated cone with two cut out cones; consider the axial section of these cones.

2. $z = \arccos \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} + (-1)^k \arcsin \frac{2}{5 \sqrt{5}} + k\pi$. Note. Divide both sides of the equation by $\sqrt{5}$, after which you get $\sin (z - t)$ on the left, where $\cos t = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$, $\sin t = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$.

3. $x = 10$. Note. Denote $\lg x$ by $y$ and reduce the equation to the form

$$\lg x \cdot \frac{1}{3} \pi \sin^4 \alpha \cos^2 \alpha.$$

Note. Having examined the axial section of the cone, find the height and radius of the inscribed cone.

2. $t_1 = \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi$, $t_2 = (-1)^k \frac{\pi}{6} + k\pi$. Note. Reduce the equation to the form $2\sin^2 t - 3\sin t + 1 = 0$.

3. $x_1 = 0,01; x_2 = 0,1; x_3 = 10; x_4 = 100$. Note. Represent $x$ as $10^{1g}x$ and reduce the equation to the form $\lg^4 x - 5\lg^2 x + 4 = 0$.

Physics

1. $\alpha = 0,005$ glad.

Note. Due to the smallness of the angle $\alpha$, consider $tg \alpha = \alpha$.

2. $v = 2,8$ m/s.

Note. The ball rotates around the nose in a circle of radius $R = l - h$. Use the law of conservation of energy.

3. $P = \frac{E^2 R'}{(R' + r)^2} \approx \frac{E^2}{R'}$, where $R'$ is the load resistance, different for different connection methods. With a parallel connection, the amount of energy released per unit of time is 4 times greater.

4. $v = \sqrt{10} \cdot 10^7$ m/s.
