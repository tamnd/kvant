Factory-technical college
at the Moscow Automobile Plant named after. I. A. Likhacheva

Our plant - college is one of the youngest institutes in Moscow.

Eighty years ago, in 1966, its first graduate was qualified as a mechanical engineer.

The university has three faculties: automotive, mechanical and technological and evening.

Specialists who graduated from the automotive or mechanical-technological faculties work in the field of design, calculation and research of automobile, engine and bodywork, technical mechanical engineering, tool production, repair and modernization of metal-cutting equipment, electrophysical and electroelectric methods of metal processing, design of manufacturing and repair of technical bases and equipment of mechanical and assembly shops, technical literary production, metal forming, metal science, processing and technical thermal processing. At the evening faculty, in addition to those listed, training is also conducted in the specialty “economics and organization of the mechanical engineering industry.”

The main feature of the preparation of specializations at the automotive and mechanical-technological faculties is the organizational combination of theoretical and industrial training. Students of these faculties are enrolled in the event of passing the entrance exams, employees of ZIL, AZLK, 1 GPP and their films, as well as high school graduates sent for training by these factories.

The training system involves the transfer of theoretical training (“study week”) with work in production (“work week”).

During the “study week”, students study, as in other institutes, and during the “work week” they work at one of the base factories, fulfilling the production program. During the “work week,” theoretical classes are held four times a week for four hours in the morning or evening, depending on the wall at the plant.

During the entire period of study (6 years), students receive half the monthly salary and half the stipend every month.

A scholarship, increased by 15% compared to the usual one, is received by all successful students of the plant-college school.

Students of the first three years work in jobs, and in the fourth year they are transferred to engineering positions.

Engineers - graduates of a plant-technical college, who know the production well, where they worked for six years, have a significant time to immediately enter into the work of the plant and actively participate in solving the tasks assigned to them than graduates of trained day jobs.

The institute is doing a lot of work to introduce modern methods of teaching and monitoring students' knowledge. At the departments of mathematics, physics, graphics, automation, the learning process is carried out with the widespread use of such educational complexes and electronic equipment as “Ogonyok”, “KISI”, “Nairi”, etc.

Over the past two years, a program has been developed and an experiment has been conducted that makes it possible to organize entrance exams to our institute with the widespread use of the Minsk-32 electronic calculating machine. Next year, the machine will not only create a variety of ticket options for applicants, but will also help assess their level of preparedness.

Below are typical options offered on the entrance exams in mathematics and problems offered on the exam in physics in 1974.

**Mathematics**

**Option 1**

1. At the base of the primid there is a rhombus, one of the angles of which is equal to α. The lateral borders are equally inclined to the plane of the base. A plane is drawn through the midpoints of two adjacent sides of the base and the apex of the primid. This plane makes an angle equal to β with the plane of the base. The sectional area formed by this plane is equal to S. Find the side of the rhombus.

2. Find all solutions to the equation
(2 sin x − cos x)(1 + cos x) = sin²x.

3. Solve the equation
$$5^2(\log_2 2+x) - 2 = 5^x+\log_2 2.$$

**Option 2**

1. Triangle ABC is braced around a line lying in the plane of this triangle, passing outside it through vertex A and equally inclined to sides AB and AC. Find the volume of the bracing body if AB = a, AC = b and BAC = α.

2. Solve the equation
$$2 \sin z - \cos z = \frac{2}{5}.$$

3. Solve the equation:
$$\lg (\lg x) + \lg (\lg x^3 - 2) = 0.$$

**Option 3**

1. The height of the cone is equal to H, the angle between the generatrix and the height is equal to α. Another cone is inscribed into this cone so that the top of the second cone coincides with the center of the base of the first cone, and the corresponding generatrices of the cones are mutually perpendicular. Find the volume of the inscribed cone.

2. Solve the equation
$$3(1 - \sin t) + \sin^4 t = 1 + \cos^4 t.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$x^{1/3}x - 51gx = 0,0001.$$

**Physics**

**Vilet 1**

The beam passes through a biconvex thin lens and intersects the focal plane at a distance of 5 mm from the main focus. Lens focal length F = 1000 mm. Find the angle between the incident beam and the main optical axis of the lens.

**Vilet 2**

A mathematical mat, the length of which is l = 1000 mm, is deflected to a horizontal position and then released. Under the horizontal axis around which the matnik begins to move, parallel to it at a distance of h = 700 mm there is a thin rod (see figure), around which further bracing of the matnik occurs. Find the speed of the weight at the top point of the trajectory after the thread touches the rod.

**Vilet 3**

To the source with e.m. With. E and internal resistance r, a tile with two heating elements of resistance R each (r ≤ R) is connected. There is a heated kettle of water on the stove. Should resistances R be connected in parallel or in series so that the water in the kettle boils faster?

**Vilet 4**

An electron, flying between the plates of a capacitor, the length of which is l = 300 mm, deviates by d = 8 mm from the first direction, parallel to the plates of the capacitor E = 1 sq/m. Consider the field of the internal capacitor in water to be uniform.
