Once again about the clock-calendar

About 13 years ago, from a mathematics teacher, I learned about a calendar clock similar to those described in the 12th issue of «Kvant» for 1973.

But it turns out that it is not at all necessary to make a calendar where the amendments for each month are indicated. The amendments are easily filled in by the number of letters of each word in the following sentence, where it is necessary to take into account that the numbers 7 and 0 are equivalent:

Say (7) January
me (3) February
days (3) of March
months (6) of April
and (1) May
one (4) June
every (6) July
from (2) August
numbers (5) of September
name (7) October
to you (3) November
sme (5) December

It should be remembered that each year also has its own amendment. For example, they will find a pattern themselves. Pay special attention to the high year adjustment.

To find the serial number of the day of the week, you should also add the month and year correction to the date. If the received amount is not more than 7, then it will be the serial number of the day of the week. If it is greater than 7, then you need to take the remainder when dividing it by 7, which will be the answer.
