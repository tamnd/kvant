⟦folio 57⟧

Magazine «Kvant» for 1973 for successful performance in experimental and theoretical fields; V. Serenin (Khasyn village, Magadan region, northern highway, 8th grade) was awarded a prize from the Krasnoye Sormovo plant, established for an Olympiad participant who came from the “farthest point” of the Soviet Union; I. Sinser (Lisakovsk, Kustanya region, village school No. 1, 8th grade) was awarded a prize from the Khokhloma factory, established for a girl who won the Olympiad; A. Slyusar (Izhevsk, Udmurt Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, school No. 30, 8th grade) was awarded the prize of the Gorky Regional Committee of the Komsomol as the youngest winner of the Olympiad; G. Bespalova (Gorky, village school No. 40, 9th grade) was awarded a prize from the USSR Ministry of Education, established for a girl who won the Olympics; V. Dobrynin (Khmelnitsky village no. 17, 9th grade) was awarded a prize from the Leninskaya Smena newspaper for an original solution to problems; S. Kachur (village of Ivanovtsy, Mukachevo district, northern highway, 9th grade) was awarded the prize of the Krasnoe Sormovo plant, established for the winner of the Olympiad from a rural area; A. Bezlesky (Donetsk, s. sh. No. 10, 10th grade) was awarded the journal file «Kvant» for 1973 for successful studies in theoretical and experimental courses.

Academician A.V. Gaponov-Grekhov spoke at the closing of the Olympids. He congratulated the winners and wished them further success. Then the Olympiad participants watched a large concert of children's amateur art groups at the Automobile Plant's Palace of Culture.

In conclusion, we would like to wish greater success to the future Olympians.

Readers suggest tasks

1. Prove that $(1 + 6 \cdot 0) + [1 + 6 (0 + 1)] + [1 + 6 (0 + 1 + 2)] + \{1 + 6 [0 + 1 + 2 + \dots + (n - 1)]\} = n^3$.
   L. A. Maksimov

2. Prove that the equation $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz = 1$
   has only three integer solutions to the unknowns $x, y, z$.
   N. F. Maksimov

3. Prove inequalities:
   a) $(1 + x)^n + (1 - x)^n \leq 2^n$ at $n > 1$, $|x| \leq 1$;
   b) $(1 + x)^n + (1 - x)^n \geq 2^n$ at $0 < n < 1$, $|x| \leq 1$.

4. A, B, C are the angles of an acute triangle ABC. What's more:
   $$\sin A + \sin B + \sin C$$
   With. Comrade Berkovayko

5. Prove that $1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 4 \cdot 5 + \dots + n (n + 1) (n + 2) = \frac{(n + 1) (n + 2) (n + 3)}{4}$.
   A. S. Savin

6. Solve equation $$\frac{x + y + z}{4} = \overline{x, yz}$$.
   A. A. Beznosok

7. Solve equation $$\overline{abcd} + \overline{bcd} + \overline{cd} + d = 3022$$.
   E. Yu. Guel
