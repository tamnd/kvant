Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov

We place below samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and tickets for an oral exam in physics, intended in 1973 for applicants to the natural faculties of Moscow University.

Mathematics

Faculty of Chemistry

1. Alik, Borya and Vasya bought three-specialty pencils and notebooks. Alik bought 4 pencils and 2 notebooks, Borya - 6 pencils and a notebook, Vasya - 3 pencils and a notebook. It is obvious that the amounts paid by Alik, Borya and Vasya form a geometric progression. How much does a notepad cost?

2. Solve inequality

$$\log_4 (5 - 3^x) \cdot \log_2 \left( \frac{5 - 3^x}{8} \right) \geq -1.$$

3. Find all real $x$ satisfying

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} = (1 - x^2)^{-\frac{\sin^2 x}{\sin x + 1}}.$$

4. The plane of the base of a regular quadrangular straight line passes through the center of the ball, and in the larger section it turns out that the circle of the ball is inscribed in the base of the straight line. Another ball is placed inside the straight line, touching the side faces of the straight line and the first ball. It is known that the radius of the second ball is equal to 2, and the point of contact of the balls is distant from the base of the straight line at a distance equal to one three times the height of the straight line. Find the volume of the straightness and the magnitude of the arc angle at the lateral edge of the straightness.

5. A square CEKM is inserted into a right triangle ACB so that its vertex K lies on the hypotenuse AB, and the vertices E and M lie on the legs. The side of this square is related to the radius of the circle inserted into triangle ACB as $(2 + \sqrt{2}) : 2$. Find the angles of triangle ACB.

Faculty of Biology

1. Three masons (of different qualifications) laid a brick wall, with the first mason working for 6 hours, the second for 4 hours and the third for 7 hours. If the first mason worked for 4 hours, the second for 2 hours and the third for 5 hours, only $2/3$ of the total work would have been completed. How many hours would it take a mason to complete the class if they all worked together for the same amount of time?

2. Solve the equation

$$2 \cos 2x - 1 = (2 \cos 2x + 1) \mathrm{tg} x.$$

3. Solve the equation

$$\log_{\sin 2x} (tg x + \ctg x) = 1 - \log_{\sin 2x}^2 2.$$

4. Through the midpoint $M$ of side $BC$ of parallelogram $ABCD$, the area of ​​which is 1, and the vertex $A$, a straight line is drawn intersecting the diagonal $BD$ at point $Q$. Find the area of ​​the quadrilateral $QMCD$.

5. Find all values ​​of the real parameter $a$ for which the inequality

$$4^x - a \cdot 2^x - a + 3 \leq 0$$

has at least one solution.

Faculty of Geology

1. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} x + \sin (x + y) = \frac{3}{2}, \\ 3x - \sin (x + y) = \frac{5}{2}. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve inequality

$$4^{-x} + \frac{1}{2} - 7 \cdot 2^{-x} - 4 < 0.$$

3. Two quantities left point $A$ simultaneously and in the same direction. The first quantity moves at a rate of $7 \text{ km/ч}$, the second at a rate of $10 \text{ km/ч}$. After 30 minutes, a third magnitude left point $A$ in the same direction, which after some time caught up with the top of the magnitude, and another hour and a half after that, it caught up with the second magnitude. Determine the speed of the third magnitude.

4. Solve inequality

$$\log_2 (2x - x^2) \left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right)^4 > 0.$$

5. On the data plane of a right angle, touches one side of the angle, intersects the other side at points $A$ and $B$, and intersects the bissertis of the right angle at points $C$ and $D$. It is known that $AB = \sqrt{6} \text{ cm}$, $CD = \sqrt{7} \text{ cm}$. Find the radius of the circle.

Postal Faculty

1. The total weight of the tourist group’s equipment is 202 kg. If we distribute it so that each boy has to carry 16 kg, and each girl has to carry 9 kg, then 4 kg will remain undistributed. Therefore everything

2. What's more:

$$\arccos \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) \cdot \log_{81} \left( \frac{1}{27} \right)$$

or

$$\sin \frac{43\pi}{6} \cdot \mathrm{tg}^3 \left( -\frac{8\pi}{3} \right) \cdot \mathrm{ctg} \frac{4\pi}{3}$$?

$$\cos x (\sqrt{3} + \cos x) - 1 = \frac{\cos 2x}{1 - \sin 2x} - \sin x (\sqrt{3} + \sin x).$$

$$\log_2 (x^2 + 7) = 5 + \log_2 x - \frac{6}{\log_2 \left( x + \frac{7}{x} \right)}.$$

Faculty of Geography

1. Two excavators were working to lay two parallel pipelines. The first of them started work 30 minutes earlier than the second. When the second excavator dug 27 m, it turned out that it lags behind the first by 1 m. How many meters per hour does each of the excavators dig if it is known that the second digs 4 m more per hour than the first?

2. Simplify an expression

$$5^{\log_{1/5} (1/2)} + \log \sqrt{2} \frac{4}{\sqrt{7} + \sqrt{3}} + \log_{1/2} \frac{1}{10 + 2\sqrt{21}}.$$

3. Side $AB$ of square $ABCD$ is a chord of some circle, and all other sides of the square lie outside this circle. The length of the tangent segment drawn from the vertex $C$ to the same circle is equal to 2. What is the diameter of the circle if the side of the square is 1?

4. Find solutions to the equation

$$3^{x^2 + 4x} = \frac{1}{25},$$

satisfying the inequality $x > -3$.

5. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{3} \sin 2x - 2 \cos^2 x = 2 \sqrt{2} + 2 \cos 2x.$$

Physics

Ticket 1

1. Point charges $q_1 = -1,7 \cdot 10^{-8} \text{ к}$ and $q_2 = 2 \cdot 10^{-8} \text{ к}$ are located from the point charge $q_0 = 3 \cdot 10^{-8} \text{ к}$ at distances $l_1 = 2 \text{ cm}$ and $l_2 = 5 \text{ cm}$, respectively. How much work $A$ must be done to swap the places of charges $q_1$ and $q_2$?

2. Variable movement. Average and instantaneous speed. Acceleration. Speed ​​graph of uniformly alternating motion with initial speed. Free fall of bodies.

3. Laws of light reflection. Constructing an image in a plane mirror. Construction of an image in spherical mirrors.

Ticket 2

1. An aircraft of mass $m = 2,5 \text{ m}$ with the motor turned off descends, gliding, at a constant speed $v = 144 \text{ km/ч}$ from a height $h_1 = 2 \text{ km}$ to a height $h_2 = 1 \text{ km}$, having covered a distance $l = 10 \text{ km}$. What power $W$ must the aircraft motor develop so that it can rise to a height $h_1$, covering the distance $l$ at the same speed?

2. Magnetic interaction of currents. Magnetic field. The force acting on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field. Magnetic field induction. Ammeter. Voltmeter.

3. Converging and diverging lenses. Lens formula. Constructing an image.

Ticket 3

1. A thin-walled cylindrical vessel with a height of $h = 1 \text{ m}$ and a bottom area of ​​$S = 0,1 \text{ m}^2$ is immersed in a pool of water. The vessel is filled with liquid oil with density $\rho = 800 \text{ к/м}^3$. From the bottom of the vessel to the surface of the water, the distance is $H = 2 \text{ m}$. Find the force $F$ acting from inside the vessel on its bottom. Atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 10^5 \text{ к/м}^2$.

2. Two types of electricity. Interaction of electric charges. Coulomb's law. The influence of the environment on the force of interaction of charges. Permittivity.

3. Projection apparatus. Camera. Magnifier. The beam path in these devices.

Ticket 4

1. The point lies on the main optical axis of a diverging lens with focal length $f = 25 \text{ cm}$. The distance from the lens to the image of this point is $b = 15 \text{ cm}$. By what distance $l$ will the image of the point move if the line itself is moved by a distance $L = 2 \text{ cm}$ in the direction perpendicular to the main optical axis?

2. The concept of potential. Field potential for precise charging (no output). The work of moving a charge in an electric field. Potential difference. Relationship between potential and direction for a uniform field.

3. Phenomena confirming the complex structure of atom. Methods for observing particles. Structure of atom. Emission and absorption of energy by an atom.
