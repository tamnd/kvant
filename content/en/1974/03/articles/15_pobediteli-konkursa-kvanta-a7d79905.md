⟦folio 32⟧

WINNERS
"QUANTA" COMPETITION

In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the Weighted Union Olympiad, schoolchildren who regularly submit especially original and complete solutions to the problems of “Problem «Kvant»” receive the right to participate in the regional rounds of the Weighted Union Olympiad along with the winners of regional and city olympiads.

Over the past year, 1973, the editors received more than six thousand letters with solutions to problems. The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» together with the Organizing Committee of the Weighted Union Olympiad selected the authors of the correct and most interesting solutions.

Below is a list of schoolchildren - winners of the competition «Kvant» - who received the right to participate in regional, regional and resource (in the Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic and allied resource without regional division) Olympiads in 1974.
