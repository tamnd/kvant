Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than May 1, 1974 to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, Nauka Publishing House, magazine «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: Problem «Kvant», M251, M252 “or”... F263”. Send problems from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The most difficult ones are marked with asterisks.
