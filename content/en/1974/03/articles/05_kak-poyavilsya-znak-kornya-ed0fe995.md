In this case, with a strong deviation of $n_3$ from $n_n$, an electron field is formed that will push out particles of one sign (present in excess) and delay the departure of particles of another sign. Such a mechanism, which automatically maintains the equality of $n_3$ and $n_n$, passes to act in the case when $r \ll r_D$. In volumes with linear dimensions significantly smaller than $r_D$, the electron fields arising when $n_3$ deviates from $n_n$ will be too small to have a noticeable effect on the motion of individual particles.

Now we can put a more specific meaning into the concept of “plasma”. Until we are dealing with a relatively small number of charged particles that are not able to create a field strong enough for it to significantly affect the surface of each particle, it makes no sense to talk about the presence of some new state of matter. A new form of matter - plasma - corresponds to such a state when the number of electrons of the present ions is large that even small mixed electron components with respect to the ionic component are impossible due to the strong electron fields that arise when the equality between $n_3$ and $n_n$ is violated. Thus, the ionized gas has the right to become plasma only if condition (3) is satisfied with a sufficient margin.

It should be emphasized that quasi-neutrality of plasma is observed only within sufficiently large volumes. If we isolate a cube inside the plasma on the side $x$, which is significantly smaller than $r_D$, then within this cube the number of ions can differ significantly from the number of electrons. the smaller the ratio $x/r_D$, the greater the difference between the magnitude of the ratio $n_3/n_n$ and unity.

How did the root sign appear?
Extracting the root is indicated by the sign $\sqrt{}$. Not everyone knows that this is a vision of the Latin letter $r$, the first in the Latin word “gadix”, known as “root”.
In the 16th century, the sign of the root was not a lowercase, but an uppercase letter $R$, and the first letter of the Latin words “cadratus” - “square” (q) or “subisc” - “cubic” (c) was placed next to it to indicate which root needed to be extracted. For example, they wrote $R.q.4352$ together with the new one $\sqrt{4352}$.
