PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

PHYSICS
During January - March 1974, at the physics department of television physics and mathematics courses for entering universities *), lectures were given and practical classes were conducted on the topic “Fundamentals of Electrodynamics.” To familiarize readers of the magazine with the level of difficulty of homework (which are systematically published in the Moscow Speaks and Shows diary), we present below several tasks on the laws of constructed current, on the magnetic interaction of currents, on the phenomenon of electromagnetic induction and alternating current.

1. Capacitor $C = 10 \text{ мкф}$ is included in the constructed current circuit (see Fig.). Determine the charge of the capacitor if $R = 4,0 \text{ ом}, E_1 = 5,0 \text{ ом}, E_2 = 2,0 \text{ ом}$ and $r_1 = r_2 = 1,0 \text{ ом}$.

2. An element with internal resistance $r = 2 \text{ ом}$ is shorted to resistance $R_1 = 8 \text{ ом}$. What other resistance $R_2$ should be connected to this element so that the power released in the external circuit is the same as in the first case?

3. The electric stove has two heating bodies. When one heating is turned on, $V_1 = 0,5 \text{ л}$ water boils after $t_1 = 10 \text{ мин}$; When the second heating is turned on, $V_2 = 1,0 \text{ л}$ water boils after $t_2 = 15 \text{ мин}$. How many minutes will it take for $V_3 = 2,0 \text{ л}$ water to boil on a litre, if it is heated in succession in the same set?

4. Hydrogen released during the electrolysis of a solution of table salt in water is collected in a vessel with a volume of $V = 0,5 \text{ л}$. After some time, the pressure of hydrogen into the vessel becomes $p = 1,3 \text{ amm}$ at a temperature of $t = 27^\circ \text{C}$. Find the work done by the current source during electrolysis if the potential difference between the electrodes is $U = 25 \text{ в}$.

5. According to the topic, along a straight parallel conductor of negligibly small cross-section, located in the air at equal distances $a = 20 \text{ cm}$ from each other, currents $I_1 = 4,0 \text{ а}, I_2 = 6,0 \text{ а}$ and $I_3 = 10 \text{ а}$ flow in the same direction. Find the magnetic induction at points equidistant from all three conductors.

6. In one magnetic field with induction $B = 0,5 \text{ мл}$, a metal rod of length $l = 1 \text{ м}$ is uniformly treated. Since the treatment is perpendicular to the magnetic field induction vector. Determine the frequency of treatment of the rod $v$, if the emf. induction arising at the ends of the rod, $E_{\text{инд}} = 2 \text{ в}$.

7. Along two vertical sides, located in a magnetic field with induction $B = 2 \text{ мл}$, a conductor with resistance $R = 4 \text{ ом}$ and mass $m = 10 \text{ с}$ slides without friction at a constant speed, without losing electrical contact. Determine the speed of movement of the conductor if the distance between the sides of the frame $l = 1 \text{ м}$ and the magnetic induction vector is perpendicular to the plane of the frame.

8. At the end of the power transmission lines with a total resistance of $R = 50 \text{ ом}$, a field transformer with a transformation coefficient of $k = 5$ is installed. The consumer receives power $P = 400 \text{ вт}$ from the transformer at current $I = 10 \text{ а}$. Determine the active resistance of the secondary winding of the transformer if the voltage at the beginning of the lines is $U = 500 \text{ в}$. Active co-

**Mathematics**

In February, a very special telecourse was held for students of the mathematics department. Those invited to their own were offered two options, including tasks on previously covered topics. The interview was conducted under conditions similar to those found during entrance exams. We publish the texts of obihi varnat.

**Option 1**

1 (Belarusian University, 1973). Prove that the expression

$$\frac{1 - 2\sin^2\left(\alpha - \frac{3\pi}{2}\right) + \sqrt{3}\cos\left(2\alpha + \frac{3\pi}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6} - 2\alpha\right)},$$

where $\alpha \neq \frac{\pi}{12} + \frac{\pi n}{2}, n = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots$ does not depend on $\alpha$.

2. In triangle $ABC$ there are two known angles: $\alpha = \alpha$, $\beta = \beta$. From the vertex $B$ the height $BH$ and the median $BM$ are drawn. Find the area of ​​triangle $BMH$ if $BH = h$.

3 (UDN, 1973). Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 = 2(x + y), \\ x^3 + y^3 = 4(x + y). \end{cases}$$

4 (MIPT, 1971). Solve equation $\cos^2 x + \cos^2 2x = \cos^2 3x$.

**Option 2**

1 (MEPhI, 1971). Find two positive integers knowing that their sum is 85 and their least common multiple is 102.

2. Two circles $h$ and $l$ of radii $R$ and $r$ ($R > r$) touch at point $A$. Determine the sides of an equilateral triangle, one of the vertices of which is at the point $A$, the other vertex lies on the circle $h$ and the third - on the circle $l$.

3 (MSU, 1973). Find all pairs of values ​​$x, y$ that are solutions to the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} \sin x + \frac{1}{\cos y} = 2\sqrt[3]{14}, \\ \frac{\sin x}{\cos y} = \sqrt[3]{196} - 2. \end{cases}$$

4 (Leningrad Polytechnic Institute, 1972). Solve equation $ctg x - 8\cos^2 x + tg 2x = 0$.

In the matret, lectures are given on phones on the topics: “Exponential and logarithmic functions”, “Logarithms and their properties”, “Exponential equations and inequalities”, “Logarithmic inequalities”. In this issue we offer a number of homework problems for telecourse students.

**Exercise**

1. Simplify an expression

$$\left[ \left(\log^4 b + \log^4 a + 2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} - 2 \right]^{1/2},$$

if $1 < a < b$.

2. Arrange in ascending order of numbers

$$a = \log_{63} 27, b = \log_{21} 9, c = \log_{189} 81.$$

3. Solve equation $(x - 1)2^x + x^2 - 2x + 1 = 0.$

4. Solve equation $2(2^x + 2^{-2x}) + 7(2^x - 2^{-x}) - 19 = 0.$

5. Solve equation $x + \lg(2^x - 3) = \lg 4 + x \lg 5.$

6. Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} x^{\log_3 y} + 2 \cdot y^{\log_3 x} = 27, \\ \log_3 x - \log_3 y = 1. \end{cases}$$

7. Solve inequality

$$\sqrt{1 + \log_4 x} - \sqrt{3\log_4 x} > 2\log_4 x - 1.$$

8. Solve inequality

$$\left(\sqrt{7 - x} - \sqrt{x + 9}\right)\log_3 \frac{2x^2 + x - 3}{3} \geq 0.$$
