⟦folio 72⟧

Quests

1. On opposite banks of the river, two palm trees grow opposite each other. The height of one of them is 10 meters, the other is 15 meters, the distance between the bases of the palm trees is 25 meters. A bird sits on the top of each palm tree. Suddenly the birds noticed a fish swimming to the surface of the river between the palm trees. The birds rushed to the fish and reached it at the same time.

How far from the base of the taller palm did the fish swim?

2. It's raining outside. When will a bucket in the back of a truck fill up quickly: when the truck is stationary or when it is moving?

3. Grass grows in the meadow. They let 9 cows into the meadow, they cleared the meadow in 4 days. If 8 cows were allowed into a meadow, they would eat all the grass in 6 days.

How many cows can feed in a meadow while the grass is growing?

4. A candle is balanced on a pan balance. Will the balance be disrupted when the candle is lit? If it breaks, then in which direction?

5. In order to determine the unknown resistance, having at our disposal an ammeter and a voltmeter, we once assembled the circuit shown at figure 1, another time at figure 2. We measured the voltage and current. In what case will the resulting resistance value be close to the true value?
