Project plane

Let us mentally supplement the general plane with points of another “straight line,” which we will call “infinitely distant” (we will also call its points “infinitely distant”). Let's connect the elements of the plane with the new introduced elements.

First, we will assume that any straight line of the plane intersects with a line at infinity and, moreover, at a single point (this point, naturally, will be at infinity, since it belongs to the line at infinity).

Secondly, we will assume that any line parallel to the given one intersects the line at infinity at the same point as the given one. then this point is the intersection point of all lines parallel to this one (in the sense of parallelism on a common plane).

Now, with central projection, the straight line $l_1$ can be considered the image of the “infinitely distant straight line” of the plane $Q$, and the image of the straight line $l_2$ - the “infinitely distant straight line” of the $P$ plane, and only for the point of intersection of the “infinitely distant straight line” of the plane $Q$ with the straight line $l_2$ there is no place on the straight line $l_1$, that is, this point will remain “at infinity” (and similarly for the “infinitely distant line” plane $P$ and line $l_1$).

What are the properties of the resulting projective plane? And this depends on from what point of view you look at them. It is most natural to consider those properties of the projective plane that do not change during central projection. Indeed, in this case, the difference between the lines on the original plane and the line at infinity is eliminated, since the properties of the straight line at infinity $Q$ will in this case completely correspond to the properties of the line $l_1$ of the plane $P$.

We list the main of these properties:

1) For any two points, there is one and only one line to which they simultaneously belong.
2) Any two lines intersect at one and only one point.

In this case, the angles between the straight lines are also given; their values ​​are not preserved during the central design, and the distances between the points are not preserved. Each of these circumstances impoverishes the geometry of the projective plane.
