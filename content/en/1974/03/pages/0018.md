Now let’s add to doxa Ш four infinitely distant fields: field $O_x$, in which all horizontals intersect, field $O_y$, in which all verticals intersect, and two fields $M_1$ and $M_2$, in which two sets of diagonals intersect respectively (Fig. 1).

The board Ш, additionally infinitely removed by the fields $O_x, O_y$, $M_1$ and $M_2$, is called a projective chess board P. The analogy between the above consideration of the projective plane and the resulting dock P is very visible. Instead of points of the projective plane (finite and infinitely distant), here we are dealing with fields of doxi P (respectively finite or infinitely distant). Since “chess” directions are only horizontals, verticals and diagonals, we limit ourselves to only four directions in the plane, and therefore to four infinitely distant fields. We can assume that these fields lie on the infinitely distant direct doxy Sh.
