Test 5. An interesting solution to this problem is to prepare a model from a paper strip from which it would be possible to obtain an interlacing (in part, the general purpose) of n wheels using only one through cut. (Topic credits M. Brooke and J. Madachy.)

The secret of the solution lies in the preliminary bending of the strip along its length even before cutting and in the method of gluing the cut ends of the bent strip.

Start with a strip folded once along its length (Fig. 4). Twist it a full turn (360°) and glue the ends together, placing “domikom” one end over the other. Now cut the double layer of glued tape along its middle line - you get three rings linked in pairs.
