Chess-projective problems

Projective chess offers rich and unexpected possibilities compared to the usual game. Expanding the area of ​​"guarding" actually leads to a change in the strength of the figures. At the same time, new original ideas arise. It is no coincidence that chess composers often resort to projective chess in their research. They will be especially popular in Yugoslavia *).

In the starting position of a chess-projective problem, the pieces will typically be located inside a regular 8x8 board. Moreover, its drawing without edges is like a fragment of the board Ш. This means that the solution should be sought on the chess-projective board P.

Now we will analyze two chess-projective problems. We will write all the options in them in the usual chess notation.
