⟦folio 15⟧

On the pages «Kvant» we have already discussed how to play chess on various boards obtained using geometric transformations from an ordinary chessboard. It is in this way that, for example, cylindrical and toroidal chess mats arise *). Now we will get acquainted with the game of chess on another unusual board. This game is based on elements of design geometry, which is why chess is called design chess.

Project plane
It is discussed in this issue of the magazine in an article by A.P. Savin (see p. 9). Let us briefly repeat: a number of additional points are added to the general plane. Each of these points corresponds to a family of parallel straight lines of the plane and is called the infinitely distant point of the design plane. Parallel lines intersect at a point infinitely distant. Different sets of parallel lines correspond to different infinitely distant points—each direction has its own point. The set of all infinitely distant points is connected “directly”; it is called the infinitely distant straight line of the design plane. The points and lines of the original plane are called end points.

So, on the design plane, all straight lines must intersect, and at a single point: the final point, if they are not parallel on the general plane, and at infinity, if they are parallel. If one of the lines is infinitely distant, then it intersects the second line at a point at infinity.
