⟦folio 52⟧

The law of conservation of energy in mechanics can be obtained using Newton's laws and knowing expressions for forces depending on the distances between bodies or their parts (gravitational forces and elastic forces).

The basic concepts used are work, kinemic energy, potential energy and total energy. These concepts, with the exception of potential energy, are not particularly complex. Nevertheless, the greatest number of misconceptions arise in the study of mechanics precisely in connection with the law of conservation of energy. The reason, apparently, is that, on the one hand, the study of mechanics is not always clearly visible in the sense of basic concepts, and on the other hand (and this is the main thing), often, to facilitate understanding of the essence of the matter, simple partial cases are considered, but then, on their basis, more general inclinations are thoughtlessly made, which sometimes turn out to be untenable. The purpose of this article is to dwell in detail on the misunderstandings associated with the concepts of work and energy in mechanics.
