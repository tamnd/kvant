A brick placed along it will travel a path $s - l$. After this they will move at the same speed $v$.

The sliding friction force $F_1 = kmg$ will do positive work on the brick, which will increase the kinetic energy of the brick:

$$A_1 = kmg(s - l) = \Delta K_1 = \frac{mv^2}{2}.$$

The work done by the friction force $F_2 = -kmg$ acting on the cart will be negative, which will cause a decrease in the kinetic energy of the cart:

$$A_2 = -kmgs = \Delta K_2 = \frac{Mv^2}{2} - \frac{Mv_0^2}{2}.$$

Adding these equations term by term, we get that

$$\frac{(M + m)v^2}{2} - \frac{Mv_0^2}{2} = -kmgl,$$

that is, the kinetic energy of the system is equal to the work done by the friction force on a path equal to the relative movement of the brick and the cart.

So, the work of the friction force leads to the loss of kinetic energy of the system. But at the same time, under the influence of the friction force, the potential energy of the system does not increase, as happens under the influence of conservative forces. This is a consequence of the fact that the friction force depends on the distances between the bodies, and on their relative velocities. As a result, the work of these forces depends on the shape of the body's trajectory, and not just on its initial and final position in space. The friction forces acting inside the system change the kinetic energy of the system in the same way as external forces. Therefore, in the presence of friction forces in a closed system, its mechanical energy kills:

$$\Delta E = A_{\text{tp}} < 0.$$

In connection with the above, the following question may arise. It is known that the friction force can lift a brick on a vehicle moving at a constant speed. Doesn't this mean that the work of this force increases the potential energy of the brick-Earth system? Of course not. In this case, the positive work of the friction force is equal to the negative work of the gravity component along the inclined plane. Because of this, the kinetic energy of the brick does not change. The potential energy of the brick increases, since the force of interaction between the Earth and the brick, that is, the force of gravity, does negative work when lifting the brick.

In this reporting system, the cube's initial speed is $v = \sqrt{2gh}$ and its final speed is zero.
