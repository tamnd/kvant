Successful series

REVIEWS, BIBLIOGRAPHY

Many readers «Kvant» are aware of a series of popular science books and brochures on mathematics produced by Moscow publishing houses, aimed at secondary school students. In the first place among the average of these series is the brochure “Popular Lectures on Mathematics” published since 1950 - to date, 52 books from this extensive collection have already been published! In the younger series “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” 8 books on mathematics were published *), and the “Library of the Mathematical Circle” will consist of 12 rather large books. All these books were published by the main editorial office of physical and mathematical literature of the Nauka publishing house; The same publishing house also took the initiative to publish the “Mathematical Library” (7 books were published), perhaps somewhat more complex than “Popular Lectures on Mathematics” or “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School”, but in basic freshness also accessible to a real schoolchild.
