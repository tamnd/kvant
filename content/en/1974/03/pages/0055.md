Kinetic energy

The simplest concept is kinetic energy. Using the definition of work and Newton’s second law, it is easy to show that in all cases the work of any dream $F$ is equal to the change in the kinetic energy of the body $K = \frac{mv^2}{2}$:

$$A = (F \Delta s) = \Delta K.$$ (2)

The nature of the dream is completely unimportant here. These may be dreams of gravity, dreams of elasticity, or the force of tremium.

The kinetic energy of an individual body is determined by its mass and speed and does not depend on whether this body interacts with other bodies or not. The amount of kinetic energy of the body generator, like the work of sleep, depends on the reporting system.

The kinetic energy of a body system is equal to the sum of the kinetic energies of the body included in this system.

Potential energy - the energy of interaction of a body

It is not always emphasized that potential energy in mechanics is the energy of interaction of at least two bodies. The concept of potential energy refers to a system of bodies, and not to one body. If a system has several bodies, then the total potential energy of the system is equal to the sum of the potential energies of all pairs of interacting bodies (any body interacts with each of the others).

Obviously, when deriving a formula connecting the change in potential energy with the work of forces, one of the bodies of the system is taken as stationary. So, when the distribution of load on the Earth under the influence of gravity is considered, then the mixing of the Earth can be claimed. Therefore, the work of the interaction forces between the Earth and the load is reduced to the work of only one force acting on the load. Or another example. A compressed or extended arm acting on a body is usually fixed at one end, and this end of the arm does not move (in fact, it is attached to the globe). In this case, the work is performed only by the elastic force of the deformed arm applied to the body.

Because of this, the potential energy of a system of two bodies is used to be considered as the energy of one body. This can lead to confusion.
