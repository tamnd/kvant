Novosibirsk State University

Novosibirsk State University has the following faculties:
mathematical with departments of mathematics, applied mathematics and mechanics;
physical; natural science with departments of chemistry and biology; geological geophysical;
humanitarian with departments of Russian language and history; economic with a special focus on economic science.
The university is located in Akademgorodok, 20 kilometers from Novosibirsk. Students are trained at the scientific institutes of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences: hydrodynamics, mathematics, computer center, nuclear physics, theoretical and applied mechanics, thermal physics, semiconductor physics, automation and electrometry, geology and geophysics, inorganic chemistry, organizational chemistry, chemical kinetics and combustion, catalysis, source, philology and philosophy, cytology and genetics.
In this issue we publish samples of written exams in mathematics and physics at NSU in 1973.

Mathematics
Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Faculty of Physics, Faculty of Economics

Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_3 (1-y^2)^2 =$$
$$= 5 + \sqrt{14 \log_{\sqrt{3}}(1-y) + 7 \log_{\sqrt{3}}(\frac{1+y)^2}{3}} - 5.$$

2. In triangle ABC, the bissertis of angle BAC is $a$. A circle constructed on this bissertis as a diameter divides sides AB and AC in the ratios 2:1 and 1:1, counting from point A. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x + \sqrt{3} \cos x = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} + \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - x\right)}.$$

4. Given a quadrangular paramida SABCD (SA = SB = SC = SD = 5), the base of which is the rectangle ABCD from the side AB = CD = 4, AD = BC = 3. A plane is drawn through the digonal BD at an angle of 30° to the plane of the base, intersecting the edge SC at the point $S'$. Find the volume of the paramid S'BCD.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_{\sqrt{2}}(3x^2 - 1) + 2 =$$
$$= \sqrt{1 + \log_{\sqrt{x}}x^3 + \frac{6}{\log_{3x^2-1}2\sqrt{2}}}.$$

2. One of the lateral sides of the trapezoid is perpendicular to the base and equal to 2a. On this side, as a diameter, a circle is built, which divides the other side into three segments. The ratio of the lengths of the segments is 1:2:2 (counting from the upper base). Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\cos^2 x - \cos^2 3x} = \sin 2x.$$

4. In a regular tetrahedron SABC, through vertex A, parallel to edge BC, a plane is drawn perpendicular to the side face of SBC (S is the vertex of the tetrahedron, ABC is the base). Find the radius of the ball tangent to the edges SA, SB, SC and the drawn plane. The edges of a tetrahedron are equal to $a$.
