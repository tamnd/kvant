The equation becomes $u = 5 + \sqrt{14u - 19}$. Transferring 5 to the left side, squaring and solving the resulting quadratic equation, we find $u = 22$, $2\log_3(1 - y^2) = 22$, from which $1 - y^2 = 3^{11}$, $y^2 = 1 - 3^{11}$. Resolved no.
