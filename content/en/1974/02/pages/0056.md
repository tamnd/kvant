It was recommended to solve the first three problems first, and it was also emphasized that in order to receive a high grade, it is not necessary to solve all the problems.

In the versions of the written exam in physics given below, after the conditions of each problem, its score in points and the percentage of those who solved this problem are indicated in parentheses.

Option 1

1. On the table there are: two light sources - an incandescent light bulb and a laser, a deep parallel beam of rays; photocell; a galvanometer for recording the photo-eye and a plane-parallel glass plate. The light source is turned on and a photo is recorded. Then a plate is placed in the path of the light, and the current is again recorded. Once the source is a light bulb, the second time a laser. It turned out that after the insertion of the plate, the photoperiod increased in the first case, and decreased in the second. Explain why. (5 6., 48%)

2. Find the charge on the capacitor $C$, if the circuit shown in figure 1 carries a constant current. For example, at terminals $U$, resistances in the circuit $R_1$ and $R_2$. (3 6., 82%)

3. An infinite cone with an opening angle $90^\circ$ moves towards the center of a resting ball with a speed $v$ (Fig. 2). The direction of the velocity coincides with the axis of the cone. At some points we break it into a very large number of sedges, which fly evenly in all directions at the same speed $v$. What part of the sedges ends up on the cone? Influence of weight*) claims. (5 6., 52%)

4. A cylindrical plug of radius $r$ and height $h$ (Fig. 3) falls into a cylinder of radius $R$, particularly filled with water. The initial height of the bottom end of the plug above the water level is $H$; the initial speed is zero. How much energy will be released as heat after the movement of the plug and water stops? Cork density $ρ$, water density $ρ_0$, free pressure acceleration $g$. (66., 36%)
