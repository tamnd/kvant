Option 3
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_x(x^2+1) = \sqrt{\log_x[x^2(1+x^2)]+4}.$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, the lower base $AD$ is 2 times larger than the upper one, equal to $a$, the angle $A$ at the base is $45^\circ$, and the circles built on the lateral sides as diameters touch each other. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\frac{1+\mathrm{tg} x}{1-\mathrm{tg} x}} = \sin x + \cos x.$$

4. A ball is inscribed in a cube with edge $a$. A plane is drawn through the midpoints of two adjacent edges of the cube and touches the ball. Find the cross-sectional area of ​​the cube by this plane.
