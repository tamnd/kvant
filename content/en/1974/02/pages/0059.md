⟦folio 57⟧

Tasks

1. A group of schoolchildren were buying various albums. Each student bought albums of the same type, and as many as he bought one album of this type. All the guys paid with coins of 10 kopecks, each received change, and this change did not match for any two schoolchildren.

What kind of change could the schoolchildren receive and what was the largest number of schoolchildren in this group?

2. A rowboat is spitting along the river and there is a piece of wood next to it.

What is easier for a rower: to overtake a chip by a few meters or to lag behind it by the same amount?

3. In the following example, numbers are replaced with letters:

$$\begin{array}{c}
\text{dеталь} \\
+ \\
\text{dеталь} \\
\hline
\text{изделие}
\end{array}$$

Restore the recording.

4. An adult and a child are standing on different banks of a stream. Each person holds a board in their hands, slightly shorter than the distance between the banks. How can they switch places?

5. Find a number whose sum of digits is equal to the difference between 328 and the number itself.

6. The carriage is illuminated by six lamps connected in series. On each of them it is written: 110 bm, 25 bm. One of the bulbs overheated, and it was replaced with another, which says: 110 bm, 40 bm. Will this bulb burn brighter than the others?
