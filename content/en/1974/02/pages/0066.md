5. Turning the capacitor $180^\circ$ around the horizontal axis means placing positive and negative charged plates in places.

Since the work of charge transfer in the power plant field does not depend on the shape of the path, then for the determination we will assume that the lower plate is fixed, and the upper plate is “carried” through the lower one and again placed at a distance $d$ from the stationary one.

Let us separately calculate the work in the field created by the stationary plate and in the external field E. If at the first stage the field of the lower plate itself did the work of merging the plates, then at the second stage it was necessary to perform the same work of moving the plates apart. Consequently, the total work in the first field is zero, and only the work against the external field remains, which is equal to
$$A = FS = 2qEd.$$
It was possible to calculate work as the change in energy of the capacitor:
$$A = W_2 - W_1 = \frac{CU_2^2}{2} - \frac{CU_1^2}{2} = \frac{Cd^2}{2} [(E + E_K)^2 - (E - E_K)^2] = 2Cd^2EE_K = 2qEd.$$
