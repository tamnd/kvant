Do-it-yourself topological experiments
B. A. Kordemsky

In this article, we invite you to get acquainted with topology, a science that studies those properties of figures that remain unchanged when stretched without breaking or rolled without gluing. To do this, you have to perform some experiments: color the map, free yourself from the rope “captivity,” cut the paper strips into non-distributing rings.

In fact, topology itself began with the discovery of the secrets of some interesting geometric puzzles that occupied Leonhard Euler and other mathematicians in their time.
