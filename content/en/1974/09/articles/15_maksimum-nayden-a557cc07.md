⟦folio 52⟧

Maximum found

In «Kvant» 1973, No. 10, the note “Maxium?” was published. The editors have received many letters with responses to the problem presented in this note. All the answers provided by readers turned out to be correct. Indeed, the record work that was mentioned in the context of the problem and which was found in Japan is 7448. It is very pleasant that many readers were interested in this problem, but, unfortunately, not everyone got their solutions, and therefore, in most cases, it is impossible to find out how the correct answer was obtained. The solutions of those readers who dreamed them up, as a rule, consisted of sorting out possible options.

Of course, finding a solution using a table or computers does not present any logical difficulties, although it is rather cumbersome and uninteresting. Therefore, the most popular solutions are those that used logical analysis of reduction in the situation problem and prove the uniqueness of the resulting answer. There are few such solutions. First of all, reports on the decisions of Andrei Mishin (Leningrad), Sergei Ternovoy (Volgograd) and Sergei Gladkovsky (Georgievsk, Stavropol Territory). These readers managed to significantly reduce the number of combinations considered and quickly come to the right decision. Comrade's decision Chelyabova (Makhachka) is complete and correct, but it is very cumbersome.

It is impossible not to mention the youngest of the readers who figured out the correct answer to this problem - sixth-grader Lenio Chekhov from Moscow.
