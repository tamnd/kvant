You need to remove the stake from the rope without untying the knots at the ends of the rope. The plates and stakes easily pass through the “eye”. The balls do not pass through the ear, but they freely pass through the hole in the ring. Of course, the plate does not fit through the hole in the ring. The thickness of the ring is 4 mm, the inner diameter of the ring is 15 mm, other dimensions are indicated at figure.

To our readers

A subscription for 1975 to the beginning of the popular physico-mathematical journal «Kvant» is announced.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics and love solving problems.
Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet taken up physics or mathematics, but would like to take up these sciences.
Now comes a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work. In 1975, articles introducing the material of new programs will be systematically published on the pages of the magazine.
Many materials will be published for 10th grade students preparing to enter college.
In 1975, «Kvant» will continue to print articles for younger schoolchildren. The scope of these publications will be significantly expanded.
«Kvant» is also useful as a teacher. Its materials can be used at meetings of mathematical and physical circles and in extracurricular activities.
Read in «Kvant» in 1975:
Satyns devoted to experiments that can be carried out on models of both physical and mathematical occurrence.
Thus, the article by G.V. Skrotsky will talk about the camera obscura. And in the article by E. G. Belaga “Knots on the Mathematics Table,” readers will read about topological experiments.
An article by N. Ya. Vilenkin and V. P. Linevsky will be devoted to the life and work of S. V. Kovalevskaya.
Those readers who are especially interested in optics will find on the pages of the magazine an article on holography and an article by the famous foreign physicist W. Weiskopf “How light interacts with matter.”
Geometry lovers will read with interest I. F. Shargiin’s article about the world of geometry with more than three changes. Of particular interest to them are some problems of combinatorial geometry, which can be solved using elementary methods. In a series of articles by B.V. Gnedenko, some issues of the theory of probability and mass processes will be covered.
A large number of articles will be related to the new program at the school. Thus, in the articles by A.G. Mordkovich, schoolchildren will become familiar with the concept of a derivative and its application in solving problems.
For the fifth year now, the section of the magazine “Problem Book «Kvant»” has been teaching schoolchildren to solve problems of increased difficulty in mathematics and physics. Schoolchildren from cities and villages take part in the work of this section. Readers who regularly link correct solutions are eligible to participate in the Olympiads field. The editors advise schoolchildren who are connecting solutions to problems.
For applicants to universities in 1975, articles on the following topics will be published. Mathematics: current action of transformation of irrational equations; checking the solution; equations with parameters; proof of inequalities; use of monotonicity of functions when solving equations and inequalities; solving trionometric equations; solving problems on bodies of rotation; rectangular projection when solving stereometric problems, “hidden” data in the conditions of the problem.
Physics: laws of ideal gases; electrical current, transmission lines; transformers; construction of images (plate, lens, mirror); optical instruments; eye; interface.
We will continue to introduce our readers to new books that are of interest to them, stories about the work of physics and mathematics schools, about Olympiads, about various types of work with schoolchildren, etc.
