To the tasks
(see «Kvant», 1974, No. 8, p. 35)

1. If the number $k$ is on the right, then every $4k - 1$ number, that is, numbers of the form $k + (4k - 1)n$, is crossed out on the right, and numbers of the form $4(k - 1)n - k$ are crossed out next. On the right, the numbers $m$ are crossed out so that $4m - 1$ is divisible by $4k - 1$, and on the next - also that $4m + 1$ is divisible by $4k - 1$.

2. Denote the required relations by $l$, $m$ and $n$ and express the planes of triangles $AL_1N_1$, $AL_2N_2$, etc. through $l$, $m$, $n$ and $S_{\triangle ABC}$.

3. 6) Draw a straight line $FE$, $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta AFE$.

To problems «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren"
(see «Kvant», 1974, no. 8)

1. Not indicated. You need to start with the tallest seventh grader; rearrangements can be made according to parameters (swap the tallest one with the one who occupies his “rightful” place, and so on). For each such permutation, the condition formulated in the problem will be preserved (proof).

2. Using a fishing rod, you can balance a fish and a loaf of bread, as shown in figure 1. Then

$$m_1 = m_2 \frac{l_2}{l_1},$$

where $m_1$ is the mass of the fish, $m_2$ is the mass of the loaf, $l_1$ and $l_2$ are the distances indicated at figure, which can be changed using any units of length.

3. The number $n^2 + n + 1$ is odd for any purpose $n$.

4. One of the possible diagrams is shown in figure 2. With this position of the switches, the lamp does not light up.

5. Let's reduce all the numbers on the board by the same number so that the smallest number on the board becomes zero. From it to the largest number you can reach in no more than 198 transitions (to state cells), so the largest number does not exceed 198 20 = 3960. If each number does not appear on the board more than twice, then there are no more than 2 3960 = 7920 numbers on the board, and according to the condition there are 10,000 of them.

6. The density of the ball is $0,9 e/cm^3$, and the density of aluminum is $2,7 e/cm^3$, therefore, the ball is not solid, it has a cavity. Place the ball in a container of water. He will float in the water; 0.1 of its volume will be above water. If the cavity is not in the center of the ball, then at different positions in the water it will rotate so that the cavity is always in the highest position (Fig. 3).
