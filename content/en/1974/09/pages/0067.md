⟦folio 65⟧

S. Grekhov made an automatic device for feeding lines. This will allow you to control the work with the device and, which is very important in practice, significantly reduce the treatment time.

An interesting report was made by P.I. Vanov (Novosibirsk, 10th grade) “Tunable dye lasers,” which talked about the design of a liquid laser with smooth frequency tuning, created by a group of students in the laboratory of the Novosibirsk Institute of Nuclear Physics.

In the laboratory of technical measurements of the scientific and technical society of students in the city of Knev, for many years, research has been carried out to create electrical devices that can be used in school physics experiments. The reports by V. Koval and P. Movchan (Knev, 10th grade) spoke about current and voltage stabilizers created in the laboratory, high-quality successful electrical signals, a stable signal generator, etc.

V. Drachev (Novosibirsk, 9th grade) in his report “Fish-Eye Camera” spoke about a device he made for taking photographs with a viewing angle close to 180°.

***

One should not think that at the conference, schoolchildren served or read reports from morning to evening. The hosts organize very interesting expertise for the guests in Tbilisi and its surroundings - in Jvari, Mtskheta and Sagurami, as well as in the house-museum of I. G. Chavchavadze.

On March 29, a meeting was held between the conference participants and the editors of our magazine. Deputy editor-in-chief M. L. Smolyansky spoke to the schoolchildren. He spoke about the work of the editors, about the magazine’s plans for 1974-1975. Schoolchildren took an active part in the meeting. They expressed their opinions about the journal, about individual articles, and talked about which areas of mathematics and physics they were especially interested in. Then the editorial staff answered numerous questions from schoolchildren.

On the morning of March 30, a “blitzpolympada” lesson in mathematics was held. Here are some of the problems proposed in this competition.

1. Divide the clock face into three clocks by two faces so that the sums of the numbers in each clock are the same.

2. What's more: ate or ate?

3. Is it possible to use five bronze coins (no. 1, 3 and 5 kopecks) to deduct the amount of 16 kopecks?

4. There are 70 balls in a box: 20 red, 20 yellow, 20 green and 10 black. How many balls must be subtracted from the box (in the dark!) so that there are at least 10 one-ball balls?

The closing ceremony of the conference took place on the afternoon of March 30. The guests thanked the hosts for their hospitality, and the hosts invited everyone present to the next conference.

As a result of the conference, some schoolchildren were loaded with diplomas and certificates.

Certificates of the Georgian Komsomol Central Committee were received by: Nikolay Almatsevil (Tbilisi), Alexander Budarin (Yerevan), Alexander Voronov (Novosibirsk), Aleksed Davidov (Moscow), Vladimir Drachev (Novosibirsk), Mark Drachinsky (Tbilisi), Victor Zubko (Kyiv), Anatoly Ivanov (Moscow), Pavel Ivanov (Novosibirsk), Victor Imkin (Riga), Maya Miminovil (Tbilisi), Vakhtane Pkhakadze (Tbilisi), Vladimir Sanadze (Tbilisi), Andrey Sergeev (Moscow), Levan Surguladze (Tbilisi), Medea Toronjadze (Tbilisi) and Samson Shtamashil (Tbilisi).

A number of awards from our magazine.

Commemorative issues of the magazine with authors were given to: Elena Bekaya (Batumi), Aleksadr Budarin (Yerevan), Vladimir Koval (Kyiv) and Vladimir Kharlanov (Kyiv).

The annual signature for 1975 was loaded with: Maya Miminovil, Marina Sankidze, Alexander Timoshin and Medea Torondzhadze. In addition, some of the most interesting reports will be published on the pages of the journal (for example, Medei Toronjadze’s report has already been published).

What can I say in conclusion?

Our magazine has already written about the following schools with a physical and mathematical bias (see «Kvant», 1972, No. 2, 5). These rallies have been held regularly since 1971. It is gratifying that now students of physics and mathematics boarding schools have organized their own scientific conferences, which are very interesting and meaningful.

We are confident that in the near future, conferences of physics and mathematics boarding schools will gain credibility and will be held not only in Tbilisi, but also, say, in Novosibirsk, Pira and other cities of our country. We wish them further success.

A. N. Vilenkin, V. A. Tikhomirova
