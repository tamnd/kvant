⟦folio 68⟧

Quests

1. There are less than 50 students in the class. For the test work, $1/7$ students received A's, $1/3$ - B's, $1/2$ - C's. The rest of the work turned out to be unsatisfactory. How many such works were there?

2. There is a body of mass $M$ and several weights of identical masses $M$, made of different materials. What weight should be used to balance a body on a scale in a vacuum so that the balance is not disturbed in the air?

3. Some people raised the number to the third power. The resulting three-digit number was written in reverse order to obtain a prime number. Find the original number.

4. It is known that light travels the distance from the Sun to the Earth in approximately 8 minutes. If light travels instantly, would we on Earth have seen the sun rise 8 minutes earlier?

5. The plant director arrives at the station at 8 a.m. every day. At the same time, a car arrives at the station and takes the director to the plant, located in a village several kilometers from the station.

One day the director arrived at the station at 7 am and walked along the highway towards the plant. Soon he met his car, got into it and arrived at the plant 12 minutes earlier than usual.

At what time did the rector meet his car?
