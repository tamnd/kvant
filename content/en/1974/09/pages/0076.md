Branch-barometer

One of the most interesting features of living organisms is to anticipate changes in external conditions and adapt to them in advance. Many plants and animals have the ability to “feel” certain phenomena and influences that humans cannot even sense.

For example, in Japan, fish are specially bred in aquariums, and long before an earthquake they begin to throw about in the aquarium, warning the owner about the possibility of danger. How the fish know that an earthquake is coming is still a mystery.
