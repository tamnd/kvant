⟦folio 60⟧

New books

In this issue we continue to publish summaries of books published in 1974 that are of interest to our readers.

The following books will be published in the third quarter of 1974 (orders for them can be sent through Book by Mail stores):

Mathematics

Publishing house
"Science"

1. Zaitsev V.V., Ryzhkov V.V., Skanavi M.I. Elementary mathematics (volume 35 sheets, circulation 400,000 copies, price 1 rub. 08 kopecks, 2nd edition, revised and supplemented).

The book is a guide for applicants to universities. It systematically presents material corresponding to the program of entrance examinations in mathematics to higher educational institutions. A large number of solved problems makes reading the book much easier.

2. Korovkin P. P. Inequalities (volume 4 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 14 kopecks, 4th edition).

P. P. Korovkin’s brochure is published in the series “Popular Lectures on Mathematics”. It is devoted to some substituted inequalities that play a large role in mathematics.

The large number of problems given in the book makes it especially valuable for schoolchildren.

The book is intended for high school students.

3. Mark HF A.I. Wonderful sinutsy (volume 5 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 18 kopecks, 2nd edition).

In a very lively and interesting way, the author tells the reader about the closest “relatives” of the circular sine - the hyperbolic and leminismatic sines. The book is designed for a thoughtful reader. Strong 10th grade students can read it.

Physics

Publishing house
"Science"

1. Seleznev Yu. A. Fundamentals of elementary physics (volume 30 sheets, circulation 300,000 copies, price 94 kopecks, 4th edition).

The book is intended primarily for people preparing to enter higher education institutions. It consistently sets out the entire course of “school” physics. A large number of problems (with solutions) helps to better assimilate the material.

2. Mechanics. Edited by A. S. Akhmatov (volume 30 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 29 kopecks, 2nd edition).

This book is the third part of a four-volume physics course for American high schools (Part 1 - The Universe, Part 2 - Optics and waves, Part 3 - Mechanics and Part 4 - Electricity and structure.atoma).

The book is useful for physics teachers and schoolchildren in grades 8-10 who are interested in physics issues.

3. Lange V.N. Experimental physical problems for ingenuity (volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 14 kopecks).

The book contains 116 problems in which it seems possible to come up with a way to measure certain quantities using the most primitive and elementary instruments. At the end, an analysis of the problem is given. The book is written in a lively and interesting manner. All physics lovers will read it with satisfaction.

4. Koshkin N. I., Sharkovich M. G. Handbook of elementary physics (volume 13 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 69 kopecks).

The reference book briefly summarizes theoretical information, the formation of basic and physical laws. It also provides reference tables and graphs.

The directory is intended for a wide range of readers.

5. Yavorsky B. M., Detlaf A. A. Handbook of Physics (volume 46 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 2 rubles 63 kopecks, 6th edition).

This reference book is essentially a textbook for a course in general physics. It contains definitions of all basic physical concepts, the formation of physical laws and the essence of the phenomena it describes. It reflects the main sections of classical and modern physics.

The reference book can be used by a physics teacher in a secondary school, as well as by most advanced 10th grade students.

6. Kapitsa P. L., Theory, experiment, practice (volume 20 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 90 kopecks).

The book is a collection of speeches and articles by the extracted Soviet physicist, Academician Pyotr Leonidovich Kapitsa. In these speeches, Pyotr Leonidovich talks about the experimental results he obtained in different years, and about the creative education of youth.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

7. Levantovsky V.I. Mechanics of space flight in an elementary presentation (volume 36 sheets, circulation 15,000 copies, price 1 rub. 26 kopecks, 2nd edition).

The book lays out the fundamentals of cosmodynamics in a rigorous, but at the same time quite accessible manner. It examines delayed flights, flights to the Moon, interplanetary and international flights.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
