⟦folio 40⟧

WINNERS
COMPETITION
"QUANTA"

The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» established special prizes for the most interesting solutions to problems contained in the “Kvant Problem”.
The editors received for 1973-1974. about 3000 letters with solutions to problems placed in the “Quantum Problem” section. Among the authors of these letters, the editorial collection selected schoolchildren who regularly sent especially original and successful solutions. They are loaded with an annual subscription to «Kvant» magazine for 1975.
In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the All-Union Olympiad, these schoolchildren were admitted to the regional rounds of the 1975 All-Union Olympiad on a par with the winner of the regional mathematical and physics Olympiads.

1. GONCHAROV A. (Nikopol, Dnepropetrovsk region, 9th grade)
2. DOKUCHAEV Y. (Leningrad, 10th grade)
3. IVASCHUK V. (Kyiv, 10th grade)
4. KORSHUNOV S. (Monino village, Moscow, region, 10th class)
5. LYUBICH M. (Kharkov, 10th grade)
6. MELNIK S. (Kharkov, 10th grade)
7. MIKHLIN L. (Kursk, 9th grade)
8. REMEEV A. (Tashkent, 10th grade)
9. RESHETNYAK V. (Kyiv, 10th grade)
10. RYZHIKOV V. (Akhtubinsk, 10th grade)
