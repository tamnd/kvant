TV
PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

On September 1, classes began at the Moscow preparatory television courses for applicants to universities.

The main purpose of television preparatory courses is to provide assistance to those entering universities. During the academic year, television preparatory programs are offered cycles of programs in physics, mathematics and the Russian language, which are based on the “Program of entrance examinations for applicants to higher educational institutions of the USSR.” Telecourses aim to systematize students’ knowledge and teach them to apply their knowledge when solving specific issues and problems. Through telecourses, students will be able to get acquainted with the lecture form of conducting classes and acquire independent work skills.

Television classes continue throughout the school year. The television preparatory listener is offered mandatory homework assignments of various difficulties. Periodically, control work is carried out on the sections eaten.

The rules for formatting answers to assignments are indicated in the annotations of classes, which will appear in one envelope addressed to the courses “Application Form”, “Registration Cards” and an envelope with your return address.

The “application form” is drawn up on a postcard, on the front side of which you should write your address and telephone number (home or office), if available. The reverse side of the card is designed as indicated on the sample. In the upper right corner, be sure to put the letters corresponding to the departments in which you want to occupy (if the student is going to study in all departments, he must write the corresponding three letters: “F” corresponds to the department of physics, “M” - mathematics, “P” - Russian language, and indicate in the text of the application the names of all departments).

The “registration card” is also issued on a postcard. On the front side write your address, clean design as indicated on the sample. A photograph should be glued to the upper left corner (size 3 cm × 4 cm). A separate “Registration Card” is filled out for each branch.

On letters with applications addressed to courses, the student must put all three letters (“F”, “M”, “P”), which are indicated in the application form, in the upper left corner of the envelope.
