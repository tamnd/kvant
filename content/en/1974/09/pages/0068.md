XIV Mathematical Olympiad for Ukrainian schoolchildren

From March 25 to March 30, 1974, the XIV Republican Olympiad in Mathematics was held in the city of Dnepropetrovsk. The winners of regional Olympiads were invited to it - those who, at subject competitions, demonstrated flexibility of mind, originality in mathematical thinking, and mathematical literacy.

300 schoolchildren came to Dnepropetrovsk: 54 seventh-graders, 66 eighth-graders, 86 ninth-graders and 94 tenth-graders.

Dnepropetrovsk warmly and cordially welcomed future mathematicians. Open Olympiads took place on March 25 at the Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren of Dnepropetrovsk. All participants presented the competition to gather in the Palace hall. The chairman of the XIV Olympiad, the rector of Dnepropetrovsk State University, Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR V. I. Mossakovsky, addressed them with a welcoming speech. Vladimir Ivanovich told the children in detail about Dnepropetrovsk University, about the city, congratulated them on the start of important competitions and wished them success.

The first round of the competition took place on March 26. Students in each class were presented with three problems, and three problems were presented on the second day of the competition on March 27. On both the first and second days, it took three hours to solve the problems.

The work of the zhorni, as an experienced director, was led by the representative of the Ministry of Education of the Ukrainian SSR A.P. Shatkovsky.

All student work was carried out carefully and conscientiously. Analysis of the task with an in-depth analysis of typical errors was carried out immediately after checking the work.

In the breaks between tours and analysis of problems for schoolchildren, we familiarize ourselves with Dnepropetrovsk. Kind households - teaching school in Dnepropetrovsk - accompanied their guests around the city, showing them all the places of interest. The children visited the Museum of Komsomol Glory, the Theater for Young Spectators, and visited city schools.

A week flew by unnoticed. The Olympic flag was lowered on March 30. At the closing ceremony the results were summed up.

First degree diplomas were received in 7 classes: Vladimir Vovk (Chervonogradskaya school No. 1, Lviv region), Ner Kalika (school No. 32, Kiev) and Mark Nudelman (no. 50, Odessa);

in 8 classes: Dmitry Limvienko (school no. 1, Sevastopol), Serey Liftsits (school no. 27, Kharkov);

in 9 classes: Alexander Blokha (school no. 27, Kharkov), Ilya Bakhumutsky (school no. 52, Lvov), Alexander Reznikov (school no. 145, Kiev), Ilya Yunus (school no. 27, Kharkov);

in 10 classes: Grigory Sklyar (school no. 27, Kharkov), Nikolay Chernov (school no. 95, Krivoy Rog, Dnepropetrovsk region), Nikolay Sherbina (school no. 80, Dnepropetrovsk).

17 people received second degree diplomas and 29 people received third degree diplomas. 242 people received diplomas of participants in the XIV Olympiad for schoolchildren of Ukraine. In addition, many children received diplomas, prizes and memorable gifts established by higher educational institutions of Dnepropetrovsk.

Here are some of the problems proposed at the Olympiad.

8th grade

1. Find the minimum of an expression
$$\frac{a_1}{1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots + a_{1974}} + \frac{a_2}{1 + a_1 + a_3 + \dots + a_{1974}} + \dots + \frac{a_{1974}}{1 + a_1 + \dots + a_{1973}},$$
if $a_i \geq 0$ and $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_{1974} = 1$.

2. There are four lines in general position given on the plane (that is, every three of them form a triangle). One of them is parallel to the median of the triangle formed by the other straight lines. Prove that these properties are also true for all others.

9th grade

3. Prove that there is an infinite number of natural numbers that cannot be represented in the form
$$a_1^6 + a_2^6 + \dots + a_7^6,$$
where $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_7$ are natural numbers.

10th grade

4. $n$ rays are drawn from a point in space so that any two of them form an angle of at least 30°. Prove that there are no more than 58 rays.
