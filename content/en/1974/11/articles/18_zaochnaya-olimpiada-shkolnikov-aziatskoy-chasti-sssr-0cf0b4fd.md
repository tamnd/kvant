Correspondence Olympiad
Schoolchildren
Aznat part of the USSR

In this issue we publish the texts of correspondence problems for physics, mathematics and chemistry Olympiads for students of schools and other secondary educational institutions in Siberia, Kazakhstan, the Far East and Central Asia.

The Olympics are held by the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences together with public education organizations.

Those who successfully cope with the tasks of the correspondence Olympiad will be invited to the region, regional, Resursivan Olympiads on equal terms with the winner of the Olympiad region. Invitations will be sent by the Olympiad Committee to home addresses.

After each task, the classes for whose students it is proposed are indicated in parentheses. To become a winner of the Olympiad, it is not necessary to solve many problems. There are plenty of other witty solutions for one. But solving a problem doesn’t just mean giving the right answer; it is necessary to justify it, to give precise evidence. The tasks do not require any knowledge beyond the high school curriculum. Solutions to the problem for each subject must be sent in a separate standard format envelope. On the envelope, indicate in which subject the problems were solved (mathematics, physics or chemistry). The letter must include a stamped envelope with a return home address; In this envelope you will be sent a notice of the results of the work. Solutions must be written very clearly. There is no need to rewrite the task conditions, but be sure to indicate task numbers.

At the beginning of the letter, write clearly:

1. Your last name, first name and patronymic.
2. Postal home address (detailed).
3. Class, school, college, technical, etc., address.
4. Last name, first name and patronymic of your teacher in this subject.

The last day you can still send a decision is February 1, 1975, postmarked. Letters sent later will not be considered.

Participants of the Olympiad send decisions to the address: 630090, Novosibirsk, 90, Olympiad.
