Praise for Gray Wolf
Reading the fairy tale “Ivan Tsarevich and the Gray Wolf” *), you are involuntarily surprised:
Ivan Tsarevich does practically nothing, but he gets all the treasures - the Firebird, the golden-maned horse and even Helen the Beautiful. The Gray Wolf adds everything to Ivan Tsarevich. However, things are not quite as they seem at first glance.

Ivan Tsarevich passes the entrance exam - he does not sleep at night, like his brothers, and thanks to this he sees the Firebird. He even manages to grab the Firebird by the tail. True, he only has one feather left in his hand.

Therefore, it seems to us that it was no coincidence that the Gray Wolf drew attention to Ivan Tsarevich and ate his horse.

*) This refers to the Russian folk tale as retold by A. Tolstoy (M., Children's Literature, 1973).

Gray Wolf is a brilliant teacher. He uses Ivan the Princess for training. First, he forces Ivan the Princess himself to try to solve all the problems: to get the Firebird from Tsar Afron, then the golden-maned horse from Tsar Kusman, and, finally, Helen the Beautiful from Tsar Dalmatia. Any task becomes truly attractive only after you try to solve it. When Princess Ivan, under the leadership of the Gray Wolf, appreciated all the treasures, the Gray Wolf himself solves all the problems for him.

As a result, Ivan Tsarevich gets everything he wanted. However, he comes to pass another test - to be killed by his own brothers, envious competitors who did not pass the entrance exam. Here we must once again pay tribute to the Gray Wolf, who in this difficult hour falls to Princess Ivana. One can only wish every hardworking student such a leader.

We liked the teaching method belonging to the Gray Wolf, and we want to present several tasks as a continuation of this fairy tale.

About how the Gray Wolf shows that not every desire can be fulfilled

So, to get ready, Ivan Tsarevich marries Elena the Beautiful. But that was not the case. Many guests came to the wedding, and suddenly King Afron, King Kusman and King Dalmatia came. They say:

“You deceived us, Ivan Tsarevich, you took everything.” Well, oh well. Let's tell you riddles. If you don’t figure it out, we’ll take it all back.

King Afron was the first to hold the stove, and he said this:

“Tomorrow I will have several guests, but not all of them are friends.” I will only say that each of them has an even number of friends. Proof that I can seat them at round tables so that each guest sits next to his friends.

Here Ivan Tsarevich once again called the Gray Wolf, although he said goodbye to him forever. Gray Wolf asks:

“What, Ivan Tsarevich, are you sitting there, sad and hanging your head?”

- How can I not be sad, Gray Wolf? How can I seat guests at round tables?

Gray Wolf answers:

— I solved a lot of problems, and I solved a lot of them. But this problem cannot always be solved. Call, Ivan Tsarevich, seven of your guests, each one will counter you.

Ivan Tsarevich invited seven guests. Here the Gray Wolf turned each of them into a point, and these points were connected by arcs. And here Ivan Tsarevich figure 1 saw. And this Gray Wolf figure wished this:

- If two points are connected by an arc, then it means that the guests who turned into these points will be friends. So, know that there is no way to seat all these guests at round tables.

Ivan Tsarevich ran to Tsar Afron and showed the counter-measure of the Wolf. The king, as soon as he saw the counter-attacker, was frightened where he stood and fell there. And when he came to his senses, he said: Fig. 1.

About how Ivan Tsarevich forgot about the “isthmus”

King Kusman says:
- Hear me, Ivan Tsarevich, serve. In total, I have ten cities in my kingdom, some of them are connected by roads. An even number of roads depart from each city. You, Ivan Tsarevich, leaving the capital city, take all the roads once and turn back to the capital.
Tsarevich Ivan prepared even better than before. He went again to the Gray Wolf. And Gray Wolf to him:
- Don’t be too harsh, Ivan Tsarevich, but listen to my advice. Leave the capital on one road and then drive as you want, but only so that you don’t drive along the same road twice. Make sure you don’t turn into the capital ahead of time, and also make sure that no road crosses for you.
Ivan Tsarevich was delighted and did not notice how he found himself in the capital of Tsar Kusman. He jumped on a maned horse and galloped along the roads. He liked the Wolf’s advice: go as you want, so he rushed off, only making sure not to follow his own trail!
Whether it's long or short, he comes back to the capital. He looks at all the roads from the capital, which means he can’t go any further, and he won’t be able to pass a few more roads. Let’s remember the Wolf’s last advice, but he forgot to ask what he called the “isthmus”! So he returned with nothing.
The Gray Wolf began to reprimand him:

About how Ivan Tsarevich himself agreed on what is called the “isthmus”
“Let me, Gray Wolf, I’ll think about how I can solve Kusan’s problem,” and again Ivan Tsarevich looked at figure 2.
Ivan Tsarevich thought for a long time and finally realized what a fatal road had become for him. If the roads along which he traveled are erased from the earth one after another (Fig. 6), then that road turns out to be bad, after the “erasure” of which the whole kingdom is divided into such two parts that you can’t get from one to the other. This means that there was no need to turn onto the isthmus road (the Wolf drew it with a dotted line).
Ivan Tsarevich began to look on the map for another way, such that not a single road was an isthmus, and he found it. “Eh,” thought Ivan Tsarevich, “thanks to the Wolf for drawing the map and giving good advice.” He went to King Kusan and showed him the way. He drove away.

Fabulous solution
All that remained was for Tsarevich Ivan to solve the first problem - the one that Tsar Afron had asked for. He went into an open field and thought. The Gray Wolf praised him and sent him to King Afron.
Ivan Tsarevich says:
- You, King Afron, will have $n$ guests, each of them will have $2m$ friends. I’ll explain to you how to seat these guests at round tables.
- Build a city for each guest, and if two guests are friends, then you will walk the road between these cities. Then go around these roads the same way I went around the kingdom of Kusanna. And where to go, there is an arrow on the road (Fig. 7).
It turns out that each city has $m$ roads with arrows leading to it, and along other $m$ roads arrows go out of it. This is because the number of times you leave the city, the number of times you will leave.

Now woo them all the same way I wooed King Dalmatia.

After that, you can tell the guests. Take any guest and seat him at a round table. On the right, next to him, seat the one with whose local the first guest was engaged. It turns out that the first guest is sitting with his brother-in-law. Place his brother-in-law to the right of the second guest too. So he goes on and on. So you will have a round table.

If not all the guests were seated this way, then another table will have to be set up. Again, take some guest who didn’t make it to the first table, and seat his brother-in-law next to him. And then do everything like the first time.
