⟦folio 74⟧

Quests

1. Voloda wrote on the blackboard:
$$1*2*3*4*5*6*7*8*9=21$$
and together with the stars he put either a plus or a minus. Sasha transferred several signs to the opposite ones, and as a result, together with the number 21, he received the number 20.

Is it possible to say that at least one of the boys made a mistake when calculating the result?

2. Candle flame temperature ~1600°C. The melting point of iron is 1400°C. Why doesn't the nail melt on the candle? (And if you bring an iron copka into the flame on a heat-resistant handle, it will melt.)

3. Restore multiplication notation:

ODE
RICE
PAT
IND
SOP
SPORT

4. Will a Segner wheel rotate in a vacuum? (The spider-like pinwheel you see here in the picture is the Segner wheel.)

5. Find five numbers, knowing that their sums of three are respectively 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 10, 12, 14, 16 and 17.

6. Here is an image of a cube on a plane. Draw, without lifting the pencil from the paper, one continuous line that would intersect once at a time all 16 segments that make up the figure.

Where should this line begin and where should it end?
