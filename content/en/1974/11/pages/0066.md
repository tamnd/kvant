⟦folio 64⟧

In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the fourth quarter of 1974. Orders for books should be placed through specialized stores or “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house
"Science"

1. Sominsky I. Ya. Method of mathematical induction. 8th edition. Volume 3 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 36 kopecks

This book is dedicated to the presentation of one of the most important methods of mathematics - the method of mathematical induction. The clarity of presentation and well-chosen examples have earned this book wide popularity.

The book is available to high school students. It can be successfully used in math circle classes.

2. Savelyev L. Ya. Combinatorics and probability. Volume 25 l., circulation 10,000 copies, price 2 rubles. 42 kopecks

The book is devoted to the presentation of the elementary theory of probability and its applications. In the first chapters, the author breaks down the issues of combinatorics using a large number of examples. The book is written in well-written language. A large number of varied examples and problems add to the value of the book. The book is intended for high school students, mathematics and physics teachers. It can be successfully used for extracurricular activities and math classes.

Publishing house
"World"

3. Wenninger M. Modell mnodernannikov. Volume 20 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 1 rub. 40 kopecks

This book introduces readers to such symmetrical figures as polyhedra. In addition to theoretical principles, the book describes in detail methods for making regular and rectangular, convex and star-shaped polyhedra. A large number of photographs and drawings provide clarity to the material presented.

The book is accessible to a wide range of readers. It can be successfully used in math circle classes.

4. Golomb S. U. Polyomino. Volume 11 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 56 kopecks.

This book outlines a number of problems associated with covering chess and similar boards with sets of special figures, called by the author “polyominoes”*) by analogy with dominoes, which are the simplest special case of such figures. Golomb's task contains simple conditions and is entertaining with great mathematical content. Problem solving does not require specialized knowledge, but requires a determination of ingenuity.

Schoolchildren, students, and teachers will read the book with interest.

5. Steinhauz G. Problems in Polish. Volume 25 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 36 kopecks

The author of this book has won a great reputation among our readers. His books “Mathematical Kaleidoscope” and “One Hundred Problem” have long been a bibliographic editor.

Steinhaus's new book is written in a bright, captivating language, is one of the best books in popular literature and will undoubtedly bring a lot of joy to its readers. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

Physics

Publishing house
"Science"

1. Bukhovtsev B.B., Krivenkov V.D. et al. Collection of problems in elementary physics. 4th edition. Volume 30 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 94 kopecks.

The collection contains problems for all sections of the elementary physics course. Most problems in all sections of the elementary physics course. Along with relatively easy problems, a large number of “Olympic” type problems are given.

The book is intended for high school students. It can be successfully used for self-education.

2. Physics (part IV), edited by A. S. Akhmatov. Volume 35 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 47 kopecks

This book is the final part of an American high school textbook on a general physics course. The entire course consists of four parts: “The Universe”, “Optics and Waves”, “Mechanics”, “Electricity and the Structure of the Atom”. Each part contains relevant material taken from the book "Methodological Guide"
