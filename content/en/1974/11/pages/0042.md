Tasks

M291—M295, F303—F307

M291. On sides $A_2A_3$, $A_3A_1$, $A_1A_2$ of triangle $A_1A_2A_3$ squares with centers $O_1$, $O_2$, $O_3$ lying outside the triangle are constructed. Prove that:

a) segments $O_1O_2$ and $A_3O_3$ are equal in length and mutually perpendicular;

b) the midpoints of the segments $A_3A_1$, $O_1O_2$, $A_3A_2$ and $A_3O_3$ are the vertices of the square;

b) the area of ​​this square is 8 times less than the area of ​​the square with center $O_3$.

V. M. Fishman

M292. Numbers from 1 to 50 are written on the board. You are allowed to erase any two numbers and write them together as one number - the modulus of their difference. After repeating this procedure several times, one number remains on the board. What number could this be?

F. G. Shleifer

M293. Given a triangle $C_1C_2O$. It carries bissertis $C_2C_3$, then in triangle $C_2C_3O$ - bissertis $C_3C_4$ and so on. Prove that the sequence of angle values ​​$\gamma_n = \frac{1}{2}C_{n+1}C_nO$ tends to a limit, and find this limit if $\frac{1}{2}C_1OC_2 = \alpha$.

A. Vernard, M. Felshtin and I. Tkachev, 10th grade students

M294. Prove that if $a, b, c, d, x, y, u, v$ are real numbers and $abcd > 0$, then $(ax+bu)(av+by)(cx+dv)(cu+dy) \geqslant (acuvx+bcuxy+advxy+bduvy) \times (acx+bcu+adv+bdy)$.

V. P. Fedomov

M295*. Sections of a convex polyhedron by parallel planes $p_0, p_1$ and $p_2$ ($p_1$ is located between $p_0$ and $p_2$ at the same distance $h$ from the point and the other) have areas $S_0, S_1$ and $S_2$ respectively. There is no vertex of the polyhedron between $p_0$ and $p_2$.
