⟦folio 28⟧

King Afron became angry:
“I don’t understand you, Ivan Tsarevich.”
I need to seat my guests so round, and you’re talking about some cities.

Ivan Tsarevich will answer him:
- Be patient, listen to the end.
Let each of your ERO guests have a place, so that as many good fellows as guests, there will be as many beautiful girls. Now look; if an arrow goes from some city - guests to another city - guests, then introduce this guest to the sister of the second one.

Since a shooter leaves each city $m$, and a shooter enters each city $m$, it turns out that every guest knows $m$ girls, and every girl $m$ knows good fellows.

Now woo them all the same way I wooed King Dalmatia.

After that, you can tell the guests. Take any guest and seat him at a round table. On the right, next to him, seat the one with whose local the first guest was engaged. It turns out that the first guest is sitting with his brother-in-law. Place his brother-in-law to the right of the second guest too. So he goes on and on. So you will have a round table.

If not all the guests were seated this way, then another table will have to be set up. Again, take some guest who didn’t make it to the first table, and seat his brother-in-law next to him. And then do everything like the first time.

And Ivan Tsarevich showed three figure 7 for clarity.

King Afron looked and looked and did not understand this wisdom, but he took his word for it. With that, he left for his place.

And Ivan Tsarevich bowed to the Gray Wolf and said goodbye to him forever. He married Elena the Beautiful, and they began to live and live happily ever after.
