Batumi Medea Illarionovna Zhenti

Praise for Gray Wolf
Reading the fairy tale “Ivan Tsarevich and the Gray Wolf” *), you are involuntarily surprised:
Ivan Tsarevich does practically nothing, but he gets all the treasures - the Firebird, the golden-maned horse and even Helen the Beautiful. The Gray Wolf adds everything to Ivan Tsarevich. However, things are not quite as they seem at first glance.

Ivan Tsarevich passes the entrance exam - he does not sleep at night, like his brothers, and thanks to this he sees the Firebird. He even manages to grab the Firebird by the tail. True, he only has one feather left in his hand.

Therefore, it seems to us that it was no coincidence that the Gray Wolf drew attention to Ivan Tsarevich and ate his horse.

*) This refers to the Russian folk tale as retold by A. Tolstoy (M., Children's Literature, 1973).
