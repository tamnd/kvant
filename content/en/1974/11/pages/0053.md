⟦folio 51⟧

When heated at constant pressure, a gas receives an amount of heat

$$Q = c_p m \left( T' - T_0 \right).$$

From the equation of state of an ideal gas

$$p_0 V_0 = n R T_0 \text{ и } p_0 V_1 = n R T'$$

we have:

$$T' - T_0 = \frac{p_0 \left( V_1 - V_0 \right)}{n},$$

$$Q = c_p m \frac{p_0 \left( V_1 - V_0 \right)}{n}.$$

Dividing equality (1) by equality (2), we obtain

$$1 = \frac{c_V}{c_p} \frac{V_0 \left( p_1 - p_0 \right)}{p_0 \left( V_1 - V_0 \right)},$$

From here

$$\frac{c_p}{c_V} = \frac{V_0 \left( p_1 - p_0 \right)}{p_0 \left( V_1 - V_0 \right)},$$

I. Sh. Slobodetsky
