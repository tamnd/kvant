⟦folio 26⟧

- Don’t rush to rejoice, first solve the problem, and then talk.

The Gray Wolf turned the good fellows into blue dots, and the red maidens into red dots, and even renumbered them so as not to get confused. Then these points were connected by segments so that it became clear who was friends with whom. And Tsarevich Ivan saw figure 3. He began, as he had to, to separate the young men and the girls into pairs with green lines, and this is what he got (see Fig. 4): two young men (7 and 10) were left alone and he offended two girls (2 and 7).

Ivan Tsarevich grabbed his head and began to cry. The Gray Wolf consoled him:

- It’s okay, Ivan Tsarevich, I’ll correct your decision. How do you know the method of alternating chains? Better watch what I'm going to do. Now I’ll put another 7th youngster.

Gray Wolf looked at drawings 3 and 4 and started drawing a new drawing. He took the 7th young man and connected him with the 10th red girl he knew with a red segment, looked again at Figure 3, and she and the 8th groom had a green linear connection. Then from the 8th young man he ate a red line. This line led to the 2nd girl, who had not yet been set (see Fig. 4, 6).

The Gray Wolf ended up with the following chain: red segment, green segment, red segment, ... Says the Wolf:

- This is what is called an alternating target. Now we place the red segments on the green ones, and remove the green one. The rest, as you wanted, we will match. - And the wolf moved the picture 4, and in 4, 6.

- Now the 7th fellow and the 2nd girls will not be married. Well, tell me, what should we do with the 10th fellow, how to position him?

Then Ivan Tsarevich took drawing 4, drew it, and then corrected the matchmaking once again. He ended up with drawing 5.

So Ivan Tsarevich wooed everyone.

Suddenly he and the Wolf hear someone talking to them from an oak tree with their heads. I looked, and there the Little Crow was sitting, the son of that Raven who had brought the body earlier.
