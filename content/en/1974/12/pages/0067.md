“Honestly, it’s impossible to run away in such a refrigerator,” said John Messner. “If not all 80 are below zero now, then 79 are true.” (Temperatures are in Fahrenheit.)

“If at 45° below zero you take off your glove and hold your hand before your eyes, you can see that shavings of steam rise from each finger...” (Here the temperature is indicated in Celsius).
Which of the mentioned temperatures is actually lower if it is known that in a Fahrenheit thermometer the temperature of a mixture of snow and ammonia is taken to be approximately 18°C, and the boiling point of water is 212°F.
