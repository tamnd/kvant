BALL TOUCHING THE EDGES OF A POLYHEDON

In recent years, university entrance exams often offer problems that involve a ball touching all the edges of a polyhedron. This article is related to this topic.

Let the ball touch all the edges of a certain polyhedron. Then the following statements are true (prove them yourself):

(1) each face of the polyhedron intersects the surface of the ball along a circle tangent to the edges of the polyhedron, that is, along a circle inscribed in the face; thus, the faces of the polyhedron will also be polygons in which a circle can be inscribed;

(2) the base of the perpendicular, lowered from the center of the ball to any face of the polyhedron, is the center of the circle inscribed in this face;

(3) the perpendiculars, restored to the planes of the faces at the centers of the inscribed circles, intersect at one point equal to the one formed from all the edges of the polyhedron - in the center of the ball;

(4) the segment of the perpendicular dropped from the center of the ball to the edge of the polyhedron is equal to the radius of the ball.

Now let's consider some types of polytopes for which the indicated ball exists.
