Physics

At the physics department of telephone physics and mathematics courses for applicants to universities in Matra - straight from 1974, the section “Oscillations and Waves” is studied. Lectures and practical classes discuss issues related to mechanical vibrations and waves, sound, electromagnetic vibrations and waves. In conclusion, a review lecture on wave processes will be given.

Below are typical homework assignments for the section “Oscillations and Waves.”

1. Loads of different masses are suspended from two identical weightless vertical springs. The periods of oscillation of springs with loads correspond to $T_1 = 1,0$ s and $T_2 = 1,1$ s. Determine the difference in the length of the springs in the equilibrium position.

2. Mathematical so that the distance between the extreme positions of the matrix is ​​$2A = 6,0$ cm, and the maximum speed of movement of the matrix is ​​$v_{\max} = 0,40$ m/s. Determine the oscillation period of the matrix.

3. The vibration frequencies of two mathematical matrices located in the same place on the Earth are related as $n = \frac{v_1}{v_2} = 0,90$. Determine the lengths of matrices $l_1$ and $l_2$ if their difference is $\Delta l = l_1 - l_2 = 21$ cm.

4. The kinetic energy of the mathematical matrix as it passes through the equilibrium position is equal to $W_K = 0,10$ J. Determine the magnitude of the restoring force at a time equal to one-eighth of the matrix oscillation period, if the length of the thread is $l = 1,0$ m and the mass of the matrix point is $m = 1,0$ ke.

5. A material point of mass $m = 2,0$ e with charge $q = +8,0 \cdot 10^{-6}$ k, fixed at the end of an insulating thread of length $l = 1,2$ m, is located in the electric field of the stressed axis so that the angle made by the thread with the vertical is $\alpha = 20^\circ$. Find the period of revolution of the point if the force lines of the electric field are vertical and directed upward.

6. In a uniform electric field, the force lines of which are horizontal, a material point of mass $m = 15$ e with a charge $q = 3,0 \cdot 10^{-6}$ k is suspended on a thin thread $l = 35$ cm long. Find the period of natural oscillations of the point if the electric field strength is $E = 4,0 \cdot 10^4$ v/m.

7. Determine the length of the transverse wave propagating along the cord if the distance between two close points having the same displacement is $l = 0,40$ m, and the phase difference between the oscillations of these points is $\varphi_1 - \varphi_2 = 2\pi/3$.

8. A coil with inductance $L = 0,13$ e is connected to an alternating current network in series with a capacitor embone $C = 1,5$ uF. Determine the thermal power released in the coil if the effective value of the voltage in the network is $U = 100$ V, the frequency is $v = 400$ ec, the active resistance of the coil is $R = 15$ ohm.

9. Two flat electrodes of the same formula are lowered parallel to each other into an electrolyte with a dielectric constant $\varepsilon = 24$. Find the specific resistance of the electrolyte if at frequency $v = 10^6$ the active resistance and capacitance of the electrolyte resistance are the same.

10. In an alternating current circuit with a frequency of $v = 50$ eng in series with a resistance of $R = 100$ ohm, first coils with an inductance $L = 0,20$ en are connected in turn, and then a capacitor embone $C = 10$ µF. Determine the ratio of the amplitude values ​​of currents flowing through resistance $R$.

11. When the em-value of the variable capacitor changes by $\Delta C = 50$ nф, the resonant frequency of the circuit increases from $v_1 = 100$ ke to $v_2 = 120$ ke. Determine the inductance values ​​of the counter.

12. The oscillatory circuit, persistent at the frequency $v = 15$ mcs, consists of a coil with inductance $L = 5,0 \cdot 10^{-6}$ en and a flat capacitor in which the gap between the plates is filled with mica. Find the width of the hole if the dielectric constant of mica is $\varepsilon = 6,0$, and the area of ​​the plates is $S = 15$ cm².

13. The oscillatory circuit consists of a coil with inductance $L = 15 \times 10^{-6}$ en and two series capacitors, after the development of them, the length of the electromagnetic wave emitted by the circuit changed by $\Delta \lambda = 20$ m. Find the value of the capacitor capacitance if the capacitor capacitance of the second one is $C_2 = 120$ nf.
