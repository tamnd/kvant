Therefore, in addition to the gravity force $P = mg$, the electric force $f = qE$ acts on the ball. Therefore, in the formula for the period of oscillation, it is necessary to replace $P$ with $F_1 = mg + qE$ if the force $f$ is directed downwards, and with $F_2 = |mg - qE|$ if it is directed upwards. Since $T_1 < T_2$, then

$$T_1 = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{ml}{mg + qE}},$$

$$T_2 = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{ml}{|mg - qE|}}.$$

From these formulas one finds

$$l = \frac{T_1^2 (mg + qE)}{4\pi^2 m} = \frac{T_2^2 |mg - qE|}{4\pi^2 m}.$$

If $mg > qE$, then the ball swings under the thread suspension point before and after recharging the capacitor. In this case

$$T_1^2 (mg + qE) = T_2^2 (mg - qE)$$

and therefore

$$E = \frac{mg (T_2^2 - T_1^2)}{q (T_1^2 + T_2^2)} \approx 250 \text{ $\theta$/м}.$$

If $mg < qE$, then the ball, after recharging the capacitor, swings above the suspension point. At the same time

$$T_1^2 (mg + qE) = T_2^2 (qE - mg)$$

and therefore

$$E = \frac{mg (T_2^2 + T_1^2)}{q (T_1^2 + T_2^2)} \approx 1690 \text{ $\theta$/м}.$$
