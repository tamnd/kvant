H. Goldbach (1690-1764) was a scientist by training and, as a mathematician, worked at the Academy for a very short time. The main part of his activity in Russia took place in the Department of Foreign Affairs, where he held a major post. He entered mathematics primarily as the author of the famous Goldbach problem. This problem can be given several almost equivalent formulas, we will give one of them: any integer greater than two can be represented as the sum of two prime numbers. This fact was replaced by Goldbach with four numbers of the first tens. For two hundred years, there was no visible approach to solving Goldbach's problems. And only in the thirty years of our century such paths were found in the works of L. G. Shirelm and I. M. Vinogradav.
