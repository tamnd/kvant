PREPARING FOR UNIVERSITY

Mathematics

In April, at the mathematics department of telecourses, lectures are given on the topics: “Complex numbers”, “Task for composing equations”, “Straight lines and planes in space”, “Polyhedra”, “Surfaces and volumes of bodies”. Some homework problems on these topics are presented to the reader «Kvant» for independent solution.

1. Find real values ​​of $x$ for which the complied numbers

$$z_1 = \sqrt{x^2 - 3} + 3 - i \sin \frac{\pi x}{4}$$

And

$$z_2 = \sqrt{x^2 + 5} + 1 - i \sin^2 \frac{\pi x}{4}$$

are conjugate.

2. The product of a certain natural number and a number written in the same digits, but in reverse order, is equal to 1008. Find the original number.

3. Water flows evenly into the pit. 10 identical pumps operating simultaneously can pump out water from a filled pit in 12 hours, and 15 identical pumps operating simultaneously can pump out water in 6 hours. In how many hours can 25 similar pumps operating simultaneously pump out water from a filled pit?

4. The total surface area of ​​a rectangular parallelepiped is 192 $cm^2$; if the thickness of each of the edges is one centimeter, then the total surface will decrease by 70 $cm^2$. Find the length of the diagonal of the parallelepiped.

5. In an inclined parallelepiped, two mixed lateral faces have planes $S_1$ and $S_2$ and form a dihedral angle equal to 150°. Find the volume of a parallelepiped if its side edge is equal to $a$.

6. The base of the parallelepiped is an equilateral trapezoid, the bases of which are $a$ and $2a$. The side faces are parallel to the base plane; parallel height $a$. Find the lateral surface of the parallels.

7. The side of the base of a regular quadrangular parallel is $a$, the height of the parallels is $h$. A section is drawn through the side of the base of the parallels and the middle of the side edge, which do not intersect on this side. Determine the distance from the top of the parallels to the plane of this section.

8. The dihedral angle at the lateral edge of a regular quadrangular parallel is equal to $\alpha$. Determine the dihedral angle at the base of the parallels.

Test No. 4

1. Solve the equation

$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 \left(x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{4}.$$

2. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} \lg (x^2 + y^2) = \lg (2 \cdot 5^{\log_2 2500}), \\ \lg (x + y) - \lg (x - y) = \frac{\log_5 49}{\log_5 100}. \end{cases}$$

3. Solve inequality

$$\frac{\sqrt{8 - 2x - x^2}}{x} < 1.$$

4. Solve the equation

$$2 \cos^2 x + \sin \frac{x}{3} + 1 = 0.$$

5. Solve inequality

$$\log_x (3x) \leq \sqrt{\log_x (3x^3)}.$$

Test No. 5

1 (MSU, 1972). From two points $A$ and $B$ located on the river (in the current below $A$), the distance between which is 36 km, two boats simultaneously leave in pursuit of each other (the first leaves from point $A$) and meet 3 hours after leaving. The speed of the second boat in still water is four times the speed of the river flow. The distance from point $A$ to point $B$ and back is covered by the first boat in 24 hours. Find the speed of the river flow.

2 (MEPhI, 1972). The stamp collection consists of three albums. The first of the albums contains one fifth of the stamps, the second contains several gray ones, and the third contains 303 stamps. How many stamps are in the collection?

3 (MSU, 1972). A ball of radius $R$ is inscribed in the straight line ABCDA'B'C'D', the lower base of which is the rhombus ABCD, and AA', BB', CC', DD' are the side edges. Find the cross-sectional area of ​​straight planes passing through the vertices $A$, $B$ and $C'$, if it is known that $BAD = \alpha$.
