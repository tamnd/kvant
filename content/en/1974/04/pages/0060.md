4. $S = \sqrt{4q^2 + \frac{p^2}{12}}$. Solution.

It is clear from the condition that $BM$ and $CK$ are the medians of the triangle $ABC$, so the base of the altitude in pairs is the point $O$ of intersection of these medians (Fig. 3). Let us denote the height in pairs by $H$, the edge $BC$ by $a$. The height of the triangle $ABC$, obtained from the vertex $A$ to the side $BC$, is through $h$ (this height is not drawn in the drawing). According to the condition

$$p = \frac{ah}{2}$$

(4)

In the trapezoid $MKBC$ (why is it a trapezoid?) we draw the height $FL$ passing through the point $O$. Then $DF$ is the height of the triangle (section) $MDK$, and $DL$ is the height of the triangle (face) $BDC$ (prove it!). Since $FL = h/2$ (why?) and since $FO:OL = 1:2$ (where does this follow?), then

$$q = \frac{a}{4} \sqrt{H^2 + \frac{h^2}{36}}$$
