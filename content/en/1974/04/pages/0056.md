⟦folio 54⟧

Quests

1. Cargo to the city $A$ is supposed to be delivered by river to a certain point $B$, and then by automatic route along the highway $BA$ (see figure). The cost of transportation by river is half that by highway. How to check highway $AB$ so that transportation costs are as low as possible?

2. Two cellulon balls of equal masses but different diameters are held in a vessel with water just below the surface of the water. If you let go of the balls, which one will jump higher? (Resistance numbers are not taken into account.)

3. While walking around the city, three mathematics students noticed that the driver of the machine gun had grossly violated the traffic rules. The students did not remember the car number (four digits), but each of them noticed one of its features:

1) the first two digits of the number were the same;
2) the last two digits matched;
3) the number was a perfect square.

Is it possible to find out the car number using this data?

4. There is a metal charged ball on an insulating handle. How can the charge of this ball be completely transferred to the electroscope?

5. There are 5 loudspeakers installed on the square, divided into two groups: one with 2, the other with 3 devices. The distance between the groups is 50 m. Where should you stand so that the sounds of both groups are of equal strength?
