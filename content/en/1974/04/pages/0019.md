Jacobi was one of the most notable representatives of that new phase in the history of physics, when its results immediately, in the form of the most important factor, passed into technology, electromagnetism turned into electrical engineering. Physicists Jacobi and Lenz in 1839 sailed along the Neva on a motor boat built by Immi with two galleys, which developed one horsepower from 64 Grove elements. In the physics laboratory, various new telegraph apparatus were built and studied, and galvanoplasty was developed in various forms, the replaced samples of which serve as decoration for the Physics Institute of the Academy of Sciences. Physicist Jacobi, together with Lenz, designs a lightning rod for a powder magazine and is the inventor of electric mine fuses.

The name of Academician Jacobi, an outstanding physicist, a brilliant electrical engineer and inventor, should rightfully be placed along with other leading names of academicians in physics - Euler, Lomonosov, Petrov...

Genrikh Ivanovich Wild (1833-1902) - organizer of the Tsarist and Russian meteorological network, author of futometric and polarization instruments, an outstanding researcher of terrestrial magnetism. Russia G.I. Wild completed the 27 best creative years of his life. The Russian meteorological network before Wild's work consisted of only 31 stations; when he left, it had 650 stations equipped with new instruments...

Boris Borisovich Goltsyn (1862-1916)… Goltsyn’s scientific work first proceeded in various directions (critical states of matter, X-rays, spectroscopy, physiological optics). The optical works of V.V. Goltsyn are very interesting and important... Among them, the experimental proof of the spectroscopic Dopper phenomenon *), which continues and highly refines the experiments of A. A. Belopolsky, was especially important. At the beginning of the new century, Goltsyn's interests concentrated on a new geophysical discipline - seismology, of which Goltsyn can rightfully be considered one of the founders. Goltsyn gave the first experiments, developed equipment, organized a fairly extensive network of seismological stations...

[*] When the radiation source and the receiving device move relative to each other, the frequency of the received radiation does not coincide with the frequency of the emitted radiation. Thus, the spectral lines of registration of its radiation turn out to be mixed in comparison with the spectrum of the emitted radiation.]
