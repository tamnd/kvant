This was the time of development of the Newtonian legacy, on the one hand, its strengthening, on the other - criticism. The 18th century itself did not produce results in physics that were even approximately equivalent in significance to Newton’s. Electrostatics and optics, mainly geometric, have advanced very much; Much preparatory work in the 18th century was done in the doctrine of heat, but still fundamentally new and more in electromagnetism, optics and heat should have been done by the physicist of the 19th century.

Against the general background of physics in the 18th century, the works of Euler, Lomonosov, and Elinus have a very powerful place. At the same time, in the field of optics, Euler and Lomonosov prepared the ground for the development of the theory of light waves.

This is the general scientific significance of our academic physics in the 18th century. But it also had its own special feature, which was of significance for Russia.
