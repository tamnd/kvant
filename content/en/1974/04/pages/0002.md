The engraving shown here depicts the former palace of Tsarina Praskovya Fedorovna (right), which personally housed the Academy of Sciences, including its physics office. Nearby is the building of the Kunstkamera of Peter I, also transferred to the Academy of Sciences. The engraving is taken from the book “Plan of the capital city of St. Petersburg with images of those who knew one of the proslekts, published by the works of the Imperial Academy of Sciences and Arts” in 1753.
