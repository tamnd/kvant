⟦folio 47⟧

Moscow State University
named after M.V. Lomonosov

We have already talked about the faculties of physics and mathematics at Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov *). Below are the materials of the 1973 entrance exams in mathematics and physics at these faculties.

Matematika

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$2(\cos x)^{5/2} - \cos 2x = 1 + \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{3}}(1 - \sqrt{\cos x}).$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, point $K$ is the middle of the base $AB$, point $M$ is the middle of the base $CD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if it is known that $DK$ is the bisector of the angle $D$ of the trapezoid, $BM$ is the bisector of the angle $B$ of the trapezoid, the lowest angles at the lower base of the trapezoid are $60^\circ$, and its perimeter is 30.

3. Solve inequality
$$5x + \sqrt{6x^2 + x^3 - x^4} \log_2 x > (x^2 - x) \log_2 x + 5 + 5\sqrt{6 + x - x^2}.$$

4. The points $K$ and $M$ are the corresponding edges $AB$ and $AC$ of the triangular paramid $ABCD$ whose base area $ABC$ is $p$. Find the area of ​​face $BDC$ if section $MDK$ has area $q$, and the base of the altitude of the paramid falls at the intersection point of the medians of triangle $ABC$.

5. Find all pairs of numbers $x, y$ satisfying the conditions
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{2x^2y^2 - x^4y^4} = y^6 + x^2(1 - x), \\ \sqrt{1 + (x + y)^2} + x(2y^3 + x^2) \leq 0. \end{cases}$$

*) See «Kvant», 1973, No. 4.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$1 + \cos 2x + \cos^2 x \log \sqrt{3} tg^2 x + 3 \sin x = 2 \sin x \log_{1/3} tg^3 x.$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, the points $K$ and $M$ are the corresponding midpoints of the bases $AB$ and $CD$. It is known that $AM \perp DK$ and $CK \perp BM$, and the angle $CKD$ is equal to $60^\circ$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if its height is 1.

3. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{2 - 5x - 3x^2} + 2x > 2x \times 3^x \sqrt{2 - 5x - 3x^2} + 4x^2 \cdot 3^x.$$

4. In the triangular paramid $ABCD$, through the edge $AD$, equal to $a$, and the point $E$—the middle of the edge $BC$—a section is drawn that forms, with the grams $ACD$ and $ABD$, the corresponding angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Find the volume of the paramid if the cross-sectional area $ADE$ is equal to $S$.

5. Find all pairs of numbers $x, y$ satisfying the conditions
$$\begin{cases} 4y^2 - 2x^2 = \sqrt{2(x + 2y)^2 - (x + 2y)^4}, \\ x^4 + 2 \leq 4y(x^2 - 1). \end{cases}$$

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

Option 3

1. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sin y \cos x + \sin x = 0, \\ 2 \cos^2 y + \sin y \sin x = \cos 2y \cos x. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_2 x - \frac{4}{25} \left( \frac{x^2 - 14x + 51}{50} \right) \leq 0.$$

3. In a right triangle $ACB$, from the vertex of the right angle $C$ the bisector $CL = a$ and the median $CM = b$ are drawn. Find the area of ​​triangle $ACB$.

4. Points $A, B, C, D, E, F$ lie on the surface of a ball of radius $\sqrt{2}$.
