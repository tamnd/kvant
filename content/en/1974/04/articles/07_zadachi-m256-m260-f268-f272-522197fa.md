M256. A polygon is described around a circle. The points of contact of its sides with the circle are the service points of the second polygon inscribed in this circle. Prove that the product of the distances from an arbitrary point M of the circle to the sides of one polygon is equal to the product of the distances from this point to the sides of the second.

(Under consideration from point M to a side, the distance to the straight line on which this side lies is trampled.)

A. N. Chernyev

M257. For what natural numbers $n \geq 2$ the inequality
$$x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \ldots + x_n^2 \geq p(x_1 x_2 + x_2 x_3 + \ldots + x_{n-1} x_n)$$
holds for any real numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ if $p = 1$; 6) $p = \frac{4}{3}$; 8) $p = \frac{6}{5}$?

M258. There are three points K, L, N on the bottom plane. It is known about a quadrilateral that it is convex and that the midpoints of some of its three sides lie at given points K, L, N. Find the set of points that can hit:
a) the middle of the quadrilateral;
b) the vertex of this quadrilateral.

A. P. Savin
