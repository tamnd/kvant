Moscow State University
named after M.V. Lomonosov

We have already talked about the faculties of physics and mathematics at Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov *). Below are the materials of the 1973 entrance exams in mathematics and physics at these faculties.

Matematika

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$2(\cos x)^{5/2} - \cos 2x = 1 + \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{3}}(1 - \sqrt{\cos x}).$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, point $K$ is the middle of the base $AB$, point $M$ is the middle of the base $CD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if it is known that $DK$ is the bisector of the angle $D$ of the trapezoid, $BM$ is the bisector of the angle $B$ of the trapezoid, the lowest angles at the lower base of the trapezoid are $60^\circ$, and its perimeter is 30.

3. Solve inequality
$$5x + \sqrt{6x^2 + x^3 - x^4} \log_2 x > (x^2 - x) \log_2 x + 5 + 5\sqrt{6 + x - x^2}.$$

4. The points $K$ and $M$ are the corresponding edges $AB$ and $AC$ of the triangular paramid $ABCD$ whose base area $ABC$ is $p$. Find the area of ​​face $BDC$ if section $MDK$ has area $q$, and the base of the altitude of the paramid falls at the intersection point of the medians of triangle $ABC$.

5. Find all pairs of numbers $x, y$ satisfying the conditions
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{2x^2y^2 - x^4y^4} = y^6 + x^2(1 - x), \\ \sqrt{1 + (x + y)^2} + x(2y^3 + x^2) \leq 0. \end{cases}$$

*) See «Kvant», 1973, No. 4.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$1 + \cos 2x + \cos^2 x \log \sqrt{3} tg^2 x + 3 \sin x = 2 \sin x \log_{1/3} tg^3 x.$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, the points $K$ and $M$ are the corresponding midpoints of the bases $AB$ and $CD$. It is known that $AM \perp DK$ and $CK \perp BM$, and the angle $CKD$ is equal to $60^\circ$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if its height is 1.

3. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{2 - 5x - 3x^2} + 2x > 2x \times 3^x \sqrt{2 - 5x - 3x^2} + 4x^2 \cdot 3^x.$$

4. In the triangular paramid $ABCD$, through the edge $AD$, equal to $a$, and the point $E$—the middle of the edge $BC$—a section is drawn that forms, with the grams $ACD$ and $ABD$, the corresponding angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Find the volume of the paramid if the cross-sectional area $ADE$ is equal to $S$.

5. Find all pairs of numbers $x, y$ satisfying the conditions
$$\begin{cases} 4y^2 - 2x^2 = \sqrt{2(x + 2y)^2 - (x + 2y)^4}, \\ x^4 + 2 \leq 4y(x^2 - 1). \end{cases}$$

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

Option 3

1. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sin y \cos x + \sin x = 0, \\ 2 \cos^2 y + \sin y \sin x = \cos 2y \cos x. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_2 x - \frac{4}{25} \left( \frac{x^2 - 14x + 51}{50} \right) \leq 0.$$

3. In a right triangle $ACB$, from the vertex of the right angle $C$ the bisector $CL = a$ and the median $CM = b$ are drawn. Find the area of ​​triangle $ACB$.

4. Points $A, B, C, D, E, F$ lie on the surface of a ball of radius $\sqrt{2}$.

Segments AD, BE and CF intersect at one point S, which is located at a distance of 1 from the center of the ball. It is known that the volumes of the SABC and SDEF paramides are in the ratio 1:9, the volumes of the SABF and SDEC paramides are in the ratio 4:9, and the volumes of the SAEC and SDBF paramidas are in the ratio 9:4. 5. On each of the indicated segments, the speeds of the runners are constant and equal for the first runner 10 km/h, 16 km/h and 14 km/h, respectively, and for the second runner 12 km/h, 10 km/h and 16 km/h, respectively. The third runner at points B and C is not alone and at a lower speed only once along the entire route. Is triangle ABC acute or obtuse?

Faculty of Physics

Option 4

1. Solve the equation
2 sin x + cos 2x = 1.

2. Solve the equation
$$\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{3x} - \left(\frac{1}{8}\right)^{x-1} - 128 = 0.$$

3. The sum of the first fifth terms of a geometric progression is 62. It is known that the fifth, eighth and eleventh terms of this progression are the corresponding first, second and tenth terms of an arithmetic progression. Find the first term of a geometric progression.

4. A primo circular cone with vertex S is inscribed in the triangular paramid SPQR so that the circle of the base of the cone is inscribed in the base PQR of the paramid. It is known that $PSR = \pi/2$, $SQR = \pi/4$, $PSQ = 7\pi/12$. Find the ratio of the area of ​​the lateral surface of the cone to the area of ​​the base PQR of the paramid.

5. Given a circle with center O. A diameter AB and a chord CD are drawn through a point inside the circle. From point D a perpendicular DF ​​is dropped onto the chord AC (F is between A and C), and from point F a perpendicular FH is dropped onto AB. Point M is the midpoint of the chord CD. It is known that AM = 2MO, 8AH = 5AB. Find AMO.

Physics

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

Vilet 1

1. A small body slides without initial speed from a smooth inclined plane, the length of which is l = 16 cm, and the angle of inclination is α = 30°. Then it falls onto a smooth horizontal plane located at a distance h = 20 cm from the lower end of the inclined plane. What is the greatest height H from the horizontal plane to which a body rises after an impact on it, if this impact is absolutely elastic?

2. Electrolysis. Farad's laws.

3. Converging and diverging lenses. Lens formula. Constructing an image in lenses.

Ticket 2

1. A small ball of mass m = 1.3 c, having a charge q = 2·10⁻⁵ κ, is suspended on a thread inside a charged ball capacitor, the plates of which are horizontal. The period of small oscillations of this ball is T₁ = 1 s. If we place the signs of the charges of the capacitor plates in reverse, maintaining the external charges, then the period of oscillation of the ball will become T₂ = 1.5 s. Find the voltage E of the electric field inside the capacitor.

2. Laws of Boyle - Mariotte, Gay-Lussk, Charles. Charts of these laws. Absolute temperature scale. Circled Boyle-Mariotte-Gay-Lussk law.

3. Luminous flux, luminous intensity, illumination.

Ticket 3

1. A small lamp is suspended above the table, located at the top of a cone-shaped lampshade, with an angle between the axis and the generatrix α = 30°. A plane-parallel glass plate with a refractive index n = 1.5 was placed on the path of the rays parallel to the table surface. In this case, the diameter of the light spot on the table decreased by d = 2 cm. What is the thickness h of the plate?

2. Examples of oscillatory movements. Harmonic vibrations. Period, frequency and amplitude of oscillations. Oscillation period of the mathematical matrix (without output).

3. Magnetic induction flux. Electromagnetic induction. Electromotive force of induction. Lenz's law. The phenomenon of self-propulsion.

Faculty of Physics

Below are some problems from the entrance exam options for the Faculty of Physics.

1. Two small bodies, the masses of which are in the ratio 1:2, simultaneously begin to slide without friction along the inner surface of a hemisphere of radius $R$ (Fig. 1). The impact of bodies is completely inelastic. At what angle $\alpha$ from the vertical does the body deviate after the impact?

2. At the edges of a moving platform, the length of which is $l = 10$ m, the weight is $P = 500$ kg, there are two people: the weight of the first is $P_1 = 60$ kg, and the second is $P_2 = 40$ kg. At moment $t = 0$ they begin to run along the platform towards each other, and the speed of the first is twice the speed of the second. How far $x$ will the platform roll when the first person reaches the end? Neglect the friction of the platform on the rails.

3. A piece of ice floats in a vessel with water, in which a shotgun pork has been stuck. The mass of ice is $M$, the mass of the crusher is $m$, the temperature of the water in the vessel is $0^\circ$ C. What is the least amount of heat that needs to be expended for the crusher to begin to sink? Density of ice $\rho_1$, latent heat of fusion of ice $\lambda$, density of lead $\rho_2$.

4. The cycle consists of two isochors and two isobars (Fig. 2). The gas temperatures at points 1 and 3 are equal to the corresponding $T_1$ and $T_3$. Determine the work done by one gram-molecule of gas per cycle if it is known that points 2 and 4 lie on the same isotherm.

5. When a light bulb is connected to a network with a voltage of $U = 200$v, it consumes power $W_1 = 40$vm and burns brightly, and the temperature of the bulb is $t_1 = 3000^\circ$C. When connected to a network with $U = 100$vm, the light bulb consumes power $W_2 = 25$vm and is still lit, since the temperature of the bulb is $t_2 = 1000^\circ$C. Find the resistance values of the bulb light bulbs $R_0$ at a temperature $t = 0^\circ$ C.

6. Determine the concentration of electrons in the beam of the cathode ray tube of the oscilloscope near the screen. The cross section of the beam is $S = 1$ mm², the current strength is $I = 1,6$ mA. Electrons flow out of the cathode without an initial speed and are accelerated between the cathode and the anomer by an electric field with a potential difference of $U = 28500$ V.

Electron charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ k, mass $m = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ k$^2$.

7. Two rays fall on the hemispherical mirror CAD of radius $R = 30$ cm, parallel to the lines $AB$, which passes through the center $O$ and is perpendicular to CD (Fig. 3).

One ray passes at a distance of $a = 24$ cm and the other at a distance of $a_1 = 1$ cm from the line $AB$. After reflection from the mirror, these rays will intersect $AB$ at some two points. Find the distance $x$ between these points.

8. A glass prism with a refractive angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$ and a glass refractive index $n$ and a thin line with a focal length $f$ are placed on the table. The main optical axis of the lens is perpendicular to the vertical face of the prism, onto which a horizontal parallel beam of light of a determined wavelength is directed. At what distance $x$ from the main optical axis will there be a point at which the rays passing through the lens and the lens will meet?
