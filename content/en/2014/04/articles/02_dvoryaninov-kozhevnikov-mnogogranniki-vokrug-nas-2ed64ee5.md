Polyhedra around us

You can start getting acquainted with the world of polyhedra without waiting for stereometry lessons in high school. Sometimes it is enough to look at some of the creations of architects. Most modern high-rise buildings have the shape of rectangular parallelepipeds. They are sometimes somewhat casually called boxes (in English, the long “rectangular parallelepiped” is also often replaced by the short “box”). This is easily explained - it is easier to build boxes. True, there are unusual buildings among the boxes. Agree, it’s not often that you come across a house with an almost cubic shape, like the city library of Stuttgart (photo 1)!
