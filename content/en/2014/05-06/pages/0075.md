Department of Mathematics

Initially, VZMSH was a mathematical school (and the letter M in the abbreviation meant “mathematical”). Gradually

⟦column⟧

But the school has expanded and turned into a multi-subject one, but the mathematics department remains the most numerous and in demand in the school. Our main task is an in-depth study of the most important issues of the school mathematics course, we expand school knowledge and help prepare for exams. We accept students for all courses from 0 to 5 (which corresponds to grades 6–11 of a general education school) based on the results of the introductory work. After enrollment in school, the student receives benefits under the VZMS program, the vast majority of which are written specifically for students in the mathematics department of VZMS. The student studies the proposed topics, solves problems and completes a test on each topic, which is checked, reviewed by the school teacher and, along with all comments and advice, sent to the student. Each student is assigned the same teacher for all years of study, who can always be asked questions if necessary. The ability to constantly maintain contact between a student and his teacher greatly alleviates the difficulties inevitably associated with distance learning. If you want to study individually, then you need to complete the introductory work, the conditions of which are presented below. Solutions to problems that were successfully solved must be written down in a regular student notebook in a square and sent by a simple parcel (please do not roll the notebook into a tube!) to the school. Entry papers are also accepted by email: priem@math-vzms.org. In this case, the work must be in the form of a doc or PDF file; you can also scan the text and send it by email. On the cover of the notebook (or at the beginning of the work sent by email), indicate: last name, first name, patronymic (in block letters), year of birth, basic education (how many grades of high school will be completed by September of the next school year). Next to the serial number of the task, it is indicated in parentheses for which class students (meaning the class in which you plan to study from September 1) this task is intended. You can, if you wish, additionally solve problems addressed to older classes. Take your time, and if tasks don’t work out, return to them several times. You may not be able to solve all the problems in your class; please send solutions to those that you managed to solve. Don’t forget to justify your decisions; a “bare” answer to a problem is not considered a solution. The deadline for submitting the entry work is June 1, 2015. “Collective student” groups (for all courses) are accepted without introductory work. You can view all the details related to the admission procedure, payment and training process on the department’s website or by asking questions by email or phone. Website of the mathematics department: www.math-vzms.org Electronic mailbox of the mathematics department: priem@math-vzms.org
