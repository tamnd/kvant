Part-time department (training in elective groups) Tel./fax: (498) 744-63-51, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Optional groups can be organized in any general education institution by two or three teachers - physics, mathematics and computer science; in some cases, training in only one subject is allowed. The leaders of the elective accept students who have successfully completed the introductory task of the ZFTS. A group (at least 7 people) is admitted to ZFTS upon the application of the director, written on the letterhead of a general education institution (a sample can be viewed in the “part-time department” section of the ZFTS website), which must indicate the surnames, first names, patronymics of the leaders of the optional group and an alphabetical list of students (full name indicating the class of the current academic year and final grades for entrance assignments in selected subjects, address, telephone, fax and e-mail of the school). Notebooks with students' work are checked by teachers of physics, mathematics and computer science and are not sent to ZFTSH. The work of the heads of elective classes can be paid by the educational institution as the management of specialized elective classes by providing the ZFTSH with the relevant information. Leaders working with students will receive educational and methodological materials during the academic year (programs in physics, mathematics and computer science, assignments on program topics, solutions to assignments with brief recommendations for assessing student work); be invited to advanced training courses for physics and mathematics teachers, conducted at MIPT. Students' work is checked and assessed by the leaders of the optional groups, and they send statements with final grades for each task and a final statement for the year to the ZFTSH.

Introductory assignment in physics

1. A train of 15 cars and a locomotive passes over a bridge of length L = 300 m. From the moment the train entered the bridge to the moment it left it, $t_{1} = 50 c$ passed. During this time, the passenger returning to his carriage traveled a distance equal to the length of four carriages. Determine the speed of the train if it is known that the passenger was walking at a speed v = 1 m/s relative to the car. The length of the locomotive is equal to the length of the car. 2. A bus and a motorcycle are moving along a long straight section of highway. At 12:10, they, moving towards each other, approached each other and were at a distance of $L_{1} = 10$ km from each other. At 12:50 the bus and the motorcycle were already moving away from each other and were at a distance of $L_{2} = 30$ km from each other. Determine the clock readings at the moment they meet. The bus and motorcycle were in motion for the entire specified time and did not change direction. 3. On the horizontal bottom of a vessel with vertical walls lies a steel cube with an edge l = 4 cm. So much water was poured into the vessel that the water level coincides with the top edge of the cube. The area of ​​the bottom of the vessel is $S = 25 см^{2}$. The cube begins to be lifted vertically upward at a speed v = 1 mm/s. How long after the start of movement will the cube be out of the water?
