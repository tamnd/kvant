Questions

1. Here is a piece of Pushkin's prose: Marya Gavrilovna was brought up on French novels and, consequently, was in love. The subject she chose was a poor army ensign who was on leave in his village. It goes without saying that the young man was burning with equal passion and that the parents of his beloved, noticing their mutual inclination, forbade their daughter to think about him, and he was received worse than a retired assessor. a) Try to analyze the lexical, morphological and syntactic features of the text from the perspective of a native speaker of modern Russian. Is everything clear to today’s young reader? b) Select 5 epithets that, from your point of view, accurately describe the language and style of Pushkin the prose writer. 2. Remember the “talking names” of the heroes Fonvizin, Griboyedov, Gogol, Chekhov, Bulgakov... Which ones did you especially like and remember and why? 3. Continue the synonymous series: “run away” - “let it go” - ... Use both words and phraseological units. In parentheses, indicate to which style of speech the proposed lexical units belong. 4. The word (ON) MEETING can refer to different parts of speech. Come up with 3 sentences to prove and illustrate this statement.

Department of History

The History Department announces enrollment for a distance learning course. Studying at the department will allow everyone, including residents of the most remote cities and villages, to broaden their horizons and prepare for entering a university. Those who successfully complete the course receive a VZMS diploma. You can continue your education at our school by taking special courses. Why do you need to study history? First of all, it's just interesting. It is interesting to know how people once lived, what they dressed in, what they ate, what they thought, what they read, how they got married, what they fought for and “what they got into.” Secondly, it's useful. Only by knowing the past can one understand the present and predict the future. We will help you figure it out. Experienced teachers write books especially for you. You will be one of the first to know the latest news from the world of history! Historical education in an envelope is a modern form of additional education. According to our books, you will perform special tasks and tell us what you have unearthed. We will tell you how to proceed further. After all, in essence, the work of a historian consists of these excavations. The historian-archaeologist, digging the earth and sand, finds grains of bygone times; the historian-archivist digs through a pile of papers and takes out from the archives and even from private correspondence everything that can allow him to understand the image of time; a historian and theorist reads archaeological literature like a fascinating novel

Exercise

1. Guess who it is i Veteran of the Russian revolutionary movement. i Held senior government positions. i Flew twice in a bomber across the front line. i He fell into disgrace twice in the post-war period. i Was expelled from the Communist Party, but again received a party card at the very end of his long life. i Received the nickname “Mr. No” in the West. i Brother is a famous composer. The wife spent three years in exile. i Cities, villages, mountains, factories, and institutes were named after him. 2. Describe, in no more than 7 sentences, a historical portrait of the last First Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee.

For high school students preparing to take the Unified State Exam in social studies, the history department announces enrollment in the “Social Studies” course. Social science is a set of social disciplines that study all aspects of human activity. Some teachers call it a vinaigrette. Quite an apt comparison. Just as a vinaigrette is a composite dish, social studies consists of different disciplines. Mixing and complementing each other, they create a complete picture of society. The course program lasts one year. The training is correspondence in nature and aims to give school graduates - both large cities and small villages - deep knowledge in social disciplines, to prepare them for successfully passing the Unified State Exam.
