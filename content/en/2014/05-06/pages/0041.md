Competition named after A.P. Savin

"Math 6–8"

We continue our next competition on solving mathematical problems for students in grades 6–8. Send solutions to the problems within a month after receiving this issue of the journal to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant» or by email: savin.contest@gmail.com (marked “Contest “Mathematics 6–8”). Be sure to include your name, class, and home address. As before, we welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by mathematical clubs. We ask circle leaders to provide an email address or contact phone number. According to tradition, the winning clubs of the correspondence competition are invited to the final in-person tournament.

6. All natural numbers from 1 to 2015 are written on the board - some of them in red marker, the rest in blue. The largest blue number is equal to the number of blue numbers, the smallest red number is half the number of red numbers. How many red numbers are written on the board? S. Dvoryaninov

7. A chip was placed at some 15 points of the circle. The chips are numbered 1, 2,..., 15 sequentially in a clockwise direction. In one operation, you are allowed to move one of the tokens to any point on the circle where there is no token at that moment. In what is the least number of operations you can get the chips to be in reverse order? Folklore

9. A 6 ¥ 10 ¥ 15 brick is made from cubes 1 ¥ 1 ¥ 1. The brick is pierced with a needle along its large diagonal. How many cubes did the needle pierce? Folklore

10. A circle inscribed in triangle ABC touches sides AB and AC at points M and N. A straight line $l_{A}$ is drawn through the midpoint of side BC, perpendicular to MN. Lines $l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$ are defined similarly. Prove that lines $l_{A}, l_{B}$ and $l_{C}$ intersect at one point. R. Gordin

How Busenka learned to multiply by eleven

D.KOKHAS, K.KOKHAS

BUSENKA WALKED THROUGH THE FOREST AND UNEXPECTEDLY FELT something unusual. Or rather, I didn’t feel it, but smelled it. The smell was slightly sour and a little scary, with light floral undertones. Busenka looked around suspiciously, sniffed again and with a loud squeal rushed to the nearest birch tree. There was a rustling sound behind the birch tree, then a rumbling sound, and from behind the trunk a cute, but very toothy head of some monster looked out. Monstropithecus! - Oh, Gargoyles, it's you. “And I was already scared,” Busenka sighed with relief.
