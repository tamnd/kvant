PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 5-6–2014” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2356” or “F2363”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics can also be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively. Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2357, M2358, M2360–M2364 were offered at the final stage of the XL All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.

Problem M2356–M2365, F2363–F2372

M2356. A virus was placed in a cell on a checkered plane. The virus lives for one minute, after a minute it disappears, but leaves behind offspring - in each of the four cells adjacent to its cell, a new virus appears. figure shows the stages of virus reproduction (number and location) at time points t = 0, 1, 2, 3 min. Calculate the number of viruses in the initial field at t = 10 min. V. Rastorguev

M2357. Let us call a natural number good if among its divisors there are exactly two prime numbers. Can 18 consecutive natural numbers be good? O. Podlipsky

M2358. a) Trapezoid ABCD with bases AB and CD is inscribed in a circle Ω. Circle $\omega$ passes through points C, D and intersects segments CA, CB at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$, respectively. Points $A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ are symmetrical to points $A_{1}$ and $B_{1}$ relative to the midpoints of segments CA and CB, respectively. Prove that points $A, B, A_{2}$ and $B_{2}$ lie on the same circle. b) Sphere $\omega$ passes through the vertex S of the pyramid

SABC and intersects edges SA, SB and SC secondarily at points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$, respectively. The sphere Ω circumscribed about the pyramid SABC intersects $\omega$ along a circle lying in a plane parallel to the plane ABC. Points $A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ are symmetrical to points $A_{1}, B_{1}$ and $C_{1}$ with respect to the midpoints of the edges SA, SB and SC, respectively. Prove that points $A, B, C, A_{2}, B_{2}$ and $C_{2}$ lie on the same sphere. I. Bogdanov

M2359. A regular 1001-gon is drawn on the board, the vertices of which are numbered 1, 2, ......, 1001. The same regular 1001-gon is cut out of cardboard. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, ..., 1001 (each number once) at the vertices of a cardboard 1001-gon so that for any overlap of 1001-gons, equal numbers appear at some vertex (the cardboard polygon can be turned over)? V. Senderov

M2360. In the Republic of Mathematicians, they chose the number $\alpha > 2$ and issued coins in denominations of 1 ruble, as well as in $\alpha^{k}$ rubles for each natural number k. Moreover, $\alpha$ was chosen so that the denominations of all coins, except the smallest one, are irrational. Could it turn out that any amount of natural rubles can be collected with these coins, using coins of each denomination no more than 6 times? I. Bogdanov, S. Berlov
