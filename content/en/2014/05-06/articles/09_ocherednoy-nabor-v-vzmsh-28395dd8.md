Another recruitment at VZMSH

The All-Russian Correspondence Multi-Subject School (VZMSH), part of the structure of the Moscow Lyceum “Second School” and operating at the M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University, is enrolling students for the fifty-first time. This school was created on the initiative of academician I.M. Gelfand in 1964. For many years, I.M. Gelfand headed the Scientific Council of the school. VZMSH is a state institution of additional education, available to everyone, and not only for schoolchildren, to expand their knowledge in one or more of the following fields of science: mathematics, biology, philology, physics, economics, history (listed in chronological order of opening of departments). Now VZMSH, together with other scientific and pedagogical institutions, is conducting research on the development of new interactive technologies in education and the translation of part of its educational and methodological complexes into the language of modern telecommunications. During the existence of VZMSH, several hundred thousand schoolchildren and thousands of circles - groups “Collective student of VZMSH” received certificates of completion. Education at school is CORRESPONDENT, i.e. starting from September–October 2015, all applicants will systematically receive materials specially designed for distance learning, containing a presentation of theoretical issues and methods of reasoning, various tasks for independent work, sample solutions to problems, business games, tests and practical tasks. Students' test papers will be carefully checked and reviewed by VZMS teachers - students, graduate students, teachers and researchers of Moscow State University, as well as other universities and institutions where the school has branches. Many of the teachers themselves graduated from VZMSH at one time and therefore understand especially well how important it is to indicate, in addition to specific shortcomings, ways to eliminate existing gaps in knowledge, recommend additional literature, scold for inattention and praise for noticeable (and sometimes even the smallest) progress and hard work. Those admitted to VZMSH will be able to learn about fascinating things that are often left behind the pages of a school textbook, get acquainted with interesting non-standard problems and try their hand at solving them. For many, it will be a revelation that problems exist not only in mathematics, physics and chemistry, but also in biology, philology, economics and other sciences. Solving problems will help clarify and make interesting many sections that seemed unclear and boring. One of the features of the VZMS curriculum and manuals is that they were created by talented scientists and experienced outstanding teachers working at the forefront of science. To successfully study at a correspondence school, you will have to learn to work independently and productively with a book, to express your thoughts competently, clearly, briefly and clearly, and, as you know, not everyone can do this. Perhaps our correspondence school will help you choose a profession and find your place in the world around you. All those who complete the VZMSh program receive diplomas. Although they do not provide formal advantages, the admissions committees of many universities take into account that the holders of these certificates have worked selflessly for a long time to acquire knowledge and have learned to work independently creatively, which means that they will make good students and, in the future, competent, thoughtful, widely educated specialists. You will be able to receive our assignments both by regular mail and by e-mail, and also take part in testing new interactive training programs. To enter the VZMSH, you must successfully complete the entrance test. The selection committee is interested, first of all, in your ability to reason, your attempts (even if not entirely successful at first) to think independently and draw conclusions.
