LV International Mathematical Olympiad

From July 3 to July 13, 2014, the LV International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) was held in South Africa. The venue for the Olympics was practically the southernmost point of the African continent - the city of Cape Town. Wonderful excursions to Table Mountain, the Cape of Good Hope, and the botanical garden allowed participants to see the unique beauty of nature and get acquainted with the diverse local flora and fauna. MMO is one of the largest intellectual competitions for schoolchildren. In 2014, 560 winners of national Olympiads from 101 countries took part in the Olympiad. This year, the Russian national team included school graduates Daniil Klyuev from St. Petersburg (FML 239), Muscovites Andrei Volgin (gymnasium 1543) and Dmitry Krekov (Education Center “Fifty-Seventh School”), Maxim Didin from Pereslavl-Zalessky (gymnasium), Alexey Volostnov and Nikita Chernega from Kazan (both from gymnasium 122 named after Zh.A.
