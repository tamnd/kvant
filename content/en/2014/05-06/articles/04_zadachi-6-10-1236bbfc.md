6. All natural numbers from 1 to 2015 are written on the board - some of them in red marker, the rest in blue. The largest blue number is equal to the number of blue numbers, the smallest red number is half the number of red numbers. How many red numbers are written on the board? S. Dvoryaninov

7. A chip was placed at some 15 points of the circle. The chips are numbered 1, 2,..., 15 sequentially in a clockwise direction. In one operation, you are allowed to move one of the tokens to any point on the circle where there is no token at that moment. BUSENKA WALKED THROUGH THE FOREST AND UNEXPECTEDLY FELT something unusual. Or rather, I didn’t feel it, but smelled it. The smell was slightly sour and a little scary, with light floral undertones. Busenka looked around suspiciously, sniffed again and with a loud squeal rushed to the nearest birch tree. There was a rustling sound behind the birch tree, then a rumbling sound, and from behind the trunk a cute, but very toothy head of some monster looked out. Monstropithecus! - Oh, Gargoyles, it's you. “And I was already scared,” Busenka sighed with relief.
