2.

Harry Potter has 100 marbles in his box - red, white and green. Three of them are magical, they change color from time to time (to any of these three). One day, Harry Potter looked into the box and saw that there were more red balls than white ones, and more white balls than green ones. Looking in a minute later, he saw that everything had become the other way around: there were more greens than whites, and more whites than reds. How many white balls did he see when he looked into the box for the first time? D. Maksimov with arrows. The first hand rotates at a speed of one revolution per hour, the second makes 2 revolutions per hour,..., the 150th hand makes 150 revolutions per hour. The clock was started from a position where all the hands were pointing straight up. When two or more hands meet during the operation of a watch, the hands immediately fall off. How long after starting will the hand rotating at 74 revolutions per hour fall off? K.Kokhas

3.

Gentlemen always tell the truth to those they know and lie to strangers. Once 50 gentlemen gathered, and each said to each of the others one of the phrases: I have an even number of acquaintances in this company or I have an odd number of acquaintances in this company. Could it be that the first phrase was spoken exactly 2013 times? A. Solynin

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8. These problems were proposed at the regional round of the LXXX St. Petersburg Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.

a) Cut a checkered rectangle measuring 9 ¥ 10 squares into several squares so that among them there are exactly two squares with an odd side. The cuts should go along the sides of the cells. b) A checkered rectangle measuring 19 ¥ 20 cells is cut into several squares (all cuts go along the sides of the cells). What is the smallest number of squares with an odd side that can be among them? K. Sukhov
