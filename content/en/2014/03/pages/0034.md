inertia forces

Inertia again? After all, quite recently - in the last issue of Kaleidoscope - it was already discussed. That’s right, these issues are thematically connected. One can say that one continues the other, but does not replace it or repeat it. Please note that only one word is added to the title - “strength”. Today's stories are intended to discuss how this addition expands the conversation about inertia and takes it beyond the framework of the concept formulated last time. What are these mysterious forces that haunt both theorists and practitioners? Is it possible to manage them, to put them at our service, if for decades it has not been possible to come to a common conceptual denominator? Don’t worry: a person has mastered how to curb the forces of inertia and learn how to secure machines and mechanisms where they could show their “violent temper.” And I was able to find application for them in the most modern micromechanical devices - from robotics to virtual reality systems. And he managed to explain many natural phenomena with their help. Moreover, a meticulous analysis of questions that seemed clear to some and mysterious to others led to an understanding of the very foundations of the structure of the world and to the emergence of new fundamental theories describing this world. This is how we – “by inertia” – were carried far! Let us pay tribute to this tricky but wonderful concept, as well as to the great scientist who long ago started a story related to this concept - Galileo Galilei. Especially in the year of his 450th anniversary.
