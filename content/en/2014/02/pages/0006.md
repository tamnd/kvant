On the one hand, quantum mechanics has already been born, the Schrödinger equation is known. This equation allows us to describe the atom, i.e. an electron jumping in its orbits around the nucleus. But no one knows whether quantum mechanics is suitable for studying the nucleus or not. In addition, there is the Dirac equation (this is a relativistic generalization of the Schrödinger equation). It has a non-trivial property: it has two solutions, one solution with positive energy, the other solution with negative energy.
