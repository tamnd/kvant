New adventures of Pinocchio

Geometric fairy tale

S. DVORYANINOV

WHEN BURATINO WAS VERY LITTLE, dad Carlo took him for walks. But Pinocchio grew up and began going to elementary school. And old Carlo just got a lot of orders for the manufacture of wooden furniture, his work increased, and his free time, accordingly, decreased. That’s when Pinocchio began to ask to be let go for a walk alone. – Papa Carlo, our town is surrounded on all four sides by such beautiful forests, groves and parks that look like forests! It's so wonderful there! Let me go there! And in order not to get lost, I will take a compass with me! “Well,” his father answered, “you’re not little, but I won’t give you a compass, but a GPS navigator.” Tomorrow, for starters, visit the Square Forest, and I will set up the navigator so that it shows you the direction perpendicular to the sides of this forest. And so Pinocchio went to the Square Forest after school. - Well, how was the walk? – Dad Carlo asked him. Did you like it? - Yes, of course, only I walked the same path from edge to edge twice, back and forth (Fig.1,a). Left the starting point S and returned to it. Can I choose a more interesting route? - Can. Go to the Quadrangle Forest tomorrow. And the navigator will show you directions along the diagonals of this quadrangle. Buratino came out of the city gates, immediately found himself at the edge of the forest and turned on the navigator. I reached one side of the quadrangle, then the other, and so on, all the time moving in parallel

And surprisingly, he found himself again at the beginning of his journey - at the starting point S! “Papa Carlo,” Pinocchio said in the evening, “I was lucky today: I started my journey at a wonderful point, which coincided with the end point of my journey!” – What’s unusual here? If you start at any point on the side and move parallel to the diagonals of the quadrilateral, you will always return to the starting point. – Okay, I’ll check this statement tomorrow, I’ll start from a different point. Let's see if I go back to the beginning. – Even if you return, you will only confirm my words, but you will not prove the statement. And in general, no matter how many examples there are to confirm the statement, they do not prove anything. Then for several more days Pinocchio walked through the Quadrangular Forest, starting his journey at different points, but always returning to the starting point.

A week later, Pinocchio went to the Triangular Forest. The name is clear: the forest had the shape of a triangle. – Papa Carlo, set the navigator so that I return to the starting point of my journey again. - asked Pinocchio. “I’m used to such travels, and the song says: but it’s so nice to return under the roof of your home!” – Okay, tomorrow the navigator will take you parallel to the sides of the Triangular Forest. Again Buratino came to the edge of the forest and hit the road. After two turns, he again found himself at the city wall, but not where he began his journey (Fig.1,c). “The navigator must have gone astray,” Buratino suggested. – It happens that technology fails. Well, I'll check and walk through the forest one more time. His new route consisted of three new segments parallel to the sides of the triangle. But then, at the end of the journey, his finishing point coincided with the starting point! - No, the navigator is working.
