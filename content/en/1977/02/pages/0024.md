Tasks

M426 — M430; F438 — F442

M426. A table of $n \times n$ cells is filled with numbers from 1 to $n$ as shown in figure 1. For each $n$ it is possible to select $n$ cells in it so that no two cells belong to the same row or column and so that all the numbers in the selected cells are different?

A. Nenatsev

M427. a) Prove that there exists an odd number $n$ such that for any even $k$ none of the numbers in the infinite sequence
$$k^k + 1, k^{k^k} + 1, k^{k^{k^k}} + 1, \dots$$
is not divisible by $n$.

b) Prove that for every natural number $n$ there is a natural number $k$ such that each of the terms of the infinite sequence
$$k + 1, k^k + 1, k^{k^k} + 1, k^{k^{k^k}} + 1, \dots$$
is divisible by $n$.

S. Lavrenchenko, 9th grade

M428. $(m - 1)n + 1$ people are participating in the Olympiad. Prove that among them there are either $m$ participants who do not know each other in pairs, or there is one participant who is familiar with at least $n$ Olympiad participants. Will the statement of the problem remain true if the number of participants in the Olympiad is reduced by one?

E. Turkovich

M429. a) How many solutions does the equation have?
$$|x| - 1977 \{x\} = 1978?$$
(Here $|x|$ is the integer part of $x$, and $\{x\} = x - |x|$.)

b) Prove that for any $p \neq 0$ and $q$ the equation
$$|x| + p\{x\} = q$$
has a $||p||$ or $||p|| + 1$ solution.

Zh. Satarov

M430. a) Prove that any convex plane figure of area $S$ can be placed in the line 2S.
