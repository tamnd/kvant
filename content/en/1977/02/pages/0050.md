From the cones being congested, it follows that their bases touch the edge ZB at the same point F, and therefore [SF] will be a common generator. It is clear that [ZB] $\perp$ [SF]. Therefore, the triangle ZFS is right triangle. Triangle SOE is also a right triangle, since SO is the altitude of the cone. Let's put $\widehat{ZBS} = \varphi$; then $\widehat{ZSF} = \varphi$. We have: $|OE| = R$; $|SO| = h$; $\cos \varphi = \frac{|FS|}{|ZS|}$ (from $\triangle ZFS$). But $|FS| = |ES|$ as generators, then $\cos \varphi = \frac{|ES|}{|ZS|} = \text{ctg} \widehat{SEZ}$. From $\triangle SOE$: $\text{ctg} \widehat{SEZ} = \text{ctg} \widehat{SEO} = \frac{R}{h}$. Thus, $\cos \varphi = \frac{R}{h}$; $\varphi = \arccos \frac{R}{h}$.

Let us also note. If we put $\widehat{ZES} = \beta$, then from the solution of this problem we obtain the following:

$$\cos \varphi = \text{ctg} \beta.$$ (*)
