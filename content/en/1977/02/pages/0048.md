III. KEY SKILLS

The examinee must be able to:
1. Perform arithmetic operations on numbers; round numbers to a given precision. Perform actions on approximate values ​​using practical techniques; use tables.
2. Carry out identical transformations of polynomials, fractions containing variables, expressions containing square roots, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions; be able to oblige on which set the exact equality of expressions is constant.
3. Build graphs of functions specified in the program; explore functions using arbitrary; solve problems of finding extreme values; solve problems on calculating the areas of curvilinear trapezoids using periforms.
4. Solve equations and inequalities of the first and second degree and equations and inequalities applicable to them; solve systems of equations and inequalities of the first and second degree and those applicable to them.
5. Solve problems on composing equations and systems of equations, solve combinatorial problems.
6. Draw geometric figures in a drawing and make the simplest constructions on a plane, construct sections of polyhedra and figures of rotation and their simplest combinations.
7. Perform operations on vectors (addition, subtraction, multiplication by a number, scalar multiplication) and use the properties of these operations.
8. Use geometric representations when solving algebraic problems and problems from the beginnings of analysis; use the methods of algebra and the beginnings of analysis when solving geometric problems.
