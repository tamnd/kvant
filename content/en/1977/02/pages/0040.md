⟦folio 38⟧

Quests

1. a) Two eccentrics build a broken line on an infinite sheet of paper in a square, adding one edge of length 1 in turn from either side, and it is forbidden to go through the same segment twice. The weirdo who can't make a move loses. Prove that the first weirdo can't lose, and the second weirdo can't lose.

6) The two eccentrics continue to play. Now they take turns rejecting the volleyball net $n \times n$ cells, cutting one nitke each time. The weirdo whose cut breaks the net into two pieces loses. Who wins?

2. Some numbers when divided by 1976 and 1977 give a remainder of 76. What remainder will this number give when divided by 39?

3. The centaur chess piece moves like a knight, but cannot move two squares up and one to the right, or two squares down and one to the right. Can a centaur go around the entire chessboard, hitting each square only once, and return to the original square?

4. On the very left field of the checkered stripe $1 \times 1977$ there are three buttons. Sasha and Lucy play the following game: each of them can move any button (but only one move) to the right to any number of fields. The one who has nowhere to go loses. Prove that Lucy, starting, can ensure victory for herself.

5. There is a square section of the left on the side of 1 km. The forest consists of trees with a diameter of 50 cm. Tanya found out that it is impossible to pass through this forest in any straight line from one side to the opposite. Prove that there are at least two thousand trees in the forest.
