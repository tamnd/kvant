Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

In this issue we publish samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and problems for an oral exam in physics, proposed at Moscow State University at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics and the Faculty of Physics in 1976.

Mathematics

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

1. Solve the equation
$$\log_{\sin(-x)} \left( \sin \frac{x}{2} + \sin \frac{3x}{2} \right) = 1.$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\frac{7}{9^x} - 2 \geq \frac{2}{3^x} - 1.$$

3. In the triangle $ABC$ ($\widehat{C} = 90^\circ$; $\widehat{B} = 30^\circ$, $|CA| = 1$) the median $CD$ is drawn. In addition, a straight line is drawn from point $D$ at an angle $15^\circ$ to the hypotenuse, intersecting the segment $BC$ at point $F$. Find the plane of triangle $CDF$. Indicate its approximation value in the form of a decimal fraction with an accuracy of 0.01.

4. Three balls, among which there are two identical ones, touch the plane $P$ and, in addition, touch each other in pairs. The vertex of a right circular cone belongs to the plane $P$, and the axis of the cone is perpendicular to this plane. All three balls lie in a cone, and each of them touches some generatrix of the cone. Find the cosine of the angle between the generatrix of the cone and the plane $P$, if it is known that in a triangle with vertices at the points of contact of the balls with the plane $P$, the vertex of one of the angles is equal to $150^\circ$.

5. The numbers $r$, $s$, $t$ are such that $r < s < t$. In addition, it is known that after substituting each of the three numbers $r$, $s$, $t$ together with $y$ into the equality
$$x^2 - (9 - y)x + y^2 - 9y_r + 15 = 0$$
at least one of the other two numbers will be contained among the roots of the resulting quadratic equation. Prove that $-1 < r < 1$.

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. Given: a parallelogram with sides equal to $\sqrt{19}$ and $\sqrt{2}/6$ and an angle $45^\circ$ between them and a square with sides equal to $3\sqrt{2}/5$. Determine which is greater: the area of ​​a parallelogram or the area of ​​a square.

2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 7^y \cdot \log_5 x = -2, \\ 4 \cdot 7^y + \log_5 x = 2. \end{cases}$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2 + \text{ctg} x - \sin^2 x} - \sqrt{\frac{4}{17} - \sin^2 x} = \sqrt{\frac{30}{17} + \text{ctg} x}.$$

4. On a given plane there are two intersecting circles. The first has a center at $N_1$ and a radius equal to $5\sqrt{2}$; the second at the point $N_2$ and the radius equal to 8. The segment $N_1N_2$ intersects both circles, and the value of the angle $FN_2N_1$, where $F$ is one of the points of intersection of the circles, is equal to $45^\circ$. The vertex $L$ of the right triangle $KLM$ is the point of intersection of the first circle and the segment $N_1N_2$, and the side $KM$ is the chord of the second circle perpendicular to the line passing through the points $N_1$ and $N_2$. Find the sides of triangle $KLM$ if it is known that $|KL| > 8$.

5. Find all positive numbers $a$ for which there exists an infinite number of numbers $x$ and $y$ that simultaneously satisfy the following conditions:
$$18x^2 + \frac{3}{2}(1 - a)(x^3 + 9x) - \frac{1}{8}a(x^2 + 9)^2 \leq 0,$$
$$\frac{6x}{x^2 + 9} = \frac{1}{9y} + \frac{ay}{3} + \frac{a}{6},$$
$$y > 0.$$

Faculty of Physics

1. Solve the equation
$$1 - 2\sqrt{2}\cos^3 3x + \cos 6x = 0.$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_{\sqrt{3}}(4 - x) < 4 + 2\log_3(x + 3).$$

3. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \frac{x - 2y}{2} + \frac{x - 2y}{4} = 20, \\ \frac{x}{2} + 4 - \frac{y}{2} = 10. \end{cases}$$

4. In a triangle $KLM$, all sides of which are distinct, the bissertis of angle $KLM$ intersects side $KM$ at point $N$. Through the point $N$ a straight line is drawn intersecting the side $LM$ at the point $A$ such that $|MN| = |AM|$.

5. All plane angles of the trihedral angle $SPQR$ ($S$ is the vertex) are right angles. Physics

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics and Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. Two bodies with masses $m_1=100$ e and $m_2=600$ e are connected to each other using a weightless inextricable thread and a system of blocks (Fig. 1). One end of the thread is fixedly fixed. A body of mass $m_1$ slides along an inclined plane making an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the horizon. Determine the acceleration $a_2$ of a body with mass $m_2$. Neglect the tension and mass of the blocks.

2. Balls of equal mass are attached to the ends of a wire of length $l=0,2$ m, according to the middle at right angles. The wire was hung at an angle on the nail $A$ (Fig. 2), and then deflected so that one leg of the wire became horizontal and the other vertical, and was released without a push. Find the model of the speed of the balls at the moment of passing the equilibrium position. The wire is considered rigid and weightless. Neglect training.

3. A thread wound on a spool is tied to a nail driven into the wall. The coil hangs touching the wall, as shown in figure 3, and the thread makes an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the wall. The radius of the coil axis is $r=1$ cm, the radius of its necks is $R=10$ cm. Find the minimum value of the friction coefficient $\mu$ between the wall and the coil.

4. On the cylinder of the navigator, the end of which is fixed at the top point of the inclined plane. The cylinder lies on an inclined plane as shown in figure 4, with the navigator having a horizontal direction. Cylinder mass $m=10$ kts. Find a model of the force $\vec{F}$ of the pressure of the cylinder on the plane.

5. In the middle of the cylinder, hermetically closed at both ends and fixed at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon, there is a piston of mass $m=1$ kts (Fig. 5). Piston area $S=10$ cm². The air pressure under and above the piston is the same and is equal to $p=1,5 \cdot 10^4$ h/m². With what initial acceleration will the piston move if it is first moved slowly, increasing the volume under it by $n=1,5$ times, and then released? Neglect training.

6. With small loads of the same electric charge, the ball is in equilibrium on two smooth non-conducting planes equally inclined to the horizon, located at the vertices of an equilateral triangle (Fig. 6). The charge of balls 1 and 2 is the same and is twice the charge of ball 3. Find the ratio of the masses of the second and third balls.

Faculty of Physics

1. A rocket launched in a vertical direction from the ground moves with a constant acceleration $2|\vec{g}|$ for $t=50$ sec. Then the move is instantly turned off. Determine the maximum lift height of the rocket. Neglect resistance to acceleration. Assume that $|\vec{g}|=10$ m/sec².

2. A small body of mass $m$ freely falls from a height $h$, falls into the middle of a homogeneous board of mass $M$ and instantly floats to it (Fig. 8). The board lies horizontally on the jump, the coefficient of elasticity of which is $k$. Determine the maximum roll (that is, change in length) of the jump. Neglect the mass of the jump.

3. A small body $M$ begins to slide without an initial velocity from the top point of the inclined plane. the inclined plane is mounted on a horizontal table as shown in figure 9 and has a height of $H$ and an inclination angle of $\alpha$. The coefficient of friction between the body and the plane is $\mu$. At what horizontal distance from the lower end $A$ of the inclined plane will the body fall to the floor if the height of the table is $h$?

4. How much coal will be required to transport a convoy of ships over a distance of $L=100$ km, if the towing cable pulls with a force of $|\vec{F}|=80$ kn, and a tug without a caravan with the same moving power speed is $n=4$ times higher, dropping the same amount of coal per hour? Assume that water resistance is proportional to speed. Efficiency of ship traffic $\eta=10\%$. Tellota of coal removal $q=7000$ kcal/kg.

5. The steel tank contains sloping air at a temperature of $t_1=-23^\circ\text{C}$. There is a safety valve on the tank. The valve opens if the pressure in the tank increases by 2 amps. When the tank was heated to $t_2=27^\circ\text{C}$, 10% of the gas mass came out of it. What was the gas pressure initially in the tank? Neglect the tell distribution of the reservoir.

6. In two volumes there are $N_1=10^{19}$ in one and $N_2=4 \cdot 10^{18}$ molecules of the same gas in the other. The volumes are brought into thermal contact. In the initial state, the internal energy of the first gas was $W=1,9$ J greater than that of the second. In the steady state, the average energy per share of one molecule in the first volume decreased by 25%. What is the internal energy of the gas in the first volume? Gas is considered ideal. Neglect body exchange with surrounding bodies.

7. A cubic glass vessel at a temperature of $t$ is filled with a liquid whose weight is $\mathcal{P}$. When the vessel is heated to a temperature of $t_1$, part of the liquid flows out and its weight becomes equal to $\mathcal{P}_1$. Determine the glass volume distribution coefficient is $\beta$.
