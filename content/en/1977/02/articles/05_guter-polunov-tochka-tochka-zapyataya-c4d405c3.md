Giovanini Antonio Magini (1555-1617) was a professor at the University of Bologna. He used the "decimal point" in the tables of his book "Plok Triangles", published in 1592 in Venice.

The Swiss Joost Burggi (1552–1632) was first a watchmaker and mechanic, helping to build and repair astronomical instruments, and then assisted Kepler in processing astronomical observations and other calculations. In 1592, he compiled a table of sines and wrote a manual on arithmetic. In them he systematically used decimal fractions. In 1616, Kepler wrote about it: “... since fractions will often be obtained, and it is desirable for me to use short numbers, then note that all the numbers after the o sign belong to the fraction as the numerator, but the denominator is not written to it, but it is always a round decimal number with as many zeros as there are digits after the sign... This type of calculation with fractions was invented by Joost Bürggi... Thanks to this, with integers numbers and fractions can be treated as one number in all basic operations.”
