In 1975, the publishing house “Mir” published the book “Polymino” by S. V. Golomb. A lot of space in this book is devoted to explaining which shapes can and cannot be covered with so-called pentamino. (You can see the complete set of pentamino at figure.) There are also problems in this book for which the author did not know the solutions. Our reader from Moscow, eighth-grader Sasha Razborov, managed to solve one of them.

From a complete set of pentamino, construct two rectangles 3x5 and 5x9 at the same time.
At figure you see the solution he obtained. Here are some more problems from this book.
Fold (or prove that it is impossible to do) from a complete set of pentamino two rectangles simultaneously with dimensions: a) 5x8 and 4x5, 6) 4x5 and 4x10.
