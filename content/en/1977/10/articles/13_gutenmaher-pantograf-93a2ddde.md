Pantograph

as needed, it is enough that in the initial position points A, X and Y are on the same straight line. Then in any other position they will also be on the same straight line and the trajectory of point Y will be the beginning of the trajectory of point X. Therefore, if an object with point X is the outline of some figure, then point Y will draw the outline of a similar figure.

Apparently, the reader will not be difficult to answer my friend’s question himself, compare the stability of his pantograph and the pantograph shown in the textbook, and also answer the test questions.

1. Will the trajectory of point Y be similar to the trajectory of point X if
   1°) a=5, b=3, c=10, d=6;

When examining the operation of a pantograph, the question naturally arises: what size figures can it draw?

Having started researching the work of the pantograph, we happily made inquiries and found out that the pantograph was described, apparently, back in 1635 by Christan Scheiner in the book “Pantographia seu Ars delineandi res quaslibet”, and the word “pantograph” in translation is known as “drawing everything”.
