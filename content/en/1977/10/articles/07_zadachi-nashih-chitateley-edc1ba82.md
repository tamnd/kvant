⟦folio 33⟧

Exercise
our readers

1. In wet weather, when moving quickly on a size without “wings”, a large stripe remains on the back of the size from the splashes that have fallen on it. Why?
G. Barvayann (Ochamchira)

2. When playing billiards, sometimes a situation arises that allows you to drive two balls into different beams with one blow. Is it possible to do this with the position of the balls shown at figure?
Yu. Balatsov (Moscow)

3. A thin plano-convex lens, having a focal length of $F$ in air, is built into the wall of the aquarinum, in which a liquid with a refractive index of $n$ is poured. At what distance from the lens will a parallel beam of light incident on the spherical surface of the lens coincide?

A. Shvedov (Zaporozhe)
