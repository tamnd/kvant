XX YEARS

On October 4, 1957, for the first time in human history, an artificial Earth satellite was launched. This date marks the beginning of the human space age. We can be proud that this era began in our country - the first satellite was launched into the Soviet Union.

Academician S.P. Korolev - Chief designer of the first space rocket systems.

Launch of the Soyuz spacecraft.

The world's first artificial Earth satellite.

⟦folio 23⟧

Over the past twenty years, numerous scientific studies have been carried out in space. Suffice it to say that the Soviet Union alone has launched over a thousand different space exploration devices.

Space exploration is taking giant strides. Our country - the pioneers of astronautics - takes priority in milestone events related to space exploration. The Soviet Union was the first to launch a satellite with a person on board - the world's first cosmonaut was a citizen of the Soviet Union, communist Yuri Alekseevich Gagarin. The first person to go into outer space was a citizen of the Soviet Union, communist Alexei Arkhipovich Leonov. The Soviet Union was the first to launch multi-seat aircraft, automatically dock aircraft in orbit, photograph the far side of the Moon, and softly land the station on

The first cosmonaut Yu. A. Gagarin.

The first female cosmonaut V.V. Nikolaeva-Tereshkova.

Cosmonaut A. A. Leonov is the first person to go into space.

The influence of cosmic sciences on the study of the Universe is enormous. Space research has had a stimulating effect on the development of scientific and technical thought, economics and culture. They led to a sharp rise in the level of science and technology in such relevant and advanced areas as aerodynamics, tellophysics, chemical physics, radio engineering and television technology, the technology of automatic control systems, the use of electronic machines in industry, and the problem of miniaturization of technical structures.

Currently, space research is entering a new phase. Twenty years after man entered space, they are increasingly becoming a normal type of scientific research activity, and space is becoming a workplace and laboratories for scientists. After the first, one might say, reconnaissance experiments, the era of systematic research began, and the coming decades will undoubtedly be marked by even greater achievements.
