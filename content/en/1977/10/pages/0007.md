At our institute, Friedman taught a course in mechanics. And in our student library there was a printed course of lectures given by Friedm at the Naval Academy. It was called “Experience in the hydromechanics of skimmed fluid.” We didn’t yet understand what this meant. And only later did we learn that it was the first to solve the problem of the movement of a liquid or gas at very high speeds, when the liquid or gas fundamentally cannot be considered ideal and their skimability must be taken into account. Now this work of Friedman has become a generally accepted theory on which the entire fluid mechanics of supersonic speeds is based. This branch of fluid mechanics is called gas dynamics. So the founder of gas dynamics was our professor A. A. Fridman. Unfortunately, he died very soon - in 1925, when he was only 37 years old.
