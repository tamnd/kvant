It gave birth to great freedom and ease, the absence of any formality and the replacement of external manifestations of respect on the part of students with real deep respect, often turning into enthusiastic admiration. This respect made it impossible for the freedom and spontaneity in the relationship between N.N. Luzin and his students to degenerate into any kind of familiarity.
