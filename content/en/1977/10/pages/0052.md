Quests

1. a) In a three-digit number, I crossed out the first digit from the left, then the resulting two-digit number was multiplied by 7. The result was the original three-digit number. Find such a number.

6) In a three-digit number, I crossed out the middle digit, resulting in a number that is 6 times smaller. Find such a three-digit number.

c) Find all three-digit numbers that, when the middle digit is crossed out, decrease by 7 times, 9 times.

2. There are four shapes in a row on the table: a triangle, a rhombus, a circle, a square. The colors of these figures are: green, yellow, blue, red. In what order are the figures lying and what is the color of each of them, if the red figure lies between the green and blue, to the right of the yellow figure lies a rhombus, the circle lies to the right of the triangle and the rhombus, and the triangle does not lie on the edge, and, finally, the blue figure does not lie next to the yellow figure?

3. Tamara will be as old as 1977, what is the sum of the digits of the year of her birth. What year was she born?

4. The square table $n \times n$ is filled with natural numbers from 1 to $n^2$, and the first line contains consecutive numbers from 1 to $n$, the second from $n + 1$ to $2n$, and so on.

Prove that if you cut a square $m \times m$ from this table, then the sum of the numbers in it will be divisible by $m^2$.
