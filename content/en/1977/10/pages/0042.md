Problem
quantum

M466—M470; F478—F482

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the current school curriculum. More difficult tasks have been given asterisks. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than December 1, 1977 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant».
After the address on the envelope, write the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example, “M466, M467” or “F478”. Solutions to problems in each of the subjects (mathematics and physics), as well as new assignments should be sent in separate envelopes. Please also send problems from different issues of the journal in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of the decision verification).
At the beginning of each letter, please indicate your first and last name, school number and class in which you are a teacher. Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (mark on the envelope: “Problem «Kvant», new problems in physics or “... new problems in mathematics”). After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us.

M466. Among the 1977 coins, 50 are counterfeit. Each fake coin differs from the real one by 1 g (in one direction or the other). There are bowl scales with an arrow showing the difference in mass of one bowl and another. In one weighing, you need to find out about one selected coin whether it is fake or real. How to do this?

S. Fomin

M467. Points D and E divide sides AC and AB correctly of triangle ABC in the relations |AD| : |DC| = |BE| : |EA| = 1 : 2. Lines BD and CE intersect at point O. Prove that angle AOC is a right angle.

A. Krasnodemskaya

M468. Points A, B, C and D of the plane are such that for any point M of this plane the scalar product of the vectors $\vec{MA} \cdot \vec{MB}$ and $\vec{MC} \cdot \vec{MD}$ are not equal to each other. Prove that $\vec{AC} = \vec{DB}$. Is the opposite statement true?

Yu. Ionin

M469. a) Equation $x^4 + ax^3 + bx + c = 0$ has four different real roots. Prove that $ab < 0$.

b*) The equation $x^n + a_1x^{n-1} + \dots + a_{k-1}x^{n-k+1} + a_{k+1}x^{n-k-1} + \dots + a_n = 0$ has $n$ different real roots. Prove that $a_{k-1}a_{k+1} < 0$.

V. Vavilov
