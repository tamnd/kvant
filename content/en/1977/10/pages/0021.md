The meetings of individual groups of the seminar, which, as already mentioned, took place at the home of D. F. Egorov, also had a lively, although completely “chamber” character. Dmitry Fedorovich himself always set the tone for these meetings.

Formed back in 1914-1916. the group of senior students of N. N. Luzin consisted of D. E. Menshov, A. Ya. Khinchin, M. Ya. Suslin and me. By 1919-1920 it was replenished with a number of young mathematicians: P. S. Uryson, L. A. Lyusternik, M. A. Lavrentyev, N. K. Bari, several other students and students of N. N. Luzin. Thus, very different teams arose, which were soon joined by two such outstanding mathematicians as A. N. Kolmogorov and L. G. Shnirelman. Among the mathematicians of a slightly older generation, V. V. Stepanov immediately joined this team. Somewhat later, this team included P. S. Novikov and L. V. Keldysh. This is how the famous Lusitania arose - a community of young mathematicians, welded together not only by the dark friendly relations between his colleagues, but also by ardent love and a lively, unlimited interest in mathematical science.
