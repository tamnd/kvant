Finally, at the conclusion of the group's work, one or two group members had to make a presentation at the plenary meeting of the seminar. Thus, one report was devoted to series with permanent members, there were two reports on functional series, etc. The plenary meetings of D. F. Egorov’s seminar brought together virtually all actively working mathematicians in Moscow and in this regard, in some places, they even competed with the mathematical society. These plenary meetings were always very successful. The reports given at them, in most cases, evoked interesting statements from numerous cases, among which N.N. Luzin and V.V. Stepanov, who, however, had completely different temperaments, were especially active.
