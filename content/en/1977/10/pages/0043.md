6) $$\frac{1}{C_n^1} + \frac{1}{C_n^2} + \frac{1}{C_n^3} + \dots + \frac{1}{C_n^n} = \frac{n+1}{2^{n+1}} \left( \frac{2}{1} + \frac{2^2}{2} + \frac{2^3}{3} + \dots + \frac{2^n}{n} \right).$$

L. Kurlyandchik, A. Lisitsky

F478. Exactly such a cube rests on a cube lying on a smooth horizontal surface, and the cubes face up with their parallel faces. The speed of the oncoming cube before the collision was directed at an angle α to the side faces (Fig. 1). How will the cubes move after a collision if the friction coefficient between the faces of the cups is μ?

F479. To determine the unknown volume $V_1$ of cylinder I, assemble the diagram shown in figure 2. The volume of cylinder II is $V_2$ and $V_2 \ll V_1$.

First, the system is pumped down to a pressure $p_0$ close to zero. Then tap B is closed and cylinder I is filled with gas to pressure $p_a$. After this, valve A is closed, valve B is opened and the pressure established in the system $p_1$ is changed with pressure gauge C. Then, with valve B closed, the cylinder is filled with gas again to pressure $p_a$, valve B is opened and the pressure established in the system $p_2$ is changed. This operation is repeated n times. Show that the ratio $\frac{p_n - p_0}{np_a}$ is constant and equal to $\frac{V_2}{V_1 + V_2}$.

A. Mitrofanov

F480. The ship is driven by a water-jet engine, which emits a stream of water from the hull at a speed u. Every second a mass of water μ is released, which is taken from the river. At what speed of the ship is the efficiency of the propulsion system maximum? Neglect the friction force and water resistance.

F481. In an old battery consisting of n batteries connected in series, the internal resistance of one of the batteries increased sharply and became equal to $\rho = 10 \, r$, where r is the internal resistance of a normal battery. Considering e. d.s. all batteries are the same, determine at what load resistance R the power released at the load will not change if the damaged battery is short-circuited.

F482. A drop of water falls evenly in the air. How much does the radius of curvature $R_1$ of the drop surface at its top point differ from the radius of curvature $R_2$ at the bottom point, if the distance between these points is $d = 2 \cdot 10^{-3} \, \text{m}$? The water surface coefficient is $\sigma = 7 \times 10^{-2} \, \text{H/m}$.
