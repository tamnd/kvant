Preparatory courses at MISS

In this issue we publish an article about extramural and correspondence courses for university preparation at MISS. Similar courses have been created at all universities. These courses are especially useful for those who live far from large cities, have graduated from school a long time ago and want to test their knowledge and take advantage of gaps during the time remaining before exams. To enroll in preparatory courses, you need to send a letter to your chosen university (to the address of the preparatory courses), including an envelope with your address, in which you will receive preparation instructions.

Courses to prepare for entering universities at the Moscow Institute of Civil Engineering. V.V. Kuibyshev has been functioning for about twenty years. These courses provide an opportunity for industrial workers with work experience and demonized warriors who have had a significant break in their studies to repeat the high school program on the job and prepare for university entrance exams; they help tenth grade students to more deeply understand the most difficult issues of the school curriculum. In addition, preparatory courses are a transitional student from school to college; classes at preparatory courses accustom them to new forms of education that today's applicants and tomorrow's students will encounter in college: lectures, seminars, bell classes; taught to work with a book.

Classes on chickens are conducted according to both the new and old programs. The courses are carried out in several streams with preparation time from 1 to 10 months.

Mathematics and physics classes in very preparatory classes are conducted as follows. Applicants are given lectures on the main sections of the course, with the main focus being on questions that often cause difficulty in answering exams. For each topic covered, practical demonstrations are regularly carried out, during which typical problems for this section are examined, and then “homework” is done, consisting of 20-25 problems that need to be solved, and a list of theoretical questions that need to be given special attention. “Homework Reception” is similar in form to Kuzov’s Kolokovsky.

Twice a year (in January - February and in May) preparatory course workers take written and oral tests. These tests take place in an environment similar to an exam room. Test tickets are compiled according to the exam type; tests are accepted by members of the subject commission. All this is a good psychological preparation for future applicants, helps them assess the level of requirements for university entrance exams, identify shortcomings in their knowledge and take timely measures to eliminate them. Options for three tickets for the spring written test in mathematics and assignments from the test tickets in physics are given at the end of the article.

Admission to very preparatory courses begins on September 1 and continues until July 1 (for different streams). Applicants to the courses provide a document on secondary education or a certificate from the place of study, a certificate from the place of work (for the work of all young people), one photograph, a receipt from the post office for the transfer of 26 rubles. to the bank account of the institute.

The institute sends teaching aids in mathematics, physics, Russian language and literature, as well as tests, to those who have completed correspondence courses. Test work consists of answering theoretical questions and solving problems. The study of each topic ends with a test, which must be sent to the institute within the specified time schedule (two tests in mathematics and physics are given at the end of the article).

In the process of completing the test, the employee can contact teachers with questions of a theoretical and practical nature, as well as send consultations once a month (if possible).

Correspondence course students who have completed tests are invited to take very courses before exams. Classes are held daily, except Sunday, from July 1st to July 31st (applicants are provided with dormitory accommodation).

To enroll in correspondence courses, you must send an application addressed to the rector to the course address. After this, you will receive prepared instructions for admission to the courses.
