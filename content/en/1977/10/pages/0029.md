Two months after the return of the Zond-5 station (in November 1968), the Soviet automatic probe Zond-6 launched to the Moon, which, in accordance with the flight program, carried out two photography sessions of the Earth and the Moon (Fig. 2) and made a masterly descent to the Earth’s surface. As a result of the flight of the Zond-6 station, for the first time in the world, photographs of the far side of the Moon were obtained and delivered to Earth, which made it possible to begin compiling topographic maps.
