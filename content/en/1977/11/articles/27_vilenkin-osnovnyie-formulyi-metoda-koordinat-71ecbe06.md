Basic formulas of the coordinate method

Coordinates of vector $\vec{a}$
$$\vec{a} = x\vec{i} + y\vec{j} + z\vec{k} = (x; y; z)$$
$$x = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{i}, y = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{j}, z = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{k}$$

Coordinates of point $M$
If $\overrightarrow{OM} = (x; y; z)$ then $M (x; y; z)$

Relationship between the coordinates of the vector $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and the coordinates of its beginning $A (x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and end $B (x_2; y_2; z_2)$

Parallel transfer formulas $\vec{p} = (a; b; c)$, $M (x; y; z)$
$$\vec{p} (M) = M_1 (a + x; b + y; c + z)$$

Sum and difference of vectors $\vec{a} = (x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and $\vec{b} = (x_2; y_2; z_2)$
$$\vec{a} + \vec{b} = (x_1 + x_2; y_1 + y_2; z_1 + z_2)$$
$$\vec{a} - \vec{b} = (x_1 - x_2; y_1 - y_2; z_1 - z_2)$$

Product of the vector $\vec{a} = (x; y; z)$ and the number $p$
$$\vec{p a} = (px; py; pz)$$

Scalar product of non-Leaf vectors $\vec{a} = (x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and $\vec{b} = (x_2; y_2; z_2)$
$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}| \cdot |\vec{b}| \cdot \cos (\vec{a}, \vec{b})$$
$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 + z_1z_2$$

Vector length $\vec{a} = (x; y; z)$
$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$$

Distance between points $A(x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and $B(x_2; y_2; z_2)$
$$|AB| = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}$$

Angle between vectors $\vec{a} = (x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and $\vec{b} = (x_2; y_2; z_2)$
$$\cos(\vec{a}, \vec{b}) = \frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{a}| \cdot |\vec{b}|} = \frac{x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 + z_1z_2}{\sqrt{x_1^2 + y_1^2 + z_1^2} \sqrt{x_2^2 + y_2^2 + z_2^2}}$$

Homothety formulas with center $O(0; 0; 0)$ and coefficient $k$, $M(x; y; z)$
$$H_O^k(M) = M_1(kx; ky, kz)$$

Plane equation
$$ax + by + cz + d = 0$$

opening contain dot $O(0; 0; 0)$ at
$$ax + by + cz + d > 0 \quad d > 0$$
$$ax + by + cz + d < 0 \quad d < 0$$

closed
$$ax + by + cz + d \geq 0 \quad d \geq 0$$
$$ax + by + cz + d \leq 0 \quad d \leq 0$$

Vector perpendicular to plane $\alpha: ax + by + cz + d = 0$
$$\vec{n}_\alpha = (a; b; c)$$

Equation of a plane containing the point $A(x_1; y_1; z_1)$ and perpendicular to the vector $\vec{n} = (a; b; c)$
$$a(x - x_1) + b(y - y_1) + c(z - z_1) = 0$$

Angle between planes $\alpha: a_1x + b_1y + c_1z + d_1 = 0$, $\beta: a_2x + b_2y + c_2z + d_2 = 0$
$$\vec{n}_\alpha = (a_1; b_1; c_1), \vec{n}_\beta = (a_2; b_2; c_2)$$

Area $S_{\pi p}$ of the orthogonal projection onto the plane $\beta$ of a polygon of area $S$ lying in the plane $\alpha$
$$S_{\pi p} = S \cdot \cos(\alpha, \beta)$$

Equation of a sphere of radius $R$ with center at point $S(a; b; c)$
$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = R^2$$

Equation of a plane tangent to the sphere $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = R^2$ at point $A(m; p; q)$
$$(m - a)(x - a) + (p - b)(y - b) + (q - c)(z - c) = R^2$$
$$(a - m)(x - m) + (b - p)(y - p) + (c - q)(z - q) = 0.$$
