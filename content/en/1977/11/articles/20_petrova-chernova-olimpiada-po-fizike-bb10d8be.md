in physics

The final stage of the XI All-Russian Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held from April 14 to 19 of this year in the city of Frunze. The participants in this stage, as always, were the winners of the Resursivan Olympids, zonal Olympids held in the RSFSR, the Olympids of the cities of Moscow and Leningrad, and the winners of the X All-Union Olympids, who received Diplomas of I and II degrees. In total, 136 people came to Frunze: 50 tenth graders, 47 ninth graders, 39 eighth graders.

The opening of the final stage of the Olympics took place on April 14. The wonderful Minister of Public Education of the Kyrgyz SSR Z. D. Dzhantakova, the representative of the jurisprudence of the Olympiad in Physics, Professor of the Kyrgyz State University L. V. Tuzov, and others addressed the participants with welcoming words and wishes for success.

On April 15, the first - theoretical - round began. As always, he walked through the classes. The 8th grader was given 4 problems, the 9th grader - 5 problems, and the 10th grader - 5 problems. 2. Two ice floes are moving forward with the same absolute speed: one to the north, the other to the west.
