Prove that in the hexagon $T_1 \cap T_2$ the diagonals connecting opposite vertices are parallel to the sides of the triangle $T_1$ and intersect at one point. (M452)

2. Given an infinite number sequence $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$, it is known that $\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( a_{n+1} - \frac{1}{2} a_n \right) = 0$. Prove that $\lim a_n = 0$.

4. In some countries, you can travel from each city to any other in minutes to the rest of the cities. The cost of each such trip is known. Two routes have been compiled for the cities of the country. Each of these routes includes each city exactly once. When drawing up the first route, we were guided by the following principle: the starting point of the route is chosen randomly, and at each next step, among the cities through which the route does not yet pass, the one with the lowest cost of trains from the previous city is selected (if there are several such cities, then any of them is selected); and so on until all the cities have been passed. When compiling the second route, the starting city is also chosen randomly, and at each next step, among the cities through which the route does not yet pass, the one with the lowest cost of trains in each of the previous cities is selected. Prove that the total cost of travel on the first route is not greater than the total cost of travel on the second route. (M459)
