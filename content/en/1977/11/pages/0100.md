Following the decisions of the XXIV and XXV Congresses of the CPSU, our country is developing nuclear energy on a large scale. Powerful nuclear power plants of various types are being built on the European territory of the Soviet Union. The mighty atomic icebreakers “Lenin” and “Arktika” are operating successfully. The nuclear-powered icebreaker Sibir is preparing for its first voyage. In the tenth five-year plan, the total capacity of Soviet nuclear power plants is 12-15 million kilowatts.

TACC photographs show one of the reactors of the Leningrad Nuclear Power Plant (the plant's power is 2 million kilowatts) and the Soviet nuclear icebreaker Arktika, which is available to Polyus for the first time.
