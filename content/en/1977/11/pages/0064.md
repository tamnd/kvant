Not a single 10th grade student had the proposed problems. Since the research round was conducted as an experiment, the jury decided to consider the results of the first day as the main ones. The results of the second day could only improve them.

In their free time from solving problems, the guys made several excursions around Tallinn and its museums (after all, Tallinn is a city-museum of the Middle Ages, one of the most beautiful and interesting cities of the Soviet Union), visited the laboratories of the largest university in Tallinn - the Attempt Institute, visited the Estonia Opera and Ballet Theater, met with scientists and Talin schoolchildren. On April 18 and 19, several interesting lectures were given to the children: “Lobachsky Geometry and Celestial Mechanics” (lecturer - MSU Professor V. A. Alekseev), “How the distances between curves change” (lecturer - MSU Associate Professor V. A. S Kvortsov), “Number Systems” (lecturer - Professor I. M. Yaglom), “Abel-Ruffini Theorem” (lecturer: Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences D. B. Fuks), “Group Theory and Optimization Problems” (lecturer: Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. K. Tolpygo). The chairman of the jury for the 8th grade, Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences N. N. Konstantinov, conducted a few Mathematical Club classes with eighth-grade students. There was an organizational meeting of the jury with students from Tallinn mathematics schools.

So far we have been talking only about the winners (after all, all participants in the All-Union Olympiad are winners of school, regional, and, finally, resource school Olympiads). And now we turn to lovers of the “queen of sciences” who were not among the winners. Don't be upset! Failure at the Olympics is not at all an indicator of inability to do mathematics. Many mathematicians do not belong to the “sports” or “Olympic” type. They are slow to solve difficult problems and prove complex theorems. It is important to be a successful and hardworking person, deeply interested in science.
