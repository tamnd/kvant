I will accumulate a huge amount of non-standard size products. The fact is that mostly unskilled boys and girls worked at the plant, and there were no qualified workers left - they were recognized in the army. It was necessary to consider the possibility of using parts rejected due to size. The solution was found in the following way. Since the devices could be considered beyond repair, it was proposed to divide all the parts into several groups according to quantitative criteria. Parts from the corresponding groups were already capable of mating (assembly), and the resulting devices provided sufficient accuracy. As a result, a huge reserve was obtained for increasing the production of products that were extremely necessary for the front. In addition, the plant managed to free up large areas for production purposes.

During the Great Patriotic War, theoretical research in the field of mathematics did not stop. At that time, many excellent studies were carried out in the fields of algebra, topology, probability theory, functional analysis, function theory and geometry. The development of theoretical mathematics was absolutely necessary for

left-war development of both science and the entire national economy. This created an excellent basis for the subsequent development of atomic physics, the creation of electronic computer technology, and the first victories in the liberation of outer space.

The post-war years are characterized by a rapid process of mathematization of meanings and practical activities. Mathematicians were required to solve problems of organizing production and to study biological processes, to advance in the field of physics and to create technical systems, to study economic issues and to rationally locate industrial enterprises. But this is not enough. It turned out that mathematical methods provide assistance to archeology and history, linguistics and medicine. New directions of mathematical thought appeared. The mathematical logic of the device is unequally greater than before the war.
