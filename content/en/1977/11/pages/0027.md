This listing of new branches of mathematics, in the formation and development of which Soviet mathematicians participated, can be continued further. However, we do not have the opportunity to touch on all of them, even including them. That is why we are forced to remain only on a few lines of thought that, for one reason or another, are of particular interest.

First of all, we should mention a large area of ​​research related to finding extreme solutions. The importance of this city of research has fully developed only recently: given the external scale of the national economy, the unsuccessful use of materials and resources leads to huge overexpenditures; an unsuccessful choice of direction of movement replaces the delivery of passengers and cargo; unsuccessful distribution of work between equipment leads to incomplete use of machines and equipment. The first problems of this kind appeared a long time ago and were known back in Ancient Greece.

Immediately before the Great Patriotic War, L. V. Kantorovich considered a number of new problems for finding maximum and minimum values ​​related to the distribution of work, cutting of materials, organization of transportation, etc. These problems served as the beginning of the creation of a new mathematical theory - linear programming, which received rapid development after the war. Now these primary formulations of problems have been obtained in a general formulation, solution methods have been proposed for them, an interesting theory has been constructed, these results have been extended to more general cases (nonlinear programming).

A large area of ​​research, caused by the need to entrust automatic control of technological processes, the movement of aircraft, spacecraft, etc., was developed by L. S. Pongyagi and his students.
