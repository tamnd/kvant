Gilberti's problems and Soviet mathematics

The number one event at the International Congress of Mathematicians, held in August 1970 in the world famous French resort town of Niszcz, was the famous solution of Gilberti's decimal problem. The hero of the day was the twenty-year-old Soviet mathematician Yu. V. Matiyase in Chi, whose report was included in the agenda of the congress above and beyond the compiled program. Thus ended the 70-year history of this problem by Gilberti.

The solution to each of Gilberti's twenty-year problems, even each partial success in solving them, is accepted by the entire mathematical world as a major mathematical achievement. David Gilbert was born in 1862 in Königsberg. In this city he graduated from school and university, here he began his path in science, where he was successful from the very first steps. In 1899, his work “Foundations of Geometry” was published, which constituted an era in the foundations of mathematics.
