⟦folio 90⟧

New books

In this issue we continue to publish summaries of books published in the fourth quarter of this year and of interest to our readers.

Most of them can be purchased through specialized “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Nuuka"

1. Volynsky B. A. Spherical laborometry. Volume 6 l., circulation 15,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.

The book examines questions of geometry on the surface of a sphere, spherical bigons and triangles, as well as the correspondence between the basic elements of spherical triangles. Formulas relating the sides and angles of spherical triangles are derived.

The book is intended for people interested in astronomy. It can be used in astronomical circle classes.

2. Pogorelov A.V. Elementary geometry. 3rd edition, add. Volume 15 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 43 kopecks.

This book, written by a prominent geometrician, contains material that is somewhat beyond the scope of the school curriculum. However, it can serve well as a guide for people interested in geometry and for mathematics teachers in secondary schools.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

3. Vorotnikov I. A. Entertaining drawing. 3rd edition, revised. Volume 13 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 55 kopecks.

The entertaining form of the tasks and exercises contained in the book goes well with their clearly practical content. The proposed graphic tasks are based on a wide range of phenomena and facts of everyday life. They are solved with the help of the knowledge that students receive from the school drawing course.

Publishing house "Knowledge"

4. "Number and Missel". Collection of articles. Volume 9 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.

The collection was prepared by a group of leading Soviet scientists - corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences N. N. Monseev, A. A. Lyapunov, academician A. A. Dorodnitny and others.

The authors, in a popular scientific form, talk about what new mathematical modeling methods have brought in solving biological, sociological, economic and other problems.

The collection is intended for a wide range of readers.

Books in the series “Mathematics and Cybernetics”

5. Venzel E. S. Probability theory (first step).

Author. brochures - a prominent Soviet scientist - popularly talks about the theory of probability, how this discipline developed, how it has found wide application in almost all branches of modern science, as well as how scientific its study is.

The brochure is a kind of preface to an in-depth study of probability theory.

6. Gnedenko B.V., Kantorovich L.V. et al. Mathematics for the national economy.

The collection includes articles by major Soviet scientists - academicians L. V. Kantorovich, V. P. Tikhonov, academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B. V. Gnedenko, etc. The authors talk in an interesting and accessible way about the most important scientific problems posed by the entire course of development of the national economy and successful mathematicians; about the theory of linear programming, queuing theory, optimal scheduling, operations research and other important applications of mathematics.

Physics

Publishing house "Nuuka"

1. Smorodinsky Ya. A. Tyagodence. (Library of the Physics and Mathematics School.) Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 17 kopecks.

The main part of the book is devoted to the application of the law of universal gravitation to the solution of simple problems related to the fall of bodies, the movement of planets and spacecraft. Solving problems does not require knowledge of higher mathematics. Using only vector algebra formulas, brief information about which is given in the appendix. The book outlines the history of the science of gravity, the laws of motion of bodies in a gravitational field, and the changes that the general theory of relativity brought with it.

The book is intended for high school students and technical students.

2. Gladkova R. A. et al. Collection of problems and questions in physics. Volume 20 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 64 kopecks.

Mathematical, placed in the book, completely covers the entire school curriculum for the physics course. The given tasks for individual topics are considered in order of increasing difficulty. At the beginning of each paragraph, examples of solving typical problems with detailed explanations are used.

The book is intended for high school students and technical students, as well as for people preparing to enter college on their own.
