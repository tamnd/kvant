F483. With what horizontal acceleration must a wedge with an angle $\alpha$ (Fig. 1) move so that the load lying on it rises upward, if the friction coefficient between the load and the wedge is $\mu$?

F484. Inside a sphere of radius $R$, uniformly charged with volume density $\rho$, there is a grounded metal sphere of radius $r$ (Fig. 2). Find the dependence of the potential of this system on the distance to the center of the sphere.

F486. figure 4 shows a diagram of the mass spectrometer. In the lowerizer $A$, ions are formed, which are accelerated by a voltage of $U = 10$ kV and enter through the gap $C$ into a magnetic field with induction $|\vec{B}| = 0,1$ T. After turning, they fall on the photographic plate $D$ and cause it to turn black. At what distance from each other will the stripes $H^{+}, ^{2}H^{+}, ^{3}H^{+}, He^{+}$ be located on the photo plastic? What must be the width of the slit for stripes $^{16}O$ and $^{15}N$ to separate?

F487. The beetle is photographed at two scales: from a distance $l_{1} = 3F$, where $F$ is the focal length of the object, and from a distance $l_{2} = 5F$. How many times must the diameter of the object's aperture be changed so that the illumination of the image on film in both cases is the same? Consider that the diameter of the object in both cases is much less than $F$.
