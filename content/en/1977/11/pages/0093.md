4. Khurgin Ya. I. Yes, mute or maybe...
Volume 10 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 33 kopecks.

When managing technical processes, legal apparatus, economic and social systems, it is often necessary to make decisions in an environment of chance, in the presence of errors and interference. Then probabilistic-statistical methods will come to the fore. The study of the functioning of living organisms from the standpoint of control theory is based on the same methods. In a straightforward manner, without the use of complex mathematical apparatus, using simple examples from the field of natural and technical matters, the book talks about modern mathematical statistics, the theory of statistical inference and decision making, the construction of mathematical models of real phenomena and the statistical theory of experiment.

The book is intended for high school students and anyone interested in modern management theory.

5. E Fremov Yu. N. In the depths of the Universe. Edition 2, revised. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 35 kopecks.

The book is dedicated to the structure of the world of stars and galaxies around us, the history of its study and the method of determining distances in the Universe. It is precisely the development of methods for finding distances that made it possible to recognize the Universe: only knowing the distance of a celestial object can one say something about its nature. The history of astronomy is the history of disputes about distances: first the Sun and the Moon, then stars and, already at the beginning of our century, galaxies.

The book is written lively and well illustrated. Designed for a wide range of people with secondary education and high school students who are interested in the problems of astronomy.

7. Fabrikant V. A., Glazunov A. G. Accessibility of physics to the national economy. Volume 7 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 21 kopecks.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
