Soviet physicists are leaders in the field of research into the problems of controlling thermonuclear reactions and the possibility of using thermonuclear energy for world purposes. Particularly important results were obtained at the TOKAMAK experimental thermonuclear installations created at the I.V. Kurchatov Institute of Atomic Energy.

The photograph posted here shows the most powerful installation of this type - TOKAMAK-10. The hydrogen plasma produced in it has record characteristics. With this installation we already have a developed thermonuclear reaction under laboratory conditions.

In the image and likeness of Soviet tokamaks, thermonuclear installations were created in many countries around the world. TOKAMAK-10 will serve as a prototype for future thermonuclear power plants.
