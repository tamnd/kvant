put $BD_1$, and rotate around the $BD_1$ axis by 60° (Fig. 3). You can rotate it first and then move it. After the rotation, the point $B_1$ appears in the plane $BA_1D_1$ (prove this yourself), so it is easy to find that after the translation the segment $OB_1$ (the image of the segment $BB_1$) will intersect the segment $A_1D_1$ at the point $L$ such that $|LD_1| = 3a/4$. The total part of the cubes will be made up of two triangular pairs $D_1KLM$ and $OKLM$, the side edges of each of which form right angles in pairs, and the lengths of these edges are equal to $3a/4$. From here we get the answer:

$$V = 2 \cdot \frac{1}{6} \cdot \left( \frac{3}{4} a \right)^3 = \frac{9a^3}{64}.$$

Task 3. Prove that if a regular metrahedron of a surface is 90° around a straight line connecting the midpoints of any two of its crossing edges, and then obtained a surface metrahedron 90° around a straight line connecting the midpoints of any two of its crossing edges, the last metrahedron coincides with the original one.

If through each edge of a regular metrahedron a plane is drawn parallel to the opposite edge, then these planes will bound the cube (Fig. 4). Connecting the vertices of the cube with segments “through one”, we again get a metrahedron - the original one (at figure red) or another one (blue). It is easy to see that with the turns described in the task, the red metrahedron turns into a blue one, and the blue one into a red one. Therefore, after two rotations, the metrahedron will turn into itself.

Exercises

1. A regular triangular paramid consists of the base $a$ surface at the side at an angle of 60°. Determine the volume of the volume of the original part and the surface of the paramids, if the side faces of the paramids are right triangles.

2. Two cubes with edge $a$ receive one from the other surface at 60° around the diagonal surface. Find the volume of their volume.

3. Two identical regular metrahedrons with edge $a$ have a common height, and the vertex of each of them lies in the center of the base of the other. The base of the second metrahedron of the surface is 60° relative to the base of the first. Find the volume of their volume.

4. Two identical regular metrahedrons with edge $a$ have a common segment connecting the surfaces of two opposite edges, but one surface metrahedron is at 90° with respect to the other. Find the volume of their volume.
