Experimental problems of the Physics Olympiad

On April 17, an experimental round of the final stage of the XI All-Union Schoolchildren Olympiad in Physics took place in the laboratories of the Faculty of Physics of the Kyrgyz State University. Each participant in the Olympiad was asked to solve two experimental problems. Let's talk about these problems and their solutions.

8th grade

Task 1. Determine the density of the metal found in one of two pieces of plastamine, if it is known that the masses of plastamine in the common pieces are the same. Evaluate the power of obtaining results. It is not allowed to extract metal from plasticine.

Equipment: 1) weight with weights, 2) glass of water, 3) stamps.

Using scales with weights, you can determine the mass of a piece of plasticine with metal ($m_{\pi\pi}+m_{\mu}$), the mass of pure plasticine ($m_{\pi\pi}$) and, by their difference, the mass of metal ($m_{\mu}$) found in one of the pieces of plasticine. By lifting these pieces of plasticine first in air and then in water, you can find the protruding force and, knowing the density of water, you can calculate the volumes of the pieces ($V_{\pi\pi}+V_{\mu}$ and $V_{\pi\pi}$). The volume of metal ($V_{\mu}$) can be determined by the difference between these volumes. Then the density of the metal

$$\rho_{\mu}=\frac{m_{\mu}}{V_{\mu}}.$$

There were two options: in some pieces of plastine, finding a piece of pure plastine with a mass equal to the mass of plastine in the composite piece, projectilin was given (in an indefinite amount). In this case, to solve the problem, it would be necessary to take a piece of pure plasticine, the mass of which is equal to the mass ($m$) of the composite piece (volume $V$), and determine its volume ($V_0$).
