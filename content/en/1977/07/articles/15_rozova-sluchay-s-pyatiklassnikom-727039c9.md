⟦folio 48⟧

It all started with letters.

Letter one. “Hello, Andrey! We recently had an Olympiad in mathematics at our school. I solved all the problems, but I'm not sure about the last one. There was no time to check if I found all the numbers; Yes, even if I had time, I still wouldn’t go through all the double-digit numbers, because it’s boring! Decide and write what your answers will be, otherwise I doubt it. This is the task.

In some two-digit numbers, I crossed out the digit, and it decreased by 31 times. Which number and in what number did you cross out?

I got three answers: 31, 62, and 93, and the first number must be crossed out. Surrey."

Letter two. “Hello, Seryozhka. You gave the correct answer and, if you agree, then solve our Olympiad problem.

A three-digit number is 26 times larger than the two-digit number written as its last number. Find this number. How many maxim numbers are there in total? Andrey".

Having received this letter, Seryozhka contacted, and then Andrey tried to solve the problem. But this time there was no agreement, although Seryozhka tried out a lot of numbers and tried to compose them himself. Then he thought that maybe the problem could be solved with an equation. But what should we take for x? Three digit number? How then to write down the condition of the problem?

Suddenly it “ dawned on him ” that all numbers are written using less than ten digits. In the problem, a three-digit number is unknown. Here are the three unknowns:

number of hundreds - x,
number of tens - y,
number of units - z.

And the condition of the problem will be written like this:
100x + 10y + z = 26(10y + z),
or, after simplifications,
4x = 10y + z.

Here Seryozhka thought again - there is one equation, but there are three unknowns. But as soon as he remembered that x, y, z are numbers, things started going again. In the last equality on the right there is 10y + z - a two-digit number, which means that on the left 4x is also a two-digit number. This will happen only for x = 3, 4, ..., 9. And the answer is immediately obtained:

| x | 10y + z | y | z | Answer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | 12 | 1 | 2 | 312 |
| 4 | 16 | 1 | 6 | 416 |
| 5 | 20 | 2 | 0 | 520 |
| 6 | 24 | 2 | 4 | 624 |
| 7 | 28 | 2 | 8 | 728 |
| 8 | 32 | 3 | 2 | 832 |
| 9 | 36 | 3 | 6 | 936 |

Now Seryozhka had no doubt that he had found all the numbers. Then he crossed out his own, and Andrey asked to send more problems. Here they are.

Task and

1. Find all two-digit numbers that are divisible by a) 2; b) 3; c) 4; d) 5; e) 6; e) 7; g) 8; h) 9, and at the same time they give partials equal to the sum of the digits of the dividend.

2. In a three-digit number, all the digits are different and there is no zero among them. The digits of this number were swapped, and all the (different) three-digit numbers receiving were added. Prove that this sum is divisible by 222.

Is this statement true if the original three-digit number contains identical digits or is there a zero?

3. When dividing a certain two-digit number by 6, the remainder was equal to the first digit of the dividend, and when dividing the same number by 10, the remainder was equal to the second digit of the number, and the quotient was 3. Find all such numbers.
