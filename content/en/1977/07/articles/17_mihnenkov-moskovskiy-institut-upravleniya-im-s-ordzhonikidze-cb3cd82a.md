Moscow Institute of Management named after. S. Ordzhonikidze

The scientific and technological revolution, the most clear expression of which came in the second half of the twentieth century, is characterized by a significant consolidation of the growth rate of social production on the basis of enormous achievements in the field of science and technology. However, one of the most striking manifestations of the scientific and technological revolution that influences the development of the economy is the sharp increase in the complexity of managing the national economy. In this regard, the importance and role of scientific management organization is constantly increasing.

Organization of management is not only a complex multifaceted science, but also an art; mastering the court of the possible is only possible when armed with fundamental knowledge of theory and practice.

The Moscow Institute of Management (MIU) is the only institute in our country that trains specialists in management organization.

Science has enriched the arsenal of management methods and tools. Economic and mathematical methods, electronic embroidery technology, systems analysis, operations research - all this is only a small part of what a specialist in the field of management organization needs to know. Therefore, at our institute we will specialize in the field of management, reading the purpose of a number of special disciplines, such as the theory of management of social arbitrariness, organization of management of arbitrariness, socio-psychological foundations of management, modeling of management processes, theory of automated systems, economic cybernetics, foreign management experience, etc.

The institute adopts an industry-specific training principle, in accordance with which specialists are trained for a number of industries: mechanical engineering, energy, metallurgical industry, chemical industry, construction, urban management, automated transport.

In addition to specialists in management organization, the institute trains specialists in automated control systems (for the same sectors of industry and transport) and cyber economists for various sectors of the national economy.

A characteristic feature of studying at the institute is the combination of the educational process with elements of scientific research. At the institute there is a research laboratory for national economic management and a scientific and embroidery center, which cooperate with many enterprises in our country.
