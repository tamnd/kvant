⟦folio 47⟧

«Kvant» for younger students

Quests

1. How many two-digit numbers are there where the tens digit is greater than the ones digit?

2. A piece of wire 102 cm long needs to be cut into pieces 15 cm and 12 cm long, but so that there are no scraps. How to do this? How many solutions does this problem have?

3. Can a figure consisting of five points have one center of symmetry and exactly one axis of symmetry?

4. How many ways can you fold a triangle from a rectangle measuring 3x5 cm by cutting the rectangle into two parts with one straight cut? Methods are considered different if they lead to non-congestive triangles.

5. In actions on figure, each digit is protected by a certain letter. Decipher this entry, and then write down the letters by number in ascending order (from 0 to 9). What word did you come up with?
