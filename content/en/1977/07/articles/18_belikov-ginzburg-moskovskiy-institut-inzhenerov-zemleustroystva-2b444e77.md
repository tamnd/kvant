⟦folio 52⟧

The Moscow Institute of Land Engineering Engineers is the only specialized higher educational institution in the country that trains land surveyors, rural slab architects and agricultural surveyors. The Institute is one of the oldest universities in the country, a major scientific and methodological center on land development, rural architecture and geodesy in the field of agriculture.

The institute has three faculties: earth engineering, architecture of rural populated areas and engineering geodesy.

The Faculty of Earth Engineering is the leading one at the institute. He trains earth engineers. What is so attractive about this profession?

First of all, it must be said that a person who devotes himself to the cultivation of land deals with the substitutive nature of our country, with its great and very important wealth - the land. An earthmoving engineer is involved in allocating sites for the raftering of industrial enterprises, large hydraulic structures, highways, etc., draws up projects for international networks of economic and domestic earthmoving, develops measures for land reclamation, combating soil erosion, and others.

One of the main assistants of a land planner is a plan or map. An earth surveyor must be good at compiling them, which is why geodesy is studied in the first year. And in order to know what the quality of the land is, what is best to grow on it, whether it is possible to plant a garden in this or that area, where it is necessary to plant forests, and where to continue roads, students study soil, agriculture and plant growing, the basics of animal husbandry, mechanization of agricultural production, agricultural reclamation and water supply.

The Faculty of Architecture of Rural Settlements was created in 1965 and trains architects in rural soffits.

The CPSU Program teaches ways to gradually transform collective farm villages and villages into integrated urban-type settlements with comfortable residential buildings, public utilities, household enterprises, cultural and medical institutions.

Graduates of the faculty are engaged in architectural design of villages, residential and cultural buildings, mechanized livestock and poultry farms and other industrial complexes. This faculty studies special disciplines:

architectural design, figure, history of arts and architecture, structural mechanics, design of buildings and structures, technical and organizational construction production, as well as a cycle of engineering and agricultural disciplines.

During their studies, students complete course projects and work, and for practical training they are enrolled in temporary work in design organizations, where they will carry out real projects for collective and state farms.

Graduates of the faculty are assigned to design institutes for rural strufting, located in the capital, regional and regional centers, and also work as district architects, providing architectural guidance and control over trussing in rural areas.

The Faculty of Engineering Geodesy trains geodesic engineers for agriculture. The specialty of geodesy is one of the most ancient and romantic. Geodesy, hydraulic engineering and other specialties leading the development of uninhabited lands, as a rule, go with a topographic map in hand. This means that surveyorsISTS have already worked on these uninhabited lands.

The geodesic engineer has a broad position both in the field of ground survey methods and in the field of processing images obtained from aircraft.

Methods of hired topographic surveys are studied in courses in geodesy, engineering geodesy, higher geodesy, and useful astronomy. When studying basic factors and aerial photography, deciphering aerosims, factor measurements and engineering factors, students master the most progressive and high-performance methods for creating maps and plans.

In geodesy and factor measurements, advances in the field of radio electronics and computer technology have recently been widely used. The curriculum provides for the study of special courses in radio electronics and computer technology.

Graduates of the faculty are sent to work in aerial photo-geodetic enterprises, in earth engineering design institutes and other organizations.

Some of the faculty's graduates are involved in helping developing countries by carrying out surveying work for agricultural purposes.

Those entering the institute take the following exams:

at the Faculty of Earth Engineering and the Faculty of Engineering Geodesy - in mathematics (written and oral), physics (oral), Russian language and literature (written);

to the Faculty of Architecture of Rural Populated Areas - in figure (gypsum head and gypsum architecture detail), drawing (contour base with given parameters), mathematics (oral), physics (oral), Russian language and literature (written).
