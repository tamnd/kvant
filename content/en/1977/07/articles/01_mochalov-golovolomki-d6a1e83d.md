Amazing numbers

The recording given here has an amazing property. Look at it from the lower left corner of the magazine. You will see a correctly completed addition example. Now he will look from the lower right corner. What do you see? Another correctly executed addition example! In addition, it uses all numbers from 1 to 9.
Try to find another such recording of numbers from 1 to 9.

Crossumber
You need to write one number in each cell (the red lines are the boundaries of the numbers).
Horizontally: A. A number with sequential killing digits. D. Power of a certain number. D. The square of a certain number. E. A number with sequentially returning digits. H. Product of three consecutive integers.
Vertically: B. A multiple of 11. C. An odd number. D. Cube of a certain number. D. Square of a prime number. G. Sum of heels of consecutive integers.
L. Motsalov
