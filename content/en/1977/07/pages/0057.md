A little about exams

These “advices” to the examinee are based on the experience of students at the Moscow Institute of Physics. They were published in the newspaper “For Teaching”. The editors of the decision reprinted the note, believing that the councils would press future applicants.

Classification

Exams vary depending on the subject in which they are taken (for example, physics, mathematics, English, etc.) and the type of these exams (entrance, graduation, life, etc.).

To make the exams as comprehensive as possible, they are combined into so-called sessions.

In addition, examinations can take place in two forms: written and oral. About them in order.

Written

The question of passing the exam is divided into three sub-questions.

1. How to write a paper so that it is rated as high as possible.
2. How is the teacher known?
3. How and what is the best way to use the exam.

The first question has been studied well enough by almost everyone, so there is no need to write about it, the third is not published due to special messages.

It remains to consider the second question. It requires a creative approach and transforms the imagination of any person with unlimited possibilities. The most interesting, of course, are the new methods, since the old ones have already been studied by the examiners.

Here is one of the original solutions. Choose a place in the classroom that is most difficult to access (far from the aisle, small distance between rows, only the next one, etc.). About twenty minutes after the start of the exam, when the teacher unfolds the latest issue of the newspaper, raise your hand (just don’t do it first, otherwise he will remember you). When he finally gets to your place, squirm around and tell him the issue has sorted itself out. Post-ear actions require certain tactics. Ask the duty teacher any questions, starting with an unclearly directed letter or an unclearly formed task condition and ending with when and how many times you can go out and where you need to go. Be sure that after a while he will not notice you. Teler is capable of cheating work.

Oral

Oral exams deserve attention no less than written ones. After the “Councils of Examiners” were published (see “Physicists continue to joke,” Mir Publishing House, 1968, p. 103), it became even more difficult to pass them. Therefore, we propose several rules that can make it easier for applicants to pass the oral exams.

Tips for the examinee

1. First of all, let the examiner understand that your future career, as well as your personal life, depends little on his assessment of your knowledge. Put it in place from the beginning.
2. Be friendly with your examinee, but be gentle. Just don't notice other examinees. Don’t bother to hear questions asked by another author: if you can answer them, you will have excessive self-confidence, if not, you will feel insecure, but meanwhile, they have nothing to do with you.

3. Make the examiner understand (or at least accept) your method, especially if the method is unusual. This will turn the examiner away from the constructed thoughts and force him to look not only at the answer, but also at the solution.

4. If you have a good memory, do not show this to the examiner. Output everything that can be output. Particularly good imprinting is possible; even more imprinting can be done; they are combined into so-called sessions.

5. Avoid the words: “you know from the school course...” or “at the institute they study this in more detail...”. The examiner forgot the school course a long time ago, and, as it turns out, he completed it at the institute only recently, perhaps even part-time, so he has not yet had time to study the program. In general, avoid links. Next, the problem is simple, and its solution is in all textbooks, think in polymnouts and answer as if it just dawned on you.

6. Don't ask the examiner what he gave you. Beg with a smile and leave the audience. If you are told to want results behind a door, don't stand near it. Go to the buffet. This will make it easier for everyone to think that the exam is not the most important thing in life for you, and will increase respect for you.
