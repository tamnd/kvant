⟦folio 51⟧

Students have their own computer center at their disposal, where modern computers are installed, including computers of a single series. Many studies conducted by students were awarded medals and diplomas from VDNKh, the Ministry of Higher and Secondary Special Education of the USSR, and the Central Committee of the Komsomol.

The social life of MIU students is wide and multifaceted. The glorious labor deeds of students participating in the construction of the BAM, livestock complexes in the Smolensk region and a number of lines in Bulgaria and Czechoslovakia are marked by many government accolades. Since 1974, the Institute has had a Faculty of Public Professions (FOP), which includes the following departments: lectures and propaganda, artistic professions, as well as sports and tourism and excursion work.

Unlimited opportunities are provided to sports lovers who will soon have their own sports complex.

Below are examples of options for the entrance written exam in mathematics and examples of problems for the oral exam in physics at MIU in 1976.

Mathematica

Option 1

1. A circle of radius 4 cm is inscribed in a triangle. One side of the triangle is divided by a point of tangency into segments of length 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the lengths of the other two sides.

2. Solve inequality
$$|x-6| > |x^2-5x+9|.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$3\cdot 16^x+2\cdot 81^x=5\cdot 36^x.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{2}(\cos 5x+\cos 7x)-\cos^2 2x+\sin^2 3x=0.$$

5. Reduce to a form convenient for logarithm:
$$\text{tg} x+\text{tg} y+\text{tg} z-\frac{\sin(x+y+z)}{\cos x\cos y\cos z}.$$

Option 2

1. The height length of a right quadrangular truncated straight line is equal to H, the side edge and diagonal of the straight line are inclined to the plane of its base at angles α and β. Find the lateral surface area of ​​the straight line.

2. For what values ​​of p does the system of inequalities
$$-9 < \frac{3x^2+px-6}{x^2-x+1} < 6$$

is satisfied for all real values ​​of x?

3. Solve the equation
$$x^2\cdot \log_2 27\cdot \log_9 x=x+4.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\sin 9x=2\sin 3x.$$

5. Calculate without table
$$\text{tg} 9^\circ-\text{tg} 27^\circ-\text{tg} 63^\circ+\text{tg} 81^\circ.$$

Option 3

1. The lengths of the two sides of the triangle are equal to a and b. Find the length of the triangle if the angle opposite this side is twice the angle opposite side b.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3x^2+1}+\sqrt{x^2+3}=\sqrt{6x^2+10}.$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\log_0,1(x^2+1)<\log_0,1(2x-5).$$

4. Solve the equation
$$1+\cos t+\cos 2t+\cos 3t=0.$$

5. Prove that if a+b≥1, then the inequality a^4+b^4≥\frac{1}{8} holds.

Physics

1. A weight of mass m=30 z is attached to the end of a weightless rod, which rotates uniformly in a vertical plane around the other end, making n=5 rps. The length of the rod is l=30 cm. What is the tension in the rod when the weight passes the top and bottom of its trajectory?

2. Two cylinders containing the same gas were connected by a tube with a tap. In the first cylinder the gas pressure is p_1=8\cdot 10^5 h/m^2, in the second cylinder p_2=6\cdot 10^5 h/m^2. The capacity of the first cylinder is V_1=3 l, the second one V_2=5 l. What pressure (in mm rm. cm) will be established in the cylinders if the tap is opened? The temperature is constant. The volume of the tube can be changed.

3. A capacitor with a capacity of C_1=2 µF is charged to a voltage of U_1=100 V, and a capacitor with a capacity of C_2=0.5 µF is charged to a voltage of U_2=50 V. After charging, the capacitor was connected with the same poles. How much heat is released as a result of this connection?

4. An on-layer coil containing N=1000 turns of wire is placed in a single-mode magnetic field parallel to its axis. The magnetic field induction uniformly changes at a rate of |ΔB|/Δt=10^{-2} ml/sec. The radius of the coil is r=2 cm. A capacitor with a capacity of C=10 μF is connected to the ends of the coil. Determine the charge on the capacitor.

5. The luminous point S is located on the main optical axis of the diverging lens. The optical power of the lens is D=-2.5 dnmr, and the distance from the lens to the minimum image of the point is 30 cm. Where is point S? Construct the course of the rays.
