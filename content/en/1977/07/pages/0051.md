university exams
in 1976

Kuibyshev State University was opened in 1969. Currently, a university campus is being built near the Volga bank. The buildings of the mechanics, mathematics and physics faculties have already been erected, and a student dormitory building has been built.

The main goal of the university is to prepare specialists of the highest qualification for work in research institutions, in industrial subject areas, in higher and secondary specialized educational institutions and secondary schools.

Preparation of specialties in the field of mathematics, mechanics and physics is carried out at the faculties of mechanics, mathematics and physics.

Mathematics students specializing in applied mathematics, functional analysis and function theory, differential and integral equations.

Mechanical students specializing in aero- and hydromechanics, as well as mechanics of deformable solids.

Physics students specializing in theoretical physics, optics and spectronics, radiophysics and electronics, physics of semiconductors and dielectrics, solid state physics.

Below are options for the entrance written exam in mathematics and problems from the tickets for the oral exam in physics at the mechanics, mathematics and physics faculties of Kuibyshev University in 1976.

Mathematics

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

Option 1

1. One of the two communicated cones rests with its apex on the base of the other cone, the length of its generatrix is ​​l, and the angle at the apex in the axial section is 2α. The angle at the apex of the axial section of the second cone is 2β. Find the volume of the common part of the cones if the ratio of the lengths of their heights is equal to k.

2. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{x-2}\sqrt{x-1}=1$$

3. Solve inequality (a>0)

$$\log_2(\sqrt{x^2-2ax+1}-1) \leq 1$$

4. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} \sin x+\sin y=\sin(x+y), \\ |x|+|y|=1. \end{cases}$$

Option 2

1. A paramid is inscribed in a cone whose angle at the apex of the axial section is 2α, the base of which is a right triangle with an acute angle β. A cutting plane is drawn through the hypotenuse of the base so that the ratio of the sectional area to the area of ​​the base of the paramid is equal to k. Determine the angle of inclination of the cutting plane of the base and the permissible values ​​of k.

2. Solve the equation

$$2x+3-\sqrt{x^2-2x-3}=0$$

3. Solve inequality

$$4-\lg x \leq 3\sqrt{\lg x}$$

4. Solve systems of equations

$$\begin{cases} tg x tg z=3, \\ tg y tg z=6, \\ x+y+z=\pi. \end{cases}$$

Faculty of Physics

Option 3

1. The ratio of the lengths of two segments enclosed between parallel planes is equal to k, and the angles that each of these segments make with one of the planes is in the ratio 2:3. Find the values ​​of these angles and the permissible values ​​of k.

2. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{x-1}-\sqrt{x+3-4\sqrt{x-1}}=2$$

3. Solve the equation

$$\cos^2x-\cos^23x+3\cos^22x=0$$

4. Solve inequality

$$x^{-[1\lg^2x+1\lg x^3+3]}\log_x\sqrt{2} \leq \frac{x}{\sqrt{2}}.$$

Option 4

1. In a regular quadrangular paramid, a plane is drawn through two lateral edges that do not belong to the same face. The paramidal plane ratio is k. Find the angle between two adjacent side faces and the permissible values ​​of k.

2. Solve the equation

$$2x+1-\sqrt{x^2-3x+1}=0.$$
