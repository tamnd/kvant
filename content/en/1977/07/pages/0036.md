F466. Figure3 is taken with a photograph of the tracks of the part in the cloud chamber. The decay of gas nuclei filling the cloud chamber is caused in this case by the action of fast neutrons on them. The cloud chamber was filled with a mixture of hydrogen (H₂), alcohol vapor (C₂H₅OH) and water (H₂O) and placed in a magnetic field with an induction of 1.3 ml. The magnetic induction vector is directed perpendicular to the figure plane.

1) Determine the energy of the proton that appears at point A. The trajectory of this proton is curve AA′. Why does the curvature of the proton trajectory change? Determine the energy of the proton at point C of its trajectory.

The mass of a proton is 1.67·10⁻²⁷ kcal.

2) Determine which element's nucleus decayed at point A if the tracks of the part starting at this point are identified as tracks of two protons and two α particles. (10 grades)

F467. The Moon is simultaneously photographed from the same side from the Earth and from the Moon's satellite. The satellite's orbit is steep. The diameter of the image of the Moon in a photograph taken on Earth is 4 mm, and on the Moon’s satellite it is 250 mm. Find the period of revolution of the Moon's satellite in its orbit if both images were made using identical objects with a focal length of 500 mm. Accept that the acceleration of gravity on the Moon is 6 times less than on Earth and the distance from the Earth to the Moon is 380,000 km. (10 grades)

M411. Three segments with ends on the sides of the triangle, parallel to its sides, pass through one point and have the same length x. Find x if the side lengths of the triangle are a, b, c.

On figure 1 there are three shaded triangles similar to ABC, each of which has one side of length x.

Consider the sides of three shaded triangles parallel to side AC of length b; Let's denote the lengths of these sides by b₁, b₂ and b₃. Note that the sum of their lengths is 2b (b = |AC|): b₁ + b₂ + b₃ = 2b, so $\frac{b_1}{b} + \frac{b_2}{b} + \frac{b_3}{b} = 2$ is the sum of the similarity coefficients of these triangles equal to two. Therefore, $\frac{x}{a} + \frac{x}{b} + \frac{x}{c} = 2$, from where

$$x = \frac{2abc}{ab + ac + bc}.$$

A. Yagulchi
