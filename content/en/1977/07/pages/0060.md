It is known that cause-and-effect relationships are closely related to space-time relationships. The latter were briefly touched upon by Prutkov in the aphorism:
Shelks the mare in the nose - she hits the tail.
Problems of cause-effect relationships are vigorously discussed by physicists in connection with the hypothesis of the existence of taxins - particles moving in the void at superluminal speed. It turns out that the possibility of such movements is not rejected by the special relative theory (and its creation). The difficulties here lie precisely in the problem of cause and effect.
One might think that it was the Taxins who stood in the mind's eye of Kozma Prutkov when he formulated a deep aphorism, which we recently found in his notebook and expressed to him in sonorous brass:
«Cogitatu, ergo sunt»,
that is
“Thinkable means they exist.”
It is appropriate to recall that Prutkov highly valued Descartes, most pityingly, known to a wide circle of readers for his consonant aphorism:
«Cogito, ergo sum»,
that is
“Thought means I exist.”
