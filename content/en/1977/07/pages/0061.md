⟦folio 59⟧

Prutkov's newly discovered forism better solves not only the problem of taxes, but also provides a solid scientific foundation for the issues of financing the costly foundations of a single magnetic charge - the Dirac monopole, quarks and other objects.

Astronomy

Human! He raised his gaze from earth to heaven - what an amazing order we see there!

Comment. For the sake of objectivity, it should be noted that the fruits of K. Prutkov’s thoughts did not always lead to the correct conclusion, and sometimes give rise to false consequences. Thus, the above observation about the arrangement of the stars in a “surprisingly worthy order” (quite fair and subtle) apparently served as the reason for the following statement (belonging to an unknown theologian): “The divine origin of all things follows from the fact that any three stars that are not located on the same straight line occupy the vertices of a triangle.”

Quantum mechanics

When throwing stones into the water, look at the circles they form; otherwise such throwing will be empty fun.

Comment. It is well known that in 1924, the French physicist Louis de Broglie proposed that the movement of a body of mass m with speed v can be associated with a wave process characterized by wavelength λ. The connection between these complementary characteristics of a physical object (corpuscular and wave) is expressed by the famous de Broglie formula: λ = h/mv (h is Planck’s constant). It seems undoubted that it was these messages that occupied K. Prutkov in his experimental research, which was reflected in the commented phorism. An unbiased mind, armed with knowledge of modern quantum mechanics, cannot fail to see in this phorism a direct anticipation of the independently expressed views of de Broglie. The forism of Kozma Prutkov also contains an acknowledgment of further reflections on the stone-circular (read - corpuscular-wave) parallel, perhaps, to the conditions of the French scientist.
