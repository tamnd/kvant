New books

In this issue we publish annotations on books on mathematics and physics available to schoolchildren, published in the second quarter of 1977. Most of them can be purchased through specialized “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"

1. Ezhov I. I., Skorod A. V., Yadrenko M. I., Elements of combinatorics. Translation from Ukrainian. Volume 6 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 85 kopecks.

Combinatorics is one of the branches of discrete mathematics, which has acquired important significance in connection with its use in probability theory, mathematical logic, number theory, computer technology, and cybernetics. Knowledge of combinatorics must be presented to a variety of specialties - physicists, chemists, biologists, linguists, code theory specialists.

This book outlines the basic concepts of combinatorics and methods for solving combinatorial problems. In this case, the concept of set and operations on sets are systematically used, since most of all can be formulated as problems in the theory of finite sets. Particularly noteworthy are the method of arbitrary functions and the method of trajectories. These methods are important in themselves, since they are used not only in combinatorics, but also in other areas of modern mathematics.

The book is intended for high school students. The book is based on lectures given to students at the Ukrainian Correspondence School of Physics and Mathematics.

Publishing house "Mir"

2. Lindgren G. Entertaining cutting tasks. Translation from English. Volume 12 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 85 kopecks.

Among the “mathematical entertainments,” tasks involving cutting and folding functions of a traditional activity have a place of honor. The author of this book, Harry Lindgren, is not a professional mathematician, he is just a humble employee of the patent office in Canbert (Australia); and geometric cutting is his hobby. At first glance, the tasks collected and invented by Lindgren may seem endlessly diverse. However, most of them use only a few types of cuts (as a rule, these are those with the help of which one can obtain another from one parallelogram). And it turns out that there are more or less standard principles that allow you to find the right solution. This is how we accept and connect Lindgren’s book.

The book is based on the problem of equal areas and equal areas of physical equalities, known since ancient times. For flat polygons, equivalence is also a sufficient condition for their equivalence - this fact was established back in 1832 by the Hungarian Farkas Boya and a year later by the German office worker Gerwin. However, in the case of spatial polygons, this is no longer the case: in 1900, Max Denip, a student of the famous David Hilbert, showed that the equivalence of polygons is not a sufficient condition for their equivalence, and indicates additional conditions necessary for the equivalence of two equivalent polygons. (In 1974, Hadwiger’s student Jean-Pierre Sidler proved that the necessary conditions formulated by M. Dem are also sufficient.)

The problem associated with cutting problems still attracts the attention of prominent mathematicians; many related problems have not yet been solved. However, in this book all constructions are carried out mainly for plane polygons and thus fit within the framework of the Boye-Gerwin theorem formulated above. The subject of the book is essentially one problem: it is required to cut a given flat function into the possible number of parts from which it is possible to add another flat function, equal in size to the first. The requirement for a minimum number of parts is essential here - in all cases, breaking the function into fewer parts is best.

Geometric cutting tasks are far from being exhausted. The technique described by Lindgren is not at all mandatory. Anyone who is communicative enough and takes the time will always find a worthy cutting opening. So this book presents the reader with a wide range of personal creativity. “Challenge your cutting skills,” Lindgren writes. “I hope this book will convince you that such a pastime is not too tedious and not at all trivial.”

Physics
Publishing house "Science"

1. China's urban A.I. Order and disorder in the world of amoms. 5th edition, rev. and additional Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 33 kopecks.

⟦folio 91⟧

The book is a popular presentation of the doctrine of the structure of matter - from the simplest molecules to the most complex biological systems. Authored an original and unique approach to this larger topic. Issues of atomic structure are considered as manifestations of order and disorder in the world of atoms. This approach is very fruitful, since along with bodies for which the words “order” or “disorder” are an exact characteristic of the arrangement of particles in them, there are often bodies in which order and disorder are inseparable from each other. The problem of the structure of biological substances - mucules, tissues, cells, not to mention the molecules of proteins and nucleic acids, is an interesting illustration of the peculiar order and disorder in living matter.

The book is written very vividly, reads with great interest and is intended for a wide range of readers familiar with the basics of physics.

2. Kapitsa P. L. Experiment, theory, practice. Volume 19 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 89 kopecks.

This book contains the reports of Academician P. L. Kapitsa, made by him in various years to a wide audience.

Some of these speeches contain a popular presentation of the experiments he conducted, the other part is devoted to a presentation of the activities of the distinguished scientists, and an analysis of their scientific creativity is given.

Most of the speeches published in the book are devoted to the issues of creative education of youth, training and selection of scientific personnel.

The book also includes speeches and articles devoted to the organization of science, strengthening its connection with practice, prospects for the development of science, problems of the relationship between man and nature, and the struggle for peace and progress.

The book will be of interest to a wide range of readers.

Publishing house
"Graduate School"

3. Milkovskaya L. B. Let's repeat the physics. 3rd edition, revised. and additional Volume 30 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 1 rub. 09 k.

This manual is intended for applicants. It is compiled in full accordance with the physics program for applicants to universities. Each chapter is devoted to one of the topics of the program. Along with coverage of this topic, it contains examples of solving the problem, tasks for independent solutions and questions for repetition.

The book will be useful for all individuals independently preparing to enter universities.

Publishing house
"Education"

4. Levitan E. P. Astrophysics for schoolchildren. Volume 8 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 25 kopecks.

In an interesting and accessible form for students, the author talks about modern problems in astrophysics. The book touches on a wide range of astrophysical phenomena and objects: the Sun, stars, galaxies, quasars, planets.

The book is extremely enlivened by a large number of problems and questions intended for independent solutions.

5. School astrophysical calendar for the 1977/78 academic year. Compiled by M. M. Dageev. Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.

The calendar contains basic information about the Sun, Moon, planets, stars and other celestial objects, as well as reference data necessary for independent decisions in the 1977/78 academic year.

The book is intended primarily for astrophysical circles.

"Atomizat"

6. Pogodin S. L., Libman E. P. How a modern approach was added. Edition 2nd, add. Volume 18 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

Based on the richest archival materials, the book provides a lively and entertaining history of the creation of the domestic radio industry.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

7. Blokhintsev D.P. The birth of a peaceful atom. Volume 5 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.

The author of the book is a prominent scientist in the field of nuclear physics, Hero of Socialist Labor Dmitry Ivanovich Blyukhintsev - since the creation of the interesting Institute of Nuclear Research in Dubna (from 1956 to 1965). Now he heads the laboratory of theoretical physics at this institute. The name of D.I. Blyukhintsev is associated with the creation of the world's first nuclear power plant. In the 50s, as director of the Institute of Physics and Energy, the author of the book supervised the design and construction of the world's first nuclear power plant. From the position of a direct observer, he talks about this great event, about the first attempts at the peaceful use of nuclear energy, about the first peaceful atomic construction.

The book is written in lively language and takes the reader to a difficult and exciting time, to a society of interesting people and exciting scientific problems. The reader himself will feel the atmosphere of focused creative fun and experience the joy of a winner.

The book is designed for a wide range of readers and will be of interest to both specialists and schoolchildren.
