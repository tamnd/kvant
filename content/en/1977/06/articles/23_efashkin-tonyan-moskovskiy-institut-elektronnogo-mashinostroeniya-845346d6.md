Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering
Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering (MIEM) is one of the youngest universities in Moscow. Its creation is a consequence of the rapid development of radio electronics, high-voltage and electromagnetic instrumentation, computer technology and mathematical support for modern computers, which required a large number of specialties combining deep engineering knowledge with excellent physical and mathematical training.

MIEM has the following faculties: semiconductor and electrical engineering, automation and computer engineering, radio engineering, applied mathematics, evening and two special faculties of applied mathematics and automated control systems.

MIEM also has a preparatory department, where classes are conducted on a full-time basis. At the preparatory department, advanced work and those demonized from the ranks of the Soviet Army study for 8 months. All students in the preparatory department receive the same scholarship as first-year students, and upon successful passing of the final exams, they are enrolled in the 1st year of the institute without additional exams.

A characteristic feature of the training of engineers at MIEM is the combination of the functional and applied nature of education, which provides for high physical and mathematical training.

Naturally, this consistency is taken into account when selecting applicants for offensive units.
