Moscow State Pedagogical Institute named after. V. I. Lenina

Moscow Order of Lenin and Order of the Red Banner of Labor State Pedagogical Institute named after. V.I. Lenin (MGPI) is the country's senior higher pedagogical educational institution. The Institute was created on the basis of the Higher Hen Courses, organized in Moscow in 1872, and below is the largest educational, pedagogical and research center in the public education system.

The Faculty of Mathematics of Moscow State Pedagogical Institute has 7 departments: mathematical analysis, algebra, geometry, number theory, computational mathematics and programming, methods of teaching mathematics, and physics. Over the years, students study mathematical analysis, algebra and number theory, geometry, computational mathematics and programming, theory of analytical functions, control of mathematical physics, probability theory, mathematical logic, general physics and theoretical mechanics, astronomy, methods of teaching mathematics, pedagogy, psychology and a whole range of other special general educational and socio-economic disciplines. In addition, students, of their choice, take special courses on a variety of issues of modern mathematics and participate in special mathematical and methodological seminars.

Students master the skill of teaching mathematics in high school, the ability to pass on the fundamentals of this science, their love and passion for mathematics to the next generation in classes on methods of teaching mathematics and in teaching practice with the best teachers in Moscow schools.

In the computer center of the Faculty of Mathematics of the Moscow State Pedagogical Institute, students get acquainted with modern computer technology and acquire skills in working on the most modern electronic embroidered machines. The faculty also provides great opportunities for students' scientific activities. The scientific student society involves students in research work in mathematics, on the issues of teaching mathematics and programming in high school. The best works of students are celebrated at public events, city and weighty reviews of student scientific works.

The Physics Faculty of Moscow State Pedagogical Institute trains physics teachers for secondary schools in the specialty: physics and astronomy.

The faculty consists of 6 departments: general and experimental physics, theoretical physics, solid state physics, methods of teaching physics, mathematical physics and general technical disciplines.

In addition to the main courses, all departments teach a variety of special courses, organizational work in special practices, scientific circles and problem laboratories.
