Moscow Institute
steel and alloys
The Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys has existed for its entire existence as the metallurgical department of the Moscow City Academy, created on the directive of V.I. Lenin by decree of the Council of People's Commissars of September 4, 1918. Now the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys of the Order of the Red Banner of Labor is the leading metallurgical and metallurgical university in the country. It trains engineering and scientific personnel for ferrous and non-ferrous metallurgy, as well as for a number of new branches of science and technology.

In our institute, everyone who is passionate about mathematics, physics, chemistry will find a specialty to their liking, or metallurgy today is connected in the darkest way with these functional sciences. We train specialists in the field of metallurgy of ferrous, non-ferrous, rare, radioactive metals, high-temperature materials, diamonds and super-hard alloys, physics of metals, physical-chemical research methods, semiconductor technology, economics and cybernetics of metallurgical processes.

The Faculty of Physics and Chemistry is of particular interest to our readers. Let's talk about it in a little more detail.

The development of technology places ever higher and more diverse demands on metal materials. For example, you need alloys that operate at temperatures close to absolute zero and exceeding thousands of degrees, or magnetic alloys that are strongly magnetized or easily demagnetized. Recently, it has become especially important to acquire superconducting alloys, which are already used to create strong magnetic fields, and in the future - to transmit electronic energy over long distances.

To solve various problems associated with the creation and study of new materials, specialists are needed with knowledge of both technology and physics and mathematics at the level of graduates of university physics departments. Such specialists are trained by the Faculty of Physics and Chemistry of the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys (MISIS).

Nine departments of the faculty supervise the training of engineers in the following areas of specialization:

1) physics of metals (specialization: study of the electronic and atomic structure of metals, metal physics of high-strength alloys, precision alloys, superconducting materials);

2) physics and chemical research of metallurgical processes (specialization: cybernetics of metallurgical production, high-temperature materials, corrosion and protection of metals, metallurgy of ferrous metals, production of artificial diamonds and superhard materials);

3) metal science, equipment and technological processing of metals.

The Institute of Solid State Physics of the USSR Academy of Sciences participates in the training of specialists in metal physics, where students conduct research work and where a number of special courses are taught.

Research work of students in departments begins from the first year, and from the fourth year special time is allocated in the academic schedule for participation in research work. When performing this work, students master the methods of modern experiment (nuclear spectroscopy, electron parametric resonance, pentgen and electron-optical analyses, internal friction, study of mechanical, thermodynamic and other properties of materials in a wide temperature range, etc.).

Graduates of the Faculty of Physics and Chemistry are sent to work in research and design organizations and in factory laboratories.

The high level of mathematical preparation of our students is evidenced by their convincing victory at the VI Moscow City Optical Competition “Student and Scientific and Technical Program” (1st place in the preparation of leading technical universities in Moscow).
