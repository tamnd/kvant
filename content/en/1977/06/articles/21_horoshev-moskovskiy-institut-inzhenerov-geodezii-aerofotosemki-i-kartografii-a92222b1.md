Moscow Institute of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartography Engineers

Human economic activity is technically connected with the natural features of the Earth, so everyone needs good maps. Using the map, you can quickly get acquainted with the area of ​​interest to us, with its landscape, location of natural deposits and industrial facilities. The map also plays a great role in the defense of the country.

But you can't create a good map. Creation of a map of communication technology with a number of special sciences, primarily geodesy, aerial photography and cartography. Nowadays these sciences are unthinkable without modern equipment. Research into outer space and the depths of the ocean is carried out with instruments, the main part of which is optical information processing tools, operating both in the visible and invisible parts of the spectrum. In this regard, an important role is played by optical instrumentation, whose task includes the development, design and manufacture of high-precision optical instruments, aerial photographs, optical and laser rangefinders, gyroscopic devices for determining the direction of the true meridiana, etc.

Specialists for the needs of cartography are trained by one of the oldest institutes in the country - the Moscow Institute of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartography Engineers. It was founded on May 14, 1779, when, under the Moscow International Specialization, specialties: marine geodesy), applied geodesy, space geodesy; aerial photogeodetic with a specialty in aerial photogeodesy; cartographic with a specialty in cartography (specialties: design and compilation of maps, map publication); optical-mechanical, training broad-profile specialties in optical and optical-electronic devices; correspondence faculty with specialties: applied geodesy, aerial photogeodesy, cartography; evening optical-mechanical faculty.
