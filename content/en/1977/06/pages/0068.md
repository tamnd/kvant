Aftermath
Since Nikolai Nosov wrote the wonderful poem “Vitya Maleev at school and at home,” the teaching of mathematics at school has changed a lot. Now the subject is called mathematics, and not argumentation, and its content has also changed. The current third or fourth grader will no longer agonize over the problem of nuts. He will denote the number of nuts the girl has by “x”, and then the boy will have “2x” nuts. Thus, there were “3x” nuts in total, and it is known that there were 120 of them. This means that 3x = 120, hence x = 40, 2x = 80. Solution problem.

As for the problem of “saws and axes,” the almighty “x” can come to the rescue here too. Let us recall the final form of the problem, which it took after simplification:
“12 axes and 3 saws cost 84 rubles, 12 axes and 5 saws cost 100 rubles.” Let us denote the cost of the saw by “x”, then from the first condition 12 axes cost 84 - 3x rubles, and one ax (84 - 3x): 12 rubles. Let us now write the second condition:
$$12 \cdot (84 - 3x) : 12 + 5x = 100$$
or
$$84 - 3x + 5x = 100,$$
whence x = 8 (rubles). Again, the standard method comes to the rescue with SEL, which leads to the goal without much thought.

However, don’t you think that Vanya Pakhomov’s solution to the city is more beautiful? She captivates us with the simplicity and elegance of logical reasoning, leading to the solution of a problem. Try solving problems not only in the standard way (using an equation), but also simply using logical reasoning. Write to us which problems from textbooks for grades 3-5 are easier to solve using logic than using an equation.
