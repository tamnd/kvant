M446. A circle of radius 1 rolls on the outside of a circle of radius $\sqrt{2}$. At the initial moment of time, the point of contact of the circles is marked with sticky red paint. When rolling, the shown points of one and the other circle of the new ones paint the points from which they touch (Fig. 1). How many different points of the stationary circle will be dirty by the time the movable circle makes 100 revolutions around the stationary circle?

M447*. In an acute triangle $ABC$, the segments $BO$ and $CO$ (where $O$ is the circumcenter) are extended until they intersect at points $D$ and $E$ on the sides $AC$ and $BC$ of the triangle. It's a shame that it's $\widehat{BDE} = 50^\circ$ and $\widehat{CED} = 30^\circ$. Find the angles of the triangle $ABC$ and prove the equalities $|AE| = |ED|$, $|CE| = |CB|$, $|CD| = |CO|$.

M448*. Prove that the centers of all ellipses inscribed in a given quadrilateral lie on a line passing through the midpoints of the diagonals of this quadrilateral.

Isaac Newton

M449. a) $n$ identical balls move along one straight line. What is the maximum number of collisions between them that can occur?

b) * The same question for three balls of masses $m_1$, $m_2$ and $m_3$.

c) * Try to prove that if $n$ different balls move along the same straight line, then the total number of balls between them is finite.

(In these problems, the balls are considered as material points that collide with each other absolutely urgo, i.e., with the conservation of the total momentum and energy, and it is assumed that all occurring collisions are only pairs of e: three or more balls do not end up at the same point at the same time.)
