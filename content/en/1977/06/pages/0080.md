introductory
university exams
in 1976

Leningradsky
state university
them. A. A. Zhdanova

Leningrad State University was described in detail in «Kvant» No. 6 for 1975 and No. 4 for 1976. In this issue we present options for the written entrance exam in mathematics at various faculties of Leningrad State University in 1976.

Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes

1. A $p$ beam of some information ($6um$ is a unit of information quantity) is first entered into a cybernetic device, then this information is processed by the cybernetic device according to a given program, and then the resulting new information is output to a printing device. Determine the shortest possible duration of operation of a cybernetic device by example, processing and issuing information, if it is known that the speed of information processing is equal to $c$ beam/sec and 3 times less than the sum of the speeds of input and output of information, and the amount of information at the input and output is the same.

2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{2(x - \sqrt{x^2 - a^2})} > \frac{x + a}{5\sqrt{x - a}}$$
for all values ​​of the parameter $a$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x + \sqrt{\sin x + \sin 2x - \cos x} = \cos x.$$

4. Given an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with sides of length $a$. On sides $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ segments $AA', BB'$ and $CC'$ of equal length are laid out in directions $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ so that the area of the triangle $A'B'C'$ is $k$ times less than the area of the triangle $ABC$. Find the lengths of the indicated segments.

5. Given a circle of radius $R$ with center at point $O$ and its diameter $AB$. Through the point $P$, lying between $O$ and $A$, a chord $CD$ is drawn, perpendicular to diameter $AB$. It is known that the sum of the surface areas obtained by rotating the radius $OC$ around diameter, and the spherical segment obtained by rotating the arc $AC$, is equal to the area of ​​the spherical segment obtained by rotating the arc $CB$. Find $|OP|$.

Faculty of Physics

1. The lunar cart is approaching a cliff, and at the initial moment of time its speed is $v$, acceleration $a$ ($a \geqslant 0$), distance to the volume $S$ (counting along the straight line along which the cart is moving). After what time, counting from the initial moment, must a command be given from the Earth to turn on the brakes (in this case, the acceleration of the cart becomes equal to $a_1$) so that the cart stops at the edge of the volume? The distance from the Earth to the Moon is $L$, the speed of propagation of the radio signal is $c$.

2. In a regular quadrangular pair $ABCDH$, the length of the side of the base is $a$, the side edge $b$, a plane is drawn parallel to the diagonal of the base $AC$ and the edge $DH$. Find the maximum possible area of ​​the figure resulting from the primacy of this plane.

3. Find the roots of the equation
$$2(\sin x - \cos x + 1) \times
\times \left(2\cos^2 \frac{x}{2} + \sin x \cdot \mathrm{tg} x - 1\right) +
\sin \frac{x}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{x}{2} = 0,$$
lying in the interval $|x| < 10$.

4. Solve inequality
$$\log_{x+2}(x^2 - 2x + p) \geqslant 2$$
for all values ​​of the parameter $p$.

5. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases}
x + \frac{1}{y} = 1, \\
xy^2 + \frac{1}{x^2y} = 1 + \frac{y}{x}.
\end{cases}$$

Chemical, psychological and economic faculties

1. At the initial moment of using the galvanic cell, one of the internal electrodes contained 20 e of pure rutinium. As a result, recovery during the first day was 5% less compared to the percentage increase during the second day. Determine the percentage increase in pure rutiny during the first day, if it is known that during the second day the amount of pure rutiny is 1.648 e.

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_a(x - 1) + \log_a x > 2.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\sin 2x}{2} = \frac{\sin^3 x + 3\cos^3 x}{\sin x + 3\cos x}.$$

4. Given a triangle $ABC$ with sides $|BC| = a$, $|AC| = b$, $|AB| = c$. Through the point $D$, lying on the side $AB$, straight lines are drawn parallel to the sides $BC$ and $AC$; $E$ and $F$ are the points of intersection of these lines with sides $AC$ and $BC$. It is known that $|DE| = |DF|$. Find $|AD|$.
