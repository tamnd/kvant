It is not difficult to report that the roots of equation (6) will be those and only those roots of the equation

$$3 + \sqrt{x} = (3 - x)^2,$$

(7)

which satisfy the inequality $0 \leq x \leq 3$, that is, $x \in [0; 3]$. Taking the equation (7) together, a more general equation with the parameter

$$a + \sqrt{x} = (a - x)^2,$$

(8)

coinciding with equation (7) at $a = 3$. By writing equation (8) as a quadratic relative $a$:

$$a^2 - a(2x + 1) + (x^2 - \sqrt{x}) = 0,$$

We easily get two equations:

$$x + \sqrt{x} + 1 - a = 0, \quad x - \sqrt{x} - a = 0,$$

or, assuming $a = 3$,

$$x + \sqrt{x} - 2 = 0, \quad x - \sqrt{x} - 3 = 0.$$

Having solved these equations and displaying those roots that satisfy the condition $x \in [0; 3]$, we find the root of equation (6): $x = 1$.
