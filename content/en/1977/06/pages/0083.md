Mathematics

Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\sin x + \sin 2x + \sin 3x = 1 + \cos x + \cos 2x.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$9^{1/x} = 6^{1/x} + 2 \cdot 4^{1/x}.$$
3. Solve inequality
$$\log_{1/3}[(x + 3)(x - 5)] > -2.$$
4. The ratio of the area of ​​the lateral surface of a regular triangular straight line to the area of ​​its base is $k$. Find the angle between the side edge and the height of the straight line.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\cos 2x - 3 \cos x = 4 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2}.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$7^{\lg x} - 5^{\lg x + 1} = 3 \cdot 5^{\lg x - 1} - 13 \cdot 7^{\lg x - 1}.$$
3. Solve inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{\sqrt{x + 2}} < 3^{-x}.$$
4. Find the ratio of the surface area and volume of the sphere corresponding to the total surface area and volume of the cone circumscribed around it with an equilateral triangle in the axial section.

Option 3
1. Solve the equation
$$2 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2} + \cos 2x = 1.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$2 \lg \lg x = \lg (3 - 2 \lg x).$$
3. Solve inequality
$$3^{2x + 5} \leq 3^{x + 2} + 2.$$
4. In a regular truncated quadrilateral rectangle: length $d$ of the diagonal, magnitude $\alpha$ of the dihedral angle at the lower base and length $H$ of the height. Find the volume of a truncated rectilinear.

Physics

1. The motorcyclist traveled 0.4 of the way between two cities at a speed of 72 km/h, and the rest of the way at a speed of 54 km/h. Determine the average speed of the motoriclist.
2. A freely falling body traveled the last 10 m in 0.25 sec. Determine the height of the fall and the speed at the last moment of the fall.
3. Determine the tension of the cable during a uniformly accelerated lowering of the elevator car with a mass of 400 kHz, if in 10 seconds it covered a distance of 30 m.
4. An ice floe floats in the sea, part of which with a volume of 195 m³ is above the water. Determine the volume of the entire ice floe and its underwater part. The density of ice is 0.9 e/cm³, the density of sea water is 1.03 e/cm³.
5. A bullet of mass 9 e, flying horizontally at a speed of 400 m/sec, pierces a log 30 cm thick and flies out of it at a speed of 100 m/sec. What is the average force of resistance to the movement of a bullet in a log?
6. What is the density of nitrogen at a temperature of 0 °C and a pressure of 10⁵ h/m²?
7. Determine the speed of an electron passing through an accelerating potential difference of 300 b. The initial speed of the electron is assumed to be zero. Electron mass $m = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kts, charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ kts.

The Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys has existed for its entire existence as the metallurgical department of the Moscow City Academy, created on the directive of V.I. Lenin by decree of the Council of People's Commissars of September 4, 1918. Now the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys of the Order of the Red Banner of Labor is the leading metallurgical and metallurgical university in the country.
