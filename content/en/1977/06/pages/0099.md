ARRANGE THE NUMBERS

In the circles of the figure depicted on figure, it is necessary to place the numbers from 1 to 24 so that the sum of the numbers in the two blue circles on each blue line is equal to 25, the sum of the numbers on each red line is equal to 50, and the sum of the numbers at the vertices of each of its identical squares marked with a green star is also equal to 50.

Try to find at least one such arrangement of numbers.
