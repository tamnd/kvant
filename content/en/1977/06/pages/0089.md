An hour after this, a second courier left $A$ on a motorcycle, who, navbar and giving him an order, immediately moved back at the same speed and returned to $A$ at the moment at which the first courier reached $B$. $$\sin x \cdot \sin 3x = \frac{3}{2} - 2\cos 2x.$$

Determine the length of the chord $CD$.

Physics
1. The radius of Mars is 0.53 the radius of the Earth, and its mass is 0.11 the mass of the Earth. Determine how many times the force of gravity on the surface of Mars is less than the force of gravity on Earth.
2. An ice floe of constant thickness floats in the water, protruding above the surface by $a = 2\text{ cm}$. What is the mass of the ice floe if its area is $S = 150\text{ cm}^2$? Ice density $\rho = 0,92\text{ e/cm}^3$.
3. A ball of mass $m = 200\text{ e}$, suspended on a thread of length $l = 20\text{ cm}$, is deflected from the equilibrium position so that the thread makes an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with the vertical. The ball imparted a speed $|\vec{v}| = 2\text{ m/ сек}$ in the direction perpendicular to the thread. What should the thread be like so that the ball does not break it when moving?
4. A bullet of mass $m = 10\text{ e}$ hits a wooden block $M = 990\text{ e}$, suspended by a length of thread, and gets stuck in it. Determine the speed of the block immediately after the bullet hits it, if the speed of the bullet is $|\vec{v}_0| = 500\text{ m/ сек}$. What part of the kinetic energy of the bullet transferred to the body?
5. A closed cylinder of volume $V = 1\text{ л}$ is divided into two parts by a movable weightless piston. In one part of the cylinder there is a gas at temperature $t_1 = 0^\circ\text{C}$, in the other - what is the mass of this gas at temperature $t_2 = 100^\circ\text{C}$. Determine the volumes $V_1$ and $V_2$ of parts of the cylinder when the piston is in equilibrium. The piston and walls of the vessel are tello-proprinic.
6. Two charges, being in the air at a distance $r_1 = 10\text{ cm}$, act on each other with a force $|\vec{F}_1| = 56\text{ дин},$ and in some non-conducting liquid at a distance $r_2 = 5\text{ cm}$ with a force $|\vec{F}_2| = 4\text{ дин}.$ What is the dielectric constant in the liquid?
7. Find the internal resistance of the generator if it is known that the power squeezed out in the external circuit is the same for two values ​​of external resistance: $R_1 = 5\text{ ом}$ and $R_2 = 0,2\text{ ом}$.
8. In one magnetic field, the inductance of which is $|\vec{B}| = 1\text{ m}\lambda$, we find the area of ​​the turns in a plane $S = 10^{-2}\text{ m}^2$, located perpendicular to the magnetic lines. Turn resistance $R = 0,2\text{ ом}$. What charge will flow through the turn if the field disappears?
9. The distances from the object to the lens and from the lens to the image are the same and equal to $a = 0,4\text{ м}$. How will the position and size of the image change if the object is at a distance $l = 20\text{ cm}$ in the direction from the lens?
