New books

We continue to publish summaries of books on mathematics and physics that are accessible and interesting to our readers, which will be published this year. Given the numerous requests, we will also place annotations on some books published by the Molodaya Gvardiya and Children's Literature publishing houses.
In this issue we will talk about books published in the first quarter of 1977. Most of them can be purchased through specialized “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. M. Gardner. Mathematics of studies and lanes, trans. from English Edition Z-e, stereotype. Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The name of Gardner, the leading writer of the American popular science magazine Scientific American, seems to be known by a large tool of our readers. This book is the first of his books translated into Russian. It represents an attempt to indicate an area of ​​modern mathematical focus. Most of the material in the book is taken from specialized literature on magic tricks, rather than from entertaining mathematical literature.
For the most part, the author himself does not formulate in the language of the material the patterns underlying his experts. He only describes the actions of the “magician” - overt and secret. But the reader will undoubtedly find it convenient to reconstruct the corresponding algebraic or geometric idea from the author’s explanations. In the most interesting cases, the author's presentation is accompanied by a small editor's note, revealing a mathematical subtriple. These notes are located at the end of the book.

M. Harder's book will be of interest to participants in school mathematics circles, their leader, and an adult mathematics enthusiast.

Publishing house "Mir"
2. G. Freidengel. Mathematics in science and around us (In the world of science and technology). Volume 14 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 95 kopecks.
Hans Freudengel is a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy, the author of more than 200 works on topology, group theory, analysis, geometry, mathematical logic, philosophy and history of science. He is a brilliant popularizer of mathematics.
His present book consists of seven closed chapters.
The first chapter (“Man Measures the Universe”) talks about measuring the world. First - the small world in which we live, our Earth; then the big world, uniting the Sun and the Moon, and finally, the entire vast Universe.
The second chapter (“To Infinity”) begins with a story about living rooms and uncountable sets, about the power of a set, about comparisons, about comparisons of a set, about numbers; the question of whether we treat natural numbers correctly is discussed.
The third chapter begins with a story about the number system, about how the ancient Egyptians, the inhabitants of Babylon, the Greeks, the Europeans of Charlemagne and our contemporaries wrote down numbers. It tells about games that allow a strictly legal theory (in part, the game “Nim” is described), about gambling games, the creation of automatic computing machines, about “thinking” machines that play various games (in part, chess), about machines that translate from one language to another. The question of whether machines can mislead is being discussed.
The fourth chapter - “The ABC of Life” - opens up to the reader a modern, special world - the world of the biological cell.
The fifth chapter (“The art of drawing is bad”) is devoted to topology. Here it is defined what a surface is, a genus of a surface, a variety, a projective plane; what are one-to-one and continuous mappings, degree of mapping. Mappings of a circle and a sphere onto themselves are considered. Finally, a wonderful book is constructed covering the entire square (Peano curve).
In the sixth chapter, the author moves from concrete weights and scales to abstract groups, which are considered using linear programming: I transform the problem, the problem of a traveling salesman and the problem of drawing up a schedule of tablecloths and napkins in a restaurant.
The last chapter is called "World in Beans." It tells about the symmetries of physical functions, about reflections, translations and rotations on a plane and in space. The concept of a group is introduced; it is determined what the author of physics is and what motion is and what motions are in space; defines what orientation is.
What is not changed by the structure of the book is the absence of what is commonly called “technical” in mathematics: the author managed to select topics of mathematical

Publishing house "Vishcha School"

3. G.I. Drinfeld, Quadrature of the circle and transcendence of number. Volume 3 l., circulation 13,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.

This book was published at the end of 1976 in the series “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School”. It talks about the well-known problem of “squaring the circle,” which has been intersected for many centuries since ancient times. The conclusion that this problem is unsolvable belongs to the famous Leonhard Euler; He also suggested that the number n cannot be the root of any algebraic equation with rational coefficients, thereby allowing the existence of transcendental numbers and the transcendence of numbers. Evidence of all these facts is given in the book.

The book is intended for high school students who are interested in mathematics. In addition to presenting the theory, it contains many interesting exercises and tasks.

4. A. A. Belsky, L. A. Kaluzhin. Division with remainder. Volume 5 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.

This book is also from the “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” series. It will develop some important number theories. A proof of the theory of uniqueness of decomposition into prime factors is given, talks about the Euclid algorithm, Diophantine equations, and residues. Examines representations of numbers in various positional number systems.

The book will certainly be of interest to high school students and mathematics teachers.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Grigoriev V.I., Myakishev G.Ya. Strengths in nature. 5th edition. Volume 20 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 83 kopecks.

This book, written in a language that is easy and accessible to schoolchildren, introduces the reader to the world of physical concepts. Considers the decomposed types of interactions in nature, the authors show the unity and dismantled world of physical phenomena.

This book will be read with interest by high school students interested in physics.

2. Seabrook W. Robert Wood is a modern wizard of the physics laboratory. 3rd edition, rev. Volume 16 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 04 k.

The book deals not only with Wood’s purely scientific research, but also with his travels, with solving the mystery of King Tutankhamun’s purple gold, with Wood’s participation in revealing the ideas and disparities between the “inventors” of “N-rays” and “death rays” and other equally interesting things.

The book is intended for independent schoolchildren to expand their knowledge of physics and test them on given tasks that require communication and a lot of attention.

5. Z and gel F. Yu. Lunar horizons. A manual for students. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.

This book is published in the “World of Knowledge” series, it is intended for schoolchildren in grades 8–10. It tells a fascinating story about the movement of the Moon, its origin and nature, as well as the prospects for its exploration by means of astronautics. The author summarizes all the new data about the Moon obtained, in particular, by Soviet lunar rovers and American cosmonautics.

6. Temko S.V., Soloviev G.A., Milantev V.P. Physics revealing the mines of the Earth. Volume 7 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 25 kopecks.

The authors, in an interesting and accessible form for secondary school students, talk about modern physical methods of studying the Earth and exploring mineral deposits. Readers will find in the book information about the application in geophysics of physical phenomena and patterns known from the school physics course.
