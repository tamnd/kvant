B) Listing the rules for silt parquet flooring.

A parquet is a partition of a plane into polygons in which every two polygons either do not intersect, or have exactly one common vertex, or have a common side, and the union of the sides of all polygons is a flat ornament. A parquet is called regular if all the polygons of the partition are regular (possibly with a different number of sides) and any vertex of the parquet can be transferred to any other vertex by some translation that maps the entire parquet onto itself. The partitions A) and B) depicted in figure 10 are not parquets at all in the sense defined above; partitions C) and D) are parquets, but not regular ones, and, finally, partitions D) and E) are regular parquets (explain).

Problem 3. Find all possible regular parquets.

This problem was already formed in «Kvant»: in the article by A. N. Kolmogorov “Parquets from regular polygons” («Kvant», 1970, No. 3). This article outlines a possible approach to listing all the correct parquet floors. We report the answer: the number of different, up to similarity, regular parquets is 11.

Tasks for our readers

Prove the following relations between the elements of an acute triangle ABC:

$$\frac{|C_0A_0| \cdot |A_0B_0|}{h_a^2} + \frac{|A_0B_0| \cdot |B_0C_0|}{h_b^2} + \frac{|B_0C_0| \cdot |A_0C_0|}{h_c^2} = 1,$$

where $A_0, B_0$ and $C_0$ are the bases of the heights omitted from the vertices $A, B, C$, respectively; $h_a, h_b, h_c$ are the lengths of the corresponding heights.
