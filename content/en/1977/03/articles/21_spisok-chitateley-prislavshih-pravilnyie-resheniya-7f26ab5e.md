To the article “Ormenents”
(to exercises)

1. For "order 4" figures there are four variables: parallel translation and three rotations. For figures of “order 2” - also four; two of them are rotations or translations, and the other two are sliding symmetry (see note on p. 25).

2. a), b), d), e), g), h) - parallel transfers; b) and e) - turns by 180°.

3. Figure 1, a and 1, b - ornaments of type VIII, figure 1, b - type IV, 1, f - type III, 1, e - type V.

Figure 2, a and 2, b - ornaments of type II, figure 2, b - ornaments of type III.

4. All ornaments have axes of sliding symmetry, in which, among the generating variables, lateral symmetry is also built in (for some ornaments, the axes of sliding symmetry are exactly the axes of basic symmetry).
