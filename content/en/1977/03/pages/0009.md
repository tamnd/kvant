Sleepless nights, excessive work and herbal stress will affect the health of the young scientist. He falls ill and spends about a year in various treatments. He is forbidden to read, write, or engage in any mental activity. This was the most painful period in the life of A. G. Stoletov. Finally, a council of professors allows him to attend classes with students. And immediately all the doctors’ instructions to take care of your health are forgotten, Alexander Grigorievich once again completely devotes himself to pedagogical and scientific activities.

At that time, Moscow University, like other higher educational institutions in Russia, did not have a physics laboratory. So that scientific research, Russian scientists were forced to study abroad, A. G. Stoletov set himself the goal of creating such a laboratory. He writes letters, petitions, knocks on the thresholds of bureaucratic offices, proves that the university cannot but have its own laboratory. After all, physics is an experimental science. A physicist is not a mathematician; he cannot create science only at his desk.

The entire year 1870 was spent in efforts to build the first physical laboratory in Russia. In the same year, students Aleksandra Grigorievich and his workmate began to gather at Stoletov’s apartment to service various physical problems. A physics circle emerges, which laid the foundation for the Stoletov physics school, which, in turn, gave rise to the school of Russian physicists.
