⟦folio 55⟧

Cats and dogs

When smart dogs and three cunning cats, after mutual contacts, found themselves on opposite areas of the square (see figure) and began to solve a very important problem for both sides: how they could fit in places with each other, but at the same time, so that no new fights would arise, not only not meet with each other, but not even end up in neighboring areas.

As a result, the following strategy was chosen: dogs and cats sit on areas, but from time to time either a cat or a dog runs along the alley to the neighboring area.

The cats believe that with their joint efforts, in 32 runs (theirs and the dogs' runs together), they will be able to fit in places with the dogs. The dogs don't agree with them.

Who's right?

L. Mochalov
