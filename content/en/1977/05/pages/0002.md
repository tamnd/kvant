⟦folio 36⟧

PASCAL'S SNAILS

In this figure you see a family of curves called "Pascal's snails". Each of them is obtained as follows. Let's draw a circle of a certain radius on the plane and use a circle cut out of paper of the same radius “with a handle” - a bar in which holes are made. We will roll the circle outside along the drawn new circle without slipping, and insert a pencil into one of the holes in the handle. Then the pencil will outline just Pascal's snail.

The red line at figure is a cardionade, which is one of Pascal's snails (the pencil was inserted into a point on the circumference of a moving circle).

Other methods of constructing and properties of Pascal's snail are described on p. 36.
