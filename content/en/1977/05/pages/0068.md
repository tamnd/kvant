To our readers

The subscription continues for 1977 for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant»

«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 5–10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to study exact sciences on a server in the future.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren whose interest in the exact sciences still lingers.

Review articles are published on the pages of our journal, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still awaiting solution; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are born.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems from various Olympiads and simply interesting problems.

The “Laboratory «Kvant»” section talks about educational experiments that can be done at home.

What questions and tasks can an applicant expect during the entrance exams? The reader will find answers to these and many other questions that one might encounter when applying to a university in the “Applicant Practice” section.

Our magazine is also useful for the reader. Fundamental changes have now been made in school courses in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» is trying its best to highlight these changes on its pages, publishing an article on the new program.

In 1977, «Kvant» opened a new column: “On the pages of school textbooks.” The articles in this section break down the most important issues of mathematics studied in school.

In 1977, a significant expansion of «Kvant» for younger students.

The magazine constantly publishes solutions to books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
