1. Let us write down the laws of conservation of energy and momentum:
$$\frac{m_1|\vec{v}_1|^2}{2} + \frac{m_2|\vec{v}_2|^2}{2} = Q,$$
$$m_1|\vec{v}_1| = m_2|\vec{v}_2|$$
(index “1” refers to the neutron, and “2” to the beryllium nucleus). From here
$$E_1 = \frac{m_1|\vec{v}_1|^2}{2} = \frac{Qm_2}{m_1+m_2} = \frac{7}{8}Q = 2,95 \text{ Мэв},$$
$$E_2 = Q - E_1 = 0,42 \text{ Мэв}.$$

2. From the heat balance equation
$$Q = C_V \frac{m}{\mu}(T_1 - T)$$
and ideal gas equations of state
$$pV = \frac{m}{\mu}RT \text{ и } p_1V = \frac{m}{\mu}RT_1$$
Let's find the pressure established in the vessel:
$$p_1 = p\left(1 + \frac{RQ}{C_V pV}\right) \approx 2p = 1520 \text{ мм рм. cm.}$$

3. Let us denote the new charge on the capacitor by $q_1$. The potential difference between the plates is equal to
$$\mathcal{E} = \frac{q_1}{\varepsilon_0 S} \cdot \frac{3}{4} d + \frac{Q}{2\varepsilon_0 S}\left(\frac{d}{2} - \frac{d}{4}\right),$$
where $\mathcal{E} = \frac{q}{\varepsilon_0 S} d$. Hence $q_1 = \frac{8q - Q}{6}$, and $\Delta q = q_1 - q = \frac{2q - Q}{6} = -\frac{1}{3} \cdot 10^{-9} \text{ к.}$

4. Let's write down the laws of conservation of energy and momentum for the atom - photon system:
$$hv = hv_0 + \frac{|\vec{p}|^2}{2Am_p},$$
$$|\vec{p}| = \frac{hv}{c}.$$
From here we get
$$hv = hv_0 + \frac{(hv)^2}{2Am_p c^2}.$$
Physical meaning has only one root
$$hv = Am_p c^2 - Am_p c^2 \sqrt{1 - 2\frac{hv_0}{Am_p c^2}}.$$
Let's try to simplify this expression. Since $hv_0 \ll Am_p c^2$ (you can verify this by substituting specific values),
$$hv \approx Am_p c^2 - Am_p c^2\left(1 - \frac{hv_0}{Am_p c^2}\right) = hv_0.$$
This allows you to make the following replacement:
$$|\vec{p}| = \frac{hv}{c} \approx \frac{hv_0}{c}.$$
Then finally
$$hv \approx hv_0 + \frac{(hv_0)^2}{2Am_p c^2},$$
And
$$v \approx v_0\left(1 + \frac{hv_0}{2Am_p c^2}\right).$$
