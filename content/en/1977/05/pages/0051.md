Ticket 1

1. A man slides down a sled making an angle $\alpha = 6^\circ$ with the horizon. The mass of the sled $M$ is twice the mass of the person $m$. The shadow coefficient of the sled on the slope surface is $\mu = 0,2$. How should a person move relative to the sled so that the sled moves downhill evenly?

2. The cylinder contains 14 g of nitrogen under a light piston at 27°C. What amount of heat does he need to provide when producing an increase in volume by 4%? The relative molecular weight of nitrogen is 28.

Note. For small changes in volume $\left(\frac{\Delta V}{V} \ll 1\right)$, use the approximate formula $\left(1 + \frac{\Delta V}{V}\right)^{-1} \approx 1 - \frac{\Delta V}{V}$.

3. A flow of conducting liquid (molten metal) flows through a ceramic pipe. To measure the velocity of the liquid, the pipe is placed in a uniform magnetic field, perpendicular to the axis of the pipe, and two electrodes are fixed in the pipe, forming a flat capacitor (Fig. 1). The potential difference between the electrodes is measured. Determine the flow velocity if the magnetic induction of the field is $|\vec{B}| = 0,01$ ml, the distance between the electrodes is $d = 2$ cm, the measured potential difference is $U = 0,4$ mV.

4. In an equilateral rectangular glass prism, the base $AB$ and the side face $BC$ are smooth, and the face $AC$ is matte (Fig. 2). The prism stands on the newspaper. Observe, looking through the edge $BC$, to see a part of the text located under the base $AB$, equal to $\alpha = 0,895$ (in area). What is the refractive index of glass?
