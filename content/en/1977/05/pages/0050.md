⟦folio 48⟧

The Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology trains research physicists in the latest areas of physics and technology.

The peculiarity of the educational system adopted at the Physico-Technical Institute is that here fundamental higher education is combined with special training in the so-called basic research institutes and design bureaus, where special departments of the institute have been created.

At the first currencies, students receive general training.

Mathematics, general and theoretical physics, social sciences are studied in the scope of university courses, so that the future of special good knowledge, which forms the basis of the researcher’s physics education. Quick familiarization with the increasing flow of scientific and technical information contributes to in-depth study of foreign languages.

Special and independent research work at the base institute begins already in the second or third year.

At the base institute, the student attends lectures in his specialty, participates in scientific seminars, and is involved in the research work of the institute’s laboratories. The future specialist deals with the latest instruments, participates in solving not model problems attached specifically to his practice, but actual problems facing the laboratory. The thesis work of each MIPT student is, as a rule, included in the thematic plan of the base institute. In the creative atmosphere of a scientific team, the student undergoes invaluable school education. Working under the direct supervision of a prominent scientist largely determines the success of his future independent activities.

Among the basic institutes of MIPT is the leading scientific center of Moscow (see «Kvant», 1972, No. 6, 1975, No. 5).

The Institute trains specialists in eight faculties: Faculty of Radio Engineering and Cybernetics, Faculty of General and Applied Physics, Faculty of Aerophysics and Space Research, Faculty of Molecular and Chemical Physics, Faculty of Physical and Quantum Electronics, Faculty of Aeromechanics and Flight Engineering, Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics, Faculty of Physics and Energy Problems.

At each of the faculties, training is carried out in a wide range of specialties, covering most areas of modern physics, technology and cybernetics.

Ticket 1

1. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{2x+8} = \sqrt{2x-4} + 2\sqrt{3x-3}.$$

2. Solve inequality

$$\log_2(x^2 - 4x + 4) + 2x > 2 - (x + 1)\log_{1/2}(2 - x).$$

3. Two circles touch internally. A straight line passing through the center of a changing circle intersects the larger one at points A and D, and the smaller one at points B and C. Find the ratio of the radii of the circles if $|AB| : |BC| : |CD| = 2 : 4 : 3.$

4. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} 3tg\frac{y}{2} + 6sinx = 2sin(y - x), \\ tg\frac{y}{2} - 2sinx = 6sin(y + x). \end{cases}$$

5. The volume of a regular four-level primid SABCD is equal to V. The height SP of a primid is an edge of a regular tetrahedron SPQR, the face plane of which PQR is perpendicular to the edge SC. Find the volume of the common part of these primids.
