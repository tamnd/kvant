The construction of triangulation of triangle $ABC$ into “large” triangles (point c) of the problem is based on the same idea.

Let points $K, L$ and $P$ be the same as before, $KN$ and $LM$ be perpendiculars to $AC$, point $N_1 = [AP] \cap [KN]$.

Take a point $N_2$ on the segment $KN_1$ and connect it with points $A, P$ and $C$ (Fig. 2). Let us denote by $M_1$ the intersection point of $[LM]$ with $[CN_2]$: $M_1 = [CN_2] \cap [LM]$. On the segment $LM_1$, take the point $M_2$ and connect it with the points $A, N_2$ and $C$. Let $M_3$ denote the point of intersection of $[KN]$ with $[AM_2]$; on the segment $KM_3$, take the point $M_4$, connect it with the points $A, M_2$ and $C$, etc.

Continuing this process, we obtain (for the same reason as above) the eliminated triangulation of triangle $ABC$ into “large” triangles $ABP, APN_2, CN_2P, AN_2M_2, CN_2M_2$, ... (the number of triangles can be made as desired).
