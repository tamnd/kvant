⟦folio 54⟧

Technical Bonds

Option 1

1. To the two starting numbers, the same number was added to the right. The difference between the resulting four-digit number and the square of the original two-digit number was divided by 4% of the square of the original number; in the partial they received half of the original number, and in the remainder - the original number. Find the original two-digit number.

2. The sum of the first five terms of a geometric progression is 93, and the sum of the first ten terms of the same progression is 3069. Find the sum of the first fifteen terms of this progression.

3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\lg^2 x - 2\lg x - 15}{\sqrt{1000-x}} = 0.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\cos x}{1 + \sin x} + \mathrm{tg} x = 2.$$
5. Find the length of a segment parallel to the bases of the trapezoid, the ends of which lie on its lateral sides, knowing that this segment divides the trapezoid into two equal figures, and that the bases of the trapezoid have lengths a and b (a>b).
6. Find the length of the height of the cone with the largest volume that can be inscribed in a ball of radius R.

Option 2

1. Two aircraft models began to simultaneously move in the same direction with a headwind, the speed of which was v m/sec. The first model lasted t seconds less in flight than the second, but did a m longer. Which of these models will travel farthest in calm weather? (The flight time of the model does not depend on the wind.)
2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{2\lg^2 x - 5\lg x + 2}{\sqrt{x - 200}} \ge 0.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{1 + \sin x} + \cos x = 0.$$
4. Find three numbers that form a geometric progression if their sum is 21 and their product is 216.
5. The side length of the square ABCD is 10 cm. On its sides there are segments $[AA_1], [BB_1], [CC_1], [DD_1]$ of length x each, with $A_1 \in [AB], B_1 \in [BC], C_1 \in [CD], D_1 \in [DA]$. Prove that the quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ is a square and find the value of x at which the area of ​​this square is smallest.
6. Find the volume of the primid DABC if $[DB] \perp (ABC)$, multiplying two zeros at the edge AC, is equal to φ, $|DC|=l$, $A\overset{\frown}{CB} = \pi/2$, $C\overset{\frown}{AB} = \alpha$.
Option 3
1. Cities A and B are located on the banks of a river, with A downstream. From these cities, two boats simultaneously sailed towards each other. Accessible simultaneously to cities B and A, the boats turned back and met at a distance of 20 km from the first meeting point. If the boats, leaving A and B at the same time, swam against the current, then the boat leaving A would catch up with the boat leaving B, 150 km from B. Find the distance between A and B.

2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{1 - \log_5(x^2 - 2x + 2)} < \log_5(5x^2 - 10x + 10).$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x + \sin 2x + \sin 3x = 1 + \cos x + \cos 2x.$$
4. Find the denominator of an infinite decreasing geometric progression whose sum is 4 times the sum of the first five terms.
5. A rectangle is inscribed in triangle ABC, two of whose vertices lie on side AC, and the other two on sides AB and BC. Find the maximum area of ​​such a rectangle if $|AC| = 12$ cm, $|BD| = 10$ cm, where $[BD]$ is the height of triangle ABC.
6. Find the volume of a ball inscribed in a cone, the generatrix of which is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle α, and the lateral surface is equal to Q.

Option 4

1. Two quantities left point A at the same time in the same direction, the first at a speed of 8 km/h, and the second at 10 km/h. After 30 minutes, a third magnitude came out from point A in the same direction, catching up with the first, and after another 1 $\frac{1}{2}$ hour, a second magnitude. Find the speed of the third magnitude.
2. Solve the equation
$$\log \sqrt{x - 1} (2x - 14) = 2.$$
3. Solve inequality
$$\cos 2x \cdot \cos \frac{\pi}{8} - \sin 2x \cdot \sin \frac{\pi}{8} \ge 0, 5\sqrt{2}.$$
4. The tangents of the positive angles of a triangle form an arithmetic progression. Prove that then the cosine of the angles of this triangle form an arithmetic progression.
5. A quadrilateral is described around a circle of radius R, two sides of which are parallel to each other and the other two are congruent. Find the smallest area of ​​such a quadrilateral.
6. Find the volume of a regular quadrilateral that is parallel to each other and the other two are congruent. Find the smallest area of ​​a triangle whose height is H and whose plane angle at the vertex is α).
