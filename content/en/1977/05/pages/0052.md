Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics MIPT

The Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics (FUPM) is the youngest faculty of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. In 1976, MIPT celebrated its thirtieth anniversary, and FUPM turned only 7 years old. At the same time, this is the very first and oldest among similar faculties at universities in the country. At the faculty, academicians B.V. Bunkin, V.M. Glishkov, A.A. Dorodnitsin, N.N. Inozemtsev, A.A. Samarsky, V.A. Trapeznikov; Corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences O. M. Belotserkovsky, N. N. Monseev, G. S. Pospelov.

What prompted the creation of this faculty? What makes it different from “traditional” physics departments?

To answer these questions, we must first talk about the main directions of the faculty. As its name suggests, we have two profiles for training students: applied (computational) mathematics and mathematical physics and management.

Do you remember how Fonvizinsky Mitrofanushka answered the question whether “door” is an adjective or a noun? The answer was: “Adjective. Because it is attached to its place. The closet door over there isn’t hung, so it’s a noun.”
