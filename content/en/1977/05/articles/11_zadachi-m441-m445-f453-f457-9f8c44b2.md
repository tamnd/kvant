M441. An arbitrary point $P$ is taken inside a convex $2n$-gon. A straight line is drawn through each vertex and point $P$. Prove that there is a side of the polygon with which none of the drawn lines has common points (except, perhaps, the ends of the side).

G. Gurevich

M442. Given a prime number $p>2$. For each $k$ from 1 to $p-1$ we denote by $a_k$ the remainder when dividing the number $k^p$ by $p^2$. Prove that
$$a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + \dots + a_{p-1} = (p^3 - p^2)/2.$$
C. Ochitin

M443. There is a table of $n \times n$ cells, each cell of which initially contains the number 0. You are allowed to arbitrarily select $n$ numbers in different rows and different columns, and increase each of them by 1.
a) Is it possible to obtain the tables shown in Figures 1 and 2 in a few steps?
b) Is it possible to get a table with pairwise distinct numbers?
b) * What kind of tables can be obtained in $T$ steps?

F. Shleifer

M445. The centers of identical disjoint circles are at the centers of regular hexagons covering the plane as indicated in figure 5. Let $M$ be a polygon with vertices at the centers of the circles. Let's color those circles or their parts (others) that lie inside $M$ red. Show that the sum of the degree magnitudes of the red arcs is $C \cdot 180^\circ$, where $C = C(M)$ is an integer, and give this number a geometric interpretation.

A. Sosinsky

F453. The weight system shown in figure 6 rests on a smooth horizontal table. Masses of cubes $m_1, m_2$ and $M$. A cube of mass $m_2$ is held at a height $l$ above the table. If the system is left to itself, it will begin to move, and the upper cube will slide along the lower one. The coefficient of friction between the cubes is $k$. How far will the bottom cube move by the time the mass cube $m_2$ touches the table?

F454. If voltage $u_0 = 220 \text{ В}$ is applied to the primary winding of an unloaded transformer, then the voltage on the secondary winding will be $u_1 = 127 \text{ В}$. What voltage will be at $u_0 = 220 \text{ В}$ across the load $R = 10 \text{ Ом}$ connected to the secondary winding of this transformer?

The active resistance of the first winding of the transformer is $r_1 = 2 \text{ Ом}$, and the secondary winding is $r_2 = 1 \text{ Ом}$. The internal resistance of the generator is still zero.

V. Skorovarov
