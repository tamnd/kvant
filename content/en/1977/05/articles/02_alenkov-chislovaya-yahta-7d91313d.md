LABYRINTH - HEXAMINO
This figure is made up of 35 hexaminoes.
a) Connect the extreme figures with lines passing through all the hexaminoes, and only once; You can move from figure to figure only through the sides (you cannot through the vertices of the corners).
b) Draw a closed polyline passing (subject to the same conditions) through all 35 hexaminoes.
L. Motsalov

"NUMBER YACHT"
This yacht is made up of thirteen lines with small circles. Place the numbers from 1 to 11 in the circles so that the sum of the numbers in the circles of any row is equal to 13.
Yu. Alenkov

“QUANTUM-WAVE” PUZZLES
In each of these puzzles, some numbers are encrypted in letters. Decipher the examples.
V. Radunsky
