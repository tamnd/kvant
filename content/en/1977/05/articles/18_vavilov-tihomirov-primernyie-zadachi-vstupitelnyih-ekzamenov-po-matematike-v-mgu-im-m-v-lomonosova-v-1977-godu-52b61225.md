It is now generally accepted that without a mathematical description of such systems, a program is impossible, either biologically or economically. Management is currently more of a “physical” science than physics. Galileo defined the task of physics this way: “To measure what is measurable, to make measurable what cannot be measured.” Modern physics has already defined and is engaged in the first half of this task. In the new science - management theory - both sides of the program are equally important.

At the same time, the basic concepts of this science are quickly being mathematized, and over the past ten years, such mathematized control problems have emerged that they are often classified as the field of pure mathematics: theoretical cybernetics, theory of algorithms, algorithmic languages, theory of ingr, discrete mathematics, systems theory, etc.

When choosing a specialty, you often focus on those sciences in which the most accessible ones have been obtained so far. Physics is one such science. However, let us remember that the problem of releasing atomic energy was formulated in the 20s. Those who entered the physics departments at that time solved this problem in the 40s and 50s. And only then did the press start talking about it, and applicants poured into the physics departments.

And Max Planck dreamed of devoting himself to mathematical economics, but realized that economics was too complex a science for his time, and only then did he decide to take up theoretical physics.

Currently, management as an exact science is just emerging. She needs her Galileos, Newotons, Einsteins and Plancks. They will be those who are now teaching this science. It is possible that in the future they will be able to say in the words of Dirac: “I am grateful to fate that I was born on time: if I had been several years older or younger, I would not have had such brilliant opportunities.”

Without a doubt, in this sense, the Faculty of Management and Applied Mathematics is the most promising of the existing faculties at MIPT. However, this is also the most difficult department to study. Here is the most famous curriculum and student loading.

Sample tasks for university entrance exams in mathematics in 1977

Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

Moscow State University named after. M.V. Lomonosov, like other universities in the country, will conduct entrance exams in mathematics in two programs in 1977 (see «Kvant», No. 2).

The tasks proposed below are considered to help schoolchildren graduating from school in 1977 under the new program prepare for entrance exams in mathematics at various faculties of Moscow State University.

1. Algebra and began analysis

1. Find the intervals of occurrence and decrease, examine for extremum and plot graphs of the following functions:

a) $y = x^4 - 4x^2$;

b) $y = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x$.

2. Application of differential calculus.

a) Find the abscissa of the points in the graph of the function $y = \frac{1}{2} \sin 2x - \sin x$ defined on the interval [0; 2π], the tangents to the graph of this function are parallel to the straight line $y + x - 1 = 0$.

b) Find the angle between two tangents drawn from the point (0;-1) to the graph of the function $y = x^2$.

3. Maximum and minimum assignment.

a) Find the critical points and maximum values ​​of the following functions:

$y_1(x) = |x^2 - 3x + 2|$, $x \in [-5; 4]$;

$y_2(x) = |(x+1)(2x - 5)|$, $x \in [-2; 3]$.

b) Inscribe a straight line with the largest area in a semicircle of uniform radius, one of the sides of which lies on the diameter.

4. Solve equations

a) $\cos x - \sin x = \sqrt{1 + \sin x \cos x}$;

b) $\log_{2x^2 - 2}(3x^2 + x - 4) = \log_8 16 - \log_{27} 3$.

5. Solve inequalities

a) $|x^2 - 5x + 6| > \left| \frac{x}{2} - 1 \right|$;

b) $\log_8 \cos x \leq \log_{1/27} 3$;

c) $\frac{1}{\lg x} + \frac{1}{(1 - \lg x)} > 1$;

d) $\sqrt{2x + \sqrt{x^2 + 5}} \geq x + 1$.

6. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the following lines:
a) $y = x^2, x + y = 2$;
b) $y = x, y = x + \sin^2 x, x = \pi/2 (x > 0)$.
7. Problems with parameters.
a) Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the function $f(x) = x^3 - ax - 1$ has a maximum point $x_0$ at which $f(x_0) > 0$.
b) At what value of the parameter $a$ are the tangents to the graph of the function $y = x^3 - a^2 x$, drawn at the points with abscissas $x = 0$ and $x = a$, redistributed?
b) For what value of the parameter $a$ solve the equation $|x^2 - 5x + 6| = ax$.
g) For what value of the parameter $a$ solve the inequality
$$\frac{ax - 1}{x - a} > 1.$$

8. Systems of equations and inequalities.
a) Find the set of solutions to the inequality system
$$\begin{cases} 1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq 4, \\ x + y \leq 1, \\ x - 2y \leq 2. \end{cases}$$
b) Find the set of solutions to the equation system
$$\begin{cases} |xy - 4| = 8 - y^2, \\ xy = 2 + x^2. \end{cases}$$
b) Find the set of solutions to the inequality system
$$\begin{cases} 6 \cdot 2^{4y} + 2^{2y} - 12 > 0, \\ 2^{2y} + 2^{y+1} - 8 < 0. \end{cases}$$

11. Geometry

1. At what relative position of the lines $p$ and $q$ the equality $S_p S_q = S_q S_p$ is satisfied, where $S_p$ and $S_q$ are symmetric transformations with respect to the indicated lines.
2. Show that if $\alpha_1 + \alpha_2 = 0$, then $R_{O_2}^{\alpha_1} R_{O_1}^{\alpha_2}$ is a parallel translation $a$, and for $\alpha_1 + \alpha_2 \neq 0$ a rotation $R_0^\alpha$. Determine the length and direction of $a$ in the first case, $O$ and $\alpha$ in the second.
3. In the plane, two mutually perpendicular lines $OX$ and $OY$ and two points $A$ and $B$ are given such that $A \in [OX), B \in [OY)$, and $|OA| = |OB|$. Let $l_1 = R_0^\alpha((OY))$ and $l_2 = R^{-\alpha}A((AB))$, where $\alpha \in \left[ -\frac{\pi}{2}; \frac{\pi}{2} \right]$.

At what values ​​of $\alpha$ are the straight lines $l_1$ and $l_2$
a) parallel;
b) perpendicular?

4. Find the area of ​​triangle $ABC$ if $|AC| = 3, |BC| = 4$ and the medians $AK$ and $BL$ are perpendicular.

5. The lines $a_1, a_2$ and $a_3$ are parallel, and the distances from the line $a_2$, which contains $a_1$ and $a_3$ in the strip with edges, to the other two are $b$ and $c$. Find the side length of an equilateral triangle whose vertices are located one at a time on lines $a_1, a_2$ and $a_3$.

6. On the side $AB$ and the extension of the base $AC$ of the isosceles triangle $ABC$ we take the points $D$ and $E$ corresponding so that $|AE| = |BE| = b, |BD| : |AD| = 1:3$. Find the radius of the circle inscribed in triangle $ADE$, if it is known that $|AB| = a$ and $b > a$.

7. An equilateral triangle with side length $a$ is rotated by an angle $\alpha$ ($0 \leq \alpha < 360^\circ$) around its center. Find the area of ​​intersection of the original and rotate the triangle.

8. Inside the equilateral triangle $ABC$ the point $O$ is taken so that $|OA| = \sqrt{19}, |OB| = \sqrt{13}, |OC| = \sqrt{17}$. Calculate $\overrightarrow{OB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$.

9. In the tetrahedron $ABCD$ the edge lengths satisfy the corresponding
$$|AB|^2 + |DC|^2 = |AC|^2 + |BD|^2 = |AD|^2 + |BC|^2.$$
Prove that three pairs of crossing edges of a tetrahedron are mutually perpendicular.

10. The plane passes through the vertex $A$ of the triangular line $SABC$, bisects the median $SK$ of the triangle $SAB$, and intersects the median $SL$ of the triangle $SAC$ at a point $D$ such that $|SD| : |DL| = 1:2$. In what ratio does this plane divide the volume of the straight line?

11. There is an inscription in the cube with a truss touching all the faces of the cube. Find the length of the chord that is cut on the straight truss and passes through two crossing two crossing edges of the cube.

12. Points $P_1 \in [A_1A_4], P_2 \in [A_3A_4']$, $P_3 \in [A_1'A_2'']$ are chosen on three edges of the cube $K_1 = A_1A_2A_3A_4A_1'A_2'A_3'A_4'$ so that
$$|P_1A_4| : |P_1A_1| = 1:2,$$
$$|P_2A_3| : |P_2A_3'| = 1:3,$$
$$|P_3A_2'| : |P_3A_1'| = 1:2.$$

Cube $K_1$ is externally attached to cube $K_2 = B_1B_2B_3B_4B_1'B_2'B_3'B_4'$ so that $A_1 = B_1, A_1' \in [B_1B_1']$, $A_2 \in [B_1B_2]$, and $|A_1A_2| : |B_1B_2| = 1:3$. The plane passing through the points $P_1, P_2$ and $P_3$ divides the cube $K_2$ into two parts. Find the ratio of their volumes.
