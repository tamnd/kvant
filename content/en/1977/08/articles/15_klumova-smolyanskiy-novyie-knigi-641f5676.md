New books

In this issue we place brief annotations on books on mathematics and physics published in the 111th quarter of 1977 and of interest to our readers.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"
1. Ignatiev E. I.
In the kingdom of ingenuity, in 3 books, book 1. Volume 10 sheets, circulation 200,000 copies, price 33 kopecks.

Ignatiev's books, first published in 1908, were a huge success and were subsequently republished several times. They made a real revolution in the entire teaching of school and extracurricular mathematics. These books are not a “methodology”, not a “textbook” and not a “problem book” in the general sense of the word: the problems collected in them are of an entertaining nature, and they can be classified as “mathematical games and entertainment”.

The book is divided into single sections containing problems in order of increasing difficulty. Among the problems are arithmetic, combinator, puzzles, problems related to moving matches, etc., problems of a geometric nature, problems on graphs (in particular, the famous Euler problem on Königsberg places and all kinds of problems on crossings) and joke problems.

Generally speaking, there is no need to read and understand such books “in a row.” Everyone can first take the section that interests him most, then move on to any other, etc. Since among the problems there are also those whose conditions and analysis do not require almost any key mathematical training, this book can be safely recommended for independent reading and study, even by primary school students. However, “there is no age limit,” since every reader will find something for himself in the book.

The 2nd and 3rd books will be released in 1978-1979. The previous edition was published in 1926.

2. Reid K. Gilbert. Translation from English Volume 20 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 1 rub. 24 k.

The book is a translation of the biography of the outstanding German mathematician David Hilbert (1862-1943). It was written based on the memories of Hilbert’s students and friends, as well as on the basis of his correspondence with his friend, the famous German mathematician G. Minkowski. The book vividly reproduces the picture of the mathematical life of Europeans in the 19th and first third of the 20th centuries. It is successfully complemented by a translation of G. Weyl's article, which provides a detailed analysis of Hilbert's mathematical works.

Designed for a wide range of readers, the book will be of interest to lovers of mathematics and people interested in the history of science.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
3. Belyaeva E. S., Monakhov V. M. Extreme problems. A manual for students. Volume 3 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.

This manual is intended for high school students. It contains many problems from different branches of science and technology, the solution of which comes down to finding the optimal values ​​of a certain function. These are the so-called extreme problems. Problems of this kind and methods for solving them have found wide practical application and constituted a new direction in applied mathematics. This manual begins with elementary problems of finding the maximum and minimum, which schoolchildren are partly familiar with from the school mathematics course, and ends with the simplest linear programming problems.

4. In Ilenkin N. Ya., Mordkovich A. G. Limits. A manual for teachers. Volume 4 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.

The concept of limit is one of the main ones in the new school mathematics course. On its basis, such important concepts as continuity, arbitrary, and integral are introduced. For schoolchildren, the concept of limit is determined by the definition of difficulty. Taking this into account, the authors reveal this concept on the basis of visual representations; By successively refining these ideas, they then obtain a strict definition of the limit. Based on the consideration of the limits of a sequence and functions, numerical and power series are introduced and a theory of the degree of a function is constructed.

The manual is intended for high school students and will be useful for mathematics teachers.

Publishing house "Knowledge"
5. Pukhnachev Yu. V. Uchisl application of mathematics (handbook of mathematical concepts). Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 24 kopecks.

The author, in an interactive and popular form, introduces the reader to the methods of applying mathematics in those areas of knowledge that until recently were traditionally considered not amenable to “mathematization”: linguistics, jurisprudence, planning, etc. The book contains the basic concepts of mathematical analysis, probability theory, geometric models. It will be read with interest by schoolchildren, as well as specialists who use mathematics in their practical activities.

6. Physics and mathematics Olympiads. Collection. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 24 kopecks.

This book contains materials from the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympids. Almost all data problems with solutions, most of the solutions are illustrated.

The book is intended for schoolchildren in grades 7–10 and can be used in physics and mathematics clubs.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Piisky A. A. Problems in physics. Volume 20 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

This collection contains both problems related to traditional branches of physics, and problems on the theory of relativity, quantum mechanics, statistical, wave and quantum optics, atomic and nuclear physics.

Nan more difficult problems are provided with detailed solutions or indicated.

The book is intended for students of secondary schools, preparatory departments of institutes, as well as for all those who independently prepare for university entrance exams.

2. I Vorsky B. M., Detlaf A. A. Handbook of physics. Edition 7th, rev. Volume 46 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 2 rubles. 63 k.

The reference book provides definitions of all basic physical concepts, formulates the basic physical laws and the essence of the phenomena described by them.

The reference book reflects all sections of classical and modern physics.

The directory is intended for a wide range of readers.

3. What are physicists thinking about? Inlet 11. Lasers. Collection of popular science articles. Translation from English. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The collection “What Physicists Are Thinking About” publishes attempts to become major foreign physicists who have made a direct contribution to the issues under consideration.

This eleventh issue is dedicated to lasers - one of the most published discoveries in modern physics. The articles published in the collection introduce readers to the operating principles, applications and prospects of lasers. They are written in a lively and engaging manner and are generally accessible to high school students.

4. Started with Kane F.S. Time and its measurement. 4th edition, revised. Volume 15 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The book describes some of the processes occurring in the united world, as well as in atoms, atomic nuclei, planets, stars and galaxies. The duration of these processes is described, in one rumor it is equal to only millionths of billions of fractions of a second, in others - billions of years. Thus, a “cut” of the world was made along one of its main coordinates, namely time. This approach allows us to show how the concept of time was gradually visualized and improved and to what functional achievements this reduction resulted.

This volume is told in a form accessible to readers with a secondary education, as well as to high school students aspiring to science.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

5. Gulo O. D. N. A. Umov. Volume 8 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 25 kopecks.

This book, published in the “People of Science” series, is dedicated to one of the greatest Russian physicists - N. A. Umov. It talks about the life, scientific and pedagogical activities of the scientist. Particular attention is paid to the scientific works of Umov.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

6. Makarenya A. A., Rysev Yu. V. D. I. Mendleev. Volume 10 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 70 kopecks.

This book from the “People of Science” series tells about the life and outstanding discoveries of the great Russian scientist D. I. Mendleev.

His path to science and the significance of his works are shown (the doctrine of periodicity, gas laws, etc.).

The book uses materials from the museum-archive of D. I. Mendleyev.

The book is intended for high school students.

7. Tarasov L.V. Optics, laser birth. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 35 kopecks.

The author of this book, published in the “World of Knowledge” series, talks in an interesting and accessible way about new optical phenomena observed when a laser beam passes through a medium and is collectively called “nonlinear optics.”

The book is accessible to a wide range of readers.

8. Kuprin M. Ya. Physics in agriculture. Volume 12 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 65 kopecks.

In this book, the author talks about the application of the laws of physics in agriculture based on agricultural production. Students receive information about agricultural machines and devices based on the laws of mechanics, tellots and electronics. They study “how many hectares can be cultivated in a day”, “how cotton is harvested with an air blower”, “at what temperature do seeds germinate”, “how to clear seeds from weeds” and many other interesting and useful substances.

The book will guide rural school students in choosing a profession.
