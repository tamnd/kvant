To our readers

A subscription for 1978 is announced for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant».

«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 5–10 who love mathematics and physics.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren whose interest in exact sciences is still dormant.

On the pages of our journal, articles of a review nature are published, telling about the achievements of science and about problems that are still waiting to be solved.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems for university entrance exams, problems for various Olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Our magazine is useful to the reader as well. Fundamental changes have now been made in school courses in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» publishes materials for mathematics and physics courses, materials for elective classes, and articles explaining and expanding the new program.

The materials published in the section “Through the Pages of School Textbooks” will cover the most subtle and important issues of mathematics raised in school textbooks.

In 1978, «Kvant» opens a new section: “The Art of Programming.” This section will be devoted to a story about human interactions with computers and teaching various algorithmic languages ​​in which programs that machines can understand are written.

What questions can an applicant expect at the entrance exams for new programs? The reader will find the answer to this and many other questions in the “Applicant Practice” section.

In 1978, we are planning a significant expansion of the «Kvant» rubric for younger schoolchildren.

The magazine constantly publishes solutions to books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

THE MAGAZINE IS DISTRIBUTED BY SUBSCRIPTION ONLY.

When subscribing, refer to our index 70465. The price of the issue is 30 kopecks.
