Quests "Last numbers"

1. Let the number $a$ be divisible by neither 2 nor 5.
Prove that when the number $A=a^{5 \cdot 10^{n-2}}$ is raised to a natural power, its last $n$ digits do not change ($n \geq 4, a \in \mathbb{N}$).
2. Let the number $b$ not be divisible by 2, but divisible by 5.
a) Prove that when the number $B=b^{2^{n-1}}$ ($n \in \mathbb{N}$) is raised to a natural power, its last $n$ digits do not change.
6) Let $r, q, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \geq n$. Prove that the last $n$ digits of the expressions $b^{2^{n-1}q+r}$ and $b^r$ are respectively equal.
3. Let the number $c$ be divisible by 2, but not divisible by 5.
a) Prove that when the number $C=c^{4 \cdot 5^{n-1}}$ is raised to a natural power, its last $n$ digits do not change.

6) Let $r, q, n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \geq n$. Prove that the last $n$ digits of the expressions $c^{4 \cdot 5^{n-1}q+r}$ and $b^r$ are respectively equal.
4. Let $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Prove that when the number $M=m^{10^{n+1}}$ is raised to a natural power, its last $n$ digits do not change.
5. Find the last three digits of the expression $3^{105}+4^{105}$.
6. Prove that the difference $3^{999}-2^{999}$ turns out to be the digits 1979.
7. Prove that if the expression

weighed to any natural power, then more than 200,000 of its last digits will not change.
8. Prove that for any natural number $n$ the difference
