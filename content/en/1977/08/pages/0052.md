To solve this problem, we will use the law of conservation of energy. To prepare a force $\vec{F}_1$ or $\vec{F}_2$ of the corresponding moment, and forces of a larger diameter have $n$ teeth, and forces of a smaller diameter have $n-1$ teeth. Training in the macro-chimo sili system, 缺损om, the load rose by $\frac{n-(n-1)}{2}$ links, that is, to a height of $h_1 = \frac{1}{2N}$ meters, and its potential energy increased by

$$\Delta \Pi_1 = m |\vec{g}| h_1 = m |\vec{g}| \frac{1}{2N}$$

(m is the mass of the load). Let us denote by $A_{\text{тр}}$ the work of the friction force. Then, according to the law of conservation of energy

$$|\vec{F}_1| \frac{n}{N} = m |\vec{g}| \frac{1}{2N} + A_{\text{тр}}.$$

Now let the upper block, under the action of force $\vec{F}_2$, make one full revolution clockwise. In this case, the force $\vec{F}_2$ will do work

$$A_2 = |\vec{F}_2| l_2 = |\vec{F}_2| \frac{n-1}{N},$$

the potential energy of the load will change by the following values:

$$\Delta \Pi_2 = -m |\vec{g}| h_2 = -m |\vec{g}| \frac{1}{2N},$$

and the work of the friction force will remain the same as in the first case (since the absolute value of the movement is the same, and the friction does not depend on the direction of movement). Therefore we can write

$$|\vec{F}_2| \frac{n-1}{N} = -m |\vec{g}| \frac{1}{2N} + A_{\text{тр}}.$$

In addition, according to the conditions of the problem

$$|\vec{F}_1| = k |\vec{F}_2|$$

Solving the system of equations (1)–(3), we find

$$|\vec{F}_1| = m |\vec{g}| \frac{k}{n(k-1)+1},$$

$$|\vec{F}_2| = m |\vec{g}| \frac{1}{n(k-1)+1}.$$
