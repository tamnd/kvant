First return to Earth

Participants in the military conflict on the planet used a mathematical theory that is well known here on Earth - the theory of cerrus. It is used in cases where there is no information about the enemy’s plans. Initially, this theory developed in relation to gambling, from which it got its name. But then its applications became very diverse - from economics to military affairs. It should be noted that the initial provisions of the cerr theory are not indisputable. And it is applicable, strictly speaking, only when the game is repeated quite a few times. However, there are supporters of its use as a one-time solution (among them is General Rol).

Among the many interesting problems in game theory is the problem of mixed strategies. Often the information available to the decision maker is insufficient. Under these conditions, it is impossible to indicate any one unconditionally best strategy, and one has to resort to using a mixed strategy - with some possibilities of using several options, as was first proposed by the French mathematician Emile Borel (1871-1956). Subsequently, linear programming was used to find optimal mixed strategies.

Mixed strategies require a small amount of random numbers (the simplest versions of such sensors are a coin, a watch with a second hand; there are also more advanced random number sensors implemented on a computer). If a mixed strategy is not delivered by a random number sensor, but is assigned, there is a danger that the enemy will guess which option is waiting for him and will be able to act more effectively. But against a random mixed strategy, the enemy has only one remedy - the use of his optimal mixed strategy, also determined randomly.

The Lilacs did not use several other important game theory terms that are useful for the reader to become familiar with. Thus, the very first table showing the possibility of loss of sgenevs is called the cerra matrix. And the value v - the average damage that will be caused by the genie if he uses the optimal mixed strategy - is called the price of cerra.

The method of reasoning proposed by General Nakhit - not knowing what the enemy will do, consider all varnantens to be equally credible - is accepted by the English mathematician of the 18th century T. Bayes.
