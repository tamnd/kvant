3. Tymeladze

Game theory

Verda or Kalimna?

Never in the entire hundred years of the Great War have the Garths at the Lilac headquarters conferred for so long. Vapors of molybdenum oxide swirled above a large group of generals who were arguing bitterly at the blackboard with the following table:

AROKER LOSSES
PURPLE STRATEGY OUR STRATEGY
ATTACK ON KALIMNA
SHEETING OF VERDA
PROTECTION OF VERDA
6
3
PROTECTION OF KALYMNA
1
5

Generalissimo Dor glanced sideways at the sign “Molybdenum is not described here,” but did not explode towards it, but only silently nodded to Mots, introducing him to the floor.

“The Filotovs can only have two plans: either a starship attack on Kalimna, or a shelling...” General Mots ordered (“this presence of molybdenum will kill him in the end,” thought Dor, but Mots was already continuing) “... or the shelling of Verda.” In my opinion, we must devote all our efforts to the defense of Verda. Then, if the enemy attacks her, we will only lose three combat jokers.

- What if the Filotovs come to Kalimna? - Lud is impatient together. “Then we can’t spare six of our jokers.”

He smugly threw another portion of molybdenum into himself and continued:

— Kalimna is our main support. All forces to protect her! Then the attack on Kalimna will bring Filotov only one joker.
