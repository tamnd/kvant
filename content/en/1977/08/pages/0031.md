We are interested in the smallest value of $v$, so $L$ should be the lowest. The task of finding from system (4) $x_1$ and $x_2$, at which the function $L(x_1, x_2)$ takes the lowest value, is called the linear programming problem in the life of that distant planet *). Given two unknowns $x_1$ and $x_2$ this problem can be solved graphically. Each of the inequalities of system (4) defines a half-plane on the coordinate plane $x_1Ox_2$, the intersection of these half-planes forms a quadrilateral (see Fig. 1), and equation $L = x_1 + x_2$ defines a straight line intersecting the coordinate axes at points $x_1 = L$ and $x_2 = L$. With different $L$, parallel lines are obtained.
