⟦folio 59⟧

This book contains materials from the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympids. Almost all data problems with solutions, most of the solutions are illustrated.

The book is intended for schoolchildren in grades 7–10 and can be used in physics and mathematics clubs.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Piisky A. A. Problems in physics. Volume 20 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

This collection contains both problems related to traditional branches of physics, and problems on the theory of relativity, quantum mechanics, statistical, wave and quantum optics, atomic and nuclear physics.

Nan more difficult problems are provided with detailed solutions or indicated.

The book is intended for students of secondary schools, preparatory departments of institutes, as well as for all those who independently prepare for university entrance exams.

2. I Vorsky B. M., Detlaf A. A. Handbook of physics. Edition 7th, rev. Volume 46 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 2 rubles. 63 k.

The reference book provides definitions of all basic physical concepts, formulates the basic physical laws and the essence of the phenomena described by them.

The reference book reflects all sections of classical and modern physics.

The directory is intended for a wide range of readers.

3. What are physicists thinking about? Inlet 11. Lasers. Collection of popular science articles. Translation from English. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The collection “What Physicists Are Thinking About” publishes attempts to become major foreign physicists who have made a direct contribution to the issues under consideration.

This eleventh issue is dedicated to lasers - one of the most published discoveries in modern physics. The articles published in the collection introduce readers to the operating principles, applications and prospects of lasers. They are written in a lively and engaging manner and are generally accessible to high school students.

4. Started with Kane F.S. Time and its measurement. 4th edition, revised. Volume 15 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The book describes some of the processes occurring in the united world, as well as in atoms, atomic nuclei, planets, stars and galaxies. The duration of these processes is described, in one rumor it is equal to only millionths of billions of fractions of a second, in others - billions of years. Thus, a “cut” of the world was made along one of its main coordinates, namely time. This approach allows us to show how the concept of time was gradually visualized and improved and to what functional achievements this reduction resulted.

This volume is told in a form accessible to readers with a secondary education, as well as to high school students aspiring to science.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

5. Gulo O. D. N. A. Umov. Volume 8 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 25 kopecks.

This book, published in the “People of Science” series, is dedicated to one of the greatest Russian physicists - N. A. Umov. It talks about the life, scientific and pedagogical activities of the scientist. Particular attention is paid to the scientific works of Umov.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

6. Makarenya A. A., Rysev Yu. V. D. I. Mendleev. Volume 10 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 70 kopecks.

This book from the “People of Science” series tells about the life and outstanding discoveries of the great Russian scientist D. I. Mendleev.

His path to science and the significance of his works are shown (the doctrine of periodicity, gas laws, etc.).

The book uses materials from the museum-archive of D. I. Mendleyev.

The book is intended for high school students.

7. Tarasov L.V. Optics, laser birth. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 35 kopecks.

The author of this book, published in the “World of Knowledge” series, talks in an interesting and accessible way about new optical phenomena observed when a laser beam passes through a medium and is collectively called “nonlinear optics.”

The book is accessible to a wide range of readers.

8. Kuprin M. Ya. Physics in agriculture. Volume 12 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 65 kopecks.

In this book, the author talks about the application of the laws of physics in agriculture based on agricultural production. Students receive information about agricultural machines and devices based on the laws of mechanics, tellots and electronics. They study “how many hectares can be cultivated in a day”, “how cotton is harvested with an air blower”, “at what temperature do seeds germinate”, “how to clear seeds from weeds” and many other interesting and useful substances.

The book will guide rural school students in choosing a profession.
