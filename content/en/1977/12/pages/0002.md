This figure depicts one of the remarkable curves - the cardion (as it was named in 1741 by the French mathematician Castillon because of its resemblance to the heart (zharbια in Greek - heart).

Here is one way to build it. We select an arbitrary point on the circle and draw all possible chords through it (at figure they are black). Let’s take an arbitrary one of these chords and draw from its second end another, “red” chord, congested with the “black” one. The evaluative (see «Kvant», 1977, No. 9, note “Nephroid”) of the family of these “red” chords will be the cardion.
