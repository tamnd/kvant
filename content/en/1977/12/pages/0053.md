At the right

1 (LPI, 1975). The lateral surface of a regular decagonal straight line is equal to S, and the base area is equal to Q. Find the volume of the straight line.

2 (Kazan State University, 1972). The area of ​​the base of a right triangular straight line is equal to $\sqrt{13}$, and the value of the plane angle at its vertex is four times greater than the value of the angle between the side edge and the base. Find the lateral surface area.

3 (MSU, Mechanics and Mathematics, 1973). Points K and M are the midpoints of the edges AB and AC of the triangular line DABC, $S_{\triangle ABC} = p$. Find $S_{\triangle BCD}$ if $S_{\triangle DKM} = q$ and the base of the height of the straight line falls at the point of intersection of the medians of the base of ABC.

4. The basis of a parallel paralleliped is a square. Diagonal sections are drawn through its adjacent sides, forming an angle $\alpha$ with the base plane, and an angle $\beta$ between themselves. Prove that $\cos^2\alpha = \cos \beta$.

5. In a triangular straight line with base area Q, lateral surface area S and the sum of dihedral angles at the base edges $3\alpha$, the vertex is projected at the point of intersection of the base medians. Prove that $Q \leq S \cos \alpha$.
