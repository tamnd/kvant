Problem 7. At the end of a homogeneous platform of length $l$ there are two targets whose masses are $m_1$ and $m_2$ (Fig. 7). The first one hit the middle of the platform. What distance x must the second target move along the platform for the shallowing to return to its original place? Find the conditions under which the problem has a solution.

Let's find the coordinates of the center of mass of the system at the initial and final moments and equate them (since the center of mass remained in the same place). Moreover, beyond the origin of the coordinates of the point where the target mass $m_1$ is located at the initial moments. Then

$$\frac{m_2 l + M l/2}{m_1 + m_2 + M} = \frac{m_1 l/2 + m_2 (l-x) + M l/2}{m_1 + m_2 + M}$$

(here $M$ is the mass of the platform). From here

$$x = \frac{m_1 l}{2m_2}.$$

Obviously, if $m_1 > 2m_2$, then $x > l$ - the problem loses its meaning.
