The system, consisting of weights, a string and a block, is not closed. From the outside, the system is affected by the gravitational forces of the loads $m_1g$ and $m_2g$ and the force applied to the block from the suspension side, equal to the double tension force of the string $T$ (see Fig. 8). Let us write the equation of motion of the system in projections onto the vertical axis $Y$, directed downward:

$$\left(m_1 + m_2\right)a_c = \left(m_1 + m_2\right)\left|g\right| - 2\left|T\right|.$$

The force $T$ can be found by writing the equation of motion for each load separately (remember that $a_1 = -a_2 = a$):

$$m_1a = m_1\left|g\right| - \left|T\right|,$$
$$-m_2a = m_2\left|g\right| - \left|T\right|,$$

where

$$a = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\left|g\right|,$$
$$\left|T\right| = \frac{2m_1m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\left|g\right|.$$

Substituting the resulting value $\left|T\right|$ into our original equation, we find that

$$a_c = \left(\frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\right)^2\left|g\right|.$$

The problem can be solved in another way. It follows directly from the definition of the center of mass that

$$\vec{a}_c = \frac{m_1a_1 + m_2a_2}{m_1 + m_2}$$

(here $\vec{a}_c$ is the acceleration of the center of mass, $a_1$ and $a_2$ are the accelerations of loads $m_1$ and $m_2$, respectively), or in projection.

axis on axis $Y$:

$$a_c = \frac{m_1a_1 + m_2a_2}{m_1 + m_2}.$$

Considering that

$$a_1 = -a_2 = a = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\left|g\right|,$$

we get

$$a_c = \left(\frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}\right)^2\left|g\right|.$$

1. Two people are standing on skates at a distance of $l$ from each other. One of them throws a ball of mass $m$, the other picks it up after a period of time $t$. At what speed will the person throwing the ball slide if his mass is $M$?

2. Along a wedge with an angle at the base $\alpha$, which can move on a smooth horizontal table, a winding and automatic pen moves with a relative constant $u$. How high is the speed of the wedge and what is the force of pressure on the wedge? Wedge weight $M$, automatic $m$. The car will move when the wedge accumulates.

3. A gymnast with mass $M$, carrying a load of mass $m$, jumps at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon with an initial speed $v_0$. At the moment when they reach the greatest height, he throws the load back with a horizontal speed $u$ relative to himself. How much did the jump distance increase from throwing the load?

4. The projectile is thrown by the gun with an initial speed $v_0$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. At the top point of its parabolic trajectory, the projectile breaks into two fragments with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$. After the explosion, a fragment with mass $m_1$ falls vertically, having an initial speed of $u$. Start the equation of motion of the second shard.

5. A boat of mass $M$ stands motionless in the lake. At the stern and bow of the boat, at a distance $l$ from each other, there are fishermen with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ ($m_1 > m_2$). The fishermen change places. How much does the boat move? Neglect water resistance.

6. On the edge of a resting raft of mass $M$ there are $n$ boys, the mass of each of which is $m$. Start the speed of the raft after the boys jump with the same horizontal speed $u$ relative to the raft: a) simultaneously; b) line by line. In which case will the speed of the raft be greater? Neglect water resistance.

7. There are two tanks on the cart, connected by a tube with a tap. One of them is filled with water (Fig. 9). When you open the tap, water flows into another tank. Describe the nature of the cart's motion.
