Tasks

M476—M480; F488—F492

M476. a) Prove that if the vertices of a convex $n$-gon lie in the nodes of the checkered paper, and there are no other nodes on its sides, then $n \leq 4$.

6) Let space be divided by three families of parallel planes into identical cubes. The tops of the cups are called nodes. Prove that if all $n$ vertices of a convex polyhedron lie at nodes, and there are no other nodes on its edges, faces, or inside the polyhedron, then $n \leq 8$.

S. Mironov

M477*. Given a polynomial $P(x)$ with integer coefficients such that for each natural number $x$ the inequality $P(x) > x$ holds. Let's define the sequence $\{b_n\}$ as follows: $b_1 = 1$, $b_{k+1} = P(b_k)$ for $k \geq 1$. It is known that for any natural number $d$ there is a member of the sequence $\{b_n\}$ divisible by $d$. Prove that $P(x) = x+1$.

M478. In the volleyball tournament, each two teams played one match.

a) Prove that if for any two teams there is a third that won against these two, then the number of teams is at least seven.

6) Construct an example of such a seven-team tournament.

b)* Prove that if for any three teams there is a third that won against these three, then the number of teams is at least 15.

M479. Are there a) six, 6) 1000 distinct natural numbers such that for any two $a$ and $b$ of them, the sum $a + b$ is divisible by the difference $a - b$?

Problems M477–M479 were proposed by S. Konyaenin

M480.* The sequence $c_n$ is constructed according to the following rule: $c_1 = 2$, $c_{n+1} = [3c_n/2]$ for $n \geq 1$*. Prove that

a) in this sequence there are infinitely many even numbers and infinitely many odd numbers;

6) the sequence $e_n = (-1)^{c_n}$ is non-periodic;

b) there is a number $\gamma$ such that $c_n = \left[ \frac{3}{2} \right)^n \gamma$.

*) Here $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$.

⟦folio 35⟧

F488. A rectangular wire frame with side dimensions $a = 0,020$ m and $b = 0,030$ m is immersed in soapy water, due to which a soap film is formed on it. When observed in reflected light, the angle of incidence of which is $\alpha = 30^\circ$, the film appears green ($\lambda = 5 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m).

1) Is it possible to determine the mass of this film using a balance that is accurate to 0.1 mg? The density of the soap solution is $\rho = 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, the refractive index of the film is $n = 1,33$.

2) What color will the thinnest film that satisfies the conditions of the problem appear if light is incident on it and then reflected perpendicular to the film?

Note. Take into account that when light is reflected from a denser medium, the phase of the wave changes abruptly by l.

X International Olympiad in Physics

F489. Electrons are accelerated in an electron gun by an electric field, passing through a gap whose direction is $U = 10^3$ B. Having flown out of the gun at point $T$, the electrons then move along a straight line $TT'$ (Fig. 1). At point $M$ at a distance of $a = 5,0$ cm from point $T$ there is a target, and straight line $TM$ forms an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with straight line $TT'$.

1) What should the induction $\vec{B}$ of a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the drawing plane be for electrons emitted from the gun to hit the target?

2) What should the induction $\vec{B}_1$ of a uniform magnetic field parallel to the straight line $MT$ be for electrons to hit the target?

Assume that $|\vec{B}|$ and $|\vec{B}_1|$ do not exceed 0.030 T.

X International Olympiad in Physics

F490. When a neutron flow passes through a 1 mm thick cadmium plate, the amount of part in the beam decreases by 15%, and their speed does not change. What fraction of the neutron flux passes through a 10 mm thick cadmium plate?

F491. A large thin conducting plate of area $S$ and thickness $d$ is placed in a uniform electric field $\vec{E}$, perpendicular to the plate. How much heat will be released in the plate if the field is turned off?

P. Zubkov

F492. Two round cylinders with diameters $D$ and $d$ (Fig. 2) lie on a rough plane. A cord is wound around a large cylinder, to the end of which a horizontal force $\vec{F}$ is applied. Determine at what friction coefficient $\mu$, identical for all contacting surfaces, can a large cylinder be dragged through a soap bar. What should the absolute value of the force $\vec{F}$ be in order for this to be possible?

V. Kerzhentsev
