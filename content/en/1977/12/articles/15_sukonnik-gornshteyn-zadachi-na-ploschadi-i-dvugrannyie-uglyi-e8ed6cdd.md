Let (Fig. 5) $(BCD) \perp [AO]$, $[AE] \perp [BD]$, $\hat{AEC} = \alpha$, $S_{\Delta BDC} = Q$, $S_{\Delta ABD} = S$. Then, projecting the face ABD onto the face BCD, we obtain the equality $Q = 3S\cos \alpha$, and projecting the faces BCD, ABC and ACD onto the face ABD, we obtain the equality $S = Q\cos \alpha + 2S\cos \beta$. $$3\cos^2 \alpha + 2\cos \beta - 1 = 0.$$
