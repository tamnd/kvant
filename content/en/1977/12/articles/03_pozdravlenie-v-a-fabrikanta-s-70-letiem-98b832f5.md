⟦folio 2⟧

And the member of the editorial board of our journal, full member of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Professor Valentin Aleksandrovich Fabrikant, turned 70 years old.
The editorial collection and editors of the magazine «Kvant», on their own behalf and on behalf of many thousands of readers, congratulate Valentin Aleksandrovich on his semi-dessert and wish him health, many years of life and new great successes in his noble work as a scientist and teacher.
