Fig. 7.

Points $A_1$ and $A_2$ can move along a circle of radius $R$ with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$. Note that the most possible value of the distance $|A_1A_2|$ is $2(l + R)$ (Fig. 5,a), and the most equal is $2(l - R)$ (at $l \geq R$) or $0$ (at $l \leq R$) (Fig. 5,a, 6). It follows that for the existence of the Watt mechanism it is necessary that the parameters $d$, $R$ and $l$ satisfy the inequalities
$$l - R \leq d \leq l + R.$$
We will say that the section $M_1M_2$ of the curve extends from the line segment $[M_1M_2]$ by no more than $k\%$, if each point $M$ of this section of the curve is removed from the segment $M_1M_2$ at a distance not exceeding $a_0 = \frac{k}{100}|M_1M_2|$ (meaning the length of the redistribution from point $M$ to the segment $M_1M_2$; Fig. 6).
