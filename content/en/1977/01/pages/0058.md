Physics and mathematics boarding school at Moscow State University. M. V. Lomonosova

For more than 13 years, specialized boarding schools have been operating in Kyiv, Leningrad, Moscow, Novosibirsk, Yerevan and Tbilisi. The goal of these schools is to provide teenagers living in distant centers with the same opportunities for advancement in science as students in university cities. We asked the board of trustees of the boarding school at Moscow State University, academician A. N. Kolmogorov, and the head of the educational department for the mathematical cycle V. V. Vavilov to tell us about their school.

Many readers «Kvant» have probably seen the television film “Ask, Boys,” which, in particular, talks about the Internet at Moscow State University. This boarding school accepts students who have successfully completed the eighth grade and live in the Central European part of the USSR, the Urals, the Volga region, the North Caucasus and Belarus. The selection of future schoolchildren is carried out by the selection committee based on competitive exams in major subjects, which are set daily during regional competitions. The dates of these exams can be found in the region’s department of public education or in the Ministry of Education of the relevant resource office (it is not necessary to attend the Olympiad for admission to a boarding school; students who missed the exam for any reason can apply directly to the school, its address: Moscow, 121357, Kremenicheskaya, 29, boarding school, admissions office).

If your dream of studying science came to you only in the ninth grade, then you can also take part in the exam - we also accept students for one year in the tenth grade. True, the competitive requirements here are especially high (there are only 60 places in the one-year stream).

Based on the exam results, the best candidates are called to the summer training camp (FMS anniversary), where final enrollment is made (in 9th or 10th grade).
