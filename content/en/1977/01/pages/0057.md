1. The tank together holds 1000 liters of water. Every day 600 liters are consumed, and at night they should move half the amount that is in the tank in the morning. On Monday morning the tank was full. Will there be enough water in the tank on Thursday?

2. Are there natural values ​​$k, m, n$ for which $(2k+1)^2-(2m+1)^2=4(2n+1)$ is true.

3. A straight line parallel to one of the sides of a triangle divides it into equal figures. In what ratio does this line divide the other two sides of the triangle?

4. Prove or disprove the following statements:

a) For $n^2-1(n\geq 5)$ to be divisible by 24, it is sufficient that $n$ be a prime number;

b) For $n^2-1(n\geq 5)$ to be divisible by 24, it is not possible for $n$ to be a prime number.

5. Each student was given a terradi. If there were several fewer students, then each would receive 18 terradis, and if there were more of them by the same number, then some would receive 4 terradis, and the rest - 5 terradis. How many terradis did each student receive?

6. Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. The first circle passes through the center of the second. The chord $BD$ of the first circle intersects the second circle at the point $C$, which divides each other $ACB$ such that $AC:CB=1:2$. In what ratio does the point $D$ share the other $ADB$?

7. Find even numbers, the first three of which form an arithmetic progression, and the last three form a geometric progression, if it is known that the sum of the extreme numbers is 4 and the sum of the middle numbers is 2.

8. In a rhombus $ABCD$ the angle $BAD$ is acute. The circle inscribed in this rhombus touches the sides $AB$ and $CD$ of the corresponding one at points $M$ and $N$ and intersects $[CM]$ at the point $P$, and $[BN]$ at the point $Q$. Determine $|BQ|:|QN|$ if $|CP|:|PM|=9:16$.

9. Two statements are given:

a) equation $x+\sqrt{x}=a$ has no solution;

b) the inequality $4x^2+(a-3)x+1\geq 0$ is valid for all values ​​of $x$.

For what values ​​of $a$ is one of these statements true and the other false?

10. A column of soldiers moves at a constant speed, stretching over 50 meters. The messenger, having left the formation, walks at a constant speed to the end of the column, then turns and reaches the beginning of the column, turns again and goes to his place in the formation. During this time, the column will advance 120 meters. How many meters did the messenger walk?

11. A sports horse with an area of ​​0.9 hectares, shaped like a straight ruler, must be erected with a wooden fence from the north and south, and from the east and west - they were carried out. Installing one meter of a wooden fence costs 5 rubles, a wire fence costs 2 rubles. 1200 rubles were allocated for construction. Is this amount enough?

12. The sequence $(x_n)$ is given recursively: $x_0=2$, $x_1=\frac{3}{2}$, $x_{n+1}=\frac{3}{2}x_n-\frac{1}{2}x_{n-1}$. Find the formula for $x_n$. Does lim $x_n$ exist?

13. There are two direct roads from point $B$: one at point $A$, the other at point $C$, and $ABC=120^\circ$. From $A$ to $B$ and $C$ two cars are called simultaneously. At the moment when the car that left $A$ passed the next part of its journey, the distance between the cars became the smallest. What is the ratio of the speeds of the cars?
