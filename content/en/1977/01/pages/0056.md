⟦folio 54⟧

Students admitted to 3FTS, and leaders of physics and technology circles will regulate tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these problems recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical problems and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 problems for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH or its filpals, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

Moscow students receive full-time classes in physics and mathematics 2 times a week according to the 3FTS program in evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out based on the results of completing the 3FTS introductory task.

Each student completes the introductory task independently in Narus and carefully rewrites it into one school terrace. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment. Send the Terrada in a large envelope with a simple parcel post. Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Place the certificate on the inside cover of the terradi. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered.

On the outer side of the terradi cover, paste a sheet of paper filled in according to the sample:

1. Region (krai or ASSR) Kemerovo region.
2. Last name, first name, patronymic Valery Ivanovich Glukhov
3. Eighth grade school school No. 6, st. Shishchina, 26
4. Number and address school school school No. 6, st. Shishchina, 26
5. Profession parent and position father mechanic, Czech master mother accountant
6. Detailed home address 623023, e. Prokopyevsk, st. Urozhaynaya, 11, apt. 20

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 10, 1977 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be sent back.

Enrollment in the school will be carried out by the application office of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology and by order of the director of 3FTSH. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1977.

Terradies with completed tasks are assigned to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, for 3FTSH.

Students of the Arkhangelsk, Volgodsk, Kalinin, Kirov, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod, Pskov regions, Karal and Komi Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republics send work to the address: 198904, Old Peterfo, st. May 1, 100, Leningrad State University, filpal 3FTSh at MIPT.

Below are introductory tasks in physics and mathematics. In the physics assignment, problems 1-5 are intended for the study of 7th grades, tasks 1, 4-8 - for the study of 8th grades, tasks 1, 6, 8-12 - for the study of 9th grades. In the introductory mathematics task, tasks 1-5 are for 7th grades, 4-10 - for 8th grades, 7-13 - for 9th grades.

Physics

2. Find the average speed of the train if it is known that the passage of individual sections of the distance, the lengths of which are in the ratio 1:3:4:2, required time intervals in the ratio 2:4:3:1, and on the last section the speed was 80 km/h.
