⟦folio 48⟧

Moscow State University
them. M. V. Lomonosova

In this issue of the kurnal we publish samples of the options for the written exam in mathematics that were offered to applicants to some natural sciences faculties of Moscow State University in 1976. (A version of the Mechanics and Mathematics, Physics Faculties and the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics of Moscow State University will be published in one of the next issues of the journal.) We remind readers that entrance exams for natural faculties of Moscow University are held in July.

Faculty of Geography

1. A large pump pumps 2 $m^3$ more water per hour than a medium one, and a medium one pumps 2 $m^3$ more water than a small pump. Two medium and two small pumps running filled a tank with a capacity of 48 $m^3$. Immediately after this, two medium and two large pumps were turned on, which filled another tank of the same capacity. The entire work takes 9 hours. How many cubic meters of water does an average pump pump per hour?

2. Solve the equation
$$4\sqrt{x+2} = |x+1|+4.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$1-\sin 2z=\cos z+\sin z.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$\log \frac{1}{2}x) (x^2-10x+22)>0.$$

5. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, diagonal $AC$ is perpendicular to side $CD$, and diagonal $DB$ is perpendicular to side $AB$. On the extensions of the lateral sides $AB$ and $CD$ beyond the smaller base $BC$ of the trapezoid, the segments $BM$ and $CN$ are laid off so that a new trapezoid $BMNC$ is obtained, similar to the trapezoid $ABCD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid $ABCD$ if the area of ​​the trapezoid $AMND$ is $S$ and the sum of the angles CAD and BDA is $60^\circ$.

Faculty of Geology (department of general geology)

1. Find all solutions to the equation
$$2\log_{10}x=5+\log_x1000.$$

2. In the fleece carber there are two vehicles for removing rock. At the beginning of the working day there were 20 $m^3$ rocks in the carber. At first, only the first conveyor worked and lifted 2/5 of the rock. Then it was turned off and at the same time the second conveyor was turned on, which lifted the rest of the rock. As a result, all work was completed in 4 hours 20 minutes. If both conveyors were used simultaneously from the beginning to the end of the work, then it would take 2 hours to lift all the rocks. Determine the randomness (the amount of rock lifted per hour) of each transport, if it is known that the first transport has more of it than the second.

3. Find all solutions to the equation
$$-3\frac{|1-\cos x|}{1-\cos x}\sin x=\sin x-2\sin 2x.$$

4. Find all solutions to a system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{1-x}-\sqrt{2y-x}=1, \\ \sqrt{1-2y}+\sqrt{2y-x}=4. \end{cases}$$

5. In the parallelogram $ABCD$ the lengths of the diagonal $|BD|=\sqrt{10}$ and the sides $|AB|=3$, $|BC|=1$ are known. A circle lying in the plane of a parallelogram $ABCD$, radius $\sqrt{31/2}$ and centered at point $B$, intersects with a second circle that passes through points $A$ and $C$. It is known that tangents drawn through one of the intersection points of these circles are mutually perpendicular. Find the radius of the second circle.

Faculty of Geology (department of geophysics)

1. Two pipes are connected to the exchange tank 24 $m^3$. Through the first pipe, water can only pour out at a rate of 2 $m^3/4$, and the second pipe can only fill the tank. First, when the tank was empty, both pipes were opened simultaneously. After the tank was filled with water, the first pipe was closed, and the second continued to fill the tank. As a result, the tank was filled in 28 hours 48 minutes. How much water does the second pipe supply per hour?

2. Find all solutions to a system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\sin(\frac{\pi}{4}-x)}} (3\cos 2x-6\mathrm{tg}y+2)=0, \\ 18\sin^2 x-2\mathrm{tg}y-3=0. \end{cases}$$

3. Find all solutions to the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2}\log_3+x^2 (x^2-6)^2<2+\frac{1}{12}\log \sqrt{2}-\frac{1}{64}.$$
