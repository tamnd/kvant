Objectives of the entrance test at VZMSH in 1977

1. In the equality of two fractions, the numerators and denominators of which are binary numbers, the numbers are marked by letters: identical - identical, different - different:

$$\frac{KY}{PE} = \frac{KA}{KY}$$

What number does each letter mean (it is enough to give all possible answers)?

2. Given a rectangle ABCD. Find the set of points M for which

$$|MA| + |MB| = |MC| + |MD|.$$

3. 4. In a right triangle, a and b are the lengths of its legs, c is the length of the hypotenuse, h is the length of the height lowered to the hypotenuse. Prove that c + h is greater than a + b.

5. Find all solutions to the system of equations

$$\begin{cases} x^2 = y + z, \\ y^2 = z + x, \\ z^2 = x^2 + y^2. \end{cases}$$

7. The car and the cyclist left simultaneously from A to B. Having traveled a third of the way, the cyclist stopped and moved off only when the car was a third of the way to B. The car, having reached B, drove back to A without stopping. Who will come first: the car in A or the cyclist in B?

8. It is known that the numbers 2077 and 100 when divided by the natural number a give the same remainders. Find the number a.

9. A large rectangle is divided into 1 cm×1 cm cells. A number is written inside each cell. It is known that the sum of the numbers in each horizontal row is equal to 1, and in each vertical column is equal to 2. Can the area of ​​a rectangle be equal to 1976 cm²?

Correspondence School of Physics and Technology

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute is recruiting students from all-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in the 8th, 9th and 10th grades for the 1977/78 academic year.

The form of education in our school differs from the work taught to children, taking into account the lessons. Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches you to work with additional literature, and express your thoughts concisely.

Over the 10 years of work, 3FTS has provided its graduates with good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics, many of whom have become students leading the challenge of our country.

The goal of our school is to help students study independently in physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting to 3FTSH, predestination is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where our school especially needs help.

3FTS also accepts physics and technology circles, which can be organized locally on the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The circle leaders recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. The circle is accepted into the 3FTSH if the school director reports to the 3FTSH the name of the circle leader and a list of circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).
