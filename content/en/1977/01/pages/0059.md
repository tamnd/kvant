⟦folio 57⟧

To take university courses with a complete and clear understanding of the essence of the matter. Going beyond the general school course, we do not, however, consider university education. All this ensures the rapid entry of school graduates into independent scientific work. This is facilitated at school by assignments in physics and mathematics workshops, special courses and seminars, clubs and consultations (in the evening).

Lectures at the Physics and Mathematics School are given by professors and associate professors of Moscow University. However, there are not many lectures - two hours a week in mathematics and two hours in physics. Classes in mathematics and physics are taught by experienced teachers, graduate students and students of Moscow State University (many of them are our graduates). The Sata system of teaching mathematics and physics at school is strong in its traditions, the creation at school of a special atmosphere in which interest in science, sports and love of nature, interest in literature and art, social work - all together support the education of a full-fledged person.

The final assessment of the results of the boarding school’s work can be obtained from an analysis of the conditions of graduates of past years. Graduates of 1964-1969, a significant part of them, completed graduate school and completed candidate dissertations (and so far only one, a doctoral dissertation). They have published hundreds of scientific papers, some of them outstanding. Some of them were invited to speak about their scientific results at scientific conferences and international congresses.

Several thousand people come to us for written exams. And although we cannot accept them all, we strongly advise you to bravely try your hand. Our country urgently needs great scientists and simply talented scientists. Don't deprive us of the opportunity to find them!

We invite all lovers of mathematics and physics to our school!
