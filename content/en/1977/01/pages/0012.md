⟦folio 10⟧

This brown gnome is about seven hundred years old.
In his “youth” he was a kind of spiritual homemaker. He poured body into the figurine and placed it on the smoldering corners. Soon steam began to escape from the gnome's mouth. The figurine was placed in front of the fireplace so that the stream of steam was directed under the stove. So the gnome fanned the flames in the stove. The hole in the gnome's body is the result of an "accident".

While this figurine may be considered simply a fun toy, it certainly appears to be an example of a physical phenomenon being used. After all, LIZES and MAGNETS, before becoming scientific instruments, were considered just toys. And the discovery of the gyroscopic effect was led by an ordinary toy top.
Currently, this gnome is kept in the Vienna Kunsthistorisches Museum.
