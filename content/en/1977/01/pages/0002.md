⟦folio 18⟧

DECREE OF THE PRESIDIUM OF THE SUPREME COUNCIL OF THE USSR

on the awarding of the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee
Hero of the Soviet Union, Hero of Socialist Labor
Comrade BREZHNEV Leonid Ilyich
Order of Lenin and the second Gold Star medal

For outstanding services to the Communist Party and the Soviet state in communist construction, active, fruitful activities in managing the peace and security of peoples, for great personal contribution to the victory over the Nazi invaders in the Great Patriotic War, to strengthening the economic and defense power of the Soviet Union and in connection with the semi-celebration of the birth of the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee, Hero of the Soviet Union, Hero of Socialist Labor, Comrade Brezhnev Leonid Ilyich with the Order of Lenin and the second Gold Star medal of the Hero of the Soviet Union.

Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR N. PODGORNY.
Secretary of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet of the USSR M. GEORGADZE.

Moscow, Kremlin, December 18, 1976
