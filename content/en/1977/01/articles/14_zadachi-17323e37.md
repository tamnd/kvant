⟦folio 40⟧

Quests

1. 10 players took part in the world championship among professionals in tic-tac-poly on an endless checkered board. The loser of the game, having lost hope of the main prize, left the championship. What is the maximum number of participants who could win two games each? (In “tic-tac-poly” on an endless board, the winner is the one who places five of his icons in a row along one line - vertical, horizontal or diagonal; there are no ties.)

2. When one brain teaser was asked why he solves problems so successfully, the answer was:

H:E=0, I'M GETTING OLD I'M GETTING OLD

Try deciphering this entry.

3. In this example for multiplication (see figure), the same numbers are encrypted with the same letters, different numbers are encrypted with different letters, any numbers can appear together with asterisks. Decipher the example.

4. In an incorrect match made of matches (see figure), rearrange one match so that it turns into a correct one.
