⟦folio 26⟧

Tasks
M421 — M425; F433 — F437

M421*. For what natural numbers $m$ and $n$ ($m < n$) can we shade some cells of an infinite sheet of checkered paper black so that any rectangle of size $m \times n$ contains exactly one black cell? Start with a simpler problem: consider the case of $m=2$ and $n=3, 4, 5$ (Fig. 1).

S. Okhitin, 10th grade student

M422. Divide an arbitrary triangle into seven equilateral triangles, three of which are congruent to each other.

A. Khabelashvili

M423*. Prove that for any real $x, y$ and $z$ the following inequality holds:
$(x^2 + y^2 - z^2)(x^2 + z^2 - y^2)(y^2 + z^2 - x^2) \leqslant (x + y - z)^2(x + z - y)^2(y + z - x)^2$.

R. Sheichvit

M424. Through each vertex of the tetrahedron a plane is drawn containing the center of the circle circumscribed about the opposite face and perpendicular to the opposite face. Prove that these four planes intersect at one point.

A. Yagubyants

M425*. Is there a natural number $N$ such that every rational number between and one can be represented as a sum of $N$ numbers that are the inverses of the natural numbers?

F433. Two identical plano-convex lenses with a focal length of $F=20$ cm each are located at a distance of 30 cm from each other. The optical axes of the lenses coincide, the lenses face each other with their flat sides. Where will the image of a source located at a distance of 40 cm from the left lens be located if the space between the lenses is filled with glass with a refractive index the same as that of the lenses?

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate your first and last name, school number and class in which you will study.
