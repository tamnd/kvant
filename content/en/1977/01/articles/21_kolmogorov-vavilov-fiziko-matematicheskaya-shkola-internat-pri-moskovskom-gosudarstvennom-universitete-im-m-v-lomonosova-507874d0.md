Physics and mathematics boarding school at Moscow State University. M. V. Lomonosova

For more than 13 years, specialized boarding schools have been operating in Kyiv, Leningrad, Moscow, Novosibirsk, Yerevan and Tbilisi. The goal of these schools is to provide teenagers living in distant centers with the same opportunities for advancement in science as students in university cities. We asked the board of trustees of the boarding school at Moscow State University, academician A. N. Kolmogorov, and the head of the educational department for the mathematical cycle V. V. Vavilov to tell us about their school.

Many readers «Kvant» have probably seen the television film “Ask, Boys,” which, in particular, talks about the Internet at Moscow State University. This boarding school accepts students who have successfully completed the eighth grade and live in the Central European part of the USSR, the Urals, the Volga region, the North Caucasus and Belarus. The selection of future schoolchildren is carried out by the selection committee based on competitive exams in major subjects, which are set daily during regional competitions. The dates of these exams can be found in the region’s department of public education or in the Ministry of Education of the relevant resource office (it is not necessary to attend the Olympiad for admission to a boarding school; students who missed the exam for any reason can apply directly to the school, its address: Moscow, 121357, Kremenicheskaya, 29, boarding school, admissions office).

If your dream of studying science came to you only in the ninth grade, then you can also take part in the exam - we also accept students for one year in the tenth grade. True, the competitive requirements here are especially high (there are only 60 places in the one-year stream).

Based on the exam results, the best candidates are called to the summer training camp (FMS anniversary), where final enrollment is made (in 9th or 10th grade).

3. Before lights out. Charts, charts...
4. FMS stands for “Football and Mathematics School”, that’s how the guys’ names stand for it. Everyone at school is interested in sports - from students to the director.

To take university courses with a complete and clear understanding of the essence of the matter. Going beyond the general school course, we do not, however, consider university education. All this ensures the rapid entry of school graduates into independent scientific work. This is facilitated at school by assignments in physics and mathematics workshops, special courses and seminars, clubs and consultations (in the evening).

Lectures at the Physics and Mathematics School are given by professors and associate professors of Moscow University. However, there are not many lectures - two hours a week in mathematics and two hours in physics. Classes in mathematics and physics are taught by experienced teachers, graduate students and students of Moscow State University (many of them are our graduates). The Sata system of teaching mathematics and physics at school is strong in its traditions, the creation at school of a special atmosphere in which interest in science, sports and love of nature, interest in literature and art, social work - all together support the education of a full-fledged person.

The final assessment of the results of the boarding school’s work can be obtained from an analysis of the conditions of graduates of past years. Graduates of 1964-1969, a significant part of them, completed graduate school and completed candidate dissertations (and so far only one, a doctoral dissertation). They have published hundreds of scientific papers, some of them outstanding. Some of them were invited to speak about their scientific results at scientific conferences and international congresses.

Several thousand people come to us for written exams. And although we cannot accept them all, we strongly advise you to bravely try your hand. Our country urgently needs great scientists and simply talented scientists. Don't deprive us of the opportunity to find them!

We invite all lovers of mathematics and physics to our school!
