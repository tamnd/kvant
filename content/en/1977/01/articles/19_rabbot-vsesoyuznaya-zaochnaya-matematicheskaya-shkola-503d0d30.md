⟦folio 52⟧

Reception is announced

Weighty correspondence mathematics school

The All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) admits students from rural classes. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into the school.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1977. School education is free.

Students accepted into the school will be regulated by problems that contain explanations of theoretical issues and problems to solve. The duration of training is three years.

The tasks of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 20, 1977. Once your papers are reviewed (around July 1977), you will be notified whether you have been accepted into the school. Moreover, the work will take into account not only the number of solved problems, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without justification may not be read. If a task may contain missing elements, then all of them must be indicated.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. Entry papers will not be sent back and solutions for them will not be issued.

In the envelope along with your notebook you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14 cm x 6 cm with your postal address (we will stick it on the envelope when we send you a response).

Paste a piece of checkered paper onto the cover of the notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the following sample (initially the work will not be checked):

Region
Last name, first name
Year of birth
Class
School (full name)
Last name, first name, missing mathematics
Place of work and position of the parent
