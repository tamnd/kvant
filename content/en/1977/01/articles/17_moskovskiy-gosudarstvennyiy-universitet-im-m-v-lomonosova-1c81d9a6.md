Moscow State University
them. M. V. Lomonosova

In this issue of the kurnal we publish samples of the options for the written exam in mathematics that were offered to applicants to some natural sciences faculties of Moscow State University in 1976. (A version of the Mechanics and Mathematics, Physics Faculties and the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics of Moscow State University will be published in one of the next issues of the journal.) We remind readers that entrance exams for natural faculties of Moscow University are held in July.

Faculty of Geography

1. A large pump pumps 2 $m^3$ more water per hour than a medium one, and a medium one pumps 2 $m^3$ more water than a small pump. Two medium and two small pumps running filled a tank with a capacity of 48 $m^3$. Immediately after this, two medium and two large pumps were turned on, which filled another tank of the same capacity. The entire work takes 9 hours. How many cubic meters of water does an average pump pump per hour?

2. Solve the equation
$$4\sqrt{x+2} = |x+1|+4.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$1-\sin 2z=\cos z+\sin z.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$\log \frac{1}{2}x) (x^2-10x+22)>0.$$

5. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, diagonal $AC$ is perpendicular to side $CD$, and diagonal $DB$ is perpendicular to side $AB$. On the extensions of the lateral sides $AB$ and $CD$ beyond the smaller base $BC$ of the trapezoid, the segments $BM$ and $CN$ are laid off so that a new trapezoid $BMNC$ is obtained, similar to the trapezoid $ABCD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid $ABCD$ if the area of ​​the trapezoid $AMND$ is $S$ and the sum of the angles CAD and BDA is $60^\circ$.

Faculty of Geology (department of general geology)

1. Find all solutions to the equation
$$2\log_{10}x=5+\log_x1000.$$

2. In the fleece carber there are two vehicles for removing rock. At the beginning of the working day there were 20 $m^3$ rocks in the carber. At first, only the first conveyor worked and lifted 2/5 of the rock. Then it was turned off and at the same time the second conveyor was turned on, which lifted the rest of the rock. As a result, all work was completed in 4 hours 20 minutes. If both conveyors were used simultaneously from the beginning to the end of the work, then it would take 2 hours to lift all the rocks. Determine the randomness (the amount of rock lifted per hour) of each transport, if it is known that the first transport has more of it than the second.

3. Find all solutions to the equation
$$-3\frac{|1-\cos x|}{1-\cos x}\sin x=\sin x-2\sin 2x.$$

4. Find all solutions to a system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{1-x}-\sqrt{2y-x}=1, \\ \sqrt{1-2y}+\sqrt{2y-x}=4. \end{cases}$$

5. In the parallelogram $ABCD$ the lengths of the diagonal $|BD|=\sqrt{10}$ and the sides $|AB|=3$, $|BC|=1$ are known. A circle lying in the plane of a parallelogram $ABCD$, radius $\sqrt{31/2}$ and centered at point $B$, intersects with a second circle that passes through points $A$ and $C$. It is known that tangents drawn through one of the intersection points of these circles are mutually perpendicular. Find the radius of the second circle.

Faculty of Geology (department of geophysics)

1. Two pipes are connected to the exchange tank 24 $m^3$. Through the first pipe, water can only pour out at a rate of 2 $m^3/4$, and the second pipe can only fill the tank. First, when the tank was empty, both pipes were opened simultaneously. After the tank was filled with water, the first pipe was closed, and the second continued to fill the tank. As a result, the tank was filled in 28 hours 48 minutes. How much water does the second pipe supply per hour?

2. Find all solutions to a system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\sin(\frac{\pi}{4}-x)}} (3\cos 2x-6\mathrm{tg}y+2)=0, \\ 18\sin^2 x-2\mathrm{tg}y-3=0. \end{cases}$$

3. Find all solutions to the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2}\log_3+x^2 (x^2-6)^2<2+\frac{1}{12}\log \sqrt{2}-\frac{1}{64}.$$

4. In the parallelogram $ABCD$ from side $|AD|=12$ and diagonal $|AC|=16$ a segment $EF$ is drawn connecting point $E$ of side $BC$ with point $F$ of diagonal $BD$. Points $E$ and $F$ are chosen such that $\frac{|EC|}{|EF|} = \frac{1}{4}, \frac{|FD|}{|BF|} = \frac{1}{3}$.

It is known that the point $M$ of intersection of the diagonal $AC$ with the segment $EF$ divides this segment in the ratio $\frac{|EM|}{|MF|} = \frac{1}{3}$. Find the lengths of side $AB$ and diagonal $BD$.

5. Find all numbers $p$ for which there is a unique number $x$ that simultaneously satisfies the following condition: $\sin \pi x=0, (2x+14p^2-7)(4x-4p^2-15) \leq 0$.

Faculty of Postal Science

1. From the cities $A$ and $B$ two trains approached each other at the same time. They moved non-stop at a constant speed and met 30 hours after leaving. Find how long it took each train to reach its destination if the first train arrived at point $B$ 25 hours later than the second one arrived at point $A$.

2. Given an equilateral trapezoid, the length of the upper base is half the length of the lower one. Determine in what ratio the lower base is divided by a straight line that is parallel to the diagonal, passes below and cuts off a triangle with an area 4 times less than the area of ​​the trapezoid.

3. Solve the equation
$$2(\sin^2 2x+1)=\sin 8x+6\cos^2 2x.$$
4. Find all values ​​of the parameter $\alpha$ for which the sum of the squares of the roots of the equation
$$x^2+x\cos \alpha-\frac{\sin 2\alpha}{2}-1=0$$
$\sqrt{2/2}$ is greater than the larger root of the equation
$$x^2-x(3+\sin^2\alpha)+\sin^2\alpha+2=0.$$
5. Solve equation $\log_{x-1}(1+2x^4-x^6)<0$.

Faculty of Biology

1. Solve the equation
$$|\cos x-\sin x|=1+\sin 2x.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\log_x\frac{1}{7}+\log_{\frac{1}{7}}x>10|\log_{\frac{1}{7}}x|.$$
3. There are two aqueous solutions No. 1 and No. 2 of substances $A$ and $B$, various mismatches of substances $A$, $B$ and water. In solution No. 1 there is 4 times more substance $B$ than substance $A$, and the same amount of water as substance $B$. By mixing 3 keras of solution No. 1, 6 keras of solution No. 2 and adding 1 kera of water, we get a new solution in which substances $A$ are 2 times less than substances $B$, and 3 times less than water. It is required to determine the weight ratio of substances $A$, $B$ and water in solution No. 2.

4. A circle with center $O_1$ is written into an equilateral trapezoid. Another circle with center $O_2$ located outside the circle touches the large base and side of the trapezoid. Both circles intersect and have a common chord $MN$. It is known that $\widehat{MO}_1N=60^\circ, \widehat{MO}_2N=120^\circ, |MN|=2$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

5. Prove that the equation $2x-x^2=\frac{2}{x}$ has no positive solutions.

Faculty of Chemistry

1. The excavator was tasked with digging three identical channels. The first and second excavators were present for work at the same time. The third began work when the second had dug a fifth of his canals, and finished when the first had to dig a quarter of his canals. The productivity of the second excavator is 2 m/h less, and the productivity of the first is 4 m/h less than the productivity of the third excavator. Find a productive third excavator.

2. Solve inequality
$$2x^2-|x|\log_3 78+\log_3 26<1.$$
3. In the triangle $ABC$ the bicestris $BD$ is drawn. The values ​​of angles $ADB$, $BAD$, $ABD$, $ACB$ in this order form an arithmetic progression. Find the length of the altitude of the triangle $ABC$ dropped from the vertex $C$ if it is known that $|AB|=1$.

4. Find all solutions to the equation
$$4-2\sin x-3\cos x=
=4\cos^2\left(x-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)+\sqrt{3}(2-\sin x-2\cos x),$$
which are also solutions to the equation
$$2\sin x=\text{ctg}x+3\text{tg}^2x+\sqrt{3}$$

5. In kufa $ABCDA'B'C'D'$, where $[AA']$, $[BB']$, $[CC']$ and $[DD']$ are parallel edges, the plane passes through the diagonal $A'C'$ of the cube face and through the middle of edge $AD$. Find the distance from the middle of the edge $AB$ to the plane $π$, if the length of the cube edge is 3.
