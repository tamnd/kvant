Correspondence School of Physics and Technology

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute is recruiting students from all-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in the 8th, 9th and 10th grades for the 1977/78 academic year.

The form of education in our school differs from the work taught to children, taking into account the lessons. Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches you to work with additional literature, and express your thoughts concisely.

Over the 10 years of work, 3FTS has provided its graduates with good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics, many of whom have become students leading the challenge of our country.

The goal of our school is to help students study independently in physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting to 3FTSH, predestination is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where our school especially needs help.

3FTS also accepts physics and technology circles, which can be organized locally on the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The circle leaders recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. The circle is accepted into the 3FTSH if the school director reports to the 3FTSH the name of the circle leader and a list of circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS, and leaders of physics and technology circles will regulate tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these problems recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical problems and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 problems for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH or its filpals, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

Assignment for the introductory test at 3FTSH in 1977

Physics

1. Without resorting to weighing on a scale, determine the average density of a chicken egg.
2. Find the average speed of the train if it is known that the passage of individual sections of the distance, the lengths of which are in the ratio 1:3:4:2, required time intervals in the ratio 2:4:3:1, and on the last section the speed was 80 km/h.

3. Communicating vessels in the form of cylindrical tubes 10 cm long, the cross-sectional areas of which differ by half, are connected by a rubber hose and filled with water up to half the height. A cylinder of julium 10 cm high was placed in a narrow tube. How much did the water levels in the vessels change if the cross-sectional area of ​​the juli cylinder is practically the same as the cross-sectional area of ​​the narrow tube?

4. The fuel was obtained by mixing equal volumes of gasoline and alcohol. What amount of heat is released when 1 kcal of the mixture is burned?

5. A direct electric current is passed through the nitrate solution, first with a force of 200 A for 5 hours, and then with a force of 150 A for 8 hours. What is the mass of sulfur released from the solution if

6. Using experimental data, estimate the average density of the Sun.

7. After rolling down the mountain, the sled begins to move along a horizontal section of the road at a speed of 2 m/sec. The coefficient of friction between the runners of the sled and the road is 0.02. How far will the sled travel in 15 seconds?

8. Passengers do not experience indirect errors unless their weight more than doubles during the flight. What is the maximum acceleration in level flight allowed by this condition?

9. A pendulum of mass 1 ka was given such a shock that it made a full revolution in the vertical plane. In the upper position of the pendulum, the tension in the thread is half the tension force at rest, when the load hangs calmly on the thread. What is the tension in the string as the pendulum passes through its equilibrium position?

10. The water is heated electronically at constant power. What will take longer - to heat the body from 10 to 20 or from 80 to 90°C?

11. A certain amount of a mixture of argon and hydrogen is introduced under a heavy piston, which slides without friction inside a vertically located evacuated cylinder. As a result, the piston moves upward and is located at a height of 2/3 of the height of the cylinder, counting from its bottom. However, over time, the piston moves downward, since the materials from which the piston is made turn out to be permeable to hydrogen. The final piston is located at a height equal to 1/4 of the height of the cylinder. These ratios of the masses of argon and hydrogen in the mixture.

Matematika

1. The tank together holds 1000 liters of water. Every day 600 liters are consumed, and at night they should move half the amount that is in the tank in the morning. On Monday morning the tank was full. Will there be enough water in the tank on Thursday?

2. Are there natural values ​​$k, m, n$ for which $(2k+1)^2-(2m+1)^2=4(2n+1)$ is true.

3. A straight line parallel to one of the sides of a triangle divides it into equal figures. In what ratio does this line divide the other two sides of the triangle?

4. Prove or disprove the following statements:

a) For $n^2-1(n\geq 5)$ to be divisible by 24, it is sufficient that $n$ be a prime number;

b) For $n^2-1(n\geq 5)$ to be divisible by 24, it is not possible for $n$ to be a prime number.

5. Each student was given a terradi. If there were several fewer students, then each would receive 18 terradis, and if there were more of them by the same number, then some would receive 4 terradis, and the rest - 5 terradis. How many terradis did each student receive?

6. Two circles intersect at points $A$ and $B$. The first circle passes through the center of the second. The chord $BD$ of the first circle intersects the second circle at the point $C$, which divides each other $ACB$ such that $AC:CB=1:2$. In what ratio does the point $D$ share the other $ADB$?

7. Find even numbers, the first three of which form an arithmetic progression, and the last three form a geometric progression, if it is known that the sum of the extreme numbers is 4 and the sum of the middle numbers is 2.

8. In a rhombus $ABCD$ the angle $BAD$ is acute. The circle inscribed in this rhombus touches the sides $AB$ and $CD$ of the corresponding one at points $M$ and $N$ and intersects $[CM]$ at the point $P$, and $[BN]$ at the point $Q$. Determine $|BQ|:|QN|$ if $|CP|:|PM|=9:16$.

9. Two statements are given:

a) equation $x+\sqrt{x}=a$ has no solution;

b) the inequality $4x^2+(a-3)x+1\geq 0$ is valid for all values ​​of $x$.

For what values ​​of $a$ is one of these statements true and the other false?

10. A column of soldiers moves at a constant speed, stretching over 50 meters. The messenger, having left the formation, walks at a constant speed to the end of the column, then turns and reaches the beginning of the column, turns again and goes to his place in the formation. During this time, the column will advance 120 meters. How many meters did the messenger walk?

11. A sports horse with an area of ​​0.9 hectares, shaped like a straight ruler, must be erected with a wooden fence from the north and south, and from the east and west - they were carried out. Installing one meter of a wooden fence costs 5 rubles, a wire fence costs 2 rubles. 1200 rubles were allocated for construction. Is this amount enough?

12. The sequence $(x_n)$ is given recursively: $x_0=2$, $x_1=\frac{3}{2}$, $x_{n+1}=\frac{3}{2}x_n-\frac{1}{2}x_{n-1}$. Find the formula for $x_n$. Does lim $x_n$ exist?

13. There are two direct roads from point $B$: one at point $A$, the other at point $C$, and $ABC=120^\circ$. From $A$ to $B$ and $C$ two cars are called simultaneously. At the moment when the car that left $A$ passed the next part of its journey, the distance between the cars became the smallest. What is the ratio of the speeds of the cars?
