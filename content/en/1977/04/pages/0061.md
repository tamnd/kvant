⟦folio 59⟧

From the eighth grade, independent work in the section begins. Each member of the society receives a specific topic and a scientific consultant, and is assigned to a scientific institution.

Work in sections is carried out in two directions:
1. Theoretical classes, lectures by scientists, seminars, student reports on the topics of their research, reports on outstanding scientists.
2. Research work under the guidance of a scientific consultant.

Sections are divided into groups depending on the research topics. For example, the physics section is divided into the following groups: theoretical physics, physics of semiconductors, electronics and electronic devices, physics of the atomic nucleus, optical radiation and spectroscopy, general physics, pulsed and gas electronics, solid state physics. These groups work on the basis of the Institute of Applied Physics of the Academy of Sciences of the MSSR, the State Physics University, and the Electrophysical Faculty of the Chisinau Polytechnic Institute. The mathematics section is divided into groups: mathematical logic, modern issues of algebra, applied mathematics and programming, geometric transformations, source of mathematics. They work on the basis of the Institute of Mathematics of the Academy of Sciences of the MSSR, the Faculty of Mathematics of the Chisinau State University, and the Departments of Mathematics of the Polytechnic and Agricultural Institutes.

All members of the society have membership cards, in which their personal plans for research work are entered and it is noted (signed by the scientific supervisor of the section) how the work is being carried out. The most distinguished members of society receive references upon graduation that provide them with certain advantages when entering higher educational institutions in Moldova in the relevant specialization.

More than 150 scientists, college teachers, and graduate students work with seniors in the scientific community, engaging them in scientific research.

Important events in the life of the VIITORUL society are annual and republican scientific and creative conferences of students. In addition to members of the society, famous scientists, as well as schoolchildren invited from other resource persons, study in them.

Thus, at the III city conference in Chisinau, held under the motto: “Ability, work, talent,” 20 reports were read on various problems in physics and 7 in mathematics, and a mathematical Olympiad was held.
