⟦folio 7⟧

There are tongues, the movement of which creates sound vibrations in the air.

Not any vibrations can be excited in a pipe, but only those to which the pipe is tuned. The tuning of a pipe is determined by the correspondence between its length and the wavelength. In a pipe closed at both ends, it is possible to excite only such waves (standing) for which half the wavelength is placed an integer number of times in the pipe. To perform musical works on the organ, you need to excite a variety of sounds in the pipes. The organs therefore contain thousands of pipes.

In a similar way, electromagnetic oscillations are established in closed metal pipes. The length of the pipe must be well matched to the frequency of electromagnetic oscillations at which it must operate. This carefully made, polished, studied tube is the linear accelerator's resonator and is placed along its drift tube. To excite vibrations, a radiator is inserted into the pipe - an antenna or coil.

figure 3 shows a picture of the lines of the high-frequency field that creates in the resonant at some point in time. The arrows depict the new electric field lines. The magnetic field lines on figure are not shown. They look like rings surrounding bundles of electric field lines. After half the period of electromagnetic oscillations, the new lines will change their direction to the opposite.

The electric field voltage created in the resonant is maximum near its axis and drops almost to zero near the walls. If this were not so, then the electric field would create large points in the walls, which would lead to heating of the walls - a waste of power. A field of this configuration can be created in a pipe only if its diameter is matched to the wavelength. We see that both the length and transverse dimensions of the resonant must be selected very carefully.

Drift tubes placed in the resonator somewhat distort the picture of electromagnetic oscillations. A space free from vibrations appears inside the tubes, since the field cannot penetrate through their conducting walls. The field will be squeezed out of the drift tubes into accelerating gaps.

By accelerating particles to very high energies, there is no need to “hide” particles in drift tubes. Through the accelerator, ordinary electromagnetic waves can be transmitted. If the speed of the particle is close to the speed of light, then, once it hits a favorable section of the wave, where the directional vector of the electric field has reached the magnitude of the direction, the particle will accelerate all the time.

The 《very large》 energies that we talked about can differ from each other for different particles. For protons and other heavy particles, these are energies of the order of many thousands of MeV. And for electrons, the energy of the order of several MeV is already very large.

The accelerators we have just talked about are called traveling wave accelerators. A large linear accelerator with a traveling wave for accelerating electrons to an energy of 2000 MeV was built at the Physico-Technical Institute of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR in Kharkov.
