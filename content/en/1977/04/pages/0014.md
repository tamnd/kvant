The trajectories of electrons under such conditions have the form of a circle touching each other at one point, as shown in figure 7. An accelerating gap (resonator) is placed at a point common to all circles. Having passed through it again, the frequency accelerates and moves to the next circle. Other electrons move along previous circles.

For the micropron to work successfully, it is necessary that electrons arrive at the accelerating gap at the moment when the field has the desired direction. It can be shown that this condition is satisfied if the frequency, when passing through the accelerating gap, acquires an energy equal to (or a multiple of) its rest energy $m_0 c^2$. (This calculation is very simple, and using the formulas we have already given, the reader can do it independently.) Then each next turn of the electron trajectory will take longer than the previous one, for a time equal to one or several periods of oscillation of the accelerating field. And each time it will enter the resonator at the moment when the accelerating field is directed in the right direction.

Such accelerators can only be built for electrons, because for heavier particles the required energy increase is fantastically large. And to accelerate electrons, microprons are successfully used and make it possible to obtain continuous beams of high intensity.

Exercises

1. In linear accelerator technology, the length of the shaft tubes is expressed not through the period of electromagnetic oscillations, but through their wavelength. In this case, along with the particle speed $v$, the relative speed $\beta = v/c$ is introduced, where $c$ is the speed of light. Obtain the corresponding formula.

$$E^2 = p^2 c^2 + m_0^2 c^4.$$

In this formula, $E$ is the total energy equal to $K + m_0 c^2$. Show that the formula

$$p = \frac{1}{c} \sqrt{K (K + 2 m_0 c^2)},$$

which we used is equivalent to this correspondence. (We remind you that for protons $m_0 c^2 = 938$ MeV.)

3. The phasotron in Dubna accelerates protons to an energy of 680 MeV. How many times should the frequency of the accelerating high-frequency field be during the acceleration time? Assume that the magnetic field does not depend on radius.

4. Show that for the micropron to work successfully, it is necessary that each time it passes through the accelerating gap, the particle acquires energy $m_0 c^2$ (or several $m_0 c^2$).
