10. Let n be the longest stroke. Then, in order to mark a field with a flat or a minus, it is enough to know the signs only in the previous n fields. Divide the strip into successive groups of n cells. According to the Dirichlet principle (see «Kvant», 1977, No. 2, p. 17), the arrangement of signs in any two groups coincides. Let these two groups start from cells k and p, respectively, with k < p. Then the arrangement of signs on cells k, k + 1, ..., p - 1 coincides with the arrangement on cells p, p + 1, ..., p + (p - k - 1) and so on. In other words, there is a period of length p - k starting from cell k.
