Option 3
(specialty: applied mathematics)

1. Pupils of the ninth and tenth grades took part in the chess tournament. All tenth graders scored 27 points. The number of points scored by tenth graders in games against ninth graders is 21 more than the number of points scored by ninth graders in games against tenth graders. How many ninth and tenth graders participated in Turin, if the winning laps start like 5:2.

2. Prove that for all values ​​of $x$ and $y$ the inequality holds
$$x^2 + xy + y^2 + 2x - 2y + 4 \ge 0.$$

3. Determine the angles of a right triangle, knowing that the radius of the circle described around it is related to the radius of the inscribed circle as 5:2.

4. Find $\alpha + 2\beta$ if $3\sin^2\alpha + 2\sin^2\beta = 1$, $3\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin 2\beta$, $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$, $0 < \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$.

Physics

Faculty of Automation and Computer Science

1. In a time of $t = 10$ sec the body traveled a distance of $s = 18$ m, while its speed increased $n = 5$ times. Assuming the motion to be uniformly accelerated, determine the absolute value of the acceleration of the body.

2. A ball, the dimensions of which can be preserved, is suspended on a weightless and inextensible thread of length $l$. The ball moves at a constant speed along a circle in a horizontal plane. Determine the linear speed of the ball if the thread, describing the finite surface during its movement, makes an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical.

3. A small ball hangs on a thin silk thread in the space between the horizontal round plates of a flat air condenser. Ball charge $q = 10$ units. charge of SGSE. When the capacitor plates were given a charge of $|Q| = 980$ units. charge of SGSE, the tension force of the thread increased by 2 times. Determine the tension in the thread when the ball is in the field of a charged capacitor. The radius of the capacitor plates is $R = 10$ cm; Massin threads can be saved.

4. A primrose capsule made of rigid fiber floats in the water. The mass of the box is $m = 100$ e, the bottom area is $S = 50$ cm², the height is $H = 6$ cm. Determine the height of the above-water part of the box. The density of water is $\rho_B = 1$ e/cm³.

5. If three identical elements connected in parallel are shorted to an external resistance $R = 0,3$ om, the same power will be released on it as in the case of a series connection/nine of the same elements. What is the internal resistance of one of the elements?

6. Ultrafilt rays with a length of $\lambda_1 = 0,3$ µm, hitting the cathode of photozlegans, cause a flow of photozlegans at a speed of $|v_1| = 10^6$ m/sec. What wavelength of light must be used to irradiate a photosolvent so that the kinetic energy of the photosolvent becomes $E_2 = 4 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J? Planck's constant $h = 6,62 \cdot 10^{-34}$ j·sec; speed of light $c = 3 \cdot 10^8$ m/sec; mass of electran $m_e = 9,1 \times 10^{-31}$ ke.

All other faculties

1. The first half of the train's journey is silk at a constant speed 1.5 times greater than the second half. The average speed of the train along the entire route is $|v_{cp}| = 43,2$ km/h. What are the speeds of the train at each half of the journey?

2. Two gria with masses $m_1 = 7$ke and $m_2 = 11$ke rise at the ends of a thread, thrown over a stationary block. At first, the griyas are at the same height. How long after the start of movement does the lighter gria become $h = 10$ cm higher than the heavier one? Take care of the mass of the block and thread, as well as air resistance.

3. A mathematical mat, the length of which is $l = 25$ cm, makes $n = 120$ movements for $t = 2$ minutes. Determine the acceleration of gravity from these data.

4. The gas was heated from temperature $27^\circ\text{C}$ to temperature $39^\circ\text{C}$. By what percentage does the volume of gas accelerate if the pressure remains unchanged?

5. A homogeneous conductor has a resistance of $R_1$. How many equal parts must this conductor be divided into so that the total resistance of its parts connected in parallel becomes equal to $R_2$?

6. A bullet of mass $m = 10$ e, flying at a speed of $|v| = 10^3$ m/sec, hits an ice floe. Determine the mass of the ice that it stretches, if it is known that 50% of the kinetic energy of the bullet turned into heat. The initial temperature of the ice floe is $0^\circ\text{C}$, the specific temperature of the ice floe is $\lambda = 3,3 \cdot 10^5$ j/ke.

7. The object is located at a distance $2F$ from the optical center of the scattering line with a focal length $F$. Where and what will be the image of this object? The decision to devote to the drawing.
