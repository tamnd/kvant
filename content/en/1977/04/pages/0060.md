Scientific Society of Students "VIITORUL"

On October 2, 1976, a scientific and theoretical conference of schoolchildren was held in Chisinau, in the Central Lecture Hall of the Knowledge Society. More than six natural sciences with Moscow scientists - doctors of physical and mathematical sciences, professors of Moscow universities V. Kresin, V. Tychinsky and V. Levin. The meeting was opened by the famous Moldovan physicist, academician of the MSSR Academy of Sciences S. Radutsan. The guys listened attentively to stories about the achievements and problems of low-temperature physics, quantum electronics, and the future of modern mathematics.

Most of those who came to this meeting were members of the scientific society of schoolchildren "VIITORUL", which means "FUTURE" in Russian. This society arose in 1971 at the Republican Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren on the initiative of scientists from Moldova. Now there are more than 2,000 members in its ranks.

The joint resolution of the biryu of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Moldova and the Ministry of Public Education of the MSSR on the creation of the Republican Scientific Society of Students says: “Now, when a large number of interest clubs, clubs, and optional groups of high school students are successfully operating in the schools of Chisinau and the republic, when Olympiads, conferences and competitions in various fields of significance have become a tradition, there is an opportunity and need to move to a high-quality new form of work on the development of cognitive interests and creative abilities of students." This new form became the scientific society “VIITORUL”.

The goals and objectives of the society are formulated in its charter as follows:

1. The society aims to involve high school students in scientific research work, familiarize them with the methods and techniques of simple scientific research, and teach them the ability to handle the instruments and equipment necessary for research.

2. The society expands the knowledge and horizons of students in various fields of science, technology, art, literature, instills interest and skills in scientific research, helps in choosing a specialty and educates the public educational branch of science.

3. The goal of the society is not only to enable schoolchildren to master the material for the first time, but to educate students as propagandists of any branch of science.

4. The duty and responsibility of high school students is to appreciate Soviet science and be proud of it. To deeply study and promote creative scientific thinking, to know their life, work and creative path, learn from them passionate love for their Motherland, their people, to bring out their best traits - high ideology, patriotism, citizenship, hard work, determination.

The creation of a scientific society was preceded by a lot of preparatory work: shows, competitions, conferences. Those wishing to become members of the society must submit recommendations from teachers of the relevant disciplines and the school’s Komsomol committee.

How is this society organized?

The work of the VIITORUL society continues under the general leadership of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Moldova and the Ministry of Public Education.

A large role is assigned to the Student Council, which includes representatives of all sections. Members of this council take an active part in the organization of all events, in the development and implementation of scientific plans. They make presentations to school and vocational school students, participate in visiting sections, publish their own newspaper, maintain contact with other scientific societies and, first of all, with the Student Scientific Society of Moldova.

Students in grades 8-10 are accepted into the VIITORUL society. But since 1975, seven classics can also be candidates for membership in the society. They attend section meetings, participate in all public events of the society and scientific excursions, prepare speeches about the life and work of outstanding scientists, and review popular science magazines.
