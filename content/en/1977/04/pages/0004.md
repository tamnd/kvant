ACCELERATORS

L. Goldin

The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» has long asked me to write an article about accelerators. I delayed this for several years. Does the reader of the magazine need to know about the almost military tricks that physicists and engineers are constantly using in order to make these colossal, incomparable structures work: isn’t it all too special? Then I finally made up my mind, realizing that the material could be presented in such a way that the reader would not only understand the structure of the zoological tree of the accelerator family, but would also gain information and ideas that would always be useful to him. To what extent I succeeded, my readers will say.

For several decades now, nuclear and particle physics has been determined by what accelerators scientists have at their disposal. And today, each new major accelerator will be preceded by new discoveries and changes in basic theoretical principles. To study the depths of the Universe, hypothetical instruments are built. Multimeter mirrors of optical telecopes and antennas of radio telecopes stretching for kilometers did not surprise anyone. However, even larger installations - huge multi-climeter accelerators - are being created for study
