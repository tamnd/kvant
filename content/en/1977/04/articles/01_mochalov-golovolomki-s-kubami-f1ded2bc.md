CUBE CHAMELEON

The faces of the 27 cubes are painted in certain colors so that by adding more cubes (size 3x3x3) from these cubes, you can get a red cube, you can get a blue cube, or you can get a green cube. How are the edges of these 27 cubes colored?

COLLAPSIBLE CUBE

The bosses gave the kindergarten eight identical wooden figures (see figure), from which a cube measuring 4x4x4 was folded. The children took the cube apart into parts, but cannot put the cube back together. Help them.

L. Mochalov
