⟦folio 57⟧

Machine graphics

1. At figure 1, the machine drew a family of ellipses. There seems to be some pattern to their arrangement. However, these ellipses have neither a common center nor a common focus, and they are not similar to each other.
Can you think of where that figure came from? Is it possible to “see” him somewhere, say, “in nature”?

2. The family of lines at figure 2 is more complex. These are not even ellipses, but the outer line (with corners), apparently, is not from this family at all, although it is related to it.
Where could this figure come from?
Yu. Kotov
