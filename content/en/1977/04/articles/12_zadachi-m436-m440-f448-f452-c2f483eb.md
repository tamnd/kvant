⟦folio 30⟧

Tasks

M436 — M440; F448 — F452

M436. Given 20 numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{10}, b_1, b_2, \dots, b_{10}$. Prove that a set of 100 numbers (not necessarily different) $a_1 + b_1, a_1 + b_2, \dots, a_{10} + b_{10}$ can be divided into 10 subsets, each containing 10 numbers, so that the sum of the numbers in each subset is the same.

S. Berkolaiko

M437. Prove that an odd number that is the product $n$ of different prime factors can be represented as the difference of the squares of two natural numbers equal to $2^{n-1}$ in various ways.

O. Goncharik,
S. Sergey

M438. All possible pairs of tangent circles (Fig. 1) fit into this segment. For each pair of circles, a straight line tangent to them is drawn through the point of contact. Prove that all these lines pass through one point.

Z. Skopets

M439*. a) Prove that the equation
$$ax^k + bx^l + cx^m = 1$$
(where $a, b, c$ are real numbers, $k, l$ and $m$ are natural numbers) has at most three positive roots.

b) Prove that the equation
$$a_1x^{k_1} + a_2x^{k_2} + \dots + a_nx^{k_n} = 1$$
(where $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ are real numbers, $k_1, k_2, \dots, k_n$ are natural numbers) has at most $n$ positive roots.

b) Prove that the equation
$$ax^k(x+1)^p + bx^l(x+1)^q + cx^m(x+1)^r = 1$$
(where $a, b, c$ are real numbers, $k, l, m, p, q, r$ are natural numbers) has no more than 14 positive roots.

A. Kushirenko

M440. Cube $100 \times 100 \times 100$ is made up of a million unit cups. Using a skewer, draw a straight line passing through the centers of the cups and parallel to the edge of the cube.
