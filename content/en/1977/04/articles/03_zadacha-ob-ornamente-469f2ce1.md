The principle of constructing this ornament is quite simple: each side of the square is divided into two equal parts and all division points are connected in pairs by segments to each other.
Try to answer a series of questions related to this ornament. At how many points do the segments inside the square intersect? How many intersection points are there at which three segments, four segments, heels, etc. intersect? How many intersection points can lie on the sides of squares with vertices at the intersection points (find the most and most numbers)? Are there other regular polygons present on figure? We are waiting for your letters with answers.
