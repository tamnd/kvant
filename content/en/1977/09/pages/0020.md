In the new issue «Kvant» for 1976, the possibility of discovering superheavy elements, which should appear in the periodic table under numbers 116, 124, 126, was discussed.

Let us recall that the discovery, if confirmed, would pose a very difficult question for physicists: how superheavy atoms got into the mica samples where they were discovered.

So, the most and most powerful Reflector phone was built and located in the Soviet Union. The question will naturally arise: can we expect an even bigger phone to be made in the not too distant future? This has to be answered in the negative, since modern technology opens up completely different, new ways for space exploration. This is, first of all, the path that radio astronomers have already taken - synthesizing images produced by several types of telescopes of more modest sizes, installed close to each other or even at some distance from one another. In this case, the addition of images can not be done continuously, but selectively - only in relatively rare moments of excellent visibility and the most clear images, which, of course, can only be done with the help of modern electronic technology.

Another promising path is to move observational tools beyond the atmosphere: to artificial Earth satellites and even to the Moon, where a solid basis for installing even larger instruments can be obtained. Steps in this direction have already been taken and are beginning to yield interesting results.
