⟦folio 44⟧

Repetition tasks

At the beginning of the school year, we offer students in grades IX-X a somewhat general task. For each of the questions listed below, five possible answers are given (a, 6, c, d, e), of which exactly one is correct. Find and hold it. Record the time it took you; Compare your answers with those given at the end of the issue.

These tasks were offered at the republican mathematics olympiads in 1977, and they were given from 30 to 45 minutes to complete. On average, out of 10 answers, only six were correct. How will it be for you?

IX class

1. How many arrows can be left on figure 1 so that they define a function with domain $X \subset \{x_1, x_2, x_3\}$? (Please list all possibilities.)

Fig. 1.

Answer. a) Only 3; 6) all 6; b) from 0 to 3; d) from 0 to 6; d) from 3 to 6.

2. At what values ​​of $m$ is the fraction $\frac{m^3 - m}{2\sqrt{m}}$ equal to 0?

Answer. a) $m \in \{0; -1; 1\}$; b) $m = 0$; d) $m \in \{-1; 1\}$; e) $m = 1$.

3. What graph does the equation $x^2 - y^2 = 0$ have?

Answer. a) Let there be a set; b) point; b) straight; d) combining two lines; d) circle.

4. For what values ​​of $a$ and $b$ is the relation $\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{-a} \cdot \sqrt{-b}$ valid?

Answer. a) $a = b = 0$; b) $a \in [-\infty, 0]$; b) $a \in [-\infty, 0]$; b) $a$ and $b$ of the same sign; d) for any $a$ and $b$; d) under no $a$ and $b$.

5. The number $A$ is specified with an accuracy of 0.01, the number $B$ with an accuracy of 0.02. With what accuracy can you indicate the number $2A - B$?

Answer. a) 0.01; b) 0.02; b) 0.04; d) 0.00; d) the answer depends on the values ​​of $A$ and $B$.

6. In a triangle, the lengths of the sides are 3.5, $n$, where $n$ is a natural number. Specify the possibility of the value $n$.

Answer. a) $n \geq 1$; b) $n \geq 3$; b) $n \leq 8$; d) $3 \leq n \leq 7$; d) $3 \leq n \leq 5$.

7. Specify the value $\cos(90^\circ + \alpha)$.

Answer. a) $\sin \alpha$; b) $-\sin \alpha$; d) $-\cos \alpha$; d) this value is undefined.
