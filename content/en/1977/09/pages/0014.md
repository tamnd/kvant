Both Galileo's telephone and Kepler's telephone were subsequently called refractors (from the Latin word frango - I break). The desire to obtain the highest possible magnification led to the construction of very long telescopes, which affected the quality of the image: its brightness decreased. This could be avoided by increasing the diameter of the object, that is, increasing the amount of light passing through the phone *). However, an increase in the size of the object leads to a sharp deterioration in the clarity of the images - they become blurry and with colored stones. This is due to the disadvantages of lenses - primarily spherical and chromatic aberration.
