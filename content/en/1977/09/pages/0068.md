To our readers

A subscription for 1978 is announced for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant»

«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 5-10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to study the exact sciences on a server in the future.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren whose interest in the exact sciences is still dormant.

Review articles are published on the pages of our journal, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still awaiting solution; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are distributed.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems from various Olympiads and simply interesting problems.

The “Laboratory «Kvant»” section talks about educational experiments that can be done at home.

What questions and tasks can an applicant expect during the entrance exams? The reader will find answers to these and many other questions that arise when applying to a university in the section “Practicum for applicants.”

Our magazine is also useful for the reader. Fundamental changes have now been made in school courses in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» always tries to highlight these changes on its pages; an article on the new program is published.

In 1977, «Kvant» opened a new column, “Through the Pages of School Textbooks.” The articles in this section break down the most subtle and important issues of mathematics studied in school. In 1978 we will significantly expand this section.

We also anticipate a significant expansion of section «Kvant» for younger students in 1978.

The magazine constantly publishes solutions to books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
