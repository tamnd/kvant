Scientific creativity of students

In the spring of 1977, the Secretariat of the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Board of the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the Chairman of the All-Union Council of Scientific and Technical Societies and the Chairman of the Board of the All-Union Society “Knowledge” adopted a joint resolution “On the Scientific Society of Students.” This resolution approved the approximate Regulations on the Scientific Society of Students (SSC) and set the task of “taking specific measures to create scientific societies of students everywhere.”

The goal of the NOU is to attract schoolchildren to active creative activities. In our country, scientific associations of students have long been successfully operating: the Small Academy of Sciences (MAN) “Iskatel” (Crimean region), the Chelyabinsk Scientific Society of Students, the scientific society of students “Vintorul” (Chisinau), the mathematical society “Ponsk” (Malaya Vishera), the scientific and technical society of students of Norilsk. The Palaces and Houses of Pioneers and Schoolchildren will make a significant contribution to the development of students’ cognitive activity and creative abilities. Numerous scientific associations, clubs and sections for students have been created at research and design organizations, at computational centers and secondary specialized educational institutions. The results of the first All-Axis meeting of active student societies eloquently testify to the great and fruitful work of the members of the NOU (this should be discussed in «Kvant», 1975, No. 9).

Student scientific societies face important challenges. NOUs are recognized to actively support schools in the communist education of students, in their all-round development, in developing a creative attitude towards work, to instill in schoolchildren an interest in studying the fundamentals of social, political, humanities, natural and mathematical sciences, to introduce schoolchildren to the methods and techniques of scientific research and reading scientific literature available to them.

The editorial board and editorial staff of the journal «Kvant», warmly supporting the decision to create a network of scientific societies for students, intend to systematically help these societies in their activities, and tell readers about the experience of the best societies.

The material for the work of NOU members could be cycles of thematic related articles published in the journal on physics or mathematics, the preparation of reports and messages on these articles, collective discussion of individual problems, clarification of what is not clear with the help of or scientific supervisor, solving problems and generalization of these solutions, etc. Rich opportunities for creative work of NOU members are opened up by descriptions of experiments and various tasks published in the journal. It is supported that many of these problems are, in essence, topics for meaningful independent research.
