⟦folio 48⟧

Cut out the square

The editors received over 1000 compositions according to the announced competition (see «Kvant» No. 2, 1977). 24 compositions were displayed for publication: 1 - A. Ryabchkov (Gomel region); 2, 7, 9 - A. Mozharova (Tambov); 3 - A. Tsarev (Chernivtsi); 4, 17, 18 - A. Potapov (Moscow region); 5 - N. Trifonov (Krasnoyrsky region); 6 - I. Oleynikova (Stavropol Territory); 8 - S. Petrash (Leningrad); 10 - A. Soifer (Perm); 11, 12 - S. Gubenina (Ust-Kamenogorsk); 13 - I. Tsygankov (Sevastopol); 14, 15 - A. Parfirov (Ulyanovsk); 16 - O. Shats (Moscow region); 19 - N. Tarasov (Novgorod region); 20, 21 - S. Koryak (Dnepropetrovsk); 22 - O. Dymkova (Penza); 23 - P. Kapalov (Penza); 24 - L. Tanacheva (Udmurt Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic).

According to the results of the competition, the compositions of sixth-graders Sveta GUBENINA and ninth-graders Ira OLEINIKOVA and Tony MOZHAROVA, which are loaded with a subscription to «Kvant» for 1978, were recognized as the best.
