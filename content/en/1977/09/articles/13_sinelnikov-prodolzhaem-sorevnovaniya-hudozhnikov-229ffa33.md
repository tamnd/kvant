8. ABCD is a square. Find the image of the vertex $A$ when the composition $S_b \circ S_a$ is symmetrical relative to figure 2 axes $a$ and $b$.

Answer. a) $A$; 6) $B$; b) $C$; d) $D$; e) a point that is not the vertex of a square.

9. In triangle ABC, the segment $AM$ is the median. Specify the vector $\vec{x} = \overrightarrow{AM} + \overrightarrow{BM}$.

Answer. a) $\overrightarrow{AB}$; 6) $\overrightarrow{AC}$; b) $\overrightarrow{BC}$; d) $\overrightarrow{BA}$; e) $\overrightarrow{CA}$.

10. The height of a regular triangle ABC is equal to 1. At what distance from the vertex $A$ should a line parallel to the line $BC$ be drawn so that it divides triangle ABC into two parts of equal area?

Answer. a) $1/2$; 6) $2/3$; b) $3/4$; d) $\sqrt{2}/2$; e) $\sqrt{3}/2$.

1. In how many different ways can 12 people be assigned to work in two shifts of 6 people?

Answer. a) $C_{12}^6$; 6) $A_{12}^6$; b) $\frac{1}{2} C_{12}^6$; d) $C_{12}^6 \cdot C_{12}^6$; e) $P_6$.

2. Given two real numbers $x = 0,1093$ ... and $y = 0,3006$ ... How many digits after the decimal point can be given for their sum $x + y$?

Answer. a) four; 6) three; b) two; d) one; d) none.

3. Which of the properties of monotonicity and boundedness does the sequence $a_n = -\frac{1}{n^2}$ have?

Answer. a) increases and is not limited; 6) decreases and is not limited; b) increases and is limited; d) decreasing and limited; d) not limited and not monotonous.

4. The function $f$ is differentiable at the point $x_0$. What is the limit?

$$\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} (f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)).$$

Answer. a) 0; 6) $f(x_0)$; b) $-f(x_0)$; d) $f'(x_0)$; d) this limit does not exist.

5. Find an arbitrary function $(x^{10} + 1)^{10}$.

Answer. a) $10(x^{10} + 1)^9$; 6) $100(x^{10} + 1)^9$; b) $10x^9(x^{10} + 1)^9$; d) $100x^9(x^{10} + 1)^9$; e) $x^9(x^{10} + 1)^9$.

6. The plane intersects the edges $AB$, $BC$ and $CD$ of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ (not at the vertices). What other edge will this plane intersect?

Answer. a) [AC]; 6) [AD]; b) [BD]; d) none; d) two more ribs.

7. $\vec{a}$, $\vec{b}$, $\vec{c}$ are vectors in space, $(\vec{a}, \vec{b}) = 90^\circ$, $(\vec{b}, \vec{c}) = 90^\circ$. What is the possibility of meaning $\varphi = a, c$?

Answer. a) $\varphi = 0^\circ$; 6) $\varphi = 90^\circ$; b) $\varphi = 180^\circ$; d) $0^\circ \leq \varphi \leq 90^\circ$; e) $0^\circ \leq \varphi \leq 180^\circ$.
