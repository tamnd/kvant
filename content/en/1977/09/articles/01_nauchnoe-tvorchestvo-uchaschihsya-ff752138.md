Scientific creativity of students

In the spring of 1977, the Secretariat of the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Board of the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the Chairman of the All-Union Council of Scientific and Technical Societies and the Chairman of the Board of the All-Union Society “Knowledge” adopted a joint resolution “On the Scientific Society of Students.” This resolution approved the approximate Regulations on the Scientific Society of Students (SSC) and set the task of “taking specific measures to create scientific societies of students everywhere.”

The goal of the NOU is to attract schoolchildren to active creative activities. In our country, scientific associations of students have long been successfully operating: the Small Academy of Sciences (MAN) “Iskatel” (Crimean region), the Chelyabinsk Scientific Society of Students, the scientific society of students “Vintorul” (Chisinau), the mathematical society “Ponsk” (Malaya Vishera), the scientific and technical society of students of Norilsk. The Palaces and Houses of Pioneers and Schoolchildren will make a significant contribution to the development of students’ cognitive activity and creative abilities. Numerous scientific associations, clubs and sections for students have been created at research and design organizations, at computational centers and secondary specialized educational institutions. The results of the first All-Axis meeting of active student societies eloquently testify to the great and fruitful work of the members of the NOU (this should be discussed in «Kvant», 1975, No. 9).

Student scientific societies face important challenges. NOUs are recognized to actively support schools in the communist education of students, in their all-round development, in developing a creative attitude towards work, to instill in schoolchildren an interest in studying the fundamentals of social, political, humanities, natural and mathematical sciences, to introduce schoolchildren to the methods and techniques of scientific research and reading scientific literature available to them.

The editorial board and editorial staff of the journal «Kvant», warmly supporting the decision to create a network of scientific societies for students, intend to systematically help these societies in their activities, and tell readers about the experience of the best societies.

The material for the work of NOU members could be cycles of thematic related articles published in the journal on physics or mathematics, the preparation of reports and messages on these articles, collective discussion of individual problems, clarification of what is not clear with the help of or scientific supervisor, solving problems and generalization of these solutions, etc. Rich opportunities for creative work of NOU members are opened up by descriptions of experiments and various tasks published in the journal. It is supported that many of these problems are, in essence, topics for meaningful independent research.

There is no need to think that schoolchildren’s research work can only be of an educational and cognitive nature, that they are only able to present abstracts of articles or solve “known” problems. The experience of many educational institutions shows that schoolchildren also have access to server-based content problems, the solutions of which are interesting for science and technology. As an example, let us give a message recently published by the Izvestia newspaper: “The Antison device, created by Gorky radio amateurs - designer A. S. Sherbakov and schoolchildren I. Burov and E. Seednev, occupied specialists. This device is sensitive to the weakening of driver’s attention while driving.” Let us also recall the results of the first All-Russian meeting of active student societies: of the works presented in the villages, 79 found various applications in the national economy, and 37 were published in magazines and collections.

The editors of «Kvant» always pay special attention to the materials sent by our schoolchildren readers. The magazine's consultants try to evaluate each work in detail and comprehensively, give useful advice on its continuation and completion, and answer questions. To answer questions of general interest, the magazine has created a special section “Ask and we will answer.” The most advanced and meaningful materials from schoolchildren appeared in the form of articles on the pages of the magazine; Over the past years, about twenty such articles have been published in «Kvant». And now the magazine's readers will find the work of schoolboy Vadim Knizhnik from Moscow. The magazine intends to continue this practice in the future.

The magazine's competition for solving problems included in the "Problem Book" «Kvant» is very popular. The editors of the journal invite all members of NOUs interested in physics and mathematics to participate in this competition.

Members and leaders of student scientific societies, especially newly created ones, will undoubtedly find it interesting and useful to get acquainted with the experience of other non-governmental educational institutions. Here, for example, is a letter sent to «Kvant» on behalf of members of the mathematical society of the school branch of the MAN “Iskatel” Pavel Kornyakov, Oleg Konnik and Alexa ンド Furniture.

“We inform you that we still have a school department of MAN “Iskatel” of Crimea. The department continues to search for new, more interesting, useful forms of work. Now the department includes four societies: mathematical, physical, chemical and biological. The Mathematical Society holds competitions, competitions, evenings, public reviews of knowledge, mathematical “fights”, etc. All this gives positive results. Members and candidates for membership of the MAN of our school are winners of district, city and regional olympids in mathematics.

The Crimean team at the Republican Olympiad, as a rule, includes students from our school No. 40 in Simferopol. And our young mathematicians perform quite well. At the last Olympics in Ukraine, our students took two first and two third places, and our ninth-grader Alexa Bespalov was included in the Ukrainian team.

All teams at our school subscribe to the magazine «Kvant» and solve problems published in this magazine. A year-old graduate of our school, Oleg Godin, was awarded a one-year subscription to the magazine «Kvant» for the successful solution of problems...”

The editors of «Kvant» intend to inform their readers about the most interesting scientific societies of students and about the works of members of NOUs. We invite the heads of the physics and mathematics sections of NOU to write in the journal about their plans, topics and forms of classes, and about the scientific research of their students.
