⟦folio 46⟧

Quests

1. One citizen wanted to drink a glass of juice. He walked up to the kinok and laid out in front of the seller all the money he had - paper bills and change. The saleswoman counted the money, put it in the general receipts, then poured a glass of juice worth 9 kopecks and gave out the change. But she was wrong: she gave as many kopecks as she was supposed to give in rubles, and she gave as many rubles as she was supposed to give in kopecks.

The citizen, without counting, put the money in his pocket and left, and when he came home, he discovered that he had four times more money than he had. How much money did the citizen have?

2. A kilogram of ice cream costs 40 kopecks compared to a kilogram of chocolate ice cream. Andrey and Victor each ordered 150 g of ice cream, and Andrey had twice as much ice cream as chocolate, and Victor had equal amounts of both. Whose portion is expensive and by how much?

3. The rhombus $ABCD$ was “inflated” into the quadrilateral $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ (see figure: $A_1 \in |DA|$, $|A_1A| = |AD|$, etc.). The area of ​​a rhombus $ABCD$ is equal to $S$. Prove that $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ is a parallelogram and find its area.

4. Why do the scissors that tailors use have short handles and long blades, and the scissors that cut metal leaves, such as tin, have long handles and short blades?
