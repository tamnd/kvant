⟦folio 41⟧

We continue the publication of stereometric problems with drawings, which began in the 2nd issue «Kvant» for this year (see also covers of numbers 3, 5 and 6). Below are the conditions of the two problems; The first of them was presented at the application exams at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology in 1984. The second problem is interesting because it considers a configuration that is not very easy to imagine. To give the reader the opportunity to think about it for themselves, we will place a drawing for it on the cover of one of the following issues.

Problem 1. At the base of the primid SABCD lies the rectangle ABCD, in which $|AC|=2|AB$. The height of the prim passes through the intersection point of the diagonal rectangle. The sphere touches plane ABCD and all lateral edges of the primid. The volume of the primid begins if the radius of the sphere is $R$ and the angle SDC is $\alpha$.

The plane passes through the point $S$, touches the indicated sphere and intersects the lines

BD and AC respectively at points $P$ and $Q$ ($|DP| > |BP|$, $|AQ| > |CQ|$). Find the ratio $|BP|:|BD|$ if:
a) $|BP| = |CQ|$,
b) $|CQ| = 3|AC|$.

Problem 2. There are six objects, two identical balls of radius $R_1$, two more identical balls of radius $R_2$, a very long cylinder with a base radius $r$ and a plane. They are located in space so that each of them touches the other five (the cylinder touches its side surface). Express $R_1$ and $R_2$ using $r$.
