Collector's Corner

ATOM IN PHILATELIERS

At first glance, the stamps reproduced here are chosen completely randomly; they have no thematic unity. There are portraits of famous physicists, Nobel Prize winners Frederic Jollus-Curie and Shibitiro Tomonaga, and Soviet stamps dedicated to the struggle for peace, Lenin’s GOELRO plan and the 26th Congress of the CPSU, the 1967 World Exhibition in Montreal and the Exhibition of National Economic Achievements. There are also stamps associated with scientific conferences and the International Atomic Energy Agency. But look closely and you will see that each stamp depicts the planetary atoma model, the theory of which was created by the great Danish physicist Niels Bohr. In this place he would have used this years. Bohr's atoma model has become a kind of symbol of our atomic age.

V. Rudov
