XI All-Russian Schoolchildren Olympiad

According to established tradition, the final, zonal stage of the All-Russian Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry Olympiad for schoolchildren in grades 8-10 takes place during the spring school holidays. The zonal stage is preceded by school, district, city and regional (territorial) Olympiads, based on the results of which each region (region) is presented with one winner from each class in each subject.

This year, the final stage of the Olympiad was held in four cities - Smolensk (for the Northwestern zone), Kazan (for the Central zone), Grozny (for the Southwestern zone) and Omsk (for Siberia and the Far East). In addition, at the same time, the final stage of the Olympiad took place in physics and mathematics boarding schools at Leningrad, Moscow and Novosibirsk universities.

The mathematics task for the final stage of the Olympiad consisted of five problems, the solution of which took four hours. As the test showed, students had the most difficulty in problems in geometry, combinatorics and analysis. The problem of factoring a polynomial caused significant difficulties for eighth-graders. The jury members discussed the competition tasks in detail with the students and analyzed typical mistakes.

Especially the final stage of this year’s Mathematics Olympiad was a practical round, in which schoolchildren from grades 9 and 10 participated. The tasks proposed in the practical type aroused great interest among both the Olympiad students and the team leaders: for the first time, microcalculators were used at our Olympiads. Although the results of solving these problems were not taken into account when determining the winners of the Olympiad, schoolchildren who performed well in the practical type were awarded certificates and special prizes. Summing up, the Central Organizing Committee decided to consider the possibility of including the practical round task among the competitive tasks of the Olympiad.

The final stage of the Physics Olympiad was held in two types - theoretical and experimental. The theoretical type task included five tasks, the experimental type - two tasks. Four hours were allotted to complete each task. According to the results of the test, it turned out that the transmission caused the most difficulties for eighth-graders, and the second transmission of the theoretical type for ninth-graders.

Schoolchildren who took I-IV places at the zonal stage of the All-Russian Olympiad were loaded with diplomas, certificates and memorable gifts. From among the prizes, the RSFSR team was formed to participate in the All-Union Olympiad.

The teachers who prepared the prizes for the Olympiad were also awarded special certificates.

Below are the problems in mathematics and physics of the final stage and the names of the prizes of the XI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren.
