When a body is on a horizontal surface, the force pressing it to this surface is equal to gravity. If the surface is not horizontal, but makes some angle with the gravity, then the larger this angle ($a_2 > a_1$, and $N_2 < N_1$), the smaller the pressing force. And accordingly, the larger the inclined plane, the less the sliding force ($F_{τp_2} < F_{τp_1}$).

The cabinet does not move until the “driving” force applied to it becomes equal to μN. Until this moment, the force of rest always balances the “moving” scene, and the cabinet remains motionless.
