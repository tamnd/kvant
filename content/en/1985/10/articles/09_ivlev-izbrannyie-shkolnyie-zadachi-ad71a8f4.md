Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. For what natural n is the fraction $\frac{8n+71}{5n+46}$ reducible?

2. Solve the equation $x^2 + \frac{9x^2}{(x+3)^2} = 16$.

3. A circle of radius R contains two diameters, the angle between which is equal to a. Prove that the distance between the bases of perpendiculars dropped from an arbitrary point on the circle to these diameters does not depend on the position of the point on the circle, and find it.

4. Prove that the number $n^2+5n+16$ is not divisible by 169 for any natural number n.

5. Construct a square if given 4 points: one point on each of the lines containing the sides of the square.

Ninth grade
6. Factor the expression $(1+x+x^2+x^3+\dots+x^n)^2-x^n$, where n is a natural number, n ≥ 3.

7. The altitudes of the triangle intersect at point H. It is known that $|HC|=|AB|$. Find the vertex angle C.

8. In the data space there are two crossing lines a and b. Draw a line intersecting both given lines and: a) passing through a given point M not lying on a and b; b) parallel to the given line l. In what case is a solution possible?

9. a) Under what condition are the straight lines $y=k_1x+b_1$ and $y=k_2x+b_2$ perpendicular?

6) Find the shortest distance from the point paraboles $y=x^2-8x+16$ to the straight line $y=-2x+1$.

10. In the data space there are two mutually intersecting perpendicular lines a and b. Find the set of midpoints of segments AB of given length d such that A ∈ a, B ∈ b.

Tenth grade
11. How should a regular tetraedр be placed in space so that its orthogonal projection onto a given plane has the largest area? Find the value of this plane if the edge of the tetraedra has length a.

12. Solve the equation $\log_2^2(x+y)+\log_2^2(xy)+1=2\log_2(x+y)$.

13. Prove Simpson's formula: the empty area of ​​the section of a body by the plane $P_x$, drawn perpendicular to the coordinate axis Ox through this point with coordinate x, is expressed at $x \in [a; b]$ by a polynomial in x not higher than the third degree (the body is enclosed between the planes $P_a$ and $P_b$); then the volume of the body V can be calculated using the formula

$$V = \frac{h}{6} (S_a + 4S_{cp} + S_b),$$

where h = b - a, $S_a = S(a)$, $S_b = S(b)$, $S_{cp}$ is the area of ​​the “middle” section - the section of the plane $P_{(a+b)/2}$, corresponding to the coordinate $x_0 = (a+b)/2$.

Find the length of the shortest path passing along the surface of this paralleliped and connecting point E with point F, symmetrical to point E relative to the center paralleliped
