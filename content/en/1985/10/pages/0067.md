HEXAGONAL CHESS

There are ten known varieties of chess, which use non-teaching boards, pieces or rules of the game. We have already talked about cylindrical chess and chess on parallel boards. Non-standard boards are rarely used for server games.

But there is one exception - hexagonal, or, otherwise, hex chess. The hexagonal chess board has the shape of a hexagon. Two versions of the game were chosen, one by the Soviet geologist I. Shafran, the other by the engineer V. Glinsky. The use of chess has become widespread in the world, let's first talk about them.

The hexagonal board consists of 91 fields of three colors (see page 1 of the cover); it has 11 verticals from “a” to “l” (except “j”), the fields of each are numbered from bottom to top. The role of the horizontal is played by the diagonals, to the left of the line “f” parallel a1 - f1, and to the right parallel f1 - l1. In addition to the total set of pieces, each side receives one bishop and one pawn. The initial arrangement is shown on the cover, the moves of the pieces are shown on the next figure.

The pawns of the three central files can make the first move immediately and three squares forward, the rest only two. Castling is possible - short, if the rook approaches the king, and he steps over it, and long, if the king approaches the rook, and it breaks over him.

In Polish chess, the board is a regular hexagon, and perhaps such a geometric four-sequential board will “beat” Saffron in popularity to Glinsky.

The theory of hex chess has not been developed; moreover, the variety of moves and options is significantly greater than in ordinary chess, and it is more difficult to predict the opponent’s response.

The geometry of the hexagonal weight board is unique. Thus, the vertical “f” is the axis of symmetry, and the field f6 is the central field (on a common board, the center consists of four fields - d4, d5, e4, e5). Although the board is larger, the path of a knight between any squares takes no more than four moves (on the common board a1 to h8 a knight is added in 6 moves). It is curious that any piece can make a move while maintaining controlROL over the previous squares. This property is especially interesting in the horse. For example, with g9 he attacks e7, and continues to control it, moving to d9 or h6. The knight can also move along the triangle, gaining tempo from his partner.
