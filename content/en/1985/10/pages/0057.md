Experimental tour

8th grade

1. When two combs with different pitches of teeth are applied, an alternation of translucent and dark areas occurs, called moiré. Explore how the speed of movement of the molus depends on the speed of movement of the combs.

Equipment: two combs, graph paper.

2. Determine the mass of the block in several ways.

Equipment: school diameter gauge, block, tripod with coupling and bar, ruler, thread.

9th grade

1. Measure the elementary electric charge.

Equipment: direct current source with a regular voltage, a vessel with a solution of table salt (sodium chloride), two metal rods (electrodes), a glass tube with a stopper, a milliammeter, a key, a clock with a second hand, a plate, a ruler.

2. Determine the ratio of the resistance of the two resistors by using a voltmeter with an unknown internal resistance.

Equipment: 3336 coin cell battery, school voltmeter, two resistors, connected wires.

10th grade

1. Estimate the sizes of olinic acid molecules, assuming that it spreads on the surface of water in a monomolecular layer.

Equipment: 0.15% solution of olinic acid in alcohol, pipette with water, ruler, scales and grip, lipopodium or talc, glass.

It is known that the density of olinic acid is ρ=895 kg/m³, and the chemical formula is C₁₈H₃₄O₂.

2. Propose a measurement method that allows you to measure the unknown EMF of a current source using no more than two assembled electrical circuits.

Equipment: current source, two voltmeters.
