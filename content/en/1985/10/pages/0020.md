For what follows, we will need some auxiliary concepts that are interesting in themselves. The following paragraph is devoted to their presentation.

Blending and symmetrizing polygons

The concepts presented below were introduced into geometry by G. Minkowski, who used them in his famous studies on convex sets.

Definition 6. A mixture (half-sum) of two exact sets is the set of midpoints of all segments whose ends belong to this set (one end to the first set, and the other to the second). (At figure 8 the mixture of segments AB and CD is shown.)

Exercise 19. a) A mixture of parallel lines is a straight line that bisects the stripes between them.

6) The mixture of segments lying on parallel lines is the middle line of the trapezoid (parallelograph) formed by these segments.

b) The mixture of parallel strips of width $H_1$ and $H_2$ is a strip of width $\frac{H_1+H_2}{2}$ parallel to them.

d) The mixture of circles of radius $R_1$ and $R_2$ is a circle of radius $\frac{R_1+R_2}{2}$.

20. The reference strip of mixing two polygons is the mixture of the reference strips of these polygons parallel to it.
