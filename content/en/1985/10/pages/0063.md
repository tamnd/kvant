8th grade

1. From the problem statement it is clear that the direction of the battery is $U_6=U_1=3$ V. In order for the battery not to discharge, the current in the circuit must be provided only by the source. That's why
$$U_2=U_1+I_1R \approx 6,3 \text{ В}$$
(here $I_1 \approx 0,55$ A is the current in the light bulb in the direction $U_1$ on it).

2. The beam must be positioned so that it is in equilibrium when the reaction force from the stop $A$ is equal to zero. then the maximum mass of the load will be equal to
$$M=\frac{F_0}{g}-m,$$
and the distance from the stop $B$ to the right end of the battery will be equal to
$$x=\frac{L}{2}\frac{m}{M+m}=\frac{L}{2}\frac{mg}{F_0}.$$
There is no limit on $l$.

3. The stone moves in a circle (Fig. 11) with radius
$$R=2l\cos \alpha=l\sqrt{\frac{m^2}{2\gamma^2l^2}}\left(\sqrt{1+\frac{16\gamma^2l^2}{m^2}}-1\right).$$
Note. The angle $\alpha$ is determined from the equation of motion of the stone, written for projections onto the directions of the radius and tangent to the circle:
$$F\cos \alpha=m\omega^2R, F\sin \alpha-\gamma^2\omega^2R^2=0,$$
where $F$ is the tension force of the rope.
4. The cube will come off the suction cup at a depth (not from the top edge of the cube)
$$h=\left(\frac{Q_T}{Q_B}-1\right)a-\frac{p_{aTM}}{Q_B}g=2 \text{ м.}$$

9th grade

1. Initial air humidity $\varphi=50\%$. Note. From the conditions of the problem it is clear that after the first drop, the water vapor contained in the air became saturated.

2. The amount of pumped oil will increase by $(d_2/d_1)^4=16$ times.

3. The cylinder will do
$$N=\frac{(1+\mu^2)\omega_0^2R}{4\pi\sqrt{2}\mu g}$$

rpm Note. The initial kinetic energy of the cylinder is equal to the work against the friction force $F_1$ and $F_2$ (Fig. 12). The values ​​of $F_1$ and $F_2$ can be found from the condition of immobility of the cylinder axis:
$$F_1+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}mg-N_2=0, F_2+N_1-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}mg=0,$$
where the support reaction forces $N_1$ and $N_2$ are related to the friction forces by correspondences $F_1=\mu N_1$ and $F_2=\mu N_2$.

4. The fuse will blow at load power
$$P_2=P_1\sqrt{\frac{t_{\pi\pi}-t}{t_1-t}}\frac{1+at_1}{1+at_{\pi\pi}} \approx 1,4 \text{ кВт.}$$

Note. For evaluation, we can assume that the load power is equal to the power consumed from the mains.
