Quests

1. Rearrange the two matches so that the equation shown at figure becomes true.

2. What is the expression G·R·U·Z·I·YA / T·B·I·L·I·S·I’, if the letter corresponds to numbers, and identical letters have the same numbers, and different ones have different ones?

3. One fine autumn evening I was walking to the station along a country road. The mud that flew out from under my boots settled on the cuffs of my trousers. Arriving at the station, I discovered that the inside of my trousers was dirty up to the knees. But the splashes of mud did not rise that high. Why did I get so dirty?

4. The number 6116 has the following property: no matter which pair of its digits you take, the last digit of their sum is the same as the last digit of the sum of the other two digits. How many four-digit numbers have this property?

5. I covered the children's cube with clean paper, divided each side into four squares, and tried to write an integer into each square so that the sum of this number and the four numbers in the adjacent squares would equal 13 (see figure; adjacent squares are those that have a common side). However, nothing worked for me. Maybe you will succeed? Or is this impossible to do?
