⟦folio 30⟧

The Earth is in a circular orbit, the radius of which is 1.25R, where R is the radius of the Earth. At some moments, the satellite is ejected from the station in the direction of its movement and goes into an elliptical orbit with an apogee at a distance of 10R from the center of the Earth. At what m/M ratio will the satellite meet the station after completing one revolution around the Earth?

A. A. Biryukov, V. V. Danilyuk

F959. At relative air humidity r₁=50%, the water drunk in the saucer evaporated in the open air in a time t₁=40 minutes. How long would it take for water to evaporate if r₂=80%?

F960. In order to get two perfectly identical coils, they are wound on a non-magnetic core simultaneously using wires folded together (Fig. 1). One of the coils is connected via key K to battery B with voltage U₀, the second to resistor R. The key is closed. Calculate the current through the resistor. Draw a graph of the current through the battery versus time.

After time t the key is opened. How much heat will be released in the resistor starting from this moment?

The inductance of each coil is L. The battery is considered ideal. Neglect the resistance of the wires.

F961. To maintain a constant water temperature in a flow-through aquarium, use two identical heaters (Fig. 2). In normal mode, one of them is used, and if a second heater is connected in parallel, then the cold water consumption has to be increased 3 times. How should the cold water flow be changed if the heaters are connected in series? What should the cold water flow rate be if one spiral with a power of P=100 W is turned on? Cold water temperature t₁=10 °C, water temperature in the aquarium t₂=27 °C. The water is quickly mixed.

A. R. Zilberman

F962. Point sources located on the main optical axis of the lens form a uniformly luminous filament (passing through the focus of the lens). Find the law of change E(x) of illumination at points lying on the same axis on the other side of the lens at large distances x from it (x≫F, where F is the focal length of the lens).

M. A. Vorontsov, V. E. Kuntstsyn
