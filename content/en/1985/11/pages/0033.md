Tasks

M951—M955; F963—F967

M951. All sides of the convex hexagon ABCDEF are equal to 1. Prove that the circumradius of one of the triangles ACE and BDF is at least 1.

E. Horvath (Hungary)

M952. Let us denote by {x} the fractional part of the number x; {x} = x - [x], where [x] is the largest integer without exceeding it.

a) Give an example of a number a such that
{a} + {1/a} = 1.

6) Prove that such a number a cannot be rational.

I. Varga (Romania)

M953. There are 6 points on a plane, no three of which lie on the same line. All 15 straight lines connecting these points in pairs are drawn. What is the largest number of points (other than data) at which three of these 15 lines intersect?

V. V. Prasolov

M954. a) A rectangle with sides a and b is inscribed in a triangle so that all its vertices lie on the sides of the triangle. Let a₁ and b₁ be the lengths of the projections of the triangle onto lines parallel to sides a and b of the rectangle. Prove equality
$$\frac{a}{a_1} + \frac{b}{b_1} = 1.$$

6) An arbitrary tetrahedron is inscribed with rectangular parallel edges a, b and c so that all its vertices lie on the surface of the tetrahedron. Let a₁, b₁ and c₁ be the lengths of the projections of the tetrahedron onto straight lines parallel to edges a, b and c. Prove equality
$$\frac{a}{a_1} + \frac{b}{b_1} + \frac{c}{c_1} = 1.$$

V. N. Dubrovsky
