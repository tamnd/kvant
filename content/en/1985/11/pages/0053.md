The final stage of the 19th All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics took place on hospitable Belarusian soil in the city of Mogilev from April 6 to 13. 162 schoolchildren, represented by teams from the All-Union Resource Schools, Moscow, Leningrad and some special schools, took part in the modern ones - winners of the previous, resource school, stage of the Olympiad and possessed diplomas of I and II degrees at the Weight-Union Olympiad last year. Traditionally, the host team also took part in the Olympiad - three schoolchildren from Mogilev.

This year the Olympiad was dedicated to the 40th anniversary of Victory in the Great Patriotic War, and it is deeply appreciated that it took place in Belarus, which suffered so much in the bloody battle with the enemy, and where the memory of the war heroes is held sacred.

At the grand opening of the final stage of the Olympiad, held in the beautiful Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren, the representative of the Olympiad organizing committee, Deputy Minister of Education of the Armed Forces of the Soviet Socialist Republic M. M. Krugley Tello congratulated the participants on the beginning of improvement.
