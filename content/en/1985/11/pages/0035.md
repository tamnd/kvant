It is known that Napoleon devoted part of his free time to busy mathematics. He was credited with the following red theorem: “If equilateral triangles are constructed on the sides of a triangle towards the outside, then their centers will be the vertices of the equilateral triangle.” This triangle is called the "outer Napoleon triangle". The “internal triangle of Napoleon” is constructed in a similar way.
