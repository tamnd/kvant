Tasks

1. A millionaire approached a man standing on the sidewalk. “Did you not notice the license plate of the blue dump truck that just passed?” “Of course I did. The second movement number is obtained from the first by rearranging the digits, and if the second is subtracted from the first, you get the sum of the digits of one of them.” “Thank you,” said the millionaire, and wrote down the number in the notebook. What number did he write down?

2. Cut the “boat” (see figure) into two parts so that they can be folded into a square.

3. There was a stack of identical books on the table. I carefully pulled one of the books from the middle of the stack. We “went” with her and the books were lying on her. The books that lay below remained in place. Why?

4. In the expression 19 * 83 put the asterisks together so that the resulting number becomes an exact cube.

5. The ends of one side of the quadrilateral were connected to the middle of its opposite side (see figure). Prove that if the area of ​​the resulting triangle is equal to the area of ​​the quadrilateral, then this quadrilateral is a trapezoid or a parallelogram.
