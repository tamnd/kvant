The final stage of the 19th All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics took place on hospitable Belarusian soil in the city of Mogilev from April 6 to 13. 162 schoolchildren, represented by teams from the All-Union Resource Schools, Moscow, Leningrad and some special schools, took part in the modern ones - winners of the previous, resource school, stage of the Olympiad and possessed diplomas of I and II degrees at the Weight-Union Olympiad last year. Traditionally, the host team also took part in the Olympiad - three schoolchildren from Mogilev.

This year the Olympiad was dedicated to the 40th anniversary of Victory in the Great Patriotic War, and it is deeply appreciated that it took place in Belarus, which suffered so much in the bloody battle with the enemy, and where the memory of the war heroes is held sacred.

At the grand opening of the final stage of the Olympiad, held in the beautiful Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren, the representative of the Olympiad organizing committee, Deputy Minister of Education of the Armed Forces of the Soviet Socialist Republic M. M. Krugley Tello congratulated the participants on the beginning of improvement.

10th grade

1. Solve the inequality

$$|\sin x - \sin y| + \sin x \cdot \sin y \leq 0.$$

2. A convex pentagon ABCDE is drawn on a plane. Let's construct the point $A_1$, symmetrical to the point $A$ relative to the point $B$, point $B_1$, symmetrical to the point $B$ relative to $C$, ..., point $E_1$, symmetrical to the point $E$ relative to the point $A$, and after that the correspondence pentagon ABCDE. Prove that using a compass and ruler, knowing the location of the points $A_1, B_1, C_1, D_1, E_1$, you can reconstruct the pentagon ABCDE.

3. The sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ is given by the rules: $a_{2n} = a_n$ for $n \geq 1$ and $a_{4n+1} = 1$, $a_{4n+3} = 0$ for $n \geq 0$. Prove that this sequence has no period.

4. There are $n$ straight lines ($n > 2$) on the plane, dividing the plane into several regions. Some of these areas are colored, with no two colored areas touching at the border. Prove that the number of colored regions does not exceed $(n^2 + n)/3$.

Second day

8th grade

5. There is a cube, a cubic box with a lid of the same dimensions and six colors. Each side of the cube and one of the sides of the box are painted red. Prove that a cube can be placed in a box in such a way that each face of the cube is adjacent to a face of the box colored with a different color.

6. Dynamite $A_0A_5$ divides a circle centered at $O$ into two semicircles. One of them is divided into five equal arcs $A_0A_1, A_1A_2, A_2A_3, A_3A_4, A_4A_5$. Line $A_1A_4$ intersects $OA_2$ and $OA_3$ at points $M$ and $N$. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments $A_2A_3$ and $MN$ is equal to the radius of the circle.

7. Pupils of the school mathematics club made a computer that turns four numbers ($a, b, c, d$) into four ($a - b, b - c, c - d, d - a$) by pressing a button. Prove that if not all numbers in the original quadruple are equal, then after some number of button presses a quadruple will be obtained, at least one of the numbers of which is greater than 1985.

8. The numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., $2n - 1$, $2n$ are divided into two groups of $n$ numbers each. Let $a_1 < a_2 < \dots < a_n$ be the numbers of the first group, written in ascending order, and $b_1 > b_2 > \dots > b_n$ be the numbers of the second group, written in descending order. Prove that

$$|a_1 - b_1| + |a_2 - b_2| + \dots + |a_n - b_n| = n^2.$$
