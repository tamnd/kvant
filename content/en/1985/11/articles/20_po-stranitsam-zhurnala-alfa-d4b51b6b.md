QUANTUM SMILES

Through the pages of Alpha magazine

"Alpha" ("Alpha") is a mathematical magazine for schoolchildren, which is published in the GDR and will celebrate its 20th anniversary next year. The articles in the magazine are available to students in grades 5-6. They are often accompanied by cartoons. Some of them are placed here.
