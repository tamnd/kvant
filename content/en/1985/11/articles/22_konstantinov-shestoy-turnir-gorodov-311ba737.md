The desire to spend their strength is typical of young people. A person who goes to competition will not leave the opportunity to come from the cities hosting the All-Union Olympiad, and sometimes even from other cities, expressing the readiness of the specialization to come for this. For a number of years this form has been popularized, and now we can say that it has been found to some extent. This is the Tourist of Cities. The Central Organizing Committee of Turir was created, headed by Academician V. S. Pugachev, and in some cities - local organizing committees. Most likely, this form will continue to change. To give an idea of ​​this competition, we talked about past competitors.

The initiative to hold the first tournament belongs to the Council of Young Scientists and Specialists under the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Latvia. Schoolchildren from Riga, Moscow and Kyiv took part in the tournament. Then the circle of participating cities gradually expanded. In the 1984/85 academic year, 28 cities with a total population of 31 million people participated in the sixth tournament. The tournament was held in mathematics, and in three cities - also in information. In the future, the thematic expansion of the tour is expected. A special commission selected tasks and sent them to cities. The good tradition of tourists is a good task. True, some of them are difficult, but they give a lot of points (points for completely solving each problem were reported along with the conditions). This is quite natural for general-level competition; schoolchildren in our country at international mathematical Olympiads took first place approximately the same number of times as all other countries combined. Moreover, the difficulty of the task is realistic - each task was solved by one of the participants, and those who participated in «Kvant» (in some cases - in the successful version) - these are M711-M715 («Kvant», 1981), M756, M757 (1982), M807, M808, M816, M818-M820 (1983), M851, M863, M864, M866-M870, M883-M886 (1984), M914, M923, M927, M936-M939 (1985).
