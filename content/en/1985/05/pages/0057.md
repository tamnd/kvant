Mathematics
Oral examination

1. Simplify the expression
a) $\frac{1 + \cos \alpha + \cos 2\alpha + \cos 3\alpha}{\cos \alpha + 2\cos^2 \alpha - 1}$;
b) $\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{x-1}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}} \right) : \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{x-1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}} \right)$
at $x = \frac{a^2 + b^2}{2ab}$ and $b > a > 0$.

2. Calculate without tables
a) $\sin 20^\circ \cdot \sin 40^\circ \cdot \sin 60^\circ \cdot \sin 80^\circ$;
b) $\cos \frac{\pi}{5} \cdot \cos \frac{2\pi}{5}$;
b) $\lg tg 3^\circ \cdot \lg tg 6^\circ \cdot \lg tg 9^\circ \cdot \ldots \cdot \lg tg 87^\circ$.

3. Prove the identity
a) $32\sin^6 \alpha = 10 - 15\cos 2\alpha + 6\cos 4\alpha - \cos 6\alpha$;
b) $tg 3\alpha = tg \alpha \cdot tg (60^\circ + \alpha) \cdot tg (60^\circ - \alpha)$;
b) $\cos^2 \alpha - \sin^2 \beta + \cos^2 (\alpha - \beta) = 2\cos \alpha \times \cos \beta \cdot \cos (\alpha - \beta)$.

4. Solve the equation
a) $4^{-\frac{1}{x}} - 6^{-\frac{1}{x}} = 2 \cdot 9^{-\frac{1}{x}}$;
b) $\log_2 x + \log_3 x + \log_4 x = 1$;
b) $\sin x + \sin 2x + \sin 3x = \cos x + \cos 2x + \cos 3x$.

5. Solve the inequality
a) $\frac{1}{3^x + 5} < \frac{1}{3^{x+1} - 1}$;
b) $\log_1 + x (2 - x) < 1$;
b) $\sqrt{x+9} + \sqrt{2x+4} > 5$.

6. Build a graph
a) $|x| + |y| = 2$;
b) $y = |x^2 + x| - x^2$.

7. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} xy = 40, \\ x^2y = 4. \end{cases}$$

Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. A train whose mass is $m = 4 \cdot 10^6$ kg moves at a speed of $v = 10$ m/s. Before stopping, trains to slow down. Braking force
niya $F = 2 \cdot 10^5$ N. What distance will the train travel $t = 1$ minutes after the start of braking?

2. Two masses with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ are connected by a thread thrown over a stationary block. Determine the tension force of the thread when the loads move. Neglect the mass of the block and the friction in the axle.

3. Find the work that must be done to compress the spring by $\Delta l = 20$ cm, if it is known that the elastic force of the spring is proportional to the magnitude of the deformation and under the action of a force $F_0 = 30$ N the spring compresses by $\Delta l_0 = 1$ cm.

4. A ball of mass $m$ is suspended on a thread of length $l$. The thread is deflected at an angle $\alpha = 90^\circ$ from the vertical and released. Determine the maximum thread tension.

5. A small body of mass $m$ slides without friction from the top of a hemisphere of radius $R$. At what height will the body lift off from the surface of the hemisphere?

6. A load with a mass of $m = 2$ kg falling from a height of $h = 5$ m penetrates into soft soil to a depth of $l = 5$ cm. Determine the average resistance force of the soil.

7. The vrusk slides first along an inclined plane with a length of $l = 50$ cm and a height of $h = 10$ cm, and then along a horizontal surface (the transition is smooth), after which it is left. Determine the coefficient of friction, considering it the same if the block travels a distance of $s = 1,5$ m on a horizontal surface before stopping.

8. Two pieces of ice weighing $m = 200$ g each at a temperature $T = 273$ K rub against each other in a vacuum with the help of a motor developing a power of $N = 10$ W. How long will it take for the ice to melt? Specific heat of melting of ice $\lambda = 3,35 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg.

9. A speck of dust with a mass of $m = 10^{-8}$ g “hangs” between the plates of a horizontal flat force $K$ which is applied to an attraction of $U = 5$ kV. The distance between the plates is $d = 5$ cm. What is the dust protection?

10. Two charges $q_1 = 4 \cdot 10^{-8}$ Kl and $q_2 = 2,5 \cdot 10^{-8}$ Kl are in the air at a distance of $r_1 = 1$ m from each other. How much work must be done to compress the charges to a distance of $r_2 = 0,2$ m?
