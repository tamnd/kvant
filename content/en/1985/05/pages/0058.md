3. Let $|BC| = l$, spring tension force $T$, support reaction force $N$, friction force $F$ (Fig. 1). The block does not rotate if the sum of the moments of forces about the axis passing through the point $B$ is equal to zero. When turning the wheel clockwise:
$$N_1l \sin \alpha - Tl \sin \alpha + \mu N_1l \cos \alpha = 0$$
where
$$F_1 = \mu N_1 = \mu T \sin \alpha / (\sin \alpha + \mu \cos \alpha)$$
When turning the wheel counterclockwise:
$$N_2l \sin \alpha - Tl \sin \alpha - \mu N_2l \cos \alpha = 0$$
where
$$F_2 = \mu N_2 = \mu T \sin \alpha / (\sin \alpha - \mu \cos \alpha)$$
As a result, we have $F_2 = F_1 \frac{tg \alpha + \mu}{tg \alpha - \mu}$ at $\mu < tg \alpha$.
At $\mu \geq tg \alpha$ the wheel cannot be rotated counterclockwise.
