The hall of the legendary “Katyushas” (see photo on the first page of the cover) is a symbol of the abbreviated present of the largest troops that led to victory in the Great Patriotic War. It is also a symbol of the zero effort of scientists, engineers and workers who created armed victories. Read about the work of scientists carried out before and during the war in the articles “Physics to the Front” and “Mathematics and Mathematicians in the Great Patriotic War.”
