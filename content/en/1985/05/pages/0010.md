⟦folio 8⟧

Responsive and intensive work in difficult conditions.

Of course, we could also talk about the assistance that physicists provided to the front. But I thought that the examples given were enough to convincingly show how scientists supported the success of our armed forces.

It is difficult to speak to a lesser extent about other sciences involved in military affairs. I will only mention that the Soviet school of physical chemists, headed by Academician N. N. Semenov, has been studying combustion and explosion processes for a long time. During the war, these scientists had to use the accumulated reserves for direct military purposes and actively contribute to our historical victory.

After the war, the Germans realized that our science and technology were up to the demands of the times. And indeed, Soviet scientists, in particular physicists, used their patriotic duty to help the front in the most vague way.
