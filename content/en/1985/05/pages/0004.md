⟦folio 2⟧

40 years old
Great Victory

It has been forty years since the day when the peoples of the whole world solemnly celebrated victory over Hitler's fascism. They had to pay a dear price for this victory. The Soviet people made a decisive contribution to the defeat of the fascist police, who conquered almost all European countries and subjugated their entire economic power. History knows that in those years there was no other country capable of such a feat. More than twenty million Soviet people died in this war, which we rightly call the Great Patriotic War, and among them there were many very young people who, like our readers, stood on the threshold of their lives. The war shook up the entire people - everyone rushed to the front. Boys and girls who had not reached legal age tried by hook or by crook to get into the active army. Especially many schoolchildren fought behind enemy lines, in underground organizations and partisan detachments. Young Guards of Krasnodon, young scouts in the detachments of Kovpak, Fedorov, Popudrenko, heroes of the Crimean catacombs, orphans adopted by military units - the memory of them will forever remain in our hearts.

In those years, our army received enormous help from scientists. From the very first days of the war, they everywhere switched to developing problems related to strengthening the country's defense. Thanks to their success, our army acquired types of weapons that were much superior in their characteristics to the enemy’s weapons - rocket artillery (Katyushas), T-34 tanks, Il-2, Yak-3, Pe-2 aircraft, anti-tank guns with improved shells. Soviet science withstood the harsh test of war with honor.

The Soviet Union is the main stronghold of the world. Thanks to the peaceful policy of our people, there were no wars in Europe for forty years. While fighting for peace, we firmly remember that it is not straightened out, but achieved.

Aggressive imperialist forces threaten us with a new war. They spend colossal amounts of money on improving weapons of mass destruction and increasing their reserves, fueling the transfer of the arms race into outer space. But the ensuing years convincingly showed that Soviet scientists, together with the entire people, are capable of solving the most complex defense problems and quickly crushing all imperialist hopes for military superiority. This happened with atomic and thermonuclear weapons, missile weapons, multi-charge warheads, etc. This happened recently with cruise missiles. This is the guarantee of our safety and the safety of all people.

The peoples of the world have long been actively fighting against imperialist aggressive plans, and we invariably see our great Motherland in the forefront of these fighters.
