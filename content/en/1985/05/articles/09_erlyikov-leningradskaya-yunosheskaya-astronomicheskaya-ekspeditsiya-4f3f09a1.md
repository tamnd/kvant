Conclusion from the author

Of course, my story is a quick overview of the “urgent” problems and difficulties that specialists encountered when designing the first missions to comets in human history. A lot has been left “overboard” - after all, it is impossible to embrace the immensity (and especially in magazine status). Among this “much” are already solved scientific and technical problems associated with the Vega project, and the questions that scientists “ask” the comet... But I would like to dwell a little on one “philosophical” question, which probably has arisen more than once.

Why all this? Isn't it better to spend money on household needs? Build, for example, more residential buildings, factories, sanatoriums? We can answer briefly: man does not live by bread alone. But you can go into more detail.

Programs in applied fields of science and technology, those that create machine tools, cranes, grain harvesting machines, produce medicine, etc., are impossible without a program in fundamental research, although at first glance they are not related to each other.

Artificial satellites of the Earth and ships controlled by astronauts, originally intended for the study of near-Earth space - an area that would seem to be a needy economy - today provide free information about our earth's natural resources, about the occurrence of minerals on the surface of our planet, and allow us to make long-term meteorological forecasts, which are so necessary in national economic activity.

I do not want to become the basis of the predictions of what missions to comets in general and Halley’s comet in particular may give to man in the future. The next perihelion passage of Halley's Comet is due to occur in 2061, and the specialists of all space programs have not neglected to think about future generations: the scientific information received by the experts will be collected in the World Data Center so that the children and grandchildren of perfect people can use it the next time this wonderful comet returns.

Leningrad Youth Astronomical Expertise

On October 16, 1982, the appearance of Halley's Comet was recorded in the constellation Canis Minor. Since that day, astronomers around the world have been monitoring the movement of the comet through the constellation. Scientists have already excavated its elements and calculated the effective one, which will help observers study this unique natural phenomenon.

The Soviet research program for Halley's comet (SOPROG) devoted more space to amateur observation. This is due to the fact that such observations, despite the speed of astronomical equipment deployed by amateurs, can make a significant contribution if carried out according to a single program.

In Leningrad, amateur astronomical expertise has been constantly operating for many years, in which people of various ages and professions participate. A youth expert group is being organized to observe Halley's comet. Its age range is from 13 to 20 years. It will be attended by schoolchildren, vocational school students, students of technical skills and adults (about twenty people in total).

According to calculations, in the Leningrad area the conditions for observing the comet will generally be unsatisfactory, and at favorable times the comet will be so weak that it can only be seen with the help of special astronomical instruments. And although in October - December 1985 a number of experiments and preliminary work are still planned to be carried out in the vicinity of Leningrad, the question arose of equipping experiments in the southern regions of the country, convenient for observation.

Coordination of centers for amateur observation SOPROG allocated an area of ​​the city of Alma-Ata for the Leningrad Youth Expertise. Work there will take place approximately from March 5 to April 27, 1986.

Preparations for the examination are already underway. A preliminary scientific program has been drawn up. It includes photography, spectral analysis of the comet, determination of its shape, size, Blocks of the comet itself and its nucleus, etc. The time service will work in the examination.

The examination materials and the results of scientific observations will be transferred to the SOPROG information collection center.
