Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov (MSU) announces admission of students to grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher education, primarily to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of solving the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 1 to the address: 119899, ​​Moscow, GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3FSh. The decision of the 3FS admissions committee on enrollment will be communicated by October 15. Verified introductory assignments will not be sent back.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on the sections of physics studied in high school. Solved tasks are evaluated, solved and sent back. Students of the 9th grade of 3FS at the end of the year are transferred to the 10th grade based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task
Those entering the 9th grade of 3FS solve tasks 1-4, and those entering the 10th grade solve tasks 4-7.

1. "Meeting point" Two balls are thrown vertically upward with a time interval t from the same point with a speed v each. How long after the second ball is released will they collide?

2. "Coin on a wedge." A coin lies on a wedge with angle α. With what smallest degree must the wedge move along the horizontal plane in order for the coin to freely move down?

3. "Horsepower" What power does the horse develop when moving the sleigh if it pulls it uniformly uphill at speed u? The mass of the sled is m, the coefficient of friction is μ, and the angle of inclination of the mountain is α.

4. "Fast-Light Speed." Two rods intersect at an angle of 2α and move with equal speeds v perpendicular to themselves. What is the speed of the point where the arrows intersect? Can she exceed the speed of light?

5. "Related Downloads". When loading q₁, q₂ and q₃, connected to each other by two threads of length l, are on the same straight line. What is the tension force in the threads?

6. "An unknown formula." Find the formula for the compound of nitrogen with oxygen, if m=1 g of it in the gaseous state in a volume V=1 l is created at a temperature t=17 °C pressure p=314 hPa.

7. "Black box". An amperator, a resistance resistor R=1 Ohm and a constant voltage source U₁=5 V (series connection) were connected to a box with two terminals. The amperor showed a current I₁=1 A. When another voltage source U₂=20 V was turned on, the amperor showed a current I₂=2 A. What is inside the box?
