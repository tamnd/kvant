In this problem, we again used auxiliary constructions to construct a series of triangles that allowed us to carry out the necessary calculations.

Exercise
1. Make sure that the equations in the problems from the article in «Kvant», 1983, No. 2 are obtained by a method that completely coincides with the given diagram.

2. The height of a regular triangular paramid is $h$. The intersection points of the heights of each of the lateral faces and the vertices of the paramid lie on the surface of a ball of radius $r$. Find the volume of the paramid.

3. In the trapezoid $ABCD$ the point $K$ is the middle of the base $AB, M$ is the middle of the base $CD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if it is known that $DK$ is the bissertis of the angle $D, BM$ - the bissertis of the angle $B$, the largest of the angles at the lower base $AB$ is equal to $60^\circ$, and the perimeter of the trapezoid is 30.

4. In the dan plane there is a right angle. A circle with center located inside this angle touches one side of the angle, intersects the other side at points $A$ and $B$, and intersects the bissertis of the angle at points $C$ and $D$. The length of the chord $AB$ is $\sqrt{6}$ cm, the length of the chord $CD$ is $\sqrt{7}$ cm. Find the radius of the circle.

5. In triangle $ABC$, angle $A$ is equal to $a$, and the opposite side is equal to $a$. Find the other two sides if it is known that side $a$ is the geometric mean of the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of this triangle.

6. The edge of the cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is equal to $a$, the point $E$ is the middle of the edge $AA_1$. On the continuation of the edge $DA$ the point $F$ is taken so that $|FA|=a/2$. Find the radius of the smaller sphere passing through the points $E$ and $F$ and touching the planes $BB_1C_1C$ and $DD_1C_1C$.

7. In a regular quadrangular paramid, the center of the inscribed ball divides the height of the paramid in the ratio $p:q (p<q)$. Determine the angle of inclination of the rib to the base.

8. Through the point $O$, lying on the edge of two angles of magnitude $\varphi$, a straight line $OA$ is drawn, lying in one corner and forming an angle $a$ with the edge at two angles, and a straight line $OB$, lying in the other corner and forming an angle $\beta$ with the edge. Find the angle $\lambda$ between these lines (Fig. 8).

9. Take an arbitrary 6 elements given by an (irregular) tetrad and calculate all its other elements.

Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. Which number is greater
   $$\frac{55555553}{55555557} \text{ или } \frac{666666664}{666666669}?$$
2. (Newton's task). The grass throughout the meadow grows equally thick and fast. It is known that 60 cows would eat all the grass in 24 days, and 30 cows in 60 days. How many cows would eat all the grass in 100 days?

3. Based on the appearance of the graph of the quadratic function $y=ax^2+bx+c$, determine the signs of the coefficients $a, b$ and $c$ (Fig. 1).

4. The median and altitude drawn from one vertex of a triangle divide its angle into three equal parts. Find the angles of the triangle.

5. a) Prove that the angle with a vertex inside the circle changes, we obtain the magnitude of the arcs enclosed between its sides, and the magnitude of the arcs enclosed between the extensions of its side, and the angle with the vertex outside the circle - the resulting magnitude of the arcs enclosed between the sides (Fig. 2):

$$\alpha = \frac{1}{2} (\cup AB + \cup CD),$$

$$\beta = \frac{1}{2} (\cup AB - \cup CD).$$

When solving problems 6) and c) you can use formulas (1) and (2).

6) In the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ between the extensions of opposite sides (Fig. 3) are known. Find the angles of the quadrilateral.

c) In triangle $ABC$, point $O$ is the center of the inscribed circle, point $A_1$ is the point of intersection of line $AO$ with the circumscribed circle. Prove that $|BA_1| = |OA_1| = |CA_1|$.

Ninth grade

6. Calculate Product

$$\left(1 + \frac{1}{1 \cdot 3}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 4}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{3 \cdot 5}\right) \dots \left(1 + \frac{1}{99 \cdot 101}\right).$$

7. Find the equation of the common tangent to parabolas

$$y = x^2 + 4x + 8 \text{ и } y = x^2 + 8x + 4.$$

8. The orthogonal projection of the right angle $AOB$ onto the plane $\alpha$ is again a right angle. Prove that either ($AO$) or ($BO$) is parallel to the plane $\alpha$.

9. Find a piece:

a) $\sin 10^\circ \sin 50^\circ \sin 70^\circ$;

b) $\cos \frac{\pi}{15} \cos \frac{2\pi}{15} \dots \cos \frac{7\pi}{15}$.

10. In a triangle $ABCD$ with base lengths $|AD| = a$ and $|BC| = b$, a segment is drawn with ends on the lateral sides of the trapezoid, parallel to the base and dividing it into two equal trapezoids. Find the length of this segment.

Tenth grade

11. A plane drawn through the center of a ball inscribed in a cone parallel to the base divides this cone into two parts of equal volume. Find the angle at the vertex of the cone section.

12. Solve the equations:

a) $(x^2 - x + 1)^2 + 2(x^3 + 1) = (x + 1)^2$;

b) $\sqrt[3]{(x + 1)^2} - 2\sqrt[3]{x^2 - 1} = 8\sqrt[3]{(x - 1)^2}$.

13. Find the area enclosed by one “half-wave” sine $y = \sin x$ and the tangents drawn to the graph of the sine at the abscissa points 0 and $\pi$ (Fig. 4).

14. It is known that $\log_7 12 = a$, $\log_{12} 24 = b$. Find $\log_{54} 168$.

15. a) Give an example showing that two altitudes of a tetrahedron (or their extensions) may not intersect.

6) In the tetrahedron $ABCD$ the heights $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $CC_1$ and $DD_1$ are drawn. Prove that if lines $AA_1$ and $BB_1$ intersect, then lines $CC_1$ and $DD_1$ also intersect.
