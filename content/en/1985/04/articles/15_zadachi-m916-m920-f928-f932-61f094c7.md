M916. From the middle of each side of an acute triangle, perpendiculars are dropped to the other two sides. Prove that the area of ​​the bounded immishtirulnik is equal to half the area of ​​the triangle.

M917. a) What is the length of the maximum series of consecutive unlucky tickets? 6) How many such series of maximum length are there? (Ticket numbers are considered to range from 000000 to 999999 inclusive; a ticket is called lucky if the sum of the first three digits of its number is equal to the sum of the last three digits.)

M918*. The radius of the inscribed circle of a triangle is 1, and the lengths of its sides are integers. Prove that these numbers are 3, 4 and 5.

B. V. Praslov

$$\int_0^{\pi/4} tgx dx + \int_0^1 arctgx dx = \frac{\pi}{4}.$$

M920. a) Find at least one solution to the equation
$$x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = x^2y^2z^2$$
in natural numbers.
6)* Prove that the equation
$$x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = nx^2y^2z^2$$
has a natural solution only for $n=1$ and $n=3$ and find all these solutions.

P. A. Mazov

L. A. Ashkinazi

F930. The surface charge densities on the straight plates of a flat capacitor are +σ and -σ. The distance between the plates is much smaller than the size of the plates. Determine the direction of the electric field at point A (see figure 2).

F931. A point source is located at a distance of 2F from a lens with focal length F. A screen (Fig. 3) is located at a distance of 3F from the lens. Behind the lens there is a round plate of radius R, which transmits light unevenly (the radius of the plate is not less than the radius of the lens). In this case, the resulting spot on the screen has uniformly the maximum possible illumination. How is the “bandwidth” of the plate distributed along its radius?

A. A. LanUDEC

F932. Tuning a guitar is as follows: by clamping a second string in a certain place, they are added so that it sounds in unison with the first, then the other strings are tuned in the same way. Can a person who has absolutely no ear for music (that is, cannot distinguish sounds by pitch) tune a guitar?

I. I. Mazin
