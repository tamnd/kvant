Oral exam assignment
Faculty of Physics
1. A body, moving uniformly accelerated from a state of rest, has traveled a certain distance. What is the ratio of the average speed of the body in the second half of the path to the average speed in the first half?
2. Two flasks are mounted above a vessel with water, top down, so that the pots are in the water. Fully filled with water. At the same time, the water level in them is different. Explain the observed phenomenon. How to change the water level in only one of the flasks?
3. The graph shown at figure or will plot the process of converting water vapor into ice. Using the graph, determine what the initial amount of steam is and how much of it turns into ice.
4. A ray of light falls at an angle a=30° on the surface of the water poured into the aquarium. There is a flat mirror at the bottom of the aquarium. How many times does the mass of water and the output of rays change if water is replaced by another liquid whose refractive index is twice that of water? The fluid level is the same.
Faculty of Mathematics
1. From points A and B, two bodies simultaneously called each other began to move. After they are repeated, the first body, after t_1=10 s, approaches point B, and the second, having traveled s=100 m in t_2=40 s, approaches point A. Determine the speed of movement of the body if the movement was uniform and rectilinear.
2. A hermetically sealed tin can is filled with water. One small hole was made in the lid, bottom and side wall of the jar. How can you stop water from flowing out of the jar by closing only one of the holes?
3. A current source with EMF b = 30 V and internal resistance r = 6 Ohm is shorted to two resistors connected in parallel. In this case, the amount of heat released in the first resistor is three times more, and in the second - six times more than in the internal resistance of the source. How much current flows through each resistor and through the source?

How can we experimentally determine whether a cartridge case is charged or not, if its charge is much less than the charge formed by the available plates rubbing against each other?
