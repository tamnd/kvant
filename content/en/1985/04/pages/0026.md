Note that schoolchildren often say, for example, that the cosine theorem allows a triangular triangle to be expressed in terms of two and the angle between them. This is not entirely true: this formulation assumes that we know two sides and the angle between them, and we want to calculate the triangular one, which is why this theorem is mainly used in this case.

It would be more accurate to say that the cosine theorem relates the values ​​of a triangle and one of the angles: this formulation significantly expands the scope of its application. And the formula of the cosine theorem can be resolved with respect to any of the values ​​included in it.
