⟦folio 27⟧

In this problem, we again used auxiliary constructions to construct a series of triangles that allowed us to carry out the necessary calculations.

Exercise
1. Make sure that the equations in the problems from the article in «Kvant», 1983, No. 2 are obtained by a method that completely coincides with the given diagram.

2. The height of a regular triangular paramid is $h$. The intersection points of the heights of each of the lateral faces and the vertices of the paramid lie on the surface of a ball of radius $r$. Find the volume of the paramid.

3. In the trapezoid $ABCD$ the point $K$ is the middle of the base $AB, M$ is the middle of the base $CD$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if it is known that $DK$ is the bissertis of the angle $D, BM$ - the bissertis of the angle $B$, the largest of the angles at the lower base $AB$ is equal to $60^\circ$, and the perimeter of the trapezoid is 30.

4. In the dan plane there is a right angle. A circle with center located inside this angle touches one side of the angle, intersects the other side at points $A$ and $B$, and intersects the bissertis of the angle at points $C$ and $D$. The length of the chord $AB$ is $\sqrt{6}$ cm, the length of the chord $CD$ is $\sqrt{7}$ cm. Find the radius of the circle.

5. In triangle $ABC$, angle $A$ is equal to $a$, and the opposite side is equal to $a$. Find the other two sides if it is known that side $a$ is the geometric mean of the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circles of this triangle.

6. The edge of the cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is equal to $a$, the point $E$ is the middle of the edge $AA_1$. On the continuation of the edge $DA$ the point $F$ is taken so that $|FA|=a/2$. Find the radius of the smaller sphere passing through the points $E$ and $F$ and touching the planes $BB_1C_1C$ and $DD_1C_1C$.

7. In a regular quadrangular paramid, the center of the inscribed ball divides the height of the paramid in the ratio $p:q (p<q)$. Determine the angle of inclination of the rib to the base.

8. Through the point $O$, lying on the edge of two angles of magnitude $\varphi$, a straight line $OA$ is drawn, lying in one corner and forming an angle $a$ with the edge at two angles, and a straight line $OB$, lying in the other corner and forming an angle $\beta$ with the edge. Find the angle $\lambda$ between these lines (Fig. 8).

9. Take an arbitrary 6 elements given by an (irregular) tetrad and calculate all its other elements.
