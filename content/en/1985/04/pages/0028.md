Example 2. There are the figures shown at figure 6, a. We need to find the radius of the smallest circle (with center at C).

The radius $r_1$ is defined trivially: $r_1 = a(1 - 1/2)/2$. To determine $r_2$ we have the lengths of the segments $AB$, $AO$ and $BO$, which is not enough to construct the equation. Therefore, we construct 2 triangles $QAB$ and $QBO$ and a rectangle $QPLB$, redistributing $QB$ to the segment $AO$. The rectangle allows you to transfer the size $r_2$ to the segment $QP$, after which we get a picture similar to example 1. The length $QB$ is expressed in two ways:

$$|QB|^2 = (r_1 + r_2)^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2 = (a - r_2)^2 - (a/\sqrt{2} + r_2)^2.$$

From the equation we can obtain $r_2$. The object to be expressed in two ways in the following connection (Fig. 6, 6) is the segment $FC$.
