ANNIVERSARY OF SPACE COMMUNICATIONS

20 years ago, on April 23, 1965, the first artificial communications satellite “Molniya-1” was launched into orbit in our country, and soon with its help began transmitting Central Television programs, as well as telephone conversations, telephone and photo telegraph messages from Moscow to Vladivostok and back. Such satellites are placed into highly elongated elliptical orbits with a maximum distance from the Earth's surface of about 40,000 km and an orbital period of about 12 hours. Within 6-8 hours they are visible from all over our country, and their retranslation devices transmit a variety of information between remote friends from each other point. Molniya communications satellites made it possible to create the Orbita space television communications system.

The creation of space communications is a major event in the history of Russian science and technology. It was marked by the issuance of special postage signs both in our country and abroad. The selection shown here reproduces stamps and a postal block depicting the Soviet Molniya communications satellites.

V. Rudov
