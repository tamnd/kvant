Physics
Oral exam objectives

Problems 2, 4 are presented at the entrance exams for the Faculty of Physics and Mechanics, 5 - for the Radiophysics Faculty, 6, 8 - for the Electromechanical Faculty, 1, 7 - for the Hydraulic Engineering Faculty and 3 - for the Evening Faculty.

1. The cyclist travels the first third of the journey at a speed of $v_1=2,0$ m/s, the second at a speed of $v_2=3,0$ m/s, and the last third at a speed of $v_3=5,0$ m/s. Find the average speed of the cyclist.

2. A metal washer slides along a weightless rubber cord of length $l_0$. The trem force acting between the cord and the washer is constant and is equal in modulus to $f$. Cord stiffness $k$. Find the amount of heat released during this process. What part of the work done by the tremiya force acting on the cord will be converted into heat?

3. The vapor density is $\rho=2,4$ kg/m$^3$ at a temperature of $T=295$ K and a pressure of $p=10^5$ Pa. Find the mass of one kilomole and one molecule of vapor, considering it an ideal gas. The universal gas constant is considered equal to $R=8,3$ J/(mol K).

4. The glass plate completely fills the gap between the plates of a flat capacitor, the capacitance of which, in the absence of the plate, is $C_0$. The capacitor is connected to a constant voltage source $U$. Find the mechanical work that must be done against electrical forces to remove the plate from the capacitor.

5. In the circuit depicted at figure 1, the emf $\mathcal{E}$ and $\mathcal{E}_0$ sources, the resistances $R$ and $R_0$ resistors, and the capacitance $C$ of the capacitor are known. The internal resistances of the sources are inescapably small. Find the charge on the plate of capacitor 1.

6. Two light bulbs were switched on first in series and then in parallel (Fig. 2). The resistance of the first bulb is $R_1=360$ Ohm, the second - $R_2=240$ Ohm. Which of the lamps consumes more power and how many times in the first and second cycles?

7. Two mathematical matrices starting from oscillating simultaneously. During the same time, the first matrix makes $N_1=16$, and the second $N_2=8$ oscillations. Find the ratio of the lengths of the magnets.

8. The angle of incidence of light on the boundary of two media (when it passes from the first medium to the second) is $a=30^\circ$. The absolute indicator of the second environment assumption is $n_2=2,40$. Find the absolute exponent of the assumption of the second medium if the angle between the reflected and assumed rays is $\delta=90^\circ$.

$$\log_3(2x+21)-\log_3(4-x)=2^{\log_2 6-1}.$$

$$\sin^2\left(\frac{5\pi}{2}+\beta\right)+\sin(\pi-\beta)\cdot(1+\text{ctg}\beta)+\frac{\sin^2\beta}{\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}-\beta\right)(1+\text{tg}\beta)}=\cos\beta-\sin\beta.$$

4. Two circles touch with an internal edge at point $N$. The segment $MN$ is the diameter of the larger circle. The chord $MK$ of the larger circle touches the smaller circle at point $C$. Prove that $NC$ is a bisector of $\angle MNK$.
