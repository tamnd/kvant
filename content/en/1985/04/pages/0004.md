Soviet Science Day

Over the course of several decades, Soviet science has traveled an impressive path, crowned with many world-famous achievements. This path can be illustrated by the statement of our outstanding creators of science. They talk not only about the features of the science of socialization, but also about the most important features of the Soviet scientist.

Technical and social progress are closely related to each other. October served as the basis for massive social transformations, which in turn had a profound impact on the scientific and technological revolution.

Academician M. V. KELDYSH

Soviet science, far from extremes, combines healthy, strong practicalism, determined by the task of socialist construction, with that internal logic of scientific thinking, which requires the correct formulation and solution of scientific problems.

Academician S. I. VAVILOV

It is the progress of functional knowledge that changes seemingly established points of view in science, opens up new areas in science and technology, radically changes the technological, leads to the emergence of new materials and opens up the opportunity to use modern, new, often unexpected phenomena in areas that have nothing to do with the original area of ​​research.

Academician A. P. ALEXANDROV

... the development of science is not only the result of a genius process of individuals, but rather the fruit of the organized and purposeful work of many simple, but very real, conscientious and hardworking people.

Academician A. I. BERG

In the organizational case of applied mathematics, in the dialectical unity of the abstract and the concrete, the most vivid and philosophically significant feature of Soviet mathematics appears.

Academician P. S. ALEXANDROV

... modern physics is a kind of two-faced Janus. On the one hand, it is a science with a burning gaze, which strives to penetrate into the depths of the great laws of the mathematical world. On the other hand, it is a fundamental technology, a workshop of bold technical ideas, a pillar of defense and the driving force of continuous industrial progress.

Academician L. A. ARTSIMOVICH

... to explore, capable of deep philosophical analysis, enriched with the concept of what is happening on the entire huge front of science, with an understanding of the existence of social processes, signs and values, such a researcher, among other things, takes a broader look at the problems of his scientific field, sees what escapes the gaze of some professional ascetic.

Academician R.V. KHOKHLOV

Science is the common heritage of man, and the task of a true scientist is to enrich this stock of knowledge available to everyone.

Academician A. N. KOLMOGOROV

The Soviet scientist is a leader of his country, therefore the ideals of his life and work coincide with the ideals that all citizens of his country live. His scientific work is driven by the same sense of purpose that drives the entire country.

Academician P. L. KAPITSA

I believe that human reason will prevail and people will not allow the achievements of science to be used for purposes incompatible with humanism and human morality.

The task of scientists will be not only to develop scientific research, but also to fight for its use for the benefit of the common people of the world.

Academician I. I. ARTOBOLEVSKY
