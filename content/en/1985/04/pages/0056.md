Leningrad State University named after. A. A. Zhdanova

Matematika
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)

1. The frequencies of $x, y$ genes $a, b$ are accordingly converted as a result of one type of selection into new frequencies

$$x' = \frac{x(kx + y)}{kx^2 + 2xy + ky^2}, \quad y' = \frac{y(x + ky)}{kx^2 + 2xy + ky}$$

genes $a, b$ of the next generation. Determine the initial frequencies if it is known that the adaptation coefficient is $k = 1/2$, and in the next generation for every 40 genes $a$ there are 33 genes $b$.

2. Solve the equation

$$\cos 3x = -\cos x \text{ ctg}\left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \text{ ctg}\left(x + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right).$$

3. At what value of $b \in [0; 1]$ is the maximum absolute error of the approximate formula

$$\sqrt{x} \approx b \left(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{3}{4} x - \frac{x^2}{8}\right) + (1 - b) \left(\frac{3}{4} + \frac{3}{8} x - \frac{x^2}{64}\right)$$

on the interval [1; 4] will be the smallest?

4. In a circle of radius $R$ a viscanary triangle. Find a point on the circle for which the sum of the distances to the two nearest vertices of the triangle is the given value $L$.

5. A ball of radius $R$ and a circle of radius 5/4 $R$ have a common center $O$. The point $A$ is located in space such that $|AO| = 5R/4$ and the set of points on a given circle that can be connected to $A$ by a straight line that does not intersect the ball is an arc corresponding to the central angle $a$. Find the angle between $OA$ and the plane containing the given circle.

Option 2
(faculty of mathematics and mechanics)

1. On the interval $[\frac{1}{4}, 10]$ determine the maximum value of the absolute error of the approximate calculation $y = \sqrt{x}$ according to the following rule: if $(k - 1)^2 \leq x < k^2$ ($k = 1, 2, \dots$), then

$$\sqrt{x} \approx \frac{3}{8} k + \frac{3x}{4k} - \frac{x^2}{8k^3}.$$

2. Solve the equation

$$\sin 4x(2 + \sin 14x) = 2 \text{ ctg} 3x \cdot \cos 4x.$$

3. Under what conditions on the coefficients $a, b$ linear replacement

$$x = aY + bZ, \quad y = aZ + bX, \quad z = aX + bY$$

leaves the cubic shape unchanged

$$Q(x, y, z) = x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz?$$

4. In an equilateral trapezoid, the diagonal is perpendicular to the side, the larger base is $a$, and the sum of the smaller base and the side is 3a/4. Find the smaller base.

5. Through the vertex $A$ of a regular tetrahedron $ABCD$, whose edge is equal to $a$, a plane parallel to $CD$ is drawn. It is known that the section of a $AEF$ tetrahedron by a plane is a triangle of perimeter $p$. Find $|BE|$.

Option 3
(Faculty of Physics)

1. When two particles collide, moving towards each other, as a result of merger and subsequent splitting, a series of new particles with a total mass of $M$ is released. It is known that new particles are separated in different directions making an angle $a$ with the direction of movement of one of the vertices of the apex particles, and this second part had an impulse $P$ before the collision. Determine the lowest possible rate of fusion of the colliding particles.

2. Solve the equation

$$\text{ctg} x + \sin 2x = \text{ctg} 3x.$$

3. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} \log_x \left(\frac{y}{9}\right) + \frac{3x}{y(x + 9)} = 0, \\ (y - 2)^{-1} = (y - 2)^{-\log_9(x + 8)}. \end{cases}$$

4. The vertices of one square lie on the border of the second square. Find the ratio of the lengths of the segments into which these vertices divide the sides of the second square, if it is known that the ratio of the areas of the squares is $p$.

5. A regular triangular paramid is inscribed in a cylinder so that one of its lateral edges is a generatrix of the cylinder. Find the volume of the paramid by the radius $R$ of the base of the cylinder and by the magnitude of the arc angle $a$ at the lateral edge of the paramid.
