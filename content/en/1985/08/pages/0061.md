Assignments for the correspondence collective competition TUF-VIII

Most of the tasks are formed on the basis of specific physical phenomena and are designed to conduct server-side theoretical and experimental research, going beyond the “school” approach. The conditions of the problem are formed as briefly as possible and allow for various interpretations and degrees of simplification.

1. “Come up with it yourself.” Create a task-problem yourself and solve it.

2. "Anchor". How to declare that an anchor weighing 5 tons reliably holds a ship weighing 10 thousand tons?

3. "Poisson's ratio." It is known that any longitudinal deformation of a solid body also causes its transverse deformation. Explain this phenomenon. Propose a theoretical calculation of Poisson's ratio for metals. Conduct experimental and theoretical studies on the rubber rod.

4. "Double glazing". In residential premises in the central zone of the USSR, it is customary to install double frames on windows. Do you think we should switch to using triple frames?

5. "Oil film" “In 1757 I was on board one of the 96 ships of the flotilla sailing from Leesburg. I noticed that the surface of the water around the ships was surprisingly smooth, while around the others there was a strong disturbance caused by the sharp gusts of wind. Puzzled by the table at the strange distinction, I finally turned to the ship and asked what it could mean. “The cooks,” he said, “just like me, just poured greasy water through the scupper, and it got a little greasy on the sides of these ships.” In the captain’s intonation there was a slight shade of contempt expressed by a person who does not know some generally known truths.” (Venjamin Franklin)

Explain the phenomenon noticed by scientists. Conduct experiments on pouring oil onto a rough surface of water.

6. "Self-oscillations." Many people know a children's toy: a long duckling bends down to a trough of water and, showing itself, “tortures” the water, then throws it away from the trough, but after a while it bends down again and “tortures,” etc. From a physics point of view, this is an example of self-oscillations that occur in the presence of an external source of energy.

Design and make a visual device or toy that demonstrates self-oscillations.

7. "Surface Charge". It is known that two diopoles located at a distance r from each other, as shown in figure, come together with a force F~p₀²/r⁴, where p₀=ql is the diopole moment.

How will the diopoles interact if their moments are variable in time: p₁(t)=p₂(t)=p₀sinωt?

9. "The power of a magnet." Experimentally study the distance dependence of the force of attraction of a steel ball to the pole of a position magnet. Explain the results. Specifically study the behavior of magnetized and non-magnetized balls.
