The head of the circle should write a letter to the director of 3Sh with a request to send the first assignment and additional materials for it.

An application for admission to the mathematics or physics department of 3Sh can be sent along with the solutions to the corresponding first assignment, published below, no later than October 25.

Solutions to problems need to be written down in a simple student's notebook. leaving fields for replacement. On the cover of the terdie, please include the same information about yourself as on the application. Send your work along with your application only in a simple parcel post (do not bend or roll the paper into a tube). In the solution section, send out a piece of paper measuring 6x10 cm with your address written on it (stick it on the envelope when you send the answer).

At the address: 630090, Novosibirsk-90, st. Pirogova, 11, Zaonia School at NSU.

First math assignment
8th grade
1. How many kilograms of copper must be smelted from 2 kg of copper-seredba alloy containing 5% seredba to produce an alloy containing 2% seredba?
2. On the coordinate plane, draw a set of points (x, y) whose coordinates satisfy the conditions
$$2y - 1 \leq |x - 1| - 2x.$$
3. In a trapezoid, through the point of intersection of the diagonals, a segment is drawn parallel to the base, the ends of which lie on the sides. Find the length of this segment if the lengths of the bases are a and b.
4. How many times do you need to add the number 8 to make the total $8^{100}$?
5. A point moves along the hypotenesis of a right triangle. At what position of the point will the distance between its projections onto the sides be the smallest?
6. How to use a compass and ruler to construct a parallelgram using angles and diagonals?
9th grade
1. From points A and B, the distance between which is 22.4 km, two bicycles are called at the same time. If they move towards each other, they will meet in 0.5 hour. And if they blow in one direction (in the direction from A to B), then one of them owes the other in 3.5 hours. Find the speeds of the bicycles.
2. On the coordinate plane, draw a set of points (x, y) whose coordinates satisfy the conditions
$$|y^2 + 1| - 2y \geq |x - 1| - x.$$
3. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + xy + x = 10 \\ y^2 + xy + y = 20. \end{cases}$$
4. Triangle ABC is equilateral. Prove that the vector $2\vec{AB} + \vec{BC}$ is perpendicular to the vector $\vec{BC}$.
5. Find the last digit of the number 7$^{1985}$.
6. How to use a compass and ruler to construct a triangle using two sides and the median lying between them?

10th grade
1. Find the volume of a rectangular cone if it is known that its lateral surface, when unfolded on a plane, gives a sector of a circle of radius 10 and with a central angle $72^\circ$.
2. Based on the results of the three teams, it turned out that the first and second teams together produced twice as many parts as the third, and the first and third together produced three times as many as the second. Which team took first place?
3. How to construct a segment of length $\sqrt{7}$ using a compass and a ruler if a segment of length 1 is given on the plane?
4. Find a polynomial with integer coefficients for which the number $\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{7}$ is a root.
5. An equal trapezoid is described by circularity, the lengths of its bases are a and b. Find the length of the diagonal of this trapezoid.
6. Prove that for any a from the interval [0; π/2] the inequality $\cos(\sin a) > \sin(\cos a)$ holds.

First physics assignment

1. The barge is a box measuring $10 \times 4 \times 2$ m. Its weight with cargo is $M = 50$ t. Is it possible to load a couple more containers weighing $m_0 = 20$ t each onto the barge? Execution installation conditions.
2. In the circuit shown at figure 1, the resistances of all data resistors are in ohms. Which of them releases more heat when the circuit is connected to the target by points A and B?
3. A weight is weighed in an unbalanced position with weights of mass $m_1 = 2,5$ kg, and on the other with weights of $m_2 = 3,6$ kg. Find the mass of the load. (The weight of the cups and rocker arms of the scales is a complaint.)
4. The rubber tube is completely filled with resistance.
