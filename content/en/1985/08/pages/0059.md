VII Moscow Tour for Young Physicists

Time changes by the hour, And by the time of a person's life; But tell me, how do you change the depth of the Eastern Ocean?

Kozma Prutkov

The VII Moscow Tourism was held by the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov from October 1984 to February 1985. In this modern era, 40 schools in Moscow and the Moscow region attended high school students.

First place and the challenge tournament prize were awarded to the team of school No. 47 (captain - O. Vinogradova), second place - team of school No. 7 (R. Vakko) and Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University (A. Mikheev), third place - team of school No. 57 (V. Sadov), No. 82 village. Chernogolovka (V. Barzkin), No. 542 at MEPhI (O. Zaboronsky) and No. 679 (A. Nikitin). The winner of the captains' competition was A. Mikheev (Physical School No. 18). For the best report at the tournament, A. Vuyvolova (secondary school No. 7) was awarded a certificate of honor. For active participation in the tournament and demonstrated deep knowledge of the physics of human life, he presented certificates and gifts to 17 schoolchildren. The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University awarded physics instruments to OLS schools whose teams showed high results.

Below we publish the tasks of the TUV-VII physicist. You can use them when holding physics Olympiads and quizzes at school, science evenings, physical education classes, and in physics clubs.

Homework for tour physicists

Students were given one month to complete these tasks.

1. "Optical incendiary device." “In these thoughts of mine, it seems to me that it is possible to achieve the goal in only one way: by collecting the focuses of several lines or mirrors in one place, where, with their combined forces, they will produce heat greater than that known so far.” (M. V. Lomonosov)

How do the maximum heating temperature and the heating rate of an object depend on the number of beams in the device proposed by M. V. Lomonosov? What will the replacement of solar beams with laser beams lead to?

2. "Coil". A coil of copper wire is wound in several layers on a paper frame. Determine the number of turns of the coil without unwinding it. Estimate the ohmic resistance, inductance and capacitance of the coil. For the experiments, a coil was given with the following parameters: wire diameter 0.8 mm, coil diameter 30 mm, winding length 20 mm, number of turns 125.

3. "Benard cells". Create a setup to demonstrate the Benard cell. In your installation, how does the size of the cells depend on the temperature difference between the lower and upper layers of liquid and on the thickness of the liquid layer?

Approximate installation parameters:

1 - brass plate 1-3 mm thick;
2 - a piece of pipe made of heat-resistant material (hard rubber, plexiglass); internal diameter 100 mm, height 30 mm. The pipe should be attached to the plate with epoxy resin;
3 - transparent cover (glass).

The liquid being tested is sunflower oil, in which you need to add a pinch of silver (fine alamine powder). Pour a thin layer of liquid into the resulting bath, close the lid and begin uniformly heating the bottom plate - you will soon find Benard cells. (See also «Kvant», 1977, No. 4, p. 24.)

4. "Snow cover".
“The frost was wrapped in snow:
“Make sure you don’t freeze!”

(From a children's song)

“It is calculated that if the entire Earth were covered with snow, the average temperature of its surface would drop from the current +15 °C to −88 °C.”

(From a scientific book)

These two quotes contain a physical paradox - snow makes it warm and... cold. Resolve this paradox.

5. "Performance". Perform a performance on a physical topic with the participation of team members and Bolshoi. Performance duration: 5 minutes. The genre is arbitrary.
