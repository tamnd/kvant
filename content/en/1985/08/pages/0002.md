This design, which was exhibited in 1983 in Moscow at an exhibition dedicated to natural forms and architectural bionics, was created by the architect Yu. S. Lebedev. From a mathematical point of view, it is interesting in that it models a rather intricate surface with self-intersection, formed by rectangular segments. In the model, these segments are small rods, closed wire systems. They lower five studied twisted bindings in trains, as if hanging on the sides and on the diazonalli of the cadarata located on top.
