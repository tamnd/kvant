⟦folio none⟧

It is well known that a plane can be covered (without overlaps or gaps) with squares, regular triangles and hexagons. The colorful mosaic presented to your attention shows that the plane can also be covered with “T letters” made up of 10 squares. Thus, one of the problems formulated in the article by B. Grionbaum and J. Shepard “Some problems associated with flat brains” from the wonderful book “Mathematical Flower Garden” edited by M. Gardner (M.: Mir, 1983) is solved. The solution to this problem (and some other problems from the article by B. Grionbaum and J. Shepard was sent to us by A. Azamov.
