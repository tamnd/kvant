Correspondence school at NSU

At Novosibirsk State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol operates a correspondence school (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 of secondary schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students’ interest in the natural sciences.

There are 5 departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, chemical and economic. In the mathematical, physics and chemical departments, students in grades 8-10, in biology - only students in grades 9, in economics - only students in grades 10.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also include mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs (or electives), which work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. The leaders of each circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the corresponding subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader reports to 3SH his last name, first name, patronymic and the height of the room, a list of the circle (indicating the final grades for the assignment period), signed by the school director and sealed.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional teaching materials. The work of law students is checked in 3SH, and the work of circle members is checked by its leaders (at the request of the leaders, some of the work of circle members can be checked in 3SH).

Every year, some students in grades 8-9 of 3Sh are invited to the Summer School at NSU (which runs from August 1 to August 22). Here they, together with the winner of the Vesbiir Olympiad, attend lectures to local scientists, solve interesting problems at seminars, get acquainted with the university and research institutes of the Academic Town, separate and have fun. During the winter holidays, 3Sh students from nearby regions are invited to the Winter School at NSU.

To become a student at the Correspondence School at NSU, you must send an application with the requested first assignment to the director of 3Sh by September 30. The application must be written on a postcard and formatted in the following format:

1. Last name, first name, patronymic (in full, block letters)
2. The class in which you are a teacher in your school
3. Department 3Sh where you want to study (you can specify two departments)
4. Detailed home address with mandatory indication of post office code
