Traditional holiday of young mathematicians

The holiday of young mathematicians in the hospitable capital of Soviet Adjara - Batumi - has long become traditional. Every year, young mathematicians from different parts of our country come here for the autumn school holidays. The organizers of the holiday - the Council of Trade Unions of Adjara, the Ministry of Education of the Adjara Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, the Adjara branch of the All-Union Society "Knowledge", the regional committee of the vocational education of educators, the city department of public education and the Komsomol town, the Batumi Republican Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren, secondary schools No. 7 and 22, boarding school No. 2 - tried to make the holiday program more interesting and varied than in previous year.

The central place of the holiday is the conference, at which an authoritative jury consisting of mathematicians and teachers, and young guests of the holiday will hear reports and participate in their discussion. This year, the jury, headed by a professional from Tbilisi State University N. Kakhniashvili, had a heavy workload - a record number of reports were heard in the fifth sections (about 60). Interesting reports were presented by students from Moscow schools No. 444, 57 and 91. The best reports of the jury included the reports of a tenth-grader from Leningrad school No. 45 R. Alekseev “On some cyclic inequalities”, a student of Leningrad vocational school No. 253 M. Mikheev “Measuring the radius of a ball with a small compass and ruler”, Sverlov tenth-grader F. Tolstov “RAPIRA language interpreter for ES COMPUTER”, tenth grader of Yerevan Physics School No. 1 V. Stepanov “Sequence Convergence”, student of the 444th school A. Ermakov about the algorithm of the game “Bulls and Cows”. A number of interesting reports were presented by Batumi schoolchildren.

And, as always, the most interesting was the traditional mathematical KBH. KBH began with a greeting, then teams for a short presentation on the topic “Mathematics on a journey” - this was “homework”. After that, 12 teams - KBH students - were offered a variety of competitions: for the best poem with a given rhyme, the best cover for the magazine «Kvant», the best problem in «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren, and, of course, a lot of mathematical tasks.

Among them were joke problems: A man was born on June 1, 40 BC. z., and died on June 1, 40 AD. h. How many years did he live? (answer: 79 years - there was no year zero). There were also more challenging tasks: Arrange 6 points on a plane so that any three of them form an equilateral triangle (answer: these six points are the vertices and center of a regular pentagon).

KBH ended with a competition for captains, the winner of which was the team of Mr. Riggi Y. Antimirov. The captain of the Batumi team, A. Smirnov, also performed excellently: he was the only one who gave the correct answer to one of the three tasks of the captains’ competition: what is the greatest number of parts that four planes passing through one point can divide space into (answer: 14), and this brought his team to first place. As a result, the first place at KBH was shared by the teams of Batumi and Baku, the second - by the teams of school No. 57 of Moscow and Sverdlovsk, the third - by the teams of Leningrad Vocational School and Rigga.

The holiday program was very eventful. The schoolchildren visited the Akhalshensky state farm, made excursions to the Botanical Garden, shared the museum and the State Museum of Adjara. The program of the holiday included a meeting with the magazine «Kvant», at which the hosts and guests of the holiday congratulated our magazine on its anniversary - the fifteenth anniversary of the publication of the first issue «Kvant». An interesting programmer performed at the evening occupied by the mathematicians of the Basilica. On the eve of the closing ceremony, the traditional “Spark of Friendship” took place - one of the most symbolic points of the program. All those present expressed warm gratitude to the initiator and founder of this wonderful gathering of young mathematicians - the honored resource teacher Medea Illarionovna Zhgenti.
