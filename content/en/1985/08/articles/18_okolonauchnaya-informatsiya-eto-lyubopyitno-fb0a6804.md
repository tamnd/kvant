QUANTUM SMILES

Educational information

Scientists at Academy Town identified the latest signals from an unknown civilization. The beginning of the 6th signal corresponds to 12 o'clock Moscow time.

The Institute of Metrology has approved a new unit of time in the SI: the ciominute.

For fans of the Rusik cube: a theorem has been proven that the CD can be brought to the initial state from any position in no more than 6 moves. The proof used a Rusik cube, a wide brush and a set of six paints.

During the mathematics exam, teacher A. E. Ivanov took away the cheat sheet from student B. Petrov and, looking at the desired sheets, recognized his handwriting from fifteen years ago.

The collection “Selected Works” has been published. The collection contains 64 works of the type 2×2=4, 5×5=25, 6×6=36, 7×7=49, etc.

Electricians will be interested in the new edition of I. A. Goncharov’s book “Breakage”.

For applicants who have completed literature in writing, a complete collection of essays has been published. Those interested can contact the VRD office.

The entire natural series of numbers was learned by student Vasya Semechkin. The fenomena demonstration will continue in room 215.

This is interesting!

Axioma 1. What is written with a pen cannot be cut down with an axe.
Axioma 2. A forest can be cut down with an axe. Consequence. The forest cannot be described with a pen.

Axioma 3. They have been waiting for the promised for three years.
Axioma 4. Seven do not wait for one.
Consequence. If seven wait three years for what is promised, they are promised more than one thing.
