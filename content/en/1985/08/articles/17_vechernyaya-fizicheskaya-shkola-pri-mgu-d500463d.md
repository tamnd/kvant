F932. Tuning costs is as follows: pressing a second string in a certain place, adding it so that it sounds in unison with the first, then other strings are tuned in the same way. Can a person who has absolutely no ear for music (that is, cannot distinguish sounds in pitch very well) adjust the cost?

The air pressure in the space through which the sound wave passes differs from the average atmospheric pressure by the value $\Delta p$, which changes over time according to the law

$$\Delta p = p_0 \cos \omega t,$$

where $p_0$ is the amplitude of excess pressure, characterizing the loudness of the sound (determined by the natural conditions of excitation of the sound), $\omega$ is the oscillation frequency, characterizing the tone (pitch) of the sound (determined by the oscillation frequency of the sound source).

Let's imagine that we held two guitar strings at the same time with equal force, tightening them in such a way that, if precisely tuned, they would produce sounds of the same pitch. The total additional air pressure will change over time according to the law

$$\Delta p_c = p_0 \cos \omega t + p_0 \cos ((\omega + \delta \omega) t + \varphi) = 2 p_0 \cos \left( \frac{\delta \omega}{2} t + \frac{\varphi}{2} \right) \cos \left( \left( \omega + \frac{\delta \omega}{2} \right) t + \frac{\varphi}{2} \right),$$

where $\delta \omega$ is the frequency difference, which determines the degree of “out of tune” of the instrument, $\varphi$ is a possible phase shift in the vibrations of the strings.

Since in our problem $\delta \omega$ is small, vibrations on the service are perceived as a sound of frequency $\omega + \delta \omega / 2 \approx \omega$, but the volume of the sound will change over time with frequency $\delta \omega / 2$, that is, with a period $T = 4\pi / \delta \omega$. It is clear that the smaller $\delta \omega$, the longer the period $T$. Increases in this period must be added by tightening the tuners on the guitar.

Evening physical school at Moscow State University

The Evening Physics School (VPS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces enrollment of students in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of the VFS is to help students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum. Classes at the school are conducted in the form of lectures given every two weeks and weekly seminars. In addition, students will be able to visit the faculty’s scientific laboratories and get acquainted with the main directions of modern physics at lectures by leading scientists. For tenth graders the organization offers elective classes in mathematics. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of the VFS.

Admission to the VFS is based on the results of the owner, which will be held starting from September 24. Working youth are enrolled without competition. To enroll in the school, you must personally fill out an application at the Komsomol Committee of Physics of Moscow State University (with the attachment of two factors measuring $3 \times 4$ cm). Applications can be submitted from September 5 to September 21, every day, except Sunday, from 16 to 18 hours.
