Meeting with a magazine reader

On June 7, a meeting took place between the editorial collection of the magazine «Kvant» and mathematics teachers taking advanced training courses for secondary school teachers at Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov. This meeting, held at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, was attended by the deputy editor-in-chief of the journal, Yu. P. Solovyov, and members of the editorial collection answered numerous questions regarding the essence of the equation.

Emphasizing the great importance of the journal in the development and interest of students in physics and mathematics, many spoke at the meeting of teachers at the same time with critical comments and constructive suggestions aimed at further studying «Kvant». The state of the discussion showed that the magazine still has little room to better and more fully satisfy the needs of readers, to become more accessible and popular among schoolchildren, teachers, students and mathematics enthusiasts. Therefore, the resulting useful conversation between members of the editorial staff and teachers will be taken into account when planning the further work of the magazine.

H. P.
