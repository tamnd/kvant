Our profile

Dear readers! To improve the work of the journal, we would like to know no less about the materials published in «Kvant» in 1985. Please answer the questions in our questionnaire.

1. Place of study (city, village, class, course) or work (profession, specialty) and age.
2. What do you think are the most and least interesting math articles this year?

3. The most and least interesting articles in physics this year.
4. The most successful and unsuccessful, in your opinion, magazine cover this year.
5. What sections of the magazine do you like, and what new sections should be published?
6. What materials this year helped in your studies; What were you looking for in «Kvant» in 1985 but didn't find?
7. What would you like to read about on the pages of our magazine in 1986?

Send your answers on a separate sheet of paper, keep the numbering of questions, until February 20, 1986.
