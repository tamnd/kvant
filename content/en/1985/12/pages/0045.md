Problem 2. The screen $B$ is located in the focal plane of the collecting line (Fig. 4). Light falls on the lines from an extended source $S$, reflected by two edges of a plane-parallel glass plate. Show that the interference patterns on the screen $B$, created by different elements of the source $S$, coincide with each other.

At each point $P$ of the screen there come rays that, before the lens, and therefore before the plate, were parallel to each other, that is, they had a completely definite inclination to the edges of the plate. The distance is one of the rays coming from the source. From the point $A$ of division of the ray into reflected and refracted to the observation point $P$, ray 2 (“refracted”) travels an additional distance $AC+CM$ in the glass, and in the air its path is shorter than ray 1 (“reflected”), by a segment $AD$. (From points $D$ and $M$, both rays pass through the lens to point $P$ in the same time, that is, their optical paths are the same here.) From here it is clear that the difference in the path of parallel rays 1 and 2 interfering at point $P$ does not depend on the position of point $A$ of the parallel ray on the plate.
