Ordered sets of numbers and inequalities
1. The pairs of numbers $(|a|^3, |b|^3)$ and $(|a|, |b|)$ are ordered in the same way. Pozotomu
$$a^4 + b^4 \geq |a|^3 \cdot |a| + |b|^3 \cdot |b| \geq |a|^3 \cdot |b| + |b|^3 \cdot |a| \geq a^3 b + b^3 a.$$
2. The triples of numbers $a^2, b^2, c^2$ and $bc, ac, ab$ are ordered in opposite ways. Pozotomu
$$a^2 bc + ab^2 c + abc^2 \leq a^3 b + b^3 c + c^3 a.$$
3. Considering oppositely ordered triplets
$$(a^2 b^2, b^2 c^2, c^2 a^2); \left( \frac{c}{ab}; \frac{a}{bc}; \frac{b}{ac} \right),$$
we get the following inequalities:
$$\frac{a^3 b}{c} + \frac{b^3 c}{a} + \frac{c^3 a}{b} \geq a^2 b^2 \cdot \frac{c}{ab} + b^2 c^2 \cdot \frac{a}{bc} + c^2 a^2 \cdot \frac{b}{ac} = 3abc,$$
similarly, $\frac{ab^3}{c} + \frac{c^3 b}{a} + \frac{ca^3}{b} \geq 3abc$, adding which, we arrive at the required.
