Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling students from high schools and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1986/87 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students study independently to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting to 3FTSH, preference will be given to students living in rural areas and workers' settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. A circle is accepted into 3FTS if the school director provides the names of the leader and a list of members of the circle (by grade, indicating the final grades for the introductory assignment). All materials on organizing clubs should be sent to 3FTSH before May 25, 1986.

Students of 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical tasks and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH or its filps, and the work of members of the circle is checked by its leader.

Moscow students receive full-time classes in physics and mathematics (according to the 3FTSH program) twice a week at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools). Recruitment to these groups is carried out either based on the results of the 3FTS introductory task, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics (for information by phone 408-51-45).

Each student completes the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently. The work must be done in Russian and carefully copied into one school terdal. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment. Terdal, put a simple parcel in a large envelope (just don’t roll it into a tube). Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the document. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. On the outside of the printed cover, paste a sheet of paper filled in according to the sample:

1. Region (region or ASSR)
2. Last name, first name, patronymic
3. Class
4. School number and address
5. Profession of parent and position held:
   father
   mother
6. Detailed home address
   307239, Kursk region, Kurchatov, st. Kosmonavtov, 10, apt. 76.

To receive an answer to the introductory task, send an envelope with your home address to the terdal.

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 1, 1986 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be processed. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1986.
