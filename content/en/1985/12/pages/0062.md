By turning on the electric motor and starting the stopwatch connected to the computer, a table $\varphi - t - \Delta t$ is obtained on the computer display, where $\varphi$ is the angle of rotation of the disk, $t$ is the time elapsed from the start of measurements, $\Delta t$ is the time of half a revolution of the disk. Using this table, a graph $\varphi(t)$ is constructed during acceleration and measurement of the disk (curves $a$ and $b$ on figure 16).

Then new tables are compiled for plotting $\omega(t)$:

$$\omega(t) = \pi/\Delta t = \pi/(t_2 - t_1),$$

where $t_1$ and $t_2$ are the registration times of two consecutive passages of the near-induction sensor rod. The average angular velocity $\omega$ is calculated for time $t = (t_1 + t_2)/2$. Graphs of the dependence of $\omega(t)$ during acceleration and measurement of the disk are presented on figure 17 (curves $a$ and $b$).

Next, the values ​​of angular acceleration $\beta$ are calculated. The average angular acceleration in the time interval from the $(N-1)$th to the $(N+1)$th registration of the rod is equal to

$$\beta_N = \frac{\omega_N + 1 - \omega_N - 1}{((t_N - t_{N-1}) + (t_{N+1} - t_N))/2},$$

where $\omega_N - 1$ is the angular velocity in the interval from $t_{N-1}$ to $t_N$, and $\omega_N + 1$ is in the interval from $t_{N+1}$. Graphs of dependence $\beta(t)$ for acceleration and measurement are shown on figure 18 (curves $a$ and $b$). The moment of forces during motor acceleration is equal to $M_{\text{ускк}} = I \beta_{\text{ускк}}$, where $I$ is the moment of inertia of the disk. Since $M_{\text{ускк}} = M_{\text{bp}} - M_{\text{тр}} = M_{\text{bp}} - I \beta_{\text{зам}}$, then $M_{\text{bp}} = I(\beta_{\text{ускк}} + \beta_{\text{зам}})$. These expressions allow us to construct graphs $M_{\text{bp}}(\omega), M_{\text{тр}}(\omega)$ and $M_{\text{ускк}}(\omega)$ (curves $a$, $b$, $e$ on figure 19). Graphs of the dependence of motor power on angular speed are constructed based on formula $P_{\text{bp}} = M_{\text{ускк}}(\omega), P_{\text{тр}} = M_{\text{тр}}(\omega), p_{\text{ускк}} = M_{\text{bp}}(\omega)$ (curves $a$, $b$ and $e$ on figure 20).
