Tasks

1. I have a wonderful phone number: the first three digits are the same, the remaining four digits are also the same. Moreover, the sum of all the digits of the number is equal to two numbers, the first digit of which coincides with the first digit of my phone number, and the last one with the last. Find this seven digit number.

3. There are 11 checkers on a round table: 5 black and 6 white. Five guys sitting at the table are playing the following game. First, everyone takes two checkers, then the beginner takes the remaining checker. If he has all three checkers of the same color, then he wins and the game ends, if not, then he keeps two checkers of the same color for himself, and passes the third to his partner on the right. If he has all the checkers of the same color, then he wins, if not, then he acts in the same way as before, etc. Can it be done in such a way that everyone makes at least two moves?

4. Ask a friend to stand with his back to the wall, with his heels against the wall, and then try to reach his toes with his fingers without bending his knees. He probably won't get it. Why?

5. A regular octagon is divided into parts, which are colored as in figure. Prove that the sum of the areas shaded by each color is the same.
