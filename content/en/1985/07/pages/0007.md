So, in the microworld, the state of an electron is determined by setting the probability of its detection at different points in space. In our visual model of the probability measure there will be a density of marks, and one can imagine that these marks form a kind of cloud that determines the way of life of the electron.

How are probability cloud devices? Just as in classical mechanics Newton’s laws determine the movements of bodies, quantum mechanics has its own equation from which one can find the “smear” of an electron in space. Such an equation was invented in 1925 by the original physicist Erwin Schrödinger (note, before the correspondence of uncertainties was discovered, that is, before the reason for the smearing of microparticles became clear - this happens in physics). The Schrödinger equation describes atomic phenomena quantitatively, accurately, and in detail. But it’s impossible to talk about this without complex mathematics.
