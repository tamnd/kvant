⟦folio 30⟧

From old experiences

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. A. VARLAMOV

Under this heading, we publish articles about topics and, at the same time, scales in physics.*) Today, readers are invited to talk about how easy it is to “achieve” electric charges at home, how the Leyden jar was “discovered” and what experiments can be carried out with it.

The makki depicted on the upper figure are not at all the attributes of a priest of some pagan flame. In the past century, with their help, venerable physics professors demonstrated the phenomenon of electrical repulsion in their lectures at universities. To do this, a mask with a hair wig was mounted on a dielectric stand and charged from a power plant. Sometimes one of the students (from among the most inquisitive) was invited to demonstrate. Like the mask, it was installed on an insulating stand and connected to the conductor of the power plant machine. After some time, when a sufficiently large charge had accumulated on him, the hair on his head stood on end, and a faint crackling sound could be heard in the quiet hall - this was electrical charges flowing from the ends of his hair.

There are many different fun experiments with electricity that are quite easy to reproduce on your own. We have already talked about some experiments earlier (see «Kvant», 1983, No. 5, p. 29), and now we would like to introduce a few more.

In Gaston Tisandier’s book “Scientific Entertainment,” published more than a hundred years ago, there is a description of a very easy-to-make power plant machine, with the help of which you can easily “receive” the electrical charges necessary for experiments.

Take a lacquered metal tray 35-40 cm long and place it on the table on several glass glasses (Fig. 1). Cut a sheet of thick wrapping paper to the shape of the tray so that it fits well on the flat part of the tray. Using melted soup, glue two paper strips to the sheet to lift it up.

Bring the prepared sheet of paper to some heat source, for example, to a stove, so that the paper dries well and gets as hot as possible. After this, quickly place it on a wooden table and rub it vigorously with a dry and stiff clothes brush - thanks to the electrification of the surface, the sheet of paper will acquire a certain charge.

Now, holding the ribbons, transfer the sheet of paper to the tray, ground the latter by touching its edge with your finger, and immediately lift the paper. As a result of this, the tray will be charged (and with a charge of the opposite sign to the paper).
