⟦folio 28⟧

A stream of water and... a moving boat

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
L. V. TARASOV,
M. L. TARASOV

Several times already on the pages of “Laboratory «Kvant»” a very interesting and important physical phenomenon has been talked about - the presence of a reaction of an outflowing scab and the absence of a reaction of an inflowing scab of liquid (see «Kvant», 1978, No. 9; 1980, No. 9).
This issue publishes another article on this topic.

Let's start with a story about one forgotten children's toy that was extremely popular in the 30s. It was called “Self-propelled boat”. Now this toy is no longer sold in stores, but it is not difficult to make it yourself at home or in the “Skillful Hands” circle.

The device of the “Self-propelled boat” is shown in figure 1. On the boat 1 there is a sealed rigid flat box 2, which can be heated by a candle 3 (in modern experiments, it is better to use a dry alcohol tablet together). A curved metal tube 4 comes out of the box under the bottom of the boat (the tightness of the connections is ensured by soldering 5). For great effect, one side of the box can be made in the form of a clicking membrane (see "Young Technical", 1983, No. 8, p. 65).
