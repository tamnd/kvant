Tasks

M931 — M935; F943 — F947

M931. A circle is inscribed in triangle ABC and touches sides AB, BC and CA at points C₁, A₁ and B₁ respectively. It is known that the lengths of the segments AA₁, BB₁ and CC₁ are equal. Prove that triangle ABC is regular.

A. N. Dranshinkov

M932. In a square cage on a side of 1 m there is an ancondda 10 m long. Baron Monchhausen claims that at any time he can shoot an ancondda in 6 places at once with one shot. Isn't Baron too old? (Ancondda can be considered an arbitrary broken line of length 10 located inside a 1x1 square.)

S. V. Gashkov

M933. 13 knights from k different clans (1<k<13) are sitting at a round table. Everyone holds a gold or silver cup, and there are exactly k gold cups. King Arthur ordered the knights to simultaneously pass the cups to their neighbors on the right, then do the same again, etc. Prove that at some points there are no knights from the same clan holding golden cups.

A. A. Bolotov

M934.* There are 2n (n≥2) points in space (so that no 4 lie in the same plane) and n²+1 segments are drawn with endpoints at these points. Prove that the drawn segments form a) at least one triangle; 6) at least n triangles.

M935. (The nut problem.) If inside a regular 2n-gon with side a and center O we place an arbitrary regular 2n-gon with side a/2, then it will cover the point O. Prove this statement: a) for n=2, 6)* for n=3, c)* for any natural number n>1.

S. V. Gashkov

F943. A small body falls from a great height to the ground. Assuming that the impact of a body on the ground is abscilitono elastic, determine the acceleration of the body immediately after it bounces off the ground.

A. G. Shikhkerimov
