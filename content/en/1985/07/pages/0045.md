F944. On a rigid body moving translationally along a rough horizontal surface, at the moment of time $t_0=0$, when the speed of the body is equal to $v_0$, the force $F(t)$ begins to act, directed all the time along the vector $\vec{v}_0$ and returning with time. After time $t$ the speed of the body turns out to be equal to $v_t$, and $v_t=5$ m/s if $v_0=1$ m/s, and $v_t=13$ m/s if $v_0=10$ m/s. Determine the dependence $v_t=f(v_0)$ for all possible $v_0$.

R. L. Mkrtchyan, O. M. Khudaverdyan

F945. Determine the ratio $\eta_1/\eta_2$ of the efficiency coefficients of two cyclic processes carried out with an ideal gas (Fig. 1): the first process is 1-2-3-4-1, the second process is 5-6-7-4-5.

F946. Two identical exact charges $q>0$ are located at a distance $l$ from each other. What is the minimum distance to the symmetry plane $\Pi$ (Fig. 2) with the help of a line coming out of the left charge at an angle $\alpha$ to the straight line connecting the charges? At what angle to the plane of symmetry will this line be located when removed to a large distance from the charges?

S. S. Krotov

F947. The shell of the space station is a blackened sphere, the temperature of which, due to the operation of the equipment inside the station, is $T=500$ K. What will the temperature of the shell be if the station is surrounded by a thin black spherical screen of almost the same radius as the shell? The amount of body emitted per unit surface area is proportional to the fourth power of absolute temperature.

A. N. Buzdin
