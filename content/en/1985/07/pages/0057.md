1. In a thermally insulated state there is water, the mass of which is $m_1=1$ kg, and the temperature is $t_1=30^\circ C$. Ice is placed in water at a temperature of $t_2=-20^\circ C$. After some time, the vessel contained water with a temperature of $t_3=20^\circ C$. Find the mass of ice placed in the container. The specific temperature of water is $c_1=4,2$ kJ/(kg K), the specific temperature of ice is $c_2=2,1$ kJ/(kg K), the specific melting temperature of ice is $\lambda=0,33$ MJ/kg. The heat capacity of the vessel can be neglected.

Let us denote the mass of ice by $m_2$. Then the amount of heat received by ice when it is heated to the melting temperature $t_0=0^\circ C$ will be equal to

$$Q_1=c_2m_2(t_0-t_2).$$

Turning into water (at a constant temperature!), this ice will receive the amount of heat

$$Q_2=\lambda m_2.$$

The resulting water, upon subsequent heating to a temperature $t_3$, will receive the amount of heat generated

$$Q_3=c_1m_2(t_3-t_0).$$

The water initially found in the vessel, when cooled to a temperature $t_3$, will give up the amount of heat

$$Q_4=c_1m_1(t_1-t_3).$$

Using the heat balance equation $Q_1+Q_2+Q_3=Q_4$, we find the mass of ice lowered into the vessel:

$$m_2=m_1 \frac{c_1(t_1-t_3)}{c_1(t_3-t_0)+c_2(t_0-t_2)} \approx 0,09 \text{ кг.}$$

Problem 2. Water was introduced into a heat-insulated vessel containing ice weighing $m_1=3$ kg at a temperature $t_1=-10^\circ C$, the mass of which was $m_2=0,5$ kg and temperature $t_2=10^\circ C$. Find the mass of ice in the vessel after thermal equilibrium has been established. Specific temperature of ice $c_1=2,1$ kJ/(kg K), specific temperature of ice $c_2=4,2$ kJ/(kg K), specific melting temperature of ice $\lambda=0,33$ MJ/kg. Ignore the heat capacity of the vessel.

This problem differs from the previous one in that it is not known in advance what final state of aggregation the system will be in, in particular, what its final temperature will be. To answer this question, let's find, first of all, the amount of heat that is required in order for the weight in the vessel.

heat to melting temperature $t_0=0^\circ C$:

$$Q_1=c_1m_1(t_0-t_1)=63^\circ C$$

If all the water introduced into the vessel is cooled to this temperature, an amount of heat will be released

$$Q_2=c_2m_2(t_2-t_0)=21 \text{ кДж}<Q_1.$$

Having turned into ice, all the water can release more heat

$$Q_3=\lambda m_2=165 \text{ кДж}>Q_1.$$

Thus, we find that the final temperature of the system will be equal to $t_0=0^\circ C$, and some of the water will freeze. Its mass $\Delta m$ will be found from the heat balance equation:

$$\Delta m=\frac{Q_1-Q_2}{\lambda} \approx 0,1 \text{ кг.}$$

Therefore, after equilibrium is established, there will be $m'_1=m_1+\Delta m \approx 3,1 \text{ кг льда.}$ in the vessel
