$$\frac{8x^2-2,5}{16x}+\frac{8x}{16x^2-5}=1$$.

In your answer, write down the number of integers that satisfy this inequality.

7. Simplify and calculate without table
$$\sin \frac{\pi}{5}+\sin \frac{13\pi}{15}+\sin \frac{23\pi}{15}+\sin \frac{\pi}{6}$$.
8. Solve the equation
$$1-\sin 3x=\left(\sin \frac{x}{2}-\cos \frac{x}{2}\right)^2$$.
In your answer, write down the number of roots of the equation that belong to the segment [π/2; 2π].
9. In triangle ABC, the median AM is perpendicular to the median BN. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC if length AM is 3 and length BN is 4.
