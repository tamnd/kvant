⟦folio 50⟧

Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$1 + \log_6 (4 \cos^2 x - \cos x - 1) = \log_6 (4 - 7 \cos x).$$
2. Each of the two vessels contains an aqueous solution of acetic acid. The first vessel contains 15% acid, and the second contains 75% water (by volume). How many liters of each solution must be taken in order to mix them to obtain 2 liters of a new solution containing no more than q% acid?
3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\frac{x}{4}} + 2 = c + \sqrt{\frac{x}{4}} - 3.$$
4. The height SO of the regular triangular prim SPQR has length h and makes an angle of magnitude β with the side edge SP. Find the cross-sectional plane adjacent to the plane passing through the edge PR, if it is known that this plane has the smallest possible value.
Option 2
1. Solve the inequality
$$(x^2 + 13,3x + 44,1) \cdot \sqrt{\log_{0,3} |x + 6|} \ge 0.$$
2. Points R and F are on the river that runs into the lake, and points E and Q are on the lake. The steamer covers the routes from points E and Q to Ustyaraki (along the lake) in p hours and in p+5 hours, respectively, and the routes from Ustyaraki to points F and R in 11 hours and 13 hours, respectively. At what speed did the steamboat move across the lake, if the speeds of the steamboat in still water and the river are constant, and the paths from E to F and from Q to R are equal to the corresponding 105 km and 131 km.
3. Find all values ​​of c for which the system
$$\begin{cases} \left(ctg x - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)(x + c) = 0, \\ -4 \leq x - 2 \end{cases}$$
has only one solution.

4. For a right truncated quadrangular prim $PQRTP_1Q_1R_1T_1$, the radius of the circle described around its lower base $PQRT$ is equal to r, and the lateral face makes an angle of magnitude γ with the plane $PQRT$. A circle is inscribed into the square formed by the intersection of this prim with a plane parallel to the plane $PQRT$, which is the base of a right cone with the vertex at the point of intersection of the diagonals of the square $PQRT$, and the secant plane is drawn so that the cone has the largest possible volume. Determine the height of this cone if $|P_1Q_1| = a$.

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. Two bodies A and B with masses $m_1=1,5$ kg and $m_2=0,45$ kg, respectively, are suspended by threads from a light beam whose arms have lengths $l_1=0,6$ m and $l_2=1$ m, with body A lying on the floor (Fig. 1). At what minimum angle α should body B be deflected when suspended, so that after releasing it, body A comes off the floor?
2. A bullet with a mass of $m_1=9$ g, having a speed of $\vec{v}_0 (v_0=160 \text{ m/c})$, directed at an angle α=30° to the horizon (Fig. 2), pierces a dock with a mass of $m_2=0,3$ kg lying on supports, after which it rises to a maximum height of $H=45$ m above the level of the supports. How high will the dock jump? Air compliance is claimed.
3. A ball suspended on a thread was given an initial speed in the horizontal direction. When the thread deviated at an angle α=30° from the vertical, the acceleration of the ball turned out to be directed horizontally. Find the angle of maximum thread deflection.
4. A puck sliding along a smooth floor with a speed $v_0=12 \text{ m/c}$ rises onto a springboard, the upper part of which is horizontal, and shoots off it (Fig. 3). At what springboard height h will the puck's flight range s be maximum? What is this range?
5. A body of mass $m_1=1$ kg lying on a long horizontal board of mass $m_2=2$ kg was given an initial speed of $v_0=2 \text{ m/c}$ (Fig. 4). What path will the body travel relative to the board if the friction coefficient between them is μ=0.2, and there is no friction between the board and the plane?
6. In a closed, evacuated cylinder, a friction-free sliding piston AB is suspended from the outside, the equilibrium position of which is at the bottom of the cylinder (Fig. 5). A certain amount of air is introduced under the piston so that the piston rises to a height of $h_1=10$ cm at an air temperature of $t_1=27^\circ\text{C}$. To what altitude at air temperature $t_1=27^\circ\text{C}$. To what altitude at air temperature $t_1=27^\circ\text{C}$.
