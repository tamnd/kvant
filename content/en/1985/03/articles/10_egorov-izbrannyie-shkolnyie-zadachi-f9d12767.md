⟦folio 22⟧

Since flax is sensitive to red and infrared light, the luminescent anode emits blue, phylote and even ultraviolet rays. In other words, when an invisible infrared image is projected onto the photocathode, a visible image is observed at the anode.

We have outlined here only the principle diagram of the operation of the electron-optical conversion created by Holst and his co-workers in 1933. Their experiments proved the possibility of converting a long-wavelength neid image into a short-wavelength visible image using the photoaction effect.

Over the past fifty years, photoelectric technology has come a long way. Currently, it is used not only in science, but also in scientific research, military affairs, and technology.

Selected School Problems

(Based on Lewis Carroll's book "The Knot Tale")

8th grade

1. Given the lengths $a$ and $b$ of two adjacent sides of a quadrilateral and the acute angle $a$ between them. In addition, it is known that the angles of a quadrilateral adjacent to each of these sides are right. Find: a) the remaining sides of the quadrilateral; b) area of ​​the quadrilateral.

2. Prove that the sum of the squares of two different natural numbers, multiplied by the sum of the squares of two different natural numbers, can be represented as the sum of the squares of two natural numbers.

3. Two travelers leave the hotel to the top of the mountain at 3 pm and return at 9 pm. Their route passes through level ground, either uphill or downhill. On level ground they move at a speed of 4 miles per hour, uphill at 3 miles per hour and downhill at 6 miles per hour. How far do they travel during their walk? Also determine, in a period of up to 30 minutes, the moment of ascent to the top of the mountain, considering that when moving to the top of the mountain, the length of the ascent sections is greater than the length of the descent sections.

4. On the basis of this triangle, find a point such that the straight line connecting the perpendiculars drawn from this point to the sides is parallel to the base.

5. Inside a given angle with vertex $B$ there is given a point $P$. Draw a line through point $P$ that bounces off the corner of the triangle with the smallest area.

9th grade

6. Two trips to Putrovno at 6 am on the same day. Both walk along the same road and in the same direction. Pedestrian $B$ is initially ahead of pedestrian $A$ by 14 miles. Both run from 6 am to 6 pm. On the first day $A$, moving throughout the day at a constant speed, passes 10, on the second - 9, on the third - 8 miles, etc. Pedestrian $B$, also moving at a constant speed throughout the day, passes 2 on the first day, on the second - 4, on the third - 6 miles, etc. Where and when are pedestrians $A$ and $B$ will they talk to each other?

7. Two circles intersect so that their common chord subtends central angles of 30° and 60°. What part of the circle enclosed by the smaller circle is inside the larger circle?

8. In this circle, inscribe a trapezoid with one base twice as large as the other and a maximum area.

9. A glass of lemonade, 3 sandwiches and 7 biscuits costs 1 shilling 2 pence. A glass of lemonade, 4 sandwiches and 10 biscuits costs 1 shilling 5 pence. How much does 2 glasses of lemonade, 3 sandwiches and 5 biscuits cost? (1 shilling equals 12 pence)

10. In this acute triangle, write a triangle whose sides at each of its vertices form angles on the side of the given triangle.

10th grade

11. Let $p$ be the half-perimeter of some triangle, $S$ its area, and $V$ the volume of a right parallelepiped whose edges are equal to the sides of the triangle. Find the sum of the squares of the side of the triangle.

12. Several people are sitting around a round table. Each of those sitting has a certain number of shillings. The first has 1 shilling more than the second, the second has 1 shilling more than the third, etc. The first person sitting gives a shilling to the second, the second two shillings to the third, etc. Each person gives the next one 1 shilling more than he himself paid, as long as this is possible. As a result, one of the people sitting was equilateral. Find the side length of the hexagon if the side lengths of the triangle are $a$, $b$ and $c$.

13. From a given triangle, three triangles are cut off with straight, parallel sides of the triangle so that the remaining hexagon is equilateral. Find the lengths of the sides of the triangle equal to $a$, $b$ and $c$.

14. Three cylindrical towers with base radii 1, 2, 3 are placed on a plane. Find a point on the plane from which their width will seem the same.

15. All faces of the paramid triangles are congruent triangles with sides $a$, $b$, $c$. Find the generalities of this paramida.
