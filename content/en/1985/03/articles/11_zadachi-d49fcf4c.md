⟦folio 23⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Tasks

1. In the numerical formula shown at figure, arrange the + and - signs so that the indicating equalities are satisfied. There is no need to put a sign between some adjacent numbers by circling them into one number.

2. During the debriefing of the scrap metal collection competition, it turned out that 6 "A" collected more scrap metal than 6 "B" and 6 "C" together, but 6 "A" and 6 "B" collected as much scrap metal as 6 "B" and 6 "D", in addition, 6 "C" and 6 "D" collected more scrap metal than 6 "A" and 6 "B". How were the places distributed in the competition of these classes?

3. There were three cards with numbers on the table. First, they added up the largest possible number, then added up the next largest number. Their sum turned out to be 1233. What numbers were on the cards?

4. The sum of 13 different natural numbers is 92. Find these numbers.

5. When I washed the glasses in hot water and set them to dry upside down on a smooth table, they began to crawl along the table. I remembered that when I washed them with cold water, this did not happen to them. Why did the glasses start moving?

These tasks were offered to us:
L. P. Mochalov, A. V. Sidriansky,
N. K. Antonovich, A. P. Savin.
