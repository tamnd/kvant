Tasks

M911—M915; F923—F927

M911. On sides AB and CD of a convex quadrilateral ABCD, arbitrary points E and F are selected, respectively. Prove that the midpoints of the segments AF, BF, CE and DE are the vertices of a quadrilateral, and its area does not depend on the choice of points E and F.

M912. Prove that a) the polynomial $x^2$, b) any polynomial can be represented as the difference of two polynomials, each of which is a monotonically increasing function.

B. P. Pikulin

M913. Tangent circles to the triangle ABC drawn at points A and B intersect at point P. Prove that line PC a) intersects side AB at point K, dividing it in the ratio $AC^2:BC^2$;
b) is symmetric to the median drawn from C relative to the bissertrix of angle C of the triangle.

S. Litovchenko, 10th grade student.

M914. On the island of Seroburomalin there are 13 gray, 15 brown and 17 crimson chameleons. If two chameleons of different colors meet, they simultaneously change their color to a third (gray and brown both become crimson, etc.). Is it possible to obey in such a way that after a while all the chameleons will be the same color?

V. G. Ilchev

M915*. Prove that for any positive numbers a, b, c, d the inequality is true

$$\frac{a}{b+c} + \frac{b}{c+d} + \frac{c}{d+a} + \frac{d}{a+b} \geq 2.$$

L. D. Kurlyandchik

F923. On a smooth horizontal plane lie two disks touching each other, fastened with a non-stretchable thread (Fig. 1). A disk of radius R begins to rotate around a fixed vertical axis passing through its center, and the linear rotation speed increases with time. At some moments, a disk smaller than radius r breaks away from the large disk (stops touching it). At what angle will the large disk rotate by this moment? There is no friction between the discs.

⟦folio 26⟧

Assume that all the mass of the disk is concentrated at its center.

L. G. Markovich

F924. In hot weather, to quickly cool the water, pour it into ice-covered molds taken from the freezer of the refrigerator. The water is “rolled” from one edge of the mold to the other, then poured out. Evaluate whether more or less water becomes during this operation.

L. A. Ashkinazi

F925. Two identical massive pipes roll down slides of the same height, but with different profiles (Fig. 2). Along the first slide, the pipe moves throughout the entire section without slipping; on the second slide there is an absolutely smooth section (section 1-2), but at the end of the path (at the foot of the slide) the pipe moves again without slipping. Which pipe at the end of the slide has the fastest speed?

A. I. Buzdin

F926. In the space there are 1985 non-contacting metal balls, the charges of which are equal, respectively, $q, -2q, 3q, -4q, ..., -1984q, 1985q$ ($q > 0$). Prove that among them there is a ball whose surface charge density is non-negative. The distances between the balls are finite.

V. A. Yasinsky

F927. A charged particle travels through a scinitillator counter, which has the shape of a parallelepiped, and the scinitillator molecules lying along its trajectory emit light. Light at each point is emitted isotropically, that is, evenly in all directions. What fraction of light energy is released from the scinitillator in the air if the refractive index of the scinitillator substance is $n=1,6$? Neglect the absorption of light in the scinitillor.

Note: the solid angle at the vertex of the cone is equal to $\Omega=2\pi(1-\cos \theta)$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the axis of the cone and its generatrix.

S. A. Khoroshov
