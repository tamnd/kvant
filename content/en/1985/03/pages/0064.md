9. Immediately after closing the key, the charge and voltage on the capacitor are zero. Therefore, the current $I_1$ flowing through the source at this moment in time is equal to
$$I_1 = \frac{U_1}{R_1}.$$

Once the capacitor is charged, no current flows through it. Hence,
$$I_2 = \frac{U_2}{R_1 + R_2},$$

where $I_2$ is the current flowing through the source after charging the capacitor. The voltage at the source then $U_1$ and $U_2$ are related to its emf and internal resistance $r$ formula
$$U_1 = \mathcal{E} - I_1 r, U_2 = \mathcal{E} - I_2 r.$$

Eliminating the quantities $r$ from these equations, we obtain
$$\mathcal{E} = \frac{U_1 I_2 - U_2 I_1}{I_2 - I_1} = \frac{U_1 U_2 R_2}{U_1 (R_1 + R_2) - U_2 R_1} = 12 \text{ B}.$$

10. Indeed, the image of the ball will exist when the ball is above the focal plane of the lens. The maximum lifting height of the ball $H$ is
$$H = \frac{v_0^2}{2 g}.$$

The flight time of the ball $t$ above the focal plane can be found from the equation
$$H - F = \frac{g (\tau/2)^2}{2},$$

where $F = 1/D$ is the focal length of the lens. From here
$$\tau = 2 \sqrt{\frac{2(H - F)}{g}} = 2 \frac{v_0}{g} \sqrt{1 - \frac{2 g}{v_0^2 D}} \approx 1,5 \text{ c}.$$
