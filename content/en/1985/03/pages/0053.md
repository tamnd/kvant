⟦folio 51⟧

Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{\sqrt{(x+2)^2}-8x}{\sqrt{x}-\frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}}$$
and calculate its value at $x=1,21$.
2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{8x^2-2,5}{16x}+\frac{8x}{16x^2-5}=1$$.
In your answer, write down its larger root.
3. Find the distance from the origin to the point of intersection of the graph of the function $y=\sqrt{9-x}-\sqrt{4-x}$ with the line $y=1$.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2 \frac{3x+3}{x+6} \leq 1$$.
In your answer, write down the number of integers that satisfy this inequality.
5. Find a two-digit number whose quotient when divided by the product of its digits is $2\frac{2}{3}$, and the difference between the required number and the number written with the same digits, but in reverse order, is 18.
6. Find the difference between the largest and smallest values ​​of the function $f(x)=x^3-6x^2+9x+1$ on the interval [0; 2].
7. Simplify and calculate without table
$$\sin \frac{\pi}{5}+\sin \frac{13\pi}{15}+\sin \frac{23\pi}{15}+\sin \frac{\pi}{6}$$.
8. Solve the equation
$$1-\sin 3x=\left(\sin \frac{x}{2}-\cos \frac{x}{2}\right)^2$$.
In your answer, write down the number of roots of the equation that belong to the segment [π/2; 2π].
9. In triangle ABC, the median AM is perpendicular to the median BN. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC if length AM is 3 and length BN is 4.
10. One of the vertices of the cube and the middle of its edges emerging from the opposite vertex serve as the vertices of the paramid. Find the volume of the paramid if the length of the cube's edge is 6.
Option 2
1. The sum of the terms of an infinite geometric progression is 4, and the sum of the squares of its terms is 48. Find the sum of its first four terms.
2. Solve the equation $f'(x)=0$ if $f(x)=\sqrt{2x-1}-2x$.
3. Solve the equation
$$\log_5(x-2)+\log_5(x^3-2)+\log_5(x-2)=4$$.
4. Solve the inequality
$$(0,5)^{x^2+3x} \geq 0,125 \cdot 2^{-x}$$.
