4. A homogeneous rod $AB$ rests on a rough floor and is kept from falling by a horizontal thread $BC$ (Fig. 5) attached to the vertical wall. The coefficient of friction between the rod and the floor is $\mu=0,5$. At what angle of inclination of the rod to the horizontal will the rod be in equilibrium?

5. A cylinder closed by a piston contains $m=0,2$ kg of carbon dioxide. Determine the work done when the gas is heated isobarically by $\Delta T=88$ K.

6. Determine the pressure of saturated water vapor at a temperature of $t=17$ °C if there is $m=0,476$ kg of water vapor in a room with a volume of $V=50$ m³ at a relative humidity of $\varphi=65\%$ and the indicated temperature.

7. In the circuit isobaric at figure 6, the switch $K_1$ is closed and the capacitor is charged. Then key $K_1$ opens and key $K_2$ closes. Find the emf of the batteries if the direction on the first capacitor on the left after closing the switch $K_2$ becomes equal to $U=0,5$ V. The capacity of all capacitors is the same.

8. A mathematical matrix is ​​a ball with a mass of $m=1$ g, suspended on threads of length $l=36$ cm. What will be the period of the flask matrix if, having given the ball a positive charge of $q=6,7 \cdot 10^{-9}$ C, the matrix is ​​placed in a uniform electric field in the direction of $E(E=3 \cdot 10^5$ V/m), the solo lines of which are directed vertically downwards?

9. A square frame with side $a=50$ cm is placed in a uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the magnetic field lines. Determine the magnetic field induction if it is known that when the magnetic field disappears within $\tau=0,01$ s, the average value of the induction emf arising in the frame is $\delta=50$ mV.

10. A beam of parallel rays of light $b=20$ cm wide emerges from a glass plate in the air through the flat boundary of the plate. Determine the width of the beam in air if the angle of incidence of the beam on the glass-air interface is $a=30^\circ$, and the refractive index of the stack is $n=1,8$.
