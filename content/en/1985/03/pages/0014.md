And the real name of this ruler is entropy.

4. Order and disorder in alloys

Let's summarize some results. We know that the energy factor tends to order the arrangement of atoms in the nodes of the crystal lattice. This leads either to the formation of an ordered structure or to the decomposition of the alloy into pure elements. On the other hand, the entropy factor tends to disorder the system, to create some kind of chaotic arrangement of atoms.
