Assume that all the mass of the disk is concentrated at its center.

L. G. Markovich

F924. In hot weather, to quickly cool the water, pour it into ice-covered molds taken from the freezer of the refrigerator. The water is “rolled” from one edge of the mold to the other, then poured out. Evaluate whether more or less water becomes during this operation.

L. A. Ashkinazi

F925. Two identical massive pipes roll down slides of the same height, but with different profiles (Fig. 2). Along the first slide, the pipe moves throughout the entire section without slipping; on the second slide there is an absolutely smooth section (section 1-2), but at the end of the path (at the foot of the slide) the pipe moves again without slipping. Which pipe at the end of the slide has the fastest speed?

A. I. Buzdin

F926. In the space there are 1985 non-contacting metal balls, the charges of which are equal, respectively, $q, -2q, 3q, -4q, ..., -1984q, 1985q$ ($q > 0$). Prove that among them there is a ball whose surface charge density is non-negative. The distances between the balls are finite.

V. A. Yasinsky

F927. A charged particle travels through a scinitillator counter, which has the shape of a parallelepiped, and the scinitillator molecules lying along its trajectory emit light. Light at each point is emitted isotropically, that is, evenly in all directions. What fraction of light energy is released from the scinitillator in the air if the refractive index of the scinitillator substance is $n=1,6$? Neglect the absorption of light in the scinitillor.

Note: the solid angle at the vertex of the cone is equal to $\Omega=2\pi(1-\cos \theta)$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the axis of the cone and its generatrix.

S. A. Khoroshov
