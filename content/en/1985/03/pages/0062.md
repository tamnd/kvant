1. Let us write down the equilibrium condition of the feed at the moment of separation of body A (see Fig. 1 in the statue):
$$T_2 l_2 - T_1 l_1 = 0,$$
where $T_1=m_1 g$ is the tension force of the left thread, $T_2$ is the tension force of the right thread.
The tension force $T_2$ will be maximum at the moment when the right thread takes a vertical position. Let us write down for this moment Newton’s second law and the law of conservation of mechanical energy:
$$T_2 - m_2 g = \frac{m_2 v^2}{l},$$
$$\frac{m_2 v^2}{2} = m_2 g l (1-\cos a),$$
where $l$ is the length of the thread, $a$ is the desired angle of its deflection. From the last two equations α is found:
$$\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2} \left( 3 - \frac{m_1 l_1}{m_2 l_2} \right) = \frac{1}{2}, \alpha = 60^\circ.$$

Let us denote by $v_{1y}$ and $v_{2y}$ the projections of the velocities of the bullet and the board on the vertical axis after the bullet leaves the board and write down the law of conservation of momentum in the projection onto this axis, as well as the law of conservation of energy for the bullet and the board:
