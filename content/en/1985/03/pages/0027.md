⟦folio 25⟧

Tasks

M911—M915; F923—F927

M911. On sides AB and CD of a convex quadrilateral ABCD, arbitrary points E and F are selected, respectively. Prove that the midpoints of the segments AF, BF, CE and DE are the vertices of a quadrilateral, and its area does not depend on the choice of points E and F.

M912. Prove that a) the polynomial $x^2$, b) any polynomial can be represented as the difference of two polynomials, each of which is a monotonically increasing function.

B. P. Pikulin

M913. Tangent circles to the triangle ABC drawn at points A and B intersect at point P. Prove that line PC a) intersects side AB at point K, dividing it in the ratio $AC^2:BC^2$;
b) is symmetric to the median drawn from C relative to the bissertrix of angle C of the triangle.

S. Litovchenko, 10th grade student.

M914. On the island of Seroburomalin there are 13 gray, 15 brown and 17 crimson chameleons. If two chameleons of different colors meet, they simultaneously change their color to a third (gray and brown both become crimson, etc.). Is it possible to obey in such a way that after a while all the chameleons will be the same color?

V. G. Ilchev

M915*. Prove that for any positive numbers a, b, c, d the inequality is true

$$\frac{a}{b+c} + \frac{b}{c+d} + \frac{c}{d+a} + \frac{d}{a+b} \geq 2.$$

L. D. Kurlyandchik

F923. On a smooth horizontal plane lie two disks touching each other, fastened with a non-stretchable thread (Fig. 1). A disk of radius R begins to rotate around a fixed vertical axis passing through its center, and the linear rotation speed increases with time. At some moments, a disk smaller than radius r breaks away from the large disk (stops touching it). At what angle will the large disk rotate by this moment? There is no friction between the discs.
