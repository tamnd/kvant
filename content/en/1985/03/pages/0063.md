3. When the thread is deflected by an angle $a$, the acceleration components directed along the thread and tangential to the trajectory of the ball are determined by the formulas

$$a_H = \frac{v^2}{l}, a_K = g \sin a,$$

where $v$ is the speed of the ball, $l$ is the length of the thread. Since the acceleration of the ball at this moment is directed horizontally, the process of vectors $\vec{a}_H$ and $\vec{a}_K$ on the vertical axis are identical in magnitude:

$$a_H \cos a = a_K \sin a,$$

or

$$\frac{v^2}{l} \cos a = g \sin^2 a,$$

where

$$v^2 = g l \frac{\sin^2 a}{\cos a}.$$

Next, we write the law of conservation of energy for the ball:

$$\frac{m v^2}{2} + m g l (1 - \cos a) = m g l (1 - \cos a_m).$$

Solving this equation for cos $a_m$, we obtain

$$\cos a_m = \cos a - \frac{v^2}{2 g l} = \cos a - \frac{\sin^2 a}{2 \cos a} = 0,73,$$

$$a_m = 43^\circ.$$

4. The speed of the puck at the top of the springboard $v$ can be found using the law of conservation of energy

$$\frac{m v^2}{2} = \frac{m v^2}{2} + m g h.$$

The height of the springboard and the flight distance of the puck are related to the flight time $t$ by the formulas

$$h = \frac{g t^2}{2}, s = v t.$$

From the equalities written above, we obtain the dependence of the puck’s flight range on the height of the springboard:

$$s = \sqrt{\frac{2 h}{g} \left(v_0^2 - 2 g h\right)} = 2 \sqrt{\left(\frac{v_0^2}{4 g}\right)^2 - \left(h - \frac{v_0^2}{4 g}\right)^2}.$$

Obviously, the flight range will be maximum provided

$$h = \frac{v_0^2}{4 g} = 3,6 \text{ м},$$

and the maximum range itself

$$s = \frac{v_0^2}{2 g} = 7,2 \text{ м}.$$

5. For the body-board system, the law of conservation of momentum is satisfied

$$m_1 v_0 = (m_1 + m_2) v,$$

where $v$ is the speed of the system after the body has stopped sliding relative to the board. The change in the kinetic energy of the body is due to the work of the friction force $F_{\tau p1}$ acting on the body from the side of the board:

$$\frac{m_1 v^2}{2} - \frac{m_1 v_0^2}{2} = -F_{\tau p1} l_1,$$

where $l_1$ is the path traveled by the body relative to the board. On the other hand, the kinetic energy of the board increases due to the work of the friction force $F_{\tau p2}$ acting on the board from the side of the board:

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} l (2 a - 5 g)} \approx 0,66 \text{ м/с}.$$
