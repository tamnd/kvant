Since 1982, an experiment has been conducted at the Moscow Energy Institute to conduct an oral exam in mathematics using a computer. In 1984, such an exam was held at one of the faculties. We conduct one version of the exam task, consisting of 20 examples and a task. The applicant writes down the answer on a special form. The computer checks the correctness of the answer.
