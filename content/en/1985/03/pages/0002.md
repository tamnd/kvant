Shown here is a surface model
from a unique collection stored
at the Moscow State University
tee. The collection models were
made in Germany
at the beginning of the century under the leadership
great German
mathematician Felix Klein.
The depicted surface is
it's geometric
image of one of the integrals of the form
$$\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^3+ax+b}}$$
in the complex area.
Correspondence to the surface of the primitives -
so-called
elliptic functions - have
numerous applications
to geometry, number theory,
analysis and mechanics.
