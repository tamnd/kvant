⟦folio 62⟧

The listed reports were completed by students of the Physics School at Moscow State University under the guidance of school teachers and staff of the Physics Faculty of Moscow State University.

The report of Oleg Kozvsky (school No. 444) “Barotropic model of the atmosphere” was very attractive. In this work, the issue of the formation of atmospheric vortices - cycles in the Earth's atmosphere, taking into account its daily rotation, was theoretically considered. The author demonstrated a well-expressed hydrodynamics apparatus, and numerical estimates characterizing the development of a cyclone were carried out on a computer. The results obtained, in general, correctly describe the main features of the development of the cyclone. We believe that this work is of scientific interest. The review report by S. Frolov (school No. 444) “Renewable Energy Sources” was also very interesting.

The length of the note does not allow us to present the topics of all reports here. Let us just note that they were all interesting. In general, the conference was dominated by two creative and scientific passions.

Employees of the Institute of Problems of Mathematics of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Moscow State University, members of the editorial board and members of the editorial staff of the journal «Kvant» took part in its work.
In the future, this conference will become traditional, and we hope to expand the composition and geography of its participants.

Teachers of Physics and Mathematics School at Moscow State University
A. V. Belov, A. A. Egorov
