Luminescence has found its practical application, primarily in fluorescent lamps (fluorescent lamps). In such lamps containing mercury vapor, a gas discharge is excited, which is accompanied by a glow containing mainly ultraviolet rays. The walls of the lamps, coated with phosphors of a special composition, absorb ultraviolet light and convert it into visible light.

Such lamps are much more economical than incandescent lamps. In addition, the spectral composition of their light is close to that of daylight and is therefore more pleasing to the eye.
