⟦folio 2⟧

Happy new school year!

The editorial board and editors of the magazine «Kvant» congratulate our young readers on the new school year! And this year in many ways should be truly new, unlearning.

The implementation of school reform continues, and the process of intensifying the national economy is gaining momentum. In accordance with the resolution of the party and government, the course “Fundamentals of Informatics and Computer Science” is being introduced in all secondary educational institutions of the country. A wide experiment has begun on the use of computer technology in school enterprises. Instead, better and deeper mastery of computer technology by young people should become an important factor in strengthening scientific and technological progress in the country.

I would like you to see behind the new line in the school schedule what great importance the present day attaches to this measure, which raises the work of the school to a new level of quality, corresponding to the conditions and needs of society and the needs of society.

A little time will pass, and you will join the ranks of a multi-million army of workers and engineers, scientists and technicians, teachers and designers, machine operators and office workers. You will find yourself in factories where numerically controlled machines operate, in laboratories where hundreds of electronic devices and instruments operate, in classrooms where teaching is carried out using modern electronic computers. Without exaggeration, we can say that the world in which you will live and work will be a computerized world, full of the latest technology. This is not science fiction, or rather, yesterday’s fiction, taking on real features at an unprecedented pace before our eyes.

In order for this, sometimes only guessed today, to be technically within your control, you need to prepare for meeting it in advance, already in school. That is why the task of equipping students with the knowledge and skills to use modern computer technology becomes so important.

Our magazine is also intended to help solve this problem. As always, he will strive to awaken interest in physics and mathematics - the sciences underlying modern technology, and introduce you to the achievements of physical and mathematical sciences and their application in the national economy.

And along with this, «Kvant» becomes meaningful for the successful delivery of a new educational course, talking about the development, current state and prospects of computer technology, how it is used in various fields, and the problems of mathematics and physics associated with it. Stats and notes on these topics will appear regulated on the pages of our magazine.

We hope that some, as the programs say, “a dose of algorithmicity” turns out to be useful for our readers. You are responsible for editing responses to these publications and suggesting new topics.

We wish you success in the new academic year!
