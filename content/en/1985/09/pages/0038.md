Tasks

M941—M945; F953—F957

M941. Given a regular (4k+2)-gon $A_0A_1$ ... $A_{4k+1}$ with center $O$. Prove that the sum of the segments cut by the angle $A_kOA_{k+1}$ on the lines $A_1A_2k$, $A_2A_2k-1$, ..., $A_kA_{k+1}$ (see figure 1 for $k=2$) is equal to the radius $OA_0$ of the circumcircle of a (4k+2)-gon, if $k=2$, 6) $k$ — any natural number.

I. F. Sharyzin

M942. The numbers 1, 2, ..., 2n—1, 2n are divided into two groups of $n$ numbers in each. Let $a_1<a_2<...<a_n$ be the numbers of the first group in ascending order, $b_1>b_2>...>b_n$ be the numbers of the second group in descending order. Prove that $|a_1-b_1|+|a_2-b_2|+...+|a_n-b_n|=n^2$.

V. V. Proizvolov

M943. The sequence $a_1, a_2, ...$ is given by the rule: $a_{2n}=a_n$ for $n \geqslant 1$ and $a_{4n+1}=1$, $a_{4n+3}=0$ for $n \geqslant 0$. Prove that this sequence has no period.

Yu. V. Nesterenko

M944*. A regular hexagon is divided into 24 equal triangles, as in figure 2. All 19 nodes of the resulting figure contain different numbers. Prove that among 24 triangles the partition has at least 7 such, at the vertices of which the number points are written in ascending counterclockwise order.

A. A. Berzinysh

M945*. Given a strictly recurrent unbounded sequence of positive numbers $a_1, a_2, ...$. Prove that for all sufficiently large $k$ the following inequality holds:

a) $\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\frac{a_2}{a_3}+\...+\frac{a_k}{a_{k+1}}<k-1$;

b) $\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\frac{a_2}{a_3}+\...+\frac{a_k}{a_{k+1}}<k-1985$.

L. D. Kurlyandchik

F953. To fine-tune the rotation frequency of the electric grinder disk (33$\frac{1}{3}$ revolutions per minute), marks are placed on its side surface, which are illuminated with a neon lamp at a frequency of 100 flashes per second.
