These "bugs" with many laps are called integrated circuits. Inside each of them is a tiny semiconductor crystal containing tens or hundreds of thousands of electronic “parts.” With the help of such circuits, electronic computers with powerful memory and record speed are created. New technologies will make it possible in the near future to create microminator computing devices on a single chip that are not inferior in their capabilities to a large modern computer.

You can read about what the “kickboxes” of these most complex devices are in the notes of the new section “Semiconductor elements of computer technology.”
