⟦folio 29⟧

Tasks

1. Petya was visiting his grandmother. On Saturday he boarded the train and arrived home on Monday. Petya noticed that on this Monday the number coincided with the number of the carriage in which he was traveling, that the number of his seat in the carriage was less than the number of the carriage, and that on that Saturday, when he boarded the train, the number of the day was greater than the number of the carriage. In what car and in what place did he travel?

2. Which part of the square's area is larger (see figure): shaded red or shaded blue?

3. Icicles on the roofs of houses, as a rule, rise vertically, and on tree branches they often form such “fans” as on figure. Why?

4. Arrange more than 11 natural numbers in the circles of the star so that the sum of the four numbers at the vertices of each heel of the sectors is equal to 25.

5. Prove that out of 18 consecutive three-digit numbers at least one is divisible by the sum of its digits.

These tasks were offered to us
S. V. Dvoryaninov, V. V. Proizvolov,
D. A. Rakcheev, N. I. Abilov, S. L. Eliseev.
