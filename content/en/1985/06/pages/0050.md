We offer a set of entrance exams in mathematics and physics for some universities - Belarus (1), Voronezh (2), Donetsk (3), Kiev (4), Omsk (5), Tashkent (6), Ural (7) and institutes - Gorky Polytechnic (8), Leningrad Technical (9), Riga Polytechnic (10), Tomsk Polytechnic (11), Tbilisi Pedagogical (12), PM Moscow Medical (13), Minsk Radio Engineering (14).

Mathematics
Written exam
Algebra

1. (5) A natural number is given. If you add the number 2 to its digital notation on the right, you get a number that is divisible by 9 without a remainder. Moreover, the quotient of the division is greater than the given number by 21. Find this number.

2. (5) Two combines are harvesting a grain field. After the first one worked on the field for 2 hours, and the second one for 5 hours, it turned out that half of the field had been removed. Then they worked together for 1.5 hours and cleared another quarter of the field. Only the first combine did the rest of the work. Determine how much time he had for this.

3. (2) Two automations, having pressed cities A and B to point at each other, each at its own speed, met after 6 hours. The first car takes 2 hours more to travel 2/5 of the way from A to B than the second car to travel 2/15 of the way from B to A. How long does it take each car to cover the distance between A and B?

4. (2) The number of tens of a two-digit number by 4 greater numbers of its units; the product of this number and the sum of its digits is 496. Find this number.

5. (10) The sum of the first three terms of an arithmetic progression is 66, and the product of the second and third terms is 528. Find the sum of the first forty terms.

6. (3) Two bodies, located at a distance of 310 m from one another, simultaneously begin to move towards each other. The first body moves uniformly with a speed of 1 m/s, and the second body moves uniformly with an initial speed of 1 m/s and an acceleration of 3 m/s². How soon will they meet?

$$\frac{1}{5} : \left(\frac{17}{40} + 0,6 - 0,005\right) \cdot 1,7$$

8. Simplify the expression:

6) (10)
$$\frac{(ab^{-1}+a^{-1}b+1)(a^{-1}-b^{-1})^2}{a^2b^{-2}+a^{-2}b^2-(ab^{-1}+a^{-1}b)}$$

$$\times \frac{x^2}{y^2} \cdot y^2 - \frac{2y}{x-y}.$$

13. (6) What is the expression $\sin a + \cos \frac{a}{2}$ equal to if $\cos a = -\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{\pi}{2} < a < \frac{3\pi}{2}$.
