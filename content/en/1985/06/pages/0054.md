Objectives of oral and written exams Mechanics

1. (11) The speed of the electric train increased from $v_1=21,6$ km/h to $v_2=108$ km/h on a track of $l=54$ m. Determine the acceleration of the train. Present the result in SI units.

2. (10) What distance does a freely falling body travel during the third second of movement ($g=10$ m/s$^2$)?

3. (10) At what initial speed must a body of mass $m=2$ kg be thrown vertically downward so that after $t=1$ s its kinetic energy is equal to $E_k=2,5$ kJ ($g=10$ m/s$^2$)?

4. (10) Drops of water fall from the roof of a building $h=16$ m high at regular intervals, with the first hitting the ground at the moment when the heel is separated from the roof. Determine: a) time of drop falling; b) the distance between the triangle and the fourth drop in the air when the first drop hits the ground.

5. (1) A ball is thrown vertically upward from the surface of the earth. Some point $A$ the ball passes twice: after time $t_1$ and time $t_2$ after the start of movement. Determine the height of point $A$ by the surface of the earth. Air compliance is not taken into account.

6. (5) From a helicopter located at a height of $h=500$ m, a stone was dropped. How long will it take for the stone to reach the surface of the earth if: a) the helicopter is not moving; b) the helicopter rises at a speed of $v_0=4,9$ m/s? Air compliance is not taken into account.

10. (9) A length of tube with a diameter of $d$ is lowered into a tank with liquid, to which a cylindrical disk of thickness $h$ and diameter $D$ fits tightly at the back. What fraction of the volume of the cube will be in the upper liquid?
