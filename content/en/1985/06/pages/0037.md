F938. The pilot of a spacecraft moving at a speed of $v=1$ km/s was then directly on the course of an astroid with a diameter of $d=7$ km, when a distance of $l=8,5$ km remained to its surface. The cosmonaut immediately turned on the emergency thrusters, which in a negligible time impart to the ship an additional speed of $\Delta v=300$ m/s, the direction of which is set by the astronaut. Can a ship avoid a collision?

A. V. Andranov

F939. The bottom of the vessel is inclined at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the horizontal. The bottom has a hemispherical convexity of radius $R$ (Fig. 1). The height of so much liquid above the bulge is $H$. What vertical force is exerted by the fluid on the convex part of the day? The density of the liquid is $q$.

L. G. Markovich

F940. Why is clay mortar used in brick kilns to hold bricks together, and not, for example, cement mortar (although it is harder)?

V. A. Ilyin

F941. There is a square metal frame $ABCD$ (Fig. 2) in a magnetic field of direct rectilinear current. The frame is moved to a new position, shown in figure by the dotted line. This can be done in two ways: an even repeat around the $CD$ side, or an even parallel carry along the $AD$ side. Which method will produce more tell if the time it takes to move the frame to a new position is the same in both cases?

L. E. Shmits

F942. A plane light wave is incident normally on screen $\partial$. How will the illumination of the screen at point $M$ (Fig. 3) change if a semi-infinite opaque plate $\Pi$, parallel to the screen, is placed on the path of the wave?

D. A. Kuptsov

M926. Prove that
$$x^2 + y^2 = u^2 + v^2 = 1, \quad xu + yv = 0$$
implies
$$x^2 + u^2 = y^2 + v^2 = 1, \quad xy + uv = 0.$$

S. V. Duzhin

M927*. In the plane a finite set of points, no three of which are collinear, is chosen. Some line segments joining pairs of these points are drawn. We are allowed to change the segments in the following way: if, say, $AC$ intersects BD, we can delete both, replacing them by
a) the segments $AB$ and $CD$,
b) the segments $AB$ and $BC$.
