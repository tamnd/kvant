«Kvant» for younger students
(see «Kvant» No. 5)

1. Since 10a+b is divisible by a, it follows that b is divisible by a. And from the divisibility of this number by b it follows that either a is divided by b, or 2a is divided by b, or 5a is divided by b. In the first case a=b, which gives the numbers 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, 66, 77, 88, 99, in the second the numbers 12, 24, 36 and 48, and in the third the number 15.

2. Yura is a Muscovite, Tolya is a Gorkovite, Alyosha is a Sverlovite, Kolya is a Permyak, Vitya is a Leningrader.

3. Let the first two digits form a two-digit number x, and the second - a two-digit number y, then by condition 100x+y=x²+2x√y+y. Hence x=100-2√y. The four-digit number 100x+y is smaller the smaller x is, and from the expression x through y it follows that x is minimal when y=81 is the maximum two-digit number that is a perfect square. Hence the required number is 8281.

4. If the entire bottle were heated evenly, then all its parts would expand evenly. But glass is a flat conductor of the body. Therefore, the cork will heat up less than hot, therefore, it will expand less, resulting in a gap between it and the hot bottle.

5. a) 264:12=22 or 264:22=12; 6) 390:13=30; c) 288=2·12²; d) 10·1-9=1; e) 100·1-99=1; e) 99+99=198; g) 11·91=1001; h) 11·1=9+2.
