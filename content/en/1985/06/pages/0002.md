The purple cribs shown in figures a and b are called epicyclopeds - they calculate the point of a moving circle as that circle rolls along the inside of a stationary circle. These curves, as well as the elongated and shortened epicyclopede, hypocyclopede and elongated hypocyclopede (figure b, 2, e and f, respectively) are described in the article by S. G. Gindikin in this issue of the journal.
