Fig. 12.

7. (10) The focal length of the lens of the projection device is $F=0,25$ m. What increase in range does the projection device give if the screen is removed from the lens at a distance of $f=4$ m?

8. (9) The point moves at a speed of $v_1=1$ m/s perpendicular to the main optical axis of a collecting line with a focal length of $F=20$ cm, crossing the axis at a distance of $d=60$ cm from the line. At what speed does the image of the point move?

9. (1) An object in the form of a rod is located along the main optical axis of a thin collecting line so that its ends are removed from the line at distances $d_1=3/2$ F and $d_2=5/4$ F, where F is the unknown focal length of the line. How many times is the length of the image greater than the length of the object itself?

10. (1) An accurate light source is located on the main optical axis at a distance of $d=60$ cm from the collecting line, the focal length of which is $F=40$ cm. The lens was cut into two halves along its diameter and symmetrically separated by a distance of $r=1$ cm in the direction perpendicular to the main optical axis.
