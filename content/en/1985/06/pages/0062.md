2. a) 69999. Solution. Let the number $a$, which has a sum of digits $k$, turn out to be exactly $n$ nines ($n=0, 1, 2, ...$). Then the sum of the digits of the number $a+1$ is equal to $k+1-9n$.
Therefore, $9n-1$ must be divisible by 7. The smallest such $n$ is equal to 4. Next, we find the answer by selection. 6) Among 13 consecutive natural numbers $a$ at least 7 belong to the same ten, the sum of their digits is seven consecutive natural numbers, therefore one of them is divisible by 7. The number 13 in the conditions of the problem cannot be changed to a smaller one: among the 12 numbers 994, 995, ..., 1005 there is not a single one with a sum of digits divisible by 7.

3. S/30. Solution. Let's carry out $MN \parallel AC$ (Fig. 9).
Then $MN=\frac{1}{2}LC$. On the other hand, triangles $AKL$ and $MKN$ are similar, hence $AL/MN=AK/MK=1/2$. Therefore, $AL=MN/2=CL/4$, that is, $AL=AC/5$. Next, $S_{AMC}=\frac{1}{2}S$, $S_{AKC}=S_{AMC}/3=S/6$, and finally $S_{AKL}=S_{AKC}/5=S/30$.

4. $x=y=z=1$ or $x=y=z=-1$. Solution. Subtracting the second from the first equation, we get
$$x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-xz=0,$$
whence $(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2=0$, but this means that $x=y=z$.

5. Let the required triangle $ABC$ be constructed, $A'$ and $A''$ are points symmetrical to the points $A$ relative to the side of the angle (Fig. 10). Since $A'B=AB$, $A''C=AC$, the permeter of triangle $ABC$ is equal to the length of the broken line $A'BCA''$. The positional permeter will be the smallest in the case when $B$ and $C$ are the points of intersection of $A'A''$ from the side of the corner (Fig. 11).

6. Let's factor $x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz$ into factors, one of which turns out to be $x+y+z$. We have: $x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz=(x+y)^3+z^3-3xy(x+y+z)=(x+y+z)((x+y)^2-(x+y)z+z^2)+(x+y)^2-3xy)=(x+y+z)(x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-xz)$.

7. $2\sqrt{m_1^2+m_2^2}/3$. Solution. The area of ​​the quadrilateral $ABMN$ (Fig. 12) is equal to $\frac{1}{2}m_1m_2\sin \alpha$, where $\alpha$ is the angle between the diagonals $AM$ and $BN$ of this quadrilateral. On the other hand, $S_{ABMN}=S_{ABC}-S_{NMC}=\frac{3}{4}S_{ABC}$, therefore $S_{ABC}=4S_{ABMN}/3=2m_1m_2\sin \alpha/3$.

It follows from the condition that $\sin \alpha=1$. Therefore, medians $AM$ and $BN$ are perpendicular. It remains to apply the Pythagorean theorem to the triangle.

8. $2^{2800}>3^{3500}$. Solution. Since it is enough to prove that $2^{799}>3^{500}$, that is, the inequality $2^{800}/3^{500}>2$. We have: $2^{800}/3^{500}=(256/243)^{100}=(1+13/243)^{100}$. Let us use the inequality theorem $(1+\alpha)^2\geq1+2\alpha$, which is true for any $\alpha$. We get $(1+13/243)^{100} \geq(1+26/243)^{50} \geq(1+52/243)^{25} >(1+52/243)^{24} \geq(1+104/243)^{12} \geq(1+208/243)^{6} \geq(1+416/243)^{3} >2^3>2$.
