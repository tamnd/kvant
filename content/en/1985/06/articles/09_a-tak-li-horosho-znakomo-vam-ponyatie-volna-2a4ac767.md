WAVE
THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST REMARKABLE CONCEPTS IN PHYSICS, FOUND IN A VARIETY OF MANIFESTATIONS IN PRACTICALLY ALL ITS FIELDS. WAVES

spreads across the surfaces of the oceans and in their thickness, in the interstellar void and in crystal lattices, along the passages of power lines, allowing us to reach a variety of colors and common sounds. There are sand waves and sset waves, earthquakes and ocean tsunami - also wave movements, only on a gigantic scale. There are waves that have not yet become familiar to physicists, for example, waves in transport streams, in chemical reactions, in the heart and nervous system, in social biological organisms, in star systems - the galaxy. According to the figurative expression of scientists, the waves “scattered” from physics and covered almost all the sets of processes in living and inanimate nature. And the most interesting thing is that all these waves are mathematically similar, that is, they can be described by the same equations. That’s why it’s so important to “make friends” with this concept, because you, too, may have to come across it more than once in the most unexpected way.
