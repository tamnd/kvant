Example. Construct a graph of the function $y=x^2-|x|$.

Solution. At $x \geq 0$, the graph of this function coincides with the graph of the function $y=x^2-x$ (Fig. 1, ​​$\delta$), but the function $y=x^2-|x|$ is even, therefore, its graph is symmetrical about the axis $Oy$ and looks as shown in figure 1, $\delta$.
