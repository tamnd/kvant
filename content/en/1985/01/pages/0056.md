Introductory test assignment for the Maltese Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics in 1985
1. Expand the polynomial $x^4+2x^2+9$ into the product of two square trinomials.
2. Point $D$ is inside triangle ABC. Prove that $AD+BD$ is less than $AC+BC$.
3. From the vertex of a right angle of a triangle, the median, bissertis and altitude are drawn. Prove that the angle between the median and the Bisserti is equal to the angle between the Bisserti and the altitude.
4. 6 schoolchildren met. Prove that among them there are either three, each of whom is familiar with the other two, or three, each of whom is not familiar with the other two (it is believed that for two schoolchildren there are only two possibilities: either they are familiar or not familiar with each other).
5. Find all pairs of numbers $x, y$ satisfying the equality
$$x^2+y^2+1=xy+x+y.$$
6. How many numbers that are not divisible by either 3 or 7 are there among the first million natural numbers?

Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MISiS

Filpal All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School - Correspondence Physics and Technology School at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys of the Order of the October Revolution and the Order of the Red Banner of Labor - announces enrollment of students in Vosmysh and ninth grades for the 1985/86 academic year.

The goal of the school is to assist students in in-depth study of the secondary school curriculum in physics and mathematics.

The mathematics curriculum is fully consistent with the VZMS course. The physics course was developed by teachers of the Department of General Physics and the Department of Theoretical Physics of the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys and meets the requirements for entrance exams of universities, primarily at MISiS.

The work of schools is organizational as follows. Four to five times a year, students are reviewed test assignments in physics and mathematics, along with brief information on the theory and examples of problem solving. Test papers sent to students are checked and sent to students along with grades and comments. As an annual assignment, tenth grade students are sent materials from the entrance exams to MISiS for past years.

Classes at 3FTSH (MISIS) will begin on October 1, 1985. To enroll in a school, you must send an application indicating the last name, first name, patronymic, full home address, profession and position of the parents, and also attach a certificate from the school indicating the class and annual grades in physics and mathematics. The application and certificate, together with the solution to the first assignment in mathematics, which is written about in the article “New admission to the All-Union Correspondence School of Mathematics” in this issue of the course, must be sent no later than May 5 to the address: 117049 Moscow, Leninsky Ave. 4, MISiS, 3FTSH.
