⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. The girl replaced each letter in her name with its number in the Russian alphabet and got the number 2011533. What was her name?

2. Three school friends bought 14 priozhki, and Kolya bought 2 times less than Vitya, and Zhenya bought more than Kolya, but less than Vitya. How many priozhki did each of the comrades buy?

3. One day I was laying dominoes on the table, trying to put together some interesting figure. After I got the figure depicted on figure, I still had a hollow in my hands. Then I noticed that in each horizontal row and each vertical row the sum of points is the same. I rewrote the picture, putting symbols along with the glasses. Recently I found this picture among the papers, but I forgot that I recognized each sign. Help me restore their meanings.

4. Find all numbers that are 13 times the sum of their digits.

5. It is known that the killing moon is turned with a bulge to the left, like the letter C - the first letter of the word “old”. The nailing month is turned with a convexity to the right, like the rounding of the letter P - the first letter of the word “growing”. Is this rule true everywhere?

These tasks were offered to us by: V. Kudryashov - 10th grade student, Penza, V. Valko - 7th grade student, Moscow, M.V. Varga, N.K. Antonovich, A.L. Toom.
