Physics
Oral exam assignment

1. Two blocks with masses $m_1=0,4$ kg and $m_2=0,6$ kg move without friction at uniform acceleration under the action of a force ($F=2$ N) applied to the second block. With what acceleration are the blocks moving? What is the tension force of the thread connecting them?

2. A ball of mass $m=100$ g is suspended from a thread. In the natural position, the thread was placed horizontally and the ball was released. What is the tension force of the thread if at a given moment it forms an angle $\alpha=30°$ with the horizontal direction? What tensile strength should the thread have so that it does not break in this experiment?

3. The body weighs $P=3$ N in air, $P_B=1,8$ N in water, and $P_{ж}=2,04$ N in a liquid of unknown density. What is the density of the liquid?

4. A horizontally flying bullet hits a wooden block lying on a smooth horizontal plane and pierces it. Determine how much of the energy the bullet transferred to the body. Bullet mass $m=10$ g, beam mass $M=1$ kg, initial bullet speed $v_0=500$ m/s, bullet speed after departure $v=300$ m/s.

5. When making ice in a household refrigerator, it took $\tau_1=16$ minutes to cool the water from $T_1=289$ K to $T_2=273$ K and another $\tau_2=1$ h 20 minutes to turn it into ice. Based on these data, determine the specific heat of solidification of water. Specific heat of water $c=4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K).

6. The stove was lit in the room and the temperature rose from $t_1=16^\circ\text{C}$ to $t_2=21^\circ\text{C}$. The air pressure in the room did not change. How much of the air left the room?

7. Determine the EMF and internal resistance of the current source if, at a current strength of $I_1=2$ A, a power of $P_1=3$ W is released in the external circuit, and at a current strength of $I_2=4$ A, a power of $P_2=4$ W is released.

8. The electric stove has two sections in the heater. When one section is turned on, the water in the pan boils in $\tau=8$ minutes, and when the second section is turned on (without the first) - in $\tau_2=20$ minutes. How many minutes will it take for the water to boil in the pan if both sections are turned on: a) in parallel? b) sequentially? The heating conditions are the same in all cases.

9. At the bottom of a vessel filled with water to a height of $H=2$ m, there is an accurate light source. A round disk floats on the surface of the water, with the center of the disk located above the light source. At what minimum radius of the disk will no ray of light escape through the surface of the water? The refractive index of water is $n=4/3$.

10. At what distance from a lens with a focal length of $F=20$ cm must an object be placed so that its actual image is $\Gamma=4$ times larger than the object itself?
