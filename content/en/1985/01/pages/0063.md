Volume $V = S_{ABCD} \cdot |NN_1| = \frac{1}{2} |AC| \cdot |BD| \cdot |NN_1|$.

Triangle $MNN_1$ is rectangular: $|NN_1| = \sqrt{|MN|^2 - |MN_1|^2}$. Considering that by condition $|AC| = 4, |BD| = 2, |MN| = \sqrt{5}$, we obtain $V = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot 2 \cdot \sqrt{5 - |MN_1|^2} = 4 \sqrt{5 - |MN_1|^2}$.

It remains to find the length of the segment $MN_1$.

Let $O$ be the point of intersection of the diagonals of the rhombus $ABCD$. The point symmetrical to the point $M$ relative to $O$ lies on the segment $BC$, divides it in the ratio 2:3, counting from the point $B$, and, therefore, coincides with the point $N_1$. Thus, $O$ is the midpoint of the segment $MN_1:|MN_1|=2|ON_1|$. In a rhombus $ABCD$ $|BO|=1, |OC|=2, (BO)\perp (OC)$.

Therefore, $|BC|=\sqrt{5}, |BN_1|=\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}, \cos \alpha =\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$, where $\alpha=\widehat{OBC}$. By the cosine theorem for triangle $OBN_1$ we have $|ON_1|^2=|BO|^2+|BN_1|^2-2|BO| \cdot |BN_1| \cos \alpha=1$.

Hence $|ON_1|=|MO|=1, |MN_1|=2, V=4$.

To answer the second question of the problem, we prove the lemma.

Lemma. Let $A \neq A_1$ and $M \neq N$ be points in space located on nonparallel lines $(AA_1)$ and $(MN)$. Let the plane $P$ be chosen from all planes passing through the straight line $(MN)$ on which the projection of the segment $AA_1$ has the minimum length. Then the plane $P$ is repredicular to the plane $MNN_1$, where $N_1$ is a point in space such that $\overrightarrow{AA_1}=\overrightarrow{N_1N}$.

Proof. Let $K$ be the base of the redistributive dropped from the point $N_1$ to $(MN)$, and $P_*$ be the plane passing through the line $(MN)$ and the redistributor of the line $(N_1K)$ (the plane $P_*$ is redistributive to the plane $MNN_1$). Let us prove that $P_*$ is $P$, which will complete the proof of the statement.

Let us take an arbitrary plane $Q$ passing through the line $(MN)$ and different from the plane $P$. Let us denote by $L$ the base of the redistributive dropped from the point $N_1$ onto the plane $P$ (Fig. 6). Let us check that the length $|LN|$ of the projection of the segment $N_1N$ onto the plane $Q$ is greater than the length $|KN|$ of the projection of this segment onto the plane $P$ (the projection plane of the segments $AA_1$ and $N_1N$ onto any plane are the same, this will mean that $P_*=P$). By construction, $(N_1K) \perp (KN)$. Applying the theorem on three redistributives, we obtain that and $(LK) \perp (KN)$. But this means that the triangle $LKN$ is right-angled, and the segment $LN$ is its hypotenuse. Therefore, $|LN|>|KN|$. Q.E.D.
