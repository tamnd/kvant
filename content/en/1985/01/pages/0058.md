Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2 \sin x} = -\sqrt{3} \mathrm{tg} x.$$
2. The base side of a regular triangular straight line is $a$; the side edge is equal to $b$. Determine its volume and cross-sectional area passing through the side of the base perpendicular to the side edge opposite this side.
3. What does $a \geq b$ mean? Prove that if $a > b$ and $b \geq c$ then $a > c$.
4. Arrange the following numbers in ascending order:
$$1; 0,37; \frac{65}{63}; \frac{61}{59}; \mathrm{tg} 33^\circ; \mathrm{tg} (-314^\circ).$$
5. Prove that if $f(x) = \frac{x^2 + 2x - 1}{3}$, then
$$2f(x+2) + f(-x-1) = x^2 + 4x + 4.$$
Is the opposite statement true?
