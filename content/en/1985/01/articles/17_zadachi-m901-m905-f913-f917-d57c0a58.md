Tasks

M901—M905; F913—F917

M901. Bisquetrixes AK and BM of triangle ABC intersect at point O. Prove that if OK=OM, then either angles A and B of the triangle are equal, or angle C is equal to 60°.

M902. The natural series 1, 2, 3,... is divided into several arithmetic progressions. Prove that at least one of these progressions has a first term divisible by its difference.

A. B. Kelarev

M903. Is there a convex polyhedron, or the section of which by a plane not passing through the vertex is a polygon with a) an even number, b) an odd number of sides?

A. A. Dorogovtsev

M904. For every natural number
$$A=10^n a_n+10^{n-1} a_{n-1}+\dots+10 a_1+a_0$$
(with decimal notation $a_n a_{n-1}...a_1 a_0$) put $D(A)=a_n+2 a_{n-1}+2^2 a_{n-2}+\dots+2^{n-1} a_1+2^n a_0$.
For example, $D(1985)=1+2 \cdot 9+2^2 \cdot 8+2^3 \cdot 5=91$, $D(91)=9+2 \cdot 1=11$, $D(11)=3$.
a) Prove that for any natural number $A=A_0$ in the sequence $A_1=D(A_0)$, $A_2=D(A_1)$,... there is a number $A^*=A_k<20$ for which $D(A^*)=A^*$.
b) *What is $A^*$ equal to $A=19^{85}$?

E. I. Gurvits, A. L. Fedorov

M905. Prove that the equation $4x^n+(x+1)^2=y^2$ for natural numbers x and y a) has no solutions for n=1, 6) has at least two solutions for n=2, c)* has an infinite number of solutions for n=2, d)* has no solutions for natural numbers n≥3.

M. Garayev (10th grade student)

V. I. Chivilev

F914. A closed vessel with a volume V = 33.6 dm³ contains nitrogen and = 1 mole of water. At temperature t=100 °C, the pressure in the vessel is p=2·10⁵ Pa. Determine the amount of nitrogen in the vessel.

S. M. Korshunov

F815. A liquid with dielectric capacity ε flows between the plates of an insulated parallel-plate capacitor with a speed v. A magnetic field with induction B is directed perpendicular to the speed $\vec{v}$ and parallel to the plates. Determine the voltage on the capacitor plates. Distance between plates a.

A. I. Kirkinsky

F916. In the circuit shown in figure 1 (D is an ideal diode), switch K is closed for a time τ and then opened. At the moment of opening, the current in the inductor is equal to I₀. How long after the switch is opened does the current Iₙ in the coil reach its maximum value if it is equal to 2I₀? Construct a graph of Iₙ versus time t (0<t<∞).

A. I. Kirkinsky, E. A. Romishchevsky

F917. The object is located between the lens and a flat mirror, perpendicular to the main optical axis of the lens. The mirror, lens and object are enclosed in a housing made of translucent matte plastic (Fig. 2). Such a system creates two images of an object and an image of a lens. Both images of an object have the same dimensions, regardless of the distance between the lens and the object. What notification does the lens appear with?

E. P. Kuznetsov
