⟦folio 33⟧

PUZZLES

1. Place the numbers from 1 to 12 in empty circles so that the same amount is counted along three circles and six straight lines. How to find this amount without arranging the numbers?

2. Solve a number puzzle (different letters will have different numbers, same letters will have the same numbers).

3. Four identical king dice are placed as shown at figure. How many points does the bottom edge of the bottom bone contain?

4. Cut this polygon into smaller polygons and assemble them into a square.
