Many of our readers are interested in modeling and searching for new forms of star-shaped manifolds. The variety shown on this figure is presented by our reader A.P. Trofienko, a worker from the city of Gelendzhik. This manifold is a union of four octahedrons (their visible parts are shown in different colors), which are arranged in a certain “maximal metric” sense. With this arrangement, the resulting figure has many elements of geometry. In particular, the meter axes of the fourth and third order, that is, such straight lines that the polygon is aligned with itself when rotated around these straight lines at angles that are multiples of 90° and 120°. Find these axes. Try to list all the elements of the stellation of this stellate manifold.
