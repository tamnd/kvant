Here you can see the number writing systems invented by people in different eras. Top left xy - numerical ceroglyphs of ancient Egypt; to the right are clioscript records of the numbers of ancient Babylon (2500-2000 BC). In the second row from the left are numbers in the records of the ancient LEMENT and Indian Maya (in the lower territory of Peru); to the right are records of the numbers of Autek elements (XI-XVII centuries). In the bottom row on the left is the ancient Greek alphabetical notation of numbers and next to it is the familiar ancient Greek number system.
We provide readers with the entry 1985 in each of these number systems.
