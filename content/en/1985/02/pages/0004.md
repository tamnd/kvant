⟦folio 2⟧

On December 28, 1984, at the age of 77, the outstanding Soviet physicist, twice Hero of Socialist Labor, laureate of the Lenin and State Prizes of the USSR, academician Isaac Kushelevich Kikoin, died.

I.K. Kikoin paid great attention to the education of the younger generation. On his initiative, the magazine «Kvant» was created, of which he was editor-in-chief for 15 years. He headed the organizing committee of the All-Union School Olympiad and was the author of a number of school textbooks.

I.K. Kikoin often met with schoolchildren. The last time he spoke to him was during the presentation of the School of Natural Sciences at the Institute of Atomic Energy. I. V. Kurchatov Lenin Komsomol Prize. His performance is a kind of completion of youth.

“I want to tell you what I was thinking about, accepting this award,” I. K. Kikoin said to the audience. - I thought that here I am, an academician, the leader of a large team, I have a lot of achievements, but without any doubt, all this - position, degrees, titles - I would exchange for your youth, for your 15, maybe 17, years.

And I need to change because during my long life I didn’t have time to enjoy my beloved physics, I didn’t have enough time, I see clearly now - I didn’t have enough. But there was not a single day in my life, not a day off, not a holiday, not a vacation, when I didn’t do it. I often have dreams about physics. And still there was not enough time. You yourself have found out how it is when you live your life.

So don’t waste time now. It still won’t be enough, but at least it won’t be so offensive. You know, scientist is not a job title or place of work. Bot, he entered the laboratory - and began to think about science, and became a scientist. This is wrong. Being a scientist is a constant and often painful, sometimes wonderful state. Bot, that’s what I was thinking about when they handed me this chest.”

Let this parting word from I.K. Kikoin remain with you forever, as well as the bright memory of the outstanding scientist.
