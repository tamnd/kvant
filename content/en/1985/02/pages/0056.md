Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)
1. Solve the equation
$$x^2 + 3x + |x + 3| = 0.$$
2. Average equation
$$\frac{\cos 2\pi x}{1 + tg \pi x} = 0$$
find the one whose distance to the number $\sqrt{13}$ on the number line is the smallest.
3. From the middle $D$ hypotenuse $AB$ of a right triangle $ABC$ a ray is drawn, perpedicular to the hypotenuse and intersecting one of the legs. The segment $DE$ is plotted on it, the length of which is equal to the polyline of the length of the segment $AB$. The length of the segment $CE$ is equal to 1 and coincides with the length of one of the legs. Find the area of ​​triangle $ABC$. Present as a decimal fraction the approximate value of this area with an accuracy of 0.01.
4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2^{3x+1} + 2^{y-2} = 3 \cdot 2^{y+3x}, \\ \sqrt{3x^2 + 1 + xy} = \sqrt{x+1}. \end{cases}$$
5. In a quadrilateral paramid $SABCD$, the base $ABCD$ has the property of being symmetrical to the diagonal $AC$, whose length is 9, and the point $E$ of intersection of the diagonals of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ divides the segment $AC$ so that the length of the segment $AE$ is less than the length of the segment $EC$. Cutting the middle of the side edge paramides $SABCD$, a plane is drawn parallel to the base and intersecting with the ribs $SA$, $SB$, $SC$, $SD$, respectively, at points $A', B', C', D'$. The resulting polygon $ABCDA'B'C'D'$, which is part of the paramides $SABCD$, is intersected by the plane $a$ along a regular hexagon whose side length is 2. Find the area of ​​the triangle $ABD$ if the plane $a$ intersects the segments $BB'$ and $DD'$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Chemistry)
1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\log_3(1-x)}{x^2-3} \geq 0.$$
2. The excavator grows the pit with constant randomness. After he finished the job, the second excavator began to dig the same pit, also with constant randomness. The time that both excavators spent digging two pits is 4 $\frac{1}{12}$ times greater than the time that both excavators would spend digging one pit together. The first excavator operates with greater randomness, and the total randomness of common excavators is 70 m³/h. What is the arbitrariness of each excavator?
3. Determine at what values ​​$x$ the function
$$f(x) = x^2 (18 \sin 3x - 45 \cos 3x) + x (12 \cos 3x + 30 \sin 3x) + 68 \sin 3x - 170 \cos 3x$$ has minimums.
4. A regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ with a vertex $S$, in which the length of the base edge is equal to 2, and the length of a side edge is equal to $\sqrt{3}$, contains a regular quadrangular paramid $S'A'B'C'D'$ with a vertex $S'$, in which the lengths of all edges are equal to each other. In this case, the vertex $S'$ lies in the plane $ABCD$, and the vertices $A', B', C', D'$ lie on the quadrangular paramides $SABCD$. Prove that the vertex $S'$ lies at the intersection point of the diagonals of the square $ABCD$, and find the volume of the paramid $S'A'B'C'D'$.
5. Find all $a$ for which the inequality
$$-\frac{1}{2}|a+2| \cdot |x+a+4| + \left(|a+2| - \frac{a^2+4a+3}{|a+2|}\right) \times |x+2| - \frac{1}{2}|a+2| \cdot |x-a| \geq -2$$ holds exactly for two different values ​​of $x$.
Option 3

$$\sqrt{4+2 \log_2 \left(1-\frac{8x}{(2x+1)^2}\right)} = \log_2 \left(\frac{2x+1}{2x-1}\right) + 2 \log_2 \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right).$$
4. At the base of the quadrangular paramidium $SABCD$ lies a rhombus $ABCD$ on the length side $a$. The length of the diagonal $AC$ of a rhombus is 1.5 times the length of its side. The base height of the paramidia coincides with the center of the rhombus, and its length is 1.5 times greater than |AC|. A secant plane is drawn through point $A$ and the middle of edge $CS$, forming an angle of 45° with the plane of the base of the paramidium. What is the area of ​​the paramidium on this plane? How many such planes can be built?
5. Solve the system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} y^4 - 4y^3 - 16y^2 - 8xy - 4x^2 + 32y + 64 = 0, \\ \sin (5\pi x) - \sqrt{x(x-6)+13} \times \cos \left(\pi \left(y^2+2x+\frac{1}{2}\right)\right) + \sin \left(\pi (2y^2-x)\right) = 0. \end{cases}$$
