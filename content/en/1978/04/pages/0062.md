⟦folio 60⟧

Unusual dobies

Scientists at the mathematical laboratorium of VNIIPOCHEMI were surprised to discover that in physics there are unusual dimensional additions - when dividing one scientist by another, a third is often obtained. For example, if you divide one Volga by one Ampere, you get one Ohm: 1B/1A=1 Ohm. It turned out that there are quite a lot of dobes of this kind. In the example given, the fact that 1 Ohm easily turns into 10 m (ten meters) is also thought-provoking. Research in this direction continues.

Mysterious coincidence

After many years of theoretical and experimental research at the VNIIPOCHE Institute, a remarkable fact was established - the number π and the acceleration of free fall g are related with great accuracy by the correspondence π² = g. It is known that the acceleration of gravity depends on latitude. Experiments are currently underway to determine the dependence of the number π on the same parameter.
