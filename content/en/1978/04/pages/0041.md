⟦folio 39⟧

Quests

1. A three-digit number is given. Let's find the sum of its digits. Then we find the sum of the digits of the result. What was the original number if it and the two resulting sums can be written as shown in figure (like digits correspond to like digits)?

2. There are several jugs, among which there are two jugs of different shapes, as well as two jugs of different colors. Prove that among them there are two jugs of the same shape and different colors.

3. A natural number A is such that when divided by A, any odd number and its cube give the same remainder. Find all such numbers.

4. Black ink was burned onto a white surface. Is it true that:
a) there are two points of the same color located at a distance of 1 m;
b) are there two points of different colors located at a distance of 1 m?
