Amazing calculator

Many of you have probably heard about small computers that fit in your pocket and can calculate “anything.”
This article talks about one of these machines: the Elektronka B3-18 microcalculator. The dimensions of this device are 160×90×46 mm, and the weight is only 400 grams, but thanks to the achievements of modern electronics, this “magic box” contains more than 60 thousand elements: transistors, resistors, capacitors...! How did you manage to shrink so many electronic devices into such a small box? It is impossible to talk about the arrangement of Elektronka and its reduction in one article. Therefore, we will limit ourselves to talking about her mathematical abilities.

We had to watch people who first picked up “Electronics” in their hands. Most of them tried to figure out its occurrences on their own, without looking at the instructions. Please, this way of mastering the calculator is the most interesting. But for our great readers this path is closed. Therefore, we invite you to call on your imagination and imagine that you have an “Electronic” in your hands.

The first step, of course, is to press the “on” button (everyone will tell you this). In this case, the number 0 and a comma are displayed in the rightmost digit of the digital display. Let's find the sum 7+3 using a calculator. It is natural to press the keys with the images of numbers and the operation sign. So, press the “7” key (see figure) - the number 7 appears on the digital display, displayed in green. Next, press the “+” key - no changes are observed. Then we press the “3” key: the previous number disappears from the board and the number 3 appears in its place. Finally, we press the “=” key, and 10 appears on the board - the result of solving the problem (note that all numbers - 7, 3, 10 - are displayed on the right side of the board).

It is clear that any argumentative example with single-digit numbers should be solved in exactly the same way. What about multi-digit numbers? Press key 7 twice in a row. “77” lights up on the display. What if we need the number 65? Which key should you press first?
