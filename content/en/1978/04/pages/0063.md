⟦folio 61⟧

The record has been broken!

It is well known that weightlessness is achieved if the body is in free flight. Bent down, for example, from a table, a person is in a state of weightlessness during the flight. One of the VNII-POCHYU employees made 10⁷ jumps from the table, thereby exceeding the time spent in a state of weightlessness by both Yu. A. Gagarin and all other cosmonauts.

Before official approval by international organizations, a record was established, the mentioned employee asked, out of modesty, not to reveal his name.

Ponski new talents
were successful

In order to identify young talents, the institute’s staff constantly conducts extensive work with young people of different ages. This year one of the employees decided to check relevant activities in kindergartens. Due to the specifications of the latter institutions, all questions in the questionnaire required either a positive (“yes”) or a negative (“no”) answer. At the very first visit, a genius child was discovered: four-year-old Tolya Kostyukov, with such a survey, answered correctly any number of complex scientific questions with a 50% probability.
