⟦folio none⟧

Points A and B are connected by two roads.
At point A there is a hare, at point B there is a wolf. The wolf wants to catch the hare; the hare can run away from the wolf along the BG road (see figure). The hare is allowed to move along the roads only in the indicated directions, without crossing from one road to another and turn only at points A and B.
The wolf is allowed to move in any direction; at point O, choose any of the four directions. Both the hare and the wolf can stop at any point on their way.
How many times must the hare's speed exceed the wolf's speed for the hare to escape? Throughout the entire movement, the hare and the wolf see each other, they move evenly and the wolf begins to move first. The path segments OA, OB, OC, OE, CD, DE, AF and BF are equal to each other.
How does the answer change if the road towards BG is blocked?

E. Zuser
