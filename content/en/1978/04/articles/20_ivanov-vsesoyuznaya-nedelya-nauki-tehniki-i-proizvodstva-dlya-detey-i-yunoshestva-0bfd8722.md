A significant week of science, technology and production for children and youth

From January 3 to January 9, 1978, another important week of science, technology and production for children and youth, dedicated to the 60th anniversary of the Lenin Komsomol, was held everywhere. This is one of the most widespread and effective forms of work to promote scientific and technical knowledge among schoolchildren, their career guidance, broadening their horizons and developing creative abilities. Every year, about 16 million people take part in the Week’s events.

The grand opening of the Week took place in Moscow. At the opening, 200 students were invited to the capital of our Motherland - winners of scientific and technical shows, improvers, members of scientific societies, young innovators and designers from all the allied resource centers and their mentors - leaders of children's scientific and technical associations. Among them are 21 laureates of the HTTM show, 28 students from VDNH of the USSR, 108 winners of resource and regional performances, Olympiads, 74 people are members of VOIR and NOU.

On behalf of the organizers of the Week - the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the Central Committee of VOIR, the Supreme Scientific and Technical Organization and the weighty society "Knowledge" the students of the Week were led by the Secretary of the Central Committee of the Komsomol 3. G. Novozhilova, Deputy Minister of Education of the USSR V. M. Korotov. Academician, Director of the Institute of Space Research R.Z. Sagdeev shared their thoughts on the development of scientific and technical creativity of students; Hero of Social Labor, caretaker of the Moscow plant of electronic devices Yu. N. Dyakov; Honored Inventor of the RSFSR, Professor of the Bauman Moscow Higher Technical University L. N. Reshetov; science fiction writer D. A. Bilenkin.

The program of the Week was intense and interesting. At the plenary sessions and eight sections of the Week in Moscow, 87 people spoke with reports on work in technical creativity circles, a section of student scientific societies. The performances were accompanied by a demonstration of models, instruments, and construction kits. The works presented at the section speak about the diversity of interests of the children and go beyond the scope of the school curriculum. Many of them were carried out as specified by research institutes, industrial and agricultural enterprises, and are used in the national economy. At the “Young Technologies for the Motherland” show organized here, about 50 models, devices, and devices made by the participants in the opening of the Week were presented, some of which found application in the national economy.

The speeches of Ninya Karabanova, a student of school No. 54 in Chisinau, a member of the Council of the Republican Scientific Society of Students “Vitoorul”, the USSR champion in aircraft modeling, a fifth-grader from Sverdlovsk Vladimir Ivanov, were received with particular interest; candidate for master's sports, 10th grade student from Almaty Sergei Dvornikov; Secretary of the school organization VOIR, twice VDNH medalist Kirill Bulaev from secondary school No. 4 in Yaroslavl.

During the sections, there was a recorded conversation about the work of student scientific societies, which were highly recognized by high school students. Yuri Kozlov from Ukraine, Timur Daniyar - Khodzhaev from Uzbekistan, Moscow, Alexander Aslanov spoke about the work of their non-state educational institutions.

Participants of the Week met with prominent scientists, innovators of production, laureates of the Lenin Komsomol Prize in the field of science, promoted enterprises, research institutes, edited popular science magazines, vocational technical journals in Moscow, the house-museum of S.P. Korolev, the USSR Air Force Museum, at the VDNH of the USSR. On January 6, a group of students of the Week, interspersed with mathematics, physics and astronomy, promoted «Kvant» in the editorial office of the magazine. The guys met with members of the magazine's editorial board and editorial staff, got acquainted with the process of working on the issues and the editorial plans for 1978. At the end of the meeting, a small competition was held to solve entertaining problems, and the five winners of this competition - Sadar Gashimov (Sumgant), Timur Daniyar-Khojaev (Tashkent), Igor Zalivsky (Novopoltsk), Joseph Sveklo (Grodno) and Grigory Shappro (Moscow) received prizes - issues of the magazine with a commemorative inscription and authorships of the editorial staff.

Similar events took place throughout the country. Conferences of members of non-governmental educational institutions, performances of technical creativity by schoolchildren, and olympiads in mathematics, physics, and science were held in the resource centers. In schools, out-of-school institutions, technical scientific and technical centers there were festivals of science and students, actually educational films, subject evenings, demonstration performances of young modelers and designers. Millions of children became participants in meetings with young inventors and innovators, ex-

The Week was organized and interesting in Ukraine, Georgia, Moldova, Latvia, Gorky, Yaroslavl, Sverdlovsk and many other regions of the country.

Thus, in the Perm region, the opening of the Week of Science, Technology and Production took place in the region's House of Technology. During the Week, about 20 thousand schoolchildren participated in the regional exhibition of national economic achievements, more than 2 thousand attended excursions to universities, Olympiads in mathematics, physics, and chemistry were held, 150 members of scientific and technical associations of schoolchildren lived in a special winter camp, exchanged work experience, and participated in various competitions.

During the Week, much attention was paid to the promotion of accessible science, technology and production over the 60 years of Soviet power, familiarizing students with the progress of implementation of the decisions of the XXV Congress of the CPSU, plans of the X Five-Year Plan.

Many kind words were said to the children's older friends - teachers, advanced workers, collective farmers, national economic specialists, scientists, students - all those who introduce schoolchildren to the world of modern science, technology, economics, technical production, who pass on their knowledge, skills and habits to them.

The all-important week of science, technology and production for children and youth contributed to the unification of the efforts of all interested organizations in improving the scientific and technical creativity of schoolchildren. It showed that our time is characterized not only by the quantitative growth of artillery research and technical agricultural experience, but their creativity is now often directed by the tasks of scientific institutions and enterprises.

The implementation of the Week contributed to the solution of the tasks defined in the resolution of the Central Committee of the CPSU and the Council of Ministers of the USSR “On further improvement of the training and education of students in secondary schools and preparing them for work.”
Yu. Ivanov
Instructor of the School Youth Department of the Komsomol Central Committee
