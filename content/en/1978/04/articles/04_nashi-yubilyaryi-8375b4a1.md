OUR ANNIVERSARY

ANDREY NIKOLAEVICH KOLMOGOROV

April 1978 marks the 75th anniversary of the birth of the first deputy editor-in-chief of the journal «Kvant», a mathematician issued by the state, Hero of Socialist Labor, laureate of the Lenin and State Prizes of the USSR, academician Andrei Nikolaevich Kolmogorov.

In addition to Andrei Nikolaevich’s fruitful scientific work, we devote a lot of time and energy to the pedagogical problems of secondary school. He is one of the authors of a new program in mathematics, the author and scientific editor of a number of textbooks for secondary schools, and chairman of the Mathematics Commission of the Educational and Methodological Council of the USSR Ministry of Education.

The editorial collection and editorial staff of the magazine «Kvant» on my own behalf and on behalf of many of our readers, I congratulate Andrei Nikolaevich on his semi-decembration and wish him many years of life, great personal happiness, new successes in scientific and pedagogical activities.

ALEXEY IVANOVICH MARKUSHEVICH

Performer: 70th anniversary member of the editorial board of the National Journal, full member of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences, Professor Alexey Ivanovich Markushevich.

Alexey Ivanovich’s fruitful scientific work was associated with a large organization of work on the restructuring of school education in our country. He is one of the authors of a new mathematics curriculum for high school. Under the editorship of Alexey Ivanovich, I compiled a number of textbooks for secondary school according to the new program: mathematics textbooks for grades 4 and 5, “Alebra-6”, “Alebra-7”, “Alebra-8”. The editorial collection and editorial staff of the magazine «Kvant» on my own behalf and on behalf of many of our readers, I congratulate Andrei Nikolaevich on his semi-decemsi and wish him health, many years of life and new great successes in his noble work as a scientist and teacher.
