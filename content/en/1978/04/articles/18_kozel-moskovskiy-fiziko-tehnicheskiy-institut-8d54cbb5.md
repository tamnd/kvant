Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology

Below are published samples of options for entrance written exams in mathematics and physics in 1977.

Mathematica

Option 1

1. Solve the equation
   $$\sin 2x - \sin^2 x = 2\sin x - 4\cos x.$$
2. Solve inequality
   $$\frac{5 - 4x}{3x^2 - x - 4} < 4.$$
3. Solve the equation
   $$\lg^2(4 - x) + \lg(4 - x) \cdot \lg\left(x + \frac{1}{2}\right) = 2\lg^2\left(x + \frac{1}{2}\right).$$
4. The road passes through points A and B. We left at the same time and in the same direction: from A - motorcyclist (in the direction of B), from B - a cyclist. Motoriklst reached the cyclist at a distance of a km from B. If the motoriklst and the cyclist left simultaneously from A to B, then at the moment the motoriklst reached B, the cyclist would be b km behind him. Determine the distance between points A and B (the speeds of the motorcyclist and the cyclist are constant).
5. The base side ABCD of the correct primid SABCD has length a, the lateral edge has length 2a. Consider segments with ends on the diagonal BD of the base and the side edge SC, parallel to the plane SAD.
1) One of these segments is drawn through point M of the diagonal BD such that DM : DB = 1 : 3. Find its length.
2) Find the shortest length of all considered segments.
6. The graphs of the function y = $\frac{1}{x}$ and y = $5 - \frac{3}{2}x$, considered in the first quarter of the coordinate plane (x > 0, y > 0), intersect at points A and B. The hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle is parallel to the Ox axis, two of its vertices lie on the first graph, and the third on the segment AB. Find the lengths of the sides of the triangle.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
   $$2\log_2 \frac{x - 7}{x - 1} + \log_2 \frac{x - 1}{x + 1} = 1.$$
2. Solve the equation
   $$\frac{1}{\sin x} + \frac{1}{\sin \left(x - \frac{3\pi}{2}\right)} = 4\sin \left(x + \frac{5\pi}{4}\right).$$
3. In a right triangle ABC, leg BC has length a and forms an angle α with hypotenuse AC. Point D is located on leg BC and has the smallest sum of squared distances to straight lines AC and AB compared to other points on segment BC. Find the length of segment BD.
4. Find four numbers with the following properties:
   a) the sum of the first and fourth numbers is 14, and the sum of the second and third is 12;
   b) the first, second and third numbers form a geometric progression in that order;
   b) the second, third and fourth numbers form an arithmetic progression in that order.
5. The base side of a regular triangular primid ABCA₁B₁C₁ has length a. Point D is the middle of edge AB, point E lies on edge A₁C₁. Line DE forms angles α and β with planes ABC and AA₁C₁С, respectively. Find:
   1) prism height;
   2) the radius of the ball with the center of the segment DE tangent to the planes ABC and AA₁C₁С.
6. Find the area of ​​the figure, which is specified on the coordinate plane of the system nervevents
   $$\begin{cases} \sqrt{3x^2 + 3y^2} \geq 2y + 1, \\ y + 4 \geq 2\sqrt{3} |x|. \end{cases}$$

Option 2

1. A block of mass $m$, the dimensions of which are shown in figure 3, stands on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\alpha$. A force $\vec{F}$, parallel to the inclined plane, begins to act on the block. At what value of this force will the block topple? It is known that the block will not slide off the inclined plane.

2. In a cylindrical vessel with a cross-sectional area of ​​$S_1=200$ cm$^2$ and a height of $h_1=30$ cm, find the volume of $V=4$ liters of water.
