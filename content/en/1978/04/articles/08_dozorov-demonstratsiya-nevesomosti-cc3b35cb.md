Zero gravity demonstration

The state of weightlessness is achieved in free flight. A satellite in orbit, a freely flying stone, and a jumped up person are in a state of weightlessness. A weight suspended from a thread is weightless in free flight and, therefore, does not exert tension on the thread. It is easy to make a device that makes it possible to “observe” the state of weightlessness.

At figure 1 a schematic diagram of the device is given.

In the “normal” state, the weight (Γ) pulls on the thread, the elastic plate (VII) bends, breaking the contact between the terminals (K1 and K2) of the circuit. In this case, the lamp (L) included in the circuit naturally does not light up. If the entire device is thrown up, the load is in a state of weightlessness, and therefore does not stretch the thread. The elastic plate is in a non-bent position, the terminals are closed, and the light comes on. The light comes on only if the entire device is weightless. Please note that the state of weightlessness occurs both when moving up and when moving down.

The adjusting screw (B) allows you to set the terminals so that they are slightly spread out when the device is stationary. The entire device is mounted inside a transparent box. figure 2 shows a general view of the device.

Some practical tips for making the device. In order to be able to use both a large (flat) battery and a small “Krona”, it is better to focus on a larger flat battery during manufacturing. Most often, the device contains a battery, so access to it should be easy; you can attach it to the damaged part of the device, and make small holes in the housing for the connected wires.

Any thin strip of elastic metal, even half a blade for a safety razor, is suitable as an elastic plate.
