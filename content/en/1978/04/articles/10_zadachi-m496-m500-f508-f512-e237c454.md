Tasks

M496-M500; F508-F512

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with asterisks. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than July 1, 1978 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, editorial office of the magazine «Kvant», “Problem book «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example: “M496, M497” or “F508”. Solutions to problems in each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new assignments, should be sent in separate envelopes. Please also send assignments from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these tasks (on the envelope place: “Problem book «Kvant», new problems in physics” or “... new problems in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate your first and last name, school number and class in which you study.

M496. Which six-digit numbers are greater: those that can be represented as the product of two three-digit numbers, or those that cannot be represented?

S. Fomin

M497. On sides BC, CA and AB of acute triangle ABC, arbitrary points A₁, B₁, C₁ are taken; Circles are constructed on segments AA₁, BB₁ and CC₁ as on diameters. Prove that the three common chords of pairs of these circles intersect at the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle ABC.

V. Gutenhacher

M498*. For every natural number n ≥ 3, indicate the smallest k such that any n points in the plane, no three of which lie on the same line, can be separated by k straight lines. (I will divide these points by lines if for any two of these points there is a line from which they lie on opposite sides.)

N. Vasiliev, A. Egorov

M499. Let's call a number balanced if in its decimal notation a certain beginning coincides with a certain end (for example, the numbers 1971, 19219 are balanced, but the numbers 1415145 are not.) Prove that there is a number that, after adding any of the 10 digits to its right, becomes balanced.

G. Gurewitz

M500. N first-graders perform in one row (shoulder to shoulder). On the command “nale-VO”, everyone turns 90° at the same time, some to the left, and others to the right. Exactly a second later, everyone who came face to face with her neighbor turns “spin” - 180°. After another second, each person who now finds himself face to face with his neighbor again turns 180°, etc. (Fig. 1).

a) Prove that after a finite time the motion will stop.

b) * What is the maximum amount of time that the movement in formation could continue?
d) Let the line be infinite in both directions, and at the command “halle-BO” only a finite set of K first-graders turned to the right, and the rest turned to the left. Then, according to the rule of the problem (in italics), the movement would continue indefinitely. Prove, however, that the movement will stop after a finite time if this rule is replaced by the following: the first classic turns 180° only if the first (his neighbor) and the third of those standing in front of him are facing him (Fig. 2).

G. Kurdimov, Yu. Neretin

F508. The period of Mercury's revolution around the Sun is 88 Earth days, and around its axis - 59 Earth days. What is the length of day and night on Mercury?

E. Levitan

F509. There is a current in the lake that captures only the top layer of water. A raft with a sound-collecting installation floats along the current. At the bottom, ahead of the current, there is a stationary source of sound. An observer on the raft discovered that at some instants of time the sound reaches the raft in a direction making an angle α with the vertical. What angle is made with the vertical at this moment in the direction towards the sound source?

The speed of the current is v, the speed of sound in water is c, the thickness of the moving layer is much less than the depth of the ocean.

G. Kotkin

F510. A strong rubber mesh is stretched over the rubber ball, the nicks of which run along the meridians of the ball. What shape will the ball take if the pressure in it is increased?

F511. The plates of a flat capacitor with area S form a small angle α (α << 1) with each other. The average distance between the plates is d. Draw an approximate picture of the power lines of the power plant field of the capacitor and find the capacitance of the capacitor.

F512. The target shown in figure 3 includes an alternating current of frequency v = 50 Hz. Using a commutator, an alternating direction between points a and b, and then a direction between points b and c is supplied to the input of the osilograph. The osilograph screen produces images of two curves. Figure 4 was made from a photograph of the painting on the osilograph screen. Determine the inductance L of the circuit if C = 32 μF, R = 65 Ohm.

B. Nzhnik
