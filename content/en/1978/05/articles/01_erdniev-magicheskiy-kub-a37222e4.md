MAGIC CUBE

figure shows a 3×3×3 cube. Numbers from 1 to 27 must be placed in the cells so that the sum of three numbers in any of the indicated 37 directions is equal to 42.
After you do this, try to solve the problem without a hint - come up with a different arrangement of numbers.

P. Erdiev

TO OUR READERS

In each issue of our magazine, in the “Problem Book «Kvant»” section, problems in physics and mathematics are published. Every day the editors receive several thousand letters from readers with solutions to these problems. Among the authors of these letters, the editors select schoolchildren who have solved the most problems and sent the most original solutions. These schoolchildren receive a special prize established by the editorial board of the magazine «Kvant», and receive the right to participate in the Republic Olympiads in mathematics and physics along with the winners of regional and city Olympiads.

Coming up with an interesting problem is usually more difficult than solving it. More difficult, but more interesting. Try to come up with problems and send them to us.

Authors - schoolchildren of the best problems will be awarded special bonuses «Kvant», and the problems themselves will be published in the journal.

We are waiting for your letters.

TO OUR AUTHORS

If you decide to send us an article, we ask you:
1) Retype it on a machine in two copies, double spaced, and write the formulas by hand (we will only consider handwritten text if the author is a schoolchild).
2) Also draw two examples of drawings (which example is on a separate sheet).
3) If the text of your article takes no more than 12 typewritten pages, then put it together with the excerpts in an envelope and sew it on to us.
4) Do not forget to tell us your full last name, first name and patronymic, home address and telephone number.
Please keep in mind that we do not issue refunds.
