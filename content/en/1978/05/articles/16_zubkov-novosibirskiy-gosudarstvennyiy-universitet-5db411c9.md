Novosibirsk State University

The main task of NSU is to train highly qualified specialists - researchers in the fields of mathematics, mechanics, physics, chemistry, biology, geology, economic cybernetics, history and language, mainly for Siberia and the Far East.

About four thousand students, graduate students and interns study at the university. It consists of the following faculties: mechanics and mathematics, physics, natural sciences, geological physics, economics and humanities.

Admission exams take place from July 11 to July 25 (acceptance of documents from June 20 to July 10), enrollment at the university is carried out until July 28.

All students (except those living in Akademgorodok) are provided with a hostel.

NSU graduates are sent to work in academic and industrial research institutes, computer centers, research laboratories, as well as teaching at universities.

The preparation of specialties at NSU is based on the use of the scientific and pedagogical potential of the Novosibirsk Scientific Center of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences. This center includes 22 scientific institutes. Nearby, scientific centers of the Siberian Branch of the All-Russian Academy of Agricultural Sciences and the Siberian Branch of the Academy of Medical Sciences are being built.

The Novosibirsk Scientific Center and University is essentially a single scientific and educational complex. The main part of the pedagogical work at the university is carried out from the institutes of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences - academicians, corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences, doctors and candidates of science. This ensures a high theoretical level of teaching and constant improvement of curricula in accordance with the latest scientific achievements.

Another fundamental feature of studying at NSU is the mandatory long-term research practice of students at the institutes of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences. Thanks to the proximity of NSU to the research institutes of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences, students have the opportunity to participate in seminars, scientific meetings and conferences held there, get acquainted with the latest scientific achievements and unsolved problems of science, meet with prominent scientists from other cities of the country and from abroad, and directly participate in scientific research. Only employees with degrees and titles are involved in the scientific supervision of students. Coursework and theses are carried out on current topics being developed in the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences; they are organized into the scientific production of its institutes. NSU students go on internships to socialist countries.

Students also acquire independent research skills in the research sector of NSU. The development of useful skills is facilitated by the wide participation of students in the daily scientific student conference, which, in terms of coverage of creative student youth, has gone beyond the boundaries of NSU, becoming significant.

The university is located 30 km from the center of Novosibirsk, on the territory of Academgorodok, in a picturesque forest on the shore of the Ovskoe Sea. Near the educational buildings there are dormitories, dining halls, and a sports complex.

The life of students at the university is rich and varied. Interest clubs are active, amateur artistic activities are widely developed, and Days of physicists, mathematicians, chemists, geologists, and initiation into students are held. Student construction teams are working energetically. The Interclub has been operating for many years, and the Week of International Other Life is held, ending with a traditional political maveka. NSU has excellent opportunities for sports, which is greatly facilitated by the magnificent Siberian nature.

Physics
In 1977, at the Faculty of Physics of NSU, each applicant was offered 6 problems during a written exam in physics. 5 hours were allotted to solve all problems. In each option there were three types. First of all, these are generally pleasant computational problems ranging from complex to complex, requiring ingenuity, deep knowledge, and the ability to understand an unusual or conventional physical situation.

In the last - sixth - it was necessary to explain the physical phenomenon shown by the examiner on the demonstration table. Here it was important to consider the effectiveness and average possible physical factors to highlight the main one.

Finally, the fifth task is the evaluation task. The text of the option indicated that what was described in this task was a good sign for the applicant; he can himself choose the values ​​of the physical quantities necessary for the solution and obtain a numerical answer. To solve this problem, you need to understand, grasp the physical phenomenon that forms a simple (since you only need to estimate) physical model of this phenomenon, take reasonable values ​​of the physical parameters and, finally, correctly use the system of units, obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. Note that a rough estimate, an order of magnitude estimate, is an almost obligatory initial stage of constructing an experiment, designing an installation, and theoretical development.
