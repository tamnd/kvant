A simple reasoning with a thread for the left end of the arc $AB$ and combining the results obtained together, we will construct a tangent diagram corresponding to the arc $AB$ (Fig. 7).

If the arc of a curve is not convex everywhere in one direction, if it, like the curve $AB$ on figure 8, has a point at which the tangent to the curve passes from one side to the other (such a point is called the break point), then in this case it can be divided by this point into two convex arcs. If now for each of these arcs we construct a tangent diagram corresponding to them, and then “superpose” one picture on another (see figure 7, 9 and the final figure 10), then we will obtain a tangent diagram for the entire arc.
