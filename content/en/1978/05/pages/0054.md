⟦folio 52⟧

Faculty of Physics

1. An alternating voltage is applied to the plates of a flat capacitor, located vertically, creating an electric field of intense $E = A \cos \omega t$. Find the law of motion and construct a graph of the curve along which the electron will move, located between the plates of the capacitor, taking into account the force of gravity. At the initial moment, the electron ends up and is at a distance of $l$ from the nearest plate. The mass of the electron is $m$, the charge is $e$, and $l$ is so large that at any time the electron does not reach the plates of the capacitor.

2. Find three positive numbers such that their product is greatest, provided that the sum of the first and second numbers is $a$, and the sum of the first and third numbers is $2a$.

3. Find those solutions to the equation

$$6 \text{tg} x + \frac{5}{\text{tg} 3x} = \text{tg} 2x,$$

which belong to the integral

$$\int -\frac{\pi}{2}; \frac{\pi}{2} \Bigg[.$$

4. How many ways can you place $n$ identical balls into $k$ boxes?

5. Three balls touch the plane of a triangle with sides $a$, $b$ and $c$ at its vertices and touch each other in pairs. Find the radii of the balls.

Department of Economic Cybernetics and Department of National Economy Planning of the Faculty of Economics, Faculty of Chemistry and Faculty of Psychology

1. In the integral $]0; 1[$ find the subset of those $x$ for which the inequality is true

$$\left(\frac{1}{81}\right)^8 + \log_a x > \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2a} x.$$

2. Find all solutions to the equation

$$8 \cos x + 6 \sin x - \cos 2x - 7 = 0,$$

lying in the interval $[0; 2\pi]$.

3. How many positive integers are there, less than $10^4$, in which all digits are different in the decimal system?

4. Given a curved trapezoid bounded by the lines $y = 0$, $x = a$ ($a > 0$) and $y = x^3$. What part of a planar trapezoid is the area of ​​the triangle cut off from this trapezoid by the tangent to line $y = x^3$ at point $x = \frac{2a}{3}$?

5. Determine the height of the cone inscribed in a ball of radius $R$ and having the largest lateral surface area. What is the largest lateral surface area?

Faculty of Biology and Geography

1. Solve inequality

$$\sqrt{x+3} - \sqrt{x-1} > \sqrt{2x-1}.$$

2. Find all solutions to the equation

$$\frac{3(1-\sin 3x)}{\sin x - \cos 2x} = 2 \cos 2x - 7,$$

lying in the integral $]-\pi; \pi[$.

3. How many positive integers are there, less than $10^4$ and divisible by 4, in the decimal system of which only the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 5 occur, and so that the same digit is not repeated?

4. A tangent is drawn to the graph of the function $y = (x+1)\sqrt{x}$ at the point where the slope is equal to 2, and the tangent does not pass through the origin. Find the points of intersection of this tangent with the coordinate axes.

5. What should be the radius of the base of a cone with a given lateral surface area $S$ in order for the volume of the cone to be greatest?

Faculty of Geology and Department of Political Economy, Faculty of Economics

1. Solve inequality

$$\sqrt{x^2+x} > 1-2x.$$

2. Find all solutions to the equation

$$\sin 7x + \cos^2 2x + \sin x,$$

lying in the integral $]-\frac{\pi}{4}; \frac{\pi}{4}[$.

3. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{2-\log_x 9} = -\frac{\sqrt{12}}{\log_3 x}.$$

4. Calculate the area of ​​the normal curve of the triangle lying in the 1st quadrant and bounded by the lines $xy = 4$, $x = y$, $y = 2x$.

5. Vector $a$, number of vectors $b = (12; -16; -15)$, forms an acute angle with axis $Oz$. Given that $|a| = 100$, find its coordinates.
