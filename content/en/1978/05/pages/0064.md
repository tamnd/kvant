Let us now turn to the first inequality

$$\sqrt{3x^2 + 3y^2} - 3 \geq 2y + 1. \quad (2)$$

The domain of definition of the left side is the set of points on the coordinate plane satisfying the condition

$$x^2 + y^2 \geq 1. \quad (3)$$

Let's consider two cases: a) $2y + 1 < 0$ and b) $2y + 1 \geq 0$. In case a), inequalities (2) and (3) are equivalent, since under condition (3), (2) is also satisfied.

In case b) both sides of inequality (2) are non-negative, therefore, squaring both sides, we obtain the inequality

$$3x^2 + 3y^2 - 3 \geq (2y + 1)^2, \quad (4)$$

which can be reduced to the form

$$(y + 2 - \sqrt{3}x) (y + 2 + \sqrt{3}x) \leq 0.$$

Since $x \geq 0$ and $2y + 1 \geq 0$, the expression $y + 2 + \sqrt{3}x$ is always positive; reducing by it, we get

$$y + 2 - \sqrt{3}x \leq 0.$$

Note that in case b) inequality (3) follows from (4).

We finally obtain that the original system, subject to the condition $x \geq 0$, in accordance with the above cases a) and b) is equivalent to a set of two systems

a) $$\begin{cases} y < -\frac{1}{2}, \\ x^2 + y^2 \geq 1, \\ y \geq 2\sqrt{3}x - 4; \end{cases}$$

b) $$\begin{cases} y \geq -\frac{1}{2}, \\ y \leq \sqrt{3}x - 2, \\ y \geq 2\sqrt{3}x - 4. \end{cases}$$

Let us now construct a figure on the coordinate plane, which is given by the combination of systems a) and b) under the condition $x \geq 0$. To do this, we examine the relative position of the straight line with the equation $y = -\frac{1}{2}$, $y = \sqrt{3}x - 2$, $y = 2\sqrt{3}x - 4$ and the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. The lines $y = \sqrt{3}x - 2$ and $y = 2\sqrt{3}x - 4$ intersect the axis $Oy$ at points $A(0; -2)$ and $E(0; -4)$, respectively, and the axis $Ox$ at the same point $B\left(\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}; 0\right)$ (Fig. 8). Let us prove that the line $(AB)$ is tangent to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. From the point $O$ we lower the perpendicular $[OC]$ to $(AB)$, and from the point $C$ we lower the perpendicular $[CF]$ to $(OA)$. Note that $OAB = 30^\circ$. Then from the right triangles $ACO$ and $CFO$ we obtain $|OC| = 1$, $|OF| = \frac{1}{2}$, $|CF| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. It follows that straight line $(AB)$ touches the circle at point $C\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}; -\frac{1}{2}\right)$, and straight line $y = -\frac{1}{2}$ passes through the point of tangency.

Fig. 8.

System a) under the condition $x \geq 0$ specifies the intersection of four sets: the first set consists of points located below the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ and on the circle itself, the third - from points located above the straight line ($BE$) and on the straight line itself, the fourth - from points located to the right of the axis $Oy$ and on the axis itself. This intersection gives the figure $DCKE$ (at Fig. 3 it is indicated by vertical shading). Similarly, we find that system 6) defines the triangle $BCK$, including its sides (on Fig. 8 it is indicated by horizontal shading). Thus, the original system of inequalities under the condition $x \geq 0$ defines a figure $CBED$ bounded by segments $[CB], [BE], [ED]$ and another circle $DC$. Its area can be calculated using the formula

$$S = S_{\Delta ABE} + S_{\Delta AOC} - S_C,$$

where $S_C$ is the area of ​​sector $COD$ with a central angle equal in magnitude to $60^\circ$. Thus,

$$S = \frac{1}{2} |AE| \cdot |OB| + \frac{1}{2} |AO| \cdot |CF| - \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{7\sqrt{3} - \pi}{6}.$$

The dotted line together with the segment $[DE]$ on Fig. 8 limits the part of the figure given to the original system under the condition $x < 0$.

Answer: $\frac{7\sqrt{3} - \pi}{3}$.
