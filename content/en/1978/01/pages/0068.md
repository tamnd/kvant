If you stack it, applying base to base, you can achieve identical antiprisms («Kvant», 1976, No. 1, p. 2), you get the multifaceted one shown on the left.

The lateral surface of this polyhedron can be obtained as follows. Draw a rectangle with a flap as shown in the drawing. Cut horizontal lines on the reverse side. These lines define the internal edges of the polygon.

Cut diagonal lines from the front side. They define the outer edges of the polygon. The polyhedron development is ready. When rolling it into a “cylinder”, you should press with your fingers on the centers of the diamonds of the mesh.

The result will be the body shown on the left. On its surface there are two families of helical lines (see article on p. 55).

If each side of the rhombus is replaced with some kind of curve, you get a “extensive ornament”. By replacing a sheet of paper with fireproof film, you can thus make yourself a lampshade with an amazing geometric design.

Think about how the 14-hedron and the “cylinder” on the left are related.
