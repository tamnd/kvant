Problem 2. 4 moles of gas we carry out the process depicted in figure 4. At what section is the maximum gas value?

First of all, let's draw a graph of this process in coordinates $p, V$ (Fig. 5). To do this, from the Mendeleev-Clapeyron equation we find the volume of gas at points 1, 2, 3 and 4:
$$V_1 = 0,1 \text{ м}^3, V_2 = 2V_1 = 0,2 \text{ м}^3, V_3 = V_2 \text{ и } V_4 = 0,5 \text{ м}^3.$$
From figure 5 it immediately follows that in section 2-3 no work is done (the process is ishoric):
$$A_{2-3} = 0.$$
In section 3-4 (with an ishoric process) it is equal to the area of ​​the corresponding straight skier:
$$A_{3-4} = p_3(V_4 - V_3) = 1,5 \cdot 10^4 \text{ Дж}.$$
Let us now estimate the work during the isothermal process 1-2. It is obvious that the area under section 1–2 of the isotherm is less than the area of ​​the trapezoid 1–2–$V_2 - V_1$. This latter is equal to $1,5 \times 10^4 \text{ Дж}$, that is, equal to the area of ​​the straight skier corresponding to section 3-4. Thus,
$$A_{1-2} < A_{3-4} \text{ и } A_{2-3} = 0.$$
Consequently, the maximum gas in section 3-4 is equal.

Problem 3. A gas occupying a volume $V_1 = 2 \text{ м}^3$ at a pressure $p_1 = 4 \cdot 10^5 \text{ Pa}$ undergoes a circular process (cycle), consisting of several stages. First, the gas is cooled isochorically to a temperature at which its pressure is $p_2 = 10^5 \text{ Pa}$. Then it is ishorically cooled to the initial maximum state, so that its pressure changes with the change in volume according to the law: $p = \alpha V$ ($\alpha$ is a constant value). Draw a graph of this circular process on the $pV$-diagram and find the perfect gas equal.

As follows from the conditions of the problem, the state of gas 1 and 3 is represented by points lying on the line $p = \alpha V$ passing through the origin of coordinates (Fig. 6). This means $p_1 = \alpha V_1$ and $p_3 = \alpha V_3$.

Taking into account the fact that $p_3 = p_2$, we obtain $V_3 = \frac{p_2V_1}{p_1} = 0,5 \text{ м}^3$.

The work in a circular process is numerically equal to the area of ​​the figure limited by the graph of this process, in this case, the area of ​​the triangle 1 2 3:

$$A = \frac{1}{2} (p_1 - p_2) (V_1 - V_3) = \frac{(p_1 - p_2)^2 V_1}{2p_1} = 2,25 \cdot 10^5 \text{ Дж}.$$
