New trick
to the All-Union correspondence
math school

The All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into the school.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1978. School education is free.

Students accepted into the school will be regulated by tasks that contain binding theoretical questions and problems to solve. The VZMS program is closely related to the school curriculum and is aimed at in-depth study of the main issues of the school mathematics course. The duration of training is three years.

The objectives of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 1, 1978. After checking the work (around July 1978), VZMSH will inform all those who took part in the competition about the results of the work. The advantage when entering the user is schoolchildren living in rural areas and in workers' settlements.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from regular school problems, solving them does not require additional knowledge of mathematics. To enter school, it is not necessary to solve all problems without exception. When evaluating work, they will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without justification may not be read. If there are several different answers to a problem, then you need to indicate them all.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student's terradi in Klateku. Entry papers will not be processed and solutions will not be issued.

In the envelope along with your notebook, you must include a piece of paper measuring 14 cm × 6 cm with your postal address (we will paste it on the envelope when we send you the answer).
