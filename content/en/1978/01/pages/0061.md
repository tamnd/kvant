Correspondence School of Physics and Technology

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute (MIPT) is recruiting students from eight-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR into grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1978/79 academic year. (In some cases, reception from other allied resources is allowed.)
The form of training in 3FTSH differs from the usual work with accounting at the level. Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches you to work with additional literature, and express your thoughts in a concise manner.

3FTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom become students at leading universities in our country.

The goal of our school is to help students study physics and mathematics independently. That is why, when admitting to 3FTS, preference is given to students living in rural areas and working-class villages, where the help of our school is especially needed.

3FTS also accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized locally at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. The circle is accepted into the 3FTSH if the school director informs the 3FTSH files of the circle leaders and a changed list of the circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical tasks and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitive tasks at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked in 3FTSH or its films, and the members of the circle are checked by its leader.

Moscow students receive classes in physics and mathematics according to the 3FTSH program twice a week at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), which are also recruited based on the results of completing the 3FTSH introductory task.

Each student completes the introductory task independently. The work is to be done in Russian and carefully copied into one school terdal. The order of tasks should be the same as in the assignment. Send Terdal in a large envelope with a simple parcel post. Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Place the certificate on the inside cover of the document. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. A sheet of paper filled in according to the following pattern was placed on the outside:

1. Region (krai or ASSR) Kemerovo region.
2. Last name, first name, patronymic Glushkov Valery Ivanovich
3. Wax class
4. Number and address of school: school No. 6, st. Shitskina, 26
5. Profession parent and position father mechanic, master Czech seamstress
6. Detailed home address 653023, Prokopyevsk, st. Lenina, 2, apt. 15

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 10, 1978 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be processed.

Admission to the school is determined by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology and by order of the director of 3FTSH. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1978.
