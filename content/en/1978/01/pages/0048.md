Problem 6. Name the work performed by the mass $m = 0,2$ kkv. in the process of advabatic expansion, if it cooled from $T_1 = 300$K to $T_2 = 268$K. Molar mass of the body $\mu = 4 \times 10^{-3}$ kcal/mol.

In an advabatic process ($Q=0$), the work of a gas is equal to the change in its internal energy, taken with the opposite sign:
$$A = -\Delta U.$$
For helium (helium is a monatomic gas)
$$\Delta U = \frac{3}{2} \frac{m}{\mu} R \Delta T = \frac{3}{2} \frac{m}{\mu} R (T_2 - T_1).$$

From here
$$A = -\frac{3}{2} \frac{m}{\mu} R (T_2 - T_1) \approx 2 \cdot 10^4 \text{ Дж}.$$
