Assignment for the introductory test at VZMSH in 1978

1. There are 4 points A, B, C and D on the prima so that |AB| = 1, |AD| = 4, |CD| = 2. What can |BC| be equal to?

2. To the number 60, add two digits on the right and left so that the resulting six-digit number is divisible by 1977. (It is enough to find one such number.)

3. What values ​​can a take if |a+b| ≤ 1 and |a-3b| ≤ 2?

4. A square is given on the plane with its center at point O. At this point there is a projector that illuminates an angle of 90°. In my early days, the projector illuminated a corner whose sides passed through the vertices of the square. Then the projector began to rotate around point O. At what point in time will the area of ​​the illuminated part of the square be greater: when the projector is 45° or 72° on top?

5. Ivan Aleksadrovich Khlestakov was invited to manage the delartament and, within three days, add 30,000 courvers. The following is known: if on the first day two large curver were added, then the total number of curver would be the fourth power of the number on which more curver were added on the third day than on the second. How many courvers were added to Khlestakov each day? (Give all possible answers.)

6. Given an acute angle with a vertex at point B and point A on one of its sides. Find point K on the other side of the angle so that the radius of the circle circumscribed about triangle BAK is the smallest.

7. Find all pairs of numbers (x; y) satisfying the equation x² - xy - 2y² = 18.
