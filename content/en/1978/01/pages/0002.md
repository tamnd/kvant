On this figure you see two curves generated by a computer. The equation of these curves in the polar coordinate system has the form:

$$\rho = 35,5 + 10,5 \sin(80 \varphi) \sin\left(\frac{5}{2} \varphi\right),$$

$$\rho = 18,5 + 11,5 \sin\left(\frac{79,5}{16} \varphi\right) \sin\left(\frac{1}{32} \varphi\right).$$

(We wrote about the polar coordinate system in «Kvant» No. 9, 1977, p. 51). These curves fill the graphs of signals modeled by amplitude. Try to find the periods of these curves, that is, the smallest positive $T$ such that $\rho(\varphi) = \rho(\varphi + T)$.

Yu. Kotov.
