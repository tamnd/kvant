Correspondence School of Physics and Technology

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute (MIPT) is recruiting students from eight-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR into grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1978/79 academic year. (In some cases, reception from other allied resources is allowed.)
The form of training in 3FTSH differs from the usual work with accounting at the level. Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches you to work with additional literature, and express your thoughts in a concise manner.

3FTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom become students at leading universities in our country.

The goal of our school is to help students study physics and mathematics independently. That is why, when admitting to 3FTS, preference is given to students living in rural areas and working-class villages, where the help of our school is especially needed.

3FTS also accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized locally at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. The circle is accepted into the 3FTSH if the school director informs the 3FTSH files of the circle leaders and a changed list of the circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical tasks and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitive tasks at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked in 3FTSH or its films, and the members of the circle are checked by its leader.

Moscow students receive classes in physics and mathematics according to the 3FTSH program twice a week at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), which are also recruited based on the results of completing the 3FTSH introductory task.

Each student completes the introductory task independently. The work is to be done in Russian and carefully copied into one school terdal. The order of tasks should be the same as in the assignment. Send Terdal in a large envelope with a simple parcel post. Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Place the certificate on the inside cover of the document. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. A sheet of paper filled in according to the following pattern was placed on the outside:

1. Region (krai or ASSR) Kemerovo region.
2. Last name, first name, patronymic Glushkov Valery Ivanovich
3. Wax class
4. Number and address of school: school No. 6, st. Shitskina, 26
5. Profession parent and position father mechanic, master Czech seamstress
6. Detailed home address 653023, Prokopyevsk, st. Lenina, 2, apt. 15

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 10, 1978 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be processed.

Admission to the school is determined by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology and by order of the director of 3FTSH. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1978.

Objectives of the introductory test at 3FTSH in 1978

Physics

1. It is known that to measure thin wires or plates, precision measuring instruments are used: for example, a caliper or a micrometer. Unfortunately, you did not have such devices at hand. What would you do to measure the thickness of one- and two-kopeck coins as accurately as possible? A sheet of paper in a book or notebook? Foils for wrapping chocolates? Describe the method you used and provide the measurement results.

2. Why, besides securing the rails of the railway track, do they put sleepers under them?

3. The entire route from the vacation spot to the city is completed at an average speed of 32 km/h, partly on foot, partly by bus and then by train. At what speed is each segment of the path traveled if their lengths are in the ratio 1:4:45, and the corresponding time intervals are in the ratio 4:1:20?

4. The cube is assembled from a large number of well-fitted wooden cups of the same volume, made from various wood species: cork with a density of 0.2 g/cm³, oak with a density of 0.7 g/cm³, cedar with a density of 0.55 g/cm³ and ebony with a density of 1.2 g/cm³. What is the average density of a cube if the number of cups of these materials are taken accordingly in relative height?

5. Carbon tetrachloride, water and kerosene are sawn into a cylindrical vessel. Determine the pressure at the bottom of the vessel if the masses of all liquids are the same, and the upper level of the lightest liquid, kerosene, is at a height of 23 cm from the bottom of the vessel. The densities of the liquids correspond to 1.6 g/cm³, 1.0 g/cm³ and 0.8 g/cm³. Take the free fall acceleration to be 10 m/s².

6. In what ratio should the volumes of alcohol and gasoline be taken so that the specific temperature of the mixture of these flammable substances is equal to 4.2·10⁷ J/kg?

7. When repairing the electrical supply, the spiral had to be shortened by 25% of its original length. How will the power released in the tile change?

8. A smooth disk of radius R, the plane of which is horizontal, rotates around its axis with a rotation frequency n=40 rpm. A small body detaches from the surface of the disk at a distance R/2 from the axis and slides along the disk without friction. How long will it take for it to slide off the disk?

9. When buying nylon fishing line in stores, the fisherman decided to ask what maximum load it can withstand. But after some fiddling around, he came up with a way to determine this value using a 0.5 kg weight and a tape measure. Think about it too. Describe the method you came up with and explore how the strength of the fishing line depends on its diameter. Present the measurement results in table form.

10. Is it possible to lower an object weighing 45 kg from a roof using a rope whose maximum strength is 400 N without breaking the rope?

11. In the early days of its existence, the directorate was usually filled with hydrogen. This provided the necessary lifting force, but the use of the director for military purposes had to be immediately abandoned: a small hit from a bullet or sedge or the impact of a blast wave led to the ignition of hydrogen. But one day during the First World War, the directorate of the opposing side appeared over London, which remained frequent, despite being hit by a bullet. It turned out that it was filled with gel. When this became known, one of the experts said that helium cannot provide the necessary lifting force: helium is twice as heavy as hydrogen, therefore, the lifting force is reduced when entering. Is this true? Explain.

12. Add an amount of hot water into two identical vessels. The air was pumped out of the vessels, and the vessels (above) were held together with a very thin rubber tube and balanced on a scale. Will the balance of the scales become unbalanced over time? Explain.

13. The canister for making carbonated water has a volume of 5 cm³ and contains carbon dioxide under a pressure of 15 atm. Is it possible to use a technical scale with a weighing accuracy of 10 mg to replace the difference in the mass of a full and “empty” can? The temperature is assumed to be 0°C.
