Geometry questions

Check your answers with those given at the end of the issue. To complete this task, no special knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum is required.

B. Mutual arrangement of straight lines and planes
5 (VIII). There are three points in a plane that do not lie on the same line. How many lines are there in this plane that are equidistant from these points?
a) One, 6) two, c) three, d) infinitely many, e) none.
6 (IX, X). How many planes are there that are equidistant from the four given points that do not belong to the same plane?
a) One, 6) two, c) three, d) four, e) infinitely many.
7 (VIII, X). How many lines are there that divide a given triangle into two parts of equal area?
a) One, 6) two, c) three, d) four, e) infinitely many.
8 (IX, X). How many lines are there that intersect each of the three given pairwise skew lines?
a) Infinite many, 6) none, c) one, d) two, e) three.
9 (IX, X). The orthogonal projection of the right angle $AOB$ onto some plane is the angle $A_1O_1B_1$. What values ​​can angle $A_1O_1B_1$ have?
a) Only $90^\circ$, 6) any from $0^\circ$ to $180^\circ$, c) any from $0^\circ$ to $90^\circ$, d) any from $90^\circ$ to $180^\circ$, e) any from $45^\circ$ to $135^\circ$.
10 (X). The plane intersects the cube, but does not pass through any of its vertices. How many cube edges can this plane intersect?
a) 3 or 4, 6) from 3 to 5, c) from 3 to 6, d) from 3 to 7, e) from 3 to 10.
