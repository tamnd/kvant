Tasks

M481—M485; F493—F497

M481. In the sequence of natural numbers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$, each term $a_{k+1}$ is equal to the sum of the squares of the digits in the decimal notation of the number $a_k(k \ge 1)$. Prove that for any $a_1$ the number 1 or the number 89 occurs in this sequence.

V. Panfilov

M482. The cross section of a regular tetrahedron is a quadrilateral. Prove that the perimeter of this quadrilateral is greater than 2$a$, but less than 3$a$; where $a$ is the length of the tetrahedron edge.

V. Proizvolov, A. Savin

M483. a) Prove that the ratio of the radius of the incircle of a right triangle to the sum of the squares of the lengths of the medians drawn from the acute angles does not exceed 1/5.

6) Find the maximum value that this ratio can take.

Y. Temralev

M484. For what $n$ is there a convex $n$-gon that can be cut into several regular polygons (not necessarily identical)?

S. N. Mironov

M485*. a) Prove that the number $e$ is contained between the numbers $a_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$ and $b_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^{n+1}$ for any natural number $n$.

6) Prove that the sequence $c_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{4n}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$ is monotonically increasing and the sequence $d_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{2n}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$ is monotonically decreasing.

c) Divide the segment $[a_n; b_n]$ into four segments of equal length. Which of them contains the number $e$?

d) Divide the segment $[a_n; b_n]$ into eight equal parts. Which one contains $e$?

e) What if the segment $[a_n; b_n]$ is divided into $2^k$ equal parts (in this problem it is interesting to get the answer for sufficiently large $n$, for example, for $n > 2^k$)?

⟦folio 29⟧

(Recall that $e = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n = 2,718...$, see the textbook “Algebra and Principles of Analysis 10”, pp. 106, 113.)

T. Martynenko, R. Ushakov

F493. What influence does the Moon have on the trajectory of the Earth around the Sun?

F494. In the circuit shown at figure 1, $R_2 = 90 \text{ Om}$, $R_3 = 300 \text{ Om}$, $R_4 = 60 \text{ Om}$ and $L_2 = 900 \text{ F}$. What are the meanings of $R_1$ and $L_1$ if no current flows through galvanometer $G$ regardless of whether a direct or alternating current source is connected to terminals $a$ and $b$?

XXIV Olympians of the People's Republic of Poland (1975)

F495. Three immiscible liquids with densities $\rho_1$, $\rho_2$ and $\rho_3$ fill a closed thin cylindrical tube, forming a ring whose plane is vertical (Fig. 2). A liquid with a density of $\rho_1$ fills an arc of a ring with an angle of $\alpha_1$, and a liquid with a density of $\rho_2$ fills an arc with an angle of $\alpha_2$. What angle $\alpha$ forms with the vertical radius of the ring drawn to the boundary of these liquids? Superficial effectiveness can be claimed.

F496*. A slightly diverging beam of positive ions with a small raster angle $\alpha$ (Fig. 3) is admitted into the cylindrical capacitor at point $A$. All of them in the beam have the same energy. Those onns whose velocity vector at point $A$ is directed perpendicular to $AO$, moves along a circle of radius $|AO|=r_0$, concentric with the capacitor plates. Prove that a beam of onns will be focused at a point $B$ such that $\widehat{AOB} = \pi \sqrt{2}$. Determine the maximum beam width.

F497. A disk of radius $r$ rotating with angular speed $\omega$ is thrown with speed $v$ at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. The plane of the disk remains vertical during its movement. Find the radius of curvature of the trajectory of the highest point of the disk at the moment when the disk reaches its maximum height of rise.
