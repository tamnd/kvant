Task 2. Determine the elongation of the steel stepped rod (Fig. 4, a), to which axial forces $\vec{F}_1$ and $\vec{F}_2$ ($|\vec{F}_1| = 20\text{kH}, |\vec{F}_2| = 30\text{kH}$) are applied. The lengths of the rod sections are equal to $l_1 = 1\text{m}$ and $l_2 = 2\text{m}$, the cross sections are equal to the corresponding $S_1 = 1\text{cm}^2$ and $S_2 = 2\text{cm}^2$,_module $I\text{Онеа} E = 2 \cdot 10^5\text{MH/m}^2$. The rod is not weightless.

Obviously, the total elongation of the stepped rod is equal to the sum of the elongations of its sections:
$$\Delta l = \Delta l_1 + \Delta l_2.$$
To determine $\Delta l_1$ and $\Delta l_2$, we find the longitudinal elastic forces $\vec{N}_1$ and $\vec{N}_2$, which arose, respectively, in the lower and upper sections of the rod.

Calculate the rod by plane $I$ and write the conditions of the cut off part of the rod in projections onto the axis $X$ (Fig. 4, b):
$$N_1 + F_1 = 0.$$
From here
$$N_1 = -F_1 = |\vec{F}_1| = 20\text{kH}.$$
Let's find $\vec{N}_2$ in the same way. Let us draw the section plane $II$ and write the relation

corresponding to the equilibrium condition for the cut-off part (Fig. 4, e):
$$N_2 + F_1 + F_2 = 0,$$
or
$$N_2 = -F_1 - F_2 = |\vec{F}_1| - |\vec{F}_2| = -10\text{kH}.$$
The negative sign of $y$ $N_2$ indicates that the longitudinal force $\vec{N}_2$ is directed downward; therefore, in the upper section the rod is said.

According to Hooke's law,
$$\Delta l_1 = \frac{N_1 l_1}{ES_1}, \quad \Delta l_2 = \frac{N_2 l_2}{ES_2},$$
And
$$\Delta l = \Delta l_1 + \Delta l_2 = \frac{1}{E} \left( \frac{N_1 l_1}{S_1} + \frac{N_2 l_2}{S_2} \right) = 0,5 \cdot 10^{-3}\text{m} = 0,5\text{mm}.$$
Problem 3. A load of mass $m = 2$ t supporting two identical round steel rods, hingedly connected at node $A$ and making an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with the vertical (Fig. 5). Determine the cross-sectional diameter of each rod if we allow stress for steel $\sigma_{\text{доп}} = 160\text{MH/m}^2$.

Consider node $A$. Three forces act on it: the weight of the load $\vec{P}$ ($|\vec{P}| = m|\vec{g}|$) and the reaction forces of the rods $\vec{R}_1$ and $\vec{R}_2$. Let us write down the conditions of the node $A$ in projections on the coordinate axes $X$ and $Y$:
$$-|\vec{R}_1| \sin \alpha + |\vec{R}_2| \sin \alpha = 0,$$
$$|\vec{R}_1| \cos \alpha + |\vec{R}_2| \cos \alpha - m|\vec{g}| = 0.$$
From here
$$|\vec{R}_1| = |\vec{R}_2| = |\vec{R}| = \frac{m|\vec{g}|}{2\cos \alpha}.$$
