The astrolabe - “grabbing the stars” - has faithfully served human nature for more than one and a half thousand years, helping astronomers and navigators. The design of this device is based on the properties of the so-called stereographic projection. You can read more about this on p. 50.

The astrolabe you see in this photograph was made in 1532 by the Nuremberg master G. Hartmann. It is made of gilded brass and, like many antique devices, is a real work of art. However, it was absolutely no less important to use it.
