Active excadron

(see Quantum No. 11)

The minimum active excadron from the heel of the knights is depicted at figure 1 a (initial position 1). In fact, three and four horses are not enough (see this for yourself - it’s not that simple). Five knights from position 1 can be transferred to positions 5a and 10. The difference between positions 1 and 10 is that one of them is shifted relative to the other by one vertical square. It is obvious that an infinite number of such transitions can be sequentially carried out in the vertical direction. The difference between positions 1 and 5a is that one of them is shifted relative to the other by one cell vertically, one cell horizontally and rotated by 180°. Thus, the polygon for moving the excadron from the heel of the knights, occupying position 1, is the entire infinite chessboard.
