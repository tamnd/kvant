⟦folio 7⟧

Corner reflectors are a device that is used to reflect radar or optical beams in the opposite direction to the original one. Until recently, optical corner reflectors were used only as modest road warning devices - on bicycles, automatic vehicles, trains, and road signs. They were called reflectors *). Currently, corner reflectors are widely used: they are used in navigational radar, meteorology, and space research.

How does a corner reflector “work”?

Its device is very simple. figure 1 shows various types

*) “Kataphot” (Greek) - a light reflector facing the source.

Corner reflectors. This can be a triangular prism, the two side faces of which are mutually perpendicular (Fig. 1, a). These faces (ОAA'О' and OBB'O') are coated on the inside with a thin layer of materials that reflect light well. The corner reflector can be a tetrahedral paramid (Fig. 1, b), three mutually perpendicular faces of which (AOB, BOC and AOC) are silver-plated. Radar corner reflectors are three mutually perpendicular flat plates (Fig. 1, ​​c), made of materials that reflect radio waves well.

As you can see, a corner reflector can be schematically represented as a system of mutually perpendicular flat mirrors. To better understand the principle of operation of a reflector, consider how the path of light rays of a system of two such mirrors ($3_1$ and $3_2$ to figure 2) changes.

Ray S, incident on mirrors $3_1$ at an angle $\phi_1$, is reflected from it (at point A), hits mirrors $3_2$ at an angle $\phi_2$ and is reflected from it (at point B). It is easy to see that as a result of reflections, the beam “turns” by an angle $2\phi_1 + 2\phi_2 = 180^\circ$, that is, the reflected beam is parallel to the incident beam and is directed in the opposite direction.

The role of two such mirrors is performed in the reflector-prism (Fig. 1, a) faces OAA'O' and OBB'O'. A ray of light incident on the “hypotenuse” face BAA'B', after reflection in the prism, comes out in the opposite direction. Note that filling the ray on the hypotenuse face has no effect.
