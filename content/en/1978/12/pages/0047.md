The elastic forces $\vec{N}$ arising in each rod are equal in magnitude to the reaction forces:

$$|\vec{N}| = |\vec{R}| = \frac{m |g|}{2 \cos \alpha}$$

From the condition of the strength of the rods it follows that

$$\sigma = \frac{|\vec{N}|}{S} \leq \sigma_{\text{доп}}$$

or

$$S = \frac{\pi d^2}{4} \geq \frac{|\vec{N}|}{\sigma_{\text{доп}}} = \frac{m |g|}{2 \sigma_{\text{доп}} \cos \alpha}$$

Then the diameter of each rod

$$d = \sqrt{\frac{4S}{\pi}} \geq \sqrt{\frac{2m |g|}{\pi \sigma_{\text{доп}} \cos \alpha}} = 1,25 \cdot 10^{-2} \text{m} = 1,25 \text{ см.}$$

We accept $d = 1,25 \text{ см.}$.

In conclusion, let us consider the case when statics problems cannot be solved at all if the bodies included in the systems are considered to be absolutely solid. See figure 6 for examples of three such systems. In the case of a rod fixed at both ends (Fig. 6, a), to determine two unknown reaction forces $\vec{R}_A$ and $\vec{R}_B$, only one condition can be written (in projections onto the $X$ axis).

In a system consisting of three rods (Fig. 6, b), to determine the three reaction forces ($\vec{R}_1, \vec{R}_2$ and $\vec{R}_3$), only two equilibrium conditions can be written (in projections on the axes $X$ and $Y$). For the system shown in figure 6, b, to determine three unknown forces ($\vec{R}_1, \vec{R}_2$ and $\vec{R}_3$), only two equilibrium conditions can also be written (the projections of all forces on the vertical axis being equal to zero and the moments of all forces being equal to zero relative to, for example, the point $A$).

Such systems are usually called statically indeterminate systems. How can we find all the unknown reaction forces in these cases? It turns out that it is possible to construct additional equations based on the condition of compatibility of deformations.

Let us show how such problems are solved.

Problem 4. In the urine $C$, located on the axis of a cylindrical rod fixed at both ends (Fig. 6, a), to determine two unknown reaction forces $\vec{R}_A$ and $\vec{R}_B$.

Let us write the equilibrium equation of the rod in projections onto the $X$ axis:

$$R_A + R_B + F = 0.$$

To determine two unknowns ($R_A$ and $R_B$), one equation is not enough. Let's create another equation - the equation of compatibility of deformations (displacements).
