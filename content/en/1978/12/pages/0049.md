About visa preparation offices

Since 1969, at higher educational institutions, in accordance with the resolution of the Central Committee of the CPSU and the Council of Ministers of the USSR, preparatory departments have been opened for working and rural youth, demobilized soldiers of the Soviet Army.

Below we publish an article by the head of the preparatory department of the Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering (MIEM) V. Tonyan, talking about the work of this department.

It is well known that the number of high school graduates who become direct participants in the labor process is growing every year. Many graduates are admitted to serve in the Armed Forces of the USSR.

It is quite obvious that several years of work in production or military service, although they shape the character of a young man and prepare him for a conscious choice of future specialty, nevertheless lead to a decrease in the level of knowledge he received at school. There is nothing terrible and, even more so, shameful in this - everyone knows that without systematic repetition, without constant training, many facts are forgotten, skills and abilities are weakened and even completely lost.

Pozmot, sometimes at the entrance exams of universities there were people of completely different social status: one of them had already passed the prepared order, carefully chosen a specialty, often already worked in this specialty, but have a gap in knowledge, others - growing up schoolchildren - have fresh, rehearsed knowledge, but, as a rule, not too deep ideas about the selected information. Consequently, schoolchildren actually had significant advantages over the first category of applicants.

This situation required measures to provide real assistance in entering universities for young producers drawn from among the advanced workers, collective farmers and demobilized members of the Armed Forces of the USSR. Work and collective farmers sent to the preparatory department must work for at least one year, that is, a full 12 months before the start of classes. Those sent to the preparatory department submit an application, a referral to the preparatory department (signed by the head of the enterprise or the commander of a military unit), a document on secondary education (in the signature form), a character reference, 6 factors (a simmock without a headdress, measuring 3x4 cm²) and a medical certificate of health (form No. 286).

A demobilized person who does not have a referral from the command of a military unit and ends up working at the same enterprise where he worked before being recognized in the army can enter the preparatory department in the direction of this enterprise if his total work experience at this enterprise is at least one year in total - before military service and after demobilization.

Very often you can come across the idea that it is just as difficult to enter the preparatory department as universities, and that applicants are required to know the extent of the university entrance exam program. This is completely untrue. The very existence of preparatory departments presupposes that persons who have certain gaps in knowledge are accepted, and the task of the preparatory departments is precisely to bridge these gaps, help students restore lost knowledge, and also acquire new material necessary for further successful university studies.

Admission to the preparatory department is carried out through an individual oral interview, the whole of which is not only to clarify the volume of knowledge of the incoming potential, the general level of development, and to identify the degree of consciousness of the given type chosen by him. The peculiarity of this is a more or less close acquaintance with the special practical work identified by him in the chosen specialization. The interview is conducted in two subjects from among those studied at the preparatory department of this institute. At MIEM, for example, such subjects are mathematics and physics.
