MAGIC DODECAHEDRON

If from the set of numbers 1, 2,...,25 we remove the numbers 1, 7, 13, 19, 25, then the remaining twenty numbers can be placed at the twenty vertices of the dodecahedron so that the sums of the numbers at the vertices of each face are the same. However, from such arrangements you see on the cover. It is curious that with this arrangement there are still five vertices that give the same magical sum of 65.

The development and the model show twenty vertices of the pasterns passing through such pasterns, with each five forming a regular pentagon, the plane of which is parallel to a certain face of the dodecahedron. But that's not all. Our arrangement of numbers also has other patterns. Try to find them yourself.

E. Krivoshcheev
