⟦folio 22⟧

Problem
quantum

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the Newstein school curriculum.
The more difficult problems are marked with an asterisk.
Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than February 1, 1979 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant».
After the address, write on the envelope the numbers of the problems for which you are sending solutions (for example, “M536, M537”, or “F548”). Solutions to problems in each of the subjects (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems should be sent in separate envelopes. Also send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope place: Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics or “... a new problem in mathematics”). After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it. Of course, not all problems are published for the first time. Problems M537 - M540 were proposed at the XX International Mathematical Olympiad (see p. 55).

M536. a) Prove that any rectangle of size $m \times 2n$ cells can be tiled with two layers of dominoes (tiles $1 \times 2$ cells) so that each tile of the upper layer rests on two different tiles of the lower layer.
6) A rectangle measuring $2k \times 2n$ cells is already covered with one layer of dominoes. Prove that it can be tiled with a second layer so that the same condition is satisfied (i.e., so that tiles of different words do not coincide).

M537. The circle touches the inner circle of the equilateral triangle $ABC$, as well as the equal sides $AB$ and $AC$ of this triangle at points $P$, $Q$ respectively. Prove that the midpoint of the segment $PQ$ is the center of the circle circumscribed by triangle $ABC$.

M538. The set of all natural numbers is the union of two disjoint subsets $\{f(1), f(2), \dots, f(n), \dots\}$, $\{g(1), g(2), \dots, g(n), \dots\}$, where $f(1) < f(2) < \dots < f(n) < \dots, g(1) < g(2) < \dots < g(n)$ and $g(n) = f(f(n)) + 1$ for all $n \geqslant 1$. Identify $f(240)$.

M539. Let $P$ be a given point inside a given sphere and $A, B, C$ be arbitrary three points of this sphere and let the segments $PA, PB, PC$ be mutually perpendicular. Let $Q$ be the vertex of the parallelepid defined by the segments $PA, PB$ and $PC$, diagonally opposite to $P$. Determine the geometric location of points $Q$.

M540.* The international society consists of representatives from six different countries. The list of members of the society consists of 1978 names, numbered 1, 2, ..., 1978.

Prove that there is at least one member of the society whose number is equal to the sum of the numbers of two members from his country or double the number of some member from his country.

F548. The jet mass cart $m$ describes a dead loop along a vertical circular road of radius $R$ with a constant linear speed $v$. How much work is done by the friction force when moving the cart from the lowest position to the highest one? The friction coefficient between the cart and the road is $\mu$.

O. Savchenko

F549. Two balls with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ are connected by a stiffening spring $k$; the spring is located horizontally and is not deformed. The balls are simultaneously given velocities $v_1$ and $v_2$, as shown in figure 1, with $|v_1| = |v_2| = v$. Find the maximum lifting height of the system and the maximum deformation of the spring.

F550. Two washers are put on a rough copper cone (covered with a fine notch, like a file): an aluminum one with a radius of $r=1$ cm and an iron one with a radius of $R=3$ cm. The distance between the washers is $a=6$ cm. How much will this distance change if the cone and washers are heated by $\Delta t=200$ K? The thermal coefficients of linear expansion of copper, aluminum and iron are $\beta_1=2,5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$, $\beta_2=1,7 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$ and $\beta_3=10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$, respectively. The cone is located vertically, with the top apex.

G. Kotkin

F551. The mass $m$ is suspended from two springs with stiffness $k_1$ and $k_2$ using a thread and a block (Fig. 2). Find the period of small oscillations of the load. The thread and the block are considered weightless.

I. Slovodechkiy
