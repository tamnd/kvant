About visa preparation offices

Since 1969, at higher educational institutions, in accordance with the resolution of the Central Committee of the CPSU and the Council of Ministers of the USSR, preparatory departments have been opened for working and rural youth, demobilized soldiers of the Soviet Army.

Below we publish an article by the head of the preparatory department of the Moscow Institute of Electronic Engineering (MIEM) V. Tonyan, talking about the work of this department.

It is well known that the number of high school graduates who become direct participants in the labor process is growing every year. Many graduates are admitted to serve in the Armed Forces of the USSR.

It is quite obvious that several years of work in production or military service, although they shape the character of a young man and prepare him for a conscious choice of future specialty, nevertheless lead to a decrease in the level of knowledge he received at school. There is nothing terrible and, even more so, shameful in this - everyone knows that without systematic repetition, without constant training, many facts are forgotten, skills and abilities are weakened and even completely lost.

Pozmot, sometimes at the entrance exams of universities there were people of completely different social status: one of them had already passed the prepared order, carefully chosen a specialty, often already worked in this specialty, but have a gap in knowledge, others - growing up schoolchildren - have fresh, rehearsed knowledge, but, as a rule, not too deep ideas about the selected information. Consequently, schoolchildren actually had significant advantages over the first category of applicants.

This situation required measures to provide real assistance in entering universities for young producers drawn from among the advanced workers, collective farmers and demobilized members of the Armed Forces of the USSR. Work and collective farmers sent to the preparatory department must work for at least one year, that is, a full 12 months before the start of classes. Those sent to the preparatory department submit an application, a referral to the preparatory department (signed by the head of the enterprise or the commander of a military unit), a document on secondary education (in the signature form), a character reference, 6 factors (a simmock without a headdress, measuring 3x4 cm²) and a medical certificate of health (form No. 286).

A demobilized person who does not have a referral from the command of a military unit and ends up working at the same enterprise where he worked before being recognized in the army can enter the preparatory department in the direction of this enterprise if his total work experience at this enterprise is at least one year in total - before military service and after demobilization.

Very often you can come across the idea that it is just as difficult to enter the preparatory department as universities, and that applicants are required to know the extent of the university entrance exam program. This is completely untrue. The very existence of preparatory departments presupposes that persons who have certain gaps in knowledge are accepted, and the task of the preparatory departments is precisely to bridge these gaps, help students restore lost knowledge, and also acquire new material necessary for further successful university studies.

Admission to the preparatory department is carried out through an individual oral interview, the whole of which is not only to clarify the volume of knowledge of the incoming potential, the general level of development, and to identify the degree of consciousness of the given type chosen by him. The peculiarity of this is a more or less close acquaintance with the special practical work identified by him in the chosen specialization. The interview is conducted in two subjects from among those studied at the preparatory department of this institute. At MIEM, for example, such subjects are mathematics and physics.

Let's tell you in more detail about the preparatory department at our institute.

At MIEM, classes are held at the preparatory department of production with a break from production for eight months - from December 1 to the end of June. The form of education is full-time only. The busy system is as close as possible to the body system: lectures are given for dryers, practical classes are conducted; They write tests, do tests, and colloquisms. When carrying out control activities, modern computer technology, specially designed for this purpose, is widely used.

Education at the preparatory department is carried out according to specially developed programs that provide for a more in-depth, comprehensive study of the school course.

All dryers in the department enjoy the same rights as students of the institute. They receive a stipend - the same as first-year students, they use the institute's laboratories, the library, and they are entitled to benefits for travel by rail and when flying on airplanes.

Classes are held in the buildings of the institute. The department studies mathematics, physics, Russian language and literature, and also includes physical education classes.

Students of our preparatory department are, one might say, “zero” year students and live a full student life. And the student years are not only years of hard work in the classroom, laboratories, and libraries, but also a time of interesting things, a time of perky student songs, and the romance of the truss squads. During the summer and winter holidays, most of the dryers, together with the students of the institute, go to the institute’s sports and recreational camp, located on the picturesque shore of the Ruza reservoir. The camp hosts training sessions for athletes - members of the institute's national teams.

At the end of the training, the dryers will take final exams. These exams are aimed at determining the level of assimilation by the students of the completed program, and determining how ready they are for further study at the university. Exams are fundamentally different from entrance exams, primarily in that they are not competitive in nature: everyone who passes the final exams is enrolled in the institute.

On the other hand, in many problems the most sensible thing to do is to draw up equations. And here a common “tactical” mistake is often encountered: the construction is often taken without any thought as an unknown obligatory thing that is sought in the problem, although this is far from always reasonable.

Let us consider, as an example, a problem—one of the most disliked type constructions—on “pools”:

Three rubles are connected to the set. Through the first ruble the complovan can be filled 5 hours faster than through the second, and through the first and second together - three times faster than through the third. How long will it take to fill the layout when all rubbish are working simultaneously, if the throughput of the first rubbish is 80% higher than that of the third?

Let us denote by x, y, z the throughput capacities of each of the tribes, measured, for example, in m³/h. In order to determine the time of filling the pit, we need to know its volume. Let us denote this volume by V. To harmonize the units of measurement, we assume, of course, that the volume is also measured in m³. Then rubles can fill the pit, respectively, for $\frac{V}{x}$, $\frac{V}{y}$ and $\frac{V}{z}$ and we get the correspondence:

$$\frac{V}{x} = \frac{V}{y} - 5; \quad 3 \frac{V}{x+y} = \frac{V}{z}, \quad x = 1, 8z.$$

We got 3 equations with 4 unknowns. We will solve the resulting system of equations, not paying attention to the fact that the volume V is unknown to us. Expressing x and y in terms of z (from the 3rd and 2nd equations), and then substituting the resulting x and y values ​​into the first equation, we get the equation

$$\frac{V}{1,8z} = \frac{V}{1,2z} - 5,$$

or $\frac{V}{z} = 18$.

Well, how can you find out the filling time now? It is clear that in 1 hour, three rubles together, $(x+y+z)$ m³ arrives and, therefore, it will take

$$\frac{V}{x+y+z} = \frac{V}{4z} = \frac{9}{2}(ч).$$

Significant emphasis on property equations of equations and inequalities. In this case, the main emphasis is not on testing calculation and transformation techniques or mastering cunning solution techniques, but on the conscious application of known properties of functions, which should be included in the base of values ​​as a mandatory minimum.

Thus, to solve the equation $|34x^2 - 7x - 22| = -2$ it is not at all necessary to find out (in accordance with the definition of the module) for which x the expression $34x^2 - 7x - 22$ is positive or negative, although this is where, as a rule, well-trained applicants begin solving the solution for entrance exams. From the well-known property of the modulus it immediately follows that this equation has no solutions - after all, the model of a number is a non-negative number!

When solving the inequality $|\sqrt{x-2} - 1| > -1$ if the inequality is not true, the part is declared that it holds for any x. In fact, it holds for any x included in its domain of definition, i.e. for $x \geq 2$. Equation and inequalities

$$\sqrt{x-2} + 4 = 0, \quad \sqrt{x-3} + \sqrt{2-x} \leq 2,$$

$$3 \sin^2 x + \sin^3 x < 100,$$

$$4 \cos^2 x + \cos^3 x = 5$$

can be solved immediately if you know well and correctly use the properties of the functions included in them.

The first equation has no solutions, since the argumentative square root, by its definition, cannot be a negative number. The first equation has no solutions, but for a different reason - its domain of definition does not contain a single value of the variable: in fact, the first term makes sense at $x \geq 3$, and the second - at $x \leq 2$, and these two conditions are not simultaneously satisfied. To solve the second inequality, it is enough to say that sine cannot be greater than 1, so the left-hand side cannot be greater than 4. Therefore, this inequality holds for any x. As for the last equation, it can be solved by similar reasoning, but the end of the perfect is different: the fact is that $4 \cos^2 x \leq 4, \cos^3 x \leq 1$, and therefore the right side can be equal to 5 only for $x = 1$. Therefore, this equation is equivalent to the equation $\cos x = 1$, i.e., it is satisfied at $x = 2\pi k$, where $k$ is any integer.
