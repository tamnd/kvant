⟦folio 37⟧

Exercise

1. Multi-colored dots. Inside the rectangle, there are multi-colored dots at the grid nodes (see figure). Connect points of the same color with non-intersecting broken lines of the same length so that the links of the broken line are parallel to the sides of the rectangle, and the length of the broken line is maximum.

2. Ten digits. a) Write the numbers from 0 to 9 in the squares so that you get three correct addition examples.

6) Write the numbers from 0 to 9 in the circles so that you get two correct examples of multiplication.

3. A square is made up of $(n+1)^2$ square cells. Some cells are painted black. Prove that it is always possible to color $3n$ cells in such a way that for any deletion of $n-1$ columns and $n-1$ rows of the square, at least one white cell remains.

4. At the Piner meeting, it turned out that every boy was familiar with $n$ girls and every girl was familiar with $n$ boys. Prove that the same number of girls and boys arrived at the gathering.
