Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

Below we publish options for entrance written exams in mathematics at the humanities faculties of Moscow State University in 1978.

Mathematics

Department of Political Economy, Faculty of Economics

1. Solve the equation
$$\log_{(2-2x^2)}(2-x^2-x^4) =
= 2 - \frac{1}{\log_4(2-2x^2)}.$$

2. In the plane, given is a square $ABCD$ (with sequentially designated vertices) and a point $O$. It is known that
$$|OA| = |OC| = 10, \quad |OD| = 6\sqrt{2}$$
the side length of the square does not exceed 3. Find the area of ​​the square. Where is the point $O$ located – outside or inside the square?
3. Find all values ​​of the parameter $c$ for which the system
$$\begin{cases} -4x + cy = 1 + c, \\ (6 + c)x + 2y = 3 + c \end{cases}$$
has no solution.
4. A certain number of wires have been made. If you wind it on coils that can hold 800 m of wires, then one coil will not be wound completely. If you use only coils that can accommodate 900 m of wires, then you will come across 3 fewer such coils, and one coil will again not be completely wound. If you wind the wiring only on coils that can handle 1100 m, then you will find 6 fewer such coils, but all the coils will be completely wound. How many meters of wiring were made?
5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$, for each of which the function
$$y = 5ax - \sin 8x - a \cdot \sin 3x - 3x$$
increases and has no critical points on the entire line.

Department of Planning and Economic Cybernetics, Faculty of Economics
1. Find all roots of the equation
$$|x^2 + 2x - 4| + 2x + 6 = 0,$$
satisfying the inequality $x + \sqrt{18} < 1$.
2. In the quadrilateral $ABCD$, the angle at vertex $B$ has magnitudes $90^\circ$, and the diagonal $BD$ is the bisector of this angle. It is known that
$$\frac{|AB|}{2} = \frac{|BD|}{4\sqrt{2}}.$$
Find the cosine of the angle between the vectors $BC$ and $AD$.
3. Solve the equation
$$\log_3(x - \frac{3}{x}) = \frac{1}{\log_4(3x)} + 3.$$
4. There are three alloys. The first contains 45% tin and 55% lead, the second contains 10% altitude, 40% tin and 50% lead, the third contains 30% altitude and 70% lead. From them it is necessary to create a new alloy containing 15% height. What is the highest and highest percentage of lead that this new alloy can contain?
5. Find the set of all real numbers $b$, for each of which the function
$$f(x) = 16(b+1)\sin x - \sin 2x - (16b^2 + 32b - 10)x$$
is decreasing on the entire number line and has no critical points.

2. The diagonal $BD$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ is the diameter of the circle circumscribed about this quadrilateral. Calculate the length of the diagonal $AC$ if $|BD| = 2$, $|AB| = 1$, $ABD : DBC = 4 : 3$.
3. Pedestrian, velichispedikt and mosiklist move along the highway in one direction at constant speeds. At the moment when the mighty leaf reached its size, the pedestrian was 3 km behind them. By how many kilometers did Velichispedict overtake the pedestrian code at the moment when the pedestrian code of the real mosiklist?
4. Find the set of all such pairs of real numbers $(a, b)$ such that for each of them, for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$,
$$a(\cos x - 1) + b^2 = \cos (ax + b^2) - 1.$$
