Solutions to VZMSH problems

This article will provide a solution to some problems from the introductory work to the VZMSH in 1978 («Kvant», 1978, No. 1).

3. What values ​​can a take if
$$|a + b| \leq 1 \quad \text{и} \quad |a - 3b| \leq 2$$
Answer: $-\frac{5}{4} \leq a \leq \frac{5}{4}$.

The easiest way to solve this problem is graphically. Let us take a coordinate system on the plane and find a region consisting of points $(a; b)$ that satisfy the inequality. It is easy to report that this is a parallelogram with vertices $(\frac{5}{4}; -\frac{1}{4}), (\frac{1}{4}; \frac{3}{4}), (-\frac{5}{4}; \frac{1}{4})$ and $(-1/4; -3/4)$.

4. On a given plane there is a square with a center at point O. At this point there is a projector that illuminates a 90° corner of the vertex. At the initial moment, the projector illuminates a corner whose sides pass through the two vertices of the square. Then the projector is rotated around point O. At what point in time will the area of ​​the illuminated part of the square be greater: when the projector rotates 45° or 72°?

The area of ​​the illuminated part is the same all the time and is a quarter of the area of ​​the square. Indeed, the sides of an angle of 90° with a vertex in the center of the square, together with their continuations, divide the square into 4 congested parts, turning into each other when turning by 90°, 180°, 270°.

5. Ivan Aleksandrovich Khlestakov was placed under control and 30,000 couriers were sent within three days. The following is known: if twice as many couriers were sent on the first day, then the total number of couriers would be the fourth power of the number on which more couriers were sent on the third day than on the second. How many couriers were sent to Khlestakov each day? (Give all possible answers.)

Let them send x on the first day, y on the second, and z couriers on the third. It follows from the condition that
$$2x + y + z = (z - y)^4.$$
On the other hand, it is obvious that
$$30 000 \leq 2x + y + z \leq 60 000,$$
where
$$30 000 \leq (z - y)^4 \leq 60 000.$$
By counting we are convinced that in this interval lie only
$$14^4 = 38 616 \quad \text{и} \quad 15^4 = 50 625.$$
In each of these cases it is easy to answer:
$$\begin{cases} x = 8 616, \\ y = 10 685, \\ z = 10 699 \end{cases} \quad \begin{cases} x = 20 625, \\ y = 4 680, \\ z = 4 695. \end{cases}$$

7. Find all pairs of integers (x; y) satisfying the equation
$$x^2 - xy - 2y^2 = 18.$$
The left side can be expanded:
$$(x + y)(x - 2y) = 18.$$
Note that the difference between the factors on the left side is 3. So, 18 must be factored into 2 factors, the difference of which is divisible by 3. This can be done in four ways, each of which gives its own answer:
$$18 = 3 \cdot 6, \quad 18 = (-3) \cdot (-6),$$
$$18 = 6 \cdot 3, \quad 18 = (-6) \cdot (-3),$$
$$\begin{cases} x = 4 \\ y = -1 \end{cases} \quad \begin{cases} x = -4 \\ y = 1 \end{cases} \quad \begin{cases} x = 5 \\ y = 1 \end{cases} \quad \begin{cases} x = -5 \\ y = -1. \end{cases}$$
