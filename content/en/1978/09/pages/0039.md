F533. A dumbbell of length $l$ stands in the corner formed by a smooth plane (Fig. 1). The bottom ball of the dumbbell is moved horizontally for a very small distance, and the dumbbell begins to move. Find the speed of the lower ball at the moment when the upper ball comes off the vertical plane.

A. Zilberman

F534. Skylab astronauts, using a special radar altimeter, discovered that the ocean surface in the Bermuda Triangle area was 25 meters below the normal level (see, for example, the journal Science and Life, 1976, No. 9). Assuming that this “deflection” can be explained by the presence of a spherical cavity filled with water under the ocean house, estimate the radius of this cavity. The depth of the ocean is $h = 6$ km, the average density of terrestrial rocks is $\rho = 3 \times 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

A. Stasenko

F535. The potential energy of interaction between two identical loaded small balls with charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ is equal to $W_1 = \frac{q_1 q_2}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0 l}$, where $l$ is the distance between the centers of the balls. If the balls are connected with a wire, the charges of the balls will become equal, and the potential energy of interaction of the balls will become equal to $W_2 = \frac{(q_1 + q_2)^2}{16 \pi \varepsilon_0 l}$. Since $(q_1 + q_2)^2 > 4 q_1 q_2$ (at $q_1 \neq q_2$), then $W_2 > W_1$, that is, the potential energy of interaction between the two forces increases! Where does the extra energy come from?

F536. The rigid rectangular structure $AOB$ ($AOB = 90^\circ$) rotates around the vertical axis $OO'$, so that $AOO' = \alpha$ (Fig. 2). Using wheels that can slide freely on sides $AO$ and $OB$, a lightweight rod $KE$ of length $2a$ is placed on the structure. A small massive ball is attached to the middle of the rod. At what angular speed of rotation of the structure will the rod take a horizontal position?

S. Krotov

F537. By polishing a metal plate it can be made mirror-like. Estimate what the maximum height of the roughness remaining after polishing can be so that the plate specularly reflects a beam of light with a wavelength of $\lambda = 5 \cdot 10^{-8}$ m, incident on it at an angle of $\alpha = 45^\circ$.
