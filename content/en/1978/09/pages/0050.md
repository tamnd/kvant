Distance
Moshkin's steppes
(from letters to a friend)

Hooray! I came up with my own distance!
(first letter)

Mishka, hello! Last year, when I was in the sixth grade, our teacher Pyotr Ivanovich told us that distance can be called not only what is obtained when changing with a ruler, but in general everything that has properties 1-3 from paragraph 4 of “Geometry 6”.

I decided to come up with my own distance. I remembered the “teλεphone model”, with the help of which Pyotr Ivanovich proved to us that the third property of distance cannot be derived from the first two *). I remembered and thought: when I want to call someone, I pick up the phone,

I dialed the number and they were already talking. This means that the “telephone distance” from me to everyone I call is the same, say, 1 minute. The minute it takes to dial a phone number. But, of course, I don’t call myself: when I ask, I answer right away. Therefore, the distance from me to me is zero! Others, of course, in a similar case will serve in the same way as me. So it occurred to me: consider the distance between any two of my planes equal to zero if these planes coincide, and equal to one if they are different (Fig.1).

Is my “distance” a distance? Does it have the right properties?
