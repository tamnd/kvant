⟦folio 52⟧

In this issue, after the summer holidays, we are resuming the traditional section “Applicant's Workshop”.

Readers will find in it articles that explain the most important and complex issues of school courses in mathematics and physics, and provide future applicants with characteristic, typical mistakes. The presentation of theoretical principles, as a rule, is accompanied by an analysis of problems taken from the practice of admission examinations to higher educational institutions.

Of course, the articles “Applicant's Practicum” cannot replace school textbooks. Yes, they don’t have such a goal: these materials are designed for those who have already mastered the facts contained in the textbook. When outlining the topics of the articles, the editors used the experience of meeting exams and took into account the suggestions contained in letters from readers.

In addition to the materials that readers will find in future issues of the magazine, when preparing for exams, we recommend that you familiarize yourself with the articles published in previous issues. Here is a thematic list of these articles, starting with the 1976-1977 academic year *).
