Active excadron

A group of knights placed on an infinite chessboard is called a squadron if the knights of this group can successively make an infinite number of moves so that after each move each of them is protected by at least one knight of the same group. Obviously, a squadron cannot consist of less than three horses. In fact, in the squadron, in addition to the knight making the move, there must be a knight that protects it before making the move and after making the move. Let us remember that a chess knight changes the color of the square it occupies during its move; it can be argued that both mentioned functions cannot be performed by one knight. A squadron is active if, moving across the chessboard, it can occupy one of its knights on any predetermined square. Find the minimum active excadron.
