1. A block of mass $m$, the dimensions of which are shown in figure 9, stands on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\alpha$. On the block on

the force $\vec{F}$, parallel to the inclined plane, begins to act. At what absccit value of this force will the block topple over?
It is known that the block will not slide off the inclined plane.

2. How much work must be expended to rotate a sled with a load (total mass $m=30$ kg) up a hill $H=10$ m high? The angle of inclination of the slide to the horizon is $\alpha=30^\circ$, and the friction coefficient between the sled and the slide decreases linearly along the path from $\mu_1=0,5$ at the bottom to $\mu_2=0,1$ at the top.

3. A bag of flour slides without initial speed from a height of $H=2$ m along a board inclined at an angle of $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the horizontal. After descent, the bag lands on a horizontal surface. The friction coefficient of the bag on the board and horizontal surface is $\mu=0,5$. At what distance from the end will the bag stop?
