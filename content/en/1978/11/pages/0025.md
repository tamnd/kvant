M489. Given three numbers a, b, c. Let us construct three sequences (aₙ), (bₙ), (cₙ), for which a₁ = a, b₁ = b, c₁ = c and

$$a_{n+1} = \frac{b_n + c_n}{2},$$
$$b_{n+1} = \frac{c_n + a_n}{2},$$
$$c_{n+1} = \frac{a_n + b_n}{2}$$

*) For the solution to problem M486, see the article by L. Kurlyandik “High degrees” (p. 13); the solution to problem M488 will be published later.

As a guiding message, let us consider together the numbers a₁, b₁, c₁ points A₁, B₁, C₁, about which we first assume that they do not lie on the same straight line. The numbers aₙ+1, bₙ+1, cₙ+1 are associated with the midpoints Aₙ+1, Bₙ+1, Cₙ+1 of the corresponding sides of the triangle AₙBₙCₙ. We will get three sequences of points on the plane. It is easy to understand that the median segments of the triangle A₁B₁C₁ lying inside ΔA₂B₂C₂ are medians in ΔA₂B₂C₂. Therefore, the intersection points of the medians of both three triangles coincide. From here it is clear that if, together with the original problem, we solve a similar problem for points on the plane, then the corresponding one is essentially assumed and coincides with the point of intersection of the medians of the original triangle A₁B₁C₁ (and all subsequent triangles). This point is the center of gravity of the system of three starting points.
