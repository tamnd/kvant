Maximum friction force value
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 1 max}}| = \mu M_1 |\vec{g}| = 7 \text{ H} < |\vec{T}_1| = 10 \text{ H},$$
This means that the friction force alone cannot balance the block. As soon as it reaches its maximum value, the second thread will stretch—a tension force $\vec{T}_2$ will appear.

The first block will end if
$$|\vec{T}_1| = |\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 1}}| + |\vec{T}_2|,$$
Where
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 1}}| = |\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 1 max}}| = 7 \text{ H}.$$
From here
$$|\vec{T}_2| = |\vec{T}_1| - |\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 1 max}}| = 3 \text{ H}.$$
Two forces act on the second block: the thread tension force $\vec{T}_2$ and the friction force $\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 2}}$ (Fig. 3). Since
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 2 max}}| = \mu M_2 |\vec{g}| = 10 \text{ H} > |\vec{T}_2| = 3 \text{ H},$$
the frictional force $\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 2}}$ can balance the force $\vec{T}_2$:
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. 2}}| = |\vec{T}_2| = 3 \text{ H}.$$
Problem 2. The block lies on the board. We lift the board by one edge. How does the absolute value of the friction force acting on the block depend on the surface of the board $\alpha$ (Fig. 4)? Friction coefficient between

between a block and a board $\mu$, the mass of the block $m$.

At sufficiently small angles of inclination, the block will end on the board.

In this case, the static friction force $\vec{F}_{\text{тр. п.}}$ is balanced by the force $\vec{F}$ - the component of the gravitational force of the block, directed along the inclined plane:

Fig. 4.

$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. п.}}| = |\vec{F}| = m |\vec{g}| \sin \alpha.$$
As the angle $\alpha$ increases, the static friction force increases, and at a certain angle $\alpha_0$ it reaches its maximum value
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. п. max}}| = |\vec{F}_{\text{тр. ск.}}| = \mu |\vec{N}|$$
m |$\vec{g}| \sin \alpha_0 = \mu m |\vec{g}| \cos \alpha_0.$
From here
$$tg \alpha_0 = \mu, \text{ и } |\vec{F}_{\text{тр. п. max}}| = m |\vec{g}| \frac{\mu}{\sqrt{1 + \mu^2}}.$$
With a further increase in the angle $\alpha$ the body slides along the board, and the friction force is the sliding friction force:
$$|\vec{F}_{\text{тр. ск.}}| = \mu |\vec{N}| = \mu m |\vec{g}| \cos \alpha.$$
