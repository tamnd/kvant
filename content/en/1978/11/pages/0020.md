We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the current school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with asterisks.

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than January 1, 1979 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant».
After the address, write on the envelope the numbers of the problems for which you are sending solutions (for example, “M531, M534” or “F543”). Solutions to problems in each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new assignments, should be sent in separate envelopes. Also send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes.
Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope).
Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope put: “Problem «Kvant», new problems in physics” or “...new problems in mathematics”). After formulating the problem, we usually indicate who proposed it. Of course, not all problems are published for the first time.

M533*. A convex polygon is special if some of its three diagonals intersect at one point. Prove that for every special heptagon there is a vertex A that has the following property: for any $\varepsilon > 0$, the vertex A can be shifted to a distance less than $\varepsilon$ (without changing the remaining vertices), so that the resulting heptagon is non-singular.
