Task 3. A rectangular block, the dimensions of which are shown in figure 6, is crushed evenly along the horizontal plane by a rope; the tilt angle of the component $\alpha$ can be changed. The coefficient of friction between the block and the plane is $\mu$. At what value $\alpha_0$ does the block begin to rise?

Let's consider all the forces that act on the block. These are the rope tension force $\vec{T}$, the gravity force $\vec{mg}$, the support reaction force $\vec{N}$ (equal in absolute value to the normal pressure force) and the sliding force $\vec{F}_{\text{тр. ск.}}$ (since the block is moving).
