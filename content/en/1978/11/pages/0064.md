On April 23, Omsk universities held meetings of the remaining sections of the anniversary conference of NOUs. City school students presented another 92 reports on physical, medical, biological, engineering and other topics. Many of the reports were real research; seven reports prepared by members of the circle at the Siberian Autonomous Road Institute were reports on research into the work of engines.

Often schoolchildren try their hand at several clubs at once. For example, E. Bulvko (school No. 112) made one report on the topic “Determination of the main toxic components in the exhaust gases of internal combustion engines using the gas chromatic method,” and together with her friend T. Krivopuzkova - another one, on determining the strontium content in potatoes and plants. The use of fertilizers in agriculture is useful; together with fertilizers, the soil gets into the soil, in particular, solntsium, which accumulate in potato clubs and plants. The strontium content can be determined by a spectral method known to any high school student. How this is done specifically was told and shown by the girls who prepared a report in a circle at the Agricultural Institute.

Where all these guys go next - they will decide for themselves. But you can be sure that this choice, like the choice of some other active members of the scientific society of Omsk, will be conscious.

Olympiad problems

1. Let $f(x) = \frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$. Graph the function
$$y = \lim_{n \to \infty} f\left(\frac{f\left(\dots f(x)\right)\dots}{n}\right)$$
(5 points, 1 team).

2. Let electric charges corresponding to $q_1, q_2, \dots, q_n$ be concentrated at points $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_n$, and let $O$ be some point. In electrostatics vector
$$\vec{d} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} q_i \vec{O}_i$$
is called the dipole moment of the system of charges relative to the point $O$. It is clear that if the sum of the charges is zero, then the dipole moment does not depend on the choice of point $O$ (3 points, 0 team).

3. Let $f(x) = a_0 + a_1x + \dots + a_6x^6$ be some polynomial of the sixth degree. It is known that $f(k) = f(-k)$ is for $k=1, 2, 3$. Prove that $f(x)$ is an even function (3 points, 4 team).

4. How many parts and how should a segment of given length $a$ be divided so that the product of the lengths of all resulting segments is as small as possible? (10 points, 2nd team).

5. Let $m$ and $n$ be natural numbers. Prove that
$$\min \left\{ \sqrt{n}; \sqrt{m} \right\} \leq \sqrt{3} \sqrt{3}$$
(2 points, 0 team).

6. Prove that three products $(1-a)b, (1-b)c, (1-c)a$ composed of positive numbers $a<1, b<1, c<1$ cannot simultaneously be greater than 1/4 (2 points, 3 team).

8. Find the set of points on the plane equidistant from two segments $(-1 \leq x \leq 4, y=0)$ and $(-6 \leq y \leq 2, x=0)$. Consideration of the segment - the distance to the nearest point of the segment (4 points, 7th team).

9. Find the remainder when dividing the polynomial $2x^{123756} + 2x^{27345} + x^{1384} - 3x^{792} + x^{29} - x^7$ by the polynomial $x^2 - 1$ (4 points, 2 team).

10. Graph the function $y=\ln(x+\ln(x+...))$ (7 points, 1 team).

11. Find the volume of the figure formed by the intersection of two circular cylinders of radius $R$ if their axes intersect each other at right angles (6 points, 0 team).

13. A circle is inscribed in an equilateral triangle with area $S$. Angle at the vertex of the triangle $\alpha$. At what $\alpha$ is the area of ​​the inscribed circle maximum? (3 points, 3 team).

14. The tourist climbed the mountain, rested there and went down along the same path. Getting up and resting took a lot of days, and the whole journey took less than two days. Prove that there is a place where the tourist was at the same time of day (3 points, 4 team).

15. Solve equation $(\sqrt{3}-1)\sin 3x+2\sin 2x+2\sin x=0$ (5 points, 6 team).

16. In a straight trapezoid $ABCD$, angles $A$ and $D$ are right, side $AB$ is parallel to $CD$, side lengths $|AB|=a$, $|CD|=b$, $|AD|=c$. On the side $AD$ the point $M$ is taken so that the angle $CMD$ is twice the angle $BMA$. In what ratio does the point $M$ divide the side $AD$? (5 points, 4th team).

17. Solve equation $\left(\frac{9}{4}\right)^y - 3 \cdot \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^y + 2 = 0$ (2 points, 52 team).

18. Three geological horses. The distance to the camp is 13.5 km. How quickly can they get to the camp if the speed of a person is 4 km/h and the speed of a shoad is 6 km/h? (Two people cannot sit on one shoad, horses cannot be allowed without a guide.) (5 points, 6 team).
