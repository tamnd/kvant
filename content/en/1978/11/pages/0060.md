RFMS at Yakut University

Yakutia is a land of diamonds, northern lights and permafrost, frosty winter nights and dazzling summer sun. Six years ago, Ismail Shakhbazovich Aliyev, a candidate of physical and mathematical sciences, a student of academician Anatoly Ivanovich Maltsev, came to this region six years ago. For twenty years he has been working with students, and he finds them... on the school bench.

At first there was a summer physics and mathematics school on the banks of the Great Lena River, in one of the suburbs of the city of Yakutsk. In the summer of 1972, children from more than thirty regions of Yakutia gathered in it. The goal was that they lived in the high school building, rested and worked - a group of YSU students held Olympiads for them, came up with interesting problems.

The winners of the Olympiad at the summer school became the first students of the Republican Physics and Mathematics SchoolOLS (RFMS) at Yakut State University.

The director of the schoolOLS, Ismail Shakhbazovich, told us a lot about his teacher, Anatoly Ivanovits Maltsev, we all loved him very much and called ourselves “Shkimtsy”, which means “the guys from the schoolOLS named after. Maltsev".

The boarding school is located on the university campus. We occupy just one floor of a student dormitory. In the first year of its existence, the school had only three classes: eight, nine and ten. But the first performance at the Republican Mathematical Olympiad brought the FMS teams first place. This success made it clear to those around us that we exist and are needed. Since then, we have been constantly striving to be first.

The newspaper “Youth of Yakutia” publishes daily assignments for correspondence physics, mathematics and chemistry Olympiads, which are invented by the Shkim residents themselves and their mentors. Based on the results of this Olympiad, a new enrollment is underway at the RFMS. All our fymyshats come from the regions, from the Taj naslegs, they are starting life for the first time without their relatives, in an unfamiliar city. Most of them were in the city for the first time. All this, naturally, leads to the fact that they begin to bite. But the education system, the relationship between students and teachers, the rhythm of life in our school is such that by the end of the first month the children are carried away by their studies, sometimes they even forget to send news home.

The most important thing is that in our school children are taught to work independently, think, that is, draw their own conclusions from what they read and hear. The mathematics curriculum includes some of the topics studied at the university. For example, they teach the courses “Number Theory”, “Galois Theory”, “Graph Theory”, “Projective Geometry”, “Combinatorics” and “Mathematical Logic”. There is a scientist working at the school.
