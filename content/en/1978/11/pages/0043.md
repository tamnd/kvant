⟦folio 41⟧

Note that if the abacus has a bone on both sides of the vertical partition, then they should be reset: $1 + \bar{1} = 0$. A little training and you will learn how to use this unusual abacus.

Don't think that the number system I told you about is just mathematical fun. In the USSR and Czechoslovakia, computers were built that operate on this number system. The Soviet car is called “Setun” (named after the river near Moscow). It was created by engineers and mathematicians of the computer center of Moscow University. They were attracted by two features of this number system: the absence of “sign rules” when performing arithmetic operations on numbers and the ease of obtaining the opposite number - the opposite number of a given number is obtained if each digit “1” of a given number is replaced by a “1” and vice versa.

For his invented abacus and a good report on the balanced ternary system, Ano Shakhovsky was elected a full member of the Minor Academy of Sciences.
