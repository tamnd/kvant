Moscow Institute of Radio Engineering, Electronics and Automation

The Moscow Institute of Radio Engineering, Electronics and Automation (MIREA) trains broad-spectrum engineers with research skills in the main specialties of modern radio electronics, automation and systems engineering. MIREA graduates work at enterprises in relevant industries, in research institutes, design bureaus, computer centers, and are engaged in the creation and operation of the most unique modern equipment - from microcircuits that form the basis of any product in the radio-electronic industry, to the most complex electronic computers, which are the main link of any modern system for the rapid receipt and processing of information.

The full-time department of MIREA has three faculties: radio engineering systems and devices, automated control systems, electronics and quantum devices.

Mathematics

Written exam options

Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{4}{x+\sqrt{x^2+x}}-\frac{1}{x-\sqrt{x^2+x}}=\frac{3}{x}.$$

2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x^2+y^2=41, \\ x+y=9. \end{cases}$$

3. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\cos 4x+2\sin^2 x=0,$$
satisfying conditions $|x|<1$.

4. The larger base of the trapezoid is 24 cm. Find its smaller base, knowing that the distance between the midpoints of its diagonals is 4 cm.

5. Prove that $|\sin n\alpha| \leq n|\sin \alpha|$ for any natural number $n$.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{9-5x}=\sqrt{3-x}+\frac{6}{\sqrt{3-x}}.$$

2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x^2+xy=28, \\ y^2+xy=-12. \end{cases}$$

3. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sin 9x=2\sin 3x,$$
satisfying conditions $0<x<2$.

4. A circle with a radius of 2 cm is inscribed in a rhombus, which is divided by its diagonal into two equilateral triangles. Find the side of the rhombus.

5. Prove that for any natural number $n>1$ the following inequality holds:
$$\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n+n}>\frac{13}{24}.$$
