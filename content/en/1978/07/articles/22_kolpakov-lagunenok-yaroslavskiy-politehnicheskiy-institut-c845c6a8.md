Leningrad Financial and Economic Institute named after. N. A. Voznesensky

In the center of Leningrad, with one façade facing Sadovou Street and the other facing the Griboedov Canal, behind a monumental cast-iron wall rises a remarkable building with a six-column portico. It was built at the end of the 18th century according to the design of the architect J. Quarrenghi, and not when it housed the Assignation Bank. Now here are the classrooms of one of the leading economic institutions in the country - the Leningrad Financial and Economic Institute named after. N. A. Voznesensky.

Nowadays, truly widely educated and highly qualified modern economic people emerge from its walls. In addition to a deep knowledge of general economic disciplines, they receive a good mathematical education. LFEI students, in addition to the general course of higher mathematics, are taught probability theory and mathematical statistics, operations research, special courses on the application of mathematical methods and models in various fields of economics.

The full-time department of LFEI has six faculties: financial and economic, planning-economic, accounting and economic, economics and logistics planning, economics and scientific organization of labor.

LFEI graduates work in enterprises, research and production associations, and research and design institutes.
