Moscow Energy Institute

MPEI is one of the largest higher technical educational institutions in the country, training engineering and scientific personnel in all areas of modern energy (including nuclear), electrical engineering, thermal engineering, radio engineering, electronics, automation and computer technology.

The full-time department of the institute has 10 faculties: electrical power engineering, thermal power engineering, industrial thermal power engineering, power engineering, electromechanical, electrification and automation of industry and transport, electronic engineering, automation and computer technology, automatic, energy-physical.

45 specialties - this is the range of our educational work. More than twenty thousand students are now studying at our institute's degrees. MPEI branches operate in Smolensk and Kazan.

Curricula and programs of all specialties provide for successful physical and mathematical training.
