Shown here are the sequential positions of the rotary pump in operation. For example, the ones in which the potors rotate are visible from figure. The profile of each torus consists of two congested epicyclopid arcs and two congested hypocyclopid arcs.

Each rotor is a kind of gear with two teeth. Epicyclopids are the heads of gear teeth, while hypocyclopids are the spaces between the teeth. Read more about the geometry of gear teeth on p. 50
