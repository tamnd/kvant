$$\sqrt{f_{n-2}^2} \cdot xy + f_{n-2} \cdot (x+y) + 1 = \frac{f_{n-2}x_n + 1}{x_{n-1}} xy,$$
or
$$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{f_{n-2}xy} = -f_{n-2} + \frac{f_{n-2}x_n + 1}{f_{n-2}x_{n-1}}.$$

$$\frac{1}{x_1} + \dots + \frac{1}{x_{n-1}} + \frac{1}{f_{n-1}} = A,$$

$$\frac{1}{x_1} + \dots + \frac{1}{x_{n-2}} + \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{f_{n-2}xy} = A.$$

$$\frac{f_{n-2}x_n + 1}{x_{n-1}} = f_{n-2}^2 + 1.$$
