8. The work done when lifting a log by just the end is equal to $A_1 = mgh_1$, where $h_1$ is the distance from the thin end to the center of gravity of the log. When lifting a log by the thin end $A_2 = mgh_2$, where $h_2$ is the distance from just the end to the center of gravity. According to condition $A_1 - A_2 = A$. Solving the system
$$\begin{align*}
h_1 + h_2 &= l \\
mg(h_1 - h_2) &= A,
\end{align*}$$

we find
$$h_1 = \frac{1}{2}\left(l + \frac{A}{mg}\right).$$
