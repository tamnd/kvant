Quests

1. Twins task. In each of the four cryptographic examples, find what the values ​​of X and Y are equal to:

$$\begin{array}{c c c c c}
\times & XYZ & \times & XYZ & \times \\
\times & XYZ & \times & XYZ & \times \\
\cdot & Z & \cdot & Z & \cdot \\
\cdot & Y & \cdot & Y & \cdot \\
\cdot & X & \cdot & X & \cdot \\
\cdot & X & \cdot & X & \cdot \\
\end{array}$$

2. Oral task. Semicircles are constructed on the sides of a rectangle of area S as on diameters (see figure). What is the area of ​​the shaded figure? (For “small ones” we report Pyfador’s theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, where a and b are the lengths of the legs, and c is the length of the hypotenuse).

3. Without crossing twice. Without lifting the pencil from the paper, draw a line that would intersect once at a time all 16 segments that make up the flat figure shown in figure.

4. Anya thought of a natural number. She divided the cube of this number by 7 with the remainder. Borya again divided the quotient received by Anya by 7 and also with the remainder. Vera divided the new quotient by 7 and did not say whether the remainder was obtained or not. Finally, Gala divided Verin’s result by 7. The quotient turned out to be equal to 10, but there was also a remainder.

Dina, having learned everything that was done with Anya’s number, guessed this number. What number did Anya have in mind?
