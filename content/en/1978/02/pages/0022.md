Before moving on to the main presentation, we give a definition of the new space around the axis.

A rotation of space is any movement of space in which a set of motionless points forms a straight line (it is called the axis of the surface). When space rotates around the axis of each plane, the plane perepredicular to the axis of rotation transforms into itself.
