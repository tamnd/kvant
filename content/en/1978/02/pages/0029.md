M489. Three numbers $a, b, c$ are given. Let us construct three sequences $(a_n), (b_n), (c_n)$ with $a_1 = a, b_1 = b, c_1 = c$ and
$$a_{n+1} = \frac{b_n + c_n}{2}, b_{n+1} = \frac{c_n + a_n}{2}, c_{n+1} = \frac{a_n + b_n}{2}$$

for any $n=1, 2, 3, \dots$. Prove that all these three sequences have a common limit and find it.

I. Burmistrovich

M490. $p$ is a prime odd number. Given $p=1$ integers that are not divisible by $p$. Prove that by replacing some of these numbers with their opposites, one can obtain $p=1$ integers whose sum is divisible by $p$.

S. Fomin

F498. The dynamite of a thin-walled cylindrical glass is $d$, height is $h$. The walls and bottom of the glass are the same thickness. Water is poured into a glass. At what water level is the center of gravity of a glass of water at its lowest position?

F499. Two glass balls of radii $r$ and $R$ are connected by a thin long glass tube and filled with air. The middle tubes are caplyartuti. Can this device measure the ambient temperature?

F500. A bell can be seen on a pole at a height $h$ above the ground. At what distance from the pillar is the sound heard loudest if the speed of sound is $c$ and the speed of the wind blowing horizontally is $v$?

F501. On the edge of a table of height $H$ there is a ball of radius $R$, moreover, $R \ll H$. The ball begins to slide off the table without friction. How far from the table will the ball land?

F502. There is a round hole in the lid of a closed box with a height of $h=1$ m. How will the illumination of the day change under the answer if a lens with an optical power of $D=1$ ditre is inserted into the answer? The box stands under the open sky, covered evenly by a film of clouds.

The wording of problem M480, published in No. 12, 1977, includes an error. Problem M480 c) should be read as follows: the sequence $C_n$ is determined by the conditions $C_1=2, C_{n+1}=[3C_n/2]$ for $n \geq 1$; prove that there exists a number γ such that $C_n=[(3/2)^n\gamma]+1$ (here $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$). (In No. 12, a unit was omitted from the formula for $C_n$.) The deadline for sending solutions to this problem is extended until June 1, 1978.
