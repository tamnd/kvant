Let us illustrate the same approach by proving the well-known theorem about the inscribed angle.

Theorem. The magnitude of the inscribed angle is equal to half the angular magnitude of the duen *) on which it rests.

Proof. Angle ABC is inscribed in a circle with center O. Let us draw lines l and m through O, perpendicular to its sides. These lines will pass through the midpoints of chords AB and BC, respectively.
