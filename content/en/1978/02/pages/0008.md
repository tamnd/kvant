From the Bragg equation (equation (2)) it follows that for a given wavelength there is a minimum value of the distance $d$ between the planes at which selective reflection is possible. This distance is equal to $\lambda/2$ (since sine cannot be greater than one). On the other hand, from the above formula of mechanical distances (formula (1)) it is clear that the smallest value $d$ corresponds to the smallest values ​​$h$ and $k$.
