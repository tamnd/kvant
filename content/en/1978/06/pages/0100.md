Kepler's Cosmic Cup, which you see here, illustrates the gel-centric model of the world system.
According to Kepler, the spheres on which the orbits of the planets are located are successively inscribed and described by about five regular polyhedra - octahedron, inklosahedron, dodecahedron, tetrahedron and cube.
