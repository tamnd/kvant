⟦folio 57⟧

Quests

1. Ten athletes are participating in modern running: Andrey, Victor, Efim, Zakhar, Ivan, Klim, Mikhail, Nikolay, Timofey and Fedor (Fig. 1). The first athlete to reach the finish line received 9 points, the second - 8 points, the third - 7, etc., until the last one, who received 0 points. How the points were distributed among the beta participants is reflected in figure 2. Using this figure, determine how many points each of the ten athletes received. Substitute together the numbers 67,480, 2,401,240,564, 953,564 with the initial letters of the names of the athletes who received the corresponding number of points. What did you get?

2. Bend a figure from soft wire, which, when parallelly projected onto different planes, produces the letters C, L, O, G, written as shown in figure 3.

3. Two girls play this game: they take turns tearing off the petals of a daisy. In one move, you can tear off either one petal or two adjacent (from the very beginning) petals. The girl who picks the last petal wins. Prove that the second girl can always win (the daisy has more than two petals).

4. Five brothers live: Ivan, Stepan, Andrey and twins Sergey and Agey. The product of the years Sergei and Agey is equal to the year of Andrey's birth, and the product of the years of Andrey and Stepan is equal to the year of birth of Ivan. What is the name of Andrei’s son, if he is five times younger than his namesake uncle?
