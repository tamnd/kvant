“There probably isn’t a boy in the world who doesn’t play with a magnifying glass (lens).” Thus begins to become “Are you familiar with the lens?” But to get to know the lens, you don't have to have a magnifying glass. These drops of dew on an oak leaf are also lenses. If you have never looked at dew from this point of view, slow down. And you see the thin veins of the leaf like an intricate network of roads, and the cobweb is like a thread. And every droplet focuses the sunlight.
