Let's talk about certain

What does mathematics consist of? Of course, first of all, from theorems and axioms (“Geometry 6”, paragraph 6). The most important part of mathematics is proof. N. Bourbaki’s landmark treatise “Principles of Mathematics” *) begins with the words: “Since the time of the Greeks, to say “mathematics” means to say “proof.”

Along with this, definitions play a fundamental role in mathematics (“Geometry 6”, paragraph 2) - after all, before proving theorems about certain mathematical objects, one must understand what kind of objects they are. Why “prove”! Even in order to simply understand the theorem “An incisive to a circle is perpendicular to the diameter passing through the point of tangency,” we must know what a “circle”, “an incisive to a circle”, “a point of tangency”, “diameter”, “perpendicular” are.

*) A group of French mathematicians, united under the pseudonym “Nicolas Bourbaki,” set themselves the goal of writing a complete treatise on modern mathematics. The treatise has been published in separate editions in Paris since 1939. In our country, a translation of the treatise began to be published in 1958. The treatise is not yet finished. Since mathematics is also moving forward while writing the treatise, it is possible that it will never be completed.
