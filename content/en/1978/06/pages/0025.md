This keeper Kepler introduced himself as follows: “The harbinger of cosmographic research, containing a cosmographic secret”). In this work, he tries to construct a gel-centric system of the world, establishing a numerical relationship between the distances of the planets from the Sun and the sizes of regular polyhedra. According to KepLER, a cube is inscribed in the sphere on which the orbit of Saturn is located, the next sphere is inscribed in it - with the orbit of Jupiter, then the tetrahedron, the sphere of Mars, the dodecahedron, the sphere of the Earth, the icasahedron, the sphere of Venus, the octahedron, the sphere of Mercury are successively inscribed *). At the center of this entire system of spheres and polyhedra is the Sun.

Kepler sent his “The Secret of the Universe” to Galileo Galilei and Tycho Brahe. Both scientists answered directly. Galileo welcomed the appearance of another adherent of the Copernican system. Brahe, expressing his negative attitude towards Keper's conclusions, drew attention to the independence of Keper's thinking, his importance of astronomy, and his skill and perseverance in calculations. To personally meet the young scientist, Brahe invites him to his place.

At this time, changes occur in Keper's personal life. In April 1597, he married the miller's daughter Barbara Müller, and a year later they had a son, Heinrich. After missing only two places, the child dies. The same area also affects daughter Susanna, who gave birth a year later. Kepler has a hard time dealing with the deaths of his children. And then there are other inconveniences. At the beginning of 1598, in Graz, the genius of the Protestants, to which Kepler belonged, had time. In September of the same year, under penalty of death, all Protestant priests and school teachers were asked to leave the city. Kepler is forced to flee the city. True, an exception was soon made for him, and a month later Kepler returned.
