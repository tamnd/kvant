Moscow Institute of National Economy named after. G. V. Plexanova

MINCH them. G. V. Plexanova is one of the oldest and largest economic institutions of the Soviet Union. About 15 thousand students study at its eleven faculties. More than 500 graduate students work in 47 departments of the institute.

The institute has the following faculties: general economics (specialties - national economic planning, labor economics), economic cybernetics, trade economics (trade economics, catering economics, accounting in trade and industry), financial (finance and credit in industry and trade, price planning and pricing), commodity science and organization of trade in food products, commodity science and organization of trade in industrial goods, industrial economics, economics and logistics planning, technical (process engineer technical and public catering), mechanical (mechanical engineer for machines and apparatus of food production), correspondence (process engineer for technical and public catering, economics of public catering).

In all faculties, except technological, mechanical and correspondence, the entrance exam in mathematics is written; at the technical faculty - oral; in mechanical - both written and oral; for correspondence courses - oral in the specialty “engineer-technologist” and written in the second specialty.
