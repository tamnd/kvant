$$\frac{m\omega^2}{p_0S} x^2 - x + l = 0,$$

In the first case, the behavior of the balls directly follows from the laws of conservation of energy and momentum during an elastic collision of two identical balls (balls 1 and 2 collide first, and then 2 and 3). In the second case, only the rubber gasket delays the process of transmitting impulse from the first ball to the second. And this leads to this. Let's imagine the process of transmitting impulse in steps (not smoothly, as it actually happens). The deformed rubber imparts some impulse to ball 2, which then to ball 3. During this time, the deformation of the rubber, and hence its transmitted impulse, returns ball 2 to accelerate again, accelerates ball 3 and increases its momentum. This will happen until ball 2 eventually moves away from the rubber gasket. From this point on, balls 2 and 3 will appear to have practically the same speeds.
