7. It is required to build several houses with a total living area of ​​40,000 m². The cost of building one house with a living area $S$ consists of the cost of the function, proportional to $\sqrt{S}$, and the cost of the rented part, proportional to $S \sqrt{S}$. When building a house with a living area of ​​400 m², 80% of the costs go to the function. How many houses should be built to keep costs as low as possible?

8. The power expended on the movement of the steamboat is proportional to the cube of its speed. Find the most economical speed of the steamboat when moving against the current, the speed of which is $a$ km/h.

9. There is a battery with internal resistance $r$. What resistance should be connected to the battery so that the power released on it is minimal?
