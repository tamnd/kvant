V. Barbaumov, V. Ermakov, G. Savchenko, P. Saevtov

The Moscow Automotive Institute (MAMI) was founded in 1939. One of the largest scientists in the field of automatic machine industry, Academician E. A. Chudakov, took part in its creation.

MAMI is the leading educational institute in our country, training specialists in the design of automatic equipment and tractors, mechanical engineers, test engineers, and engineering production technologists.

The institute consists of 4 faculties: automatic and tractors, automatic and tractor engines, technical and automation of mechanical engineering, lettering and forging and stamping production.

Graduates of the institute, who have a good theoretical background, are engaged in solving complex and interesting technical problems. Currently, some MAMI graduates are sent to research organizations, specialist design bureaus involved in the design of new brands automatically. Graduates of the institute deal with issues that require advanced knowledge of mathematics and physics: the critical speed of automatic tires, automatic vibrations on the suspension (theory of random processes), the strength and vibrations of automatic engines and transmissions (methods of mathematical physics), automatic devices of various kinds (the theory of automatic regulation), stability of automatic motion and much more.

For students interested in mathematics and physics, the departments of higher mathematics, applied mathematics, physics, theoretical mechanics, theory of mechanisms and machines, electronic and automatic organizations offer specialized seminars and scientific circles.
