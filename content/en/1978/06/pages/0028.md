Kepler's mother was saved from the fire of the Inquisition thanks to her son, but at what cost! For six years, a world-famous scientist engaged in science was forced to fight ignorance, stupidity, and cruelty.

Kepler returns to Linz. Here he finishes his work on tables of logarithms, which he began in the summer of 1619, when he became acquainted with D. Napier’s book “Description of Amazing Tables of Logarithms” (1614). Since Kepler had to calculate a lot, he could not pass the mismo of Napier's invention and compiled his own tables of logarithms, which were more similar in structure to modern ones.

In the summer of 1624, Kepler finally completed the compilation of new actinomic planetary tables, on which he had been working for 22 years. Kepler considered this work to be the main work of his life. The old tables of planetary movements were inaccurate, and the new Keplerian tables were impatiently awaited by sailors and astronomers, compiled by caldarians and astrologers. After the publication in 1627 of the “Rudolfin Tables” (named after Rudolf II), they served people for almost two centuries, only at the beginning of the 19th century they were replaced by more accurate ones.

The last year of Kepler's life was again overshadowed by hardships, deprivations and wanderings. There is no money to live or to publish my works. The persecution of the Proteants began in person. They were all asked to either move to the directory or leave the city within six months. Kepler and the employee of his printing house are allowed to remain in the city until the completion of work on the Rudolphin Tables.

The Thirty Years' War has been going on for eight years now. Linz, like the whole of Upper Austria, is occupied by the bazaar troops, who behave like conquerors.
