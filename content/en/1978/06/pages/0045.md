M510. In the book “Hungarian Mathematical Olympiads” the following problem is given (No. 148): “Prove that for all positive $\alpha < \pi$ the inequality holds
$$\sin \alpha + \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\alpha + \frac{1}{3} \sin 3\alpha > 0.$$
Prove the following generalization of this inequality: for all $0 < \alpha < \pi$ and natural $n$
$$\sin \alpha + \frac{1}{2} \sin 2\alpha + \frac{1}{3} \sin 3\alpha + \dots$$
$$\dots + \frac{1}{n} \sin n\alpha > 0.$$
I. Birder, R. Ushakov

F520. In a flow calorimeter (Fig. 4), the gas to be tested is passed through a pipeline and heated using a spiral. The gas enters the calorimeter at a temperature of $t_1 = 20^\circ\text{C}$. With a heater power of $W_1 = 1$ kW and a gas flow rate of $m_1 = 540$ kg/h, the gas temperature behind the heater turned out to be the same as with a heater power of $W_2 = 2$ kW and a gas flow rate of $m_2 = 720$ kg/h. The gas pressure in the pipe is the same everywhere. Find the temperature of the gas $t_2$ if its specific heat at constant volume is $c_V = 21$ J/(mol K) and the molar mass of the gas is $M = 29$ kg/kmol.
