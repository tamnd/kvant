Moscow Civil Engineering Institute named after. V. V. Kuibysheva

Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Engineering and Construction Institute named after. V.V. Kuibysheva (MISI) trains civil engineers of a wide profile to work in construction organizations, in design and research institutes. In itself, MISI solves the most important problems related to the design and construction of unique structures, the development of the theory of calculation of structures, and scientific research for various branches of the modern construction industry.

At present, when in our country the volume of residential, cultural and industrial construction is increasing every year, the profession of civil engineering is one of the most necessary and interesting. A civil engineer designs and builds cinemas and stadiums, hydrosanitary and heating stations, factories and housing buildings, develops new building materials and new construction machines, plans cities and towns, deals with the problem of sea water retention, construction economics and many other issues.

MISS them. V.V. Kuibyshev was founded in 1921. Currently, the institute has more than 15 thousand students and about 600 graduate students. The institute has 54 departments, the professional teaching staff of which includes 80 professionals and doctors of science, more than 400 associate professors and candidates of science.

A characteristic feature of training is the combination of educational and research work. Students who have an inclination for scientific work are sent to graduate school after completing a research diploma.

Graduates of the institute work in many cities of the country and abroad. Muscovites are sent to work in Moscow organizations, striving to make our capital an exemplary city.

Currently, the institute includes construction-technological, mechanical, engineering-pedagogical faculties and faculties of industrial and civil engineering, thermal construction, hydraulic engineering, urban planning, water supply and drainage, thermal and gas research and ventilation, automated control systems, technical operation of buildings, economics and construction organization.

The institute has preparatory departments for part-time and correspondence preparatory courses.
