Moscow Institute of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartography Engineers

We have already written in detail about MIIGAIK in «Kvant» (1977, No. 6).

Currently, the institute has the following faculties: geodesy with specialties in astronomy and geodesy (specialization: marine geodesy), applied geodesy, space geodesy; aerial photogeodesy; with a specialty in aerial photography; cartography with a specialty in cartography (specialization: design and compilation of maps, publication of maps); optical-mechanical, training broad-profile specialties in optical and optoelectronic devices. The institute has a correspondence faculty specializing in applied geodesy, aerial photography, cartography, and an evening optical-mechanical faculty.

Since the 1977/78 academic year, the institute began training specialties in the field of natural resources research (at the aerial photo-geodetic faculty). Students of this specialty study higher mathematics and physics according to an extended program. The tasks of natural resource research majors include developing the most effective methods for searching for minerals, protecting the environment, etc. The duration of training in this specialty is 5 years 6 months.
