Yaroslavl State University was founded in 1918 by decree signed by V.I. Lenin. Due to the difficult situation in the country after the civil war, in 1924 it was reorganized into a pedagogical institute. In 1970 - the year of the centenary of the birth of V. I. Lenin - the university resumed its work. Now the university has 5 faculties: mathematics, physics, economics, biology and psychology, history and law.

The Faculty of Mathematics has two departments - mathematics and applied mathematics.

Students of the Faculty of Physics specialize in theoretical physics, solid state physics and radiophysics.
