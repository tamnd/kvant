Vitebsk Technological Institute of Light Industry was opened in 1965. Currently he is training specialists for the light and mechanical engineering industries.
More than 4 thousand students study at six faculties of the institute in full-time, evening and correspondence forms of study.
The Faculty of Mechanical Technology prepares mechanical engineers to work in departments, laboratories and workshops of clothing and shoe factories, model houses, as well as technological artists in the artistic design of knitted and textile products.
The Faculty of Engineering and Economics trains economic engineers for enterprises in the light and textile industries and public service enterprises.
The Institute also has evening and correspondence faculties.
