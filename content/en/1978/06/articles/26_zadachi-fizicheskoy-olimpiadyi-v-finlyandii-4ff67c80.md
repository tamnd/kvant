Objectives of the Physics Olympiad in Finland

In the first five tasks you need to choose the correct answers from among those proposed.

1. figure 1 shows a level diagram of some atom, and figure 2 shows several emission spectra. Which spectrum belongs to this atom?

2. In the experiment on heating an alium cube, the following results were obtained: mass of the cube $m = 1,01$ kg, initial temperature $t_1 = (18,4 \pm 0,5)^\circ C$, final temperature $t_2 = (31,0 \pm 0,5)^\circ C$, heating time $\tau = 215$ s, power supplied during heating $P = (52 \pm 1)$ W. What, according to this experiment, is the specific heat of aliominium and the error in its measurement:
a) $(880 \pm 10)$ J/(kg K);
b) $(880 \pm 25)$ J/(kg K);
b) $(880 \pm 55)$ J/(kg K);
d) $(880 \pm 60)$ J/(kg K);
d) $(880 \pm 90)$ J/(kg K)?

3. Capacitors $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected in series (Fig. 3). Capacitor $C_3$ is connected between points $B$ and $D$, while the direction between points $A$ and $D$ does not change. All capacitors have the same capacitance. When connecting capacitor $C_3$: a) the charge on capacitor $C_1$ increases; b) tension between points $A$ and $B$ increases; b) the charge on the capacitor $C_2$ increases; d) the energy of capacitor $C_2$ decreases.

6) $T = AL \sqrt{\frac{\rho}{E}}$;
b) $T = \frac{AE}{\rho} \sqrt{\frac{L}{|g|}}$?

(Here $A$ is a dimensionless constant, $g$ is the acceleration of free pressure.) Explain your answer.

6. The acceleration of free pressure on the Earth's position is 9.83 m/s$^2$. The radius of the Earth is 6.36·10$^6$ m. At what height above the pole is the acceleration equal to 9.78 m/s$^2$?

7. Complete the following nuclear reactions:
a) $n \rightarrow p + \dots$;
b) $p \rightarrow n + \dots$; b) $p \rightarrow -1e \rightarrow \dots$; d) $-1e \rightarrow +1e \rightarrow \dots$; e) $^2_1H + \gamma \rightarrow x + n$; e) $^{197}_{79}Au + n \rightarrow x + \gamma$.

8. Cubes with masses $m$, $2m$ and $m$ are connected by a long flexible thread thrown over the blocks (Fig. 5). The masses of blocks and threads are negligible. There is no training in blocks. A cube of mass $2m$ is released. a) What will be the speed of this cube when it drops 0.5 m? b) What is the equilibrium position of the system?

9. The length of a thin metal wire with a heavy ball at the end is 2.0 m. The wire can move in a vertical plane perpendicular to the magnetic field; The initial position of the wire is horizontal (position OA at figure 6). Magnetic field induction $|\vec{B}| = 0,25$ mG. What is the potential difference between the ends of the wire at the moment it passes the OC position?
