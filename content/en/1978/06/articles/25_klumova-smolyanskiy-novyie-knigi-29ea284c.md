Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Leathwood J. Mathematical mixture. Translation of mathematicians' words into English contains many important and profound results in various fields of mathematics. He is also known as an insular interlocutor, with a wide range of interservers, who reacts quickly to any mathematical question.

His small book “A Mathematical Mixture” (in the English original - “Miscellaneous Notes of a Mathematician”), written immortally, can be answered to the rare genre of the interlocutor of mathematical estimates - miniator. The themes of Libra glasses are varied. Among them are mathematical anecdotes, moments of mathematical self-obography, small historical studies, interesting achievements, original and unexpected evidence. Discussion of some special issues (ballistics, celestial mechanics, etc.) requires mathematical techniques; Also, passages (they are marked with asterisks) when reading can be read without difficulty understanding the rest of the text.

We get pleasure from this book even when flipping through it, viewing it safely.

2. Gel fond A. O. Solving equations in integers. 3rd edition. Volume 3 l., circulation 50,000 copies.

Solving in integer algebraic equations with integer coefficients in more than one variable is one of the most difficult problems in number theory. The most outstanding mathematicians of antiquity, for example, the Greek mathematician Pythogoras, the Alexandrian mathematician Dufant, and the best mathematicians of a more recent era: Fermat, Euler, Lagrange, dealt with these problems a lot. Despite the number of works of outstanding mathematicians, there are no general methods in this area. The problem of solving equations in integers has been fully studied only for second-degree equations in two variables. For equations above the second degree with two or more weight variables, not only is the task of finding all the solutions in integers difficult, but even the simpler task of establishing the existence of a finite or infinite set of such solutions is difficult.

This paper presents the reader with some interesting results obtained in the theory of solving equations in integers. The theorems formulated in it are provided with proofs in cases where these proofs are quite simple.

3. Solodovnikov A. S. System linear inequalities. 2nd edition, add. Volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies.

This small book introduces the reader to various aspects of the theory of systems of linear inequalities: from the geometric side of the issue and those methods associated with it for solving systems (translated into geometric language, specifying a system of linear inequalities with two or three variables familiarized with a polygonal area on a plane or, accordingly, a convex polyhedral body in space), with a number of algebraic properties of the system, with fundamental issues of linear programming (linear programming - This is one of the branches of the theory of systems of linear inequalities).

The book describes the set of all solutions to systems of inequalities with two and three variables (easily variable to systems with any number of variables), and questions of consistency and inconsistency are studied. The last section proves the duality theorem of linear programming.

The book is intended for high school students and all math lovers.

4. Abramov S. A. Mathematical constructions and programs. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

This book, accessible to high school students, focuses on the algorithmic structure of mathematical problems on a convex polyhedral body in space. There are a large number of examples for independent solutions.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"
1. Landu L. D., Kitaygorodsky A. I. Physics for everyone. Molecules. 4th edition, rev. and additional Volume 10 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

The book was written by one of the leading physicists in our country, Academician L. D. Landu, together with his student Professor A. I. Kitaigorodsky. The purpose of the book is to give the reader in a public form a clearly presented idea of ​​the main ideas and the latest achievements of modern physics. It is a natural continuation of a previously published book by the same authors (see «Kvant», 1978, No. 3, p. 60). It outlines issues related to molecular physics: various physical states of matter, properties of liquid and solid solutions, crystal structure, etc.

The book is freshly written, original, well designed and intended for a wide range of readers.
