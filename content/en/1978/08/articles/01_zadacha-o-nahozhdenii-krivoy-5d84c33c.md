This wonderful painting was “made,” like the painting on the second page of the cover, by a computer using a program compiled by Yu. Kotov. It was obtained using a moving ellipse, the major axis of which always passes through a fixed point—the center of symmetry of the picture. The center of the ellipse moves along a certain curve. Try to find this curve.
