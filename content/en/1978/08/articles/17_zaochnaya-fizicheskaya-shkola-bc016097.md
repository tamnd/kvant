⟦folio 61⟧

The Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of MSUOb announces enrollment of students in grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher educational institutions, primarily to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out on the basis of the results of the entrance task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 20 to the address: 117234, Moscow, Lenin Hills, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, Zoological Physical School. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your home address written on it and two copies of a questionnaire written on sheets of thick paper measuring 7x12 cm and filled out according to the following template:

The decision of the 3FS admission committee on enrollment will be communicated before November 1, 1978. Verified introductory assignments will not be sent back.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on sections of physics studied in the corresponding classes of high school. The solution evaluates the task, solves it and sends it to the student (for this, each time, along with the solved test task, the student must be sent an envelope with a written home address). Students in grade 9 of 3FS at the end of the year are transferred to grade 10 based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.
