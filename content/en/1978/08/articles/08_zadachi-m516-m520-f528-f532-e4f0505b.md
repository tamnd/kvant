M516. Three machines print pairs of natural numbers on cards. The machines work as follows. The first machine, having read the card (a; b), issues a new card (a+1; b+1); the second, having read card (a; b), issues card $\left(\frac{a}{2}; \frac{b}{2}\right)$ (it only works when a and b are even); the third, given two cards (a; b) and (b; c), gives card (a; c). In addition, the machines return all read cards.

Let there initially be one card with a pair of numbers (5; 19). Can I use it in any order to get the card: a) (1;50)? 6) (1;100)?

c) Let there initially be one card (a; b), a <b>, and we want to get card (1; n). For what n can this be done? (8-10 grades)

V. Gutenmacher

M517. An n-gon of area S is inscribed in a circle of radius R. A point is located on each side of the n-gon. Prove that the perimeter of an n-gon with vertices at offset points is not less than 2S/R. (8-9 grades)

V. Dubrovsky

M518. The numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ belong to the segment [a; b], where 0 < a < b. Prove the inequality

$$\left(x_1 + x_2 + \ldots + x_n\right) \left(\frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2} + \ldots + \frac{1}{x_n}\right) \leq \frac{(a+b)^2}{4ab} n^2.$$ (9 grades)

S. Fomin

M520. The distance is a sequence of numbers $x_n = (1 + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3})^n$. Each of them is brought to the form

$$x_n = q_n + r_n \sqrt{2} + s_n \sqrt{3} + t_n \sqrt{6},$$

where $q_n, r_n, s_n, t_n$ are integers. Find your limits

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{r_n}{q_n}, \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{s_n}{q_n}, \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{t_n}{q_n}. \quad (10 \text{ кл.})$$
