Evening physical school

At the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University, for students in grades 8-10 in Moscow and the Moscow region, the Evening Physics School (VPS) has been running for three years. Schooling is very... Seminars are held once a week to analyze problems in the main sections of the school physics course. Seminars are conducted by graduate and senior students. Much more complex issues are discussed in lectures given by teachers from the Faculty of Physics.

For VPS students, excursions are organized to the laboratories of the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University, the Research Institute of Nuclear Physics and the State Astronomical Institute named after. Sternberg. Excursions to the university's scientific laboratories give schoolchildren the opportunity to become familiar with the methods of modern physical experiments and imagine the main directions of research work at the Physics Faculty of Moscow State University.

The next competitive enrollment at the VFS is in September of this year. Schoolchildren in grades 8–10 can participate in the competition (additional enrollment is carried out for grades 9 and 10). To participate in the competition, you must submit an application to the director of the Higher School of Physics by September 20, fill out a form at the Komsomol committee of the Faculty of Physics, and also submit 2 photographs measuring 3x4 cm.

Applications will be accepted from September 2 to September 20 from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm annually (except Sundays) at the Komsomol Committee of the Physics Faculty of Moscow State University (Physics Faculty building, room 2-47).

Admission to the school is random based on property results. The requirements imposed on the property do not go beyond the school curriculum.

School address: Moscow, Lenin Hills, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics. Phone for inquiries: 139-26-56.

Directions: metro to the “University” station, then by buses 103, 119, 130, trolleybuses 4, 34, 49 to the stop “ul. Lebedev".
