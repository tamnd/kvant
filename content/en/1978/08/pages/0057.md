2. Butkikov E.I., Bykov A.A., Kondratyev A.S. Physics for post-university students. Volume 30 l., circulation 500,000 copies, price 1 rub.

The book is a textbook for university applicants. Along with the presentation of program material, much attention is paid to the analysis of solutions to physical problems. The mathematical apparatus used is fully consistent with the school curriculum.

The book is intended for high school students.

3. Mayer V.V. Views experience with ultrasound. (Library of physics and mathematics school.) Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 22 kopecks.

The book discusses methods for producing ultrasound with a frequency of 20-150 kHz using homemade generators. It describes experiments illustrating the basic physical properties and practical applications of ultrasonic waves. The proposed devices and experiments are characterized by maximum simplicity, accessibility and can be easily reproduced at home. The purpose of the book is to teach the reader to think through, set up and analyze experiments in physics, to promote the development of creative abilities of schoolchildren in the field of physical experimentation.

The book is intended for high school students. It can be used in the work of physical and technical circles.

4. Kitaygorodsky A.I. Physics for everyone. Electrons. Volume 10 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

This book is a natural continuation of the book by L. D. Landu and A. I. Kitaigorodsky “Physics for Everyone” (see «Kvant», 1978, No. 6, p. 83).

The book sets out the basic facts concerning electron current and electromagnetic field. Much attention is paid to the mechanism of current passage through liquids, metals and semiconductors and modern ideas about dielectrics and magnets.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

5. Dageev M. M. Solar and lunar eclipses. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

The book, in a form accessible to schoolchildren, talks about the movement of the Earth and the Moon, the conditions of visibility of the Moon and the reasons for the change of its phases, the causes of solar and lunar eclipses. Recommendations for simple astronomical observations are given.

The book is intended for tenth grade schoolchildren and teachers of physics and astronomy.

6. Mak s and mach V. A., Komarov V. I. In the labyrinths of stars (oriented according to the sky). Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

The book, in a popular form, introduces the reader to the picture of the starry sky and the method of orientation by celestial bodies. It was written based on the experience of studying the starry sky accumulated at the Moscow Planetarium during training with aviation pilots and cosmonauts. It describes the starry sky of only the northern but also the southern hemispheres of the Earth. The final sections of the book are devoted to issues of astronomical orientation during space flight, on the Moon, and on neighboring planets of the Solar System.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers about the deep connection that exists between the modern theory of gravity and the theory of elementary particles.

High school students will read the book with great interest.
