⟦folio 54⟧

New books

In this issue we publish short English texts on books on mathematics and physics that are accessible and interesting to our readers, weekends in 1978.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Markushevich A.I. Wonderful bloods. 3rd edition, expanded. Volume 2 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 7 kopecks.

This booklet is a reprint of one of the first editions of the Popular Lectures in Mathematics series. It is based on a lecture given by the author to Moscow schoolchildren from rural and military classes. In his brochure, the author uses the following definition: a curve is the trace of a moving sink. They consider straight lines and circles - the two simplest and at the same time the most substituted in their properties in the book. After this, the author proceeds to the story of ellis, cyperbola, paabola; It turns out that all these three curves can be obtained by cutting the cone into a plane: the ellipse, hyperbola and paabola are finite sections. Finally, it talks about Lemniscus and Cyclola, which are replaced by the properties of the Cyclonde, because of which it deserves the name “brachistochrone”. Currently, the edition has added information about the chain line along which the target is located, suspended at two ends, and about the Archimedes spiral (it is described by a bug crawling along a uniformly rotating ruler). Most information about the curves is presented without evidence.

The brochure assumes preparation for 7-8 grades of secondary school.

2. Bogolyuyu obov A. N. Gaspard Monge (1746-1818). Volume 12 l., circulation 15,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

This book (from the “Scientific Biographies” series) tells about the life and work of a remarkable French scientist who made an important contribution to the creation of descriptive, analytical and differential geometry. Gaspard Monge was prominent in his activities and took an active part in the organization of science and higher education in France.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

3. S. Ira Zh inov S. Kh., Matveevskaya G. P. Abu Raikhan Beruni and his mathematical works. Volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.

The book tells about the life and mathematical work of Abu Raikhan Beruni, a great scientist and encyclopedist of Central Asia, whose thousandth anniversary of his birth was recently celebrated by UNESCO in all countries of the world. Beruni determined the radius of the globe. Five centuries before Copernicus, he expressed the idea of ​​the possibility of the Earth rotating around the Sun. Beruni has an exceptionally wide range of interests - mathematics, astronomy, mineralogy, geography, geodesy, history, linguistics and others; Historians of science call the first half of the 11th century the era of Beruni.

Publishing house "Mir"

4. Bizam D., Herceg J. Multicolored lezika. Volume 21 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 2 rubles.

A new book by Hungarian mathematicians D. Bism and J. Herceg (their first book, “Game and Logic,” translated into Russian, was published by the Mir publishing house in 1975) introduces the reader to more than 150 logical problems. Vibrant and engaging, these challenges convey important information needed to solve server problems in a playful way. Reading a book does not require special knowledge; all you need is the ability (or willingness to learn) to think logically.

With its original design and illustrations, “Multi-Color Logic” is accessible to the widest range of readers.

5. Strashevich St., Brovkin E. Polish mathematical descriptions. Volume 16 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 30 k.

This book is a translation of an anniversary collection of selected problems, published for the 25th anniversary of the use of mathematical descriptions (1950-1975). The collection is supplemented by problems proposed at the third type olimpad of 1976-1977.

The book continues the series “Tasks and Olympics”, published by “Mir” since 1975. It will be useful for high school students, applicants, students, school and university mathematics teachers, heads of mathematical circles, as well as anyone who enjoys this task.
