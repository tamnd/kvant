Achimedian problems

The partition of the “figure stomaxiona” (Fig. 1) proposed by Achimedes has the following property: the areas of all 14 parts are in a rational relation to the area of ​​the original figure.

Problem 1. Prove that the areas of individual parts of the original parallelogram, expressed in forty-eighths of its area, have the values ​​​​shown in figure 1 in red numbers.

The following problem is taken from the book “Geometric puzzles and paralogisms” by Emile Fourret (Odessa, Mathesis publishing house, 1912):

Task 2. Group the elements of stomaxiona so that the areas of the new parts obtained are expressed (in forty-eighths of the area of ​​the original parallelogram):

a) three equal integers,
b) three consecutive integers,
b) integers from 1 to 8 and the number 12.

Note that all three points of the problem can be solved without scattering the original parallelogram, but by drawing dividing lines between the elements.
