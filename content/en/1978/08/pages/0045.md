⟦folio 43⟧

F486. figure 11 shows a diagram of the mass spectrometer. In conzamore A they form sons, which are accelerated at the applied $U = 10$ kV and enter through the target $C$ into a magnetic field with induction $|\vec{B}| = 0,1$ T. After turning, the sons fall on the phomoeffic plate $D$ and cause it to blacken. At what distance from each other will the son stripes $H^+$, $2H^+$, $3H^+$, $He^+$ be located on the plate? What must be the width of the targets for the son bands $^{16}O^+$ and $^{15}N^+$ to separate?

Fig. 11.

Accelerated by an electric field, they acquire kinetic energy

$$\frac{mv^2}{2} = qU,$$

where $m$ is the mass of the son and $q$ is its charge. Consequently, they enter the mass spectrometer chamber at a speed

$$|\vec{v}| = \sqrt{\frac{2qU}{m}}.$$

In this chamber they are acted upon by the Lorentz force

$$|\vec{F}| = |\vec{v}| |\vec{B}| q,$$

due to which they move in a circle of radius $R$. According to Newton's second law

$$\frac{mv^2}{R} = |\vec{v}| |\vec{B}| q,$$

where

$$R = \frac{m |\vec{v}|}{|\vec{B}| q} = \frac{1}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2mU}{q}}.$$

Ions of the same type are collected on photoplastic at a distance of $l = 2R$ from the targets. The distance between sweet sons with the same charges $q$, but different masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ is

$$\Delta l = 2\Delta R = \frac{2}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2U}{q}} (\sqrt{m_2} - \sqrt{m_1}).$$

So, for dreams $H^+$ and $2H^+$

$$\Delta l = \frac{2}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2U}{q}} (\sqrt{2m_p} - \sqrt{m_p}) \approx 0,12 \text{ м},$$

where $q = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ K is the charge of monovalent sons, $m_p = 1,67 \cdot 10^{-27}$ kg is the proton mass.

For dreams $2H^+$ and $3H^+$

$$\Delta l = \frac{2}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2U}{q}} (\sqrt{3m_p} - \sqrt{2m_p}) \approx 0,09 \text{ м},$$

and for $3H^+$ and $He^+$

$$\Delta l = \frac{2}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2U}{q}} (\sqrt{4m_p} - \sqrt{3m_p}) \approx 0,08 \text{ м}.$$

Obviously, the son traces on the photoplastic will not overlap if the son width $d$ is less than $\Delta l$. In particular, for sones $^{16}O^+$ and $^{15}N^+$

$$d < \frac{2}{|\vec{B}|} \sqrt{\frac{2U}{q}} (\sqrt{16m_p} - \sqrt{15m_p}) \approx 0,04 \text{ м}.$$
