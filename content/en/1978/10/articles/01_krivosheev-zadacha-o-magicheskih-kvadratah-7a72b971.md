This figure is made up of four 5x5 magic squares. We remind you that a magic square is called a square, which has the same sum of numbers in rows, columns and diagonals. A set of four squares is remarkable in that the sums of the numbers in the “single-digit” cells of these squares are the same.
Can you find a set of three magic squares with the same property?

E. Krivoshcheev
