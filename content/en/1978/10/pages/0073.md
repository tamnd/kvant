⟦folio 71⟧

T. Petrova

Olympida
in physics

The XII All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren, held this year, was dedicated to the 60th anniversary of the Komsomol.

The final stage of the Physics Olympiad took place in the capital of the Turkmen SSR, Ashgabat. Its participants were 138 students: 39 eighth-graders, 50 ninth-graders and 49 tenth-graders. All of them are winners of district, city, regional and republican Olympiads in 1978 or winners of the previous All-Union Olympiad.

The opening of the final stage of the Olympids took place on April 13 at the city Palace of Pinoye. The Minister of Public Education of the Turkmen SSR B. Palvanova addressed the participants with a dark greeting. O. Ovezgeldev, Chairman of the Olympiad Jury, Vice-President of the Academy of Sciences of the TSSR, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, wished the children great success in the upcoming Olympic wrestling and in further studies in any field - physics. On behalf of the Komsomol members of the resurblik, the Secretary of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of the TSSR Zh. Charyeva wished the participants success at the Olympiad. The representative of the USSR Ministry of Education M. Grabilenkov in his greeting expressed confidence that the creative work at the Olympids, the dedication with which the children prepare for these competitions, is undeniable
will definitely help them in later life and work.

The first round of the final stage of the Olympiad - theoretical - took place on April 14. Participants were given the following tasks:

8th grade

1. The pendulum is a light rod of length l with a load of mass M at the end (Fig. 1). A light cylindrical bushing with an internal radius R is attached to the other end and is placed on a rotating horizontal axis. Friction coefficient between the bushing and the axis μ. Determine the angle of deflection of the rod from the vertical in equilibrium.

2. The graph (Fig. 2) shows the dependence of the current through the automatic light bulb on the voltage across it. The light bulb is connected to a constant voltage source U = 10 V in series with a resistance R = 4 Ohms. Determine the power of the light bulb.

3. The projectile explodes at a certain point in its trajectory into two fragments. On figure 3, made on a certain scale, the positions of the outside and one of the sedges at successive equal intervals of time are marked with crosses. Find the positions of the second sedge at the corresponding moments of time if it is known that it is at point B at the moment when the first sedge is at point A. The arrow at figure shows the direction of the acceleration of free fall. Describe your method of construction.

4. A hole of area S₂ was drilled in the bottom of a cylindrical pan of area S₁ and a plastic tube (Fig. 4) was inserted into it. The mass of the pan with the tube is equal to M. The pan stands on a flat sheet of rubber upside down. Carefully pour water from the top of the tube. To what level can you pour water without it flowing out from below?
