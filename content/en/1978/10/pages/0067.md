XII All-Union Olympiad
SCHOOLCHILDREN

N. Rozov, M. Smolyansky

Olympics
in mathematics

From April 13 to 18, the final round of the XII All-Union Schoolchildren Olympiad in Mathematics took place in Tashkent. 153 schoolchildren took part in it: winners of the Resource Olympiads and children who received diplomas of I and II degrees at the final type of the XI All-Union Olympiad. The final section was also hosted by a team of vocational school students (3 people) - winners of the Olympiad of vocational schools in Leningrad - and a team of the hosts of the Olympiad - schoolchildren from Tashkent (3 people).

In total, 38 eighth-graders, 63 ninth-graders, and 52 tenth-graders improved in the final type.

The grand opening of the final.type took place on April 13 in the assembly hall of the Presidium of the Academy of Sciences of the Uzbek SSR. Chairman of the organizing committee, Deputy Minister of Education of the Uzbek SSR. V.V. Barylnikov congratulated the participants of the final type Olympiad on the beginning of improvement. The chairman of the jury of the Olympiad, academician A. N. Kolmogorov, and the deputy chairman of the jury in

Vice President of the Academy of Sciences of the UzSSR, Chairman of the Supreme Council of the UzSSR Academician S. Kh. Sirazhdinov, Secretary of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Uzbekistan Sh. N. Makhmudova and other comrades.

Participants of the Olympiad laid flowers at the monument to V.I. Lenin and the grave of the Unknown Soldier. Young mathematicians took part in a traditional communist cleanup day.

The final stage of the Olympiad was held in two types, which took place on April 14 and 15.

Below we present the conditions for the problems in mathematics proposed at the XII All-Union Olympiad.

Quests

1. Let us denote by $a_n$ an integer close to $\sqrt{n}$. Find the amount

$$\frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \frac{1}{a_3} + \dots + \frac{1}{a_{1980}}.$$

2. Inside the quadrilateral $ABCD$ there is a point $M$ such that $ABMD$ is a parallelogram. Prove that if $CBM = CDM$ then $ACD = BCM$.

3. Prove that for no natural number $m$ the number $1978^m$ - 1 is divisible by $1000^m$ - 1.
