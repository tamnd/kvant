Below we reproduce an excerpt from the novel, where we are talking about one of the most important questions in the philosophy of the natural division of continuous motion into the first units, the source of most human misconceptions.

The so-called sophism of the ancients is known, which consists in the fact that Achilles will never talk forward and the soul of the scoop, despite the fact that Achilles walks ten times faster than the scoop: as soon as Achilles passes the space separating him from the scoop, the scoop will pass ahead of him one tenth of this space; Achilles will go through this tenth, the scoop will go through one hundredth, etc. ad infinitum. This task seemed insoluble to the ancients. The meaninglessness of the decision (that Achilles would never finish speaking to the scoop) stemmed from the fact that the first units of movement were arbitrarily allowed, while the movements of both Achilles and the scoop were carried out continuously.

By accepting smaller and smaller units of movement, we only get closer to solving the problem, but never achieve it. Only by admitting an infinitesimal quantity and an ascending progression from it to one tenth and taking the sum of this geometric progression do we achieve a solution to the problem. A new branch of mathematics, having achieved the art of dealing with infinitesimal quantities, and in other more complex issues of motion, now provides answers to questions that seemed insoluble.

This new, unknown to the ancients, branch of mathematics, when considering issues of motion, admits infinitesimal quantities, that is, also at which the main condition of motion (absolute continuity) is restored, thereby correcting the inevitable mistake that a person cannot help but make when considering individual units of motion together with continuous motion.
