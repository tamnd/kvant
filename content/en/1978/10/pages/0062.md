Non-crossing paths

Imagine that you need to continue paths connecting pairs of $k$ points, and do this in such a way that the paths do not intersect each other. For which $k$ can this be done? No matter how hard you try, if these points lie on a plane or on a sphere, then you won’t be able to do more than try in this way. True, it is quite difficult to prove this strictly.
