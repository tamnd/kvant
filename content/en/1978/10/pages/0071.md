At the official stage of the XII All-Union Olympiad, 11 first degree diplomas were awarded (4 diplomas in 8 classes, 4 diplomas in 9 classes and 3 diplomas in 10 classes), 32 second degree diplomas (11 in 8 classes, 10 in 9 classes and 11 in 10 classes) and 24 third degree diplomas (4 - in 8 grades, 9 - in 9 grades, 11 - in 10 grades); in addition, 80 participants were loaded with complimentary reviews.

We publish the names of the winners on p. 82.

Particularly noting the successful performance of schoolchildren from the city of Angarsk at the Olympiad, the jury awarded the Angarsk city department of public education a special prize.

Many participants received special prizes established by various organizations and enterprises of Uzbekistan.

A special prize from the magazine «Kvant» was awarded to 8th grade student *Badim Sintsov* (Komsomolsky village, Tyumen region).
