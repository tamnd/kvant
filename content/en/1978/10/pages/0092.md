⟦folio 90⟧

Matematika - for the future worker

Who to be, what to devote your life to, where to go to study - these are the questions every young person faces on the threshold of independent life. Many, solving these issues, enter vocational institutions after the 8th grade, where, together with a secondary education, they receive a worker's specialty. For example, in Leningrad the number of students in vocational schools already exceeds the number of students in grades 9-10. Modern vocational schools have excellent classrooms, laboratories, reading rooms - in a word, everything that is necessary to receive a full-fledged general education. Of course, offices and equipment are not the most important thing. Now a lot is being done to ensure that the good traditions accumulated by our school are transferred to vocational schools. «Kvant» already wrote about olympids in mathematics and physics for vocational school students, during the three years of their organization in Leningrad. Twice a team of professional students performed at the final type of the All-Union Mathematical Olympiad. This year, more than a group of Leningrad vocational school graduates have been recommended for admission to double departments of universities. In many schools there are mathematical and physical circles, mathematics weeks are held, and research workers and institute teachers give lectures to the children.

Sometimes they ask the question - why does a future worker need difficult mathematical concepts, problems, circles, Olympids? We will not campaign at length for doing mathematics. I would like to talk about the enormous satisfaction that a person receives for whom mathematics becomes a friend and a necessary companion. There are more and more such people studying at vocational schools, and the magazine «Kvant» will be happy to help them in their promise with our ancient and eternal young science.

In February of this year, the IV Physics and Mathematics Olympid for professional students took place in Leningrad, which has become traditional; For the first time, the Olympiad was held in chemistry. The Olympics were held in two rounds. Almost all students from 149 vocational schools in the city and region took part in the first type of Olympiad. The second and final round, organized by LETI this year, brought together about 500 winners of the first type. We solved problems for four hours, then actively participated in the analysis of solutions
