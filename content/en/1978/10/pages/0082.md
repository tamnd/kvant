6) Determination of inductance $L$ and capacitance $C_2$.

We connect the generator to terminals 2-3 and the avometer as an ampere (Fig. 5, 6). By changing the frequency of the generator, a minimum current strength is added to the circuit. The frequency $v_p$ at which resonance occurs is related to the parameters of the circuit:

$$v_p' = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC_2}}.$$

We close terminals 1—3, turn on the avometer as an amperator, connect the generator to terminals 2—(1, 3) (Fig. 5, 8). Changing the generator part, we find the resonant part $v_p'$:

$$v_p' = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{L(C_1 + C_2)}}.$$

The found values ​​of the frequencies $v_p$ and $v_p'$ allow us to calculate $L$ and $C_2$.
