M526—M530; F538—F542

M526. a) Prove that the area of ​​a convex quadrilateral is
$$S = \frac{tg\varphi |a^2 - b^2 + c^2 - d^2|}{4},$$
where $a, b, c$ and $d$ are the lengths of successive sides of the quadrilateral, $\varphi$ is the size of the angle between its diagonals, $\varphi \neq 90^\circ$.

6) Is it possible to find the area of ​​$S$, knowing $a, b, c$ and $d$, if $\varphi = 90^\circ$?

U. Alla

M527. Let $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n$ be real numbers, $0 \leqslant x_i \leqslant 1$. Prove that the quantity
$$x_1 + x_2 + \dots + x_{n-1} + x_n = x_1x_2 \cdots x_nx_1$$
does not exceed
a) 1 at $n=3$;
b) 2 at $n=4$;
b) $^* [n/2]$ for any $n \geqslant 3$.

V. Batyshev, 10th grade student

M528. There is a piece on each square of the chessboard (Fig. 1). The chips must be presented so that the distance between each pair of chips does not decrease compared to the distance between them at the original location. How can this be done? (The distance between the pieces is the distance between the centers of the cells they occupy.)

M. Bershadsky

M529. a) Polygon $M'$ is the image of the convex polygon $M$ with coefficient $k = -1/2$. Prove that there is a parallel translation $T$ such that $T(M') \subset M$.

6) At what coefficients $k < 0$ is a similar statement true for the convex polygon $M$ in space?

H. Nechevmaev
