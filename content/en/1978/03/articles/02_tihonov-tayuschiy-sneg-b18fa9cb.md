MELTING
SNOW

These photographs were taken in different places and in different weather. Look at them and try to answer these questions. In what weather (cold or thawed) did the snow melt on different roofs? Which of these roofs is better than thermal insulation? Why does snow cover the peaked roof so “selectively”? And on one roof the snow lies in clumps.
Why?
Why did the snow melt at the foot of the birch trunk?
A bizarre snow garland on the branches was formed simply during a heavy snowfall. It was found and formed in a forest near Moscow by Lena Levkovich.

V. Tikhonov
