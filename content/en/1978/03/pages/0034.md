Will the statement be true for any quadrilateral (1), two triangles (2 and 3), three angles (4, 5 and 6), four “infinite triangles” - areas bounded by a segment and two rays (7, 8, 9 and 10) and an “infinite quadrilateral” - an area limited by two segments and two rays (11).
