Physical law

Great discoveries are made purely by listening.

The Hedgehog and the Lion meet purely listening in the forest.
“Get ready, Hedgehog,” says Lev, “now I’ll hit you.”
The Hedgehog got ready: he curled up into a ball, you won’t understand where his soul is and where his heels are.
The lion waved and - bang! What's the matter? According to all calculations, the Hedgehog should have flown three meters, but he flew only one and a half meters. And for the remaining one and a half the Lion flew away. And these meters seemed not enough for him: he put his tail between his legs and off he went!
“An interesting phenomenon,” thought the Hedgehog, “I’ll have to check it out!”

He began to check it, as required in a scientific study. I did experiments on both wolves and bears.
Everything was confirmed: the stronger the blow, the farther the animal flies. This is how the Hedgehog discovered the law: action is equal to reaction.
This was a great scientific discovery. Until now, they had only acted in the forest, but no one had dared to resist. Now I perceived everything in spirit. Hares, beavers, gophers - all the small creatures of the forest came out of their holes, stealthily towards the Lion.
“Well,” he says, “hit it!”
The lion began to strike. He interrupted the people - it’s scary to watch.

- This is not according to the law! - the little one is indignant. - According to the law, action is equal to reaction!
Lev strikes. He doesn't care about laws.
And then one Suslik was found. The experience was summed up by the additional Hedgehog Law:
Action equals reaction - this is a physical law.
But where physical force operates, physical laws are inactive.
In science, this law is extracted under the name of the Hedgehog-Suslin law.

From the book by F. Krivin “The Teaching of Fairy Tales”
