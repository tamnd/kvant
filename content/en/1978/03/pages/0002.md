March 1978 marks the 70th anniversary of the birth of the editor of the journal «Kvant», outstanding Soviet physicist, deputy director of the Institute of Atomic Energy I.V. Kurchatov, Hero of Socialist Labor, laureate of the Lenin and State Prizes of the USSR, academician ISAAC KONSTANTINOVICH KIKOIN.
In addition to his fruitful scientific work, Isaac Konstantinovich devotes a lot of time and energy to the pedagogical problems of secondary school. He is one of the authors of the new physics program and textbook “Physics 8”, chairman of the Physics Commission of the Educational and Methodological Council of the USSR Ministry of Education and chairman of the Central Organizing Committee of the All-Union School Olympiad.
The editorial board and editors of the magazine «Kvant», on their own behalf and on behalf of many thousands of readers, congratulate Isaac Konstantinovich on his seventieth birthday and wish him many years of life, great personal happiness, new successes in scientific and pedagogical activities.
