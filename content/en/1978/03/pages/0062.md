Publishing house "Mir"
5. Barr S. Russia of straw-breakers. Translation from English. Volume 23 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 2 rubles.
This collection continues the series of books on fascinating mathematics. It is made up of three small books written by American writer and mathematics enthusiast Stevens Barr. Published in the United States in three separate editions: “Russian Puzzles,” “New Russian Puzzles,” and “Topological Experiments,” these books were a great success among readers, even more than the success of Barr’s fictional works.
The first two books (in this edition they are combined in the first part) contain fascinating and witty puzzles, not only mathematical ones, but also “puzzles from everyday life.” The third book, in a popular and engaging form, introduces the reader to topology, one of the most important branches of modern mathematics. Barr's "Topological Experiments" can certainly contribute to the awakening of interest in topology and the development of some topological intuitions.
The book will be accessible and interesting to a wide range of readers.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Kompanets A. S. Symmetry in the macro- and microworld. Volume 10 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.
This book consists of 3 popular science articles: “About symmetry”, “Symmetry in the microworld” and “The world of the theory of relativity”. All these articles are subordinated to one idea - to show the role of symmetry in modern physics.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers and is quite accessible to high school students.
2. Kresin V.Z. Severity and superfluidity. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.
The book talks in an interesting and fascinating way about the need for supercontinuity and superfluidity, which can be achieved at low temperatures. Avoiding excessive enthusiasm for mathematics, the author places the main emphasis on a qualitative explanation of the corresponding patterns.
The book will be read with interest by high school students interested in physics.
3. Landdu L. D., China city A. I. Physics for everyone. Physical tag. 4th edition, rev. and additional Volume 10 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.
The purpose of this book is to give the reader in a popular form a clear idea of ​​the basic ideas and achievements of modern physics.
The book is written original, fresh and with great taste. Good design of the book promotes understanding of the issues presented.
The book is designed for a wide range of readers.
4. Eggel F. Yu. Astromons by observer. Volume 10 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.
The book provides a fascinating account of how stroms observe the sky (from ancient times to the present day). The reader will become acquainted with the main astronomical instruments - from the telescope to the radio telecom.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
5. Ryabov Yu. A. Two celestial themes. 3rd edition, revised. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.
The book contains a popular account of the history of the emergence of celestial mechanics and its main problems concerning the specific movements of celestial bodies: planets, asteroids, satellites of planets, stars. A large section of the book is devoted to the movement of artificial Earth satellites and automatic interplanetary stations.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.
6. Heisenberg V. The meaning and beauty of physics. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 65 kopecks.
The book contains popular science articles and speeches by Werner Carl Heisenberg (1901-1976), a classic of modern science, one of the creators of quantum theory. The collection also includes articles about Heisenberg by prominent Soviet and foreign physicists.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Mir"
7. Elshtein P. To the young designer of rockets and spaceships. Translation from user. Volume 16 l., circulation 20,000 copies, price 1 rub. 15 k.
The book is intended for children interested in the design of space rockets. It examines a wide range of issues related to the calculation of parameters and design of models of rockets and spacecraft. These are recommendations for the production of various fuel mixtures, ensuring launch safety, as well as methods that guarantee a safe landing of the model using brake (parachute) stability. A detailed description of the record-breaking models, awarded with the highest awards at the latest international competitions, is given, the original models of rocket-propelled Soyuz spacecraft, a rocket with folding wings in flight, a two-stage “boomerang rocket”, which always returns to the starting point, etc.
The book is intended for the general reader.

The book is designed for a wide range of readers.
