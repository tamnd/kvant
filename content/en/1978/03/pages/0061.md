Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Vorobiev N. N. Fibonacci numbers. 4th edition. Volume 6 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.
Fibonacci numbers, which arose from the meanings of the rabbit breeding problem, which is nearly seven hundred and fifty years old, still remain one of the most fascinating chapters of elementary mathematics. Assignments related to Fibonacci numbers are considered in school math circles, offered at math competitions, and presented in many

some popular books on mathematics. This book provides the most detailed description of the number-theoretic properties of Fibonacci numbers; in part, some complex issues related to divisibility are examined. In addition, the connections of Fibonacci numbers with continued fractions and geometry are demonstrated, and their role in the theory of ponics is shown.
The book is intended for high school students.
2. Postnikov M. M. Fermat's theorem. Introduction to the theory of algebraic numbers. Volume 8 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 31 kopecks.
The book is an introduction to the theory of algebraic numbers. The basic concepts and ideas of this theory are presented in it in connection with Fermat's theorem. One of the goals of the books is to convince the reader of the depth and complexity of the problem associated with Fermat’s theorem, and of the complete futility of its elementary proof.
The book is presented in such a way that even a reader with minimal training can grasp its main ideas.
The book is intended for high school students (the first chapters), students, teachers and all mathematics lovers who want to get acquainted with the theorems of algebraic numbers in its classical aspect.
3. Sobol I.M. Monte-Carlo method. 3rd edition, add. Volume 3 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.
This book is written on the basis of lectures given by the author over several years to the dryers of the Faculty of Computer Science. Public university. In 1971 she was awarded a certificate of honor. All-Union competition for the best teaching aids for national universities.
The Monte Carlo method is a numerical method for solving mathematical problems by modeling random variables. Sometimes the Monte-Carlo method is called the greatness of the complex number, the geometric study of complex polynomials in complex variables and gives the geometric idea of ​​​​proving the fundamental theorem of algebra (that every polynomial of degree n has exactly n roots).
Each chapter of the book is equipped with an addition that relates not to plane, but to space.
The book will be useful for high school students, as well as teachers and higher schools.
