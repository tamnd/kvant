3. Let us write down the law of conservation of energy taking into account the change in energy of the “larger” body - the airplane. In the first reporting system
$$\Pi = \frac{mv^2}{2} + \Delta K'_c,$$
in the second system
$$\Pi = \frac{m (v + u)^2}{2} - \frac{mu^2}{2} + \Delta K'_c.$$
In the first reporting system, the aircraft has accumulated, and the change in its energy during a shot can be neglected. Let's show it.
$$\Delta K'_c = \frac{M \Delta u^2}{2};$$
the change in speed $|\vec{u}|$ is determined from the law of conservation of momentum: $|\Delta u| = \frac{m |\vec{u}|}{M}$; hence,
$$\Delta K'_c = \frac{mv^2}{2} \cdot \frac{m}{M}.$$
In the “Earth” reporting system, the change in the energy of the aircraft cannot be neglected, since before the shot it had a speed of $u$ relative to the Earth. Let's calculate the change in energy of the aircraft $\Delta K'_c$:
$$\Delta K'_c = \frac{M (u - \Delta u)^2}{2} - \frac{Mu^2}{2} = - M |\vec{u}| |\vec{u}| + \frac{M \Delta u^2}{2}.$$
Since the change in aircraft speed $|\Delta u|$ is an invariant quantity, then $|\Delta u| = \frac{m |\vec{u}|}{M}$, and
$$\Delta K'_c = - m |\vec{u}| |\vec{u}| + \frac{mv^2}{2} \cdot \frac{m}{M}.$$
So,
$$\Pi = \frac{mv^2}{2} + \frac{mv^2}{2} \cdot \frac{m}{M},$$
$$\Pi = \frac{m (v + u)^2}{2} - \frac{mu^2}{2} + \frac{mv^2}{2} \cdot \frac{m}{M} - m |\vec{u}| |\vec{u}|.$$
From these two expressions it also follows
$$(v + u)^2 = (v + u)^2.$$
