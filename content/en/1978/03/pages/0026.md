Fig. 9.

BLN cools it down a little, bringing out the externalities of both the limitations of the model and the “half-truth” it contains.

The BLN model is capable of simulating only one structure: a two-dimensional hexagonal close-fit structure. And true crystals have many structures. The possibilities of a “dead” model are enormously powerful: in three-dimensional space, balls can be positioned in a myriad of ways and, therefore, any mental structure can be modeled. In addition, the BLN model in its modern version is two-dimensional. Its authors also tried to implement a spatial (multilayer) bubble model, but experimenting with it turned out to be not at all easy, and the model did not take root. In our laboratory, we implemented both a two-dimensional and three-dimensional model of BLN and were convinced that a three-dimensional practical one was not viable.

Let's not blame the model for its weaknesses, which were mentioned and which were kept silent. Let us be grateful to her for her strengths.
