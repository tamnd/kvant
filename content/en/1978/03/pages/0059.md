Problem 2. The change in the kinetic energy of the bullet in the considered cut-off systems is different:

$$\Delta K_1 = -\frac{mv^2}{2},$$
$$\Delta K_2 = -\frac{mv^2}{2} + m |\vec{v}| |\vec{u}|,$$
$$\Delta K_3 = -\frac{mv^2}{2} - m |\vec{v}| |\vec{u}|.$$

According to the theorem on kinetic energy - $A = \Delta K$ - the work of the external force, the force acting on the bullet from the side of the mountain, is also different. In other words, the consequence of relative movement in various systems is cut off by the relative work of the external force, and, consequently, the change in kinetic energy of the non-closed system of bodies is also relative.

The paradox we came to in this problem is caused by a recording error

$$Q = -\Delta K.$$

This expression is valid only for closed systems of bodies, since for such systems the change in the kinetic energy of all bodies of the system is inverant (i.e., unchanged) relative to any inertial cutoff systems.

So in problem 2, the amount of released body is $Q = |\Delta K_\pi| + |\Delta K_3|$, where $\Delta K_\pi$ and $\Delta K_3$ are the changes in the kinetic energies of the bullet and the Earth. However, the answer obtained in point 1) is correct, this is the amount of heat that is released, no matter in which system we solve the problem:

$$Q = |\Delta K_1| = \frac{mv^2}{2}.$$

Let's prove this statement. To do this, let us strictly carry out all our previous reasoning:

1) In the cut-off system in which the Earth (mountain) initially ends, the energy of the system is equal to the kinetic energy of the bullet $\frac{mv^2}{2}$. When a bullet hits a mountain, the Earth acquires a certain speed $\vec{u}$, which we can find from the law of conservation of momentum: $M |\vec{u}| = m |\vec{v}|$, hence $|\vec{u}| = \frac{m}{M} |\vec{v}|$. Consequently, the Earth acquires kinetic energy $\Delta K'_3 = \frac{Mu^2}{2}$, and the law of conservation of energy all systems must be written in the form

$$|\Delta K_\pi| = \frac{mv^2}{2} = \frac{m}{M} \cdot \frac{mv^2}{2} + Q,$$

or

$$Q = \frac{mv^2}{2} - \frac{m}{M} \cdot \frac{mv^2}{2}.$$

Using the conditions of the “larger” body - $m \ll M$, we get

$$Q = \frac{mv^2}{2} = |\Delta K_1|!$$
