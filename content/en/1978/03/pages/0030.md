Relation (*), although it does not allow constructing the segment $CD$ using a compass and ruler, often turns out to be useful in the practical solution of problems of this type. If there is one settlement on each bank, then the relation (*) gives $\cos \alpha + \cos \beta = 0$, from where $\beta = \pi + \alpha$ (Fig. 6) - that is, the segments $AC$ and $BD$ must be parallel. In this case, the segment $CD$ can be constructed using a compass and a ruler (construct it!).

So, we have seen that the mechanical method turns out to be very useful for solving geometric problems of the type considered. In conclusion, we offer readers several tasks to solve on their own.
