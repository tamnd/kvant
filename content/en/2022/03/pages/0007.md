In addition to developing the actual design of the tower, Vladimir Grigorievich comes up with a unique construction method. There were no cranes of the required height at that time, and structures were usually built in forests. But according to Shukhov’s telescopic method, even scaffolding was not needed! All sections of the tower were assembled on the ground. Blocks were installed on the top ring of the finished structure, through which the cables went to the Fig. 9 bottom of the new section. Its bottom was pulled together so that it would pass through the equal upper neck of the already erected structure. And then manually, using winches, the next section rose to the top. (Fig. 8, 9) “The rise of the second section began on April 16, 1921. On the 18th it was raised by 8 m, on the 19th by 16, on the 20th by 24. On the 21st during the day by half a meter. The section was installed at seven o’clock in the evening on April 21, 1921,” an entry from Shukhov’s diary.

How many blocks are needed - how many points to lift the next section? The mathematical answer is 3. But if one winch lags a little, the structure will warp and fall. If we assume the possibility of any two winches falling behind, then even five blocks are not enough. At the first stages - the sections are heavy, and, in addition, the method is being tested - Shukhov uses 6 blocks arranged in a regular hexagon. And only the last two sections were decided to be raised on three blocks. Not everything went smoothly during the construction of the tower. From the diary of V. G. Shukhov: “June 29, 1921. While lifting the fourth section, the third broke. The fourth fell and damaged the second and first at seven o'clock in the evening" (Fig. 10). The commission's findings confirmed that the accident occurred due to poor-quality metal supplied, and not design errors. But we need to repair the first two sections, rebuild the third...

From V. G. Shukhov’s diary of 1921: “October 27. The rise of the third section has begun. Cold wind. Work is going smoothly. The hexagon is working very well so far. October 28. The rise continues. The section is raised halfway up the second floor. October 29. The climb is over […]. After lunch, consolidation began. Additional lifts of 2 and 3 inches. November 18. The fourth section is almost finished. I was on the tower.
