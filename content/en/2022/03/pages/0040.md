Final stage

The final stage of the Olympiad was held on February 24–26, 2022 in a remote format on the portal https: exam.distant.msu.ru. To participate in the final stage, certain technical requirements were imposed on the test subject’s workplace (these can be found on the Olympiad website). The assignment for students in grades 7–9 consisted of four problems on topics studied as part of the physics program for the basic secondary school. The tasks for students in grades 10–11 were compiled in full accordance with the 2021 Unified State Exam Codifier in Physics and covered the main sections of the Codifier, namely: 1) mechanics, 2) molecular physics and thermodynamics, 3) electrodynamics and 4) optics.
