PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2690 – M2693 were offered at the regional stage of the XLVIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics. Problem F2697 – F2700 was offered at the Engineering Olympiad for schoolchildren in the 2021/22 academic year. The author of the problems is S. Muravyov.

Find the largest possible value of n. A. Antropov
