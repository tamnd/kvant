November 24, 25. I was on the tower. The upper bipeds are installed and the section is ready to be lifted. November 28. I was on the tower. In the evening we loaded the bottom and raised it 6 inches. November 29. The section began to rise. It's cold on the tower. Frost 13 qC. November 30. The rise continues. By evening the section was raised 40 meters. December 1st. Raised to the top. The section went beyond the sixth ring. December 2. By 12 o'clock the section was in place. At 2 o'clock steeplejacks, 12 people, rose to secure the position. Frost 12 qС. December 3. The work continues to secure the fourth section. December 24. The fifth section is ready for lifting. December 26. Start of lifting the fifth section. […] Test of climbing on three bipeds instead of six. December 27. The fifth section is raised to half the height.

From V. G. Shukhov’s diary of 1922: “February 9. In the morning the sixth section was installed. The weather is calm, -8 qС. By 4 o'clock 8 legs were strengthened. The tower is beautiful, only the fifth section has a sparse grid. February 10. All 24 legs of the sixth section are secured. February 14. The ring of the sixth section is installed, and the tower is thus completed. We started installing the top with the obligation to complete the work in 10 days. The work of processing and installing four sections and repairing the two lower sections lasted five months. All work lasted two years. 2.5 years have passed since the conclusion of the contract with Telesvyaz for the construction of the tower. February 21. The upper booth was raised, and the wooden mast began to be raised. A drawing of the flag is given. February 22. The wooden mast was raised and we began to jib. February 23. One jib is raised, with traction. The rise of the second jib began. February 28. The tower is finished and the mast is erected."

Over the past decades, several high-rise hyperboloid structures have appeared in different countries. And all their authors acknowledge the enormous contribution that the great Russian engineer and scientist Vladimir Grigorievich Shukhov made to the development of such structures.
