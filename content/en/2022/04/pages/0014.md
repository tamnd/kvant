Bashmakov’s new supervisor, Dmitry Konstantinovich Faddeev, is the head of the algebra laboratory (and at the same time a good pianist). A few years later, on the basis of boarding school No. 45, a Correspondence Mathematics School, the famous ZMSH, was created, which still captivates thousands of children from all over the big country with mathematics (including one of the authors of this text). I remember Bashmakov’s first methodological book “Equations and Inequalities”, published in the series “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” in 1976. This small book became the forerunner of a whole line of school textbooks on mathematics, some of which are unique. For example, a textbook for the humanities is not a simplification of a school mathematics course, but gives an idea of ​​mathematics from a more philosophical perspective. This textbook reflects not only Bashmakov’s methodological ideas as a mathematics teacher, but also the author’s passion for Silver Age poetry and fine arts. From 1977 to 1991, Bashmakov headed the Department of Higher Mathematics No. 2 at the Leningrad Electrotechnical Institute. The department was created specifically for Bashmakov - with the goal of organizing a strong mathematical school at LETI. Bashmakov assembled a strong team at the department, connecting scientists actively working in mathematics (topologist V.M. Kharlamov, analyst E.M. Dynkin, specialist in discrete mathematics S.V. Fomin, logician S.V. Solovyov, specialist in probability theory V.A. Egorov, etc.) with specialists in methods of teaching mathematics. The traditions laid down by Bashmakov are still preserved by the department. In the 90s, Bashmakov was elected to the Leningrad City Council, where he headed the Education Commission and participated in the reorganization of education authorities. At this time, according to his plan, a Center for the Development of Alternative Education is created, within which the city’s initiative teams in various areas of school activity are united. These teams became pioneers in introducing new pedagogical technologies into the city’s education. On the initiative of Bashmakov, the “Mathematics for All” program was launched in Russia, the connecting link of which was the international competition “Kangaroo”, collecting

reaching up to two million Russian schoolchildren annually. Mark Ivanovich organized similar competitions in other subjects, for example, the “British Bulldog” in English, the close-in-spirit “Golden Fleece” competition in the history of world culture, etc. At the age of 30, i.e. Quite late, Bashmakov became seriously interested in mountaineering, and in all his hobbies he achieved perfection. Mark Ivanovich became the chairman of the mountaineering section at the university. Students watched with bated breath as he climbed a tall brick building in the university courtyard - the building of the Twelve Colleges - to secure a safety net for the section participants. Mark Ivanovich Bashmakov devoted more than 50 years to mountaineering, and in terms of the time he spent in the mountains, half his life, as he himself believed. The last time he was in the mountains was in 2021 on his birthday - according to tradition, he celebrated this day on the mountain peaks. And since his birthday is in February, he has become one of the active supporters of winter ascents. For conquering five seven-thousanders he was awarded the “Snow Leopard” badge. Another side of Mark Ivanovich’s multifaceted nature was his passion for book graphics. His collection contains more than 500 books, true works of art. These books were published in a small number of copies, with the artist and writer being co-authors. They merge the world of literature, the world of graphics and the world of printing. In 2013, the Hermitage hosted the first exhibition of books from Bashmakov’s collection, entitled “The Artist’s Book”. Later, such exhibitions were held every year, and Mark Ivanovich often conducted excursions himself. In 2015, the Artist’s Book Cabinet was opened in the eastern wing of the General Staff Building, and Mark Ivanovich became an adviser to the director of the Hermitage. Hermitage employees consider Bashmakov a new type of collector - not so much a collector of books, but a thoughtful researcher of them. In all his activities, Mark Ivanovich Bashmakov was extremely talented. The bright memory of him as a man of great stature and extraordinary talents will forever be preserved by everyone who was lucky enough to communicate and work with him. S. Pozdnyakov, S. Entina
