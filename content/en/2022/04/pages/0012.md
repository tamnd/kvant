This formula proves that the limiting value of the efficiency of heat exchange organized according to the Machine method is equal to unity. Now we are ready with good reason to draw a final conclusion: A large number of “honest” exchange processes can lead to “completely dishonest” results. By increasing the number of glasses, you can cool the former hot water almost to zero, and heat the water in the initially cold glasses almost to a temperature of $T_{0}$. - Well done, Masha! “I came up with a good method while some were looking out the window,” our teacher said and for some reason looked at me. “But those lagging behind still have chances. Of course, in a sense, it will not be possible to surpass the Machine method - in the limit it gives an efficiency value equal to one. But it is clear that efficiency cannot be greater than one. But who knows, what if there is a method that gives a faster pursuit of unity than Machines 1 n? Think about it.
