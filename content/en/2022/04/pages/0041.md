3. (8) Is it true that from any convex quadrilateral one can cut three halved copies of this quadrilateral? A.Yuran

4. (9) For each of the nine natural numbers n, 2n, 3n,…, 9n, write down the first digit from the left. For some natural number n, can there be no more than four different digits among the nine written down? A.Tolpygo

5. (9) Sasha’s collection consists of coins and stickers, and there are fewer coins than stickers, but there is at least one. Sasha chose some positive number t! 1 (not necessarily an integer). If he increases the number of coins by t times without changing the number of stickers, then his collection will have 100 items. If instead he increases the number of stickers by t times without changing the number of coins, then he will have 101 items. How many stickers could Sasha have? Find all possible answers and prove that there are no others. A. Galochkin

6. (9) Some squares of the 100 and 100 board are painted black. In all rows and columns where there are black cells, their number is odd. In each line where there are black cells, place a red chip in the middle black cell. In each column where there are black cells, we will place a blue chip in the middle black cell. It turned out that all the red chips are in different columns, and the blue chips are in different rows. Prove that there is a cell containing both blue and red chips. B.Frenkin

9. (11) In a Cartesian coordinate system (with the same scale along the x and y axes), a graph of the exponential function $y 3^{x}$ was drawn. How can you restore the y-axis using a compass and ruler? M. Evdokimov

10. (11) The spaceship is located in a half-space at a distance a from its boundary. The crew is aware of this, but has no idea in which direction to move to reach the boundary plane. The spaceship can fly in space along any trajectory, measuring the length of the path traveled, and has a sensor that gives a signal when the boundary is reached. Can a starship be guaranteed to reach the border, covering a path no more than 14a long? M. Evdokimov

11. (11) In a convex 12-gon, all angles are equal. It is known that the lengths of some ten of its sides are equal to 1, and the length of one more is equal to 2. What could be the area of ​​this 12-gon? M. Evdokimov

12. (11) A diagonal is drawn in an isosceles trapezoid. A beetle crawls along the contour of each of the resulting two triangles. The speeds of movement of beetles are constant and identical. The beetles do not change the direction they walk around their contours, and along the diagonal of the trapezoid they crawl in different directions. Prove that for any initial position of the beetles they will meet someday. P. Borodin
