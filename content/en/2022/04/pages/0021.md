F2704. A straight, clear-walled plastic tube, open at both ends, with a circular internal cross-section. Then the tube, maintaining its vertical position, was raised, and a column of mercury a 20 cm long remained in it. Holding the open end of the tube above the bucket, the tube was slowly turned over 180°, and now its open end was above the surface of the mercury. The column of mercury remained near the end of the tube (now the top). External atmospheric pressure 760 mm Hg.
