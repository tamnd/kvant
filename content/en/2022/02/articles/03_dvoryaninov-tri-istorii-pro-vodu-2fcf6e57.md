However, it turns out that there is something interesting and instructive to tell about him too. So... Story one. At the end of December, Tolik Vtulkin had to wake up very early - their family was supposed to arrive at the airport for the first morning flight. Tolik noticed that the water from the cold tap was much warmer than usual. And from the tap with hot water, on the contrary, cool water flowed. Tolik guessed what was going on. Obviously, at night the water flow is small, and the water in the pipes inside the house stagnates. In this case, cold water heats up and hot water cools down. The second story. In recent years, two-tap mixers have been replaced by ball mixers. Such mixers, also called joystick or articulated, like any other, are used to mix cold water with hot water and obtain the required temperature as a result of the required water flow. And here we are faced, at first glance, with a paradox. Every operating faucet, as already mentioned, is characterized by two parameters: water flow and its temperature. These two parameters (output) are a response to two other parameters - hot water flow and cold water flow. In old taps we turn two knobs and get what we want. And the ball valve has only one lever! How can we control two parameters with one lever? Previously, even the coachman had a left rein and a right one, and with a whip he set the horses to the desired speed. In fact, there is no paradox. The position of the ball valve lever is determined by two parameters, just as the position of a point on the surface of the globe is also determined by two parameters - longitude and latitude. Let's assume that in the evening we set the desired outlet water temperature and want to return to it in the morning. In this case, we only reduce the latitude (i.e. water flow) to zero. In this case, valve 2 operates. As a result, the ball valve will “remember” the temperature, and there will be no need to regulate it next time. This circumstance is declared one of the advantages of ball mixers.
