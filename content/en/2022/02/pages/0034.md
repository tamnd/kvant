Are you so familiar with physical principles?

"You can't see the forest for the trees." In the same way, the multitude of particular physical laws sometimes obscures the deeper, more basic statements called principles, although these are often what they embody. Our column did not hide them - the “Kaleidoscope” presented the principles of relativity, conservation, superposition, and virtual displacements... But, of course, their list is not limited to this, and today we will get to know some more of this noble cohort. Those of you who continue to study science seriously will have amazing encounters with new principles, including some that go beyond even the vast discipline of physics. Well, what remains is fundamentally, sparing no effort, to move towards their comprehension. It's worth it...
