It would seem, what is so interesting about the water supply? The invention, of course, is old and very useful, but nothing more. However, it turns out that there is something interesting and instructive to tell about him too. So... Story one. At the end of December, Tolik Vtulkin had to wake up very early - their family was supposed to arrive at the airport for the first morning flight. Tolik noticed that the water from the cold tap was much warmer than usual. And from the tap with hot water, on the contrary, cool water flowed. Tolik guessed what was going on. Obviously, at night the water flow is small, and the water in the pipes inside the house stagnates. In this case, cold water heats up and hot water cools down. The second story. It was in the kitchen. Tolik opened the cold water tap, but boiling water poured out of the tap. He almost burned his hand! How did this happen? figure 1 shows the mixer that was in Tolik’s kitchen. Pay attention to the mixer jib. To get water, taps are opened and then closed. But after this, a considerable amount of it remains in the gander. Moreover, if hot water flowed from the mixer, then it will remain in the gander. If you now immediately open the cold water tap, the cold water will begin to displace the hot water. And first of all, the hot water remaining there, which has not had time to cool, will pour out of the mixer. So be careful! After opening the tap, let the water drain a little, and only then carefully test to see if it is too hot. As they say, if you don’t know the ford, don’t poke your nose into the water.
