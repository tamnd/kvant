The process of making a zero-resistance surface can be imagined as follows. In the case of a figure of rotation (see Fig. 19), we take a cylinder, and then cut out from its middle part and throw out two truncated cones. In the case of two prisms (see Fig. 20, a) we take a rectangular parallelepiped and from its middle we cut out and throw out some of it (a non-convex polyhedron). It is natural to strive to ensure that the relative volume of the ejected part (i.e., the ratio of this volume to the volume of the original body, cylinder or parallelepiped) is as small as possible. Then a spaceship of this shape will fit more cargo. Which of the two methods is better? Let's formulate this question as an exercise.
