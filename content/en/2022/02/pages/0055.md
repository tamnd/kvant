Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (ZPTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University) (MIPT) is recruiting students in grades 7–10 from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation to study in grades 8–11 at the ZPTS for the 2022 23 academic year. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics, higher mathematics and the department of chemistry of MIPT, as well as MIPT graduates and other specialists who have extensive experience working with gifted students. The full training program is designed for 4 years - from 8th to 11th grade inclusive, but you can start training from any of the specified classes. Students of all departments upon completion of the eleventh grade receive a certificate of completion of the ZFTS and can add additional points upon admission to MIPT (detailed information is available on the MIPT website in the section “Changes in the procedure for recording individual achievements”). An online lecture on ZFTS programs is available for students and leaders of elective groups. Lectures are given by MIPT teachers.

CORRESPONDENCE DEPARTMENT (individual distance learning)

Phone: (495) 408-51-45 Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of the online Olympiad on the website https: zftsh.online in the subjects chosen for study. The Olympics starts on 03.23.2022 at 9.00 and ends on 03.27.2022 at 21.00 (Moscow time). You are given 4 hours to complete tasks for each subject. The decision of the admissions committee will be communicated by June 1, 2022 to the email address specified during registration. A certificate of enrollment in ZFTSH will also appear in your personal account. When registering on https: zftsh.online, you must attach a clearly readable copy of your school certificate.

FULL-TIME DEPARTMENT (correspondence $обучение^{с}$ by visiting face-to-face consultations)

Phone: (925) 755-55-80 VK group: https: vk.com vftsh Evening counseling centers operate for students in Moscow and the Moscow region under the ZFTSH program. Recruitment is carried out in September in two stages: • correspondence stage - testing on the website http: zftsh.online, • full-time stage - oral exams. More detailed information about full-time enrollment will be posted on the ZFTS websites in August 2022. Classes for full-time students are held in the educational buildings of MIPT in the cities of Dolgoprudny and Zhukovsky. Education for schoolchildren is free (as part of the admission plan); for those who do not pass the competition, only paid education is possible.
