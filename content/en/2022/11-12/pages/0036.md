Alexander Belyaev Prize

The Alexander Belyaev Literary Prize (“Belyaev Prize”) is an annual Russian literary prize awarded for scientific, artistic and popular science works. Named in honor of the Russian Soviet science fiction writer Alexander Romanovich Belyaev (1884–1942, author of the novels “The Head of Professor Dowell”, “Amphibian Man” and other works).

The Belyaev Prize was first awarded in 1990, among the laureates that year were Arkady and Boris Strugatsky for their science fiction novel “The Doomed City.” In 2022, the Alexander Belyaev Prize in the category “the magazine for the most interesting activity during the year” was awarded to our friends and colleagues - the magazine «Kvant». «Kvant» – a magazine for the curious. The main readers are schoolchildren, but adults can also find something interesting for themselves in it. The magazine is not only

talks about mathematics and physics, but also publishes informative, yet accessible and fascinating articles on linguistics, biology, and the history of science. «Kvant» has maintained the tradition of monthly problem competitions, the answers to which readers send to the editor, and the editors correspond with schoolchildren. Children whose names appear in their favorite magazine at the end of the year, when the list of competition winners is published, receive a powerful impetus for their studies. School library workers in different regions of Russia note that «Kvant» has become one of the most popular items among both schoolchildren and teachers. The design of the magazine is created by a large team of artists. It is always witty and varied, the magazine is a pleasure to pick up. The last page of the magazine cover is a colorfully illustrated, entertaining challenge. For each New Year, calendars are formed from them and from other picture tasks of the past year, which are already published not only in Russia. Materials from all issues for all years can also be found in Almanacs «Kvant». The magazine also publishes its own Library, three books have already been published in it. The magazine «Kvant», the first issue of which was published in January 2012, has become one of the brightest phenomena of Russian education in the 21st century. We congratulate the founder and permanent editor-in-chief of «Kvant» Sergei Dorichenko and his team on the well-deserved award and wish further success and expansion of the magazine’s audience!
