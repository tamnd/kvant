Statistically top class

K.KOKHAS

- It looks like you gained weight during your vacation? – Ogryza asked, making tea. On the table lay a freshly opened box of chocolates, there were bowls of jam and candied nuts, and cakes on a platter. “It was hard not to get fat,” answered Woodpecker Spyatel. – I lived in the “7 stars” hotel! - What a ridiculous name. — This is the hotel rating. Seven stars means “statistically highest class of service.” When you check into a hotel, they tell you: “We guarantee a very high class of service, but, unfortunately, although we try our best to prevent this, sometimes “unwanted incidents” occur that are beyond our control. Their probability is extremely low, but still positive.” - What other incidents? “For example, if I went on vacation with the cockroach Kuzka, then Kuzka would definitely turn out to be an “incident.” Seeing him, any head waiter would immediately lose his human appearance and begin to pour dichlorvos on everyone. But there are simpler “incidents” - you can drown, choke, fall out of a window... “Therefore, we offer you to check into our hotel in several copies at once. To do this, we will parallelize you. It is absolutely safe and does not hurt. You will get 5 Spyatlov woodpeckers, or 10, or 1000 - whatever you order. Even if one of them is unlucky, and this is an extremely rare case, nothing like this will happen to the rest of the clones. And we will generate for you an average impression of service, which will completely make up for a rare unpleasant incident and actually make its probability equal to zero.” - “How can I fit into your hotel in so many copies?” - “Nothing, nothing, don’t worry, we have an infinite number of rooms, and there will still be some available.”
