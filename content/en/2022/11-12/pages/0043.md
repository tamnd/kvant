IN MEMORY OF J.M.RA BB OTA

Joseph Mikhailovich Rabbot (03/07/1943 $–^{22.11.2022)}$

On November 22, 2022, Joseph Mikhailovich Rabbot, one of the brightest specialists in the field of mathematics education in our country, passed away. Joseph Mikhailovich's activities were extremely versatile: he taught at school and university, led math circles at Moscow State University, gave lectures throughout the country for schoolchildren and teachers, wrote books, numerous articles and textbooks on mathematics, actively worked in the magazine «Kvant» from the moment of its creation, organized various mathematical Olympiads, and worked on the jury of the All-Union Mathematical Olympiad for twelve years. In 1966, the organizer and director of the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School (VZMSh, later the All-Russian Correspondence Multi-Subject School), one of the greatest mathematicians of the 20th century, I.M. Gelfand, invited a talented graduate of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, an experienced leader of the university student

Zhenya Rabbot's mug to teach at his school. For half a century, Joseph Mikhailovich worked as deputy director of the VZMSH, and for many years headed its mathematical department. VZMSH is a unique institution; its wonderful teachers worked by correspondence by mail with children passionate about mathematics and physics from the most remote places of our country. This work was led by J.M. Rabbot. Personal contact with metropolitan mathematicians, their attention and support were of enormous importance for a student from a distant outback and influenced the choice of a future profession. Thousands and thousands of specialists in various fields of science and its applications are grateful to Joseph Mikhailovich Rabbot for the fact that many years ago they received from him by mail, first assignments and manuals, then reviews of their work, words of support, and an invitation to come to Moscow. Happy memory!
