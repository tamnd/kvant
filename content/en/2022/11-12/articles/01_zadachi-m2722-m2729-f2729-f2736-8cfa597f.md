M2723. It is known that among several banknotes whose denominations are pairwise different natural numbers, there are exactly N counterfeit ones. In one check, the detector determines the sum of the denominations of all real banknotes included in the set we have selected. F2732. Three non-conducting light rods of equal length 2R are rigidly fastened at their midpoints so that their ends are located at the vertices of a regular hexagon. At the ends of each rod there are identical small balls with identical masses m. Two balls located in adjacent corners of the hexagon have the same electric charges Q, and the remaining balls are not charged. A structure made of rods can rotate around its vertical axis of symmetry, i.e. all the balls can move along the same circular path of radius R. The experimenter Vasya has another long non-conducting rod with the same small ball at the end. And the charge of this ball is –Q. The structure rotated very slowly. Vasya waited for the moment and quickly placed the ball on his rod exactly in the middle between two adjacent balls of the structure. At that moment, when the distances between his (stationary) ball and the balls of this pair began to differ by a factor of two, Vasya quickly moved his ball to a great distance from the structure. Vasya performed this operation N times. At what angular velocity does the structure rotate after this? There is no friction.
