only when there is some kind of inheritance, primarily in the form of land. Of course, Professor Thomson did not have any land ownership. He preferred to have an apartment on the university campus, like many other university teachers. However, when, in accordance with the university regulations, he ceased to be a teaching professor due to his age, and became “merely” an emeritus professor, he would not be allowed to live on campus. So what does Kelvin do? He has friends on campus and has spent his whole life there. And although he already had his own luxurious home, he does not want to leave his native place for a long time and is enrolled ... as a university student who has the right to live on campus. And he becomes the oldest student in the history of high school. And that’s what Kelvin is all about! When it was necessary to decide on the title 7 years earlier, he was not very worried about this and jokingly suggested the title “Lord Cable”, since his services to the crown and England in laying the transatlantic cable were the main motivation for the award. Thomson at this time was already planning to build his own house in order to spend his holidays in it. He chose a place of amazing beauty at the mouth of the Largs River, 50 kilometers from Glasgow. And one day his friends gathered in his university apartment to help solve the problem of a specific name for the title. It was a hot summer day and the whole company went to swim in the pond on the university grounds.
