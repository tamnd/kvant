Another recruitment at VZMSH

Our contacts: https: vzms.ru https: lycuz2.mskobr.ru +7(926)280-28-20 vzms2015@mail.ru (mail, reception) https: vk.com vzmsh 119234 Moscow, Vorobyovy Gory, Moscow State University, Second Academic Building, room. 307

The All-Russian multi-subject correspondence school invites schoolchildren and teachers from all regions of Russia, near and far abroad to study under additional distance (online) education programs. VZMSH is the oldest center of additional education in Russia. It was founded in 1964 on the initiative of outstanding mathematicians, academicians I.G. Petrovsky (at that time rector of Moscow State University) and I.M. Gelfand. Currently, VZMSH is a structural subdivision of the famous Moscow Physics and Mathematics Lyceum “Second School”. We teach mathematics, physics, biology, Russian language and literature, history, and social studies. Our teachers are employees of M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University and other leading universities and academic institutes. They have written an extensive library of unique textbooks on mathematics, history, social studies and other subjects. The methods they created allow for distance learning (online) at the highest level. Education of high school students at VZMS is aimed primarily at preparing for Olympiads and the Unified State Exam. However, we believe that even the highest grade cannot be an end in itself. We study primarily with students for whom self-development comes first, who want to know ABOVE (beyond the school curriculum) and acquire additional knowledge in a variety of subjects. More than 200 thousand schoolchildren graduated from VZMSH. Many scientists, researchers, and teachers emerged from their ranks. For many, studying at VZMSH helped them better orientate

VZMSH, unlike courses or clubs, is a real school, with its own program, rhythm, tests, two-way communication between student and teacher. We provide our materials in electronic form, constantly communicate with our students through social networks, tell them about what is not included in the curriculum, but helps their development, introduce books and articles by our teachers, conduct video conferences and video lectures. At VZMSh there is a special form of work - the “Collective Student” group. This is a group of schoolchildren working according to VZMSH methodological manuals under the guidance of a school teacher using the same programs and manuals as individual students. To help teachers - curators of such groups - special methodological and educational materials are being developed. Students, under the guidance of a teacher, discuss together the material covered and the assignments received. As our many years of experience show, many teachers perceive cooperation with VZMS as their own study. Studying in the “Collective Student” format has the following advantages: contact with the teacher simplifies learning, which is especially important for those students who find it difficult to organize their time independently, without a mentor; joint activities create a creative atmosphere in which there is room for both competition and cooperation; materials sent from VZMS can serve as a good basis for the teacher to organize a club or elective at school, help prepare students for participation in olympiads and passing the Unified State Exam; students are admitted to VZMSh without completing the introductory task; Tuition fees per student are lower than for individual tuition. You can learn more about the training programs, register, complete introductory tasks, and also submit an application for the “Collective Student” group here: math-vzms.org You can start training at any time! We will tell you more about our departments and provide the conditions for the corresponding introductory work.
