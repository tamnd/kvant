People of science, as we see, are not at all alien to the desire to joke, and physicists and mathematicians are by no means an exception. Moreover, thanks to the exact disciplines, a unique genre of “scientific” humor was born, sometimes understandable only to specialists, but for the most part still accessible to the public. Schoolchildren laugh at teachers, students laugh at professors, and teachers laugh at students. Scientists make fun of each other, and of themselves, and they all make fun of the TV presenters who regal us with “Fibonacci numbers” and the “hadron collider.” Amusing stories, sometimes flavored with sarcasm and paradoxes, are also a means of combating ignorance, because it is known that “there is only a grain of joke in every joke.” «Kvant» has never parted with the humorous column, and today we encourage you to smile along with Kaleidoscope.
