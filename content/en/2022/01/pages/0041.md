To control his movement in outer space, the hero of the film “The Martian” made a hole in the glove of his spacesuit using wire cutters and, directing the stream of air escaping from the hole, changed his speed of movement. Estimate the amount of force that pushed this hero and the acceleration he acquired. And also analyze the situation shown in figure (still from the film). The astronaut moves forward for a long time, accelerating.

If the cross-sectional area of ​​the hole is $S 1 см^{2}$, and the air pressure inside the spacesuit is $p 10^{5}$ Pa, then the estimate for the force is as follows:

F = Sp = 10 N.
