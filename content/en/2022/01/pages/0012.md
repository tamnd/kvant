We cannot construct them, but we assume some of their properties. In particular, they are similar to the surfaces we constructed above, and also have regular polygons at the top. But the optimal surfaces are more “smooth”. To give the concept of smoothness a strict meaning, we will have to explain some terms. Smoothness means that the surface has no points or edges, i.e. At each point a single tangent plane can be drawn. Near the point of contact, this plane approximates the surface well. Moreover, if you continuously move the point of contact along the surface, then the plane also changes continuously.
