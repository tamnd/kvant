OLYMPICS

18TH INTERNATIONAL JUNIOR SCIENCE OLYMPIAD (IJSO-2021)

The 2021 Junior Olympics were held in Dubai (United Arab Emirates) in a remote format. 400 boys and girls from more than 70 countries took part in the competition. According to the terms of the Olympiad, participants must be no older than 15 years. The Olympiad included three rounds: test, theoretical and experimental, during which participants completed tasks in physics, chemistry and biology. All six members of the Russian team won Olympic gold medals: Vyacheslav Bobkov (school 1589, Moscow), Daniil Gavrilov (Republican Lyceum for Gifted Children, Saransk, Republic of Mordovia), Grigory Zutler (school 179, Moscow), Ivan Myakutin (school 179, Moscow), Egor Potapov (Republican Lyceum for Gifted Children, Saransk, Republic of Mordovia),
