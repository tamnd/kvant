17. Both the circle and the rectangle can be easily cut into any number of identical pieces. Is there a figure with the same property that has neither a center of symmetry nor an axis of symmetry? (The parts must be identical in both shape and area.) Folklore

18. a) In a white checkered square 101 u 101 several cells are painted black. Each pair of adjacent cells is one of three types: both cells are white, both cells are black, or cells are of different colors. Could it be that the number of pairs of all three types is the same? b) The same question about the square 100 u 100. N. Avilov

19. A regular triangle with side 3 and a regular triangle with side 4 intersect to form a convex hexagon with perimeter 7. Prove that the corresponding sides of the triangles are parallel. P. Kozhevnikov

20. The Fibonacci sequence is a sequence of numbers in which the first two numbers are equal to 1, and each subsequent number is equal to the sum of the previous two. Here are the first few numbers, Fibonacci: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13,... a) For what n can the first n numbers of the Fibonacci sequence be divided into two groups containing equal numbers, so that the sums of the numbers in these groups are equal to each other? b) For what n can the first n numbers of the Fibonacci sequence be divided into more than two groups (not necessarily containing equal numbers) so that the sums of the numbers in these groups are equal to each other? I. Akulich
