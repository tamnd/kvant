How can it be that bombarding particles of high energy cannot cross the barrier from the outside, while internal particles, which have only half as much energy, manage to leak out? ...Before I closed the journal, I already knew what really happened in such a case. This was a typical phenomenon that would have been impossible in classical Newtonian mechanics, but was actually expected in the new wave mechanics.” Gamow is sometimes referred to as a “three-time non-Nobel Prize laureate.” This can be understood in two ways - as the fact that Nobel Prizes were received three times by those who used his ideas, and as the fact that he himself received three “Nobel class” results, but did not receive a corresponding prize. Both are true. Gamow himself writes: “With the ever-accelerating speed of scientific research and the rapidly increasing number of people involved in this, it happens more and more often that important discoveries are made simultaneously and independently of each other by two or more scientists, or even several of their teams. The same thing happened with my theory of radioactive alpha decay, which was put forward simultaneously by Ronald Gurney together with Edward Condon. In fact, their paper, which was published in Nature, was received by the publishers several days earlier than mine, which was published in Zeitschrift für Physik.
