EXPERIMENTS and OBSERVATIONS

Summer is ahead. Of course, the holidays are a great opportunity to relax and have fun at the dacha or in the camp, at home or in nature. But there it is quite possible in practice, “live”, to be convinced of the action and omnipotence of physical laws - after all, they grew out of experiments and observations. And it is not necessary to have complex equipment for this; often all that is needed is a careful look, the simplest available tools, ingenuity and basic calculations. We hope that you will not spend the coming months in idleness, but will give free rein to your curiosity and ingenuity, work with both your hands and your head, find answers to accumulated questions and be puzzled by new ones in anticipation of future school years. And our magazine will help you. We offer a handful of summer exercises to warm up.
