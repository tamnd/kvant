One of us took turns taking Tolino’s place and repeating all his actions. Then it turned out that Tolik staged this performance not only for entertainment and that his tower looked like... the famous Ostankino (Fig. 3). When everyone had calmed down a bit, Ivan Petrovich reminded us that a month ago, on November 4 (as was said, the events described date back to 1967), a television signal went on air from the Ostankino Tower for the first time. At the time of completion, our tower was the tallest structure in the world. We also learned that the idea of ​​​​building a concrete tower was proposed by engineer, architect and scientist in the field of building structures Nikolai Vasilyevich Nikitin (1907–1973). Before this, all such structures were made of metal. The strength and stability of the television tower is ensured by the tensioned cables running inside it. These cables - there are 149 of them - press the concrete rings of the tower one to another, like Tolina's fishing line. This made it possible to make the foundation of the tower relatively shallow - only about five meters. The cone-shaped base of the tower resembles an inverted lily flower with strong petals and a thick stem. The design proposed by Nikitin was based on the work of Yuri Vasilyevich Kondratyuk (1897–1942), the author of an unrealized windmill project on Mount Ai-Petri. Yu.V.Kondratyuk is one of the founders of astronautics. In 1919, independently and simultaneously with K.E. Tsiolkovsky, he obtained the equation of motion of a rocket and proposed its multi-stage design, gave a diagram of the combustion chamber of a rocket engine, and calculated the optimal flight path to the Moon. Subsequently it was called the “Kondratyuk route”. This is how one little trick by Tolya Vtulkin helped us learn the big secrets of the Ostankino TV tower and much more connected with it.

There was a crater with a diameter of 1.2 km and a depth of 230 m (Fig. 2). According to experts, before the explosion, the Arizona asteroid moved at a speed of 45–60 thousand kilometers per hour, had a mass of 300,000 tons and a size of 50 m. Much more impressive evidence of catastrophic collisions of asteroids with the Earth was found in Yucatan (Mexico) and Wrederford (South Africa). The diameter of the crater in Yucatan is 180 km, in Wrederford - 300 km. The Yucatan crater is formed by an asteroid about 10 km in size that crashed into the Earth 65 million years ago, the Wrederford crater is formed by a 10-15 km asteroid that exploded 2 billion years ago. The fall of the first asteroid is associated with the death of dinosaurs in the Cretaceous period, and the fall of the second is associated with global geophysical and biological changes that occurred in the Proterozoic era.
