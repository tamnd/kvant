EXAMINATION MATERIALS

In 2022, the version of the Unified State Exam in Physics consisted of 30 tasks: 23 tasks in the first part (with a short answer) and 7 tasks in the second part (with a detailed answer). Of the tasks in the first part, 12 were scored with a maximum of 1 point, 11 with a maximum of 2 points. Of the 11 two-point tasks, five require you to select all correct statements out of five given. The first two tasks of the first part are interdisciplinary, in response to each of them you need to demonstrate knowledge of various branches of physics. Of the tasks in the second part, one was of high quality (24, maximum 3 points), two of medium complexity (25 and 26, maximum 2 points), three of increased complexity (27,28, 29, maximum 3 points), and for the last 30th task you could get a maximum of 4 points (3 points for the solution and 1 point for justifying the applicability of physical laws and the correctness of writing formulas). In total, a maximum of 54 primary points could be obtained for the entire option. The 2023 options will differ little in structure from the 2022 options.

A demo version of the Unified State Exam-2023 can be found on the FIPI website.
