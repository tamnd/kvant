NOW YOU WILL FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED at the meeting of our school physics club in December back in 1967... When we had all gathered and sat down at our desks, our teacher Ivan Petrovich entered the class, followed by Tolik Vtulkin through the wide open door. With both hands he held in front of him a sheet of plywood, on which some cylindrical packages of vitamins (Fig.1) were scattered.

After these words, Tolik sat down at the teacher’s table with the most serious look, frowned and began to look steadily at the plywood clutched in his hands. We froze, not knowing what to expect. And suddenly - lo and behold! – the cylinders began to move. They slowly moved along the plywood, approaching one another. We froze in bewilderment. It seemed like a minute passed, and then all the cylinders formed into one long cylinder. Everyone didn't take their eyes off him. - That's not all, look further! – Ivan Petrovich interrupted the silence. After these words, Tolik leaned back in his chair and, as if after hard work, exhaled sharply. At the same moment, the motionless cylinder seemed to shake - and a vertical tower appeared on the plywood. She was quite tall, but she didn’t fall. Tolik even tilted his plywood from side to side, but the tower stood steadily, as if rooted to the spot. Then Tolik suddenly rose from the table, and the tower crumbled. White cylinders rolled on the plywood. The whole class made an enthusiastic noise, and some even clapped their hands. - Really, guys, you didn’t know that this was possible? – Tolik turned to us with a serious look. – Each of you can repeat this experience, you just have to really want it. Let him try to do this...” he looked around the class. Here everyone was vying about who should be first. In general, we decided that Masha would repeat the experience. She positioned herself in front of the table and concentrated. Without blinking, she looked ahead. Masha even blushed from tension. And again everything repeated: the cylinders first came closer together, and then the tower grew. Masha was shocked. - How's it going, guys?
