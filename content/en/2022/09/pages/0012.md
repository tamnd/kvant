There were more semiconductors, but then the term “semimetals” arose, and just as Pluto, without asking his consent, was transferred from planets to dwarf planets, so some semiconductors were transferred to semimetals. Now, in addition to those listed above, among the elements semiconductors are considered one of the modifications of phosphorus (black phosphorus) and one of the modifications of tin (gray tin). The use of a particular material does not depend on the name, but on its properties and conditions of use. For example, if the medium or device itself is very low-resistance, then a substance that is not usually perceived as an insulator can be used as an insulator. Thus, in generators of short powerful pulses, water, which is usually considered a conductor, can be used for insulation. But still, some of the impurities are removed from it to increase resistance. If a capacitor has conductivity and a voltage is applied to it, it will generate heat. It must be removed, otherwise the capacitor will begin to heat up, while the insulation resistance will drop, the current will increase even more, and so will the heat generation, and in some conditions their avalanche growth begins. This is the mechanism of the so-called electrothermal breakdown. However, we will not apply too much direct voltage to the capacitor, but will not apply the permissible voltage to it, but alternating voltage. We will find that the capacitor heats up, and the heating depends on the frequency - as it decreases, the heating decreases. It turns out that there is some factor that depends on frequency, and it is called “dielectric loss”. Heating due to dielectric losses is used both in industry and in everyday life - it is this that heats food in a microwave oven or microwave. Their mechanism at the elementary level is simple - an alternating electric field “pulls” the ions, the energy of the electromagnetic field is converted into mechanical energy of molecules, and it is transferred through collisions to other molecules - and the object is heated -
