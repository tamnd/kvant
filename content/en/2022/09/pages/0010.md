The voltage across the inductance is proportional to the derivative of the current, and it shifts the phase of the sinusoidal signal, but in relation to the capacitor in the opposite direction - the current does not lag behind the voltage, but leads it. Moreover, the thinner they are, the greater the resistance of the plates, and from the point of view of increasing capacity for a given volume (reducing volume and weight is a constant task and concern), it makes sense to make them thinner. Here, as is usually the case in technology, the parameters of the product are connected, so by doing our best to improve one, we worsen the other. So there are no simply the best parameters or products; different ones turn out to be the best for different tasks. The second limitation is more difficult to notice, because resistance is often discussed in school, but inductance is rarely discussed. Meanwhile, every conductor has not only capacitance, but also inductance. This means that when a current flows through it, a magnetic field with energy $LI^{2} 2$ arises, and when the current changes, due to a change in the magnetic field caused by it, an emf arises equal to LdI dt (or, if it’s easier for you, L ' I ' t, when the current, and with it the field, grows uniformly and changes by ' I over time ' t ). The fact that a conductor has capacitance and inductance means that it itself is a resonant circuit tuned to the appropriate frequency, and the current through it cannot change significantly in a time much less than the oscillation period. Therefore, if we want to discharge a capacitor in a very short time, and in physics and technology this task arises often, we use special non-induction capacitors - with, if possible, reduced inductance. By the way, the words “capacitor” and “capacitance”, the words “resistor” and “resistance”, as well as the words “coil” and “inductance” are often used as synonyms. (If you wander into radio engineering, amateur radio and similar forums, don’t be alarmed.) Although, of course, a capacitor, resistor and coil are devices, and capacitance, resistance and inductance are the physical parameters of these devices: “capacitor capacity 1 μF”, “resistor resistance 1 kOhm”, etc.
