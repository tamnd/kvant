Tasks

1.

Arrange all the numbers from 1 to 9 in the circles of the left triangle so that the equality c 2 = a 2 + b 2 holds, where c is the sum of the numbers located on the hypotenuse, a, b are the sum of the numbers located on the legs.

2.

Among 999,999 bus tickets with six-digit numbers, those whose sum of the first three digits matches the sum of the last three are considered lucky. We will call unlucky tickets the numbers of which contain exactly three consecutive
