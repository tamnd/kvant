Thus, a powerful low-frequency disturbance caused by the eruption of the Indonesian volcano Krakatoa in 1883 circled the globe twice. ...with distance from the epicenter of a nuclear explosion, the shock wave turns into an acoustic wave, and short waves decay faster than long waves, and at large distances only low-frequency oscillations persist. Bell's invention of the telephone was preceded by his thorough study of acoustics and many years of work at the Boston school for the deaf and dumb, who were also intended to use sound amplifiers and devices designed by him for teaching speech understanding. ...the peculiarity of freshly fallen snow to absorb mainly high frequencies was noticed by the English physicist Tyndall, who combined acoustic and optical studies. And Rayleigh, who was looking for something common in all oscillatory processes, was able to explain the increase in the tone of the echo in a pine forest by better scattering and reflection of short sound waves by thin needles than long ones, as in the scattering of light in the atmosphere. ...in one of the rooms of the conservatory in the Australian city of Adelaide, it was impossible to listen to the piano being played - the hall resonated so piercingly and sharply.
