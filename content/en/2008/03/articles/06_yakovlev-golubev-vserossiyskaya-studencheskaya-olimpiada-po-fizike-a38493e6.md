All-Russian Student Olympiad in Physics

According to the results of the next All-Russian Olympiad among students of technical universities, which took place in November 2007, the team of the Moscow State Technical University (MSTU) took first place in the team competition. N.E. Bauman (133 points), second place - the team of Samara State Aerospace University named after. Academician S.P. Korolev (95 points), third place - the team of the Ufa State Aviation Technical University (85 points). In the individual competition, first place was won by A. Pudovkin (MSTU named after N.E. Bauman, 48 points), second place was won by D. Kovaleva (MSTU named after N.E. Bauman, 46 points), and N. Troshkin (Samara State Aerospace University named after Academician S.P. Korolev, 44 points) took third place. A special jury prize was awarded to a student of the Russian State University of Oil and Gas named after. I.M. Gubkina K. Kornishin - for the original solution to two problems of this Olympiad and for consistently successful participation in city and all-Russian rounds of student Olympiads.

OBJECTIVES OF THE OLYMPICS

1. The cyclist moves with speed v. The distance between the wheel axles is l, the center of gravity of the cyclist with the bicycle is in the middle between the wheels at a height h from the road. Determine the minimum braking distance if braking is possible only with the rear wheel. 2. An inextensible thread is passed through a hole in the ceiling, to the end of which is attached a load of mass m, spun in a horizontal plane to a speed v and having a radius of rotation relative to the vertical axis R. How much work must be done to slowly pull the load twice as close to the ceiling? 3. A spacecraft of mass M with a low-thrust engine $F_{т}^{=} mg$, having a speed v, approaches a planet with radius R and surface acceleration g from a great distance. Determine the minimum characteristic velocity required for the vehicle to enter a low orbit around the planet. Characteristic speed is the speed that a spacecraft will receive in free space with the same fuel consumption. 4. A chain of mass m and length l is suspended by the upper link, and the lower link touches a horizontal platform of mass M, kept in balance from below by a system of springs with a total stiffness k. The chain was released, and at the moment of contact of the upper link with the platform, the platform dropped from the equilibrium point by an amount b and reached a speed v. Determine the impulse transferred by the chain to the platform during the fall, and the amount of heat released during this process. 5. A ball of radius R and mass M, standing on a horizontal plane, was spun around the vertical axis to an angular velocity $\omega$. The ball was then struck, imparting momentum $p^{=} MR\omega$ along a horizontal line passing through the center of the ball. Determine the speed with which the ball will roll if there is no slipping between the ball and the plane. 6. A cyclic process carried out with a gas consists of an isochore, an isobar and an isotherm. Determine the maximum efficiency of the cycle if the pressure can vary from $p_{1}$ to $p_{2}. 7.$ Two point charges Q and –Q are located at a distance of 3l from each other. A body is placed in the middle between them, consisting of two metal balls of radius r = l, connected by a very thin metal rod coinciding with the line connecting the charges. Determine how much the interaction force between charges will change if the length of the rod is l. 8. A jumper with mass m and resistance R lies on two horizontal parallel rails, the distance between which is equal to a. A source with emf E is connected to the rails for a time t. What vertically directed magnetic field B must be created so that the speed of the jumper reaches its maximum value after the source is turned off? Assume that exp(1.26) = 3.52. 9. In the path of a plane light wave there is a circular hole in which only a small part of the first Fresnel zone is placed for the observation point P, the light intensity at which is $I_{0}$. A special lens was placed in the hole, and when light passes through it, the wave front becomes conical. Determine the intensity of light at the observation point P if, in the presence of a lens, 5 Fresnel zones are placed in the hole. The publication was prepared by M. Yakovlev, V. Golubev
