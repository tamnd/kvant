Vasya thought of three natural numbers. For each pair of numbers, he found the difference between their product and their sum. It turned out that one of these differences is negative, and the other is positive. What is the sign of the third difference? B.Frenkin

In a country where every resident is either a knight or a liar (knights, as you know, always tell the truth, and liars always lie), a company of 19 aborigines gathered at a round table. Each of those gathered declared that both of his neighbors were liars. A scandal broke out, as a result of which part of the company left the feast. After this, each of those remaining announced with satisfaction that now both of his neighbors were knights. “And in fact, there is not a single liar among you now,” the last of those leaving the company agreed with them. Meanwhile, the “renegades” organized a new meeting, again at a round table. Each of those sitting at this table said that among his neighbors there is exactly one knight. How many people remained in their seats after the company split? V. Letsko
