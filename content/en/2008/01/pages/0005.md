ABOUT

KIKOINE,

UNITS

kilograms, and centimeters into meters. The third unit used in the article was the second, but it is a second in both SI and GHS. However, I dared to say that the author may not like such a replacement, because in cosmology it is not customary to use meters in scientific texts; it is more common to use centimeters or parsecs. “I’ll call Zeldovich now,” said IKK and dialed his number. “Yasha,” IKK said into the phone, “you wrote a wonderful article, and we will publish it for your birthday in «Kvant».” But it is necessary to replace the CGS units (cm, g, s) with SI units (m, kg, s). I don’t know what Zeldovich answered, but a storm appeared on the IKK’s face again. He hung up the phone, sat silently for a while, and then said to me:

“THIS IS THE QUANTUM THAT ISAAC BUILT...”

A. SAVIN

Here is the Quantum that Isaac built, And here is the student, Who occasionally likes to boast, That she understands everything on a whole page In the Quantum that Isaac built.

Here is the author of the article - a famous scientist, Who wrote it so enthusiastically For this extremely serious girl, Who occasionally likes to boast, That she understands everything on a whole page In the Quantum that Isaac built.

But the reviewer is a long-time member of the editorial board, who read this essay, imagining himself as a pretty girl, who vainly pisses at the page, trying to understand what is said in the Quantum that Isaac built.

And this is the editor who edited this article, leaving only the author’s name in place, so that even the dumbest girl in the world could boast at least once a year that she understands everything on a whole page In the Quantum that Isaac built.

And this is an artist in deep prostration, trying to invent those illustrations that will so enchant the girl that she will immediately read the page in the Quantum that Isaac built.

This poem is taken from a special issue of the magazine «Kvant», published in a single copy for the 75th anniversary of I.K. Kikoin.

– Yasha says that if he writes in SI units, then everyone will laugh at him. - So what are we going to do? – I asked. “Nothing,” said the IKK, “let’s leave the GHS units in the article.” But let’s also write SI units in parentheses. After that, he finally smiled, and I realized that there would be no storm. Perhaps this episode does not deserve such a long exposition. But I think it is important to emphasize once again that the IKK understood absolutely everything. He was not stubborn at all, but he was disciplined. What he was supposed to demand from others, he tried to do himself. And one more thing. Ya.B.Zeldovich and K.K.Kikoin were very responsible people; they could not publish an article that in any sense would look “raw.” This is the story.

Here is the editor-in-chief - a great academician, He rules (without demanding money for this), So that everything is done only this way and that way, Since he is this very Isaac, Who fell head over heels in love with the girl, Who pokes her finger at the page, Trying to understand what is said in the Quantum that Isaac built.
