Department of Computer Science

Admission is open to the “Programming for Beginners” course. Anyone with an education of at least 7 years of secondary school is accepted into the department. To successfully complete practical tasks, you must be able to work on a computer. During the year of study, students will master the basic constructions of the Pascal language, study the simplest algorithms and, as a final work, write a game program. To enroll, you must send a form with answers to the questions below. Attention! Send your answers to the survey questions on a double notebook sheet, indicating on the first page the information important to us: full name, class you are graduating from, full (with index!) postal address, e-mail (if you have one). Write detailed answers to the questions. The deadline for sending the questionnaire is May 15, 2009.

Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology (FZFTSH) at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is recruiting students from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation for the 2009/10 academic year. FZFTSH at MIPT has been operating as a state educational institution for specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 80 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its graduate. The school is financed by the Federal Education Agency. Education for students living in the Russian Federation, within the framework of the approved admission plan, is free. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (state university), which trains highly qualified specialists in modern areas of science and technology. Leading industry and academic research institutes and scientific and production associations of the country (base organizations of MIPT) take part in their preparation. Teaching at MIPT is taught by famous teachers and scientists, among whom there are about 100 members of the Russian Academy of Sciences. A Physics and Technology education allows you not only to work successfully in science, but also to navigate life well. The goal of our school is to help students interested in physics and mathematics to deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, as well as to contribute to the professional self-determination of students. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2009/10 academic year is carried out for part-time, part-time and full-time courses.

Correspondence department (individual training) Tel./fax: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@pop3.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing the introductory task
