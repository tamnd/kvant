PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent no later than March 1, 2009 to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 6–2008” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2111” or “F2118”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics can also be sent to the email addresses math@kvant.info and phys@kvant.info, respectively. Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2112 and M2115 were offered at the XLIX International Mathematical Olympiad, and problem M2113 was offered at the XXIX Tournament of Cities.

Problem M2111 – M2115, F2118 – F2122

M2111. One of the cells of the endless checkered strip is colored. Initially, the chip is at a distance of n cells from the colored one. A die is thrown, and if k points are rolled ( k O { 1,2,3,4,5,6 }), the chip moves k squares towards the colored square. The process continues until the chip enters a colored square (win) or until it passes a colored square (lose). At what natural number n is the probability of winning $p_{n}$ greatest? Find this highest probability value. V. Letsko

M2112. Let H be the point of intersection of the altitudes of an acute triangle ABC. A circle with center in the middle of side BC, passing through point H, intersects line BC at points $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$. Similarly, a circle centered at the midpoint of side CA passing through point H intersects line CA at points $B_{1}$ and $B_{2}$, and a circle centered at the midpoint of side AB passing through point H intersects line AB at points $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$. Prove that points $A_{1}, A_{2}, B_{1}, B_{2}, C_{1}, C_{2}$ lie on the same circle. A. Gavrilyuk

M2113. A polynomial of degree n >1 has various real roots $x_{1}, x_{2}, K, x_{n}$, and its derivative has roots $y_{1}, y_{2}, K, y_{n} -_{1}$. Prove that the arithmetic mean of the numbers $x_{1}^{2}, x_{2}^{2}, K, x_{n}^{2}$ is greater than the arithmetic mean of the numbers $y_{1}^{2}, y_{2}^{2}, K, y_{n}^{2} -_{1}$.

M. Murashkin

M2114. Prove that there are infinitely many perfect squares that do not end in zero, in which any non-zero digit is odd. V. Senderov

$М2115^{*}$. Let ABCD be a convex quadrilateral in which BA is equal to BC. Let us denote the inscribed circles of triangles ABC and ADC by $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$, respectively. Suppose that there is a circle $\omega$, which touches the continuation of the segment BA beyond point A, the continuation of the segment BC beyond point C and touches the lines AD and CD. Prove that the common external tangents to circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ intersect at circle $\omega$. V. Shmarov

F2118. The cart moves along a horizontal table under the action of an inextensible thread tied to it. The thread is thrown through a small block fixed at a height H above the table plane. The free end of the thread is pulled out at a constant speed $v_{0}$, directed horizontally. Find the speed of the cart and its acceleration at the moment when the angle between the thread and the horizon is $45^\circ$. M. Uchitelev
