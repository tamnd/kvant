Department of Economics

Training at the department is carried out in two main programs: “Applied Economics” and “Economics and Geography”. The Applied Economics program includes the study of the fundamentals of economic theory, as well as an introduction to business practices in the business game of correspondence. Students of the “Economics and Geography” program, in addition to studying the fundamentals of economic theory, become familiar with physical and economic geography, and participate in absentia in exciting travels around the world. Those who complete one of the main programs are offered an elective specialization: “Fundamentals of Entrepreneurship and Management”, “Accounting and Financial Analysis”, “World Economy”, “Russian Economy: Past, Present and Future”. Anyone with an education of at least 7 years of secondary school is accepted into the department. Training is carried out either individually or in small groups (2-4 people). There is no “Collective student” form of education in the department. Students in grades 10–11 who want to simultaneously prepare for entrance exams to the economic faculties of M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University and other economic universities are offered a special program “Economics PLUS”, which includes, along with economic disciplines, an in-depth study of several additional subjects: mathematics, social studies, Russian language and literature. The program includes preparation for the Unified State Exam in those subjects for which economic universities accept Unified State Exam results as entrance exams. For schoolchildren who are interested in geographical science and intend to enroll in the geography department of Moscow State University or another university, there is the “Geography PLUS” program, created by teachers from the OL VZMSH and the geography department of Moscow State University based on the experience of preparatory courses in geography at Moscow University. The entrance work for students is given in the form of a test. Send solutions only on postcards indicating the full postal address and zip code, last name, first name and patronymic (all in block letters); Be sure to indicate the source of information about OL VZMSH and write “Economics, entrance test-2009”.
