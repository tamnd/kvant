Full-time department (training at evening consultation centers) Tel.: (495) 409-95-83, e-mail: zftsh@pop3.mipt.ru For students in Moscow and the Moscow region, evening consultation centers operate under the FZFTSH program; recruitment to them is carried out based on the results of entrance exams in physics and mathematics and interviews, which take place in the second half of September.

The FZFTS programs at MIPT are specialized additional educational programs and are the same for all departments. In addition, students from all departments will be invited to participate in the Physics and Mathematics Olympiad “PHYSTECH-2009”, which is held on the basis of MIPT and in a number of Russian cities at the end of March and mid-May, in other intramural and correspondence Olympiads of MIPT and its faculties, as well as in competitions, tournaments and conferences. For students in grades 9–11, MIPT hosts a Saturday lecture in physics and mathematics according to the FZFTS program. Lectures are given by institute teachers, usually the authors of assignments. You can read more about these events on the FZFTS website: http://www.school.mipt.ru At the end of the school year, students who successfully completed the FZFTS program are promoted to the next grade, and graduates (eleventh graders) receive a school completion certificate with final grades in physics and mathematics, which is taken into account during the interview for admission to MIPT.
