4.

Are there natural numbers x, y, z such that $x Ч^{2} x + y Ч^{2} y = z Ч^{2} z$? V. Senderov

2.

The ten-digit number 2100010006 is remarkable in that it “describes itself,” i.e. its first digit is equal to the number of ones in it, the second – to the number of twos, ..., the ninth – to the number of nines, the tenth – to the number of zeros. Are there two ten-digit numbers, each of which describes the other in the same way? I. Akulich

5.

In the city of Udiraevsk, the road network is designed in such a way that from each intersection you can get to any other intersection, and there are no dead ends in the city. At the beginning, Elusive Joe and two policemen are at different intersections, and then begin to move along the roads. In one turn, Joe moves to an adjacent intersection or remains in place; In the next move, the police simultaneously move to the intersections adjacent to them. Draw one of the possible road maps of the city of Udiraevsk, on which Elusive Joe, knowing the location of the police, can always avoid capture. K. Kaibhanov

These tasks are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.

3.

a) A triangular pyramid is made of six segments of different lengths. Is it true that from the same segments you can always make two separate (non-touching) triangles? b) Two separate (non-touching) triangles are made from six segments of different lengths. Is it true that you can always make a triangular pyramid from these same segments? G. Galperin
