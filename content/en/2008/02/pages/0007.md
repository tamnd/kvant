And he cited, as an example, an incident that occurred while working on the atomic project: “Kurchatov asked for an educational program on the issue of isotope separation for senior management. Since everyone seemed very businesslike and knowledgeable, I explained my thoughts in detail. Moreover, I became interested in the topic. When he finished, he looked at the audience and by their appearance he realized that they were completely bewildered. What was there to do, start all over again? Igor Vasilievich saved me. I started asking the simplest questions, one after another. I answered as popularly and in detail as possible. So the two of us reached an understanding.” * * * During one of the visits, the conversation turned to a controlled thermonuclear reaction.
