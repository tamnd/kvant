Task 2. Find the invisible The thermometer has an open scale ranging from 30 to $50^{о}$ C. Heat it in a glass of hot water to a temperature slightly above $50^{о}$ C, remove it and wipe it. Place it on a napkin. Carry out the necessary measurements so as to establish the dependence of the rate of change in the readings $\Delta T \Delta t$ of the thermometer on its readings, i.e. on temperature T. Plot a graph of $\Delta T \Delta t$ versus T. Determine the air temperature in the classroom (justify the method and find the value). Equipment: thermometer with partially sealed scale, glass, hot water source in the classroom, napkins, stopwatch.

Problem 2. Glass thickness A glass block with foil glued to one side is in a shell that precludes direct measurement of thickness. The opposite side is covered with translucent tape, which has a small hole. Using the proposed equipment, determine the thickness of the glass and its refractive index. Assess the errors. Equipment: glass block in a shell, laser pointer, pencil, graph paper, measuring compass, tape. Note: Laser pointers have a limited lifespan, so only turn them on while taking measurements.
