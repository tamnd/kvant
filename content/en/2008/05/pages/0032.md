Selected Tournament Challenges

According to tradition dating back to the first tournaments, most of the tournament's problems were new and original. Of these, we tried to choose those where there is, albeit a small, miracle: a little more follows from the conditions than can be expected, or there is an unexpected twist and brevity in the solution. Each problem indicates which classes it is most suitable for and who its author is. Difficult tasks are marked with asterisks, especially difficult ones are marked with two asterisks.

1 (6–7). Several points are marked on the surface of a Rubik's cube 3 → 3 → 3 so that in each of the 54 squares, including its border, exactly one point is marked. What is the smallest number of points that can be marked? A. Shapovalov

2 (6–7). Given a natural number whose digits are all odd, except one. Can it be divided by 2008? S. Tokarev
