PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent no later than January 1, 2009 to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 5–2008” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2101” or “F2108”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics can also be sent to the email addresses math@kvant.info and phys@kvant.info, respectively. Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2101, M2102 were offered at the IV stage, and problem M2103 – M2110 - at the V stage of the XXXIV All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.

M2101. Given a quadratic trinomial f(x) = x + ax + b. It is known that for any real number x there is a real number y such that f (y) = f (x) + y. Find the largest possible value of a. D. Tereshin

M2102. Red and blue numbers are placed around the circle. Each red number is equal to the sum of its neighbors, and each blue number is equal to half the sum of its neighbors. Prove that the sum of the red numbers is zero. I. Bogdanov

M2105. A circle $\omega$ with center O is inscribed in the angle BAC and touches its sides at points B and C. A point Q is chosen inside the angle BAC.
