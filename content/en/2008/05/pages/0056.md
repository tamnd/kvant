XLII All-Russian Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics

From April 21 to April 27, 2008, the final stage of the All-Russian Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Akademgorodok on the basis of Novosibirsk State University (NSU). The winners of the district stage of the current year's Olympiad and the diploma winners of three degrees of the final stage of the last year's Olympiad were invited to attend. A total of 218 schoolchildren took part in the final stage of the Olympiad. As usual, the Olympiad was held in two rounds - theoretical and experimental. The tasks of the theoretical round were developed by the Central Methodological Commission, and the tasks of the experimental round were prepared by teachers and staff of NSU. Below are the conditions of the tasks and the list of winners of the final stage of the Olympiad.
