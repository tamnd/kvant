Size and shape effects in molecular physics

S. VARLAMOV, A. GUDENKO create relationships connecting the macroscopic physical parameters of a substance in a gaseous state - temperature T, pressure p, volume V and amount of substance $\nu$. These are the famous gas laws, which are combined into one Mendeleev–Clapeyron law: $pV = \nu RT$, where R is the universal gas constant. Reference data for condensed matter, i.e. substances in liquid or solid state, and their phase transitions are also given in school textbooks, but in such a way that the impression of arbitrary disordered chaos arises.
