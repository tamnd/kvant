Equal size and equal composition

Theorem (Bolyai–Gervin). Any two equal-sized polygons on a plane are equivalent. The main idea of ​​the proof: reduce each of the polygons to an equal-sized (equal in area) square (1). Steps. Any polygon can be divided into triangles (prove it!). Each triangle can be divided into parts, from which a “not very elongated” rectangle (2–3) is formed, and then a square (4). Several squares can always be redrawn into one square (5).
