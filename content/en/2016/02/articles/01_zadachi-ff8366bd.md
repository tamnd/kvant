"QUANT" FOR JUNIOR SCHOOLCHILDREN

Tasks

1.

Find any two different natural numbers greater than five, which both in sum and in product give a palindrome. (Recall that a number is called a palindrome if the numbers in it go from left to right in the same order as from right to left, for example: 717, 55, 3223.) A. Menshchikov

⟦column⟧

4.

Petya and Vasya inscribed circles of the same radius of 5 cm in a rectangle. Then each of them connected the midpoints of the sides of their rectangle and got a rhombus (as in figure). Prove that the sides of these rhombuses are the same and find their lengths. G. Galperin

2.

Snow White baked a cake for the holiday, cut into squares and decorated with cherries, as shown in figure. Having cut off the corner square (lower right), she wants to divide the rest of the cake into 7 pieces of equal size and shape so that each of the seven dwarfs gets a whole cherry. Help Snow White do this. M. Evdokimov

5.

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

The knights of the two clans gathered in the castle for negotiations and sat in some order at a large round table. It turned out that there are as many knights, to the right of whom sits a knight from another clan, as there are knights, to the right of whom sits a knight from his own clan. Prove that the total number of knights is divisible by 4. G. Galperin

3.

One wise man remarked: “At last the year has come such that a number of grains equal to the number of the year can be laid out on the squares of a chessboard so that no two squares have equal numbers of grains.” In what year did this story take place? E. Bakaev, A. Zaslavsky

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.
