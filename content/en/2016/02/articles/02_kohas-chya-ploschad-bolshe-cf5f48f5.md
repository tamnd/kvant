Whose area is larger

K.KOKHAS

USENKA AND HER FRIENDS – OGRZA THE MOUSE, Kuzka the cockroach and Ushasya – celebrated the birthday of the woodpecker Spyatla. Entertaining competitions, wonderful food and a cake with elephants given to the hero of the occasion made the holiday interesting and memorable. How suddenly...

- Save yourself, whoever can! - the woodpecker Spyatel shouted and jumped out the window. The guests scattered. Ogryza dived into the basement, Kuzka disappeared behind the closet, and Busenka, grabbing Ushasya by anything she came across (and she got caught, of course, was the tail), jumped into the safe of the woodpecker Spyatla, throwing out the Crystal Python figurine. The room began to fill with the shiny rings of the Ukkha python. - Where is the birthday boy? – Ukkh asked. -Where have the gossss gone? - he continued, looking around, constantly sticking out his long forked tongue - sniffing. “Oh, that’s where you got into,” he said, noticing the safe. “Get out,” Ukkh said sternly and looked at Ushasya through the keyhole so that he wanted to drop everything and run, jump, crawl, squeeze through, fly and throw himself straight into Ukkh’s mouth. Fortunately, Busenka held the door tightly. - We won’t get out! - she said. – The door is closed, and the cross-sectional area of ​​the keyhole is 3 square

Ukkh swallowed a huge piece of cake (“Mmmm... what delicious elephants!”), put a bowl of vitamin salad from cucumbers with a sharp ear into his mouth and thought. His gaze brightened a little. “The keyhole has a rather complex shape,” he finally said. - How did you calculate its area? “First, eat that jar of jam,” Busenka said, without letting go of the door, “and then we’ll talk!” Ukkh grabbed a jar of jam with the tip of his tail and obediently swallowed its contents. Frowning and looking around, he threw an unopened package of cookies into his mouth. Busenka waited 20 seconds and opened the safe door. – The baked apples were clearly a success! – she advised, pointing to a plate of apples. Ukkh immediately took a bite of one apple and nodded approvingly. – How did I calculate the area? Yes, as usual. What is the area of ​​a figure? This is such a number. Firstly, it is non-negative. Secondly, the area of ​​a very simple figure, such as a rectangle, is equal to the product of the lengths of its sides. Thirdly, if a small figure is contained in a large one, then the area of ​​the small figure cannot be greater than the area of ​​the large one
