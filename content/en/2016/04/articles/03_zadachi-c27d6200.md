3.

Two racing cars - red and green - drove from city A to city B along the same road, starting and finishing at the same time. At the same time, the green car never overtook the red one. Could it be that at least 90% of the time the green car was going faster than the red one? Folklore

The store sells boxes of pencils. Among them there are at least five boxes of different prices (no two of them cost the same). Whatever two boxes Vasya buys, Petya can always buy two boxes, spending the same amount of money. What is the smallest number of boxes of pencils that should be on sale? N. Martynova
