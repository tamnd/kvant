Competition named after A.P. Savin

"Math 7–9"

We are starting another competition for solving mathematical problems. They are designed primarily for students in grades 7-9, but we welcome participation from students of all ages. Send solutions to the problems you can solve no later than December 1, 2016 by email to: savin.contest@gmail.com or by regular mail to: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant» (marked “A.P. Savin Competition”). In addition to your first and last name, indicate the city, school and class in which you study, as well as your return postal address. We welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by teams (in this case, one work is sent with a list of participants). You can participate starting from any tour. The winners will receive diplomas from the magazine «Kvant» and prizes. We wish you success!

2. In the trapezoid ABCD, point M is the middle of the lateral side CD. Prove that if point M lies on the bisector of angle A, then it also lies on the bisector of angle B. E. Bakaev

3. The four logicians A, B, C and D sit around the round table in that order (if you go clockwise).
