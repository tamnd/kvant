Problem 1. Bicycle and boat A boat starts from pier A to pier B downstream of the river, and at the same time a cyclist starts along the shore, moving unevenly. The distance between the piers is L = 5 km. Information about the speed of the cyclist is transmitted to the captain of the boat, and he, reacting instantly, maintains the speed of the boat relative to the water equal to the speed of the cyclist. Having reached pier B, the boat quickly turns around and meets the cyclist at a distance s = 4 km from pier A. How much longer did the boat float downstream of the river than against the current before meeting the cyclist? River flow speed u = 5 km/h.

D. Podlesny
