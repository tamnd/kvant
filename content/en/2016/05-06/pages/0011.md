Down consists - from the point of view of mechanics and thermal engineering - of individual elastic thin elements. When we deform (wrinkle) clothing containing down, the elements move relative to each other, slide and stop in a new position due to friction and elasticity - elasticity presses them together, and friction prevents them from sliding. The same thing happens when we crumple fabric or paper.
