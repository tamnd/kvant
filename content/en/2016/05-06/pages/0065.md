Department of Mathematics

We teach mathematics to schoolchildren in grades 6–11 by correspondence. We accept individuals for individual training based on the results of the introductory work and send benefits. The curator leads the training: draws up a program, checks assignments and answers questions. We accept training in a group (“Collective Student”) upon application from the group leader. We send benefits to the entire group. Students send one collective work on each topic for testing to the VZMSH. We accept students for all courses from 0 to 5 (i.e. students in grades 6–11). Send your application and introductory work to the admission email address: priem@math-vzms.org or to the school's postal address: 119234 Moscow V-234, Vorobyovy Gory, Moscow State University, mathematical department of VZMSH.
