OLYMPICS

LVII International Mathematical Olympiad

From July 6 to July 16, 2016, the LVII International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) was held in Hong Kong (PRC). Hong Kong is one of the few cities in the world that has had the honor of hosting the IMO twice (the first time was in 1994). The Olympiad participants lived in a campus located in tiers on a mountain, which offered a beautiful view of one of the many bays of the South China Sea. MMO is one of the largest intellectual competitions for schoolchildren. In 2016, 602 winners of national Olympiads from 109 countries took part in the Olympiad. This year, the Russian team included school graduates Pavel Gubkin (St. Petersburg), Muscovites Ruslan Salimov, Ivan Frolov and Grigory Yurgin, as well as tenth-graders Georgy Veprev (Rybinsk, Yaroslavl region) and Nikita Karagodin (St. Petersburg). The leading positions of the Russian mathematical olympiad school were confirmed by the inclusion of two problems from those proposed by our country in the MMO versions. At the same time, problem 5 (authors N. Agakhanov, I. Bogdanov) was recognized by the international jury as the best algebra problem in the shortlist (a list of 30 problems selected by the problem committee for the Olympiad), and problem 3 (author A. Gaifullin, the absolute winner of the IMO in 2000) - both the most difficult and the most beautiful problem of the Olympiad. The Russian team performed quite successfully at the Olympics, losing only to the US team in the number of gold medals won. And Grigory Yurgin was among the 14 participants in the Olympiad who managed to solve problem 3. The final stage of the team’s preparation for the IMO - summer training camps - as last year, took place at the Sirius Educational Center (Sochi). Classes with the team were conducted by the teachers of the training camp: associate professor of MIPT, Ph.D. I.I. Bogdanov, teacher of FML 239 of St. Petersburg, Ph.D. S.L. Berlov, teacher of Mechanics and Mathematics, Moscow State University. M.V.Lomonosova, Ph.D. A.A. Glazyrin, teacher of additional education in St. Petersburg A.S. Golovanov, professor at MIPT, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences V.L. Dolnikov, associate professor at MIPT, Ph.D. P.A. Tereshin, programmer G.R. Chelnokov. The team leaders thank Dmitry Yurievich Deukhen, who has been supporting the Russian team in international mathematical competitions for many years.

Objectives of the Olympiad

1. Given a triangle BCF with a right angle at vertex B. Point A on line CF is such that FA = FB and F lies between A and C. Point D is chosen so that DA = DC and AC is the bisector of angle DAB. Point E is chosen so that EA = ED and AD is the bisector of angle EAC. Point M is the middle of segment CF. Let the point X be such that AMXE is a parallelogram (in which AM P EX and AE P MX ). Prove that lines BD, FX and ME intersect at one point.
