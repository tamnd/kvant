V European Mathematical Olympiad for Girls

From 10 to 16 April 2016, the fifth European Girls Mathematical Olympiad (EGMO) was held in Romania. The Olympics were held in the picturesque town of Busteni, located in a mountainous region in the north of the country. In total, 147 schoolgirls from 38 countries took part in the Olympiad. In addition to teams from European countries, teams from the USA, Mexico, Ecuador, Japan, Saudi Arabia, India, and Israel took part in the Olympiad outside the official competition. The level of complexity of the Olympiad tasks was very high. Most participants had difficulties even with the simplest tasks (problems 1 and 4), and the most difficult problems of the Olympiad (problems 3 and 6) were quite comparable to problems of average complexity at the International Mathematical Olympiads. Russia was represented at the EGMO Olympiad by: Lyalina Albina (Izhevsk, Lyceum 29, 11th grade), Sonina Alexandra (Yaroslavl, school 33, 11th grade), Dmitrieva Maria (Moscow, Lyceum “Second School”, 10th grade), Tolokno Isabella (St. Petersburg, FML 239, 10th grade). Our girls showed an excellent result. They won 3 gold and 1 silver medal, taking first place in the official team competition (with a total of 122 points). The Americans finished second (111 points), third place went to the girls from Bulgaria (99 points). Our Maria Dmitrieva became the best in the individual ranking among all the Olympiad participants; she solved all the problems and scored the maximum possible number of points. Detailed information about the Olympiad is on the official website: www.egmo.org
