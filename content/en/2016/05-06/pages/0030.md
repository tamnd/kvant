"QUANT" FOR JUNIOR SCHOOLCHILDREN

Tasks

1.

Sharik and Matroskin milked 10 liters of milk, poured it into two buckets and took it home. Sharik got tired and poured some of the milk from his bucket into Matroskin’s bucket. As a result, Sharik’s milk became three times less, and Matroskin’s – three times more. How much milk does Matroskin have? Jury of the Lomonosov Tournament
