Another recruitment at VZMSH

The All-Russian Correspondence Multi-Subject School (VZMSH), which is part of the structure of the Moscow Lyceum “Second School” and operates at the Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov, is enrolling students for the fifty-third time. This school was created on the initiative of academician I.M. Gelfand in 1964. For many years, I.M. Gelfand headed the Scientific Council of the school. VZMSH is a state institution of additional education, available to everyone, and not only for schoolchildren, to expand their knowledge in one or more of the following fields of science: mathematics, biology, philology, physics, history (listed in chronological order of opening of departments). Now VZMSH, together with other scientific and pedagogical institutions, is conducting research on the development of new interactive technologies in education and the translation of part of its educational and methodological complexes into the language of modern telecommunications. During the existence of VZMSH, several hundred thousand schoolchildren and thousands of clubs and groups “Collective Student of VZMSH” received certificates of completion. Education at school is CORRESPONDENT, i.e. starting from September–October 2017, all applicants will systematically receive materials specially designed for distance learning, containing a presentation of theoretical issues and methods of reasoning, various tasks for independent work, sample solutions to problems, business games, tests and practical tasks. Students' test papers will be carefully checked and reviewed by VZMS teachers - students, graduate students, teachers and researchers of Moscow State University, as well as other universities and institutions where the school has branches. Many of the teachers themselves graduated from VZMSH at one time and therefore understand especially well how important it is to indicate, in addition to specific shortcomings, ways to eliminate existing gaps in knowledge, recommend additional literature, scold for inattention and praise for noticeable (and sometimes even the smallest) progress and hard work. Those admitted to VZMSH will be able to learn about fascinating things that are often left behind the pages of a school textbook, get acquainted with interesting non-standard problems and try their hand at solving them.
