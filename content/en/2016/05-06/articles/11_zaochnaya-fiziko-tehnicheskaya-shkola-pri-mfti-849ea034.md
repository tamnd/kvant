Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (CPTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (State University) (MIPT) enrolls students in grades 7–10 from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation into grades 8–11. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 95 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its former student. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by councils from teachers of specialized departments of MIPT. Education at the school is conducted in four scientific and technical subjects - physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry. In 8th grade, only physics and mathematics are studied. In grades 9–11, these subjects are supplemented by the subject “Mathematical foundations of computer science and ICT” (computer science) and chemistry. Students can choose to study one, two, three or four (in grades 9, 10 and 11) subjects. The goal of our school is to help students in grades 8–11 of general education institutions who are interested in scientific and technical subjects, deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, and also contribute to their professional self-determination. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2017/18 academic year is carried out for part-time, full-time and part-time departments. ZFTSH programs are specialized additional educational programs and are the same for all departments.
