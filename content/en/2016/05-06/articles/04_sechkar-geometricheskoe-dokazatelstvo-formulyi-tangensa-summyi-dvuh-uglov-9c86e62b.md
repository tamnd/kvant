Evgeniy Yakovlevich Gik (05/10/1943–10/24/2016)

On October 24, 2016, Evgeniy Yakovlevich Gik, a mathematician, chess player and chess writer, long-time author and presenter of the “Chess Page” of our magazine, passed away. By the time he graduated from the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, Evgeniy Yakovlevich was already a famous chess player, a participant in the USSR championship. The intersection in one person of two wonderful worlds in which he was a professional, complemented by literary abilities, made him one of the most famous chess writers and journalists. To this day, many remember the exciting story written by Evgeny Yakovlevich together with playwright Leonid Zorin about the game Lutikov-Taimanov from the last round of the 1969 USSR Championship. A game that not only decided the fate of one of the tickets to the interzonal tournament, but also an enchanting one, as Evgeniy Yakovlevich himself called it. The “Chess Page” appeared as a permanent section of the magazine «Kvant» in 1980. For 36 years, Evgeniy Yakovlevich, in each issue of the magazine, talked about interesting events on the “chess-mathematical board” and shared purely sports chess news with readers. Moreover, this was first-hand news, since he was personally acquainted with most of the outstanding modern players, and as a journalist he was present at all historical matches and tournaments. In 2015, this creative longevity in Evgeniy Yakovlevich’s constant and continuous collaboration with the magazine «Kvant» was awarded a special certificate from the International Record Registration Agency Interrecord. In total, “The Chess Page” was published 291 times. Evgeniy Yakovlevich Gik was an active and enthusiastic person, the total number of books he published was close to 200. For readers and employees of the magazine «Kvant», he was a tireless, bright and authoritative guide in the world about which Mikhail Tal once said: “The chess virus is contagious!”
