6. There are $n \ge 2$ segments on the plane so that any two of them intersect at an interior point, and no three of them have a common point. Ivan chooses one of the ends of each segment and puts a frog in it facing the other end of this segment. Then he claps his hands n – 1 times. With each clap, each frog immediately jumps forward to the next intersection point on its segment. Frogs never change the direction of their jumps. Ivan initially wants to place the frogs so that no two of them will ever be at the same intersection point at the same time. a) Prove that Ivan can always achieve what he wants if n is odd. b) Prove that Ivan can never achieve what he wants if n is even.

XLVII International Physics Olympiad

The International Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics in 2016 was held in Switzerland, in the city of Zurich. 450 schoolchildren from 90 countries came to the Olympiad. Together with them, 250 adult members of delegations took part in the Olympiad. Participants in the Olympiad were offered three theoretical problems (theoretical round) and two experimental problems (experimental round). All tasks were worth 10 points. Thus, each participant could score a maximum of 50 points. The Russian national team included: Alexander Artemyev (Kirov, Kirov Physics and Mathematics Lyceum), Maxim Eliseev (Mordovia, Republican Lyceum for Gifted Children), Ilya Kochergin (Moscow, Lyceum “Second School”), Ivan Uteshev (Mordovia, Republican Lyceum for Gifted Children), Vasily Yugov (Perm, school 146 with in-depth study of mathematics, physics, computer science). In the unofficial team competition, our team, scoring 207.8 points, took fourth place, behind China (231.3 points), South Korea (225.8 points) and Taiwan (211.6 points).
