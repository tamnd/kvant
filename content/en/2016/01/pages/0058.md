KVANT 2016/№1

6. A homogeneous rod 2 m long, moving along its length along a rough horizontal surface, begins to cross the boundary, beyond which the surface becomes smooth. The speed of the rod at this moment is 1.6 m/s. What distance will the rod travel from this moment until it stops if the coefficient of friction on the rough surface is 0.2? 7. A small washer slides from a height of 1 m from an inclined plane forming an angle $45 ^\circ$ with the horizon. At the end of the descent at the base of the inclined plane, the puck hits the wall in a perfectly elastic manner and rises up the inclined plane. To what height will the puck rise after impact if the coefficient of friction of the puck on the plane is 0.25?

⟦column⟧

8. Two identical bodies with masses of 5 kg are connected by an undeformed spring with a stiffness of 15 N/m and lie on a horizontal floor. What minimum speed, directed along the axis of the spring, must be imparted to one of the bodies in order for it to move the other body? The friction coefficient for each body is 0.1. 9. On a smooth floor there is a board with a mass of 1.5 kg, on which lies a block with a mass of 490 g. A bullet with a mass of 10 g, flying horizontally along the board at a speed of 100 m/s, hits the block and gets stuck in it. How far will the block move along the board if the coefficient of friction between them is 0.5?
