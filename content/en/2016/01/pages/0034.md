...what is lost in strength is gained in speed. Simon Stevin

In a state of equilibrium, force produces no real effect; it evokes only a simple desire to move; but it must always be measured by the effect which it would produce if it acted in the absence of any obstacle. Joseph Louis Lagrange

The movement of a system of material points connected with each other in an arbitrary way..., at every moment occurs in the most perfect... agreement with the movement that these points would have if they all became free, i.e. occurs with the least possible coercion... Carl Friedrich Gauss

This approach is called the principle of virtual (i.e., possible or imaginary) work, because to apply it we must imagine that our system has moved a little, even if it actually did not move or is unable to move at all. Richard Feynman

Are you so familiar with the connected movement?

Excerpts from the works of a whole line of prominent scientists testify to their long-term existence - several centuries! – attention to the topic of our today's issue. What is striking in this “summary” is the painstaking search for the most general laws underlying mechanics, the so-called first principles, or, in the words of Lagrange, a certain “general formula”. From these universal principles, scientists hoped to derive, as special cases, all the variety of discovered laws. And they succeeded! The creation of analytical mechanics - an alternative to the Newtonian approach - turned out to be extremely productive not only for solving its own problems, but its methods grew from classical physics to modern physics, closely connecting different historical scientific eras. You may have to face this in the future, and you will appreciate both the intellectual power of your predecessors and the amazing beauty of the results they obtained. But this is ahead - far and high. What should we, who have not yet grown out of the “pants” of school physics, do now? You couldn't help but notice that the epigraphs constantly mention obstacles, restrictions imposed on the position or movement of mechanical systems - what are called connections. And this is nothing more than inclined planes, point hinges, rigid and light rods, inextensible and weightless threads, ideal blocks... Do you know the characters? So let’s now, without fear, “get in touch” with the heroes of our topic, because one of the meanings of the word “connection” is contact. Let it be close and fruitful.
