Pinocchio and his swing

S. DVORYANINOV

-T

YOU MUST HAVE A DIFFICULT TASK? – Papa Carlo asked Pinocchio, looking up from his workbench. - Why did you decide that? – Buratino was surprised. - Yes, everything is very simple. There is a book in front of you, but you haven’t flipped through it for a long time and haven’t opened a new page. This is what happens when you are thinking about a complex math problem. My book is not about mathematics at all, I am reading about the adventures of Baron Munchausen. And this is what I thought about. One day this baron allegedly pulled himself out of the swamp by his hair. Everyone understands that this is impossible according to all the laws of physics and that this never happens. But here's what I remembered. When I was very little, you pushed me on a swing. I sat down in the armchair, and from time to time you nudged him in time. I also asked you: “Stronger!” Higher! Even higher! Everything is clear here - I couldn’t push myself. But yesterday the boys and I were swinging on a swing, and here the situation was completely different. I jumped onto the swing and then, without any help, I swung very hard. If you had been around at that time, you would probably have asked me to be careful. But that's not the point. It seems to me that this rocking is akin to pulling yourself out of a swamp. But no one can do the latter, but everyone can swing on a swing. Why is this so?

⟦column⟧

There is probably some kind of contradiction hidden here and there is some kind of secret. I would like to sort this issue out. And it’s so interesting that I’m ready to stop reading a fascinating book about the adventures of Munchausen. So how do we swing on a swing? “Well, we’ll figure it out,” Papa Carlo shook off the shavings from his apron and sat down at the table. - Give me the paper. First we must remember about energy. Namely, mechanical energy. There are two types of such energy... “Yes, I know,” Buratino confidently picked up. Energy can be kinetic and potential. When I accelerate on a scooter, I do work. Both the scooter and I are moving on it with increasing speed. The higher the speed, the greater the kinetic energy. Then for some time I can roll at a constant speed by inertia. If at the same time my road goes up, then due to the accumulated kinetic energy I can roll up the hill. As you move upward, the speed decreases and the kinetic energy also decreases. Eventually I will stop, the kinetic energy will become zero. But the energy will not disappear, it will turn into potential energy. This energy is associated with height. I ended up on a hill, and therefore I have potential energy. Now I can turn around and roll down. In this case, the potential energy will decrease - it will turn into kinetic energy of motion. - Well, I see that you know physics well and you understand that your energy also changes when you swing on a swing. You find yourself at the very top and stop for a moment - your potential energy is the highest, but your kinetic energy is zero. At the lowest point of your trajectory, the potential energy is zero, and the kinetic energy is the greatest. It is important that with such oscillations of yours - this is what the swing of a pendulum is called, and your swing is the same pendulum - the law of conservation of energy K + P = C is satisfied, where K is kinetic energy, P is potential energy, C is a constant value for oscillations with a given amplitude. Yes, let me explain: amplitude is the range of vibrations.
