PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 3–2016” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2421” or “F2428”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics can also be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively. Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2421–M2428, F2428–F2434

M2421. The company recorded its expenses in rubles for 100 budget items, receiving a list of 100 numbers (each number has no more than two decimal places). Each accountant takes a copy of the list and finds an approximate amount of expenses, proceeding as follows. First, he selects any two numbers from the list, adds them, discards the decimal places from the sum (if any) and writes the result instead of the selected two numbers. With the resulting list of 99 numbers, he does the same, and so on, until there is only one integer left in the list. It turned out that in the end all accountants got different results. What is the largest number of accountants that could work in the company? M. Evdokimov

M2422. The rectangle p ¥ q, where p, q are coprime natural numbers, p < q, is divided into unit squares. A diagonal is drawn from the lower left corner of the rectangle to its upper right corner. It cuts off triangles from some squares. Find: a) total perimeter; b) the total area of ​​all these triangles. A.Tolpygo

M2423. Circles $\omega_{1}$ and $\omega_{2}$ of the same radius intersect at points $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ (Fig. 1). Circle $\omega$ touches circle $\omega_{1}$ externally at point $T_{1}$ and circle $\omega_{2}$ internally at point $T_{2}$. Prove that the lines $X_{1} T_{1}$ and $X_{2} T_{2}$ intersect on the circle $\omega$. Ch. Latham (Luxembourg)

M2426. There are 64 cities in the country, some pairs of them are connected by road, but we do not know which ones. We can choose any pair of cities and get an answer to the question whether there is a road between them.
