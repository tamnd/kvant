4. Dad decided to take Vasya with him on winter fishing. Dad’s inventory included two extra lead weights with the same mass. The first was a cube, and the second was a cylinder, the height of which was equal to the length of the cube's edge. Small hooks were attached to the middle of one of the faces of the cube and to the center of one of the bases of the cylinder. At some point, Vasya got bored and started experimenting with these weights. 1. On the planet of the Shedar star system, one of the charging points for robot performers serves one robot at a time at a speed of 60 robots per hour (r/h). One day the robots arrived at the point within 8 hours. The first hour they came at a speed of 70 r/hour. During the second hour, the rate of arrival of robots gradually increased, reaching 80 r/h by the end. Then, during the third hour, the rate of arrival of robots gradually decreased and dropped back to 70 r/h by the end of the hour. In the fourth hour, the rate of arrival of robots dropped evenly, amounting to 20 r/h by the end of the hour and then remained this way for the fifth and sixth hours. At the seventh hour there was again a uniform increase in the rate of arrival of robots - up to 100 r/h at the end of the hour, and at the eighth hour there was a uniform decrease in the rate of arrival to 60 r/h. The robots strictly follow the order, and after charging they immediately leave the point. 1) How long did the point work that day if all the robots were charged? 2) What is the longest duration of the robot’s stay at the charging point (in the queue and at the charging station itself) on this day? A. Frolov

The girls' rheostats are different, but on each of them the slider moves in a straight line and is in the middle position at the beginning of the game. For Alice, the distance between the extreme possible positions of the slider is $L_{A} = 36$ cm, and for Vasilisa – $L_{B} = 40$ cm. 1) Alice “goes” first. She moved her slider 4 cm to the right, and the ammeter began to show a current of $I_{1} = 90$ mA.
