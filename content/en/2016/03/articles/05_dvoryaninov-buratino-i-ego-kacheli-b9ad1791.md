Pinocchio and his swing

S. DVORYANINOV

-T

YOU MUST HAVE A DIFFICULT TASK? – Papa Carlo asked Pinocchio, looking up from his workbench. - Why did you decide that? – Buratino was surprised. - Yes, everything is very simple. There is a book in front of you, but you haven’t flipped through it for a long time and haven’t opened a new page. This is what happens when you are thinking about a complex math problem. My book is not about mathematics at all, I am reading about the adventures of Baron Munchausen. And this is what I thought about. One day this baron allegedly pulled himself out of the swamp by his hair. Everyone understands that this is impossible according to all the laws of physics and that this never happens. But here's what I remembered. When I was very little, you pushed me on a swing. I sat down in the armchair, and from time to time you nudged him in time. I also asked you: “Stronger!” Higher! Even higher! Everything is clear here - I couldn’t push myself. But yesterday the boys and I were swinging on a swing, and here the situation was completely different. I jumped onto the swing and then, without any help, I swung very hard. If you had been around at that time, you would probably have asked me to be careful. But that's not the point. It seems to me that this rocking is akin to pulling yourself out of a swamp. But no one can do the latter, but everyone can swing on a swing. Why is this so?

⟦column⟧

There is probably some kind of contradiction hidden here and there is some kind of secret. I would like to sort this issue out. And it’s so interesting that I’m ready to stop reading a fascinating book about the adventures of Munchausen. So how do we swing on a swing? “Well, we’ll figure it out,” Papa Carlo shook off the shavings from his apron and sat down at the table. - Give me the paper. First we must remember about energy. Namely, mechanical energy. There are two types of such energy... “Yes, I know,” Buratino confidently picked up. Energy can be kinetic and potential. When I accelerate on a scooter, I do work. Both the scooter and I are moving on it with increasing speed. The higher the speed, the greater the kinetic energy. Then for some time I can roll at a constant speed by inertia. If at the same time my road goes up, then due to the accumulated kinetic energy I can roll up the hill. As you move upward, the speed decreases and the kinetic energy also decreases. Eventually I will stop, the kinetic energy will become zero. But the energy will not disappear, it will turn into potential energy. This energy is associated with height. I ended up on a hill, and therefore I have potential energy. Now I can turn around and roll down. In this case, the potential energy will decrease - it will turn into kinetic energy of motion. - Well, I see that you know physics well and you understand that your energy also changes when you swing on a swing. You find yourself at the very top and stop for a moment - your potential energy is the highest, but your kinetic energy is zero. At the lowest point of your trajectory, the potential energy is zero, and the kinetic energy is the greatest. It is important that with such oscillations of yours - this is what the swing of a pendulum is called, and your swing is the same pendulum - the law of conservation of energy K + P = C is satisfied, where K is kinetic energy, P is potential energy, C is a constant value for oscillations with a given amplitude. Yes, let me explain: amplitude is the range of vibrations.

– Therefore, if I want to swing stronger and stronger, i.e. rise higher and higher, I must increase the value of this constant C. And this can be achieved by increasing either the value of K or the value of P. I see! When you pushed my baby swing earlier, you increased my kinetic energy. Now my comrades can act the same way if they are nearby. But even if they are not there, I myself, alone, can swing stronger and stronger. How is this achieved? This is my main question! – Buratino said with enthusiasm. – Let’s find out what kind of energy – kinetic or potential – you can increase while on a swing. You cannot push yourself, so all hope remains for increasing potential energy. But you can really increase it. And what are you doing for this while on the swing?.. Eh?.. Do you remember?.. “Yes,” Buratino was delighted. I remembered! Standing on a plank, I alternately bend my knees and then straighten them, i.e. I take turns squatting and straightening up. And when I straighten up, i.e. I stand up to my full height, then I do work to raise my center of mass (or center of gravity), and this work turns into the potential energy of the swing. But then, when I squat, potential energy - here Buratino fell silent and an expression of disappointment appeared on his face. – So what does potential energy do there? – Papa Carlo encouraged him. – Potential energy decreases, and, therefore, the total energy of the mechanical system under consideration does not receive any increase. “I can’t swing stronger,” Buratino concluded with disappointment. - Let's not rush, but be careful. After all, experiments, i.e. your experience, they say that swaying occurs! Don’t give up and don’t be discouraged, look for a theoretical explanation for this phenomenon. - They are looking for mushrooms in the forest, but where to look for an explanation? - Pinocchio became depressed. - Like where? In your reasoning! It happens that you solved an equation, checked the answer and saw that you were wrong. You begin to decide again, carefully carrying out all the calculations. So it is now. Here I will give you a little hint. Let's start from the very beginning. You swing back and forth on a swing with a constant amplitude on your legs bent at the knees. And at the lowest point you instantly stood up to your full height. Do you think that after this the amplitude (i.e. span) will increase?

- Certainly. After all, I raised myself, did work, increased the potential energy, and therefore the total energy of the system. “So there is a way to increase the potential energy.” Maybe not by much, but increase it. I would like to repeat this technique. Right? In other words, it is advisable to stand up to your full height again. But now you are already standing and it is impossible to repeat this technique. How to be?.. – You can stand up to your full height only from a state in which your legs are bent at the knees. And so now I have to sit down,” Buratino agreed. The most important thing is to choose the moment when to do this. If you bend your knees at the lowest point of the trajectory, then the loss of potential energy will be the same as its increase was before. The total effect of the two actions will be zero, we will return to the original oscillation mode. It's best to sit down... Think about when. To give the correct answer, look at two identical stairs. Only one of them stands vertically (like a fire escape near a house), and the other is inclined. If you climb, say, 10 steps on each staircase, then in which case will you climb to a greater height - a vertical staircase or an inclined one? It is appropriate to remember here that the leg of a right triangle is less than the hypotenuse. Everything is clear to me! - Buratino said cheerfully. Once at the highest point of the trajectory, I must immediately squat down and bend my knees. In this case, my vertical displacement will be less than my vertical rise, which was at the bottom point of the trajectory. Therefore, the loss of potential energy will be less than its increase. As a result of two actions - straightening at the bottom point and squatting at the top point - the swing will receive an increase in energy, the amplitude of oscillations will increase. We can repeat such actions, thereby pumping energy into our mechanical system. In this case, our swing turns out to be a pendulum of variable length. Bend your legs at the knees - you get a long pendulum of length L, stand up to your full height - you get a short pendulum of length l. – Well, we’ve sorted out the physics. You can continue your reading, and I can continue my work. But it should be noted that we have given the simplest explanation of how a swing swings. We did not take into account all the circumstances. But this is a topic for another conversation.
