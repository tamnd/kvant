"QUANT" FOR JUNIOR SCHOOLCHILDREN

Tasks

1.

Can the number 2015 be represented as the sum of five consecutive natural numbers? T. Volosnikova

⟦column⟧

4.

2.

We have two birch trees and two rowan trees growing in our yard. When Vasya looks out of his window, he sees two birch trees standing between two rowan trees. When Petya looks out of his window, he sees two rowan trees standing between two birch trees. How can this be? S.Dvoryaninov

What is the smallest number of figures that the square $7 \times 7,$ can be completely cut into if the figures are three-cell corners and five-cell figures, as in figure? The figures can be rotated and flipped over. N. Agakhanov, O. Podlipsky

5.

Borya, Valya and Gera composed a theorem: Given two polygons having equal areas. Then one of them can be cut into 100 pieces, from which another can be folded. Is the Bory, Vali and Hera theorem true? P. Kozhevnikov

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

3.

Eleven people sit around a round table - each is either a knight or a liar. A knight always tells the truth, and a liar always lies. Each of them was asked: “Who is sitting to your right?” Could all eleven answer “liar”? N. Agakhanov, O. Podlipsky

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.
