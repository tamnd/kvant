Tasks

1.

To repair the propeller, Carlson needs to buy 3 blades and 1 screw. The store sells blades for 120 ore and cogs for 9 ore. But after purchasing for at least 250 era, they give a 20% discount on all subsequent purchases. Will Carlson be able to repair the propeller if he only has a 360 øre with him? E. Bakaev

4.

When our fingers are cold, we blow on them to warm them up. When we burn our hand, we also blow to cool it. How are the positions of our lips different in these situations? Why does the flow of air from the mouth warm in one case, and cool in another? Folklore

2.

On the top face of the cube $3 \times 3 \times 3$, a cube $1 \times 1 \times 1$ was glued to the central square $1 \times 1$. How to divide the resulting figure into 7 equal ones? One way to write down the answer is to draw each layer of the figure separately (bottom, middle, top and topmost cube) and on each cube write the number of the part to which it belongs. E. Bakaev

3.

Each of the artists of the creative association “Patience and Labor” has his own work schedule. Six of them paint one painting every two days, and the remaining eight artists paint one painting every three days. From September 22 to 26, they painted a total of 30 paintings. How many paintings will they paint on September 27? E. Bakaev

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8. These problems were proposed at the XXXVIII Tournament named after M.V. Lomonosov.

A square frame was placed on the ground and a vertical pole was placed in the center of the square. When fabric was stretched over this structure from above, it turned out to be a small tent. If you place two identical frames side by side, place a vertical pole of the same length in the center of each and stretch fabric on top, you will get a large tent. The small tent required 4 square meters of fabric. How much fabric is needed for a large tent? M. Raskin
