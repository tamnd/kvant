OLYMPICS

The final stage of the XLI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics

The final stage of the XLI All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held from April 23 to April 29, 2015 in the capital of Tatarstan, Kazan. 355 schoolchildren from 50 regions of Russia took part in the Olympiad, showing the highest results at the regional stage. Traditional guests took part in the Olympiad: teams from Bulgaria and the People's Republic of China. As in previous years, on each of the two days of the Olympiad, participants were offered 4 tasks for 5 hours of work. Despite the fact that the tasks of the Olympiad were quite difficult, the schoolchildren successfully completed them. And in each class there were participants who solved all 8 problems. According to tradition, after the Olympiad, schoolchildren took part in a vote in which they chose the most beautiful problems, in their opinion. The winners in this competition were: in 9th grade – problems 6 (I place), 8 (II place) and 7 (III place); in 10th grade – 7 (I place; note that last year its author Maxim Didin was the winner of the All-Russian and then the International Olympiad for Schoolchildren), 3 (II place) and 8 (III place); in 11th grade – 4 (I place), 6 (II place) and 3 (III place). Most of these problems are included in the “Problem Book «Kvant»” of this issue. It is worth noting the high level of organization of the Olympiad, thanks to which it turned into a real holiday for the best young mathematicians of the country. At the closing ceremony, the awards and diplomas were presented to the winners of the Olympiad by the Minister of Education and Science of the Russian Federation D.V. Livanov, and Fields Medal laureate S.K. Smirnov spoke with parting words for gifted schoolchildren. In addition to traditional excursions, the Olympiad program included popular science lectures by famous mathematicians: “Simple and Complex Problems” by A. Shen, “What is a Random Walk?” S. Smirnova, “The complexity of calculations, and why you can’t live without it” by E. Hirsch. Below are the conditions of the tasks and the list of winners and prize-winners of the final stage of the XLI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics.
