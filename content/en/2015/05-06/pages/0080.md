Guess who this is: Russian industrialist, calico magnate, philanthropist. i His wife and friend, Vera Nikolaevna, nee Mamontova. i The young merchant, with a guide and a map, traveled to all European museums and became a keen connoisseur of painting. i At the age of 24 he founded a private gallery of Russian painting in his family home. i At the age of 60, he donated his gallery to Moscow. i Of all the artists contemporary to him, he preferred the Itinerants. i I dreamed of finding Rublev’s icons, but luck smiled on another collector. i Became the first director of the museum he founded, spent all his savings on it, but out of modesty he never showed up for his own anniversaries. i Completely unselfish. His wallet was always open to those in need. i I never bargained with artists. For high school students preparing to take the Unified State Exam in social studies. In 2007, the “History” department opened the “Social Studies” course. Social science is a set of social disciplines that study all aspects of human activity. Some teachers call it a vinaigrette. Quite an apt comparison. Just as a vinaigrette is a composite dish, social studies consists of different disciplines. Mixing and complementing each other, they create a complete picture of society. The correspondence school of physics and technology at MIPT is 50 years old

ZFTSH programs are specialized additional educational programs and are the same for all departments.

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (ZPTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) recruits students in grades 7–10 from general educational institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation into grades 8–11. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 95 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its former student. The scientific, methodological and general management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (state university). Education at the school is conducted in three scientific and technical subjects - physics, mathematics and computer science. In grades 8–9, only physics and mathematics are studied. In grades 10–11, the subject “Mathematical foundations of computer science and ICT” (computer science) is added. Students can choose to study one, two or three (in grades 10 and 11) subjects. The goal of our school is to help students in grades 8–11 of general education institutions who are interested in scientific and technical subjects, deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, and also contribute to their professional self-determination. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2016/17 academic year is carried out for part-time, part-time and full-time courses.

Correspondence department (individual distance learning) Tel./fax: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of the introductory assignment in the subjects chosen for study. The full training program is designed for 4 years from 8th to 11th grades inclusive, but you can start training from any of these classes. During the academic year, in accordance with the ZFTS program, the student will receive assignments in physics, mathematics and computer science for each topic, and then the recommended author’s solutions to these assignments along with a verified work. The assignments contain theoretical material, analysis of typical examples and problems on the relevant topic, and 8–12 test questions and problems for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones. The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics and higher mathematics at MIPT, as well as MIPT graduates and other specialists. Examples of assignments can be viewed on the ZFTSH website: http://www.school.mipt.ru. The work of part-time students is checked by students, graduate students and graduates of MIPT (80% of them are graduates of our school). The deadline for sending solutions to the introductory assignment is no later than March 1, 2016. Checked entrance papers are not sent back to the applicant. The decision of the Admissions Committee will be sent in July 2016.
