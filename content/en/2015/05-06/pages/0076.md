Below you will find brief information about each department of the Higher Medical School and the conditions for entrance examinations.

Department of Mathematics

We accept studies in all courses from 0 to 5 (which corresponds to grades 6–11 of a secondary school). You can get all the information about the work of the mathematics department on the department's website: http://www.math-vzms.org After enrollment in school, the student receives benefits under the VZMSH program, the vast majority of which are written specifically for students in the mathematics department of the VZMSH. The student studies the proposed topics, solves problems and completes a test on each topic, which is checked, reviewed by the school teacher and, along with all comments and advice, sent to the student. Each student is assigned the same teacher for all years of study, who can always be asked questions if necessary. This is how we help students study by correspondence. In addition, we accept schoolchildren for a group form of education - “Collective Student”. In this case, several students work on our manuals under the guidance of a school teacher (usually) and send one collective work on each topic to VZMSH. Admission to a correspondence school for group study is carried out upon the application of the group leader (school teacher) without introductory work. The number of copies of manuals that are sent to the “Collective Student” group is determined by the head in accordance with the Agreement on Scientific and Pedagogical Cooperation, which the school where the group works concludes with VZMSH. If you want to study individually, then you need to complete the introductory work, the conditions of which are presented below. The solutions to the problems that you managed to solve must be written down in a regular student notebook in a square and sent in a simple parcel (please do not roll the notebook into a tube!) along with an application for admission to the school. Entry papers and applications are also accepted by email: priem@math-vzms.org. In this case, the work must be formatted as a *.doc or *.pdf file; you can also scan the text of the work and attach it to the application for admission. The application for admission is written in free form. Please indicate: last name, first name, patronymic, year of birth, basic education (how many grades of secondary school will be completed by September of the next school year; it would be convenient for us

Tasks

1 (6–7). Is there an integer whose product of digits equals 2015? What about 2016? 2 (6–8). We brought 100 identical pairs of shoes to the store, where they were mixed. One hundred people bought two shoes, then 25 of them came complaining that the shoes were mismatched. Prove that there is at least one other customer who did not make it to the store and has the same complaint. 3 (6–10). Tile the plane with squares, two of which are identical, and the rest are all different in pairs. 4 (6–11). Two cyclists rode simultaneously towards each other from points A and B, met 70 km from A and continued moving at the same speeds. Upon arriving at their destinations, everyone rested for the same amount of time and headed back at the same speed as before. Cyclists meet 90 km from B. Find the distance AB. 5 (8–10). Given integers m and n, about which it is known that 23n = 19m. Prove that the number n + 2m is divisible by 13. 6 (8–10). Several points are drawn on the plane, all the distances between which are different. Each point was connected by a line segment to the point closest to it. Prove that the resulting segments do not intersect. 7 (8–10). Find all natural numbers x such that $x^{4} + x^{2} + 1$ is a prime number. 8 (8–10). Suggest at least two different ways to use a compass and ruler (without divisions) to divide a given segment into three equal parts. 9 (8–11). There are 2016 matches on the table. Two players take turns removing 1 or 2 matches. The one who takes the last match wins. Who will win: the beginner or his partner and how should he play for this? 10 (8–11). The sum of the diagonals of a quadrilateral is contained between its semi-perimeter and perimeter. Is this statement always true? Any answer needs proof! 11 (8–11). At what natural value of a is the number 3a + 1 divisible by the number a + 3?
