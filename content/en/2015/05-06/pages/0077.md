Tasks

1. Imagine that you need to keep animals in captivity for your research. Your task is to minimize the damage to animals that they will receive from such housing and provide them with the most comfortable living possible. Also, your animals must be in such conditions that they can reproduce. Under what conditions would you keep the following animals: grape snail, sea angel, octopus, axolotl, Central Asian turtle, zebra finch, quail, rat, rabbits, minks, macaques a) in a laboratory vivarium; b) in a zoo? Please note that in a zoo setting, you must place animals in such a way that they are within view of visitors. 2. Why do you think a person needs baby teeth? Or is this a meaningless atavism? 3. What properties of microorganisms can be studied without a microscope? 4. How do you understand why the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded in the fall of 2015? State in your own words the essence of the discovery and its significance for the further development of medicine. 5. It is known that organisms are unicellular, multicellular and colonial. Give as many differences between these organisms as possible. How to distinguish coloniality from true multicellularity? 6. What discovery in the field of biology do you think is most anticipated at the moment? Try to explain why the discovery you have chosen is important, what prospects it opens up and why it has not been made yet.

Tasks

1. Find the ratio of the volumes of the part of the human body immersed in water after exhaling in the Dead Sea and after inhaling in fresh water, if due to inhalation the volume of the human body increases by 8%. The density of salt water in the Dead Sea is $\rho_{c} = 1,3$ g $см^{3}$, the density of fresh water is $\rho_{п} = 1,0$ g $см^{3}. 2.$ The resistance of a wire cube is measured by connecting contacts to the vertices lying on one of the large diagonals. What is the maximum number of times that the value of this resistance can be increased by cutting through some of the wires? 3. When hardening a steel part with a mass of m = 80 kg heated to $t_{д} = 800 ^\circ C$, it is immersed in a bath of water with a volume of V = 50 l and a temperature of $t_{в} = 20 ^\circ C$. Find what fraction of the water has evaporated if the final state temperature of the system is $t = 80 ^\circ C$. Neglect the heat capacity of the bath and heat exchange with the external environment. Specific heat capacity of steel $c_{c} = 460$ J (kg $\cdot ^\circ$ C), water $c_{в} = = 4200$ J (kg $\cdot ^\circ$ C), specific heat of vaporization of water $\lambda = 2256$ kJ kg, density of water $\rho = 1000$ kg $м^{3}. 4.$ A car and a motorcycle drive uniformly along intersecting roads (Fig.1) If a car leaves from point A, then it arrives at the intersection $\Delta t_{1} = 1$ minutes earlier than a motorcycle, which simultaneously starts moving from point B. If a car starts from point B, and a motorcycle starts from point A, then the car arrives at the intersection $\Delta t_{2} = 4$ minutes earlier. Find the shortest distance between $транс-^{Рис.} 1$ tailors in the second case, if in the first it is equal to $d_{1} = 100$ m. 5. Water is poured into the aquarium to a level h = 40 cm. A point light source can move above the aquarium, describing a semicircle with a radius R = 40 cm in a plane perpendicular to the surface of the water. In the same plane, on the surface of the water, a match of length l = 5 cm floats, the middle of which coincides with the center of the semicircle. Find the ratio of the lengths of shadows from a match on the bottom of the aquarium with the light source at the top point and at a height R 2 from the surface of the water. The refractive index of water is n = 1.33. 6. On the stretch between metro stations, the train must move at a constant speed v = 70 km/h for 80% of the time. What maximum speed could the train reach if the driver forgot to turn off acceleration during acceleration, but was subsequently able to bring the train to the station?
