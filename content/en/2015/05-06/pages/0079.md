⟦folio 77⟧

INFORMATION

Education at the economics department is correspondence; students receive all educational materials via the Internet. Those who will complete the 7th (or higher) grade in 2016, the duration of study is 1 year, can study at the economics department. The economics department program includes the study of economic theory and familiarity with the practice of economics and business. This can be a good basis for further study of entrepreneurship and management, accounting and finance, the world economy - the profession of an economist requires broad knowledge. While studying at the economics department, you will be able to meet in absentia students, graduates and teachers of the M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University, learn about a variety of wonderful professions in economics, one of which you may choose for yourself in the future. To enter the department, you need to complete the assignment given below in the introductory work.

Exercise

Small business in Russia is still underdeveloped, for example, compared to many European countries or the United States, but it is a very important part of the market economy. In this regard, creating your own small company in Russia is an unknown and very interesting project for many young people. Maybe you also dream of realizing your business idea when you graduate from higher education? Now you can start self-development in this area. This year we invite you to reflect on the statements of the family of bear businessmen from the cartoon “Ernest and Celestine” (2012).

⟦column⟧

Bear: It’s simple: if you want to make money, you need sugar to spoil other people’s teeth, not yours. It's clear? Little Bear: Uh-huh... (sighs)... yes.

Questions

Write a short essay in which you answer the following questions: 1. What goals, in your opinion, does a small business owner set or should set? 2. What goals do the Bear and the She-bear from the cartoon set for themselves? How would you characterize their small business strategy? 3. How long has the Bear family been in business? How long do they plan to continue their business? 4. Did Lyon’s parents convince him that eating candy was bad for him? Why do you think yes or why not? Send your answers by email: zadanie@econ4all.ru or by regular mail. Regardless of how you send your answer, be sure to indicate your full postal address and zip code, last name, first name, patronymic and class (in which you will study in the fall of 2016). Title the letter: “Economics, introductory task 2016.” The deadline for submitting the entry work is September 1, 2016. Our addresses on the Internet: http://econ4all.ru, http://vk.com/econ4all_group

Department of History

The bear is the owner of a store that sells teeth. The bear is the owner of a sweet shop. Lyon the bear is their little son.

At dinner Bear: Should I add some more peas, honey? Little Bear: No, I want sweets! Bear: Lyon... just don't start. We have already explained to you a hundred times that you cannot eat sweets!! Little Bear: Why?? Bear: This, my boy, is in the interests of trade. On one side of the street, dad spoils his teeth... Bear: ... and on the other, mom replaces rotten teeth. Bear: And when you grow up, both affairs will pass to you. Not one fortune, but two, my boy!! Ha! Bear: If, of course, you smile at your clients... Bear: As my old man said, healthy teeth make a smile.

Studying at the history department will allow everyone, including residents of the most remote cities and villages, to broaden their horizons and prepare for entering a university. Those who successfully complete the course receive a VZMS diploma. Why do you need to study history? First of all, it's just interesting. It is interesting to know how people once lived, what they dressed in, what they ate, what they read, how they got married, what they fought for and “what they got into.” Secondly, it's useful. Only by knowing the past can one understand the present and predict the future. We will help you figure it out. Experienced teachers write books especially for you. You will be one of the first to know the latest news from the world of history! We will keep in constant contact with you. According to our books, you will perform special tasks and tell us what you have unearthed. After all, in essence, the work of a historian consists of these excavations. The historian-archaeologist, digging the earth and sand, finds grains of bygone times; the historian-archivist digs through a pile of papers and takes out from the archives and even from private correspondence everything that can allow him to understand the image of time; a historian and theorist reads archaeological literature like a fascinating novel
