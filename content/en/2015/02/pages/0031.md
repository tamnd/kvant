No, she's just going to swap bodies with the Red Queen (Fig.2). 2. Perhaps everything that happened to Alice in Through the Looking Glass was just a dream she had. Here is a short excerpt that talks about the Black King's dream. - Darling, isn't it? – asked Tweedledum. Alice found it difficult to agree with him. The King was wearing a red nightcap with a tassel and an old dirty robe, and he lay under a bush and snored with such force that all the trees shook. - You can snore your own head! – Tweedledee remarked. “Lest he catch a cold,” worried Alice, who was a very caring girl. - After all, he is lying on the damp grass! - He's dreaming! - said Tweedledum. – And who do you think he’s dreaming about?! “I don’t know,” answered Alice. - No one can say this. - He dreams about you! - Tweedledum shouted and clapped his hands joyfully. – If he hadn’t seen you in his dreams, where would you be, I wonder? Here is Gardner's commentary on this passage: Alice dreams of the King, who dreams of Alice, who sees the King, and so on, like two mirrors placed in front of each other...
