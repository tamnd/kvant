Tasks

1.

What is the largest a) eight-digit; b) can a seven-digit number be typed on this calculator that will look exactly the same if the calculator is turned to $180 ^\circ$ (i.e. “upside down”)? L. Shteingarts

2.

A mathematician with five children went into a pizzeria. Masha: I’d like it with tomatoes and without sausage. Vanya: And for me with mushrooms.

a) Write in each circle at figure a number other than zero, so that the sum of the digits in the top two circles is 7 times less than the sum of the remaining digits, and the sum of the digits in the two left circles is 5 times less than the sum of the remaining digits. b) Prove that the problem has a unique solution. A. Shapovalov

4.

Cut the hexagon shown at figure into four identical shapes. You can only cut along the grid lines. E. Bakaev

Dad: Yes, with such picky people you obviously can’t get by with one pizza... Will a mathematician be able to order two pizzas and treat each child to the one he asked for, or will he still have to order three pizzas? E. Bakaev

A monkey becomes happy when he eats three different fruits. What is the largest number of monkeys that can be made happy with 20 pears, 30 bananas, 40 peaches and 50 tangerines? Justify your answer. A. Shapovalov
