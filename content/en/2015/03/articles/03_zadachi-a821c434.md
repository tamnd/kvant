"QUANT" FOR JUNIOR SCHOOLCHILDREN

Tasks

1.

There are three types of fish in the aquarium: gold, silver and red. If the cat eats all the goldfish, there will be 1 less fish than 2/3 of the original number. If the cat eats all the red fish, there will be 4 more fish than 2/3 of the original number. Which fish – gold or silver – are there more and by how much? I. Vysotsky, I. Raskina

Try to unravel these three puzzles, which are in no way connected with each other. But they are all located together, since each left word “hides” the right one. Letters must be replaced by corresponding numbers, other than zero. Note that each of these puzzles has a unique solution. 1) JOKE: 17 = DUCK 2) WATERMELON: 17 = BISON 3) BULL: 17 = CLUB L. Shteingarts

In the yard where there are four intersecting paths, there is one apple tree (as shown in figure). Plant three more apple trees so that there are equal numbers of apple trees on both sides of each path. E. Bakaev

3.

Volodya runs along a circular distance at a constant speed. There is a photographer at two points along the distance. After the start, Volodya was first closer to the first photographer for some time, then for 3 minutes - closer to the second photographer, and then again closer to the first. How long did it take Volodya to run the entire circle? V. Kovaldzhi

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8. Problems 1, 2, 5 were proposed at the XXVI Mathematical Festival, and a variation of problem 3 was proposed at the LXXVIII Moscow Mathematical Olympiad.

Smeshariki live on the banks of a pond in the shape of an equilateral triangle with a side of 600 m. Krosh and Barash live on the same bank, 300 m from each other. In the summer, Losyash walks 900 m to Krosh, and Barash walks 900 m to Nyusha. Prove that in winter, when the pond freezes and you can walk straight on the ice, Losyash will again walk the same number of meters to Krosh as Barash walks to Nyusha. E. Bakaev, A. Khachaturyan
