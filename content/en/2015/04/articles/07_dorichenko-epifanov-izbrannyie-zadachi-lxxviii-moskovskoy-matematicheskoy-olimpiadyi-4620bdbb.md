3. One mole of an ideal gas participates in the cyclic process 1–2–3–1 of a heat engine operating in heat engine mode. In state 1, the gas has a temperature of $T_{1}$ and a volume of $V_{1}$. It is known that all gas transitions from one state to another are polytropic. The polytropic index of process 2–3 is one more than the polytropic index of process 3–1 and one less than the polytropic index of process 1–2. In process 1–2, the gas volume increases k times. One of the processes in the cycle is isothermal, and in this process the volume of gas changes its value within the widest possible range in this cycle. 1) Determine the volume and temperature of the gas in state 3. 2) Draw on the pV diagram a cycle corresponding to the conditions of the problem, indicating for each of the processes its polytropic index. Reference. A process during which the heat capacity of the gas does not change is called polytropic: C = const. The equation of such a process has the form $pV^{n} =$ const, or $p_{1} V_{1}^{n} = p_{2} V_{2}^{n}$. The value n is called the polytropic index. E. Vishnyakova
