4. The tourists lit a fire and set water to boil in a pot with a flat bottom and vertical walls, filling the pot to n = 3/4 of its volume. When the water boiled, the pot was not removed from the fire, and $t_{1} = 10$ minutes after the start of boiling, the amount of water in the pot decreased by $\eta_{1} = =$ 34%. At that moment it started to rain, but the tourists continued to keep the fire going because a group of people with food was delayed. Over the next $t_{2} = 8$ minutes, the amount of water in the pot decreased by another $\eta_{2} =$ 8% of its original value. It is known that an empty pot placed vertically on the ground would be filled to the top in the rain in $t_{3} = 64$ minutes. Determine the temperature of the raindrops before they enter the pot. Specific heat capacity of water c = 4200 J (kg $\cdot ^\circ$ C), specific heat of vaporization of water $r = 2,2 \cdot 10^{6}$ J kg. Assume that the thermal power supplied to the water in the boiler is maintained constant all the time and the rain intensity does not change. M.Romashka

2. A round ring of radius R is fixed in a vertical plane, onto which a bead of mass m is placed at the top point. After a small push, the bead begins to slide down the ring under the influence of gravity. All frictional forces can be neglected. 1) With what force does the bead press on the ring at a point lying on its horizontal diameter? 2) What is the modulus of the bead's momentum at the moment when it does not press on the ring? M. Semenov

3. The faucet has two taps – cold and hot. The taps are identical in their design - it is such that the amount of water flowing through each tap in one second is proportional to the angle of rotation of the tap when it is opened. If you turn the cold tap to an angle of $\alpha_{1} = 180 ^\circ$, and the hot tap to an angle of $\beta_{1} = 60 ^\circ$, water will flow from the mixing tap at a temperature of $t_{1} = 36 ^\circ C$. If you turn the cold tap to an angle of $\alpha_{2} = 120 ^\circ$, and the hot tap to an angle of $\beta_{2} = 90 ^\circ$, then water with a temperature of $t_{2} = 48 ^\circ C$ will flow from the mixing tap. Find the temperature of the water flowing from the mixing tap when the cold tap is turned to an angle of $\alpha_{3} = 160 ^\circ$ and the hot tap is turned to an angle of $\beta_{3} = 80 ^\circ$. Neglect heat losses in the mixer. M.Romashka
