Some other properties

In conclusion, we present several more properties of finite sections without proof.

The property of conjugate diameters, proven for the ellipse **), with some variations, is transferred to the parabola and hyperbola. In a parabola, the set of midpoints of parallel chords lies on a ray parallel to the axis of symmetry of the parabola - each such ray is the diameter of the parabola. In one branch of the hyperbola, the midpoints of the parallel chords also lie on the ray. The straight line on which this ray lies passes through the center of the hyperbola and is called diametrical. Explore for yourself where the midpoints of parallel chords of another type lie - segments, one end of which lies on one and the other on the other branch of the hyperbola.
