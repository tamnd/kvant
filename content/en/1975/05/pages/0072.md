For more than 11 years, specialized boarding schools have been operating at Moscow, Leningrad, Novosibirsk and Kiev universities. These schools are attended by high school students (grades 8-10) who have shown an interest and aptitude for studying mathematics, physics, chemistry, and biology.

Let's talk in more detail about the boarding school at Leningrad University. It accepts students who have graduated from high school or the eighth grade, living in the Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Kaliningrad, Kirov, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions, the Karelian and Komi ASSR, the Latvian, Lithuanian and Estonian SSR.

Interviews with those wishing to enter a boarding school are conducted at regional, zonal, resource and All-Union Olympids. In addition, in June, in each region and resource center, exams are held in major subjects for everyone who wants to enroll in a boarding school. The dates of these exams can be found in the field of public education or the Ministry of Education of the relevant resources.

The overwhelming interest of boarding school graduates in continuing their education at Leningrad University.

Much attention is paid to various Olympids. A certain tradition has already developed in holding the Olympics. We hold competitions for beginners, the first round of school-wide competitions, in which all boarding school students participate, and the second round, the tasks of which are close in level of difficulty to those of the All-Union competitions.

Boarding school students successfully compete at All-Union Olympids. Suffice it to say that they became winners and recognized at these Olympids more than sixty times. Fifteen times boarding school students became winners and recognized in International Olympids.

We invite all lovers of mathematics and physics to our scale!

A. A. Bykov, Yu. I. Nonin
