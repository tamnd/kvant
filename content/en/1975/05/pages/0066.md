⟦folio 64⟧

Moscow State University
them. M. V. Lomonosova
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Faculty of Physics, Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

We spoke in detail about these faculties of Moscow State University in «Kvant», 1973, No. 4. Here we place the written exam options in mathematics offered to those entering these faculties in 1974, and the tasks offered in the oral exam in physics.

Mathematica

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics
Option 1

1. Solve the equation:
$$\sqrt{1 + \sin x} + \cos x = 0.$$

2. Solve inequality:
$$\sqrt{1 - \log_5(x^2 - 2x + 2)} < \log_5(5x^2 - 10x + 10).$$

3. A circle is circumscribed around triangle ABC. The median ADprod must intersect this circle at point E. It is known that AB + AD = DE, angle BAD = 60° and AE = 6. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC.

4. In a triangular paramid ABCD, edge DC = 9, edge DB = AD, and edge AC is perpendicular to face ABD. A sphere of radius 2 touches face ABC, edge DC, and face DAB at the intersection point of its medians. Find the volume of the paramid.

5. Find all real values ​​of α, for each of which there are even integers (x, y, u, v) satisfying the equalities:
$$x^2 + y^2 = (111 - \alpha)(\alpha - 89),$$
$$50(u^2 - v^2) = \alpha(15u + 5v - \alpha).$$
Option 2

1. Solve the equation:
$$\sqrt{2\sin 2x} + 2\sin x = 0.$$

2. Solve inequality:
$$\sqrt{1 + \log_2(7x^2 + 14x + 8)} \leq 1 + \log_8(7x^2 + 14x + 8).$$

3. A circle is circumscribed around triangle ABC. A tangent to this circle is drawn through point B until it intersects with the continuation of side CA at point D. It is known that AB + AD = AC, segment CD = 3, angle BAC = 60°. Find the perimeter of triangle ABC.

4. At the base of the triangular paramid ABCD lies triangle ABC, in which angle BAC = 60° and angle ACB is right. Face BCD makes an angle of 60° with face ABC. Edge BD = 2. The sphere touches edges AB, AC and face BCD. The center of the sphere - point O - lies on the base of the paramid and the segment OD is perpendicular to the plane of the base of the paramid ABCD. Find the length of edge AC.

5. Find all real values ​​of α, for each of which there are even integers (x, y, u, v) satisfying the equalities:
$$(x + y)(x + y + 20) = (140 - \alpha)(\alpha - 80),$$
$$\alpha(8u^2 + 2v^2 - \alpha) = (4u^2 - v^2)^2.$$

2. Solve inequality:
$$x^2 - 2|x + 1| < 0.$$

3. Solve the equation:
$$2\sqrt{3}\log_16x - \sqrt{3}\log_2x - 6 = 0.$$
