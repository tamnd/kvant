10. Let's construct an image of an object in flat and spherical mirrors (Fig. 5):

$$\frac{\mathrm{tg}\,\varphi_2}{\mathrm{tg}\,\varphi_1} = N = 1{,}8, \quad \mathrm{tg}\,\varphi_1 = \frac{y_1}{2a},$$

$$\mathrm{tg}\,\varphi_2 = \frac{y_2}{a+b}, \quad \frac{y_2}{y_1} = \frac{y_2}{y_0} = \frac{b}{a}.$$

From these equations we obtain $b = 9a$. But

$$\frac{1}{a} - \frac{1}{b} = \frac{2}{R}.$$

From here

$$R = \frac{9a^2 \cdot 2}{8a} = 54 \text{ см}.$$

11. When a light wave passes from one medium to another, the frequency $\nu$ is maintained, but the wavelength $\lambda$ and the speed of light propagation change: $\lambda_0 = \dfrac{c}{\nu}$,

$$\lambda_1 = \frac{v}{\nu} = \frac{c}{n\nu} \ (n \text{ — показатель преломления}$$

glass). Hence $n = \dfrac{\lambda_0}{\lambda_1} = 1{,}43$.

⟦column⟧

The angle of incidence $\varphi$ and the angle of refraction $\psi$ are related by the relation $\dfrac{\sin\varphi}{\sin\psi} = n$. By condition $\varphi + \psi = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Thus,

$$\mathrm{tg}\,\varphi = n = 1{,}43, \quad \varphi = 54°30'.$$
