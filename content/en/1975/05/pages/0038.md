$$\frac{(x - a)^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1,$$

or

$$y^2 = \frac{2b^2}{a} x - \frac{b^2}{a^2} x^2.$$

Noticing that $p = \frac{b^2}{a}$ (we can directly calculate this; see Fig. 7.6)

Fig. 8.

and $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{a^2 - c^2}{a^2} = 1 - \varepsilon^2$, we can write this equation like this:

$$y^2 = 2px + (\varepsilon^2 - 1)x^2.$$

The hyperbolic equation is reduced to exactly the same form (here the curve is shifted so that the right end of the focal axis is at the origin (see Fig. 8, 6) and $x$ is replaced by $x + a$).
