The equivalent force of elasticity of two springs acting on each ball is equal to $3kx$. According to Newton's second law for this ball (let's direct the $x$ axis in the direction of mixing of the ball)

$$a_x = \frac{F_x}{m} = -\frac{3kx}{m} = -\frac{3k}{m} x.$$

Let's compare the resulting formula with the equation of harmonic oscillators:

$$a_x = -\omega^2 x.$$

Hence the cyclic frequency

$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{3k}{m}}.$$

In the equilibrium position, the speed of the balls is maximum, and the springs are not deformed, that is, $E_1 = K_1 = \frac{3mv_0^2}{2}$ ($v_0$ – velocity amplitudes).

In the extreme position, the speed of the balls is zero, and the displacement is maximum and equal to the amplitude of the flasks $A$. In this case, the elongation of each spring is equal to $A \mid \bar{3}$, therefore

$$E_2 = \Pi_2 = 3 \frac{k(A\sqrt{3})^2}{2}.$$

$$\frac{3mv_0^2}{2} = 3 \frac{k(A\sqrt{3})^2}{2} \text{ и } v_0 = \sqrt{\frac{3k}{m}} A.$$
