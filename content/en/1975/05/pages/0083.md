In May 1945, the Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against Nazi Germany ended victoriously.
At the cost of 20 million lives

We will use here stamps issued for the 25th anniversary of the defeat of the Nazi police near Moscow and the 20th anniversary of the victory of the largest troops at Stalingrad and the Kursk Bulge. Here is a stamp dedicated to one of the largest operations of the final stage of the war—Operation Bagradion, as a result of which Belarus and our troops were liberated above the borders of East Prussia. With it, our people not only defended the gains of the Great October Revolution, but also liberated the peoples of Europe from fascism.
About three hundred stamps and blocks were issued in our country, telling their story of the Soviet people in the Great Patriotic War. Some of them came out during the war. They were dedicated to the heroic exploits of our warriors, partisans and those who, through selfless labor in the rear, created weapons and supplied the front with everything necessary. However, most of these stamps were issued after the end of the war. They were issued for various anniversaries. We reproduce some of these stamps in the photographs shown here.
At the top you see a stamp with the famous wartime poster by artist I. Tondze “The Motherland Calls”, which recognizes joining the people’s militia.
