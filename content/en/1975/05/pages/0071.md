Head of the Department of General Physics of FALT, Professor V. N. Zhigulev:

“Physics at Physics and Technology is the main subject! The unity of physics and technology for modern science and production is very clearly reflected in physics and technology.

Modern aviation and rocket technology uses the latest physical achievements. Therefore, a future specialist, a FALT graduate, must have a wide range of physical knowledge.

In the first year, FALT students study mechanics, which includes issues of special relativity, relativistic dynamics and field theory, molecular physics and thermodynamics, which covers the fundamentals of statistical physics and physical kinetics. In the second year, the courses “Oscillations and Waves” and “Electricity and Magnetism” are studied in parallel. The section “Oscillations and Waves” in the course of general physics is original; physical phenomena and processes that are related in the sense of the physical (oscillatory and wave) form of motion are studied here. The final section, Quantum Physics, is a year-long course covering the second half of the second year and the first half of the third year. Here students study the basic principles of modern atomic and nuclear theory, as well as solid state physics, quantum statistics and radiation physics.

Deputy Head of the Department of Higher Mathematics, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, Associate Professor V. M. Shalov:

“MIPT students study the following compulsory mathematical disciplines: mathematical analysis, analytical geometry, fundamentals of linear algebra, differential equations, equations of mathematical physics, theory of functions of a complex variable, probability theory.

In addition, in the third and fourth years of study, students of all faculties study the basics of computational mathematics and programming. All students undergo practical training on modern computers, study the algorithmic languages ​​ALGOL and FORTRAN.
