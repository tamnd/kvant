From the above it is clear that parabola is a boundary case between ellipses and hyperbolas. For a circle (ε=0) the second focus is with the first. With increasing eccentricity, the second focus moves away from the first, but as long as ε is less than 1, the curve remains an ellipse, constructed stretching. At ε=1, the ellipse is deformed into a parabola (its second focus “goes to infinity”), and then (for ε greater than 1) the second focus will again appear on the focal axis (on the other hand, inside the second branch of the hyperbola).
