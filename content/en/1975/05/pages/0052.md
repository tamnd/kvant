M321. There is a rectangular table of area 1. Prove that for any ε>0 it is possible to indicate a system of rectangular napkins covering this table (the edges of the napkins are parallel to the edges of the table), such that any of its subsystems consisting of non-overlapping napkins has an area less than ε.

A. V. Brailov

straight line so that all the letters a will be one from the straight line, and the letters b will be on the other (Fig. 1). Prove that no more than 2N - 2 letters are written.

S. V. Fomin
