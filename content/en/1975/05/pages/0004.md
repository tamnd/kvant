⟦folio 2⟧

Great feat of the Soviet people

On May 9, the Soviet people and together with them all progressive humanity celebrate the thirtieth anniversary of the victory over Hitler's Hermann. This great victory was won not only by the Soviet armed forces fighting on the front line, but also by the entire Soviet people under the leadership of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union.

Soviet scientists, flesh and blood of the people, did not stand aside either. They made a significant contribution to the victory, fighting at the front and working in scientific laboratories.

Let’s not forget the rally in the Communist Auditorium of Moscow State University on Mokhovaya, when the university Komsomol organization declared itself mobilized “to carry out any task of the party and government - at the front, in factories, in transport, on collective and state farm fields.” Front-line scientists fought selflessly against the enemy; all their thoughts were aimed at victory. But even then, in the days of hot battles, they remained faithful to science. The remarkable Soviet mathematician and teacher A. Ya. Khinchin wrote to one of them in March 1945:

“...You made me think about why during this war such very young fighters like you, with every little break, rush with all passion to their favorite work - to the work to which they devoted themselves before the war and from which they were torn off by the war. This was not the case during the last peaceful war. Then the young man who went to the front almost always felt that his life had changed, that everything he had lived before had become impossible for him. But now there are those who, in between battles, write dissertations and defend them when they come on a short vacation! Is it because you feel with your whole being your PATH work and your favorite pastime - science, art; practical activity - as two links of one and the same great cause? And if this is so, then isn’t this feeling one of the main driving forces behind your victories, which we here in the rear so admire?..”

Not everyone lived to see the day of the great victory. Many scientists gave their lives for the independence of our Motherland. We say their names with reverence.

Hero of the Soviet Union Evgenia Maksimovna Rudneva is a graduate of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University. Before the war, she headed the department of the All-Union Astronomer and Geodetic Society. Guard senior lieutenant, shtuman of the Guards Aviation Regiment, she flew 645 combat missions. The diaries and letters of Evgenia Maksimovna Rudneva are collected in the book “While the Heart Is Beating,” published by the Molodaya Gvardiya publishing house.
