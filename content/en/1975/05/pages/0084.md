To our readers

The subscription for 1975 to the popular scientific and mathematical journal «Kvant» continues.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics and love solving problems.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet become interested in physics or mathematics, but would like to become better acquainted with these sciences.

Now there will be a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work. In 1975, articles explaining the material of the new programs will be systematically published in the pages of the magazine.

Many materials will be published for 10th grade students preparing to enter college.

In 1975, «Kvant» will continue to print articles for younger students. The scope of these publications will be significantly expanded.

«Kvant» is also useful for sales. The materials published in «Kvant» can be used in mathematics and physics classes and in elective classes.

Read in «Kvant» in 1975:

Strata dedicated to experiments that can be performed on models of both physical and mathematical origin.

A large number of articles will be devoted to the new program at school. Thus, in the articles by A.G. Mordkovich, schoolchildren will become familiar with the concept of a derivative and its application in solving problems.

Bot has been teaching schoolchildren how to solve advanced problems in mathematics and physics for the fifth year in a section of the magazine “Kvanta Problem Book.” Schoolchildren from cities and villages take part in the work of this section. Readers who regularly submit correct solutions receive the right to participate in the Olympics. The editors provide consultations to schoolchildren who submit solutions to problems.

For those entering universities in 1975, articles on the following topics will be published. Mathematics: ijdectiveness of transformation of irrational equations; checking the solution; equations with parameters; proof of inequalities; use of multi-relative functions in solving equations and inequalities; solving trigonometric equations; solving problems on bodies of rotation; rectangular projection when solving stereometric problems; “hidden” data in the task conditions.

Physics: laws of ideal gases; electric current, transmission lines; transformers; construction of images (plastic, lens, mirror); optical instruments; eye; interference.

We will continue to introduce our readers to new books that are of interest to them, talk about the work of physics and mathematics schools, about Olympiads, about various types of work with schoolchildren, etc.

Subscriptions to «Kvant» are accepted without restrictions at Soizpechat subscription collection points, post offices, and post offices. When subscribing, refer to our index 70465. The price of the issue is 30 kopecks.
