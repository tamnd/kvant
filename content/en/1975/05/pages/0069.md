4. Two identical washers 1 and 2 of mass M each lie motionless on an absolutely smooth surface. Between them, on the lines connecting the centers of these washers, there is washer 3 with mass M/4 (same dimensions), which moves with speed v in the direction of the second washer (Fig. 2). Assuming that the collision washer is absolutely elastic, determine the velocity of all three washers by the magnitude and direction after the collision stops.

5. A spring (Fig. 3) is inserted between two identical fixed cylinders filled with the same gas. In the initial state, the distance between the pistons and the cylinder house is l₀, the initial temperature is T₁. Then the gas in both cylinders was heated to a temperature T₂, and the pressure in them increased n times.

Find how much the length of the spring has changed.

6. An additional mass m = 4 g of nitrogen is pumped into a vessel with a volume V = 10 l, filled with air at a pressure p₀ = 1 am and a temperature t₀ = 0 °C, at a temperature of 0 °C. The vessel is closed and heated to a temperature t₁ = 100 °C. Determine the pressure pₓ of the gas mixture in the vessel at this temperature. For nitrogen μ = 28.

7. There are two exact charges: negative - q with mass m and positive + Q with mass M. At what distance d from each other should the charges be located so that in an external uniform electric field with intense E they accelerate as one unit (i.e. without changing their relative positions)?

8. The electrical target consists of batteries with e. d.s. E₁ and E₂, resistances R₁, R₂ and R₃ and an unknown battery Eₓ connected as shown in figure 4. Connected to section AB is a voltmeter V with a large internal resistance. Determine at what value of e. d.s. Eₓ of an unknown battery, the voltmeter readings do not change when key K is closed. Neglect the internal resistance of the batteries.

10. A person looks into a wavy spherical mirror and sees a direct image of his eye. The angular size of this image is N = 1.8 times greater than the angular size of the image that was obtained in a flat mirror placed at the same distance a = 24 cm. Determine the radius of curvature R of the wavy mirror.

11. In air, the wavelength of monochromatic light is λ₀ = 6000 Å. When passing into glass, the wavelength changes and becomes equal to λ₁ = 4200 Å. At what angle φ does light fall on a flat air-glass interface if the reflected and refracted rays form a right angle?
