Laboratory classes in mathematics usually involve working on electronic or keyboard computers. It may very well be that your school does not have such machines, and you don’t know where to look for them.

However, you are not a first-grader who is taught everything by a teacher. If you intend to work in applied mathematics in the future, you need to take the initiative, go to exhibitions where computers are exhibited, or at a research institute or design bureau where these machines are used, and figure out what a computer is. Here, “open days” held in many cities in the spring can help you. These days, schoolchildren - future students - are introduced to the institute.

Why is consultation needed at the institute? Of course, not just for the teacher to give you advice. The teacher knows how to give advice, but do you know how to ask questions? Asking questions is very important to check whether you understand everything correctly, whether there is some kind of trained process - an exam.
