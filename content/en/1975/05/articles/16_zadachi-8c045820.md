## Tasks

1. Ira, Tanya, Kolya and Lenya were picking mushrooms. Tanya collected the most, Ira -
no less than everyone else.
Is it true that girls collected more mushrooms than boys?
2. In a vessel containing 50 g of ice at a temperature
$-10\celsius$, pour 100 g of water, the temperature of which is $+10\celsius$.
What will be the temperature of the water in the vessel? Heat capacity and thermal conductivity
Neglect the vessel.
3. Prove that $1^n+2^n+3^n+4^n$ is divisible by 10 if and only if
$n$ is not divisible by 4.
4. There is a nail with a small heavy head on the table. How to move a horseshoe magnet towards it so that the head and point stick to the magnet
simultaneously?
5. In how many ways can you read a word, moving from letter to letter?
"triangle"? figure shows one of the routes. Can you count
all possible ways?
