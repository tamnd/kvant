XXX years of victory over Hitler's fascism

In May 1945, the Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against Nazi Germany ended victoriously.
At the cost of 20 million lives

We will use here stamps issued for the 25th anniversary of the defeat of the Nazi police near Moscow and the 20th anniversary of the victory of the largest troops at Stalingrad and the Kursk Bulge. Here is a stamp dedicated to one of the largest operations of the final stage of the war—Operation Bagradion, as a result of which Belarus and our troops were liberated above the borders of East Prussia. On the stamp there is an image

With it, our people not only defended the gains of the Great October Revolution, but also liberated the peoples of Europe from fascism.
About three hundred stamps and blocks were issued in our country, telling their story of the Soviet people in the Great Patriotic War. Some of them came out during the war. They were dedicated to the heroic exploits of our warriors, partisans and those who, through selfless labor in the rear, created weapons and supplied the front with everything necessary. However, most of these stamps were issued after the end of the war. They were issued for various anniversaries. We reproduce some of these stamps in the photographs shown here.
At the top you see a stamp with the famous wartime poster by artist I. Tondze “The Motherland Calls”, which recognizes joining the people’s militia.
Hitler's troops suffered major military defeats in battles near Moscow, near Stalingrad and near Orel and Kursk.

monument to women: four pieces, symbolizing the four-feront, as a result of whose joint actions the operation was carried out. In the background is a map depicting the direction of the main attacks of the Soviet troops.
The photo also shows a stamp issued in December 1967, depicting the Monument to the Unknown Soldier - a monument to all palm trees in the Great Patriotic War. On May 8, 1967, on the eve of Victory Day, the Eternal Flame of Glory was lit at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier from a torch delivered from Leningrad from the Field of Mars, where the fighters of the Great October Revolution were buried.

To our readers

The subscription for 1975 to the popular scientific and mathematical journal «Kvant» continues.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics and love solving problems.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet become interested in physics or mathematics, but would like to become better acquainted with these sciences.

Now there will be a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work. In 1975, articles explaining the material of the new programs will be systematically published in the pages of the magazine.

Many materials will be published for 10th grade students preparing to enter college.

In 1975, «Kvant» will continue to print articles for younger students. The scope of these publications will be significantly expanded.

«Kvant» is also useful for sales. The materials published in «Kvant» can be used in mathematics and physics classes and in elective classes.
