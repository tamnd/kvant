FALT - what is it?

INFORMATION

The editors receive many letters asking for information about institutes that train specialists for various fields of science and technology, where it is necessary to function in mathematics and physics.

Many of our readers are interested in the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. In 1972, our magazine published an article that talked about MIPT («Kvant», 1972, No. 6). This institute has seven faculties. Today we are publishing a story about the Faculty of Aeromechanics and Flight Engineering. This material was prepared by Faculty Associate Professor A. L. Stasenko.

The Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute trains scientists in the latest technology and modern physics for the institutes of the USSR Academy of Sciences, research institutes and design bureaus.

Already from the second year of study, MIPT students are gradually included in the work of teams of scientists, and students’ theses, as a rule, are included in the research plan of the corresponding laboratory or institute. It is clear that it is very important to bring the student closer to the base institute, even geographically. Here we will talk about one of the MIPT faculties - the Faculty of Aeromechanics and Flight Technology (FALT), - where this problem has been solved, please, perfectly.

We present the first word to December of the Faculty, Professor L. A. Simonov:

“Our faculty was organized in 1965. A special educational building and a dormitory for students were built for it.

Over the past 25 years, aviation technology has gone from piston to supersonic jet aircraft to rocket technology. Aviation has taken a leading position in passenger and cargo transport, and we can expect its further development.

It is necessary to master flight at speeds exceeding supersonic speeds, up to orbital flight speed, using the air at all altitudes.

Methods for solving scientific problems of aircraft technology can be theoretical and experimental.

A FALT graduate must master methods—both theory and experiment.

FALT provides information at the modern level about aerodynamics, gas dynamics, flight mechanics, strength in relation to aircraft of various types and their power plants.”

And here is what the heads of the leading departments of the institute say.

Head of the Department of Aerodynamics, Professor V.V. Sychev:

“The subject of study of aerodynamics is the movement of liquids and gases. Man has always sought an explanation for the observed patterns in the flow of rivers, beneficial winds and free-flowing seas. But please, only attempts to create heavier-than-air aircraft raised questions about the need for systematic research in this area with the aim of creating a rigorous mathematical theory that explains the occurrence of lift and hydrodynamic drag.

Theoretical research in the field of fluid and gas mechanics is based on many developments in mathematics. Some of them are generally on a “hydrodynamic” basis - these are, for example, vector and tensor analysis, a number of sections of the theory of functions of a complex variable and, especially, methods for studying and solving nonlinear equations with partial arbitrary ones, since only the mechanics of liquids and gases deals with the latter (except for the general theory of relativity).

Head of the Department of Flight Mechanics, Professor, Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences G. S. Byushgens:

“The Department of Flight Mechanics trains three professors to specialize.

1. Aerodynamics of hip aircraft. The task of researching this specialization is to develop problems of aerodynamics of wings and other elements of an aircraft, to work out the aerodynamics of the aircraft as a whole; development

Head of the Department of General Physics of FALT, Professor V. N. Zhigulev:

“Physics at Physics and Technology is the main subject! The unity of physics and technology for modern science and production is very clearly reflected in physics and technology.

Modern aviation and rocket technology uses the latest physical achievements. Therefore, a future specialist, a FALT graduate, must have a wide range of physical knowledge.

In the first year, FALT students study mechanics, which includes issues of special relativity, relativistic dynamics and field theory, molecular physics and thermodynamics, which covers the fundamentals of statistical physics and physical kinetics. In the second year, the courses “Oscillations and Waves” and “Electricity and Magnetism” are studied in parallel. The section “Oscillations and Waves” in the course of general physics is original; physical phenomena and processes that are related in the sense of the physical (oscillatory and wave) form of motion are studied here. The final section, Quantum Physics, is a year-long course covering the second half of the second year and the first half of the third year. Here students study the basic principles of modern atomic and nuclear theory, as well as solid state physics, quantum statistics and radiation physics.

Deputy Head of the Department of Higher Mathematics, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, Associate Professor V. M. Shalov:

“MIPT students study the following compulsory mathematical disciplines: mathematical analysis, analytical geometry, fundamentals of linear algebra, differential equations, equations of mathematical physics, theory of functions of a complex variable, probability theory.

In addition, in the third and fourth years of study, students of all faculties study the basics of computational mathematics and programming. All students undergo practical training on modern computers, study the algorithmic languages ​​ALGOL and FORTRAN.

Over 20 special mathematical courses are also taught for senior and postgraduate students of MIPT, among which the student can choose his own special training based on his scientific interests.

These are the country’s leading scientific centers, and here students of relevant specialties from MIPT attend lectures by prominent scientists and do internships.”
