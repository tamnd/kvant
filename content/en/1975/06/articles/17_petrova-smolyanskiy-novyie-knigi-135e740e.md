New books

In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the second quarter of 1975. Orders for books must be placed through specialized stores or “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Doneddo A. Euclidean planimetry. Volume 15 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 1 rub. 20 k.
The book provides a strict presentation of the planietry course. The system of axioms adopted by the author is quite close to the system of axioms adopted in our high school.
The book will be useful to secondary school teachers, high school students and students of pedagogical institutes and universities.
2. Poya D. Mathematics and plausible reasoning. Volume 34 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 1 rub. 85 k.
This book was written by the Hungarian mathematician and remarkable teacher D. Poyai, widely known in the Soviet Union. Presented in excellent language, it vividly and in an accessible form acquaints the reader with the ways in which new facts are added in mathematics, and how to relate to this or that hypothesis.
The book is of great interest to anyone interested in mathematics, psychological creativity, and history.

3. Postinkov M. M. Stable polynomials. Volume 12 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
The book systematically sets out the basis of the theory of stable polynomials. Using only information known from the high school course, she introduces the reader to the most important ideas of classical algebra and analysis.
The book is intended for secondary school teachers, schoolchildren, students and general mathematics lovers.
4. Kurosh A. G. Allebraic equations of arbitrary stages. Edition 2nd. Volume 2 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 7 kopecks.
This book is based on lectures given by the author at Moscow State University for high school students. A prominent scientist and electrical teacher A. G. Kurosh talks lively and fascinatingly about the general methods of the theory of algebraic equations. The book is useful for all mathematics lovers and especially for high school students.
5. Markushvich A.I. Return of the sequence. Edition 2nd. Volume 3 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.
The author, in a lively and accessible form for schoolchildren, talks about the emergence of sequences (including arithmetic suspetic sequences, etc.).
The author not only sets out the general theory, but also cites interesting historical facts related to its origins. The book is written in good language and will be read with interest by high school students.
6. Gik E. Ya. Mathematics on a chessboard. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The book talks in a lively and entertaining way about the various connections that exist between mathematics and chess:

about mathematical legends dedicated to the origin of chess, about playing machines, about the mathematics of chess tournaments. Much attention is paid to mathematical problems and puzzles associated with the chessboard. All known types of such problems are considered (problems about the board, about routes, about the arrangement and strength of pieces, etc.). The problems “about the move of a knight” and “about eight queens”, which were studied in mathematics by Euler and Gauss, are given.
7. Tumakov I. M. Anis Leon Lebec. Volume 5.5 l., circulation 10,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
The importance of the Lebetz integral for modern mathematics and its applications is very great, but very little is known about the author of this integral, the French mathematician Anis Leon Lebetz, an honorary member of the Multi-Academy of World Sciences and mathematical societies. The book by I. M. Tumakov, prepared for the 100th anniversary of the scientist’s birth, fills this gap. It provides biographical information about Lebec, gives an overview of his scientific works, and shows the origins and significance of Lebec's discovery.

Publishing house "Vyshaya Shkola"
8. Kopenn T. A., Malinov M. P. A manual on mathematics for applicants to technical schools. Volume 18 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 74 kopecks.
This manual is intended for those entering technical schools based on the 8th grade of secondary school. They fully comply with the program of entrance examinations to technical schools.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"
1. Zubov V. G., Shalnov V. P. Problems in physics. Edition 10th. Volume 16 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 55 kopecks.

⟦folio 52⟧

The collection contains assignments in physics in the scope of the high school curriculum. All questions given are provided with solutions or detailed instructions. The assignment will be useful for senior high school students, as well as those preparing to enter universities.

2. Elementary physics textbook. Edited by G. S. Landsberg. Volume I. 9th edition. Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 11 k.

This book has gained wide and lasting fame. Its main advantages are the depth of presentation of physical processes, simplicity of presentation and excellent living language.

The first volume is devoted to the presentation of mechanics, heat and molecular physics. The book is intended for high school students and physics teachers.

3. Geguzin Ya. E. Why and how the emptiness disappears. Volume 12 l., circulation 22,000 copies, price 75 kopecks.

The book tells and fascinates about the history of the development of the physical ideas of the modern theory of sintering - the basis of the technological process of pin metallurgy and the production of refractory materials. It is shown how new ideas in the field of sintering physics were formed in the technology of the interrelation between theory, experiment and technology.

4. Karpov I. I., Lisensky Yu. I. Quarks. Volume 9 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 60 kopecks.

The book is related to one of the interesting problems of modern physics of elementary particles - the quark hypothesis. They have not yet been discovered in elementary parts. But this hypothesis has already turned out to be very useful for science, allowing us to obtain many new conclusions that were confirmed in experiments. In the book, in an accessible form, the main provisions of the quark hypothesis are explained, those physical concepts and ideas are clearly understood, without the knowledge of which it is impossible to imagine its essence.

Publishing house "Mir"

5. Peirsol I. Cavitation. Volume 5 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 44 kopecks.

The book talks about such soaring phenomena of caviation. Using a large amount of practical material, the author shows the harmful and beneficial aspects of this phenomenon. The book is of interest to all physics lovers.

6. Chadd G. Sound. Volume 11 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 89 kopecks.

What is sound, what is its nature, why does sound evoke different assessments - this is an incomplete list of issues raised in this book. The book is written vividly and popularly. It will be read with interest by a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

7. School astronomical calendar for the 1975/76 academic year. Compiled by M. M. Dageev. Volume 6 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 22 kopecks.

The calendar contains basic information about the Sun, Moon, planets, stars, etc., as well as reference data necessary for observing various astronomical phenomena. The book is useful to all astronomy lovers and especially members of astronomical circles.

8. Verzilin N. M., Korsunskaya V. M. V. I. Vernadsky. Volume 7 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 19 kopecks.

This book is a biographical sketch of the life and scientific activities of the prominent Soviet scientist academician V. I. Vernadsky. The book is of interest to high school students.

Publishing house "Atomizat"

9. Vasiliev M.V., Stanyukovich K.P. Into the depths of the inexhaustible. Volume 12 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.

The authors vividly and fascinatingly talk about the evolution of human understanding of the structure of materials and its development. The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

10. Rydnik V.I. Laws of the atomic world. Volume 15 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The author talks in a lively and fascinating manner about the basic concepts of quantum mechanics and the laws of the atomic world he discovered. The book is presented for a wide range of readers.

11. Frank-Kamenetsky D. A. Plasma is a color state of matter. 4th edition. Volume 9 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.

The book was written by a famous Soviet scientist and popularizer of science. The author, in a lively and accessible form, tells the reader about the nature of plasma, the prospects for using thermodynamic energy and many other possible applications of plasma. The book is written for a wide range of readers.

An action-packed tale about the search for art treasures taken by the Nazis during the war from a Polish museum.

6. Strugatsky A., Strugatsky B. Unassigned meetings. Volume 26 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 50 k.

7. Shatalov V., Rebrov M. People and space. Volume 6 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 49 kopecks.

8. Veltistov E. Winner of the impossible. Volume 27.5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 93 kopecks.
