## Tasks

1. A square with a quarter cut out can be easily cut into 4 congruent pieces (see figure). Can you cut a square into 5 congruent pieces if this quarter is not cut out?
2. Two friends, tall and short, diverge at the same speed
from the lantern in different directions. Whose shadow moves faster?
3. When dividing a certain number by 13 and 15, the resulting quotients are the same,
but the first division had a remainder of 8, and the second - without a remainder. Find this number.
4. In Martin Gardner's recent book, Mathematical Novels.
there is such a puzzle. A “glass” is made from four matches, in which lies
cherry (see figure). It is necessary, by moving only two matches, to move the “glass”
so that the cherry is on the outside. According to M. Gardner, many
unsuccessfully racked their brains over this puzzle.
Can you solve this problem? Try also solving this problem for a glass.
5. Once I spent the first Tuesday of the month in Leningrad, and the first Tuesday
after the first Monday - in Riga. Next month I'm first Tuesday
spent in Pskov, and the first Tuesday after the first Monday in Vladimir.
On what date and what month was I in each of these cities?
