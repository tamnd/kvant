Task 6. Naming real solutions of the equation

$$\frac{x^2}{x-1} + \sqrt{x-1} + \frac{\sqrt{x-1}}{x^2} = \frac{x-1}{x^2} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x-1}} + \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{x-1}}.$$

Despite the simple formulation of the problem, its solution raises some difficulties. Trying to get rid of irrational thoughts “at any time” does not lead to anything good. The symmetry of the given equation should be used. His ODZ: $x > 1$. Let $a(x) = x^2, b(x) = x-1, c(x) = \sqrt{x-1}$. then the given equation can be written in the form $\frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{c} + \frac{c}{a} = \frac{b}{a} + \frac{c}{b} + \frac{a}{c}$, from which

$$\frac{(c-b)(b-a)(a-c)}{abc} = 0.$$

Thus, solving the original equation is equivalent to solving three equations: $a(x) = b(x), b(x) = c(x), c(x) = a(x)$, which in this case are easily solved. Answer: $x = 2$.
