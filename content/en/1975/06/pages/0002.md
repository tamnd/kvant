In the above figure you see a schematic section of a neutrino detector installed near a nuclear reactor. In this detector, for the first time, it was possible to register “traces” of the most elusive of all elematic particles - the neutrino. The main elements of the detector are bases (I, II, III) with a liquid scinitillator, equipped with a system of photocells.
The fission of uranium nuclei is accompanied by the appearance of neutrinos and antineutrinos. The capture of an antineutrino by a proton results in the production of several γ quanta, which produce light flashes in the scinitillator.

A system of photocells converts light signals into electrical signals, simultaneously amplifying them.
You can read more about the discovery and properties of neutrinos in the article by B. G. Eroozolimsky (p. 10).
