**3.** Let $u = \log_2(1 - x - x^2)$. Then $u = \dfrac{a}{u} + b$, that is $u^2 - bu - a = 0$. The solvability condition for this quadratic equation is $b^2 + 4a \geqslant 0$ (for all $b > 0$), therefore $a \geqslant 0$. Further, as $x$ changes from $0$ to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, the square trinomial $1 - x - x^2$ monotonically decreases from $1$ to $1/4$, and $u$ decreases from $0$ to $-2$. Consequently, we need to find a condition for the non-negative $a$ under which for all $b > 0$ the equation $u^2 - bu - a = 0$ has a solution in the interval $-2 < u < 0$. We have $u_{1,2} = \dfrac{1}{2}(b \pm \sqrt{b^2+4a})$.

$u_1 = \dfrac{1}{2}(b + \sqrt{b^2+4a})$ is an extraneous solution, since for $a \geqslant 0$ and $b > 0$ the inequality $\dfrac{1}{2}(b + \sqrt{b^2+4a}) \geqslant 0$ is valid.
