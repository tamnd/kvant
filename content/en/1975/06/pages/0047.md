**Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes**

Option 1

1. A cargo ship runs between the two ports, the speed of which linearly depends on the weight of the cargo being transported and with a load of 2000 tons it is 3 times less than with a load of 1000 tons. In addition, it is known that with a load of 2000 tons, the ship travels the distance between ports in 25 hours. How long will it take it to cover this distance with the greatest cargo turnover? What is the ship's load at maximum cargo turnover? (Cargo turnover is the product of the weight of the cargo being transported and the speed of the vessel.)

2. Arrange three numbers in ascending order

$a_1 = \log_{1/2} \sin 2x$, $a_2 = -1 - \log_2 \sin x$,

$$a_3 = \log_{1/2} (1 - \cos 2x),$$

if it is known that $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

3. Find all real $a$ for which, for all $b > 0$, there exists in the interval $0 < x < \dfrac{1}{2}$ a solution to the equation

$$\log_2 (1 - x - x^2) = a\log_{1-x-x^2} 2 + b.$$

4. Given an equilateral triangle with side $a$ and a circle touching one of the sides of the triangle and dividing the second side into two equal parts. In addition, it is known that the center of the circle lies on the third side of the triangle. Find the distance from the center of the circle to the nearest vertex of the triangle.

5. The base of the pyramid is an isosceles triangle, the sides of which are equal to $b$, and the angle at the base is equal to $\alpha\ \left(\alpha > \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$. Each of the lateral edges of the pyramid forms an angle $\beta$ with the plane of the base. Find the area of ​​the section passing through the height of the pyramid and the vertex of angle $\alpha$.

Option 2

1. The speed of a freight train depends linearly on the number of its cars, and it is known that the speed of a train of 60 cars

equal to $\dfrac{2}{3}$ the speed of a train of 40 cars. In addition, it is known that the locomotive crew fulfills the transportation plan 100% with the highest freight turnover. (Freight turnover is the product of the number of cars and the speed of the train.) By what percentage will the locomotive crew fulfill the transportation plan when making a freight train of 40 cars?

2. Arrange three numbers in ascending order

$a_1 = 1 + \log_2 \cos x, \quad a_2 = \log_2 (1 + \cos 2x)$,

$$a_3 = 1 - 2\log_{\frac{1}{2}} \sin 2x,$$

if it is known that $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

3. Find all real $a$ for which, for all $b < 0$, there exists in the interval $4 < x < +\infty$ a solution to the equation

$$a\log_{\left(1 - \frac{2}{x}\right)} 4 = \log_4\left(1 - \frac{2}{x}\right) + b.$$

4. Given an isosceles triangle with base $a$ and a circle with center at one of the vertices of the triangle. It is known that one of the sides of a triangle is divided by a circle into three equal parts. Find the radius of the circle.

5. The base of the pyramid is an isosceles trapezoid, whose sides and smaller base are equal to $a$, and the acute angle is equal to $\alpha\ \left(\alpha > \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$. Each of the lateral edges of the pyramid forms an angle $\beta$ with the plane of the base. Find the cross-sectional area passing through the height of the pyramid perpendicular to the bases of the trapezoid.
