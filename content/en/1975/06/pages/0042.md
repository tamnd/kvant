⟦folio 40⟧

And this despite the fact that the requirements for applicants are not very high. The problems offered in the entrance exams are exactly the same as in school problems. If the inspector has doubts, a higher score is given. And the mathematical preparation of applicants, as evidenced by examiners of all types, is declining from year to year. “Every year we are forced to simplify the tasks for written exams,” says the projector of the Moscow Standard Instrumental Institute. This is also evidenced by a comparison of the options for university entrance assignments.

In technical, economic and many other universities, the entrance exam in mathematics is the main one. It is the mathematics exam that shows the quality of an applicant’s preparation for studying at a university, shows whether a given applicant can successfully study at a given university. Very often it is at the mathematics exam that the question is decided: to be or not to be a student. Why does the math exam also play an important role?

If a student at a chemical university did poorly in chemistry at school, this is, of course, bleak, but it is predictable: he will study the chemistry course at the university “from the very beginning.” If he has poorly cleared trigonometric equations and transformations or makes mistakes in operations with degrees, believing, for example, that $x^{-3} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}x}$, he will not be able to successfully study at an engineering or economics school. Physics and chemistry, theoretical mechanics and strength of materials, probability theory and statistics, as well as other engineering and economic sciences will require clear, fast, automated skills in calculations and transformations. And the university won’t teach all this again. Otherwise, it is fair to say that the university program is built on the mathematical foundation laid in high school.

It’s not so bad if the student doesn’t know the formula for volume parameters: it can be found in any reference book; it is much worse if the student cannot use the correct formula to solve the problem or does not know which formula to apply.

“Matematika is a language,” said the leading American physicist W. Gibbs. This is the language in which the conditions of arbitrary, technical, economic, and military problems are expressed, and solutions to these problems are obtained; it is also an apparatus with the help of which the same chemistry, the same strength of materials, the same economic sciences are applied. However, one of the most important tasks of today's engineer (economics, researcher, scientist) is the formalization of the problems to be solved, their formulation in the language of mathematics.

Further, a hundred years ago, the engineer Cyrus Smith, having found himself on the “Tannsteven Island” (remember Jules Verne!), could apply his mathematical knowledge very widely and not without crawling. And science, technology and economics today are simply unthinkable without the use of mathematics, without the ability to use it correctly, quickly and accurately.

The importance of mathematics and its applications has especially increased in recent decades. Every engineer, economist, researcher is forced to use the help of a computer; engineers and economists cannot do without linear programming and other mathematical methods; Probability theories and mathematical statistics are playing an increasingly important role.

How does a written entrance exam in mathematics differ from a school test, from problems solved in class? Methods of solution? Difficulty of the task? The scope of the program?

Not at all. Neither one nor the other, nor the third.
