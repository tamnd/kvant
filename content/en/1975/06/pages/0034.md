⟦folio 32⟧

To manage an organization that has arisen as a result of specialization, you need a specialist in the field of organization.

Knowledge in the field of functional disciplines, primarily mathematics with its applications, theoretical mechanics and physics, loses its significance over time. The authors of the book “Matematika and Logic” write about this in several categories, but in an expressive form. Retrospective and Prospects” (“World”, 1971) M. Katz and S. Ulam: “In one respect, mathematics stands apart from other sciences: none of its results can be crossed out by the further development of science. However, the proven theorem will never become false, although it may later become clear that it is only a trivial special case of some simpler truth. Mathematical knowledge is not subject to revision, and its total stock can only increase.”

Knowledge of the totality of laws of nature, mathematical constructions, theorems, the ability to think logically, compare and analyze facts based on a dialectical-materialistic worldview - this is an essential part of an engineer’s scientific talent. The resolution of the Central Committee of the CPSU and the Council of Ministers of the USSR “On measures to further improve higher education in the country” precisely directs our higher school towards studying the general laws of nature, acquiring creative knowledge in the field of functional disciplines, and the ability to conduct independent research work. The study of instructions, descriptive disciplines, details of the design of units, technically, etc. is relegated to the background.
