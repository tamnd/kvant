M326. The chord of the circle is removed from the center by a distance $h$. A square is inscribed in each of the segments subtended by a chord so that two adjacent vertices of the square lie on the arc, and the other two on the chord (Fig. 1). What is the difference in the lengths of the sides of these squares?

M327. There are $N$ people in the company. Each of them likes exactly $k$ people from this company. At what minimum $k$ can we say that there will definitely be two people from this company who like each other?

*A. Kolodin, 10th grade student*

M328. A fly and two spiders crawl along a regular tetrahedron. The fly crawls only on the ribs, and the spiders crawl all over the surface. The maximum speed of a fly is 2 times the maximum speed of spiders.

a) Prove that for any initial position the spiders will be able to catch the fly.

b) Is this true if the maximum speed of a fly is more than 2 times the maximum speed of spiders?
