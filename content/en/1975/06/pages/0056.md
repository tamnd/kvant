⟦folio 54⟧

Tasks

1. A square with a cut out “quarter” can be easily cut into 4 congested parts (see figure). Is it possible to cut a square into 5 congested pieces if this quarter is not cut out?

2. Two friends - tall and short, diverge from the lantern in different directions at the same speed. Which one moves faster?

3. When dividing some numbers by 13 and 15, the same partials were obtained, but the first division had a remainder of 8, and the second - without a remainder. Find this number.

4. Martin Gardner's recent book Mathematical Novels contains such a puzzle. A “glass” is made from four matches, in which the cherry lies (see figure). You only need to move two spokes, move the “glass” so that the cherry is on the outside. According to M. Gardner, many have flawlessly racked their brains over this puzzle.

Can you solve this problem? Try also solving this problem for the "rhyomka".

5. Once I spent the first Tuesday in Leningrad, and the first Tuesday after the first Monday in Riga. In the next place I spent the first Tuesday in Pskov, and the first Tuesday after the first Monday in Vladimir. What date and what place was I in each of these cities?
