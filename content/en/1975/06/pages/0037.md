“The spread of mathematics in breadth is accompanied by its penetration in depth; mathematics now occupies a prominent position in the life of society. The traditional view of mathematicians has also changed: the place of Paganel-like eccentrics has been taken in this view by young people in cowboy shorts who go in for skiing. An increasing number of parents want to enroll their children in schools with a mathematical focus.” (V.A. Uspensky, from the preface to the book “Mathematics in the Modern World”).

What is the secret of this fashion? Of course, it is not that the profession of a mathematician is achievable with little effort. On the contrary, it is not possible to identify many specializations that require as much effort and time as mathematical specialization requires. This can be fully understood by those who do mathematics themselves: solve problems, prove statements, make their own large or small contribution to the development of this science. Consequently, the point is different - the real effect that successful applications of mathematical methods bring in solving practical problems and the confidence in the unlimited growth of this effect. He may need some exposure to operations research to make rational decisions in a wide variety of situations. Certain branches of mathematics will undoubtedly take their place in his general education.”

By the way, in connection with operations research (understood as activities subordinated to a single plan and aimed at achieving certain goals), the humorous statement of the famous specialist T. Saaty is not without interest: “Operations research is the art of giving bad answers to those practical questions that are given even worse answers by other methods.” However, there is some truth in every joke: the methods of applied mathematics are often applicable in situations in which other approaches do not give any acceptable results.
