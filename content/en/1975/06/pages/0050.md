If the problem is solved in general form, then it leads to an equation of the third degree, but the given numerical values ​​of the quantities greatly simplify the solution. The difficulty of the task is medium.

Let $u$ be the speed of the river flow (in $м/с$), $v$ be the desired speed of athlete No. 2 (in $м/с$), $s$ be the distance from $A$ to $Б$ (in $м$). The following system of equations follows from the conditions of the problem:

$$\begin{cases} \dfrac{s}{1-u} = \dfrac{s}{v-u} + 300, \\[2mm] \dfrac{s}{2} \\ \dfrac{1-u}{} + \dfrac{s}{2} \\ \dfrac{1+u}{} = 600, \\[2mm] \dfrac{\dfrac{s}{2}}{1-u} + \dfrac{\dfrac{s}{2} - 150}{1+u} = \dfrac{150}{v-u} + 300. \end{cases}$$

Subtracting the 2nd from the 3rd equation, we find

$$v = \frac{u+1}{2u+1} + u.$$

On the other hand, equating the expressions for $s$ found from the 1st and 2nd equations, after simple, albeit lengthy calculations, we obtain that

$$v = \frac{u+2}{2u+1}.$$

From here, excluding $v$, we arrive at the equation
$$2u^2 + u - 1 = 0,$$
which has only one positive

root $u = 1/2$. Therefore $v = 1\dfrac{1}{4}$ m/s.

Problem 4. *There is an inclined plane located at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon. At a distance $s$ from it on the second inclined plane there is a material point $M$. What angle should this plane make with the horizon so that a material point, moving along it without friction, would reach the first plane in the least time? What is this time like? Assume that the line of intersection of inclined planes is horizontal.*

Let $\beta$ be the desired inclination angle of the second plane, $l$ be the length of the path of the material point along this plane (see Fig. 1). Obviously $l = s/\sin(\alpha+\beta)$. Acceleration $g\sin\beta$ acts on the second inclined plane. Therefore, the time of movement of a material point along the second plane is equal to

$$t = \sqrt{\frac{2l}{g\sin\beta}} = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{g\sin\beta\sin(\alpha+\beta)}} =$$

$$= \sqrt{\frac{4s}{g[\cos\alpha - \cos(\alpha+2\beta)]}}.$$

The required minimum time $t_{\min}$ corresponds to case $\cos(\alpha+2\beta) = -1$, whence

$$\beta = \frac{\pi-\alpha}{2}, \quad t_{\min} = \sqrt{\frac{2s}{g\cos^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}}}.$$
