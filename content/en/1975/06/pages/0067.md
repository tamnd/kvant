## Game

## "hexagon"

The top figure depicts 12 game elements - figures, each of which is given a name. Each of them is composed of six equilateral triangles. Such figures are called triangular hexamino. The shapes do not have a "bottom" or "top" side. The hexagon figure does not participate in the game until the end of the game.

The hexagon game can be played by two or more participants.

The first player (by condition or by lot) takes any of the pieces and places it on the table - a piece in the game. The next player, in his next move, takes one of the remaining pieces and places it so that it touches those already lying along a solid line, the length of which is equal to three units of the length of the perimeter of the pieces (the unit of length is the length of the side of the triangle, the component part of the figure). If the player cannot pick up such a piece from those not yet used, then he can take any piece on the table that goes to the boundary of the contour and move it according to the rules to another place (again by touching in a row along three units of perimeter length). When all the pieces are in the game, the next moves involve rearranging the pieces. But the game may end before such a need arises.

The goal of the game is to create a situation after which a move with the hexagon figure is possible. The player who created such a situation gets an extra move with this piece and wins.

The bottom figure shows an example of a game (possibly the shortest).

L. P. Mochalov
