Loviyam: $O$ is removed from the lines $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$ by the same distance equal to $OD$ and lies outside $\triangle ABC$; $D$ lies on the segment $BC$.

S. The task “Construct $\triangle ABC$ according to bissertik themes” is formulated as follows.

A straight line $l$ is drawn, points $O$, $D$, $E$ and $F$ are offset on it. Construct the points $A$, $B$ and $C$ so that at $\triangle ABC$ the bissertis $b_A$, $b_B$ and $b_C$ equal the correspondence of $OD$, $OE$ and $OF$.

5. About “arbitrary points”

If you were able to construct a straight line $p$ from Exercise 1 with a compass and ruler, then it means that you did not read point 3 carefully: none of the elemental constructions can be applied to the initial configuration $\{l, A\}$, since $\alpha$ and $\beta$ require that at least two different points be offset in the configuration, and $\gamma$ - that at least two different lines.

In order to be able to start working, we will require that at least two different points are always offset in the initial configuration. After this, exercise 1 is reformulated as follows.

U razzhene 2. Let a straight line $l$ be drawn on the plane and a point $A$ offset on it. In addition, let another point $B$ (different from $A$) be offset on the plane. Construct a line $p$ passing through $A$ and perpendicular to $l$.

Y razzhene 3. Let at least two different points be offset in the initial configuration. Prove that it is then possible to repeatedly use the elemental constructions $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ - mark the point,

a) started belonging to some of the drawn lines - or even to any fixed segment (or arcs) of this line,

b) started not belonging to any of the lines of the original configuration,

c) it belongs (or does not belong) to any figure formed by drawn lines (circle, triangle, etc.).
