The directory contains admission rules and entrance exam programs for all types of the Soviet Union in 1975.

Science fiction literature

Publishing house "Mir"
1. Human-computer. Sat. science fiction works. Volume 16 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 12 k.
The collection is based on the novel “Computer Man” by the American science fiction writer M. Crichton. The collection also includes fantastic works by English, Hungarian and other authors on the topic “Medicine and Science Fiction”.

Publishing house "Young Guard"
2. Glazov G. Forced descriptive. Volume 14.5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.
An action-packed tale about the search for art treasures taken by the Nazis during the war from a Polish museum.

Publishing House "Children's Literature"
3. Davydov Yu. Deep leaf fall season. Volume 33 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 60 k.
The author talks in a poignant and entertaining way about the last days of Narodnaya Volya.
4. Faibyshenko Yu. The decisive hour. Volume 13 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 78 kopecks.
An adventure story about geologists who entered into a fight with representatives who were trying to drag the search group into a performance against the Motherland.

5. Vitkovic V. Rated ball. Volume 11 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 45 kopecks.
This book is about the interactions between man and nature, about the protection of nature and the environment.
6. Strugatsky A., Strugatsky B. Unassigned meetings. Volume 26 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 50 k.
The new book by famous Soviet science fiction writers will include three stories: “A Case of Murder,” “The Kid,” and “Roadside Picnic.” All three stories are based on one common theme - contact with introduced civilizations.
7. Shatalov V., Rebrov M. People and space. Volume 6 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 49 kopecks.
In this book, USSR pilot-cosmonaut V. Shatalov and journalist M. Rebrov talk about the joint Soviet-American experiment “Soyuz - Apollo”.
8. Veltistov E. Winner of the impossible. Volume 27.5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 93 kopecks.
A story about the adventure of an Electroponics Mike, an Electroponics Dog and their friends.
9. Podolny R. Ways of peoples. Volume 14 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 60 kopecks.
The book is devoted to the problems of the emergence and formation of peoples on our planet.
