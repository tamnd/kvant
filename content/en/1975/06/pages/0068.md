On this figure you see twenty-three circles. Each of them is defined by points of tangency either with reference circles and lines (white on figure) or with each other. The centers of the support circles lie on the vertical diameter of the largest of them. The intersections of support circles with this diameter are either edges

its point, or its middle, or the point dividing it in the ratio 1:3.
Equally colored circles seem congruent, don't they? But maybe our eyesight is deceiving us? The difference between them cannot be determined by measurement. Is it possible to prove that the figures are congruent? This is discussed on p. 21.
