On the first page of the cover you see one of the basic formulas of Moscow University. If you ask what shape the thermometer cabinet has, then you, of course, will answer - the shape of a circle. However, in the formula you see not a circle at all, but some kind of circular ellipse. You can read about this curve and some of its properties in the article by I. N. Bronstein on p. 2-8.
