upstream the river is $x - v$, downstream the river is $x + v$. Let us denote the distance between points $A$ and $B$ by $S$. From the conditions of the problem we obtain a system of equations

$$\begin{cases}(x-v)\,a + v\cdot a = S,\\[2mm] \dfrac{S}{x-v} + \dfrac{S}{x+v} = \dfrac{S}{v}\,.\end{cases}$$

Искомое время равно $\dfrac{S}{v}$. The first equation of the system is transformed to the form $ax = S$, and the second equation of the system can be reduced to a quadratic equation with respect to the relation $\dfrac{x}{v}$:

$$\left(\frac{x}{v}\right)^2 - 2\left(\frac{x}{v}\right) - 1 = 0,$$

where

$$\left(\frac{x}{v}\right)_1 = 1 + \sqrt{2}\,, \quad \left(\frac{x}{v}\right)_2 = 1 - \sqrt{2}\,.$$

According to the conditions of the problem, $x > v > 0$, therefore the second solution does not satisfy the conditions of the problem. Now

$$\frac{S}{v} = \frac{ax}{v} = a\left(1 + \sqrt{2}\right).$$
