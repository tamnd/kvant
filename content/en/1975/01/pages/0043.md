**M304.** We will denote by a circle some (yet unknown) operation applicable to any two non-negative integers $a$ and $b$ and resulting in also a non-negative integer $a \circ b = c$. Let the operation “$\circ$” satisfy the following conditions:

1) $a \circ b = b \circ a$;

2) if $a \circ b = c$, then $b \circ c = a$;

3) if $a \circ b > c$, then $b \circ c < a$ or $a \circ c < b$.

a) Find $0 \circ 0$; $0 \circ 1$; $1 \circ 1$; $0 \circ 2$.

b) Prove that $0 \circ a = a$ and

$$1 \circ a = \begin{cases} a+1, \text{ если } a \text{ четно},\\ a-1, \text{ если } a \text{ нечетно}.\end{cases}$$

c) \* Prove that there is at most one operation that satisfies the conditions of the problem.

d) \* Prove that such an operation exists and indicate a rule that allows you to calculate $a \circ b$ given $a$ and $b$.

*A. A. Grigoryan*
