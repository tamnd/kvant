## Tasks

1. The old pirate, dying, bequeathed to the heir to find the treasure buried on the island along three landmarks - the chapel, the oak and the elm - as follows: first, go from the chapel to the oak and from it to the right at a right angle for the same distance, stick a stick in this place; then walk from the chapel to the elm tree and from it to the left at a right angle for the same distance, stick another stick in this place, in the middle of the segment connecting the sticks, a treasure is buried.

Arriving on the island, the heir saw that the oak and elm were in place, and not a trace remained of the chapel. Help him find the treasure.

2. When *order* was multiplied by 99,999, they got a number whose last three digits were 705. What number is represented by the word “order”? (Identical letters replace identical numbers.)

3. 100 chips are in a row. Any two chips that are next to each other can be swapped. Will you be able to place the chips in reverse order?

4. Baron Munchausen talks about the following “true incident” that happened to him.

He took a running start to jump across the swamp. During the jump, he noticed that he would not make it to the opposite bank. Then, right in the air, he turned back and returned to the shore from which he jumped. Why is this not possible?

5. The bully Vasya sawed off two opposite corner squares of size $3 \times 3$ cells from the chessboard. Is it possible to go around the remainder with a knight and return to the original square?
