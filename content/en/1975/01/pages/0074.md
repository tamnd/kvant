Since then, seven issues of this international yearbook have been published. And each of them contains several interesting articles about the prospects for the development of various fields of physics, astronomy, and cybernetics.

None of the physics lovers will deny themselves the pleasure of reading articles on such topics as: “Can physical science end?” (author - Professor A. S. Kompaneets, issue 2), “Macromicrosymmetric Universe” (academician M. A. Markov, issue 6), “Physics of the 20th century” (W. Weiskopf (USA), issue 4), “Birth of elementary particles” (academician Ya. B. Zeldovich, issue 5), “Ways of thermonuclear research” (academician L. A. Artsimovich, issue 6).

Every astronomy lover will want to quickly read the articles “Flights to Asteroids” (X. Alfven (Sweden), issue 6), “The Present and Future of Astrometry (Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences M. S. Zverev, issue 6), “Energy Problems of Astrophysics” (Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences V. V. Sobolev, issue 6), “The Problem of Prestellar Matter” (Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences L.V. Mirzoyan, issue 4), “Pulsars” (Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences I.S. Shklovsky, issue 4), “Quasars” (V. Zonn (Poland), issue 5).

Published issues talk about future charged particle accelerators (articles by academicians A.L. Mints, M.A. Markov and J. Hamilton (USA)), about unsolved problems of elementary particle physics (articles by J. Chu (USA), A. Salam (Pakistan)), on the prospects for the development of quantum electronics (articles by academicians N.G. Basov, A.M. Prokhorov, academician of the Academy of Sciences of the BSSR B.I. Stepanov), about the problems of solid state physics (article by academician I.M. Lifshits). Problems of communication between humans and computers are discussed in the article by Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences N. P. Buslenko.

Only the mathematics in these yearbooks has not been very lucky so far. It is represented by only two articles - an article by Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B.V. Gnedenko “On the Future of Applied Mathematics” and an article by Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the BSSR N.P. Erugin “Mathematics - a barometer of civilization.”

The editorial board of the yearbook strictly adheres to the principles proclaimed in the preface to the first issue. The topics of the articles are relevant, and their authors are well-known Soviet and foreign scientists who have made a great contribution to the development of the areas of science under consideration.

Young people are actively interested in unsolved problems of science. And this is natural. After all, it is those who are still sitting at their school desks today who will have to solve many of these problems in the future. But only a little can be learned about these problems from school textbooks. We have to turn to popular science and science fiction literature. Unfortunately, these books, brochures, and articles often contain “problems” and “hypotheses” that have nothing to do with science. The Future of Science annuals are free from this shortcoming. Everything they talk about has a strict scientific basis, excites and occupies the attention of modern scientists.

The collections “The Future of Science” are written popularly. Most of the articles included are accessible to high school students. And we readily recommend them to our young readers.
