**Physics**

**1.** A frame with an area of ​​$S = 1$ *dm²* made of wire with a resistance of $R = 0{,}45$ *ohm* rotates with an angular velocity of $\omega = 100$ *rad/s* in a uniform magnetic field with induction $B = 0{,}1$ *tl*. The axis of rotation of the frame lies in its plane and is perpendicular to the magnetic induction vector **B**. Find the amount of heat $Q$ that will be released in the frame in $N = 10^3$ revolutions. Neglect self-induction.

**2.** Mounted on the cart is an isotropically emitting point light source and a lens in a frame attached to one end of a spring with a stiffness of $k = 2$ *n/m* (Fig. 1). The other end of the spring is attached to a cart under the light source. A frame with a lens of total mass $m = 200$ *g* can move without friction along a cart moving perpendicular to a vertical wall. Determine the acceleration **a** with which the cart must move so that the beam of light emerging from the lens creates a central bright spot on the screen, the illumination of which does not change while the cart is moving. The light source is located on the main optical axis of the lens with a focal length of $F = 8$ *cm*. The length of the unstretched spring is $l_0 = 9$ *cm*.

**4.** A charged ball of mass $m = 10$ *g*, suspended on an insulating inextensible thread, moves with a constant angular velocity $\omega = 10$ *rad/s* along a circle of radius $r = 5$ *cm* (Fig. 2). Under the suspension point $A$ there is another, stationary charged ball $B$, and the distances $AO$ and $BO$ to the center of the circle $O$ are the same, and the angle $\alpha = 45^\circ$. The charges on both balls are the same. Find their value $q$.

**5.** The oscillatory circuit consists of an inductor and a capacitor, which consists of three metal plates with an area of ​​$S = 10$ *cm²* each, separated by a dielectric with a thickness of $d = 1$ *mm* with a dielectric constant of $\varepsilon = 4$. The capacitor plates are connected to the circuit as shown in figure 3. At what value of inductance of the coil $L$ will the circuit be tuned to the wavelength $\lambda = 2{,}5$ *km*?

**6.** There is a gas in a vertical cylindrical vessel with smooth walls under a piston with mass $m = 10$ *kg* and cross-section $S = 50$ *cm²*. When a vessel moves vertically with an acceleration of $a = 1$ *m/s²*, the height of the gas column under the piston decreases by $\eta = 5\%$ compared to the height in the vessel at rest. Assuming the gas temperature in the vessel remains constant, determine the external pressure $p_0$. The piston is sealed against the walls of the vessel.
