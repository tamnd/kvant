## Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

We talked in detail about the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Engineering Physics Institute in «Kvant» No. 1 for 1973.
Below are options for the written exam in mathematics and problems for the oral exam in physics in 1974. The content of theoretical questions in physics fully corresponds to the program of entrance exams for applicants to universities.

Mathematics

Option 1

**1.** From point $A$ a motor boat set off up the Volga, and from point $B$ a raft left at the same time downstream. After $a$ hours they met and then moved without stopping. Having reached point $B$, the boat, without stopping, turned back and caught up with the raft at point $A$. It is assumed that the boat's own speed was constant all the time. How long did the raft and boat float?

**2.** The radius of the base of the cone is equal to $r$, and the generatrix is ​​inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\varphi$; A pyramid is described near this cone, having at its base a right-angled triangle with an acute angle $\alpha$. Determine the volume and lateral surface of the pyramid.

**3.** Solve inequality

$$\log_{\frac{4}{3}}\left(\sqrt{x+3}-x\right)>0.$$

**4.** Solve the equation

$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 x + \sin 2x + a = 0.$$

Option 2

**1.** A piece of alloy weighing 6 *kg* contains copper. A piece of another alloy weighing 8 *kg* contains half as much copper as a percentage of a piece of the first alloy. A certain part was separated from the first piece, and a part twice as large in mass as from the first piece was separated from the second piece. Each of the separated parts was fused with the remainder of the other piece, after which two new alloys were obtained with the same percentage of copper. What is the mass of each of the parts separated from the pieces of the original alloys?

**2.** Given a circle of radius $R$. Two radii $OA$ and $OB$ are drawn from its center,

forming the angle $\alpha$. An equilateral triangle is inscribed in the smaller segment of the circle cut off by the chord $AB$, one of the sides of which is perpendicular to the chord $AB$. Find the side of this triangle.

**3.** Solve inequality

$$\log_{\frac{1}{3}}(x-1) + \log_{\frac{1}{3}}(x+1) + {} \\ {} + \log_{\sqrt{3}}(5-x) < 1.$$

**4.** Solve the equation

$$\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(1-\sin x\cos x\right).$$

Option 3

**1.** A pedestrian and a cyclist depart from point $A$ to point $B$ at the same time. Having reached $B$, the cyclist turns back and meets a pedestrian 1 hour after the start of the movement. After the meeting, the pedestrian continues to go to $B$, and the cyclist turns and also goes to $B$. Having reached $B$, the cyclist turns back again and meets the pedestrian 40 minutes after the first meeting. Determine how long it will take the pedestrian to cover the distance from $A$ to $B$.

**2.** Calculate the volume of a regular triangular pyramid if the plane angle at the vertex is $\alpha$, and the radius of the circle described around the side face is $r$.

**3.** Solve the equation

$$\log_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x}}} 10\cdot\log_{10}(x^2-3x+2) = \\ = -2 + \log_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x}}} 10\cdot\log_{10}(x-3).$$

**4.** Solve the equation $\sin^2 x = a^2\sin^2 3x$ $(a>0)$.
