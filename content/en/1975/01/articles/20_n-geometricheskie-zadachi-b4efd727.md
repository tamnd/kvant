## Geometric problems

1. The altitudes $AK$, $BL$ and $CM$ of the acute triangle $ABC$ are extended to the intersection with the circumcircle at points $D$, $E$, $F$ respectively. Let $P$, $Q$, $R$ be points on the circle such that $\smile AP = \frac{1}{3} \smile ABD$, $\smile BQ = \frac{1}{3} \smile BCE$, $\smile CR = \frac{1}{3} \smile CAF$, and all arcs have the same direction. Prove that $\smile AR = \frac{1}{3} \smile ACD$, $\smile CQ = {}^1\!/\!_3 \smile CBF$, $\smile BP = {}^1\!/\!_3 \smile BAE$, and the triangle $PQR$ is equilateral.

2. Given a trapezoid $ABCD$ with parallel sides $AB$ and $CD$, $AB < CD$. Using a compass and ruler, construct a point $H$ on the side of the trapezoid such that a line $HK$, parallel to the base of the trapezoid, divides it into parts whose areas are in the ratio $m : n$.

3. Let $O$ be the center of the circle circumscribed around the triangle $ABC$, $R$ its radius, $H$ the point of intersection of the altitudes of the triangle $ABC$ and $U$, $V$, $W$ the points of intersection of the circumscribed circle with the lines $AH$, $BH$ and $CH$. Prove that the lines drawn through the points $U$, $V$, $W$ parallel to the lines $OA$, $OB$ and $OC$, respectively, intersect at one point $P$. Prove also that if $H'$ is a point on the extension of the segment $OH$ such that $OH = HH'$, then $PH' = \dfrac{OH^2}{R^2}$.

4. Construct a triangle inscribed in a circle, knowing one of its vertices $A$ and the point $H$ of intersection of the altitudes of the triangle.

5. Given a circle, a straight line $AB$ and a point $X$ on a line. Construct a circle tangent to line $AB$ at point $X$, so that the common tangent of the given and drawn circles has a given length $l$.
