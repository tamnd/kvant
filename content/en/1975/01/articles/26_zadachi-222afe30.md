## Tasks

1. The old pirate, dying, bequeathed to his heir to find buried on the island
treasure according to three landmarks - the chapel, the oak and the elm - as follows: first
walk from the chapel to the oak tree and from it to the right at a right angle the same distance, stick a stick in this place; then walk from the chapel to the elm and from it to the left at a right angle the same distance, stick it in
there is another stick, in the middle of the segment connecting the sticks, a treasure is buried.
Having sailed to the island, the heir saw that the oak and elm were in place, and from the chapel
not a trace remained. Help him find the treasure.
2. When $\overline{\text{заказ}}$ was multiplied by $99\,999$, we got the number
the last three digits of which were 705. What number is indicated by the word
"order"? (Identical letters replace identical numbers.)
3. 100 chips are in a row. Any two chips that are next to each other can be changed
in some places. Will you be able to place the chips in reverse order?
4. Baron Munchausen talks about the following “true incident”,
happened to him.
He took a running start to jump across the swamp. While jumping, he noticed
that it will not jump to the opposite shore. Then, right in the air, he turned back and returned to the shore from which he jumped. Why is this not possible?
5. Hooligan Vasya sawed off two opposite corners from the chessboard
square of size $3\times3$ cells. Is it possible to go around the remainder with a knight and return to the original field?
