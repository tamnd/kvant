Exercises

1. An acute triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ is circumscribed about an acute triangle $A_2B_2C_2$, the sides of which are tangent to the circle circumscribed about triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ at its vertices. Near the triangle $A_2B_2C_2$ the acute-angled triangle $A_3B_3C_3$ is described in a similar way, and so on, up to and including the triangle $A_{n+1}B_{n+1}C_{n+1}$. Find the angles of triangle $A_{n+1}\,B_{n+1}\,C_{n+1}$.

2. A triangle $A_2B_2C_2$ is described near the triangle $A_1B_1C_1$, the vertices of which coincide with the centers of the excircles in the triangle $A_1B_1C_1$. Around the triangle $A_2B_2C_2$ the triangle $A_3B_3C_3$ is described in a similar way and so on. Find the angles of triangle $A_{n+1}B_{n+1}C_{n+1}$. Prove that triangle $A_{n+1}B_{n+1}C_{n+1}$, as $n$ increases indefinitely, tends to an equilateral triangle.

3. The bisectors of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ are extended until they intersect with the circumcircle of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ at points $A_2$, $B_2$, $C_2$, respectively. The bisectors of triangle $A_2B_2C_2$ intersect the circumcircle at points $A_3$, $B_3$, $C_3$, and so on, respectively. Find the angles of triangle $A_{n+1}B_{n+1}C_{n+1}$.

4. Triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ is inscribed with triangle $A_2B_2C_2$, formed by the projections of the center of the inscribed circle onto its sides. Triangle $A_2B_2C_2$ is inscribed in the same way into triangle $A_3B_3C_3$ and so on. Find the angles of triangle $A_{n+1}B_{n+1}C_{n+1}$.

## Tasks

1. Solve equation $x^4 - 3x^3 + 3 = 0$.

2. Show that for any real $p$ such that $|p| \leqslant \sqrt[4]{4q}$, Eq.

$$x^4 + px^3 + q = 0, \quad q > 0$$

has no real solutions.

3. Show that for any real $p$ such that $|p| \leqslant \sqrt[4]{12}$ the equation

$$x^4 - px^3 + 3 = 0$$

has no real solutions.

4. Find real solutions of the system $x^3y = -4$, $x - y = 1{,}5$.

*G. Yu. Zaitsev*
