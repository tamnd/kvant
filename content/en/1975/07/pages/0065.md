Izhevsk Mechanical Institute

The Izhevsk Mechanical Institute has five full-time faculties and trains engineers in the following specialties.

Faculty of Mechanics and Technology: technical mechanical engineering; metalworking machines and tools; machines and technological processing of metals by pressure; equipment and technological welding production.

Instrument-making faculty: electronic computers; design and production of radio equipment.

Faculty of Mechanical Engineering: automatic and semi-automatic machines; hardware

Faculty of Engineering and Economics: economics and organization of the mechanical engineering industry; organization of mechanized processing of economic information.

Faculty of Civil Engineering: Industrial and Civil Engineering; agricultural construction.

Below are the options for the 1974 entrance written exam in mathematics to our institute.

Option 1

1. The base of a straight line is an equilateral triangle, the base of which is $a$, and the angle at the base is $\alpha$. Determine the volume of a straight line if its lateral surface is equal to the sum of the areas of its bases.

2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{4}(7+1g x) = 10^1+1g x.$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{x-7}{\sqrt{4 x^2-19 x+12}} < 0.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$tg 2x+ctg x=8 \cos^2 x.$$

Option 2

1. A cone is inscribed in a ball of radius $R$, the image of which is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\alpha$. Find the volume of the cone.

2. Solve the equation
$$2\sqrt{x+1}-\frac{4}{\sqrt{x+1}}=\sqrt{2 x-2}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sec x-1=2 \sin \frac{x}{2}.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$\log_0,3(x^2+2x)<0.$$

Option 3

1. The base of a right parallelepidea is a rectangle inscribed in a circle of radius $R$, and one of the sides of the base rests on an arc equal to $2\alpha$. Find the volume of this parallelepidea if its lateral surface is equal to $S$.

2. Solve the equation
$$lg^2(100x)+lg^2(10x)+lg^2x=14.$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{1-x}{\sqrt{0,5^2x}}-\sqrt{0,25^x-6}<0,$$

4. Solve the equation
$$1-\cos^2 2x=\sin 3x-\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+x\right).$$
