According to the definition, a circle should also be considered a node - the simplest of all nodes, or, as it is also called, a trivial node.

Looking at the top knot on figure 1, you can see that it can be easily untied, unless its loops cover the parts of the base of the ship.

Exercise 1. Show that the top knot at figure 2, 6 can be untangled and turned into a circle.

Knots that, like the top one, can be untied are called ties of the trivial world, or simply trivial.

Extensive experience in the art of “weaving” trivial (but often very intricate) knots is gained by fans of the popular game with a rope stake. One of these knots called “Jacob’s Ladder” is depicted on figure 5*).
