Mathematical specialties in technical

Secondary specialized educational institutions are an integral part of the public education system of the USSR. The system of secondary specialized education provides training in more than 450 specialties. This makes it possible for the younger generation to receive, along with general education at the level of secondary school, the necessary professional development. It is necessary to introduce scientific principles of training and education, to fully develop a creative attitude to the acquisition of knowledge, and the skills of independent organization of organizational and social-political work. Graduates of secondary specialized educational institutions must navigate the fundamental changes occurring in the field of technical and technological knowledge, deep knowledge of economics and organizational professional structure, and competently service new equipment and complex automated systems. “Soviet specialty,” said the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee, Comrade.
