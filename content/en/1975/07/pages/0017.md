§ 8. Arithmetic of knots

If two knots are tied side by side on the same rope, then the resulting knot is called the product or composition of these two knots.

The operation of node composition is very similar to the operation of multiplying integers.

1. The role of the “unit” in the “multiplication” of two knots is obviously played by the trupal knot—the circle. Denoting it by the letter E, and the composition of two nodes A and B by A # B, we obtain A = E # A = A # E (see Fig. 14, a).

2. Composition is a commutative operation:

A # B = B # A.

This is easy to prove by running one knot “through” another along the rope (see, for example, Fig. 14, b-e).
