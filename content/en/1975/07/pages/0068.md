⟦folio 66⟧

Quests

1. In the room in which the squad council met, there were chairs with 4 legs and stools with 3 legs. When all the puners were seated, there were no empty seats left, and the sum of the prickly legs of those sitting and the legs of the seats turned out to be equal to 39.
How many chairs and how many stools were there in the room?

2. The scuba diver lost his orientation underwater. How can he tell which way is up and which way is down?

3. Moscow schoolchildren Nikolai and Vladimir met at Vladimir's. Nikolai said: “If you subtract from the two-digit number of this house the number formed after rearranging its digits, you will get the number of that house. What house do I live in?
Vladimir said: “This is an easy problem,” and immediately solved it.
What kind of houses did the schoolchildren live in?

4. In order for the cut hay to dry, it is often turned. Why does this help?

5. How do you put “+” signs between the digits of 987654321 to make the total 99?
How many solutions does this problem have?
