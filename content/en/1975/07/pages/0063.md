Lviv Polytechnic Institute
Lviv Order of Lenin Polytechnic Institute is one of the oldest higher technical educational institutions in the country; it was founded in 1844 as a technical Academy, which in 1877 was changed into the Higher Polytechnic Scale, and in 1921 into the Lviv Polytechnic.
Rapid development began in September 1939, after the restoration of the western regions of Ukraine into a single Ukrainian Soviet Socialist State, when the Lviv Polytechnic was reorganized into a polytechnic institute.
