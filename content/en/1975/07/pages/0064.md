Below are examples of options for written exams in mathematics and physics at the Lviv Polytechnic Institute in 1974.

**Mathematics**

**Option 1**

1. A triangle ABC, in which the angle at vertex A is α and the angle at vertex B is β, is expressed by the vector AB equal to c. Find the volume of the body of revolution.

2. Three numbers form a geometric progression. If the second number is increased by 2, the progression becomes arithmetic, and if after that the last number is increased by 9, the progression again becomes geometric. Find these numbers.

3. Solve equation $\cos^2 x - (\sin 35^\circ + \cos 35^\circ) \cos x + \frac{1}{2} \sin 70^\circ = 0$.

4. Solve equation $\log_2 x - \log_4 x = \log_2 x + 16$.

**Option 2**

1. The example base is a trapezoid, the upper base and sides of which are equal to each other, the lower base is equal to α and the type angle is equal to α. All side ribs form an angle β with the plane of the base. Find volume examples.

2. A motor boat covers a distance of 60 km between two points there and back without stopping in 6 hours 15 minutes. Find the speed of the river current if the boat's own speed is 20 km/h.

3. Solve equation $\cos 2x \cos x = \cos 5x \cos 4x$.

4. Solve inequality $\lg \frac{x+1}{2x-3} \geq 0$.

**Option 3**

1. The base of the example is a right triangle, one of the acute angles of which is equal to α, and the radius of the inscribed circle is equal to r. Each of the side faces forms an angle α with the plane of the base. Find volume examples.

2. In 3.5 hours of work, one die press can produce 42% of all ordered parts. The second press can produce 60% of all parts in 9 hours of work, and the speed of work on the third press is related to the speed of work on the second as 6:5. How long will it take to complete the entire order if all three presses work simultaneously?

3. Solve equation $-\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = \cos \frac{x}{2}$.

4. Solve inequality $\frac{1}{5 - \lg x} + \frac{2}{1 + \lg x} < 1$.

**Physics**

**Option 1**

1. Specific heat of vaporization.

2. Electrical capacity. Electrical capacity of the sphere. Capacitors.

3. The juggler threw the ball vertically upward. When the ball reached the top of its path, a second ball was thrown with the same initial speed. At what height h did the ball meet if the throwing height is H=4.9 m?

4. What is the thickness H of a plane-parallel glass plate if a point marked with ink on the bottom side of the plate is observed at a distance h = 5 cm from the top surface? The line of vision is perpendicular to the surface of the plate. Stack guess score n=1.6. For small angles tan α≈sin α≈α.

**Option 2**

1. Free fall of bodies. Acceleration of free fall of a body.

2. Brownian motion.

3. A small ball carrying a charge q₀=10 units is suspended on a thin silk thread by the inter-drive plates of the capacitor, suspended in the air. SGSE charge. What amount of charge must be imparted by the capacitor plates in order for the ball to deviate from the vertical at an angle α=45°? The mass of the ball is m=4 g, the area of ​​each capacitor plate is S=314 cm².
