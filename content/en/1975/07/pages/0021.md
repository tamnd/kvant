Fig. 17. How spheres with handles are bent.

From exercises 12 and 13 it follows that the genus of any node is an integer.

Having eliminated the ambiguities in the definition of the genus of a node, we now prove that the genus of the composition of two nodes is equal to the sum of the genus of the components.

It is not difficult to prove the inequality P (A ≠ B) ≤ P (A) + P (B): take two spheres with 2P (A) and 2P (B) handles and the nodes A and B located on them are “cut”, and connect the spheres into a “dumbbell” with 2 (P(A) + P(B)) handles so that the nodes form the composition A # B (see, for example, Fig. 17). Obviously, knot A#B cuts the "dumbbell".
