The photo you see on the front page of the cover was taken at the Space Research Institute of the USSR Academy of Sciences. Vladimir Syromatnikov, head of the Soviet working group (right) and Ray Larson, vice president of Rockwell International, discuss the results of the latest docking of flight samples of docking units.

Photo by A. Mokletsov APN.

It was not by chance that the boa constrictor appeared on our cover. He ended up here, “tied up” in a knot. Mathematicians deal with knots; Moreover, there is a beautiful mathematical theory - the theory of knots. You can get acquainted with this theory by reading the article by E. G. Belaga “A knot on a mathematician’s table”, published in this issue of the magazine (see p. 6). There you will meet our “boa constrictor” again, but this time “without a head” and without a “tail”.
