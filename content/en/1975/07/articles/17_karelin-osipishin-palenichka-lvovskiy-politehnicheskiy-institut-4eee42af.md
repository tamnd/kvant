Lviv Polytechnic Institute
Lviv Order of Lenin Polytechnic Institute is one of the oldest higher technical educational institutions in the country; it was founded in 1844 as a technical Academy, which in 1877 was changed into the Higher Polytechnic Scale, and in 1921 into the Lviv Polytechnic.
Rapid development began in September 1939, after the restoration of the western regions of Ukraine into a single Ukrainian Soviet Socialist State, when the Lviv Polytechnic was reorganized into a polytechnic institute.
Now the institute has the following permanent faculties: energy, electromechanical, radio engineering, automation, electrophysical, thermal engineering, mechanical and technological, mechanical and mechanical engineering, engineering

The Faculty of Automation trains specialties in applied mathematics with the qualification of “engineer-mathematician”. Of the 50 disciplines that students study in this specialty, more than half are mathematical computer programming, mathematical logic, numerical methods and algorithms, linear algebra and analytical geometry, general algebra, mathematical analysis, differential equations, graph theory and combinatorika, functional analysis, equations of mathematical physics, random processes, probability theory and mathematical statistics and much more.

Graduates of this specialty are sent to work in design bureaus, computer centers, and research institutes.
