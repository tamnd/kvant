Mathematical specialties in technical

Secondary specialized educational institutions are an integral part of the public education system of the USSR. The system of secondary specialized education provides training in more than 450 specialties. This makes it possible for the younger generation to receive, along with general education at the level of secondary school, the necessary professional development. It is necessary to introduce scientific principles of training and education, to fully develop a creative attitude to the acquisition of knowledge, and the skills of independent organization of organizational and social-political work. Graduates of secondary specialized educational institutions must navigate the fundamental changes occurring in the field of technical and technological knowledge, deep knowledge of economics and organizational professional structure, and competently service new equipment and complex automated systems. “Soviet specialty,” said the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee, Comrade. L.I. Brezhnev is a person who has mastered the fundamentals of Marxist-Leninist teaching, clearly sees the political goals of the party and the country, has broad scientific and practical training, and is fluent in his specialty.” *)

A large influx of mathematics practitioners with secondary technical education has led to the fact that mathematics began to be studied in more depth and in a broader way, with a variety of applied aspects, in technical fields. New specialties have been introduced and introduced. These involve in-depth study of mathematics and physics.

Specialty No. 1750 - “applied mathematics”. This specialty was discussed in detail in «Kvant», 1974, No. 6. Let us recall that the program in mathematics (algebra and basic analysis, geometry), which in general mathematical training in secondary specialized educational institutions provides for 350-400 hours, here is designed for 540 hours; the “mathematical analysis” part has been expanded, highlighting

We are included in the independent course “Linear Algebra and Analytic Geometry”. In addition, there are many other mathematical subjects in this specialty. The course of study lasts 2 years 10 months for those who have completed 8th grade and 1 year 10 months for those who have completed 10th grade. Those who graduate from a technical school in this specialty receive the qualification “computer-mathematician”.

Specialty No. 1735 - “programming for high-speed mathematical machines.” The training program (3 years 6 months for those who have completed 8th grade, 2 years 6 months for those who have completed 10th grade) provides for extensive general mathematical training in the amount of 668 hours. In addition, the following subjects related to mathematics are taught in this specialty: numerical methods, programming and algorithmic languages, mathematical statistics, fundamentals of economics and mathematical methods in planning. Those who complete training in this specialty are sent to work in computer centers, laboratories, design bureaus, programming departments as assistant engineers; at the same time they receive the qualification “technician-mathematician-programmer”.

Specialty No. 0661 - “information processing in automated control systems (ACS)” - is completely new, its introduction is expected this year. Those who complete 8 classes will study in this specialty for 3 years 10 months, and those who complete 10 classes will study for 2 years 8 months. The mathematics course is designed for 517 hours. In addition, the following subjects related to mathematics are taught: computational mathematics, fundamentals of programming and algorithmic languages, mathematical support for automated control systems. Those who graduate from a technical school in this specialty will receive the qualification “automated control system information system operator” and will be assigned to work in computer centers, laboratories and departments of those institutions that develop or use automated control systems.

In-depth study of mathematics helps to improve the level of teaching in other disciplines, in particular physics.

A new program has been created for these specialties: “Physics with fundamentals of electrical engineering”, designed for 279 hours. The curriculum provides for the study of physics over three semesters.

The peculiarity of the program is that it considers the ideas of classical and modern physics simultaneously. In particular, the basic concepts of the theory of relativity are introduced in parallel with the repetition of elements of mechanics and are further used in the study of electrodynamics and the physics of the atom and the atomic nucleus. Here it is especially worth noting the importance and expediency of using the fundamentals of mathematical analysis, which students of specialties No. 1750, 1735, 0661 master, which allows one to avoid dogmatism when studying a number of issues, for example, such as inductive and capacitive reactance, effective value of alternating current, Ohm’s law for an alternating current circuit, etc.

The course ends with statistical laws of molecular physics, quantum mechanics, atomic and nuclear processes.

The strengthening of the role of mathematical methods in the study of physics determines the increasing role of the demonstration experiment and its significant complication. In particular, the program provides for the widespread use of oscillographic methods, a large number of laboratory works, the quality of which is much higher than in physics programs for other specialties. This especially applies to works from the sections “Electrodynamics”, “Oscillations. Alternating electric current."
