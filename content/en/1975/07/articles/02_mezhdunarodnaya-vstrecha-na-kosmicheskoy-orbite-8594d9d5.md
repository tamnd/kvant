INTERNATIONAL MEETING
IN SPACE ORBIT

July 1975 will forever go down in the history of astronautics. In accordance with the agreement between the USSR and the USA on cooperation in the exploration of outer space for peaceful purposes, the first joint flight under the ASTP program - the Apollo-Soyuz experimental flight - was scheduled for this place.
The most important moment of this experiment will be the docking of the ships.

The docking and undocking of spacecraft is an extremely important element of space programs. Today it is necessary when delivering astronauts to orbital research stations and returning them to their native Earth. In the future, without it, it will be impossible to assemble complex multi-purpose orbital stations in near-Earth space, which will be assembled from special blocks for various purposes, one by one delivered into orbit using carrier packages. It is also a necessary part of future human flights to the planets of the solar system. In this case, the spacecraft remains in an orbit remote from the planet as a base station from which returning automatic zones and research cosmonauts are sent to the surface of the planet. Finally, without docking, it is also impossible to provide assistance to spacecraft crews in trouble.

Both the Soviet and American space research programs carried out many flights with dockings in outer space. However, the designs of Soviet and American ships are very different from each other, and the docking of the Soyuz and Apollo spacecraft required the designers and cosmonauts to solve additional, fundamentally new problems. In connection with the ASTP program, a large amount of work was carried out in the Soviet Union and the United States to develop new systems and components of spacecraft. The cosmonauts underwent complex ground training in conditions as close as possible to those planned for a joint Soviet-American flight. In December 1974, the Soyuz-16 spacecraft flew. The crew of the ship - A.V. Filippenko and N.N. Rukovodshchik - tested a new docking unit in space conditions and performed maneuvers in orbit characteristic of the upcoming joint flight with the Apollo spacecraft.

The joint flight of the Soyuz and Apollo spacecraft, a round-robin space experiment, is important for the development of international scientific relations and will help strengthen mutual trust between the USSR and the USA and further defuse international tension.
