In «Kvant», 1973, No. 7, we already talked about MIEM. In this issue we publish versions of the entrance written exam in mathematics at MIEM in 1974.

Option 1

1. The two teams, working together, had to sow a field of 234 $га$ in 117 hours. However, when the first one sowed 39 $га$, its productivity increased by 6.25 $га/ч$. When the second team sowed 39 $га$, its productivity also increased by 1.5 $га/ч$. As a result, the field was sown in $T=55$ hours. Find the productivity of each brigade. Find all values ​​of $T$ for which the problem has a solution.

2. Solve the equation

$$2(\cos x + \sin x)\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+x\right) = \cos 2x.$$

3. Solve inequality

$$\log_{(x+1)^2}(x+3) > 1.$$

4. At what values ​​of $a$ are the roots of the equation

$$x^2 - (2a+1)x + 3a = 0$$

lie between the numbers 1 and 4?

5. In a regular quadrangular pyramid, a plane is drawn through the side of the base, perpendicular to the opposite side face and dividing this face into a triangle and a trapezoid, the areas of which are in the ratio 1:3. Determine the total surface of the pyramid if the side of its base is $a$.

Option 2

1. The driver had to transport 120 machines from one city to another. After he transported 48 machines, he was given another, more powerful truck, charging 4 more machines per trip. As a result, the total number of trips made by the driver decreased by three. How many machines did the first truck take in one trip?

2. Solve the equation

$$1 - 3\cos x + \cos 2x = \frac{\mathrm{cosec}(\pi - x)}{\mathrm{ctg}\,2x - \mathrm{ctg}\,x}.$$

3. Solve inequality

$$2x + \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} > 0.$$
