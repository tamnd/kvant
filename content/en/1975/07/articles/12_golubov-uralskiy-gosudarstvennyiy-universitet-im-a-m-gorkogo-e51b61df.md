Mathematics
Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics
Option 1

1. Are there values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which any solution to the inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 + 4x + 3} \geq -1 - x$$
is a solution to the equation
$$|x - a| - |x + 1| = 2$$
and, conversely, every solution to an equation is a solution to an inequality?

2. Lines $l_1$ and $l_2$ intersect at vertex $B$ of triangle $ABC$. Construct a triangle $ABC$ if, in addition to these lines, the location of the vertex $A$ is located on the plane and it is known that the median of the triangle lies on $l_1$, and the altitude lies on $l_2$.

3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{1}{\log x^2 \left( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6} x - x^2 \right)} \leq \log_x x^2.$$
4. It is given that $\alpha = \beta + \gamma$. Prove that $\sin^2 \alpha + \sin^2 \beta + \sin^2 \gamma + 2 \cos \alpha \cos \beta \cos \gamma = 2$.

Option 2

1. Are there values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which each solution to the inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 + 4x + 7} \leq x + 3$$
is a solution to the equation
$$2^{x+2} - |2^{x+1} - a| = 2^{x+1} + 1$$
and, conversely, is every solution to an equation a solution to an inequality?

2. Three points are located on the plane, two of which are the midpoints of the side of the triangle, and the third is the intersection point of its medians. The remaining elements of the triangle were erased. Use a compass and ruler to reconstruct the triangle.

3. Solve inequality
$$|\log|x|(4 + 3x - x^2) - 1| \leq \frac{1}{2} \log \sqrt{x-1}(x-1).$$

4. It is given that $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the interior angles of some triangle, and $tg\alpha, tg\beta, tg\gamma$ form an arithmetic progression. Prove that
$$2\cos \beta = \cos(\alpha - \gamma).$$
