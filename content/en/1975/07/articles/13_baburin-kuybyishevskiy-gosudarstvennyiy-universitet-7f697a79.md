Kuibyshev State University

For Kuibyshev State University, 1974 was remarkable for the first graduation of young specialists (specialties: mathematics, mechanics, physics, chemistry, biology, Russian language and literature, law) and the opening of an evening university department (specialties: mathematics, physics, law).

Below are some options for the entrance exam in mathematics to Kuibyshev State University in 1974, after the option number in parentheses it is indicated which applicants for which specialties wrote this option: MM - “mathematics and mechanics”, F - “physics”. Applicants for these specialties who have successfully passed the written tests

Option 1 (MM)
1. The basis of the parameters is an equal trapezoid with bases a and b and angle φ between the diagonals. The angles of inclination of the side faces of the parametils, containing the parallel sides of the trapezoid, relate to the plane of the base as 1:2. Find the volume of parametils if its vertex is projected at the point of intersection of the diagonals of the base.
2. Let the numbers a, b, c, d form an arithmetic progression. Prove that $(x-a)(x-b)(x-c)(x-d)+m^2 \geq 0$
for any real values ​​of x, if we take an m such that $2m \geq |ad-bc|$.
How can the conditions of the problem be generalized so that the main idea of ​​its solution remains the same?
3. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 1 + \log_x y \log_3 x = 4, \\ 2\log^4 x^2 - \frac{1}{2} \log_{\sqrt{x}} y^4 = \frac{10}{3}. \end{cases}$$
4. Solve the equation
$$x^2 + 2x \cos (x-m) + 1 = 0.$$
Option 2 (MM)
1. On one of the faces of the dihedral angle φ < $\frac{\pi}{2}$ there lies a square. One side of the square forms an angle α with the edge of the dihedral angle. Determine the angles between the diagonals of the square and the other face of the dihedral angle.
2. Solve inequality
$$\log \frac{x}{2} + \log \frac{x}{4} < \frac{\log_2 x^4}{\log_2 x^2 - 4}.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^{29} x - \cos^{17} 10x = 2.$$
4. Solve systems of equations
$$\begin{cases} \frac{1}{3} \sqrt[9]{9} = \left(\frac{1}{81}\right)^{-\frac{x}{4y}} \\ 16 \sqrt[9]{2^x + 3y} = \frac{x}{y}. \end{cases}$$
