## Tasks

1. In the room in which the squad council met, there were chairs with 4 legs and stools with 3 legs. When all the pioneers sat down, there were no empty seats left, and the sum of the number of legs of those sitting and legs of the seats turned out to be equal to 39.
How many chairs and how many stools were there in the room?
2. The scuba diver lost his orientation underwater. How can he tell which way is up and which way is down?
3. Moscow schoolchildren Nikolai and Vladimir met at Vladimir's.
Nikolai said: “If you subtract a number from the two-digit number of my house,
formed after rearranging its numbers, you get your house number. What house do I live in?
Vladimir said: “This is an easy task,” and immediately solved it.
What kind of houses did the schoolchildren live in?
4. In order for the mown hay to dry, it is often turned. Why does this help?
5. How to put the signs “$+$” between the digits of the number 987654321 so that
the total is 99?
How many solutions does this problem have?
