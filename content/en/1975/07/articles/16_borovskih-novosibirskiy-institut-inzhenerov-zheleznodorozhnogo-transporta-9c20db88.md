The Novosibirsk Institute of Railway Transport Engineers is one of the country's leading institutions for training scientific and engineering personnel and conducting research in the field of railway transport. NIIZhT includes a full-time department, an evening department, a correspondence department and evening preparatory courses. The institute has 7 faculties, ready-made specialists in 14 specialties: construction of railways, fetters and track management; places and tunnels; construction, road machinery and equipment; operation of railways; industrial and civil construction; water supply and sewerage; economics and organization of railway transport; accounting; economics and organization of construction in railway transport (E); electrification of railway transport; diesel locomotives and diesel locomotive facilities; varon production and varon farming (B); automation, telemechanics and communications in railway transport (AT); organization of mechanized processing of economic information (MOI).

Those entering the institute for technical and engineering-economic specialties take entrance exams in mathematics (written and oral), physics (oral), Russian language and literature (written). The written exam takes 3 astronomical hours.

Those entering the accounting specialty take exams in mathematics (orally), history, geography, Russian language and literature.
