⟦folio 58⟧

You, who play sports, regularly compete with challenge teams and production teams in Moscow and other cities. A large group of students is part of the combined teams of base factories, MGS and CS DSO "Trud". A number of students who are members of the USSR national teams in skating sports study at the plant-college school.

Below are examples of entrance exams in mathematics and physics at the plant-technical college in 1975.

Matematika

Option 1

1. In a regular triangular prime, the angle between the diagonal of a side face and another side face is $\alpha$. Determine the lateral surface of the abutment, knowing that the edge of the base is equal to $\alpha$.

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_3(2^x - 1) - \log_3(2^x - 2^x) = -2 + x \log_3 2$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg} x + \text{tg} 2x = 2 \cos^2 x$$

Option 2

1. A cube is inscribed into the cone from side $a$ (the base of the cube lies on the base of the cone). Determine the volume of a cone if its generatrix is ​​inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\alpha$.

2. Solve the equation
$$\lg 2 + \lg (4^{x-2} + 1) = \lg 6 + \lg \left(2^{x-2} + \frac{5}{3}\right)$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos^4 \frac{x}{2} - \sin^4 \frac{x}{2} + \sin x = \cos 2x$$

Option 3

1. A ball is inscribed in a cone, the generatrix of which is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $\alpha$. The volume of the cone is $V$. Find the surface of the ball.

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_7 [x + \log_2 (9 - 2^x) + 4] = 1$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x + \sin^2 2x = \sin^2 3x$$

Physics

Option 1

1. Propagation of oscillations in control environments. Longitudinal and transverse waves. Speed ​​of propagation of vibrations. Wavelength. The relationship between wavelength, vibration speed and frequency.

2. Work and tellotah. Number of tellots, units of measurement. Specific heat capacity of a substance. Formula for calculating the number of tellots required to heat a body.

3. A rocket, the mass of which is $M = 5 \cdot 10^3 \text{ kg}$, rises in the vertical take-off section under the action of the traction force of the jet propulsors equal to $F = 2,45 \cdot 10^5 \text{ N}$. Determine the acceleration with which the rocket rises and the weight of the astronaut in the spacecraft at takeoff, if the astronaut’s mass is $m = 70 \text{ kg}$. Acceleration of free fall on the weight of the pointing rocket is taken equal to $9,8 \text{ m/c}^2$.

Option 2

1. Rectilinear uneven motion of a material point. Average and instantaneous speeds. Uniform linear motion. Acceleration. Equation of uniform motion.

2. The phenomenon of electromagnetic induction. The condition for the occurrence of e.m.f. induction. Experimental confirmation of the law of electromagnetic induction. Direction of induction current. Lenz's law.

3. How should two lenses be positioned, one of which is diverging with focal length $F_1$, and the other is its own with focal length $F_2 = 2F_1$, so that rays parallel to the main optical axis of the lenses, passing through the lenses, remain parallel.

Option 3

1. Electrostatic field of precise charge. Precise charge field potential. Potential difference. The work of moving a charge in an electrostatic field (using the example of an exact charge field). Expression of work through potential difference.

2. The law of universal gravitation. The physical meaning of the gravitational constant. Gravity and body weight.

3. In a closed tank of volume $V = 50 \text{ л}$, the presence of water of mass $m = 100 \text{ л}$ under normal conditions ($p_0 = 1,01 \cdot 10^5 \text{ h/m}^2$, $T_0 = 273^\circ\text{K}$). Determine the pressure $p$ of humid air if the tank is heated to $\Delta T = 100^\circ\text{C}$. Water vapor is considered an ideal gas.
