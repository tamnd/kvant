Tasks

M351—M355; F363—F367

M351. Reconstruct the triangle if three points are offset on the plane: O is the center of the circumscribed circle, P is the center of gravity, and H is the base of one of the altitudes of this triangle.

M. M. Imershivili (9th grade student, Tbilisi)

M352 *. Let $n$ be an integer for which
$$n < \left(45 + \sqrt{1975}\right)^{30} < n + 1.$$
Prove that $n$ is odd.

D. K. Faddeev

M353 *. Let $ABCD$ be an arbitrary triangle. Prove that:
a) the sum of the dihedral angles of a triangle whose edges are $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ is less than 2π;
b) the sum of all dihedral angles of a triangle is greater than 2π but less than 3π;
b) the sum of the cosines of all dihedral angles of a triangle is positive and does not exceed 2, and this sum is equal to 2 if and only if all the faces of the triangle;
d) if $|AB| + |CD| = |BC| + |DA|$, then the sum of dihedral angles with edges $AB$ and $CD$ is equal to the sum of dihedral angles with edges $BC$ and $AD$.

N. F. Sharynn

M354. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., $4n + 2$ at the vertices and midpoints of a side of a regular $(2n + 1)$-gon so that the sum of the three numbers at the ends and midpoints of each side is the same for all sides?

Consider the case $n = 3$, $n = 8$ as examples.

S. T. Berkolaiko
