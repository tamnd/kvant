F322. The two stars are orbiting around a common center of mass with the post-moan stars in absolute velocity $v_1$ and $v_2$ with a period of $T$. The maximum mass of the star and the distance between them.

Since the velocities of stars rotating around the center of mass of the system are constant in absolute value, the stars move in circles, and these circles are concentric and their common center is located at the center of mass of the system. The relative positions of the stars and the common center of mass (points $O$) are shown in figure 2.

Let us denote by $m_1$ and $m_2$ the masses of the stars, and by $r_1$ and $r_2$ the radii of the corresponding circles. The centripetal acceleration ($v^2/r$) is imparted to each star by the gravitational force $F = \gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$, where $r = r_1 + r_2$ is the distance between the stars.

Let us write down the equation of motion of stars:
$$\gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} = m_1 \frac{v_1^2}{r_1}$$ and $$\gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} = m_2 \frac{v_2^2}{r_2}.$$

Velocities of stars with treatment period and radii of corresponding circles:
$$v_1 = \frac{2\pi r_1}{T}$$ and $$v_2 = \frac{2\pi r_2}{T}.$$
