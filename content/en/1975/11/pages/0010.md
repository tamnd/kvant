⟦folio 8⟧

Fig. 1. A device for studying the interaction of conductors with current. AB is a fixed conductor, EDF is a movable conductor mounted on a glass axis EF. To protect against vibrations, the device is covered with a glass cover. (Figure Ampere)

Fig. 2. The first device to study the influence of the Earth's magnetic field on the solenoid. AB is a solenoid wound on a thin glass tube and suspended like a compass needle. The device turns out to be insufficiently mobile, and the expected effect was not recorded. (Figure Ampere)

Fig. 3. A device for studying the influence of the Earth’s magnetic field on a coil with current. ABCD - moving coil with current. The second (fixed) coil, larger than the radius, fixed a vertical plane perpendicular to the magnetic Merdian at a given point. When the current was turned on, the ABCD turn rotated until it coincided with the stationary turn. (Figure Ampere)
