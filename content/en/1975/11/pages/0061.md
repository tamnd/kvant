## IX All-Union Olympiad for Schoolchildren

Last year, by order of the USSR Minister of Education, a new “Regulations on the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for Schoolchildren” was approved. Basically, the dates and procedure for holding the Olympiad have been slightly changed. Let's tell you a little about the new “Regulations”.

### Procedure for holding the Olympiad

The Olympiad is held during the academic year in five stages. The first stage is school Olympiads. The second stage is regional (city) Olympiads. The third stage is the Olympiads of autonomous republics, territories and regions. The fourth stage is the Republican Olympiads. The fifth stage - the final stage of the All-Union Olympiad - is held in April.

### Olympiad participants

1. One team each from the Union republics, the cities of Moscow and Leningrad, the Main Political Directorate of the Soviet Army and Navy and the Ministry of Railways of the USSR take part in the final stage of the All-Union Olympiad.
2. The size of the team for each subject is determined in accordance with the number of students in the republic. Last year at the Olympiad there were 48 people from the RSFSR, 12 people from the Ukrainian SSR, 6 people each from the BSSR, KazSSR and UzSSR, from the rest of the republics, the teams of Moscow and Leningrad, as well as the Ministry of Railways and Gl. Political departments of the Soviet Army and Navy - 3 people each.
The city hosting the final stage of the Olympiad has the right to take part in it with 3 people.

Each team must include the same number of students from grades 8-9-10-(11). Additionally, the winners of the final stage of the previous Olympiad, who took I-II places, are included in the team.
