⟦folio 20⟧

Household electrical stores sell lamps of the following wattages: 15w, 25w, 40w, 60w, 100w, 150w and 200w. The reader sees that, with the exception of the last value, this is a slightly corrected R5 series.

If we abandon the too high requirements for calculation accuracy, then we can assume that the product of preferred numbers is also a preferred number (why?).

Therefore, the planes (volumes) of rectangles (parallelepipeds) will be preferred, if only their linear dimensions are expressed in preferred numbers. The same is true for the area of ​​a circle and the volume of a cylinder, since the preferred number of 3.15 is close to the number of liters. This is another argument for using preferred numbers when compiling sets of standard sizes.

2. Prove the “law of conservation” of the number of i-numbers with a “logarithmic” distribution (p. 18).
3. Prove that the quantities
$$\max \left[ \min \left( \frac{x_0}{x} + \frac{x}{x_0}, \frac{x_1}{x} + \frac{x}{x_1}, \dots, \frac{x_n}{x} + \frac{x}{x_n} \right) \right],$$
where $1 = x_0 \leqslant x_1 \leqslant \dots \leqslant x_{n-1} \leqslant x_n = 10$, takes the smallest possible value when the numbers $x_k$ form a geometric progression.
