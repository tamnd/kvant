Sam Loyd's Puzzle

Baseball game
In order to play this baseball, there is no need to know the rules of the game for real baseball (this game is more like rounders). figure shows a game board consisting of 49 squares, each of which can be covered with a piece.
There are usually two players (there may be more players). Players stock up on chips of “their” color and place them one by one on free fields. Once all the fields are covered with chips, players count the number of fours of their chips that are on the same line.
Example If your chips are placed on fields A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, then you score 5 points. The winner is the partner who scores the most points. Task. The chips cover 20 balls. What is the maximum number of rows of four chips that can be there?
