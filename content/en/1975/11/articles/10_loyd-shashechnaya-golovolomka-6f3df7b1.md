⟦folio 29⟧

Sam Loyd's checkers puzzle

Old Rube Perkins, who for forty years had not lost the title of champion of his district in checkers, once lost his temper, a fourth-grader prodigy. inflicted a decisive defeat on him. When the child prodigy won three checkers in one from Perkins in the second game, the champion finally lost his head and, during the first game as shown in figure, vowed never to sit at the board again.

Try it. re-knit the chessboard from the eighth pieces that have it. There is no need to cut out pieces from figure for this. Copy them or transfer them to tracing paper.
