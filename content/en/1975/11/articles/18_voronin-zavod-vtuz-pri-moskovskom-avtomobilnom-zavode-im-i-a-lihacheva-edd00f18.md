The factory-technical college, as a new and important form of higher education, is penetrating ever deeper into the production environment. At the same time, the training of students is based on the organizational combination of students’ theoretical knowledge with their production work at enterprises.

The factory-technical college at ZIL was created fifteen years ago and, together with other factories-technical colleges, has come a long way in organization and has become on par with the largest universities in the country. Now more than 2,500 of its graduates work at automobile factories in the capital and many other cities of the Soviet Union.

The plant-technological college at ZIL has three faculties: automotive, mechanical and technological and evening.

The Faculty of Automotive trains specialists in the field of design, calculation and research of cars, engines and bodies, as well as the manufacture of car parts using cold and hot stamping methods.

The Faculty of Mechanical Technology trains specialists in the field of technological machine building, metal-cutting equipment, tool production, technological foundry production, metal science, equipment and technological heat treatment, economics and organization of the machine-building industry. In addition, it has a number of specialists: repair and modernization of metal-cutting equipment, electrical exhaustion

skin and electronimic processing methods, etc.

The evening faculty provides training for senior engineering and technical workers of base factories who are qualified as mechanical engineers. They receive a second higher education in economics through the evening system with a duration of study of 3 years.

The plant-technical college accepts employees of the enterprises ZIL, AZLK, GPZ-1, Kamaz and their branches, as well as graduates of secondary schools sent to study at these plants, and trains engineers for these enterprises. Graduates of the plant-technical college successfully work at basic factories, become true production managers, actively solving problems of scientific and technological progress of production.

What is the peculiarity of training engineers in the “plant-technical college” system?

This is primarily an alternation of theoretical training with work in production. With a total training period of 5 years and 10 months over 11 semesters, students study alternately with and without interruption from work, alternating by week.

In the first year, students (mostly high school graduates, they make up about 90% of the intake) acquire a working specialty. In the second-fourth year, students work in the workshops of the base plant in working positions in accordance with

in accordance with the specialty being studied, participate in the implementation of the production program, undergo engineering and production training according to the programs of the major departments under the guidance of plant consulting engineers and teachers of the plant-technical college. By the time they transfer to an engineering position (usually by the end of the fourth year), students have a working qualification of at least 3 categories. In the fifth and sixth years, mandatory internship is provided for students in engineering and technical positions (engineers, senior technicians and technicians) in laboratories, design bureaus and departments of base plants. During this period, students prepare for the upcoming graduation project and complete graduation projects. Project topics, as a rule, are directly related to the work performed by students in production. This allows you to significantly increase the number of real diploma projects to be implemented at enterprises.

Students and teaching staff actively participate in the implementation of the “Comprehensive Plan for Social and Economic Development” of the Moscow Automobile Plant named after I. A. Likhachev and other basic factories of our university in the areas of “Increasing the technical level, quality and reliability of products”, “Development of equipment and production technology”, “Research and development work”.

Faculty and students participate in economic contracts and state budget research work on the subject of base plants. To carry out the most important contractual work, part of the full-time students of the plant-technical college is seconded by the base plant ZIL to the disposal of the NIS of the plant-technical college.

The training of mechanical engineers for basic plants is carried out in accordance with the qualification requirements developed by the plant-technical college and the main specialists of ZIL.

Fulfillment of these requirements in combination with the necessary theoretical training ensures the preparation of qualified specialists with a wide range of skills for a specific production. Labor hardening, ideological conviction, and knowledge acquired within the walls of the institute allow graduates of the plant-technical college to quickly integrate into the life of work collectives and actively participate in solving the tasks assigned to them. So, although this year the technical college plant released only its tenth class, among its graduates there are plant directors, deputy directors, chief plant mechanics, deputy chief engineers, heads of departments and units. At ZIL alone, more than 10 workshops are headed by graduates of the plant-technical college.

During the entire period of study, students of the factory-college school receive monthly $^1/_2$ monthly salary and $^1/_2$ monthly stipend, increased by 15% compared to scholarships for students at regular full-time universities. All successful full-time students are provided with a scholarship at the plant-institute.

Students of the plant-college school have the opportunity to take an active part in the sports life of one of the largest sports clubs in the country “Torpedo” ZIL, sports clubs “Moskvich” and “Podshipnik”.

At the factory-college, an extensive university Spartakiad is held annually, attracting almost all first- to fifth-year students. Students also compete at the ZIL Winter Spartakiad, where they show excellent results. The university has sections for volleyball, basketball, football, handball, skiing, and athletics. ## IX All-Union Olympiad for Schoolchildren

Last year, by order of the USSR Minister of Education, a new “Regulations on the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for Schoolchildren” was approved. Basically, the dates and procedure for holding the Olympiad have been slightly changed. Let's tell you a little about the new “Regulations”.

### Procedure for holding the Olympiad

The Olympiad is held during the academic year in five stages. The first stage is school Olympiads. The second stage is regional (city) Olympiads. The third stage is the Olympiads of autonomous republics, territories and regions. The fourth stage is the Republican Olympiads. The fifth stage - the final stage of the All-Union Olympiad - is held in April.

### Olympiad participants

1. One team each from the Union republics, the cities of Moscow and Leningrad, the Main Political Directorate of the Soviet Army and Navy and the Ministry of Railways of the USSR take part in the final stage of the All-Union Olympiad.
2. The size of the team for each subject is determined in accordance with the number of students in the republic. Last year at the Olympiad there were 48 people from the RSFSR, 12 people from the Ukrainian SSR, 6 people each from the BSSR, KazSSR and UzSSR, from the rest of the republics, the teams of Moscow and Leningrad, as well as the Ministry of Railways and Gl. Political departments of the Soviet Army and Navy - 3 people each.
The city hosting the final stage of the Olympiad has the right to take part in it with 3 people.

Each team must include the same number of students from grades 8-9-10-(11). Additionally, the winners of the final stage of the previous Olympiad, who took I-II places, are included in the team.
