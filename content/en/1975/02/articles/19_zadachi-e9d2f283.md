## Tasks

1. In the city of Vasyuki, each family occupied a separate house. One day
day each family moved into a house previously occupied by another family. To commemorate this day, the city council decided to paint all the houses red and blue
or yellow colors, and so that for each family the colors of the old and new
the houses did not match. Will the city council be able to do this?
2. You and your friend are going down into a deep, deep mine. In your hands
lever scales on which a 1 kg load is balanced. Your friend's -
spring scales on which a load weighing also 1 kg hangs. Will the scale reading change when you go very deep?
3. 1974 numbers are written in a circle. If you read them clockwise,
starting from a certain place, we get a number divisible by 27. Prove that
that if you read it from any other place, you will also get a number,
divisible by 27.
4. You need to heat the water on an alcohol lamp. For this purpose, two glasses are offered: one made of thick glass, the other of very thin glass. Which
glass will you choose?
5. Can three people, having one two-seater motorcycle, overcome
distance of 60 km in three hours? Pedestrian speed is 5 km/h,
motorcycle speed (with or without load) - 50 km/h.
