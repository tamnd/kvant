M306. One corner square (1x1) (Fig. 1) has been removed from the chessboard (8×8). What is the smallest number of equal triangles (equal in area) that the remaining part of the board can be cut into?

V. P. Fedomov

M307. The plane is divided into identical hexagonal rooms (Fig.2). Some walls have doors in such a way that for any vertex at which three walls (sides of hexagons) meet, there are doors in exactly two walls. Prove that any closed path through such a labyrinth passes through an even number of doors.

V. P. Golubyatnikov

Yu. I. Ionin

M310. Prove that among $n$-digit numbers there are more than $8^n$ such numbers in whose decimal notation no group of digits (in particular, no group of digits) appears twice in a row.

G. A. Gurevii

F318. At what $C_1$ the capacitance of the capacitor system shown in figure 3 is: a) $C$; b) $kC$ ($k \neq 1$); b) $C_1$?

A diverging lens $\Pi_2$ with a focal length of -2$F$ was placed between the screen and lens $\Pi_1$. To obtain a clear image of the object, the screen had to be viewed at a distance equal to $F$. F320. In the pumped state, the container $V = 1 \text{ л}$ contains 1 $\text{е}$ uranium hydride $\mathrm{UH}_3$. When heated to a temperature of $t = 400^\circ \mathrm{C}$, the hydride completely decomposes into uranium (atomic weight $A = 238$) and hydrogen. Find the pressure of hydrogen in the vessel at this temperature.

F322. Two stars rotate around a common center of mass with constant absolute velocities $v_1$ and $v_2$ with a period of $T$. Find the masses of the stars and the distance between them.
