## At the training camp in the fifth b

“Today I will show you a trick,” announced pioneer leader Lena. - Do you agree?

- We agree! - the voices of fifth graders were heard.

- What's the trick? - asked Seryozha. - I also know the needle trick...

“I’ll show you an arithmetic trick,” Lena said. “Let each of you think of a number.” Just don’t tell me,” Lena announced. Now multiply this number by 5. Add 4 to the product and double the resulting amount. Ready?

“Now add another 99,” Lena said, “and each of you will get a three-digit number.” Leave only the last digit in this number and cross out the first two.

- What do you mean: cross it out? - Yura asked.

“For example, you got the number 364,” Lena said. — If you cross out the first two digits, then only 4 will remain.

“To what’s left,” Lena continued, “add 28, and then divide by 5.”

Sonya, who was sitting on the last desk, quickly counted and laughed.

- Why are you laughing? - Lena asked. - Come here. “And she said quietly in Sonya’s ear:

- You got 7.

“That’s right,” Sonya was delighted. - How did you find out?

- How much did I get? - Seryozha asked impatiently.

Lena came up to him and said quietly:

- You got 7.

Then Lena approached everyone in turn and said to each:

- You got 7.
* * *

How could Lena know that everyone got seven?
