To geometric problems
(see «Kvant», 1975, No. 1, p. 63)
1. It is easy to check that the points $A, B, C$ bisect the arcs $EF, FD, DE$.
2. Divide side $BC$ by point $F$ in relation to $m:n$. On $BC$ describe a semicircle and restore to $BC$ at the point $F$ the perpendicular $FG$ (point $G$ – on the semicircle). Continue $CB$ and $DA$ until the intersection of $E$. Draw a circle with center $E$ and radius $EG$, intersecting $BC$ at $H$. Through $H$ draw $HK$ parallel to $CD$.
3. It is easy to see that $H$ is the center of the circle inscribed in UVW. Hence, $OA \perp VW$ (since $A$ bisects $VW$).
4. Draw the chord $AM$ through $H$ and bisect $HM$ with the point $D$. Through $D$ draw the chord $BC$ perpendicular to $AM$. Triangle $ABC$ – the desired one.
5. Draw a straight line $A'X'B'$ parallel to $AXB$ at a distance $c$ from it ($XX' \perp AB$), where $c$ is the radius of the given circle. Connect the center $O$ of the circle with $X'$ and divide $OX'$ by the point $Q$ so that $OQ \cdot OX' = l^2$. Draw a circle with center $E$, touching $A'B'$ at $X'$ and passing through $Q$. The circle with center $E$ and radius $EX' \pm c$ is the required one (the sign $y$ depends on whether the points $E$ and $O$ are on the same side of $AB$ or on different sides).
