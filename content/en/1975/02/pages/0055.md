A simple experiment to determine the energy of the explosion: in a calm impact, pieces of paper would fall at his feet, and when, a few seconds after the explosion, the front of the shock wave arrives, they will fall somewhat cymbantirammidal in the direction of propagation of the wave. From the distance from the point of explosion and the displacement of the impact of the shock wave, the energy of the explosion can be calculated. Fermi carried out these calculations in advance, preparing a table from which he could immediately determine the energy as a result of such a crude but simple measurement... Characteristically, his answer turned out to be very close to the result of carefully carried out official measurements. But the latter appeared a few days after studying the records, and Fermi received his drawing in a few seconds.
