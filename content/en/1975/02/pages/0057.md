⟦folio 55⟧

New books

In 1975, we continued publishing materials in the “New Books” section.
As in past years, in this section we will publish short summaries of books that have already been published and books that were published in 1975 and are of interest to our readers. In connection with the multi-state processes of readers, we will also publish annotations on some books published by the publishing houses “Young Guard”, “Children’s Literature”, “World” and “Knowledge”.
In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the first quarter of 1975.
Orders for books must be placed in a timely manner through specialized stores or Book-by-mail stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house
"Science"
1. Markushevich A.I., Celje functions. Volume 5 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 15 kopecks, 2nd edition.
Elementary functions studied in secondary school are either entire functions, or quotients of two entire functions, or inverse functions of these functions.
This book introduces readers to the general theory of entire functions and their most important properties.
The book is written in an interesting and entertaining way. It is interesting for high school students and will also be useful for advanced mathematicians.
2. Mosteller F., Fifty occupied probabilistic problems. Volume 6 sheets, circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks, 2nd edition.
Most of the problems presented in this book can be solved in an elementary way. To solve them, the knowledge acquired in high school is quite sufficient, and only some of them require knowledge of a course of analysis that goes beyond the scope of the school mathematics course.
The book will be useful for high school students taking mathematics and physics courses.
3. Vorobyov N. N., Divisibility criteria. Volume 4 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 14 copies, 2nd edition.
The book outlines some elements of questions in number theory, in particular, questions in the theory of divisibility. Outlining the signs of divisibility, the author touches on some issues of the theory of relations and the theory of algorithms.
4. Fomin S.V., Number systems. Volume 3 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 10 kop., 3rd edition.
In this small brochure, the author paints in detail about the different number systems. In an accessible and compelling form, the author describes the history of the origin, properties and application of various number systems.
The book will be useful to high school students and students of mathematics and physics.
5. Vilenkin N. Ya., Popular combinatorics. 12 sheets, 100,000 copies, 40 copies.
Combinatorics is one of the branches of mathematics, the knowledge of which must be presented in a variety of specialties. Combinatorial problems come precisely to the attention of physicists, chemists, biologists, linguists, code specialists and many others. Combinatorial methods underlie the solution of many problems in probability theory and its applications. The book describes interesting combinatorial problems and methods for solving them in a popular form.

Publishing house "Mir"
6. Dudeney G. E., Selected problems and puzzles. Volume 25 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 49 k.
This book presents more than 500 problems and puzzles of the famous English scientist and popular mathematician.
All readers who love mathematics will read the book with great satisfaction and great usefulness.

Physics
Publishing house
"Science"
1. Bakanina L.P., Belonuchkin V.E., Kozel S.M. et al., Collection of problems in physics. Volume 21 sheets, circulation 300,000 copies, price 67 kopecks, 3rd edition.
This collection of problems for an elementary physics course contains problems of increased difficulty, which, however, do not go beyond the scope of the high school curriculum. All problems are provided with solutions or detailed instructions.
Most of the problems in this collection were used during the introductory exams at the Moscow Physical Institute.
The book is intended for high school students, registered physicists and people independently preparing for entrance exams to the Moscow Physical Institute.
2. Demin V.G., The fate of the solar system. Volume 13 sheets, circulation 20,000 copies, price 45 kopecks, 2nd edition.
《Is the solar system falling apart?》 is the main question addressed in this book.
The book is intended for high school students with a background in physics and astronomy.
