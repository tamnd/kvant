⟦folio 58⟧

Quests

1. In the city of Vasyuki, each family occupied a separate house. One day, each family moved into a house previously occupied by another family. To commemorate this day, the city council decided to paint all the houses red, blue or yellow, so that for each family the colors of the old and new houses would not match. Does the city council have permission to do this?

2. You and a friend are going down into a deep, deep mine. You have in your hands a lever scale on which a load of 1 kk is balanced. Your friend has a spring scale on which a load weighing also 1 kk weighs. Does the scale change when you go very deep?

3. 1974 numbers are written in a circle. If you read them clockwise, starting from a certain place, you will get a number divisible by 27. Prove that if you read it from any other place, you will also get a number divisible by 27.

4. You need to heat the water in an alcohol lamp. For this purpose, two glasses are offered: one made of thick glass, the other of very thin glass. Which glass will you choose?

5. Can three people, having one two-seater motorcycle, cover a distance of 60 km in three hours? The speed of a pedestrian is 5 km/h, the speed of a motorcycle (with or without load) is 50 km/h.
