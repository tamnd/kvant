Fifth Mathematical Olympiad MESI

The Olympiad is held in two types: I - round - correspondence and II - full-time and correspondence.

Schoolchildren who are successfully compared with I.type problems and who sent solutions no later than March 15, 1975 (according to postal time) will be allowed to participate in the second part-time and part-time.type. Moscow schoolchildren will be invited to MESI for full-time.type. Nonresident students participate in the second.type in absentia. Each Olympiad participant admitted to the second.type will be aware of this. To speed up the receipt of a response from the institute, please attach an envelope with your address to the letter.

MESI address: 119435, Moscow, B. Savvinsky lane, 14, “Olympiad-75”.

Problem solving is carried out in Russian in the student's terradi. All works submitted to the Olympiad will be checked by computer. Therefore, we ask you to place the answers to the tasks filled in in the following table at the end of the terradi

Problem numbers 1 2 3 4 5

Answers

First type task:
1. Find the whole part of a number
$$10\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{3}+\ldots+\sqrt{1975}}}.$$
2. Solve the system of inequalities:
$$\begin{cases} 3^x+4x-9|-8| \leq 3^x-4x-1, \\ 2^{x^2}>x+1. \end{cases}$$
3. Find (in degrees) the smallest positive value of $x+y$ if
$$(1+tgx)(1+tgy)=2.$$
4. From the vertex of the right angle of the triangle a perpendicular is dropped to the hypotenuse. On this perpendicular, as on a diameter, a circle is built. Tangents are drawn from the ends of the hypotenuse to this circle, touching it at points M and N and intersecting it when continued at point L.
Find the lengths of the tangents MZ and NZ if the hypotenuse is 6.
5. A ball of radius 2.5 touches face ABC of paramid ABCD at point C and edge BD at point D. The extension of edge AD intersects the surfaces of the ball a second time at point K so that CK is the diameter of the ball.
Find the volume of the paramid if BD=10, AC=12.
I. G. Venetsky, Yu. I. Sorkin
