Blend [p. 17, 25, 42, 56]

On the first page of the cover you see a photograph of part of a peacock's tail. The bright eyes of the tail feather can change color. This is explained by the fact that the color of the peacock is caused by diffraction. The diffraction coloration of birds and insects is described in the statue of V. I. Arabadzhi, published in this issue of the magazine on p. 18.
