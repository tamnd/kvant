In the case of a multilayer diffraction grating, the condition for the formation of diffraction maxima is described by the equation

$$k\lambda = 2l \cos \theta_0, \tag{5}$$

where $l$ is the distance between adjacent lattice layers. This equation is obtained by determining the path difference $\Delta = \Delta_1 + \Delta_2$ between rays $1$ and $2$ (Fig. 5) and replacing $\Delta$ for the diffraction maxima through $k\lambda$.

Assuming $k = 1$, from expression (5) we obtain

$$\cos \theta_0 = \frac{\lambda}{2l}. \tag{6}$$

It follows that diffraction by a multilayer structure is possible at $2l > \lambda$.
