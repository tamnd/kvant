After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: “Problem «Kvant», M316, M317” or “... F328”. Solutions to problems in each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems, are sent in separate envelopes. Please send assignments from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking your decisions). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (mark on the envelope: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). After forming the task, we indicate to the conversation who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk.

Tasks

M316—M320; F328—F332

M316. a) Prove that the sum of the squares $k$ of consecutive natural numbers cannot be the square of an integer if $k$ is 3, 5, 7 or 9.

6) Come up with 11 consecutive natural numbers whose sum of squares is the square of the integer.

E. G. Gotman

M317*. On some planets, each country borders no more than 7 others. Every country has a reserve of gold. It is required to distribute gold so that each two countries bordering each other differ in the amount of gold by no more than 13 times. Prove that the distribution of gold can be organized so that each country loses no more than half of its gold.

G. V. Egorov

M318. In triangle $ABC$ bissertrices $[AD]$ and $[CE]$ are drawn. Prove that $|CE| \cdot |AB| = |AD| \cdot |BC|$ if and only if at least one of two conditions is satisfied: 1) $|AB| = |BC|$; 2) $\widehat{ABC} = 60^\circ$.

A. P. Sabin

M319. A circle $\gamma$ and a point $P$ inside it are given on the plane. Consider the set of tetrahedra $ABCD$, all four of which are congested, and the triangle $ABC$ is attached to the circle $\gamma$ so that its medians intersect at the point $P$.

a) At what position of the point $P$ inside $\gamma$ do tetrahedra also exist?

6) Prove that the vertices $D$ of such tetrahedra are located at one of two fixed points in space (symmetrical to the relative plane).

I. F. Sharyzin

M320*. What convex $n$-gons can be divided into triangles so that no two of the triangles in the partition have common (completely coinciding) sides? (On figure 1 it is shown that the triangle can be divided in this way.)

A. Pechkovsky

F328. Rigid rods of lengths $l_1$ and $l_2$ are hingedly connected at point $A$. Their free ends move away from each other uniformly with the speeds of the corresponding $v_1$ and $v_2$, directed along one straight line (Fig. 2). Find the acceleration of point $A$ at that moment; when the rods make an angle $90^\circ$ with each other. The movement of the rods occurs in one plane.

B. B. Bukhovtsevo

F329*. Many of you have apparently noticed that the moment you step on wet sand, it becomes lighter. This is due to the fact that the sand becomes drier. But as soon as you take your foot back, the trail left by the foot immediately fills with water. Explain this phenomenon.

F330. In a cylinder under a weightless piston of area $S$ there is air at atmospheric pressure $p_0$ and temperature $T_0$. The internal volume of the cylinder is divided into two equal parts by a fixed horizontal partition with a small response. A load of mass $M$ is placed on the piston, under the influence of which the piston reaches the partition. Find the temperature of the air inside the cylinder if the cylinder walls and the piston do not conduct heat.

B. A. Lugovtsovo

G. L. Komkin

V. G. Serbo
