Tournament of three mathematicians

Three young mathematicians decided to hold a chess tournament among themselves. However, they were somewhat upset by the fact that every time two of them play, the third is forced to watch the battle instead of eating at the board himself.
Then mathematicians showed all their ingenuity and came up with such a cunning arrangement of the boards in which there is no “simple” and all three games are played simultaneously.

The moment we approached the table, very interesting positions appeared, depicted in the diagrams. Everywhere there was a White move. Try to find out how the tournament ended, if chess actions in these positions are present. Note that the results of the tournament were somewhat consistent with how well our players were familiar with the geometry of the chessboard.
