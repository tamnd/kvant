If the cylinder moves with a speed of $v$, then due to the incompressibility of the liquid it must pass in the opposite direction between the cylinder and the walls with a speed of $u$ so that $S_1 v = S_2 u$, that is, $\pi r^2 v = \pi (R^2 - r^2) u$; hence $u = r^2 v / (R^2 - r^2)$. From the law of conservation of energy $\rho \pi r^2 lv^2 / 2 + \rho_0 \pi (R^2 - r^2) lu^2 / 2 = (\rho - \rho_0) \pi r^2 lgh$. Thus, $v = \sqrt{\frac{2gh (\rho / \rho_0 - 1)}{\rho / \rho_0 + r^2 / (R^2 - r^2)}}$; $a = \frac{v^2}{2h} g \left( \frac{\rho / \rho_0 - 1}{\rho / \rho_0 + r^2 / (R^2 - r^2)} \right)$.

To problems «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren"
(see «Kvant», 1975, no. 3)
1. Use the test of divisibility by 3.
2. The size of an object that we see depends not only on its size, but also on the distance at which it is located from us. (You can “obscure” the Moon with a match head.) All objects visible from the varon’s window move the same distance per unit of time. But the same segment appears smaller to us the further it is from us.
3. 18 seconds.
4. When the temperature of the water (and the box) reaches 100 °C, the water boils (at 100 °C the paper does not burn). Subsequently, the temperature of the water (and the box) does not increase, since the body is used to maintain the boiling water.
5. Even one weight at 200 °C is enough. During the first weighing, place a weight at 200 °C on the scale cup and pour all the cereal onto the scale cups so that the scales are balanced. Then on one cup there will be 4 ke 400 °C, on the other - 4 ke 600 °C of cereal. During the second weighing, we similarly scatter 4 ke 600 °C, we get 2 ke 400 °C and 2 ke 200 °C. During the third weighing, from 2 to 200 °C we weigh 200 °C.
