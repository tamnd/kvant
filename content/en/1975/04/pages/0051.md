Option 3

1. Solve inequality $(0,05)^{-\log(x-0,25)}x \geq (2\sqrt{5})^{\log(x-0,25)}(4x-1)$.

2. An equilibrium trapezoid is described around a circle of radius $R$. Find its area if it is known that the distance between the points of contact of the circle with the lateral sides is $R\sqrt{3}$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x - \cos x = \sqrt{4-3(\sin x + \cos x)}$$.

4. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases}
\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y+z} = \frac{1}{a}, \\
\frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x+z} = \frac{1}{b}, \\
\frac{1}{z} + \frac{1}{x+y} = \frac{1}{c}.
\end{cases}$$

5. Given a cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of edge $AA'$, and point $N$ is the midpoint of edge $A'B'$. The plane drawn through the points $M$, $N$ and $C$ splits the cube into two parts. Find the ratio of the volumes of these parts.

$$tg x + ctg x = 2(\sin 2x + \cos 2x).$$
