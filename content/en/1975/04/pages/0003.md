On the first page of the cover you see the famous fountain “Friendship of Peoples” (Moscow, VDNH). The jets of flowing water have a very characteristic shape - a parabola shape. You can read about this curve and some of its properties in the article “Parabola” (p. 9). Please note that compared to this photo, the parabolas in the stat are depicted “golden down”, but this does not bother you with the path: the interesting thing is the curve itself, and not its location.
