## Daily allowance
Earth rotation

The article by Ya. A. Smorodinsky “Coriolis Force” (see p. 2 of this issue of the magazine) describes in detail the force of inertia that acts on bodies moving relative to a rotating reference frame. Such a “natural” system is, first of all, the Earth. There are many phenomena, for example, the deflection of freely falling bodies, unequal erosion of banks by rivers, the occurrence of air vortices and winds, etc., which are explained by the action of the Coriolis force.

Historically, the first experiment that confirmed the rotation of the Earth around its axis was Foucault's experiment. This article talks about a simple experiment that clearly demonstrates the daily rotation of the Earth.

Imagine a large cylindrical vessel ending in a funnel (Fig. 1). The vessel is filled with water and suspended from the ceiling using a long cord. At the initial moment, when the hole in the funnel is closed, the vessel is at rest relative to the Earth. What happens if the hole is opened? For simplicity of reasoning, we will assume that the experiment is carried out at the north pole of the Earth. In the place of the vessel where the funnel is located, the flowing water has not only a vertical, but also a horizontal velocity projection.

Let us denote by $\mathbf{v}_0$ the horizontal projection of the speed of water relative to the vessel. The value $\mathbf{v}_0$ depends on the height of the water column above the given

level in the funnel and from the distance to the axis of the vessel.

The presence of speed $v_0$ causes the emergence of the Coriolis force. figure 2 shows the directions of the Earth's rotation, velocities $v_0$ and Coriolis forces $F_К$ for a certain layer of water. The Coriolis force acting on each small element of the mass of water has a rotational moment relative to the axis of symmetry of the vessel. This causes the water in the vessel to spin. Since there is friction between the water and the walls of the vessel, the vessel will also begin to move, and the direction of movement of the vessel coincides with the direction of rotation of the Earth.

As water flows out of the container, the magnitude of the velocity $v_0$ decreases and hence the Coriolis force acting on the water decreases (since $F_К \sim v_0$). What will be the nature of the movement of water under the influence of a variable force $F_К$? The magnitude of the acceleration imparted by this force decreases, while the speed of rotation of the water increases, but the further, the slower.

The rotation of the vessel causes the thread on which it is suspended to twist. In this case, a moment of elastic deformation forces arises, which counteracts the twisting of the thread, and therefore the rotation of the vessel.
