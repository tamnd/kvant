The answer can only be that in the rotating reporting system there is another force that not only compensates for the centrifugal force of inertia directed outward, but also imparts to the body centripetal acceleration directed towards the axis of rotation. Let us exclude $r$ from the expression for the magnitude of the Corilis force. The linear speed of a point located at a distance $r$ from the rotation axis is equal to $\omega r$, and therefore we can write that

$$F_K = -2m\omega v_{кас}$$

(the minus sign still means that the Corilis force in our case is directed towards the rotation axis).
