## Novosibirsk State University

We have already written about Novosibirsk State University (see «Kvant», 1973, No. 5). Let us now tell you in more detail about the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of NSU.

Graduates of this faculty have deep knowledge in such fundamental areas of mathematics as mathematical analysis, algebra and logic, and differential equations. They can take an active part in the development of new trends in technology and scientific methods necessary to solve important national economic problems.

The Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of NSU has two departments: the Department of Mathematics and the Department of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics.

The Department of Mathematics trains specialists in the following sections: differential equations and equations of mathematical physics, theory of functions and functional analysis, mathematical logic, programming, algebra, geometry and topology, probability theory, mathematical economics, and computational mathematics.

The specialization of students in the Department of Mathematics is concentrated around the main areas of modern mathematics.

The Department of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics trains specialists in the following sections: hydrodynamics, gas dynamics, theory of elasticity and plasticity, dynamic meteorology, mathematical geophysics, computational mathematics, theoretical cybernetics.

Students of the department of applied mathematics and mechanics receive a broad mathematical education and master the basics of a number of applied disciplines; they acquire skills in formulating and solving problems arising in various fields of technology.

Mathematics

**Mechanics, mathematics, physics and economics faculties**

Option 1

1. Solve inequality

$$2^{4x+2}\cdot4^{-x^2}-3\cdot2^{-x^2+2x+2}+5\geqslant0.$$

2. Tangents and a secant are drawn from a point outside the circle, and the points of tangency and the points of intersection of the secant with the circle are the vertices of a certain trapezoid. Find the ratio of the bases of the trapezoid if it is known that the angle between the tangents is $60°$.

3. Solve the equation

$$\sqrt{\cos2x-\sin4x}=\sin x-\cos x.$$

4. For what real $a$ system

$$\begin{cases}x^2=y^2=z^2,\\x+y+z=3a,\\xy=z-\dfrac{4}{9}\end{cases}$$

has real solutions satisfying the condition $xyz\geqslant0$? Find these solutions.

5. In a triangular pyramid $SABC$, edge $SA$ is perpendicular to the plane of the triangle $ABC$, $AC=CB=a$, $AS=AB=\,=a\sqrt2$. A plane is drawn through the middle of edge $AC$, perpendicular to edge $SB$. Find the distance from the vertex $A$ to this plane.

Option 2

1. Solve inequality

$$5\cdot9^{2x+2}\cdot3^{x^2-1}-2^4\cdot3^{\frac{x^2}{2}+1}\cdot9^x+1\leqslant0.$$

2. In the trapezoid $ABCD$, the side $CD$ is perpendicular to the base $AD$, $BC=a$, $AD=b$, $a<b$. Based on $AD$ there is a point $M$ such that $MB$ is perpendicular to $AC$ and $MC$ is perpendicular to $BD$. Find the height of the trapezoid.

3. Solve the equation

$$\dfrac{\sin x-\cos3x}{1+\sin3x}=\dfrac{1}{2}.$$

4. For which real $m$ are both roots of the equation

$$2y^2-(3m+1)y+m^2+m=0$$

satisfy the inequality

$$y^2-my-3m-1\geqslant0?$$

5. At the base of the pyramid $SABC$ lies a right triangle $ABC$. Edge $SB$ is perpendicular to the plane of the base, $SB=AB=AC=a$. A plane is drawn through the middle $AB$ perpendicular to $SC$. Find the cross-sectional area.
