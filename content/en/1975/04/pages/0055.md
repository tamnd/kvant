⟦folio 53⟧

Tasks

1. Four young philatelists Mitya, Tolya, Sasha and Petya bought postage stamps. Each of them bought stamps from only one country, and two of them bought Soviet stamps, one Bulgarian, and one Czech. It is known that Mitya and Tolya bought stamps from two different countries. Mitya and Sasha, Petya and Sasha, Petya and Mitya, and Tolya and Sasha also bought stamps from different countries. In addition, it is known that Mitya did not buy Bulgarian stamps. Determine which country brands each of them bought.

2. Why should you try to “enter” the water vertically when diving?

3. Let $x$ be the sum of the cubes of ten consecutive natural numbers, and $y$ be the sum of these same natural numbers. Prove that $x^2 - y^2$ is divisible by 300.

4. Two vessels of equal volumes are filled to the top with warm water. To cool the water, place one vessel on ice, and place a large piece of ice on top of the other vessel. In which vessel will the water cool faster?

5. I am as many times younger than my business as I am older than my sister. How old am I if my sister is not yet seven years old, and I and my child are already 84 years old?
