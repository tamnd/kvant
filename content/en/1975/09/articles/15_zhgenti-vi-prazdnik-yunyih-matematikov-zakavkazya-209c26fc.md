## VI celebration of young mathematicians of Transcaucasia

“Once upon a time, many believed
That zero means nothing"—
- ringing voices come from the stage. The sixth celebration of young mathematicians is underway.

This time, schoolchildren from the Physics and Music School named after. Komarov and members of mathematical circles of the Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren named after. B. Dzneladze from Tbilisi, FMS No. 145 from Kyiv and FMS No. 173 from Baku. Guests and active participants in the celebration were members of the editorial board of the journal «Kvant», mathematicians from Moscow and Tbilisi universities.

The holiday was organized by the Trade Union Council of Adjara, the Education Department of the Batumi City Executive Committee, the Batumi City Committee of the Komsomol of Georgia and school No. 7 in Batumi. The head took part in the celebration. Department of the Ministry of Education of Georgia L. S. Mnatobishvili.

The holiday took place in November, but preparations for it began much earlier, in the summer. For the second year in a row, Batumi School No. 7 is organizing a summer mathematics school. In the summer of 1974, at the Beshumi resort, in one of the buildings of the Republican Pioneer Camp, there was a slogan: “You can’t take a step without mathematics!” 35 future eighth-graders of school No. 7 spent the summer in the mountains at an altitude of 2000 m. Members of the editorial board «Kvant», research fellows from Moscow State University and Leningrad State University, studied mathematics, physics and astronomy with them.

Classes with schoolchildren were conducted by professors A. A. Kirillov, V. P. Palamodov and O. S. Berlyand, mathematicians and physicists from Moscow State University M. S. Polyakova, V. L. Gutenmacher, A. N. Zemlyakov, M. L. Kozeltsev, Yu. B. Andreev, mathematicians from Leningrad B. M. Bekker and Yu. I. Ionin, head. Department of Mathematics «Kvant» L. G. Makar-Limanov.

The classes were summed up by two Olympiads. The champions of the 1974 summer school were *Marina Toropygina* and *Viola Angelo*, second places were taken by *Sasha Stupin*, *Amiran Melua*, *Zaza Garibov*, *Ramaz Bezhanidze* and *Ira Martynova*.

Mathematical KVN took place on the day. The jury, chaired by L. S. Mnatobishvili, included representatives of «Kvant», heads of delegations.

According to tradition, the teams were first asked to depict how Newton discovered the law of universal gravitation and “play out” some geometric theorem. For some, the apple fell on Newton, for others it did not fall, and for others there was no apple at all. Well, science historians are still debating whether there was an apple. For the pantomime, the teams chose various theorems: Falles' theorem, theorems on the sum of the angles of a triangle, the theorems on the perpendicular and oblique, and others.

Then the fun part began—solving problems. Here are some of them:

1. Ostap Bender organized the distribution of elephants to the population in the city of Arbatov. 27 union members and 35 non-union members attended the distribution. Union members were given the same number of elephants, and non-union members were given the same (but possibly different) number of elephants. During the distribution it turned out that under this condition it can be carried out in only one way.

What is the greatest number of elephants O. Bender could have?

⟦column⟧

2. There are $k$ high-rise buildings in Moscow. A man drives along the ring highway around Moscow and sees them walking in different orders. Can he see all possible options for following them if: a) $k=3$; b) $k=4$?

3. Prove that a natural number is a perfect square if and only if it has an odd number of divisors (including 1 and the number itself).

The most difficult tasks were offered to team captains. The winner was a student of Kyiv school No. 145 A. Reznikov.

The first place in KVN was taken by the people of Kiev - 39 points; second - the team of the FMS named after. Komarova - 30 points; in third - Batumi - 21 points.

The holiday ended on November 9 with an evening of entertaining mathematics. Schoolchildren of the FMS named after. Komarov showed a production based on the article by M. I. Reitman “Transport Problem” («Kvant», 1974, No. 7).

After this, the students of the school named after. Komarova and members of the mathematical circles of the Palace of Pioneers proposed entertaining problems (see photographs).

Sixth-graders from school No. 7 performed a humorous dramatization “About Zero.”

Students from school No. 7 played the role of mathematical magicians and wizards. They easily guessed the number of brothers, windows in the room, and floor number. The second mathematical trick involving guessing the intended mathematical figure was based on converting numbers from the decimal number system to binary.

In conclusion, the students of school No. 7 showed M. Romanenko’s play “Living Geometry”.

At the closing ceremony of the holiday, the head of the education department of the Batumi City Executive Committee, D. A. Glonti, presented certificates and memorable gifts to the participants of the holiday. The awards were also presented by the editors of the magazine «Kvant». Numbers «Kvant» with autographs of members of the editorial board were received by the teams of school No. 145 of the city of Kyiv for winning the KVN and school No. 7 of the city of Batumi for the good organization of the holiday, as well as schoolchildren who actively participated in the holiday of mathematics: M. Varamashvili, V. Kotaria and V. Maskhulia from Tbilisi, A. Reznikov and I. Shilenkina from Kyiv, A. Georgyan and V. Krasilovsky is from Batumi.

In conclusion, we express the hope that the sixth holiday of young mathematicians will remain in the memory of each of its participants for a long time, and those who were so far only spectators at this holiday will certainly understand that mathematics is a very interesting science and one needs to study it a lot and seriously.

M. I. Zhgenti
