⟦folio 71⟧

Give it to me
Story-task

One Sunday in May, seventh-graders Kolya and Petya arrived at the dacha of Petya’s parents, which was empty at that time, to prepare for the last test in physics.

The day was hot and the room was stuffy.

“You know what,” said Petya, “in this heat my brain doesn’t work well.” Let's turn on the refrigerator and wait until it cools the room a little.

Kolya didn’t mind. An hour after turning on the refrigerator, when the temperature in the room dropped slightly, the guys took up their textbooks.

However, they didn't have to work for long. This time Kolya begged:

“Petya, I didn’t have a good breakfast today, and my appetite is so worked up that physics doesn’t even cross my mind.” It wouldn't hurt for us to have a little snack.

Petya examined the dacha supplies.

— Would you like soft-boiled eggs? he asked. (Kolya nodded his head.) Only... I found two electric drinks, but neither of them had a spiral. Oh, here are some spirals. Maybe we can connect two spirals in series and put them on one liter so that the water boils faster?

Kolya thought about it.

“In my opinion,” he said, “the water will boil faster if the spiral is not lengthened, but rather shortened.”
