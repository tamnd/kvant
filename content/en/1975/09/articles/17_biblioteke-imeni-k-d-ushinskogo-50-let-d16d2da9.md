Now we will briefly talk about several works that were not reported at the meeting, but, in the opinion of the jury, they deserve high praise.

Igor Gavrilov (Omsk, s. sh. No. 64, 10th grade) presented a report on the topic: “Study of the influence of the temperature regime of the ZIL-130 engine cooling system on its power and economic indicators and the toxicity of exhaust gases.” The report describes the results of two years of work, in which the author was directly involved, from the creation of an experimental setup to the receipt of some specific results. The work was carried out at the Siberian Autonomous Road Institute.

“Crystals, their role in nature and science - this is the title of the abstract by Lyuba Barabatseva (Sverdlovsk, village no. 262, 9th grade). This is a very detailed and informative story about the structure and properties of crystals.

Several abstracts were devoted to the history of physics. Among them it is worth noting the work “M. V. Lomonosov is the founder of the molecular-kinetic theory,” which was written by Alexander Pavlom (Chita, school No. 42, 10th grade).

By decision of the jury in the physics section, the following were awarded diplomas: L. Altukhova, S. Amelkin, L. Barabatseva, V. Volinsky, I. Gavrilov, S. Golub, A. Kipchatov, D. Morozov, A. Pavlom, A. Poltorak.

The following scientific societies of students received certificates for good work: section of experimental physics p. w. No. 2, Dzerzhinsk, Gorky region, scientific society «Kvant» p. w. No. 13 Saratov, scientific society "Photon" p. w. No. 70, Sverdlovsk, section of theoretical physics of the scientific and technical society of Norilsk.

The magazine «Kvant» awards were received by: O. Barakin, M. Popov and the scientific society of students from. w. No. 48 Volgograd.

At the closing of the meeting, the high quality of the presented works was emphasized. 79 of them have already found various applications in the national economy, 37 works of schoolchildren, completed in scientific societies, have been published in magazines and collections. Some of the works are presented at the exhibition.

The delegates of the rally expressed great heartfelt gratitude to the teachers who laid the foundation of knowledge for the participants of the rally, and to the scientists who directly supervised the work of scientific societies of schoolchildren.

V. N. Berezin, A. N. Vilenkin
B. G. Pshenicher, V. A. Tikhomiroda

The K. D. Ushinsky Library is a rich repository of pedagogical books, magazines and newspapers, and well-equipped reading rooms... But, above all, it is a faithful friend and assistant to other pedagogical libraries, including school ones.

The library provides extensive bibliographic services to teachers, studies and develops problems of library science and scientific bibliographic information.

We congratulate all library workers and wish them great creative success in their noble work.
