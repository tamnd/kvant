## WINNERS OF THE QUANTUM COMPETITION

The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» established special awards for the most interesting solutions to problems included in the “Problem Book «Kvant»”. During the 1974-75 academic year, the editors received over 3,000 letters with solutions to problems from the Problem Book «Kvant». Among the authors of these letters, the editors selected schoolchildren who regularly sent particularly successful and original solutions. They are rewarded with a one-year subscription to «Kvant» magazine for 1976. In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the All-Union Olympiad, these schoolchildren were admitted to the republican rounds of the 1976 All-Union Olympiad along with the winners of city and regional olympiads in mathematics and physics.

1. H. ABDULLIN (Almaty)
2. G. AIZIN (Brest)
3. M. GEDALIN (Tbilisi)
4. G. GOLUBTSOVA (Moscow)
5. O. GODIN (Simferopol)
6. V. GUSEINOV (Nakhichevan)
7. M. IMERLISHVILI (Tbilisi)
8. S. LIFITS (Kharkov)
9. I. SOKOLOV (Moscow)
10. Y. PHILOSOPHOV (Saratov)
11. S. FINASHIN (Leningrad)
12. B. YATSALO (village Morochno, Rivne region, Ukrainian SSR)

For successful participation in the IX All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad, an annual subscription for 1976 to the magazine «Kvant» is awarded to:

1. G. ABDULLAYEV (Kirovobad)
2. L. ARBUZOV (Norilsk)
3. A. AUZINSH (Riga)
