The invariance of the laws of nature does not mean the coincidence of the observed phenomena themselves. However, the same phenomenon, considered in different reporting systems, is usually described by different quantitative characteristics. So, despite the fact that the laws of the fall of a body in a train, moving uniformly and rectilinearly, and at the station are the same, an object that has fallen from a carriage shelf moves differently in the reporting systems associated with the train and with the station. In the first case, it falls along a vertical straight line, and in the second, along a parabola. The reason for this is due to the different conditions of the conditions. A body that did not have a horizontal velocity projection on the train has it in the reporting system associated with the station.
