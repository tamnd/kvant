⟦folio 66⟧

Periodic face-to-face consultations are also included.

Servants call for face-to-face tests and consultations; they actively and systematically study on telephones. Those who successfully pass the tests will receive a Certificate of Completion from the Moscow Preparatory Telephones at the end of the academic year.

In the coming school year, ministers will offer two daily programs in mathematics, two in physics and one in the Russian language. For the convenience of students (especially those who work different shifts), these programs will be repeated in the same week, but on different days and at different times.

Classes on telephones will consist of two cycles: the main one (it will be legislated in May 1976) and the defense-consulting one (it will be broadcast in June 1976). In the lessons of the second cycle, they consider issues that cause the most difficulties when studying and repeating educational material.

The reception area for programs “For applicants to universities” in the 1975/76 academic year expanded compared to previous years - these programs will be able to be watched by residents of Moscow, the Belarusian USSR, the Lithuanian USSR, the region - Moscow, Volgodsk, Gorky, Ivanovo, Kalinin, Kaliningrad, Kaluga, Kostroma, Lipetsk, Ryazan, Smolensk, Tula and Yaroslavskaya.

Moscow preparatory telephones accept everyone - both those who have already completed secondary education, and students - graduates of school, vocational schools, and secondary specialized institutions. Admission to telephones is carried out without a competition.

To enroll, the minister must send in one envelope to the course address the “Questionnaire - Application”, “Registration Card” and an envelope with his return address.

The “application form” is drawn up on a general postal card. On the front side of it you should write your address and telephone number (home or office), if available. The reverse (blank) side of the card is filled with answers to questions, a sample of which we provide. All entries must be made parallel to the narrow side of the card. In the upper right corner, look at the letters corresponding to the departments in which you are going to fill out (if you intend to fill out in all three departments, then write three letters: “F” - physics, “M” - mathematics, “R” - Russian language). Under the answers to the questions, write the text of the application for admission (see sample) listing the departments you have chosen, put the date and signature.

The “registration card” is also issued on a postcard. Be sure to write your address on the front side; clean design as indicated on the sample. Glue a photo (size 3 cm × 4 cm) to the upper left corner. The inscription “Registration Card” is written one centimeter below the edge of the postcard. All recordings are performed parallel to the narrow side. For each branch, a separate “Registration Card” is filled out; in the upper right corner, one of the letters “F”, “M”, “R” corresponding to the selected branch is written.

On letters with applications addressed to courses, the minister must write in the upper left corner of the envelope the same letters “F”, “M”, “R”) as in the “Application Questionnaire”.

In an envelope with the minister’s return address, he will be sent a “Notice of Enrollment on Telephones” and “Annotation of Telephone Knowledge.” The “Notification of enrollment on telephones” indicates the number of the code attached to the employee, the “Annotation” contains the lesson program, literature recommendations, guidelines for completing homework and registration rules, tips on how to independently record your success, etc.
