To the note “524288 Japanese puzzles”
(see “Quant” No. 1, 4th pp. region)

The editors have received many letters with solutions to “related” problems. But only four readers succeeded in solving the proposed puzzle. These are S. Zh. Levitin (Dnepropetrovsk), L. A. Luzina (Leningrad), A. S. Bezborodov (Ivanovo), V. N. Dobnikov (Omsk).

Two more readers completed the puzzle by turning over the figures: V. N. Duplenko (Dnepropetrovsk) and S. Kardashevsky (Makeeva).

Puzzles of this type are called "polyominoes" (similar to dominoes). They were invented by the famous mathematician S. V. Golomb. You can read about them in books: S. V. Golomb. Polyimino (World, 1975) and M. Gardner. Mathematical short stories (Mir, 1974).

It remains to be said that neither Golomb nor Gardner managed to collect puzzles of the “7Δ” type, namely they were proposed by the reader of Quantum. They were first folded in Japan. At figure 3 the solution of S. Zh. Levitin is given.
