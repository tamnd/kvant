After graduating from the university (1917), A.K. Kharadze, on the recommendation of D.F. Egorov, was left at the university to prepare for a professorship, but in 1918 he began working at Tbilisi University and took doctoral exams in Tbilisi.

Archil Kirillovich remembers his first lecture at the university - on higher algebra - with satisfaction. Lectures on higher algebra, given by Kharadze in the first years of his teaching career, formed the basis of his textbook in the Naruzin language. Archil Kirillovich is also the author of the first textbook in the Naruzin language on higher mathematics for students.

The scientific interests of A.K. Kharadze are grouped around classical questions of analysis and function theory. They are reflected in the monograph “Mean Value Theorems Applied to Polynomials” (1947) and in numerous journal articles.

Archil Kirillovich is a brilliant lecturer. High culture of speech and clarity of presentation will immediately captivate the listener. The author of these lines especially remembered two lectures by Archil Kirillovich. The first is an introductory lecture for first-year students (1938). The lecturer spoke with inspiration about mathematics, its abstract nature, and the interconnection of its different branches. The second is a lecture on the topic “What is differential calculus”, given in 1963 to a student in Tbilisi.

At first, Archil Kirillovich graduated from the department of algebra and geometry, and later headed the department of mathematical analysis, which he still heads today.

The first graduate of the faculty was in 1924. It consisted of one person - Levan Petrovich Gokilli (1901-1975). In 1935 he defended his doctoral dissertation, and in 1961 he was elected a corresponding member of the Academy of Sciences of the GSSR.

L.P. Gokilli made a significant contribution to the training of group mathematicians. The author of these lines and many of his colleagues still remember his original lectures on mathematical analysis.
