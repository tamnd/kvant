The message of Serey Golub (Chelyabinsk, s. sh. No. 69, 10th grade) “Study of the light resistance of polymers” aroused great interest. Currently, polymer materials are widely used in technology and in everyday life. To obtain oxidized polymers during the polymerization process, water-antorganic dyes are added as additives, which affect the physicochemical properties of the polymers, in particular their light fastness. Under the influence of light, processes of destruction of both the dye and the polymer itself occur. To study the light resistance of polymers, that is, their ability to retain their original properties under the influence of light, was the goal of the work done by a group of students under the guidance of employees of the Department of Experimental and Theoretical Physics of the Chelyabinsk State Pedagogical Institute.

The next day the section was held at the Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova. The institute's senior researcher, Doctor of Physics and Mathematics N.S. Babaev, told the participants about the main directions of the institute's work.

The most interesting messages of this day were the messages of Dimitry Morozov (Dzerzhinsk, Gorky region, rural highway No. 2, 9th class) and Aleksadr Kipchatov (Saratov, rural highway No. 9, 10th class).

D. Morozov's report is called “Electronic instruments for measuring mechanical quantities in chemical engineering.” First of all, Dmitry spoke about the work of the experimental physics section of the Young Physicists Club, of which he is a member. This section was created at secondary school No. 2 in Dzerzhinsk 10 years ago, led by physics teacher Lev Vasilyevich Pigaltsin. The children at the school have created a programmed learning room in which they can monitor the knowledge of students in all grades and in all subjects. Many demonstration and laboratory equipment in physics were made during the work of the section. And this academic year, contact was established with the Research Institute of Chemical Engineering. In particular, the work described in the report is being carried out (it has not yet been completed) on the instructions of this institute. One of the departments of the institute is working on the creation of an explosion meter - a device for measuring the explosion of various liquids. The design being developed resembles a medical syringe. D. Morozov and S. Zolotov were asked to design electronic devices to measure the speed of movement of the explosion meter from the capillary and the fluid pressure on the explosion meter piston. Now these devices have already been made and are being tested.

A. Kipchatov, in his report “MHD generation of electric energy,” spoke about the magnetohydidynamic principle of obtaining electric energy. What is this principle? It is known that when a conductor moves in a magnetic field, an electric current will appear in the conductor. d. With. induction. This is how a common electric current generator works. If the solid conductor is replaced by an electrically conductive gas moving at high speed, then it will also have an inductance of electrical current. The report discussed the history of creation, various types of MHD generators, and the challenges that scientists faced when working on the creation of such generators.

The third day of the section's work took place at the Institute of Creative Body Physics of the USSR Academy of Sciences. The participants were warmly greeted by the scientists of this institute. The head of the laboratory of physics and mathematics, E. M. Nadgorpin, and Doctor of Physics and Mathematics, Lenin Prize laureate V. L. Brode, told the children what the institute’s scientists are working on, what problems exist today in the physics of the creative body. For example, studying the properties of quasistries (phonons, holes, extons, dislocations) is one of the areas of scientific research. The fundamental practical problem that the institute is working on is to learn how to make creative bodies with directional macro properties.

During the experiment, we visited various laboratories and departments of the institute and got acquainted with the work of the computer center.
