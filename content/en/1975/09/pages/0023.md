Further, the chess player is haunted by sporting failures; he can be proud of individual achievements and live in the beauty of chess. For him, in every Turin there is a prize “for the most beautiful game” (further this prize is not officially established).

The world of chess is a place for all kinds of people. Kruskop, my first teacher - now they say “coach”, before they used to say “teacher”, and I liked it better - was not apparently a chess player. He had the first category. But he was just a teacher, an educator, he loved chess and loved children. And it is no coincidence that many of his students devoted their lives to chess in one way or another. I think this man was happy. He showed children the beauty of chess...

— Mikhail Nekhemievich, already at the age of 23 you entered the golden chronicle of chess. Recognition, words, admiration - you achieved all this at a very early age.

— A chess player, like, apparently, a mathematician, can never tell himself that he has achieved everything. That would be the end. Indeed, after winning a major tournament, after winning the world champion title, a moment of emptiness comes, it becomes a little boring. But time passes, and the appetite for combat awakens, and the chess hunger disappears. New tours, new plans - not only sports, but also creative. Fortunately, chess is changeable. Today you are a world champion, tomorrow you are already an ex-champion. There is always something to fight for.

— You can’t say that your chess path was a success. Has it ever happened that chess bothered you and made you angry? Have you ever wished that you had given them your whole life?

— Yesterday, during a game with grandmaster Vaganian, there was a similar moment. The opponent moved his knight to g1, and suddenly it turned out that I no longer had a win... I was incredibly upset. Speaking from the heart, you see, chess is my world. Not a house, not a fortress in which I hide from life’s anxieties, but rather a world - a world in which I live a full life, in which I express myself... I love the atmosphere of mothers, tournaments, chess discussions. I can't imagine being on a desert island without a partner, unless Friday had to play chess with me. I don't like playing behind closed doors. I love the public.
