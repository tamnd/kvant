6) (see Fig. 6 stats). On the “main” interval $[-\pi, \pi]$ the equation of function $u(t)$ has the form

$$u_0 = |t|, t \in [-\pi, \pi].$$

Shifting the graph $u_0$ along the x-axis, we get

$$u_n = |t - 2n\pi|, t \in [\pi(2n-1), \pi(2n+1)].$$

If $n$ runs over all integer values, then the resulting equation is the equation of the exclusive function $u(t)$. Note that the equality holds

$$u(t) = \arccos(\cos t) = |t - 2n\pi|,$$

$$t \in [\pi(2n-1), \pi(2n+1)]$$

c) (see Fig. 7). On the "main" interval $\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right]$ the equation has the equation

$$u_0 = \begin{cases} t, t \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]; \\ -t + \pi, t \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right]. \end{cases}$$

Shifting the graph by $n2\pi$, we get

$$u_n = \begin{cases} t - 2n\pi, \\ t \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi, \frac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi\right]; \\ -(t - 2n\pi) + \pi, \\ t \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2} + 2n\pi\right]. \end{cases}$$

After conversion, two lines can be replaced with one:

$$u_m = (-1)^m [t - m\pi],$$

$$t \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2} + (m-1)\pi, \frac{\pi}{2} + m\pi\right],$$

$$(m = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots).$$

(For $m = 2n$ the first row is obtained; for $m = 2n + 1$ the second.) The equality holds:

$$u(t) = \arcsin(\sin t) = (-1)^m [t - m\pi],$$

$$t \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2} + (m-1)\pi, \frac{\pi}{2} + m\pi\right],$$

$$m = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots.$$

3. On $[0, 2\pi]$ the function has the equation

$$u_0 = \begin{cases} A \sin t, t \in [0, \pi]; \\ 0, t \in [\pi, 2\pi]. \end{cases}$$

Equation of all functions at $n = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots$

$$u(t) = \begin{cases} A \sin(t - n2\pi), \\ t \in [2\pi n, \pi(2n+1)]; \\ 0, t \in [\pi(2n+1), \pi(2n+2)]. \end{cases}$$

Two lines can be replaced with one:

$$u(t) = \frac{1 + (-1)^m}{2} A \sin t,$$

$$t \in [m\pi(m+1)\pi],$$

$$m = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \ldots.$$

4. The graph of the function $S(t)$ on $[0, \pi]$ is obtained by shifting this sine line: on $\frac{\pi}{2}$ – to the right (along the x-axis) and on $A$ – up (along the ordinate axis). That's why

$$S_0 = A \sin\left(t - \frac{\pi}{2}\right) + A = -A \cos t + A,$$

$$t \in [0, \pi].$$

Shifting the graph $S_0$ by $n\pi$ to the right and by $n \cdot 2A$ up, we obtain the equation of the function:

$$S(t) = -A \cos(t - n\pi) + A(2n+1),$$

$$t \in [n\pi, (n+1)\pi],$$

$$n = 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots.$$
