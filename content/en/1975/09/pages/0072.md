⟦folio 70⟧

Tasks

1. a) 50 guests are sitting at a round table, of which 25 are wives. Prove that there is a guest whose both neighbors are wives.

6) Let the woman be 26, and let two guests break their cutlery. Prove that the table can be rotated so that both broken utensils end up with the wife.

2. Instead of letters starting with figure, you need to select numbers (the same letters correspond to the same numbers) so that the specified matches are fulfilled.

3. Three comrades Petya, Tolya and Vitya approached the parking lot of automatic rifles and motorized vehicles. Admiring the cars, Petya, having nothing better to do, counted all the vehicles - there were 45 of them. Tolya counted all the wheels - there were 115 of them. There were two times fewer motorized scooters with a sidecar than there were motorized scooters without a sidecar. How many cars were there in the parking lot and how many motorized scooters?

4. Write down the first 10 prime numbers one after the other in ascending order. In the resulting multi-digit number, cross out half of the digits so that the number formed by the remaining digits is a) the smallest; b) the most.

5. A small vessel containing water was lowered into a pan of water. The pan is put on gas, the water in the pan begins to boil. Will water boil in a small vessel?
