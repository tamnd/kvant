New books

In this issue we continue to publish summaries of books published in 1975 that are of interest to our readers. The next book will be published in the third quarter of 1975 (orders can be sent through Book by Mail stores).

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Babinskaya I. L. Problems of mathematical Olympiads. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 28 kopecks.
The collection contains problems in arithmetic, algebra and geometry. It is composed of tasks recommended for cloud and resource Olympiads, and tasks for cloud and all-round Olympiads.
The collection contains a large number of problems designed for middle grades (starting from fourth grade). All problems have answers and instructions or detailed solutions.
The book can be successfully used in the work of mathematical circles, in the preparation and conduct of Olympiads.
It is intended for secondary school students, mathematics teachers, and leaders of mathematics clubs.

Publishing house "Mir"

2. Bizam D., Herzer J. Nera and moose. Per. from Hungarian Volume 21 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 29 k.
Book by Hungarian mathematicians D. Bizam and J. Herzer

the price is dedicated to logical tasks.
Using user-directed means, the authors teach the reader the ability to think consistently and solve problems, “thinking, but not calculating.” The book is equipped with a carefully developed system of special indexes that help to orientate the specific tasks.
The book is of interest to the widest range of readers - lovers of mathematics.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Elliot L., Wilox W. Physics. Per. from English, ed. 3-e. Volume 51 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 1 rub. 25 k.
The book is an elementary textbook on physics, which is intended for a reader who is new to physics and has the most basic information from mathematics. The method of presentation of materials is thought out so that the reader, moving from the simplest and most inculcating concepts to more complex ones, constantly ponders an essential physical phenomenon, searches for and finds the right solutions himself, and is an active participant in the process of learning and mastering the basic laws of physics.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers: school students, people engaged in self-education, and is of great interest for prospective physics students in high school.
2. Yavorsky B. M., Seleznev Yu. A. A reference guide to physics for high school students, college applicants and self-education. Volume 27 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 96 kopecks.
The “Reference Guide” provides definitions of all basic physical concepts, formulates physical laws and briefly explained phenomena.
The manual contains information on all sections of the physics course that are studied in high school and secondary specialized education. Some chapters provide examples of problem solving.
The Physics Guide is intended for high school students. They can be used by applicants in preparation for sample university exams, as well as by those who are interested in physics and are engaged in self-education.
3. Elementary textbook of physics, vol. II. Electricity and magnetism, ed. G. S. Landsberg ed. 9th. Volume 30 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 94 kopecks.
The advantage of the course is an in-depth study of the physical side of the development of processes in nature and technology. The materials are presented in simple, clear language. The book is richly illustrated.
Designed for prospective physicists and high school students. can serve as a guide for self-education.
4. Eggel F. Yu. Treasure of the starry sky. Guide to Creatures and the Moon, ed. 3-e, corrected. and additional Volume 20 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 40 k.
A popular story about the starry sky. The reader learns from the book about the brightest stars and the most interesting celestial bodies located in a particular constellation, about dividing the starry sky into constellations, how to find constellations, which constellations are visible in winter, spring, summer and autumn.
