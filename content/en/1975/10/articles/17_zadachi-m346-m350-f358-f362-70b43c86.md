Tasks

M346 — M350; F358 — F362

M346. Point K is the midpoint of side AB of square ABCD, and point L divides diagonal AC in the ratio 3:1 (Fig. 1). Prove that angle KLD is a right angle.

Yu. G. Bodamurov (10th grade student, Kumaisi)

M347. Two people play this game. The first one guesses two numbers from 1 to 25, and the second one must guess them. He can name any two numbers from 1 to 25 and find out from the first how many of the numbers he named - 0.1 or 2 - coincide with the mystery ones. In what minimum number of questions can he definitely determine the hidden numbers?

A. A. Grizoryan

M348. A 10x10 table contains numbers from 1 to 100 in order. Then in each row and in each column, exactly half of the numbers have a minus sign. Prove that in the resulting table the sum of all numbers is equal to zero.

S. M. Adeev

M349. What condition must the length of a side of a triangle satisfy in order for a triangle composed of a) altitudes, 6) medians, c) bisectors of a given triangle to be similar to the given one?

A. P. Savin

M350*. The bishop begins to move from the white corner square of the chessboard of dimensions n×m (n and m greater than 1). Having reached the edge of the board, the bishop

Rotates at right angles (Fig. 2). Once in the corner, he stops.

a) For what $n$ and $m$ will the bishop go around all the white squares on the board?

b) How many squares will he go around on the board $n \times m$?

Consider boards of size $10 \times 15, 10 \times 25, 15 \times 25$ as examples.

E. Ya. Gik, A. V. Zhornitsky

F358. A non-uniform rod of length $l$ can stand against a vertical wall, forming an angle of at least $45^\circ$ with the floor. The friction coefficient of the rod on the floor and on the wall is $\sqrt{3}$. At what height is the center of gravity of the rod?

F359. In a cylindrical vessel under the piston there is saturated water vapor at a temperature of $t = 20^\circ\text{C}$. During the isothermal slow movement of the piston into the cylinder, an amount of heat of $Q = 20$ kcal was removed. How much work was done by external forces acting on the piston?

F360. Acceleration is measured using a curved tube filled with water containing an air bubble (Fig.3). The tube is bent in a circular arc. How is the position of the bubble related to the acceleration of the tube?

F361. A straight conductor of length $l$ and mass $m$ is suspended on two stiffness springs $k$ in a horizontally uniform magnetic field with an indication $B$ (Fig. 4). When the key $K$ is closed, the capacitor $C$, charged to a potential difference $U$, is closed to the conductor and discharged. In this case, vibrations of the conductor occur. Determine the amplitude of these oscillations if the discharge time of the capacitor is much less than the oscillation period of the conductor.

F362. A layer capacitor consists of three metal parallel plates of area $S$. The spaces between the plates are filled with dielectrics with dielectric constants $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ and resistivities $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$. Thicknesses of the dielectric layer are $d_1$ and $d_2$. The capacitor is under constant voltage $U$. Determine the charge of the middle plate at a steady current in the circuit.
