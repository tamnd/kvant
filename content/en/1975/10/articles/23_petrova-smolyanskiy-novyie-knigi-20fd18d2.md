⟦folio 55⟧

New books

In this issue we publish summaries of books published in the fourth quarter of 1975. Orders for books must be placed through specialized stores or “Book by mail” stores.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Markushevich A. I. Celje funkits. 2nd edition. Volume 5 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.

All the most important functions studied in a high school mathematics course are either integers (polynomial, exponential function, sine, cosine), or quotients of two entire functions (fractional-rational function, tangent, cotangent, etc.), or a finite inverse of the above. However, elementary functions are only the simplest representatives of a broad class of entire functions.

This book introduces the reader who has not mastered this theory to the most important properties of entire functions. The book is intended for all persons wishing to expand and deepen their mathematical knowledge.

2. Lysenko V.I. Nikolai Ivanovich Fuss (1755-1825). Volume 7 l., circulation 10,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

The book covers the life, scientific and pedagogical activities of mathematician, mechanic, astronomer Nikolai Ivanovich Fuss (1755-1825), a full member of the St. Petersburg Academy of Sciences, its permanent secretary, collaborator and close associate of the famous Leonhard Euler. The book uses materials from the Archive of the USSR Academy of Sciences and rare printed sources, provides an overview of the works of N. I. Fuss, and provides an extensive bibliography.

The book is intended for all lovers of the history of mathematics.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

3. Reader on the history of mathematics. Edited by A. P. Yushkevich. Volume 23 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 90 kopecks.

This book contains a selection of original texts on arithmetic, algebra, number theory and geometry, characterizing the development of these areas of mathematics, starting with ancient Babylon and ancient Egypt.

The book will be useful to mathematics teachers, high school students, and anyone interested in mathematics and its history.

4. Kordemsky B. A. Mathematics studies randomness. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 38 kopecks.

The book is a semi-artic introduction to probability theory. It is designed for high school students. Using specific examples taken from real life and various fields of science, the reader gradually becomes acquainted with his own image of the world of random events, with their basic laws.

The manual talks about the creators of science, giving a brief overview of the initial stages of its development. At the end of each chapter you have access to this set of tutorials for independent solutions.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Elementary physics textbook. Edited by G. S. Landsberg. Volume III. (Vibrations and waves, optics, atomic structure.)

9th edition. Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 13 k.

The advantage of the course is the depth of presentation of the physical side of development and technology. The material is presented in simple, clear language. The book was rich in use.

Designed for physics teachers and high school students. can serve as a valuable tool for self-education.

2. Grebennikov E. A., Ryabov Yu. A. Poshk and the discovery of planets. Volume 8 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.

The book tells in a popular form how three planets of the solar system were discovered, invisible to the naked eye - Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. The reader will learn about the laws governing the motion of solar system bodies and how these laws have been applied to the search for new planets. Separate chapters of the book are devoted to the discovery of small planets—astroids, as well as the search for planets closer to the Sun than Mercury and farther from the Sun than Pluto.

3. Idelson N.I. Sketches on the history of planetary theory. Volume 30 l., circulation 20,000 copies, price 2 rubles. 20 k.

The book by the famous Soviet astronomer Professor N.I. Idelson consists of separate sources of scientific essays, studies, in which he talks in a fascinating way about the classical theories of celestial mechanics: the theory of gravity, the theory of the figure of the Earth, the theory of the movements of celestial bodies.
