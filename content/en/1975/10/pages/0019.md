- Will our gun not reach them?
- No, it’s not enough just from this_maxina. Now, if only she could be rolled onto that platform! Hey, Jean-Baptiste... Oh, this is more serious,” Etienne groaned, clutching his chest.
- What's wrong with you, monsieur?
- Citizen Etienne, not Monsieur! You are no longer in the Benedictine monastery, boy, but in the revolutionary army. While they're calling me out, roll the cannon with the guys to that area over there.
- Yes, citizen Etienne!
“Wait,” Pierre said together. — Will the site hold up? If it collapses, we'll lose our only gun.
“This is our last chance,” said Etienne, losing consciousness.

- Well, I’ll try! I came to the barricades from the university, from this swamp where mediocrity proliferates. I swear not to return there until professors are banned from lecturing on cheat sheets. So?

- Let's say; but keep in mind that the columns together will only support 4 tons if the load is placed in the center of the site. And we will have to lower the cannon, so that its center of gravity will be at point M - otherwise we won’t be able to shoot through the mansion.
