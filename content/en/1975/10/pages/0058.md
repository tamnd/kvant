Tasks

1. It was a hot day, and four married couples, while walking, drank 44 glasses of lemonade during the day. Anna drank 2 glasses, Maria - 3, Sophia - 4, Daria - 5. Andreev drank the same amount as his wife; Borisov drank twice as many glasses as his wife; Vasilyev is second more than his wife, and Gruzdev drank four times more glasses of lemonade than his wife.
Who is married to whom?
2. Let us take a set of four-digit numbers in which the thousands and tens digits are odd, and the hundreds and ones digits are even, and each of these four digit numbers does not have the same digits. How many numbers from this set are divisible by nine? How much is divisible by three?
3. Petya had one ruble in coins of no more than 10 kopecks. Vasya took one coin of each type from Petya, and Petya had 15 kopecks less. How many coins did Petya have of each denomination, if there were 4 more coins of the highest denomination than all the other coins?
4. In the division example, identical numbers were replaced by identical letters (and different ones by different ones); you see what happened at figure. Try to restore the example.
5. The thermometer is quickly removed from the molten tin. And at the first moment the mercury table rises a little. Explain why this happens.
