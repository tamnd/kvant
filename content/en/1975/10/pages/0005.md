Gorky wrote: “I observed with what modest heroism, with what courage the creators of Russian science experienced painful hunger and cold, I saw how they worked and how they died. My impressions during this time have formed into deep and respectful admiration for you - heroes of free, fearless, exploring thought. I think that Russian scientists, their life and work during the war and blockade gave the world a great lesson in courage and endurance.”

Constant support and assistance to science from the Communist Party and the Soviet government caused an unprecedented scientific upsurge in our country. In a relatively short historical period of time, Soviet physics, mathematics, mechanics and astronomy have reached the most advanced frontiers of world science. Soviet scientists have successfully solved a number of unusually complex scientific and technical problems. They created atomic and hydrogen weapons, forever burying the imperialists' hopes of military superiority. They paved the way for humanity to the peaceful use of atomic energy. Our country's scientists are successfully implementing a diverse program of exploration and use of outer space. They provide enormous assistance in the development and improvement of electronic computers. Thanks to the work of our scientists, entire new areas of physical and mathematical sciences have emerged.

Scientists from all countries are now following the development of Soviet science with great attention. It is not without reason that many scientific journals published in our country are translated and republished in the most developed capitalist countries. The Russian word “sputnik” has entered unchanged into all languages ​​of the world, and the date of the launch of the first Soviet artificial Earth satellite is often considered in foreign countries as the beginning of a new era in the organization of science and education. Physicists from all countries, without translation, use another scientific term - “Tokamak”, which was born in our country. “TORIDAL CHAMBER in a MAGNETIC FIELD” - this is what Soviet scientists called their unusually promising experimental setup for studying hot plasma and controlled thermonuclear reactions. Similar installations have been built and are now being built in many countries.

The USSR Academy of Sciences is the true headquarters of Soviet science. It unites hundreds of leading scientists heading individual areas of modern science in our country. Its numerous institutes and research centers located throughout the Soviet Union employ a huge army of scientists.

For more than a quarter of a century, an unprecedented scientific and technological revolution has been taking place on our planet, covering virtually all areas of science and technology. Before our eyes, science has become a direct productive force of society; it is firmly united with production and has an extremely important, determining influence on it. The progress of production - the improvement of technology, tools, methods of processing materials, methods of controlling not only machines, but also entire factories - these days is inextricably linked with the progress of science. The world of socialism, by actively participating in this revolution, acquires everything necessary to build communism. The fruits of this revolution open the way for us to the material and technical basis of communism and the construction of a communist society.

It is difficult to overestimate the role of science in the construction of communism. After all, such a social system has never existed on Earth before, and we simply have no one here to learn from. Scientific analysis, Marxist-Leninist research methods - this is the compass by which we check the correctness of our path.

Long live Soviet science and its general headquarters - the USSR Academy of Sciences!
