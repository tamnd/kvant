Perpetual motion machine?

Imagine that two balls (let's call them $A$ and $B$) are immersed in a liquid. The density of the liquid is $\rho$, the density of the balls is $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$, respectively, and their volumes are $V_1$ and $V_2$. We will assume that the liquid occupies all space, the balls interact with each other and the liquid by gravitational forces, and their density is such that $\rho_1 < \rho < \rho_2$.

Let us consider the behavior of balls in a liquid. The ball $A$ is subject to an attractive force from the side of the ball $B$ and an attractive force from the “mirror image” of the ball $B$—a ball of liquid of volume $V_2' = V_2$. The forces of attraction of the ball $A$ to all other volumes of liquid are compensated. Since $\rho_2 > \rho$, the resulting force acting on the ball $A$ will be directed towards the ball $B$. The same reasoning shows that we will consider the resulting force acting on the ball $B$, but the values ​​of these resulting forces acting on the ball $B$ will be different.

The child is directed away from the ball $A$. The forces acting on the balls will impart acceleration to them. Thus, ball $B$ will run away from $A$, and ball $A$ will catch up with $B$. By selecting the sizes of the balls and their densities so that the reported accelerations are the same (for example, let the sizes of the balls be the same, and $\rho = \frac{\rho_1 + \rho_2}{2}$), we obtain a system that can be accelerated by smaba to arbitrarily high speeds. But what about the law of conservation of energy? And implus? Is it possible to create a perpetual motion machine in this way?

Alas! A perpetual motion machine will not work. In our reasoning, which is given on the paradox, we scored one more force acting on each ball. Try to find this strength. Find how the balls will actually behave. Consider what will happen if both balls are heavier or lighter than the liquid. You will see remarkable phenomena: in some cases the balls will attract each other, in other cases they will repel each other - just like electric charges. Try to solve this problem as fully as possible, consider all possible options and use your imagination. You will get the satisfaction of a real scientific study.
