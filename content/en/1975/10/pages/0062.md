Way of the fly

The fly landed on the top left square of the chessboard (see figure) and then crawled across all the white squares. However, she never crawled onto the black square or walked along the same intersection (where the vertical and horizontal lines intersect) more than once.

Could you draw the path of the fly? It can be continued by moving in 17 direct courses.

Rebuses

1. In the path, put the corresponding numbers in the squares so that by performing the indicated arithmetic operations sequentially (from left to right and top to bottom), the result is the number after the equal sign.

The rebus is designed so that the fifth column coincides with the fifth row. Not a single number in the rebus is equal to zero and does not begin with zero (numbers can end with zero).

2. Along the way, put the corresponding numbers in the squares so that the indicated matches are fulfilled. Not a single number in the rebus is equal to zero and does not begin with zero (numbers can end with zero).

3. In the multiplication example, one of the numbers is replaced everywhere by a square, and circles are placed along with the other numbers. Try to restore the example.
