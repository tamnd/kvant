Who won?

Imagine a round table divided into 6 equal sectors: $A, B, C, D, E, F$, at which 6 players sit, each in their own sector (see figure). In the center of the table is a rotating disk. It has arrows and numbers from 0 to 5 on it, as shown in figure. During the game, the disc is rotated 5 times. After each spin, each player marks the number that ends up near his sector (if the arrows point to the border of the sectors, then this rotation is not taken into account). The player with the most points after 5 spins wins. On figure you see the distribution of points after the first spin, as a result of which player $C$ is ahead with the fifth points. It is also known that after the second rotation the forward turns out to be $D$. Finally, after 5 spins, player $A$ won. That's all that is known about the results of the game. It would seem not so much. But this is quite enough to determine how many points each player received in the end. Try it!
