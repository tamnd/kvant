Casting vote

A convention of the Amalgamated Society of Itinerant Beggars (better known as the Union of Beggars) met to decide whether to go on strike to demand shorter hours and more begging. It was decided that during the voting, those members of society who cast their votes in favor would remain standing, and those who were against would be blown away.

“Gentlemen,” said the chairman of the meeting after the votes were counted, “I have the convenience to inform you that the strike was approved by a majority of votes, constituting a quarter of the opposition.” (Loud cheers.)

“Mr. Chairman,” he shouted from behind, “some of us were unable to sect.”

Correct the error

Hilde Wilson demanded to multiply a certain number by 409, but she made the mistake that children make when starting to study arithmetic: she placed the first digit of the product by 4 not under the third digit from the right, as it should be, but under the second. (We all did this in childhood, when a zero was found in a multiplier.) As a result of this small mistake, Hilda received a number that differed by not less than 328,320 from the correct answer.

What number did Hilda multiply by 409?
