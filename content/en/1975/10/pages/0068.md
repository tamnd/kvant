TO OUR READERS

The subscription for 1976 to the popular science physico-mathematical journal «Kvant» continues.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 5-10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to take part in optimization, and are eager to engage in the exact sciences in the future.
Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren who have not yet become interested in physics or mathematics, and whose interest in the exact sciences is still dormant.
The magazine publishes on its pages a review of scientific achievements and problems that are still awaiting solution; stories from trained people about how scientific discoveries are born.
The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were proposed at the entrance exams in various universities, problems that were proposed for optimization, and simply interesting problems.
Our magazine is also useful for teachers. Nowadays, a radical revision of school curricula in mathematics and physics is taking place. «Kvant» is actively involved in this work.

In 1976, «Kvant» will publish a series of articles on the new program and elective courses.
What questions and tasks can challenge an applicant during the entrance exams? How do these exams happen? The reader will find answers to these and many other questions in the “Applicant Practice” section.
In 1976, we plan to expand section «Kvant» for younger students. Currently, students in grades 4-7 do not have assignments that correspond to the new program. We hope to fill this gap in our publications.
The magazine constantly publishes results on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
