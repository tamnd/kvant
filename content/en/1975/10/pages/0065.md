Eternal wanderer. The secret of the impossibility of getting into “house D” is that all cells are divided into two sets: type $\nabla$ and type $\Delta$. All cells of the form $\nabla$ are filled with odd numbers, and cells of the form $\Delta$ are filled with even numbers. When moving from a cell to an adjacent cell, the cells are crossed. Obviously, if a path takes a route consisting of the number of balls indicated in the “odd” cell ($\nabla$), it inevitably ends up in the “even” cell ($\Delta$). If a series of balls makes a path from cell $\Delta$, in which an even number is written, then it again ends up in the “even” cell ($\Delta$). And the traveler’s house belongs to the set of “odd” cells of the form $\nabla$. Consequently, the traveler never gets home.
