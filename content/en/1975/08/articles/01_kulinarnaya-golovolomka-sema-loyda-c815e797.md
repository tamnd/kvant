SAM LOYD'S CULINARY PUZZLE

Sam Loyd (1841–1911) was an extremely inventive American puzzle designer. He published thousands of challenging problems, mainly mathematical and chess. He designed his works himself, and even typed and printed some of his own. The puzzle we now feature was published in the book “Sam Loyd’s Puzzles,” published in the author’s homeland, Philadelphia, a year after his death.

HOW TO SEPARATE CHEESE?

An experienced chef has to cut such a cylindrical piece of cheese into as many pieces as possible using five straight cuts. But how many pieces he gets out of it, he keeps secret. Try to unravel this secret.
