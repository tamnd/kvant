This year. The height is measured during the passage of the luminary through the celestial meridian, when this height is maximum. To do this, use special goniometric devices. Back in the Middle Ages, the first simple goniometric instrument, called a Jacobshtab, appeared, and in the 30s of the 18th century a sextant (Fig. 2) was invented in England - a goniometric instrument that was used for seconds for orientation on the open sea and in the air. During observation, the sextant is held in the hand, which allows you to compensate for the ship's pitching motion. Usually they combine the image of the Sun (or at dusk a star) with the line of the sea horizon. With some observations in this way, heights above the horizon are accurate to the minute of arc or even somewhat more accurate. On land, where the tool can be firmly installed, use more precise tools, such as a multi-tool. With its help, angular distances are measured with an accuracy of 1″ and even more accurately.
