## To problems «Kvant» for younger students
(see «Kvant», no. 7)

1. 4 chairs, 3 stools. Hint: The problem reduces to solving in integers the equation $4x+3y+2(x+y)=39$, i.e. $6x+5y=39$, whence $x+5(x+y)=39=4+5\times7$.

2. It is necessary to monitor the air bubbles coming out of the apparatus that provides breathing; You might drop some heavy object.

3. If you subtract from a two-digit number the two-digit number formed after rearranging its digits, you get $9k$, where $k$ is the difference between the first and second digits of this number. If $k<8$, then more than one two-digit number can be named that, as a result of the described subtraction, gives the required result (house number). In this case, Vladimir could not solve Nikolai's problem. This means $k=8$, and then the required number is 91, and Nikolai’s house number is 72.

4. The surface area from which evaporation occurs increases. The hay dries more evenly and quickly.

5. $9+8+7+65+4+3+2+1=99$, $9+8+7+6+5+43+21=99$. Indication: You need to consistently find the places where you need to put a plus sign. It is clear that the sum must include single and double digit numbers, but 98 cannot be taken, 87 too, since the sum of the remaining numbers is greater than 12, etc.

## On geometric inequalities
(see «Kvant», No. 7, p. 65)

1. Use the inequality $m_a+m_b+m_c<a+b+c$ and the identity

$$m_a^2+m_b^2+m_c^2=\frac{3}{4}(a^2+b^2+c^2).$$

To prove the left side of the inequality, use the fact that $ab+bc+ca\leqslant a^2+b^2+c^2\leqslant 2(ab+bc+ca)$ and that the medians can be used to form a triangle.

2. Using the identities $c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos C$ and $ch_c=ab\sin C$, prove that

$$a^2+b^2-h_c^2\leqslant\frac{a^2b^2}{h_c^2}=4R^2.$$

3. Use relations of the form

$$\cos\frac{A}{2}=\frac{w_a}{2}\left(\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right)$$

and inequalities

$$\cos\frac{A}{2}+\cos\frac{B}{2}+\cos\frac{C}{2}\leqslant\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2},\quad \frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}\geqslant\frac{9}{x+y+z}\text{ при }x>0,\ y>0,\ z>0.$$

To prove the second inequality, use the inequality

$$\cos\frac{A}{2}\geqslant\frac{2w_a}{b+c}\text{ и т. п.}$$

## To algebraic problems
(see «Kvant», No. 7, p. 20)

1. Open the brackets and apply Vieta's theorem.

$$x^{17}-1=(x-1)[x^8-ax^7+(2-a)x^6-{}$$
$$-(3a-2)x^5+(10-a)x^4-(3a-2)x^3+{}$$
$$+(2-a)x^2-ax+1][x^8-bx^7+\dots+1].$$

2. The required equation has the form

$$x^3-9cx^2+(b^2+27c^2)x-c(4b^3+27c^2)=0.$$

3. Set $r-1=\rho$ and use Newton's binomial formula.

4. Multiplying, we get $(x+y)(x+z)\times(y+z)=\pm abcxyz$, hence $\dfrac{ax}{y+z}=\pm1$.

So, $y+z=\pm ax$, $z+x=\pm by$, $x+y=\pm cz$. Eliminating $x$, $y$, $z$, we obtain the required equalities $a+b+c\pm2=abc$.
