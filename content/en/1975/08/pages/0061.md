⟦folio 59⟧

About number systems

Dear Seryozha!

Do you remember our meeting in Batumi?
In a conversation with me, you admitted that you were planning to become an astronaut. To my remark that to achieve this goal you will need to study diligently, thoroughly learn biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, you responded in a very original way - you began to demonstrate your knowledge. It turns out that you know mathematics very well, you even love it. And then I decided to ask you a “tricky” question: “why is the equality 7+8=15 true and can the equality 7+8=16 be true?”

You were quite surprised, even somehow confused, but soon found yourself and answered: “because 7+8=15, and the equality 7+8=16 is incorrect!” It was noticeable that you yourself were not satisfied with your answer. I was going to explain what was going on here, but, unfortunately, we did not have the opportunity to continue the conversation, and I promised you to write a letter on this topic to «Kvant», which you, like me, love very much. I keep my promise. So, first question.
