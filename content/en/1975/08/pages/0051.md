⟦folio 49⟧

Quantum problem solver

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than October 1, 1975 to the address: 117071, Moscow, V-71, Leninsky Prospekt, 15, Nauka Publishing House, magazine «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: “Problem «Kvant», M336, M337” or “...F348”. Solutions to problems in each of the subjects [mathematics and physics], as well as new problems, should be sent in separate envelopes. Please also send assignments from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking your decisions). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope put: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). After creating a task, we are promised to indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The most difficult tasks are marked with asterisks.

In this issue, “Problem «Kvant»” is composed of problems from the latest All-Union Optimization and their generalizations.

Tasks

M336 — M340; F348—F352

M336. In the given plane there is a finite set of polygons, each two of which have a common point. Prove that there is a line that has a common point with each of these polygons. (10 grades)

S. V. Fomin

M337. Given an equilateral triangle ABC with side length 1. The first player chooses point X on side AB, the second player chooses point Y on side BC, then the first player chooses point Z on side AC.

a) The goal of the first player is to obtain a triangle XYZ with the largest area, the second player - with the largest area. What is the largest area the first one can provide?

b) The goal of the first player is to get the XYZ triangle with the largest perimeter, the second player - with the largest perimeter. What is the smallest perimeter that the first one can provide? (8-9 grades)

M. D. Bronstein

M338. Several zeros, ones and twos are written on the board. It is allowed to erase two unequal numbers and write one number together, different from the erased ones (2 together 0 and 1, 1 together 0 and 2, 0 together 1 and 2). Prove that if, as a result of several such operations, only one number remains on the board, then it does not depend on the order in which the erases were made. (8th and 10th grades)

S. V. Fomin

M339. Given a horizontal strip on a plane, the edges of which are parallel lines, and n straight lines intersecting this strip. Which two of these n lines intersect internally?
