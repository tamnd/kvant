What are longitude and latitude?

The position of a point on the Earth's surface is determined by two coordinates: geographic latitude and geographic longitude. Do you know where these names came from? In the second century AD. e. Greek astronomer and geographer Claudius Ptolemy introduced the concept of length and width in relation to the measurement of countries, applications to the Mediterranean Sea, stretched from west to east. He called the results of measurements along the sea length, and across them - width. When translated into Russian, instead of these words, which can be attributed to any objects, special terms were introduced to characterize the position of the body on the earth's surface - longitude and latitude. Although these concepts, clearly illustrated on the globe, are widely known, not everyone knows their exact definition.

It is often said that latitude φ is the angular distance of a given point from the earth's equator, and longitude λ is the dihedral angle between the plane of a given meridiana and the plane of a conventionally selected initial meridiana. Consequently, the network of parallels and meridians plotted on the globe allows us to indicate the geographic coordinates of any point on the globe. Naturally, the coordinate value is necessary for orientation. But, being at some point on Earth, we ourselves must be able to determine our geographical coordinates. As is known, this is done with the help of astronomical observations. Suppose we have calculated the latitude and longitude of the point where we are located. Can we now, having found a point on the globe with such coordinates, be sure that we are located exactly at this point on the Earth? No! Even if the point on the globe is indicated with extreme accuracy.

The fact is that the Earth is not a sphere, and the globe is only a simplified visual model of the Earth. It is the irregularity (non-spherical) shape of the Earth that leads to discrepancies between the geographical coordinates obtained from astronomical observations and the coordinates determined for a given point on the globe on the map.
