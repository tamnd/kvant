This astronomical clock was made in 1572. The outer scale of the upper dial is divided into 24 hours - twice 12. On this scale, three hands indicate solar, lunar and sidereal time. The other two scales of the dial determine the time, counted from sunrise or sunset (these scales are also divided into 24 divisions). The astrolabe, installed at the top of the clock, shows the position of the Sun relative to the constellations of the Zodiac.
The clock is currently kept in the Kunsthistorisches Museum in Vienna.
