## Moscow Institute of Petrochemical and Gas Industry

Our institute is known in all corners of the country, because the professions of oil and gas workers are perhaps the most popular in our time. The development of new oil and gas fields opens up wide opportunities for graduates of the Ministry of Economy and State Enterprise, the country's largest higher educational institution for training engineers and scientists for the oil, gas, petrochemical industries, and construction of oil and gas pipelines.

The Institute was created in 1930 on the basis of the Mining Academy and now it has six faculties: gas-oil geology, geophysics and geochemistry; gas and oil field; chemical technology; mechanical; automation and computer technology; engineering and economics. In addition, there are evening and correspondence faculties.

Specialists who have graduated from our institute are engaged in the exploration, design and operation of oil and gas fields, the study of gas and gas condensate wells and formations, oil and gas refining processes, design the largest oil and gas pipelines, work in organizations that design, start-up and commissioning of machinery and equipment for fields, compressor stations, chemical production equipment, participate in the development of problems of oil and gas geology, economics, radiation chemistry. At the institute you can get about 20 different specialties.

Currently, an engineer must be aware of the latest achievements of the scientific and technological revolution, understand and use mathematical methods and economic and mathematical models. The curricula of the specialties “Automated control systems”, “Automation and telemechanics” and some others include such modern disciplines as the mathematical foundations of cybernetics, systems engineering, probability theory and mathematical statistics, and operations research. Students specializing in “Applied Mathematics” now study linear algebra, functional analysis, theory of functions of a complex variable, probabilistic processes and other deep sections of higher mathematics.

The institute has created a computer room specifically for students. Here they master computer technology and apply it in coursework and diploma design, as well as in scientific work.

Graduates of the institute, among the most gifted students, continue their studies in graduate school. Admission to graduate school is carried out every year in a number of specialties, some of which are related to technical cybernetics and computer engineering.

The institute has a computer center where research problems are solved. Electronic computers are also used to control students' knowledge. In recent years, the processing of all entrance exam results and the enrollment of applicants is carried out using a computer.
