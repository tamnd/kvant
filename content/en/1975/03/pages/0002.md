There is a lot of surprising and, at first glance, strange in the movement of the familiar children's toy spinning top, or spinning top. Both mathematicians and physicists were engaged in explaining the properties of this movement. Sofya Vasilyevna Kovalevskaya obtained remarkable results in solving the problem of the movement of a top. We talk about the life of the outstanding Russian mathematician in this issue of the magazine (see p. 2).
