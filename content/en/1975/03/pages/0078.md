$$\vec{OG'_{ij}} = \frac{1}{n-2}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} \vec{OA_k} - \vec{OA_i} - \vec{OA_j}\right)$$

(see formula (2), p. 39).

The line $l_{ij}$ drawn through $G'_{ij}$ perpendicular to $A_i A_j$ is parallel to the line $OG_{ij}$. Therefore, for any point $L$ of the line $l_{ij}$ we have

$$\vec{OL} = \vec{OG'_{ij}} + \lambda \cdot \vec{OG_{ij}},$$

that is

$$\vec{OL} = \frac{1}{n-2}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n} \vec{OA_k} - \vec{OA_i} - \vec{OA_j}\right) + \lambda \frac{\vec{OA_i} + \vec{OA_j}}{2},$$

or

$$\vec{OL} = \frac{1}{n-2}\sum_{k=1}^{n} \vec{OA_k} + \left(\frac{\lambda}{2} - \frac{1}{n-2}\right)\left(\vec{OA_i} + \vec{OA_j}\right).$$

For $\lambda = \dfrac{2}{n-2}$ we get that

$$\vec{OL_0} = \frac{1}{n-2}\sum_{k=1}^{n} \vec{OA_k} = \frac{n}{n-2}\vec{OG},$$

where $G$ is the centroid of this system of points. Therefore, the point $L_0$ belongs to all lines $l_{ij}$, and

$$\frac{|\vec{OL_0}|}{|\vec{OG}|} = \frac{n}{n-2}.$$
