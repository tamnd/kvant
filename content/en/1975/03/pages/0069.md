⟦folio 67⟧

Comrade in mathematics and problems in physics offered in the exams in 1974.

Mathematics

Option 1

4. Two couriers simultaneously come out to each other from points $A$ and $B$ and meet after some time. If the first one had left 1 hour earlier, and the second one 0.5 hours later, then they would have met 18 minutes earlier. If the first one had left 0.5 hours later, and the second one 1 hour earlier, then the meeting point would have moved 5600 meters. What is the speed of each courier?

2. Solve the equation
$$x + \lg(1 + 2^x) = x \lg 5 + \lg 6.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$4 \sin^4 2x + \sin^2 4x = 2.$$

4. The base of the example is a rectilinear triangle with an acute angle $\alpha$. This triangle is inscribed at the base of the cone. The vertex of the examples coincides with the middle of one of the generatrices of the cone. Find the ratio of the volume of a cone to the volume of examples.

Option 2

1. What two-digit number is less than the sum of the squares of its digits by 11 and greater than their double product by 5?

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_2 \log_3 \frac{x+2}{x-2} < \log_1 \frac{1}{2} \log_1 \frac{x-2}{x+2}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos x - 2 \sin \left( \frac{3}{2} \pi - \frac{x}{2} \right) = 3.$$

4. The base of the primordium is an equilateral trapezoid with an acute angle $\alpha$. The radius inscribed at the base of the circle is $R$. Determine the volume of the abutment if its height is $H$.

Option 3

1. Two pedestrian codes simultaneously stitched each other from points $A$ and $B$. Everyone walks at a constant speed without stopping and, having arrived at their final destination, immediately turns back. When they met 4 km more than the second one, they passed 4 km more than the second one. Continuing onward, the first pedestrian arrived at $A$ 1 hour after the second meeting, and the second pedestrian at $B$ 2.5 hours after this meeting. What is the speed of each pedestrian?

2. Solve inequality $(0,2) \frac{2x-3}{x-2} > 5.$

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos 2x + \sin 3x + \sin 5x = 4 \cos x - 1.$$

4. The cylinder is intersected by a plane parallel to the axis and cutting off the arc $\alpha$ ($\alpha < \pi$) from the base circle. The diagonal of the section is equal to $l$ and makes an angle $\beta$ with the base plane. Find the volume of the cylinder.

Physics

1. A car weighing 20 m moving along a horizontal track will pick up another car weighing 30 m and moves with it, so that the cars continue to move together. In this case, the total mechanical energy of the cars changes by $6 \cdot 10^3 \text{ дж}$. By what amount did the speed of the first car exceed the speed of the second car before the clutch?

2. The satellite flies in a circular orbit around the Earth. What is its linear speed if at the altitude where the satellite flies, the force is $n$ times less than on the surface of the Earth?

3. Two elastic balls move towards each other, and the speed of one of them (heavier) is $\alpha$ times higher than the speed of the other. After the impact, the more massive ball stopped. What is the ratio of the masses of the balls?

4. A stone is thrown from the ground at an angle $30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The kinetic energy of the stone at the top point of the trajectory is 45 J. What is the potential energy of the stone at this point? Air resistance is challenging.

5. A tram weighing 20 m passes at the same speed along two sections of the track - convex and concave (with the same radius of 100 m), while developing the same mechanical power of 25 kW. On the concave section, the pressure of the tram on the rails is $4 \cdot 10^4$ n greater than on the convex section. What is the arc force of the tram?

6. The first escape velocity at the surface of a certain spherical planet is 1 km/s. What is the average density of the substance of this planet if its diameter is 3.75·10^6 km²?

7. A ball whose density is 4 times greater than the density of the liquid falls in a vessel with liquid (without initial speed). Another ball flares up from the day of this vessel in a time $\sqrt{3}$ times longer than the time of fall of the first ball. How many times is the density of the flaring ball less than the density of the liquid?

8. In the field of a stationary positive charge of 10^-9 k, a positive mass of 10^-26 kcal with a charge of 1.6·10^-19 kcal moves towards it. When the speed hits 10^5 m/s, its kinetic and potential energy are equal. At what minimum distance will it approach the charge?

9. In a flat capacitor with a potential difference on the plates of 4 kV, charged particles (with charges of 10^-10 kcal and -2·10^-9 kcal) are separated from the plates and accelerated towards each other simultaneously with the plates in the field of the capacitor. What is kinetic energy?
