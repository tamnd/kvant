3. Let us write down the conditions for electron motion in a circle (Fig. 1):

$$\frac{mv^2}{r} = 2F \sin \alpha.$$

Here $r = \frac{Rtg\alpha}{2}$, $F = \frac{e^2}{\left(\frac{R}{2\cos \alpha}\right)^2}$ (in the SGSE system of units).

To determine the angle $\alpha$, consider the equilibrium conditions of one of the protons. Along the axis, two forces act on the proton: the repulsive force $F_1 = e^2/R^2$ from the other proton and the component $F$ cos $\alpha$ of the force of extension towards the electron. From the equality of these forces we obtain that $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$. Then finally

$$v^2 = \frac{4e^2}{mR} \sin^2 \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{4e^2}{mR} \left(1 - \cos^2 \alpha\right) \cos \alpha,$$

where

$$v \approx 1,23 \frac{e}{\sqrt{mR}}.$$
