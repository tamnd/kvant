⟦folio 44⟧

WINNERS
COMPETITION
"QUANTA"

In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the All-Union Olympiad, schoolchildren who regularly submit particularly original and complete solutions to the problems of “Problem «Kvant»” receive the right to participate in the regional rounds of the All-Union Olympiad along with the winner of the district and city Olympiads.
Over the past year, 1974, the editors received more than seven thousand letters with solutions to problems. The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» together with members of the Organizing Committee selected the authors of the correct and most interesting solutions.
Below is a list of schoolchildren - the winner of the competition «Kvant» - who received the right to participate in the regional Olympiads in 1975.
