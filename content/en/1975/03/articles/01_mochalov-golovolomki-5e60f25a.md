Puzzles

Labyrinth - alphabet
The letters of the alphabet are written in 64 square cells. Starting with the letter A in the upper left corner, draw a broken line that does not intersect itself, which passes through exactly 33 letters of the alphabet and ends in the lower right corner with the letter Z. The broken line should not pass through the vertices of the small squares.

Pentamino is a solitaire game.
12 pentamino pieces arranged in a 12x10 rectangle. Try placing these pentamino pieces on the remaining yellow field (you can turn the pieces over).

L. P. Mochalov
