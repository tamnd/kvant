Ask - we answer

We received a letter from reader L. Skorokhodko from the city of Kyiv. In it he asks:
“...Please advise what and how to read according to the eighth grade curriculum in order to learn how to solve any problems! Where can you get such books!..."
At our request, a member of the editorial board of the magazine «Kvant», Corresponding Member of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR, Honored School Teacher of the RSFSR Semyon Isaakovich Schwarzburg, answers L. Skorokhodko’s questions.

It all depends on what tasks are meant. The theory, examples, exercises and tasks given in textbooks for mass schools are aimed mainly at the conditional level of the compulsory program. Among these tasks there are, however, more complex ones that are not required for all students; It is believed that such problems should be solved by strong students who are more interested in and capable of mathematics. But in order to solve even these complex problems from the puzzle, you do not need any additional knowledge beyond the compulsory school curriculum. So if you want to solve any problems from the school problem book, study the school textbook properly! Of course, I don't mean simply memorizing theorems and proofs. Theorems and proofs are not enough to be learned; they must be understood.

If you are interested in mathematics, want to study it on the server, want to solve “non-school” problems, then school textbooks are no longer enough - you need additional books. There are a lot of such books: all kinds of textbooks and problems have been published for extracurricular and extracurricular classes, for classes with in-depth study of mathematics, for technical students with mathematical specializations. One could cite here the concept of a huge list of existing literature. But will this answer the question of the letter?

Mathematics books are a very important source of learning, but they must be used skillfully.

Mathematical literature must be read “with a pencil in hand.” You should definitely try to solve the tasks found in the text yourself. You can look at the solution given in the book if the problem doesn’t work out, or when the problem has already been solved - compare your solution with the “kij” one, test yourself and, perhaps, learn something new. Only such active reading will bring you real benefit. In addition, the development of mathematical creativity greatly contributes to communication with friends in extracurricular and extracurricular activities, participation in mathematical clubs, olympiads, mathematical evenings, quizzes, presentations, preparation of abstracts and much more. But the paramount role in studying any subject, especially mathematics, is played by perseverance, hard work and systematic study, no matter what abilities you have. Indeed, in the process of work it is capable

There is no doubt that articles published in «Kvant» can be of great help in independent and in-depth study of mathematics. Some of the articles can also be used for extracurricular activities. By the way, a few words about electives in mathematics - after all, as already noted, they play a significant role in improving mathematical culture. To date, there are already established elective courses in mathematics, programs for them have been approved, and manuals have been published. Mathematical electives have become widespread among schoolchildren in grades VII-X. Here, students become familiar with material that goes beyond the compulsory school curriculum and learn to solve interesting and difficult problems. Schools are now switching to a new mathematics curriculum. The traditional school course is undergoing radical changes - from now on it will include many issues that were previously studied only in electives. The school mathematics course will be improved in the future; One of the sources of its renewal and enrichment is the topic of elective and extracurricular activities.

We hope that Semyon Isaakovich's answer will be useful to those who have decided to study mathematics. We recommend that you get acquainted with books from the following series: “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School”, “Library of the Mathematical Club”, “Manuals for elective classes in mathematics”. Books in these series are published quite regularly. Those of them that have already been published, try to get them from the library. We usually announce new books in advance in our magazine. The magazine "Mathematics at School" publishes many good problems.
