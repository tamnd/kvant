⟦folio 34⟧

Try now to solve several problems on finding the path of light propagation between two points in an optically inhomogeneous medium, if the law of change in the speed of light is specified in this medium.

Task 1. The speed of light varies according to the law $c(x, y) = k \cdot (y - a)$. Prove that light between two points will propagate along two semicircles with diameters on the line $\{y = a\}$ (and the “starting” point is on this line).

Task 2. The speed of light varies according to the law $c(x, y) = \frac{k}{\sqrt{a - y}}$. Prove that in this case the light between two points will propagate along two parabolas.

So far in all problems the speed of light depends only on $y$. If an optical medium such as this dependence is more complex, for example, the speed of light is constant not on horizontal lines, but on some curves - the line of constancy of the speed of light - then the light between two points will propagate along such a curve $L$, for which

$$\frac{\sin \alpha \left(c(x_0, y_0)\right)}{c(x_0, y_0)} = \text{const},$$

where $\alpha \left(c(x_0, y_0)\right)$ is the angle between the tangent to the curve $L$ and the normal to the line of constant speed of light $c(x, y) = c(x_0, y_0)$.

Task 3. The speed of light varies according to the law $c(x, y) = k \sqrt{1 - r^2}$, where $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ is the distance from the origin. Prove that in such a medium, light between two points will propagate along two circles perpendicular to a circle of radius 1 with a center at the origin.

Task 4. The speed of light varies according to the law $c(x, y) = kr$. Prove that in this case the light between two points will spread along two zonopicloud (see Newmon’s exercises at the end of the article “Secrets of Cyclops”, «Kvant», 1975, No. 8, p. 27).

If in problems 1-4 $c(x, y)$ is interpreted as the speed value of inhomogeneous mechanical motion, then the trajectories of light propagation obtained by solving these problems will be brachistochrones for corresponding mechanical systems.
