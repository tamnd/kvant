On July 14, the closing ceremony of the Olympiad took place, where the final results were summed up. The winners were awarded diplomas and valuable gifts.

The teams of the GDR, USSR, Hungary and Poland performed most successfully, all of whose members were awarded diplomas of I, II, III degrees.

The winner of the Olympiad was *Sergei Korshunov* (USSR), who scored the highest number of points (43).
