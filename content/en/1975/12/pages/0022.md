We will talk about why this property is called “optical” below, but now let’s turn to hyperbola and parabola. The optical properties of these lines are clear from figure 3 and 4. On figure 3 the points $A$ and $B$ are the focuses of the hyperbola, and on figure 4 the point $A$ is the focus, and the line $d$ is the directrix of the parabola. In both cases, the optical property is expressed by the condition $\angle 1 \cong \angle 2$.

For a hyperbola, the proof of the optical property is similar to the proof carried out for the ellipse.
