2. Axims of order

Axima III₁. For any point O of line p, we divide the set of points of line p that are opposite from O into two non-empty sets mak, such that:
a) for any two points A and B belonging to different sets, point O lies between A and B;
b) if points A and B belong to the same set, then one of them lies with the other point and point O.
The sets discussed in Axim III₁ are called open rays, and the union of each of them with a point O is called a ray with origin O.
Axima III₂. For any consideration of a on a given ray c at origin O there is one and only one point A whose consideration from point O is equal to a: |OA| = a.
Axima III₃. If point C lies between points A and B, then points A, B, C belong to the same line.
