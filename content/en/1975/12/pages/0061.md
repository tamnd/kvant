Based on the results of two days of competition, the Hungarian team had the best result, followed by the teams of the GDR, USA and USSR. Moreover, only in three teams - Hungary, East Germany and the USSR - all participants received awards.

**Tasks of the first day of competition**

**1.** Let $x_i$, $y_i$ and $(i = 1, 2, 3, \ldots, n)$ be real numbers such that $x_1 \geqslant \geqslant x_2 \geqslant \ldots \geqslant x_n$ and $y_1 \geqslant y_2 \geqslant \ldots \geqslant y_n$. Prove that if $z_1$, $z_2$, ..., $z_n$ is any permutation of the numbers $y_1$, $y_2$, ..., $y_n$, then

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - y_i)^2 \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - z_i)^2$$

(Czechoslovakia, 6 points).

**2.** Let $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$, ... be an arbitrary infinite sequence of positive integers such that $a_k < < a_{k+1}$ $(1 \leqslant k)$. Prove that infinitely many terms $a_m$ of this sequence can be represented as $a_m = x a_p + y a_q$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive numbers and $p \neq q$ (UK, 7 points).

**3.** On the sides of an arbitrary triangle $ABC$ outside it (in the plane $ABC$) triangles $BPC$, $CQA$ and $ARB$ are constructed so that

$$\widehat{PBC} = \widehat{CAQ} = 45°, \qquad \widehat{BCP} = \widehat{QCA} = 30°,$$
$$\widehat{ABR} = \widehat{BAR} = 15°.$$

Prove that $\widehat{QRP} = 90°$ and $|QR| = = |RP|$ (Netherlands, 7 points).

**Tasks of the second day of competition**

**4.** Let $A$ be the sum of the digits in decimal notation of the number $4444^{4444}$, and $B'$ be the sum of the digits of the number $A$ ($A$ and $B$ are also considered in decimal notation). Find the sum of the digits of the number $B$ (USSR, 6 points).

**5.** Is it possible to mark 1975 points on a circle of radius 1 so that the lengths of the chords connecting any two of them are expressed in rational numbers? (USSR, 6 points).

**6.** Find all polynomials $P$ with respect to two variables that have the following properties:

1) $P$ is a homogeneous polynomial of degree $n$, that is, for all real $t$, $x$, $y$ $P(tx, ty) = t^n P(x, y)$;

2) for all real $a$, $b$, $c$
$$P(a + b, c) + P(b + c, a) + \\ + P(c + a, b) = 0;$$

3) $P(1, 0) = 1$
(UK, 8 points).

*Z. I. Moiseeva, V. A. Skvortsov,*

## VIII Olympiad in Physics

In July 1975, the VIII International Physics Olympiad among schoolchildren was held in the city of Güstrow (GDR). Teams from Bulgaria, Hungary, East Germany, Poland, Romania, USSR, Czechoslovakia, France, and Germany took part in the Olympiad.

Members of the USSR team were schoolchildren - winners of the All-Union Physics Olympiad, who showed the best results at the training camp:

Leonid Avdeev - graduate of physics and mathematics boarding school No. 165 in Novosibirsk;

V a d i m B o r u - graduate of secondary school No. 28 in Zaporozhye;

S ergey Korshunov is a graduate of secondary school No. 1 of the village. Monino, Shchelkovsky district, Moscow region;

Yuriy M a k e d o n o v - graduate of secondary school No. 37 in Kalinin;

Evgeniy Shakhnovich is a graduate of secondary school No. 6 in Kalinin.

On July 7, all participants of the Olympiad gathered in Berlin and headed to Güstrow by bus.

On July 9, a theoretical round was held, at which participants were offered three tasks (the texts of the tasks are given at the end of the article). The maximum number of points in this round was received by: Radu Oprea (Romania), Sergei Korshunov (USSR), Antoni Lachkovsky (Poland), Domenic Delande (France).
