ХVII International School Olympiad
in mathematics

In July of this year, the XVII International Mathematical Olympiad for Schoolchildren was held in the Bulgarian city of Burgas. You can read more about this Olympiad in the article on p. 57. The editorial board and editorial staff of «Kvant» congratulate the members of the Soviet team who successfully performed at the Olympiad.

In this sim you see the participants, the Soviet math team. From left to right are sitting: A. Reznikov, I. Yunus, V. A. Skovortsov (team leader), Z. I. Moniseeva (deputy team leader), B. Yusin; standing: G. Shmelev, T. Khovanova, S. Finashin, Yu. Neretin, A. Kornyushkin.
