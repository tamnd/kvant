To the article “XVII Olympiad in Mathematics”
1. By opening the parentheses in the inequality being proved, it is easy to verify that it is equivalent to the inequality
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i z_i \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i y_i.$$
Let us prove this inequality by induction. For $n=1$ it is valid for $n=k$. Let's check it for $n=k+1$. If $z_1 = y_1$, then by the induction hypothesis
$$\sum_{i=2}^{k+1} x_i z_i \leqslant \sum_{i=2}^{k+1} x_i y_i,$$
and adding $x_1 y_1$ to this inequality, we obtain the inequality being proved.
Let now $z_1 = y_m, m \neq 1$ and $y_1 = z_l, l \neq 1$. Since $x_1 \geqslant x_l$ and $y_1 \geqslant y_m$, then $(x_1 - x_l)(y_1 - y_m) \geqslant 0$, opening $x_1 y_1 + x_l y_m \geqslant x_1 y_m + x_l y_1$. Therefore, if in the sum $\sum_{i=2}^{k+1} x_i z_i$ the terms $x_1 z_1 = x_1 y_m$ and $x_l z_l = x_l y_1$ are replaced by the terms $x_1 y_1$ and $x_l y_m$, then the sum will not decrease.
