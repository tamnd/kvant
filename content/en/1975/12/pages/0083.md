VIII International Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics

In July of this year, the VIII International Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics took place in the city of Güstrow (GDR). You can read more about this Olympiad in the article on p. 59.
Redkopletiya and the editors of «Kvant» congratulate the members of the Soviet team who successfully performed at the Olympics.
