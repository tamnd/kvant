Tasks

M356—M360; F368—F372

M356. From the point $M$, taken inside the triangle $A_1B_1C_1$, perpendiculars $MA_2, MB_2, MC_2$ are dropped onto lines $B_1C_1, A_1C_1$ and $A_1B_1$, respectively. Then from point $M$ perpendiculars $MA_3, MB_3, MC_3$ are dropped onto straight lines $B_2C_2, A_2C_2, A_2B_2$ (Fig. 1), and so on. Prove that in the resulting sequence of triangles $A_1B_1C_1$,

$$A_2B_2C_2, A_3B_3C_3, \dots \text{ треугольник } A_{3n+1}B_{3n+1}C_{3n+1} \text{ подобен треугольнику } A_1B_1C_1.$$

Y. N. Sukonic

M357. Prove that if $x + \frac{1}{y} = y + \frac{1}{z} = z + \frac{1}{x}$ then $x = y = z$ or $x^2y^2z^2 = 1$.

M358. Prove that any $n$-gon ($n \geqslant 4$) has at least one diagonal that lies entirely inside the $n$-gon, and find out what is the smallest number of such diagonals a $n$-gon can have (for each $n$).

A. Khomodov

M359*. A small ball moves inside the billiard, which has the shape of an ellipse with focuses $A$ and $B$, elastically reflected from its sides along the broken line $P_1P_2P_3P_4 \dots (P_1, P_2, \dots$ - points of the ellipse). Prove that if the link

⟦folio 37⟧

$P_1P_2$ does not intersect the segment $AB$, then a) all subsequent links $P_2P_3, P_3P_4, \dots$ do not intersect the segment $AB$; b) all these links touch the same ellipse. (Think about how to construct this ellipse.)

A. N. Zemlyakov

M360*. For the sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ it is known that $|a_1| = 1$ and $|a_{k+1}| = |a_k| + 1$ for each $k = 1, 2, \dots$. Find the smallest possible value of the sum $|a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_n|$ if:
a) $n = 1975$;
b) $n = 1976$.

V. P. Golubyatnikov

F368. A rocket of mass $m$ starts at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon. The rockets move for $t$ seconds, creating $F$ thrust and ensuring the rocket moves in a straight line. Neglecting the change in rocket mass and air resistance, determine the height $h$ at which engine operation stops.

V. A. Pozozhev

F369. Three identical loaded balls are held together by non-conductive threads forming a right triangle $BAC$ (Fig. 2). Angle $ABC$ is equal to $\alpha, BC = l$. With what acceleration will the charges begin to move if the thread $BC$ is cut? The charge of the balls is $q$, the masses are $m$.

F370. A tube whose diameter is much less than its length is rolled into a flask

Fig. 2.

along the radius $R$. The ring is placed vertically and filled with liquid, except for a small area near point $A$ (Fig. 3), in which there is an air bubble. The bubble begins to float. Find his speed at the moment when he passes point $B$. Bubble length $l$. Neglect the friction of water against the walls of the tube.

F371. The mass cannon $M=200\,\text{кг}$ fires a mass cannonball $m=20\,\text{кг}$ at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizontal. The charge of gunpowder is $r=5\,\text{кг}$, its heat is $q=1000\,\text{ккал}/\text{кг}$. At the moment the cannonball leaves the cannon, Baron Munchausen sits on it. Its mass is $\mu=70\,\text{кг}$. How close will a Munchausen cannonball fall with the same charge of gunpowder? How much do you need to increase the charge of gunpowder in order for Munchausen to hit the same place where the cannonballs fell before?

Take the efficiency of the shot equal to $\eta=10\%.$ Assume that the gun is on a smooth surface on which it can slide without friction.

A. L. Stasenko

F372. Two thin positive lenses with focal lengths $F_1$ and $F_2$ are located on the same axis. Using this lens system, an image of an object was obtained, and it turned out that the size of the image does not depend on the distance from the object to the lens system. Find the distance $l$ between the lenses.

E. P. Kuznetsov
