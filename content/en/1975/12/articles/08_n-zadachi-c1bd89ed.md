## Tasks

1. Prove that if $a$ and $b$ are roots of equation $x^4 + x^3 = 1$, then $ab$ is a root of equation $x^6 + x^4 + x^3 - x^2 - 1 = 0$.

2. Prove that if $x \neq y$, $y \neq z$, $x \neq z$ and

$$\frac{k\,(1+yz)-y-z}{y-z} =$$

$$= \frac{k\,(1+zx)-z-x}{z-x} =$$

$$= \frac{k\,(1+xy)-x-y}{x-y} = u,$$

then $3u^2 - k^2 + 1 = 0$.

3. Prove that if $xy\,(x+y) = c$, $yz\,(y+z) = a$, $zx\,(z+x) = b$, $xyz = d$,

then $a+b+c+2d = \dfrac{abc}{d^2}$.

4. Prove that if $A$, $B$, $C$ are the values ​​of the angles of a triangle, then for any natural number $n$ the inequalities are valid

$$(-1)^{n+1}\,(\cos nA + \cos nB + \cos nC) \leqslant 3/2,$$

and $-1 \leqslant (-1)^{n+1} \times$

$$\times \cos nA \cos nB \cos nC \leqslant 1/8.$$

5. Prove that if $x$, $y$, $z$ are any real numbers and $a$, $b$, $c$ are sides of a triangle of area $\Delta$, then the inequality

$$\frac{xy}{ab} + \frac{yz}{bc} + \frac{zx}{ca} \leqslant$$

$$\leqslant \left(\frac{ax+by+cz}{4\Delta}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}},$$

where equality occurs if and only if

$$\frac{x}{a\,(b^2+c^2-a^2)} =$$

$$= \frac{y}{b\,(c^2+a^2-b^2)} =$$

$$= \frac{z}{c\,(a^2+b^2-c^2)}\,.$$
