Second solution. Let us describe a parallelogram $E_1F_1K_1L_1$ around the quadrilateral $ABCD$ so that its sides are parallel to the diagonals of the quadrilateral $ABCD$ (Fig. 7). Let diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $O$. Then $\overrightarrow{OE}_1 = 2\overrightarrow{OE}$, $\overrightarrow{OF}_1 = 2\overrightarrow{OF}$, etc. Therefore, parallelograms $E_1F_1K_1L_1$ and $EFKL$ are prepared, the coefficient of preparation is 2. Therefore, the area of ​​parallelogram $E_1F_1K_1L_1$ is 4 times the area of ​​parallelogram $EFKL$. Next we will use Problem 13.

Problem 19. Prove that if the diagonals of a convex quadrilateral are concussive, then the area of ​​this quadrilateral is equal to the product of the length of its midlines.

Problem 20. Prove that the area of ​​a convex quadrilateral is equal to the product of the length of its middle lines and the sine of the angle between them.
