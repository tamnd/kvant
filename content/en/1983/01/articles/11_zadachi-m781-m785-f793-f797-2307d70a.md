Tasks

M781—M785; F793—F797

M781. Construct a line parallel to side AC of a given triangle ABC and intersecting its sides AB and BC at points D and E, respectively, such that |AD| = |BE|.

L. V. Kim

M782. Prove that if the sum of two natural numbers is 30,030, then their product is not divisible by 30,030.

S. V. Fomin

M783. a) At what maximum n is the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} 1 < x < 2, \\ 2 < x^2 < 3, \\ 3 < x^3 < 4, \\ \dots \\ n < x^n < n+1 \end{cases}$$
has a solution?

6) For which n there are two progressions - arithmetic a₁, a₂, a₃, ..., aₙ+1 and geometric b₁, b₂, b₃, ..., bₙ, such that a₁ < b₁ < a₂ < b₂ < a₃ < ... < aₙ < bₙ < aₙ+1?

M784. A spherical planet moves in a circle around a star and rotates around an axis, and the axis of condensed rotation is inclined to the orbital plane at an angle α (for our Earth α = 66.5°). Find the dependence of the duration T of the shortest day of the year at a given point on the surface of the planet on the geographic latitude φ of this point. (The angular velocity of the planet’s rotation in its orbit is much less than the angular velocity of the planet’s rotation around its axis). Write a formula for the function T = T(φ) and draw an example graph.

A. P. Savin

M785. a) Regarding the increasing sequence of positive numbers a(n), n = 1, 2, 3,..., it is known that for any natural number k > 1 there is a number bₖ such that a(kn) ≤ bₖa(n) for all n. Prove that it exists

⟦folio 40⟧

positive numbers $c$ and $\alpha$, for which $a(n) \leq cn^\alpha$ for all $n \geq 1$.

Will this statement remain true if, under conditions
b) replace the words “any” with “some”?
c) not require that the sequence $a(n)$ be increasing?

M. U. Gafurov

F793. On the question of a spherical surface of radius $R$ from a height $H = R/8$ near the axis of symmetry small balls fall (with zero initial velocity). Assuming the impacts of balls on the surface to be absolutely elastic, show that after the first impact each ball hits the lowest point of the surface. Mutual collisions of balls are not taken into account.

I. V. Krivenkov

F794. Two identical body-solided calometers of height $h = 75$ cm are filled one third with ice, the other with water at a temperature of $t = 10^\circ\text{C}$. Water from the second calometer is poured into the first, and the calometer is two-thirds full. After the temperature in the calometer is established, its filling level will increase by $\Delta h = 0,5$ cm. What was the initial temperature of the ice in the calometer? Плотность льда — $\varrho_{\text{л}} = 9 \cdot 10^2 \text{ кг/m}^3$, воды — $\varrho_{\text{в}} = 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$; specific heat of melting of ice - $\lambda = 3,4 \times 10^5 \text{ Дж/kg}$; удельная тепломкость льда — $c_{\text{л}} = 2,1 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$, воды — $c_{\text{в}} = 4,2 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$.

A. I Buzdin

F795. Water flows through a long channel with a rectangular cross-section, inclined towards the horizon. We can assume that the friction force of water against the bottom and banks of the channel is proportional to the average flow velocity and inversely proportional to its depth. During a Flood, the amount of water flowing through the channel cross-section in one second doubles. How does the average flow rate change?

G. L. Kotkin

F796. On the main optical axis $AB$ of a collecting lens with a focal length $F$ there is a flat mirror rotating with an angular velocity $\omega$ around an axis perpendicular to the axis $AB$ (see Fig.ok). A parallel beam of rays falls on the mirror, which, after reflection, is focused on a screen located in the focal plane of the lens. Find the speed of the light spot on the screen at the moment when it passes the focus of the lens.

E. I. Palchikov
