New admission to the All-Union Correspondence School of Mathematics

All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov (abbreviated VZMSH) announces an abundant, twentieth enrollment of students for the 1st year.

Generally, admission to VZMSH was carried out based on the results of the introductory test, but in the new year we decided to admit to the 1st year simply on the basis of applications from all seventh grade students and vocational school students interested in mathematics, with the exception of those living in the cities. Moscow and Leningrad.

Education at VZMSH is free, the program lasts three years. During this time, our students will regularly receive study guides from us, which contain binding theoretical questions and problems to solve.

It's not easy to study with us. Here, desire alone is not enough; diligence, perseverance, and willpower are also needed: after all, you need to study the theory on your own, with a pencil in hand, and then (which is the most interesting, but also the most difficult) - to solve more problems and complete a test. Assignments are sent approximately once a month.

Your test papers will be carefully checked and decided by VZMS teachers - students, graduate students and teachers of Moscow State University and other universities in the country that have VZMS filpals working on the same program and manuals. As a rule, tests consist of two parts: a mandatory one, containing relatively simple tasks, and an additional one, which is performed by stronger students.

The VZMS program is aimed at in-depth study of the main issues of the school mathematics course. During your studies at VZMSH, you will become more thoroughly familiar with the coordinate method, deepen your knowledge of integer arithmetic, solve many interesting problems in geometry, mathematical analysis and algebra, and get an idea of ​​written and oral entrance exams in various.by.

Studying at VZMSH will help you learn to work independently with a book, as well as clearly, concisely and competently express your thoughts on paper; These qualities will certainly be useful to you in the future. So, VZMSH is waiting for your applications.

To enroll in the school, you must send an application no later than March 1, 1983 (by postmark) to the address “117234, Moscow, B-234, Moscow State University, VZMSH. For example." or the address of the relevant filpal. Students living in the North-West of the RSFSR (in Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions, Karal ASSR), in the Baltic Union resource countries and in Belarus (except for Vitebsk and Gomel regions) should send to the address: 193130, Leningrad, 8 Sovetskaya, 3, ZMSH.

The application must indicate: regions and exact postal address with zip code; last name, first name and patronymic of the student, class and school in which he studies.

As your first task, you should study the article by N. B. Vasiliev and V. L. Gutenkhacher “Arithmetic and principles of calculation” (see p. 30).
