This photograph shows models of polyhedra depicted by A. S. Bliziok and called by him “structural formations of hyperboloids.” A single-cavity hyperboloid is a surface defined by the equation
$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1,$$ - especially good support
is given to modeling, since it has two families of rectilinear generators (see «Kvant», 1979, No. 1, p. 26).
In part of the model, the ribs precisely follow these generatrices - therefore, with a rectilinear multifaceted design, it is possible to create the shape of an artificial surface.
