⟦folio 36⟧

Tasks

1. Ivan Tsarevich went wherever his eyes looked to look for Vasilisa the Beautiful, who had been devoured by Kash, when Leshy met him. “I know,” he says, “I happened to go there on the way to the kingdom of Kashevo. I walked for four days and four nights. The first day and night was a straight road to the north, and I walked a third of the way. Then he turned west and continued through the forest for a day and covered half that time. And for three days he walked through the forest, already to the south, and came out on a straight road leading to the east. I walked along it a hundred miles in one day and ended up in the kingdom of Kashevo. You are as fast a walker as I am, go, Ivan Tsarevich, take a look, on the fifth day you will be visiting Kashay.” “No,” answered Ivan Tsarevich, “if everything is as you say, then tomorrow I will see my Vasilisa the Beautiful.” Is he right? How many miles has Leshy walked and how far does Tsarevich Ivan think?

2. A three-digit number is conceived, in which one of the digits coincides with any of the numbers 543, 142 and 562, and the other two do not coincide. What is the intended number?

3. In this number puzzle (see figure) almost nothing is unknown, but it allows only one solution. Find him.

4. A ping pong ball was thrown up. What will take longer - the ascent or the descent?

These tasks were offered to us
V. Zhuravlev, V. V. Proizvolov,
V. N. Denisenko, A. P. Savin.
