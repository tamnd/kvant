Assuming the impacts of balls on the surface to be absolutely elastic, show that after the first impact each ball hits the lowest point of the surface. F794. Two identical body-solided calometers of height $h = 75$ cm are filled one third with ice, the other with water at a temperature of $t = 10^\circ\text{C}$. Water from the second calometer is poured into the first, and the calometer is two-thirds full. After the temperature in the calometer is established, its filling level will increase by $\Delta h = 0,5$ cm. What was the initial temperature of the ice in the calometer? Density of ice - $\varrho_{\text{л}} = 9 \cdot 10^2 \text{ кг/m}^3$, water - $\varrho_{\text{в}} = 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$; specific heat of melting of ice - $\lambda = 3,4 \times 10^5 \text{ Дж/kg}$; the specific heat of ice is $c_{\text{л}} = 2,1 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$, water - $c_{\text{в}} = 4,2 \cdot 10^3 \text{ Дж/(кг·К)}$.

A. I Buzdin

F795. Water flows through a long channel with a rectangular cross-section, inclined towards the horizon. We can assume that the friction force of water against the bottom and banks of the channel is proportional to the average flow velocity and inversely proportional to its depth. During a Flood, the amount of water flowing through the channel cross-section in one second doubles. How does the average flow rate change?

G. L. Kotkin

F796. On the main optical axis $AB$ of a collecting lens with a focal length $F$ there is a flat mirror rotating with an angular velocity $\omega$ around an axis perpendicular to the axis $AB$ (see Fig.ok). A parallel beam of rays falls on the mirror, which, after reflection, is focused on a screen located in the focal plane of the lens. Find the speed of the light spot on the screen at the moment when it passes the focus of the lens.

E. I. Palchikov
