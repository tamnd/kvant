Matematika
and production

Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR
B. V. GNEDENKO

The purpose of each science is to search for the truth, develop research methods, and also to use the acquired knowledge to solve practical problems. This applies to all sciences, including mathematics. At all times, mathematics has fulfilled its obligations to faithfully assist practice in its most diverse manifestations. Initially, the range of applications of mathematics was quite limited - the use of arithmetic in mutual calculations, the use of geometry in determining distances, and calculating volumes and areas. Even in the ancient world, mathematics began to be used to study astronomical phenomena - the movement of luminaries, eclipses of Lunya and the Sun. Later, when mankind decided to go out to the open sea, interesting and complex problems of orientation in space without visible earthly landmarks arose. New problems of applied mathematics arose, which served as the subject of research by outstanding mathematicians of the 18th and 19th centuries.

A significantly new period in the development of the application of mathematics began in connection with the creation of elements of mathematical analysis in the 17th century. Of course, the influence of practice on the emergence of new concepts of mathematics related to motion, which led to the creation of differential and integral calculus, and later the theory of differential equations, which was exclusively perfect.
