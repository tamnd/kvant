This design of thin wires and stylized sticks, called by its creator E. Handlokov a “rod-cable multi-organic”, was demonstrated last year in Moscow at the exhibition “Architectural bionics, new forms and designs.”
The airiness and grace of its lines are combined with the complexity of the geometric design. For lovers of mathematics, “rod-cable polyorganic” by E. Handlokov -

source of interesting questions. I would like to understand its geometry, to find out to what extent it is an interweaving of regular polyorganics, as it seems at first glance. I would also like to understand the mathematical basis of such structures, to understand why threads and sticks without a single rigid connection can form a rigid configuration.

The photograph that we reproduced on the first page of the cover was taken from space using special equipment.
Note the winding river bed. In the area visible in the photograph, the river flows through a lowland. What did you explain?
Is it clear that rivers flowing even along the main plain meander, and on the meanders one bank is usually flat, and the other is round?
