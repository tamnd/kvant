Entrance workshop

Below are published materials from university entrance exams in 1982.

Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology

Matematika
Written exam

Option 1

1. Solve the inequality
$$81^x + 3^{2x+1} > 54.$$
2. In trapezoid $ABCD\ BAD = 90^\circ, ADC = 30^\circ$. A circle whose center lies on segment AD is tangent to lines AB, BC and CD. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid if you know that the radius of the circle is R.
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} tg y - tg x = 1 + tg x \cdot tg y \\ \cos 2y + \sqrt{3} \cos 2x = -1. \end{cases}$$
4. From the point $M(1; 1)$ tangents are drawn to two branches of the hyperbola $y = \frac{k}{x}$ ($k < 0$), tangent to these branches at points A and B, and the triangle MAB is regular. Find the coefficient k and the area of ​​triangle MAB.
5. In a regular hexagon, take $SABCDEF$ (S is the vertex), the length of the base side is 2. The vertices K and M of the rhombus KLMFF lie on the edges AB and SD respectively and $|KM| = 3$, and the segment KL intersects the edge SB. Find the volume of the prima.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\log_3 x - \frac{2}{1 + \log_2 7} = \frac{6}{3 + \log_3 x}.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\cos 3x}{\sin 3x - 2 \sin x} = tg x.$$
3. The isosceles triangle $ABC$ ($\hat{C} = 90^\circ$) and the triangle $DEF$ are arranged so that point D lies on side AB and point E lies on the extension of side AB beyond point A. The segment KL is the midline in general triangles, and the area of ​​quadrilateral $DKLB$ is $\frac{5}{8}$ the area of ​​triangle $ABC$. Find $DEF$.
4. Find the area of ​​the figure, which is specified on the coordinate plane of the system of inequalities

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 \geq 10 \\ 3x^2 - 4x - 32 \leq 0 \\ (3x - 2y)(3y - x + 10) \geq 0. \end{cases}$$

5. In the correct example $ABCA_1B_1C_1$, the length of the side edge is 3. Point M is the middle of the edge AC, point N lies on the edge $B_1C_1$, and point P belongs to the face $AA_1B_1B$ and is removed from the plane ABC at a distance of 1. It is known that an angle of 30° is formed by the straight lines PM and PN with the plane $AA_1B_1B$ and the straight line PN with the plane $BB_1C_1C$. Find the volume of the prima.

Option 3

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\frac{x+5}{2x+1}} - \sqrt{5x-3} = 0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2 - x \left( \frac{3-x}{4-x} \right) < 1.$$
3. The square $ABCD$ and the circle are arranged so that the circle touches the line AC at point C, and the center of the circle lies on the same side of the line AC as point D. Tangents to the circle drawn from point D form an angle of 120°. Find the ratio of the area to the area of ​​a circle bounded by a given circumference.
4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 3 \cos(4x-2y) = \sqrt{2} \cos(2x-2y) \\ \sqrt{2} \sin(x+y) = 3 \sin(y-x). \end{cases}$$
5. Spheres with centers at points $O_1$ and $O_2$ of radii 3 and 1, respectively, touch each other. Through a point M, distant from $O_2$ by a distance of 3, two straight lines are drawn, each of which touches both spheres, and the points of tangency lie on the straight lines on one side of the point M. Find the angle between the tangents if it is known that one of them forms an angle of 45° with the straight line $O_1O_2$.

Physics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Dog C runs at a constant speed $v_0$ along the path AB making an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal tension wire MN (Fig. 1). A light, horizontal hanging rope of length l is tied to the dog evaluator. The cable is connected to a pole K of mass m, which slides along the wire without any friction. Find the tension in the cable at the moment when the ring and the dog are at equal distances from the intersection T of the path and the wire.
2. A mole of an ideal gas is heated at constant pressure, and then, at a constant volume, it is transferred to a state with a temperature equal to the initial temperature $T_0 = 300$ K. It turned out that as a result, the amount Q = 5000 J was transferred to the gas. How many times does the volume occupied by the gas change?
