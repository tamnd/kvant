have projections - mathematical support, without which the machine is inoperable. Thus, mathematics itself was now turning into a production product!

It is very interesting to note how the idea of ​​which branches of mathematics are important for production has changed. Initially, only elementary arithmetic, geometry and mathematical analysis were used for these purposes. But gradually it turned out that new questions require new mathematical means of research. Thus, the emergence of advice among the applied disciplines of the theory of functions of a complete variable. Production management and computer programming made mathematical logic a direct productive force. Taking into account molecular and submolecular phenomena in electronic circuits requires widespread use of methods and results from the theory of probability and the theory of random processes. Production optimization led to the creation of new disciplines (linear and convex programming, optimal control, etc.). Computational methods have led to the widespread use of algebra, functional analysis, and probability theory. In essence, mathematics, even in its most abstract parts, turned out to be necessary for practice, including for organizing production.

There are no limits to the application of mathematics. As technical and in-depth roles and place of mathematical methods in practical life develop.
