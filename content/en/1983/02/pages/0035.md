$$\frac{S_{OB_2C_1}}{S_{OB_1C_2}} = \frac{0,5 \cdot b_2c_1 \sin \varphi}{0,5 \cdot b_1c_2 \sin \varphi} = \frac{b_2c_1}{b_1c_2},$$

$$\frac{b_2c_1}{b_1c_2} = k.$$ (2)

The ratio of the volumes of paramids is written similarly (the bundle consists of tetrahedra $OA_1B_1C_1$ and $OA_2B_2C_2$). Let us find, for example, the volume of the paramid $OA_1B_1C_1$ (Fig. 8). Let $C_1H$ be its height lowered to the base $OA_1B_1$, then

$$V_{OA_1B_1C_1} = \frac{1}{3} \cdot S_{OA_1B_1} \cdot |C_1H| =$$

$$= \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} a_1b_1 \sin \alpha \cdot c_1 \sin \beta =$$

$$= \frac{1}{6} a_1b_1c_1 \sin \alpha \sin \beta,$$

where $\alpha = A_1OB_1$ and $\beta$ is the angle between edge $OC_1$ and the base. Exactly the same

$$V_{OA_2B_2C_2} = \frac{1}{6} a_2b_2c_2 \sin \alpha \sin \beta,$$
