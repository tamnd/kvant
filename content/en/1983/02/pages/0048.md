M772. The workshop has five different machines. Training one worker to operate one machine costs 1,000 rubles. With each minimum cost, it is possible to train 8 workers so that, in the absence of any three of them, all the machines can be used at the same time? Each worker can only work on one machine at a time.

Answer: 20,000 rubles. Each machine must have the work of at least four workers: if only three (or less) workers can work on a machine, then in their absence the machine will stand idle. Therefore, training costs cannot be less than 5·4·1000=20,000 rubles. As can be seen from Tables 1 and 2, which define two different training schemes, this amount can be dispensed with. (In the tables, in the column of each worker, the machines on which he is trained to work are indicated.) The conditions of the problem are also met by all the tables obtained from tables 1 and 2 of the perset columns (re-measuring of workers) and rows (re-measuring of machines).

◆ Transform the conditions of the problem:
$$\vec{0}=\vec{A}\vec{N}+\vec{B}\vec{P}+\vec{C}\vec{M}=(\vec{A}\vec{B}+\vec{B}\vec{N})+(\vec{B}\vec{C}+\vec{C}\vec{P})+(\vec{C}\vec{A}+\vec{A}\vec{M})=(\vec{A}\vec{B}+\vec{B}\vec{C}+\vec{C}\vec{A})+(\vec{B}\vec{N}+\vec{C}\vec{P}+\vec{A}\vec{M}),$$
that is, $\vec{A}\vec{M}+\vec{B}\vec{N}+\vec{C}\vec{P}=\vec{0}$. This means that by plotting the vectors $\vec{A}\vec{M}, \vec{B}\vec{N}$ and $\vec{C}\vec{P}$ one after another, starting from an arbitrary point Q (see figure), we obtain triangle QKL.
Let us denote the side lengths of the triangle QKL by x=|QK|=|AM|, y=|KL|=|BN| and z=|LQ|=|CP|. Then |AB|=|AM|+|MB|=|AM|+|BN|=x+y, since according to the structure of tangents drawn from one point, |BM|=|BN|; similarly |BC|=y+z, |CA|=z+x. Triangles ABC and QKL have corresponding parallel sides, so they are similar and |AB|:|QK|=|BC|:|KL|=|CA|:|LQ|. Hence,
$$\frac{x+y}{x}=\frac{y+z}{y}=\frac{z+x}{z}=\frac{(x+y)+(y+z)+(z+x)}{x+y+z}=2.$$
So x+y=2x, y+z=2y. Hence x=y=z and |AB|=|BC|=|CA|.
