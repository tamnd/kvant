⟦folio 2⟧

Programming is the second literacy

Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences
A. P. ERSHOV

This article is based on the author's speech at the opening of the International Federation for Information Processing and UNESCO conference "Computer Applications in Education" in Lausanne (Switzerland) in July 1981. The text of the speech has been revised specifically for «Kvant».

I recognize that comparing programming to literacy is risky in many ways. On one side of our comparison there is an unusual profession that requires ability and long training, and on the other there is a common property, the most important property of a modern person.

Nevertheless, I will immediately try to explain why this comparison is respectful.
