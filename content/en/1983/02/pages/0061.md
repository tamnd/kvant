⟦folio 59⟧

Leningrad State Pedagogical Institute named after. A. I. Herzen

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of mathematics)
1. Simplify at $x > 1$ the expression

$$\frac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1} \cdot \sqrt[4]{3+2\sqrt{2}} + \sqrt[3]{(x+12)\sqrt{x}-6x-8}}{\frac{x-\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{1-2x+x^2}} - \sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1} \cdot \sqrt[4]{3-2\sqrt{2}}}.$$

2. Solve the inequality

$$\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{\left|\frac{x-1}{x+2}\right|} \geq \frac{4}{9}.$$

3. Find the monotonicity intervals of the functions $y = \frac{3}{2} \ln^2 x - \ln^3 x$.

4. On the bases $AB$ and $CD$, squares are constructed outside the trapezoid. Prove that the straight line connecting their centers passes through the intersection point of the diagonal trapezoid.

5. A ball of radius $R$ is inscribed in a paramid, at the base of which lies a rhombus with an acute angle $a$. The lateral faces of the paramid are inclined to the plane of the base at an angle of $φ$. Find the volume of the paramid.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Simplify the expression

$$\left[ \frac{xy^{\frac{3}{2}}-yx^{\frac{3}{2}}}{x^2+y^2} : \frac{(xy)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{(\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y})^2-3(xy)^{\frac{1}{2}}} \right] \times \left( \frac{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}}{xy^{\frac{1}{2}}+yx^{\frac{1}{2}}} \right)^{-1}.$$

2. Solve the equation

$$(0,4)^{1\lg^2 x+1} = (6,25)^{2-\lg x^3}.$$

3. Solve the equation

$$2 \cos 2x + \cos^2 \frac{x}{2} - 10 \cos \left(\frac{5\pi}{2} - x\right) + \frac{7}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \cos x.$$

4. Find the maximum and minimum points of the functions $f(x) = \frac{(x-1)^2}{x^2+1}$.

5. In a regular quadrangular paramid, the plane angle at the vertex is $60^\circ$. Prove that one of the two-dimensional angles of this paramid is half the size of the other.

Oral exam objectives
1. Knowing that $\lg 64 = k$, find $\lg^3 \sqrt{25}$.

2. Determine the sign of the number sin (2 arctan $\frac{1}{3}$ + arccos $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$).

3. Solve the equation

a) $\frac{1}{3} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{\log_3 \frac{x-2}{x-3}} = \frac{\log_3 3}{x-2}$;

b) $\log_2 \log_3 x + 1 = \log_2 \log_3 (4x-3)$;

b) $1-3 \sin^2 \alpha \cdot \cos^2 \alpha = \frac{7}{16}$.

4. Solve the inequality

a) $(0,3)^{\log_2(\log_3(x^2-19))-2} > 1$;

b) $\left| \frac{2-3|x|}{1+|x|} \right| < 1$;

b) $4 \log_{64}(x-3) + \log_2^5 > 50$.

5. Find the domain of functions

a) $\arccos (4-x)$ $\sqrt{x^2-x-12}$;

b) $\log_7-2x (x^2+11x-26)$;

b) $(\sin x+\cos x)^2 - \frac{1-\cos 4x}{2 \sin 2x} - \cos x$.

6. Find the set of function values

a) $(\sin x+\cos x)^2 - \frac{1-\cos 4x}{2 \sin 2x} - \cos x$;

b) $1-6 \cos^2 x+6 \sin^2 x$.

7. Graph the Function

a) $\frac{2x(2x+3)}{\sqrt{4x^2+12x+9}} - 4$;

b) $\cos^2 x \cdot \frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sin^2 x}{2}$.

8. A circle is inscribed in a rhombus. Each side of a rhombus is divided by a point of tangency into segments whose lengths are $a$ and $b$. Find the area of ​​the circle.

9. Lines are drawn through a point inside a triangle parallel to the sides. The areas of the triangles formed in this case are $S_1$, $S_2$ and $S_3$. Find the area of ​​this triangle.

10. Given a circular ring whose area is $Q$. Determine the length of the chord of the large circle tangent to the smaller one.

11. From the point $O$, which does not belong to the line $b$, a perpendicular $(OB)$ and an oblique $(OM) (M \in b, B \in b)$ are drawn to $b$. Prove that $\overrightarrow{OB} \cdot \overrightarrow{OM}$ does not depend on the position of the point $M$ on the line $b$.

12. Determine the area of ​​the total surface of the cone if the angle between the generatrix and the plane of the base is $a$, and the area of ​​the axial section is $Q$.

13. The height of a regular quadrangular paramid is 6 cm, the flat angle at the apex of the paramid is $60^\circ$. Find the volume of the paramid.
