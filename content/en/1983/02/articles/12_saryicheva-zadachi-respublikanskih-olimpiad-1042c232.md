⟦folio 51⟧

Exercise
republican
Olympics

Many of our readers have already joined the initial stages of the next cycle of the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren. «Kvant» daily in one of the latest issues talks about the final (all-Union) stage and publishes a complete list of tasks proposed there. Here we print some problems from the republican olympids of 1981 and 1982. Tasks 1-5 are taken from the recommended list of tasks for the republican stage, compiled by the methodological commission in mathematics of the Central Organizing Committee of the Olympiad; the remaining problems were proposed by local juries. After the problem, the class in which it was proposed is indicated in parentheses.

1. The numbers $a, b, c$ satisfy the correspondences
$$\frac{-a+b+c}{a} = \frac{a-b+c}{b} = \frac{a+b-c}{c}.$$
What can a number equal?
$$p = \frac{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)}{abc}?$$

2. Prove that for any numbers $p$ and $q$ the total length of the segments of the axis $Ox$ on which the inequality $|x^2+px+q| \leq 2$ holds does not exceed 4. (9)

3. An everywhere defined function $f$ for any $x$ and $y$ satisfies the correspondence $f(xy)=f(x)\cdot f(y)-f(x+y)+1$, and $f(1)=2$. Find: $a) f(m)$ for an arbitrary integer $m$; $6) f(\frac{m}{n})$ for an arbitrary rational number $\frac{m}{n}$. (9)

4. Prove that the polynomial $3x^{1982}+4$ cannot be represented as the sum of the squares of three polynomials with integer coefficients. (10)

5. Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$; lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $M$, and lines $AD$ and $BC$ intersect at point $K$. Prove inequality $S_{ABCD} > (|AB| \cdot |CD| \times \sin \widehat{AMD} + |BC| \cdot |AD| \cdot \sin \widehat{AKB})/2$. (10)

6. Prove that the polynomial of numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 1413, 1414 can be divided into groups so that the sum of the numbers in each group is equal to 1981. (8)

7. Find the length of the side of the smallest square in which three circles of radii 1, 2, 2 can be placed without overlap. (8)

8. The sequence $(a_n)$ is defined as follows: from the sequence of natural numbers 1, 2, 3, ... we take the first three numbers (that is, $a_1=1$, $a_2=2$, $a_3=3$), then we skip the next two numbers and take three numbers (that is, $a_4=6$, $a_5=7$, $a_6=8$), we skip even numbers, we take three numbers, skip six, etc. Calculate $a_{1000}$. (8)

9. The national football championship takes place in two rounds. At the end of it, it was found out that the teams scored points in an arithmetic progression, and the last team had 17 points. How long did the team participate in the championship? (8)

10. All diagonals are drawn in the hexagon. What seven angles between the diagonals or diagonals and the sides must be noted so that from the equality of these angles to each other it follows that the pentagon is regular? (8)

11. Compute for each $a$ the limit $(\sqrt{[(n+a)^2]}-n)$, where $[x]$ denotes the largest integer not exceeding $x$. (9)

12. Let $m_a, m_b, m_c$ be the lengths of the medians $\triangle ABC$. Prove that $m_a^2+m_b^2+m_c^2 \geq \frac{3}{4} \sqrt{abc} (\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}+\sqrt{c})$. For which triangles is the equal sign achieved? (9)

13. The point with coordinates $(x(t), y(t))$ moves in the plane so that at each moment of time $t$ the equality $y'(t) = \frac{1}{x(t)}, x'(t) = -\frac{1}{y(t)}$ is satisfied. It is known that at some point in time the coordinate point is (12; 3). Can this point at some instants of time have coordinates (6; 5)? (9)

14. Prove the inequality
$$\left(1 + \frac{1}{2^{51}}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{2^{52}}\right)\left(1 + \frac{1}{2^{53}}\right) \dots \times \left(1 + \frac{1}{2^{100}}\right) < 1 + \frac{1}{2^{49}}.$$ (9)

15. How many links can a closed broken line in space contain if its adjacent links are mutually perpendicular? (10)

16. Prove that the area of ​​the figure bounded by the $Ox$ and $Oy$ axes, the rectangle of the function $y=\frac{\sin x}{x}$, is not less than 1. (10)

17. Prove that if the inequality $|a_1\sin b_1x+a_2\sin b_2x+\dots+a_n\sin b_nx| \leq |\sin x|$ holds for all $x \in |-1; 1[$, then $|a_1b_1+a_2b_2+\dots+a_nb_n| \leq 1$. (10)

18. Is there a pair such that the sum of the plane angles of each polyhedral angle is a) $150^\circ$, b) $210^\circ$, c) $180^\circ$? (10)

19. Bus tickets are numbered with natural numbers from 000 001 to 999 999. How many digits 1 are there on tickets? (10)

20. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x^2+y^2+z^2=14, \\ xy+xz+yz=11, \\ xyz=6. \end{cases}$$ (10)
