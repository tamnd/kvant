QUANTUM SMILES

When in 1981, Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences A.P. Ershov received an offer to give an introductory report at the opening of the conference “Use of Computers in Education” in Lausanne, he came up with the idea of ​​illustrating his report not only with technical diagrams and graphs, as is explained, but also with drawings displaying his main ideas in an associative form. Such figure were made by his travel agent M. M. Zlatkovsky; they show simultaneously with the report using Diaxol. Here we publish a selection of these figure. As captions for them, we placed excerpts from the article by A. P. Ershov “Programming is the second literacy” (p. 2).

With words and example, the elder (teacher) taught the younger (student) the task. Understanding was not a purely enlightened insight, but mastery of examples of the matter being studied.

The process of accumulating knowledge was separated from its application, new forms of the language of knowledge and new assessments of the construction of truth appeared.

We all remember the simple and succinct words of Maxim Gorky: “Love books, the source of knowledge.”

Currently, man's ability to transfer knowledge to a machine is hopelessly behind the ability to create this machine.

We are literally programming our organization; the very development of the organization is the implementation of a genetic program.

Computers are already in many schools and will be coming there in increasing numbers.

Literacy and programming complement each other, and together they form a new idea of ​​the harmonies of the human mind.
