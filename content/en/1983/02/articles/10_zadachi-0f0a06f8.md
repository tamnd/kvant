⟦folio 35⟧

Exercise

1. Guess the economy of the shapes of the figures. Which piece should be placed next?

2. The ratio of two numbers is equal to a decimal fraction, the integer part of which is equal to the divisor, and the divisor is written after the decimal point. What are these numbers?

3. The turntable was immersed entirely in the stream so that its axis was horizontal and perpendicular to the direction of the flow: Will it spin?

4. The text is encrypted using a table: each number is associated with one of the three letters located below it in the table, and the “*” sign is associated with a space or one of the letters “yu”, “ya”. Decipher the following question and answer it:
5343934*150413*6*8156215044414*
*305041080?

These tasks were offered to us
N. I. Bovsunovsky, A. K. Zvonkin,
B. A. Mukushev, A. P. Savin,
