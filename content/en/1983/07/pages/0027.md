About the decimal notation of some numbers

In two books published by the Prosveshcheniye publishing house in 1980, about the meanings of the numbers $n$ and $e$ it is said that 707 decimal places were found for $n$, and more than two thousand for $e$. Let us present more complete data on these and some other numbers obtained using a computer.

In 1961, 100,625 decimal places for the number $n$ and 100,265 decimal places for the number $e$ were found. In 1964, more than a million digits of the number $e$ were calculated, and in 1967 - 500,000 digits of the number $n$, but these results were not published.

For the sign number $1 : \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ (“golden”)

2,878 characters calculated.

In 1970–71, one million digits of the number $\sqrt{2}$ were obtained. When checking the result, let's square it and it turns out to be one, followed by a decimal point and 1,000,082 nines.

About large prime numbers. In 1963, the 23rd Mersen prime number $^*$) $2^{11 213} - 1$ was found. It contains 3,376 digits and is fully presented in the book by M. Gardner “Mathematical Novels”, 1974, p. 349.

In 1971, the 24th number $2^{19 937} - 1$ was found, in 1978 - the 25th number $2^{21 701} - 1$, and in 1979 - the 26th number $2^{23 209} - 1$ and the 27th number $2^{44 497} - 1$.

Let's find the digits of the digits, as well as several first and last digits of the last of the listed Mersen numbers. The numbers $2^p - 1$ and $2^p$ have the same number of digits. Indeed, if $2^p$ had one more digit, it would end in zero. But this is impossible, since not a single power of 2 ends in zero. So, the digit of digits of the 27th Mersen prime number is equal to the number of digits of $2^{44 497}$. We have:

$$\lg 2^{44 497} = 44 497 \cdot \lg 2 = 44497 \cdot 0,3010 2999 57 = 13394,93172.$$

The characteristic of the logarithm is 13394, therefore, the number itself contains 13,395 digits. The mantissa of a logarithm is 93,172, so the number starts with the digits 8545.

It can be proven that the last three digits will be 6, 7 and 1. So:

$$2^{44 497} - 1 = 8545...671.$$
