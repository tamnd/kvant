On June 30, 1908, a meteorite fell 70 km from the village of Vanavara. This phenomenon had the character of a real catastrophe.
This is how the artist D. M. Utenkov saw the taiga near Vanavara in 1980.
This catastrophe is described in an article by V. A. Bronshten.
