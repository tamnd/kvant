And very often it is difficult to choose the best one from all the variety of options. For example, where to locate factories for the production of some new type of product? What capacities should be selected for these plants? If you make a few large factories, then the cost of production will be low, these products will be needed further. If you make many small factories, then you will have to transport less products, but the cost of manufacturing the product in small enterprises increases. To solve such and similar problems that are of more popular significance, thousands of electronic computers are continuously working, sorting through thousands of options per second.
