Dirichlet's theorem (1842). For any real number a and natural number m, there also exists p∈Z and q∈N such that q≤m and

$$\left| a - \frac{p}{q} \right| < \frac{1}{q^m}.$$

Proof. The interval [0, 1] is the union of m intervals

$$\left[ 0, \frac{1}{m} \right], \left[ \frac{1}{m}, \frac{2}{m} \right], \dots,$$

$$\dots, \left[ \frac{m-2}{m}, \frac{m-1}{m} \right], \left[ \frac{m-1}{m}, 1 \right]$$
