I. E. Tamm - man and scientist

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
B. M. BOLOTOVSKY

It often happens that physical laws and phenomena are named after scientists - discoverers: Newton's laws and Newton's rings, Ohm's law, Faraday's laws, the Mössbauer effect... And what do we know about the people after whom these laws and phenomena are named? What is their contribution to the development of science? What kind of scientific shift have they prepared for themselves? What human qualities do they have?

The answer to most of these and similar questions cannot be found in textbooks. And for those who are interested in physics, it is especially useful to read books that tell about the life and work of scientists. These books will expand our knowledge of physics, because we get an idea of ​​the progress of the development of science (in textbooks, as a rule, only the result of development is given). But perhaps more importantly, they introduce us to the personality of the scientist.

Memories of I. E. Tamm

Recently, the Nauka publishing house published a book of memoirs about the outstanding Soviet scientist I. E. Tamm. *)

Igor Evgenievich Tamm (1895-1971) - theoretical physicist, academician of the USSR Academy of Sciences, USSR State Prize laureate, Nobel Prize laureate. The range of his scientific interests includes: classical electrodynamics, electronic theory, quantum theory of solids, nuclear physics, elementary particle physics...

I. E. Tamm is the author of Plodov’s physical theories.

*) Memoirs of I. E. Tamm (M., “Science”, 1981).

A number of important discoveries are associated with his name. For example, he discovered the existence of such states of electrons on the surface of a solid body, when the electrons are “tied” to the surface: they cannot escape either inside the body or outside. These states are called “Tamm surface states”. They play a big role in solid state physics.

A more laborious article was published in the book about I. E. Tamm. His colleagues and friends write, his students (now famous Soviet scientists) write, his students’ students write, his summer camp comrades write, and even people who saw him only a few times write. They write about what an amazing, joyful and indelible impression Igor Evgenievich made on them, what a wonderful person he was, how he influenced those around him by his example. The authors of the memoir are not professional writers. An important reason was needed for each of them to look behind the pero. This reason is the same for all authors - deep respect and love for Igor Evgenievich.

Read the book of memoirs and you will see that overall it turned out to be interesting and useful.
