Hut on chicken legs

To build the “hut on chicken legs”, the photo of which is on the fourth page of the cover, you will not need anything other than a few boxes of matches. The matches in the hut are held “by themselves”, without the help of glue or any other fastening devices. Having understood the idea of ​​​​building this hut, you will be able to build many other similar structures in the future, perhaps in a more varied way. In the meantime, we recommend that you strictly adhere to the description below.

Let's start with the choice of building materials. For construction, the thinnest matches are best. The thinner the matches, the more of them you will need, but the simpler the hut will be. In the process of construction, matches are thrown outwardly, then they are perfectly illuminated from others in thickness, as well as bent ones.

Then we will prepare the construction site. Take some kind of solid base for it, for example a knik, on which you will, if necessary, turn the unfinished structure.

Finally, you can start construction. Place 5-6 matches parallel to each other on the base in a direction away from you, leaving a gap between them approximately the thickness of the match. (In the future, we will call the direction away from us *longitudinal*, and the direction perpendicular to it, that is, from left to right, *transverse*.) On the first layer, place the same second one in the transverse direction. These two layers form the floor of the hut. Now build the walls in the form of a well (two matches on the right and left in the longitudinal direction, two on them in the transverse direction, etc.). The width of the well should be greater than the width of the floor: when viewed from above, there should be a gap of one spine between the walls and the outer spokes of the floor. All wells should contain 4-5 longitudinal pairs of matches and the same number of transverse ones. The last pair of pins should go in the transverse direction. Place two layers of ceiling on top: longitudinal and above it, transverse.

Now comes the most delicate work - installing vertical matches in the walls. Place a coin of a suitable size on the hut (usually a three-kopeck coin is suitable). Pressing the coin to stiffen the structure, install the vertical spokes of the walls with their heads up. You should start from the right (or left) wall, since the spokes of the front and rear walls will hold on to the spokes of the floor with their tips, which promise to have some kind of roughness that impairs adhesion. Place the first spike in the right front corner - inside the well, but outside the floor and ceiling (you can temporarily move the longitudinal layers of the floor and ceiling to the center so that they do not interfere). Place the next spokes along the right wall, through them with the transverse spokes of the floor and ceiling. You can expand the spokes across the transverse layers using another spoke. All vertical spokes should be pushed down to the base. Having finished installing the vertical matches of the right wall (they should be on one head than in the transverse layers), install a similar left wall (this is where you will be able to rotate the stall horse).

Although the front and back walls have not yet been completed, what is there is enough to create a stable structure! While pressing down on the roof from above with a coin, simultaneously pinch the front and back walls with your fingers so that these walls are flush as far as they will go. Make sure that all the wall spokes are twisted. Now you can raise the resulting frame (it is better to continue removing the front and back walls). The spokes of the lowest layer will remain on the base, but the rest of the spokes will hold on! Under no circumstances should the right and left walls be removed; on the contrary, it is advisable to move them out as far as possible so that there is enough space for the front and back walls.

Installing the vertical matches of the front and back walls can, in principle, be done “in the air”, however, if you do this, you risk seeing your structure instantly turn into a chest match. Therefore, assemble the front and back walls as described above, and install the vertical spokes of these walls on the base. The extreme spokes of these walls have already been installed as part of the right and left walls. The installed spokes must pass inside the well, but in transverse layers.

After making sure that all the spokes have entered the spaces intended for them, alternately remove the right wall from the left and the front from the back, simultaneously pressing on the ceiling. It is necessary to lick all the spikes all the way. To do this, you may have to press on some spokes separately. After this operation, the weight of the read frame is obtained (photo 1).

(After the hardest part of the job is behind you, you can relax a little and take a closer look at what you have created. Please note: the “cube” you have built is not really a cube; its faces vary in size. Think about how to make a real cube.)
