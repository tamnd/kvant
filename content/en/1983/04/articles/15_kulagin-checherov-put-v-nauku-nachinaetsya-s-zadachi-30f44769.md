The path to learning starting with the task

Fifteen years ago at the Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova (IAE) began operating a physics and mathematics school for high school students. When biology was added to the subjects studied at the school, it was decided to call it the School of Natural Sciences (SNS).

It was the first school of this type at a major research institute. Its creation was initiated by the Komsomol Committee and the Party Committee of the Institute. The work of the OLS school was greatly helped by the leading teaching of the IAE, academicians A. P. Aleksandrov, L. A. Artsimovich, B. B. Kadomtsev, I. K. Kikoin, corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences I. I. Gurevich, V. M. Galitsky and much more.

What tasks were set for SHEN? There are three main ones.

Firstly, teach schoolchildren physics, mathematics, biology, deepening the school course, connecting it with the latest science and technology, using the rich potential of the Institute of Atomic Energy.

Secondly, identify capable students as early as possible, help them pass themselves and choose the right future path.

Finally, the third task is to develop in students Soviet citizenship, a materialistic worldview, everything that is included in the concept of communist education of youth. The Institute's Scientific Council paid special attention to this when speaking at a meeting related to the school.

And for fifteen years now, in the evenings, the lights come on in a two-story building located not far from the Institute, and students of 8th, 9th, 10th grades from many districts of Moscow come to it (after studying at a comprehensive school). Here they meet with their teachers two (or even three) times a week - researchers and engineers from the Institute of Atomic Energy, graduate students and senior students of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.

How is student selection carried out at school? This issue has been resolved for several years. The difficulty was that students coming to the entrance exams have a very wide range of knowledge. And not only due to their abilities, but also due to the fact that they represent almost forty different schools in Moscow. At the same time, the selection coefficient should be 4-5, that is, out of four or five applicants, only one can be accepted into the SHEN.

Numerous manuals published by SHEN and written by its teachers can give an idea of ​​further sections. The average number of books published in the “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” can also be called books such as “Mechanics”, “Laws of Electromagnetism”. At the end of the tenth grade, the story about individual reflections of physics is resumed. But it is conducted only by teachers, but also by the leading teaching of the Institute, invited to the school. Suffice it to recall the lectures of professors Ya. A. Smorodinsky, L. A. Mikazlyan, Yu. S. Lazurkin and many others, who brought together students, teachers of SHEN and a very large number of employees of the Institute. They provided a complete picture of the work of an engineer and scientist.
