⟦folio 53⟧

Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov announces enrollment of students in grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher education, primarily to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out on the basis of the results of the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 15 to the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, Correspondence School of Physics. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your home address and two copies of a questionnaire written on sheets of thick paper measuring 7x12 cm and filled out according to the following template:

The decision of the 3FS admissions committee on enrollment will be communicated by October 15. Verified introductory assignments will not be sent back.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments in various sections of physics studied in high school. Solved assignments are graded, solved and returned to students. Those who complete the 9th grade of 3FS are transferred to the 10th grade based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training course receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task

Those entering the 9th grade will be offered tasks 1-4 to solve, those entering the 10th grade - tasks 3-6.

1. A wedge with an apex angle of 90° and base angles α and β located on a horizontal table. Along its side edges, two bars of the same mass begin to slide simultaneously without friction. Will the wedge slide across the table (there is no friction between the wedge and the table)?

2. In a boat of mass M and length L there is a person of mass m. How far will the boat move if a person is moved from one end to the other?

3. A cylinder of mass M lies on an inclined plane with an inclination angle of 30° to the horizontal.

The cylinder is held at rest by a thread that goes around it, one end of which is fixed on an inclined plane, and the other is pulled vertically upward. What is the tension in the thread?

4. Estimate the mass of the Sun, knowing that the average radius of the Earth's orbit is 149·10⁶ km.

5. When the tip of an electric light bulb, the volume of which is 1 liter, breaks off at a depth of 1 m below the surface of the water, m = 998.7 g of water enters it. What was the gas pressure in the light bulb? Atmospheric pressure is normal.

6. A positive electric charge Q is uniformly distributed over a thin wire ring of radius R. Find the dependence of the model of the electric field strength on the axis of the ring on the distance r to the center of the ring.
