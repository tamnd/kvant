In conclusion, a few words characterizing certain features of Planck’s appearance as a person and his views.

Along with being busy with physics, Planck was interested in music throughout his life. However, in his youth, he even hesitated in choosing between physics and music. Planck was a non-professional panist, director of the academic choir and played the organ. He often played music with Einstein, who played the violin. Until a very old age, Planck was engaged in tourism and mountaineering. There is a photo of 84-year-old Planck on a three-thousand-meter peak in East Tyrol. In his nature there was a desire to overcome obstacles, although in general life he was a modest, dashing and serene person. Planck experienced great loss in his life. In the war of 1914, his eldest son was mortally wounded near Verdun; during fascism, his second son was executed for participation in an office conspiracy against Hitler.

Planck repeatedly showed friendly feelings towards our country. In 1925, he visited the Soviet Union in connection with the 200th anniversary of the USSR Academy of Sciences. To the question of a Komsomolskaya Pravda correspondent: “What did Soviet science give to the West?” — Planck replied: “Russian science has made a major contribution to world science in recent years. The works of Russian physicists, especially Iofe and Lazarev, are well known to me and my comrades. The names of many Soviet scientists are known in Germany.” The following statement by Planck was published in the Pravda newspaper: “I will take away from your country the joyful thought that not only scientists, but also the government and the public care about science.”

Planck's scientific ideas received universal recognition and brought him worldwide fame. He was elected a member of the academies of sciences in almost all countries, including the USSR Academy of Sciences.

Max Planck died in 1947 at the age of 89. His name has forever gone down in the history of science.

Our cover

《You diligently raised virgin soil,
And I only occasionally dropped a bouquet of flowers》

- this is what the German physicist Arnold Sommerfeld, who developed Bohr’s quantum theory of atoma, wrote in a letter to Max Planck. Planck, believing that in these lines Sommerfeld was clearly being unfair to his own merits, answered him with the following fourth:

“The flowers that you and I plucked,
They complemented each other wonderfully.
And you and I are the only two of them
The most beautiful wreath ever.”

The first page of the cover reproduces a photograph taken by G. M. Grechko and Yu. V. Romanenko from the Salyut-6 space station. They photographed the so-called zodiac light - the phenomenon of sunlight spreading in nearby space. Then the image underwent special processing, during which zones of different illumination received different flowers. This method makes it possible to assess the state of the interplanetary medium, its pronunciation, the degree of dustiness and change these values.
