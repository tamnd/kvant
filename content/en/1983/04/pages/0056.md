⟦folio 54⟧

Moscow State University
them. M. V. Lomonosova

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(x^2-3) - \log_2(6x-10) + 1 = 0.$$
2. A convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is circumscribed around a circle with center at point $O$, with $|AO| = |OC| = 1, |BO| = |OD| = 2$. Find the perimeter of the quadrilateral $ABCD$.
3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{5 \sin x + \cos 2x} + 2 \cos x = 0.$$
4. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the expression
$$\sqrt{3x^4-2-x^8} \cdot \sin(\pi(2x+16x^2))$$
makes sense and does not vanish.
5. Given a triangular pair $ABCD$ with vertex $D$, whose faces $ABD$ and $ACD$ are right triangles, the edge $AD$ is perpendicular to the median of the base $AK$ and $|AD| = |AK|$. The section of the paramid by a plane not passing through the midpoints of edges $AD$ and $BC$ is the equilibrium trapezoid $EFGH$ with bases $EF$ and $GH$, and point $E$ bisects edge $BD$, and point $G$ lies on the edge $AC$ and $|AG| = 3|GC|$. Find the ratio of the plane of the trapezoid $EFGH$ to the plane of the face $BCD$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_x(2x^2-3x-4) = 2.$$
2. In a trapezoid $ABCD$ inscribed in a circle of radius $r = 2$, the diagonal $AC$ is the Bissert angle $BAD$, and the length of the base $AD$ is twice the length of the base $BC$. Find the plane of this trapezoid.
3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2 \sin x \cdot \sin 2x} = \sqrt{5 \cos x + 4 \sin 2x}.$$
4. Find all values ​​of $x$ for which the expression

$$\sqrt{4x^4-3-x^8} \cdot (1-\cos(2\pi(2x+21x^2)))$$

makes sense and does not vanish.
5. In the quadrilateral paramid $ABCDE$, the base $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, and the faces $ADE$ and $BCE$ are right triangles. Edge $BC$ is perpendicular to the median $EP$ of faces $CDE$ and $|BC| = |EP|$. The section of the paramid by the plane is the equilibrium trapezoid $GKHL$, the vertices of which $G, K, H, L$ lie, respectively, on the edges $AE, BE, CE, DE$, with $|GE| = 3|GA|$ and $|CH| = |EH|$. Find the ratio of the area of ​​the trapezoid $GKHL$ to the area of ​​the face $ABE$.

Option 3
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)
1. Which number is greater: $\sqrt{8}$ or $2^{2\log_2 5 + \log_2 \frac{9}{2}}$?
2. Find all $x$ for which the function $y = 6 \cos^2 x + 6 \sin x - 2$ takes the most value on $R$.
3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{\sqrt{2-x} + 4x-3}{x} \geq 2.$$
4. The plant had several identical presses that stamped parts, and the plant produced 6,480 parts per day. After the reconstruction, all presses were replaced with more arbitrary, but also identical ones, and their number increased by three. The plant began to produce 11,200 parts per day. How many presses were there originally?
5. For all $a$, solve the equation
$$|x+3| - a|x-1| = 4$$
and determine for which $a$ it has exactly two solutions.
6. A circle with center at point $O$ is circumscribed around the triangle $ABC$. The tangent to the circle at point $C$ intersects with the line bisecting the angle $B$ of the triangle at point $K$, and the angle $BKC$ is equal to half the difference between the angle $A$ and the angle $C$ of the triangle. The sum of the lengths of sides $AC$ and $AB$ is $2+\sqrt{3}$, and the sum of the distances from point $O$ to sides $AC$ and $AB$ is 2. Find the radius of the circle.

Option 4
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{2} \cos \frac{x}{2} + \sqrt{2} \cos \frac{x}{4} = 0.$$
2. For what values ​​of $a$ is the solution to the equation
$$10x-15a=13-5ax+2a$$
more than 2?
3. It is known that $\log_b a = \sqrt{3}$. Calculate
$$\log \left(\frac{\sqrt{a}}{b}\right)\left(\frac{3\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}\right).$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$5^x-3^{x+1} > 2(5^{x-1}-3^{x-2}).$$
