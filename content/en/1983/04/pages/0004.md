Mathematics and technology in space

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. A. VINOKUROV,
Doctor of Technical Sciences
V. S. MITIN

The emergence of aviation and astronautics is inextricably linked with the use of mathematics to analyze the basic problems of flight, design and calculation of aircraft and rockets. The first question, hotly discussed at the dawn of aviation at the end of the 19th and beginning of the 20th centuries - the question of whether heavy impact devices could fly - was fundamentally resolved by the Russian scientist, aviation theorist N. E. Zhukovsky. Using purely mathematical methods (namely, using the theory of a function of a complex variable), Zhukovsky came up with a formula for the lift of a wing:

$$F = \varrho v \Gamma$$

(here $\varrho$ is air density, $v$ is wing speed, $\Gamma$ is “circulation”, a certain value depending on the shape of the wing profile).

Beginning with Zhukovsky, the most modern mathematical apparatus is used in theoretical aviation, and the problems that arose in the analysis of practical problems in aviation followed the main creation of new areas of mathematics.

The solution to a number of key aviation problems is associated with the names of famous mathematicians and mechanics of our country. We will only focus on the flutter problem. This phenomenon was discovered in the 30s of our century, when all-metal aircraft began to be built with a flight speed of 200-300 km/h. It turned out that at certain critical speeds, strong vibration of the aircraft—flutter—occurred, as a result of which the aircraft often collapsed in flight. The secret of this terrible thing for rafts is the secret of aircraft designers from many countries. The Soviet mathematician (president of the USSR Academy of Sciences) M.V. Keldysh managed to solve the flutter problem. He showed that flutter has a resonant nature, and, based on this, he proposed certain changes in the wing design. The very first flights of the aircraft, arranged according to Keldysh’s recommendations, produced excellent results.
