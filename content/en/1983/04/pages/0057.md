Option 5
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3} \sin 2x - \cos 2x = \sqrt{3}.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2 \frac{x}{x-1} \leq -1.$$

3. Solve the inequality $f(g(x)) < g(f(x))$, where $f(x) = 2^x - 1$, $g(x) = 2x + 1$.

4. The cone lies on the horizontal plane P, touching its lateral surface. The area of ​​the base of the cone is $S_1$, the area of ​​the lateral surface is $S_2$. At what height (above P) is the highest point of the cone?

5. For what values ​​of the parameter p does the equation
Does $$(x-p)^2[p(x-p)^2-p-1] = -1$$ have more positive roots than negative ones?

Option 6
(Faculty of Biology)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\log_1\left(1+x-\sqrt{x^2-4}\right) \leq 0.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x + \cos^2 3x = 1.$$

3. A cube is inscribed in the regular quadrangular paramid SABCD. The vertices of one of the faces of the cube lie on the base of the ABCD paramid. The vertices of the opposite face of the cube lie on the lateral edges of the paramid. It is known that $|SA| = |AB| = a$, that is, the length of the side edge of the paramid is equal to a and equal to the length of the side of its base. What is the volume of a cube?

4. Find the equations of those tangents to the graph of the function $y = \frac{x^3+1}{x}$, which, together with the coordinate axes, bound the triangle of area $\frac{1}{2}$.

5. Points P and Q are located on side BC of triangle ABC such that $|BP|:|PQ|:|QC| = 1:2:3$. Point R divides side AC of this triangle such that $|AR|:|RC| = 1:2$. What is the ratio of the area of ​​the quadrilateral PQST to the area of ​​the triangle ABC, where S and T are the points of intersection of line BR with lines AQ and AP, respectively?

Option 7
(faculty of soil science)

1. A fast train went from point A to point B. At the same time, a freight train came towards him from B to A, which met the ambulance $\frac{2}{3}$ hours after departure. The distance between points A and B is 80 km, the posedas moved at constant speeds. At what speed did the fast train move if it traveled 40 km in $\frac{3}{8}$ hours more than the time it took the freight train to travel 5 km?

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2-3 \cos 2x} = \sqrt{\sin x}.$$

3. Find the maximum value of the function $f(x) = |x^3+6x^2+9x+1|$ on the segment $[-3; 1]$.

4. A circle of radius 3 is inscribed in equilateral triangle ABC (where $|AB| = |BC|$). Line p touches this circle and is parallel to line AC, but does not coincide with it. The distance from point B to line p is 3. Find the distance between the points where the circle touches sides AB and BC.

5. The altitude length SO of the regular triangular paramid SABC is equal to 1, and the side lengths of the base ABC are equal to $2\sqrt{6}$. Points M and N are the midpoints of segments AC and AB. Calculate the radius of a sphere inscribed in the SAMN paramidus (that is, touching all lateral faces and the base).
