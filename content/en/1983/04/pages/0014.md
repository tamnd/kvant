⟦folio 12⟧

On April 25, Academician Andrei Nikolaevich Kolmogorov, one of the founders of the magazine «Kvant» and permanent first deputy editor-in-chief, will turn 80 years old.

The life of A. N. Kolmogov is inextricably linked with Moscow University, where he became a student in 1920 and where he was later a professor and dean; Now Andrey Nikolaevich heads the Department of Mathematical Logic and the Department of Mathematics of the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

By universal recognition, A. N. Kolmogov is one of the leading mathematicians of our time. Andrei Nikolaevich's works relate to a wide variety of branches of mathematics and its applications, ranging from the most abstract sections to such applied areas as hydrodynamics and control theory. During the Great Patriotic War, A. N. Kolmogov made a great contribution to the theory of artillery firing calculations. However, his work on probability theory brought him the most fame - Andrei Nikolaevich put this science on a solid axiomatic foundation and significantly enriched many of its sections (limit theorems, Markov processes, complexities of numerical sequences).

A. N. Kolmogov is not only an outstanding mathematician, but also an excellent teacher who trained many talented scientists. Among his students are academicians A. I. Maltsev, S. M. Nikolsky and Yu. V. Prokorrov, corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences I. M. Gelfand, and among mathematicians of the younger generation - doctors of physical and mathematical sciences V. I. Arnold, Ya. G. Sinai and the untimely death of V. M. Alekseev.

The pedagogical talent of A. N. Kolmogov manifested itself not only in the education of mathematical research: Andrei Nikolaevich taught a lot in a general secondary school, founded a boarding school at Moscow State University and taught there for 15 years; in the sixties, Andrei Nikolaevich headed the Commission for the reform of mathematical education and wrote a number of school textbooks.

The editorial board, the editorial board and the editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» congratulate Andrei Nikolaevich on his birthday and wish him good health and new successes in his teaching activities and in science.

Conversation with Andrei Nikolaevich Kolmogorov

We are in an old wooden house in the village of Komarovka near Moscow, where Andrei Nikolaevich usually spends the end of the week. Bright, modestly furnished room. In one of the corners there is an old but high-quality record player with special shelves for records. The walls are lined with shelves of books. In the middle of the room there is a large table with many books, reprints of articles, manuscripts, and art albums. Andrei Nikolaevich sits by the window at a small desk. Next to a lush typewriter and neatly folded sheets of paper covered with writing, there is a tape recorder on which our conversation is recorded. We bring to your attention a transcript of this conversation.
