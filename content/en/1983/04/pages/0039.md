— What to do if the enemy started to fire first, but doesn’t know how to shoot correctly?

Master's degree We know “winning positions” - when the number of beads that remains after a move is a multiple of 10. So you need to move to such a position as soon as the opportunity arises.

— How did you unravel the secret of this cerra?

Master's degree I began to reason from the end of the game, and not from its beginning. Let's say I won, that is, I took the last beads. I could only do this if the opponent had previously left 1 to 9 beads on the table. To force him to do this, I had to leave 10 beads on the table after my previous move. This is a winning position. In the same way, the next (actually the previous) winning position is to leave 20 beads. Winning positions are then periodically repeated. The very last, that is, the very first winning position is to leave 60 beads on the table. We know that you can get into it with the first move - by taking 6 beads.

As you can see, my secrets are very simple: think from the end of the game and find winning positions. And, of course, notice how they repeat it periodically.
