⟦folio 15⟧

— Do you go in for sports? Which one?

— I have never been involved in competitive sports. If I'm not mistaken, I've only competed in a 10K flat race three times in my life.

But I have always been very fond of walking and boating, and making long journeys by canoe or boat. I really like swimming, it’s similar in the mountains. In all these activities, I appreciate not only their health benefits, but the joy of the promise of nature that they bring.

I have always loved swimming in the surf. On sunny skiing days I like to do more skiing in just shorts. During such ski runs like this, I like to swim in the middle of the sun-drenched sgugovs in the river that has just opened up from the ice. However, I do not advise you to oblige you to imitate me in all this - you can simply enroll in some sports section that attracts you.

— Andrey Nikolaevich, what would you like to wish our readers?

— I myself am a scientist, and, of course, first of all, I wish our readers to make one or another contribution to science, big or small. I note, however, that if all our readers began to write independent scientific works, then scientific journals would not withstand such pressure. Therefore, I have a statement and a more modest wish - that your school passion for mathematics will be useful to you in later life. In «Kvant» we are just trying to show you (perhaps not enough) how diverse the applications of mathematical science are.

The conversation was recorded by A. V. Sosinsky
