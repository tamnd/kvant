Master of Games
visiting
y «Kvant»

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. L. GUTENMACHER

We invited the Master of the Game *), a well-known specialist in game theory, to the editorial office - he knows a lot of old games and comes up with new ones.

— Dear Master, please tell us about your goals.

Master's degree Let's start with the simplest game, which we will call.

A game for beginners. There are 66 beads on the table. The first player can take from 1 to 9 beads, the second player takes from 1 to 9 beads from the remaining ones, and so on. The one who takes the last beads wins.

The game is called a “Game for Beginners” - this has a double meaning. Firstly, the game is simple and using its example it is convenient to demonstrate methods of reasoning for novice mathematicians. Secondly, in this game, if played correctly, the starting player wins - the one who goes first.

— How will you care if you start?

Master's degree I will take 6 beads - there will be 60 left. No matter how the opponent goes, the next move I can make is that there will be 50 beads left, then 40, then 30 and so on until there are 0 beads left. I won.
