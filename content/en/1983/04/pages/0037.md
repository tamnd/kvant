⟦folio 35⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Tasks

1. My friend Sasha once told me: “The day before yesterday I was 10 years old, and next year I will turn 13 years old.” Could this be possible?

♦
2. The two altitudes of a triangle do not change the sides on which they are lowered. Find the angles of the triangle.

♦
3. Let's break the match in half. Let's break one half again. Let's try to break one of the resulting pieces in half again. Why does it become more and more difficult to break a match each time?

♦
4. Find all five-digit numbers equal to the cube of the number formed by their last two digits.

♦
5. What number should be put together with the “?” in the sequence 17, 23, 13, 11, ?, 15?

♦
These tasks were offered to us
S. M. Korshunov, A. P. Savin, S. R. Sefibekov,
S. M. Ushintsky.
