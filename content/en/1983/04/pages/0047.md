Let us use the identity $q^k - 1 = (q - 1)(q^{k-1} + q^{k-2} + \dots + 1)$. Putting $q = 1 + \alpha$ in it, we obtain that for all $k \ge 1$

$$(1 + \alpha)^k = 1 + \alpha((1 + \alpha)^{k-1} + (1 + \alpha)^{k-2} + \dots + 1).$$
