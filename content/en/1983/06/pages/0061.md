5. A homogeneous beam with a mass of $m=60$ kg and a length of $l=4$ m rests on a smooth floor and protrudes $B$, located at a height of $h=3$ m above the floor (Fig. 4). The beam forms an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the vertical and is held in place by a rope $AC$ stretched close to the floor. Calculate the rope tension, the floor reaction, and the protrusion reaction $B$.

6. A rectangular trapezoid is cut from a homogeneous sheet material. The base of a trapezoid is twice its height and its upper base is equal to one unit of length. Determine the graphical location of the center of mass of the trapezoid.

7. Two passengers of the same mass $m=70$ kg are on a platform standing motionless on rails. Platform weight $M=280$ kg. Each passenger starts running from the same relative platform with a speed of $u=6$ m/s. What speed will the platform gain if they jump in one direction simultaneously and sequentially? What if in different directions?

8. A bullet flying at a speed $v_0$ pierces several identical boards located at some distance from each other. In which board will the bullet get stuck if its speed after passing the first board is $v_1=0,8$ $v_0$?

2. A Primogol ponton, the mass of which is $M=700$ kg, has dimensions: length $l=5$ m, width $d=3$ m and height $h=0,7$ m. Find the draft of the ponton without load and the maximum group capacity with a height of sides above the waterline $h'=0,2$ m.

Tell phenomena

1. Determine the density of air at pressure $p=3,8 \cdot 10^4$ Pa and temperature $t=0^\circ C$, if under normal conditions the density of air is $q_0=1,29$ kg/m$^3$.

2. Room volume $V=100$ m$^3$. What is the mass of air leaving the room when the temperature rises from $t_1=10^\circ C$ to $t_2=25^\circ C$, if the atmospheric pressure is $p_a=102$ kPa? Air density under normal conditions is $q_0=1,29$ kg/m$^3$.

3. Two vessels of the same volume $V=10^{-2}$ m$^3$ are connected by a narrow pipe with a tap. The tap is closed. At the same temperature $T=300$ K, the oxygen pressure in one vessel is $p_1=0,8 \cdot 10^5$ Pa, in the second - $p_2=1,2 \cdot 10^5$ Pa. Find the mass of gas transferred from one container to another when the tap is opened, if the temperature remains unchanged. The mass of one mole of oxygen is $q=0,032$ kg/mol, the universal gas constant is $R=8,31$ J/(mol K).
