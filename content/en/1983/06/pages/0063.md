Acceleration of free fall from pretenbrets height.

3. A ball of mass $m=10$ g and charge $q=2 \cdot 10^{-4}$ C is suspended on an insoluble thread $l=25$ cm long in the field of a flat horizontal capacitor. The potential difference between the plates of the capacitor is $U=120$ V, the distance between them is $d=30$ cm. What is the period of oscillation of the ball on the thread?

4. The oscillatory circuit, consisting of an inductor and an air capacitor, is tuned to a wavelength of $\lambda_1=300$ m. In this case, the distance between the capacitor plates is $d_1=4,8$ mm. What should this distance be so that the circuit is tuned to the wavelength $\lambda_2=240$ m?

Optics

1. Two plane mirrors form a right dihedral angle. In a plane perpendicular to the edge of the angle, a ray of light falls on one of the mirrors at an angle $\alpha=60^\circ$. The point of incidence is located at a distance of $d=10$ cm from the corner edge. Determine the angular and linear displacement of the dihedral beam with respect to the incident one.

2. A ray of light falls on a glass plate with refractive index $n=1,5$. What is the angle of incidence of the ray if the angle between the reflected and refracted rays is $\gamma=90^\circ$?

3. The limiting angle of total reflection of the stack at the boundary with air is $\alpha_{01}=42^\circ$ ($\sin 42^\circ=0,669$), and at the boundary with water $-\alpha_{02}=62^\circ 30'$ ($\sin 62^\circ 30'=0,887$). Find the speed of light in water.

4. An accurate light source is placed on two vessels filled with liquid to a height of $h$. A round opaque plate floats on the surface so that its center is above the light source. What radius must this plate have so that no ray can escape through the surface of the liquid?

5. A flat mirror lies on the horizontal bottom of a reservoir. What is the depth of the reservoir if the ray reflected from the mirror emerges on the surface of the water at a distance of $l=3$ m from the point of its entry into the water? The angle of incidence of the beam on the surface of the water is $\alpha=30^\circ$, the refractive index of water is $n=1,33$.

6. The collecting line gives a real image of the predmeta, magnified by $\Gamma=2$ times. Determine the focal length of the line if the distance between the line and the image is predmeta $f=24$ cm.

7. The path of beam 1 passing through the scattering lens is shown in figure 10. In what direction does beam 2 travel after the lens?
