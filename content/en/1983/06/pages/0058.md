**Geometry**

1. The sides of the triangle $ABC$ are separated by the points $M, K, P$ such that $|AM|:|MB| = |BK|:|KC| = |CP|:|PA| = 1:2$. Find the area of ​​the triangle bounded by lines $AK, BP$ and $CM$ if the area of ​​triangle $ABC$ is $m$.

2. Find the angle of a triangle if it is known that the sides enclosing this angle are equal to $a$ and $b$, and the bisector of the angle is equal to $l$.

3. On the leg $BC$ of the right triangle $ABC$ as a diameter, a circle is constructed that intersects the hypotenuse $AB$ at the point $D$ so that $|AD|:|DB| = 3:1$. Find the perimeter of a triangle if the length of the altitude dropped from the vertex of the right angle is 3.

4. The area of ​​the parallelogram $ABCD$ is equal to $S$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of the side $AB, F$ the midpoint of the side $BC$, and $P$ the intersection point of the segments $EC$ and $AF$. Find the area of ​​the quadrilateral $BFPE$.

5. A trapezoid is described around the circle, the sides of which form angles $a$ and $b$ with the larger of the parallel sides. Determine the radius of the circle if the area of ​​the trapezoid is $Q$.

6. The radii $OA, OB, OC$ are drawn in the circle. Find the magnitude of the angle $AOB$ if $\overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{OB} = \overrightarrow{OC}$.

7. Plane angles $ASB, BSC, CSA$ terrad $SABC$ are equal to $60^\circ, 60^\circ, 30^\circ$. Calculate the cosine of the angle between the vector $\overrightarrow{SB}$ and the vector bisectrice of the angle $ASC$ if $|SA| = |SB| = |SC| = a$.

8. The quadrilateral $ABCD$ is defined by the coordinates of the vertices $A(-1; 2; 3), B(-1; 3; 1), C(-1; 7; 3), D(-1; 6; 5)$. Prove that $ABCD$ is a right triangle.

9. A plane is drawn through the side of the lower base of a regular triangular feature, intersecting the opposite side edge at point $S$ and inclined to the plane of the base at an angle $a$. Find the area of ​​the lateral surface of the resulting paramid with vertex $S$, if the length of the sides of the base is $a$.

10. Given a cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ whose edge is equal to $a$. Through the diagonal $AC$ of its face $ABCD$ a plane is drawn parallel to the line $BO_1$, where $O_1$ is the center of the face $A_1B_1C_1D_1$. Determine the area of ​​the resulting cross-section.

11. Find the cross-sectional area of ​​the paramid by the plane passing through the vertex $A$ and the points $M$ and $N$, which divide the edges $SB$ and $SD$ in the same ratio of 2:1, counting from the point $S$.
