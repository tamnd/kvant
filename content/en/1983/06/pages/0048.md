$$\frac{1}{2!} + \frac{2}{3!} + \ldots + \frac{n-1}{n!}$$
(here $k!$ denotes the product $1 \cdot 2 \cdot \ldots \cdot k$).

M810*. Prove that any convex polygon contains a rectangle whose area is no less than 1/4 the area of the polygon.
F. B. Vainstein

P818. Two rigid rods each of length $l$ are linked by a hinge at point $A$ (see figure Pisc. 1). The rod $BA$ is fixed at point $B$, while point $C$ slides along the directing line $BC$. The rod $BA$ begins to rotate with constant angular velocity $\omega$ around $B$ in the plane of the figure. What maximal velocity and acceleration will $C$ reach if the rods are in line with $BC$ ($\beta AC = \pi$) at the initial moment?
L. G. Marcovich

P819. Three small solids, whose mass ratio is 3:4:5 (the mass of the lightest is $m$) are held at three distinct points on the inside of a smooth half-sphere, fixed in its lowest point to a horizontal surface. At some moment the solids are freed. What maximal amount of heat can be irradiated by this system? For what initial position of the solids will this occur? All collisions are absolutely non-elastic.
S. K. Strokov

P820. The process $A—B—C—A$ takes place for one mole of ideal monoatomic gas $P$ (see figure Pisc. 2). Find the efficiency of the cycle.
A. G. Isergin, Ju. P. Malishev, V. R. Saulit

M. V. Semenov

a) square any number using no more than six operations;

a) Let us present a scheme for squaring any number $x$ ($x \neq 0$) in 6 operations. We will denote intermediate results by $x_1$, $x_2$, ..., $x_6$ ($x_6 = x^2$); Of course, they can be remembered and used repeatedly in subsequent calculations. So, we need to sequentially calculate: $x_1 = 1/x$, $x_2 = x+1$, $x_3 = 1/x_2 = 1/(x+1)$, $x_4 = x_1 - x_3 = 1/x - 1/(x+1) = 1/(x^2 + x)$, $x_5 = 1/x_4 = x^2 + x$, $x_6 = x_5 - x = x^2$.
