b) $a(x^2-x+1)=3x+5$ has 2 different roots?

15. Solve the system
a) $\begin{cases} \log_1(xy)+y+2=0 \\ 3^{2+y}+17=\frac{18}{xy} \end{cases}$
b) $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{4-2x}>\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{3x^2-2x+4}$
c) $\frac{2^{4x}+4^{x+1}+3}{2^x-1-4^x}<0$;
d) $2\log_1(\log_2 x)>\log_1(6-\log_2 x)$;
e) $\log_{|x-1|}\frac{7}{4}-x)>2$.

a) $\begin{cases} x^2+y^2\leqslant16 \\ 3y-6\leqslant0 \\ 2y+6\geqslant0 \end{cases}$
b) $\begin{cases} -x+y+1\geqslant0 \\ x+y-2\leqslant0 \\ 2x+8\geqslant0 \end{cases}$.

19. At what values ​​of the parameter $a$ the equations of the equation
$$\sqrt{9-x^2}\geqslant-a^2x$$
form an interval of length $\frac{15}{4}$? What is this gap?

20. Find the domain of functions
a) $2^{3+\sqrt{x^2-25}}+\frac{x-4}{x-6}$;
b) $4\pi+\frac{1}{x\sqrt{x+1}}+\log_2(2-x)$;
c) $\log_{2x-3}\frac{1-x}{x-3}$;
d) $\frac{\lg\sin x}{\sqrt{15x-x^2}-54}$.

5. Find the intervals of increasing and decreasing function $6-3x^2+x^3$.

9. Find the most and most values ​​of the function $f(x)=x^3+3x^2+5$ on the segment $[-1;1]$.
10. The movements of the first and second material points along one straight line are given by the corresponding equations $s_1(t)=t^3-3t^2+3t+2$ and $s_2(t)=\frac{1}{3}t^3-t^2$.
Find the maximum speed of the first point in the interval of decreasing speed of the second point.
