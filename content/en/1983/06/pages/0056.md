⟦folio 54⟧

Tasks
entrance exams
in mathematics and physics
in various schools
in 1982

We offer a selection of problems for entrance exams in mathematics and physics in 1982 at 6 universities (Voronezh, Gorky, Kiev, Tartu, Tashkent and Ural), 4 pedagogical institutes (Kazan, Odesk, Omsk and Yaroslavl) and 3 polytechnic institutes (Chisinau, Pijk and Tomsk), as well as the Moscow Architectural Institute.

Mathematics
Written exam
Algebra

1. We mixed a thirty percent solution of hydrochloric acid with a ten percent solution and got 600 g of a fifteen percent solution. How many grams of each calculator were taken?

2. A man moves away from the foot of a 60 m high tower at a speed of 8 km/h. What is his velocity of movement away from the top of the tower when he is 80 m from the base? The movement will occur in the horizontal plane.

3. Find $x$ from proportion

$$x \cdot 34 \frac{2}{5} = 0,4 + 8 \cdot \left(5 - 0,8 \cdot \frac{5}{8}\right) - 5:2 \frac{1}{2}$$

$$= \frac{90}{1 \frac{7}{8} \cdot 8 - 8,9 + 2,6 : \frac{2}{3}}.$$

4. Simplify

a) $$\left(\frac{3 \sqrt{3} - 2 \sqrt{2}}{5 + \sqrt{6}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}}\right) \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}} + \frac{6}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2}};$$

b) $$a^{\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{a - a^{-2}}{a^{\frac{1}{2}} - a^{-\frac{1}{2}}} + \frac{1 + a^{-2}}{a^{\frac{1}{2}} + a^{-\frac{1}{2}}} + \frac{2}{a^{\frac{3}{2}}}.$$

5. Prove that the meaning of the expression

$$\left(\frac{a^{\frac{1}{4}} + b^{\frac{1}{4}}}{a^{\frac{1}{4}} - b^{\frac{1}{4}}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{a^{\frac{1}{4}} - b^{\frac{1}{4}}}{a^{\frac{1}{4}} - b^{\frac{1}{4}}}\right)^2 : \frac{b - 2 \sqrt{ab} + a}{\sqrt{a} (\sqrt{b} - \sqrt{a})}$$

does not depend on the values ​​of $a$ and $b$.

6. Simplify and continue the expression

$$x = \left[ \frac{4 \sqrt{2} \sin \frac{\alpha}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{\alpha}{2}}{(1 - \cos 2 \alpha)^{\frac{3}{2}}} - \frac{1}{\cos \alpha \left(\cos^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)} \right]^{\frac{1}{5}}$$

7. Prove that $16 \sin 10^\circ \cdot \sin 30^\circ \cdot \sin 50^\circ \cdot \sin 70^\circ \cdot \sin 90^\circ = 1$.

8. Prove that if $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = \pi$ then $\cos^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \beta + \cos^2 \gamma = 1 - 2 \cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta \cdot \cos \gamma$.

9. The sum of thirty progressions is 72 times less than the sum of her next sixty progressions. Find the ratio of the fiftieth term of the progression to its tenth term.

10. Solve the equation

a) $$\frac{2}{x} - \frac{1}{1-x} = 2;$$

b) $$\left(\frac{1}{8}\right)^x \cdot 4^{\frac{x^2}{2}} = 8^{-\frac{2}{3}x};$$

b) $$\frac{2^{x^2} \cdot 5^{x^2} - 0,1^3 \cdot (10^{3-x})^2}{\sqrt{x+2}} = 0;$$

d) $5 \cdot 4^x - 3 \cdot 10^x - 2 \cdot 25^x = 0;$

e) $1 - \lg (5x) = \lg (x - 1);$

e) $\log_2^2 x + 3 = 2 \log_2 x^2;$

g) $5 \log_4 x + 3 \log_2 x = 8;$

h) $\sin x (\sin x + \cos x) = 1;$

i) $\sin^4 x + \sin^4 \left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{4};$

j) $\cos^3 x + \cos^2 x - 4 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2} = 0;$

k) $tg 5x - tg 3x - 2 tg 2x = 0;$

m) $\sin 2x (2 \sqrt{3} \sin x + 2 \cos x) + \sqrt{8} = \cos 2x (2 \sqrt{3} \cos x - 2 \sin x).$

11. Find the solution to the equation

$$\sin x - \cos x = \frac{3}{2},$$

which satisfy $|x| < 3$.

12. For each of the roots of the equation

$$4 \cos x \cdot \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - x\right) = \sqrt{3}$$

does the expression $2x^2 + 4x - 3$ have the smallest value?
