QUANTUM SMILES

Joke problem
Physics graduate students at Moscow State University learned about this problem from mathematics graduate students about three years ago; the author of the problem could not be more precisely identified. It seems that it was specially created for specialists in the theory of gravitationalization, but it is also useful for readers of Kvanat to try their hand at it, especially for students in grades 4-5.

So, the task:
Two people stand on balconies of different floors of a multi-story building, located one above the other on the same vertical. However, in time (for example, according to a radio signal of the exact time), they shout to each other: “Ah”.
Which of them will succeed in screaming from the ground earlier - the bottom one or the top one? To ensure that your own scream does not interfere with the service, the duration of the sound signal can be considered very short.
S. S. Khilkevich

From scientific folklore
Two people on an impact ball will sing in an unfamiliar place.
- Listen! - they shout to a random passerby. -Where are we?
He looks at them outwardly and shouts in response:
- You are on the impact ball!
“He’s probably a mathematician,” one astronaut says to another.
- Where did you get this from?
- Firstly, he thought. Secondly, he is absolutely right. And thirdly, it is completely unclear how his answer can be used at all.
