Applicant's workshop

Below are published materials from university entrance exams in 1982.

Leningrad State University
them. A. A. Zhdanova

Matematika
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)

1. For what $a$ systems of equation
$$\begin{cases} y = \sin(a + \pi x) + \sin(a - \pi x) \\ 3 \sin a = \frac{1}{1 + y^2} \left( \frac{3}{2} + x + \frac{1}{2x^2} \right) \end{cases}$$
has a unique solution in the region $x \in \left[ 1; \frac{3}{2} \right], y \in \left[ -\infty; 0 \right]$?

2. Solve the equation
$$2(\sin 3x + \cos x) = (\sin x + \cos x)^2 + (\sin 2x + \cos 2x)^2.$$

3. Solve the inequality
$$\cos x + \sqrt{2 + \cos x} \leq 0.$$

4. In a convex pentagon $ABCDE$ with common sides $P, Q$ of sides $AB, CD$ and $S, T$ of sides $BC, DE$ are connected by segments $PQ$ and $ST$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the connected segments $PQ$ and $ST$. Find the length of segment $MN$.

5. Given a cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$ with edges of length $a$. On the edge $AA'$ a point $E$ is taken so that $|AE| = \frac{1}{4} a$. Find the volume of a pyramid whose vertex coincides with the point $A'$, and the base is a section passing through the points $D, E$ and the middle of the edge $BB'$.

Option 2
(Physics Department)

1. An archer stands on the shore above a cut-off volume whose height is $H$. The initial velocity vector of the arrow makes an angle $a$ with the horizon and at the apex is equal to $v$. After what time $T$ after the start of the flight will the arrow reach a height $h$ above the water? Neglect the size of the arrow and the height of the shooter.

2. At what $x$ function
$$f(x) = \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3 + \sqrt{4} + x}}} + \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3 + \sqrt{4} - x}}}$$

takes the most meaning?

3. Give the negative roots of the equation
$$\frac{1}{\sin x} + \frac{1}{\sin 2x} + \frac{1}{\sin 4x} = 0,$$
satisfying inequality
$$x^3 + 5x^2 - 3x - 3 > 0.$$

4. Calculate the values ​​of tan 37°30′.

5. In a regular quadrangular straight line, the sides of the base are equal to $a$, the side edge forms an angle $a$ with the plane of the base. A cube is inscribed into this straight line so that its four vertices lie on the apophas of the straight line, and the base belongs to the base of the straight line. Find the volume of the cube.

Option 3
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. By mixing 2 cm$^3$ of three substances, we obtained 16 g of the mixture. It is known that 4 g of the second substance occupy a volume 0.5 cm$^3$ larger than 4 g of the third substance. Find the density of a third substance if it is known that the mass of the second substance in the mixture is twice the mass of the first.

2. The altitude of a right triangle dropped to the hypotenuse is $h$. What is the shortest length of a medana that bisects the longer leg?

3. Solve the equation
$$8 \sin^2 x + \sin 2x = 5.$$

4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 3^x - 2^{y^2} = 77 \\ \frac{x}{3} - \frac{y^2}{2} = 7. \end{cases}$$

5. Solve the equation
$$13^{\frac{x}{13}} + 13^{\frac{x}{13}} - 1 + \dots = 15^{2x} + 15^{2x} - 1 + \dots$$

Option 4
(Faculty of Economics and Faculty of Psychology)

1. The candles have the same length, but different thicknesses. The first candle was lit one hour earlier than the others, lit at the same time. At some points, the first and second candles became the same length, and two hours later the first and third candles became the same length. How many hours is the first side of the candle, if the second side is 8 hours, and the third is 12 hours? It is assumed that the speed of the candles does not depend on the duration of combustion.

$$\begin{cases} \log_{|xy|} (x^2-y^2) = 1 \\ \log_{|xy|} (x-y) = 0. \end{cases}$$

3. Express the values ​​of lg 640 in terms of the values ​​of $a = \lg 62,5$.

4. Find the number of roots of the equation $3x^4 + 8x^3 - 6x^2 - 24x - 10 = 0$ on the interval [0; 3].

Physics
Written exam
Faculty of Physics
Option 1
1. A wedge, the inclined surface of which forms an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon, can move translationally along a horizontal surface (Fig. 1). With what force $F$ should the thread be pulled in the horizontal direction so that the block moves up the wedge? With what accelerations will the block and the wedge move? The mass of the wedge is $M$, the mass of the block is $m$. Friction, thread mass and block mass claim.
2. Is a gas expanding according to the law $pV^2 = const$ heated or cooled?
3. The electrical circuit shown in figure 2 has been assembled. How will the voltmeter reading change if switch $K$ is open? The resistance of the voltmeter is assumed to be infinitely large.
4. A screen is placed on the path of a plane monochromatic wave, at point $A$ of which an indicator is placed to record the radiation energy brought per unit time per unit area of ​​the screen. How many times will the instrument reading change if a collecting lens is placed in front of the screen at a distance $L$ from it so that point $A$ lies on its main optical axis (Fig. 3)? Plot graphs of the dependence of the desired value on $L$ for a fixed focal length of the lens $F$ and on $F$ for a fixed $L$. Loss of energy in the face of the claim.
5. Theoretical question. Transformer. Transmission and distribution of electricity.

Option 2
1. A ball of mass $m$ hits a stationary ball of mass $M$ with a speed $v$ along the line connecting their centers. After being removed, the speed of the first ball becomes half that of the original one. Find the ratio of the total kinetic energy of the obikhhars after removal to the initial kinetic energy of the attack ball.
2. One mole of a monatomic ideal isochoric gas is cooled so that its pressure decreases by a factor of $n$. The isobaric gas then expands so that its volume increases $n$ times. Determine the number $n$ if it is known that the total amount of heat imparted to the gas during these transitions is two times less than its initial internal energy.
3. An electrical circuit has been assembled, shown at figure 4. How will the ammeter reading change if the key $K$ is closed, that is, the points $A$ and $B$ are short-circuited?
4. Rays from the Sun fall on a small flat mirror of a rectangular shape and, after reflection, fall on a screen where the received solar rays do not fall. What shape does the illuminated spot on the screen have?
5. Theoretical question. The concept of wave and quantum properties of light.
Oral exam assignment
Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics, Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes, Faculty of Chemistry and Geology
1. Along the wooden frames forming an angle $\alpha$ with the horizon, insert a box behind the rope. What is the toe friction coefficient of the box if the least force required is obtained when the rope makes an angle $\beta$ with the vertical?
2. A body of mass $m$ is acted upon by a constant force $F$, directed horizontally, during time $t$. The coefficient of friction of a body on the horizontal surface on which it lies is equal to $\mu$. How far will the body travel before stopping?
3. Two stars with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ move in such a way that the distance between their centers remains unchanged. Determine the nature of the motion of stars (trajectory, speed, acceleration), noting their interaction with other celestial bodies.
4. Air with mass $m=2\mathrm{kg}$ is in a cylinder closed by a movable piston. How much work will be done when air is heated isobarically by $\Delta T=100$ K?
5. One mole of hydrogen is heated at constant pressure. What amount of heat must be imparted to hydrogen in order for its volume to escape? How much work will be done by the gas?
