⟦folio none⟧

To visually depict various processes occurring in the world of elementary particles, physicists use special drawings called graphs. Several such graphs are drawn above.

1. Electromagnetic interaction of two electrons.
2. The birth of photons during the annihilation of both electron and positron. The production process is in two stages, each of which emits one photon.

3. Neutron beta decay according to the old theory: a proton, an electron and an antinetrino are released simultaneously.
4. Beta decay according to a new theory - the theory of electroweak interaction.
5. The birth of two W bosons during the annihilation of a proton and an antiproton. (More often, some other particles are emitted together with one of the bosons.)
6-7. Examples of decays of the formation of W-bosons. You can read about the discovery of W bosons in the “Science News” section.
