⟦folio 39⟧

Tasks

1. They paid a ruble for the book, and there is still as much left to pay as there would have been left to pay if they had paid for it as much as there was left to pay.
   How much does the book cost?
2. Arrange the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 in circles on figure so that the sums of the heels of the tracks of numbers located along the “segments” are equal to each other.
3. What is the most divisible number of the product of any three consecutive four numbers?
4. Two people play tic-tac-toe on a 3x3 board according to modified rules: each can place both a tic and a toe on their turn. The winner is the one whose move produces three identical icons in a row (vertically, horizontally or diagonally - as in regular tic-tac-toe). Who wins this game - the beginner or the second player? And how?
5. On a sunny day, I was driving a car on a wet road with Uncle Seryozha, an experienced driver. Lumps of dirt flying from under the wheels of cars in front fell on the windshield. When the car stopped at a red traffic light, I asked:
   - Uncle Seryozha, can I quickly wipe the glass with a wet cloth?
   “No way,” answered Uncle Seryozha, “otherwise you’ll have to do this all the time!”
   Explain what's going on here.

These tasks were offered to us
N. K. Antonovich, M. A. Mnatsakanyan,
