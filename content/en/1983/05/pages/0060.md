Physics

In 1982, entrance exams in physics at MISIS were conducted in written form. Each applicant was offered a separate option containing ten problems in various sections of physics. The results of the work were processed on a computer.

Option

1. Material point of linear motion from point $A$ to point $B$. The first half of the path the point moves at a speed of $v_1 = 20$ m/s, and the second half - at a speed of $v_2 = 30$ m/s. Determine the average speed $v$ of the point in the area $AB$.

2. Two bars with masses $m_1 = 1$ kg and $m_2 = 2$ kg, respectively, located on a horizontal surface and connected to each other by a non-stretchable and inextensible thread capable of withstanding a maximum breaking number $F = 5$ N. Determine the maximum force $F_M$ with which you can pull a block of mass $m_2$ along a straight line that is a continuation of the thread, which this thread does not broke off. (There is no tranny between the bars and the surface.)

3. A small flat body begins to move upward along the overhead plane with an initial speed of $v_0 = 10$ m/s. Determine the distance $l$ that the body will travel along the overhead plane until it comes to a complete stop if the angle of the overhead plane to the horizon is $\alpha = 30^\circ$, and the friction coefficient between the body and the surface of the overhead plane is $\mu = 0,6$.

4. In a narrow glass tube, one end of which is sealed, there is air, locked by a table of ruti닐 with a length of $l = 20$ cm. When the tube stands vertically with the end up, the volume of air under the table of ruti닐 is equal to $V_1 = 1,5$ cm$^3$. What volume $V_2$ will the trapped air occupy when the tube is in a horizontal position, if the atmospheric pressure is $p_a = 750$ mm Hg. Art., and the air temperature is constant?

5. A lead bullet traveling at a speed of $v = 300$ m/s hits a steel plate and is left behind. By how many degrees $\Delta t$ does the temperature of the bullet increase if $\alpha = 40\%$ of its kinetic energy at the moment of impact was used to heat the plate and the surrounding air?

6. Two point charges $q_1 = 3,2 \cdot 10^{-10}$ Kl and $q_2 = 8 \cdot 10^{-12}$ Kl are in a vacuum at a distance of $r_1 = 72$ cm. What work $A$ must be done to liquefy them to a distance of $r_2 = 32$ cm?

7. A flat air capacitor having a distance between plates of $d_1 = 1$ cm, charges up to a potential difference of $U_1 = 100$ V, and then disconnected from the network. Determine the potential difference $U_2$ between the capacitor plates if they are separated to a distance $d_2 = 2$ cm.

8. The heater of an electric kettle consists of two spirals of equal resistance. When connecting the spirals in parallel and plugging them into the network, the water in the kettle boils in $t_1 = 3$ minutes. After what time $t_2$ will water boil, having the same mass and the same initial temperature, if the heater spirals are connected in series and connected to the same network?

9. A vertical rod $l = 2$ m long, completely covered with water with a propolymerity index $n_B = 1,33$, rests on the horizontal do of the tank. Determine the length of the shadow $x$ from the rod at the bottom of the tank if the Sun's rays fall on the surface of the water at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$.

10. The potassium surface exhibits a flux of monochromatic light with a wavelength of $\lambda = 0,331 \cdot 10^{-6}$ m. Determine the speed $v$ of electrons ejected from potassium surfaces if the electron work function is $A_B = 2,2$ eV.
