regu. The surface speed of waves in water is v. After what time does the wave arrive at point O after the stone is placed on it, if the stone is compacted at a distance L from the shore?

time t circle of radius R=vt (t=0 at the moment the stone falls). Relative to the shore, the center of this circle—point A at figure—moves at speed $\vec{u}$. By the moment of time t, when the wave reaches point O, point A will move to point $A'$, having passed the path $|AA'|=u\tau$.

We will find time t from the condition (see figure)
$$L^2+(u\tau)^2=(v\tau)^2.$$
From here
$$\tau=\frac{L}{\sqrt{v^2-u^2}}.$$
P. I. Zubkov

F799. The height of a vertical water fountain from the level of the pump outlet pipe is equal to H. How many times should the pump power be changed so that the total height of the fountain remains the same after connecting a vertical pipe of the same diameter with a height h<H to the pump outlet pipe?

The mass of water passing per unit time through the cross-section of the pump outlet pipe is equal to m=qvs, where q is the density of water, v is the speed of water flow in the pump outlet pipe, s is the cross-sectional area of ​​the pipe. The power of the pump is equal to the energy “carried away” by water per unit time, that is, equal to N=mv^2/2. In order for the water in the fountain to rise to a height H, its speed at the height from the pipe must be equal to v=\sqrt{2gH}.

Therefore, the pump power should be equal
$$N=\frac{mv^2}{2}=\frac{1}{2}qs(2gH)^{3/2}.$$
We obtain a similar expression for power in the case when an additional pipe of height h is connected to the output pipe of the pump. The “output” water speed in this case should be $v'=\sqrt{2g(H-h)}$, and the power should be
$$N'=\frac{1}{2}qs(2g(H-h))^{3/2}.$$
Thus, in order for the total height of the fountain to remain the same, the pump power must be reduced by
$$k=\frac{N'}{N}=\left(1-\frac{h}{H}\right)^{3/2}.$$
P. I. Zubkov
