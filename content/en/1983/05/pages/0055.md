⟦folio 53⟧

Applicant's workshop

Below are published materials from university entrance exams in 1982.

Leningrad State University
them. A. A. Zhdanova

Matematika
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)

1. For what $a$ systems of equation
$$\begin{cases} y = \sin(a + \pi x) + \sin(a - \pi x) \\ 3 \sin a = \frac{1}{1 + y^2} \left( \frac{3}{2} + x + \frac{1}{2x^2} \right) \end{cases}$$
has a unique solution in the region $x \in \left[ 1; \frac{3}{2} \right], y \in \left[ -\infty; 0 \right]$?

2. Solve the equation
$$2(\sin 3x + \cos x) = (\sin x + \cos x)^2 + (\sin 2x + \cos 2x)^2.$$

3. Solve the inequality
$$\cos x + \sqrt{2 + \cos x} \leq 0.$$

4. In a convex pentagon $ABCDE$ with common sides $P, Q$ of sides $AB, CD$ and $S, T$ of sides $BC, DE$ are connected by segments $PQ$ and $ST$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the connected segments $PQ$ and $ST$. Find the length of segment $MN$.

5. Given a cube $ABCDA'B'C'D'$ with edges of length $a$. On the edge $AA'$ a point $E$ is taken so that $|AE| = \frac{1}{4} a$. Find the volume of a pyramid whose vertex coincides with the point $A'$, and the base is a section passing through the points $D, E$ and the middle of the edge $BB'$.

Option 2
(Physics Department)

1. An archer stands on the shore above a cut-off volume whose height is $H$. The initial velocity vector of the arrow makes an angle $a$ with the horizon and at the apex is equal to $v$. After what time $T$ after the start of the flight will the arrow reach a height $h$ above the water? Neglect the size of the arrow and the height of the shooter.

2. At what $x$ function
$$f(x) = \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3 + \sqrt{4} + x}}} + \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3 + \sqrt{4} - x}}}$$

takes the most meaning?

3. Give the negative roots of the equation
$$\frac{1}{\sin x} + \frac{1}{\sin 2x} + \frac{1}{\sin 4x} = 0,$$
satisfying inequality
$$x^3 + 5x^2 - 3x - 3 > 0.$$

4. Calculate the values ​​of tan 37°30′.

5. In a regular quadrangular straight line, the sides of the base are equal to $a$, the side edge forms an angle $a$ with the plane of the base. A cube is inscribed into this straight line so that its four vertices lie on the apophas of the straight line, and the base belongs to the base of the straight line. Find the volume of the cube.

Option 3
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. By mixing 2 cm$^3$ of three substances, we obtained 16 g of the mixture. It is known that 4 g of the second substance occupy a volume 0.5 cm$^3$ larger than 4 g of the third substance. Find the density of a third substance if it is known that the mass of the second substance in the mixture is twice the mass of the first.

2. The altitude of a right triangle dropped to the hypotenuse is $h$. What is the shortest length of a medana that bisects the longer leg?

3. Solve the equation
$$8 \sin^2 x + \sin 2x = 5.$$

4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 3^x - 2^{y^2} = 77 \\ \frac{x}{3} - \frac{y^2}{2} = 7. \end{cases}$$

5. Solve the equation
$$13^{\frac{x}{13}} + 13^{\frac{x}{13}} - 1 + \dots = 15^{2x} + 15^{2x} - 1 + \dots$$

Option 4
(Faculty of Economics and Faculty of Psychology)

1. The candles have the same length, but different thicknesses. The first candle was lit one hour earlier than the others, lit at the same time. At some points, the first and second candles became the same length, and two hours later the first and third candles became the same length. How many hours is the first side of the candle, if the second side is 8 hours, and the third is 12 hours? It is assumed that the speed of the candles does not depend on the duration of combustion.
