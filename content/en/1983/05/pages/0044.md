Tasks

M801 — M805; F813 — F817

M801*. Prove that for any natural number $n$ the equality

$$[\sqrt{n}] + [\sqrt[3]{n}] + [\sqrt[4]{n}] + \ldots + [\sqrt[n]{n}] = [\log_2 n] + [\log_3 n] + [\log_4 n] + \ldots + [\log_n n]$$

([$x$] means the integer part of the number $x$).

V. V. Kisil

M802. On the sides $AB$ and $BC$ of triangle $ABC$, as on the hypotenuses, right triangles $APB$ and $BQC$ with equal angles of magnitude $\beta$ and their common vertex $B$ (see Fig. 1) are constructed outside it. Find the angles of triangle $PQK$, where $K$ is the midpoint of side $AC$.

L. P. Kuptsov

M803. The sum of two different numbers $x$ and $y$ is a natural number, the sum of their inverse numbers $1/x$ and $1/y$ is also a natural number. What could $x$ and $y$ be?

R. A. Mazov, A. P. Savin

M804. Point $O$ is the middle of the axis of a right circular cylinder, $A$ and $B$ are diametrically opposite points of the circle of the lower base of the cylinder, $C$ is some point of the circle of the upper base that does not lie in the plane $OAB$. Prove that the sum of the dihedral angles of a trihedral angle $OABC$ (with vertex $O$) is 2π.

I. K. Zhuk
