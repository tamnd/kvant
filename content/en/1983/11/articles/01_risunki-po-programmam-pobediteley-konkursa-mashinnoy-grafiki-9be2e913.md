⟦folio 100⟧

Here are reproduced four figure, executed by computer using a program written by users of the competition by the graphics machine of the magazine «Kvant» (see No. 9 of 1981). Figure in the lower right corner was obtained by Richard Havlik (Czechoslovakia) using the standard program. In the micrograph with high magnification, it is shown how the plotter “draws” lines: it simply densely places points along the calculated line. The remaining figure were executed using a program in Pascal using the Shpada-5 graphic system, created by Novosibirsk schoolchildren. “Centaur” was invented by Sveta Bezborodova from Rubtsovsk (Altai Territory), “Linny Sierpinsky” -

Sereka Batalov from Arzamas, “Lion Cub” was drawn on a computer according to the program of Dima Kornilin from Gorky. In addition to the schoolchildren mentioned above, high school students P. Dementyev (Gorky), O. Mashukov and M. Gorbatenko (Insskaya station, Novosibirsk region), A. Krapivin (Kyiv), E. Dvurechensky (Lipetsk), E. Kuzenkov (n. Nizhny Novgorod Gorky region), A. Merkulova, S. Terekhov and T. Farina became the winners of the competition. (Novosibirsk), L. Khusnutdinova (Sverdlovsk) and junior schoolchildren M. Khlebalov (Moscow), A. Evstigneev and D. Parkhomchuk (Novosibirsk).
