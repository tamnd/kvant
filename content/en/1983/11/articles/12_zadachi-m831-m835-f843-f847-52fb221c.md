M831. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the midpoints of the side $AB$ and $CD$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$, and let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$. Prove that if the lines $MN$ and $PQ$ are perpendicular, then $|BC| = |AD|$.

M833*. The sequence $x_n$ is given by the conditions:

$$x_1 = 2; \quad x_{n+1} = \frac{2+x_n}{1-2x_n} \quad (n=1, 2, 3, \dots).$$

Prove that a) $x_n \neq 0$ (for all $n$);

6) this sequence is non-periodic.

V. E. Matizen

M834. An irrigation plant located at point $O$ serves a circle of radius 100 m with center $O$. With such installations it is necessary to completely irrigate a square field of 1 km×1 km.

a) The foreman assumes 64 installations at the vertices of the square grid on the side parallel to the edges of the field (Fig. 1). Within what limits can side $a$ of a square grid vary?

6) Eighth-grader Vitya claims that it is possible to irrigate a field using only 46 such installations. Is Vitya right?

H. B. Vasiliev

F843. A wheel of radius $R$, located at a height $H$ above the ground, rotates with an angular velocity $\omega$.
