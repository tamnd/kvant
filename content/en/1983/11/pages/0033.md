⟦folio 31⟧

Tasks

1. The magazine «Kvant» consists of 16 double sheets inserted into each other. On which double sheet will the sum of the numbers indicating the page numbers be greatest?

2. Find a two-digit number that is twice the product of its digits.

3. Solve the path of "geographical" puzzles (see figure). In each rebus, identical letters correspond to identical numbers, and different numbers correspond to different ones.

4. The dominoes are laid out in a circular pattern, with the sum of points in each vertical and each horizontal row being the same (see figure). The point values ​​on the dice, except for the “dummy” ones, are protected by letters. Decipher them.

5. A father and son are observing a solar eclipse, and the topic of their conversation is the Sun and the Moon. “Dad,” the boy asked, “how many times is the Sun farther from us than the Moon? “As far as I remember,” answered the father, “387 times.” “Then I can calculate how many times the volume of the Sun is greater than the volume of the Moon.” “Please, you’re right,” the father answered after thinking. How many times is the volume of the Sun greater than the volume of the Moon?

These tasks were offered to us
M.V. Varda, A.M. Kozlov, M.A. Mnachakyan, E. Suleymanov (10th grade student at school No. 10 in Sheki), A. Kholin (7th grade student at school No. 1 in Kokchetav).
