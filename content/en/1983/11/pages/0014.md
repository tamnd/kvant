What is smettria? Intuitively, each of us has the ability to distinguish smetmetric from non-metric. Smetric bodies can always be divided into equal parts and even in many ways. The equality (or congruence) of two parts of a figure means that they can be brought together by moving them. Their “equal surroundings” means that this movement can be chosen so that the entire figure moves into itself. A movement that transforms a certain figure into itself is called its transformation of geometry or self-alignment. So, a figure is smettryn if it has at least one transformation of smettria (other than identical).
