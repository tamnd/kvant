10th grade
Task 1
Determine the emf and internal resistance of the current source.
Equipment: the current source under study, a current source with a known emf and a known internal resistance, a spiral of nichrome wire, a more sensitive non-radiating device for measuring current, connecting wires.
The first part of this assignment is no different from the second assignment for grade 9.
To measure the unknown internal resistance of a current source $r_x$, a galvanometer with a shunt ($R_1$) and an additional resistance ($R_2$) should be connected first to the source under study, then to the known one:
$$I_3 = \frac{\mathcal{E}_x}{r_x + \frac{R_r R_1}{R_r + R_1} + R_2}, \quad I_4 = \frac{\mathcal{E}_x}{r + \frac{R_r R_1}{R_r + R_1} + R_2}.$$
From here, reasonably neglecting the resistance $R_r R_1/(R_r + R_1)$ (since it is much less than $R_2$), the desired value $r_x$ is found.
A method for simultaneously determining $r_x$ and $\mathcal{E}_x$ was proposed by A. Gnilovskaya (Leningrad). Connecting a galvanometer to a known source with a shunt, first of additional resistance, and then with additional resistance $R$, such that the readings of the galvanometer in the second case are 2 times less than in the first: $I_2 = I_1/2$, we obtain that $R = r$ (since $R_r \ll R$). Now, knowing the value of resistance $R$, connect with a shunt and with additional resistance $R$, as well as without it to an unknown source:
$$I'_2 = \frac{\mathcal{E}_x}{r_x + R}, \quad I'_1 = \frac{\mathcal{E}_x}{r_x},$$
where
$$r_x = \frac{I_2}{I_1 - I_2} R, \quad \mathcal{E}_x = I_1 r_x.$$

To determine $\mathcal{E}_x$ it is necessary to know the abscilliton value of the current $I_1$. For this purpose, you need to irradiate the galvanometer, knowing that when the device is fully deflected without additional resistance connected to a known source, the current strength is equal to $I = \mathcal{E}_x/r$.
