7th grade
1. A number is placed near each vertex of the triangle. Write next to each side of this triangle a number equal to the sum of the numbers at its ends. Now add each number near the top to the number near the opposite side. Prove that all three sums obtained will be equal to 3. Prove that all numbers are equal to 1.
2. Prove that the midline of a triangle bisects the median that intersects it.
3. Prove that for any natural number $n$ the fraction $(n^2+3n+3)/(n+1)$ is irreducible.

8th grade
1. On a sheet of checkered paper 1982x1982, a number is written in each cell. It is known that in every three cells covered by the figure, the sum of the numbers is 3. Prove that all numbers are equal to 1.
2. Nine identical books cost 11 rubles and kopecks, and thirteen such books cost 15 rubles and kopecks. How much does one book cost?
3. Inside the triangle $ABC$ on the median $AM$ lies the point $O$. Prove that the areas of triangles $AOC$ and $AOB$ are equal.
4. Let $p$ be a prime number. How many natural numbers are there less than $p^2$ and coprime to $p$?

9th grade
1. Given 110 different natural numbers. Prove that you can find 2 numbers that coincide in at least two digits.
2. Prove that $a+b \leq 4$ if $a^2+b^2 \leq 4$.
3. Let the midlines of the quadrilateral divide its area in half. Prove that this quadrilateral is a parallelogram.
