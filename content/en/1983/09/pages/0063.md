8th grade

1. Let $n$ and $k$ be natural numbers ($k \leq n$), $[a; b], [a_1; b_1], [a_2; b_2], \dots, [a_n; b_n]$ be segments on the number line. It is known that the number of segments $[a_i; b_i]$ containing the point $x \in [a; b]$ is equal to $k$ for any point $x \in [a; b]$. Prove that for any $i = 1, 2, 3, \dots, n$ $[a; b] \subset [a_i; b_i]$. (It is assumed that for any $i$ $[a; b] \cap [a_i; b_i]$ is not empty.)

2. Prove that $a^4 + b^4 \geqslant 1/8$ if $a + b \geqslant 1$.

3. Prove that in a convex quadrilateral the sum of the lengths of the diagonals is greater than the sum of the lengths of the segments connecting the midpoints of opposite sides.

4. The natural number $n$ is such that the numbers $2n + 1$ and $3n + 1$ are the squares of certain natural numbers. Prove that the number $n$ is divisible by 8.
