From this issue we will begin to publish materials from the XVII All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for Schoolchildren.
Below are the tasks of the initial stages of this Olympiad using the example of the Moscow City Olympiad in Physics and the Sverdlovsk Olympiad in Mathematics.
The following issues will tell about the All-Russian Olympiad and the final type of the XVII All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad for schoolchildren.
Let us recall that the next XVIII All-Union Olympiad begins in November 2. and will be held in several stages: in November - December there will be school, district and city Olympiads, in January 1984 - resource (ACCP), regional and regional Olympiads, in March - Olympiads of resource resource groups, in April the final round and closing of the Olympiad will take place.
We hope that the problems published below will be useful to you in preparing for the next All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren.

Moscow City Olympiad in Physics 1982/83

District tour
8th grade

1. There is a round box with an internal diameter D on the table, and in it there is a washer of radius R. There is no trenne between the box and the washer. The box as a whole begins to move with a constant speed v₀, directed along the line of the centers of the box and the washer. At time t=t₀ the washer moves away from the box. Assuming all impacts are absolutely elastic, find the time dependence of the position of the center of the puck x₀ and its speed v₀ relative to the table, starting from moment t₀. Draw graphs of x₀(t) and v₀(t).

2. At the end of a homogeneous stick of mass 4.4 g, a homogeneous alimine ball of radius 0.5 cm is suspended on a weightless thread. The stick is placed on the edge of a glass of water, adding an equilibrium position at which half of the ball is immersed in water. Determine the ratio in which the length of the stick is divided by the fulcrum. The density of alimina is 2.7·10³ kg/m³, the density of water is 10³ kg/m³. Neglect surface tension forces at the boundary of the ball and water.

3. Two homogeneous rods of uniform length with masses m₁ and m₂, fastened in the form of the letter Γ, form a right angle and lie on a rough horizontal surface. The system is pulled using a thread attached to the apex of the corner and the parallel surface. What angle α does the thread make with a stick of mass m₁?

4. A constant voltage U is connected to a wire frame in the form of a tetrahedron at two vertices. The resistance of each edge is equal to R. The exclusion of which of the edges of the frame will lead to the greatest change in the current I in the circuit? What is this maximum change in current ΔI_max? Neglect the resistance of the supply wires.

9th grade

1. Two connected homogeneous sticks of the same length with masses m₁ and m₂ form the letter T lying on a rough horizontal surface (a stick of mass m₂ is attached to the middle of a stick of mass m₁). The system is pulled parallel to the surface by a thread tied to the end of a stick of mass m₁.

2. With small calfs, the points of the intrasmooth fixed hemispherical part of radius R. The large circle of the bowl is horizontal. At some point in time the bodies are released. All collisions are completely inelastic - the bodies are cut down and then move together. What is the maximum amount of body that will be released in this system? At what initial arrangement of bodies will this be realized?
