Question 10. How many images of the point S will be obtained in a system of two mutually perpendicular plane mirrors (Fig. 4, a)?

Typical wrong answer. Two images: $S_1$ and $S_2$ (Fig. 4, b).

Error analysis. In addition to the images $S_1$ and $S_2$, formed by rays reflected from only one mirror, there is another image - $S_3$ (Fig. 4, c). It is formed by rays reflected first from one mirror and then from another.

Question 11. Why is energy released when a uranium nucleus fissions?

Typical wrong answer. Each particle in the nucleus has a binding energy of about 7.5 MeV; the total binding energy is about 1800 MeV. Thus, enormous energy is concentrated in the core. It is released when the core is destroyed.

Error analysis. By definition, binding energy is the energy that must be expended to separate a nucleus into its constituent particles. Or, in other words, this is the energy that is released during the formation of a nucleus from individual particles.

What energy is released during the fission of a uranium nucleus? The fact is that the binding energy per particle in the uranium nucleus is 7.5 MeV, and for each particle of shards formed during the fission of uranium, there is about 8.5 MeV. Thus, the decomposition of a uranium nucleus requires 7.5 MeV of energy per particle, and the formation of shards releases 8.5 MeV of energy per particle. The difference between these energies, which is approximately 200 MeV per nucleus, is released during the fission of the uranium nucleus.
