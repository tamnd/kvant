Physico-mechanical and radio science faculties

1. For a process with an ideal monatomic gas, depicted on figure and representing a linear dependence of pressure on volume, find: a) the work done by the gas during expansion from state 1 to state 2; 6) change in internal energy; c) the amount of heat received by the gas.

2. Inside the ball, uniformly charged throughout the volume with a constant density $\rho=9 \times 10^{-8}$ C/$\mathrm{m}^3$ and dielectric food $\varepsilon=1$, has a spherical density in which there are no charges. The center of the cavity is displaced relative to the center of the ball by a distance of $a=25$ mm. Find the model and direction of the field inside the cavity if it is known that the direction of a uniformly charged ball (at $r<R$) is described by the formula
$$\vec{E}=\frac{\rho}{3\varepsilon_0}\vec{r},$$ where $\vec{r}$ is a vector directed along the radius from the center and is equal in magnitude to the distance from the center of the ball, $R$ is the radius of the ball. Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0=8.85 \times 10^{-12} \mathrm{F/m}$.

3. A particle with a removed charge $q/m=10^{-5}$ C/kg moves rectilinearly under the influence of an electric field of strength $E=200(1-2x)$ V/m, where $x$ is the distance from the point at which the particle first poled. Find: a) the distance traveled by the parts to the point where it stopped; 6) acceleration of the particles at this point.

4. The current density flowing through a copper wire is $j=1,0$ A/$\mathrm{mm}^2$. Assuming that for every copper atom there is one free electron, estimate the distance the electron will travel if it moves a distance of $d=10$ mm. Copper density $q=8,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/$\mathrm{m}^8$, molar mass $\mu=64$ kg/kmol.

5. From the point $A$, lying on the axis of a straight solenoid, an electron flies out with a speed $v=5 \cdot 10^7$ m/s at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the axis. Magnetic field induction $B=31,4$ mT. Find the distance from the axis to the point where the electron hits the screen located perpendicular to the axis at a distance of $l=10$ cm from the point $A$.

Public faculties

1. The particle moves in the $XOY$ plane with a constant acceleration $\vec{a}=(a=10 \mathrm{m/c}^2)$, the direction of which is opposite to the positive direction of the $OY$ axis. 2. Some mass of molecular hydrogen occupies a volume of $V_1=1 \mathrm{m}^3$ at a temperature of $T_1=250$ K and a pressure of $p_1=2$ atm. Find the dielectric capacity of the dielectric.
