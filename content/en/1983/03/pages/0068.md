This complex pattern was drawn by the BESM-6 computer plotter in August 1982 during the VII All-Union Summer School for Young Programs in the Novosibirsk Academic Town. True, the plotter drew with black ink on white paper, and we used a pattern on a green background. It is curious that not only the program for performing this figure, but also a system of instructions (Graphical system “Shpada-5”), which allows you to draw on a computer,” was created by schoolchildren in Novosibirsk.
