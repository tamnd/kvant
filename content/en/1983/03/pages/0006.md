The main conclusion that Maxwell made from his equations is that an electromagnetic field, changing periodically over time, should propagate in space in a wave-like manner - the same way, for example, as sound waves propagate from a vibrating string or other sound source. But the speed of propagation of electromagnetic waves should, according to Maxwell, be equal to the speed of propagation of light, that is, 300,000,000 m/s, which is about a million times greater than the speed of sound in air. It is this circumstance that Maxwell brought to the general agreement that light is represented by electromagnetic waves (electromagnetic theory of light).
