⟦folio 6⟧

Rudolf Mössbauer
answer questions from the magazine editors
«Kvant»

In 1958, German physicist Rudolf Mössbauer discovered new methods for studying substances - Mössbauer spectropy and Mössbauerography, which are used in various fields of physics, engineering, chemistry, biology, and medicine.

In September 1982, Professor Mössbauer was elected a foreign member of the USSR Academy of Sciences. We met with him and asked him to answer our questions.

— What, in your opinion, are the most pressing problems in physics?

There are a lot of problems in physics. The greatest progress can be expected in the field of elementary particles. Here, questions about the stability of the proton, neutrano masses and the existence of the Dirac monopole are highlighted. Of course, there are many problems in astrophysics and cosmology. On the other hand, it should also be noted that biophysics is an important area. Now, for example, we are starting to study the diamicca of large biological molecules. Finally, the most important problem, which I will consider as a certain aspect of engineering problems, is controlled thermonuclear fusion. Many discoveries are made by chance; it is difficult to predict the actual time frame for solving individual issues.

— How did you become a physicist?

At school I liked both mathematics and physics; I was also interested in chemicals. Loved to experiment. I was very interested in electronics. Together with a group of comrades, we made telephones and amplifiers. It was my passion for electronics that had a decisive influence on my choice, and I became a physicist.

— Do you advise young people to get a physics education and become physicists?

Certainly. Physics studies fundamental phenomena. Physics education makes it easy to retrain if the need arises to move into other areas of study, for example, biology or chemistry. Physical laws are used everywhere in the world.

— What should modern education be like?

I believe that there is no need to literally change the education system; it needs to be adapted to the needs of modern science. In physical education, it is very important to remember the fundamental points and not focus too much on the details. Now modern electronics, minicomputers and computers will invade the life of literally every person. This fact should also be taken into account even at the very initial stage of education.

— What mathematics does a physicist need?

Although physicists often rely on intuitive considerations in their work, the importance of mathematics is essential. The better a physicist knows mathematics, the more successfully he can work. You need to be good at practical mathematics. Mathematics is the language of physics. However, one should distinguish between the needs in mathematics for a theoretical physicist and an experimental physicist. There is a danger of getting too involved with computers. Many young people rely so much on them that they stop thinking for themselves.

— Your wishes as a reader «Kvant».
Considering the present and fruitful. You learn a lot over the years of study. When studying mathematics and physics, do not forget that this is only part of universal human science and culture.
