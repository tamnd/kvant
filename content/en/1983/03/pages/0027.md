⟦folio 25⟧

Electric discharge in the laboratory

The photograph reproduced on the first page of the cover was taken in the educational laboratory of the Department of Technical Directions of Lenin and the Order of the October Republic Energy Institute, one of the leading technical universities in the country.

The photograph shows part of the laboratory of the installation, with the help of which methods of protecting high-voltage equipment are developed. In particular, it uses the dependence of the voltage caused by the fog form of the gas discharge on the capacitance of the discharge gap.

A hemisphere-shaped electrode is visible in the foreground. Behind it is a glass plate, and even further away is a metal ground plane. A high voltage is created between the electrode and the plane. The resulting electrical discharge develops along the surface of the glass, passing through successive different stages (first corona, then spark and finally arc discharge). The photograph shows the process of transition of a corona discharge into a spark discharge.
