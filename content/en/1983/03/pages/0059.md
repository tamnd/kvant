Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\frac{x}{4^2}+5} \leq \frac{1}{2x+1-1}.$$

2. Towns $A$ and $B$ are located on a straight highway. Two pedestrian codes were simultaneously embroidered from city $A$ to city $B$, and quantities were sent from city $B$ to city $A$ at the same moment in time. Having traveled the $k$ part of the path from $B$ to $A$, I met the first pedestrian. Then, traveling $\frac{2}{3}$ all the way, he met the second pedestrian. At what distance from them at the moment of their meeting is the first pedestrian? Pedestrian speeds and magnitudes are constant, $|AB|=S$.

3. The basis of the parameters $SABCD$ is the rectangle $ABCD$, the length of the diagonal $AC$ is equal to $d$, and $CAD=\varphi$. 3. Solve the inequality
$$2\log_4(x-a+1)+\log_2(x-3-2a) \ge 2.$$

Determine the cross-sectional area of ​​the straight lines by this plane if it is known that $ABC=\beta$.
