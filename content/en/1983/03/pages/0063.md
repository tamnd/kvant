1. Let's write down Newton's second law for the first load at the moment the rod passes the vertical position:

$$m_1 (v^2/l) = m_1 g - N.$$

Here $v$ is the speed of the load, $m_1 g$ is the gravitational force, $N$ is the force with which the rod acts on the load (in absolute value it is equal to the force $F$ of the action of the load on the rod). To find the speed $v$ we use the law of conservation of energy for a system of two weights connected by an incredible rod:

$$(m_2 - m_1) g l = (m_1 + m_2) (v^2/2).$$

From the resulting equations we find the required force $F$:

$$F = N = m_1 g (3m_1 - m_2) / (m_1 + m_2) = (1/3) m_1 g = 2 H.$$
