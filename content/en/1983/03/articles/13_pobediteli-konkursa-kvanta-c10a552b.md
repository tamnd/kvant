Winners
competition «Kvant»

Every year our magazine holds a competition among schoolchildren to solve problems from the Problem Book «Kvant». In accordance with the decision of the organizing committee of the All-Union Olympiad, schoolchildren who won this competition receive the right to participate immediately in the fourth (republican) round of the All-Union Olympiad.

Below is a list of winners of the «Kvant» 1982 competition who received the right to participate in the 1983 Republican Olympiads. All of them are also awarded with books in the “Library «Kvant»” series with author names.
