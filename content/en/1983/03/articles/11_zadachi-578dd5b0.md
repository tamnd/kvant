⟦folio 38⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Quests

1. Lenya and Kolya went to visit Bora, but they forgot his apartment number. Kolya said that if you add 10 to this number, you get an exact cube, and Lenya said that if you subtract the number 10 from the apartment number, you get an exact square. What apartment does Borya live in?

2. Masha needed sewing thread No. 20. She didn’t have such threads, but epic threads No. 40 and No. 10. What should she do? (A license plate is defined as the ratio of the length of a thread in meters to its mass in grams, that is, it is numerically equal to the length in meters of 1 gram sheet.)

3. The balloon, inflated with air, was weighed on a scale. Then they inflated it even more and weighed it again. Will the scales read the same?

4. What number should be put together with the “?” in the sequence 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 22, 24, 31, 100, ?, 10,000?

5. Vertex A of triangle ABC is connected by a segment to point M on the opposite side of the triangle. Vertex B is connected to point K on the side of the triangle opposite to vertex B. Can segments AM and BK intersect and bisect?

These tasks were offered to us
N. V. Vasiliev, A. N. Vilenkin,
S. V. Kazakov, L. Z. Karelin, A. P. Savin
