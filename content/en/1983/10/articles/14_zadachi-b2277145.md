⟦folio 38⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Tasks

1. Is the pattern on figure symmetrical?
2. There are two red balls, two blue balls, two green balls, two yellow balls and two white balls. Several multi-colored balls were placed on one side of the scale, and second balls of these colors were placed on the other. At the same time, the left side of the scale tipped. However, it was changed that no matter what pair of balls of the same color are placed in places, the scales will either be in balance or the right cup will be overweight. With what number of balls on the scales can this happen?
3. Rearrange one match in each row (see figure) so that the last one turns out to be the correct equality.
4. The square is cut into rectangles so that no point of the square is the vertex of four rectangles at once. Prove that the number of square points that are vertices of rectangles is even.
5. A steel ball floats in the river. Will the diving depth increase or decrease if the temperature increases?

These problems were suggested to us by G. A. Galperin, V. V. Proizvolov, N. A. Rodina, L. M. Salakhov, L. A. Shteingats
