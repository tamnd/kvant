V Moscow horizontal bar for young physicists

The V Moscow horizontal bar was held by the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University from December 20, 1982 to April 3, 1983. The organizing committee of the Turinik was headed by the Vice-President of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician E. P. Velikov, the jury was a professor of the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University V. L. Bonch-Bruevich. 40 schools in Moscow and the Moscow region took part in this modern high school class.

As usual, the horizontal bar was carried out in three stages.

I round - correspondence collective competition. The school was offered 20 problems for a period of two months for collective solution. Based on the results of this competition, teams from 15 schools were admitted to the second round.

2nd round - qualifying rounds, which determined the financial tournament. Qualifying physiognomies were held in Moscow schools according to the instructions of the correspondence competition.

The third round - the financial tour was held at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. Its program included: a homework competition, a captain's competition, a physical competition for three teams - a financial tournament, a large-scale competition, and loading of the winners.

The 1st degree diploma and the challenge prize of the horizontal bar were awarded to the teams from school No. 57 and No. 7 in Moscow, the 2nd degree diploma to the team from school No. 710 in Moscow, the 3rd degree diploma to the teams from school No. 444 in Moscow, No. 82 village. Chernogolovka and Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University. For winning individual horizontal bar competitions, 17 schoolchildren were awarded certificates and gifts. The Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University awarded the OLS schools whose teams showed high results in this condition with valuable physical instruments. Magazine «Kvant» awarded a number of schoolchildren with books in the “Library «Kvant» series with authors’ names.

The VI horizontal bar of young physicists will study on October 15, 1983. The ass of the correspondence collective competition

The conditions of the tasks are formulated as briefly as possible. Necessary additional data and qualifications should be inserted using common sense. You can use these tasks when organizing quizzes at school, evenings of entertaining science, tours for young physicists, and use them in physics clubs.

1. "Goll." “...a powerful blow! Go-o-ol!!!" What is the maximum pressure in a soccer ball when it is kicked?

2. "Rain". Estimate how many times the amount of poplar fluff in the air will decrease after a thunderstorm (Moscow, June, thunderstorm of medium strength and duration).

3. "Earth's gravitational field." How does the voltage of the Earth's gravitational field depend on the distance to its center?

4. "Earth's Electric Field". Suggest a method and change the voltage of the Earth's electric field.

5. "Explosion". On a small island in the Kuril ridge, the physicist was hunting for butterflies. Suddenly, at the moment when the butterfly folded its wings, it was sharply carried 10 cm to the sides. It turned out that 300 km from the observed physicist, a volcanic eruption began - it produced the first powerful explosion with high gases and ash. Estimate the energy of this explosion.

6. "Swamp". Explain why a person can drown in more, even if the average density of the swamp environment is significantly greater than the density of water.

7. "Well". They say that on a bright sunny day you can see the stars from a deep well. Explain what stars can be observed this way and what the well should be like for this.

8. "Marina Trench". What is the water pressure at the bottom of the deepest ocean fin?

9. "Balloon". A balloon with a volume of 1000 m³ is in equilibrium at a height of H = 300 meters above the Earth's surface. Suddenly a golden eagle (m=10 kg) sat on top of him. How will the balloon descend?

10. "Balloon" Explore the dependence of excess pressure in a balloon on its diameter.

11. "Paraffin". Measure the coefficient of volumetric expansion of paraffin in the temperature range 0-80°C.
