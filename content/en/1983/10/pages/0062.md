Main Problems Competition

The team was given two hours to solve these problems and submit written reports to the Turir jury.

1. "Helium". The experimenter makes a deep vidho, and then with a deep breath fills the lungs and airways with gel (through a gasold tube). After this, the experimenter pronounces dry several sounds of his speech. Firstly, the height becomes several times higher. Secondly, the timbre of the voice is greatly distorted, making it unrecognizable. As the helium is exhausted, practically after the first breath of air, the normal sound of the voice is restored. Explain why the pitch of the human voice is impaired in this experiment. Make numerical estimates.

2. "Magnet". It is known that using a magnet you can lift weights. In the experiment demonstrating the lifting force of a magnet, the following were used: a torid ceramic magnet, to which a handle made of non-magnetic materials was glued for convenience, a lifting platform in the form of two steel disks connected along the axis by a rod, and a set of removable weights. It turned out that the maximum weight of the load that can be lifted with this magnet is $P_1$.

If now a thin strip of tin is tightly placed on the side surface of the magnet, the lifting force of the magnet will increase significantly: $P_2>P_1$. In the experiment: the outer diameter of the magnet is 60 mm, the inner diameter is 30 mm, the width is 15 mm, the thickness of the tin strip is 0.15 mm, $P_1=25$ N, $P_2=35$ N. How can the column explain the significant increase in the lifting force of the magnet?

3. "Refractive index". Determine the refractive index of the glass from which the cylindrical rod is made.
