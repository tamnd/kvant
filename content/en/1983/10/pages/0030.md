Before the school Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. G. GAIN

Today's math club lesson should be considered as a preparatory lesson for the Olympiads.

For many schoolchildren, math Olympiads are the first step in getting to know foreign mathematics. At the Olympiads, children learn to apply their knowledge to solve non-standard problems; they perform with peers from other schools and cities. Typically, at the Olympiad, problems are offered that can conventionally be called algebraic, geometric, numerical and logical. We bring to your attention several such problems at the level of school and district olympiads (the number in brackets indicates the class in which the problem is proposed*)). You will find brief solutions and instructions for all problems in the next issue of the magazine.

Let's start with algebraic and geometric problems - they, please, do not need comments.

1. There are keys at the bottom of the lake. A herd of 183 elephants could drink up a lake in one day, and a herd of 37 elephants could drink up a lake in 5 days. How many days will it take one elephant to drink up the lake? (7)

3. Given non-zero numbers $a, b, p, q$ such that $p+q=1$ and $\frac{p}{a}+\frac{q}{b}=\frac{1}{pa+qb}$. Prove that $a=b$. (8)

4. Find all numbers $a, b, c, d$ such that

$$\begin{cases} abc=a+b+c, \\ bcd=b+c+d, \\ cda=c+d+a, \\ dab=d+a+b. \end{cases}$$ (8)

5. Find all numbers $x, y, z$ such that

$$\begin{cases} x^4+y^4+z^4=3, \\ x^5+y^5+z^5=3, \\ x^6+y^6+z^6=3. \end{cases}$$ (9)

6. Determine the value of the parameter $k$ if it is known that the inequality $\frac{x^2+kx+10}{x^2+3x+10} \leq 1$ holds for all $x$. (9)

7. At what $a$ does the equation $1+\sin^2\alpha x=\cos x$ have a unique solution? (10)

8. Solve the equation $x^3-[x]=4$, where $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$. (10)

9. Find
