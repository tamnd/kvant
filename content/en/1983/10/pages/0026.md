At first, Euler always accepted Catherine's offer to take part in the reorganization of the Academy. He brought with him a detailed project, and he did not strive for the autonomy of the Academy, but, for example, focused on the intertwining of the activities of the Academy and development institutions. However, it gradually became clear that the Empress was not inclined to entrust Eulerr with the leadership of the Academy. Euler learned another lesson that enlightened monarchs like to be taught their place.
