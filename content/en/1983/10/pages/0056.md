QUANTUM SMILES

Science helped
Once, chemistry professor A. Borodin, working on the opera “Prince Igor,” sat at the piano and, looking at an empty sheet of music (I’m not complaining), thoughtfully fingered the keys. Someone (who exactly has not yet been established) placed several sheets of paper on his piano, which he had written with chemical formulas. And composer A. Borodin played what was on these sheets of paper.
This is how the famous Poleskas were born.

About the work of a scientist
Amperad's absent-mindedness is still legendary.
One day a reporter came to interview him.
- They say that you are always in a low position? - was the question.
- Don’t talk! - answered the scientist. - Sometimes you spend the whole day to find these damn glasses. And they, as always, end up on the forehead... So this morning I can’t find them!

Master's answer
— Why did I become a geometer? - Pythagoras said to his students. - Probably because as a child I was often put in a corner for a long time.

Inflated fact
Brothers Joseph and Etienne Montgolfier are known to be the inventors of a balloon, which they glued together from thick paper, filled with hot air and launched in 1783 in Paris. The brothers are considered to be the first to carry out the deception operation, although the tests of the ball were successful.

E. A. Svistinov

Good night!
It is known that D. I. Mendeleev once saw a table in a dream, which was later called “D. I. Mendeleev’s periodic system of elements.” It was the discovery of the century.
But, unfortunately, they didn’t let him sleep until the end; they woke him up too early. That's why the table was left with just a few empty cells.
