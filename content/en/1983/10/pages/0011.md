Non-Inertial Reporting Systems
Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
L. G. ASLAMAZOV

When Kolernik replaced the geocentric system of the world of Ptolemy with a heliocentric system and explained the movement of all planets in it, he produced a solution in human thinking. For many centuries, people considered the motion of all bodies only relative to the Earth. In modern language, they used a single, dedicated reporting system associated with the Earth, although the movement of the planets and the Sun was described in a very complex way.

Later, from the works of Galileo and Newton, it became clear that not only the reporting system associated with the Sun and the fixed stars is suitable for describing motion, but also any other systems that move uniformly and rectilinearly in relation to it. This is how inertial reporting systems were discovered, in which the motion of bodies is controlled by simple Newtonian laws.

Well, what happened next with the earthly reporting system? After all, for us the Earth is, after all, a separate system; When studying the movement of bodies on Earth, most often you can completely forget about the Sun and stars. Strictly speaking, this reporting system is not inertial, since the Earth rotates around its axis and the Sun. However, the accelerations associated with these movements, we, and usually we make only a small mistake, use Newton's laws to describe the movement. Usually, but not always.
