QUANTUM SMILES

The company sued him, accusing him of stealing valuables belonging to it. However, the court acquitted the attacker, citing the current “Code of Punishments.” According to the article of this document, only things that had mass and certain dimensions could be stolen. The Senate, which reconsidered the company's policy, determined that electronic energy could be considered "movable property, although not a thing."

A more ingenious case is described in the scientific monograph of the German scientist P. Grassmann “Questions of Thermodynamic Analysis” (Moscow, “Mir”, 1965).

In the scribery, one of the separated cellars was cooled using a central refrigeration unit. A pipeline with brine crawled through the cellar of one merchant. The brewery owner soon discovered that the merchant was using brine to cool his cellar and accused him of theft. However, the judge stated: “In accordance with Article 242 of the Criminal Code, theft is the unlawful taking of someone else’s property. In this case there is no representation, since the merchant does not appropriate the brine, but returns it back to the brewery pipeline.”

Then the owner of the brewery turned to the following instructions and confirmed his point of view with the following argument: “This is not about stealing brine, but about stealing energy. If I cool a merchant's cellar, I must consume more of the electronic length of the refrigeration machine." The objection of the new physical judge was only justified: “After all, Brine received tello, that is, energy from the merchant’s cellar, therefore, energy was not stolen from you, but, on the contrary, you received energy as a gift.” We all agree that the judge was wrong, but not everyone will immediately say what his mistake was.
