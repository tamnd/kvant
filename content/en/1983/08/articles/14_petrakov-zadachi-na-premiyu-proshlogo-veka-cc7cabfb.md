“Prize problems” have passed centuries

Candidate of Pedagogical Sciences
I. S. PETRAKOV

In 1884, Kyiv University professor V.P. Ermakov published the “Journal of Elementary Mathematics” at his own expense. The magazine was distributed by subscription throughout the country. Many prominent mathematicians of that time were involved in the editorial work.

Two years later, Ermakov did not have enough funds - a journal called “Bulletin of Experimental Physics and Elementary Mathematics” began to be published and edited by E. K. Shpachinsky. However, the composition of the editorial board has not changed. Ermakov continued to take an active part in its work.

Since 1899, the editor of the Vestnik was the later famous geometer V.F. Kagan. The publication of the "Vestnik" continued until January 1917.

Since 1885, “Prize Problems” began to be published in the magazine (In our country, this was the first problem-solving competition held in the general press.) Let us present the first five problems (their terminology, of course, differs from ours).

Problem 1 (1885). Find three integers such that the sum of their squares is divisible by their product without a remainder. Show that the problem has an infinite number of solutions, although it is impossible to formulate a general expression for the original numbers *). Show that the quotient cannot equal any number other than 3 and 1. Give all solutions. Not exceeding 1000.

Problem 2 (1886). Prove the following propositions: if the sum of the squares of two relatively prime numbers is factorized, then each factor can also be expressed as the sum of the squares of two numbers.

Problem 3 (1887). Dante two equal disjoint circles; on two common internal tangents to them two arbitrary points F and F' are taken. From each of these points one more tangent can be checked to each circle; Let the tangents drawn from the point F and F' to one circle meet at point A, and to the other circle at point B. It is required to prove that line AB is parallel to the line.

*) What the authors meant by “general expression” is somewhat unclear. The compilers of Problem «Kvant», most likely, would not have accepted such a common formulation.
