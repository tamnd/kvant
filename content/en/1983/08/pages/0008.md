Recently, on the new research vessel “Akademik Mstislav Keldysh”, owned by the Institute of Oceanology named after. P.P. Shirshov of the USSR Academy of Sciences, an expedition to the Atlantic to develop active methods for ocean research took place (students from the Department of Hydrocosmosa Physics of the Faculty of General and Applied Physics of MIPT also took part in it).

The research used a deep-sea equipment complex, which was a thermal sphere with a diameter of about half a meter, which contained a powerful (several kilowatt) generator and an actuator that controlled the generator upon command from above. Outside the sphere were the study and applicator of sound. This set could be lowered by cable to any depth from the surface to 5000 m. With its help, it was possible to make a detailed “section” of the ocean from the surface to the day.

Generally, the reader is interested in the prospects for the development of new methods of ocean research. What does the future hold for us?

The development of science occurs, as a rule, in leaps and bounds. For some period of time, there is an accumulation of experimental material, a meaningful interpretation of the observed facts. The result of this is a qualitative leap in theory, noticeably moving us along the path of approaching the truth. At the same time, a lot of seemingly unshakable things and some of the ideas are being re-imagined. Then comes the next stage of finding the research results. And so on. In my opinion, the active ocean is currently in the stage of accumulation of fact-

Comrade I think that this will be quite long, since the ocean is large, changing and diverse, and the frontier of research is still relatively narrow, which is largely due to the insufficient technical basis of research. There will be new, more advanced instruments and equipment - there will be new results. Exploring the ocean requires an expensive, often bulky scale.

Already today there are ideas on how to obtain almost continuously information about the characteristics of the World Ocean throughout its entire water area. This method has received praise as active tomography. Its essence is as follows. Along the perimeter of the ocean area, the characteristics of which need to be known, several systems are firmly installed. The systems alternately study action impulses and apply them. The received signals carry information about temperature, salinity and other parameters of the environment through which they propagated, that is, about the region of the ocean “outlined” by the systems. As a result, it will be possible to continuously obtain characteristics of the ocean environment. The value of such information is very clear.

There are, of course, many difficulties on the way to solving this problem. First, we need to solve it theoretically; secondly, it is necessary to permanently install many quite powerful emitting systems throughout the World Ocean and control their changes. Thirdly, it is necessary to create a fleet of powerful computers that could process a huge amount of information in a relatively short time. And much, much more...

This task is undoubtedly of an international scale and is not yet today. However, I have no doubt that humanity will solve it.
