⟦folio 44⟧

- So the rope is ready. Who will climb first?

Styopa quickly and easily climbed the rope, grabbed a branch and wanted to swing. But the branch was wet and slippery, and Styopa wisely went down. Vanya's turn came. However, he did not have enough strength to rise even halfway.

- Now, if there was a movable block... Then I would have gotten in - after all, a movable block gives a double gain in strength! - he said dreamily.

- Or maybe a stationary block would help you? - asked Pyotr Ivanovich.

“Well, no,” answered Vanya. “You can’t test me.” Firstly, we don’t have a fixed block. And secondly, a non-moving block does not give a gain in strength... How about throwing a rope over a branch. I will hold on to one end, and you and Styopa will pull me up at the other.

“Still, think about whether it’s possible to win by force with the help of a fixed block,” said Pyotr Ivanovich.

The friends thought for a long time. We looked at the branches, at the rope, at each other...

“I found it!” shouted Styopa. “First of all, I found a stationary block: the branch is slippery, the rope’s friction against it will be very small, and it will move along it almost like on the wheel of a stationary block.” Secondly, I figured out how to double the strength: I threw a rope over our “fixed block”, with one end of the rope

I tie it around my waist and climb on the other end. My weight will be distributed on both ends, and I will have to pull on the rope with half the force!

“Well done!” Pyotr Ivanovich exclaimed joyfully.

“... kid!” the echo answered him.

7. Did you gain in work by rising in this way?

And he asked Styopa about the echo:

8. The echo returned the word “well done” after 1 second. At what distance from us is the obstacle from which the sound was reflected?

* * *

In the evening the travelers sat around the fire. The full moon rose over the river.

—Can you approximately determine the size of the Moon? - asked Pyotr Ivanovich. - All you need to know for this is the distance from it to the Earth. It is approximately 384,000 km. You can do all other measurements yourself. Think about how to do this. And let this be the last question for today.
