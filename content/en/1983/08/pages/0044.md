E. E. SEMENOV

On a July afternoon, a raft set sail from the picturesque village of Ust-Utka, on the banks of the picturesque Ust-Gusi River. There were three travelers on the raft: seventh grade student Styopa Moshkin, his friend Vanya Sinitsyn and Pyotr Ivanovich. Pyotr Ivanovich led a physics and mathematics circle at school and was an avid traveler. Pyotr Ivanovich stood at the oar at the bow, Styopa controlled the oar at the stern, and Vanya pushed off from the bottom with his pole. The raft reached the middle of the river. A flag fluttered above a tent set up on a raft. Euclid was depicted on one side and Archimedes on the other.
“Look,” Styopa suddenly shouted, “Oboe!”
Indeed, about fifty meters astern, with his nose slightly raised above the water and his ears flattened, Stepin’s beloved dog Oboe was blazing in the middle of the river. A few more seconds - and Oboe became a quarter member of the crew.
“Well,” said Pyotr Ivanovich, “now, guys, look to the side.”
