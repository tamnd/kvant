F831. An uncharged conducting thin-walled spherical shell is placed in a uniform electric field. When the field direction is $E_0$, the shell breaks. At what minimum field direction will a shell of twice the radius and having the same wall thickness rupture?

S. K. Strokov

F832*. A square non-deformable superconducting frame on the side $a$ is located horizontally and is in a non-uniform magnetic field, the induction of which varies in space according to the law

$$B_x = -kx,$$
$$B_y = 0,$$
$$B_z = kz + B_0.$$

Frame mass $m$, inductance $L$. At the initial moment, the center of the frame coincides with the origin, and the sides are parallel to the $X$ and $Y$ axes. The frame is released. How will it move and where will it end up after time $t$?

O. Yu. Nikshina

Problems

M816—M820; P828—P832

M816. The natural numbers $a$ and $b$ can be obtained from each other by changing the order of their digits. Prove that

a) the sum of digits of the numbers $2a$ and $2b$ are equal;

b) the sum of digits of the numbers $a/2$ and $b/2$ (when $a$ and $h$ are even) are equal;

c) the sum of digits of $5a$ and $5b$ are equal.

A. D. Lisitski

M817. The point $K$ lies on side $BC$ of triangle $ABC$. Prove that the relation

$$|AK|^2 = |AB| \cdot |AC| - |KB| \cdot |KC|$$

holds if and only if $|AB| = |AC|$ or $BAK = CAK$.

A. L. Toom
