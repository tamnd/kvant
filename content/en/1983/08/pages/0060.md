You sat down at the board and immediately realized how difficult it is to win: your opponent is blocking all your attempts to reach the coveted top five and is threatening to win. How to win?

Let's start from the end. All attacks, unless, of course, the opponent allowed an elementary blunder, are completed by constructing one of the forks: 4-4, 4-3 or 3-3 (Fig. 3; number 4 indicates fours - checks, number 3 - threes - half checks).

On the 34th move, White made a half-check, to which Black responded with a series of checks (moves 35, 37, 39,..., 51), ending with a 4-3 fork, after which White was forced to resign.
