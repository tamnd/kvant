⟦folio 41⟧

Exercise

1. Decipher the number puzzle:
+ ONE
+ ONE
MANY
Identical letters correspond to the same numbers, different ones - different numbers.

2. Take a six-digit number that is divisible by at least one of the numbers from the list: 7, 13, 11, 37. Move the first digit to the end of the number. Check that the resulting number will again have the same divisor from the list as the original one. Why?

3. From eight figures made from regular triangles (see figure), make one large regular triangle.

4. There are two rooms in my apartment. The number expressing the area of ​​the first room in $m^2$ is one more than the number expressing the perimeter of this room in $m$, and the area of ​​the second room in $m^2$ is one less than its perimeter. What are the dimensions of these rooms, if we know that the length and width of each room are expressed in a whole number of meters?

5. Sitting in the dentist's chair, Paschent noticed that, along with the doctor's other instruments, he had also warmed up the mirror. “Will you place the mirror in hot water for sterilization?” - asked the paschent. “Not only for this,” answered the doctor. What else is it for?
