FMS at universities - 20 years

“at the University there must be a winterization, without which the University is like arable land without seeds”
M. B. Lomonosov

In 1963, specialized physics and mathematics boarding schools were created at Moscow, Leningrad, Kiev and Novosibirsk universities.

The initiators of the creation of these schools were academicians A. D. Aleksandrov, P. S. Aleksandrov, S. T. Belyaev, V. M. Glushkov, A. N. Kolmogorov, I. K. Kikoin, M. A. Lavrentyev, I. G. Petrovsky, corresponding members of the USSR Academy of Sciences A. A. Lyapunov, D. K. Faddeev, D. V. Shirkov and others.

The experiment turned out to be successful. For twenty years now, these schools have provided a good supply of scientific personnel in our country.

Following the first four FMS, similar schools were opened in Yerevan, Tbilisi, Almaty and other cities.

Among the FMS graduates are several dozen doctors of science and hundreds of candidates of science. Many graduates are students and graduate students of the country's largest universities and already perform their first scientific work as students.

A. N. Kolmogorov wrote: “The task of these schools is to provide students living far from large scientific centers with the same opportunities as students of the best schools in Moscow and other university cities, therefore, for example, the Physics and Music School at Moscow State University does not accept schoolchildren from Moscow and other university cities.”

It is impossible to enter the Physics School. Each of them has its own traditions of holding receptions, but the general requirement is that you need to show a real keen interest in science and the ability to think outside the box.

As a rule, the selection of candidates begins at regional and resource mathematical and physics Olympiads. More information about the admission procedure can be found in the area of ​​individual public education.

You should not think that you need to have some kind of exceptional talent to get into Physics and Music School. First of all, it requires real passion and a willingness to work a little harder than when studying at a regular school.

Teaching mathematics and physics at the Physics School is carried out according to programs developed by teams of their teachers. And although the programs are wider and deeper than the mass school programs, they do not duplicate the VZVoz courses. The purpose of training at the Physics and Mechanics School, said academician M. A. Lavrentiev, is “to teach you to think, invent, carry out experiments, and teach you to direct all your strength to solve a difficult problem.”

Interesting students are not limited to just mathematics or physics. Schoolchildren go on hikes and play sports. Thus, students of the Moscow boarding school deciphered the abbreviation FMS as “football and mathematics school.” Football, volleyball and basketball matches between teams of students and teachers are a long-standing tradition of the FMS.

Our country’s top scientists, writers, artists, and artists visited the “fymyshat”. In short, school staff do everything to ensure that school life is fulfilling, so that the school becomes a real home for one’s education.

Our magazine congratulates the teams of the school’s anniversaries, all graduates, students and teachers of these schools and wishes them all happiness and great creative success. Good luck to you, “fymyshata”!

Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (ZFTS) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling eight-year-old and secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1984/85 academic year.

The goal of this school is to help students in independent studies to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting to ZFTSH, preference is given to the department for students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.
