⟦folio 31⟧

Exercise

1. Let's take two two-digit numbers and change them. Let $A$ be their product. Now in each factor we rearrange the numbers and the resulting numbers also go over; we get the number $B$. Prove that the number $A$—$B$ is divisible by 99.

2. Show that on figure exactly half the area of ​​a regular five-pointed star is shaded.

3. Two grandmasters were returning to the hotel after the next round of the annual chess tournament.
“Three-eighths of Turin are behind,” said one.
“Yes,” answered the other. “And more and more plots are being built every year.” Just think, as many games have already been played as were played in the entire tournament last year!
“If things continue like this,” the first one grinned, “then in a few years there will already be 30 people playing in the tournament...
How many chess players played in the tournament?

4. Is there a figure on a plane that has neither axes of symmetry nor a center of symmetry, but transforms into itself after rotating around a certain point through an angle less than 360°?

5. In the figure puzzle, each color corresponds to a number. However, some colored circles are covered with black masks. Find the numbers corresponding to each of the colors and the colors of the circles under the masks. (In this rebus, in addition to the rules for adding numbers, the rules for adding colors are also followed.)

These tasks were suggested to us by N. I. Avilov, V. N. Vetrov, N. N. Mikhailenko, S. N. OLEXNIK, D. B. Fuks.
