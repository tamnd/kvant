Games, puzzles

This series of designs shows how to make the beautiful paper stars featured on the cover of «Kvant» No. 5 for this year. To do this you will need four 1x30 cm paper strips for each star and... a little patience. The stripes should preferably be two-color (for example, like here: red on one side, white on the other). Fold each of the four strips in half, hook them as shown in figure 1. Then braid them sequentially, following Figures 2-8. The production of “white corners” (Fig. 3) ​​is shown separately below (Figures a - c). Keep in mind that in the paintings, to save space, the long ends of the strips formed in the first steps are pre-cut. The ending of the star weave is quite clever.
We wish you good luck!
