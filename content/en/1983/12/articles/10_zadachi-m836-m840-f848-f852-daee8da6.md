⟦folio 35⟧

Tasks

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given asterisks. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than February 29, 1984 to the address: 103006, Moscow, K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. G2-83” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M836, M837” or “F848”. Solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of the decision verification). The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study.

Problems M836–M840 were proposed at the XXIV International Mathematical Olympiad (p. 46).

M836—M840; F848—F852

M836. Let $A$ be one of the points of intersection of two circles with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$, $P_1P_2$ and $Q_1Q_2$ the common tangents, $M_1$ and $M_2$ the midpoints of the chords $P_1Q_1$ and $P_2Q_2$ of these circles (Fig. 1). Prove that the angles $O_1AO_2$ and $M_1AM_2$ are equal.

I. F. Sharytsin

M837*. Let $a, b, c$ be positive integers, each two of which are coprime. Prove that the largest integer that cannot be represented in the form

$$xbc + yca + zab$$

(where $x, y, z$ are non-negative integers), equals

$$2abc - ab - bc - ca.$$

M838. All points lying on the sides of the regular triangle $ABC$ are divided into two sets $E_1$ and $E_2$. Is it true that for any such partition in one of the sets $E_1$ and $E_2$ there is a triple of vertices of a right triangle?

M839*. Is it possible to choose 1983 natural numbers not exceeding $10^5$ such that among the selected numbers there is not a single triple of numbers that form an arithmetic progression (i.e., not a single triple $a, b, c$ containing $a + c = 2b$)?

M840*. a) Prove that if $a, b, c$ is the length of the side of the triangle, then the inequality holds

$$a^2b(a - b) + b^2c(b - c) + c^2a(c - a) \geq 0.$$

Find out in what cases it turns into equality.

b) Prove that for any positive numbers $a, b, c$ the following inequality holds:

$$a^3b + b^3c + c^3a \geq a^2bc + b^2ca + c^2ab.$$

F848. A ball rolls along a smooth horizontal circular groove of length $l$. The speed $v$ of the ball is known with an accuracy of $\varepsilon = \pm 5\%$. For what values ​​of $v$ can we say with certainty that after a time $t$ after the ball is at point $A$ (Fig. 2), it is not at point $B$?

E. I. Rabkin

F849. A dry thermometer in the ischrometer room shows a temperature of $t_1 = 18,0^\circ\text{C}$, and a wet thermometer shows a temperature of $t_2 = t_1 - \Delta t = 15,0^\circ\text{C}$. Do not use a table; determine the relative humidity in the room. The pressure of saturated water vapor at the indicated temperatures is, respectively, $p_1 = 15,5$ mm Hg. Art. and $p_2 = 12,8$ mmHg. Art. The molar temperature of water evaporation is $l = 4,07 \times 10^4$ J/mol; The specific heat of the air is $c = 20,8$ J/(mol • deg) (at constant volume).

I. S. Solodovnikov

F850. figure 3 shows the experimentally obtained dependence of the elastic force of the spring on its length. Find the period of small oscillations of a load of mass $m = 60$ g suspended vertically on this spring in a gravitational field.

V. I. Komov

F851. Find the force of interaction between two non-conducting halves of a ball of radius $R$, each of which is uniformly charged throughout the volume with densities $q_1$ and $q_2$, respectively. The dielectric permeability of the ball materials is considered equal to unity.

I. V. Grebnev

F852. A uniformly charged stake of mass $m$ rolls along a horizontal non-conducting plane without slipping. After turning on the horizontal magnetic field with the indication $\vec{B}$, perpendicular to the plane of the ring (Fig. 4), the pressure force of the ring on the plane was halved. At what speed did the stake roll if its charge is $q$?

L. G. Markovich
