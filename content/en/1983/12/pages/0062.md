2) After the switch is opened, free undamped oscillations occur in the circuit consisting of capacitors and inductors (Fig. 4). This circuit is equivalent to an ordinary oscillatory circuit with parameters $C = C_1 + C_2$ (parallel connection of capacitors) and $L = L_1 L_2 / (L_1 + L_2)$ (parallel connection of coils). Therefore, the frequency of free electromagnetic oscillations in the circuit is

$$v_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{LC}} = \frac{\omega_M}{2\pi} \approx 15,9 \text{ кГц.}$$

3) With a parallel connection of ideal coils we have

$$L_1 \frac{di_1}{dt} = L_2 \frac{di_2}{dt} = U.$$

Here $U$ is the voltage across the parallel connecting coils and capacitors. It follows

$$L_1 i_1 = L_2 i_2 + L_2 i_0,$$

where $i_0$ is a constant. From the problem data for time $t_0$ we find

$$i_0 = \frac{1}{L_2} \left( L_1 i_{01} - L_2 i_{02} \right) = -0,15 \text{ A.}$$

This physical result means that a direct current circulates in the coils. The reason for this current could be, for example, a change in the external magnetic field.

Applying Kirchhoff's 1st law, we get

$$i_1 + i_2 + i_{C_1} + i_{C_2} = i_1 + \frac{L_1}{L_2} i_1 - i_0 +$$

$$+ \left( C_1 + C_2 \right) \frac{dU}{dt} = i_1 \left( 1 + \frac{L_1}{L_2} \right) - i_0 +$$

$$+ L_1 \left( C_1 + C_2 \right) \frac{d^2 i_1}{dt^2} = 0,$$

or

$$\frac{d^2 i_1}{dt^2} + \frac{1}{CL} i_1 = \frac{1}{CL_1} i_0.$$

The solution to this differential equation is

$$i_1 = I_1 \cos \left( \omega_0 t + \varphi \right) + \frac{L}{L_1} i_0,$$

where $I_1$ and $\varphi$ are the amplitudes and initial phase of current oscillations in the coil with inductance $L_1$, $\omega_0 = 1 / \sqrt{LC}$ is the circular frequency of free oscillations, coinciding with the resonant frequency $\omega_M$. Now let’s find the current in section $AB$:

$$i_{AB} = i_1 + i_{C_1} = i_1 + C_1 \frac{dU}{dt} = i_1 + L_1 C_1 \frac{d^2 i_1}{dt^2} =$$

$$= I_1 \cos \left( \omega_0 t + \varphi \right) + \frac{L}{L_1} i_0 - \frac{L_1 C_1}{LC} I_1 \cos \left( \omega_0 t + \varphi \right).$$

From the problem data we have $L_1 C_1 / (LC) = 1$, therefore

$$i_{AB} = \frac{L}{L_1} i_0 = \frac{L_2}{L_1 + L_2} i_0 = -0,1 \text{ A.}$$

We found that in section $AB$ only direct current flows. This result could be prevented by noting that in our circuit $C_1 L_1 = C_2 L_2 = CL$. This means that the oscillatory processes in circuits $C_1 L_1$ and $C_2 L_2$ occur with the same frequency $\omega_0$ independently of each other.

4) The amplitude of current oscillations $I_1$ in a coil with inductance $L_1$ will be found from the law of conservation of energy. Considering the processes of undamped oscillations in the circuit $C_1 L_1$ and excluding the constant component of the current in the coil, equal to $i_{AB}$, we write

$$\frac{L_1 \left( i_{01} - i_{AB} \right)^2}{2} + \frac{C_1 U_0^2}{2} = \frac{L_1 I_1^2}{2}.$$

From here we will find

$$I_1 = \sqrt {\left( i_{01} - i_{AB} \right)^2 + \frac{C_1}{L_1} U_0^2} \approx 0,204 \text{ A.}$$
