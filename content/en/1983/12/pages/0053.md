⟦folio 51⟧

According to Djordje Ciuc, there is a winner, but there are no vanquished ones. It is important not only to win the test, but also to self-learn in it. Each participant in the experiment went through a difficult journey and demonstrated their dedication to physics and the ability to creatively apply its laws. This is a great achievement.

Then, Professor Konstantin Plevitsa was presented to the International Commission, read out the official decision of the commission on the results of the experiment, and a ceremony was held to award the winner with diplomas and recognition.

In conclusion, the words were presented to the head of the Swedish delegation, Professor Lars Silverberg, who conveyed an official invitation to hold the XV International Physical Test for Schoolchildren in Sweden at the end of June 1984.

Representatives of three youth physics and mathematics journals attended the opitpad in Bucharest: the Southwestern journal “Young Physicist”, the Hungarian “Secondary School Mathematical Journal” (this journal also has a physics section) and «Kvant». During the experiment, they repeatedly exchanged opinions on various issues: about working with gifted youth, about national physical tests, about the role of youth magazines, etc. Heads of delegations from a number of socialist countries took part in the conversations. It is gratifying to note that the participants talked to evaluate the magazine «Kvant», which is well known abroad. Experience «Kvant» is widely used by youth physics and mathematics magazines in other countries.

In conclusion, on behalf of the entire Soviet delegation, I would like to thank the Romanian organizing committee for its topics and friendly welcome.

I would also like to wish success to all readers «Kvant» in the upcoming Opitpad countries.
