These examples, alas, are not invented. They were once discussed on the pages of newspapers and magazines.

It's easiest to blame journalists. Empty and busy. But the thirst is sensational, even tenacious among scientists. There are countless examples of this. You can't list them all. Here's one. Ten years ago, the “problem” of magnetic water was discussed very lively. At some factories, it seemed to be clearly established: it was impossible to pass ordinary water through a magnetic field, and not a very strong one, as technological processes improved dramatically. The scale on the walls of water heating boilers was significantly reduced, the service life of pipes increased and, in general, a lot of benefits were observed.

I have not verified the accuracy of the facts, but I am willing to admit - all rights. Water, ordinary water, is not just H₂O, but also many chemical compounds dissolved in it, these are microparticles, both mineral and biological. I think everyone has encountered soft and hard water in everyday life. For example, in city A, soap washes away dirt perfectly, but in city B, washing your hands and even washing off the soap itself is not a problem. And, it would seem, everything is clear with magnetic water. Small (invisible) ferromagnetic impurities (for example, iron ore particles) can be retained in the magnetic field, deposited on the walls, and the water freed from them can then produce less scale. That's it.

No! It's too simple. And unthinkable obligations arose: the magnetic field changes the structure of water, leads to a fundamentally new chemistry unknown to science, etc., etc. For a physicist, all this sounds on the same note as the hymn to Dean's machine.

Please, do not go into details, just stick to words: the fact that a magnetic field, even in tens of tests, will practically not change anything in the chemical structure, is established as strictly as Newton’s laws.

There are many similar stories repeated. What is the matter, why, along with natural, normal explanations, the most incredible ones are so often attracted?

Maybe human nature has a desire for a Miracle? (Remember the old film “The Feast of St. Jurgen.”)

Teller, after a brief “scientific expert”, let’s return to myths again.

Let's turn to telephati, or, scientifically speaking, extrasensory perception. The situation is the same. Many years of research in various countries, dozens, hundreds of sensations and... not a single reliable fact.

Let us recall that the most important requirement for any scientific work is the reproduction of the experiment. A scientific result is only a result when it is repeated (or can be repeated) many times by independent groups. There is not a single verified fact here.

Not a single one! (By the way, if it turned out that such a phenomenon existed, no sensation would have occurred. Well, it exists, and that’s good.)

For example, it has been established that some types of constant magnetic field are constant and (more importantly) analyze the information received. By the way, telefati fans often cannot even clearly say what exactly they mean. In general, extrasensory perceptions are called perceptions and reactions that are not related to the human “senses” known to us (touch, smell, taste, sight and hearing). But such reactions are well known facts. And there is nothing to talk about here. It is well known that we respond to changes in atmospheric pressure and fluctuations in frequency information. The electronic magnetic field also affects us and is influenced by powerful radio stations. It is also well known that living organisms emit, albeit very weak, electron magnetic waves.
