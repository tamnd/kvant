⟦folio 18⟧

First of all, simple hypotheses will be considered in scientific work.

In myth-making the opposite is true.

They are waiting and demanding a miracle. Space aliens... Even if they are small and green.

The author once had to give lectures where he tried to recognize a reasonable approach to the issue of aliens. And I became convinced how difficult it is to fight superstitions. The myths of the 20th century in their nature, internal structure and origin do not differ from the unreasoning faith of our ancestors. Any arguments of reason acted on fans of space aliens just as effectively and beneficially as, say, doubt about the reality of Mohammed’s journey to heaven, expressed among pilgrims in Mecca.

People wanted a miracle.

With what incredible success the charlatan film “Remembrance of the Future” spread around the world. It is difficult to imagine anything more absurd than the arguments for visiting in the past

necks of the Earth by guests from outer space than references to the gigantic monolithic pltites of the Baalbek veranda or Inca temples, or to the kilometer-long figure (identification marks?) in the Peruvian Nazca desert.

After yourself. Now, after spacecraft flights, we are firmly convinced: there is no and never was intelligent life on the other planets of our Solar System. Therefore, if some civilization visited Earth, then its place is the fault of the star system. The technical level of such a civilization relates to ours in much the same way as our atomic stations relate to the flint ends of the Pithecanthra. Indeed, quasi-scientific discussions about photon rockets, quite fashionable twenty years ago, are interesting only in the sense that they perfectly showed how incredible international benefits are for us. Incredible not to the level of our technology today. It would be poledy. They are incredible and unimaginable even if we extrapolate our possibilities as wildly as we like, that is, fantasize almost uncontrollably. The words “almost uncontrollably” introduce a fantasy somehow limited by our knowledge of the laws of nature. You can, for example, mentally use ideal nuclear fuel, super-strong materials, attract any “miracles” of science known to us...

The most basic analysis immediately shows that despite all this, human flights to other star systems remain an absolutely unrealistic dream. If they are possible, then only with the help of the still unknown. Only if something fundamentally new is revealed to us. By the way, the best science fiction writers feel this very well, and in order to preserve the “authenticity” of their fiction, they have long and successfully used subspace, null transportation, hyperspace, etc. for space flights.
