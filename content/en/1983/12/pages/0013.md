The Moscow school valued the solution of specific difficult problems, and general concepts in it, as generally in mathematics, arose in connection with the solution of specific problems. Let me note that one of the principles of N.N. Luzin’s success as the creator of a scientific school was precisely the fact that he put forward interesting problems in his published works, at seminars and in conversations.

The role of mathematical physics in modern science

(See beginning on p. 2)

Subsequently, the development of theoretical and mathematical physics, especially quantum physics, fully confirmed these thoughts. This gave the opportunity to the greatest scientist of our time, N.N. Bogolyubov, to say the following (1963): “The basic concepts and methods of quantum field theory are becoming more and more mathematical.”

* * *

The development of science in recent decades has shown that the methods of mathematical physics are initially open to problems of physics, mechanics and astronomy, that is, for those sciences in which the most advanced
simple forms of the movement of matter penetrate almost all sections of modern natural and technical sciences and a number of sections of the humanities. Mathematical modeling is widely used in geophysics, chemistry, geology, biology, economics, sociology, economics, medical, psychological, linguistichesque. Although mathematical models are studied in these sciences, and differ from the model of mathematical physics, many methods for studying these models (exact or approximate, using electronic computers) are preserved and, after proper adaptation, are further developed.

Thus, the well-known point of view is confirmed that “science is only sufficiently perfect when it manages to use mathematics.” Modern mathematics with developments in methods and apparatus is a powerful means of scientific and technological progress.
