Finally, we can assume that individual people, due to some random circumstances, have the ability to generate more powerful radiation. Or, for example, transmit and perceive information in the ultrasonic range. After all, dogs hear high-frequency sounds that are inaccessible to us. And moreover, the frequency range perceived by a large reader of the magazine «Kvant» is significantly wider than that of any person over fifty years of age. As we age, high frequencies are perceived worse and worse by our hearing. In the end, we can assume the ability of a person to accumulate small electronic charges, for example, on the tips of his fingers. Such hypotheses do not contradict the laws of nature, and the skeltics can be answered that we still know very little about some of the mechanisms of activity of living beings, including the mechanisms of our nervous system and our brain. Accordingly, we do not know how to accurately assess the potential capabilities of a human organization (however, this applies equally to all living beings). Not only the mechanisms of thinking, instinct, and the transmission of heredity are mysterious. Every day there are non-languages ​​and thousands of simpler questions.

So, we can assume that under certain conditions certain people exhibit non-educational abilities. True, repeating, there is not a single strictly established fact yet, if we talk, for example, about telepathy. But I repeat again, it is possible to allow for certain human organization capabilities unknown to us.

But... There is the most important no. Any hypothesis should not contradict the laws of nature known to us.

We know: both inanimate and living matter are subject to the law of physics. Moreover, for known living systems nuclear interactions do not play any role. Therefore, all reactions and actions of living organizations are associated only with electromagnetic interactions and the gravitational field. Neither

There are no special “fields” specific to the stomach. All scientific experience convinces us of this. Experience in biophysics, biochemistry, chemistry, physics - in general, all natural sciences.

Therefore, when conversations begin about unknown “biofields” or from magazine to magazine (it happened) an article is reprinted about how innocent crumbs slowed down radioactive decay with an effort of will, or, at worst, messages from clairvoyants with a learned ability to immediately reveal long-perfect ideas are published (and it was also written that paper endures everything), we can safely say: all this belongs to the genre of stories about flights we lead to help.

Finally, “flying saucers” and space aliens. The subject of scientific discussion in this case can only be the fact that, apparently, phenomena are sometimes observed in the atmosphere that we cannot satisfactorily explain.

For example, we still don’t know how “ordinary” ball lightning works. There are several hypotheses. The laboratory cannot perceive lightning. And the question is open.

In the same way, one can suggest several appearances of “saucers”, or - so as not to divide the adherents of space aliens - unidentified flying objects (UFOs). However, such a hypothesis was recently developed at the Institute of Oceanology of the USSR Academy of Sciences. Apparently, you can come up with others. But the point is that UFO fans in the overwhelmingly large system are least interested in natural explanations. The situation here is surprisingly reminiscent of “magnetic water”. The principle is the same: natural, normal explanations are not allowed in the competition.
