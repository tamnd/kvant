The volume occupied by this "extra" water (it is highlighted at figure 3) is equal to $V_B = Sh$; the mass of this water - $m = \rho_B V = \rho_B Sh$ - is equal to the mass of an ice cube; but the mass of the cube is $m = \rho_B V_B = \rho_B a^3$ (a is the length of the edge of the cube). Problem 7. The resistance of iron wiring, the mass of which is 390 g, is 5 Ohms. Determine the length and cross-sectional area of ​​the wiring.

Solution. The wiring resistance is equal to $R = rl/S$, where $r = \frac{9,8}{10^8} \, \text{Ом} \cdot \text{м}$ is the resistivity of the iron (we denoted it as $r$ so as not to be confused with the density $\rho$). The mass of the wiring is $m = \rho l S$, where $\rho = 7880 \, \text{кг/m}^3$ is the density of iron. From the last equality we express $l$ through $S$: $l = m / (\rho S)$; substitute this value into the expression for $R$: $R = rm / (\rho S^2)$. From here we find $S$:

$$S = \sqrt{\frac{r m}{R \rho}}$$

$$\frac{(9,8/10^8) (\text{Ом} \cdot \text{м}) \cdot 0,39 \, \text{кг}}{5 \, \text{Ом} \cdot 7880 \, \text{кг/m}^3} \approx \frac{1}{10^6} \, \text{м}^2 = 1 \, \text{мм}^2.$$

Now we find $l$:

$$l = \frac{m}{\rho S} = \frac{0,39 \, \text{кг}}{7880 \, \text{кг/m}^3 \cdot (1/10^6) \, \text{м}^2} \approx 50 \, \text{м}.$$

Answer: 50 m, 1 mm².

For different readers, the wiring length ranged from 5 cm to 500 m. In this regard, let’s talk about one mistake. More precisely, this is not an error itself, but a consequence of an error made in the decision. You received an answer, but you can’t verify it - the answer is not given in the task. This absurdity speaks for itself. And in other cases, observation, communication, and intuition will help you assess the reasonableness of the obtained values. Let's return to our task. Problem 8. Two slider restats, designed for a resistance of 20 ohms each, one is wound with wires below the line, and the other with nichrome wires. The lengths of the wires used for the windings are the same. Which wire has the largest cross-sectional area? How many times?

Let us write down the solution to this problem briefly:

$$R = \rho_{ник} \frac{l}{S_{ник}} = \rho_{ник} \frac{l}{S_{ник}};$$

$$\frac{S_{ник}}{S_{ник}} = \frac{\rho_{ник}}{Q_{ник}} = \frac{1,1 \, \text{Ом} \cdot \text{м}^2 / \text{м}}{0,4 \, \text{Ом} \cdot \text{м}^2 / \text{м}} = 2,75.$$

Answer: The cross-sectional area of ​​the lower wiring is 2.75 times larger.
