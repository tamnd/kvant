XIV International Physics Olympiad

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. M. KOZEL

From July 5 to July 14 this year, the XIV International Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren will take place in the capital of the Socialist Republic of Romania, Bucharet. 16 countries took part in the Olympiad: Australia, Bulgaria, Hungary, Vietnam, East Germany, Holland, Cuba, Poland, Romania, USSR, Finland, France, Germany, Czechoslovakia, Sweden, Yugoslavia.

The team of each country consisted of five participants - students and graduates of secondary and vocational schools - and two leaders. The age of the participants did not exceed 20 years.

The USSR team was represented by the winners of the World Olympiad:

Anton
Alekseev is a student of the 10th grade. No. 239 Leningrad,

Valdis
Birzvalks is a graduate of the S.S. No. 1 Riga,

Andrey
Gnilovskaya is a graduate of the secondary school. No. 239 Leningrad,

Michael
Dyyachkov is a graduate of the secondary school. No. 82 Chernogolovka village, Moscow region,

Vladimir
Molanov is a graduate of the school at Kiev State University.

The team leaders were the head of the laboratory of the Research Institute of Content and Teaching Methods of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR O. F. Kabardin and senior researcher at the same institute V. A. Orlov.

According to tradition, economic physicists were involved in organizing and conducting the Olympiad. From among them, the Olympiad Working Group was formed. Her task included preparing tasks for the theoretical and experimental rounds of the Olympiad and preliminary checking the participants’ work. All results were approved by the International Commission chaired by the Dean of the Faculty of Physics of the University of Buchara, Professor Konstantin Plevitsa. The commission included representatives of all participating countries.

The Olympics were organized very well. All participants lived in a well-maintained student complex. They had the opportunity not only to create creative works, but also to separate them. During the rest days between the theoretical and experimental tours, an excursion to the resort town of Snagov was organized. After the experimental tour, the participants spent three days of rest at the Kostinshtina International Youth Camp on the Black Sea coast. The guests also got acquainted with the work of a number of scientific institutions of the National Physical Center and visited the laboratories of one of the physics and mathematics of Bucharest.

At the Olympiad, participants were presented with the following tasks:

Theoretical tour

An ideally reflective wall is installed at the origin of coordinates, perpendicular to the axis $X$. The particle starts from a point with coordinate $x_0 = 1,0$ m with kinetic energy $E_k = 10,0$ J.
