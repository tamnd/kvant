M821. Solve the equation
$$x^3 + x^2 + x = -\frac{1}{3}.$$

Let's rewrite the equation in the form $3x^3 + 3x^2 + 3x + 1 = 0$; we get the sum of cubes: $(x+1)^3 + 2x^3 = 0$. Let's write it in the form $(x+1)^3 = -2x^3$ and take the cube root from both sides: $x+1 = -x\sqrt[3]{2}$; hence $x = -\frac{1}{1 + \sqrt[3]{2}}$.

Yu. I. Ionin

M822. Cards of four colors - n green, n red, n blue and n yellow - are stacked so that after four cards the color is repeated (for example, 1st, 5th, 9th, ..., (4n-3)th cards - red, 2nd, 6th, ..., (4n-2)th - yellow, etc.).
Several of the cards on top shone, were turned over without rearranging, and randomly stood between the remaining ones. After this, the pile was divided into n small piles of four cards. Prove that each of these four contains cards of all four colors.

Let us number the colors of the cards with the numbers 们 1, 2, 3, 4 so that the initial arrangement of the cards in the deck corresponds to the sequence
12341234...1234

(the left end of this sequence corresponds to the bottom of the deck, the right - to its top).

We divide the deck into two parts as shown in figure 1, after which we turn over the cards of part II and randomly stand between the cards of part I. In the new deck, the first (bottom) four will contain $k$ cards from the beginning of part 1 (1, ..., $k$) and $4-k$ cards from the end of part II ($k+1$, ..., $4$) - see figure 2; Obviously, each color will be represented once. The rest of parts I and II (without the first four cards) will take the form shown in figure 3. Before moving the data, let's change the colors: $(k+1)$th color will receive number 1, $(k+2)$th color - number 2, ..., 4th number - number $(4-k)$, 1st color - number $(4-k)+1$, ..., The $k$th color is number 4.
