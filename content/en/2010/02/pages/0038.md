4.

People have long been able to obtain the low temperature required for storing food without refrigerators. To do this, they put ice in the cellars in the winter, and in the summer they mixed the crushed ice with salt. When mixing ice with salt, the temperature of the mixture can drop to $-^{21} \infty^{C}$ (check this experimentally), as in modern freezers. Why do wipers sprinkle the paths with salt in winter? Do they want to lower the temperature outside? E. Sokolov

5.

The wise lama had two lazy students. One day the lama decided to teach them a lesson and offered to play an ancient game. They sat down at a round table on which 999 matches were poured. The student who sits on the left hand of the lama is allowed to take either 1, 3, or 5 matches per turn. The student who sits on the right hand of the lama takes either 2, 4, or 6 matches per turn. Lama, due to his wisdom, always takes exactly 1 match. The first move is made by the llama, then moves are made in turn clockwise. Anyone who cannot make a move misses it. The one who takes the last match wins. Who will win? Folklore
