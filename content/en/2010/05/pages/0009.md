OUR OBSERVATIONS

"HEAVEN'S GATE"

“Cloud Gate” is the name of the sculpture installed in Chicago, in the center of Millennium Park. Residents of the city call the sculpture “Bob” or “Drop” - and it’s true, it looks like a drop

⟦column⟧

most. The ellipsoidal “Drop” is made of stainless steel plates and polished to a mirror smoothness. It lies on four-meter-high arches, which are a kind of gate. The author of Heaven's Gate is British sculptor Anish Kapoor. He said he wanted to combine the sky and Chicago skyline in his work, “so that someone would see in my sculpture a reflection of floating clouds, and someone else would see in it a reflection of the tallest buildings in the world. And besides, since the sculpture is made in the shape of a gate, everyone can enter the large inner recess-chamber and observe their mirror reflection in it in much the same way as they observe the mirror image of the city in the outer part of the sculpture.” We include photographs of the sculpture “Heavenly Gates” taken by our regular author G. Galperin. It seems to us that they perfectly complement W. Arnold's article on reflections in cylindrical mirrors.
