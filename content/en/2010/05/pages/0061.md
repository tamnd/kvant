Moscow Student Olympiad in Physics 2010

May 22 at the Moscow State Technical University (MSTU) named after. N.E. Bauman passed the city (II) round of the All-Russian Physics Olympiad for students of technical universities. Based on the results of the competition, the first five teams were invited to participate in the final (III) round of the Olympiad. This is the team from MSTU. N.E. Bauman, who scored 189 points, the team of the National Research Institute "MISIS" (127 points), the team of the Russian State University of Oil and Gas named after. I.M. Gubkina (92 points), the team of the Moscow Energy Institute (66 points) and the team of the Moscow State University of Transport (57 points). Winners in the individual competition (all from MSTU named after N.E. Bauman): R. Login - first place, A. Panfilov - second place, K. Shiriazdanov - third place.

OBJECTIVES OF THE OLYMPICS

1. A charge q with mass m moves in a viscous medium with a drag coefficient k in a transverse uniform magnetic field B with an initial speed v. Determine the path that the charge will travel when its velocity vector rotates by $360^\circ. 2.$ A solid cube with edge c and mass m slides with one face along a horizontal surface with a friction coefficient equal to 1. At the initial moment, the speed of the cube is $v_{0}$, and it runs over a narrow transverse strip of width a ≪ c with a friction coefficient equal to 5/3. At what speed will the cube move after crossing the strip if it is known that it has not turned over? 3. Three cylinders of radius R lie on top of each other on a smooth horizontal surface. Their axes are parallel to the surface and to each other. The masses of the lower and upper cylinders are equal to m, and the mass of the middle one can be neglected. At the initial moment of time, the system lost stability and began to move so that the middle cylinder began to be squeezed out to the side. Determine the speed of the upper cylinder at the moment of contact with the lower one, if there is no slippage between the cylinders. 4. Determine the maximum temperature to which a body can be heated if there is a heat reservoir of infinite heat capacity with temperature T and an ideal heat engine with a maximum degree of compression, i.e. the ratio of the maximum volume of an ideal gas in a heat engine to the minimum, equal to $\varepsilon$. Gas adiabatic coefficient $\gamma. 5.$ A metal sphere of radius R is divided along the diametrical plane, and the halves are isolated from each other. One hemisphere is charged with charge q, and the interaction force between the halves is equal to f. Determine what the interaction force will be if the second hemisphere is charged with charge Q. 6. Determine the electric field strength at all points of the plane passing through the boundaries of two parallel half-planes directed in different directions, if the distance between the half-planes is a and they are uniformly charged with a surface charge density $\sigma$. The angle between the plane and half-planes is equal to $\alpha. 7.$ If a metal body of capacitance C is charged to a potential of $\phi$, and then brings it to another metal body whose capacity is $C_{1}$ and touches it, then the potential of the second body after removing the first will become $\phi_{1}$. Determine to what potential the second body can be charged by repeating this operation multiple times. 8. Determine the interaction force per unit length between two parts of a cylindrical conductor of radius R with a current uniformly distributed over its cross section with density j, obtained by cutting the cylindrical conductor with a plane passing through the axis of the cylinder. 9. The intensity of the first main maximum of the diffraction pattern of the grating is $I_{0}$. Every third slot of this lattice was closed. What were the intensities of the first, second and third main maxima of the new diffraction grating?
