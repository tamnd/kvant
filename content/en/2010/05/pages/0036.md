3.

On the island live liars (always lie), truth tellers (always tell the truth) and cunning people (they lie every now and then). One day three wise islanders, strangers to each other, met and the following conversation took place between them. A: I don’t know if there are liars among us. B: I don’t know if there are truth-tellers among us. Q: I don’t know if there are cunning people among us. A: I don’t even know if there are liars among you. B: I don’t even know if there are truth-tellers among you. Q: I don’t even know if there are cunning people among you. Which one is which? A. Khachaturian
