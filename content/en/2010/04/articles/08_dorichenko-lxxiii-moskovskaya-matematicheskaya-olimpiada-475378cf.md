1. Thin transverse rings are drawn on the sausage loaf. If you cut along the red rings, you will get 5 pieces, if you cut along the yellow rings, 7 pieces, and if you cut along the green rings, 11 pieces. How many pieces of sausage will you get if you cut into rings of all three colors? A. Shapovalov

Elves lie when they talk about dwarves, but in other cases they tell the truth. One day two Forest Mountain residents said: A: I stole all my gold from the Dragon. B: You're lying. Determine whether each of them is an elf or a gnome. I. Raskin

4. At the exchange office two types of transactions are carried out: 1) give 2 euros - get 3 dollars and a candy as a gift; 2) give 5 dollars - get 3 euros and a candy as a gift. When the rich Buratino came to the exchanger, he only had dollars. When he left, there were fewer dollars, euros never appeared, but he received 50 candies. How many dollars did this “gift” cost Pinocchio? I. Raskin

6. There were 30 cups of tea at regular intervals on the edge of the round rotating table. The March Hare and Dormouse sat down at the table and began to drink tea from some two cups (not necessarily adjacent ones). When they finished their tea, the Hare turned the table so that in front of each of them there was again a full cup. When these cups were empty, the Hare turned the table again (perhaps to a different angle), and again a full cup appeared in front of everyone. And this continued until all the tea was drunk. Prove that if the Hare always turned the table so that his new cup stood one after the other from the previous one, then they would also be able to drink all the tea (i.e., both cups would also be full every time). I. Raskin
