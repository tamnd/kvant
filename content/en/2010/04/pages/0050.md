4 (6). 5,000 film lovers gathered at the convention, everyone had seen at least one film. They are divided into two types of sections: either they discuss a film that all members of the section have seen, or everyone talks about a film they have seen that no one else in the section has seen. Prove that everyone can be divided into exactly 100 sections. (Sections of one person are allowed: he writes a review about the film he saw.) I. Mitrofanov

4. The rook moved across the chessboard $8 \times 8$ without passing through the same square twice or jumping over squares. In this case, all turns to the right were made in black cells, and to the left - in white cells. What is the greatest number of cells that could be passed through? A. Shapovalov

5. Sasha and Luda are playing a game. Sasha must build a described 13-gon with one given side, and Lyuda wants to stop him. First, Sasha names the side number - the number k from 1 to 13. Then Lyuda sets the length of this side - a real positive number s. Sasha will win if he now describes a 13-gon around the unit circle, where the length of the kth largest side is equal to s. Can Luda stop him? (A side is k-th in size if there are k – 1 sides not shorter than it and the remaining 13 – k sides not longer than it.) A. Shapovalov

1. Thin transverse rings are drawn on the sausage loaf. If you cut along the red rings, you will get 5 pieces, if you cut along the yellow rings, 7 pieces, and if you cut along the green rings, 11 pieces. How many pieces of sausage will you get if you cut into rings of all three colors? A. Shapovalov

2. Only elves and gnomes live in Forest Mountain. Dwarves lie when they talk about their gold, but otherwise they tell the truth. Elves lie when they talk about dwarves, but in other cases they tell the truth. One day two Forest Mountain residents said: A: I stole all my gold from the Dragon. B: You're lying. Determine whether each of them is an elf or a gnome. I. Raskin
