Times change, and technology changes along with $ними.^{13}$ Here we are talking about buoys that float on the surface of the ocean and transmit information from the boundary of two media: air and water. In the last few years, a network of deep-sea buoys (the so-called ARGO system) has appeared - a real miracle of modern technology. Such a buoy drifts at a given depth under water. Once every 10 days, a program is activated, according to which it changes its overall density and begins to move vertically, passing depths from 2 km to the surface and measuring the temperature and salinity of the water with a frequency of several meters. Once on the surface, the buoy transmits the accumulated information to the satellite, and transmits it several times to avoid accidental loss of information. After a few hours, it dives to its normal depth. There are now more than 3 thousand such buoys, and they fairly evenly cover the World Ocean with the exception of the polar seas. Usually, only the temperature of the water surface directly affects the air temperature, but in a strong storm, mixing of the surface layers of water occurs, which are included in the heat exchange.
