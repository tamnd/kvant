Meteorological observations:

distributed in space and time

V.GORDIN

Introduction

Why does the wind spin in the ravine, lift up leaves and carry dust, When the ship in the motionless moisture eagerly awaits His breath? A.S. Pushkin. Egyptian nights

Alexander Sergeevich answers this question like this: “Because the wind and the eagle // And the heart of a maiden has no law.” However, scientists, both before and after the writing of these wonderful poems, tried to find these laws. After all, it will be interesting, beautiful, and perhaps useful. Member of the Paris Academy of Sciences L.M.A. Navier proposed equations describing the dynamics of a viscous fluid several years before the publication of Egyptian Nights. Of course, there was a fair distance from these equations to practical weather forecasting...

What types of weather observations are there?

I have been sending meteorological observations since 1729, through which, not without great incessant effort and labor, I have invented many new things that are relevant to the weather conditions of the local climate, and everything can still be seen from the proper letters I submitted to the Academy. G.Kraft

Let's clarify what meteorological observation is. For example, we look out the window - pedestrians are walking with umbrellas. Or in fur coats. We can draw some conclusions. The pilot is informed on approach to the airfield: visibility is now poor, but, judging by the radar, a small window in the clouds is approaching. In three minutes it will be clear whether to land or go to an alternate airfield. These are observations that are used immediately. The variety of weather observations entering the database of the Hydrometeorological Center of Russia is about $10^{6}$. And, apparently, there will be more. Modern weather forecasting technologies make it possible to provide forecasts for several days with fairly high accuracy. These technologies are the result of centuries of progress.
