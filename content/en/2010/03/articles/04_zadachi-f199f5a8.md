The mathematicians' headquarters consists of several rooms connected by corridors in a circle. There is a board in every room. The spy entered one of the rooms with a supply of chalk. How can he determine the number of rooms in the headquarters if he can walk through the rooms of the building, write with chalk on the blackboard and erase from the blackboard? (Initially, something could have been written on the boards; a spy cannot take the board out of the room.) M. Prasolov
