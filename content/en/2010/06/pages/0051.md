The next recruitment to the OL VZMSH

Open Lyceum "All-Russian Correspondence Multi-Subject School" (OL VZMSH) of the Russian Academy of Education, operating at Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov is recruiting students for the forty-seventh time. OL VZMSH is a state institution of additional education, and not only for schoolchildren. “OPEN” means accessible to everyone who wants to expand their knowledge in one or more of the following fields of science: mathematics, biology, philology, physics, economics, chemistry, law, history, computer science (listed in chronological order of opening of departments). Now the OL VZMSH, together with other scientific and pedagogical institutions, is conducting research on the development of new interactive technologies in education and the translation of part of its educational and methodological complexes into the language of modern telecommunications, in particular, on the organization of the Internet department of the OL VZMSH. During the existence of VZMSH, several hundred thousand schoolchildren and thousands of circles - groups “Collective student of VZMSH” received certificates of completion. Education at school is CORRESPONDENT, i.e. starting from September–October 2011, all applicants will systematically receive materials specially designed for distance learning, containing a presentation of theoretical questions, methods of reasoning, various tasks for independent work, sample solutions to problems, business games, tests and practical tasks. Students' test papers will be carefully checked and reviewed by VZMS teachers - students, graduate students, teachers and researchers of Moscow State University, as well as other universities and institutions where the school has branches. Many of the teachers themselves graduated from VZMSH at one time and therefore understand especially well how important it is to indicate, in addition to specific shortcomings, ways to eliminate existing gaps in knowledge, recommend additional literature, scold for inattention and praise for noticeable (and sometimes even the smallest) progress and hard work. Those admitted to the OL VZMSH will be able to learn about fascinating things that are often left behind the pages of a school textbook, get acquainted with interesting non-standard problems and try their hand at solving them. For many, it will be a revelation that problems exist not only in mathematics, physics and chemistry, but also in biology, philology, economics and other sciences. Solving problems will help clarify and make interesting many sections that seemed unclear and boring. One of the features of the VZMS curriculum and manuals is that they were created by talented scientists and experienced outstanding teachers working at the forefront of science. It is not without reason that at the X World Congress on Mathematics Education, which was held in the summer of 2004 in Denmark, the story about the 40-year work of the mathematical department of the OL VZMSH aroused genuine interest and approval of the participants. To successfully study at a correspondence school, you will have to learn to work independently and productively with a book, to express your thoughts competently, clearly, briefly and clearly, and, as you know, not everyone can do this. Perhaps our correspondence school will help you choose a profession and find your place in the world around you.

⟦column⟧

All those who complete the OL VZMSH program receive diplomas. Although they do not provide formal advantages, the admissions committees of many universities take into account that the holders of these certificates have worked selflessly for a long time to acquire knowledge and have learned to work independently creatively, which means that they will make good students and, in the future, competent, thoughtful, widely educated specialists. If you have such an opportunity, you will partially communicate with our school via the Internet - the further, the more. To enter the OL VZMSH, you must successfully complete the entrance test. The selection committee is interested, first of all, in your ability to reason, your attempts (even if not entirely successful at first) to think independently and draw conclusions. Admission preference is given to those living in rural areas, towns and small towns, where there are no large scientific centers and educational institutions and where additional education can only be obtained by correspondence. Solutions to the problems of the introductory work must be written in Russian in a regular student notebook in a square (for some sections - on a postcard or on a double notebook sheet; see below). Those wishing to enroll in several departments at once send each work in a separate notebook. On the cover of the notebook, indicate: last name, first name, patronymic, year of birth, basic education (how many classes of high school will be completed by September 2011), full postal address (with zip code), where you learned about OL VZMSH (from «Kvant», from friends, from correspondence school posters, etc.), which department you want to enroll in. Entry papers will not be sent back. Without introductory work, only upon application, winners of regional (territorial, republican) rounds of All-Russian Olympiads in relevant subjects, as well as participants in the final rounds of these Olympiads, are accepted for individual training. Students of OL VZMSH partially reimburse the costs of their education. At the request of those who are unable to pay this fee, the OL VZMSH is ready to contact the school, the public education authority, or another sponsor with a request for this benefactor to pay the corresponding expenses. In addition to individual training, all departments of the Higher School of Music, except economics and biology, have a “Collective student” form of training. This is a group of students working under the guidance of a teacher (school teacher, university teacher, student or other enthusiast), usually using the same manuals and programs as individually. Admission to these groups is carried out until October 15, 2011. To enroll in a group, a statement from its leader is required (indicating his profession and position, with a list of students and a message about what class they will be studying in from September 2011); the application must be signed by the group leader, certified and signed by the head of the institution where the group will work. Work with “Collective Student” groups can be paid for by schools as an elective activity.
