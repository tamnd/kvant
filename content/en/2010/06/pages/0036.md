The segments NP, NQ and NA divide triangle ABC into a triangle and two quadrilaterals. It turned out that the mentioned quadrilaterals are two equal trapezoids. How do their reasons relate?

PUZZLES

Tangram

(See the beginning on the 2nd cover page)

«Kvant» already wrote about the tangram in No. 5 for 1989. In this issue we offer several more tasks. Below you see 13 convex polygons. To fold each of them, you will need the entire set of seven tans. Try this. It turns out that it is impossible to add other convex polygons from seven tans. This was established by Chinese mathematicians in 1942. In conclusion, let’s formulate one funny problem. Anya gave her younger brothers Bor and Vita a tangram for the New Year. There was only one task for them: to fold a square, using all the parts for this. A few days later, after many futile attempts to solve the puzzle, Borya became convinced that it was impossible to do it from his set. Looking at Borya's set, Anya guessed what was wrong: she made a mistake and put two parts in the wrong box! As a result, one brother received a set of five parts, and the second - nine. Borya suggested telling Vita about this, but Anya thought a little and realized that Vita’s set could still be used to make a square. So which parts did Anya pack incorrectly? E.Epifanov
