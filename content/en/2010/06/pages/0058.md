INTRODUCTORY PHYSICS TASK 1. The student begins to fill an initially empty vessel with vertical walls with water, while measuring the water level. The rate of water entering the vessel is $m_{0} = = 50$ g s. Based on the measurement results, the student constructed a graph of the water level in the vessel versus time (see figure). Determine: 1) the rate of water level rise; 2) cross-sectional area of ​​the vessel. 2. A boat going up the river met a raft at the bridge and continued moving. Turning back after a time of $\tau_{=}$ 1 hour, the boat caught up with the raft at a distance L = 4 km from the bridge downstream. Determine the speed of the river flow. The speed of the boat relative to the water (speed of the boat in still water) remains constant throughout the entire movement. 3. The rod consists of two cylindrical rods of the same cross-section. The first rod is made of material with density $\rho_{1}$ and has a length $l_{1}$. The second rod is made of a material with higher density. If you cut off a part of length $\Delta l = 0,5 l_{1}$ from the first rod, then the mass of the entire rod will decrease by 10%. How would the mass of the entire rod change if only a portion equal to half its length were cut off from the second rod?
