Department of History

Studying at the history department will allow everyone, including residents of the most remote cities and villages, to broaden their horizons and prepare for entering a university. Those who successfully complete the course receive a diploma. Why do you need to study history? First of all, it's just interesting. It is interesting to know how people once lived, what they dressed in, what they ate, what they read, how they got married, what they fought for and “what they got into.” Secondly, it's useful. Only by knowing the past can one understand the present and predict the future. We will help you figure it out. Experienced teachers write books especially for you. You will be one of the first to know the latest news from the world of history! We will keep in constant contact with you. According to our books, you will perform special tasks and tell us what you have unearthed. After all, in essence, the work of a historian consists of these excavations: the historian-archaeologist, digging the earth and sand, looks for grains of bygone times; the historian-archivist digs through a pile of papers and takes out from the archives and even from private correspondence everything that can allow him to understand the image of time; a theoretical historian, like a fascinating novel, reads archaeological tables, dry reports, statistics and uses them to reconstruct the living fabric of a bygone life. The historian has a special profession: he is an investigator, prosecutor and lawyer of the time in one person. The introductory task below must be completed on a double sheet of paper. The deadline for submitting the entry work is June 1, 2011.
