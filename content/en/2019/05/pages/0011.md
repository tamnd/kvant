D.I.MENDELEEV AND THE PERIODIC SYSTEM OF ELEMENTS

Leev - about his alleged invention of Russian forty-proof vodka. In fact, the introduction of a state excise tax on vodka occurred back in 1844, when Mendeleev was not even ten years old. Subsequently, Mendeleev worked a lot on the theory of solutions. This was the subject of his doctoral dissertation, in which he established that there is a certain optimal mixing ratio of various liquids, at which the density of the mixture is maximum. But for ethyl alcohol and water it was not 40% at all, but a completely different value - two parts of alcohol to one part of water, which was certainly not suitable for a folk alcoholic product. And in France, this very ratio was adopted, based on the works of Mendeleev, for French absinthe - a mixture of ethyl alcohol with a tincture of 14 herbs. But the stories about Mendeleev, as a famous master of the suitcase business, had a real basis. Even at the very beginning of his teaching career in Simferopol and Odessa, his financial situation was very difficult. And Mendeleev found a way to correct it. He invented a special varnish that made the fiber base of the suitcase very durable and at the same time light. But besides this, such a suitcase looked like a very expensive leather product. Subsequently, this skill became the scientist’s “hobby.” He relaxed with pleasure, making suitcases and giving them away to his friends. However, the best rest for Dmitry Ivanovich was chess. At his home parties, he spent most of his time at the chessboard, which did not prevent him from participating in interesting conversations. And he was a very strong player. It is impossible to characterize the activities of Dmitry Ivanovich Mendeleev and his achievements with one, even the most capacious definition. Chemist L.A.
