Conclusion

So how can we answer the question posed in the title of this article? Is sound possible in space? Of course not. Sound exists and propagates only in an elastic medium. Sound is elastic vibrations of the medium, i.e. fluctuations in its density, pressure, temperature. Interstellar space is an extremely rarefied environment. Atoms of interstellar gas travel billions of kilometers from collision to collision. Sound in such conditions is almost impossible. But the Universe was born with sound. Sound vibrations in the early Universe largely determined its modern structure. The impetus for the formation of galaxies was acoustic vibrations of the plasma of the early Universe. Therefore, without sound our Universe would not exist! Sound is involved in space exploration. Studies of the structure of planets and asteroids, the discovery of water on the Moon - all this was done with the help of sound. Sound helps explore the structure of the Sun. Sound has no competition in the study of electrically conducting media: plasma, oceans, the internal structure of planets, where electromagnetic waves do not penetrate. Thus, without sound, our ideas about the cosmos, its structure and structure
