"Mathematical Etudes" published the second, expanded and updated edition of the book "Mathematical Component". Editors and compilers of the book: N.N. Andreev, S.P. Konovalov, N.M. Panyunin; graphic designer R.A. Koksharov. The stories collected in the book tell both about the mathematical “component” of the greatest achievements of civilization, and about the mathematical “filling” of familiar, everyday things. All authors are famous scientists. The fascinating, popular-descriptive style of presentation makes the book's materials accessible to a wide range of readers. The collection is richly illustrated. The first edition won the Enlightener Prize (2015) and was awarded the RAS Gold Medal for outstanding achievements in the field of dissemination of scientific knowledge (2017). The second edition features new authors and new subjects. The volume of the book has more than doubled. In essence, this is a new book, written in a proven style. The main parts of the book are supplemented by sections “Bookshelf”, “Additions, comments, literature”, “Index”. The Bookshelf features time-tested books that enable the reader to gain a deeper and more comprehensive understanding of the world of mathematics. The section “Additions, comments, literature” develops and discusses the topics discussed in the main parts of the book. The “Index” section classifies the book’s plots in detail; it reflects both the areas of mathematics and the terms used. The book is published by decision of the Academic Council of the Steklov Mathematical Institute of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
