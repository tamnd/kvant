Engineering Olympiad for schoolchildren

For several years, a number of leading technical universities in Russia - National Research Nuclear University MEPhI, Russian University of Transport (MIIT), St. Petersburg State Electrotechnical University "LETI", Nizhny Novgorod State Technical University named after R.E. Alekseev and Samara National Research University named after Academician S.P. Korolev - have been holding an unusual Olympiad dedicated to physics in technology, physics around us, physics in human life. The organizers called this Olympiad the Engineering Olympiad for schoolchildren to emphasize that it is more than just physics. In the tasks of the Olympiad, there were problems devoted to the operation of many engineering devices - a rolling mill, a car differential, the Lipkin-Posselier mechanism, a suspension bridge, a liquid pump, a corner reflector and the physics on the basis of which they work. It seems to us that the task of the Engineering Olympiad for schoolchildren slightly shortens the gap between school physics and physics that helps people live, and shows how interesting the profession of an engineer can be. In the 2018/19 academic year, the qualifying rounds of the Engineering Olympiad for schoolchildren were held at more than 30 sites in our country, as well as in Armenia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan. The Engineering Olympiad for Schoolchildren is included in the List of Olympiads for Schoolchildren, therefore its winners and prize-winners receive special rights for admission to any universities in the Russian Federation, and citizens of foreign countries can apply to study at Russian universities at the expense of the state budget of the Russian Federation (within the framework of Rossotrudnichestvo quotas of the Russian Federation). Below are selected problems of the final round of the Engineering Olympiad of the 2018/19 academic year. (Problems not included here were published in the “Problem Book «Kvant»” in No. 5 for 2019.)
