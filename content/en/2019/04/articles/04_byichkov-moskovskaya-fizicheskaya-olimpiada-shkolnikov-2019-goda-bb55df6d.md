What kind of load can little Nils lift with himself, pulling himself up with his hands on a horizontal tree branch? The mass of the big Nils is 30 kg. Clue. This happens because shaking reduces the frictional forces between grains of sand. Let's consider experience. There is sand at the bottom of the cylindrical vessel.
