OLYMPICS

Moscow Physical Olympiad for Schoolchildren 2019

SELECTED TASKS

7th grade

1. Nils on a branch (1st round) Nils Holgersson's height after the dwarf's spell decreased by 10 times. The body proportions remained the same. When he was big, his arms were barely strong enough to pull himself up on the horizontal bar. What kind of load can little Nils lift with himself, pulling himself up with his hands on a horizontal tree branch? The mass of the big Nils is 30 kg. Clue. The strength of muscles is proportional to their cross-sectional area. S.Varlamov

2. “Liquid” sand (1st round) If a vessel with sand is subjected to vibration (in other words, shaken), then an interesting phenomenon can be observed - the sand becomes like a liquid. Heavy objects (with high density) sink in this “liquid” sand, and light (for example, wooden) objects, on the contrary, float to the surface, even if they were initially at the bottom of the vessel. This happens because shaking reduces the frictional forces between grains of sand. Let's consider experience. There is sand at the bottom of the cylindrical vessel. Pebbles are poured on top of the sand. The vessel is vibrated and the sand becomes “liquid.” The pebbles gradually sink to

3. Vasily and concrete (2nd round) Vasily needs to pour the foundation of a country house, for which he needs $9,4 м^{3}$ concrete, while Vasily’s budget is limited to 50,000 rubles. It is known that to obtain concrete it is necessary to mix cement with sand and crushed stone, and the greater the volume fraction of cement in the mixture, the better the quality of concrete and the greater the value of the concrete grade. In the store, Vasily found out that the price of cement is 180 rubles. for 50 kg, the price of sand is 102 rubles. for 50 kg, the price of crushed stone is 130 rubles. for 40 kg, and the ratios of volumes of materials for different grades of concrete are indicated in the table. Unfortunately, some of the data in the table was lost.

1) Help Vasily fill in the empty cells in the table. It is known that the volume of sand in concrete grade M400 is two times less than the volume of crushed stone. 2) Determine what is the best brand of concrete Vasily can afford. Density of cement $U_{ц} 1200$ kg $м^{3}$, density of sand $U_{п} 1600$ kg $м^{3}$, density of crushed stone $U_{щ} 2100$ kg $м^{3}$. N. Trushnikov
