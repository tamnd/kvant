physics+technology

Today we will focus your attention on the MATERIALS used in technology. The history of their development is the history of civilization. From the very first attempts to adapt - for the construction of dwellings or hunting, for battles or domestic needs - the stones and pieces of wood surrounding him, at first unconsciously, he came across the need to understand their properties. Hard and soft, strong and crumbly, elastic and fragile, they sometimes posed difficult riddles to the fragile mind. But thinking about them prompted the search for patterns that explain these properties. The need not only for applied experience passed on from generation to generation, but also for its comprehension grew as we mastered more and more new natural materials provided by nature. This need especially increased when man encroached on the creation of artificial materials. And here it was no longer possible to do without science, which integrally included physics. Of course, now we can take only a cursory glance at a tiny fraction of the innumerable multitude of materials that surround us and their varied characteristics. But even those that are covered in the school course can give an idea of ​​​​the huge field of knowledge called materials science - another fruitful union of physics and technology.
