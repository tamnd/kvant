The direction of the stations is coordinated with their location on the train route. At the same time, the passenger also looks at the door - whether other people in the carriage will interfere with his exit or whether he should stand closer to the door. This means that the circuit he needs should be located above the door through which the passenger exits. Now it becomes clear why inside the same carriage we see two different diagrams of the same metro line. Both diagrams correspond to the actual locations of stations along the metro line. When we move our gaze from one diagram to another, we turn to $180\infty$. Figure 1 corresponds to the case when the train approaches Lubyanka from Chistye Prudy, and figure 2 – when from Okhotny Ryad. In the new metro trains, instead of paper diagrams, electronic ones appeared, on which the movement of the train is displayed by running lights. They give reason to remember other lines of the same poem “Street”:

And the lanterns are burning so dimly, They can’t stand still. So they flicker here and there...

car at the Barrikadnaya station through the door shown at figure 3. Looking at the diagram, tell me: where is the train moving - left or right? This question cannot be answered! Electronic displays in a subway car (possibly to save production) are of only one type; the lights on them run from right to left. Such displays are located on both sides of the car. In this case, the direction of movement of the lights coincides with the direction of movement of the train only on one side of the car. If they do not coincide, a person experiences a condition called cognitive dissonance. You know the phenomenon of resonance; dissonance, one might say, is its opposite. Dissonance in music is a violation of harmony, an incorrect combination of tones that makes an unpleasant impression on listeners. In general, dissonance is something that brings discord into something, comes into conflict with something. We hope that our readers - future engineers and designers - will take into account all circumstances in their work, and everything that they create will not lead to any dissonance.
