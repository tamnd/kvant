The merit of Townes, Basov and Prokhorov is that they developed methods for creating an environment with an inverse population of levels. Basov and Prokhorov implemented a method for amplifying microwave radio radiation and called these devices masers (the first letter of the name is from microwave). Only with the help of masers has it become possible to communicate between terrestrial and artificially created cosmic sources and receivers of highly directed radiation. Townes and his colleagues focused their efforts on developing amplifiers (generators) of radiation in the optical range (the first letter l in the word “laser” corresponds to the English light). A so-called three-level ruby ​​laser was created, in which the inverse state of population was achieved due to non-radiative transitions between excited atoms of aluminum and its impurity, chromium. The necessary support for the excited states of atoms was carried out by irradiating a transparent ruby ​​crystal with a sufficiently powerful spiral lamp with a wide range of radiation. Such a laser could operate continuously. It quickly became clear that the laser could become an extremely powerful and precise technical tool. The concentration of energy in a narrow laser beam creates strong light pressure. First of all, it could be used in precision metal processing - cutting, welding and the like. But for this it was necessary to increase the power of the beam. The obstacles to this in continuous lasers are overheating of the emitting medium and destruction of reflecting mirrors by light pressure. Therefore, in principle, high-power lasers could only be pulsed. In pulsed lasers, their power limit became $10^{3}$ W, which gave the pressure exerted by the beam on the target on the order of

Amplification of chirped pulses

The easiest way to translate the word “chirping” is to chirp, something reminiscent of a bird’s chirping. Back in 1960, chirp was the name given to frequency modulation of radio waves used to amplify the power of radars. However, transferring this idea from radio waves to the optical range with frequencies tens of thousands of times higher required a completely different solution, both theoretically and practically. Initially, the stimulated laser pulse is stretched thousands of times in length and time. Accordingly, the radiation power is reduced by thousands of times.
