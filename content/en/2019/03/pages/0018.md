M2538. Petya places 500 kings on squares of the board $100 u^{50}$ so that they do not beat each other. And Vasya places 500 kings on white squares (in a chess coloring) of a 100 and 100 board so that they do not beat each other. Who has more ways to do this?

Answer. Vasya has more ways. The idea of ​​the solution is to associate with each Petya's arrangement some Vasya's arrangement so that different arrangements are associated with different ones, and in addition, indicate Vasya's arrangement that is not associated with any Petya's arrangement.
