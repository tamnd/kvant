FINAL STAGE

The final stage of the Lomonosov Olympiad was scheduled for February 22, 2019. For students of all classes, this stage was carried out face-to-face at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University and at three regional sites in the cities of Astana (Kazakhstan), Belgorod and Nevinnomyssk. The assignment for students in grades 7–8, as well as grades 9, consisted of four problems on topics studied as part of the physics program for a basic secondary school. The tasks for students in grades 10–11 were compiled in full accordance with the 2019 Unified State Exam Codifier in Physics and covered the main sections of the Codifier, namely: 1) mechanics, 2) molecular physics and thermodynamics, 3) electrodynamics and 4) optics. The typical assignment included four different sections consisting of problems and clarifying questions on the theory.
