KVAN T 2019/No. 3

Left, right - where is the side?

S. DVORYANINOV

THE RUSSIAN POET VASILY Ivanovich Sirotin LIVED IN THE 19TH CENTURY. Most of his work is irretrievably lost. His most famous poem, which has survived to this day, is called “Street”. It was published in 1859. Here are his first lines: Since I am returning home to my place, the street seems strange to me. Left, right side where? Street, street, you, brother, are drunk. Now I’ll explain why I remembered this. I'm riding home on the Sokolnicheskaya line of the Moscow metro. I should get off at Lubyanka station. Above the exit from the car is a diagram indicating the stations of this line (Fig.1). Okay, very convenient.

The next day the same thing. Evening, metro, approaching Lubyanka. I stood at the door, getting ready to go out. Above the door there are the usual names, but now on the diagram the stations are indicated in a different order for some reason (Fig.2). He looked back, looked back. Above the opposite door is a diagram, as in figure 1. Here the names of the stations are like a reflection in the mirror figure 2. But then the train stopped, the doors swung open, and I stepped out onto the platform. And now - questions. Why are the names of stations on different diagrams in the same carriage?

We advise readers to stop reading now, turn on their spatial imagination and think about these questions. Just in case, let us remind you that if a passenger train has a “Moscow-Samara” sign on it, then this train goes from Moscow to Samara. And on the way back, on the same carriage there will be another sign “Samara-Moscow”. Yes, still. On the Sokolnicheskaya Line, metro trains move on the right. At each station, exit to the platform on the left (taking into account the direction of the train). And we continue our story. Reflecting on what has been said, one should take into account the psychology of a passenger in a subway car. This passenger wants, of course, to get to the desired station as quickly as possible. From time to time he glances at the movement diagram, mentally noting his movement on it. Naturally, stations should be listed from left to right - this is how we read and write.
