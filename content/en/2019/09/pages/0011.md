Europe, using lithium-ion batteries as storage devices and a charge/discharge cycle efficiency of 85%, the production of these batteries requires energy 15 times (!) higher than the current annual energy consumption in Europe. Recently, the transition to so-called hydrogen energy has been widely discussed. The name itself hints at a new way of producing energy. But in fact, this is one of the options for an energy storage device in which hydrogen is first obtained from water, expending energy, then it is stored (in cylinders or fuel cells) and, finally, released as needed (for example, in a car). When applied to the global energy sector with a large share of wind and solar generation, this method turns out to be not very effective. Indeed, the efficiency of obtaining hydrogen from water is 65–70%, and the efficiency of obtaining electricity from hydrogen is $\sim$ 50%. Thus, the total efficiency is only 30–35%,

THE PROBLEM OF CONSTRUCTING THE MOST accurate or convenient to use map of the earth's surface has been known since ancient times. The mapping of the earth's surface (or part of it) onto a plane is called a cartographic projection (map) and associates a point on the surface with a point on the plane.
