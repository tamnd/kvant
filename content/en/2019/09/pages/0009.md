Capacity factor of renewable and non-renewable energy sources. The variable and even unpredictable nature of wind and solar power generation means that the respective units are not used optimally and capacity utilization decreases. At nuclear power plants this coefficient reaches 95–98% (on average it is above 80%). For wind turbines, it is approximately 17% on average in Europe, 15–20% in Germany, 15% in China, 25% in the USA, where there are many areas where strong winds constantly blow. For solar power plants, the capacity utilization factor is about 10% in Germany, 15% in France, 15–10% in the USA. This means that the installed wind and solar generation capacity, i.e. that which could be obtained under optimal conditions significantly exceeds the power that is actually generated.
