For other metals the situation is even worse, except that Al behaves better precisely at 0.25–0.4 μm (reflects 90%). So the two of them pretend to be record holders. It is possible to increase the reflection, and significantly, by “enlightening” - applying interference coatings to the metal. This way you can get a reflection of 50% at a wavelength of 0.01 microns - and this is almost an x-ray.
