At the Tel Aviv Olympiad, participants were offered three theoretical problems and two experimental tasks. In contrast to the trend of recent years to prepare problems, including those on modern, non-school sections of physics, this year’s Olympiad committee prepared problems exclusively on “classical” sections of physics that are well known to schoolchildren. Many team leaders noted this year's set of problems as one of the best sets in the last few years. The maximum number of points that each student could score was 50.
