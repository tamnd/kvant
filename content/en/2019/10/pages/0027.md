3.

Yossi has three devices that determine whether there is jam in the donut. One of the devices always gives the correct answer, the second always gives the wrong answer, and the third gives random answers. One day Yosya bought three donuts. He knows that exactly one of them is with jam. How can he be sure to determine which one it is without breaking donuts? Olympics "Zuta" (Israel)
