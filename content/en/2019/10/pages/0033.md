OLYMPICS

LX International Mathematical Olympiad

The anniversary LX International Mathematical Olympiad was held in July 2019 at the University of Bath, located in Somerset in southwestern England. Among the attractions of Bath, the abbey and baths founded in the 7th century stand out - a legacy of Roman Britain. An hour's drive from Bath is an even more ancient monument - the mysterious Stonehenge and the city of Salisbury, famous for its cathedral. All these places were visited with interest by the Olympiad participants in their free time from solving problems. 621 schoolchildren from 112 countries took part in the Olympiad - this is another record both in the total number of participants and in the number of participating countries. This year our country was represented by school graduates Oleg Smirnov, Valery Kulishov (both from Moscow), Vladimir Petrov (St. Petersburg), as well as tenth graders Ivan Gaidai-Turlov (Moscow), Alexey Lvov (Novosibirsk), Timofey Kovalev (Astrakhan - Moscow). Our guys won 2 gold and 4 silver medals. By tradition, we present a table with scores for the tasks of our team members (each task was scored out of 7 points) and the results of the first ten countries in the team ranking.
