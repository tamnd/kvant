LX International Mathematical Olympiad

The anniversary LX International Mathematical Olympiad was held in July 2019 at the University of Bath, located in Somerset in southwestern England. Among the attractions of Bath, the abbey and baths founded in the 7th century stand out - a legacy of Roman Britain. An hour's drive from Bath is an even more ancient monument - the mysterious Stonehenge and the city of Salisbury, famous for its cathedral. All these places were visited with interest by the Olympiad participants in their free time from solving problems. 621 schoolchildren from 112 countries took part in the Olympiad - this is another record both in the total number of participants and in the number of participating countries. This year our country was represented by school graduates Oleg Smirnov, Valery Kulishov (both from Moscow), Vladimir Petrov (St. Petersburg), as well as tenth graders Ivan Gaidai-Turlov (Moscow), Alexey Lvov (Novosibirsk), Timofey Kovalev (Astrakhan - Moscow). Our guys won 2 gold and 4 silver medals. physics+technology

Alas, it is known that one of the powerful catalysts for scientific and technological progress at all times has been military conflicts. The history of the creation of various types of WEAPONS testifies to the increasing involvement, especially in recent centuries, of inventors, engineers and scientists in the development of guns and cannons, machine guns and tanks, fighters and bombers, cruise and ballistic missiles, submarines and aircraft carriers... Physics was closely intertwined with the production of weapons, the apotheosis of which was the appearance of atomic and hydrogen bombs in the 20th century. Of course, this connection could not but affect the school, as can be judged by the names of the subjects - “civil defense”, “initial military training” and, of course, my favorite lessons on life safety. However, most military topics are reflected in the physics course: we had plenty to choose from for today’s Kaleidoscope plots. To be fair, we note that many inventions and discoveries in the field of defense migrated to the civilian world - and we will return to this in our section.
