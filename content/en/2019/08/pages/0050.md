Olympics “Conquer the Sparrow Hills!”

PHYSICS

Olympics “Conquer the Sparrow Hills!” has been running since 2005. From the very beginning, it arose as a social project aimed at finding talented schoolchildren in all regions of Russia. In the 2018/19 academic year, the Physics Olympiad was held for two categories of schoolchildren: “junior” (grades 7, 8 and 9) and “senior” (grades 10 and 11). The qualifying stage of the Olympiad was held remotely: after receiving the tasks, each participant was given a week (168 hours) to complete the task. The final stage was held in person in six cities of the Russian Federation: Zheleznovodsk, Kemerovo, Moscow, Chelyabinsk, Nizhny Novgorod, Ufa. The Olympiad traditionally uses original problems of a fairly high level of complexity, the most complex and interesting ones in the creative part of the qualifying stage. Some of them do not have a “single correct” solution, since the participant can use different models of the phenomena under consideration, and the assessment of his solution depends on the adequacy and level of accuracy of the model used, and on the correctness of the solution within the framework of the chosen model. Below are examples of tasks from both stages of the Physics Olympiad for “senior” schoolchildren.
