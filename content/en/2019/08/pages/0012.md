AS YOU PROBABLY GUESS, light is needed in order to see. The division of substances and, in general, everything that surrounds us into transparent and opaque arose historically. It was important for a person to see the object, i.e. something opaque, light-scattering, reflecting or emitting, and seen through a transparent medium - the atmosphere (always) and sometimes plus through a vacuum (to admire the Moon) or water (when hunting from the shore with a spear). Then, with the development of optics (magnifying glasses, glasses, telescopes) and the beauty industry (mirrors), this division became stronger. Therefore, in the textbook, all substances are divided into transparent and others. The optical properties of transparent media are characteristic
