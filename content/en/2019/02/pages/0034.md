One of the most in-demand professions today is engineer. Moreover, the engineer is innovative, a special specialist, armed with knowledge of the 21st century, who understands new technology and creates the technology of tomorrow. What is necessary for his education, what should he receive at school? Of course, first of all, basic training in the exact sciences, where, naturally, physics plays an important role. The connection between this science and technology will be discussed in the upcoming issues of Kaleidoscope. It would be naive to think that we will be able to touch even a small part of the huge and varied world of their contacts. But it is precisely the school physics course that tells you what exactly is worth paying attention to, what topics make it possible to choose interesting and useful stories that reflect their interaction. Now we will focus on CONSTRUCTION - one of the oldest areas of human technical activity. Of course, the clear influence of science on architecture has only become noticeable in the last few centuries. Before this, for thousands of years, people accumulated knowledge, developed technical acumen and showed ingenuity, relying on the experience of their ancestors and their own intuition. However, we, even having gained access to gigantic volumes of modern information, should not neglect this “golden fund”. After all, we are often only surprised at how efficient old technical discoveries are today. For example, simple mechanisms or tools for arranging and repairing our homes.
