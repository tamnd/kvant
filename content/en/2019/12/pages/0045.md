INFORMATION

Students of all departments who have successfully completed the ZFTS program, upon completion of the 11th grade, receive a certificate with final grades in the subjects studied in the 11th grade. The certificate is taken into account for admission to MIPT in accordance with the rules for admission to MIPT and the Procedure for taking into account individual achievements of applicants (https://pk.mipt.ru/ bachelor/2020_ID). Students of all departments will be invited to participate in the Physics and Mathematics Olympiad “PHYSTECH - 2021”, which is held on the basis of MIPT and in a number of Russian cities in February or early March, as well as in other full-time and extramural Olympiads of MIPT and its faculties. For students and leaders of elective groups there is an online lecture in physics, mathematics and chemistry according to the ZFTS program. Lectures are given by MIPT teachers (as a rule, the authors of the assignments). More information about these events can be found on the ZFTS website. Education at ZFTSH is free. For students living outside the Russian Federation, only paid education is possible at part-time or part-time courses.

Correspondence department (individual distance learning) Telephone: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing the introductory assignment in the subjects chosen for study. Schoolchildren entering the correspondence department complete the introductory task on the website https://zftsh.online using the built-in editor or by attaching scanned copies or photographs of work completed in a notebook. Work completed in notebooks and sent by mail will not be accepted. When registering at https://zftsh.online, you must attach a legible copy of your school transcript. The introductory assignment must be submitted for review no later than March 1, 2020. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated

Attention to schoolchildren already studying at the correspondence department of ZFTSH If a student is already studying at ZFTSH and wants to add another subject for the next year, it is necessary to complete the introductory task in this subject on the website https://zftsh.online by March 1, 2020. You need to complete the introductory task from your existing account. There is no need to register again. In such cases, the decision of the admissions committee is not sent, and a certificate of adding a subject and assignments for it become available to the student in his personal account in July if the decision of the admissions committee is positive. Training on the zftsh.online platform During the academic year, in accordance with the program, the student receives access to assignments on the subjects studied in his personal account on the website https://zftsh.online. The student completes assignments on the website using the built-in editor or by attaching scanned copies or photographs of work completed in the notebook. After the completion date, the work is checked on the website by the ZFTSH teachers assigned to the student. As soon as the work is checked, the student sees his work with a review and the author’s solutions to the control part of the assignment.
