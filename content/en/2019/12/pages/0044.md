Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (CPTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University) (MIPT) enrolls students in grades 7–10 from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation into grades 8–11. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 100 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its former student. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. Education at the school is conducted in four scientific and technical subjects - physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry. In 8th grade, only physics and mathematics are studied. In grades 9–11, these subjects are supplemented by the mathematical foundations of computer science and ICT (computer science) and chemistry. Students can choose to study one, two, three or four subjects. The number of assignments per year by grade and subject is presented in the table:

The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics, higher mathematics and the department of chemistry of MIPT, as well as MIPT graduates and other specialists who have extensive experience working with gifted students. The assignments contain theoretical material, analysis of typical examples and problems on the relevant topic, and 8–12 test questions and problems for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones. Examples of tasks can be viewed on the ZFTSH website. The goal of our school is to help students in grades 8–11 of general education institutions who are interested in scientific and technical subjects, deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, and also contribute to their professional self-determination. ZFTSH programs are specialized additional general developmental programs and are the same for all departments. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2020/21 academic year is carried out for part-time, full-time and part-time departments. The full training program is designed for 4 years - from 8th to 11th grades inclusive, but you can start training from any of the specified classes. According to the regulations on ZFTSH, a student can study in only one department of ZFTSH.
