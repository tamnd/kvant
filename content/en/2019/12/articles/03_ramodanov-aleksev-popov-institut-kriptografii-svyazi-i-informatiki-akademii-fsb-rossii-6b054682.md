OPTION 1

1. How many times will the gravitational force between two identical homogeneous balls decrease if at first the balls were in contact with each other, and then one of the balls was moved away to a distance equal to twice the diameter of the balls? 2. What mass of water must be additionally evaporated in a room of volume $49,8 м^{3}$ in order to increase the relative humidity from 25% to 50% at a temperature of 27 q C? The distance between the plates in a flat capacitor is 10 mm. The potential difference between the plates is 300 V. What force from the electric field will act on a charge of 1 nC introduced into the capacitor? 4. Find the magnitude of the ball's velocity at those moments when its acceleration in magnitude is part D of the acceleration amplitude ( D 1 ). 6.
