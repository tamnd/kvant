National Research University "MIET"

For many years, the National Research University "MIET" has been holding olympiads in mathematics, physics and computer science for high school students, which are very popular among the country's schoolchildren. Since 2015, the RHYTHM MIET Olympiad for schoolchildren has been held in correspondence (distance) form. Every year more than 2000 people take part in 9 sections of this Olympiad. In 2018, the university organized the full-time MIET Physics and Mathematics Olympiad, which takes place in several dozen cities in Russia and the CIS countries. Since 2019, the Electronic Nanoworld Olympiad for schoolchildren has also been held. Below are the physics problems proposed at the final round of the correspondence Olympiad “RHYTHM MIET”, as well as physics options for students in grades 10 and 11 of the full-time MIET Physics and Mathematics Olympiad.
