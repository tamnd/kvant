PHYSICS DAYS IN DUBNA

In mid-April, the VI All-Russian scientific festival “Days of Physics” was held in Dubna, Moscow Region. It was organized and conducted by the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research, the JINR Educational and Scientific Center and the Interschool Physics and Mathematics Elective. For several days, the Mir House of Culture turned into one large laboratory: experiments were demonstrated in the small hall, and the quest “Periodic Law” was held in the large and exhibition halls. Then the traditional mathematical festival and master classes took place, and Cosmonautics Day was marked by the launch of rockets at Molodezhnaya Polyana. High school students demonstrated and explained to visitors various physical laws using equipment they had designed. These experiments, always bright and sometimes unexpected and thought-provoking, invariably attract a large number of viewers - from preschool to retirement age. They are also attractive for participants, since, judging by their reviews, these events are not held in this format anywhere except Dubna. In addition, non-resident participants were able to get to know Dubna by going through an exciting quest around the city and visiting the JINR Museum of the History of Science and Technology, and playing the intellectual game “What? The participants were Moscow gymnasium No. 1514, physics and mathematics lyceum from St. Petersburg, and a team from Bryansk. For the first time, guys came from Volgograd, Glazov and Istra near Moscow. From the Dubna teams, guys from the physics and mathematics electives of the UC JINR and the Dubna Lyceum always actively participate. All the experiments presented by the guys are not easy. We always welcome experiments related to particle physics or nuclear physics. In the year of the anniversary of the discovery of the Periodic Law, our traditional quest for Physics Days participants is dedicated to this event. The guys receive a map - the Periodic Table - and, going through stations in the large hall of the House of Culture, solve various tasks on history, geography, even linguistic tasks, learn what role chemical elements play in our everyday life, which of them were synthesized in Dubna, and in the end receive a well-deserved prize. Physics teacher M.V. Akimova (Lyceum No. 1 of Volgograd): We are participating in the festival for the first time, and I learned about Physics Days a year and a half ago at the school of physics teachers held by the UC JINR (after which I also went to school at CERN). We sent here two papers that my girls prepared and waited for the decision. When they found out that both jobs had passed, the children were very happy. We have a physics and mathematics lyceum, and physics and mathematics are taught in depth, and project work is also carried out in physics. We participate in regional festivals and competitions. But here it’s more research work, beautiful experiments. We prepared them for about four months.
