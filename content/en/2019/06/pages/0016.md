TASK OF NICK “QUANTA”

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2562 – M2564, M2565a were offered at the final stage of the XLV All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics. Problem F2569–F2572 were offered at the SUSC Internet Olympiad in the 2018/19 academic year. The author of the problems is S. Varlamov.

Problem M2562–M2565, F2569–F2572

M2562. Each point A of the plane is associated with a real number f (A). It is known that if M is the intersection point of the medians of triangle ABC, then f ( M ) = = f ( A ) + f ( B ) + f ( C ). Prove that f ( A ) = 0 for all points A. A. Golovanov

M2563. Pasha and Vova play the next game, taking turns making moves. Pasha begins. Initially, the boys have a large piece of plasticine in front of them. In one move, Pasha can cut any of the available pieces of plasticine into three parts. Vova, on his own, chooses any two pieces and molds them together. Pasha wins if at some point among the available pieces of plasticine there are 100 pieces of the same mass. Can Vova stop Pasha from winning? R. Efremov, D. Belov
