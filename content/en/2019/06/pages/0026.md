physics+technology

How could a person engage in construction and process various materials - and this is exactly what was discussed in our previous physical and technical issues - if it were not possible to move various loads and even move around? So he had to rack his brains to find devices that would allow him to transport more and faster, in other words, to solve TRANSPORT PROBLEMS. For many centuries, wheeled carts, oars and sails helped us out - with their help we were able to explore vast spaces and even discover new continents. But “horsepower” and tailwinds were replaced by new cars, much more powerful and faster. Actively “absorbing” the achievements of science, transport technology, literally over the last two centuries, has radically changed the idea of ​​terrestrial scales and the time of reaching any point on our planet. Today we can no longer be surprised by drones and unmanned vehicles, hydrofoils and hovercraft, solar-powered aircraft and tourist flights in low-Earth orbits. What will happen tomorrow? Let's try to imagine our transport future, however, starting so far only from well-known means of transportation.

Why in wheeled ships the propeller - the wheel - was not completely submerged in the water, while in modern ships the propeller is completely submerged in water?
