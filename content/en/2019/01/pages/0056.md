Attention to schoolchildren already studying at the correspondence department of the ZFTS If a student is already studying at the ZFTS and wants to add another subject for the next year, it is necessary to complete and send the introductory assignment in this subject to the ZFTS. Be sure to indicate your personal number on the form. To add a subject, students studying on the zftsh.online platform must complete the introductory task for this subject on the website https://zftsh.online/ The decision of the admissions committee in such cases is not sent, and the additional subject becomes available to the student in their personal account if the decision of the admissions committee is positive.

Part-time department (training in optional groups) Tel.: (498) 744-63-51, e-mail: fakultativ@mipt.ru Optional groups can be organized in any general education institution by two, three or four teachers - physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry, in some cases training in one subject is allowed. The leaders of the elective accept students who have successfully completed the introductory task of the ZFTS. A group (at least 7 people) is admitted to ZFTS upon the application of the director on the letterhead of a general education institution (a sample can be viewed in the “part-time department” section of the ZFTS website), which must indicate the surnames, first names, patronymics of the leaders of the optional group in subjects and a named alphabetical list of students (full name in alphabetical order, indicating the class of the current academic year and the final grades for the introductory assignment for selected subjects), address, telephone, fax and e-mail of the school. The application can be sent by regular mail, enclosing an envelope for a response to the ZFTSH with the return address of one of the managers to the ZFTSH address (marked “Optional”),

or send it in scanned form (with signatures and seal) to e-mail: fakultativ@mipt.ru before May 25, 2019 to the ZFTSH address (marked “Optional”). Notebooks with students' work are checked by teachers of physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry, and are not sent to ZFTSH. The work of the heads of elective classes can be paid by the educational institution as the management of specialized elective classes by providing the ZFTSH with the relevant information. Leaders working with students will receive educational and methodological materials during the academic year (programs in physics, mathematics, chemistry and computer science, assignments on program topics, solutions to assignments with brief recommendations for assessing student work); be invited to advanced training courses for physics and mathematics teachers, conducted at MIPT. The students' work is checked and evaluated by the leaders of the optional groups, and they send statements with final grades for each task and a final report (grade 11) for the year to the ZFTSH, a sample is available on the ZFTSH website.

Full-time department (correspondence education with attendance of face-to-face consultations) Tel.: (925) 755-55-80, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Evening consultation centers operate for students in Moscow and the Moscow region under the ZFTSH program. Recruitment is carried out in September in two $этапа:_{i}$ correspondence stages - testing on the website $http://zftsh.online/,_{i}$ face-to-face stage - oral exams. More detailed information about admission to the full-time department is posted on the ZFTS website in early September. Classes for full-time students are held in the educational buildings of MIPT in the cities of Dolgoprudny and Zhukovsky.
