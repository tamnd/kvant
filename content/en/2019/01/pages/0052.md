2. (4) Find all natural numbers n that satisfy the condition: the numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 2n can be divided into pairs so that if you add the numbers in each pair and multiply the results, you get a square of a natural number. Folklore

3. A checkered rectangle of size 7 x 14 was cut along the grid lines into squares 2 x 2 and corners of three cells. Could there be: a) (1) as many squares as there are corners; b) (3) more than corners? M. Evdokimov

(5) Nastya has five identical-looking coins, among which three are real - they weigh the same - and two are fake: one is heavier than the real one, and the second is the same amount lighter than the real one. At Nastya’s request, the expert will perform three weighings on two-cup scales without weights, which she will indicate, and then report the results to Nastya. Can Nastya choose the weighings in such a way that, based on their results, it is guaranteed to identify both counterfeit coins and indicate which of them is heavier and which is lighter? R.Zhenodarov
