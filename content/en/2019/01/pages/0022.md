Its graph will be a surface - a paraboloid of revolution, which is obtained by rotating the parabola around its axis. Let us “raise” each point $X из^{X}$ onto a paraboloid. At each “raised” point X c we draw a plane tangent to the paraboloid. Tangent planes cut out a polyhedron described near a paraboloid (Fig.8). Voronoi noticed that if we project this polyhedron onto a plane, then the projections of the faces coincide with the Voronoi regions. Thus, the task of constructing a Voronoi diagram for a given set is reduced to constructing a convex polyhedron. This approach is widely used in modern
