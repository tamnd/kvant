Tel./fax: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing introductory tasks in the subjects chosen for study. ZFTSH is gradually switching to working on an online platform. Therefore: students entering the 8th and 9th grades of the correspondence department complete the introductory task on the website https://zftsh.online/; schoolchildren entering the 10th and 11th grades of the correspondence department complete the introductory task in a notebook, draw it up in accordance with our requirements (see below) and send it by Russian Post no later than March 1, 2019.

Training on the zftsh.online platform During the academic year, in accordance with the program, the student receives access to assignments on the subjects studied in his personal account on the website https://zftsh.online/. The student completes assignments on the website using the built-in editor or by attaching photographs of work completed in a notebook. After the completion date, the work is checked on the website by the ZFTSH teachers assigned to the student. As soon as the work is checked, the student sees his work with a review and the author’s solutions to the control part of the assignment.

How does training work for grades 10 and 11? During the school year, in accordance with the ZFTS program, the student receives (takes it from his personal account on the website http://www.school.mipt.ru/) assignments for each topic in the subjects studied. Completes them, draws them up in a school notebook and sends them to ZFTSH by Russian Post. Checked works, together with the author's solutions to these tasks, are also sent back by Russian Post. In both cases, training is carried out according to the same programs and tasks, graduates receive the same Certificates of Completion, and students’ work is checked by students, graduate students and graduates of MIPT. The only difference is in the method of submitting tasks.
