7. There are at least two cities in the virtual computer state. Some pairs of cities are connected by a road, and from any city you can get by road to any other (hereinafter, moving from road to road is allowed only in cities). If it is impossible to return to this city after starting from a city without passing twice along the same road, the state is called simple, otherwise it is called complex. Petya and Vasya play such a game. At the beginning of the game, Petya indicates on each road the direction in which you can move along it, and places a tourist in one of the cities. Next, during the turn, Petya moves the tourist along the road in the allowed direction to the neighboring city, and Vasya, in response, changes the direction of one of the roads entering or leaving the city where the tourist ended up. Vasya will win if at some point Petya is unable to make a move. Prove that: a) (5) in a simple state Petya can play in such a way as not to lose, no matter how Vasya plays; b) (7) in a difficult state, Vasya can guarantee himself a victory, no matter how Petya plays. M. Didin
