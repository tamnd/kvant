And Sakharov figured out how to explain the asymmetry of the Universe with such triple symmetry of the microcosm. Astronomers have long discovered that the Universe is asymmetrical in time. It is expanding - its constituent galaxies are moving away from each other. Today the Universe is wider than yesterday. And pose-pose-pose...yesterday? Let's run the cosmological film backwards. The expansion of the Universe now looks like compression, galaxies are approaching each other and merging. At the same time, the elementary particles themselves come closer together. The Universe begins to feel the laws of the microworld. It was in that era, called the Big Bang, that the asymmetry of the Universe - according to Sakharov's idea - took shape in the processes that were then seething in every micropoint of outer space, T-asymmetry made it possible to give rise to the now observed C-asymmetry - different contents of particles and antiparticles. In addition to the wing of the universal butterfly, which is visible to astronomers, the theoretical physicist Sakharov mentally saw another wing that opened before the Big Bang (Fig.7). This butterfly itself is CPT-symmetrical, but does not allow you to see it entirely
