THEORETICAL TOUR

9th grade

Problem 1. Navigation A ship moving uniformly and in a straight line passed point A, located at a distance L = 5 km from pier B (Fig. 1). Some time later, two boats set off from the ship and from the pier towards each other. Redraw figure into the solution form and, using a compass and a ruler without divisions, determine the point at which the ship was at the moment the boats met, if it is known that: • the boats were moving in a straight line at the same speeds, constituting 3/8 of the speed of the ship; • the time of movement of the boats from their start to the meeting is also equal to W; • when the boats met, the ship again found itself at a distance L from the pier. Describe the sequence of formations and find the distance (in kilometers) the boat travels in time W. There is no wind or current.
