Theoretical tour 7th grade Task 1. Navigator Experimenter Gluck drove a car to the station, the distance to which is s 10 km. For the first part of the journey he drove at a speed of $v_{1}$, and then at a speed of $v_{2}$. The on-board navigator showed not only the route, but also the estimated time until arrival at the station. Interestingly, the inscription “$W_{0} 12$ min 00 s left” appeared three times: at times $W_{1} 0$ min 30 s, $W_{2} 3$ min 00 s, $W_{3} 12$ min 00 s. Determine $v_{1}, v_{2}$ from these data, as well as the time of the entire movement W. The readings of the Gluck navigator were calculated as the ratio of the remaining distance to the average speed of the car at the distance traveled up to this point in time. D.Rubtsov

8th grade Problem 1. Average speed The tank drove the nth part of the entire journey through swampy terrain at a speed of $v_{1} 8$ km h. Then for the nth part of the entire time it drove along the highway at a speed of $v_{2} 32$ km h. Finally, for the remaining part of the journey it moved along a country road at a speed equal to the average speed $v_{cp}$ along the entire route. Calculate $v_{cp}$. At what values ​​of n is such a movement possible? V. Slobodyanin

Problem 2. Hidden mass A load with mass $m_{1}$ is suspended to the left end of a non-uniform rod, hinged on a fixed support, and a load with mass $m_{2} 4,0$ kg is suspended to the right end of the rod. The system is in equilibrium. Then the load with mass $m_{2}$ was removed, a load with mass $m_{1}$ was transferred in its place, and a load with mass $m_{3} 2,5$ kg was hung on the left end, and the system was again in equilibrium. Determine at what mass values ​​$m_{1}$ this is possible. It is known that the center of mass of the rod is to the right of the fulcrum. V. Slobodyanin
