The final stage of the LV All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics

This year, the final stage of the All-Russian Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in the city of Tyumen. Both the leaders of the regional stage of the current year (250 people) and those who were supposed to participate in the final stage of last year (140 people) arrived at the Olympiad. Below are the conditions of the tasks of the theoretical round and the list of participants who received diplomas as winners of the Olympiad.
