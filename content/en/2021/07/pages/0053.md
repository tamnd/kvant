Theoretical tour 7th grade Task 1. Navigator Experimenter Gluck drove a car to the station, the distance to which is s 10 km. For the first part of the journey he drove at a speed of $v_{1}$, and then at a speed of $v_{2}$. The on-board navigator showed not only the route, but also the estimated time until arrival at the station. Interestingly, the inscription “$W_{0} 12$ min 00 s left” appeared three times: at times $W_{1} 0$ min 30 s, $W_{2} 3$ min 00 s, $W_{3} 12$ min 00 s. Determine $v_{1}, v_{2}$ from these data, as well as the time of the entire movement W. The readings of the Gluck navigator were calculated as the ratio of the remaining distance to the average speed of the car at the distance traveled up to this point in time. D.Rubtsov

Task 2. Pumping the press In order for the hydraulic press suspended by the upper piston to remain at rest, a vertical force $F_{1} 50$ N (Fig.1,a) must be applied to its lower piston. If the press is turned over and suspended by another piston, then to balance it, a vertical force $F_{2} 200$ N (Fig.1, b) will need to be applied to the lower piston.
