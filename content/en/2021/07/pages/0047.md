5. The tangents drawn at vertices A and C to the circumcircle of triangle ABC intersect at point P. Then the straight line BP is symmetrical to the median BM with respect to the bisector of angle ABC (Fig.14). The straight line BP is called the simedian of the triangle. You can read about many interesting properties of the symmedian in wonderful articles [1], [2].

A difficult example In the end, let's look at such a difficult problem from the All-Russian Olympiad, in which several classical plots are intertwined at once: 6. In triangle ABC (AB BC), point I is the center of the inscribed circle, M is the midpoint of side AC, N is the midpoint of the arc ABC of the circumscribed circle (Fig.15). Prove that IMA INB. The median IM of triangle AIC is immediately visible. What about the IN segment? In which triangle could the segment IN be the median? A hint to the desired direction of search can be the fact that BN is the outer bisector of angle B of triangle ABC, because point N is the midpoint of arc ABC. Therefore, let's supplement figure with the centers of excircles of triangle ABC - points $I_{a}, I_{b}, I_{c}$ (Fig.16). Then points A, B and C in triangle $I_{a} I_{b} I_{c}$ are the bases of the altitudes (why?). Consequently, the circumcircle of triangle ABC in triangle $I_{a} I_{b} I_{c}$ passes through the bases of the altitudes, i.e. is the circle of nine points of this triangle. As you know, the circle of nine points also passes through the midpoints of the sides of the triangle. Whence we conclude that point N is the midpoint of the segment $I_{a} I_{c}$. Now we have two similar triangles AIC and $I_{a} II_{c}$, whose medians IM and IN give the desired equality of angles. Having read the decision above, the reader will most likely rightly be indignant: “How could anyone have guessed such a decision?” To this we note that this task, difficult in itself, served as an impetus for the creation of a separate effective and efficient method for solving problems, which is the subject of a fascinating article [3].
