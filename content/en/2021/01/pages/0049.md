Note that Lemma 2 can be generalized as follows. Lemma 4. There are 2n points marked on the line (the distance between neighboring points can be different!). The points were divided into n pairs and in each pair two points were connected by a segment. Then the greatest possible value of the sum of their lengths is achieved if and only if all the segments intersect the middle (the interval between two points of average order). Make sure that the same three proofs of Lemma 2 work for Lemma 4 too!

Exercise 4. In how many ways can these n segments be drawn so that they all intersect the middle?
