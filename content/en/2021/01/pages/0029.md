M2623. In a one-round football tournament, all teams scored different numbers of points. If a victory were given not three, but two points, then all teams would also have a different number of points, but the place taken by each team would be different. With what minimum number of commands is this possible?

Answer: at 12. Let us first assume that the number of teams is even: n 2k. Note that with two points for a victory, the team with the larger difference between the number of wins and the number of losses takes a higher place. Since these differences are different for all teams, the team in first place can have a difference (and therefore the number of victories) that is no less than k. The team ahead of it when switching to the three-point system must have at least two more wins (each team gets an additional number of points equal to its wins) and therefore must
