With the help of a special device called a controller and a motor, the electrical energy stored in the battery is converted into mechanical work without loss. We can assume that when moving at a constant speed, all mechanical work is performed by the scooter’s motor against the force of air resistance, which is proportional to the square of the scooter’s speed. 1. Disco ball (10 points) A disco ball is a ball with a mirror surface consisting of hundreds or thousands of faces, each of which is a small flat mirror. It is usually suspended from the ceiling from a device that rotates it evenly around a vertical axis. When the ball is illuminated by spotlights, viewers see numerous reflections (“bunnies”) running along the floor, walls and ceiling of the room (photo on figure 7). Mirror balls
