At the first iteration, three through holes of square cross-section are made in the cube. The axes of the holes are mutually perpendicular, perpendicular to the faces of the cube and pass through the middles of the faces. The length of the side of the square lying in the section of the hole is equal to 1 3 the length of the side of the face of the cube. In the second iteration, similar holes are made in small cubes formed on the faces of a large cube, and so on. Let there be a workpiece in the form of a cube with a side length of 1 m, made of plastic with a density of 1000 kg $м^{3}$, as well as a device with which you can make square holes of arbitrarily small size in this workpiece.
