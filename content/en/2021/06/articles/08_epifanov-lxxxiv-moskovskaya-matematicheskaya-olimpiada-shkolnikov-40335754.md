LXXXIV Moscow Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren

On March 28, 2021, the next Moscow Mathematical Olympiad was held (simultaneously with a difficult version of the spring round of the Tournament of Cities, many of the problems were common). Here are some problems from the Olympiad (the class in which it was proposed is indicated in parentheses after the problem number). All conditions and solutions can be found on the website olympiads.mccme.ru mmo.

Selected problems 1.(8) Baron Munchausen claims that to any two-digit number you can add two more digits to the right so that you get a complete square (for example, if the number 10 is given, then we add 24 and get $1024 32^{2} ).$ Is the baron right? M. Evdokimov

6.(9) Positive numbers a and b are such that a b a b. Which is greater: a b or ab? V. Kleptsyn, A. Gusev

2.(8) Mitya bought a round cake with a diameter of 36 centimeters and 13 thin candles for his birthday. Mitya doesn’t like it when candles are too close, so he wants to place them at a distance of at least 10 centimeters from each other. Will all the candles fit on the cake? D. Mukhin

3.(8) In the regular pentagon ABCDE, point F is marked - the middle of CD. The perpendicular bisector of AF intersects CE at point H. Prove that line AH is perpendicular to line CE. A. Blinkov

4.(8) Each of the 16 compartments of the 4 u 4 box contains a gold coin. The collector remembers that some two coins lying next to each other (neighboring on the side) weigh 9 grams, and the rest - 10 grams. In what minimum number of weighings on a scale showing the total weight in grams can these two coins be determined? M. Evdokimov

7.(9) The cells of a paper square 8 and 8 are painted in two colors. Prove that Arseny can cut from it along the grid lines two squares 2 u 2 that do not have common cells of the same coloring. (Colorings that differ in rotation are considered different.) L. Popov, A. Gusev

8.(10) A natural number is written on the board. If you erase the last digit (in the units place), then a non-zero number will remain, which will be divisible by 20, and if the first digit is divisible by 21. What is the smallest number that can be written on the board if its second digit is not equal to 0? M. Evdokimov

9.(10) Given an isosceles trapezoid, the sum of its sides is equal to the larger base. Prove that the acute angle between the diagonals is not greater than 60q. A. Blinkov

10.(10) There is a checkered strip that is infinite in one direction, the cells of which are numbered with natural numbers, and a bag with ten stones. There are initially no strips of stones in the cells. You can do the following: – move the stone from the bag to the first cell of the strip or back; – if there is a stone in the cell number i, then you can move the stone from the bag to the cell number i 1 or vice versa.
