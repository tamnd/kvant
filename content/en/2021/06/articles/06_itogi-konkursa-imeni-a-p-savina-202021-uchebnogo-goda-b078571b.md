Bogdan Chernov (10th grade), Ekaterina Kazakova (9th grade), Lev Gofshtein (9th grade), Mikhail Korolev (10th grade), joint mathematical group of the “Success” Center of the Gatchina Municipal District and the Information and Methodological Center of the Luga Municipal District of the Leningrad Region (head Sergey Pavlovich Pavlov): Denis Strekozov (5th grade), Dorokhova Sophia (7th grade), Timofey Krylov (7th grade), Ivan Markin (8th grade), Daniil Tyukov (10th grade), Valery Eronin (10th grade).

Congratulations!

The winners and prize-winners will be sent diplomas from the magazine «Kvant» and prizes from the International Center for Education and Science. In addition, the most active participants in the competition will also receive prizes.
