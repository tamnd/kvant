PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2646, M2647 was proposed at the final stage of the XIII Olympiad named after L. Euler. Problem F2653–2656 were offered at the 2021 Moscow Physics Olympiad.

Problem M2646–M2649, F2653–2656 M2646. Koschey the Bessmertny opened an account at Sperbank. Initially, the account had 0 rubles. On the first day, Koschey puts $k (^{k}$ on the count! 0) rubles, and each next day adds one ruble more than the day before (on the second day it is $добавляет^{k} 1$ rubles, on the third$^{k}$ 2 rubles, etc.). Each time, immediately after Koschey deposits money into the account, the total amount of the account is halved by the bank. Find all such k for which the amount in the account will always be expressed as an integer number of rubles. S. Berlov
