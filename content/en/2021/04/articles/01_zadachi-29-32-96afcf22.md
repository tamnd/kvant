But I would really like the absolute democracy of our Universe to be its integral property. Therefore, let’s not rush to conclusions now and wait until the fine details of the distribution of the cosmic microwave background radiation are subjected to “even more refined analysis.” And yet, I can’t get my head around two things: what we see is the obvious “undemocratic” figure 3,b, and the fact that we received reasoning

29. Each cell of a square table contains 1 or 1. The sum of all numbers in the table is 1. Is it possible to determine what their product is equal to? B.Frenkin

30. Points K, L, M and N lie on sides AB, BC, CD and DA of quadrilateral ABCD. Each point divides the corresponding side in the ratio 1 2 (for side AB either AK KB 1 2, or BK KA 1 2, etc.). Could it turn out that the area of ​​the quadrilateral KLMN is greater than the area of ​​the quadrilateral ABCD? S.Dvoryaninov

31. There are 50 points marked on the circle. Consider all triangles with vertices in them. Could there be exactly 2 times more obtuse triangles among them than acute triangles? E. Bakaev, S. Dvoryaninov

32. Consider the sequence of numbers 1, 2, 3, …, n. In one step you can swap any two numbers. Find the number of sequences that can be obtained from the initial one in n 1 steps, but cannot be obtained in fewer steps. V. Rastorguev
