During air nuclear tests, neutrons produced in huge quantities transformed many elements of the atmosphere and soil into radioactive isotopes. The main environmental hazard was the radioactive isotope of carbon$^{14}$ C. As part of carbon dioxide, it spread throughout the world, and there was no protection against it. And Sakharov, with all his analytical power, calculated the probabilistic effect of this isotope on humanity. By this time, radiobiological medicine had accumulated extensive statistics on radiation sickness and its effect on the body. The processing of these statistics horrified Sakharov. He calculated that as a result of aerial testing of nuclear weapons, the average duration of human life on Earth would be reduced by several years, not to mention the suffering of sick people (primarily the number of cancer diseases would increase). Sakharov not only brought these scientific results to the attention of the country's leaders, but also published them in scientific journals. Here is an excerpt from his 1958 article, which had the title “Radioactive carbon of nuclear explosions and non-threshold biological effects”: “Genetic effects in the cells of irradiated people, up to incurable diseases, will certainly appear even in the most distant descendants, up to several dozen generations.... The specificity in the moral aspect of this problem is the complete impunity of the crime (nuclear air tests), since in each specific case of death or illness of a person it cannot be proven that the cause lies in radiation.... We, each of us, must proceed from moral criteria.... An international agreement was signed on a complete ban on nuclear tests in three environments - in the atmosphere, in water and in space. What remains are underground tests. But they were more expensive and did not always give the desired results. The final victory of Sakharov’s idea occurred only in 1988, when an agreement was concluded on a complete ban on any nuclear tests and the slow systematic destruction of the gigantic arsenals of the United States and the USSR, accumulated over 40 years, began. And then, back in 1955, Sakharov still had no doubts about the need for the country of the work that he was doing in KB-11. Yes, not only for the country, but perhaps for all of humanity. Nuclear parity between the two countries possessing these weapons seemed then to be the salvation of humanity from destruction in a war using hydrogen bombs. Everyone (including us, young scientists) who was involved in the orbit of creating and testing nuclear weapons believed the same thing. Perhaps this is how it really happened. Sakharov was then concerned about moving the tests underground, with minimal environmental hazard. And the main work of our small group was precisely the study of underground explosions, including for their industrial use. And we took part in aerial tests of nuclear weapons as specialists in explosions and shock waves. Even at the nuclear test site, we did not stop our scientific work, using conventional explosives and the ability of the military organization to carry out large underground explosions of conventional explosives. Sakharov was very interested in the question: is it possible to determine the energy of the explosion (“power” of the bomb) from the manifestations of an underground explosion on the surface of the earth, primarily from a seismic wave?
