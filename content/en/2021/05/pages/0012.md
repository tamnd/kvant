This was confirmed by the enthusiastic opinion of him as a wonderful person, which we knew from everyone who communicated with him. He understood well what we said, although it was a completely unfamiliar area of ​​science to him. And everything interesting for us that we heard from him about our work, he framed it as if we knew about it without him and figured it out ourselves. He did not hesitate to say that he did not understand something, attributing this only to his lack of understanding, and not to our inability to clarify the matter. And he said how we have already done a lot to solve the problems associated with underground testing. This attitude towards us and our work literally inspired us. Moreover, it was accompanied by openness and humor. Communication with Sakharov went differently. We felt that he was “stronger” than Kurchatov in a scientific sense; he almost instantly understood an area that was completely new to him. But there remained a certain stiffness and detachment in his communication, accompanied, however, by impeccable politeness and respect for us. In his “Memoirs” he himself writes about his difficulty in communicating with other people. But now I think that the main reason for our different impressions from communicating with Kurchatov and Sakharov was the fact that it was at that time that Andrei Dmitrievich Sakharov began to seriously think about nuclear testing and his role in the creation of thermonuclear weapons. After the test of the “Tsar Bomba” in 1961, the slow “poisoning” of Sakharov, who had previously lived “in an ivory tower,” began with thoughts about the political situation in the world and the role of our country in world history. In 1968, Sakharov delivered his famous letter to the heads of governments of all countries, including the leadership of the USSR. In his letter, he put forward three major political objectives. The first is the cessation of the arms race and the gradual destruction of nuclear weapons stockpiles.

The second is the final elimination (in the USSR) of the consequences of the cult of personality, recognition of the activities of the party and state leadership of the country before 1953 as criminal and requiring repentance, and the provision of real freedom and political rights to every citizen of the country. Third, the fight against the threat of world hunger, which, according to many futurologists of those years, threatened humanity. Today it is clear that the first and third proposals of Sakharov have been accepted for implementation by the majority of countries. The letter gained worldwide fame. It was not published in our country, but was widely distributed in the so-called “samizdat”. Naturally, it aroused the anger of the country's leadership, since it not only did not coincide with the official ideology of that time, but also put forward the idea of ​​convergence - the bringing together of the capitalist and socialist worlds and the demand for openness and the rights of every person. Sakharov was removed from secret work, dismissed from the post of Chief Theorist of KB-11 and other administrative positions and assigned to the lowest possible position for an academician - senior researcher in the theoretical department of the Physical Institute of the Academy of Sciences. After this, Sakharov focused on activities in the field of protecting the rights of specific people whose political views did not coincide with the official doctrine. It is well covered not only in his own Memoirs, but also in numerous articles and books about that time. This activity consisted of attempts to attend and speak at court sessions where unjust sentences were passed, to provide material and moral assistance to convicts and their families, and to attract the world community to each case of conviction.
