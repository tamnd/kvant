Department of History

Studying at the history department will allow you to broaden your horizons, prepare for history olympiads, and get a high score on the Unified State Exam, Unified State Exam and other exams. Each section of the course is dedicated to a specific period of Russian history, accompanied by many illustrations, historical documents, sets of dates, and dynastic tables that cannot be found in a regular textbook. Such materials will help not only remember what has been covered, but also understand the content of the era. After all, we teach not just to remember, but also to think! For each completed task, we give our own comment: the student not only knows whether he answered correctly, but finds out why this particular answer is correct or incorrect. We recommend the best textbooks and manuals, but we offer our own, original, time-tested course. On our forum you can ask questions to teachers. They respond immediately. The training program is designed for high school students.
