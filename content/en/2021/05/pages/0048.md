KVANT 2021/№5

Oral round for 11th grade

1. Each of the functions f x and g x is defined on the entire number line and is not strictly monotone. Could it be that both their sum and their difference are strictly monotonic on the entire number line? D. Shnol

2. Petya and Vasya take turns painting the edges of the N-gonal pyramid: Petya – red, and Vasya – green (an edge cannot be painted twice). Petya begins. Vasya wins if, after all the edges are colored, a broken line consisting of green edges leads from any vertex of the pyramid to any other vertex. Otherwise, Petya wins. Which player can act in such a way as to always win, no matter how his opponent plays? A. Glebov

3. Point I is the center of the incircle of triangle ABC, and T is the point of tangency of this circle with side AC. Let P and Q be the orthocenters of triangles BAI and BCI, respectively. Prove that points T, P, Q lie on the same line. Folklore
