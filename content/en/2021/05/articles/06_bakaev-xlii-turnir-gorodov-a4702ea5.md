1. Each of the functions f x and g x is defined on the entire number line and is not strictly monotone. Could it be that both their sum and their difference are strictly monotonic on the entire number line? D. Shnol

2. Petya and Vasya take turns painting the edges of the N-gonal pyramid: Petya – red, and Vasya – green (an edge cannot be painted twice). Petya begins. Which player can act in such a way as to always win, no matter how his opponent plays? A. Glebov
