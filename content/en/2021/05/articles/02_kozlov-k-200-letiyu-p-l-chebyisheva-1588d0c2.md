FROM THE HISTORY OF SCIENCE

To the 200th anniversary of P.L. Chebyshev

V. KOZLOV

In May of this year, the mathematical community celebrated the 200th anniversary of the birth of the great Russian mathematician Pafnutiy Lvovich Chebyshev. By creating the famous St. Petersburg mathematical school, Pafnutiy Lvovich thereby laid the foundation for the progressive development of all Russian mathematics. Pafnutiy Lvovich enriched mathematics with outstanding results that brought him worldwide fame. He made a decisive contribution to the theory of distribution of prime numbers, proved the “Bertrand postulate” about the presence of prime numbers in the range from n to 2n – 2 (n! 3). P.L. Chebyshev obtained pioneering results in probability theory, formulating in general form the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem. These results were subsequently supplemented and expanded by his students A.M. Lyapunov and A.A. Markov. Pafnutiy Lvovich solved a difficult problem about the conditions for the integrability of a binomial differential in elementary functions, which is mentioned in all textbooks on mathematical analysis. He is the creator of the constructive theory of functions; Everyone knows the Chebyshev polynomial, which deviates least from zero. This line of research arose from Chebyshev's desire to improve flat hinge mechanisms. He constructed such mechanisms with his own hands, including the famous plantigrade machine, exhibited at

World Exhibition in Paris in 1878 (see "Kaleidoscope «Kvant», figure 1). Finally, let us mention Chebyshev networks in differential geometry, which were introduced by him in connection with the practice of drawing geographical maps. In general, P.L. Chebyshev is distinguished by the organic connection of his research in pure mathematics and its various applications. This amazing style of creativity of P.L. Chebyshev brings him closer to another great genius - C.F. Gauss. Pafnuty Lvovich was an influential and respected member of the St. Petersburg Academy of Sciences. The USSR Academy of Sciences established a gold medal named after. P.L. Chebyshev for the best research in mathematics (it is awarded, of course, in our time). In the historical building of the Presidium of the Russian Academy of Sciences, in the foyer of the meeting room, there are four busts of famous members of the academy. These are Mikhail Lomonosov, Leonard Euler, Aaron Lerberg (historian) and Pafnuty Chebyshev. Life and work of P.L. Chebyshev give all of us (and especially young people) the right guide in studying mathematics. To paraphrase Laplace’s words about Euler, we can say: read Chebyshev, study Chebyshev. He is a teacher and mentor of all Russian mathematicians.
