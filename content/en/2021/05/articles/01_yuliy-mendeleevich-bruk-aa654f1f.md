YULIY MENDELEEVICH BRUK

Julius Mendeleevich Brook passed away. The life and fate of Yuli Bruk is inextricably linked with the theoretical department of FIAN, where he was engaged in scientific work for many years, with physics Olympiads (he participated in the organization and conduct of many All-Union and All-Russian Olympiads) and with the journal «Kvant». Although he joined the editorial board of the magazine only in 1981, his close relationship with «Kvant» began not even with the release of the first issue of the magazine in 1970, but much earlier. After academician P.L. Kapitsa expressed the idea of ​​​​creating a children's physics and mathematics journal in 1964, Yulia actively took up the implementation of this idea. No wonder he was considered the most profound and informed historian «Kvant». And not only a historian, but also an active author, who wrote wonderful articles and came up with many interesting problems. In memory of him, we reproduce one of Julia Brook’s articles in this issue of the magazine. But first, let’s give the floor to Yulia’s co-author and his close friend Albert Leonidovich Stasenko.

In one of the old articles on history «Kvant» it flashed: “the legendary Julius Mendeleevich Brook.” His most active activities in the formation of the magazine and his communication with famous academic fathers were emphasized. Yulia had the talent of a writer, was an inexhaustible source of physical subjects, and was distinguished by the rigor of a calculator and the responsibility of a creator. Another whole layer of his activity is his active participation in the organization of the All-Union and All-Russian Physics Olympiads. Once on the plane, on the way to one of the Olympics, they were proving the universality of Julia: Brookhaven, Innsbruck... And someone remembered the ancient Roman Julius: “And you, Brook!” Yulia had a social circle of literally global scale, and followed the destinies of friends scattered all over the world.
