Our contacts: https: vzms.ru https: lycuz2.mskobr.ru +7(926)280-28-20 vzms2015@mail.ru (mail, reception) https: vk.com vzmsh 119234 Moscow, Vorobyovy Gory, Moscow State University, Second Academic Building, room. 307

The All-Russian multi-subject correspondence school invites schoolchildren and teachers from all regions of Russia, near and far abroad to study under additional distance (online) education programs. VZMSH is the oldest center of additional education in Russia. It was founded in 1964 on the initiative of outstanding mathematicians, academicians I.G. Petrovsky (at that time rector of Moscow State University) and I.M. Gelfand. Currently, VZMSH is a structural subdivision of the famous Moscow physics and mathematics “Lyceum “Second School”. We teach mathematics, physics, biology, Russian language and literature, history, and social studies. Our teachers are employees of M.V. Lomonosov Moscow State University and other leading universities and academic institutes. They have written an extensive library of unique textbooks on mathematics, history, social studies and other subjects. The methods they created allow for distance learning (online) at the highest level. Education of high school students at VZMS is aimed primarily at preparing for Olympiads and the Unified State Exam. However, we believe that even the highest grade cannot be an end in itself. We primarily train students for whom self-development comes first, who want to know BEYOND THAT, beyond the school curriculum, and to acquire additional knowledge in a variety of subjects. More than 200 thousand schoolchildren graduated from VZMSH. From their ranks came many scholars

VZMSH, unlike courses or clubs, is a real school, with its own program, rhythm, tests, two-way communication between student and teacher. We provide our materials in electronic form, constantly communicate with our students through social networks, tell them about what is not included in the curriculum, but helps their development, introduce books and articles by our teachers, conduct video conferences and video lectures.
