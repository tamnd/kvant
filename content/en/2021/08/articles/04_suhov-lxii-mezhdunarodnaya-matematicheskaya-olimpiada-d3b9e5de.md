OLYMPICS

LXII International Mathematical Olympiad

In July, the main mathematical competition for schoolchildren of the year took place - the International Mathematical Olympiad for Schoolchildren. Due to the ongoing pandemic, like last year, the Olympiad was held separately in more than 140 special examination centers equipped with video surveillance, where two rounds of the Olympiad were held on July 19 and 20. As in 2020, the organizing country of the IMO was Russia, the central organizing committee of the Olympiad worked in St. Petersburg on the basis of the Russian State Pedagogical University named after. A.I. Herzen. 619 young mathematicians from 107 countries took part in the Olympiad. Our country was represented at the Olympiad by: Ivan Bakharev (St. Petersburg, 10th grade), Aidar Ibragimov (Kazan/Moscow, 11th grade), Matvey Isupov (Izhevsk, 11th grade), Andrey Shevtsov (Moscow, 11th grade), Danil Sibga-
