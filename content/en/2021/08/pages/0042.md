Problem 8. Mirror Earth At the initial moment of time, the ISS (international space station), flying in a circular orbit around the Earth at an altitude of H 400 km above its surface, crossed the segment connecting the centers of the Earth and the Sun, and directly under the ISS, the astronauts could observe through a clear sky a complete calm on a large area of ​​the Pacific Ocean. After what time T will the image of the Sun in the ocean approach the ISS at a speed of u 0.4 km s? At what distance L from the ISS will the image be at this moment in time? Consider the Earth to be a ball with radius R 6400 km, the acceleration of free fall on the Earth's surface is g 10 m $с^{2}$. S.Varlamov
