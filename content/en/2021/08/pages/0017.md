M2652. 100 tourists arrived at the hotel at night. They know that the hotel has single rooms 1, 2, ..., n, of which k are under renovation (but they don’t know which ones), and the rest are free. Tourists can agree on their actions in advance, after which they take turns to check in: everyone checks the rooms in any order, finds the first available room that is not under renovation, and stays there overnight. But tourists don’t want to bother each other: you can’t check a room where someone has already checked in. For each k, indicate the smallest n at which tourists are guaranteed to be able to check in without disturbing each other.
