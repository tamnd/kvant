EXAMINATION MATERIALS

National Research University "MIET"

For many years, the National Research University "MIET" has been holding olympiads in mathematics, physics and computer science for high school students, which are very popular among the country's schoolchildren. Since 2015, the RHYTHM MIET Olympiad for schoolchildren has been held in correspondence (distance) form. Every year more than 2,000 people take part in 9 sections of the Olympiad. In 2018, the university organized the full-time MIET Physics and Mathematics Olympiad, which takes place in several dozen cities in Russia and the CIS countries. Since 2019, the Electronic Nanoworld Olympiad for schoolchildren has also been held. Below are the problems in physics of the final stage of the Olympiad "RITHM MIET", options for the Physics and Mathematics Olympiad MIET and selected problems of the Olympiad "Electronic Nanoworld".
