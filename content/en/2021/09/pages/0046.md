we see. Indeed, it is enough to look out the window to be convinced of this. One day, listeners asked me a question: does a porthole distort the image? Moreover, one in which the glass is not flat, but convex. In passing, we note that such glass can withstand greater pressure than flat glass, if this pressure is from the convex side. For example, as in the case of the bathyscaphe porthole. This is situation 3, where the dashed line shows how the glass deforms under pressure, and it is clear that all layers “work in compression.” In contrast to situation 4, when all layers “work in tension.” Glass, like many other materials, can withstand greater loads in compression than in tension. Let us note, first of all, that a convex porthole can be designed in different ways. If the thickness “radially” within the window is the same, then the radii themselves turn out to be different, their difference is exactly equal to the thickness of the lens. And the centers from which the radii are drawn coincide - situation 5. Another option - the radii are the same, but drawn from different centers - situation 6. The points of “equal inclination” a and b in the first case are located on the same radius, and in the second - at the same distance from the axis. The actual geometry of the porthole depends on the manufacturing technology, and there are several such technologies. In particular, if the sheet softens and bends on the mandrel, then this is situation 5, and if the porthole is stamped, placed between two forms - a punch and a matrix, then most likely this is situation 6. However, in neither case, the beam arriving at point a is absolutely not obliged to hit point b! Only the ray shown at figure will behave this way, and other rays arriving at point a from different directions will arrive on the second surface not at point b, but at other points with different inclinations. In other words, a curved sheet does not behave optically like a flat sheet, i.e. obviously distorts the image. But does the ordinary flat window glass with which we started the conversation behave well? Let's take a closer look at the situation.
