Problem 4. Physics in medicine (20 points) To measure some technical characteristics of a medical syringe, experimenter Gluck assembled the setup shown in figure 7. He secured the syringe under study in a vertical position. Instead of a needle, a thin flexible tube was attached to it, the second end of which was connected to a hole in the bottom of a cylindrical vessel. Gluck then measured the difference in the levels 'h of water in the vessel and the syringe, at which the syringe piston began to move upward during the $плав-^{Рис.} 7$ rise of the vessel. It turned out that the value of ' h depends on the mass $m_{г}$ of the load attached to the upper stop of the piston. He presented the results of measuring the dependence ' $h m_{г}$ in a table that also shows the value ' $h_{x}$ for a load of unknown mass $m_{x}$. Determine the area S of the piston and the sliding friction force $F_{тр}$ between the piston and the wall of the syringe. To do this: 1) derive the theoretical dependence ' $h m_{г}$; 2) plot the experimental dependence ' $h m_{г}$; 3) use the graph to determine $F_{тр}$ and S; 4) find the unknown mass $m_{x}$ of the load in the sixth row of the table.

Note. The mass of the piston can be neglected; there is no air gap between the piston and water in the syringe; water density U 1.0 $10^{3}$ kg $м^{3}$; acceleration g 10 N kg.
