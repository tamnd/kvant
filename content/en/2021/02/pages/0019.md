Conclusion

We fully admit that after reading the article, a thoughtful reader may have legitimate questions. –You know, I have no doubt about the correctness of the answers received above. But the logic you used to solve problems seems very unusual. In your problems, inanimate plates themselves choose fixed points, a fixed stop causes an expanding square to rotate, velocities generate forces and these forces are directed against the velocities. All this is very different from our ordinary World. This is not Newton's World, in which forces generate changes in speeds. This is not Aristotle’s World, in which forces generate velocities directed along the forces. This is a completely different World. – You are right, much in our reasoning was unusual. But there are no mistakes in them. Imagine a mechanical puppet theater made by a skilled mechanic. The dolls in this theater move, dance, meet and separate. The movement of the dolls is controlled by a dozen springs and rods hidden by the master behind the scenery. And, of course, if we look closely at the operation of the mechanism, we will find here Newton’s laws, Hooke’s law, and our usual reasoning. But we humans also have another ability. We can forget about the mechanisms and see before us only the heroes of a fascinating play, beautiful ladies and gallant gentlemen who live according to their own laws. According to laws that we will not find in mechanics, but which we very often find in life.
