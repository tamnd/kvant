Problem 3. Flash in a cube (12 points) In a cube of a substance with refractive index n 2, a point source emitted a short-term flash, the light from which diverged uniformly in all directions. Light is not absorbed by the substance of the cube. What values ​​can the fraction K of the flare energy released outside take, depending on the position of the source inside the cube? Indicate at what positions of the source this fraction is minimal, at which it is maximum, and what it is equal to. When light falls on an interface, part of its energy, depending on the angle of incidence, is reflected, and part passes through the interface.

Note. When solving, you may need the formula for the surface area of ​​a spherical segment: S = 2 S Rh, where R is the radius of the sphere, h is the height of the segment.

A. Apolonsky
