Let us call such a group of edges a (weak) link if, after the actions of the evil wizard, at least t edges remain in it, and a strong link if at least t 1 edges remain in it. Note that if a group of edges is a link, then there are two edges in it whose ends are the gnomes sitting next to each other at the original table. We call such edges neighboring. If the link is strong, then such two edges are not the only ones. Note that at least k – 1 groups of edges are links (the converse requires removing t edges each from the two groups that we want to make non-links, i.e. a total of 2t n 1 edges) and at least k – 2 groups of edges are strong links, unless n! 3 (in the case of k 2 the statement is obvious; for k! 2 the converse requires removing t – 1 edges from three groups, i.e. a total of 3t – 3 edges, which is more than n 2t – 1 if n! 3 and, accordingly, t! 2). For k 2, one of the two groups of edges is a link, let the corresponding two edges be $( 1_{i},2_{i} ), ( 1_{i} + 1,2_{i} + 1 )$. At k! 2 and n! 3 we have (at least) k – 1 links, one of which may not be strong. Let us fix a pair of neighboring edges in this link; Next, let's move on to neighboring links (perhaps in one direction or two) and select a pair of adjacent edges in them so as not to involve the same two gnomes (one of them is possible). This choice is available to us, since all links (except, perhaps, the first one considered) are strong. Let's remove in the original graph (consisting of k cycles) the edges between the gnomes that participate in the neighboring edges we have selected, and add the neighboring edges themselves; It is easy to see that this set of edges provides a cycle over all vertices. Then we have 3 weak links and k – 3 strong links. If k! 3, then there is a weak link in which at least one of the neighboring links is strong. Therefore, we can choose k – 1 links so that among them there are 2 weak links and these weak links are not adjacent. Next, we select a pair of adjacent edges on each link (starting with the weak ones), as in the previous paragraph. On any strong link, a choice between these weak ones is possible, since in the case of n 3 on a strong link, you can choose a pair of adjacent edges in three ways, and out of these three options, at most two are not available - one on each side. Finally, the following situation remained: n 3, k 3, and after the actions of the evil wizard, there were 2 edges left in each group. Then, due to the fact that the edges between the first and last tables are drawn with a shift of 1, we can select 2 groups so that different pairs of gnomes are involved on the table they share.
