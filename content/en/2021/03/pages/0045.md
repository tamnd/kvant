A sphere symmetrical with respect to a given line l is a sphere of the same radius, the center of which is symmetrical with the center of the original sphere with respect to line l. I. Bogdanov

5. There are $99^{2}$ shorties living in Flower City. Some of the short ones are knights (always tell the truth), and the rest are liars (always lie). Houses in the city are located in squares 99 and 99 (in total there are $99^{2}$ houses located in 99 vertical and 99 horizontal streets). There is exactly one short guy in every house. The house number is indicated by a pair of numbers x; y, where 1 d x d 99 is the number of the vertical street (numbers increase from left to right), and 1 d y d 99 is the number of the horizontal street (numbers increase from bottom to top). The flower distance between two houses numbered $x_{1} ; y_{1}$ and $x_{2} ; y_{2}$ is called the number $U x_{1} x_{2} y_{1} y_{2}$. It is known that on every street - vertical or horizontal - there live at least k knights. In addition, all the shorties know in which house the knight Znayka lives. You want to find his house, but you don’t know what Znayka looks like. You can approach any house and ask the short man living in it: “What is the flower distance from your house to Znayka’s house?” At what minimum k can you be guaranteed to find Znayka’s house? V. Novikov
