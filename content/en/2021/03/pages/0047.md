Problem 2. The road to the canal (10 points) Yarik and Prokhor, after a physics class, went for a walk along the bank of a long straight canal. Yarik walked, and Prokhor rode a bicycle. The graph of the dependence of the distance l between them on Yarik’s displacement s is shown on figure 1. At first the boys moved at constant speeds, but, tired, Yarik made a halt, at the end of which he called Prokhor and asked him to drive up to him, after which he continued moving at the same speed in the same direction. Prokhor turned around and, more than doubling his speed, headed towards his friend. As a result, the guys met 1 hour 55 minutes after they broke up. Determine how far Prokhor traveled from the beginning of the walk until he met Yarik, how many times Prokhor increased his speed after the turn, how long Yarik rested at the rest stop, what is Yarik’s speed. Justify the uniqueness of your answers. M. Zamyatnin
