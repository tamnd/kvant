The promised continuation of the conversation about the mutually multiplying union of these scientific fields is growing today with a third dimension. STEREOMETRY, coupled with physics, especially emphasizes the connection between concrete, tangible bodies and their images, rising to the convention of high abstraction. The simplest – they are fundamental – principles of geometry, studied in school, have evolved to the point of being able not only to describe the properties of the immediate environment, but also to embrace the entire imaginable (and inconceivable!) space of the Universe, to penetrate into the most complex structure of our world. The inseparability of geometric and physical approaches to solving a significant number of problems every now and then leads to their “migration” from one subject area or Olympiad to another. Acting as communicating vessels, the exact sciences are integrated, producing (to use a fashionable expression) a synergistic effect. Let's try to “ride” this centaur!
