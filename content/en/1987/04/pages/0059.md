We have: $m_1+m_2=5$, $m_1>0$, $m_2>0$, and the number of intersection points is $1+m_1m_2$; the latter is 5 or 7. Suppose that each line passes through at least two points of intersection. If the intersection point is less than 5, then there are 4 of them. Let us denote them by $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$; then our lines are lines $AB$, $AC$, $AD$, $BC$, $BD$ and $CD$. These lines must not have an intersection point other than $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, so $AB\parallel CD$, $AC\parallel BD$ and $AD\parallel BC$. From the first and third, the following is that $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, but the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a parallelogram cannot be parallel. Contradiction.

The numbers $x$ and $y$ are coprime: if $x$ and $y$ are divisible by $p$, then $2x$ and $2x+1$ are divisible by $p$, and therefore 1 is divisible by $p$. The number $2x+2y+1$ is divisible by $x$ and by $y$; this means it is divisible by $xy$, hence $2x+2y+1 \geqslant xy$. Let $x \leqslant y$; then either $2x+2y+1 < 5y$ or $x=y=1$. In both cases $xy < 5y$ and $x < 5$. This leaves two possibilities: $x=1$ and $3$ divided by $y$ or $x=3$ and $7$ divided by $y$.
