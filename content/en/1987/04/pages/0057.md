10. The optical axis of the lens $OO'$, the luminous point $S$ and its image in the lens $S'$ (Fig. 2) are given. Find the position of the focal point of the lens using a ruler and pencil.

Leningrad State Pedagogical Institute named after. A. I. Herzen

Mathematics
Written exam

Option 1
(mathematics department)
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_{x+1}(x-0,5)=\log_{x-0,5}(x+1).$$
2. Find $\cos 2\alpha$ if it is known that $2\mathrm{ctg}^2\alpha+7\mathrm{ctg}\alpha+3=0$ and the angle $\alpha$ satisfies the inequality:
a) $\frac{3\pi}{2} < \alpha < \frac{7\pi}{4}$; b) $\frac{7\pi}{4} < \alpha < 2\pi.$
3. Solve the system of inequalities
$$\sqrt{x^2-9x+20} \leq \sqrt{x-1} \leq \sqrt{x^2-13}.$$
4. A Wissan triangle has a circle of radius 3. Calculate the lengths of the sides of the triangle if one of them is divided by a point of tangency into segments with lengths 4 and 3.
5. In a regular quadrangular paramid, there is a cube so that four of its vertices are on the apothems of the paramid and four are in the plane of the base. All edges of the paramid are equal to $a$. Calculate the total surface and volume of the cube.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{a-1}} - \sqrt{a+1} : \frac{1}{\sqrt{a+1}} - \sqrt{a-1} : \frac{1}{(a-1)\cdot\sqrt{a+1}} - \frac{1}{(a+1)\cdot\sqrt{a-1}}.$$
2. Two bodies began to move simultaneously and are moving in a straight line towards each other. However, one of them travels 7 m in each minute, the other travels 24 m in the first minute, and in each subsequent minute it travels 4 m less than the previous one. How many minutes later did both bodies meet if the initial distance between them was 100 m?
3. Solve the equation
$$1 + \sin 2x = \sin x + \cos x.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$3^{\lg x} + 2 < 3^{\lg x^2} + 5 - 2.$$
5. The base of a right prism is a triangle with sides 5 and 3 and angle $120^\circ$ between them. The largest of the flat lateral faces is 35. Find the volume of the prism.

Option 3
(chemistry department)
1. Check equality
$$\sin 20^\circ \cdot \cos 10^\circ + \cos 160^\circ \cdot \cos 100^\circ = 1.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$3 \cdot 16^x + 2 \cdot 81^x = 5 \cdot 36^x.$$
3. For what values ​​of $p$ are both roots of the equation $x^2+2(p+1)x+9p-5=0$ negative?
4. Mixed 30% hydrochloric acid with 10% and obtained 600 g of 15% solution. How many grams of each solution were taken?
5. There is a straight prism in a vissan cylinder, the base of which is an equilateral triangle with an obtuse angle equal to $a$. Find the volume of the cylinder if it is known that the lateral side of the base of the prism is equal to $b$, and the diagonal larger than the lateral side is equal to $l$.

Option 4
(individual pedagogical faculty)
1. Prove accuracy
$$\cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta - \cos(\alpha + \beta) = \mathrm{tg} \alpha \cdot \mathrm{tg} \beta.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(2x-1) + \log_2(x+5) = \log_213.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^{x^2+5x} > 25^{-3}.$$
4. Simplify and calculate the value of the expression
$$\sqrt[6]{4x \cdot (11+4\sqrt{6})} \cdot \sqrt[3]{4\sqrt{2x}-2\sqrt{3x}}$$ at $x=50.$
5. The base of a line, using equalities, whose sides are 10 and 18, and the area is 90. The height of the lines passes through the point of intersection of the diagonals of the base and is equal to 6. Determine the lateral surface of this line.

Physics
Oral exam assignment
1. To leave the Earth, the package must reach a speed of $v=11,2$ km/s. At what height and after what period of time during a vertical rise will this happen if the package moves with a constant scale $a=30g$? What is the level of free fall at this altitude? The radius of the Earth is $R=6,37 \cdot 10^6$ m. Assume that the gravitational force acting on the stone changes according to the law $F=mgr/R$, where $r$ is the distance from the stone with mass $m$ to the center of the Earth.
2. If a through channel could be dug through the center of the Earth, how long would it take a free-falling stone to reach the opposite end of the channel? The radius of the Earth is $R=6,37 \cdot 10^6$ m. Assume that the gravitational force acting on the stone changes according to the law $F=mgr/R$, where $r$ is the distance from the stone with mass $m$ to the center of the Earth.
3. Find the dependence of the model of the sliding friction force of a body of mass $m$ on the inclination surface of the plane $a$ in the interval $0 \leq a \leq \pi/2$. The sliding friction coefficient is $\mu$.
4. Is it possible to reach the body from a well $h=12$ m deep using a pump located on the surface of the Earth and capable of creating any low air pressure in a pipe lowered into the well?
