(II) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} p - b = r \mathrm{ctg} \frac{\beta}{2}, \\ b = 2R \sin \beta, \end{array} \right.$

(III) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} p - c = r \mathrm{ctg} \frac{\gamma}{2}, \\ c = 2R \sin \gamma. \end{array} \right.$

Eliminating a from system (I), we obtain for $a$ the identity
$a^3 - 2pa^2 + (p^2 + r^2 + 4Rr)a - 4pRr = 0.$

Excluding the angles $\beta$ and $\gamma$, respectively, from the second and third systems, we obtain exactly the same correspondences for $b$ and $c$. This means that the numbers $a, b, c$ are the roots of the cubic equation $x^3 - 2px^2 + (p^2 + r^2 + 4Rr)x - 4pRr = 0,$ from which a whole series of identities follows that can be described by Vieta’s formulas. For example,
$$ab + ac + bc = p^2 + r^2 + 4Rr,$$
$$\frac{1}{ab} + \frac{1}{ac} + \frac{1}{bc} = \frac{1}{2Rr}.$$
S. Ts. Konrinski

Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. The longest side of the triangle has a length of 5 cm, the shortest side has a length of 1 cm. What is the most fruitful side that such a triangle can have?
2. Find the locus of the intersection points of the altitudes of triangles inscribed in a given circle.
3. Six different lines on a plane are not all parallel and not all pass through the same point. What is the most possible number of points of intersection of these lines?
4. Find all pairs of positive integers $x, y$ such that $2x + 1$ is divisible by $y$ and $2y + 1$ is divisible by $x$.
5. Which numbers are greater among the integers from 1 to 1000,000 inclusive:
a) divisible by 11, but not divisible by 5 or divisible by 12, but not divisible by 7?
b) divisible by 13 but not divisible by 6 or divisible by 15 but not divisible by 26?
Ninth grade
6. A circle of radius 1 was inscribed into the angle $a$. Then a second circle was inscribed, touching the first circle from inside the angle. Then they inscribed a third circle touching the second circle from inside the corner, and so on. Find the radius of the 1000th circle.
7. On the plane dan, convex polygon $P$. Let us denote by $P_a$ a figure composed of points located at a distance $a$ from $P$ (i.e., points $A$ for which there is a point $B$ inside or on the boundary of the polygon such that $AB \leq a$). The area of ​​the figure $P_1$ is $S_1$, the area of ​​the figure $P_2$ is $S_2$; find a flat polygon $P$.
8. A circle is drawn on the face of the checkered paper, the radius of which is 10 times larger than the side of the checkered paper. It is known that the circle of the number $a, b, c$ is the roots of the cubic equation $x^3 - 2px^2 + (p^2 + r^2 + 4Rr)x - 4pRr = 0,$ from which follows a whole series of identities that can be described by Vieta’s formulas. For example,
$$ab + ac + bc = p^2 + r^2 + 4Rr,$$
$$\frac{1}{ab} + \frac{1}{ac} + \frac{1}{bc} = \frac{1}{2Rr}.$$
