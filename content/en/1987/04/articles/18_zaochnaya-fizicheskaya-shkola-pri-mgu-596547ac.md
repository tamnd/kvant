Correspondence Physics School at Moscow State University

Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov announces admission of students to grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for the entrance exams in physics to higher education. Upon admission to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University, a certificate of completion of 3FS is given to the admissions committee on the property.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of solving the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 1 to the address: 119899, ​​Moscow, GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3FSh.

Introductory task

Those entering the 9th grade of 3FS need to solve problems 1-4, and those entering the 10th grade need to solve problems 4-7.

1. "Accident". A body fell out of the gondola of a balloon rising upward at a constant speed $v_0=10$ m/s. At what height will the ball be at the moment when the body reaches the ground, if at the moment the body emerges the ball is at a height of $H=240$ m? Air resistance claim. Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s².

2. "Bubble" In a vessel filled with water of density $q$, an air bubble of volume $V$ emerges with acceleration $a$. Find the force of pressure exerted by the vessel on the support. The mass of the vessel together with water is $M$.

3. "Return". The material point begins to move in a straight line with constant acceleration. After time $T$ after the start of movement, the sign of acceleration changes to the opposite, but its magnitude remains unchanged. Determine how long after the start of movement $t$ the point will return to its original position?

4. "Metal Treaty" On a trihedral prim, the left side of which forms an angle $\alpha<45^\circ$ with the horizon, and the upper corner is a straight line, metal balls fall in a uniform stream from the same height. In which direction will the primo move? Considering only one hit of each ball. Consider the impacts to be elastic. Let's make a claim.

5. "Meeting". Two points move in a straight line towards each other with initial velocities $v_1$ and $v_2$ and constant accelerations $a_1$ and $a_2$, directed opposite to the corresponding velocities at a given point in time. At what maximum initial distance will the points meet during the movement?

6. "Charges in the field." In a uniform electron field there is a negative charged body with mass $m$ and charge $-q$ and a positive charged body with mass $M$ and charge $+Q$. Charges, more precisely, the line connecting them coincides with the direction of the field, the direction from each other, and in what order should the charges be located in relation to the direction of the field so that they can move with the same acceleration?

7. "Roll". The paper roll unwinds so that the speed of the end of the paper tape is constant and equal to $v$. At the initial moment the radius of the roll is $R$. What is the angular velocity of rotation of the roller at time $t$? The thickness of the paper tape is $d$.
