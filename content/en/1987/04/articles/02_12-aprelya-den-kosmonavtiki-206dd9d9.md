April 12 — Cosmonautics Day

Time moves inexorably forward, moving us away from that memorable day when the first inhabitant of the Earth rose into space. Yuri Gagarin's death was not only his personal feat. This was a feat of many Soviet people who calculated, designed, built and tested the Vostok-1 spacecraft and prepared the cosmonaut for the flight. Since then, our country has successfully continued its work on the exploration and use of outer space for peaceful purposes.

On February 20, 1986, the Mir orbital research station was launched into space for the first time. It is based on six docking nodes and represents the basic block for creating a multi-purpose raft complex with special orbital models - a real orbital scientific and technical institute. Now cosmonauts Yu. Romanenko and A. Laveikin worked at the Mir station.

An outstanding success of Soviet cosmonautics is the automatic exploration of Halley's comet by the Bega-1 and Bega-2 stations. The Soviet space orbital observatory "Astron", founded by RENTen's telephone, at the end of February 1987 investigated a unique celestial phenomenon - a supernova explosion in our neighboring galaxy - the Large Magellanic Cloud. And ahead - new projects.

In the summer of 1988, it is planned to launch two spacecraft designed to explore Mars and its satellite Phobos. Preparations are underway for the flight of joint space crews with the participation of Bulgarian, Syrian and French space scientists.

The Soviet Union is actively expanding the scope of international cooperation in space. The Bega stations used equipment developed by scientists from Bulgaria, Hungary, East Germany, Poland, Czechoslovakia, Australia, France and Germany.

We are active opponents of the billionization of space. Therefore, we insist on the elimination of the American Star Wars program, which threatens the very existence of man. And in this struggle we are supported by the greater stemli.
