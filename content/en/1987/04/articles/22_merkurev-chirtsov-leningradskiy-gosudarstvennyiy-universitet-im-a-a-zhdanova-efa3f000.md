⟦folio 52⟧

Leningrad State University named after. A. A. Zhdanava

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculties: mathematics and mechanics, physics, applied mathematics - control processes)

1. Two workers together produce 136 parts in 8 hours. If the first worker made 2 fewer parts per hour, and the second one more, then the second worker would spend 4 minutes less on the production of one part than the first. How many parts per hour of production of the first worker?

2. Solve the inequality $3x + \sqrt{5x + 9} < -5$.

3. For which $a$ is the greater of two numbers $5a - 1$ and $|2a|$ equal to the square of the lesser?

4.1 (for the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics). The distance from the base of the height dropped from the vertex $A$ of the triangle $ABC$ to the vertex $B$ is half that of the vertex $C$. Find the angle $A$ if you know that $\frac{AC}{AB} = a$.

4.2 (for the physics department). On side $AB$ of length $c$ of triangle $ABC$ point $D$ is selected. Through the point $D$ two lines are drawn parallel to the corresponding $AC$ and $BC$ and intersecting the side $AC$ at the point $F$ and the side $BC$ at the point $E$. Find the angle $BD$ if you know that $AC = b$, $BC = a$, $\frac{BE}{AF} = a$.

4.3 (for the Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes). A circle is inscribed in a right triangle. The point of tangency with the hypotenuse divides the hypotenuse in the ratio $a$. In what ratio do the points of contact divide the legs?

5. A ball of radius $R$ is inscribed in a triangular paramid, at the base of which lies an equilateral triangle with angle $a$ at the base. The lateral faces of the paramid are inclined to the plane of the base at an angle of $\beta$. Find the volume of the paramid.

Option 2
(chemistry department)

1. If 3 kg of substance $A$ is added to a mixture of two substances $A$ and $B$, then its percentage will double. If you add 3 kg of substance $B$ to the original mixture, then the percentage of substance $A$ will be halved. Determine the weight of $A$ and its percentage in the mixture.

2. Solve the inequality $x < \sqrt{\frac{5x + 2}{3}}$.

3. Solve the equation

$$\cos x + \sin x = \frac{0,5 + \sqrt{\frac{3}{8}}}{\cos x}.$$

4. Find the ratio of the radii of the inscribed and circumscribed circle of an equilateral triangle with angle $a$ at the base.

5. Find the cross-sectional area of ​​a regular triangular paramid passing through the vertex of the paramid and the midpoints of the two sides of the bases, if it is known that the length of the side of the base is $a$, and the angle between the section plane and the base is $a$.

Option 3
(faculties of geodratics and geology)

1. A cyclist left the city $A$ to the city $B$, the distance between the measles is 100 km. After this, a rider came out of $A$, who, naturally, moved back at the same speed and returned to $A$ at the moment in which the first access $B$. What is the speed of the first cyclist if the speed of the second is 30 km/h?

2.1 (for the Faculty of Geodratics). Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} 4x + 2y = 12, \\ \sqrt{3x - 2y} = \sqrt{5 + x - 3y}. \end{cases}$$

2.2 (for the Faculty of Geography). Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{x^2 - 7x} - \sqrt{x - 3} = 0.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{-\frac{3}{2}} \cos 2x = \sin x.$$

4. Solve the inequality
$$(\log_{x+1}x) \cdot \log_{\frac{1}{x}}(x - 2) > 1.$$

5. Two circles of radii $r_1$ and $r_2$ ($r_1 > r_2$) are given on the plane. Find the distance between the centers of the circles if it is known that it is $k$ times greater than the distance between the points of tangency of the common external tangent.

Option 4
(Library of Behavior Faculty, Department of Mathematical License of the Faculty of Physics)

1. From city $A$ to city $B$, the distance between measles is 270 km, leaving the traveler. He covered the first part of the 120 km long journey by car, and the second by motorcycle. He drove the rest of the way by car in 1 hour. The speed of a motorcycle is 15 km/h less than the speed of a car. Find the speed of the car if it is known that the average speed of the traveler is 54 km/h.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2x + 1} + \sqrt{6x + 4} = 1.$$

3.1 (for the department of science and mathematics license). Solve the equation
$$\sin x + 2 \sin^3 x + \sqrt{2} \sin 2x = 0.$$

3.2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1 - \sin x}{\sin 2x} \leq \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$$

4.1 (for the department of supervision). A right triangle has an area of ​​24 cm and a circle. The tangency point with the hypotenuse divides the hypotenuse in a ratio of 2:3. Find the lengths of the sides of the triangle.
