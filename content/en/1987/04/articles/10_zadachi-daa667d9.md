⟦folio 31⟧

Tasks

1. Replace the asterisks with figure numbers so that the equality is true.
2. In 1987, the age of the older of the two brothers became equal to the sum of the digits of the younger brother's year of birth, and the age of the younger - the sum of the digits of the older brother's year of birth. How old are they if one is 7 years older than the other?
3. One day Petya tried to connect a light bulb to two batteries as shown in figure. However, the light bulb did not light up, although it did light up from each of the batteries. Why?
4. The bus driver installed reels of tickets with numbers from 537000 to 537999 in one ticket office, and in the other with numbers from 462000 to 462999. Which of the reels contains more “lucky” tickets (i.e., those such that the sum of the first three digits is equal to the sum of the next three digits)?
5. A segment connecting the midpoints of two opposite sides of a convex quadrilateral divides it into two quadrilaterals having equal areas. Prove that these sides are parallel.
These tasks were suggested to us by a 7th grade student from Murom Olya Zevaleva, a 10th grade student from the Pushkin district of the AzSSR Natich Shirinov, S.V. Dvoryaninov, A.G. Samosvat, V.V. Proizvolov.
