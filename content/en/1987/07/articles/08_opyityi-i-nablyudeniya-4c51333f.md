Experiments and observations

In this summer issue of “Kaleidoscon”, we are temporarily absent from the tradition of discussing basic physical concepts and present you with some experiments. To do this, you can use literally everything that is at hand, or carefully observe the phenomena around you. You will see that behind many events that are very simple at first glance, there is a completely serious physics that requires thought and ingenuity. For those who want to correctly believe their conclusions not only experimentally, but also theoretically, we advise you to look into the next issue of the journal.
