Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk Order of Labor Red Replacement State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol operates a correspondence school (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 in general education schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students' interest in the natural sciences.

There are 5 departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic. Mathematics, physics and chemistry departments accept students in grades 8-10, biology departments only accept students in grades 9, and economics departments accept only students in grades 10.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also accommodate mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives that work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. The head of the circles recruits and enrolls students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the relevant subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader informs 3SH of his last name, first name, patronymic and sends a posted list of clubs (indicating the total grades for the first assignment), signed by the school director and certified by a seal. After this, members of the krug are considered students of 3Sh.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional materials. The work of correspondence students is checked in 3SH, and the work of circle members is checked by its leaders (at the request of the leader, some of the work of circle members can be checked in 3SH).

To become a student at the Correspondence School at NSU, you must send an application to the director of 3Sh, written on a postcard, with a request to send the first assignment. The deadline for sending the application is no later than September 20 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). The application must also provide the following information about yourself:

Your last name, first name, patronymic (in full, block letters)
The class in which you are a teacher in your school
Department 3Ш, where you will declare a teacher (you can indicate two)
Detailed home address with mandatory indication of post office code

The head of the circle must send a letter to the director of 3Sh with a request to send the first assignment and additional materials for it.

Enrollment in 3Sh is made on the envelope when they answer.

First (introductory) math assignment

8th grade

1. In some books, only 1422 digits were needed to number the pages. How many pages are there in the book?

2. Two opposite sides of the rectangle were lengthened by 20%, and the other two were lengthened by 20%. How did the area of ​​the rectangle change as a result?

3. Prove that any odd number and half of the next even number are coprime numbers.

4. Draw on the coordinate plane a set of points whose coordinates (x; y) satisfy the conditions

$$|2x - y| > x + 1.$$

5. How to use a compass and ruler to construct a trapezoid based on the bases and corners?

9th grade

1. A grasshopper can look exactly 0.5 m in any direction. Can he move exactly 7.3 m in a few steps?

2. Are there any integers that are reduced by a factor of 57 when their first (left) digit is crossed out?

3. The lengths of the legs of a primagonal triangle are equal to a and b. A square was built on its hypotenuse on the outer side. Find the distance from the center of the square to the top of the rectangle.

4. Prove that the number $n^p$, where n and p are natural numbers, p > 1, is the sum of n consecutive odd numbers.

5. Solve the equation

$$\frac{x^2}{4} + 1 - \frac{4}{x} = 0.$$

6. A circle with center at point O is inscribed in an angle and touches its sides at points A and B.

10th grade

1. On the coordinate plane, draw a set of points whose coordinates $(x; y)$ satisfy the conditions
$$|y - 1| \geqslant y - |x| + x.$$

6. Find the sum of the squares of the three real roots of the equation $x^3 - 6x^2 + 8x + 0,1 = 0$ (the roots of the equation are not found).
