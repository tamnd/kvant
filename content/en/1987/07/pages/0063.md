Young programs in Leningrad

In mid-March, spring in Leningrad was just coming into its own. The bright sun was already a reminder of the approaching school holidays. The programmer, who had gathered these days at the Palace of Culture of Education Workers in Leningrad, clearly had no time for the holidays. Everyone was in a very working mood. A scientific and technical conference on school information was held here. For the sixth year in a row, schoolchildren and students, students of vocational schools, teachers and visa teachers gather for such conferences. Guys from Tallinn and Leningrad, Moscow and Shchoshkar-Ola, Novosibirsk and Ulyanovsk, Sverdlovsk and Vladivostok, Tbilisi and Vilnius spoke about their work...

The fact that such representative conferences of young programmers are held in Leningrad is, of course, no coincidence. Back in 1979, Leningraders took an active part in the work of the All-Union Correspondence School for Young Programmers, organized by the magazine «Kvant» together with the Computer Center of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences. At the same time, a full-time school for young programmers appeared in Leningrad. In 1986, already 45% of Leningrad schoolchildren received access to a computer. Leningrad enterprises also provided great assistance to schools in this regard,

providing schoolchildren with the opportunity to work on their own computers. Many programs of Leningrad schoolchildren are included in the school educational process. And Leningrad enterprises order and pay for configuration development.

Experience filled with school information in different cities of the country shows that children can do a lot. They create meaningful and interesting packages of educational programs for courses in physics, mathematics, Russian and foreign languages, chemistry, music, etc., successfully “competing” with professional developments of such programs.

The organizing committee of the conference was headed by Academician A.P. Ershov and State Prize laureate Professor M.V. Ignatev. In total, about three hundred reports were presented at the conference. The conference jury, which evaluated the reports, awarded 68 works with special diplomas. The jury included Leningrad scientists and the organizers of the conference - representatives of the International Committee of the Leningrad Public Scientific and Technical Societies for Work with Youth, the board of the Scientific and Technical Society of Radio Engineering, Electronics and Communications named after. A. S. Popov, the Leningrad Institute of Aviation Acquisition, the Main Directorate of Public Education of Leningrad and other organizations.

Academician A.P. Ershov, scientists and methods from various scientific centers of universities spoke not only about successes, but also about the problems and prospects for the widespread introduction of computer technology into schools. The conference participants had the opportunity to demonstrate their developments and programs directly on computers installed in the halls of the Palace of Culture and in the School Information Center of the Oktyabrsky District of Leningrad located nearby.

The main work took place in the sections: “Training programs”, “System software”, “Application software”, “Information systems”, “Game programs”, “Target games”. At each of them there was something to feel and something to enjoy. And, alas, there was a very tight time crunch - the conference lasted only two days. And there is certainly a need to carry out and expand such work; it is very useful for both schoolchildren and teachers of the fundamentals of information and computer technology. Please, it is not an exaggeration to say that the exchange of experience and personal contacts are an essential factor in the successful development of school information.

Guests of Leningrad visited museums and schools of the city, together with Leningrad schoolchildren they visited historical places and theaters of Leningrad. We parted with sadness, of course. See you next year. Good luck to all of you creatively, guys!

G. N. Brovina

Studying in the computer room at the School Information Center.
