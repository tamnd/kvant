⟦folio 43⟧

Tasks

1. Uncle Alyosha is twice my age, and the digits of the number expressing my age are equal to the sum and difference of the digit of his age. How old am I?

2. The doors in the electrical salon move on rollers along guides located not horizontally, but obliquely. Why?

3. During the New Year's carnival, Anton ran into his class and saw an example left on the board for raising a number to the fifth power. He erased three digits from the result and got * * 198* * 7. What number was restored to the fifth power?

4. You can lace sports shoes as shown in figure in many ways due to the differences in lacing studied. How many such ways are there?

5. One day on the stairs I found a page terdal. It contained the following statements:
“There is one incorrect statement in this statement.”
“There are two false statements in this statement.”
“In this case, these statements are incorrect.”
Which of these statements is true?
