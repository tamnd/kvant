M1052. Prove that out of n quadrilaterals cut off from a convex n-gon by diagonals, no more than n/2 can be circumscribed circles. Give an example of an octagon that has 4 such quadrilaterals.

$$AB + CD = BC + AD, \quad BC + DE = CD + BE.$$

$$AB + DE = AD + BE.$$ (*)
