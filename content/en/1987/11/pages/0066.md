⟦folio 64⟧

Attention
our readers

Signatures are accepted for a fundamental scientific reference publication in the fifth volume of the “Physical Encyclopedia” (Soviet Encyclopedia Publishing House).

The encyclopedia is intended for researchers, engineers, teachers and graduate students working in various fields of physics; may be useful for senior students of physics and engineering-physics faculties of universities and colleges, high school physics teachers, astronomers, chemists, biology, and mathematicians will also be interested in it. 6000 articles arranged in alphabetical order contain reviews on individual sections of physics, materials on the most important physical theories, physical laws, phenomena and concepts, main objects of research in physics, research methods, the most important installations, instruments, units of physical quantities.

The editor-in-chief is one of the founders of quantum electronics, laureate of the Lenin, State and Nobel Prizes, twice Hero of Socialist Labor, Academician A. M. Prozhorov.
The first volume will be published in 1988.
Estimated cost of the volume is 7 rubles. 80 kop.

Subscriptions are accepted by bookstores that distribute the signature publication.
Upon subscription, a deposit of 5 rubles is made, which is credited upon receipt of the last volume.
