The mathematical problem also became of great importance in connection with ensuring high quality of mass industrial products. Individual quality control of each manufactured product required a huge amount of control. It was impossible to do this, since there was already a catastrophic shortage of labor. It was at this time that the idea of ​​constructing a theory of statistical product quality control arose. A. N. Kolmogorov took an active part in its creation. Currently, this theory is undergoing vigorous development along two paths: 1) checking the quality of already manufactured batches (acceptance control) and 2) managing the quality of the product during the manufacturing process (technical or operational control).

But this is not enough. It turned out that mathematical methods assist archeologists and historians, linguists and physicians. New directions of mathematical thought appeared. Mathematical logic acquired incomparably greater importance than before the war.
