8. Let $n$ be a natural number. Consider the polygon $T_p(n) \subset T_p$ and consists of all numbers less than $2^n$!. Thus, the set $T_0(n)$ consists of $n$ numbers $2^0$!, $(2^1)!$, ..., $(2^{n-1})!$, and the set $T_1(n)$ consists of numbers of the form
$$a_0(2^0)! + a_1(2^1)! + \dots + a_{n-1}(2^{n-1})!$$,
where $a_i \in \{0; 1\}$.

Let further $N_p(n)$ be the number of numbers in $T_p(n)$. We must prove that $N_{1987}(n) < (2^n)! - 1$.

By forming sums of numbers from polygon $T_p-1(n)$ and bringing similar terms at $(2^i)!$, we can write any number from $T_p(n)$ as $\beta_0(2^0)! + \beta_1(2^1)! + \dots + \beta_{n-1}(2^{n-1})!$. Let $A_p(n) - 1$ be the maximum value of the coefficients among all numbers from $T_p(n)$. Then for any $(2^i)!$ the coefficient of $(2^i)!$ takes at most $A_p(n)$ different values. Therefore $N_p(n) \leq (A_p(n))^n$.
