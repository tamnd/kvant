XXI National Olympiad in Mathematics

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences V. V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences S. V. REZNICHENKO,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. M. SLINKO

The final stage of the XXI All-Union Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in April 1987 in Frunze. 29 teams took part in it: 14 teams from joint resource groups, teams from four zones of the RSFSR, Moscow and Leningrad, as well as 8 teams from specialized physics and mathematics schools and the Frunze team. A total of 162 schoolchildren took part in the Olympiad: 3 seventh-graders, 43 eighth-graders, 58 ninth-graders and 58 tenth-graders (the seventh-graders solved the problems proposed for the eighth-graders).

The grand opening of the Olympiad took place on April 12 in the film building of the V.I. Lenin Museum, and back before it, the Olympiad participants laid flowers at the monument to V.I. Lenin. The Minister of Education of the Kyrgyz SSR M.V. Bazarkulov, the head of the department of school, science and educational institutions of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Kyrgyzstan A.A. Akayev, the senior methodologist of the Scientific Methodological Council of the Minipros of the SSR T.A. Sarycheva, the head of the department of school youth of the Central Committee of the Komsomol Kyrgyzstan

L. M. Skudina, chairman of the jury, academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR V. V. Gnedenko, deputy. Deputy Chairman of the Jury Director of the Institute of Mathematics of the Academy of Sciences of Kyrgyzstan, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. N. Botashev and others. The Minister of Education of the USSR S.G. Sherbakov addressed the participants of the Olympiad with a written greeting.

From the first minutes of their stay in the hospitable land of Kyrgyzstan and throughout the entire Olympiad, the participants were surrounded by the warmth and attention of the households that turned the Olympiad into a real celebration of young mathematicians. The program of the Olympiad included the traditional evening of friendship between the peoples of the USSR, a meeting with scientists and resource workers at leading enterprises, visits to theaters and excursions. Participants will remember for a long time the excursion to the Ala-Archa Gorge, where the All-Mountain Mountaineer is located.

The final stage of the Olympiad was traditionally held in two rounds, held on April 13 and 14. At each of them, the student was given four problems, which were given five hours to solve.
