GEOMETRIC TOPICS

Many chess problems have a geometric basis; everything seems to be built on the intersection of lines.

Бристольская тема (тема прокладки пути). This topic was opened by the English Hilly problem and will consist of clearing the line.

F. Hilly, 1861
Checkmate in 3 moves.
1. Rh1! Черные в цугшанге, конь неподвижен из-за 2. Фd6×, на любой ход слона следует 2. Фb1! и, поскольку ладья заблаговременно уступила место ферзю, 3. Фg1×.
Anti-Bristol theme. This time the further figure blocks the path of another figure.

P. Copping, 1961
Checkmate in 3 moves.
1. Qe2! with the threat of 2. Qf3+ Ke4 3. Q:e4×. Black is forced to attract his queen: 1...Qc3 2. N:b7, 1...Qd4 2. Kxc7, 1...Qe5 2. Nf7 - each time with the inevitable checkmate 3. Nb6× or 3. Ke7×. From both checkmates in the initial position he launched 2...Cf6, now with his queen.

A valve (a topic closer to physics) is formed by two black figures, one of which, opening the line of action of the second, simultaneously blocks it along the other line. If one of the figures, opening the line of action of the second, simultaneously blocks the third, then this is a double valve.

L. Isaev, S. Levman, 1928
Checkmate in 2 moves.
After 1. Ng5, defending against the threat of 2. Ke6×, the black knight on d5 must retreat (1...fg 2. C:e5×), opening the way for his queen. In three variations, the knight covers the queen along other lines: 1...Kb4 2. Kb5×, 1...Kc3 2. Jd3×, 1...Ke3 2. Kf3× - a simple valve; in three other variations the path to other pieces is blocked: 1...Ke7 2. Cc5×, 1...Kc7 2. Qc5×, 1...Kf4 2. Qe4× - double valve.

The horse wheel is a mechanism for unwinding the game of one of the knights, located in the central part of the board.

K. Mansfield, 1958
Checkmate in 2 moves.
There are eight possibilities within the white knight's range, but seven of them are subtly refuted by the black queen's moves: 1. Kf1 Qa2! 1. Kd1 Qc8! 1. Kc2 Фa1!, 1. Kc4 Фa5!, 1. Kd5 Фe8!, 1. Kf5 Фd8! and 1. Kg4 Qb8! The most strンツod decides 1. Kg2! with inevitable swearing. Horse theme

A cross is a mechanism for playing a king, queen or rook four times at equal distances horizontally and vertically, an asterisk is a similar game along diagonals.

V. Rudenko, 1980
Checkmate in 3 moves.
1. Kd5! with the threat of 2. Nb6+ Ke8 3. Rf6×. 1...Cd6 2. Rl:c5+ Ke6 3. N:g5×, 1...Cd8+ 2. Rb6+ Ke8 3. Kd6×, 1...Cf8 2. Rd6+ Ke8 3. Rd8× and 1...Cf6 2. Rc7+ Ke6 3. Kf4×. Rook cross in response to the bishop star!

The focal theme is the elimination of control of the black queen (rook) over one of the two squares he launched along different lines, called focal.

V. Holtzhausen, 1909
Checkmate in 3 moves.
The white knight is ready to declare mate with b7 or b3. 1. Bc4 is refuted by 1...Qf3! — the queen again takes aim at both focal fields. White tentatively plays 1. c4! and only after 1...dc (1...Qf3 2. Cd3!) carry out the main idea 2. Bc4!
