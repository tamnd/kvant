I All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in computer science

In 1988, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, together with the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Ministry of Higher and Secondary Special Education of the USSR, the USSR State Committee on Computer Science and Informatics, the USSR Academy of Sciences and the Weighty Union Society "Knowledge" held the Weighty Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in informatics.

The All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics, physics and chemistry in 1988 will be held for the 22nd time, the forms of its implementation and the applied topics of stable problems. The Computer Science Olympiad is being held for the first time. Therefore, when organizing it, it is advisable to use forms processed in modern ones in other subjects (as far as possible), and the topics of problems processed in various modern schoolchildren in computer science.

In those union resources where appropriate conditions exist, the district (city) and regional stages of the Olympiad can be held. Pupils of all ages, creative basic school computer science courses, are allowed to progress.

Republican Olympiads will be held on Matrovsky holidays. Packages of a given recommended nature for the Olympiads of the resources stage will be compiled by the methodological commission on computer science of the Central Organizing Committee. This commission will carry out functions similar to the analytical functions of the subject methodological commissions of the Central Organizing Committee.

Based on the results of the Resursikan Olympiad, a team of schoolchildren is formed for the Final stage of the Olympiad in fiction.

The final stage of the Olympiad will be held in Sverdlovsk from April 15 to April 22, 1988. The choice of Sverdlovsk as the venue for the Final stage is not accidental - there are united by the missions of local party and Soviet organizations, the regional and city public education authorities, the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics of the Ural Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences and a machine version has been implemented in all school cities (in the amount of 102 h) course on the fundamentals of information science and computer technology. This became possible thanks to the energy and vigor of many people, first of all, the member of the Presidium of the Ural Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician N. N. Krasovsky and the head of the Department of Information Science and Computer Science of the Sverdlovsk Pedagogical Institute V. G. Zhitomirsky.

The final stage of the Olympiad, in which there are participating teams from each union resource and the cities of Moscow, Leningrad and Sverdlovsk (as the host city of the Olympics), will be held in two rounds.

In the first (theoretical) round, 2-3 problems will be proposed, the solution of which does not involve the use of computer technology. In the second (machine) round, two tasks will be offered, for which the use of computers will be ensured.

In conclusion, we present the problems proposed at the 1st International Programming Olympiad, which took place in the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic in August 1987 (Soviet schoolchildren also participated in it; this Olympiad will be discussed in detail in the journal “Informatics and Education.”)
