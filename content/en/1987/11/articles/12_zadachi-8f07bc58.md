⟦folio 31⟧

Tasks

1. After the end of the chess tour in one round (each played against each other once), all five of its participants A, B, C, D, D, listed here in the order of their places, exchanged impressions.
   “I didn’t think that I would be the only one who wouldn’t experience the bitterness of defeat,” said V.
   “But I’m the only one who didn’t manage to win a single victory,” D. said darkly.
   Try to reconstruct the standings table using this data: how everyone played with the other participants.
2. Why does the snow squeak underfoot on a frosty day?
3. Decipher the number puzzle GRANDFATHER + GRANDMOTHER + TURNIP =
   =FAIRY TALE,
   in which identical letters correspond to identical numbers, different ones correspond to different ones, and GRANDFATHER>GRANDMOTHER>TURNIP.
4. From a set of weights with masses of 1 g, 2 g, ..., 101 g, are 100 weights consumed divided into two piles of 50 pieces each so that the masses of the piles are the same?
5. In an equilateral triangle, the midpoints of the sides and their projections onto the base are offset. Two straight lines are drawn through the offset points (see figure). Show that the resulting pieces can be used to form a rhombus.

These tasks were offered to us
S. G. Guba, A. P. Savin, A. M. Domashenko,
V. V. Proizvolov, V. D. Vyon
