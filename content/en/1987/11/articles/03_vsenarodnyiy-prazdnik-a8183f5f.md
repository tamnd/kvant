National holiday

“Komsomol members! Boys and girls!
Seize the legacy of the great Lenin!
Learn to work highly productively, creatively, actively!”
From the confessions of the CPSU Central Committee on the 70th anniversary of the Great October Socialist Revolution

70 years have passed since the day when all power in our country passed into the hands of the people. it is difficult to imagine more difficult conditions than those in which the construction of a new society began. Destruction, hunger, illiteracy, hostile environment... But Lenin and the Bolshevik party managed to rally the people for a heroic struggle. The Soviet Union, the world's first socialized country, became a powerful industrial power.

Together with the whole country, our science developed and strengthened. And if, in the words of the Soviet physicist Academician L.A. Artsimovich, “all the major physicists of Tsarist Russia could easily be seated on one sofa,” then today we have more than a hundred physical research centers alone. Soviet physicists, mathematicians, astronomers and representatives of other natural sciences enjoy international recognition. Physics and mathematics are the leaders of the scientific and technological revolution, and our scientists have made a fundamental contribution to the development of these sciences. The Soviet school played a major role in the construction of socialism.

The anniversary, as V.I. Lenin taught, is an occasion for critical analysis has passed. Conducting such an analysis, we see that the socialism built in our country is still imperfect, it has huge reserves that need to be fully revealed and implemented in a short time. Young people sometimes think that the time of revolutionary transformation is long gone. But life refutes these ideas. The revolution continues. It is precisely this that requires from us enormous acceleration and gradual restructuring in all spheres of activity. A stubborn struggle lies ahead, and you will have to participate in it during your school years.

Each of you, dear readers, needs to once again carefully study the materials of the XXVII Congress of the CPSU and the plenums of the CPSU Central Committee, the documents of the XX Congress of the Komsomol, and thoughtfully re-read Lenin’s works created after the victory of the revolution. How many substitute thoughts are there in V.I. Lenin’s speech alone at the III All-Russian Komsomol Congress! And not all of them are questions in life. But Lenin’s works are a true compass in the complex and long process of comprehensive transformations that await you both at school and beyond its threshold.
