The solution to problem M1038, published in this issue of the journal, states that any rectangle $6k \times 2n$, where $k \geq 1$, $n \geq 3$, can be divided into “knots” of three cells so that the “knots” do not form a single rectangle smaller than the size.
The solution states that in this case there are necessarily centrally symmetric partitions - one of them is shown on figure 1. This partition of the square $12 \times 12$ is also interesting because when repeated on $90^\circ$ the cyclic menjiorities of the colors of the “knots”: green turns into red, red into yellow, yellow into blue, blue into red.
figure 2 shows the evolution of the “knot”, after which figure 1 turns into the ring mozan shown in figure 3.
