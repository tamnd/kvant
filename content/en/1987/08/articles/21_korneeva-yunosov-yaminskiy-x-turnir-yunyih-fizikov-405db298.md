Tasks for the correspondence collective competition TUF-X

1. Come up with it yourself. Propose original projects for the technical and scientific use of high-temperature superconductivity.

2. «Вечирпемник». Design and manufacture a portable radio receiver that does not use power sources. Test parameter: $x=P/Lm$, where $P$ is the sound pressure at a distance of 1 m from the receiver, $L$ is the maximum linear size of the receiver, $m$ is the mass of the receiver.

3. Camera obscura. Take a group portrait of your team using the cover camera. Justify the physical principles of obtaining a high-quality signal using such a device.

4. Электрическая цепь. Several nodes ($n<10$) are connected to each other by batteries with known emf and $r$. Create a computer calculation program to determine the potential difference between the first and second nodes. The criterion for the quality of the program is the time from the start of data entry into the computer (tables of EMF values ​​and $r$) until the correct result is issued.

5. Metrology. Determine the maximum accuracy of measuring length with a steel ruler.

6. Vacuum seller. An astronaut entrepreneur decided to supply vacuum from outer space to physical laboratories around the world. What are the chances of his venture being successful?

7. Sun at sunset. The visible disk of the Sun at sunset is solid. Experimentally modify and characterize these distortions. Calculate the theoretical ratio of the horizontal and vertical dimensions of the solar disk, tangent linearly to the horizon.

8. Color television. You need to design a four-color TV. What flowers would you choose as your main flowers? Should the lighting equipment change in this case?

9. The ninth wave.

Tips for a Turin debutant

How to solve a problem for a tournament player. The conditions of the tournament often confuse the debutant. And it’s not even clear to him what, in fact, to decide, and it’s not at all clear how to decide! In this case, you need to do as physicists do. The problem must be used as much as possible, narrowed, limited within the framework of simple and clear assumptions, and posed a clear and clear question that can be answered. Then it comes to getting a high-quality answer - similarity messages, the dimensional method and your intuition will help you here. But it is impossible to limit these. The decision must be strict and complete. Feel free to experiment. Remember that almost all tournament tasks allow for experimental research. We warn you against being overly enthusiastic about the apparatus of higher mathematics. Turir does not pursue such goals. First of all, it is the qualitative side and the physical essence of the problem that must be illuminated.

How can we work collectively? To do this, organize a meeting (meeting). The first meeting is organizational. Invite everyone who is interested in the tournament. Here, read the tasks of the tournament, and let those who wish to choose tasks to solve. It doesn’t matter if some problems want to be solved a lot, but some are not covered. The main content of the first meeting is the beginning of work. It is hardly worthwhile to immediately find a captain and discuss the composition of the team, but a “coordinator” of the meetings is absolutely necessary. He must know all the states of affairs: when the status of the next meeting is, what tasks will be discussed, what has already been solved and by whom, what is in the process of being resolved, what has not yet been solved at all, etc.

The next meetings are business meetings. They are used to solve the problem and, after critical comments and clarification, are accepted or sent for development. If a decision is made, it is advisable not to put it off until tomorrow, finalize it and put it in the folder of ready-made decisions. At such meetings, creative teams are formed and the composition of the team is gradually determined. You will soon find out that one participant in the meeting can solve the problem, others are strong in organizational terms, others do not solve the problem, but put forward fresh and sound ideas, and some are strong with their critical remarks or are simply able to create an atmosphere of empirical uplift and creative intensity. They all have their place and are needed by the team. In mid-October, you will need to check what has been done - let the tutor know. If it is okay that some of the problems have not been solved or have not been solved at all, then now they need to be distributed among work groups and set deadlines in order to develop a joint solution to these problems (perhaps in a simplified form - the rules of the tournament allow this).

At the last meeting it is necessary to form a team and choose a captain.

Who is your assistant? First of all, a student of physics. They will not solve problems for you, but they can advise or criticize your decisions. In organizational terms, their help is absolutely necessary. He must know about the state of affairs: when the status of the next meeting is, what tasks will be discussed, what has already been solved and by whom, what is in the process of being resolved, what has not yet been solved at all, etc.

Parents can help. From them you can use detration approaches, help in what school cannot give you now. This mostly applies to your relatives and friends.

Students who are alumni of your school can help. Intelligent interaction with them will bring you many benefits. However, finding and promoting them should increase your independent and active work on solving the problem. If you managed to “find” a solution to a problem without solving it, then the value of such a “feat” is not a statement.

Finally, the book is your main assistant. Many of the problems raised in the TUF tasks may, to some extent, have already been resolved. Creative research has already obtained results (do not forget to provide a link to the source). The book can suggest a lot and teach a lot.

We wish you good luck and creative success.
T. P. Korneeva, E. N. Yunosov, N. V. Aminsky
