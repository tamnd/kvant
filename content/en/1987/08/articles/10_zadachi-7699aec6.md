⟦folio 37⟧

Quests

1. Let $A$ and $B$ be two different digits, the digit $A$ is not equal to zero. We will consider numbers made up of these numbers. Show that the correspondence $\overline{AB} \cdot \overline{AAA} = \overline{ABA} \cdot \overline{AA}$ does not hold for any $A$ and $B$, the correspondence $\overline{AB} \cdot \overline{AAA} = \overline{ABA} \cdot \overline{AA} + 1$ does not hold for only one pair of numbers $A$ and $B$, and the correspondence $\overline{AB} \cdot \overline{AAAA} = \overline{ABAB} \cdot \overline{AA}$ always holds.

2. Try to arrange all 28 dominoes into four piles so that the sums of the points in the piles are four consecutive prime numbers.

3. Solve the number puzzle (see figure). Identical letters must correspond to identical numbers, different ones must correspond to different ones.

4. The compass needle was fixed on a flat cork and the cork was lowered into the water. How will the traffic jam behave?

5. Substitute the multi-point numbers for figure together so that the sentence turns out to be correct.

These tasks were suggested to us by A. A. Azamov, A. M. Domashenko, A. G. Gein and F. L. Tolstov, S. S. Krotov, A. P. Savin.
