M1039. Points A, B, C, D are the vertices of the tetrahedron. Prove that a) if $\overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{DB} \times \overrightarrow{CA} = \overrightarrow{DC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$, then all three of these scalar products are equal to 0;
b) if three angles between opposite edges of a tetrahedron are equal, then they are straight.

a) For any four points A, B, C, D the identity is true
$$\overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{DB} \cdot \overrightarrow{CA} + \overrightarrow{DC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 0.$$ (1)

This becomes obvious if we represent its left side as $\bar{a} \cdot (\bar{c} - \bar{b}) + \bar{b} \cdot (\bar{a} - \bar{c}) + \bar{c} \cdot (\bar{b} - \bar{a})$, where $\bar{a} = \overrightarrow{DA}, \bar{b} = \overrightarrow{DB}, \bar{c} = \overrightarrow{DC}$. From (1) the statement of the problem immediately follows.

6) Let the angle between the opposite edges of the tetrahedron be equal to $\alpha$, then the angles between the vectors $\overrightarrow{DA}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}, \overrightarrow{DB}$ and $\overrightarrow{CA}, \overrightarrow{DC}$ and $\overrightarrow{AB}$ are equal to $\alpha$ or $180^\circ - \alpha$. Therefore (1) can be rewritten as follows:
$$\cos \alpha (\pm DA \cdot BC \pm DB \cdot CA \pm DC \cdot AB) = 0.$$
