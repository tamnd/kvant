Before studying the properties of this vortex system, let's try to answer the question: why does bleed form in warm water rotating at a constant speed?

Warm liquid, poured into an open jar, continuously evaporates. Due to evaporation, a thin cold film is continuously formed on the surface of the liquid, which has a higher density than the rest of the liquid. This cold water tends to sink down, and in a non-collapsing liquid such a descent is irregular and the water “flows down” in separate streams - “thermals”.
