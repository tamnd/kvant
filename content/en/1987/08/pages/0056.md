Evening physical school at Moscow State University

The Evening Physics School (VPS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces enrollment of students in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the next academic year.
The main goal of the VFS is to help students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum. Classes are conducted in the form of lectures, given once every two weeks,

and daily seminars. In addition, students can visit the faculty's scientific laboratories and get acquainted with the main directions of modern physics at lectures by leading scientists. Those who wish to organize elective classes in mathematics and basic information education receive a certificate of completion of the High School of Physics.

Admission to the VFS is based on the results of the owner, which will be held starting from September 23. To enroll in the school, you must personally fill out an application at the Komsomol Committee of Physics of Moscow State University (with the attachment of two factors measuring $3 \times 4$ cm). Applications can be submitted from September 7 to September 19 every day, except for restoration, from 16 to 18 hours. Working youth are enrolled without competition.

VFS address: 119899, ​​Moscow, GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, VFS. Phone: 939-26-56.
