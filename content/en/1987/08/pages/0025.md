⟦folio 23⟧

Tasks

M1056—M1060, F1068—F1072

M1056. Each cell of the square table 1987×1987 contains a number not exceeding modulo 1.
In any 2x2 square of this table, the sum of the numbers is 0. Prove that the sum of all the numbers in the table does not exceed 1987.

A. S. Merkuryev

M1057. Two players alternately write down natural numbers not exceeding p on the board. The rules of the game prohibit writing divisors of already written numbers on the board. The player who cannot make another move loses.
a) Find out which player has a winning strategy for p=10 and indicate it.
b) Find out which player has a winning strategy for p=1000.

D. V. Fomin

M1058. A non-empty set of nodes is marked on an integer lattice. In addition, a finite set of vectors with integer coordinates is given. It is known that if all given vectors are plotted from any marked node, then among their ends there will be more marked nodes than unmarked ones. Prove that there are infinitely many marked nodes.

D. G. Flaas

M1059. The graph of the function y=f(x), which defines the entire number line, transforms into itself when rotated by an angle π/2 around the origin.
a) Prove that the equation f(x)=x has exactly one solution.
b) Give an example of such a function.

A. V. Klyushin

M1060. There are two closed broken lines on the plane, each with an odd number of links. All straight lines containing links of these broken lines are different, and no three of them intersect at one point. Prove that from each broken line one can select one link so that they are opposite sides of some convex quadrilateral.

A. Serdyukov, D. G. Flaas

F1068. A projectile flying vertically breaks into three equal fragments at the top point of its trajectory. One of the shards, moving vertically, flew away after time T₁ after the shot, the other two flew away simultaneously after time T₂ (T₁<T₂). Find the height H at which the shell exploded.

Yu. G. Pavlenko

F1069. At an angle of magnitude α, according to a thin smooth rod, a light loop of length l with a small weight attached to it is put on; the angle is set vertically (Fig. 1). Find the distance from the load to the vertex of the angle in the equilibrium position and the period of small oscillations of the load in the plane of the angle.

I. I. Mazin
