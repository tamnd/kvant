EVAPORATION, BOILING, MELTING?

Are you familiar with the processes?

To these, of course, we should add the reverse processes—condensation and crystallization. Together they characterize certain changes in the state of matter. These states are very familiar to you: solid, liquid, gaseous. However, they do not exhaust the entire variety of possible states. Over time, you learned about plasma, about the neural state of matter, and how you can go to these states. For now, let's deal with not exotic, but ordinary transformations of matter, and it turns out that they are ordinary only at first glance.
