Let us first find the forces acting on the block, assuming that it moves together with the platform, i.e., according to the law $\vec{r}(t)=\vec{A}\sin 2\pi ft$, where the vector $\vec{A}$ is directed at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the vertical. According to Newton's second law

$$m\vec{g}+\vec{N}+\vec{F}=m\vec{a},$$

(1)

where $m\vec{g}$ is the gravity stack force, $\vec{N}$ is the normal reaction force of the platform, $\vec{F}$ is the static friction force acting on the block, directed horizontally (to the right or left), $\vec{a}=-\vec{A}\omega^2\sin \omega t$ ($\omega=2\pi f$). Let us write (1) in projections onto the horizontal x and vertical y axes (see Fig. 1):

$$F_x=m a_x=\frac{m A \omega^2}{\sqrt{2}}\sin \omega t,$$

(2)

$$N-m g=m a_y=-\frac{m A \omega^2}{\sqrt{2}}\sin \omega t.$$

(3)

Considering that the static friction force is $|F_x| \leq \mu|N|$, from (2) and (3) we obtain:

$$\left|\frac{m A \omega^2}{\sqrt{2}}\sin \omega t\right| \leq \left|\mu m g-\frac{\mu m A \omega^2}{\sqrt{2}}\sin \omega t\right|.$$

(4)

The right side of inequality (4) for $\sin \omega t<0$ is always greater than for $\sin \omega t>0$. Therefore, the minimum value of the vibration amplitude at which the block will crawl along the site should be sought at $\sin \omega t>0$.

The equal sign corresponds to the limiting situation when static friction is sufficient to impart to the block the same horizontal acceleration as the movement of the platform, and in this case (4) can be used as an equation to find the moment of time $t_1$ when the block creeps along the platform:

$$\sin \omega t_1=\frac{\mu g \sqrt{2}}{(\mu+1) A \omega^2}.$$

(5)

Such a moment $t_1$ exists if equation (5) has a solution. To do this, its right side must be less than one, i.e. $\mu g \sqrt{2}<(\mu+1) A \omega^2$, or

$$A>\frac{\mu g \sqrt{2}}{(\mu+1) \omega^2}.$$

(6)

Thus, the sliding of the block along the platform will occur when the amplitude $A$ of its oscillations satisfies inequality (6). A graphical solution to equation (5) is shown at figure 2. Starting from moment $t_1$, the block slides to the right relative to the platform. At time $t_2$, the friction force becomes able to provide the block with the same horizontal acceleration as that of the platform, but slipping continues for some time after $t_2$, since by time $t_2$ the speed of the block relative to the platform is not zero.
