⟦folio 25⟧

F1023. The light bulb is located at a distance $l_1$ from the shelf at a height $l_2$ from the floor. When it explodes, the debris scatters in all directions with the same speed $v$. Find the radius of the circle on the floor where the rocks will hit. Consider that hitting the rocks against the ceiling is abscilton urrusia, and hitting the floor is non-elastic; the shards do not reach the walls.

Let us first assume that there is no ceiling, and in this case we will find the maximum radius of the spot on the floor in which the salts lie.

Let us introduce a coordinate system with the origin at the point where the light bulb socket is located (see figure). The conditions for the fall to the ground after time $t$ of the salt, which has been released at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon, will be as follows:

$$\begin{cases}
-l_2 = vt \sin \alpha - \frac{gt^2}{2}, \\
R = vt \cos \alpha.
\end{cases}$$

Obviously, we need to select the angle $\alpha$ so that the value $R$ is maximum. Let us transform for this equation (*) to the form

$$\begin{cases}
\frac{gt}{2} - \frac{l_2}{t} = v \sin \alpha, \\
\frac{R}{t} = v \cos \alpha.
\end{cases}$$

Hence, for the quantity $R$ we obtain the inequality

$$R^2 = (v^2 + gl_2)t^2 - \frac{g^2 t^4}{4} - l_2^2 = \left(\frac{v^2 + gl_2}{g}\right)^2 - l_2^2 - \left(\frac{gt^2}{2} - \frac{v^2 + gl_2}{g}\right)^2 \leq \left(\frac{v^2 + gl_2}{g}\right)^2 - l_2^2.$$

Thus,

$$R_{\max} = \frac{v}{g} \sqrt{v^2 + 2gl_2},$$

flight time $t^*$ salt for such a distance

$$t^* = \frac{1}{g} \sqrt{2(gl_2 + v^2)};$$

we will find the angle of outflow from the equation

$$\cos \alpha^* = \frac{R_{\max}}{vt^*} = \sqrt{\frac{v^2 + 2gl_2}{2(v^2 + gl_2)}}.$$

We find the height $h^*$ of this trajectory above the level of the light bulb socket using the formula

$$h^* = \frac{(v \sin \alpha^*)^2}{2g} = \frac{v^2}{2g} (1 - \cos^2 \alpha^*) = \frac{v^4}{4g(v^2 + gl_2)}.$$

Let's remember now about the ceiling. Obviously, if $l_1 \geq h^*$, then

$$R_{\max} = \frac{v}{g} \sqrt{v^2 + 2gl_2}.$$

In the case when $l_1 < h^*$, i.e. $l_1 < \frac{v^4}{4g(v^2 + gl_2)}$, the maximum radius of the spot on the floor will come from shards whose maximum trajectory height (above the cartridge level) is $l_1$ (their trajectory will be tangent to the ceiling). To prove this, it is enough to consider at least one piece of debris reflected from the ceiling at point $A$. After reflection, the level of the cartridge will intersect at the angle OUTLET. Consequently, the trajectory of this shard will be larger than the “tangent” trajectory, and they will never intersect. This means that the salt that touches the ceiling will spill horizontally further than all the others that hit the ceiling. The shards that are not woven to the ceiling cannot move when they move to the ground.
