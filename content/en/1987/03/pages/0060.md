Moscow Institute of Radio Engineering, Electronics and Automation

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{7x^2+8x+10}-\sqrt{7x^2-8x+10}=2x.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos 8x-2\sin 2x\sin(2x-\pi/2)=1.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_x 2\leq \log_{\sqrt{x}+2} 2.$$
4. In triangle ABC, a point P is taken on a side through which two straight lines are drawn parallel to the sides of the triangle. The radii of the circles circumscribed around the resulting two new triangles (with a common vertex P) are equal to R₁ and R₂. Find the radius of the circle circumscribed by triangle ABC.
5. The distance between settlements A and B is 36 km. A pedestrian walks from A to B at a speed of 6 km/h. At the same time, a cyclist leaves B towards A, whose maximum speed is 15 km/h and minimum speed is 10 km/h. After meeting the pedestrian, the cyclist rode towards A for another 20 minutes, then turned and returned to B. Find the minimum difference in the time the pedestrian and the cyclist were nailed to B.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{10x^2+8x+7}+\sqrt{10x^2-8x+7}=8x.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos 6x+4\cos\left(\frac{3}{2}\pi+\frac{3}{2}x\right)\sin\frac{3}{2}x=1.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_x 3\leq \log_{2x+3} 9.$$
4. Through a point P taken inside triangle ABC, three straight lines are drawn, each of which is parallel to a side of the triangles (with a common vertex P) equal to S₁, S₂ and S₃. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC.
5. The distance between settlements A and B is 220 km. A car leaves from B towards A, moving at a speed of 60 km/h. At the same time, a bus leaves from A to B, the minimum speed of which is 40 km/h, and the maximum speed is 50 km/h. After the meeting, the car drove for another 20 minutes towards A, then turned and returned to B. Find the maximum difference in the time the bus and the car arrived at B.

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. At what angle to the horizon must the body be thrown so that the maximum height of its rise is three times the flight range?
2. There is a frog sitting on one end of the floating board. At what minimum speed must the frog jump to get exactly to the other end of the board? The mass of the board is M, the mass of the frog is m, the length of the board is l.
3. A body of mass m₁, moving with a speed v, collides with a body at rest and a post-elastic impact is scanned away from it with a speed v/2, directed at an angle of 90° to the original direction of motion. Determine the mass of the body at rest.
4. A light spring with stiffness k and length l stands vertically on the table. A small ball of mass m falls onto it from a height h above the table. What is the maximum speed the ball will have as it moves downwards?
5. The core disintegrates into two shards with masses m₁=3·10⁻²⁵ kg and m₂=10⁻²⁵ kg. Determine the speed of the core fragments if their total kinetic energy is W=6·10⁻¹¹ J. Before the decay of the core fragments.
6. Connected with other boards in one vessel is a column of oil of height h₃=30 cm, in another column of height h₄=20 cm. Determine the difference in mercury levels in the vessels. Density of mercury $\rho_p=13,6\times 10^3$ kg/m³, oil $\rho_m=0,9\times 10^3$ kg/m³, water $\rho_b=10^3$ kg/m³.
7. The power released by resistors with resistance R₁=10 Ohm and R₂=3 Ohm is the same when these resistors are connected in series and in parallel. Find the internal resistance of the source to which the resistors are connected.
8. A square frame with current I=5A can rotate freely around the horizontal side. When the vertical magnetic field was turned on, the frame deviated at an angle α=30°. Find the magnitude of magnetic induction if the density of the materials from which the frame is made is $\rho=8,6\times 10^3$ kg/m³, and the cross-section of the wire is S=2 mm².
9. A wire circuit, shaped like an equilateral triangle on side a=20 cm, is placed in a uniform magnetic field with induction B=1 T so that the plane of the counter makes an angle a=30° with the direction of the field. At some point in time, the magnetic induction begins to decrease uniformly and after a period of time Δt drops to zero. Determine this period of time if it is known that the induced emf $C=100$ V returns in the circuit.
10. On the main optical axis of the collecting line, at a distance of d₁=20 cm and d₂=40 cm from it, there are two precise light sources. Find the shape of the line distance if the images of these sources end up at the same point.
