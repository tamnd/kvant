5. Rationale. Inequality (1) is equally satisfied by one of two systems of inequalities:

$$\begin{cases} 0 < x < 1, \\ x - a < x^2, \end{cases}$$ or $$\begin{cases} 0 < x < 1, \\ a > x - x^2, \end{cases}$$ or $$\begin{cases} x > 1, \\ a < x - x^2. \end{cases}$$
