1) Let us write down the basic equation of the rotational motion diagram for the $n$th particle:
$$kR ((u_{n-1} - u_n) + (u_{n+1} - u_n)) = I \ddot{u}_n/R,$$
where $R$ is the radius of the circle, $I = mR^2$ is the moment of inertia of the particle, $\ddot{u}_n = \frac{d^2 u_n}{dt^2}$, or
$$\ddot{u}_n = \omega_0^2 (u_{n-1} + u_{n+1} - 2u_n).$$
From here we get
$$\ddot{u}_1 = \omega_0^2 (u_3 + u_2 - 2u_1),$$
$$\ddot{u}_2 = \omega_0^2 (u_1 + u_3 - 2u_2),$$
$$\ddot{u}_3 = \omega_0^2 (u_2 + u_1 - 2u_3),$$
where $\omega_0^2 = k/m$.
2) The system of equation (2) has a solution of a superposition of harmonic oscillations - normal modes. To find them, we substitute the equations of harmonic oscillations into the system
$$u_1 = a_1 \cos \omega t, u_2 = a_2 \cos \omega t, u_3 = a_3 \cos \omega t,$$
we add up all the equations of the system and get
$$\omega^2 (a_1 + a_2 + a_3) = 0.$$
It follows: a) $\omega = 0$; b) $a_1 + a_2 + a_3 = 0$, which corresponds to the frequency value $\omega = \sqrt{3} \omega_0$.
