F1015. The circuit shown in figure contains 50 different amperes and 50 identical voltmeters. The readings of the first voltmeter are $U_1=9,6$ V, the first ampere is $I_1=9,5$ mA, the second ampere is $I_2=9,2$ mA. Using these data, determine the sum of the readings of all voltmeters.

The current flowing through the amperor $A_1$ is equal to the sum of the currents flowing through the voltmeter $V_1$ and the amperor $A_2$, i.e.
$$I_1 = I_{V1} + I_2;$$
therefore, the current flowing through the voltmeter $V_1$ is
$$I_{V1} = I_1 - I_2 = 0,3 \text{ мА},$$
and the resistance of the voltmeter -
$$R = \frac{U_1}{I_{V1}} = \frac{9,6}{0,3 - 10^{-3}} \text{ Ом} = 32 \text{ кОм}.$$
According to the conditions of the problem, all voltmeters are the same, i.e., the resistance of each of them is 32 kOhm.

Let's write down the sum of the voltage shown by all voltmeters:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{50} U_{Vi} = \sum_{i=1}^{50} I_{Vi} R = R \sum_{i=1}^{50} I_{Vi}.$$
But $\sum_{i=1}^{50} I_{Vi} = \text{это ток, текущий через амператор } A_1$, i.e.
$$\sum_{i=1}^{50} I_{Vi} = I_1.$$ Pozotomu
$$\sum_{i=1}^{50} U_{Vi} = RI_1 = 304 \text{ В}.$$
