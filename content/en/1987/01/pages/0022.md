F1014. The mots granitor, which releases the tell, is fixed on a tell-out platform, blown with air, the temperature of which is 30 °C. figure shows the temperature distribution at the site. Determine the power dissipation. It is known that a plate uniformly heated to 70 °C dissipates power $P=10\text{Bt}$ at an air temperature of 20 °C. Telo output is proportional to the temperature difference between the plate and the air.

Divide the plate into identical small sections (there are 130 such sections in figure). It is clear that the power $P$ dissipated by the plate is equal to the sum of $\sum p_i$ powers dissipated by individual sections.

The power that is carried away from each section is equal to

$$p_i = p_0 \cdot \Delta t_i,$$

where $\Delta t_i$ is the temperature difference between the area and the air, $p_0$ is the proportionality coefficient. It is known that at an air temperature of $t_{B1}=20\text{°C}$, a power of $P=10\text{Bt}$ is carried away from the plate, uniformly heated to $t_1=70\text{°C}$. In this case, the same power is carried away from each section

$$p = \frac{P}{130} = p_0(t_1 - t_{B1}).$$

From here comes $p_0$:

$$p_0 = \frac{P}{130(t_1 - t_{B1})} = \frac{10}{130 \cdot 50} \text{Bt/град} = 1,54 \cdot 10^{-3} \text{Bt/град}.$$
