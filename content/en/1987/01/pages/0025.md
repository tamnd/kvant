⟦folio 23⟧

Tasks

1. King Arthur ordered the artist figure for his shield, which had the shape of a four circle, with a request to paint it in three colors: yellow - the color of kindness, red - courage and blue - wisdom. When the artist brought figure, the squire said that there was more courage in the drawing than intelligence. However, the artist was able to prove that there is an equal amount of both. How?
2. Solve the number puzzle written in the picture. The same letter corresponds to the same numbers, and different numbers correspond to different numbers.
3. One day I got lost in the forest. I was just about to stop to make a fire and spend the night when I tripped over a water pipe. It is clear that you need to go along the pipe, but in which direction? Where the water flows, because it flows to people. How can you determine where the water is flowing?
4. The fruit harvest this year was excellent. We made 19 jars of jam. I arranged them on three shelves in the cellar so that each shelf held the same number of liters of jam. On the first shelf I put one large and four medium cans, on the second - two large and six liter cans, and on the third - one large, three medium and three liter cans. How many liters of jam did we make?
5. The two gentlemen were extremely difficult in their vaccinations and apparently therefore could not tolerate each other. One day, while walking along the street, they saw each other and at the same time turned into the park: one at gate A (see picture), the second at gate B. Everyone decided to go around this well-known park, walking exactly once along each path. Show that if they are always surprised at the same speeds, then they will definitely meet.
These tasks were suggested to us by A.P. Savin, a 9th grade student at the Karmelavsky Lit secondary school. SSR German Safin, M. I. Lobak, V. D. Vyon, V. V. Proizvolov.
