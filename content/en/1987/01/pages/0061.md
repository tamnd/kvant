2. After opening the valve, a process of gas movement takes place, as a result of which the same pressure $p_0$ and temperature $T_0$ are established in the cylinders. The pressure of a mixture of gases (according to Dalton's law) is determined by the sum of the partial pressures of the components, and the temperature of the mixture is determined by the law of conservation of internal energy of the system. Thus we have

$$p_0 = \frac{(v_1 + v_2)RT_0}{(V_1 + V_2)},$$
$$v_1C_1T_1 + v_2C_2T_2 = (v_1C_1 + v_2C_2)T_0,$$

where $v_1 = m_1/M_1$ and $v_2 = m_2/M_2$ are the number of moles of nitrogen and argon corresponding. The initial temperatures of gases $T_1$ and $T_2$ are found from the equation of state:

$$T_1 = p_1V_1/(Rv_1),$$
$$T_2 = p_2V_2/(Rv_2).$$

Then we finally get

$$T_0 = \frac{C_1p_1V_1 + C_2p_2V_2}{R(v_1C_1 + v_2C_2)} \approx 306\text{ K},$$
$$p_0 = \frac{(v_1 + v_2)RT_0}{V_1 + V_2} \approx 2,1 \cdot 10^6\text{ Pa}.$$

3. We present a detailed analysis of the solution to this problem, although shorter solutions are possible. Let us first prove that the contact, moving under the influence of gravity and the Ampere force, will oscillate around the equilibrium position. Let's choose the positive direction of the coordinate axis down (in the direction of gravity), and the positive direction of going around the contour formed by the coil, rails and contact - counterclockwise (Fig. 1). Let at a given moment the contact move downward at speed $v$, the current $I$ in the contact flows in the positive direction, and the magnetic field $\vec{B}$ is perpedicular to the plane figure and directed away from us. Let us write down the equation of contact motion:

$$mv' = mg - IBl,$$

where $v'$ denotes a speed arbitrary in time, that is, the acceleration of the contact (with the chosen direction of the current in the context, the Ampere force on the right of the left hand slows down the movement of the contact). When the contact moves, the magnetic flux $\Phi = Blx$, an arbitrary closed loop, changes, and an inductive emf $\mathcal{E} = -\Phi'$ arises, which is balanced by the Samonidukichi emf $-LI'$ that arises in the coil:

$$\mathcal{E} - LI' = 0.$$

When the contact moves downwards, the magnetic flux through the circuit increases and the emf arising in the context causes the appearance of a current in the context, turning it into an increase in flux, that is, braking the movement of the contact. With the chosen direction of bypassing the counter, this means that in Ohm’s law this EMF is void with a positive sign. Since $|\mathcal{E}| = |\Phi'| = Blx' = Blv$, Ohm’s law in our case has the form

$$Blv - LI' = 0.$$

Having multiplied the equations of motion of the contact in time and substituted $I$ from the previous equality, we finally have

$$v'' + \frac{B^2l^2}{mL}v = 0.$$

We obtained the oscillation equation for the contact speed $v$ and, therefore, proved that the contact motion is oscillatory in nature. We will not solve this differential equation, but will use the law of conservation of energy.

Suppose that by this moment the contact has dropped down by $x$, has a speed of $v$, and the current in the circuit is $I$. According to the law of conservation of energy, the potential energy of the contact is converted into its kinetic energy and stored in the inductor in the form of magnetic field energy:

$$mgx = mv^2/2 + LI^2/2.$$

The contact speed is maximum in the equilibrium position $x_0$, when the gravitational force is balanced by the Ampere force:

$$mg = BlI_0.$$

It follows from Ohm's law that the current in the circuit is proportional to the contact displacement, therefore

$$Blx_0 = I_0L,$$

and the work of gravity to the equilibrium position can be written as follows:

$$mgx_0 = mg \frac{I_0L}{Bl} = L \left( \frac{mg}{Bl} \right)^2.$$

Thus, the law of conservation of energy gives

$$L \left( \frac{mg}{Bl} \right)^2 = \frac{mv^2}{2} + \frac{L}{2} \left( \frac{mg}{Bl} \right)^2,$$

and finally

$$B = g \frac{\sqrt{mL}}{v_0l}.$$

When solving this rather complex problem, many applicants made a physical mistake, believing that the kinetic energy of contact is gained by working with the Ampere force, and not by gravity. In fact, one side of the work of gravity was converted into the kinetic energy of contact, and its other side turned into the energy of the magnetic field in the inductor.
