Happy New Year!

The year 1987 arrived - the second year of the XII Five-Year Plan, the year of the seventieth anniversary of the Great October Revolution. It will be a year of intense work in the conditions of continuously deepening perestroika, rooted in the socio-economic development of our country. It is necessary to consolidate the successes achieved after the April (1985) plenum of the CPSU Central Committee and the XXVII Party Congress, and confidently move forward along the path of intensifying the national economy. Revolutionary transformations of the productive forces and improvement of production relations and the entire social life of society are coming. Much will have to change in the modern system of education and training. Socialism has huge reserves in all spheres of society, and these reserves need to be put into action as quickly as possible.

The XX Congress of the Komsomol is ahead. He will discuss the problems that life poses to the Komsomol and youth at the stage of radical restructuring of our economy. On the agenda is the expansion of the rights of the Komsomol (including multimillion-dollar artillery), the development of its initiative, independence, and increased responsibility to the country. This makes it possible to even more actively involve young people - the most dynamic, leading part of society - in solving national problems.

Our dear readers! You will be presented with a most interesting life in the conditions of a continuously developing scientific and technological revolution. As the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee M.S. Gorbachev said, the current five-year plan is the most difficult, complex, but also the most interesting five-year plan. Think about these words - after all, they are your immediate future. It will require considerable knowledge and skills from you. One of the most important requirements of life is the ability to learn independently. The country needs highly educational workers dedicated to the process of acquiring new knowledge and good technical knowledge of this process. And our magazine will strive to help you develop these critical skills.

We wish you success in the new year!
