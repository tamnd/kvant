Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Points A(3; 1) and B(-2; 4) are located on the coordinate plane. Find the coordinates of point C lying on the bisector of coordinate angles I and III and equally distant from points A and B.

2. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{9x + \frac{1}{2x+1}} \cdot (9 - 25x^2) \ge 0.$$

3. Find all values ​​of the parameter a for which the equation
$$(1-a) \mathrm{tg}^2 x - \frac{2}{\cos x} + 1 + 3a = 0$$
has more than one solution on the interval $0; \frac{\pi}{2}$.

4. The length of side AB of parallelogram ABCD is 2.$BAD = 45^\circ$. Points E and F are located on the diagonal BD, with $AEB = CFD = 90^\circ$, $BF = \frac{3}{2}$ BE. Find the area of ​​the parallelogram.

5. The length of the base edge of the regular triangular paramid SABC (S is the vertex) is 8. Points K and L are located on edges AB and AC, respectively, with AK=7, AL=4. It is known that for a given paramid there is a single cone, the vertex of which coincides with the point K, the center of the center lies on the straight line SC, and the segment KL is one of the generators. Find the volume of the cone.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\log \sqrt{x} (5x)} \cdot \log_5 x = -2.$$

2. Point D lies on side BC of right triangle ABC ($C = 90^\circ$), with $AB = 5$, $ADC = \arccos \frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}$, $DB = \frac{4\sqrt{10}}{3}$. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC.

3. Solve the system of equations
$$\sin x = \sin 2y,$$
$$2 \sin (4x+8y) \sin (3x+10y) + 5 \cos (7x+2y) = 4.$$

4. The length of the height of a regular quadrangular paramid SABCD (S is the vertex) is $\sqrt{3}$ times the length of the supporting edge. Point E is the midpoint of the apothem of face ASB. Find the angle between line DE and plane ASC.

5. For each number p on the coordinate plane, we consider the set M of all points whose coordinates (a; b) satisfy the conditions a>0, b>0, a+b>1, 3ap < bp+2p^2 and are such that the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} px^2 + 2xy + y^2 = b^2, \\ 3x + y = a \end{cases}$$
has no solutions.

a) Find the area of ​​a polygon whose interior region is a polygon if p = $\frac{21}{5}$.

6) Find all real p for which the polygon M is inside the region of the polygon.
