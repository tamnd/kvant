a tank of an experimental setup filled with liquid with a refractive index n=1.6. The passage of a particle through a liquid is accompanied by a glow. After a short period of time after the particle hit the tank, the device was turned on, located at point C, which recorded at the moment of turning on two candles, points A and B (the experimental diagram on a certain scale is shown on figure; a top view is given, points C, A and B lie in the horizontal plane). Explain the observed phenomenon and use figure to find the speed of the particle. Neglect the deceleration of a particle in a liquid.

Indeed, at the moment the device was turned on, light arrived at the device, previously emitted at different times from points A and B. It took time $t_1$ for light to travel the path BC longer than the time $t_2$ to travel the path AC. Consequently, at point B the particle was earlier than at point A, i.e. the particle moves from right to left (from B to A). For time $t_2 - t_1$ light

in a liquid, let $l_2 - l_1$ (see figure), and let the particle be $l_3$. As can be seen from figure, $l_3 > l_2 - l_1$. So, really $u > v$.

Knowledge and find from the equation
$$\frac{l_2 - l_1}{v} = \frac{l_3}{u} \Rightarrow u = v \frac{l_3}{l_2 - l_1} = \frac{c}{n} \frac{l_3}{l_2 - l_1}.$$
Having determined the relation $l_3: (l_2 - l_1)$ from the drawing, we obtain $u = 0,83 c$.

A. I. Buzdin
