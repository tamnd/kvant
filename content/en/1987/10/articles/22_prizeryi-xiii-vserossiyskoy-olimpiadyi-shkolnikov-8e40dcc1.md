Experimental tour
8th grade
1. There are three resistors in the black box. Draw a possible diagram of their connection.
Equipment: school amperor, school voltmeter, power supply, school rheostat, connecting wires, connecting key.
2. Explore the process of collision of two washes, we are on the face of the paper, located on a horizontal table. Determine whether the law of conservation of mechanical energy is satisfied.
Equipment: a sheet of graph paper, two five-kopek coins, a student's protractor ruler.
9th grade
1. Determine the dependence of the current gain of the transistor in a circuit with a common emitter on the current in the collector in the current range $0\div 30$ mA.
Equipment: low-power transistor type MP42, micropermeter 500 μA, 4.5 V battery, variable resistor 30 kOhm, sheet with the cyclic transistor shown on it, milliammeter 50 mA.
2. Determine the dependence of the angle of inclination at which the drop begins to roll down the glass on the mass of the drop.
Equipment: glass plate, transor, pipette, beaker, vessel with water.
10th grade
1. Measure the resistance of 4 resistors.
Equipment: 4.5 V battery, 5 mA milliammeter, 5 V voltmeter, unknown resistors, key, connecting wires.
2. Investigate the dependence of saturated water vapor pressure on temperature.
Equipment: a vessel with water, a heater (alcohol lamp, dry alcohol, electric water), a test tube with divisions, a thermometer, a barometer (one per class), a socket and a foot.
