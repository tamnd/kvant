⟦folio 39⟧

Quests

1. It is not difficult to show that a regular five-pointed star has a sum of angles of 180°. Prove that an arbitrary five-pointed star will have this sum of angles.

2. I managed, by taking the numbers 1, 2, 3 and 4 twice, to write a remembered number, with one digit between the ones, two digits between the twos, three between the threes, and four digits between the fours. What number is this?

3. Place the numbers from 1 to 8 in the circles of the figure shown on figure, so that the numbers in the circles connected by segments differ by at least two.

4. My wall clock is behaving very strangely. In the first half of each hour they are 2 minutes fast, then in the second half they are two minutes behind. What is the reason for this behavior of the watch?

5. A point taken inside an equilateral triangle is connected to all vertices. In addition, perpendiculars are dropped from it to all sides of the triangle. Show that the sum of the areas of the red triangles (see figure) is equal to the sum of the areas of the blue triangles.

These tasks were suggested to us by Aleksandr Korshkov, A.P. Savin, A.M. Domashenko, A.A. Panov, V.V. Proizvolov, a student of the 9th grade of scale No. 8 in Mozyr.
