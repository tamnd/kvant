Tasks located along the chain

I’ll say right away that I don’t know who came up with these problems. Some of them were told to me by Oleg Vladimirovich Dolgov. Maybe you saw him on the TV screen, because he has long been performing in the room of experts in the telephone show “What? Where? When?". But although he knows it, it turned out that he also does not know who the author of these problems is. In addition to the problems told to me by O. V. Dolgov, there are also other problems of this type - I have heard some of them before, but I also could not establish who their author was. I really liked the problems, and I decided to present them on pages «Kvant». I hope that you will also like them and, in addition, perhaps one of the readers will indicate to us their author (or authors). Or maybe the authors themselves see their tasks printed and announced. So, these are the tasks.

Problem No. 1. How many operations do you need to do to put meth free in the refrigerator?

After O.V. Dolgov asked me this problem, I started thinking... Dolgov came to my aid:

- I'll tell you how the first problem is solved. In order to place non-meth in the refrigerator, three operations are required:

1. Open the refrigerator.
2. Place the meth free in the refrigerator.
3. Close the refrigerator.
From this decision I learned to myself what is meant by the word “operation”. Each of the actions specified in the decision is an operation. After that I was given the following task.

Problem No. 2. How many operations must be done in order to put a giraffe in the refrigerator?

After thinking a little, I said:

“Probably, in order to put a giraffe in the refrigerator, you need to do more operations than to put non-meth in there.”

- Why? - asked O.V. Dolgov.
— Because a giraffe doesn’t fit into the refrigerator. It must be folded first.

“There’s no need to fold it,” said O. V. Dolgov, “the refrigerator can fit in it if there’s no one else there.”

- Then, as in the previous problem, three operations are enough - open the refrigerator, put the giraffe in, close it.

- No, that's wrong. This time we need to perform four operations,” said O.V. Dolgov. And he listed these operations:

1. Open the refrigerator.
2. We take out the non-meth.
3. We put the giraffe down.
4. Close the refrigerator.

Do I need to explain anything?! I forgot that the refrigerator was busy! After the first task, there was no meth left in the refrigerator.

Then I was asked

Problem No. 3. There is no meth and the giraffe is on land at a distance of 1 kilometer from the river bank. Which of them will reach the water faster?

When we solve such a problem, thinking is free. However, I thought about it and said:

— The giraffe will run quickly, its holes are long.

“Wrong answer,” said O. V. Dolgov.
- Which answer is correct?

“It’s more likely that he’ll run to the shore without any meth.”

- Why?
— Because the giraffe remained in the refrigerator.

I said incorrectly:

- Forty steps... that's ten times more than four steps. This means the boy will break ten legs. But perhaps this is the wrong answer?

“Wrong,” said Katya.
- Which answer is correct?

— The boy will only break one leg.
- Why?
- Because he has already broken one leg, and he only has two.

Afterwards, Katya and I decided that it wouldn’t be a good idea to look into this problem about a boy,

I feel sorry for him - it’s a very big deal when a person breaks his leg. We decided to solve this problem not about a boy, but about a chair: the chair falls from its leggings, and its legs break in the process. A chair, of course, is the same pity, but less than a person. And, besides, the task seemed very interesting to us. However, the chair is not spared for such a task.
That's all the tasks I know, arranged in a chain. Perhaps readers know other such problems? Then send them to our magazine.
V. M. Bolotovsky
