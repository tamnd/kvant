Tasks

M1066 — M1070, F1078 — F1082

M1066. Six points are located on the plane so that all pairwise distances between them are not greater than 1. Prove that from them it is possible to select three points whose pairwise distances are strictly less than 1.

S. G. Salnikov

M1067. Prove that for non-negative numbers $x, y, z$ satisfying the condition $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$ the following inequality holds:

$$\frac{x}{1-x^2} + \frac{y}{1-y^2} + \frac{z}{1-z^2} \geq \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

V. E. Matizen

M1068. Given an angle $AOB$ (A and B are points on the sides of the angle). Construct a line $l$ passing through the vertex $O$ so that the areas of triangles $AOC$ and $BOD$, where $C$ and $D$ are the bases of the perpendiculars dropped from the points $A$ and $B$ to the line $l$, are equal.

P. O. Vurdin

M1069. Some cities only allow paired volumes of apartments (if two families exchange apartments, they do not participate in other exchanges on the same day). Prove that any complex project involving apartments of several families can be completed in two days. (It is assumed that both before and after each exchange, the family lives in a separate apartment.)

N. N. Konstantinov, A. I. Shirelman

M1070. The tetrahedron is intersected by three planes, each of which is parallel to its two opposite edges and equally distant from them. Prove that the sum of the squared areas of these three sections is 4 times less than the sum of the squared areas of the faces of the tetrahedron.

V. N. Dubrovsky

F1078. A long thin thread with a weight at the end is thrown over a pulley and tied to the bow of a model boat that can drink down a long straight channel of water (Fig. 1). The boat and load are released, and the speed of the boat (in cm/s) is recorded every second. Unfortunately, only the end of this entry has survived:

5,65; 6,44; 6,96; 7,31; 7,54; 7,70; 7,80; 7,86; 7,91.

Using these data, determine the speed of the boat 0.1 seconds after it was released.

A. A. Sheronov

F1079. If the cold water tap is fully open and the hot water tap is closed (Fig. 2), then the bathtub is filled in $t_1 = 8$ minutes; If you attach a hose with a shower at the end to the outlet, the filling time will increase to $t_2 = 14$ min. When the cold water tap is closed and the hot water tap is fully open, the filling time is $t_3 = 12$ min; under the same conditions, but with a shower at the end - $t_4 = 18$ min. How long will it take to fill the bathtub if both taps are fully open? And if at the same time a hose with a shower is attached?

B. E. Skorovarov
