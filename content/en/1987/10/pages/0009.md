Unfortunately, today there are no objects that could be considered “suspicious” from such a point of view. To one degree or another, on the basis of astrophysical hypotheses and physical models, every anomaly in the behavior of certain cosmic bodies can be explained. At least to the extent that known facts allow us to do so. At the same time, radio astronomers consider one of their main tasks to be the search for objects with anomalous characteristics. This direction has a chance of detecting truly anomalous objects.

The main question of our time is the question of peace. Could international cooperation in space be an alternative to the Star Wars program? How do you imagine it?

Of course, peaceful international cooperation in any form is an alternative to the miliarization of outer space. It is impossible to saturate the near-Earth orbits of numerous military spacecraft and at the same time expand international scientific or, say, commercial projects into gray road construction. These two directions come into deep conflict. And it is no coincidence that the parties to peaceful space, the star world, actively oppose the use of space for military purposes.

Today there are already quite a lot of specific areas of space research in which international cooperation is carried out, in particular, this is the Intercosmos program, which includes the study of outer space by unmanned vehicles, rafted flights, etc. As part of international cooperation, research into Halley’s comet was successfully completed in 1986. In mid-1988, I learned how to implement the Phobos project. Its goal is to study one of the satellites of Mars. The initiators of the project were Soviet scientists, and the scientific equipment for Phobos is being prepared by specialists from different countries.

2001: A Space Odyssey is just around the corner. What will it be like? After all, it contains scientists and external readers of the journal «Kvant». What can you wish for them?

I think her script will be written when today's readers «Kvant» become specialists. There is not much left before that, maybe 5-7 years. And the earlier schoolchildren take an active creative position, the greater results they will achieve (and it is not at all necessary to wait until they reach their fifties!).

I wish them success!
