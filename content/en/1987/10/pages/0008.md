The study of extradalactic objects will lead to the creation of new models of the formation and evolution of the Universe, the formation of galaxies and star systems, in particular the Solar System.

- At the dawn of the space age, there was a lot of hope that going into space would make a real discovery of external life; even man’s dream of meeting his brothers in mind seemed about to happen. Perhaps this is what gave space flights a special romance. However, in actual fiction this theme was central (you can use the replacement of I. A. Efremov’s novel “The Andromeda Nebula,” which appeared at the same time). Years passed, but scientists not only failed to detect life on other celestial bodies, many of them even believe that life on Earth is unique. What's new for space exploration in this regard?

— In space research, the search for extraterrestrial life occupies an important place. If the flight program allowed for the opportunity to shed additional light on this issue, such an opportunity, as a rule, was not missed. Special instruments were installed on spacecraft (for example, on the landing block of the Martian Viking there was a biological laboratory). And although it was not possible to find signs of life of inaction anywhere, I thought it was too early to draw pessistic conclusions. Of course, what we know about Mars today makes the discovery of even the most primitive forms of life on it unlikely. And in general there is little chance that this will be removed anywhere in the Solar System. However, research has yielded a lot of important data about the so-called pre-biological evolution of molecules. An almost complete set of amino acid bases is present both in the lunar soil and in the metoritic substance that falls to Earth. This means that the so-called abiogenic synthesis—the preparation of the initial “building blocks” for further jumping to the gene level—takes place under cosmic conditions in the presence of all possible environmental radiation, such as X-rays. And of course, during future flights to Mars and other celestial bodies more distant from us, such a possibility of biological or at least pre-biological research will be explored.

— Many scientists consider it advisable to look for manifestations of the activity of external civilizations among celestial bodies, in the “behavior” of which there are any anomalies. At one time, the attention of astronomers was attracted by the Tunguska meteorite, the satellites of Mars, some comets... Are there any celestial bodies today that astronomers are “on suspicion”?
