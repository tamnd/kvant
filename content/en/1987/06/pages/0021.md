⟦folio 19⟧

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given asterisks. After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time.

Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than September 1, 1987 to the address: 103006 Moscow K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». Solutions to problems from different issues of the journal or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 6-87” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M1046” or “F1058”. In the column “...sender’s address”, please write your first and last name separately. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of the decision verification).

The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics or “... a new problem in mathematics”).

At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and the class in which you study.

M1046. In triangle ABC, angle A is 60°. Prove that one of the bisectors of the angle formed by the altitudes drawn from vertices B and C passes through the circumcenter of this triangle.

V. Pogrebyan, 10th grade student, Vinnitsa

M1047. In a chess tournament played in one round, at least 3/4 of all games played at some point ended in a draw. Prove that at this moment two participants scored the same number of points.

M. Bona, high school student, Hungary

M1048*. One of the two players (“beginner”) places a knight on a certain square of the chessboard. Then the players take turns moving the knight according to the general rules (letter “Γ”), but you cannot place the knight on the field where it has already been beaten. The one who has nowhere to go loses. Who can achieve victory (regardless of the opponent’s actions) - the beginner or his partner a) on an 8x8 board; 6) on the board m×n, where m≥n≥3?

V. Zudilin, 10th grade student, Veltsy

M1049. We will say that another cylinder C2 is inscribed sideways into cylinder C1 if two generatrices of the second cylinder lie on the bases of the first, and even points of the circles of the base of the second lie on the lateral surface of the first (Fig. 1). Taking cylinder C1, whose ratio of diameter to height is equal to k, we write cylinder C2 into it sideways (if possible), write C3 into it sideways, write C4 into it, etc. At what values ​​of k a) can we write C2, but not C3; 6)* you can enter Ts10, but not Ts11; 8)* is it possible to enter an infinite sequence CN (n=1, 2, ...)?

V. Stolin, 11th grade student, Vilnius

M1050. On the segment [−1, 1], k different points are selected, for each the product of the distances to the remaining k−1 points is calculated, and S denotes the sum of the reciprocals of these k products. Prove that a) S≥2 for k=3; 6)* S≥4 at k=4.

L. D. Kurlyandchik

F1058. The ball rolls along the edge of straight iron ACB (Fig. 2) with speed v without slipping; distance AB is equal to the radius of the ball. Which points on the ball have the maximum speed? What is the speed?

S. S. Krotov

F1059. At a certain point in space, it is necessary to create the maximum possible directional field (gravitational acceleration), having at disposal a given mass of matter of constant density.

a) What form should the substance be given?
b) What should be the relative position of the body and the point?

E. N. Yunosov, I. V. Yaminsky
