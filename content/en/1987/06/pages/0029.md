⟦folio 27⟧

Tasks

1. Replace the letters with numbers so that you get true equalities (see figure). In this case, the same numbers must correspond to the same letters, and different numbers to different ones.

2. They say that Tortila did not give the golden key to Pinocchio as simply as A.N. Tolstoy said, but brought out three boxes: red, blue and green. On the red box it was written “Here lies the golden key”, on the blue one - “The green box is empty”, and on the green one - “Here sits a viper.” Tortila read the inscriptions and said: “Indeed, in one box there is a golden key, in the other there is a viper, and the third is empty, but all the inscriptions are incorrect.”

Where is the golden key?

3. Kolya, Viti and Yura had 12, 14 and 22 nuts. When Kolya gave Vita as many nuts as Vitya had, then Vita gave Yura as many nuts as Yura had, and Yura gave Kolya as many nuts as Kolya had left, all the guys had the same amount of nuts. How many nuts did each have?

4. The dashing captains of the sailing ships transporting cotton from Australia to England were not eager to fully load their ships, although they were interested in transporting more cotton. Why?

5. A convex quadrilateral was cut along two straight lines connecting the midpoints of opposite sides. Show that from the resulting four pieces it is always possible to add a parallelogram.

These tasks were proposed to us by: V. N. Rusanov, A. P. Savin, S. V. Dvoryaninov, L. P. Motsalov, V. V. Proizvolov.
