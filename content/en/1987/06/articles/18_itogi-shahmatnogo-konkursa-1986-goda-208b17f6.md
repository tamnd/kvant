Results of the 1986 chess competition

The 1986 chess competition unexpectedly broke all records - the editor received more than 3,000 letters for the first tasks. Apparently, after the world championship matches between Kasparov and Karpov, interest in chess increased, and more chess players appeared among the readers of «Kvant». As the competition progressed, the tasks became more difficult and the number of participants, of course, decreased. But still, hundreds of readers stood the test to the end! Initially, it was planned to award II and I categories for the best solutions, however, as we found out, most of those who solved the rules (more than 16 tasks) already had a second category, or even a first. Therefore, it was decided to assign 1st category to all 40 winners of the competition (some of them for the second time!).
Among the competition participants there are many whose envelopes you don’t even need to open - the post office’s decisions are probably correct. These are such experienced solvers as A. Ryabov, I. Kvasha, V. Medvedev, V. Moskalenko, M. Germanov, D. Krasikov, A. Boyko and others. They have repeatedly won the competition, respectively, received magazine diplomas, prizes, and first category. This time, we included in the winners those participants who are not so excessive in fame.
