VIII Moscow Olympiad in Programming

The Moscow City Olympiad in programming for schoolchildren was held on February 1, 1987 by the Training and Production Center with the institutes INEUM Minipribora and the Institute of Pilot Sciences of the USSR Academy of Sciences. It hosted 320 students from 21 districts of Moscow. Below are the objectives of this Olympiad. (Participants were asked to choose and solve only two problems.)

1. Rokzak. From the given n items, select those such that their total weight is less than 30 kg, and their cost is the greatest. Enter the total cost of the selected items.

More precisely. Given a number n < 100 and two arrays of positive numbers A[1:n], B[1:n]. Choose pairwise distinct numbers i1, i2, ..., ik such that
A[i1]+A[i2]+...+A[ik] < 30,
a
B[i1]+B[i2]+...+B[ik] = max.
Enter only the max values.

2. Half multiples. The set of numbers A is specified by the conditions:
a) 1 ∈ A,
b) if k ∈ A, then 2*k + 1 ∈ A and 3*k + 1 ∈ A, and the set A does not contain other numbers.
List the first n < 1000 numbers of set A in ascending order.
Here is the beginning of this printout: 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 13, 15, 19, ...

3. Pairs of cubes. How many ways can a given natural number n be represented as the sum of two cubes of natural numbers:
n = i3 + j3?

Rearranging the terms does not give a new method. The root extraction program should not be used.

4. Changelings. Given numbers n < 1000 and a numeric array A[1:n]. Find a segment of the array of maximum length in which the first number is equal to the last, the second to the penultimate, etc. Find the length of this segment.
