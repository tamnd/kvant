Aftermath of exams

Quanti, that one and the same event can be looked at from different points of view. In the exam, these are the points of view of teachers and applicants. These are the parallels we were able to draw between the statements of examiners and applicants, which we overheard in the midst of the entrance exams.

Teachers

One of my applicants got confused, but oh well. Pretended not to notice.

Eh, I shouldn’t have given one a B today. After all, I’ve already seen that I don’t know anything. I was just in a big hurry.

And this fake guy doesn’t understand anything.

I tell him: “You know, but not five points. There are people who know better.”

I have one question for her. Doesn't answer. I'm second. Doesn't answer. I give her a bad grade - she's in tears!

Applicants

I outsmarted the teacher. He made a mistake, but answered the question in such a way that he didn’t even notice.

Eh, bad luck! The examiner was in a hurry somewhere, otherwise I would have taken my rightful five points.

And this little guy doesn’t understand anything.

He tells me: “You know well, there are few guys who know better than you, but...”.

He asks a difficult question, I give him an answer right away. He is the second question - I am the answer again. And he suddenly says: “You, girl, don’t know anything.” Well, then I told him that he was wrong.
