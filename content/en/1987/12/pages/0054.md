Air should be considered as an ideal gas.

Task 2

A beam of electricity flies out from a precise source $P$ and flies into the magnetic field $B$ of an annular (toridal) coil along the linear field (Fig. 2). Let the opening angle of the electric beam $2a_0$ be very small ($a_0\ll 1$). Electric injection occurs on the middle radius $R$ of the torid using an accelerating voltage $U_0$. It is assumed that model $B$ is built. Neglect the electrostatic interaction between the electrons in the beam.

1) To move the beam along the linear side, it is necessary to apply a uniform magnetic field with induction $B_1$, which deflects the beam. Calculate $B_1$ for an electrode that moves along a circular path of radius $R$ inside the torid.

2) Calculate the model $B$ of an inductive magnetic field facsing a beam at four points spaced by l/2 relative to each other (see Fig. 2). Note: when studying the trajectory of the electric field, the curvature of the linear magnetic field can be neglected.

3) Without a deflecting field $\vec{B}_1$, the electric beam does not remain in the toroid, but leaves it with a drift movement perpendicular to the toroid plane. Show that the radial deviation of the electron from the injection radius $R$ is finite. Determine the direction of the drift speed. Note: The opening angle of the electron beam should be neglected. Use the laws of conservation of energy and angular momentum.

Data: $e/m=1,76 \cdot 10^{11}$ C/kg, $U_0=3$ kV, $R=50$ mm.

Task 3

When sinusoidal oscillations of current and voltage propagate along an infinite $L-C$ chain (Fig. 3), the phases of voltage oscillations on the capacitors of two consecutive cells differ by a constant amount $\varphi$.

1) Determine the dependences of $\varphi$ on $\omega$, $L$ and $C$ ($\omega$ is the cyclic oscillation frequency).

2) Determine the speed of wave propagation if each cell has length $l$.

3) Under what conditions does the speed of propagation of oscillations weakly depend on $\omega$ and what is the value of the speed in this case?

4) Propose the design of a mechanical model similar to the electrical circuit described above, and derive equations that justify the validity of this model.

Auxiliary formulas:

$$\cos \alpha - \cos \beta = -2 \sin \left( \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \right) \sin \left( \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} \right),$$

$$\sin \alpha - \sin \beta = 2 \cos \left( \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} \right) \sin \left( \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} \right).$$
