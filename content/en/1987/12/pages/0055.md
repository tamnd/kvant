At the second meeting of the International Commission, which took place on July 8, the experimental task was reviewed and, after discussion, approved. According to the preliminary forecast, everyone wanted the experimental task to be optical - the city of Jena is famous for its largest optical enterprise, Carl Zeiss Jena, for the production of optical instruments and optical glass. A hint of an optical theme was also seen in the official emblem of the Olympics (see the intro to the article). And indeed, the experimental task turned out to be on geometric optics.

On July 9, in the premises of one of the best schools in Zenena - at the secondary school named after. Julius Schachel II - an experimental round of the Olympids took place.

Here are its conditions.

Experimental tour

Determine the refractive indices of the prism and liquid.

1) When using only one recognition, determine its own refractive index $n_1$ in two different ways. Represent the problem in the form figure and use it to derive formulas for calculating the refractive index.

Auxiliary materials: marking sheet with angles of 30°, 60° and 90°, graph paper, ruler, white paper.

2) Using two such identical parameters, determine the refractive index of the liquid $n_2$; and $n_1 > n_2$. Represent the problem in the form figure and use it to derive formulas for calculating the refractive index.

Auxiliary materials: two rectangles with angles of 30°, 60° and 90°, graph paper, ruler, white paper, Petri dish, round stand, liquids.

Formulas:

$$\sin(\alpha \pm \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta \pm \cos \alpha \sin \beta.$$
