Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of Labor Red Replacement Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling eight-year-old and secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR and the Ukrainian SSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1988/89 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students study independently to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. When admitted to the 3FTS, pre-option is allocated to students living in rural areas and workers' settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circles recruit and enroll students (at least 8-10 people) who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the surnames, first names, absences of the circle leaders and a list of members of the circle (indicating the class in the 1988/89 academic year and the final grades for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). All materials on the organization of circles and an envelope for a response about the admission of a circle to 3FTSH with a return address addressed to one of the leaders of the circle should be sent to 3FTSH (141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTSH, indicating “Circle”) before May 25, 1988. (Notebooks with the work of members of the circle are not sent to 3FTS.) The work of the head of correspondence physics and technology clubs can be paid for by schools upon the proposal of 3FTS at MIPT as elective classes.

3FTS students and heads of physics and technology clubs will benefit from assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as the recommended 3FTS solutions to these tasks. The assignments contain theoretical material and development of typical problems and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 assignments for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH and its branches, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

From Moscow schools, classes in physics and mathematics are held twice a week according to the 3FTSH program in evening advisory centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out or according to a decisive educational agreement for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). All materials on the organization of circles and an envelope for a response about the admission of a circle to 3FTSH with a return address addressed to one of the leaders of the circle should be sent to 3FTSH (141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTSH, indicating “Circle”) before May 25, 1988. (Notebooks with the work of members of the circle are not sent to 3FTS.) The work of the head of correspondence physics and technology clubs can be paid for by schools upon submission of 3FTS at MIPT as elective classes.
