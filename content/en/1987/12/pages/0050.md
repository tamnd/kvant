XXVIII International Mathematical Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences V.V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences Yu. P. SOLOVIEV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. A. FOMIN

The XXVIII International Mathematical Olympiad was held in Havana, the capital of the Republic of Cuba.

Teams from 42 countries took part in the Olympiad, 6 people per country. The USSR schoolchildren team included winners of the All-Union Olympiads of recent years: Ilya Vinder (Leningrad, school No. 239), Lev Borisov (Minsk, school No. 19), Sergey Ivanov (Leningrad, school No. 533), Igor Pukhov (Moscow, school No. 57), Stanislav Smirnov (Leningrad, northern route No. 239), Konstantin Styrkas (Chernogolovka settlement, Moscow region, northern route No. 82).

This year, the International Mathematical Olympiad was held for the first time in Latin America, the first socialist country in the Western Hemisphere. The Central Committee of the Communist Party of Cuba and the Cuban government made a lot of efforts to ensure that the Olympiad in Cuba turned into a bright celebration of mathematics. The organizational work was led by a member of the Political Central Committee of the Communist Party of Cuba, Minister of Education Jose Raus Fernandez; The work was legally supervised by famous Cuban mathematicians Miguel Jimenez Pozo and Guiermo Lopez Lagomasino.

Havana welcomed the Olympic participants on July 4th. During the Olympics, the children made several exciting excursions around the city and its environs, played in the historical museum, in the national pioneer camp named after. Jose Marti, took a trip to Playa Giron, and of course, got acquainted with the meaning of Havana beaches.

July 10 and 11 were math competition days. They took place in a specialized school exactly 6 hours from the country. The USSR schoolchildren team included winners of the All-Union Olympiads of recent years: Ilya Vinder (Leningrad, school No. 239), Lev Borisov (Minsk, school No. 19), Sergey Ivanov (Leningrad, school No. 533), Igor Pukhov (Moscow, school No. 57), Stanislav Smirnov (Leningrad, northern route No. 239), Konstantin Styrkas (Chernogolovka settlement, Moscow region, northern route No. 82).

Thus, the USSR team, having scored 235 points out of 252 possible, won 3 gold and 3 silver medals.

In the unofficial team standings, the participating countries were distributed as follows: Romania (250), Germany (248), USSR (235), East Germany (231), USA (220), Hungary (218), Bulgaria (210), China (200), Czechoslovakia (192), England (182), Vietnam (172), France (154), Australia (150), Inderland (146), Australia (143), Canada (139), Sweden (134), Yugoslavia (132), Brazil (116), Greece (111), Turkey (94), Spain (91), Morocco (88), Cuba (83), Belgium (74), Iran (70), Norway (69), Finland (69), Colombia (68), Mongolia (67), Poland (55), Iceland (45), Cyprus (42), Peru (41), Italy (35), Algeria (29), Kuwait (28), Luxembourg (27), Uruguay (27), Mexico (17), Nicaragua (13), Panama (7).
