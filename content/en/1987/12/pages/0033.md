⟦folio 31⟧

Quests

1. It is known about three prime numbers that one of them is equal to the difference of the cubes of the other two. What are these numbers?

2. In some ancient clocks, intended for use in the open air, the clock was magically made in the form of a long tube ending in a vessel containing rue. For what purpose was this done?

3. Solve the number puzzle (see figure). The same letter corresponds to the same numbers, different letters correspond to different numbers.

4. There are seven houses on the Semidvorye farm. It is curious that no matter which three houses we choose, the distance between at least one pair of them is 100 meters. Draw a plan for the location of houses on the farm.

5. Tartarin, traveling through Africa, once stopped overnight on the shore of a small lake with the purest water (springs bubbled at the bottom). However, in the morning a herd of elephants approached the lake. Tartarin counted 183 heads. The next morning they left, leaving a muddy puddle in the lake. A few years later, Tartarin returned to this place. The lake was full of water again, but in the morning the elephants appeared again. This time there were 37 elephants in the herd and they had enough water for 5 days. Leaving the shores of the lake, drunk until the day, Tartarin thought: in how many days will one elephant devastate the lake?
