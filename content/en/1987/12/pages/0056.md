The final meeting of the International Commission to sum up the results of the XVIII International Physics Olympiad took place on July 10. The three first prizes were received by: C. Malureanu (Romania) - 49 points, C. Necula (Romania) - 45 points and B. Baker (Holland) - 44 points.*) Second prizes were awarded to 10 participants. Among them is A. Bibkov, the best in our team, who ultimately scored 39 points. 29 participants received third prizes, among them D. Vudko - 31 points and A. Goldin - 34 points. Two more members of the Soviet team - G. Vilklyaks and D. Glushenkov (who scored 30 and 27 points) - received depressed answers.

In the unofficial team competition, the USSR team, having scored 161 points, shared 5th and 6th places with the Hungarian team, behind the teams of Romania (208 points), Germany (181 points), China (175 points) and the GDR (162 points). The very fact of our team’s unsuccessful performance could probably be explained by external reasons - the circle of Olympiad participants is constantly expanding, the team’s level of preparedness is growing, students in Romania, for example, go to school not 10, but 12 years, it is simply impossible to always be first, etc. However, even a cursory analysis of the existing system of preparing the corresponding team indicates that there are a number of shortcomings in this matter. For example, the selection system as a whole requires greater flexibility; further deepening of individual work with schoolchildren and strengthening of the material and technical base of summer and winter camps are necessary. In a word, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, using all its accumulated work experience, must say its decision.

On July 12, at the official closing of the XVIII International Olympiad for schoolchildren in state physics, the award and special signs of the winner were presented. A. Bibkov received a specialization for the most original solution to a problem of theoretical type 3. C. Malureanu (Romania) received a specialization for the best solution to problem 2 of theoretical type. Another specialization (there were only three) was awarded to D. Serban from Romania, who showed the highest result among girls (42 points - second prize). Many thanks are given to the organization of the committee, which ensured the exceptional performance of the Olympics, ensuring cordiality, hospitality and goodwill. The Olympiad participants were very pleased with the cultural program offered to them.

Regarding task M1065
The editors have received several letters, from which it is clear that not all readers correctly understood the conditions of problem M1065. It implies that all generators in vector representations are different. We are extending the deadline for sending solutions to this problem until March 1, 1988. A special note will be devoted to its solution.
