3. Use Cauchy's inequality
$$|a_1b_1+a_2b_2+\dots+a_nb_n| \leq \sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+\dots+a_n^2} \times \sqrt{b_1^2+\dots+b_n^2}$$ and equality $x_1^2+x_2^2+\dots+x_n^2 = 1$, we get
$$|x_1|+|x_2|+\dots+|x_n| \leq \sqrt{n}.$$
