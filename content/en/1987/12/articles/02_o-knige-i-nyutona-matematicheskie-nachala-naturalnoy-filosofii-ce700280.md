M. WHISTON'S SCHEME of the SOLAR SYSTEM EPITOMISD. Town is annexed
A Translation of part of General Scholium at the end of the second Edition of S. L. H. Nettlew Principia. Concerning God.

In this issue we continue our acquaintance with Newton’s “Mathematical Principles of Natural Philosophy” (see the article “Newton’s Great Book”).
This book had a huge impact on the development of science and human thought.
The mechanics constructed by Newton makes it possible to analyze and generalize many complex phenomena, including astronomical ones.
The “Scheme of the Solar System” reproduced here, published in 1724 by the English scientist W. Wiston (who after Newton took a professorship at Cambridge), is an attempt to present the “Newtonian system of the world”, based on the laws of motion and the law of universal gravitation formulated in the “Principles”.
