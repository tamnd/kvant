Farthest quasar

Some of the new quasars contain a modern element. Astropomers are trying to find a quasar that would be located further than all those already known.

This summer, using SIM cards made in Australia, a quasar was discovered, which is located at a distance of about 13·10⁹ light years from us. In addition to being very distant, the discovery quasar is also very bright. The energy of its radiation is 10,000 times higher than the energy of the average galaxy.

The distance to a celestial object located on a pillar can be estimated only by the magnitude of the mixing of the lines of the spectra of the atoms of this object, which is associated with the expansion of the Universe. However, calculating mixing values ​​into distances based on modern data is done with large errors. The uncertainty in the calculations arises here due to the fact that it is still not possible, according to various definitions, to construct the “scattering” of galaxies v with a distance R between them:
