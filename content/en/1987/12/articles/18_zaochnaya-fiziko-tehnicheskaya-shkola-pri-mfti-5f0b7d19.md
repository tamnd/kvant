Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of Labor Red Replacement Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling eight-year-old and secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR and the Ukrainian SSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1988/89 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students study independently to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. When admitted to the 3FTS, pre-option is allocated to students living in rural areas and workers' settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circles recruit and enroll students (at least 8-10 people) who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the surnames, first names, absences of the circle leaders and a list of members of the circle (indicating the class in the 1988/89 academic year and the final grades for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). All materials on the organization of circles and an envelope for a response about the admission of a circle to 3FTSH with a return address addressed to one of the leaders of the circle should be sent to 3FTSH (141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTSH, indicating “Circle”) before May 25, 1988. (Notebooks with the work of members of the circle are not sent to 3FTS.) The work of the head of correspondence physics and technology clubs can be paid for by schools upon the proposal of 3FTS at MIPT as elective classes.

3FTS students and heads of physics and technology clubs will benefit from assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as the recommended 3FTS solutions to these tasks. The assignments contain theoretical material and development of typical problems and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 assignments for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH and its branches, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

From Moscow schools, classes in physics and mathematics are held twice a week according to the 3FTSH program in evening advisory centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out or according to a decisive educational agreement for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). Introductory task Physics

1. A car traveled from one city to another in t hours at a speed v₁. He traveled back for t hours at a speed of v₂, and the rest of the way at a speed of v₃. Determine the average speed of movement along the entire route.

2. Two bicycles are traveling at a speed of 35 km/h. One of them sets a speed of up to 45 km/h, travels at this speed for 10 km, turns and, without slowing down, returns to the other bicycle, which moves at the same speed. How much time passed from the moment the first cyclist went ahead until he returned to his partner?

3. The rise of a body in air must have the value P. The rise of the same body in a liquid of density ρ₀ must have the value P₁. What is the density of the substance from which the body is made?

4. 1.5 kg of ice is in a heat-insulated state at a temperature of 0 °C. 1 liter of boiling water at a temperature of 100 °C is poured into a vessel. What temperature will be established in the vessel? The specific heat capacity of water is 4.2 kJ/(kg °C), the specific heat of melting of ice is 335 kJ/kg.

5. Cold (10 °C) water was poured into the casstro and placed on the electric water supply. After 10 minutes the water began to boil. How long will it take for it to completely evaporate? The specific heat capacity of water is 4200 J/(kg °C), the specific heat of evaporation is 2.26·10⁶ J/kg.

6. Freely falling without the initial speed of the body in the last second the protein travels 3/4 of the entire way. How long did it take for the body to fall?

7. Find the minimum period of a satellite of a planet with a density of 3000 kg/m³.

8. When two heaters are connected in parallel with voltage U₁, power P₁ and P₂ are allocated to them. What power will be released by each of these heaters if they are connected in series with voltage U₂? The resistance of the heaters does not change.

9. A parachutist weighing 80 kg descends on a parachute at a set speed of 5 m/s. What will be the set speed if a small person weighing 40 kg descends on the same parachute? The drag force is proportional to the square of the speed.

10. A billiard ball hits exactly the same ball at rest. Provision of absolute control (no energy consumption during impact). At what angle can the balls fly apart?

11. When the gas was heated at a constant volume by 1 °C, the pressure increased by 0.2%. At what initial temperature is the motor vehicle located on a curved road with a radius of 80 m, if the coefficient of friction between the tires of the motor vehicle and the asphalt is 0.5?

13. Some quantities of gas are heated from 300 K to 400 K. In this case, the volume of gas changes in proportion to the temperature: V = aT. The initial volume of gas is 3 l, the pressure changes at the end of the process, turning out to be equal to 1 atm. How much work was done by the gas in this process?

14. The 120 liter vessel is divided into two parts by a thin movable partition. Two moles of water are placed in the left one, and two moles of nitrogen are placed in the right one. The temperature is maintained at 100 °C. Determine the volume of the right side of the vessel.
