Mathematics
Written exam

1. Calculation
$$\frac{(3,2-1,7):0,003}{\left(\frac{29}{35}-\frac{3}{7}\right) \cdot 4:0,2} - 2,5.$$
2. Simplify
$$\left(\sqrt[3]{a^2+b^3+a+b^2}\right) \frac{\sqrt[3]{a^4-b^3+b^2}\sqrt{a}-ab}{ab^{-1}+b-b^4a^{-1}-b^2}.$$
3. Find $tg^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)$ if $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{1}{3}$.
4. Find the sum of the first twenty odd natural numbers.
5. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x-4}=2\sqrt{x}.$$
6. Solve the equation
$$\sin(x+30^\circ)-\sin(x+210^\circ)=2\sin 495^\circ$$
if $90^\circ<x<180^\circ$.
7. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\log_x\sqrt{5}}=-\log_x5.$$
8. The third term of the geometric progression is 9/16, and the second is 64 times greater than the fifth. Find its denominator.
9. Solve the inequality
$$3^{2x-3}-9^x+27^{2x/3}<675.$$
10. Two circles of radii 12 cm and 7 cm with centers at points $O_1$ and $O_2$ are given on the plane, touching a certain line at points $M_1$ and $M_2$ and lying on one side of this line. It is known that $O_1O_2:M_1M_2= \sqrt{5}/2$. Find $M_1M_2$.
11. Solve the inequality
$$|x|>2\sqrt{x^2-2x+1}.$$
12. Find the quantities $A=\sqrt{x_1x_2}+\sqrt{y_1y_2}$, where $(x_k,y_k)$, $k=1,2$ are solutions of the system
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}}+\sqrt{\frac{y}{x}}=\frac{41}{20}, \\ x+y=41. \end{array} \right.$$
13. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{\log_2^2 x+4\log_2 x}<\sqrt{2}(4-\log_{16}x^4).$$
14. The volume of the cone is 7π. Find the volume of a regular quadrilateral line in a cone.
15. Define perpendicularity of a line and a plane.
16. State the Pythagorean theorem.
17. Derive the formula for the sine of a multivariate argument.

Physics
Written exam
Option 1
1. In a certain region of space, uniform constant magnetic and electric fields ($B=0,3$ T, $E=300$ kV/m) are created. The proinon moves perpendicular to the fields, without deviating from the straight trajectory. Find the speed of its movement.

2. A farsighted person begins to sharply distinguish the next object from a distance of one meter. What optical power does he need?

3. There is a block at a distance $l$ from the edge of the table. The friction coefficient between the block and the table is $\mu$. Exactly the same second block hits the block with a speed of $v$ and only this one hits the edge of the table. At what distance from the edge of the table will the block hit the floor if the height of the table is $H$? At what minimum speed will the block hit before it falls off the table? Dimensions of the bar compared to $l$ pretenbrets. The impact is considered to be absolutely elastic.

4. In a cylindrical vessel under a very light piston there is $m=3$ kg of water at $t=20^\circ\text{C}$. When water was heated, a heat amount of $Q=1017$ kJ was reported. To what height does the piston rise if the atmospheric pressure is $p=10^5$ Pa and the cross-sectional area of ​​the vessel is $S=3$ dm²? Specific heat of vaporization of water $r=2,26 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg, universal gas constant $R=8,31$ J/(mol K). The change in the volume of water during evaporation, as well as its warm distribution, pretenbrets.

5. Capacitor $C$ is charged and disconnected from the battery. Another capacitor with a capacity of $C_1(C_1 \neq C)$ is connected to this capacitor, then the capacitors are separated with an energy of $w=200$ MeV. Electron charge $e=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, Avogadro's number $N_A=6 \times 10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$.

2. The balls are connected to each other by identical threads so that a regular triangle is formed. The system lies on a smooth horizontal table. What charges of equal magnitude and sign must be placed on the balls so that the plane of the triangle doubles in size? Thread stiffness $k$, initial length $l$.

3. Attaching a permanent shunt resistance to the amperor increases the current measurement limit by 3 times. Another shunt resistance increases the measurement limit by 7 times. How many times will the amperator limit increase if both of these resistances, previously connected in series, are used as a shunt?

4. The light source is located on the main optical axis of the coincapating line with a focal length of $F=30$ cm. The distance from the source to the line is $d=90$ cm. A flat grain is placed behind the line perpendicular to the optical axis; at what distance from the line should it be placed so that the rays reflected from the grain are lost absolutely through the line and form a parallel beam?
