Kyiv State University
them. T. G. Shevchenko

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. The height of a regular triangular prim is equal to $h$, and its lateral edge is inclined to the plane of the base of the prim at an angle $\varphi$. Find the volume of the prima.

2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_3 x + 1 \geq 3 \log_2 x$$.

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{\frac{1 + \sin x}{6}} + \sin x = 0.$$

4. The side of a rhombus $ABCD$ is equal to $a$. The points $M$ and $N$ are respectively the midpoints of its side $AB$ and $CD$, $E$ is the interior point of the side $BC$, and the segments $AE$ and $MN$ intersect at the point $O$. The ratio of the area of ​​the quadrilateral $OECN$ to the area of ​​the rhombus $ABCD$ is $k$ and $\cos A = \frac{4k - 1}{3}$. Find the segment $ED$. For each value of $k$ does a rhombus that satisfies the conditions of the problem exist?

5. Find the sum of all those seven-digit numbers that are divisible by 15 and written only in digits 0.1.

Option 2
(radiophysics faculty)

1. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{25}(6 - x)\log_5 x < 1$$.

2. Solve the equation
$$\text{tg} x = \text{tg}^3\left(\frac{\pi}{4} - x/2\right).$$

3. Two vessels are filled with liquids whose densities are $d$ and $D$. Moreover, they weigh $q$ and $Q$ kg, respectively, including the weight of the vessels themselves. Find the ratio of the volumes of the vessels if the weight of each of them is the same part of the weight of the liquid contained in it.

4. A ball of radius 2 touches all edges of a triangular base. The center of the ball lies inside the prim at its height and is located at a distance $2\sqrt{3}$ from the top. Prove that the primacy is correct. Find the height of the prim.

5. The material point $M_1$ moves with a speed of 2 m/s along the axis $ox$ in the negative direction, and the material point $M_2$ moves with a speed of 3 m/s along the straight line $y = \sqrt{3}x$ in the direction of the vector $(1, \sqrt{3})$. At the initial moment of time, point $M_1$ had coordinates $(5; 0)$, and point $M_2$ had coordinates $(-\frac{3}{2}; -\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2})$. At what point in time will the distance between points be minimal?

Option 3
(chemistry department)

1. From 40 tons of ore, 20 tons of metal containing 6% impurities are smelted. What percentage of impurities is contained in the ore if the metal does not get into the slag formed during smelting?

2. Solve the equation
$$\sin 2x - \cos 2x = 1 - 2(\cos x - \sin x).$$

3. Solve the inequality
$$2 \log_2^2 |x - 1| + \log^2 \frac{(x - 1)^2}{8} \leq 9.$$

4. The sum of the eleven initial terms of a geometric progression is 20% of the sum of its subsequent eleven terms. How many times is the first progression less than the eleventh term?

5. These are two correct ideas with equal grounds. The angle of inclination of the side rib to the plane of the base in the first of them is twice as large as the angle of inclination of the side rib to the plane of the base in the second, and the volume of the first prim is 4 times greater than the volume of the second. Find the angle of inclination of the side rib to the plane of the base in the first priming.

Option 4
(library faculty)

1. Solve the equation
$$\sin \frac{x}{2} + \cos x = 1.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_0.5 \frac{x - 4}{2x + 5} > 1.$$

3. The base of the triangle is $a$. Find the length of the segment that bisects the plantain and is parallel to the base.

4. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} x + y + z = 18, \\ y + z + x + y = 30, \\ z + x + y + z = 24. \end{cases}$$

5. The dihedral angle at the base of a regular quadrangular base is $a$, and the volume of the base is $v$. Find the area of ​​the side face of the prim.
