Physics
Oral Exam Objective

1. The projectile breaks at the top point of its trajectory at a height of $h=320$ m into two parts, the masses of which are in the ratio 1:2. $\tau=4$ s after the explosion, the heavier part falls to the ground below where the explosion will occur. At what distance from the place of the shot will the lighter part of the projectile fall if the heavier part fell at a distance of $s=1200$ m? Neglect air resistance.

2. A vertical pipe with a piston is fixed in a reservoir so that its lower end is immersed in water. The piston, which initially lay on the surface of the water, is slowly raised by a special device to a height of $H=15$ m. What work does the device do? Piston area $S=10^{-2}$ cm²; atmospheric pressure $p_0=10^5$ Pa; water density $\rho=10^3$ kg/m³; The mass of the piston can be neglected.

3. Two identical balls are tied with a non-pulling thread and thrown over a non-pulling block, with one of the balls immersed in a container with liquid. At what steady-state speed will the balls move if it is known that the steady-state speed of falling of a single ball in the same liquid is $v_0$? Assume that the resistance force to the movement of a ball in a liquid is proportional to its speed; fluid density $\rho_0$; density of ball materials $\rho$; training in the block can be ignored.

4. A gas filling a volume of $V=0,1$ m³ at a pressure $p=10^5$ Pa expands isobarically. At the same time, its abscissa temperature increased by 2 times, and the internal energy changed by $\Delta U=26$ kJ. What mass of coal needed to be compressed for this if $\eta=20\%$ the amount of heat released during compaction was spent on heating the gas? Specific heat of removal $q=30$ MJ/kg.

5. A ball of mass $m=2$ g and charge $q=10^{-6}$ C, suspended on a dielectric thread $l=1$ m long, oscillates in the vertical plane. A single-pod horizontal power field is created in the oscillation plane. The angle formed by the extreme positions of the threads during oscillations is $2\alpha=90^\circ$, and the angle between the vertical positions is $\beta=15^\circ$. Determine the potential difference between the extreme points of the ball's trajectory.

6. How much heat will be released in the resistor after the switch is closed (Fig. 1)? Neglect internal source resistance, wire resistance and losses associated with electromagnetic radiation.

7. The electron is deposited into a single-pod magnetic field (Fig. 2). At point $A$ it has a speed $v$, which makes an angle $a$ with the direction of the field. In what direction of the magnetic field inductance value can the electron be at point $C$? The electron $e$, its mass $m$, distance $AC=L$, vector $\vec{v}$ and segment $AC$ lie in a plane parallel to the field.

8. A parallel beam of light with width $l$, containing two spectral components with wavelengths $\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_2$, is incident on a plane-parallel glass plate at an angle $a$. The stack guess exponent for these wavelengths are different and are $n_1$ and $n_2$ respectively. Determine the minimum plate thickness at which light passing through the plate will propagate in the form of two separate beams, one of which contains only one spectral component.

9. At a distance of $d=80$ cm from the scattering line with a focal length of $F=20$ cm, an accurate light source is located on its main optical axis. The lens was moved in a direction perpendicular to the optical axis. Determine where and how much you need to move the source so that its image is in place.

The kinetic energy of deuterium and tritium nuclei before the reaction is not taken into account.
