Are you really familiar with this?

The development of physics is accompanied by the establishment of a variety of conservation laws, which state that in isolated systems certain quantities cannot arise or disappear. The idea that such laws exist arose in the mists of time: the saying of Lucrenius given in the epigraph reflects ancient views. Today, physicists know quite a lot of such laws, some of them are familiar to you - these are the laws of conservation of implus, energy, zarad. Further study of physics allows us to learn that there are very uneducated laws of conservation, for example, strangeness, quadruplicity and focality. But first, let’s deal with those that you should know well.
