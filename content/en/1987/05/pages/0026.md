Problem solutions

M1021. Allupinst wants to climb a cliff 1000 m high. After a new climb in a ladder at the foot of the cliff, he can climb, sewing a rope, at a speed of 40 meters per hour, and after a cold night on the rock - 30 meters per hour. He climbs the finished rope at a speed of 400 meters per hour. In how many days can he reach the top if he works on the rock (including climbing the rope) for six hours a day? (The time of descent and other operations is not taken into account.)

P1053. A uniform elastic string slides inside a smooth pipe (in the Earth’s gravitational field). The sections $AB$ and $BC$ of the pipe are semicircles of radius $R$, the points $A, B, C$ are on the same vertical (see figure Pic. 1), the length of the string is $l=2\pi R$. Find all the points of the string where the tension is zero at the moment when the string’s lower extremity is at the point $C$.

I. Yu. Poteryaiko

P1054. Two rods of identical shape are made from materials with different elasticity properties: Young’s modulus of the material equals $E_1$ and $E_2$ respectively. The rods are glued together end to end and covered with a coat of the same paint, so that the sample obtained looks like a uniform rod. What value of Young’s modulus for the “material” constituting the rod will be measured by the unsuspecting experimentator, who is unaware that the rod is not uniform?

V. A. Davydov

P1055. The electric apparatus II is connected to a source of alternating current of 220 V via a capacitor of $C=0.5$ µF (see figure Fig. 2). The ammeter shows a current of $I=0.01$ A, the woltmeter shows $U=180$ V. Find the power from the source used by the apparatus. The ammeter and voltmeter are assumed ideal.

A. R. Zilberman

P1056. In the circuit shown on figure 3 the switch $K_3$ is turned on at first, while the switches $K_1$ and $K_2$ are turned off. At a certain moment $K_1$ is switched on, and in $t_1=0.1$ seconds later so is $K_2$. In another $t_2=0.2$ seconds $K_3$ is switched off. Find: 1) the maximum tension on the capacitor; 2) the current through the coil $L_1$ in time $t_3=1$ s after the switch $K_1$ was turned on. The resistance of the wires may be neglected, the diodes assumed ideal; $L_1=1$H, $L_2=0.5$H, $C=10$µF, $U_0=10$V.

A. R. Zilberman

P1057. A weight for bodybuilding, consisting of two solid spheres of mass $m$ each joined by a light rod of length $2b$, hangs horizontally on two inelastic wires of length $l$ whose distance is $2a$ (see figure Fig. 4). Find the period of small rotational oscillations of the weight.

M. M. Tsypin

Problem solutions

M1021 — M1025; F1033 — F1037

Answer: in five days.

Let $x_n$ denote the highest altitude to which a climber can climb in $n$ days ($n=1, 2, \dots$). If by the end of one day he reaches a height of $h$ meters, the next day he can rise to a height of $h+6\cdot 30=h+180$ meters if he remains insensible on the rock, and to a height of $h+\left(6-\frac{h}{400}\right)40=h+240-h/10$ meters if he goes down insensible. It is clear that the first option is more profitable if $180>240-h/10$, that is, $h>600$, the second - if $h\leq 600$. Thus,

$$x_{n+1}=\begin{cases} x_n+240-x_n/10, & x_n\leq 600, \\ x_n+180, & x_n\geq 600. \end{cases}$$
