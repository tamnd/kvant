Problem 4. From all triangles ABC with a given base BC and a given vertex angle A, find the triangle that has the largest median drawn to the base.

"Solution". Let ABC be an equilateral triangle and A'BC an arbitrary triangle that shares a common base BC with triangle ABC and an angle A' equal to angle A (Fig. 4).
Then point A' lies on arc BAC of the circle circumscribed about triangle ABC. Let us denote the center of this circle by O and the midpoint of side BC by D. Mediana AD of an equilateral triangle ABC is its axis of symmetry. Therefore, point O lies on AD and, therefore, AD=AO+OD. From triangle A'OD we have A'D<A'O+OD=AO+OD=AD. This means that of all triangles ABC with a given base BC and a constant angle A at the vertex, the equilateral triangle has the largest median.
