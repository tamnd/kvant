3. At 12 o'clock, a car and a truck, respectively, left the cities $A$ and $B$ following each other at constant speeds. On the way from $B$ to $A$, the truck stopped for more than an hour to load, after which, continuing to move at the same speed, it met the passenger car at 14:12. Determine the time it takes a truck to arrive in the city $A$, if it is known that the speed of the truck is less than the speed of the passenger car, and that each car on the way from $A$ to $B$ spends an integer number of hours.
