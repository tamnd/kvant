Time to create
time to wood!

Our dear readers and schoolchildren! A significant event has occurred in your life. After a long period of active preparation, the 20th Congress of the Komsomol took place. It opened a new era in the history of the Komsomol and became a good practical school for perestroika and democratization of one of the most massive public organizations in our country. We must think hard about the materials of the congress, they must be carefully studied and discussed in groups. After all, they touch on all aspects of the lives of young people and subject them to open, pure analysis.

Our Komsomol has considerable merits, marked by high awards from the Motherland. But it was not spared the elements of stagnation, biocratization, and formalism that we have encountered in recent decades, both in the economy and in social activities. Without the necessary conditions to independently overcome these negative phenomena, many of you lost social activity, became isolated in a circle of personal interests, and tried to adapt to the surrounding activities.

The decisions of the congress, as they are practically implemented, will lead to a radical renewal of all aspects of the activities of the Komsomol. They recognize you for activity, self-efficacy, self-government. After all, you have to participate in the revolutionary renewal of socialist society. Think, argue, search, propose, insist, achieve, in a word, join in the open struggle for the improvement of our Soviet school, our socialist society. Master knowledge, expand your horizons, strengthen your will, discipline your character. Persistent study is the key to successful work in the future of life. But don’t cram, but try to understand the material you’re studying and find your interest in each school subject.
