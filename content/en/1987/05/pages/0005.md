⟦folio 3⟧

It is clearly stated about this: “The acquisition of knowledge is directed work. However, in the creation of pupils and students there is still a conviction that the period of study is only an order for work, it is not life itself, but preparation for it.” This wrong belief must be put to rest in peace and forever.

The report of the Komsomol Central Committee also set the following task: “Let’s, comrades, get down to something new for us - searching for talents. After all, this is a careless asset of society.” Talents awaken early, even at the school desk. But you can’t notice them, support them, or nurture them. This is exactly what our «Kvant» strives for, awakening interest in physical and mathematical sciences and trying to help the independent development of young talents.

Elect as Komsomol members those who are talented, honest, hardworking, accepting, and who can act in the interests of the student body. Choose and support them with your initiative, specific activities that are interesting to you. The time of constant compromisers and adherents, dodgers and Tunyads is becoming a thing of the past.

Turn to Lenin more often, to his works of the subsequent period. Answers to many questions about the place of youth and the Komsomol in the life of society can still be found today in his speech at the III Congress of the RKSM, in the central idea of ​​this speech that the main task of the Youth Union is to learn communism.

You have an unusually interesting and difficult life ahead. Remember that the time is not far when your captivity will bear responsibility for the future of socialism, for the destinies of man. Speaking at the congress, General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee M. S. Gorbachev said, addressing the youth: “The development of civilization today is building the line between the possible and the impossible. You have at your disposal such colossal scientific and technical power and intellectual potential, which have not been in the hands of any generation of the human race that has lived on Earth... And, taking possession of this power, remember that you are creating the future.”
