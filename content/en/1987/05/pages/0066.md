Our cover
(see «Kvant» No. 4, 4th page of cover)

Let's number the ball prims as shown in figure 6. Sequentially roll the prims 7, 2, 2, 1, 1, 4, 4, 6, 6, 10, 11, 11, 8, 8, 2, 2, 1, 4, 6, 10, 10, 11, 11, 8, 2, 7, observing the general direction of movement clockwise around the circle marked with asterisks. We get a cyclic permutation 1→2→8→11→10→6→4→1. Similarly, you can get a symmetrical “cycle” 2→3→5→9→12→11→7→2. These two cycles allow you to obtain any distribution of colors of the prims.
