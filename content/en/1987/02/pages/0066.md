3. $\frac{a^3 \sqrt{6}}{2}, \frac{a^3 \sqrt{15}}{4}$. Solution. Let us choose the vectors $\vec{AB} = \vec{m}, \vec{AC} = \vec{n}, \vec{BC}' = \vec{p}$ as a basis and denote by $t$ the unknown cosine of the angle between the vectors $\vec{m}$ and $\vec{n}$. The table shows the scalar products of vectors of this basis. Let $B'H$ be the height of the prism. Then $B'H = \vec{BH} - \vec{BB'} = x\vec{m} + y\vec{n} - (\vec{m} - \vec{n} + \vec{p}) = (x - 1)\vec{m} + (y + 1)\vec{n} - \vec{p}$. Independent $x, y, t$ are found from the system of equations, obtained from the condition: $B'H = \sqrt{3} \cdot BH, BH \cdot \vec{m} = 0, B'H \cdot \vec{n} = 0$. This system has two solutions: $x = 1, y = -1, t = 0$ and $x = -\frac{1}{2}, y = -1, t = -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$, from which two possible values ​​for the volume of the prism are found.

5. a) $\frac{a \sqrt{15}}{3}$; 6) $\frac{a \sqrt{10}}{4}$. Solution. Let $O$ be the center of the base of the examples. We form a basis from the vectors $\vec{OS} = \vec{m}, \vec{OB} = \vec{n}, \vec{OC} = \vec{p}$. These vectors are mutually perpendicular in pairs, and their lengths are easy to find; $|\vec{m}| = \frac{a \sqrt{7}}{\sqrt{2}}, |\vec{n}| = |\vec{p}| = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}$. Let us define the position of the points $M$ and $N$ on the segments $BD$ and $SC$ by the equalities $\vec{OM} = x\vec{n}$ $(-1 \leq x \leq 1)$ and $\vec{SN} = y \cdot \vec{SC} = y(\vec{p} - \vec{m}) (0 \leq y \leq 1)$. Then $\vec{MN} = (1 - y)\vec{m} - x\vec{n} + y\vec{p}$. Since straight line $MN$ is parallel to plane $SAD, MN = z \cdot \vec{SA} + t \cdot \vec{SD} = -(z + t)\vec{m} - t\vec{n} - z\vec{p}$. Comparing two expansions of the vector $\vec{MN}$ into the basis $(\vec{m}, \vec{n}, \vec{p})$, we arrive at the system of equations $1 - y = -z - t, x = t, y = -z$. Let's express $y, z$ and $t$ through $x$: $y = \frac{x + 1}{2}, z = -\frac{x + 1}{2}, t = x$. Therefore, $\vec{MN} = \frac{1}{2}((1 - x)\vec{m} - 2x\vec{n} + (x + 1)\vec{p})$, hence $MN = \frac{a}{2} \sqrt{6x^2 - 6x + 4}$. If $DM:DB = 1:3$, then $x = -\frac{1}{3}, MN = \frac{a \sqrt{15}}{3}$. $MN$ takes its smallest value at $x = \frac{1}{2}$. This value is $\frac{a \sqrt{10}}{4}$.
