5. $-\frac{1}{2} \leq a \leq \frac{2}{3}$. Solution. Expressing $x$ from the first equation of the original system and substituting it into the second, we obtain an equivalent system

$$x = by - az^2,$$

$(b+2)(2b-3)y = 2abz^2 + 8z + 8$.

If in $b \notin \{-2; \frac{3}{2}\}$, then the system is consistent for any value of $a$ (it is enough to take $z=0$). If $b = -2$, then the system has a solution if and only if it has roots of the equation $-4az^2 + 8z + 8=0$, that is, for $a \geq -\frac{1}{2}$. Similarly, for $b = \frac{3}{2}$, we obtain that $a \leq \frac{2}{3}$.

$$QS^2 = RS^2 + RQ^2 - 2RS \cdot RQ \cdot \cos 60^\circ = 28,$$

$$PO^2 = PQ^2 - \left(\frac{QS}{2}\right)^2 = 49.$$

So, the required volume of the primer is equal to

$$\frac{1}{3} PO \cdot QT \cdot QR \cdot \sin 120^\circ = 28\sqrt{3}.$$
