⟦folio 38⟧

WHAT THEY CAN ABOUT
TELL THE NUMBERS

Doctor of Pedagogical Sciences
N. A. RODINA

How much energy do you think a student spends on homework? This question will probably confuse many readers: is it possible to count this energy? How? We answer: it is possible; here is the answer: on average about 500 kJ. And this is how we received this answer. We opened on page 118 a book with such a seemingly prosaic title - “Handbook of Physics and Technology”, and...

Further actions, reader, will become clear to you if you open this book. And we think that if you do this, you will no longer be able to open your eyes from its pages, although the main content of the book is tables, graphs, and numbers. “Numbers also have their own language, the ability to speak, to tell about various phenomena and natural phenomena...” It is impossible not to agree with these words of the book’s author, Anatoly Sergeevich Enochovich. The numbers will tell us a lot of new and interesting things...

You will learn, for example, that the length of the ciliate slipper is 300 micrometers, and the length of the Nile River - the longest river on Earth - is 6700 km, that the distance between telegraph poles is 50-60 m (Table No. 19).
