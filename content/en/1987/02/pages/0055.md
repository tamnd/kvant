Option 5
(faculty of biology)

1. Find the most optimal negative coordinate equation
$$\sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) + \cos \left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \sqrt{2}.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$2^x - 1 < 6 \cdot 2^{-x}.$$
3. Two ski jumps from the linnistra at the same time at constant speeds along the same route, with the speed of the first ski jump being 7/6 of the speed of the second. Following him 20 minutes later, a third skier set off, which, moving at a speed of 18 km/h, caught up with the second skier 30 minutes faster than the first. What is the speed of the first skier?
4. In triangle $ABC$, the length of side $AC$ is 3 $\sqrt{3}$, the length of side $BC$ is $\sqrt{13}$. Point $M$ lies on side $AC$, point $N$ lies on side $BC$, and lines $MN$ and $AB$ are parallel. The segment $MC$ is 2 times longer than the segment $AM$. The visector of angle $BAC$ intersects line $MN$ at point $R$, lying between points $M$ and $N$, and the radius of the circle circumscribed about triangle $AMR$ is $\sqrt{6} + 3 \sqrt{3}$. Find the length of side $AB$.
5. Find all values ​​of $b$, for each of which the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2x^2 - 2xy + 10y^2 = b^4 - 6b^3 + 9b^2 - 19 + \sqrt{85}, \\ x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2 = 4 \end{cases}$$ has at least one solution.

Option 6
(faculty of postal science)
1. Solve the equation
$$\sin x = 2 \sin \left( x - \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\log_5 (1-x) < \log_5 (x+3).$$
3. When two pumps of different power operate simultaneously, the pool is filled with water in 8 hours. After repairing the pumps, the productivity of the first one increased by 1.2 times, and the second by 1.6 times, and with both pumps operating simultaneously, the pool began to fill in 6 hours. How long will it take to fill the pool with the first pump after renovation?
4. Find the planar triangle formed by the x-axis and two tangents drawn from the point $A(0; 3)$ to the graph of the function $y = \frac{1}{2} x^2 - x + 5$.
5. In triangle $ABC$, point $M$ bisects side $BC$, and point $K$ lies on side $AC$, and the length of segment $AK$ is 4 times less than the length of side $AC$. Segments $AM$ and $BK$ intersect at point $O$. The length of the segment $AM$ is 5, the length of the segment $BK$ is 10. Find the length of the segment $MK$ if the angle $AOB$ is 135°.

Option 7
(Zeodraphic Faculty)
1. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x-1}{x^2+6x-4} > \frac{1}{6}.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\log_x^2 \sqrt{5} + \log_x \frac{1}{125} = \frac{11}{8}.$$
3. Three tanks of the same volume begin to be filled with water at the same time, and the first tank receives 120 liters of water per minute, and the second - 40. It is known that at the initial moment the first tank is empty, and the volume of water in the third tank is half that in the second, and that all three tanks will be filled at the same time. How many liters of water flow into the third tank in one minute?
4. In a convex quadrilateral $ABKC$, the length of side $AB$ is $\sqrt{3}$, the length of diagonal $BC$ is 1, and the angles $ABC$, $BKA$ and $BKC$ are 120°, 30° and 60° respectively. Find the length of side $BK$.
5. For each value of $a$ satisfying inequality $0 < a < 1$, find the smallest value of the expression $\frac{1}{2} (x^2 + y^2) - a (x-y)$ given $\sin (\pi xy) = 0$.

Option 8
(Zeodraphic Faculty)
1. Solve the equation
$$\sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \sin x.$$
2. The side lengths $AB$, $BC$ and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$ form an arithmetic progression in that order. Find the ratio of the height of the triangle $ABC$ dropped from the vertex $A$ to the radius of the inscribed circle.
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{2 \cdot 6^x - 4^x - 15}{6^x - 9^x - 5} = 3.$$
4. The student construction team equipment is a direct sports ground with an area of ​​0.1 g, installed on the opposite longer sides - stands, and on the other two sides - a wire mesh. The cost of installing one linear meter of stands and netting is equal to the corresponding 7 rubles. and 3 rub. 820 rubles were spent on the installation of stands and nets. Find the lengths of the sports ground builder.
5. Let $a > 0$, $b > 0$. Find solutions to the inequality
$$\sqrt{\frac{1}{x^2} - \frac{1}{a^2}} > \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{b}.$$
6. For all values ​​of the parameter $p \leq 9$, find solutions to the equation
$$3 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{tg} \left( \frac{\pi}{15} \sin x - \frac{3\pi}{5} \right) \cdot \sin \left( \frac{2\pi}{7} \sin^2 x + \frac{3\pi}{14} \right) + \cos^2 \left( \frac{5\pi}{14} - \frac{\pi}{7} \cos (2x) \right) = 6 \mathrm{tg}^2 \left( \frac{\pi}{15} \sin x + \frac{2\pi}{5} \right) - p$$ on the segment $[0; 2\pi]$.
