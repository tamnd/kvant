It seems that commas are independent - rolling the cube on a plane. But if you combine it with the well-known Cera “15”, you will get a qualitatively new interesting Cera-year-breaker. To do this, the square fics in cerra “15” must be replaced with equal cubes, still leaving one space free; The “move” is that one cube is rolled over an edge onto a free one to obtain different types of “turnovers” - this is what A. Dremov and G. Shevchova called their version of this cerra. They are "reversible" and are shown at figure. Each cube here is painted in 6 colors similar to Rubik's cubes; There are square protrusions made on the edges, which, together with the sills glued to the bottom of the box, support rolling and also allow you to see the color not only of the top, but also of the place. Of course, the cubes need to be marked in one way or another; Now you can solve a problem typical of all puzzles of this kind (the same cerrei “15”, Rubik’s cube, “Vashilon Tower”)... - a great many of them have appeared in recent years): find a chain of moves that transforms one given arrangement of cups into another. By changing the size of the cubes, the dimensions and shape of the field, you can change the side faces of each cube. We will consider the arrangement of cups to be “proper” when all the cubes have faces of the same color facing the same direction, and the bottom corner is empty; there are 24 such locations (how much?). How many moves do you have to get from one correct position to another? (We know the solution in 100 moves.)
