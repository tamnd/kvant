⟦folio 37⟧

Tasks

1. I have three buckets at home, each of which together is a whole number of liters. If you pour the full first bucket of water into the second, then the water will take up 2/3 of its volume, and if you pour the full first bucket into the third, then the water will take up 3/4 of its volume. However, I filled a three-liter barrel with water, first pouring out the first bucket, then the second, then the third bucket, but the barrel was not yet full. How many more liters of water could you pour into it?

2. Solve the arithmetic puzzle shown at figure. In it, different letters correspond to different numbers, and identical numbers correspond to the same ones.

3. In the UK and USA, temperature is usually measured using the Fahrenheit scale, which defines the melting point of ice as 32° and the boiling point of water as 212°. Is there a temperature at which the number of degrees Celsius and Fahrenheit will be the same?

4. The Chief of the Secret Service draws up the following mutual instructions for his seven main agents: Agent 001 follows the one who follows Agent 002, Agent 002 follows the one who follows Agent 003, etc.; agent 007 is watching over those who are watching over agent 001. But suddenly an order came - to hire another agent 008. Will the boss be able to draw up similar instructions for all agents?

5. There is a cube and six identical cross-shaped figures cut out of paper (see figure). The area of ​​each paper figure is equal to the area of ​​one side of the cube. Is it ethically possible to cover the entire surface of a cube with pieces of paper?
