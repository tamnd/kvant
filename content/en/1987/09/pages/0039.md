Who's dancing with whom?

At the ballroom dance competition in the city of Dolgoprudny, the first three places were taken by couples from the dance group "Terpikhora" of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The correspondent of the institute's multi-reflection "For Sciences" Comrade Petrov wanted to know who was dancing with whom, and received the following answers:

I am Svetana. Every girl is younger than her partner by Zgoda

And I am Igor. We are all 115 years old together

My name is Yulia. Together with Igor, I am about 3 years old.

I'm Anton. I and Yulia are 40 years old.

But that’s all, who’s dancing with whom?

I am Makim. The youngest among us is Ira.

Ira is me.

I addressed everyone who is studying mathematics, elementary or higher, and is interested in its field, and I say: “Of course, we will learn to prove, but we will also learn to prove”:

D. Polya

Mathematics should be taught in school with the same goal that the knowledge acquired here can be used for general needs in life.

N. I. Lobachevsky
