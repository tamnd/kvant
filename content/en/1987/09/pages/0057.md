II Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren at MIPT

In «Kvant» No. 11 for 1986, I talk about the first scientific and technical conference of Schoolchildren at MIPT and invited to send abstracts and works to the next conference, scheduled for April 1987. The students responded very quickly. At the beginning of March this year, 220 works from 33 cities of the country were sent to MIPT. Omsk and Lvov, Sverdlovsk and Tula, Kyiv, Chelyabinsk, Tbilisi, Moscow, Saratov, Zaporozhye and many other cities were indicated in the return addresses of large parcels with abstracts, graphs, diagrams and computer programs received by the conference organizer. And so on April 19, 1987, the II Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren was held at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. About 100 participants from the RSFSR, Ukraine, Belarus and Kazakhstan came to it.

After the grand opening of the conference, the sections of mathematics, physics, theoretical physics and computer science began, at which the participants made presentations on their work. Then there was a viewing of “poster reports” - works that were not heard at the sectional sessions.

The Schoolchildren's performances showed the main use of the conference before Olympiads and other competitions: the opportunity to develop a topic of interest for a long time and in depth. The best work came from the guys who were creative and carefully carried out the experiment or who worked through a large amount of literature on the topic of the essay, and not from those who rewrote a chapter from a university textbook in a few days. In addition, speaking at the conference is as close as possible to real scientific work, which requires a creative approach to the topic and a lot of time and labor.

Various physical experiments were demonstrated, including the effectiveness of high-terparity superconductivity, discovered in early 1987, literally two months before the conference.

The conference ended with the loading of active participants with books on physics and mathematics (with the authors' names and wishes), diplomas from MIPT, diplomas and badges from the journal «Kvant». But the main reward for the Schoolchildren was probably self-study at the conference, famous with MIPT - one of the leading universities in the country, and meetings with scientists.

And now the conference organizing committee is waiting for new works. Any student can become a participant in the conference. To do this, you need to write an abstract (7-25 pages), independently analyzing an interesting question in physics, technical mathematics or programming. However, a copy of the abstract must be sent before February 1, 1988 to the address: 141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, organizing committee of the III Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren, the second one must be kept for yourself. All questions on the topics of abstracts and organization of the conference can be found out by calling ZFTSH: 408-51-45.

A. V. Sapozhnikov, member of the conference organizing committee
