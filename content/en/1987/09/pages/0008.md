— How can you comment on the latest achievements in this area?

— Superconductivity, which we are talking about now, is extremely interesting both from the point of view of functional science and, mainly, from the point of view of emerging promising technical applications. Physicists face the difficult task of explaining the nature of the discovered high-temperature superconductivity in new materials. Today it is clear to everyone that it differs from the graft type; there are several alternative hypotheses about the mechanism of the discovered high-temperature superconductivity, but there is no unambiguous solution to the problems yet. It may soon be possible to increase the temperature of transition to the superconducting state by another 100-200 K. Now there is no longer any reason to deny this possibility. But even the available conditions open up unprecedented prospects. In the next few years, maybe five to seven, thanks to the discovered phenomenon, a colossal revolution will take place in many technical areas. But to implement it, special personnel are needed - not those who deal with physical problems, but personnel of chemical technologists who are looking for new materials. Their activities are sometimes even “anti-scientific,” that is, the train of thought of these scientists is incredible from the point of view of the usual scientific approach. As an example, I can cite a joke that the Inland theoretical physicist Hendrik Casimir recently told me. He worked for a long time in the research laboratories of a radio engineering company and is well versed in engineering. This joke, in a sense, reflects the way technologists think. For a long time it was not possible to spray the French on Germany. And then Casimir said that it was necessary to place rhenium as an intermediate layer. The logic behind the choice of Rhine was that... between France and Germany the Rhine flows as a “binding” natural element.

The essence of the matter is that the technicians who create new materials have their own vision, sometimes very strange (from the point of view of a physicist), some properties of the figure, their own logic, which is not clear to everyone, but nevertheless leads to the goal. As in any creativity, especially related to Japanese, here it turns out to be very important, in addition to the lasting, it has some kind of specificity. It must be said that in the West such scientists are, as they say, number one. I think that somewhere in our country, say, in metallurgy, similar unique products are known. But in the field of physics, I find it difficult to name such people. And not because they don’t exist. I'm sure there is. But they are not used, they are not isolated. This is a big flaw in the organization of our science.
