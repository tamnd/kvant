The molecular-kinetic theory of matter, studied in school, originates in ancient times - with the atomistic hypothesis. Having gone through debts and a tolerant path, this theory acquired a legitimate form at the end of the 19th - beginning of the 20th centuries thanks to the works of outstanding scientists - P. Clausus, J. Maxwell, L. Bolshin, A. Einstein, Ya. I. Frenkel. The microcoptic, or molecular, approach to the study of thermal phenomena has proven to be extremely fruitful and allows us to paint an extraordinary picture of the processes occurring in nature and in the creation of the target model. In this issue of Kaleidoscope, we invite you to examine some questions based on the concepts of molecular kinetic theory.
