Small intercosmos

The Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Academy of Sciences of the USSR, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the USSR State Committee for Vocational and Technical Education and the All-Union Society “Knowledge”, together with the USSR Cosmonautics Federation and the Exhibition of the Dostina National Economy of the USSR, held from February to November 1981 the All-Union Competition for School and Professions Students or the Best Space Experiment Project, dedicated to the 20th anniversary of Yu. A. Gagarin’s flight. The competition is called “Small Intercosmos”. It is carried out in order to further develop cognitive activity, imagination, deepen the socially useful direction of scientific and technical creativity of students, prepare them for work, promote the environment of schoolchildren and vocational school students in the Soviet Union and socialist countries in the liberation of outer space.

The competition can be attended by both individual students and teams of young science and technology enthusiasts, which are of interest to specialists and can be implemented in the coming years.

Contest participants can develop and offer:

— scientific experiments with the help of which you can gain new knowledge in astronomy, physics, chemistry, biology, medical geography, geophysics and other sciences;
- technical experiments and proposals aimed at improving existing space technology (the use of new components and accessories, improving the working and living conditions of astronauts at the station, organizing their access), at creating new systems for launching into orbit, energy and communications systems, vehicles for moving astronauts and cargo in outer space and on celestial bodies, as well as at using in space instruments and devices used on Earth;
— technical experiments aimed at creating industrial production in space (welding of metals, growing crystals, producing alloys);
— applied experiments related to using the capabilities of astronautics to solve national economic problems. For example, geological, agriculture and forestry, communications, navigation, environmental protection, meteorology. This group includes proposals for the use of advanced space technology on Earth.

The following may be proposed for the competition:

— ideas for creating new space technology, conducting experiments or research, ensuring their value and feasibility; developed technical proposals or preliminary designs with drawings and descriptions; models, layouts, instruments, designs of experimental devices and systems with the necessary parameters.

Projects eligible for participation in the competition are those that can be implemented in the cabin of a rafted and free spacecraft, in outer space, on the surface of planets or other celestial bodies, projects for mercenary devices associated with an astronaut, proposals for the use in terrestrial conditions of means that are now used only in space conditions.

All works must be submitted unspecified to the Central Organizing Committee by September 20, 1981. Proposals for conducting technical experiments, brief characteristics and photographs of layouts, models, devices are sent to the Central Station of Young Technologies of the Ministry of Education of the RSFSR (103055, Moscow, Tikhvinskaya St., building 39). Ideas and projects of scientific and technical experiments - to the Moscow City Palace according to drawings and schoolchildren (117334, Moscow, Vorobyovskoe highway, building 11). If the idea of ​​a scientific experiment is violated and the corresponding device is available, a description of the device is sent along with a statement of the idea.

The work should be designed so that it can be displayed at an exhibition. This could be an album, a notebook with drawings attached to it, a photograph of a layout or model with a donation of text.

It is necessary to indicate the last name, first name, patronymic, place of residence and occupation of the author (class and number of school, vocational school course, out-of-school institution, section of a scientific society), who was the leader or consultant of the work.

The authors of the best work will be submitted to Moscow for the final stage of the competition, held in November 1981. We are observing interesting proposals prepared for implementation at the present stage, which will be included in the space research program, as well as for demonstration at the Exhibition of Economic Achievements of the USSR.

All participants in the final stage will be awarded student diplomas and badges. Experiments will be organized for them in Star City, the Museum of Cosmonautics, and meetings with cosmonauts and scientists. For each of the areas of the competition, 3 winners are determined. They will be awarded with personalized prizes of Yu. A. Gagarin, K. E. Piolkovsky, S. P. Korolev, M. V. Keldysh, bodies and badges raised in space.
