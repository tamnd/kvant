⟦folio 33⟧

Quests

1. As a result, the image on figure corresponds to different numbers for different letters, and identical numbers for identical letters. Decipher the rebus so that all six equalities (for example, AEE - MKK = MKK) simultaneously become true.
2. All numbers in the series
0, 4, 18, 48, ?, 180,...

obtained using a certain formula. Which one exactly? What number is missing?
3. Write the numbers in the circles at figure so that each next number in the direction of the arrows is obtained from the previous one using the action indicated above the arrow.
4. Prove that the area of ​​an equilateral trapezoid whose diagonals are perpendicular is equal to the square of its height.
5. The natural number M consists of 1980 ones and 1983 twos, and the remaining digits are zeros. Could this number be an exact cube?
6. In rectangles on figure the sum of the numbers in each line is the same. The sums of the numbers in each column are also equal to each other. Try folding each of these rectangles from the domino set.

These tasks were offered to us
E. Rekstin, V. Roshakhovsky,
S. Sefibekov, G. Shishkina
