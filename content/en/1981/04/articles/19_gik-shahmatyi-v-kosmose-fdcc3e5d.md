When the final part of the match in Bagino was postponed, it was analyzed not only on the ground. Cosmonauts Vladimir Kovalenok and Alexander Ivanovkov were on board the Salyut-b space station at that time and asked for their position to be provided. Having placed the pieces on a special chessboard, adapted for playing in zero gravity, they quickly became convinced that Karpov's position was completely won. Finding out the next day that their assessment was confirmed, the cosmonauts sent a congratulatory telegram to the world champion. So Anatoly Karpov received one of the first congratulations on his victory in the match from Space!

The first chess match between Earth and Space took place 10 years ago. The space team was represented by the crew of the Soyuz-9 spacecraft - V. I. Sevastyanov and A. I. Nikolaev, Colonel General N. P. Kamanin and cosmonaut V. V. Gorbako played for the Earth. The cosmic battle continued for several turns and, after a sharp struggle, ended in peace.
