About the formation of a young scientist

Addressing young people, I consider it necessary to discuss the issue of the authority of elders and the independence of young people.

It must be said that this extremely important question arises very early, when a young person chooses a field of activity. This choice should be made according to recognition, and not according to the accepted opinion of the environment, close circles, not in the opinion of parents, not from considerations of representation. Authorities must be respected, attached to their opinions, taking into account them and using their experience, but not following them blindly, if you have the ability to think for yourself, if you have your own opinion. Another thing is that this own opinion should not be a whim or a tribute to the regime, it should be creatively formed and even expressed to young people with natural full and deep consideration of experience, opinion, and advice from people who are authoritative for him.

It must be said that without the ability to think for oneself, without the ability to develop one’s own opinion, without the desire to defend this opinion, it is pointless to even try to choose research work as a field of activity.

But now the choice has been made, the education necessary to begin with has been received, and the young man begins research work. It is extremely important for him now to maintain and further develop the ability to learn, especially
