3. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ there are values ​​$x$ such that the numbers $5^{1+x}+5^1x$, $\frac{a}{2}$, $25^x+25^{-x}$ form an arithmetic progression?

$$\begin{cases} bx+y=ac^2, \\ x+by=ac+1 \end{cases}$$

Option 2
(Faculty of Geology and Geophysics and Faculty of Natural Sciences)

$$\cos^2 x(1-\sin x)=1+\sin^3 x.$$

$$\log_6 2(1+2^{-x})-\frac{x}{1-\log_{\frac{1}{2}}3}=\log_6 a.$$

5. Let $x$ run through the set of solutions to the inequality
$$x^4-4x^2+3\leq 0.$$
What is the maximum value of the expression
$$1+ax-x^2?$$

Physics

Written exam
Faculty of Physics
Each one makes a fortune out of five problems of three types. Five hours were given to solve the problems.
The first three tasks are calculated, of varying difficulty: from standard to comparative, requiring ingenuity, deep knowledge, and the ability to understand a non-privibuous or conventional physical situation.
The fourth task is an assessment task. To solve it, you need to understand the physical phenomenon under consideration, come up with a simple (since only an estimate is needed) physical model of this phenomenon, select reasonable values ​​of physical quantities and, finally, obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. In the text of the problem it was stated that the applicant can choose the physical quantities and their numerical values ​​necessary for solving the problem. The experience of counter exams at NSU shows that a significant proportion of applicants enrolled in the Faculty of Physics cope with the assessment tasks.
The fifth task is a demonstration task, in which one must oblige a physical phenomenon demonstrated in the audience. Here it is important to understand the essential phenomena and environments of various physical factors to highlight the main one.
