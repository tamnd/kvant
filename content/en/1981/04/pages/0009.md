⟦folio 7⟧

Our photo essay is dedicated to the three international space crews who worked on the hospitable Soviet space station Salyut 6 in 1980, hosted by Leonid Popov and Valery Ryumin. These crews included Valery Kubasov and Hungarian cosmonaut Bertalan Farkas (May 26 - June 3), Viktor Gorbatko and Vietnamese cosmonaut Pham Tuan (June 23 - June 31), Yuri Romanenko and Cuban cosmonaut Arnaldo Tamayo Mendez (September 18 - September 26).

Back in 1976, the Soviet Union put forward the idea of ​​joint flights by international space crews of socialist countries. The friendly assistance of our specialists ensured successful accelerated training of foreign space scientists.

As a result of the implementation of an extensive program of scientific and technical research, developed jointly by scientists of the Soviet Union and socialist countries, a lot of valuable information was obtained that will bring enormous assistance to the national economy of the countries of the socialist commonwealth.
