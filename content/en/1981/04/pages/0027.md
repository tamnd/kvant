**M631. Two-digit numbers from 19 to 80 are written out in a row. Is the recipient's number 192021...7980 divisible by 1980?**

Since 1980 = 99 · 20, and 99 and 20 do not have common divisors, it is enough to find out whether the number A = 192021...7980 is divisible by 20 and 99. The divisibility of A by 20 is obvious. Next, note that 100 to any power when divided by 99 leaves a remainder of 1, so the number

$$A = 19 \cdot 100^{61} + 20 \cdot 100^{60} + \dots + 79 \cdot 100 + 80$$

when divided by 99 gives the same remainder as the number

$$B = 19 + 20 + \dots + 79 + 80 = \frac{19 + 80}{2} \cdot 62 = 99 \cdot 31.$$

So, it turns out that the number B is divisible by 99; therefore, the number A is divisible by 99, and at the same time by 1980.

**M632. The cargo, packed in containers, must be delivered to the return Salyut space station. The number of containers is at least 35, the total cargo weight is 18 tons. There are seven Progress transport ships, each of which can deliver 3 tons of cargo into orbit. It is known that these ships can simultaneously deliver any 35 of the available containers. Prove that they can deliver all the available cargo into orbit at onceズ.**

In general, a container weighing more than 0.5 tons is heavy, and a container whose weight does not exceed 0.5 tons is light. Since the total mass of the cargo is 18 tons, the number of heavy containers is less than 18:0.5 = 36, that is, does not exceed 35. Therefore, according to the conditions of the problem, all heavy containers can be loaded onto existing ships (adding, if necessary, light containers to a total number of 35).

After loading heavy containers, only light containers may remain. We will show that all these containers can be loaded into ships one by one. In fact, if at some loading step a container of mass $x < 0,5$ t cannot be loaded onto any of the seven ships, then each ship has already loaded more than (3-x) tons of cargo, in total more than 7(3-x) tons have been loaded, and less than 18-7(3-x) = 7x-3 tons of cargo remain. But then $x < 7x—3$, whence $x > 0,5$, contradicts the assumption. We demand proof.
