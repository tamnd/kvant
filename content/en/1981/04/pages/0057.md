VII All-Russian meeting of young innovators and designers

From August 3 to August 10, 1980, in the Krasnodar Territory in 1963, the first school organization of the All-Union Society of Inventors and Innovators (VOIR) appeared. Now there are over one hundred thousand people of this society in the country.

The next meeting was attended by 285 participants; among them are 2 authors of inventions, 23 participants of VDNKh, 54 laureates of the All-Union Scientific and Technical Creativity Show of Youth, 134 authors of rationalizers of proposals, 218 winners of regional and regional shows, exhibitions, and competitions.

The guys greeted with stormy applause the message that the meeting had received a greeting telephone message from twice Hero of the Soviet Union, coshonaut pilot of the USSR V.V. Gorbatko. The participants of the meeting sent a response telephone to orbit by cosmonaut V.V. Gorbatko and Pham Thuan.

All days at the rally there was a performance at which 350 exopaths were presented.

The introductory hall of the performance was dedicated to models and models related to the life and work of V. I. Lenin. Of great interest was the model of the steam code "Bore", on which V.I. Lenin, after crossing the ice of the Gulf of Finland, sailed to Sweden. Finnish tourists who visited the Leningrad Pioneer Palace spoke about the existence of this parocode; They also sent photographs of this parocode and documents about it. The parocode model was made by Alexander Rozhdestvensky, a student at school No. 163 in Leningrad.

The exposition of the performance consisted of 4 sections: 1) young technicians - school; 2) young innovators and designers - industry; 3) young innovators and designers - agriculture and forestry; 4) young innovators and designers - construction and transport.

The jury reviewed all exhibits of the performance; 62 of the best exhibits were displayed at VDNH; 146 works were allowed to be defended at section meetings. The jury assessed not only the technical solution and design of the submitted works, but also their relevance and expediency, as well as the ability of the authors to present and justify their ideas. Preference was given to works that had been put into practice or had a certificate of invention or rationalization proposal. Let's talk about some of the works.

The USSR State Committee for Inventions and Discoveries issued a certificate for the invention of a group of students from the Omsk Region Young Technical Station. They created an installation for distributing cows, which allows them to automatically feed 1,200 cows within one and a half to two hours according to a predetermined program. Installation of the project in manufacturing and maintenance and will provide significant economic benefits.

The second copyright certificate for the invention was received by school No. 239 in Leningrad, Leonid Zhukovsky. Its device allows you to reduce the waves that form in the swimming pool and interfere with achieving good results. The dimensions and cost of the pool do not increase.

Both inventions have already been put into practice.

It is gratifying to note that hundreds of proposals made by schoolchildren are used in schools, in industry and agriculture.

Thus, in the training and production plant (TPK) No. 1 in Sverdlovsk they not only developed a new universal workbench, but also manufactured 60 such workbenches.
