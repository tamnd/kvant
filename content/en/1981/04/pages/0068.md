DEDICATED TO GAGARIN'S FEAT

Exactly twenty years have passed since the launch of the first spacecraft with a person on board. Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin opened the way to space for man. Ten other heroes have already followed this path, but the feat accomplished by Gagarin does not fade over the years.
Gagarin's flight received a huge response in international philately. Many countries issued postage stamps and blocks with a portrait of Yuri Gagarin and an image of the Vostok-1 spacecraft on which he made his flight. A large number of stamps and blocks dedicated to this topic were issued by postal departments of many countries for the tenth and fifteenth anniversary of the flight (in 1971 and 1976). They continued to come out in subsequent years. Please, there has not been a single year in which new postal signs dedicated to Gagarin’s feat did not appear.
Our selection reproduces postage blocks and stamps issued in the Soviet Union, Polshe, Czechoslovakia, Cuba, the Republic of Equatorial Guinea and the Democratic Republic of Sao Tome and Prisilip after 1975 and dedicated to Yuri Gagarin.

V. Rudov
