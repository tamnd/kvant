Devices for determining the ability to transfer in the area of ​​the station of young technical workers of the Sverdlovsk region have found application in medical institutions of the city of Sverdlovsk. A universal medical kit for the diagnosis and treatment of a number of diseases, designed by the radio engineering circle of the city station of young technical students in the city of Blagoveshchensk, Amur Region, is approved by the medical institute and is used practically. A portable digital anemometer, created by the large electronics station of the young technical staff of the Irkutsk Scientific Center, is used for meteorological research in city schools. Small-sized agricultural machinery manufactured in the Altai, Krasnoars, Stavropol territories, Moscow, Pskov, Lipetsk, Belgorod and Gorky regions is successfully used in school training and experimental sites.

Based on the results of the count, many participants were loaded with various certificates of honor, diplomas and memorable gifts. The best teams, circles, young technical stations, pinoy palaces, and VOIR organizations were also noted.

The level of protection of their work by the students was generally high. The activity of opponents was also high. Let us note the most striking performances of the participants:

Svetlana Kanrova, a student at school No. 3 in Grozny, protected a set of original instruments for performing a physics workshop; Larisa Kosyakova, a student at the Nikolskaya eight-year school in the Rostov region, demonstrated a visual educational aid in chemistry; Anatoly Terentev, a student at school No. 77 in Sverdlovsk, built a universal workbench for school workshops, and Aleksadr Kvarkhava, a student at school No. 75 in the Lazavesky district of the Krasnoars region, built a universal woodworking machine; Alla Ivanova, a student at the Mednovskaya sanatorium boarding school, spoke about the use of natural materials in the design of the school and schoolroom; Alexey Kitchenko, a student of school No. 156 in Novosibirsk, defended the master of geography equipped at the school; Pavel and Pyotr Zubko, students of school No. 14 in Ryazan, protected the depicted training device; Igor Chekin, a student at school No. 28 in Petrozavodsk, spoke about the original design of an electronic watch with a sound alarm; Alexey Kolmogorov, a student at school No. 9 in Sverdlovsk, secured a device to determine the ability to transfer attention; Oleg Kos, student of school No. 95 in Novosibirsk, - a set of radio equipment for working in a circle; Andrey Chelnokov, a student of the Mekon secondary school in the Kurgan region, a universal workbench “Cricket”, which allows you to perform drilling, engraving and polishing work; Arsen Avetisyan, a student at school No. 25 in Pyatgorsk, - a gear-cutting machine for making gears used for models and objects in technical creativity; Tatyana Ivashenko, a student at Olginskaya secondary school No. 2 in the Rostov region, spoke about the development of stamps and technical equipment for making beech from foil (with the help of the created device, all school walls are decorated and orders from various enterprises are fulfilled); The participants were offered a large cultural and educational program. A solemn ceremony of laying flowers at the monument to V.I. Lenin took place. The participants visited the legendary Malyuzemlya, the proud hero Novorossky, and the nearby industrial enterprises of Krasnoars. The competition of fantastic projects was exciting.
