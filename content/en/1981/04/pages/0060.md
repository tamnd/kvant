Chess in space
April 12, 1981 marks twenty years since the anniversary of the first manned space flight. The space flight accomplished by Yuri Gagarin is “captured” in the following diagram.

V. Korolkov, 1961. Checkmate in 5 moves.
The chess pieces contained in the center of the board symbolize the Earth, half of which (the white pieces) are illuminated by the Sun. The spaceship "Vostok" (white queen) makes its historical revolution around the Earth: 1.Fa1+! Kd5 (1... Qc3 2.Q:c3+ Kd5 3.Qb4 Kc6 4.Kc3 and 5.Qb6×) 2.Qh1+! Kd4 3.Qh8+! Kd5 4.Q:a8+! Kd4 5.Qe4×.
Another space exhibit was written by two famous chess composers, one of whom, the late B. Chess is very popular among astronauts. Many of them like to spend their access behind the chessboard. Suffice it to say that the chairman of the USSR Chess Federation is twice Hero of the Soviet Union, USSR pilot V.I. Sevastyanov. The next stage is associated with it, in which each letter B, I, C is a separate task.

E. Pogosyants. B, I, C - checkmate in one move.

The trick of all three positions is that Black's previous move is impossible, that is, the turn to move everywhere belongs to them.
(B) 0...L:b8 1.cbK×!; 0...ab 1.L:a8×; 0...b3 1.c8Q×! (I) 0...Kf7+ 1.R:f7×; 0...Kg6+ 1.L:g6×. (C) 0...Kp:g1 1.Рf1×. Each figure in all three reposes plays a specific role, that is, there is not a single extra in the doxa.

When the final part of the match in Bagino was postponed, it was analyzed not only on the ground. Cosmonauts Vladimir Kovalenok and Alexander Ivanovkov were on board the Salyut-b space station at that time and asked for their position to be provided. Having placed the pieces on a special chessboard, adapted for playing in zero gravity, they quickly became convinced that Karpov's position was completely won. Finding out the next day that their assessment was confirmed, the cosmonauts sent a congratulatory telegram to the world champion. So Anatoly Karpov received one of the first congratulations on his victory in the match from Space!

The first chess match between Earth and Space took place 10 years ago. The space team was represented by the crew of the Soyuz-9 spacecraft - V. I. Sevastyanov and A. I. Nikolaev, Colonel General N. P. Kamanin and cosmonaut V. V. Gorbako played for the Earth. The cosmic battle continued for several turns and, after a sharp struggle, ended in peace.
