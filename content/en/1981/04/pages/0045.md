⟦folio 43⟧

Reader advise

In the general editorial mail «Kvant» there are quite a lot of letters and notes from our readers - students, teachers, just a mathematics lover, which relate to the topic of the “Applicant's Practicum” section.
In cases where private issues are at issue, the editors respond to the authors personally. But a number of letters and notes contain useful comments of a theoretical nature, outline methods for solving certain types of problems, express interesting messages, provide methodological advice, and suggest independently composing exercises. Although these messages and tasks are not always new and original, some of them, in our opinion, are advisable for those who are preparing for university entrance exams.
Not considering it possible to publish such letters and notes in their entirety, the editors decided to prepare another collection of materials, compiled from letters from readers, with the necessary additional comments.

The experience of admission exams shows that geometry problems cause particular difficulties for seniors. Although the problems offered to applicants for their solution require only facts and formulas known from the school course, not everyone can see in a specific drawing for a specific problem which theorems need to be used and where. This can only be learned in one way - enough practice in solving the problem.
It is important to note that when solving problems, memory is automatically enriched with various ideas, connections, techniques, facts - subsequently it is precisely this additional information

The formation will help you cope with new tasks faster.

One of the “least favorite” figures to enter is the trapezoid; traditional trapezoid problems are considered difficult. Our reader V. Varvarkin (Alexandria) suggests reminding applicants of several useful facts regarding the trapezoid.

Let the trapezoid ABCD with bases BC and AD have diagonals intersecting at point O. Then the following statement is true:

— triangle AOB and DOC are equal;

— the product of the areas of triangles AOD and BOC is equal to the product of the areas of triangles AOB and DOC;

- a segment connecting the same bases BC and AD passes through point O;

— point O is the midpoint of a line segment contained inside a trapezoid, passing through the point and parallel to the bases of the trapezoid.

Let us give another useful proposal: if a circle can be written in an isosceles trapezoid, then its diameter is equal to the height of the trapezoid and is the geometric mean of the lengths of the bases of the trapezoid.

We invite the reader to independently list the properties of a trapezoid.

Exercises

1. Through the point of intersection of the diagonals of a trapezoid, a straight line is drawn parallel to its bases. Find the length of the segment of this line enclosed between the sides of the trapezoid if the lengths of its bases are equal to a and b.

2. Let O be the point of intersection of the diagonals of the trapezoid ABCD with the bases BC and AD. Prove that
$$S_{ABCD} = (\sqrt{S_{AOD}} + \sqrt{S_{BOC}})^2.$$

3. Let points E and F be the midpoints of the base BC and AD of the trapezoid ABCD, P the intersection point of the segments BF and AF, Q the intersection point of the segments ED and CF. Prove that the line PQ is parallel to the bases of the trapezoid.
