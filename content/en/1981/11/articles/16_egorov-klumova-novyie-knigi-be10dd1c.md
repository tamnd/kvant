New books

In this issue we continue to publish short summaries of books on mathematics and physics, accessible and interesting to our readers, published in 1982 by the Nauka publishing house. Most bookstores distributing literature on this topic, as well as “Book by mail” departments, accepted pre-orders (postcards) for these books without restrictions until the end of 1981. We ask all busy readers to place such orders. This will help the publishing house more accurately plan and distribute the circulation of published books (especially translated books, the circulation of which is stipulated by agreements with foreign publishing houses). In parentheses we indicate the quarter in which the book is expected to be published.

Mathematics

1. M. Gardner Mathematical tricks and secrets (translation from English). 4th edition, stereotype. (I quarter) Price 30 kopecks.

M. Gardner is a prominent American popularizer of science. This book of his talks about modern mathematical tricks. For the most part, the author does not formulate the laws underlying them. He only describes the actions of the “magician” - overt and secret. The reader will undoubtedly enjoy restoring the corresponding algebraic or geometric idea.

2. B. V. Gnedenko, A. Ya. Khinchin. An elementary introduction to probability theory. 9th edition, stereotype. (I quarter) Price 30 kopecks.

This book has gone through all the editions in our country and has been translated in many countries around the world. It differs from other books on probability theory primarily in that it places minimal requirements on the mathematical knowledge of readers - to understand all its sections, a school course is quite enough. All kinds of theoretical concepts and inferential rules arise on the basis of practical examples.

3. E. I. Ignatiev. In the kingdom of ingenuity. 4th edition, stereotype. (I quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

This book was first seen in 1908 and was a huge success in its time. From 1908 to 1926 it was reprinted several times. Then she was unheard of and was “returned to life” again only in 1977. The entire circulation sold out immediately. The problems, collected by E. I. Ignatiev, are of an entertaining nature: they can be classified under the heading of “mathematical games and entertainment.” There are argumentative, geometric, combinator problems here; game problems, graph problems and joke problems. This book is also available to elementary school students.

4. M. M. Postnikov. Introduction to algebraic number theory. (II quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

This book is a revised and expanded edition of the brochure by the same author, “Fermat’s Theorem,” published in 1978. In it, the ideas and concepts of the theory of algebraic numbers are presented in connection with Fermat’s Last Theorem: the reader is explained that their appearance is not accidental and the discussion is based on the logic of solving a specific problem. The presentation is carried out concentrically so that even a minimally prepared reader can hear the main ideas.

5. Ancient challenging problems in mathematics (retelling and processing by Yu. V. Nesterenko, S. N. Olekhnika, M. K. Potapova) (IV quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

The book contains problems from ancient Russian manuscripts, as well as books published before 1800, interesting both in content and in the method of solution. The tasks are accompanied by notes of a historical nature.

6. G. Steinguz. One hundred tasks (translation from Polish). 3rd edition, stereotype. (II quarter) Price 50 kopecks.

In the preface to his book, G. Shteinguz wrote that he “tried to compose problems that arise in a simple way from geometric phenomena or from real circumstances” and that his goal is “to introduce the reader to the practice of that universal method of interpreting the phenomenon that the named mathematicians gave, to clothe them with the transition from the practice of high school to real mathematics and to show them this science using accessible material.”

All problems included in the book are provided with complete solutions.

7. S. A. Abramov. Programming elements. (I quarter) (“Popular lectures on mathematics.”) Price 20 kopecks.

This book is useful when getting started with programming for the first time. It talks about what an algorithm is, algorithmic languages, translation, and an operating system. The text is accompanied by the task.

8. M. N. Arshinov, L. E. Sadovsky. Codes and mathematics. (IV quarter) (Library «Kvant».) Price 35 kopecks.

Coding theory, the probabilistic element of which is critography (the art of recording the true content of a message), has now grown into a vast and branched area with its own range of objectives and tasks. It mainly deals with solving problems that arise during the transmission of information over communication lines. The authors, speaking about the clearly applied direction of coding theory, also point to its connection with various branches of mathematics: number theory, algebra, combinatorial theory, and probability theory.

9. M. I. Bashmakov, B. M. Bekker, V. M. Gollkhov. Math problems. Alebra and analysis. (II quarter) (Library «Kvant».) Price 35 kopecks.

The tasks collected in the book represent the main

Physics

1. Yu. A. Khramov. Physics: Biographical reference book. 2nd edition, corrected. and additional (IV quarter) Price 2 rub. 40 k.

The book presents biographies of more than a thousand reporting and foreign physicists. Almost all queues are accompanied by photographs. The historical range of the book is from Aristotle to the present day.

2. L. D. Landdu, A. I. Kitaigorodsky. Physics for everyone. Physical body. 5th edition, corrected. (I quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

L. D. Landdu, A. I. Kitaigorodsky. Physics for everyone. Molecules. 5th edition, corrected. (II quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

A. I. Kitaygorodsky. Physics for everyone. Electrons. 2nd edition, revised. (III quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

A. I. Kitaygorodsky. Physics for everyone. Photons and nuclei. 2nd edition, revised. (IV quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

The books focus on functional laws and concepts of physics, as well as the latest achievements of modern physics. The books are written in an entertaining and lively manner, but they require a careful, thoughtful approach from the reader.

The first of four books (Physical Bodies) is devoted to the basic law of motion; The law of universal gravitation and its application for calculations of cosmic velocities and various geophysical phenomena are described in detail.

The second book (Molecules) talks about the structure of matter and various physical states of matter, introducing the reader to the basic laws of thermodynamics.

The third book (Electrons) sets out the laws of quantum physics. Much attention is paid to modern ideas about electricians and magnets.

The contents of four books (Photons and Nuclei) consist of basic information about electromagnetic waves, problems of thermal radiation, and the study of spectra. Separate sections are devoted to the special theory of relativity and wave mechanics.

3. Ya. I. Perelman. Entertaining physics. In 2 books.

Book 1. 21st edition, corrected. (III quarter) Price 40 kopecks.

Book 2. 21st edition, corrected. (IV quarter) Price 45 kopecks.

Written by a teacher and a teacher who has been in tune for more than half a century, the book is read with interest by the modern reader. The author, turning to the range of phenomena of lived life, to the field of technology, to the pages of science fiction novels, strives to teach the reader to see the essence of the phenomenon, to understand the physical. The books contain many interesting problems, paradoxes, and physical puzzles.

4. L. M. Blinov, S. A. Pikin. Liquid crystals. (III quarter) (Library «Kvant») Price 35 kopecks.

The book draws from specialists - experimenters and theorists - about the world of liquid crystals.

The book will introduce the reader to the power supply effect in nematics. The operation of electronic dials is based on this effect. Cholesteric liquid crystals occupy the minds of those who want to learn about thin-film televizons. The reader will read about laser pens and flat-screen TVs in the chapter on smectics. The structure of liquid crystals - rasters - is of great importance for the life and functioning of the organization - for blood circulation, the change in its oxygen, the functioning of brain cells, for the work of various cellular membranes.

5. A. A. Borovoy. Like a particle destroyer! (I quarter) (Library «Kvant») Price 30 kopecks.

This book is a story about interesting and sometimes dramatic Japanese elementary parts. It is impossible to imagine the discovery and study of elementary particles without special devices - detectors. The diversity of detectors is caused by differences in the properties of particles and the intensity of their interaction with matter. Therefore, significant attention in the book is paid to these processes, functional laws and concepts of physics.

The book is based on lectures given by the author of the Faculty of Natural Sciences at the Institute of Atomic Energy named after. I. V. Kurchatova.

6. L. V. Tarasov, A. N. Tarasova. Conversations on the refraction of light. (I quarter) (Library «Kvant») Price 30 kopecks.

In an interesting and fascinating way, the authors talk about the well-known refraction of the refractive index, its control using an electron field and sound waves. The questions discussed in the book are accompanied by interesting problems.

7. A. L. Efros. Geometry of disorder. (III quarter) (Library «Kvant») Price 35 kopecks.

This book is about a new mathematical discipline - patronage theory. It explains the penetration of liquid over long distances into porous bodies, the destruction of rocks (the formation of microcracks), the anomalous properties of supercooled water, the electrical conductivity of highly heterogeneous systems; based on the protection theory, building various models of the metal-insulator transition, etc.
