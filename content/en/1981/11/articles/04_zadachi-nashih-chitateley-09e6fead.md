Tasks for our readers

1. The so-called Heugens impact transformer is a trench in which $n$ bodies lie. Their masses $m_1$, $m_2$, ..., $m_n$ are related by the condition $m_i/m_{i+1} = k > 1$. If the first body begins to move with a speed of $v_1$, then after all collisions (collisions are absolutely elastic), the last body leaves the trench with a speed of $v_n \gg v_1$ (historically, this is one of the first ways to obtain high speeds). Find expressions for the ratios $v_n/v_1$ and for the efficiency $K\Pi D = (m_n/m_1)(v_n/v_1)^2$ as a function of $k$ and $n$. Show that for a given $v_n/v_1$ it is essential to have $k$ for which $K\Pi D$ is maximal.

L. Ashkinazi
