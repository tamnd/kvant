XV All-Union
Schoolchildren's Olympiad

V. Vavilov, A. Zemlyakov

Olympida
in mathematics

From April 15 to 22, the final stage of the XV All-Union Schoolchildren Olympiad in Mathematics took place in Almaty. 148 schoolchildren took part in it: 39 eighth-graders, 47 ninth-graders and 62 tenth-graders. All these guys are winners of the Resurublikan and zonal Olympiads, as well as schoolchildren who received I and II degree diplomas at the final stage of the XV All-Union Olympiad.

This year the Olympics was dedicated to the XXVI Congress of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union.

The final stage of the Olympics took place in two rounds, which took place on April 17 and 19. On each day, the student proposed four problems, which were allotted 5 hours to solve. When selecting the task, the methodological commission adhered to the same principles as last year («Kvant», 1980, No. 11, p. 45). Below we present the conditions of all tasks; in parentheses next to the conditions of each task it is indicated who proposed it. At the end of the article you will find the solution to all problems not included in Problem «Kvant», 1981, No. 7).
