F683. figure shows source $AB$ and its face image $A'B'$. Determine by construction the location of the line and its focal length.

The descriptive center of the lens lies at the point $O$ of the intersection of lines $BB'$ and $AA'$. Point $D$, which is the intersection of lines $AB$ and $A'B'$, lies in the plane of the lens. Consequently, the lens is located along the straight line $OD$; $MN$ ($MN \perp OD$) is the main optical axis of the lens.

Rays $Aa$ and $Bb$ after refraction will intersect at the focus line ($Aa \parallel Bb \parallel MN$). Therefore, $F$ is the focus of the line.
