M711. The diagonals of a convex quadrilateral ABCD inscribed in a circle with center O are mutually perpendicular. Prove that the broken line AOC divides the quadrilateral into two parts of equal area.

V. Varvarkin

M712. Prove that any positive number can be represented as the sum of nine numbers whose decimal notations contain only the digits 0 and 7.

E. Turkevich

M713. M is a set of points on a plane. A point O of a plane is called an “almost center of symmetry” of a set M if one point from M can be eliminated such that for the remaining set O is a center of symmetry in the general sense. How many “almost centers of symmetry” can a finite set have?

V. Prasolov

M714*. N friends learned N news at the same time, and each learned one piece of news. They began calling each other and exchanging news. You can convey as much news as you like in one conversation. What is the minimum number of calls required for everyone to know all the news? Consider three cases: a) N=64, 6) N=55, b) N=100.

A. Anjan

M715*. The primo corner is divided into cells (Fig. 1). On some cells there are chips, and the arrangement of the chips can be transformed as follows: if for some chips the cell adjacent to the top and the cell adjacent to the right are free, then a chip is placed in these cells, and the old chip is removed. First, one chip is placed in the corner cell. Is it possible to use the indicated operations to clear the corners of a) three, 6) six, b) ten cells, shown in Figures 2, a, 6, b, from chips?

M. Kontsevich
