1. The required area is 32(√2−1).

Let us denote by $S_{чч}$ the area of ​​intersections of black k cells of the top board with black cells and cells of the bottom board, and by $S_{чб}$ the area of ​​intersections of black k cells of the top board with white cells and cells of the bottom board. Let us similarly introduce the notations $S_{чч}$ and $S_{чб}$. Then $S_{чч} + S_{чб} + S_{чб} = S$, where $S$ is the area of ​​the cephalopod on figure 4.

Fig. 4.

If from the initial position we move the top board around the center by 90°, then its black cells will turn into white, and its white cells will turn into black. So, $S_{чч} = S_{чб}$ and $S_{чб} = S_{чб}$. If from the initial position we overlay both boards by 90°, then the black cells will turn into white on both boards, so $S_{чб} = S_{чб}$ and $S_{чб} = S_{чб}$. Therefore $S_{чч} = S_{чб} = S_{чб}$, that is $S_{чч} = \frac{1}{4} S$. All that remains is to find $S$.
