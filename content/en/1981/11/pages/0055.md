The table shows that, on average, Olympiad participants successfully completed the task of the theoretical round.

An experimental round took place on April 19. Each participant completed two experimental tasks. Here are their terms:

Experimental tour
8th grade
1. Build a simple electric motor and test it in action.
Required materials and accessories: an axle with a commutator, two iron plates 1.5x6 cm for the anchor, two iron brackets for attaching the anchor, wiring, a strip of paraffin paper for insulation, a stand, a power source, connecting wires, a horseshoe magnet, tools.
2. Determine the heater power.
Required materials and accessories: DC source (up to 32 V), 250 ml beaker with water, spiral, thermometer (0°C - 100°C), connecting wires, key, stopwatch, graph paper.
Mixing. The capacity of water is $c=4200$ J/(kg K).

9th grade
1. Determine the charge on the capacitor plates at a voltage of 40 V.
Required materials and accessories: capacitor, microammeter, resistor, stopwatch, switch.

2. Determine the heat of the unknown liquid from the known heat of water (the liquid is immiscible).

Required materials and accessories: iron core choke, active resistance, capacitor, AC voltmeter, AC voltage source.

2. a) Determine the sensitivity of the photocell: 1) in short circuit mode; 2) in active load mode.
6) Plot a graph of the dependence of the efficiency of a photocell on illumination at one value of load resistance.
b) Use a photocell to check the illumination law.

Required materials and accessories: photocell, variable resistance, DC voltmeter, DC ampere, incandescent lamp, power supply for incandescent lamp.
