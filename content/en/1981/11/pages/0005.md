Such a gap between challenges and scientific institutions turned out to be extremely harmful for personnel training, so it was necessary to bridge this gap.linkedin. For this purpose, the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology was created, in which the teaching of dough students is connected with scientific work; they participated on the most advanced equipment, they are trained by young scientists who are actively working in science, and, finally, physical scientists have the opportunity to begin scientific activities from the second or third year. Thus, all those shortcomings in the organization of our science, which are associated with its rapid growth, were largely linked.

The success of this system is undoubted. There are more and more educational institutions working on the same principle as MIPT, which trains young scientists. However, no matter how successfully such a system has worked over the course of these years, it still has significant shortcomings that need to be addressed and corrected, and our task is to diagnose these shortcomings and look for ways to correct them.

The first graduating class of the present MIPT has now grown up, so that Comrade Belotserkovsky, our old physicist, has become the rector of our institute. Thanks to the fact that he successfully completed his course with us and well understood the spirit and importance of the teaching and learning system in physics, he is extremely pleasant and easy to work with, and we often serve with him those events that are necessary to improve our work.

We notice that we still have big problems in our professorship; we do not always manage to attract the best professors to teach young people. And there is one more drawback that I will mention. The Institute does not yet perform all the functions that it could perform. I also want to talk about these functions. As for the selection of professorships, then, as you know, we have good professors, there are also average ones, and there are even lower ones. There's nothing you can do about it. It will always be like this.

Perhaps the most difficult thing is that the teaching of basic disciplines is not well provided for us. In former times, teaching courses in basic subjects in higher educational institutions - general physics, chemistry, mathematics, mechanics - was entrusted to the most eminent scientists, and it was considered an extremely honorable task to also teach courses. Now that has changed, it's hard to say why. Because from the point of view of educating young people, it is very important, of course, that the basis of knowledge is given by major scientists who would lay the foundation and tell young people what they need to build knowledge. If the foundation is not reliable enough, then all the knowledge will not be used on its feet.

How to improve the situation, how to ensure that the best professors, the best teachers, the best scientists teach the course at the university? It would seem that one could use modern technology, say, to make a movie in which a lecturer, the most prominent scientist in a given field (or even a group of scientists), would teach students physics, or chemistry, or mathematics.

Of course, this will attract the best professors to teach students. But let's see what actually comes out of this. Perhaps the administration of the institute will welcome such an initiative: the number of staff positions will be reduced and there will be no need to attract and look for teaching staff. From the point of view of the ministry - the same amenities. By making one film, they will be able to reduce staff and reduce costs for universities. Some students would be happy, because after all, it is more comfortable to sleep in dark cinema halls than in light ones.
