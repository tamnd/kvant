Let's generalize the Pythagorean theorem

So, in a right triangle $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$,

where $a, b, c$ are its quadets and hypotenuse. Note now that if we have figures $A, B$ and $C$ whose areas $S_A, S_B$ and $S_C$ are $ka^2, kb^2$ and $kc^2$, respectively, then $S_A + S_B = S_C$. In particular, the “Pythagorean correspondence” is satisfied for areas of similar figures constructed on the sides of a right triangle*).
