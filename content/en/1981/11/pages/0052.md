Physics Olympiad

From April 15 to 22, the final stage of the XV All-Union Schoolchildren Olympid in Physics, which was dedicated to the XXVI Congress of the CPSU, took place in Tashkent.

140 students from the combined teams of all allied resource workers, the cities of Moscow and Leningrad, the combined teams of the Ministry of Railways and the Main Political Directorate of the USSR Ministry of Defense competed in solving problems compiled specifically for the Olympiad by famous physicists and teachers from leading institutions of the country. All students of the final stage are winners of resource specialists at this year’s Olympiad or recognized by the previous All-Union Olympiad, having won diplomas of the 1st and 2nd degrees.

The grand opening of the Olympiad took place on April 16 in the assembly hall of the Tashkent State Pedagogical Institute named after. Nizami. The Minister of Education of the Uzbek USSR S. Sh. Shermukhamedov wished all participants private and porn wrestling and expressed hope that members of the Uzbekistan teams will perform “at home” more successfully than at previous Olympics. The chairman of the jury of the XV All-Union Olympiad in Physics, Honored Scientist Uzbekina, addressed the children with parting words.
