The opposite sides of the cube, made of yellow, easy-to-cut material, are painted blue, green and red. In the top row the cube is shown from four angles. The first figure of the second row shows the cube after a whole piece has been cut out of it. The other pictures in the second row show how this cut cube will look from other angles.
We invite you to test your spatial imagination: on checkered paper, draw and color the remaining twelve angles of the four cut cubes A, B, C, D, shown in the bottom row.
