⟦folio 40⟧

V. Ryzhik

Geometric gift

Fedya and Vasya were not tormented by the question: “What to give each other for their birthday?” Each of them gave the other a problem or theorem he had invented—something from mathematics. Vasya's birthday was approaching.

“Do you remember,” Fedya asked Vasya, as if casually, “how to carry out a transfer from a point to a plane?”

Vasya began to move his pencil along the paper. He walked for about five minutes, and then sighed:

- No, I don’t remember.
“You’re not alone,” Fedya sympathized.

(You, reader, try to do this construction quickly and correctly.)

“Well, then look what I figured out,” Fedya continued. — Take the plane α and point A outside it. On the plane α we draw a straight line a - any one. From point A we carry out a redistribution AB to straight line a. In the plane α we draw a straight line BC, redefining the straight line a. From point A we carry out a redistribution of AD to straight line BC (see figure). All.

- What is “everything”? - Vasya was surprised.
- Actually, everything. The transfer from point A to the plane α is constructed.
- And the proof? - Vasya asked.
- Please. Let us draw through point D any line intersecting line a; Let's call the intersection point K. Let's draw another segment AK. From right triangles ADB and ABK we obtain

$$|AK|^2 = |AB|^2 + |BK|^2 = |AD|^2 + |BD|^2 + |BK|^2.$$

From right triangle KBD -

$$|BD|^2 + |BK|^2 = |KD|^2.$$

This means $$|AK|^2 = |AD|^2 + |KD|^2.$$ But then angle D in triangle ADK is a straight line, that is (AD) ⊥ (DK). The straight line AD is redistributed two-metrically by a straight line in the α plane. — BC and KD; according to the property of the recurve of a line and a plane, it is recurve of the plane α. Well, how?

“I like it,” said Vasya. - The main thing, I already memorized, is the formation. One overload, the second and the third. “He waved his finger in the air, pretending to overextend himself.

“And the evidence is clear,” he continued. - From three right-angled triangles, we can deduce that the fourth triangle is also right-angled.

“You’re right,” Fedya confirmed. — By the way, from these same triangles one can establish a proof of the theorem about three redistributions. Here is an envelope for you: it contains both the construction and the evidence.

Here his voice took on a slightly solemn tone.

— This envelope is my birthday present. By the way, there is still something to think about.

A few days later Vasya came to Fedya.
