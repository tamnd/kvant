3. The number of diagonals of a convex heptagon is 14 (prove it!). If any two diagonals are parallel, then the angle between them is ()°, which is less than 13°. Let it not be like that now.
Let us select a certain point O on the plane and draw through 14 straight lines parallel to the diagonals of the heptagon. These straight lines will divide the plane into 28 angles, totaling 360°. Suppose that each of these angles is not less than 13°, then their sum will be at least 28 • 13° = 364°, but in fact it is equal to 360°. Contradiction!
