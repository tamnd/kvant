⟦folio 24⟧

Tasks

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After forming a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time. The mathematics problems in this issue were presented at the XXII International Olympiad for Schoolchildren, held in the United States of America.

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than February 15, 1982 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 12–81” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example, “M716, M717” or “F728”. Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision verification in this envelope). Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in two copies along with your solution to this problem.

M716—M720; F728—F732

M716. From the point $P$ inside a given triangle $ABC$ perpendiculars $PA_1, PB_1, PC_1$ are dropped onto lines $BC, CA$ and $AB$. For what points is $P$ inside $\triangle ABC$ large?

$$\frac{|BC|}{|PA_1|} + \frac{|CA|}{|PB_1|} + \frac{|AB|}{|PC_1|}$$

takes the smallest value?

M717. Given natural numbers $n$ and $r$, $1 \leqslant r \leqslant n$. Consider all possible subsets of the set {1, 2, ..., $n$} consisting of $r$ numbers, and in each choice the smallest number. Prove that the arithmetic mean of all chosen numbers is $(n+1) / (r+1)$. (For example, for $n=3$, $r=2$ we get three subsets {1, 2}, {1, 3}, {2, 3} and the arithmetic mean is $(1+1+2)/3 = 4/3$).

M718. Find the smallest value of the expression $m^2+n^2$ for all possible pairs of $(m; n)$ natural numbers such that $1 \leqslant m \leqslant 1981$, $1 \leqslant n \leqslant 1981$ and $|n^2-mn-m^2|=1$.

M719. a) For which $n \geqslant 3$ there is a set of $n$ consecutive natural numbers that have the following property: the smallest of these $n$ numbers is a divisor of the least common multiple of the remaining $n-1$ numbers?

6) For what $n \geqslant 3$ is there a unique set of $n$ consecutive numbers that have the specified property?

M720. The following is known about the function $f$, defined on the set of all pairs of non-negative integers $(x; y)$:

$$1^\circ. f(0, y) = y+1,$$
$$2^\circ. f(x+1, 0) = f(x, 1),$$
$$3^\circ. f(x+1, y+1) = f(x, f(x+1, y))$$

for each pair $x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0$. Find the value of $f(4, 1981)$.

F728. Three small balls with identical masses - red, blue and green, are fixed by an inverse frame at the vertices of an equilateral triangle with sides $l$. The system is placed on the main horizontal surface and rotated with a period of $T$ around the center of mass.
