⟦folio 37⟧

Quests

1. In equalities
   $$a:b=c, c+d=e, e-f=g,$$
   $$g \cdot h=10i+j$$
Place the letters 10 numbers 0, 1, ..., 9 together so that you get correct equalities.

2. In a rug woven from multi-colored ropes (see figure), there is a rope ring, when cut, the rug splits into three parts so that two of these parts are made up of the same number of ropes, and the third is made up of just one rope. Specify this ring.

3. Prove that any convex heptagon has two diagonals whose angle is less than 13°.

4. Then Ira suggested cutting a “checkered” rectangle measuring 8x9 (see figure) along the grid lines into rectangular strips 1x6. Will Ira be able to do this?

5. The first line contains three integers: $a, b, c$. In the second line below them three differences are written: $a-b, b-c, c-a$. The numbers in the third line are formed from the numbers in the second line according to the same law, etc. Show that the number 279 cannot appear in any of the lines below the seventh.

These tasks were offered to us
F. Bartenev, A. Kalinin, A. Savin,
A. Shvetsov.
