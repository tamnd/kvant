XII International Physics Olympiad

From July 1 to July 10 this year, the XXII International Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in the People's Republic of Bulgaria, in the city of Varna. This Olympiad was a record for the number of participants: 14 countries joined their teams. These are Bulgaria, Hungary, Vietnam, East Germany, Italy, Poland, Romania, USSR, Finland, France, Germany, Czechoslovakia, Sweden and Yugoslavia.

The USSR team was represented by the winners of the All-Union Physics Olympiad:

Alexander Gutin is a graduate of secondary school No. 2 in Klintsy,
Vladislav Derevyanko - graduate of secondary school No. 145 in Kyiv,
Andrey Mushinsky is a graduate of secondary school No. 23 in Minsk,

Soviet, Bulgarian and Vietnamese delegations at the Monument of Soviet-Bulgarian Friendship.

Photo by Hristo Mintsev

Ilya Solodovnik is a graduate of secondary school No. 179 in Moscow,
Igor Shubenin is a graduate of secondary school No. 52 in Riga.

The leaders of our team were the head of the laboratory of the Research Institute of Content and Teaching Methods of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR O. F. Kabardin and the inspector of the Ministry of Education of the USSR M. V. Grabilenkov.

As usual, the working jury consists of economic turnover and verification of tasks. The results were approved by the International Commission, which included representatives of all participating countries. The famous Bulgarian scientist Professor A. Raev was elected Chairman of the International Commission.

The Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of Belarus, the Dimitrovsky Komsomol, and the Varna City Council paid much attention to the Olympiad. The Olympics were perfectly organized. All participants lived in a new, comfortable building of the Teacher Improvement Institute on the Black Sea coast.
