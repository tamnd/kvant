M677. Inside an acute triangle ABC, a point M is chosen, which is
a) the point of intersection of the medians;
b) the point of intersection of the bisectors;
b) the point of intersection of heights.
Prove that if the radii of the circle inscribed in triangles AMB, BMC, AMC are equal, then triangle ABC is regular.

a) The areas of triangles AMB, BMC and AMC (Fig. 1) are the same - they are equal to $\frac{1}{3} S_{ABC}$ (prove this).

Since the area S of the triangle, its semi-permeter p and the radius r of the connection circle inscribed in it by the correspondence S=pr, the permeters of triangles AMB, BMC and AMC are also the same.

Let us now assume that triangle ABC is irregular; let, for example, $|AB| > |BC|$. Then angle BDA is obtuse; therefore $|AM| > |MC|$, so that the permeter of triangle AMB is greater than the permeter of triangle BMC - a contradiction.
