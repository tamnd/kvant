⟦folio 49⟧

An experimental tour is underway.

During a meeting of the International Olympic Commission. Chairman of the commission A. Raev speaks.

Greetings from A. Raev to the reader of our magazine.

kov O. F. Kabardina and G. S. Tarasyuk. The method proposed by G. S. Tarasyuk for assessing the difficulty of olympiad problems was immediately used by the olympiad jury, and the calculation showed that the coefficient of difficulty of the problems of this olympiad was within the normal range.

Participants in the round table discussion also spoke about the important role of physics and mathematics magazines for schoolchildren. Professor Kunfalvi, one of the founders of the International Physics Olympiad, spoke in detail about the Hungarian physics and mathematics journal for schoolchildren. He emphasized that in such magazines it is necessary to publish difficult problems that schoolchildren could solve at home, since not every creative person is able to quickly solve problems and run in the Olympics. The speech of the Bulgarian participant P. Bozarova spoke about the 期刊 magazine «Kvant» for preparing for the Olympiads and developing the creative abilities of Bulgarian schoolchildren. L. G. Aslamazov spoke about the history of the creation of «Kvant» and the experience of its work with schoolchildren in the USSR. The representative of Yugoslavia D. Gruzik reported on the magazine for schoolchildren “Young Physicist” published in his country.

At the final meeting of the International Commission, a plan for holding the next Physics Olympiad was adopted. The XIII Olympiad is scheduled to be held at the end of June 1982 in Germany, XIV - in Romania in 1983, XV - in Sweden in 1984, XVI - in the GDR in 1985. A proposal was made to also involve international organizations in hosting the Olympiad, such as the European Physical Society, the International Union of Pure and Applied Physics, and UNESCO.

In conclusion, on behalf of the entire Soviet delegation, I would like to thank the Bulgarian Olympic organizing committee for the warm, friendly welcome, which we received literally at every step.
