It's hard to believe that the orange rubber "pretzel" that encloses the iron ring with both properties of its "ears" (figure top left) can be so deformed without cutting or gluing that it will only enclose the ring with one ear (as shown above right). But this can actually be done if the “pretzel” is cut, skimmed, inflated and stretched. How? If you don't tell us yourself, look at the "Answers, Directions, Solutions" section.
Naturally, there is a desire to peel off the second ear from the ring - after all, they have equal rights! However, you can’t unhook the earcup.
Now think about whether it is possible to untangle a pretzel with one ear hooked behind another (figure below).
