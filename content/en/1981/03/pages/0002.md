The engravings reproduced on the first and second pages of the cover are called “The Knot” by the 16th-century German artist Albrecht Duer and the contemporary Dutch artist Maurice Escher. In the language of mathematics, Duerr's engraving shows an engagement of several knots (can they be uncoupled? Which of them can be untied?), and Escher's engraving shows a trefoil knot (can it be untied?). “Colored Knots” will help you answer these questions.
