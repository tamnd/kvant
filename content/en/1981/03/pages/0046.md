To calculate the extension of all arrivals, the following reasoning is used. We will divide the undistributed income into $N$ equal parts. Let's consider a piece of the parish separated from its free end $i$ by pieces (Fig. 3). The force $T_i$ with which the selected area acts on these $i$ pieces with mass $\frac{m_0}{N} i$ imparts to them an acceleration $a = F_0/m_0$; hence,

$$T_i = \frac{i}{N} m_0 a = \frac{i}{N} F_0.$$

The same absolute force acts on the selected area of ​​the parish. Let the force $T_{i+1}$ act on this section from the right. Then

$$T_{i+1} - T_i = m_i a = \frac{F_0}{N}.$$

As $N$ increases, the value $F_0/N$ decreases, and for sufficiently large $N$ we can assume that $T_{i+1} - T_i \approx 0$, that is, $T_{i+1} \approx T_i = \frac{i}{N} F_0$. In other words, for large $N$ we can assume that

$$T_i = \frac{F_1 - F_2}{N} i + F_2 = kN \cdot \Delta l_i.$$
