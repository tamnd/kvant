THREE MORE LASKER MATCHES

We continue our story about the matches for the world chess crown. In the eighth match, Lasker and Tarsh met. In terms of creativity, Grandmaster Tarsh was in many places an opponent of the world champion. He searched for absolute truth in chess and strove to make the best move in any position. Lasker rejected this method of play and tried to find individual keys for each partner. As in the three previous matches, Lasker added a convincing victory (with a score of 10.5:5.5).

Em. Lasker (1868—1941)
