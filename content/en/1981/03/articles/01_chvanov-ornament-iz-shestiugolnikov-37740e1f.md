⟦folio none⟧

The pattern depicted on this cover page is obtained by sequentially repeating two simple operations. First, the shaded regular hexagon is reflected relative to each of its sides. The resulting “flower”, consisting of the original hexagon and six of its congested prints of the same color, is inscribed in a regular hexagon of large sizes (this creates unpainted areas), with which the reflection operation is repeated relative to each side (while preserving the coloring). The new figure is again inscribed in the regular hexagon, etc. (The ornament shown on the cover is the result of three steps.)
Similar interesting ornaments can be obtained by taking a regular triangle or square as the initial $n$-gon. Try to draw them.

V. Chvanov
