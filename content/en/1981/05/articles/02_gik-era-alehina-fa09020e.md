ERA OF ALEKHIN
A. Alekin played his first match as a world champion with E. Bogolov in 1929. At that time, Alekin had no equal opponents in the chess world, which was confirmed in this match. The match, which was planned to consist of 30 games, was interesting, but ended in a major victory for the champion with a score of 15.5:9.5. The repeat match, which took place five years later, was not much different from the previous one. Alekin’s advantage was again disorderly - 15.5:10.5. Here is an episode from the sixteenth game of the second match.

Alexander Alekin (1892 - 1946)
