With in-depth study

At school No. 13 in the city of Saratov there are only grades 7-10. During the competitive selection of applicants, an entrance interview and written work in mathematics are carried out. There are usually twice as many applications as there are places.

Students of classes with in-depth study of mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology - permanent sections are always located at universities in Saratov, and they always pass entrance exams with confidence.

The task called “Radical” aroused great interest among eighth-graders. However, on a stand made in the shape of a radical, the condition of the problem was posted, and a few days later - its solution, given by one of the students. It was originally intended to help clarify more complex problems; Only the strongest students participated in the solution and analysis. But gradually we washed ourselves down with the rest - and “Radical” gained wide popularity. And if there was not enough information to solve a problem, the teacher indicated additional literature.

“Later, we began to display 2-3 solutions, completed by different students and displayed in editing modes,” says mathematics teacher Victoria Iosifovna Igumnova. “And universal service allows us to determine which of these solutions make the most sense.

“Many guys, noticing shortcomings in the posted decisions, offer their own attempts,” says Ira Azarenko, a member of the Radical editorial team. — If the editor does not find a refutation, the author immediately makes an amendment in red paste and is immensely proud of his site. And some bring new tasks, displayed from different books and popular science magazines.
