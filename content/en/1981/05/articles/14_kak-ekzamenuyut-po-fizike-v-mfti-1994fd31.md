In conclusion, we note that everything said in this article has practical significance only when the distance from the object to the line or mirror is comparable to the focal length of the line or mirror. If the specified distance is large (compared to the focal distance), the image of the object, regardless of its inclination to the optical axis, will be in the focal plane perpendicular to the optical axis of the system. Photographers, for example, know this well: using short-focus objects, they get almost equally sharp images of both close and distant objects.

How to take the physics exam at MIPT

The magazine's editors receive many letters asking them to tell us about how entrance exams in physics are conducted at various universities. We bring to the attention of readers an interview taken by the correspondent of the newspaper “For Sciences” of the Moscow Physical Institute L. Sanaev with the deputy head of the department of general physics of this institute, Professor S. Kozel. The interview is republished with minor changes and abbreviations.

— Tell me, how many years have you taken the entrance exams?
- With short breaks for twenty years.
— What, in your opinion, is the distinctive feature of the physical reception system?
— MIPT prepares personnel to work at the forefront of modern science. This explains the high admission criteria: the most capable are selected.

The contingent of applicants is very wide, from graduates of rural schools to graduates of Moscow physics and mathematics schools. We study this and strive to find out the first line of not formal knowledge, but abilities that can subsequently be developed. The task is, of course, difficult. But, apparently, the reception of communication solves it quite successfully.

— Please tell us about the written and oral exams and the connection between them.

— The written exam is carried out first, it cuts off a certain part of weakly prepared applicants - 30-40 percent. Then comes the oral exam. It should be noted that the grade received for the written work will never affect the grade for the oral exam. Often someone who gets a C on a written paper then gets an A; Sometimes it’s the other way around, but it’s a city decision.

— A little more detail about the tasks of written work.
— By tradition, we give four problems corresponding to the main branches of physics: mechanics, heat, electricity, optics. Of these, at least two are of medium difficulty, one is slightly more difficult and one is difficult. This leads to a strong differentiation of assessments.

Before starting written work, examinees are warned: start solving problems that are easiest for you.

— What do you value most in an answer in an oral exam?
— The oral examination consists of short, qualitatively oriented questions from the examiner. There is no opportunity to listen to lengthy speeches. It’s a big deal when examinees don’t give a memorized answer, but show an understanding of physics.

— There are usually rumors among high school students that school preparation is not enough to enter MIPT. Is this true?

— The tasks and questions proposed in the exams do not go beyond the scope of the school curriculum, but we require a deeper understanding, which, unfortunately, is rarely achieved when studying a subject at school. In general, you need some preparation, and the best preparation is solving problems of increased difficulty, for example from physics collections or the magazine «Kvant».
