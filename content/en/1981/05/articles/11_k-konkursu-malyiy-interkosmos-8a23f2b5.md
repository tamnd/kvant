For the competition
"Small Intercosmos"

The previous issue of our magazine contained information about the All-Union competition for school and professional students called “Small Intercosmos”. Below we provide an approximate list of problems proposed by specialists to the participants of this competition.

1. Earth - Space - Earth

— It is known that the “ceiling” of aviation is about 30 km, and the lower limit of satellite flight is about 180 km. Suggest a physical principle of flight at intermediate altitudes.

— When launching spacecraft, the first stages of the free package disappear, falling to Earth. Develop options for using these stages of the package.

Suggest:

- economic methods of delivering cargo into orbit, a project for a space plane that can be used not only for flights into orbit, but also as an international vehicle;

- the direction and forms of joint work of schoolchildren and space station crews in the interests of the national economy (for example, parallel observations of schoolchildren on Earth, and of space from orbit, of the state of crops, water basins, forests, etc.);

- a way to save space in the event of an emergency on an orbital spacecraft. One of the rescue options is to return the cosmos to Earth using individual means;

— a way to simulate various space conditions on Earth;

— a method for determining the chemical state of the soil by studying an area from space, identifying a deficiency or excess of nutritional substances;

— forms of teaching space science and technology in solving environmental problems;

- a method for studying the Earth's natural resources.

2. House in orbit

— During long flights, the state of space is negatively affected by the monotony of the situation. Suggest a way to change the situation on the station at the request of the cosmos.

3. Industry in space

— Experiments are carried out under time conditions to grow crystals and obtain

alloys from materials with different removed weights. Suggest other technology processes that can be applied in space.

— The use of ordinary earthly tools in non-space is difficult (for example, hammering a nail with a hammer is difficult). Think of tools (hammer, screwdriver, wrench, etc.) that can be used in a non-airborne environment.

— A large number of spacecraft that have exhausted their service life are in circulation in various orbits around the Earth. Develop projects to use them.

Suggest:

— a designer device for quickly cooling liquid metal (to a solid state) and keeping it in suspension on board an orbital station;
— a method for measuring the frequency of viburnum oscillations of water freely floating in the station cabin;
— methods for measuring the deposited surface under non-rebreather conditions, as well as the temperature of liquid metal in a non-contact manner;
— a constructor for an amplifier (made of glass or quartz), in which you can place a solid material (metal or semiconductor) that completely fills the volume of this amplifier. It is important that when heated it does not crack due to changes in the volume of materials;
— how to obtain solar energy in space in large quantities and transport it to Earth;
— how to directly use the energy in space that will be received by solar power plants in orbit;
— chemical processes that successfully occur in non-remote conditions;
- a project for space chemical production, the raw material of which would be the material of the planets of the solar system and the astroid.

4. In outer space

— Develop means of fixing and moving astronauts on the surface of the spacecraft.
— On Earth, in the air and in water, with the help of special devices (for example, a bicycle), a person can move, using only his muscular strength. Come up with a way to travel in space between two nearby ships based on this principle.
— Develop the design of a robotic simulator with remote or program control for carrying out installation work in low-Earth orbit in outer space.
— If a satellite has an orbital altitude of 36,000 km and is launched in the equatorial plane to the sides with the daily rotation of the Earth, then it will always be above the same point on the Earth’s equator. This is very convenient for using such satellites as radio and television repeaters. Suggest other ways to use such spacecraft.
— Observing the same object simultaneously by two or more radio telecommunications
Pami, astronomers obtain high angular resolution (radiometry method). Is it possible and how exactly to create the same optical interferometer based on two or more telecopes?
— It is known that communication with an astronaut outside the ship can be maintained via radium and television channels; assume an additional (emergency) type of communication between the astronaut and the station crew.
“Photography and filming are of great importance in the scientific program of space exploration. At the same time, the modern design of the spacesuit does not allow the eyepiece to be brought to the eye. Develop ways to bring the conditions for filming in space closer to those on Earth.
Propose the transportation of a means of communication between two spaceships (spacecraft).

Consider:

— what should be the management bodies, the organization of the workplace in these devices;
— designer of individual transportation means for moving astronauts-assemblers in open space in near-Earth space;
— designer of a portable removable device for transporting cargo during installation work in space exploration;
— methods of transportation, in addition to jet, that can be used in space exploration;
— method and equipment for conducting an experiment in space exploration to assess hand-eye coordination and speed of movement of the cosmonaut’s reaction;
— your own project for astronomical support in space;
— methods of using lasers in outer space;
— means for carrying research instruments overboard the spacecraft at a distance of several tens of meters with their subsequent return to the spacecraft.

5. Exploring the Solar System

— In a few decades, flights to any body in the solar system will begin. Think about where and for what reasons it is best to organize support for:
a) studying the physics of the Sun;
b) studying stars and distant galaxies.
— In literature, I have expressed the idea of ​​creating an artificial atmosphere on the Moon. Develop a project to implement this idea.
— The fastest mode of transport on Earth is an airplane. Develop a design for an apparatus adapted for the rapid transportation of boats and cargo in the conditions of Venus and Mars.
— On giant planets, there are ways to significantly increase angular resolution to facilitate the work of astronomers on such a planet.
