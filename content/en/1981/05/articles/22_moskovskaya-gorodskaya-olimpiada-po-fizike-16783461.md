Moscow City Olympiad in Physics

In March 1980, the traditional 41st Olympiad in Physics took place, held by the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. Since 1978, for students in grades 8-10, this Olympiad is also the Moscow City Olympiad.

The first qualifying round is held in the city districts, and the next three rounds are held at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. In the first, second and third rounds, each participant in the Olympiad is offered five theoretical problems. The last round is experimental; The winners of previous rounds are invited to attend.

At two open doors of the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University, the winners are presented with diplomas and books on physics. Based on the recognition of the city Olympiad, a Moscow team is formed to participate in the All-Union Physics Olympiad.

We bring to your attention the most interesting theoretical problems of the 1980 Moscow City Olympiad. After each task, the class in which this task is offered and its author are indicated.

1. There is a cube on a horizontal surface. A rod was placed on top of it, the lower end of which is hinged to the surface (Fig. 1). At what angles α between the rod and the surface will the system be in equilibrium if the friction coefficients $\mu_1$ between the rod and the cube and $\mu_2$ between the cube and the surface satisfy the relations $\mu_1\mu_2=1$? (8 grades)

V. Ilyin

2. A body slides without initial velocity from the top of an inclined plane with an angle of inclination α. The friction coefficient μ between the body and the plane changes with increasing distance x from its top according to the law $\mu=bx$. The body stops before reaching the base of the inclined plane. Find the time t elapsed from the beginning of the body's motion until it stops. (10 grades)

G. Pustovalov

3. The elevator operator of a high-rise building, being a punctual person, hung nitrogen on the wall at atmospheric pressure $p_0$ and temperature $T_0$. At some point in time, the top and one of the side edges of the sauce became permeable to nitrogen. At what values ​​of m and T does the vessel begin to move?

7. In a uniform magnetic field with indication $\vec{B}$ ($|\vec{B}|=0,1$ T), there is a square frame on the side $l=5$ cm (Fig. 4). Frame resistance $R=1$ Ohm. What force must be applied to the frame in order to move it out of the magnetic field at a constant speed of $\vec{v}$ ($|\vec{v}|=4$ cm/s)? The plane of the frame remains perpendicular to the direction of the magnetic lines; the field has a sharply defined border, two sides of the frame are perpendicular to this border.

M. Semenov

4. The length of the slow mathematical matrix changes over time as follows: $l=l_0(1+\alpha t)$, where $\alpha$ is a constant and $\alpha t \ll 1$. Write down the formula for the dependence of the mixing time s magnet on the equilibrium position. The softener is removed from the equilibrium position only and at the initial moment of mixing the matrix is ​​equal to zero. The amplitude of oscillations $s_0$ is considered constant. (10 grades)

G. Myakishche
