Mathematics

Written exam
Option 1

(faculty of mathematics and mechanics)

1. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ does the inequality $ax^2+2ax+0,5 \geq 0$ hold on the entire number line?

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_{(x^2)}[(2x-1)^2] + \log_{(x^3)}[\left(\frac{1}{2}-x\right)^3] = 2 \int_{1/4}^{3/4}(4t-1) dt.$$

3. In a semicircle of radius $R$ there is a vissan trapezoid $ABCD$ such that its base $AD$ is the diameter and its vertices $B$ and $C$ lie on the circle. What is the magnitude of the angle at the base of the trapezoid $ABCD$, which has the largest perimeter?

4. Find all solutions to the equation

$$1 + (\sin x-\cos x) \sin \frac{\pi}{4}=2 \cos^2 \frac{5}{2} x,$$
which satisfy the condition $\sin 6x<0$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)

1. Solve inequality
$$|2^x-1|+x \geq x \cdot 2^x.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$3 \log_{27}(3^{x^2-2x+2})=\left[\sin^2(x-1+\frac{\pi}{4})\right]'.$$

3. What angle does the chord $AC$ form with the diameter $AB$ if the product of the length $AC$ and the length of the perpendicular $CD$ lowered by the diameter $AB$ has the greatest value?

4. Find all values ​​$a$ from the segment $[-\pi; 0]$ satisfying the equality

$$\sin a+\int_a^2 \cos 2x \, dx=0.$$

Oral exam assignment

Faculty of Physics

1. A gas with a mass of $m=1$ kg and a specific heat capacity at a constant volume of $cv = 700 \text{ Дж}/(\text{кг} \cdot \text{К}),$ is heated at a constant pressure of $p=2 \cdot 10^5$ Pa. What is the specific heat of the gas in this process if its temperature increases by $\Delta T=2$ K and its volume increases by $\Delta V=0,001 \text{ m}^3$?

2. What is the voltage at the battery terminals, the electromotive force is $\delta=12$ V, and the internal resistance is $r=2$ Ohm, when charging and discharging with a current of $I=1$ A?

3. At a height $h$ above the water surface (pre-fill indicator $n=4/3$) an accurate light source is located. Where will the image of this source given by the flat mirror of the household be located if the depth of the vessel with water is $d$?

4. The distance between two precise light sources is $L=48$ cm. Where between them should you put your own line with a focal length of $F=18$ cm so that the images of both sources fall at the same point on the main optical axis?

1. Three identical small balls weighing $m=0,02$ g each are suspended at one point on silk threads $L=30$ cm long. What equal traces of resistance $R_1$ resistance $R_2=10/3$ Ohm increases the current to $I_2=8$ A. Find the internal resistance of the battery $r$ and its electromotive force $\delta$.
