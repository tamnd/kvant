Tasks

M681 — M685; F693 — F697

M681. a) Come up with integers $a, b, c, d$ such that the numbers $a^2+b^2, a^2+b^2+c^2, a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2$ are squares of integers.
b) Is there a sequence consisting of squares of integers such that for any $n$ the sum $n$ of its first terms is the square of the integer?

G. Grigoriev

M682. Inside the triangle $\triangle$ you need to place the triangle $\triangle_1$ so that each of the three squares built on the sides of the triangle $\triangle_1$ has two vertices lying on different sides of the triangle $\triangle$ (Fig. 1).
a) Prove that the medians of triangle $\triangle$ are perpendicular to the sides of triangle $\triangle_1$.
b) Is such a construction possible for any acute triangle $\triangle$?

A. Yagubyants

M683. Several circles of the same size are placed on the table so that no two overlap. Prove that the circles can be expanded into four colors so that any two touching circles will be painted in different colors. Find an arrangement of circles in which three colors are not enough for such a coloring.

G. Ringel

M684*. The two play the following version of “sea battle”. One player places on the board $n \times n$ a certain number of non-overlapping “ships” $n \times 1$ (perhaps none). The second player simultaneously makes a series of blows on the board squares and receives an answer from the opponent for each square - hit or miss. What minimum number of fields should be struck so that the location of all his ships can be unambiguously determined from the enemy’s responses? Consider three cases: a) $n=4, 6)$ $n=10$, c) $n$ is any natural number.

E. Gik

M685*. Two subsets of the set of natural numbers are called from the other a digr by an integer. (For example, sets of numbers and odd numbers are constructs.) Is it possible to split the set of natural numbers into an infinite number of (disjoint) infinite constructs?

A. Fedorov

F693. On a fixed wedge forming an angle α with the horizon, there lies a non-stretchable rope (Fig. 2). One end of the rope is fixed at point A. A small weight is attached to the lower end of the rope (at point B). At some point in time, the wedge begins to move to the right with constant acceleration a. With what acceleration does the weight move while it is on the wedge?

S. Krotov

F694. N identical balls move along a long straight trough inclined at an angle α to the horizontal without friction. What is the maximum number of collisions that can occur in the system at arbitrary initial positions and speeds of the balls? The collisions of the balls are considered to be absolutely elastic.

E. Surkov

F695. In the simplest model, a star is considered as a gas ball in equilibrium in its own gravitational field. Assuming that the gas consists of completely infused hydrogen atoms and gel, estimate the temperature of the star. Star mass M, radius r; the relative content of hydrogen in the gas is n.

V. Tugushchev

F696. A capacitive voltmeter is a flat air capacitor, one of the plates of which is fixed motionless, and the second can move translationally in a direction perpendicular to the plane of the plates. A stiffening spring k (Fig. 3) is attached to the movable plate. A measure of the applied voltage is the change in the gap between the plates. What is the maximum voltage that can be changed with such a device? Area of ​​the plates S, gap between the plates at zero stress d.

D. Pavlov
