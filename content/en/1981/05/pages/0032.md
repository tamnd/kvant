⟦folio 30⟧

For the competition
"Small Intercosmos"

The previous issue of our magazine contained information about the All-Union competition for school and professional students called “Small Intercosmos”. Below we provide an approximate list of problems proposed by specialists to the participants of this competition.

1. Earth - Space - Earth

— It is known that the “ceiling” of aviation is about 30 km, and the lower limit of satellite flight is about 180 km. Suggest a physical principle of flight at intermediate altitudes.

— When launching spacecraft, the first stages of the free package disappear, falling to Earth. Develop options for using these stages of the package.

Suggest:

- economic methods of delivering cargo into orbit, a project for a space plane that can be used not only for flights into orbit, but also as an international vehicle;

- the direction and forms of joint work of schoolchildren and space station crews in the interests of the national economy (for example, parallel observations of schoolchildren on Earth, and of space from orbit, of the state of crops, water basins, forests, etc.);

- a way to save space in the event of an emergency on an orbital spacecraft. One of the rescue options is to return the cosmos to Earth using individual means;

— a way to simulate various space conditions on Earth;

— a method for determining the chemical state of the soil by studying an area from space, identifying a deficiency or excess of nutritional substances;

— forms of teaching space science and technology in solving environmental problems;

- a method for studying the Earth's natural resources.

2. House in orbit

— During long flights, the state of space is negatively affected by the monotony of the situation. Suggest a way to change the situation on the station at the request of the cosmos.
