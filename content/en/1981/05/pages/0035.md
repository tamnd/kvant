⟦folio 33⟧

Exercise

1. Decipher the arithmetic example depicted at figure. Here, different letters correspond to different numbers, identical letters correspond to the same ones, and about the letter A it is known that an even number corresponds to it.

2. Find ten consecutive natural numbers, among which a) there is not a single prime number; b)exactly one prime number; c) two prime numbers; d) three prime numbers; d) four prime numbers.
How many prime numbers can there be among ten consecutive natural numbers?

3. On the plane, around points A and B, two rods rotate in the same direction, and rod AA₁ rotates twice as slow as rod BB₁ and at the initial moment ABB₁=90° and BAA₁=45°. At the point M of the intersection of these rods, a ring is put on them. Prove that the ring will move along an arc of a circle with its center at point B. Find the radius of this circle.

4. In the circles of the figure depicted at figure, arrange the numbers from 1 to 19 so that the sum of the numbers located in the circles-vertices of all regular hexagons (there are nine of them!) is constant.

5. In each of the three horizontal rows (see figure), shift one knitting needle so that all six equalities (vertical and horizontal) are true.

These tasks were suggested to us by N. Avilov, N. Antonovich, N. Mikhailenko, S. Savastok.
