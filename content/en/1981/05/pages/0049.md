Gorky State University named after. N. I. Lobachevsky

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(radio faculty)

1. Find all real values ​​of $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$x - 3 < \sqrt{x^2 + 4x} - 5.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos x = \sqrt{3} \sin x + 2 \cos 3x.$$
3. Determine the dimensions of the cylinder with the largest volume if its total surface area is 2π.
4. For harvesting, several identical combines were allocated, which could harvest the field in 24 hours if they started working at the same time. But it was an accident that they started working one after another at regular intervals, and then each worked until the cleaning was completed. How long did it take to harvest the crop if the first combine worked 5 times longer than the last?
5. Prove that $53^{53} - 33^{33}$ is divisible by 10.

Option 2
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

1. Solve the equation
$$\sin 2x + \sin^4 \frac{x}{2} = \cos^4 \frac{x}{2}.$$
2. Find all real values ​​of $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log_{0.25} \frac{35 - x^2}{x} \geq -\frac{1}{2}.$$
3. The sum of the third and ninth terms of an arithmetic progression is equal to the smallest value of the trilen $2x^2 - 4x + 10$. Find the sum of the equal first terms of this progression.
4. Calculate the area of ​​an equilateral trapezoid if its height is $h$ and its side is visible from the center of the circumscribed circle at an angle $60^\circ$.

5. Three workers took part in the competition. The first of them produced twice as much output as the second, and the second and third produced 3 times more than the first. What place did each worker take in the competition?

Option 3
(Faculty of Mechanics, Mathematics and Physics)

1. Find all real values ​​of $x$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log_3 \log_{1/2} \sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}} \geq 1.$$
2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \cos x + \cos y = 1/2, \\ \sin^2 x + \sin^2 y = 7/4. \end{cases}$$
3. The dynamism of a circle of radius $R$ is the base of a regular triangle. Calculate the area of ​​that part of the triangle that lies outside the given circle.
4. Find the most and most values ​​of the function $y = 5 + 4 \cos x - \sin^2 x$ on the segment $[-\frac{\pi}{2}; \frac{\pi}{2}]$.
5. Imagine the number 19 as the difference of cubes of natural numbers.

Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. A weightless vertical stand is installed on a block of mass $2m$, to which a ball of mass $m$ (Fig. 1) is suspended on weightless bars of length $l$. A block can slide across a horizontal table without friction. What will be the speed of the block at the moment the ball passes the bottom point, if the block initially accumulated and the ball was released from point $A$ without an initial speed?
2. Two bodies of masses $m_1 = 4$ kg and $m_2 = 8$ kg, connected by a thread, are moving along an inclined plane forming an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the horizon. The friction coefficients between the inclined plane and the bodies are different: for the first body it is equal to $\mu_1 = 0,1$, and for the second $\mu_2 = 0,2$. What is the force of the string if a body of mass $m_1$ moves with horizontal acceleration $|\vec{a}| = g/2$.
