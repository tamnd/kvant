⟦folio 2⟧

M. Lavrentiev

Invitation
into science

What qualities distinguish a scientist, what character traits should a young man who decides to devote himself to science cultivate in himself?
It is not easy to answer this question briefly.

Let's look at scientists who have done a lot in science. It is characteristic that, regardless of the specialty and talent, each of them invested enormous personal work in science. In certain periods of life (and these periods lasted for years), directed labor reaches the top. Research, forgetting about finishing, works 14-16 hours every day. So words about work in science are not a phrase. This is the law. It remains valid for our time.

What else is typical for research? Complete disconnection from “extraneous” matters that would prevent him from realizing the completed idea. Such “disconnection periods” last, as life shows, for several minutes. From the siege of the fortress, the scientist moves on to its shtum. This is a very important stage, and here a person’s ability to complete the task is tested. During such periods, the researcher trains himself again and again, learns to overcome emerging difficulties, quickly solve puzzling problems that always surround the problem with an invisible at first glance, but very stable fortress wall. Every stage that the wall has fallen, you are already in the fortress, but in front of you there is only a new one with a fortified one, which was not visible before. Like in war: fish, wire, off-road... This happens in mathematics, in engineering - in any field. Therefore, a young scientist needs to prepare himself for Great Patience, to quickly navigate and change tactics in time, call for help or build new “siege weapons” himself.

Academician Mikhail Alekseevich Lavrentiev (1900-1980) - State Prize laureate, honorary chairman of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences.

Strachy is reprinted with minor abbreviations from the collection “Lenin. Sci. Youth", published in 1980 by the Nauka publishing house.
