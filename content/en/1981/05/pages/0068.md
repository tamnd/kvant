We were unable to find any deep mathematical meaning in this design, made of thin cardboard by V. Gamayunov. But this does not prevent us from admiring how surprisingly completely dissimilar geometric shapes sometimes interact: six long beams with a square cross-section penetrate the frame of a complex star-shaped polyhedron, (union of the heel of cubes - see the article “Complex polyhedron” in «Kvant», 1979, No. 1, p. 27), touching its edges, but never destroying their design. Not only mathematicians are attracted by this design - the Soviet architect V. A. Somov took it as the basis for the design of an administrative building in one of the Italian cities.
