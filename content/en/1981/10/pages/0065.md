2. Let there be $a$ in the first bag, $b$ in the second, $c$ in the third and $d$ kg of flour in the fourth. Then
$$\begin{cases} a + b + c \geq 60, \\ a + b + d \leq 50, \\ a + c + d \leq 40, \\ b + c + d \leq 30. \end{cases}$$

Subtracting the second, third and fourth from the first inequality in turn, we get $c - d \geq 10, b - d \geq 20, a - d \geq 30$, whence $c \geq 10, b \geq 20, a \geq 30$, and the fourth - $d = 0$. Therefore, $c \geq 10, b \geq 20, a \geq 30$ and $a + b \leq 63$
