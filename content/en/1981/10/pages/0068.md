TO OUR READERS

The subscription for 1982 to the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant» continues.
«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 6–10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to study the exact sciences on a server in the future. Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren whose interest in the exact sciences is still dormant.
Review articles are published on the pages of our journal, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still awaiting solution; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are born.
The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems from various Olympiads and simply interesting problems, as well as puzzles, crosswords, teawords, and rebuses.
The section “Lab «Kvant»” talks about interesting experiments that can be done at home.
What questions and tasks can an applicant expect during the entrance exams? The reader will find answers to these and many other questions that one encounters when entering a university in the section “Applicant Practice.”
The articles in the section “On the pages of school textbooks” break down the most complex and important questions of mathematics presented in school curricula.
A significant place in the magazine is occupied by information about the work of scientific societies of students, summer schools, competitions and shows of scientific creativity of schoolchildren.
Each issue has a section «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren,” in which, along with entertaining tasks, articles are also published that are accessible to students in grades 6-7.
The magazine constantly publishes solutions to popular science books on physics, mathematics, astronomy and astronautics.

«Kvant» is distributed by subscription only and is not available in retail products. Subscriptions to our magazine are accepted throughout the year.
