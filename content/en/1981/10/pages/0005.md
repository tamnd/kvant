⟦folio 3⟧

Physicists will have to solve enormous problems that should ensure the future prosperity of man.

Modern chemistry develops in close connection with physics, mathematics and biology. Its new areas include high-energy chemistry, which studies chemical transformations under the influence of radiation, light, laser radiation, the action of shock waves, and plasma processes. Electricity is developing, new electrical current sources will be created. Work in the field of corrosion and protection of metals from corrosion is of exceptional national importance. New polymer materials will be created - heat-resistant, high-strength, resistant to aggressive chemical environments, materials for medical purposes. In connection with the problem of full use of limited natural raw materials, petrochemistry, coal chemistry, shale chemistry, peat and wood chemistry are expanding. A special place is occupied by the organization of chemistry with its millions of individual chemical substances that have already found application or may become necessary in the future. Organic chemists synthesize drugs against the most dangerous diseases of humans and animals. Work on the synthesis of artificial food is gaining real importance.

Biology has long been considered a purely descriptive science. However, deep penetration into the existence of biological phenomena led to the intensive development of living cell biology, biochemistry and physiology of animals, humans and plants, genetics, biophysics, and research into the process of photosynthesis. Molecular biology has exceptional attractiveness and prospects not only for understanding the mechanism of life of phenomena, but also for controlling life processes. The interests of many natural sciences effectively intertwine here.

Now we have about a thousand colleges, in which almost 5 million students study. Enormous numbers that no country in the world knows!

Now the Soviet Union has the largest number of scientific workers in the world - almost 1.3 million people. Industry needs raw materials, and geology is tirelessly exploring the depths of the earth in search of new sources of raw materials. The country needs specialized agriculture, forestry, technological food products and consumer goods. We need economists, health workers, teachers, arts and cultural workers and many other specialists. When these specialties are listed, another thought comes to mind. how to replace this, that our country will always need more and more specialties, that no graduates of our colleges will know what unemployment is.

In my opinion, young people’s inclination towards one profession or another is usually revealed very early, perhaps in the fifth or sixth grade of school. Therefore, sometimes even in the pre-university period, a young man acquires some professional horizons, gets a general idea of ​​the future, about work, even manages to collect a library of more or less popular science books, collects clippings from newspapers about his favorite profession... In this case, he, still very young in age, develops a sense of business adulthood that impresses us around us. It’s especially good when you’ve already tried to make something with your own hands - whether it’s fascinating model airplanes or robots, whether you’ve assembled radios, don’t use electricity, you know how to handle chemicals... Infantility at an age close to adulthood is a big drawback. What I called business adulthood is the key to successful and deep understanding of the profession. After all, one must master the chosen specialty perfectly, know it down to the last detail, and be able to do everything with one’s own hands.
