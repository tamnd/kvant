TWENTIETH IS EXTRA

In sixteen free circles, arrange the numbers from 1 to 20, with the exception of 6, 12, 18 and one more number, so that the sums of the numbers located on each of the nine indicated circles and each of the three indicated dynamers are the same.

A. Medyanik
