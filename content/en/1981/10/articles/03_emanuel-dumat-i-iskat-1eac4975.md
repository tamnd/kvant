Think
and search

I will talk about the most pressing problems that today’s schoolchildren and students will have to solve, about the problems that will “transition” into the 21st century, about what qualities special new paganism should forge in themselves.

In the multi-million army of Science today there are many specialized units, but I would like to say a few words about the basic, fundamental sciences - mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology.

Abstract in form, science - mathematics - is increasingly becoming a tool for studying a harmonious and strict description of the phenomena with which the natural, social and technical sciences deal. The development of science shows that in the near future the role of mathematics will increase so much that, no matter what specialty a person masters, he will need an excellent modern mathematical apparatus for work.

In the outgoing 20th century, mankind has been enriched in the field of physics with such revolutionary discoveries as the fission of uranium nuclei (as a result of which atomic technology and energy arose), the discovery of thermonuclear fusion (associated with a huge release of energy), quantum generators - lasers (which have found the widest range of applications), the creation of transistors (which made it possible to develop modern radio electronics and electronic computer technology). Physicists will have to solve enormous problems that should ensure the future prosperity of man.

Modern chemistry develops in close connection with physics, mathematics and biology. Its new areas include high-energy chemistry, which studies chemical transformations under the influence of radiation, light, laser radiation, the action of shock waves, and plasma processes. Electricity is developing, new electrical current sources will be created. Work in the field of corrosion and protection of metals from corrosion is of exceptional national importance. New polymer materials will be created - heat-resistant, high-strength, resistant to aggressive chemical environments, materials for medical purposes. In connection with the problem of full use of limited natural raw materials, petrochemistry, coal chemistry, shale chemistry, peat and wood chemistry are expanding. A special place is occupied by the organization of chemistry with its millions of individual chemical substances that have already found application or may become necessary in the future. Organic chemists synthesize drugs against the most dangerous diseases of humans and animals. Work on the synthesis of artificial food is gaining real importance.

Biology has long been considered a purely descriptive science. However, deep penetration into the existence of biological phenomena led to the intensive development of living cell biology, biochemistry and physiology of animals, humans and plants, genetics, biophysics, and research into the process of photosynthesis. Molecular biology has exceptional attractiveness and prospects not only for understanding the mechanism of life of phenomena, but also for controlling life processes. The interests of many natural sciences effectively intertwine here.

Now we have about a thousand colleges, in which almost 5 million students study. Enormous numbers that no country in the world knows!

Now the Soviet Union has the largest number of scientific workers in the world - almost 1.3 million people. Industry needs raw materials, and geology is tirelessly exploring the depths of the earth in search of new sources of raw materials. The country needs specialized agriculture, forestry, technological food products and consumer goods. We need economists, health workers, teachers, arts and cultural workers and many other specialists. When these specialties are listed, another thought comes to mind. how to replace this, that our country will always need more and more specialties, that no graduates of our colleges will know what unemployment is.

In my opinion, young people’s inclination towards one profession or another is usually revealed very early, perhaps in the fifth or sixth grade of school. Therefore, sometimes even in the pre-university period, a young man acquires some professional horizons, gets a general idea of ​​the future, about work, even manages to collect a library of more or less popular science books, collects clippings from newspapers about his favorite profession... In this case, he, still very young in age, develops a sense of business adulthood that impresses us around us. It’s especially good when you’ve already tried to make something with your own hands - whether it’s fascinating model airplanes or robots, whether you’ve assembled radios, don’t use electricity, you know how to handle chemicals... Infantility at an age close to adulthood is a big drawback. What I called business adulthood is the key to successful and deep understanding of the profession. After all, one must master the chosen specialty perfectly, know it down to the last detail, and be able to do everything with one’s own hands. Renno troubleshoots problems; it is as beautiful, graceful and aesthetically pleasing as playing a musical instrument virtuoso.

Professionalism is a good quality that must be developed in every possible way. And there is one more trait that you should try to develop in yourself to the level of development of your science. Knowing what has already been formed, the solution that formed the basis of this or that arbitrariness, construction, get used to thinking about whether it is possible to solve the problem in a different way. Try to find new solutions yourself, more effective, original ones. Invent, invent!

I am deeply convinced that it is possible - and should! - develop similar traits in yourself. Original creativity must become widespread. A creative critical approach also helps in the education of incidia, which is absolutely necessary for specialization.

In his famous letter to youth, published back in 1935, the great physicist I. P. Pavlov wrote about modesty. He wrote very strictly, demandingly, insisting on the persistent accumulation of knowledge, saying that no matter how highly others value your knowledge, you must always have the courage to say to yourself: “I am ignorant.” Written harshly and harshly. By the way, while a person is still young, business immodesty, lack of self-criticism and complacency are sometimes not so noticeable, but if he, growing up, continues to be like this, the harm he brings to society can become very noticeable. Especially if such a person finds himself in a leadership position.

There is an old and well-known truth - “a student must surpass his teacher.” The concept of “teacher” should not be taken in the literal sense of the word. The teacher may not bother with the student, choosing various truths in his head. But the student must take from the teacher everything good and valuable that he has, and almost further, create and develop that new, progressive that is inherent in him. He who is called a teacher usually feels a deep sense of satisfaction when his students go beyond himself. Only in a few sad cases does a teacher fight after his students. And this is rather a rare exception. Therefore, look carefully at your teachers. Get satisfaction from the fact that you will learn to express thoughts in their style, and then, perhaps, you will be able to develop your own, which may turn out to be more original, comprehensible, and interesting. If a teacher writes scientific papers, monographs, textbooks, read them, grasp the logic of his reasoning, learn to write the way he does, and then develop your own handwriting, your own style of presentation.

No matter how great these tips are, they can be useful and can contribute to professional growth.

In conclusion, I would like to wish you the following: you have entered an age when the value of every day you live is so great that you have no right to yourself and to society to take breaks for nothing. Think, search, learn, create yourself and the world - every day!
