I would like to express a few thoughts addressed directly to young people going into science.

Young people have enormous advantages, which they do not always realize and therefore do not have the opportunity to use. After all, youth is a huge wealth that gives every person, but, unfortunately, only for a while. Not losing it, eliminating it quickly is the main condition for your subsequent life to be productive, meaningful and joyful.

Young years are the most creative years in a person's life. It is what he manages to do in his youth that remains with him forever, forms the foundation that he then uses throughout his life. Time wasted in youth is irretrievable. To consume it exclusively for entertainment, or for purely formal teaching, or the thoughtless use of duties means to rob oneself, to destroy the abilities that have not yet developed in oneself. Therefore, one must treat one’s young years with special attention and with special demands.

Some young people naively believe that youth is the time when you need to take everything from life. And they don’t really know what “everyone” is. And to take everything from life means to absorb into oneself a huge mass of knowledge, including skills, to improve and deepen them in the continuation of a creative conscious life. And, of course, give it back to the community. Life experience shows that the sooner you find your goal, your place in life, the more fruitful and meaningful all your future activities will be. This can be seen in the example of great Russian and Soviet scientists: Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky, Dmitry

It is common for youth to aim at big things, to make decisive changes, to comprehend major global problems. This is wonderful, but any most beautiful idea will remain a toy, a soap bubble, if it is not implemented. Therefore, we must try not only to plan, invent, propose, but always to do, to do a specific task and be able to bring it to the end. After all, it is the sum, the integral of all specific affairs that essentially creates all our social wealth, all the potential in any area of ​​human activity.

Young people who are interested in sports know well that in order to achieve results and maintain athletic shape, a lot of work is needed. This applies even more to creative abilities. Only if you train your intellect, load it with tasks, search for solutions, only then will it not weaken and will develop and grow stronger.

One should not think that this applies only to young people who are engaged in scientific research. Nalo tries to develop a creative approach to life in any field of activity. Only this approach makes it possible to deeply understand the reality of things, phenomena, processes in production, in the economy, and in public life. First of all, you need to pronounce in depth, in the very case, the shirata will come only later as a consequence. Beware of a superficial approach to knowledge - this will close your path to creativity.

The most important thing in a professional scientist is to be able to reveal the discrepancy between the knowledge accumulated by society and the discovered phenomena or facts that may seem, at first glance, to be errors in an experiment or in the course of reasoning. A thorough study of the contradiction that has arisen and the search for a way to resolve it, as a rule, only allows us to look at things more deeply, but also often opens up new paths in science.

I remember an incident that happened in one of the Volga cities. I was invited to the university to read a report on problems in mathematics, and I accepted with satisfaction. After the report, one of the interviewees, who called himself an associate professor at the university, came up to me and said:

- It’s good for you in Academy Town, you have all the problems you want there, but where can we get them from us?

I was not only amazed, but also downright shocked by this question.

- Allow me, dear colleague, take on any topic, work, and as soon as you come across something incomprehensible, here is your problem... Take on it and solve it.

The specialty of the two-thousandth year seems to me to be widely educated not in one field of science, but at least in two or three: the laws of development of the scientific and technological revolution will certainly present such a “bill.”

When I use the term “broad,” I mean, of course, the thoroughness and serviceability of knowledge. And I can clearly see the path to forming such a specialty. After graduating from a university (let’s take a university as an example), the main task of its graduate is to enter the field of personnel worth the work that will be entrusted to him. Most likely, in a narrow scientific problem. By exploring it, a young specialist actually deeply develops his own creative skills, skills. This takes three to five years. During this period, the young specialist becomes—cannot help but become—a “professor” in his “narrow field.”

After this, he needs to switch to a funny field of science, perhaps even far from the narrow problem in which he specialized. Enter a new area of ​​knowledge - that is, master
