⟦folio 52⟧

The “device” for the experiment is made with your own hands.

Jury members prepare experiments for a physics competition.

Photo by E. Shablyzin and A. Klopotovich firing double-barreled guns. One puck hits the center with a bullet, and the other hits the side. Bullets get stuck in washers. Which puck will quickly reach the wall? There is no training.

The answer is simple: since the initial impulse of the pucks with bullets is the same and under the given “puck-bullet” conditions the impulse is conserved, then both pucks will reach the wall at the same time. But many who solved this problem gave the following reasoning: the energy of a bullet that hits the puck in the side is spent on rotation and translational movement of the puck, and the energy of the second bullet is spent only on translational motion. This means that the second puck has greater kinetic energy and moves faster. Where is the mistake here?

Problem 2 (theoretical physics). One ball lies on the table, and the other is suspended on a thread. Which ball has the greater thermal heat if they are exactly the same?

Let us remember that specific heat is the amount of heat that must be transferred to a body in order to heat it by one degree. Since the balls are the same, the change in their internal energy is the same. However, when heated, bodies expand. In this case, the center of gravity of a ball lying on the table rises, and that of a ball hanging on a thread lowers. Sledova-
