Botvinnik loses and... wins
The fiftieth years passed under the sign of rivalry between Mikhail Botvinnik and Vasily Smyslov. If in the 1954 match the challenger was limited to an honorable draw, then in 1957 he already managed to climb to the top (score 12.5:9.5). Bot episode from the sixth game of the match.

Botvinnik prepared excellently for the rematch and a year later confidently regained his crown (12.5:10.5). He started the match with three “ordinary” victories, and after the 15th game the score could have become 10:5.
