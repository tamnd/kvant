A year of great discoveries. The year 1610, which began with the discovery of the satellites of Jupiter, was extremely happy for Galil the astronomical scientist: he made almost all of his remarkable astronomical observations in this year. On July 25, Galileo again observed “Jupiter in the morning in the East with his retinue.” After this, he discovered “another extraordinary miracle.” He reports his discovery to Florence, keeps it secret until publication: “The star of Saturn is not only one, but consists of three, which seem to touch each other, but do not move or change among themselves..., and the middle one is about three times larger than the two side ones; and they are arranged in this form.”
