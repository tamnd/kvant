⟦folio 50⟧

Quests

1. At what values ​​of $n$ in the expression
   *1**2*...*$n$ = $n+1$
   Is it possible to arrange the signs “+” and “—” together * so that it turns out to be a true equality?

2. A triangle with sides 3 is divided into two small triangles with sides 1 (see figure).

a) Arrange the numbers from 1 to 9 in them so that the sums of the four numbers in the three triangles with sides 2 are the same.

6) What values ​​can this amount take?

3. Two children, completely confused about counting the days of the week, were left on the way to school to figure it all out.

“When “the day after tomorrow” becomes “yesterday,” said Prisilla, “then “today” will be as far from resurrection as the day that was “today” when “the day before yesterday” was “tomorrow.”

On what day of the week was this vocal babble uttered?

4. Is it possible to color a square grid of size $n \times n$ ($n \geqslant 3$) in six colors so that any rectangle $2 \times 3$ contains cells of all six colors?

5. In the fairy-tale country of Perra-Terra, among other common people, live Kababas and Barabas. Each kababas is familiar with six kababas and nine barabas. Each kababas is familiar with ten kababas and seven barabas. When is there more cababas or barabas in this country?

These tasks were suggested to us by Yu. Alekkov, F. Bartenev, A. Cabrizon, N. Nechvetaev. Problem No. 3 is taken from the book by S. Loyd “Mathematical mosaic” (M., “Mir”, 1980, problem 31).
