Tasks

M691—M695; F703—F707

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the current school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given asterisks. After forming a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The problems in this number were proposed at the final round of the All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren. The number in brackets indicates the class in which the tasks are offered. Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than September 15, 1981 to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 7 - 81” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M691, M692” or “F703”. Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision review in this envelope). Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in two copies along with your solution to this problem.

M691. We will say that a number has property (K) if it decomposes into the product of K consecutive natural numbers greater than 1. a) Find a K such that some number N has both properties (K) and (K+2).

6) Prove that numbers that have both properties (2) and (4) do not exist. (8)

M692. Points $C_1, A_1, B_1$ are taken on the sides, respectively, $AB, BC, CA$ of triangle $ABC$ so that $|AC_1| : |C_1B| = |BA_1| : |A_1C| = |CB_1| : |B_1A| = 1:3.$

Prove that the perimeter $P$ of triangle $ABC$ and the perimeter $P_1$ of triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ are related by the inequalities a) $P_1 < \frac{3}{4}$ P; 6) $P_1 > \frac{1}{2}$ P. (8)

V. Turchaninov

M693. In some of the last 1000 inhabitants. Every day, each of them shares the news they learned yesterday with all their friends. It is known that any news becomes known to all residents of the village. Prove that you can select 90 residents so that if you tell them all some news at the same time, then after 10 days it will become known to all residents of the village. (9)

N. Kartashov, A. Savin
