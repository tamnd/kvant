ON STAMPS - FRANKLIN

The outstanding American natural and public figure Benjamin Franklin, whose 275th birthday is celebrated in this issue of Quat, left a significant mark on world philately. Several dozen postage stamps are dedicated to him. A large number of them, naturally, were released in the USA. The first American stamp with Franklin's portrait was issued in 1847. Together with a stamp depicting George Washington, it formed the first series of stamps with owners throughout the United States. 28 American stamps dedicated to Franklin were added to it. Two of them, released in 1908 and 1922, are shown in our selection. Stamps with Franklin's image traditionally opened new postage series in the United States.

In 1956, a number of countries solemnly celebrated the 250th anniversary of Franklin's birth. In our country, a ceremonial meeting of the Academy of Sciences was dedicated to this event. Franklin's anniversary was celebrated by issuing special postage stamps. We will reproduce here the stamps of the USSR, Romania and Bulgaria dedicated to this event.

V. Leshkovtsev
