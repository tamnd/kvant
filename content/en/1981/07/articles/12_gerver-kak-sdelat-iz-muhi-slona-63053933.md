To transform a goat into a wolf, it seems that only two substitutions are necessary: ​​you need to replace “z” and “a” with “v” and “l”. If at least one of the replacements

h - c; z - l; a - c; a - l
led to a meaningful word, then we would thereby get a chain of three words that turns a goat into a wolf. However, after the indicated substitutions we arrive at the sets k, o, b, a; k, o, h, l; k, o, l, a; k, o, z, v, none of which - no matter how you rearrange the letters in it - gives a meaningful word. Glory-exile

The tasks for creating a chain that were offered to you were arranged like this: given words, you need to connect them with a chain (arbitrary or the shortest). The words were specially selected so that they could actually be transformed into each other.

It must be difficult to select such pairs? Probably, for most pairs of words, it is impossible to turn one into another (there is not a single chain connecting them)?

Try making mini pairs. If you take four-letter words, you will be surprised to discover that using their mail will always turn them into each other. Contrary to expectation, the difficulty is not in finding words that can be connected in a chain, but, on the contrary, in finding four-letter words that cannot be connected in a chain.

This is not true for long words. Among them are common “glory-exile”, which (further according to the new rules) cannot be connected in a chain with any other word.
