⟦folio 35⟧

Tasks

1. "Permutations." Have you ever had to rearrange the letters in words to form different meaningful words? For example, like this: mother - darkness, river - square, sickle - Persian, uedar - glad. Choose a paired word for the words: coxa, kleusi, colb, pheasant, spring, lettuce, splash, crackle, repair, picet, tsolka, cordon, vector, sector, teleson, art, sect, rock, pirate, axe, hood, falcon, boat, board.

Sometimes “permutations” give not a pair, but three, four, five or even six words:
march - ? - ?, octopus - ? - ?, height - ? - ? - ?, cleaver - ? - ? - ?, author - ? - ? - ? - ?.

What other “rearrangements” can you come up with?

2. In the cells of the “pyramid” shown at figure, embroider all ten numbers (0,1,...,9) so that along the horizontal you get four squares of natural numbers. How many solutions does the problem have?

3. From ten matches you can easily make two pentagons. But making two pentagons and five triangles from the same number of matches is not so easy. Try it!

4. There are 1980 points on a sheet of paper, which are the vertices of a regular 1980-gon. Two players take turns connecting these points with segments. In one move, the cut is made to connect any two points so that the drawn segments do not intersect. The one who has no move loses. Who wins when played correctly: the beginner or his partner?

These tasks were offered to us
A. Abbasov, M. Gerver, A. Kravchaty,
