⟦folio 64⟧

CHESS PHILATELY

In sports philately, stamps dedicated to chess do not occupy much of a place. However, they come out regularly. In our country alone, fifteen of them were produced (with toothless blades and a block). In large systems, the issue of such stamps is associated with major international chess competitions, but there are others.

In our selection, on the third page of the cover from the top there is a series of Cuban stamps dedicated to those issued by 显微镜 - world champions Capablanca and Lasker, as well as Philator, Steinitz and Segura. Next comes the stamps and block of the Arab principality of Al-Fujairah, which feature chess pieces made of bone or valuable wood and representing beautiful works of applied art. Below are rouge stamps dedicated to the Chess Olympiad that took place in 1980 on the island of Malta.
