Physics
Oral exam assignment

1. A body thrown vertically upward returns to the ground after a time of $t = 3$ s. What was the initial speed of the body? What is the maximum height the body has been to? Ignore air resistance.

2. A cart with sand rolls at a speed of $|\vec{v}_1| = 1$ m/s on a horizontal surface without friction. A ball of mass $m = 2$ kg flies towards the cart with a horizontal speed $|\vec{v}_2| = 7$ m/s. After meeting the cart, the ball got stuck in the sand. At what speed and in what direction will the cart move after meeting the ball? Trolley weight $M = 10$ kg.

3. A negative charge was placed midway between two exact positive charges $q_1 = q_2 = q = 2 \times 10^{-10}$ Cl. What is the magnitude of this charge if the system is in equilibrium?

4. A ball of mass $m = 10^{-3}$ kg moves from point $A$ with potential $\varphi_1 = 600$ V to point $B$, whose potential is $\varphi_2 = 0$. Determine the speed of the ball at point $A$ if at point $B$ its speed is $|\vec{v}| = 0,2$ m/s. Ball charge $q = 10^{-8}$ Cl.

A. Nazaretov, G. Tkachev, G. Shadrin

Moscow Institute of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartography Engineers

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Optical Instrumentation Engineering)

1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{3(x-2) + 4\sqrt{2x^2-3x+1}}{2(x^2-1)} = 1.$$

2. Find the domain of a function
$$\log_{x+0,5}\frac{x^2+2x-3}{4x^2-4x-3}.$$

3. Prove that $\triangle ABC$, whose vertices are located at points $A(1; 0; 1)$, $B(1; 1; 0)$ and $C(1; 1; 1)$, is straight. Find the distance from the origin of the coordinate system to the center of the circle circumscribed about this triangle.

4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1+\mathrm{tg} x}{1-\mathrm{tg} x} = (\sin x + \cos x)^2.$$

5. Find the area of ​​the figure bounded by lines $y = 2x^2 + 1$, $y = x + 2$ and $y = 1,5$.

Option 2
(faculty of geodesy)

1. Solve the equation
$$3 \cdot 16^x + 2 \cdot 81^x = 5 \cdot 36^x.$$

2. Find the domain of a function
$$y = \log_{100x}\frac{2\lg x + 2}{-x}.$$

3. The given vectors are $\vec{a}(1; 1; -1)$, $\vec{b}(5; -3; -3)$ and $\vec{c}(3; -1; 2)$. Find vectors, collinear vectors $\vec{c}$, whose length is equal to the length of vector $\vec{a} + \vec{b}$.

4. Solve the equation
$(\sin x + \cos x)^2 + (\sin x - \cos x)^2 = 1 - \cos 2x.$

5. Examine the function for monotonicity
$$y = \frac{x^2-2}{2x+3}.$$

Option 3
(aerial photogeodetic and cartography faculties)

1. Solve the equation
$$-\frac{1}{3}\lg 0,001 + \lg 3\sqrt{27} + 3\sqrt{2x} = 2.$$

2. Solve inequality
$$x < \sqrt{3}-2x.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{\cos x} = \sin x + \cos x.$$

4. For what $x$ and $y$ is the vector $\vec{a} = x\vec{i} + y\vec{j} + 2\vec{k}$ orthogonal to the vector $\vec{b} = \vec{i} - \vec{j} + \vec{k}$ and the inner product of $\vec{a}$ by $\vec{c} = \vec{i} + 2\vec{j}$ equal to four?

5. Find the area of ​​the figure bounded by the lines $y = \frac{4}{x}$, $y = 0$, $x = 2$ and $x = 4$.

N. Bolotina,
