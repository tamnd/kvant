⟦folio 3⟧

At the same time, it should be said that the fundamental principles of any branch of science are few and, generally speaking, very simple. Simple is true, but to understand these simple principles (which should be the main result of education) it is necessary to master the meaning of the concepts used, the existing characteristic quantities, the correct definition of various objects and learn the operations of theoretical and experimental actions with the help of which such simple hypotheses and laws are formulated. Possession of modern methods and skills in theory, experiment, and practice is mandatory for success in science and technology.

Thus, the main thing in education and creative work is the accumulation of knowledge, methodological skills and understanding. It should be emphasized that understanding is impossible without knowledge, but knowledge of various factors and even effective methods of dealing with them is not necessarily associated with understanding the day of the matter. You can meet people who know a lot and understand little. Serious successes are associated with actual understanding, therefore it is necessary to add to life, in science and in practice, understanding, and only that knowledge that is absolutely necessary for understanding is especially important. Consequently, success in education and in creative work requires a maximum of understanding while using a minimum (often very large now) of necessary information. When teaching, the teacher must draw from a huge accumulation of information exactly that which will provide the required understanding. Then young scientists will easily be able to navigate this branch of knowledge and find the necessary additional information in the course of their specific activities.

In many sciences, as well as in art, systematic and correct training from a young age is required. Incorrect or only deliberate assimilation of knowledge from a young age has harmful consequences, since it is difficult and sometimes impossible to relearn.

It is necessary to develop in every possible way enthusiasts' interest in acquiring knowledge, which arises and flourishes precisely in the team - at school and university. In education and in educational activities, it is necessary to instill in young people an independent, critical perception of facts in the development of scientific knowledge and a self-critical attitude towards their own actions.

Self-responsibility, obsession, deep inner nourishment stimulates the understanding of existing nature and scientific achievements and certainly contributes to the commitment to one’s own actions.

Each of you is a member of a team, a team of colleagues, comrades of the older and younger generations. Without communication and work in a team, without the technology of working with a team, it is now generally impossible to achieve any significant results.

Fundamental knowledge is acquired in youth - at school and university, but the process of improvement and further learning continues throughout a person’s creative activity. However, you need to remember that it is in your youth that you acquire basic knowledge and methodological skills, and this will leave its mark on all your future activities.

Dear friends! Wide doors are open to you for creative activity for the benefit and glory of our Motherland, for the benefit of all humanity. Do not miss this opportunity from a young age, it will certainly serve as a source of deep inner satisfaction for you, great joy and happiness that you will find in your work activity.
