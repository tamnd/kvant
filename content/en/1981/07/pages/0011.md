⟦folio 9⟧

Coplay Medal awarded to Franklin for his discoveries in the field of electricity.

During the struggle over the issue of the lightning rod, all methods were used, slander, insinuation, both personally against Franklin and against his friends. Franklin remained very supportive, regardless of personal attacks, and invariably said that in matters of science, the truth is revealed only by experience. Indeed, experience resolved this dispute, but many tens of years later, when the doctrine of lightning discharges and the electric field reached the modern level. Now we see that this whole dispute had no basis, since for a general lightning rod it does not matter how it ends, with a point or a blunt end. At a short distance from the ground, the geometric shape of the end of the lightning rod cannot significantly influence the distribution of the electric field above the ground.

But one of the leading specialists in lightning discharges, Dr. Schonland, points out that the process of neutralizing the charge of a cloud through a quiet discharge, predicted by Franklin, can still be carried out, but only when the tip of the lightning rod is at such a great distance from the ground that it is comparable to the height of the cloud. This is the case for lightning rods placed on the tallest American skyscrapers; then it is actually possible to observe from the tip of the rod a quiet discharge that does not turn into lightning. Schonland adds that it must certainly have been a feeling of just satisfaction for Franklin if he could have known it.

I’ll just briefly talk about Franklin’s activities in other areas.

in science, since, in addition to the described meanings of achievements, he also has achievements in other areas.

Franklin is engaged in geophysics, gave a map of the Gulf Stream test, invented a musical instrument with glass balls, an economic stove, which is still distributed in America and France, street lamps, double glasses for senile distant people, and much more. In addition, thanks to his public character and lively mind, Franklin advised a lot and contributed to the development of science. Of course, information about most of these consultations has sunk into eternity, but some have reached us.

For example, Louis XVI asked Franklin to be a member of the commission on the value of the method of treatment proposed by Dr. Mesmer, who used the so-called “animal magnetism.” Interestingly, Dr. Guillotin, the inventor of the guillotine, participated in the same commission. Franklin denied the existence of animal magnetism, but believed that it was not a harmful method of treatment, since it entertained wealthy people without causing harm, which cannot always be said about other unsubstantiated medicinal methods of treatment.

Franklin was very approving of the flights of the Montgolf brothers.

Franklin believed that scientific achievements are the property of all people and the concern for the development of world science should stand outside of political and military contradictions.
