Such chains are much easier to make than ordinary chains. Indeed, in a conventional chain it is necessary to weld the internal links, having previously engaged them with neighboring ones, while in our chains all links (except for the two end ones) are made independently. It is not without reason that this design of the product is used in everyday life (door chains - see photo 2) and for decoration (ancient pendants of the peoples of the Caucasus - see photo 1).
