⟦folio 2⟧

Happy new school year!

Beautiful summer days fly by quickly. The school holidays are over. And again the bell invites you to spacious, bright classrooms, you meet old friends, share impressions, and make plans for the future.

For many of you, the school summer was not only a meat of relaxation, but also a meat of hard work. About three million students spent their holidays in labor and recreation camps and helped collective and state farms grow and harvest crops. Student school teams were created in 45 thousand schools. They united 2.5 million students in grades 8–10. The guys worked in the fields, in forestry departments, at manufacturing enterprises, construction sites, and in the service sector. The school of labor training on the threshold of independent life is extremely useful for our youth.

This year is a significant one in the life of our people. This is the year of the XXVI Congress of the CPSU, the first year of a new, identical five-year plan. Congress paid great attention to the state of school affairs. In the report of the General Secretary of the CPSU Central Committee L.I. Brezhnev, in particular, he said: “An important milestone has been reached - the transition to compulsory universal secondary education has been completed. The main thing today is to improve the quality of education, labor and legal education in school, to eliminate formalism in assessing the results of teacher and student work, to strengthen the connection between learning and life, to improve the preparation of schoolchildren for useful work.”

In the same five-year period, our country must achieve new major successes towards increasing efficiency and improving the quality of products in all levels of the national economy. These requirements are dictated by life and, above all, by the ongoing scientific and technological revolution. Using its achievements, you can radically change the effectiveness of any work, any professional activity. This means that the demands placed on workers in virtually any workplace are growing. These requirements can only be met by successfully mastering the achievements of modern science and technology.
