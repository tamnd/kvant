⟦folio 5⟧

We are obliged to help, but not to discriminate. Scientific dogma is harmful. It hindered the development of science in ancient times, during Lomonosov’s time, and in our days.

However, among young scientists there are also those who, from the very first steps, themselves try not to look for “untrodden paths.” They are dissatisfied with this arrangement, desire insufficient leadership, and are inclined to move along the “freeways” of science, mistakenly believing that such movement is fast and more effective.

In most cases, such young scientists, after a year or two, begin to feel that the path of real science is not here, that sometimes they need to go completely off-road, across virgin lands, where every step costs enormous labor. But it is precisely in this work that the will of a scientist and a strong desire for independent work are formed. If such a complete turnaround does not occur in the consciousness of a young man or girl, they should not devote themselves to science.

Scientific leadership should never reach the level at which young people are taken by the hand. I always used this method back in the days when I was only five or six years older than my young students. And never in my life did I regret it.

You can work creatively and with passion in all areas of science and technology. This is confirmed by many wonderful examples of the work of our youth, building huge cities and factories, raising millions of hectares of virgin land, creating complex machines and new materials. And one should not think that a young scientist is only one who works in accordance with the tools of academicians and professors, has scientific significance or an academic degree. Science is driven forward by young innovators, rationalizers, inventors, each working hard in their own field, creating a lot of new things that form the foundation of the majestic edifice of science.

In the search for something new, failures are inevitable. But a true scientist should not be afraid of them and should not give up. After all, it is the teachers who often help in finding the right path, showing in which direction one should not move, and in which one one should work even harder. Therefore, it often happens that analyzing unsuccessful experiments leads to great discoveries.

Scientific work in any field opens up an inexhaustible source of joy. Discoveries and achievements, increasing the glory of our great Motherland, thereby bring glory and great satisfaction to individual scientists.

Be bold in science, do not give up in the face of difficulties! Before the youth lies an inexhaustible virgin land that will bring the people a harvest of unprecedented abundance.
