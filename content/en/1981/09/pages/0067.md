Timman. For A. Karpov, perfection was like a dress rehearsal before the world championship match, and, naturally, he was forced to play with restraint, without revealing opening secrets from the wounds of time (maybe that’s why the world champion only once started the game with any move 1.e2 - e4). Kasparov, L. Polugaevsky and V. Smyslov).
