Below is the entry assignment for Physics and Mathematics. In physics, problems 1-5 are intended for students in the sixth grade, problems 3-9 - for students in the eighth grade, and problems 6-12 - for students in the ninth grade. In mathematics, problems 1–5 are for eighth grades, 4–10 for eighth grades, and 7–13 for ninth grades.

Introductory task

Physics

1. To pump water from a well $h=7$ m deep, a pump is used, the useful engine power of which is $N=500$ W. How long will it take the engine to pump out $V=10$ m³ of water from the well?

2. A bucket contains a mixture of water and ice with a total mass of $m=10$ kg. What amount of ice was in the mixture if, when adding $V=2$ liters of hot water with a temperature of $t_1=80^\circ$ C, the temperature of the water in the bucket turned out to be equal to $t_2=10^\circ$ C?

3. An electrical circuit consisting of resistances $R_1$, $R_2$ and $R_3$ is connected to two constant voltage sources $U_1$ and $U_2$ as shown in figure 1. Under what conditions will the current through resistance $R_1$ be zero?

4. The speed of the swimmer relative to the water is equal in absolute value to $v=0,5$ m/s, the speed of the river flow is $u=0,3$ m/s. In what direction must a swimmer move in order for him to reach the opposite point on the other side? How long will it take him to swim if the width of the river is $l=40$ m?

5. A balloon of mass $M=120$ kg descends at a constant speed. How much ballast must be thrown out for the ball to begin to rise at the same speed? The Archimedean force $F_A(|F_A|=980$ N) is considered constant.

6. A car with a mass of $M=2$ t rises uniformly along a highway with an inclination angle of $\alpha=12^\circ$. Determine how much the pressure of the front and rear wheels will be turned off on the highway, if it is known that the distance between the axles of the wheels is $L=3$ m, and the center of the automatic transmission is located at equal distances from the axles at a height of $H=1$ m.

7. The circuit shown on figure 2 consists of two identical resistances $R_2=R_3=R$ and two identical non-linear resistances $R_1=R_4$, the current-voltage characteristic of which has the form $U=\alpha I^2$, where $\alpha$ is a non-linear constant coefficient. At what voltage of the power source $U_0$ is the current through the galvanometer G equal to zero?

8. At what length of day would bodies at the Earth's equator weigh half as much as those at the pole? The radius of the Earth is $R_3=6400$ km.

9. A man of mass $m=70$ kg jumps from the shore into a boat standing in still water. Its speed is horizontal and equal in absolute value to $v_0=3$ m/s. How far will the boat move? The friction force of a boat on water is proportional to speed, the proportionality coefficient is $k=35$ N·s/m.

10. There are $n=2$ moles of ideal gas in the cylinder under the piston. Determine the initial temperature of the gas if, when imparting an amount of heat $Q=18$ kJ to it, the volume increases $k=2,5$ times. Molar heat procedure of a gas at constant pressure $C_p=21$ J/(mol K).
