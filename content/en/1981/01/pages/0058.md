⟦folio 56⟧

A new example, the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes and vocational school students. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their surrounding areas are not accepted at the school.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1981. School education is free.

Students admitted to the school will regularly receive assignments that contain binding theoretical questions and assignments to solve. The VZMS program is closely related to the school curriculum and is aimed at deepening the study of the main issues of the school mathematics course. The duration of training is three years. Everyone who successfully completes the tasks receives a certificate of completion of the VZMSH.

The tasks of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enroll in VZMSh must send the solution to these problems no later than March 1. After checking the work (around July), VZMSH will inform everyone who took part in the competition about the results of the work. The advantage when entering the user is schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from common school problems, solving them does not require additional knowledge of mathematics. To enroll in school, it is not necessary to complete all assignments without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each task must be justified. An answer without justification may not be read. If a task cannot be proven, a solution to it is not issued.

In the envelope along with the notebook, you must put a piece of paper measuring 14 cm x 6 cm with the full postal address (this piece of paper will be pasted on the envelope with the information to the Admissions Committee of the Higher School of Music on the results of checking the entrance work).

Introductory test assignment at VZMSH in 1981

1. Zhenya lost 20% in the spring, then gained 30% over the summer, lost 20% again in the fall, and gained 10% over the winter. Has his weight remained the same this year, decreased or increased?

2. The numbers 1, 2, ..., 9 make up three three-digit numbers (each digit is used
only used once). Let's consider the difference between the largest and smallest of them. What, at the very least, can this difference be equal to?

3. A trapezoid is constructed, the diagonals of which are perpendicular. Prove that the length of its height is not greater than the length of the midline.

4. Is it possible to grow 25 numbers in a 5x5 square?
