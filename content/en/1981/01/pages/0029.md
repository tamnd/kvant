F674. Sulfuric anhydride $SO_3$ in an amount of $\gamma_1 = 1$ mole was placed in a closed vessel and heated to a temperature of $T_1 = 1000$ K, at which $SO_3$ partially dissociates into sulfur dioxide $SO_2$ and oxygen: $SO_3 = SO_2 + \frac{1}{2}O_2$. The degree of dissociation under these conditions turns out to be $\alpha_1 = 0,2$ (that is, 20% of the first molecules present were dissociated $SO_3$). When $n_2 = 0,4$ mol $SO_3$ were placed in the same vessel, it turned out that in order to obtain the same pressure as in the first experiment, the gas must be heated to a temperature of $T_2 = 200$ K. Determine the degree of dissociation $SO_3$ in the second experiment. All substances in both experiments are in a gaseous state.
