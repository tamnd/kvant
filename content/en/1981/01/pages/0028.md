M661. On the shore of the round lake there are four scriptures K, L, P, Q. From the scripture K a boat sails, from L a boat. If the boat goes straight to P and the boat goes straight to Q, then they will collide at some point X of the lake. Prove that if the star sails to Q and the boat to P, then they will reach these scriptures at the same time.

N. Vasiliev

M662. The piggy bank contains four rubles in copper coins. Prove that these coins can be used to pay off three rubles of lost money.

A. Kushirenko, G. Kushirenko

M663. Find all prime numbers p for which the number 2^p + p^2 is also prime.

C. Mayzus

M664*. Given a quadrilateral ABCD of area S. Let us denote the points of intersection of the altitudes of triangles ABC, BCD, CDA, DAB by H, K, L, M, respectively. Prove that the area of ​​the quadrilateral HKLM is also equal to S.

B. Batyrev, V. Trofimov

M665*. A light display consists of several light bulbs, each of which can be in two states (on or off). There are several buttons on the remote control, and when you press each of them, the state of a certain set of light bulbs simultaneously changes (each button has its own). At first the lights don't light up.

a) Prove that the number of different patterns that can be obtained on the scoreboard is a power of two.

b) How many different patterns can be obtained on a display consisting of m × n light bulbs arranged in a rectangular shape, if each horizontal and each vertical row of light bulbs can be switched with buttons (check your answer for the necessary values ​​of m, n)?

b) Think of other examples of boards and sets (switchable by buttons) in which the number of patterns can be found.

H. Grieberg, 10th grade student
