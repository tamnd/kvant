M661. On the shore of the round lake there are four scriptures K, L, P, Q. From the scripture K a boat sails, from L a boat. If the boat goes straight to P and the boat goes straight to Q, then they will collide at some point X of the lake. Prove that if the star sails to Q and the boat to P, then they will reach these scriptures at the same time.

N. Vasiliev

M662. The piggy bank contains four rubles in copper coins. Prove that these coins can be used to pay off three rubles of lost money.

A. Kushirenko, G. Kushirenko

M663. Find all prime numbers p for which the number 2^p + p^2 is also prime.

C. Mayzus

M664*. Given a quadrilateral ABCD of area S. Let us denote the points of intersection of the altitudes of triangles ABC, BCD, CDA, DAB by H, K, L, M, respectively. Prove that the area of ​​the quadrilateral HKLM is also equal to S.

B. Batyrev, V. Trofimov

M665*. A light display consists of several light bulbs, each of which can be in two states (on or off). There are several buttons on the remote control, and when you press each of them, the state of a certain set of light bulbs simultaneously changes (each button has its own). At first the lights don't light up.

a) Prove that the number of different patterns that can be obtained on the scoreboard is a power of two.

b) How many different patterns can be obtained on a display consisting of m × n light bulbs arranged in a rectangular shape, if each horizontal and each vertical row of light bulbs can be switched with buttons (check your answer for the necessary values ​​of m, n)?

b) Think of other examples of boards and sets (switchable by buttons) in which the number of patterns can be found.

H. Grieberg, 10th grade student

F674. Sulfuric anhydride $SO_3$ in an amount of $\gamma_1 = 1$ mole was placed in a closed vessel and heated to a temperature of $T_1 = 1000$ K, at which $SO_3$ partially dissociates into sulfur dioxide $SO_2$ and oxygen: $SO_3 = SO_2 + \frac{1}{2}O_2$. The degree of dissociation under these conditions turns out to be $\alpha_1 = 0,2$ (that is, 20% of the first molecules present were dissociated $SO_3$). When $n_2 = 0,4$ mol $SO_3$ were placed in the same vessel, it turned out that in order to obtain the same pressure as in the first experiment, the gas must be heated to a temperature of $T_2 = 200$ K. Determine the degree of dissociation $SO_3$ in the second experiment. All substances in both experiments are in a gaseous state.

V. Belonukkin

F675. To change large currents in circuit $CC$ (Fig. 1), a shunt Ш is used, in parallel with which a changing device Г with an internal resistance $r = 10$ Ohm is connected through resistances $R_1 = 2$ Ohm and $R_2$. In position $A$ of switch P, all the voltage of the device corresponds to the current in circuit $I_1 = 10$ A. What resistance $R_2$ should be taken so that in position $B$ of switch P, all the voltage of the device corresponds to current $I_2 = 100$ A?

The shunt resistance is considered to be much less than the internal resistance of the device.

L. Lutsansky

F676. A square wire frame with perimeter $4a$ and mass $m$ imparts a certain initial velocity in the horizontal direction. The frame moves in a vertical plane, all the time being in a magnetic field perpendicular to the plane of the frame (Fig. 2). The field induction changes according to the law $\cdot B(z) = B(0) + kz$, where $k = const$. The frame resistance is $R$. After some time, the speed of the frame becomes constant and equal to $v$. Find the initial speed imparted to the frame. Gravity acceleration $g$.

V. Mozhaev

F677. Using a system of concentric mirrors (Fig. 3), an image of the Sun was captured on the screen. The radii of the mirrors are $R_1 = 12$ cm, $R_2 = 30$ cm. What should be the focal length of a thin lens in order for it to produce an image of the Sun of the same size?

E. Kuznetsov
