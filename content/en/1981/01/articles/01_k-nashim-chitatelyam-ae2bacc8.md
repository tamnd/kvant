To our readers

By subscribing to our magazine, you have already made your choice, and we do not need to encourage you to take up physics or mathematics. The topic of our conversation with you is different.

1981 is a significant year. This is the first year of the new, eleventh, five-year plan. It is also the year of the XXVI Congress of our great Communist Party. The congress will outline new milestones in the development of our Motherland and set new tasks for all the people on the path to building a communist society.

The XXV Congress of the CPSU oriented us all towards the struggle to increase the efficiency and quality of various forms of labor. Your work is your study. Considering can be done in different ways. Learning becomes joyful when a student masters the skills of mental work. Then every day he comes across discoveries - mathematical, physical, astronomical... And in this sense he becomes like a scientist. Scientists are discovering something that no one else knows. The student discovers something that he has not yet learned, although science already knows it. By solving problems, mastering new concepts and laws, you are engaged in real scientific work. After all, scientists first of all come to find out what others have already done in the field of interest to them, and only then can they move on.

Welcoming the first readers of our magazine, the Chairman of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician M.V. Keldysh wrote in it in January 1970: “... I would like to wish young mathematicians and physicists, all readers «Kvant» greater success in accounting, in work, in acquiring and improving their knowledge.” Promoting the acquisition and improvement of your knowledge is the main goal of the journal. We want those of you who have already mastered physics or mathematics to find in «Kvant» material for independent reflection, to develop your mind and abilities. We would also like to help other schoolchildren become interested in our science.

This year on pages «Kvant» you will get acquainted with the talks of the largest Soviet physicists and mathematicians about our school, about the role of youth in the development of science. At the end of the year, on the 60th anniversary of V.I. Lenin’s speech at the III Komsomol Congress, the Nauka publishing house published an interesting collection of articles entitled “Lenin. Science. Youth". Unfortunately, this book is difficult to share and few of you will be able to get acquainted with it. Therefore, we decided to publish some articles from this book on the pages of our magazine. The series of these articles opens with the speech of the Chairman of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician A.P. Aleksadrov, at the XVIII Congress of the Komsomol.

We will not hide from you that we ourselves are not happy with everything in our magazine. Some of the articles we publish are overly difficult and dry, we rarely talk about unsolved scientific problems, and there is little humor in the magazine. We strive to increase the efficiency and quality of our journal, to make it more interesting, lively, and more accessible (of course, without reducing their scientific level).

In an effort to improve the magazine «Kvant», we really need the advice of our readers. Therefore, we turn to you - write to our editor about what you like and don’t like in our magazine, what you would like to read about in it, what questions in the field of mathematics, physics, astronomy and astronautics concern you. We welcome your letters.

We wish you more success in the current academic year.
