Solving the problems of option 1

1. Let us denote the seventh term of the arithmetic progression $a_7$ by $x$, and the difference of the progression by $d$. We have
$$\begin{cases} a_2 = x - 5d, & a_{12} = x + 5d, \\ a_4 = x - 3d, & a_{10} = x + 3d. \end{cases}$$

According to the condition
$$\begin{cases} a_2 \cdot a_{12} = 1, \\ a_4 \cdot a_{10} = b. \end{cases}$$

Therefore, by virtue of (1),
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - 25d^2 = 1, \\ x^2 - 9d^2 = b. \end{cases}$$

From this system we easily find $x^2$ and $d^2$:
$$x^2 = \frac{25b - 9}{16}; d^2 = \frac{b - 1}{16}.$$
In this case, very indo, for the solution to exist there must be $b \geqslant 1$.

Answer. $\left\{\frac{\sqrt{25b - 9}}{4}; -\frac{\sqrt{25b - 9}}{4}\right\}$.

Applicants, as a rule, solved this problem correctly. Some of them made mistakes when solving the equation $x^2 = \frac{25b - 9}{16}$.
