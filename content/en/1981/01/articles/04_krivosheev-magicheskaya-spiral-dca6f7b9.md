CEILINGS

The ceilings of multi-modern buildings (stations, cafes) are made in the form of a rod system, the geometric basis of which is the “dense packing” of tetrahedrons and half-frames (see «Kvant», 1976, No. 9, p. 25). The upper plane of one of the options is like this

rod system (Fig. 1) - square lattice; four sides are attached to each square, forming, together with this square, a semi-ctahedron. The upper surface of the system is usually covered with sheet materials that act as roofs or floors between floors. The lower part of the lattice is left empty, presenting the viewer sitting in the back
le, neat rod pattern.

However, such a lattice can be easily enriched by covering it with triangular faces. An example of such ornamentation is the ceiling in the main hall of the Leningradsky railway station in Moscow. This ceiling is covered with rigid pieces of sheet material. But ceilings can also be draped with a whole piece of materials.

Magic spiral

In a 5x5 square, you can arrange twenty-five consecutive numbers 1, 2, ..., 25 so that their sums along the columns, rows and diagonals of the square equal the number 65. Such a square is called a fifth-order magic square. Pyatyshevny figure on the fourth page of the cover is a modification of it.

The sums of the numbers located along the fifths emerging from the center of the spiral are equal to the same constant 65 (4+23+17+11+10 = 16+15+9+3+22 = ...). All five numbers located on concentric circles are equal to the same constant (8+16+4+12+25 = 2+15+

+23+6+19 = 21+9+17+5+13 = ...). In addition, if any number on one of the lines, except the middle one, is added to four numbers located at the ends of the same diametrically opposite stripes, we again get the magic sum 65 (for example, 14+(6+17)+(5+23) = 20+(6+4)+(12+23) = 2+(24+10)+(18+11) = 8+(5+11)+(24+17)). Thus, we get another twenty-fifths located diametrically opposite to this straight line, respectively on the inner and outer circles (21+(4+12)+(18+10) = 13+(16+4)+(22+10) = 5+(16+8)+(22+14) = 17+(8+25)+(14+1) = 9+(12+25)+(18+1)). The magic sum 65 is also obtained by adding the fifth numbers located at the vertices and in the center of the internal four-line four-line stripes, corresponding to this straight line, respectively on the inner and outer circles, and when adding the numbers ultimately returning to the original number, that is, to one (in this case, the quadratina set turns out to be composed of numbers of different colors).
