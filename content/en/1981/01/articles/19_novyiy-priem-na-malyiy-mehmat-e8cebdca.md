New example on a small mechanism

For seventh grade students living on the territory of the European part of the RSFSR (with the exception of rural areas) and Belarus, students will be admitted to the Small Mechanical Faculty (MMMF) - a correspondence mathematical school at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, which is a department of the Higher School of Mechanics and Mathematics.

The MMMF program, aimed at deepening knowledge in the most important sections of the school curriculum and developing students’ skills in independent mathematics studies, was compiled under the guidance of faculty professors. This program takes into account the peculiarities of entrance exams in mathematics to the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University and other universities.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1981. Small mechanic training is free. Duration of training: 3 years.

Those wishing to enter the small mechanism must send the decision of the test work of the VZMSH no later than April 15, 1981 to the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, MSU, mex.-mat. F-t, MMMF. The requirements for the design of the work do not differ from the requirements of the VZMSH.

Participants in regional olympiads in mathematics can be accepted to the small mechanism on the basis of an application and a document confirming participation in the olympiad.

For Moscow schoolchildren there are Evening Math (7-9 grades) and Sunday Preparatory (10 grades) schools. For information about them, call 139-33-43.
