⟦folio 58⟧

6. What forces must be applied to the ends of a steel rod with a cross-sectional area $S=10$ cm² in order to prevent it from expanding when heated from $t_1=0^\circ C$ to $t_2=30^\circ C$? Elastic modulus of steel $E=21 \cdot 10^{10}$ H/m²; temperature coefficient of linear expansion of steel $\alpha=1,2 \cdot 10^{-5} K^{-1}$.

7. At each vertex of the square there is a charge +Q. What size negative charge should be placed in the center of the square so that the system of charges is in equilibrium?

Yu. Metseryakov,
V. Smirnov
