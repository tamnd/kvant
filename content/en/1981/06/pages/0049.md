Let us construct an image of the source $S$ in the upper half of the median line (Fig. 5). The ray $SO$, parallel to the optical axis, after refraction in the upper part of the line is pushed through its focus $F_1$. Beam $SA$, refracted in the lens, must meet the parallel ray $KO_1$ at point $M$ of the focal plane of the lens (ray $KO_1$ passes through the optical center $O_1$ of the upper half of the lens without being refracted).

The intersection of the extended rays $AM$ and $OF_1$ gives the image position $S_1$ of the source at a distance $f$ from the lens.
