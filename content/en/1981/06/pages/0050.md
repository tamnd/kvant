From figure 5 it is clear that in this case the area of ​​overlap of the rays is limited and lies between the lens and the intersection point of the rays $AS_1$ and $BS_2$. At a distance $l$ from the lens, the size $|CE|$ of the overlap area is maximum. Here you can observe $N = \frac{|CE|}{x}$ bands, where $x = \frac{\lambda}{\Omega}$ is the width of the band. The convergence angle is $\Omega = \frac{|S_1S_2|}{f-l}$, therefore

$$N = \frac{|CE|}{\lambda} \frac{|S_1S_2|}{f-l}.$$

From the similarity of triangles $F_2F_1O$ and CEO we find

$$|CE| = |F_2F_1| \frac{l}{F} = \frac{al}{F},$$

and from the similarity of triangles $AOC$ and $S_1S_2C$ -

$$|S_2S_1| = |AO| \frac{f-l}{l} = \frac{D-a}{2} \frac{f-l}{l}.$$

Then finally

$$N = \frac{|CE|}{\lambda} \frac{|S_1S_2|}{f-l} = \frac{a(D-a)}{2F\lambda} = 450 \text{ полос.}$$

As the observation plane moves away from the lens, the stripes become narrower ($\Omega$ increases). The width of the overlap region up to plane $CE$ increases and then decreases, and moreover, faster than the width of the strip. Therefore, the maximum number of stripes is observed in the $CE$ plane – the widest section of the overlap area.

Problem 3 (1980). From the exact monochromatic source $A$ the exact monchromatic source $B$ is moved away (the sources are coerect and in-phase) until at point $O$, where interference is observed, darkening occurs (Fig. 6). (The distance between $A$ and $B$ is equal to $d = 2\pi m$.) Distance between sources

**Fig. 6.**

com $A$ and screen $L = 9$ m. How much do you need to move the screen to source $A$ for darkening to occur at point $O_1$?

When the source $B$ moves away, the first darkening at point $O$ will occur provided that the difference in the wave paths from $B$ and $A$ is equal to 1/2 the wavelength:

$$\Delta r = |BO| - |AO| = \frac{\lambda}{2},$$

or

$$\sqrt{L^2 + d^2} - L = \frac{\lambda}{2}. \tag{1}$$

If the screen is brought closer to the sources at a distance $l$, the minimum at point $O_1$ will correspond to a path difference of 3/2 wavelength:

$$\Delta r_1 = |BO_1| - |AO_1| = \frac{3\lambda}{2},$$

or

$$\sqrt{(L-l)^2 + d^2} - (L-l) = \frac{3\lambda}{2}. \tag{2}$$

Let us transform expressions (1) and (2) and use the approximate formula $(1+x)^{1/2} = 1 + \frac{x}{2}$ for $x \ll 1$:

$$\sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{d}{L}\right)^2} - 1 = \frac{\lambda}{2L},$$

$$\sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{d}{L-l}\right)^2} - 1 = \frac{3\lambda}{2(L-l)},$$

or

$$d^2 = \lambda L,$$

$$d^2 = 3\lambda(L-l).$$

From here

$$l = \frac{2}{3} L = 6 \text{ м.}$$
