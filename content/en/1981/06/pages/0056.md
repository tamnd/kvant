Physics

Oral exam assignment
(faculties of mathematics and physics)

1. Two weights of masses $m_1 = 3$ kg and $m_2 = 6,8$ kg hang on the ends of a thread crossed over a block. The change weight is $h = 2$ m below the large one. The loads began to move without an initial speed. How long does it take for them to reach the same height?

2. A load of mass $m = 10$ kg is moved uniformly along a horizontal straight line, applying a force directed at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Determine the model of this force if the friction coefficient is $\mu = 0,2$.

3. A ball of mass $m = 100$ g, suspended on a thread $l = 20$ cm long, is deflected from its equilibrium position by an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$. The ball reported a speed of $|\vec{v}_0| = 2$ m/s in the direction perpendicular to the thread. How should you read the threads so that the ball does not break the thread when moving?

4. When the load sat motionless on a vertical spring, its elongation was $\Delta l = 5$ cm. Then the load was pulled back and released, as a result of which it began to oscillate. What is the period of these oscillations?

5. An ice floe of constant thickness floats in water, protruding $h = 2$ cm above the surface. What is the mass of the ice floe if its area is $S = 150$ cm$^2$? Ice density $q_\perp = 0,92$ g/cm$^3$.

6. The cylindrical vessel is divided into two parts by a movable piston. What will be the equilibrium position of the piston if a certain weight amount of oxygen is placed in one part of the vessel and the same amount of hydrogen in the other? The total length of the vessel is $l = 85$ cm.

7. It took $\tau_1 = 15$ min to heat some masses of water from $t_0 = 0^\circ C$ to boiling temperature using an electronic heater. After this, it took $\tau_2 = 1$ hours 20 minutes to convert all this water into steam under the same conditions. Using these data, determine the specific heat of vaporization of water.

8. How many times will the power released in a conductor with a resistance of $R_1 = 6$ Ohm change if a second conductor with a resistance of $R_2 = 9$ Ohm is connected in parallel to it? Internal source resistance $r = 4$ Ohm.

9. Find the internal resistance of the generator if it is known that the power released in the external circuit is the same for two values ​​of resistance of the external circuit: $R_1 = 5$ Ohm and $R_2 = 0,2$ Ohm.

10. The lens gives a threefold magnification of an object located at a distance of $d = 10$ cm from its plane. Find the focal length of the lens.
