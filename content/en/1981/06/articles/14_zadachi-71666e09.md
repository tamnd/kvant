⟦folio 43⟧

Exercise

1. According to the schedule, the train from Moscow to Frunze departs daily at 23 hours 50 minutes and is on the way for 75 hours, and the train from Frunze to Moscow leaves daily at 12 hours 50 minutes (Moscow time) and is on the road (for technical reasons) for 73 hours 28 minutes. The train, having arrived at the final station, departs back on the same day. How many trains are needed to ensure uninterrupted operation of the Moscow-Frunze route and back?

2. Of the three squares: $2 \times 2$, $3 \times 3$ and $6 \times 6$ (see figure), you need to add one. How to cut these squares so that the number of parts is minimal?

3. The circular disk is divided into 21 sectors; the sectors are the same; each of them contains a certain number. In the second disk, fixed on the same axis with the first, 5 windows are made so that with any rotation of the second disk from some initial position through an angle multiple of $\frac{2\pi}{21}$, one of the numbers of the first disk is visible in each window. It is known that with any such rotation of the second disk, the sum of the numbers visible through the windows is equal to zero. Prove that the sum of all numbers written on the first disk is equal to zero.

4. Zoo. In each of the puzzles shown on figure, the same numbers correspond to the same letters, and different numbers correspond to different ones. Decipher these reburs.
