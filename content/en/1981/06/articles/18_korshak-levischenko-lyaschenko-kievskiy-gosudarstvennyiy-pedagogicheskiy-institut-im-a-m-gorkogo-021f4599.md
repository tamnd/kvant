5. Calculate limit
$$\lim_{x \to -1} \frac{2x^2 - x - 3}{2x^2 + 5x + 3}.$$

V. Popov, S. Sadikhov

Kyiv State Pedagogical Institute named after. A. M. Gorky

Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1
(specialty "mathematics" of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics)

1. A cone is inscribed in a regular triangular paramid, the generatrix of which is inclined to the plane of the base at an angle α. Find the volume of this paramid if the side edge is equal to b.
2. Solve inequality
$$\lg (x - 1) + \lg (x - 2) < \lg 6.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\cos 2x + \cos x + 1 = 0.$$
4. Find the most value of a function
$$y = (2 - x)(x + 2).$$

Option 2
(specialty “physics and astronomy” of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics)

1. In a truncated cone, the diagonals of the axial section are mutually perpendicular, and the generatrix forms an angle α with the plane of the large base and is equal to l. Determine the surface area and volume of the truncated cone.
2. Determine the area of ​​the figure bounded by the graphs of the function y = 3x - x³, y = 0.
3. Solve inequality
$$\log \frac{1}{2} \left( x + \frac{1}{2} \right)^2 > 0.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$2 \cos^2 x + \sin^2 2x = 1 + \cos^2 2x.$$

Option 3
(Faculty of General Chemical Disciplines)

1. In a regular triangular truncated paramid, the edges of the lower and upper bases of the corresponding are equal to a and b (a > b), the two angles y of the edge of the lower base are equal to α. Determine the volume of the truncated paramid and its surface area.
2. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg} x = \cos x.$$
3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{2x^2 - 3x - 459}{x^2 + 1} > 1.$$
4. Calculate limit
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{(n - 3)(n + 5)}{2n^2 + 3n - 2}.$$

Oral exam assignment
1. Solve the equation
a) $$\frac{\log_5 (2x + 3)}{1 - \log_5 (2x + 3)} + \frac{3}{5 - \log_5 (2x + 3)} = 0;$$
b) $$\sin x \cdot \cos x \cdot \cos 2x = -\frac{1}{8}.$$
2. Solve inequality
a) $$\log_2 (2 + x) < 1;$$
b) $$\sin \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - x \right) < \frac{1}{2};$$
b) $$2x^2 - 5|x| + 3 \geq 0.$$
3. Find the limit
a) $$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\frac{1}{2} + 1 + \frac{3}{2} + \ldots + \frac{n}{2}}{n^2 + n + 3};$$
b) $$\lim_{x \to \frac{\pi}{8}} \frac{\cos 4x}{\sin 2x - \cos 2x}.$$
4. Explore function
a) $$y = \frac{3}{2} \cos \left( 2x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) + 1;$$
b) $$y = \frac{x^2}{x^2 + 3}$$

and plot it.
5. Find the domain of a function
$$y = \log_2 \sin x.$$
6. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the graph of the function y = x² + 1 and the tangents drawn to this graph at the points with abscissas x = 0 and x = 2.
7. Find the equation of tangents to the graph of the function y = 2x² - 4x at the points of intersection of this graph with the x-axis.
8. Calculate $$\int_0^\pi \sin x \cdot \cos 3x \, dx.$$
9. Calculate ctg $$\frac{\alpha}{2},$$ if $$\sin \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}.$$
10. What is greater, sin 1980° or cos 1980°?
11. Set a formula for a function that is the inverse of a function
$$y = \frac{1}{2} (3x - 3).$$
12. What positive real number a, when summed with its inverse $$\frac{1}{a}$$, produces the smallest sum?
13. Sea water contains 5% sun. How many kilograms of water must be added to 80 kg of seawater so that the salt content in the latter is 4%?
14. The sum of the first and fifth terms of the arithmetic program is 26, and the product of the second and fourth terms is 160. Find the sum of six terms of the program.
