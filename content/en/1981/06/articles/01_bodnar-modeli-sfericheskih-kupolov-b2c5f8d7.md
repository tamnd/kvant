⟦folio none⟧

These "spherical domes" are constructed from intertwined (but not glued) paper wheels.

In the bottom row is a photograph of the same dome, taken from two different points. This dome is made up of six wheels. A reader familiar with semi-controlled polytopes («Kvant», 1978, no. 1, p. 8 and 1980, no. 12, p. 9) will recognize in its frame an inklodode frame (its faces are 20 regular triangles and 12 regular pentagons).

The top row shows another canopy of six wheels on the left, and another canopy on the right with ten. All these figures have rich eronic symmetry (that is, they are self-combined in many different ways; in particular, any koludo can be translated into any other).

You can feel this by making similar models yourself, or better understand it by reading the above articles and the article in «Kvant», 1980, No. 7, p. 9.

O. Bodnar
