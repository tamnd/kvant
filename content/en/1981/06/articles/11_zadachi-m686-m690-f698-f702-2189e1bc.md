Tasks

M686—M690; F698—F702

M686. For any number $x \geqslant 1$ is the equality true?
$$[\sqrt{x}] = [\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}]?$$
(Here $[y]$ denotes the integer part of the number $y$.)
V. Prasolov

M687. a) In a nine-nut primica, all 9 lateral ribs and all 27 diagonals of the base are colored: some are red, the rest are blue. Prove that there are three vertices of a primid that serve as vertices of a triangle, all sides of which are colored the same color.
6) Is a similar statement true for the octagonal primid?

H. Nenov (Bulgaria)

M688. Given natural numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ such that $a_k \leqslant k$ ($k=1, 2, \dots, n$). Prove that one of the expressions
$$a_1 \pm a_2 \pm a_3 \pm \dots \pm a_n$$
equals zero.
S. Nenashev

M689*. Prove that it is impossible to form a rectangle from identical tiles in the shape of equilateral trapezoids with bases of 3 cm, 1 cm and a height of 1 cm.
S. Rukshin

M690*. a) Inside a convex polygon of area $S_1$ and perimeter $P_1$ there is a convex polygon of area $S_2$ and perimeter $P_2$. Prove the inequality
$$2 \frac{S_1}{P_1} > \frac{S_2}{P_2}.$$
6) Form and prove a similar statement for convex polygons.
A. Kelarov

F698. At some discretion, parts are moved using two conveyors, the belts of which move in mutually perpendicular directions.

⟦folio 32⟧

directions with the same absolute value of speed $v_0$ (Fig. 1). In this case, the part, calling on transporter II, stops in the middle of the belt. The speed of transporter II was increased by $n$ times. How should the speed of conveyor I be changed so that the parts still stop in the middle of the belt of conveyor II? Neglect the dimensions of the parts; assume that the transition to transporter II occurs without impact.

A. Omelyanchik

F699. A curved capillary of radius $r$ (Fig. 2), completely filled with liquid, rotates around the vertical axis $OO'$. At what angular velocity of rotation does the liquid begin to pour out of the capitlar? Liquid density $q$, surface tension $\sigma$, the liquid completely wets the capitol; The dimensions of the capital are indicated on figure 2.

A. Buzdin

F700. There are two conductors $A$ and $B$ of arbitrary shape. Initially, conductor $A$ has a charge $Q$, but conductor $B$ is not charged. The conductors are brought into contact (Fig. 3), and charge $q$ flows onto the conductor $B$. Some additional charge $q_x$ was imparted to the contacting conductor, and as a result, charge $q$ appeared on the conductor $A$. Determine the charge $q_x$.

S. Krotov

F701. figure 4 shows the temperature distribution along a thin homogeneous thermally insulated rod at some point in time. How will the temperature distribution change in the future? What distribution will be established after a sufficiently long time?

O. Savchenko

F702. Two systems, each of which consists of two identical masses $m$, connected by a spring of rigidity $k$, move along a smooth horizontal surface towards each other with the same speed $v_0$. At some point in time, the distance between the systems is $L$ (Fig. 5). Determine the time after which the distance between the same masses again equals $L$. The collision of the system is considered to be absolutely elastic.

V. Mozhaev
