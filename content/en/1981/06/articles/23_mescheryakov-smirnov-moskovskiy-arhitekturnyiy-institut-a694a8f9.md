7. An electron with an energy of $W = 10^3$ EV appears in a uniform electric field of intensity $|E| = 800$ V/cm at an angle of $\alpha = \pi/2$ to the field lines. What should be the direction and magnitude of the magnetic field induction so that the electron does not experience deviation from rectilinear motion? The mass of an electron is $m_e = 9,1 \times 10^{-31}$ kg.

8. After charging the capacitor with capacity $C$ from a constant voltage source $U$, switch $K$ switches the inductance coil $L_1$ (Fig. 2). Harmonious oscillations with a current amplitude of $I_{m1}$ appear in the circuit. The experiment is repeated according to the previous scheme, replacing the coil with another, inductance $L_2 = 2L_1$. Find the current amplitude $I_{m2}$ for the second case.

9. A point light source is placed on the optical axis of a collecting lens with a focal length of $F_1 = 30$ cm at a distance of $d_1 = 120$ cm from it. On the other side of the lens, a diverging lens is placed in its focal plane. What is the focal length $F_2$ of the diverging lens if the ray, after passing through the second lens, appears to originate from the source itself?

10. One of the plates of a flat capacitor with a distance between the plates of $d = 10$ mm is illuminated by X-rays, which remove photoelectrons from it. We assume that the velocities of these electrons are identical in absolute value and equal to $|\vec{v}| = 10^6$ m/s. The electrons are collected on the second plate. From every square centimeter of $(S_1)$ the plate has, $n = 10^{13}$ electrons escape in one second. After what time $t$ will the photocurrent stop? Electron charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, mass $m_e = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, electric constant $\varepsilon_0 = 8,85 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m.

Mathematics

Oral exam objectives

1. The numbers $a^2, b^2, c^2$ form an arithmetic progression. Prove that the numbers $\frac{1}{b+c}, \frac{1}{c+a}, \frac{1}{a+b}$ also form an arithmetic progression.

2. Graph the function
$$y = |x-1| + |x-2| + x.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$5^{\lg x} = 50 - x^{\lg 5}.$$

4. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} (3x+y)x-y = 9, \\ x-y\sqrt{324} = 18x^2 + 12xy + 2y^2. \end{cases}$$

5. Solve inequality
$$\log_2 2 \cdot \log_2 x 2 \cdot \log_2 4x > 1.$$

6. Prove the same gesture
$$\sin^4 \alpha \cdot \cos^2 \alpha = \frac{1}{16} - \frac{1}{32} \cos 2\alpha - \frac{1}{16} \cos 4\alpha + \frac{1}{32} \cos 6\alpha.$$

7. Find the limit
$$\lim_{x \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{2 \sin^2 x - 3 \sin x + 1}{x - \frac{\pi}{2}}.$$

8. Through the origin of coordinates, draw a straight line dividing a curved triangle with a vertex at the origin, bounded by the lines $y = 2x - x^2$, $y = 0$, $x = 1$, into two equal parts.

9. The complete surface of a regular quadrilateral straight line is equal to $S$, and the flat angle of the lateral face at the vertex is equal to $a$. Find the height of the straight line.

Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. A small body slides off the top of a sphere of radius $R$. At what height from the apex will the body leave the surface of the sphere? Ignore the rubbish.

2. A chandelier with a mass of $m = 100$ kg is suspended from the ceiling on a metal chain $l = 5$ m long. Determine the height at which to tilt the chandelier so that the chain does not break during subsequent swings. It is known that chain rupture occurs at a tension of $T = 1960$ N.

3. A hemispherical cup with radius $R$ rotates around a square axis with a constant angular velocity $\omega$ (Fig. 1). There is a ball of mass $m$ in a cup that rotates with it. Find the angle $\varphi$ that determines the equilibrium position of the ball in the cup.

4. A falling cm from a height of $h = 1,2$ m with a load hammers its own, which from the impact goes into the ground $s = 2$ cm. Determine the average force of the impact and its duration if the mass of the load is $M = 5 \cdot 10^2$ kg, and the mass of the object is less than the mass of the load.

5. The rod $AB$ rests on a rough floor and a smooth projection $C$ (Fig. 2). Distance $|AC| = 0,75|AB|$. At what triangle ratio will the rod make an angle $a = 45^\circ$ with the floor in the equilibrium position?
