Animals
at the market

Birds, animals and bugs
We bought shoes:
Scooped flamingos
They each took a shoe,
Leopard and jaguars
They put on two pairs,
And when the beetle appeared,
I immediately demanded six pieces.
Everyone who was at the market that day
I bought myself some boots.
And a satisfied seller
In the end he got excited:
Birds, beasts and bugs
He gave me a cap.
Coming back, old and young
I was happy with my new thing.
The market was empty at the moment:
All goods have been sold out.
Locking your stall,
The seller summed it up:
Sold forty shoes
I gave out ten caps.
How long were there at the market?
Birds, animals and bugs?

V. Illarionov
