⟦folio 4⟧

I. Frank

The way to teach

Trying to look into the future, youth dreams. Happy is the one who does not part with his dream in later life. The closest person to me is the one who, from a young age, strives to study science. Not only in dreams, but also in reality, science is incredibly fascinating. But to become a scientist, you must be sincerely and selflessly devoted to science and not be afraid of difficulties.

The first steps are obviously made easier by teachers, if, of course, they not only know, but also love science. Both the topic of the first work and the method of completing it are often suggested by the leader. This is good, since gaining experience is necessary, and the scientific environment in which a beginner finds himself is important for the future. A beginner can and should be helped to learn, and yet he can only learn by himself. At the same time, the first steps taken independently, which please as some achievements, often lead to disappointment. It often turns out that the result obtained has already been found by someone earlier, and the idea that seems new is in fact not only not new, but may have already been abandoned as erroneous. But you shouldn't be upset. The most important and most valuable thing in science is the element of creativity. Walking a section of the path on your own, even one covered by others, is useful and often necessary. Nevertheless, after several such “failures,” the mistake arises that everything in science is known. In reality the obligation is different. Initial knowledge involuntarily suggests the path along which many have already traveled. The ability to think in your own way does not arise immediately; it is developed through difficult and focused work.
