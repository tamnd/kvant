⟦folio 22⟧

Tasks

M666—M670; F678—F682

M666. Prove that the least common multiple $n$ of the natural numbers $a_1 < a_2 < \ldots < a_n$ is at least $na_1$.

A. Razborov

M667. Construct a triangle $ABC$ if its smallest angle $\widehat{A}$ and segments of length $d = |AB| - |BC|$ and $e = |AC| - |BC|$ are given.

N. Vasiliev

M668*. The sequence $(x_i)$ is determined by the conditions

$$x_1 = 1, x_2 = 0, x_3 = 2, x_{n+1} = x_{n-2} + 2x_{n-1}$$

Prove that for any natural number $m$ there are two adjacent terms of this sequence, each of which is divisible by $m$.

G. Kozlov

M669. The quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. Prove that

a) the segment connecting the midpoints of the angles $AB$ and $CD$ is perpendicular to the segment connecting the angles $BC$ and $AD$;

b) the centers of the circles inscribed in triangles $ABC, BCD, CDA$ and $DAB$ are the vertices of the rectangle.

K. Malkhasyan, I. German

M670*. a) Given several points, non-tortic pairs of which are connected by lines (the points of such pairs are called neighbors). The number of neighbors of each point is odd. At the initial moment, all points are colored in two colors - red and blue. Then, every minute, a simultaneous recoloring of the point will occur according to the following rule: each point whose most neighbors have a different color from it changes its color; otherwise, its color is retained.

Prove that a moment will come from which some points will not change, and some will have the same point.

b) Will this statement remain true if it is not assumed that each point has an odd number of neighbors?

O. Kozlov
