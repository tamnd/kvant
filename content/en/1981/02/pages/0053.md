4. At some distances behind the thin negative line with a focal length of $F_n = 10$ cm there is a spherical concave mirror. The lens + mirror system creates a spicy life-size image of the object. Then the mirror was moved away from the line by $l = 2$ cm, and again a life-size image of the object was obtained. Determine the distance from the object to the line.

Option 2

1. The nuclear reaction $^14\text{N} + ^4\text{He} \rightarrow {}^{17}\text{O} + p$ can occur if alpha particles incident on stationary nitrogen nuclei have an energy exceeding the threshold energy $E_n = 1,45$ MeV. How much should the energy of α-particles be greater than the threshold so that the kinetic energy of the protons formed in the reaction is zero?

2. In a horizontal cylinder with cross section $S$ there is a massive piston (Fig. 7). At the initial moment, the piston is held at a distance $l_0$ from the bottom of the vessel, for which a force $F$ had to be applied to it. After the force $F$ ceases, the piston begins to move without friction. At what distance from the bottom will the piston have the highest speed? The process is considered isothermal. The external pressure is $p_0$.

3. The oscillatory circuit, consisting of an inductor and a capacitor, is connected through a switch $K$ to a source of constant EMF with internal resistance $r$ (Fig. 8). Initially the key $K$ is closed. After setting the stationary mode, the key is opened, and a oscillator with a period of $T$ appears in the circuit. In this case, the voltage amplitude on the capacitor is $n$ times greater than the battery emf. Find the inductance of the coil and the capacitance of the capacitor. Neglect the ohmic resistance of the coil.

4. The object and its image, created by a thin positive line, are on one side of the line. The distance between the object and the image is $a_1 = 4$ cm. Exactly the same image of the same object is obtained using a spherical mirror having the same focal length as the line. In this case, the distance between the object and the image turned out to be equal to $a_2 = 8$ cm. Determine the focal length of the line.

Option 3

1. What minimum horizontal force $F$ must be applied to a block of mass $m = 1$ kg located on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination $α = 30°$ in order for the block to dig (Fig. 9)? The coefficient of friction between the block and the inclined plane is $μ = 0,2$.

2. From a cylinder with a volume of $V_1 = 200$ l containing helium at a temperature of $T_1 = 273$ K under a pressure of $p_1 = 2 \cdot 10^6$ N/m², part of the gas was consumed, which under normal conditions occupied a volume of $V_2 = 1000$ l. When measuring the pressure in the cylinder again, a value of $p_2 = 1,4 \cdot 10^6$ N/m² was obtained. At what temperature was this measurement taken?
