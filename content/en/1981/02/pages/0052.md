The volume $V_{np}$ of prism $EFGHE_1F_1G_1H_1$ is equal to $V_{np} = a^2b = \lambda^2(1 - 3\lambda)\frac{H^3}{2}$. Since $\frac{1}{24}H^3 = V$, the final result is

$$V_{np} = 12\lambda^2(1 - 3\lambda)V,$$

where $\lambda \in ]0; 1[$.

Now you can answer question a). The value of $\lambda$ in this case is $\frac{1}{1+4} = \frac{1}{5}$, and therefore $V_{np} = \frac{24}{125}V$.

In order to answer question 6), we find the maximum of the function $V_{np}(\lambda)$ on the interval $]0; 1[$. Let us equate the derivative $V'_{np}(\lambda) = 2\lambda - 9\lambda^2 = 0$ to zero, hence $\lambda = \frac{2}{9}$. Since at $\lambda < \frac{2}{9}V'_{np}(\lambda) > 0$, and at $\lambda > \frac{2}{9}V'_{np}(\lambda) < 0$ the function $V_{np}(\lambda)$ has at $\lambda = \frac{2}{9}$ the most value on the interval $]0; 1[$, equal to $V_{np}\left(\frac{2}{9}\right) = \frac{16}{81}V$.

Answer. a) $\frac{24}{125}V; 6)\frac{16}{81}V$.
