⟦folio 48⟧

Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$10 \cos^2 x - 16 \sin x = \cos 2x + 15.$$

2. A circle of radius $\sqrt{7}$ with center at point (2; 3) is given on the coordinate plane. Find all points outside this circle whose coordinates satisfy the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 3x + 3y - \frac{1}{2} = 4, \\ (2x - y)^2 = \frac{9}{4}. \end{cases}$$

3. The lengths of the sides $AB$ and $CD$ of the trapezoid $ABCD$ are 8 cm and 10 cm, respectively, and the length of the base $BC$ is 2 cm. The bisector of the angle $ADC$ passes through the middle of the side $AB$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

4. The height of a regular quadrangular paramid is twice the diagonal of its base, the volume of the paramid is $V$. We consider regular quadrangular features written into a paramid such that their lateral edges are parallel to the diagonal of the base of the paramid, one lateral face belongs to this base, and the vertices of the opposite lateral face lie on the lateral surface of the paramid. Find:

a) the volume of that feature, the plane of the lateral edge of which divides the height of the paramid in a ratio of 4:1, counting from the top;
b) the maximum value of the volume of the considered recognitions.

5. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2x^2y - xy^5z = z^2, \\ xz + 3y^4z^2 = 10x^2y^5, \\ 5y^4z + 3xy^8z^2 = 2x^2. \end{cases}$$

Option 2

1. Solve inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^x - 32 \left(\frac{1}{8}\right)^{x^2-1} < 0.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\log_{\cos x} \left( \frac{\sin 2x}{\sqrt{2}} + \cos x - \sin^2 x - \sqrt{2} \sin x \right) = 2.$$

3. There are two mutually perpendicular and intersecting chords $AB$ and $CD$ in a circle. It is known that $|AB| = |BC| = |CD|$. Determine which is greater: the area of ​​a circle or the area of ​​a square with sides $AB$.

4. Find all values ​​of $a$ for which the system of equation
$$\begin{cases} 2^x(y+1)(1-y \cdot 2^x) = a^3, \\ (1+2^x)(1-y \cdot 2^x) = a \end{cases}$$
a) has no solutions;
b) has an infinite number of solutions. In case 6), it contains all the solutions.

5. The length of the base side of a regular quadrangular paramid is $a$, the length of the apothem of the paramid is $\frac{3}{2}a$. The orthogonal projection of the paramid onto a plane perpendicular to one of the lateral faces is an isosceles trapezoid. Find the area of ​​this trapezoid.

Option 3

1. Solve the equation
$$2x - \sqrt{x^3 + 2x^2 - 3x} = 0.$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_{\frac{x-1}{5x-6}} (\sqrt{6} - 2x) < 0.$$

3. A circle is inscribed in an equilateral trapezoid. The distance from the center of the circle to the point of intersection of the diagonals of the trapezoid is related to the radius as 3 to 5. Find the ratio of the perimeter of the trapezoid to the length of the circle.

4. On the coordinate plane, we consider the set $N$ of all points whose coordinates $(a; b)$ satisfy the conditions $a < b$, $|a| < 3$, $|b| < 3$ and such that the equation
$$\left(a^3 - b^3\right)x^4 + (3a + b)x^2 + \frac{1}{a-b} = 0$$
has no roots.

a) Does the point $P(-2; -1)$ belong to the set $N$?

b) Find the area of ​​the polygon whose interior region is the set $N$.

5. On edge $BB_1$ of cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ we take point $F$ so that $|B_1F| = \frac{1}{3} |BB_1|$, on edge $C_1D_1$ we take point $E$ so that $|D_1E| = \frac{1}{3} |C_1D_1|$. What is the most significant value that the relation $\frac{|AP|}{|PQ|}$ can take, where the point $P$ lies on the ray $(DE)$, and the point $Q$ lies on the straight line $A_1F$? Solution to problem option 1

1. Expressing $\cos^2 x$ and $\cos 2x$ in terms of $y = \sin x$, we obtain the system
$$\begin{cases} 4y^2 + 8y + 3 = 0, \\ |y| \leq 1, \end{cases}$$
whence $y = -\frac{1}{2}$. It remains to solve equation $\sin x = -\frac{1}{2}$.
