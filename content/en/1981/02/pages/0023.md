Robert Wood's Pseudo Lens

(For the beginning, see p. 18)

From here, according to the formula $\frac{1}{d} + \frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{F}$, the distance of the pseudolens can be found by the formula: $F = 19,5$ cm. Pseudolenses with a diameter of about 6 mm have a distance of the order of 10 cm.

What is the principle of operation of a pseudolens? Glycerol straightness index $n_r = 1,47$; and the water straightness indicator is $n_B = 1,33$. Water, diffusing into a homogeneous cylinder consisting of iron and glycerin, will displace glycerin from it. In this case, the cylinder becomes heterogeneous, and its straightness index gradually decreases from the center to the edges. And also the system, as already mentioned, behaves like a converging lens.

Note that after about a day the composition becomes homogeneous again, and pseudolinza loses its amazing properties.
