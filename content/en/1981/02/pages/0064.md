Each sandbox is acted upon by the gravity force $mg$ and the normal reaction force $N$. Let's project forces on the horizontal and vertical coordinate axes and write the corresponding equations of Newton's second law:
$$|\vec{N}| \sin \alpha = m\omega^2 R \sin \alpha,$$
$$|\vec{N}| \cos \alpha - mg = 0.$$
Solving these equations together, we get
$$\left(m\omega^2 R - \frac{mg}{\cos \alpha}\right) \sin \alpha = 0,$$
from which two solutions are possible: 1) $\sin \alpha = 0$;
2) $\cos \alpha = \frac{g}{\omega^2 R}$. If $\omega^2 R < g$, only the first solution is implemented, that is, the sandboxes remain at the bottom of the sphere. If $\omega^2 R > g$, both solutions are realized, with the first corresponding to an unstable equilibrium, and the second to a stable equilibrium position.
