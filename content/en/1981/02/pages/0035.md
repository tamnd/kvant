1. On a hot summer day, representatives of several companies gathered for negotiations.
At first, when discussing bilateral treaties, two people sat at each table - and the ministers placed a bottle of mineral water on each table.
Then the representatives divided into groups of three - and the ministers brought each group a bottle of lemonade.
Finally, four people sat at each table - and a bottle of fruit juice was placed on each table. In addition, each representative drank a bottle of Pepsi-Cola.
After the meeting, 50 empty bottles were collected from all tables.
(It is known that all the drinks served were drunk and he never took a single bottle with him.)
How many people attended the meeting?
