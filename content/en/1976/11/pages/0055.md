Plant-technical college at the Moscow Automobile Plant named after. I. A. Likhacheva

Factory-technical college at the automobile plant named after. I. A. Likhacheva is a new type of higher education institution, organizing a combination in its program of theoretical and derivative training of special qualifications. The Institute trains engineers for industrial training at ZIL, AZLK, 1-GPZ and their branches located in 15 cities of the Soviet Union.

The plant-technological college at ZIL has three faculties: automotive, mechanical and technological and evening. The Faculty of Automotive trains mechanical engineers in the design and research of automobiles, automobile piston engines and automobile bodies, and the training of engineers in the specialty of automobile bodies is the only one in the Soviet Union. The automotive faculty includes the following specialties:

1. Car and tractors with special car bodies.
2. Internal motors.
3. Machines and technological processing of metals by pressure.
4. Machines and technological cooking production.
5. Economics and organization of the machine-building industry.
The Faculty of Mechanical Technology trains mechanical engineers in the following specialty:

1. Metal science, equipment and technological terminology of metal processing.
2. Mechanical engineering technology, machines and tools with specialties:
   a) technological equipment;
   b) tool production;
   c) repair and modernization of metal-cutting equipment;
   d) electronic and electronic methods of metal processing.
3. Machinery and technological foundry production.

4. Automation and complete set of mechanization of machine building.

The evening faculty trains mechanical engineers in the above-mentioned specialties.

The preparation of highly qualified specialties is carried out by highly qualified teaching staff with the involvement of leading specialties in basic subjects.

The institute has a military department, where upon completion of training young men are assigned an officer. All young men receive a professional driver's license.

Students of the institute will take advantage of all the benefits established for full-time students, as well as benefits provided for workers of such large factories as ZIL, AZLK, 1-GPZ.

For the entire duration of their studies, students receive a monthly salary of 0.5 months for professorial time, and 0.5 months of a stipend for study time, increased by 15% compared to the stipends of full-time students.

What is the peculiarity of training engineers in the “plant-technical college” system?

In the 1st year of full-time faculties of the institute, classes are conducted as in regular institutes. Starting from the 2nd year of study, derivative activities are carried out with and without interruption with a weekly alternation, i.e., during the academic week, students study in the classrooms of the institute, in the next working week, students work at base factories (ZIL, AZLK, 1-GPZ), participating in the production of the main products of items.

The institute's curriculum in the subject industrial training section states that in the second and third courses, students work at workplaces as work depending on the chosen specialty (assemblers, mechanics, repairmen, machine operators, stampers, etc.). Starting from the fourth year, students are transferred to engineering technical positions, where they will perform the functions of the technical departments of the institute; students conduct independent research work related to solving problems facing the base plants.

The general translation program for the training of engineers, despite the participation in the production process of base plants, provides for the study of general translation disciplines in a volume equivalent to the volume in a general higher technical educational institution.
