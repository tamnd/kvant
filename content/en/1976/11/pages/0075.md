⟦folio 73⟧

Quests

1. Pop and Balda are playing for “shel-bans” in the next game. Without showing each other, they each write a sequence of 1976 plus or minus signs. After this, signs are written out in a circle: the first sign from the Popa set, the first sign from the Baldy set, the second sign from the Popa set, the second sign from the Baldy set, and so on. Balda gives Pop as many shelbans in as many places on the plane next to the minute. How should Pop play in order to get fewer Shelbans in the worst case scenario?

2. Pupils of two gray-haired classes bought 737 textbooks. Everyone bought the same number of books. How many seventh graders were there and how many textbooks did each of them buy?

3. At figure you see two multiplication tricks. In each example, each letter has its own number. Which?

4. Nine numbers a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, k are different from zero. Prove that among the numbers aek, dhc, bfg, -gec, -ahf, -bdk there is at least one positive and at least one negative.

5. Imagine that a funnel is filled with small metal filings, which flow freely from the “spout” of the funnel. A metal wire is stuck into the sawdust, the other end of which is wound around a glass rod. What will happen to sawdust if you rub a stick with a piece of woolen material? To make sure your “prediction” is correct, try to reproduce this simple experiment.
