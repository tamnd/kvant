⟦folio 65⟧

Olympiad for physicists

1. The Chairman of the Yugori Olympiad in Physics, Professor S. S. Shushkevich, presents the awards to the winner.

2. Olympiad winners who received 1st degree diplomas: E. Ponomarev, O. Lishchenko (both winners of the Competition «Kvant»), R. Sharipov and A. Sorokin.

3. Yu. Mukharsky, who received a third degree diploma, is the winner of the Competition «Kvant».

4. I. Gavrilov, awarded a second degree diploma; the youngest participant in the final round of the Olympiad.

5. Kh. Abdulin, who received a third degree diploma; winner of the Competition «Kvant».

6. V. Bulatov, awarded a II degree diploma and specialization for the best solution to problems in the theoretical round.

7. L. Chernykh, who received a third degree diploma and specialization for the best performance of an experimental task.
