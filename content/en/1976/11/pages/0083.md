MULTI-COLORED PARALLELEPIPED

At figure you see a scan of twelve
cups painted in 6 colors: red,
yellow, green, blue, white and black.
Glue the cubes along these developments, and
then fold them into a rectangular
paraallepiped 2×2×3 so that on
each of its side faces measuring 2x3
all six colors were present. At the same time
the cups must match equally
colored grains.

L. Mochalov
