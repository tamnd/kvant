F381. In a device for determining the isomotic state (mass spectroeraphy), singly charged potassium units with atomic weights $A_1 = 39$ and $A_2 = 41$ are first accelerated in an electric field, then enter a uniform magnetic field peredicular to the direction of their movement (Fig. 15). During the experiment, due to the imperfection of the equipment, the accelerating mechanism is placed around the average value $u_0$ by amounts of ±Δu. With what relative power $\frac{\Delta u}{u_0}$ must the accelerating value be maintained so that the potassium isomotive does not overlap?

A moving charged particle in a magnetic field is acted upon by the Lorentz force, the perpendicular velocity of the particle is equal in absolute value to qvB. Here q is the charge of the particle, v is its speed, B is the magnetic field induction. In a uniform magnetic field, a particle will move in a circle, the radius R of which can be found from Newton’s second law:

$$m \frac{v^2}{R} = qvB, \quad R = \frac{mv}{qB},$$

where m is the particle mass. If we use the law of conservation of energy

$$\frac{mv^2}{2} = qu,$$

then the radius of the trajectory can be expressed in terms of the accelerating potential u:

$$R = \frac{mv}{qB} = \frac{1}{B} \sqrt{\frac{2um}{q}}.$$

This correspondence shows that the radius of the trajectory depends on the product um. When the accelerating potential changes, the radius of the trajectory of each of the potassium beams will change (see Fig. 15). To obtain non-overlapping, the following condition must be met:

$$(u_0 + \Delta u)m_1 < (u_0 - \Delta u)m_2,$$

or

$$m_1 + \frac{\Delta u}{u_0}m_1 < m_2 - \frac{\Delta u}{u_0}m_2,$$

where m₁ and m₂ are the masses of potassium ions, proportional to the atomic weights A₁ and A₂, respectively. From here

$$\frac{\Delta u}{u_0} < \frac{m_2 - m_1}{m_2 + m_1} = \frac{A_2 - A_1}{A_2 + A_1} = 0.025 = 2.5\%.$$

Note that modern experimental technology makes it possible to record the accelerating potential with much higher accuracy (by several orders of magnitude).

S. Kozel
