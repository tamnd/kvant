⟦folio 71⟧

New books

In this issue we place annotations on books on mathematics and physics published in the fourth quarter of 1976 that are of interest to our readers.

Matematika

Publishing house "Science"

1. Bashmakov M. II. Equations and inequalities (Library of the School of Physics and Mathematics). 2nd edition, revised. Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 14 kopecks.

The range of issues discussed in the book is quite limited - it deals almost exclusively with algebraic equations and inequalities and devotes quite a bit of space to interesting and important problems in proving inequalities. There is no complex theory here; most of the book consists simply of examples. On the other hand, although in the book, in general, the most attractive, sometimes attractive, comes to break and create new ones.

The book is of a distinctly “technical” nature. There are many problems in it that require only good knowledge of school mathematics, and are close to competition problems for entering college. The examples shown in the text require careful analysis with a pencil in hand.

The book ends with “Brief Summary”; they collect basic concepts and give ten useful “Tips”.

The book is intended for schoolchildren in grades 9-10, teachers and people who study mathematics independently.

2. Vasiliev N. B., Gutenacher V. L. Straight lines and curves (Library of the Physics and Mathematics School). 2nd edition, revised. Volume 6 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 16 kopecks.

The book contains about two hundred problems in elementary geometry. The problems are divided into several cycles, which include both traditional topics: problems on finding geometric places (sets of points), problems on replacement points in a triangle, construction problems, and quite difficult problems on maximum and minimum, including conditional extremum, and Olympiad problems. There are quite a few worthy problems in the book that require a little research.

The reader will become familiar with the use of the coordinate method and set-theoretic language in geometrics (understandable intersections and unions of sets), transformations, families of linear level functions on the plane, and kinematics. In this form he talks about ellipses, hyperbolas, parabolas, some curves that arose during the movement of figures, and the properties of tangents to these curves. The appendix to the book provides a summary of the basic formulas of the coordinate method.

The book will be useful for students in grades 8-10, teachers, and leaders of mathematical circles.

3. Shklyarsky D. O., Chentsov N. N., Yaglom II. M. Selected problems and theoreticians of elementary mathematics. Arthemetic and algebra (Library of mathematical circles). 5th edition, revised.

Volume 25 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 93 kopecks.

This collection of problems - the first issue of the "Library of the Mathematical Circle" series, published for the first time in 1950 - is compiled based on materials from one of the oldest circles - the circle at Moscow University. M. V. Lomonosov. Unlike most problem books intended for schoolchildren, it does not aim to deepen or consolidate the reader’s meanings acquired at school. The purpose of the collection is to introduce the reader to a number of methods and ideas that are new to him and to lead him to independent mathematical creativity. Therefore, in the collection there are no problems, for the solution of which it is enough only to formulate the meaning of a school mathematics course. There are also few types of “intelligence” problems that are most accustomed to students: on artificial methods of solving equations and systems of equations, on construction. But the collection contains many problems with non-standard formulations that require new approaches to be solved.

Much more attention is paid to those sections of elementary mathematics that are continued in modern scientific research. Some cycles of problems, revised and adapted for schoolchildren, present questions that are generalized in relation to “higher mathematics” (elements of number theory and probability theory, difference equations, etc.). Individual problems are taken from essays in mathematics classes and from articles published in server-based mathematical journals.

Although this collection may seem difficult to a reader accustomed to standard problems, we are confident that most of the problems are accessible to a real student.
