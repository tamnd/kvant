⟦folio 72⟧

Publishing house
"Education"
4. Morozova E. A., Petrokov I. S., Skortsov V. A. International mathematical olympids. 4th edition, revised. and additional Volume 15 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 55 kopecks.

The main content of the book is the texts of problems (with solutions or instructions) of international mathematical olympids. In addition, the book contains the most interesting problems sent to the jury of international olympids, but not included in the mathematical competitions, as well as some problems proposed at the national olympids of the participating countries (England, Sweden, Yugoslavia, Hungary, Romania, East Germany, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia, Poland, the Soviet Union). The fifth edition of the book additionally contains materials from the last five olympids - from XII to XVI.

The book will certainly bring pleasure to all high school students who love to solve difficult problems, and will be useful to the leader of mathematical circles.

5. Laptev V.L. Lobachevsky and his geometry. Volume 5 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.

This brochure tells the story of the life of the great Russian scientist Lobachevsky and provides an accessible presentation of his geometric ideas. At the end there is an overview of the further development of non-Euclidean geometry and modern applications of Lobachevsky geometry in mathematics and physics.

The book is addressed to students in grades 8-10.

Physics

Publishing house "Science"
1. Kapitsa P. L. Experiment, theory, practice. 2nd edition. Volume 25 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 07 k.

This book is a collection of articles and speeches by academician P. L. Kapitsa. In an extremely lively and witty manner, Kapitsa talks about his meetings and joint work with outstanding physicists of our time.

A number of articles are addressed directly to young people.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

2. Makovetsky P.V. Look at the root! 3rd edition, rev. and additional Volume 18 l., circulation 380,000 copies, price 69 kopecks.

The book contains original problems in physics and related fields of science (cosmonautics, astronomy, etc.).

The paradoxical nature of the task is often supported by their nomoristic illumination and comic elitraps (taken from the aphorisms of Kozma Prugtkov).

The book is intended for high school students and can be successfully used in physical circles.

Publishing house "Mir"
3. Feynman R., Layton R., Sands M. Feynman lectures in physics. Translation from English Vol. 1. Modern science of nature. Laws of mechanics. Vol. 11. Space, time, movement. 2nd edition. Volume 25 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 1 rub. 94 k.

The physics course of the famous American physical theorist Richard Feynman has won wide international recognition. The first edition of this course in 9 issues was published in the Soviet Union several years ago by the same publishing house. This publication was published during 1976-1978.

Simplicity of presentation, beautiful and clear language, absence of cumbersome mathematical calculations

making these books accessible to high school students.

4. Erden-Gruz T. Fundamentals of the structure of matter. Translation from Hungarian. Volume 25 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 46 k.

In popular form, the author examines issues of the structure of matter: general principles of the physics of the microworld; structure of atoms, atomic nucleus, molecule; the nature of chemical bonds; structure of crystalline and non-crystalline bodies. The author dwelled in detail on the practical use of research in the field of the microworld.

Atomizate
5. Artsimovich L.A. Chimo every physicist should know about plasma. Volume 8 l., circulation 80,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.

This book is intended for the curious reader who wants to obtain the latest information about the modern field of science - plasma physics.

At first, physicists were interested in plasma as a kind of conductor of electronic current, and also as a source of light. Now it is already considered as a natural state of matter heated to a very high temperature, and as a dianimic system - an object of application of electronic physics.

6. Starz J. Molecules of life. Translation from English Volume 6 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.

The book presents in a lively and accessible form the elementary concepts of biomics and describes the “molecules of life,” that is, those molecules that take part in life processes.

The book is of definite interest to a wide range of readers.
