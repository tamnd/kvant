presents a short mathematical study and that the jury recommends that in one (maximum two) of them (of their choice) each participant should go as far as possible. These tasks were divided by nature so that everyone could find a task to their liking. It must be said that preparing such tasks is a very difficult task, since it is necessary to compose tasks of approximately the same level, quite complex and varied in topic. The jury received a number of interesting papers in which there was fairly complete progress in solving one or two problems. In general, the jury found the experiment useful and believes that tasks of such a research plan are desirable not only at the final type of Olympiad, but also at various stages of work with schoolchildren.

The participants' work was checked by class commissions. Several jury members participated in the discussion of each work. Then an analysis of the problem was organized for the Olympiad participants and team leaders. Participants had the opportunity to familiarize themselves with the results of checking all works and, if necessary, appeal the decision of the jury.

All this required a lot of stress from the jury members, who sometimes had to work until 3-4 o’clock in the morning.

The intense work of the Olympiad participants was replaced by active, varied recreation. The participants of the Olympiad performed interesting and exciting experimental experiments at the Pampir refrigerator factory, at the unique Nurek hydroelectric power station, examined a high-rise dam, and went down to the turbine hall; We enthusiastically got acquainted with the Gissar public school, the Gissar fortress, and visited VDNKh and the Republican Museum of Local Lore. This eventful program was interspersed with meetings with new friends - students of Dushanbe schools. The friendship that arose from the first days of the Olympids continued: the guys exchanged addresses, wrote letters, and made plans for meetings.

In their free time, the guys were given a series of interesting lectures: “Minimum distances”, “Different infinities”, “Billiards”, “Fundamentals of mathematical logic”, “Assignment for the Moscow Mathematical Olympiad”.

The editorial office of the magazine «Kvant» was carried out several times in connection with schoolchildren. Almost all “Olympians” are active readers of our magazine. At these meetings, which were lively and interesting, the schoolchildren expressed a number of wishes, which the editors will try to take into account in their work.

We would like to note with satisfaction the very good organization of the Olympiad. Both the Olympics itself and the guys’ access are organized very clearly and interestingly.

Based on the results of two days, the jury of the X All-Union Mathematical Olympiad decided on the presence of 12 first, 25 second and 33 third prizes. In addition, it was decided to mark 29 participants with similar reviews of the 1st degree, and 32 participants with similar reviews of the 2nd degree.

Many children received signs of organization and enterprise for participants in the final stage of the All-Union Olympiad.

The special feature of the magazine «Kvant» was loaded with 8th grade student from Moscow Viktor Galperin.

Samvel Abadzhyan, Pavel Borovikov, Vyacheslav Krotov, Andrey Letchkov, Bidina Midodashili, Rustam Ubaidulaev, Alexey Folin, Olga Sharapova were awarded for excellent success in the journal registration Olympiad «Kvant» (signature for 1977).
