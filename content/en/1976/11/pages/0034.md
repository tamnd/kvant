M412. In the city, every square has at least three streets. All streets have one-way traffic so that you can drive from any square to any other. M413. For which positive numbers $a$ is the following statement true: for any function $f$ defined on the segment $[0,1]$, continuous at every point of this segment and such that $f(0) = f(1) = 0$, the equation $f(x + a) - f(x) = 0$ has a solution?

a) First clarify this question for the case $a = 1/2$.

6) Prove that for $a = 1/n$, where $n$ is a natural number, the stated statement is true.

b) Prove that for the remaining positive $a$ it is not true.

When solving this problem, the following property of continuous functions can be useful: if the function $g$ is defined on the segment $[a, b]$, is continuous at each point of this segment and at the ends of its membership of different signs, then between $a$ and $b$ will find a point $c$ at which $g(c) = 0$.
