It is much easier for engineer graduates of a plant-technical college who have thoroughly studied the organization and economics of production during their studies to immediately get involved in the rhythm of production and actively participate in the implementation of the enterprise’s planned tasks.

As practice has shown, our engineering graduates have successfully worked at base enterprises and their branches, becoming true production managers who actively solve the problems of scientific and technological progress.

So, although this year the plant-technical college held only its tenth graduation, among its graduates there are plant directors, deputy directors, chief plant mechanics, deputy chief engineers, heads of departments and units. In Zila alone, more than 10 workshops were proclaimed by graduates of the plant-technical college.

Students of the plant-college school have the opportunity to take an active part in the sports life of one of the largest sports clubs in the country “Torpedo” Zil, sports clubs “Moskvich” and “Podshipnik”.

The plant-college school annually hosts the Shirokayauz Spartakis, which attracts practically all first- to fifth-year students. Students make excellent progress. The second includes volleyball, basketball, football, handball, skiing, and athletics. All students learn to swim. Students involved in sports regularly participate in activities with college teams and production teams in Moscow and other cities. A large group of students is part of the combined teams of base factories, MGS and CS DSO "Trud". A number of students who are members of the USSR national teams in certain sports study at the plant-college school.

Students from the plant-technical college participated in the USSR national team in the Olympic Games in Montreal.

The full-time department of the institute accepts persons working at base factories (ZIL, AZLK, 1-GPZ), Moscow schoolchildren sent for training by these factories, and non-resident applicants sent by the administration of films at the base factory. Students sent to record films from the base plants of the Avto-Zil, AZLK association, as well as from KAMAZ, are presented with a pleasant community.

Those entering the 1st year of the plant-technical college at Zila will take entrance exams within the scope of the high school curriculum in the following subjects: mathematics (written and oral), physics (written) and socialization.

Entrance exams are taken in three terms: July (for evening and daytime faculties), August (for daytime faculties) and September (for evening faculties).

Below we provide options for entrance exams in mathematics and physics in 1976.

Matematika

Option 1

1. A cone, the height of which is $H$ and the radius of the base is $R$, is fixed in a plumb position with its apex down. Water is poured into the cone to a height of $h$ and an iron ball of radius $r$ is inserted and completely immersed in it. At what height is the water level?

2. Solve inequality
$$\log_{x^2}(x^2 - 4x + 3) > 1.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$1 + 2\cos 2x + 2\cos 4x + 2\cos 6x = 0.$$

Option 2

1. A right triangular straight ball. Find the radius of the ball if the length of the side of the base of the line is $l$ and the height of the line is $h$.

2. Solve the equation
$$(4 + \sqrt{15})^x + (4 - \sqrt{15})^x = 62.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$5\sin 2x + \sin x + \cos x = 1.$$

Physics

1. A horizontal stone is thrown from a circular river bank with a height of $h = 20$ m at a speed of $v_0 = 15$ m/sec. How long will it take for the stone to reach the water? At what speed will it fall into the water? Acceleration of free fall is assumed to be $g = 10$ m/sec².

2. Two weights are suspended from the endstone, thrown over a fixed block: on the left with a mass of $m_1 = 40$ e, on the right - with a mass of $m_2 = 120$ e. Determine thread tension. Take $g = 10$ m/sec².

3. The disk moves in a horizontal plane, making $n = 30$ rpm. The body lies at a distance of $r = 20$ cm from the axis of rotation. What must be the coefficient of friction to prevent it from being thrown off the disk?

4. A projectile with mass $m_1 = 10$ ke, flying horizontally at a speed $v_1 = 500$ m/sec, hits a wall of sand with mass $m_2 = 10^4$ ke and gets stuck in it. What will be the speed of the car if, before the projectile hit, it was moving at a speed of $v_2 = 36$ km/h in the same direction as the projectile?

5. A steel cutter with mass $m = 400$ e was heated to a temperature $t_1 = 800^\circ\text{C}$ and immersed in water for ordering. Water volume 10 l, temperature $t_2 = 20^\circ\text{C}$. To what temperature will the cutter cool? Specific heat of water $c_B = 4,19 \cdot 10^3 \text{ дж}/(\text{кг} \cdot \text{сради})$, steel $c_{\text{СТ}} = 460 \text{ дж}/(\text{кг} \cdot \text{сради})$. Round the answer to the nearest whole number.
