The reader interested in the purely mathematical problems of the works of Adams and Le Verrier will not ignore the appendices in which the authors present in processed form the calculations of Adams and Le Verrier, which ultimately led to the discovery of Neptune. He will also be recognized with the truly gigantic work that was extended by Lovel and Tombaugh, co-authors of the decade-long discovery of the solar system - Pluto.

We can safely say that the overwhelming number of readers, having started reading “Ponski and the Discoveries of the Planets,” will not leave the book until the celestial objects that have the right to be called members of our planetary system have been studied, and the same can be hammered by us...”

Such a preface was prescribed by the authors of the books “Ponski and the Discoveries of the Planets”*). They set themselves the task of trying to tell a wide range of readers about the events associated with the discovery of the celestial early planetary system in the 19th and 20th centuries, while revealing the role of the events, showing their significance for the development of our ideas about the structure of the Solar system. The reader of this book learned not only about the evolution of scientific proposals on the fate of discoveries and scientists, but about much more.

This book is intended for a wide range of readers (starting with high school students), for all those who are interested in the history of science in general and the history of astronomy in particular.
