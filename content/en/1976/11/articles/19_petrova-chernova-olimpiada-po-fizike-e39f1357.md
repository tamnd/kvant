Physics Olympiad

The final round of the X All-Union Olympiad in Physics took place this year in Minsk, the capital of the Belarusian SSR. Its participants were the winners of the Resurrection Olympids, as well as masters of the previous All-Union Olympids. In total, 144 schoolchildren came to Minsk: 42 eighth-graders, 48 ​​ninth-graders and 54 tenth-graders.

On April 15, the grand opening of the final round of the X All-Union Physics Olympiad took place at the Republican Boarding School for Music and Fine Arts. The participants were wished success in the upcoming struggle: to replace the Minister of Education of the BSSR R.I. Sernov, to represent the jury of the Olympida by the head of the Department of Nuclear Physics of the Belarusian State University, Professor S.S. Shushkevich; The introduction of the USSR Minister of Education M. A. Prokofiev was read by the representative of the USSR Ministry of Education M. V. Grabilenkov.

On April 16, the first theoretical round of the Olympiad took place. As always, 8th graders were offered 4 problems, 9th graders and 10th graders were given 5 problems each. The 8th and 9th graders were given 4 hours each to solve the problem, and the 10th grader - 5 hours.

On April 17, the guys were separated. And the jury checked the work. After testing, we found out that the most difficult problem for eighth-graders was problem No. 3 (only 2 people solved it), and the easiest was problem No. 1 (the texts of the problems are given at the end of the article). Of the ninth-class students, only 2 people solved problem No. 5, and almost all of them successfully completed task No. 3. Among the ten-class students, problem No. 3 caused the most difficulty (only 4 people completed it), but problem No. 2 solved a lot of problems.

On April 18, the second—experimental—round of the Olympiad took place. As last year, experimental tasks were completed by all Olympiad participants. But, unlike previous Olympiads, this time 2 experimental problems were offered in each class.

April 19 was perhaps the most difficult day for the jury members. Again, all theoretical works were reviewed, the results of experimental work were discussed, and after careful analysis and weighing, the best ones were named.

On April 20, at the ceremonial closing of the X All-Union Olympiad in Physics, the decision of the experimental work was announced to its participants, and after careful analysis and weighing, the best ones were named.
