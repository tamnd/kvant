$$A_2 = \frac{q}{r_1} - \frac{q}{r_2}.$$

The work of moving a single charge from point $N$ to point $N_2$ is equal to the sum of the two works found:

$$A_1 + A_2 = \frac{q}{r} - \frac{q}{r_1} + \frac{q}{r_1} - \frac{q}{r_2} = \frac{q}{r} - \frac{q}{r_2}.$$

Repeating these calculations, we obtain that the work of moving a single charge from the same point $N$ to point $N_3$, located at a distance $r_3$ from the field source, is numerically equal to

$$A_1 + A_2 + A_3 = \frac{q}{r} - \frac{q}{r_3}.$$

Thus, the work of moving a single charge from one point in the field of the exact charge $q$ to any other point depends only on the distances of these points to the charge $q$. The work of moving a single positive charge from point $N$ in infinite space is not an integral part of this system.

Tasks for our readers

1. Prove that from any set $10^n$ of natural numbers one can be eliminated, and the remaining ones can be divided into subsets of 3 numbers each so that the sum of the numbers in each subset gives a remainder of 0 or 1 when divided by 3.

A. Verkhovod (Almaty)

2. Prove inequality $a_{ij} \geq 0$;
   a) $\sqrt{c(a-c)} + \sqrt{c(b-c)} > \left| \frac{c}{b} - \frac{c}{a} \right| \times \sqrt{ab}$, where $0 < c < a$, $c < b$;
   b) $\left( \frac{c}{a} + \frac{c}{b} \right) \cdot \sqrt{ab} < \sqrt{(a+c)(b+c)} + \sqrt{(a-c)(b-c)} \leq 2\sqrt{ab}$, where $0 < c < a$, $c < b$.
   c. Berkovayko (village Kotovo, Stary Oskol district)
