Experimental tour

Problems for this type were prepared by teachers and researchers of the Department of Nuclear Physics and Peaceful Use of Nuclear Energy of the Belarusian State University. V.I. Lenin.

8th grade

1. Using a direct current source, resistors, an amperor and a voltmeter, determine the connection diagram and electrical parameters of the parts located in the box.

2. Determine the weight of the load using a shirring rod, a stubby rod, a school diameter meter, a load whose weight exceeds the measurement limits of the diameter meter, thread, or graph paper.

9th grade

1. Measure atmospheric pressure. Equipment: 1) glass tubes, 2) rubber tube, 3) cork, 4) shite, 5) ruler, 6) glass of water.

2. Determine the capacitance of the capacitor. Equipment: 1) capacitors - 2 pcs., 2) electric power source. d. pp., 3) voltmeter, 4) connected conductors, 5) stopwatch, 6) reference resistances - 3 pcs.

10th grade

1. Determine what electrical parts are in the box and how they are connected using an ampere-voltmeter.

2. Determine the refractive index of the liquid in the glass. You can use a ruler, a light bulb, a battery and a screen.
