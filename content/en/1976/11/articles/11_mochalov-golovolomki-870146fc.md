⟦folio 31⟧

PUZZLES

Chips on the field

On the playing field, consisting of 25 cells and 2 partitions (see figure), there are 20 chips: 10 red and 10 green.

In one move, you can move any chip to any free cell along free cells.

What is the minimum number of moves needed to swap red and green chips?

Domino star

Arrange all 28 dominoes in the form of a seven-pointed star (four dominoes on each ray), but so that:
1) dice with 0.1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 points went into the center;
2) at the ends of the beam there were also all the points from 0 to 6;
3) in each ray, the bones are placed according to the rules of the domino game: 0 to 0, 1 to 1, etc.;
4) The sums of points for domino tiles were the same in all beams.

Clock face

There are 12 grains drawn on the circle, the thirteenth is in the center. Some grains are connected by lines; on the outer grains there are 12 chips with numbers from 1 to 12 (see figure).
Our goal: having won one chip per free chip along the lines, bet each chip on the chip with its number.
The problem has a solution consisting of 49 moves (movements).
Or maybe you can find a shorter one?

L. Mochalov
