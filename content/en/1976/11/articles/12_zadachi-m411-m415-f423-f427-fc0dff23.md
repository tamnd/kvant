Tasks
M411—M415; F423—F427

M411. Three segments with ends on the sides of the triangle, parallel to its sides, pass through the same point and have the same length $x$ (Fig. 1). Find $x$ if the side lengths of the triangle are $a, b, c$.

A. Yagubyants

M412. In the city, every square has at least three streets. All streets have one-way traffic so that you can drive from any square to any other. Prove that it is possible to ban traffic on one of the streets (in the area between two squares) so that it is still possible to drive from any square to any other.

A. Goldbere

M413. For which positive numbers $a$ is the following statement true: for any function $f$ defined on the segment $[0,1]$, continuous at every point of this segment and such that $f(0) = f(1) = 0$, the equation $f(x + a) - f(x) = 0$ has a solution?

a) First clarify this question for the case $a = 1/2$.

6) Prove that for $a = 1/n$, where $n$ is a natural number, the stated statement is true.

b) Prove that for the remaining positive $a$ it is not true.

When solving this problem, the following property of continuous functions can be useful: if the function $g$ is defined on the segment $[a, b]$, is continuous at each point of this segment and at the ends of its membership of different signs, then between $a$ and $b$ will find a point $c$ at which $g(c) = 0$.

H. Yaelom

M414. a) Of the heel of triangles cut off from a given convex pentagon, the area of ​​four is $S$, the area of ​​the fifth is $3S/2$. Find the area $x$ of the pentagon.

A. Tikhomirov

M415. What is the maximum number of kings that can be placed on a horizontal board $n \times n$ without them being next to each other? A toric chessboard is obtained from a general size $n \times n$, in which the upper and lower horizontal lines, as well as the left and right verticals, are considered glued. On a horizontal board, from each square the king can be pinned to eight adjacent squares (Fig. 2).

A. Footer

F423. The mass of the balloon together with the rope trailing behind it is equal to $M$ (Fig. 3). The Archimedean buoyant force acting on the ball is $F$, the friction coefficient of the rope on the ground is $\mu$. The air resistance force acting on the balloon is proportional to the relative air speed of the balloon $F_c = -\alpha v$. Find the speed of the ball relative to the ground if a horizontal wind blows at a speed of $u$.

A. Trubachev

F424. In order for light bulbs designed for a network voltage of 110 $\theta$ to be switched on with a voltage of 220 $\theta$, you can use a rheostat, which can be turned on according to schemes $a$ and $b$ (Fig. 4). Find the efficiency of each of the circuits. The resistance of the light bulb is 1000 $\Omega$, and the resistance of the rheostat is 2000 $\Omega$.

F425. A metal plate with a charge $Q$ rises into the space between the plates of an uncharged flat capacitor. In this case, gaps $l_1$ and $l_2$ remain between the plate and the plates of the capacitor. The areas of all plates are the same and equal to $S$. Determine the potential difference between the capacitor plates.

F426. In a smoke burst from opaque parts of radius $r_1 = 5$ µm with a substance mass content of $m = 0,04$ g per cubic meter of air, the visibility range is $l_1 = 50$ m. How much substance in a cubic meter of air is sprayed by another source of the pellet, which creates particles of radius $r_2 = 10$ µm, if visibility is reduced to $l_2 = 20$ m?
