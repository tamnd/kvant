Tasks

M401—M405; F413–F417

M401. Inside an acute triangle ABC, a point P is given such that $APB = \widehat{ABC} + 60^\circ$, $\widehat{BPC} = \widehat{BAC} + 60^\circ$, $\widehat{CPA} = \widehat{CBA} + 60^\circ$. Prove that the intersection points of the continuations of the segments AP, BP, CP (beyond point P) with the circumcircle $\Delta ABC$ lie at the vertices of an equilateral triangle.

A. Yagubyants

M402. Prove that there is no strictly increasing sequence of non-negative integers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ for which the correspondence $a_{nm} = a_n + a_m$ holds for any n and m.

Yu. Ionin

M403. Prove that if in a convex polygon an even number of edges emerge from each vertex, then in any section of it by a plane that does not pass through any of its vertices, the result is a polygon with four sides.

M404. The first n volumes of the encyclopedia are on the shelf. You can carry out the following operation with it: take any three adjacent volumes and place them between any two volumes, as well as at the beginning or end of a row, without changing the order of these three volumes. Is it possible, after applying the specified operation several times, to arrange them in ascending order of volume numbers (regardless of the initial arrangement of volumes)?

A. Savin

M405. On the chessboard of size $99 \times 99$ the figure F is marked (this figure will be different in points a), b) and c)). In each cell of the F figure there is a beetle. At each moment the beetles flew up and landed again in the cells of the same figure F; Moreover, several beetles could eat in one cage. After the flight, any two beetles that occupied adjacent cells ended up back in adjacent cells or ended up on the same cell. (Neighbouring are cells that have a common side or a common vertex.)

F413. There is an ideal blocking layer with a p-n junction. The thickness of this layer is d, the dielectric constant is ε. Draw a graph of the electric field strength and potential in the layer, assuming the charge density distribution in the layer is as shown in figure 3. (9 cells)

F414. The stiffness coefficient of a rubber band of length l and mass m is equal to k. A ring made from this rope rotates with angular velocity ω in a horizontal plane around a vertical axis passing through the center of the ring. Determine the radius of the turning ring. (8 grades)

F415 *. In a simplified way, an atom of a gel can be represented as a system in which two electrons oscillate around a common center—a stationary nucleus. Using this model, try to estimate the approximation of the dielectric constant of a liquid gel in a constant electric field, taking into account that gel strongly absorbs ultraviolet radiation at a wavelength λ = 0.06 μ. Liquid gel density ρ = 0.14 e/cm³.

S. Kozel

F416. An airplane, having flown a distance l₁ along a straight line AB, gets from point A to point B in time t₁. Then he travels the distance l₂ along the straight line BC from point B to point C in time t₂ and returns to point A along the straight line CA, covering the distance l₃ in the time t₃. During the flight, the wind blows, the speed of which does not change throughout the flight. Determine the wind speed if the speed of the aircraft relative to the air is constant in absolute value throughout the entire flight.

F417. The heat-conducting cylinder contains 5 e of hydrogen and 12 e of oxygen. The mixture is set on fire. Determine the pressure and temperature in the vessel if it is known that when 1 mole of water is formed, 2.4·10⁵ ds are released. The volume of the vessel is 100 liters. The initial temperature of the mixture of oxygen and hydrogen is 20 °C. The heat capacity of hydrogen at constant volume is 14.3 kJ/kg deg, and that of water vapor is 2.1 kJ/kg deg.
