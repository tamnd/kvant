Then each of these $k$ points is connected to at most $(20-k)$ points, and each of these $(20-k)$ points is connected to at most $k$ points. Hence the total number of segments is no more than $[k(20-k)+(20-k)]^2 = 100-(10-k)^2$. 3. The SIZ estimate follows from the inequality about the arithmetic mean and the geometric mean. Indeed, $x_{n+1} = \frac{x_n^3}{5} + \frac{1}{15x_n} + \frac{1}{15x_n} \geq 4\sqrt[4]{\frac{1}{5\cdot15\cdot15}} = \frac{4\sqrt[4]{3}}{15} > \frac{1}{3} > \frac{1}{5}$.

To obtain an upper bound, we solve the inequality $x_n > x_{n+1}$. We have:

$$x_n > \frac{x_n^4+1}{5x_n} \text{ или } x_n^4 - 5x_n^2 + 1 < 0,$$

i.e. $$\frac{5-\sqrt{21}}{2} < x_n^2 < \frac{5+\sqrt{21}}{2},$$

because

$$x_n > 0, \text{ получаем, что если } \sqrt{\frac{5-\sqrt{21}}{2}} < x_n < \sqrt{\frac{5+\sqrt{21}}{2}}, \text{ то } x_n > x_{n+1}.$$

This means that if $1 \leq x_n \leq 2$, then $x_{n+1}$ is less than 2. If $x_n < 1$ (the case $x_n > 2$ cannot exist, since $x_1 = 2$), then $x_{n+1} < 2$.

It follows from this that $x_n < 2$ for all $n > 1$, and the midnight inequality is proven.
