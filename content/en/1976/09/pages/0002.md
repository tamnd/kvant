You have already seen on the covers of our magazine more than once reproductions of paintings by the wonderful Dutch artist M. Escher (1898-1971). In almost all of his works, extraordinary ingenuity is combined with precise mathematical calculations. Escher called this picture, in which the transition from a flat image to a three-dimensional one was made, “Sky and Water.”
