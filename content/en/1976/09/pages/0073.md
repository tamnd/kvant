1. A group of eighth-graders decided to go on an excursion to Leningrad after the end of the school year. Every month each student contributed the same amount of money for everyone, and in 7 months 640 rubles were collected. 01 kop. How many students were there in the class and how much money did each bring in monthly?

2. There are nuts in three boxes. The first has 6 fewer nuts than the other two combined, and the second has 10 fewer nuts than the first and third combined. How many nuts are in the third box?

3. “Give me a foothold and I will change the world.” This statement was made by Archimedes after he discovered the rule of leverage. Since there was no suitable fulcrum (and still isn’t), he could not prove this statement experimentally. However, theoretically it is not difficult to verify that Archimedes somewhat overestimated his capabilities (and the capabilities of the lever). Try to calculate how far the free end of the lever would have to be moved in order to lift a body whose mass is equal to the mass of the Earth, that is, approximately 6.10²⁴ kg, by at least 1 mm; The average force created by a human hand is approximately 5 N.

4. A piece fell out of the book. The first page of the piece is numbered 387, and the last page number consists of the same numbers, but written in a different order. How many pages fell out of the book?

5. Prime numbers have only two different divisors: one and the number itself. What numbers have only three different divisors?
