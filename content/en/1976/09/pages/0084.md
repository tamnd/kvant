TO OUR READERS

A subscription for 1977 is announced for the popular science physics and mathematics journal «Kvant».

«Kvant» is addressed to all schoolchildren in grades 5–10 who love mathematics and physics, love solving problems and want to study exact sciences on a server in the future.

Our magazine is also useful for those schoolchildren whose interest in exact sciences is still dormant.

Review articles are published on the pages of our journal, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved; stories about scientists, about how scientific discoveries are born.

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems for university entrance exams, problems for various Olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Our magazine is also useful for teachers. Fundamental changes have now been made in school courses in mathematics and physics. «Kvant» struggles greatly to cover these changes on its pages, the public according to the new program.

In 1977, «Kvant» opens new sections: “Through the Pages of School Textbooks” and “The Art of Programming.” The articles in the first section will examine the most important issues of mathematics studied at school. The second section will be devoted to a story about the interaction between a person and a computer, teaching various algorithmic languages ​​in which programs that machines can understand are written.

What questions and tasks can an applicant expect for university entrance exams, tasks for various Olympiads and simply interesting tasks.

The magazine constantly publishes solutions to books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.
