⟦folio 17⟧

Cardan formula and geometry

A. Reznikov

You are familiar with how to use the coefficients of a second-degree equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ to determine how many real roots it has. If $p^2 - 4q > 0$, then there are two roots, if $p^2 - 4q = 0$, then there is one root, and finally, if $p^2 - 4q < 0$, then there are no roots.

In this issue on p. 14 a similar condition is derived for the third degree equation $x^3 + px + q = 0$. Using this condition, you can get one clever inequality connecting the elements of an arbitrary triangle. Here's how it's done.

Let $ABC$ be a triangle on the side $a, b, c$; $p$ is its semiperimeter, $S$ is its area, and $R, r, r_a, r_b, r_c$ are the radii of the circumscribed, inscribed and out-circles. It turns out that the following relationships are correct:

$$r_a + r_b + r_c = 4R + r, \quad r_a r_b + r_b r_c + r_c r_a = p^2, \quad r_a r_b r_c = p S.$$ (1)

Equalities (1) mean that the numbers $r_a, r_b, r_c$ are the roots of the following equation:

$$x^3 - (4R + r)x^2 + p^2 x - p S = 0.$$ (2)

This follows from the fact that $r_a, r_b, r_c$ are roots of the polynomial $(x - r_a)(x - r_b)(x - r_c) = x^3 - (r_a + r_b + r_c)x^2 + (r_a r_b + r_b r_c + r_c r_a)x - r_a r_b r_c$.

Having written down for equation (2) the conditions for the validity of all three of its roots*), after several transformations and using the formula $S = pr$, we arrive at the following inequality:

$$\left[ p^2 - (2R^2 + 10Rr - r^2) \right]^2 \leq 4R (R - 2r)^3$$ (3)

From (3) it follows that

$$R \geq 2r$$ (4)

So what

$$\left| p^2 - (2R^2 + 10Rr - r^2) \right| \leq 2 \sqrt{R(R - 2r)^3}$$ (5)

Equality in (3) and in (5) is achieved when two of the numbers $r_a, r_b, r_c$ are equal, that is, when the triangle is equilateral.

Battles

1. Prove formulas (1). To do this, derive the following expressions for $S: S = rp = r_a(p - a) = r_b(p - b) = r_c(p - c) = \frac{abc}{4R} = \sqrt{p(p - a)(p - b)(p - c)}$.

2. Find out the conditions for achieving equality in (4).

3. Prove that $16Rr - 5r^2 \leq p \leq 4R^2 + 4Rr + 3r^2$.

4. Investigate for which equilateral triangles the number $p^2 - (2R^2 + 10Rr - r^2)$ is non-negative.

*) See p. 14. To use the condition given there, continue with the substitution $x = y + \frac{4R + r}{3}$.
