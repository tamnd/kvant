In 1976, the third edition of M. S. Navashin’s book “Telecon of an Amateur Astronomer”, published at the end of 1975, went on sale - the only manual on amateur television in the reported literature. It talks about making your own televopa reflector, ready for server-based astronomical research. The book gives all the instructions necessary for this. The author of the book, one of the founders of accountable amateur television, had the opportunity to check everything he wrote about. The book was published after the death of the author, and a number of specialists were involved in the final preparation of the manuscript, who wrote additional sections devoted to both the design of televops and the methods of testing them.

At the beginning of 1976, a book by doctors of physical and mathematical sciences E. A. Greben and Yu. A. Ryabov, “Powders and Discoveries of Planets,” went on sale, most of which is devoted to the remarkable and dramatic history of the discovery of the eighth planet of the solar system - Neptune.

In one of the upcoming issues of our magazine we will talk about this book in more detail.

Two more popular science books should be published at the very end of 1976. One of them, the book by Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences I. A. Klimiin “Astronomy of Our Days,” requires a certain amount of preliminary preparation for reading. The author set himself the goal of talking about a wide range of issues considered by modern astronomy, about the basic concepts and laws of astronomy, explaining the physical essence of phenomena. He often resorts to formulas and graphs. In terms of presentation style, the book is close to the articles published in the magazine «Kvant».

Another book - “The Exploding Universe” by V.V. Kaetinsky and V.N. Komarov - popularly talks about all the major revolutions in modern astronomical science. The authors pay main attention to heated discussions that are accompanied by the birth of new ideas about the Universe associated with the discoveries of recent years.

Finally, let us note a book that is absolutely necessary for every qualified astronomy lover. This is the “Astronomical calendar (diary) for 1977.” The first parts of the calendar are a collection of effective data on astronomical phenomena in 1977 (coordinates of the Sun and Lunya; data on the moments of their sunrise and sunset and other information on each day of the year; a description of the visibility of the planets, accompanied by pictures of their movement across the sky; data on solar and lunar eclipses, on variable stars, on stars and planets covered by the Moon and a number of other materials). The second part (“Appendices”) publishes articles on the achievements of various departments of astronomy, a review of space research, materials dedicated to memorable dates in astronomy, instructions for observation, a bibliographic index of astronomical literature and other useful information.

New books

In this issue of the magazine we place short annotations on books on mathematics and physics published in the third quarter of 1976.

Matematika

Publishing house "Science"

1. Dorofeev G.V., Potapov M.K., Rozov N.Kh. A manual on mathematics for university applicants. 5th edition, revised. Volume 30 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 04 k.

Apparently, this book is well known to all schoolchildren preparing for university entrance exams - it has already gone through four editions and enjoys well-deserved popularity among teachers.

The fifth edition of the book differs significantly from the previous ones: the book has been revised by the authors by more than half. Changes were made related to the new school curriculum in mathematics; along with the old competitive problems, completely new ones were placed, in particular, versions of written exams in mathematics, which offered admission to Moscow State University in 1975.

2. Lurie M.V., Aleksandrov V.I. Problems for composing equations. Volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 14 kopecks.

This brochure is also addressed to applicants to universities: it is devoted to the traditional section of elementary mathematics - the task of composing equations. In it, problems are classified by types and methods of solution; The features of solving problems of each type are explained in detail.
