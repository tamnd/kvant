HOURGLASS

Fifteen chips are placed in the circles of the upper triangle at figure. It is necessary to transfer all the chips to the lower triangle, observing the following rules.
1) Chips can move either to an adjacent circle, or jump over an adjacent chip to the next free circle.
2) Movements and jumps can only be carried out along lines connecting the circles.
3) A move is also considered a cascade of jumps when a piece (according to paragraph 2) makes jumps one after another (but not movements).
How many moves will it take you to solve this problem?
L. Mochalov
Fig. E. Nazarova

Addition of three numbers is protected on figure. (Identical symbols indicate identical numbers.) The problem has a unique solution. Find him.
