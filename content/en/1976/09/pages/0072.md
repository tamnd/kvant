3. Body V.K.I. World without form. Translation from Bulgarian Volume 12 l., circulation 35,000 copies, price 62 kopecks.

This book is a guide to fluid dynamics. With the help of simple and accessible explanations, using a large number of examples from life, the author gives a figurative picture of this science, its development, achievements and problems. All basic aspects of hydrogasdynamics, popular and at the same time strictly distances numerous hydrogasdynamic problems are covered. A special section is devoted to issues of aegoridodynamics, where the author supports the priority of Russian aviation science and technology.

4. Brin k vort B.J., Solar energy for humans. Volume 16 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 01 k.

The book is devoted to the problem of using human solar energy. The growth of our planet's population and rapid technological progress require new sources of energy. Solar energy with its inexhaustible potential opens up enormous prospects for humans. The origin of solar energy, its interaction with the earth's atmosphere, the problems of converting solar energy into an electronic device, the services for these purposes, the prospects for the use of solar energy - these are the range of issues considered by the author of the book.
