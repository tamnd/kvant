⟦folio 59⟧

Memories of upcoming exams

The summer holidays are over, the new school year begins. It also begins in our “Applicant Workshop”. For many of you, this is the last academic year; in the spring you will have to answer: what did you learn at school, are you able to study at college? And therefore, this year you will not only have to complete the school course in mathematics and physics, but also repeat everything you have learned earlier in order to be “fully armed” by spring.
The article published below will not teach you how to solve this or that specific problem. But it will help you develop the right approach to any task, get out of a dead end if you find yourself in one, and pay attention to the most essential aspects of the task. That is why we are opening our new academic year.

It started a long time ago, back in 7th grade. When I came across difficult problems in planometry, and then in stereometry, I sometimes turned to my father for help, but at the same time, “real help” he offered:
- Let's decide "at speed"!
He sat at his table, and I sat at mine. Each one “pulled away” alone!
- Ready! - the father will cheerfully say, look at his watch and... hide his piece of paper in the table, and at this time I am still “digging” somewhere in the middle of the decision.
When I finish, we compare the answers and discuss the best solution method. It happened, however, to the husband: Mishka Tumanov is already on the hockey rink, but nothing works out for me, and the answer is thrown up from time to time -

gives me “leading questions.” One day I started a conversation:
- Dad! Why do you decide faster? Is my intuition working poorly?
- This is possible, but the main thing is that you decide haphazardly!
— ?!
- You waste a lot of time when you start thinking about a solution without having yet built a good, clear drawing with the data from the problem conditions marked on it. After all, without this, there is still no incentive for your billions of brain cells to begin joint, complex work, which is usually called “logical thinking.” Your brain, with all its convolutions, is the same computer, but a computer will never be able to work without ordered source information.
- Is that all? Is this the only thing I need to fix?
- No, of course not! There are many techniques to make your head work faster and more purposefully. All of them at once, please, and you won’t remember!
- What should we do?
- Let's do this! Here's a sheet of cardboard for you. Graph it into cells, measure them, and every time we compare and analyze our solutions, write briefly in these cells what helped us find a solution to the problem! In a year or two you will have good material, and then intuition will appear!...

- And according to what “commandment” will you perform your task today?
