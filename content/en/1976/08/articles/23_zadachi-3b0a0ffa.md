## Tasks

1. At figure the outline of the bulb is depicted, consisting of arcs of equal
circles. Cut it in two straight lines so that the resulting pieces
it was possible to fold a square. Is it possible to fold a square from the second figure in a similar way?
2. A group of 21 boys received 200 nuts. Prove that no matter how the guys divide these nuts, there will be two who will get equally
nuts (maybe not a single nut).
3. In 1815, the English physicist Children performed such an experiment. He connected two platinum wires of equal lengths but different diameters to Volta's battery. One time the wires were connected in series, and the other
- in parallel. In the first case, only a thin wire was heated, and in the second, only a thick one.
And for 25 years scientists could not explain the results of this experiment.
Can you?
Hint: assume that the amount of heat given off
conductor to the surrounding space, proportional to the surface area
conductor and the temperature difference between the conductor and the surrounding space.
4. Find all convex polygons with the following property:
the base of a perpendicular drawn from any point inside a polygon to any side lies inside that side.
