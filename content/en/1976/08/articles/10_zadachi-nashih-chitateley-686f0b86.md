Figures 3 and 4 show 2 countermeasures (the red circles mark the squares not covered by the knight).

In conclusion, we pose a number of tasks:

1. Is it true that the horse did not begin its movement from some field?

2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{xy^m}{m+1} = \frac{xyy \dots y}{m+1}$$
where $m$ is an integer.

3. Through the vertices $A, B$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ there are drawn lines intersecting $O$ at one point.

Let us denote $\alpha = \widehat{OAC}$, $\beta = \widehat{OBA}$, $\gamma = \widehat{OCB}$, and each of these angles will be considered positive if it contains the corresponding interior angle of the triangle or is itself contained in it, equal to zero if its sides coincide, and negative otherwise. Let $O$ be one of the six remarkable points of the triangle; $ABC$ be the inscribed or circumcenter, the orthocenter, or one of the three excentric centers. Prove that then $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
