Correspondence physics and mathematics schools

In recent years, in our country, distance learning of physics and mathematics for schoolchildren has taken on a large scale. Here is an incomplete list of correspondence schools: All-Union Correspondence Mathematics School (B3MSh), Correspondence Physics and Technology School at MIPT (3FTS), North-West 3MSH, 3MSH at Vilnius University, 3MSH at Minsk University, 3MSH at Kiev University, 3MSH at Novosibirsk University.

In December 1975, the first All-Union scientific and practical conference on the problems of correspondence mathematical education of schoolchildren was held in Moscow, organized by the Research Institute of Content and Teaching Methods (NII SiMO) of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences and V3MSH. The conference was timed to coincide with the tenth anniversary of the work of the eldest of the correspondence schools - V3MSH, in many ways it concerned experience and specifically V3MSH.

The spring conference was representative. 117 people from 37 cities took part in its work - teachers from universities, pedagogical and technical schools, researchers, secondary school teachers, representatives of the Ministry of Higher and Secondary Special Education of the USSR, representatives of the magazines “Mathematics at School”, «Kvant», “Pioneer”.

The correspondence school faces more challenges:

1) to bring to schoolchildren an interest in mathematics and its applications in various branches of knowledge;
2) increase the level of mathematical culture of students;
3) provide rural schoolchildren with the opportunity to study mathematics in depth and systematically;
4) improve the overall level of teaching mathematics and related academic subjects.

At the conference it was noted that V3MSh successfully solves these problems. Correspondence school students receive masterfully presented manuals that deepen and expand the materials of the school mathematics course. By completing tasks, children learn to navigate the world of books and work with scientific literature. Practice shows that these manuals can be used in school math clubs and summer math schools.

The best teaching aids, repeatedly tested in the work of V3MSH, created by a team of authors under the leadership of Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences I. M. Gelfand, are published en masse in the series “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” by the publishing house “Nauka”. Here are some of them: Gelfand I.M., Glagova E.G., Kirillov A.A. “Coordinate method”; Gelfand I. M., Glagova E. G., Shnol E. E. “Functions and graphs (basic techniques)”; Bashmakov M. organization of work. Enthusiasm has become a tradition at VZMSH. This is the pedagogical passion of about 500 mathematicians: teachers, graduate students, students, trying to convey to students a passion for any activity, and the passion of the students themselves for mathematics. Having completed two or three tasks, the students began to feel that back-end studies in the exact sciences were unthinkable without a fee, as well as without discipline and organizational work. The correct answer to a problem without an explanation of the progress of solving the problem in the VZMS, as a rule, is not read out. The quality of the explanation, its originality, rationality, completeness, clarity, the gradually developing mathematical culture of the language - this is what is most highly valued at VZMS.

All these factors lead to the fact that the number of students in correspondence schools is constantly growing. So, if in 1964 there were 1,300 students at VZMSH, now there are about 2,500 schoolchildren in one Moscow group.

The popularity of VZMS is given by the “Collective Student” groups - school mathematical circles that work on assignments from the All-World Mathematical School under the guidance of their mathematics teacher. There are now more than 700 such groups at VZMSH, with more than 10 thousand people studying in them. “Collective Student” groups, made up of students in grades 8-9, are admitted to the VZMSH without competition in September - October upon application from the mathematics teacher. In addition to the Moscow group, VZMSH unites 29 films at various universities and pedagogical colleges, where about 3,700 more schoolchildren study.

A representative of this school, L.V. Kovantsova, spoke about the work of the Kyiv Correspondence School. She noted the important role that television plays in the process of distance learning. Another form of correspondence education is, as the deputy editor-in-chief of “Kvaenta” M.L. Smolyansky spoke about it at the conference, the active use of materials in external work.
