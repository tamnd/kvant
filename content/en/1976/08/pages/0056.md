How to calculate the atomic nucleus at home
John Slate

Everything around us - beds, cigarette cases, rubber bags - is made of atoms (Fig. 1). Moreover, each of these atoms has a nucleus that deserves calculation no less than other nuclei. And yet, an ordinary person does not risk taking up this business without the help of the state. However, the nuclear calculation has long rightfully taken its place in our residential buildings.

Using the instructions below, anyone can build their own nuclear device. Atoms are everywhere, and they belong to everyone.

Selection of atoms
Looking around - at home or on the street - collect a few atoms. But only the most high-calorie ones will be useful. There is no hassle with cheap goods - that’s a fact. Therefore, it is better not to take bent or broken atoms.

Natural atoms are better in quality, but secured ones are also good. You just need to add water to them. Tree atoms are great for calculations. Take long-fiber wood, but not knotted wood, because these knots are simply impossible to untie if they are clamped in synchrotrain.

Parse atom
On figure 2 a schematic representation of an atom is given. The core is in the center. The particles inside the nucleus are nuclear particles. Everything else is just electrons, and before getting down to business, they certainly need to be put aside.

Whole atoms should be kept in chamber a (see Fig. 2), nuclei in the middle chamber, and electrons later. This arrangement will make it easier to immediately find what you need when you need it. It is useful to fill in the following: electrons are not ejected at all. They get caught later.

Figure 2 is made out of scale. Nuclei are always small whole atoms, so chamber b can be narrowed at your discretion.

Make sure you always have a supply of atoms on hand.

Build atom
Since the core is too small, you will fall into a nuclear trap. It is believed that the most effective trap is the atom itself. So take a nucleus and surround it with electrons. Place the finished atom in the atom clamp, as shown in figure 3. This will be an atom-integer (AT).

Make a synchrotron
After you have firmly secured the AC with the front part facing outwards, the core needs to be deciphered with something. Generally, it is “bombarded” with atomic particles. Since particles move randomly, they must be accelerated and directed in the desired direction - generally from left to right.
