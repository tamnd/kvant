To the article “Priitsip Ferma”
1. Derivation of the law of reflection (see Fig. 1).
When light propagates in a homogeneous medium, the path of the shortest time corresponds to the shortest path. Let light fall from point A to point B, reflecting from a mirror at point O. Let the light transmitted by it be equal to
$$l = AO + OB = \sqrt{a^2 + x^2} + \sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2}.$$
To find the minimum of the function t(x) we need to take the derivative of l(x) with respect to x and

$$\frac{x}{\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}} = \frac{c - x}{\sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2}};$$

2. Derivation of the law of refraction (see Fig. 2):
$$t = \frac{l_1}{v_1} + \frac{l_2}{v_2};$$
$$l_1 = \sqrt{a^2 + x^2};$$
$$l_2 = \sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2};$$
$$t = \frac{1}{v_1} \sqrt{a^2 + x^2} + \frac{1}{v_2} \sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2};$$
$$\frac{dt}{dx} = \frac{1}{v_1} \frac{2x}{2\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}} + \frac{1}{v_2} \frac{-2(c - x)}{2\sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2}} = 0;$$
$$\frac{1}{v_1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}} = \frac{1}{v_2} \frac{c - x}{\sqrt{b^2 + (c - x)^2}};$$
$$\sin \alpha = \sin \beta,$$
or
$$\frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin \beta} = \frac{v_1}{v_2}.$$

To the article “Experiments with powder figures”
1. When you hold the leaves of electronic paper with the finger of one hand and run the toothpaste on the paper with the finger of the other, the potential of your body is equal to the potential of the electronic paper. Therefore, the potential difference between the two fingers and the sheet of electron paper is zero, and powder figures are not formed.
2. For the formation of powder figures, a variable potential difference between the finger and the electron paper is required. If there was no current in the circuit at all, and there was only this potential difference in the barrel, then the piston figures would all be equal. That is why currents in the phase conductor of the network can be detected using powder figures.
