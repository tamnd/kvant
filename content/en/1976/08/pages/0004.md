⟦folio 2⟧

In this issue we celebrate the anniversary of the greatest mathematician of the 17th century, Pierre Fermat. Fermat contributed to almost every branch of mathematics in the 17th century. His works predated the advent of infinitesimal calculus and probability theory. Fermat can deservedly be considered one of the creators of analytical geometry. But his work in number theory is especially important.

Pierre Fermat is responsible for three articles in this issue. The life, scientific interests and discoveries of Fermat are described in the article by I. Bashmakova. In the article by B. Martynov, Fermat’s analysis polynomials are formulated and proven. Finally, in the article by L. Turiyansky he talks about the only work of Fermat related to a physical problem - the law of refraction of light. And Fermat approached this problem from a purely mathematical point of view: in it he applied his method of solving extremal problems. In essence, this was the first application in physics of that branch of mathematics that is now called calculus of variations.
