TIGERS IN A CATTLE

A square box measuring 5x5 contains 24 1x1 ingots (one ingot is free). Watchmen are painted on four of the lithics, tigers on another four, and pieces of lattice on sixteen.

In the initial position, the tigers are in the cage, the guards are outside the cage (the empty space is in the center of the box). By moving the castings inside the box, you can release the tigers and hide the guards from them in the cage. How to do this? What is the minimum number of ingot movements required for this? Is it possible to give one watchman “to be eaten by tigers”?

L. Mochalov
