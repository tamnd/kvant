Blend (p. 16, 27, 29, 35, 55)

We received a letter from our reader from Crimea, five-year-old Sveta, with a copy of figure, which you see on the first page of the cover. She and her friends drew such a figure during a math class. Based on the sketch sent by Sveta, a program was compiled for a machine that could “knack” for drawing. Made by machine figure and shown on the cover. Although the figure consists only of straight line segments, eight wing lines are clearly visible on figure. Will you be able to determine what these closed ones are? However, we warn you that this task is quite difficult.
