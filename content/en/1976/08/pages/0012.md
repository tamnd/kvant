Fermat's Little Theorem

In a letter to Frenicle de Bessy dated October 18, 1640, Fermat made the following statements: if the number $a$ is not divisible by the prime number $p$, then there is an exponent $\lambda$ such that $a^{\lambda} - 1$ is divisible by $p$, with $\lambda$ being a divisor of the number $p - 1$. In particular, $a^{p-1} - 1$ is always divisible by $p$. This statement is called Fermat's little theorem. It is fundamental in all elementary number theory.
