Tasks

1. figure shows the outline of a flask consisting of two circles. Cut it in two straight lines so that the resulting pieces can be folded into a square. Is it possible to fold a square from the second figure in a similar way?

2. A group of 21 children received 200 nuts. Prove that no matter how the guys divide these nuts, there will be more that will get the blame for the nuts (maybe not a single nut).

3. In 1815, the English physicist Children performed such an experiment. He connected two platinum wires of the same length but different diameters to Volt's battery. One time the wires were connected in series, and the other time in parallel. In the first case, only a thin wire was heated, and in the second, only a thick one.

And for 25 years, studies could not determine the results of this experiment. Can you?

Instruction: assume that the amount of heat given off by the conductor to the surrounding space is proportional to the surface area of ​​the conductor and the temperature difference between the conductor and the surrounding space.

4. Find all convex polygons that have the following property: the base of a perpendicular drawn from any point inside the polygon to any side lies inside that side.
