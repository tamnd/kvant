To the article “Problems for proof”
(cm. «Kvant» № 7)

1. a) According to the rule for subtracting vectors $\vec{CD} = \vec{OD} - \vec{OC}$. If points $O$ and $D$ do not coincide, then points $A, O, B$ and $D$ are the vertices of the rhombus. In both cases $\vec{OD} = \vec{OA} + \vec{OB}$. Therefore, $\vec{CD} = \vec{OA} + \vec{OB} - \vec{OC}$.

To calculate $|CD|$ we square this equality. Let us find the scalar product of the vectors $\vec{OA}$ and $\vec{OB}$. We have $\vec{AB} = \vec{OB} - \vec{OA}$, i.e. $|AB|^2 = |OA|^2 + |OB|^2 - 2\vec{OA} \cdot \vec{OB}$, whence $2\vec{OA} \cdot \vec{OB} = 2R^2 - c^2$.

Likewise

$$2\vec{OB} \cdot \vec{OC} = 2R^2 - a^2,$$

$$2\vec{OC} \cdot \vec{OA} = 2R^2 - b^2.$$

Then we get

$$|CD|^2 = 3R^2 + (2R^2 - c^2) - (2R^2 - a^2) - (2R^2 - b^2) = R^2 + a^2 + b^2 - c^2.$$

6) From a) it follows that

$$R^2 + a^2 + b^2 - c^2 \ge 0.$$

Using the formulas $a = 2R \sin \hat{A}$ and cos $2\hat{A} = 1 - 2\sin^2 \hat{A}$, we obtain the required inequality.

Equality holds if and only if $|CD| = 0$, i.e. when $\hat{A} = \hat{B} = 30^\circ$ and $\hat{C} = 120^\circ$.

2. a) Prove that

$$\overrightarrow{OG} = \frac{1}{3} (\overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{OB} + \overrightarrow{OC}).$$

3. a) Note. $\vec{DM} = \vec{OA} + \vec{OB} + \vec{OC} - \vec{OD}$. Having calculated the scalar square of the vector $DM$, you get the formula

$$|DM|^2 = 4R^2 + a^2 + b^2 + c^2 - (a_1^2 + b_1^2 + c_1^2),$$

where $a = |DA|$, $b = |DB|$, $c = |DC|$, $a_1 = |BC|$, $b_1 = |CA|$, $c_1 = |AB|$, $R = |OA|$.

4. Note. Prove that

$$\overrightarrow{OG} = \frac{1}{4} (\overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{OB} + \overrightarrow{OC} + \overrightarrow{OD}).$$

Solutions to problems 2 and 4 can also be obtained using the well-known Lazrange formula (see «Kvant», 1975, No. 3, p. 41).

5. Note. Express the vectors included in the equality being proved in terms of the vectors $\vec{OA}, \vec{OB}, \vec{OC}$ and $\vec{OD}$, where $O$ is an arbitrary point.
