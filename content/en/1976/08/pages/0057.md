More information

The above instruction concerns the allocation of a single core. However, once you get inspired, you will want to distribute more and more. Therefore, the following follows: provide each core with a synchropon and place all of them almost parallel to each other. You will need some more wire.

Safety first

First and foremost, we need to find a way to protect ourselves from an uncontrolled decay reaction. First, decide which kernel to split, and finish splitting this particular kernel and no other. This means that you need to somehow mark the selected nuclei, say, red. Another way is to remove all other kernels from a given zone. Then you can scatter the remaining ones without noise or shouting.

Don't forget to smile

Finally, about the dangers of radioactivity. Further, radioactivity is not always taken into account.

This means that by accumulating atomic raw materials, you must protect yourself and your loved ones from gamma rays and other things that can be emitted from the bunker. So in addition to the popular collector's posters like "Work in Progress" and "Don't Forget to Smile," why not include one that says, "Keep Away."

No accident

Don't forget that critical mass (CM) is also a safety issue. After you probably don’t set yourself the goal of an accident, you should try to take control of the factors leading to the accident. One of them is KM. Therefore, your structure should be designed only for critical mass. As you go along, you will soon gain the necessary experience.
