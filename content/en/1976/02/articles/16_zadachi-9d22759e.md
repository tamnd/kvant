## Tasks

1. And Kashchei said to Ivan Tsarevich: “You may live until tomorrow morning. In the morning
When you appear before my eyes, I will think of three numbers - $a$, $b$, $c$. Tell me three numbers - $x$, $y$, $z$. I’ll listen to you and tell you what $ax+by+cz$ is equal to. Then
Guess which $a$, $b$, $c$ I have in mind. If you don’t guess, head off your shoulders
Down with".
Ivan Tsarevich became sad and went to think. We should help him.
2. A ball with a diameter of
4 cm. Can you get the ball without turning the container over?
3. In the morning, the laboratory assistant weighed an open vessel with freshly boiled oil on a particularly precise scale. At the end of the day, when the oil had cooled, she weighed
vessel again. The weighing result was different. Why?
4. Lucy moved to a new eight-story house. It has two entrances and four apartments on each floor. When in the yard the guys asked Lucy what
she lives in an apartment, she said: “And you guess. But I will answer all questions
answer “yes” or “no”.
One boy said: “I will ask if it’s true that you live in the 1st
apartment, in the 2nd, $\ldots$, in the 63rd? I'll need sixty at most
three questions."
“And fourteen is enough for me! - the smallest one shouted. “I can find out the floor in seven questions, and the apartment in another seven.”
How many questions could you ask to find out Lucina’s apartment number?
