⟦folio 45⟧

Puzzles
Sam Loyd

"Croquet"
The young lady you see here was wondering how to get the ball through all the turns from A to Z in the fewest number of straight strokes.
Hints on what to do.

"The Fox and the Goose"
In how many moves can you swap the places of geese and foxes? In the final position, the geese should be on squares 3 and 4, and the foxes on squares 5 and 6.
Start moving the figures with the fox. Each figure moves to an adjacent field (circle) in one move.
