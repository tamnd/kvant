On February 24, 1826, at a meeting of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics of Kazan University, N.I. Lobachevsky made a report in which it was shown that the significant fifth postulate of Euclid is unprovable. A problem that had defied the efforts of mathematicians for two thousand years was solved in a completely unexpected way!
N.I. Lobachevsky and his substitutive discoveries are described in articles in this and the next issue of our magazine.
