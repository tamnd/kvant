Lobachevsky

The quoted words of Lobachevsky, as well as those taken as the epigraph to this article, are from his speech “On the Most Important Subjects of Education,” delivered on July 5, 1828, and which is programmatic for all of Lobachevsky’s activities as an educator of youth. There is no doubt that it was the educator of youth that Lobachevsky first of all felt himself to be in the variety of his rector’s duties. All other aspects of his organization of activity constituted only a frame for the two main aspirations of his life: creative scientific activity and educational activity. This latter was perceived by him with exceptional breadth and covered all aspects of the young man’s formative personality, from physical development to scientific specialization. Problems of education are of interest to Lobachevsky in all their scope and in the most ardent way. Since 1818, he has been a member of the School Committee, which led secondary and lower schools, and since then has not lost sight of issues of university education and all aspects of his own school teaching. In particular, Lobachevsky, who constantly chaired the commission for admission examinations to the university, knew very well with what knowledge a schoolchild of that time came to the end of his higher education.

Lobachevsky himself has the full diversity and wide life of interests included in his ideal of the harmonious development of a person's personality. And he demanded a lot from the young man who came to the university to study: he first of all demanded from him that he be a citizen who “with high knowledge forms part and glory of his fatherland.” He emphasizes that “mental education alone does not complete education.”

Let us note, by the way, that Lobachevsky paid attention to the physical development of youth, in particular, he introduced the introduction of gymnastics in high school.

Lobachevsky can serve as an example, probably, of the greatest person nominated by our almost two hundred years of university life. And even if he did not write a single line of independent scientific research, we should remember him as one of our most significant university activities. But the fact of the matter is that Lobachevsky, in addition, was also a brilliant scientist.
