Problem solutions

M326—M329; F339—F342

Let us introduce the following notation (Fig. 1): $O$ is the center of the circle, $|AB| = a$ is the length of the side of the large square, $|A'B'| = a'$ is the length of the side of the smaller square, $(OH) \perp (BB')$. Points $A$, $H$ and $A'$ lie on the same line. Let's drop the peredicle from point $O$ to $AA'$: $(OK) \perp (AA')$. Let

$$k = \frac{a}{|AH|} = \frac{a'}{|A'H|} = \frac{|KH|}{h}.$$

Then

$$a - a' = k(|AH| - |A'H|) = 2k|KH| = 2k^2h.$$

From $\triangle ABH$:

$$|AB|^2 + |BH|^2 = |AH|^2,$$

i.e.

$$|AB|^2 + \left(\frac{|AB|}{2}\right)^2 = |AH|^2,$$

or

$$k^2 + \left(\frac{k}{2}\right)^2 = 1,$$

where

$$k^2 = \frac{4}{5},$$

and therefore $a - a' = \frac{8}{5}h.$

N. Vasiliev
