Let's move on to statement $2^\circ$ (we believe that the spider is allowed to marvel at the entire volume of the tetrahedron). It follows directly from the following lemma:

If at time $t$ the fly is not understood and is at the vertex of the metrahedron, then in time $1$ it can crawl to other vertices of the metrahedron (i.e., in the time interval from $t$ to $t + 1$ the spiders will not be able to understand the fly).

Let's prove this lemma. Let muxa be at vertex $A$. Let's connect the points $\Pi_1$ and $\Pi_2$, where the spiders sit, with this vertex; we obtain the segments $\Pi_1A$ and $\Pi_2A$. Let us prove that there is an edge of the tetrahedron emanating from the point $A$, which forms with each of the segments $\Pi_1A$ and $\Pi_2A$ angles that do not change $30^\circ$. Indeed, if there were no such edge, then one of the segments, for example, the segment $\Pi_1A$, would form an angle less than $30^\circ$ with at least two edges emanating from $A$, for example, with $AB$ and with $AC$. But then for the plane angles of the three-edge angle $ABC\Pi_1$ it would be $\widehat{BAC} = 60^\circ$, $\widehat{BA\Pi_1} < 30^\circ$, $\widehat{CA\Pi_1} < 30^\circ$, which is impossible (should be $\widehat{BA\Pi_1} + \widehat{CA\Pi_1} \geqslant \widehat{BAC}$).

To make it clear, the muxa must move with maximum speed along the edge that forms smiles with the segments $\Pi_1A$ and $\Pi_2A$ that do not change $30^\circ$. After a unit of time, it will complete the other end of this edge. Let us prove that on the selected edge spiders will not be able to understand the fly. Indeed, let some spider, for example $\Pi_1$, appear a fly at time $t + \Delta t$ at point $P$ (belonging to the selected edge; see figure 4), then

$$|AP| = \Delta t \cdot v_{M_{\max}} = \Delta t$$
$$(v_{M_{\max}} = 1),$$
$$|\Pi_1P| \leqslant \Delta t \cdot v_{\pi_{\max}} < 1/2 \Delta t = 1/2 |AP|$$
$$(v_{\pi_{\max}} < 1/2 v_{M_{\max}}).$$

Let's lower the perpendicular $PQ$ to $\Pi_1A$; we get

$$|\Pi_1P| \geqslant |PQ| = |AP| \cdot \sin(\widehat{AP}) \geqslant \frac{1}{2} |AP|,$$

since $\Pi_1AP \geqslant 30^\circ$ due to the choice of edge.

So, on the one hand $|\Pi_1P| \geqslant \frac{1}{2} |AP|$, and on the other $|\Pi_1P| < \frac{1}{2} |AP|$ is a contradiction; therefore, spiders will not be able to understand the fly in the time interval from $t$ to $t + 1$, and it will fly to another vertex of the tetrahedron. The lemma, and therefore the statement $2^\circ$, is proven.
