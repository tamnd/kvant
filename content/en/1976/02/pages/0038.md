Problem
Kwana
Tasks
M366—M370; F378—F382

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than April 1, 1976 to the address: 113035, Moscow, Zh-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, magazine «Kvant». After the address on the envelope, write the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: “Kwana problem, M366, M367” or “... F378”. Solutions to problems in each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems, should be sent in separate envelopes. Please also send problems from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of your decisions in this envelope). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope put: “Kwana problem”, a new problem in physics or “... a new problem in mathematics”). After creating a task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk.

M366. Is it possible to arrange several triangles on a plane so that two vertices of each of them lie on the sides (but not at the vertices) of other triangles?

V. Kolosov

M367. Can the product of a) three; b) four consecutive natural numbers equal some power of some natural number (square, cube, etc.)?

D. Fleishman, teacher 10th grade

M368. Prove that the intersection of three right circular cylinders of radius 1, whose axes are pairwise perpendicular to each other (but not necessarily intersecting), are contained in a certain ball of radius $\sqrt{3/2}$.

S. Fomin

M369*. Given an acute triangle ABC. H is the point of intersection of its heights, γ is a circle with center H lying inside this triangle. Construct a triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ circumscribed about the circle γ and inscribed in triangle ABC (so that

$$A_1 \in [BC], B_1 \in [AC], C_1 \in [AB].$$

D. Izaak

M370*. Let a, b, c be a triple of positive numbers. Let’s form a new track from it:

$$|a-b|, |b-c|, |c-a|,$$

then from this triad, according to the same rule, the next one, etc. Is it necessary to meet 0 among the numbers obtained in this way if the initial numbers are a) integers; b) are they valid?

F378. Airplane passengers did not experience any nuisance unless their weight more than doubled in flight. What is the maximum acceleration in level flight allowed by this condition?

V. Smirnitsky
