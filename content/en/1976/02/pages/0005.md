⟦folio 3⟧

The revolution rises to a new, qualitatively higher level of production, energy, materials and management.

Social society opens up wide scope for new productive forces emerging in the course of the scientific and technological revolution. But the full development of these replacement opportunities requires persistent creative work of all workers. The task of historical importance - to organizationally combine the achievements of the scientific and technological revolution with the advantages of the socialist economic system, set by the General Secretary of our Party of Goods L. I. Brezhnev in the report to the XXIV Congress of the CPSU, still remains one of the main tasks that we will encounter on the path to communism.

The problem of a sharp increase in product quality and labor efficiency can be successfully solved only with the help of modern science. Physics and mathematics should make a major contribution to its solution. They are still widely used in determining product quality. Modern methods of mass product quality control are based on probability theory and mathematical statistics. Achievements in optics, RENT ray physics, ultrasound physics, magnetism, and nuclear physics are the scientific basis for a variety of control and measuring equipment. Optical and RENT spectral analysis, magnetic and ultrasonic flaw detection, gamma radiation from radioactive sources have long been successfully used in production to measure product thickness and detect hidden defects.

Modern physics provides industry with fundamentally new methods of influencing materials. Concentrated beams of fast electrons, ions, a laser beam, powerful neutron fluxes created by atomic reactors, ultra-strong magnetic fields and ultra-high pressures make it possible to influence the entire thickness of the product, simplifying the crystal lattice and giving the product incredible strength, wear resistance, reliability and durability. Physics opens the way to new technological processes and methods of production, changing the thickness of a product and detecting hidden defects.

Let's take as an example the creation of nuclear engines for maritime transport - icebreakers, submarines. The Soviet nuclear-powered icebreakers "Lenin" and "Arktika" conduct caravans of ships in winter in such high Arctic seas that are completely insufficient for conventional icebreakers at this time of year. Soviet nuclear submarines successfully circumnavigate the world without ever surfacing the ocean surface throughout their entire journey.

Thanks to the outstanding achievements of radio electronics and reactor technology, the possibility of creating new mechanisms for a radio relay and television communication system has opened up, which makes it possible to transmit television programs of any state to all other countries of the world. To implement it, it will be necessary to launch several synchronous (formed around the Earth at the same speed with which the Earth rotates around its axis) artificial Earth satellites in the equatorial regions, equipped with powerful relay devices. This will create a qualitatively new system of information transmission.

Nuka reveals to a person great prospects for the development and improvement of all types of labor. And these possibilities can be realized most fully only under socialism.
