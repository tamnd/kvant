To the article “A simple answer to a “complex” problem”
1. The point $E$ is such that $|BE| = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2}$. Point and consider equilateral triangle $DCE$ and then right triangle $CBE$.
2. Indicate and consider the point of intersection of the segments $EK$ and $FL$ with the vertices $A, B, C, D$; consider the areas of the resulting eight triangles.
3. $h = a \cdot \sqrt{\frac{b}{a + b}}$. Point and consider the parallel translation of diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ so that point $C$ of diagonals $AC$ coincides with point $B$, and point $B$ of diagonals $BD$ coincides with point $C$. In the resulting right triangles $EBM$ and $MCF$, express the sum of the lengths of the projections of the legs $BM$ and $CM$ onto the hypotenuses $EM$ and $MF$ through the desired height $h$ and sides $a$ and $b$.
4. $|AC| = \sqrt{ab}$. Point out and consider the similarity of triangles $ABC$ and $ADC$, from which $\frac{|AC|}{|AB|} = \frac{|CD|}{|AC|}$.
5. $|BC| = a \cdot \sqrt{1 + \frac{r}{R}}$. Pointing and considering extend the chord $BA$ of the larger circle $O$ until it intersects the smaller circle $O_1$ at point $D$, consider similar triangles $AOB$ and $AO_1D$ and find the length of the chord $AD$. Then in circle $O_1$ use the correspondence between tangent $BC$ and secant $BD$.
6. $|BM| = R \cdot \cos \left( \frac{24}{25} \right) \times \left[ 1 - \cos \left( \frac{1}{2} \arcsin \frac{24}{25} \right) \right] = \frac{5}{24} R$.
Note. Construct a right triangle $EFK$, where $E$ is the point of tangency of the given circle with sides $AB, EF$ lies on the radius $EO$ of this circle and is the height of the parallelogram $ABML$; $EK$ is the side of the parallelogram $EBMK$. Show that in this triangle the acute angle $\widehat{EKF} = \arcsin \frac{24}{25}$, the leg $EF$ is equal to $R - R \cos \left( \frac{1}{2} \arcsin \frac{24}{25} \right) = \frac{R}{5}$, having previously proved that $\widehat{MOE} = \frac{1}{2} \arcsin \frac{24}{25}$.
7. $|DM| = \sqrt{|AM|^2 + |BM|^2 - |CM|^2} = 7$. Note. Construct a circle concentric to the given one, passing through the point $M$. Taking point $N$, diametrically opposite point $M$ on this circle, consider parallelograms $AMBN$ and CMDN.
8. $\widehat{AMK} = \arctg (\cos \alpha)$. Note. Draw the chord $CM$, drop perpendiculars $KD$ to $AM$ and $KE$ to $CM$, and consider right triangles $KDM$ and $KEC$ with two congruent sides: $|MD| = |KE|$ and $|KD| = |KC|$.
