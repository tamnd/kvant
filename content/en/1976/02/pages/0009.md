On March 26, 1814, Lobachevsky was promoted to adjunct of pure mathematics; The 1814-1815 academic year is Lobachevsky’s first year of university teaching; He teaches a course on number theory on Gauss and Legendre at the university. (Lobachevsky also read number theory “according to Gauss” in the 1815-1816 academic year.) On July 7, 1816, Lobachevsky was confirmed as an extraordinary professor at Kazan University. In the 1816-1817 academic year, he taught a course in elementary algebra, geometry and trigonometry (plane and spheri) at the university - “from his own notebooks,” that is, without relying on any specific literary source; in 1817-1818 he read a course in differential and integral calculus according to Monge and Lacroix. The lectures on geometry given by Lobachevsky in the 1816-1817 academic year are of particular interest to us, since in these lectures he apparently for the first time came close to the question, the solution of which was the glory of his entire life - the question of the axiom of parallels. True, this first contact with the main subject of all his theoretical activity was still completely traditional - Lobachevsky, as well as his numerous ideas in the field of non-Euclidean geometry, began with attempts to prove the signs of Euclid’s axiom. Now, grateful to Lobachevsky’s further work, we know that these attempts could not lead to a satisfactory result. But such a beginning was, of course, not only natural, but also the only one possible both from a historical and psychological point of view. In any case, we must assume that it was at this time, when Lobachevsky was about 24 years old, that his first geometric works arose.

Lobachevsky's youth is over. A period of full disclosure of a rich and diverse personality began in all ways of interaction with the world around her: scientific creativity began, exceptional in its abstract mathematical power, his multifaceted educational work began and quickly developed, using the unyielding energy and passion for the work of the professor - soon in all respects the first professor of Kazan University, his ardent participation in all aspects of the work, organization and construction of the university began, which then turned into a twenty-year full and sole leadership of all university life.

II.

As already mentioned, Lobachevsky has been a professor at Kazan University since 1816; two years later, his administrative and organizational activities began, various forms of which accompanied him further during the three decades of his life: in August 1818, Lobachevsky was appointed a member of the school committee. During these years, dark clouds were gathering over Kazan University, and over the entire Russian education in general; The reactionary aspirations of the last decade of the reign of Alexander I were heard every year and became more and more oppressive. The coming era in the history of Russian culture is known as the “Magnitsky era”, named after the trustee of the Kazan educational district, famous for his obscurity.
