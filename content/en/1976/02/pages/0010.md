⟦folio 8⟧

Anatomical Theater of Kazan University. Arch. M. P. Korinfsky, 1834 - 1842 Photo from the 1910s.

It’s time to take your first steps in the field of organizational university activities. Meanwhile, as we will now see, his work in this field is developing rapidly: in December 1819, Lobachevsky, together with Professor Verderamo, received an assignment to put in order the university library, which was in an extremely neglected state. A month later, in January 1820, with Verderamo leaving the university, all the work of putting the library in order fell entirely on the shoulders of Lobachevsky. As with any work he undertakes, Lobachevsky responds to his difficult and tedious library duties with selfless conscientiousness, spending a lot of time and effort on their implementation. Nevertheless, the situation was such that in June 1821, he, “deceived by the reliable idea of ​​bringing the library into a new order,” asked to be released from work in the library.

At the end of 1820, Lobachevsky was given even more responsible responsibilities: on November 19, he was elected dean of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics. However, this activity of his ends in the summer of 1821, approximately simultaneously with his library activities.

Lobachevsky's professorial activity in these years received significant new content: he was entrusted with the departments of physics and astronomy. In this regard, Lobachevsky did not teach mathematics for two academic years, 1819-1820 and 1820-1821, devoting himself entirely to teaching physics and science.
