On February 25, 1822, Lobachevsky was elected professor, and in the spring of the following 1823, the duties of dean fell on him for the second time.

However, even a year earlier - in the spring of 1822 - Lobachevsky found himself faced with new, complex and tedious responsibilities that had nothing to do with his scientific activities: a construction committee was being formed to build new and put in order old university buildings, of which Lobachevsky was a member.

As always, Lobachevsky approaches his new responsibilities not only with conscientiousness and service, but also with great passion. Three years later, in 1825, Lobachevsky was already the chairman of the construction committee. Having specially studied both the engineering technology of construction and architecture itself, Lobachevsky largely became the manager of construction work, both technically and artistically. Many of the most architecturally successful buildings on the territory of Kazan University are the implementation of Lobachevsky’s construction plans. These include an anatomical theater, a library, and an astronomical observatory.
