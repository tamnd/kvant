At the Faculty of Mathematics, students study mathematical analysis, theory of functions of a real variable, complex variable, calculating algebra, analytical geometry, other branches of geometry, probability theory, programming for electronic computers, a course in physics, astronomy.

Future physicists study theoretical mechanics, other branches of theoretical physics, electrical engineering, acoustics, astronomy, as well as special branches of mathematics: mathematical analysis, analytical geometry. The Physics Department has separate foreign language physics majors; graduates of this department can teach physics not only in their native language, but also in a foreign language (English).

The institute has many laboratories with modern equipment: electrical engineering, radio engineering, molecular acoustics, etc. There is a laboratory in which students are engaged in programming and working on the BESM-4 computer. Within the framework of the student scientific society (HCO), students conduct scientific research, to one degree or another, independently. For those who have performed well in this regard, there is a postgraduate course.

Graduates of the institute work as secondary school teachers, and postgraduate students also teach in higher education. Graduates of the department of physics in a foreign language work abroad, in schools in developing countries, as well as in Soviet schools with an increased volume of teaching a foreign language.

MOPI has a preparatory department with a training period of 1 year.

Faculty of Mathematics

Option 1

1. At the base of the parallelepiped there is a rhombus $ABCD$ with an angle $60^\circ$ at the vertex $A$. The perpendicular to the plane of the base, restored at the vertex $A$, passes through the point of intersection of the diagonals of the other base. The lateral edge makes an angle $\alpha$ with the plane of the base. The side of the base is $\alpha$. Determine the volume of the parallelepiped.

2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{align*}
x^2 - 12xy + 13y^2 + 18x - 2y - 18 &= 0, \\
x + 4\sqrt{xy} + 3y &= 0.
\end{align*}$$

3. At the foot of the mountain, on opposite sides of the pass $C$, there are points $A$ and $B$, the distance between which along the road connecting them is 8 km. The pass is considered a point. The speed of a tractor on a descent is 1 km/h higher than its speed on an ascent. The time taken by the tractor to move from $A$ to $B$ is $t_1$, and the time taken to move back from $B$ to $A$ is $t_2$.

a) Determine the speed of the tractor on the descent if it is known that $t_1 = 5$ hour is 3 min 17 sec, and $t_2 = 6$ hour is 56 min 43 sec.

b) Assuming that the tractor takes a total of 12 hours to go there and back, he indicates the limits within which the parameter $t_1$ can be set so that the problem has a solution.

Option 2

The largest side face in terms of plane is inclined to the base plane at an angle $\alpha$. Determine the volume of the parallelepiped.

2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{align*}
3x + \sqrt{xy} - 14y &= 21, \\

3. Upon reaching the island, the river at point $A$ divides into two branches $ACB$ and $ADB$, and at point $B$ it converges again. The speed of the current in the outer sleeves is the same and equal to 3 km/h. To go around the island in the direction $ACBDA$, a motor boat spends time $t_1$, and to go around it in the direction $ADBCA$ - time $t_2$. One of the branches is 6 km longer than the other.

a) Determine the speed of the boat in still water, assuming $t_1 = 7$ hour, $t_2 = 7$ hour 10 min.

b) Considering $t_2 - t_1 = 10$ min, indicates the range of possible values ​​of the parameter $t_1$ for which the problem can have a solution.
