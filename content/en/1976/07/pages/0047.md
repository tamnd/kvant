Physics.

Oral exam in physics for construction at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State Pedagogical Institute named after. V.I. Lenin is carried out in strict accordance with the physics program.

The exam ticket consists of two questions and one task. Each of the tickets is designed in such a way as to cover the possibility of testing a larger number of sections of physics. For example, here is the text of one of the tickets:

1. The concept of abscilottic zero. Abscilot temperature scale. Unified Boyle-Marint-Gay-Lusac law.
2. Luminous flux. The power of light. Illumination.
3. Exercise. A copper ball with an internal cavity weighs 2.64 n in air and 2.21 n in water. Determine the volume of the internal cavity of the ball. Take the density of copper equal to 8.8 e/cm³.
3. A flat body with a mass of 4 kg moves along a circular iron located in a vertical plane (see figure). Determine the pressure force of the body on the iron at point $B$, if it is released at zero speed from point $A$.
4. A body weighing 2 kg slides along an inclined plane 18 m long, forming an angle of 30° with the horizon. What amount of heat is released when a body rubs against a plane if the initial speed of the body is zero and at the base is 6 m/sec?
5. A cylinder with a capacity of 36 liters contains oxygen under a pressure of 20 atm at a temperature of 20 °C. What volume does this gas occupy under normal conditions?

6. Find e.d. With. and the internal resistance of the battery, if at a current of 5 A the power consumed in the external circuit is 12.5 W, and at a current of 8 A it is equal to 16.8 W.

7. What current does an electric boiler with a capacity of 8 liters consume if, with an efficiency of 80%, the water in it is heated from 20 °C to 100 °C in 25 minutes? Mains voltage 220 V.

8. What amount of aluminum will be released at the cathode in 6 hours during the electrolysis of Al₂(SO₄)₃ when a current of 1.5 A is passed through the electrolyte? The atomic weight of aluminum is 27.

9. The receiving circuit consists of a coil with inductance $2 \cdot 10^{-6}$ en and a capacitor with a capacity of 1800 nf. What wavelength is the circuit designed for?

10. The screen is located at a distance of 2.45 m from the coin line. The focal length of the line is 25 cm. At what distance from the line should an object be placed in order to get a clear image on the screen?

G. Shadrin, D. Moore

Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute
them. N. K. Krupskaya

The Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute was founded in 1931 by the incision of N.K. Krupskaya, and in 1958 it was named after her.

The institute has many faculties, we will talk about mathematics and physics. Both faculties prepare mainly teachers for work in secondary schools.

A modern teacher must have a very high level of training, and new curricula provide for the preparation of highly qualified specialties. A significant place in the programs is occupied by courses in pedagogy, psychology, natural sciences, as well as methods of teaching mathematics.
