3. a) $2 \text{km/час};$ b) $4 \text{час} < t < 8 \text{час}$. Note. Let us put $AC = s$, the speed of the tractor on the descent will be denoted by $v$; then the speed on the rise is $v - 1$. According to the condition

$$\begin{cases} \frac{s}{v - 1} + \frac{8 - s}{v} = t_1, \\ \frac{8 - s}{v - 1} + \frac{s}{v} = t_2, \end{cases}$$

whence $(t_1 + t_2)v^2 - (t_1 + t_2 + 16)v + 8 = 0$,

$v = 2 (\text{km/час}).$ Now $t_1 = 4 + \frac{s}{2}$ and $4 < t_1 < 8$ since $0 < s < 8$.

$$\begin{cases} \frac{s + 6}{v + 3} + \frac{s}{v - 3} = t_1, \\ \frac{s}{v + 3} + \frac{s + 6}{v - 3} = t_2. \end{cases}$$
