⟦folio 52⟧

New books

In this issue of the magazine we place brief annotations on books on mathematics and physics published in the second quarter of 1976 that are of interest to our readers.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"
1. Alekseev V.V. Abel’s theorem in problems and solutions. Volume 10 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 33 kopecks.
From this book you will learn how to solve algebraic equations of the 3rd and 4th degree with one unknown, why there are no general formulas (in radicals) for solving an equation of a higher degree. However, one of the main purposes of this book is to enable the reader to try his hand at mathematics. For this purpose, almost all of the material presented is presented in the form of specific examples and a large number of problems, accompanied by instructions and solutions.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers interested in server mathematics (starting with high school students), and does not assume any special knowledge presented. It can also be used in the work of a mathematical circle.

2. Lyubich Yu. I., Shor L. A. Kinematic method in geometric problems. 2nd edition, revised. (Popular lectures on mathematics). Volume 3 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.

It turns out that the “theory of speed”—kinematics—can be useful for solving geometric problems. Sometimes connections between the values ​​of segments, angles, etc. in geometric funcs is more complex than the connections between the rates of change of these quantities during the deformation of the func. Therefore, when solving a geometric problem, it is useful to imagine what will happen to the elements of the funkur under consideration if some of its points learn to move; the dependence of some elements on others can become clearly evident, and the solution to the problem will literally catch your eye.

The brochure by Lubitsch and Shor demonstrates the application of kinematics to problems of elementary geometry with several examples and provides problems for independent solution. The necessary information from kinematics and vector algebra is preliminarily presented.

The book is intended for high school students.
3. Zaitsev V.V., Ryzhkov V.V., Skanavi M.I. Elementary mathematics. 3rd edition. Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 11 k.

The book contains a systematic presentation of the course in elementary mathematics and consists of two parts: 1) algebra and elementary functions and 2) geometry. It fully corresponds to the program of introductory courses in mathematics to higher education. In addition to theoretical material, the book contains a large number of problems, many of which are solved in the text.

This book may be useful for high school students preparing for course copies. In particular, it can be recommended as a teaching aid for preparatory departments of colleges.

4. Nikiforovsky V. A., Freiman L. S. The Birth of New Mathematics. Volume 12 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

The book tells about the work of four outstanding scientists of the 17th century - Descartes, Fermat, Torricelli and Roberval. These scientists developed the main innovations of mathematics, they participated in the creation of differential and integral calculus, which was finally formalized and completed later by Newton and Leibniz.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers interested in the history of mathematics.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
5. Kordemsky V. A. Mathematics studying randomness. Volume 7 l., circulation 120,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.

There are no elements of probability theory in school curricula. The selection of available school books on this subject is also not very extensive. Kordemsky's book is, apparently, the only book so far that captivatingly introduces the reader to probability theory, helping to independently master the initial concepts and methods of this science, as well as the simplest apparatus of mathematical statistics.

In the initial part of the book, a free form of presentation with the use of gaming material predominates; gradually the book becomes more “server-sided”, but does not lose its accessibility for schoolchildren.

Publishing house "Vishcha school" (Kyiv)
