⟦folio 44⟧

Mathematics

Option 1
1. A regular quadrangular prism is inscribed in a cylinder. The diagonal of the prism forms an angle $\alpha$ with the side face, the height of the prism is $h$. Find the lateral surface of the cylinder.
2. Solve the equation
$$|1 + \log \frac{1}{3}x| = 3 + |2 - \log \frac{1}{3}x|.$$
3. Find the domain of a function
$$y = \sqrt[4]{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{x^2 - 3x} - 2,5} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{\log_3 x - \log_2 x}}.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 2x - 4 \cos^4 x = \sin 4x.$$
Option 2
1. The base of a right prism is a right triangle with a circumscribed circle radius equal to $R$. Determine the area of ​​the section formed by the diagonals of the largest and smallest side faces and the side of the base, if the diagonals are inclined to the base at angles $\alpha$ and $3\alpha$.
2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{\frac{x}{y}} + \sqrt{\frac{y}{x}} = \frac{7}{\sqrt{xy}} + 1, \\ x \sqrt{xy} + y \sqrt{xy} = 78. \end{cases}$$
3. Solve inequality
$$\log_4 (2x^2 + x + 1) - \log_2 (2x - 1) \leq -tg \frac{7}{4}\pi.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{1 + \sin 2x} = \sqrt{2} \cos 3x,$$
if $\pi < x < \frac{3}{2}\pi$.

Physics
1. With the surface of a long mountain inclined at an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizon, a stone is thrown in the horizontal direction with a speed of $v_0 = 20$ m/sec. Determine the time $t$ of flight of the stone. Ignore air resistance.
2. The surface charge density of a uniformly charged infinite plane located vertically is $\sigma = 1,73 \cdot 10^{-4} \text{ к/м}^2$. A ball of mass $m = 1$ e hangs on a weightless thread in the field of this plane. The charge of the ball is $q = 3$ units. SGSE charge. What angle $\alpha$ does the thread form with the plane? Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0 = 8,85 \cdot 10^{-12} \text{ф/м}$.

3. In a uniform magnetic field with induction $B = 0,02 \text{ mл}$ there is a conductor of length $l = 10 \text{ cm}$ located perpendicular to the lines of magnetic induction. A current $I = 2 \text{ a}$ flows through the conductor, the value of which is maintained constant. Under the influence of field forces, the conductor moved a distance $s = 5 \text{ cm}$. Find a job $A$ field forces.
4. A luminous point is located on the main optical axis of a convex mirror with a radius of curvature $R = 72 \text{ cm}$. Distance from point to mirror $d = 150 \text{ cm}$. Find the distance $f$ from the image of this point to the mirror. Build an image.

Matematika
Option 1
1. In a truncated cone, the diagonals of the axial section are mutually perpendicular, and the generatrix makes an angle $\alpha$ with the plane of the lower base and is equal to 1. Determine the volume of the truncated cone.
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos 2x - 3 \cos x = 4 \cos^2 \frac{x}{2}.$$
3. Solve system of equations ($a > 0, b > 0$)
$$\begin{cases} \log_a x + \log_a y = 2, \\ \log_b x - \log_b y = 4. \end{cases}$$
4. Solve inequality
$$0 < \frac{x}{3 - x} < 1.$$
