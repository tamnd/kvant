⟦folio 49⟧

Petr Chistyakov

Ilya Gavrilov

The Moscow team to perform at the final (fifth) stage of the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad included

in mathematics:
Stepan Orevkov (n. 57, 8th grade), Mark Spivakovsky (n. 57, 9th grade), Yuri Burov (n. 2, 10th grade);

in physics:
Sergey Rylov (n. sh. 2, 8 classes), Petr Chistyakov (n. sh. 2, 9 classes), Sergey Yakovenko (n. sh. 179, 10 classes).

The Moscow city team to participate in the republikansky (fourth) stage of the olympiad included

in mathematics:
Victor Galperin (n. 57, 8th grade), Yuri Baryshnikov (n. 91, 9th grade), Yuri Burov (n. 2, 10th grade);

in physics:
Ilya Gavrilov (n. 19, 8th grade), Natalya Runova (n. 179, 9th grade), Alexander Semenov (n. 179, 10th grade).

You can judge what problems were at the Moscow Physics and Mathematics Olympiad from the “Quenta Problems” published in the previous issue of our magazine.
