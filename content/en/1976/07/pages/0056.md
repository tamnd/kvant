⟦folio 54⟧

Exercise

1. In some kingdoms, every two people are either friends or enemies. Every person can at some point quarrel with all his friends and make peace with all his enemies. It turned out that every three people could become friends in this way. Prove that then all people in the state can become friends.

2. For which prime numbers $p$ are the numbers $2p+1$ and $4p+1$ also prime?

3. Find the set of centers of triangles $OBA$ for which vertex $O$ is fixed, and vertices $A$ and $B$ lie on two circles of the same radius. What happens if the radii of the circles are not equal?

4. Prove that a plane can be colored with 9 colors so that no two points of the same color are at a distance of 1 m.

5. The steamer sails from city $A$ to city $B$ and back. Will the ship spend the same time if the city is located:
   a) on the river bank,
   b) on the shore of the lake?
   The speed of the ship relative to the water is constant.
