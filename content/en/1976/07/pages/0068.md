In this issue we continue the exhibition of machine creativity. This time on the cover there are geometric patterns obtained using the Algraf program at the institute, which deals with the experimental design of living buildings. Such programs help architects in their work.
On figure on the first page of the cover you see a projection onto a hemisphere of a cellular grid lying in the equatorial plane of the hemisphere. (Try to determine the center of projection.) Figure This machine performed using a program that performs a curvilinear coordinate transformation. We invite you to determine the principle of constructing the drawing on the fourth page of the cover yourself.
