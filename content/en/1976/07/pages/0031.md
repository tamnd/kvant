M395*. At the top of a regular $n$-gon with center at point $O$ the numbers (+1) and (-1) are placed. In one step, the cut changes the sign of all numbers located at the top of any regular $k$-gon with center $O$ (at the same time, we also allow 2-gons, taking under the 2-gon a segment with the midpoint at the point $O$). Prove that in case a), 6), c) there is an initial arrangement of (+1) and (-1) such that in no number of steps can a set of only (+1) be obtained from it:

a) $n=15$,
b) $n=30$,
b) $* n$ - any number greater than 2.
d) $*$ Try to find out for an arbitrary $n$ how many different arrangements (+1) and (-1) there are such that none of them can be obtained from any other in several steps. Prove, for example, that for $n=2100$ there are $2^{480}$ such arrangements. (10 grades)

S. Fomin

How will the charges of the capacitors and the current in the circuit change over time after the switch is closed? Propose a mechanical oscillating system similar to this electrical one. (10 grades)
