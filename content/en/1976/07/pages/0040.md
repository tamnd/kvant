In the practical activity of a person, one constantly has to solve various problems related to decision-making, and each time a person tries to choose the optimal, most profitable one from all possible solutions, often relying on common sense and his experience. Analysis of economic situations and decision-making constitute the main content of the work of production managers of all ranks, and increasing the scale of production. its success leads to increased responsibility for decisions made. This explains the need to create scientific methods for assessing decisions made and training personnel for organizing production at all levels.

In recent decades, a new field of applied mathematics—mathematical economics—has been rapidly developing. The need for specialization in the use of computers and economic-mathematical methods in managing the social economy is growing from year to year. The first in our country to calculate the value, specialized in training management personnel for various sectors of the national economy, is the Moscow Institute of Management. S. Ordzhonikidze. We will talk about one of the faculties of this institute - the Faculty of Economics Library (FEC).
