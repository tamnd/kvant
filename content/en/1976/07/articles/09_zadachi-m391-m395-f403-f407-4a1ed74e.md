Tasks

M391—M395; F403—F407

M391. a) In the sequence $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$, the numbers $x_0$ and $x_1$ are natural and less than 1000, and each of the remaining numbers is equal modulo to the difference of the two previous ones. Prove that one of the numbers $x_1, x_2, \dots x_{1500}$ is equal to 0. (10 keys)

6) In the sequence $x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots$, the numbers $x_0$ and $x_1$ are natural and less than 10000, and each of the numbers $x_2, x_3, \dots$ is equal to the smallest of the differences of the two previous numbers. Prove that $x_{20}=0$. (8th grade, 9th grade)

S. Fomin

M392. Three pedestrians are walking along those coastal roads at constant speeds. At the initial moment they were not on the same straight line. Prove that they can be on the same line no more than twice. (10 grades)

N. Vasiliev

M393. Find the amount

$$\varphi(0) + \varphi\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) + \varphi\left(\frac{2}{n}\right) + \dots + \varphi(1),$$

if $\varphi(x) = \frac{4^x}{4^x + 2}$.

M. Levin

M394. a) *. Vectors $\vec{a}, \vec{b}, \vec{c}, \vec{d}$ are given on the plane, the sum of which is 0. Prove the inequality

$$|\vec{a}| + |\vec{b}| + |\vec{c}| + |\vec{d}| \geqslant |\vec{a} + \vec{d}| + |\vec{b} + \vec{d}| + |\vec{c} + \vec{d}|.$$

Prove a similar inequality:

6) for four numbers and

b) * four vectors in three-dimensional space, the sum of which is 0. (9 cells, 10 cells)

M395*. At the top of a regular $n$-gon with center at point $O$ the numbers (+1) and (-1) are placed. In one step, the cut changes the sign of all numbers located at the top of any regular $k$-gon with center $O$ (at the same time, we also allow 2-gons, taking under the 2-gon a segment with the midpoint at the point $O$). Prove that in case a), 6), c) there is an initial arrangement of (+1) and (-1) such that in no number of steps can a set of only (+1) be obtained from it:

a) $n=15$,
b) $n=30$,
b) $* n$ - any number greater than 2.
d) $*$ Try to find out for an arbitrary $n$ how many different arrangements (+1) and (-1) there are such that none of them can be obtained from any other in several steps. Prove, for example, that for $n=2100$ there are $2^{480}$ such arrangements. (10 grades)

S. Fomin

F403. In an electrical circuit of two identical capacitors emkom $C$ and an inductor $L$ connected in series (Fig. 1), at the initial moment one of the capacitors has a charge $q_0$, the second is not charged.

How will the charges of the capacitors and the current in the circuit change over time after the switch is closed? Propose a mechanical oscillating system similar to this electrical one. (10 grades)

F404. Determine the efficiency of the reactor engine as a heat engine and its traction force. A rocket engine uses hydrogen as fuel and liquid oxygen as an oxidizer. Secondary hydrogen consumption 24 kcal/sec. The speed of gas flow from the reaction nozzle is $4,2 \cdot 10^3$ m/sec. The calorific value of hydrogen is $1,1 \cdot 10^8$ J/kk. (9 grades)

F405. A homogeneous thin-walled sphere of radius $R$ was cut into two parts and fastened together, as shown in figure 2. At what height is the center of gravity of the glass if the height of its stem is $h$? (8 grades)

F406. The satellite moves in a circular orbit at a distance from the Earth's surface equal to its radius $R$. At some point, a station is launched from a satellite to another planet, after which the rest of the satellite moves in an elliptical orbit touching the surface of the Earth at a point opposite to the station point.
