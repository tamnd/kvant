⟦folio 48⟧

Moscow city
Olympiad for schoolchildren

“As I congratulate you on your victory, I want to warn you that no one can determine how much you deserve the award like you yourself. The main thing that the assessment should be based on is not passion, unsatisfied requirements, but whether you have managed to fall in love with mathematics. Success will come. If you have taken up mathematics completely, then you will have more than once the opportunity for creative work and the discovery of your abilities. But never forget that, first of all, mathematics must be for you, and not for you...”
From the speech of the Chairman of the Journey Olympada in Mathematics, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Professor A.V. Arkhangelsky.

On a March Sunday morning to the buildings of Moscow University, the Pedagogical Institute. V.I. Lenin, Institute of Railway Transport Engineers and Physico-Technical Institute of Specialization. These are the sections of the Moscow City Olympiad, the third stage of the All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics and physics.

More than 9,000 young mathematicians and more than 3,500 young physicists took part in the previous regional stage.

Almost 2,000 schoolchildren took part in the city Olympiad in mathematics, and about 1,400 in physics. Now a little about the winning secondary students.

About 150 mathematicians and almost 80 physicists were awarded various diplomas and prizes, and some of them received the right to further participate in the All-Union Physics and Mathematics Olympiad.
