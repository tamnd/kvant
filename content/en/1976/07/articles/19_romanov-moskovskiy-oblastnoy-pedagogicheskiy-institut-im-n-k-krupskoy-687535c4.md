Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute
them. N. K. Krupskaya

The Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute was founded in 1931 by the incision of N.K. Krupskaya, and in 1958 it was named after her.

The institute has many faculties, we will talk about mathematics and physics. Both faculties prepare mainly teachers for work in secondary schools.

A modern teacher must have a very high level of training, and new curricula provide for the preparation of highly qualified specialties. A significant place in the programs is occupied by courses in pedagogy, psychology, natural sciences, as well as methods of teaching mathematics.

At the Faculty of Mathematics, students study mathematical analysis, theory of functions of a real variable, complex variable, calculating algebra, analytical geometry, other branches of geometry, probability theory, programming for electronic computers, a course in physics, astronomy.

Future physicists study theoretical mechanics, other branches of theoretical physics, electrical engineering, acoustics, astronomy, as well as special branches of mathematics: mathematical analysis, analytical geometry. The Physics Department has separate foreign language physics majors; graduates of this department can teach physics not only in their native language, but also in a foreign language (English).

The institute has many laboratories with modern equipment: electrical engineering, radio engineering, molecular acoustics, etc. There is a laboratory in which students are engaged in programming and working on the BESM-4 computer. Within the framework of the student scientific society (HCO), students conduct scientific research, to one degree or another, independently. For those who have performed well in this regard, there is a postgraduate course.

Graduates of the institute work as secondary school teachers, and postgraduate students also teach in higher education. Graduates of the department of physics in a foreign language work abroad, in schools in developing countries, as well as in Soviet schools with an increased volume of teaching a foreign language.

MOPI has a preparatory department with a training period of 1 year.
