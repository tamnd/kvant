## Tasks

1. In a certain kingdom, every two people are either friends or enemies. Every
a person can at some point quarrel with all his friends and make peace
with all enemies. It turned out that every three people can do this
become friends. Prove that then all people in the state can become
friends.
2. For which prime numbers $p$ are the numbers $2p+1$ and $4p-1$ also prime?
3. Find the set of centers of gravity of triangles $OBA$ for which
vertex $O$ is fixed, and vertices $A$ and $B$ lie on two circles
the same radius. What happens if the radii of the circles are not equal?
4. Prove that a plane can be colored with 9 colors so that no two points of the same color are located at a distance of 1 m.
5. The steamboat sails from city $A$ to city $B$ and back. Will the ship spend the same time if the cities are:
on the river bank,
on the shore of the lake?
Speed ​​of the ship relative to the water
is constant.
