Physics
1. An illuminated slit with a height of $h=5$ cm is projected using a collecting lens with a focal length of $F=10$ cm onto a screen located at a distance of $f=12$ cm from the lens. Find the size of the image of the slit on the screen.
2. Which is the most powerful installed at the end of a two-derivative line, having a resistance of $R=10$ ohm, if the current source develops a power of no more than $P=6$ kW at a voltage of $U=1000$ V?
3. In an electronic kettle with a power of 800 W, you can boil $V=1,5$ liters of water at a temperature of $t_1=20^\circ\text{C}$ in a time of $t=20$ minutes. Find the efficiency of the kettle.

Written examination in mathematics, 1975
Option 1
1. The basis of the parameters is a rhombus with an acute angle $\alpha$. The side faces are inclined to the base plane at an angle $\varphi$. Determine the volume of the parameter if the radius of the circle hanging into the rhombus is $r$.
2. Solve the equation
   $$\lg 2 + \lg (4x^2 + 9) = 1 + \lg (2x^2 + 1).$$
3. Solve the equation
   $$\sin 3x + \sin 2x = \sin x.$$
4. Solve inequality
   $$\frac{x^2 - 5x + 4}{x + 1} > 0.$$
5. Simplify an expression
   $$2 \left(x^2 + \sqrt{x^4 - 1}\right) \times \left[\sqrt[3]{(x^2 + 1) \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{x^2}}} + \sqrt[3]{(x^2 - 1) \sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{x^2}}}\right]^{-2}.$$
Option 2
1. The generatrix of the cone touches the ball inscribed in the cone along the parallel at $60^\circ$. Find the volume of the cone if the radius of the ball is 2.
2. Solve the equation
   $$4x^2 - 17 \cdot 2x^4 = 0.$$
3. Solve the equation
   $$\cos 4x = -2 \cos^2 x.$$
4. Solve inequality
   $$\lg (35 - x^3) / \lg (5 - x) > 0.$$
5. Simplify an expression
   $$\left[(a - b) \cdot \sqrt{\frac{a + b}{a - b}} + a - b\right] \times \left[(a - b) \cdot \left(\sqrt{\frac{a + b}{a - b}} - 1\right)\right].$$
G. Melemieva
