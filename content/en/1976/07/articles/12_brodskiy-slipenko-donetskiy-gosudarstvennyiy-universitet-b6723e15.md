⟦folio 37⟧

In 1965, a scientific center of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR was founded in the capital of the industrial Donbass, which included a number of research institutes and a university. Over the 10 years of its existence, Donts State University (DonSU) has become one of the largest universities in Ukraine. Currently, nine faculties of Donetsk State University prepare special higher qualifications for the national economy, scientific institutions, higher and secondary schools in the specialty: mathematics, applied mathematics, physics, technical cybernetics, chemistry, biology, economic cybernetics, biology, physiology of plants and animals, national economic planning, etc.

The largest department of DonSU is mathematics. It consists of 7 departments that train specialties in probability theory and mathematical statistics, theory of functions and functional analysis, differential equations, computational mathematics, control theory, theoretical and applied mechanics, and mathematical support for automated control systems.

An important place in the training of mathematicians is the acquisition of skills in working with computer technology. All graduates of the faculty are prepared to work on modern computers. This is facilitated by the good mathematical base of the university: the computer center of DonSU is the largest in Ukraine.

About 75% of the faculty’s graduates are sent to work in scientific institutions, computer centers, and

Tiya, the rest are teachers of secondary schools, vocational schools, technical schools, and higher education institutions. In July, Donetsk State University runs local preparatory courses for working and rural youth, and review lectures on mathematics are given to all applicants.

The university also operates a preparatory department for working-class and rural youth.

Option 1
(specialty - applied mathematics)

1. A regular triangular paramida with two angles at the lateral edge $2\alpha$ is inscribed in a ball of radius $R$. Find the volume of the paramid.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt[n]{(x+1)^2} + 4\sqrt[n]{(x-1)^2} = 4\sqrt[n]{x^2-1}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos^4 x + \sin^4 x - 2\sin 2x + \frac{3}{4}\sin^2 2x = 0.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$\log_x - 2(x^2 - 8x + 15) > 0.$$

5. A 20 liter vessel is filled with alcohol. A certain amount of alcohol is poured from it into another, equal to it, and the rest of the water is added, the mixture is added to a personal vessel. Then $6\frac{2}{3}$ liters are poured from the first into the second, after which both vessels contain the same amount of alcohol. How much alcohol was initially poured from the first vessel into the second?

Option 2
(specialty - mathematics)

1. Vospedict is sent at a certain speed from point $A$ to $B$, from here from $A$ at a distance of 60 km. Then he returns at the same speed, but one hour after departure he makes a stop for 20 minutes. After this, he continues his journey, increasing his speed by 4 km/hour. What are the limits of the embedding speed if it is known that on the way back from $B$ to $A$ it took no more time than from $A$ to $B$?

2. In a regular triangular paramida, the angle of inclination of the side edge to the plane of the base is $\alpha$. Determine the dihedral angle at the lateral edge.

3. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} xy(x+y) = 30, \\ x^3 + y^3 = 35. \end{cases}$$
