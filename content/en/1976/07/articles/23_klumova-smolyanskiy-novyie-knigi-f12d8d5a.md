⟦folio 52⟧

New books

In this issue of the magazine we place brief annotations on books on mathematics and physics published in the second quarter of 1976 that are of interest to our readers.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"
1. Alekseev V.V. Abel’s theorem in problems and solutions. Volume 10 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 33 kopecks.
From this book you will learn how to solve algebraic equations of the 3rd and 4th degree with one unknown, why there are no general formulas (in radicals) for solving an equation of a higher degree. However, one of the main purposes of this book is to enable the reader to try his hand at mathematics. For this purpose, almost all of the material presented is presented in the form of specific examples and a large number of problems, accompanied by instructions and solutions.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers interested in server mathematics (starting with high school students), and does not assume any special knowledge presented. It can also be used in the work of a mathematical circle.

2. Lyubich Yu. I., Shor L. A. Kinematic method in geometric problems. 2nd edition, revised. (Popular lectures on mathematics). Volume 3 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.

It turns out that the “theory of speed”—kinematics—can be useful for solving geometric problems. Sometimes connections between the values ​​of segments, angles, etc. in geometric funcs is more complex than the connections between the rates of change of these quantities during the deformation of the func. Therefore, when solving a geometric problem, it is useful to imagine what will happen to the elements of the funkur under consideration if some of its points learn to move; the dependence of some elements on others can become clearly evident, and the solution to the problem will literally catch your eye.

The brochure by Lubitsch and Shor demonstrates the application of kinematics to problems of elementary geometry with several examples and provides problems for independent solution. The necessary information from kinematics and vector algebra is preliminarily presented.

The book is intended for high school students.
3. Zaitsev V.V., Ryzhkov V.V., Skanavi M.I. Elementary mathematics. 3rd edition. Volume 36 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 1 rub. 11 k.

The book contains a systematic presentation of the course in elementary mathematics and consists of two parts: 1) algebra and elementary functions and 2) geometry. It fully corresponds to the program of introductory courses in mathematics to higher education. In addition to theoretical material, the book contains a large number of problems, many of which are solved in the text.

This book may be useful for high school students preparing for course copies. In particular, it can be recommended as a teaching aid for preparatory departments of colleges.

4. Nikiforovsky V. A., Freiman L. S. The Birth of New Mathematics. Volume 12 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 80 kopecks.

The book tells about the work of four outstanding scientists of the 17th century - Descartes, Fermat, Torricelli and Roberval. These scientists developed the main innovations of mathematics, they participated in the creation of differential and integral calculus, which was finally formalized and completed later by Newton and Leibniz.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers interested in the history of mathematics.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
5. Kordemsky V. A. Mathematics studying randomness. Volume 7 l., circulation 120,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.

There are no elements of probability theory in school curricula. The selection of available school books on this subject is also not very extensive. Kordemsky's book is, apparently, the only book so far that captivatingly introduces the reader to probability theory, helping to independently master the initial concepts and methods of this science, as well as the simplest apparatus of mathematical statistics.

In the initial part of the book, a free form of presentation with the use of gaming material predominates; gradually the book becomes more “server-sided”, but does not lose its accessibility for schoolchildren.

Publishing house "Vishcha school" (Kyiv)
6. Yaglom I. M. Problems of attaching balls. Volume 3 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 12 kopecks.

⟦folio 53⟧

This book consists of two chapters. The first chapter is devoted to problems about circles and balls, it formulates the “problem of 13 balls”, with which the names of such outstanding scientists as D. Gregory and I. Newton are associated, and talks about the history of its solution, and the second chapter deals with the problem of polygons, polyhedra and arbitrary figures. The problem, closely related to the “13-ball problem,” opened a new large branch of geometry called “discrete or combinatorial geometry.” This geometry studies extremal geometric problems associated with finding “good enough” locations of a finite number of points or figures.

The main role in Yaglom’s book is played by 16 problems, related thematically, but mathematically almost independent; so he can safely skip tasks that seem very difficult or difficult for the reader. The book also formulates hitherto unsolved problems; Some of them may well serve as a topic for independent work in the field of combinatorial geometry.

The book is accessible to high school students and can be useful in mathematical circles.

Physics
Publishing house "Science"

1. Selitser S.I. Blitz questions, collection of problems. Volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 14 kopecks.

The book is a collection of problems in physics and covers all the program material of the school course. The problems presented in the book are based on various natural phenomena that are generally not paid attention to.

The book is intended for students in grades 8-10 of secondary school. It can be successfully used for extracurricular activities, for physical classes, as well as for independent preparation for university entrance exams.

2. Eskin V.E. The world of invisible giants. Volume 8 l., circulation 25,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.

The production of synthetic polymers is now one of the leading places in the national economy. The role of natural polymers in the functioning of living organisms is extremely important. The study of the complex structure of polymers is the subject of macromolecular physics.

The book clearly and vividly talks about the structural features and properties of polymer molecules, physical phenomena and the methods used to study them.

The book is accessible to high school students and is of interest to a wide range of readers new to polymers.

3. Kaganov M.I., Lifshits I.M. Quasiparticles (idea and reasons for quantum physics). Volume 6 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.

The book, written by prominent theoretical physicists, is dedicated to the story of the world of various quasiparticles. The authors popularly explain to the reader the amazing properties of backgrounds and magnons, plasmons and extrons, electrons and “holes”. They explain why, by studying the properties of solids, one can understand the world of quasiparticles.

The book is accessible to high school students and can be successfully used in elective classes.

Publishing house "Mir"

4. Bova V. New astronomy. Translation from English. Volume 10 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 52 kopecks.

This book is devoted to the presentation of modern issues of astronomy - radio astronomy, infrared, pentgen, gamma astronomy, etc.

It also reflects the sensational discoveries of recent decades - quasars, superstars, pulsars, “black holes”, exploding galaxies, etc. Lively and vivid language, imaginative, fascinating narration make the book interesting for a wide range of readers.

Publishing house "Knowledge"

5. Rydnik V.I. Field. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies. price 25 k.

This book talks about the nature of physical interactions. Already in the further past, scientists and physicists created various hypotheses about the “structure” of the real world, tried to explain the whole variety of physical interactions, in particular, with the help of the idea of ​​ether, filling all cosmic space. The author vividly and fascinatingly talks about the scope of these ideas in terms of the concept of physical fields.

The book is accessible to high school students and can be used in elective classes.

6. Levantovsky V.I. Transport space systems. Volume 3.5 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 11 kopecks.

The brochure talks about one pressing problem of modern space technology - the transition from single-use rocket launch vehicles to reusable transport spacecraft.

The brochure is designed for a wide range of readers.
