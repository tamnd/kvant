1. A “diver” floats when his weight is equal to the weight of the displaced water. If the toy is made of an elastic material (for example, rubber), then by blowing air through a tube, you can make the “Diver” sink (as the volume decreases, the pushing force decreases). As you dive, hydrostatic pressure increases, which further compresses the toy. There is a critical depth, starting from which, without blowing the “Vodolaz”, the slope of the current slope is such that the pushing force is less than the weight of the “Vodolaz”.
