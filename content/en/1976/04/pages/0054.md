Quests

1. There is a queen on the f8 square on the chessboard. The two take turns moving the queen either a few squares down, a few squares to the left, or a few squares to the left - down diagonally. The winner is the one who drives the queen into the lower left corner - on square a1. It is known that in this game a beginner, if he plays correctly, always wins, no matter how well his partner plays. How should a beginner play to win? How many moves does he need?

And who wins if the game is played correctly - the beginner or his opponent, if at first the queen stands on the e8 square?

2. There was a tent on the summer cottage site. When frosts began, the tent was taken down and they decided to dig up the area. It turned out that the dry ground directly under the tent had time to freeze more than the surrounding, wetter ground. How can I explain this?

3. There are 35 students in the class. Of these, 20 are involved in the math club, 11 are in the “skillful hands” club, 10 guys do not go to these clubs. How many “mathematicians” are engaged in “skillful hands”?

4. Place identical squares on a plane that do not overlap each other, so that the following condition is satisfied: no matter how you paint these squares with trays, some two squares of the same color will definitely have a common section of the border.
