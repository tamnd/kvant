Sam Loyd's Puzzle

SATSN A PUZZLE-GAME
BY SAM LOYD

Game - "Catching"
Players take ten chips and place them one by one. The beginner places his bet on any of the 36 fields. His partner also places a chip, but suffers so that it does not fall on the same line as the opponent’s chip. If it does end up on the same line as the opponent’s chip, he takes it and puts the chip that took it in its place. The player takes as many of the opponent's chips in a row as he can. Having taken a chip, the player makes a move again, placing another chip. To win, a player must take all of the opponent's pieces.

Puzzle - "Catching"
You need to place six chips so that no two are on the same straight line.
