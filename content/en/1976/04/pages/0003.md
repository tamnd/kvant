Sweep (p. 19, 55)

In the third issue of the magazine we wrote about various models of the Lobachevsky plane. In part, the article by S. Giidnikin talks about the Poincaré model in the upper half-plane, and the article by V. Boltyansky talks about the same model, but in a circle. The remarkable Dutch artist M. Escher managed to break the Lobachevsky plane (in the Poincaré model inside a circle) into congested (of course, on the Lobachevsky plane) parts of two types: “devils” and “angels” (see the first page of the cover). Escher called this painting "Heaven and Hell."
