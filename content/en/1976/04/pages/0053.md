The authors of “Flatland” (a Novel about the fourth dimension using the Square) and “Sferlandia” (a Novel about the use of space and the expanding Universe using the Hexagon) chose an unusual path of “post-comprehension” of geometry. Both Abbott and Burger have a goal: to teach mathematics the way the world has taught children - through play. One could say of these books that they are truly good science fiction; they can be placed somewhere between the works of Lewis Carroll and Jonathan Swift's Gulliver.

The world in which Square lives is inhabited by two-dimensional creatures. Their idea of ​​“space” is essentially E-Clidean geometry on the plane. And Kvadrat’s idea of ​​the emergence of the existence of a third, and even Thursday, measured by each of his “contemporaries” is poor and dangerous, that they are putting Kvadrat in prison.

The world of Hexagon is much more complex than the world of his “grandfather” - this is a sphere. The “geometry” of the Spheradians is again the geometry of two dimensions (but on a sphere), however, Burger leads the reader to the idea of ​​three-dimensional space, the geometry of which may not be Euclidean.

Written with gentle humor, Abbott and Burger's books enjoy deserved success in their homeland. We hope that they will also be of interest to our readers.

5. Trigg Ch. Problems with sizomics. (Problems and Olympiads.) Volume 16 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 87 kopecks.

This book by C. Trigg, an American mathematician and teacher, opens a new series of books from the Mir publishing house - “Problems and Olympiads,” which will include problems offered in mathematical countries. The problems collected in Trigg's book were published in various and sometimes gray mathematical journals. They are formulated rather complexly, but, as a rule, they allow for simple, elegant and original solutions. Among the authors of such solutions are famous American mathematicians.

Most tasks are within the capabilities of a high school student.

1. Kogan B. Yu. One hundred problems in electrical engineering. Volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 14 kopecks.

This collection of problems is published in the “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” series. It consists of simple electrical problems that are challenging in nature. Detailed solutions are given for all problems.

The book is intended for students in grades 9-10.

2. Perelman Ya. I. Entertaining physics. Book 1. Edition 19th. Volume 12 l., circulation 400,000 copies, price 46 kopecks.

3. Perelman Ya. I. Entertaining physics. Book 2. Edition 19th. Volume 15 l., circulation 400,000 copies, price 53 kopecks.

These books were written by a famous popularizer of science. The purpose of the book is not so much to impart new knowledge to the reader as to expect the knowledge already available. Despite the fact that the book was written a long time ago, it is “outdated” only to a very small extent.

Designed for those interested in physical education, secondary school students and individuals engaged in self-education.

4. Kompanets A. S. Laws of physical statistics. Shock waves. Superfine substance. Edition 2nd. Volume 15 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

Popular scientific articles about the basic laws and some important applications of statistical physics were written by the eminent Soviet theoretical physicist Prof. A. S. Kompanets. In a subtly and at the same time strictly scientific form, the author talks about statistical physics, about the basic laws governing physical systems of many particles, and about special issues important for practice. To understand the book, no special knowledge is required, but you must read it carefully and thoughtfully.

The collection is intended for high school students.

5. Shklovsky I. S. Universe, life, mind. Edition 4th. Volume 22 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 1 rub. 10 k.

This is a new edition of the famous book devoted to the fascinating problem of the emergence of the development of modern astronomy. The author of the book is a famous Soviet astronomer. Designed for a wide range of readers interested in the successes of modern natural research.
