2. Let us write down the Mendeleev-Clapeyron law for dry exposure and water vapor in a vessel:

$$p_B V = \frac{m_B}{\mu_B} RT,$$

(1)

$$p_\Pi V = \frac{m_\Pi}{\mu_\Pi} RT.$$

(2)

All values ​​with the index “B” refer to the influence, and with the index “P” - to the parameters. According to Dalton's law, the pressure in a vessel is equal to the sum of the partial pressures of the gases contained in it:

$$p_B + p_\Pi = p_0.$$

(3)

According to the definition of relative humidity, the saturation water vapor pressure $p_H$ is equal to

$$p_H = \frac{p_\Pi}{\varepsilon} 100.$$

(4)

and according to the conditions of the problem

$$m_B + m_\Pi = M.$$

(5)

From the system of equations (1) - (5) we find

$$p_H = \frac{100}{\varepsilon (\mu_B - \mu_\Pi)} \left( p_0 \mu_B - \frac{MRT}{V} \right).$$
