3. In the absence of heat losses, the performance of the distiller is determined only by the power of the heater. Indeed, the mass $\Delta m$ of evaporated (distilled) water depends on the amount of heat released: $\Delta m \lambda = U^2 \Delta t/R$.

Here $\lambda$ is the specific heat of water vaporization, $R$ is the resistance of the heater, $U$ is the voltage of the network to which the distiller will be connected. Then $q = \frac{\Delta m}{\Delta t} = \frac{U^2}{R \lambda}$.

Let's find the distiller's performance for each spiral separately: $q_1 = \frac{U^2}{R_1 \lambda}$ and $q_2 = \frac{U^2}{R_2 \lambda}$, for serially connected spirals ($R_3 = R_1 + R_2$): $q_3 = \frac{U^2}{(R_1 + R_2) \lambda}$ and for parallel connected spirals $\left( R_4 = \frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2} \right)$:

$$q_4 = \frac{U^2 (R_1 + R_2)}{R_1 R_2 \lambda}.$$

Hence $\frac{q_4}{q_3} = \frac{(R_1 + R_2)^2}{R_1 R_2} = \frac{(q_1 + q_2)^2}{q_1 q_2} = 4,5$.

4. When the magnetic flux changes in the coil, an electric induction force $\mathcal{E}_i = -\frac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta t}$ arises, and a current $l = \frac{\mathcal{E}_i}{R} = -\frac{1}{R} \frac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta t}$ flows through the closed coil.

According to the conditions of the problem, a magnetic field kills a uniform one. This means that a constant current flows through the coil, so the value of the flowed charge is $q = l \Delta t = -\frac{1}{R} \Delta \phi$.

Change in magnetic flux $\Delta \phi = \Phi_2 - \Phi_1 = -BSN = -BN \pi d^2/4$.

$$q = \frac{B \pi d^2 N}{4 R} \approx 5.10^{-3} \kappa.$$

5. Let's denote the distance from the camera lens as $d$, and the distance from the lens to the screen as $f$. Lens linear magnification $\Gamma = \frac{f}{d e}$. Also, $d + f = L$. Hence $d = \frac{L}{1 + \Gamma}$ and $f = \frac{\Gamma L}{1 + \Gamma}$.

Now, using the linear formula

$$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{1}{d} + \frac{1}{f},$$

Let's find the lens distance using the formula: $F = \frac{df}{d + f} = \frac{\Gamma L}{(1 + \Gamma)^2}$, where $\Gamma = \frac{4 m}{4.10^{-3} m} = \frac{3 m}{18.10^{-3} m} \approx 166,7$.

Finally $F = \frac{\Gamma L}{(1 + \Gamma)^2} \approx 10 \text{ cm}$.
