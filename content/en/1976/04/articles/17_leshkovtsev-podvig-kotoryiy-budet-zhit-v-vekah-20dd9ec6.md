A feat that will live for centuries

15 years have passed since the day when the Soviet spacecraft Vostok lifted the first cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin to the sky-high heights. Man found himself face to face with outer space. The heroic flight was completed successfully.

15 years is a short period of time in history. But how much has been done during this time in the field of research and use of outer space! Many Soviet cosmonauts made their attempts on the Vostok, Soyuz spacecraft and Salyut space stations, and some of them have already been launched into outer space twice. The study of Lunya and the planets of the Solar System developed intensively. Space research has become more international. The Intercosmos scientific programs are being successfully implemented, and the first Soviet-American space program Soyuz-Apollo has been completed.

Yuri Gagarin's flight was marked by the release of a large number of postage stamps in many countries. We talked about some of these brands in the fourth issue of our magazine for 1973. Paying deep respect to Gagarin's feat, many countries continue to issue postage stamps commemorating his flight. A particularly large number of such stamps were issued in 1971, on the tenth anniversary of the flight. We present here Soviet and foreign stamps depicting Yuri Gagarin and the Vostok spacecraft, issued after 1970.
