⟦folio 55⟧

In general, to make sure that “A is true if and only if B is true,” you need to make sure that condition B is both necessary and the conclusion A is sufficient. If the fulfillment of conditions B is sufficient, but not necessary, or necessary, but not sufficient, then the expression containing the words “Then and only then” turns out to be incorrect (false).

Task 3. Which of the following statements are true:
a) A triangle is equilateral when it is equilateral.
b) x < 4 is necessary and sufficient for it to be 2x < 2³.
g) The diagonals of the quadrilateral ABCD are mutually perpendicular to the moed and the molkomoed when ABCD is a delmoid (a delmoid or rhomboid is a convex quadrilateral that has one axis of symmetry, which is its diagonal).
d) For a parallelogram, there is the same area of ​​the moed and the milk moth when they have the same length of the bases and the length of the height.

Considering the location of the rib tracts, we became acquainted with the important words “sufficient” and “necessary” and established what dependencies these words characterize. Another important word caught the attentive reader’s eye: “Only.” The following examples illustrate the great importance of this little word.

Artists competition

This competition is good for groups of 5-8 people. One student clearly pronounces the coordinates of a point in succession, and the rest mark the points in a straight carbon Cartesian coordinate system and connect each point to the previous straight line segment. If the points are found correctly, the result will be a schematic figure of some animal or bird.
