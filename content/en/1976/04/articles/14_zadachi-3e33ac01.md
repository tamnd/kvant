## Tasks

1. There is a queen on the f8 square on the chessboard. Two in turn
move the queen either a few squares down or a few squares
to the left, or a few cells to the left - down diagonally. The one who wins
whoever drives the queen into the lower left corner - onto square a1. It is known that in this
game, a beginner, if he plays correctly, always wins, no matter how well
nor did his partner play. How should a beginner play to win?
How many moves will he need?
And who wins if the game is played correctly - the beginner or his opponent, if
Does the queen initially stand on square e8?
2. There was a tent on the summer cottage site. When the frosts began,
The tent was taken down and they decided to dig up the area. It turned out that dry land
directly under the tent managed to freeze stronger than the surrounding
wetter soil. How to explain this?
3. There are 35 students in the class. Of these, 20 are involved in a math club,
11 - in the “skillful hands” circle, 10 guys do not go to these circles. How many
“mathematicians” are engaged in “skillful hands”?
4. Place eleven identical squares on a plane, not overlapping each other, so that the following condition is satisfied: no matter how you paint these squares with three colors, some two squares of the same color will definitely have a common section of the border.
