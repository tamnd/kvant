Leningrad State University
them. A. A. Zhdanova

Leningrad State University was described in «Kvant» No. 6 for 1975 and 1976. In this issue we have given the written entrance exam in mathematics at various faculties of Leningrad State University in 1975.

Faculty of Mathematico-Mexican and Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes

1. At the end of the first operating cycle, there are $a$ liters of water containing $p\%$ water. 2 cl of powder were used from each box, and then the same amount of new powder was added, and powder containing $q\%$ water was added to the first box, and $3q\%$ water was added to the second box. As a result, the second box contained 1.5 times more water than the first. How much water was in each box at first?

2. Solve system equation
$$\begin{align*}
(4y^2 - y + 6) \cdot 2^x &= 20y, \\
x + \log_2 y &= 2.
\end{align*}$$

3. Solve the equation
$$\log_3 \cos 2x - 2 \log_3 \mathrm{tg} x = 1 + \log_3 (\sin x + \cos x) + \log_3 (\cos x - \sin x).$$

4. In a right triangle, the hypotenusea is equal to $c$ and three times the height drawn from the vertex of the right angle. Find the legs.

5. A two-legged equilateral triangle rotates about an axis passing through the point of intersection of its altitudes parallel to the longer side. Determine the volume of a body of rotation if the obtuse angle is $\alpha$ and the opposite side is $a$.

Faculty of Biology and Geography

1. A team of lumberjacks must prepare 400 $m^3$ firewood. The loggers completed this work within 3 days, since they annually harvested 30 $m^3$ more firewood than planned. How much firewood did the brigade prepare annually?

2. (Only for the Faculty of Biology). Solve inequality
$$5\sqrt{x} + \frac{5}{2\sqrt{x}} \leq 2x + \frac{1}{2x} + 4.$$

2a. (Only for the Faculty of Geography). Solve the equation
$$5x + \frac{5}{2x} = 2x^2 + \frac{1}{2x^2} + 4.$$

3. Solve system equation
$$\begin{align*}
x + y &= 2y \cdot \log_x y, \\
x^{\lg y} &= 1.
\end{align*}$$

4. (Only for the Faculty of Biology). Using the sides of the triangle $a$ and $b$ and the median $m_c$ determine the remaining medians.
