New books

This year we continue to publish annotations on books on mathematics and physics, published in 1976, which may be of interest to our readers. Due to numerous requests from readers, we will also publish annotations on some books from the publishing houses “Young Guard”, “Children’s Literature” and “Knowledge”.
In this issue we will talk about books published in the first quarter of 1976.
Most of these books can be purchased through specialized Book by Mail stores.

Mathematics
Publishing house "Science"

1. Po I and D. Mathematical discoveries. Translation from English. 2nd edition. Volume 31 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 64 k.

The name of the outstanding mathematician and teacher D. Polya is well known for his numerous scientific works. However, the most popular among mathematics lovers are his two-volume “Tasks and Theorems from Analysis”, as well as the books “How to Solve a Problem?” and Mathematics and Plausible Reasoning, republished in 1975; in the mathematical literature there are no books equal to it in terms of accuracy of analysis and fascinating presentation.

The book “Mathematical Discoveries” has the same character. “Mathematical discoveries” by D. Polya refers to obtaining any (however modest!) result, for example, simply solving a problem. The problems discussed in this book require knowledge beyond the previous high school curriculum, but their difficulty often exceeds the school level. For some problems, their complete solution (albeit in a rough form); for others, only the first few steps are outlined, and sometimes only the final result is indicated.

2. Gnedenko B.V., Khinchin A.Ya. Elementary introduction to the theory of probability. 8th edition, revised. Volume 8 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 27 kopecks.

The role of probability-theoretic methods in various fields of knowledge is increasing every year. The book by Gnedenko and Khinchin differs from other books on probability theory in that it predetermines the minimum requirements for mathematical values ​​of readers - the school course is completely enough for a free understanding of all its sections.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

3. Vilenkin N. Ya. Induction. Combinatorics. Volume 3 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 10 kopecks.

Currently, in high school mathematics courses, questions related to the methods of mathematical induction and combinatorics. The transition of the school mathematics course to theoretical-mixture principles requires a new presentation of these sections. This is the presentation given in the brochure. The examination of the method of mathematical induction is based on the Peano axiom system; talks about deductive and inductive methods of proof, examines a large number of interesting examples of the use of mathematical induction to prove identities and inequalities. In the section on combinatorics, we calculate the number of subsets of a given cardinality and the number of mappings of a given type (for example, we find out how many monotonic mappings exist, i.e., order-preserving mappings; we count how many different mappings there are from one set to another, etc.). A formula is derived for the number of placements with repetitions; Applications of combinatorics to probability theory are given.

The book will certainly be useful to all high school students, especially ninth grade students, as well as mathematics teachers and heads of mathematical circles.

Publishing house "Mir"

4. Abbott E. E. Flamland. Translation from English. Burger D. Sferlandia. Translation from Dutch Volume 16 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 1 rub. 04 k.

A series of translations of books by foreign authors little known to the Soviet reader on the subject of mathematics, which was opened by three books by M. Gardner (“Mathematical puzzles and developments”, “Mathematical leisure” and “Mathematical novels”), received the expected openings. There have already been 8 books published in this series. In 1976, it was replenished with another book.

This book combines two works written at different times and by different people: "Flamland"

was written in 1880 by the English educator Edwin E. Abbott, and “Sferlandia” was written in 1957 by the Dutch mathematician Dionysus Burger, who continued and developed the themes raised by Abbott.

The authors of “Flatland” (a Novel about the fourth dimension using the Square) and “Sferlandia” (a Novel about the use of space and the expanding Universe using the Hexagon) chose an unusual path of “post-comprehension” of geometry. Both Abbott and Burger have a goal: to teach mathematics the way the world has taught children - through play. One could say of these books that they are truly good science fiction; they can be placed somewhere between the works of Lewis Carroll and Jonathan Swift's Gulliver.

The world in which Square lives is inhabited by two-dimensional creatures. Their idea of ​​“space” is essentially E-Clidean geometry on the plane. And Kvadrat’s idea of ​​the emergence of the existence of a third, and even Thursday, measured by each of his “contemporaries” is poor and dangerous, that they are putting Kvadrat in prison.

The world of Hexagon is much more complex than the world of his “grandfather” - this is a sphere. The “geometry” of the Spheradians is again the geometry of two dimensions (but on a sphere), however, Burger leads the reader to the idea of ​​three-dimensional space, the geometry of which may not be Euclidean.

Written with gentle humor, Abbott and Burger's books enjoy deserved success in their homeland. We hope that they will also be of interest to our readers.

5. Trigg Ch. Problems with sizomics. (Problems and Olympiads.) Volume 16 sheets, circulation 50,000 copies, price 87 kopecks.

This book by C. Trigg, an American mathematician and teacher, opens a new series of books from the Mir publishing house - “Problems and Olympiads,” which will include problems offered in mathematical countries. The problems collected in Trigg's book were published in various and sometimes gray mathematical journals. They are formulated rather complexly, but, as a rule, they allow for simple, elegant and original solutions. Among the authors of such solutions are famous American mathematicians.

Most tasks are within the capabilities of a high school student.

Physics

Publishing house "Nuka"

1. Kogan B. Yu. One hundred problems in electrical engineering. Volume 5 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 14 kopecks.

This collection of problems is published in the “Library of the Physics and Mathematics School” series. It consists of simple electrical problems that are challenging in nature. Detailed solutions are given for all problems.

The book is intended for students in grades 9-10.

2. Perelman Ya. I. Entertaining physics. Book 1. Edition 19th. Volume 12 l., circulation 400,000 copies, price 46 kopecks.

3. Perelman Ya. I. Entertaining physics. Book 2. Edition 19th. Volume 15 l., circulation 400,000 copies, price 53 kopecks.

These books were written by a famous popularizer of science. The purpose of the book is not so much to impart new knowledge to the reader as to expect the knowledge already available. Despite the fact that the book was written a long time ago, it is “outdated” only to a very small extent.

Designed for those interested in physical education, secondary school students and individuals engaged in self-education.

4. Kompanets A. S. Laws of physical statistics. Shock waves. Superfine substance. Edition 2nd. Volume 15 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.

Popular scientific articles about the basic laws and some important applications of statistical physics were written by the eminent Soviet theoretical physicist Prof. A. S. Kompanets. In a subtly and at the same time strictly scientific form, the author talks about statistical physics, about the basic laws governing physical systems of many particles, and about special issues important for practice. To understand the book, no special knowledge is required, but you must read it carefully and thoughtfully.

The collection is intended for high school students.

5. Shklovsky I. S. Universe, life, mind. Edition 4th. Volume 22 l., circulation 150,000 copies, price 1 rub. 10 k.

This is a new edition of the famous book devoted to the fascinating problem of the emergence of the development of modern astronomy. The author of the book is a famous Soviet astronomer. Designed for a wide range of readers interested in the successes of modern natural research.
