⟦folio none⟧

In a square (with a cut out center) there are 12 terms (figures of 4 cells) and 12 squares (figure on top). Erase the 12 partitions separating the squares from the terms, so that this square (without a center) is filled with the 12 terms (shapes of 5 cells) shown below.

FOUR AND HUNDRED

Starting from the number 1 in the upper left corner, draw a broken line to the lower right corner with the number 9. In this case, you can move from number to number only either to the right or down; the number of “turns” should be 4; the sum of the numbers crossed out by the broken line must equal 100.

L. Motsalov
