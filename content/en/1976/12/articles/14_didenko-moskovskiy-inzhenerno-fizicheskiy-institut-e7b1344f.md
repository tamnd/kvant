## Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

The Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Engineering Physics Institute (MEPhI) was founded in 1942. The greatest scientists of our country, headed by I.V. Kurchatov, took part in the creation of the institute. MEPhI trains broad-spectrum research engineers in a number of the latest areas of science. Duration of training: 5 years and 6 months.

MEPhI is one of the leading universities in the country. MEPhI includes five faculties, one branch, and a preparatory department. The Institute has established a faculty for advanced training of teachers of the country's universities in physics, an All-Union School of Nuclear Physics and a Department of Physics, which is responsible for preparing and broadcasting on central television lectures on general physics for part-time students and educational programs on physics and mathematics for those entering universities.

The Faculty of Experimental and Theoretical Physics trains physicists and mathematical engineers for research work in the field of theoretical and experimental physics, development of modern physical installations and systems.

The Faculty of Technical Physics produces physics engineers specializing in the field of theoretical and experimental research of nuclear, thermophysical, gas-dynamic, molecular-kinetic and electromagnetic processes, design and operation of physical installations and instruments, as well as in the field of creation and research of new materials.

The Faculty of Automation and Electronics graduates physics engineers specializing in the creation and operation of electrophysical installations, automatic control systems for technological and physical processes, and the development of electronic devices of modern technical systems.

The Faculty of Cybernetics trains systems engineers and mathematical engineers in the development and mathematical support of modern high-speed electronic computers and automated control systems.

The Special Faculty of Physics (SFF) is organized at MEPhI and the Physical Institute named after. P. N. Lebedev of the USSR Academy of Sciences in 1972. It is a new form of education and prepares physics engineers in the latest areas of physics. The faculty enrolls students from non-Moscow higher educational institutions who have an education of two and a half years in the faculties of physics and physics and mathematics and who have shown a penchant for scientific work. Training at SFF is carried out according to individual plans. Currently, students from 28 universities and 4 polytechnic institutes are studying at SFF.

The MEPhI branch in Obninsk trains thermal physics engineers for the development and operation of nuclear power plants and installations, systems engineers for the design and operation of automated control systems, and mathematical engineers for the use of computer technology.

The preparatory department operates as a separate faculty. For one academic year, advanced workers, collective farmers and demobilized soldiers study mathematics, physics, Russian language and literature; they receive a scholarship as junior students. After successfully passing the final exams, students of this department are enrolled in the 1st year of MEPhI without additional entrance exams.

Over a thousand students are enrolled in paid preparatory courses and courses for working youth.
