## How to measure height?

⟦figure⟧

Below we describe several (eight) ways to solve the following important problem, both theoretically and appliedly: “How to determine the height of a multi-story building using a sufficiently long rope and a mercury barometer with a body height of one meter.”

The described methods can be applied to solve a wide class of similar problems (measuring the height of the Eiffel Tower, TV tower in Ostankino, Chomolungma, etc.).

The first method (trivial). Climb with a barometer to the roof of a building, tie a rope to the barometer, lower the barometer to the pavement, and then lift it. Measure the length of the rope needed for this.

Second method (direct). Holding the barometer vertically, climb the stairs and mark the length of the device on the wall. By counting the number of marks, obtain the height of the building.

Note. If you measure the height of one floor using the indicated method and multiply the resulting value by the number of floors, then too large an error will be made.

The fourth method (geometric). Take the barometer outside on a sunny day. Install it vertically. Measure the length of its shadow and the length of the building's shadow. From the similarity of triangles, calculate the required height of the building.

Fifth method (sociological). Interview all residents of the house and average the height values ​​​​they named. Offer a barometer as a prize.

Sixth method (kinematic). Knowing the number of beats of your pulse per minute, measure the number of its beats during the fall of a vertically oriented barometer from the roof of a building. Using the formula $h=gt^2/2$, calculate the height of the building.
