 XVIII Olympida
in mathematics

 XVIII International Mathematical Olympiad took place in July 1976 in Lienz (Austria). Teams from 18 countries took part in it: Austria, Bulgaria, Great Britain, Hungary, Vietnam, East Germany, Greece, Cuba, the Netherlands, Poland, Romania, USSR, USA, Finland, France, Czechoslovakia, Sweden, Yugoslavia. (Representatives of Germany were present as observers.) Each country's team consisted of eight schoolchildren and two leaders, except for Cuba, whose team consisted of three schoolchildren and leaders.

For most participants, the XVIII International Olympiad is a natural continuation of the national Olympids. In our country, after the Republican Olympics, their winners participated in special qualifying competitions. Then, taking into account the results shown by the guys at the All-Union Olympics

the years of this and past years, and the final composition of the team was determined. It included: Yuri Burov (school No. 2 in Moscow), Alexander Goniarov (school No. 13 in Nikopol, Dnepropetrovsk region), Petr Grinevich (school No. 204 in Moscow), Serdey Mironov (school No. 6 in Safonov, Smolensk region), Nikita Nechvetaev, Boris Solomyak and Serdey Fikashin (all - FMS No. 45 Leningrad), Tamyana Khovanova (school No. 444 in Moscow).

The trip to the International Olympiad was preceded by a local training camp, traditionally held at the school in memory of V.I. Lenin in Gorki Leninskiye. Since the composition of the team was determined in advance, this year’s training camp was purely training in nature - the guys worked without the additional nervous stress caused in previous years by the uncertainty of which of them would go to the International Olympiad. As a result, the team was in good shape.
