1. Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral satisfying the conditions of the problem. Let's denote $|AB| = a$, $|BD| = b$, $|CD| = c$. Under conditions $a + b + c = 16$ ($cm$). Let us estimate the area of ​​the quadrilateral $ABCD$:

$$32 \leq \frac{1}{2}ab + \frac{1}{2}bc$$ (since $S_{\triangle ABCD} = S_{\triangle ABD} + S_{\triangle BCD}$), and equality is achieved only if $b = 8$ ($cm$). This means $b = 8$ ($cm$), $ABD = BDC = 90^\circ$, and from here it easily follows that $|AC| = 8\sqrt{2}$ ($cm$).

2. Let us make the change $x = 2\cos \alpha$, then $P_1(x) = 2\cos 2\alpha$, $P_2(x) = (2\cos 2\alpha)^2 - 2 = 2\cos 4\alpha$ and then by induction it is easy to show that $P_n(x) = 2\cos (2^n\alpha)$.

This equation will be rewritten in the form $2\cos(2^n\alpha) = 2\cos \alpha$, from which $\sin \frac{(2^n - 1)\alpha}{2} \times \sin \frac{(2^n + 1)\alpha}{2} = 0$, $\alpha_1 = \frac{2k\pi}{2^n - 1}$, $\alpha_2 = \frac{2l\pi}{2^n + 1}$, $x_1 = 2\cos \frac{2k\pi}{2^n - 1}$, $x_2 = 2\cos \frac{2l\pi}{2^n + 1}$ ($k = 0, 1, \ldots, 2^{n-1} - 1$; $l = 1, 2, \ldots, 2^{n-1}$). It remains to prove that all these roots are different, since the polynomial $P_n(x) - x$ has degree $2^n$ and cannot have more than $2^n$ roots. The difference between roots within series follows from the polynotistic nature of the function $\cos \alpha$, and the difference between roots from different series follows from the mutual primality of the numbers $2^n - 1$ and $2^n + 1$.
