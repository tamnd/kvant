D. S. Rozhdestvensky (1876-1940).

He chose optics as a narrow specialty, although there were no scientific supervisors in this area of ​​physics at the university. Moreover, most university professors believed that their job was to teach, and not to engage in science. But Dmitry Sergeevich, in his own words, “didn’t give a damn about these views... and started working.” Independence and independence in science were the main features of his personality.

Working alone, designing and assembling the equipment necessary for wholesale, was not easy even for such a persistent and hardworking person. Therefore, the research began in 1903 and was formalized in the form of a master's thesis and completed only in 1912. True, three years later he had already defended his doctoral dissertation and was soon elected head of the Physics Institute of Petrograd University. This was a rare case in Russian physics when someone managed to make a major contribution to world science while remaining at home, in Russia. Concluding the last list of D. S. Rozhdestvensky, we point out that in 1925 he was elected corresponding member of the Academy of Sciences, and in 1929 - academician.

How did this outstanding Soviet scientist enrich science?

He began his research by studying the anomalous dissertation of light in the vapor of the solid metal sodium. In Newton's famous experiment, light passing through a glass element was decomposed into a color spectrum. From this experiment it follows that the refractive index depends on the color (or wavelength). This dependence is called dissertation. Since the sign in Newton's experiment refracts violet rays most strongly and red rays weakest, we can argue that the shorter the wavelength, the stronger the refraction. For arbitrary bodies in the visible region of the spectrum, the dependence of the refractive index on the wavelength is described quite well by the following formula:

$$n = a + \frac{b}{\lambda^2},$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constant quantities characterizing the given substance. This dependence is called normal dissertation.
