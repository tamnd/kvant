F383. A chain of mass m and length l is placed on an elastic circular cone with an apex angle of 2α. The cone together with the chain is driven with an angular velocity w along the vertical axis, coinciding with the symmetrical axis of the cone. The plane of the chain is horizontal. Find the chain tension.

Let us consider the forces acting on a small section of a chain of length Δl. This is the gravitational force G = mg Δl/l, the force N of the normal reaction of the cone surface and the tension force of the chain F acting on the section Δl from neighboring sections (Fig. 2). The sum of the projections of all forces on the vertical axis OY must be equal to zero, since the chain does not move at all in the vertical direction:

$$N \sin \alpha - G = 0.$$

(1)

The sum of the projections of all forces on the horizontal axis OX provides the centripetal acceleration of the chain element:

$$2F \sin \varphi - N \cos \alpha = \frac{m}{l} \Delta l \omega^2 r.$$

(2)

Here r = \frac{l}{2π} and sin φ = \frac{\Delta l}{2r} = \pi \frac{\Delta l}{l} (Fig. 3). Solving equations (1) and (2) together, we obtain

$$F = \frac{m}{2\pi} \left( \frac{g}{\text{tg} \alpha} + \frac{\omega^2 l}{2\pi} \right).$$

F384. Two interacting particles 1 and 2 with masses m1 and m2, respectively, form a closed system. figure 4a shows the trajectory of particle 1 and the position of both particles at the instant of time, the coefficient of particle 1 is v, and the velocity of particle 2 is −3v. Construct the trajectory of particle 2 if $\frac{m_1}{m_2} = 3$.

According to the conditions of the problem, the impulse (amount of motion) of this system is equal to zero:

$$m_1 v + m_2 (-3 v) = 0,$$

since zero momentum of a closed system means that its center of mass remains stationary all the time.

In the case of two particles, the center of mass is located on the segment connecting the particles, and divides this segment in a ratio inverse to the mass ratio.
