But thanks to the State Optical Institute, these works developed so successfully that by 1927 our country had completely stopped purchasing optical glass from abroad. The Soviet optical industry established the production of not only binoculars and stereo scopes, but also microscopes, spectrographs, telescopes and other complex scientific equipment.

The grandiose scientific and technological revolution taking place at the present time is extremely successful in science and production. Science now plays a huge role in the life of society, it has become an immediate productive force that is preparing a revolutionary revolution in all modern industries - in metallurgy, and in mechanical engineering, and on truss sites, and on collective and state farm fields. These changes required a radical restructuring of production, consolidation and consolidation of enterprises, the creation of firms and scientific and production associations. A. N. Kosygin’s report to the XXV Congress of the CPSU said the following on this matter: “In the tenth five-year plan, the creation of production associations in industry will be completed. Associations are a qualitatively new phenomenon in the management of industrial production. They represent a mechanical connection of an enterprise, and a single production-economic complex, in which the organization merges science and production, specialization and cooperation are widely developed.”

D. S. Rozhdestvensky attached great importance to the interaction of science with production. So, in 1936 he wrote: “We are winning one victory after another over the old world. We are taking first place in a number of industries and are continuously winning new first places... Now it’s our turn to organize our own science, to show our strength in science and, most importantly, in the scientific organization of industry. Especially in such an industry as optical industry, since it is inseparable from optical science.”

It is not surprising that the State Optical Institute was among the initiators of the creation of scientific and production associations. The Leningrad Optical Association, organized on his initiative more than 10 years ago, which, in addition to GOI, includes special design bureaus, optical factories and other divisions, is working very successfully. Recently, the largest optical telephoto lens in the world with a mirror diameter of 6 meters was created in the plant.

We would like to end this article with a bold and far-sighted dream, which D. S. Rozhdestvensky expressed back in 1919, at the end of his speech at the celebration of the first anniversary of the State Optical Institute.

“A vision of the near future appears before the eyes. Every atom is known, the possibilities of connections into molecules are explored and can be calculated at any moment. Chemistry is no longer an experimental science, but a theoretical one. Just like an architect has a method for calculating buildings in his reference book, so does an ordinary chemist, sitting in his office, use certain equations and tables to find the implementation of the most complex chemical compounds necessary for life. This is power over nature, its subjugation of man to an almost non-optical degree - it is the essence and soul of technology. It will be given by the knowledge of atoma... we cannot even predict how human life will be transformed in the coming decade, when the riddle of atoma will be solved, when thousands, ten thousand scientists will apply the will to solve, along with other problems, this, perhaps, the most important, when science will hourly approach life.”
