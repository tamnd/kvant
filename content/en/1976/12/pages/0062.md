Problems of the XVIII International Mathematical Olympiad for Schoolchildren
1st day
1. The area of ​​a flat convex quadrilateral is 32 $cm^2$, and the sum of the lengths of two opposite sides and one diagonal is 16 $cm$. Indicate all the values ​​that the line of the other diagonal can take. (Czechoslovakia, 5 points.)
2. Let $P_1(x) = x^2 - 2$, $P_k(x) = P_1(P_{k-1}(x))(k=2, 3, \dots)$. Prove that for any natural number $n$ all roots of the equation $P_n(x) = x$ are real and distinct. (Finland, 7 points.)
3. A Primogols box can be completely filled with single cubes (the edges of the cubes are parallel to the edges of the box). If we fill the box with cubes of volume 2 with edges parallel to the edges of the box, then the maximum number of such cubes will fill only exactly 40% of the volume of the box. Indicate the internal dimensions of all boxes for which this is the case ($\sqrt[3]{2} \approx 1,2599$). (Netherlands, 8 points.)
2nd day
4. Find the maximum value that the product of several natural numbers can take, the sum of which is 1976. (USA, 6 points.)
5. In a system of $p$ equations with $q=2p$ unknowns
$$\begin{cases} a_{11}x_1 + \dots + a_{1q}x_q = 0, \\ a_{p1}x_1 + \dots + a_{pq}x_q = 0 \end{cases}$$
coefficients $a_{ij} \in \{ -1, 0, 1 \}$. Prove that there is a solution $(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_q)$ of this system such that all $x$ are integers, for some $j (1 \leq j \leq q)$ $x_j \neq 0$ and for all $j (1 \leq j \leq q)$ $|x_j| \leq q$. (Netherlands, 7 points.)
6. The sequence $\{u_n\}$ is defined as follows: $u_0 = 2$, $u_1 = 5/2$, $u_{n+1} = u_n(u_{n-1}^2 - 2)^{-1}u_1 (n \geq 1)$. Prove that for $n \geq 1$
$$[u_n] = 2^{(2^n - (-1)^n)/3},$$
where $[x]$ is the largest integer not exceeding $x$. (UK, 7 points.)

IX Olympiad
in physics
From July 1 to July 7, the next IX International Physics Olympiad among schoolchildren was held in Budapest. Such Olympiads began to be held in 1967. The first International Olympiad was organized on the initiative of the Polish People's Resource Society. Schoolchildren from five socialist countries took part in it: Bulgaria, Hungary, Poland, Romania and Czechoslovakia. Already in the next 1968, students from the German Democratic Resource Center, the Soviet Union and Yugoslavia joined the participants in the Olympiad from these countries. Since then, our country’s team has been participating in all Olympiads. And in 1970, Moscow hosted students of the IV International Physics Olympiad.
The Olympiad includes solving three theoretical problems and performing one experimental work. The theoretical and experimental tours are held on different days. You are given 5 hours to solve the problem and complete the experiment. All problems are prepared by the country hosting the Olympics. This country also provides verification of the work, but the results of the verification are finally approved by the International Commission, which consists of the leaders of all teams.
