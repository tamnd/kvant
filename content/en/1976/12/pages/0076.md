1. Let us denote the speed of the boat in still water by $y$ ($\kappa m/час$), and the speed of the river flow ($\pi lota$) by $x$ ($\kappa m/час$). From the problem conditions we have the following system of equations:
$$\begin{cases} \frac{96}{y+x} + \frac{96}{y-x} = 14, \\ \frac{96}{y+x} + \frac{72}{y-x} = \frac{24}{x}. \end{cases}$$

To solve it, we introduce a new unknown $z = y/x$. Multiplying the second equation of the system by $x$, we find
$$\frac{96}{z+1} + \frac{72}{z-1} = 24,$$
whence after simple transformations we obtain the quadratic equation $z^2 - 7z = 0$. But $z \neq 0$, therefore $z = 7$. Substituting $y = 7x$ into the first equation of the system, we have:
$$\frac{6}{x} + \frac{8}{x} = 7,$$
whence $x = 2$.
Answer: speed of the boat is 14 $\kappa m/час$, speed of the river is 2 $\kappa m/час$.
