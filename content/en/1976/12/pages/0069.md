1. On the occasion of the election of Miraflores as President of Anchuria, a Russian dinner was organized. 666 guests sat at the round table, most of whom were dogs. Let's call the two sitting on either side of each guest his neighbors; two sitting through one from him on both sides - his “second neighbors”, etc.

Miraflores noticed that for each fox mountain, one of his second and one of his four neighbors are fox. How many lions were there at dinner?

3. The word “TOLYA” was made from matches (see figure). Rearrange one match to make a woman's name.
