M416. There are $n$ points $A_1, \dots, A_n$ on the plane, no three of which lie on the same straight line. What is the maximum number of segments with ends at these points that can be checked so that no triangles with vertices at these points are obtained?

A. Grigoryan, M. Primak, S. Fishbein

M417. On the surface of a cube with edge 1 there is a closed broken line. On each face of the cube there is at least one link of the broken line. Prove that the length of the broken line is at least $3\sqrt{2}$.

B. Provozvolov

M418. Prove that for any natural number $n$ the following inequalities hold:

$$n \left( \sqrt[n]{n+1} - 1 \right) \leq 1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \dots + \frac{1}{n} \leq 1 + n \left( 1 - \frac{1}{n\sqrt{n}} \right).$$

A. Gein
