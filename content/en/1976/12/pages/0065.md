There was no official team competition at the Olympics. But, of course, everyone counted how many points each team scored, how many bonuses and certificates its members received. In particular, our team received three prizes, one second prize and one certificate. Our team members scored a total of 192.75 points. The Romanian team scored 181 points, the GDR team 174 points.

In conclusion, I would like to note the very good organization of the IX International Olympiad in Physics. These Olympiad participants are obliged to the Ministry of Public Education of Hungary, the Physical Society named after. R. Eotvos and the Department of Atomic Physics of the University of Budapest. All the work was carried out very well, despite their multilingual nature. The International Committee had almost no complaints about the assessments. At the Olympics, trust, mutual understanding and friendship reigned.
