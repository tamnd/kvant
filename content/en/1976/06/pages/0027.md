Let us denote the extended ascent by x, and the extended descent by y. (You can use a level place without taking into account, since on it the speeds of the grandfather and grandson are the same.)
Then the grandfather’s time on ascents and descents is $\frac{x}{6} + \frac{y}{8}$, and the grandson’s is $\frac{x}{4} + \frac{y}{20}$. Let it be known which of these numbers is the other, which we will write as follows: $\frac{x}{6} + \frac{y}{8} \sqrt{\frac{x}{4} + \frac{y}{20}}$.
Transforming, we get: $9y \sqrt{10} x$. If the grandson returned earlier, then $9y > 10x$, from where $y > x$. If the grandfather returned earlier, then $9y < 10x$, which is possible both with $y < x$ and with $y > x$.
