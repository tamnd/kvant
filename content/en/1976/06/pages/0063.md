In «Kvant», 1973, No. 7, we talked about the Ural Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after. A. M. Gorky. In this issue we provide samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and problems for an oral exam in physics at the faculties of mathematics, mechanics and physics in 1975.

Mathematics
Matematiko-mechanical faculty

Option 1

1. The first weaving factory produced 80,640 m of fabric in k days (k is an integer), and the second weaving factory, one working day longer, produced 139,840 m of the same fabric. It is known that in one day the second factory produces at least 8000 m of fabric more than the first. How many meters of fabric does each factory produce in one working day?

2. A circular segment is inscribed into a right-angled triangle so that its chord lies on the hypotenuse, and the arc equal to π + 2α touches the leg. One of the acute angles of a triangle is equal to α. The end of the segment chord closest to the vertex of the angle $\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha$ coincides with the middle of the hypotenuse. Prove that the angle α satisfies the correspondence stg α = -2 cos 2α.

3. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + 2xy - 3y^2 = 0, \\ x|x| + y|y| = -2. \end{cases}$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\log \cos x \left( \cos x - \frac{1}{4} \sin x \right) = 3.$$

Option 2

1. The workshop has 8 machines, each of which produces 5 products per day. The workshop received an order for 440 products. Before starting work, it turned out that k machines were out of order. After the production of 150 products, the old ones were repaired and another 2k machines of the same productivity were put into operation. It is known that the workshop fulfilled the order on the 10th day (before the end of work for the day). How many products did the shop produce for this order during the 10th day of work?

2. A circular segment is inscribed into a right triangle so that its chord of length l lies on the hypotenuse, and the arc containing 240° touches the leg. Find the hypotenuse of a triangle if one of its acute angles is 30°.

3. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - x - y^2 = -0,25, \\ \sqrt{x} + \sqrt{y} = 0,5. \end{cases}$$
