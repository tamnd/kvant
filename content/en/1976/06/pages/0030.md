M386. A square room is partitioned off by partitions parallel to the walls, making the rooms slightly smaller. The side length of each room is an integer. Prove that the sum of the lengths of all partitions is divisible by 4.

M387*. Is there a natural number such that if you add it to itself on the right, you get a perfect square?

V. Kukushkin
