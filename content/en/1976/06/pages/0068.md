⟦folio 66⟧

Quests

1. Three identical boxes contain two balls: one contains two black balls, the other contains two white balls, the third contains a white and a black ball. Each box has a table: one shows two white balls, the other two black, the third a white and a black. But it is known that the contents of each box do not correspond to the table. How, after taking only one ball from only one box, rearrange the tables on the boxes according to their contents?

2. A fish swims in the lake. It floats horizontally all the time. The bottom of the lake is very nervous. The fish swims first over a deep depression, then over an underwater mountain, then falls under an overhanging rock. What forces act on the fish in these three cases?

3. Three incorrect equalities were added from matches (see figure). Rearrange one match in each equation so that the equations become true.

4. Two identical rectangular magnets were connected once as shown in figure a, another time as shown in figure b. Draw the magnetic field induction lines in these two cases.

5. On figure the division process is protected (by a corner), in which the numbers are protected by beeches. Decipher the example.
