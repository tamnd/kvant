5. What to do at school?

Of course, master mathematics, or applied mathematics - this is primarily mathematics. It is necessary to master the practical method of reasoning, learn to give precise definitions, and train in solving problems of various types, in particular problems that develop logical and algorithmic thinking.

Do not neglect “test” problems, the solution of which requires representative management. Unfortunately, text problems in a school mathematics course, as a rule, have little in common with the problems faced by an applied mathematician. However, the important habit is not to be shy in front of non-mathematical texts!

To fall in love with calculations, starting with the roughest estimates of the order of magnitude and the roughest calculations and ending with the most accurate calculations using all the methods known to you and available computing tools.

Masters physics and certain sections of other disciplines (including those not included in the school curriculum) in which mathematics is used. Learn to think in “physical” language, conduct informal reasoning, learn to use heuristic definitions, conduct heuristic proofs typical of applied disciplines.

To begin with, we suggest you solve several problems and a list of useful books and articles.

Exercises

1. Prove that nothing happened during the game of hex.
2. Prove an estimate of the total number S of strategies for the first hex game:
$$121 \cdot 119^{120} \cdot 117^{118} \cdot \dots \cdot 101^{102} < S < 121 \cdot 119^{120} \cdot 117^{118} \cdot 3^4.$$
3. Compare S with the possible number of elements of parts in the Universe.
4. In the wall of the “Horns and Hooves” club of the Interturnaya Gazeta, a message appeared that the fire chief of the Kama elevator, Matvey Tolstobryukhov, calculated that the capacity of this storage facility is 839522634175293648209 grains of wheat. List two gross errors in this calculation.
5. Derive a simple approximate formula for increasing the length of the day (compared to December 22) on January Nth, depending on N.
6. The axes of two circular cylinders of radii R₀ and R intersect at right angles. Obtain approximate formulas for the volume V of the resulting body with R₀=const and R→0 or R→∞.
