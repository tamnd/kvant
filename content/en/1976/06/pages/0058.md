3. Let the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ intersect at point $H$. Prove that
$$|AH| = 2R|\cos\hat{A}|, \quad R = \text{radius описанной окружности треугольника};$$
6) $|AH| = |BC| |ctg\hat{A}|$. In particular,
a) if $|AH| = R_0$, then $\hat{A} = 60^\circ$ or $\hat{A} = 120^\circ$;
b) if $|AH| = |BC|$, then $\hat{A} = 45^\circ$ or $\hat{A} = 135^\circ$.
Note that these two examples are given as problems 5.1 and 5.3 in the brochure “Mathematical Modern Geometry” (“Hayka”, 1974), where incorrect answers are indicated (oblique angles are forgotten). See also the magazine “Mathematics at School”, 1970, No. 4, p. 44; 1974, No. 5, p. 73.

E. Gotman (Arzamas)

4. Given a triangle $ABC$, side $a, b, c; r$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle, $R$ is the circumscribed circle, $S$ is its plane. Prove that
a) $tg\frac{\hat{A}}{2} + tg\frac{\hat{B}}{2} + tg\frac{\hat{C}}{2} \leq \frac{9R^2}{4S}$;
b) $\cos^2\frac{\hat{A}}{2} + \cos^2\frac{\hat{B}}{2} + \cos^2\frac{\hat{C}}{2} \geq \frac{27r}{8S}$.

For each triangle it is enough to solve this problem.
