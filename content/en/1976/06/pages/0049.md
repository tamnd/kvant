The fact is that many problems, especially of an economic, social, social type, are so complex that they cannot be formalized by means of modern mathematics. In other words, it is impossible to construct a mathematical model of the phenomenon under consideration. In such a situation, there will be a particularly acute need for a combination of formalized and informal approaches, a combination of mathematical and heuristic methods, calculations and intuition. This path leads to the concept of a simulation model. The simulation model depicts and imitates the process being studied; it serves for a comprehensive analysis of it, allows you to vary various process parameters and study the consequences of these changes. Some elements of the simulation model may allow formalization, while others may not. In all cases, however, it is assumed to create the capabilities of a machine (quickly reading a larger number of options) and intuition, human experience. Simulation modeling is the most important direction in improving applied mathematics. You can learn a lot about this in a fascinating book.

N. N. Monseeva “A Mathematician Asks Questions” (M., “Znanie”, 1974).
