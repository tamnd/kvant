1. Before the introduction of new machines, the workshop worked for $\frac{150}{40 - 5k}$ days, and after the introduction of $-\frac{290}{40 + 10k}$ days. From the conditions of the problem we obtain the inequality
$$\frac{150}{40 - 5k} + \frac{290}{40 + 10k} < 10,$$
whence $10k^2 - 39k + 32 < 0$. Since $k$ is an integer, from the last inequality we find $k = 2$. This means that before the introduction of new machines the workshop worked for 5 days, then for another 4 full days 12 machines worked in the workshop, i.e. in 9 days the workshop produced $150 + 4\cdot 5\cdot 12 = 390$ products. Thus, on the 10th day of work on this order, the workshop produced 440 - 390 = 50 products.

3. The first equation of the system can be represented as

$$\left(x - \frac{1}{2} - y\right)\left(x - \frac{1}{2} + y\right) = 0.$$

If $x = \frac{1}{2} + y$, then from the second equation we find $\sqrt{\frac{1}{2} + y} + \sqrt{y} = \frac{1}{2}$. The last equation has no roots. If $x = \frac{1}{2} - y$, then the second equation of the system is reduced to the form $\sqrt{\frac{1}{2} - y} + \sqrt{y} = \frac{1}{2}$. Hence $\sqrt{y} < \frac{1}{2}$, i.e. $y < \frac{1}{4}$. But for $y < \frac{1}{4}$ $\sqrt{\frac{1}{2} - y} > \frac{1}{2}$ holds, so the last equation also has no roots. Thus, the system has no solution.
