We present without proof the main theorem about finite fields.

Theorem. The number q of elements of any finite field is the power of the prime number q=p^m. On the contrary, for any q=p^m, where p is a prime number and m is an arbitrary natural number, there is a unique finite field F_q with q elements *).

The validity of this theorem implies the existence of a finite affine plane of order q=p^m.

*) The proof of this theorem can be found in van der Waerden’s classic book “Modern Algebra”, vol. 1, p. 153 (Gostekhidat, 1947). The only field must be understood in the sense that any other field with the same number of elements is isomorphic to the field F_q; between the elements of these two fields one can establish such a one-to-one correspondence so that zero corresponds to zero, one to one, sum to sum, and product to product.
