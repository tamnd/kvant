Hello, dear Seryozha!
I received your letter the other day. It really appealed to me. You write that you carefully read the note “On power systems” in «Kvant» No. 8 for 1975 and solved all the problems proposed there. How can one doubt this when at the end of the letter it says: “Written in Moscow, on Sunday, September 112, 30400”? *). Oh, what an original person you are, Seryozha! True, I don’t quite understand why you wrote down the date and year in different systems. However, in this case, it is your right.

Well, now let's get down to business.
1. As I promised, today we will decide about the double power system. This is the simplest simplification system. There are only two digits in it - 0 and 1. The number 2, that is, the base of the system, is written as 10. You, of course, know this very well.

*) September 14, 1975. The first letter to Seryozha («Kvant», 1975, No. 8, p. 59) talked about different simplification systems. Thus, the number 14, written in the decimal system, that is, 1410 (index 10 is the base of the silicate system) is the number 112 in the ternary sigil system; since 1410 = 1.3² + 1.3 + 2.3⁰. And the number 197510 in the five-digit system is written as 30400, since 197510 = 3.5⁴ + 0.5³ + 4.5² + 0.5¹ + 0.5⁰.

Here is how the numbers of the first ten are written in the double system: 1,10,11,100,101,110,111,1000,1001,1010.

The addition table in this system consists of a single equality: 1+1=10. (The equalities 0+0=0, 0+1=1+0=1 can be omitted - after all, adding zero does not change the number!)

What about the multiplication table? Strictly speaking, there is no multiplication table in the dual system - you just need to know that any number multiplied by zero is zero, and that multiplying by one does not change the number.

Arithmetic operations on multi-digit numbers in the double system are performed according to the same rules as in the decimal system. Let's look at specific examples.

1) Let's add the numbers 101101 and 10100. Let's put one number under the other, observing the digits:

+ 101101
10100

1+0=1; 0+0=0; 1+1=10, — 0 we write, 1 in our mind; further, 1 + 0 = 1, and also 1, it will be 10, - again we write 0, 1 - in our mind; finally 1, and another 1 (what was “in the mind”) gives 10. So, we get:

+ 101101
10100
1000001

Let's check the correctness of our calculations. To do this, we write these numbers in the decimal system. We have:
$$101101_2 = 1 \cdot 2^5 + 0 \cdot 2^4 + 1 \cdot 2^3 + 1 \cdot 2^2 + 0 \cdot 2^1 + 1 \cdot 2^0 = 45_{10},
$$
$$10100_2 = 1 \cdot 2^4 + 0 \cdot 2^3 + 1 \cdot 2^2 + 0 \cdot 2^1 + 0 \cdot 2^0 = 20_{10}.$$
The sum of these numbers should equal 65. So it is: $1000001_2 = 1 \cdot 2^6 + 0 \cdot 2^5 + 0 \cdot 2^4 + 0 \cdot 2^3 + 0 \cdot 2^2 + 0 \cdot 2^1 + 1 \cdot 2^0 = 65_{10}.$
