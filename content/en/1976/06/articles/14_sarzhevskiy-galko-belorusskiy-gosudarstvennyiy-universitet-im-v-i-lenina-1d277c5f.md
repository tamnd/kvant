Belarusian State University
them. V. I. Lenina

The Belarusian Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University was established in 1921. Over the course of its existence, it has grown into one of the largest universities in the country. In particular, in three faculties (physics, mechanics and mathematics) and the faculty of applied mathematics, the university trains highly qualified personnel in the physics and mathematics process. In this article we will briefly talk about the Faculty of Physics of BSU.

The Faculty of Physics of BSU has two departments: physics and radiophysics. The physics department admits 325 people to the first year. The specialization of students is carried out, where students deal with issues of quantum mechanics, field theory and elementary particles, statistical physics, the theory of relativity, the theory of atomic and molecular spectra. The remaining departments produce mainly experimental physicists, special science in optics and spectroscopy, solid state and semiconductor physics, tellophysics, biophysics, nuclear physics, etc.

The Department of Radiophysics accepts 100 people for the first year. Here students specialize in radio engineering and physical electronics, electrophysics, electronic mathematical machines, etc.

Students of the Faculty of Physics, starting from the 2nd-3rd year, are involved in scientific work, which is carried out at the departments of the faculty, in the laboratories of the Research Institute of Applied Physical Problems at the BSU, at the institutes of the Academy of Sciences of the BSSR and other research institutes in Minsk.

The university has a preparatory department that trains specialists for physics and other faculties of the university.

Applicants to the faculties of physics, mechanics and mathematics, chemistry and the faculty of applied mathematics take an oral exam in physics. The exam papers contain three questions on the program for applicants to universities and one task. The task option for applicants entering the Faculty of Physics and the Faculty of Physics are different. Below are several tasks and options that were offered to applicants entering BSU in 1975.
