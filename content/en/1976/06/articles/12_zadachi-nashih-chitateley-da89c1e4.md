## Tasks for our readers

1. At the base of the right parallelepiped $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ lies
parallelogram $ABCD$ with an acute angle $\widehat{DAB}=\gamma$. Diagonals
$AB_1$ and $BC_1$ of the lateral faces form angles $\alpha$ with the plane of the base
and $\beta$. Determine the angle between these diagonals.
2. Given are the geographic coordinates of two points on a sphere of radius $R$:
$\phi_1$, $\theta_1$ and $\phi_2$, $\theta_2$ ($\phi$ is latitude, $\theta$ is
longitude). Determine the distance on the sphere between these points.
V. Khrenov
(Voronezh)
3. Let the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ intersect at point $H$. Prove that
$|AH|=2R|{\cos\widehat A}|$, where $R$ is the radius of the circumscribed circle
triangle;
$|AH|=|BC|\cdot|{\ctg\widehat A}|$.
In particular,
if $|AH|=R$, then $\widehat A=60^\circ$ or $\widehat A=120^\circ$;
if $|AH|=|BC|$ then $\widehat A=45^\circ$ or $\widehat
A=135^\circ$.
Note that these two examples are given as problems 5.1 and 5.3 in the brochure “Mathematical Competitions. Geometry" (Science, 1974), where incorrect answers are indicated (obtuse angles are forgotten). See also magazine
“Mathematics at school”, 1970, No. 4, p. 44; 1974, No. 5, p. 73.
E. Gotman
(Arzamas)
4. Given a triangle $ABC$ with sides $a$, $b$, $c$; $r$ — radius
the circle inscribed in it, $R$ - the circumcircle, $S$ - its area. Prove,
What
$$\tg\dfrac{\widehat A}2+\tg\dfrac{\widehat B}2+\tg\dfrac{\widehat C}2
\le\dfrac{9R^2}{4S};$$
$$\dfrac{\cos^2\dfrac{\widehat A}2}a+\dfrac{\cos^2\dfrac{\widehat B}2}b
+\dfrac{\cos^2\dfrac{\widehat C}2}c\ge\dfrac{27r}{8S}.$$
For which triangle is equality achieved?
T. Raikov
(Bulgaria)
