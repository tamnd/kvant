Moscow Technical Institute

The Moscow Technical Institute has the following faculties: mechanical-technological (trains process engineers); chemical-

⟦folio 63⟧

Faculty of Technology (trains chemical and process engineers); Faculty of Engineering and Economics (graduates of the faculty receive the qualification of engineer-economist in accounting); Faculty of Arts and Technology. The institute has a daytime preparatory department and an evening preparatory course.

Since the 1974/75 academic year, all the first courses have been taught according to the new curriculum, in which the role of mathematics in the training of engineers has been significantly increased. At the MIT faculties, in addition to the general course of higher mathematics, they teach courses in probability theory and mathematical statistics, mathematical programming, as well as a number of special ones, in particular, the foundations of the mathematical theory of experiment. The greatest mathematical training is provided at the faculties of engineering, economics and mechanics and technology; a course in computational mathematics has been introduced at the faculty of chemical technology. Computer training has been introduced at all technical faculties.

At different faculties, the requirements for mathematics are different, which is taken into account when compiling options. Below are some options for the written entrance examination in mathematics and the problems of the oral examination in MIT in physics in 1975.
