The Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Electrotechnical Institute of Communications (MEIS) was founded in 1921. Now the institute has 6 faculties, where more than 6 thousand students study.

The Faculty of Automation, Telemechanics and Electrical Engineering trains engineers with a specialty in Radio Engineering. Over the past two decades, radio has been used to control spacecraft and lunar explorers, photograph the surface of Venus, transmit television programs to all corners of the planet, and much more. Engineers who graduated from the institute with a degree in Radio Engineering master all the applications of radio for the needs of the national economy, cultural development and defense of our Motherland and are sent to work in research institutes and industrial enterprises that develop and manufacture electrical equipment.

The Faculty of Engineering and Economics trains engineers in the specialty “Organization of mechanized processing of economic information.” Graduates of the faculty are sent to research institutes, design organizations, computer centers and planning and financial departments and departments of the ministry, where they develop automated control systems, operate them and manage the technical process of processing economic information (in computer centers).

The Faculty of Automation of Communications Enterprises is the only faculty in the USSR that trains electrical engineers with a specialty in “Machines and Equipment of Communications Enterprises.” Modern postal machines are complex electromechanical units containing electronic control devices. In Moscow, the capitals of the Union resource and large cities of the country, there is a wide network of highly mechanized post offices based on production lines for automated processing of postal items. Technical progress in the field of postal services has created a need for highly qualified specialists in the automation of production processes in postal service enterprises, who have a good knowledge of electronics and mechanics.

Graduates of the faculty work in design and engineering organizations, research institutes and organizations that install, configure and test equipment for communications enterprises.

Entrance exams to MEIS are held in August. Below are the written math exam options and the 1975 oral exam problems.
