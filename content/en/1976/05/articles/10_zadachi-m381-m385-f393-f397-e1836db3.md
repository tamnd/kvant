M381—M385; F393—F397

M381. 6 educational class activists 30 different commissions. Each two commissions differ in composition, but necessarily “overlap”, that is, they have a common member. Prove that it is possible to form another commission that intersects with each of these 30 commissions.

S. Fomin

M832. Given a polynomial
$$f(x) = x^n + a_1x^{n-1} + a_2x^{n-2} + \dots + a_n$$
with integer coefficients and natural numbers $k$ and $p$. Prove that if none of the numbers $f(k)$, $f(k+1)$, ..., $f(k+p)$ are divisible by $p+1$, then the equation $f(x) = 0$ has no rational roots.

T. Raikov

M383. Let $a$ and $b$ be two natural numbers. Prove that if $ab$ is even, then it will also find the natural numbers $c$ and $d$, which are $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = d^2$, and if $ab$ is odd, then such $c$ and $d$ cannot be found.

M. Gerver

M384. Two identically oriented squares $OABC$ and $OA_1B_1C_1$ have a common vertex $O$. Prove that the lines $AA_1$, $BB_1$ and $CC_1$ pass through the same point.

Z. Skopets

We will denote by $N(nF)$ the number of nodes that lie on the boundary $nF$. Let $S(F)$ denote the area $F$ (the area of ​​one cell is 1). Prove that
a) $N(nF)$ is a polynomial in $n$;

In three-dimensional space, a convex polygon $F$ is given, all of whose vertices are integer points (a point is called integer if all three of its coordinates are integers). Assume that the origin $O$ is a vertex $F$, and denote by $nF$ the polygon obtained from $F$ with a distance of $n$ times relative to the origin (each point $nF$ is obtained from some point $F$ by multiplying all coordinates by $n$, where $n$ is natural number). We will denote by $N(nF)$ the number of integer points in the polygon $nF$ and on its boundary, and by $N(\bar{nF})$ we will denote the number of integer points on the boundary $nF$. Let $V(F)$ denote the volume of the polygon.

e) Prove that there is no formula that would be true for any polygon $F$ described above and would express $V(F)$ in terms of $N(F)$ $N(\bar{F})$.

g) Come up with a formula that expresses $V(F)$ in terms of

h)* Prove the formulas obtained by solving e) and g).
