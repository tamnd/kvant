Figure for the cover of this issue were made not by an artist, but by an electronic computer. Such machines and the ability to draw help students at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys when studying a course in theoretical physics and theoretical mechanics. They allow you to “observe” some rather complex patterns and processes. The design line drawn on the first page of the cover is the trajectory of a charged particle moving inside the power station of a charged cylinder in a constant uniform magnetic field directed along the axis of the cylinder. The medium filling the cylinder resists movement, which leads to the dissipation of the particle's energy.

On the fourth page three more such trajectories are drawn, obtained under different initial conditions, and the hysteresis loop that is well known to you.
