Leningrad Department of Lenin Electrotechnical Institute named after. V. I. Ulyanova (Lenin) conducts her nacology from the technical school of the postal and telegraph department, opened in St. Petersburg in 1886. It was the first specialized electrical engineering higher education institution in Russia and one of the first in the world. In 1889 it was renamed the Electrotechnical Institute. The first elected director of ETI was Aleksandr Stepanovich Popov.

Currently, LETI is one of the largest electrical engineering institutes in the country and trains 12 thousand students in various fields.

The Faculty of Radio Engineering trains engineers in the specialty “Radio-electronic devices”, “Radio engineering” and “Design and production of radio equipment”, who are sent to radio factories, research institutes and design bureaus that develop and manufacture modern radio engineering kits for various purposes.

The Faculty of Electronic Engineering trains research engineers, designers, and technological instruments for various fields of science and technology. Students of the faculty are trained in the specialty “Electronic devices”, “Industrial electronics” and “Optical-physical devices” and after graduation they are engaged in the development and implementation of instruments and installations in such areas as laser technology, ion processes, electron beam processing, electron optics, plasma physics and a number of others.

The Faculty of Automation and Computer Engineering trains electrical engineers with a wide range of skills in the means and systems of technical cybernetics. The faculty pays special attention to the mathematical training of future engineers. Engineers are trained in the specialties “Automation and telemechanics”, “Automated control systems”, “Electronic engineering equipment”. Graduates of the faculty work in the field of designing computers, developing tools and languages ​​for communicating with them, creating computer-based automated control systems and automatic devices that operate at a distance from the operator and without taking into account a person.

The Faculty of Electronics and Automation trains electrical engineers in the following specialties: “Electric drive and automation of industrial installations”, “Electrothermal installations”, “Automatic control systems”. The development and research of automatic control systems using computer technology, electronic, semiconductor, magnetic, logical and digital automation devices, as well as the creation of complete systems based on these devices, characterize the focus of training young specialists of this faculty.

The Faculty of Electrical Engineering prepares students in the following specialties: “Electroacoustics and Ultrasound Engineering”, “Semiconductors and Dielectrics”, “Semiconductor Devices” and “Electronic Medical Equipment”. A distinctive feature of the faculty is the in-depth physics and mathematics training of its graduates, who are distributed to research institutes, design bureaus and factories.

The Faculty of Naval Electrical and Electronics Engineering and Automation is the youngest faculty of LETI. It was organized in 1967. The faculty trains engineers in the following specialties: “Electrical equipment of ships”, “Gyroscopic instruments and devices” and “Radio engineering” with a specialty “Ship radio engineering and communications”. Graduates of the faculty take part in the design, installation and commissioning of ship electronic stations, ship electric motors, automatic control systems, complete ship automation using digital computers, complex radio engineering and gyroscopic devices.

Full-time students are widely involved in research work in the student scientific society.
