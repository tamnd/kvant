The unified socialist education system implemented in the GDR is rightfully considered one of the best in the world. Further on the sympathy of Western German commentators often talk about “a miracle in the public education system of the GDR.”

“One of the greatest achievements of the German Democratic Republic is the creation of a Unified Socialist Education System,” said USSR Minister of Education M. A. Prokofiev, speaking at the VII Pedagogical Competition in Berlin.

The Republic’s successes in economic development can also be called a miracle: over the 25 years of the existence of the GDR, the volume of industrial production increased 5.7 times*. The high level of culture, science, and industry is largely due to an effective education system.

In the GDR, universal compulsory ten-year political education was implemented. The most dedicated schoolchildren, who are good and excellent students, receive the right to enter a 12-year school, which gives them a certificate of maturity and the opportunity to enter a university without exams.

The volume of the mathematics course of the compulsory ten-year school is not large, it does not contain complex proofs, research, does not include numerical sequences and limits, logarithms, transformed trionometric expressions and much more.

However, already in the ten-year school, server attention is paid to technical transformation and calculated, taking into account the necessary degree of accuracy and rounding, the use of slide rules and numerical tables. In addition, in the 7th grade, descriptive geometry is studied - methods of constructing projections, images of spatial bodies, and the ability to “read” drawings.

Graduates of the ten-year school will take 4 written exams: in mathematics (without division into algebra and geometry), in German language and literature, in Russian and either physics, chemistry, or biology - at the discretion of the school’s pedagogical council. In addition, two oral exams are conducted, one of which is a natural-mathematical cycle, the other is a social-humanitarian one. Graduates of the two-year school will take similar exams to obtain a matriculation certificate.
