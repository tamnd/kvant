⟦folio 72⟧

TASKS
OUR READERS

1. Is it possible to arrange the numbers 0, 1, 2, ..., 9 in ten circles on figure so that the sums of the even numbers on each of the lines are equal?
M. Karajyan
(r. Stepanavan)

2. Is it possible to arrange on a chessboard
a) 5 rooks and 5 knights,
b) 6 rooks and 6 knights so that no piece is different?
S. Okhimin (Orenburg)

3. Residents of a multi-storey building use personal equipment for both descent and ascent. If there is no call and the person is free, then he is automatically directed to a certain floor. Which floor should the elevator be directed to so that the average waiting time for the elevator by all residents is the shortest?
B. 3. K.
4. Find the prime number $p$ if it is known that the numbers $p^2 - 2, 2p^2 - 1$ and $3p^2 + 4$ are also prime.
5. In the set of the first ten thousand natural numbers, there are subsets of numbers ending at 1 and which can be represented as $8^m + 5^n$ (m, n - natural). How many elements does this subset contain?
K. Kurbanov (village of Tsuggi, Dagestan Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic)
