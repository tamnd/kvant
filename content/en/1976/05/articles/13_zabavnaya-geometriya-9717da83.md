Consider this question: What is the geometric integration of the two resulting weanings, and what role does the BD-height condition play?

Exercises
1. (NSU, 1969). Three lines are drawn through a certain point inside the triangle, corresponding to parallel sides of the triangle. These lines divide the triangle into six parts, three of which are triangles with areas $S_1, S_2, S_3$. Find the area of ​​this triangle.
2. A circle of radius $r$ is inscribed in triangle $ABC$. Three tangents are drawn to this circle, corresponding to parallel sides of the triangle $ABC$. Circles of radii $r_1, r_2, r_3$ are inscribed in the three triangles thus formed. Prove that $r=r_1+r_2+r_3$.
3. (MAI, 1972). From an arbitrary point $D$, taken on the basis $AB$ of the triangle $ABC$, two straight lines are drawn parallel to the sides $BC$ and $AC$, intersecting their corresponding ones at points $F$ and $K$. Find the sum of the lengths of the circles circumscribed by triangles $ADK$ and $DBF$, if the radius of the circle circumscribed by triangle $ABC$ is equal to 2.
4. (MAI, 1972). In the triangle $ABC$, the side $AC$ is equal to $b$, the side $AB$ is equal to $c$, and the bissertis inside it of the angle $A$ intersects the sides $BC$ at the point $D$ such that $DA=DB$. Find the length of side $BC$.
5. (MIPT, 1972). In an equilateral triangle $ABC$ ($AB=BC$), a circle is constructed at a height $BD$ as a diameter. Through the points $A$ and $C$ the tangents $AM$ and $CN$ are drawn to the circle, the extensions of which intersect at the point $O$. Determine the ratio $\frac{AB}{AC}$ if $\frac{OM}{AC}=k$ and the height $BD$ are greater than the base $AC$.
6. (MIPT, 1972). On sides $AB, AC$ and $BC$ of regular triangle $ABC$ there are corresponding points $C'$, $B'$, $A'$ so that triangle $A'B'C'$ is regular. The segment $BB'$ intersects the side $C'A'$ at the point $O$, and $\frac{BO}{OB'}=k$. Determine the ratio of the area of ​​triangle $ABC$ to the area of ​​triangle $A'B'C'$.
7. (MIPT, 1972). On sides $AB, AC$ and $BC$ of regular triangle $ABC$ there are corresponding points $C_1$, $B_1$ and $A_1$ so that triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ is regular. Altitude $BD$ of triangle $ABC$ intersects side $A_1C_1$ at point $O$. Find the ratio $\frac{BO}{BD}$ if $\frac{A_1B_1}{AB}=k$.
8. (MIPT, 1973). In the triangle $ABC$, through the point $M$, which lies on the side $BC$, lines are drawn parallel to the sides $AB$ and $AC$. The area of ​​the parallel gram formed in this case is $\frac{5}{18}$ the area of ​​the triangle $ABC$. Find the ratio $\frac{BM}{MC}$.
9. (MIPT, 1973). In the equilateral triangle $ABC$ ($AB=BC$), the bicissertis $AE$ intersects the altitude $BD$ at the point $O$, with $\frac{OB}{OD}=3$. In what ratio does the height $AF$ divide the height $BD$?

Fun geometry
The "comma" equation shown at figure 1, in polar coordinates $\rho$, $\varphi$, is (at $\varphi>0$)

$$\rho = \frac{(c_1+c_2) \pm (c_1-c_2)}{2} \times \frac{l_1 - |l_1|}{2l_1} + \frac{l_2 - |l_2|}{2l_2},$$

where $c_1 = \frac{a\varphi}{\alpha}$, $c_2 = \frac{a\varphi}{2\pi+\alpha}$, Fig. 1

$$l_1=\varphi-\alpha, l_2=\varphi-\alpha-2\pi$$ (on figure $\alpha=\frac{3\pi}{2}$). Its addition to the editorship of Yu. Dadrich (Perm).
