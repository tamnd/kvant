To the article "Trojans"

1. The vector $r_1$ drawn from the point $m_1$ to the bracing center is the sum of two vectors directed from $m_1$ to $m_2$ and from $m_1$ to $m_3$. The magnitudes of these vectors are $\frac{m_2}{m_1+m_2+m_3} R$ and $\frac{m_3}{m_1+m_2+m_3} R$ (see Fig. 1).

A parallelogram with such sides is similar to a parallelogram of forces $F_{12} = \gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{R^2}$ and $F_{13} = \gamma \frac{m_1 m_3}{R^2}$, or the sum of these forces $F_1$ should be directed towards the center of bracing. From consideration of similarity

$$F_1:r_1 = \gamma \frac{m_1 m_2}{R^2}: \frac{m_2}{m_1+m_2+m_3} R =$$

$$= \gamma \frac{m_1 m_3}{R^2}: \frac{m_3}{m_1+m_2+m_3} R.$$

From here

$$\omega^2 = \frac{a_1}{r_1} = \frac{F_1}{m_1 r_1} = \gamma \frac{m_1+m_2+m_3}{R^3}.$$
