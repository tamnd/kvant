Take a glass tube about half a meter long and 4-7 mm in diameter. However, hold it by the middle with your hand, and with the other hand, smoothly, without much pressure, move it from the end of the tube to its middle with a linen tripe moistened with alcohol or cologne. At the same time, you will hear a fairly strong clean sound.

This experiment is very simple in concept, but in order for it to work, you need to use a small glass tube, which is completely determined by how well you can excite the vibrations of the tube with the trip, since it is the trip that is the source of energy for the vibrations of the tube in this case *).
