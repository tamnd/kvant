13. $x = \frac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi$ ($k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots$). Note. This equation in ODZ is equivalent to the equation

$$1 - \cos x - \sin x = \cos 2x - \sin 2x,$$

(which is reduced to form)

$$(\cos x + \sin x)(2 \sin x - 1) = 0.$$

The roots of the equation $\cos x + \sin x = 0$ are not included in the ODZ of the original equation: for each of them, due to correspondence (4), we have $\cos 2x - \sin 2x = 1 - (\cos x + \sin x) = 1$, and in the ODZ the condition $\cos 2x - \sin 2x \neq 1$ must be satisfied. If $\sin x = 1/2$, then either $\cos x = \sqrt{3}/2$ or $\cos x = -\sqrt{3}/2$. In the first case

$$1 - \cos x - \sin x = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} < 0,$$

whereas in the ODZ the condition $1 - \cos x - \sin x > 0$ must be satisfied. In the second case, the conditions defining ODZ are met.
