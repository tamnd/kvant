The potential difference between points $A$ and $B$, created by an external current source, became equal to zero. The current in the deformation circuit will immediately stop. In the case of a non-deformation circuit, a decrease in current to zero cannot occur instantly, since according to the law of electromagnetic induction, when the current decreases, an emission will occur. d.s. self-niduction, which seeks to keep the current at the same level. As a result, a decrease in current from the value $I_0$ to zero will occur over some time. During this time, a certain amount of heat will be released in the circuit. The energy due to which heat will be released is drawn from the magnetic field energy “stored” by the circuit.
