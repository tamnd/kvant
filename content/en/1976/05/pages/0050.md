M381—M385; F393—F397

M381. 6 educational class activists 30 different commissions. Each two commissions differ in composition, but necessarily “overlap”, that is, they have a common member. Prove that it is possible to form another commission that intersects with each of these 30 commissions.

S. Fomin

M832. Given a polynomial
$$f(x) = x^n + a_1x^{n-1} + a_2x^{n-2} + \dots + a_n$$
with integer coefficients and natural numbers $k$ and $p$. Prove that if none of the numbers $f(k)$, $f(k+1)$, ..., $f(k+p)$ are divisible by $p+1$, then the equation $f(x) = 0$ has no rational roots.

T. Raikov

M383. Let $a$ and $b$ be two natural numbers. Prove that if $ab$ is even, then it will also find the natural numbers $c$ and $d$, which are $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = d^2$, and if $ab$ is odd, then such $c$ and $d$ cannot be found.

M. Gerver

M384. Two identically oriented squares $OABC$ and $OA_1B_1C_1$ have a common vertex $O$. Prove that the lines $AA_1$, $BB_1$ and $CC_1$ pass through the same point.
