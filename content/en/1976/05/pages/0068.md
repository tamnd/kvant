Matematika
Option 1

1. The student had to find the product of the number 136 by some two-digit number in which the ones digit is twice the tens digits. Absentmindedly, he placed the digits of a two-digit number in places and obtained a product of 1224 greater than the true one. What is the true product?

2. Solve the equation (ctg x - 1) (1 + sin 2x) = 1 + ctg x.

3. Prove identity
$$\text{tg} \left( x - \frac{3}{4} \pi \right) (1 - \sin 2x) = \cos 2x.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$x^2 - 1g^2 x - 1g x^2 - \frac{1}{x} = 0.$$

5. Prove that the number $k^3 + 5k$ is divisible by 3 for any natural number $k$.

Option 2

1. Two railways were simultaneously sent out of the port, one of them going south and the other going east. After 2 hours, the distance between them was 174 km. Find the average speed of each vehicle if it is known that one of them, on average, travels 3 km more per hour than the other.

2. Solve the equation
$$2 \cos 2x + 2 \text{tg}^2 x = 5.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$2^{x+\sqrt{x^2-4}} - 5(\sqrt{2})^{x-2} + \sqrt{x^2-4} - 6 = 0.$$

4. Prove identity
$$1 - \frac{1}{4} \sin^2 2x + \cos 2x = \cos^2 x + \cos^4 x.$$

5. For what $\alpha$ and $x$ does the inequality hold?
$$\log_2 x + \log_x 2 + 2 \cos \alpha \leq 0?$$

Physics

1. Two trains move on courses intersecting at point $O$ at an angle $60^\circ$, with equal speeds of 20 knots (mph) each (Fig. 1). Determine the minimum distance (in millimeters) between the trolleys during the movement, if at the initial moment they were at a distance of 20 and 30 miles, respectively, from point $O$.

2. A cannon standing on the equator fires a cannonball along the equator to the east, and then the exact same cannonball to the west. The magnitude of the powder charges and the elevation angles of the gun barrel are the same in both cases. Compare the distances from the cannon to the place where they hit the ground for both cannonballs.

3. How much work must be done to transfer a satellite from a circular orbit with a radius of $8 \cdot 10^3$ km to a circular orbit with a radius of $10^4$ km, if the total mechanical energy of the satellite in the first orbit is – 25 GJ?

Indication and Use the analogy between Coulomb's law and the law of universal gravitation, which allows us to calculate the potential energy of a satellite in a circular orbit.

4. Two small balls with radii $r_1 = 1$ cm and $r_2 = 2$ cm, located at a distance of $l = 100$ cm from each other, are attached to a battery with e. d. With. $\mathcal{E} = 3000$ b. Find the force of electrostatic interaction between the balls. The influence of connecting wires claims. Before connecting to the battery, the balls had no charge.

5. The point source of light and the observer are located on opposite sides of the claims of an indispensably thin partition on a straight line perpendicular to the plane of this partition. Will the source's brightness change for the observer if the space to the left of the partition (where the light source is located) is filled with a medium with a relative refractive area $n$? Confirm your answer by calculation.
