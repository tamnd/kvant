WINNERS
COMPETITION
"QUANTA"

In accordance with the decision of the Organizing Committee of the All-Union Olympiad, schoolchildren who regularly complete especially oriented and complete solutions to problems in the “Problem Book «Kvant»” receive the right to participate in the resource-based type of the All-Union Olympiad.

Over the past year, 1975, the editors received many letters with solutions to problems. The editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» together with members of the Organizing Committee selected the authors of the correct and most interesting solutions.

Below is a list of schoolchildren - winners of the competition «Kvant» - who received the right to participate in the 1976 Resource Olympiads.
