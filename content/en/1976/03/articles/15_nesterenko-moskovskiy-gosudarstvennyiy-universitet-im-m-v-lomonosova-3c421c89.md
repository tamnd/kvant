Moscow State University

In this issue we provide samples of options for a written exam in mathematics and examples of problems for an oral exam in physics, proposed at Moscow State University in 1975. Exams for all science faculties are held in July.

Matematika

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

1. Solve inequality
$$\log_3 \frac{1}{3} (x^2 + 4x + 6) + 6 \sqrt{\log_3 (x^2 + 4x + 6)} \geq 8.$$
2. Find all pairs of numbers $(x, y)$ satisfying the condition $y \geq 0$ and the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 4x^2 + (y + 1)^2 - 32 = 31 \cdot 2x^2 + (y + 1)^2, \\ \cos [\pi \cdot (x^2 + y^2)] = 1. \end{cases}$$
3. Two equal rhombuses $ABCD$ ($AB \parallel CD$, $AD \parallel BC$) and $APQR$ ($AP \parallel QR$, $AR \parallel PQ$) have a common vertex $A$ and lie in the same plane. It is known that $\Rightarrow BAD = \Rightarrow PAR = \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$, point $R$ lies inside the rhombus $ABCD$ and angle $RAD$ is equal to $\beta$. Sides $BC$ and $QR$ intersect at point $K$. Find the angle $BAK$.
4. A regular triangular example $SABC$ with vertex $S$ and base $ABC$ is inscribed with a ball of unit radius; The dihedral angle between the base and the side face is $60^\circ$. Prove that there is a unique plane intersecting the edges of the base $AB$ and $BC$ at some points $M$ and $N$ such that $MN = 5$, tangent to the ball at a point equidistant from the points $M$ and $N$, intersecting the extension of the height examples $SK$ for the point $K$ at some point $D$. Find the length of the segment $SD$.
5. Without using a table, find all values ​​$x$ in the interval $-4 < x < -2,5$ that satisfy the condition
$$\log \frac{1}{5} (\sin x - \cos 6x + \frac{2}{3}) = \log \frac{1}{5} (\sin 3x - \cos 8x + \frac{2}{3}).$$

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{x^2 + 4x - 5} - 2x + 3 > 0.$$
2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \sin (2x + \sin^2 y) = 0, \\ x - 3\sin^2 y = -2. \end{cases}$$
3. A rectangular boundary is given. It is known that a certain straight line parallel to the bases cuts it into two trapezoids, in each of which a circle can be inscribed. Determine the bases of the original trapezoid if its sides are $c$ and $d$, and $c < d$.
4. Two trucks are enough from the warehouse to the construction site to carry the same amount of cripple and the same amount of cement, and each of them first reached the crimp, and then the cement, carrying a load of the same weight for each trip. The first truck started work 40 minutes earlier and finished 40 minutes later than the second. At the same time, the time interval between the end of the kripcha trucks was no more than 20 minutes.
If the first truck started work 1 hour 5 minutes earlier than the second truck, there was no change, then the second truck finished work 55 minutes earlier than the first, and the time interval between the trucks finishing work was at least 25 minutes.
If the randomness of the first truck were reduced by 2 tons per hour, and the randomness of the second truck did not change, then the first truck would spend twice as long to complete the entire job as the second truck would do.
How much cement was added to the construction site?
5. Given a triangular example $ABCD$. The crossing edges $AC$ and $BD$ and $AD$ and $BC$ in this example are perpendicular, the edges $AB$ and $CD$ are equal, all edges of the example are tangent to a ball of radius $r$. Find the area of ​​face $ABC$.

Faculty of Biology

1. Two workers worked the same hours on the first day, creating the same parts. At the same time, the worker produced 30 more parts than the second one. The next day, the worker increased labor productivity by 1.2 times and finished work 1 hour earlier than on the first day.
