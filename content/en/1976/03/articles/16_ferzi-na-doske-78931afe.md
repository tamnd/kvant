Queens on the board

In the article “Chess-mathematical problems of S. Loyd” (Kvant, 1975, No. 6), the following problem was formulated.

On the board $n \times n$ $2n$ queens are considered poppy, so that on each vertical line on its optical axis. A flat mirror is placed behind the lens perpendicular to the axis. At what distance from the lens must a plane mirror be placed so that the rays reflected from the mirror, having passed through the lens a second time, become parallel? Lens focal length $F$.

For four and odd $n$ the solutions are slightly different. The figures show the distances of queens to boards $8 \times 8$ and $9 \times 9$, which are easily generalized for any $n > 1$. See for yourself.
