## Who is who?

In the market square of one small town there were three old men with very smart parrots. For a certain fee, each parrot answered questions asked of him. Residents of the town knew that one of the parrots would tell the truth for any price, the other would lie no matter how much you gave him, and the third would respond according to the price and mood: if he decided that they didn’t give him enough, he would lie, if he decided that it was enough, he would tell the truth. But which of the parrots is a liar, which is a cunning one, and which is an honest one, no one knew until... a mathematician came to town.

-Who is sitting next to you? - he asked the first parrot.

“Liar,” was the answer.

-Who are you? - the mathematician asked the second parrot.

- I'm a cunning one!

-Who is sitting next to you? - he asked the third parrot.

- Absolutely honest parrot! - was the answer.

And the mathematician immediately said which of the parrots is really absolutely honest, which is a liar, and which is a cunning one.

Can you do this?
