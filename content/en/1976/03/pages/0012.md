Another model was invented by Ani Poincaré, dealing purely analytically with questions of the theory of functions of a complex variable. He unexpectedly discovered that the transformations appearing in him can be interpreted as displacements in the Lobachevsky plane. This discovery made such a strong impression on him that many years later he recalled how it came to him “without any seemingly previous thought” when he climbed onto the omnibus during an excursion to Coutances. Ten years later, Poincaré made a remarkable addition to his model - he laid a “physical” foundation for it. This article is dedicated to the story of the Poincaré model.
