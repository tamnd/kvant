Hydrogen atom

Let's consider another problem - about quantizing the energy of hydrogen atoms. This problem was first solved by Niels Bohr. He calculated the spectrum of hydrogen atoms using the so-called planetary model. Although the calculated electron energies matched the observed spectrum, this model does not allow the calculation of other characteristics of atoms.

According to modern concepts of quantum mechanics, the planetary model only very conditionally reflects the actual behavior of electrons. In this hydrogen, a single electron seems to form an electron cloud around the nucleus - a cloud of negative charge, the density of which characterizes the probability of finding an electron in a given region inside the atoms.
