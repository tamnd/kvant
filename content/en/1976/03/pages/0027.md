Sam Loyd's Puzzle

Szm Loyd (1841-1911) was an extremely inventive American puzzle designer.

Many of his puzzles, if formed slightly differently, turn into problematic mathematical problems, allow generalization, and lead to new problems.

But Lloyd presents the tasks in an engaging, playful way. Lloyd published thousands of challenging problems (not only mathematical, but also linguistic and chess).

He himself made drawings for the problems, moreover, sometimes he typed and printed his works himself. The puzzle we present here was published in the book Sam Loyd's Puzzles, published in Philadelphia in 1912.
