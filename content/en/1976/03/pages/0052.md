⟦folio 50⟧

Who is who?

In the fish square of one small town there were three old men with very smart people. For a certain fee, each populya answered the questions asked of him. Residents of the city knew that one of the Populians would tell the truth for any payment, another would lie, no matter how much you give him, and the third would agree to the payment and mood: he decides that they didn’t give him enough - he will lie, he decides that there is enough - he will tell the truth. But which of the Populians is a liar,

No one knew who was cunning and who was honest until... a mathematician came to town.

-Who is sitting next to you? - he asked the first populi.

“Liar,” was the answer.
-Who are you? - he asked the third populai.

- I'm a cunning one!
-Who is sitting next to you? - he asked the third populai.

- Absolutely honest populya! - was the answer.
And the mathematician immediately said which of the Populians was truly absolutely honest, who was a liar, and who was a cunning one.
Can you do this?
