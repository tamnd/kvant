⟦folio 68⟧

Tasks

1. — We have 35 people in our class. And you can imagine how different it was with 11 classmates...
   “This can’t be,” Vitya Ivanov, the Olympic winner, immediately answered his host.
   Why did he decide this?
2. Victor was floating a plastic boat in the bathtub, loaded with metal parts from the designer. Suddenly the boat tilted and parts spilled into the water. Has the water level in the bath changed?
3. 55 schoolchildren took part in the Olympiad. They all did their jobs. When checking each problem, one of three ratings was given: “+” - the problem was solved, “-” - the problem was solved, but not solved, “0” - the problem was not solved. After checking all the works, it turned out that no two works coincided with the simultaneous quantitative assessments of “+” and assessments of “-”.
What is the smallest number of problems that could be proposed at the Olympiad?
4. When leaving the city for a walk, the hostess took various products with her. Since vinegar and sunflower oil do not mix, she poured both liquids into one bottle.
Is it possible to extract some salad vinegar for the father and some salad oil for the children so that the rest of the oil and the rest of the vinegar remain in the bottle?
