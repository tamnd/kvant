Faculty of Biology

1. Two workers worked the same hours on the first day, creating the same parts. At the same time, the worker produced 30 more parts than the second one. The next day, the worker increased labor productivity by 1.2 times and finished work 1 hour earlier than on the first day. The second worker on the second day produced 5 parts per hour less than on the first day, and worked 7/6 times more than on the first day. It is known that on the second day each of the workers produced 420 parts. How many parts per hour did the second worker produce on the first day?
2. Find all values ​​of the parameter $p$ for which the quadratic equation
$$(4x)^2 + \left(2\frac{1}{p} + 4 - 24\right)x + 1 = 0$$
has exactly one solution.
3. A convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle, and its side $AB$ is the diameter of the circle. Diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at point $M$. It is known that $BC = 3$, $CM = \frac{3}{4}$, and the area of ​​triangle $ABC$ is second greater than the area of ​​triangle $ACD$. Find the length of the segment $AM$.
4. Solve the equation
$$|\sin x + 2\sin 2x + \sin 3x| = 1 + 2\cos x + \cos 2x$$
5. Find all integers $n$ satisfying the inequality
$$\log \left(2\log \frac{\pi}{5} - n + 13\right) \leq 0.$$

Faculty of Chemistry

2. The two mines were improved during the first square. The March production of the second mine was 400 $m$ less than the February production of the first mine. The January output of the first mine was equal to its March output and equal to the February output of the second mine. According to the results of the first two months, the production of the first mine was less than the second mine by 1000 $m$. What is more and by how much: the January production of the first mine or the January production of the second mine? It is known that if the January production of the second mine was equal to its March production, then the production of the second mine for the months of January, February and March would be more than the production of the first mine for the same months by 1300 $m$.
3. In triangle $ABC$, the cosine of angle $ACB$ is equal to $\frac{2}{3}$, $AC = 3$, $BC = 9$. The point $D$ lies on the side $BC$ such that $CD = 3$. Find the ratio of the area of ​​the circle circumscribed by triangle $ACD$ to the area of ​​the circle circumscribed by triangle $ABD$.
4. A ball of radius $R$ touches all the lateral faces of a triangular rectangle at the middle side of its base. The segment connecting the vertex of the straight line with the center of the ball is divided in half by the point of intersection with the base of the straight line. Find the volume of the straight line.
5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the system
$$\begin{cases} \sin(3a - 3y) + 3\sin x = 0, \\ 2\log_4(a - y) + 2\log_4(2\sqrt{y}) = \log_2\sqrt{y} + 3\log_8(2x) \end{cases}$$
has four the number of solutions.
