⟦folio 82⟧

The landmark theoretical physicist of the first half of the 20th century, Paul Ehrenfest, together with his wife Tatyana Alekseevna Afanasyeva, lived in Russia from 1907 to 1912. He had a great influence on the development of Russian physics. Famous Soviet physicists and mathematicians A.F. Ioffe, D.S. Rozhdestvensky, A.A. Fridman, S.N. Bernshtein and others attended the home seminar he organized. In 1912, he moved to Leiden (Holland), where he became head of the university department of physics together with the outstanding Dutch physicist Hendrik Lorentz, who retired due to his age. Until the last days of his life, Ehrenfest maintained friendly relations with many Soviet physicists and a great interest in our country and its young science.

Many Soviet scientists sent abroad visited Ehrenfest. In his house, a special room was allocated for guests, who, before leaving, traditionally signed one of the walls. We reproduce here two fragments of these signatures. On the first, the signatures of the American physicist Roberta Millikan, who was the first to measure the electron charge, and Albert Einstein are clearly visible. On the second fragment we see the signatures of Professor A. A. Eikhenvald and Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences A. I. Shalnikov (marked with numbers 4 and 5). In addition to them, there are also signatures of Einstein, Niels Bohr, Erwin Schrödinger, Paul Dirac, Wolfrang Pauli and the outstanding German mathematician Felix Klein.
