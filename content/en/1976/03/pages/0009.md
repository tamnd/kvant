- Absolutely right! And this theory $P$ is warehouse geometry! It turns out that from the “material” of Euclid’s geometry it is possible to construct a model of Labachevsky’s geometry.

- So what, Labachevsky knew about this?

- No; but he built another wonderful model. He managed to build a model of warehouse geometry from the “material” of his geometry.

- So, if everyone were confident in the inaccuracy of Labachevsky’s geometry and doubted the correctness of Euclid’s geometry, then this model could convince everyone that Euclid’s geometry is also consistent! But it would be desirable for everything to be the other way around.

- You now see that Labachevsky understood well what a model was and how the consistency of its geometry could be proven. It was necessary, having taken geometry as the initial theory $P$, to construct from its “material” a model of theory $Q$—Labachevsky’s geometry. But Labachevsky failed to do this. Such models were found only after the death of the scientist.

- Who did this?

- Beltrami, Cayley, Klein, Poincaré and other scientists *).
