Substituting the expressions for $F_{\text{упр}}$ and $mg$ into formula (*), we obtain:
$$F + k\Delta x_0 = k(\Delta x_0 + h),$$
or
$$h = \frac{F}{k}.$$
Thus, the plates will not touch if
$$\frac{F}{k} < \frac{1}{2} l_0,$$
or
$$k > \frac{2F}{l_0} = \frac{2qE}{l_0} = \frac{\varepsilon_0SU^2}{l_0^3}.$$

F347. The spaceship is orbiting around Lunya in a circular orbit of radius $R = 3,4.10^6$ m. At what speed must the debris be thrown out of the spacecraft, tangentially to the trajectory of the ship, so that it lands on the opposite side of Lunya? How long will it take for the sedge to fall to the Moon? Please note that the acceleration of free putting on of chalk from Lunya is six times less than on earth. The radius of Lunya is equal to $1,7.10^6$ m.

The pennant ejected from the ship must move in an elliptical orbit tangent to the surface of Lunya (Fig. 11). The major axis of this orbit is $R + R_\text{л}$, where $R_\text{л}$ is the Lunyi radius. Let's find the speed $v_1$ that the pennant should fly at the maximum distant point $A$ of this orbit (i.e., at the point of ejection of the pennant from the ship). To do this, we will use the law of conservation of energy and Kepler's second law.

The gravitational force between the pendant and the Moon is similar to the force of interaction between two point electric charges of opposite signs. Therefore, knowing the expression for the potential field of the exact charge, we can, by analogy, write the expression for the potential energy of the pennant at point $A$ in the form *)

$$\Pi_A = -\gamma \frac{M_\text{л} m}{R}$$

($M_\text{л} - \text{масса Луньи}, m - \text{масса вымплела}$). At point $B$—the point of tangency of the pennant trajectory of the Lunyi surface—the potential energy is equal to

$$\Pi_B = -\gamma \frac{M_\text{л} m}{R_\text{л}}.$$

The kinetic energy of the pennant at points $A$ and $B$ is

$$K_A = \frac{mv_1^2}{2} \text{ и } K_B = \frac{mv_2^2}{2},$$

where $v_2$ is the speed of the pennant at point $B$. According to the law of conservation of energy

$$\frac{mv_1^2}{2} - \gamma \frac{M_\text{л} m}{R} = \frac{mv_2^2}{2} - \gamma \frac{M_\text{л} m}{R_\text{л}}.$$

Reducing by $m$ and taking into account that $\gamma \frac{M_\text{л}}{R^2_\text{л}} = g_\text{л}$, we get

$$\frac{v_1^2}{2} - g_\text{л} \frac{R^2_\text{л}}{R} = \frac{v_2^2}{2} - g_\text{л} R_\text{л}.$$

(*) See, for example, the article by S. Kozel “Physical analogies”, «Kvant», 1975, No. 11.
