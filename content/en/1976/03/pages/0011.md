When the history of Lobachevsky’s geometry is told today, one may get the impression that if the creators of non-Euclidean geometry had proven its consistency, it would have been favorably accepted. However, it was not the lack of this evidence that bothered the critics most of all. People are accustomed to the fact that geometry deals with our real space and that this space is described by non-Euclidean geometry. It is characteristic that Gauss singled out geometry from other branches of mathematics, considering it similar to the mechanics of experimental science. But at the same time, Gauss, just like Lobachevsky and Bolyai, understood that, firstly, the possibility of logical, harmonious geometric constructions, for which there is no physical reality - “imaginary” geometries, and, secondly, it is not only indisputable that the built-in scales in our world are dominated by Euclid’s geometry. However, what only a few mathematicians understood was absolutely inaccessible to non-professionals. The affirmation of Lobachevsky's geometry, they measured the non-Euclidean yardsticks of their geometric intuition - and received an inexhaustible source for wit.
