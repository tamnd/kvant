⟦folio 38⟧

Tasks
M371—M375;F383—F387

Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than May 1, 1976 to the address: 113035, Moscow, Zh-35, st. B. Ordynka, 21/16, editorial office of the magazine «Kvant». Lastly, write on the envelope the solutions to which problems you are sending, for example: “Problem «Kvant»” M371 or “...F383”.
Solutions to problems in each of the subjects (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems, please send in separate envelopes. Please also send problems from different issues of the magazine in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking your decisions). Send the conditions of the original problems proposed for publication in two copies along with your solutions to these problems (on the envelope put: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. The more difficult problems are marked with an asterisk.

M371. Each cell of the chessboard contains an integer from 1 to 64, with different numbers in different cells. In one question, indicating any set of fields, find out the set (set) of numbers in these fields. In what is the least number of questions you can find out the number in each cell?

S. Fomin

M372. Given triangle ABC. Prove that the condition $ACB \geq 120^\circ$ is necessary and sufficient for the inequality $|AP| + |BP| + |CP| \geq |AC| + |BC|$ to be satisfied for any point P in the plane.

P. Khaidukov

M373. a) All natural numbers (written in the decimal system) are divided into two classes. Prove that any infinite decimal fraction can be cut into finite pieces such that all of them, except perhaps the first piece, belong to the same class.
b) The same problem, but the natural numbers are divided not into two, but into several classes.

M374. Let $a, b$ and $c$ be positive numbers, $a > c$ and $b > c$. Prove the inequality
$$\sqrt{c(a-c)} + \sqrt{c(b-c)} \leq \sqrt{ab}$$
A. Reznikov

M375. Inside a convex polyhedron of volume 1, the $3 \cdot (2^n - 1)$ points are marked.
Prove that it is possible to cut from it a convex polyhedron of volume $(1/2)^n$ that does not contain any marked points inside it.

B. Makeev

F383. A chain of mass $m$ and length $l$ is placed on the main circular cone with an apex angle $2\alpha$. The cone together with the chain rotates with an angular velocity $\omega$ around a vertical axis coinciding with the symmetry axis of the cone. The plane of the chain is horizontal. Find the tension of the chain.
