Let's try, together with the Poincaria, to construct the geometry of their space. Like us, when living in Euclidean space, some statements concern Poincarine very strongly; they accept them without proof (axioms) and deduce from them more complex statements (theorems). For us, looking at Poincaria from the outside, all these statements will look different than for Poincaria (for example, the rays are straight for us!), so we will “translate” Poincarine’s formulations into our “prosaic” Euclidean language and prove it in our own way.

For example, Poincariers know that through two different points of a half-plane there passes a single semicircle perpendicular to the $x$-axis, or a vertical ray (proof!); see figure 2. Note that the physical explanation of this statement, which consists in the fact that light between two points will propagate along a single path, is the same for Poincaria and for us (however, for geometry this explanation has no evidentiary force).
