## Linguistics + mathematics

On Sunday, November 21, 1976, at 10 a.m., the first round of the next XIII Olympiad in linguistics and mathematics will take place in the building of the humanities faculties of Moscow State University on the Lenin Hills. Anyone can take part in the Olympics.

Linguistics and mathematics! Where does this combination come from? Modern linguistics strives for logical clarity and rigor of presentation, characteristic of the exact sciences, primarily mathematics. That is why mathematical disciplines occupy a large place in the educational programs of the Department of Structural and Applied Linguistics of the Faculty of Philology of Moscow State University. Mathematics gives linguists not only and not so much specific results and theorems, but rather a strict and formal style of thinking.

Below are two problems proposed at previous Olympics. The subject “linguistics” is not in the school curriculum. Therefore, the conditions of the proposed problems

Problem 1

Three Georgian phrases are given:

1. ლამაზი შვილების კარგი ამხანაგი
2. კარგი ამხანაგის ლამაზი შვილი
3. ლამაზი ამხანაგების კარგი შვილები

Two of them are translated into Russian as “good friend of beautiful sons” and “good sons of beautiful friends.”

Determine the translation of each Georgian phrase.

Problem 2
