⟦folio 52⟧

XXV Olympida
in physics
in Poland

All students of the last two grades of any type of secondary school, as well as the most capable students of lower grades, can take part in the physical Olympiad. The Olympiad takes place in 4 stages - introductory, I, II and III rounds. All Olympiad tasks are developed by the Main Olympic Committee.

Participation in the introductory and 1st rounds is by correspondence, and in the 2nd and 3rd rounds - very much so. Each participant solves the problems of the introductory and first rounds at home and can use any books and manuals. The teacher can help the student with clarifications that do not, however, lead directly to solving the problem. It is not necessary to solve all the problems, since those who participated in the introductory.type and scored enough points in the I.type are admitted to the 11th round. The solutions to each student's entrance assignment will be checked by their teacher in accordance with their own assessment criteria. Starting with I.type, solutions are reviewed centrally and scored on a 10-point scale - from 0 to 10 points for each problem. In experimental tasks, the theoretical and experimental parts are assessed separately (thus, a total of 20 points can be scored for one task). I.type tasks are checked by members of the District Olympic Committees, who draw up a list of participants admitted to the 2nd round. II.type (full-time) competitions are held in those cities in which the District Olympic Committees operate. The participants who performed most successfully at the regional modern round are invited to the III (All-Polish) round, which takes place in Warsaw.

All participants in the third round (regardless of the results of their performances in this.type) are exempt from physics exams for the matriculation certificate and upon admission to universities (where there is an entrance exam in physics).

Winners of the Physics Olympids are exempt from all entrance exams for admission to the faculties of exact sciences of universities, political universities and the Military Technical Academy.
