The boy holds a glass vessel filled with water in his hands. Having focused the sun's rays using this peculiar line, the boy will clamp a bunch of straw.

Have you ever wondered why, by focusing the sun's rays, you can light a fire, but you cannot light paper using a lens with starlight? If this interests you, read the article “In the Focus of the Lens” (p. 13).
