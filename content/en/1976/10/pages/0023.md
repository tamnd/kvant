⟦folio 21⟧

At the IX City Conference of Young Physicists in Kyiv (April 1975), an 8th grade student at secondary school No. 145 Stanislav Sokkin made a report on the topic “Waves in a stream in the presence of an obstacle.”
The report talked about experiments carried out with a stream of water or some other liquid.
These experiments, very simple and visual, not requiring any special equipment, make it possible to get acquainted with a rather complex physical phenomenon - the appearance of capillary waves on the surface of a liquid.
Here we publish (with some abbreviations) the text of this report.

If you look carefully, you can see many interesting phenomena even in the flow of water. Try to describe and explain one of them.

We place a plate (or even just a finger) under a thin water stream at a distance of 1-7 cm from the tap (where the flow can still be considered laminar). Immediately, part of the stream above the plate will become like an accordion (Fig. 1). What happened?
