M406. A circle of radius $R$ is divided by points $A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4$ into four equal arcs. Prove that the sum of four powers of distances from an arbitrary point on the circle $M$ to the point $A_k$ does not depend on the position of the point $M$, and
$$|A_1M|^4 + |A_2M|^4 + |A_3M|^4 + |A_4M|^4 = 24R^4.$$
Yu. Babenko

M407. Given two natural numbers $n$ and $m$, $n > m$. Prove that $n$ can be represented as the sum of two natural numbers, one of which is a divisor of $m$, and the other has no common divisor with $m$ except one.
S. Konyakin

M408. A rectangle similar to the original ones was made from 30 load-bearing rectangles. What could be the side length ratio of this rectangle?
P. Pankov

M409. 1000 numbers are written in a row on a line. The second line of numbers is written under it according to the following rule: under each number $A$ in the first line, a natural number is written indicating how many times $A$ occurs in the first line. From the second line, the third is obtained in the same way: under each number $B$ of the second line, a natural number is written down, indicating how many times $B$ occurs in the second line. Then the fourth is built from the third line, the fifth from the fourth, and so on.
a) Prove that a certain line coincides with the next one.
b)* Moreover, prove that the 11th line coincides with the 12th.
c)* Give an example of a first initial line for which the 10th line does not coincide with the 11th.
M. Seroya
