⟦folio 56⟧

Quests

1. Master of Sports Sedov, candidate master Chernov and first-class student Ryzhkov met at the club before the start of the tournament.
   “Please note,” the black-haired one noted, “one of us is gray-haired, the other is red-haired, and the third is black-haired.” But no one's hair color matches their last name. It's funny, isn't it?
   “You’re right,” the master confirmed.
   What color is the candidate for master's hair?

2. The gold medalist of the school football championship scored 7 points, the middle medalist - 5, and the bronze medalist - 3. How many points did the last place team score? How long did the team participate in the championship?
   (For a win, 2 points are given, for a draw - 1, for a loss - 0; if two teams scored the same number of points, then the places are determined by the difference between goals scored and goals conceded.)

3. Five chips each have one number: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. Select four of these chips and arrange them in a row so that the best four-digit number is the square of some integer.

4. Do you think there will be a candle burning in a rocket in which all bodies are in a state of weightlessness?

Fig. E. Nazarova
