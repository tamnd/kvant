⟦folio 63⟧

Dear reader!

To improve the work of the magazine, it is very important to us your opinion about the materials published in the magazine, about the extent to which the magazine takes into account your aspirations and interests in its work. We ask you to answer the questions in the questionnaire briefly, underline the answer with which you agree, and fill in the blanks. We will, of course, be very grateful to you for all the advice and suggestions that you would like to supplement our questionnaire. So that we can quickly use your advice, we ask you to send us the completed questionnaire as quickly as possible, preferably immediately upon receipt.

Write to us at the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, «Kvant», “Questionnaire”.

1. Your last name, first name and patronymic.

2. Age, class or profession and specialty.

3. Residences and school.

4. Are you a subscriber «Kvant»:
   a) 1 year; 6] 2 years; b] 3 years; d] more than 3 years!

5. What is your attitude towards the magazine in general:
   a) like; b) not very much; b] don't like it.

6. What do you like best about our magazine:
   a) mathematics; b) physics; b] equally - mathematics and physics; d] I don’t like either mathematics or physics!

7. As you read our magazine, have you become more interested in:
   a) mathematics; b) physics!

8. Can you cope with the tasks from the “Problem” section?
   a) often; b) not very much; b] fails!
