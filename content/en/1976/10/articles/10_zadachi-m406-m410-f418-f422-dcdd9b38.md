Tasks

M406—M410; F418—F422

M406. A circle of radius $R$ is divided by points $A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4$ into four equal arcs. Prove that the sum of four powers of distances from an arbitrary point on the circle $M$ to the point $A_k$ does not depend on the position of the point $M$, and
$$|A_1M|^4 + |A_2M|^4 + |A_3M|^4 + |A_4M|^4 = 24R^4.$$
Yu. Babenko

M407. Given two natural numbers $n$ and $m$, $n > m$. Prove that $n$ can be represented as the sum of two natural numbers, one of which is a divisor of $m$, and the other has no common divisor with $m$ except one.
S. Konyakin

M408. A rectangle similar to the original ones was made from 30 load-bearing rectangles. What could be the side length ratio of this rectangle?
P. Pankov

M409. 1000 numbers are written in a row on a line. The second line of numbers is written under it according to the following rule: under each number $A$ in the first line, a natural number is written indicating how many times $A$ occurs in the first line. From the second line, the third is obtained in the same way: under each number $B$ of the second line, a natural number is written down, indicating how many times $B$ occurs in the second line. Then the fourth is built from the third line, the fifth from the fourth, and so on.
a) Prove that a certain line coincides with the next one.
b)* Moreover, prove that the 11th line coincides with the 12th.
c)* Give an example of a first initial line for which the 10th line does not coincide with the 11th.
M. Seroya

M410. A circle of a great circle is drawn on a sphere of radius 1, which we will call the equator. It will be convenient for us to use others

geographical terms: stripes, meridian, parallel.

a) Let us define a function $f$ on this sphere, which, corresponding to any point on the sphere, becomes the square of the distance from this point to the equatorial plane. Check that this function has the following property:
if $M_1, M_2, M_3$ are the ends of a triangle of mutually perpendicular radii (*)
spheres, then $f(M_1)+f(M_2)+f(M_3)=1$.

In all the following paragraphs, $f$ is an arbitrary non-negative function on the sphere, which vanishes at all points of the equator and has the property (*).

6)* Let $M$ and $N$ be points of the same meridian, located between the northern stripe and the equator. Prove that if point $M$ is further from the equatorial plane than point $N$, then $f(M) \geq f(N)$.

c)* Let $M$ and $N$ be arbitrary points of the sphere. Prove that if point $M$ is further from the equatorial plane than $N$, then $f(M) \geq f(N)$.

d)* Prove that the function $f$ coincides with the function described in part a).

A. Lodkin

F418. It is practically impossible to separate wetted window panes that are folded together from each other if you try to tear one pane from the other. Why?

F419. In some galaxies, a system of planets similar to our solar system has been discovered. The average densities of the planets and the Sun in this system are two times less than the average densities of the planets and the Sun in our system, and the sizes of all celestial bodies are three times less than the sizes of the corresponding bodies in our system. How many Earth days does a year last on the discovered analogue of the Earth?

F420. On a bracket $ACBOE$ of 6 non-vertical rigid rods of equal length, hingedly connected (Fig. 1), a mass load $m$ is suspended at point $C$. Is the rod $AB$ stretched or sloped? Find the elastic force in this rod.

B. Bukhovtsev

F421. A thin cylinder of radius $R$ was spun to an angular velocity $\omega$ and placed in a corner (Fig. 2). The sliding friction coefficient between the corner walls and the cylinder is $k$. Determine how many revolutions the cylinder will make before stopping.

F422. Half of the spherical capacitor is filled with a dielectric with a dielectric capacity of $\varepsilon$ (Fig. 3). Find the ratio of charge densities on the upper and lower halves of the capacitor and its capacitance.
