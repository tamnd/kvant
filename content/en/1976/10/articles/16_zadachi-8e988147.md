## Tasks

1. Master of Sports Sedov, candidate master Chernov and first-class student Ryzhov
met at the club before the start of the tournament.
“Please note,” the black-haired man noted, “one of us is gray-haired, the other
red-haired, and the third black-haired. But no one's hair color matches
surnames. It's funny, isn't it?
“You’re right,” the master confirmed.
What color is the candidate for master's hair?
2. The gold medalist of the school football championship scored 7 points,
silver - 5, bronze - 3. How many points did the winning team score?
last place? How many teams participated in the championship? (For winnings you get
2 points, for a draw - 1, for a defeat - 0; if two teams scored
the same number of points, then places are determined by the difference between goals scored and goals conceded.)
3. Five chips each have one number: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. Select four of these chips and arrange them in a row so that the resulting
a four-digit number was the square of some integer
numbers.
4. Do you think a candle will burn in a rocket in which all bodies are in a state of weightlessness?
