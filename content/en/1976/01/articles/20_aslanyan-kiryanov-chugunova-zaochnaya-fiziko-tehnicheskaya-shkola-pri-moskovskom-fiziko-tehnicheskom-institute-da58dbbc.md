Correspondence School of Physics and Technology
at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology announces enrollment of students for the 1976-77 academic year.

For the 10th year now, the correspondence school of physics and technology at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology has announced enrollment of students for the 1976-77 academic year.

The form of teaching in our school is different from the way you are accustomed to working with a teacher in the classroom. Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches you to work with additional literature, and express your thoughts concisely.

Graduates of 3FTS receive good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics. Many graduates of 3FTS enter various types of countries and successfully study in them.

3FTS accepts students from eight summer and secondary schools located primarily on the territory of the RSFSR.

The goal of our school is to help students study independently in physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting students to schools, preference is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where 3FTS assistance is especially needed.

3FTS also accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized locally at the initiative of two subjects - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have completed the 3FTS introductory task. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director reports to 3FTSH the names of the circle leader and a changed list of the circle by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks include theoretical material and analysis of the nature of problems on the topic, as well as 10-14 problems for independent solution. The tasks are selected to vary in complexity: from simple to complex (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTSH or its filps, and the members of the circle are checked by its leader.

Moscow students receive classes in physics and mathematics 2 times a week according to the 3FTSH program in evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools), admission to which is carried out based on the results of the 3FTSH introductory task.

Each student completes the introductory task independently. The work must be done in Russian and carefully copied into one school terrace. The order of tasks must be the same as in the assignment. Send the notebook in a large envelope with a simple parcel post. Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you study, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the terradi. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered.

Below is an introductory assignment for physics and mathematics. In the physics assignment, problems 1-5 are intended for 7th grade students, 3-8, 12 for 8th grade students, 6-12 for 9th grade students. Introductory assignment in physics

1. It was necessary to respond promptly with 100 e of vegetable oil or mannol cool, but there were no scales in the house. Describe how you managed to cope with this task.
2. Find the average speed of the train if it is known that the first third passed at a speed of 50 km/h, the second - 75 km/h, and the last third - at a speed twice the average speed in the first two sections.
3. A hermetically sealed box weighing 0.1 kg and measuring 5 × 10 × 10 cm floats on the surface of the water. An aluminum weight was hung from below the box so that it was completely submerged in water, but continued to float in it. Find the mass of the weight if the density of aluminum is 2.7 e/cm³ and the density of water is 1 e/cm³.
4. Is it possible to use a barometer to determine the height of a building? Carry out the necessary measurements and calculations and compare the result with the actual height.
5. The fuel mixture is prepared from dry sawdust, peat and coal, the masses of which are taken at the relative height of 1 keg of such a mixture, if the specific heats of combustion of the corresponding combustible substances taken are 10⁷, 1.4·10⁷ and 3·10⁷ J/kg?
6. Two resistances connected in parallel release a power of 144 bm in an electric current circuit with a force of 4 a, and when

The consequent connection releases a power of 36 bt at a current strength of 1 a. Find the values ​​of these resistances.

7. Using experimental data for the acceleration of gravity and the length of the Earth's equator, estimate the average density of the Earth. Describe other possibilities for determining the average density of plans.

8. A thin thread of nylon, stretched horizontally, is equal when the middle threads suspend a load weighing 2 kts. How much weight can be suspended on it in a vertical position? Take into account that at the moment of breaking the thread made an angle of 1° with the horizon. Take into account that for small angles sin α ≈ α.

9. In a cylinder with a volume of 2 liters under a weightless piston we find the historical amount of ideal gas. A load with a mass of 20 kilotons was placed on the piston, as a result of which the piston dropped 40 cm. How many times did the temperature of the gas change after moving the piston? Piston cross-sectional area 50 cm², atmospheric pressure 1 amm.

10. Will a balloon filled with gel be able to lift a load of mass 150 kt if its volume is 150 m³ and the mass of its shell is 8 kt? The pressure and temperature of the gel inside the ball and the air outside are the same and are respectively equal to 1 amm and 15°C. The relative molecular weight of helium is 4, air is 29.

11. When does 1 m³ of air contain more moisture: in autumn (cold rain, temperature +3°C) or in dry summer (temperature +30°C, relative humidity 20%)?

12. It is proposed to measure the density of part of the granulated sugar. Describe in detail the method you used and the result of your measurements. What other possibilities can be offered for measuring the density of a piece of granulated sugar?

Introductory math assignment

1. Find the value of an expression
$$\frac{\sqrt{a+bx}+\sqrt{a-bx}}{\sqrt{a+bx}-\sqrt{a-bx}}$$
at
$$x = \frac{2am}{b(1+m^2)} (a>0, b>0, m>0).$$

2. The depositor took from the savings bank first 1/6 of his children, then 50 rubles. and finally 2/5 of the remaining amount. In just three times he took 510 rubles. How many children did he have left in the sphere after this?

3. Construct a rhombus along a given side of length a, if it is known that the lengths of the diagonals are related as m:n.

4. A two-digit number divided by the sum of its digits gives a part of 4 and a remainder of 3; If the digits of this number are rearranged, you will get a number that is 5 greater than the hex sum of its digits. Find this number.

5. Is the fraction $\frac{14m+3}{21m+4}$ cancelable, where m is an integer?

6. Find all real values ​​of a for which the roots of the equation
$$(a+1)x^2+2ax+a+3=0$$
put.

7. In a right-angled triangle, find the ratio of the lengths of the legs if the lengths of the altitude and the median extending from the right angle are in the ratio 40:41.

8. The sum of terms and the difference of programming arguments are positive. If you increase the difference of the program by 2 without changing its first term, then the sum of its terms will increase by 3 times. If the difference of the original program increases by 4 times without changing the first term, then the sum of the terms of the program will increase by 5 times. Determine the difference of the original program.

9. In equilateral triangle ABC ($|AB|=|BC|$), a circle is constructed at the height BD as a diameter. Through points A and C, tangents AM and CN are drawn to the circle, the extensions of which intersect at point O. Determine the ratio $\frac{|AB|}{|AC|}$ if $\frac{|OM|}{|AC|}=k$ and the height BD is greater than the base AC.

10. The bus from point A is automated and moves non-stop at constant speeds between A and B. Their first meeting occurred 42 minutes after the start of movement, and 2 hours 34 minutes after the start of movement, the automated one overtook the bus for the first time. How long after the start of movement will the vehicle and the automated vehicle be at point A at the same time for the first time?

11. Prove the method of mathematical induction herabenity
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{1}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}>\sqrt{n}$$
for n≥2.

12. Find the number of all three-digit numbers made up of the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 so that each number contains no more than two identical digits.

13. The sequence $\{x_n\}$ is given by the recurrence formula
$$x_1=\frac{a}{2}, x_n=\frac{a}{2}+\frac{x_n^2-1}{2}, n\geq 2$$
and 0<a<1. Find the limit of the sequence.
