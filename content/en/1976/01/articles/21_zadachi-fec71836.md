## Tasks

1. The Ododo language has only two sounds: “D” and “O”. Two words mean one thing
and the same if one is obtained from another using a certain quantity
the following operations: skipping consecutive sounds DO or OODD and adding OD combinations to any place. Do the words ODD and DOO mean the same thing?
2. Two copper tubes are lowered into the water to great depths. One tube is sealed
on both ends, and on the other, one end is open. What happens to the pipes at depth?
3. Restore the division record in which some “escaped” digits
replaced with asterisks.
4. It is known that the length of the shadow cast by an object changes throughout the day. The shortest shadow is at noon; in the evening the shadow “grows”. Is there a place on Earth where the length of the shadow does not change during the day?
5. The game “Who will be the first to name the number 100” involves two people. One calls any
an integer from 1 to 9 inclusive. The second one adds any
an integer from 1 to 9 that he likes and names the amount. To this
sum, the first one again adds any integer from 1 to 9 and calls a new one
amount, etc. The one who first names the number 100 wins. In this game
the beginner always loses unless his opponent opens one
secret. What is the secret, the game key, which provides the second player
victory?
