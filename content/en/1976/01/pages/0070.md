⟦folio 68⟧

New note
to the All-Union correspondence
math school

The All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into the school.
Classes will begin on September 1, 1976. School education is free.
Students admitted to school will regularly receive assignments that contain learning theoretical questions and problems to solve. The duration of training is three years.
The tasks of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must submit solutions to these problems no later than March 20, 1976. After your work has been reviewed (around July 1976), you will be advised whether you will be accepted into the school. Schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements will have preference for admission.
Although some of the introductory tasks differ in appearance from regular school ones, no additional knowledge of mathematics is required to solve them. In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to solve all problems without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without any justification may not be read. If there are different answers possible in a problem, then you need to indicate them all.
The work must be completed in Russian in student's text in the classroom. Introductory works are not processed and decided not to be issued.
In the envelope along with your notebook you need to include a piece of paper measuring 14x6 cm with your postal address (we will stick it on the envelope when we send you a response).
Glue a piece of checkered paper onto the cover of the notebook, drawing and filling it according to the following pattern (otherwise the work will not be checked):
Region
Last name, first name
Year of birth
Class
School (full name)
Last name, first name, patronymic teacher of mathematics
Place of work and position of the parent
Full postal address
