Curious record

Mrs. Drew, a housewife from the American city of Waterloo, spent five years and 2,473 liters trying to retype all the numbers from ones to a million on a machine. And she was prompted to this by the statement of her son, at that time still a schoolboy, who claimed, referring to the words of the teacher, that not a single person in the world had ever counted up to a million, in hours from units.

Determine whether Mrs. Drew typed the numbers on one or both sides of the sheet (a standard typewritten page has 30 lines of 60 characters each; between each length there is a comma and a space, which takes two characters).

Geometric problem

The four points A, B, C and D do not lie in the same plane. The points K∈[AB], L∈[BC], M∈[CD] and N∈[DA] divide these segments in the relations

$$\frac{|AK|}{|KB|} = \alpha, \frac{|BL|}{|LC|} = \beta,$$

$$\frac{|CM|}{|MD|} = \lambda, \frac{|DN|}{|NA|} = \gamma.$$

Find what condition the numbers α, β, γ and N must satisfy in order for the points K, L, M and N to lie in the same plane.

Form and solve a similar problem for three points on a plane.
