⟦folio 60⟧

In 1976, our magazine will continue the traditional section “Applicant's Practicum”. Editorial mail, meetings with readers «Kvant» indicate that the “Applicant's Workshop” arouses great interest among schoolchildren, teachers, students and even ... parents, whose specialty is far from these sciences.

It’s quite natural: questions about what tasks are offered to applicants, how best to organize preparation for admission exams, concern many. The editors «Kvant» will be able to answer such questions whenever possible. We will publish articles on various topics of the entrance examination syllabus; in these articles, the presentation of theoretical material is accompanied by an analysis of tasks taken from the practice of admission exams. The pages of the magazine will contain the texts of the options offered last year to those entering various countries in the country. Readers will see for themselves that to solve the problems of the entrance exams, it is enough to have a good and deep knowledge of the general school courses in physics and mathematics.

The authors of a number of letters to the editor are interested in how the program of entrance exams is changing in connection with the transition to new programs in mathematics and physics at school.

In 1976, the programs for entrance exams in mathematics and physics did not change - they completely correspond to the old high school courses.

It is no secret that some applicants can easily prepare for exams and pass the competition with the help of tutors. However, experience shows that applicants “trained” by tutors often receive only formal deficiencies in their knowledge, and this is quite easily revealed in entrance exams. The key to true success is systematic and regular independent work. Anyone who really wants to become a student will achieve this if, without wasting time, they begin to actually repeat the theory and actively train in solving problems. We hope that the Applicant's Workshop will be a good advisor to applicants.

When preparing for entrance exams, it will be useful to get acquainted with those materials from the “Applicant's Workshop” that have already been published in the magazine. Thematically, a list of articles for applicants, published in 1970-1973, can be found in «Kvant» No. 1, 1974, p. 52. Below is a list of such articles published in 1974 and 1975.
