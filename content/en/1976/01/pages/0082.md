Ticket 2

3. Let us denote by $p_1$ and $p_2$ the air pressure in parts of the cylinder with volumes $V_1$ and $V_2$, respectively. Let us write the equation of motion of the piston:

$$m\omega^2 r = p_2 S - p_1 S$$

(1)

($\omega^2 r$ - centripetal acceleration, $p_2 S$ and $p_1 S$ - air pressure forces on the piston). According to the Boyle-Mariotte law

$$p_1 V_1 = p_0 V_0$$ and $$p_2 V_2 = p_0 V_0.$$

(2)

From equalities (1), (2) and the obvious correspondence $V_1 + V_2 = 2V_0$ we find

$$V_1 / V_2 = \frac{m\omega^2 r}{p_0 S} + \sqrt{\left(\frac{m\omega^2 r}{p_0 S}\right)^2 + 1} = 1,28.$$

Ticket 3

3. A system removed from a position of stable equilibrium experiences the braking effect of the forces of the power plant field. Therefore, it will turn until the change in the kinetic energy of the system becomes equal to the work of the power plant forces:

$$-2 \left(mv_0^2 / 2\right) = A.$$

(1)

The work $A_1$ performed by the field, let us direct, on a positively charged ball is equal to $A_1 = q \left(\varphi_0 - \varphi_1\right)$, where $\varphi_0$ and $\varphi_1$ are the field potentials in the initial and final positions of the ball (see Fig. 3, where the blue lines show the equivalent surfaces of a uniform electric field). For a homogeneous field $\varphi_0 - \varphi_1 = -Ed = -E (l / 2)(1 - \cos \alpha)$. Then $A_1 = -qEl (1 - \cos \alpha) / 2$. The field will do the same work on a negatively charged ball. Hence,

$$A = 2A_1 = -qEl (1 - \cos \alpha).$$

(2)

From (1) and (2)

$$\cos \alpha = 1 - \frac{mv_0^2}{qEl} = 0,5.$$

Hence $\alpha = 60^\circ$.

To the problem “Prisoners of King Dadon”

(cm. c. 18)

Those chambers in which the key is turned an odd number of times will be open, that is, those to which an odd number of the descendants of King Dadon approached, or, in other words, the number of which has an odd number of divisors.

An odd number divisor has only exact squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100 (for non-squares x, each divisor a corresponds to a divisor x/a), which is why King Dadon released ten captives.
