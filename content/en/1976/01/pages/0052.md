F373*. Two billard balls, one of which is initially buried, experience an elastic “oblique” collision. The line passing through the centers of the balls upon collision makes an angle $60^\circ$ with the direction of the initial movement of the flying ball. During the collision, the balls are deformed, and part of the kinetic energy of the ball is transferred into the potential energy of elastic deformation of the balls, which, when the balls come apart, again transforms into kinetic energy. Determine the maximum number of parts of the energy of the balls that transforms into the energy of elastic deformation during the impact. The balls are considered to be completely smooth.

F374*. At the bottom of a glass of water there are several capillary tubes sealed at one end and filled with air. What is the temperature of the water at the bottom of the glass if the atmospheric pressure is $10^5$ k/$m^2$? Take the coefficient of surface tension of water equal to 57 dynes/cm. Assume that the pressure of saturated water vapor near 100°C increases by 27 mm rm. see when the temperature rises by 1°.
