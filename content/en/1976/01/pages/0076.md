How to draw a five-pointed star?

A regular five-pointed star is beautiful, isn’t it? We see it on the coat of arms and flag of our Motherland, it shines on military caps and on the spiers of the Kremlin towers.

Who didn’t try to draw this star as a child? Probably everyone. But the result was something lopsided and awkward, something like figure 1.

First try
At school you were introduced to tools for geometric constructions - compasses and rulers.

Let's try to resume our attempts to draw the correct five-pointed star. First we ask ourselves, what does correct mean? Probably, it is necessary that the five vertices be connected by segments of equal length. The ruler will allow us to make the sides of the star straight, and with the help of a compass we will try to make them equal.

Let's get started. We construct an angle with vertex A. On its sides we lay equal segments AB and AC (Fig. 2). There are three peaks. Just two more to find and everything is fine. Similarly, we draw an arc (3) of a circle of the same radius with a center at point C. The second of the remaining vertices (E) should lie on it.
