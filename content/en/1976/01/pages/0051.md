⟦folio 49⟧

Quantum problem book

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published here are non-standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the current school curriculum. Many tasks will be understandable to students in grades 7-8. Along with relatively easy problems, there are also those that are not easy for specialists - mathematicians and physicists - to solve. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. If you can’t quickly cope with a task, don’t give up, try to return to it again - in a day, in a week. If a problem interests you, then do not put it off even after a solution has been found - think about how to write down the solution in the most diverse and convincing way, how you can generalize the problem, clarify or strengthen its result, what problems you have encountered that are close to it. We will be glad to receive solutions to the problem and messages related to them from readers.

In the second part of the section “Problem Book «Kvant»” we post solutions to problems (about six months after the publication of the conditions) taking into account the questions sent by readers. Essentially, solutions to problems are small notes, and we advise those who did not receive «Kvant» or did not solve problems to get acquainted with them. In odd numbers «Kvant» (Nos. 1, 3, ...) we present the names of the readers who sent us the correct solutions to the problems. Schoolchildren who regularly submit interesting and complete solutions throughout the year receive an invitation to Resource Olympiads in mathematics or physics (these Olympiads usually take place during spring break). In addition, the editors establish several special premii for the winners of our ongoing problem-solving competition.

Of course, not all of our problems are published for the first time. After formation, we usually indicate who proposed this task to us. Coming up with a new original problem - making a small independent “discovery” - is even more difficult than solving a ready-made cast iron problem. If you succeed, send us the problem along with the solution. Write how your problem arose: have you encountered similar problems or solution ideas, what general phenomena or mathematical theorems is associated with your new result. We will publish the most beautiful and interesting problems in the “Problem Book” or other sections «Kvant».

Our address: 113035, Moscow, Zh-35, st. B. Ordynka, 21/16, editorial office of the magazine «Kvant».
On the envelope after the address, write the solutions to which problems you are sending (for example: “Problem M361, M362” or “F373” or “a new problem in mathematics (in physics)”). Send solutions to problems for each subject (mathematics and physics), as well as new problems for each subject in separate envelopes. The formulation of new tasks (as well as any other materials) intended for publication in the journal) must be sent in two copies. We receive a lot of letters from readers and try to answer all our regular correspondents. We would like to make it very easy for you to format solutions carefully in order to facilitate the work of the editors and consultants of the “Problem Book «Kvant»” section. At the beginning of the letter, I work out the spelling of my last name, first name, patronymic, home address, as well as the class and school where you are a teacher (and your “number” if you are a VZMSH student). Enter the letter with your address written on it (for a response after checking the solutions). We will be able to read letters from readers only if they are written in Russian and sent no later than a month and a half after the publication of the corresponding issue. The deadline for sending answers to problems in this issue is March 1, 1976.
