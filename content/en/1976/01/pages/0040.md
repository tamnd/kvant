For a thorough study in such cases, spectral analysis is used. Each chemical element has its own and unique emission spectrum and, accordingly, absorption spectrum. If a piece of lead from which the dobe was made is thrown into the flame of, for example, an electric arc, then in the spectrocopto it will be possible to see “traces” of impurities in the form of corresponding black lines against the background of a continuous spectrum of arc radiation. By the “intensity” of these black lines one can judge the amount of each impurity.
