Let us denote by $v$ the speed of the rod, and by $v_1$ the speed of water moving between the rod and the walls of the tube at the moment when the rod rose to a height $h$ from the level at which its speed was equal to zero. Equating the volume $\pi r^2 v \Delta t$ of liquid displaced by the rod in a short period of time $\Delta t$, the volume $\pi (R^2 - r^2) v_1 \Delta t$ of liquid passed between the rod and the tube during the same time is found

$$v_1 = \frac{r^2}{R^2 - r^2} v.$$

During the time while the rod rises to a height of $h$, a mass of liquid equal to $V\rho (V = \pi r^2 l - объем стержня),$ will also fall by $h$, then the decrease in the potential energy of the rod and liquid is equal to $V\rho g h - m g h$. The kinetic energy of the system is equal to $\frac{m v^2}{2} + \frac{m_1 v_1^2}{2}$, where $m_1$ is the mass of the moving fluid. Write the kinetic energy of a liquid in this form:

$$\frac{m_1 v_1^2}{2} = \frac{\pi (R^2 - r^2) l \rho}{2} v_1^2 = \frac{V \rho r^2}{2 (R^2 - r^2)} v^2 = \frac{m' v^2}{2},$$

where $m' = \frac{V \rho r^2}{R^2 - r^2}$.

Using the law of conservation of energy, we get

$$V \rho g h - m g h = \frac{m v^2}{2} + \frac{m' v^2}{2},$$

where

$$v^2 = 2 \frac{V \rho - m}{m + m'} g h.$$
