⟦folio 73⟧

Quests

1. The Ododo language has only two sounds: “D” and “O”. Two words mean the same thing if one is obtained from the other using a certain number of the following operations: skipping consecutive sounds DO or OODD and adding OD to any place. Do the words ODD and DOO mean the same thing?

2. Two copper tubes are lowered into the water to great depths. One tube is sealed at both ends, and the other has one end open. What will happen to the pipes at depth?

3. Restore the division notation in which some of the “escaped” digits are replaced with asterisks.

4. It is known that the length of the shadow cast by an object changes throughout the day. The shortest shadow is at noon; in the evening the shadow “grows”.
Is there a place on Earth where the length of the shadow does not change during the day?

5. The game “Who will be the first to name the number 100” involves two players. One calls any integer from 1 to 9 that he likes and names the amount. To this sum, the first one again adds any integer from 1 to 9 and names the new sum, etc. The one who first names the number 100 wins. In this game, the beginner always loses if only his opponent reveals one secret. What is the secret, the key of the game, which ensures victory for the second player?
