5. Is the fraction $\frac{14m+3}{21m+4}$ cancelable, where m is an integer?

7. In a right-angled triangle, find the ratio of the lengths of the legs if the lengths of the altitude and the median extending from the right angle are in the ratio 40:41.

8. The sum of terms and the difference of programming arguments are positive. If you increase the difference of the program by 2 without changing its first term, then the sum of its terms will increase by 3 times. If the difference of the original program increases by 4 times without changing the first term, then the sum of the terms of the program will increase by 5 times. 9. In equilateral triangle ABC ($|AB|=|BC|$), a circle is constructed at the height BD as a diameter. 10. The bus from point A is automated and moves non-stop at constant speeds between A and B.
