⟦folio 65⟧

Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

We spoke in detail about the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Engineering Physics Institute in «Kvant» No. 1 for 1973. In this issue we present options for entrance exams in mathematics and tickets for exams in physics at MEPhI in 1975.

Mathematics
Option 1
1. Three skaters cover the distance, moving evenly. Through $m$ minutes after the start of a third of the distance, he has only a fraction of the distance left that the first distance can cover in $n$ minutes, and the second - in $p$ minutes. Determine how many minutes it will take to cover the total distance of each of the skiers, if the speed of the third skier is equal to the resulting speeds of the first and second.
2. In the triangular paramide $SABC$, all plane angles of triangular angles with vertices at points $A$ and $B$ are equal to $\alpha$, $AB=a$. Determine the volume of the paramid.
3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{3 \log_a x + 6}{\log_a^2 x + 2} > 1.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$2 \cos x \cdot (\cos x - \sqrt{8} \cdot \tg x) = 5.$$

Option 2
1. A boat descends downstream a river for a distance of 10 km, and then rises against the current for a distance of 6 km. The speed of the river is 1 km/h. Should the boat's own speed lie within each limit so that the entire trip takes from 3 to 4 hours?
2. Find the volume of a regular triangular paramid, knowing the radius $r$ of the ball inscribed in it and the angle of inclination $\alpha$ of its lateral face to the plane of the base.

Option 3
1. Water can enter the pool through two pipes of different capacities. A smaller pipe passes 1 $m^3$ of water in 1 second. The pool has a faucet through which 1 $m^3$ of water can flow out within a second. Find, within each limit, the throughput of the larger pipe can change so that in a time that does not change 3 $c$ and no more than 4 $c$, 16 $m^3$ enter the pool, and 10 $m^3$ enters with the simultaneous action of both pipes with the faucet closed, and 6 $m^3$ with the action of only the larger pipe and the faucet open.
2. A non-intersecting diagonal of two adjacent side faces of a rectangular parallelepiped inclined to the plane of its base at angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Find the angle between these diagonals.
3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{\lg^2 x - 3 \lg x + 2}{\sqrt{100-x}} \geq 0.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\sin^4 x - 2 \cos^2 x + a^2 = 0.$$
