⟦folio none⟧

This colorful geometric pattern is not the fruit of an artist’s imagination, but an accurate mathematical drawing depicting the so-called Brillusium zones. Each of the zones presented here is given a different color. Their shapes are different, but their areas are the same (they are equal to the area of ​​the central square). Where Brillusium zones come from and why they have this property can be found out in the article by A. B. Goncharov in this issue of the journal.
