F863. In a conceptual hole with an angle of 2a, a ball jumps at the top, elastically reflecting off the wall of the hole. The points of collision of the ball with the wall lie at the same height (Fig. 1); the time from impact to impact is constant and equal to $T$. What is the average catching speed of the ball around the axis of the hole if the maximum speed of the ball $v$ lies in the range from $\frac{1}{2}gT$ to $\frac{gT}{2\sin \alpha}$?

figure 2 shows: 1 - the plane in which the velocity vectors of the falling and reflected ball lie, 2 - the horizontal plane, 3 - the plane tangent to the surface of the hole at the point of impact. The time from impact to impact will remain constant only if plane 1 is perpendicular to plane 3, and the angle $\varphi$ - the angle between the ball's velocity vector immediately after the impact and the horizontal plane - is such that sin $\varphi = gT/2v$. In this case, cos $\gamma$ (γ is the positive of the angle between the projections of the ball velocities before and after the impact on the horizontal plane) can be determined by the formula

$$\cos \gamma = \frac{tg \varphi}{tg \alpha} = \left( tg^2 \alpha \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{g^2 T^2}{4v^2} \right) \right)^{-1/2}.$$
