Basics of electrodynamics

2. The field is created by two precise charges $q_1=4 \cdot 10^{-9}$ Kl and $q_2=9 \cdot 10^{-9}$ Kl. At what points is the directivity equal to zero if the distance between the charges is $l=2 \cdot 10^{-1}$ m?
