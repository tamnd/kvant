1. How much oxygen does a climber produce with each breath at an altitude where the air pressure is $p=50 \cdot 10^3$ Pa? The change in air temperature with altitude can be ignored.

2. How many degrees must the gas be heated at constant pressure so that its volume doubles compared to the volume at $0^\circ C$?

4. A cylinder with a volume of $V=1,0 \cdot 10^{-3}$ m³ contains gas. Find the density of the gas at normal pressure $p_0=101,3$ kPa and the experimental temperature.
