Summer camp-83
Grand opening of the 1983 summer camp in the village. Frunzenskoe.

The meeting of schoolchildren with MAH veterans is led by its chairman V. N. Kasatkin.

The lesson with schoolchildren is taught by MAH veteran, below Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences B.I. Fuchs.

Meeting of schoolchildren with scientists.

go Komsomol. Its president V.N. Kasatkin spoke very well about the importance of MAH for Crimean schoolchildren at a meeting of schoolchildren with scientists: “If a schoolchild in Crimea wants to try his hand at science or technology, then he knows where to go. Every higher educational institution in Simferopol, Sevastopol, and Kerch runs MAH training courses. There are MAH branches in many schools in the cities and region.”
This is the great achievement of The Seeker. Let us wish him further successful work.

A. N. Vilenkin
