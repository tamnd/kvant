This drawing, made by a computer using the C: Batalov program, shows a family of twenty cycles (they are broken by the scylicet plotter) and the corresponding zvolots (red curves). After reading the “Geometrical Page” in this issue of the magazine, you learned that blue tsiklovs are evolutes of red curves (since the curve along which an evolute is constructed will always be its involute).

The family of cycles is obtained as follows. A wheel of radius I rolls without slipping along the vertical axis to the right of it. On a straight line passing through the center of the wheel, 20 points are marked (in increments of 0.2 starting from the center). The trajectories of these points when the wheel moves will be our 20 cycles.
