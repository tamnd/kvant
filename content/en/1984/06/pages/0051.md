Problem 2. A ball of radius $r$ hangs on a thread of length $l$ and touches a vertically located electric motor shaft (Fig. 3). Shaft radius $R$. At what angular velocity will the ball stop pressing on the shaft?

Let us consider the forces acting on the ball at a low angular velocity while the ball is still exerting pressure on the shaft (see Fig. 3). It is acted upon by gravitational forces $m\vec{g}$, thread tension force $\vec{T}$ and shaft reaction forces $\vec{N}$. As the angular velocity increases, the centripetal acceleration of the ball must also increase, which is possible only by decreasing the modula of the reaction force $\vec{N}$, since the forces $m\vec{g}$ and $\vec{T}$ remain constant.
