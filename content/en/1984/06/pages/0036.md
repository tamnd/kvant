⟦folio 34⟧

F878. Two smooth irons forming angles α with the horizon are located in the same vertical plane (Fig. 1). From point A of one iron and from point B of another iron, two balls are simultaneously produced without initial velocities of initial forging. The time of movement to the horizontal plane of the first ball (from point A) is $t_1$, the second - $t_2$. How long after the balls started moving was the distance between them the smallest?

P. IIIkhaliev

F879. A car of mass M, mounted on springs with stiffness $k$, rolls along a horizontal section of track with a speed $v_0$. After passing a straight inclined section of track with a height difference H, making an angle α with the horizon (Fig. 2), the car acquires a speed of $v_x$. Determine the value of $v_x$, transforming by friction in the wheels. Consider that the period of oscillation of the varon on springs is much less than the time it takes to travel the inclined section of the track and much longer than the time it takes to travel the rounded section connecting the inclined and horizontal sections of the track; the springs “spring” only in the direction perpendicular to the plane of the rails.

I. P. Krylov

F880. The pliers consist of two identical parts, connected by an axis at point O (Fig. 3). What is the force acting on the axis if the free ends of the clamps are compressed with a force F? There is no train in the axle.

L. G. Markovich

F881. Two thermal processes are performed over the gas, heating it from the same state to the same final temperature. On the $p$-V-diagram, processes are depicted by straight lines (Fig. 4). In which process is more heat imparted to the gas?

A. I. Buzdin, S. S. Krotov

F882. In the diagram shown at figure 5, $R_1 = R_2 = R_3 = R$, $C_1 = C_2 = C$. Determine the magnitude of the current through the source with EMF $\beta$ a) at the first moment of time after closing the key K; 6) after a longer period of time.

S. K. Strokov
