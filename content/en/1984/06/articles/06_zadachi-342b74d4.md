Exercise

1. Write the numbers from 1 to 9 into the squares so that all nine of these numbers are used and the indicated equalities are satisfied.

2. A circle and a square have the same area. A square was hung into a circle, and a circle was hung into a square. What is greater: the area of ​​a square inscribed in a circle or the area of ​​a circle inscribed in a square?

3. What number should be placed along with the “?” in the sequence: 7, 17, 37, 77, ?, 317,...?

4. Find the key to the “gibberish letter” - a secret script previously used in Russia for diplomatic correspondence: “Paichik tyug t “kamashmlyutoy chmaroke” - kayponili, nimeryashveya sh Mollii izsya tsinsoraki-geltoy nemenilti.”

5. A vase is reflected in a large mirror. Rita moved the mirror a meter, and the reflection moved a meter. Zhenya moved the mirror a meter, and the reflection moved two meters. Lily moved the mirror a meter - the reflection did not move at all. Grisha didn’t touch the mirror, but the reflection moved his LOB by a meter. How do you explain all this?
