The task in pictures

Prince Vladimir welcomed three Bogatyrs to himself.

Which of you caught the Nightingale the Robber?

It's no good to tail. Therefore, we decided that each of us would give three speeches. He will tell the truth twice, and lie once. After that, decide for yourself who caught the Nightingale the Robber.

Alyosha Popovich did it.

It wasn't me who did it.

I have long wanted to accomplish a feat.

There are many brave warriors in Rus'.

I was in a different place at that time.

It wasn't me who did it.

Alyosha Popovich did it.

Ilya was in a different place at that time.

It's a difficult task, but I'll try to solve it.

I know where Nightingale the Robber lived.

Text by A. P. Savin, figure E. V. Nazarova
