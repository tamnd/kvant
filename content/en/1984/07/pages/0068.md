Here is a two-color stereo energetic (the so-called analyph), which should be viewed through two-color glasses or filters. The drawing consists of two parts: the polygon (top left) is drawn manually, and the complex “architectural-geometric composition” is built on the ALGRAF automated graphic system created by Yu. V. Kotov. The analysis should be viewed through filter glasses; For the right eye you need a blue filter, for the left - a red one. The magazine should lie on a horizontal surface in front of you. By repeating it, you need to find a position in which the objects will be perceived as standing “vertical”. If it is not possible to select light filters, and the picture is “double,” those interested can try to copy the drawings with paints selected with the appropriate available filter. More details about analyphs are described in the article by A. I. Klimanov in this issue of the magazine.
