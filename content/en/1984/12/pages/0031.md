In my opinion, the correct and completely clear conditions of the problem are already the beginning of the solution.

Sasha. Then let's continue with the solution. A person takes a breath, that is, draws in air,....

Alyosha. Wait. What does it mean to draw in air? A Bodox person does this: by tightening his muscles, he expands his chest, the volume of his lungs increases, and the air in them expands. As a result, the density of the air in the lungs and in the position communicating with it decreases, and its pressure becomes less than the pressure of the outside air. The air will be mixed from a place of higher pressure, that is, outside, to an area of ​​lower pressure, that is, into the lungs.

Sasha. And since a person, while inhaling, touches the surface of the tea with his lips, and touches it tightly, this is what will happen: atmospheric pressure acts on the entire surface of the tea, and on the surface adjacent to the person’s lips, the air pressure in the position of the mouth acts, and it is less than atmospheric pressure. Everything is clear! I told you that the reason for everything is atmospheric pressure! Again you say: wait?

Alyosha. I'll tell you. Why does atmospheric pressure, which we know acts on tea from top to bottom, cause tea to rise up?

Sasha. Because liquids and gases transmit pressure in all directions without change. This is Pascal's law.
