Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling students from eight-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1985/86 academic year.

The goal of this school is to help students study independently to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. That is why, when admitting to 3FTSH, preference is given to students living in rural areas and workers' settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the names of the circle leader and a changed list of circle members (by grade, indicating the final grades for the 3FTSH introductory task). All materials on organizing the circle should be sent to 3FTSH before May 25, 1985.

Students of 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The assignments contain theoretical material and analysis of typical problems and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 problems for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitive tasks at MIPT). The work of law students is checked by the 3FTSH or its filps, and the work of members of the circle is checked by its leader.

With Moscow students, I will be assessed in physics and mathematics (according to the 3FTS program) twice a week at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools). Recruitment into these groups will be determined either based on the results of completing the 3FTS introductory task, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics (for information, call 408-51-45).

Each student completes the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently. The work must be carefully rewritten (in Russian) into one school terrace.
