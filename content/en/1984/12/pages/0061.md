Experimental tour

Problem 1
We put together a diagram and indicate the conditions of the task. We connect an oscilloscope to the terminals of the circuit and to the capacitor and get two oscilloscopes shown in figure 10. From time $t_1$ to $t_2$ the capacitor is charged from the network, and from $t_2$ to $t_3$ it is discharged through a resistor. According to Ohm's law, for a circuit containing a charged capacitor and a resistor,

$$\frac{q}{c} + q'(t)R = 0,$$

where

$$\ln \frac{q}{q_0} = -\frac{t}{RC}, \quad q = q_0 e^{-\frac{t}{RC}}.$$
or $U = U_0 e^{-\frac{t}{RC}}$.

Let us denote the product RC by t and find its value from the oscilloscope:

$$u_{t_2} = 0,3 \text{ B}, \quad u_{t_3} = 0,1 \text{ B}, \quad t_3 - t_2 \approx 1,8 \cdot 10^{-3} \text{ c},$$
$$\tau_1 \approx 1,65 \cdot 10^{-3} \text{ c}.$$

Let's draw a graph of the dependence $u = u(t)$ on a semi-logarithmic scale (Fig. 11), from which it can be seen that from 3 to 5 ms the graph is a straight line (which means the dependence is truly exponential). From the graph we find

$$\tau_2 = \frac{1}{\text{tg} \alpha} \approx 1,55 \cdot 10^{-3} \text{ c}.$$
