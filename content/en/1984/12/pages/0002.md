15-th INTERNATIONAL PHYSICS OLYMPIAD
XV INTERNATIONAL PHYSICS OLYMPIAD

The artists who painted this poster allowed themselves a little courage. They symbolically hinted at the themes of the problems that the Olympiad participants had to solve.
The organizing committee, concerned about the “non-disclosure of Olympic secrets,” decided to distribute the poster only after the end of the official composition. All participants and guests of the Olympiad received it as a subenir. Was the organizing committee's fears justified? The answer to this question can be found in the article “XV International Physics Olympiad”.
