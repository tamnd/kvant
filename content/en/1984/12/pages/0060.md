Task 2

Let $A$ be the center of mass of the body in the unperturbed state (Fig. 7). Let's find its coordinates $x_0$ and $y_0$ at the initial moment of oscillation, which are equal to the amplitude value:

$$x_0 = \frac{\omega a(\delta L/2)(L/3)}{\omega aLh} = \frac{\delta L}{6h},$$

$$y_0 = \frac{\omega a(2/3\delta)(\delta L/4)}{\omega aLh} = \frac{\delta^2}{6h}.$$

(From here it is clear that $x_0 > y_0$.) During the process of oscillations, the coordinates of the center of mass change according to the law $x = x_0 \cos \omega l$, $y = y_0 \cos \omega l$, where $\omega$ is the oscillation frequency.

According to the law of conservation of energy, the initial reserve of potential energy of the system is equal to the reserve of critical energy when the system passes through the equilibrium position:

$$\omega g \frac{\delta^2 L}{6} = \frac{\omega aLh}{2} \left( (x'(t))^2 + (y'(t))^2 \right),$$

or

$$\frac{g \delta^2}{3h} = \omega^2 \left( \frac{\delta^2 L^2}{36h^2} + \frac{\delta^4}{36h^2} \right).$$

Taking the second term in comparison with the first on the right side of the equality, we obtain

$$\omega = 2 \frac{\sqrt{3gh}}{L},$$

where does the oscillation period come from?

$$T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{\pi L}{\sqrt{3gh}}.$$

This formula can be verified by comparing the values ​​of $T_{\text{вычч}}$ calculated from it with the values ​​of $T_{\text{экс}}$ given in the table. It is easy to notice that $T_{\text{экс}} = 1.1 T_{\text{вычч}}$. For an estimate of $T_{\text{вычч}} \approx 3$ hours, therefore, the scale unit in the diagram corresponds to approximately 2 hours. Other solutions to this problem have been proposed. For example, assuming that a standing wave has formed and knowing the wave distribution speed $v = \sqrt{gh}$, we obtain $T = 2L/\sqrt{gh}$.

It was also possible to imagine the entire reservoir as a large number of communicating vessels, in each of which oscillations occur with a period of $T = 2L/\sqrt{gh}$. Etty, the solutions proposed by the Soviet participants in the Olympiad were evaluated equally with the official solution.
