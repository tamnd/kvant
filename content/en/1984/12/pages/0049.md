⟦folio 47⟧

Great Britain, Islamia and Norway took part in the Olympiad. The French team did not come from the traditional participants.

According to the existing international status of the Physical Olympiad, each country can send a team of five schoolchildren. The winners of the XVII All-Union Olympiad, who performed well at the winter training camp in 1983 and successfully performed at the XVIII All-Union Olympiad, were named candidates for the USSR team. Thus, seven schoolchildren, high school graduates, were invited to the final summer camps:

Alexander Abanov - s.sh. No. 170 Krasnodar,
Anton Alekseev - s.sh. No. 239 Leningrad,
Alexander Deshkovsky - s.sh. No. 11 Baranovichi,
Lev Zakrevsky - N.S. No. 50 Minsk,
Igor Kurinikov - s.sh. No. 1, Gatchina,
Sergey Orlov - s.sh. No. 842 Moscow,
Igor Poteryayko - Physics School No. 2 in Kyiv.

According to established tradition, all schoolchildren invited to the training camp are exempt from final school exams and receive the right to enter any university in the USSR without entrance exams.

The final training camps included theoretical classes, laboratory work, and four internal olympids. Based on the results of these training camps, the Co-team
of the Soviet Union, the XV International Physical Olympid included: A. Alekseev, A. Deshkovsky, L. Zakrevsky, S. Orlov and I. Poteryayko. The head of the delegation was a senior researcher at the Research Institute of Content and Teaching Methods of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences, O. F. Kabardin, and the pedagogical leader of the team was a senior researcher at the same institute, V. A. Orlov.

The organization and holding of the XV International Physics Olympid was entrusted to the Swedish Physical Society and the Swedish Ministry of Education. The chairman of the Swedish Physical Society, Professor Bendt Svensson, a specialist in the field of particle physics and cosmology, was appointed chairman of the organizing committee. Lars Silverberg, a specialist in the field of nuclear physics, was appointed Secretary General of the Olympiad.

Scientists from the University of Stockholm were involved in preparing the tasks and conducting the theoretical type. Preparation and implementation of the experimental type were entrusted to the National Higher Political School of Stockholm. The approval of all the results of the Olympiad and changes in the right to hold it were carried out by the International Commission, which, along with the chairman of the organizing committee and the secretary general, included representatives from each participating country - the head of the delegation and the pedagogical leader of the team. Observers from Great Britain, Hungary, and others also took part in all discussions (without voting rights).
