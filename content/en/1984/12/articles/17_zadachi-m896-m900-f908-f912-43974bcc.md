M896 — M900; F908 — F912
M896. For the convex quadrilateral ABCD, it is known that a circle with diameter AB is tangent to the straight line CD. Prove that a circle with diameter CD touches line AB if and only if lines BC and AD are parallel.

M897. Find at least one pair of integers (x, y) such that the number $(x+y)^7-x^7-y^7$ is divisible by 7, but the number $(x+y)xy$ is not divisible by 7.

M898*. For odd natural numbers $a<b<c<d$ the following conditions are met: $ad=bc; a+d=2^k$ and $b+c=2^m$, where $k$ and $m$ are non-toric natural numbers. Prove that a) $a=1$; b) for each $m \geqslant 3$ there is, and only one, set of numbers $a, b, c, d, k$ that satisfies these conditions.

M899. Let's call the environment of a non-integer number $x$ replacing it with one of the two nearest integers ([x] or [x]+1).
a) Prove that in any equality $x_1+x_2+\ldots+x_m=y_1+y_2+\ldots+y_n$ all non-integer terms can be rounded so that the equality remains true.
b) A table of $m$ rows and $n$ rows contains non-toric numbers, and their sums over rows $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_m$ and over rows $b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ are integers. Prove that all non-integer numbers in the table can be rounded so that the row and row sums are rounded off by the corresponding sums $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_m, b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$.

A. P. Savin

M900. Is it possible for the projection onto the plane of a convex polyhedron with 6 faces to be a) an octagon? b) a 9-gon?
b)* What is the largest number of sides that the projection of a convex polyhedron with $n$ faces can have?
M. D. Kovalev

After what time does the ring begin to slip relative to the cylinder, if the coefficient of friction between the ring and the cylinder is $\mu$? What will be the angular velocity of the ring?

F912. A direct current is created in a superconducting wire coil by connecting it to a current source through a switch $K_1$ (Fig. 3). After this, a battery of two identical capacitors connected in parallel is connected to the ends of the coil. The key $K_1$ is opened, after which the current begins to flow through the capacitors, charging them. At the moment of time when the voltage on the capacitors is maximum and the current is zero, the double switch $K_2$ is transferred from position 1 to position 2, so that the capacitors are connected in series, and the voltage on the coil doubles. Since the indicativeness of the coil has not changed, but the voltage has increased, the maximum current through the coil, and therefore the stored energy, will be greater than the first equal ones. Find the error in the above reasoning.

D. V. Pavlov
