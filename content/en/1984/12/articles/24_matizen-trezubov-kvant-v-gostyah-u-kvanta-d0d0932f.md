QUANTUM SMILES

«Kvant» visiting «Kvant»

For fifteen years, the student club «Kvant» has been operating at the Faculty of Physics of the Novosibirsk State University.
This year, many club members reached our magazine, and we invite readers to take a traditional walk with the club «Kvant» through the e-neighborhood of NSU - the Novosibirsk Scientific Center.

Our journey begins in the main building of NSU. Here comes student Ivanov, whistling Newton's third law. More recently, Ivanov increased the fame of the faculty by zero. He just doesn’t know WHEREOF! He doesn’t know algebra, he doesn’t know analysis, he doesn’t know quantum mechanics...

On the third floor the door of the dean's office darkens. Students' interests are reflected here. Opposite the dock door is announced. “In the dean’s office, the special course “Methods of Reporting” begins. First lesson: “Sequential research method.” A good-natured conversation can be heard from behind the door: “I go to the exam, and there are students - cut them, don’t kill them!” Posted first. It is estimated that physics students have already taken 30% more tickets than were on the tables!

Dormitory. Physics students live here. Until then, students knew their dormitory like crazy. Teleron it sparkles with fresh paint. There's a crash coming from the windows - it's the physicists throwing out their stuff.

Let's turn to the student canteen. The jokes about her don't flow the same way as the cafeteria workers. Nevertheless, the cooks are unhappy: “No matter how you feed the student, he still comes to eat!” Let's look at the menu. Good news: a new dish has been prepared in the canteen - “We’ll wait until Monday” cutlets.
Our path leads to the NSU sports complex. Along the route with the lortu, student Zaletaev. Need it! Its time is a full 10 seconds less than the time of free fall from the same height!

We go out onto the avenue, where numerous research institutes are located. To the right along the course is the computer center. At the request of the shopping center workers, a new general computing machine is being developed here.

In the distance rises the building of the Institute of Nuclear Physics. A lot of interesting things happen here too. We read the announcements at the entrance. “To break down the expensive side of the device, laboratory No. 8 needs a student on time.” “Attention of stavers: installation No. 5 in the switch position LEFT is off, RIGHT is broken.”

On the door to the basement there is an inscription: “Functional Research Laboratory.” Below it is a new formula for the base of natural logarithms: e=mc². The most important achievement of the laboratory was the development of his principle of equivalence of time and energy: there is a mass of rest if and only if there is a mass of free time. We are leaving the Institute of Nuclear Physics. It smells burnt. These are internship students burning places behind them...

To the left is the Institute of Automation. Scientists at this institute have created a model of a parasitic robot. The work was a success - the model does not work.

Semiconductor Institute. The laser time marker proposed here allows you to determine the number of seconds in a minute with an accuracy of up to the seventh digit.

We go out onto Morskoy Avenue. Institute of Economics. In connection with inflation in the West, economists have proposed lowering the exchange rate of world constants by 15-20%.

The Academy cultural center has a large cultural program. Recently a telepathy session took place here. Telegate Natorny has been trying to read at least one thought on the face of student Vaskin for three and a half hours. Failed. Know ours!

Nearing the end of our journey. On the left is the post office and phone number. Here comes student Sidorov. He just reported to his parent: “Taking into physics is difficult. But we won’t!”
We return to NSU. During our absence, a telephone message came: “In connection with the filikaya at NSU, all students flew out of MSU.”
News will be allowed from the loudspeaker. “According to the USSR hydrometeorological center, in Akademgorodok there is a slight cooling at night, up to 2-3 degrees below absolute zero. Good night, dear comrades, we bid you farewell..."
The tour through the materials of the club «Kvant» was led by V. Matizen and O. Trezubov
