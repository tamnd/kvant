⟦folio 27⟧

Exercise

1. How many green cups must be placed on the left side of the scale so that the scale shown at figure is in balance?

2. On the checkered plane, three rays AB, AC and AD are drawn from point A (see figure). Prove that angle BAC is equal to angle CAD using the properties of a square mesh.

3. I recently found last year’s table of the hockey tournament between the b-min classes of our school. Only a small part of the records has been preserved on it. Try restoring the table.

4. The figure shown at figure needs to be divided into 6 equal parts, making cuts only along the grid lines. How many ways can you do this?

5. figure shows an electrical circuit with a bell, a light bulb timing and time buttons, each of which connects one pair of contacts in the free state, and another pair when pressed. The circuit is included. How will it work when you press one of the buttons?

These tasks were offered:
M. Koman (Czechoslovakia) and B. N. Gordeychik.
