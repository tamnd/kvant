Origami
(folding paper figures)
A. I. KLIMANOV
Folding figures of animals and birds from paper is an ancient art, perhaps most popular in Japan, where it is called “origami.” All figures are made from whole straight sheets of paper (one or two), without the help of scissors or glue (glue is used only for fastening “halves” of figures made up of two sheets). The procedure for making the figures is shown in the drawings.
The initial stage of work on the figures is shown in the drawings, indicated by numbers. Guided by the given drawings, make a model of animals and birds, then try to fold (without a drawing) a figurine of a giraffe (see photo on the 4th page of the cover). Models of a giraffe and a camel are made from two blanks of the same size. Once you master the “secrets” of modeling, you will learn how to compose your own compositions.
