Problem solutions

M861 — M865; F873 — F877

Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be the given numbers, $b_1=\{a_1\}, b_2=\{a_1+a_2\}, \dots, b_n=\{a_1+a_2+\dots+a_n\}$ ($\{x\}$ is the fractional part of $x$). All numbers $b_1, \dots, b_n$ lie in the interval $[0; 1]$. Let's divide it into $n+1$ equal intervals $\Delta_0=[0; 1/(n+1)]$, $\Delta_1=[1/(n+1); 2/(n+1)]$, ..., $\Delta_n=[n/(n+1); 1]$. If at least one of the numbers $b_i$ falls into one of the extreme intervals $\Delta_0$ or $\Delta_n$, then the statement of the problem will obviously be satisfied for the sum $a_1+\dots+a_i$. Otherwise, $n-1$ intervals $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_{n-1}$ will contain $n$ points $b_1, \dots, b_n$, so at least one of them will contain two points, $b_k$ and $b_l$. Let $k>l$, then $a_{l+1}+a_{l+2}+\dots+a_k=(a_1+\dots+a_k)-(a_1+\dots+a_l)=\epsilon$ is the number ($|b_k-b_l|<1/(n+1))$.

It is easy to see that the number $1/(n+1)$ cannot be reduced under conditions of reduction - it is enough to take all the numbers $1/(n+1)$.

S. Yu. Orevkov

M862. a) Inside the given surface of the triangle, indicate the set of all points $M$ such that the distance from $M$ to its side is equal to themselves.

b) Inside the given surface of the triangle, indicate the set of all points $M$ such that the distance from $M$ to its side is equal to themselves.

a) Answer: the required set is the interior of a triangle bounded by the linear averages of the given triangle (Fig. 1).

Let $d_a=d_1, d_b, d_c$ be the distance from point $M$ to side $BC, CA, AB$ given by 三角形. For the existence of a triangle with sides of length $d_a, d_b$ and $d_c$, it is necessary and sufficient to satisfy three inequalities: $d_a<d_b+d_c, d_b<d_a+d_c$ and $d_c<d_a+d_b$, or $d_a<d/2, d_b<d/2, d_c<d/2$, where $d=d_a+d_b+d_c$, but $d$ is the height of the triangle $ABC$ (if $l$ is the length of the side of the triangle, then $ld/2=(ld_a+ld_b+ld_c)/2=S_{MBC}+S_{MCA}+S_{MAB}=S_{ABC}$).
