A little about tennis

One hundred and ten years ago, retired English major W. Wingfield patented a game he invented called tennis. However, the roots of tennis go further back - to the French Middle Ages and even to the times of ancient Egypt. Nowadays, 120 million people play tennis in 193 countries (note that only 40 million play football) - this is one of the most useful, exciting, and profitable sports. In terms of the pace of development and growth in popularity, it has been surpassing all other sports for a number of years. Tennis, being an indispensable sport for people who successfully engage in mental work (especially mathematicians, physicists, chess players), can deliver not only physical, but also enormous intellectual satisfaction. No wonder they say that tennis is played with your hands, but you win with your head. According to one famous tennis player, for a high-class game you need three components: the endurance of a stayer, the speed of a sprinter and the rapid thinking of chess, playing freely in deep ceyton.
