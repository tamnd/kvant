About 1200 years ago, the greatest medieval scientist, astronomer and mathematician Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khorezmi was born.
The significance of his works is evidenced by the fact that the word “algebra” arose from the name of his main mathematical work, and the word “algorithm” is a modification of his name “al-Khorezmi”.
