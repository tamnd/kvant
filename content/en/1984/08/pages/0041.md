Tasks

1. What mistake did the artist make in the screensaver for the evening television program “Good night, boys”?

2. The height of the triangle is 2 times less than its base, and one of the angles at the base is 75°. Prove that the triangle is equilateral.

3. First-grader Natasha was given a homework assignment to express a given integer number of hours in seconds. She wrote the answer beautifully in her terradi and immediately closed it. Arriving at school, Natasha discovered that two numbers had spread into blots. On top of that, she also wrote down exactly what number of hours needed to be expressed in seconds. What numbers should be written along with the blots?

4. Slava borrowed a book from a friend for three days. On the first day he read half a kilo, on the second - a third of the remaining pages, and on the third day he read a number of pages equal to half the pages read on the first day. Did Slava manage to read the book in three days?

5. Solve a number puzzle

$(HE)^2 = SHE$

$(HE$ and $SHE$ are “he” and “she” in English).
