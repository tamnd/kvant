Particularly worth mentioning is the activity of Zhorni, which deals with the “mathematical support” of optimization. The main job of the zhorny is to select a problem and organize testing for optimization itself. If the second requires qualification, pedagogical skill is also added, then the first requires a lot of, imperceptible and disorderly work. It must be said that the Leningrad optimization work is based on the principles of “novelty” in the selection of tasks, that is, offering only those tasks that have not previously been proposed in any optimization (otherwise the participants find themselves in unequal conditions). In addition, the task must be elementary, that is, it must be possible to solve it within the scope of the school curriculum. This decision should, if possible, be based on original (for a schoolchild) ideas. They should be transparent enough so that its presentation on optimization does not take a lot of time. Finally, the most important thing is that the task must be beautiful. The meaning of this spitet is clear to anyone who has at least once received satisfaction from solving an optimization problem.
