The physical and mathematical tour, which was decided to be held daily, is a memorial to a man who lived by the affairs of youth.

The tour is held in three stages for students in grades 5-7 in Evpatoria. In the correspondence course, schoolchildren solve problems published in the city newspaper “Evpatoria Health Resort”.

The second stage is the perfection of the six-school team, which showed the best results in the first stage. Schoolchildren and other schools who showed the best results in the first stage will be invited to participate in this stage.

The third stage is a competition between the two best school teams based on the results of the first two stages.
