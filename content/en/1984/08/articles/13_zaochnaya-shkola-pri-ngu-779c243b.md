Correspondence school at NSU

At Novosibirsk State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol runs a correspondence school (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 of secondary schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students’ interest in the natural sciences.

There are 5 departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic. Students in grades 8-10 are admitted to the mathematics, physics and chemistry departments; for the biological department - grades 9-10; for the economics department - only 10th grade students.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh also accepts mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs, which can be organized at the school on the initiative of teachers. The leaders of each circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the corresponding subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the head of the circle informs 3SH of his last name, first name, patronymic and sends a changed list of members of the circle (indicating the final grades for the assignment period), signed by the school director and certified by the school seal.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional teaching material. The work of undergraduate students is checked in 3Sh, and members of circles are checked by its leaders. At the request of the leaders, the work of members of circles (or parts of them) can be checked in 3Sh.

The best students in grades 8-9 of 3Sh are invited to the Summer School at NSU, which runs from August 1 to August 22. Here they, together with the winners of the Vesibirsk Olympiad, attend lectures of prominent scientists, solve interesting problems at seminars, get acquainted with the university and institutes of the Academy Town, and find entertainment in an organized manner. During the winter holidays, 3Sh students from nearby regions are invited to the Winter School at NSU. Club leaders also take part in the work of these schools.

To become a student at the Correspondence School at NSU, you must send an application written on a postcard to the director of 3Sh by September 30 with a request to send the first assignment. The application must indicate

a) your last name, first name, patronymic;
b) the class in which you are a teacher;
b) department 3Sh where you want to study;
d) detailed home address with the obligatory indication of the post office code.

The head of the circle must send a letter to the director of 3Sh with a request to send the first assignment and teaching materials.

An application for admission to the physics or mathematics department of 3Sh can be sent along with the solution to the corresponding first assignment, published below, no later than October 15. For admission, it may be worthy of solving one or two problems well.

Please send only a simple parcel post. In your solution sheet, include a piece of paper measuring 6x10 cm with your address written on it (we will paste it on the envelope when we send the answer).

Our address: 630090, Novosibirsk, 90, st. Pirogova, 11, Correspondence school at NSU.

First physics assignment

Tasks 1-5 are for eighth graders, 5-11 for ninth graders, and 9-12 for tenth graders.

1. The hole in the wall of a cylindrical vessel with water is closed with a square-section plug on side a (Fig. 1). The upper edge of the plug is at a distance h from the water level in the vessel. Find the force with which the water pushes the cork. Density of water q.

2. Equal volumes of water and kerosene are poured into a U-shaped tube, so that the length of the container for each liquid is l = 10 cm. Find the difference in liquid levels. Density of water q_B=1000 kg/m³, kerosene q_K=800 kg/m³.

3. An electric stove operating from a network with a voltage of U₀=220 V consumes power P₀=600 W. What will be the power of the tile if the value is equal to the voltage U=127 V? The resistance of the tile can be considered constant.

4. A wire of length l has a resistance R. The ends of the wire are closed and the resulting spike is connected to the terminals so that one of the parts of the spike has length a. Find the circuit resistance between the terminals.

5. The hoop was hung on a nail driven into the wall and tilted to the side (in a vertical plane). At what point on the wall should another nail be driven in so that the hoop rests on it?
