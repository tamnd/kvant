Tasks

M876—M880; F888—F892

M876. On a circle tangent to the sides of an angle with vertex O, two diametrically opposite points A and B (different from the points of tangency) are selected. The tangent to the circle at point B intersects the sides of the angle at points C and D, and the line OA at point E (Fig. 1). Prove that the lengths of the segments BC and DE are equal.

A. S. Merkuryev

M877. From a sheet of checkered paper measuring 29 × 29 squares, 99 squares of 2 × 2 each were cut out. Prove that you can cut another square from it.

S. V. Fomin

M878. Prove that if the sum of the plane angles at the vertex of a paramid is greater than 180°, then each lateral edge of the paramid is less than the half-perimeter of its base.

Yu. I. Ionin, A. V. Smirnov

M879. For five integers a, b, c, d, e, it is known that the sums a+b+c+d+e and a²+b²+c²+d²+e² are divisible by an odd number p. Prove that the number a⁵+b⁵+c⁵+d⁵+e⁵ – 5abcde is also divisible by p.

S. V. Fomin

M880*. In the sequence 1, 0, 1, 0, 1, 0,... each term, starting from the seventh, is equal to the last digit to the sum of the six previous ones. Prove that this sequence does not contain six numbers in a row..., 0, 1, 0, 1, 0, 1,....

A. S. Merkuryev

F888. figure 2 shows a schematic diagram of a gravimeter - a device for measuring the Varnatzian acceleration of free fall g. In heat-conducting gas cylinders 1 and 2 pressures are p₁≈2·10⁴Pa and p₂≈3·10⁴Pa, respectively. Cylindrical vessels A and B with a radius of 10 cm and a tube connecting them are filled with rue. A very light liquid (toluene) is poured on top of the mercury; this liquid fills vessels A and B with cylinders 1 and 2. Describe the process of measuring the Varnatzian acceleration g using this device. What is the magnitude of the Varnatse g that can be registered?

What about this gravimeter, if it is known that with changes at different points, changes in the difference in horizontal displacements only in capillaries can reach several millimeters (up to 1 cm)? The temperature is kept constant during the change; density of mercury $\rho = 13,5 \times 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

Yu. M. Brook

F889. The sun is at angular height f above the horizon. At what angle to the surface of the Earth must a body be thrown in a vertical plane passing through the Sun so that the body travels the longest path along the Earth?

V. A. Nakhshin

F890. Iron crystals at temperatures up to 910 °C consist of cell elements in the shape of a cube with an edge length of $a_1 = 2,87 \cdot 10^{-10}$ m. Iron atoms are located at the vertices of the cube and in its center (Fig. 3). This type of element - with a body-centered cubic lattice - is called alpha iron ($Fe_a$). At temperatures above 910 °C, Kristalliza cells of iron are a face-centered cube with an edge length of $a_2 = 3,63 \cdot 10^{-10}$ m. Atoms are located at the vertices of the cube and at the centers of its faces (Fig. 4). This type of element is called gamma iron ($Fe_\gamma$). However, what is the density of iron in these states? The relative atomic mass of iron is $\mu = 55,847$.

I. M. Nizamov

F891. How will the electrical capacity of a conducting sphere change if a dent is made in it?

R. L. Enfidjyan

F892. A ray of light directed along the radius falls on a glass ball of radius $R$. At a distance $r$ from the center of the ball, light is scattered evenly in all directions by a small air bubble formed during the making of the ball. Determine the dependence of the fraction of light emerging from the ball on $r$. Stack refractive index $n$. Neglect light losses in the glass.

A. A. LanUDEC
