X-RAY
In «Kvant» No. 11, 1983, we looked at several examples on the topic of tying this combinational technique very often found in games. And today there is another technique in turn, which is called the through action of figures or, in other words, RENTZENOM.

Šandi - Pogač
(Hungary, 1963)
So far we can hear the motives for pinning - 1...C:e5 is not allowed, and after 1...f6 2.Qd5+ Npf8 Black has many threats, but he is ruined by pinning -3.Bb4! Strong move 1...Qd7, after 2.Qd4 Qe8 3.f4 Ce7! White is already dying due to pinning, but 3.Qd6 R:d6 4.J:d6 should lead to a draw. But this result does not eliminate Black. After 1...Q:h2+!! the enemy comes to surrender. The first impression is that there is some kind of misunderstanding on the board, because between the black queen on h2 and the bishop on d6 there is a white queen, that is, the black queen is not protected. But this is the idea of ​​penetrena - the rays emitted by the bishop pass through the white queen, and it is as if it does not exist for the bishop. After 2.Q:h2 B:h2+ 3.Kp:h2 R:d1 Black has a decisive material advantage.

The front f7 point turns out to be a weak point in Black's camp, and the x-ray combination allows us to reveal this weakness. Here is a classic example on this topic.

Botvinnik - Vidmar
(Nottigham, 1936)
White bishop and rook ascend to the f7 square through the knights. 1. Nxf7! R:f7 (1...Kp:f7 2.C:d5+ with a win) 2.C:f6 C:f6 (2...K:f6 3.J:f6! C:f6 4.Q:c8+) 3.J:d5 Qc6 4.J:d6!! Only now the bishop's aim on the f7 pawn has become visible, 4.Rs5? now it was losing due to the answer 4...C:d4+! 4...Qe8 5.J:d7. The blacks were happy.

Sabo - Doda
(Romania, 1969)
The queen on g5 fixed her gaze on the white king. Pawns that come across along the way don't count. 1...Kf3+! 2.gf K:f5 3.Qf2 (3.ef gf+ 4.Kpf2 Qg2+) 3...g3! 4.Qe1 (4.hg K:g3 5.Qg2 Qe3+ 6.Jf2 R:c3 with the threat of 7...Ke2+) 4...gh++ 5.Kpf2 (5.Kp:h2 Kpg7 and Rh8+) 5...Qg3+ 6.Kp2 Kd4+. The whites were happy.
