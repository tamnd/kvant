⟦folio 53⟧

New admission to the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes and vocational school students, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

To enter the school you need to complete a period of assignment.

Task 1. Study the article “Systems of Linear Equations” in this issue of the journal (see p. 24) and solve the following problems from this article: main problems - Nos. 1—6, 8, 9, 12, 14, 16, 18; additional tasks - No. 7, 10, 20, 21.

Solutions to the problem must be completed in Russian in a student's squared notebook. The tasks must go in the order in which they are given in the stat, first the condition, then the solution. You need to paste a piece of paper onto the cover of the terradi, draw it and fill it out according to the following pattern:

The deadline for sending assignment 1 is no later than May 5, 1984 (by postmark).

In order for the task to be read, you need to solve most of the main tasks. If you successfully complete this task, then starting in September 1984 you will receive all further tasks. It is expected that some of the assignments will be given in the magazine “Quant” (the magazine “Quant” is distributed only by subscription, and subscription is accepted in all post offices from any location).

The first task must be completed at the address: “119899, ​​Moscow, Moscow State University, VZMSH. We will accept you" or to the address of the corresponding file. Branches of the VZMSH at universities are available in the cities of Voronezh, Gomel, Donetsk, Dushanbe, Ivanovo, Izhevsk, Kazan, Krasnoyarsk, Kuibyshev, Makhachkala, Odessa, Rostov-on-Don, Sverdlovsk, Cheboksary, Chelyabinsk, Chernovtsy, Elista, Yaroslavl; branches at pedagogical institutes - in the cities of Abakan, Birsk, Blagoveshchensk, Bryansk, Vitebsk, Kirov, Leninabad, Lutsk, Magadan, Orel, Pavlodar, Slavyansk, Smolensk, Terpolol, Ulyanovsk, Uralsk, Tselinograd, Cherepovets, Chita, Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk; There is also a branch in Dubna at the Institute of Nuclear Research and a department at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys.

Students living in the North-West of the RSFSR (in Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions, Karelian Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic), in the Baltic resource unions and in Belarus (except Vitebsk and Gomel regions), must send assignments to the address: “193130, Leningrad, 8th Sovetskaya st., 3, ZMSH. Let's see."

Schoolchildren who did not have time or were unable to enroll in VZMSH have the opportunity to participate in the “Collective student of VZMSH” groups. Each such group is a school mathematical circle, working under the guidance of its own accounting program according to the VZMSH program. Admission to these groups is carried out until September 20, 1984 in two streams: for those who will begin studying in the 8th grade in September 1984, and for those who will begin studying in the 9th grade.

Admission to the “Collective Student” group is without competition. To organize a group, a statement from mathematics, leading the circle, indicating the list of students is sufficient. The application must be completed by the school principal and stamped. The work of the leaders of the group “Collective student of VZMSH” can be paid for by schools at the request of VZMSH as elective classes. The application should be sent to VZMSH.
