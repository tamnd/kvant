Two types of electricity

(From the history of physics)

Last year marked 250 years since the existence of two types of electricity first became known. In 1733, the French physicist Charles Francois Dufay (1698–1739) published articles in a French and English journal in which he described the results of his wholesale electrification of various bodies.

Glass and resin. From numerous and cleverly performed experiments, Du Fay concluded that there are two types of electricity. One electricity occurs when rubbing copal (fossil resin), antar (also fossil resin), wax, lye and polyhydrates substances. Another appears when rubbing glass, rock crystal, precious stones, six, etc. Poset Dufay called the first of them resin, and the second glass electricity. A body possessing any of the two types of electricity attracts light bodies (it is this property that has been designated by the word “electricity” since ancient times). The difference consists, as Dufay found out, in the fact that bodies charged with the same electricity (glass or resin) repel each other, but if one body is charged with glass and the other with resin electricity, then they mutually accept each other.

This is how the functions of electricity were established: the existence of two types of electricity and the existence of electric forces of cauterization and repulsion. Naturally, the question arose about how this or that electricity appears in naturalized bodies. At that time it was only possible to build agreements about this.

Franklin vibe. One such agreement was formulated in 1750 by the American physicist (as well as a well-known government and social activist, one of the leaders of the American colonies’ struggle for independence) Benjamin Franklin (1706-1790).

According to Franklin, each body contains a special electric substance (fluid, as they said then), something in the electric fluid repels each other, but is strongly attracted by the particles of the body, so that every body acts on the electric fluid like a sponge, drawing in the body (the particles of the electric fluid are much smaller than the particles of the body itself, otherwise they could not let the internal body through). But the presence of electrical fluid in the body does not make it narcissistic, if it is contained in the body in some, so to speak, normal quantity. When one body is rubbed by another, part of the electric liquid flows from one body to another, and then both bodies become nazlestriovannye. The body in which the electric liquid flowed and in which its excess is created in comparison with the normal amount becomes the owner of glass electricity. The second body, in which the electric liquid is less than the normal amount, is charged with resin electricity. However, Franklin gave these two types of electricity other variations.
