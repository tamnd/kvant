sewing Putting $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2} = v$, we rewrite
this system in the form
$$\begin{cases}
x - yv = a\sqrt{1 - v^2} \\
-xv + y = b\sqrt{1 - v^2}.
\end{cases}$$

Having solved it as a linear system of relative
$x, y$, we get
$$\begin{cases}
x = \frac{a + bv}{\sqrt{1 - v^2}} \\
y = \frac{b + av}{\sqrt{1 - v^2}}.
\end{cases}$$
