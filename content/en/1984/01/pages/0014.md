⟦folio 12⟧

Instruments with which Jollus-Curie was able to isolate radioactive elements.

Thorin worked with the young German physicist W. Gentner, who specialized in counters. Jollu... asked him to check the correctness of their action...

The next morning, Frederick and Irene found a note from Genter on their desk, in which he assured that the counter was in perfect order.”

So, when the source of α-particles was taken away, the alimine foil continued to study positrons! They were emitted for some time, while their number halved every three minutes, strictly following the law of radioactive decay, which Rutherford discovered for natural radioactive isotopes.

The reaction of interaction of α-particles with aliminium has now presented itself in a new way -

$$\begin{align*}
27Al + \frac{4}{2}\alpha &\rightarrow 30P + \frac{1}{0}n \\
30P &\xrightarrow{T_{1,2}=3\text{ min}} 30Si + e
\end{align*}$$

- and for the first time the words “artificial radioactivity” were uttered.

...Frédéric Jollu-Curie rises to the podium and continues Irene’s story:

“Having obtained the sources of the first results with the help of conclusions of a purely physical nature, we set out to support our hypotheses with the help of... chemical evidence of the implementation of transformations.”

Physicists temporarily turned into chemikus in order to highlight the formation

cast phosphorus from aluminum. This was not easy to do: after all, the half-life of phosphorus is short - only about three minutes. In addition, it is generally impossible to isolate several radioactive atoms. However, you can first add to them a significant amount of inactive phosphorus-31, which has exactly the same chemical properties, and then carry out the extraction. Phosphorus-30 atoms will follow the properties of their “brothers” - phosphorus-31 atoms.

Irradiated aluminum foil and a piece of red phosphorus were placed in a test tube with hydrochloric acid. Hydrogen was released from the test tube along with hydrogen phosphide, which were collected in another test tube. The entire operation was completed in a matter of minutes. Then the test tube with gases was brought to the counter, and it began to count positrons. Now it was possible to celebrate the victory!

Discovery conquers the world

“The number of currently known artificially obtained radionsotopes already exceeds the number of natural radioelements that are found in the earth’s crust,”

- said F. Jollus-Curie.
