⟦folio 39⟧

Quests

1. Sasha paid attention to the number of the automated vehicle that drove up to his house. Interesting! If you add the digits of the second number to the first number: 85+8+7, you get 100, and if you add the digits of the first number to the second number: 87+8+5, you also get 100. How many such numbers are there in total?

2. In the multiplication example, put the missing numbers together with asterisks.

3. Place the numbers from 1 to 12 in circles so that the sums of the numbers on each of the six straight lines, on each of the three circles and at the vertices of each of the three rhombuses are the same.

4. Vadik had 3 times less nuts than Kostya. If Vadik is given the same number of nuts, taking them from Kostya, then the two will have an equal share. How many nuts did Kostya have?

5. “The fire is half a mile away from us, and the wind is carrying it towards us with terrible speed...

- What is there fire!... In your opinion, is this fire?... Well, well done, let's get to work... Take hold of this low, limp grass and hold it out...

The old man walked to the opposite edge... and, choosing a bunch of the driest stems, put them on the shelf of his gun. They instantly burst into flames...

“Now,” said the old man, ... “you will see how fire fights fire!”

So in F. Cooper’s novel “Prairie” he describes one of the ways to fight a steppe fire - setting fire to the steppe from the opposite side. Try to explain what the secret of this method is.
