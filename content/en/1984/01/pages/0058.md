Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Find the domain of the function
$$f(x) = \log_2 \sin x + \sqrt{9x - 2x^2} - 4.$$
2. A point $M$ (2; 4) is given on the coordinate plane. Consider triangles in which two vertices are symmetrical to the relative axis $Oy$ and lie on the arc of the parabola $y = 3x^2$, identified by the condition $-1 \leq x \leq 1$, and the point $M$ is the midpoint of one of the sides. Among these triangles, the one with the largest area is selected. Find this area.
3. In the equilateral trapezoid $ABCD$ the angles at the base $AD$ are equal to $30^\circ$, the diagonal $AC$ is the bisector of the angle $BAD$. The bisector of the angle $BCD$ intersects the base $AD$ at the point $M$, and the segment $BM$ intersects the diagonal $AC$ at the point $N$. Find the area of ​​triangle $ANM$ if the area of ​​trapezoid $ABCD$ is equal to $(2 + \sqrt{3}) \text{ cm}^2$.
4. Find all solutions to the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 5 \sin x \cdot \sin y + \frac{5}{2} \cos x \cdot \cos y = 2 \\ \cos x \cdot \sin y - 2 \sin x \cdot \cos y = \frac{6}{5}, \end{cases}$$
satisfying conditions $0 \leq x \leq \pi, 0 \leq y \leq \pi$.
5. On the edge $SB$ of the paramid $SABC$ the points $D$ and $E$ are selected so that $|SD| = |DE| = 1, |BE| = 2$. Sections of the paramid by planes perpendicular to the edge $SB$ and passing through the points $D$ and $E$ have areas of 5 and 16, respectively, and the first of these sections is a triangle, one of the vertices of which divides the edge $SA$ in a ratio of 2:1, counting from the vertex $S$. Find the volume of the paramid.
