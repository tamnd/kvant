Petr Leonidovich Kapitsa

On April 8, 1984, at the age of 90, the largest physicist of our time, an outstanding organizer of Soviet science, a member of the Chairman of the USSR Academy of Sciences, director of the Institute of Physical Problems, twice Hero of Socialist Labor, one of the initiators of the creation of the journal «Kvant» and a member of its editorial board, Academician Pyotr Leonidovich Kapitsa, died.

P. L. Kapitsa made a major contribution to the development of the physics of magnetic phenomena, physics and technical temperatures, and physical electronics. He was a member of the London Corpole Society, a foreign member of the academies of sciences in the USA, Sweden, Denmark, Poland, India and a number of other countries, and a laureate of the USSR State Prizes and the Nobel Prize.

P. L. Kapitsa actively participated in the Pagush movement of scientists for peace. He passionately loved young people and was one of the founders of the Moscow Physico-Technical Institute. The bright memory of him will be preserved in the history of Soviet science.
