⟦folio 36⟧

Naughty Tasks

from the oral exam in mathematics

This note will present several simple problems taken from the practice of oral exams in mathematics. But the empty-headed person does not look down on them and will not sweep away those who solved them with mistakes. Deciding on an oral exam is not as easy as at home at a table: volition, unfamiliar examiners, pressure on the applicant, and responsibility often leads to haste, loss of the necessary prudence, and sometimes to irreparable mistakes “out of the blue.” To avoid them, you need to approach an easy question asked in an oral exam no less seriously than a difficult one. Don’t give up on calculations - by thinking a little in advance, you can avoid failure or simplify the course of the solution, thereby raising yourself in the examiner’s chapters.

In oral entrance exams, I like to give this problem: Solve the equation sin x = π/3.

Very often the applicant immediately writes

$$x = (-1)^k \arcsin \frac{\pi}{3} + k\pi, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z},$$

and in response to the question: “So how many decisions will he have?” - he answers, “Infinitely many.” And sometimes it continues the previous equality like this:

$$x = (-1)^k \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + k\pi, \quad k = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots,$$

(!)

Sitting at the table, the reader probably said a long time ago that π/3 > 1 and therefore this equation has no solutions. It is important to demonstrate this level of communication and composure in an exam environment. I hope that other simple tasks (with a little trick) from my exam arsenal will help you with this. I advise you to solve them yourself, then look at the commentary on them and only if necessary, look at the end of the magazine in the section “Answers, instructions, solutions.” If there are a lot of mistakes, practice - there are additional tasks for this at the end of the article.

Tasks
1. Solve the equation sin √x = -1.
2. Solve for x the inequality ax + b ≥ 0.
3. Solve the system

$$\begin{cases} |x - y| = x + y, \\ |x + y| = x - y. \end{cases}$$

4. Solve the inequality x sin 3x > 3x.
5. Solve the inequality √4 + 3x - x² > -2.
6. Solve for x the inequality

$$\frac{x^2 - 2x + 2^{|x|}}{x^2 - 1} < 0.$$

7. Which is greater: log₂ 3 or log₅ 8?
8. Graph the function

$$y = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x^n}{1+x^n}, \quad x \in [0; +\infty].$$

9. On the segment AB, as on the diameter, a semi-circuit is constructed. Points C and D divide the half-frame into three congested parts. Prove that the area adb of the figure F, bounded by the chords AC and AD and the duo CD, is equal to one third of the area of ​​the half-frame.
10. A four-barrel is given, three of which are blunt. Prove that the diagonal of a non-triangle containing the vertex of its acute line is greater than its other diagonals.
11. Calculate the sum of all vectors, the beginning of each of which lies in the center of a regular n-fold, and the ends are located at the vertices of this n-fold.
12. Is there a manifold with 25 edges?
13. At the base of the three-spoke paramda SABC lies a regular triangle ABC from side d, the lengths of the side edges SA, SB, SC are equal to a, b, with the corresponding one. On the side edges SA, SB and SC, points P, Q and R are respectively chosen so that |SP|:|SA| = |SQ|:|SB| = |RC|:|SC| = 1:2. In what relation does the PQR plane make the volume of the SABC paramda?

Comments on tasks
1. For some reason, many applicants say that this equation has no solutions, since the function y = √x does not take negative values. Solutions are easy to find.
2. “Answer” x ≥ b/a is incomplete!
3. Many applicants spend a long time drawing areas on the Oxy plane and looking for their intersections, but they often make mistakes. Take advantage of the symmetry of the system.
4. Here again the property |sin x| makes life easier. ≤ 1.
