3. Let $D$ be the point at which the bissertier of angle $A$ of triangle $ABC$ intersects its side $BC$, $|BC|=a$, $|AB|=c$, $|AC|=b$, $|BD|=x$, $|DC|=y$, $|AD|=l$. Prove that:

$$\frac{b}{c}=\frac{y}{x}$$,
6) $l^2=bc-xy$.
4. Simplify the expression
$$\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}+\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}}.$$
5. Which of the two numbers is greater:
a) $2^{300}$ or $3^{200}$; b) $2^{91}$ or $5^{35}$?
Ninth grade
6. In the math circle where eighth-grader Misha attends, more than 93% of the participants are ninth-graders. What is the smallest possible number of members of this circle?
7. How many planes are there that are equally distant from all the vertices of a tetrahedron (an arbitrary triangular pyramid)?

8. Let $\frac{a}{b}<\frac{c}{d}$, where $b>0$ and $d>0$. Prove that
$$\frac{a}{b}<\frac{a+c}{b+d}<\frac{c}{d}.$$
9. Data of planes $a$ and two points $A$ and $B$ on one side of this plane. Point on the plane $a$ such a point $C$ for which $|AC|+|CB|$ takes the smallest value.
10. At what angle is the parabola $y=x^2$ visible from point $M(0;-0,25)$?
Tenth grade
11. The sum of the squares of the plane numbers $a$ and $b$ is equal to one. Prove that $a^{10}+b^{10}<1$.
12. Let a sphere of radius $r$ be inscribed into a polygon of volume $V$ with a full surface $S$. Prove that $V=\frac{1}{3}Sr$.
13. A spatial quadrilateral (that is, not all of its vertices lie in the same plane) is described around a sphere. Prove that all four points of contact lie in the same plane.
14. It is known that the axial section of a straight circular cone has an area two times smaller than the maximum area of ​​all its sections passing through the top of the cone. Find the angle at the apex of the axial section of the cone.
