Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculties of mechanics and mathematics, physics, economics)
1. Solve the equation
$$1 + 2\sqrt{2} \sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = 2 \sin x.$$
2. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ among the solutions of the inequality
$$\log_2 (x - 100) - \log_2 \frac{|x - 101|}{(105 - x)} + \log_2 \frac{|x - 103|}{(105 - x)} > a$$
contains a single integer?
3. The distance between the centers of the two circles is 5r. One of the circles has a radius of $r$, the second - 7r. The chord of the larger circle touches the minor circle and is divided by the point of contact in a ratio of 1:6. Find the length of this chord.
4. Given a cube $ABCD'B'C'D'$ with a base $ABCD$ and side edges $AA'$, $BB'$, $CC'$ and $DD'$. Since $\alpha$ is perpendicular to the line $A'C'$, and the plane $\beta$ is parallel to the line $CD'$. What is the smallest possible angle between the planes $a$ and $b$?
5. The nine-digit integer $A$, written in decimal notation, is obtained from the integer $B$ by moving the last digit to the first place. Moreover, it is known that the number $B$ is coprime with the number 18 and $B > 22222222$. Find the most and least averages of numbers $A$ satisfying these conditions.
Two natural numbers are said to be coprime if they have no common divisors other than one.
Option 2
(faculties of geological-geophysical, natural sciences)
1. Solve the equation
$$\sin 2x \cos x = \tg 3x \sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) - \cos 2x \sin x.$$
2. In triangle $ABC$, the radius of the incircle is $\frac{10}{3}$, the cosine of angle $C$ is $\frac{5}{13}$, and the area of ​​the triangle is 60. Find the sides of the triangle.
3. Along a 400 m long running track from one old line, two runners (an advanced and a beginner) started simultaneously. The speed of the discharge is 200 m per minute more than the speed of the beginner for 20 seconds. During this time, the beginner overtook the discharger. What could be the speed of the discharger so that without remaining to stop, he could finish the distance first, if it is known that the speeds of the runners during movement are unchanged and constant?
4. At what values ​​of the parameter $a$ for any action number $x$ the inequality
$$\log_2 (a^2 - 2) [ (a^2 - 1)x^2 + 2x + 2] > 1?$$
5. Given a rectangular paralleliped $ABCDA'B'C'D'$, the base of which is a square $ABCD$ with a side of length 3, the side edges $AA'$, $BB'$, $CC'$ and $DD'$ have a length of 5. An equilateral triangle is located in space so that one of its vertices coincides with the vertex $C$ of the paralleliped, and the other two are decomposed into lines $BB'$ and $C'D'$, respectively. Find the length of the medina of this triangle.

Physics
Written exam
Faculty of Physics
Each option consists of five tasks of three types.
The first three tasks are computational, of varying difficulty: from standard ones to relatively complex ones, requiring ingenuity, deep knowledge, and the ability to understand an unprivileged or conventional physical situation.
The fourth task is an assessment task. To solve it, one must try to consider a physical phenomenon, formulate a simple (since only an estimate is needed) physical model of this phenomenon, select the results, numerical values ​​of physical quantities, and, finally, obtain a numerical result that more or less corresponds to reality. The text of the problem supports that the applicant can choose the physical quantities and their numerical values ​​necessary for solving the problem.
The fifth task is a demonstration task, in which you need to explain a physical phenomenon demonstrated in the classroom. Here it is important to try to highlight the main phenomenon and environment of various factors.
Five hours were given to solve the problem, starting from the end of the demonstration.
Below, after the text of each problem, the percentage of those who solved it is indicated in brackets.
