2. A body with a mass of $m = 2$ kg is pulled along a smooth horizontal surface with the help of a spring, which during movement is stretched by $\Delta l = 1$ cm. The stiffness of the spring is $k = 100$ H/m. Determine the acceleration with which the body is moving.
3. A body without an initial speed begins to slide along an inclined plane with a height of $h = 5$ m. The angle of inclination is $a = 30^\circ$, the friction coefficient is $μ = 0,05$. Find the time of movement of the body.
4. Find the force of a heavy weight acting on a body of mass $m = 12$ kg, raised above the Earth to a distance equal to the force of the Earth’s radius.
5. A ball with a mass of $m = 0,5$ kg, suspended on an undisturbed thread of length $l = 1$ m, flails in a vertical plane. Find the tension force of the thread at the moment when it forms an angle $α = 60^\circ$ with the vertical. At this moment the speed of the ball is $v = 1,5$ m/$c$.
6. A block of length $L$ and mass $M$ is nailed to the upper edge of the doxy, the length of which is $l$ and mass $m$ (see figure). The board is fixed at point $O$ and leaned against the wall at an angle $α$ to the base. At what horizontal force $F$, applied at a height $h$, will the doxy equilibrium not be disrupted if the wall is removed?
7. A body of mass $m$ rolls down from a mountain of height $h$ and is left behind. What work needs to be done to lift $m = 3000$ t of truss materials to a height of $h = 12$ m. What is the power consumed by the crane motor if the motor efficiency is $η = 60\%$?
9. A body with a mass of $m = 2$ kg and a volume of $V = 1000$ cm$^3$ is located in a lake at a depth of $h = 5$ m. What work must be done when it rises to a height of $H = 5$ m above the surface of the water?
