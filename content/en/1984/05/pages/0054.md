where the primes are placed at the value related to the state of the system after stretching the coil, $I_a$ is the amplitude value of the current (at the moment of stretching, the current is maximum).

The coil is circuited when the charge on the capacitor is maximum and, therefore, the current in the circuit is zero. Therefore, during shatininikai no work is done.

With low damping, the loss of energy due to the release of heat at the resistance is small compared to the total energy of the system, so we can assume that over the period the amplitude of the oscillations changes little, the current is almost sinusoidal. Then we can obtain the following estimate for energy losses for half a period:

$$\Delta W_- = I_a^2 R \frac{T}{2} = \frac{1}{2} I_a^2 R \pi \sqrt{LC},$$

where $I_a$ is the effective current value.

The oscillations will be undamped if $\Delta W_+ \geqslant \Delta W_-$, that is

$$\frac{LI_a^2}{2} \frac{\Delta l}{l} \geqslant \frac{1}{2} I_a^2 R \pi \sqrt{LC},$$

where

$$\frac{\Delta l}{l} \geqslant \pi R \sqrt{\frac{C}{L}}.$$

A. I. Kirkinsky

5. Let $n>2$ be a fixed natural number. View the series

$$f(z) = z^n + z^{n^2} + z^{n^3} + ...;$$

$$[f(z)]^2 = c_1z + c_2z^2 + c_3z^3 + ...$$

and show that the number of solutions $(k_1; k_2; k_3)$ of the Fermat equation $k_1^n + k_2^n = k_3^n$ is equal to $c_m$ for $m=k_3^n$.

6. Find $v_n$ — the number of different partitions of a convex $n$-gon into triangles disjoint by diagonals (for example, $v_5=5$, $v_6=9$).

Note. Put $v_2=v_3=1$ and show the recurrence relation

$$v_n = v_2v_{n-1} + v_3 \cdot v_{n-2} + ... + v_{n-1}v_n, n \geqslant 3.$$

Use this to prove the relation $(\alpha(z))^2 - \alpha(z) = z$, where $\alpha(z) = v_2z + v_3z^2 + v_4z^3 + ...$, find $\alpha(z)$ from there and differentiate.

Answer. $v_n = \frac{(2n-4)!}{(n-2)!(n-1)!}$. These numbers are called Catalan numbers.
