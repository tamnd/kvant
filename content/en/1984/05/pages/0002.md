Here is a photograph of three loaded particles recorded in a bubble chamber. Some tracks are single, crossing the entire field of the photograph and correspond to particles that enter the camera from the outside. Others are paired tracks (similar to rams of the genus), twisting in opposite directions. They begin at points inside the chamber and correspond to the structure of electron-polytron pairs during the collision of γ-quanta with the nuclei of atoms of the living substance filling the chamber. You can read about how the process of formation of an electron-polytron pair and the reverse process of annihilation of an electron and a positron occurs in the article “Physics 8, 9, 10”, published in this issue of the journal.
