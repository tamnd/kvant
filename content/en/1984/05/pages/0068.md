In September 1983, the world celebrated the 200th anniversary of the death of the great St. Petersburg mathematician Leonardo Euler (1707-1783). The specially created Euler Committee at the Academy of Sciences of the GDR held a scientific conference scheduled for this date with the participation of foreign mathematicians. For the opening of the conference, a commemorative medal was issued from medienschcode porcelain (see the image below), a stamp with a portrait of Euler and one of his most significant formulas e—k+f=2, envelopes with a facsimile of his signature and an embossed portrait were published, and special postal newspapers were organized. Such an envelope was sent to the editorial office by A. Ya. Halamizer, a participant in the conference dedicated to the memory of Euler.
