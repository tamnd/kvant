On the contrary, the suppression of dynamics and volition is manifested most strongly in another simple geometric figure - a spiral. A spiral is a flat line formed by a moving point, which moves away according to a certain law from the beginning of a ray that rotates uniformly around its origin. If the point moves away uniformly from the origin ($r=k\varphi$, where $r$ is the distance from the origin, and $\varphi$ is the angle of rotation of the ray), then an Archimedes spiral is obtained. If the point is removed according to the exponential law ($r=ke^{m\varphi}$, figure 1), then a logarithmic spiral is obtained. A special case of a logarithmic spiral is the golden spiral of interest to us: it is obtained if the beginning of the ray and the parameters $k$ and $m$ of the exponential law are chosen so that the spiral passes through three of the four vertices of each of the “golden rectangles” successively constructed on figure 1.

Problem. Find the parameters $k$ and $m$ for the golden spiral.
