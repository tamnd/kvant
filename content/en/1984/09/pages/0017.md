Even the ancients introduced the golden ratio into consideration - such a division (“section”) of a given segment of length $a$ into two unequal parts $x$ and $y$, $a=x+y$, $x>y$, in which the changing part relates to the larger one in the same way as the larger part relates to the whole, that is, $x:y=x:a$. From this definition it follows

$$\frac{a-x}{x} = \frac{x}{a}$$

or $x^2+ax-a^2=0$. Solving this quadratic equation and discarding the negative root, we find

$$\frac{x}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2} \approx 0,62.$$
