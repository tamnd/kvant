Consulting: world chess champion, international grandmaster A. E. Karpov. The page is run by E. Ya. Gik, Master of Sports of the USSR in chess, Candidate of Technical Sciences.

LAST WORKOUT

Before the fight for the chess crown, the challenger undergoes service match tests, and as a rule, turns out to be in better shape than the world champion. Therefore, before a match with a challenger, the champion also suffers from competing in high-ranking tournaments. This year, when the legend was still going on in Vilnius, A. Karpov went to the international compilation in Oslo, and from there to the super tournament in London. This was his last big practical training before the match with G. Kasparov (except for his participation in the match between the USSR national team and the world team, in which A. Karpov won against U. Andersson).

Apparently, the world champion successfully solved the problems of pre-match preparation - in both tours he won the first prize, demonstrating an interesting, creative game.
