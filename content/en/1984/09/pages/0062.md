6. "Light Spots" The lantern, consisting of a casing and a lamp frame (220 V, 150 W), is covered at the front with light-transmitting black paper. The length of the spiral of the light bulb is 3 cm, the distance from the spiral to the black paper is 6 cm. There is a screen at a great distance from the lamp (5 m). What will appear on the screen if holes with a diameter of 1 mm are punched in black paper? 2 mm? 3 mm? What happens if you punch a lot of holes?
