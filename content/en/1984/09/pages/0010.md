The use of superconducting magnets for these purposes, which create strong magnetic fields without the expenditure of enormous energy, is very valuable. Our country already operates the world’s first superconducting system for thermonuclear fusion installation “Tokamak-7” and has developed the “Tokamak-15” installation, which will accumulate magnetic energy of 600 million jouley. The creation of next-generation devices designed for higher energies is simply impossible without the use of superconductivity.

When studying solids, molecules and atoms, nuclei, it is necessary to create strong magnetic fields in small volumes, as well as very uniform magnetic fields. Superconducting magnets are not replaced in such cases and are now widely used in physical laboratories. Small, super-strong solenoids, complete with a cooling system, have become an industrial product, the production of which is increasingly expanding.

The energy of the future is not only about new energy sources. New efficient ways to store and transmit electricity need to be developed. Superconductors offer their services here too. Scientists at the University of Wisconsin (USA) have developed a project for an electricity storage system. A giant superconducting coil with a diameter of more than 100 meters will be installed in a special tunnel made in the mountains. It will use refrigeration units with liquid gel to maintain a temperature close to absolute zero. The undamped superconducting current in such a coil will store a gigantic energy of 4·10¹¹ J. The installation is planned to be created by 1987.

What about lossless energy transmission via superconducting cables? For now, one can only dream of power transmission lines that, “bathing” in liquid helium, would carry electricity without loss over vast distances. But superconductivity has not yet said the last word to Veda. It is quite possible that materials will appear that will become superconductors at the temperature of liquid nitrogen. And then everything will change immediately. Fantasy becomes reality. I would like to believe that Kwan’s readers will see this.

Traditional autumn holiday for young mathematicians

At the beginning of November, young mathematics lovers will once again gather in the host city of Adjara, Batumi. The traditional Batumi holiday is a long-time friend and honorer of “Kvan”: last year it celebrated its 15th anniversary. This small anniversary was marked by a record number of delegations (14), participants (more than 200), and reports at the conference (43). All the more credit goes to the organizers of the holiday, who were able to clearly think through and implement its entire program. Among them, it is necessary to note the workers of the Ministry of Education of the Adjara Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic (L. A. Chanturaya and V. M. Tsulukidze), the city of Batumi (A. V. Gotishvili), the Knowledge Society (D. U. Chkhendze), the Regional Council of Professionals (Sh. M. Okropiridze) and, of course, the main initiator and founder of this wonderful gathering of young mathematicians - Honored teacher of the Republic Medea Illarnovna Zhgenti.

Over 15 years, the residents of Batumi have developed strong traditions of holding their holiday and much of their experience can certainly be used in other cities, not only at conferences that bring together accountants from different places, but also at meetings of several schools in one city. This also applies to the conference itself, which occupies a central place, that is, the reports of schoolchildren and their services (including the jury members - mathematicians and teachers), and the evening of entertaining mathematics, and the competition for mathematical degrees and, of course, everyone’s favorite cheerful mathematical KVHa (the presenter of which, according to tradition, is the main “entertainer” of our magazine A.P. Savin).

The truly festive mood of the participants last year was facilitated by the magnificent sunny weather, which was not entirely usual for this time in Batumi. She adorned excursions to the famous botanical garden and business finance, the Novra demonstration, at which the guests really enjoyed the live paintings dedicated to the 200th anniversary of the Gerogreevsky tract - the union between Georgia and Russia, walks along the Primorsky boulevard.
