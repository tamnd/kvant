⟦folio 29⟧

Exercise

1. Is it possible in a 400-digit number 84198419...8419 to cross out several digits at the beginning and at the end so that the sum of the remaining digits is equal to 1984?

2. Place numbers that are not equal to 0 into the cells of a 4x4 table so that the sum of the numbers in the corners of each 2x2, 3x3 and 4x4 square is equal to 0.

3. The numbers in the series are 1 2...100. Two players take turns placing the +, - and × signs between them (the next sign can be placed on any free space). Prove that the player who makes the first (and therefore last) move can ensure that the final result is an odd number.

4. 175 Shaltayev is more expensive than 125 Boltaev, but cheaper than 126 Boltaev. Prove that there are not enough rubles to buy 3 Shaltai and 1 Boltai if each Shaltai and each Boltai costs an integer number of kopecks.

5. The links AB and CD of the hinged quadrilateral ABCD intersect at point O; in this case AB=CD and BC=AD. Prove that AO=CO and BO=DO.

These tasks were offered in grades 5-7 at the largest 50th Leningrad Olympiad. Their authors: A. G. Goldberg, S. L. Genkin, S. V. Fomin.
