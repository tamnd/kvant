⟦folio 56⟧

We recommend reading

Scientists and representatives of exact sciences around the world recently celebrated the 275th anniversary of the birth and 200th anniversary of the death of the 18th century genius, member of the St. Petersburg Academy of Sciences, Leonard Euler. 31 years of his scientific activity took place in St. Petersburg (1727-1741 and 1766-1783), 25 years in Berlin (1741-1766). In the fifth issue, on the fourth cover page, we included information about some events in memory of Euler.

Two small books about Euler have also been published. Professor A.P. Yushkevich briefly outlined the main events in Euler's life and his most important achievements in various fields of mathematics.

The first part of A. Yakovlev’s book represents a significant number of copies by the scientist; in the second part he talks about some mathematical problems and tasks that Euler deals with and that are understandable to students in grades IX-X.

Our readers can choose any of these books according to their background and interests.
1. A. P. Yushkevich. Leonardo Euler. M.: Knowledge, 1982.
2. A. Ya. Yakovlev. Leonardo Euler. M.: Education, 1983 (Series “People of Science” - a manual for students).
