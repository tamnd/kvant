QUANTUM IS FUCKING FUCKED

Cysinus hydropanic

(examiner notes)
The time for university entrance exams has passed. How much excitement and anxiety they caused. The mothers and fathers of those admitted were worried, their aunts and uncles were worried. The applicants themselves were worried about the exams. They were worried and... out of excitement they sometimes said things that later, remembering, they themselves smiled. But that comes later, after the exams. And during the exams, they wondered why the examiners frowned while answering their answers, and sometimes even put marks, seeing which, the examiners themselves frowned.
All sorts of answers can be heard in exams, especially entrance exams.

...When an applicant was asked during the exam: “What is your name?”, she quickly answered: “Can I think about it?” - “What is there to think? Tell me your name,” the examiner repeated the question, to which, after some thought, the answer followed: “You know, it seems we didn’t go through this.”
At the physics exam, after the examiner suggested moving on to the answer to the next question, the boy smartly said: “On to the question about the question of the next question.” In response to a request

During the chemistry exam, one applicant said that before entering the university he was... “an equipment agent,” and when I talk about gases, he called carbon dioxide... “angular.”
During entrance exams, some are so worried that together they say “hydroponics”: “Hydropanic”, together “cosine” - “kisine”, their annual plan turns into “annual”, and the posta stimulator turns into a “simulator” rosta. “The rosta simulator allows, using hydropanics, to complete the annual plan for collecting beaver crops on irrigated fields ahead of schedule,” the applicant said smartly during the exam.
During the answers to the entrance exams, new sciences were even born - “Mocrobiology”, “Boltany” and others.

Yes, all kinds of answers can be heard in exams, because any exams are a challenge, and entrance exams even more so. Therefore, one must approach exams seriously, without volition. From now on - no hydropanic! Boldly forward to the shtur kisinus!

F. M. Tsehovolsky
