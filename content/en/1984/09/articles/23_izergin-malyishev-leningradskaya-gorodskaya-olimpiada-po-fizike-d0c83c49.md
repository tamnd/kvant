Leningrad City Olympiad in Physics

The City Physics Olympiad for students in grades 8-10 is organized by the Faculty of Physics of Leningrad State University. A. A. Zhdanova and the Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren named after. A. A. Zhdanova. The Olympiad is held in three rounds - school (about 30 thousand students), district (about 5 thousand students) and city (600 students). The tasks of the regional and city tours are the same for all participants (the city tour is carried out in two stages - theoretical and experimental). Jorri consists of scientists and the best physics students of Leningrad State University.

The results of the Olympiad are summed up in team and individual competitions. From the winners in the individual competition, a Leningrad team is formed to participate in the All-Union School Olympiad.

Below are some problems proposed in 1983 and 1984 at the Leningrad Olympiads in Physics.

District tour
8th grade
1. A pipe, the cross-section of which is a regular hexagon on side l, is rolled so that the angular velocity relative to the axis of rotation is constant and equal to $\omega$. Draw the trajectory of the turbine center and find the average speed of its forward motion. There is no slippage.
2. Two vertical cylinders (Fig. 1) are placed at the bottom of an empty vessel with height H. The density of the materials around the cylinders is $e = 0,5 \text{ г/cm}^3$, the areas of their bases are $S_1 = 10 \text{ cm}^2$ and $S_2 = 5 \text{ cm}^2$, and their heights are $h_1 = h_2 = 1 \text{ cm}$. What happens to the cylinders if the vessel is filled to the brim with water? Atmospheric pressure $p_a = 1 \text{ atm}$.

3. Estimate what speed the tennis partners can convey to the ball when playing real tennis, if each player makes three hits and the maximum speed of the ball is $v_0$. How long will this take if the length of the table is $L$?

9th grade
1. You stand, without touching the ground, on a swing that is at rest (Fig. 2). Is it possible to rock them? If yes, then explain how this can be done and what force will give you, along with the swing, an impulse in the horizontal direction.
2. Two tell machines operate on cycles ABCDA and ABC'D'A corresponding (Fig. 3). Which machine is more efficient? In either case, the working fluid is one mole of an ideal monatomic gas.
3. A single conductor of limited dimensions has a charge $q > 0$. Prove that the charge density on it is non-negative.

10th grade
1. A plane mirror is located at a distance of 2F from the collecting line with a focal length F (Fig. 4). Construct an image of segment AB, find its distance L=F from the line.
2. The ball, fixed outward, performs a harmonious oscillation in the horizontal plane with an amplitude $x_m$. At what distance from the equilibrium position must the wall of the elastically reflecting ball be placed so that the period of oscillation is reduced by one third?
3. Harmonic oscillations with amplitude $U_m$ are excited in the LC circuit. At the moment when the capacitor is fully charged, its plate is quickly bent, doubling the capacitance. Will this affect the oscillation and how?

City tour
8th grade
1. The driving wheels of the right and left tracks of the tank rotate in the same direction with angular speeds $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ corresponding ($\omega_2 > \omega_1$). Find the radius of the circle along which the tank is moving and its angular velocity. Tank width l, driving wheel radius r. The middles of the tracks do not slip relative to the ground.
2. Two particles move along the same straight line. The graphs of the velocities of these particles are shown on figure 5. At time t=0, both particles were at the same point. Find the minimum distance between particles at times t≥T.
3. On an inclined plane with angle α, two identical one-sided cubes stand on top of each other. The friction coefficient between them is μ, and between the lower cube and the friction plane is very large. Angle α increases slowly. Describe what will happen to the cups (quantitatively if possible).
