In this picture, the artist managed to “turn inside out” the Rubik’s cube. To be more precise, it shows the inversion of a Rubik's Cube image relative to the black circle containing it. You can read about the inversion in «Kvant» No. 5 for this year on p. 26. I wrote about the Rubik’s cube «Kvant» several times, the last time in No. 9 for 1983 on p. 33.
