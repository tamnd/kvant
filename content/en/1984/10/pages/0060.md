8th grade

1. Determine the density of the unknown liquid.

Equipment: a vessel with water, a vessel with an unknown liquid (salt solution), two glass tubes, two rubber tubes, a ruler, a platen, a glass tee, scissors, a Schintz pump.

Attribution. When determining the density of an unknown liquid, contact of this liquid with water must not be allowed.

2. Determine the friction coefficient of cellulonade on wood.

Equipment: cellulon ball for real tenin with a response (3-4 mm), a wooden student's ruler, a vessel with water (200 ml), fine dry sand.

9th grade

1. Determine the specific heat of fusion of an unknown crystalline substance.

Equipment: a vessel with hot water, two identical test tubes, an unknown substance, a thermometer, a watch with a second hand.

The density of matter and water, as well as the specific heat and heat capacity of water, should be considered known: $\rho_{\text{вв}}=1,7 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m³, $\rho_{\text{вд}}=10^3$ kg/m³, $c_{\text{вв}}=4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K).

2. Investigate the dependence of the stiffness of springs made independently from copper wire on the length of the resulting spring and its diameter.

Equipment: copper wire with a diameter of 1-2 mm, cylindrical rods of various diameters (for example, 1, 2, 3 cm), a ruler, a set of weights, a rod with two legs, thread.

10th grade

1. Investigate the dependence of the period of small vibrations of a ruler placed on a cylindrical body on the length of the ruler and the diameter of the cylinder.

Equipment: three cylinders of different diameters (for example, 2, 4, 8 cm), three rulers of different lengths (for example, 25, 50, 100 cm), a clock with a second hand.

2. Determine the area in which you can simultaneously see a luminous light bulb located on the main optical axis of the collecting lens and its image (observation is carried out with one eye).

Equipment: light bulb on a stand, current source, collecting lens, connected wires, ruler.
