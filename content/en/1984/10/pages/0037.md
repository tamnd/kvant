⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. “Hello, Katya! We got a telephone! I'll tell you the number. It, like yours, is five-digit. The first digit is a prime number, the next two digits are a two-digit prime number, and the last two digits are obtained from the previous pair by rearrangement and form a perfect square. So what is my phone number?

2. Is it possible to have a triangle with sides $a=7$ cm and $b=2$ cm if it is known that the height dropped to the third side of this triangle is the geometric mean of the other two heights?

3. Propose the design of a device based on the principle of communicating vessels, designed to check the horizontality of classical walls.

4. 223 liters of oil were brought to the store in cans of 10 and 17 liters. How many cans were there?

5. Solve the crossumber, that is, fill in the cells of the square with numbers, like the cells of a crossword puzzle, according to the instructions.
   Horizontal:
   $a=(a по вертикали)^2$,
   $h=(f по вертикали) \times 2$,
   $i=(c по вертикали) + (g по вертикали)$.
   Vertical:
   $b=(g по горизонтали)^2$,
   $d=(a по горизонтали) \times (e по горизонтали)$.

The task was proposed by M. A. Rollova, S. R. Sefbekov, V. D. Vyon, N. K. Antonovich, A. P. Savin
