For thousands of years, this day has “moved” somewhat in relation to the constellations of the Zodiac. Note that “aries” and “taurus” are obsolete names for ram and bull (cf. “sheep” and “calf”). By Strel, that is, a shooter, they understood a centaur armed with a bow and arrows; under Capricorn - a goat with a fish tail; The fish were represented in the form of two fish connected by a braid. The name of the Zodiac (from the Greek word zodion - “animal”) is explained by the fact that most of the constellations of the Zodiac have the appearance of animals.

The Greeks had 21 constellations in the northern Zodiac, and 15 constellations to the south (the constellations of the southern hemisphere were worse for the Greeks, since in ancient times travelers rarely even reached the equator).

The formation of constellations is explained by the figures that were obtained by connecting the stars that form the constellation with lines. Different peoples “drew” these figures in different ways. For example, in the bucket of the Big Dipper the Greeks saw a bear, and the Arabs saw a funeral procession - a coffin, in front of which there are mourners, headed by the “leader of the mourners.” Some constellations are connected with each other: Bootes, that is, the shepherd, was considered by the Greeks as a watchman for bears. Six constellations - the northern constellations Cepheus, Cassopenus, Andromeda, Perseus, Pegasus and Cetus - are also connected by a common legend about the ethereal king Cepheus (Cepheus is the Latin form of this name), his wife Cassopeia and his daughter Andromeda. The constellation Hercules received its name only in modern times; the Greeks called it “Kneeling”. The constellation Eridanus also received its name in modern times, the Greeks called it “River”. Eridanus is the ancient name of the Greeks for Po, as well as one of the names of the mythical son of the Sun Phaeton, who, according to legend, fell to the ground and drowned in Po.

The constellation of the Ship Argo was subsequently divided into the Poop, Sails, Compass and Carina, and from small stars not included in the previously known constellations, new constellations were formed: Leo Minor, Canes Venatici, Shit (originally Sobieski's Shit), Chanterelle, Jasper, Lynx, Unicorn and Sextant.
