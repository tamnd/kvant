$$N_2 \cos \alpha + N_1 \sin \alpha - mg \cos \alpha - f_{1y} = 0,$$
or
$$N_2 \cos \alpha + N_1 \sin \alpha - mg \cos \alpha - Mg \cos \alpha = 0. \quad (2)$$
From equation (1) and (2) we find $N_1$ and $N_2$:
$$N_1 = Ma \cos \alpha, \quad N_2 = (M+m)g - Ma \sin \alpha.$$
Let us write the second condition, considering the moments of forces relative to the point $B$ (Fig. 3):

$$N_2 l \cos \alpha - mg \frac{l}{2} \cos \alpha - f_{1x} \sin \beta = 0,$$
where $x$ is the coordinate of the beetle at this moment. Considering that $f_1 \sin \beta = f_{1y} = Mg \cos \alpha$, and substituting the found values ​​$N_1$ and $N_2$, we get

$$Mgl - Mal \sin \alpha + mg \frac{l}{2} - Mgx = 0.$$

Let's rewrite this equation as follows:

$$x - l \frac{2M+m}{2M} = -a \frac{l \sin \alpha}{g} \quad (3)$$

and introduce the notations $x - l \frac{2M+m}{2M} = z$, $\frac{g}{l \sin \alpha} = \omega^2$. Given that $a = x'' = \left( z + l \frac{2M+m}{2M} \right)'' = z''$, equation (3) can be written as follows:

$$z'' = -\omega^2 z.$$

The equation we obtained is the equation of harmonic oscillations. In the general case, the solution to such an equation is written in the form

$$z(t) = A \sin \omega t + B \cos \omega t, \quad (4)$$

and the constructed $A$ and $B$ are found from the initial conditions. In our case, the initial conditions (at $t=0$) are:

$$x(t_0) = z(t_0) + l \frac{2M+m}{2M} = 0 \Rightarrow z(t_0) = -l \frac{2M+m}{2M},$$

$$x'(t_0) = v_0 \Rightarrow z'(t_0) = v_0.$$

Substituting $t=0$ and $z(t_0)$ into equation (4), we obtain

$$B = -l \frac{2M+m}{2M}.$$

Differentiating equation (4) and substituting $t=0$ and $z'(t_0)$, we obtain:

$$A = \frac{v_0}{\omega}.$$

Thus,

$$z(t) = \frac{v_0}{\omega} \sin \omega t - l \frac{2M+m}{2M} \cos \omega t,$$

and therefore

$$x(t) = \frac{v_0}{\omega} \sin \omega t + l \frac{2M+m}{2M} (1 - \cos \omega t). \quad (5)$$
