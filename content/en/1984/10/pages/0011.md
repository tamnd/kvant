3. Weight

From a number of experiments carried out under a variety of conditions, we can conclude that a body infinitely distant from all other bodies cannot move by acceleration, if we consider the movement relative to some well-defined reporting systems, which are called inertial reporting systems. Such a body is either at rest or moves uniformly and is motionless relative to the inertial reporting system. If a body moves with acceleration, then it is always possible to indicate another body or other bodies, the influence of which causes the accelerated movement of this body. In such cases, they say that bodies interact with each other. Let's consider the simplest case - when two bodies interact. Experience shows that in this case both bodies receive accelerations whose directions are mutually opposite. As for the accelerated model of both bodies, they can be different. But the ratio of the accelerated model to both bodies remains constant, regardless of the conditions under which the interaction occurs. Thus, it does not depend on the relative position of these bodies in space, does not depend on time, does not depend on the speed of both bodies, does not depend on the environment, etc. This relationship depends only on the properties of the interacting bodies themselves.

In physics, only those properties of a body are studied that can be expressed by numbers, and to study certain properties in physics, physical quantities are introduced that characterize these properties. When it comes to the acceleration of a body caused by the influence of another body on it, then this acceleration can be greater or less. The greater the acceleration of a body, the greater, obviously, the change in its speed over a given period of time. And vice versa, if the acceleration of a body is small, this means that over the same period of time its speed changes little. Speed ​​cannot change instantly - any change in the speed of a body requires a certain period of time.
