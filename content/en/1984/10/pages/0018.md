If you need to calculate the fifth root, divide both sides of the equation $x^5 = a$ by $x$, and then extract the fourth root, after which you get the recurrence relationship
$$x_{n+1} = \sqrt[4]{a/x_n}, n=0, 1, 2, \dots$$
Taking $x_0=1$, we write down the algorithm for extracting the fifth root on the “Elektronka BZ-14M”:

$$a\sqrt[4]{-1/x} \times a = \sqrt[4]{-1/x} \times a = \sqrt[4]{-1/x} \dots$$
... ind($\sqrt[4]{a}$),

Obviously, the steppe roots $2^k(2^m-1), 2^k(2^m+1)$, where $k=0, 1, 2, \dots, m=2, 3, 4, \dots$, can be extracted in the same way.

It remains to say a few words about the choice of the initial approximation. When extracting roots in all considered cases, the initial approximation can be $x_0=1$ or even any number $x_0 \in [1, a]$. This will not affect the convergence of the iterative process (3).
