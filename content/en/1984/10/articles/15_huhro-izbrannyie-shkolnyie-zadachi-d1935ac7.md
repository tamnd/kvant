By squaring both sides of this equation and transferring all terms of the resulting equation to the left side, we arrive at the equation
$$x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0,$$
having roots $x_1 = 2$ and $x_2 = 5$. When squaring, roots could appear, a construction for the original equation, so the found roots must be checked. Substituting the first root $x_1 = 2$ into equation (9), we obtain the incorrect numerical equality $-1 = 1$, which means that $x_1 = 2$ is the constructed root for the original equation (9). Substituting the second root $x_2 = 5$ into equation (9), we obtain the correct numerical equality. And, therefore, $x_2 = 5$ is the root of the original equation.
So, equation (9) has a single root $x = 5$.
Here's another example. Let's solve the equation
$$\log_7(x^2 - 4) = \log_7(4x - 7).$$
By potentiating, we arrive at the equation
$$x^2 - 4 = 4x - 7,$$
whose roots are $x_1 = 1$ and $x_2 = 3$. During potentiation, roots could appear, a construction for the original equation, so the found roots must be checked. The number $x_1 = 1$ does not satisfy equation (10), since it does not make sense as a logarithm of negative numbers, therefore $x_1 = 1$ is not the root of this equation. The number $x_2 = 3$ turns the original equation into a correct numerical equality.
So, equation (10) has a single root $x = 3$.

Selected School Problems

Here we present a number of tasks proposed for the Olympiad areas in the Novosibirsk region. Some of the middle ones are quite simple, there are tasks that are more demanding, but they are all close to the school curriculum. In parentheses after the assignment number it is indicated in which classes it was offered.

Eighth grade
1 (7-8). Two figures, Vasya and Petya, simultaneously left point A to point B. Vasya traveled the entire way at a constant speed, and Petya, having first traveled for 1 hour at a speed of 12 km/h, traveled the rest of the way at a speed of 14 km/h and arrived at B 5 minutes earlier than Vasya. If Petya drove at a speed of 12 km/h all the time, then he would arrive at B 6 minutes later than Vasya. Find the distance from A to B.
2 (7-8). A person's age in 1959 turned out to be equal to the sum of the digits of the year of his birth. Find the year of birth of this person if it is known that he was born in the 20th century.
3 (7-8). A circle with center at point O is inscribed in triangle ABC. At point T it touches side BC. It is known that $\angle BOT: \angle COT = 3:4$. Find the length of the sides BC if the radius of the circumcircle is 3 and the angle A is 30°.
4 (7-8). Prove that if positive numbers x and y satisfy the inequalities x+y>2,6 and x^2+y^2<4, then xy>1.
5 (7-9). Is it possible to cut a square into a 1980 square smaller size? And for 1981? (The resulting squares may not be the same.)
