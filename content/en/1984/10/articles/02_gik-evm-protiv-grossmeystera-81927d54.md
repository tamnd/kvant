COMPUTER VS GRANDMASTER

In «Kvant» No. 6 for this year I talk about two games by A. Karpov with microcomputers. Sometimes there are grandmasters with large computers.

An interesting game (one on one) was played between the ex-world champion of the average chashin program “Bell” and the ex-world champion in the correspondence game, special in the field of machine chess, G. Berliner.

If there are still very few grandmasters in the computer service game (the best programs play their best games at the candidate master level), then in a close game or a simultaneous game session they from time to time take over the chess players. Once, for example, the computer defeated the English grandmaster M. Steen.

The most difficult stage of the game for a computer is the endgame, since enumeration of options (the main weapon of a computer) has a very relative value here. However, if the ending is of a tactical, combinational nature, then the machine can show its best qualities. This is how the sans game between the computer and grandmaster W. Brown ended.
