Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. Let K, M, N be the corresponding bases of the altitudes AK, BM, CN of the acute triangle ABC. Prove that:
a) triangles AMN, BNK and CKM are similar to triangle ABC (and to each other), and for each of them the similarity coefficient is equal to the cosine of their common angle with triangle ABC;
6) AK, BM, CN are the bisectors of the angles of triangle KMN.
2. a) The eighth term of a geometric progression is equal to 10. Find the product of all its terms from the first to the fifteenth inclusive.
6) Find the sum
$$x + 2x^2 + 3x^3 + 4x^4 + \dots + nx^n$$
3. In triangle ABC, let point M divide side AB in the ratio m:n, counting from vertex B, and point N divide side BC in the ratio p:q, counting from vertex B. Prove that the ratio of the areas of the triangle BMN and BAC is equal to $\frac{m}{m+n} \cdot \frac{p}{p+q}$.
4. In arthmetic progression
$$S_m = S_n$$
Find $S_{m+n}$ ($S_k$ denotes the sum of the first k terms of the progression).
5. a) Let the chords AB and CD of one circle intersect at point E. Prove that $AE \cdot EB = CE \cdot ED$.
6) Let a tangent EA (A is the point of tangency) and a secant EBC (B and C are points of the circle) be drawn to it from a point E lying outside the circle. Prove that $EA^2 = EB \cdot EC$.
7. Can two opposite faces of a quadrilateral pair be perpendicular to the base?
8. a) Is it true that the sum of two rational numbers is equal
$$\cos \frac{2\pi}{5} + \cos \frac{4\pi}{5} + \cos \frac{6\pi}{5} = -\frac{1}{2}$$
Convert the following sums into a product:
6) $\sin \alpha + \sin 2\alpha + \sin 3\alpha + \dots + \sin (n\alpha)$;
8) $\sin^2 \alpha + \sin^2 2\alpha + \sin^2 3\alpha + \dots + \sin^2 (n\alpha)$.

Tenth grade
11. Let the ball touch all the edges of the tetrahedron. Prove that the sums of the lengths of pairs of its crossing edges are equal.
12. Let the plane intersect the side edges of the tetrahedron so that the ratios of the lengths of the corresponding edges are equal to the corresponding a/b, c/d, e/f. Prove that the volumes of the cut-off and original tetrahedra are in the ratio $\frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{c}{d} \cdot \frac{e}{f}$ (compare with Problem 3).
13. Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ be the angles of the triangle. Prove that:
a) $\cos \alpha + \cos \beta + \cos \gamma \leq \frac{3}{2}$;
6) $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} \cdot \sin \frac{\beta}{2} \cdot \sin \frac{\gamma}{2} \leq \frac{1}{8}$.
14. Graph the functions:
a) $y = \cos(\arccos x)$;
6) $y = \cos(3\arccos x)$;
b) $y = \arcsin(\sin x)$.
15. Let all the plane angles at one of the vertices of the tetrahedron be removed. Prove that this vertex, the point of intersection of the medians of the opposite eggin and the center of the ball circumscribed about the tetrahedron lie on the same straight line.
