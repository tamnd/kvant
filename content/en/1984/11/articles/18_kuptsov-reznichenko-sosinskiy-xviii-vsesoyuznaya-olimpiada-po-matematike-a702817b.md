ХVIII All-Russian Mathematics Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
L. P. KUPTSOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. V. REZNICHENKO,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. B. SOSINSKY

From April 11 to 18, the final stage of the XVIII All-Scholastic Olympiad in Mathematics was held in Ashgabat. In the capital of Soviet Turkmenistan, 159 schoolchildren (43 eighth-graders, 54 ninth-graders and 62 tenth-graders) gathered - winners of Resource Olympiads, winners of the Moscow and Leningrad City Olympiads and schoolchildren awarded I and II degree diplomas at the previous All-Olympiad. The jury, working under the leadership of Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B.V. Gnedenko, included 52 people - representatives of the USSR Academy of Sciences, the Academies of Sciences of the Turkmen and Moldavian SSRs, Moscow, Leningrad and Novosibirsk State Universities, the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, and the country's largest research institutes. Among them is the senior teacher of the Turkmen State University, the organizer of all resource Olympiads in Turkmenistan B.B. Berdiev.

From the first minutes of their stay on the hospitable Turkmen land, the participants of the Olympiad were surrounded by love and attention. At the Ashgabat airport, the participating teams were met by students from city schools, representatives of regional departments of public education and the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Turkmenistan.

Upon registration, each participant in the Olympiad and the team leader were given a commemorative sovereign package with books and postcards telling about Turkmenini, memorial signs, the Olympiad program and an invitation to take part in its grand opening.

On April 12, at 15:00, all participants of the Olympiad, members of the jury and organizing committee, team leaders will gather at the city Palace of Pinners. Accompanied by the orchestra, a solemn procession to the monument to V.I. Lenin began. There was a guard of honor at the monument, and Olympiad participants laid flowers. And then the grand opening of the Olympiad took place in the conference hall of the Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Tajikistan. The State Anthem of the USSR and the Anthem of the Turkmen SSR were used. The Minister of Education of the Republic of Tajikistan M.A. Olieva addressed the participants of the Olympiad with these words of greeting. Successful performance, creative, interactive work, and honorable competition were wished to the children by the Vice-President of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR O. O. Ovezgeldyev, the jury was represented by Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B. V. Gnedenko, the first secretary of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Turkmenistan Zh. K. Charyeva, the rector of the Turkmen State University G. M. Myalikgulyev. Present at the opening of the Olympiad were: Department of Science and Educational Institutions of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Turkmenistan E. O. Ovlyakulev, head. Department of Science and Educational Institutions of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Tajikistan S. K. Kakaev. The grand opening was concluded with a concert by members of amateur artistic groups of the Ashgabat Palace of Pinners.

The participants wrote the assignments for the final stage of the Olympiad on April 13 and 14. The assignment for each of the two days consisted of four problems, the solution of which took five hours. Below are the conditions for all tasks. Some of them are included in task «Kvant» (the corresponding number is indicated after the task conditions).

2. Prove that for any non-negative numbers $a$ and $b$ the following inequality holds:
$$\frac{1}{2}(a+b)^2 + \frac{1}{4}(a+b) \geqslant a\sqrt{b} + b\sqrt{a}.$$

3. On the plane there are two equilateral triangles $A_1B_1C_1$ and $A_2B_2C_2$, the vertices of which are measured clockwise. From an arbitrary point $O$, vectors $\vec{OA}, \vec{OB}, \vec{OC}$ are rejected, equal, respectively, to vector $A_1A_2, B_1B_2, C_1C_2$. Prove that points $A, B, C$ are also vertices of an equilateral triangle.

L. P. Kuptsov

4. There are four colors and an infinite number of square tiles with a side of length 1. It is allowed to paint the sides of the tiles so that the colors of all sides of each tile are equal, and glue the tiles to each other with sides of the same color. For what numbers $m$ and $n$ can these tiles be glued into a rectangle of size $m \times n$, in which each side is painted the same color and the colors of all sides are different?

V. A. Ufnarovsky

9th grade

1. Prove that for all real $x > 0, y > 0$ and for all real $a$ the following inequality holds:
$$x^{\sin^2 a} \cdot y^{\cos^2 a} < x + y.$$

V. I. Burenkov

2. There is a cube and two colors: red and green. Two people play this game. The beginner selects 3 edges of the cube and paints them red. His partner chooses 3 ribs from those that have not yet been painted and paints them green. After this, the beginner again paints 3 ribs red, and then 3 ribs green - his partner. It is prohibited to repaint the rib in a different color or paint it twice with the same red color. The winner is the one who is the first to paint all the edges of any face red. Is it true that a beginner will definitely win if he plays correctly?

N. Kh. Adakhanov

3. $n \geqslant 3$ natural numbers are written in a circle so that for each number the ratio of the sum of its neighbors to it is a natural number. Prove that the sum of such ratios: a) is not less than 2n; b) less than 3n (M875).

O. R. Musin

4. A circle with center at point $O$, cut into triangle $ABC$, touches its side $BC, AC, AB$, respectively, at points $A_1, B_1, C_1$. The segments $AO, BO, CO$ intersect the circle at points $A_2, B_2, C_2$, respectively. Prove that the lines $A_1A_2, B_1B_2, C_1C_2$ intersect at one point.

I. F. Sharyn

10th grade

1. For which integers $m$ and $n$ does the equality hold?
$$(5+3\sqrt{2})^m = (3+5\sqrt{2})^n?$$

Yu. V. Mikheev

2. $n$ different real numbers were written down in ascending order on the line. Below it, on the second line, were written the same numbers, only, perhaps, in a different order. For each pair of numbers written one below the other, the sum was calculated. These amounts formed the third line. It turned out that the numbers in the third line are also arranged in ascending order. Prove that the first line matches the second.

A. V. Anzhans

3. Given a triangle $ABC$. Through the point $P$ we drew straight lines $PA, PB, PC$, which intersected the circumcircle of this triangle at points $A_1, B_1, C_1$, other than the vertices of the triangle. It turned out that the triangle $A_1B_1C_1$ is congested with the triangle $ABC$. Prove that there are at most eight points $P$ with the specified property.

I. F. Sharyn

4. Positive numbers $x, y, z$ satisfy the system of equations

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + xy + \frac{y^2}{3} = 25, \\ \frac{y^2}{3} + z^2 = 9, \\ z^2 + zx + x^2 = 16. \end{cases}$$

Calculate the values ​​of $xy + 2yz + 3zx$.

A. A. Bolotov

The guys coped quite successfully with the tasks proposed on the first day. As expected, problems 8.4, 9.3, 9.4, 10.3 and 10.4 caused the least difficulties. Problem 9.3 turned out to be especially difficult. Only two participants were able to correctly solve both points. Geometric problem 10.3 also turned out to be very difficult, only 3 participants presented an accurate solution. However, in none of the classes was there a task that no one could solve. The jury noted with satisfaction that the ninth-graders solved the rather difficult problem 9.4 significantly better than expected, and many of the ribs also managed solutions that turned out to be simpler than the solution known to the jury.

On April 13, after completing work on the competition tasks, the ribs took part in a sports festival.
