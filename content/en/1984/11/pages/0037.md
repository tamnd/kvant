M891. A circle touches two sides of a triangle and its two medians. Prove that this triangle is equilateral.

A. A. Muratov

M892. a) Prove that among the numbers $2^m+2^k$, as well as among the numbers $3^m+3^k$, there are infinitely many squares, and among the numbers $4^m+4^k$, $5^m+5^k$ and $6^m+6^k$ there is not a single square of an integer (here $m$ and $k$ are natural numbers, $m \neq k$).

6)* Are there squares among the numbers $7^m+7^k$?

A. I. Zaichik

M893. Each two of the $n$ computer blocks are connected by a wire. Is it possible to cover each of these wires in one of $n-1$ colors so that $n-1$ wires of different colors come from each block, if $n=6$; 6) $n=13$?

V. B. Alekseev

M894. a) The sum of the heel of non-negative numbers is 1. Prove that they can be arranged in a circle so that the sum of the heel of pairwise products of connected numbers does not exceed 1/5.

6)* There are $n \geqslant 4$ non-negative numbers placed around a circle, the sum of which is equal to 1. Prove that the sum of all $n$ pairwise products of connected numbers does not exceed 1/4.

S. B. Gashkov, A. N. Dranitsnikov

M895*. Prove that the cross-sectional area of ​​a cube by a plane tangent to the sphere inscribed in it does not exceed half the area of ​​the cube. Unwind the case when this section is a) a triangle, 6) a quadrilateral. C) Prove that in case a) the total surface area of ​​the tetrahedron cut off from the cube is less than the area of ​​the cube.

S. B. Gashkov, I. F. Sharytsin

F903. On the upper generatrix of the main cylinder of radius $R$, the axis of which is inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon, a flexible weightless rope of length $l$ (Fig. 1) is fixed. A small weight is attached to the other end of the rope. Define:
1) the length of the hanging part of the rope in the load equilibrium position;
2) the period of small vibrations of the load in a vertical plane parallel to the cylinder axis.

S. S. Krotov
