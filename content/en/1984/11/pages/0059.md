Meeting with readers
This year, traditional advanced training courses for secondary school teachers took place at Moscow University. On June 12, mathematics teachers, students of these courses, met with members of the editorial board of the journal «Kvant».

At a meeting with teachers, led by the deputy editor-in-chief of the magazine

«Kvant» Yu. P. Solovyov, sewn by members of the editor N. V. Vasiliev, N. Kh. Rozov, head of the mathematics department of the journal A. V. Sosinsky. In their speeches, they spoke about the history, work and plans of the magazine, the tasks set for the magazine, which represented the forms of secondary and vocational schools.

Many of those present took an active part in the ensuing discussion; the qualified assistance that the magazine provides to them during electives and extracurricular activities, the importance «Kvant» to cultivate students' interest in physics and mathematics. In their speeches, teachers made and criticized the editorial board of the journal; specific advice and proposals were made for its further improvement.
