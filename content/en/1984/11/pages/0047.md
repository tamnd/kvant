ХVIII All-Russian Mathematics Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
L. P. KUPTSOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. V. REZNICHENKO,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. B. SOSINSKY

From April 11 to 18, the final stage of the XVIII All-Scholastic Olympiad in Mathematics was held in Ashgabat. In the capital of Soviet Turkmenistan, 159 schoolchildren (43 eighth-graders, 54 ninth-graders and 62 tenth-graders) gathered - winners of Resource Olympiads, winners of the Moscow and Leningrad City Olympiads and schoolchildren awarded I and II degree diplomas at the previous All-Olympiad. The jury, working under the leadership of Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B.V. Gnedenko, included 52 people - representatives of the USSR Academy of Sciences, the Academies of Sciences of the Turkmen and Moldavian SSRs, Moscow, Leningrad and Novosibirsk State Universities, the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, and the country's largest research institutes. Among them is the senior teacher of the Turkmen State University, the organizer of all resource Olympiads in Turkmenistan B.B. Berdiev.

From the first minutes of their stay on the hospitable Turkmen land, the participants of the Olympiad were surrounded by love and attention. At the Ashgabat airport, the participating teams were met by students from city schools, representatives of regional departments of public education and the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Turkmenistan.

Upon registration, each participant in the Olympiad and the team leader were given a commemorative sovereign package with books and postcards telling about Turkmenini, memorial signs, the Olympiad program and an invitation to take part in its grand opening.

On April 12, at 15:00, all participants of the Olympiad, members of the jury and organizing committee, team leaders will gather at the city Palace of Pinners. Accompanied by the orchestra, a solemn procession to the monument to V.I. Lenin began. There was a guard of honor at the monument, and Olympiad participants laid flowers. And then the grand opening of the Olympiad took place in the conference hall of the Academy of Sciences of the Republic of Tajikistan. The State Anthem of the USSR and the Anthem of the Turkmen SSR were used. The Minister of Education of the Republic of Tajikistan M.A. Olieva addressed the participants of the Olympiad with these words of greeting. Successful performance, creative, interactive work, and honorable competition were wished to the children by the Vice-President of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR O. O. Ovezgeldyev, the jury was represented by Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B. V. Gnedenko, the first secretary of the Central Committee of the Komsomol of Turkmenistan Zh. K. Charyeva, the rector of the Turkmen State University G. M. Myalikgulyev. Present at the opening of the Olympiad were: Department of Science and Educational Institutions of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Turkmenistan E. O. Ovlyakulev, head. Department of Science and Educational Institutions of the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Tajikistan S. K. Kakaev. The grand opening was concluded with a concert by members of amateur artistic groups of the Ashgabat Palace of Pinners.

The participants wrote the assignments for the final stage of the Olympiad on April 13 and 14. The assignment for each of the two days consisted of four problems, the solution of which took five hours. Below are the conditions for all tasks. Some of them are included in task «Kvant» (the corresponding number is indicated after the task conditions).
