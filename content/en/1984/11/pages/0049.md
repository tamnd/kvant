7. There are $n + 1$ weights with a total weight of $2n$ and a scale with two cups in equilibrium. The weight of each weight is expressed as a natural number. The weights are placed in turn from the scales: first the heaviest (or one of the heaviest), then the heaviest of the remaining ones, and so on. In this case, each subsequent weight is placed on the cup of the scale that is lighter at the moment, and if the scales are in balance, then on any of the cups. Prove that after all the weights are on the scales, the scales will be in equilibrium.

A. V. Anzhans

6. Four different points are taken on a straight line, designated in order by the letters $A, B, C, D$. Prove that for any point $E$ not lying on the line $AD$ the following inequality holds:
$$|AE| + |ED| + |AB| - |CD| > |BE| + |CE|.$$
S. V. Gashkov

7. The sequence $(x_n)$ is given recurrently:

$$x_1 = 1; x_2 = -1; x_{n+2} = x_n^2 + 1 - \frac{1}{2} x_n,$$
if $n \geqslant 1$. Prove that the sequence $(x_n)$ has a limit and find it.

N. Kh. Adakhanov

8. The white cells of a chessboard of size $1983 \times 1984$ contain the numbers 1 or -1 so that for any black cell the product of the numbers in the white cells adjacent to it is equal to 1. Prove that this is possible only if all the written numbers are equal to 1.

O. V. Lyashko
