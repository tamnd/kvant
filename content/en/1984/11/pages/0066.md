4. Let x be the price of Humpty and y the price of Humpty.
Then 175x−125y=25(7x−5y)≥25, and 126y−175x=7(18y−25x)≥7.
Posmotu y=126y−125y=126y−175x+175x−125y≥7+25=32.
So, Dumpty costs at least 32 kopecks,
but Humpty is worth more than $\frac{5}{7}y=\frac{5}{7}\cdot 32,$
that is, no less than 23 kopecks.
Posmot for the purchase of 3 Shaltai and 1 Boltai will need no less than $3\cdot 23+32=101$ kopecks.
