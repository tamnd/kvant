10th grade

1. In one of the projects, it was planned to use a solar sail with an area of ​​$S=1$ km$^2$ for spacecraft flights in the Solar System. The sail unfolds as the vehicle moves around the Sun in Earth's orbit, the radius of which is $R_3=1,5 \cdot 10^8$ km. During further movement, the sail is constantly oriented perpendicular to the sun's rays, the pressure of which in the earth's orbit is $p=10^{-5}$ Pa. 1) At what mass of the spacecraft can it fly away from the Solar System? 2) At what maximum mass can the apparatus reach Mars, the radius of which is $R_M=2,3 \cdot 10^8$ km? The gravitational influence of the Earth and other planets is not taken into account. The product of the mass of the Sun and the gravity constant $GM_C=1,3 \cdot 10^{11}$ km$^3/c^2$.

2. Polzunov's tell machine (one of the first tell machines) worked as follows. Water vapor was supplied to a copper cylinder with a light piston at a temperature of $100^\circ C$. At the same time, the piston rose, making a stabbing stroke. Then the steam in the cylinder was cooled by external water vapor at a temperature of $0^\circ C$ and condensed. The piston descended and through a system of blocks lifted a load, for example coal from a mine. Let us assume that to prepare steam from water with a temperature of $0^\circ C$, 1 ton of coal was burned. Neglecting trine and tell losses, you determine how much coal was lifted from a mine 100 m deep. The combustion temperature of coal is $q=3,1 \cdot 10^7$ J/kg, the heat of water is $c=4200$ J/(kg K), the specific heat of vaporization of water is $r=2,26 \cdot 10^6$ J/kg, the universal gas constant is $R=8,3$ J/(mol K).

3. The fried part, located at infinity at rest, is gravitated into a uniformly fried half-ring along the line $AB$ (Fig. 9). The ratio of the particle velocities at points $A$ and $B$ is known and equal to $v_A/v_B=n$. Find the ratio of particle accelerations at these points.

4. Two flat layers of thickness $a$ of each uniform pair are paired with a volumetric pair with density $q$ and $+\varphi$ (Fig. 10). A particle with a negative pairing $e$ and a mass $m$ approaches the positive pairing layer with a speed $\vec{v}$, directed at an angle $\alpha$ to the surface of the layer. 1) At what speed value will the particle not be able to produce a negative layer? 2) After how long and at what distance from the point $A$ is the particle in this service positive layer?

5. A narrow beam of light falls normally on a glass ball of radius $R$ with an index of incidence $n$. At a distance $r$ from the center, the beam scatters evenly in the entire direction on a small air bubble formed during the manufacture of the ball. What part of the light scattered by the bubble will come out of the ball? The loss of light in the glass is a complaint. When names and e.
