⟦folio 33⟧

Quantum
for younger students

Exercise

1. Two two-digit prime numbers are obtained from each other by rearranging the digits, and their difference is a perfect square. What are these numbers?

2. Sportsmen run along the ten paths of the park in the direction of the arrows. Each athlete runs each track in 1 minute, and only one athlete runs along one of the tracks every minute, two on the other, three on the third, etc., ten to 10 athletes. Place the track numbers on figure.

3. Mom cooked compote and jelly in identical saucepans and, opening the lids, put them on the window to cool. Half an hour later I touched the parts. Which one was warmer?

4. There were two identical numbers written on the board. Styopa assigned 100 to one of them in front, and 1 to the back to the second. It turned out that the first is exactly 37 times larger than the second. What numbers were written on the board?

5. Cut out a piece of checkered paper from a sheet of paper that consists of the smallest integer number of cells such that, when playing cross-pick, the beginner always wins (to win at cross-pick, you need to place three of your icons in a row).

These tasks were offered to us by: N.K. Antonovich, L.P. Mochalov, A.V. Vyalko, A.N. Mesnyakin, A.P. Savin
