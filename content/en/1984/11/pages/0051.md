ХVIII All-Round Olympiad in Physics

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
Y. A. SAMARSKY,
L. V. CHERNOVA

For the eighteenth time, the All-Union Olympiad in Physics brought together talented schoolchildren from all the allied resource schools - the winners of this year's Resource Olympiads and recognizing the previous All-Olympid in Physics - to study in the responsible modern final stage of the Olympiad. This time, physical modern events were held from April 11 to 18 in the capital of Armenia - Yerevan. It is no coincidence that the Olympics met its “coming of age” in Soviet Armenia, a resource center with remarkable traditions of physical science.

A special brochure “Physics in Armenia” was published for the participants of the Olympiad, which opened with a greeting from the Chairman of the USSR Academy of Sciences, Academician A.P. Aleksadrov and the Chairman of the Central Organizing Committee of the All-Scholastic Olympiad for Schoolchildren, Academician I.K. Kikon:

“Dear friends!

We warmly congratulate you on the fact that you have been awarded the right to take part in the XVIII All-Russian Olympiad in Physics. Self-participation in the Olympiad is already a big victory, which you achieved with your hard work and talent. We want to emphasize that talent without hard work is worth little...

In a few years, you, who teach us about the Olympids, will be included in large-scale and back-end work on the application of physics in various fields of technical and other sciences...

These days you are provided with a performer. There were no easy tasks at the World Physical Olympiad.

We sincerely wish you to stand out as a performer. We wish you great success in your studies and future professional work.”

At the grand opening, which took place on April 12, all speakers were unanimous in their good wishes for the Olympians.

On the morning of April 13, the theoretical round of the final stage of the Olympiad took place, in which 144 schoolchildren took part. Eighth-graders were offered 4 problems (4 hours were given to work), ninth-graders and tenth-graders were each given 5 problems (5 hours were given to solve them). Let us present the conditions of these problems (some of them were published in the Problem Book «Kvant» in 8-10 issues of the magazine for this year).

Problems of the theoretical tour

8th grade

1. The water heating battery maintains a room temperature of $t_0=+18^\circ\text{C}$ at an outside temperature of $t_1=-20^\circ\text{C}$. When the outside air temperature dropped to $t_2=-22^\circ\text{C}$, and the modified N=1 kW stove was turned on in the room, it turned out that the temperature in the room did not change. How much heat does a water heating battery release per second?

2. To change the distribution of wind speed over height, use balloons that have a constant vertical lifting speed. When launching such a ball, a guess was made about the ball's elevation above the horizon from time (Fig. 1). If the wind speed at the Earth's surface is equal to zero, and the distance from the point of launch of the ball to the observed one is equal to 1 km (Fig. 2), determine the height of the rise of the ball 7 minutes after launch and the wind speed at this height.
