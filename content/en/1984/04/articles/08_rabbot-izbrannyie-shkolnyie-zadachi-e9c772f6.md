Selected School Problems

Under this backdrop, in the “School in Kvant” section, mathematics assignments related to the school course will be published. Unlike the “Kvant Problem Book”, here we will place fairly well-known problems - “school math class”. Their solutions (usually short, but not always very convenient!) will be published at the end of the same issue of the journal. By systematically analyzing these problems, you will become acquainted with many useful tips that will be useful to you on school tests and exams, when applying to a university or at an Olympiad. We hope that Selected School Problems will be of interest to both teachers and students preparing to become teachers.

Eighth grade
1. Factor the expressions:
a) $a^4 + 4b^4$;
b) $a^4 + b^4$.
2. Prove that there are 4 points in a grenade—the midpoints of the bases, the intersection point of the diagonals, and the intersection point of the extensions of the lateral sides—all lying on the same straight line.
3. a) Let two parallel lines and a segment $AB$ on one of them be given on the plane. Construct the midpoint of segment $AB$ using one ruler.
4. What figure does the equation $|x| + |y| \leq 1$ define on the coordinate plane?
5. When the excavators have to dig out the graven. At first, the first of them worked as long as it would have taken the second and third excavators to dig half of the trenches. Then the second of them worked for as long as it would have taken the first and third excavators to dig half of the trenches. Finally, after this, the third excavator worked as long as it would have taken the first and second excavators to dig half of the trenches. As a result of this work, the excavators dug out the entire trench. How many times faster would they have dug the trench if they all worked together instead of individually?

8. Let the function $f(x)$ be given by the formula
$$f(x) = (x^2 + 5x - 8)^{100} \cdot x^5.$$
(1)

If we perform all the actions indicated on the right side of formula (1) and the terms given below, then the function $f(x)$ will be written as a polynomial:
$$f(x) = a_0x^{205} + a_1x^{204} + a_2x^{203} + \dots + a_{204}x + a_{205},$$
(2)
where $a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{205}$ are some numbers (coefficients of the polynomial).
a) Find the sum of all coefficients
$$S = a_0 + a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_{204} + a_{205}$$
of the indicated polynomial standing at odd powers $x$.
b) Find the sum of the coefficients
$$S_4 = a_1 + a_3 + a_5 + \dots + a_{205}.$$
9. Prove that any function $f(x)$ defining on the entire number line can be represented as the sum of an even and an odd function.
10. Let an altitude in a terrad (an arbitrary triangular straight line) drawn from the vertex pass through the point of intersection of the altitudes of the triangle located at the base. Prove that:
a) the intersecting edges of a triangle are mutually perpendicular;
b) the remaining heights of the triangular straight line intersection of the heights of the corresponding boundaries;
b) all four altitudes of the triangle intersect at one point;
g) the sums of squares of the length of each pair of crossing edges are the same.

6) Prove that $\sin 18^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4}$.
12. What figure is defined in the coordinate space by the equality $|x| + |y| + |z| \leq 1$?
13. Solve the equations:
a) $(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3})^x + (\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3})^x = 2;
b) $(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3})^x + (\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3})^x = 2^x.
14. Simplify the expressions:
a) $\log_a tg \theta + \log_a tg 2^\theta + \dots + \log_a tg 88^\theta + \log_a tg 89^\theta;

15. Prove that the sum of the distances from an arbitrary point taken inside or on the boundary of a regular triangle to its faces is equal to the height of the triangle.
