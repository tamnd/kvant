⟦folio 32⟧

Tasks

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given asterisks. After formation, we usually indicate who offered it to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue can be sent no later than June 30, 1984 to the address: 103006, Moscow, K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 4 - 84” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M856, M857” or “F868”. Solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (in this envelope you will receive the results of the decision verification). The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study.

ATTENTION!

Problem M846 should be solved in a more general form: prove that the arithmetic mean of the lengths of the sides of an arbitrary convex polygon is less than the arithmetic mean of the lengths of its dizonals. The deadline for sending its decisions is extended until June 30.

M856—M860; F868—F872

M856. a) Construct a quadrilateral, knowing the lengths of its sides and the length of the segment connecting the diagonals.

6) Under what conditions does the problem have a solution?

I. Z. Titovich

M857. Among the 1984 first natural numbers (from 1 to 1984), we note those that can be represented as the sum of five integer non-negative powers of two (that is, five not necessarily different numbers 1, 2, 4, 8, ...). Which numbers are greater: marked or not marked?

S. Stadnichenko

M858. For the values ​​of α, β and γ angles of a certain triangle, the relation is satisfied

$$\sin^2 \alpha + \sin^2 \beta = \sin \gamma.$$

a) Find α, β, γ if it is known that the triangle is isosceles (consider all cases).

6) Can a triangle be acute?

b)* What values ​​can the greatest angle of a triangle take?

P. B. Gusyatikov

M859. Find the smallest positive number α such that for any square trinomial f(x) satisfying the inequality |f(x)|≤1 for 0≤x≤1, the inequality |f'(1)|≤α holds.

V. P. Pikulin

M860.* a) Let O and R be the center and radius of the triangle ABC, Z and r the center and radius of its incircle, K the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle with the vertices at the points where the incircle touches the side of triangle ABC. Prove that point Z lies on the segment OK, and |OZ|:|ZK|=3R/r.

6) Let a, b, c be the side lengths of triangle ABC, $\vec{n}_a, \vec{n}_b, \vec{n}_c$ be unit vectors perpendicular to the corresponding sides of the triangle and directed outward (Fig. 1). Prove that

$$a^3\vec{n}_a + b^3\vec{n}_b + c^3\vec{n}_c = 12S \cdot M\vec{O},$$

where S is the area of ​​triangle ABC, M is the intersection point of the medians. O is the center of the circumscribed circle.

Chan Kuan

F868. Rings $O$ and $O'$ are put on vertically fixed rods $AB$ and $A'B'$. An inextensible thread is tied to ring $O$, passed through ring $O'$ and secured at point $A'$ (Fig. 2). At the moment $\angle AOO' = \alpha$, ring $O'$ moves downward at speed $v$. Find the speed of the ring $O$ at this moment.

S. S. Krotov

F869. The vertical length of the brick pipe is filled with cast iron. The lower end of the pipe is maintained at a temperature of $T_1 > T_{nл}$ ($T_{nл}$ is the melting point of cast iron), the upper end is maintained at a temperature of $T_2 < T_{nл}$. The thermal conductivity of molten cast iron is $k$ times greater than that of solid iron. What part of the metal is in the molten state?

A. I. Buzdin

F870*. A monatomic ideal gas expands so that its molar temperature $c_x$ remains constant; in this case, the gas does work $A = 165$ J. Then the gas is isochorically heated to the initial temperature, for which an amount of heat $Q = 125$ J is supplied to it. Determine $c_x$.

A. A. Sheronov

F871. Initially, the uncharged capacitor embone $C$ will be connected to a series-connected battery with voltage $U_0$ and coils with inductance $L$. The current through the coils first increases and then decreases. At the moment when the current becomes zero, the capacitor is disconnected from the circuit and connected again, swapping its outputs. What is the maximum current that will flow through the coils after this? How will this current change if the switching process is repeated 1984 times? Neglect circuit resistance.

A. R. Zilberman

F872. A long sled, sliding on very smooth ice, hits the asphalt and stops before reaching half its length. After this, the sleigh is abruptly given its original speed, and it stops again. How does the time for braking relate in the first and second cases? What about braking distances?

S. S. Krotov
