FOURTH WORLD COMPUTER CHAMPIONSHIP

In «Kvant» we have already talked about three world championships among computers. The fourth took place last fall in New York. Like all previous ones, it was held according to the Swiss system; 22 programs played out the championship title in the fifth rounds. The first two world champions - “Kanisa” and “Chess” - did not participate in the competition this time.

The winner was the program “Krayblitz” (USA). The new champion, playing on the most powerful machine with a speed of more than 100 million operations per second, scored 4.5 points. “Bebe” (USA) and “Evit” (Canada) were half a point behind. Before the final round, Krablitz was half a point ahead of the previous world champion Bell, and everything was decided in a battle between them. This game turned out to be the most interesting in the tournament.
