⟦folio 29⟧

Exercise

1. At what ratio of the radii of the gears will the system shown in the upper figure rotate? And the system on the next figure?
2. Solve the number puzzle:

$$\frac{ВАГОН}{+ВАГОН}$$

$$\frac{СОСТАВ}{COSTAB}$$

3. Each side of the cube is divided into 4 squares and each square is surrounded by one of three colors: blue, yellow or red so that squares that have a common side are surrounded by different colors. How many blue, red and yellow squares can there be?
4. Let's consider some two-digit number, for example 13. Multiply this number by 20 and add it to the original number - we get the number 273. Multiply the resulting number by 481 - we get the number 131,313, in the notation of which the original number 13 is repeated three times. This amazing property holds for any two-digit number. Why?
5. The time is stamped on the electronic clock: hours and minutes. How much time per day is the number 2 carved in at least one place? Find the corresponding times for the remaining numbers: 0, 1, 3, 4, ..., 9.

These tasks were offered to us by: P. V. Molodchik, S. N. Oleksnik, V. V. Proizvolov, A. P. Savin, V. P. Chichin.
