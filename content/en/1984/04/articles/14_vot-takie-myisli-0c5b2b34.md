QUANTUM SMILES

These are the thoughts

(from reader responses to issue 530
"Club of the Curious")

I don’t know what a “solenond” is, but I think that a large metal reaction occurred with this same solenond under the influence of current, and due to the low current power, not with all the metal, but only with a part, that is, with the surface.

(H. R., Tselinograd)

Current movement is one-way movement from source to consumer. When an electric current passes through a metal, thermal energy is generated. But how does she get through most? From bottom to top! This means that the thermal energy from the movement of current decreases upward. With an increase in thermal energy, the movement accelerates and a moment will come when one state of aggregation of the metal is replaced by another, that is, crystalline - liquid. Gravity will cause the steel to fly out.

(O.P., Mozdok)

I thought that if a fight breaks out on the spot, then people or anyone else will stay in the middle so as not to be thrown out of place. Atoms in a conductor behave the same way, but if you add energy to the resulting pipes, they will melt.

(N.N., Perm)

When the body, in this case a large one, heats up, the range of vibrations will increase so much that the order in the arrangement of the part in the crystals will be disrupted. But the Solenond, having started to work, will emit magnetic waves, and these magnetic waves will maintain the order of the part in the crystals only in a circular orbit and in certain layers (this depends on the Solenond). But this will not affect the internal elephants, and therefore the metal will flow out of the larger one. All that remains is the metal on which the solenond acts, that is, the finished product - a pipe.

(P.K., Baku)

With a team contract, the work is completed in 150 minutes.

(K. R., Ritsa)

Well, let the tenth person put the finished products in a box.

(P. M., Dzhambul region)

The result is a web of roads, from which it is clear that there is a lot of unnecessary things. So the best thing is to make an area between the enterprises and pave it all.

(Private A.P.)
