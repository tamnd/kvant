COSMONAUTICS DAY

23 years ago, on April 12, 1961, the first cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin made a space flight on the Vostok spacecraft. This famous date is canceled in our country as a national holiday “Cosmonautics Day”.
Every year, specialists create postage stamps and sometimes postage blocks for this holiday. From them you can trace the chronological achievements of our country in space exploration. These stamps and blocks depict various stages of exploration of Lun, Venus and Mars using automatic mechanical stations and various automatic devices, showing Soviet spaceships and return stations. The stamps tell about meteorological satellites and communication satellites, about the first human spacewalk, about the joint Soviet-American space flight "Soyuz-Apollo", about the international space program "Intercosmos", successfully implemented with the active participation of the Soviet Union.
We will reproduce here a postage block and several stamps dedicated to Cosmonautics Day.

V. Rudov
