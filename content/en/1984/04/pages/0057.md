Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of technical library)
1. Solve the equation
$$2\sin^2 x + \mathrm{tg}^2 x = 2.$$
2. The product of the first three terms of a geometric progression is 1728, and their sum is 63. Find the first term and denominator of this progression.
3. Find the domain of definition and intervals of increasing and decreasing function $f(x) = \sqrt{2}\left(\frac{\lg^2 x + 1}{2\lg x} - 1\right)^{1/2}\left(\frac{\lg^2 x + 1}{2\lg x} + 1\right)^{1/2}\lg x$.
4. In triangle ABC, let the middle line parallel to side AB intersect the bisector of angle B at point P, and let K be the point of tangency of the incircle with side BC. Express the vector $\overrightarrow{KP}$ in terms of the vectors $\vec{e}_1$ and $\vec{e}_2$ of unit length and co-correlated with $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{CA}$, respectively, if $|AB| = 18, |AC| = 12$ and $|BC| = 11$.
Option 2
(physicomechanical and radio science faculties)
1. Solve the equation
$$8^{\frac{2}{x}} = (\log_2 16)(2^{\frac{x+3}{x}} - 3).$$
2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\sqrt{52-x^2}}{2-x} = 1.$$
3. Given the function $f(x) = \mathrm{tg}\left(x - \frac{3\pi}{4}\right) \times (1-\sin 2x) - \cos 2x - 1.$
Find the domain of the function $y = f'(x)$; prove that $f'(x) = 0$ at all points $D(f'(x))$ and $f(\sqrt{7})$ is equal to an integer.
4. Let the quadratic equation
$$(3a+2)x^2 + (a-1)x + 4a+3 = 0$$
has roots $x_1, x_2$. Find all $a(a \in R)$ such that $x_1$ and $x_2$ satisfy conditions $x_1 < -1 < x_2 < 1$.

Oral exam assignment
1. The car starts moving from a standstill, accelerates quickly and after some time reaches a speed of $v = 10$ m/s. After this, the motor was turned off and automatically slowed down until it came to a complete stop. Find the autonomous speed along the entire route.
2. The rocket flies vertically upward and emits hot gases successively in two equal portions. The speed of gas outflow relative to the rocket is constant and equal to u. What should be the time interval between the portions being removed for the rocket to reach its highest altitude? Assume that fuel waste will occur instantly. Air resistance is challenging.
3. When an air bubble rises from the bottom of the lake to the surface, its volume increases 3 times. Find the depth of the lake.
4. In a cylinder with a volume of $V = 0,04$ m³, there is gas under a pressure of $p = 1,5 \cdot 10^5$ Pa at a temperature of $T = 300$ K. Determine how many gas molecules are in the balloon.
5. Three identical loaded particles, each with mass m and charge q, were placed at the vertices of a right-angled triangle on the $r_0$ side. Then the particles were simultaneously released, after which they began to scatter symmetrically under the influence of Coulomb repulsive forces. Find: 1) the speed of each part depending on the distance r between the particles; 2) the work done by the Coulomb forces acting on each particle when the parts fly apart at a very large distance from each other.
6. Both in parallel and in series connection of two identical batteries with emfs and internal resistance r, power P = 16 W is released on an external resistor, the resistance of which is R. How much power is released through this resistor if only one of the batteries is connected to it?
7. A semi-regular ACDE circuit with a sliding bridge of length l is in a single-mode magnetic field peredicular to the plane of the circuit (see Fig. 1).
