Example 3. In the triangle CAB (Fig. 4) the median $CD$ is drawn. On the sides $CA$ and $CB$ the points $K$ and $L$ are also selected, which is $|CK|=4|KA|, 2|CL|=|LB|$.
In what respect does the point $P$ of intersection of the lines $KL$ and $CD$ divide the median $CD$? In what ratio does the point $P$ divide the segment $KL$?

Solution. Let us consider a system of material points 1C, 4A, 4B, 8C (so $K = \frac{1C+4A}{5}, D = \frac{4A+4B}{8}, L = \frac{4B+8C}{12}$) and denote by $P$ the barycenter of this system. Then
$$P = \frac{1C+4A+4B+8C}{17} = \frac{(1C+4A)+(4B+8C)}{17} = \frac{5K+12L}{17},$$
that is, $P \in [KL]$;
$$P = \frac{(4A+4B)+(1C+8C)}{17} = \frac{8D+9C}{17},$$
that is, $P \in [CD]$.

Therefore, $P$ is the intersection point of the segments $KL$ and $CD$. From the written equalities it follows, according to the rule, that $|KP|:|PL| = 12:5, |CP|:|PD| = 8:9$.
