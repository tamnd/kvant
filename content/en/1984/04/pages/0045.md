This way a chain of any length can be assembled. When you decide to finish it, a new open ring is no longer added to the last open ring.

Finally, a few tips. Don't make very long chains. With the number of links, the large tridiatic tremiya forces, proportional to the weight of the chain and its length, become so great that they interrupt the movement of the ring, and the elongated wave can build up the middle of the chain. To make the trick more effective, you can assemble a chain of rings of different colors in approximately equal quantities and in apparent disorder. But at the top and bottom of the chain, place the rings in such a way that “resetting” the ring from above will result in a ring of the same color appearing at the bottom. This will make the ring slip along the chain more impressive.
