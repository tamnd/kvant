Mathematics

3. The subsectrix of an obtuse angle α of an equal trapezoid divides the major side of length a in the ratio 1:2. What is the area of ​​the trapezoid?

$$\begin{cases}
8 \sqrt{\frac{1}{4} (3 \log_8 x + \log_5 (y+1))^3} - \\

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. A boat floats from point B to point A, located higher up the river, and at the same time a boat floats from A to B. The boat reaches B, turns around and floats to A, then turns around, etc. The speeds of the boat and boats in still water are v and 14v/209, respectively. When the boat had covered half the distance between A and B, a second boat came out towards it from A to B. Determine the speed of the river if, at the moment when the second boat has passed halfway, the boat has reached B eight times.

2. The angle α is acute, and β is obtuse, and
$$\cos \alpha = \frac{2}{\sqrt{13}}, \ tg^2 \beta = 51 - 10\sqrt{26}.$$

Prove that α + 2β = 9π/4.
3. Select a set of points on the plane whose x and y coordinates satisfy the inequality $a^{y+3}|x| < 1 + 2a^{-y-3}|x|$, where a is a positive number.
4. Solve the equation
$$\frac{6}{7} \sqrt{1+tg^2 y} + 4 \cos^2 \frac{y}{2} = 5.$$
5. The steps and bottom of the well, which has a depth of H, were reinforced with a layer of concrete of thickness h. The initial section of the well is an equilateral triangle from side a. Find the volume of concrete used.

Option 3
(Faculty of Psychology; Department of Economic Cybernetics, Faculty of Economics; Department of Mathematical Linguistics, Faculty of Philology)

1. A car left city M for city L, and after t hours a car left from L for M. They were met another T hours later. Find the transit time of each car if their speeds are constant and they approach their destinations at the same time.
2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{4}{25^{-x}+8+16 \cdot 25^x} - \frac{5^x}{1+4 \cdot 25^x} + \frac{\sqrt{15}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{23}}{5} + \frac{1}{100}.$$
3. An equilateral triangle is inscribed in the smaller of two concentric circles of radii 1 and 2. Find the areas into which we divide the large circle by straight lines containing the sides of the triangle.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{(32 \sin^8 x)} + 4 \sin^6 x \leqslant 1 - \sin^4 x.$$
5. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log |y| x^2 + \log_x^2 |y| = 2, \\ x^4 + yx^2 = 10+y. \end{cases}$$

Determine the value of x. How many times does the temperature change?
