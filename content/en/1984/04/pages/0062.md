$V = \frac{m}{M} \frac{v}{2}, \frac{m}{M} < 1$.

The ratio $\beta$ in the case under consideration is equal to $\beta = \frac{mv^2/2 - m(v/2)^2/2 - MV^2/2}{mv^2/2} = \frac{1}{4}\left(3 - \frac{m}{M}\right)$.

$mv = (m + M) \frac{v}{2}$ and $\frac{m}{M} = 1$.

$\beta = \frac{mv^2/2 - (m + M)(v/2)^2/2 - MV^2/2}{mv^2/2} = \frac{1}{2}$.
