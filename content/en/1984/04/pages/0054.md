Correspondence Physics School at Moscow State University

Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov announces admission of students to grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher education, primarily for the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of the entrance task, which must be sent before September 10 to the address: 119899, ​​GSP, Moscow, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3FS. Please also enclose two copies of the questionnaire in the letter, written on sheets of thick paper measuring 7x12 cm and filled out according to the following template:

Last name, first name, patronymic

School number and address
school number 10, st. Pushkin, 3.

The decision of the 3FS admissions committee on enrollment will be communicated by October 15. Checked entrance papers will not be sent back.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on various sections of physics studied in high school. Solved assignments are assessed, solved and returned to students. At the end of the year, 9th grade 3FS students are transferred to 10th grade based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task

Admission to the 9th grade 3FS solves problems 1-4, and admission to the 10th grade solves problems 4-7.

1. Two Cathars swam at different speeds in the same direction along the river. When they drew level, a lifebuoy was thrown into the water from one of them. Some time after this, both Cathars simultaneously turned back and, at the same speeds relative to the water, headed towards the place where the circle was thrown. Which one met the circle earlier?

2. Two bars with masses $M_1$ and $M_2$, connected by an insoluble thread, are on a horizontal plane. Forces $\vec{F}_1$ and $\vec{F}_2$ are applied to them, making angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ with the horizon. The friction coefficients of the bars on the plane are the same and equal to $\mu$. Forces $\vec{F}_1$ and $\vec{F}_2$ are less than the weight of the bars in absolute value. Find the acceleration of the system and the tension of the thread if the system moves in the direction of the force $\vec{F}_1$.

3. Some forces only a body of mass $m=16$ kg up a smooth inclined plane with a length of $l=3,1$ m and an inclination of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizon. The speed of the body at the base of the inclined plane was $v_0=0,6$ m/s, and at its upper edge - $v=3,1$ m/s. What is the work done by the force?

4. Two identical carts, on which there are two identical wipers, move by inertia at the same speeds parallel to each other. At some point in time, snow begins to fall on the carts in a uniform stream. The janitor standing on one of the carts is constantly throwing snow away, and the janitor is sleeping on the second cart. Which cart will cover the same distance the fastest?

5. Two identical droplets of water each have one extra electron, and the force of electric repulsion of the droplets is balanced by the force of their mutual gravity. What are the radii of the droplets?

6. Two identical cylinders containing gas at a temperature of $t=0^\circ C$ are connected by a narrow horizontal tube with a diameter of $d=5$ mm, followed by a drop of rutga. The droplet divides the weight of the vessel into two equal parts of volume $V=200$ cm$^3$ each. How far will the droplet move if one balloon is heated by $\Delta t=2^\circ C$ and the other is cooled by the same amount? Neglect changes in vessel volume.

7. An electron moves with a speed $v_0$ into the space between the plates of a flat capacitor (through a hole in the lower plate) at an angle $\alpha$ to the plane of the plates. The distance between the plates is $d$, the potential difference is $U$, the lower plate is positively charged, the upper one is negatively charged. What curve does the electron follow as it moves? How close will it be to the top plate? Neglect strength.
