⟦folio 33⟧

F868. Rings $O$ and $O'$ are put on vertically fixed rods $AB$ and $A'B'$. An inextensible thread is tied to ring $O$, passed through ring $O'$ and secured at point $A'$ (Fig. 2). At the moment $\angle AOO' = \alpha$, ring $O'$ moves downward at speed $v$. Find the speed of the ring $O$ at this moment.

S. S. Krotov

F869. The vertical length of the brick pipe is filled with cast iron. The lower end of the pipe is maintained at a temperature of $T_1 > T_{nл}$ ($T_{nл}$ is the melting point of cast iron), the upper end is maintained at a temperature of $T_2 < T_{nл}$. The thermal conductivity of molten cast iron is $k$ times greater than that of solid iron. What part of the metal is in the molten state?

A. I. Buzdin

F870*. A monatomic ideal gas expands so that its molar temperature $c_x$ remains constant; in this case, the gas does work $A = 165$ J. Then the gas is isochorically heated to the initial temperature, for which an amount of heat $Q = 125$ J is supplied to it. Determine $c_x$.

A. A. Sheronov

F871. Initially, the uncharged capacitor embone $C$ will be connected to a series-connected battery with voltage $U_0$ and coils with inductance $L$. The current through the coils first increases and then decreases. At the moment when the current becomes zero, the capacitor is disconnected from the circuit and connected again, swapping its outputs. What is the maximum current that will flow through the coils after this? How will this current change if the switching process is repeated 1984 times? Neglect circuit resistance.

A. R. Zilberman

F872. A long sled, sliding on very smooth ice, hits the asphalt and stops before reaching half its length. After this, the sleigh is abruptly given its original speed, and it stops again. How does the time for braking relate in the first and second cases? What about braking distances?

S. S. Krotov
