Option 8
(Department of Geophysics, Faculty of Geology)

1. Find all real solutions to the equation
$$ (x+1)\sqrt{16x+17}=(x+1)(8x-23). $$

2. In the triangle $ABC$, the angle $BAC$ is a straight line, the lengths of the side $AB$ and $BC$ are equal to 1 and 2, respectively. The bissertis of the angle $ABC$ intersects the side $AC$ at the point $L$, $G$ is the point of intersection of the medians of the triangle $ABC$. Which is longer, length $BL$ or length $BG$?

3. Solve the inequality
$$ \log_{\sin \frac{\pi}{3}}(x^2-3x+2) \geq 2. $$

4. Find all pairs of real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfying the system of equations
$$ \begin{cases} 3\sin 3x+\cos y=-4, \\ x+y=\frac{3\pi}{2}. \end{cases} $$

5. According to the schedule, the boat passes along the river, the current speed of which is 5 km/h, the path from $A$ to $D$ is 15 km long in 1 hour. At the same time, leaving point $A$ at 12 o’clock, he arrives at points $B$ and $C$, distant from $A$ at a distance of 11 km and 13 km, respectively, at 12:20 and 12:40. It is known that if the boat moved from $A$ to $D$ without stopping at a constant speed $v$ (relative to water), then the sum of the abscile values of deviations from the schedule nailed to points $B$, $C$, $D$ would not exceed the time reduced by half an hour, required for the boat to travel 5 km at speed $v$ in standing water. Which point - $A$ or $D$ - is located upstream?

6. At the base of the paramid $SABC$ lies an equilateral triangle $ABC$, in which the side lengths $AB$ and $AC$ are equal to 1, and the cosine of the angle $BAC$ is $\frac{4}{5}$. Edge $SA$ is perpendicular to edges $AB$ and $AC$, angle $BAC$ is twice as large as angle $BSC$. Inside the paramid there is a straight circular cylinder, the generatrix of which is paramedna $BC$. What is the most possible lateral surface area of ​​such a cylinder?

Option 9
(Department of General Geology, Faculty of Geology)

1. Find all real solutions to the equation
$$ (x+1)\sqrt{x^2+x-2}=2x+2. $$

2. In triangle $ABC$, angle $BAC$ is a straight line, side lengths $AB$ and $BC$ are equal to 1 and 3, respectively. Point $K$ divides side $AC$ in a ratio of 7:1, counting from point $A$. Which is longer, length $AC$ or length $BK$?

3. Solve the inequality
$$ \log_3(5x^2+6x+1) \leq 0. $$

4. Find all pairs of real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfying the system of equations
$$ \begin{cases} \cos 4x+\sin 2y=-2, \\ x-y=2\pi. \end{cases} $$

5. The bus passes the path $AE$, consisting of sections $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DE$ with a length of 10 km, 5 km, 5 km, 6 km, respectively. At the same time, according to the schedule, leaving point $A$ at 9 o’clock, it passes point $B$ at 9 $\frac{1}{5}$, point $C$ at 9 $\frac{3}{8}$, point $D$ at 9 $\frac{2}{3}$.

At what constant speed $v$ should the bus move so that the sum of abscile values ​​of deviations from the schedule nailed to points $B$, $C$, $D$ does not exceed the time reduced by half an hour required for the boat to travel 5 km at a speed $v$ in still water. That the distance from the center of the lower base to the edge $BC$ is 11 $\frac{1}{16}$ the length of the median $AM$ of the triangle $ABC$. Find the volume of paramid $SABC$.

Option 10
(specialties “economic science” and “national economic planning” of the Faculty of Economics)

1. Solve the equation
$$ x^2+11+\sqrt{x^2+11}=42. $$

2. Find all $x$ satisfying the equation
$$ \sqrt{4-x^2}(\sin 2\pi x-3\cos \pi x)=0. $$

3. Solve the inequality
$$ \log_3(x^2-2)<\log_3\left(\frac{3}{2}|x|-1\right). $$

4. In triangle $ABC$, the medians dropped to sides $AC$ and $BC$ intersect at right angles. The length of side $AC$ is $b$, the length of side $BC$ is $a$. Find the length of side $AB$ of triangle $ABC$.

5. The store sells red and blue pencils. A red pencil costs 17 kopecks, a blue pencil costs 13 kopecks. You can spend no more than 4 rubles 95 kopecks on the purchase of pencils. When ordering blue pencils, the number of red pencils should not differ by more than five. It is necessary to enclose the maximum possible total number of red and blue pencils, while the number of red pencils must be enclosed as little as possible. How many red and blue pencils can be concluded under the given conditions?

6. For each non-negative value of $a$, solve the inequality
$$ a^3x^4+6a^2x^2-x+9a+3 \geq 0. $$

Option 11
(specialty “political economy” of the Faculty of Economics)

1. Solve the equation
$$ 2^{x+2}\cdot 5^{x+2}=2^{3x}\cdot 5^{3x}. $$

2. Find all $x$ satisfying the equation
$$ \lg 3x=\lg 5x. $$
