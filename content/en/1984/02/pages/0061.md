Faculty of Chemistry and Faculty of Soil Science

1. The package engine, launched vertically upward from the surface of the Earth, operated for a time of $t=1$ min and imparted to the package a constant acceleration of $a=3g$. What is the maximum height $h$ reached by the package? The free fall acceleration is considered constant and equal to $g=9,8 \text{ m/c}^2$. Neglect air resistance.

2. Two balls of the same radius $R=1$ cm were dropped from a balloon: one was aluminum (density $\varrho_1=2,7 \cdot 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$), the other was iron (density $\varrho_2=7,8 \cdot 10^3 \text{ kg/m}^3$). The balls are connected by a long thin thread. Find the tension of the $T$ threads after the movement of the balls is established due to air resistance, that is, they acquire constant speeds.

3. The sealed cylinder is divided into equal parts by a gas piston. In one half of the state there is an ideal gas with molar mass $M_1$, in the other - $M_2$. The masses of gases, their volumes and temperatures are the same. The piston was released. By what fraction $\Delta V/V$ of its initial value does the volume of one of the parts of the cylinder increase (or the volume of another part decrease) after establishing equilibrium at the same temperature?

4. Two circuits are made from two identical conductors—a square and a round. Both circuits are placed in the same plane in a one-dimensional, time-varying magnetic field. In a round circuit, the inductance is constant current $I_1=0,4$ A. Find the current strength $I_2$ in a square circuit.

5. A converging light beam falls on a diverging lens with focal length - $F$ and coincases at a point lying in the focal plane on the other side of the lens. At what distance $f$ from the lens is the beam coinset if the scattering lens is replaced by a coinciding one with focal length + $F$?

2. $x, \frac{1}{6} + 4\pi k, x_2 = \frac{5}{6} \pi + 2\pi l(k, l, Z)$. Note. This equation is equivalent to the system

$$\begin{cases}
\cos 2x = \sin x, \\
\sin x + \cos \frac{x}{2} > 0.
\end{cases}$$

The function $\cos \frac{x}{2}$ has a period $4\pi$.

3. $\frac{1}{2}(p-a)^2tg \frac{a}{2}$. Note. $|BK| = p-a$.

$$F(z) = |12z-5| - |12z-7| + |24z+13|.$$

$$\begin{cases}
