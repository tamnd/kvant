The relationship between these two components of the complete durability of the product depends on the properties of the material itself and on many external factors, but, fortunately, the second period most often extends over a fairly long period.

The ability of materials to work in the presence of growing cracks in it is called survivability. Of course, the accuracy of determining the duration of survivability depends on the sensitivity of the method used to detect cracks.

On the railway, each passenger can observe the specific questioning of this situation from the window of the carriage. Many people have probably seen flaw detector cars standing on the tracks, but rail inspection is still mostly done manually. If you travel a lot by rail, you may have seen the following picture: a worker in nauchniki is moving along the track, pushing a cart on wheels in front of him; device on a trolley - ultrasonic flaw detector. The worker walks around his section of the road once every 10 days, and if there is a crack in the rail being checked that is larger than a penny, a warning signal is generated in the science. The rail will be replaced with a new one, and the threat of disaster has passed.
