Option 4
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\cos \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + \cos \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + 4 \sin x = 2 + \sqrt{2} (1 - \sin x).$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\left(\sqrt{x^2 - 4x + 3} + 1\right) \log_5 \left(\frac{x}{5}\right) + \frac{1}{x} \left(\sqrt{8x - 2x^2 - 6} + 1\right) \leq 0.$$
3. A triangular prism $ABCA'B'C'$ with a lower base $ABC$ and side edges $AA', BB', CC'$ is dissected by a plane passing through points E, F, C, where point E is the middle of edge AA', and point F lies on edge BB', and $|BF|:|FB'| = 1:2$. Find the volume of the part of the prism $ABCA'B'C'$ enclosed between the cutting plane and the lower base of this prism, if it is known that the volume of the prism is equal to V.
4. Find the coordinates of the point lying on the graph of the function $y = 1 + \cos x$ at $0 \leq x \leq \pi$ and least distant from the line $x\sqrt{3} + 2y + 4 = 0$.
5. The values ​​of the variables x, y are real numbers. Find all integer values ​​of n for which the system of equation
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 6x^2 + 24y(x + y) + 2(3n - 2)x + 4(3n - 2)y + 3 = 0, \\ 4(x^2 + y^2) + (4n + 2)y + 2n^2 = 8xy + (4n + 2)x + \frac{5}{2} \end{array} \right.$$
has a solution. Given the found integer values ​​of n, find all solutions of this system.

$$8 + 6|3 - \sqrt{x + 5}| > x.$$

Option 6
(faculty of postal science)

1. The field is divided into three sections. During the day, half of the first plot and 3/4 of the second plot were plowed, and the third plot, which makes up a quarter of the entire field, was completely plowed. The area of ​​the field plowed per day is twice as large as the area of ​​the second plot. What fraction of the area is the area that is washed in a day?
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos \left(x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \cos 2x = 1.$$
3. Find the real x and y satisfying the system of equations
$$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + 2y + 1 = 3, \\ 4x + 4y = 32. \end{array} \right.$$
4. In triangle ABC, the bisector of angle ABC intersects side AC at point K. It is known that |BC| = 2, |KC| = 1, |BK| = $\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC.
5. Find all values ​​of α that satisfy the condition 2 < α < 5 and for which the equation
$$\log_2(3 - |\sin \alpha|) = \cos \left(\pi x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$$
with respect to x has at least one solution satisfying the condition 2 ≤ x ≤ 3.
