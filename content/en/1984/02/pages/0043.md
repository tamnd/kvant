Problem
quantum

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are not standard, but solving them does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are given stars. After forming a task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these tasks are published for the first time. Solutions to the problem from this issue can be sent no later than April 30, 1984 to the address: 103006, Moscow, K-6, st. Gorky, 32/1, «Kvant». In the “To” column, write: “Problem «Kvant» No. 2 - 84” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example, “M846, M847” or “F858”. Solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) are assigned in different envelopes. Include an envelope with your address written on it (you will receive the results of the decision verification in this envelope). The condition of each original problem proposed for publication is assigned in a separate envelope in duplicate with your solution to this problem (on the envelope place: “Problem «Kvant», a new problem in physics” or “... a new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study.

M846. Prove that the arithmetic mean of the side lengths of a regular polygon is less than the arithmetic mean of the lengths of its diagonals.

V. F. Lev

M847. The square is divided into $n \times n$ cells. Two players take turns tracing one side of the same square (you cannot trace the same side twice). Who will win if played correctly if
a) the player who first builds a closed line wins;
b) the player who is forced to build a closed line first loses.

I. Vetrov, A. Kodan

M848. a) Graph the function
$$f_0(x) = \left| x - 1 \right| - 2 \left| x \right| - 3$$

b) figure 1 shows the graphs of three “piecewise linear” functions $f_1, f_2, f_3$. Write the formulas for them in the form
$$y = kx + b + c_1 |x - a_1| + \dots + c_m |x - a_m|,$$
where $k, b, c_i, a_i$ are some numbers, $m$ is the number of break points of the graph ($a_i$ are the abscissas of break points, $i = 1, 2, \dots, m$).

c) Write down the function $f_0$ from point a) in the same form.

d) * Some functions are a combination of linear functions, “modulo” and the operation of addition, and the modulo sign is used in its notation $n$ times (in example a) $n = 4$. What is the maximum number of breaks (at each $n$) that its graph can have?

P. G. Satyanov

M849. From the numbers 1, 2, ..., 7, taken in different orders, seven seven-digit numbers are made. Prove that the sum of the seventh powers of several of these numbers cannot equal the sum of the seventh powers of the remaining numbers.

G. A. Galperin
