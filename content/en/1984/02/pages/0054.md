Kitchen weather
Once again, mother announces to six-year-old Marina why it is raining. Now I brought her to the kitchen:
- Look here. When water boils in a pan, steam settles on its lid, and then, as it cools, it falls back into the pan in drops. So it will rain.

Sorry about the argon
When in 1895 the English scientists Rayleigh and Ramsay discovered the first inert gas - argon (for this discovery they were later awarded the Nobel Prize) and introduced the “zero” column for noble gases in the periodic table, most scientists were skeptical about this achievement. The extremely patient and correct Rayleigh replied: “No doubt the result is very strange. Indeed, I see some signs that the anomalous properties of argon will be overcome as a kind of indictment against us. But we worked very conscientiously. The facts stuck to us, and all we can do now is apologize for ourselves and for the gas.”
(Based on materials from the journal “Uspekhi Fizicheskikh Nauk”, 1966, vol. 88, No. 1, p. 149.)
