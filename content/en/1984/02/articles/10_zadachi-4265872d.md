⟦folio 33⟧

Tasks

1. There were matches in the box. Their number was doubled, and then 8 matches were removed. The rest of the matches were doubled again, and then 8 matches were taken away again. When this operation was performed for the third time, there was not a single match left in the box. How many were there at first?

2. A and K are different numbers. Another 7 digits in the multiplication example are replaced with asterisks. Reconstruct the numbers in this example.

3. What can be said about points A, B and C on a plane if it is known that for any point M of this plane the distance AM is less than at least one of the distances BM and CM?

4. Sasha and Grisha often rode bicycles along a forest path from one end of the forest to the other. One day Grisha, having ridden 2 km through the forest, met Sasha, who was repairing his bicycle. The friends talked about school matters, and Grisha moved on. Having reached the edge of the forest, he noticed that 24 minutes had passed since he entered the forest. We turned around, he went in the opposite direction and after 2 km he again met Sasha, who was repairing his bicycle, but in a different place. “How far have you traveled?” asked Grisha. “Only 900 meters,” Sasha answered him. Determine the time of their first conversation if it is known that Grisha drove all the time at the same speed of 10 km/h.

5. The two conical-shaped vessels shown in figure are filled to the top with the same amount of water. The bottoms of the vessels have the same answers. From which vessel will the water flow out faster if these answers are opened?

These tasks were suggested to us by R. Yu. Vinokur, V. D. Vyun, G. A. Galperin, A. S. Kozinsky, S. R. Sefibekov.
