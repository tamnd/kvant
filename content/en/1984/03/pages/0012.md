Cubic parabola
Academician L. S. PONTRYAGIN

To understand this article, you need to be able to calculate the derivative of a polynomial and know the theorem that if a function $f(x)$, defined on the interval $a_1 \leq x \leq a_2$, has a positive derivative on the interval $a_1 < x < a_2$, and perhaps has a derivative equal to zero at the ends of the interval, then it returns on the entire interval $a_1 \leq x \leq a_2^*$).

So for two points $x_1, x_2$ satisfying the condition

$$a_1 \leq x_1 < x_2 \leq a_2$$

(1)

there is inequality

$$f(x_1) < f(x_2).$$

(2)

In addition, you need to know what the second and third derivatives of the function $f(x)$ are. The second derivative $f''(x)$ is the derivative of the derivative of the function $f(x)$, that is, it is given by the formula

$$f''(x) = \left(f'(x)\right)'.$$

(3)

The third derivative $f'''(x)$ function $f(x)$ is the derivative of the second derivative, that is, it is given by the formula

$$f'''(x) = \left(f''(x)\right)'.$$

(4)

The article is devoted to the study of the graph of a function

$$y = g(x) = x^3 + a_1x^2 + a_2x + a_3.$$

(5)

This graph is called a cubic parabola.

$$y = f(x) = x^3 - px,$$

(6)
