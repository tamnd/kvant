⟦folio 34⟧

Tasks

1. When dividing the number 2 • 3=6 by 4, we get a remainder of 2, when dividing the number 3 • 4=12 by 5, we get a remainder of 2. Is it true that the remainder of division, the product of two consecutive numbers by the number following them, is always equal to 2?

2. Two little kids ride their bikes late at night. One of them has a bicycle with large wheels, and the other has small ones, but they eat at the same speed. Which of them has a brighter light, powered by a dynamo on the wheel rim?

3. Is there a three-digit number equal to the product of its digits?

4. Half-circles are built on the radii of a quarter circle, as on diameters (see figure). What has a larger area: the total (red) part of these half-circles or the (blue) part of the quarter circle that is not covered by it?

5. The number 9, which started the three-digit number, was moved to the end of the number. The result was a number 216 less. What was the original number?

These tasks were offered to us:
H. K. Antonovich, S. B. Dvoryaninov,
S. H. OLEXNIK, A. P. Savin, A. G. Samosvat.
