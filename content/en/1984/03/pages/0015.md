Let us now deal with the general cubic parabola (5). Let us first find out how many real roots the polynomial (5) has.

Consider equation (14)
$$x^3 - px = c.$$

As already mentioned, for $p < 0$ this equation has only one real root. For $p = 0$ this equation also has only one real root, with the exception of the case $c = 0$, when it has a triple root $x = 0$. If $c = 0$, equation (14) has three roots at
$$f(x_2) \leq c \leq f(x_1)$$
(see (15)), where
$$f(x_1) = \frac{2}{3}p\sqrt{\frac{p}{3}}, \quad f(x_2) = -\frac{2}{3}p\sqrt{\frac{p}{3}},$$
(see (12)), so inequality (15) can be written in the form
$$|c| \leq \frac{2}{3}p\sqrt{\frac{p}{3}},$$
(31)

and for extreme values ​​$c$ from the interval (31) has one simple and one double root. Outside the segment (31), equation (14) has only one real root.

Polynomial (5) can be reduced to the form
$$\eta = f(\xi) = \xi^3 - p\xi$$
(32)

substitution of variables
$$x = \xi + \alpha, \quad y = \eta + \beta.$$ (33)

Such a replacement means a parallel shift of the coordinate system.

Making substitution (33), we get
$$\eta = \xi^3 + (3\alpha + a_1)\xi^2 + (3\alpha^2 + 2a_1\alpha + a_2)\xi + (\alpha^3 + a_1\alpha^2 + a_2\alpha + a_3) - \beta.$$ (34)

In order for the polynomial (5) to be reduced to the form (32), it is sufficient that the conditions be met
$$3\alpha + a_1 = 0, \quad \alpha^3 + a_1\alpha^2 + a_2\alpha + a_3 - \beta = 0.$$ (35)

From the first equation of system (35) we have
$$\alpha = -\frac{a_1}{3}.$$ (36)

Substituting this equation for $a$ into the second equation of system (35), we obtain
$$\beta = \frac{2a_1^3}{27} - \frac{a_1a_2}{3} + a_3.$$ (37)

Assuming that $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are given by formulas (36) and (37), we can rewrite (34) as
$$\eta = f(\xi) = \xi^3 - p\xi,$$
(38)

Where
$$p = \frac{a_1^2}{3} - a_2.$$ (39)

The geometric shape of the cubic parabola (5) coincides with the geometric formula of the parabola (38). The latter depends on the sign of the quantity $p$ (39).
