10. Let us write down the laws of conservation of momentum and energy for the proton-atom gel system:

$$mv_0 = -mv_0/2 + Mv,$$
$$\frac{mv_0^2}{2} = \frac{mv_0^2}{8} + \frac{Mv^2}{2} + W,$$

where $m = 1$ a.e.m. $= 1,67 \cdot 10^{-27}$ kg – proton mass, $M = 4$ a.e.m. is the mass of atoms of the gel, $W$ is the excitation energy of atoms. From here we will find

$$W = \frac{3}{8} mv_0^2 \left(1 - \frac{3m}{M}\right).$$

Returning to its original state, atom emits a quantum of light, the energy of which is equal to the excitation energy:

$$hv = W,$$

where does the wavelength of the emitted light come from?

$$\lambda = \frac{c}{v} = \frac{hc}{W} = \frac{8hc}{3mv_0^2(1-3m/M)} \approx 0,6 \cdot 10^{-6} \text{ m}.$$
