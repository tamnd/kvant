$$\sqrt{\sin x - \frac{1}{2}} \cdot \log_3 (x - 2) = 0.$$

$$f(x) = (2x - 1) \cdot \sqrt[4]{x - a}.$$

3. The basis for the calls $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ is the equilateral triangle $ABC$, whose $|AB| = |AC|$, $\hat{A} = 2\alpha$ and height $AD$ have length $h$. The side face $BB_1C_1C$ is a rectangle, the plane of which makes an angle of magnitude $\varphi$ with the plane of the base. A plane is drawn through the point $A_1$, perpendicular to the edge $AA_1$ and intersecting the calls. Find the volumes of polyhedra on which the cutting plane splits the call if $|BB_1| = b$.

4. A set of identical albums and identical Kingstons costs 40 rubles, and the price of an album is 2 rubles, and the price of kings is 3 rubles. If the price of the album was $k$ rubles, kings - 3 rubles, then the weight of the groan set was 39 rubles. How many albums and how many kings are there in a set, if it is known that there are at least as many kings as there are albums?

Option 2

1. The difference between the product of the twenty-first and thirteenth terms of an arithmetic progression and the product of the fourteenth and twenty-second terms of the same progression is equal to $p$. Determine the difference of this progression if the sum of its sixteenth and nineteenth terms is equal to $q$.

2. A plane passing through the point $A$ of the lateral edge $PQ$ of a regular triangular straight line $PQRT$ and parallel to the edge $TR$ intersects the straight line so that the section is a triangle, all internal angles of which have the same size. Find the area of ​​this triangle if it is known that the apothem of the lateral face of the straight line is equal to $k$, the lateral face $PTR$ makes an angle of magnitude $\varphi$ and $|AQ| = |0,75|AP$ with the plane of the base.

3. Find the integrals of a monotically increasing function
$$f(x) = (c - 3) \cdot 5^x - (3c + 4) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^x + 7.$$

4. Solve the equation
$$\cos^3 \frac{x}{3} \cdot (2 \cos x - \sin 2x) = \left(3 \sin x - \frac{5}{6} \sin^2 x\right) \cdot \sin^3 \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{x}{3}\right).$$
