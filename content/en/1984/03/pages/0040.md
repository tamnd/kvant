⟦folio 38⟧

F863. In a Konchek hole with an apex angle of 2α, a ball jumps, elastically reflecting from the wall of the hole. The points of collision of the ball with the wall lie at the same height (Fig. 2); the time from impact to impact is constant and equal to G. What is the average angular velocity of the ball around the axis of the hole if the maximum speed of the ball v lies in the range from $\frac{1}{2}gT$ to $gT/2$ sin α?

O. Ya. Savchenko

F864. A boy slowly climbs a snowy hill and drags a sled behind him on a rope. The rope is always parallel to the tangent to the slide at the point where the boy is. The top of the slide is located at a height H and at a distance l from its base (Fig. 3). How much work will the boy do when lifting the sled up the hill? The mass of the sled is m, the friction coefficient of the sled on the snow is μ.

A. I. Buzdin

F865. Estimate the size of a mercury atom if you know: σ is the surface tension coefficient of mercury (in units of $\frac{\text{энергия}}{\text{площадь}}$), q is the density of mercury, r is the specific heat of vaporization of mercury.

Y. Rodionov, A. Yakovlev

F866. The electric bath is connected through an ideal diode to an alternating voltage network (Fig. 4). Find the readings of the thermal ampere (A) if it is known that during the time t=1 hour m=1.078 g of copper is released to the cathode. Electrochemical equivalent of copper k=0.33·10⁻⁶ kg/Kl.

B. A. Mukushev

F867. Imagine that you are standing in front of a large sheet of thick clear glass (for example, in front of a glass display case). How to determine the thickness of glass if there is no access to its edges (say, the edges of the glass are walled up in the walls), you cannot enter the room behind the glass, and you cannot break the glass? You have at your disposal a ruler, a square, paper, a pencil and a pocket calculator for making calculations.

M. V. Semenov

M851. The square ABCD is of length 1. Points P and Q are chosen on sides AB and AD respectively so the perimeter of ABC is 2. Prove that $\widehat{PCQ} = 45^\circ$.

A. B. Khodulev

M852. Suppose x, y, z are the lengths of a triangle's sides. Prove that the number

$$\left| \frac{x-y}{x+y} + \frac{y-z}{x+z} + \frac{z-x}{z+x} \right|$$

is less than a) 1; b) $1/8$.

A. V. Ermilov
