1. A tape and a ball of thread.
The tape is washed from one reel to another. Prove that during the entire rewinding time, the sum of the squares of the radius $R$ of the circle formed by the wound part of the tape and the radius $r$ of the circle on the other reel is constant:
$$R^2 + r^2 = \text{const.}$$

A similar conservation law for cubes of radii ($R^3 + r^3 = \text{const}$)
the approximation is performed by rewinding

threads from one ball to another. Why?
2. Pendulum. A camera is fixedly installed in front of the swinging magnet and many pictures are taken on the same kadar. Pictures were taken exactly every second, one after the other, with instantaneous exposures.
As a result, the superimposed picture turned out to be the same as on figure - two positions of the magnet, symmetrical relative to the vertical.
What is the most possible value of the period of oscillation of a magnet?
M. Mamikon
