Moscow State University
them. M. V. Lomonosova

Matematika

Written exam

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

There is a lot to be afraid of during the entrance exams in mathematics to the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, believing that applicants are offered puzzlingly difficult problems. These fears are unfounded. Entrance examinations are conducted in accordance with the “Program of entrance examinations for applicants to higher educational institutions of the USSR.” The tasks offered during the entrance exams require only a good knowledge of this program to be solved, being able to use the rules and techniques learned at school. No additional knowledge is required from applicants. Often young people, taught by the programs of physics and mathematics circles and the tasks of various olympiads, do not decide to apply for mechanics at Moscow State University. Meanwhile, many of them are well prepared and could successfully participate in the competition.

Two or three tasks in the written exam options are quite simple and fully accessible to anyone who has mastered the school curriculum. However, it is not easy to get a high mark on a written exam, since, along with easy problems, the options also include more complex problems, the solution of which requires a fairly high mathematical culture.

In 1979, four hours were allotted for completing written work. To receive a positive mark, it was enough to solve two problems correctly.
