⟦folio 12⟧

V.I. Lenin at the GOELRO map.

Destroying the basic ideas of classical physics, which are at the same time habitual images of Venyakovsky thinking, developing in every person as a result of hurtful experience, new physics broke at several points with the ability to imagine processes visually, using models. According to the great representative of classical, mechanical physics W. Thomson: “The true meaning of the question: are we a concept, or are we not a concept of a physical phenomenon? - property to the following: can we build our own mechanical model or not? There were new facts, however, and their meaning now remains unclear. Given this state of affairs, at a certain stage of development, the only possible way to create a physical theory is a mathematical description of the found laws and further extrapolation of the obtained mathematical forms up to new experience confirming the extrapolation.

Combining experimental data and mathematical forms and relying on the classical scheme, a modern physicist builds his theory. But on this inevitable path it is extremely easy to get carried away on the wrong trail.

“The great success of naturalgonium,” writes Lenin, “approaching such homogeneous and simple elements of matter, the laws of motion of which allow mathematical processing, gives rise to the oblivion of the matter of mathematics. “Matter disappears”, only equations remain. At a new stage of development and, supposedly, in a new way, the true Kantian idea is obtained: reason is prescribed by the laws of nature”*).

The collapse of the mechanical worldview and the enormous growth of mathematical symbolism carried physicists either towards a stubborn, stubborn non-recognition of the new physics, hopeless attempts at a mechanical explanation of non-mechanical phenomena,

*) V.I. Lenin. PSS, vol. 18, p. 326.
