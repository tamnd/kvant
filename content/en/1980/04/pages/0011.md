⟦folio 9⟧

V.I. Lenin signed the decree “On admission to higher educational institutions of the RSFSR” and “Resolution of the Council of People’s Commissars on preferential admission to higher educational institutions of representatives of the proletariat and the poorest peasantry.” The next step towards attracting workers to higher education institutions was the “Resolution of the Council of People’s Commissars on workers’ faculties,” adopted on September 17, 1920.

Workers' faculties played a huge role in the transformation of higher education, filling the VSU classrooms with workers and peasants.

All measures taken to restructure the education system were aimed at educating young specialists who would enthusiastically take on the restoration of the national economy and economy and would achieve success in socialist science, literature and technology. Speaking at the III All-Russian Congress of the Russian Communist Youth Union on October 2, 1920, V.I. Lenin said: “Only by radically transforming the matter of teaching, organizing separate branches.

Speaking at the XXV Congress of the CPSU, L. I. Brezhnev said: “The need for further service improvement of the entire general education system and, first of all, the secondary school is obvious. In modern conditions, when the amount of knowledge necessary for a person increases sharply and rapidly, it is no longer possible to place the main bet on the conditions of a certain sum of facts. It is important to instill the ability to independently replenish your knowledge and navigate the rapid flow of scientific and political information. There's a lot of work ahead of us here. Of course, the work is prudent, thoughtful, without unnecessary disruption or hasty decisions. What is required here? Apparently, the best training for teachers, the introduction of teaching methods themselves in accordance with the requirements of life, and the provision of modern teaching aids for schools, including educational ones”**).

*) V.I. Lenin. PSS. vol. 41, p. 301.
**) Materials of the XXV Congress of the CPSU (M., Politizdat, 1976), p. 77.
