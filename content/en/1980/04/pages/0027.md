Analysis of the results obtained

Let's summarize. What can be proven by continuing these experiments? Firstly, there is a lens in the eye. Secondly, that the cornea of ​​the eye is very thin and the curvature of its physical features is practically the same. Thirdly, that the refractive indices of the aqueous humor and vitreous body differ very little from the refractive indices of the cornea and lens. Fourthly, that accommodation of the eyes is caused by a change in the curvature of the surface of the lens.
