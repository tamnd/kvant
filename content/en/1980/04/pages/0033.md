⟦folio 31⟧

F628. In the upper tube of length $H=152$ cm, sealed at the lower end, there is a stolnik of air of height $h=76$ cm, locked with a stolnik of rutia (Fig. 2). Atmospheric pressure is $10^5$ Pa, and temperature is $t_0=17^\circ\text{C}$. To what temperature $t_1$ should the air in the tube be heated so that all the rue pours out?

E. Butikov, A. Bykov, A. Kondratev

F629. The mass ball $m$ is suspended from the outside of the rigidity $k$ (spring pendulum). The suspension point of the spring performs a vertical harmonic oscillation with amplitude $a$ and frequency $\omega$. How does the ball move during steady oscillation?

F630. An element of an atomic battery is a capacitor, on one of the plates of which a radioactive drug is applied, emitting α-particles at a speed of $v_0=2,2 \cdot 10^6$ m/s. Determine the emf of this element. The ratio of the charge of an α-particle to its mass is $k=4,8 \cdot 10^7$ C/kg.

F631. A wire spiral connected to a city network is heated by electric current. Half of the spiral begins to be cooled (for example, with water). How does this affect the amount of heat generated by this half of the coil? the other half? all in a spiral?

V. Gorbunova

F632. Estimate how many times the illumination of a sunbeam, which is received on a vertical wall at noon, is less than the illumination by direct sunlight, if the sunbeam is sent to the wall by a mirror with a diameter of $d=10$ cm from a distance of $l=50$ cm.

Chess competition

Dear guys!

Over the several months of existence of the chess page «Kvant» we have become convinced that among you there are many fans of chess, this ancient and eternal young game. We have received many letters from you with solutions to problems published on the chess page. In your letters you ask various questions on the topic of chess, and also offer to notify the number of problems for independent solution. All this led us to the idea of ​​announcing a Chess Competition «Kvant». We will start this competition from the second noon. In it you will be offered chess and chess-mathematical problems, etys, combinations, puzzles.

Solutions to competition problems should be sent (in one copy and in a separate envelope) to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka, 21/16, editing the journal «Kvant». Don’t forget to mark the envelope: “Chess competition” and the magazine number (for example: “No. 7-80”).

The deadline for submitting answers to assignments is the last day of the next month. So, you will need to send decisions under item No. 7 no later than August 31, 1980.

The results of the Chess Competition «Kvant» will be announced at the beginning of next month. We will invite the winner of the competition to the editorial office of the magazine for a meeting with the world chess champion Anatole. Karpov. The winner will be awarded chess and mathematical literature authored by A. Karpov.
