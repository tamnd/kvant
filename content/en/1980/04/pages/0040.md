N. Mikhailova

Fruit
"enlightenment"

On Sunday afternoon I calculated that after the morning trip to the cinema with Fedka I had four kopecks left, and by Wednesday we were ordered to bring a new drawing book to school. My mood has deteriorated. We need to go to dad and ask for money again.

“Dad,” I said, “I need money for a drawing book. Give it, please." “Interesting,” dad replied. “And to whom did my mother and I give the evening fifty kopecks?”

“Dad, Fedka and I went to the cinema today. And how much Fedka loves lemonade and puff pastries, you just can’t imagine!”

“Everything is clear,” said dad. -You fell victim to others. It’s impossible to force you, poor thing, to drink lemonade, and you can’t even put a straw in your mouth. But I’m ready to do anything for a friend!”

“Dad, come on, stop mocking me. I love lemonade and straws too. It’s just that Fedka didn’t have any money today, and we wasted my fifty kopecks.”

“Another conversation! - Dad answered. - However, if you are still applying for fifty kopecks, you will have to work hard. If you solve a problem, you’ll get fifty kopecks. Agreed?".

"Agreed. Give me the problem quickly, otherwise I don’t have time to solve it.”

“Okay, I’ll give you until Tuesday. And the task is this: Imagine that you and Fedka are now going to the store. How many of you need a drawing book? We came to the store and suddenly it turns out that Fedya lacks one kopeck to buy a notebook, and you are thirty. You pooled your money to buy at least one notebook, but you still didn’t have enough money. How much does a notebook cost?

Dad wrote the conditions for me on a piece of paper. I went to my room and began to think, but until lunch I didn’t come up with anything. Then they came as guests, and then they sent me to bed.

On Monday, I immediately ran home after school, delivered the conditions and sat down to solve it again.

So, I told myself, I have 4 kopecks left. And I didn’t have 30 kopecks for a notebook:
