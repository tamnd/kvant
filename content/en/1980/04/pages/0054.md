2. The weightless lever can rotate freely in a vertical plane around a horizontal axis passing through point O (Fig. 2). The masses $m_1$ and $m_2$, attached to the ends of the lever, are located, respectively, at distances $l_1$ and $l_2$ from the axis. 3. With what acceleration will two bodies rigidly fastened together and having masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ move along an inclined plane? The friction coefficients between the bodies and the inclined plane are $k_1$ and $k_2$, respectively. The angle of the inclined plane is $\alpha$.

4. An ideal gas does work. In this case, the state of the gas changes in a closed cycle consisting of two isochores and two isobars (Fig. 3). The gas temperatures at points 1 and 3 are $T_1$ and $T_3$, respectively. Determine the work done by one mole of gas if it is known that points 2 and 4 lie on the same isotherm.

5. We mixed $V_1=1$ m³ of air with humidity $\alpha_1=20\%$ and $V_2=2$ m³ of air with humidity $\alpha_2=30\%$. Both portions were taken at the same temperatures. Determine the relative humidity $\alpha$ obtained in the air, considered an ideal gas.

6. In a cylinder under the piston in a space of $V_1=1,5$ l there is air and saturated water vapor at a temperature $t_1=20^\circ\text{C}$. What will be the relative humidity of the air in the cylinder if the volume is reduced to $V_2=0,1$ l and the temperature is increased to $t_2=100^\circ\text{C}$? The pressure of saturated water vapor at $20^\circ\text{C}$ is $p_{h1}=2,2 \cdot 10^3$ Pa. (Unsaturated vapor contained in air is considered an ideal gas.)

7. A parallel-plate capacitor was connected to a constant voltage source $U=300$ V. Then the source was disconnected. How much work must be done to move the capacitor plates to a distance twice that of the first analog? The initial capacitance of the capacitor is $C_0=100$ pF $=10^{-10}$ F.

8. In the circuit shown at figure 4, the resistances are $R_1=2$ ohm, $R_2=6$ ohm, $R_3=8,95$ ohm and $R_4=3$ ohm. To what temperature must the resistance $R_3$ be heated so that no current flows through the galvanometer? The temperature coefficient of resistance is $\alpha=0,1 \cdot 10^{-3}$ K$^{-1}$. The initial temperature of resistance $R_3$ is equal to $t_0=20^\circ\text{C}$.

9. The optical system consists of a thin converging lens with a focal length of $F_1=4$ cm and a thin diverging lens with a focal length of $F_2=-3$ cm. Distance between lenses $l=2$ cm. Determine at what points (at what distance from the lenses) parallel rays of light ooze, incident on the system from one side and the other?

10. The ends of a piece of pipe, the inner side of which is blackened, enters with resistance (on one side) and resistance (on the other side) of a lens. Pipe diameter $D=3$ cm, length of pipe section $l=12$ cm. From the side of the collecting lens, a wide beam of parallel rays falls onto the tube along its axis. The rays that pass through the pipe emerge from it as a parallel bundle with a diameter of $d=1$ cm. What are the focal lengths of the lens?
