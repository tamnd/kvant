2. A thin disk of mass $M=500$ g and radius $R=15$ cm (Fig. 6) lies on a horizontal table. A thin weightless vertical rod $l=40$ cm long is fixed in the center of the disk. A small ball weighing $m=300$ g is suspended from the upper end of the rod on a weightless inextensible thread. The length of the thread is less than the length of the rod. The ball is set in motion so that it describes a circle in a horizontal plane around the rod. What maximum angle $\alpha$ can the thread make with the rod so that the disk does not come off the table? Assume that due to friction the disc cannot slide on the table.

3. A tennis ball will be hit with a packet at the very surface of the Earth, giving it an initial speed of $\vec{v}$ ($|\vec{v}|=20$ m/s), directed at an angle of $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the horizon. The ball flies towards a vertical wall, moving in a plane perpendicular to this wall, and experiences an absilton elastic collision with the wall. The step is located at a distance of $l=15$ m from the point of impact. At what distance $x$ from the point of impact will the ball fall to the ground? Neglect air resistance.

4. Dry air fills a closed vessel with a volume of $V=25$ l at a pressure of $p_1=10^5$ Pa and a temperature of $t_1=-23^\circ\text{C}$. A piece of ice weighing $m=9$ g is placed in a vessel and the vessel is heated to a temperature of $t_2=127^\circ\text{C}$. Determine the pressure of moist air. The gas constant is $R=8,3$ J/(mol·K), the pressure of saturated water vapor at a temperature $t_2=127^\circ\text{C}$ is $p_h=2,5 \cdot 10^5$ Pa.

5. An aluminum cone with an angle between the axis and the generatrix equal to $\alpha=30^\circ$ stands on a horizontal table. A thin iron ring is placed on the cone, the plane of which is $h=5$ cm below the top of the cone. By what amount $\Delta h$ does the distance from the top of the cone to the plane of the ring change if the temperature of the cone and ring increases by values ​​$\Delta t=100^\circ\text{C}$? Linear expansion coefficients of aluminum $a_1=2,4 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$ and iron $a_2=1,2 \cdot 10^{-5}$ K$^{-1}$. There is no friction of the ring on the surface of the cone.

6. The plates of a flat capacitor are square-shaped on the side $a=20$ cm. The distance between them is $d=6$ mm. The plates are connected to a source of direct current, the emf of which is $\varepsilon=500$ V. A square glass plate, the dimensions of which are such that it ultimately fills the entire space inside the capacitor, is pushed into the space between the plates, as shown in figure 7, at a constant speed $\vec{v}$ ($|\vec{v}|=2$ mm/s). Find the current $I$ flowing through the wires from the source to the capacitor while the plate is moving. Neglect edge effects. The dielectric constant of the stack is $\varepsilon=7$. Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0=8,85 \times 10^{-12}$ Ф/m.

7. The electrical circuit consists of a constant voltage source connected in series, a resistance of $R=500$ Ohm, and a flat capacitor whose plate area is $S=4$ cm$^2$, and the distance between the plates can be changed. If the plates are shifted until they come into contact with each other, the flow in the circuit is $I=0,2$ A. What magnitude will the charges be on the plates if the plates are spaced apart by a distance of $d=2$ mm? Neglect the internal resistance of the source. Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0=8,85 \cdot 10^{-12}$ Ф/m.

8. A rigid conducting frame of square shape lies on a horizontal non-conducting surface and is in a magnetic field, the induction line of which is parallel to the two sides of the frame. The mass of the frame is $m=20$ g, the length of its sides is $a=4$ cm, the absolute value of magnetic induction is $|\vec{B}|=0,5$ T. What strength $I$ direct current must be passed through the frame so that one of its sides begins to rise?

9. The inner surface of the cone, covered with a reflective layer, forms the final grain. A thin luminous thread is stretched along the axis of the inner cone. At what minimum angle $a$ of the cone will the rays indicative of the thread be reflected from the surface of the cone no more than once?

10. The object is located at a distance of $d=10$ cm from a converging lens with a focal length of $F=20$ cm. How many times does the image size change if a diverging lens with the same focal length is placed in place of the converging lens?
