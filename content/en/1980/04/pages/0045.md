1) Pyramid with height $SO_1$.

$$S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} (20 + 20 + 32) r_1 = 36 r_1 \ (cm^2); 36 r_1 = 192; r_1 = h_1 = 5 \frac{1}{3} cm.$$

Hence $V = 341 \frac{1}{3} (cm^3)$.

2) Pyramids with heights $SO_2$ and $SO_3$ constructs. We have

$$S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} (|AC|r_2 - |BC|r_2) = 16 r_2 \ (cm^2); 16 r_2 = 192; r_2 = h_2 = 12 cm; отсюда V_2 = V_3 = 768 (cm^3).$$

3) Pyramid with height $SO_4$. We have

$$S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} (|AB|r_4 + |BC|r_4 - |AC|r_4) = 4 r_4 \ (cm^2). 4 r_4 = 192; r_4 = h_4 = 48 cm; V_4 = 3072 (cm^3).$$

Answer: $\{341 \frac{1}{3} cm^3; 768 cm^3; 3072 cm^3\}$.

Our cover

Soviet vippels - at the Luna-12 station and on Venus

On October 22, 1966, a space rocket was launched towards the Moon in the Soviet Union. An automatic station “Luna-12” was installed on board the rocket, which entered a rural-centric orbit and became the third Soviet artificial satellite of the Moon. However, one of the tasks of this station is to receive and transmit to Earth photographic images of individual areas of the lunar surface, taken from a relatively close distance. A special photo-television device installed on board the station photographed the lunar surface and transmitted the images to Earth via a television channel. On January 19, 1967, the flight program of the Luna-12 station was successfully completed. During the three months of the flight, the station conducted a number of scientific studies and transmitted unique photographs of the lunar surface to Earth.

* *

In order to more fully study Venus and obtain a large amount of scientific information about it, on January 10, 1969, the automatic station “Venera-6” was launched in the Soviet Union. Station "Venera-6" carried out scientific research plans together with

station "Venera-5", launched on January 5, 1969. On May 16 and 17, 1969, the station's descent modules sailed down into the atmosphere of Venus, performed an extensive set of scientific measurements and transmitted to Earth valuable information about the characteristics and properties of the atmosphere of this, still largely mysterious, planet. A pennant with a base relief of V.I. Lenin and the State Emblem of the USSR were delivered to the surface of Venus. The new success of Soviet science and technology, achieved thanks to the hard work of the entire Soviet people, was dedicated to the centenary of the birth of the organization of the Communist Party, the founder of the Soviet state, the leading worker of the whole world, V. I. Lenin.
