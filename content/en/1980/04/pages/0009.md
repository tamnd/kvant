⟦folio 7⟧

V.I. Lenin and N.K. Krupskaya are middle peasants of the village of Kashino on the opening day of the rural electric station.

The creation of a commissariat of education was marked, and on November 9 of the same year the Decree of the State Commission for Public Education was adopted. 15 departments of public education were created. Among them are departments for the introduction of universal literacy, out-of-school education, school medicine and hygiene, school construction, technical schools and political education. Lenin writes: “Our cause in the school field is the same struggle for the overthrow of the bourgeoisie: we openly declare that school is outside of life, outside of politics - it is a lie and philistinism.”*).

Under the leadership of Vladimir Ilchch, employees of the Education Commissariat carried out a lot of work in the construction of a new school. Lenin repeatedly pointed out the importance and complexity of this work, and everyone tried to warn his comrades against mistakes and excesses. Vladimir Ilchch constantly spoke about the need for skillful use of the cultural heritage of the old system.

During these years, Lenin’s provisions on the school were organizationally enshrined in the “Regulations on the Unified Labor School of the RSFSR” dated September 30, 1918.

Article 1. All schools of the Russian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic, which are under the jurisdiction of the People's Commissariat for Education, are given the name “Unified Labor School”.

Article 2. The unified school is divided into 2 levels: 1st - for children from 8 to 13 years old and 2nd - from 13 to 17 years old.

Article 3. School education for levels 1 and 2 is free.

Article 5. In schools of the 1st and 2nd levels, joint education is introduced.

Article 6. Teaching in schools of any kind of believer and performing religious rites in schools is not allowed.

* V. I. Lenin. PSS, vol. 36, p. 77.

V.I. Lenin is a high school student.
