Stat the loop

Two ringers $A$ and $B$ are stuck into the plane, and a rope loop is thrown over them (see Fig.ok). Remove the loop

from the stakes, without tearing it and without lifting it above the staker, very week, it is impossible; If you remove any of them, then this can be done.

The problem is a noose thrown over three piercers, which cannot be removed from them, but if you remove any piercer, there will be two.

V. Berezin
