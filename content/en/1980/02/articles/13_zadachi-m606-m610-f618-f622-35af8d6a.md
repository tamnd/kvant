Tasks

M606 — M610; F618 — F622

M606. The function $f$ is such that for all real $x$

$$f(x+1) + f(x-1) = \sqrt{2}f(x).$$

Prove that $f$ is a periodic function.

E. Turkevich

M607. a) Cut the square into equal trapezoids.

b) Cut an equilateral right triangle into equilateral trapezoids.

c) Prove that any polygon can be cut into equidistant trapezoids.

V. Lev

M608. On checkered paper (side of cell 1) a $n$-gon is drawn, all sides of which lie on the grid lines and have an odd length.

a) Prove that $n$ is divisible by 4.

b)* Prove that for $n=100$ the area of ​​this $n$-gon must be odd. Find out what the parity of the area is for other $n$.

M. Kontsevich, 10th grade student

M609. a) The lengths of the projections of a convex polygon of area $S$ onto two mutually perpendicular lines are $l_1$ and $l_2$. Prove that $S \leq l_1 l_2$.

b) The lengths of the projections of a convex polygon of volume $V$ onto three mutually perpendicular lines are $l_1, l_2$ and $l_3$. Prove that $V \leq l_1 l_2 l_3$.

c)* The areas of projections of a convex polygon of volume $V$ onto three mutually perpendicular planes are equal to $S_1, S_2$ and $S_3$. Prove that $V \leq \sqrt{S_1 S_2 S_3}$.

Yu. Smirnov

M610. We fix $k \in N$. a) Consider the set of all sets of integers $a_1, \ldots, a_k$ such that $0 \leq a_1 \leq a_2 \leq \ldots \leq a_k \leq k$; let us denote the number of such sets by $N$. Let us consider among them those for which $a_k = k$; let their number be $M$. Prove that $N = 2M$.

b) Let us impose an additional constraint on the sets under consideration: the sum $a_1 + \ldots + a_k$ is divisible by $k$. Let the corresponding numbers be $N'$ and $M'$. To-

show that $N' = 2M'$. (From figure 1 it is clear that for $k = 3$ these numbers are equal to $M = 9, N = 18; M' = 4, N' = 8$.

A. Tolytso

F618. Is it possible to connect two incandescent lamps with a power of 60 W and 100 W, designed for a voltage of 110 V, in series into a 220 V network, if the voltage on each of the lamps is allowed to exceed 10% of the rated voltage? The current-voltage characteristic of a 100 W lamp is shown in figure 2.

A. Zilberman

F619. The luminous point is located on the main optical axis at a distance of $d = 40$ cm from the diverging lens with a focal length of $F = 10$ cm. The point is shifted to a distance of $h = 5$ cm in a plane perpendicular to the main optical axis. How much and where should the lens be moved so that the image of the luminous point returns to its old position?

Leningrad City Olympiad in Physics, 1979

F620. If you place an object with a mass greater than $m_0$ in the center of a square table, the table legs will break. Find many points on the table where you can place an object of mass $m_0/2$ without fear of breaking the table.

O. Batitsev

F621. The end of the thread wound on the spool will be thrown over the nail broken into the wall (Fig. 3). The thread is pulled at a constant speed $v$. At what speed does the center of the spool move at the moment when the thread makes an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical? The outer radius of the coil is $R$, the inner radius is $r$.

F622. When and how many times is the air humidity (water vapor density) greater than absc: in November at a temperature of $t_1 = 0^\circ C$ and a humidity of 95% or in the summer at $t_2 = 35^\circ C$ and a humidity of 40%, if the saturated vapor pressure at $t_1 = 0^\circ C$ is $p_1 = 6 \cdot 10^2$ Pa, and at $t_2 = 35^\circ C$ is $p_2 = 5,5 \cdot 10^3$ Pa?
