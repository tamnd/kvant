Solution. Let $MN$ be the common perpendicular to the edges $AB$ and $CD$ of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ with equal-sized boundaries (Fig. 7, a). It is easy to understand that the orthogonal projection of a tetrahedron along the line $AB$ is a triangle $ADC$ (Fig. 7, b), in which the side $AD$ is congruent to the height $DD_1$ of the face $ABD$, and the side $AC$ is congruent to the height $CC_1$ of the face $ABC$, and the segment $MN$ is projected to the height $AN$ of triangle $ADC$. Since triangles $ABC$ and $ABD$ have the same areas and a common base $AB$, their heights dropped onto this base are equal, therefore, at figure 7, b $|AD|=|AC|$; which means that point $N$ (the base of the altitude of an equilateral triangle) divides the segment $CD$ in half. Using the useful property of projection and repeating this reasoning in relation to the remaining edges of the tetrahedron, we conclude that the common perpendiculars of all pairs of crossing edges of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ pass through the midpoints of the corresponding edges and, therefore, are the symmetry axes of the tetrahedron. For example, with axial symmetry with the axis $MN$ (Fig. 7, a), the vertex $A$ will go into $B$, $B$ - into $A$, $C$ - into $D$, $D$ - into $C$, so the face $ABC$ is congruent to the face $ABD$, and the face $ACD$ is congruent to the face $BCD$. In the same way it is proved that $\triangle ABD \cong \triangle ACD$.

Exercises
1. Given two crossing lines $a$ and $b$. Find the set of points $X$ dividing in a constant ratio the segments $AB$ ($|AX|:|XB|=k$), where $a$ the point $A$ runs along the line $a$, $B$ runs through the line $b$; 6) the same thing, but with the conditions that the straight line $AB$ remains parallel to the given plane $a$ all the time.

2. Given a closed non-locking four-way polygon $ABCD$ (“spatial quadrilateral”). Prove that points $K$, $L$, $M$, $N$, taken on its links $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$, respectively, lie in the same plane if and only if $\frac{AK}{KB} \cdot \frac{BL}{LC} \cdot \frac{CM}{MD} \cdot \frac{DN}{NA} = 1$.

3. Given a regular hexagonal paramid $SABCDEF$ with vertex $S$. On edges $SA$ and $SC$ points $K$ and $L$ are taken so that $|SK|:|KA|=3$, $|SL|:|LC|=1/3$, point $M$ is the middle of $a$) edges $SF$, 6) edges $SE$. Determine which edges of the paramid are intersected by the plane ($KLM$) and in what ratio they divide it.

4. (MIPT, 1975). The area of ​​the trapezoid $ABCD$ is equal to $S$, the ratio of the lengths of the bases is $|AD|:|BC|=3:1$. Line segment $MK$ is positioned so that it is parallel to side $CD$ and intersects side $AB$, and line segment $AM$ is parallel to line segment $BK$. Determine the area of ​​triangle $BKC$ if $|AM|:|BK|=3:2$, $|MK|:|CD|=1:3$ (find all solutions).

5. (MIPT, 1976): On the diagonals $AB_1$ and $CA_1$ of the lateral faces of the triangular lines $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ the corresponding points $D$ and $E$ are located so that $(DE) \parallel (BC_1)$. Define relation $|DE|:|BC_1|$.

6. (MIPT, 1977). The base side $ABCD$ of the regular paramid $SABCD$ has length $a$, the lateral edge has length $2a$. Examine the segments with ends on the diagonal $BD$ of the base and sideways edge $SC$, parallel (SAD). Find the shortest length of all segments under consideration.

7. (M462). The plane intersects the lateral edges of a regular quadruple paramid at points spaced from the vertex at distances $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$. Prove that $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{d}$.

8. (MSU, Mechanics and Mathematics, 1978). The volume of the tetrahedron $ABCD$ is equal to 5. A plane is drawn through the midpoints of the edges $AD$ and $BC$, intersecting the edge $CD$ at the point $M$. In this case, the ratio of the length of the segment $DM$ to the length of the segment $MC$ is equal to $\frac{2}{3}$. Calculate the cross-sectional area of ​​the tetrahedron by the indicated plane if the distance from it to the vertex $A$ is equal to 1.

9. (MSU, Geological Faculty, 1974). Given a cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$, the edge length of which is 1 cm. On the edges $AA_1, BB_1, DD_1$, the corresponding points $K$, $P$ and $M$ are taken so that $|AK|:|A_K|=1:3$, $|BP|:|B_P|=3:1$, $|DM|:|D_M|=3:1$. Find the volume of a paramid whose base is a section of a cube by a plane passing through points $K$, $P$ and $M$, and the vertex is located at point $A_1$.

10. In the parallelepiped $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ the areas of the faces $ABB_1A_1$ and $BCC_1B_1$ are equal, the corresponding $ACC_1A_1$ and $BDD_1B_1$ are $s_1$ and $s_2$. Show that $s_1^2 + s_2^2 = 2(S_1^2 + S_2^2)$. Express the angle at the edge $AA_1$ using two angles through these planes.
