⟦folio 51⟧

Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology

Below are published materials from the written entrance exams of 1979. Five hours were allotted for work in both mathematics and physics.

Mathematica

Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2-x} + \frac{4}{\sqrt{2-x}+3} = 2.$$
2. Solve inequality
$$\frac{x-1}{\log_3(9-3^x)} \leq 1.$$
3. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\sin(2x - \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sqrt{2}\sin^2x,$$
satisfying inequality
$$\log_{\cos^23}(1 + \sin(7x+5)) < \sin 8x.$$
4. The extensions of the altitudes AM and CN of an acute triangle ABC intersect its circumcircle at points P and Q. Find the radius of the circumcircle if $|AC| = a, |PQ| = \frac{6}{5}a.$
5. In terrada ABCD, two angles at edges AB and BD are right, $\widehat{ACD} = a$. The top of the cone coincides with one of the tops of the terraer, the circle of the base of the cone is inscribed in one of the boundaries. Find the angle in the axial section of the cone.
6. Point N is located on the bank of a river, the width of which is 1 km, and the speed of the current is 1 km/hour. Point M is located at least 3 km downstream on the other bank. A fisherman leaves point M and walks along the shore towards N at a speed of 4 km/hour. In due course, a carrier sails from point N on a boat, crosses the river, and, having caught the fisherman, transports him to point N. The boat moved back and forth in a straight line, and the direction of movement was chosen so that the shortest possible time elapsed from departure to return, equal to $\frac{9}{8}$ hours. The speed of a boat in still water is 4 km/hour. Find the distance (downstream) between points M and N.

Option 2
1. Solve inequality
$$0,4^x - 2,5^{x+1} > 1,5.$$
2. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_2y = \log_4(xy - 2), \\ \log_9x^2 + \log_3(x-y) = 1. \end{cases}$$
3. A circle of radius R drawn through the vertices A, B and C of the straight trapezoid $ABCD$ ($\widehat{A} = \widehat{B} = \frac{\pi}{2}$) intersects the segments AD and CD respectively at points M and N so that $|AM|:|AD| = |CN|:|CD| = 1:3$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.
4. Solve the equation
$$\sin x + \sin^2x + \cos^3x = 0.$$
5. Two trains leave points A and B towards each other at the same time. Each of them moves at first uniformly accelerated (the initial speeds of the train are zero), and then, when they reach a certain speed, uniformly. The speed ratio of the uniform motion of the train is $\frac{4}{3}$. At the moment of meeting, the trains had equal speeds, and arrived at points B and A approximately simultaneously. Find the ratio of the train accelerations.
6. The base of the right paralleleped $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is the square ABCD. Find the most possible angle between straight line BD$_1$ and plane BDC$_1$.

Physics

Option 1

Ball 1 is given a speed $\vec{v}_0$, it hits ball 2 first, then ball 3 and is abandoned. 3. Connect parallel-connected coils with inductance L and resistance R through a key K and a source with a constant emf E and internal resistance r (Fig. 2). At the initial moment of time, switch K is open and there is no current in the circuit. What charge Q will flow through resistance R after switch K is closed? Neglect the ohmic resistance of the coil.
