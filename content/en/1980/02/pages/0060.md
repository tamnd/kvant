I. Nesterova and I. Sherbakova (Magnitogorsk, school No. 28, 9th grade) in their report “The use of mathematical methods to determine the level of development of teachers and schoolchildren” spoke about the work they carried out in the preparatory group of kindergarten No. 137 and with first-graders of their school. The girls proposed a questionnaire of 5 questions and then processed it using mathematical methods (hereinafter referred to as computers). It turned out that general development should be characterized by the ability to debug puzzles and carry out classification, and a first-class student should be characterized by the ability to solve problems (operational thinking)). Magnitogorsk schoolgirls intend to continue this work.

The library of the Kazan Institute of Chemical Technology donated the results of the work of O. Maksimov (Kazan, school No. 18, 9th grade) to the Department of Chemistry for use. In his report “Using an equation with LIMITING factors in modeling microbiological processes,” he spoke about a mathematical model of the growth of yeast cells under conditions of limiting incoming nitrogen and carbon, according to which the program for the Narin-K computer was compiled. This work was carried out in the programming group of the club of young chemists and mechanics, whose members took up the mathematical support of research work carried out by students and employees at the department of chemistry in the library of the Kazakh Chemical Technology Institute.

There were also many interesting works in the physics section. Thus, L. Maisterenko (Volgograd, school No. 111, 9th grade) in her report “Research of electromimic corrosion currents and development of a corrosion rate meter” spoke about the work carried out by a group of students from her school together with the Department of Physics of the Volgograd Civil Engineering Institute. A. V. Shilnikov, head. Department of Physics WISI, gave the following review about it: “The new method studied by the authors, after further verification, should be proposed for implementation in practice.” This work was carried out on the topic “Development and implementation of electromimic protection of odd tanks from corrosion” to provide Nizhnevolzhsknefta.
