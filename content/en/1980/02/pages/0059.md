II All-Russian gathering of NOU activists

In July 1979, in Chelyabinsk, the Ministry of Education of the RSFSR, the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the All-Union Council of Scientific and Technical Societies and the Chelyabinsk Regional Council of People's Delutos held the II All-Russian meeting of activists of scientific societies of students *). Let us remember that the first non-state educational institution appeared in 1964. In 1975, the first All-Russian gathering of NOU activists was held in Moscow. In March 1977, the Central Committee of the Komsomol, the Ministry of Education of the USSR, the Supreme Council of NTO and the Board of the All-Union Society “Znanie” approved the Model Regulations on NOU. As a result of these measures, the number of non-state educational institutions has recently increased significantly, and about 300 non-state educational institutions are already successfully operating.

The location of the second meeting was not chosen by chance - the Chelyabinsk National Educational Institution, one of the first in the country, is the ray of the Leninist Komsomol, more than 5 thousand schoolchildren are involved in its 73 sections and 37 films. Members of the Chelyabinsk NOU have about 2 thousand creative works, about 200 of them have been published.

There were 11 sections at the meeting, in which 6-15 reports were heard. The rally was characterized by excellent leadership of the sections. The jury included representatives of scientific institutions in Moscow, Leningrad, Chelyabinsk and other cities.

Chairman of the Council of Curators of the Chelyabinsk Non-Governmental Educational Institution N. N. Mikhailov, speaking at the closing of the meeting, noted the following criteria that guided the life of the meeting when evaluating the works.

The first is utility. As you begin your research, ask yourself directly and courageously—who needs this? What does the above research reveal? When a fifth-grader solves puzzles or invents entertaining crafts, you can and should be happy about this. He's playing. But when a high school student begins to play science, when he takes on a problem for research, the solution of which satisfies only his own curiosity, but does not represent the slightest interest for science and practice, that is enough. Curiosity, intuition, imagination are essential qualities of research. But I would like efficiency and quality - two words that have become the motto of the ten-five - to become the motto of foreign science, so that the results of your research, no matter how modest they are, would be useful. Therefore, the jury very highly appreciated the research that has implications for production, school life, and solving the problem of our own life - study, access. I would like there to be more such studies, so that the synergy of “school + science + production” would be stronger.

The second criterion is the seriousness of the work. Here the jury was decisively in favor of superstition. When a person strives to solve global problems, not based on the knowledge contained in a school textbook, it produces a challenging, even comical impression. It seems to him that he is expressing genius revelations, but he is ignorant. He wants to amaze, but turns out to be funny. Remember the words of K. Marx that in science there is no broad side of the agreement and that only he can reach its shining peaks who, without fatigue and difficulties, climbs its rocky path. He climbs, but doesn’t bend over, doesn’t look! Read a lot, engage in self-education, you must patiently master the wealth of knowledge that is filled with science, you must be able to do more, difficult, sometimes boring, but necessary preparatory work. But brilliant, global, large-scale discoveries will not surprise you. They're just getting closer.
