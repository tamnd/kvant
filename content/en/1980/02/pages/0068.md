MSU ON BADGES

The main building of Moscow University on the Lenin Hills, a remarkable work of architecture that became a symbol of Soviet science and higher education, was repeatedly depicted on artistic signs (1,2). Interesting “professional” signs of faculties and scientific departments of Moscow State University. Here are some of them: (3) - Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, (4) - Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics, (5) and (6) - Faculty of Physics (in the first there is a stylized image of the symbol $\sqrt{1}$, popular among physics students, the idea of the second was suggested by the famous phrase of Archimedes “Give me a fulcrum, and I will move the Earth!”), (7) - Research Institute of Mechanics, (8) - Scientific Research Institute of Nuclear Physics (badge issued for the 25th anniversary of SINP). A special physiomatic boarding school No. 18 was created at Moscow State University; its graduates receive a special badge (9). MSU hosts national and international scientific conferences and conferences. On the membership badges of participants in these forums you can often see the silhouette of the main building of Moscow State University (10) - the badge of a participant in the competition of the International Union of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics). The last badge (11) was issued in connection with the 200th anniversary of Moscow University.

N. Rozov
