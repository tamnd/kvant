The law of conservation of total energy and the theorem that the sum of the squares of the diagonals of a parallelogram is equal to the sum of the squares of its sides lead to the relation

$$v_1^2 + v_2^2 = 2v^2 + \frac{4}{m}(E_1 - 2E_2)$$

And

$$v_1^2 + v_2^2 = 2v^2 + 2v_0^2,$$

where

$$v_0 = \sqrt{\frac{2}{m}(E_1 - 2E_2)}.$$

Obviously, two cases are possible: a) $v \geq v_0$; b) $v < v_0$. Let us first consider case a), which exactly corresponds to figure 5, and find the maximum possible angle $\varphi$. To do this, we use the cosine theorem for triangles $ACD$, $ABD$ and $ACB$ and get

$$\cos \varphi = \frac{v_1^2 + v_2^2 - (2v_0)^2}{2v_1v_2} = \frac{v^2 - v_0^2}{\sqrt{(v^2 + v_0^2)^2 - (2vv_0 \cos \alpha)^2}}.$$

From here

$$\cos \varphi \geq \frac{v^2 - v_0^2}{v^2 + v_0^2}$$

And

$$\varphi_{\max} = \arccos \frac{v^2 - v_0^2}{v^2 + v_0^2},$$

where $v_0^2 = \frac{2}{m}(E_1 - 2E_2)$.

At the same time $v_1 = v_2 = \sqrt{v^2 + v_0^2}$.

Case b) is considered independently and make sure that $\varphi_{\max} = \pi$.

Exercises

1. On the path of a body of mass $m$, sliding along a smooth table with a speed $\vec{v}$, there is a stationary unfixed slide with mass $M$ and height $H$ (the profile of the slide is chosen at figure 6). Determine the speeds of the body and the slide after the body leaves the slide. The body moves without leaving the slide; there is no friction between the body and the slide.

2. At what angle $\beta$ do two identical smooth balls fly apart after an absolutely elastic impact? Before the impact, one of the balls was buried, and the other was moving at a speed $\vec{v}$, directed at an angle $\alpha$ to the line of the centers of the balls.

3. A ring with radius $R$ and a small ball inside the ring can move along a smooth table surface. At some point the ball elastically collides with the ring. Before the impact, the ring is buried, and the ball moves at a speed $\vec{v}$, which makes an angle $\alpha$ with the radius drawn to the point of impact. Find time until the next strike.

4. Two smooth identical balls of mass $M$ each accumulate on a smooth horizontal surface, touching each other. The third ball, also of radius, but with mass $m$ moves along the surface with a speed $\vec{v}$ passing through the point of contact of the stationary balls. Determine the speed of the balls after an absolutely elastic impact.
