$f_k(k \geqslant 3)$ contains at least one of the two previous numbers $f_{k-1}$ or $f_{k-2}$.

A. Odessky, 9th grade student, D. Fuchs

M660. Blue and red dots are placed on the circle (Fig. 2a). It is allowed to add a new red dot and simultaneously change the color of each of the two adjacent dots (Fig. 26), or remove the red dot and change the color of each of the adjacent dots (Fig. 2c). Let there initially be only two red dots (it is not allowed to leave more than two dots). Is it possible to obtain on a circle using several such operations
a) two dots - blue and red;
b) 8 red dots;
b) one red and 6 blue dots;
d) * two blue dots?

K. Kazarnovsky

F668. A weightless thread is thrown through a fixed block. A mass load $m_1$ is attached to one end. To the other end, a mass load $m_2$ is suspended from the outside with rigidity $k$. The length of the spring in the ungrown state is $l_0$. Find the amplitude of oscillations that the load $m_2$ will perform when the system is left to itself. There is no swing across the thread.

G. Kotkin

F669. Invert a thin-walled glass with a cork house of thickness $d$ floating at the interface of two liquids with densities $\varrho_1$ and $\varrho_2$. The top of the glass is at a height $H$ above the interface (Fig. 3). Glass section $S$. How far does the glass rise if there is a hole in the bottom of the glass?

F670. Show that the maximum speed that an α-particle can impart to a proton upon collision is 1.6 times the initial velocity of the α-particle.

F671. There is 1 liter of hot water with a temperature of $T_1$ and 1 liter of cold water with a temperature of $T_2$. Using hot water, cold water is heated. Is it possible to make the final temperature of water that was initially cold become higher than the temperature of water that was initially hotter?

A. Kravchaty, 9th grade student.

F672. A load with a resistance of $R_h=10$ Ohm is connected to the secondary winding of the included step-down transformer with a transformation coefficient of $K=220/127$. Network voltage $U=220$ V, resistance of the primary winding of the transformer $R_1=3,6$ Ohm, resistance of the secondary winding $R_2=1,2$ Ohm. Determine the voltage across the load.

V. Skorovarov
