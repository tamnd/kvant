Our artist depicted here the “biography” of a Hungarian hinged cube. Hidden inside this plastic math puzzle toy is an ingeniously designed hinge mechanism that allows you to rotate any "tile" of nine colored cubes. The center close-up shows the beginning of the rotation (approximately 60°) of the “near” tile counterclockwise. The large cube is bordered by smaller images of transformations of the cube, obtained by similar rotations. The top center shows the cube in its “starting position” (all faces are the same color); to the right of it is a cube after rotating two horizontal tiles (top and bottom); then rotate two vertical tiles (which ones?). See for yourself how the events unfolded. Pay attention to the symmetrical coloring obtained in the middle of the vertical rows. Watch how, when you rotate two horizontal tiles of the second cube from the left in the top row, the initial position appears again - the peculiar life cycle of the hinged cube has ended!

For the rules of playing this puzzle and the problems that arise, see p. 32
