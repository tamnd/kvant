The oscillation frequencies produced by crystal oscillators are typically on the order of $10^4 - 10^6$ Hz. To obtain rarer time stamps, you need an electronic frequency divider that reduces the oscillation frequency by a strictly known number of times *). Synchronous electric motors operating on alternating current of this reduced frequency rotate the clock hands and close the contacts of the relay, which supplies the exact time signal. Observations of a group of pendulum clocks and the first quartz clocks in 1937 showed that the length of the day changes abruptly on average per year by 0.002 s and at the same time gradually increases by 0.0016 s per 100 years *). This means that the maximum accuracy of determining the second using a clock “the Earth rotating around its axis” does not exceed $\frac{2 \cdot 10^{-3}}{86400} \approx 0,2 \cdot 10^{-7}$.

In 1956, the International Bureau of Weights and Measures decided to move to a more stable standard of time - the rotation of the Earth around the Sun. As this, use the duration of the tropical year - the period of time between two successive passages of the center of the Sun through the point of spring equilibrium. Since the length of the tropical year is not strictly constant due to the disturbance exerted by the planets on the Earth's movement, one of them was chosen - 1900.

*) The uneven rotation of the Earth is mainly caused by the fact that during the ebb and flow of the tides, parts of the energy of rotational motion are spent on overcoming the control forces of deformation of the main solid part of the Earth and the forces of internal friction during the movement of large masses of water in the oceans and air in the atmosphere.
