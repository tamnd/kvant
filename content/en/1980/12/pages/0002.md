⟦folio none⟧

The polyhedra shown here are obtained from the heel of regular polyhedra by mediating their vertices and edges (think about how this is done). These polyhedra have a remarkable property: the vertices of each of them are “structured in the same way.” This means that any vertex of such a polyhedron, by means of rotation around a certain axis, can be transferred to any other vertex so that the polyhedron is compatible with itself.

Polyhedra arranged in this way are called positively transitive. For such polyhedra, see p.9

To the first cover page

In the article by A. Savin “Olympic stakes” («Kvant», 1980, No. 7) the question was raised about the arrangement on a plane in the form of a “rosette” of several tracks, interlocked so that no two stakes are linked, while all the posts together are linked.

The editors have received several letters containing examples of such engagements. We publish the example of Kiev resident M. Kornilov on the cover of this issue of the magazine, and A. Azimov from Tashkent gave us the same example.
