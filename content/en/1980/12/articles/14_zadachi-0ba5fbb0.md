⟦folio 31⟧

Tasks

1. Fill the cells of the drawn square with natural numbers so that the sums of the numbers in each row, each column and diagonals of the square are the same (such squares are called match squares) and equal 1980.

2. What kind of figure is this, adding which to the end of an arbitrary natural number, we get the same number added to this figure and the product of this number by this figure?

3. Take eight matches and a button and form them into a fish as shown in figure. Now rearrange three matches and a button so that the fish looks exactly the same, but is burning in the opposite direction.

4. There are more than three natural numbers written in a circle, the sum of which is 37. It is known that the sums of any three consecutive numbers are equal to each other. What numbers are written in a circle?

5. In addition to the hour and minute hands, the alarm clock also has a bell hand. One watchmaker created a mechanism with the help of which this hand began to move evenly so that it always bisected the angle between the hour and minute hands.
How many revolutions will such an arrow make per day?

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Baboyan, F. Bargenev, S. Bely, A. Cabrizon, A. Savin
