The tasks of the previous rounds of our chess competition were quite serious. Well, now, on the eve of the New Year, you can allow yourself to joke a little. Moreover, trios and even duets on the chessboard serve as a good source of chess humor. In today's examples and problems, players will take backward moves, give another player a head start, be swapped pieces, etc., etc.
