Exercise
our readers

1. It is known that
$$F\left(\frac{x+1}{x-2}\right)+2F\left(\frac{x-2}{x+1}\right)=x.$$
Find $F(x)$.

N. Adtsezalov

2. Let the equality $x^2+y^2=z^2$ be given, where $x, y, z, m>1$ are natural numbers. Prove that $x, y, z$ cannot form a progression, neither arithmetic nor geometric.

M. Ibraimov

3. Let us denote by $S(A)$ the sum of the digits of the number $A$. Prove that the sequence $\left(\frac{S(n^2)}{S(n)}\right), n\in\mathbb{N}$ is unbounded.

Is a similar statement true for the sequence $\left(\frac{S(n)}{S(n^2)}\right)$?

E. Turkevich

When there is no computer at hand

Obvious identity
$$a^2=(a-d)(a+d)+d^2$$
may be useful when dealing with squared numbers:
$$43^2=(43-3)(43+3)+3^2=40\cdot 46+9=1849,$$
$$43^2=(43+7)(43-7)+7^2=50\cdot 36+49=1849,$$
$$257^2=(257+43)(257-43)+43^2=300\cdot 214+43^2=64200-1849=66049.$$
A. Krits
