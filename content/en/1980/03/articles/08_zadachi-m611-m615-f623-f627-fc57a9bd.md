Tasks

M611—M615; F623—F627

M611. On the chord AB of a circle with center O, take an arbitrary point M. Through points A, M and O, draw a circle intersecting the first circle at points A and C. Prove that $|MB| = |MC|$.

S. Kolpakov, 10th grade student

M612. The increasing sequence of natural numbers ($a_n$) is such that $a_{n+1} \leq 10a_n$. Prove that if all the numbers $a_n$ are written side by side (without spaces or commas), then the resulting sequence of numbers will not be periodic.

A. Karadulyan

M613. On the sides of triangle ABC, similar triangles are constructed to the outside

$$ADB, BEC \text{ и } CFA \left( |AD| |DB| = |BE| |EC| = |CF| |FA| = k; \right)$$

$$\widehat{ADB} = \widehat{BEC} = \widehat{CFA} = \alpha.$$

Prove that:

a) the midpoints of the segments AC, DC, BC and EF - the tops of the parallelogram;

b) this parallelogram has two angles of magnitude $\alpha$, and the side length ratio is $k$.

L. Kuptsov

M614. For each natural number $n$, let $S(n)$ denote the sum of the digits of all natural numbers from 1 to $n$ (in decimal notation):

$$S(1) = 1, S(2) = 3, S(3) = 6, ..., S(9) = 45, S(10) = 46, S(11) = 48, S(12) = 51, ...$$

a) Find $S(100)$.

b) Prove that $S(10^k - 1) = 45k \cdot 10^{k-1}$ for all $k = 1, 2, ...$

b) Prove that for a two-digit number $\overline{ab}$

$$S(\overline{ab}) = 5a^2 + ab + 41a + b(b+1)/2.$$

d) Find a similar formula for three-digit numbers.

⟦folio 27⟧

E) Embroider S(1980).

Andrzej Paszewicz
(Poland)

M615. Prove that the perimeter of any section of a triangular paramid by a plane does not exceed most of the perimeters of its faces.

V. Senderov

F623. A variable capacitor with an initial capacitance $C_0$, charged to a voltage $U$, is connected to a resistor with a resistance $R$ (Fig. 1). How should the capacitance of the capacitor change over time so that a constant current flows through the circuit? What power is developed by external forces, due to which the capacitance of the capacitor changes?

F624. In the process of the vessel, closed by a flat piston with a diameter of $D=5$ cm, there is a small amount of water (Fig. 2). The diameter of the process is $d=2$ mm. If at a constant temperature $t=20^\circ\text{C}$ the piston is lowered by $H=10$ cm, then the water level in the process increases by $h=1$ mm. Find the saturated vapor pressure of water at temperature $t=20^\circ\text{C}$.

F625. The jet engine shown in figure 3 is made from an eggshell. The area of ​​the reaction in the shell is $s=3$ mm². What is the maximum thrust force of such an engine if the temperature of the water in the shell is maintained at 100°C?

F626. A gymnast falls from a height of $H=12$ m onto a horizontal stretched elastic net, which is deflected by $h=1$ m. Estimate how many times the maximum force acting on the hypnast from the net is greater than the force of gravity if the size of the net is much larger than $h$ and the mass of the net is small compared to the mass of the person.

Leningrad City Olympics, 1979

F627. While you were solving problems from «Kvant», the potatoes that were boiling on the stove were boiled, the water boiled away and the inside of the pan burned. Where should cold water be poured so that the carbon deposits bounce off more easily - inside the pan or on its outer surface?

L. Ashkinazi
