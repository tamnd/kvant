CHINE NUMBER “THE 8TH OF MARCH”

Fill the “eight” with squares of integers so that each square begins in the cell with the prime digit and ends in the cell with the next digit, and all the squares are different.
The word “chain number” comes from the English words chain (“chain”), number (“number”).

V. Radunsky
