At the initial moment T=0, the station is at point (0, H), and the ship is at the origin (0, 0). Create programs that solve the following problems:

* a) Draw the flight path of the ship.
* b) Draw cartoon frames depicting the ship approaching the station (projection speed - 24 frames per second).
* b) Determine the flight time of the ship before meeting the station.
* g) Solve problems a) - b) for the case when at the initial moment the ship is at point (A, 0), where A>0, that is, the ship is flying towards the station.
* d) Solve the previous problems when the ship makes a maneuver, that is, its ordinate Y(T) is a known function of time T, and the velocity component along the OX axis remains constant.

Note e. When compiling a program, we can assume that the speed of the ship changes direction not interruptively, but at regular intervals of time T1, for example T1=1/24 s.
