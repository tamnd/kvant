Tasks

M611—M615; F623—F627

M611. On the chord AB of a circle with center O, take an arbitrary point M. Through points A, M and O, draw a circle intersecting the first circle at points A and C. Prove that $|MB| = |MC|$.

S. Kolpakov, 10th grade student

M612. The increasing sequence of natural numbers ($a_n$) is such that $a_{n+1} \leq 10a_n$. Prove that if all the numbers $a_n$ are written side by side (without spaces or commas), then the resulting sequence of numbers will not be periodic.

A. Karadulyan

M613. On the sides of triangle ABC, similar triangles are constructed to the outside

$$ADB, BEC \text{ и } CFA \left( |AD| |DB| = |BE| |EC| = |CF| |FA| = k; \right)$$

$$\widehat{ADB} = \widehat{BEC} = \widehat{CFA} = \alpha.$$

Prove that:

a) the midpoints of the segments AC, DC, BC and EF - the tops of the parallelogram;

b) this parallelogram has two angles of magnitude $\alpha$, and the side length ratio is $k$.

L. Kuptsov

M614. For each natural number $n$, let $S(n)$ denote the sum of the digits of all natural numbers from 1 to $n$ (in decimal notation):

$$S(1) = 1, S(2) = 3, S(3) = 6, ..., S(9) = 45, S(10) = 46, S(11) = 48, S(12) = 51, ...$$

a) Find $S(100)$.

b) Prove that $S(10^k - 1) = 45k \cdot 10^{k-1}$ for all $k = 1, 2, ...$

b) Prove that for a two-digit number $\overline{ab}$

$$S(\overline{ab}) = 5a^2 + ab + 41a + b(b+1)/2.$$

d) Find a similar formula for three-digit numbers.
