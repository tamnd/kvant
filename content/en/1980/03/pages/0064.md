5. The fraction $\frac{3n - m}{5n + 2m}$ can be reduced by 11.

Note. From the conditions of the problem it follows that $3n - m = kp$ and $5n + 2m = kq$, where $p$ and $q$ are mutually prime. From the resulting equations it is not difficult to deduce that $k$ is divisible by 11 and even that $k = 11$.
