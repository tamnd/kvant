⟦folio 38⟧

I. Yufanova

A fairy tale with physics questions

There lived an old man with three sons. Lived at the edge of the world. There is silence all around, the places are impassable.

The sons have grown up and ask their father to let them leave their home. They want to wander around the world, see people, and get their minds blown.

We agreed to travel together: to go where each of us would take turns leading. First, the eldest led everyone south. We walked 10 miles. Then the middle one went forward. We walked another 10 miles, but now to the west. It was the youngest's turn. He and his brothers walked 10 miles north. And they found themselves, to their surprise, ... in their own home, from where they had recently set off on their journey.

Where did the old man live with his sons?

We were ready to hit the road again. We came to a fork in three roads and went our separate ways. We agreed to meet in the same place three years later.

They wandered around the world for a long time, learned various crafts and marveled at the wonders. They returned home on the appointed day and told about their adventures.

The elder brother decided first:

“One day I was driving along the road and saw a clearing. In the middle a huge spruce tree grows, and a squirrel frolics around it.

Magical, probably. I would like to catch it, I think. She will sing songs and nuts with golden grizzly shells. And the kernels in these nuts are made of pure emerald.

I took a step towards the tree, and the squirrel jumped behind the trunk. I hid. Only shows his face with curious eyes.
