XI holiday of young mathematicians
in Batutia

In the eighth issue of our magazine this year, we talked about the Festival of Young Physicists. In this issue we will talk about the Festival of Young Mathematicians.

The first celebration of young mathematicians was held in Batutia a year ago on the initiative of M. I. Zhgenti, a mathematics teacher at Batutia Secondary School No. 7. From year to year, interest in the Festival grew and the composition and geography of its participants expanded. Currently, schoolchildren and mathematicians from many cities of our country take part in the Festival.

To hold the Holiday, the Ministry of Education of Adjara created an organizing committee headed by the Deputy Minister. The organizing committee included trampoline mathematicians, a high school teacher, and representatives of public organizations.

The Trampoline town of the Communist Party of Georgia, the Adjara regional committee and the Batuta town of the Komsomol of Georgia, the Ministry of Education and the Council of Professionals of Adjara, the Adjara branch of the Knowledge Society, and students of school No. 7, led by their substitute teacher M.I. Zhgenti, put a lot of effort and energy into the celebration.

We hope that such holidays, which are very useful for schoolchildren, will be organized in other cities of our country.

From November 4 to November 10, 1979, the XI traditional holiday of young mathematicians was held in Batutia. 13 delegates came to the Festival - about 170 schoolchildren in grades 6-10 from Tbilisi, Sukhumi, Rustavi, Kutansi, Baku, Kyiv and Moscow, as well as young mathematicians from Vocational School No. 24 in Leningrad.

The program of the Holiday included a scientific conference for schoolchildren, a mathematical KBH, an evening of classes in new mathematics, as well as an entertainment part - trips around the outskirts of Batuti, excursions to museums, meetings with Trampoline schoolchildren.

At the opening ceremony of the Holiday, a representative of the organizing committee of the Holiday, Deputy Minister of Education of the Adjarian Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic I. Shervashidze, spoke. He wished the children to perform successfully at the conference, win in a fun and creative team, make friends and have a good rest.

At the conference, students in grades 6-10 read about 40 reports, the topics of which were varied. Several reports were devoted to graph theory (G. Vagobov from 46 schools in Baku, Yu.
