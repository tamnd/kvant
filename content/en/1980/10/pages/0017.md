...In 1933, LFTI celebrated its fifteenth anniversary. In addition to the official program, we have prepared a comic performance for this anniversary. It began with a final triumph with a meeting of the academic council of the institute. Abram Fedorovich Ioffe made a report at the meeting. He began his speech with the words: “Comrades, I want to tell you about the 15-day activities of our institute.” When someone from the audience corrected him with “15 years,” Abram Fedorovich repeated that he was making a report specifically about 15 days of activity, because over the last 15 days, the institute’s staff had carried out such work to prepare for the celebration of the anniversary, which, from his point of view, was comparable in scale to their 15 years of scientific activity. Naturally, the report was repeatedly interrupted by the laughter of the audience and applications. After the report, a number of employees were presented with awards, each of which was accompanied by pointed comments. Then the scientific secretary of the council announced a series of greetings for the anniversary. Among them were, for example, a telephone message from Boyle - Marriott: “Connections with the anniversary, we inform your institute of the law $pV = \text{const}$ teak we discovered, we hope you will successfully apply it to your scientific work teak Boyle - Marriott teak classic Père Lachaise teak 1776.” There was also a congratulation from Ostal Bender from Rio de Janeiro: “Illuminating the city with teak photozems, send a million of your photozems.”

Our entertainment program ends with a play called “Nuclear Affairs” performed by a puppet theater under the direction of the wonderful artist Evgeniy Demeni. The heroes of the play were, of course, the institute’s employees, including Abram Fedorovich Ioffe.
