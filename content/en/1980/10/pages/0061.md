⟦folio 59⟧

Most of the reports made a good impression not only with their content, but also with their successful presentation.

As at previous editions, the mathematical KBH aroused great interest among students and numerous spectators. The strengths of the teams were so close that the first place was shared by two teams: school 208 in Kyiv and school 91 in Moscow; The second place was taken by the team of the 46th Baku school, and the third place was shared by three teams: the 7th school of Batumi, the 19th school of Moscow and the Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University. Muscovite M. Kontsevich won the captains' competition.

KBH participants were offered interesting tasks. Try and solve some of them:

1°. Solve the equation SHISH$^i$ = SHABASH. (Here the letters A, B, I, Ш must be replaced with numbers so that the correct equality is obtained.)

2°. There are 23 coins in denominations of 1, 2, 3 or 5 kopecks, making a total of 48 kopecks. Is there a two-kopeck coin among this set of coins?

3°. (Proposed at a captain's competition.) A finite set of points is given on a plane. Two people play. In one move, you can connect any two points of the Lyceum, and in such a way that no more than two lines come out of each point. The line must not cross. The one who fails is the one who cannot make a move.

Note that in the last problem the captains were unable to find a strategy that leads to winning. maybe we will honor this itself.

The plots of land are not only of good order. But also good deposits. They took an excursion to the Botanical Garden and played in the dolphinarium. On November 7, the guys were guests at a festive demonstration and visited the local history museum and the Museum of the Revolution.

The meeting of young mathematicians will be remembered for a long time by all participants as a bright and joyful celebration of creativity and other children from various allied resources.

B. Geidman, B. Davidovich, A. Zemlyakov
