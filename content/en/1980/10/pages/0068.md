This intricate sixth-order surface was drawn by the EC-1022 machine according to the program of Yu. Kotov. Geometric surfaces are very unique: they are closed, but have two holes—topologists call such surfaces “pretzels.” And algebraic geometers are interested in particular because it contains six circles - they are highlighted in red. The analytical surface is obtained from... three circles using a simple technique: you can read about this in detail on p. 62.
