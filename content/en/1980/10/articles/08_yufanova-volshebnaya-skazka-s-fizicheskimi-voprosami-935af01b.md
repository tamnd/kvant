I. Yufanova

A fairy tale with physics questions

There lived an old man with three sons. Lived at the edge of the world. There is silence all around, the places are impassable.

The sons have grown up and ask their father to let them leave their home. They want to wander around the world, see people, and get their minds blown.

We agreed to travel together: to go where each of us would take turns leading. First, the eldest led everyone south. We walked 10 miles. Then the middle one went forward. We walked another 10 miles, but now to the west. It was the youngest's turn. He and his brothers walked 10 miles north. And they found themselves, to their surprise, ... in their own home, from where they had recently set off on their journey.

Where did the old man live with his sons?

We were ready to hit the road again. We came to a fork in three roads and went our separate ways. We agreed to meet in the same place three years later.

They wandered around the world for a long time, learned various crafts and marveled at the wonders. They returned home on the appointed day and told about their adventures.

The elder brother decided first:

“One day I was driving along the road and saw a clearing. In the middle a huge spruce tree grows, and a squirrel frolics around it.

Magical, probably. I would like to catch it, I think. She will sing songs and nuts with golden grizzly shells. And the kernels in these nuts are made of pure emerald.

I took a step towards the tree, and the squirrel jumped behind the trunk. I hid. Only shows his face with curious eyes.

I think I’ll go around the tree, go up behind the squirrel and catch it.

I walked along the round clearing, without approaching the spruce. And the squirrel, the cunning one, is moving in the opposite direction from me. Yes, so precisely that only the eyes are visible from behind the trunk.

I walked around the clearing four times, but still didn’t catch the squirrel.”
Has the older brother walked around the squirrel at least once? Which one is moving faster? What did the squirrel see: was the older brother standing or moving?

The middle brother continued the story:

“Once I was on the road all day, and in the evening, when it’s time for sleep for a person, for an animal, for a bird, I arrived and quickly earned my money.

I slept a lot or a little, but the noise and whistle woke me up. I feel, brothers, that I am floating through the air. I realized that I would earn money on the plane and now I’m flying on it. I was happy about this adventure. Yes, apparently it's a little early.

At first I couldn’t sit down. He dangled his legs down - he sat comfortably, comfortably, but there was a lot of wind in his legs. It’s so to the side that they are located at an acute angle to the body. And it's very cold.

Somehow I settled down, stretching my legs along the carpet. And then the sun went down. It looked even more like tooth to tooth was missing.”
How can the flight of the middle brother be compared to the flight of an aerostat or an airplane? How can you see the sun setting behind the horizon again? At what speed should a sunset miniplane fly so that the sun always warms its passengers?

The younger brother told his story to them:

“I had the opportunity, brothers, to visit a garden of fabulous beauty. There are no visible flowers there. The pomegranate trees are painted exactly red, there are so many fruits on them. There were also apple trees with golden fruits. The air around is caged, fragrant. Everywhere you look, a nightingale sits and sings songs.

But the most beautiful thing in that garden was the princess, the daughter of the owner of the garden. Yes, an evil wizard bewitched her and turned her into a duck. The beautiful girl was only allowed to swing on the swing. She loved this entertainment. If the swing moves, she is a girl on it. As soon as they stop, at that very moment the girl turns into a duck and disappears so quickly that they only saw her.

I learned that if you look at the motionless princess for a long time, the magic will disappear. So I did. He freed a beautiful princess from the spell of an evil wizard, for which he received a reward.”
How did the younger brother manage to do this?
