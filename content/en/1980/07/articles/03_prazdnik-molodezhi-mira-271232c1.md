World Youth Festival

Now the whole world's attention is focused on Moscow, where sportsmen are having a wonderful debate in strength, agility and speed.

The selection of the capitals of the USSR as economic hosts of the XXII Summer Olympic Games is a natural phenomenon, determined by the historical course of the development of sports. Today the whole world will recognize the enormous contribution that the sportsmen of our country have made to strengthening the modern Olympic movement.

Four years ago, when a similar celebration of world sports was held in Montreal, Leonid Ilyich Brezhnev wrote in his telephone message to members of the International Olympic Committee, the Organizing Committee and participants of the XXI Summer Olympic Games:

“The USCP supports and will continue to support the modern Olympic movement. Now the Soviet people are preparing for the 1980 Moscow Olympics and will do everything to ensure that it takes place at a high level, giving new impulses to others and the world.”

The Soviet capital did everything to prepare for the holiday of the planet's Olympians. All of Moscow, moreover, all Soviet people, perceived the Olympics as their own, dear cause.

And now the years of labor, the years of unrest are behind us. The creation work of the hailstone has been completed. Yes, hailstones: after all, 76 different large objects were being built or reconstructed at the same time; some of them are unparalleled and essentially unique. The middle ones are an indoor stadion for 45,000 spectators and a swimming pool on Mira Avenue, the CSKA football and athletics complex, a rowing canal, a kolschevaya track and an indoor volodrome in Krylatskoye, Sports Palaces in Izmailovo and Sokolniki, an equestrian center for professionals in the Bitsa area, the Druzhba sports hall. A general reconstruction of the Central Stadium named after V.I. Lenin was carried out, where the opening ceremony of the Games took place; a composite of seven sports is being held. New hotels have been erected, the Olympic air terminal “Sheremetevo-2” has been built, all services of the huge city, dressed in a festive parade, have been brought into exemplary order.

One of the most important features of the 1980 Olympics is its cultural program, which began last year. In the best theaters and concert halls, on open stage venues, in museums, and performance halls, there is a wide display of Soviet multinational art. Isn’t it significant that during the Olympics alone there are about 600 performances and 1,500 concerts in our capital! All this, including screenings of Soviet cinema, vernissages, excursions to historical places of the city and its environs; will allow participants and guests of the Olympics to appreciate the spiritual wealth of our people, our country, to understand the place of meeting the cultural needs of Soviet people in our way of life, to imagine what a significant contribution we make to the treasury of world culture.
