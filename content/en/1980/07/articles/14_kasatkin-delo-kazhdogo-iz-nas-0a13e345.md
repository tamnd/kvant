Case
each of us
By tradition, the daily final sessions of the Small Academy of Sciences of Schoolchildren of Crimea “Seeker” are devoted to one topic. The XVII session, held last summer, was held under the motto “Environmental protection is the business of each of us.”
At the session, the results of the year-long unusual expedition of the members of the “Seeker” in their native Crimea were summed up. Thousands of schoolchildren learned about how the purity of mountain rivers and lakes is protected, how the banks are strengthened and replacement Black Sea beaches are improved, how birds of the markets are preserved, how people protect forests and replenish their wildlife. The expedition participants learned about how fishing in the Black Sea is regularized, how the life of dolphins is studied, and much, much more.

The problem of environmental protection has been studied with equal importance by horse scientists and astronomers, chemists and cybernetics, archaeologists and mathematicians. More than two hundred messages, so-called creative contributions, were prepared by members of all nine sections of the “Seeker”. Here are some of them: “Biological protection of the earth - a thin ozone layer”, “The danger of biological pollution of other planets”, “Research of the actrimatate in Yalta” - reports prepared by young astronomers; “Computers in the service of ichthyologists”, “Ecological boundaries of space experiments” - works of young cybernetics; “The role of terrestrial plants in air purification”, “Study of the filtration properties of plants”, “Medicinal plants” are examples of creative contributions from horse biologists; “Physico-chemical problems of waste disposal”, “Solar future of energy”, “Hydrogen energy” - contributions from members of the physics section.

A large group of scientists from Crimea, Kyiv and Moscow is involved in the research. A representative commission consisting of four doctors and dozens of candidates of science answered numerous questions from schoolchildren at the press conference.

Members of the “Seeker” addressed all schoolchildren with the following confession:

study the nature of the native land, the features of its flora and fauna;

be promoters of nature conservation;

learn the basics of caring for plants and animals, take advantage of your love for nature;

be active encouragers of adults in protecting the environment;

With your personal work, maintain an increase in the number of green spaces, take care of the birds;

Be intolerant of manifestations of irresponsible attitude towards nature.

V. Kasatkin
