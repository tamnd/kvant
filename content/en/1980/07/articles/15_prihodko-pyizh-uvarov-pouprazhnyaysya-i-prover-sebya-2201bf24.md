⟦folio 41⟧

Try to solve the problems presented in 1978-1979. on written entrance exams at the physical, physical-technical and radiophysical faculties of Kharkov State University. A. M. Gorky is one of the oldest universities in our country, which this year celebrates its 175th anniversary.

1. Find the most in example a) and the most in example b) values ​​of the functions $\varphi_q(x)$ depending on the parameter $q$:

a) $\varphi_q(x) = \frac{q^2}{x} - \frac{1}{x-2}$, $x \in [3; 4]$, $q \in ]1; +\infty[$;

b) $\varphi_q(x) = \frac{1}{x^4-4qx^2+5q^2}$, $x \in [-1; 2]$, $q \in \mathbb{R}$.

2. At what value of the parameter $a$ the tangents to the parabolas $y_1(x)$ and $y_2(x)$ at the point of their intersection form an angle $\varphi$:

a) $y_1(x) = x^2 + 3x$, $y_2(x) = x^2 + x + a$, $\varphi = \pi/3$, $a > -1$;

b) $y_1(x) = x^2 + 3x - 4$, $y_2(x) = x^2 - x + a$, $\varphi = \pi/4$, $a > -2$.

3. For all valid values ​​of the parameter $a$, find values ​​$x$ that satisfy the equation:

a) $\int_0^{2x} (t - \log_2 a) dt = 2 \log_2 \frac{2}{a}$;

b) $\int_0^{4 \cdot \log_2 a} (t^3 - 2at) dt = 5a^2$.

4. Find $L(a)$ by passing to the limit for all permissible values ​​of the parameter $a$:

a) $L(a) = \sqrt{\lim_{x \to a} \frac{3(\sqrt{1+x}-\sqrt{1-x}) (x^2-4ax+3a^2)}{2x(x^3-a^3)}}$;

b) $L(a) = \log_{2a} \left[ \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{ax(a^2-a+x)}{\sqrt{1-ax}-\sqrt{1+ax}} \right]$.

5. Find the most minimum value of the function $f(x)$:

a) $f(x) = a^{2x} - 2 \cdot a^{x+2} + 4 \cdot a^2 + 7$;

b) $f(x) = \log_2^2 x + \log_2 x^{4a} - 6a + 2$.

6. a) Find the sum $S$ of all pairwise scalar products of different vectors, the beginning of which is located in one of the vertices of the octahedron, and the ends - in its remaining vertices, if the length of the edge of the octahedron is $l$.

6) Find the sum $S$ of all pairwise scalar products of different vectors, the beginning of which is located at one of the vertices of the cube, and the ends - in its remaining vertices, if the length of the edge of the cube is $l$.

7. a) Given a cube $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ with edge $a$. $P \in [C_1D_1]$, $|C_1P| = |PD_1|$, $Q$ — center of face $AA_1B_1B$. Find:

$1^\circ$ the distance between the crossing lines ($PQ$) and ($AC$);

$2^\circ$ is the distance between the crossing lines ($PQ$) and ($AC$).

6) The distance between two straight lines $i_1$ and $i_2$ crossing at an angle $a$ is $h$. Find the distance of the points $A \in i_1$ and $B \in i_2$, equistoriasm from the bases $C \in i_1$ and $D \in i_2$ promised to redistribute ($CD$) to these lines, if $|AC| = |BD| = a$.

8. Find $p$ and $q$ from the condition:

a) $\lim_{x \to \infty} \left( \frac{px^2+1}{qx+2} + x-2 \right) = -1$;

b) $\lim_{x \to \infty} \left( \frac{2x^2-1}{x+1} + px+q \right) = 1$.

9. Explore the function $y(x)$ and plot its graph:

a) $y = \cos (3\arcsin x)$;

b) $y = \sin (2\arctg x)$.
