⟦folio 52⟧

Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute named after. N. K. Krupskaya

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of mathematics)

1. Prove that the numbers $\frac{1}{\log_3 2}, \frac{1}{\log_6 2}$, $\frac{1}{\log_{12} 2}$ form an arithmetic progression.

2. Find all values ​​of $\alpha$ belonging to the segment $[0; 2\pi]$ and satisfying the equation

$$\int_{\pi/2}^{\alpha} \sin x \, dx = \sin 2\alpha.$$

3. Two sides of a triangle are equal to 6 cm and 8 cm. The medians drawn to these sides are mutually perpendicular. Find the length of the triangle.

4. Solve the system of equations:

$$\begin{cases} x + y + \sqrt{x+y} = 20, \\ x^2 + y^2 = 136. \end{cases}$$

5. The side of the base of a regular quadrangular straight line $SABCD$ is equal to 2, the height is $\sqrt{2}$. Find the distance between the side edge $SA$ and the diagonal of the base $BD$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)

1. Find the set of positive values ​​of $a$ satisfying the equation

$$\int_{0}^{a} (3x^2 + 4x - 5) \, dx = a^3 - 2.$$

2. Solve equation $\sqrt{\log_2 x} - 0.5 = \log_2 \sqrt{x}$.

3. The lateral sides of an isosceles trapezoid intersect at right angles as they continue. Determine the sides of a trapezoid if its area is $12 \, \text{cm}^2$ and its height is 2 cm.

4. Solve the equation

$$\sin 2x = \cos^4 \frac{x}{2} - \sin^4 \frac{x}{2}.$$

5. Given a cube $ABCD_1B_1C_1D_1$. Calculate the angle between lines $BC_1$ and $BK$, where $K$ is the midpoint of edge $AA_1$.

Physics

Oral exam assignment
(Faculty of Physics)

1. A body with a mass of $m_1 = 3,0$ kg slides along a horizontal plane under the action of a load with a mass of $m_2 = 1,0$ kg attached to the end of a cord. The cord is tied to a body of mass $m_1$ and will boil over a stationary block. Determine the acceleration of the system and the tension in the cord. Let's neglect the training.

2. In a boat of mass $m_2 = 60$ kg. The boat floats at speed $\vec{v}_1(|\vec{v}_1| = 2 \, \text{m/c})$. A person jumps from a boat in a horizontal direction with a speed of $\vec{v}(|\vec{v}| = 4 \, \text{m/c})$ relative to the boat. Find the speed of the boat after pressing the person forward along the boat's movement.

3. At what final speed can planes land on an airfield landing area of ​​length $l = 800$ m when braking with an acceleration of $\vec{a}(|\vec{a}| = 5,0 \, \text{m/c}^2)$?

4. When a certain mass of gas was heated by $\Delta T = 1$ K at constant pressure, this mass of gas increased by $n = 1/350$ parts of the original volume. Find the initial temperature of the gas.

5. A cylinder with a capacity of $V = 20$ l contains carbon dioxide weighing $m = 500$ g under a pressure of $p = 1,3$ MPa. Determine the temperature of the gas.

6. A piece of tin is lowered into a vessel containing $m_1 = 2,35$ kg of water at $t_1 = 20^\circ \text{C}$, heated to $t_2 = 230^\circ \text{C}$. The temperature of the water in the vessel increased by $\Delta t = 15^\circ$. Calculate the mass of tin $m_2$. Ignore the evaporation of water. The specific heat capacities of water and tin are $c_1 = 4,19 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K) and $c_2 = 2,5 \cdot 10^2$ J/(kg K), respectively.

7. The distance between two exact charges $q_1 = 10^{-6}$ C and $q_2 = -10^{-6}$ C is equal to $r = 10$ cm. Determine the force acting on the exact charge $q = 10^{-7}$ C, removed $r_1 = 6$ cm from the first and $r_2 = 8$ cm from the second charge ($\varepsilon_0 = 8,85 \cdot 10^{-12} \, \text{Ф/m}$).

8. A galvanic element gives a current of $I_1 = 0,2$ A to an external resistance of $R_1 = 4$ Ohm. If the external resistance is equal to $R_2 = 7$ Ohm, then the element gives a current of $I_2 = 0,14$ A. What current does it give if it is short-circuited?

9. What magnification does a projection lamp provide if its lens with a focal length of $F = 18$ cm is located at a distance of $f = 6,0$ m from the screen?

10. The sun's rays fall on the surface of the water at the angular height of the Sun above the horizon $\alpha = 30^\circ$. What kind of child are the rays in water after refraction? The refractive index of water is $n = 1,33$.
