M631. Two-digit numbers from 19 to 80 are written out in a row. Is the resulting number 192021... ...7980 divisible by 1980? (8)

V. Fedotov

M632. The cargo, packed in containers, must be delivered to the Salyut orbital space station. The number of containers is at least 35, the total cargo weight is 18 tons. There are seven Progress transport ships, each of which can deliver 3 tons of cargo into orbit. It is known that these ships can simultaneously deliver any 35 of the available containers. Prove that they can deliver all the available cargo into orbit at once. (8-9)

A. Kolotov

M633. A point E is given on the diameter AC of a certain circle. Draw a chord BD through it so that the area of ​​the quadrilateral ABCD is the widest. (9)

I. Sharytsin

M634. Let us denote by S(n) the sum of all digits of the natural number n.
a) Is there a natural number n such that n + S(n) = 1980?
b) Prove that at least one of any two consecutive natural numbers can be represented as n + S(n) for some third natural number n. (8)

S. Konyatsin

F643. Two packages with identical initial speeds, directed horizontally, simultaneously launch from the south and north poles of the Earth. After a time τ = 3 hours 20 minutes, the packets were at their maximum distance from each other. Determine the maximum distance between packages. The acceleration of free fall on Earth is considered known. The radius of the Earth is $R_3 = 6400$ km. (10)

S. Kozel

F644. A circuit consisting of two capacitors with capacitances $C_1$ and $C_2$ ($C_2 > C_1$) and two ideal diodes $\varDelta_1$ and $\varDelta_2$ (Fig. 1) is connected to an alternating voltage source $u = U_0$ cos $\omega t$. Determine the dependence of the voltage on the capacitors on time in the set mode. Draw the resulting dependencies on a graph. The resistance of an ideal diode in the forward direction is zero, in the reverse direction it is infinity. (10)

V. Skorovarov

F645. The loudspeaker has a diffuser with a cross-sectional plane $S = 300$ cm$^2$ and a mass $m = 5$ g. The resonant part of the diffuser is $v_0 = 50$ Hz. What is its resonant part if you place the loudspeaker on the wall of a closed box with a volume of $V = 40$ l (Fig. 2). The calculation is carried out under the assumption that the air temperature inside the box does not change when the diffuser oscillates. (10)

A. Zilberman

F646. A directed flow of electricity howls out of a thin wide gap at a speed of $v = 10^5$ m/s (Fig. 3). The concentration of electrons in the flow is $n = 10^{10}$ particles/m$^3$. At what distance from the slit will the beam thickness double? Electron mass $m = 9 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, electron charge $e = 1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ C, dielectric constant $\varepsilon_0 = 8,85 \cdot 10^{-12}$ F/m. (9)

A. Zilberman

F647. A large dzhedeva drop left the clouds in calm weather at high altitude. At the moment when the acceleration of the drop was equal to 7.5 m/s$^2$, its speed was 20 m/s. Near the Earth, the drop fell at a constant speed and, hitting the side glass automatically, left a mark on it at an angle of 30° to the vertical. Will a traffic police inspector fine a driver for speeding if the permitted speed is 60 km/h? Consider the air resistance force to be proportional to the square of the drop velocity. (9)

V. Belonukkin
