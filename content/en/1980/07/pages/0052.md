Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1

1. The sum of an infinite geometric progression made up of odd-numbered terms of an infinite geometric progression is $\frac{64}{3}$, and the sum of a progression made up of even-numbered terms of the same progression is $\frac{32}{3}$. Find the progression.

2. It assigns one of the bases of a regular triangular to the great circle of a ball of radius $R$, and the vertices of the other base belong to the surface of this ball. Determine the height of the prism at which the sum of the lengths of all its edges will be greatest.

3. Solve the equation
$$\cos 3x - \cos 7x = \sqrt{3} \sin 2x$$

4. Find the domain of a function
$$y = \log_2(2^{-x} - \frac{1}{2})$$

5. Determine at what values ​​of $a$ the inequality is true
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}} \int \left( \frac{3}{2} \sqrt{x} + 1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \right) dx < 4.$$

Option 2

1. All members of the arithmetic progression are natural numbers. The sum of its nine consecutive terms, starting from the first, is greater than 200 but less than 220. Find the progression if its second term is 12.

2. A regular triangular prism is inscribed in a sphere, the lengths of all edges of which are equal to $a$. A similar prism with its lower base lies on the upper base of this prism, and the vertices of its upper base belong to the sphere. Find the length of the edge of the second prism.

3. Find the largest and smallest values ​​of a function
$$f(x) = 4x^3 - 9x^2 - 12x$$
in the interval $[-1; 1]$.

4. Solve the equation
$$\log_3 (5-x) + 2 \log_3 \sqrt{3-x} = 1.$$

5. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the lines (make figure) $y = (x-1)^2$, $y=x+1$.

Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. Two bodies are thrown vertically upward from one point, one after the other with integral $t = 2$ s, with identical initial velocities $|v_0| = 50$ m/s. After how long and at what height will the bodies meet?

2. A small body slides without friction from the top of a hemisphere of radius $R$. At what height will it be rejected from the surface of the hemisphere?

3. A gun with a mass of $M = 450$ kg moves in the horizontal direction. The mass of the projectile is $m = 5$ kg and its initial speed is $|v_0| = 450$ m/s. When fired, the barrel refuses by $s = 45$ cm. Determine the average value of the braking force developing in the Ordium recoil device.

4. A cylinder with a volume of $V = 10$ l contains hydrogen at a temperature of $t = 20^\circ$ C under a pressure of $p = 10^7$ Pa. What amount of hydrogen was released from the cylinder if $m = 50$ g of water was formed when the remaining hydrogen was completely removed?

5. At what depth does the air bubble have a diameter second smaller than that of the water surface if the barometric pressure at the water level is $p_0 = 10^5$ Pa? The water temperature is considered constant at any depth. The density of water is $q = 10^3$ kg/m³.

6. A measuring device with an internal resistance of $R = 75$ Ohm has a scale of $n = 150$ divisions. Division value $U_0 = 10$ mV. How to use this device to change: a) current up to $I = 1,5$ A; b) voltage up to $U = 750$ V?

7. A near-linear conductor with a mass of $m = 3$ kg, through which a current of $I = 5$ A flows, rises vertically in a single-pot magnetic field with an induction of $|\vec{B}| = 3$ T, moving at an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the magnetic lines. After $t = 2$ s after the start of movement, he reaches a speed of $|\vec{v}| = 10$ m/s. Determine the length of the conductor.
