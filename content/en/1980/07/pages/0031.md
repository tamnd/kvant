$$\frac{r}{R} = \frac{I}{I_3 + I_4} = \frac{1}{5}.$$

F588. Figure 1 (top view) is a photograph of smoke plumes coming from three locomotives moving along a straight section of railway track. The speed of the first locomotive is $|\vec{v}_1| = 50 \text{ km/ч}$, and the second one is $|\vec{v}_2| = 70 \text{ km/ч}$, the directions of their movement are indicated on figure by arrows. What is the speed of the third locomotive?

First of all, let's find out what determines the direction of the smoke plume. A cloud of smoke released by a locomotive at point $A$, after time $t$ will be blown by the wind to point $C$ such that $\vec{AC} = \vec{ut}$, where $\vec{u}$ is the wind speed (Fig. 2). The train, after time $t$, ends up at point $B$ such that $\vec{AB} = \vec{vt}$, where $\vec{v}$ is the speed of the train. It is obvious that the direction of the smoke plume goes along the vector of the difference between the vectors $\vec{AC}$ and $\vec{AB}$, or, what is the same, along the vector $d$, equal to the difference between the vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.

Let's find the wind speed. Let's draw the vector $\vec{v}_1$ on an arbitrary scale; then from the beginning $O$ of the vector $\vec{v}_1$ we draw the vector $\vec{v}_2$ on the same scale; from the ends of the vectors $\vec{v}_1$ and $\vec{v}_2$:

Let's draw straight lines parallel to the corresponding smoke plumes (Fig. 3). The point $K$ of intersection of these lines is connected to the point $O$. Vector $\vec{OK}$ represents the wind speed vector on the scale we have chosen, since the difference vector $\vec{OK} - \vec{v}_1$ goes along the smoke plume from the first locomotive. Let's draw a plume of smoke released by it, which should go along the difference vector $\vec{OK} - \vec{v}_3$. Let's draw a straight line through $K$, parallel to the smoke plume from the third locomotive. The intersection of this line with the vector $\vec{v}_2$ defines $\vec{v}_3$. Having measured the length $\vec{v}_3$, knowing the scale in which all vectors are constructed, we find $|\vec{v}_3|$:

$$|\vec{v}_3| = 40 \text{ km/ч}.$$
