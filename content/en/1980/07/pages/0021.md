ABOUT OVO

The main thing is that no matter how carefully we try to put an egg in a cup, we cannot change the laws of physics. An egg may not grow, and a skilled cook never does, but it will inevitably rise vertically due to the acceleration of gravity according to the Joule-Lenz law.

From the book by V. Pokhlebkin “The Gains of Good Cooking.”

"Abovo" - translated from Latin, it literally means "from the egg." In a figurative sense, this expression is used when one wants to indicate the original, primary something. It was this figurative meaning that the ancients included as the main one in the words “abovo”. Without knowing it themselves, they thereby released the egg of the sophissim that has existed since time immemorial: what appeared first - the chicken or the egg? We will leave this question aside, and will deal with some physical phenomena, choosing as a research tool... a chicken egg.

Who does not remember the fatal reason for the division between Lilliput and the empires of Blefuscu, described in Guliver's Travels? This is
The order was a decree from the Emperor of Lilliput, ordering all his subjects, under pain of death, to break eggs from the sharp end. Guliver believed that choosing the end from which to break the egg was the master's business. Whatever you want, break it from there. The authors completely agree with Guliver, but still: which end of the egg is easier to break? Solving this problem will help you choose the right tactics in the “boiled egg battles” that so often arise at breakfast in Pinoneer camps.

What should you do: attack the enemy or wait for the attack yourself? Should I choose a larger egg or a small one? keep it with the sharp or blunt end towards the enemy? - these are the main questions of tactics in such a battle. There is an opinion that the attack wins. However, with a uniform movement of the egg, it makes no difference whether to attack yourself or wait for the enemy to attack.
