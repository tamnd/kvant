Mathematics

Written exam
Option 1
(exam in algebra and principles of analysis at general technical faculties)

1. Give the definition of an exponential function. Form and prove the properties of the function $y = a^x$, $0 < a < 1$.

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{6x - x^2} - 5 = 2x - 6.$$

3. Form a rule for finding the smallest and largest values ​​of a function differentiable on an interval. Calculate the largest and smallest values ​​of the function $y = 2 - 3x^2 - x^3$ on the interval [-3; 1]. Construct a graph of the function on this segment.

4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\log_4 \frac{x+1}{x+2}} \leq \frac{1}{\log_4 (x+3)}$$.

5. Find the set of all numbers $a \in \mathbb{R}$, for each of which the line passing through the point $M(1; 2)$ intersects the graph of the function $y = \frac{a}{x}$ at two points, the sum of the ordinat coordinates is $a$.

Option 2
(exam at the Faculty of Applied Mathematics)

1. Examine for extrema and monotonicity of the function $y = \frac{2x}{x^2 + 9}$ and plot it.

2. Find all solutions of the equation $(4\cos x + 1)^2 - 2|4\cos x + 1| = 3$ that lie within the segment [0; 7].

3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_{(3x+5)}(9x^2 + 8x + 8) > 2.$$

4. In a regular quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ with vertex $S$, the angle between adjacent lateral faces is $a$ and the lateral edges have a length equal to 1. Find: 1) scalar product $\vec{AS} \cdot \vec{AB}$; 2) the length of the vector $\vec{AK}$, if the point $K$ is the middle of the edge $BS$.

5. The line touches parabolas $y = -x^2 + 4x + 1$ and parabolas $y = 3x^2 + 4x + 7/3$. Write the equation of this line. For what values ​​of $k$ and $b$ does the line $y = kx + b$ have no common points with any of the parabolas?
