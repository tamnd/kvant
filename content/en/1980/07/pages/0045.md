Moscow Aviation Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze

This year, the Moscow Order of Lenin Academy of Sciences Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze (MAI) turns 50 years old. In a relatively short period of time, the institute turned into one of the largest machine-building industries in the country. Graduates of the institute work at the forefront of the aviation industry, in the latest fields of science and technology. Suffice it to say that USSR pilot-cosmonauts V.N. Volkov, A.S. Ivanchenko, V.N. Kubasov, V.V. Lebedev, V.I. Sevastyanov studied at MAI.

The Institute has nine faculties: aircraft and helicopter engineering, aircraft, propulsion systems, automatic control systems for aircraft, radio electronics of aircraft, economics and organization of aircraft production, aircraft installations, applied mathematics, general engineering training.

From the first years of the institute they are widely involved in research work in departments and in student design bureaus. Thus, students designed and built a sports flying aircraft «Kvant», which set two world records in 1979, and contributed to the development of the IL-86 airbus. On October 26, 1978, the first student artificial Earth satellite, developed by MAI students, was launched into space orbit.

Great opportunities are provided for sports fans: the MAI sports club has more than 30 sections.

In the Institute’s Palace of Culture, students can always find something to do: play a minator or an agitator in the student theater
