⟦folio 43⟧

Kyiv State University named after. T. G. Shevchenko

Matematika

Written exam

Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_4 x + \log_4 y = 1 + \log_4 9, \\ x + y = 20. \end{cases}$$

2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{9^x + 3^x} \geq 9 - 3^x.$$

3. A cone is described around a ball of volume $V$ in such a way that its volume is the smallest of all possible. Find the volume of the cone.

4. At what value of $a$ does the line $y = a$ divide the area of ​​the figure bounded by lines $y = 0$ and $y = 2 + x - x^2$ in half?

5. Solve the equation
$$\frac{2 \mathrm{tg} \frac{x}{2}}{1 + \mathrm{tg}^2 \frac{x}{2}} = y^2 - 4y + 5.$$

Option 2
(Faculty of Cybernetics)

1. Let
$$\max (A, B) = \begin{cases} A, если A \geqslant B \\ B, если B \geqslant A. \end{cases}$$

Calculate the area of ​​a flat figure bounded by parts of the lines max $(x, y) = 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ lying in the first square.

2. Knowing the radius $r$ of the base of the cone, determine the radius of the section that is parallel to the base and divides the volume of the cone in half.

3. Solve the equation
$$2 \sin^2 x - 5 \sin x \cdot \cos x - 8 \cos^2 x = -2.$$

4. For what values ​​of $a$ does the equation $ax^2 = \ln x$ have one root?

Option 3
(faculties of physics and radiophysics)

1. The point moves rectilinearly according to the law
$$s(t) = -\frac{1}{6} t^3 + 3t^2 - 5$$
(time is measured in seconds, distance traveled in meters). Define:
1) the moment in time when the acceleration of a point is zero;
2) At what speed is the point moving at this moment?
2. A ball is inscribed in a cone whose height is equal to the radius of the base. Find the ratio of the volume of the sphere to the volume of the cone.

3. Solve inequality
$$x + 2 < \sqrt{x + 14}.$$

4. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log_3 (\log_2 x) + \log_{\frac{1}{3}} (\log_{\frac{1}{2}} y) = 1, \\ xy^2 = 4. \end{cases}$$

5. Find the most and most values ​​of a function
$$y = \sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{8} \right) \cdot \cos \left( x - \frac{\pi}{24} \right).$$

Oral exam objectives

1. Solve equations
a) $\log_3 (\cos x) + \log_{\frac{1}{3}} (-\sin x) = 0$;
b) $tg |x| = |tg x|$;
b) $\pi \sin x = \left| x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right| - \left| x - \frac{3\pi}{4} \right|$.

2. Solve inequality
$$|x + 2|^{\frac{1}{x^2 - x - 6}} \geq |x + 2|^{\frac{2}{x^3}}.$$

3. Find multi-relativity intervals and extrema points of the function
$$y = \left| |x^2 - 1| - 2 \right|.$$

Construct a graph of this function.

4. Graph the function
$$y = \begin{cases} l^x - 1, \text{ если } x < 0, \\ \sqrt{4x - x^2}, \text{ если } x \geqslant 0. \end{cases}$$

5. For what $a$ function
Does $$f(x) = x^3 + 3(a - 7)x^2 + 3(a^2 - 9)x + 1$$ have a positive maximum point?

6. For what $m$ function
$$f(x) = 2e^x - me^{-x} + (1 + 2m)x - 3$$

monotonically increases on all axes?

7. For what $a$ the equation
$$3x \lg x = 1 + a \lg x$$ has: a) one solution; 6) two solutions?

8. Depending on the parameter $a$, how many roots does the equation have?
$$x^2 \cdot e^{2|x|} = 4a.$$

9. How many roots belonging to the interval $[-\pi; \pi]$ does the equation have?
$$3 \sin x + \sin 3x = 2a$$

depending on parameter $a$?

10. At what $a$ is the sum of the cubes of the roots of the equation
$$6x^2 + 6(a - 1)x - 5a + 2a^2 = 0$$

biggest?

11. Draw a set of points on a plane
$$\{(x; y) | x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 2y + 1 = 0 \}.$$

Write the equation for the image of this set at the parallel intersection $\vec{p} = (0; -1)$.

12. Find the values ​​of $a$ for which the set of equations
$$\{(x; y) | x^2 + y^2 + 2x \leqslant 1 \} \cap \{(x; y) | x - y + a \geqslant 0\}$$
