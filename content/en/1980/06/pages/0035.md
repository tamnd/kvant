What does it mean to “know”?

Probably, many of the readers «Kvant» are planning to enter university after finishing school. Of course, everyone is concerned about the question of what requirements are imposed on knowledge. What and how do you need to know? Let's try to explain this with a few examples.

Matematika consists of axioms, theorems and proofs. Along with this, definitions play a fundamental role in mathematics. Knowing mathematics means knowing all its parts. Moreover, each part requires its own special approach.

For example, what does it mean to know the definition of the limit of a function? This is what we heard in a mathematics lesson in a Moscow school.

***

Teacher. What does equality mean?

$$\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = b?$$

Student. Equality (1) means that the number b is the limit of the function f as x tends to a.

Teacher. This is not the answer yet. What is the meaning of equality (1)?

Student. Equality (1) means that for any positive number e there will be a positive number δ such that for all x≠a satisfying the inequality |x-a|<δ, the inequality |f(x)-b|<ε will hold.
