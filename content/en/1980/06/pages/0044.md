⟦folio 42⟧

Options for university entrance exams in 1979

Leningrad State University named after. A. A. Zhdanova

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of mathematics and mechanics)
1. How many roots does the equation have?
$$f(x) - af'(x) = 0,$$
if $f(x) = (x-x_1)(x-x_2)(x-x_3)(x-x_4)$, $a$, $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$, $x_4$ are real numbers ($x_1 < x_2 < x_3 < x_4$)?
2. Solve the equation (at $a \in \mathbb{R}$)
$$\log_a(1-8a^{-x}) \geq 2(1-x).$$
3. Solve the equation (at $a \in \mathbb{R}$)
$$a \cdot \sin x + \mathrm{tg} x + 1 = \frac{1}{\cos x}.$$
4. A circle of radius $R$ is inscribed in the equilateral curve $ABCD$; $K$ is the point of intersection of the diagonal graphezia, $[AB]$ is its major base, $KBA = a$. Find the radius of the circle tangent to the given circle and the diagonal segments $[KB]$, $[KC]$.
5. The points $M, N, P, Q$ are located in space so that $(MN) \perp (PQ)$, $(MP) \perp (NQ)$. Prove that and $(MQ) \perp (NP)$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)
1. For which $a (a \in [0; 1])$ the area of ​​the figure bounded by the graph of the function $y = f(x)$ and the lines $x = 0, x = 1, y = f(a)$ has the most and the most values, if $f(x) = x^a + 3x^\beta$, $a, \beta$ are real numbers, and $a > 1$, $\beta > 1$?
2. For what $a (a \in \mathbb{R})$ is any solution to the equation
$$\log_3(x^2 - 3x + 7) / \log_3(3x + 2) < 1$$
will also be a solution to the inequality $x^2 + (5-2a)x \leq 10a$?
3. Solve the equation (at $a \in \mathbb{R}$)
$$(8a^2 + 1) \sin^3 x - (4a^2 + 1) \sin x + 2a \cdot \cos^3 x = 0.$$
4. The lengths of the legs of a right triangle are $a$ and $b$, the length of the bisserris of a right angle is $l$. Is it true that $l \leq \sqrt{\frac{ab}{2}}$?
5. The sphere passes through the center of a ball inscribed in a regular triangle and touches three edges of the triangle emanating from the same vertex. Find the radius of the sphere if the length of the edge of the triangle is $a$.

Option 3
(Faculty of Physics)
1. The width of the river is $a$ m, its flow speed is $w$ m/sec, the speed of a swimmer in still water is $v$ m/sec ($v < w$), and the speed of his movement on land is $u$ m/sec. In what shortest time can a swimmer get to a point located on the opposite bank opposite the place from which he begins the crossing? Assume that in the water the swimmer does not change the movement he chose at the beginning of the crossing and that the velocity vector of the river flow is parallel to its banks, which are assumed to be straight.
2. For what $a (a \in \mathbb{R})$ does the point $(a; a^2)$ located inside the triangle bounded by the graph of the function $y = f(x)$ and the lines $x = 0, x = 1, y = f(a)$ have the most and most values, if $f(x) = x^a + 3x^\beta$, $a, \beta$ are real numbers, and $a > 1$, $\beta > 1$?
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^6 x + \cos^6 x = 1 + \sin 4x.$$
4. Find $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n$ if
$$x_n = \frac{1}{1 \cdot 2} + \frac{1}{1 \cdot 4} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3} + \frac{1}{4 \cdot 7} + \dots + \frac{1}{n(n+1)} + \frac{1}{(3n-2)(3n+1)}$$
5. Three large circles of the same radius $R$ touch each other in pairs. Three small circles have the same radius and touch each other in pairs. Find the radius of a small circle if each of the small circles also touches two large ones.
