⟦folio none⟧

These photographs show the egg and three of its "modifications" obtained using the following general design. Let us draw several lines (straight lines, circles, etc.) on a plane on which there is some image. The set of these lines dividing the plane into “elementary regions” is called the “cut grid”.
Let the cut grid have some symmetry; then some elements of the region can be rearranged in accordance with this symmetry. If an elementary region (or several) itself has symmetry, then it can, in addition, be “rotated” in accordance with it.
It was in this way that three “modifications” of the egg in the photographs were obtained. Follow how.

V. Koleychuk
