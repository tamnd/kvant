Geometry

7. Points H, K, M are the midpoints of the edges AB, BC and CD of an arbitrary tetrahedron ABCD (Fig. 6). What other edges of the tetrahedron, besides those indicated, does the plane (HKM) intersect?

A) AD; B) BD; B) AC; D) The plane (HKM) does not intersect any other edges; D) It is impossible to determine.

8. How many planes are there that pass through a given point and are parallel to two given parallel lines?

A) None;
B) One;

9. Which of the following vectors is equal to the sum of the vectors $H\vec{M} + D\vec{A} + C\vec{M}$, where points H and M are the midpoints of the edges AB and CD of the tetrahedra ABCD (see Fig. 6)?

A) 0; B) HC; B) AC; D) AH; D) HA.

10. The scalar product of two vectors is equal to - 2. What can you say about the angle between these vectors?

A) Sharp;
B) Dumb;
B) Straight;
D) It is impossible to determine whether an angle is acute, obtuse or straight;
D) The scalar product cannot equal - 2.
