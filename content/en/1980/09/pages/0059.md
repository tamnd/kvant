It will not surprise tenth-class students, of course, that the area obtained as a result of executing program (10) is called INTEGRAL. In the school mathematics course (“Algebra and the beginnings of analysis 10”, paragraph 101) it is said that the interval from a non-negative continuous function is the area of ​​the corresponding linear boundary curve. Thus, program (10) is a typical example of the simplest numerical interpolation program.

The method of numerical insertion used in this program is called the rectilinear method: in the body of the cycle, the areas of the rectilinear elements are calculated, from which an approximation of the value of the calculated calculated area is added. Exactly the result from the DELTA step, as well as from the selected trapezoid method: the resulting area is made up of elementary trapezoids (Fig. 10).
