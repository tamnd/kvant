However, if for some reason a section of the solenoid winding loses its superconducting properties, an accident will occur. In this area, the current will release a large amount of heat and an explosion will occur. Come up with the simplest device to eliminate such accidents (do not try to come up with any circuits with a relay that opens the circuit - they will not help).
