⟦folio 47⟧

A. Zemlyakov

Test yourself

Opening our section “Through the Pages of School Textbooks” in the new school year, we invite our readers, as a warm-up before the start of the school year, to test their knowledge over the past year. To do this, they should complete the public task below: 9th graders - for VIII grade, 10th graders - for IX grade. For each of the 10 questions you need to choose the only correct answer; It will take 30 minutes to complete the entire task.

This task is offered at the republican rounds of the All-Union Mathematical Olympiad in 1980. You should not, just once, think about solving tricky, “Olympid” problems: the task consists of simple questions that test students’ knowledge of the main topics of mathematics in grades VIII-IX.

So, sharpen your pencils, time yourself...

VIII class

Algebra

1. Which of the following formulas gives the paraboly shown at figure 1?

A) $y = -x^2 + 2$;
B) $y = -x^2 + 2x$;
B) $y = -x^2 - 2x$;
G) $y = -(x-1)^2$;
D) $y = -(x-2)^2$.

2. What does the graph of the equation $x^2 + xy = 0$ represent?

A) Point;

B) Direct;
B) Union of two lines;
G) Circumference;
D) Let there be a set.

3. Reduce the following fraction:
$$\frac{x^3 - y^3}{x^2 - y^2}.$$

A) $\frac{x^2 + xy + y^2}{x + y}$;
B) $\frac{x^2 - xy + y^2}{x + y}$;
G); $\frac{x^2 - xy + y^2}{x - y}$;
D) $\frac{x^2 + xy - y^2}{x + y}$.

4. For what values ​​of $x$ is the equality $\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$ true?

A) For all $x$;
B) Only at $x \geqslant 0$;
B) Only at $x \leqslant 0$;
G) Only at $x = 0$;
D) Under no $x$.

5. What is the domain of definition of the expression $(a - 5)^{-\frac{1}{3}}$?

A) $] -\infty; +\infty[$;
B) $] -\infty; 5[U]5; +\infty[$;
B) $[5; +\infty[$;
G) $]5; +\infty[$;
D) $\emptyset$.

6. What is the set of solutions to the inequality $(0,1)^x > 100$?

A) $] -\infty; 2[$;
B) $]2; +\infty[$;
B) $] -\infty; -2[$;
G) $] -2; +\infty[$;
D) $\emptyset$.

Geometry

7. What is the value of sin $\alpha$ if $\cos \alpha = -\frac{1}{2}$ and $0^\circ < \alpha < 180^\circ$?

A) $\frac{1}{2}$;
B) $-\frac{1}{2}$;
B) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$;
G) $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$;
D) Cannot be determined.
