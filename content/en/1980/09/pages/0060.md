⟦folio 58⟧

New books

We continue to publish annotations on books on mathematics and physics that are accessible and interesting to our readers. In this issue we talk about two series of popular science books published by the publishing houses “Science” and “Pedagogy”.

Publishing house "Science"
Library Series «Kvant»
This series begins airing this year. It is primarily intended for schoolchildren interested in physics and mathematics. In the second and third quarters, 7 books from this series will be released, varied in their topics, but with a common idea - to interest schoolchildren, show them all the beauty and attractiveness of mathematics and physics, and arouse their interest in these sciences.

Mathematics
1. Alexaンドrov P. S. Introduction to the theory of eruption. Volume 10 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
This book, written by one of the leading Soviet mathematicians P. S. Aleksaンドrov, is an elementary introduction to group theory.
The concept of a group is one of the most important concepts in modern mathematics. Group theory methods are widely used in geometry, topology and algebra. Very important and profound applications of group theory are found in quantum mechanics, nuclear physics and crystallography.
Written in simple and clear language, equipped with a large number of geometric illustrations, this book is one of the best textbooks in the world mathematical literature for an initial introduction to group theory.

2. Ore O. Proposition in number theory. Translation from English. Volume 8 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 45 kopecks.
The book by the famous Norwegian mathematician O. Ore reveals the beauty of mathematics using the example of one of its oldest branches - number theory. (In «Kvant», 1979, No. 12 published Open Book.)
The book's presentation of the fundamentals of number theory is largely unconventional. Along with the theory of comparison and information about number systems, there are stories about magic squares and solving arithmetic puzzles. At every opportunity, the author points out the possibility of practical application of the presented results and introduces the reader to the current state of number theory and unsolved problems.

3. Mochalov L.P. Puzzles. Volume 7 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The book contains about 200 puzzles - challenging tasks that require intelligence and intelligence to solve. Many of the puzzles presented in this book were first seen on the pages of our magazine and received a large readership.

Physics
1. Bronstein M.P. Atoms and electrons. Volume 10 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
This book was written in 1935 by the famous Soviet physicist and popularizer of science, Professor Matvey Petrovich Bronstein. For the first time, in a small edition, it was published in Leningrad and almost immediately became a bibliographic rarity.
The book tells in an accessible and popular way about the development of atomism, how the masses of atoms and their sizes were first measured, what works and experiments led to the discovery of electrons and the elucidation of the structure of atoms.

This edition of the book ends with a brief account of the life and scientific activities of M. P. Bronstein.
2. Aslamazov L. G., Slobodetsky I. Sh. Problems in physics. Volume 10 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
The authors of this collection have been organizing and conducting various physical optimizations for a number of years, including the All-Union Optimization in Physics.
The collection contains tasks that have a “physical twist”. Solving them does not require knowledge beyond the school curriculum, but a clear and precise understanding of the basic laws of physics and the ability to apply your knowledge in a specific situation is necessary.
Some tasks are research. To solve them, you need to do a little independent research.
Most of the problems included in the collection are not schematic, but relate to various natural phenomena. Some tasks are evaluative in nature.
Many problems were used in physical optimization, part of the problem was published in the journal «Kvant». At the end of the collection, solutions to all problems are given.
3. Life in a home laboratory. Volume 10 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
This book is compiled from the best papers published in the «Kvant» Laboratory.
They describe relatively simple experiments that the reader can do on his own at home, using simple equipment, often made by himself.
The reader will be able to learn how to grow criticals, investigate the operation of magnetic mechanisms, conduct experiments with light, and use a gramophone record or a ball from a bearing. All these experiments will help improve physical laws and impart skills to independent physical research.
