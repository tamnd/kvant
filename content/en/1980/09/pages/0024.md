About waves on the sea and fish in puddles

When throwing pebbles into the water, look at the circles they form; otherwise such throwing will be empty fun.

Kozma Prutkov

Of all the many types of waves studied by physicists, water waves are the most accessible to observation. Concepts of amplitude, wavelength, etc. for them are visual, and those that arise during their distribution are familiar and interesting. At the same time, many of the patterns of their propagation are the same as for light or sound waves, and therefore, by “throwing pebbles into the water,” a lot can be understood.

Waves on water are a very wide range of phenomena. This is the sea surf and fish in puddles, huge storm surges and the slow rise of water during high tides, ship waves and tsunamis crushing everything in its path.

The study of water waves began a very long time ago. The first theory - the theory of tides - goes back to Newton and is a direct application of his theory of universal gravitation. Further, in the study of phenomena on water there were periods of lull, and its own “golden age” associated with the names of Rayleigh and Stokes. But what’s surprising is that very interesting results in this classical branch of science were obtained quite recently. With their help, it was possible to explain experiments carried out in the last century.

In this article we would like to consider some phenomena that have arisen on the surface of a liquid, in particular water. Maybe after this you will look a little differently at the circles diverging from a thrown stone.
