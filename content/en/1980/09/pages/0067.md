CHESS COMPETITION

PAT ON THE BOARD

The dramatic incident that occurred in the game Hübner - Adorjan suggested to us the next theme of the competition - stalemate on the board! Of course, in the practice of grandmasters, things rarely end in stalemate, but sometimes, as we see, such amazing events happen. The next position arose 75 years ago.

20th game of the rematch for the world championship to M. Tal (1961).

Very often, stalemate occurs when a pawn is promoted to a queen, but the goal is achieved if a weaker piece, usually a rook (less often a bishop or knight), is placed on the board together with the queen. This technique in composition is called “weak transformation”.

The classic stage, with only four pieces, in which there is a full fight on both sides, is presented in competition task No. 1. In all chess literature it is difficult to find such an unexpected and red fig with such limited material.

1. F. Saavedra, 1895 White starts and wins.

Of course, in the 20th century, Saavedra's stalemate idea was repeatedly improved. One of the best variations is represented by competition task No. 2.

2. M. Liburkin, 1931 White starts and wins.

3. A. Troitsky, 1910 Whites start and do nothing.

The veiled stalemate trap, which at one time went around the entire world chess press, was set up by M. Botvinnik in This position could arise during the second completion of the game. Black's position looks hopeless: after 1...Rd1 2.JL:c6+ Npb7 3.JLf6 R:d4+ 4.Kpb5 and 5.JLf7+ he must resign. However, Botvinnik, during his home analysis, found an unexpected salvation: 1...Rb4+!! Now 2.Kp:b4 leads to stalemate, and 2.Kpa3 R:d4 gives Black a simple draw. During the additional play, two moves before the position on the diagram, Tal discovered a tricky trap, went a different way, but he no longer had a win. The game ended in a draw on move 121. And the next day Botvinnik easily defeated the upset world champion and regained his chess crown.

In chess composition, the stalemate idea is one of the most popular. In one case White is saved thanks to a stalemate, in others he wins by avoiding a subtle stalemate trap.
