⟦folio 60⟧

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling students from eight-year and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in the 8th, 9th and 10th grades for the 1980/81 academic year.

The goal of this school is to help students study physics and mathematics independently. That is why, when admitting to 3FTSH, pre-option is given to students living in rural areas and workers’ settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

3FTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom become students of the country's leading universities.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized locally at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the 3FTS introductory task. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the names of the circle leader and a changed list of circle members by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will regularly receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. 3FTSH tasks contain theoretical material, an analysis of the nature of the task and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple tasks and more complex ones (at the level of competitive tasks at MIPT). Work departments

All students are tested in 3FTSH or its films, and the work of members of the circle is checked by its leader.

Moscow students are given classes in physics and mathematics. They are held twice a week according to the 3FTSH program in evening consultation points (in a number of Moscow schools), recruitment to which is carried out either based on the results of the 3FTSH entrance task, or based on the results of an interview in physics and mathematics. The interview will be held in the second half of September (for information, call 216-00-05, ext. 2-59).

Each student must complete the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently in Russian and carefully copy it into one school journal. The order of the task will be the same as in the task. Send the terrada in a large envelope, a simple parcel post (just do not roll the terrada into a tube). Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you are studying, indicating your class. Paste the certificate on the inside cover of the terradi. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. On the outside of the terradi, glue a sheet of paper filled in according to the sample shown below.

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 1, 1980 (according to the postal ITEM of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be sent back.

Admission to the school is carried out by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1980.
