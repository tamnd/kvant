The story of how Galileo discovered the laws of motion

S. Gindikin

The first foundations of dynamics were laid by Galileo. The action of forces do was considered exclusively in the case of their equilibrium; and although the accelerated movement of freely feeding bodies and the curved movement of thrown bodies also assigned a constant acting force to gravity, it was not possible to establish the laws of this combined phenomenon, depending on such a simple reason. Galileo was the first to take this step and opened up a new and limitless area for the development of mechanics. This discovery... now constitutes the most significant and indelible part of the merit of this great man. Indeed, to discover the satellites of Jupiter, the phases of Venus, sunspots, etc., requires only a telephone and observation, but it takes an exceptional genius to establish the laws of nature on phenomena that have always been before everyone's eyes and yet have eluded the attention of philosophers.
