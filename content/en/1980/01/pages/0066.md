1. Let us denote the speed of the pedestrian who left $A$ by $v_1$, who left $B$ by $v_2$, and the required distance by $x$. Since the conditions of the problem do not specify on which side of the middle of the path the second pedestrian was located when the pedestrian who left $A$ walked $\frac{1}{3}$ of the path, and on which side of the second pedestrian the first pedestrian was located when the pedestrian who left $B$ walked half the way, distances must be expressed using the modulus:

$$\begin{cases} \left| \frac{x}{v_1} \cdot v_2 - \frac{x}{2} \right| = 3, \\ \left| \frac{x}{v_2} \cdot v_1' - \frac{x}{2} \right| = 2. \end{cases}$$

From here

$$3 \left| \frac{1}{v_2} - 1 \right| = 4 \left| \frac{v_2}{v_1} - \frac{1}{2} \right|.$$

Next we need to solve the equation

$$3 \left| \frac{1}{y} - 1 \right| = \left| \frac{y}{3} - \frac{1}{2} \right|,$$

distanced three cases: $0 < y \leq 1$, $1 < y \leq \frac{3}{2}$, $y > \frac{3}{2}$.

Answer. $\{12$ km, $15 + 3\sqrt{17'}$ km, $6$ km\}.

2. Let $SABC$ be a given paramid ($S$ is a vertex). Let us drop a perpendicular from $C$ to ($AS$). Its base $F$ will lie inside the segment $AS$, if $\widehat{ASC} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ (in this case, and $\widehat{BFC} < \frac{\pi}{2}$), at the point $S$, if $\widehat{ASC} = \frac{\pi}{2}$, and on the continuation of the segment $AS$ beyond the point $S$, if $\widehat{ASC} > \frac{\pi}{2}$ (then $\widehat{BFC} > \frac{\pi}{2}$). Let's denote $\widehat{BFC}$ by $\alpha$.

The required edge length is

$$\frac{r^3 \sqrt{6}}{3 \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} - 1}$$

strike through the given angle between the planes $\varphi$. If $\alpha \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$, then $\varphi = \alpha$; if $\alpha > \frac{\pi}{2}$, then $\varphi = \pi - \alpha$ (“Geometry 9”, §38).

Answer. If $\alpha \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$, $l =$

$$= \frac{r^3 \sqrt{6}}{3 \text{tg}^2 \frac{\alpha}{2} - 1};$$

if $\alpha > \frac{\pi}{2}$, $l =$

$$= \frac{r^3 \sqrt{6}}{3 \text{ctg}^2 \frac{\varphi}{2} - 1}.$$

3. $A = 7$, $B = -6$, $C = 3$. $\{ -\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{3} \}$.

Physics

3. $q = \frac{m g l^2}{U a (1 - 1/\sqrt{5})} \approx 5,4 \cdot 10^{-7}$ Cl
4. $T = \frac{2\pi}{v} \left( \frac{a}{Da-1} - l \right) \sin 2\alpha \approx 20$ p.
