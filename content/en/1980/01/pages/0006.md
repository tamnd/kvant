I want to reveal the basic laws of the photo effect.

See figure for a diagram of his experiments. The electronic target, consisting of batteries B and a galvanometer G, also includes two metal plates $K_1$ and $K_2$, one of which is made in the form of a grid. Electric arc D is a source of ultraviolet rays. While carrying metal plates, Stoletov observed the passage of current in an “open” electrical circuit. We now know that the current carriers are electrons, which eject ultraviolet radiation quanta from the metal cathode $K_2$ and rush to the anode $K_1$.

As a result of his experiments, Stoletov established the nature of the dependence of the strength of the photocurrent flowing through the circuit on the voltage applied to the plates. It is true that with increasing voltage, the photocurrent first increases to a certain value (it is called the saturation current), after which it remains practically constant. This means that all electrons that left the cathode $K_2$ under the influence of light reach the anode $K_1$ and often flow through the electrical circuit. Stoletov also showed that the strength of the saturation photocurrent is proportional to the light flux incident on the cathode, or, in modern terms, to the number of light quanta incident on the cathode. The laws of the photoelectric effect, discovered by Stoletov, played an important role in substantiating the quantum theory of radiation and found many valuable practical applications.

At the beginning of 1874, Umov published the work “Equations of Energy Motion in Bodies.” It contains the idea of ​​the flow of different types of energy in different media. Umov showed that many physical processes (for example, the propagation of light waves or vibrations in a solid) are associated with a continuous flow of energy. He was the first to introduce the concept of energy density, the speed of its propagation, the direction of energy flow, giving them strict physical definitions. Based on the law of conservation of energy, Umov obtained the general law of energy propagation. The physical properties of this law are as follows: the change in energy in a given volume of some medium per unit time is equal to the energy flow through the surface limiting this volume. Today these ideas of the Minds have become generally accepted. But at that time, Umov encountered deep misunderstanding on the part of senior physicists. Only ten years later, in 1884, the English physicist Poyntig published a widely known article on the transfer of electromagnetic energy, the main conclusions of which coincided with the conclusions of the more general theory of Minds.
