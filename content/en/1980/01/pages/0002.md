The complex geometric structure depicted on the cover is made up of ten similar parts. Each of them consists of composite rhombic two-diaphragms and hexagons (see photograph). Rhombic two-diagrams (there are 15 of them in each part: 10 in the outer ring, 5 in the inner) are constructed as follows: take a dodecadron and place a rhombus on each of its edges (there are 30 of them) so that its short diagonal coincides with the edge; the sizes of the rhombuses (all rhombuses are the same) and their positions are chosen so that the rhombuses are shaped by their sides, forming a convex tridiatagon (check that this is possible!); the “equatorial 吻c” of ten rhombuses has been removed from the tridiatgranic; gluing together the two remaining “caps”, we get our two-diagram.

Hexagons are made up of rhombuses of the same dimensions and come in two types (how much?); they form a complex network of “corridors” between “rooms” - two diagonals.

This structure served as the basis for an experimental architectural project for a residential village for the Far North.
