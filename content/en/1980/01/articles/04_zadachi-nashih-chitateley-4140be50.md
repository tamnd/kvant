Our Readers' Assignment

The derivative is useful to use to prove inequalities both with one variable («Kvant», 1979, No. 10) and with several variables. Here are examples of such problems sent by our readers:

1. Prove inequality
$$\frac{tg\beta}{tg\alpha} > \frac{\beta}{\alpha}$$

for numbers $\alpha, \beta$ satisfying

$$0 < \alpha < \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}.$$

L. Kurlyandik

2. Prove inequality
$$\sin^2\alpha + \sin\beta \sin\gamma \leq \frac{25}{16},$$

in which $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the angles of the triangle.

S. Berkolaiko
