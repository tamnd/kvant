⟦folio 46⟧

«Kvant» has been published for ten years - and for ten years the column “Applicant's Workshop” has invariably appeared on its pages. Editorial mail, meetings with students and teachers, and reader questionnaires sent to the editor show that this section is of great interest to many young readers of the magazine.

And this is understandable: future applicants (especially those who live in rural areas, far from scientific and pedagogical centers) want to know what tasks are offered to applicants, what the admission exam is, and how best to prepare for it. The editors «Kvant» strive to answer such questions as fully and comprehensively as possible.

First of all, in the “Applicant's Workshop” readers will find articles devoted to a detailed explanation of the most important and complex questions of programmers of exams in mathematics and physics, analysis of typical problems, mainly taken directly from variants of university entrance exams, and analysis of characteristic, especially common mistakes of applicants. In addition, the pages of this section will regularly contain texts of problems for written exams and samples of problems for oral exams that were offered last year in various calculated completions of our country.

Having become acquainted with the publications of the “Applicant's Workshop”, readers can easily make sure that in order to successfully pass the entrance exams, no additional knowledge is required in comparison with school courses in mathematics and physics. However, one should not think that it would be enough to simply read school textbooks again—it is necessary to carefully analyze and deeply assimilate the theoretical material, to gain creative and lasting skills in solving problems. This can only be achieved through real, hard work, systematically, systematically during the entire time remaining before the exams.

The stats of the “Applicant’s Practicum” cannot (and they don’t even put one) to replace school textbooks. They are written in the expectation that the reader already has the conditions and tasks to solve independently. In addition to the materials that will appear in the “Applicant's Workshop” in future issues of the magazine, when preparing for entrance exams it would be useful to get acquainted with some articles «Kvant» published in past years. A topical list of articles is included below; The list contains the year, journal number and page where the article begins. (It should be borne in mind that in articles published before 1977, sometimes there are terms and designations that differ from those accepted in the school now; we hope that the attentive reader can easily overcome this difficulty.)
