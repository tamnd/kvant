⟦folio 51⟧

Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Below are published materials from the 1979 entrance exams.

Mathematics

Written exam

Option

1. From points A and B, I stitched two pedestrians at the same time to each other. When the pedestrian starting from A had covered 1/3 of the path, the second pedestrian was 3 km from the middle of the path. Find the distance between A and B if it is known that when the pedestrian starting from B had walked halfway, the first pedestrian was 2 km away from him. Pedestrian speeds are plotted.

2. In a regular triangular primid, the length of the radius of the circle inscribed in the base is equal to r, and the values ​​between the planes of the lateral faces of the primid are equal to φ. Determine the length of the edge of a cube whose volume is √3 times the volume of the given primid.

3. Find A, B, C such that the function is of the form

$$f(x) = Ax^2 + Bx + C$$

satisfied the condition $f'(1) = 8$, $f(2) + f''(2) = 33$, $\int f(x) dx = \frac{7}{3}$.

4. The area of ​​the figure bounded by the curve $y = \sin 2x$, lines $x = \frac{\pi}{6}$, $x = a$ and the x-axis is 0.5. Determine a.

Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. The upper end of a homogeneous rod rotates freely around the zontal axis O (Fig. 1). The lower end of the rod rests on a block lying on a smooth table. The mass of the rod is $m = 1,50$ kg, the angle between the rod and the vertical is $a = 30^\circ$. If the block is pulled to the right with a force of $\vec{F}(|\vec{F}| \ge 2,45$ N), it moves from its place. With what force does the rod press on the block during its movement?

2. Gaseous hydrogen weighing $m = 1$ kg at an initial temperature $T_1 = 300$ K is cooled isochorically so that its pressure drops η=3 times. The gas is then expanded at constant pressure. Find the work done by the gas if in the final state its temperature turns out to be equal to the initial one.

3. A small ball with a mass of $m = 20$ mg, loaded with electricity, is suspended on a thin insulating thread $l = 60$ cm long. At a distance $l$ from the suspended ball in the same horizontal plane there is a metal sphere with a radius $a = 3$ cm (Fig. 2). If you charge the sphere by applying a voltage of $U = 8$ kV relative to the Earth, the thread with the ball deviates from the vertical at a maximum angle of 90°. Find the charge q on the ball.

4. In the setup shown in figure 3, the precise light source S is located on the main optical axis of a thin lens at a distance of $|SO| = a = 0,6$ m from it. Lens optical power $D = 2$ ditr. Behind the lens there is a flat mirror, the normal $\vec{n}$ to which an angle $a = 15^\circ$ is created with the main optical axis of the lens. The distance from the lens to the mirror is $|ON| = l = 1$ m. The mirror rotates at a constant angular speed around the main optical axis of the lens, while the source of motion is distributed uniformly around the circumference at a speed of $v = 0,31$ m/s. Find the rotation period of the mirror.
