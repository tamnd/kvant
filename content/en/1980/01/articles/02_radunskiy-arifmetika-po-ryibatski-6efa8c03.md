TO OUR READERS

In each issue of our magazine, in the section “Problem book «Kvant»” problems in physics and mathematics are published. Every day the editors receive several thousand letters from readers with solutions to these problems. Among the authors of these letters, the editors select schoolchildren who solved the most problems and wrote the most original solutions. These schoolchildren are awarded a special prize established by the editor of the magazine «Kvant», and receive the right to participate in the Republican Optimization in Mathematics and Physics. Coming up with an interesting new problem is usually more difficult than solving it. More difficult, but more interesting. Try to come up with problems and send them to us.

Schoolchildren who are the authors of the best problems will be awarded special bonuses «Kvant», and the problems themselves will be published in the journal.

Problems must be sent along with solutions. Each task should be presented on a separate sheet.

If the task is taken, then the source from which it was taken must be indicated.

For problems not accepted for publication, correspondence with the authors is not conducted and the text of the problems is not returned to the authors.

We are waiting for your letters.

TO OUR AUTHORS

If you decide to send us an article, we ask you:
1) Retype it on a machine in two copies, double spaced, and write the form by hand (we only consider handwritten text if its author is a schoolchild).
2) Also draw in two copies (each copy on a separate sheet).
3) The article should not contain more than 12 typewritten pages.
4) Do not forget to tell us your full last name, first name and patronymic, home address and telephone number.
