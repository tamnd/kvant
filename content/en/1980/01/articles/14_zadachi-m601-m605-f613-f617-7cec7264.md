Exercise

M601—M605; F613—F617

M601. Prove that in any triangle $ABC$ the midpoint of side $BC$ lies on the segment connecting the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $ABC$ with the point of the circle circumscribed about this triangle, diametrically opposite to the vertex $A$, and bisects this segment.

G. Groshev, 10th grade student

M602. In Pascal's triangle, the beginning of which is depicted at figure 1 (each “inner” number is equal to the sum of the two sides above it), in the next line there are three numbers in a row, forming an arithmetic progression.
a) Which next line contains the same event?
b) Prove that it will occur in an infinite number of lines, and indicate all the numbers of such lines.

A. Avramov

Fig. 1. In the $n$th line, the $k$th number in order is equal to
$$C_n^k = \frac{n \cdot (n-1) \cdot \dots \cdot (n-k+1)}{1 \cdot 2 \cdot \dots \cdot k}.$$

Fig. 2.

M603*. Solve the system of equations
$$x + \frac{3x - y}{x^2 + y^2} = 3, \quad y - \frac{x + 3y}{x^2 + y^2} = 0.$$
L. Kuptsov

M604. a) Andrey, Victor, Sergey, swimming underwater, simultaneously emerged at points $A_0, B_0, C_0$ and immediately dived again, and Andrey decided to swim a third of the way to Victor in a minute, Victor - a third of the way to Sergey, Sergey - a third of the way to Andrey. A minute later they emerged again (points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ - see Fig. 2) and decided to repeat this maneuver - already in half a minute; then - in a quarter of a minute, etc.
Where and when will they meet?
b) Inside a sphere of radius 1 km there are a million points numbered from 1 to a million. Every second, simultaneously, each point moves to the next one by number at $1/3$ distance to this point; the last point moves towards the first in the same way. Prove that after some time all the points will converge inside a certain sphere of radius 1 mm.

F. Shleifer

M605. On the plane there are marks $2n+1$ of different points. Let us number them with the numbers 1,2,...,2n+1 and the distance of the next transformation of the $R$ plane: first, symmetry is made relative to the first point, then - relative to the second, etc. - up to the $(2n+1)$th point.
a) Prove that this transformation $R$ has a single “fixed point” (a point that maps into itself).

⟦folio 32⟧

Let's consider all possible ways to measure our $2n + 1$ points (in numbers 1,2,...,$2n + 1$). Each such numbering corresponds to its own transformation of the plane $R$ and its own fixed point. Let $F$ be the set of fixed points of all these transformations.

6) Specify the set $F$ for $n = 1$.
c) What is the maximum and what is the minimum number of points that the set $F$ can contain for each $n = 2,3,...$?

A. Talalay

F613. A box of mass $M$ stands on a horizontal table. In the external compression box $k$ a mass load $m$ (Fig. 1) is suspended. At what amplitude of oscillations of the load does the box begin to bounce on the table?

L. Bakanina

F614. A closed vessel contained nitrogen gas at room temperature $t_0 = 20^\circ\text{C}$ and pressure $p_0 = 10^5$ Pa. Some liquid nitrogen with a temperature of $t_1 = -196^\circ\text{C}$ (the boiling point of nitrogen at normal atmospheric pressure) was injected into the vessel. The liquid nitrogen quickly evaporated, after which the temperature in the vessel turned out to be $t_2 = -140^\circ\text{C}$. After the vessel warmed up to room temperature, a pressure of $p = 1,5 \cdot 10^5$ Pa was established in it. Determine the molar heat of evaporation of liquid nitrogen. The heat capacity of nitrogen gas at constant volume is $C_V = 20,8$ J/(mol K).

V. Belonchkin

F615. In the case of a non-self-sustaining gas size, the dependence of the point $I$ through the gas discharge tube on the voltage $U$ between the electrodes of the tube has the form shown in figure 2. A tube with a series-connected ballast resistance $R = 3 \cdot 10^8$ Ohm is connected to a source with a constant EMF $8 = 6$ kV. Find what current will be established through the tube and what the voltage on the tube will be. The internal resistance of the source is taken into account.

V. Mozhaev

F616*. The length of the filament of an electric light bulb is $l$, and the diameter is $d$. What are the length $l_1$ and diameter $d_1$ of another light bulb designed for the same mains voltage, but with twice the luminous flux, if the efficiency of both light bulbs is the same?

F617. Newspaper text, photographed twice with a Zenit camera with a lens having a focal length of 50 mm:
1) from the shortest distance allowed for this lens $a = 0,5$ m;
2) after attaching the lens to the camera through an extension pole with a height of $h = 25$ mm (also from the minimum possible distance).

Find the ratio of the gases obtained on photographic film in these two cases.

V. Deryabkin
