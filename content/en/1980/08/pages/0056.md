The diversity of the research carried out at FIAN was revealed to the children from the speech of the institute’s staff. Lenin Prize laureate Professor O. N. Krokhin spoke about the use of lasers in medicine and the challenges of engineering technology in connection with the need for widespread introduction of laser surgery. Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences I. M. Dremin told the children about the work of the theoretical department of the institute, about the problems of theoretical physics being solved there.

How to make a light guide at home? Is it possible to obtain electrical energy using a unified theory of weak electromagnetic interactions? What are the benefits of laser surgery? What laser powers are available today? These and other various and sometimes unexpected questions were asked to the scientists.

We taught the guys about the physics problems that are being solved today that are important for our country, they indicate the line where what is known today ends and what should become known tomorrow begins. From first-hand experience, the guys got a clear idea that scientific success is just an overcoming moment in a difficult, at times difficult, painful, continuous chain of discoveries of physical truth.

The first day of the Festival ended with an experiment in the FIAN laboratories. In some of them, the children had to work and experience their first touch of a real physical experiment.

The next day, the guys had a meeting with the editorial staff of the magazine «Kvant», this very popular magazine among the guys. During the meeting, a very useful exchange of views took place about what life was like «Kvant». With great interest, the participants of the Festival listened to the story of the editor-in-chief of the journal, Academician I.K. Kikon, about the problems and tasks being solved «Kvant». Deputy editor-in-chief V. A. Leshkovtsev drew the attention of future physicists to those mysteries of nature that can be unexpectedly discovered very close by, one might say, under your feet.

The children learned about the problems of optoelectronics and thermonuclear energy on Wednesday from Lenin and State Prize laureate Professor Yu. M. Popov. Academician of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences V. A. Fabrikant told the children about the great role of getting acquainted with the works of schoolchildren in the formation of a future scientist.

On Thursday, the guys have been waiting for the long-awaited issue of the magazine “Nature”. Deputy editor-in-chief of the magazine V. A. Goncharov introduced the children to some of the authors of this magazine. The meeting took place in a very warm and relaxed atmosphere. The world of other sciences suddenly opened up before the children: geography, biology...

The last day of the Young Physicists Festival coincided with the final stage of the Junior Physicists Tour. The top three teams hid steps in financial physics. Knowledge of physics, keen skills, and resourcefulness were demonstrated financially; it was necessary to solve rather complex experimental problems within a tight time frame using improvised instruments and devices.

However, not only science was the built companion for the children. What awaited them was Ogonyok with a disco, a concentrate of chamber music from the works of Beethoven, Mozart, Bartok, and a meeting with physicist Sergei Nikitny using homemade songs.

The three-day conference, at which 17 reports were presented, clearly demonstrated the versatility of the students’ interests, their ability and desire for server-based scientific work. The success of the conference was largely facilitated by a group of leading scientists from the Lebedev Physical Institute, headed by the laureate of the State Prize of Ukraine, Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences G. A. Askarn.

Application”, “Some observed higher topics”, “Study of the physical effects that arose in an ordinary bath filled with water”, “Observation of the flight of splashes”, “Modeling the interaction of defects in crystals”, “Study of the absorption of laser radiation by pairs of iodine molecules”, “Paradox of the magnetic screen”, “Non-teaching effectiveness and paradoxes of the special theory of relativity”, “Effects of the “all” droplet - a simple list of topics independently carried out research speaks for itself. But I would like to talk about some reports in more detail.

A number of works are devoted to the physics of crystals. Thus, Y. Billyalov and M. Shaykhev (school No. 18) developed and demonstrated a demonstration experiment that makes it possible, using the bubble model, to understand and study the laws of behavior of defects (dislocations) in crystals. A. Demkov, M. Lyudomirsky, M. Makarchuk and A. Panfilov (school No. 179) analyzed the experience of merit, and quite successfully, on film and showed this film to their comrades at the conference. A. Kushnerov (school No. 2) developed his art of expressing crystals with double, four and sixth axis symmetries. At the same time, in his report he gave a detailed overview of the formed crystals and their applications.

A group of students from school No. 18 set themselves an interesting physical task - to determine such a function of a fundamental quantity as the specific electron mystery (e/m). They approached the solution of this problem in two ways - the focusing method and the magneton method. The first series of experiments was carried out by V. Ivanov, and the second was carried out by S. Medvedev, A. Pavlov and A. Povalyaev. In this case, practically identical results were obtained, close to the real value. It’s gratifying that the guys approached the experiments very competently, analyzed possible errors, and saw ways to improve the accuracy of the experiment.
