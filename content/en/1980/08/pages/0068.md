In this mysterious figure by the Dutch artist M. Escher, the left and right hands, helping each other, escaped from the flat world of a sheet of paper, which was too fluid for them, into freedom, into the third dimension. Figure causes bewilderment: the usual relationships between figure and the drawing, between the plane and space, between left and right are broken. It illustrates, in part, the complex physical and mathematical concept of orientation, touched upon in one of the articles in this issue (p. 2). Indeed, try to server-side answer a naive, at first glance, question - how does the right hand differ from the left? Now you will probably agree - further, in the simplest phenomena of nature, a lot of mysterious things are often hidden.
