It seems unlikely that during the Great Posture’s stay in Kazan, where he collected materials about Pugachev’s uprising, he did not meet with the Great Geometer. This could have happened in the library, or in the hospitable house of the Lobachevskys (remember: the wife of N. I. Lobachevsky is the half-sister of I. E. Velikopolsky, an old friend of Pushkin), or, finally, in the apartment of the professional Fuchs, where Pushkin was a guest and Lobachevsky visited. About his participation in literary evenings with his university colleagues can be read in a letter sent from Kazan on January 21, 1832 to I. E. Velikopolsky; it says: “We were at a literary evening at the Fuchs’... N.I. Lobachevsky read poetry from the work of Mme Fuchs and several times almost wanted to write... Baratynsky sat with his eyes downcast all the time”*)

Thus, the meeting of the two geniuses, one way or another, must have taken place in September 1833. The response to Pushkin’s visit was, apparently, the appearance in print of Lobachevsky’s poem “The Volga Flood at Kazan,” the full text of which is given below. How good does its final chord sound:

Here is an image of the peaceful power of Russia!
Its spill is not scary.
Greatness curbs strength,
Here, everywhere, submissive to him.

Volga flood near Kazan

Queen of rivers in solemn flow
To the distant waters of the vast Caspian Sea,
You're trying to go to Kazan on a date
With it ancient materials to the Tatar cities!
You hug her from all sides, like a friend,
And with a trembling stream you greet the ray
And quietly you lay down the gifts from the blue ramen
On the shores of Bulak...

What a noisy feast!... But what is secret and important
What is told to her by the verb of your waves?
Is it your own joy, is it your brave wars?
And do you fill the days of ancient glory?
Oh! before and Kazan arrogantly walked
When the water is full, the spring is free
The stormy Iratya flooded widely
Submissive fields of the surrounding countries.
Her time has passed: the warrior descendants,
Like a dirty or dried up river,
Like insignificant fragments of a glorious place,
Now the silence is only faintly loud.

So everything disappears!... But the Angel of Desolation
Is it really possible that he will come to blows on you too?
Is it possible that your waves will also cease to desire -
And the Volga will be overgrown with marsh grass?
And where your winged ships glided,
Will your tired leg get stuck?
Where did they bring fun with them -
The orphaned shores will fall silent!...
No!... your existence until the eternity of the product
Like a clear memory of great deeds.
The great is preserved immortally for centuries
And nothingness is not his lot.

Are you striking the fields desolate?
Do you live up to the hopes of the villagers?
No! your waters are always fragrant, the honor of grateful countries,
Nourished by you, only enriched!
You and the earth are harmless beauty,
And bright in your undisturbed streams,
As if in a clear conscience, the heavens shine.

Here is an image of the peaceful power of Russia!
Its spill is not scary.
Greatness curbs strength,
Here, everywhere, submissive to him.
Does he boldly strive for the proud Balkans,
Or will it lead from the Ararat Mountains to Iran?
One of the isolated ice chains is breaking,—
And the furious tyrant is pacified.
For when the treacherous ones are destroyed,
Russia will not come to an end;
The power will not soon be crushed
A crown immortalized by kindness.
