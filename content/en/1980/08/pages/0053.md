Schoolboy about the astronomical picture of the world

Over the years of school life, students study many different academic subjects, each of which equips them with the basics of a particular science. But, in addition, they all solve a common and very important task: they form a scientific worldview. Its component is scientific attenism, which is a holistic, consistent system of dialectical-materialistic ideas. The basis of worldview and attenism is a natural picture of the world, consisting of a set of philosophical generalized data from physics, astronomy, chemistry and other natural sciences.

Recently (at the end of 1979) the publishing house "Prosveshcheniye" published the book "Atenism and the Scientific Picture of the World." Its author, the famous popularizer of astronomical science V.N. Komarov, set himself the task of proving the falsity of various religious ideas. This proof is not formed in one phrase, it is not contained in one logical idea or some fact (as is generally the case in the exact sciences). It, like a focal point, contains the entire sum of our knowledge, the entire body of scientific data about the surrounding system and transformations of the surrounding world, all the achievements of advanced human thought, all the practical activities of mankind.

Without trying to “announce the undeclared,” the author of the book draws the reader’s attention only to the most important facts in the worldview of relations from various fields of science about the Universe. For example, the philosophical position that the process of cognition is a dialectical process of movement towards absolute truth through intermediate relative truths, is clearly emphasized by the changing astronomical picture of the world. From the naive ancient picture of the world to the geocentric system of Aristotle - Ptolemy, from geocentrism to geolucentrism (the great Copernican revolution) and, finally, from it to the modern picture of a non-stationary, expanding and even “exploding” Universe - this is the process of cognition of the Universe.

Astronomy has proven that the Universe evolves and changes over time. This means that what has passed in the Universe is not the same as the present, and the present is not the same as the future. However, man has managed to penetrate the secrets of past eras and stands on the path of scientific prediction of the future. The main “key” to understanding the past of cosmic objects, as the author of the book under review rightly emphasizes, is primarily in the study of their current states. V.N. Komarov compares the work of scientists dealing with the production and development of space objects with the work of detectors who restore the picture of production using the inevitable (sometimes barely noticeable) traces left at the production site.

Recently, astronomers have acquired a powerful “ally” - a “fossil” (relict) study, first discovered about fifteen years ago. This study carries unrepresented information about the era, which separated “only” a few fading years from the beginning of the expansion of the Metagalaxy. And this is not the limit. If relic neutrinos are discovered, we will be very close to the beginning of the expansion. It turns out that only (teler without quotes) 0.3 seconds separate the moment of birth of the still elusive relic neutrinos from the beginning of expansion.

I would like to note one more feature of V.N. Komarov’s book - it introduces the reader to the “inevitability of a strange world”, in which things are becoming less and less interesting. Some astrophysical and cosmological phenomena are practically impossible to imagine, but they can be understood, given a physical interpretation, and the corresponding mathematical apparatus found. It is known, for example, that we live in curved, non-Euclidean space. True, the curvature of space is noticeably manifested only on a cosmic scale, in the world of gigantic cosmic masses of materials.

The space of the Universe has no boundaries, but it can be finite or infinite. Which one depends on the curvature of space, and the curvature is determined by the average density of the materials of the Universe. As scientists believe, if the average density is less than a certain specific value, the space is open and infinite, but if it is more, the space is closed and finite. It is very difficult to figure out which of these two possibilities is realized. The totality of available data seems to indicate that we live in an infinite, open Universe. However, even if this is proven, one should not calm down - the space of the Universe is finite and closed in another.

E. Levitan
