M636. The set $A$ consists of integers, its smallest element is 1 and its largest element is 100. Each element of $A$ except 1 is equal to the sum of two (possibly equal) numbers belonging to $A$. Among all the sets $A$ that satisfy these conditions, indicate the set with the minimum number of elements. (10)

Yu. Nesterenko

M637. Given a regular triangle $ABC$. Some lines parallel to line $AC$ intersect lines $AB$ and $BC$ at points $M$ and $P$ respectively. Point $D$ is the center of triangle $PMB$, point $E$ is the middle of segment $AP$. Determine the angles of the triangle $DEC$. (9)

A. Kuptsov

M638. Some cells of an infinite sheet of checkered paper are colored red, the rest are blue, so that each rectangle of 6 cells of size $2 \times 3$ contains exactly two red cells. How many red cells can a rectangle of 99 cells of size $9 \times 11$ contain? (8)

N. Kartashov

M639. In the tetrahedron there are $ABCD$ ($AC \perp BC$) and $(AD) \perp (BD)$. Prove that the cosine of the angle between lines $AC$ and $BD$ is less than $|CD|/|AB|$. (10)

Yu. Nesterenko

F648. The hinge structure consists of three rhombuses, the sides of which are in the ratio 3:2:1 (Fig. 1). The vertex $A_3$ moves in the horizontal direction at a speed $v_0$. Determine the velocities of vertices $A_1$, $A_2$, $B_1$ at the moment when all the angles of the structure are right. (8)

S. Krotov

F649. The bucket contains a mixture of water and ice. The mass of the mixture is 10 kg. The bucket was brought into the room and they immediately began to change the temperature of the mixture. The resulting graph of the dependence $t$ (τ) is depicted in figure 2. The specific temperature of water $c_B = 4200$ J/(kg K) and the heat of fusion of ice $\lambda = 3,4 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg are known. Determine how much ice was in the bucket when it was brought into the room. The heat capacity of the bucket is a problem. (8)

A. Buzdin

F650. To obtain a voltage, the value of which depends little on temperature, the assembled circuit according to figure 3. The current-voltage characteristics of dida D at three different ambient temperatures $t_1 = 125^\circ\text{C}$, $t_2 = 25^\circ\text{C}$, $t_3 = -60^\circ\text{C}$ are shown in figure 4. The source voltage $U = 6$ V at a temperature $25^\circ\text{C}$ and with increasing temperature increases by $25 \cdot 10^3$ V/deg. Find the voltage between clamps $A$ and $B$ at $t = 25^\circ\text{C}$ and the dependence of this voltage on temperature. (9).

A. Zilberman

F651. A science fiction story describes an emergency situation in which an actron of mass $M = 100$ kg ends up at a distance of $l = 100$ m from a ship with a glass of frozen water. Providing sublimation (evaporation) of ice, actrons return to the ship. Is this method of return realistic? Estimate how long it takes actrons to return to the ship. Consider that ice sublimation occurs at a constant temperature $T = 272$ K. The saturation vapor pressure at this temperature is $p_H = 550$ Pa. The universal gas constant is $R = 8,3 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kmol·K). Set the dimensions of the glass and the mass of ice yourself. (9)

A. Stasenko
