Chess competition «Kvant»

Six Oscars

This year, A. Karpov won the Oscar prize for the sixth time, which is awarded to the best chess player of the year based on the results of a survey of chess journalists from different countries. This “anniversary” was reflected in the wreath of tasks dedicated to the world champion by the international master of chess composition E. Poghosyants. Before you are six diagrams showing 12 problems (all of them competitive). A pair of problems on each diagram is represented by two letters, on the left - K, and on the right - A (Anatoly Karpov). Nine figures participate in all artistic performances. This is also a certain symbol - A. Karpov became a first-class player at the age of 9, a grandmaster at 19, and received his sixth Oscar for his success in Turin in 1979.

We draw your attention to the fact that there are many averages of these problems that are so-called “reggograde”, that is, they require finding out whose first move is in them (although mat is given by White everywhere). One important detail that you must keep in mind when solving problems: if Black's last move in the initial position of the problem is impossible (of course, this must be proven every time), then it means that it is now his turn to move. In such a problem, Black moves first (their move is zero, he does not initiate the solution), and after that White announces checkmate to the Black king in a given number of moves.

Solutions to these problems should be sent in one copy in a separate envelope to the address: 113035, Moscow, M-35, B. Ordynka 21/16, journal editorial office «Kvant» - no later than September 30, 1980. The envelope must be marked “Chess competition, No. 8-80.”
