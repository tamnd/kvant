North-Western correspondence mathematical school at Leningrad University. A. A. Zhdanov (SZMSH) and some films of the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosov (VZMSH) announce additional admission of 8th grade students to the 1st year of individual education, and admission of school mathematics clubs of 8th and 9th grades to the “Collective Student” groups.
The main goal of correspondence mathematics schools is to help students study mathematics at a high school level, teach them to think independently and overcome difficulties in reading books on mathematics.
ZMS students regularly receive assignments in mathematics containing an explanation of theoretical material, the study of which helps to solve the proposed problems.
Those who express a desire to study at a correspondence mathematics school must, before September 15, 1980, send their application to the director of this school. In the application you must indicate your last name, first name, class, school number and your full postal address.
In the envelope, include two pieces of paper measuring 14 cm x 6 cm with your postal address written on it.
All those who submitted applications will be given the first task. Enrollment will be random based on the results of its implementation.
In “Collective Student” groups, several students can study under the guidance of their mathematics teacher.
Enrollment is made before October 1, 1980 on the basis of an application from the group leader indicating the class and surname of the students, certified by the school seal and the signature of the director.
SZMSH accepts students from schools in Belarus (except Vitebsk and Gomel regions), Latvian, Lithuanian and Estonian SSR, Karelian ASSR, Arkhangelsk, Kaliningrad, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions.
VZMSH accepts students from schools in the Bashkir, Dagestan, Mordovian, Udmurt and Chuvask ASSR, Krasnoyarsk Territory, Amur, Volgodsk, Gomel, Dzhezkazan, Donetsk, Kokchetav, Kostroma, Kuibyshev, Oryol, Penza, Saratov, Smolensk, Turgai, Ulyanovsk, Ural, Tselinograd, Chernovician and Yaroslavl regions.
SZMSH address: 228197, Leningrad, st. Savushkina 61, ZMSH, reception.
VZMSH address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, MSU, VZMSH, reception.

The Evening Physics School (VPS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces enrollment of students in grades 8-10 for the 1980/81 academic year.
The main task of the VFS is to help high school students study physics more deeply within the scope of the school curriculum. In addition, HFS students will be able to get acquainted with the main directions of modern physics and visit scientific laboratories of the Faculty of Physics. Classes are conducted in the form of lectures and seminars.
Classes at the Higher School of Physics are conducted based on the results of the qualification. To participate in it, you must submit an application and submit two photo cards measuring 3x4 cm.
Applications will be accepted from September 5 to 27 at the Komsomol Committee of Physics of Moscow State University daily (except Sundays) from 16.00 to 18.00.
VFS address: 117234, Moscow, Lenin Hills, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, VFS.
Phone for inquiries: 139-26-56.

A few questions about astronomy
(see «Kvant» No. 5, p. 42)

1. At any point on the equator, the length of the day is always equal to the length of the night.
2. There is a change of seasons at the equator. Although the Sun is always above the horizon for exactly 12 hours, its maximum height above the horizon varies during that day.
3. At the equator, twice a year - on the days of the spring (March 21) and autumn (September 23) equinoxes - the Sun is at its zenith at noon. Thus, the hottest days at the equator occur in spring and autumn.
While in the middle latitudes there is one seasonal cycle per year, at the equator there are two cycles.
4. No, not intermediate. At the equator, on the day of the summer solstice (as well as on the day of the winter solstice), the height of the culmination of the Sun is lower compared to other days of the year.
