F665. In a thermally conducting state, there are two liquids with removed heat capacities $c_1$ and $c_2$, separated by a non-teleconducting partition. Liquid temperatures vary. The partition is removed, and after thermal equilibrium is established, the difference between the initial temperature of one of the liquids and the temperature established in the vessel turns out to be half the difference in the initial temperatures of the liquid. Find the ratio of masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ of the first and second liquid.
