To accurately determine the refractive index, it is necessary to make several changes at one angle of incidence or construct a graph $\sin \beta = f(\sin \alpha)$ using experimental data. According to theory, this dependence should be linear: $\sin \beta = \frac{\sin \alpha}{n}$. The use of the graphical method is equivalent to averaging. Using a microcalculator allows you to do without an inclinometer and can be done in a short time.

Considering that the participants of the Olympics would be interested in reproducing experimental problems in their school after returning home, the organizers gave them a set of equipment (signal converter, dynamic loudspeaker, oscillating circuit and transparent rectangular plate).
