⟦folio 52⟧

It is certain that our youth will always be a worthy successor to the deeds of the first Komsomol members and will strive to acquire knowledge in order to benefit their Motherland.

The rector of the Latvian State University. Petra Stuchki Professor V. O. Miller.

On April 18, the first round of the final stage of the Olympics took place - the theoretical one. Eight-class students were given 4 problems (4 hours were given to solve them), and ninth-class and ten-class students were given 5 problems (5 hours were given to solve them). Here are the conditions for these problems (almost all the problems are included in the Problem Book «Kvant» - see No. 7 and 8 for this year):

**Theoretical tour**

**8th grade**

1. The hinge structure consists of three rhombuses, the sides of which are in the ratio 3:2:1 (Fig. 1). Vertex $A_3$ moves in the horizontal direction at speed $\vec{v}_0$. Determine the velocities of vertices $A_1$, $A_2$ and $B_1$ at the moment when all the angles of the structure are right.

2. A small droplet leaves a cloud in calm weather at high altitude. At the moment when the acceleration of the drop became equal to 5 m/s², its speed was 7.5 m/s. Near the ground, a drop falls at a constant speed. When a drop hits the side window of a moving automatic, it leaves a mark on it at an angle of 45° to the vertical. Will a traffic police inspector fine a driver for speeding if the speed limit is 60 km/h? Consider the force of air resistance to be directly proportional to the speed of the drop.

3. There is a mixture of water and ice in the bucket. The mass of the mixture is 10 kg. The bucket was brought into the room and they immediately began to change the temperature of the mixture. The resulting graph of the dependence $T(t)$ is depicted on figure 2. The specific temperature of water $c = 4200$ J/(kg K) and the specific temperature of ice $\lambda = 3,4 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg are known. Determine how much ice is in the bucket when it is brought into the room. Neglect the heat capacity of the bucket.

4. An electrical circuit consisting of resistors $R_1$, $R_2$ and $R_3$ is connected to two DC voltage sources $U_1$ and $U_2$ as shown in figure 3. Under what conditions will the current through resistor $R_1$ be zero?
