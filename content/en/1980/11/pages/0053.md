On the other hand, since $x_0, y_0 \leqslant N$,

$$[x_0^{3/2}] + [y_0^{3/2}] \leqslant 2N^{3/2}.$$

Thus, for any natural number $N$ the inequality must be satisfied

$$\frac{N^2}{M} \leqslant 2N^{3/2},$$

that is, $N \leqslant (2M)^2$, which is obviously not true. The required has been proven.

Note that this proof works for any equation of the form $[x^a] + [y^a] = a$, where $0 < \alpha < 2$. For the value $\alpha = 3/2$, several participants in the Olympiad for a constructive solution to this problem, pointing out for an infinite number of values ​​$a$ a series of 1980 solutions to this equation.

Physics Olympiad

The XIV All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren, held this year, was dedicated to the 110th anniversary of the birth of V.I. Lenin.

From April 17 to 22, the final stage of the Physics Olympiad took place in Rita. 147 schoolchildren took part in it: 41 eighth-graders, 49 ninth-graders and 57 tenth-graders. All of them are winners of this year’s Resource Olympiad or recognized by the previous All-Union Olympiad.

The grand opening of the final stage of the Olympids took place on April 17 in the Palace of Pioneers. The Minister of Education of the Latvian SSR M. Ya. Karklin wished the schoolchildren more success in the upcoming Olympic wrestling and expressed the hope that active creative work, realism in achieving goals and the determination with which the children are preparing for the Olympics will help them in their future lives.
