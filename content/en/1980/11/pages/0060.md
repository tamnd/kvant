2. Study of the path of rays in a perforated rectangular plate
Task 1. Suggest a method (methods) for constructing the path of rays in a clear rectangular plate and measuring the angles of refraction β at different angles of incidence α, using only graph paper.

Task 2. Experimentally study and plot the dependence of the angle of refraction β on the angle of incidence α.

Task 3. Using the results obtained in task 2, determine: a) the refractive index of the material from which the clear-cut rectangular plate is made; b) angle of refraction β₀ at angle of incidence α₀ = 90°.

Given: a clear-cut rectangular plate measuring 20×32×100 mm (planes 32×100 mm are matte), a microcalculator, graph paper.

If you put a plate of matte surface on graph paper, you will notice that the continuation of line AB is linear CD (Fig. 7, a). Marking points B and C and the projections of the faces of the plate, we construct the path of the rays. This method can achieve high accuracy in determining the angles of incidence and refraction. A more economical method may be when the plate is placed on graph paper with the erased edge. In this case, the angles are determined by the formulas (Fig. 7, b)

α = arctan $\frac{a}{c}$ and β = arctan $\frac{b}{c}$.
