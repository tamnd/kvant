1. Determination of the liquid surface coefficient

Determine the surface area coefficient of a given liquid, using a liquid whose surface area coefficient is known, a vessel with a liquid whose surface area coefficient is known, a vessel with a liquid whose surface area coefficient needs to be determined.

Using the formula $h = \frac{2\sigma}{\rho g r}$ for the height of liquid rise in a capillary for two different liquids, we obtain an expression for determining the surface area coefficient σ of an unknown liquid (copper sulfate solution and table coli) through the surface area coefficient σₙ of a known liquid (water):

$$\sigma = \frac{h \rho}{h_n \rho_n} \sigma_n.$$
