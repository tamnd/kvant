XIV Total
Schoolchildren's Olympiad

V. Vavilov, A. Zemlyakov,
I. Klumova

Olympida
in mathematics

From April 16 to April 23, the final stage of the XIV All-Scholastic Olympid in Mathematics took place in Saratov. 158 schoolchildren took part in it: 39 eighth-graders, 60 ninth-graders and 59 tenth-graders. All these guys are winners of the Resource Olympiads, as well as schoolchildren who received I and II degree diplomas at the final stage of the XIII All-Resource Olympiad. Among the participants in the Olympidad were also the Olympiad management team - Saratov schoolchildren (3 people) and a team of vocational school students (3 people) - the winner of the Olympiad of Leningrad vocational technical schools, who performed outside the competition.

This year the Olympiad was dedicated to the 110th anniversary of the birth of V.I. Lenin. On April 16, in the assembly hall of the Saratov Treaty of Pioneers and Schoolchildren, the open final stage of the Olympiad took place in a solemn atmosphere. The Chairman of the Olympiad Organizing Committee, Deputy Representative of the Saratov General Regiment Committee I.F. Yalnycheva congratulated the participants in the final stage of the Olympiad on the beginning of their perfect year.

Young mathematicians laid flowers at the monuments to V.I. Lenin and the fighters of the socialist revolution of 1917 and took part in the All-Union Leninist communist subbotnik.

The final stage of the Olympiad took place, as usual, in two rounds, which took place on April 18 and 20.

When selecting problems, the methodological commission was guided by the following principles: 1) the tasks should cover most sections of the school curriculum covered by the students; 2) tasks must meet “different tastes”, that is, contain tasks on planometry and stereometry, tasks on algebra and the beginning of analysis, tasks of analytical type and tasks of an “olympid” nature; 3) tasks as a whole should be of moderate complexity so that each participant in the final stage solves at least one problem and at least half of the participants complete the entire task.

On each day, schoolchildren were given four problems, which took 5 hours to solve. Below we present the conditions of all tasks.

This year, the jury scored each task in advance. The maximum sum of points in each type is 30. (The number of points for the task is indicated in parentheses after the condition.) Participants in the Olympiad were not informed of these prior scores in advance.
