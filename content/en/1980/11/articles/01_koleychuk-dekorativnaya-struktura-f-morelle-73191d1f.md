The photograph shows a decorative structure created by the French designer François Morellet in 1962. The space of the sphere, about two meters in diameter, is filled with a cubic lattice of aluminum tubes. When the sphere rotates or the viewer walks around it, free transformations of spatial images associated with the symmetries of the cubic structure are observed.
