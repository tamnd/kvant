⟦folio 29⟧

Quests

1. There are four cards on the table (see figure).
It is known that each card has a letter on one side and a natural number on the other. Which cards just need to be turned over to find out whether a sentence is true or false? If on one side of the card there is a capital letter, then on the other there is a quadruple - the fourth number?

2. Four football teams: “Start”, “Kometa”, “Raketa” and “Vympel” - each played one match with each other. The judge produced a table containing the results of their meetings. The typist printed a table from the sample and the department of its fate. But it turned out that the printing machine (it was very old) printed almost nothing (see figure). However, the judge agreed that the remaining matches ended with the scores 2:0, 1:1, 2:2, 3:1, 5:3. Help fate fill out the table! (In the “Balls” column, the number of goals scored is recorded on the left, and the number of missed goals is recorded on the right. 2 points start for a win, 1 point for a draw.)

3. Given 1980 consecutive natural numbers: Is it possible to raise each number to some even power so that the sum of the resulting numbers is the square of the natural number?

4. Ten identical coins form an equilateral triangle pointing “down” (see figure). Arrange three coins so that you get an equilateral triangle directed “up”.

These tasks were offered to us
E. Abdulaev, N. Nikolskaya,
D. Remorov, A. Halamayer
