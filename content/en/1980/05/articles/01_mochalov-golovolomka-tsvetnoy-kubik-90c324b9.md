COLORED CUBE PUZZLE

From rectangular parallelepipeds 6 × 6 × 1, painted in six different colors: red, yellow, blue, green, white and black (at figure the developments of parallelepipeds are shown), lay out the cube so that each face of it represents a square in which all six colors were represented horizontally, vertically and diagonally (that is, each color would appear once).
