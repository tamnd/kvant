The Great Patriotic War interrupted the teaching activities of A.F. Zhinkin. On November 15, 1941, he went to the front and took part in the battles on Malaya Zemlya. It was there that the commander of the political department of the army, Comrade L. I. Brezhnev, presented A. F. Zhinkin with a party card.

For his military services, A.F. Zhinkin was awarded many military orders and medals.

After the end of the war, he continued his teaching activities in Vinnytsia. teacher at the Department of Mathematics Methods, producing visual aids.

After retiring in 1970, A.F. Zhinkin never ceases to be involved in teaching and social activities - he will give lectures to young people, instilling in them a love of mathematics.
