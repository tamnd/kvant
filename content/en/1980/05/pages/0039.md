F634. A rod $AB$ of length $l$ and mass $m$ rests on the wall with its lower end and is held in an inclined position (Fig. 2) with the help of a thread.
The thread is tied to the wall at point $C$, and to the rod at point $D$ such that $|AD| = 1/3|AB|$. The angles made by the thread and the rod with the wall are $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively. Find the possible value of the friction coefficient between the rod and the wall.

F635. Two identical automatics with a mass of $m = 10^3$ kg are moving up the same mountain road. The dependence of the height $h$ of the road on the distance $x$ before the start of the climb is shown in figure 3. Each automatic vehicle constantly develops a power of $P = 12$ kW, and the force of resistance to the movement of the automatic vehicle is proportional to the square of its speed: $|\vec{F}| = \alpha|\vec{v}|^2$, where $\alpha = 1,4$ kg/m. On the last horizontal section of the road, the distance between the machines is 100 m. What was the minimum distance between the machines when they were moving?
