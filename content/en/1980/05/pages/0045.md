⟦folio 43⟧

Tasks

1. A cube was glued together from paper. It is clear that it can be cut into six congruent squares. Is it possible to cut it into twelve congruent squares?
2. figure depicts the family tree of one Komi family, the ancestor of which was a certain Tikhon Fedorovich. Here are all his descendants (in the Komi language): Pedot Tikon, Ostap Tikon, Tikon Vasya, Pedot Vasya, Padei Ostap, Tikon Padei, Padei Illya, Tikon Pedot. It is known that there are no average women. Set what name corresponds to each node in the tree.
3. Find two numbers whose difference and quotient would be equal to the heel.
4. Show that the area of ​​the blue figure (see figure) is half the area of ​​the rectangle.
5. In the books of Novgorod scribes of the 15th century, the following measures of liquid bodies are mentioned: barrel, nozzle and bucket. From the same books it became known that 1 barrel and 20 buckets of kvass are equal to a third barrel of kvass, and 19 barrels, 1 nozzle and 15.5 buckets are equal to 20 barrels and 8 buckets.
Can historians use this data to determine how many baits are in a barrel?
6. Consider three-digit numbers that begin and end with the same digit, for example 343. Prove that if the sum of the first and second digits is divisible by 7, then the entire number is divisible by 7.
