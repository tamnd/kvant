About torture 3

For this experiment you will need a metal mesh or flame spreader for a gas stove, a gas burner or a regular gas stove. To avoid any confusion, be very careful when conducting the experiment.

“If you cover a gas flame with a mesh, then the combustion does not pass beyond the mesh (Fig. 3, a); in the same way, if unlit gas is passed through the mesh and then ignited, then the flame is not transmitted downward (Fig. 3, b). In both cases, the metal of the mesh distinguishes a significant part of the body from the gas, which spreads throughout the entire mesh and is gradually lost in the air; The heat remaining in the gas is not sufficient for combustion to continue.”

About torture 4

If you somehow change the density of the same body, it can be made to either float or sink in the liquid.

“So, a fish, changing the volume of its body through its swim bladder, rises, falls and stays in place.

In the Cartesian diver, a glass ball $a$, filled partly with water, partly with air and having a rusty appearance, floats on the surface of the water (Fig. 4). By pressing on the cumulus membrane $b$ and removing the air that presses on the water, we force the water to enter the ball and remove the presence of air in the ball. The ball becomes heavier, as a result of which it sinks and, with proper pressure, can stay inside the liquid. When the pressure stops, say the air in the ball will force out the water, and the ball will rise again.”

About torture 5

“If the steel bar being magnetized is small, then just touching a strong magnet is enough to magnetize it. To magnetize large steel strips, the following method proposed by Duhamel is used. The steel strip $AB$ is placed on two different strips $S$ and $N$ of two magnets (Fig. 5). Then two other magnets $P$ and $Q$ are placed obliquely in the middle so that $P$ touches the magnetized strip with the northern stripe, and $Q$ with the southern stripe, and pass them along strip $AB$ from its middle to the ends. Then the magnets are raised, placed again in the middle $AB$ and the previous action is repeated several times... It is useful to turn the magnetized bar and subject it to the collaborator by tapping it.”
