The main part of the faculty, purchased in Ashgabat, was present for classes in December 1941.

After wintering in Ashgabat, the faculty moved to Sverdlovsk in the summer of 1942, where conditions for classes were better. And in the spring of 1943, the joyful news came to the students: Moscow University was returning to Moscow! In the summer, the faculty is re-established in Moscow, but difficulties in the life of the faculty have increased. The faculty staff has increased significantly (155 students were admitted to the 1st year), but there were only a week of classes, and the reading rooms were not working. At this time, it was often possible to meet our student with a textbook and a cup of notes on the metro, running several times a day “from Sokolniki to the Park” and back, on the tram - from the House of Unions to the hostel in Ostankino, and during the spring exam session - in Alexander Park, near the Kremlin - opposite the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

Koseni 1944, the faculty auditorium was completely restored. On the initiative of students, a scientific student conference on mathematics and mechanics was held, and in the spring of 1945 the first scientific conference “Lomonosov Readings” took place. The Lomonosov Prize was awarded to Professor Kh. A. Rakhmatulin, who made a report on the topic: “Propagation of linear waves upon impact on a flexible thread.”
