Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University
during the Great Patriotic War

“...The surname columns are carved in gold on marble. Next to two are gold stars. Hero of the Soviet Union. There are seven of them on mexmate in total. Six of them are students. I don’t know if any university has a second such geronic faculty.” This is what the Izvestia newspaper wrote on June 9, 1979. Students of the faculty, former facts, new associate professors of Moscow State University I. A. Tyulina and I. Z. Pirogov talk about this geronic faculty - the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, its life during the Great Patriotic War, about some of its teachers and students - participants in the war.

Front-line soldiers
On June 21, 1941, Zhenya Rudneva wrote in her diary: “All tests passed! All exams! All! All! Life is beautiful and amazing!”
And the next morning, a terrible war fell upon our entire people. The Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, the university, the whole country boiled like a torn anthill: some hurried to the military registration and enlistment offices, others went to the labor front and defensive work; free shelons and columns stretched to the west.
