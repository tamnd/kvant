⟦folio 36⟧

Tasks

M621—M625; F633—F637

M621. A $n$-gon is circumscribed around a circle. An arbitrary point $P$ inside the circle is connected to all its vertices and points of tangency. The resulting $2n$ triangles are colored alternately red and blue. Prove that the product of the areas of the triangles is equal to the product of the areas of the blue triangles.

U. Alla

M622. Prove that the number of solutions to the equation
$$x^3 + y^2 = z^3 + t^2 + 1$$
in natural numbers, not exceeding $10^6$, less than the number of solutions to the equation
$$x^3 + y^2 = z^3 + t^2$$
in natural numbers, not exceeding $10^6$.

V. Vavilov

M623. a) How many axes of symmetry does the cube have? Regular triangular paramid?
b) * Prove that if some polyhedron has $k$ symmetry axes ($k \ge 1$), then $k$ is odd.

V. Senderov

M624. Find the sequence $(a_n)$ defined by the conditions $a_1 = 1$,
$$1 + \sum_{d} (-1)^{n/d} a_d = 0,$$
where the sum $\sum$ is taken over all divisors $d$ of $n$ (including $d = 1$ and $d = n$).

For example, if $n = p$ is a prime number, then (*) takes the form $1 + (-1)^{p/1} a_1 + (-1)^{p/p} a_p = 0$, hence $a_p = 2$ if $p = 2$, and $a_p = 0$ if $p > 2$.

V. Abramovich

M625.* On the coordinate plane, four points with rational coordinates are given that do not lie at the vertices of the parallelogram, and no three of them belong to the same straight line. It is allowed to draw a straight line through any two already obtained points and mark the intersection point of any two drawn straight lines. Prove that the set of points that can be obtained in this way is the set of all points in the plane.
