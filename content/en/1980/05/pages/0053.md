Option 2
(Faculty of Geology and Geophysics and Faculty of Natural Sciences)

1. Two cars are driving towards each other and will meet in 6 days. If the first car lasted 1.8 days, and the second - 1.6 days, then together they would travel 520 km. If the first one had covered 2/3 of the route taken by the second one, and the second one had covered 1/3 of the route taken by the first one, then the first one would have had 2 fewer days to do this than the second one. How many kilometers does each car travel per day?

2. Solve system equation
$$\begin{cases}
\frac{x+y}{x-y} + 6 \frac{x-y}{x+y} = 5 \\
xy = 2.
\end{cases}$$

3. Solve the equation
$$2 \cos 2x - 2\sqrt{3} \sin 2x + 2\sqrt{3} \sin x - 6 \cos x - 1 = 0.$$

4. In a small quadrilateral ABCD, angle ABC is right, |AB| = |BC| = a. From vertex D, as from the center, a circle is drawn touching sides AB, BC and the line passing through vertices A and C. Determine the area of ​​intersection of the circle bounded by this circle and the quadrilateral ABCD.

5. The length of the cube edge $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ is equal to 1, $ABCD$ is the base, $AC$ is the diagonal of the base, $AA_1, BB_1, CC_1, DD_1$ are the side edges. Points M, N and K lie on edges $AA_1, D_1C_1$ and $CC_1$, respectively, with $|A_1M| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, |D_1N| = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, |CK| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$. A line l passes through point K and is parallel to line MN. Find the length of the part of the straight line l contained inside the cube.
