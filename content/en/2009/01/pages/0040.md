KVANT 2009/№1

Several rhyming physics problems

V.AKIMOV

Problem conditions

1. Boat on the river A boat is sailing along the river, It is sailing from afar. Suddenly, a gust of wind blew the cap off the sailor.

A quarter of an hour flew by until they figured it out and with the same speed they chased after the cap.

Where did you manage to catch up with her? Here is a question for an expert, If a river flows four miles in an hour.

2. Match in your hands, break the match in half, break part again. Tell me, why is it more difficult to break it a second time?

6. Pencil in a decanter Malvina dropped a pencil into a decanter of water. The pencil did not drown, Pinocchio noticed.

The pencil sank three-quarters of the way in. Now tell me, what was his density?

7. Tame hippopotamus It was in Africa, someone told me. Maybe where is Tunisia now, or maybe... where is Lesotho? The greedy leader collected taxes from the people with the help of a sacred tame hippopotamus.

He sailed on a boat with scales and a soldier. The people balanced the hippopotamus with gold. I fed the animal and the scales broke. People laughed at the ruler's greed.

3. Sisyphus' work Sisyphus drags his stone up the mountain from time immemorial, as usual. For him there will never be unemployment.

Then the leader became enraged. He obliged the soldier, so that the warrior could measure the tribute before sunset! Simplicity was enough for a complex task. What would you do in this case?

He slipped a lever under the stone: Part four of it. Won three times in strength. It's like nothing!

8. Treasure in a jug The fisherman cried in the boat that there was still no luck, He cast his confused glance at the water. Suddenly I saw an object on the waves, almost completely submerged in the water.

With what gain will it be, will he defeat the “enemy”, if he now takes hold of the second edge of the lever?

4. Clown on a tightrope A clown walks on a tightrope. He holds half a log in his hands. For an artist, what is more important – the weight of the log or the length?

He threw himself into the river, all wet and chilled. Soon the boat lifted from the abyss with a wax seal on the stopper a three-liter jug, ancient, made of clay.

5. Ball on a hill We rolled the ball up a hill. We still don’t understand to this day: Where did he move longer - Up the mountain or down the mountain again?

Having opened the jug, our hero was stunned, the Soul sang like a thousand pipes - the “Golden” were rolling. From now on, the family will not sleep on an empty stomach.
