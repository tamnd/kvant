Let's consider all the questions in order. In order for the tea to brew, the teapot must remain at a high temperature for a sufficiently long time - that is why the teapot is made of a substance with poor thermal conductivity (porcelain). The smaller the surface area of ​​a body (all other things being equal), the slower it cools, and the smallest surface area is that of a ball.
