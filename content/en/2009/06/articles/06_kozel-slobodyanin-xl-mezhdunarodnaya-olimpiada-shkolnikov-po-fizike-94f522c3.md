XL International Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics

This year, the International Physics Olympiad (IPO) for schoolchildren was held in Mexico, in the city of Merida. Due to the alarming epidemiological situation in this country, some teams refused to participate in the Olympics. Only 316 schoolchildren from 69 countries arrived in Merida (for comparison, last year in Vietnam there were 376 participants from 76 countries). The Russian national team included: Tregubov Dmitry - Kirov, FML, teachers-mentors Kanin Pavel Evgenievich, Gyrdymov Mikhail Vladimirovich, Zemlyanov Vladislav - Urai (Khanty-Mansiysk Autonomous Okrug), gymnasium, teacher-mentor Kozlovskaya Zoya Georgievna, Kudryashova Nina - Biysk, Biysk Lyceum of the Altai Territory, teacher-mentor Apolonsky Alexander Nikolaevich, Doroshenko Andrey - Omsk, lyceum 92, teacher-mentor Yulika Aleksandrovna Afanasyeva, Starkov Grigory - Noyabrsk (Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous District), school 7, teacher-mentor Tkachuk Igor Viktorovich. The Russian team was led by Stanislav Mironovich Kozel, professor at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT), and Valery Pavlovich Slobodyanin, associate professor at MIPT. MIPT Associate Professor Mikhail Nikolaevich Osin worked as an observer as part of the Russian delegation. As in previous years, eight candidates for the Russian team were invited to the last three-week summer training camp, where they practiced skills in experimental work on complex modern equipment and additionally studied elements of special relativity, wave optics, nuclear physics and a number of other topics included in the MFO program. During the training camp, the team was supported by teachers from the Department of General Physics of MIPT, the Scientific Research Center of Moscow State University, researchers from institutes of the Russian Academy of Sciences, as well as Physics and Technology students - winners of the International Physics Olympiads of previous years.
