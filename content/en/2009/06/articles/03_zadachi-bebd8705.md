KVANT 2009/№6

Tasks

1.

Mr. Twister inherited several factories. During his life, 7 factories went bankrupt, and he divided the rest equally among his seven sons. The youngest son put 6 factories into the wind during his life, and divided the rest among his seven sons. His youngest son auctioned off 5 factories, but according to family tradition, he divided the rest among his seven sons. During the lifetime of his youngest son, 4 factories went bankrupt, but when it came to the inheritance, there was nothing to divide - the failed businessman had only one factory left. How many factories did Mr. Twister initially have? A. Khachaturian

4.

Petya left point A of the flat plain and walked 1 m to the south, 2 m to the west, 3 m to the north, 4 m to the east, 5 m to the south, 6 m to the west, 7 m to the north, 8 m to the east, etc. Having walked a total of 5 km, Petya got tired and sat down to rest. At what distance from point A did this happen? S. Dorichenko, T. Golenishcheva-Kutuzova

2.

The planet has the shape of a ball. Is it possible to draw 2009 meridians and 2010 parallels on it so that they divide the planet into sections of equal area? G. Galperin

5.

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

There are 9 identical-looking coins. One of them is fake, which is lighter than the real one. One of the coins stuck to one of the pans of a scale without weights. There is no time to tear it off. How to find a counterfeit coin in two weighings? (It may also be stuck.) A. Shapovalov

3.

Two pumps together fill the pool in two hours, but separately they fill it in a whole, but different number of hours. How many hours does each pump take to fill the pool? N. Konishchev

These tasks are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.
