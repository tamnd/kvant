Tasks

Department of Biology

Admission to the department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of the introductory work. Schoolchildren who are studying in 8th or 9th grade this academic year, regardless of their place of residence, can take part in the competition. Education for eighth-graders lasts 3 years, for ninth-graders – 2 years. Eighth grade students need to solve problems 1–5 of the introductory work below, and ninth graders need to solve problems 2–6. You can use facts found in the literature and your own ideas in your answers. For information gleaned from books, please provide links to sources. Along with your work, please send a stamped, self-addressed envelope (to send the decision to the admissions committee). The deadline for submitting the entry work is no later than May 20, 2010.
