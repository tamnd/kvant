Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Federal Correspondence School of Physics and Technology (FZFTSH) at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is recruiting students from general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation for the 2010/11 academic year. FZFTSH at MIPT has been operating as a state educational institution for specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 80 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its graduate. The school is financed by the Federal Education Agency. Education for students living in the Russian Federation is free. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (state university). The goal of our school is to help students in grades 8–11 of general education institutions who are interested in physics and mathematics, deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, and also contribute to their professional self-determination. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2010/11 academic year is carried out for part-time, part-time and full-time courses.

Correspondence department (individual training) Tel./fax: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@ mail.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing the introductory task in physics and mathematics given below. The full training program lasts 4 years, i.e. for grades 8–11, but you can enroll in any of the specified classes. During the academic year, in accordance with the FZFTS program, the student will receive assignments in physics and mathematics for each academic topic (5 assignments in each subject for the 8th grade, 6-7 assignments in each subject for the 9th, 10th and 11th grades), and then the recommended author's solutions to these assignments along with the tested work. The assignments contain theoretical material, analysis of typical examples and problems on the relevant topic, and 8–12 test questions and problems for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics and higher mathematics of MIPT, as well as MIPT graduates. The work of part-time students is checked by students, graduate students and graduates of MIPT (80% of them are graduates of our school). The deadline for sending the solution to the introductory task is no later than March 1, 2010. Checked entrance papers are not sent back to the applicant. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 2010. Outside of competition, the FZFTS accepts the winners of regional, regional, republican, and all-Russian Olympiads in physics and mathematics for the 2009/10 academic year. Before May 15, 2010, they must send to the FZFTSH the completed introductory work in physics and mathematics, along with copies of diplomas confirming participation in the above-mentioned Olympiads. Send a notebook with completed assignments (in physics and mathematics) to the address: 141700 Moscow region, Dolgoprudny, Institutsky lane, 9, FZFTSH at MIPT.
