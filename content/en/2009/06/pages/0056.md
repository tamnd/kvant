Department of Economics

Training at the department is carried out via the Internet and e-mail (there is also a traditional form of training with materials sent by regular mail). Anyone who has completed 7th grade can enroll in the economics department; the duration of study is from 1 to 4 years. Training is individual or in the “Collective Student” group. The department's program includes the study of economic theory and familiarity with the practice of economics and business. The department's courses include fundamentals of economics, entrepreneurship and management, accounting and finance, world economics and geography, and economic history. We will help 9th grade students better prepare for the State Final Certification (GIA-9) in Russian language and mathematics. Students in grades 10–11, in addition to the economics course, will receive the necessary training in Russian language and literature, mathematics, and social studies, which will help them better pass the Unified State Exam, successfully participate in subject Olympiads, and enroll in the best universities in the country. Students of the economics department will be able to get acquainted with teachers, students and graduates of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov. They will conduct master classes for you and introduce you to wonderful professions in economics, one of which you may choose for yourself in the future. During training, students become participants in an exciting business game, which will be conducted via the Internet or by correspondence. Department of “Morality, Law, Law” (law and civics)

Schoolchildren in grades 8–11 and “Collective Student” groups are offered two courses. 1) One-year course “Conversations about human rights, morality, law, law and state.” The course provides modern ideas about the basic concepts related to law, law and the state, and talks about the basics of Russian legislation and human rights. Examples of court cases are analyzed and general cultural examples related to the focus of the course are given. 2) One and a half year course “Conversations about the foundations of democracy.” We suggest taking the courses in this order. And only high school students, if they do not have time to complete both courses in a row, but definitely want to take the second course, can start directly from it. Those wishing to study must provide their full postal address, email address (if any), last name, first name and patronymic, how many classes have been completed. When assessing the entrance work, the questions of which are given below, we take into account the age (basic education) of the applicant.
