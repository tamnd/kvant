TODAY, OUR LONG SPACE Flight STARTED, - this is how the captain of the first interstellar ship “Earth - Andromeda” began his speech. – As you know, there are 50 men and 50 women here, and we have been flying for 50 years. So everyone should start families. Each of you is unmarried and must marry here: this was the condition of admission to the expeditionary force. “We remember, and many of us will be happy to get married today.” We have known each other for at least a year. - Fine. But I think that everyone should get married, and in such a way that there is no adultery on this ship. Everyone laughed heartily. “Captain, you are an idealist,” someone said. “I’m quite realistic,” the captain smiled. “It’s impossible on Earth, but we can do it here.” - How is this? – everyone continued to laugh. “Can I ask,” the captain was unbending, “why is married man A cheating on his wife with married lady O?” – What’s incomprehensible here, captain? Because they like each other more than their own spouses. – And A easily finds the beauty O who prefers him to her own husband? - It couldn’t be simpler! – some were simply rolling with laughter. – But this is on Earth! “The captain looked sternly at the crew, and the laughter disappeared by itself. “And I intend to marry us in such a way that no man A will be able to find a woman O whom he would like more than his own wife and who would prefer him to his own spouse.” “Do you mean, captain, that every woman who likes A more than half of him does not like A himself?” At least not more than her own husband. - Exactly. Conversely, A prefers his wife to all those who would happily change their partners to him. - Good idea, captain! If you succeed, you will solve the age-old problem of marital stability.
