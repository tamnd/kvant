KVANT 2009/№3

Tasks

1.

Along the 60 km long road there are several stumps (more than one). The first tourist walks along the road at a speed of 5 km/h and rests near each stump for the same integer number of hours. The second tourist rides a bicycle at a speed of 12 km/h and rests on each stump twice as long as the first tourist. The tourists left and arrived at the same time. How many stumps are there along the road? O. Ivanova, K. Kokhas

⟦column⟧

4.

There are 250 hobbits living in the village. Each hobbit lives in a separate house. In the evenings they go to visit each other. In one evening, each hobbit, if he goes to visit, visits everyone who can be found at home, and does not appear at home that evening. In what minimum number of evenings can it happen that among any two villagers at least one visits the other? A. Evnin

2.

Having placed a tea bag in a mug of water, lift it by the string above the mug - the bag will begin to rotate. Why is this happening? A. Kovaldzhi

5.

In a convex hexagon ABCDEF, all interior angles are equal. It is known that AB = 2, CD = 5, DE = 7, EF = 1. Find BC and AF. Folklore

3.

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

There are 1 gold, 3 silver and 5 bronze medals. It is known that one of them is fake: lighter than the real one. Real medals made from the same metal weigh the same (but those from different metals do not weigh the same). How to find a fake medal after 2 weighings on a cup scale without weights? A. Shapovalov

These tasks are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.
