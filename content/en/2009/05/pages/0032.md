Selected Tournament Challenges

1 (7–8). Three brothers Anton, Bori and Vasya have the same birthdays. It turned out that when Anton turns 12 years old, the sum of the ages of the other two brothers will be divided by 12 without a remainder. The same thing will happen when Bora turns 12 years old. Prove that the same will happen when Vasya turns 12. A. Shapovalov

2 (7). In the isosceles triangle ABC (AB = AC) the bisector BD is drawn. A line passing through point D perpendicular to BD intersects side BC at point K. Prove that BK = 2CD. Folklore

3 (7–8). Three stalkers reached the Stone Anomaly. From there, a 100 m long path leads to the treasure. Stalkers know that the first person to follow the path will be petrified in a random place and that the same fate awaits the second one. Both will come to life at the moment when the third one walks along the path and the total distance from him to the two petrified companions will be exactly 100 m. Can stalkers get to the treasure without the risk of being petrified forever? A. Blinkov, I. Raskina

4 (8). Three circles pass through point P and intersect in pairs at points A, B and C. It is known that the center of one circle lies on the straight line AP, and the center of the other lies on the straight line BP. Prove that the center of the third circle lies on the line CP. B.Frenkin

5 (8). Is it possible to divide all natural numbers from 1 to 2009 into two groups so that the sum of the numbers in one group is equal to the product of the numbers in the other group? A. Shapovalov

6 (8). A 222-gon with sides along the boundaries of the cells is drawn on checkered paper. What is the smallest number of cells this polygon can consist of? A. Shapovalov
