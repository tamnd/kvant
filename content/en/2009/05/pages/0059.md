OLYMPICS

Moscow Student Olympiad in Physics 2009

May 24 at the Moscow State Technical University (MSTU) named after. N.E. Bauman hosted the next Moscow City Olympiad in Physics among students of technical universities. 75 students from 8 universities in Moscow took part in the Olympiad. According to the results of the team competition, the team from MSTU took first place. N.E. Bauman, who scored 155 points, second place went to the team of the Moscow State Institute of Steel and Alloys (MISiS) (129 points), third place went to the team of the Moscow State Institute of Electronic Technology (80 points). In the individual competition, first place was won by I. Ivanov from MSTU. N.E. Bauman, who scored 50 points, second place went to A. Vyatskikh (MSTU named after N.E. Bauman, 46 points), third place went to P. Karpov (MISiS, 31 points).
