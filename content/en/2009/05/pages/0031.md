Competition named after A.P. Savin

"Math 6–8"

We continue our next competition on solving mathematical problems for students in grades 6–8. Send solutions to problems within a month after receiving this issue of the journal to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant» or by email: math@kvant.info (marked “Competition “Mathematics 6–8”). Be sure to include your name, class, and home address. As before, we welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by mathematical clubs. We ask circle leaders to provide an email address or contact phone number. According to tradition, the winning clubs of the correspondence competition are invited to the final in-person tournament.

6. 2,000 people have registered on the new website “Razgovori.ru”. Each of them invited 1000 people to be their friends. Two people are declared friends if and only if each of them has invited the other to be friends. What is the smallest number of pairs of friends that could be formed? A. Evnin

7. Is it possible to cut a square into 9 squares and color them so that you get 1 white, 3 gray and 5 black squares, and the same-color squares would be equal, and the multi-colored squares would not be equal? N. Avilov

Summer tournament named after A.P. Savin

Every year at the end of June, schoolchildren who are passionate about mathematics gather in a beautiful place in the Kostroma region to compete in solving problems. The tournament is named after Anatoly Pavlovich Savin, founder of the “Mathematics 6–8” competition of the magazine «Kvant». Thanks to the competition in 1993, this tournament of mathematical battles arose. The organizers of the tournament are the magazine «Kvant» and the educational program “Big Change” (headed by G.V. Kondakov). This summer, the teams gathered for 7 days (from June 26 to July 2, 2009) near the city of Sudislavl at the picturesque recreation center “Berendeyevy Polyany”. Many of the guys came here much earlier - they studied at the summer math school, which began on June 11, after which they decided to stay for the tournament. This year, teams from grades 6–8 played against each other: 8 teams from grades 6, 12 teams from grades 7, and 8 teams from grades 8. Schoolchildren came from Moscow, St. Petersburg, Ivanovo, Kostroma, Tambov and Chernogolovka. The Tambov team was invited to the tournament as the winner of the “Mathematics 6–8” competition of the magazine «Kvant». On the day of arrival (June 25), a lively mathematical game “Magic Square” was immediately played. The winners were the teams “Fractal” (6th grade, St. Petersburg), “Intellectual” (7th grade, Moscow) and “218-B” (8th grade, Moscow). The next day, an oral team Olympiad was held, according to the results of which the teams were divided into leagues by class, with the 7th grade being divided into two leagues. Next, mathematical battles took place in each league. The last day of the tournament is the final battles, which determined the winners in each league. In the 8-grade league, the Moscow-South team took first place. It was collected by T.P. Zorina at the June summer mathematics school in 2008. In just over a year, the guys have won various tournaments, including this tournament last year. In the 7th grade major league, the best team was the MCSME club team led by A.V. Spivak. The best sixth-graders are the guys from the Fractal club in St. Petersburg; their advantage turned out to be noticeable both during the fights and during the team Olympiad.
