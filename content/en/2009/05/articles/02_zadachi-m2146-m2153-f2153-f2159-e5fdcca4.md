PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems from this issue should be sent no later than January 1, 2010 to the address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send solutions to problems from different issues of the magazine or in different subjects (mathematics and physics) in different envelopes. On the envelope, in the “To” column, write: “Problem book «Kvant» No. 5–2009” and the numbers of the problems whose solutions you are sending, for example “M2146” or “F2153”. In the “From” column, please write your first and last name legibly. In the letter, enclose an envelope with your address written on it and the required set of stamps (in this envelope you will receive the results of checking the decisions). Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics can also be sent to the email addresses math@kvant.info and phys@kvant.info, respectively. Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication in a separate envelope in duplicate along with your solution to this problem (mark on the envelope: “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in physics” or “Problem book «Kvant», new problem in mathematics”). At the beginning of each letter, please indicate the school number and class in which you study. Problem M2149 and M2150 were offered at the Leonhard Euler Mathematical Olympiad.

Problem M2146–M2153, F2153–F2159

M2146. 2008 graphs of quadratic trinomials were drawn on the coordinate plane. Could it turn out that for each of them there is a straight line that has common points with any graph except it? N. Agakhanov, I. Bogdanov

M2147. Each cell of an infinite checkered plane contains a real number in such a way that all horizontal and vertical sequences of numbers that are infinite in both directions are periodic. Prove that there are infinitely many horizontal sequences with different shortest periods if and only if there are infinitely many vertical sequences with different shortest periods. E.Sign

M2148. The numbers 1, 2, 3, ..., 100 are written in a circle in some order. Petya calculated 100 sums of all triplets of neighboring numbers and wrote the smallest of these sums on the board. What is the largest number that could be written on the board? P. Kozhevnikov

M2149. In triangle ABC, sides AB and BC are equal. Point D inside the triangle is such that $\angle$ ADC $= 2 \angle$ ABC. Prove that twice the distance from point B to the outer bisector of angle ADC is equal to AD + DC. S. Berlov

M2150. In the country of Leonardia, all roads are one-way. Each road connects two cities and does not pass through other cities. The Department of Statistics calculated the total number for each city

⟦column⟧

residents in cities from which roads lead to it, and the total number of residents in cities where roads from it lead. Prove that for at least one city the first number is no less than the second. N. Gravin

M2151. Number triangle 1

1

1 2

1 3

1 2

1

1 3 6 7 6 3 1 1 4 10 16 19 16 10 4 1

... compiled according to the following rule. The first line contains one number equal to 1. The k-th line (k = 2, 3, ...) contains 2k – 1 numbers, each of which is equal to the sum of three numbers: the number above it and its two neighbors in the previous line (if some of these three numbers are missing, then they are considered equal to 0). Prove that in the middle column there are no numbers that give a remainder of 2 when divided by 3. K. Kaibhanov

$M2152^{*}$. A pair ( p, q ) of distinct primes is said to be special if there exists a that can be represented as $a = x^{p} + y^{p} = z^{q} + t^{q}$ for some natural numbers x, y, z, t, but cannot be represented as $u^{pq} + v^{pq}$, where u and v are natural numbers. Prove that any prime number p is included in an infinite number of special pairs. V. Senderov
