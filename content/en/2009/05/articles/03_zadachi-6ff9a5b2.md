A mushroom is called bad if it contains at least 10 worms. If a mushroom has less than 10 worms, it is considered good. There are 90 bad and 10 good in a basket

A crowd is walking along the corridor. Before the fork in the corridor there is a divider. He directs one person to the left and the other to the right, so that the flow is divided into two equal parts. Using several of these separators, a quarter of the flow or three-eighths of it can be separated. Is it possible to separate a third of the flow? V.Arnold
