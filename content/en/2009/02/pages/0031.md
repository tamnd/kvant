1.

4 teams participated in the one-round football tournament. The team that scored 3 points took “pure” second place. Restore the results of all matches. A clear second place means that there are no more teams with the same number of points and there is exactly one team with more points. A win is worth 3 points, a draw is 1 point, and a loss is 0 points. A. Blinkov

2.

Lena went to walk the puppy in the park. As soon as she found herself at the beginning of a 41 m long path running along the park, her friend Katya appeared at the end of this path. The puppy began to run with joy from Lena to Katya and back and ran back and forth several times until the friends met at a distance of 13 m from the beginning of the path. In total, the puppy ran 85 m. How far did he run in one direction and how far in the other? G. Galperin

The factory produces sets of five white elephants of various sizes and weights. If you arrange the elephants by height, then according to the standard the difference in the masses of neighboring elephants should be the same. Will the inspector be able to check this using a pan scale without weights? What if the sets consisted of four elephants? A. Shapovalov
