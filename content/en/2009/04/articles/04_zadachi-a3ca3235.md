KMS

Tasks

1.

Solve the rebus DECL = SMALL + YES + DEL, if DECL should be as small as possible. (Different letters indicate different numbers, and the same ones indicate the same ones.) M. Akhmedzhanova

⟦column⟧

4.

There are four rooks in the corners of the chessboard. With each move, the rook moves (horizontally or vertically) until it touches another rook or the edge of the board. Collect all the rooks in the four central squares. A. Shapovalov

2.

Baron Munchausen says that he has a multi-digit palindromic number (it is read the same from left to right and from right to left). Having written it on a paper tape, the baron made several cuts between the numbers and received the numbers 1, 2, ..., N on pieces of tape (each exactly once). Is the Baron bragging? A. Shapovalov

5.

If a rectangle with an irregularly shaped hole cut out in it is hung by vertex A, side AB forms an angle $30^\circ,$ with the vertical, and if by vertex B - angle $60^\circ.$ What angle does side AB form with the vertical if the rectangle is hung by the middle of this side? E. Sokolov

3.

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

Before the price increase, tea with two gingerbreads cost 1 ruble. When all prices rose (by the same percentage), the ruble was only enough for tea and one gingerbread. Then prices rose again, by the same percentage as the first time. After that, was there even enough ruble for tea? I. Akulich

These tasks are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8.
