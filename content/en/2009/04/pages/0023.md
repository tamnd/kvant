PROBLEM WORKER

F2145. The Earth's daily satellite rotates in a circular orbit lying in the equatorial plane. As a result of a short-term activation of the braking engine, the satellite's speed decreases by 1 m/s, but the direction of the speed does not change. Find the change in the satellite's orbital period. A.Povtorov

F2146. A rabbit runs in a straight line, its speed is always equal to $v_{0} = 5$ m s. At a point $L_{0} = 100$ m from this line, a fox sits. She notices the rabbit and gives chase when it is at a minimum distance from the mentioned point. The fox runs at the same speed; the fox's speed vector is directed at any moment to the point where the rabbit is located. Find the maximum acceleration of the fox during the chase. The fox and the rabbit are considered to be material points. A. Starov

F2147. Petya and Vasya have a small automatic water heater in their dacha kitchen. It is a five-liter container with good thermal insulation and a two-kilowatt electric heater. The container is always connected to a water pipe through which cold water can flow into it, and at the bottom there is a faucet through which hot water can be drawn. The heater is equipped with a relay with a regulator that allows you to set the desired water temperature. The heater is used mainly for washing dishes, and since no one washes dishes at night, it is turned off at night. And then the brothers began to argue. Petya believed that they were acting economically by turning off the power supply to the device at night. Vasya believed that there was no difference - after all, during the night the water cools down greatly, and in the morning the heater turns on for a longer time, and if you leave the power supply on, then during the night the heater will turn on many times, but for a short time, maintaining the set water temperature. To resolve the dispute, the brothers did an experiment. They set the temperature regulator to $46 ^\circ$ C. It turned out that in 9 night hours the water cooled to $30 ^\circ$ C. The air temperature in the kitchen at night was 16 ° C. Is this data enough to estimate how much you will have to pay per month if you turn off or not turn off the power to your water heater at night? The cost of a kilowatt-hour is taken to be 3 rubles. I. Leenson

F2148. The saturated vapor pressure of water at +20 °C is 1000 Pa, and at a temperature of +20.5 °C it increases to 1020 Pa. Using these data, determine the molar heat of evaporation of water at +20 °C. Z. Rafailov

Find the maximum charge on the capacitor. The circuit elements are considered ideal. R.Alexandrov
