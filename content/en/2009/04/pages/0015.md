Problem 7. Show that any spring graph on a sphere can be represented as the difference of two graphs with positive stresses.

The algorithm exactly repeats the algorithm for constructing P(G, f) for a graph with positive stress. We believe that their numbering corresponds to the order of traversing the edges of the graph clockwise. Therefore, from such polygons, by transferring them in parallel, it is possible to assemble a certain object P ( G, f ), defined by such a construction up to parallel transfer. P(G,f) is called a virtual polyhedron. Now it is clear what needs to be done to construct the Minkowski difference of two polyhedra K and L: i Convert the polyhedra into spring graphs. i Take the difference of the spring graphs. i Construct a virtual polytope P ( G, f ) corresponding to the difference of the graphs.

Let us give an explanation for advanced readers. The fact is that during Minkowski summation, the parallel faces of polyhedra add up. Therefore, it is natural to expect that the faces of the Minkowski difference K – L should serve as the Minkowski differences of the faces of the polyhedra K and L. It is this phenomenon that is encoded in the construction of the virtual polyhedron.
