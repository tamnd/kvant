11 (grades 6–8). The cells of the rectangular table m u n are painted in three colors: blue, red, green. In one move, you can select three cells that form a corner, and at the same time recolor them as follows: blue cells - red, red - green, green - blue. For what m and n can the table be made one-color, regardless of the initial coloring? O. Mangina
