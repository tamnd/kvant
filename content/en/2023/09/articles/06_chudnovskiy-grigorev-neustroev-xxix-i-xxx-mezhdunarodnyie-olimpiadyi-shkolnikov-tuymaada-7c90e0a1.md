Last July, 2022, the Tuymaada International Olympiad in physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry was held remotely, and in July of this year, 2023, again in a full-time format. Participants in the Physics Olympiad competed, as usual, in two leagues: senior and junior (the jury distributes participants into leagues depending on their age and class). According to the program of the Physics Olympiad, participants can be offered problems on any topics contained in the in-depth school curriculum. Authors of original problems (both theoretical and experimental) can send them to the methodological commission (achudn@mail.ru) - the best problems will be included in the final set and will be published after the Olympiad in educational journals. Problem 4. Uranium ball A uranium ball of thermal conductivity k and radius R, placed in a stream of water at a temperature of $T_{0}$, is irradiated by a stream of neutrons so that, as a result of the nuclear fission reaction, volumetric power q is released. Problem 5. Gas tee A small symmetrical tee connects three identical vessels with rarefied helium. All tubes of the tee are symmetrically filled with porous material, penetrated by many parallel channels, the diameter of which is much less than the free path of the molecules. In the center of the tee there is a small space free of porous material. The entry of a molecule into a channel from any side means that the molecule has a certain probability of escaping from this channel back through the same hole and an additional probability of escaping through the opposite hole of this channel, and these probabilities do not depend on temperature and are the same for all channels. Initially, the helium in all three vessels had a pressure of $p_{0}$ and some of the same temperature. Then, maintaining the same temperature in the first vessel and inside the tee, they increased the temperature in the second vessel twofold, and in the third - threefold. What pressures $p_{1}, p_{2}, p_{3}$ and $p_{4}$ will be established, respectively, in three vessels and in the free space inside the tee after a long time? S.Varlamov

At what angular velocity Z did the dish with mercury rotate in this experiment? The lunar disk is visible from Earth at an angle of J 0 5 q. Gravity acceleration g 9 8 m $с^{2}$. V.Efimov
