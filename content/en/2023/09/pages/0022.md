Problems in mathematics and physics

PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2762 (for arbitrary n) was proposed at the 19th International Zhautykov Olympiad. Problem F2769-F2772 were offered at the final stage of the LVI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics.

Problem M2762–M2765, F2769–F2772

M2762. The sum of n 100 non-zero real numbers (not necessarily distinct) is equal to zero. For each of the ways to select several (at least one) of these numbers, the sum of the selected numbers was calculated and all the resulting sums were written down in a line in non-increasing order. The first number in the line is S. Find the smallest possible value of the second number in the line. A. Golovanov

M2763. Given a natural number k t 2. Prove that natural numbers with an even sum of digits give all possible remainders when divided by k. P. Kozlov, I. Bogdanov

M2765*. Given 101 coins and two-pan scales. In one weighing you can compare the weights of two coins. Let's call a majority a subset of 51 coins of the same weight. For what is the least number of weighings it is possible to determine with a guarantee whether there is a majority among the given coins? K. Knop

F2769. The side wall of a cubic thin-walled aquarium (a u a u a) was broken. They decided to press the new wall (a u a) with spring clips. The first spring with stiffness 2k was fixed at the middle of the upper rib of the new wall, and the second spring with stiffness k was fixed at the middle of the lower rib. After this, the aquarium was moved towards the vertical wall (Fig.2). All surfaces where the new wall comes into contact with the aquarium are flat and smooth. The new wall is rigid, completely covers the adjacent side walls and bottom and is parallel to the plane of the wall. The friction force at the point of contact of the wall with the table is negligible.
