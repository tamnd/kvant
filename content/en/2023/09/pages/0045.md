Last July, 2022, the Tuymaada International Olympiad in physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry was held remotely, and in July of this year, 2023, again in a full-time format. Participants in the Physics Olympiad competed, as usual, in two leagues: senior and junior (the jury distributes participants into leagues depending on their age and class). According to the program of the Physics Olympiad, participants can be offered problems on any topics contained in the in-depth school curriculum. Authors of original problems (both theoretical and experimental) can send them to the methodological commission (achudn@mail.ru) - the best problems will be included in the final set and will be published after the Olympiad in educational journals.
