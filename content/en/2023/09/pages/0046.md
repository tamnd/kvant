Below are theoretical problems for the senior league.

Theoretical Tour 2022

Problem 1. Center of mass of the loading platform A platform of mass M 2000 kg and length L 80 m moves without friction with an initial speed of $v_{0}^{1}$ m s. When the platform was already 3 4 of its length past the loading hatch, it opened and crushed stone began to pour out of it at a flow rate of $P^{100}$ kg s. Find the speed $v_{c} t$ of the center of mass of the system consisting of a platform and spilled rubble, depending on the time after opening the hatch. Give the answer in general form and for the specified numerical data. A. Manakov, A. Chudnovsky

Problem 2. Unwinding a rubber ring A thin rubber ring with a density of $U^{1500}$ kg $м^{3}$ was put on a rough cylinder of a slightly larger diameter so that the ring stretched and a mechanical stress T 6 kPa arose in it. To what maximum linear speed v can the ring be untwisted by rotating the cylinder around its axis? Other bodies do not interact with the ring. A. Chudnovsky

Problem 3. Adiabatic fall of a heavy piston A heavy piston begins an adiabatic fall in a vertical cylinder filled with a monatomic ideal gas (vacuum above the piston). The ratio of the initial force of gas pressure on the piston to the force of gravity of the piston is a small value M 0 01.

⟦column⟧

1) Estimate the ratio $h H_{min} H_{0}$ of the minimum height $H_{min}$ of the piston above the bottom of the vessel to the initial height $H_{0}$. 2) Estimate the ratio $P p_{max} p_{0}$ of the maximum pressure $p_{max}$ of the gas in the cylinder to the initial $p_{0}$. 3) Estimate the ratio $t T_{max} T_{0}$ of the maximum temperature $T_{max}$ of the gas in the cylinder to the initial $T_{0}$. A. Vlasov
