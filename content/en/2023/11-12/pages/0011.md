The most important scientific organizations in our country are the Russian Federal Nuclear Center - the All-Russian Scientific Research Institute of Experimental Physics (RFNC-VNIIEF) with its scientific campus and 25 thousand scientific staff. This fall, the first masters from the new branch of MSU named after M.V. Lomonosov, located in Sarov, came to work there. Their final master's theses and planned scientific activities are devoted to attophysics. Attophysics is the future. This is, first of all, the further development of femtosecond technologies, which means new materials, new medications and drugs, new faster electronic devices and much more that is difficult to predict in advance. And if you fantasize a little, you can imagine that attophysics is paving the way to the generation of zeptosecond $( 10^{21}$ c) and iotosecond $( 10^{24}$ c) radiation pulses, with the help of which it will be possible to “look” not into the atom, but into the atomic nucleus and “see” how mysterious quarks are combined in triplets into protons and neutrons and what the mechanism of nuclear transformations is.
