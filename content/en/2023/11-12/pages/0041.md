KONK URS IM ENI A.P.S AVINA

We continue our next competition to solve mathematical problems. The tasks are designed mainly for students starting from grades 8–9, and we advise younger students to try their hand at the magazine competition «Kvant» (see website kvantik.com). Send solutions to problems you can solve to: savin.contest@gmail.com. In addition to your first and last name, indicate the city, school and class in which you study. We welcome participation in the competition not only by individual schoolchildren, but also by teams (in this case, one work is sent with a list of participants). You can participate starting from any tour. The winners will receive diplomas from the magazine «Kvant» and prizes. Assignments, solutions and results are published on sites.google.com/view/savin-contest We wish you success!

9. At noon, cyclists Alyosha and Vasya left from point A to point B, and cyclists Borya and Grisha met them from point B to point A. Everyone was driving at some constant speed. After some time, all four met at the same time, after which Alyosha and Grisha went to point A, Borya to point B, and Vasya to one of these points, and he arrived fourth (later than everyone else). What time did Grisha arrive? T. Kazitsyna, B. Frenkin

10. On a chessboard 8 u 8 there is a piece on cell c4. How many ways are there to place 9 rooks on the board that do not attack each other? (The rook hits squares in its own row and in its own column, but does not hit through the chip.) P. Kozhevnikov

11. Several coins will be laid out in front of you and the audience. They will tell you in secret about each coin, what its mass is, and the audience will only be told that the mass of each coin is 2 g or 3 g, and together their mass is N g. Will you always be able to do just one weighing in front of the audience on a cup scale without weights, after which they will also understand about all the coins, what is the mass of each coin? Answer the question of the problem: a) for N 77, b) for all N. A. Gribalko

14. There are 7 sofas in a circle, with only 50 people sitting on them, with at least one person on each sofa. Each one said, “On the next couch clockwise, exactly half the people are taller than me, and exactly half are shorter.” What is the greatest number of people who could tell the truth? T. Kazitsyna
