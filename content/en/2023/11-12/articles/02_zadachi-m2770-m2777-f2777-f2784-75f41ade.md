Problems in mathematics and physics

PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2770 – M2772 was offered at the XLV Tournament of Cities, problem M2774 was offered at the South Russian Mathematical Olympiad “Assara”, and problem M2776 and M2777 were offered at the June training camp for candidates for the International Mathematical Olympiad team. Problem F2777–F2784 were offered to participants in the qualifying rounds of the Phystech-2024 Olympiad. The author of these problems is V. Plis.

Problem M2770–M2777, F2777–F2784

M2770. A hole in the shape of a triangle was made in a square sheet of paper of area 1 (the vertices of the hole do not extend to the border of the sheet). Prove that it is possible to cut a triangle of area$^{1}$ from the remaining paper 6. A. Yuran

M2771. For what largest N is there an N-digit number with the following property: in its decimal notation, among any several consecutive digits, some digit occurs exactly once? A. Glebov

M2772. The white squares of the chessboard $доски^{100} u^{100}$ contain 100 bishops, including white and black. They can make moves in any order and capture bishops of the opposite color. What is the smallest number of moves that is known to be sufficient to leave one bishop on the board? A. Gribalko

M2774. In a checkered square 50 u 50, each cell is painted in one of the 100 given colors so that all colors are present and it is impossible to cut a single-color domino from the square (i.e. a rectangle 1 u 2). Galia wants to recolor all the cells of one color into another color (out of the given 100 colors) so that it is still impossible to cut out a single-color domino. Is it true that Galia will definitely be able to do this? G. Sharafetdinova

M2775. Is there an infinite periodic sequence of digits for which the following condition is satisfied: for any natural number n, from this sequence of digits one can cut out (as a subword) a natural number divisible by $на^{2} n$? P. Kozhevnikov
