PHYSICS + ECOLOGY

Ecology is now a whole complex of sciences aimed at rational environmental management and protection of living organisms. Over millions of years, nature has “learned” to overcome natural changes and disasters, developing various ways of adapting to them. But in a historically short period of time, man has multiplied the negative technogenic impact on the environment, threatening the safety of all biota. This is a contribution to global warming, the growth of industrial and household waste, electromagnetic pollution, radioactive contamination, littering of near space... It is obvious that without careful and balanced application of the achievements of science itself, including physics, it is impossible to cope with the adverse consequences of scientific and technological progress. So in many school topics we discover, sometimes unexpectedly, an environmental component.
