It's interesting that...

...the term “ecology” was proposed in 1866 by the German naturalist Ernst Haeckel and initially denoted a discipline that was supposed to study the relationships of organisms and the communities they form among themselves and with the environment. ...the Earth's thermal radiation is absorbed mainly by water vapor, clouds and carbon dioxide. The relative concentration of $CO_{2}$ in the atmosphere has increased so much that over the past 100 years the average temperature of the Earth has risen by half a degree. ...another unpleasant consequence of global warming, an increase in ambient temperature, leads to a significant decrease in the efficiency of thermal power plants. …the annual production of chemical current sources amounts to many billions of units; If you turn them all on at the same time, you can get power comparable to all the power plants in the world. Their disposal is a major environmental problem. ...in 1967, under the auspices of the UN, the Outer Space Treaty was signed, which, among other things, determined the level of sterility of spacecraft to reduce the risk of contamination of other planets with terrestrial microorganisms. ...the real record holder for sensitivity among physical methods is neutron activation analysis (NAA), which makes it possible to find out not only qualitatively, but also quantitatively, how many atoms of a particular isotope are contained in a sample. With its help, it was possible to determine the concentration of toxic elements in Baikal sponges, confirming the contamination of the lake with heavy metals. ...experiments at charged particle accelerators serve, among other things, to solve problems of radiation biology associated with the implementation of long-term manned flights to the Moon and Mars, namely, to assess the radiation risk of astronauts. ...in the south of France, the largest construction project of the 21st century continues - the construction of a thermonuclear reactor, heralding the beginning of the development of clean and safe energy.
