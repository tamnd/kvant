PHYSICS + SPORTS

The history of interaction between science and sports, albeit timidly and unconsciously, dates back at least to the Olympic Games that originated in Ancient Greece. And since then, for a long time, athletes have been worried about the question: how to improve their achievements? By persistent training, developing strength and speed - so to speak, just relying on natural abilities? Or rely on the achievements of scientists, new products of inventors and engineers? Moreover, when unusual sports began to appear at first, where you can’t do without equipment and special equipment: auto, motorcycle and bicycle racing, sailing regattas, mountaineering, surfing, alpine skiing, ski jumping... And yet, what else is a person capable of? It seems that we are entering an era of merging the natural and the artificial, when new technologies can fundamentally change ideas about our capabilities, which, of course, will greatly affect sports. In the meantime, armed with the Olympic motto, let's try to find a lot of sports stories in the school physics course.
