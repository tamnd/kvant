Our tendons, playing the role of a kind of springs, return about 90% of the potential energy to kinetic energy, which leads to high efficiency of cyclic movements. The power expended by an adult varies from 80 W at rest to 10 kW during sprinting. ... already in 1671, Isaac Newton explained the deflection of a rotating ball by the occurrence of a transverse pressure difference during its flight. In 1852, a similar interpretation was given by the German physicist Heinrich Magnus, after whom this effect is named, and its mathematical theory was presented in 1877 by Lord Rayleigh. ... in 1697, the Swiss scientist Johann Bernoulli showed that the shape of the steepest descent curve in the absence of friction, which was of interest to Galileo, is a cycloid.
