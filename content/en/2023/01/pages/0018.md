Heat flows near the Earth. The average fluxes of radiant heat per unit surface are indicated; thermodynamic equilibrium is established explicitly; there is no exchange of radiation between the atmosphere and the solid surface. It's the same with Mercury. Mars has a rarefied atmosphere, but still its greenhouse effect adds its 8 q C. And to Earth it adds almost 40 q C. If our planet did not have such a dense atmosphere, the Earth’s temperature would be $на^{40} q^{С} ниже.^{Сегодня}$; it averages $15 q^{С}$ across the globe, but it would be $25 q^{С}$. All the oceans would freeze, the Earth's surface would turn white with snow, the albedo would increase, and the temperature would drop even lower. In general - a terrible thing!
