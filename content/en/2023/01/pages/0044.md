MIPT CORRESPONDENCE SCHOOL OF PHYSICS AND TECHNICAL

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (ZFTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University) (MIPT) is recruiting students in grades 7–10 of general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) to study in grades 8–11 for the 2023–24 academic year. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics, higher mathematics, the department of chemistry and the laboratory of genomic engineering at MIPT, as well as other specialists who have extensive experience working with gifted students. Training at ZFTS is conducted in physics, mathematics, computer science, chemistry and biology (six assignments per year in each subject). The full program is designed for 4 years - from 8th to 11th grades inclusive, but you can start training from any of these classes. ZFTSH has three departments: correspondence, full-time and part-time. Training in all departments is conducted according to unified natural science programs. Students receive a certificate upon completion of 11th grade. Also, graduates can add additional points when entering MIPT (detailed information is available on the website of the MIPT Admissions Committee https: pk.mipt.ru in the “List of Olympiads that count”). An online lecture on ZFTS programs is available for students and leaders of elective groups. Lectures are given by MIPT teachers. Entrance tests for all departments will be held from March 1 to May 31, 2023 in the format of an online Olympiad on the website https: zftsh.online (when registering, you must attach a certificate from the school). You can apply to study one or more subjects. You have 4 hours to complete each introductory task. The decision of the admissions committee will be known after June 5, 2023. Education for schoolchildren living in the Russian Federation is free of charge as part of the admission plan. For schoolchildren living outside the Russian Federation or who did not pass the competition, only paid education is possible. When choosing this department, the student must objectively assess his ability to attend face-to-face classes once a week lasting 1 hour 30 minutes. in grades 8-10 and 2 hours 15 minutes. in 11th grade in each subject.

INTENSIVE AND CORRESPONDENT DEPARTMENT (training in optional groups) Telephone: (498) 744-63-51 Optional groups can be organized in any educational institution in one or more subjects. Groups are formed by teachers based on lists of schoolchildren who have successfully completed the ZFTS introductory Olympiad. Classes are held at general education institutions under the guidance of teachers. When choosing this department, a student should first check with his school teachers whether this educational institution has an elective ZFTS in the subjects of interest to him. If there is no such elective and there are no plans to organize it, then admission to ZFTSH is possible only in the correspondence department. Education at ZFTSH is free for state general education institutions. For students living outside the Russian Federation, only paid education is possible.

CORRESPONDENCE DEPARTMENT (individual correspondence education) Telephone: (495) 408-51-45 This department is for those schoolchildren who do not have the opportunity to attend full-time classes in the cities of Dolgoprudny or Zhukovsky, Moscow region, or at school No. 2107 in Moscow, and for those whose schools do not have optional groups working under ZFTS programs. Training is conducted individually. Correspondence students independently study the theoretical part of each assignment, complete tests and tasks on the zftsh.online platform, and submit everything they managed to solve for verification within the prescribed time frame. Each student is assigned a teacher (student, graduate student or MIPT graduate) who checks and reviews the students’ work. After verification, students will have access to their own solutions.
