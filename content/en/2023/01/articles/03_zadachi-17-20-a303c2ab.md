17. Quantik wrote an integer on each face of the cube (all six numbers are different). Then at each vertex he wrote the sum of numbers on the three faces containing this vertex. Noutik wrote out the eight amounts received in an ascending row. Could it happen that all the differences between neighboring numbers in this series are the same? S. Polozkov

18. There is a pier, a fortress and a village in different places on the island. The straight line distance from the pier to the fortress is 3 km, from the fortress to the village – also 3 km. Petya received reliable information that a treasure was buried on the island. The straight line distances to the treasure from the pier, fortress and village are known. Petya found such a place, but found neither treasure nor traces of previous excavations. How many kilometers from the pier to the village? B.Frenkin

19. An equilateral triangle with side n is divided by segments into equilateral triangles with side 1. Let X n be the total number of resulting triangles. $Например,^{X} 2 5$ (one triangle with side 2 and four with side 1), and $X_{4}^{27}$ (see figure). Find: a) X 10; b) general formula for X n. E. Bakaev

20. Kings are placed on all black squares of the chessboard. Each move, one of them moves to a free cell on the side or diagonally. In what is the smallest total number of moves that all the kings can end up back on the black squares without a single king ending up in the square on which it originally stood? I. Akulich
