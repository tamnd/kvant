MIPT CORRESPONDENCE SCHOOL OF PHYSICS AND TECHNICAL

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (ZFTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University) (MIPT) is recruiting students in grades 7–10 of general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) to study in grades 8–11 for the 2023–24 academic year. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. The assignments are prepared by experienced teachers from the departments of general physics, higher mathematics, the department of chemistry and the laboratory of genomic engineering at MIPT, as well as other specialists who have extensive experience working with gifted students. Training at ZFTS is conducted in physics, mathematics, computer science, chemistry and biology (six assignments per year in each subject). The full program is designed for 4 years - from 8th to 11th grades inclusive, but you can start training from any of these classes. ZFTSH has three departments: correspondence, full-time and part-time. Training in all departments is conducted according to unified natural science programs. Students receive a certificate upon completion of 11th grade. Also, graduates can add additional points when entering MIPT (detailed information is available on the website of the MIPT Admissions Committee https: pk.mipt.ru in the “List of Olympiads that count”). An online lecture on ZFTS programs is available for students and leaders of elective groups. Lectures are given by MIPT teachers. Entrance tests for all departments will be held from March 1 to May 31, 2023 in the format of an online Olympiad on the website https: zftsh.online (when registering, you must attach a certificate from the school). You can apply to study one or more subjects. You have 4 hours to complete each introductory task. The decision of the admissions committee will be known after June 5, 2023. Education for schoolchildren living in the Russian Federation is free of charge as part of the admission plan. For schoolchildren living outside the Russian Federation or who did not pass the competition, only paid education is possible.
