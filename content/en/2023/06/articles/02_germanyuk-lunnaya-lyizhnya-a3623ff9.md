Lunar ski track?

E. GERMANYUK

PERHAPS MANY OF US have skied, sledded or snowboarded at least once in winter. But, as it turned out, you can ride not only on snow, but also on sand in the hot desert. This is confirmed by a large number of slides and videos with skiers somewhere in the desert of Saudi Arabia or some other place. True, this is not a descent from the snowy slopes. But many people do it quite successfully (Fig. 1, 2). Looking at all this, one might ask the question: “Is this possible, say, on the Moon?” Well, of course, not slide down a high mountain, but just ride a sleigh from the moon

Secondly, another inconvenient point is that people on the Moon are in massive, clumsy spacesuits. (Fig. 7). Thus, the total mass of the spacesuit for the Apollo astronauts was more than 90 kg. The weight of a person in a spacesuit on the Moon is 6 times less than on Earth, but nevertheless, no one has canceled the mass and inertia. If you fall, it will not be very easy to get up. In addition, the center of gravity of a person in a spacesuit is shifted relative to the center of gravity of a person without a spacesuit. As a result, the astronaut has to stand with a slight inclination to the surface of the Moon. All this makes moving an astronaut on skis almost impossible and even dangerous.
