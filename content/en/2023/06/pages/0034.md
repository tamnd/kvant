PHYSICS + MEDICINE

Unusually intensive progress in medicine is happening before our eyes, increasingly absorbing the latest achievements not only of digital technologies, but also of fundamental physical discoveries. Clinics sometimes resemble interiors from science fiction films about laboratories of the future, striking with the abundance of diagnostic and therapeutic equipment, and the “people in white coats” working with it - the research scientists. Radiography, laser scalpels, proton therapy, ultrasound, MRI... - this list is only growing, only emphasizing the growing role of science in the noble cause of healing, preserving health and prolonging human life. We all had (and still have!) to turn to doctors one way or another. Let's try to look at the area of ​​our interaction with them from the stages of a school physics course.
