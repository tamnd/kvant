At the first stage of the Project, it was not the scientific questions themselves that were decisive, but the organization of work in all areas. The main areas of work were: i the creation of special geological departments for the search for uranium ores and the organization of work on the extraction of these ores at the discovered deposits; i creation of technologies for producing ultra-pure uranium and construction of chemical plants for this purification; i scientific development of several technologies for extracting the uranium-235 isotope from natural uranium and construction of plants for this purpose; i creation of technology for producing an artificial fissile element (plutonium) from natural uranium and construction of corresponding plants; i the creation of a special design bureau for scientific and technical work on the manufacture of the “products” themselves, as atomic (nuclear) bombs began to be called in all secret documents; i creation of special measuring equipment and a test site for nuclear weapons. In all these areas, Kurchatov selected the most competent and active leaders, administrative and scientific. And he himself became not only a physicist, but also a geologist, a chemist, and a builder. His word was decisive in determining the timing of the work and the need for material and financial costs. And these costs were unusually high. The ultra-high degree of purification of uranium and plutonium and the low content of the required isotope in natural uranium required repeated (thousand-fold) repetition of the same technological operations.
