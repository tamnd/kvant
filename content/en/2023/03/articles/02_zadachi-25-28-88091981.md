25. Is it possible to color all natural numbers in three colors so that the sum of any two numbers of different colors is colored in a third color? F.Nilov

26. Many identical quadrangular tables were brought to the office, each table has all sides of different lengths. It turned out that 3 such tables, and 4, and 5, can be placed in a circle with equal angles to the center so that there is no gap between adjacent tables. How many of these tables can be placed in a circle with the same sides facing out and without gaps between adjacent tables? List all options and prove that there are no others. E. Bakaev

27. What is greater - the number of ways to fill a table of 2 and 100 with the numbers 1, 2, ... ...200 (each number appears once) so that the sum of the numbers in each of the 102 rows (rows and columns) is even, or so that each such sum is odd? E. Bakaev, P. Kozhevnikov
