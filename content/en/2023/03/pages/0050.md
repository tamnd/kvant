Problem 4. Catch-up Two cars simultaneously start moving from point A to point B along a straight road. It is known that the first car travels at a constant speed $v_{1}$ without stopping until point B. At point B, it stops and waits for the arrival of the second car. The second car moves with a constant speed $v_{2}$, less than $v_{1}$. After some time, he stops and, when the first car reaches point B, he continues moving again at the same speed $v_{2}$. figure 3 shows a graph of the distance between cars versus time up to the moment $t_{1} 4$ min. At time $t_{2} 14$ minutes the cars met. 1) Determine the speeds of cars $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$. 2) For how long did the second car rest? 3) Find the distance L between points A and B. A. Evseev
