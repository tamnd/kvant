Problem 5 (E. Bakaev, LXXIX Moscow Mathematical Olympiad, 2016). On the median AM of triangle ABC there is a point K such that AK BM. In addition, AMC 60 q. Prove that AC BK. Solution. The presence of an angle of 60q and many equal segments clearly hint at the construction of an auxiliary equilateral triangle. The classic solution is to extend the median AM and construct the triangle BMD (Fig. 5), but the equilateral triangle EMC is also useful. Indeed, regardless of the location of points K and E on AM, the segments AE and KM are equal either as the sum or as the difference in the lengths of the segments ME AK and the segment EK.
