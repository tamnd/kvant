M2744. A regular 100-gon was cut into several parallelograms and two triangles. Prove that these triangles are congruent.

Let's choose an arbitrary direction and then consider it horizontal. For each parallelogram with horizontal sides, color the top side blue and the bottom side red. We will do the same with all the existing horizontal sides of the triangles (if the triangle is below the side, paint it blue, otherwise - red). And if the 100-gon has horizontal sides, then we’ll paint them the other way around: the top one red and the bottom blue. Now each horizontal segment on figure is colored once in blue (“bottom”) and once in red (“above”), so we spent equal amounts of blue and red. But in each parallelogram, and in our 100-gon, the colors blue and red were used equally. Therefore, if you erase them and leave only two triangles, then they also contain equal amounts of blue and red. In other words, if one has a blue horizon
