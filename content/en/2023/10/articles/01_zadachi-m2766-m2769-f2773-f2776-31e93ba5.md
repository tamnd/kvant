PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problems M2766, M2768 were proposed at the training camp for candidates for the team for the International Mathematical Olympiad. Problem F2773 was offered at the International School Olympiad “Tuymaada” in 2023, and problem F2774-F2776 in 2022.

Problem M2766–M2769, F2773–F2776

M2766. Given a natural number n. The “Master Sudoku” field represents $собой^{n} n 1 2$ cells $квадрата^{n} u^{n}$ lying on or below the main diagonal. The master guesses n cells of this field, one in each column, and tells the student about each row how many hidden cells are in it (the student is also aware that there is exactly one hidden cell in each column). The student should be able to use this information to reliably restore the cells hidden by the Master. How many different types of Sudoku can a Master create? T. Amdeberhan, M. Rubi, F. Petrov

M2767. It is easy to prove that in a right triangle, the sum of the radii of the inscribed and three excircles is equal to the perimeter. Prove that the converse is also true. I. Weinstein

M2768*. Given a natural number n. Pairwise distinct non-zero integers $a_{1},^{…}, a n$ are such that the number $x_{1}^{a} x_{2}^{a} …^{…} x^{a} n$ is divisible by $число_{1}^{a} a_{2}^{…} a n$ for any $целом^{x.}$ Find the largest possible value of a n. F. Petrov, K. Sukhov
