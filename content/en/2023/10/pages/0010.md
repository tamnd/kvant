Moreover, two situations are possible: when the capacitor is ultimately controlled (directly or through voltage) by a person and when the control signal is created by a device or circuit. In the second case, the stability of the parameters of these capacitors (for example, with temperature changes) can be no less important than for permanent capacitors. If a person controls, the situation may be simpler; temporary stability and weak dependence on temperature may be less important.
