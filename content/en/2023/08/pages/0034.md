PHYSICS + PAINTING

Oh, these artists! How many riddles they unwittingly posed to scientists who were trying to figure out how and why the artists’ creations affected viewers. For centuries there have been debates about how to comprehend binocular vision, whether there is a rational basis for the methods of spatial artistic constructions, what are the features of visual perception, what is such an unusual category as color... The achievements of science, and not least physics, the provision of increasingly sophisticated research equipment have more than once made it possible to advance in the understanding of problems that are sometimes inaccessible to natural scientific analysis. However, what interesting discoveries were made along this thorny path! Join us on a fascinating scientific and artistic excursion.
