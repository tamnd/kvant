SUBSCRIBE TO THE MAGAZINE ANNOUNCED
"QUANT" FOR 1971

«Kvant» is a popular science physics and mathematics journal of the USSR Academy of Sciences and the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences. It is intended primarily for schoolchildren in grades 8–10. Among its readers are many teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics and mathematics, as well as students of pedagogical institutes. Older physics and mathematics lovers also read it with interest.

«Kvant» publishes articles and notes devoted to modern problems of science, as well as many different materials that expand and deepen school courses in physics and mathematics.

It contains a wealth of information for those preparing to enter college.

The regular sections of the magazine - "Quent's Problem Book", "Quant's Laboratory", "Applicant's Workshop" - contain many interesting tasks and home experiments.

«Kvant» provides its readers with regular information about new books, about the work of physics and mathematics schools and clubs.

Among our authors are famous Soviet and foreign scientists, young scientists, and teachers. Schoolchildren can also report on their first discoveries, laboratory work or independently constructed devices on the pages of our magazine.

Subscription starts on September 1st. The price of the room is 30 kopecks. The cost of an annual subscription is 3 rubles 60 kopecks. Subscription index 70465.
