If the ledge is in an even area, $(n-1):2$ of the wheels will roll over it. If the protrusion is located in an odd area, $(n+1):2$ wheels. Option 1, task 3. After closing the key $K_1$ and $K_2$ (the key $K_3$ is open) on the upper plates of the capacitors $C_1$ and $C_3$ the charges will be $-C_1E_1$ and $C_3E_2$, respectively. Solving the system of equations (1) and (2), we get the answer.
Option 2, problem 5. figure shows the forces acting on the block and the wedge. The equation of motion of a body along the axes of a fixed coordinate system $xOy$ associated with iron will be written as follows:
$$ma_{1x}=N\sin\alpha,$$
$$ma_{1y}=N\cos\alpha,$$
$$Ma=F-N\sin\alpha,$$
$$a_{1y}=tg\alpha\cdot(a-a_{1x}).$$

In these equations $a_{1x}$, $a_{1y}$ are the accelerations of the wedge along the axes $x$ and $y$, $a$ is the desired acceleration of the block and $N$ is the force taking the block and the wedge. Equation (4) relating the acceleration of the wedge relative to the block. On figure the dotted line shows the positions of the block and the wedge at time $t$ after the start of the movement, as well as the initial position of the wedge. The segments $h$ and $l$ represent the components of the movement of the wedge relative to the block. The values ​​of these segments are related to the relations $h=ltg\alpha$. Substituting $h=\frac{1}{2}a_{1y}t^2$ and $l=\frac{1}{2}(a-a_{1x})t^2$ into it, we obtain equation (4). Solving the system of equations (1) - (4) for $a$, we find the answer.
