Faculty of Philology

1. There are three pipes installed into the pool through which water flows into it. The first and second pipes together fill the pool 35/18 hours faster than the first and third pipes together fill it in 10 hours. What part of the pool does the second pipe fill per hour if it alone takes $\frac{7}{5}$ times longer to fill than the first pipe alone?
2. Solve the equation:
$$\log_{\sin x} \left( \sin x - \frac{1}{4} \cos x \right) = 3.$$
3. Solve inequality:
$$2\log_3 x - \log_1/5 (4 - x) \leq \log_3 (x - 1)^2 + 2\log_2 (10 - x).$$
4. N people exchanged handshakes. How many handshakes were there?
5. Indicate at least one pair of positive integers $k_1$ and $k_2$ such that $36 \cdot k_1 - 25 \cdot k_2 = 1$.
6. Prove, without using tables, that $\log_4 9 > \log_5 11$.
7. Does a triangle have an obtuse angle whose sides are 10, 14 and 17 respectively?

Faculty of Psychology

1. A sphere contains a diameter $AB$ and two equal chords $AM$ and $AN$, each at an angle $a$ to the diameter. Find the angle between the chords if the segment $MN$ is visible from the center of the ball at an angle $\beta$.
2. Find all positive numbers $a$ for which all distinct non-negative values ​​$x$ satisfying the equation $\cos [(8a-3)x] = \cos [(14a+5)x]$ and arranged in ascending order form an arithmetic progression.
3. Prove, without using decimals, that $\log_3 16 > \log_16 729$.
4. The distance between stations $A$ and $B$ is 360 km. At the same time, two trains leave from $A$ and from $B$ towards each other. Trains left $A$ and arrive at station $B$ no earlier than 5 hours later. If his speed had been 1.5 times higher than it actually was, then he would have met the second train more than 2 hours after he left $A$. Which train is faster?
5. Prove that
$$2x^2 + 5y^2 + 3z^2 - 6xy - 2xz + 5yz > 0,$$
if $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 > 0$.

Department of Political Economy

1. Five camping tents and twenty stopley sleeping bags 490 RUR. After a ten percent discount on one tent and a five percent discount on one bag, for the same number of tents and bags you need to pay 457 rubles. How much is one stop for one tent and how much is one stop for one bag?
2. Solve inequality: $x^2 + x - 10 < 2/x - 2$.
3. In an acute triangle $ABC$, angle $A$ is equal to $a$, side $AC$ is equal to $b$. From the vertex $B$ the median $m$ is drawn. Calculate the area of ​​triangle $ABC$.
4. Solve the equation: $(3 - \text{ctg}^2 x)\sin 2x = 2(1 + \cos 2x)$.
5. Find the maximum value of the fraction $\frac{3x - 2}{4x - x^2 - 6}$ if $x$ can take any real value.

Department of Economic Library

1. Solve the equation
$$\left( 4\sqrt{\cos \frac{x}{2}} - 5 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)^2 + \sqrt{2} \left( 4\sqrt{\cos \frac{x}{2}} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - 5 \right) - \frac{\cos x}{2} = 0.$$
2. There are four art clubs at the pioneer camp. Give each member of the circle the same set of colors. In the first and fourth circles together, and in the second and fourth circles together there are even more pioneers than in the first and third circles together. There are no more than ten pioneers in the third circle. How many pioneers are in each circle, if the price in rubles of one paint is equal to the number of members of the first circle divided by ten, and 52 rubles were paid for paints? 80 kopecks?
3. A circle is inscribed in the trapezoid $ABCD$. Find the difference in the lengths of the diagonal segments $AC$ located in a circle if the height of the trapezoid is $h$, and the angles at the base $AD$ are $a$ and $\delta$.
