The force of gravity in this two-dimensional world behaves differently than in our space. The only difference is that it changes in inverse proportion to the distance between the bodies, and not the square of the distance, as in the world we are accustomed to.

Astria rotates around an axis, like any planet. For example, in which it rotates is called east, the opposite is called west. Hinton seemed to place his flat actriyans (inhabitants of Astria) on the “rim” of the planet. They can mix and remain in the plane of the Astria disk. Trees grow and houses stand in the same plane. Therefore, when encountering a tree, the actor must either climb over it or surround it. In order for one to pass the other, two inhabitants of the planet must move either one under the other, or jump over each other, as two acrobats would do on a tight rope.
