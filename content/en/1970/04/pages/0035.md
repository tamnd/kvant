4. Two flat mirrors $3_1$ and $3_2$ (Fig. 2) are installed close to each other at an angle $a=15^\circ$ to the vertical. A thin converging lens is placed at a distance of $L=8,3$ cm from the mirror connection line so that its main optical axis is perpedicular to the mirror connection line. Between the mirrors and the lens on the main optical axis of the lens at a distance of $R=2$ cm from the mirror connection line there is a point light source $H$. Determine the distance between the images of the light source that are obtained using the lens. The focal length of the lens is $f=8$ cm. The partition $K$ protects the lens from rays and light from the light source.

5. A ball $A$ of mass $m$, fixed on a long weightless spoke, forms a pendulum, which can oscillate, curing around the axis $O$ (Fig. 3). The ball $A$ is deflected to a height $h$ and released without initial velocity. At the bottom point of its trajectory, the ball $A$ is contained with two balls $B$ moving towards it, the mass of which is $M$. The ball $B$, like the ball $A$, is fixed on a long weightless knitting needle, moving around the axis $O_1$. Immediately before the collision, the velocities of the balls are directed along the lines connecting their centers. What speed $v$ must the ball $^1$ $B$ have before the collision so that the ball $A$ rises to the same height $h$ after the collision? The impact is absolutely elastic. Friction in wasps and ovozdux should not be taken into account.
