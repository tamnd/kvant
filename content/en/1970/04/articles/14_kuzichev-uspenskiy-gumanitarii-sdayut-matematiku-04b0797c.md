HUMANITIES
RENTAL
MATHEMATICS

Given the already tired division into “physicists” and “lyricists,” “physicists” include not only physicists, but also chemists, engineers, biologists, geologists, mathematicians, and “lyricists” include not only lyrical posers, but also philologists, social scientists and, in general, representatives of the humanities.
The complexity of the idea of ​​​​two polar categories of schoolchildren - one fascinating chemistry, physics, mathematics and actively participate in technical modeling classes; other topics of interest include history, art, psychology and are published in school literary magazines. The first should go to colleges, as well as physico-mathematical and chemical-biological faculties; the second - to institutes and faculties of humanitarian professionals.
However, when high school students are thinking about choosing a place for further study, they should not forget that the last decades have seen the deep penetration of mathematics into many branches of knowledge traditionally considered “humanitarian.” Suffice it to say that mathematics is taught today at the faculties of economics and psychology and at the department of structure and applied linguistics of the philological faculty of Moscow University.
This is not the place to talk about the role of mathematics in humanities research, or at least about the role of mathematical disciplines in teaching at these faculties of Moscow State University. In this article we present only the problems in mathematics proposed at the humanities faculties of Moscow State University at the written entrance exams in August 1969. However, the versions of the written tasks partly reflect the requirements for the mathematical preparation of those entering certain faculties and departments.
