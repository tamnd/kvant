RELEASED BY THE MSU PUBLISHING HOUSE, the book “Introduction to Electronics” by candidate of physical and mathematical sciences A. M. Khazen is addressed to schoolchildren who are interested in physics, mathematics and especially electronics and radio engineering.

The book is intended for the general reader and at the same time written on a good physical level. The clarity of the presentation and the original examples given are combined with sufficient rigor. It can be said with certainty that for a schoolchild who reads this book carefully, many of the understandings of electronics will become clear.

Young readers will be interested to know that a fundamental vacuum diode can be used to create an electric current generator operating from an external source of the body, how a linear electron accelerator and a traveling wave tube are similar and different, what an explosive magnetic generator is, etc.

The book highlights key issues for various classes of electronic devices. Despite the large number of specific devices described in the book, it is not overloaded with examples.

The books can be recommended as a guide for schoolchildren who have chosen technical disciplines or physics as their future specialization.
