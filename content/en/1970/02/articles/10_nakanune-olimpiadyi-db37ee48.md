⟦folio 46⟧

The day before
olympids

For those who take part in the final section of the All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren, April turns out to be “hot”. It is in this place that the event will take place: young mathematicians - in Simferopol (from 23 to 29), physicists - Sverdlovsk, chemists - Voronezh (from 8 to 15). Four representatives from each of the region, regional and resource Olympids, eight Muscovites, six Leningraders, guys who took first and second places at the previous Olympiad, and four from special physics and mathematics interns will come here.

The very next day after the grand opening of the Olympiad, its business part begins - all participants will be charged for written work in their “specialty”. They will have to solve 4-5 problems each. Physicists and chemists, in addition, will have an experimental tour on April 12th.

The most pleasant and easiest day is the closing of the Olympiad and the presentation of awards.

Thirteen graduating class students - eight mathematicians and five physicists, who successfully performed at the All-Union Olympiad, will receive tickets to the next international Olympiad this summer.

The magazine «Kvant» will constantly inform its readers about everything that happens on these modern ones.
