F9. On the horizontal table there is a weight attached to the table using a long spring. At first the spring was not stretched. The weight is then moved 20 cm from its equilibrium position and released. The weight began to oscillate along the spring. Due to friction, the amplitude of its oscillations per period decreases by 7%. How many oscillations will the weight make before stopping? At what distance from the equilibrium position will it stop?
