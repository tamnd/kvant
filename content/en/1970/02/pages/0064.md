Answers to questions about physics

1. Due to its high specific heat, water warms up more slowly than air. Therefore it is colder than air. When you get out of the water, the droplets of water remaining on your body will evaporate. Absorbing a lot of heat, they take it away not only from the surrounding air, but also from the body. The body cools and the air begins to seem colder than the water.

2. Most products contain water. As it evaporates, it freezes on the coldest part of the refrigerator - the evaporator. Therefore, the evaporator is covered with a thick snow coat, which has low thermal conductivity. This leads to a decrease in heat flow from the chamber, and the temperature in the refrigerator does not drop enough.
