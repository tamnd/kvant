Option 1

1. Points A and B are on roads intersecting at an angle ACB=60°. You can get from point A to B by bus - first along one road to intersection C, then along the other - spending 11 minutes. If you go from A to B in the right direction, it will take 1 hour. 10 minutes, and if you first take the shortest route to the road on which point B is located, and then take the bus, then even more time, even if there is a bus right away.

What is the distance from point A to the intersection if the pedestrian speed is 3 km/h and the bus speed is 30 km/h? (Roads are considered straight.)

2. In triangle ABC, bissertrices AD and BE are drawn, intersecting at point O. It is known that segment OE has length 1, and vertex C lies on the circle passing through points E, D, O. Find the sides and angles of triangle EDO.

3. Find all values ​​of x that simultaneously satisfy the following conditions:

$$\cos 13x = \cos x,$$
$$\cos 2x + \sin 5x = 1,$$
$$|x| < 3.$$

4. The primordial processes of a plane quadrilateral into two mutually perpendicular planes are squares with sides of 2. Find the perimeter of the quadrilateral, knowing that one of its sides is equal to $\sqrt{5}$.
