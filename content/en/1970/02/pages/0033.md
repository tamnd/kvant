Let us present the results of one of Perrin's experiments. The depth of the cuvette was, as we have already said, 100 microns (that is, 0.1 mm). Readings were made at heights of 5, 35, 65 and 95 microns above the level of the bottom of the cuvette. It turned out that the average part number at 35 microns was half that at 5 microns, the part number at 65 microns was half the part number at 35 microns, and the part number at 95 microns was half the part number at 65 microns.

So: if the heights form an arithmetic cut, then the numbers of grains form a geometric cut.
