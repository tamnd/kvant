The more ordered the directions of the magnetic moments of radioactive atomic nuclei, that is, the higher the degree of their polarization, the more clearly the difference in the intensity of the gamma ray emitted by such nuclei in different directions is manifested. But gold is not a ferromagnetic material, and the magnetic field of even very strong magnets cannot cause a noticeable orientation of its atomic nuclei.

However, B. N. Samoilov and V. V. Sklyarevsky showed that if radioactive gold is included in a ferromagnetic substance, such as iron, the resulting alloy is cooled to an ultra-low temperature (below 0.3 ° K) and placed in a strong magnetic field, then the production of gamma radiation disappears. Gold nuclei “more willingly” emit gamma rays in some specific directions. Later it turned out that the atoms of any element, once in a ferromagnetic material, are influenced by the polarized atoms of the ferromagnet. They are affected by magnetic fields of up to a million oersteds, causing ordering in the orientation of any atoms. Thanks to this, it will be possible to carry out studies with the nuclei of non-magnetic elements on a number of physical effects (the so-called superfine splittings), which were previously carried out only on the ordered orientation of the nuclei of magnetic elements.

This discovery makes it possible to polarize the nuclei of many atoms and obtain unique information about the atomic nuclei themselves, the electron shells of atoms and the properties of solids. Using it, one can already determine many values ​​of the magnetic moments of excited atomic nuclei.

The State Prize was also awarded to two famous Leningrad mathematicians O. A. Ladyzhenskaya and N. N. Uraltseva “for a series of works on boundary value problems for linear and quasalinear parabolic equations”, published in 1962-1967.

Parabolic equations are one of the types of mathematical physics equations that explore the most important problems in the natural and technical. They describe a large group of physical phenomena associated with the spread of bodies, diffusion of various substances, changes in their concentration and other processes in which the values ​​of physical quantities (temperature, concentration, etc.) are equalized.

The works of O. A. Ladyzhenskaya and N. N. Uraltseva considered the main problems of solving various equations of parabolic type and obtained important results that make a significant contribution to the general theory of differential equations.
