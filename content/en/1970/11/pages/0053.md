ALL-UNION CORRESPONDENCE OLYMPINAD

Olympiad conditions

We publish the texts of the problems of the All-Union Correspondence Olympiad in mathematics, physics and chemistry for students of schools and other secondary educational institutions. This Olympid, which has already become traditional, is an integral part of the All-Union Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry Olympids.

Those who successfully cope with the tasks of the correspondence Olympiad will be invited to the regional (territorial, resource) Olympiads on an equal basis with the winner of the regional Olympiads. Invitations will be sent to public education organizations at home addresses. The best of the winners of regional and resource competitions will take part in the final type of the Weight-Union Olympiad, and will also be invited to the summer school of the Siberian branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences.

Before each problem, the classes for whose students it is offered are indicated in brackets, but you can also try your hand at problems for older classes. To become an Olympiad winner, you don’t have to solve many problems. Sometimes a witty solution to one is enough. But solving a problem doesn’t just mean giving an answer; it is necessary to justify it, to give precise evidence. The assignment does not require any knowledge beyond the high school curriculum. Solutions to the problem for each subject must be sent in a separate standard format envelope. On the envelope indicate the name of the item. The letter must include a double stamped envelope with a return home address and a stamp (in this envelope you will be sent an extract about the results of the work). Solutions must be written very legibly. The conditions of the tasks are not rewritten, but their numbers are indicated.

At the beginning of the letter, do not forget to indicate (legibly):
1. Your last name, first name and patronymic.
2. Postal home address (detailed).
3. Class, school (school, technical, etc.), address.
4. Last name, first name and patronymic of your teacher in this subject.

Aftereffect in Siberia, Kazakhstan, the Far East and Central Asia: Novosibirsk, 90, Olympida;
- living in the Karal and Komi Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republics, in the Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Kaliningrad, Kirov, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod and Pskov regions of the RSFSR: Leningrad, V-164, Leningrad State University, Olympida;
- residents of the rest of the territory of the European part of the RSFSR, including the Urals: Moscow, V-234, Moscow State University, Olympida;
- residents of the Estonian SSR: Tartu State University, Olympida;
- those living in other resource areas - to the university of the capital of their resource area, marked “Olympida”.
The Central Organizing Committee appeals to all parents, teachers of mathematics, physics, chemistry and school directors with a request to familiarize themselves with the conditions of the tasks of high school students.

We wish all Olympiad participants the greatest success.

Central organizing committee of the Weight-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren
