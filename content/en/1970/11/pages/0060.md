Let $V$ be the volume of pyramid $ABCD$. When calculating the volume $V_1$ of the pyramid $A_1B_1MD$, it is advisable to take face $A_1B_1D$ as its base. then the height $H_M$ will be half the height $H_C$, since $M$ is the midpoint of the segment $CD$. We have:

$$V_1 = \frac{1}{3} S_{\triangle A_1B_1D} H_M = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{9}{16} S_{\triangle ABD} \times \frac{H_C}{2} = \frac{9}{32} \left( \frac{1}{3} S_{\triangle ABD} H_C \right) = \frac{9}{32} V.$$

The volume of the bottom of the pyramid $V''$ will be $\frac{23}{32}$ of the volume of the pyramid $ABCD$. Now there are objects at the top and bottom of the pyramid: $V' : V'' = 9:23$.
