the reverse process is the restoration of the visual cortex. Rod, twilight vision has very high sensitivity, but it is achromatic. Rods do not sense color. At sufficiently high brightness, the cone mechanism of vision comes into force. Cone vision is color sensitive. There are many convincing experiments leading to the conclusion that cones contain three types of photochemical sensitive pigments with maximum sensitivity in the red, green and blue regions of the spectrum. Varying degrees of expression of all three pigments give our consciousness the sensation of color and allow us to see the world in all its diversity of colors, shades, halftones and transitions.
