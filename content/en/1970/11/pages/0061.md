By placing a review by Professor A.F. Khrustalev on one of the textbooks in mathematics for university applicants and fully agree with his negative assessment, the editors want to draw attention to the fact that in recent years the number of such manuals has sharply increased, which make the tasks of specific exams seem like a special “science”, mastering which becomes impossible without special instruction; This often has a bad effect on the practice of school teaching of mathematics, forces the teacher to adapt to such competitive inventions, which are promised to have nothing to do with the actual requirements for the mathematical training of future students, diverge from the spirit of the new school curriculum in mathematics, and often turn out to be lower in scientific terms.

A. F. Khrustalev

Increasing knowledge requirements for those entering higher education institutions has led to a great demand for teaching aids for in-depth study of secondary school disciplines, in particular mathematics. The average editions of these publications have high pedagogical merits and are distinguished by successful preparation of material. However, sadly, publishers and books are written carelessly, with significant logical flaws, and sometimes gross errors. Their authors, it is believed, do not feel the great responsibility that they take upon themselves when speaking to young people preparing for entrance exams with ill-conceived recommendations.

The Leningrad organization of the Knowledge Society has published a manual on mathematics*) intended for those entering higher technical educational institutions. As indicated in the preface, the authors of the book pay great attention to the key point of the theory, indicating methods and techniques for solving multiple examples and problems of increased difficulty.

We will not discuss now some of the general methodological guidelines of the authors or the advisability of including certain sections in the manual (especially in connection with their inconsistency with the entrance exam program and the level of requirements for applicants). Very serious complaints about the book are caused by the rather numerous inaccuracies and even mistakes made by the authors when solving many examples.
