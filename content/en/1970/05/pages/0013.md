The meter was conceived as a natural unit of length - one forty-millionth of the earth's meridine. But over time, it ceased to be such a unit. The circumference of the Earth was clarified, the platinum rod was changed, and now the sample meter is not one forty millionth of the circumference of the globe, but simply some fixed length. Therefore, there was a need to return to a natural standard that occurs in nature and is easily reproducible. Otherwise there would be a terrible mess if suddenly the international meter stage was lost, cleaned or damaged for some reason. In addition, the existing stage did not provide length measurements with the accuracy required by science and technology in need.
