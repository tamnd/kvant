⟦folio 52⟧

THE NEXT TIME IS COMING FOR ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS TO HIGHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS OF THE COUNTRY. It is typical that year after year applicants become better prepared to join the ranks of the student body. In this, they are greatly assisted by the teaching staff of the school, part-time and correspondence preparatory courses operating at a large number of colleges, and various lecture halls organized by organizational institutes together with branches of the Knowledge Society... The opportunities for current applicants to strengthen their knowledge are extensive, but, unfortunately, soldiers of the Soviet Army, students in rural areas and some working youth are deprived of some of these opportunities. In such cases, television can come to the rescue.

For several years now, the training program studio of Central Television has been providing assistance to applicants entering universities. At the end of the academic year, television preparatory mathematics courses were created for the first time. They started working in October 1969. The course program consistently covers all the main sections of elementary mathematics within the scope of the requirements for applicants in the entrance exams. Qualified higher education staff are involved in teaching the courses.

The course work is structured as follows. On the one hand, teachers give lectures on each topic, conduct practical classes on solving problems, organize consultations, give out test papers, analyze the test work after a certain period of time, analyzing typical mistakes made by cases when solving a particular problem, answer numerous letters, give out homework, and monitor the current educational work of each service.

On the other hand, course students are absent daily according to the established form (coupons) for attendance at the next television lesson and for completing the corresponding homework, and participate in tests (completed tests are sent to the studio for checking). In their letters to the studio, they ask questions of both organizational and mathematical nature, express various kinds of wishes, recommendations, suggestions on the essence of the material being studied and the organization of classes.

After each regular program, the listener, having completed his homework, responds to the studio with a coupon in which he reports the result of his work. These results are assessed in points according to a specially developed rating scale and are simply added to the listener’s record card. The submitted test work must be checked and evaluated in points, and, in addition to the results of solving problems, self-study in the test work is assessed. The scorecard shows the level of activity of the listener.
