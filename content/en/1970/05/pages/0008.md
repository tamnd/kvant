The main characteristics are the speed of performing arithmetic operations and the amount of memory. The first Soviet electronic computers BESM, M-2, Strela, Ural had a speed of 200-2000 arithmetic operations per second and a memory capacity of 1024-2048 cells. These machines were tube machines and had several thousand vacuum tubes each.

Nibaulier distributed machines of the late sixties are BESM-4, M-220, Minsk-22, Razdan-3 with speeds of about 25,000 arithmetic operations per second and a RAM capacity of 8192-32,768 cells. The BESM-6 machine, with a RAM of 32,768 cells, already has a speed of up to a million arithmetic operations per second. In these machines, vacuum tubes have already been completely replaced by semiconductor elements (transistors), which have a significantly smaller volume and require significantly less power.

The use of transistors has made it possible to create small-sized desktop electronic keyboard machines that replace electromechanical ones. They are distinguished by high speed and complete noiselessness, which is very important for large computing laboratories. Therefore, desktop keyboard electronic machines are beginning to replace electromechanical ones. Our cars “Vyatka”, “Iskra”, “Orbita” and some others have gained wide popularity.

The first successes in the creation of computers made it possible to use them for new special tasks of various kinds.

It is not very difficult, at least theoretically, to “build” a computer into a control one. Let's consider the problem of controlling some technological process, for example, the process of producing a chemical substance in a special reactor.

The course of the process is determined by the state inside the reactor: the concentration of the starting substances and the resulting substance, temperature and pressure inside the reactor, etc. Process control usually consists of changing controlled parameters, such as feed rate and temperature. In manual control, the operator, based on instrument readings, determines the state of the process at a given moment and, having determined the values ​​of the adjustable parameters that are most advantageous for a given state, brings them to the required values.
