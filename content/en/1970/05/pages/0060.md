"mathematical analysis"*)

M. L. SMOLYANSKY

The rapid development of computer technology, the introduction of high-speed computers in all areas of the national economy, science and technology, has led to the emergence of new specialties. One of them is the specialty “computer programmer”. And as the fleet of computers increases, the need for programmers, computers and other specialists servicing these machines will constantly increase. By preparing the program and computing, both computing educational institutions and technical ones are remembered. They are also prepared in some schools. Now there are already several dozens, if not hundreds, of schools and classes in which mathematics is taught at an advanced level. However, complete teaching in these schools is greatly hampered by the lack of textbooks specifically designed for such schools. The schoolchild had to either use books written for calculating educational institutions, or limit himself to the material given in school during lessons.

Now two books have been published: “Algebra”**) and a solved book, the main goal of which is to correct this situation. Both of these books form an inseparable whole.

*) N. Ya. Vilenkin, S. I. Shvartsburg, Mathematical analysis, textbook for grades IX, X of secondary schools with special mathematics, “Prosveshchenie”, 1969.
**) N. Ya. Vilenkin, R. S. Guter, S. I. Schwartzburg, B. V. Ovchinsky and V. G. Ashkinez, Algebra, “Enlightenment”, 1968

Who are these books addressed to? Of course, first of all, the authors had in mind students from schools with a mathematical specialization. But not only they will benefit from these books. All students whose interests extend beyond the scope of the school curriculum will learn a lot from them. Both high school mathematics teachers and pedagogical students will find a lot of materials in it.

Books presenting an advanced course in mathematics can be written in different ways. You can remove special attention to complex algebraic transformations, confusing trionometric identities, and solving globe-breaking equations. On the other hand, it is possible to present without significant changes the mathematical disciplines that are covered in the calculations of educational information. The authors of the book “Mathematical Analysis” take a different path. Of course, after studying this book, the student will learn to solve trionometric and exponential equations, and prove various trionometric identities, and differentiate and interpret various functions. But, unlike most books on calculus for students, the solved book puts a lot of emphasis on clarifying the meaning of the basic concepts that students encounter.

The book begins with a presentation of the theory of active numbers. It is possible that the authors devote too much space to this issue. But without a clear understanding of what a real number is, it is impossible to fully understand what a limit is, or even what a limit is.
