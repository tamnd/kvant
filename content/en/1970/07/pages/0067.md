A magic square is a square drawn into cells, each of which contains a number so that the sums of the numbers in any row, in any column and in both diagonals of the square are equal.

This 6x6 magic square can be made from the green 3x3 squares that are visible at figure. (Check!).

The task is to fill the cells of a 3x3 red square so that:
a) the red square was magical;
b) the entire large 9x9 square was also magical.
