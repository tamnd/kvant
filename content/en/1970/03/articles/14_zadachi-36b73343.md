M13. Prove that if the difference between the largest and smallest $n$ real numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ is $d$, and the sum of the models of all $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ pairwise differences of these numbers

$$\sum_{i<j} |a_i - a_j|$$

is equal to $s$, then

$$(n-1)d \leqslant s \leqslant \frac{n^2}{4}d.$$

a) more than half of black faces;
b) the area of ​​the black edges is more than half the area of ​​half the surface of the polygon,

then a ball cannot be inscribed in this polygon.

M15. A square table is filled with $n \times n$ non-negative numbers so that the sum of the numbers in each row and the sum of the numbers in each column is 1. Prove that from the table you can select $n$ positive numbers, no two of which appear in any column or row.

F12. Two identical heavy steel balls rotate on light rods of length $l$ and $2l$ around a point $O_1$, and $O$ the distance between which is $3l$ (Fig. 3). At the initial moment, the balls are at points $A$ and $B$, having speeds $v$ and $2v$, respectively. How many times will the balls collide in time $t$? How long will it take for the balls to collide $k$ times? The impacts of the balls are considered to be absolutely elastic.

G. L. Kotkin

F13. Two identical cylinders with pistons are connected by a tube (Fig. 4). There is water in the cylinders. Identical cylindrical glasses with equal amounts of water are placed on top of the pistons. Then a body of mass $m$ is lowered into one of the glasses, and a body of mass $M$, which do not sink, is lowered into the other.

At what distances from each other will the ends of the pistons and the water levels in the glasses be when the system reaches equilibrium? The day area of ​​the glasses is $S_1$, the area of ​​the pistons is $S_2$.

F14. Two pipes with sections $S_1$ and $S_2$ are connected to each other and plugged with pistons whose masses are $m_1$ and $m_2$ (Fig. 5). After an explosion in the space between the pistons, the pistons fly out of the pipes. One of them flew out at a speed of $v$. At what speed did the second one fly out if: a) the pipes are fixed and cannot mix, b) the pipes are not fixed and their total mass is $M$? Neglect the friction of the pistons against the pipe walls.

F16. A car of weight $P$, both axles of which are driven, starts moving. The automatic engine operates with a constant power of $W$, the coefficient of friction between the wheels and the road is $k$. Find the dependence of the automatic speed on time and draw a graph of this dependence. Neglect air resistance and friction in the mechanisms.

A. T. Drozdov
