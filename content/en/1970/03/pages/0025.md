Lily discovered the independence of the period of oscillation of a pendulum from amplitude - and immediately, carried away, mistakenly extended this law to any amplitude; discovered the constancy of the earth's acceleration - and immediately concluded that it is independent of the distance to the Earth! This is how the founder of almost all areas of mechanics was mistaken *).

Thus, in a thick mixture of pronunciation and error, without rights and wrongs, science developed. But at the same time, everyone we were talking about were geniuses: their works have been in use for several centuries; they have advanced humanity many decades into the future (and what, if not the number of man-years saved, determines the degree of talent?); it took more time to understand and master their ideas - a great truth is rarely born simple and understandable **).

The presented history of the development of science, of course, is extremely simplified and written in crude masks, sometimes with a popular condensation of facts. But it is clear that development does not proceed along the straight line of a telephone pole, but rather along the complex curve of the tree of science, the branches of which are inclined at different angles to the truth and are gradually straightened to rise to a new language of knowledge.

*) About the existence of geniuses. Newton, out of scientific conscientiousness, did not publish his law of universal gravitation for 20 years, trying to derive Kepler’s laws from it, and then spent many years arguing about nature with Hooke; did not publish his discovery of differential and integral calculus, trying to find a general formula for the integral, and then proved his nature in a dispute with Leibniz. For Newton lived only in science, throughout his life he was lonely and could not sacrifice his only joy in life - weak, although weak his discoveries would have been enough for many.
