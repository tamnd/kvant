4. An observer examines a distant object using an astronomical telescope (Kepler telescope). Lenses with focal lengths $F_1 = +30\text{ cm}$ and $F_2 = +5\text{ cm}$ are used as the object and eyepiece of the telescope. An observer sees a clear image of an object if the distance between the object and the eyepiece of the scope is between $L_1 = 33\text{ cm}$ and $L_2 = 34,5\text{ cm}$. At what distances are absences observed ranging from $L_1 = 33\text{ cm}$ to $L_2 = 34,5\text{ cm}$?

Option 2 (MIPT)

1. The flywheel of radius $R = 20\text{ cm}$ is mounted on a fixed axis of radius $r = 2\text{ cm}$. The friction force between the max wheel and the axle is constant and equal to 100 kg. To make it easier to remove the max wheel from the axis, a force $F = 8\text{ kГ}$ is applied to its rim, creating a torque relative to the axis (Fig. 2). With what minimum force $N$ do you need to pull the max wheel along the axis in order to remove it?

2. The pressure gauge consists of three glass tubes (Fig. 3). There is a pipe at the bottom of the pressure gauge, and oil in the left elbow above the pipe. How much does the pressure $P$, monitored by the pressure gauge, change if the oil level in the thin open tube rises by $\Delta h = 10\text{ mm}$? The ratio of the density of the pipe and oil is $\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2} = 14$, and the ratio of the cross-sections of the tubes is $\frac{S_3}{S_2} = 2$, $\frac{S_2}{S_1} = 50$.

3. A flat capacitor with horizontal plates is connected to a battery with an emf. With. $U$ and placed in a vessel, which is gradually filled with kerosene ($\varepsilon = 2$). Write it down as a formula and graph the dependence of the field strength in the center of the capacitor on the thickness of the kerosene layer inside it. The distance between the plates of the capacitor is $d$.

4. The distance between an object and its direct and twice magnified image obtained using a thin lens is $L$. Find the optical power of the lens and find out the nature of the image (real or minimal).
