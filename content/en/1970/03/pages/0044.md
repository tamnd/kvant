1. WHERE DID THE WORD COME FROM?
"SINUS"

The word "sine" is of Latin origin. If we look in the Latin-Russian dictionary, we see the following meanings of this word: 1) curvature, curvature, bending, convexity; 2) loose, pocket, fold of that (ancient Roman clothing) on ​​the chest; 3) dress, clothing; 4) breasts, hugs; 5) tender love, care;

6) middle, center; 7) refuge, shelter; 8) bay, bay; 9) vladina, deepening, failure. The word “sinus” is well known in the meaning of “sinus”, “cavity”. However, none of these many meanings have anything to do with sine in trigonometry. Where did this term come from?
