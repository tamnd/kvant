When one body moves relative to another, touching it, the atoms of these bodies “cling” and swing each other. If you are not afraid of vulgarization, then this process can be imagined like this: a person runs away from the pendants hanging nearby and swings them. Or better yet: the pendants are interconnected. By swinging one or more, all the others are forced to move (Fig. 1).

Vibrations of atoms located on the surface of the body are transmitted to atoms located in the depths. The transfer of energy from the surface to the depths of the body is a complex process that has not yet been fully traced (although its general features are understood).
