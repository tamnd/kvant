3. A body of mass $m$ moves vertically with acceleration $a$. Determine what force $F$ acts on the body.

Answers:
1) $F = m(g + a)$;
2) $F = m(g - a)$;
3) $F = m \frac{ga}{g + a}$.

4. Two lenses with focal lengths $F_1$ and $F_2$ are placed side by side (Fig. 8). Determine the focal length of such a system.

Answers:
1) $F = F_1 + F_2$;

2) $F = \frac{F_1 F_2}{F_1 + F_2}$;
3) $F = \frac{F_1 F_2}{F_1 - F_2}$.

5. Two charged balls of radii $R_1$ and $R_2$ are located at a distance from each other much greater than their radii. The charges of the balls are $q_1$ and $q_2$ respectively. Find the charges $q'_1$, $q'_2$ and the potential f of the balls after they are connected with a wire.

Answers:
1) $q'_1 = q_1 \frac{R_1}{R_2}$; $q'_2 = q_2 \frac{R_2}{R_1}$;
$\varphi = \frac{(q_1 - q_2)}{R_1 + R_2}$.
2) $q'_1 = \frac{(q_1 + q_2) R_1}{R_1 - R_2}$;
$q_2 = -\frac{(q_1 + q_2) R_2}{R_1 - R_2}$;
$\varphi = \frac{q_1 + q_2}{R_1 - R_2}$.
3) $q'_1 = \frac{(q_1 + q_2) R_1}{R_1 + R_2}$;
$q'_2 = \frac{(q_1 + q_2) R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$;
$\varphi = \frac{q_1 + q_2}{R_1 + R_2}$.
