For those who want to get acquainted with experiments in physical optics in more detail, we can recommend the recently translated book by J. Gregg, “Olytics with Sight” (Mir Publishing House, Moscow, 1970). This book provides a description of a large number of experimental elements for studying the functioning of the eye. All of them are quite accessible to schoolchildren, and most of their weight is remunerative. The reader should only be warned that in those places where the author touches on physical optics, the language of the book becomes far from scientific.

In the library you will probably also find Minart’s book “Light and Color in Nature” (Nauka Publishing House, Moscow, 1969) and Artamonov’s book “The Illusion of Vision” (Nauka Publishing House, Moscow, 1969).
