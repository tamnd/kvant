TO OUR READERS

The signature on the magazine «Kvant» for 1971 continues.

«Kvant» is a popular science physics and mathematics journal of the USSR Academy of Sciences and the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences. It is designed primarily for schoolchildren in grades 8-10. However, it will also be of interest to teachers, especially those who teach clubs or elective classes in physics and mathematics, as well as to all lovers of physics and mathematics.

The main content of the magazine is a “physics and mathematics school”, i.e. materials that help to better know physics and mathematics, learn to apply these sciences to teach various phenomena and processes that we encounter in practice, and learn to solve problems!

The reader will find many problems in the magazine. Among them are problems that were offered at entrance exams for various sports, problems that were offered at olympiads, and simply interesting problems.

Notes describing physical instruments and experiments will help the reader to set up and prove a physical experiment.

The magazine publishes review articles on its pages, telling about the achievements of science and problems that are still waiting to be solved, stories about scientists, about how “science is done”, how scientific discoveries appear.

The magazine constantly reports scientific news, places solutions on books - already published and those still being prepared for publication.

The main authors of the journal are famous Soviet and foreign scientists, young scientists and teachers. The magazine also provides its pages to schoolchildren to describe instruments and experiments, explain interesting questions and tasks.

Many materials are designed for server-side work with a pencil in hand.
