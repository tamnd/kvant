In this and subsequent issues, the section “Kvant’s Problem” is composed of problems proposed at the All-Union Optimization in Physics and Mathematics in 1970. After each problem, the class in which it was offered is indicated in parentheses.

F48. A rope is wound around the armature shaft of the dynamo, to which a weight is attached. As the weight descends, it rotates the anchor. When the armature was sufficiently untwisted, a load resistance was connected to the terminals of the machine. Draw on a graph the dependence of the speed of rotation of the anchor on the time from the moment the load begins to move. (X)

G. I. Kosourov

F49. The U-shaped tube is filled with water. Air has been removed from one elbow; the air pressure in the other leg at temperature $t = 20^\circ\text{C}$ is equal to atmospheric pressure. Both ends of the tube are sealed. The difference between the water levels in the elbows is 15 m. What will be the difference in water levels in the elbows if the tube is heated to $100^\circ\text{C}$? (IX-X)

B. B. Bukhovtsev

F51. Photographing a tiger from a distance of less than 20 meters is dangerous. What size is a camera obscura with a hole 1 mm in diameter so that a tiger in photographs would be striped? The distance between stripes on a tiger's skin is 20 cm. (X)

F52. The ball is thrown vertically upward. What is greater: the rise time or the fall time? (VIII)

F53. The vessel contains two immiscible liquids with specific gravities $d_1$ and $d_2$ and layer thicknesses $h_1$ and $h_2$, respectively. A small object is lowered from the surface of the liquid into a vessel, which reaches the bottom just at the moment when its speed becomes zero. What is the density of the materials from which the body is made? (VIII)

M. M. Balatsov
