What is Elipis? Elipis elements

An elipis is a figure called

obtained by the deformation of the circle relative to its diameter (Fig. 5).

Let $a$ be the radius of the circle; then its diameter $cd$, lying on the axis, will transform into itself $(CD=2a)$, and the diameter $eh$ perpendicular to it will transform into the segment $EH$ $(EH=2ka)$. Let us denote the length of the segment $OE$ $(=OH)$ by $b$. Then $ka=b$.

The segments $CD$ and $EH$ are called the axes of the ellipse; the numbers $a$ and $b$ will be called the semi-axes of the elipis. If $k>1$ (stretch), then $b>a$ (Fig. 5).
