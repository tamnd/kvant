To the article “Ball together lenses”

If $R_1$ and $R_2$ are the distances from the screen with the response to the light source and to the observation point, $r$ is the response radius, $a$ is the number of dark wheels in the diffraction pattern, including the dark spot in the center, then the wavelength $\lambda$ can be calculated by the formula
$$\lambda = \frac{r^2}{m} \frac{R_1+R_2}{2R_1 R_2}.$$

When deriving the formula, the squares of small quantities $m\lambda$ and $r^2/2R_1$ are neglected.
