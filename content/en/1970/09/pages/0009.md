It turns out that we have arrived at a contradiction? It is pleasant to come to a contradiction if you have previously assumed the opposite of what you want to prove. But, as you probably remember, we did not make such an assumption. How can this be?

The only choice that can be made is that our concept of a “comprehensive” dobby, as a dobby admitting of a finite description, is incorrect and has led to a contradiction. To make sure of this, let's give another example. It will no longer concern infinite dobbies, but natural numbers. Each such number can be described by a finite text (for example, by naming it), so there is no need to talk about “immenseness”.

However, it will find numbers that are not described in our universal library, that is, numbers for which there is no description in Russian, containing no more than a million characters. The latter follows from the fact that the number of volumes in a universal library is finite. Among the natural numbers that have not received a description, there are the smallest ones. We have proven the following statement:

There is the smallest natural number that cannot be specified in Russian text containing no more than one million characters.

The highlighted phrase contains this thirty-five characters. But this is thirty-five less than a million, and therefore we can be congratulated on one more contradiction: the above phrase unambiguously describes the mentioned number!

This is a real criticism of the concept of “verbal portrait”. Do you think the descriptions can also be considered phrases:

“α is an infinite tenth dobby, the signs of which with numbers divisible by the number of steps taken by Napoleon on the day of the Battle of Privato coincide with the corresponding signs of the number l, and the remaining signs are equal to zero.”

“To determine the k-th sign of dobby δ, you need to count the number of goals scored in all football mats played in the k-th year of the new era (not according to Moscow time), and take the last digit.”

“The fifth sign of γ is equal to 1 if in the notation √2 of the infinite decimal addition the digit 7 occurs an infinite number of times, otherwise it is equal to 3; all other signs of γ are equal to 2.”

I tried very hard to come up with absurd descriptions that would nevertheless define the only infinite doob. It is unlikely that we will agree to accept them as “verbal portraits” of Dobei. And the point is not even their absurdity, the important thing is that they do not provide a factual description of these dobes. We did not even exclude the possibility that the description could use already established information from the past (α), facts from the future (δ). In addition, the final text may require that an infinite number of prodedurs be sold to determine a single decimal place (γ). The same K. Prutkov provides against such errors:

“If everything that was past was present, and the present continued to exist at odds with the future, who would be able to figure out: where is the cause and where are the consequences?”

The main inconvenience, which, in fact, led us to a contradiction, is that the “verbal portrait” could use information about the set of all “verbal portraits”, in which he himself should be included as one of the elements.

The fact that this situation, which in logic bears the name of a “vicious circle,” is a source of contradiction, was known from the time of the semi-legendary sage Epimendas, who lived in the 6th century BC. e., who, being critical (that is, living on the island of Crete), declared: “All critical people are liars.” For simplicity, we will classify all people as liars, who always tell lies, and right people, who always tell lies.
