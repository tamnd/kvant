We interact with the trainer at every step. It would be more accurate to say that without training we cannot take a single step. This is not even due to the fact that friction is of a complex nature, but rather to the fact that experiments with friction are very sensitive to surface treatment and are therefore difficult to reproduce.

Here's an example. The English physicist Hardy studied the dependence of the friction force between glass plates on temperature. He carefully treated the plates with bleach and washed them with water, removing fats and dirt. Friction increased with temperature.
