LABORATORY "QUANTA"

CRYSTALS FROM BALLS

G. I. KOSOUROV

The first step to understanding, explaining and predicting the properties of a crystal is to determine its structure. Knowing the configuration of atoms in a crystal lattice and understanding the symmetry of their arrangement, it is possible, for example, to say in advance whether the crystal will be piezoelective, that is, whether an electrical voltage will appear on its facets during mechanical rolling; will the crystal have a ferroelective transition, which will occur when the temperature is determined and will result in an electric field arising inside the crystal; will a light wave of a double particle appear in a substance if laser light is shined through it, etc. The structure of a crystal is a passport that can tell a lot about its owner.
