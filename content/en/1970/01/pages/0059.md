⟦folio 57⟧

ENTRANCE CONTROL TEST OF ZMSH 1970

1. The mathematician walked home along the bank of the stream upstream at a speed one and a half times greater than the speed of the stream, and held a stick and a hat in his hands. At some points, he threw his hat into the stream, confusing it with a stick, and continued up the stream at the same speed. After a while he noticed the mistake, threw the stick into the stream and ran back at twice the speed of the hats in the mess. Having caught up with the plum hat, he instantly fished it out of the water, turned and walked along the current at its original speed. 10 minutes later he met a stick floating in the stream. How much earlier would he have gotten home if he had not confused the stick with the hat?

2. For every triangle ABC, is there a point P such that all three points, symmetrical points P with respect to the lines AB, BC and AC, lie on the circumcircle of triangle ABC?

3. For what values ​​of a is the difference between the roots of the equation ax² + x - 2 = 0 equal to 3?

4. The student took 31 exams over five years of study. In each subsequent year he passed more exams than in the previous one. In the fifth year there are more second exams than in the first. How many exams are there in the fourth year?

5. The bases of an equal-volume trapezoid are 4 cm and 8 cm, its area is 21 cm². Which side does the bissertis of the angle intersect with a larger base—the smaller base or the lateral side of the trapezoid?

6. All digits of a four-digit number that is a perfect square can be reduced by the same number so that the result is a four-digit number that is also a perfect square. Find all such numbers.

7. Is it possible for the sum of the distances from a point lying inside a convex quadrilateral to all its vertices to be greater than its perimeter?

8. One of the three gangsters, known in the city of Ch. under the nicknames Archie, Boss and Wesley, stole a briefcase with money. During interrogation, each of them made three statements:

   Archie: 1) I didn't take the briefcase.
   2) On the day of the theft, I left the city of Ch.
   3) Wesley stole the briefcase.

   Boss: 1) Wesley stole the briefcase.
   2) Even if I had taken it, I would not have confessed.
   3) I already have a lot of money.

   Wesley: 1) I didn't take the briefcase.
   2) I've been looking for a good briefcase for a long time.
   3) Archie is right in saying that he got out of Ch.

During the investigation, it was found that out of the three statements of each gangster, two were true and one was incorrect. Who stole the briefcase?

9. Find integers x and y such that x > y > 0 and x³ + 7y = y³ + 7x.

10. A square area measuring 100 m × 100 m is lined with 1 m × 1 m square tiles in four colors: white, red, black and gray - so that no two tiles of the same color collide with each other (i.e., do not have a common side or top). How many red litas can there be?

11. Solve the equation (x² + 6x - 4) (x² + 6x - 3) = 12.

12. Given triangle ABC. Find points K and H, lying respectively on sides AB and BC, such that BK = KH = HC.

13. Find all numbers p such that p² + 13 is also prime.
