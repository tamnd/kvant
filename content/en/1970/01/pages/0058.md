⟦folio 56⟧

Only eighth grade students are accepted to ZMSH. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into ZMSH. Classes at the school will begin on September 1.

ZMSH has two courses. Students who successfully complete school will receive a certificate of completion. School education is free.

This issue «Kvant» offers problems that serve as an introductory test for correspondence mathematics schools at Moscow State University and Leningrad State University. Those who want to enter the ZMSH must submit solutions to these problems no later than March 10, 1970. After checking the work (around June 1970), you will be informed whether you have been accepted into the ZMSH.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from the general school ones, no additional knowledge of mathematics is required to solve them.

In order to be accepted at school, it is not necessary to solve all problems without exception. When evaluating work, it will take into account not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without any obligations may not be read. If there are several possible answers to a task, then all of them must be indicated.

Work must be completed in Russian in a student notebook. Entry papers will not be sent back.

Please do not roll the notebooks into a tube when sending them. In the envelope along with the notebook you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14 cm × 6 cm with your postal address written on it (we will stick it on the envelope when we send the answer).

Glue a sheet of checkered paper onto the cover of your notebook, drawing and writing it according to the following pattern (otherwise your work will not be checked!):

Region:
Last name, first name, year of birth:
School [full name]:
Class:
Last name, first name, patronymic teacher
mathematics:
