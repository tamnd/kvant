⟦folio 59⟧

TASKS
ON WRITTEN EXAMINATIONS

Option
for those who built
to 9th grade

1. Solve system equation
$$\begin{cases} x^3 + y^3 = 9, \\ x^2y + y^2x = 6. \end{cases}$$

2. In triangle ABC, points P, Q and R are the bases of the altitudes dropped from vertices A, B and C respectively. Prove that $\angle ABQ = \angle APR$.

3. In what year were people born who were so old in 1969, what is the sum of the numbers of their year of birth?

4. Why is fog, consisting of drops of transparent water, not transparent?

5. There is a glass of water on the outside scale. A plug of volume V and density p floats in water. The cork is pushed under the water with a finger so that it is completely submerged. What will the scales show?

Option
for those who built
to 10th grade

1. Solve system equation
$$\begin{cases} x(y + z) = 3, \\ y(x + z) = 4, \\ z(x + y) = 5. \end{cases}$$

2. In the trapezoid ABCD with bases AD=a, BC=b (a>b), a segment MN is drawn parallel to the base and dividing the area of ​​the trapezoid in half. Find the length of this segment.

3. Find all pairs of integers x and y satisfying the equation
$$x^2 - 6xy + 5y^2 = 11.$$

4. At the bottom of one of the communicating vessels lies a piston of weight P. The force of its friction against the walls is equal to F. How much water must be poured into the other vessel for the piston to move to a height h?

5. The box is placed onto the conveyor belt without an initial speed. The belt moves with speed v. Friction coefficient k. How far will the box travel relative to the tape until its speed becomes equal to v?

1. Find the sum of the squares of the roots of the equation without solving it:
$$x^2 + px + q = 0.$$

2. Which of the two numbers is greater: $2^{300}$ or $3^{200}$?

3. The graph of the quadratic trinomial y=ax²+bx+c is given. What are the signs of the numbers a, b and c?

4. Prove that the sum of the medians of a triangle is less than the perimeter and greater than $\frac{3}{4}$ the perimeter of this triangle.

5. Trapezine bases a and b (a>b). Find the length of the segment parallel to the bases of the trapezoids, passing through the point of intersection of the diagonals and enclosed between its sides.

6. Prove that circles constructed on the sides of a convex quadrilateral as diameters will completely cover the quadrilateral.

7. Two circles of radii r₁ and r₂ are tangent to a given line. Find the locus of the points of intersection of their common internal tangents, provided that the circles can move along a given straight line in an arbitrary manner.

8. A piece of ice floats in a glass of water with a piece of pork embedded inside. The ice has melted. How will the water level in the vessel change?

9. The cannon fires at an angle α. Initial projectile speed v. A screen is placed at a distance L from the gun, from which the projectile is elastically reflected. At what distance from the gun will it fall?
