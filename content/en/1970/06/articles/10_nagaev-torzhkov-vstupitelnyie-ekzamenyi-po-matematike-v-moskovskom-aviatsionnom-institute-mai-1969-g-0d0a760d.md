Most colleges and universities in the country conduct two entrance exams in mathematics: one oral and the other written. MAI also conducts two exams, but both of them are written.

The first written work contains one theoretical question in geometry or trigonometry, two problems in geometry and two examples, including one equation, in trigonometry.

The second written work contains one theoretical question and four examples or problems from algebra.

Let us now present typical options in geometry, trigonometry and algebra.

Option 1

1. Prove a theorem on the change of an angle whose vertex is inside and outside the circle.

2. Prove point

$$\left( \mathrm{tg} 2\alpha + \frac{1}{\cos 2\alpha} \right) (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha) = \sqrt{2} \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha \right).$$

3. In the trapezoid $ABCD$ the sum of the angles at the base $AD$ is $90^\circ$. The lower and upper bases are equal to 7 and 3, respectively. Determine the segment connecting the midpoints of the bases.

4. Two mutually perpendicular generatrices of a right circular cone divide the circumference of the base in a ratio of 1:2. Find the lateral surface of the cone if its height is $h$.

5. Solve the equation

$$4 \sin^2 x + \sec (3\pi + x) = 1.$$

Option 2

1. Derivation of the formula for the sum of $n$ terms of a geometric progression.

2. Find the complex number $z = x + iy$ if $|z + 3i|^2 - z = 3 + 2i$.

3. Solve the equation

$$\log_3 (\log_2 x - 1)^2 = \sqrt{5} \log_5 4.$$

4. The two embroidered simultaneously from $M$ and $N$ towards each other. They met at $50$ m from $N$, and then, having reached $N$ and $M$, they went back and met again at $25$ m from $M$. Find the distance $MN$ if it is known that they moved uniformly and continuously.

5. For what real values ​​of the parameter $m$ are the roots of the equation

$$\sqrt{x^2 - x - 2m^2 + 2m + 2} = \frac{x + 1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

are active and have different signs?

Many did not use this property of the median and had difficulty solving the problem. Here the complexLEX number is $z = x + iy$, and the complexLEX number is $z + 3i = x + iy + 3i = x + (y + 3)i$. The modulus of the latter is $|z + 3i| = \sqrt{x^2 + (y + 3)^2}$, and its square is $|z + 3i|^2 = x^2 + (y + 3)^2$. Thus we have $x^2 + (y + 3)^2 - x - iy = 3 + 2i$.

Two complex numbers are equal if their real parts and coefficients of their minimum parts are equal. Hence,

Solving the system, we find $x_1 = -1$ and $x_2 = 2$. From here we get two complexLEX numbers: $z_1 = -1 - 2i$ and $z_2 = 2 - 2i$.

3. Using the basic logarithmic approach, we obtain

$$\log_3 (\log_2 x - 1)^2 = 2.$$

Then they made a lot of mistakes, namely, they took the logarithm of the degree, taking the exponent of the bracket 2 out of the sign of the logarithm, and shortened the equation by two. Such a transformation in this case leads to the loss of the root, since the logarithmic expression in parentheses can be positive, while in the original equation it could be both positive and negative.

Correct solution:

$(\log_2 x - 1)^2 = 9$ or $|\log_2 x - 1| = 3$.

Hence $\log_2 x - 1 = \pm 3$, which leads to two solutions:

1) $\log_2 x = 4$ and $x_1 = 16$;
2) $\log_2 x = -2$ and $x_2 = \frac{1}{4}$.

Both roots are positive and satisfy the ODZ of the unknown $x > 0$.
