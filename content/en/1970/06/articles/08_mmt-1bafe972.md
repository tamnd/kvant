MMT

These letters stand for: MOSCOW MATHEMATICAL TECHNIQUE.

Before history, MMT was the only secondary educational institution training specialists in applied mathematics. Since the fall of 1970, training in this specialty will begin in one of the Leningrad and one of the Dnevopotrebsk technical ministries of acquisition, automation equipment and control systems of the USSR.

Duration of study at MMT: for those who have completed the 8th grade of secondary school - 2 years 10 months, and for those who have completed the 10th grade - 1 year 10 months. Those who successfully graduate from technical education receive an exciting and necessary specialty for the national economy - computer mathematician. He can work in computer centers, departments and laboratories at factories, industry associations of computer technology and design bureaus.

But the most important thing is that MMT graduates master mathematical culture in a very high sense of the word.

In 1970, another recruitment took place at MMT. Admission exams in mathematics (oral) and literature (written) will begin on August 1. If you are interested in applied mathematics and would like to receive information about MMT, we provide you with its address:

Moscow E-43, Nizhnyaya Pervomaiskaya, 14, Moscow Mathematical Technical Ministry of Automation Equipment and Control Systems of the USSR, phone: 165-64-72.

Unfortunately, there are no promises for technical demonstrations.
