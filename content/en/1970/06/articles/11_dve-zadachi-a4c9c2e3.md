IS IT ONLY 2?

Everyone is well aware of the following rules:

$$2 \times 2 = 4, \quad 2 + 2 = 4; \quad 0 + 0 = 0.$$

Consider these questions:

1. Are there other integers $a$ and $b$ that
$$a + b = ab.$$

2. Are there other rational numbers?

3. Let it also
$$a + b = ab = p,$$
where $p$ is an integer, $p \neq 0$, $p \neq 4$. Is it possible:
a) for real $a, b$;
b) for rational $a, b$.
