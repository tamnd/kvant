2. In this example, the main difficulty lies in writing the model of a complex number correctly. Here the complexLEX number is $z = x + iy$, and the complexLEX number is $z + 3i = x + iy + 3i = x + (y + 3)i$. The modulus of the latter is $|z + 3i| = \sqrt{x^2 + (y + 3)^2}$, and its square is $|z + 3i|^2 = x^2 + (y + 3)^2$. Thus we have $x^2 + (y + 3)^2 - x - iy = 3 + 2i$.

Two complex numbers are equal if their real parts and coefficients of their minimum parts are equal. Hence,

$$\begin{cases} x^2 + (y + 3)^2 - x = 3, \\ y = -2. \end{cases}$$

Solving the system, we find $x_1 = -1$ and $x_2 = 2$. From here we get two complexLEX numbers: $z_1 = -1 - 2i$ and $z_2 = 2 - 2i$.
