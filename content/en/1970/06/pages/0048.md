⟦folio 46⟧

Problem solutions
introductory work control
in 3MSh 1970

A. L. Toom

In the first issue of our magazine (page 56), as well as in the Teacher's Newspaper (February 26, 1970), problems were published that need to be solved in order to enter the Correspondence Mathematics School. ZMSH received about 6,000 letters from eight grade students with solutions to problems, about 3,000 of them were accepted at the school.

We think that the problems are being addressed not only by those who sent letters to the ZMS, but also by many other readers «Kvant», including future candidates for the ZMS, and therefore today we are publishing the solutions to the problems. Almost all problems are analyzed in great detail, “thoroughly”, in order to clarify the solution even for not very prepared readers. For the convenience of readers, we repeat the conditions of the problems here.

It’s not enough to solve a problem and get the correct answer: you also need to be able to write it down, which is not at all easy. Therefore, we advise even those who have solved the problems to read these solutions and compare them with their own. Of course, the solutions given here are not the only possible ones, but by reading them you can understand how to write solutions to problems accurately and logically.
