1. The mathematician walked home along the bank of the stream upstream at a speed one and a half times greater than the speed of the stream, and held a stick and a hat in his hands. At some points, he threw his hat into the stream, confusing it with a stick, and continued to walk up the stream at the same speed. After a while he noticed the mistake, threw the stick into the stream and ran back at twice the speed of his hat. Having caught up with the dusty hat, he instantly fished it out of the water, turned and went upstream *) at first speed. After 10 minutes, he came across a stick dusting along the stream. How much earlier would he have come home if he had not confused the stick with the hat?
