Let's solve two problems.

1. Two bodies, being at a distance $S_0=3\text{ m}$, begin to move towards each other with speeds $V_1=2\text{ m/сек}$ and $V_2=1\text{ m/сек}$. Where and when will they meet?

The problem can be easily solved graphically (Fig. 12). We will assume that the first body moves from the origin of coordinates in a positive direction, that is, up on the graph, and the second body moves from point $x_1=S_0$ in a negative direction, that is, down on the graph (the coordinate increases). Both movements are depicted as inclined straight lines, moving towards each other and intersecting at the point corresponding to the coordinate and time of meeting.

The slope of the lines is determined from the condition: $tg\alpha_1=V_1=2$, $tg\beta=V_2=-1$. The graph shows what occurs at the point with coordinates $t=1\text{ сек}$ and $x=2\text{ м}$.
