Then the bisector of the angle between the lines KN and ML is parallel to them. We learned from P. Kozhevnikov that with a suitable central projection in space, point O will go to infinity, and pairs of straight lines OA and OD, OB and OC, OP and OQ (Fig.19) will become parallel straight lines, symmetrical with respect to a fixed straight line (Gaussian straight line), which passes through the midpoints of the segments AD, BC, PQ.

S. DVORYANINOV

Wonder is the mother of knowledge. Attributed to Aristotle

NOW WE WILL TELL ABOUT ONE INTERESTING physical phenomenon. Moreover, it is amazing, and on this basis it was included in V.I. Arnold’s book “Mathematical Understanding of Nature. Essays on amazing physical phenomena and their understanding by mathematicians (with drawings by the author).” We will talk about a pendulum. Usually a pendulum is drawn as shown in figure 1. A small body is suspended from a long thread, which oscillates. Such a pendulum is called mathematical. The lower equilibrium position of the pendulum is stable. Instead of a thread, you can take a rod - we will have just such a pendulum. Now let’s turn the pendulum over, Fig. 1, make it stand, as Arnold writes, “upside down” (Fig.2). In such a pendulum, the previous point of suspension turns into a fulcrum, but according to tradition we will call it the point of suspension. It is well known that it is impossible to place such a pendulum vertically - either to the left or to the right - Fig. 2 it will definitely fall.
