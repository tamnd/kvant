4. A very thick layer of ice has a temperature of 0 q C. The polar explorer decided to conduct an experiment. To do this, he made a small hole in the ice and covered it with a layer of heat insulator into which a thin tube was inserted. Through this tube, the polar explorer very slowly poured an unknown hot liquid with specific heat c and density U into the hole. The liquid melted the ice, and the depth of the hole increased. The polar explorer discovered that there is a certain minimum value of the initial temperature of the liquid $t_{0}$, at which an arbitrarily large amount of liquid can be poured into the hole and the ice will continue to melt. In this case, the surface of the resulting water will never rise to the upper edge of the hole. Find this minimum value $t_{0}$. The specific heat of fusion of ice O, as well as the densities of water and ice $U_{в}$ and $U_{л}$ are known. M. Romashka 9th grade
