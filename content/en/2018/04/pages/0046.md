Problem 9. Prove that AX, BY, CZ intersect at one point (Fig.11,a). Solution. Consider the angle BAC, and this angle is drawn AZ and AY. Let BY intersect CZ at point D (Fig.11,b). By the theorem on isogonals, AX c and AD are isogonals of the angle BAC. Note that X and X c are isogonally conjugate points. Consequently, AX c and AX – are also isogonalized. This means that straight lines AX and AD coincide. Lines AX, BY, CZ pass through point D.
