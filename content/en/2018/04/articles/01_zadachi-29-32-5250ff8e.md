29. In Shilovsk, awl is 1% cheaper than in Mylovsk, and soap is 1% more expensive. Travel from one city to another costs 1000 rubles. A young businessman living in Shilovsk has 100 thousand rubles and dreams of getting rich, “trading an awl for soap.” Will his dreams come true? A. Zaslavsky

30. The engraver polishes the diamond, which has the shape of a convex polyhedron, gradually turning it into a diamond. He begins by first cutting off all the corners of the polyhedron diamond (small pyramids at the tops) with a sharpened flat knife. Prove that after this operation the number of all vertices of the resulting polyhedron will be even, and the number of edges will be divisible by 3. G. Galperin

31. You are given a checkered board, all the cells of which are initially black. In one operation, you can select any 5-cell figure on the board, shown on figure 1 (the figure can be rotated), and change the color of each of the cells of this figure to the opposite (black to white and vice versa). Is it possible to repaint Fig. 1 the entire board white in several such operations if: a) the board has dimensions 7 ¥ 7; b) the board measures 8 ¥ 8? S. Kostin
