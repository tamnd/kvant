atmospheric physics

Our next step on the path to the celestial - astronomical - spheres passes through the air shell of the Earth. It is studied by meteorology, the main content of which is atmospheric physics. Is it worth talking about humanity’s constant interest in atmospheric phenomena, about the colossal dependence on the processes occurring in the atmosphere? Thunderstorms, winds and hurricanes - how to protect yourself from them? Droughts or endless rains - how will they affect the crops? What is happening to the climate, is global warming threatening us? These and many other questions are vitally important and require active action from us, which science can comprehend. Over the past few centuries, the most precise instruments have been created with the help of which atmospheric parameters are monitored - pressure, temperature, humidity, precipitation, wind speed and direction. Following this, the Earth was dotted with an extensive network of synoptic stations, collecting data from vast areas. And today there is a whole “squadron” of meteorological satellites circling the planet. The information they receive is processed by powerful computers and gives us a weather forecast every minute. Does this make us feel better? Absolutely yes! Have the problems that concern us been solved? Of course not! Maybe this will work for you?
