Record mechanical parameters

L.ASHKINAZI

If there is no strength...

When asked why strength is needed, everyone will say - so that houses don’t fall. But the beauty of the situation is that even in the absence of gravity, strength is needed. Without it, we will not be able to move anything - Newton's second law! The basis for determining mechanical parameters is the following situation. We take a rod of a known cross-section and, carefully holding it by the ends so that the mechanical stresses are distributed evenly over the cross-section, we pull. While it elongates in proportion to the load and returns to its original size when the load is removed, its relative elongation is inversely proportional to its magnitude.
