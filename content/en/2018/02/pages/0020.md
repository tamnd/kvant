In the 11th–14th centuries, a change occurred with ъ and ь, which can be described by $так:^{4}$ 1) at the end of the word, the vowels ъ and ь fall out; $^{5}$ 2) then we move along the word from right to left; if we find ъ or ь, then we look at the nearest vowel to the right of it: a) if it is a dropped ъ or ь, then the ъ or ь considered at this step becomes clearer, i.e. turns into o or e respectively;
