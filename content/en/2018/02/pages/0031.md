Polyhedra made from tubes

D.PANOV, A.PUSHKAR, D.CHEBASOV

THIS ARTICLE TELLS HOW straws and small rubber rings can be used for geometric constructions. The tubes suitable for our experiments are sold in the “Veselaya Zateya” store and are called balloon sticks there. They are quite long - 37.5 cm and at the same time rigid and elastic. Small rubber rings for weaving are sold in almost any store. First, we took three tubes and fastened their ends in pairs with rubber rings. The result is an equilateral triangle (Fig.1). Why not assemble a square from the same tubes? And here a surprise awaited us. We connected four identical tubes properly, but it was not quite a square, or even not a square at all. If you put this structure on the table, it instantly turns into a rhombus, and if you lift it by the top, it ceases to be a flat figure at all (Fig.2). We called this one

So, a triangle consisting of three segments is rigid - inflexible, but a square and any other quadrangle, and pentagon, and hexagon, etc. – bendable. This is a well-known fact, and we were able to verify it with the help of tubes and rubber bands. Why not collect something spatial now?
