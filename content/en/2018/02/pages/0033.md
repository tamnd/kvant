This is how our question was answered. The theorems of Cauchy and Aleksandrov mentioned here are the most famous in the theory of polyhedra. Their formulations are quite simple, and the evidence is accessible to older students. We recommend reading the book by N.P. Dolbilin “Pearls of the Theory of Polyhedra” (Moscow: MTsNMO, 2012), dedicated to the proofs of these theorems and intended for schoolchildren. A few words in conclusion. An important classical question is the question of the rigidity and bendability of real polyhedra - polyhedra with faces. There is a beautiful theory with a long history and unexpected discoveries, partially accessible to schoolchildren. We advise you first of all to look on the Internet for the popular lecture “Flexible polyhedra and bellows”, delivered a year ago by A. Gaifullin at the XXVIII Mathematical Festival in Moscow.
