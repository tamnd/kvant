OLYMPICS

XXV International Olympiad "Tuymaada". Physics

In July of this year, the next, XXV International Olympiad “Tuymaada” in physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry was held in Yakutsk. Participants in the Physics Olympiad competed as usual in two leagues: senior and junior (the jury distributes participants into leagues depending on their age and class). The Olympics in each league consisted of two rounds: theoretical and experimental. According to the program of the Physics Olympiad, participants can be offered problems on any topics contained in the in-depth school curriculum (in the junior league - with the exception of topics related to the graduating class). Authors of original problems (both theoretical and experimental) can send them to the methodological commission (achudn@mail.ru) - the best problems will be included in the final set and will be published in educational journals after the Olympiad. The article was prepared on the basis of the methodological manual: A.V. Chudnovsky, R.E. Avanesyan, A.B. Akimov, S.D. Varlamov, V.V. Efimov, A.R. Zilberman, V.M. Muravyov, M.G. Ryndin, G.M. Tarnopolsky. XXV International Olympiad "Tuymaada". Physics. Theoretical tour. Methodical manual / ed. A.V. Chudnovsky - M.-Yakutsk, 2018.
