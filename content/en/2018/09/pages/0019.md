Interested in the question of the meaning of art in the spiritual sphere of human interests, Feynman studied the history of painting and architecture and at the age of 44 began to study painting professionally. He became a good portrait painter. Several of his personal exhibitions took place. All this became the reason for Feynman to be recognized as a “Renaissance man.” And in his seventh decade he became known throughout America. The twenty-eighth of January 1981 became a day of mourning for the American astronautics and the entire country - a short time after launch, the Challenger space shuttle exploded with seven astronauts on board. The causes of the tragedy were completely unclear - even the intervention of aliens was seriously discussed. In the end, a presidential commission was created, in which Feynman was invited to participate. Despite his cancer, he agreed to participate in this commission and, who knows, maybe this extended his life, since his participation was very active and ultimately became decisive for unraveling the cause of the disaster. He did not think about his illness and courageously overcame all the difficulties of the interoperative periods. His colleagues on the commission often did not know about the operations he was undergoing. It is characteristic that the decisive role in Feynman’s agreement to work on the commission was not even a personal appeal from the President of the United States, but a loving wife, who apparently understood that this would help her husband prolong his life. Feynman became the black sheep of the commission.
