Another recruitment at VZMSH

The All-Russian Correspondence Multi-Subject School (VZMSH), which is part of the structure of the Moscow Lyceum “Second School” and operates at the Moscow State University named after M.V. Lomonosov, is enrolling students for the fifty-fourth time. This school was created on the initiative of academician I.M. Gelfand in 1964. For many years, I.M. Gelfand headed the Scientific Council of the school. VZMSH is a state institution of additional education, available to everyone, and not only for schoolchildren, to expand their knowledge in one or more of the following fields of science: mathematics, biology, philology, physics, history (listed in chronological order of opening of departments). Now VZMSH, together with other scientific and pedagogical institutions, is conducting research on the development of new interactive technologies in education and the translation of part of its educational and methodological complexes into the language of modern telecommunications. During the existence of VZMSH, several hundred thousand schoolchildren and thousands of circles - groups “Collective student of VZMSH” received certificates of completion.
