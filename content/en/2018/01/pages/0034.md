geophysics

Astronomy is back to school! Welcoming this event, Kaleidoscope responds to it with a whole series of its releases. However, since this glorious, but difficult subject crowns the school course of natural sciences, its study requires good preparation and erudition. And this is laid down in the study of natural history, natural history, and physical geography. And you probably noticed how many questions in them required the use of physical knowledge. So we decided to start our ascent along the “stairway to heaven” from the first step - the firmament of the earth. And we were immediately faced with how “unsteady” this statement turns out to be today, as well as many seemingly obvious ideas about the world around us. We hope that geophysics, which combines the study of the lithosphere, hydrosphere and magnetosphere of the Earth, will help us understand at least a small part of them. Moreover, it will remind you how much useful devices created with the help of physics have provided, for example, when searching for minerals or preventing the consequences of natural disasters - floods, landslides and earthquakes.
