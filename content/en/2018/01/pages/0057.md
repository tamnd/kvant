INFORMATION

Department of Mathematics

We teach mathematics to schoolchildren in grades 6–11 by correspondence. We accept individuals for individual training based on the results of the introductory work and send benefits. The curator leads the training: draws up a program, checks assignments and answers questions. We accept students for training in the “Collective Student” group upon application from the group leader. We send benefits to the entire group. Students send one collective work on each topic for testing to the VZMSH. We accept students for all courses from 0 to 5 (that is, students in grades 6–11). Send your application and introductory work to the admission email address: priem@math-vzms.org or to the school's postal address: 119234 Moscow V-234, Vorobyovy Gory, Moscow State University, mathematical department of VZMSH. Our website: http://www.math-vzms.org/ Our phone: +7 495 93 93 93 0 If you want to study individually, complete the introductory work, the conditions of which are given below. Solutions to problems that were successfully solved must be written down in a regular student notebook and sent by simple parcel post along with an application for admission to the school. Entry papers and applications are also accepted by email. In this case, the work must be formatted as a .doc or .pdf file; you can also scan the text of the work and attach it to the application for admission. The application for admission is written in free form. Please provide your last name, first name, patronymic, year of birth, basic education (it would be convenient for us to read: “From September 1, 2018, I will be studying in... class”), full postal address with the zip code where you learned about VZMSH (from the Internet, from magazines «Kvant», “Science and Life”, from a teacher, parents, friends or from other sources). Please provide your email address, if available. Don't forget to indicate that you are entering the mathematics department. The deadline for submitting the entry work is June 15, 2018.

⟦column⟧

In the introductory work below, next to the serial number of the task, it is indicated in parentheses for which class students (meaning the class in which you expect to study from September 1, 2018) this task is intended. You can, if you wish, additionally solve problems addressed to older classes. Take your time, and if tasks don’t work out, return to them several times. There is still enough time until June 15th. You may not be able to solve all the problems in your class, then send solutions to those that you managed to solve. Don’t forget to justify your decisions; a “bare” answer to a problem is not considered a solution.

Tasks

1 (6–7). The prime numbers are arranged in a sequence in ascending order. Is it true that the arithmetic mean of two adjacent prime numbers cannot be a prime number? 2 (6–7). The box contains balloons: 10 red and 10 blue. The seller takes out one ball at a time without looking. How many balls does he need to pull out so that among them there are sure to be: a) two balls of the same color; b) two balls of different colors; c) three balls of the same color? 3 (6–7). Add one digit to the right and left of the number 10 so that the resulting number is divisible by 12.4 (6–8). Petya drank 1/2 glass of coffee and topped it up with milk. Then he drank 1/3 of the glass and topped it up with milk again. Finally, he drank 1/6 of the glass, topped it up with milk and drank the entire glass. What did Petya drink more: coffee or milk? 5 (7–10). a) Is it possible to number the edges of a cube with natural numbers from 1 to 12 so that for each vertex of the cube the sum of the numbers of the edges that converge at it is the same? b) A similar question if you place the numbers –6, –5, –4, –3, –2, –1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. 6 (8–10) along the edges of the cube. Two cities A and B are located on the banks of a river at a distance of 10 km from each other. A steamboat can sail from A to B and back in 1 hour. Will it take him more or less time to swim 20 km across the lake?
