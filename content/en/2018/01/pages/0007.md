ASTRONOMY

BACK

The solar corona during $затмения^{1}$ requires understanding what is happening beneath the visible surface of the Sun, and astrophysicists are now working on this. However, our Galaxy is not rich in the Sun alone: ​​there are still hundreds of billions of stars in it, and some of them can be dangerous for the Earth. We are talking about explosions of massive stars at the end of their evolution, i.e., supernova explosions. Astronomers are learning to recognize stars that are ready to explode in the near future. One candidate is Betelgeuse, the brightest star in the constellation Orion. Astronomers are watching it, waiting for an explosion. But an accurate forecast of this event is not yet possible. This requires sensitive neutrino telescopes, which do not yet exist. But they will definitely be there!

* * * So, returning to school after many years of oblivion, astronomy has noticeably changed its face. Today, “celestial science” is closely connected with other branches of natural science. Space is now perceived as a scientific laboratory with incredible capabilities. By satisfying his curiosity, man won the struggle for existence. Today, the entire Universe has become the subject of human curiosity. And this is the guarantee of our future.
