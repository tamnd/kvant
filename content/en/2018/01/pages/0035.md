...the study of earthquakes has provided a wealth of information about the internal structure of the Earth. Thus, based on the behavior of the waves they caused, the liquid core of the planet was discovered. ...since most of the Earth’s surface, almost 70.8%, is occupied by the World Ocean, it would be more logical to call our planet not the Earth, but the Ocean. To the water contained in the seas, oceans, rivers, lakes and ice, it is necessary to add, as it turned out, the water “bound” by rocks, which is only half as much. ...The Earth maintains an average constant temperature despite continuously receiving energy from the Sun. Heat removal is carried out due to radiation, the spectrum of which lies in the infrared range. ...the Earth's magnetic field is variable; over the last 200 years of observations it has been slightly decreasing. And over time intervals of tens of thousands of years, a change in the position of the poles to the opposite occurred repeatedly! ...the continental plates of the Earth, lying above a viscous, hot mantle, are in imperceptible but constant movement. According to geologists, there were periods in the history of our planet when not only individual plates moved, but the entire solid shell rotated as a whole relative to the metal core and changed the position of the earth’s axis. ...seismic “examination” of the earth’s interior carried out in recent years has revealed in the inner solid core another core, half the diameter, with a different orientation of the iron crystals that make it up.
