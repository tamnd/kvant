This pair of spheres is also touched by a sphere with center D drawn through points M and N. This means that line MN passes through the same vertex. So, the lines KL and MN intersect, which means they lie in the same plane, therefore, all four points of tangency lie on the circle - the section of the sphere by this plane. A spatial quadrilateral has the same criterion for the existence of an inscribed ball as a plane one: a ball can be inscribed if and only if the sums of opposite sides are equal. If you fix points A and C, then triangles ABC and ADC can be moved apart and closed, the “bird” will open and close its “beak”, all the while holding the ball in its beak (the ball will slide along the beak). According to Corollary 3, the lines KL and MN connecting the points of contact of this ball with the beak will always pass through the same point - point V. And this is not all that comes from the theorem about the three caps, which we proved by going into space. Try to prove a few more consequences yourself.
