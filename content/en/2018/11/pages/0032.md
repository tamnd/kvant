9. Electronic clock shows hours and minutes. Vasya went up to the watch and noticed that it was now a palindrome - the time looked like AB:BA. He decided to wait for it to happen again, but after sitting for 4 hours, he still didn’t see the second palindrome. How long does he still have to wait? Yu.Markelov

10. Prove that any integer at least 12 can be written as the sum of two composite numbers. I. Mitrofanov

11. There is a cardboard triangle ABC on the table. The following operation is performed on him several times. Select its vertex, let's denote it X, turn the triangle over and place it so that point X is in the same place as before, and angle X of the triangle coincides with its previous position. Let us denote such an operation by $P_{X}$. Prove that after the sequence of operations $P_{A}, P_{B}, P_{C}, P_{A}, P_{B}, P_{C}$ the triangle will take its original position. V. Rastorguev

12. The numbers 1, 2, ..., 2n are written in the cells of the rectangle 2 ¥ n (one number in each cell). First, the number 1 is written in the lower left corner cell, then the number 2 is written in one of the neighboring cells, then 3 is written in one of the neighboring cells that were previously occupied, etc.; all subsequent numbers are written in cells adjacent to the previously occupied ones. Prove that exactly $2^{n} -^{1} n$ exists! ways to fill out the table. V. Rastorguev
