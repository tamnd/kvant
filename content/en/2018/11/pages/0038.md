We can say that a random weak sound that gets into the microphone is amplified by the electrical system of the computer and speakers, comes back to the microphone, still passes through the amplification path, and one of the frequencies in this sound is amplified especially significantly, coinciding with some natural frequency of the system. At resonance, the signal becomes very loud. This is positive feedback. But for high-quality sound of any instrument, the design must also have negative feedback - to prevent unnecessary resonances, otherwise the sound will only irritate the ear. In essence, this is an extremely simplified model of the wonderful invention of Lev Sergeevich Theremin (1896–1993), which was called the “theremin”, only in this device the feedback was carried out electrically. The approach of the performer's hand changed the capacitance of the oscillatory circuit in the generator, and its frequency increased or decreased. This was then translated into sound, and the instrument (without touching it!) was able to reproduce melodies with a unique sound. In short, the experience offered presents a wide range of possibilities for a student - for example, choosing a topic for a project and a presentation at a student conference.
