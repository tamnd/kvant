Geometry of checkered paper

A.ONOPRIENKO

WE WILL TELL ABOUT ONE INTERESTING story, prepared based on materials from mathematical circles at the Moscow Pedagogical State University $(МПГУ).^{1}$ An ordinary checkered piece of paper is fraught with many surprises. We will discuss several problems in which constructions are made on a checkered plane. As in standard construction problems, you can use a ruler without divisions to draw straight lines, but we don’t need a compass at all. As a rule, it is a compass, and not a ruler, that allows one to “measure” segments in construction problems (not to find their length, but to construct a segment equal to a given one, for example). Here the measuring tool is the plane itself! Capital letters A, B, C, ... in all problems indicate the knots of checkered paper. We take the side of one cell as 1. Let's start our journey with one simple research task. Problem 1. a) A segment AB is drawn on checkered paper that does not pass along the sides of the cells, and point C is marked. Mark node D such that AB = CD. b) Construct all such segments. How many of them did you get? figure 1 presents different options for segments AB. Try to solve the problem for each of these options. You can choose point C at any node (it will follow from our solution that its location is not important).

Solution. a) Let’s construct a right triangle along the sides of the mesh, one of the sides of which is the segment AB (generally speaking, this can be done in two ways, but let’s fix one of them). Then we move this triangle to point C. The side of this triangle that does not go along the sides of the mesh will be equal to the segment AB, since the triangles are equal on two legs. This will be the required segment CD (Fig.2,a). The same solution is suitable for any other segment AB. b) Generally speaking, in point a) it would be possible not to construct a triangle, but to carefully consider the segment AB. In order to get to point B, we must go from point A 5 cells to the right and 2 cells up (these numbers are given for the first segment, for other segments the number of cells will be different).
