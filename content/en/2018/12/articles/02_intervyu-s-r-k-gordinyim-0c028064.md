OUR INTERVIEW

Interview with R.K. Gordin

On the sixteenth of November this year, the outstanding mathematics teacher Rafail Kalmanovich Gordin turned seventy. Almost the entire teaching career of Rafail Kalmanovich is connected with Moscow school No. 57. He joined it in March 1974 and since then has been teaching talented children in mathematics classes. Dozens of its graduates have become professors of mathematics and work at leading universities around the world: in Russia, the USA, Canada, Great Britain, France, Israel and other countries. Many of his students working in other fields of science, education, and in the field of high technology have achieved great success, also in no small part thanks to the lessons of Rafail Kalmanovich. The titles of Rafail Kalmanovich can be listed for a long time - he is an Honored Teacher of the Russian Federation, a Soros teacher, a multiple winner of Moscow grants in the field of education, a laureate of a grant from the Dynasty Foundation for Non-Profit Programs, an award from the President of Russia and an award from the Dynasty Foundation “For outstanding achievements in education in the field of natural science.” Over the past two decades, Rafail Kalmanovich has created a computer database of more than

The teacher's biography of Rafail Kalmanovich occurred in different and sometimes contradictory times. His professionalism, dignity and respectful attitude towards children remained unchanged. Rafail Kalmanovich instilled in all the teenagers with whom he worked (and these were not only future mathematicians - after all, he taught in biology classes and even in a trade and economic technical school) a great love for mathematics. And for many of us, his students, Rafail Kalmanovich became the first school teacher who addressed us as “you” and treated us like adults - gratitude for this is felt even decades after leaving school. – Please tell us about the database of geometry problems (zadachi.mccme.ru). – In 1991, a graduate of the 81st year, Misha Businier, came to me and said that he and his comrades wanted to write a program with which one could search for problems using different criteria.
