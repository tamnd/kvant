Vadik Radionov did a lot more for this system. – You have been teaching at school No. 57 for more than 40 years. Can you compare the students of that time and today - what do they have in common, what are the differences? – There is no fundamental difference. These are absolutely the same children. Well, of course, before there was more or less a monopoly - there were several schools, we had big competitions, up to 15 people per place, and in recent years - 4-5 people per place. In my opinion, the competition is decreasing due to the fact that there are many specialized schools. The concentration of very strong people in the class is now smaller, but they still exist. So I don’t see a fundamental difference - except that the concentration has changed. – Your students won competitions, there are prominent scientists, businessmen, and some returned to work at school.
