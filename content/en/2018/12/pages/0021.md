Mark Spivakovsky, Roman Smolensky, Mikhail Bershadsky studied there - they later became outstanding mathematicians, now posters with their biographies hang in the school corridor. They also gave me 8th grade and 9th grade biology, which was also wonderful. The children from all three classes were so wonderful, they treated me so well and behaved well - but at school 179 everything was terrible with discipline, I went there as if I was going to hard labor. But I couldn’t leave there; I had to work both here and there. The schools are nearby, so I was given lessons on the same day in different schools, and during the break I managed to move from one school to another. This went on for a couple of months. The end of the school year has come, no one offers me anything. I asked Lev Nilovich what would happen next year. He replied: “No questions, of course, stay here. We will also give you great guidance.” I’m happy and satisfied and I’m thinking about how I can leave school 179 now (it’s hard to work both there and there). And then the director of school 179 calls me to him and says: “We thought, asked about you - both parents and children. Opinions vary. Some say that you do not maintain discipline, the lessons are very difficult - noisy. And some say that you give good tasks. We decided to take a risk and offer you a watch and a cool tutorial.” I tell her: “Thank you, but I don’t need it anymore.” How angry she was - my God! She told me: “If you knew how much I had to go through to get you here!” Indeed – I can imagine what it was like. They took me on a small load, and Kronrod walked around, persuaded her, and Kolya persuaded her. And I took it and didn’t give a damn about it. But at that moment I actually already had a job at school 57 - and still, from her point of view, I was ungrateful. Then she remembered this to me. I didn’t have an apartment, and I was trying to find an apartment somewhere, join some kind of cooperative. She told me: “If you would stay...

If you wanted, I would arrange an apartment for you!” But I still had to complete the two classes that I had (9 and 10). So I worked at school 179 for another year. Then at school 179 everything improved. My wonderful students and friends, the Datskovsky brothers, were there - we became friends with them. One is now a mathematician, he is a professor at the University of Philadelphia, the other is a physicist. When I was in America in 1990, I met with them. In fact, everything was fine with this class, there were good children there. As a result, since March 6, 1974, I have been working at school No. 57. I really liked it here. Instantly, as soon as I came here. – Please tell us about the features of school 57. “I came with everything ready, and for a long time I myself did not understand how teaching was organized in these schools - in 57, 179, 91, where Nikolai Nikolaevich also took me. He took me to a circle. Then the math circles were on Mokhovaya Street, in the old building of Moscow State University. I didn’t understand how management felt about all this. How is it - so many people come to lessons (many people take on tasks in one class at once). All this was very strange to me, but I gradually got used to it. At school 57 I had very good classes: 7 and 8 mathematics and 9 biology. There were never any problems with them; they really wanted to learn. At the same time, I really wanted to tell them something - but I didn’t know a lot, I didn’t do anything for three years, one might say. I had to sit at night, solve problems, and get used to it all. The first time was quite difficult while I was getting into it. Thank God, there was wonderful discipline - it means a lot when they listen to you and help you. You could make a mistake - they would correct you, and it was all very friendly. I still make mistakes. They give a hint. I consider myself terribly lucky. After all, I might not have gotten here. My mother-in-law has already found me a job at an evening school - for 24 hours, 4 times a week. I was already ready to go there - if not for Kronrod, not for his exam and not for Konstantinov. Evening school would be worse.
