Tasks

1.

The conference was attended by representatives of two competing companies “Index” and “Zugl” Alexey, Boris and Vladimir. Representatives of the same company always tell the truth to each other and lie to competitors. Alexey told Boris: “I’m from the company

"Index". Boris replied: “Oh! You and Vladimir work in the same company!” Is it possible to determine from this dialogue where Vladimir works? M. Evdokimov

2.

There are several squares on the chessboard, as shown in

⟦column⟧

figure. In how many ways can you place 8 rooks that do not attack each other on unshaded squares? E. Bakaev

3.

Ilya doesn’t like speed problems at all and doesn’t remember a single formula. When he was asked how far the train would travel, he tried to multiply the given speed and time, and add them, and even divide the speed by time. “I always get the same number! This is probably the right answer!” – Ilya exclaimed. Prove that Ilya also cannot perform arithmetic operations. A. Antropov

4.

Illustrations by D. Grishukova

Cut the wire frame of the cube into two parts, from which the frames of two cubes can be soldered. The wire can be bent. S. Tokarev

These problems are primarily intended for students in grades 6–8. Problems 1 and 3 were proposed at the XLI Tournament named after M.V. Lomonosov, problems 2 and 4 - at the XXIV Tournament of Mathematical Combat named after A.P. Savin.
