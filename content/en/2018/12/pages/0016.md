The acoustic energy emitted by the ultrasonic transducer is concentrated into a volume approximately equal to the volume of a grain of rice

In the focal region, the intensity increases noticeably, and heating due to wave absorption is sufficient for thermal destruction of tissue proteins. This makes it possible to non-invasively “cauterize” the site of internal bleeding or cause necrosis of tumor tissue in deep areas of the human body. The most promising, from the point of view of expanding the use of ultrasound methods in medicine, are hemostasis (stopping bleeding), surgery and stimulation of the immune response. You can also mention ultrasonic control and intensification of drug transport. It has been experimentally shown that ultrasound can improve the transport of drugs and genes through biological barriers: cells, tissues and blood clots. Let us point out some of the main problems that need to be solved for the successful use of intense ultrasound in practice. One of the important tasks is to obtain large values ​​of the amplitude of the acoustic wave at the focus, taking into account the structure

tours of the human body. Amplification of the ultrasonic wave during focusing is necessary to ensure high intensity in a small focal area, so as not to damage other areas of the tissue along the path of ultrasound propagation. Ultrasonic skin burn is one of the characteristic side effects when using intense ultrasound, since the absorption coefficient of ultrasound in the skin is several times higher than in tissue. Therefore, in this area the acoustic intensity should be as low as possible. This procedure can be implemented using multi-element ultrasonic antennas, the radiation of which will be consistent with the structure of the body through which the radiation must pass. Technical developments to create good acoustic matching of the ultrasonic emitter with the body are also important. The fact is that ultrasonic emitters are made, as a rule, from piezoelectric ceramics. And in order to ensure the best transmission of sound energy into the human body, it is necessary to agree on the conditions for the passage of sound from solid piezoceramics to soft biological tissues. For this purpose, special contact lubricants or liquids are used.
