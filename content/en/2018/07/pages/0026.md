Problem 4. Given two squares and a segment connecting their vertices (Fig. 4). Prove that the two dotted line segments are equal and perpendicular. Note. Use the previous problem. Solution. This task is the reverse of the previous one. In it we proved that the vertex of the green square is the midpoint of the segment connecting the vertices of the red squares. But the segment has only one middle, which means that the dotted segments are equal and perpendicular, since these are the sides of the green square from the previous problem.
