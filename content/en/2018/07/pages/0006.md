(Fig.3). Such measured walking already represents a controlled sequence of falls. It is even called otherwise a series of reflexively controlled falls. At the beginning of a step, a person, standing on one leg, leans forward and begins to fall. During the fall, the leg extended forward touches the ground, and the inertia of the movement transfers the body weight to it. The knee, absorbing the fall, bends, then straightens and the process repeats. Such normal walking during daily exercise has become automatic, and the operation of the position control system during walking occurs reflexively. At the same time, a whole range of problems are solved: smooth walking, preservation of gait, minimal energy consumption. The muscles tense and relax in the right sequence.
