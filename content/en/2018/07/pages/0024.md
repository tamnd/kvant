KVAN T 2018/№7

Combinations of squares

E.BAKAEV

TO SOLVE THE PROBLEMS FROM THIS ARTICLE, it is enough to know the syllabus of the 7th grade school geometry course: signs of equality of triangles, the sum of the angles of a triangle, and also what a square is. With this “young geometer kit” we offer a path to some challenging and, we hope, beautiful problems. These problems, as often happens in geometry, can be solved in different ways. For example, many can be solved briefly using vectors or complex coordinates. But our goal is not to show the shortest solutions, but to let the reader (and solver) see the connection in the chain of problems, the geometric structure common to them. Solutions to problems are written briefly. We mean that some logical transitions are not obvious and require the reader to think independently. We also suggest in these problems not to pay attention to other possible cases of the relative arrangement of objects, but to rely on figure, which sets the condition.
