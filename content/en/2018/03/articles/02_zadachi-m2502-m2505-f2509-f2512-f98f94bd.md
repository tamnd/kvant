PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2502–M2505 were offered at the regional stage of the XLIV All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics. Problem F2509–F2512 were offered at the 2018 All-Siberian Open Olympiad for Schoolchildren in Physics.

M2502. Petya and Vasya take turns writing down natural numbers on the board that do not exceed 2018 (writing down an existing number is prohibited); Petya begins. If after a player's move there are three numbers on the board forming an arithmetic progression, that player wins. Which player has a strategy that is guaranteed to win? M. Didin, P. Kozhevnikov

M2505. There are 100 children in the company, some of the children are friends (friendship is always mutual). It is known that when any child is identified, the remaining 99 children can be divided into 33 groups of three so that in each group all three are friends in pairs. Find the smallest possible number of pairs of friendly children. S. Berlov, N. Vlasova, D. Khramtsov

F2509. An open car without a roof (railway workers call them gondola cars) with a height H = 4.5 m moves at a constant speed u = 6 m/s. As soon as the carriage passed the loader, he immediately tried to throw a small bag over the back wall of the carriage. What minimum speed v (in m/s) must be imparted to the bag? It is known that when throwing a bag, the loader “releases” it at a height h = 1 m from the ground level. Gravity acceleration g = 9.8 m $с^{2}$. Neglect air resistance. A. Kipriyanov
