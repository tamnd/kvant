Problem 2. Wire A long wire consists of three parts connected in series one after another. The first part, a quarter of the length of the entire wire, has a linear density of $O_{1} = 30$ g/dm. The second part, weighing a third of the mass of the entire wire, has a linear density $O_{2}$. The mass of the third part is equal to the sum of the masses of the first two. Determine the average linear density $O_{cp}$ of the entire wire. What is the minimum linear density $O_{2}$ for the second part of the wire? Note. The linear density of extended bodies O is the mass per unit of their length.

Problem 4. Faster, but slower A kettle with water at a temperature of $t_{0} 20 q C$ was heated on a gas burner to $t_{1} 40 q C$ in a time of $W_{1} = 2$ minutes. Wanting to speed up the heating, half of the water was poured out, and after another $W_{2} = 1$ minutes the water temperature reached $t_{2} 55 q C$. Since this also seemed slow, they poured out another half of the remaining water, but in doing so accidentally touched the burner tap, reducing its power by half. After what time $W_{3}$ will the kettle heat up to $t_{3} 100 q C$? Heat losses to the environment can be neglected.
