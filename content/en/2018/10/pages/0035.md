...dividing the sky into constellations is subjective. Thus, the 7 stars of the familiar Ursa Major Dipper in China have long been distributed into two constellations, consisting of horses and a carriage.... Pythagoras believed that several heavens revolve around the Earth - hollow spheres on which planets and stars are fixed. He considered all these luminaries (including the Sun) to be holes in dark spheres, through which the “heavenly fire” filling the Universe is visible.... the information obtained by Galileo using a telescope became the first results of experimental astrophysics. Thus, there were many more stars than previously thought; nebulae, including the Milky Way, appeared as huge collections of stars; small stars near Jupiter actually turned out to be its satellites. Having been the first to use the expression “speed of light,” Galileo proposed using a telescope in an earthly experiment to measure it.... to the long-time inhabitants of the “star zoo” - white and red dwarfs - “brown” dwarfs have recently been added.
