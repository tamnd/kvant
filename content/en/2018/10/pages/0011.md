Generalized division with weak envy

Now let's get back to the main case of envy. This time we will try not to generalize the condition of the problem, but, on the contrary, to weaken it. Let there be no general algorithm for the division yet, but perhaps it is possible to make an “almost division”, when the robber agrees that someone received more, but not more than by some initially chosen small number H. Continuing this thought further, let us ask the question: can the robbers divide the loot into two equal parts, and each of them believes that this ratio is fulfilled accurate to H? “Accurate to H” in this case means that each part differs from 1 2 by no more than H in the opinion of each of the robbers.
