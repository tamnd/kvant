Seryozha could be any number of years old, not exceeding 10. A non-leap year has 365 days. Since 365 when divided by 7 leaves a remainder of 1, the day of the week on which the birthday falls shifts by 1 with each such year (instead of Monday it will be Tuesday, instead of Tuesday it will be Wednesday, and so on). If between two birthdays there is February 29 of a leap year, then there will be a shift of 2 days. A leap year occurs either after 3 years on the fourth, or after 7 years on the eighth (but only once every 100 years). Let's record the longest sequence of days of the week falling on the same date in consecutive years between two Fridays. The days of the week will be consecutive, and in leap years one day will be skipped. In order for Seryozha to be over seven years old, at least one Friday must be missed. In one direction from this Friday (for example, towards the future) the leap year will be 4 years away, so the sequence will be as follows: Sat-Sun-Mon-Wed-Thu-Fri. In the opposite direction (to the past) it is either the same, and then the complete sequence is Fri-Sat-Sun-Tue-Wed-Thu-Sat-Sun-Mon-Wed-Thu-Fri. There are 10 years between two Fridays. In the first year Seryozha was born, and then he turned 1, 2, ..., 9 years old. Or seven non-leap years go in a row towards the past, and the sequence between two Fridays is a year longer: Fri-Sat-Sun-Mon-Tue-Wed-Thu-Sat-Sun-Mon-Wed-Thu-Fri. There are 11 years between two Fridays. In the maximum case, this will be from zero to tenth years of Serezha’s life inclusive.
