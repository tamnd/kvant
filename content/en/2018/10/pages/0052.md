6. Demonstration task (a video is shown). Two unequal balls - large and small - are suspended on threads close to each other. At the beginning of the experiment, the balls are motionless, the threads are vertical, and the centers of the balls are at the same height. One of the balls was deflected and released, after which collisions of the balls were observed. It turned out that if you deflect a small ball and release it, then after each odd collision the balls move in different directions, and after each even collision the large ball turns out to be motionless. If you deflect the large ball and release it, then after each odd collision the balls move in one direction, and after each even collision the small ball is motionless.
