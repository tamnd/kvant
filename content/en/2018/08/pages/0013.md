French physicist Bernard Bigot, and 80-year-old E.P. Velikhov became an honorary consultant of the council, and he continues to take an active part in its work. National ITER agencies have been established in all participating countries. In Russia, such an agency is part of the state corporation Rosatom. One of the most important tasks of the ITER Council is to determine the work plan and construction budget for each year and, most importantly, the distribution of work on the creation of individual components and assemblies of the reactor among the participating countries. The total estimated funding volume for 2018 is approximately 2 billion euros. It is divided into 11 parts, or shares. Russia, the USA, China, South Korea and India each have one share, Japan has 2 shares, the European Union has 4 shares, which it distributes among individual European countries (in total, 26 countries are participating in the project). The participation of these countries is not only in financing the construction of the reactor itself. They also finance research, engineering projects and the fabrication of those design elements that the ITER Council and the ITER National Agencies are tasked with completing and delivering to the construction site. Why did we highlight the issue of delivery separately? Here is a small touch that characterizes the grandiose scale of the construction. Individual parts and installations weigh tens and hundreds of tons. Thus, some parts made in Russia weighed 50 tons. They were delivered by sea to the German port of Kiel, and from there along the autobahn on multi-ton trailers to the south of France. But there have been and will be “parts” weighing 300 tons or more. How to deliver them? In 2017, gas holders for storing cooled helium were manufactured in South Korea. The mass of each gas tank is 300 tons, diameter is 6 meters, height is 30 meters. From the Far East they were delivered by sea to the inland port of the Mediterranean Sea near Cannes and loaded onto special platforms -

trailers. But to support such a mass, the trailer needs to have 354 wheels! Along the modernized 100-kilometer road, trailers moved at a speed of 5 kilometers per hour, accompanied by a convoy, at night when all traffic stopped. Now these gas tanks, installed by huge cranes, proudly flaunt in the places designated for them according to the project. Both the cranes and platform trailers were manufactured in France specifically for this construction site. More than 2,000 engineers, technicians and workers are currently employed in construction, and more than 400 employees are involved in the construction headquarters and scientific management. There is a small residential village, hotel-type dormitories, medical and child care facilities. Transport is provided for school-age children to the town of Cadarache. The total number of everyone involved in the ITER project in 26 countries is estimated at 200,000 people. It is incredibly difficult to coordinate the work of this enormous team. It is very important to meet deadlines for completing individual components to ensure continuity of work at a construction site. The current Director General of the ITER Council, Bernard Bigot, regularly visits all participating countries, observing and monitoring work in them. In Russia, the national agency for ITER involved 10 research and design centers, 5 large factories with a total number of employees of about 20,000 people. The General Director of the ITER Council at a recent meeting called Russian participation in the project the most reliable both in terms of timing and quality of work. To get an idea of ​​what is being built there in France, you need to look a little more closely at the operating principles and design of this thermonuclear reactor. But more on that next time.
