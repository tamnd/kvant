XXIV Tournament of Mathematical Fights named after A.P. Savina

The XXIV Mathematical Fights Tournament named after A.P. Savin was held from June 26 to July 2, 2018 in the Kostroma region at the Berendeyev Polyany recreation center. The tournament was attended by 34 teams of schoolchildren who graduated from grades 5–9 from Moscow, St. Petersburg, Yaroslavl, Ivanovo, Vologda and Zhukovsky. The tasks were compiled and selected by the jury under the leadership of A. Gribalko and the senior leagues: M. Khachaturyan - 5th grade, E. Bakaeva - 6th grade, A. Peshnin - 7th grade, M. Artemyev - 8th grade. Based on the results of the team Olympiad, sixth grade teams were divided into two leagues: the highest and the first. The following teams became the winners and prize-winners of the tournament (the team leader at the tournament is indicated in brackets). League of 5 classes, 1st degree diploma: Vologda Mathematical Lyceum-1 (A. Chegodaev), 2nd degree diploma: Moscow-1543-5-1 (I. Raskina), 3rd degree diploma: Moscow-1543-5-2 (I. Raskina). Major League 6 classes 1st degree diploma: Moscow-TsDO-6B (K. Skoptsov), 2nd degree diplomas: Moscow-2007-6-1 (R. Hristolyubov) and Zhukovsky-5-6 (I. Preobrazhensky), 2nd degree diploma: Moscow-1514-6-1 (O. Morozova), 3rd degree diploma: Moscow-New School-6 (N. Martynova). League of 7 classes, 1st degree diploma: Yaroslavl-7 (I.
