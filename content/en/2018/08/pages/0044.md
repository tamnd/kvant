The final stage of the LII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics

This year, the final stage of the All-Russian Physics Olympiad took place from April 9 to April 14, 2018 in the city of Tyumen. 286 schoolchildren (93 ninth-graders, 105 tenth-graders and 88 eleventh-graders) took part in the Olympiad. The Olympiad consisted of two rounds: the first was experimental, the second was theoretical. The duration of each tour was 5 astronomical hours. The Olympiad was held for each of the age groups of 9th, 10th and 11th grades. At the experimental round, each class was asked to complete 2 tasks, and at the theoretical round, they were asked to solve 5 problems. The tasks of the experimental round of the Olympiad covered the main sections of the school curriculum in physics: mechanics, thermodynamics and molecular physics, electrodynamics, oscillations and waves. The tasks of the theoretical round of the Olympiad were formed in the same order. The experimental tasks for grades 10 and 11 turned out to be traditionally difficult. But in general, the Olympiad participants successfully completed all the tasks. Before the appeal, assignments were analyzed for the Olympiad participants and the criteria for evaluating assignments were clarified. As a result, the Jury decided to award Olympiad Winner diplomas to 22 participants and Prizewinner diplomas to 104 participants (9th grade - 8 Winners and 34 Prizewinners, 10th grade - 6 Winners and 39 Prizewinners, 11th grade - 8 Winners and 31 Prizewinners). Based on the results of the Olympiad, a group of candidates for the Russian Federation was formed to participate in the International Physics Olympiad in 2019 and a group of candidates for the Russian Federation to participate in the International Science Olympiad for Juniors in 2018. Below are the tasks of the theoretical round of the final stage and the list of Winners of the LII All-Russian Physics Olympiad.
