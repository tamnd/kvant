Problem 2. A car on a conveyor A car moves along a horizontal stationary conveyor at a speed of $v_{0} 20$ m s in calm weather. In this case, half of the engine power is spent on overcoming air resistance, the other half on overcoming rolling friction. 1) The wind blew towards the car at a speed of $v_{0} 20$ m s (relative to the ground). At what steady speed $v_{1}$ relative to the ground will the car move if the power developed by the engine has not changed and the conveyor is stationary? 2) At some point, the wind died down, and the conveyor began to move at a constant speed of $v_{0} 20$ m s in the direction opposite to the movement of the car. At what steady speed $v_{2}$ relative to the ground will the car move if the power developed by the engine has not changed? Note 1. The force of air resistance is proportional to the square of the relative speed, the rolling friction force is constant. Note 2: In all cases, wheel slippage does not occur. Note 3: A third degree equation can be solved by fitting. V.Petrov
