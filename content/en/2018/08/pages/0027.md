4. (8) All natural numbers from 1 to $n^{2}$ need to be arranged in a table of size n ¥ n so that in each “corner” of three cells the sum of the numbers is not divisible by 3. For what n is this possible? A. Gribalko

5. (8) Of the sixteen coins, two are counterfeit and lighter. If you put a counterfeit coin and a real coin on a scale, the scale will remain in balance, but the two real coins outweigh the two counterfeit ones. Is it possible to find both counterfeit coins in no more than six weighings? A. Zaslavsky, K. Knop

6. (8) Petya and Vasya play a game on a strip of 1 ¥ N cells. Petya goes first, placing a chip on any cell. Next, Vasya moves it 3 or 4 cells in any direction, then Petya moves the chip in the same way, and so on. You cannot place a chip on the same field twice. The one who cannot make a move loses. Which player can guarantee victory regardless of the opponent's moves? E. Bakaev

7. (8) The Shah has N stones weighing 1, 2,..., N carats (N > 2). He wants to make a necklace out of some of them so that the masses of any two neighboring stones in the necklace differ by at least twice. What is the greatest number of stones there can be?

8. (7–8) In the corner square of the board measuring 100 ¥ 100 squares there is a chess king. Petya and Vasya take turns making moves for them so that the distance from the center of the initial cell to the center of the one in which the king is located increases after each move. The one who cannot make the next move loses. Who can win, regardless of the opponent's moves? A. Gribalko

9. (8) In a volleyball tournament, the team with a positive difference between won and lost games scored the fewest points. What is the smallest number of teams that could participate in this tournament? (Each team played one match with each, which consists of several games until three victories of one of the teams. If the game score is 3: 0 or 3: 1, then 3 points are given for a victory, 0 for a defeat; if the game score is 3: 2, for a victory - 2 points, for a defeat - 1 point.) A. Zaslavsky
