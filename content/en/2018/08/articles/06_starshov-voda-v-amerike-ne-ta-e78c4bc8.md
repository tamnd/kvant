OUR OBSERVATIONS

Water in America is not the same

M.STARSHOV

I HAVE BEEN USING FOR MANY YEARS an excellent book by the American physicist, Nobel laureate Leon Cooper, “Physics for Everyone” (M.: Mir, 1973), the word “using” is not even suitable to express my attitude towards it. But, as the old proverb says, even an old woman gets screwed. How do smart professionals write books? Easily! But almost everyone has problems with illustrations. Then the artist appears on the stage. The author gives instructions: you need to draw this way and that way. And this specialist knows his business! And on page 107 of the first volume of the mentioned wonderful book we see the marvelous figure (Fig.1). At the same time, we read the absolutely correct text: “When we lower the oar into the water, we see it broken. Our eyes and brain prefer to see it broken, rather than admit that the light in this case does not travel in a straight line.” (Unfortunately, the same figure is repeated in the book on page 213.) Here is the mysterious “Mary Celeste” (Mary Celeste, “Mary of Heaven”, former “Amazon”) with the crew missing without a trace. Or perhaps the artist imagined Huckleberry Finn's shuttle after a powerful night thunderstorm on the Mississippi. Everything is beautiful, but the oar... is a little broken, and partly in the wrong direction. What to do, such water in America! The camera sees this physical problem quite differently in a model experiment with our water (Fig.2).
