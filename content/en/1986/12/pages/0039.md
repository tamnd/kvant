⟦folio 37⟧

From triangle $C'O'D$ we have:
$$|C'D|^2 + |C'O'|^2 = |O'D|^2,$$
or
$$l^2 \mathrm{tg}^2 \beta + x^2 = R^2.$$ (2)

From (1) and (2) we find:
$$\cos \gamma = \frac{\sqrt{R^2-x^2}}{R} \sin \beta = \frac{\sqrt{R^2-x^2}}{R} \frac{\sin \alpha}{n}.$$ (3)

The physical meaning of (3) is quite clear - with increasing $x$ the value of $\gamma$ decreases, therefore, sin $\gamma$ increases, and at some $x_0$ the angle $\gamma$ reaches the value of the limiting angle of total internal reflection $\gamma_0 = \arcsin \frac{1}{n}$. Let us determine from which $x_0$ the condition sin $\gamma_0 = \frac{1}{n}$ is satisfied:

$$\frac{1}{n} = \sqrt{1-\cos^2 \gamma_0} = \sqrt{1-\frac{(R^2-x_0^2)}{R^2} \frac{\sin^2 \alpha}{n^2}},$$
where
$$x_0^2 = R^2 \left( \frac{1+\sin^2 \alpha-n^2}{\sin^2 \alpha} \right).$$ (4)

So, for all rays incident on the end of the cylinder at a distance $x \geqslant x_0 = \frac{R}{\sin \alpha} \sqrt{1+\sin^2 \alpha-n^2}$ from the vertical diameter of the end, when the ray first falls on the side surface of the cylinder, the condition of total internal reflection is satisfied. (From (4), in part, it follows that for $n^2 = 1+\sin^2 \alpha$ this condition is satisfied for all rays ($x_0=0$).)

If, at the first incidence of a ray on the glass boundary, it experiences total internal reflection, then at all subsequent incidences on the boundary it will also experience total internal reflection; if, at the first incidence on this boundary, the conditions of total internal reflection are not fulfilled, then it will continue to be fulfilled in the future. (Show this yourself.) Thus, in the absence of a light-proof plate, the photocell receives rays that fall on the input end in the area protected by figure 4, $a$. If there is plastic with a slot, the rays do not enter the photocell if the slot width is $d < 2x_0$. If $2R \geqslant d > 2x_0$, the luminous flux received by the photocell is determined by the rays that fall on the input end in the area protected by figure 4, $b$. The amount of flow is proportional to the protection area.

So: at $d \leqslant 2x_0$ Ф=0; at $2R \geqslant d \geqslant 2x_0$

$$\Phi = E \left( S_0 - 2R^2 \arccos \frac{d}{2R} + d \sqrt{R^2 - \frac{d^2}{4}} \right),$$
Where
$$S_0 = 2R^2 \arccos \frac{x_0}{R} - 2x_0 \sqrt{R^2-x_0^2} = \text{const}$$
for given values ​​of $R$, $\alpha$ and $n$ (see (4)).
