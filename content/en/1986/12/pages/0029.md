F1029. When a scab of water falls stationary on a flat saucer, the following picture can be observed: at a certain radius $r$ from the place where the scab falls, the water level is very low, and at a distance $r$ the level experiences a jump (Fig. 2). Estimate the radius $r$ if the water flow is $q$, the fall height is $H$, the height of the water step is $h$. Assume that the initial speed of water flowing from the tap is $v_0 \ll \sqrt{2gH}$.

A. A. Alabuzin, A. V. Umansky

F1030. A body of mass $m$ rises on a weightless thread with rigidity $k$. The maximum tension that the thread can withstand is $T$. The body is raised to a height $x$ from the equilibrium position and released. At what minimum $x$ will the thread break?

V. G. Kharitonov

F1031. The conduction sphere broke into several fragments, scattering great distances from each other. The fragments are connected in random order with thin wires. What is greater: the electrical capacity of the resulting system of fragments or the electrical capacity of the sphere? Neglect the capacitance of the wires.

I. M. Lutsenko, 10th grade student

F1032. On automatic vehicles, the rear view mirror is used to view the ongoing task. This mirror can be flat or convex. It is widely believed that one of the disadvantages of a convex mirror is the uneven growth of the image of objects uniformly approaching it, which is why the driver of an automatic vehicle with such a mirror pays attention to the automatic vehicle catching up in the flow of traffic only when it leaves the lane to overtake. Is this idea correct?

Find the relationship between the speed of uniformly moving water of the main optical axis of a spherical mirror of an object and the rate of change in the size of its image in the mirror. Compare a convex rear view mirror with a flat one. What are its advantages and disadvantages?

V. A. Kotov
