⟦folio 48⟧

XXVII
International Mathematical Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. P. SAVIN,
T. A. SARYCHEVA,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. A. FOMIN

When you remember the ten days spent in Poland at the XXVII International Mathematical Olympiad, you remember the streets and squares of the revived Warsaw, a small house in the park of the city of Zhelazova Wola, where Federac Chopin was born, conversations with the leader of the teams (and this time there were 37 of them), joys and sorrows during the inspection of work and much more.

But the most vivid impression was probably that moment during the official closing of the Olympiad, when the three best participants of the Olympiad, who scored 42 points out of 42 possible, were called to the stage - our Volodya Roganov and Slava Smirnov and the Hungarian Kush Giza. You could see this moment in the television program “Time”.

In addition to them, another 15 participants who scored at least 34 points also received first prizes; 41 participants received second prizes (from 26 to 33 points) and 48 received third prizes (from 17 to 25 points). American schoolboy Joseph Keane was awarded a special feature for his original solution to problem No. 3.

After the closing of the Olympics, photographs were taken for memory. The joint photograph of the two teams of the USSR and the USA attracted everyone's attention, and not only because the relaxed communication of these teams symbolized the atmosphere of friendship that reigned at this mathematics festival, but also because they shared 1st and 2nd places in the unofficial team event, scoring 203 points each.
