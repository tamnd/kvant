Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute (MIPT) is enrolling students from eight summer and secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1987/88 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students study independently to deepen their knowledge of physics and mathematics. When admitting to 3FTS, preference is given to students living in rural areas and workers' settlements, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students (at least 8-10 people) who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A circle is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the surname, first name, absence of the head of the circle and a list of members of the circle (indicating the class in the 1987/88 academic year and the final grades for the introductory assignment to the circle and mathematics). All materials on organizing the circle and an envelope for a response about the admission of the circle to 3FTSH with a return address addressed to one of the leaders of the circle should be sent to 3FTSH (141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTSH, “circle”) before May 25, 1987. (Notebooks with the work of circle members are not sent to 3FTSH.)

Students of 3FTS and heads of physics and technology clubs will receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program, as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The assignments contain theoretical material and an analysis of the characteristics of the assignment and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 problems for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of correspondence students is checked by the 3FTS and its branches, and the members of the circle are checked by its leaders.

Moscow students receive classes in physics and mathematics twice a week according to the 3FTSH program at evening consultation centers (in a number of Moscow schools). Recruitment to these groups is carried out either based on the results of completing the introductory task of 3FTSH, or on the basis of successful training in physics and mathematics (for information, call 408-51-45).

Each student completes the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics independently. The work must be done in Russian and carefully rewritten in one school terrace. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment. Send the notebook in a large envelope with a simple parcel post (just don’t weld it into a tube). Along with your decision, be sure to send a certificate from the school where you teach, indicating your class. Stick the certificate on the inside of the terrace. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered.
