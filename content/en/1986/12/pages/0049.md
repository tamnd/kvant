⟦folio 47⟧

ΔN = N₀ - N₀ · 2⁻τ/T = N₀(1 - 2⁻τ/T),
where N₀ is the initial number of radioactive atoms. According to Avogadro's law, N₀ = Nₐ m/M, where M = A × 10⁻³ kg/mol is the molar mass of cobalt. Now we finally get

$$C\Delta t = N_A \frac{m}{A \cdot 10^{-3}} \left(1 - 2^{-τ/T}\right) w,$$

from where we find the half-life T:

$$T = \frac{\tau}{\log_1/2 \left(1 - \left(C\Delta t A \cdot 10^{-3}\right)/(N_A wm)\right)} \approx 5700 \text{ с} \approx 95 \text{ мин.}$$
