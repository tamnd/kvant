**Let us denote by $A_1, B_1, X_1, Y_1$ the points of contact of the sphere with the edges BXY, XYA, YAB, ABX, respectively (see figure). Triangles $AXB_1$ and $AXY_1$ are equal (they have a common side $AX$, and $AB_1=AY_1$ and $XB_1=XY_1$ are tangents drawn to the sphere from a common point), therefore $\angle B_1AX = \angle Y_1AX$. Therefore, the sum of $\angle B_1AX + \angle AXY_1$ is equal to $\angle Y_1AX + \angle AXY_1$, that is, the external angle of the triangle $AY_1X$ at vertex $Y_1$ (at figure marked with a red bow). Likewise, the sum of $\angle A_1BX + \angle BXY_1$ is equal to the outer angle of the triangle $BY_1X$ at $Y_1$ (marked with a blue bow), so $\angle B_1AX + \angle AXB + \angle A_1BX = \angle AY_1B$. $$\angle B_1AX + \angle AYB + \angle A_1BY = \angle AX_1B.$$

Adding these equalities, we obtain on the left side the sum of angles considered in the problem, and on the right side - quantities that do not depend on X and Y (points $X_1$ and $Y_1$ are determined by the straight line $AB$ and the spheres are unique).

**a) Let $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_n$ be a sequence of numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$, arranged in ascending order. Since for any $i$ from 1 to $n$

$$\frac{i}{a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_i} \leq \frac{i}{b_1 + b_2 + \dots + b_i}$$

$$\frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{b_1} + \frac{1}{b_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{b_n},$$

it is enough to prove the inequality for the numbers $b_i$:

$$\frac{1}{b_1} + \frac{2}{b_1 + b_2} + \dots + \frac{n}{b_1 + b_2 + \dots + b_n} \leq 4\left(\frac{1}{b_1} + \frac{1}{b_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{b_n}\right).$$

Let us evaluate the terms on the left side as follows:

$$\frac{2}{b_1 + b_2} \leq \frac{2}{b_1 + b_1} = \frac{1}{b_1},$$
