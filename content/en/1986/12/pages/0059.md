9. The core was pushed horizontally, giving it a kinetic energy of 45 J. When falling into the sand, the velocity of the core was directed at an angle of 30° to the horizon. Find the amount of heat released when hitting the sand. The cannonball did not rotate during flight.

10. The puck slides across the ice of the hockey rink from one goal to another. The first half of the path, where the ice is widened, it moves in a region with a friction coefficient $\mu_1$, and the second half - with a friction coefficient $\mu_2$. Find the ratio of the friction coefficients if it is known that the speed of the puck at the first goal, in the middle of the ice and at the second goal is 4:3:1.

12. In a warm process, the volume of an ideal gas varies linearly with pressure according to the law $V=\beta p$, where $\beta$ is a constant. How many times does the gas pressure change when the temperature decreases from 450 K to 200 K?

13. Inside the installation, evacuated to a deep vacuum, there is a sealed heat-sealed vessel filled with an ideal monatomic gas. The vessel is closed on top with a heat-impregnated piston. Gas occupies volume $V$. A weight of the same mass as the mass of the piston is placed on the piston. Find the volume of the gas at the new equilibrium position.

After the chamber is thermized, an explosion occurs. Find the pressure inside the chamber after cooling the reaction products to a temperature of 373 K.

**Mathematics**

1. The boat goes down the river for 6 km and then returns back. The speed of the river is 2 km/h. Within what limits can the boat's own speed lie if it is known that the trip took at least 4 hours?

2. In a right triangle, the height, bissertation and median are drawn from the vertex of the acute angle. Prove that the dissertation divides the angle between the median and the height into two equal parts.

3. Find the last digit of the number $19^{87}-87^{19}$.

4. In the quadrilateral $ABCD$
$$\angle ABD=\angle ACD=90^\circ, AB=BD=5, CD=1.$$
Find $BC$.

5. Prove that there are no integers $x$ and $y$ such that $3x-y^2=1987$.

6. Prove that if $m$ and $n$ are natural numbers, then
$$\frac{(1+m+...+m^n)^2-m^n}{1+m+...+m^{n-1}}$$
- natural number.

7. Roots of the equation
$$x^3-3x^2+ax+1=0$$
reverse the argumentative progression. Find the value of parameter $a$ and solve the equation.

8. Find a polynomial of degree four with integer coefficients whose root is the number $\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}$.

9. Find all points on the globe that have the following properties: if you go from such a point 1 km to the south, then 1 km to the east and 1 km to the north, then you again end up at the starting point.

10. Given a parallelogram $ABCD$ of area $s$. Point $P$ lies on the line $BD$, $\vec{DP}=\vec{BD}$, point $Q$ lies on the line $DC$, $\vec{CQ}=\vec{DC}$. Find the area of ​​triangle $APQ$.

11. Solve the equation
$$4\sin^2x-4\cos x\cdot\sin y-5=0.$$

12. Regular triangles are considered on the coordinate plane, in which two vertices lie on the line $y=x+2$, and the coordinates of the triangles satisfy the inequality
$$x^2\leq y\leq x+2.$$
