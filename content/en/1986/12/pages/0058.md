Introductory task
Physics

1. A submarine moving uniformly sends out ultrasonic pulses of duration T as it moves. The pulses are reflected from a stationary object and return to the boat, and their duration is equal to r. Determine the speed of the boat if the speed of sound in water is v.

2. A man stands at a distance of 6 m from the river. A fire is burning 34 meters from the river. The distance between the perpendiculars connecting the straight bank of the river with the man and the fire is 30 m. The man runs at a speed of 5 m/s to the river, scoops up a bucket of water, then runs to the fire and pours it over. What is the minimum time he needs to do this if it takes 5 s to scoop up the water?

3. The height of the surface part of the punt boat is 20 cm. A foam plastic plate (density 0.4 g/cm³) 5 cm thick was glued to the bottom of the boat from the water side over its entire area. Determine the height of the surface part of the boat after gluing the foam plastic.

4. When making ice in a home refrigerator, it took 5 minutes to cool the water from +4 °C to 0 °C, and another 1 hour 40 minutes to turn it into ice. Determine the specific temperature of the boat if the specific temperature of the water is 4.2·10³ °C).

5. Store liquid nitrogen in a thermal container at a temperature of -195 °C in an amount of 2 liters. Within a day, half of the nitrogen had evaporated. Determine the specific temperature of nitrogen evaporation if it is known that 40 g of water melts in the same thermal container in 22 hours 30 minutes. The rate of supply of the body in thermos is proportional to the difference in temperature inside and outside the thermos. Ambient temperature 20 °C. The density of liquid nitrogen is 300 kg/m³.
