M1016—M1020; F1028—F1032

M1016. The polygon is described around a circle with center O. Let P be the center of mass of the polygon, K the center of mass of its contour. Prove that points P, O and K lie on the same line, and PO=2PK. (When determining the center P, we consider the polygon as a homogeneous plane, the center K - as a contour made of a homogeneous wire.).

I. Z. Vaishtein

M1017*. Each vertex of a regular pentagon is assigned an integer so that the sum of all five numbers is positive. The following operation is allowed: if trem consecutive vertices are assigned the numbers x, y, z and y<0, then these numbers are replaced by x+y, -y and z+y, respectively. Such operations are performed as long as at least one of the five numbers is negative. Does this process necessarily end in a finite number of steps?

M1018. Let A and B be adjacent vertices of a regular n-gon with center O. Triangle XYZ, equal to triangle OAB, first coincides with it, and then moves in the plane of the n-gon so that points Y and Z remain on the contour, and X remains inside the n-gon. What figure will point X describe when Y and Z make a full revolution along the boundary of the n-gon?

M1019. A certain finite set M of nodes (points of intersection of grid lines) is marked on a sheet of checkered paper. Prove that it is always possible to color some points of the set M white and the rest red so that on each grid line the difference between the number of white and red nodes modulo does not exceed 1.

M1020*. On a sphere of radius 1, a) a curve is drawn whose length is less than π;
b) a closed curve whose length is less than 2π. Prove that there is a plane passing through the center of the sphere that does not intersect the drawn curve. (We can consider that a curve on a sphere is a “broken line” consisting of two large circles.)

V. V. Prasolov, G. A. Galperin

F1029. When a scab of water falls stationary on a flat saucer, the following picture can be observed: at a certain radius $r$ from the place where the scab falls, the water level is very low, and at a distance $r$ the level experiences a jump (Fig. 2). Estimate the radius $r$ if the water flow is $q$, the fall height is $H$, the height of the water step is $h$. Assume that the initial speed of water flowing from the tap is $v_0 \ll \sqrt{2gH}$.

A. A. Alabuzin, A. V. Umansky

F1030. A body of mass $m$ rises on a weightless thread with rigidity $k$. The maximum tension that the thread can withstand is $T$. The body is raised to a height $x$ from the equilibrium position and released. At what minimum $x$ will the thread break?

V. G. Kharitonov

F1031. The conduction sphere broke into several fragments, scattering great distances from each other. The fragments are connected in random order with thin wires. What is greater: the electrical capacity of the resulting system of fragments or the electrical capacity of the sphere? Neglect the capacitance of the wires.

I. M. Lutsenko, 10th grade student

F1032. On automatic vehicles, the rear view mirror is used to view the ongoing task. This mirror can be flat or convex. It is widely believed that one of the disadvantages of a convex mirror is the uneven growth of the image of objects uniformly approaching it, which is why the driver of an automatic vehicle with such a mirror pays attention to the automatic vehicle catching up in the flow of traffic only when it leaves the lane to overtake. Is this idea correct?

Find the relationship between the speed of uniformly moving water of the main optical axis of a spherical mirror of an object and the rate of change in the size of its image in the mirror. Compare a convex rear view mirror with a flat one. What are its advantages and disadvantages?

V. A. Kotov

M1016. Let $P$ be the centre of mass of a polygon inscribed in a circle of centre $O, K$ — the centre of mass of its boundary. Prove that the points $P, O$ and $K$ are on one straight line and $PO = 2 \cdot PK$. (In determining the point $P$, we view the polygon as a uniform plate; for the point $K$ — as a uniform wire contour.)

I. Z. Vainshtein
