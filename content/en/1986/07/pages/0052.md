Games and puzzles

Trips
by graph

D. I. VAKARELOV (NRB)

The appearance of the famous Rubik's cube aroused great interest in puzzles and games based on the rearrangement of objects. For a mathematician, puzzles are also interesting primarily because the search for their solution is sometimes a very difficult mathematical problem that requires a lot of intellectual effort and wit.

We offer you a wide class of games that summarize the famous game of "fifteen" by Sam Lluyd. Some of them are as difficult as a Rubik's cube, and, unlike it, they are very easy to make, so anyone can come up with and make as many of these puzzles as they want. We will talk about two games in this class, and then give their general description.

Puzzle PATIENCE

Cut out eight circles the size of a five-dollar coin from cardboard and write

A= 2,1,4,6,7,0.
