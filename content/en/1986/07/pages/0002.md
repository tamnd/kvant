Today our guest is the Bulgarian scientific and popular mathematical magazine for schoolchildren “Mathematics”. The editor-in-chief of the magazine “Mathematics” R. Rusev will tell about this wonderful publication and its history on the first pages «Kvant». The problems and articles on mathematics on the following pages of the issue were written in our Bulgarian colleagues.

The great achievements of Bulgarian school mathematics - recognized places in the last two International Mathematical Olympiads, the victory of the city of Yambol at the just legalized City Tournament, the noticeable successes of the National Library of Belarus in the computerization of school education - communications specialties are due to the undoubted influence of the magazine "Mathematics". Readers «Kvant» will be able to imagine how interesting and useful the materials of our friends from Bulgaria are after reading this issue.
