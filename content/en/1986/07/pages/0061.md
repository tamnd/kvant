1. Carry out the following experiment. Using a pipette, carefully drop a drop of water onto the pan at room temperature. Calculate the time for complete evaporation of the drop. Then place the frying pan on the stove and turn it on. After two minutes, add a second drop and note the time it evaporates. Without turning off the stove, continue measuring the evaporation time of drops at two-minute intervals for 20-40 minutes. Draw a graph of the time of evaporation of a drop versus its number and explain it. It is recommended to carry out the experiment in a moderate heat, but so that in the end the temperature becomes noticeably higher than 100 °C.
