Discrete mathematics

The goals that precede the article in this direction are the following:

1. Strengthening the applied direction. The connection between discrete mathematics, the use of computers and computational mathematics, on the one hand, and applications to economics and humanities, on the other, makes the study of discrete mathematics necessary for most special secondary education.

2. Introduction to mathematical modeling. Discrete models have their own characteristics that allow them to be specialized (linear programming, mathematical models of languages, etc.).

3. Disclosure of the essential role of discrete mathematics for improving the computing culture of students.

4. Introduction to combinatorial analysis, which is becoming increasingly important in applied mathematics in our time. Many sections of probability theory and other disciplines are also associated with combinator.

5. Improving students' logical literacy.

Exactly what topics in this direction should be covered is a difficult question. But the editorial board of the magazine believes that they should first of all be ones that can be associated with the topics of the school course.

Scientific worldview

In connection with one of the main tasks of secondary school - the education of a scientific worldview - the magazine "Matematika" publishes articles showing the origin of mathematical concepts, explaining the role of mathematical abstractions, the power of mathematical methods that make it possible to simply and under certain conditions use questions of the natural world - physics, biology, astronomy and other sciences. By focusing special attention on the philosophical foundations of mathematics, we contribute to a deeper understanding of the subject and its methods, a broader view of the subject itself and its place in the system of scientific knowledge, and its role as a driving force of progress. Here are examples of articles in this direction: “Matematika and objective reality”, “Mathematical methods of punishment”, “Matematika as a profession”, “Talent and aesthetics in scientific creativity”, “Music of mathematics”, “Mathematical approach to physical considerations”.

History of mathematics

Familiarization with the history of science and scientific discoveries plays a huge role in the development of the scientific worldview of young people. The classics of Marxism-Leninism also point to the need for a scientific approach. By studying the complex path traveled by science, we can, to a certain extent, come closer to understanding its role today. Here are a few examples of articles presented for this purpose: “Ideas of N. I. Lobachevsky”, “From the history of mathematics”, “First steps from the history of counting”, “Matematika of the ancient peoples of Mesopotamia”, “From the history of logarithms”, “From number to figure”.
We also note the special place we devote to stories about the development of Bulgarian mathematics.
In the section “Famous Mathematicians,” readers are introduced to the lives of major scientists in the field of mathematics and, taking into account the level of readers’ training, with reviews of the scientific work of these scientists. We also include biographies of prominent Bulgarian mathematicians and some of their leading works.

The magazine “Matematika” strives to explore the work of students through the heading “Student Creativity” and “Competition Problems”. Back in the very first issue of the magazine, in the “Student Creativity” section, an article by a 9th grade student “Inequality between the arithmetic mean and the geometric mean of some numbers” was published.
