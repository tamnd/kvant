Figures similar to the playing fields of the considered puzzles are called graphs. Hence their common name - “travel through graphs”. More precisely, a graph is a finite set of points, called vertices, some of which are connected by arcs, called graph edges. Obviously, any graph can be associated with one or more iterations and groups of permutations that correspond to them. The reader may have already noticed that the groups of games considered are either signed variable (type $A_n$) or symmetric (type $S_n$). It turns out that this is not accidental: under some weights of general restrictions on the structure of graphs, they will always correspond to groups of either type $A_n$ or type $S_n$.
