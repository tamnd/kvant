⟦folio 64⟧

Newton - student
Newton - scientist

When Newton saw an apple fall, he found
In that slight startle from his contemplation —
'Tis said (for I’ll not answer above ground
For any sage’s creed or calculation) —

A mode of proving that the earth turn’d round
In a most natural whirl, called “gravitation”;
And this is the sole mortal who could grapple,
Since Adam, with a fall, or with and apple.

Lord Byron.

Positive illustration for a famous story
that the discovery of Newton's law of universal gravitation was brought about by a falling apple.

Newton, noticing an apple falling,
One has now learned the secrets of obligation -
The word is out. (There is no doubt:
Was it a calculation or an insight?)

And Newton proved the rotation of the Earth,
By introducing then understand gravity.
He is the first from Adam, without a doubt,
And he ate the fruit and caused the fall.
