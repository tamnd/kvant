M991. In triangle ABC, the height CH and median SC are drawn. On side AB, points E and F are chosen so that ∠ACE=∠BCF, and perpendiculars AM and BN (Fig. 1) are dropped onto rays CE and CF. Prove that points M, H, K and N lie on the same circle.

M992. Among 90 graduates of one mathematical hypnasium, each of them has at least 10 friends. Prove that any graduate can invite three friends to visit, so that among the four gathered, each will have at least two friends.

M993. a) Find 11 consecutive natural numbers whose sum of squares is the square of the natural number.
6)* Prove that for 2<n<11 there are no n consecutive natural numbers whose sum of squares is a square.

M994.* At what maximum value of k is the inequality
Is $$a^4+b^4+c^4+abc(a+b+c) \geq k(ab+bc+ca)^2$$ satisfied for all values ​​of a, b and c?

M995. The function y=f(x) for all x is defined, continuous and satisfies the conditions
$$f(f(x))=f(x)+x.$$
a) Find two such functions f.
6)* Prove that there are no other such functions.

F1003. The locomotive moves along a circular path of radius R with speed v. A light balloon of length L is attached to it, which moves at a constant speed at a height H. Determine the trajectory of the established motion of the ball.

D. Yu. Grigoriev

F1004. A thin single-pod rod, which can rotate freely in a vertical plane around a horizontal axis passing through the end of the rod, hangs motionless above the water (Fig. 2); length l, material density ρ (ρ is less than the density of water). Slowly lowering the axle, the stubble is immersed in water. Find the relationship between the angle of deflection of the stubble from the vertical and the distance from the axis to the surface of the water; plot this relationship.

D. V. Belov
