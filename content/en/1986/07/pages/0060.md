Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk Order of the Red Banner of Labor State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol runs a correspondence school (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 of secondary schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students’ interest in the natural sciences.

There are 5 departments in 3SH: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives, which work at the school under the guidance of a teacher. The head of the circle recruits and enrolls students in them who successfully complete the first assignment in the relevant subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader informs 3SH of his last name, first name, absence and sends a list of the circle’s notes (indicating the results of grades for the assignment period), signed by the school director and certified by the seal. After this, the circle is considered a 3Sh student.

Students accepted into 3SH and club leaders will receive 3SH assignments, as well as additional materials. The work of zone students is checked in 3SH, and the work of circle members is checked by its leader (at the request of the leader, some of the work of circle members can be checked in 3SH).

Every year, some students in grades 8-9 of 3Sh are invited to the Summer Scale at NSU (which runs from August 1 to August 22). Here they, together with the winners of the Vesibirsk Olympiad, give lectures to local scientists, solve interesting problems at seminars, get acquainted with the university and research institutes of the Academy Town, and have fun separately. During the winter holidays, 3Sh students from nearby regions are invited to the Winter Scale at NSU.

To become a student at the Zoological School at NSU, you must send an application written on a postcard to the director of 3Sh before September 30, with a request to send the assignment and additional materials for it.

An application for admission to the mathematics or physics department of 3Sh can be sent along with the solutions to the corresponding first assignment, published below, no later than October 15.

Solving the problem of the need to record in a simple student terrace in a cage, leaving the fields for a substitute teacher. On the cover of the terradi, include the same information about yourself as in the application. Send your work along with your application only in a simple parcel post (do not bend the notebook or fold it into a workbook). In the notebook with the solutions, send a piece of paper measuring 6x10 cm with your address written on it (it will be pasted on the envelope when the answers are given).

First math assignment

8th grade

1. The weight of the sleepers is 27.8 kg, and the weight of the oak sleepers is 45.5 kg. The weight of a dozen sleepers is 384.2 kg. How many medium pine trees?

2. The wrench has counters in the shape of a square with a side of 1 cm. Within what limits can the dimensions of the square of the nut be enclosed so that it can be unscrewed with this wrench?

3. Draw on the coordinate plane a set of points whose coordinates (x; y) satisfy the conditions

$$x \leq y - |2x - 2|.$$

4. How many real solutions does the equation have?

$$3\sqrt{x} + x + 2 = 0?$$

5. Prove that the product of consecutive natural numbers is divisible by 24.
