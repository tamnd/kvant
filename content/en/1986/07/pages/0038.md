F1005. There is an oven in which the temperature is constantly maintained at $T_1$, and a refrigerator in which the temperature is $T_0 < T_1$. A reel with a length of wire wound on it is installed in the refrigerator. The end of the wire comes out of the refrigerator, enters the oven and is attached to a reel installed there. The coils are rotated so that the wire is washed from the cold coil to the hot one, moving at a speed of $v$. Wire cross-section $s$, volumetric heat $c$. To keep temperatures $T_1$ and $T_0$ unchanged, it was necessary to increase the oven power by $\Delta E_x$ and reduce the cooling capacity of the refrigerator by the same amount. Find $\Delta E_x$. Assume that the heat exchange between the wire and the environment does not depend on the speed of the wire.

D. V. Pavlov

F1006. Determine the resistance of the resistor circuit shown in figure 3. Resistance values ​​are in ohms.

S. S. Krotov

F1007. A ray of light falls on a ball composed of two hemispheres, which are made of glasses with refractive indices $n_1$ and $n_2$ ($n_1 > n_2$). The ball begins to rotate with angular velocity $\omega$ around an axis perpendicular to the direction of the beam (Fig. 4). Find the maximum size of the light spot on the screen mounted on the ball.

L. G. Markovich

$$a^4 + b^4 + c^4 + abc(a + b + c) \geq k(ab + bc + ca)^2$$
holds for all values of $a, b$ and $c$?

$$f(f(x)) = f(x) + x.$$
a) Find two such functions $f$.
