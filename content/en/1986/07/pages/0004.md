Journal of the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League
them. Dimitrova
"Mathematics"
and its development

The People's Republic of Bulgaria is a country of a mature socialist society. Its citizens are creative individuals, fighters and transformers. He searches and analyzes, opening new paths in science and practice. Therefore, it is not enough for him to simply acquire modern knowledge; he must learn to think independently and effectively, and to approach the problems of life creatively. Hence the need for training personnel who were able to navigate changing conditions, the latest achievements of science and technology, and independently solve problems. We must prepare the generation so that it can organize the achievements of the scientific and technological revolution with the use of the socialist system, create and implement new things in science and science.

Mathematics has always been the basis of technical progress, therefore the Bulgarian Communist Party and the government considered the important issue of increasing the mathematical culture of young people.

In 1962, the Secretariat of the Central Committee of the BCP decided to create the magazine “Mathematics” for secondary school students. The magazine was to be published six times a year and was assigned the following tasks:

— introduce schoolchildren in a popular and attractive form to the major achievements of mathematics, its history and its creators;
- explain in more depth and detail the issues studied in the school mathematics course;
- support the practical applications of mathematics and its educational value;
— help students with extracurricular mathematical work;
— reflect interesting events in international mathematical life;
— introduce students to leading Russian mathematicians;
- interfere with materials on the mathematics being studied.

Our magazine began to solve these problems in its popular science articles and sections: “From the history of mathematics”, “Znamennye mathematicians”, “Competition problems”, “Problems”, “Problems for the matriculation certificate”, “Entertaining mathematics”, etc.

"School" topics

Popular science articles are related to curriculum; on the one hand, they relate to topics that expand the knowledge of schoolchildren on this issue, and on the other hand, to topics that deepen their knowledge. The averages of these topics are called:

1. Basic types of set mappings and numeric mappings.
2. Defined intergal and its applications.
3. Principles of continuity.
4. Vectors and their applications.
5. Properties of triangles and quadrilaterals.
6. Correct examples.
7. Equations and systems of equations.
8. Inequalities and systems of inequality.
9. Algebraic operations and their properties.

The journal covers not only topics from classical sections of mathematics, but also sections such as combinatorial analysis, mathematical logic, solution and graph theory, mathematical literature, many chapters of computational mathematics, that is, those sections that can be combined under a common title.
