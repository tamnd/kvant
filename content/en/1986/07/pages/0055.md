Exercises

2. Draw graphs of the action of the transformation $A^2, A^3, B^2, B^3, ABAB^{-1}A^{-1}$.
3. Let some token be stationary during the transformation $X$. Prove that it remains fixed under the transformation $P^{-1}XP$, where $P$ is an admissible transformation.

Identities like those given in Exercise 4 help simplify formulas, making their writing more compact, and in some cases, establish the identity of the formulas.
