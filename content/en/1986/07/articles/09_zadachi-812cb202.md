⟦folio 27⟧

Quests

1. Solve for number purposes the equation
$$xy + 3x - 5y = -3.$$

2. All points of a certain circle are randomly colored in two colors. Prove that there is an equilateral triangle with single-legged vertices inscribed in this circle.

3. The square of this sum was added to the sum of the digits of the two-digit number $A$ and the number $A$ was obtained. Find what $A$ is equal to.

4. Let $CM$ be the median of triangle $ABC$. It is known that $\angle CAB + \angle MCB = 90^\circ$. Prove that the triangle $ABC$ is rectangular or equilateral.

5. On a circle of radius 10 there are several non-intersecting arcs with a total length of 32. Prove that for any line there is a chord of the circle parallel to this line, the ends of which belong to two such arcs.

These tasks were offered to us by $A$. P. Savin and Bulgarian authors $K$. Kolarov, I. Tonov, S. Savchev, I. Prodanov.
