⟦folio 5⟧

From convention to convention

Outstanding successes have familiarized the same pathology. The construction of the main route of the Baikal-Amur Mainline was completed, thousand-meter oil and gas pipelines were put into operation, including the Urengoy-Uzhgorod gas transport pipeline; construction of the Sayano-Shushenskaya hydroelectric power station is being completed; A lot of work has been done to implement the Food Program. Our science also developed successfully. A series of new space flights were carried out at the Salyut-7 station, including the record flight of cosmonauts L. D. Kizim, V. A. Solovyov and O. Yu. Atkov, which lasted 237 days. Many of the tasks that our people worked to solve in the same pathology are of an additional nature. They are included in the draft of the new edition of the CPSU Program and the draft of the Main Directions of Economic and Social Development of the USSR for 1986-1990 and for the period until 2000.

The most important events in the life of our country, naturally, are reflected in stamps, as convincingly evidenced by the postage stamps reproduced here, issued during the years of the same pathology.

V. Rudov
