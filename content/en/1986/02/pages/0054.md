⟦folio 52⟧

Moscow State University named after. M. V. Lomonosova

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)

1. Solve the equation
$$6 \cos x - \frac{1}{3} = \sqrt{32 \cos x - \frac{17}{9}}.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{x-1} + \frac{3}{|x|+1} \geq \frac{1}{|x|-1}.$$

3. Some circles touch lines AB and BC respectively at points D and E. Point A lies between points B and D, and point C lies between points B and E. Find the area of ​​triangle ABC if the lengths of the sides AB and AC are respectively 13 and 1, and points A, D, E and C lie on the same circle.

4. Of the three p values: 0.24π; 0.26π; 0.6π - find those for which the equation
$$\left( \frac{p-5x}{\pi} + \log_3 \frac{3p}{\pi} \right)(6 \sin (p-x) - \sqrt{18}) = 0$$
has at least one solution satisfying the condition 0 < x < π/3.

5. On a plane a passing through the center of a ball of radius R, there is given a circle with center O₁ and radius r₁ located inside the ball. All points of this circle are connected by straight lines from point A, which belongs to the ball and is distant from the plane a at a distance R. The set of points of intersection of these straight lines with the surface of the ball different from A is a circle with center O₂ and radius r₂. Find the distance from point O₂ to plane a if the distance between points A and O₁ is equal to a.

Option 2
(Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics)

1. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 6x - 2 \cdot 3y = 2, \\ 6x \cdot 3y = 12. \end{cases}$$

2. In the trapezoid PQRS, the base length QR is 10, the diagonal length QS is 19, and the angle QSP is 30°. Find out which is greater: the length of the base QR or the length of the side RS.

3. Solve the equation
$$4 - \cos[2\pi (13x+9)^2] = 5 \cdot \sin[\pi (13x+9)^2].$$

4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{2 \log_{1-3}|x|(42x^2 - 14|x|+1)}{\log_{1-3}|x|(x - \frac{5}{6})^2} \leq 1.$$

5. Find all triples of numbers x, y, z satisfying the equation
$$\sqrt{\frac{3}{2} x^2 - 2y^2 + 2z^2 + 10z + 6y + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} x - 17} + \sqrt{3x^2 - 2\sqrt{3} (\cos \pi y + \cos \pi z)} x + 4 = 0.$$

6. All edges of the tetrahedron ABCD have equal length. On the edges AB, AC and AD, the corresponding points K, L, M are chosen so that the length of the segment KB is equal to 15, and the length of the segment MD is equal to 10. It is known that the radius of the ball inscribed in the tetrahedron ABCD is equal to $\frac{5}{2} \sqrt{6}$, and the volume of the pyramid AKLM is equal to 375 $\sqrt{2}$. Find the sum of the radii of two spheres: one inscribed in the pyramid AKLM and one circumscribed around it.

Option 3
(Faculty of Chemistry)

1. Solve the equation
$$4 (\sin 6x + \sin 4x) = \cos x (4 \sin^2 5x - 5).$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$5^{2x} - \frac{1}{3} x^2 < 5^{2x} - 2x (\sqrt{3}^5) x^2 + 24.$$

3. On the coordinate plane xOy, we consider a triangle ABC, in which vertex A coincides with the origin of coordinates, vertices B and C lie correspondingly on the parabolas y = 3x² - 10x + 2 and y = -2x² + 5x - 10, side BC is parallel to the ordinate axis, and the abscissa of vertex B belongs to the segment $\left[ \frac{3}{5}; \frac{3}{2} \right]$.

What value should the abscissa of vertex B have in order for the area of ​​triangle ABC to be greatest?

4. The height of the pyramid is 2, the base of the pyramid is a rhombus, the area of ​​which is 8, and the acute angle is π/6. Some sharqa touches a flat side face at a point lying on the side of the base of the pyramid. Prove that the straight line connecting the top of the pyramid to the center of the ball passes through the intersection point of the diagonal rhombus. Find the volume of the sphere.

5. Solve the system
$$\begin{cases} 2x + y + \log_2^3 (|y| - 2x + 5) - 20 = 0, \\ (2x + y)^2 - 7(2x + y) \log_2 (|y| - 2x + 5) - 8 \log_2^3 (|y| - 2x + 5) = 0. \end{cases}$$

Option 4
(Blues Faculty)

1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1 - x}{(2 - x)(x - 3)} + 1 = \frac{1}{2 - x}.$$

2. Find all the roots of the equation
$$3 \cos 2x - 5 \sin \left(x - \frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 1,$$

belong to the segment [π; 2π].

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{\log_5(3 - 2x)} - \frac{1}{4 - \log_5(3 - 2x)} < 0.$$

4. Point N lies on the hypotenuse LM of rectangle LKM. Point P is taken on straight line LM so that point M is between points N and P, and angle NKP is right. Find the area of ​​triangle NKM if it is known that the area of ​​triangles LKM and NKP are equal to a and b, respectively, and the angle LKP is equal to φ.
