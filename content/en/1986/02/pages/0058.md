4. Bodies with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ are located on the same vertical at a distance $h_1$ from each other, and a body with mass $m_2$ is located at a height $h_2$ from the horizontal surface (Fig. 9). A body of mass $m_1$, falling freely, is contained absolutely inelastically with a body of mass $m_2$, which is released at the moment of impact. Then this system of bodies is absolutely inelastically contained from a horizontal surface. Neglecting the change in the momentum of the bodies when they are contained with each other, determine with what force the system of bodies acts on the surface if the duration of the containment is $\Delta t$.

5. Two communicating cylindrical vessels with the same transverse segment with an area of ​​$S=10^{-2}$ m² contain mercury (Fig. 10). Water weighing $m=20$ kg is poured into one of the vessels on top of it and a weight weighing $m_1=7,2$ kg is lowered into it to float. How far $h$ will the mercury level move in the second vessel? The density of mercury is $q=13,6 \times 10^3$ kg/m³.

6. In a cylindrical vessel with a volume of $V=8,31$ m³, a gas weighing $m_1=3,2$ kg with a molar mass of $M_1=0,032$ kg/mol at a temperature of $T=300$ K was first pumped, and then a gas weighing $m_2=4,4$ kg with a molar mass of $M_2=0,044$ kg/mol was added at the same temperature $T$. What pressure is established in the vessel after a mixture of isothermal gases at a temperature of $T$ is compressed using a piston to a volume of $V/3$? Universal gas constant $R=8,31$ J/(mol K).

7. A liquid of density $q$ is poured into a $U$-shaped tube, sealed at one end with an elbow length $L$, and so much air remains in the sealed end, and the liquid level in the open elbow coincides with the edge of the tube (Fig. 11). Then some of the liquid is released through the tap. In this case, the fluid levels in the knees become equal and remain at a height of $L/2$. What was the initial height $x$ of the air column in the sealed elbow of the tube? Atmospheric pressure $p_0$.

8. Eight exact charges $|q_1|=...=|q_8|=9 \cdot 10^{-6}$ C are located at the vertices of a cube, the sides of which are $a=0,2$ m. Four positive charges are at the vertices of the lower base, four negative charges are at the vertices of the upper base of the cube. Determine the direction of the electric field at the center of the cube. Electrical constant $\varepsilon_0=8,9 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m.

9. A rod $l=0,20$ m long, at the ends of which there are small balls with charges $q_1=2 \cdot 10^{-5}$ Kl and $q_2=5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ Kl, can be wound around a vertical axis perpendicular to the rod and passing through its middle. The rod is in a uniform electric field with a direction of $E=10^3$ V/m. The lines of force are horizontal and make an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the direction of the rod. How much work is done by the electric force if the rod is rotated so that the strong lines make an angle of $\beta=45^\circ$ with its direction?

10. To what potential difference is the capacitor charged in the circuit shown in figure 12, if the source emf is $E=4,8$ V, its internal resistance is $r=1$ Ohm, $R_1=2$ Ohm, $R_3=5$ Ohm? What charge will be on the plates of the capacitor if its capacity is $C=2$ mK?

11. How many times is the efficiency $\eta_1$ of a power transmission line in a direction $U_1=200$ kV greater than the efficiency $\eta_2$ of a power transmission line in a direction $U_2=100$ kV, if the line resistance is $R=400$ Ohm and the transmitted power is $P=10^4$ kW?

12. A flexible closed conductor with a resistance of $R=100$ kOhm, forming a square on the side $a=0,1$ m, is placed in a uniform magnetic field with an indication of $B=5$ T. The plane of the square is perpendicular to the vector of the magnetic indication. Which one charges along the conductor if an equilateral triangle is made from a square without changing the plane of its location?

13. A luminous point $S$, located in a medium with a refractive index $n_1=1,33$, is viewed from a medium with a refractive index $n_2=1,5$ at a small angle to the normal drawn to the interface between the two media. What will be the apparent distance from point $S$ to the interface if the true distance is $h=0,133$ m?

14. A scattering beam of light, which is located in the board with a diameter of $d=10$ cm, passes through it and produces a round spot with a diameter of $D=5$ cm on the screen located behind the dock parallel to it. If a scattering beam with a focal length of $F=30$ cm is inserted into the rejection, the spot turns into a point. Find the distance between the board and the screen.

15. A satellite flying in a circular orbit at an altitude of $h=100$ km above the Earth is formed by a stationary camera, the objective moment has a focal length of $F=0,5$ m, making a exposure of $t=1$ s. How long will the image of the satellite be on the plate? Changes in gravity with altitude to cross. Assume that the radius of the Earth is $R=6300$ km, and the acceleration of free fall is $g=10$ m/s².
