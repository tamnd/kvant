The mystery of the Etruscans

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. S. ALEXANDROV

Archaeologists and historians are gradually collecting information about the Etruscans, a mysterious ancient people who once settled most of modern Italy. Around the middle of the 1st millennium BC. e. their witness reached a cultural and economic flowering. The Etruscan cities were also a formidable force militarily. But after the expulsion in 510 BC. e. Etruscan kings from Rome, the Etruscans in Italy had dangerous rivals in the person of the Romans. Long wars ended several centuries later with the conquest of the Etruscans, and at the beginning of our era they were completely dispersed among the motley mass of peoples of the Roman Empire. All that remains from them are a few inscriptions in a language that has not yet been fully deciphered, isolated monuments of the outstanding development of crafts and arts, and scant messages from Roman authors...

During excavations of the cities of Eturia, archaeologists were able to find a wide variety of evidence of the high level of development of the extinct civilization. The jewelry of the Etruscans and middle ages aroused universal admiration, primarily the so-called granular jewelry - genuine masterpieces of nameless masters. They are copper plates with complex patterns, lined with thousands of tiny (about 0.2 mm in diameter) gold balls. No other nation's granulated products reached such a high degree of perfection.

By the end of the 1st millennium AD. e. the art of making such jewelry was completely destroyed. Only in the 19th century did researchers present attempts to restore the secret techniques, but to no avail.
