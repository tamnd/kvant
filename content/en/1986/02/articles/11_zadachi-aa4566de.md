⟦folio 29⟧

Tasks

1. This year I posted my birthday. The number of years I have turned is remarkable in many ways.

If you subtract 2 from this number, then it will be divided by 3, and if you subtract 3 from it, then it will be divided by 2.

If you add 4 to it, it will be divided by 5, and if you subtract 5 from it, then it will be divided by 4.

If you subtract 5 from it, then it will be divided by 6, and if you subtract 6, then it will be divided by 5.

If you add 7 to it, it will divide by 8, and if you add 8 to it, it will divide by 7.

How old am I now?

2. Replace the letters with numbers so that both equalities are true:

(OH)³=VANYA, V+A+N+I=OH
(same letters should have the same numbers, and different letters should have different numbers).

3. The smoke we see is small particles of unburnt fuel. Their density is many times greater than the density of air.

Why do these particles rise up?

4. The other day I was frying pancakes. When I started to move one of them, it would not fit into its old place. We managed to place the pancakes back in the pan, just turn them all over.

Prove that you can always turn over the pancakes in the round frying pan in which they were previously placed, and give an example in which you cannot turn over one of the pancakes and put it back in its old place.

5. Cut each of the two small figures in the same way into four parts so that from the resulting eight parts you can fold a similar figure twice as large (in area).

These problems were proposed by V.D. Vyon, a 10th grade student. village Slavyanoserbsk, Voroshilovgrad region. Serzej Revkov, A. P. Savin, A. M. Abramov, L. P. Mochalov.
