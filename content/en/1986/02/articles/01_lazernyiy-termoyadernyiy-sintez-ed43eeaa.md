A wide range of work is being carried out in our country to control thermonuclear fusion. However, one of the promising areas in this area is laser thermonuclear fusion (LTF). The first page of the cover shows the central part of the multi-channel laser system “Dolphin” for research using LTS (P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute of the USSR Academy of Sciences).

With the help of the giant ring antenna of the radiotelector of the USSR Academy of Sciences (ring diameter 600 m; photo visa), astronomers can simultaneously receive radio emission from the planets of the Solar system, certain stars and other objects of the Universe that are extremely distant from us. RATAN-600 is a world record holder for information information.

Some other major scientific and technical developments will be discussed on the first pages of this issue.
