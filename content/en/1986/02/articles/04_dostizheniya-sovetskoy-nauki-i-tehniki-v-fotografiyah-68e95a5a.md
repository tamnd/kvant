On this and the following pages there are photographs highlighting some of the achievements of science and technology in our country

1. Drawings of the future tractor created by the plotter included in the EC-7054 kit. The computer allows you to calculate and depict an object from any angle in a few seconds.

2. A hydraulic washing process used to create high pressures to produce industrial diamonds.

3. In this installation, a process takes place with which scientists simulate photosynthesis: under the influence of light, compounds with a large supply of chemical energy should be formed in the solution.

4. This is how plasma spraying is carried out on parts of agricultural machines. The plasma released from the salt contains the finest powder. It is “slapped” onto the part with force, eliminating damage and defects and giving the part hardness and heat resistance.

5. Interior view of the Serpukhov accelerator at 76 GEV. Recently, a new part was discovered here - a glueball, with the help of which physicists will be able to “look” into the depths of particles that only yesterday seemed elementary.

6. Space station "Vega" on a test stand. In June, a year later, two stations heading to Halley's Comet transmitted valuable scientific information about Venus to Earth.

7. The world's first pilot plant Y-25 with a magnetohydroponic generator with a design capacity of 20 mesawatts. In such installations, thermal energy is completely converted into electrical energy.

8. One of the laser bow's professions is isotope separation. Various isotopes
