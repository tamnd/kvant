⟦folio 54⟧

Schoolchild – applicant – student – ​​engineer

Candidate of Technical Sciences
E. P. KAZANDZHAN

In this article, a mathematics teacher from one of the leading Moscow technical universities shares his thoughts on what qualities a future engineer should develop in himself. And although not all statements in these mathematical notes are indisputable, we hope that they will be useful to our readers, especially to ten-year students preparing to enter college.

Having taught mathematics at a university for many years, I was drawn to talk with some of the misconceptions in the psychology of students - applicants, students. Sometimes it seems that teachers and students speak different languages.

The first meetings are in July and August, when the admissions and university suffering begins. From year to year there are the same bewilderments: what is this arrow for? Did you just take your exams at school? Is there any attempt at all from exams? I have passed more than a hundred exams in my life, and I don’t remember any of them being useless. On the contrary, I can say that everything good in me is due to exams. What else organizes and mobilizes like this? Of course, this is not the only “doping” in training, but it is very powerful. My axiom: having passed the exam, I should learn something new about the passed subject and about myself, useful for the future; Therefore, passing the exam is a step forward in my development. And what about double exams during one summer... Alas, you have to take not two identical exams, but two different ones (fundamentally different!) according to the same program. The school exam is everyone’s lot and is the same for everyone; admission to a university is only for those who have decided to become an engineer. The school exam is aimed at the past - it is a report on the work done. And at the admissions exam, the examiner thinks about the future - will the applicant be able to become a full-fledged student, will he cope?
