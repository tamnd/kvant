The vessel is then slowly cooled until the piston touches the stops AA. Then the base of the spring is moved back to BB, the cylinder is pressed again, etc. Find the efficiency of this movement. The cylinder is filled with Zeile; piston area S=10 cm²; spring stiffness k=10 N/m, its length is not the distance of states l₀=60 cm. The external pressure is assumed to be zero.

Let us take into account that the length of the spring in the insoluble state is equal to the distance from the left wall of the vessel to line BB (see the data given on figure 1), therefore, when the gas expands (when the piston moves from stops AA to stops BB), the elastic force of the spring is proportional to the volume of gas - F_ypp=kx=kV/S, and therefore the gas pressure -

$$p_{1\rightarrow 2} = \frac{k}{S^2} V.$$

In this section, the volume of gas increases from V₁=2·10⁻⁴ m³ to V₂=4·10⁻⁴ m³. The gas pressure increases from p=k/S²V₁=2·10³ Pa to p₂=k/S²V₂=4·10³ Pa (see Fig. 2).

After the base of the spring was shifted from BB to GG, the elastic force of the spring decreased. As a result, when the vessel cools, the gas does not begin to skim immediately, but only after the gas pressure on the piston drops so much that the gas pressure force becomes equal to the elastic force of the spring. This will happen at p₃=10·0.3/10⁻³ Pa=3·10³ Pa (see Fig. 2). With further cooling, the gas will skim and the pressure will be lower according to the law

$$p_{3\rightarrow 4} = \frac{k}{S^2} (V-V_0),$$

where V₀ is the volume occupied by the gas in the case when the spring, fixed in the GG position, turns out to be insoluble: V₀=0.1·10⁻³ m³=1·10⁻⁴ m³. Thus, p₄=k/S²(V₁-V₀)=1·10³ Pa.

After the base of the spring moves from the GG position to the BB position, the elastic force will increase. Therefore, upon subsequent heating of the gas, it begins to expand only from the moment when the gas pressure force increases to the value of the elastic force. This will happen at p=p₁=2·10³ Pa; subsequently the process is repeated (see Fig. 2).

The work done by the gas in one cycle can be found as the area of ​​the parallelogram on figure 2:

$$A=0,2 \Pi a \cdot m^3=0,2 \text{Дж.}$$

The gas receives heat in sections 1→2 and 4→1; Considering that the gas is monatomic, we find:

$$Q=Q_1+Q_2=A_{1\rightarrow 2}+\Delta U_{1\rightarrow 2}+\Delta U_{4\rightarrow 1}=\frac{p_1+p_2}{2}(V_2-V_1)+\frac{3}{2}vR(T_2-T_1)+\frac{3}{2}vR(T_1-T_4)=\frac{p_1+p_2}{2}(V_2-V_1)+\frac{3}{2}(p_2V_2-p_4V_4)=2,7 \text{Дж.}$$

Thus, the efficiency of this movement is

$$\eta=\frac{A}{Q} \cdot 100 \% \approx 7,4 \%.$$
