⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. After the rain, I picked up porcini mushrooms and dragged them with force,” Nikolai Borisovich said in a mathematics lesson. “But I was mainly carrying water: after all, freshly picked mushrooms contain 90% water!” And when the mushrooms dried out, their weight decreased by 15 kg, because water now accounted for only 60% of their weight. How many kilograms of mushrooms did I bring from the forest?

2. figure shows two 9×12 rectangles colored differently in three colors. Cut the rectangle drawn on the left into 4 parts so that they can be folded into the rectangle drawn on the right.

3. Kolya and Vitya live in the same house. There are four apartments on each floor in all the entrances of their house. Kolya lives on the fifth floor in apartment No. 83, and Vitya lives on the third floor in apartment No. 169. How many floors are there in their house?

4. Ira and I went into the forest to pick berries. Ira walked to the right of me. Suddenly, from the left I heard her “Aw!”, a minute later - again “Aw!”, but this time, it seems, from behind. Why is it so difficult to determine the direction of the sound source in the forest?

5. Several identical rectangular sheets of paper were placed on the table. It turned out that the top sheet covers more than half the area of ​​each of the other sheets. In this case, is it possible to insert a pin so that it pierces all the sheets?

These tasks were suggested to us by A. Ya. Khalamizer, A. V. Shvetsov, a 10th grade student. School No. 462, Moscow A. Khamitudili, A. P. Savin, V. V. Proizvolov.
