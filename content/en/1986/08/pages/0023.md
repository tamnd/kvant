⟦folio 21⟧

What was spreading along the chain of soldiers? Each soldier remained standing in place, but at some point in time he changed his state (raised a flag). This change in state was propagated along the chain. In such cases, a wave is said to propagate.

Waves are different. In actic (sound) waves, the density of matter changes, in electromagnetic (light, radio waves, etc.) - by the direction of the electric and magnetic fields. There are terper waves, waves of concentration during chemical reactions, waves of epidemics, etc. Figuratively speaking, the waves cover the whole variety of phenomena in nature.

The simplest type of wave is a monochromatic wave, when in each place a change in state occurs over time according to a harmonic law with a certain frequency (according to the law of sine or cosine). Many of the waves found in nature can be considered monochromatic. These types of sound waves are called musical tones. They arise, for example, with the help of chamberts. Monochromatic light waves are produced using lasers. Waves close to monochromatic can be excited on the surface of water by periodically immersing an object into it. In our chain of soldiers you can also get similar waves.

Imagine that each soldier not only waved a flag, but oscillated it, periodically raising and lowering the flag. Each subsequent soldier repeats these oscillations, but with some delay (phase shift). A wave will run along the chain of soldiers. Something similar can be seen during sports festivals, when chains of people make periodic movements with a shift in phase.

This beautiful ripening is pleasing to the eye, but can waves also transmit information? Obviously not. Periodic fluctuations repeated over time do not bring us anything new, they do not transmit information. A

You can make it so that not one, but two, three or even several soldiers standing next to each other will make swings in a chain at the same time. Then the signal resistance will increase. By using signals of different lengths, it is possible to transmit not only information about the beginning of the coronation, but also information in general. The nature of the signal can be different - there are sound signals, light signals, etc.
