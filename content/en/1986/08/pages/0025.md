Interestingly, solid bodies can also “scream”. Thermal motion vibrates the atoms in the crystal lattice, and the vibrations are also transmitted throughout the body in the form of elastic waves. These are also sound waves. However, their spectrum has a maximum at very high frequencies, and in the region of audible frequencies the sound amplitudes are negligibly small (for example, even at a very low temperature of the order of 5 K, the maximum corresponds to the frequency $10^{12}-10^{13}$ Hz).
