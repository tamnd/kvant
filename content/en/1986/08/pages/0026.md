In the same way, any information can be encoded - recorded in the form of a sequence of signals and their omissions of a certain duration. Signals can be transmitted using amplitude modulation (Fig. 6). The faster we want to transmit information, the shorter these signals should be. But for reliable information transmission, the signal duration should not be less than the period of the carrier sinusoid. This limits the speed of information transfer. In fact, there is a part of the modulation of the order of $10^7$ Hz and, accordingly, the carrier part should lie in the region of tens to hundreds of metahertz. That is why during the current it was necessary to use high-frequency, and therefore ultra-short waves with a wavelength of the order of a meter, although they propagate only within the limits of visibility.
