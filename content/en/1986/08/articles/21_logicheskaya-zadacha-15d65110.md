Gulyaver in the land of geometric shapes overheard a conversation between Circle, Square, Star and Triangle. It is known that every geometric figure either always lies or always tells the truth. Answer Gulyaver's question.

$\textcircled{1}$
we are rays.

$\textcircled{2}$
you are rays!

$\textcircled{3}$
they are both rays.

$\textcircled{4}$
Eh?

$\textcircled{5}$
and we motza luchi.

$\textcircled{6}$
Who is going to tell the truth?
