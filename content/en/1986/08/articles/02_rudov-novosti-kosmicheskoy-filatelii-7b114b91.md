News of space philately

In March 1986, the international multi-purpose space experiment "Venus - Halley's Comet" was successfully completed. During the experiment, it was possible to obtain more than a thousand symbols of the comet and rich information about its gaseous shell. These data significantly defined our understanding of the nature of comets and comet tails. They are of great value for science.

The outstanding international scientific research program on Halley's Comet is reflected in philately. Two postage stamps and a postal block, issued in our country in 1985-1986, are dedicated to her. Similar postal signs appeared in a number of other countries. We will reproduce here a series of Vietnamese stamps, which depict the comet and the launch of the Veza automatic station, as well as portraits of Newton and Halley, and the DPRK postal block.
