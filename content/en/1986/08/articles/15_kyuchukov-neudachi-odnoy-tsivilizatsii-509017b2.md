Bad luck
one civilization

A. N. KYUCHUKOV (NRV)

There is a civilization on Section $AB$. Its representatives - Dots - were swollen creatures. The segment was large, the creatures were very small; and there was enough room for everyone.

With the development of science and technology, beings began to ponder the question: how to leave their native Segment and go into open Space - into the Flatness? First, we found the midpoint $C$ of the segment. The most prominent scientists proposed constructing equilateral right triangles $AA_0C$ and $BB_0C$ in Plane with right angles at the vertices $A$ and $B$. They found a way to land the spacecraft in the middle $M_0$ of segment $A_0B_0$ (Fig. 1). I accepted this project with enthusiasm, but it almost failed. Equal triangles were built by different organizations and they built them uncorrelatedly - in different Planes relative to the Section $AB$. Therefore, instead of taking off to point $M_0$, the spacecraft remained in the middle $C$ of segment $AB$. The error was corrected, and to identify the creature they decided to fly only to Verkhnyuko Flatness for now. The spacecraft successfully reached point $M_0$, and everyone was happy.

The next generation, however, did not want to limit itself to the point $M_0$. Young scientists replaced the midpoint $C$ of the segment with an arbitrary point $C', C' \in AB$, after which they again constructed in the Plane equilateral right triangles $AA'C'$ and $BB'C'$ with right angles at the vertices $A$ and $B$. They then sent a spacecraft into the middle of $M'$ segment $A'B'$ (at Verkhnyuko Flatness; Fig. 2). Imagine their surprise when it turned out that the ship again hit point $M_0$. Most scientists have decided that this coincidence is an inevitable property of Flatness. They began to look for other ways to enter open space using equilateral right triangles (probably, the technical means of civilization did not allow other methods). In the end, we managed to use the point $C_1$ in the Plane, which does not lie on the Segment $AB$. Outside the triangle $ABC_1$ we constructed equilateral right triangles $AA_1C_1$ and $BB_1C_1$ with right angles at the vertices $A$ and $B$ (Fig. 3). There have been many proposals, argued in different ways, on how to choose the point $C_1$ in Flatness. However, the most complete skeptic said that the disputes are pointless, because the middle $M_1$ of the segment $A_1B_1$, where the spaceship will go, in this case coincides with the old point $M_0$.

His words were taken as an inappropriate joke. Pivilization allocated large sums of money to turn the skeptic into practice. We chose two different points $C_1$ and $C_2$ in the Verkhnyuko Flatness and constructed the corresponding right triangles. Then they sent two ships to the middles $M_1$ and $M_2$ of the resulting segments $A_1B_1$ and $A_2B_2$. Only virtuoso skill will keep the ships from colliding! The skeptic stated that the ships avoided a collision only because of the inaccuracy in constructing the triangles.

What do you think, dear readers? Why did the points $M_0$, $M'$, $M_1$ and $M_2$ coincide?
