Task XLIX of the Moscow City Mathematical Olympiad

The final round of the 49th Moscow City Mathematical Olympiad is February 16 at Moscow University. 608 schoolchildren in grades 7–10 took part in it. Below are the problems of this round, proposed by S. B. Gashkov, M. Pentus, A. V. Ryabinin, N. N. Sergeev, N. G. Tsarkov.

7th grade
1. A quadrilateral is drawn on the face of professional paper. Give a way to fold this sheet (possibly several times) to determine whether the original quadrilateral is a rhombus.

2. Prove that for no numbers x, y, t three inequalities can simultaneously hold: |x| < |y-t|, |y| < |t-x|, |t| < |x-y|.

3. Three gnomes live in different houses on a plane and walk at speeds of 1, 2 and 3 km/h, respectively. What place for daily meetings should they choose so that the amount of time required for each of the gnomes to travel from their home to this place (in a straight line) is the least?

4. The product of some 1986 natural numbers has exactly 1986 different prime factors. Prove that either one of these numbers, or the product of several of them, is the square of a natural number.

5. It is known that in a code lock only the key with numbers 1, 2, 3 is correct, and the code of this lock is three-digit and does not contain other numbers. Write a sequence of digits of the shortest length that is sure to open this lock (the lock opens as soon as all three digits of its code are pressed in a row and in the correct order).

8th grade
1. See task 1 for grade 7. with the rhombus replaced by a square.

2. Find all natural numbers that cannot be represented as the difference of the squares of the natural numbers.

3. Prove that if $a_1 = 1$, $a_n = 1/2 (a_{n-1} + 2/a_{n-1})$ for $n = 2, \dots, 10$, then $0 < a_{10} - \sqrt{2} < 10^{-370}$.

4. The square field is divided into 100 identical sections, 9 of which are overgrown with weeds. It is known that during the year Buryan spreads to those and only those areas, each of which has at least two neighboring areas already affected by Buryan (the areas are adjacent if they have a common side). Prove that the entire field is not filled with Buryan.

5. Prove that the inequality system
$$\begin{cases} |x| > |y-z+t|, \\ |y| > |x-z+t|, \\ |z| > |x-y+t|, \\ |t| > |x-y+z| \end{cases}$$
has no solutions.

9th grade
1. Points A, B, C, D are marked on the face of the paper. Recognize the device can perform two types of operations with absolute precision: a) measure the distance in centimeters between two given points; b) compare two given numbers. What is the minimum number of operations this device needs to perform to determine for sure whether the quadrilateral ABCD is a rectangle?

2. An ant flies from point M along a plane at a constant speed. Its path is represented by a spiral, which is wound around point O and prepared on some part of it relative to this point. Can an ant travel its entire path in a finite time?

3. Solve the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} |x| < |y-z+t|, \\ |y| < |x-z+t|, \\ |z| < |x-y+t|, \\ |t| < |x-y+z|. \end{cases}$$

4. The product of some 48 natural numbers has exactly 10 different prime factors. Prove that the product of some quadrilateral ABC is extended to the intersection in D with its circumcircle. Prove that AD > 1/2 (AB + AC).

5. Circles with a radius of 1/14 are drawn on the coordinate plane with centers at each point where both coordinates are integers. Prove that any circle of radius 100 will intersect at least one drawn circle.

10th grade
1. See task No. 1 for grade 9. with the line ABCD replaced by a square.

2. The bissertis of angle A of triangle ABC is extended to the intersection at D with the circumcircle. Prove that AD > 1/2 (AB + AC).

3. Solve the equation $x^4 = 4(x > 0)$.

4. Prove that for no vectors a, b, c three inequalities can be satisfied simultaneously
$$\sqrt{3}|a| < |b-c|, \sqrt{3}|b| < |c-a|, \sqrt{3}|c| < |a-b|$$

5. Find the minimum over all a, β of the maximum of the function
$$y(x) = |\cos x + \alpha \cos 2x + \beta \cos 3x|.$$
