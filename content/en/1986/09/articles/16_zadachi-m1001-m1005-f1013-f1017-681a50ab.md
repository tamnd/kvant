M1001. There are 1001 stones in the pile. She randomly divides herself into two piles, counts the number of stones in them and writes down the product of these two numbers. Then with one of these heaps (which has more than one stone) the same operation is performed: it is divided into two and writes down the product of the numbers of stones in two new educational heaps. Then the same operation is repeated with one of the three resulting heaps, and so on, until all heaps contain one stone. What is the sum of 1000 recorded works?

A. S. Merkuryev

The mathematician is confident that even in the event of “extreme bad luck” (if the desired combination is encountered last), he will be able to enter the entrance no later than in 16 minutes. 42 sec (1002 sec). Is he right? How should he act to get home in time?

F1017. The world's largest Soviet telephone has a mirror with a diameter of $D=6$ m as a lens. How long will it take for, by comparing photographs taken on this telephone, to notice the mutual rotation of our Galaxy and the Andromeda nebula around a common center of mass?

The masses of the Galaxy and Andromeda are equal, respectively, $M_\Gamma=2,5 \times 10^{11}$ $M_0$, $M_A=3,6 \cdot 10^{11}$ $M_0$, where $M_0$ is the mass of the Sun. Photography is carried out in visible light at a wavelength of $\lambda=5 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m.
