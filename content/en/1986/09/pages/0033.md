⟦folio 31⟧

Quests

1. It was already evening, the customers had gone home, and Natasha, a young vegetable store clerk, still had half an hour left before the end of work. On the counter lay three ends of cabbage, two watermelons, a melon and a beet. To pass the time, Natasha began to weigh these vegetables and was surprised to discover that all three ends of cabbage weighed the same, and both watermelons had equal weight. More surprising was the balance in three more cases depicted in the figures. Now answer: how many times is a melon heavier than a beet?

2. Restore the numbers replaced by asterisks (see figure).

3. The predecessor of the electric light bulb, the kerosene lamp, smoked at times, which is why there is a black spot on the ceiling above it. But even above lamps with electric bulbs, a dark spot sometimes appears on the ceiling. Do electric lamps also smoke?

4. What is the smallest number of coins that can be used to pay any amount from 1 kopeck to 1 ruble without change?

5. The moment we turn off the gas under a boiling kettle, clouds of steam burst out from under its lid. Why?
