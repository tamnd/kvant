5. There is a sheet of checkered paper with size: $a) 30 \times 45, 6) 30 \times 30$ cells. The two play the next game. 6. The set $A$ consists of real numbers. It is known that the sum of any two of its elements is also its element, and any segment $[a, b]$, $a < b$ contains a segment entirely consisting of elements of the set $A$. Prove that $A$ contains all real numbers.

7. Consider the following algorithm:
Step 0. Put $n = m$.
Step 1. If $n$ is even, halve $n$. If $n$ is odd, increase $n$ by 1.
Step 2. If $n > 1$, go to step 1. If $n = 1$, finish executing the algorithm.
How many natural numbers $m$ are there for which this algorithm will execute step 1 exactly 15 times?

8. The king went around the board $9 \times 9$, raising it exactly once on each of its squares. (The king's route is not closed and possibly self-intersecting.) What is the maximum possible length of such a route if the length of the diagonal move is $\sqrt{2}$, and the length of the vertical and horizontal moves is 1?

9. The diameter of a set on a plane is the maximum distance between two of its points. (If such a thing exists.) Prove that there is a line that does not intersect any of these polygons and on each side of which lies at least one of them.
