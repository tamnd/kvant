Knowledge Day is a national holiday

This year, the national holiday - Knowledge Day - is celebrated for the third time.

V.I. Lenin at the dawn of Soviet power said that you can become a communist only by enriching your memory with the knowledge of all the riches that humanity has developed. In his address to young people at the III Komsomol Congress, he called: “Study, study and study again.” These Leninist behests found issues in the policies of our party and the Soviet state, which created opportunities unprecedented in their historical counterparts for the flourishing of education and culture in our country.

Why is so much attention paid to the acquisition of knowledge at this stage of the development of our society? The implementation of the course outlined by the 27th Party Congress to strengthen the socio-economic development of the country is associated with scientific and technological progress, with the transformation of science into the direct productive force of society. Special studies convincingly prove the high economic efficiency of education. There is evidence that 30 to 40 percent of the increase in national income in developed countries is achieved through education. The growth of education is powerful.

The CPSU and Soviet rule pay constant attention to improving education. This is confirmed by the new school reform. The period of study continues in the coming years from 10 to 11 years; Primary school becomes a four-year summer school. Measures are being taken to improve labor training and education. In the 12th five-year plan, twice as many schools will be built as in the 11th five-year plan. There will also be a return of 800 new professional sets. It is planned to open hundreds of new equestrian technical stations, young natural sciences, and sports schools. The network of students' scientific societies, clubs, and children's clubs is expanding.

The very content of school knowledge will also change. Acquaintance with new technologies, computers, and robotics will be better ensured. Students will receive deeper political knowledge and become familiar with new social processes in the country and the world as a whole. A new political magazine for high school students will begin publishing. The cycle of elective classes in the humanities, physico-mathematics, technical and biological fields is expanding in order to develop the talents and abilities of students.

On Knowledge Day, the whole country cordially greets teachers and students and wishes them new and greater success.

Vice-President of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences

Yu. K. Babansky
