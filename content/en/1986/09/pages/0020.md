Cyrano de Bergerac

The recoil effect, as is known, underlies the application of the package for space travel. This is where we come to the French package of Cyrano de Bergerac (1619-1655). This is a very unique figure in the history of world literature. Cyrano came from a seedy, dual family and was in great need all his life. In that era, only those who entered the service of the retinue of one of the rich aristocrats passed through. For Cyrano, such a path was closed due to his extremely independent and fiery character. Cyrano wrote poetic leaflets directed against the all-powerful First Minister of France, Cardinal Mazarin (“Mazarins”). He tried to get rich through gambling, but without success. Nature rewarded Cyrano with a huge caricatured nose, which caused constant ridicule, which served as a reason for an endless number of duals. And this gambler and duzlyat was a deep thinker, a follower of the materialist philosopher Gassendi *) and the creator of the wonderful science fiction novel “Another World, or States and Imperials,” published in 1657, after the death of the author. The novel is clearly atheistic in nature, and in it Cyrano wittily sings biblical legends.

What is interesting for us is that in the novel Cyrano describes his flight to the moon... with the help of a package! There is an old engraving depicting Cyrano flying towards the Moon in a basket, to which are attached packages that emit jets of fire towards the Earth, thereby raising the basket. Thus, Cyrano foresaw the use of the package for space travel three hundred years ago. It is also curious that in a novel written more than thirty years before the publication of Newton’s Elements, Cyrano claims that the attraction of the Moon will overpower the attraction of the Earth at a distance less from the Moon than from the Earth, since the mass of the Moon is less than the mass of the Earth, and even calculates the ratio of these distances (he gets, of course, the wrong numerical result - 3 together is about 9).
