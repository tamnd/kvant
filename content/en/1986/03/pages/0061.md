4. A glass tube, sealed at one end, is positioned horizontally. The air in the tube is separated from the atmosphere by a column of mercury $l=10$ cm long. The tube is moved along its axis uniformly with acceleration $a=10$ m/s$^2$, first with the sealed end forward, then open with the end forward. Determine the atmospheric pressure if in the first case the length of the air column in the tube is $n=1,3$ times greater than in the second. The density of mercury is $\varrho=13,6 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

5. A ball of mass $m$, suspended on a length of thread, oscillates. How many times will the oscillation period change if the ball is given a positive charge $q$ and placed in a uniform electric field with direction $\vec{E}$, the drain lines of which are directed vertically downward?

6. The arc lamp burns at a voltage of $U=80$V and consumes $P=800$W of power. How many degrees will the supply wires heat up $t=1$ minutes after turning on the lamp, if the wiring is made with copper wire with a cross-section of $S=4$ mm$^2$? Half of the released amount of heat is transferred to the surrounding environment, the resistivity of copper is $\varrho=1,7 \times 10^{-8}$ Ohm m, the density is $D=8,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, the specific heat is $c=395$ J/(kg K).

7. A battery with emf $\delta=12$ V, a rheostat and a coil with inductance $L=1$ H are connected in series to the electrical circuit. With a rheostat resistance of $R=10$ Ohm, there is a direct current in the proto-slime circuit. Then the resistance of the rheostat is adjusted so that the current in the circuit decreases at a constant rate of $\Delta I/\Delta t=0,2$ A/c. What will be the resistance of the circuit $t=2$ s after the current is applied? The internal resistance of the battery and the active resistance of the coil are a problem.

8. The proton is in a uniform magnetic field with inductance $B=0,4$ T perpendicular to the vector of magnetic induction $\vec{B}$. Determine to what depth in the field region a proton can penetrate if its energy is $W=1,6 \times 10^{-13}$ J. The mass of the proton is $m=1,67 \cdot 10^{-27}$ kg, its charge is $q=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19}$ Cl.

9. On the main optical axis of the collecting line there are two luminous points: at distances of $d_1=20$ cm and $d_2=40$ cm from the line. Knowing that their images are at the same point, determine the focal length of the line.

10. The camera lens is made of two lines - diverging and converging. A scattering line with a focal length of $F_1=5$ cm is located at a distance of $l=45$ cm from the photographic film. Where should a collecting line with a focal length of $F_2=8$ cm be placed to produce sharp images of distant objects on film?
