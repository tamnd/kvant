⟦folio none⟧

These four photographs show models of a closed spherical dome (next above) and three dome-shaped structures obtained by joining parts of the domes.
The spherical dome is realized in the form of a network of thin flat fonts, rigidly fastened at the intersection points. Each dome is parallel to three rings; each such family of four compartments corresponds to four sections of the sphere of equal history and parallel planes. Count the number of families and tell how the non-parallel planes of different sections are located relative to each other.
Think about what other dome shapes can be made from pieces of a spherical dome.
O. Y. Bodnar
