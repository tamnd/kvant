1. Let us write down the equilibrium conditions of the rod:

$$Mg\left(\frac{l}{2} - \Delta l\right) = 2T\left(\frac{l}{2} + \Delta l\right),$$

where $T$ is the tension force of the thread on which weights of mass $m_1$ and $m_2$ are suspended. This force can be found using Newton's second law:

$$m_1a = m_1g - T,$$

$$-m_2a = m_2g - T,$$

where

$$T = \frac{2m_1m_2g}{m_1 + m_2}.$$

Substituting this result into the equilibrium conditions of the rod and using the equation for the masses $m_1$ and $m_2$:

$$m_1^2 - m_2^2 + M + \frac{M^2}{4}\left(\frac{l/2 - \Delta l}{l/2 + \Delta l}\right) = 0.$$

Solving this equation gives

$$m_1 = \frac{M}{2}\left(1 + \sqrt{\frac{4\Delta l}{l + 2\Delta l}}\right) = 1,67 \text{ кг},$$

$$m_2 = \frac{M}{2}\left(1 - \sqrt{\frac{4\Delta l}{l + 2\Delta l}}\right) = 0,33 \text{ кг}.$$
