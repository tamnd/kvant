Physics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Amount of heat. Units of measurement. Specific heat. Formula for calculating the amount of heat required to heat a body. First law of thermodynamics.

2. Two bodies move along the coordinate axis OX. Based on the graphs of the speed of a body versus time $v(t)$ (Fig. 1), write down the speed formula for each body. Draw graphs of the dependence of the coordinates of the body on time $x(t)$, if the initial coordinate of the bodies is $x_0=0$.

3. At a temperature of $t_1=27$ °C, the gas pressure in a closed vessel was equal to $p_1=3 \cdot 10^5$ Pa. What will be the temperature of the gas at a pressure of $p_2=2,5 \cdot 10^5$ Pa?

4. What is the maximum speed that electrons can be emitted from potassium when irradiated with light with a wavelength of $\lambda=0,4$ µm? The electron work function for potassium is $A=3,2 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J, the electron mass is $m=9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}$ kg, Planck’s constant is $h=6,63 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s.

5. A frequency with a mass of $m=10^{-4}$ kg and a charge of $q=10^{-8}$ Cl emits in a region of a uniform electric field with a width of $b=0,1$ m at an angle of $\alpha=45^\circ$, and emerges at an angle of $\beta=60^\circ$ (Fig. 2). Determine the initial speed of the particle if the direction of the uniform field is $E=10^6$ V/m. The particle trajectory lies in the plane of the drawing. Disdain the force of gravity.

Option 2

1. Two types of electricity. Interaction of electric charges. Coulomb's law. Dielectric constant of a substance. Unit of measurement of charge.

2. A narrow, long glass tube, sealed at one end, was partially filled with water and turned upside down. What is in the resulting volume?

3. The candle is located at a distance of $d=10$ cm from a thin scattering line with a focal length of $F=5$ cm. At what distance from the line is the image and what is it like?

4. The voltage on the capacitor in an ideal quantity changes according to the law $U=50 \cos(1 \cdot 10^5 t)$ (V) and at the same time the maximum value of the capacitor charge is $q_m=5 \cdot 10^{-6}$ C. Determine the inductance of the counter coil.

5. A voltmeter was connected first in series, then in parallel with the resistor, into a circuit consisting of a battery and a resistor with a resistance of $R=20$ Ohm. Voltmeter readings indicate battery resistance.

Oral exam assignment

1. There is a cylinder on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination to the horizon $\alpha=30^\circ$. The cylinder is held at rest by its filament, one end of which is fixed to the top of the inclined plane, and the other is pulled vertically upward. With what force is the thread applied if the mass of the cylinder is $m=30$ g?

2. Mercury was poured into communicating vessels. Then another amount of oil $h_1=30$ cm high was poured into one of the vessels, and water $h_2=20$ cm high was poured into the other. Determine the difference in mercury levels in the vessels. The density of mercury is $\varphi=13,6 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, oil $\varphi_1=0,9 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$, water $\varphi_2=1,0 \cdot 10^3$ kg/m$^3$.

3. A glass ball with a volume of $V=0,5$ cm$^3$ falls uniformly in water. How much heat is released when the ball moves?
