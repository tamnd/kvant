2. For what is the smallest integer value $k$ the equation
Does $$(k-1)x^2-2(k+1)x+k-3=0$$ have two different roots?

3. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3x^2+1}+\sqrt{x^2+3}=\sqrt{6x^2+10}.$$

Write down the smaller root of the equation in your answer.

4. The company notified that the products were being sold daily at the same percentage. Find this number if it is known that over two years the volume of output increased by 44%.

5. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x+3}{4-x} \leq 0.04.$$

In your answer, write down the most complete solution to the inequality.

6. Solve the equation
$$\left(x^{1g}x\right)^2-100x^3=0.$$

Write down the larger root of the equation in your answer.

7. A tangent to the graph of the function $y=x^2-4x-3$ is drawn at the point of intersection of this graph with the ordinat axis. Find the area of ​​the triangle formed by the tangent and coordinate axes.

8. Find the difference between the largest and smallest values ​​of the function $f(x)=3x^4+2x^3-18x^2+15$ on the segment [-3; 1].

9. Simplify the expression and evaluate at $a=\frac{7\pi}{6}$
$$\sqrt{3} \frac{\mathrm{tg}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+a\right)-\mathrm{tg}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-a\right)}{\mathrm{tg}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+a\right)+\mathrm{tg}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-a\right)}.$$

10. Solve the equation
$$\mathrm{tg}(x+1) \cdot \mathrm{ctg}(2x+1)=1.$$

In your answer, write down the number of roots of the equation that belong to the segment [0; 15].

11. The lengths of the bases of the trapezoid are 10 and 24, the lengths of the sides are 13 and 15. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.

12. The volume of the cone is 81. The height of the cone is divided into three equal parts and planes are drawn through the division points parallel to the axis. Find the volume of the part of the cone located between the drawn planes.
