Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. The difference of the digits of the two-digit natural number $A$ modulo does not exceed 3, and the sum of the digits of the number $A$ is equal to 9. Find $A$.
2. Solve the equation $\log_{1/3}(9^x+a)+\log_3(2\cdot 3^x)=0$, $a\in R$.
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin \left( b \sqrt{x-1} + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)=0, 5, b\in R.$$
4. The base of the triangular paramid $SABC$ is the regular triangle $ABC$. The heights $SD$ and $AF$ of the lateral face $ASC$ have lengths $k$ and $h$, respectively, and all the plane angles at the vertex $S$ of the paramid are of the same magnitude. Find the area of ​​the section of the paramid by the plane passing through the midpoints of the edges $AS$, $AB$ and $BC$, if it is known that the points $A$ and $C$ are the same distance from the BSD plane.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{a}{a-3\sin^2 2x}=3.$$
2. There are two vessels with silk solution of different concentrations (by volume). The first vessel contains 4 liters of solution, the second - 6 liters of solution. If you merge them together, you get a solution containing 35% silk. If you pour together 3 liters of solution from each vessel, you will get a solution containing $a$% silk. How many liters of silk does the second vessel contain?
3. A plane is drawn through the vertex $S$ of a right circular cone, intersecting the circle of the base of the cone at points $A$ and $B$. The medians $AC$ and $SD$ of triangle $ASB$ have lengths $m_1$ and $m_2$ respectively. Determine the magnitude of the vertex angle $S$ in the axial section of the cone, if it is known that the area of ​​the triangle $ASB$ has the largest possible value.
4. The function $f(x) = \frac{1}{x^2-4x+a}$ is defined on the interval [5; 7]. Find $f'(x)$ and all values ​​of $a\in R$ for which the greatest value of the function $f(x)$ on the interval [5; 7] does not exceed 0.1.

Oral exam objectives
1. The system consists of a weightless rod with a length of $l=35$ cm, placed on a stationary prism, as well as a weightless block with two weights of masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ and a weight of $M=2$ kg ($m_1+m_2=M$), attached to the ends of the rod (Fig. 1). When the loads move relative to the block, the equilibrium of the rod takes place if the fulcrum of the rod is shifted by a distance of $\Delta l=5$ cm to the left from the middle of the rod. Determine the masses $m_1$ and $m_2$. Let's make a claim.
2. A flat washer with a mass of $m=0,2$ kg begins to slide with an initial speed of $v_0=12$ m/s up an inclined plane making an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the horizon. The coefficient is three between the washer and the plane $\mu=0,3$. What work is done on the puck by the drained trenya during the time $t=3,5$ s after the start of movement?
3. In the installation shown at figure 2, the mass of each load ($A$ and $B$) is $m=2$ kg, the angle is $\alpha=30^\circ$, the masses of the block and thread are negligible, there is no friction. Find the power that the tension force of the thread acting on the load $A$ will develop after a time $t=2$ s after the start of movement.
4. One mole of an ideal gas was transferred from state 1 to state 2 isochoric, so that its pressure decreased by a factor of $n=1,5$, and then isobarically heated to the original temperature $T_1$. In this case, the gas performed $A=0,83$ kJ of work. Find $T_1$.
5. A ball of mass $m=2$ g, having a charge $q=2,5\cdot 10^{-9}$ C, is suspended on a thread and moves in a circle of radius $R=3$ cm with an angular velocity $\omega_1=2\cdot 1^{-1}$ (Fig. 3). A ball with the same charge was placed in the center of the circle. What should the angular velocity $\omega_2$ of the expression of the ball become so that the radius of the circle does not change?
6. A ball with mass $m=1$ kg and charge $q=2\cdot 10^{-4}$ K is suspended on an insulating thread in a single-mode electric field with direction $E=3\cdot 10^4$ V/m, and vector $\vec{E}$ is perpendicular to the junction and directed blevo. The ball was moved to the right so that the thread deviated at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ from the vertical, and released. Find the tension in the thread as it passes vertically.
