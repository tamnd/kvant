The literary and historical population of Arago is of great interest. These include textbooks and various popular books. And his work “Biographies of famous actors, physicists and geometers” is still one of the fundamental studies in the history of science.

It must be said that science was not Arago’s only hobby. For the last 20 years of his life he was actively involved in politics. Elected in 1831 to the Chamber of Delates, in 1848 he entered the provisional government, becoming a military and later a naval minister. Contemporaries noted that in politics, as in science, he was characterized by particularity, impartiality, creativity of character, complicity and resourcefulness in difficult situations.

The motto of Arago’s life was the words spoken by d’Alembert to a young man who encountered difficulties in studying mathematics: “Go, go forward, and you will gain confidence.” Fidelity to this motto allowed Dominique François Arago to become one of the leaders in the development and improvement of science, industry and education in France.
