Physics

Objectives of oral and written exams

Mechanics

1 (15). The train travels at a speed of $v_1=108$ km/h. A passenger of this train, sitting at the window, sees for $t=18$ s an oncoming train, the length of which is $l=900$ m. Determine the speed of the oncoming train.

2 (17). A metro escalator lifts a passenger standing motionless on it within $t_1=1$ minutes. A passenger climbs up a stationary escalator in $t_2=3$ min. How long will it take a passenger to climb the moving escalator?

3 (17). The boy sledded down a mountain $l_1=40$ m long in $t_1=10$ s, and then drove along a horizontal section another $l_2=20$ m to a stop (Fig. 1). Find the speed at the end of the mountain, the acceleration at each section, the total travel time, and the average speed along the entire path.

4 (19). The body fell freely from a certain height $H$ and the last $h=196$ m passed in $t=4$ s. How long did it take for the body to fall? What is the height of $H$?

5 (8). A body falls from a certain height for two seconds. Find the speed with which it must be thrown vertically upward so that it rises to a height twice its original height.

6(1). A body is thrown vertically upward with a speed of $v_0=30$ m/s. Some points $A$ of the body pass twice with a time difference of $\Delta t=2$ s. Draw a graph of the speed of movement of the body and from this graph determine the height at which point $A$ is located.

7 (8). Determine at what distance from the targets it is necessary to drop the target from an aircraft flying at an altitude of $h=123$ m at a speed of $v=360$ km/h. Neglect air resistance.

8 (17). The minute hand of a clock is 3 times longer than the second hand. Find the ratio of the linear velocities of the ends of the arrows.

9 (18). A body of mass $m=10$ kg is on an inclined plane making an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Friction coefficient $\mu=0,6$. What force directed along the plane must be applied to the body in order to move it down the plane with an acceleration of $a=2$ cm/s²? Present the result in SI units and round to the nearest ten.

10 (15). A car with a mass of $m=5$ t moves at a constant speed of $v=36$ km/h along a convex place with a radius of $R=100$ m. Find the automatic pressure force on the middle of the place.

11 (8). A ball suspended on threads of length $l$ rotates in a horizontal plane so that the thread makes an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical. Determine the speed of rotation of the ball.

12 (4). At the right end of a rod with a length of $l=30$ cm is attached a ball with a radius of $r=6$ cm. Where is the center of gravity of this system if the mass of the rod is half that of the ball?

13(1). The ladder is leaned against a smooth vertical wall at an angle $\alpha=30^\circ$ (Fig. 2). Can a person climb a ladder to the middle of the ladder before it begins to slide if the coefficient of friction between the ladder and the horizontal floor is $\mu=0,3$? The mass of the stairs and friction against the wall can be neglected.

14(2). A doka of mass $M$ lies on a smooth horizontal surface. A puck with mass $M/2$ begins to slide along it at a speed of $v_0$. Due to the friction between the puck and the dock, after a while the puck will stop sliding along the board. What will be the speed of the puck?

15 (14). A body moves uniformly in a circle of radius $R=0,5$ m, possessing kinetic energy $E_k=10$ J. What is the force acting on the body, and what is the work done by this force?

16 (13). A sled with mass $m$ slides down a mountain with height $h$ and, after some distance, stops. Determine the work that needs to be done to rotate the sled back up the mountain.

17 (5). A ball of mass $m=10$ g falls from a height of $h=2$ m and is elastically reflected from a screen installed on a stationary cart, the plane of which is inclined to the horizon at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$. Find the speed of the cart after the ball is reflected. Neglect the rolling friction of the cart. Weight of the trolley with shield $M=90$ g.
