⟦folio none⟧

Tasks

1. Determine the angle that the clock hands make at 9 hours 20 minutes.

2. Decipher the numerical result:

$$\frac{+ \text{КТО}}{\text{КОТ}}$$

3. In those not so long ago times, when a person’s indispensable accessory was a colorful samovar with a teapot sitting on it, covered with an embroidered linen napkin, teapots were made with a hole in the lid (such teapots are still made today). Why is such a hole needed?

4. Find the smallest number whose sum of digits is divisible by 17, and the sum of the digits of the next number is also divisible by 17.

5. Alik, Borya, Vitya and Gena went mushroom hunting. Alik and Borya together collected as many mushrooms as Vitya and Gena did together, but Alik and Gena had fewer mushrooms than Bori and Vitya. Gena found more mushrooms than Vitya. Arrange the boys' names in descending order of the number of mushrooms each of them found.

These tasks were suggested to us by A.P. Savin, seventh-grader of the Kashkadara school of the Masalli region of the AzSSR Nazim Sadygov, A.G. Samosvat, G.A. Galperin, N.H. Rozov.
