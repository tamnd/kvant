Reminders
Lev Grigorievich Aslamazov

On April 23, 1986, the famous scientist, professor, doctor of physical and mathematical sciences, deputy editor-in-chief of the magazine «Kvant» Lev Grigorievich Aslamazov tragically died in the disintegration of creative forces. An amazingly active, hardworking, multi-talented person has left us, who managed to accomplish many good and necessary deeds in his short life.

L. G. Aslamazov was born in 1944 in the city of Batuti. In 1960 he entered the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, and after graduating in 1966, he entered graduate school at the Institute of Theoretical Physics. L. D. Landdu of the USSR Academy of Sciences, where he later worked until 1975. Lev Grigorievich's main scientific interests were concentrated in the field of the theory of superconductivity, to the development of which he made a significant contribution. Fluctuation superconductivity, discovered by L. G. Aslamazov and A. I. Larkin, created a new direction of research in the physics of superconductors. It has already been included in textbooks and monographs on the theory of superconductivity. Simultaneously with his scientific activities, Lev Grigorievich VEL carried out a great deal of scientific and organizational work, being the scientific secretary of the Institute of Theoretical Physics.

Since 1976, L. G. Aslamazov worked as an associate professor and then as a professor at the Department of Theoretical Physics at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys. Brilliant lectures, pedagogical, scientific and organizational talent earned him the well-deserved respect of colleagues and students.

He successfully combined extensive pedagogical and social work with scientific activity, largely determining the direction of scientific research at the department and in the problematic laboratorium of the institute.

Lev Grigorievich devoted a lot of time and effort to popularizing scientific knowledge and attracting talented young people to science. Since his student years, as a member of the Organizing Committee, he actively participated in the organization and conduct of All-Union School Experimental Tests, in the preparation of USSR teams for the International Experimental Tests in Physics.

L. G. Aslamazov was one of the initiators of the creation of the magazine «Kvant», a regular author, a member of the editorial collection, and subsequently deputy editor-in-chief of the magazine. His tireless, principled, interested work in searching for topics and authors of articles, lively discussion of all editorial problems that generate powerful creative impulses, a pillar necessary in the publication of the magazine.

Lev Grigorievich made a huge contribution to the organization, formation and scientific editing of the “Viblotechka«Kvant»”, in which ten editions of popular science books were published in a short period of time.

He treated any task that L. G. Aslamazov undertook, be it scientific work, editing a book or preparing a lecture, with deep responsibility, carrying it out with constant care, always bringing it to the end. He was a responsive, compassionate and at the same time very demanding person. He enjoyed the respect of his fellow workers, students, and all those close to him. The bright memory of Lev Grigorievich Aslamazov will surely remain in the hearts of those who had the good fortune to work and communicate with him.
