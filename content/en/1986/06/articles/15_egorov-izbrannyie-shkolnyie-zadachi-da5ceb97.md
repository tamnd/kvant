⟦folio 32⟧

Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} x+y+z=3, \\ 2xy-2y-z^2=4. \end{cases}$$

2. a) It is known that the median and altitude drawn from one vertex of a triangle divide the angle at this vertex into three equal parts. Prove that this triangle is a right triangle and find its acute angles.

3. Which of the two numbers is greater:
$$\frac{1985^{1986}+1}{1985^{1987}+1}$$ or $$\frac{1985^{1984}+1}{1985^{1985}+1}$$?

4. In the convex quadrilateral ABCD, find a point O such that the areas of the quadrilaterals resulting from connecting the point O with the midpoints of the sides of the quadrilateral ABCD are equal.

5. Prove that from any 100 natural numbers you can choose several numbers so that their sum is divisible by 100.

Ninth grade
6. Solve the equation system
$$\begin{cases} xy+x+y=1, \\ yz+y+z=5, \\ xz+x+z=2. \end{cases}$$
