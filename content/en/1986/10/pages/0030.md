Tasks

M1006—M1010; F1018—F1022

M1006. Two straight lines are drawn through the two vertices of the triangle, dividing it into three triangles and a quadrilateral.
a) Can the areas of all four parts be equal?
b) Which three of these parts can have equal areas? How many times does the area of ​​a quadrilateral differ from them?

G. A. Galperin, A. P. Savin

M1007. Prove that triangles with side lengths $a, b, c$ and $a_1, b_1, c_1$ are similar if and only if $\sqrt{a a_1} + \sqrt{b b_1} + \sqrt{c c_1} = \sqrt{(a_1 + b_1 + c_1)(a + b + c)}$.

V. P. Chuchin

M1008. The staircase consists of $2n+1$ steps. On the $n$ lower steps there is one stone each. Two people take turns lifting stones. The first one can move any stone up to the first free step, and the second one can move the stone down one step if it is free. The goal of the first is to place the stone on the top step. Can a second one fit him?

S. L. Eliseev

M1009. The bisector of angle $A$ of parallelogram $ABCD$ intersects lines $BC$ and $CD$ at points $K$ and $L$ respectively. Prove that the center of the circle drawn through the points $C, K$ and $L$ lies on the circle drawn through the points $B, C$ and $D$.

I. F. Sharyzin
