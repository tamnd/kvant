In the circuit shown at figure 3, the ampere showed $I_1=0,1$ A at the generator frequency $v_1=1000$ Hz; the current through the ampere was $\Delta \varphi_1=\pi/6$ out of phase with the input voltage. When the generator frequency was reduced by a factor of 100, the current increased by less than 2 times. The generator frequency was returned to its previous value and a voltmeter (Fig. 4) was connected to the circuit together with the amperator. The voltmeter showed $U_1=20$ V, and the phase shift between the voltage on the voltmeter ($u_B$) and the input voltage ($u_{BX}$) was again $\Delta \varphi_1=\pi/6$.

Using this data, find the parameters of the black box elements. How many times must the generator frequency be changed so that in the circuit with a voltmeter (see Fig. 4) the phase shift between $u_B$ and $u_{BX}$ is $\Delta \varphi_2=\pi/2$?

Measuring instruments are considered ideal. The internal resistance of the generator is negligible.

A. P. Zilberman
