KALEIDOSCOPE

Wonderful points and line

In a geometry course, you are introduced to some of the remarkable points in a triangle: the intersection points of altitudes, medians, bisectors, and perpendicular bisectors to the sides. These points are remarkable not only because the three straight lines under consideration intersect at them. We will talk about other properties of these points, as well as other wonderful lines and points in our “Kaleidoscope”.

Evangelesta Torricelli is famous not only as a physicist, but also as a mathematician. In addition to the “Torricellian void,” one of the points in the triangle is named after him. This is how she dances. First, equilateral triangles are built on the sides of a given triangle, then a circle is described around each of them

Everyone knows that the altitudes of a triangle intersect at one point - the orthocenter of the triangle. But few people know that if the circle described around a triangle is mirrored relative to its sides, then the resulting three circles will intersect at the orthocenter of this triangle.

(it's called the Torricelli circle). It turns out that Torricelli's circles intersect at one point. This point was called Torricelli's point.
