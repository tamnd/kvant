I All-Union Olympid in Physics for Students of Secondary Vocational Technical Schools

Another “coordinate” appeared on the Olympic “map” - the Olympiad in Physics for SPTU students. In terms of the form of this new Olympiad, it was no different from the traditional school one. However, when selecting tasks, it was taken into account that students of vocational schools are more closely connected with practice than schoolchildren, and therefore they should be more interested in specific, one might say, “applied” tasks.

May 20. Opening of the Olympics
Participants and organizers of the Olympiad gathered in the picturesque Komsomol town of “Boris Dzenladze” not far from Tbilisi.

The Chairman of the State Committee for Vocational Plants of the Georgian SSR N.V. Gurgenidze declared the Olympiad open. All the speakers (among them prominent scientists, teachers and the Olympiad participants themselves) came to a common opinion: the main thing is learning, and all those present have already added to a great victory, having won the right to present their resources at the Non-Union Olympiad.

In the evening, a quiz was held, during which, in a playful way, Olympids had to answer quite complex questions on physics, demonstrate their logical thinking and ingenuity. The guys' answers showed that the level of preparation of those gathered in Tbilisi was quite high, and the quiz itself served as a good warm-up before the main tests.

What will be the distance $x$ at the temperature of the liquid gel? The length of the vessel is $L=80$ cm. We rub the piston against the walls of the pretenbrets cylinder.

3. $m=1$ kg of naphthalene was placed in a vessel where the temperature (thermoate) should be maintained, with $k$ part of it melted. $M=2$ kg of water is poured into the thermoat. How much can the initial temperature of water differ from the melting temperature of phthalene so that the temperature in the thermoat does not change? Melting temperature for phthalene $t_{пп}=80$ °C, specific temperature of water $c=4,2$ kJ/(kg K), specific temperature for phthalene $\lambda=151$ kJ/kg, count $k=1/2$.

4. What resistance $R_0$ should the voltmeter have in order to change the voltage drop across a resistor with a resistance of $R=1$ kOhm (Fig. 2) with an accuracy of 1%? In the circuit between points $A$ and $B$, the source provides constant current.

5. Calculate the resistance between points $A$ and $B$ of the wire figure shown in figure 3, if the resistance of one link is $R$ (a link is the segment between two points).

6. A hydrophone installed near a large reservoir recorded several consecutive signals caused by an explosion at the bottom. The time interval between the first and second signals turned out to be equal to $T_1=1$ s, between the first and third signals - $T_2=3$ s. At what distance from the hydrophone will the explosion occur? The speed of sound propagation in water is $v=1500$ m/s, flat to the reservoir.
