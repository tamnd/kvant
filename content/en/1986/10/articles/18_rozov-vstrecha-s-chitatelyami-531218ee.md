Meeting with the reader

This summer, as in past years, at Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov conducted advanced training courses for secondary school teachers. The program of the mathematical department of pre-matric courses, in particular, the traditional meeting with members of the editorial board of the Kurnal «Kvant». This meeting took place on June 30 at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University. The editor-in-chief of the Kurnal, Yu. P. Solovyov, member of the editorial board, V. V. Vavilov, N. V. Vasilyev, N. Kh. Rozov, A. P. Savin, A. V. Sosinsky, came to visit the participants of the courses - a mathematics teacher.

Opening the meeting, Yu. P. Solovyov dwelt in detail on the tasks facing the journal today, and on the immediate and long-term plans for the editorial work. Speech at the meeting by a member of the editorial board of the kurnal, support your reports on possible formats for the implementation of the magazine in the practical activities of a school teacher. Much attention was paid to the role of the magazine in increasing the level of pre-mathematics at school, deepening the mathematical knowledge of students, and in solving currently pressing problems of their professional guidance. The listeners showed particular interest in the magazine’s materials, which are considered to help students and teachers in studying the new school subject “Fundamentals of Informatics and Computer Science.” Members of the journal's editorial board also answered numerous questions from meeting participants.
