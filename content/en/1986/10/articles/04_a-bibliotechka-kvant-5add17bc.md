⟦folio 11⟧

Library «Kvant»
This year, the Library «Kvant» - a series of popular science books for schoolchildren - is publishing its fiftieth issue - the collection “Interesting about physics and mathematics” edited by Professor L. G. Aslamazov. This collection includes the best articles and assignments published in azine years in the magazine «Kvant» under the heading «Kvant» for younger schoolchildren.”
Library «Kvant» has been published by the Main Editorial Office of Physics and Mathematics Literature of the Nauka Publishing House since 1980. The books in the series tell us how much interesting physics and mathematics there are, introduce the reader to the problems of modern science, promote the development of creative thinking and attract schoolchildren to independent server-based studies. Among the authors of the books are famous scientists actively working in various fields of physics, mathematics and related sciences.
Let us note that Biblioteka’s books are very popular not only among schoolchildren - they are read with interest by students, teachers, and everyone who is interested in physics and mathematics. We hope that having become acquainted with the Library «Kvant» at school, you will return to it in your future studies. By the way, non-tourist graduations are used by universities as aids in the study of special sections of physics and mathematics. As an example, let us cite the books by A. L. Efros “Physics and Geometry of Security”, M. N. Arshinov and L. E. Sadovsky “Codes and Mathematics”, M. E. Levinshtein and G. S. Simin “Introduction to Semiconductors”.
In 1987, the Nauka publishing house plans to publish nine books in the Library «Kvant».
Why do rivers meander and how do they erode the banks? What makes a violin string sound? How does superconductivity help decipher the magnetic field of the brain and heart? How does sound travel in the ocean? You can learn about this and many other interesting physical phenomena by reading the book “Amazing Physics” by L. G. Aslamazov and A. A. Varlamov.
The book by M. V. Balk and V. G. Boltyansky “Geometria of Masses”, using the example of three hundred problems, talks about the original method of proving geometric theorems. It is based on analogies between some geometric objects and concepts of physical mechanics.
The collection “Moscow Physical Optimization Problems” (edited by S. S. Krotov, candidate of physical and mathematical sciences) will include the most interesting and original problems (and their solutions) of Moscow urban optimization over the past fifteen years. The collection will be of interest to everyone who likes problems with a twist and is useful in preparing for competitive university exams.
The book by M. E. Levinshtein and G. S. Simin “Varys (from crystal to integrated circuit)” will popularly tell about semiconductor devices, their design and manufacturing technology, about modern semiconductor electrical power supply. This issue will be a continuation of the book by the same authors, “Introducing Semiconductors” (issue 33).
The book “Excursion into Wave Optics” by G. R. Lokshin, S. M. Kozel and V. E. Belonuchik is devoted to the problems of modern wave optics. The book will talk about coherence and the unique properties of laser radiation, diffraction, the limits set by the wave nature of light, and the possibilities of wave optics.
The concept of time is one of the most fundamental in physics. In a simple and visual form, A. D. Chernin’s book “Physics of Time” will tell about the development of our ideas about time. It sets out important issues related to the nature of time as a physical concept, the continuity and future of the Universe.
Many of the Bibliotek's books have already become a bibliographic editor, and today's high school students probably found them interesting and useful. It is for this reason that in 1987 it is planned to reissue the books by M. D. Frank-Kamenetsky “The Most Important Molecule” and Y. A. Smorodinsky “Temperature”.
The second edition in the Library is the book of the famous American theoretical physicist R. Feynman, “The Nature of Physical Laws,” in which the author talks about the fundamental laws of nature, how they are discovered, and what their features are. (And in the year itself, the Library plans to release a new book by R. Feynman, “QED (quantum electrodynamics) - the country of the theory of light and matter.”)
Those who want to replenish their library with new issues of Bibliotek «Kvant» are advised to place orders for them in bookstores in advance.
