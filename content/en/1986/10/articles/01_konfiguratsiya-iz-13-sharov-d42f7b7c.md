This card shows 12 identical balls (half-colored) attached to another identical ball (red). The existence of such an arrangement becomes modern if the balls are placed in the cells of a cubic lattice. Read the details in V.V. Proizvolov’s note “Thirteen balls in one cell.”

This configuration of balls can be imagined differently. One red ball and six synchs can be placed on a plane so that all the synch balls touch the red one; then you can roll up and put three more synch balls in three of the formations of six beams around the red ball; finally, one can imagine three more balls placed in the same way behind. Rotate this design (how?), we get the arrangement of balls shown here.
