Tasks

M976—M980; F988—F992

M976. From the vertex $A$ of the square $ABCD$ two loops are drawn, forming an angle $45^\circ$ between them. One intersects side $BC$ at point $E$, diagonal $BD$ at point $P$, the other intersects side $CD$ at point $F$, diagonal $BD$ at point $O$. Prove that the area of ​​triangle $AEF$ is twice the area of ​​triangle $APO$.

E. G. Gotman

M977. Is it possible to obtain $x$ using the operations of addition, subtraction and multiplication from the polynomials $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ if a) $f(x)=x^2+x$, $g(x)=x^2+2$; b) $f(x)=x^2+x$, $g(x)=x^2-2$; c) $f(x)=2x^2+x$, $g(x)=2x$; d) $f(x)=2x^2+x$, $g(x)=x^2$.

S. I. Kublanovsky

M978. Is it possible to place two regular triangles with sides larger than $\sqrt{2}/3$ in a square with side 1 without overlapping each other?

E. A. Karlov, I. K. Dimitrov (Bulgaria)

M979. Let $k$ and $n$ be natural numbers, $k \leqslant n$. Let us say that the set $k$ of positive numbers $a_1,a_2,...,a_k$ is less than 1 only if for any partition $n=n_1+n_2+\dots+n_k$ of the number $n$ into non-negative integer terms at least one of the numbers $a_i n_i$ is an integer ($i=1,2,...,n$).

a) For which $k$ and $n$ are there exclusive sets?

b) What are these sets?

E. A. Gorin

M980. A point $O$ is taken inside a convex polygon, 6) a polygon with vertices $A_1,A_2,...,A_n$. Prove that among the $n(n-1)/2$ angles $A_i O A_k$ ($i,k=1,2,...,n$) at least $n-1$ have values ​​from $90^\circ$ to $180^\circ$.

V. G. Boltyasky

F988. A hockey player skates across the ice on one skate. It is known that ice melts under the ridge to a depth of $h=0,03$ mm. Ridge width $d=2$ mm. Find the friction force between the skate and the skate. The specific heat of melting of ice is $\lambda=3,3 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg, the density of ice is $\rho=0,9$ g/cm$^3$. Consider the thermal conductivity of ice to be small.

M. G. Gavrilov (For the end see p. 34)
