⟦folio 57⟧

Leningrad State Pedagogical Institute named after. A. I. Herzen

Physics
Oral exam objectives

Faculty of Physics
1. A concrete cube weighing $m=2200$ kg is uniformly lifted from a reservoir from a depth of $h=11$ m by a crane. Determine the mechanical work required to lift this cube until its top edge touches the surface of the water. Concrete density $q=2200$ kg/$m^3$.
2. A non-rotor weight is attached to the suspension diameter fixed vertically in the shackle. At the same time, the load began to oscillate with a frequency of $v=5$ Hz. How much will the diameter spring be stretched after the load has completely stopped oscillating? Ignore the mass of the spring.
3. The point is located in the focal plane of the scattering line, the focal length of which is $F=10$ cm, at a distance of $a=2$ cm from the main optical axis of the line. Find the position of the image of this point in the face. You will get the result analytically and graphically.

Faculty of Mathematics
1. A body moves from a state of rest in equilibrium. Determine how many times the distance traveled by this body in the eighth second will be greater than the path traveled in the third second.
2. An electric kettle has two windings. When you turn on one of them, the water in the kettle boils in $t_1=15$ minutes, when you turn on the other - in $t_2=30$ minutes. How many minutes will it take for the water in the kettle to boil when both windings are switched on in parallel?
Industrial and Pedagogical Faculty
1. The flask was filled halfway with hot water, closed and the air began to be pumped out of it. Describe what processes will be observed.
2. When repairing the power supply, its spiral was shortened by 0.1 of its original length. How many times will the power supply change?
Faculty of Science
1. The shell exploded as it hit the ground $t_1=10$ s after being fired. The second shell exploded $t_2=5$ s after being fired. At what altitude did the second shell explode if the boom conditions did not change?
2. How much work will be done by air, whose mass is $m=200$ g, when heated isobarically by $\Lambda T=20$ K? How much heat will be transferred to the air? The specific heat of air at constant pressure is $c=14$ kJ/(kg K).
3. What energy must be consumed in order to release $m=1$ g of copper during electrolysis of copper sulfate dissolution? Electrolysis is carried out at a direction of $U=4$ V. The electrochemical equivalent of copper is $k=0,33$ mg/Kl. Ignore heating losses.

Matematika
Written exam
Option 1
1. Given the function $f(x)=3x(x-1)^2$.
1.1. Find the extrema of the function $f$ at $x<1$.
1.2. Solve the inequality $f(x)<6(x-1)$.
1.3. How many different roots does the equation $f(x)=kx$ have depending on $k$?
1.4. Are there pairs of different points on the graph of the function $f(x)$ that are symmetrical to the relative line $3x=1$?

2. Four villages $A, B, C$ and $D$ are located at the tops of the $8\times 10$ km rectangle (see figure). It is proposed to connect them with a network of roads according to the scheme indicated at figure ($|AL|=|BL|=|CK|=|DK|$).

2.1. Prove that the total length of all five sections of the road is $L(x)=x+2\sqrt{164-20x}+x^2$.
2.2. Find $x$ if $L(x)=24$.
2.3. At what value of $x$ is the total length of the road minimal?
3. These functions are $f(x)=x^2$, $g(x)=\ln x$.
3.1. Find the monotonicity intervals of the functions $f-g$.
3.2. Solve the equation $f(g(x))=g(f(x))$.
3.3. For what values ​​of $k$ does the equation $f(x)=kg(x)$ have a unique solution?

Option 2
1. Given the function $f(x)=\log_3(9-3^x)-1$.
1.1. Find the domain of the functions $f$.
1.2. Solve the equation $f(x)=0$.
1.3. Solve the inequality $f(x)<2x$.
1.4. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ does the equation $f(x)=a-x$ have at least one root?
2. An equilateral triangle ABC with angle $a$ at base AC is inscribed with a circle of radius 1 with center at point $O$.
2.1. Find the area $S(a)$ of triangle ABC.
2.2. Let $t=\text{tg}\frac{a}{2}$. Prove that $S(a)=\frac{2}{t-t^3}$.
2.3. Find the smallest value of $S(a)$.
2.4. What meanings can the ABC terminator apply?
3. Exchange cylinder 1 has a base radius $R$. The second cylinder has a radius 0.1 greater and a height 0.2 greater than the first.
3.1. Find $V(R)$—the volume of the second cylinder.
3.2. At what $R$ is the value of $V(R)$ minimal?
