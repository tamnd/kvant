25 years ago, for citizens of the Soviet Union, communist Yuri Alekseevich Gazarin opened the way to space for man.
A lot of work has been done since then; The role of space research in our lives is growing year by year. Cosmonauts comprehensively explore our planet, helping to solve important problems of the national economy.
The Soviet Union makes enormous efforts to ensure that space always remains an arena for international cooperation and global research and development. This problem is viewed differently in the United States, where the administration and the military-industrial complex are empowered to try to involve people in preparation for “star wars,” which could lead to civilization. Because of the dangers, the peoples of different countries are actively joining the fight for peaceful space. And the Soviet people consistently stand in the forefront of the fighters for peace and general disarmament.
