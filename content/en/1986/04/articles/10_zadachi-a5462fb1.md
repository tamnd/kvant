⟦folio 27⟧

Quests

1. The cheerful tourist went on foot to the rally. On the first day of the journey he walked 1/3 of the way, on the second day he walked 1/3 of the rest, on the third day the tourist walked 1/3 of the new remainder. As a result, tourists had 32 km to go. How many kilometers from the tourist’s house to the meeting place?

2. In No. 6 of the magazine «Kvant» in “Task «Kvant»” mathematics problems with numbers from M986 to M990 will be published. The first of them has a number that matches the last three digits of the year the magazine was published. And in which issue of the magazine does the number of the problem in mathematics coincide completely with the number of the year? (It is assumed that each issue will publish five mathematics problems and that the magazine will be published monthly.)

3. After frying eggs in a frying pan, I took the handle to put the frying pan on the table, but it was very hot. I wrapped the handle with a three-piece; but it was still hot. Then I wet the triochka with water. How will I feel this time?

4. The milk bag is peeled from a cylindrical paper flask (see figure). The result is a triangular paramid. For ease of transportation, the sides of the paramida are isosceles, but not equilateral, triangles. Suppose that a bag is made of paper, all of whose faces are regular triangles. How can we now cut it into a cylindrical flask, the height of which is equal to half the length of the paramida rib?

5. Decipher the numeric edges written in figure.
