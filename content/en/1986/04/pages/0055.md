Option 1
(Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics)

1. For what real values ​​$a$ is each solution to the inequality
   $(0,4)^{x^2+1} \geq (6,25)^{a-3x}$ is a solution to $x^2-6x+4 < a^2$?
2. Solve the equation
   $1+\log_{x-2}(4x-11)=2\log_{4x-11}(4x^2-19x+22)$.
3. Solve the equation
   $2\sin x=\sqrt{4+\cos 3x}$.
4. In an equilateral trapezoid circumscribed about a circle of radius $R$, the ratio of the length of the lateral side and the major base is a given number $k$. Find the length less than the base.
5. Two cutting planes are located symmetrically relative to the center of a ball of radius $R$. Find the set of possible values ​​of the positions of the full surface of the part of the ball enclosed between both planes to the surface area of ​​the ball. Assuming that the ratio is 7/8, determine the distance from the center of the ball to the cutting plane.

Option 2
(Faculty of Applied Mathematics - Control Processes)

1. In the mathematical model of economic growth of the economy, the product of some products for consumption and increase in stocks of fixed assets, $P$ (daily consumption of the product per capita of those employed in production) and $x$ (the number of workers employed in production) are related by functional dependence
   $P=\frac{x(M-x)}{x}$,
where $M$, $b$ are constants characterizing the production capabilities of the economy. For $M=250$, $b=8464$, determine the number of workers corresponding to the highest value $P$, in a farm with 80, 90, 120 and 150 jobs.
2. Solve the inequality
   $x>\log_{1/2}(4-x)+\log_{2}(6^x+(x-1)2^x)$.
3. Solve the inequality
   $2+\sin x=\frac{3(1+\cos x)}{\sin x}$.
4. The middle linear trapezoid is divided by one of the diagonals in the ratio $k$ and divides the trapezoid into two parts, the smaller of which is the area $S$. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.
5. In a regular hexagon, take the angle between the rods of the side boundaries to be $\beta$ and the side of the base to be $a$. Find the area of ​​the diagonal section of the prim containing the center of the base. (A diagonal section is a section of a prim by a plane passing through two lateral edges that do not lie on the same face.)
