13. Let us denote the bases of the trapezoid by $a$ and $b$, the heights of the corresponding triangles by $h_a$ and $h_b$. Then

$$s = \frac{a+b}{2}(h_a+h_b); \quad s_1 = \frac{ah_a}{2}; \quad s_2 = \frac{bh_b}{2}.$$

From the similarity $\frac{b}{a} = \frac{h_b}{h_a}$. Let's denote it by $\frac{b}{a} = t$. So, $b = ta, h_b = th_a$. In that case

$$s = (t+1)^2 \cdot \frac{bh_b}{2}; \quad s_1 = t^2 \cdot \frac{bh_b}{2}; \quad s_2 = \frac{bh_b}{2}.$$

This implies the desired equality.

14. $a = 5$. Solution.

$$x^2 + y^2 = (x+y)^2 - 2xy = (a-1)^2 - 2(a^2 - 7a + 14) = -a^2 + 12a - 27$$

as a function of $a$ is a square triangle defining for those $a$ for which there exist real $x$ and $y$ satisfying the conditions of the problem. These values ​​$x$ and $y$ are determined by the system

$$\begin{cases} x + y = a - 1, \\ xy = a^2 - 7a + 14, \end{cases}$$

which means $x$ and $y$ satisfy the quadratic equation $t^2 - (a-1)t + a^2 - 7a + 14 = 0$, whose discriminant is $-3a^2 + 26a - 55$. This discriminant is non-negative at $11/3 \leq a \leq 5$. Consequently, real values ​​of $x$ and $y$ exist only for those $a$ that belong to the interval [11/3; 5]. Our task is to ensure that on the segment [11/3; 5] find such $a$ at which trilen (1) takes on the most value. The vertex of parabolas $b = -a^2 + 12a - 27$ has an abscissa $a = 6$, which is located to the right of the segment [11/3; 5]. The highest value of expression (1) is therefore achieved at the right end of this interval, that is, at $a = 5$.
