F989. A model of a crawler trolley was placed on an inclined plane with an angle of inclination α=30° and released. Find the acceleration of the model. Model length l=50 cm, height h=2 cm. The tracks are made of rubber, their mass is 80% of the total mass of the model. The friction in the mechanism of the model is negligible.

A. I. Buzdin

F990. The heat engine is a gas-filled cylinder with a piston, the movement of which is limited by stops AA and BB (Fig. 1). The gas was slowly heated until the piston touched the stops BB, after which the base of the spring was mixed from the BB position to the GG position. The vessel is then slowly cooled until the piston touches the stops AA. Then the base of the spring is mixed back to BB, the cylinder is heated again, etc. Find the efficiency of this engine. The cylinder is filled with glia; piston area S=10 cm²; the stiffness of the spring is k=10 N/m, its length in the unstretched state is l₀=60 cm. The external pressure is assumed to be zero.

A. R. Zilberman

F991. In the circuit shown in figure 2, U₀=2.4 V, R=600 Ohm, r=200 Ohm, diodes D₁ and D₂ have the same current-voltage characteristics (Fig. 3). Find the current through diode D₂.

R. Z. Alexandrov

F992. When filming a cadar from the film “Gulliver in the Land of the Lilliputians,” the actor playing the role of Gulliver was placed at a distance of 4 m from the film camera with a short-focus lens, and the actor playing the role of the Lilliputian was placed at a distance of 40 m. At what distance should the camera lens be pointed so that the images of the characters on the film were equally clear?

Why is a short focal length lens used for this type of photography? Estimate the focal length of such a lens if you know that three-dimensional (non-combined) photography of people from a distance of 5 m is carried out with a lens with a focal length of 50 cm and a depth of field of 0.5 m.

E. N. Yunosov, I. V. Yaminsky
