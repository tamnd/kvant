Correspondence Physics School at Moscow State University

Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov announces admission of students to grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help secondary school students study physics more deeply as part of the school curriculum, as well as better prepare for entrance exams in physics to higher educational institutions and, first of all, to the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of solving the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 1 to the address: 119899 Moscow GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3FSh. Please enclose two copies of the questionnaire in the letter, written on sheets of thick paper measuring 7x12 cm and filled out according to the following template:

The admissions committee's decision on admission will be communicated by October 20. Verified entry assignments will not be returned.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on the sections of physics studied in high school. Solved tasks are evaluated, solved and sent back. Students in grade 9 of 3FS at the end of the year are transferred to grade 10 based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task
Admission to the 9th grade 3FS solves problems 1-4, and admission to the 10th grade solves problems 4-7.

1. "Strength." What force acts in a section located at a distance x from the end of a homogeneous rod of length l, to which a force F directed along the rod is applied?

2. "Hit". The ball hits the wall with speed v at an angle α to its normal. Determine the model of the speed of the ball after control of the wall, if: a) the wall is stationary; b) moves with speed u along its normal towards the ball; c) moves with speed u at an angle β to its normal towards the ball.

3. "Rebound". What horizontal distance will be covered by a ball thrown at a speed v=10 m/s at an angle α=60° to the horizontal if it hits the ceiling? Ceiling height h=3 m, elastic impact. Neglect air resistance.

4. "Bus". A bus is traveling along a straight highway. You can run at half the speed of a bus. You notice a bus at point A. From which area of ​​the highway can you catch the bus?

5. "Cobra". With what force does the cobra press on the ground when, preparing to jump, it rises vertically upward at a constant speed v? The mass of the snake is m, its length is l.

6. "Vacuum". Two semi-chafers are connected in spheres, from which they are partially woven, and suspended at a pole point. The hemispheres will break away from each other if a load of mass M is suspended at the point of the opposite pole. To each temperature must the air inside the sphere be heated so that it falls apart without additional load? The mass of each half-shell is m, the broken radius is r, the atmospheric pressure is p0, the initial temperature of the air and spheres is T0. Neglect the Tellian distribution of the sphere.

7. "Voltage". What is the greatest potential difference that can be obtained by having a battery with emf E and two identical capacitors?
