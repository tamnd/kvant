The inputs of type 1 AND circuit receive signals $a_n=1$ and $b_n=0$. Consequently, its output produces a signal 0. The same signal will be at the output of circuit 2 ($b_n=0; p_{n-1}=1$). A signal $p_n=1$ is generated at its output.

Next, from the output of circuit 6, signal 1 is supplied to the input of inverter 8, and from its output, signal 0 is supplied to the input of circuit AND 7. The second input of circuit 7 is supplied with a signal from the output of circuit OR 4. For the combination of input signals under consideration, it corresponds to 1.

At the output of circuit 7, 0 is generated, which is fed to the input of OR circuit 9. The second input of circuit 9 receives a signal from AND circuit 5, in this case 0. As a result, the output signal is $c_n=0$.
