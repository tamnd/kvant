ELECTRIC FIELD

32

1. Give an example when, when two differently charged bodies approach each other, the force of attraction between them decreases to zero.

2. What is the direction of the electric field at a point equally distant from the charges forming the electric dipole?

3. Show, without any arbitrary calculation, that the directionality of the field of an electric dipole is smaller, the smaller the distance between the charges forming the dipole.

4. How will a positively charged ball behave in each of the electric fields depicted in figure?

5. A charged conductor, taken in the form of a sheet, was rolled into a spool. Has the direction of the electric field at the surface of the conductor changed?

6. The charge (-q) is located in the center of the fields of the metal sphere, which carries the charge (+2q). Representation using field lines of the resulting electric field.

7. At a distance r from the conducting independent plane there is a charge (+q). With what force will the charge and plane be taken to act?

8. Can the electric field shown in figure exist in a vacuum?
