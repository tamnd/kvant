⟦folio 52⟧

Kyiv State University named after. T. G. Shevchenko

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)
1. Find the smallest value of the function $y=3x+e^{-2x}$.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{1+\sin^2x}+2\cos \frac{x}{2}=0.$$
3. Find the volume of a regular triangular pyramid if the side of its base is $a$ and the radius of the ball inscribed in the pyramid is $r$.
4. Indicate how many solutions the system of equations has
$$\begin{cases} x^2+y^2=8, \\ (y-a)(y-a\sqrt{2})=0 \end{cases}$$
depending on parameter $a$.
5. Indicate all values ​​of the parameter $a$, for each of which the following statement is true: if $-\frac{\pi}{2}<a<0$ and $-\frac{\pi}{2}<\beta<0$, and $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2}=a\cos \beta$, then $-\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha+\beta<0$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Find the smallest and largest values ​​of the function $y=2x^2-2x-1$ on the interval [0; 2].
2. Solve the equation
$$2\cos^2x-3\sin x=0.$$
3. The sum of the three numbers that form an arithmetic progression is 30. If you subtract 5 from the first number, 4 from the second, and leave the third number unchanged, then the resulting numbers form a geometric progression. Find these numbers.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2^2x-\log_{1/2}x<12-3\sqrt{\log_2^2x+\log_2\frac{x}{4}}.$$
5. The vertex of the angle $ABC$, the magnitude of which is less than 180°, is on the circle $l_1$, and one of its sides is through the center of the circle. Circle $l_2$ is inscribed in angle $ABC$ and externally touches circle $l_1$. Find at what value of angle $ABC$ the radius of circle $l_2$ is 24 times greater than the radius of circle $l_1$.

Option 3
(radiophysics department)
1. Solve the equation
$$4\cos^2x+4\sin x=1.$$
2. Find the smallest and largest values ​​of the function
$$y=-2x^3+3x^2+12x+5$$
on the interval [-2; 1].

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{2\cdot4^{x+1}-5\cdot9^x}{9^{x+1}-7\cdot4^x}<1.$$
