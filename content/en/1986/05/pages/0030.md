Tasks

M981—M985; F993—F997

M981. Prove that the number 11...1 (1986 units) has at least a) 8, 6) 28 different factors.

L. D. Kurlyandchik

M982. On sides AB, BC and CA of triangle ABC, squares ABB₁A₂, BCB₁C₂, CAA₁C₂ are constructed on the outer side. Prove that the perpendiculars to the segments A₁A₂, B₁B₂, C₁C₂, restored at their midpoints, intersect at one point.

N. Azamov (8th grade student)

M983. In a tournament with 16 tennis players each, two play one game.

a) Give an example of the distribution of game results in which any 10 participants can be placed in a circle so that each one wins over his left neighbor.

6) Prove that if condition a) is satisfied, then any 11 participants can be arranged in a circle in this way.

K. P. Kokhas

M984. Through an arbitrary point K square ABCD a straight line is drawn intersecting its opposite sides AB and CD at points P and Q. Prove that the intersection point of the circles passing through the points K, B, P and K, D, Q, different from K, lies on the diagonal BD (Fig. 1).

V. N. Dubrovsky

M985. The angle between two straight lines intersecting at point Q is the angle between their rays with vertex O, not exceeding 90°. In how many ways can three straight lines l₁, l₂, l₃ be drawn through a point O in space so that the angles between l₂ and l₃, l₃ and l₁, l₁ and l₂ correspond to the given numbers α₁, α₂, α₃? (Two points of the lines l₁, l₂, l₃ and l₁′, l₂′, l₃′ are considered the same if they are “congruent”, that is, if there is a poimetry with respect to the plane that takes l₁ to l′′ for all i=1, 2, 3.)

Upcoming questions: the answer depends on the value of α₁, α₂, α₃; for example, for α₁=α₂=α₃=30° it is not the same as for α₁=α₂=α₃=70°.

A. V. Goncharov
