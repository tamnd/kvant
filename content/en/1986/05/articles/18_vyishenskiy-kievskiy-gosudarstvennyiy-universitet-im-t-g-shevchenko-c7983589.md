Kyiv State University named after. T. G. Shevchenko

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics)
1. Find the smallest value of the function $y=3x+e^{-2x}$.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{1+\sin^2x}+2\cos \frac{x}{2}=0.$$
3. Find the volume of a regular triangular pyramid if the side of its base is $a$ and the radius of the ball inscribed in the pyramid is $r$.
4. Indicate how many solutions the system of equations has
$$\begin{cases} x^2+y^2=8, \\ (y-a)(y-a\sqrt{2})=0 \end{cases}$$
depending on parameter $a$.
5. Indicate all values ​​of the parameter $a$, for each of which the following statement is true: if $-\frac{\pi}{2}<a<0$ and $-\frac{\pi}{2}<\beta<0$, and $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2}=a\cos \beta$, then $-\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha+\beta<0$.

Option 2
(Faculty of Physics)
1. Find the smallest and largest values ​​of the function $y=2x^2-2x-1$ on the interval [0; 2].
2. Solve the equation
$$2\cos^2x-3\sin x=0.$$
3. The sum of the three numbers that form an arithmetic progression is 30. If you subtract 5 from the first number, 4 from the second, and leave the third number unchanged, then the resulting numbers form a geometric progression. Find these numbers.
4. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2^2x-\log_{1/2}x<12-3\sqrt{\log_2^2x+\log_2\frac{x}{4}}.$$
5. The vertex of the angle $ABC$, the magnitude of which is less than 180°, is on the circle $l_1$, and one of its sides is through the center of the circle. Circle $l_2$ is inscribed in angle $ABC$ and externally touches circle $l_1$. Find at what value of angle $ABC$ the radius of circle $l_2$ is 24 times greater than the radius of circle $l_1$.

Option 3
(radiophysics department)
1. Solve the equation
$$4\cos^2x+4\sin x=1.$$
2. Find the smallest and largest values ​​of the function
$$y=-2x^3+3x^2+12x+5$$
on the interval [-2; 1].

3. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{2\cdot4^{x+1}-5\cdot9^x}{9^{x+1}-7\cdot4^x}<1.$$

4. Specify all values ​​of parameter $a$ at which the system
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 + 2x \leq 1, \\ x - y + a = 0 \end{cases}$$
has a unique solution.
5. The height of the cone is equal to the diameter of its base, and the volume of the cone is $v$. Find the volume of a sphere circumscribed about a cone.

Option 4
(geological faculty)
1. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \frac{2}{x} + \frac{3}{y} = 8, \\ \frac{5}{x} - \frac{2}{y} = 1. \end{cases}$$
2. Solve the equation
$$9\cos x - 10 \cdot 3^{-\frac{1}{2} + \cos x} + 3 = 0.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\log_2(x+1)^2 + 1 < 0.$$
4. Indicate at what point the function $y = \log_{1/2}(3-2x-x^2)$ takes on its smallest value. Find this value.
5. The ratio of the area of ​​the lateral surface of the right quadrangular rectangle to the area of ​​its base is equal to $\sqrt{3}$. Find the angle between the opposite side edges of the straight line.

Option 5
(geological faculty)
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3} \sin 3x + \cos 3x = 0.$$
2. Specify the intervals of increasing and decreasing function
$$y = \frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^2}{2} - 2x + 3.$$
3. Calculate
$$\log_{\sqrt{2}}(4\sqrt{2} \cdot 3^{\log_{1/3}\sqrt{8}}).$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{1-x} > 2x + 8.$$
5. The lateral surface area of ​​the cone is $s$, and its total surface area is $p$. Determine the angle between the height and the generatrix of the cone.
Oral exam assignment
1. Explain why converting an objective fraction to a decimal always results in either a finite decimal or a periodic fraction.
2. The main section of the cone is a right triangle. The radii $r_n$ ($n=1, 2, 3, \dots$) of the balls $S_n$ form into the incoming cone?
6) What sequence do the volumes of these balls form?
b) What part of the volume of the cone is the total volume of all the balls together?
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2xz + 3yz + z^2 = 11z, \\ 3xz + yz - z^2 = 2z, \\ xz - 2yz + 2z^2 = 3z. \end{cases}$$
4. Indicate how the numbers $a$ and $b$ should be related to each other so that the inequality
$$\sqrt{x-a} > \sqrt{2x-b}$$ had a solution. In the case where they essentially form solutions? For what $a$ and $b$ is this set the interval [1; 5]?
5. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{25-x^2} \leq \frac{12}{x}.$$
6. Give an example of a function that has:
a) there are infinitely many axes of symmetry, but no center of symmetry;
b) there are infinitely many centers of symmetry, but it does not have an axis of symmetry.
7. The bases of a trapezoid are $a$ and $b$ ($a < b$). The diagonals of a trapezoid divide it into even triangles. The area of ​​the smallest of them (by area) is $S$. Indicate what kind of triangle this is. What are the areas of the other triangles?
8. The radius of circle $l_1$ is equal to $r$. Find out whether there exists a circle $l_2$ concentric with $l_1$ such that $n$ circles can be inscribed in the ring formed by these circles, each of which would touch two neighboring ones ($n$ is a given natural number, $n \geq 3$). Determine the radius of the circle $l_2$.
9. The area angle at the vertex of the right-hand $n$-gonal straight line is equal to $a$. Determine at what angle the lateral edges of the straight line are inclined to the plane of the base.
10. Prove that the graph of every function $y=ax^2+bx+c$ ($a \neq 0$) has a symmetry axis.
11. Two balls of radii $r_1$ and $r_2$ ($r_1 < r_2$) touch each other and all the generators of the cone. Determine the angle at the apex of the axial section of the cone.
12. Solve the inequality
$$\left| \log_2 \frac{x}{3} \right|^{x^2-5x-6} \leq 1.$$
13. Find the smallest and largest values ​​of the function $y=|x^2-4x|-1$ on the interval $[-\frac{1}{2}; 3].$
14. Solve the equation
$$\frac{4\sin x}{(x-3)^2} + |\sin x| = 0.$$
15. The sum of the first nineteen terms of an arithmetic progression is 76. Find the tenth term of this progression. Is it possible to define any other member of this progression?
16. Graph the Function
$$y = \begin{cases} \frac{x}{1+x}, & \text{ если } x \leq 0, \\ \log_{1/2}(x+1), & \text{ если } x > 0. \end{cases}$$
17. List all values ​​of $a$, each of which has the smallest value on the interval [0; 1] the value of the function $y=2+ax^2-x^3$ is reached at point $x=1$.
