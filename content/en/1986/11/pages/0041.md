Durable razor blades or wear-free drill bits are the first steps on this path.

No less promising is another remarkable property of amorphous metal - exceptionally high corrosion resistance. For some glasses, it exceeds the resistance of stainless steel. Everyone knows what rust is. But how to effectively deal with it is a much more difficult question. Ten laboratories in many countries around the world are engaged in it. One possible solution is to create thin amorphous elephants on a metal surface. Such protection will be much more effective today if varnishes, paints, etc. are used for this purpose. True, it is much more difficult to create such a layer. This area is currently being actively developed.

Probably, very soon products made of amorphous metal in the form of magnetic heads or blades will appear in our homes. So far, their widespread use has been hampered by the fact that the amorphous metal is produced in the form of a thin ribbon, rather than massive samples. Those simply cannot be cooled at the required speed. But research continues...
