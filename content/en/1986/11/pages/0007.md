⟦folio 5⟧

This year, our country is widely celebrating its anniversary - the 275th anniversary of the birth of Mikhail Vasilyevich Lomonosov. It often happens that the name of an outstanding scientist who has devoted himself to one field of science remains unknown to representatives of other specialties and, especially, to the general public. Lomonosov, on the other hand, considers chemists, physics, theoreticians, and geography one of the founders of his science... He left a bright mark on Russian literature and art.

He is hopelessly trying to cover in one journal article all the diversity of Lomonosov's interests and achievements - many books have been written about this. An idea of ​​the diversity of Lomonosov’s talent is given by a “mosaic” of his work and statements about him by outstanding representatives of scientific science and culture, with the publication of which our magazine celebrates the anniversary of the Great Russian Encyclopedia.
