The Department of Mathematics was headed by Academician M. A. Lavrentyev. Mechanics was taught by academician L. I. Sedov and corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences V. V. Sokolovsky, physicist - professors S. E. Khaikin and S. G. Kalashnikov.

It is quite natural that many great scientists and academicians were not yet great scientists and academicians. Our current head of the department of higher mathematics, corresponding member L. D. Kudryavtsev, for example, was still a candidate of sciences, and academician S. M. Nikolsky was a doctor.

Lectures on physics were excellently given by Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences S. M. Rytov. I treasured my notes from these lectures. And in the physics exam I got a B from him and was terribly upset.

— Were there then such elements of the physical education system as elective questions on exams, basic institutes, bosses, and government?

— The state exam in physics was passed by L. D. Land. And he took it very harshly.

There were no optional questions, but there were written tests. More difficult than now. If you scored 70-80% of the maximum number of points, then you are practically guaranteed an A. You went to the lecturer, and he didn’t so much ask questions as talk to you.

Bases and chiefs have existed from the very beginning. The main emphasis was on physics and engineering training. There were few basic institutes: six - eight.

— What changes took place in the physical education system in subsequent years?

— Over time, the fizek system has evolved, this is natural. New faculties arose. We now have a department of aeromechanics and flight technology. Over the twenty years of its existence, it has proven itself to be excellent.

Another new element was the admission of applicants in Vladivostok, Kyiv, Krasnodar, Chelyabinsk, Khabarovsk. Recruiting the strongest guys locally, training them in Dolgoprudny according to a special program and distributing them after the fourth year back to specialties that are urgently needed by a given resource or region - all this meets modern requirements, meets, if you like, the world level of personnel training.

In general, our institute began to pay great attention to “our” applicants. Correspondence and evening physics and technology schools have been created, on-site physics and mathematics optimizations are being conducted, and advertising issues of our institute newspaper “For Sciences” are being published. To process 700-800 future students, the institute “screens” 10-12 thousand schoolchildren.
