About the method of scientific research

What is required is not the kind of chemist who has understood this science from just one chapter of a book, but who has diligently practiced it with his own art... It is not the kind of mathematician who is required who is skilled only in difficult calculations, but who, having become accustomed to mathematical skill in pictures and in proofs, has the ability to deduce the modern truth in nature in an accurate and non-creeping manner... Chemistry by hand, mathematics can rightly be called physical centers.

About popular science literature

Anyone who takes the trouble to inform the public of what is contained in new writings must first of all marshal his forces. After all, he is undertaking a difficult and very complex job, in which he comes to report not on objective things and not just on generalities, but to grasp what is new and essential that lies in the work, often created by the greatest people...

A journalist is allowed to refute in new writings what, in his opinion, deserves it - although this is not his direct task and his recognition in the proper sense; but since he has already taken up this, he owes it to nature. In the descriptions one can hear the look of a learned naturalism rather than a pose; but the sincere power of delight turned naturalness into posture.

Lomonosov passionately loved science, but he thought and cared passionately about what was needed for the good of his homeland. He wanted to serve not pure science, but only humanity.

N. G. Chernyshevsky
(1828—1889)

Every touch of Russia, dear to his heart, which he looks at from the angle of its shining future, fulfills him with miraculous power... He surveys the entire Russian land from edge to edge from some bright height, admiring and not being taxed by its boundlessness and effectiveness.

N.V. Gogol (1809—1852)
