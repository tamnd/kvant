M. V. LOMONOSOV
IN DOMESTIC PHILATELY

Price 40 kopecks.
Index 70465

The first Soviet postage stamps dedicated to reporting science were issued in 1925, on the occasion of the 200th anniversary of the Academy of Sciences. They depict a portrait of M.V. Lomonosov against the background of the building of the Academy of Sciences in Leningrad. A similar composition is reproduced on a stamp dedicated to the 220th anniversary of the Academy of Sciences. On January 5, 1949, the M. V. Lomonosov Museum opened in Leningrad. This event was commemorated in Punka stamps with his portrait. The portrait of M.V. Lomonosov is also reproduced on a stamp and postal block dedicated to the 200th anniversary of Moscow State University. In 1956, a series of stamps with portraits of writers of our Motherland was issued. One of them is dedicated to M.V. Lomonosov. In 1961, on the occasion of the 250th anniversary of the birth of M. V. Lomonosov, a series of three stamps was issued - two with his portraits and one with an image of the monument to M. V. Lomonosov against the backdrop of the new building of Moscow State University.
