⟦folio 23⟧

Quests

1. How to cut the figure shown at figure into three equal parts?

2. Railway rails are secured to sleepers to prevent the rails from moving to the side. What other important role do sleepers play?

3. In the entry ***+***=***, replace the asterisks with numbers so that the equality makes sense and is correct after rotating the sheet of paper (on which it is written) by 180°.

4. The seller who brought nuts to the market to sell turned out to have the wrong lever scales and the correct kilogram weight. To answer the buyer for 2 kg of nuts, he once balanced the weight with nuts, placing it on the right pan of the scale, and another time balanced this weight with nuts, placing it on the left pan of the scale. It turned out that as a result he answered more than 2 kg of nuts (show this). What should the seller do to answer exactly 2 kg of nuts?

5. Draw three chords AB, BC, CD in a circle and mark their midpoints: M, N, K. Show that the angles BMN and NKC are equal.

These tasks were suggested to us by M. Babikov, A. P. Savin, A. V. Shvetsov, N. Kh. Rozov, G. A. Galperin.
