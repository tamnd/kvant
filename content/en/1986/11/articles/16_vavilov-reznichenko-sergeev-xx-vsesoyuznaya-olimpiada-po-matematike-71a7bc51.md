XX National Olympiad in Mathematics

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences V.V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences S. V. REZNICHENKO,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences I. N. SERGEEV

From April 24 to 29, in the homeland of V. I. Lenin in Ulyanovsk, the final round of the XX All-round Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren, dedicated to the 275th anniversary of the birth of M. V. Lomonosov, was held. In 1982, Ulyanovsk was the organizer of the final round of the All-Union Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry Olympiad for schoolchildren, and now the city is honored to host the anniversary All-Out Mathematical Olympiad. The final round of the XX Olympiad was attended by teams of 14 allied resource workers, one team each from four zones of the RSFSR, a team from Moscow and Leningrad, a school of the Ministry of Railways, seven specialized resource workers from physics and mathematics schools, as well as a team from the city of Ulyanovsk, the organizer of the Olympiad. In total, 163 schoolchildren took part in the Olympiad: 46 eighth-graders, 58 ninth-graders and 59 tenth-graders, including 27 prize-winners of the XIX All-Union Olympiad.

From the moment they arrived in Ulyanovsk, the team members and leaders were surrounded by the care and attention of the Olympiad farms. At the registration of the Olympiad participants, Tello was greeted by members of the organizer; in memory of the Olympiad, each team member and leader was awarded an anniversary medal, a traditional badge, an Olympiad program, and sovereigns. From the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences, each participant was given a library of literature on mathematics. At all events, the Olympiad is accompanied by commissars - 9th grade students from city schools, employees of the region's public education department, school teachers and university professors.

The grand opening of the XX All-Russian Mathematical Olympiad took place on April 24 on the territory of the Leninsky Emory. First, a ceremonial line-up was held at the monument to V.I. Lenin, and the Olympiad participants laid flowers. A. L. Kruglikov addressed the young mathematicians with greetings and wishes of success in the spirit of science and educational institutions at the Ulyanovsk Regional Committee of the CPSU. Then, to the sounds of the orchestra, the column of Olympiad participants headed to the Leninsky Memorial, where the opening ceremony of the Olympiad took place in the Great Hall. The participants were addressed by the secretary of the Ulyanovsk regional party committee V.N. Sverkalov, deputy. Chairman of the Central Orchestra of the All-Olympiad M. N. Tambiev, Deputy. Chairman of the Olympiad jury, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. V. Strauss. The winners of the XVIII and XIX All-Russian Olympiads addressed their friends and competitors with wishes of success. The grand opening ended with a concert of amateur performances, which was in honor of the Olympiad participants, team leaders and jury members for the best creative teams of the city's schools.

The next two days - April 25 and 26 - were the most difficult for the Olympiad participants, because on each of these days they had to solve 4 competitive problems. It is pleasant to note that the Olympiad participants, on the whole, successfully completed the proposed tasks.

The days of the Olympiad were not easy for the members of the jury, who worked under the leadership of Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the Ukrainian SSR B.V. Gnedenko. After all, the deadlines needed to be organized for checking a large number of works, conducting analyzes of the proposed solutions (many of which were very original and often unknown to the jury) and analyzing the problem with solving typical mistakes for participants in the Olympiad and the Russian Federation.
