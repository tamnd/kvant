Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$10 \cdot 4 \frac{1}{\sin^2 x} - 16 = 42 \text{ ctg}^2 x + 2.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\log \frac{1}{4} - 3x (1 + 25x^2) > 0.$$
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} y^4 + 19 = 20(x + y), \\ \sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x + 2y} = \sqrt{2}. \end{cases}$$
4. The base AD of the trapezoid ABCD (AD || BC, AD || BC) is the diameter of the circle that touches the line CD at point D and intersects side AB at point L such that |AB| = 4/√3 · |AL|. The radius of the circle is R, the angle CAD = 45°. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.
5. Point D lies on the edge BC of the regular triangular line SABC (S is the vertex), |BD|: |DC| = 2:3. The cylinder rocks the lateral surfaces of the planes SAB and SBC, one of the bases of the cylinder passes through point D, the second base has a common point with the edge SC. The lateral surface of the cylinder has a single common point with edge AC. Find the ratio of the volumes of the cylinder and straight lines.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{1}{1 - \sqrt{1-x}} + \frac{1}{1 + \sqrt{1-x}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-x}}.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\left(\frac{1}{9}\right)^x - \frac{x^2}{2} \geq 3 |3x - 12| + 2x.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$9 \log_{\sin 2x} 4 \cos^2 x + 8 \log_2 \cos x \sin x = 16.$$
4. The chords AB and AC are drawn in a circle, and |AB| = 2 cm, |AC| = 1 cm, angle
$$\widehat{CAB} = 120^\circ.$$. Find the length of the chord of the circle that bisects angle CAB.

5. A sphere is inscribed in a regular quadrangular line SABCD (S is the vertex). The length of the base edge of the line is 8, and the length of the height of the line is 3. Point M is the midpoint of the edge SD, point K is the orthogonal projection of point M onto the ABCD plane. A tangent to the sphere is drawn through point M, intersecting the plane ASC at point N so that NMK=arccos (-1/3). Find |NM|.
Option 3
1. Solve the equation
$$\log_4(2x + 9) + 1)\log_{x+2} 2 = 1.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$1 - 12 \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - 2x\right) - 12 \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} + 2x\right) = \sqrt{2} \cos^2 x + \tg \frac{7\pi}{4}.$$
3. Find all values ​​of the parameter a for which the vertices of two parabolas y=4x^2 + 8ax - a and y=4ax^2 - 8x + a - 2 lie on the same side of the straight line y=-5.
4. In the trapezoid MNPQ (MQ || NP), the angle NPM is 2 times the angle NQM. |NP| = |MP| = 13/2, |MQ| = 12. Find the area of ​​the trapezoid.
5. Given a regular hexagonal line SABCDEF (S is the vertex). Points K and L are chosen on edges ES and AF, respectively, so that |EK| = 1/5 |ES|, |FL| = 1/2 |FA|. Points R and T are located on straight lines DK and SL so that straight line RT is perpedicular to plane SAD. The length of the height of the lines is 18, |RT| = 4.
Find the volume of the straight line.
