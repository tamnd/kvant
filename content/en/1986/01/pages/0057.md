Option 2
(Physics Department)

1. Find the volume of a regular quadrangular paramid whose edge is equal to $l$ and makes an angle $\alpha$ with the plane of the base.
2. Solve the equation
$$\cos x + \frac{1}{\cos x} = 2.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$|x^2 - 4x| < 5.$$
4. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(9 - 2x) = 1.$$
5. Find the highest and highest value of the function $y = x^3 - 3x^2 + 5$ on the interval [0; 3].

Option 3
(where is the Faculty of Physics)

1. The tellyukhod sprayed 9 km across the lake and 20 km along the river in 1 hour. Find the speed of the tellyukhod when moving along the lake if the speed of the river is 3 km/h.
2. The basis of the call is a regular triangle inscribed in a circle with a radius of 2 m, its side faces are squares. Determine the scope of this call.
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin x - \sqrt{3 \cos x} = 0.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{x}{3^2} > 9.$$
5. Find the increasing intervals of the function $y = 4 + x - 3x^3$.

$$\tg(2x + 4) = 1.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$x - 3 = (5 + x) \cdot 4.$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$x^2 - 14x - 15 > 0.$$
