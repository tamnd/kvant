⟦folio 34⟧

Tasks

M961—M965; F973—F977

M961. Point E is taken on side AB of square ABCD, and point F is taken on side CD, with AE:EB=1:2, CF=FD. Will the head and the pink triangle (Fig. 1) be similar?

A. P. Savin, N. A. Paravyan

M962. Prove that for any polynomial P(x) with integer coefficients it is impossible to find such distinct integers x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ (n ≥ 3) for which the equalities P(x₁)=x₂, P(x₂)=x₃, ..., P(xₙ-1)=xₙ, P(xₙ)=x₁ would hold.

Dumitru Radu (Romania)

M963. When pairs of opposite sides of a hexagon are parallel. Prove that the segments connecting their midpoints intersect at one point.

T. Ghazaryan (10th grade student)

M964. Prove that in the sequence (aₙ) of different natural numbers satisfying the condition aₙ<100n, there is a number whose decimal notation a) contains the digit 1;
b) 1986 units occur in a row.

A. A. Stolin

M965. Given six numbers a₁, a₂, ..., a₆. To calculate “in any case” the sum of their pairwise products a₁a₂+a₁a₃+...+a₅a₆, you need to spend 15 multiplications and 14 additions. Prove how you can immediately find the sum of these numbers, the sum of their products of two, three, four and five, using only 15 additions and 14 multiplications.

N. V. Vasiliev

F973. The body begins to move rectilinearly from point A and after time T gets to point B a) with the maximum possible speed, 6) with the minimum possible speed; distance |AB| equals l. Find the speed of the body in these two cases, if the speed at point A v₀=0 and the acceleration of the body is a₀ < a < a₀.

D. A. Akopyan

F974. Determine the period of small oscillations of the system shown in figure 2; load mass m, elasticity k, AOB=α. The rod and the block are considered smooth, well, weightless. The spring is fixed at point A. Will the period of oscillation change if a load m is also fixed at point O?

L. G. Markovich
