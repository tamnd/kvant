In the list of the greatest mathematicians from antiquity to the present day, Pythagoras should certainly come first. Not only because he is the first great mathematician known to us. The name of Pythagoras and his era are associated with the emergence of mathematics as a science, its transformation from a set of useful rules and sieves into an abstract educational science.

What does it mean? Before answering this question, let us dwell on the life of Pythagoras and his era.

Epoch. The time in which this greatest scientist lived was the 6th century BC. e., was a special era not only in Greece, but also in almost the entire southern world known to Greek society. Just as in the spring, without saying a word, all the apple trees bloom at once, in the same way, at this time, in various places of the quiet backwater of the Old World, powerful waves rise: new ideas and teachings arise. In Greece at that time the first natural philosophers appeared: Thales, Anaximander, Pythagoras, Parmenides, Heraclitus, Empedocles; greatest literary poses: Archilochus and Sappho; the first creators of tragedies: Phrynichus and Aeschylus. Life is the key everywhere, numerous scientific and philosophical schools, religious sects arise - they all strive to comprehend and explain the structure of the Universe and the place of man in it. At the same time, a new state system was born in Greece - democracy - which no previous state had known.

Life. The most sketchy information has been preserved about the life of Pythagoras. According to the most common version, he was born around 570 BC. e. on the Junian island of Samoce, famous for its remarkable artistic and technical structures.
