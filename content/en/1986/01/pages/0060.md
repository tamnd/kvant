Correspondence Physics and Technology School at MISiSE

The branch of the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School - the Correspondence Physics and Technology School at the Moscow Order of the October Republic and the Order of Labor Red Replacement Institute of Steel and Alloys - announces enrollment of students in the middle, ninth and tenth grades for the 1986/1987 academic year.

The goal of the school is to assist students in in-depth study of secondary school program material in physics and mathematics.

The mathematics curriculum is fully consistent with the VZMS course. The physics course was developed by a teacher from the Department of General Physics and the Department of Theoretical Physics at the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys and meets the requirements for entrance exams to universities, primarily MISiS.

The work of the school is organized as follows. Four to five times a year, students are sent test assignments in physics and mathematics, along with brief information on the theory and examples of problem solving. Test papers sent to students are checked and sent to students along with grades and comments. As an annual assignment, tenth grade students are sent materials from the entrance exams to MISiS for past years. Students who successfully graduate from the ZFTSH enjoy preference for admission to MISiS.

Classes at ZFTSH (MISIS) will begin on October 1, 1986. To enroll in a school, you must send an application indicating your last name, first name, patronymic, full home address, profession and position of your parents, and also attach a certificate from the schoolOLS indicating your class and annual grades in physics and mathematics. The application and certificate, together with the solution to the first assignment in mathematics, which is written about in the article “A New Example in the All-Union Correspondence School of Mathematics” in this issue of the magazine, must be sent no later than May 5 to the address: 117049 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 4, MISiS, ZFTSH.
