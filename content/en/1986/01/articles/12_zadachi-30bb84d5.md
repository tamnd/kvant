⟦folio 29⟧

Quests

1. One day I decided to take a ride on the chairlift. At some point I noticed that the CHAIR coming towards me had number 95, and the next one had number 0, then 1, 2, etc. I looked at the number of my chair. It turned out to be 66. Am I halfway there? When meeting which chair will I travel halfway?

2. Here's a very simple one
Γ+O=L-O=B×O=L-O=M-K=A
Replace the letters with numbers to get true equalities; in this case, identical letters must correspond to the same numbers, and different ones must correspond to different ones.

3. A certain amount of liquid was poured into an irregularly shaped bottle. How, without any changing instruments and other vessels, can you find out whether the liquid occupies more or less than half the volume of the bottle?

4. How to find the center of a drawn circle if you only have a pencil and an ordinary ruler with parallel edges (the width of the ruler is less than the diameter of the circle)?

5. A grandfather and three grandchildren went out for a walk in the park. An acquaintance of his grandfather's, who met them, asked how old each of them was. Vanaya said: “I am younger than Petya and I am more than five years old.” Petya said:
“I’m 3 years younger than Sasha.” And Sasha said: “All of us together are 3 times less years old than grandfather, and together with grandfather we are exactly 100 years old.” How old are each of the grandchildren?

These tasks were offered to us by: A. P. Savin, L. P. Mochalov, M. I. Lobak, A. I. Demidov, N. K. Antonovich.
