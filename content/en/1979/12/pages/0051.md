XI International Physics Olympiad for SCHOOLCHILDREN

At the beginning of July, Moscow hosted participants and guests of the XI International Physics Olympiad for schoolchildren. In our country, such an Olympiad was held for the second time; for the first time, the Soviet Union organized the International Physics Olympiad in 1971.

Schoolchildren, or rather school graduates, from ten European countries came to the Olympiad in Moscow: Bulgaria, Hungary, East Germany, Poland, Romania, USSR, Finland, Germany, Czechoslovakia and Sweden. Each country's team consisted of five participants and two leaders.

The USSR team included the winners of the XIII All-Union Schoolchildren Olympiad in Physics:

Serdey Gordienko - graduate of school No. 2 in Smolevchi,
Maxim Tsypin - graduate of school No. 2 in Moscow,
Serdey Shpilkin is a graduate of boarding school No. 18 at Moscow State University,
Olee Yushchuk - graduate of school No. 145 in Kyiv,
Neor Yasonov is a graduate of boarding school No. 18 at Moscow State University.

The team leaders were V. A. Orlov, a senior researcher at the Research Institute of Content and Teaching Methods of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences, and S. M. Kabardina, a junior researcher at the same institute.
