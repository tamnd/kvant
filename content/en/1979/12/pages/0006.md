Further, the author considers curves with two foci as a special case of the class of curves with multiple foci, in which two or more foci coincide; a trick where two threads meet is considered a double focus, if three threads meet then it is a triple focus, etc.

Professor Forbes noted that the equation of a curve with two foci can easily be reduced to the form

$$\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = a + b\sqrt{(x - c)^2 + y^2}$$

i.e., the same as the families of curves known as Descartes ovals of the first kind *). Mr Maxwell
