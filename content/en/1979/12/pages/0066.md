An electric locomotive moves wagons
(cm. «Kvant» No. 11, 3rd page of cover)

The electric locomotive E drives T into a dead end, C into the place of the varon T and, having passed places, approaches C from the other side. Having again picked up T, and then T, the electric locomotive takes T out of the dead end and puts it in its place, and drives T into the dead end. Having unhooked the C and, passing through the places, E first picks up the T, and then the C. Once between the C and the T, the electric locomotive places the varons in the given places and returns to its original position.
