⟦folio 58⟧

New note
to the All-Union correspondence
math school

The All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) admits students from rural classes and vocational schools. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their surrounding areas are not accepted at the school.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1980. Education at the school is free.

Students accepted into the school will regularly receive assignments that teach theoretical questions and problems to solve. The VZMS program is closely related to the school curriculum and is aimed at deepening the study of the main issues of the school mathematics course. The duration of training is three years. Everyone who successfully completes the tasks receives a certificate of completion of the VZMSH.

The objectives of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter the VZMSH must submit solutions to these problems no later than February 1, 1980. After checking the work (approximately in July 1980), the VZMSH will inform everyone who took part in the competition the results of the work. Mostly upon admission, users are schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from regular school problems, solving them does not require additional knowledge of mathematics. To enter school, it is not necessary to solve all problems without exception. When evaluating work, not only the number of problems solved will be taken into account, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without justification may not be read. If there are several answers to a problem, you must indicate them all.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. The introductory work will not be processed, and no decision will be issued for it.

A piece of paper measuring 14 cm × 6 cm with a full postal address must be enclosed in the envelope along with the notebook (this piece of paper will be pasted on the envelope informing the Admissions Committee of the Higher School of Music about the results of checking the entrance work).

You need to paste a piece of checkered paper onto the cover of the notebook, draw it and fill it out according to the following sample (otherwise your work will not be checked):

Test results

There are 37 VZMSh films located on the territory of the USSR. Students living in the film area must send their work to the appropriate universities or institutes (for addresses, see «Kvant», 1979, No. 2, p. 59).

The rest of the schoolchildren must send their work to the address: 117234, Moscow, V-234, Moscow State University, VZMSH, for the competition.

Schoolchildren who did not qualify for the competition at VZMSH have the opportunity to take part in the “Collective student of VZMSH” group.
