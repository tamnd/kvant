CONSTRUCTION
POLYHEDRON ACCORDING TO EPURE

This cover page shows a diagram that can be easily constructed using the “red” triangle rules. Further constructions are indicated by dashed lines. Using pole arms on the diagram, according to the previously described method (see «Kvant» No. 3), you can begin to construct various polyhedra. One of them, “Sputnik,” is shown on the 1st page of the cover. Its faces on the diagram are shown in green (top of the model) and yellow (bottom of the model). The faces for the “Torch” polyhedron (upper model) are shown in blue; the faces for the star polyhedron (lower model) are shown in red.

V. Gamayunov
