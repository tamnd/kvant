⟦folio 47⟧

New books

In this issue we publish brief summaries of books on mathematics and physics published in the fourth quarter of 1979.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Science"

1. Uspensky V. A. Post Machine. Volume 4 l., circulation 50,000 copies, price 15 kopecks.
Post's machine is a very simple "abstract" (i.e., imaginary) computing machine. On Post's machine you can program - in a certain sense - any algorithms. The study of Post machines can be considered as the initial stage of learning the theory of algorithms and program input.

The brochure is also available to primary school students.

2. Arlazarov V. L., Donskoy M. V., Adelson-Velsky G. M. Ceram machines in chess. Volume 10 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 65 kopecks.
The work is related to one of the problems of the so-called artificial intelligence - the creation of a program for machines that play chess. The basic principle of such programs - enumeration of positions - is part of the enumeration method, widely used in solving problems of discrete mathematics. The book talks about the successes achieved in this area and about future prospects.

3. Simonov R. A. Kirik Novgorodian - scientist of the 12th century. Volume 6 l., circulation 30,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
In 1136, 26-year-old Novgorodian Kirik wrote a scientific treatise devoted to the unity of time counting, the basic concepts of the calendar and the use of this information in chronology. Along the way, the ancient Russian author demonstrates a high reduction in counting, making accurate calculations with numbers on the order of tens of millions. The book uses new documentary data about Kirik.

4. Steklov V. A. and Kneiser A. Perepuska. Volume 7 l., circulation 15,000 copies, price 50 kopecks.
The book tells about the life and work of two famous scientists of the late 19th - early 20th centuries: the Russian mathematician and mechanic V. A. Steklov (1864-1924) and the German mathematician A. Kneiser (1862-1930). Their general correspondence is interesting both for its scientific content and for the historical material contained in it.

5. Nikiforsky V. A. From the history of algebra of the XVI-XVII centuries. Volume 10 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 65 kopecks.
The 16th and 17th centuries are an important milestone in the development of algebra - a method for solving equations of the third and fourth degree was found and the development of algebraic symbols was basically completed. The main contributions to algebra of this time were made by Kardanov, Viet, Descartes, Newton, and Leibnitz. The present work is devoted to the analysis of their creativity.

6. Dobrovolsky V. A. Vasily Petrovich Ermakov. Volume 5 l., circulation 15,000 copies, price 35 kopecks.
The scientific, pedagogical and social activities of V. P. Ermakov (1845-1922) left a noticeable mark on the history of mathematics and its teaching. He was not only a major scientist, but also the author of many chemical works on the methods of mathematics, the organizer and publisher of the Journal of Elementary Mathematics. This book tells about the life of the scientist and his contribution to the development of the departments of mathematics.

7. Guter R. S., Polunov Y. L. John Nener (1550-1617). Volume 12 l., circulation 15,000 copies, price 90 kopecks.
The book contains a biography and analysis of the work of John Nehner, one of the greatest mathematicians of the 16th-17th centuries, the inventor of logarithms.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"

8. Fridman L. M., Turetsky E. N., Stetsenko V. Ya. How to learn to solve problems. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
In this book, the authors try to teach the reader how to solve not some specific problems, but problems in general. They give ways to solve the most common school mathematical problems and some problems of increased difficulty.

Publishing house "Mir"

9. FriedE. An elementary introduction to abstract algebra. Translation from Hungarian. Volume 20 l., circulation 75,000 copies, price 1 rub. 40 k.
The book by a major mathematician is dedicated to one of the most important and developing branches of modern mathematics - abstract algebra. Presented in simple language, it allows you to master the basic concepts of modern algebra.
