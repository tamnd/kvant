⟦folio 30⟧

Quests

1. In the examples given on figure, all ten digits are used. In the first example, the letter 0 is the number zero, the letter P is equal to the letter I, and the letter B is the letter Z, the remaining letters have independent digital values. In the second example there are no letters that have the same meaning, it is clear that H$^E$=C, and E is an odd number.

Determine the meanings of all letters in each example.

2. The boy bought postage stamps for rubles, and he bought two kopeck stamps 10 times less than one-kopeck stamps, while the rest of the stamps were five-kopeck stamps. How many stamps of each denomination did the boy buy?

3. On the diameter of the circle with the center at point O there are centers A and B of two more circles, touching the original circle and each other (see figure). Another circle, the center of which is denoted by C, touches all three distances equal to the circles, as in figure. Prove that the perimeters of triangles AOC and BOC are equal to the length of the diameter of the first circle.

4. 6th grade student Petya Ivanov came up with two new theorems:
a) if a natural number is divisible by 27, then the sum of its digits is divisible by 27;
b) if the sum of the digits of a natural number is divisible by 27, then the number itself is divisible by 27.
Will Petya be able to prove these theorems?
