⟦folio 52⟧

New paintings for the kaleidoscope

In «Kvant» No. 11 for 1978, a short note was published on dividing a regular hexagon into nine identical parts. Some broken was placed on the cover.

Having looked closely at these developments, our reader E. Kogan from Moscow noticed that in all cases the diagonals of the hexagon passing through its center divide each figure of the development into two identical parts (see figure). Thus, in each of the six regular triangles there are three congested parts, and when any of them is rotated about the center by 120°, the partition turns into itself. This remark allows us to construct partitions of a triangle and obtain new partitions of a hexagon into 18, and therefore into 9 identical parts. E. Kogan added some of the resulting partitions to the editor. What partitions can you suggest?
