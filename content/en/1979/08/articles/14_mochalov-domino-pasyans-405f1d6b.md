Null Monument

“From points A and B, the distance between which is 120 km, I will simultaneously train two hair cyclists leaving each other.” Who is not familiar with traditional school problems “on composing equations” that begin with a similar phrase? Everything is clear here: two points are two points on the depicted highway line, and the length of the line section between the points is the distance between the points.

But let's ask ourselves: what does one actually mean by the words “highway distance between cities”? After all, a real city is far from a point... To change the distance from a city, it is very necessary not only to have a unit of length, but also to establish a “reference point”, the conditions from which place the distance will be measured. In the USSR, in large cities, the point from which (or to which) the distance changes is taken to be the post office. For example, all distances from Moscow on highways are calculated from the Moscow Post Office building (Kirova St., 26). Therefore, the “distance from Moscow to Leningrad” is the distance between the Moscow and Leningrad post offices.

But in the Hungarian People's Republic, all distances are measured from a special stone sculpture installed in the center of Budalest, not far from the beautiful Chain Place. The number “0” and the letters “km” on the pedestal will indicate the beginning of all roads in Hungary. This “Zero,” as the Budapests call it, is one of the attractions of the capital of Hungary; you can see it in the photo, on the second page of the cover.

Where is the error?

Theorem. If two planes are symmetric about the origin O, then they both pass through the point O.

《Proof》 Let the planes α, β be symmetric about the origin O. If the equation of the plane α is ax+by+cz+d=0, (1) then the equation of the plane β is ax-by-cz+d=0. (2) Adding (1), (2), we get d=0. This means that (0; 0; 0) is a solution to general equations, that is, point O belongs to the general plane. Where is the error?

V. Kolmykov
