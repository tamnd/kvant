Total ray eclipse September 6, 1979

All phases of this eclipse will be visible in the extreme eastern and northeastern regions of the Soviet Union, located east of a line running roughly from Khabarovsk and Nikolaevsk-on-Amur to Okhotsk and the East Siberian Sea. To the west of this line, the Moon will rise in a partial eclipse, and to the west of the line running from Erfei Pavlovich through Aldan, Yakutsk, Sangar and Verkhoyansk to the New Siberian Islands - already in the total eclipse phase. To the west of Ulan-Ude, Barguzin, Vitim and Sukhana, the total eclipse cannot be seen, since the Moon will rise there after it ends.

During the eclipse, the Moon will be in the constellation Aquarius and will pass through the southern zone of the earth's shadow (see figure; the letter $N$ indicates the direction to the north pole of the world, that is, approximately to the Pslar star). Nan major phase of total eclipse $\Phi_m=1,10$.

Below are the moments of the eclipse according to Moscow times ($T_M$):

the beginning of a partial eclipse $12^m$ and the beginning of a total eclipse $13^m$ and the greatest phase $13^m$ and the end of a total eclipse $14^m$ and the end of a partial eclipse $15^m$

To get the same moments in local time, you need to add the difference in integers between local and Moscow time to the indicated values ​​$T_M$.
