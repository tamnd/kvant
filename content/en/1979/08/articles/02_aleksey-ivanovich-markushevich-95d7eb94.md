Alexey Ivanovich Markushevich

On June 4, 1979, at the 72nd year of his life, a member of the editorial collection of the magazine «Kvant», a famous Soviet mathematician and teacher, a prominent organizer of public education in our country, a member of the CPSU, academician of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR, professor at Moscow University, Alexei Ivanovich Markushevich, died.

A. I. Markushevich owns a large number of articles, monographs and textbooks on the theory of functions of a complex variable, on the history and methodology of mathematics. A. I. Markushevich devoted a lot of attention to the popularization of science. He is the author of a number of books addressed to schoolchildren: “Areas and Logarithms”, “Complex Numbers and Conformal Mappings”, “Recurrent Sequences”, “Series”, etc. With his active participation, such well-known publications as “Children’s Encyclopedia”, “Encyclopedia of Elementary Mathematics”, “History of Russian Mathematics” were prepared and published. Alexey Ivanovich played a major role in the organization of the popular scientific and mathematical journal «Kvant» and, as a member of the editorial board since the founding of the journal, constantly provided significant assistance in its publication. A. I. Markushevich made a noticeable contribution to the development of the Soviet school and pedagogical sciences, working for many years as the first deputy minister of education of the RSFSR, vice-president of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the RSFSR and the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences of the USSR.
