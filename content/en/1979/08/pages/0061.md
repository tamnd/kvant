The oldest technical unit in the country

Moscow Order of Lenin Institute of Engineers of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartoffini in the training of highly qualified specialists for the national economy, a significant contribution to the development of science and in connection with the 200th anniversary of its founding directed by Lenin.

The Moscow Order of Lenin Institute of Geodesy, Aerial Photography and Cartoffini Engineers traces its history back to the Konstantinovsky Surveying School, opened on May 14 (27), 1779. One of the grounds for the creation of such an educational institution was the signing by Catherine 11 in 1765 of the Manifesto on the General International Land of the Russian Empire. In 1766, the “Instruction of the Land Permanent to the General of the Entire Empire of Demarcated Lands” was published. In the specified instructions, in the part concerning filming, it was said: “Removing arbitrary images using the most reliable instrument - an astrolabe with accessories, which, having received, inspect with an attachment in its serviceability and maintain with extreme care so that they do not deteriorate.”

To organize and carry out work on the General International International Research of the Senate, there was a Mezhevaya Expedition as the Department of Education, in Moscow there was a Mezhevaya Office, in the provinces there were Mezhevye Offices; The title of landowners was established.

Naturally, the question arose about training people who mastered the art of measurements on Earth - surveyors (in modern terminology - geodesists).

By the decree of the Governing Senate of April 23, 1779, it was proposed to begin training landowners and students in the land-digging business at the Mekzhevoy Chancellery. In a report to the Senate on the Mekzhevoy expedition on May 16, 1779, the Mekzhevoy Chancellery added: “... the school... was opened today, May 14th..., the Blue Digger School is named Konstantinovsky.”

The co-founder of the school and its director was the first member (director) of the Mekzhevoy Chancellery, Chief Prosecutor of the Senate, Privy Councilor Sergei Rozhnov.

In the first year, 75 people studied at the school.
