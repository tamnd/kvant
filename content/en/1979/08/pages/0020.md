⟦folio 18⟧

R. Vinokur

Brownie, sorcerer
and... resonator
Helmholtz

In A. Vyorkov’s story “Wasteland”*) you can read about how a certain Ivan Pavlovich, who wanted to get rich, hired an artel of masons to build a “profitable” house and cruelly deceived them by paying only part of the wages. However, the “profitable” house did not bring the cobuster the desired profits - gradually all the tenants bought it. They were directed by a tanish brownie who settled in the house. The police did not help Ivan Pavlovich get rid of the “evil spirits.”

*) A. Vyurkov. Stories about old Moscow. (M., “Moscow Worker”, 1958.)

“... Two policemen were put in the apartment of the merchant who had left for the night. They sat down in the kitchen on empty boxes, brought out a bottle of water and sausage “for courage,” and began to wait for the “brownie.” “If something happens...” the senior officer instructed the junior, “you grab him, and I’ll twist his arms.”

“Ambush” drank and listened. The apartment was quiet. The wind was humming outside, a dog was howling, the gate was slamming, and suddenly... in the empty rooms someone sighed and howled. The policemen looked at each other and, without chewing the sausage, jumped up and flew out into the street like feathers.

“That’s right...”, the elder, trembling with fear, reported to the officer on duty.
- I saw it with my own eyes, your honor!... His head is in! Tail and horns - wow!
After this incident, several more baitfish left the house...”

The interest payment deadline came, there was no money. Ivan Pavlovich was arrested. And only many years later, a construction contractor, also once deceived by Ivan Pavlovich, revealed a secret to him:

“It was you, brother, Ivan Pavlovich, that the masons failed. They put empty bottles in your wall, and the wind blew through them. Go look for them now. We can’t think of where to break down a wall...”
