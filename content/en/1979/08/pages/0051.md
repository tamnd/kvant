⟦folio 49⟧

Quests

1. The difference of two integers that are perfect squares ends in 2. What digits do the minuend and subtrahend end in if the last digit of the minuend is greater than the last digit of the subtrahend?

2. Find all two-digit numbers $ab$ that have the property that the three-digit number $a0b$ is a multiple of the original number.

3. In a football tournament, each pair of teams must meet each other once. On Wednesday, after the next round, Borya calculated that seven teams still had an even number of matches! Is this possible? If so, is it possible to determine how long the team participated in the horizontal bar? (The tournament schedule, of course, is designed in such a way that no more than one team is free in each round.)

4. Kolya went for mushrooms somewhere between 6:00 and 9:00 in the morning, at the moment when the hands of his watch were aligned. He returned home between two and three o'clock in the afternoon; at the same time, the hands of his watch were directed in exactly opposite directions. How long did Kolya's walk last?

5. In how many ways can nine congestion squares: three red, three white and three blue, be arranged in the form of a square $3 \times 3$ so that in each row and in each column there are squares of all colors?
