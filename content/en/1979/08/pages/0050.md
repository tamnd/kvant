⟦folio 48⟧

F536. The rigid straight-edge structure AOB (AOB=90°) is repaired with a workpiece of the vertical axis OO′, so that AOO′=α (Fig. 14). Using rings that can slide freely on the sides AO and OB, we put a light rod KE of length 2a onto the structure. A small massive ball is attached to the middle of the rod. At what angular speed of treatment of the structure is the rod in a transmetro-distal position?

When the rod KE is in a horizontal position, the ball is healed around the axis OO′ along a circle of radius r=a sin (π/2−2α)=a cos 2α (see Fig. 14). The centripetal acceleration ω²r (ω is the angular velocity of the structure) is imparted by the ball to the reaction force R of the rod (its horizontal projection). Let's write down the equation of motion of the ball in projections on the X and Y axes (Fig. 15):

$$R_x = m\omega^2r = m\omega^2a\cos 2\alpha,$$
$$R_y = mg$$

($R_x, R_y, mg$ are the modules of the projections of the vectors $\vec{R}$ and $\vec{mg}$).

Now let's write down the equation of motion of the KE structure in projections on the X and Y axes (see Fig. 15):

$$N_1\cos \alpha - R_x' - N_2\sin \alpha = 0,$$
$$N_1\sin \alpha - R_y' + N_2\cos \alpha = 0$$

($\vec{R}' = -\vec{R}$ - ball reaction force acting on the rod; $\vec{N}_1, \vec{N}_2$ - reaction forces of the “shoulders” OA and OB of the structure acting on the rod; $R_x', R_y', N_1\cos \alpha, N_2\sin \alpha$, etc. - modules correspond to the projection; $R_x' = R_x, R_y' = R_y$).

The condition for the rod to occupy a horizontal position is that the sum of the moments of forces acting on the rod relative to the horizontal axis perpendicular to the plane of the structure is equal to zero. Let's write down the equation of moments about point C:

$$|\vec{N}_1|a\sin \alpha - |\vec{N}_2|a\cos \alpha = 0.$$

From equation (1) - (3) we find:

$$\omega = \sqrt{g/(a\sin 2\alpha)}$$
