In addition to complete HOYs, specialized associations of students successfully operate in a number of resource regions, such as the “Small Forest Academy of Sciences” in Karalia, scientific, technical and agricultural societies in the Krasnodar region, the society of young astronomers in Azerbaijan, young chemists and mechanics in Kazan, and the Rostov Regional Society of Young Local Historians.

For more than ten years he has been working in secondary school No. 5 in Uglit, Yaroslavl region, for young astronomers. Here is 技术和 theoretical experimental and practical work. The main areas of activity are: research of mass events aimed at promoting astronomical and athematic knowledge among students, parents and the population. The children built and equipped the school observatory, film and photography studio with their own hands, designed and manufactured the necessary radio equipment.

The research work focuses on the study of solar activity and its relationship with phenomena on Earth. In classes, young astronomers study the theoretical aspects of the problem, observe the dynamics of sunspots, photograph and film them, and record all the meteorological changes that occur on Earth. They developed a method for determining the depth of sunspots, covered a huge amount of observable material, and collected special literature on this issue. The guys organize celebrations of Cosmonautics Day, conduct excursions, conversations at the observatory, atest evenings, quizzes, and give lectures for school students. Krujuk school is a regular participant in the All-Union Meetings of Young Astronomers, he is awarded a bronze medal and a diploma from VDNKh.

Junior schools in the subjects of natural sciences and humanities are becoming more and more widespread. These are voluntary, mass associations of students in grades 8-10 of general education schools, created at higher educational institutions and scientific institutions with the aim of deepening knowledge, satisfying and developing the interests of schoolchildren in determining the branch of science, expanding scientific horizons and professional guidance. Teaching is carried out by scientists, research assistants, teachers, graduate students and students on a voluntary basis. Very popular environments for schoolchildren, correspondence, evening, summer school for young mathematicians, physicists, chemists, historians; teachers, doctors. Interesting experience in this work has been accumulated in Belarus, the Tatar Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, the Gorky Region, the Khabarovsk Territory, and Moscow.

An analysis of the activities of scientific societies of students of all types shows that they actively contribute to improving the quality of knowledge of schoolchildren, expanding their scientific and technical horizons, mastering the skills of independent creative work, and the perception of readiness for a creative choice of a future profession and creative work.

But so far, unfortunately, scientific societies have not been created everywhere. There are no resource, regional, city HOYs in a number of co-resource, some regional centers of the RSFSR, although there is every opportunity for their work. Young scientists and specialists from research institutes are still not sufficiently involved in this work. We are concerned that in a number of associations all the activities of children are aimed at preparing them for entering university. There is no targeted development of a program for HOY yet. Often teachers take a passive position in relation to HOY, do not use it as a good aid in their work to improve the quality of knowledge of scientists, and promote accessible science and technology.

The increase in the efficiency of HOY activities was facilitated by the publication on the pages of relevant journals, such as «Kvant», lists of work topics, assignments for scientists and specialists, institutions and organizations for young researchers.

The task and duty, first of all, of young scientists, teachers, and students is to transform each scientific society of students into a real school of creativity, perception of the future of Soviet science and technology.

Yu. Ivanov,
