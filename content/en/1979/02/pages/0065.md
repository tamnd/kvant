1. Let us denote the required amount of fresh water by $x$ kg; then from the problem statement:

$$30 \cdot \frac{5}{100\%} \cdot (x + 30)^{-1} \cdot 100\% = p\%$$

solving this equation, we find the answer: if $p \in ]0; 5]$, then we need to add

$$30 \left( \frac{5}{p} - 1 \right) \text{ кг}$$

fresh water. In the case when $p \notin ]0; 5]$, the problem has no solution.

2. Since the lateral edges of the prim $SABCD$ (see figure) make equal angles with the plane of the base, then the orthogonal projection $O_1$ of the vertex of the prim $S$ onto the plane of the base ($ABCD$) is the center of the circle circumscribed about the rectangle $ASO_1$.

Triangle $ASC$ is equilateral since $\widehat{SAC} = \widehat{SCA}$. The base side $[AC]$ of this triangle is at the same time the diagonal rectangle $ABCD$, therefore

$$|AC| = \sqrt{|AB|^2 + |BC|^2} = 5. \tag{1}$$

Since the center of the sphere circumscribed about the primus belongs to the plane ($ASC$), then the radius of the circle described about the triangle $ASC$ is equal to $R = 6,5$.

From the right triangle $AOO_1$ we have

$$\left|SO_1\right| - R^2 = R^2 - \left| \frac{AC}{2} \right|^2, \tag{2}$$

where

$$|SO_1| = R \pm \sqrt{R^2 - \left| \frac{AC}{2} \right|^2} = 6,5 \pm 6. \tag{3}$$

a) The center of the circumscribed sphere $O$ belongs to the prime $SABC$. In this case, in formula (3) you should select the plus sign. The length of the side edge is determined from the right triangle $ASO_1$:

$$|AS| = \sqrt{|SO_1|^2 + R^2} = \sqrt{212,5}.$$

From the triangle $BSC$, using the cosine theorem, we find

$$\cos(BSC) = \frac{|BS|^2 + |CS|^2 - |BC|^2}{2|BS| \cdot |CS|} = \frac{309}{325},$$

$$\widehat{BSC} = \arccos \frac{309}{325}.$$

6) The center of the circumscribed sphere of the point $O$ does not belong to the prime $SABC$. In formula (3) you should select a minus sign. Calculations similar to the previous case give

$$\widehat{BSC} = \pi - \arccos \left( -\frac{3}{13} \right) = \arccos \frac{3}{13}.$$

Answer. $\left\{ \arccos \frac{309}{325}; \arccos \left( \frac{3}{13} \right) \right\}$.

3. Let us determine the value of the unknown parameter $a$, included in the equation of the parabola, from the condition

$4x^2 + ax + 2)' |x = -5| = tg(\pi - \arctg 20)$.

Perform the actions indicated on the left side of the equality and simplify the right side of the reduction formulas, we obtain $40 - a = 20$, whence $a = 20$.

Let us determine the abscissas of the intersection points of the parabola and the straight line from the equation

$$4x^2 + 20x + 2 = -8x - 46.$$

We get

$$x_1 = -4, x_2 = -3.$$

The area of ​​the figure under consideration is determined by the integral

$$\int_{-4}^{3} \left[ -8x - 46 - (4x^2 + 20x + 2) \right] dx = -4 \int_{-4}^{3} (x^2 + 7x + 12) dx = 2/3.$$

Answer. $\frac{2}{3}$ sq. units
