An indispensable condition for the viability of societies is their connection with production, exchanges, scientific institutions, boards and councils of VOIR, HTO, and the Knowledge Society. It is very important and valuable that the activities of most non-state educational institutions are in a socially useful direction.

A few years ago, a scientific society of students was created in Sverdlovsk, which today has grown into the field of non-governmental educational institutions or, as the guys call it, the “Small Academy of Sciences”. Here, about 40 thousand schoolchildren from the region are studying in 11 departments - physics, chemistry, mathematics, social sciences and others. Their work is supervised by 3 thousand young scientists from the Ural Scientific Center, students and teachers of higher education institutions, school teachers, specialists from a number of industrial enterprises, collective farms and state farms. The heads dedicate a lot of strength and energy to working with schoolchildren. laboratories of the Institute "UralpromstroyNIProekt", transferred to the Council of Curators of the society, Doctor of Technical Sciences S. A. Timashev, young scientists, employees of the institutes of the Ural Scientific Center of the USSR Academy of Sciences I. V. Kochergina, Yu. V. Boltytsev, M. I. Gusev. A clear structure, interesting traditions, constant attention from interested organizations, a creative, emotional atmosphere allows us to achieve a high level of activity in the society.

The youth scientific society of the Moscow City Palace of Pioneers and Schoolchildren is worth its work. About 500 students attend it on a regular basis. The work is headed by the Society's Council, consisting of schoolchildren, and directed by the Council of Curators-Scientists. During the academic year, public organizations are held; the creative, emotional atmosphere allows us to achieve a high level of community activity.

In Uzbekistan, the scientific society of students from secondary school No. 110 in Tashkent is well known. The goals and objectives of the society are determined by a special resolution of the pedagogical council and the Komsomol school committee. Scientific and pedagogical leadership is carried out jointly by school and scientists of the Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan, teachers and students of a number of Tashkent universities. Agreements have been concluded between the school and the schools on the other side.

Every year in September and May, conferences are held at which plans and programs are approved, reports are reviewed, and commemorative certificates “Best Mathematician School”, “Best Physicist School”, and awards from sponsoring organizations are awarded. Between conferences, the leadership of the society is carried out by the Children's Council. The society unites sections of mathematics, physics, chemistry, history, literature, and law. The sections will consist of clubs for students in grades 6-7, problem groups for high school students.

For example, the mathematics section includes clubs “Count, guess, guess” (for 6th grade students), “Entertaining Mathematics” (for seventh and upper grade students), a permanent seminar “Search and Solutions, Research and Conclusions” and a workshop on future research (for students in grades 8-10). Each section and all group problems have scientific advisors. Members of the society study theoretical issues, carry out experimental work in the laboratories of universities and research institutes, classrooms in schools, and produce various aids, equipment, instruments for schools and the national economy.
