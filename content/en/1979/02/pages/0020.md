⟦folio 18⟧

The fact of the existence of inertial reporting systems in the world does not seem obvious at all, it goes without saying. The ancient thinker (Aristotle) ​​found it difficult to understand why bodies continue to move even after the influence of other bodies on them has ceased. Why, for example, does the arrow continue its flight after the bow string has stopped pushing it?

Gallia was the first to notice the fallacy of the belief that other bodies must maintain the motion (speed) of a given body. He asked the question: what property of the movement of a body depends on the influence of other bodies on it? No amount of speculative reasoning can answer this question. Here you need to turn directly to nature, that is, to experience.

The answer that nature gave was unexpected and is as follows: there are inertial reporting systems; in them, nothing is required to maintain the movement of a body at a constant speed - it will happen by itself.

When this answer was received, it was the largest discovery ever made. That is why the law of inertia is one of the most important laws of nature.

Interaction of bodies and acceleration. Weight

Another important fact was established by Newton from observations of the movements of bodies. It consists in the fact that when a body influences other bodies and imparts acceleration to it, this other body “responds” to it in the same way, that is, imparts acceleration to it as well. The influence of one body on another is not one-sided - the bodies interact, imparting acceleration to each other. If, for example, two balls collide (collision is an example of interaction), each of them receives acceleration. What are these accelerations? How are they directed? What are their numerical values?

Experience shows that in all cases the accelerations of interacting bodies are opposite in direction.

If, for example, a moving ball collides with the same stationary ball so that the speed of the ball is directed along the line connecting the centers of the balls (Fig. 1), then the speed of the first of them will decrease, and the speed of the second will increase. And this means that the accelerations of the balls are opposite in direction.

As for the absolute values ​​(modules) of accelerations, they can be anything. It depends on how the bodies moved before the interaction (from the initial conditions), on the nature of the interaction, on which bodies interact. But for a given pair of interacting bodies, the acceleration ratio is always the same. They do not depend either on the nature of the interaction or on the initial conditions. This means that it depends on some special property hidden in the interacting bodies themselves. This property, inherent in every body, is expressed by the amount of body mass.

Newton discovered this property and introduced into science the quantities that quantitatively characterize it - mass. What is this property?
