⟦folio 56⟧

Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology

Below are published samples of options for entrance written exams in mathematics and physics in 1978. Five hours are allotted for written work in both mathematics and physics.

Mathematica
Option 1
1. Solve the equation
$$3\cos^2x \cdot (4 \cdot 3\sin^2x - 9) = 1$$
2. Solve inequality
$$\sqrt{\log_3(9x + 18)} \leq \log_3(x + 2)$$
3. In triangle $ABC$ angle $B$ is right, medians $AD$ and $BE$ are mutually perpendicular. Determine the angle $C$.
4. Solve system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 6\cos x + 4\cos y = 5, \\ 3\sin x + 2\sin y = 0. \end{cases}$$
5. Along the river there are points $A, B, C$ (between $A$ and $C$). The speed of the river flow in section $AB$ is twice as high as in section $BC$, and is constant in each section. The boat made three trips: the first from $A$ to $C$, the second from $C$ to $A$ and the third from $A$ to $B$. In the first two flights, its speed relative to the water was the same, and in the third flight it was $1\frac{1}{4}$ times greater than in the first two. The first flight lasted 7 hours, the third - 2 hours. If the current speed in section $AB$ was the same as in section $BC$, then the boat would have made its first voyage in 6 hours (at the same speed relative to the water). How long is the second flight? (Flight is movement in one direction from the starting point to the final point.)
6. The vertex $A$ of the regular base $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ coincides with the vertex of the cone, the vertices $B$ and $C$ lie on the lateral surface of this cone, and the vertices $B_1$ and $C_1$ lie on the circumference of its base. Find the ratio of the volumes of the cone and the base if $|AB_1|:|AB|=5$.

Option 2
1. Solve inequality
$$\frac{3x^2+2}{x} - 27 > 0$$

2. Check which of the numbers $\arccos \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} + \frac{\pi n}{2}$, where $n=0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots$, is a solution to the equation
$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sin x} + \frac{1}{\cos 2x} = 6$$
3. In the expansion of the binomial $(1+x)^n$ in increasing order, the exponent $x$, the third term is four times larger than the fifth, and the ratio of the fourth term to the sixth is $\frac{40}{3}$. Find $n$ and $x$.
4. A trapezoid $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. The dynamir drawn through the vertex $A$ is perpendicular to the side $CD$. A perpendicular to the base $AD$ is drawn through the vertex $C$, intersecting the segment $AD$ at the point $M$, and the circle at the point $N$ so that $|CM|:|MN| = \frac{5}{2}$. Find the angle at the base of the trapezoid.
5. The vertices $B, C, D$ of the parallelogram $ABCD$ has a corresponding coordinate $(-3; 2), (2; 3), (3; -4)$ (BD is the diagonal). Find:
1) all values ​​of $a$ for which the coordinates of vertex $A$ are a solution to the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} 2x - y - 2a \leq 0, \\ 2x + 6y + 5a \leq 0; \end{cases}$$
2) all values ​​of $a$ for which the coordinates of at least one point of the segment $BD$ are a solution to this system.
6. The length of the cube edge $ABCD_1B_1C_1D_1$ is equal to $a$. Point $P$ is the middle of edge $CC_1$, point $Q$ is the center of edge $AA_1B_1B$. The segment $MN$ with ends on the lines $AD$ and $A_1B_1$ intersects the line $PQ$ and is perpendicular to it. Find the length of this segment.

Physics
Option 1
1. The ball hangs on a thread, resting on the wall, as shown in figure 1. The center of the ball $C$ lies on the same vertical with the suspension point $O$, the thread forms an angle $\alpha$ with the vertical, and the radius drawn to the thread attachment point $A$ makes an angle $\beta$. At what values ​​of the coefficient of friction between the ball and the wall is such equilibrium possible? Consider that $\alpha + \beta = \pi/2$.
2. In a horizontal puddle tube sealed at one end there is air with a relative humidity of $r_0=0,8$, separated from the atmosphere by a drop of mercury $l=7,6$ cm long. What will the relative humidity of the air be $r$ if the tube is placed vertically, open end down? The temperature is maintained constant, external atmospheric pressure $p=760$ mmHg. Art. Mercury does not pour out of the tube when it is turned over.
3. To change the voltage $E_3$ of the Earth's own electric field at its surface, two metal dusts were used.
