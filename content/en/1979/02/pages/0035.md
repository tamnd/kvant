F558. A small heavy ball flies through a hole inside a smooth sphere of the same mass, passing at a distance $R/2$ from the center of the sphere ($R$ is the radius of the sphere). After the ball enters, the responsible automatic system closes. Assuming the collisions between the ball and the sphere to be absolutely elastic, find the trajectories of the ball and the center of the sphere in the reporting system in which the sphere was initially dug. Determine the parameters of these trajectories and mark on them the points at which collisions will occur.
