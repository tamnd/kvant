Yarning
1. The number $x=1$ is not the root of the equation $x^2 - x = 2x - \frac{x^2}{2}$, but is the root of an equation obtained by equating the derivatives of its left and right sides. Explain the geometric meaning of this fact.
2. Construct graphs of some functions $f$ and $f'$ in the interval $]a; b[$ so that for all $x$ from this interval the following conditions are satisfied:
   a) $f(x) > \varphi(x)$ and $f'(x) = \varphi'(x)$;
   b) $f(x) > \varphi(x)$ and $f'(x) < \varphi'(x)$.
3. At what value of $a$ do curves $y = x^3 - x + 1$ and $y = 3x^2 - 4x + a$ touch each other?
4. A parabola congruent to a fixed parabola $y = x^2$ moves in such a way that the axis $Ox$ always remains its axis of symmetry. At what points will the moving parabola touch the stationary one and where will its vertex be?
5. A parabola (with vertex at point $A(-2, 0)$), whose symmetry axis coincides with the axis $Ox$, is tangent to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. Find the equation of the parabola.
6. In the plane $xOy$, find the equation of a curve from any point of which the parabola $y = x^2$ is visible at an angle of $45^\circ$.
7. The rocket moves rectilinearly according to the law $s = v_0t + \frac{at^2}{2}$. After time $t_1$ after the start of movement, some object is separated from it, which continues to move by inertia. At what point in time and what constant speed must be given to the indicated object in order for it to continue moving uniformly and catch up with the package at moment $t_2$, while having the same speed as it? Give a geometric interpretation of the problem.
8. A space rocket is launched in a straight line from a certain point in space, at which the gravity of other bodies can be neglected. Law of rocket motion $s = \frac{at^2}{2}, t \geq 0$. After how much time, counting from the beginning of the movement, must the movements be turned off so that the package, which continues to move by inertia with the accumulated speed, ends up at the moment $t_1$ at a distance $s_1$ from the first starting point? What is the geometric model of this problem?
