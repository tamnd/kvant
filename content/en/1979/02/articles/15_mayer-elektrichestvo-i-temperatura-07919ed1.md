What are conductors of electricity?
What conductors do you know? What is conductor resistance? Why does a conductor have resistance? How does resistance depend on the size of the conductor and the materials from which it is made?

You can probably answer these questions right away. And now we offer you another question: what will happen to the resistance when external conditions change - temperature and pressure? Take your time with your answer. We advise you to do several experiments first.

1. Take a burnt-out electric lamp (power 100 W, voltage 220 V), battery (type 3336L, 4.5 V), flashlight bulbs (voltage 3.5 V and current 0.26 A), connected wires and matches. Your task is to study the dependence of the resistance of a metal conductor (the spiral of a burnt-out electric lamp) on temperature.

Cut a piece of spiral about 1 cm long; At the end of this piece, insert copper wires so that there is good contact between them and the spiral. Connect a circuit consisting of a coil, a battery and a light bulb connected in series.
