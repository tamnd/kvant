We invite you to a small fur-mat

Small Mechanics and Mathematics (MMMF) is a correspondence mathematical school at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Moscow State University, which is a department of the Weighty Correspondence Mathematical School (VZMSH) *). By studying in the small mechanics and mathematics department, schoolchildren in grades 8-10 will be able to deepen their knowledge of the most important sections of the school curriculum and prepare for admission to the mechanics and mathematics department of Moscow State University or another university. Many of the assignments sent to MMMF students are based on assignments given for the mathematics exams in Mechanics and Mathematics. The development of assignments, as well as methodological instructions, is carried out by the MMMF methodological council, which is led by faculty professors.

Schoolchildren from the following regions are studying at the small mechanical engineering department: Astrakhan, Bryansk, Belgorod, Vladimir, Volgograd, Boronezh, Gorky, Ivanovo, Kalinin, Kaluga, Kostroma, Kuibyshev, Kursk, Lipetsk, Moscow, Orenburg, Oryol, Penza, Perm, Rostov, Ryazan, Saratov, Sverdlovsk, Smolensk, Tambov, Tula, Ulyanovsk, Chelyabinsk, Yaroslavl, Minsk, Brest, Vitebsk, Gomel, Grodno, Mogilev, as well as: Bashkir Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Dagestan Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Kabardino-Balkarian Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Kaltsy Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Mordovia Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, North Ossetian Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Tatar Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Udmurt Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Chechen-Ingush Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Chuvash Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Krasnodar Territory, Stavropol Territory.

Admission to the small fur-mat is carried out through the system of the region. Models can submit an application to the organizing committee of the 极限 with a request for admission to the small fur-mat.

If you are studying in the 7th grade, live in one of the specified areas and would like to study at the Small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, you can decide to enter the introductory work of the High School of Mechanics and Mathematics and send it to the small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, since schoolchildren living in the specified areas can enroll both in accordance with the completed forms of the High School of Mechanics and Mathematics and to the small Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

For Moscow schoolchildren there are Evening Math (grades 7-9) and Sunday Preparatory (grades 10) classes. Evening school is open on all days of the week. To check school hours, call 139-35-29.

*) See «Kvant», 1979, No. 1 and p. 59 of this number.
