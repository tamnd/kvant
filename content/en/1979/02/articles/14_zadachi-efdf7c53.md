⟦folio 45⟧

Kvasht
for younger students

Quests

1. Several experts took on the task of deciphering the number puzzle TAMTAM + DARKNESS = NIGHTMARE, and each of them received the correct answer, different from the answers of the others. Signs proceeded from the fact that each letter means a certain number, and different numbers are designated by different letters. How many knowers could there be?

2. Prove that the sum of any twelve consecutive natural numbers is not divisible by 4.

3. Cut the figure shown at figure into two congruent pieces.

4. There are six glasses on the table. You can turn over any five of them at a time. Is it possible, by repeating this operation several times, to place all six glasses up? Try to generalize this problem to the case of 2n glasses.

5. There are 21 points placed at the vertices of a square grid shaped like a rectangle (see figure). Is it possible to show these points in two colors so that not a single four of the same color points forms a rectangle?
