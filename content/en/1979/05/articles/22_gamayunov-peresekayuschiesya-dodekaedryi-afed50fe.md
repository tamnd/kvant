How to build a house?
(to the second cover page)

Imagine a construction site in the mountains or in space, where it is difficult to place a high crane. How to build a house in such a situation? One of the methods is shown at figure 1. The square panels are connected to each other by a hinge. In addition, they are connected by cables. If you start to “select” the cables using a winch installed in the center of the set (Fig. 2), then a house (Fig. 3) ​​will appear.

Intersecting dodecahedrons
(to the fourth cover page)

In «Kvant» No. 1 on p. 27 was a photo of a polyhedron, which is the union of the heel of cubes inscribed in a dodecahedron. How many different dodecahedra can be described around one cube? It is not difficult to agree that there are two. In this photo you see a polyhedron that is the union of two such dodecahedrons. It is clear that this polyhedron is equally granino-semiregular: for any pair of its faces, one can specify a displacement such that the polyhedron itself will take its previous position, and the first face will move to the second.
