Tasks

M561—M565; F573—F577

M561. Two triangles $A_1B_1C_1$ and $A_2B_2C_2$, the areas of which are $S_1$ and $S_2$, are arranged so that the rays $A_1B_1$ and $A_2B_2$, $B_1C_1$ and $B_2C_2$, $C_1A_1$ and $C_2A_2$ are parallel but oppositely directed. Find the area of ​​a triangle with vertices at the midpoints of segments $A_1A_2$, $B_1B_2$, $C_1C_2$.

L. Kuptsov

M562. On the segment [0; 1] a set $M$ is given, which is the union of several segments, such that the distance between any two points from $M$ is not equal to 0.1. Prove that the sum of the lengths of the segments making up $M$ is less than a) 0.55; b) 0.5.

A. Kach, 10th grade student

M563. The function $f$ is defined on the interval $[a; b]$ for 4 and has a continuous derivative $f'$ on it. Prove that inside the segment $[a; b]$ there is a point $x$ for which

$$f'(x) - (f(x))^2 < 1.$$

S. Fomin

M564. For which points $M$ of side $BC$ of triangle $ABC$ the following statement is true: $\triangle MPQ \cong \triangle ABC$, if points $P$ and $Q$ are:

a) the centers of the circles described respectively around the triangles $ABM$ and $ACM$ (Fig. 1);
b) the points of intersection of their medians;
b) the points of intersection of their heights?

E. Turkevich

M565. Let $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ be distinct positive numbers. Let us denote by $b_k$ the arithmetic mean of all possible products of $k$ given numbers ($k = 1, 2, \dots, n$):

$$b_1 = \frac{a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n}{n},$$

$$b_2 = \frac{a_1a_2 + a_1a_3 + \cdots + a_{n-1}a_n}{n(n-1)},$$

$$b_n = a_1a_2 \dots a_n.$$

⟦folio 23⟧

Prove the inequalities:
a) $b_1 \geqslant \sqrt{b_2}$;
b) $b_k^2 \geqslant b_{k-1}b_{k+1}$ ($k=2, \dots, n-1$);
b) $k^k \sqrt{b_k} \geqslant k^{k+1} \sqrt{b_{k+1}}$ ($k=2, \dots, n-1$).

M. Rosenberg

F573. Estimate how much water can be carried away in a sieve with square cells measuring $2 \times 2$ mm. The diameter of the sieve is 20 cm. The sieve threads are not wetted with water.

F574. Two balls with masses $m_1 = 0,2$ kg and $m_2 = 0,3$ kg fly towards each other with a speed of $v = 20$ m/s each. The graph of the force of interaction of the balls during a collision versus time is shown in figure 2. How much heat was released during the collision of the balls?

I. Slobodechkiy

F575. In the circuit shown in figure 3, all voltmeters and resistors are the same. The emf of the battery is $E = 5$ V, its internal resistance is low. The top voltmeter shows $U = 2$ V. What do the other voltmeters show?

A. Zilberman

F576. Along a straight line $OB$ the plane moves uniformly at a speed $v > c$ (with the speed of sound). The observer watches the flight from point $A$ (Fig. 4). At the moment when the plane is at point $O$, a special device installed on the plane emits two short sound pulses: first a pulse with a small amplitude, then a pulse with a large one. The time interval between pulses is $t$. Under what conditions will the observed be able to register first a pulse with a large amplitude, and then with a small one? The distance $OA$ is $L$, the angle $AOB$ is $\theta$.

B. Bolotovsky

F577. At an outside temperature of $t_0 = -20^\circ C$, a working battery maintains a temperature of $t_1 = 16^\circ C$ in the room. When, in addition to the battery, the power supply with a power of $P_1 = 1$ kW was turned on, the temperature in the room was equal to $t_2 = 22^\circ C$. Determine the thermal power of the battery.

When marking e. Body transfer from one body to another is proportional to the temperature difference between these bodies ($\Delta P = k\Delta t$, $k = \text{const}$).
