Cheap box

Two walls of the box, having the shape of a rectangular paralleliped and a volume of 1 m³, are made of one material, while the others are made of a material that costs 8 times less. At what size $x, y, z$ box is the cost of materials needed for its manufacture minimal?

Solution e. Let the cost of 1 m² of the second material be equal to $p$ rubles. According to the conditions, from the first material, they are adjacent, or they are not. In the first case, the cost of materials is equal to

$$S_1 = 8p(xy + yz) + p(xy + yz + 2xz) = p \cdot xyz \left( \frac{9}{z} + \frac{9}{x} + \frac{2}{y} \right) = p \left( \frac{9}{z} + \frac{9}{x} + \frac{2}{y} \right),$$

in the second -

$$S_2 = 8p2xy + p(2xz + 2yz) = 2pxyz \left( \frac{8}{z} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x} \right) = 2p \left( \frac{8}{z} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x} \right).$$

Let's apply the inequality

$$\frac{a + b + c}{3} \geqslant \sqrt{abc}$$

between the arthmetic mean and the geometric mean (see p. 18) to these expressions. We get

$$S_1 \geqslant 9 \sqrt[3]{6} p \text{ и } S_2 \geqslant 12p.$$

It is easy to see that $9 \sqrt[3]{6} p > 12p$. The cost of $S_2$ will be minimal if and only if the minimum is the sum of $\frac{8}{z} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x}$ with the last condition being satisfied if and only if $\frac{8}{z} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{1}{x}$ with the last condition.

For this, in turn, it is necessary and sufficient that $8/z = 1/y = 1/x$.

Since $xyz = 1$, it follows that $x = y = 0,5; z = 4$.

Thus, the walls of the first material must be non-adjacent and have the shape of a square with sides of 0.5 m, and the distance between them is 4 m.

R. Vinokur
