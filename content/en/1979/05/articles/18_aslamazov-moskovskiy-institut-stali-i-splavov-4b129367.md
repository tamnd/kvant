To the Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys

The Moscow Institute of Steel and Alloys has five faculties: the Faculty of Metallurgy of Ferrous Metals and Alloys, the Faculty of Engineering, the Faculty of Physics and Chemistry, the Faculty of Semiconductor Materials and Instruments, and the Faculty of Metallurgy of Non-Ferrous and Rare Metals and Alloys. These faculties prepare a specialty in the field of metallurgy of ferrous, non-ferrous, rare and radioactive metals, high-temperature materials, diamonds and superhard alloys, physics of metals, physical and chemical research methods, semiconductor technology, economics and cybernetics of metallurgical processes.
