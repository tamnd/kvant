Mathematics

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics
Option I
1. Difference
$$\sqrt{|40\sqrt{2}-57|} - \sqrt{40\sqrt{2}+57}$$
is an integer. Find this integer.
2. Find all solutions to the equation
$$\int_0^\alpha \cos(x + \alpha^2) dx = \sin \alpha,$$
belonging to the segment [2; 3].
3. In an acute triangle ABC, from vertices A and C the altitudes AP and CQ are dropped to the sides BC and AB. It is known that the area of ​​triangle ABC is 18, the area of ​​triangle BPQ is 2, and the length of segment PQ is $2\sqrt{2}$. Calculate the radius of the circle circumscribed by triangle ABC.
4. Find all values ​​of the parameter a for which the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} x^2 + 2xy - 7y^2 \geq \frac{1-a}{a+1}, \\ 3x^2 + 10xy - 5y^2 \leq -2, \end{cases}$$
has a solution.
5. The volume of the tetrahedron ABCD is equal to 5. A plane is drawn through the midpoints of the edges AD and BC, intersecting the edge CD at point M. In this case, the ratio of the length of the segment DM to the length of the segment MC is $\frac{2}{3}$. Calculate the cross-sectional area of ​​the tetrahedron by the indicated plane if the distance from it to the vertex a is 1.

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics
Option 2
1. Solve inequality
$$(x-1)\sqrt{x^2-x-2} \geq 0.$$
2. Find the minimum points of a function
$$\sqrt{3}\cos \frac{x}{2} + \sin \frac{x}{2} - \frac{x-3}{2}.$$
3. Find the equation of a line passing through a point with coordinates $\left(\frac{1}{2}; 2\right)$, tangent to the graph of the function $y = -\frac{x^2}{2} + 2$ and intersecting at two different points on the graph of the function $y = \sqrt{4-x^2}$.
4. The set A consists of different natural numbers. The number of numbers in A is greater than seven. The least common multiple of numbers in A is 210. For any two numbers in A, their most common divisor is greater than one. The product of all numbers in A is divisible by 1920 and is not the square of any integer. Find the numbers that make up A.
5. The basis of the parameter ABCEH is the convex quadrilateral ABCE, which is divided by the diagonal BE into two equal-sized triangles. The length of edge AB is equal to one, the lengths of edges BC and CE are equal. The sum of the lengths of the edges AH and EH is $\sqrt{2}$. The volume of the parameter is $\frac{1}{6}$. Find the radius of the ball that has the most common radius among all the balls that fit into the parameter ABCEH.
3. oral exam tasks
1. Find the min and max functions $f(x) = a\sin x + b\cos x$.
2. Solve the equation in natural numbers
$$A_x^2 + 3 = C_x^2 + 20.$$
3. Prove that the area of ​​a triangle is less than one if the lengths of all bisectors are less than one.
4. Prove the inequalities $3 < \pi < 4$.
5. Graph the function
$$y = \log_2 |\sin x| \frac{1}{2}.$$
6. Compute $\log_2 40$ if it is known that $\lg 15 = a$, $\log_{20} 50 = b$.
