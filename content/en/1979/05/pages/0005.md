Means; check that the function corresponds to this

$$y = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{6},$$

$$y = 1 - x + x^2 - x^3 \text{ и } y = x - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{3}.$$

Let us now turn to figure 4. It shows the graph of the function $y = \sin x$ and the graphs of polynomials that have a tangency of the first, third and fifth orders with the graph of the sine at the point $x = 0$ (check that these are the polynomials $y = x, y = x - \frac{x^3}{6}$ and $y = x - \frac{x^3}{6} + \frac{x^5}{120}$, respectively).

As can be seen from these figure, the higher the order of tangency, the more closely the graph of the polynomial is adjacent to the graph of the corresponding function in the vicinity of the point $x = 0$.
