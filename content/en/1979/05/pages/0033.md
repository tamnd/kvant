⟦folio 31⟧

Exercise

1. figure depicts a forest (made of matches) and Little Red Riding Hood, a Hindu to Grandmother. Rearrange two matches so that Little Red Riding Hood comes back.

2. A three-digit number is given. Let's find the product of its digits. Then we find the product of the digits of the result. The original number and the two resulting products can be depicted respectively as shown in figure (identical figures replace identical digits). What was the original number? How many solutions does the problem have?

3. Fill the free cells of the hexagon with natural numbers so that they contain all the numbers from 1 to 19, and in all five rows of each of the three directions (vertical and two oblique) the sum of the numbers in the same row would be the same.

4. Students Vadim and Kolya play chess. Nadya, Kolya’s younger sister, passes by. When asked how old Nadya was, Kolya answered: “If you multiply the number of today’s day of the week by the number of Nadya’s birthday and by the number of the place in which she was born, you will get the year of Nadya’s birth.” On what day of the week did Vadim and Kolya play chess?

5. Professor Tester conducts a series of tests, based on which he gives the subject an average score. After answering the last test, John realized that if he had received 97 points on this last test, then his average score would be 90. On the other hand, if he had only received 73 points on the last test, then his average score would be 87. How many tests are there in Professor Tester's series?
