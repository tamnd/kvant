Assignment - demonstrations

1. Under the influence of the field of the internal capacitor, charges are generated on the inductance ball. They are distributed so that the charges that are the same to the nearest plate of the capacitor are located further away from it, and the charges that are different are located closer. This results in a force that attracts the ball to one of the plates. When a ball touches a plate, a charge identical to the charge of the plate is transferred to it, repulsion from this plate and attraction to the other occurs. Then everything is repeated. The ball on itself transfers charges from one capacitor plate to another.

2. When the iron core is inserted, the inductance of the coil and, consequently, its inductive resistance increases. The glow of the light bulb will increase with increasing inductance of the first coil if this coil is connected in the circuit parallel to the light bulb. Similarly, the glow of a light bulb will decrease as the inductance of the second coil increases if the coil is connected in series with the light bulb. Thus, we arrive at the diagram shown in figure 16.

3. In a solid ring of inductance, the ring eddy current is of such a direction that its interaction with the magnetic field of the coil leads to repulsion of the ring (along the rod) from the coil. When the stake is cut, such a stake current cannot occur, and the ejection of the ring is not observed.
