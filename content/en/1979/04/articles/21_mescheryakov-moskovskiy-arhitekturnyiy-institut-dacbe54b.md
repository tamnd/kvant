Moscow Architectural Institute

The Moscow Architectural Institute was founded in 1866. Every year it graduates 250-300 generalist architects who specialize in the field of urban planning, architecture of living and public buildings, architecture of industrial buildings and structures, architecture and planning of rural populated areas, interfering architecture of buildings, landscape architecture, restoration of architectural monuments. The institute has four faculties: general education, urban planning, industrial engineering, living and public engineering. For five and a half years, students of the institute undergo a full course according to the program of 32 departments, providing comprehensive training for young specialists in the field of architectural design, artistic graphics, theory and history of architecture, specialized engineering, economic, social and political disciplines, physical training, and civil defense.

Studying at the institute is daytime and evening. Most of the institute's graduates are distributed among architectural design and research institutes in Moscow and the Moscow region. Pre-university preparation for those wishing to receive an education at the Moscow Architectural Institute includes a preparatory department and three types of preparatory courses - for schoolchildren in grades 9-10 in figure and drawing, for those demonized from the ranks of the Soviet Army - according to the entrance exam program, correspondence courses for residents of Moscow and the Moscow region.
