V. Gamayunov

The beautiful moire pattern on the first page of the cover was obtained using a computer.
Moiré patterns occur when two families of lines are superimposed, each containing closely spaced non-intersecting lines. When overlapping, the lines of two families should intersect at small angles. Then the distance between the stripes will be significantly larger than the distances between the lines of the family. Under these conditions, a slight mixing of the lines of one of the families can greatly change the position of the stripes of the pattern. Therefore, it is very difficult to draw a moire pattern by hand. For this purpose, it is better to use a computer and a plotter controlled by it.

The pattern reproduced on the cover was made by the ALGRAF graphic display system. However, from families of lines - parallel, evenly spaced straight lines, another - sinusoid arcs, the amplitude and wavelength of which vary according to a quadratic law.

Yu. Kotov
