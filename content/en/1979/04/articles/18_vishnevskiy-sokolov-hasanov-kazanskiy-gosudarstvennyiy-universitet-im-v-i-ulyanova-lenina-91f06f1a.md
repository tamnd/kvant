⟦folio 52⟧

Options for university entrance exams in 1978

Kazan State University named after V. I. Ulyanov-Lenin

Kazan State University was described in detail in «Kvant» No. 6 for 1976.

Mathematics (written exam)

Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics

1. Solve the equation
   $$2 \sin 6x = \text{tg} 2x - 2 \sin 2x$$

2. Find the coordinates of the vector $p$, the collinear vector $\vec{q} = (3; -4)$, if it is known that the vector $p$ forms the same angle with the axis $Ox$ and $\vec{p} = 10$.

3. The basis of the example is an equilibrium triangle, the side of which is equal to $a$, and the angle at the apex is equal to $\alpha$. All side faces are inclined to the base plane at an angle $\beta$. Find volume examples.

4. On the coordinate plane, depict a set of points whose coordinates satisfy the inequality
   $$\log_{1+y} (1 + \sin x) > 1$$

Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics

1. Solve the equation
   $$2 \sin^2 \left( x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = 2 \sin^2 x - \text{tg} x$$

2. Solve inequality
   $$| 3^x - 3 | + 9^x - 3 > 0$$

3. In a regular quadrangular example with height $H$ and base sides $a$ there is inscribed a rectangular paralleliped, the base of which is a square with side $b$, so that its lower base lies on the base of the example, and the vertices of the upper base lie on the side edges of the example. At what $b$ will the volume of the inscribed paralleliped be the smallest? Find the volume at this value $b$.

4. There are a number of columns on the road at a distance of 10 m from each other. Starting from one end post, the worker moved all the posts one by one to the other end post, and to do this he had to walk a total of 1.44 km. How many pillars were there on the road?

Faculty of Physics

1. Solve the equation
   $$\sin x - \frac{| 2 \cos x - 1 |}{2 \cos x - 1} \cdot \sin^2 x = \sin^2 x$$

2. Solve system of equations
   $$\begin{cases} \frac{x^2}{y} + \frac{y^2}{x} = 28, \\ \log_9 x - \log_1 y = \frac{3}{2}. \end{cases}$$

3. On the curve $y = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2$ find the points at which the tangent is parallel to the line $y = 3x$.

4. The dihedral angle between the lateral faces of a right quadrangular line is $a$, and the side of the base is $a$. Find volume examples.

Physics (oral exam task)

1. The cylinder of radius $R$ and mass $m$ stands in front of the height step $h \ll R$. What is the smallest horizontal force passing through the cylinder and perpendicular to the wall that must be applied to the cylinder so that it can rise up the step? Ignore friction.

2. On a rubber thread, the length of which in the unstretched state is $l_0$, a heavy ball of mass $m$ is suspended, which rotates uniformly in the horizontal plane around the vertical axis passing through the point of suspension; in this case, the thread is deflected by an angle $a$. The thread deformation is elastic, the elasticity coefficient is $k$. Find the angular velocity of rotation $\omega$.

3. An ice floe with cross-sectional area $S$ and height $H$ floats in water. How much work must be done to completely immerse the ice floe in water?

4. A ball of density $\rho_{\text{ш}}$ is suspended on a non-weight thread of length $l$ in a liquid medium of density $\rho$. Determine the period of its oscillations. The friction will run away.
