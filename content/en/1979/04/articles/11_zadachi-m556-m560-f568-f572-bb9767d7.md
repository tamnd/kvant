Problem
Kwana

M556—M560; F568—F572

M556. Is it necessary to condense two acute isosceles triangles that have equal lateral lengths and equal inscribed circle radii?

A. Edorov

M557. Given $n$ pairwise interprimes greater than 1 and less than $(2n-1)^2$. Prove that their averages will definitely meet a prime number.

A. Kolotov

M558. There are $k>1$ black sectors in the circle, the angle of each of which is less than $180^\circ/(k^2-k+1)$. Prove that the circle can be rotated around the center $O$ so that all the black sectors go into the white part of the circle.

V. Proizvolov

M559. Prove that if $x, y, z$ is the length of a side of a triangle, then

$$\left| \frac{x}{y} + \frac{y}{z} + \frac{z}{x} - \frac{y}{x} - \frac{z}{y} \right| < 1.$$

A. Ermilov

M560. The bottom of the box has two holes. It is necessary to make a convex flap of the smallest area, in any position of which the hole at the bottom of the box will be closed. Solve this problem if:

a) the bottom of the box is square $4 \times 4$, and the hole $1 \times 1$ is located as shown in figure 1;

b) the bottom of the box is a square $n \times n$ (n is odd), and the hole $1 \times 1$ is located in the center (Fig. 2);

b)* try to solve a similar problem for some other cases when the bottom and hole are convex figures.

V. Batyrev

F568. When a lithium target is bombarded with protons with an energy of at least 1.88 MeV, a nuclear reaction can occur

$$7\mathrm{Li} + p \rightarrow 7\mathrm{Be} + n.$$

At what energy of the protons produced in the reaction can neutrons fly backward from the lithium target?

⟦folio 25⟧

F569. Using an electric boiler with a power of $W=500$ W, water is heated in a saucepan. In two minutes, the water temperature increased from $85^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ to $90^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$. Then the boiler was turned off and in one minute the water temperature dropped by one degree. How much water is in the pan? The specific heat capacity of water is $4,19 \cdot 10^{3}$ J/(kg K).

E. Surkov

F570. Due to the presence of a space charge in the interelectronic space of a flat diode, the potential distribution $\varphi(x)$ between the cathode and anomer has the form: $\varphi(x) = x^2 - 2x$ (x is in mm, and $\varphi$ is in volts). Distance between cathode and anomal $d=10$ mm. The coordinate $x$ coincides with the distance to the cathode. Determine at what minimum kinetic energy an electron from the cathode surface can reach the anode. What will be the maximum acceleration of the electric currents that reach the anode?

F571. Batteries and resistors are assembled into a circuit in two ways - as in figure 3, and as in figure 3, b. Determine the points flowing through the resistors in both cases. Neglect the resistance of current sources and connecting wires. How does the current change in the case $a$ if the wires are cut at points $A$ and $B$?

F572. Peren studied the dependence on height of the number of spherical parts of a special resin - gummigut - in suspension of these parts in water. For a part with a radius of $r_1=0,212$ µm, he obtained the dependences, the graph of which is shown in figure 4 (n is the concentration of the part at a height of $h$, $n_0$ is their concentration at the bottom of the cuvette). The same graph is obtained for a part with a radius of $r_2=0,106$ µm, only the picture is stretched in height by 8 times. At the same time, it is known that the oxygen density in the earth’s atmosphere kills with height, as shown in figure 5 (ρ is the oxygen density at an altitude $H$, $\rho_0$ is at the surface of the cuvette). Determine the mass of an oxygen molecule.

The density of gummigut is $\rho_r=1,194 \text{ г/cm}^3$.
