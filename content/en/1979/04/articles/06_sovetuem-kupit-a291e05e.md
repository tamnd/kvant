If (after the indicated 100 double rounds of type A) say, 10 double rounds of type B were played, then as a result of such a small “movement of the tourer” Alik will have 10 pringrysh, Borya - 110 pringrysh, and Vasya - 100 pringrysh, i.e. property I is preserved. At the same time, Borya will have 110 wins - more than Alik (10 wins) and than Vasya (100 wins). This means that condition II is also satisfied. In addition, in the end, each scored 220 points (so condition III has not yet been met).

To the newly received turir, apply one more small movement in the form of one additional type; Regardless of its outcome, conditions I and II remain fulfilled (these conditions are stable). Let's select this additional round so that Vasya scores more points in it than each of the other players (for example, Vasya scores 2 points in it, winning against Alik and Borya, and Alik and Borya draw with each other). Thus, we have constructed a tourer in which all three conditions I, II, III are met, i.e. the tourer mentioned in the conditions of the problem is possible.
