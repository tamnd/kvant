Mathematics
(oral examination)

Ticket 1

1. Derivative functions $y = \sin x, y = \cos x, y = \tg x$.

2. Given $tg\alpha + ctg\alpha = m$.

Find: 1) $tg^2\alpha + ctg^2\alpha$

2) $tg^3\alpha + ctg^3\alpha$

3) $tg\alpha - ctg\alpha$.

3. The centers of four circles of radius $a$ are located in the upper squares on the side $a$. Find the area of ​​the figure formed by two of these four circles and lying inside the square.

Ticket 2

1. Volume of rotation figures.

2. Find the limit

$$\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{2^x + 2^{3-x} - 6}{\sqrt{2^{-x}} - 2^{1-x}}.$$

3. A statue 5 m high stands on a pedestal 6 m high. At what distance should a person 1.6 m tall (to eye level) stand to see the statue from the greatest angle?

Physics
(oral exam task)

1. A bullet of mass $m$ hits a wooden block of mass $M$ suspended by a thread of length $l$ and gets stuck in it. Determine at what maximum angle the thread will deflect if the bullet speed is $|\vec{v}|$.

2. A ball of mass $M$ rests on two smooth planes forming an angle $\varphi = 90^\circ$, and the left plane makes an acute angle $\alpha$ with the horizon. Determine the force with which the ball presses on the plane.

3. The body falls from a height of $H = 360$ m with an initial speed of $$\left|\vec{v}_0\right| = 20 \text{ m/c}$$ and goes deep into the sand to a depth of $s = 0,5$ m. Determine the average soil resistance force. Body weight $m = 0,1$ kg, do not take air resistance into account.

4. Three identical balls with a mass of $m = 0,1$ g each are suspended at one point on silk threads of length $l = 20$ cm. What identical charges should be imparted to the balls so that each thread makes an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ with the vertical?

5. An electric kettle has two windings. When one of them is turned on, water boils in $t_1 = 15$ minutes, when the second winding is turned on - in $t_2 = 30$ minutes. How many minutes will it take for the kettle to boil if the windings are connected in series? In what case is the efficiency of a kettle greater and why?

6. Find the short-circuit current for the batteries if, at a load of $I_1 = 5$ A, it supplies a power of $P_1 = 9,5$ W to the external circuit, and at a load of $I_2 = 8$ A, it supplies a power of $P_2 = 14,4$ W.
