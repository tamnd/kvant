⟦folio 12⟧

But for all bodies the product of the mass $m$ of the body and its acceleration $a$, that is, the value $ma$, remains the same value. It follows that the force $F$ is determined by the equation
$$\vec{F} = ma.$$
Newton's second law is now formulated this way: the force acting on a body is equal to the product of the body's mass and its own acceleration.

It is clear that this formulation closely corresponds to Newton’s, since it follows from it that acceleration (“change of motion” according to Newton) is proportional to force:
$$\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{F}}{m}.$$
So, force, which was only said to be a measure of the influence of one body on another, has now become a physical quantity that can be expressed in numbers. You can set the unit of measurement for it. As a unit of force, it is natural to take the force that imparts to a body with a mass equal to one (1 kg) an acceleration equal to one (1 m/s²). It is fair that this unit was given the name “newton” (N) in honor of the creator of classical mechanics.

$$\vec{F} = ma - \text{уравнение движения}$$

Newton's second law is the most important law of mechanics, because it is it that allows us to solve any problem about the motion of bodies. The fact is that acceleration $a$, included in the equation of the second law, represents rapid changes (in magnitude and direction) of the speed of a body over time:

$$\vec{a} = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}.$$
