Physics
Product "Nuka"
1. Bendr ikov G. A. et al. Problems in physics. 4th edition, revised. Volume 26 l., circulation 300,000 copies, price 95 kopecks.
This collection is intended to help applicants to universities. It contains tasks for all sections of the physics program. For large structured data problems, detailed solutions or instructions.
2. Lange V. N. Experimental physical problems for ingenuity. 2nd edition, revised. (Library of the Physics and Mathematics School.) Volume 7 sheets, circulation 300,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.
The core of the book is to cultivate new, out-of-the-box thinking. Acquaintance with the history of physics convincingly shows that the success of an experiment is often determined by the use of new, completely unexpected, measurement methods developed specifically for this case. The book contains over a hundred problems in which it is proposed to come up with a way to measure unknowns; especially for this case, a section “Hints” has been introduced. At the end of the book there is a detailed analysis of the tasks.

The book is intended for high school students interested in physics, and students of physics and mathematics schools; It may also be useful for secondary school physics teachers.
3. Yavorsky B. M., Seleznev Yu. A. A reference guide to physics for secondary school students, admission and self-education. 2nd edition, revised. Volume 30 l., circulation 200,000 copies, price 1 rub. 40 k.
The book contains proposals for all basic physical concepts, formulates physical laws and briefly explains the existing phenomena described by them. The manual contains information on all sections of the physics course that are studied in high school and secondary specialized educational institutions. Some chapters provide examples of problem solving.
The guide to physics is intended for high school students, vocational school students, technical staff and employees of preparatory departments of higher education institutions. It can be used by applicants in preparation for applicable university exams, as well as by those who are interested in physics and are engaged in self-education.
4. Perelman Ya. I. Entertaining physics. Book 1. 20th edition, revised. Volume 12 l., circulation 500,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
This book was written by the famous attempter and teacher Yakov Isidorovich Perelman. The author did not set himself the task of presenting a school course in physics. The book very popularly and interestingly presents various paradoxes, puzzles, tasks, experiments, intricate questions and stories from the field of physics. It will be understandable even to schoolchildren in grades 5-7. Despite its “considerable age,” the book is “outdated” only to a very small extent.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

Product "Mir"
5. Gerenk F. Albert Einstein. Translation from German. Volume 5 l. circulation 50,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
In the book by university professor. Humboldt in Berlin by F. Gernek tells not only about the scientific achievements of A. Einstein, but also about the main stages of his life and his philosophical views.
The book is intended for a wide range of readers.

"Atomidat"
6. Duboova E.I. The mysterious world of elementary particles. Volume 9 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The book, in a popular form, introduces the reader to the problems of the physics of elementary particles and modern experimental methods of understanding the world of particles, with the results of the latest research in this area. The main conditions and disturbances of accelerators and physical installations are described, the experimental possibilities and theoretical prospects of a new direction in the physics of elementary particles - research on colliding beams in storage collages - are discussed. The well-used book ends with a table of elemental particles with an emphasis on the “blind spots” in it.
The book is intended primarily for school graduates and physics teachers.

Product "Enlightenment"
7. Spassky B.I. physics in its development. Help for students. Volume 14 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 55 kopecks.
The author gives a historical overview of the main stages in the development of physics, from antiquity to the first half of the 20th century. In a language accessible to students, with minimal use of mathematical apparatus, without training for accuracy and depth of presentation, the book examines the development of such physical concepts as mass, force, work, temperature, etc. Much attention is paid to issues of an ideological nature.
