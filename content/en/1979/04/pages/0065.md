Then $x_0 = X \pm \sqrt{X^2 - Y}$ (see solution to problem 4). The angular coefficients of the corresponding tangents are expressed in terms of $X$ and $Y$ as follows: $K_1 = 2(X + \sqrt{X^2 - Y})$; $K_2 = 2(X - \sqrt{X^2 - Y})$. Let's calculate the tangent of the angle between the tangents and equate it to tan $45^\circ = 1$:

$$1 = \frac{K_2 - K_1}{1 + K_1 \cdot K_2} = \frac{-4\sqrt{X^2 - Y}}{1 + 4Y}.$$
