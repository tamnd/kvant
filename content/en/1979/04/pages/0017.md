Newton's third law

We have very little to say about Newton's third law. Not because the law is unimportant and does not deserve server destruction. On the contrary, it contains the most important idea of ​​mechanics. But because we have already discussed this law and we have already used it.

The law of motion, which is listed under the third number in Newton’s book and which retains this traditional number in all books on mechanics from Newton to the present day, states that forces arise during the interaction of bodies, that the forces acting on each of the participants in the interaction are identical in modulo and opposite in direction. We already talked about this in our previous article. It was indicated there that when two bodies interact, each of them receives acceleration; these accelerations are directed in opposite directions and the ratio of the accelerated model is equal to the inverse ratio of the masses of the interacting bodies. All this can be expressed by the formula

$$m_1 a_1 = - m_2 a_2.$$
