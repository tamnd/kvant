Fig. 7. Tokamak installations are not the only type of device on the basis of which scientists hope to create a thermonuclear reactor. Another type of thermonuclear devices are traps with magnetic plugs. Plasma particles are retained in them due to reflection from “magnetic mirrors” - they have an increased field induction in which field lines are damped. On figure one of such devices “Ogra-3B” is presented.

Fig. 8. The Angara-1 installation is used to heat plasma with a beam of electrons accelerated to near-light speeds. It creates electron pulses with energies of the order of 2.5 MeV at currents of up to 400 kA. Pulse duration 6·10⁻⁸ s. figure shows the device's high-voltage generator.
