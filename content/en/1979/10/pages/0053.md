Problem 5. A fixed fan is placed with power $N$, its efficiency is equal to η. What reactive force acts on the fan during its operation? Fan blade diameter $D$, air density p.
Let us denote by $v$ the model of the rate of occurrence of the flow created by the fan. Then the mass of occurrence during time $\Delta t$ is equal to $m = \rho v S \Delta t$, where $S = \pi D^2 / 4$ is the cross-sectional area of ​​the occurrence of the flow (Fig. 5), and its kinetic energy is
$$E_{\text{киин}} = \frac{mv^2}{2} = \frac{\rho v^3 S \Delta t}{2}.$$
