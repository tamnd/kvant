5. Let $\overline{abc}$ be the problem number. Then
$$|\overline{abc} - \overline{cba}| = |a \cdot 10^2 + b \cdot 10 + c - c \cdot 10^2 - b \cdot 10 - a| = 99 |a - c|.$$
Pozny, if $a = c$, that is, the last digit of the difference is equal to zero, then the answer will also be zero.

With $a \neq c$ ($0 < a \leq 9, 0 < c \leq 9$) we find that the answer has a digit in the tens place, and the sum of the digits in the hundreds and ones place is always equal to nine. Pozny, having learned that the last digit of the answer is $x$, we can immediately answer: $(9 - x)9x$ (say, if $x = 5$, then the answer will be 495).
