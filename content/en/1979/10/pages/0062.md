Our cover

Transformable cube

On the second page of the cover you see the frame of a cube made of rods fastened with threads, and three more figures obtained by “transforming” this cube. In order to make such a cube, you need to take 12 wooden rods and a strong thread (you can use a thin fishing line instead). Holes with a diameter of one millimeter should be drilled at the ends of the rods. These rods then need to be tied together as shown in figure. At the end of the rod

and the thread gets tangled. It is then passed through the hole in rod c and placed at the other end of rod a. The next thread is passed through the hole in rod b and tied at the ends of rod c. The convenient way to tie the threads is to pass them through the holes at the ends of the rod and tie knots on them. The remaining vertices of the cube should be connected in the same way. In this case, you need to drill so that all the vertices are connected equally (clockwise or counterclockwise), that is, so that when moving the cube there is a difference between them. Since each rod has two ends, two threads will “stretch” along it. The model is made!
