As follows from the solution to Problem 1, when water of mass $m$ enters a boat, the initial speed of which is directed vertically (rain!), the speed of the boat decreases and becomes equal in magnitude

$$v_k = \frac{M}{M + m} v,$$

where $M$ is the mass of the boat, $v$ is the modulus of its initial speed. From this we can see that the change in speed of the boat

$$\Delta v = v - v_k = \frac{v}{1 + M/m}$$

the smaller the larger the ratio $M/m$. Since the mass that gets into the boats must be the same, the decrease in speed will be greater for the boat from which the athlete is scooping water. Note also that when water is thrown to the side, the speed of the boat does not change, since the keel does not allow it to move in a perpendicular direction.

Thus, in which the athlete sleeps, he will cover the given distance faster and be the first to reach the finish line.
