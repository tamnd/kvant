⟦folio 20⟧

Laboratory «Kvant»

Task 1
Determine the density of the unknown liquid.

Equipment: a vessel with water, a vessel with an unknown liquid (with salt solution), a beaker, a test tube.

Task 2
Compare the surface tension coefficients of water and soap solution.

Equipment: glass tube, plate, piece of wire, vessel with water, vessel with soap solution, ruler.

Task 3
Determine the resolution of the eye and study its dependence on the diameter of the hole in the screen placed in front of it.

Equipment: a sheet of graph paper, a sheet of white paper, a needle, a tape measure or a meter ruler.
