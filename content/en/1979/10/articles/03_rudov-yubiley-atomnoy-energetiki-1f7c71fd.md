ANNIVERSARY
NUCLEAR ENERGY

It is 25 years since the launch of the world's first nuclear power plant. This is an outstanding event in human history with various atomic reactors. Our country provides enormous assistance to other socialist states in the development of nuclear energy. At the same time, a lot of work is being done to create nuclear vehicles. Three powerful nuclear icebreakers have already entered service - Lenin, Sibir and Arktika. They radically changed the conditions of navigation in the Arctic Ocean. On August 17, 1977, the icebreaker Arktika accessed the Earth's North Pole for the first time in the history of navigation.

The successes of our country in the field of peaceful use of atomic energy are reflected in reporting philatery. In January 1956, a series of three stamps was issued to commemorate the first nuclear power plant. Two of them are reproduced at the top of our selection. In 1958 and 1978, stamps with images of nuclear icebreakers were issued. We also see the icebreaker “Lenin” on the Cuban stamp reproduced here. It is curious that penguins, which are not in the North Pole, are depicted next to him.

In 1958, 1962 and 1963, stamps were issued for postal circulation dedicated to the struggle of peoples for the prohibition of atomic weapons and the use of atomic energy only for peaceful purposes. Some of them are shown in our selection along with a numbered postal block issued in honor of the Arktika raid to the North Pole.

V. Rudov
