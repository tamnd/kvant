M586—M590; F598—F602

M586. In the triangle $ABC$, which has $\hat{B}=60^\circ$, we draw bissertrixes $AD$ and $CE$ intersecting at the point $O$. Prove that $|OD|=|OE|$.

M587. Given a triple of numbers $2, \sqrt{2}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. It is allowed to replace any two of them with two of these: their sum divided by $\sqrt{2}$, and their difference, also divided by $\sqrt{2}$. Is it possible, after doing this procedure several times, to obtain a triple of numbers $1, \sqrt{2}, 1+\sqrt{2}$? (9)

M588. a) Through a point taken inside an arbitrary tetrahedron, segments with ends on the faces of the tetrahedron are drawn parallel to its edges. Prove that the sum of all six ratios of the lengths of segments to the lengths of edges parallel to them is always trem.
b) Form and prove a similar statement for a triangle (on the plane). (9)

E. Yasinovy

M589. On the plane, given a set of $n$ vectors, the length of each of which does not exceed 1. Prove that by replacing some vectors of this set with their opposite ones, you can obtain a set of $n$ vectors, the sum of which has the length
a) does not exceed $\sqrt{n}$;
b) does not exceed $\sqrt{2}$. (9)

P. Gusyatinikov, A. Plotkin

F599. A rope is thrown across a block fixed in the ceiling, on which a load of mass $M$ is balanced by a ladder with a person of mass $m$. According to what law should a person move relative to the stairs so that the reaction of the block to the ceiling is zero? The block is weightless, the rope is completely flexible, inextensible and weightless. (8)

A. Warzen

F600. In the circuit shown in figure 2, the switch is alternately closed and opened at those moments when the direction on the first capacitor is zero. Draw a graph of the voltage across the inductor versus time. (10)

F601. On the Moon, a vertical cylinder closed by a heavy piston contains argon at a temperature of $T_1$. The piston can move in the cylinder without friction. A second similar piston is carefully placed on the piston. Determine the temperature $T_2$ of the gas at the new equilibrium position of the piston. The heat capacity of the piston and cylinder, as well as heat transfer, should not be taken into account. The gas is considered ideal. (9)

F602. Capacitors with capacitances $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected to a circuit consisting of a resistor with resistance $R$ and a source with EMF $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance $r=R/3$, closing terminals 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 (Fig. 3). After the circuit, the voltage on the capacitor $C_1$ turns out to be equal to $\mathcal{E}/2$, and the potential of terminal 3 is higher than the potential of terminal 2. Determine what charge $p_0$ was on the capacitor $C_2$ before the circuit. (9).
