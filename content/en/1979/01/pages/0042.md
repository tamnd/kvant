5. More difficult tasks

11. Prove that a given segment, not parallel to a given plane, can be projected in parallel onto a segment of any length on a given plane.

12. At what angle on a given plane can a given angle be projected in parallel?

13. Prove that if their images are known for three points of a plane figure that do not lie on the same straight line, then images of all points of this figure can be obtained.

14. Prove that any triangle can serve as an image of a given triangle *).

15. Prove that the image of a quadrilateral can be any quadrilateral whose diagonals are divided by the point of intersection in the same ratios as the original one.

16. Prove that the image of a tetrahedron can be any quadrilateral with drawn diagonals.

17. The exchange cube is placed on a larger cube so that they have a common vertex and their faces are parallel. Construct a section of the resulting figure by a plane defined by three points, from which
- all three lie on three crossing edges less than a cube;
- all three lie inside three different faces larger than the cube;
- one point lies on the upper base of a small cube, and the other two on different edges of a larger cube.

*) Let us recall that the dimensions of the field image do not matter (see point 1), therefore “any triangle” means “any triangle up to similarity”.
