Zone plates

Interesting phenomena associated with the interference and diffraction of waves of various physical natures can be observed using zone plates. What is it?

Fresnel zones

Let us imagine that between the exact source of waves $A$ and the receiver of waves $B$ there is an opaque screen with a circular response (Fig. 1). According to the Huygen-Fresnel principle, each point in the response of the screen is a secondary source of waves, and from each of them the waves come to the receiver. The oscillations of all secondary sources are correct, so the waves arriving at the receiver will interfere. What determines the result of interference? Let's use the method proposed by Fresnel.

The length of the path from the source to the receiver depends on where the wave passes through the response in the screen. First, with an increase in the response radius, starting from a very small one, the interference of waves will cause an increase in the amplitude of oscillations at the receiver $B$. This will happen until the difference between the paths $\Delta l$ along the paths $AO_1B$ and $AOB$ reaches half the wavelength $\lambda/2$:

$$\Delta l = \left| AO_1 + O_1B \right| - \left| AO + OB \right| = \lambda/2.$$

A hole that satisfies this condition is called the first Fresnel zone. Denoting by $a$ the distances from the exact source to the screen and from the receiver to the screen ($|AO| = |OB| = a$), we find the expression for the radius $r_1$ by the first Fresnel zone:

$$r_1 = \sqrt{|AO_1|^2 - |AO|^2} = \sqrt{\left(a + \frac{\lambda}{4}\right)^2 - a^2} = \sqrt{\frac{a\lambda}{2} + \frac{\lambda^2}{16}}.$$

If the condition $a \gg \lambda$ is satisfied, then $r_1 \approx \sqrt{a\lambda/2}$.

Let us increase the response radius to a value $r_2$ at which the difference in path along the path $AO_2B$ and $AOB$ is equal to the wavelength $\lambda$:

$$\Delta l = \left| AO_2 + O_2B \right| - \left| AO + OB \right| = \lambda.$$

The annular zone with inner radius $r_1 = |OO_1|$ and outer radius $r_2 = |OO_2|$ is called the second Fresnel zone. The outer radius $r_2$ of the second Fresnel zone in the case under consideration is equal to

$$r_2 = \sqrt{|AO_2|^2 - |AO|^2} = \sqrt{\left(a + \lambda/2\right)^2 - a^2} = \sqrt{2\left(a\lambda/2\right) + \lambda^2/4} \approx \sqrt{2a\lambda/2} \approx \sqrt{2} r_1.$$
