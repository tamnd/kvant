8. A man drops a stone into a deep well and hears the sound of its fall after 8 s. Find the depth of the deck. Acceleration of free fall is assumed to be 10 m/s². Speed ​​of sound 330 m/s.

9. What power does a person develop when pushing a loaded sleigh with a total mass of 40 kg along a horizontal road? The friction coefficient of the runners on the road is 0.1. The sled is pulled at a constant speed of 3 m/s using a rope inclined at an angle of 30° to the horizontal.

10. A skier slides down from a springboard 50 m high, which has a curve at the foot with a radius of 10 m. Find the overload experienced by the skier when entering a horizontal section.

11. A balloon of mass $M$ floats in the air at a height $H$. On the stairs, when taking the balloon gondola, there is a person with mass $m$. How high is the balloon if a person climbs $n$ steps up the stairs? Distance between steps $h$.

12. In a room with a volume of 60 m³, a drop of two containing 10⁻⁴ g of an aromatic substance with a relative molecular weight of 1000 was evaporated. How many molecules of an aromatic substance will enter the human lungs with each breath? The lung volume is assumed to be 2.2 liters.

13. In a sealed state, a volume of 11.2 liters contains air under a pressure of 0.1 MPa. What amount of heat must be added to the air so that the pressure in the vessel increases 3 times? The molar heat of air at constant volume is taken to be 21 J/(mol K).

Matematika

1. There are 35 students in the class. Of these, 20 attend a mathematical circle, 11 attend a physical circle, 10 students do not attend any of these circles. How many students attend both circles?

2. In order for the quadrilateral $ABCD$ to be a parallelogram, it is necessary and sufficient that it have a center of symmetry. Prove it.

3. Determine all values ​​of $a$ for which the set of solutions to the inequality $x^2 - a (1 + a) x + a^3 < 0$ contains the interval [0; 1].

4. Prove or disprove the following statements: a) for the number $(1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dots \cdot (n - 1)) - 1$ to be divisible by $n$, it is sufficient that $n$ be prime; b) for the number $(1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dots \cdot (n - 1)) - 1$ to be divisible by $n$, it is necessary that $n$ be prime.

5. One quadrilateral is located inside another quadrilateral. Can the sum of the lengths of the diagonals inside a quadrilateral be greater than the sum of the lengths of the diagonals inside a quadrilateral?

6. There were three tasks on one mid: $A, B, C$. 25 schoolchildren solved at least one problem. Among the schoolchildren who did not solve problem $A$, those who solved problem $B$ were twice as many as those who solved problem $C$. There are one more schoolchildren who solved only problem $A$ than other schoolchildren who solved problem $A$. How many schoolchildren solved only problem $B$, if among the schoolchildren who solved only one problem, half did not solve problem $A$?

7. The points $D$ and $E$ serve as the midpoint of the sides $[AB]$ and $[CD]$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Prove that

$$\overrightarrow{DE} = \frac{\overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{AD}}{2}.$$

8. Two statements are given:
a) System
$$\begin{cases} (a + 4) x + 3y = a + 1, \\ ax + (a - 1) y = a - 1 \end{cases}$$

has an infinite number of solutions;

6) The lines defined by the equations $5x + 4y = 6$ and $ax + 6y = 10$ intersect in the second fourth Cartesian line of the coordinate system.

For what values ​​of $a$ is one of these statements false and the other true?

9. Prove that if an arbitrary quadrilateral $ABCD$ contains internal bissertrices, then the four intersection points of the bissertrices of the angles $A$ and $C$ with the bissertrices of the angles $B$ and $D$ lie on the same circle.

10. A schoolboy switches all his stamps into a new album. If he sticks 20 stamps on one sheet, then he will not have enough album, and if he sticks 23 stamps on one sheet, then at least one sheet will be empty. If a student gives the same album, with 21 stamps pasted on each sheet, then he will have a total of 500 stamps. How many sheets are there in an album?

11. On each of the planets of some systems there is an astronomer observing a close nearby planet. The distances between the planes are different in pairs. Prove that if there is an odd number of floats, then no one observes any float.

12. Given $k$ numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k$, with $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_k = 0$.

Calculate the limit of the sequence $(S_n)$:

$$S_n = a_1 \sqrt{n + 1} + a_2 \sqrt{n + 2} + \dots + a_k \sqrt{n + k}.$$

13. The functions $f$ and $g$ are not arbitrary at the point $x = x_0$. Is it possible to say that the function $F_1(x) = f(x) + g(x)$ also has no arbitrary ones at the point $x = x_0$?
