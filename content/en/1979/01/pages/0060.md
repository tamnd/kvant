to the All-Union correspondence
math school

The All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University (VZMSH) accepts students from rural classes and vocational school students. Schoolchildren living in Moscow, Leningrad and their suburbs are not accepted into the school.

Classes will begin on September 1, 1979. Education at the school is free.

Students accepted into the school will be regulated by tasks that contain learning theoretical issues and problems to solve. The VZMS program is closely related to the school curriculum and is aimed at in-depth study of the main issues of the school mathematics course. The duration of training is three years.

The objectives of the introductory test are published below. Those wishing to enter VZMSH must send the solution to these problems no later than March 1, 1979. After checking the work (approximately July 1979), VZMSH will inform all those who took part in the competition about the results of the work. Schoolchildren living in rural areas and workers' settlements will have preference for admission.

Although some of the introductory problems differ in appearance from regular school problems, solving them does not require additional knowledge of mathematics. To enter school, it is not necessary to solve all problems without exception. When evaluating work, you will learn not only the number of problems solved, but also the quality of the solution. The solution to each problem must be justified. An answer without justification may not be read. If there are several different answers to a problem, then you need to indicate them all.

The work must be completed in Russian in a student's checkered notebook. Entry papers will not be processed and decisions will not be made on them.

In the envelope along with the notebook you need to put a piece of paper measuring 14 cm x 6 cm with your postal address (we will stick it on the envelope when we send you a response).

The addresses to which introductory works should be sent will be indicated in the next issue of the journal (they can also be viewed in «Kvant», 1978, No. 1).

Schoolchildren who did not qualify for the VZMSH competition have the opportunity to study under the VZMSH program in the “Collective student of the VZMSH” groups.

“Collective student of the VZMSH” is a school mathematical circle, working under the guidance of a mathematics teacher of the given school according to the VZMSH program. Admission to the “Collective Student of VZMSH” groups is carried out until September 20, 1979 in two streams: for those who will study in the 8th grade from September 1979, and for those who begin to study in the 9th grade.
