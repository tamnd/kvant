Physics School at Moscow State University - 15 years

Last year marked 15 years since the creation of the first four physics and mathematics boarding schools - at Novosibirsk, Moscow, Leningrad and Kiev universities. Physics and mathematics boarding schools were established in 1963. Prominent scientists took part in creating the teaching system for these schools: in Moscow - Academician A. N. Kolmogorov and Academician I. K. Kioin, in Novosibirsk - Academician M. A. Lavrentyev, in Kyiv - Academician V. M. Glushkov, in Leningrad - Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences D. K. Fadeev. In connection with the use of the details of the educational council of the physics and mathematics boarding school at Moscow State University to A. N. Kolmogorov, the director of this school I. T. Tropin and the head of the mathematics department V. V. Vavilov with a request to tell about her work.

The scientific and technological revolution radically changed the role of science in the life of human society. Science turned into direct productivity force. The unusually rapid development of science requires more and more scientific personnel. That is why the issue of attracting talented young people into science is so relevant. And it is scattered throughout our entire country. And if it is not so difficult to notice it in capital cities, then finding talented teachers capable of independent scientific research in small towns, villages and rural areas is much more difficult.

Physics and mathematics boarding schools at the country's leading universities (in Novosibirsk, Moscow, Leningrad and Kyiv), created 15 years ago, were recognized as practical solutions to this most important state problem.

The hope that real research would emerge from them was largely justified. In particular, of the schoolchildren admitted to the Moscow Physics and Mathematics School in 1963-70, more than two hundred people subsequently completed graduate studies and completed candidate dissertations. Graduates of the boarding school at Moscow State University have published hundreds of scientific papers and made review reports at international conferences and congresses. Among the graduates of the Physics and Mathematics School there are already two Doctors of Physical and Mathematical Sciences. A similar picture is observed in three other FMSs.

In view of the successful four physics and mathematics boarding schools, similar schools have been opened in some other resource communities: Armenia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Lithuania, as well as in some autonomous resource areas of the Russian Federation.

At the Moscow Physics and Mathematics School, 360 schoolchildren study in the ninth and tenth grades. Every year we recruit 150 people for two-year training (in the ninth grade) and 60 people for one-year training (in the tenth grade). Graduates, jointly, of eight or nine classes of training schools living in the central part of the RSFSR, the Urals, the Volga region, the North Caucasus and Belarus are accepted.
