⟦folio 41⟧

Quests

1. Arrange the numbers 1, 2, ..., 9 in circles (see figure) so that the sum of the numbers along the inner triangle is equal to the square of one of these numbers, the sum of the numbers along the circumference is equal to the cube of the same number, and the sum of the numbers along the outer triangle is equal to the sum of the square and cube of this number.

2. It was a very hot day and four married couples drank 49 bottles of `coca-cola`. Anna drank 2 bottles, Betty - 3, Cessile - 4, Dorotty - 5. Mr. Adams drank as much as his wife, Mr. Brown - twice as much, Wilson - three times, Greene - four times as much as their wives. Give the last name of each of the four ladies.

3. In one tribe, female names are accepted, composed of only two letters - A and B. The combinations `AAA` and `BB` in themselves mean something, but from the composition of longer words they can be crossed out (or written) without damage. The combination `AAB` has recently been replaced by a more cultural combination `BA`, so they are now differentiated. Can you believe a tribal leader who says he has six daughters and they all have different names?

4. Solve the equation depicted on figure (the letter A corresponds to one number, the letter H to another).
