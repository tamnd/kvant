Over the past 15 years, the FMS has developed its own traditions, which have created a special atmosphere at the school, where interest in science and the habit of hard work are combined with a passion for literature and music, sports (for example, football, volleyball and basketball meetings between teams of students and teachers have become traditional), and interesting tourist trips.

Not all OLS school graduates become scientists in the fields of mathematics and physics. We are no less proud of those who become good engineers in a particular field. There are also those who choose biology or linguistics as their specialty. But we hope that all FMS graduates recall with satisfaction the atmosphere of creative work and broad cultural interests in our boarding school.

Of course, much more needs to be done to ensure that the Moscow Physics and Music School more successfully fulfills the tasks assigned to it.

Taking this opportunity, we sincerely congratulate the teams of boarding schools in Kyiv, Leningrad, Novosibirsk and our FMS on their fifteenth anniversary and wish them further success.
