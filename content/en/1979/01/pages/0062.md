⟦folio 60⟧

Twice a week, Moscow students receive a lot of lessons in physics and mathematics under the ZFTSH program at evening advisory centers (in a number of Moscow schools). Recruitment to these points occurs either based on the results of the introductory task of the ZFTSH, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics (the interview will be held in September; inquiries by phone 216-00-05, ext. 2-59).

Introductory test work in physics and mathematics, each student performs independent work in Russian and carefully rewrites it into one school terrace. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment. The terraces were transferred in a large envelope, viewed by a balderol. Along with the decision, you are required to submit a certificate from the school where you are a teacher, indicating your class. The certificate is placed on the inside cover of the terradi. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. On the outside of the terradi, glue a sheet of paper filled in according to the following pattern:

1. Region (region or ASSR)
2. Last name, first name, patronymic
3. Class
4. Number and address of schools
5. Profession parent and position father
6. Detailed home address

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 1, 1979 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be sent back.

Admission to the school is carried out by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1979.

Send the test papers with solved problems of the introductory test (required in physics and mathematics) to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, for ZFTSH.

Students of the Arkhangelsk, Vologda, Kalinin, Kirov, Leningrad, Murmansk, Novgorod, Pskov regions, Karelian and Komi Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republics send work to the address: 198904, Old Peterhof, st. May 1st. 100, Leningrad State University, ZFTSH filial at MIPT.

Students of the Amur, Irkutsk, Kamchatka, Sakhalin, Chita regions, Krasnoyarsk, Primorsky, Khabarovsk territories, Buryat, Tuva, Yakut ASSR and Chukotka send work to the address: 660607, Krasnoyarsk, st. Peresona, 7, Pedagogical Institute, branch of ZFTSH at MIPT.

Below are the objectives of the introductory test in physics and mathematics. In the physics assignment, problems 1-5 are for seventh grade students, tasks 5-11 are for eighth grade students, and tasks 7-13 are for ninth grade students. In the introductory math assignment, problems 1–5 are for seventh grades, 4–10 for eighth grades, and 7–13 for ninth grades.

Assignment for the introductory test at ZFTSH in 1979

Physics

1. The path in the garden is 2 m wide. How long can the path be covered with a layer of sand 2 cm thick if the sand is delivered by a ten-ton truck loaded to the limit? Determine the density of the sand yourself.

2. Determine the depth of the shaft, at the bottom of which the barometer shows a pressure of 860 mm Hg. Art. Take the pressure at the surface of the earth equal to 760 mm Hg. Art. Air density 1.29 kg/m³. Changes in air density with depth in the pre-tenberium.

3. Break the match into as small pieces as possible. Why are small pieces harder to break than large ones?

4. How much charcoal will be required to turn 3 kg of ice into steam at normal pressure? The initial ice temperature is 5°C. Heater efficiency is 20%.

5. The electric motor driving the pump is connected to a 220 V network. The pump supplies 500 m³ of water to a height of 20 m. How much electricity flows through the winding of the electric motor if the efficiency of the installation (moving with the pump) is 40%?

6. MotoshikLIST covered the distance between two points at a speed of 40 km/h. Having increased the speed to 80 km/h, motoshikLIST then covered half the distance. Determine the average speed of the motorcycle LIST for the entire period of movement.

7. Water is poured into a cylindrical jar. When an empty aluminum cup was lowered into the jar so that it floated, the water rose by 2.7 cm. How much will the water level in the jar change if the cup is drowned in it? Aluminum density 2.7 g/cm³.
