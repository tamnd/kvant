natie is defined in the “Dictionary of the Russian Language” by S. I. Ozhegov. We read: “Convex - having an arched surface shaped outward.” What does arched mean? We read: “Arc-shaped - having the shape of an arc.” What is an arc? "An arc is part of a circle, a circle, or a circular curved line." This is where our patience breaks: firstly, curved lines can be very diverse, and secondly, a circle is not a line. Based on this definition of convexity, can anything be said about the convexity (or slightness) of, for example, a cube? In my opinion, it’s impossible. So such a “definition” cannot be arranged by mathematics.

In mathematics, the concept convex has a clearly defined meaning. A set of lobes is called convex if, together with any two of its points A and B, the entire segment [AB] also belongs to this set.

Now it is quite clear that a cube is a convex body. But the figure at figure 1 is not convex. It is not difficult to understand that any triangle is a convex figure. And quadrilaterals can be either convex or small (Fig. 2).

Well, is it possible to say something about convex sets? Can. For example, prove (this is quite simple) that the intersection of two or more convex sets is again a convex set (we will also consider the empty set to be convex). It is very difficult to prove such a statement: through any boundary of a convex figure on a plane one can check a straight line so that the entire figure lies on one side of this straight line. Such a straight line is shown in figure 3; it is called the reference line. If the boundary point is a "corner" point, as in figure 4, several reference lines can be checked through it. Prove on your own that if some plane figure has a supporting line at each boundary point, then this figure is convex. Thus, the formed statement can be taken as the definition of a convex figure.
