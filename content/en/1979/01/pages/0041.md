4. Parallel lines and planes

In addition to the previously studied prims, two theorems about parallel planes are often used: (I) the section of two parallel planes by a straight plane is a pair of parallel lines; (II) the intersection of two planes parallel to a line l is a line parallel to l.

8. The construction of a section of a triangular paramid by a plane passing through given points C, D, parallel to edge AB, is shown in Figures 11, a-c (IX, 53, 54, 95). Here the points X₂, X₃ are obtained as the intersection of a line passing through X₁ parallel to AB with the corresponding faces (see Theorem II). We recommend the reader to solve the problem for the case when the points X₂, X₃ lie not on the edges, but on their extensions.
