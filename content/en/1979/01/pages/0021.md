Fresnel - first and second. Although the diameter of the hole in the screen increases, the amplitude of sound vibrations recorded by the microphone decreases significantly! The opening of the third zone again leads to an increase in the amplitude of sound vibrations.

The greatest effect of the amplitude of sound vibrations is found when a zone plate is placed between the sound source and the microphone, in which the first and third Fresnel zones are open, but the second zone is closed.

Zone plate for centimeter electromagnetic waves

All experiments performed with sound waves can also be done with electromagnetic waves in the centimeter range, using the same screen with ring zones cut out in it. To make the screen opaque to electromagnetic waves, it must be covered with metal foil.

In calculating the sizes of Fresnel zones for experiments with sound waves, it was not by chance that a wavelength of 3.2 cm was chosen, since this is precisely the length of the electromagnetic waves emitted by the school microwave microwave generator of electromagnetic waves.

In order for the source and receiver of electromagnetic waves to be close to accurate in their properties, it is necessary to make metal screens with round holes with a diameter of 2-3 cm in the center and attach them to the horns of the generator and receiver antennas.

Corresponding to a sound wavelength in air of 3.2 cm:

$$v = \frac{v}{\lambda} = \frac{340 \text{ m/c}}{3,2 \text{ cm}} = 10,6 \text{ кГц.}$$

By adjusting the voltage at the output of the sound generator and the ULF force, achieve a deflection of the demonstration amperator needle by approximately half the scale.

Place a screen with closed holes between the sound source and the microphone and make sure that sound waves are partially absorbed by the screen - the deflection of the ampere arrow decreases.

Now open the first Fresnel zone. With the correct location of the sound source and microphone relative to the screen with a hole, the amplitude of sound vibrations with one opening turns out to be almost twice the amplitude of vibrations in the Fresnel zone in the absence of a screen!
