Some of those who take the exam are immediately enrolled in the Physics and Mechanics School, while some (approximately 75%) after the exams receive an invitation to a summer training camp and are enrolled based on the results of these summer classes.

Experience shows that 97% of our graduates enter buses, including about 40% at Moscow University and 20% at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.

Mathematics and physics classes at the Moscow Physics School are not limited to studying the school curriculum, and the school itself is not a preparation course for entrance exams in buses. Using simple examples and problems, we strive to introduce students to the thinking style of modern science, to give them the opportunity to perceive the acquired knowledge in mathematics and physics not formally, but with the help of the mechanism by which scientific research is conducted. This is facilitated by independent work - small scientific research carried out by schoolchildren as part of mathematical and physics workshops, in optional special courses and courses. A major role in the formation and development of sustainable interest in research work at the Physics and Mathematics School is played by the scientific society of students, various kinds of conferences for schoolchildren, mathematical and physical bonds, and Olympiads. For 15 years, the FMS team at the All-Union Mathematical Olympiad won 12 first, 25 second and 23 third prizes; Our schools presented at the International Mathematical Olympiads and received 10 first, 6 second and 10 third prizes.
