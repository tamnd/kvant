Physics School at Moscow State University - 15 years

Last year marked 15 years since the creation of the first four physics and mathematics boarding schools - at Novosibirsk, Moscow, Leningrad and Kiev universities. Physics and mathematics boarding schools were established in 1963. Prominent scientists took part in creating the teaching system for these schools: in Moscow - Academician A. N. Kolmogorov and Academician I. K. Kioin, in Novosibirsk - Academician M. A. Lavrentyev, in Kyiv - Academician V. M. Glushkov, in Leningrad - Corresponding Member of the USSR Academy of Sciences D. K. Fadeev. In connection with the use of the details of the educational council of the physics and mathematics boarding school at Moscow State University to A. N. Kolmogorov, the director of this school I. T. Tropin and the head of the mathematics department V. V. Vavilov with a request to tell about her work.

The scientific and technological revolution radically changed the role of science in the life of human society. Science turned into direct productivity force. The unusually rapid development of science requires more and more scientific personnel. That is why the issue of attracting talented young people into science is so relevant. And it is scattered throughout our entire country. And if it is not so difficult to notice it in capital cities, then finding talented teachers capable of independent scientific research in small towns, villages and rural areas is much more difficult.

Physics and mathematics boarding schools at the country's leading universities (in Novosibirsk, Moscow, Leningrad and Kyiv), created 15 years ago, were recognized as practical solutions to this most important state problem.

The hope that real research would emerge from them was largely justified. In particular, of the schoolchildren admitted to the Moscow Physics and Mathematics School in 1963-70, more than two hundred people subsequently completed graduate studies and completed candidate dissertations. Graduates of the boarding school at Moscow State University have published hundreds of scientific papers and made review reports at international conferences and congresses. Among the graduates of the Physics and Mathematics School there are already two Doctors of Physical and Mathematical Sciences. A similar picture is observed in three other FMSs.

In view of the successful four physics and mathematics boarding schools, similar schools have been opened in some other resource communities: Armenia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Lithuania, as well as in some autonomous resource areas of the Russian Federation.

At the Moscow Physics and Mathematics School, 360 schoolchildren study in the ninth and tenth grades. Every year we recruit 150 people for two-year training (in the ninth grade) and 60 people for one-year training (in the tenth grade). Graduates, jointly, of eight or nine classes of training schools living in the central part of the RSFSR, the Urals, the Volga region, the North Caucasus and Belarus are accepted. Some of those who take the exam are immediately enrolled in the Physics and Mechanics School, while some (approximately 75%) after the exams receive an invitation to a summer training camp and are enrolled based on the results of these summer classes.

Experience shows that 97% of our graduates enter buses, including about 40% at Moscow University and 20% at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.

Mathematics and physics classes at the Moscow Physics School are not limited to studying the school curriculum, and the school itself is not a preparation course for entrance exams in buses. Using simple examples and problems, we strive to introduce students to the thinking style of modern science, to give them the opportunity to perceive the acquired knowledge in mathematics and physics not formally, but with the help of the mechanism by which scientific research is conducted. This is facilitated by independent work - small scientific research carried out by schoolchildren as part of mathematical and physics workshops, in optional special courses and courses. A major role in the formation and development of sustainable interest in research work at the Physics and Mathematics School is played by the scientific society of students, various kinds of conferences for schoolchildren, mathematical and physical bonds, and Olympiads. For 15 years, the FMS team at the All-Union Mathematical Olympiad won 12 first, 25 second and 23 third prizes; Our schools presented at the International Mathematical Olympiads and received 10 first, 6 second and 10 third prizes.

Over the past 15 years, the FMS has developed its own traditions, which have created a special atmosphere at the school, where interest in science and the habit of hard work are combined with a passion for literature and music, sports (for example, football, volleyball and basketball meetings between teams of students and teachers have become traditional), and interesting tourist trips.

Not all OLS school graduates become scientists in the fields of mathematics and physics. We are no less proud of those who become good engineers in a particular field. There are also those who choose biology or linguistics as their specialty. But we hope that all FMS graduates recall with satisfaction the atmosphere of creative work and broad cultural interests in our boarding school.

Of course, much more needs to be done to ensure that the Moscow Physics and Music School more successfully fulfills the tasks assigned to it.

Taking this opportunity, we sincerely congratulate the teams of boarding schools in Kyiv, Leningrad, Novosibirsk and our FMS on their fifteenth anniversary and wish them further success.
