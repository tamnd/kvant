SALAD,
INFLOW,
TELESCOPE...

Everyone is familiar with the game of fives.
The game of words - angarmmy, which we want to offer you, has something to do with it.
Plastics with letters can be moved to adjacent free cells (cells that border on a side are considered adjacent).
One move is one movement of the plastic. Guided by this rule, make the following permutations: address - Wednesday (20 moves), atlas - lettuce (22 moves), falcon - ear (36 moves), tropic - tributary (20 moves), order - whim (34 moves), waterfall - supply (42 moves), petal - telescope (48 moves), vertical - wake (64 moves).
In brackets we indicated how many moves it took us to do this. Perhaps you can complete the task faster?
C. Nedvica
