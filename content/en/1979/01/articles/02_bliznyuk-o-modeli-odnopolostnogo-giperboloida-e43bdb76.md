⟦folio none⟧

On the first page of the cover you see a model of a one-sheet hyperbolon. It can be glued together from paper rosettes like these. True, they should have not 16 folds, but 32.
The rosette is made from a sector of a circle. Before making a rosette, you need to select the following parameters - corner rosette (it can be larger than a full circle!), radius of the circle, radius less than the circle (it determines the size of the teeth) and finally, decide what fold to make. In order for the rosettes to be glued together to form a hyperbolond, their dimensions must be in a certain way according to. Try to come up with a theory for calculating sockets.
(For information about what a “mathematical” single-sheet hyperbolond is, see p. 26.)
A. Bliznik
