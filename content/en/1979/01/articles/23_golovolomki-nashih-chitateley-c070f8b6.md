Puzzles
our readers

1. In the circles of this figure (Fig. 1), arrange 13 consecutive numbers so that all six sums of the four numbers located at the vertices of the rhombuses are the same

Fig. 1.

and would be equal to 1977. It is known that one of these numbers is 496, and that it is in the central circle.

2. In the circles of the figure shown on figure 2, arrange two consecutive natural numbers so that the sums of the numbers are in the greatest and in the least

Fig. 2.

The circles around the small circle, as well as at the vertices of the selected central square, were identical and equal to sixty.

Ya. Alekseev
(Leningrad)

Answers, directions, solutions

Construction of the tract
(see «Kvant», 1978, No. 12, 3rd cover page)

The section of the tract from Moutonville to Albihouk is equally used by the inhabitants of all five villages; therefore, each village is due $\frac{1}{5}$ for the cost of constructing this site. The section from Albihuk to Bondi is shared by residents of four villages, so each of them pays $\frac{1}{4}$ of its value. So, village residents will have to pay 24, 54, 94, 154 and 274 louis, respectively (see table; villages are indicated in it by the first letters of their name).

As a fraction of the cost of the plot
A $\frac{1}{5} = \frac{12}{60}$ 24
B $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{27}{60}$ 54
B $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{47}{60}$ 94
G $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{77}{60}$ 154
D $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} + 1 = \frac{137}{60}$ 274
