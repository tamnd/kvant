Introductory test assignment at VZMSH in 1979

1. Let points A, B, C be the vertices of an unequal right triangle. In how many ways can a point D be placed on a plane so that any set of points {A, B, C, D} has: a) a center of symmetry? b) axis of symmetry? c) do the answers to questions a) and 6) change if in the task conditions we replace the words “rectangular” with the words “non-rectangular”?

2. Let us consider all possible nine-digit numbers in which each of the digits 1, 2, 3, ..., 9 appears once. Determine the most common divisor of these numbers.

3. Is it possible to shade 25 squares on checkered paper so that each of them has an odd number of shaded neighbors? (Cells are called neighbors if they have a common side.)

4. Andrey runs faster than Viti, but slower than Zhenya. They simultaneously ran along a circular path from one place in one direction and continued until all three again ran to the same place. During this time, Zhenya overtook Vitya 13 times. How many overtakings were there during this time?

5. 25 numbers were written on one line. The sum of any three adjacent numbers is positive. Can the sum of all 25 numbers be negative?

6. Find all pairs of integers (m; n) for which the equality $m^2+1=2^n$ holds.

7. Let us consider a polygon on the plane, which is the union of three parallelograms: OAHB, OBKC and OCMA. a) Is this polygon necessarily convex? b) How many sides can this polygon have? b) What perimeter can this polygon have if $|OA|+|OB|+|OC|=1$?

8. Two positive integers are guessed. Mathematician A is told their sum, and mathematician B is told the sum of their squares. If A and B combine their knowledge, then they, of course, determine the total number. But they learned these numbers during the following conversation:

B. - I don’t know what numbers these are.
A. — Their sum is more than ten.
B. - Then I know what these numbers are. Find these numbers too.

9. Is it possible to draw a rectangle ABCD and a point M on a plane so that the distances from point M to points A, B, C, D are equal, corresponding to a) 1, 2, 3, 4; b) 1, 4, 8, 7?

10. There are two machines. If you throw 5 kopecks into the first of them, then he will multiply the number given to him by 3. If you throw 2 kopecks into the second of them, then he will add the number 4 to the number given to him. What is the minimum amount of money you need to have in order to use these machines to get 1 number 1979 from a given number?

J. Rabb

Correspondence School of Physics and Technology

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Order of the Red Banner of Labor Physics and Technology Institute (MIPT) is enrolling eight-year-old and secondary school students located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 8, 9 and 10 for the 1979/80 academic year. (In some cases, admission from other allied resources is allowed.) Correspondence education instills independence skills, teaches how to work with additional literature, and express one’s thoughts in a concise manner.

ZFTS provides good additional knowledge in physics and mathematics to its graduates, many of whom become students of the leading challenges of our country.

When applying for admission to the ZFTS, students are expected to live in rural areas and workers' settlements, where our school especially needs help.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, ZFTS also accepts physics and technology circles, which can be organized on site at the initiative of two teachers - physics and mathematics. The leaders of the circle recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the introductory task of the ZFTS. The circle is accepted into the ZFTS if the school director informs the ZFTS of the names of the circle leader and the posted list of the circle by class (indicating the final grades for the introductory task).

Students admitted to the ZFTS and heads of physics and technology circles will be regulated to receive tasks in physics and mathematics in accordance with the ZFTS program, as well as the solutions recommended by the ZFTS for these tasks. ZFTSH tasks contain theoretical material and analysis of typical tasks and examples on the topic, as well as 10-14 tasks for independent solution. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). The work of animal education students is checked by the ZFTSH or its branches, and the members of the circles are checked by their leaders.

Twice a week, Moscow students receive a lot of lessons in physics and mathematics under the ZFTSH program at evening advisory centers (in a number of Moscow schools). Recruitment to these points occurs either based on the results of the introductory task of the ZFTSH, or on the basis of a final interview in physics and mathematics (the interview will be held in September; inquiries by phone 216-00-05, ext. 2-59).

Introductory test work in physics and mathematics, each student performs independent work in Russian and carefully rewrites it into one school terrace. The order of the task should be the same as in the assignment. The terraces were transferred in a large envelope, viewed by a balderol. Along with the decision, you are required to submit a certificate from the school where you are a teacher, indicating your class. The certificate is placed on the inside cover of the terradi. Without this certificate, the decision will not be considered. On the outside of the terradi, glue a sheet of paper filled in according to the following pattern:

1. Region (region or ASSR)
2. Last name, first name, patronymic
3. Class
4. Number and address of schools
5. Profession parent and position father
6. Detailed home address

The deadline for sending the decision is no later than March 1, 1979 (according to the postmark of the place of departure). Entry papers will not be sent back.

Admission to the school is carried out by the admissions committee of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. The decision of the selection committee will be communicated no later than August 1, 1979.

Send the test papers with solved problems of the introductory test (required in physics and mathematics) to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology, for ZFTSH.

Assignment for the introductory test at ZFTSH in 1979

Physics

1. The path in the garden is 2 m wide. How long can the path be covered with a layer of sand 2 cm thick if the sand is delivered by a ten-ton truck loaded to the limit? Determine the density of the sand yourself.

2. Determine the depth of the shaft, at the bottom of which the barometer shows a pressure of 860 mm Hg. Art. Take the pressure at the surface of the earth equal to 760 mm Hg. Art. Air density 1.29 kg/m³. Changes in air density with depth in the pre-tenberium.

3. Break the match into as small pieces as possible. Why are small pieces harder to break than large ones?

4. How much charcoal will be required to turn 3 kg of ice into steam at normal pressure? The initial ice temperature is 5°C. Heater efficiency is 20%.

5. The electric motor driving the pump is connected to a 220 V network. The pump supplies 500 m³ of water to a height of 20 m. How much electricity flows through the winding of the electric motor if the efficiency of the installation (moving with the pump) is 40%?

6. MotoshikLIST covered the distance between two points at a speed of 40 km/h. Having increased the speed to 80 km/h, motoshikLIST then covered half the distance. Determine the average speed of the motorcycle LIST for the entire period of movement.

7. Water is poured into a cylindrical jar. When an empty aluminum cup was lowered into the jar so that it floated, the water rose by 2.7 cm. How much will the water level in the jar change if the cup is drowned in it? Aluminum density 2.7 g/cm³.

8. A man drops a stone into a deep well and hears the sound of its fall after 8 s. Find the depth of the deck. Acceleration of free fall is assumed to be 10 m/s². Speed ​​of sound 330 m/s.

9. What power does a person develop when pushing a loaded sleigh with a total mass of 40 kg along a horizontal road? The friction coefficient of the runners on the road is 0.1. The sled is pulled at a constant speed of 3 m/s using a rope inclined at an angle of 30° to the horizontal.

10. A skier slides down from a springboard 50 m high, which has a curve at the foot with a radius of 10 m. Find the overload experienced by the skier when entering a horizontal section.

11. A balloon of mass $M$ floats in the air at a height $H$. On the stairs, when taking the balloon gondola, there is a person with mass $m$. How high is the balloon if a person climbs $n$ steps up the stairs? Distance between steps $h$.

12. In a room with a volume of 60 m³, a drop of two containing 10⁻⁴ g of an aromatic substance with a relative molecular weight of 1000 was evaporated. How many molecules of an aromatic substance will enter the human lungs with each breath? The lung volume is assumed to be 2.2 liters.

13. In a sealed state, a volume of 11.2 liters contains air under a pressure of 0.1 MPa. What amount of heat must be added to the air so that the pressure in the vessel increases 3 times? The molar heat of air at constant volume is taken to be 21 J/(mol K).

Matematika

1. There are 35 students in the class. Of these, 20 attend a mathematical circle, 11 attend a physical circle, 10 students do not attend any of these circles. How many students attend both circles?

2. In order for the quadrilateral $ABCD$ to be a parallelogram, it is necessary and sufficient that it have a center of symmetry. Prove it.

3. Determine all values ​​of $a$ for which the set of solutions to the inequality $x^2 - a (1 + a) x + a^3 < 0$ contains the interval [0; 1].

4. Prove or disprove the following statements: a) for the number $(1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dots \cdot (n - 1)) - 1$ to be divisible by $n$, it is sufficient that $n$ be prime; b) for the number $(1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dots \cdot (n - 1)) - 1$ to be divisible by $n$, it is necessary that $n$ be prime.

5. One quadrilateral is located inside another quadrilateral. Can the sum of the lengths of the diagonals inside a quadrilateral be greater than the sum of the lengths of the diagonals inside a quadrilateral?

6. There were three tasks on one mid: $A, B, C$. 25 schoolchildren solved at least one problem. Among the schoolchildren who did not solve problem $A$, those who solved problem $B$ were twice as many as those who solved problem $C$. There are one more schoolchildren who solved only problem $A$ than other schoolchildren who solved problem $A$. How many schoolchildren solved only problem $B$, if among the schoolchildren who solved only one problem, half did not solve problem $A$?

7. The points $D$ and $E$ serve as the midpoint of the sides $[AB]$ and $[CD]$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$. Prove that

$$\overrightarrow{DE} = \frac{\overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{AD}}{2}.$$

8. Two statements are given:
a) System
$$\begin{cases} (a + 4) x + 3y = a + 1, \\ ax + (a - 1) y = a - 1 \end{cases}$$

has an infinite number of solutions;

6) The lines defined by the equations $5x + 4y = 6$ and $ax + 6y = 10$ intersect in the second fourth Cartesian line of the coordinate system.

For what values ​​of $a$ is one of these statements false and the other true?

9. Prove that if an arbitrary quadrilateral $ABCD$ contains internal bissertrices, then the four intersection points of the bissertrices of the angles $A$ and $C$ with the bissertrices of the angles $B$ and $D$ lie on the same circle.

10. A schoolboy switches all his stamps into a new album. If he sticks 20 stamps on one sheet, then he will not have enough album, and if he sticks 23 stamps on one sheet, then at least one sheet will be empty. If a student gives the same album, with 21 stamps pasted on each sheet, then he will have a total of 500 stamps. How many sheets are there in an album?

11. On each of the planets of some systems there is an astronomer observing a close nearby planet. The distances between the planes are different in pairs. Prove that if there is an odd number of floats, then no one observes any float.

12. Given $k$ numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k$, with $a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_k = 0$.

Calculate the limit of the sequence $(S_n)$:

$$S_n = a_1 \sqrt{n + 1} + a_2 \sqrt{n + 2} + \dots + a_k \sqrt{n + k}.$$

13. The functions $f$ and $g$ are not arbitrary at the point $x = x_0$. Is it possible to say that the function $F_1(x) = f(x) + g(x)$ also has no arbitrary ones at the point $x = x_0$?
