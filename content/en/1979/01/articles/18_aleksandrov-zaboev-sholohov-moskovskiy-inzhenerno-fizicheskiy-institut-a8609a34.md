Moscow Engineering Physics Institute

Below are the options for the written entrance exam in mathematics and the tasks for the oral exam in physics in 1978.

Mathematics

Option 1
1. Sea water contains 5% salt by weight. How much fresh water must be added to 30 kg of sea water so that the salt concentration (by mass) is p%?
2. The basis of the parameters SABCD is the rectangle ABCD, (AB) || (CD), (BC) || (AD), |AB|=3, |BC|=4. All side edges make equal angles with the base plane. Determine the angle between lines (BS) and (CS) if the radius of the sphere described by the parameters is 6.5.
3. Find the area of ​​the figure bounded by the straight line y = -8x-46 and the parabola y = 4x² + ax + 2, if it is known that the tangent to the parabola at the point x = -5 makes an angle l-arg 20 with the Ox axis.
4. Solve the equation
$$\cos 5x \mathrm{tg} 6 |x| + \sin 5x = 0.$$

Option 2
1. Find the sum of all three-digit natural numbers that, when divided by 3, leave a remainder of 2.
2. The SABC parameters are based on the equilateral triangle ABC
$$|AB|=|AC|=a, \quad ABC=\varphi.$$ The straight line (AS) makes an angle of magnitude α with the plane of the base parameters, the plane of the side face (BSC) makes an angle of magnitude β with the same plane and SAC=SAB. Find the volume of parameters KSLC if it is known that points K and L belong to edges [AS] and [BS], respectively, and the area of ​​triangle KSL is related to the area of ​​triangle ABS as 4:25.
3. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \log \frac{1}{3}(x+y) + \log_3(x-y) = 2, \\ 2^{y^2} = 512^{x+1}. \end{cases}$$

Physics

1. A board of mass $M=500$ g floats on water. There is a frog sitting at one end of the dock at point $A$. At what minimum speed must she jump to get to point $B$ on the dock, located $l=25$ cm from point $A$? The mass of the frog is $m=150$ g. Neglect the tension between the dock and the water.

2. In a cylinder with a cross-sectional area $S=5,0$ cm$^2$ under a piston of mass $M=1,0$ kg there is a certain gas. When the absolute temperature of the gas increases by $n=1,5$ times, the piston rises up and rests against the ledges (Fig. 1). In this case, the volume of gas increases by $k=1,2$ times compared to the initial one. Determine the force with which the piston presses on the ledges. Atmospheric pressure $p_0=100$ kPa.

3. Fixed exact charges $q_1=3 \cdot 10^{-6}$ Kl and $q_2=5 \cdot 10^{-6}$ Kl are located at the adjacent vertices of the square on the side $a=0,9$ m (Fig. 2). Find the work done by the electric forces on the charge.

Charge $q=10^{-8}$ Kl when moving it from vertex $C$ to vertex $D$.

4. A long solenode containing $N=200$ turns with an area $S=1,6$ cm$^2$ is connected to a battery with an emf of $\theta=0,8$ V (Fig. 3). The resistance of the solenoid winding and the internal resistance of the battery are negligible. On the solenoid above its conductive ring with a resistance of $R=0,6$ Ohm. Find the magnetic field induction in the solenoid and the amount of heat released in the ring at time $\tau=0,06$ s after connecting the battery.

5. A parallel light beam is incident normally on the face of a glass biprism ($n=1,5$) with a small refractive angle $\alpha=10^{-2}$ rad (Fig. 4). Behind the biprism there is a thin collecting lens with a focal length $F=100$ cm. Find the distance $x$ between the points of convergence of the rays in the focal plane of the lens. Count $tg\alpha\approx sin\alpha\approx\alpha$.
