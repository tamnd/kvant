Tornado models

A tornado is one of the most hail-producing and mysterious natural phenomena. The energy of his present is so great that almost no one and nothing can withstand a fight with a tornado.

How does a tornado carry heavy objects sometimes over considerable distances? How is it formed? Modern science is not able to give comprehensive answers to these and many other questions.

On the pages of our magazine I talk about the nature of tornadoes (see the article by L. Alekseeva “Whirlwinds that “make the weather” - «Kvant», 1977, No. 8). 1. To qualify a microelectric motor of the DP-12a type (it is used in many children's toys), use a disk made of brass or tin with a diameter of 40 mm and a thickness of 0.5-1 mm. The disk must be strengthened strictly perpendicular to the shaft so that no beating occurs when it rotates. To seal the motor, lubricate the bearings in which the shaft rotates with solid or thick SEL, and cover the motor contacts to which the conductors are attached with a layer of plasticine.

At the bottom of a glass (or glass jar) with a diameter of 9 cm and a height of 18 cm, stick a plasticine cake about 5 mm thick, and attach the microelectric motor on it so that its shaft does not touch the plasticine. Secure the conductors, induce from the engine, to the wall of the glass with adhesive tape or plasticine. figure 1 shows the setup ready for experiments.

Pour water into the glass, and on top of it - a layer of sunflower oil 1-2 cm thick. Connect the leads of the microelectric motor to the flashlight battery - the disk will begin to rotate, and the liquid in the glass will also begin to rotate. After some time, the interface between water and oil begins to push downwards, the water tank will fill with oil, which
