⟦folio 60⟧

New books

In this issue we publish brief summaries of books on mathematics and physics published in the third quarter of 1979.

Mathematics

Publishing house "Nuka"
1. Grzegorczyk A. Popular locika. Translation from Polish 3rd edition. Volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The book by Andrzej Grzegorczyk is intended to satisfy the growing interest in formal logic of people who are not specialists in either mathematics or logic. Nowadays, this branch of science has acquired the most important applied significance. The theory of electronic digital machines and other “smart” automata, the study of the structure of language, deep philosophical questions of the foundations of mathematics and other sciences - this is the scope of application of formal logic. Formal logic is a science that establishes general methods (rules, schemes) for correct inferences. Inferences play a more important role in mathematics: all mathematical theories are based on precise proofs based on the derivation of the following mathematical aspects. Therefore, it is not surprising that scientists discovered the most schemes (methods) for correct inferences when analyzing mathematical reasoning. Because of this, modern formal logic is called mathematical logic, since first of all it studies mathematical reasoning. She does this using logical symbols; Therefore, modern formulaic logic is also called statement symbols. Using her example, the author shows what modern logical schemes of the right statement are based on, and the mathematical analysis of the game of fifteen.

The book can be used both for independent study by secondary school students and by teachers as the basis of an elective course.

3. Ershov A.P. Three lessons in algeb. Volume 5 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 20 kopecks.
This book is intended for the first initial acquaintance with the algorithm and contains all the necessary information to “read” algebra. The presentation is carried out in the following sequence: the first lesson is a complex description of the basic constructions of the language; the second lesson is a presentation of special cases and exceptions to the general rules of the first lesson; the third lesson is examples, or constructing the most important cases of using certain constructions.

The book can be useful to anyone who wants to study algebe.

4. Saltykov A. I., Selyushko G. A. Programming for everyone. Volume 10 l., circulation 100,000 copies, price 40 kopecks.
The book outlines the basics of computer programming. Issues related to the representation of solutions to mathematical problems in the form of algorithms and programs are considered. Basic examples of programming and construction of algorithms.

The book is intended for a wide range of readers and can be used as an independent book on programming.

Publishing house "Prosveshcheniye"
5. Berezina L. Yu. Graphs and their application. Volume 9 l., circulation 40,000 copies, price 30 kopecks.
The author introduces the reader to the basic definitions, methods and applications of graph theory. The presentation, selection of exercises and applications gives a decent complete understanding of the main ideas and methods of graph theory and its most interesting applications.
