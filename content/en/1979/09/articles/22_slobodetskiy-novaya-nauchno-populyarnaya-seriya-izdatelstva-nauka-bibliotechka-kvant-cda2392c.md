New popular science series from the Nauka publishing house - Library «Kvant»

In 1980, by decision of the Presidium of the USSR Academy of Sciences, the Main Editorial Board of the Physics and Mathematics Literature of the Nauka Publishing House will begin publishing a new series of popular science books—Libraries «Kvant». To manage the series, a special editorial department has been created, headed by academicians I. K. Kikonn and A. N. Kolmogorov.

The books published in this series will be intended primarily for schoolchildren - high school students. But we are confident that the series will be a synthesis that will be welcomed by students, teachers, university and technical teachers, as well as everyone who is interested in natural sciences and their history.

The title of the series exposes the commonality of objectives of this series and our magazine. This is, first of all, instilling patriotism, a sense of pride in responsible science, awakening interest in the pursuit of natural sciences and providing assistance in these pursuits, broadening one’s horizons, instilling the ability to think logically, etc.

The Library «Kvant» will publish new books specially written for this series, as well as reprint the best popular books on natural sciences by Soviet and foreign authors. Individual issues will be compiled from materials from the magazine «Kvant». Introducing the first books in this series. They will be released in 1980.

The book “Everyday Life in a Home Laboratorium” will be devoted to the description of projects, but interesting and educational experiments that can be done at home, using the necessary equipment. It is compiled from the best articles published in 1970-1979 in the Laboratory «Kvant». We hope that this book will help our readers to conduct their first independent physical experiments.

The second book - “Remarkable Scientists” - is compiled from articles published in our magazine dedicated to outstanding scientists: Copernicus, Kepler, Newton, Laplace, Ampere, Stoletov, Kovalevskaya, Lebedev, etc. These articles talk about the life and work of these scientists, how these works were done, what made it possible to carry out certain experiments, what improvements were made to the technical experiment on which these scientists based their hypotheses.

No doubt many readers will be interested in the three books that will be reissued in 1980. This is, first of all, the book by M. Faraday “The History of a Candle”. She is already over 100 years old. The book contains lectures given by the great English physicist for children. In it, the author talks about a candle, talks about capitular phenomena, and about the shedding of gases, and about chemical reactions, and about many other phenomena. During his lectures, Faraday showed many interesting experiments using balls, plates, toys, etc. Many of these experiments will be of interest to the modern reader. In addition to the book, written by Doctor of Physics and Mathematics V.V. Novozhivym, he talks about modern ideas about combustion.

The second book is called "Atoms and Electrons." This is the first part of a very interesting book, “Atoms, Electrons, Nuclei,” written about 40 years ago by the famous Soviet physicist and dedicated popularizer M. P. Bronstein. It is among the best popular science literature. It talks about temperature changes, as well as such interesting objects as stars, quasars, “black holes”, etc.
