⟦folio 34⟧

N. Mikhailova

Capacities

I'm not interested in going to class today. We repeat in arithmetic what we have learned. You need to insert a “greater than” or “less than” icon. This is how I draw a check, only then I turn the icon upside down - not like > or <, but like ∨. And we also count the circles - how many tens, how many units. Where there are whole dozens there are white circles, and where the steward has not reached tens there are red circles. But my dad and I have already been through all this, because my mother told him to “train” me. “Train” is when they explain arithmetic for a long time. Dad coached me and coached me and said: “I don’t understand what mom wants from us. You understand everything with me.

That's enough for today. Here's another task for you. Think about it for a couple of days. Once upon a time, a little boy like you solved it. Then he became a great scientist. Only he decided right away, but you won’t pull it off. But think, think, move your brains, this will make them bigger.”

I thought and thought, but haven’t come up with anything yet, and now the class is bothering me. Everyone is muttering something, like a distant train: “Boo-6-6, chuh-chukh-chukh.”
“Open the problem, Zhenya Matveev, otherwise you know what you’re doing,” said Zinaida Ivanovna.

I have it open, but not where everyone else has it, but where Palina’s task lies. I wiped it off in the evening, solved it, but I really wanted to sleep. I lay down, and the word “container” from the problem was stuck in my head. A beautiful word.

A container is a bucket, a pan, and a basin, even ugly, unenamelled ones. And a tin can is also a container. The capacity was just too much for me, so when I woke up, I could barely make it. And my grandmother said that at my age this is already a shame.

And this is the task. Two containers are given: 3 liters and 5 liters - and a milk tank. How to drink 4 liters of milk. I love milk. Mom says - I went to her. When there was war and there was no milk, she kept dreaming of becoming a milkmaid and drinking it all day long.

Still strange! How can it be poured from a tank without a tap?
