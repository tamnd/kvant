⟦folio 44⟧

Introducing a new section

Dear readers! Today in the magazine «Kvant» a new section “The Art of Programming” is opening.

You, of course, know what an important place electronic computers (computers) now occupy in science and other areas of human activity. The ability to operate a computer is necessary for practically everyone - mathematicians and linguists, geologist and librarians, physicists and biologists, engineers and economists, chemists and doctors.

The purpose of the new section is to teach you how to work with a computer, teach you the language of machines, explain what programming is, teach you how to write programs for a computer. By reading the article in this section, you will learn how computers work and where they are used, what an algorithm and algorithmic languages ​​are.

Programming grew out of mathematics and serves it. Therefore, the new section appears precisely on the pages of the magazine «Kvant».

In addition to the article on computer technology and programming, from September to May issue «Kvant» in the section “The Art of Programming” assignments from the Correspondence School of Programming will be published.
