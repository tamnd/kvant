PROBLEM ABOUT A TOURIST

The archipelago includes seven islands (Fig. 1). Due to the reefs off the coast, piers for ships are only available on islands 2, 4, 5, 6, 7. The archipelago has a ferry system, shown in figure. What route to visit all the islands does a tourist who uses only ferries to move from one island to another need, in order to visit each island only once, if after visiting the last of the islands he leaves the archipelago?

A. Nikko Norenkov
