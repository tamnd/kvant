Computer programs

Now is the time to ask what the special role of the computer is in this program world. The fact is that it was computers, with their appearance and existence, that made programs the subject of mathematical research and created programming as the most widespread mathematical profession.

Mathematicians and philosophers, in collaboration with biologists and engineers, have convincingly shown that the laws of control and information processing, algorithms and programs related to biological systems, on the one hand, and to automatic machines, on the other, are structured in the same way; the same mathematical abstractions and theories are suitable for them. Computers, thanks to their enormous speed and ability to execute any program, make it possible in principle to reproduce any “correct” action. This makes the computer not only our obedient and oaring helper, but also a judge of the canvases of our knowledge about the action that we want to transfer to the machine.

Let's return to our two-command program, which, as readers will probably agree, calculates the greatest common divisor of two numbers. In order for a computer to execute this program, it must, of course, “know,” for example, what it means to “subtract a smaller number from a larger one.” At the same time, she will always and successfully work to obtain a result, without having any idea what the greatest common divisor is. But the programmer, the rich program, must know very well what the machine will do and what properties the corresponding program has.

Task. Prove that the above program for any pair of original natural numbers produces a result equal to their greatest common division.

So, the program asks our complete knowledge about the problem, and, unlike a theorem or essay, in an active, repeatedly voluntary form. A machine powered by this program will calculate the greatest common divisor as well as the wisest schoolboy who has mastered argumentation. It is in the constant transfer of human ability to computers to consistently perform purposeful operations that the essence of the scientific and technological revolution associated with the automation of mental work lies.
