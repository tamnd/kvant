In the April issue «Kvant» we offered the following task to junior schoolchildren (see p. 97):

Paralelodrami is composed of three equilibrium triangles, among which there are no congestible ones. Find the values ​​of its nodes.

In the fifth issue of the journal, we indicated two solutions to this problem: parallelodra with an acute angle of 72° and parallelodra with an acute angle $\frac{360^\circ}{7}$, while introducing three figure with possible partitions (see p. 63). As reader A. Goldberg from Leningrad rightly pointed out to us, the problem has not two, but five solutions: parallelodramas with acute angles 36°, 72°, $\frac{180^\circ}{7}$, $\frac{360^\circ}{7}$ and $\frac{540^\circ}{7}$. We will apologize to the readers and, without giving a complete solution to the problem (this is a rather boring enumeration of many situations), we place only figure.
