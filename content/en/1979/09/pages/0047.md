The correspondence school is organized by the Computing Center of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences (CC SB USSR Academy of Sciences) and the editorial office of the journal «Kvant».
The school accepts students from grades 6 to 10. The full course of the school is designed for 3 years, so students in the 6th, 7th and 8th grades are primarily invited. By doing homework, schoolchildren will learn to write computer programs and master several programming languages.

However, solving a problem by machine is not really just about writing a program. It is very important to run a written program on a computer and “debug” it—to find all the errors and mistakes in the program and remove them. How can the reader be helped in launching and debugging a program if he does not yet have a computer at home or even at his school? For this purpose, the Correspondence School of Programming has its own correspondence computing center, the address of which is indicated below. To this address you can send the programs you have compiled and receive in response not only a letter from the program that read your message, but also (this is the most important thing) interesting and useful.

The best students from our school will be invited to the summer programming school in Akademgorodok near Novosibirsk, where they will already become familiar with modern computers.

Those who complete the full course of the Correspondence School will receive a special certificate.

We also invite ninth and tenth graders to our school: although the first lessons may seem very simple to them, and they will not complete the three-year course, we are sure that they will find a lot of interesting and useful things for themselves here.
