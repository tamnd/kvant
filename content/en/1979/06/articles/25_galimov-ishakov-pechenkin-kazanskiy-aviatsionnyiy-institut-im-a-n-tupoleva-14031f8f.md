Kazan Aviation Institute named after. A. N. Tupolev

Kazan Order of the Red Banner of Labor Aviation Institute named after. A. N. Tupolev has six faculties of full-time education:

Faculty of Aircraft, the oldest department of the institute, created in 1932, training engineers specializing in: aircraft, aircraft, flight dynamics and aircraft control.

The Faculty of Propulsion Apparatus, created in 1939, has the most qualified teaching staff, which conducts training in four

repair specialties. Graduates of the faculty occupy high management positions in the aviation and other industries, in scientific organizations and in higher educational institutions.

The Faculty of Automotive Engineering, opened in 1970, trains engineers in four specializations: motor vehicles and tractors, engine internal structure, metalworking and tools, machines and pressure metal processing technology. Graduates of the faculty are sent to work in the design and technological departments of machine-building and automobile assembly plants.

The Faculty of Aircraft Authorization and Processing Systems was created in 1951. It trains engineers in the following specializations: hyprosoptic instruments and devices, aviation instrumentation, aircraft electrical equipment, aviation and automotive electrical equipment. Faculty specialists apply their knowledge not only in aviation and practical technology, but also in technical lobbies of moving objects, in the design, research, operation of devices, information systems and kits for plotting and navigation parameters.

The Faculty of Radio Engineering, created in 1951, trains electrical engineers with specializations: radio engineering, radio electronic devices and systems, design and production of radio equipment.

Faculty of Computing and Control Systems: computers, design and production of electronic computing equipment, automated control systems, applied mathematics.

All faculties of the institute have student design bureaus (SKB), where students take part in research work. For example, in the Special Design Bureau of the Faculty of Aircraft, students design and build gliders, gliders, gliders and other aircraft.
