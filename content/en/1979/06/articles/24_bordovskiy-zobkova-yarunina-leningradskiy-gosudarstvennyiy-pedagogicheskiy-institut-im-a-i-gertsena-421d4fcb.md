Leningrad State Pedagogical Institute named after. A. I. Herzen

Leningrad Order of Labor Red Replacement State Pedagogical Institute named after. A.I. Herzen is one of the largest pedagogical institutes in the Soviet Union. It includes 15 faculties, 71 departments, which cover the community of modern scientific knowledge from pedagogy and psychology to computational mathematics and astronomy.

The cycle of physical and mathematical disciplines underlies the training of students in the following three faculties:

The Faculty of Physics trains secondary school teachers in two specialties: physics and astronomy, physics with the right to teach in a foreign language. The faculty consists of 4 departments: general and experimental physics, theoretical physics and astronomy, physical electronics and methods of teaching physics.

The Faculty of Mathematics trains teachers in the following specialties: mathematics and mathematics with the right to teach in a foreign language. Training at the Faculty of Organization in 5 departments: algebra, geometry, mathematical analysis, computational mathematics and methods of teaching mathematics. Students study not only the necessary physical and mathematical, pedagogical, general education and general political disciplines, but also, of their own choice, attend special courses on the most advanced issues of modern mathematical science.

The Faculty of Mathematics has a computer center where students not only get acquainted with modern computing technology, but also acquire computer skills.

In 1979, a general technical faculty was opened at the institute. During 4 years of study he will train teachers of technical disciplines, drawing and labor. Along with the cycle of general political and psychological disciplines, students of the faculty will study mathematics and physics as part of the program of technical universities. General technical training includes disciplines of three cycles: mechanical engineering, electrical training and materials processing.

The work of a teacher of technical disciplines is a difficult, but interesting and exciting job. It gives more satisfaction and opportunity to everyone who has a penchant for teaching, loves technology, is interested in technical creativity, creating layouts and models, or wants to learn this.
