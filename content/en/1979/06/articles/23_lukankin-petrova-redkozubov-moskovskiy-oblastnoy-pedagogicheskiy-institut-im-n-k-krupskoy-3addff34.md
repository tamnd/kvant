The faculty has created all the conditions for developing the creative skills of a future mathematics teacher: more than 70 specialists and specialists in mathematics and pedagogical disciplines are offered daily to 3-5 year students to choose from; students write coursework and dissertations, REFERENCES on various mathematical and pedagogical topics. The future teacher acquires his first skills in research work in clubs and scientific seminars in the student scientific society system, starting from the first year of study.

The best students of the faculty are recommended for postgraduate studies at the departments in order to become researchers or teachers at pedagogical universities after graduation.

The faculty has 7 departments: mathematical analysis, higher algebra, geometry, number theory, computational mathematics and programming, methods of teaching mathematics, physics.

The Faculty of Mathematics has a computer center where students get acquainted with modern computing technology, acquire skills in working with electronic computers and undergo special practice as a program.

Upon admission to the faculty, the following entrance exams will be taken: mathematics (written and oral), physics (oral), literature and Russian language (written).

In 1978, at the Faculty of Mathematics, applicants with a certificate average of 5.0 or 4.5 were admitted to the faculty after the first two exams (mathematics written and oral), if they scored at least 9 points on them.

Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute named after. N.K. Krupskaya was organized on the basis of pedagogical technical and vocational education. Profinentna in 1931. Currently, teacher training at the institute is carried out at 10 faculties and 34 departments, which employ 48 professors and doctors of science, over 250 associate professors and candidates of science.

The Faculty of Mathematics prepares mathematics teachers for secondary schools. Three departments of the faculty provide postgraduate training in the following specialties: mathematical analysis, methods of teaching mathematics, geometry and topology, algebra and number theory.

The Physics Faculty trains physics teachers, and the Industrial Pedagogical Faculty prepares teachers of social pedagogical discipline and labor. The departments of these faculties have postgraduate courses in the following specialties: mathematical analysis, experimental physics, theoretical physics, methods of teaching physics and labor training.

1. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the lines $y = \frac{5}{x}, y = 6 - x$.

Physics
Oral exam objectives

1. To take off, the aircraft must reach a coastal speed of $|\vec{v}| = 250$ km/h. How long does acceleration take if this speed is reached at the end of a green lane $l = 1,0$ km long? What is the acceleration of the plane? What is the average speed of the plane over this segment? The movement of the aircraft is considered to be uniformly accelerated.

2. A car with a mass of $m_1 = 3 \cdot 10^4$ kg, moving along a horizontal track at a speed of $|\vec{v}| = 1,5$ m/s, automatically engages on the move with a stationary mass of $m_2 = 2 \cdot 10^4$ kg. At what speed will a mass of $m = 3000$ t move with a constant speed of $|\vec{v}| = 72$ km/h if the friction coefficient is $\mu = 0,005$?

3. What work does an electric locomotive do in $t = 10$ min, moving a train weighing $m = 3000$ t along a horizontal track at a constant speed $|\vec{v}| = 72$ km/h, if the friction coefficient is $\mu = 0,005$?

4. In a vessel containing $m_1 = 400$ g of water at a temperature of $t_1 = 17^\circ C$, $m_2 = 10$ g of steam is introduced at $t_2 = 100^\circ C$, which turns into water. Determine the final water temperature. Neglect the volume of the vessel and the losses of the body. The specific heat of water is $c = 4200$ J/(kg K), the specific heat of vaporization of water is $L = 2,26 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg.

5. What temperature is established in a brass calorite weighing $m_1 = 160$ g, containing $m_2 = 400$ g of water at a temperature of $t_1 = 25^\circ C$, after a piece of ladd placed in water weighing $m_3 = 50$ g, taken at $t_2 = 0^\circ C$, melts? The specific heats of brass and water correspond to $c_1 = 3,8 \times 10^2$ J/(kg K) and $c_2 = 4,2 \cdot 10^3$ J/(kg K); specific heat of vaporization of water $\lambda = 3,35 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg.

6. With what force do two charges $q_1 = 0,66 \cdot 10^{-7}$ C and $q_2 = 1,1 \cdot 10^{-5}$ C interact in water ($\epsilon = 81$) at a distance of $r = 3,3$ cm? At what distance should they be placed in a vacuum so that the interaction force remains the same?

7. An electron, moving in a vacuum along the plum line of the electric field, completely loses its speed between points with a potential difference of $U = 400$ V. Determine what the speed of the electron was when it entered the electric field.

8. The emf of a flashlight battery is $G = 4,5$ V. With an external resistance of $R = 12$ Ohm, the current in the circuit is $I = 0,3$ A. Determine the internal resistance of the battery and the voltage drop across it.

9. Find the focal length of the lens if it is known that the actual image of an object located at a distance of $d = 30$ cm from the lens is located at the same distance from it.

10. The maximum kinetic energy of electrons emitted from a ruby ​​when it is illuminated by ultrafüleg beams with a length of $λ = 3,17 \cdot 10^{-7}$ m is equal to $W = 2,84 \cdot 10^{-19}$ J. Determine the electron work function and the red limit of the photoelectric coefficient for rubidium. Planck's constant $h = 6,62 \cdot 10^{-34}$ J s.
