⟦folio 48⟧

Finally, the so-called school department has been operating at the institute for many years, in which students from grades 7 to 10 in Moscow and the Moscow region study. Students of the school faculty spend their winter holidays at the MGRI geological site near Moscow, where they combine practical lessons in geology with reasonable rest. Many representatives of the school faculty take part in summer institute geological experiments.

Representatives of the school faculty participate in in-depth study of specialties in summer institute geological experiments.

There is a preparatory department at MGRI, and paid preparatory courses are operating: six-month courses for preparation for admission to full-time faculties (from February 7) and three-month courses for preparation for admission to the evening faculty (from October 1). In addition, free preparatory courses are organized for all applicants daily from July 1 to July 30.
