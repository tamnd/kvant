A task about random blurring in labyrinths

As is known, in order to prevent treasures from being lost in pairs, the Egyptians created labyrinths in them; moving along them by chance, residents often died in pit traps.

In subsequent problems, solve in detail about such labyrinths; we will assume that the traveler walks through the maze randomly, that is, when he encounters a fork, he chooses any path at random with equal probability (if there are two paths, then with a probability of 1/2, three - 1/3).
