⟦folio 38⟧

Press three balls of radii 4, 4 and 5. Each of them touches two other balls and some generatrix of the cone. Find the radius of the base of the cone if it is known that the angle between the base and the generatrix is ​​equal to 2 arc tan$\frac{1}{4}$.

4 (BMK, 1977). In $SABC$ the faces $ASC$, $BSC$ and $ASB$ are equal in size. The sum of the distances from the middle of the edge $BC$ to the faces $ASB$ and $ASC$ is one and a half times less than the height of the base lowered from the vertex $S$. Inside the prim there is a point $M$, half of the distances from which to the vertices $A$, $B$ and $C$ is equal to the sum of the distances to all faces of the prim. Find a flat surface of the prim if the length of the edge $AS$ is equal to $\sqrt{\frac{31}{11}}$.

5 (BMK, 1978). The base of the prim $ABCEH$ is the convex quadrangle $ABCE$, which is divided into two equal-sized triangles by the diagonal $BE$. The length of edge $AB$ is equal to one, the lengths of edges $BC$ and $CE$ are equal. The sum of the lengths of edges $AH$ and $EH$ is $\sqrt{2}$. The volume of the bait is $1/6$. Find the radius of the ball that has the most common radius among all the balls that fit into $ABCEH$.

Meeting with the Kwan reader

10. January, during the winter holidays, «Kvant» met with Moscow schoolchildren. The meeting took place in the Central Lecture Hall of the All-Union Society “Knowledge”. About a thousand students attended. Members of the editorial board spoke about black holes in the Universe and the depths of space civilizations, Hilbert's third problem and Conway's number, school Olympiads and university entrance exams. Academician of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences V. A. Fabrikant (see photo) shared his memories of the remarkable Soviet physicist Leonid Isaakovich Mandelstam, whose birthday will be celebrated this year. The meeting was also attended by correspondent of the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences V. G. Boltyansky, Professor Ya. A. Smorodinsky, deputy editor-in-chief V. A. Leshkovtsev, candidates of physical and mathematical sciences N. B. Vasilyev, L. G. Makar-Limanov, N. Kh. Rozov, A. P. Savin. The meeting ended with a problem-solving competition, the winners of which received memorable gifts.

V.L.
