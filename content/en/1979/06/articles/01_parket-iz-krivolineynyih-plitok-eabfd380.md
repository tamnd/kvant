This red parquet of curved tiles is obtained by deforming a regular hexagonal parquet of regular hexagons. As is easy to see, it can be extended to the entire plane. How individual tiles are arranged is shown in the diagram above. The sides of the triangles are replaced by the arcs of the circles described around them.
