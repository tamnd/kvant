Animals
on... Flatness

In fifth grade, during math lessons, you begin to study the coordinate plane. The teacher invites you to use the coordinates of a point to find it on a plane, determine the coordinates of the point, build a graph of each function... To make the tasks more fun, «Kvant» three years ago announced an “artist competition”. The coordinates of several points are specified on the coordinate plane. Neighboring points are connected by segments. If the points are found correctly, then the result will be some kind of figure: for example, an elephant, a dog, a cat, a mouse, a squirrel, an arctic fox, a penguin, a duckling, an elk, a running horse, a fox cub, a seal (these figure were sent to the editor by students of secondary school No. 5 in Sredneuralsk, Sverdlovsk region, mathematics teacher - T.
