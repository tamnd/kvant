"Probability theory" without theory

1. Reliability of the first circuit:
$$p_1 = 0,7 \times 0,8 \times 0,9 = 0,504$$
reliability of the second scheme:
$$p_2 = 0,8 \times 0,8 \times 0,8 = 0,512$$
The reliability of the second scheme is greater.

2. The game “damaged phone” corresponds to a circuit of series-connected elements, the reliability of which is determined by the variable reliability of the constituent elements.

3. If the reliability of the engine is $p$, then the reliability of two- and four-engine aircraft, respectively
$$p_2 = 1 - (1 - p)^2, p_4 = 1 - (1 - p)^4.$$

4. The path from the entrance to the treasure contains four forks, three of which have 2 continuations, one - 3. Since only one of the possible paths leads to the treasure, and the rest to the pit, the probability of finding the treasure is: $1/2 \times 1/2 \times 1/2 \times 1/3 = 1/24$, and the probability of dying: $1 - 1/24 = 23/24$.

5. From similar messages, the probability of finding treasure without wandering is 1/8. The probability of finding a treasure by making one loop is $1/8 \times 1/8$, etc.

So, the probability of ever finding the treasure is
$$p = 1/8 + 1/8 \times 1/8 + 1/8 \times 1/8 + \dots = 1/7$$
using the formula for the sum of an infinite geometric progression.
