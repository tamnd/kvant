V. Gorbunov

Moscow Aviation Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze

Moscow Order of Lenin Aviation Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze, one of the largest machine-building companies of the wall, trains specialists to work in various branches of the national industry, as well as in other new technical areas.

Academicians V.N. Yuryev, M.D. Millonshikov, famous designers D.P. Grigorovich, N.N. Polikarpov, A.S. Yakovlev, A.N. Tupolev, pilot-cosmonauts V.N. Volkov, V.N. Kubasov, V.I. Sevostyanov, V.V. Lebedev, A.S. Ivanovkov studied and worked at the institute. Currently, academicians V. P. Mishin, I. F. Obraztsov, B. N. Petrov work at the institute.

The Institute trains mechanical engineers in aircraft and helicopter construction, aircraft and aircraft; electromechanical engineers for instruments, computing devices, electrical equipment, aircraft control systems, automated control systems, installations and automated drives of aircraft; radio engineers for radio installations of aircraft, design and production of electronic switching equipment; engineer-economists in economics and organization of the mechanical engineering industry, organization of mechanized processing of economic information, automated production management systems; engineers and mathematicians in the use of computer technology and mathematical support of automated control systems; electromechanical engineers for the design of antenna-feeder devices.

A characteristic feature of studying at the institute is the combination of the educational process with elements of scientific research. The institute has a student scientific society, MAN, and 14 student design bureaus, in which students develop various models of modern technology. The average ones are the light sports aircraft «Kvant», the Shelf underwater vehicle, and a whole series of aviation equipment. On October 26, 1978, the first student satellite, developed by MAN students, was launched into orbit.
