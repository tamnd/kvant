Oral exam objectives

1. A ball is thrown at an angle $\alpha = 60^\circ$ to the horizontal with a speed of $|\vec{v}_0| = 20$ m/s. Determine the highest lift altitude and flight range. Ignore air resistance.

2. With what minimum force $\vec{F}_{\min}$ directed horizontally, must a flat block be pressed against the wall so that it does not slide down? The mass of the bar is $m = 5$ kg; friction coefficient between the wall and the block $k = 0,1$.

3. A projectile with a mass of $m_1 = 50$ kg, flying at a speed of $|\vec{v}| = 800$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the vertical, hits a stationary platform with sand and gets stuck in it. Find the speed of the platform $|\vec{u}|$ after the projectile hits, if its mass is $m_2 = 16$ t.

4. What can be the maximum volume $V_1$ of an ice floe floating in water, if it is known that an aluminum block with a volume of $V_2 = 0,1$ m³, frozen to the ice floe, gets stuck in its sinking? The densities of ice and aliminium in water are respectively $\rho_1 = 900$ kg/m³, $\rho_2 = 2700$ kg/m³ and $\rho_3 = 1000$ kg/m³.

5. Reporting the filling state of a liquid having a temperature of $t$. When heating a liquid in one of the co-
vessels to a temperature $t_1$ the liquid level is established in this state at a height $h_1$, and in the other state at a height $h_2$. Find the coefficient of volumetric expansion of the liquid.

6. A piece of ice cooled to $t_2 = -50^\circ$ C was placed in a vessel containing $m_1 = 10$ kg of water at a temperature of $t_1 = 10^\circ$ C, after which the temperature of the resulting ice mass turned out to be equal to $0 = -4^\circ$ C. How much ice is completely in the vessel? Specific heats of water $c_1 = 4200$ J/(kg K) and ice $c_2 = 2100$ J/(kg K); specific heat of melting of ice at $t_0 = 0^\circ$ C $\lambda = 3,3 \cdot 10^5$ J/kg.

7. In a uniform magnetic field of perpendicularly directed induction, the model of which is $|\vec{B}| = 0,1$ mT, a wire with a length of $l = 2$ m moves at a speed of $|\vec{v}| = 5$ m/s, perpendicular to the conductor. What EMF is induced in the conductor?

8. At a distance of $d = 0,14$ m from the top of the concave mirror there is an object whose height is $l = 0,06$ m. The focal length of the mirror is $F = 0,11$ m. Find the height $L$ of the image of the object.

9. An object measuring $l = 8$ cm must be projected onto the screen. What focal length $F$ should the lens be, located at a distance of $f = 4$ m from the screen, so that the image of the object on the screen has a size of $L = 2$ m?

10. The imaginary image of a luminous point in a scattering line is located two times closer to the face than the point itself. Find the position of the luminous point if it is known that it lies on the axis of the line. Optical line power $D = -5$ ditr.

Moscow Geological Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze

The Moscow Geological Institute (MGRI) aimed to exist as the geological department of the Moscow Mining Academy, created on the initiative of V.I. Lenin in September 1918. Currently, the Moscow Order of Labor Red Replacement Geological Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze is a specialized university that trains mining geological engineers, geophysicists, and mineral deposit developers.

MGRI has the following faculties: geological, geophysical, technical exploration and development, hydrorheological. In addition, there is an evening faculty, where they study on-the-job in all specialties of daytime faculties.
