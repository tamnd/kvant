Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1

1. Solve the equation
$$3^2 - x = 3^x - 8.$$

2. Calculate without table
$$\cos^4 \frac{\pi}{8} + 13 \cos^2 \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos^4 \frac{3\pi}{8} + \cos^4 \frac{5\pi}{8} + \cos^4 \frac{7\pi}{8}.$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\frac{(x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)}{(x + 1)(x + 2)(x + 3)} > 1.$$

4. Solve inequality
$$f'(x) \leq g'(x)$$
if $f(x) = \frac{2}{x}$; $g(x) = x - x^3$.

5. The given even points are $A(-2; -3; 8)$, $B(2; 1; 7)$, $C(1; 4; 5)$ and $D(-7; -4; 7)$.
Check that the vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{CD}$ are collinear.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$4^x + \sqrt{x^2 - 2} - 5 \cdot 2^x - 1 + \sqrt{x^2 - 2} = 6.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x = \sin 3x + \cos x (\cos x - 1).$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\left| \frac{3x}{x^2 - 4} \right| \leq 1.$$

4. At what value of $z$ are the vectors $\overrightarrow{a} = (6; 0; 12)$ and $\overrightarrow{b} = (-8; 13; 7)$ perpendicular.

5. Calculate the area of ​​a figure bounded by lines
$$y = \frac{1}{\cos^2 x}, y = 0, x = 0 \text{ и } x = \frac{\pi}{4}.$$

Option 3

1. Solve the equation
$$7^1 gx = 98 - x^1 g^7.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$2 + \cos 4x = 2 \sin^2 x.$$

3. Solve inequality
$$\left| \frac{x^2 - 5x + 4}{x^2 - 4} \right| < 1.$$

4. At what value of $z$ are the vectors $\overrightarrow{a} = (2; -3; 4)$ and $\overrightarrow{b} = (-3; 2; z)$ perpendicular.

5. Calculate integral
$$\int_3^{-18} \sqrt[3]{2 - \frac{x}{3}} dx.$$

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. Find the work that must be done to increase the speed of the body from $\overrightarrow{v}_1$ to $\overrightarrow{v}_2$ = 6 m/s along the path $l = 10$ m. A constant friction force $\overrightarrow{F}_{\text{Tp}} = 2\text{H}$ acts along the entire path. Body weight $m = 1$ kg.

2. A steel cylinder is filled with nitrogen at a temperature of $t = 12^\circ\text{C}$. Nitrogen pressure $p = 15\text{MPa}$. Find the density of nitrogen under these conditions. At what temperature will the pressure increase to $p_1 = 18\text{MPa}$? Neglect the expansion of the cylinder walls.

3. In a calorimeter of thermal specificity $C = 63\text{Дж/K}$ $m_1 = 250$ g of oil was poured at a temperature $t_1 = 12^\circ\text{C}$. After lowering a copper body weighing $m_2 = 500$ g into oil at a temperature $t_2 = 100^\circ\text{C}$, the final temperature $\theta = 33^\circ\text{C}$ was established. What is the specific heat $c_1$ of the oil according to experiment? The specific heat of copper is $c_2 = 0,38\text{кДж/(кг·К)}$.

4. Charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ are at a distance $r$ from each other. How many times must the distance between them be changed so that the interaction force remains the same if the abscultaneous value of the charge $q_1$ is increased by 4 times?

5. Four light bulbs, rated for voltage $U = 3\text{В}$ and current $I = 0,3\text{A}$, must be connected in parallel and powered from a voltage source $U_0 = 5,4\text{В}$. What additional resistance should be connected in series with the lamp chain?

6. What is the maximum speed of electrons ejected from sodium by light of wavelength $\lambda = 0,5\text{мкм}$? The red photofect boundary for sodium is $\lambda_{\text{кр}} = 0,68\text{мкм}$. Planck's constant $h = 6,62 \cdot 10^{-34}\text{Дж·с}$, electron mass $m = 9,1 \cdot 10^{-31}\text{кг}$.
