Moscow Energy Institute

The faculties and specialties of the Moscow Order of Lenin Energy Institute (MPEI) are described in «Kvant», 1978, No. 7.

In 1978, applicants entering MPEI took two exams in mathematics (written and oral - in all faculties), and in physics - one exam (written in some faculties, oral in others).

Mathematics
Written exam

Option 1
1. Simplifying the expression
$$\left( \frac{(x^2-4)^{-2}+(x+2)^{-2}-(x-2)^{-2}}{1-8x} \right)^{-1} - \left( 4 \sqrt[3]{4} \right)^{10} x^3 - 16,$$
find its limit at $x \to \frac{1}{8}$.

2. Solve system equation
$$\begin{cases} (3y^2+1) \cdot \log_3 x = 1, \\ x^2y^3+10 = 27. \end{cases}$$

3. The sum of an infinitely killing geometric progression is equal to the largest value $f(x)=3x^3-x-76$ on the interval [0; 3]; the first term of the progression is equal to the square of its denominator. Find the denominator of the progression.

4. Find all values ​​of $x$ satisfying the equation
$$\sin^4 \frac{x}{2} + \sin^4 \left( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{7}{2} \pi \right) = \sin \frac{5}{6} \pi$$
and lying on the segment $\left[ -\frac{\pi}{2}; 2\pi \right]$.

5. A plane is drawn through the vertex $C_1$ of the cube $ABCD_1B_1C_1D_1$, intersecting the edges $[BC]$ and $[CD]$ and forming an angle $\alpha$ with the face $ABCD$, and an equilateral triangle is obtained in the section. Find the cross-sectional area if the length of the edge of the cube is $a$ (distance possible cases).

Option 2
1. Simplifying the expression for $f(x)$, find $f'(x)$ if
$$f(x) = \left( \frac{\sqrt{x-2}}{\sqrt{x+2}+\sqrt{x-2}} + \frac{x-2}{\sqrt{x^2-4}-x+2} \right)^{-2} \times \left( \frac{x-1}{2(\sqrt{x}+1)} + 1 \right) \times \frac{2}{\sqrt{x}+1}.$$

2. Find the domain of a function
$$f(x) = \lg \left( \sqrt{8-2+\lg x} - \sqrt[3]{4^2-\lg x} \right).$$

3. At what subtractable will the difference be greatest if the subtrahend is equal to twice the square of the subtracted?

4. Find all values ​​of $x$ satisfying the equation
$$(1-\cos 2x) \sin 2x = \sqrt{3} \sin^2 x$$
and lying on the segment $\left[ -\pi; \frac{\pi}{3} \right]$.

5. The area of ​​the rectangular trapezoid $ABCD$ is equal to $S$, the length of the height $AB$ is equal to $h$, the magnitude of the acute angle $ADC$ of the trapezoid is $\alpha$. On the side of $[CD]$ the point $E$ is taken, so that $|CE|=|ED|$. Find the volume of the body obtained by rotating the quadrilateral $ABED$ around the straight line $(AB)$.

Physics
Written exam

Option 1
1. Current sources. Electromotive force. Series connection of EMF sources. Batteries. Ohm's law for a closed circuit.

2. The energy of the photons that irradiate the metal is three times greater than the output of electrons from the metal. What fraction of the photon energy is the maximum kinetic energy of photons emitted from the metal?

3. Two identical exact charges at a distance of $r_1=1$ m from each other. The magnitude of each charge is $q=10^{-10}$ Cl. How much work must be done to bring the charges together to a distance of $r_2=10$ cm?

4. When a stationary boundary breaks into two fragments flying along one straight line, mechanical energy $W=1000$ J is released. It is known that the mass of one fragment is greater than the mass of the other. Find the speed of the fragments. The mass of the boundary is $m=1$ kg, and the mass of the powder gases is small.

5. What are the initial volume and temperature of the gel located under the piston in the cylinder if, when the gel is cooled to $t=-23^\circ C$, a load of $M=16$ kg lying on the piston does $A=40$ J of work? Piston area $S=200$ cm², normal atmospheric pressure, gel mass $m=5$ g.

Oral exam objectives
1. A body of mass $m=20$ kg is pulled with a force $|F|=120$ N along a horizontal surface. If this force is applied at an angle $\alpha_1=60^\circ$ to the horizontal, the body moves uniformly. With what acceleration will the body move if the same force is applied at an angle $\alpha_2=30^\circ$ to the horizon?

2. A cylindrical vessel $l=1$ m long is divided into two parts by a movable piston. What will be the equilibrium position of the piston if there is a certain mass of oxygen in one part of the vessel, and the same mass of hydrogen in the other part?
