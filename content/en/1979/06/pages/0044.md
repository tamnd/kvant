⟦folio 42⟧

Electronic, but also other industries.

MIET has the following faculties:

The Faculty of Physics and Technology trains specialists in the field of the creative body and technological applications of devices and electronic circuits. During their studies at the institute, majors in this profession acquire in-depth knowledge in the field of general and theoretical physics, physics of semiconductor devices, and receive special training in the field of design, development and technological processes.

The Faculty of Physics and Chemistry trains specialists in semiconductor and dielectric materials of electronic equipment and physical and chemical processes and technical technologies.

The Faculty of Microprocesses and Technical Library trains specialists in the following areas: electronic computers, radio-electronic devices, design and production of radio equipment. During the training process, students study the technical library, radio engineering and instrument-making disciplines, computational mathematics, design of electronic computers and radio-electronic devices.

The Faculty of Electronic Engineering trains specialists in the field of design, manufacturing and automation of technological equipment for electronic equipment. Students of this faculty specialize in the field of design, automation and manufacturing of technological equipment.

At the evening department, training is conducted in all specialties listed above for daytime faculties.

Programming, training and control of students' knowledge are carried out in a specially equipped classroom using various monitoring devices and computers.

In MIET, in addition to teaching, a lot of research work is carried out. Starting from the fourth year, students combine studying at the institute with industrial practice, working three days a week at basic research institutes and factories.

The institute has comfortable dormitories. All non-residents are provided with hostel accommodation for the time being.

MIET has a stadium with a rubber-bitumen track, courts for playing volleyball, basketball and handball, tennis courts, a gym, a 25-meter swimming pool, a shooting range, an ice skating range, and sports and recreational camps in the mountains of the North Caucasus and on the shores of the Caspian Sea.

Option 1

$$\sin^4 x - \cos^4 x = \cos x.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\frac{x+5}{32} = 0,25 \cdot 128 \frac{x+17}{x-3}.$$

3. Calculate the area of ​​a figure bounded by function graphs
$$y = 4 - x^2, \quad y = x^2 - 2x.$$

4. In an equilateral trapezoid, the smaller base and side are equal to $a$. Find a larger base $b$ so that the area of ​​the trapezoid is maximum.

5. Given three non-Lue vectors $a, b, c$, each two of which are non-cliptic. Find their sum if the vector $a+b$ is collinear to the vector $c$, and the vector $b+c$ is collinear to the vector $a$.

Option 2

1. Solve the equation
$$\sin^4 x + \cos^4 x = \sin x \cdot \cos x.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$(0,4)^{1g^2x+1} = (6,25)^2 - 1gx^3.$$

3. Calculate the area of ​​a figure bounded by function graphs
$$y = \sqrt{x}, \quad y = \sqrt{4-3x}, \quad y = 0.$$

4. A point $M\left(\frac{1}{2}; 1\right)$ is given on the plane. The straight line passing through the point is formed together with the positive half-symmetry of the coordinate triangle. What is the minimum value that the area of ​​this triangle can take?

5. Vectors $a, b$ and $c$ have equal lengths and form pairwise equal angles. Find the coordinates of the vector $c$ if $a=i+j, b=j+k$.
