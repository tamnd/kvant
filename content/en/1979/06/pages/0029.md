⟦folio 27⟧

Tasks

1. In a triangle, the length of one side is 6.31 m, the length of the other side is 0.82 m. What is the length of the triangle if it is known that it is expressed in an integer number of meters?

2. There are dots on the circle: 1977 white and one red. All possible polygons with vertices at these points are considered. Which averages will be greater: with or without a red top?

3. Let $a$ be an arbitrary 1979-digit number divisible by 9. The sum of the digits of this number is denoted by the letter $A$. The sum of the digits of the number $A$ is denoted by the letter $B$. The sum of the digits of the number $B$ is the letter $C$. Find the number $C$.

4. On cell b8 of the chessboard it is written in chalk +1, and in all other cells -1. In one operation, it is necessary to change all signs to opposite ones either on one (any) vertical or on one (any) horizontal. Prove that no matter how many operations we perform, we will never get +1 in all squares of the board at the same time.

5. The problem is a joke. The hens laid three eggs in three days. How many eggs will twelve chickens lay in twelve days?
