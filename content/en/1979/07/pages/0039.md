Below we publish questions proposed in the Republican.type Weighted mathematical optimization of schoolchildren in 1979. For each question you need to choose the only correct answer.

VIII class

Algebra

1. Which of the following inequalities defines the set shown in figure 1 by hatching?

Fig. 1.

A) $y \leq x - 1$; B) $y \geq x - 1$; B) $y \leq 1 - x$; D) $y \geq 1 - x$; D) $y \leq x + 1$.

2. Using practical principles of approximate calculations, find the difference $x - y$ of the numbers $x \approx 13,6517$ and $y \approx 10,4$.

A) 3.2517; B) 3.25; B) 3.2; D) 3.3; D) the required value is not specified.

3. What formula gives the function inverse to the function $y = \frac{2}{x}$?

A) $y = \frac{x}{2}$; B) $y = \frac{2}{x}$;

B) $y = 2x$; D) $y = -\frac{2}{x}$;

D) $y = -\frac{x}{2}$.

4. For what values ​​of $a$ is the equality $\sqrt{a^2} = -a$ true?

A) for all $a$; B) at $a \geq 0$; B) at $a \leq 0$;

D) at $a = 0$; D) for any values ​​of $a$.

5. For what values ​​of $x$ is the equality $x - \frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{x}$ true?

A) for all $x$; B) at $x \neq 0$; B) at $x > 0$; D) at $x = 1$; D) for any values ​​of $x$.

6. figure 2 shows sketches of the graphs of three exponential functions $y = a^x$, $y = b^x$, $y = c^x$. What are the correspondences between the bases $a$, $b$ and $c$?

Fig. 2.

A) $c < a < b$; B) $b < a < c$; B) $a < b < c$; D) $c < b < a$; D) $a < c < b$.
