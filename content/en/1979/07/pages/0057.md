Leningrad Polytechnic Institute named after. M. I. Kalinina

The Leningrad Polytechnic Institute, founded in 1899, is one of the largest universities in the country.

The range of specialties in which training is conducted is wide. Those who wish to work on problems related to the advanced directions of modern physics participate in the physico-mechanical, radiophysics and electromechanical faculties. Mathematician engineers are trained in the specialty “computational mathematics”. For the instrumental industries that form the basis of the country's national economy, specialties are prepared by the energy and mechanical engineering, mechanical engineering, hydrochemical and physical metallurgical faculties. To work in planning bodies, in computer centers, in planning and production departments of enterprises, specialties are prepared at the Faculty of Engineering and Economics. Recently, the Faculty of Control Automation was established at the Polytechnic Institute to train specialties in automated control systems, technical cybernetics, electronic computers and automation devices. A new specialization “robots and machines” was formed at the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering.

In total, 18.5 thousand students and about 500 graduate students study at the institute in 60 specialties.
