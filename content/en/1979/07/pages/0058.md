Mathematics

Written exam

Option 1
(Hydromechanical, electron-mechanical, energy-machine-melting, mechanical-machine-melting and physico-metallic faculties.)

1. Simplify an expression

$$\frac{1 - \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^{-1}}{\sqrt[4]{ab} - \sqrt{b}} \cdot \left( \frac{3}{\sqrt[4]{a^2}} \cdot \sqrt{a^{-\frac{1}{3}}} \cdot \sqrt{b} + \frac{a - \sqrt{ab}}{(b - a) \sqrt[4]{b^{-3}}} \right) - \frac{4}{\sqrt{b}}.$$

2. Solve the equation

$$4 \log_2^2 \frac{x}{\sqrt[8]{2}} - 3 \log_2 2x = 8^{-\frac{4}{3}}.$$

3. Solve the equation

$$\log \frac{1}{7} (x^2 - 5x + 4) > -1.$$

4. A triangular paramida is inscribed in a cone, at the base of which lies an isosceles triangle. What should be the angle between the lateral sides of this triangle for the volume of the paramid to be the most shim, provided that the volume of the cone is given?

Option 2
(Physical-mechanical, engineering-economic, radiophysical faculties and the faculty of control automation.)

1. Solve inequality

$$\log \frac{1}{7} (x^2 - 5x + 4) > -1.$$

2. Solve system of equations

$$\begin{cases} \sqrt{\frac{x-y}{x+y}} + \sqrt{\frac{x+y}{x-y}} = \frac{10}{3}, \\ xy - 2x - 2y = 2. \end{cases}$$

3. The height of the cone is also the diameter of the ball. The radius of the ball is equal to $R$, the angle at the apex of the axial section of the cone is equal to $\alpha$. A cube is placed in a ball so that four of its vertices lie on the surface of the ball outside the cone, and the remaining four lie on the side surface of the cone; the planes passing through these four vertices are parallel to the plane of the base of the cone. Find the edge of the cube.

4. Prove that the following inequality holds for the angles of the isosceles triangle $A, B, C$

$$\log^2 \frac{A}{2} \cdot \log^2 \frac{B}{2} \cdot \log^2 \frac{C}{2} \leq \frac{1}{27}.$$

Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. Two bodies can move along two parallel lines. The first body moves with root $|\vec{a}_1| = 0,1 \text{ m/c}^2$.

At the moment when the first body, having a speed of $|\vec{v}_1| = 1 \text{ m/c}$, appears with the second, the second body starts and, moving uniformly accelerated, reaches the first body through $t = 20 \text{ с}$. Find the maximum lag of the second body from the first.

2. A cylindrical tube with radius $R = 1 \text{ m}$ rolls along a horizontal surface so that its center moves with root $|\vec{a}| = 4,9 \text{ m/c}^2$. Inside the tube there is a small body whose sliding friction coefficient on the inner surface of the tube is $\mu = 0,5$. At what height from the horizontal surface is the body located during this movement? Neglect the thickness of the tube walls.

3. The last roller with mass $m = 60 \text{ t}$ comes off from a train with mass $M = 600 \text{ t}$ moving at a constant speed along a straight horizontal track. How far does this car travel to a stop if at the moment it stops it moves at a constant speed $|\vec{v}| = 40 \text{ km/ч}$. The power of the diesel locomotive driving the train of valons is constant and equal to $N = 10 \text{ MBt}$.

4. A load of mass $m_1 = 1 \text{ кг}$ was suspended from a vertical elastic weightless thread and released without a push. After the damped oscillations that arose, the load stopped, and it turned out that work $A_1 = 2 \text{ Дж}$ was done against the resistance forces. How much work will be done against the resistance forces in the same experiment if a load of mass $m_2 = 2 \text{ кг}$ is suspended?

5. Two small simultaneously charged balls are fixed in a vacuum at a distance significantly greater than their linear dimensions. If you release the first ball, then when the distance $r$ between the balls is reached, its speed will be equal to $|\vec{v}_1| = 3 \text{ m/c}$; if you release the second one, then under the same conditions its speed will be $|\vec{v}_2| = 4 \text{ m/c}$. Find the speed of the balls at the moment when they move apart by a distance $r$, if the ball is released at the same time.

6. A small charged ball is suspended on a non-stretching, weightless thread; The tension force of the thread is $|\vec{F}|$. In place of the first ball, a simultaneously charged second ball of the same type is placed, and the first one deviates from the top of the initial position by an angle less than 180°. Find the new thread tension.
