The coefficient of linear expansion of the stack is $\beta = 9.10^{-6} K^{-1}$, the coefficient of volumetric expansion of oil is $\alpha = 10,18 \cdot 10^{-4} K^{-1}$.

2. There is mercury in communicating vessels; the diameter of one vessel is four times the diameter of the other. Pour water into a narrow vessel; the height of the column of water is $h = 80$ cm. How much will the level of mercury drop in a narrow vessel and how much will it rise in the shrikom? 3. Half of the gas mass was released from the cylinder, and the gas temperature dropped from $t_1 = 27^\circ C$ to $t_2 = -73^\circ C$. How many times did the pressure in the cylinder decrease?

4. In a room whose volume is $V = 100 \text{ m}^3$, the air temperature has decreased from $t_1 = 25^\circ C$ to $t_2 = 15^\circ C$. How much water vapor condensed in this case if the absolute humidity of the saturating vapor at 25°C is $23 \text{ г/m}^3$, and at 15°C – $\rho_2 = 12,8 \text{ г/m}^3$.
