M575*. On a straight line, the points $A_0, A_1, A_2, \dots, A_n$ are located in order so that the lengths of the segments $A_0A_1, A_1A_2, \dots, A_{n-1}A_n$ do not exceed 1. It is required to mark $k-1$ of the points $A_1, A_2, \dots, A_{n-1}$ in red so that the lengths of any two of the $k$ parts into which the segment $A_0A_n$ is divided by red dots differ by no more than 1. Prove, this is always possible do:
a) for $k=3$,
b) for every natural number $k<n$. (10)

V. Grinberg, V. Galperin

F583. The observer moves at a constant speed along a certain inclined line. A body thrown at an angle to the horizon intersects the observed trajectory twice with a time interval $t$. Both times the body is in front of the observer at the same distance from him. What does the body's trajectory look like from an observer's point of view?
After the second intersection, the observer changes the paths traversed by the body in successive equal time intervals of long t. Find the ratio of these paths. (8)

M. Kitsay

F854. 0.5 liters of water was poured into a small thin-walled metal casstro, the casstro was placed on the stove and, by changing the temperature of the water at different points in time, a graph of the temperature versus time was plotted. Then the water was poured out, 0.7 kg of alcohol was poured into the casstro and, placing the casstro on the same stove, a graph of the temperature of the alcohol versus time was plotted. Both graphs are shown on figure 1. Using these graphs, determine the specific heat of alcohol and the specific heat of its vaporization, if after 30 minutes of boiling the amount of alcohol in the casserole has halved. The heat capacity of the casstro is 200 J/K. Evaporation from the surface of the liquid is expected. (9, 10)
