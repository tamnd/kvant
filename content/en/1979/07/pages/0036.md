Let us see the evolution of the formation in the presence of any continuous piece of n left first-graders: after 2n-2 seconds all first-graders will become right-handed, and the movement will stop.
Let us now present the proof for the general case - when the left first-graders stand in an arbitrary manner. We need a few additional definitions. Let us call an island a segment connecting the rightmost of the left and the leftmost
one of the worst leftists. We will call a swamp the shortest segment adjacent to the island on the left and including all the vertuns (of course, there may not be a swamp).
First-graders who did not enter either the island or the swamp are called the sea (Fig. 3).
Lemma 1 (obvious). If the right of the right first-grader A stands like the right first-graders, then A is always mak and we will be right (Fig. 4).
