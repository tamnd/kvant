Physics

Oral exam assignment

1. What part of the period of the matrix load performing harmonious oscillation is within 1 cm from the equilibrium position, if the amplitude of the oscillations is 2 cm?

2. In a vessel with a volume of $V=10$ l at a temperature of $t_1=20^\circ\text{C}$ air humidity is $r=80\%$. The temperature dropped to $t_2=5^\circ\text{C}$. All condensed moisture collected in a spherical drop. What charge must be imparted to the drop so that its potential becomes equal to $\varphi=1$ V? The saturated vapor densities at temperatures $t_1$ and $t_2$ are equal to $\rho_1=17,3 \text{ г/m}^3$ and $\rho_2=6,8 \text{ г/m}^3$, respectively. The density of water is $\rho_B=1 \text{ г/cm}^3$.

3. A source of emf is connected in series to two identical flat air capacitors connected in series. How will the electric field voltage change between the plates of one of them if a dielectric with dielectric constant $\varepsilon=9$ is introduced into the other, filling the entire capacitor completely?

4. Find the ratio of the potential energy $W_\pi$ of an electron to its kinetic energy $W_K$ in the Bohr hydrogen atom.

5. A proton approaches the doubly reduced atomy of uranium $^{238}\text{U}$ along the line of centers. What was the initial speed of the proton if it approached the uranium non at the minimum distance $R=0,5 \text{ Å}$? The charge and mass of a proton are $q=1,6 \cdot 10^{-19} \text{ K}$ or $m=1,67 \cdot 10^{-27} \text{ кг}$, respectively.

6. In an electrical bath, the part is coated with inkel. Know the voltage $U$ between the electrodes, the resistivity of the power supply $\rho$, the distance $l$ between the electrodes, find the coating speed (that is, the rate of increase in the thickness $h$ of the inkel layer). The electrochemical equivalent of inkel is $k$, the density of inkel is $\rho_H$.

7. At a height $h$ above the water surface there is a point light source $S$. Where will the image $S'$ of this source, given by the flat mirror bottom of the vessel, be located when looking vertically down? The depth of the vessel with water is $d$, the refractive index of water is $n=4/3$.

8. A point light source is placed on the optical axis of the collecting lens at a distance of $d=25$ cm from the lens. On the other side of the lens - once at a distance of $a=27$ cm, another time at a distance of $b=48$ cm - a screen is placed. The illuminated center of the light spot on the screen in both cases turns out to be the same. Determine the focal length $F$ of the lens.

9. A pocket radiation dose meter is an air capacitor charged to a certain potential difference. Under the influence of irradiation, the gas is conditioned, the ions, mixing to the capacitor plates, increase the potential difference. When irradiated with 1 reentene, $n_0=2 \cdot 10^9$ vapors are formed in each cubic centimeter of air under normal conditions. How much reentry will the dosage meter show if, with a capacitor capacity of $C=3 \cdot 10^{-12}$ F, the potential difference is from $\varphi_1=180$ V to $\varphi_2=160$ V? Chamber volume $V=1,8 \text{ cm}^3$.

10. What number of foci with a wavelength of $\lambda=0,6$ µm will have a total impulse in a parallel beam equal to the average impulse of the thermal motion of the atoma gel at a temperature of $T=300$ K? The mass of the atoma gel is $m=6,65 \times 10^{-27}$ kg, Planck’s constant is $h=6,62 \times 10^{-34}$ J s, Boltzmann’s constant is $k=1,38 \cdot 10^{-23}$ J/K.
