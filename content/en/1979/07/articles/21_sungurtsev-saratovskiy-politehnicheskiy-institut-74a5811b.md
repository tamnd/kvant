Saratov Polytechnic Institute

Saratov Polytechnic Institute is currently a temporary university in the Volga region. Its history dates back to 1930, when the Automated Highway Institute was opened in Saratov. In May 1960, the Saratov Automated Highway Institute was transformed into a polytechnic. Nowadays the annual graduation rate is over 2,500 people.

The training of 17 thousand students of the institute - future engineers in 28 specialties - is carried out by over 1000 teachers, including 25 professors and almost 500 associate professors and candidates of science.

The institute has six full-time education faculties: automatic engineering, road construction, construction, mechanical engineering, energy and electronic engineering and instrument engineering. In addition, there are fils in the cities of the Saratov region - Balakovo, Balashov, Volsk and Engels. The institute has a preparatory faculty, and there are preparatory courses with varying amounts of training programs.

All students at SPI are required to master the latest computer technology, use an excellent scientific and technical library, and perform various tasks in well-equipped laboratories.
