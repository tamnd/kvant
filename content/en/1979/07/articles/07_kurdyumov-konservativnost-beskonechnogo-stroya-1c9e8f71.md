Conservatism of an endless system

This note solves problem M500g) from Zadaniak «Kvant» («Kvant», 1978, no. 4; see also 1979, no. 2). At the end of the note there are three new problems, the solutions of two of which (problems 1 and 3) are unknown.

There are endless reclassics in both directions. At the command to the left-VO, all the reclassics, except for the last one, many of them, turned to the left; the rest turned to the right. In each subsequent second, each reclassic turns 180° if the first and third of those standing in front of it turn to face it. All other reclassics remain motionless. Prove that the movement in this infinite system will stop after a finite time.

Let all the reclassicists initially face us (Fig. 1, a). After the first turn, they will all look left or right. Obviously, at the first moment those who correctly executed the command “to the left” will be right, and the “violators” will be left.

According to the condition, at the first moment there are a finite number of left reclassicals.

Let us describe the reclassics, who will turn in the next second, with vertuns (it is easy to say that at each moment there is a finite number of vertuns). We will call left-wing spinners malicious. Obviously, a left reclassical is malicious if there is a left reclassical (Fig. 1, c) in front of it in first or third place. We will draw the Vertins in green, the malicious reclassicists in red, and the right Vertins in blue (Fig. 1, c).

Before presenting the proof in general form, let us consider a special case for illustration. Let there be exactly seven left reclassifications, and they all stand in a row.
