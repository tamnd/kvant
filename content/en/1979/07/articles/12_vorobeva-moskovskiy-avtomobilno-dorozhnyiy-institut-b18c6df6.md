Options for university entrance exams in 1978

Moscow Automobile and Highway Institute

The Institute was founded in 1930 and is one of the largest higher educational institutions in the country.

More than 10 thousand students study at the institute in 14 specialties. It includes the following faculties: road construction, airport construction, road transport engineering, transportation and traffic management, expensive machinery, electrical engineering and transport control systems and built-in design-mechanical, engineering and economics.

Graduates of the institute are sent to work in industry research institutes, design bureaus, automobile industry factories, design organizations, auto repair plants, auto repair shops, large automobile farms, traffic police organizations, road construction and airfield construction.

Duration of study at the institute: full-time - 5 years, evening - 5 years and 9 months.

Students of the institute carry out extensive research work in the field of theoretical and practical problems of road transport and road engineering, road bridge and airfield construction.

Those entering MADI will take entrance exams in the following disciplines: Russian language and literature (essay); mathematics (written and oral); physics (written).

Admission exams take place from August 1 to August 20 for daytime studies, and from August 11 to September 1 for evening studies.

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Find the height of the cylinder with the largest volume that can be placed in a ball of radius R.
2. Calculate the area of ​​the figure bounded by the lines $y = \frac{1}{x} + 1$, $x = 1$ and the tangent drawn at point $(2; \frac{3}{2})$ to the curve $y = \frac{1}{x} + 1$.

3. Solve equation $\cos x - \cos 5x = 4 \cos x \cdot \sin^2 x$.
4. Solve inequality $\frac{3x + 7}{6x - x^2} < \frac{1}{x + 1}$.

5. Solve the system of equations $\begin{cases} 2x + y - 1 + 2x - y + 1 = 3, \\ \frac{1}{7} \cdot 3^x \cdot \log_3 2 + y \cdot \log_3 2 - 2 + 3^x \cdot \log_3 2 - y \cdot \log_3 2 - 2 = \frac{1}{7} \end{cases}$.

Option 2
1. Find the extrema of the functions $y = \frac{2x - 1}{(x - 1)^2}$ and indicate the intervals of increase and decrease.
2. Calculate the volume of the body formed by rotation around the $Ox$ axis of the figure bounded by the lines $y = x^3$, $y = 1$, $x = 3$.
3. Solve equation $\frac{1}{1 + \cos^2 x} + \frac{1}{1 + \sin^2 x} = \frac{16}{11}$.
4. Solve inequality $\log_2 \frac{x - 4}{2x + 5} < 1$.
5. Solve equation $5^{1 + \log_4 x} + 5^{1 \log_0,25 x - 1} = \frac{26}{5}$.
