Leningrad Higher Order of Lenin
Red Replacement School of Railway Troops and Military Communications named after. M. V. Frunze

In one of the most beautiful places in Leningrad, on Moika 96, the Leningrad Higher Order of Lenin Red Replacement School of Railway Troops and Military Communications is located. M. V. Frunze is one of the oldest military educational institutions of the Soviet Armed Forces. Created in March 1918, it is rightfully the rovesnik of October. In 1969, the school was transferred to the category of higher military educational institutions of the USSR Armed Forces.

The school trains highly qualified officers - commanders and engineers - for the railway troops and the organization of military communications.

Command faculties (training period 4 years) train officers with higher military-special education; they receive qualifications as engineers in the construction of railways, artificial structures (bridges, tunnels, etc.), in the repair and operation of railway transport and automation, telemechanics and communications devices.

At the Faculty of Engineering (training period is 5 years), cadets are trained in the same specialties as at the command faculties, with the exception of the specialties “operation of railway transport” and “operation of automation, telemechanics and communications devices,” but receive more in-depth engineering training.

To organize military communications, the school trains officers at the command department, where they receive the qualification of an engineer for the operation of railway (water, air) transport.
