Activation energy and temperature of the medium have a stronger effect on the kinetic size; they are included in the exponent. Against their background, the influence of other parameters is insignificant. Let us plot the dependences $r_K(T_0)$ for various values ​​of activation energy (Fig. 5). The graph shows that the characteristic dimensions of cargo compartments of ships (about ten meters) fall into the region $r_K$ within the dimensional limits of changes in activation energy and initial temperature. As already mentioned, a series of explosions began in connection with the increase in industrial production of ammonium integras. The technical process has been accelerated, which includes cooling the saltpeter obtained by neutralizing nitric acid with ammonia. It is quite possible that the loading of the product was carried out at a slightly elevated temperature. In addition, to prevent the fertilized granules from caking, they are coated with wax and packaged in paper bags. Laboratory studies have shown that the additive reduces activation energy. These factors contribute to the occurrence of an explosion.
