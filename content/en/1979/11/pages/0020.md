Movement of the medium around vortex rings

What happens to the environment after the vortex is formed? Relevant experiments helped us answer this question.

Place a lit candle at a distance of 2-3 m from the Theta apparatus. Let the smoke ring be launched in such a way that it does not crash into the candle flame, but passes nearby. The flame will either go out or fluctuate very strongly. This suggests that not only the visible part of the ring moves, but also the layer of air adjacent to the ring.
