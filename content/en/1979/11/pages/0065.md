1. Since the case of a small change in the concentration of the starting substances (small depth of transformation) is considered, the reaction rate can be considered constant over time. The required time $t$ is determined by the simple relation $w\tau=10^{-2}n$, or

$$\tau = \frac{10^{-2}}{zn} e^{E/kT} = 7 \cdot 10^{-12} e^{E/kT} \text{ (c)}.$$

Substituting the values ​​of activation energy and temperature, we obtain:

$$\tau_{273} = 7 \cdot 10^{19} c = 2 \cdot 10^{12} \text{ лет — время, больше чем возрастет Вселенной! Естественно считать газовый смесь при такой температуре инертной.}$$

$$\tau_{600} = 7 \cdot 10^{2} c \approx 10 \text{ минут — медленное протекание реакции.}$$

$$\tau_{800} = 0,3 \text{ с — быстрая реакция.}$$

Thus, increasing the absolute temperature of the mixture by only two times (273-600 K) changes the rate of the chemical reaction by seventeen orders of magnitude!
