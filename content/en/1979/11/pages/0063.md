At the origins of the Olympic movement

The main attention should be paid to the struggle for mastery of the basics of science, ... organization of competitions, ... academic battles of groups and schools, competitions for the best physicists and mathematicians.

From the resolutions of the VII All-Union Conference of the Komsomol (1932).

On November 28, 1933, a regional competition for pinover mathematicians took place at the Tbilisi Russian Theater for Young Spectators. On stage - 42 students from Georgian, Russian, Armenian schools; in the auditorium - friends of the “fighters of the mathematical fort”, their parents, teachers, representatives of the party and Komsomol organizations of the district.

Student of the VI grade of the 26th school Lado Tsulukidze solved 7 problems and examples in 17 minutes; After another 25 minutes, only empty tables remained on the “battlefield”: all the fighters of the mathematical fort, who had won a quick but bloodless victory, mingled with the spectators. In the meantime, the zhorns were summing up the results of the “battle,” participants in the amateur art performance device concentration concentration.

“The battle is over: two ‘wounded’, no ‘killed’...” Under this heading, the Tbilisi newspaper “Young Worker” on December 5 published detailed reports about the first regional mathematical Olympiad and awarded the winners with certificates of honor.

The Bureau of the Organization of Communist Education of Children under the Central Committee of the Communist Party (6) of Georgia approved the initiative of the 26th school and recommended disseminating experience, as well as holding “academic competitions” in physics, chemistry, biology and other disciplines.

In May 1934, in the Tbilisi system, the Ovshchegorod Olympid of Mathematicians, and in June, 30 tenth grade students - winners of the city Olympid - were invited to the Mathematical Olympid at Tbilisi University.

For their active work in preparing and holding Olympids at school and in the district, Zhorni expressed gratitude to mathematics teachers S. Vashchakmadze and T. Petrakovskaya. Below we publish the memoirs of an honored teacher and personal pensioner Sergei Evstafevich Vashchakmadze about the first experiences of holding mathematical Olympids in Georgia *).

The school is one of the most advanced. The educational department was headed by the famous teacher, author of the first collection of arithmetic problems in the Georgian language, L. Chimakadze.
