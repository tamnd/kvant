Physics Olympiad

From April 12 to 17, the final round of the XIII All-Union Schoolchildren Olympid in Physics took place in Lvov. Its participants were the winners of the Resursikan Olympids and medalists of the previous All-Union Olympids. Among them are 49 eighth-graders, 47 ninth-graders, 52 tenth-graders.

On April 13, schoolchildren completed a theoretical task. Eighth-classics were offered 4 problems, nine-classics and tenth-classics were offered 5 problems each. 4 hours were allotted to solve problems for the 8th classic, 5 hours each for the 9th classic and 10th classic.

Below we present the conditions of these tasks*).

8th grade

1. Figure 1 is taken from a photograph of smoke plumes coming from two steam locomotives moving along a straight section of road at speeds of $v_1 = 50$ km/h and $v_2 = 70$ km/h (top view). Train directions are indicated by arrows. Find the wind speed.

2. Observer of motion with constant speed along some inclined straight line. A body thrown at an angle to the horizon intersects the trajectory of the observed movement with a time interval t. Both times the body is forward, it is observed at the same distance from it. What will the trajectory of the body look like from the point of view of the observable? Subsequent solution on the left and right log.

*) Almost all problems were included in the Problem Book «Kvant» in the 7th and 8th issues of our magazine.

3. There are two heat-conducting vessels. The first of them contains 5 liters of water at a temperature of $t_1 = 60$ °C, the second contains 1 liter of water at a temperature of $t_2 = 20$ °C. First, some of the water was intertwined from the first vessel into the second. Then, when thermal equilibrium was established in the second vessel, so much water was poured from it into the first vessel so that its volumes in the vessels became equal to the original ones. After these operations, the temperature of the water in the first vessel became equal to $t = 59$ °C. How much water was intertwined from the first vessel to the second and back again?

4. Four identical amperes and a resistor are connected as shown in figure 2. Ampere $A_1$ shows 2 A, amperor $A_2$ shows 3 A. What points flow through amperators $A_3$, $A_4$ and the resistor? Find the ratio of the internal resistance of the amperator to the resistance $R$ of the resistor.

9th grade

1. Figure 3 (top view) was taken from a photograph of smoke plumes coming from two steam locomotives moving along a straight section of road at a speed of $v_1 = 50$ km/h and $v_2 = 70$ km/h (top view). Train directions are indicated by arrows. What is the speed of the third locomotive?

2. A ball of mass 2M is thrown vertically upward with speed $v_0$. A light thread of length $l < v_0^2/2g$ is tied to the ball, at the other end of which there is a groom of mass M. After what time and at what distance from the throwing point will the balls collide? The thread is absolutely rigid.

3. 0.5 liters of water was poured into a small thin-walled metal potato, the potatoes were placed on the stove and, by changing the temperature of the water at different points in time, a graph of temperature versus time was plotted. Then the water was poured out, 0.7 kg of alcohol was poured into the same potatoes and, placing the potatoes on the same stove, a graph of the temperature of the alcohol versus time was plotted. These graphs are shown on figure 4. Having obtained these graphs, determine the specific temperature of alcohol and the specific temperature of its vaporization, if during 30 minutes of boiling the amount of alcohol was poured into the potatoes by half. The heat capacity of potatoes is 200 J/K. Evaporation from the surface of the liquid.

4. The insulated vessel is evacuated to a high vacuum. The monatomic ideal gas surrounding the vessel has a temperature of $T_0$. At some points the tap is opened and the vessel is filled with gas. What temperature $T$ will the gas in the vessel have after filling it?

5. Four identical amperes and a resistor are connected as shown in figure 2. Ampere $A_1$ shows 2 A, amperor $A_2$ shows 3 A. What points flow through amperators $A_3$, $A_4$ and the resistor? Find the ratio of the internal resistance of the ampere to the resistance $R$ of the resistor.
