Experiments with water in the cold

Why do water pipes often burst and water bottles burst in winter? Why do icicles appear on the roofs of houses with the arrival of spring, and why do ridges sometimes fall to the ground along with the rain in the summer? Why does the water in deep rivers and lakes not freeze until the day?

A lot of similar waters can be formed, because freezing water has many features. You can get acquainted with some of them during experiments with water in the cold.

All the experiments offered to you are very simple; they do not require any special preparation. However, we give some advice. It is better to conduct experiments at a temperature no higher than -5°C. Remember that cylindrical glass or plastic containers with water can burst in the cold, so whenever possible, use finite-shaped containers. In some experiments you will come across a saturated solution of boiled salt. It is better to prepare it in advance by dissolving 30 g of boiled salt in 100 ml of warm water.

Having completed the experiment, he independently identifies the observed phenomena and answers all questions that arise. If you cannot do this, read the answers and explanations in the article.

So, you have to conduct the following experiments:

1. In a cold, unheated room (for example, in a barn, closet, garage) or on a balcony, place a glass plate on a table or other horizontal surface, put a glass, turn it upside down, an overturned saucer and, finally, a large basin. Using a narrow tube, drop a few drops of water onto the plate and fill the indentations in the stem of the glass and saucer with water, pour 2-3 grains of water into the basin.

When the water freezes, look closely at the surface of the ice. You will see that the frozen drops have become sharpened, and cone-shaped tubercles have formed in the middle of the recesses of the glass and saucer (Fig. 1). And in Tauu a historical ice mound grew.
