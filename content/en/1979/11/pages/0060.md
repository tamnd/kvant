At a certain viewing angle, a stripe appears at the bottom of the half-cylinder (along its entire length), through which the frosted glass is not visible (Fig. 14). As the viewing angle decreases, this band overlaps with a higher frequency half-cylinder. At a certain viewing angle, the upper boundary of this stripe coincides with the line drawn on the ground glass (see Fig. 14, $\theta$). In this case, the boundary rays (reflecting from the axis) emerge from the half-linder without being refracted along the radius (Fig. 15).
