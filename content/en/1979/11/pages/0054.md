⟦folio 52⟧

1960, when representatives of other republics and regions approached the 23rd Moscow Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren for the first time. And 12 years ago, here in Tbilisi, the First All-Union Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics took place.

The jury of the current Olympics included several “veterans” who took part in all these competitions. And among them, first of all, I would like to name N. B. Vasilyev, a researcher at Moscow University, deputy representative of the methodological commission on mathematics under the USSR Ministry of Education, one of the most active readers and enthusiasts of all-Union Olympiads.

The Ministry of Education of Georgia prepared and published a special collection “Truth Book of Young Mathematicians” for the Olympiad. One of the compilers of this collection is an honored teacher of the GSSR, director of the village. w. No. 42 Tbilisi O. I. Gaganidze - was a winner of the first mathematical Olympiad, held in Georgia in 1933. This collection contains information about the types of Olympiad participants
