On April 16, schoolchildren were told the solutions to problems of the second type, and then an individual analysis began - each Olympada participant had the opportunity to discuss their work in detail with the jury members (to file an appeal). Such a discussion is very useful, it helps the children understand what is a comprehensive solution to a problem, what strict logical reasoning is, and, as a rule, expressing their thoughts.

On April 17, in the assembly hall of Tbilisi University, the ceremonial closing of the XIII All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics took place. Based on the results of the final stage, 14 diplomas of the 1st degree, 31 diplomas of the 2nd degree and 36 diplomas of the 3rd degree were awarded. In addition, 8 eighth graders, 15 ninth graders, and 21 tenth graders were loaded with diplomas. The rest of the Olympada participants were awarded memorable student diplomas (these diplomas were established for the first time)*.

Some Olympada participants were awarded special recognition: established government institutions, public organizations, groups of arbitrary proposals of Georgia. Eighth-graders Aleksandr Babak (school no. 2, Kamenka village), Konstantin Levitsky (school no. 16, Kara-Balty), Chariyar Ashiralev (school no. 5, Kara-Kala) received special recognition from the magazine «Kvant» for their success in the Olympics; 9th graders Lola Satafova (school no. 1, Dushanbe), Bachuk Mesablivili (boarding school named after Komarov, Tbilisi), Geortsei Pavlashivili (boarding school named after Komarov, Tbilisi).
