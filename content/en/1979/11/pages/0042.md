What explains this?

Since glass is a poor conductor of heat, the hardening of water in a glass tube is very slow and uneven. Over a long period of time, two or three columns of ice are formed, which are joined to each other. Cracks appear at the joints.

In a metal tube whose walls have good thermal conductivity, the entire mass of water freezes quickly and evenly. The ice column turns out to be monolithic.

1. Through the point $K$ inside a triangle with side lengths $a, b, c$, lines are drawn parallel to the sides. The lengths of the segments between the sides are equal to $a_1, b_1, c_1$, respectively. Prove that

a) $\frac{a_1}{a} + \frac{b_1}{b} + \frac{c_1}{c} = 2$;

b) $\frac{4}{3} \leq \frac{a_1^2}{a^2} + \frac{b_1^2}{b^2} + \frac{c_1^2}{c^2} \leq 2$.

2. Through the point $K$ inside the triangle $ABCD$ with the areas of the faces $S_a, S_b, S_c$ and $S_d$, four planes are drawn parallel to the faces of the triangle. The areas of the resulting sections are equal to $S_1, S_2, S_3, S_4$, respectively. Prove that

a) $\sqrt{\frac{S_1}{S_a}} + \sqrt{\frac{S_2}{S_b}} + \sqrt{\frac{S_3}{S_c}} + \sqrt{\frac{S_4}{S_d}} = 3$;

b) $\frac{S_1}{S_a} + \frac{S_2}{S_b} + \frac{S_3}{S_c} + \frac{S_4}{S_d} \geq \frac{9}{4}$.

3. Through the point $K$ inside the triangle, lines are drawn parallel to its edges $|BC| = a, |AC| = b, |AB| = c, |AD| = d_1, |BD| = d_2, |CD| = d_3$. The lengths of these straight segments contained between the faces are equal to $a', b', c', d_1', d_2', d_3'$, respectively. Prove that

$\frac{a'}{a} + \frac{b'}{b} + \frac{c'}{c} + \frac{d_1'}{d_1} + \frac{d_2'}{d_2} + \frac{d_3'}{d_3} = 3$.
