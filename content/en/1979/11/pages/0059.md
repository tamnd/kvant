9th grade
Determine, with the smallest error, the unknown resistance, the internal resistance of the current source and its electromotive force.

Equipment: DC source, milliammeter, two resistances (known and unknown), key, connecting wires.

10th grade
1. A rectifier with negligibly low internal resistance and does not contain a ripple smoothing filter is given. Determine whether the resistance circuit is single-half or double-half.

Equipment: two identical calorimeters with identical electric heaters, thermometers, a high-capacity electrical capacitor (the voltage drop across the capacitor over a time of 0.02 s when discharging through the resistance of the heater is negligible) and a diode.

2. Determine as accurately as possible the refractive index of the glass from which the half-cylinder with a polished surface is made.

Equipment: frosted glass, square, ruler, light bulb (connected to the rectifier terminals), white and black paper.
