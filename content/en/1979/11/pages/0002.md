In the 1943 lithograph of Reptiles above, substitute Dutch artist Escher let his imagination run wild to depict an unannounced event.

...On the table abandoned by the artist, in disarray among cacti, books, sirets and matches, a model of a dodecasdrome, a bottle and a glass, a square, lies a sketch of the famous figure Escher “Yashers” (see, for example, the cover «Kvant», 1977, No. 3). And suddenly the flat jasher-patterns begin to acquire volume, wait, crawl intently and ominously across the table, and even spew out flames. But soon they return and are once again lost in their two-dimensional image, in the capable and beautiful laws of geometry.
