ELECTRIC LOGO
TRANSPORTS WARRIORS

figure shows a circular section of the railway track with place M and dead end A. On this section there is an electric locomotive E and two cars: a freight car T and a tank car C. How should an electric locomotive maneuver in order to swap places between a freight car and a tank car and end up in its original place?
