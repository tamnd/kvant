ELECTRIC LOGO
TRANSPORTS WARRIORS

figure shows a circular section of the railway track with place M and dead end A. On this section there is an electric locomotive E and two cars: a freight car T and a tank car C. Only an electric locomotive (without cars) can pass through the places. Cars can only be moved when they are coupled to an electric locomotive. Only one of the cars is allowed to stand in a dead end. How should an electric locomotive maneuver in order to swap places between a freight car and a tank car and end up in its original place?

F. Bartenev
