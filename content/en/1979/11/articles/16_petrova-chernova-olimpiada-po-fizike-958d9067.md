Physics Olympiad

From April 12 to 17, the final round of the XIII All-Union Schoolchildren Olympid in Physics took place in Lvov. Its participants were the winners of the Resursikan Olympids and medalists of the previous All-Union Olympids. Among them are 49 eighth-graders, 47 ninth-graders, 52 tenth-graders.

On April 13, schoolchildren completed a theoretical task. Eighth-classics were offered 4 problems, nine-classics and tenth-classics were offered 5 problems each. 4 hours were allotted to solve problems for the 8th classic, 5 hours each for the 9th classic and 10th classic.

Below we present the conditions of these tasks*).

8th grade

1. Figure 1 is taken from a photograph of smoke plumes coming from two steam locomotives moving along a straight section of road at speeds of $v_1 = 50$ km/h and $v_2 = 70$ km/h (top view). Train directions are indicated by arrows. Find the wind speed.

2. Observer of motion with constant speed along some inclined straight line. A body thrown at an angle to the horizon intersects the trajectory of the observed movement with a time interval t. Both times the body is forward, it is observed at the same distance from it. What will the trajectory of the body look like from the point of view of the observable? Subsequent solution on the left and right log.

3. There are two heat-conducting vessels. The first of them contains 5 liters of water at a temperature of $t_1 = 60$ °C, the second contains 1 liter of water at a temperature of $t_2 = 20$ °C. First, some of the water was intertwined from the first vessel into the second. Then, when thermal equilibrium was established in the second vessel, so much water was poured from it into the first vessel so that its volumes in the vessels became equal to the original ones. After these operations, the temperature of the water in the first vessel became equal to $t = 59$ °C. How much water was intertwined from the first vessel to the second and back again?
