M551. a) What is the smallest number of points necessary to mark inside a convex pentagon so that inside any triangle with vertices at the vertices of the pentagon there is at least one marking point?
6)* The same question for a convex $n$-gon.
Moscow City Mathematical Olympiad

M552. a) Find at least one pair $(p, q)$ of non-zero integers for which the trilenes $x^2 + px + q$ and $x^2 + qx + p$ have integer roots.
6) Find all such pairs $(p, q)$.
E Turkevich

M553*. Given a triangle $ABC$, and $|BC| < |AC| < |AB|$. On bows $BA$ and $CA$ the segments $BD$ and $CE$ are distinguished as well as $|BD| = |CE| = |BC|$. Prove that the radius of the circumscribed circle around triangle $ADE$ is equal to the distance between the centers of the circumscribed circle around triangle $ABC$ and the circle circumscribed to it.
S. Maidman

M554*. Let us call a natural number $n$ good if there exist natural numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k$ (not necessarily distinct) such that
$$a_1 + a_2 + \dots + a_k = n,$$
And
$$\frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{a_k} = 1.$$

It is known that all numbers between 33 and 73 are good. Prove that all numbers greater than 73 are also good numbers.
US Mathematical Olympiad (1978)

M555. Intersection distance
a) two;
b) three
cylinders of the same radius $r$, the axes of which are mutually perpendicular and pass through one point. How many planes of symmetry does this intersection have? What is its volume?
S. Pukhov
