⟦folio 42⟧

Quests

1. Read 14 words written in numbers:
   5916, 1378, 609136, 6094, 9721,
   591, 690958, 634, 7958, 8374,
   61274, 790968, 4242025, 79691,
to do this, replacing the example given with figure by multiplying letters by numbers (the same letters correspond to the same numbers).

2. In the language of some captivity, any combination of eight different letters I, G, R, E, T, N, O, K is a word and there are no other words. The leader of the captivity, having learned about the existence of dictionaries, instructed his courtier Linguistitis to compile a similar dictionary from all the words of the captivity. The linguist wrote down the letters in the order I, G, R, E, T, N, O, K and began to order the words in accordance with this alphabet. He must do the words YOKONTRGI. What word should he write next? And after the word IYONGTCOR? After the word IGCONTER? Can you suggest to Linguistit an easy way to organize words in a dictionary? By the way, what will be the last word?

3. The problem is a joke. There are stools and chairs in the room. Each stool has 3 legs, each chair has 4 legs. With people sitting on all the stools and chairs, there are only 39 legs in the room. How many chairs and how many stools are there in the room?

4. Two people are running up the steps of a subway escalator. One runs faster than the other. Which of them can count more steps?
