“... In his small room there were no bedspreads, carpets, paintings - only a bed, a table, a chair, a bookcase with several books and several stoves of prints. Any property was a burden to him, and in the desire to own property, he saw the deepest basis for quarrels and war between people...”
Max Born

“...Einstein was... in my impression... a lonely man... He had, of course, countless students, if by this we mean people who, by reading his books or listening to his lectures, learned from him a new view of physics, the nature of our world, learned a new worldview. But he did not create the OLS school. He had few students whom he cared about as his disciples and followers. Two lone scientists always lived in it, which sharply distinguished it from the scientific groups that are popular today. There were no two collaborations in it, under the sign of which the development of some other sciences proceeds. In recent years, Einstein worked together with some collaborators. They were usually called his assistants... Collaborators for Einstein were what he lacked in his youth..."
Robert Oppenheimer

“...Einstein’s eyes are myopic and distracted. It seems that for a long time and forever more than half of his gaze turned somewhere inside. It seems that a significant part of Einstein’s vision is constantly occupied, along with his thought, with some kind of outline of calculations. The eyes are therefore full of abstract thought and even seem a little sad. Meanwhile, in the dormitory, Einstein is an extremely cheerful person. He loves to joke... he will be swept away by good-natured, completely childish laughter. At the same time, for a moment his eyes become completely childish. His extraordinary simplicity creates a charm that just wants to somehow caress him, shake his hand, pat him on the shoulder - and do this, of course, with great respect. What you get is some kind of feeling of tender participation, recognition of great defenseless simplicity and at the same time a feeling of boundless respect...”
Anatoly Vasilievich Lunacharsky
