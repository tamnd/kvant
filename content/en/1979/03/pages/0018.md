Only the predominance of natural scientific interests over mathematical interests, but also the following timely feeling. I saw that mathematics is divided into many special areas and each of them can occupy the entire short life allotted to us. And I saw myself in the position of Buridan’s donkey, who cannot decide which armful of hay he should take. The point was, very clearly, that my intuition in the field of mathematics was not strong enough to confidently distinguish the basic and important from the rest of scholarship, which could still be dispensed with. In addition, the interest in exploring nature was undoubtedly stronger; It was not yet clear to me as a student that access to deeper fundamental problems in physics requires the most subtle mathematical methods. This became clear to me only gradually, after many years of independent scientific work. Of course, physics was also divided into special areas, and each of them could absorb a short working life without satisfying the thirst for deeper knowledge. The vast amount of insufficiently recognized empirical facts is overwhelming here. But here I soon learned to look for what could lead to depth, and to discard everything else, everything that overloads the mind and distracts from the essential...
