⟦folio 6⟧

Whether the electromagnetic field is made of quanta or, as Planck thought, the point is only that the atom emits and absorbs light in portions, like an author who measures out body or gasoline in portions.

Eischstein's point of view turned out to be correct. In his work on the photographic effect, the concept of an elementary particle - a quantum of light - was born. The first elementary part was the electron, the second was the quantum of light - photon. Since then, the list of elementary particles has been continuously updated.

IV. Brownian motion

“On the movement of suspended particles, requiring a molecular-kinetic theory of heat” was the title of the second work of the “golden year” (it was received by the editor on May 11).

Eischstein compares the movement of a molecule in a liquid and the movement of a small floating ball inside this liquid (as they say, “suspended”). Both the molecule and the microsopper ball, subject to impacts from other molecules, move in the otic fluid. Considering this similarity in more detail, Eischstein, based on the most general laws of kinetic theory, showed that the average kinetic energies of a moving molecule and a ball should be the same.

“...according to the molecular theory of heat, bodies with dimensions of the order of 1/1000 mm, suspended in a liquid, undergo visible random motion, which is obligatory for the thermal motion of molecules. Such a movement is actually observed by biologists, who call it Brownian movement” (from a letter from Eischstein to his friend Habicht in the spring of 1905).

A ball in a liquid, moving randomly, will still slowly mix in the liquid, “diffuse”
