On this figure our artist depicted the “magic circle” of the American natural scientist B. Franklin (1706-1790). Of the 64 numbers located on them, in at least 28 natural ways, eight numbers are distinguished, the sum of which is 348. These are eight numbers lying in eight concentric stakes, eight numbers lying in eight sectors of eight numbers lying in three pink eccentric stakes, three blue eccentric stakes, three yellow and three green ones. Look for other interesting properties of this configuration.
