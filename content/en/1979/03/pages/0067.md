MAGICAL
STEREO SQUARE

The cube is divided into 125 small cubes, 25 of which are placed with numbers from 1 to 25. Below is how the marked cubes will be located in each of the five horizontal layers of the cube. If we now look at the cube from above, then from the front, and then from the side, then we will see completely filled 5x5 squares (see figure above), which of which is magic, i.e. the sum of the numbers in each horizontal row, each vertical row and along the diagonal of each such square is the same.
