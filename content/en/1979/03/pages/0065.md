3. Let us select a section of the surface of a ball of a single area and find the force acting on the selected section. The electric field voltage at the surface of the ball is $|\vec{E}| = \frac{Q}{4\pi \varepsilon_0 R^2}$ on the outside and $|\vec{E}_{\text{BH}}| = 0$ on the inside. Let $\vec{E}'$ be the direction of the field created by a single surface, and $\vec{E}''$ be the direction of the field created by the entire remaining surface near the selected area. Using the superposition principle, for the field above and below the area (inside the sphere) we obtain:

$$|\vec{E}'| + |\vec{E}''| = |\vec{E}|, \quad |\vec{E}'| - |\vec{E}''| = 0.$$
From here we find the direction of the field acting on the selected area: $|\vec{E}''| = |\vec{E}|/2$. Then the force acting on a unit surface area is

$$|\vec{F}| = |\vec{E}''| \frac{Q}{4\pi R^2} = \frac{Q^2}{32\pi^2 \varepsilon_0 R^4}.$$
