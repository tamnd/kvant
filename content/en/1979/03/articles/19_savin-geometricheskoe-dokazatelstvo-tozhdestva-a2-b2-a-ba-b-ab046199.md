4. In the middle of a horizontal tube of length $L=1$ m, pumped out and sealed at both ends, there is a container of mercury of length $l=20$ cm. If the tube is placed vertically, the container of mercury will shift by $x=10$ cm. To what pressure was it pumped out?

$$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b).$$

Geometric point proof
$$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b).$$
