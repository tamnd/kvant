⟦folio 56⟧

15 years
Chelyabinsk NOU

15 years of interesting and fruitful work have passed by the Chelyabinsk Scientific Society of Students. During this time, ten thousand schoolchildren found their recognition in him. But the most important thing is that the commandment of everyone became the words: “For the Motherland - our thoughts, talents, deeds!”

Currently, the society has 68 sections and groups in institutes and 27 films in schools in the city and region, in which more than 3.5 thousand high school students are involved, passionate about various fields of science and interested in sectors of the national economy characteristic of the Southern Urals. The content, forms and methods of work of the society contribute to the fact that its members, in their everyday activities, not only acquire new knowledge and skills, but it is also customary to continue learning in voluntary work.

It is indicative for our NOU that former members of the NOU, new specialists, return to it as section leaders. Secondary teachers of the Pedagogical Institute I. Zhkov and E. Zhmaeva, candidate of medical sciences V. Tsirkin, graduate student of the Department of Mathematics of the Polytechnic Institute V. Konnov, literature teacher of school No. 1 of Chelyabinsk L. Melnikova. In total, more than 200 scientists and teachers from colleges in Chelyabinsk teach children to love science.

Over the past 15 years, the society has developed its own traditions. The best of them is the connection of research with life, with the needs of production. Thus, the work of the physiology section “The state of health of schoolchildren in different times of the year”, “Daily and seasonal fluctuations in heart rate in people of different ages”, “Oxygen capacity of the lungs of speed skaters”, carried out under the guidance of teachers of the Department of Physiology of ChSMI, are of great practical importance for the physiology of human labor and sports.

On the instructions of the Department of Analytical Chemistry of the Chelyabinsk Polytechnic Institute, students, under the guidance of senior teacher of ChPI G. T. Kiriyanova, completed a series of analyzes of iron alloys for the content of inkel, colabite, chromium, manganese and other elements. The guys determined the percentage of these components in the alloys, which helped in conducting laboratorium classes with students.

The automobile design section, together with scientists from the technical sector of the research institute, began work on creating a working model of a forward-looking wheeled tractor. “One of the further stages of the work was the development and creation of a working model for conducting laboratory research and testing the theoretical laws of side turn of wheeled engines,” this is how General Designer of ChTZ, Doctor of Technical Sciences I. S. Kavyarov assessed the work of the young automakers.

Members of the NOU covered the technology of obtaining various pitents in school conditions, and investigated the patterns of action of new metal corrosion ingredients introduced into industry. All this convincingly proves that, under the guidance of scientists, students can conduct research at a high scientific level.

Behind the pages of each work one can see painstaking, not always interesting, but purposeful and persistent work - work that brings satisfaction to the student, the materials created and spiritual values.

Creative work at non-governmental educational institutions contributes to the formation of sustainable interests of children. For large members of NOUs, the question “Who are you?” was solved relatively easily - more than 90% of the selected professions are close to the section in which they are engaged. It is also noteworthy that members of non-governmental educational institutions who entered institutes quickly find their way into scientific student societies, and those who went to work in factories soon become activists of VOIR.

Work at non-governmental educational institutions also influences the development of social activity of schoolchildren. At annual training camps at the summer camp “Young Kurchatovets” in the city of Sima - the homeland of Academician I.V. Kurchatov - the guys not only share, but also exchange work experience, gain skills in propaganda technical and political knowledge. As a result, more than 100 instructors-social activists of the NOU lead clubs in junior grades and carry out extensive propaganda work. In general, 73% of NOU members are engaged in public work.

The children chose the words “Fight, search, find and defend!” as the motto of our society. And members of the NOU have been fighting for the implementation of these words in life for 15 years now.

Reviewing the path traveled by society, the well-filmed army of Chelyabinsk schoolchildren dreams of achieving a lot, keeping in mind the words of the wonderful, accomplished writer, a favorite of young people, Nikolai Ostrovsky: “Only forward!... And nowhere else.”

T. Kuznetsova,
deputy Chairman of the Council of Circles of NOU
