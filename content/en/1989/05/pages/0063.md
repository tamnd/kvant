Thus, we see that Newton's laws represent a powerful and fairly universal research tool. After the discovery of Newton's law, the scope of their application continuously expanded. The primal procession of classical mechanics lasted a very long time, almost 150 years. By the beginning of the 19th century, physicists almost believed in its infallibility. And not only in infallibility, but also in omnipotence. The conviction arose that mechanics is capable of completely explaining the structure of the world, that everything in the world can ultimately be reduced to the movement of interacting particles; also the views of the navali mechanical.

The hope for a purely scientific explanation of the world greatly inspires materialist thinkers, especially the French enlighteners. However, many of them were haunted by the philosophical problem that arose along with the mechanism, but is still discussed today. The gist of it is this. If the world is a mechanical system, then the basic problem of mechanics can be solved for it. In other words, everything about the world at some point

It doesn’t matter that such a calculation is not within the power of human processions - the way for this exists imaginary “from above,” said the French thinker Laplace; it is important that everything in the world is completely predetermined and cannot be changed in any way. It only seems to us that we have free will and freedom of choice. In fact, all our thoughts and actions are completely predetermined, and “from above” knows what will happen to us, our children, and the whole world at any given time. This philosophical doctrine about the interconnection and interaction of phenomena of the material and spiritual world is called Laplace determininism.

Of course, the human mind could not come to terms with the lack of freedom and was looking for a way out of the impasse. First of all, further significant forces of universal gravity or Coulomb forces came under suspicion. In fact, in such cases there is something supernatural - we shift one particle a little bit, and the other, a hundred kilometers away, already knows about this and accelerates a little bit differently! However, it was not possible to detect any deviation from this rule, and physicists, gnawing at their hearts, accepted these forces as they are. This continued until the discovery of Maxwell and Einstein.

In the 60s of the 19th century, Maxwell built theories of the electromagnetic field - a new material substance through which electrical and magnetic interactions occur.
