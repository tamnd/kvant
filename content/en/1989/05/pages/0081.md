10. 1 or 5. Solution. In fact, it is true that of the three lengths of projections of a segment onto the sides of a regular triangle, one is equal to the sum of the other two. It can be proven like this. Let, when projecting a segment onto the sides of a regular triangle, its length is multiplied, respectively, by $a$, $\beta$, $\gamma$. But then, when projecting the side of this triangle onto a straight line containing our segment, their lengths are multiplied by $a$, $\beta$, $\gamma$. At the same time, it is clear that the projection of one of the sides of a triangle is the sum of the projections of its other two sides.
