⟦folio 62⟧

acting on a charge at some point in time is now determined not by where it is and how other charges are moving at the same point in time, but by what happens to it at the previous moment. In such a situation, we will no longer be able, as before, to solve the “main problem of mechanics.”

In 1905, Einstein showed that any interaction must have the same properties, since no signal can travel faster than the speed of light. (It is worth noting that Newton’s laws have not lost their meaning at all. They work perfectly when the speeds of particles are smaller than the speed of light - in this case, effective delays are insignificant.) So, questions of the mechanical view, the world turned out to be filled not only with moving particles, but also with wave fields of materials. The picture of the world has become very complicated, its development is no longer described by the solution of the main problem of mechanics,

but... free will does not subside yet. All the same, everything can be predicted - only now it is necessary to put into the “top mind” initial information not only about particles, but also about fields (and force the “top mind” to solve much more complex equations that these fields obey). How is life?

Help arrived from the other side - from physicists who were studying the atom device. It turns out that in the life of atoms there are laws that are radically different from the laws of classical physics. To describe the properties of the microworld, a special science was created - quantum mechanics. Let us note only one important feature of quantum processes - there is no predetermination in them! In many cases, it is fundamentally impossible to predict which options one should implement; one can only calculate their probability. However, even quantum mechanics does not lead to a final solution to the question of free will.

A. I. Cherpotsan
