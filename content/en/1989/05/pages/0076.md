Option 2
1. A body is thrown vertically upward from a height of $H=20$ m with an initial speed of $v_0=3$ m/s. At what height is the body $t=2$ s after the start of motion? Neglect air resistance. Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s$^2$.

2. A ball of mass $m=4$ kg, suspended on a non-stretchable and weightless thread $l=1$ m long, oscillates in a vertical plane. Find the tension force of the thread at the moment when it forms a vertical angle $a=60^\circ$. At this moment the speed of the ball is $v=1,5$ m/s. Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s$^2$.

3. There is a crowbar on a horizontal surface, the length of which is $l=1,5$ m. The mass of the crowbar is $m=10$ kg. How much will the potential energy of the scrap change if it is moved to a vertical position? Gravity acceleration $g=10$ m/s$^2$.

4. It is required to replace the force $F=5$ N with two forces acting along the same straight line, but in opposite directions. The smaller of these forces is $F_1=11$ N. What is the magnitude of the second force?

5. What volume does a gas occupy at a temperature of $T=300$ K and a pressure of $p=414$ Pa, if the number of gas molecules is $N=5\cdot 10^{24}$? Bigman's constant $k=1,38\cdot 10^{-23}$ J/K.

6. A transformer immersed in oil begins to heat up as a result of overload. What is the efficiency of the transformer if, at full power $P=60$ kW, oil weighing $m=60$ kg heats up by $\Delta t=30^\circ$ C in $\tau=4$ min of transformer operation? Specific heat capacity of oil $c=2000$ J/(kg K).

7. Two identical small metal balls charged with the same charges are located at a distance of $l_0=1$ m from each other. The charge of one of them is 4 times greater than the charge of the other. The balls are brought into contact and separated at some distance. Find this distance if the force of interaction between the balls remains the same?

8. Two conductors whose resistances are $R_1=7$ Ohm and $R_2=5$ Ohm are connected in parallel and connected to a current source. An amount of heat $Q_1=300$ J was released in the first conductor over a period of time. What amount of heat is released in the second conductor during the same time?

9. The oscillating circuit with a capacitance condensate $C_1=1$ uF is set to a frequency of $v_1=400$ Hz. If another capacitor is connected in parallel with this capacitor, then the frequency of oscillations in the counter becomes equal to $v_2=200$ Hz. Determine the capacitance of the second capacitor.

10. The object is located at a distance of $d=0,05$ m from a biconvex lens, and its minimum image is at a distance of $f=0,5$ m. What is the optical power of this lens?
