NEUTRONS SEARCH FOR A KILLER,
or Activation Analysis Records

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. S. STEINBERG

The title of this article is suggested by a detective plot, which the patient reader will become acquainted with at the very end. In the meantime, let’s take a look at things that are far from forensics and let’s talk about how you can determine the chemical composition of a substance, that is, find out which elements and in what proportion are present in a sample.

From your chemistry course, you know that this can be done using chemical reactions characteristic of substances and elements, using the methods of so-called “wet chemistry.” However, in some cases the application of these methods encounters serious difficulties. For example, when it is necessary to perform a chemical analysis of a very small sample; when extremely high accuracy of analysis of many elements at the same time is required. For example, it may require identifying one “foreign” atom among a million or 100 million others and determining what type it is.
