Moscow Higher Technical University N. E. Bauman

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. On the line $x-2y=2$, find the point through which two tangents to the graph of the function $y=x^2/2$, perpendicular to each other, pass.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sin 5x-\cos(x+\pi/2)=\sin(2x-\pi/2).$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\log_2(3x+5)=3-\log_2(x+1).$$
4. Solve the inequality
$$5^x+\frac{15}{2-5^x}<0.$$
5. A cone with an angle $\alpha$ between the generatrix and the height of the visian into a sphere of radius $R$ so that its vertex is in the center of the sphere, and the circumference of the base is on the sphere. All vertices of the lower base of a straightened triangular prism (parallel to the base of the cone) lie on the sphere, and its remaining vertices belong to the lateral surface of the cone. What should be the height and side of the base of the prism so that its lateral surface area is the flattest? Find this area value.

Option 2
1. According to the plan, one team needs to produce 240 more parts than the other in the same time. Due to the fact that 5 people are not working in the first brigade, and 4 people are not working in the second, each brigade completed the plan two days later. How many workers went to work in each team if each of them produced 6 parts a day?
2. Solve the equation
$$3\cos^2(x+\pi/2)+\cos^2x=\sqrt{3}\cos x.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\lg 2x}{\lg(4x-15)}=2.$$
4. Find all values ​​of the parameter $p$ for which the system of equation
$$\begin{cases} y=x^2-2x, \\ x^2+y^2+p^2=2x+2py \end{cases}$$
has a solution.
5. The visian ball has a regular quadrangular paramid; We use a Visian cylinder, one of the bases of which lies in the plane of the base of the paramid, and the circle of the other base touches all the lateral faces. The height of the cylinder and the radius of its base are equal to $a$. At what height of the paramid will the volume of the sphere be the smallest? Find this volume value.

Physics
Oral exam objectives
1. A rope of length $l=20$ m and mass $m=1$ kg is thrown over a nail driven into a vertical wall. At the initial moment, the rope rises symmetrically and is at rest, and then, as a result of a slight push, it begins to slide along the nail. What will be the impulse of the rope when it slips off the nail? The forces of resistance are the challenger.
2. There are identical balls at the ends and in the middle of a non-issom rod of length $l$. The rod is placed vertically and released. Assuming that there is no friction between the plane and the lower ball, find the speed of the upper ball at the moment of impact with the horizontal surface.
3. A concrete homogeneous connection with mass $m$ lies on two bodies of water with a depth $h$, greater than the length of the connection $l$. Having attached the cable to one end of the tie, it is slowly pulled out of the water so that the center of gravity of the tie rises to a height of $H$ from the surface of the water ($H>l$). What work is done in this case? The density of concrete is $n$ times the density of water. The forces of resistance are the challenger.
4. At a distance $R$ from the center of the uncharged metal ball there is an exact charge $q$. Determine the potential of the ball.
5. In a uniform magnetic field with induction $B=0,1$ T, a flat wire coil is located so that its plane is perpendicular to the induction line. The coil is connected to a galvanometer. When turning the coil through the galvanometer, a charge $Q=9,5\cdot 10^{-3}$ Cl. At what angle did you turn the coil? The coil area is $S=10^3$ cm², the coil resistance is $R=2$ Ohm.
7. At what distance from the coincipation line should an object be placed so that the distance between the object and its actual image is minimal? The linear focal length is $F$.
8. Determine whether it is possible to incize an unexcited hydrogen atom by an external electric field with a voltage of $E=10^8$ V/m.
9. The minimum electron energy required to incise a hydrogen atom is $W_0$. Determine the minimum initial energies of hydrogen and helium ions required for the incision of a hydrogen atom. Assume that the incision will pass as a result of an absolutely inelastic impact.
10. In the periodic table of elements, three elements are located side by side. Let's call them $a$, $b$ and $c$. The radioactive isotope of element $a$ turns into element $b$, which, in turn, turns into element $c$. The latter turns into an isotope of the parent element $a$. What processes are responsible for the transitions $a \rightarrow b$, $b \rightarrow c$, $c \rightarrow a$?
