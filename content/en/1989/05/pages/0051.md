⟦folio 49⟧

Adviсe
to the examiner

1. First of all, explain to the examinee that his entire professional career could collapse due to his unsuccessful answer. Emphasize to him the importance of the situation. Put it in place from the beginning.

2. Ask the hardest questions right away. If the first question is difficult or confusing enough, the examinee becomes too confused to answer the next question, no matter how simple it may be.

3. When addressing the examinee, maintain the following conditions and dryness, and be very cheerful with the examiners. Effectively address other examiners with mocking substitutes from time to time

concerns about the examinee’s answers, ignoring him as if he were not in the room.

4. Force the examinee to solve problems using your method, especially if the method is unusual. Limit the examinee by putting a lot of instructions and qualifications into each question. The idea here lies in the conditions of the task, which without this would be simple.

5. Force the examinee to make a trivial mistake and let him puzzle over it for as long as possible. Immediately after he notices the mistake, but just before he understands how to correct it, disdainfully correct him yourself. This requires high insight and precision of selection

moment that can only be achieved with more practice.

6. When the examinee begins to drown, do not help him in any way to get out. Yawn... and move on to the next question.

7. Ask the examinee from time to time questions: “Didn’t you go through this in elementary school?”
