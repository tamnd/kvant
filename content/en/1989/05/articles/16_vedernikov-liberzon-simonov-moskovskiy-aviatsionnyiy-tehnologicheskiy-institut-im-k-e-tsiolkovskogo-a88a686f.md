Moscow Aviation Technological Institute named after. K. E. Tsiolkovsky

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Solve the inequality
$$3|x-1| > x+1.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$x^3 - 1gx = 10x^2.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin 4x + \sin 12x + \cos 4x = 0.$$
4. Two buses left $A$ for $B$ at the same time. The first bus had a speed of 4 km/h more than the second, and arrived at $B$ 10 minutes earlier. Find the speeds of the buses if the distance between $A$ and $B$ is 48 km.
5. Medians of a right triangle drawn to the legs, referring as $\sqrt{2}:1$. Find the angles of the triangle.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{x+2} = x-4.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{5^x-1} > \frac{1}{1-5^{x-1}}.$$
3. Solve the equation
$$\sin^2 x - \sin^2 2x + \sin^2 3x = \frac{1}{2}.$$
4. The first term of a geometric progression is equal to 2, and the sum of the first eight terms is 5 times the sum of the first four terms. Find the ninth term of the progression.
5. The medians $AN$ and $BM$ of triangle $ABC$ are 6 and 9 respectively and intersect at point $K$, with angle $AKB$ equal to $30^\circ$. Find the area of ​​triangle $ABC$.

Physics
Oral exam assignment
1. From a hose lying on the ground, there will be a stream of water at an angle $\alpha = 30^\circ$ to the horizon with an initial speed of $v = 10$ m/s. The cross-sectional area of ​​the hose is $S = 5$ cm². Determine the mass of water in the air. The density of water is $\rho = 1000$ kg/m³.
2. What thrust force must be developed by an engine on an Earth satellite of mass $m$ in order for it to move in an orbit of radius $R$ at a speed exceeding 2 times the speed of free motion in this orbit? Earth mass $M$, graviton constant $G$.
3. Two bodies are thrown from a height of $h = 20$ m with a speed of $v_0 = 15$ m/s each. At what speeds will the bodies fall to the ground if the first body is thrown vertically upward and the second is thrown horizontally? Ignore air resistance.
4. A cylinder with a volume of $V_1 = 2$ l containing 1 mole of gas at a temperature of $t_1 = 27^\circ\text{C}$ was connected to another balloon with a volume of $V_2 = 4$ l containing 2 moles of this gas at a temperature of $t_2 = 87^\circ\text{C}$. Determine the pressure and temperature of the gas after setting the equation. Avoid heat exchange with the environment. Universal gas constant $R = 8,31$ J/(mol K).
5. A cube of mass $m = 200$g wetted with water floats on the surface of the water. The edge of the cube has a length of $a = 10$ cm. At what distance from the surface of the water is the bottom edge of the cube? Density of water $\rho = 1000$ kg/m³, surface tension coefficient of water $\sigma = 0,073$ N/m.
6. A flat-plate air capacitor with capacity $C$ is connected to a current source with voltage $U$. How much work must be done to double the distance between the capacitor plates? What work is done by the source?
7. An electron moves in a circle of radius $R = 10$ cm in a single-mode magnetic field with an indication of $B = 1$ T. Parallel to the magnetic field, a uniform electric field with an intense constant $E = 100$ V/m is excited. During what period of time will the kinetic energy of the electron double?
8. The primary winding of the transformer includes alternating current with a voltage of $U_1 = 220$ V. The current strength in the primary winding is $I_1 = 0,5$ A. Neglecting energy losses, determine the direction at the ends of the secondary winding and the transformation coefficient if the current strength in the secondary winding is $I_2 = 0,1$ A.
9. A lens gives a minimum image of an object, magnified twice, if it is located at a distance of $d = 5$ cm from it. What kind of lens is it - intrinsic or diverging? What is its focal length?
10. When a π-meson, moving at a speed of $v = 2 \cdot 10^8$ m/s, decays into two phonases, photons are recorded that fly in opposite directions. Determine the energy ratio of these photons. The speed of light is $c = 3 \cdot 10^8$ m/s.
