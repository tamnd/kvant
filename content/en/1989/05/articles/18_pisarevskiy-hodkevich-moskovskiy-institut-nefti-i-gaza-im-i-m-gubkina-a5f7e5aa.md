⟦folio 73⟧

Moscow Institute of Oil and Gas named after. I. M. Gubkina

Mathematics
Written exam option
1. Solve the equation
$$3|x-0,75|=x^2+4,5.$$
2. Calculate at $a=1,2$
$$\frac{2\sqrt{2}-0,008a^3}{2+0,2\sqrt{2}a+0,04a^2}.$$
3. Three positive integers form a geometric progression. Find the third term of this progression if its second term is 1 greater than the first.
4. Find the whole solution of the system
$$\begin{cases} \frac{7-x}{x+19} \geq 0, \\ x<-17. \end{cases}$$
5. Find the most complete solution to the inequality
$$\sqrt{5(5-x)}>-25-x.$$
6. Solve the equation
$$0,125 \cdot 4^{2x-3}=\left(\frac{0,25}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^{-x}.$$
7. Compute $4^x$ if $x=\log_2 5+\log_{0,25} 10.$
8. Solve the equation
$$\log_{0,5}(\log_2 x-1)=-1.$$
9. The parabola $y=ax^2+9x+10$ collapses at $y=15x+9$. Find the value of parameter $a$.
10. Compute $25\sin^2\alpha\cos\alpha$ if $\alpha$ is an acute angle and $tg\alpha=0,75$.
11. Calculate at $\alpha=-\frac{2\pi}{9}$
$$\frac{5(\cos 2\alpha-\cos 5\alpha)}{\sqrt{3(\sin 5\alpha+\sin 2\alpha)}}.$$
12. Find the smallest positive root of the equation in degrees
$$\sin x-1=0,5\sin 2x-\cos x.$$
13. The area of ​​an equilateral trapezoid is 32. The cotangent of the angle between the diagonal of the trapezoid and its base is 2. Find the length of the altitude of the trapezoid.
14. Circles of radii 8 and 3 are internally contiguous. Tangent lines are drawn from the center of the larger circle to the smaller circle. Find the length of the tangent.
15. At the base of the straight line lies a right angular triangle, one of the angles of which is equal to 3. The diagonal of the side face of the straight line passing through the other angle makes a right angle $45^\circ$ with the plane of the base. The height of the line is 4. Find the total surface area of ​​the line.
16. A metal ball of radius $\sqrt{2}$ is poured into a cone. The area of ​​the lateral surface of the cone is 3 times greater than the area of ​​its base. Find the height of the cone.
Physics
Written exam
Option 1
1. The speed of a body thrown vertically down from a certain height after $t_1=1$ increases compared to the initial one by $n_1=6$ times. How many times will his speed increase $t_2=2$ s after the throw? Ignore air resistance.
2. A body with a mass of $m=0,5$ kg, falling without an initial speed from a height of $H=9$ m, acquired an increase in speed of $v=12$ m/s compared to the initial speed. Find the force of air resistance, assuming it is constant. The acceleration of the gravity force is $g=10$ m/s².
3. A load with a mass of $m=7$ kg is lifted on a rope with the ground surface vertically to a height of $H=1$ m once at a constant speed, the second time with an acceleration of $a=2$ m/s². By what amount is the work in the second case greater than in the first?
4. A homogeneous ball floats on the surface of water, half immersed in water. What is the volume of the ball if a buoyant force $F=2$ N acts on it? The acceleration of the gravity force is $g=10$ m/s², the density of water is $q=1000$ kg/m³.
5. The gas located in the cylinder under the piston was heated at constant pressure so that its volume increased by 1.5 times. Then the piston was closed and the gas was heated so that its pressure doubled. What is the ratio of the final temperature (absolute) of the gas to its initial temperature?
6. In the process of making the gas, the work done was $A=1000$ J. By what amount will the internal energy of this gas increase if it is given an amount of heat twice as large as in the first case, and the process is carried out isochorically?
7. After a capacitor charged to a potential difference of $U_1=500$ V was connected in parallel with an uncharged capacitor of capacity $C_2=4$ μF, a potential difference of $U_2=100$ V was established between the plates of the capacitors. What is the capacitance of the first capacitor?
8. What charge passes through the cross section of the conductor within $t=5$ s if during this period of time the current uniformly increases from $I_1=0$ to $I_2=12$ A?
9. A ball, suspended externally, performs a vertical harmonious oscillation and, after the start of movement, covers a path equal to the fifth amplitude of its oscillation in $t=10$ s. What is the period of oscillation of the ball?
10. How many times is the mass of a photon corresponding to infrared light with $\lambda=800$ nm less than the mass of a photon corresponding to ultrafilt light with a frequency of $v=1,5\times 10^{15}$ Hz? The speed of light in vacuum is $c=3\cdot 10^8$ m/s.
