⟦folio 30⟧

Tasks
M1161—M1165, F1168—F1172

M1161. A library triangle contains 10 balls (Fig. 1) close together. Prove that if you place 9 balls in it, there will definitely be room left for the tenth one (i.e., the centers of 9 balls will be located in a triangular grid).

N. P. Dolbilin

M1162. Find all integer solutions (x, y) of the equation
$$x^3 - 13xy + y^3 = 13.$$
D. V. Fomin

M1163. Cherezha left point A and arrived at point B, moving along an arbitrary trajectory at an arbitrary speed. Following it, a second turtle left point A, which at each moment of time moved in the direction of the first (at an arbitrary speed) and eventually also arrived at point B. Prove that the path taken by the second turtle (by the time both of them arrived at B) does not exceed the path of the first.

A. H. Shen

M1164. A natural number n is called perfect if it is equal to the sum of all its divisors less than n (for example: 28=1+2+4+7+14). Prove that an odd perfect number (if such a number exists) cannot be divisible by 3, 5, and 7 at the same time.

V. V. Shabunin

M1165. Prove that a square on side n (n is a natural number), placed randomly on a sheet of checkered paper with 1x1 cells, covers at most (n+1)² grid nodes.

V. D. Kotlyar

M1168. Estimate the maximum speed of Lunovodia, a self-propelled vehicle operating on the Moon, controlled from the Earth.

V. I. Shelest

M1169. The tonearm of the turntable is a light straight rod of length L (Fig. 2), at one end of which (at point B) a sound speaker with a stylus is fixed, and the other end is fixed in a hinge, which can rotate without friction about the vertical axis A, passing at a distance R (R>L) from the axis of rotation O of the turntable disk. The needle is placed on the flat, single-water surface of a uniformly rotating disk. Find the steady-state angle α between the tonearm and linear AO.

S. F. Kim, A. I. Latynin
