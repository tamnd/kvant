Advertising!

Dear girls and boys!
If physics truly captivates you, you can realize your passion by becoming a student at the Physics Faculty of Moscow State University. M.V. Lomonosov, one of the oldest educational institutions in our country.

The Faculty of Physics of the University prepares highly qualified specialist physicists of a wide profile to work in research institutes of the USSR Academy of Sciences, in cross-disciplinary institutes and in higher educational institutions.

Physicist is
— theoretical physics and mathematical modeling,
— solid state physics and quantum optics,
— nuclear physics and astrophysics,
— geophysics and biophysics,
— physics of high-temperature superconductivity and cryoelectronics,
— physical aspects of the use of lasers in biology and medicine,
— computer physics
- and much, much more.

You can make the final choice of the area of ​​physics that is most attractive to you at the end of the second year - when assigned to departments of the faculty. Your scientific work will be supervised by the leading educational institutions of our country.

Entrance exams are held from July 4 to July 20. Acceptance of documents - from July 20 to July 3. Additional information about the faculty and the admission procedure can be obtained at the address: 119899, ​​GSP, Moscow, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, Admissions Committee; and from July 20 - by phone: 939-54-95.

Decide! Physician is waiting for you!
