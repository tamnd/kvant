⟦folio 17⟧

“Thanks to Mr. Daguerre’s invention, middle physics has received a realent that is unusually sensitive to the action of light, and a device that will play the same role in the study of light and light phenomena as a microcoson in the study of small objects. This invention creates the basis for new research and new discoveries,” said the French physicist and chemist Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac about photography in 1839. And indeed, photography immediately followed art in various fields of scientific research...

1. Daguerreotype of a personal sickle. Possibly the first astronomical photograph. (1840)

2. Flash of lightning, remove new June 18, 1847.

3. One of the first stroboscopic photographs. Having looked at photo 4, you should see what is captured on it. (Circa 1881)
