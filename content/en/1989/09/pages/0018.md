Traces of planets and stars in photographs

Let us now consider the case of slow, “frozen” movement for the eyes.

Night photography is an extremely interesting activity for a photographer, requiring great skill, hard work and determination. After all, already at dusk there is not enough light for photography, and what can we say about taking photographs in person! Nevertheless, at various photographic exhibitions, sometimes a section of the starry sky appears in the field of view of the lens, then the photographer then faces additional difficulties: when developing a negative and taking a photograph, the celestial bodies, so seemingly motionless, leave long light tracks on the photographic emulsion - these are traces of the movement of stars and planets in the sky. Of course, they can be retouched, but in the photograph shown here I did not do this on purpose, but left everything as is, in order to invite you to solve the following problem on your own: how to determine the exposure time of such a frame? The photograph shows the illuminated arch navigable Oka backwater with a signal buoy in front of the shallows. The simmock was made when twilight had already passed and night had fallen.
