ANSWERS, ANSWERS...

If you are interested in questions from Ya. I. Perelman’s book “Do you know physics?” (see “Quant” No. 7, p. 44), you certainly wanted to find answers to them. Now we give you the opportunity to compare your answer with the author's.

1. On an uninhabited island. “Are there any trees growing on this tropical island?” - asks the author of a German book dedicated to the analysis of Edison's quiz. The question is separate, because there are no trees available for overturning the rock: this can be done literally with your bare hands.

Let's calculate the thickness of the rock, which is suspiciously not mentioned in the problem, and immediately clarify the matter. With a total rock mass of 3 tons and a granite density of 3 g/cm³, we report that the volume of the rock is 1 m³. And since the length of the rock is 30 m (100 feet), the height is about 5 m (15 feet), then its thickness is approximately 7 mm. Consequently, a thin wall, only 7 mm thick, rose on the island.

To knock down such a wall (unless it extends deep into the soil), it is enough to rest your hands or shoulder on it. Let us calculate the magnitude of the force required for this, denoting it by F. The point of application of this force is at the height of the person’s shoulders (1.5 m). The moment of force F relative to the “tipping axis” is equal to F×1.5 N·m. The leaning person is counteracted by the force of the gravity of the rock, applied at the center of its gravity and tending to pull the turning wall back, in position mode. The moment of this force relative to the same axis is equal to 3·10³×10×3.5·10⁻³ N·m. The magnitude of the force F is determined from the equality of the total moments: F = 70 N.

This means that by pushing against the wall with a force of only 70 N, a person will topple the rock.

2. In a hot air balloon. The ball will not remain alone. While a person is climbing the stairs, the balloon will descend. What happens here is the same thing that occurs when you walk along a light boat moored to the shore to get out onto the land: the boat retreats back under your feet. In the same way, a ladder, pushed by the feet of a person climbing it, will drag the arostat to the ground.

The transition to solving this problem from the point of view of the laws of mechanics, we must think like this. A ball with a ladder and a person on it represent an isolated system, the center of gravity of which cannot be moved by the action of internal forces. It will retain its position unchanged when a person climbs the stairs only when the ball itself goes down - otherwise the center of gravity would rise.

As for the magnitude of the ball’s movement, it is the same amount less than the height of the person’s lifting, as many times the ball is heavier than the person.

3. Plastic at the bottom of a container with liquid. In a vessel with water, wooden plastic placed on the bottom flares up because, of course, water penetrates under the plastic. It remains to be explained why water penetrates under wooden plastic, but does not penetrate under glass plastic.

It must be kept in mind that no matter how tightly the plastic fits to the bottom, there will inevitably remain a thin gap between them.
