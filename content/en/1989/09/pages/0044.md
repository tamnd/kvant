⟦folio 42⟧

Quests

1. Can the expression $a^2 - b^2 + c^2$ be divisible by 5 if none of the integers $a, b$ and $c$ are divisible by 5?

2. While getting ready to travel by car, I discovered that the speedometer was faulty and replaced it with a speedometer from another car. When I drove away from home, the speedometer showed 131,313 km. On the highway at the 100 km mark it showed 131460, after another 70 km it showed 132558 km. When I add destinations, the counter showed 132713 km. How many kilometers have I traveled?

3. Arrange the numbers 1, 2,...,11 in the circles of the figure depicted on figure so that the sum of the numbers at the vertices of each square is the same.

4. Police officer Stepan Stepanov turned around at the sound of breaking glass and saw four teenagers running away from a broken display case. 5 minutes later they were at the police station.

Andrey stated that Victor broke the glass, while Victor claimed that it was Surrey who was in charge. Surrey assured that Victor was lying, but Yuri insisted that it was not he who did it. From further conversation they found out that only one of the guys was telling the truth. Who broke the glass?

5. Show that the length of the circle is equal to the length of the borders of the rosette, obtained from several circles of half the radius passing through its center (see figure).

These tasks were suggested to us by S. M. Usov, A. P. Savin, A. M. Domashenko, S. A. Lyashenko and V. V. Proizvolov.
