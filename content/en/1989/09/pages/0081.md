the number in the $n$th line is $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$. This means that the sum of the numbers in the first $n-1$ lines is equal to
$$1+2+\dots+\frac{(n-1)n}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \frac{(n-1)n}{2} \left( \frac{n(n-1)}{2} + 1 \right).$$
 the sum of the numbers in the first $n$ lines is equal to
$$1+2+\dots+\frac{n(n+1)}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \left( \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + 1 \right).$$

The sum of the numbers in the $n$th line is equal to the difference between the second and first sums, i.e.
$$\frac{1}{2} \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \left( \frac{n(n+1)}{2} + 1 \right) - \frac{1}{2} \frac{(n-1)n}{2} \left( \frac{n(n-1)}{2} + 1 \right) = \frac{n(n^2+1)}{2}.$$
