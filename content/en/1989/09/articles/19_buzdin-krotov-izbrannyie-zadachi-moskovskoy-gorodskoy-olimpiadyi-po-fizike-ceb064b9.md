9th grade

1. In space there are four straight lines, surrounded by two colors: two red and two blue, and any red straight line is redistributed by any blue straight line. Prove that any red or blue line is parallel.

2. In triangle $ABC$ on sides $AB, BC$ and $AC$ points $M, K$ and $L$ are taken respectively so that line $MK$ is parallel to line $AC$ and ML is parallel to $BC$. In this case, the segment $BL$ intersects the segment $MK$ at point $P$, and $AK$ intersects $ML$ at point $Q$. Prove that the segments $PQ$ and $AB$ are parallel.

3. It is known that the numbers $A_1, A_2, \dots, B_1, B_2, \dots$ form geometric progressions. Is it possible, knowing only the values ​​of $A_1+B_1, A_2+B_2, A_3+B_3$ and $A_4+B_4$, to determine $A_5+B_5$?

4. The streets of a certain city are represented on the plan as a square, divided into 25 equal cells on side 1. At the point offset at figure there is a snowplow. Find the length of the shortest route to bypass all streets so that at the end of the work the car returns to its starting point.

10th grade

1. Solve the equation
$$\lg(x-2)=2x-x^2+5.$$

2. Is there a function whose graph on the coordinate plane has a common point with any straight line?

3. Is it possible to arrange crosses and shelves on the face of a checkered piece of paper so that no three identical signs in a row can be found on any horizontal, vertical or diagonal?

4. Given $n$ different natural numbers. Prove that some infinite arithmetic progression, the first term of which is not greater than its difference, contains exactly 3 or 4 given numbers if $n=5$, $b) n=1989$.

5. Calculate, to within 2, the smallest total length of cuts that must be made to cut a unit square into a rectangle with a diagonal of 100.

6. On the edges of an arbitrary tetrahedron, a point is selected. A plane is drawn through each point of points lying on edges with a common vertex. Prove that if three of the four drawn planes touch the ball inscribed in the notebook, then the fourth plane also touches it.

Moscow City Olympiad in Physics

8th grade

1. The next slide with an angle $a$ without an initial speed is driven down by a sled. The middle third of the length of the slide is sprinkled with sand and has a friction coefficient of $μ$. At what values ​​of $μ$ will the sled reach the end of the slide? Pure ice is considered absolutely smooth.

2. Large groups of people move along the highway in one row at the same intervals and speeds. In what place does a pedestrian have more time to cross the highway: at an intersection where the speed of the groups is low (although it is still horizontal to the square of the speed. A force $f$ is additionally applied to the ball in a horizontal direction. What will be the vertical component of the ball’s speed?

3. A ball of mass $m$ under the influence of gravity falls into a liquid with a constant speed $v$. The resistance force of the ball fluid is proportional to the square of the speed. A force $f$ is additionally applied to the ball in a horizontal direction. What will be the vertical component of the ball's speed?

4. A fly, singing parallel to the table surface at a speed of $v$ at a height of $H$, noticed at some point in time a drop of honey exactly underneath it. With the help of its wings, a fly can develop acceleration in any direction not exceeding $a$. In what minimum time can a fly reach a drop of honey? There is no power (let's say it takes place in space).
