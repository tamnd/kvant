Solution. Let $H$ be the common point of the three circles, $A, B$ and $C$ the second points of their pairwise intersections (Fig. 8). Since the circle passing through $B, C$ and $H$ is symmetrical to the circumcircle of $ABH$, it contains the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $ABH$. Similarly, the circle passing through the points $A, C, H$ contains the point of intersection of the altitudes of the triangle $ABH$. Therefore, $C$ is the point of intersection of the altitudes of triangle $ABH$.

Task for independent solution

1. Points $A, B, C$ and $D$ are given on the plane such that $\angle ABC = \angle ADC, \angle BCD = \angle BAD$. Is the quadrilateral $ABCD$ necessarily a parallelogram?

2. Given a triangle $ABC$. Find all points $M$ of the plane such that $ABM, BCM$ and $CAM$ are equal triangles.

3. Prove that if the segments $AA_1, BB_1$ and $CC_1$ ($A_1, B_1$ and $C_1$ lie respectively on the sides $BC, CA$ and $AB$ of triangle $ABC$) intersect at one point and are divided at this point in the same ratio, then these segments are median triangles.

4. Inside the square $ABCD$ a point $M$ is taken such that $\angle MBD = \angle MDC = \alpha$. Find $\angle MAD$.

5. Prove that if an arbitrary straight line passing through a fixed point inside a given triangle divides its perimeter and area in equal proportions, then this fixed point is the center of the inscribed circle in the triangle.

6. Given a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$. Known angles: $\angle BCA = 40^\circ, \angle BAC = 50^\circ, \angle BDA = 20^\circ, \angle BDC = 25^\circ$. Find the angle between the diagonals of this quadrilateral.

7. Let $M$ be an arbitrary point of the plane, $a, b, c$ the sides of the triangle $ABC$ ($BC = a, CA = b, AB = c$). Prove that the inequality $aMA^2 + bMB^2 + cMC^2 \geq abc$ holds. For each point in the plane, does equality hold?

8. Given an acute triangle $ABC$. Find the locus of points $M$ such that $\angle MAB = \angle MCB, \angle MAC = \angle MBC$.

Dear readers!

Seriously, we once again appeal to you with a request: please supplement the annual subscription for the current year.

Let's remember the previous one. Permission to increase the volume of «Kvant» (our readers repeatedly asked to expand the scope of the magazine) was received last fall, when some of the subscribers had already subscribed. The editors were faced with a dilemma: either wait until 1990, or take a risk and increase the volume already in 1989. Why take the risk? Yes, because with an increase in volume, the costs of paper, printing work, author's fees and absences from Soyuzpechat increase. Accordingly, the costs and cost of the magazine should increase.

Having expressed confidence in its readers, the editors, however, did not indicate the failure of the supplement due to purely technical reasons - the refusal of some post offices to accept this supplement. Judging by the readers' gutters, there are quite a lot of such servants. And this despite the fact that the Soyuzpechat agency did not send out relevant directions several times (for example, telephone message No. 1 of April 3, 1989).

The editors of our magazine and the Soyuzpechat agency bring to the reader «Kvant» the exclusion of extraction for free delivery.

Once again we ask for a text from readers who subscribed at a price of 40 kopecks. per room, do not try and introduce the corresponding surcharge - 5 kopecks each. per room. The additional payment is optional at a separate bank in the SP-6 form indicating “Additional payment - «Kvant»”.

Editorial board of the magazine «Kvant» Azentstvo “Soyuzpechat”
