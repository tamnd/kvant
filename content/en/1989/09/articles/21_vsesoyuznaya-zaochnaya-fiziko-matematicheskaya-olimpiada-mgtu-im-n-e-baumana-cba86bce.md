Weighty correspondence physiology Olympiad of MSTU named after. N. E. Bauman

Moscow State Technical University named after. N. E. Bauman holds in the 1989/90 academic year the second All-Union Correspondence Physiomatic Olympiad (with a technical application) for students of secondary schools, vocational schools and technical schools.

The Olympiad is held in two types. The conditions for problems of the first type are published below. The conditions for problems of the second type will be sent by the Olympiad participant admitted to the second round based on the results of the first.

The solution to problems of the first type must be sent before December 15, 1989. Document all decisions in one school terradi. On the outside cover of the notepad, paste a piece of paper indicating your detailed home address (with postal code), last name, first name, middle name and place of study. Paste a certificate from your place of study indicating your class on the inside cover.

Place the notebook with solutions in a large envelope and send a simple parcel post to the address: 107005 Moscow, 2nd Baumanskaya st., 5, MSTU im. N. E. Bauman, “Olympics-89/90”.

First type task

1. How many roots does the equation have on the segment $[-\sqrt{2}; \sqrt{2}]$?

$$4\sqrt{2}|x|(x^2-1)(2x^4-4x^2+1)=1?$$

2. Compare 99! and 5099.

3. The edge of the base of the correctly four-perang prize is 2 a, and the height is $a(1+\sqrt{3})$. A sphere is drawn, passing through all the vertices of the lower base of the prize and touching its upper base. Find the area of ​​the part of the surface of the prize located inside the sphere.

4. An aircraft with an optical device on board moves uniformly above the earth's surface at an altitude of 800 m, flying around an extended axis at a speed of 360 rpm. The optical device automatically makes a sigmoid every time its lens is in the lowest position. Determine the shooting angle necessary to ensure that adjacent frames do not overlap when photographing.

5. The monatomic gas is located in a vertical cylinder under a piston that can move in the vertical direction without friction. The cross-sectional area of ​​the cylinder is $S$, volume is $V$. The cylinder is connected through a small hole to a body-position container of volume $V$, which contains no gas. At what level will the piston remain after the gas flow process is completed?

6. Two people are carrying a heavy log. The third stands in front of the first and tries to help. Will it be easier for those who carry it?
