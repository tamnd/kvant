⟦folio 23⟧

Exercise
M1196—M1200, F1203—F1207

M1196. Several (at least two) non-zero numbers are given. You are allowed to erase any two numbers a and b and write their numbers a+b/2 and b-a/2 together. Prove that after several such operations it is impossible to obtain the original set of numbers.

M1197. In triangle ABC, point M lies on side AB, point N on side BC, O is the intersection point of segments CM and AN. It is known that AM+AN=CM+CN. Prove that AO+AB=CO+CB.

M1198*. Let's call a word a line of 10 digits 0 and 1. We will consider two words synonyms if one can be obtained from the other through several operations of the following type: several numbers in a row are crossed out from the word, the sum of which is even, and the same numbers are written in their place, but in reverse order. What is the maximum number of words that do not have synonyms?

M1199*. Prove that if the equation ax²+(c-b)x+(e-d)=0 has a root greater than 1, then the equation ax⁴+bx³+cx²+dx+e=0 has at least one root.

M1200*. For which k can be placed on the circle a) 10; 6) 100; c) n arcs so that each of them intersects exactly k others?

S. A. Genkin

F1203. On a rough railway platform there is a uniformly filled container of height H and width L, which has small wheels on one side (Fig. 1). When the train accelerates, the container begins to slide to the right along the platform if the acceleration acceleration exceeds a₀. With what minimum acceleration must the train brake for the container to start sliding downwards? Ignore rolling motion.

D. Yu. Grigoriev

F1204. A small spring block of mass m can move without friction inside a rectangular box of the same mass. The box is on a table covered with a thin layer of mass (Fig.2). The friction force of the box on the table depends only on the speed u of the box moving on the table and is equal to F=-γu. At the initial moment of time, the box is finished, and the block is located at its left wall and has a speed v₀ directed to the right. How many impacts will the block make on the box if the length of the box L is much greater than the size of the block?

D. Yu. Grigoriev
