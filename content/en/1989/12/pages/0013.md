Let us have an accurate light source and screen. Then the straight line $l$, passing through the light source and not parallel to the screen, is depicted on it in the form of a point (we denote it by $A$) - see figure 13. Straight lines intersecting $l$ (and not passing through the light source) are projected into straight lines passing through $A$. More precisely, the shadow of a line that is not parallel to the screen is not a line, but a half-line, the end of which is the point of intersection of the line with the screen. If the intersection point is considered to be a straight line with $l$ lying between the light source and the screen, then the point $A$ lies on this half-line, otherwise - on its continuation (Fig. 13). If the straight line $l$ passing through the light source is parallel to the screen, then this line itself does not cast any shadow on the screen, but straight lines that intersect the straight line $l$ (and are not parallel to the screen) cast shadows in the form of a half-line parallel to $l$ (Fig. 14).

Let's return to our hyperbolide model. We will limit ourselves to considering two shadows. Firstly, we will position the hyperbolide so that its axis is parallel to the screen, and we will place the light source at the point of the throat circle furthest from the screen (the smallest circle surrounding the hyperbolide) - see Fig. 15. Secondly, we will illuminate the hyperbolide with a beam of rays parallel to one of the generatrices, and we will place the screen parallel to the edge circles of the hyperbolide ("hoops") - see. figure 16. Shadows are shown in Figures 17 and 18.

In the first case, the shadows of the generators of each family will be parallel to each other; they will be parallel to the hyperbolide formations passing through the light source. In the second case, two generatrices (the generatrix we have chosen, which is parallel to the beam of light, and a generatrix of another family parallel to it) will give exact shadows, and the shadows of the remaining generators of the shadows will be straight segments passing through the points.

All this directly follows from what was said above about the shadows of lines and the relative positions of generators; we leave the details to the reader.
