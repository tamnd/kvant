XXX
International Mathematical Olympiad

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
A. A. FOMIN

Over the thirty years of its existence, the International Mathematics Olympiad has gone through an impressive development path. The first IMO, held in 1959 in Romania, was attended by only 7 countries. This year the Olympiad brought together teams from 51 countries; Teams from India, Portugal and Tonga participated for the first time, while Japan, Denmark, Nigeria and Ivory Coast sent office observers.

XXX MMO took place in the ancient city of Brunswick, first mentioned in chronicles in 1031. This city in Germany was not chosen as the venue for the Olympiad by chance—after all, it was here that the foundations of school education in Germany were laid. The “king of mathematicians” Carl Friedrich Gauss and his student Richard Dedekind were natives of Braunschweig. Giving the data to their great correspondent, the organizers of the Olympiad used the correct seventeen-square in the Olympiad emblem. The fact is that when Gauss was 19 years old, he completely solved the ancient problem of the possibility of constructing regular polygons with a compass and a ruler. In particular, he was the first to prove the possibility of constructing a regular 17-gon.

321 schoolchildren participated in Volimpida. Our country was represented by Dmitry Ivanov (Moscow, school no. 57), Sergey Ivanov (Leningrad, school no. 239), Evgenia Malininkova (Leningrad, school no. 239), Vladimir Ragulin (Physical School No. 18 at Moscow State University), Maria Roginskaya (Leningrad, school no. 239) and Arkady Skopenkov (Physical School No. 18 at Moscow State University). Their progress is reflected in a table showing the number of points received for solving each problem, the total points of each accountant and the award received by him. Traditionally, the Olympiad was held in 2 rounds. In each type (duration 4.5 hours) three tasks were offered. All tasks were scored 7 points.

The gold medal of this Olympiad was the third received by Seryozha Ivanov at international Olympiads. Dima Ivanov received his second gold medal. The outstanding success, youth and charm of 9th grade student Zhenya Malininkova made her one of the most popular students at the Olympiad.

The verification of the work was strict: not a single error or inaccuracy in the design of the solution to the problem was passed.

The Olympics took place in a shorter period than usual and was full of excursions, meetings, evenings of relaxation, games, dancing, etc. The Federal Minister of Education and Research Jürgen W. Mellemann, the Minister of Education of Lower Saxony Horst Horman, the mayor of Brunswick Germany did a lot for this.
