Tasks

1. (Philippines). Prove that the set {1, 2, ..., 1989} can be represented as a union of pairwise disjoint sets $A_i$ ($i=1, 2, ..., 117$) such that
   a) each of the set $A_i$ has 17 elements;
   b) $S_1 = S_2 = ... = S_{117}$, where $S_i$ is the sum of the numbers of the set $A_i$.
2. (Australia). The bisectors of the angles $A, B, C$ of an acute triangle $ABC$ intersect the circle circumscribed around it at points $A_1, B_1, C_1$ of the corresponding one. Taking $AA_1$ intersects the bisectors of the external angles at the vertices $B$ and $C$ of triangle $ABC$ at point $A_0$. Points $B_0$ and $C_0$ are defined similarly.

Prove that
a) $S_{A_0B_0C_0} = 2S_{AC_1BA_1CB_1}$;
b) $S_{A_0B_0C_0} \geq 4S_{ABC}$.
3. (Hungary). Let $n$ and $k$ be fixed natural numbers, $n \geq k$. The set $S$ and $n$ points of the plane has the following properties:
   a) no three points from $S$ lie on the same line;
   b) for any point $P \in S$ there are at least $k$ distinct points from $S$ equal in length from $P$.
   Prove that
   $$\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}} \geq \frac{1}{\sqrt{AD}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{BC}}.$$

4. (Iceland). Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral whose sides $AB, AD$ and $BC$ satisfy the equality $AB=AD+BC$. Inside this quadrilateral there is a point $P$ such that $AP=h+AD$ and $BP=h+BC$, where $h$ is the distance from the point $P$ to the line $CD$. Prove that
   $$\frac{1}{\sqrt{h}} \geq \frac{1}{\sqrt{AD}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{BC}}.$$

5. (Sweden). Prove that for any $n$ consecutive natural numbers, each of which is not a power prime of an integer exponent.

6. (Poland). The permutation $(x_1, x_2, ..., x_{2n})$ of the numbers 1, 2, ..., $2n$ is known to be convenient if $|x_i - x_{i+1}| = n$ for at least one value $i \in \{1, 2, ..., 2n-1\}$. Prove that for any $n$ the major positives of all possible permutations are convenient.
