Thus, in the first task it was essential to use numerical methods of calculation, in the second - mastery of the apparatus of vector algebra, in the third - the study of phenomena at a qualitative level using the ideas of dimensions and similarity. Note that all three theoretical problems are beyond the scope of the general education physics course in our high school. Despite this, Soviet schoolchildren, on the whole, successfully coped with problems of the theoretical type, especially problems 2 and 3, each of which was completely solved by 3 participants. The greatest difficulties were caused by task 1, which proposed to understand the peculiar phenomenon of program boiling. For solving theoretical problems, our team received 94 points, which is 63% of the maximum number of points. This is the sixth result.

The purpose of the experiment was to determine the speed of propagation of ultrasonic waves in water and glycerin, using the method of physical shift of reference signals and signals transmitted through the medium. The basis of the physical content of the experimental problem was the piezoelectric effect in dielectrics.

To complete the task, students were offered modern physical equipment that was not common to the school physics classroom: a two-beam electronic oscilloscope, a standard signal generator, and piezoelectric sensors. It should be noted that over the past five years there has been a fairly stable trend of transition from home-grown (sometimes very witty) equipment to real, modern equipment. The USSR team training strategy clearly does not take this into account.

The success of solving an experimental problem depends not only on the depth of knowledge of physics, but on the performance of painstaking and laborious observations, competent presentation of the experimental results, including thorough calculations of errors. Unfortunately, this is not typical for experimental rounds of weighty physical optimization, which affected the results. Although our students scored a total of 69.5 points (70% of the maximum number), many teams exceeded this result, and we ended up only in the same place.
