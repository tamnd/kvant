Soviet schoolchildren received the following awards: N. Kuzma - 1st degree diploma, A. Korshkov - 2nd degree diploma, K. Zuev - 3rd degree diploma, V. Makeev and Yu. Uvarov - certificates of merit. Let us note that K. Zuev was a few fractions of a point short of the middle medal, and V. Makeev was short of the bronze medal.

The Lithuanian team, which competed outside the specific Dutch team, Dr. Hans Jordan, on behalf of his government, invited all participants to the next Olympics in Groinggen.

What can we say in conclusion about the preparation and performance of our team? The results of participation in the XX MFO indicate sharply increased competition from business trips of countries. Particularly noticeable is the progress of the Chinese team, which took second place at the Physics Olympiad and achieved the best team results at the Math and Chemistry Olympiads. The success rate of the US team, whose strongest member (Steven Gabser) scored the best score - 46.3, is a very high result. Therefore, looking into the future, we should seriously think about creating optimal conditions for preparing our schoolchildren for international Olympiads. The first steps in this direction were made towards the establishment of the successful USSR State Committee for Public Education and the Central Committee of the Komsomol.

Last year, for the first time, winter and summer training sessions for candidates in teams were held at the Youth Center of the Komsomol Central Committee “Olympida”, which has a number of significant facilities compared to the previous base. This includes a more convenient geographical location, a fundamentally different standard of living for schoolchildren, and greater opportunities for recreation and sports. In addition to candidates for international Olympiads, the Olympid also invited physicists, winners of the correspondence collective competition of the All-Union Touring of Young Physicists and winners of the “Problem Book «Kvant»” competition. All this contributed to the creation of an atmosphere of cordial and fair selection of the most worthy contenders in the USSR teams.

The weak point in the team’s preparation for the Olympics now is the lack of appropriate conditions for conducting experimental classes. The interests of the cause oblige us to provide for the holding of a server-based physics workshop in order to give our children the opportunity to get acquainted with modern equipment and with a variety of methods for setting up an experiment and processing its results. To this end, in the near future it is necessary to make fuller use of the experimental bases of Moscow State University and MIPT, which are actively involved in the preparation of the team, and in the future, to create an independent experimental base.
