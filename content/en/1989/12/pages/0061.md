This year, the International Physics Olympiad (IPO) for schoolchildren was held from July 16 to 24 in the capital of the Polish People's Republic. Schoolchildren from 29 countries came to Warsaw, two more teams than last year. It must be said that the number of countries participating in the Olympiad is steadily increasing from year to year.

The Olympiad was attended by the Vice-President of the European Physical Society, Mr. Deppre (Belgium), and observed from Greece, Spain, Tanland and the United Arab Emirates. The Lithuanian SSR team performed outside the competition at the Olympics. The leaders of the participating teams of the MFO were invited as guests of honor to the anniversary XX MFO: G. S. Tarasok (USSR), Dr. Wendt (GDR) and prof. Gruchi (Yugoslavia). Correspondents from scientific journals for schoolchildren from a number of European countries were also present at the Olympiad.
