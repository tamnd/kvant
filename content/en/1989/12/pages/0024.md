So, the frequency of the tide-forming force is greater than the frequency of the ocean’s own oscillations, the tides are in antiphase with the force: where the Moon most strongly pulls the water upward, there is low tide, and where it drives it towards the center of the Earth, there is high tide. True, the vindictive reader will probably remember what was said about the actual pattern of ebb and flow: “everything turns almost 90 degrees,” that is, almost in antiphase. This is explained by the fact that vibrations occur exactly in phase or antiphase with an external force only in the complete absence of energy losses and the absence of friction. And if there is almost no training, then the oscillations are almost in phase or almost out of phase.

Everything is fine with the phase now. This unexpected complications arise with amplitude.
