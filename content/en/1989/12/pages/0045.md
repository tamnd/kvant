It would be a mistake to think that only a highly experienced experimenter can assemble such a generator and make it work. The generator requires virtually no setup, and the necessary parts can be easily purchased or obtained from an old tube receiver or TV.

As for the reels, they are quite easy to make yourself. The coil $L_1$ and $L_2$ is wound onto a common cylindrical frame with a diameter of 3.5 cm and a length of 7 cm, which is easiest to glue together from thick paper (Fig. 2). The first coil consists of 6 turns of copper wire with a diameter of 2-3 mm (in any insulation), the distance between the turns is 1 cm. The second contains 10 turns of wire (in enamel insulation) with a diameter of 0.5 mm, wound close turn to turn.
