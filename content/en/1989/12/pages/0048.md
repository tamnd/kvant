Summer physics and mathematics school in the USA

In accordance with the cooperation program between the magazine «Kvant» (USSR) and the International Educational Network (USA), this summer, for the first time, Soviet American physics and mathematics summer schools were held - in the USA and in the USSR.

47 American schoolchildren who arrived in Tartu, ESSR, passed a server-based competitive selection (based on tests). Among the Soviet schoolchildren who participated in the school in Tartu were the winners of the “Problem Book «Kvant»” competition, the Tournament of Cities, and the Moscow City Olympiad in Mathematics and Physics. In Estonia, the Tartu Science Park (director J. Lippaa) took on the role of host. The curriculum provided teachers and researchers from Moscow State University. Among the lecturers were the Soviet mathematician Professor Yu. Manin.

And a group of 15 Soviet schoolchildren went to America. The delegation consisted mainly of the winners of the “Problem Book «Kvant»” competition, and the indispensable conditions for participation were English language and communication skills. The leaders of the group were the scientific secretary of the book series “Library «Kvant» Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences A. Buzdin and a member of the editorial board of the journal «Kvant»
