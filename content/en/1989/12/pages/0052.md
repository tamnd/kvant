⟦folio 50⟧

We didn’t have to complain - we talked with sellers, passers-by, and fellow travelers along the route on the ship. It was a pleasure to meet such cheerful and attractive people.

Masha Dorokhova (Chernogolovka village, Moscow region, village highway No. 82). Traveling from New York to Washington, we visited Brooklyn, or rather New Odessa. The population here consists mainly of Russian emigrants. That’s why there was a natural wave of interest among Soviet schoolchildren in this area—we all missed Russian speech. “Odessans” were also happy to talk with people “just from there.” Their interest in our country is no longer just curiosity... Brooklyn looks like a typical New York area. Ta-

What bright signs, small shops. What was especially striking was that many, especially the older generation, have never learned to speak English; they buy everything they need in “Russian” stores. We had lunch at a Russian restaurant. Dreams came true: black bread, dumplings, borscht!. Mikhail Gulko sang Russian songs for us. Most of them were about how difficult it is for emigrants far from their homeland... We, although we were not emigrants, felt nothing worse than the nanosteel ourselves. Therefore, conversations about New and simply Odessa did not stop for a long time.

Ivar Martin (Leningrad, Physics School No. 45 at Leningrad State University) and Pavel Mikheev (Moscow, Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University). One of the most powerful imprints on us was made by the ordinary American High School*. This school is located in New York State, on Long Island. Its full name is Smithtown High School West.

The story about this school can be found in the fact that there are 1,100 people studying there (15-25 people per class), but the size of this school is 3-4 times larger than the average Soviet school. On the school grounds there are 4 tennis courts, 2 football fields and one American football field. Intervershy is not inferior to her appearance. Well-equipped classrooms, a huge gym - in a word, everything that our schools do not boast. It can also be noted that this school has 3 buffets,

*) Analogous to the high school of our school. (Personal ed.)
