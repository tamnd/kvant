M1171—M1175, F1183—F1187

Note that for k ≥ 2
$$\frac{1}{h_{k-1}} - \frac{1}{h_k} = \frac{1}{kh_{k-1}h_k} > \frac{1}{kh_k^2}.$$
Summing these inequalities over k=2, 3, ..., n, we obtain the inequality
$$\frac{1}{2h_2^2} + \frac{1}{3h_3^2} + \dots + \frac{1}{nh_n^2} < \frac{1}{h_1} - \frac{1}{h_n} < 1$$
($h_1 = 1$). It remains to add 1/h₁² = 1 to both sides.
L. D. Kurlyandchik
