Mysterious, unpopular signs on figure are a coded greeting for the New Year. If you copy it onto transparent paper, cut it into 6 parts, and then intertwine the strips together (see figure above), you can read the text. There are 368,640 ways to weave, but only in one case is the film frayed.

Indeed, six strips can be laid out without intertwining 6! = 720 ways. (Taking into account possible repetitions at 180°, this number should be reduced by 2⁶.) At each weave, the strips can be laid in two ways. If there are 9 nodes, like ours, then this gives 2⁹ = 512 options, i.e. 720 × 512 = 368,640 films.

Of course, solving the problem does not consist of trying all the options. By looking closely at figure, you must prove which parts of the stripes should be visible and which parts should be hidden. When you find them, you can easily read the greeting.

You can send a similar message yourself for your friends and acquaintances. Happy New Year! A. T. Kalini
