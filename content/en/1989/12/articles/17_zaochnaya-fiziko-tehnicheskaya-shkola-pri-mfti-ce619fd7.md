Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (3FTSH) at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) is enrolling students from secondary schools located on the territory of the RSFSR in grades 9, 10 and 11 for the 1990/91 academic year.

The goal of the school is to help students interested in physics and mathematics to deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects. When admitting to 3FTS, pre-option is allocated to students living in rural areas, workers in villages and small towns, where such assistance is especially needed.

School education is free.

In addition to individual students, 3FTS accepts physics and technology clubs and electives, which can be organized in any secondary school by two teachers - physics and mathematics.

The leaders of a circle or elective recruit and enroll students (at least 8-10 people) who have successfully completed the introductory task of 3FTS. A group is accepted into 3FTSH if the school director informs 3FTSH of the departments, names, absences of its leader and the posted list of students (indicating the class in the 1990/91 academic year and the final grades for the introductory assignment in physics and mathematics). All these materials and an envelope for a response to admission to 3FTS with a return address addressed to one of the managers should be sent to the address: 141700, Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, 3FTS, indicating “Krukok” or “Optional” before May 25, 1990. (Notebooks with student work are not sent to 3FTS.) The work of directors of correspondence physics and technology clubs can be paid for by schools upon submission from 3FTS at MIPT as elective classes.

Students of 3FTS, heads of physics and technology clubs and electives will receive assignments in physics and mathematics in accordance with the 3FTS program (6-7 tasks in each subject during the academic year), as well as solutions to these tasks recommended by 3FTS. The assignments contain theoretical material and development of typical problems and examples on the relevant topic, as well as 10-14 assignments with independent solutions. These are both simple problems and more complex ones (at the level of competitive problems at MIPT). 3FTS assignments are prepared by teachers from the departments of general physics
