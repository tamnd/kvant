⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. Pupils of class 3 "A" came to the theater. At intermission they all ran to the buffet. Each boy bought a snack, and each girl bought buns. If every boy bought buns, and every girl bought a snack, then they (together) would spend one penny less. There are more boys than girls. How long?

2. The number CUBE is the cube of a certain number, and the number BUK is a prime. Find these numbers.

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle you see at figure. One letter corresponds to the same number, and different letters correspond to different numbers.

4. Show that in parallelogram ABCD, the area surrounded by red is twice the area surrounded by yellow (see figure). Points K and E are the midpoints of the corresponding sides.

5. Can a number
n!=1·2·3·...·n
(read en factorial) look like 111...100...0? Is there a number of this type - starting with ones and ending with zeros - that is divisible by 1990?

These tasks were suggested to us by N.Yu. Nechevetaev, N.K. Antonovich, P.V. Dzhandzhlava, A.I. Demidov, students from the village of Chernologovka, Moscow region. I. Soloviev and D. Feldman.
