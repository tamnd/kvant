We set its energy to decrease. We will assume that the partition between the compartments formed in the cylinder does not prevent heat exchange between them, so that the air temperatures in them are the same all the time. Denoting the air temperature at the initial moment as $T_0$, and at the moment of stopping as $T$, we obtain

$$cT_0 = cT + A.$$

Let us also write down the equation of state of the gas for these moments:

$$p_aV = RT_0,$$
$$p(V + Sx) = RT.$$

The resulting equations are sufficient to determine the value of $x$:

$$x = \frac{V}{S} \frac{c}{c + R} \frac{Mg}{p_aS - Mg}$$ at $Mg < p_aS$.

F1156. Two identical electromagnets $L_1$ and $L_2$ are connected in series to a direct current circuit (Fig. 1). Using the key $K_1$, a diode $D$ can be connected in parallel to one of them in a non-conducting direction. When the key $K_2$ is closed, the iron plate will be attracted to the electromagnets. If the key $K_1$ is open, then when $K_2$ is opened, the plate breaks away from the designated simultaneous one and moves forward, maintaining a horizontal position. If the key $K_1$ is open, then when $K_2$ is opened, the plate first breaks away from the magnet $L_1$, and then from $L_2$, which leads to its rotation. Explain once - In the first case, when the key $K_1$ is open, when the key $K_2$ is opened, the current in both electromagnets stops simultaneously, and both ends of the iron plate are “released” at the same time.
