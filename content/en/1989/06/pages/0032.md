Tasks

M1166 — M1170, F1173 — F1177

M1166. Prove the inequality $a^2pq + b^2qr + c^2rp \leqslant 0$, where $a, b, c$ is the length of the side of the triangle, $p+q+r=0$.

Y. Sh. Mustafayev, 10th grade student, Baku

M1167. How many permutations of the numbers 1, 2, ..., $n$ are there in which, for any number $i$ that is not in first place, at least one of the numbers $i-1$ and $i+1$ is to the left of $i$?

A. V. Anzhans

M1168*. The country has 1989 cities and 4000 roads (each road connects two cities). Prove that it is possible to choose a ring route passing through no more than 20 cities.

A. A. Razborov

M1169. Let $M$ be a point lying inside the rectangle $ABCD$, $S$ its area. Prove the inequality

$$S \leqslant AM \cdot CM + BM \cdot DM.$$

I. Ya. Goldsheid

M1170. The distance to partition a given convex $n$-gon into triangles with disjoint diagonals. Let us make the following transformation by restructuring: instead of some diagonals $BC$, which serves as the common side of two triangles $ABC$ and $BCD$ of the partition, we draw the diagonal $AD$ (Fig. 1). Let us denote by $P(n)$ the smallest number of rearrangements after which any partition can be transformed into any other. Prove your grades:

a) $P(n) \geqslant n-3$,
b) $P(n) \leqslant 2n-7$,
b)* $P(n) \leqslant 2n-10$ at $n \geqslant 13$.

D. V. Fomin

F1173. A long toy train, made up of a large number of cars, travels at a constant speed along horizontal rails, and then goes into a “dead loop” (Fig. 2). Train length $L$, loop radius $R$ ($R$ is significantly larger than the size of the car, but $L > 2\pi R$). At what initial speed will the train overcome the obstacle so that during movement not a single car stops pressing on the rails?

A. I. Buzdin

F1174. In a cubic sauce with a volume of $V=1$ liter there is $m=0,01$ g of gel at a temperature of $T=300$ K. Let's observe one of the molecules. How many times does she manage to top the wall of the sauce in $t=1$ minutes?

A. R. Zilberman

F1175. The UFO flies over the Earth at a constant, very high speed $v$. What speed will the station’s ground instruments record at that moment?
