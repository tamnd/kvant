Commentary by Daedalus

In photochromic glass the classical photographic reaction occurs: AgCl $\xrightarrow{hv}$ Ag+Cl. The chlorine atom, released in the photographic emulsion, immediately binds irreversibly with the iron, and the silver atom becomes the center of development. In glass, chlorine cannot move further from the silver atoms, and therefore the reaction is reversible. The equilibrium of the reaction depends on the illumination of the glass.
