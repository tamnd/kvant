6(3). A helicopter was dropped from a stationary relative ground without an initial velocity of the body. After $t_1=1$ s, the second body was dropped, also without an initial velocity. Determine the distance between the bodies after $t_2=2$ s from the beginning of the fall of the first body. Ignore air resistance.

7(13). What is the simultaneous height of the sound of a shot and a bullet when a shot is fired vertically with an initial speed of $v_0=350$ m/s? The speed of sound is assumed to be $v=340$ m/s. The resistance of the bullet movement is not taken into account.

8(11). A body with a mass of $m=0,5$ kg is thrown with a speed of $v_0=20$ m/s at an angle of $\alpha=30^\circ$ to the horizontal. Determine the greatest height of the body, the further flight and the change in the momentum of the body during the flight. Neglect air resistance.

9(12). A load is suspended on a thread, the mass of which is $m=1$ kg. The thread with the load is lowered into the air with acceleration $a=5$ m/s². Determine the tension of the thread.

10(5). A body of mass $m$ located on a horizontal plane is acted upon by a force $F$ directed downwards at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizon. The friction coefficient of the body on the plane is $\mu$. Find the acceleration of the body.

11(12). Throw it over a stationary block, to the ends of which two identical weights weighing $M=0,5$ kg each are suspended. When an additional body was suspended from one of the loads, each load, having traveled a distance of $l=1$ m, gained a speed of $v=2$ m/s. Determine the mass of the additional body. Neglect the masses of the thread and block. The thread is inextensible.

12(11). A bus with a mass of $m=2\cdot 10^3$ kg moves at a speed of $v=36$ km/h along a convex place with a radius of curvature of $R=40$ m. Determine the acceleration of the bus and the force of its pressure on the seats at the top point of the seat.

13(3). A beam with a mass of $m=50$ kg and a length of $l=10$ m rests at one end on a horizontal plane. Its other end is held with a rope so that the rope and the beam form a right angle, and the beam and the horizontal plane form an angle $\alpha=60^\circ$. Find the tension force of the rope.

14(9). A ball of radius $R$ will end on the surface of the earth. From the top point of the ball, a body slides from a state of rest, placed much smaller than the size of the ball. At what height above the surface of the earth will the body separate from the ball?

15(10). A wooden block is on an inclined plane. With what minimum force must the block be pressed against the inclined plane so that it remains at rest on it? The mass of the block is $m=0,2$ kg; the length of the inclined plane is $l=1$ m, and the height is $h=0,5$ m; friction coefficient of the block on the inclined plane $\mu=0,4$.

16(7). A car with a mass of $m=3$ t moves uniformly at a speed of $v=40$ km/h. Find the power developed by the automatic engine if the friction coefficient is $\mu=0,06$.

17(1). The horizontal table is made of two types of materials, so the coefficient of friction between the cube and the table is $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ (Fig. 3). The distance from the cube to the surface of the inclined plane at a speed of $v=2,45$ m/s. Find the amount of solid mass released during friction and the coefficient of friction of the body on the plane. The initial speed of the body was zero.

18(13). A body is thrown horizontally with a speed of $v_0=6$ m/s from a height of $H=5$ m. Determine the kinetic energy of the body when falling to the ground if its mass is $m=1$ kg.

19(5). A body weighing $m=3$ kg slides along an inclined plane with a height of $h=0,5$ m and a slope length of $l=1$ m. The body comes to the base of the inclined plane with a speed of $v=2,45$ m/s. Find the amount of solid mass released during friction and the coefficient of friction of the body on the plane. The initial velocity of lead is $c=130$ J/(kg K).

21(4). The particle hits a resting target and is reflected back with $n$ times reduced kinetic energy. Determine the ratio of the mass of the particle to the mass of the target if the content is elastic.

22(3). A piece of metal falls from a height of $h=10$ m onto the same second piece. The impact is inelastic, the pieces stick together into a single block. How much does the temperature of the block change if initially both pieces were at the same temperature? Neglect air resistance and heat exchange with the environment. Specific heat of metal $c=2,5\cdot 10^2$ J/(kg K).
