⟦folio 60⟧

There is a small snag. At our rate we will consume 3·10¹⁰ feet of action per year, and its cost is 100 dollars per foot, therefore the consumed action is 100 times the annual budget of modern influencing forces. But maybe udastore can get action at wholesale prices?

However, we will need more energy to lift it away from the Sun. Calculations show that 2.4·10⁴⁰ erg will be used for this, i.e. much more than the weight of oceanic deuterium can provide. Therefore, it will be necessary to find other sources of energy. I believe that to solve this problem we will have to resort to the synthesis of an alpha particle from four protons. When using this reaction, all the protons of the world's oceans will give us an energy of 10⁴² erg, that is, forty times greater than what is needed to tear it away from the Sun.

Sand can be used as body work. Ejecting 1000 SiO₂ molecules for each synthesized alpha particle, we will have to

eliminate only 4% of the Earth's mass. It seems to me that we can afford it. Moreover, they would not want to use up the Moon for such a purpose: after all, far from the Sun there is still no product from it. Having left the solar system and wandering in outer space, we will probably be able to replenish our reserves of mass and energy from time to time, refueling on the fly from the planets we meet along the way. There is still one fundamental obstacle standing in the way of implementing these plans: we do not know how to carry out the 4p→⁴He chain reaction. Now you see what an important problem this is. We need to redouble our efforts to solve it. Time is pressing: the Earth has already eaten two-thirds of its allotted time from the Sun.

I assure you: we will be fine in space. Perhaps we will like it so much that we will not even want to stick to the new star.
