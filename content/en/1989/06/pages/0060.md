Earth as a controlled spaceship

D. FROMAN

Speech at a bank held after a conference on plasma physics organized by the American Physical Society in November 1961 in Colorado Sprees

Since I am not very good at plasma physics and thermonuclear fusion, I will not talk about these phenomena themselves, but about one of their practical applications in the near future.

Let's imagine that we managed to invent a spaceship that moves by throwing out the products of the D-D and D-T reactions. On such a ship you can launch into space, almost there are several astroids and tow them to Earth. (The idea, however, is not new.) If the rocket was not overloaded, it would be possible to deliver 1000 tons of astroid to Earth, spending only about a ton of deuterium. To be honest, I don’t know what substance the astroid is made of. However, it may turn out that half of them consist of nickel. It is known that 1 foot of deuterium costs 50 cents, and 1 foot of deuterium costs about 100 dollars. Thus, for 1 million dollars we could buy 5 tons of deuterium, using them, deliver 2500 tons of nickel to Earth, costing 2.5 million dollars.

Not bad, right? I was already thinking about organizing the American Company for Astroid Dobilation and Delivery (AKDDA)? The equipment of such a company will be extremely simple. If Sam received a subsidy, it would be possible to establish a very profitable business. If anyone present with a large bank account would like to be one of the founders, let him come to me after the bank.
