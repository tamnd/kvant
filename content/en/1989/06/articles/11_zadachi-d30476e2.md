⟦folio 39⟧

Tasks

1. In the equality 101-102=1, move one digit so that it becomes true.

2. Vessel "Alpha" moored to the pier earlier than vessel «Kvant». Will it be able to sail earlier if the mooring rope «Kvant» (see figure) is not removed?

3. Grandfather Suleiman is 100 years older than his great-great-great-granddaughter Zulfiya. This year, Zulfiya discovered that the product of her age and the age of grandfather Suleiman is equal to 1989. How old is she?

4. Four non-farm youth associations “Green Front”, “Eco”, “Red Patrol” and “Seekers of Truth” decided to unite. The unification conference was attended by an equal number of delegates from all four associations.

Disagreements arose when choosing the name of the new association. Two names were selected for voting: “Green Thinker” and “Thinking Ecologist”.

It is known that all the delegates from the “Red Patrol” want to vote for the same name, but there is no unity among the other delegates. Among the “Green Front” delegates, just as many want to vote for the first name as the “Eco” delegates want to vote for the second. Among the adherents of the second name, one third are delegates of the “Seekers of Truth.”

What name will be chosen?

5. A square sheet of paper was cut into 6 pieces in the shape of convex polygons. Five pieces were lost, leaving one piece in the shape of a regular octagon. Is it possible to restore the original square using this octagon alone?

These tasks were suggested to us by: A.V. Korllyukov, N.P. Dolbili, A.P. Sabin, V.V. Proizvolov.
