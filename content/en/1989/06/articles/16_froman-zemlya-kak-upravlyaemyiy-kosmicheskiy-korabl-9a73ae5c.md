However, we will need more energy to lift it away from the Sun. Calculations show that 2.4·10⁴⁰ erg will be used for this, i.e. much more than the weight of oceanic deuterium can provide. Therefore, it will be necessary to find other sources of energy. I believe that to solve this problem we will have to resort to the synthesis of an alpha particle from four protons. When using this reaction, all the protons of the world's oceans will give us an energy of 10⁴² erg, that is, forty times greater than what is needed to tear it away from the Sun.

Having left the solar system and wandering in outer space, we will probably be able to replenish our reserves of mass and energy from time to time, refueling on the fly from the planets we meet along the way. There is still one fundamental obstacle standing in the way of implementing these plans: we do not know how to carry out the 4p→⁴He chain reaction. Now you see what an important problem this is.
