EIGHTH
WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP
MICROCOMPUTERS

The eighth World Microcomputer Chess Championship took place in the Spanish city of Almeria. Let us remind you that Fidelity ran in the first three championships, and Mephisto in the next four. And so these keepers met in a two-round tour match to sort out their relationship. The battle between the Fidelity and Mephisto quartets ended with the victory of Mephisto with a score of 19:13. Thus, these computers have proven for the fifth time in a row that they are the strongest in the world. Fidelity were content with the fact that one of their victories over Mephisto was recognized as the best game of the championship.

All “Fidelity” and “Mephisto” that competed in the tour match were experimental, that is, specially prepared for the World Championship in Spain. However, in addition to the central battle for the crown, two more tournaments took place in Almeria, which attracted more attention. The first is in the class of commercial computers (on sale); Mephisto also won here.
Were there any creative achievements at this championship? As usual, in such sections the machines acted quite confidently, and sometimes “worked miracles” in the endgame.
