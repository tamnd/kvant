⟦folio 66⟧

9. It says "Wolfgang Amadeus". Flip the page and turn it 180° - you will read "Amadeus Mozart." The last three ambigrams are geographical names. They all have symmetry.

**Ningury**

10. "Hungary" is Hungary. The inscription can be rotated 180°.
11. “Chicago” has the same symmetry.

**ATSANTA**

You might want to copy the ambigrams onto tracing paper - it’s easier to see them in the light.

So, we invite you to try your hand at inventing ambigrams. We will publish the best of them.
