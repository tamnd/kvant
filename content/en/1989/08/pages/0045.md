⟦folio 43⟧

Quests

1. Leva and Pasha live in the same house. Their apartment numbers are two-digit numbers with the following feature: if you add the square of the difference between the digits of the apartment number to the sum of the apartment number, you will again get this number. Find the apartment numbers of Pasha and Leva.

2. Solve the arithmetic puzzle you see at figure. Identical letters correspond to the same numbers, different ones - different ones.

3. Is it possible to cut two identical circles into an acute triangle with vertices on the circle so that one of them can fit entirely inside the other?

4. On a microcalculator, each number is written using no more than seven small segments. In the example shown at figure, each of the numbers has one segment out of place. Rearrange the segments so that the equality becomes true.

5. What is the sum of the numbers in the tenth line of the arithmetic triangle (see figure)? in accordance with a in the nth line?
