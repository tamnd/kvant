A new tale about the Curious Elephant

No, this is not a tale about that nasty Elephant’s Child that Kipling wrote about, the Elephant’s Child who lived in Africa and was beaten by his dear relatives until he learned to beat them himself. This is a fairy tale about something else, about a good Elephant’s Child, who was never beaten by his relatives and who never lived in Africa, which is very strange, because he lived in almost all countries of the world. This Curious Baby Elephant had a wonderful nose from birth, so he did not need the services of Old Crocodile, and so he ushered in a new era - the Atomic Age. And he also had many, many uncles and many, many children, and he was full of inappropriate curiosity and pestered everyone with his questions.

Then the Curious Elephant grew up and began to ask new and unheard of questions that his relatives asked. He asked his representative, the uncle philosopher, why he had such logic, and the representative uncle, the philosopher, replied that it was because he knew that he knew nothing. And then - all because of the same inappropriate curiosity - he crossed the North Sea and began to walk around England and tell everyone about the Atom. And he asked the hairy uncle Ji-Gi *) why he makes such big mistakes, and the hairy uncle Ji-Gi answered that it was all because of the romantic imagination. And the incongruous Curious Elephant went to the smoky city of Manchester, where many physicists grew up, to find the Old Crocodile**) and ask him about the Atom. And he just toyed with the Old Crocodile a little, because he was a Brave Elephant. And the Old Crocodile bared his terrible teeth and told him everything he knew about the Atom.

And the Curious Elephant went home, carrying with him many different actions and principles, and scattered them along the road. And behind him came only small animals, who selected these actions and principles and made formulas from them and

But the Curious Little Elephant made almost all the uncles believe in almost all his actions and principles, and he himself became an uncle, big, wise and peaceful, just like the wild elephant Hati. And he began to smoke a pipe and scatter a load of ash around, and some of the small animals began to imitate him and also became big and wise. And the Little Elephant built a larger house where he could live and invite big animals and little animals to visit. And he willingly played with small animals if he had even a little time.

But the dawn of the Atomic Age came too quickly, and the Little Elephant had a lot to do: after all, he had to explain to all the big animals what they needed to do. And since some of them began to do bad things, the Little Elephant became very sad. But the King gave him a small elephant, carved from ivory, so that all the animals and all his dear relatives would always remember what a kind and wise Elephant Child he was.
