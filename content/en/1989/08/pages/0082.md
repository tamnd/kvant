(see Quantum No. 7)

To construct a network of folds that allow you to fold a paper square into a regular triangle ABC on side AB of the square. To do this, the sheet is folded in half and unbent so that part of the fold line is outlined (Fig. 6). Vertex A is then placed on this line so that the edge AB of the sheet is flush against the plane of the sheet (Fig. 7). The point of the fold line on which this vertex falls is point C. Pressing corner A at point C, bend the upper part of the square along a line passing through C parallel to the bases. This will mark point C (Fig. 8), after which the sheet is folded along the lines BC and AC.
