School of Cosmonautics

In 1986, a visiting School of Cosmonautics (SC) was organized at Krasnoyarsk State University. It is held 3-4 times a year, usually in school books.

SC is a temporary creative team consisting of high school students (8-10) grades, students, graduate students, teachers and researchers. The task of this temporary creative team is professional development: for experienced teachers and researchers - improving pedagogical skills; for undergraduates and graduate students - deepening knowledge of the subject being studied and acquisition for teaching activities; for schoolchildren - choosing a profession and initial preparation for it.

The basic principles of organizing the work of the ShK are as follows: staggered deadlines in accordance with the maximum load; creating conditions for informal communication among schoolchildren, students, teachers and researchers in an intensive work environment; creating conditions for the free choice of activities by each cadet and for the development of collective forms of work “epicages”.

The tenth ShK became the first all-Union school cosmonautics with the participation of schoolchildren from Bulgaria.

It took place from January 30 to February 12, 1989 on the basis of the Higher Komsomol School at the Komsomol School in Moscow. The All-Union Youth Aerospace Society “Soyuz” took the most active part in the organization of the school, organizing and excursions to Zvezdny and the Mission Control Center, and meetings with interested people, and assistance in establishing business contacts.

For each visiting school, which generally lasts about two weeks, its own program is created, including lectures, seminars, work in the classroom or in laboratories, problem tasks, tours, a marathon, expert surveys, electives based on interests, discussion and amateur clubs.

For example, it includes an opshem program of ten ShK. The lectures were as follows: Newton's laws, gravity, conservation laws, near-space physics, thermodynamics, cosmic rays, space optics. These lectures were accompanied by seminars on exercises. Popular lectures were also given and lecture-meetings were held with leading space industry specialists on the following topics: classification of spacecraft and space systems, environmental systems, medical systems, medical process space technology, cosmology, fine arts and space, etc.

There were also organized excursions to the Kremlin, VDNKh, the Pushkin Museum, and the Korolev House Museum, where a meeting with space artists took place. The trip to Zvezdny included mathematical lectures and conversations with cosmonauts and leading space exploration specialists.
