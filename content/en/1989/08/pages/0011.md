⟦folio 9⟧

ABOUT THE GREAT NUMBER
DENOGARDUS
AND HOOK'S LAW

V. Yu. OVSIENKO

Recently, an international mathematical congress took place in the small German city of Sturmgard. The main topic discussed at the congress was the burnt handwriting, entitled "Prinitsi", by the medieval scholestine, alchemist and astrologer Denogardus. It was accidentally discovered while sorting through ancient documents stored in the city hall. It turned out that this manuscript contains amazing facts that are of great importance for modern mathematics.

Being persecuted by the Inkviz, Denogardus was forced to formalize his discoveries in the form of anagrams *) and drawings. The Inquisition failed to find the scientist during his lifetime. However, after his death, the only copy of the rukoitsi, following a denunciation by the flamer Denogardus, was confiscated and set on fire. Only the great thickness of the manuscript saved it from complete destruction. It is known that the astrologer was constantly engaged in calculating the so-called Great Number, which he believed controlled the movement of all celestial bodies (planets, comets, etc.) included in the Solar System. This Number changes all the time. Observing its change, Denogardus predicts (depending on the nature of the change) various disasters: floods, fires, wars and epidemics. Although these predictions were often accepted, his statement was not given due importance for the time being. For example, on page 666 of “Prinitspov” a great astronomical catastrophe was predicted, which the participants of the congress postponed with the fall of the Tunguska meteorite. The error in Denogardus's prediction is only 9 years. The found manuscript also contained a number of structured mathematical theorems. Denogardus called them schols*). Let us give their modern formulations.
