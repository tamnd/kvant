Selected School Problems

Eighth grade

1. Solve for number purposes the equation
$$1+x+x^2+x^3=2^y.$$

2. Solve the system of equations
$$x_1x_2x_3=1,$$
$$x_2x_3x_4=-1,$$
$$x_3x_4x_5=1,$$
$$x_{10}x_1x_2=-1.$$

3. There are 30 numbers written around a circle, each of which is equal to the modulo difference of the two numbers following it in a clockwise direction. The sum of all numbers is 20. Find these numbers.

4. Prove that in a circle of radius 1 it is impossible to select more than five points whose pairwise distances are greater than 1.

5. At the meeting, collect all sections of two hiking trips. Some of them were on both campaigns, some of them were on only one of them. In the first campaign there were 60% men, in the second - 75%. Prove that at least as many men came to the meeting as women.

Ninth grade

6. Given the function $p(x)=\frac{x^2}{1+x^2}$. Find the amount
$$p\left(\frac{1}{1}\right)+p\left(\frac{2}{1}\right)+...+p\left(\frac{100}{1}\right)+$$
$$+p\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)+p\left(\frac{2}{2}\right)+...+p\left(\frac{100}{2}\right)+$$
$$+p\left(\frac{1}{100}\right)+p\left(\frac{2}{100}\right)+...+p\left(\frac{100}{100}\right).$$

7. Prove that among numbers of the form $\frac{k(k+1)}{2}$ there are infinitely many perfect squares.

8. Find the ratio of the side of a triangle, one of the medians of which is divided by the circumference into three equal parts.

9. Let MA and MB be tangents to the circle, C be a point of the circle bounded by this circle, lying on the arc AB of the circle with center M. Prove that the intersection points of lines AC and BC with the given circle, different from A and B, are diametrically opposite.

10. Given an equilateral triangle ABC. Find the locus of points M such that triangles AMB and BMC are equilateral.

Tenth grade

11. Let a, b, c be positive numbers. Prove that
$$(a^a b^b c^c)^2 \geq a^b+c^b+c^a+b^a.$$

12. Let a, b, c, d be arbitrary numbers whose sum is 1. Prove that
$$a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2-2ab-2bc-2cd-2da \geq -\frac{1}{4}.$$

13. Prove that if one of the heights of a terraer intersects two other heights, then all four heights intersect at one point.

14. Can terraera place an arrow on each edge so that the sum of six positive vectors equals 0?

15. Prove that:
a) the numbers $tg^2 20^\circ, tg^2 40^\circ, tg^2 80^\circ$ are the roots of the equation $x^3-33x^2+27x-3=0$;
b) $\frac{1}{\cos^2 20^\circ}+\frac{1}{\cos^2 40^\circ}+\frac{1}{\cos^2 60^\circ}+\frac{1}{\cos^2 80^\circ}=40$.
