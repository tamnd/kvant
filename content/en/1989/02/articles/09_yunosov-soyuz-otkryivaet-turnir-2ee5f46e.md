"Union" opens the Tournament

Each explorer of the Universe goes his own way to the stars. The all-inclusive aerospace society Soyuz proposes to begin this journey with the participation of a number of space explorers in Touripra. Similar tours (in physics) have been conducted by Moscow University for more than ten years. And they are always unusual and exciting. Well, Turip begins...

Assignments for the correspondence collective competition
The conditions of the tasks are formed as briefly as possible. You yourself should make the necessary assumptions and reasonable processes, choose a model to describe this phenomenon and an algorithm for solving the problem, and examine the results obtained.

1. Watch. You have visited a neculo planet and are planning to return to it in ten thousand or even a million years. What clock will you leave on this planet to accurately change the time of your absence from the planet?

a) 100 meters,
b) 10 kilometers.

5. Fishing from satellite. Is it possible to lower a probe from a stationary satellite on a cable to the surface of the Earth or at least to the dense layers of the atmosphere?

6. Metoritic protection. Develop a system to protect a spacecraft from micrometerites and medium-sized meteorites. If your system does not go off, then how much time does the astronaut have to detect and answer a 1 mm diameter hole pierced by a meteorite in the ship's hull?

7. Black hole. How to imagine that your spaceship is approaching a black hole, and how to avoid such an unpleasant encounter?

8. Comet. Imagine that an icy comet with a mass of $10^{15}$ kg could reach the Earth in a few years. Develop a disaster prevention project.

10. Space experiment. Let the expedition to Mars consist of eight people. How would you distribute responsibilities in such a team? How to avoid conflict situations during the Earth-Mars-Earth flight?

11. Video. Propose a script for a 5-10 minute scientific or educational film that will not be in the space laboratory.

12. The great confrontation. Armed with a telescope and other physical instruments, you can establish systematic observations of Mars. Share your observations with us.

13. Gravitational acceleration. Justify the method of using the granite field of planets to accelerate a spacecraft. In what year and on what date would you propose to begin the probe’s flight to the edges of the Solar System, if Mars is used to speed up the solution? The starting speed of the probe is 15 km/s.

14. Atmosphere of the Moon. Imagine that you managed to create the atmosphere of the Earth on the Moon. Describe its parameters and properties. How quickly will she “lose weight” and how to maintain such an atmosphere?

15. Football on the Moon. Perhaps in the future Moon the earth will become a crappy resting place. Unfortunately, football fans will be disappointed. Offer an active game for individuals on the Moon that is as exciting as football.
