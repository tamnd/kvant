A. A. Egorov. I have been working at the boarding school since the end of 1963 - from the moment it opened, so the entire history of the school is in my memory.

The boarding school implemented and is implementing the idea of ​​its creator A. N. Kolmogov: to give a gifted student the opportunity to get involved in scientific work as early as possible (starting from the junior year of university). Thus, the main task of teaching is the development of sustainable interest, even the need for independent research and creativity. We show students different approaches to problems, teaching them to look at a problem from different angles. Unfortunately, mathematics is often taught in schools the way they teach the “Rule” in the army, and sometimes children come to us, although capable, but already inhibited by their school teachers. Uninhibiting them, getting their brains to work, teaching them to learn and instilling a taste for learning - that's what we strive to do.

D. L. Abrarov. I went from a student (graduated from a boarding school in 1977) to a school director. The years of my stay at the boarding school will be remembered for the rest of my life by my learning and interesting life. I am grateful to the teachers who support and develop our interest in mathematics and physics. In our class, we solved problems proposed by the Students' Scientific Society. Very difficult, research tasks, sometimes problems. Each student could try to solve them. There was a strong desire to answer the book and sleep off, but very soon a book was in my hands, and the days were spent in independent studies. Such difficult books as Kolmogorov and Fomin’s book “Elements of the theory of functions and the functioning of analysis” additionally fueled interest with their incomprehensibility. The matrifikum was carried out with enthusiasm (sometimes we were driven around by our lecturers when we were solving problems, huddled in some secluded corner). This, however, still exists.

V.V. Vavilov. Dmitry Leonardovich Abrarov mentioned a wonderful academic subject - mathematical practice. In the room where we are now talking, there is a stand with all the numbers «Kvant» released before the middle of the day. So, I see at least 7 covers that depict paintings made by schoolchildren while preparing assignments for practical work. What is it? Approximately once a month there are lectures at the boarding school, and then assignments for the workshop are posted. While completing assignments, schoolchildren must master the theories, make calculations and complete the graphic part of the work. These tasks are unique, they require the ability to navigate in an unfamiliar situation, independence and depth of assimilation of the material - essentially this is research work.
