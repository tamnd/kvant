Here we were talking about ways to calculate the flight of the popular “flying saucer” toy.

D. L. Abarov. Now the intern becomes a division of the University *, implementing Lomonosov’s idea: “There should be hypnasia at the University.” Of course, the material support should be better than that of a regular school. So far, unfortunately, our material and technical level does not correspond to the level of teaching. For example, now we have 2 classes with computer technology, but we need 4-5. Becoming a part of Moscow State University allows us to make fuller use of the University’s enormous potential. A training ground is being created for practicing training courses, writing books, publishing literature - a whole range of teaching aids, problem books. After all, now, to be honest, many methodological discoveries simply disappear.

A. A. Egorov. It is added to this that the first years of the boarding school’s existence were the time when, under the leadership of A. N. Kolmogorov, the modernization of school mathematics programs took place. And it was in the boarding school that Andrei Nikolaevich’s ideas were worked out. He really wanted to write a textbook for boarding school. Unfortunately, it was no longer possible for him to do this.

G. Sh. Friedman. I am a participant in the first school in Novosibirsk. It took place in the summer of 1962. More than 600 schoolchildren from all over the country gathered in Akademgorodok. The idea of ​​such a school was born back in the 30s, but it is no coincidence that it was realized only in the early 60s (the time of the thaw) - and in Siberia (far from Moscow). The school lasted 45 days! Teachers - young employees of the Siberian Branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences, graduate students,

*) In October 1988, a decree of the Council of Ministers of the USSR was issued on the organization of specializations of primary education centers of Moscow and Novosibirsk State Universities, which included the corresponding specializations of boarding schools.

NSU students - substitute assembly of people. Lectures were given by the organizers of the school, academicians M. A. Lavrentyev, G. I. Budker, and corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences A. A. Lyapunov. The kids moved away, school ended... We thought: shouldn't we bring them back? M.A. Lavrentiev accepted this idea and approved it. We found the value and ways of financing. And thus, in January 1963, we began to study at the Physics and Mathematics School at NSU.

The first scientific director of the school was A. A. Lyapunov. We taught excellently not only mathematics and physics, but also biology (the only school in the country where genetics was studied!). A leadership magazine was published and a posing club operated. A. A. Lyapunov, in addition to mathematical courses, taught an interesting course, “Earth Science,” which he himself invented. At his home, he held classes in a circle on set theory, and after the class, over tea, there were conversations about everything: science, music, painting, literature.

V. N. Dubrovsky. I studied at a boarding school in 1965-67, and in 1969, while still a student, I began teaching. The spirit of the first years of school was remembered with great satisfaction. Our favorite lecturer and teacher A.V. Sosinsky taught us not only mathematics, but also English. Most of the mathematics lessons were taught in English. I remembered the “Green Lamp” evenings: at them Yu. A. Kim and E. V. Gaidukov read poems by the Symbolists, “The Master and Margarita,” Gumilyov, Balmont. And what were the literature lessons of N.I. Gerasimov - under his influence, we read avidly, despite very intensive mathematics classes. The history lessons of R. A. Ostroy were truly fascinating. Her enormous knowledge, standard approaches, introduction to historical literature (for example, “Napoleon” by E.V. Tarle) gave us a lot: we knew history much better than graduates of training schools.
