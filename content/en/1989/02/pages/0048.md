⟦folio 46⟧

Quests

1. Sometimes sellers, when accepting chalk, weigh it. However, the seller for 50 rubles in coins of 15 and 20 kopecks. Their total mass turned out to be 800 g. How many coins were there? (A fifteen-kopeck coin (fifteenth) weighs 2.5 g, and a twenty-kopeck coin (two-kopeck coin) weighs 3 g.)
2. Place the numbers from 1 to 8 in the circles of the figure shown at figure, so that the sum of the numbers on each circle is the same.
3. My son and I went on a boat on the lake. The water reflected the coastal forest. The son said: “Let’s put it on the reflection, I want it to be under my feet.” We tried to do this, but the reflection “ran away” from us. Why?
4. The newspaper “Soviet Sport” (3.V.1987) published an interim table of one football tournament:

| | Games | Victory | Nichnyh | Damage | Various ball. | Glasses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hungary | 2 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 4—1 | 4 |
| Sweden | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1—1 | 3 |
| Spain | 2 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 3—3 | 2 |
| Ireland | 3 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3—5 | 1 |
| France | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0—2 | 0 |

Prove that there is an error in the table, and, knowing that there is only one error, correct it and indicate the results of the matches played.

5. The point of intersection of the midlines of a convex quadrilateral connecting with its vertices (see figure). Prove that the sum of the areas of the red triangles is equal to the sum of the areas of the blue triangles.
