It turns out that with the help of simple and accessible at home methods, it is possible to measure important optical characteristics of some physical devices and properties of matter.

Consider, for example, a simple method for measuring the focal lengths of a line and mirrors and the radii of curvature of their surface - this can be useful to an astronomer - and a method for determining the refractive index of a liquid - and this is useful for everyone.
