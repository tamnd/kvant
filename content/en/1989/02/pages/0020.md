At what frequencies does the Earth “ring”?

From a “fluffy stone” several meters in size it is easy to move one’s thinking to a huge rock and even further - to the entire Earth as a whole. It is interesting to find out at what frequencies such a global, i.e., covering the entire planet, resonator can “sound” and what sources are capable of generating it.

The globe, which has a complex internal structure, is also surrounded by an atmosphere, the upper layers of which (nonosphere) have high electrical conductivity. Taking all this into account, we can expect the emergence of global resonances of three types. Two of them are associated with mechanical vibrations inside the Earth and in its atmosphere, and the third is with electromagnetic waves in the gap between the earth’s surface and the ionosphere. Correspondence There are three series of natural frequencies of the global resonant, which can be estimated using the above formulas.
