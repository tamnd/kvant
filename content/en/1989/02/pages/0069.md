⟦folio 67⟧

Option 4
(chemistry department)

1. Solve the equation
$$2\log_7(x-2) = \log_7(x-10)^2 - 2.$$

2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{2} \cos x = \sqrt{-3} \sqrt{3} \sin x - 4.$$

3. Find a point in the graph of the function $y = 1 - 2x^2$ that is close to the point $(1; \frac{3}{4})$.

4. A triangle $ABC$ with side length $BC$ equal to 11 is inscribed with a circle touching side $AB$ at point $D$. It is known that the length of the segment $AC$ is equal to the length of the segment $CD$ and the cosine of the angle $BAC$ is equal to $\frac{1}{6}$. Find the length of side $AC$.

5. Find all values ​​of the parameter $a$ for which the systems of equations are equivalent
$$\begin{cases} ax + 3y = 6a - 4, \\ x + y = 2a \end{cases}$$
And
$$\begin{cases} x^2 - 2y^4 - 6x + 8 = 0, \\ x^2 + y^2 - (2a+4)x + 2(a^2 + a + 2) = 0. \end{cases}$$

Option 5
(Blues Faculty)

1. Find the named positive root of the equation
$$6\sin \frac{3x}{2} \sin \frac{x}{2} + 2\cos \frac{3x}{2} \cos \frac{x}{2} + 1 = 0.$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$2\log_2 3 + \log_2 \left(3^x - 1 - \frac{1}{9}\right) < \log_2 2 6.$$

3. Two excavators of varying power, working with constant productivity, dug a pit of a given volume in 2 hours 24 minutes. The first excavator, working alone, would complete the job 2 hours faster than the second. Find the time it would take to dig a second excavator, working alone.

4. The area of ​​triangle $ABC$ is $2\sqrt{3} - 3$ and the angle $BAC$ is $60^\circ$. The radius of the circle tangent to side $AC$ and extended to sides $BA$ and $BC$ is $\sqrt{3}$. Find the angles $ABC$ and $BCA$ of the given triangle.

5. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} \left(\sqrt{3} \cos \frac{\pi x}{4} + \sin \frac{\pi(x-2y)}{12}\right) \times \left(\sqrt{11-x^2-y^2}-4x-2y-6\right) = 0, \\ \frac{1}{2} + 2 \sin \frac{\pi x}{12} \cos \frac{\pi y}{6} = 2 \cos \frac{\pi x}{12} \sin \frac{\pi y}{6} + \sin^2 \frac{\pi x}{8}. \end{cases}$$

Option 6
(faculty of military studies)

1. Solve the equation
$$3 \sin x - 2 \text{ ctg } x = 0.$$

2. On the diameter $MN$ of the circle, the point $R$ is offset and the chord $PQ$ is drawn through it. It is known that the angles $MNP$ and $NPR$ are $60^\circ$ and $13^\circ$, respectively, and the length $PR$ is 3. Find the diameter of the circle.

3. Solve the equation
$$5 \cdot 4^x - 11 \cdot 6^x + 2 \cdot 9^x = 0.$$

4. Two types of animal feed - A and B - differ in the weight content of proteins, fats and carbohydrates. In feed A there are 5/3 times more proteins, and 1.5 times less carbohydrates by weight than fats. Feed B contains 7/2 times less protein and carbohydrates than fat, respectively. Is it possible by mixing feeds A and B to prepare a feed that contains 1.5 times more protein and 5 times more carbohydrates than fats?

5. At what values ​​of the parameter $p$ of the system
Does $$\begin{cases} x^2 + 2px + 4p^2 - 5p + 3 \geq 4\sin y - 3\cos y, \\ 0 \leq y \leq 2\pi \end{cases}$$ have a unique solution?

Option 7
(Zeodraphic Faculty)

1. Find the domain of a function
$$y = \log_2 x (x^2 + x - 2).$$

2. Solve the inequality
$$2\log_2 3 + \log_2 \left(3^x - 1 - \frac{1}{9}\right) < \log_2 2 6.$$

3. From point $A$ to point $C$, located at a distance of 80 km from $A$, a motoric list flew out. Towards him and simultaneously with him, a cyclist came out of point $B$, located between $A$ and $C$ at a distance of 5 km from $C$, and a car came out of point $C$. How long did it take for the motor-cyclist and the velocipedist to meet, if it is known that this happened 20 minutes after the car caught up with the velocipedist, and the motor-cyclist, before meeting the car, spent twice as much time on the road as the cyclist before the car passed him?

4. The triangular paramid $ABCD$ intersects the plane $P$ along the quadrilateral $EFGH$ such that the vertices $E$ and $F$ lie on the edges $AB$ and $AC$. The ratio of the sides $EF$ and $EH$ is equal to 3. It is known that the plane $P$ is parallel to the opposite edges $AD$ and $BC$, the ratio of which is $\frac{1}{3}$. Find the relation in which the point $E$ divides the edge $AB$.

5. Prove that for all $x > 0$ the inequality
$$x^2 + x + 4\pi \cos x > 0.$$

Option 8
(Zeodraphic Faculty)

1. Solve the equation
$$(x - 2)(|x| + \sqrt{3} - 1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}) = 0.$$

2. Find all solutions to the inequality
$$(x^2 + 8x + 15)\sqrt{x + 4} \geq 0.$$

3. Solve the equation
$$(2x^2 - 5x + 2)(\log_2 x (18x) + 1) = 0.$$

4. The point $O$ lies on the segment $AB$, so $AO = 13, OB = 7$. A circle of radius 5 is drawn with center at $O$. Tangents are drawn to it from $A$ and $B$, intersecting at point $M$, and the tangent points lie on one side of the line $AB$. Find the rounding radius $AMB$.
