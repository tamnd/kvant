And yet there is a law of coexistence of phases of the real. It is called the Gibbs phase rule after its discoverer.

All biographies of the American theoretical physicist J. Gibbs (1839-1903) emphasize that the life of this man was outwardly poor in his own. All his efforts are concentrated on obtaining scientific results, the brilliance of which has not dimmed to this day. More than 80 years have passed since the publication of Gibbs’ last work, and the results of his research continue to be used practically unchanged.

The phase rule has always been considered one of Gibbs' most significant achievements. The strict derivation of the rule is quite complicated. We will present it in an essentially forgiven form.
