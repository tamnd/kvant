Physics

Oral Exam Objective

1. A sheet of paper lies on a horizontal table, pressed by a homogeneous rod of mass $m$, the top end of which is hinged. What minimum horizontal force must be applied to the sheet to pull it out? The angle between the rod and the sheet is $a$, the friction coefficient between them is $\mu$. Neglect the friction between the table and the paper.

2. A thread of length $l$ with a ball of mass $m$ attached to it was deflected $90^\circ$ from the vertical and released. At what minimum vertical distance from the suspension point should a nail be placed so that the thread, leaning on it, breaks? The thread can withstand the tension force $T$.

3. Two bars lie on a horizontal plane, connected by an unstressed spring. The masses of the bars $m_1$ and $m_2$. What is the smallest constant force directed horizontally that must be applied to the first block in order for the second block to move as well? The friction coefficient of the bars on the plane is $\mu$.

4. The volume of the balloon is $V = 224$ m³, the mass of the shell is $m = 145$ kg. The ball is filled with hot air at normal atmospheric pressure. What temperature must the air volume of the shell be for the ball to begin to rise? Air temperature outside the shell $t_0 = 0^\circ\text{C}$.

5. A thin-walled rubber ball with a mass of $m_0 = 50$ g is filled with nitrogen and immersed in a lake to a depth of $h = 100$ m. Find the mass of nitrogen if the ball is in an equilibrium position. Will the equilibrium be stable? Atmospheric pressure $p_0 = 760$ mm Hg. Art., temperature in the depths of the lake $t = 4^\circ\text{C}$, molar mass of nitrogen $M = 0,028$ kg/mol. Neglect the tension of the rubber.

6. Two identical exact charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ with masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ are moving towards each other. At the moment when the distance between the charges is $r$, they have speeds $v_1$ and $v_2$. To what minimum distance will the charges merge?

7. A flat capacitor is placed in an external uniform electric field perpendicular to the plates. The charged field is $E = 1$ kV/m, the area of ​​the capacitor plates is $S = 100$ cm². What charges appear on each of the plates if the capacitor is short-circuited by a conductor? The capacitor plates are not charged until they close.

8. A ray of light falls on a stack of flat transparent plates of equal thickness, the value of each of which is $k$ times less than that of the one above it. At what smallest angle of incidence will the beam not pass through the stack? The supply indicator of the upper plates is $n$, the number of plates is $N$.

9. At what distance from a glass ball of radius $R$ should a precise light source be placed so that its image appears on the other side of the ball at the same distance? Stack supply indicator $n$. The image is created by a narrow beam of rays close to the optical axis.

10. A flat aluminum electrode is illuminated with ultraviolet light with a wavelength of $\lambda = 83$ nm. What is the maximum distance a photoelectron can move from the electrode surface if there is a retarding electric field outside the electrode directed $E = 7,5$ V/cm? The red boundary of the photoelectric effect for aluminum corresponds to the wavelength $\lambda_{\max} = 332$ nm.
