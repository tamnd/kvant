Centuries have passed from the attempts of the ancients to find patterns in the movement of planets - “wandering stars” - to the work of Copernicus. In fact, through the works of Copernicus and his subsequent work, a gigantic step was taken that determined the entire further development of astronomical science. Of course, since then, mankind has immeasurably enriched its ability to the point of being able to study the structure of the solar system directly from international spacecraft. However, modern space research has not led to a change in the fundamental laws of planetary motion, established in the days of only observable astronomy. Moreover, you will be convinced that with their help you can cope with problems that arose only with the advent of the space age.
