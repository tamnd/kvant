$$3\sqrt{\log_3 x} - \log_3(3x) \leq 1.$$

Prove that the segment $GF$ is parallel to the base $AC$.
4.2.!) Find the area of ​​the figure formed by those points $(x, y)$ of the Cartesian plane for which the equations are satisfied
$$|x+1| + |y| \leq 2, (x+2)^2 + y^2 \leq 1.$$
