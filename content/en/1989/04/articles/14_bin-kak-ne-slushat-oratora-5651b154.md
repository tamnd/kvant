How not to serve as a speaker

No speaker, no matter what his energy, has a chance of defeating the slanderers... Few of us have the courage to sleep openly and honestly during a formal speech. After a thorough investigation of this issue, I can present for the reader's consideration several original methods that have not yet been published.

Sit as deeply as you can in the cross, tilt your head slightly forward (this frees the tongue, it can see freely without making breathing difficult). Loud snoring infuriates even the most humble speaker, so the main thing is to avoid snoring and keep all airways clear. It is difficult to give accounting instructions for maintaining balance in a dream. Whatever the head can move to the side, the most stable thing for it from two hands and a tolvish is a strong support in the shape of a tripod... So your head will not fall on your chest, and the whole thing will not fall off. The closing of the eye should be hidden in the palms of the hands, while the fingers should fold the forehead into an accordion. This produces typing on the hard work of thought and somewhat puzzles the speaker. It's possible to get kicked out during nightmares, but that's a risk you take. Wake up slowly, look around and don’t start applauding right away. This may turn out to be uncertain. It’s better to wait until the final applause wakes you up.

W. W. Bean
(From the book “Physics continue to joke”)
