⟦folio 35⟧

Quests

1. Find two numbers such that when you multiply the first by 2, you get the square of the second, and when you multiply the first by 3, you get the cube of the second.

2. One day in a train carriage, Tanya began to defend words by replacing letters with their numbers in the alphabet. When she defended the arrival and departure points of the train, she was surprised to find that they were recorded using only two numbers:
   211221 — 21221
Where does the train come from and where does it go?

3. Solve the arithmetic puzzle (see figure). The same numbers must correspond to the same letters, and different numbers to different ones.

4. figure shows the dial of a watch from the Tona-Benguet company. What color is most fruitful on it: red, blue or yellow?

5. In some four-digit numbers, the digits were presented and the new number was subtracted from the original one. The result was a number written down with a new one using the same numbers. What number is this?

These tasks were offered to us by: a student of the 8th grade of the Yarak secondary school of the DASSR G. Magomedov, a student of the 10th grade from Kharkov V. Gorbachev, a student of the 7th grade from the village of Protvino, Moscow region. O. Ivanova, 9th grade student from Kharkov S. Lyashenko and 7th grade student from Belogorsk, Crimean region. N. Yablochina.
