"Union" announces a competition

The prominent young arocosmic society “Soyuz” and the Institute of Medical and Biological Problems of the USSR Ministry of Health are holding a competition for the best biological experiment in space.

In 1987, the Soviet Union launched the non-training satellite Cosmos 1877: the target “zoo” was launched into low-Earth orbit. Scientific experiments were carried out on board the biosatellite in order to understand how irrelevance affects the life of organisms. The most uneducational thing was that two experiments were invented, prepared and “conducted” by schoolchildren (students in grades 8-9). They promoted at the spaceport, participated in equipping the satellite, reported on the results at the council of scientists and even at an international symposium.

In July - August of this year, the next Soviet biosatellite is expected to be launched. There will also be space on board for an experiment prepared by schoolchildren. Last time it turned out that there were a lot of proposals, but, of course, only one, the best experiment, will work. It should be interesting (original, meaningful), realistic (feasible) and prepared independently.

If you decide to participate in the competition, then:

give a clear answer to the question of how irrevocability affects the chosen version of the project, the indicator of the life of research;

prove that the chosen organism will certainly survive and return safely to Earth (flight duration 17 days, maximum volume allocated for the experiment, 3-4 matchboxes, no light, room temperature, the atmosphere in the satellite is normal, earthly, the packaging should be used in the satellite cabin, you should also take care of food for your chosen ones).

The aftereffects of the experiment and the processing of its results after the landing of the biosatellite will have to be carried out by the Institute of Medical and Biological Problems (the organizers of the competition will cover the costs of travel, hotel accommodation and travel to the cosmoport).

Your proposals were sent to the address: 123007 Moscow, Khoroshevskoye Shosse, 76a, Institute of Biomedical Problems of the USSR Ministry of Health, Alexey Mikhailovich Alpatov.

We wish you success!
