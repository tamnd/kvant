Correspondence Physics School at Moscow State University

The Correspondence Physics School (3FS) at the Faculty of Physics of Moscow State University announces admission of students to grades 9 and 10 for the next academic year.

The main goal of 3FS is to help high school students in a deeply scientific or technical direction, as well as to successfully work in complex areas of science and technology, where two or more scientific directions meet. For example, in biophysics, geophysics, radio-gamma-infrared astronomy, chemical physics, space physics, etc.

Admission to 3FS is carried out based on the results of solving the introductory task published below. The solution to the introductory assignment must be sent before September 1 to the address: 119899 Moscow, GSP, Leninskie Gory, Moscow State University, Faculty of Physics, 3ФШ. In the letter, please include two copies of the angela, written on sheets of thick paper measuring 7x12 cm and filled out according to the sample given here.

The decision of the admissions committee on admission to 3FS will be communicated by October 20. Verified entry assignments will not be returned.

During the year, those enrolled in 3FS are sent methodological developments and test assignments on the sections of physics studied in accordance with their high school classes. The solution to the task is assessed, solved and returned to the student. Students in grade 9 of 3FS at the end of the year are transferred to grade 10 based on the grades received for solving test tasks. Those who successfully complete the training receive a certificate of completion of 3FS.

Introductory task
When we enter 9th grade 3FS we need to solve problems 1-4, and when we enter 10th grade we need to solve problems 4-7.

1. "Sharp Shooter" The barrel of the gun and the center of the target suspended on a thread are on the same horizontal line. Will the bullet hit the center of the target if at the moment the bullet leaves, the thread reverses and the target begins to fall freely? Neglect air resistance.

2. "Road and transport existence." The driver of the vehicle, traveling at speed v, suddenly saw a wide wall in front of him at a distance a. What is more profitable for him: to brake or turn? The force in both cases is considered the same.

3. "Wedge". A wedge with a right angle at the apex and with angles a and β at the base of the stone on the table. Along its side grams, bars of the same mass simultaneously begin to slide without friction. Will the wedge slide on the table (there is no training)?

4. "Chain". On a completely smooth table lies a chain hanging halfway over the edge of the table. How will the sliding time of the chain change if two identical weights are attached to both ends?

5. "Explosion". A projectile fired from an ordium at a certain angle to the horizon breaks into two identical fragments at the top point of its trajectory. Returns one salt after the explosion
