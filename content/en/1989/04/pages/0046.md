⟦folio 44⟧

One shuttle, two shuttle...

V. V. NIKOLAEV

And the flame is brighter, faith is gone,
How simple is the deceptive shuttle.
V. Ropshin, 1911

For a long time, any reputable enterprise has had to rely on three pillars. Human exploration of near-Earth space in this sense is no exception. “Whales” of astronautics are package-space systems that provide payload launch into orbit, means for its return to Earth and orbital facilities.

In the first years of the space age, the USSR and the USA adhered to similar concepts in building their space programs, including manned ones, but over the years, differences began to appear more and more often.

In 1961, the world's first spacecraft, Vostok, was launched in the USSR. A ship similar (in terms of tasks) - "Mercury" - was created in the same year in the USA. They were followed by Voskhod and Gemini. The next generation of ships - Soyuz and Apollo, although they appeared at approximately the same time (1967-1968), differed not only in design, but also in their purpose. The Soyuz spacecraft was conceived as a vehicle to work mainly with orbital stations and (subject to conditions) is in operation and complete. The number of launches reached six tens! The Progress cargo ship was also made on its basis (forty more launches). The Apollo spacecraft was built as part of the American expedition to the Moon and, after its brilliant completion (in 1972), was used several times for flights to the American Skylab station and under the program of the Soviet-American project Soyuz - Apollo.
