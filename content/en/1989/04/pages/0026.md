⟦folio 24⟧

Quests

M1156 — M1160, F1163 — F1167

M1156. Eight hockey teams compete against each other to reach the final four. (Each two meet once, two points are given for a win, one point for a draw, and 0 points for a loss.)
What is the lowest number of points that guarantees reaching the final four?

Sanzhar Khodzhev, 10th grade student

M1157. When the triangles - white, red and green - have a common interior point M. Prove that you can choose one vertex of each triangle so that the point M is inside or on the border of a triangle with vertices at the selected points of three different colors.

Imre Baranyi (Hungary)

M1158. Find the smallest value of the expression $(x+y)(x+z)$ if $x, y, z$ are positive numbers and $xyz(x+y+z)=1$.

Oleg Khristenko, 10th grade student

M1159. Using a double-sided ruler, construct an angle of magnitude $30^\circ$. The following operations are allowed:
(1) drawing a line through two points, (2) drawing a line parallel to the given one at a distance equal to the width of the ruler.

A. V. Polev

M1160. At one end $A$ of the straight road $AB$ 10 kangaroos gathered and began to play leapfrog. They take turns yoking: the first one yokes wherever he wants each time; the second yokes through the correct one so that the first is exactly in the middle between the beginning and end of the buckle, the third yokes in the same way through the second, etc., the tenth yokes the ninth, then a new series of buckles begins according to the same rules.
a) After 10 series of buckles, can all the kangaroos be collected at point B?
b) Can they collect there earlier?

S. L. Eliseev

F1163. Due to the low friction coefficient, the automatizable cannot move on ice with an acceleration exceeding modulo $a=0,5$ m/s². According to the cutting conditions, the driver needs to get from point $A$ in the shortest time to point $B$, located on a straight line perpendicular to the direction of the initial automation speed (Fig. 1). What is the minimum possible time if the distance is $AB=375$ m and the lowest speed is $v_0=10$ m/s? What trajectory should it move automatically? Answer similar questions for the case when the finish is at point $C$, $BC=200$ m.

A. N. Korotkov
