Leningrad State University

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
(faculty of mathematics and mechanics, departments of applied mathematics - control processes and physical)
1. Solve the equation
$$\frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x} + \frac{\cos x}{1+\sin x} = 2.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$3\sqrt{\log_3 x} - \log_3(3x) \leq 1.$$
3. Find all pairs $(a, b)$ of parameter values ​​$a$ and $b$ for which all real roots of equation $x^2 - ax + a = 0$ are also roots of equation $x^2 + b^2x - 8b = 0$.
4.1. On sides $AB$ and $BC$ of triangle $ABC$, points $D$ and $E$ are selected, respectively. The point $G$ is the point of intersection of the line parallel to $BC$ and passing through $D$ with the segment $AE$, and the point $F$ is the point of intersection of the segment $CD$ with the line passing through $E$ and parallel to $AB$. Prove that the segment $GF$ is parallel to the base $AC$.
4.2.!) Find the area of ​​the figure formed by those points $(x, y)$ of the Cartesian plane for which the equations are satisfied
$$|x+1| + |y| \leq 2, (x+2)^2 + y^2 \leq 1.$$
5. A ball of volume $V$ is placed in a right circular cone. What is the smallest value the volume of a cone can have?

Option 2
(Psychological Faculty, Department of Economic Cybernetics, Faculty of Economics)
1. The numbers $a, b, c$ form an arithmetic progression, and the numbers $a-1, b-4, c-3$ form a geometric progression. It is also known that the product of the extreme terms of a geometric progression is 2 greater than the middle term of an arithmetic progression. Find these numbers.
2. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{5-x^2} = x+1.$$
3.1. Find all solutions to the inequality $1+\cos 2x \geq \cos x (1+|1-2\cos x|)$ on the interval $[0; 2\pi]$.

3.2.*) Solve the inequality $|1/2 - \cos x| \geq \cos x$.
4. In an equilateral triangle, the radius of the circumscribed circle is $R$, and the radius of the inscribed circle is $r$. Find the distance between the centers of these circles.
5. In a regular triangular paramid $SABC$, a section perpendicular to edge $SA$ is drawn through the edge of the base $BC$. Find the ratio of the volumes of the parts into which this section divides the paramid, if it is known that the height of the paramid is twice the radius of the circle circumscribed around the base.

Option 3
(Faculty of Chemistry)
1. Find a three-digit number if you know that the sum of its digits is 12, and the number written with the same digits in the opposite order is 24 less than triple the original number.
2. Solve the equation
$$(7\cos^2 x - 2)\sqrt{2\cos x + 1} = 0.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{2x+3} - \sqrt{x} \geq \sqrt{3-x}.$$
4. Graph the Function
$$f(x) = \frac{x^2-3|x|+4}{x}.$$
5. In a regular triangular paramid, the height is $H$ and the radius of the circle inscribed at the base is $R$. Find the volume of the sphere tangent to the plane of the base at its center and the plane drawn through the vertex of the paramides and the middle of the two sides of the base.

Option 4
(Faculty of Bibliology, Department of Mathematical License of the Faculty of Physics)
1. Graph the Function
$$f(x) = |x-1| - |x+3/2|.$$
2. Solve the equation
$$x-1 = \sqrt{x^4-x^3-x^2-2x+1}.$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\sin(\cos x) \geq 1/2.$$
4. On the data plane there is a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$. Find the locus of the points that are the midpoints of the segments whose ends lie on $AB$ and $CD$.
5. Two equal regular triangular paramides $SABC$ and $S'A'B'C'$ are arranged such that $S=S'$, the centers of the bases $O$ and $O'$ coincide, and the angle $AOA'$ is equal to $30^\circ$. Find the volume of a part of these two paramidas, if it is known that the sides of the bases are equal to $a$, the total height of the paramidas is $h$.
