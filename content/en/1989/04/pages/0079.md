$$N \cos \alpha + F_{\text{tp}} \sin \alpha = (M + m)g,$$

$$M g l \cos \alpha = \frac{N a}{\cos \alpha}.$$
The condition for no sliding of the rod $F_{\text{tp}} < \mu N$ gives
$$(M + m)g < N(\cos \alpha + \mu \sin \alpha),$$
or
$$\left(\frac{m}{M} + 1\right) < \frac{l}{a} \cos^2 \alpha(\cos \alpha + \mu \sin \alpha).$$
The guard will not turn right if $N_1 > 0$, i.e.
$$1 + \frac{m}{M} > \frac{l}{a} \cos \alpha.$$
Taking into account the obvious condition $l \cos \alpha > a$, we obtain the answer in the form of a chain of three inequalities:
$$1 < \frac{l}{a} \cos \alpha < 1 + \frac{m}{M} < \frac{l}{a} \cos^2 \alpha(\cos \alpha + \mu \sin \alpha).$$
To simultaneously satisfy the last two restrictions, it is necessary that
$$\cos \alpha(\cos \alpha + \mu \sin \alpha) > 1,$$
i.e.
$$\mu > tg \alpha.$$
If $\mu < tg \alpha$, equilibrium is impossible.
