F1164. In construction, so-called pre-stressed reinforced concrete structures are used. The production of such a beam is carried out as follows. A steel rod with length $l_1$ is stretched to length $l_2$, after which it is filled with liquid concrete. After the concrete has hardened, the rod is released from the tensile force. Find the length of the formed reinforced concrete beam and its Young model. The cross-sectional areas of the rod and beam and the Young model of steel and concrete are considered known. Why is prestressed reinforced concrete better than plain reinforced concrete?

V. I. Klyachin

Answer: for four $n$.

Let us show that each element of the set $B$ belongs to exactly two of the set $A_i$. The presence of at least two such sets is ensured by condition c). Let us assume that some element belongs to the required places, say, $A_{2n+1}$, $A_i$, $A_j$. Let us associate with it the set $A_i$, and with each of the remaining elements $x \in A_{2n+1}$ one of the sets $A_1$, $A_2$, ..., $A_{2n}$ containing the element $x$ by condition c). Then different sets will be associated with different elements, and the set $A_j$ does not correspond to any element (otherwise some intersection $A_k \cap A_{2n+1}$, $k \neq 2n+1$ contains two elements, which contradicts conditions 6)). Therefore, the number of elements in $A_{2n+1}$ does not exceed $2n-1$. And this contradicts conditions a) and, thereby, refutes our assumption.
