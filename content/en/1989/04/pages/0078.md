$$N_1 = mg, N = F_{\text{тр}},$$
$$Nb \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}(b \sin \alpha - a \cos \alpha)mg.$$
In addition, it is necessary that the bars do not slip or tip over. Let's consider these conditions sequentially. The no-slip condition has the form
$$F_{\text{тр}} < \mu N_1.$$
Then we get
$$N = \frac{1}{2}mg \left( tg \alpha - \frac{a}{b} \right) < \mu mg,$$
And
$$\alpha < \arctg \left( \frac{a}{b} + 2\mu \right).$$
(1)
The absence of overturning of the right block (the block does not stand upright) is equivalent to the condition $N > 0$, i.e.
$$\frac{1}{2}mg \left( tg \alpha - \frac{a}{b} \right) > 0,$$
what gives
$$\alpha > \arctg \frac{a}{b}.$$
(2)
To find the absence of overturning of the left block, consider the limiting case of the beginning of overturning. In this case, the reaction force $N_1$ is applied to the left side of the base, and the equilibrium conditions for the moments of force have the form
$$Nb \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}mg.$$

There will be no capsizing when
$$Nb \cos \alpha < \frac{1}{2}mg,$$
or
$$\frac{1}{2}mg \left( b \sin \alpha - a \cos \alpha \right) < \frac{1}{2}mg,$$
where
$$\alpha < 2 \arctg \frac{a}{b}.$$
