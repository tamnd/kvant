By choosing these points arbitrarily, you can create a system of four equations to find $A, B, C, D$. However, it is more convenient to build differently. $$\frac{B}{A} - \frac{1}{C} = -\frac{7}{4}; -\frac{B}{AC} = \frac{D}{4}.$$
Since the denominators of equal fractions are equal, their numerators are equal. Equating the values ​​of the numerators at $x=0$ and $x=1$, we obtain $\frac{B-1}{AC} = -\frac{3}{2}$ and $\frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{C} = 3$. Solving the resulting system, we find two values ​​of $D$.
