⟦folio 71⟧

Moscow Institute
steel and alloys

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. Calculation
$$\frac{(13,75 + 9\frac{1}{2}) \cdot 1,2}{(10,3 - 8\frac{1}{2}) \cdot \frac{5}{9}}.$$
2. Simplify the calculation at $x = 1,33$
$$\frac{x - 1}{x + \sqrt{x} + 1} : \frac{\sqrt{x} + 1}{\sqrt{x}^3 - 1} + \frac{2}{x^{-1/2}}.$$
3. Compute $\cos \alpha$ if $tg \alpha = -3\frac{3}{7}$ and $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$.
4. Calculation of $\left( \frac{2}{\log_7 b} + 1 \right) \cdot b$ at $b = \sqrt[3]{2}$.
5. Solve the equation
$$\sqrt{3x^2 - x} + x = 1.$$
In your answer, write down the root of the equation, and in the case of several roots, their sum.
6. The oven heats up evenly. 0.5 hours after the start of heating, the oven temperature was 500 °C, and after another 1 hour - 900 °C. What was the original oven temperature?
7. Solve the inequality
$$\frac{1}{x - 4} - \frac{1}{x + 4} < 0.$$
In your answer, write down the sum of numbers that satisfy the inequality.
8. The axial cross-sectional area of ​​the cone is 12, and the length of its generatrix is ​​5. Find the ratio of the volume of the cone to the area of ​​its lateral surface.
9. Determine the value of the parameter $k$ at which the roots of the equation $x^2 + (k - 1)x + 2k - 8 = 0$ satisfy the conditions 2x₁ - x₂ = -6, where $x_1 < x_2$.
10. Solve the equation
$$\log_2 (4^x + 4) = x + \log_2 (2^{x+1} - 3).$$
In your answer, write down the root of the equation, and in the case of several roots, their sum.
11. Solve the equation
$$4\cos x \cdot \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{3} - x \right) \cdot \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{3} + x \right) = 1.$$
In your answer, write down the root of the equation (in degrees) belonging to the segment $[-\frac{\pi}{2}; \frac{\pi}{2}]$.
