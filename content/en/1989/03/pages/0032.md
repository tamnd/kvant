blows that at $k > 1$
$$t_{k+1} = \frac{a_{k+1}}{a_k} = 2 + \frac{a_{k-1}}{a_k} = 2 + \frac{1}{t_k}.$$
(4)

Let us denote by $\tau$ the positive root of the equation
$$\tau = 2 + \frac{1}{\tau} \Leftrightarrow \tau^2 - 2\tau - 1 = 0,$$
i.e. $\tau = \sqrt{2} + 1$. Then
$$t_{k+1} - \tau = t_{k+1} - 1 - \sqrt{2} = \frac{1}{t_k} + 1 - \sqrt{2} = \frac{1}{t_k} - \frac{1}{\tau} = \frac{\tau - t_k}{t_k\tau},$$
where
$$a_{k+1} - \tau b_{k+1} = (t_{k+1} - \tau) a_k = \frac{(\tau - t_k) a_k}{t_k\tau} = \frac{\tau b_k - a_k}{\tau} = \frac{\tau b_k - a_k}{\sqrt{2} + 1},$$
therefore
$$|a_{k+1} - \tau b_{k+1}| < |\tau b_k - a_k|/2.$$

Thus, the vertical distance from point $(b_k; a_k)$ to straight line $y = \tau x$ is more than doubled when moving from $k$ to $k+1$ (and the magnitude signs $a_k - \tau b_k$ are in inches). If the sequence $w$ were (at least starting from a certain place) periodic, and in the period there would be $p$ letters $A$ and $q$ letters $B$, then the entire constructed broken line would be contained in a certain strip, the edges of which would have a slope $p/q$. But the straight line $y = \tau x$ completely leaves this strip and moves away from it, since the number $\tau = 1 + \sqrt{2}$ is irrational and not equal to $p/q$. Thus, the points $(b_k; a_k)$ of the broken line, starting from some $k$, do not completely lie in such a strip. It follows that the sequence $w$ has no period.

This proof can be presented without the help of checkered paper and broken paper. In short, if among the first $n$ letters $w$ there are $\alpha(n)$ letters $A$ and $\beta(n)$ letters $B$, then for the periodic sequence $w$ the limit $\lim \frac{\alpha(n)}{\beta(n)}$ must equal a rational number; on the other hand, for its sequence $t_k = \alpha(s_k)/\beta(s_k) = a_k/b_k$ it follows from formula (4) that the limit can only be equal to $\sqrt{2} + 1$ (from (5) it is easy to see that this limit exists).

Note that we can discern a deeper connection between the number $\sqrt{2} + 1$ and the sequence $w$ (and the words $w_k$) from the letters $A$ and $B$. The point is that due to (4)

$$t_k = \frac{a_k}{b_k} = 2 + \frac{1}{2 + \frac{1}{2 + \dots + \frac{1}{2}}},$$

i.e. $t_k$ are the so-called “suitable fractions” of an infinite continued fraction

$$\tau = 1 + \sqrt{2} = 2 + \frac{1}{2 + \frac{1}{2 + \dots}};$$
