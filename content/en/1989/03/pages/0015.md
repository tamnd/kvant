Stable trajectories

The newly constructed periodic trajectories have one significant drawback - no matter how small the change in the angles of the triangle, they are destroyed (in the sense that there are no periodic trajectories in the deformed triangle near the original trajectory). Now we will construct a periodic trajectory in an obtuse triangle, free from this defect.

Let the acute angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of an obtuse triangle $ABC$ be related by the inequalities

$$\frac{\pi-\beta}{2} < k\alpha < \frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant (k+1)\alpha,$$

$$\frac{\pi-\alpha}{2} < l\beta < \frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant (l+1)\beta,$$

where $k$ and $l$ are some natural numbers. As in figure 10, $a$, we will make $k-1$ mirror reflections of the triangle $ABC$ around the vertex $A$ counterclockwise and $l-1$ reflections around the vertex $C$ clockwise - Figure 12, $a$.
