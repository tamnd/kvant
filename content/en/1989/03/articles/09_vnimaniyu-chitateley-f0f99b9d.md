Attention readers!
On the initiative of the magazine «Kvant» and the International Educational Network (USA) and with the support of a number of Soviet and American organizations, joint summer physics and mathematics schools are being held this year for the first time.

American schoolchildren spend 4 weeks at a Youth Camp in our country.

A group of Soviet children, which will include 15 schoolchildren from among the participants in the “Problem «Kvant»” competition, the All-Union Olympiad in Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science, and the All-Union Tournament of Young Physicists, will be hosted by Story Brook University (New York State). Due to the fact that the activities will be conducted in English, group members must undertake to know the language.

We hope that such schools will become traditional.
Participation in them is up to you.
We wish you success!
