Positives of the whole
(leafing through the book
M. Gardner...)

One boy is passionate about breeding goldfish, so he got tired of this activity and decided to sell all his fish. He implemented his decision in 5 stages:
1. I sold half of all my fish and another half of the fish.
2. I sold a third of the remaining fish and another third of the fish.
3. I sold a quarter of the remaining fish and another quarter of the fish.

4. I sold a fifth of the remaining fish and another fifth of the fish.
After that he had 19 fish left. Of course, he handled the goldfish with care and it never occurred to him to cut the fish into pieces. How many fish did the boy have at first? (Answer: 101 fish.)
