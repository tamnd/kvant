Novosibirsk State University named after. Lenin Komsomol

Matematika
Written exam
Option 1 (Faculty of Mechanics, Mathematics and Economics)
1. On the plane, find a point symmetrical to the point with coordinates (2; 4) relative to the straight line given by the equation $2x+y=3$.
2. Solve the equation
$$\log_{x+2}\log_2\log_{x+3}(11x^2+46x+48)=0$$
3. Circle $O_1$ with radius $3r$ touches the extension of side $AB$ of angle $ABC$, its center lies on side $BC$. Circle $O_2$ of radius $r$ touches the sides of angle $ABC$ and circle $O_1$. Find the angle $ABC$.
4. Solve the equation
$$\arcsin\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\pi}{2}\cos x\right)+\arccos\left(\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\pi}{2}\sin x\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}$$
5. At the base of the quadrangular paramid $SABCD$ lies the right angle $ABCD$, $AB=5$, $BC=2$. It is known that $SB=4$, $SA=3$, $SC=x$, $SD=y$. At what values ​​of $x$ and $y$ is the volume of the paramid the greatest, what is this volume equal to?
Option 2 (faculties of natural sciences and geology-geophysical)
1. A parabola with vertex on axis $Ox$ is tangent to the line passing through points $A(-1, -1)$ and $B(4, 4)$ at point $A$. Find the equation of the parabola.
2. Solve the equation
$$4\sqrt{2}\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\cos 2x-2\sin^2 x=\sin 2x$$
3. A circle is circumscribed around a right triangle $ABC$ with brass knuckles $AC=5$, $BC=12$. Points $E$, $G$ are the midpoints of smaller arcs $AC$, $BC$ of this circle, points $F$ are the midpoint of arcs $AB$, which does not contain the point $C$. Find the area of ​​the quadrilateral $AEFG$.
4. Solve the equation
$$\log_{x+2}\log_2\log_{x+3}(11x^2+46x+48)=0$$
5. All edges of a regular triangular prism $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ with base $ABC$ are equal to 1. Prism $AKLA_2K_2L_2$ with side edges $AA_2$, $KK_2$, $LL_2$ is symmetry to prism $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ relative to point $A$. Point $E$ belongs to the segment $AK$, $AE:EK=1:3$, point $F$ belongs to the segment $K_2L_2$, $K_2F:FL_2=3:5$. Find the length of the segment along which straight line $EF$ intersects prism $ABCA_1B_1C_1$.

Option 3 (faculties of mechanics, mathematics and economics)
1. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 7^{2x}+4^{2y+1}=85, \\ 7^x-4^y=5. \end{cases}$$
2. Find all the roots of the equation
$$\frac{\sin 2x-0,8\cos x+1}{\sin \frac{x}{2}}=5\cos \frac{x}{2},$$
lying on the segment $\left[\frac{5\pi}{6}; 2\pi\right]$.
3. In an obtuse triangle $ABC$ of area $24\sqrt{5}$ the medians $AN$, $CM$ intersect at angle arccos $\frac{2}{3}$. Find the sides of the triangle if $AN-CM=3$.
4. For what values ​​of the parameter $a$ the equation
$$ax^2+3x+2a^2-3=0$$
has only integer solutions?
5. At the base of a regular triangular prism $ABCA_1B_1C_1$ lies an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with sides 2, the side edges $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $CC_1$ are equal to 1. Point $K$ is the middle of edge $AB$, point $L$ is the middle of edge $B_1C_1$, point $M$ is the middle of edge $A_1B_1$, point $N$ is the middle of edge $AC$. Parallel planes are drawn through straight lines $KL$, $MN$. Find the volume of the part of the prism containing between these planes.

Option 4 (Physics Department)
1. Solve the equation
$$\text{ctg}x+\frac{\text{ctg}^2x}{3}+\frac{\text{ctg}^3x}{9}+\frac{\text{ctg}^4x}{27}=0.$$
2. Solve the inequality
$$\left(2^{x+1}-3^{x+1}\right)\sqrt{2^{2x}-2^{x+3}\cdot3^x+11\cdot3^{2x}} \geq 0.$$
3. In a parallel triangle $ABCD$, points $M$ are the midpoint of side $AB$. It is known that the bisector of angle $C$ divides the area of ​​triangle $AMD$ in half. Find the length of side $AD$ if $CD=4$.
4. Find all solutions to the system of inequalities
$$\begin{cases} x^2-6x+6y \leq 0, \\ y^2-2xy+9 \leq 0. \end{cases}$$
5. At the base of the quadrangular prism $ABCDA_1B_1C_1D_1$ lies a rhombus $ABCD$ with sides 5 and diagonal $AC=8$. The edges $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $DD_1$ are shuffled and touch the plane $ABCD$ at point $C$. Find the radius of the ball.
