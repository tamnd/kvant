Option 2

1. A ball suspended on a thread swings in a vertical plane so that its accelerations in the extreme and lowest positions are modulo equal to each other. Find the angle of deflection of the thread in the extreme position.

2. A material point moves in such a way that its coordinates change over time according to the law: $x = a \sin \omega t, y = a \cos 2\omega t$. Find the equation for the trajectory of a point and the law of change in its velocity modulus over time.

3. Assuming that air (M=29 kg/kmol) consists mainly of oxygen ($M_1 = 32$ kg/kmol) and nitrogen ($M_2 = 28$ kg/kmol), determine the percentage of these gases in the atmosphere.

4. In a U-tube, the air column height in the sealed left leg is $L = 300$ mm, and the excess path column height in the open right leg is $h = 110$ mm (Fig. 3). There should be a column of mercury in the right knee that its level has risen by $\Delta h = 40$ mm. How much did the level in the left knee rise? Atmospheric pressure $p_a = 760$ mm pt. Art.

5. A system of 4 resistors with a resistance of $R = 1$ Ohm each is connected to a current source with EMF $\mathcal{E} = 10$ V and internal resistance $r = 1$ Ohm. How should these resistors be connected so that maximum power is released into them?

6. The object is at a distance $L$ from the screen. Between them there is a proper line with a focal length $F$. By moving the line, you can get both an enlarged and a reduced image on the screen. Find the ratio of image sizes in general cases.

$$\frac{1}{2x+1} - \frac{2}{3x+2} \leq \frac{4}{5x+4} - \frac{3}{4x+3}.$$

3. Solve the equation
$(\sin 2x - \cos 2x)^2 + \sin 6x = 1.$
4. Solve the equation
$$x^{\log_3 x} - 1 + \log_{1/3} x = x^{3 - \log_3 x}.$$

5. In an equal trapezoid there are two tangent circles of radii $R$, each of which touches the sides and one of the sides, and the centers of the circles lie on the diagonals. Find the sides of the trapezoid.

$$\sqrt{x-1} + \sqrt{5-x} = x^2 - 6x + 7.$$
