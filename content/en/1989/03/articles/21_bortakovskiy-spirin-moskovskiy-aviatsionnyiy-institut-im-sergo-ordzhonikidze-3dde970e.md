Moscow Aviation Institute named after. Sergo Ordzhonikidze

Mathematics
Written exam
Option 1
1. The sum of the heel of the initial terms of an arithmetic progression is less than the sum of its subsequent heels of the terms by 50. How much is the tenth term of the progression greater than its second term?
2. Find the critical points of the function
$$f(x) = \sin 2x + 2 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - x \right).$$
3. Solve the inequality
$$\sqrt{\lg x} + \sqrt{25 - \lg x} \leq 7.$$
4. The area of ​​a right triangle is $P$, and the area of ​​a circle inscribed in it is $Q$. Find the area of ​​the circle circumscribed about this triangle.
5. Solve the system of equations
$$\begin{cases} 2x^2 + xy = 1, \\ \frac{9x^2}{2(1-x)^4} = 1 + \frac{3xy}{2(1-x)^2}. \end{cases}$$
6. In a right triangular prism $ABCA'B'C'$, a section is drawn through points $B, C$ and $A'$, the area of ​​which is $S$, and the distance from the section plane to the vertex $B'$ is $h$. Find the volume of the prism.

Option 2
1. Solve the equation
$$\cos \left( x - \frac{\pi}{3} \right) + \cos^2 2x = 1 - \cos^2 \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - 2x \right).$$
2. Find the ratio of the third term of a decreasing geometric progression to its fifteenth term if the sum of the two twelve terms of this progression, starting with the thirteenth, is 40% of the sum of its initial two twelve terms.
3. Find the domain of a function
$$f(x) = \sqrt{\log_{3x+1} \frac{x-2}{x-4}}.$$
4. What is the maximum volume of a regular quadrangular paramida whose lateral edge has length 1?

5. Find all solutions of the system
$$\begin{cases} 5x^2 + 8xy + 4y^2 = 4 + 4x, \\ \sin^2(\pi x) + \sin^2(\pi y) = 0. \end{cases}$$
6. Points $O(0; 0), A(0; 1), B(2; 0), C(4; 0)$ are given on the coordinate plane. Find the coordinates $(x; y)$ of point $M$ such that angle OMA is equal to angle CMB and angle MAO is equal to angle MBC.

Written exam
Option 1
1. A thread is wound around the cylinder, the end of which is fixed to a stand at the top point of the inclined plane (Fig. 1). The coefficient of friction between the cylinder and the plane is $\mu$. At what maximum value of angle $\alpha$ will the cylinder not roll down the inclined plane?
2. A ball of mass $m$ is suspended by two springs, the stiffnesses of which are $k_1$ and $k_2$ (Fig. 2). Find the frequency of the ball's vibrations. Will the frequency change if the springs are interchanged?
3. In a vessel with water, the volume of which is $V_1 = 0,25$ l, and the temperature is $t_1 = 20^\circ\text{C}$, $m_2 = 50$ g of molten lead having a temperature of $t_2 = 400^\circ\text{C}$ was placed. What temperature will be established as a result of the heat transfer? What part of the water remains in the vessel? The specific heat of water is $c_1 = 4,2 \cdot 10^3\text{Дж/(кг}\cdot\text{К)}$, the melting point of lead is $t_{\pi\pi} = 327^\circ\text{C}$, its specific temperature is $\lambda = 0,226 \cdot 10^5\text{Дж/кг}$, the specific heats of melts and solid lead are considered the same and equal to $c_2 = 1,3 \times 10^2\text{Дж/(кг}\cdot\text{К)}$. Neglect heat loss.
4. A particle with charge $q$ will accelerate to energy $W$ and flies into a flat capacitor parallel to its plates. The charge of the capacitor is $Q$, its capacity is $C$, the distance between the plates is $d$. Initially, the particle is at the same distance from the plates. How long should each plate be so that particles do not fall on its surface?

Estimate the exposure time if the athletes finish at a speed of about $v = 10$ m/s.
