Teatr shadow

What is the shape of the shadow cast on a flat screen by a circular hoop that is illuminated by a precise light source?
It depends on the relative position of the hoop, screen and light source. For example, if the entire hoop is located closer to the screen than the light source, then it will be elliptical (figure 1).

If one point of the hoop is at the same distance from the screen as the light source, and the rest of the hoop is close, then the dark is a parabola (figure 2). If one part of the hoop is closer to the screen than the light source, and the other part is further away (figure 3), then the darkness will be hyperbolic. (Fig. 3.)
