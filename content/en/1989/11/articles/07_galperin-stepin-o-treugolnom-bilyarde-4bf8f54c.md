About triangular billiards

In this note, supplementing the article by G. A. Galperin and A. M. Stepin “Periodic movements of a billiard ball” («Kvant» No. 3, 1989), we will describe some more techniques for constructing periodic trajectories in triangular billiards, in particular, we will solve problem M1155. Let us recall its conditions.

M1155. A point moves in a triangle, reflecting from its side according to the law “the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.” Periodic is a trajectory of this point that is a closed broken line and does not pass through the vertices of the triangle.
a) Prove that no triangle with different sides contains a four-link periodic trajectory.

Is there an acute triangle with a periodic trajectory 6) of 5 links? c) from 7 links?

Statement a) of this problem can be proved by contradiction. Suppose that in a certain triangle there is a four-link periodic trajectory. Then some two of its four break points A, B, C, D are on one side of the triangle, and the other two do not lie on this side. The described construction can be generalized. Let's start with an auxiliary triangle $\Delta_0$ with a base $AC$ and acute angles $\alpha$, $\beta$ at the base, for which a chain of triangles is constructed
$$\Delta - 2l, \Delta - 1, \Delta_0, \Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_{2k},
$$
intersecting the horizontal line $AC$ and the upper half-plane, and the neighboring triangles of the chain have a common side and symmetries with respect to it. Since the result of applying two consecutive segments with respect to non-parallel axes is a rotation, then the triangle $\Delta - 2l$ is rotated relative to $\Delta_0$ by some angle $n_1\alpha + n_2\beta + n_3\pi$; similarly, triangle $\Delta_2k$ is rotated relative to triangle $\Delta_0$ by angle $m_1\alpha + m_2\beta + m_3\pi$. Suppose that the pairs of chain numbers ($n_1, n_2$) and ($m_1, m_2$) are disproportionate, the triangles $\Delta - 2l$ and $\Delta_2k$ have sides perpendicular to the line $AC$ and there are no other vertices of the chain triangles on this line except $A$ and $C$. Then it turns out that by no matter how small a change in the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ one can obtain triangles $ABC$ with stable periodic trajectories. In particular, if $\Delta_0$ is a right angle triangle with $\alpha = \pi/8$, $\beta = 3\pi/8$, then triangles are obtained from distance notation, and for $\alpha = \pi/2k$, $\beta = \pi/2l$ - triangles are obtained from stata in «Kvant» No. 3 (see Fig. 12 in this article).
