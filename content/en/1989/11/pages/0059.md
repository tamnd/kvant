Parquet made of quadrangles

Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. G. BOLTYANSKY

At the VI Moscow Mathematical Olympiad (in 1940), the following task was proposed: to lay a parquet with a given quadrilateral of any shape, that is, to fill the entire plane without gaps and overlapped. Its solution can be obtained using central symmetry. Let us reflect the quadrilateral ABCD symmetrically about the middle of side AB. On figure 1 the original quadrilateral is placed with the number 1, and the symmetric one with the number 2. Now we reflect quadrilateral 2 symmetrically relative to the middle of its side BC, and the resulting quadrilateral 3 - relative to the middle of its side CD. Quadrilaterals 1, 2, 3, 4 are adjacent to their common vertex by angles A, B, C, D. And since the sum of the angles of a quadrilateral is 360°, then these four quadrilaterals will be located around their common vertex without gaps and overlapped. The same construction can be carried out around each vertex of each of its new quadrangles, which gives parquet on the entire plane (Fig. 2).
