⟦folio 34⟧

Tasks

1. Three pecks Bim, Bom and Bam enter the arena in red, green and blue shirts, respectively. Their shoes were the same three colors. Bim's shirt and shoes had the same colors. Bom's shoes and shirt were not red. Bam was wearing green shoes and a different color shirt. How were the kluuns dressed?

2. Find two such prime numbers whose sum and difference are also prime numbers.

3. Fill the rectangle on figure with numbers so that each horizontal row contains a four-digit number divisible by 92, and each vertical row contains a three-digit number also divisible by 92.

4. In this simple figure puzzle, nine different letters correspond to nine different numbers. Which?

5. How many parts are the space of the plane on which the faces of the trihedral paramid lie divided?

These tasks were suggested to us by V. D. Vyun, N. K. Antonovich, A. V. Shvetsov, L. P. Mochalov and A. P. Savin.
