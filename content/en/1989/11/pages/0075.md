M2. There is a game cube on the table with side $i_0$ towards us, side $j_0$ up. Write a program that determines the sequence of “turnings” of the cube (forward, backward, right, left), after which the cube ends up in the same place, but with the $i_k$ face towards us and the $j_k$ face up.

Application: turning is understood as rolling a cube over an edge in contact with the table without sliding. Other methods of moving the cube are prohibited.

The numbering of the faces of the cube is such that if you put it on the face with the number “5”, then the side faces will have the numbers “1”, “6”, “4”, “3” when going around clockwise, and the top will have the number “2”.
