⟦folio 72⟧

The II All-Russian Information Olympiad for schoolchildren was held from April 15 to April 22, 1989. 82 schoolchildren who won in the previous rounds of the Olympiad or in the 1st All-Union Olympiad arrived in the capital of Belarus, Minsk, as part of a 19th team (from the union republics, the Ministry of Railways, Moscow, Leningrad, Minsk).

The opening ceremony of the Olympiad took place at the Republican Boarding School for Music and Fine Arts named after. I. O. Akhremchik, which became the main base of the Olympiad. The Jorri Olympiad was headed by Academician of the Academy of Sciences of the BSSR V. A. Labunov. On the same day, the Olympiad participants began to get acquainted with computer technology. Minsk residents provided an available number of Yamahas and EC1840s. Most schoolchildren went to Yamaha, but some preferred the more powerful EC1840 personal and professional computers.

The first round of the Olympiad (theoretical) took place on April 17, the second (practical - on a computer) - on April 19. Unlike the first All-Olympiad, when evaluating problems, a coefficient was not introduced to characterize the difficulty of the problem (depending on the number of participants who solved this problem). The solution to each problem was scored between 0 and 10 points, and up to 5 points were additionally awarded for special achievements. Let us present the conditions of the problems.
