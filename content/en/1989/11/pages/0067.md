XXIII National Olympiad in Mathematics

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
V. V. VAVILOV,
Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. V. REZNICHENKO

In the final stage of the XXIII All-Union Mathematical Olympiad, held from April 15 to April 22, 1989 in Riga, 30 teams took part: 14 allied resource teams, one team from each of the four zones of the RSFSR, teams from Moscow, Leningrad, Riga and the Riga region and 8 teams of specialized boarding schools at the leading universities of the country - 164 schoolchildren (48 eighth-graders, 62 ninth-graders and 54 tenth-graders).

As usual, the Olympiad participant was given 4 problems in each of the two rounds, which were given 5 hours to solve. The assessment of the complexity of the proposed tasks in points is given in Table 1.

In Table 1, scores characterize the relative complexity of tasks within one class. In general, as the test results allow us to conclude, the task for eighth-graders turned out to be too difficult; The assignments for grades 9 and 10 turned out to be well balanced both in complexity and topic; the students liked them, as evidenced by the test results. How the participants coped with the tasks of the Olympiad can be found in Table 2, which shows the number of schoolchildren who solved each problem.

At the end of the inspection, each Olympiad participant could familiarize himself with his work and discuss with the jury members his mistakes, assessment, and performance of the work by the inspectors. At the same time, sometimes the evaluation of the work changed. Table 3 shows data on the number of winners in each class.
