⟦folio 66⟧

Computer tourer.

traditional meeting of schoolchildren with members of the editorial board of the magazine «Kvant», a mathematical battle between teams of schoolchildren and zhorny. For the first time, a computer tour was held, in which 112 schoolchildren took part - everyone who wanted to.

The work of the Olympics was covered by the local press, radio and television. The cultural program of the Olympics included excursions to Rita, to the cities of Jurmala, Jelgava and Bauska, to the Aji agricultural company, to the Ethnographic Museum, and to Rundāle Castle.

In general, the Olympiad was held at a high scientific and methodological level and was well organized. This was greatly facilitated by the high qualifications of its organizers from Latvia, in particular, the precise work of the Organizing Committee for the preparation of the final stage of the Olympics, headed by the Chairman of the First Deputy Minister of Public Education of the Latvian USSR, Comrade Kubuli-

Nya B. A. I would like to say words of gratitude to everyone who helped organize and conduct the 89 Olympics, in particular, the sponsor of the Olympics, the integrated cooperative “Inter”.

Here are the problems for the final round.

First day
8th grade
1. Each of the seven boys went to the ice cream book 3 times on Sunday. It is known that every two of them met near the knisk. Prove that at some point at least three boys met near the knisk at the same time.

A. Anjans
2. There are 77 straight bars measuring 3x3x1. Can all these bars be placed in a 7x9x11 square box (box with lid)?

A. Berzinysh
3. Let M be the tangency point of the circle inscribed in triangle ABC on side AB, T be an arbitrary point on side BC other than the vertex. Prove that three circles inscribed in triangles BMT, MTA and ATC are tangent to one straight line.

A. Anjans
4. The natural number N has exactly 12 divisors (including 1 and N). Let's put them in ascending order: $d_1 < d_2 < ... < d_{12}$. It is known that the divisor with number $d_4$ - 1 is equal to the product $(d_1 + d_2 + d_4)d_8$. Find the number N.

A. Berzinysh
9th grade
1. There are 2000 coins, of which two are counterfeit: one is lighter than the real one, and the other is heavier. How can you determine, after repeated weighings on a scale without weights, which is greater: the total weight of two counterfeit coins or the total weight of two real coins; or are these weights equal?

S. Avgustovich
2. Prove that if a, b, c are side lengths of a triangle and a+b+c=1, then the inequality is true
$$a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 4abc < \frac{1}{2}.$$

D. Tereshin
3. Points K and M are taken on sides AB and CD of a convex quadrilateral ABCD, respectively. Let L be the intersection point of the segments AM and KD, N the intersection point of the segments KC and BM.

a) Prove that if K and M are the midpoints of AB and CD, then $S_{KLMN} < \frac{1}{3} S_{ABCD}$.

6) Prove that if AK:KB=CM:MD=m:n, then
$$S_{KLMN} < \frac{mn}{m^2 + mn + n^2} S_{ABCD}.$$

D. Tereshin
