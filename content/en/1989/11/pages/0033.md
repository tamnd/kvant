Having now reflected symmetrically with respect to the straight line $BC$ the part of the three-vein trajectory $H_1H_2H_3$ lying outside the triangle $ABC$, we obtain a 5-bar periodic trajectory in $\triangle ABC$. Another 5-link periodic trajectory is obtained in the same way, if you first make a mirror image of the triangle $ABC$ relative to side $AC$.

Similarly, an acute triangle $ABC$ is constructed, containing a 7-link periodic trajectory (task $\theta$): if $\angle B < \pi/6$, then it is necessary to make two mirror reflections of the triangle $ABC$ around the vertex $B$ counterclockwise, extending the side $BA''$ of the resulting figure $\Phi = BAA'A''$ to the intersection at the point $C'$ with the continuation of the side $AC$ and construct a three-vein periodic trajectory $H_1H_2H_3$ in the acute triangle $ABC'$, entirely located in the figure $\Phi$ (Fig. 3, 6) (Belonging of the trajectory $H_1H_2H_3$ to the figure $\Phi$ is easy to prove - we leave this to the reader.) Then folding the figure $\Phi$ into a three-layer accordion, i.e., making successive inverse reflections of mirror-symmetric triangles together with the parts of the trajectory $H_1H_2H_3$ located in them, we obtain a 7-link periodic trajectory in $\triangle ABC$.
