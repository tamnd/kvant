Problem 3. Prove that if the midline connecting the midpoints of two opposite sides of a quadrilateral is equal to half the sum of the other two opposite sides, then this quadrilateral is a trapezoid (or parallelogram).

Solution. On figure 6 $MN$ and $NP$ are the middle lines; the segments $AQ$ and $MP$ are equal and parallel (since $AM$ and $QP$ are equal and parallel). If $MN = \frac{1}{2}(AB+CD)$, i.e. $AQ=MP = AB+BQ$, then the point $B$ belongs to the segment $AQ$. Therefore, $AB \parallel CD$, i.e. $ABCD$, is a trapezoid.
