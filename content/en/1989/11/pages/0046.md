Symmetry and physical properties of crystals

Once upon a time, people associated the harmony of the world with symmetry. The ancient Greeks, who valued beauty most of all in life, attached exceptional importance to it. Considering beauty to be a criterion of truth, they felt a certain hidden meaning in symmetry and perceived it as the beginning that determines the properties of the world. A celebration of the properties of the world. Celebration, however, beauty has lost its position and has become the property of only artists and poses. Interest in symmetry was also lost.

But new times have come. A new physics was formed, and it became clear that the Greeks were absolutely right. The properties of the real world are to a large extent determined by its symmetry. (We have already written about the connection between the symmetry of space and time and the laws of conservation of momentum and energy. See, for example, «Kvant», 1988, No. 5, p. 45.)

But what is this - symmetry? Let's explain with a simple example. Let's say you hold some body in your hands and return it both this way and here. And suddenly you notice that at some turn the body aligns with itself. Then they say that the body is symmetrical with respect to this rotation.
