⟦folio 58⟧

Quadrangles of different colors - central symmetry. Note that the original quadrilateral can also be non-convex—a parquet is obtained in this case as well (Fig. 4).

The constructed parquet floors make it possible to solve a number of problems related to the properties of quadrangles. The solution to each of the problems considered below can be presented without using parquet: consider only those quadrilaterals that are needed for the solution, and the remaining “traces” are parquetasters. However, our solutions are using parquet.

Task 1. Two identical convex quadrangles were made from paper. One was cut along one diagonal, the second - along the other. Prove that the four triangles obtained can be used to form a parallelogram.

The solution to this problem becomes very difficult when smoothing out the parquet shown on the first page of the cover.
