Tasks

1. According to the contract, the employee is paid 48 francs for each day worked, and 12 francs are charged from him for each day not worked. After 30 days, the employee learned that nothing had been accepted for him. How many days does the employee work during these 30 days?

2. Lyudmila is 6 times younger than her grandfather; if you put 0 between the numbers of her age, you get the age of her predecessor. How old is she?

3. A three-digit number is 5 times the product of its digits. What number is this?

4. Solve the arithmetic puzzle you see at figure. It is known that a COW is larger than a MILKmaid. As usual, the same letters correspond to the same numbers, and different numbers correspond to different ones.

5. Opposite sides of a convex hexagon are equal and parallel. Taking three vertices of the hexagon through one, we get a red triangle (see figure). Show that the area of ​​this triangle is half the area of ​​the hexagon.

Problem No. 1 is the problem of the great French mathematician of the 18th century E. Bezou; the remaining problems were suggested to us by the student of 105 schools in Odessa V. N. Rusanov, G. L. Pelts, M. V. Volkov, V. V. Proizvolov.
