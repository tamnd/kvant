Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences
S. K. VETYAEV

7. Kelvin-Helmholtz stability*)

Hydrodynamic stability, which was already discussed in the first part of the article, is complex and multifaceted. Another amazing example on this topic is the stability of a surface separating two different liquids. From different sides of this surface, the tangential components of the fluid velocities are different,

*) The beginning of this article was published in the magazine’s recollection for this year.

they seem to slide relative to each other. The stability of a sliding surface is represented by the famous German natural physicist Kelvin (W. Thomson, 1824-1907).

There are three stages of stability: an increase in sinusodal disturbances, the formation and evolution of a spiral structure, and a random decay process. Well-stability manifests itself when the influence of stabilizing factors is insignificant. These include stable tension and positive development in the density of the lower and upper layers of the liquid, when the force of gravity acts in a direction perpendicular to the rupture surface.
