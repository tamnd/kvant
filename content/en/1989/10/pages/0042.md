Are you familiar with thermodynamics?

Questions and tasks

1. What are the principles of heat balance control, based on the first law of thermodynamics?

2. Hot water is poured into an aluminum mug, the mass of which is equal to the mass of water. Did the water and the mug heat up to the same degree of cooling?

3. Why does the saw heat up to a higher temperature than the wood when sawing wood?

4. Does adding cold milk to it noticeably change the temperature of the coffee? Assume the specific heats of coffee and milk are approximately equal.

5. Why are medical heating pads filled with hot water and not hot air?

6. From the same height there are two bodies with the same initial temperature, one is copper, the other is iron. Which of them heated up more during the absciliton-inelastic collision? Neglect air resistance.

7. Two identical iron balls, one of which lies on a horizontal surface, the other suspended on a non-stretchable thread, are heated from 20 to 100 °C. How much warmth is required for this? Neglect the heat exchange between the balls and the medium.

8. Sometimes a gas, when cooled, gives off less heat than was expended when heating it. Doesn't this contradict the law of conservation of energy?

9. Is it possible to transfer a certain amount of heat to a body without causing an increase in its temperature?
