F1194. The capillary is made of two thin glass tubes with internal diameters $d_1$ and $d_2$ (Fig. 2). Introduce a large drop of water with a mass of $M$ into it. When the capillary was placed horizontally, the entire drop “crept” into the thin part, and when it was placed vertically, all the water flowed out of it. At what angles between the axis of the capillary and the vertical will the drop be located partly in a thick and partly in a thin tube? Water surface tension coefficient $\sigma$, water density $\rho$. Wetting is considered complete.

R. A. Tillo

F1195. A vertical pipe with a height of $H=1$ m and a cross-sectional area of ​​$S=50$ cm$^2$ is open at both ends. A heating element with a power of $N=100$ W is installed in the lower part of the pipe. What speed of the upward flow will be established in the pipe? Consider that the heater does not pollute the cross-section of the pipe. Atmospheric pressure $p_0=1$ atm, outside temperature is room temperature. The molar heat capacity of air at a constant volume is $C_V=2,5$ R, where $R$ is the universal gas constant.

M. M. Tsypin

F1196. The operating voltage of the light bulb, the current-voltage characteristic of which is shown in figure 3, is 3.5 V (the curve reverses at a voltage of 4 V - the light bulb burns out). Two such light bulbs are connected in series and connected to a source with a voltage of 5 V. How much current will the circuit consume? What resistor should be connected in parallel with one of the bulbs so that the voltage on the other is 3.5 V? Will any of the light bulbs burn out if this resistor is replaced with another one of the same light bulb?

A. R. Zilberman

F1197. When studying the electrical properties of a thin plate of the new compound, it was discovered that its conductivity is significantly anisotropic: along one direction (the $X$ axis) it is maximum, and along the perpendicular direction (the $Y$ axis) it is minimal. A sample was cut out of the plate in the form of a strip of length $L$ and width $d$ ($L \gg d$) at an angle $\alpha=45^\circ$ to the axes $X$ and $Y$ and a source with voltage $U_0$ (Fig. 4) was connected to the ends of the strip. By measuring the voltage between the edges of the strip in the transverse direction, we obtained the value $U$ ($U \ll U_0$). Determine the conductivity ratio along the $X$ and $Y$ axes.

A. I. Buzdin
