Obitah TUV
1988/89

During the spring school holidays from March 24 to April 2, 1989, the final of the XI Moscow Tour of Young Physicists, as well as the II Spring and International Tour of Young Physicists were held in Moscow at the Olymppets Youth Center under the Komsomol Central Committee.

The winner of the Moscow tour was the team of Physics School No. 542 at MEPhI, the second place was taken by Physics School No. 18 at Moscow State University and Northern Shore. No. 710, third - north latitude. No. 679, northern latitude No. 1 in Fryazino and the High School of Physical Education at Moscow State University.

The performances of the finalists of the Moscow Tour became indicative for the participants of the Spring and International Tours, and their active work in transferring the experience of holding Tours largely predetermined the success of the entire tour campaign this year. It must be said that in organizational terms, holding the 2nd Spring and International Tours was not an easy task. Indeed, this time Olympets brought together 38 teams of schoolchildren from almost all allied resources, many cities and towns of the country, as well as teams from Bulgaria, Hungary, Germany, the Netherlands, Poland and Czechoslovakia.

In 8 working days of Tourir, it was necessary to conduct the Spring Tour, individual selection of candidates for the USSR national team, form two USSR teams for the participant in the International.type, include foreign delegations in the work, and finally hold the International Tour. At the same time, it was necessary to take into account the varying degrees of preparedness of the team, the development of the level of understanding of the goals and objectives of Turir. But the good thing about TUF is that all the difficulties are predetermined by the organizers and participants of the Tour in their joint work. Of course, it was difficult for many, especially for newcomers to Turir - the team from Drochia, Belgorod, Kaliningrad, Stavropol, Kazan, Mishko of the Bashkir Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic, Minsk and Alma-Ata, but they also showed perseverance and hard work and put up worthy resistance to more experienced teams.
