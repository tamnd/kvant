V All-Union Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren

The Organizing Committee of the All-Union Scientific and Technical Conference of Shkolnikov announces the acceptance of works for the V HTKSh, which took place in April 1990 at MIPT. Any Schoolchild (or group of Schoolchildren) in grades 9-11 can take part in the conference. To do this, you need to consider any physical or mathematical question, or conduct an experiment, or write a computer program. The Organizing Committee encourages independent research by schoolchildren.

The work is drawn up in the form of an abstract (7-25, typewritten pages if possible), one copy of which must be sent no later than February 1, 1990 to the address: 141700 Dolgoprudny, Moscow region, MIPT, Organizing Committee of the V Scientific and Technical Conference of Schoolchildren.

All submitted abstracts are decided by specialties, the authors of the best works receive invitations to speak at HTKSH. The abstract text will not be returned.

Additional information can be obtained by calling ZFTSH at MIPT: 408-51-45.

Member of the HTKSH organizing committee
A. V. Sapozhnikov
