Selected School Problems

Eighth grade
1. Prove the inequality
$$\sqrt[5]{2} + 7 < \sqrt[9]{2}.$$
2. Solve the system of equations
$$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y+z} = \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{x+z} = \frac{1}{3},$$
$$\frac{1}{z} + \frac{1}{x+y} = \frac{1}{4}.$$
3. Let $m, n$ be natural numbers, and let $m$ be odd. Prove that the numbers $2^m - 1$ and $2^n + 1$ are coprime.
4. Using only a ruler, draw a perpendicular from a point on a circle, given the diameter of that circle.
5. Let $M$ be the intersection point of the bisectorics of interior angle $B$ and exterior angle $C$ of triangle $ABC$, and $N$ be the intersection point of bisectors of interior angle $B$ and interior angle $C$. Prove that the midpoint of the segment $MN$ lies on the circumcircle of the triangle.

Ninth grade
6. The integer $a$ has the property that the number $3a$ can be represented as $x^2 + 2y^2$, where $x$ and $y$ are integers. Prove that the number $a$ can also be represented in this form.

7. Find all triples of natural numbers $x, y, z$ such that the equality $x^n + y^n = z^{n+1}$ holds for all $n \geqslant 1989$.
8. Let $a, b, c$ be the sides of the triangle, $p$ its half-perimeter, $r$ the radius of the inscribed circle. Prove that
$$\frac{1}{(p-a)^2} + \frac{1}{(p-b)^2} + \frac{1}{(p-c)^2} \geqslant \frac{1}{r^2}.$$
9. In triangle $ABC$, medians $AD$ and $BE$ are mutually perpendicular. Prove that stg $\angle A + \text{ctg} \angle B \geqslant 2/3$.
10. Point $O$ is the center of parallelogram $ABCD$, in which $AB < BC$. On sides $AB$ and $BC$, equilateral triangles $ABE$ and $BCF$ are built to the outside. Prove that $EO < FO$.

Tenth class
11. Let $p$ be a prime number greater than 2. Find the sum of the remainders when dividing the numbers $1^p, 2^p, \dots, (p-1)^p$ by $p^2$.
12. Solve the equation $(x+1)(y^2-x^2-4)=x^2$ in integers.
13. Positive numbers $x, y, z, a, b, c$ are such that $x^2+xy+y^2=a^2, y^2+yz+z^2=b^2, z^2+zx+x^2=c^2$. Calculate $xy+yz+zx$.
14. In the quadrangular paramid $PABCD$ the lateral edges $PA, PB, PC, PD$ are equal. Prove that the sum of two angles at edges $PA$ and $PC$ is equal to the sum of two angles at edges $PB$ and $PD$.
15. Solve the equation
$$\arcsin \frac{x^2-8}{8} = 2\arcsin \frac{x}{4} - \frac{\pi}{2}.$$
