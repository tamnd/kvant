A new example at the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School

At the All-Union Correspondence Mathematical School of the USSR Academy of Pedagogical Sciences at Moscow University. M.V. Lomonosov accepts for inductive training students from rural classes of general education schools and vocational schools, with the exception of those living in Moscow and Leningrad.

The purpose of VZMS is to tell your students about many interesting things related to the school mathematics course, to teach them to solve a wide variety of problems, to teach them to work independently with a book and to competently, clearly and concisely express their thoughts on paper. All successful graduates of the VZMS (including branches and “Collective Student” groups) are issued appropriate certificates.
