⟦folio 41⟧

1. Can the sum 1 + 2 + ... + n, for some n, end in 1989?
2. While making confetti, Vasya cut a sheet of paper into 4 parts, some of the resulting pieces he again cut into 4 parts, etc. Can there be 1989 pieces at some point?
3. The sides of the correctly 1989-gon unbounded continued, as a result of which the plane was divided into many parts. Are these parts more than a million or less?
4. In 1989 I turn one hundred years old, the sum total of the numbers of the year of my birth. What year was I born?
5. Solve the arithmetic puzzle:
   XYZT
   + XYZ
   X
1989
6. A rectangular box, horizontal and vertical, was divided into 1989 cells with planks, and balls (possibly more than one) were placed in some of the cells. Is it possible to do this so that there are 300 balls in each horizontal row, and 100 balls in each vertical row?
7. Seven nines were written in a row: 9999999. Place the nimus between them so that the best expression equals 1989.
8. The triangle was cut into two polygons using a lightning cut, one of the resulting polygons was again cut into two, etc. What is the smallest number of cuts that should be made so that the total number of vertices of the resulting polygons is 1989?
9. What digit does the number 1989 end with?
