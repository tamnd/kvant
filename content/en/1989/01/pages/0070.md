⟦folio 68⟧

Introductory task
Physics

1. Two ships are moving towards each other. The speed of one ship is $v_1$, the other is $v_2$. When the distance between the ships becomes $s$, a dove takes off from one of the ships and flies to the other ship. Accessible by him, he turns sharply and flies back, etc. The pigeon flies between ships at a practical constant speed of $v$. How far will he fly until the ships meet?

2. A pedestrian walked from one city to another. When he had walked 27 km, an automatic vehicle drove out to everyone, having a speed 10 times faster than a pedestrian. They reached the second city at the same time. What is the distance between cities?

3. 4 liters of water were poured into a cylindrical vessel with a bottom area of ​​200 cm² and a height of 30 cm. A rod with a cross-section of 100 cm², the height of which is equal to the height of the vessel, is lowered into the vessel. What minimum mass must the rod have in order for it to sink to the bottom of the vessel?

4. A piece of ice with volume $V$ floats into a vessel with water, inside of which there is a piece of lead with volume $v$. How will the water level in the vessel change as the ice grows? The density of water is $1 \text{ г/cm}^3$, ice - 0.9 g/cm³, lead - 11.3 g/cm³.

5. A thermos contains equal masses of water and ice at a temperature of 0 °C. Water is poured into a thermos, the mass of which is equal to the total mass of water and ice initially found in the thermos, and the temperature is 49.9 °C. What temperature is set in the thermos? The specific heat of water is 4200 J/(kg °C), the specific heat of melting of ice is 335 kJ/kg.

6. When making ice in the refrigerator, it took 5 minutes to cool the water from 4°C to 0°C and another 1 hour 40 minutes to turn it into ice. Define

from these data, the specific heat of fusion of ice. The specific heat of water is 4200 J/(kg °C).

7. Two balls are thrown simultaneously at each other at the same speed: one vertically upward with the surfaces of the earth, the other vertically downward from a height of $H$. Find these speeds if it is known that by the time of the meeting one of the balls has traveled the distance $H/3$.

8. The radius of the Sun is 108 times the radius of the Earth, and the density of the Sun is 0.25 the density of the Earth. Find the acceleration of gravity at the surface of the Sun.

9. An electric kettle has two windings. When one winding is turned on, the kettle boils after 10 minutes, when the other winding is turned on, after 15 minutes. How long will it take the kettle to boil if the windings are turned on: a) in parallel? b) sequentially?

10. The body slides down an inclined plane with an angle of inclination of 30°, and then along a horizontal plane. Having passed along a horizontal plane a distance equal to the length of the inclined plane, the body stopped. Find the coefficient of friction.

11. An atom at rest breaks up into two parts, the masses of which are $m_1$ and $m_2$, and the total kinetic energy is $E$. Determine their speeds.

12. A certain amount of gas is heated from a temperature of 300 K to a temperature of 400 K. In this case, the volume of the gas changes in proportion to the temperature. The initial gas volume is 3 liters. The pressure changed at the end of the process turns out to be 1 atm. How much work was done by the gas in this process?

13. A load suspended on a thread of length $l$ rotates uniformly in a circle in a horizontal plane. Find the period of rotation of the load if, during its rotation, the thread deviates from the vertical by an angle $a$.
