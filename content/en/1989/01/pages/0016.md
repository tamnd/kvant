⟦folio 14⟧

Snowflakes under a magnifying glass
seemed much larger
what they really were
and waited for luxurious flowers
or tenth stars.
See how well it has been done! —
Kai said. — Snowmen are proud
more interesting than real flowers!
And what accuracy!
Oh, if only
they didn't hide!

G.-H. Andersen.
"The Snow Queen"
