Equilibrium of a mechanical system and the method of virtual displacements

He said that for the construction of one of the cathedrals in Switzerland, its architect came across blocks that allowed him to lift particularly heavy loads to great heights. He designed a very complex chain hoist, but got confused in the numerous tension forces of the cables and was never able to calculate how many people would need to be hired to service it. For help, the architect approached the famous scientist Juhann Bernoulli (1667-1748). Imagine the architect’s surprise when Bernoulli, barely looking at the drawing, immediately gave the answer. The amazed architect asked to reveal to him the secret of the pillar calculation...

Let us also try (following Bernoulli) to discuss the equilibrium conditions of a mechanical system from a slightly different position than was done in the next chapter of the school textbook “Physics 8”.

Obviously, in order to find out the conditions of equilibrium of a system, the reaction forces of mechanical connections are driven *). Connections are usually called those restrictions that are imposed on the position of individual parts of the system, or on the possibility of their movement.
