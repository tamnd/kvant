This is what the new section of our magazine will be called. In it we will publish various materials devoted to the exploration and exploration of outer space, the work of the All-Union Youth Aerospace Society.

R IS FOR ROCKET

R. BRADBURY

This huge thing, which we pressed our faces to, and felt the wind getting hot, and pressed ourselves even harder against it, forgetting who we were and where we came from, dreaming only about who we could be and where we could go...

But we were boys - and we liked being boys; and we lived in a small Florida city - and we liked the city; and we went to school - and we certainly liked school; and we climbed trees and played football, and we also liked our mothers and fathers...

And yet sometimes - every week, every day, every hour in that minute or second when we thought about the flame, and the stars, and about the limit beyond which they expected us - sometimes we liked the rockets better.

- Buddha, Muhammad, Allah and other real and semi-mythical figures! - I said and jumped away from the window with such agility that the guys fell onto the grass from the impact.

I immediately pulled on a jumper, quickly put on my shoes, put nutritional capsules in my back pocket - today we won’t have time to eat, swallow the pills, your stomach will churn - and took the vacuum elevator from the second floor down to the first.

On the lawn, the guys, all five of them, were biting their lips and jumping up and down with impatience, making angry horns.

“The last one to add to the monorail,” I shouted, rushing past them at a speed of 5 thousand miles per hour, “will be the bug-eyed Martian!”

Sitting in the cabin of the monorail, whistlingly carrying us to the Cosmodrome, twenty miles from the city - just a few minutes' ride - I felt like bugs were crawling in the pit of my stomach. Give a fifteen-year-old boy one more launch. Almost every week, small continental cargo rockets came and went on schedule, but this launch... A completely different matter - strength, power... The Moon and beyond...
