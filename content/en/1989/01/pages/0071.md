Mathematics
1. Two tourists simultaneously walked from point A to point B. The first tourist walked at a speed of 5 km/h, and the other half at a speed of 4 km/h. The second tourist walked 4/9 of the way at a speed of 6 km/h, and the rest of the way at a speed of 4 km/h. Which tourist came closer to point B?
2. The length of the midline of the trapezoid is 5 cm, and the length of the segment connecting the midpoints of the bases is 3 cm. Find the bases of the trapezoid if the angles at the larger base are 30° and 60°.
3. Find the most possible value of the quotient of a three-digit number divided by the sum of its digits.
4. What two digits must be added to the right of the number 1989 so that the resulting number is divisible by 8 and 9?
5. It is known that $a + \frac{1}{a}$ is an integer.
Prove that $a^3 + \frac{1}{a^3}$ is also an integer.
6. On the coordinate plane, consider triangles with vertices at points A (0, 0), B (1, 1) and C (x, y). Find and draw on figure the set of points C such that the triangle ABC is acute.

7. Point K is the midpoint of side AD of rhombus ABCD, and point L lies on the diagonal AC, AL=3LC. In what ratio does the intersection point of segments BD and KL divide segment BD?
8. For what values ​​of the parameter a does the equation $x + \sqrt{x} = a$ have a unique solution?
9. What values ​​can the parameter c apply if it is known that $|x^2 - x + c| \leq 1$ at $0 \leq x \leq 1$?
10. In a convex quadrilateral of area S, segments are drawn connecting opposite sides. Find the smallest possible value of the product of the lengths of the segments.
11. The average of natural numbers from 1 to 99 select those that are not divisible by either 2 or 3. Find the sum of these numbers.
12. A particle flies out from a point on the circumference of the base of the cylinder, which is reflected from the base and side surface of the cylinder according to the law: the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection, and no reflection occurs at points on the circles of the bases. It is known that the first reflection will occur at a point at the base of the cylinder, and after a series of reflections the particle returned to the starting point. Find the smallest possible number of reflections.
