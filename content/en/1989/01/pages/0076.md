Physics

Oral exam objectives

1. A bullet flying at a certain speed penetrates into a solid barer to a depth of $l=10$ cm. How deep into the same barer will a bullet with twice the speed go?

2. A bus with a mass of $m=4$ t starts from rest and on a path $l=100$ m acquires a speed of $v=20$ m/s. Find the friction coefficient if the traction force is $F=10$ kN.

3. A ball flying at a speed of $v_1=20$ m/s is thrown back by a racket strike in the opposite direction with a speed of $v_2=15$ m/s. Find what the change in momentum of the ball is if the change in its kinetic energy is equal to $\Delta E=8,75$ J.

4. A car weighing $m=2000$ kg slows down and stops after $t=4$ s, it will travel a distance of $l=40$ m. What work is done by the traction force?

5. A cylinder with a volume of $V=0,2$ m³ contains helium under a pressure of $p_1=10^5$ Pa at a temperature of $t_1=17^\circ\text{C}$. After pumping helium, the pressure increased to $p_2=3\cdot10^5$ Pa, and the temperature became equal to $t_2=47^\circ\text{C}$. How much has the mass of helium increased? The universal gas constant is $R=8,31$ J/(mol K), the molar mass of helium is $=4\cdot10^{-3}$ kg/mol.

6. It took $\tau_1=15$ min to heat a certain amount of water from $0^\circ\text{C}$ to boiling temperature using an electric heater. After this, $\tau_2=1$ hours 20 minutes were required to convert all the water into steam under the same conditions. Using these data, determine the specific heat of vaporization of water. Specific heat of water $c=4200$ J/(kg K).

7. Two identical metal balls with charges $q_1=9,5\cdot10^{-9}$ Kl and $q_2=54,5\times10^{-9}$ Kl were in the air at some distance from each other. Then they were connected for a while and placed in kerosene in their original place. The force of interaction between the balls did not change. Find the dielectric permeability of kerosene.

8. A resistor with a resistance of $R=6$ Ohm is connected to a current source with internal resistance $r=0,4$ Ohm. How many times does the power released in the external circuit change if another identical resistor is connected in series with the first one?

9. The electric motor of a crane operates under a voltage of $U=380$ V and consumes a current of $I=20$ A. What is the efficiency of the motor if the crane lifts a load weighing $m=1$ t uniformly to a height of $h=19$ m in a time of $t=50$ s?

10. A ray of light falls from a certain liquid onto its interface with a vacuum. Angle of incidence $a=30^\circ$. The reflected and refracted rays redistributed each other. Find the refractive index of this liquid.
