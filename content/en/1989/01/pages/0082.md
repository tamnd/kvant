ATTENTION SUBSCRIBERS!

This year the number of pages in our magazine is increasing by 25%, which allows us to open a number of new sections. To compensate for additional expenses, the price of each room increases by 5 kopecks. The new price of the magazine is 45 kopecks.
Unfortunately, the decision to increase the volume and cost of the journal was approved after the release of the Soyuzpechat catalog and the start of subscriptions for 1989. If you have already subscribed, we ask you to make an additional subscription at the appropriate Soyuzpechat branch for 5 kopecks per issue. For those who have not managed to subscribe to «Kvant» since the beginning of the year, we remind you that subscription to it is accepted without restrictions at Soyuzpechat agencies, post offices and post offices.
