⟦folio 42⟧

P means package

(For the beginning, see p. 34)

His name was Trent. He spoke confidently, with unforced smoothness, without beating around the bush.
I stood silently, and my mother sat in the corner looking like a confused girl. I stood and listened.
Of everything that was said, I only remember some oddities.
-...excellent grades, high IQ. Perception A-1, curiosity AAA. The necessary passion for real and patient pursuit is eight long years...
- Yes, sir.
—...we talked with your teachers of semantics and psychology...
- Yes, sir.
-...and don’t forget, Master Christopher...
Mister Christopher!
-...and don’t forget, Master Christopher, no one should know that you were selected by the Cosmonautics Committee.
- Nobody?
- Your mate and teacher, of course, know about this. But besides them, no one should know. Did you understand me well?
- Yes, sir.
Trent smiled discreetly, resting his hands on his sides.
- He wants to ask you - why is this? Why can't you share with your friends? I'll announce. This is a kind of psychological defense. Every year we select about ten thousand young people from the billion-dollar population of the Earth. Three thousand of them graduate from astronautics studies in eight years, with one specialty or another. The rest comes and returns home. They dropped out, but those around them have no need to know about it. In general, dropouts will occur in the first half of the year. It's not very pleasant to return home, meet friends and report to them that the most substitute job in the world is too tough for you. So we do everything so that the return is painless. There is another reason. Also psychological. It is so important for boys to be in charge, to be superior to their comrades in some way. By strictly forbidding you to tell your friends that you have been selected, we deprive you of half the satisfaction. And in this way we check what is most important to you: petty ambition or space itself. If you're only thinking about standing out, you're on your way. If space is your recognition, if it is everything to you, then welcome.
He nodded to his mother.
- Thank you, Mrs. Christopher.
“Sir,” I said. “One question.” I have a friend, Ralph Priory. He lives in a boarding school...
Trent nodded.
- Naturally, I cannot tell you his details, but he is registered with us. Is this your best friend? And of course you want him to be with you. I'll check his file. Let's try it out in a boarding school, you say? This is not very good. But... we'll see.
- If possible, please. Thank you.
- Come see me at the Kostodrome on Saturday at five o’clock, Mr. Christopher. Until then, not a word to anyone.
He saluted. And he left. And I took off into it in my helicopter, and at that very second my mother stood very close to me.
“Oh, Chris, Chris...” she kept repeating, and we clung to each other, and whispered something, and said something, and my mother said how important this is for us, especially for me, how substitutable, and how very, sort of like in the old days, when a person fasted, and took a vow of silence, and didn’t talk to anyone, just prayed and tried to become worthy, and went to some monastery, somewhere in the gouache, and then returns to people, and serves as a model, and brings goodness to people. So now, she said, she concluded, she asserted, this is also a kind of high order, and I will become, as it were, a particle of it, I will no longer contribute to it, but I will contribute to the Universe, I will become everything that my father dreamed of becoming, but could not, did not live...
“Of course, of course,” I muttered. “I’ll put it up, my word, I’ll put it up...” I faltered. “Jen, how... how do we tell Ralph?” How should we deal with him?
“You’ll find out, that’s all, Chris.” Tell him so. Short and clear. Don't tell him anything at all. He will understand.
- But, Jen, you...
She smiled tenderly.
- Yes, Chris, I will be lonely. But I still have my job and Ralph remains.
- You want to say...
- I'll pick him up from the boarding school. He will live here when you leave. That's what you wanted to hear from me, Chris, right?
I nodded, everything inside me seemed to melt.
- Yes, that's exactly what I wanted to hear.
