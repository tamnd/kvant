“The proposed book is like a lengthy physical “quiz”, which should help the reader determine how much he has actually mastered the basics of physics. However, this is in no way an exam question paper; Most of the questions belong to those that are hardly ever asked in the exams. For example, the book examines material that generally challenges the mismo of exam question papers, although our “quiz” questions are closely related to the elementary physics course. Despite their simplicity, the questions often contain something unexpected for the reader. Others seem so simple that everyone has a ready answer to them, which, however, often turns out to be wrong.”

This is how the book of the famous popularizer of physics and mathematics, mechanics and astronomy Ya. I. Perelman “Do you know physics?”, published in 1934, begins.
