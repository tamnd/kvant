9th grade

1. All natural numbers are written out one after another in a row:
   012345678910111213...
What number is in 1989th place in this sequence?

2. On the coordinate plane, draw the set of points $(x, y)$ for which
   $$[x]+[y]=0$$
   ([a] is the integer part of the number $a$, i.e. the largest integer, does not exceed it $a$; for example, $[-1,1]=-2$; $[3]=3$).

3. The real numbers $a, b, c, d$ are such that
   $$a+b=c+d; a^2+b^2=c^2+d^2.$$

Prove that
   $$a^3+b^3=c^3+d^3.$$

4. The bases of a trapezoid are $a$ and $b$. A straight line parallel to the bases divides it into two equal parts. Find the length of the segment cut out on the straight line by the sides of the trapezoid.

6. There are three parallel straight lines on the back plane. Using a compass and ruler, construct a square, three of whose vertices lie on these lines.
