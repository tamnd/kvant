Tasks

M1171—M1175, F1178—F1182

M1171. Let us denote the sum $1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} + \dots + \frac{1}{n}$ by $h_n$. Prove (for every natural number $n$) the inequality
$$\frac{1}{h_1^2} + \frac{1}{2h_2^2} + \frac{1}{3h_3^2} + \dots + \frac{1}{nh_n^2} < 2.$$
L. D. Kurlyandchik

M1172. What is the maximum angle that the segments $OA$ and $OB$ can make between themselves, coming from the origin $O$ of a rectangular coordinate system in space, if the point $A$ has coordinates $(x, y, z)$, and the point $B$ has coordinates $(y, z, x)$?

S. N. Vyshkov

M1173*. Three lines are drawn through one point inside a triangle of area $S$ such that two of them intersect each side of the triangle (see figure). Prove that the areas $S_1, S_2, S_3$ of the three resulting triangles satisfy the inequality
$$\frac{1}{S_1} + \frac{1}{S_2} + \frac{1}{S_3} \geq \frac{9}{S}.$$
G. N. Zaitsev

M1174*. The sequence of integers $a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots$ is given by the conditions $a_1 = 1, a_2 = 12, a_3 = 20,$
$$a_{n+3} = 2a_{n+2} + 2a_{n+1} - a_n \quad (n = 1, 2, \dots).$$
Prove that for any number $n$ the number $1 + 4a_n a_{n+1}$ is the square of an integer.

P. Kozarev and S. Doychev (Volgaria)

M1175*. For what natural numbers $n$ is the following statement true: no matter how several disjoint regular $n$-gons are laid out on a plane, one of them can be twisted in a certain direction without defining the others? (You cannot rotate a $n$-gon, that is, all rays emanating from the points of the selected $n$-gon in the desired direction should not define the remaining $n$-gons.)

D. A. Tereshin

F1178. A sheet of plywood falls in space. It turned out that at some point in time the velocities of two points of the sheet $a$ and $b$ are the same - $\vec{v}_a = \vec{v}_b = \vec{v}$ - and lie in the plane of the sheet. It also turned out that the speed of the sheet point $c$, located from points $a$ and $b$ at distances equal to the distance between points $a$ and $b$, is twice as high as the speed $v$. Where is the point on the sheet at this moment whose speeds are equal to $3v$?

K. S. Serdeev

F1179. If lightning strikes the water during a thunderstorm, then after the thunderstorm a fish is sometimes seen floating on the lake. How to explain this? After all, the probability of lightning hitting a single fisherman is small.

A. I. Buzdin
