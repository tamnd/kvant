Every body has three axes around which it can rotate in a balanced state, without revealing tendencies to pack in different directions, but in two of these three cases this equilibrium is still unstable, and there is only one axis around which a perfect stable and balanced rotation can take place. In addition, a rotating body, left to itself, after a more or less long time, eventually begins to rotate around this axis, if only there is friction to calm its swing.

To make what was said above clear, I will bring the body into rotation in such a way that allows it to take as the axis of rotation that main axis, the top whose rotation turns out to be the most stable.
