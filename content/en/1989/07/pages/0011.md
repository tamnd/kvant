The light-collecting antenna - a giant complex that unites up to 300-400 chlorophyll molecules - acts like a funnel: it collects phoons and transfers their energy to the reaction center, which, comparatively, when it receives a single phoon.

The reaction center is built from three proteins (at figure they are blue), the chains of which have different weights and structures. Two smaller chains are, as it were, surrounded by a large, long “proboscis”, rising from the lower surface of the membranes to the upper (the molecular acids that form the membrane are represented by lines in the form of a “saw”, and the artist depicted the remains of phosphoric acid with yellow circles). The creation of photosystems is one of the achievements of evolution. For the first time, the atmosphere began to become enriched with oxygen. The appearance of a powerful oxidizing agent in the air sharply boosted the efficiency of energy processes throughout the living world. The possibility of more active creativity of nature in creating complex animal forms has emerged. Purple, red, crimson and brown algae lost the battle to the chlorophyll-rich greens. Only the greenery of the plant was able to reach the present day.

Our story, we hope, seems simple and understandable. However, we have only sparsely outlined the most complex physical and chemical processes that make up photosystesis. And much is still unclear in these processes; answers to many questions have yet to be found. But progress in the study of photosythesis has been remarkable. Two Nobel Prizes have already been awarded for work in this area. The efforts of physicists and chemists, biologists and mathematicians concentrated on this problem. Lasers and electron microsolars, powerful computers and RENTE installations - all this is used by science to reveal the secrets of photosythesis.

Serodnya, of course, is still far from practical application of the knowledge gained in the course of this painstaking and expensive work. But in the future, we see the possibility of creating completely new solar cells, in the design of which the “priits” of constructing photosynthetic membranes will be used. Such energy sources will be able to produce it environmentally “cleanly” and with high efficiency. This purity, one might say “sterility”, photosythesis especially attracts us, living in a gas-filled and constantly “warming” world...

This long story grew out of a seemingly simple question: why is the grass green?
