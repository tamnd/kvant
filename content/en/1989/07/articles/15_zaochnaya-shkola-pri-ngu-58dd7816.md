Correspondence school at NSU

At the Novosibirsk Order of Labor Red Replacement State University named after. The Lenin Komsomol operates a Correspondence School (3Sh) for students in grades 8-10 of secondary schools in Siberia, the Far East, Central Asia and the Urals.

The main task of 3SH is to assist in the formation and development of students' interest in the natural sciences.

There are five departments in 3Sh: mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic. The mathematical, physics and chemical departments accept students in grades 8-10, the biological departments only accept students in grades 9, and the departments of economics accept only students in grades 10.

In addition to individual students, 3Sh can also host mathematical, physical, chemical, biological and economic clubs and electives, which operate at the school under the guidance of a teacher. Club leaders recruit and enroll students who have successfully completed the first assignment in the relevant subject. A circle is accepted into 3SH if the leader informs 3SH of his last name, first name, patronymic and sends a changed list of circle keys (indicating the results of grades for the assignment period), signed by the school director and certified by the seal.
