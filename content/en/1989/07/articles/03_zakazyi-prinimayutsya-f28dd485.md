accepted

The main editorial office of the physical and mathematical literature publishing house "Nauka" (Fizmatlit) is the largest publishing organization that produces literature on physical and mathematical sciences - mathematics, mechanics, physics, astronomy, cyber and information.

For all publications of the Main Editorial Board of Physics and Mathematics Literature, included in the annotated thematic release plan for 1990, full fulfillment of orders from bookselling organizations is guaranteed.

Below is a list of books that may be prohibited by our readers. The numbering corresponds to the thematic plan. Price is approximate.

You can order these books at your nearest (large) bookstore or at a store that has a “Book by mail” department in the next 1-2 months. There you can also get acquainted with the annotated thematic plan.
