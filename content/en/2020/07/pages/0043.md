OUR OBSERVATIONS

Wayward top

M.STARSHOV

THIS SIMPLE DESIGN, called the spinner (Fig.1), can be said to have made its way around the world, and someone even called it “a must-have office toy in 2017.” In fact, for some time almost every young person until about a quarter of a century old had a spinner and mindlessly admired its mesmerizing rotation. But fashion is always fickle, and now it's hard to remember the last time I saw a spinner in someone's hands. One such top was inherited from his grandson to his grandfather, and he began to look for a new use for the toy. First of all, the gyroscopic abilities of the turntable were obvious. Therefore, the idea arose to make it rotate not in the hands, but on some kind of support. Indeed, the spinner feels good, for example, on a vertically fixed piece of ballpoint pen refill

But most importantly, one could feel the pressure on the fingers when changing the direction of its axis of rotation. It’s clear, a gyroscope! And yesterday the idea came to attach a pinwheel to a ruler and hang the structure on a string (Fig.2). It turned out interesting, the ruler was turning. But I quickly wanted to get rid of the ruler, or at least make it lighter.

In the “useful trash” there was a strip of getinax with longitudinal slits. Through one of them, the spinner was pressed to the getinax with a small screw, and through the second slot the whole thing was suspended on a thin thread (Fig.3). And this is where the most interesting thing happens - when the spinner rotates quickly, the plate begins to slowly spin around the vertical axis. The direction of this rotation depends on the direction of rotation of the top itself, and moreover, when the direction of rotation changes, the nature of the rotation of the plate changes.
