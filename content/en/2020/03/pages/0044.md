KVA NT 2020/No. 3

More than 200 thousand – several generations – schoolchildren have graduated from VZMSH. Many scientists, researchers, and teachers emerged from their ranks. For many, studying at VZMSH helped them better navigate life, set big goals for themselves, and achieve their plans. VZMSH, unlike courses or clubs, is a real school, with its own program, rhythm, tests, two-way communication between student and teacher. There are forums for students where they can ask the teacher any question they are interested in and receive a complete and comprehensive answer. We provide our materials in electronic form, constantly communicate with our students through social networks, WhatsApp and other advanced means of communication, tell them about what is not included in the curriculum, but helps their development, introduce books and articles of our teachers, conduct video conferences and video lectures. At VZMSh there is a special form of work - the “Collective Student” group. This is a group of schoolchildren working according to VZMSH methodological manuals under the guidance of a school teacher using the same programs and manuals as individual students. To help teachers - curators of such groups - special methodological and educational materials are being developed. Students, under the guidance of a teacher, discuss together the material covered and the assignments received. Teachers often use their connection with VZMS to clarify other teaching issues not related to the work of the group. As our many years of experience show, many teachers perceive cooperation with VZMS as their own study. Studying in the “Collective Student” format has the following advantages: contact with the teacher simplifies learning, which is especially important for those students who find it difficult to organize their time independently, without a mentor; joint activities create a creative atmosphere in which there is room for both competition and cooperation; materials sent from VZMS can serve as a good basis for the teacher to organize a club or elective at school, help prepare students for participation in olympiads and passing the Unified State Exam; students accept

DEPARTMENT OF MATHEMATICS

We teach future mathematicians to work with scientific texts, clearly express their thoughts, and provide the opportunity, using our manuals, to advance in the study of various mathematical issues as far beyond the required minimum as they themselves want. We help to deepen and expand the knowledge acquired at school, prepare for the Unified State Exam, Unified State Exam and other exams. For those who already know the pleasure of solving mathematical problems, we hope to bring joy from thinking about new ones, similar and not at all similar to the previous ones. We teach all our students to reason impeccably and harmoniously. We consistently teach schoolchildren from 4th to 11th grade. You can start learning from any class.
