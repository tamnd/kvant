The goal of the biology department is to help schoolchildren deepen and expand their knowledge, as well as get acquainted with modern methods and achievements of science. The program of the biological department includes the most interesting topics that receive little attention in the basic school course: plant physiology, cytology and genetics, systematic theory, human and animal physiology and some other sections. Our manuals and educational materials, first of all, accustom schoolchildren to independent creative work, give them the opportunity to acquire the skills of expressing their thoughts in writing, putting forward and critically analyzing various hypotheses, and working with biological literature. Each student in the biology department receives a curator who checks the work and answers any questions that arise, and also helps the student see his weak points and correct them. This ensures that schoolchildren receive first-hand information and become familiar with facts and theories that have not yet been included in school textbooks. Depending on the grade - 8 or 9 - schoolchildren study at the biological department of VZMSH for 2 or 3 years. Those who complete 8 classes enter the 1st year, and 9 classes enter the 2nd year. At the end of the 3rd year, all students who have completed the training program (according to schemes 1-2-3 or 2-3) receive a diploma of completion of the biological department of the VZMSH.
