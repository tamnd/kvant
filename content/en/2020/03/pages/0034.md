union of physics and mathematics

This union, as can be seen even from the epigraphs, was by no means always cloudless. Nevertheless, over many centuries of active interaction between sciences, it was able to strengthen so much that it became unusually fruitful. For the last half century, «Kvant» has intensively contributed to this, and now our column will try to make its modest contribution. Unfortunately, mathematics and physics at school are sometimes located, as they say, “on opposite sides of the barricades,” when students do not suspect how much such a community will help them enrich their knowledge and skills. However, this can be easily verified by participating in various tournaments and Olympiads. Today we propose a small fraction of similar tasks, without, however, drawing a subject line. We believe that you will choose what to rely on when solving them - physical intuition, mathematical ingenuity, or just a joke. And “Kaleidoscope” intends to expand the range of such tasks soon.

Questions and tasks

1. At the celebration of the anniversary of the magazine «Kvant», the director of the physics and mathematics lyceum invited 24 graduates to line up in 6 rows so that each row consisted of 5 people. How did the lyceum students cope with this task? 2. Three points are selected at random on the surface of the sphere. What is the probability that they will all end up in the same hemisphere? 3. A helicopter took off from Moscow, flew 300 km to the south, then 300 km to the west, 300 km to the north and 300 km to the east, after which it landed. What latitude and longitude will it be at relative to the start? 4. Two cyclists simultaneously left points A and B towards each other and met 15 minutes after the first cyclist reached the halfway point. When 30 minutes passed after their meeting, the second cyclist reached the halfway point. How long did the first cyclist take to complete the journey? 5. Two pedestrians started simultaneously along a circular road from the same place in the same direction. The faster pedestrian overtook the other after 36 minutes. If they started in opposite directions, they would meet in 4 minutes. In how many minutes can each pedestrian walk around the circular road?
