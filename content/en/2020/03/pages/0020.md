PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2594 – M2597 were offered at the regional stage of the XLVI All-Russian Olympiad for schoolchildren in mathematics. Problem F2601–F2604 were offered this year at the first round of the Moscow Olympiad for schoolchildren in physics.

Problem M2594–M2597, F2601–F2604

M2594. It is known that for some x and y the sums sin x + cos y and sin y + cos x are positive rational numbers. Prove that there are natural numbers m and n such that m sin x + n cos x is a natural number. N. Agakhanov

M2595. a) Kolya and Dima play a game on the 8 ¥ 8 board, taking turns making moves, Dima starts. Kolya draws crosses in the cells, and Dima covers pairs of adjacent board cells with 1 ¥ 2 rectangles (dominoes). During his turn, Kolya must place one cross in any empty cell (that is, in a cell in which a cross has not yet been drawn and which has not yet been covered with a domino). During his turn, Dima must cover with a domino two adjacent cells (not yet covered by other dominoes), in which the total number of crosses is even (0 or 2). The one who cannot make a move loses. Which player has a winning strategy? b) The same game and the same question, but, unlike point a), Kolya makes the first move. M. Didin

M2596. The quadrilateral ABCD is circumscribed about the circle $\omega$. Prove that the diameter of the circle $\omega$ does not exceed the length

M2597. Let p be a prime number greater than 3. Prove that there is a natural number y less than p 2 and such that the number py + 1 cannot be represented as the product of two integers, each of which is greater than y. M. Antipov

F2601. A sealed vessel is filled with a mixture of air and water vapor and begins to cool in a thermostat with melting ice. During the cooling process, the temperature in the vessel is measured with an error of $\Delta T = = 0,5 \infty C$ and the pressure with an error of $\Delta p = 0,05$ ◊ $10^{5}$ Pa. The result is a table:

Determine the ratio of the amount of water to the amount of air in the vessel, as well as the density of the gas phase at the beginning and at the end of the process. Note that the saturated vapor pressure of water equal to 1 kPa is achieved at a temperature of about $7^\circ$ C. The molar masses of water and air are 18 g / mol and 29 g / mol, respectively. P. Kryukov
