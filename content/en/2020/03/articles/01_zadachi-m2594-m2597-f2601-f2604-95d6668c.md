Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Problem M2594–M2597, F2601–F2604

M2594. It is known that for some x and y the sums sin x + cos y and sin y + cos x are positive rational numbers. Prove that there are natural numbers m and n such that m sin x + n cos x is a natural number. N. Agakhanov

M2595. a) Kolya and Dima play a game on the 8 ¥ 8 board, taking turns making moves, Dima starts. Kolya draws crosses in the cells, and Dima covers pairs of adjacent board cells with 1 ¥ 2 rectangles (dominoes). During his turn, Kolya must place one cross in any empty cell (that is, in a cell in which a cross has not yet been drawn and which has not yet been covered with a domino). During his turn, Dima must cover with a domino two adjacent cells (not yet covered by other dominoes), in which the total number of crosses is even (0 or 2). The one who cannot make a move loses. Which player has a winning strategy? b) The same game and the same question, but, unlike point a), Kolya makes the first move. M. Didin
