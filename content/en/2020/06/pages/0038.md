XII International Olympiad Romanian Master of Mathematics

At the end of February, the Romanian Master of Mathematics (RMM) Olympiad was held for the twelfth time in the capital of Romania, Bucharest. Over the years, the Romanian Master Olympiad has established itself as one of the most challenging mathematical competitions for schoolchildren around the world. This was the plan of the organizers from the Romanian Mathematical Society. According to their plan, RMM was supposed to become an analogue of the main mathematical competition, the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), for a narrower circle of participants: teams from about 20 countries that occupy leading positions in the IMO are annually invited to RMM.

The Russian team at RMM was represented by:

Danila Demin (Sochi, 10th grade), Ivan Gaidai-Turlov (Moscow, 11th grade), Maxim Turevsky (St. Petersburg, 9th grade), Danil Sibgatullin (Kazan, 10th grade), Konstantin Myasnikov (Chelyabinsk, 11th grade).

From left to right: K. Myasnikov, M. Turevsky. I. Gaidai-Turlov, D. Sibgatullin, D. Demin
