PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2607 was offered at the final stage of the XII Olympiad named after L. Euler, problem M2608 – at the XXIII A.N. Kolmogorov Memorial Cup. Problem F2613–F2616 were offered to schoolchildren who had completed the eighth grade at the Summer Olympiad of the Scientific Research Center of Moscow State University. The author of the problems is S. Varlamov.
