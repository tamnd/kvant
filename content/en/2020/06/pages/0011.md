TRANSLATED FROM LATIN "AB OVO" literally means "from the egg." In a figurative sense, this expression is used when they want to indicate the originality, primacy of something. It was this figurative meaning that the ancients put as the main meaning in the words “ab ovo”. Without knowing it themselves, they thereby resolved in favor of the egg a sophism that had existed since time immemorial: what came first - the chicken or the egg? We will leave this question aside, and will deal with some physical phenomena, choosing as the subject of research... a chicken egg. Who does not remember the fatal cause of the discord between Lilliput and the empire of Blefuscu, described in Gulliver's Travels? It was the decree of the Emperor of Lilliput, ordering all subjects, under pain of death, to break eggs from the sharp end. Gulliver believed that choosing the end from which to break an egg is the master's business. From whatever you want, break it from that. We completely agree with this position, but we still ask ourselves: which end of the egg is easier to break? Solving this problem will help, for example, choose the right tactics in “boiled egg battles,” which used to often arise among children at the Easter table.
