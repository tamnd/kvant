It remains to show that an example arranged in this way really exists and we will not be hindered by any contradictions that we have not yet paid attention to. The construction of the example can be described as follows. Let's take a triangle, two of whose angles are less than $10\infty$, and the third is less than $170\infty$ (for example: 9q, 9q, 162q). Let's cut its larger angle into 17 more small angles (at figure $12 их^{8).}$. We will get a closed broken line, in which some of the links overlap each other. There are 19 small angles and 16 angles at $360\infty$ (at figure they are marked in red). All that remains is to move the vertices sequentially so that the sides no longer overlap each other, but at the same time small the angles remain small.
