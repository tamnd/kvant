⟦folio 8⟧

KVANT 2020/№8

Exercises 1. What is the probability that when throwing a die twice, a) two numbers with a sum of at least 10 will appear; b) two numbers, the first of which is divisible by the second? 2. A passenger arrives at a stop at a random time and waits for a bus on one of two routes, running at intervals of 10 and 15 minutes. Find the probability p p t that he will have to wait at least t minutes. 3. The segment is divided into three equal parts. What is the probability that three points randomly thrown onto a segment will end up in three different pieces? 4. Four points A, B, C, D are randomly selected on a circle. What is the probability that segments AC and BD intersect? 5. a) A diameter is drawn in a circle. A point is randomly selected on it and a chord perpendicular to the diameter is drawn through it. What is the probability that the length of the chord is greater than the radius of the circle? b) Two points are randomly selected on the circle. What is the probability that the length of the chord connecting them is greater than the radius? c) A point is randomly selected in the circle. What is the probability that a chord with a midpoint at this point is greater than the radius?

⟦column⟧

d) Solve similar problems about a chord of length r 3, where r is the radius. Comment. Problems a), b), c) are like three versions of the same thing: draw a random line intersecting a given circle; What is the probability that the length of the chord being cut is greater than the radius? But the answer is different (Bertrand's paradox)! 6. Three points are randomly selected on a circle. What is the probability that a triangle with vertices at these points a) has an angle greater than 30q; b) all angles are greater than 30q; c) all angles are less than 120q? 7. Two points are randomly selected on a segment. What is the probability that a triangle can be formed from the segments into which it is divided? 8. The plane is divided by a grid of lines into a) squares; b) regular triangles with side 1. What is the probability that a coin of diameter 1, randomly thrown onto a plane, will cover one of the vertices of the grid? 9. a) Find the probability that a convex n-gon with vertices at random points on a circle contains its center? b) Prove that the probability that n randomly selected points on a sphere lie on the same hemisphere (on the same side of some great circle) is $n^{2} n 2 2^{n}$.
