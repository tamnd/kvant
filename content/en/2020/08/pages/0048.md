Explanations of experiments

Fig. 3

Experiment 1. The answer “lies on the surface”: the mass of one ball is greater than the mass of the other. A lighter ball will roll down the inclined plane faster; in our case, it is empty. A massive ball has a greater moment of inertia than an empty one, and it needs significantly more energy to spin. To demonstrate this experiment, we placed a piece of foam rubber with a large number of massive lead pellet balls embedded in it inside the second ball. Experiment 2. There may be a “loose” substance inside the ball. As the ball rolls, the substance slowly moves from a higher position to a lower one. This slows down the movement of the ball down the inclined plane. In our version, small (no more than 1 mm in diameter) lead pellets were used for this.
