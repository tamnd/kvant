6. Encrypted password (8 points) Petya registered on the site and, in order not to forget the password word, decided to write it down in a notepad, encrypting it first. Here's what's written in Petya's notebook:

1101111111100001110000001110

What word was used for the password? Fortunately, Petya has not yet passed the Fano condition.

7. Spells (10 points) At Hogwarts, a practical lesson: 200 students walk along an alley of 200 apple trees and practice the spells “Blossom” and “Opadamus”. The first student makes all the apple trees bloom. The second one changes the state of every second apple tree, and the flowers fall off the even-numbered apple trees. The third one changes the state of every third apple tree to the opposite, the fourth does the same with every fourth apple tree, etc. How many blooming apple trees will be in the alley after the end of the lesson?

8. Bloggers (10 points) Dusya and Busya are bloggers who record audio podcasts. Today they were recording their podcasts about the problems of snowdrops in winter without snow and started recording at the same time. Dusya recorded her monologue on a phone with a single-channel microphone that supports a sampling frequency of 24576 Hz with a sound depth of 32 bits. Busya was recorded in a studio in stereo mode with four recording channels, each of which supported a sampling frequency of 98304 Hz with a sound depth of 64 bits. Dusya recorded the message for 4 minutes, then prepared it for uploading for 6 minutes (this did not affect the information volume of the file) and began uploading it to her channel, and her connection speed was
