Try to figure out the following wizard dialogue options yourself. 1 (simplification of Conway’s problem, author – T. Khovanova [1]). A: I have several children, the age of each of them is a positive integer, and their sum is equal to the number of this bus. B: How interesting! Perhaps if you tell me the number of your children, I can calculate their ages? A: No, you can't. B: Yeah! I finally found out how many children you have. What is the bus number? 2 (widely known version). A: I have three children, the age of each of them is a positive integer, and their sum is equal to the number of this bus, and the product is $–^{36.} B:$ But I can’t find out how old they are! A: Your daughter is older than all my children. B: Thank you, that's enough.

LA B ORATORY "QUAN TA"

Mystery balls

S. NOVIKOV

IN THE LIFE OF EVERY PERSON sometimes he has to deal with a closed (often unfamiliar) object, the internal structure of which can only be guessed at. Such objects become a kind of “black boxes”, when only input and output signals (or some of their values) are available to an external observer, and what is happening inside is unknown. About the behavior of a black box you can

judge only by observing the reactions of output quantities to changes in input ones. This makes it possible to ultimately reveal the “secret” of such an object. You may have already encountered black boxes - for example, in electrical problems. In our case, the black boxes are ten identical-looking plastic balls with a diameter of 75 mm, the internal contents of which are unknown. First, we will describe the behavior of the balls in different experimental situations. Try to express your thoughts explaining the reasons why the ball (or balls) behaves in a certain way in the described situation. And only then read our explanations.
