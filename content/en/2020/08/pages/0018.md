He talked with the speakers, explained to them the possibilities of developing the topics of their reports, and was always attentive, friendly and interested. When the idea of ​​creating a popular science and mathematics magazine for schoolchildren arose in the late 60s, Nikolai Borisovich actively became involved in this work. He was a permanent member of the editorial board and a regular contributor to «Kvant». On his initiative, one of the main and, perhaps, the most famous sections appeared in the magazine - “Problem book «Kvant»,” which Nikolai Borisovich invariably led from the founding of the magazine until the end of his days (he passed away on May 28, 1998). Problems were the main passion of N.B. Vasiliev. As in music and in art in general, in tasks he especially valued the true beauty and originality of the idea. He was an outstanding composer of mathematical problems. His problems - and there are many dozens of them - are distinguished by the refinement of their formulations and solutions, their depth and connections with “big” mathematics. In addition, like no one else, he knew how to see and identify the zest in a problem sent by another author, and find the most attractive

formulation, discover possible generalizations and further development of the plot of the problem, clearly and beautifully present the solution, often accompanied by comments and references to the literature - for those who want to understand the problem deeper. All this made the Problem Book «Kvant» what it is: a huge corpus of deep and meaningful problems, under the influence of which entire generations of scientists grew up. Nikolai Borisovich is also the author of more than thirty articles, no doubt among the best publications «Kvant». Many articles were co-authored with other mathematicians. The ability to see and highlight the main thing was combined in him with remarkable literary abilities; Vasiliev had a rare gift for transparently and clearly presenting the most complex mathematical subjects. Knowing this skill of his, many mathematicians brought their articles to the editorial office and asked Nikolai Borisovich to help bring them to mind. He never refused this, and in some cases the article improved so much that the authors asked him to become their co-author. For almost forty years, the magazine «Kvant» and Nikolai Borisovich Vasiliev were inextricably linked. It's hard to find a magazine issue that doesn't

It will not be an exaggeration to say that every young person who, over the past thirty years, has begun his journey into mathematics in one form or another, was one of the first to hear or read the name of Nikolai Borisovich Vasiliev. It was known to everyone - both those who participated in various mathematical Olympiads, and those who were interested in “extracurricular” mathematical articles and books, and those who were interested in the bizarre game of the mind in solving mathematical problems. This man was simply idolized by young people who were just taking their first steps in mathematics; they were proud of the opportunity to organize mathematical competitions with him, come up with problems, discuss topics and the content of articles. He was dearly loved and highly valued by his peers - for his dedication to science and selfless service to the education of young people, for the honesty of human relationships and wonderful character, for the fact that he was reliable to work with. He enjoyed undeniable authority among the older luminaries of mathematics, many of them are no longer with us, for his exceptional competence and multifaceted education, for his talent as a scientist and teacher, popularizer and organizer, for the fact that he always put only the interests of the matter at the forefront. And almost everyone called him warmly and in a family way - simply Kolya. N.H. Rozov, 1998
