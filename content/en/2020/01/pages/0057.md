How long does it take for the drops to merge?

A. VARLAMOV

HAVE YOU EVER SITTED AT A Plate Of Clear Chicken Broth Covered With Specks Of Golden Fat? If you didn’t have an appetite, then you probably tried to chase these specks around the plate, tearing apart the partitions between them or, conversely, connecting the specks together, watching how slowly they merged, taking the shape of a circle. Another observation from the same series is the fusion of mercury droplets from a broken thermometer (be careful - this is an unsafe activity, since mercury is very poisonous). True, here you don’t even have time to blink your eye before two droplets form one. What determines the time of merging of liquid droplets? Before we try to answer this question, let's talk a little about the reason for the merging of drops - the surface tension of the liquid. Moreover, we will try to look at this phenomenon from an energy point of view. Molecules located in a thin layer of liquid near the surface are in special conditions. The fact is that they have neighbors identical to them only on one side of the surface, in contrast to molecules inside a liquid, surrounded on all sides by their own kind.
