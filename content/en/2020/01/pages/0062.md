KVAN T 2020/№1

The energy of the electromagnetic field is determined at each point in space at each moment of time. An electromagnetic wave transfers energy, and the concept of energy flux density can be introduced. This is what we call a quantity equal to the field energy flowing per unit time through a unit surface. Each wave is also characterized by a phase. If, when two light beams are added, the difference in their phases remains the same all the time, we say that we are dealing with coherent beams. In the interference pattern resulting from the addition of two coherent beams, a spatial redistribution of light energy occurs. In the bright bands the energy is greater than the sum of the energies of the stacked beams; in dark stripes, on the contrary, less. The excess energy in the light stripes is just compensated by its lack in the dark ones. The total energy distributed over the entire interference pattern is exactly equal to the sum of the energies of the two interfering beams. figure 1 shows the dependence of the energy flux density in the interference pattern on the displacement along the screen, on which
