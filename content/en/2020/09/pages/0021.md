$МАТЕМАТИ_{Ч}$ESK

student Proizvolov’s scientific interests are topology. Then graduate school, teaching at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, participation in scientific symposiums, conferences, defense of a dissertation, about 15 scientific articles. For a long time he taught at VZMI (All-Union Correspondence Engineering Institute). I also read probability theory and statistics. Vyacheslav Viktorovich gave a famous lecture about sausage. The point is that an event occurs not for one reason, but for a combination of several reasons, each of which has its own weight. So dad began the lecture with the question: “Why is the sausage cut diagonally?” Gradually, the audience warmed up, and a variety of versions of answers were expressed, from humorous to strictly scientific. For example, that it is more convenient for the hand to move, that the piece of sausage is larger: the sandwich tastes better, and even that, according to science, the probability that the angle will be exactly equal to 90 degrees is 0. Vyacheslav Viktorovich wrote out ten reasons on the board, and then asked again: “So why is the sausage cut diagonally?” After working at VZMI, Vyacheslav Viktorovich worked at the Central Research Institute of Gosstroy. Here, for example, is one of the problems that was solved there. It is necessary to select the mesh size of the metal mesh, which is stretched at the construction site along the floors so that the tool does not fall through the mesh. The larger the cell, the cheaper, the smaller, the more reliable. From this time on, Vyacheslav Viktorovich began to draw up tasks. As he explained, a field was needed for the mind to play. A long-term collaboration with «Kvant» began. Then work at MIROS (Moscow Institute for the Development of Educational Systems), organization and participation in summer mathematical trips near Kostroma, selection of problems for the Moscow Mathematical Olympiad. The book “Growth Challenges” appeared. It so happened that I myself never solved my father’s problems from a sheet of paper, from a magazine or book. Dad gave me some of the tasks or their elements during the walk.

Vyacheslav Viktorovich Proizvolov (early 90s) local. Dad and I joked that he was a follower of the Peripatetics: he loved to teach on the go, while walking. For example, when I hear the words “Pythagorean theorem,” I still remember Pythagorean pants drawn with a twig in the snow. Vyacheslav Viktorovich was a very versatile person and a brilliant conversationalist. He was interested in mineralogy, classical music, history, philosophy, and poetry. It was possible to listen to him. As for the problems of mathematical education, Vyacheslav Viktorovich was always put into a bilious mood by the appearance of yet another training manual with a title like “Mathematics is easy and fun.” He was very angry, saying that there is no need, you can’t pretend that studying mathematics is easy. This is serious work. The important thing is that it can be interesting. Confirmation of this is his tasks.
