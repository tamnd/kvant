EXAMINATION MATERIALS

Unified State Exam in Physics

The 2020 Unified State Exam options in physics did not differ in structure from the previous year's options. They contained 32 tasks, and the maximum sum of primary points was 52 points. At the same time, 16 tasks (1–4, 8–10, 13–15, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25–26) were scored with a maximum of 1 point, 10 tasks (5–7, 11, 12, 16-18, 21, 24) were scored with a maximum of 2 points, 6 tasks (27–32) requiring a detailed solution were scored with a maximum worth 3 points each. The maximum sum of primary points was 54 points. In 2021, the structure of physics options will not change. Here is one of the options for the open segment in 2020.

Instructions for performing the work

To complete the examination work in physics, 3 hours 55 minutes (235 minutes) are allotted. The work consists of two parts, including 32 tasks. In problems 1–4, 8–10, 14, 15, 20, 25–26, the answer is a whole number or a finite decimal fraction. Write your answer in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the sample below into answer form No. 1. There is no need to write units of measurement of physical quantities.

The answer to tasks 5–7, 11, 12, 16–18, 21, 23 and 24 is a sequence of two numbers. Write your answer in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the sample below without spaces, commas and other additional characters into answer form No. 1.

The answer to task 13 is a word. Write your answer in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the sample below into answer form No. 1.

The answer to tasks 19 and 22 are two numbers. Write the answer in the answer field in the text of the work, and then transfer it according to the given

The answer to tasks 27–32 includes a detailed description of the entire progress of the task. In answer form No. 2, indicate the task number and write down its complete solution. When making calculations, it is allowed to use a non-programmable calculator. All Unified State Exam forms are filled out in bright black ink. You can use a gel or capillary pen. When completing assignments, you can use a draft. Entries in the draft, as well as in the text of control measurement materials, are not taken into account when evaluating the work. The points you receive for completed tasks are summed up. Try to complete as many tasks as possible and score the most points. After completing the work, check that the answer to each task is written down under the correct number.
