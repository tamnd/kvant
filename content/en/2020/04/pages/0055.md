2. Boy with a balloon (10 points) Boy Vlad runs in a circle, holding in his hand the end of a thread 2 2 m long, to the other end of which is attached a small inflatable balloon with helium inside. Vlad runs one lap in $2\pi$ seconds. The hand holding the thread moves in a circle with a radius of 2 m. The ball moves in a circle of the same radius two meters above the hand vertically. At what steady speed will the ball take off if the thread is released? We can assume that the force of air resistance is proportional to the square of the speed of the ball, and the radius of the ball is much less than the length of the thread. Gravity acceleration g = 10 m $с^{2}$. S. Varlamov, P. Kryukov
