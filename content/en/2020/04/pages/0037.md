The difficulties of swimming through the gap are shown in figure 3. Rescuers had to continuously pump water out of the cave. Therefore, there were a large number of rescuers in it, helping to pump out the water and prepare the children to leave. In this situation, all the above-mentioned features of the behavior of oxygen and carbon dioxide in a closed volume turned out to be important. To combat the gradual decrease in the amount of oxygen in the cave, oxygen delivery was organized using a special pipeline. It was decided that the accumulation of carbon dioxide in the cave poses a significantly greater danger than the lack of oxygen. By pumping oxygen through a pipeline into the upper part of the cave, carbon dioxide was displaced. The stratification of air into fractions was also taken into account - $CO_{2}$ accumulated in the lower part of the cave. That's why the children and the coach disappeared into the upper part of it. The search for the guys and the preparatory work took almost two weeks. During this time, the famous inventor and organizer of research Elon Musk (spaceships, electric cars) managed to make a miniature submarine for one person from spare parts for a rocket and deliver it to Thailand. But due to the narrow gap, they refused to use it. The situation became more and more difficult every day. The constant presence of people involved in pumping water from the cave was necessary (otherwise the cave would be completely

A look from the inside

At the level of body cells, the composition of the air environment is completely different. The oxygen content in the cells of the body is about 1–2% (with the exception of red blood cells, which can contain up to 96–98% oxygen), carbon dioxide in the cells is about 6%.
