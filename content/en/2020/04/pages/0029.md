31. A 1000-gon is inscribed in a circle; its vertices are colored alternately red and blue. What is the greatest possible number of red vertices whose angles are less than $179^\circ$? B.Frenkin

32. Consider a plane with Cartesian coordinates. Is it possible to divide it into polygons so that there is a point with integer coordinates strictly inside each polygon, and also so that a) the area of ​​each polygon is less than 0.9; b) there was a d such that each polygon had a diameter less than d and an area less than 0.9; c) was there such a d that each polygon has a diameter less than d and an area less than 1? (The diameter of a polygon is the largest distance between two points on its boundary. For example, the diameter of a square with side 1 is 2.) E. Bakaev
