Remember the famous paradox: which came first - the chicken or the egg? It cannot be resolved if we do not take into account the process of evolution and the formation of new species. But if you attract, then the egg has some priority, it is older. So, dinosaurs also laid eggs, and birds descended from one of the branches of dinosaurs. It turns out that the egg is older than the bird and in this evolutionary sense is primary... In our case, the problem of choice - which is primary (in other words, what triggers processes in the human body): oxygen or carbon dioxide - is solved as follows. Previously, oxygen was considered primary - after all, it is the main source of energy, giving impetus to all processes in the body. But now the pendulum of choice has swung towards carbon dioxide. They gradually came to the conclusion that the primary, triggering mechanism is the accumulation of carbon dioxide in the body. The accumulation of $CO_{2}$ in the body during the breakdown of fats and proteins in cells gives a signal to the brain that carbon dioxide needs to be removed from the cells - it “sits” on red blood cells and moves to the alveoli of the lungs. $O_{2}$ “sits” on the vacant seats in the “train” of red blood cells and spreads throughout the body. Therefore, the modern view of the breathing process is as follows: first, carbon dioxide is exhaled, and then oxygen is inhaled. At the same time, along with carbon dioxide, excess oxygen is exhaled.
