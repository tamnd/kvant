The map of Quadrlandia is a square of $6 \times 6$ cells. Each cell is either a kingdom or a disputed territory. There are only 27 kingdoms, and 9 disputed territories. The disputed territory is claimed by all neighboring kingdoms and only by them (i.e., cells adjacent to the disputed one on the side or top).
