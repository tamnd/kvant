KVAN T 2020/№10

⟦figure⟧

Fig. 1

F2620. The photograph (Fig.1), taken from the website webdiscover.ru, shows a rainbow, sea, buildings, trees, shadows. Using this photo and making reasonable guesses, try to estimate how high above the sea the photographer was when he took this photo.

Fig. 2

⟦column⟧

Let's assume that the land surface is flat and that the streets in the town are either parallel to each other or perpendicular (this is a reasonable assumption). Then you can find the points in the photograph that correspond to the horizon. One point is obtained if you draw lines (black) along the parallel roads of the town and they intersect at one point (Fig.2). The second point will be obtained if you draw lines (yellow) along the shadows falling on the horizontal surface from tall trees. The purple dot is the center of two visible rainbows. The red line corresponds to the virtual horizon (assuming the earth is flat). Using a triangle formed by a red line and two green lines, you can calculate the angular height of the Sun above the horizon.
