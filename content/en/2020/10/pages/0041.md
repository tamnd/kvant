O L IMPIADS

LXI International Mathematical Olympiad

The next International Olympiad (IMO) was supposed to be held in July 2020 in St. Petersburg, but due to the pandemic, the dates of the Olympiad were moved to September 18–28 and the format was forced to undergo changes. Unfortunately, it was impossible to bring the participants together, so special examination centers (equipped with video surveillance) were organized in all countries participating in the IMO, where two rounds of the Olympiad were held on September 21 and 22. Despite the difference in time zones, the tours took place in all countries "almost simultaneously", such that the latest start of the tour preceded the earliest end of the tour. There were 616 participants in the Olympics, from 105 countries. The central organizing committee of the Olympiad was located in St. Petersburg on the basis of the Russian State Pedagogical University named after A.I. Herzen. This year's Russian team included: Danila Demin (Sochi), Alexey Lvov (Novosibirsk), Maxim Turevsky (St. Petersburg), Anton Sadovnichy (Moscow), Danil Sibgatullin (Kazan, Moscow), Ivan Gaidai-Turlov (Moscow). The results of our participants are shown in the table.
