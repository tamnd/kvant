physical paradoxes

The history of physics is replete with paradoxes! Sometimes simple, requiring only a closer look and the use of already known knowledge to resolve apparent contradictions. And sometimes deep, affecting the foundations of existing theories and leading to their revision. Reflections on paradoxes occupied the minds of outstanding scientists, and their analysis played an important role in the development of modern physics. However, even within the school course there are many problems that are paradoxical in nature and seem to diverge from common sense. Their solution, we hope, will allow us to go beyond the usual, standard approaches, expand our understanding of physical phenomena, and also warn against some, sometimes offensive, mistakes. Let’s arm ourselves with the paradoxical saying “incredible, but true” - and boldly move forward!

Questions and tasks

1. In a closed, uniformly moving carriage, there is a ball in the center of the floor, and a light rubber ball inflated with hydrogen hangs above it near the ceiling. How will the ball and the ball move if the carriage suddenly brakes? Air resistance and friction of the ball on the floor can be neglected.
