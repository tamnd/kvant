The meaning of the term "meta" - beyond - is that the electromagnetic properties of the resulting structure are radically different from the properties of the individual elements. At figure 4 the term “ferroelectricity” is mentioned. This phenomenon has an analogy with ferromagnetism from the section on demagnetization. Similarly, the effect of an electric field on a ferroelectric also leads to an orientation of the ferroelectric domains, which is maintained after the electric field is removed, and the material becomes electrified. The use of metamaterials in applications to aircraft stealth is booming, promising many breakthroughs. In these structures, electromagnetic waves propagate along unusual trajectories, making it difficult to both detect objects and their properties. However, there are still many problems along this path (invisibility in a wide frequency range, preservation of the properties of metamaterials in real aircraft structures at high flight speeds, etc.). In addition, the entire aircraft body cannot be covered with metamaterials. It is difficult to make an aircraft completely invisible, but you can significantly reduce the effective dispersion area and make it difficult for radar to detect.

Coronavirus and protective mask We move on to the main section of this article. The main conclusion from the previous sections is that absolute protection has not yet been created in technology, but it is possible to greatly camouflage an object and make it difficult to detect. As one of the basic and simple measures to protect against the current coronavirus epidemic, universal wearing of masks is proposed (plus maintaining distance, plus more frequent hand washing, etc.). At the same time, it is immediately stipulated that masks do not guarantee against infection.
