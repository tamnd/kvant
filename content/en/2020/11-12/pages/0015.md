PROBLEM "QUANTUM"

Problems in mathematics and physics

We have been running this section from issue to issue since the founding of the magazine. The problems published in it are non-standard, but their solution does not require knowledge beyond the scope of the school curriculum. The most difficult tasks are marked with an asterisk. After formulating the task, we usually indicate who proposed it to us. Of course, not all of these problems are published for the first time. Solutions to problems in mathematics and physics from this issue should be sent to the email addresses: math@kvant.ras.ru and phys@kvant.ras.ru, respectively, or to the postal address: 119296 Moscow, Leninsky Prospekt, 64-A, «Kvant». Send the conditions of each original problem proposed for publication, along with your solution to this problem, to the same addresses. Problem M2630–M2632, М2633а were offered at the XLII Tournament of Cities. The author of problems F2633–F2640 is A. Vlasov.

Problem M2626–M2633, F2633–F2640

M2626. An infinite number of participants gathered for the intergalactic Olympiad, who were registered under numbers 1, 2, 3,... It turned out that for each n = 1, 2, ..., the participant with number n has at least n friends among the remaining participants (friendship is mutual). There is a hotel for participants to stay with an endless number of double rooms. Prove that participants can be accommodated in double rooms so that each room contains a pair of friends. V. Bragin, P. Kozhevnikov

M2627. An infinite arithmetic progression is given. The products of pairs of its members are considered. Prove that the products in some two different pairs differ by no more than 1. A. Kuznetsov

M2628*. There are N externally identical scales. It is known that exactly one of them is flawed - they show anything, and their weight is different from the weight of the others, which weigh the same and always show the correct result. During one weighing, you can put several scales on each of the bowls of some other scales. At what maximum N in three weighings can one be guaranteed to find damaged scales? A. Gribalko, O. Mangina
