CORRESPONDENCE DEPARTMENT (individual distance learning) Phone: (495) 408-51-45, e-mail: zftsh@mail.mipt.ru Admission to the correspondence department is carried out on a competitive basis based on the results of completing the introductory assignment in the subjects chosen for study. Schoolchildren entering the correspondence department complete the introductory task on the website https: zftsh.online in the format of an online Olympiad. The Olympics starts on 03/22/2021 at 9.00 and ends on 03/28/2021 at 21.00 (Moscow time). You are given 4 hours to complete assignments in each subject. The decision of the admissions committee will be communicated by June 1, 2021 to the email address specified during registration. A certificate of enrollment in ZFTSH will also appear in your personal account. When registering on https: zftsh.online, you must attach a clearly readable copy of your school certificate. Attention to schoolchildren already studying at the correspondence department of the ZFTSH If a student is already studying at the ZFTSH and wants to add another subject for the next year, it is necessary from 03/22/21 to 03/28/21 to complete the introductory task in this subject on the website https: zftsh.online. You need to complete the introductory task from your existing account. There is no need to register again. In such cases, the decision of the admissions committee is not sent, and a certificate of adding a subject and assignments for it become available to the student in his personal account in July if the decision of the admissions committee is positive. Training on the zftsh.online platform During the academic year, in accordance with the program, the student receives access to assignments on the subjects studied in his personal account on the website https: zftsh.online. The student completes assignments on the website using the built-in editor or by attaching scanned copies or photographs of work completed in the notebook. After the completion date, the work is checked on the website by the ZFTSH teachers assigned to the student. As soon as

The bot is checked, the student sees his work with a review and the author’s solutions to the control part of the assignment.

INTENSIVE AND CORRESPONDENT DEPARTMENT (training in elective groups) Telephone: (498) 744-63-51, e-mail: fakultativ@mipt.ru Optional groups can be organized in any general education institution by two, three or four teachers - physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry, in some cases training in one subject is allowed. The leaders of the elective accept students who have successfully completed the introductory task of the ZFTS (work is checked by group leaders and is not sent to the ZFTS). The introductory assignment for the part-time department will be posted on the website school.mipt.ru on March 29 and will be available until May 30. A group (at least 7 people) is admitted to the ZFTS upon the application of the director on the letterhead of the general education institution (a sample can be viewed on the ZFTS website in the section “departments” o “part-time” o “admission”). The application must indicate the full name. leaders of the optional group in subjects and a named alphabetical list of students (full name in alphabetical order, indicating the class the students are entering and the final grades for the introductory assignment in the selected subjects, address, phone number and e-mail of the school). The application can be sent by regular mail, enclosing an envelope for a response to admission to the ZFTSH with the return address of one of the managers to the ZFTSH address (marked “Optional”), or sent in scanned form (with signatures and seal) to e-mail: fakultativ@mipt.ru before June 1, 2021. The work of the heads of elective classes can be paid by the educational institution as the management of specialized elective classes by providing the ZFTSH with the relevant information.
