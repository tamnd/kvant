On orthocentric quadruples associated with a triangle

$ЧАН^{КУАНГ}$ HYUNG

This article talks about some generalization of problem M2553. In the process of reasoning we will also obtain the solution problem M2615. Consider an arbitrary triangle ABC. Let the circle inscribed in it have center I and touch sides BC, CA and AB at points A 1, B 1 and C 1, respectively. In problem M2553, K and L denoted the centers of circles inscribed in quadrilaterals AB 1 IC 1 and BA 1 IC 1, respectively, and H denoted the base of the altitude dropped from point C. Points P and Q were defined as the point of intersection of the bisector of angle AHC with A 1 C 1 and the point of intersection of the bisector of angle BHC with B 1 C 1, respectively. The statement of the problem was that the quadruple K, L, P, Q is orthocentric. The solution problem M2553 was given in the article by A. Zaslavsky “On one orthocentric quadruple” in «Kvant» No. 7 for 2019. Some other properties of this construction were also proved there. In particular, it was shown that the line PQ passes through C, the intersection point D of KQ and PL lies on AB, while CD is the common tangent to the circles inscribed in the quadrilaterals AB 1 IC 1 and BA 1 IC 1. The author discovered generalizations of these facts that can be formulated in the form of a theorem. Theorem.
