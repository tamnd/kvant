Correspondence School of Physics and Technology at MIPT

The Correspondence Physics and Technology School (CPTS) of the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (National Research University) (MIPT) enrolls students in grades 7–10 of general education institutions (schools, lyceums, gymnasiums, etc.) located on the territory of the Russian Federation into grades 8–11. ZFTSH has been working in the field of specialized additional education for children since 1966. Over the past years, more than 100 thousand students have graduated from the school; Almost all of its graduates enter the country’s leading universities, and every second MIPT student is its former student. The scientific and methodological management of the school is carried out by the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology. Education at the school is conducted in four scientific and technical subjects - physics, mathematics, computer science and chemistry. In 8th grade, only physics and mathematics are studied. In grades 9–11, these subjects are supplemented by the mathematical foundations of computer science and ICT (computer science) and chemistry. The goal of our school is to help students in grades 8–11 of general education institutions who are interested in scientific and technical subjects, deepen and systematize their knowledge in these subjects, and also contribute to their professional self-determination. ZFTSH programs are specialized additional general developmental programs and are the same for all departments. Recruitment for grades 8, 9, 10 and 11 for the 2021-22 academic year is carried out for part-time, full-time and part-time departments. The full training program is designed for 4 years - from 8th to 11th grades inclusive, but you can start training from any of the specified classes. According to the regulations on ZFTSH, a student can study in only one department of ZFTSH. Students of all departments who have successfully completed the ZFTS program, upon completion of the 11th grade, receive a certificate with final grades in the subjects studied in the 11th grade. When entering MIPT, a certificate of completion of the ZFTS will be taken into account primarily in the ranking, all other things being equal. Students from all departments will be invited to participate in the Physics and Mathematics Olympiad “PHYSTECH - 2022”, which is held at MIPT and in a number of Russian cities in February or early March, as well as in other full-time and extramural Olympiads of MIPT and its faculties. For students and leaders of elective groups there is an online lecture in physics, mathematics and chemistry according to the ZFTS program. Lectures are given by MIPT teachers (as a rule, the authors of the assignments). More information about these events can be found on the ZFTS website. Education at ZFTSH is free. For students living outside the Russian Federation, only paid education is possible at part-time or part-time courses.
