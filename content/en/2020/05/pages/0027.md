On May 8, 2020, Gleb Leonidovich Kotkin passed away.

G.L. Kotkin was born on September 20, 1934 in the city of Tobolsk. In 1961, after graduating from graduate school at Moscow State University, Gleb Leonidovich came to Academgorodok and began working at Novosibirsk State University at the Faculty of Physics. Over the years of work, Gleb Leonidovich became one of the most authoritative teachers of the NSU Physics Department, his true support. He conducted thousands of seminars and gave hundreds of lectures. Behind him are problems, assignments, tests, exams, olympiads, a world-famous problem book on analytical mechanics, the first computer workshops in our country on modeling physical processes and quantum mechanics, a problem book on statistical physics, textbooks for universities on statistical physics and analytical mechanics. Gleb Leonidovich successfully carried out scientific research in high-energy physics in the laboratory of theoretical physics at the Institute of Mathematics of the Siberian Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences. Work with his participation shed light on the possibility of creating photon-photon beams. Gleb Leonidovich was a wonderful teacher, attentive, but strict and principled. Among his students there are many successful and outstanding scientists. Many of them remember Gleb Leonidovich as the main Teacher in their lives. Throughout his creative life, Gleb Leonidovich devoted a lot of energy to working with schoolchildren.
