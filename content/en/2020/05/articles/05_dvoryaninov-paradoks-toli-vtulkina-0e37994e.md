Problem 3. Triangles were cut from rectangle ABCD and its perimeter was written inside each of them (Fig.2). When discussing problems 1 and 3 with friends, a question arose about the large spread in the values ​​of the perimeters of the quadrilateral FGHE and the square ABCD.
