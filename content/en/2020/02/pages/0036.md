LABORATORY "QUANTA"

Waves in a shallow dish (interference)

A.KOSOUROV

When throwing pebbles into the water, observe the circles they form. Kozma Prutkov

THERE IS NO PHENOMENA MORE UNIVERSAL IN NATURE than the propagation of waves. Waves on the surface of water, sound, light, radio, transfer of deformation from one part of a solid body to another - all these are wave processes. Quantum mechanics shows that the movement of microparticles is governed by wave laws. All these waves have different physical natures, different propagation speeds, different frequencies and wavelengths. But regardless of this, the movement of any waves has a lot in common. Having studied the laws of propagation of waves of one nature, most of them can be transferred practically without changes to waves of another type.

Published in «Kvant» No. 1, 1971.

⟦column⟧

In practice, it is most convenient to study waves on the surface of the water. What is a wave? Throw a stone into the pond. On the calm horizontal surface of the pond, scattering circles will appear. Points on the water surface that the wave has reached begin to oscillate relative to their equilibrium position. This position corresponds to a horizontal surface. The farther a point is from the place where the stone fell, the more late it “learns” about the fall of the stone. Disturbance spreads at a certain speed. The points to which the disturbance reached simultaneously are in the same stage of oscillatory motion, i.e. in the same phase. The speed of movement of this phase is the speed of the wave. It is measured in the direction normal to the wave front. For waves of any nature, it is always possible to indicate physical objects that experience disturbance under the influence of the wave, i.e. deviation from its equilibrium values. For sound, these are periodic increases and decreases in pressure. For radio waves and light, rapid changes in electric (and magnetic) field strength.
