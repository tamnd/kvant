CALENDAR

The values ​​on the ecliptic relative to the constellations should also change - by about one constellation every 2000 years. At the same time, the duration of the Sun’s stay in the “section” of a particular constellation should also change. During Hipparchus, this duration fluctuated between 28–30 days, which is recorded in the astrological canon, which is still used today. But in our time this is a completely different duration; it now ranges from 8 to 44 days. Moreover, in a modern picture of the starry sky, the solar ecliptic no longer intersects 12, but 13 constellations. The zodiac signs now include the constellation Ophiuchus (from November 30 to December 18). And the birth dates of modern people correspond to completely different (neighboring) signs of the Zodiac. From these irrefutable astronomical data it follows that all astrological forecasts (horoscopes) about the destinies of people, determined according to the ancient, two-thousand-year-old canon, are now meaningless, and if there are coincidences, they are purely probabilistic. But it is known that outstanding astronomers of the Renaissance and Modern times also made astrological forecasts. And this was for them the only source of means for living, and most importantly, for acquiring the necessary equipment and tools. Powerful and rich people paid generously for horoscopes, especially if they came true. The reason for the anticipation of the equinoxes

Hipparchus guessed that the phenomenon of the anticipation of the equinoxes, which he discovered, was associated with the rotation of the Earth around its axis, of which he was sure, like his predecessor Aristarchus of Samos. What is the connection between the rotation of the Earth and this phenomenon, which in Roman times began to be called precession? Note that in the 18th century, scientists used this word to name another phenomenon that characterizes the rotation of massive bodies that have one fixed point in a given frame of reference - gyroscopes (tops). This phenomenon is familiar to everyone from childhood. When the toy top (spinning top) is tilted, the axis of rotation begins to describe a conical surface. This gives stability to the position of the top because the average position of the axis vector remains vertical. The rotational motion of the top's axis is a consequence of the law of dynamics of the rotational motion of a rigid body. An ordinary top is affected by the moment of gravity applied to the center of mass of the top. The vector of this moment remains perpendicular to the axis of rotation all the time, which causes the rotation of this axis. And the constant rotation of the axis means its rotation (precession). The angular velocity of precession is always significantly less than the angular velocity of the top itself. Astronomers applied the laws of gyroscope precession to the rotating Earth. For the globe, the torque can only be the total moment of gravitational forces between the Earth and the massive bodies of the solar system - the Sun itself, the Moon and the planets of the solar system.
