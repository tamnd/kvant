However, we note that the author of the problem assumed a question that was not included in the condition. Namely: at what sequence of closing the keys in one or another resistor will the minimum amount of heat be released? The numerical method for solving this problem gives the following result. If the switch $K_{1}$ is closed first and voltage E is established on each capacitor at the moment the switch $K_{2}$ is turned on, then only 6.67% of the total amount of heat released in the two resistors is released in the resistor $R_{2}$. In the case when key $K_{2}$ is turned on first and key $K_{1}$ is turned on at the same time, i.e. there is no delay, then a minimum amount of heat is released in the second resistor and it is equal to 13.35% of the total amount. The minimum amount of heat will be released in the resistor $R_{1}$, if first the switch $K_{2}$ is turned on for a long time and the voltage E is established on the capacitor $С_{2}$, and then the switch $K_{1}$ is closed. In this case, 53.35% of the total heat will be released in resistor $R_{1}$. The ratio of the obtained minimum values ​​is close to the ratio of integers 1:2:8.
