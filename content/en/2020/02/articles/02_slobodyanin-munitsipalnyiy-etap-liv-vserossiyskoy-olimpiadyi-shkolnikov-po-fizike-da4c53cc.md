Theoretical tour

Senior League

Problem 1. Friction to the maximum The horizontal plane is divided into two half-planes - smooth and rough. A homogeneous ruler of mass m and length L, sliding with a speed $v_{0}$ along the smooth part along a straight line perpendicular to the interface, runs into the rough part with a friction coefficient P. Find the maximum modulus of power $N_{max}$ of the friction force acting on the ruler and the moment of time $t_{max}$ when it is reached, counting from the beginning of the ruler crossing the boundary between the half-planes. Problem 2. Water and steam A rigid vessel contains water with a mass of $m_{0} = 11$ g and its saturated steam with a mass of m = 10 g at a temperature T 100 q C. Find the total heat capacity C of the system. At temperature T, the specific heat capacity of water c 4.2 kJ kg q C and the specific heat of evaporation of water O 2.3 MJ kg are known, and small relative changes in pressure ' p p and temperature ' T T of saturated steam are related by the relation ' p p D' T T, where D 13. Problem 3. Line of force At some point A, located at a large distance from a uniformly charged segment of length L, the electric field strength vector forms small angle D with a line parallel to the segment. Find the distance x between the end of the segment closest to point A and the point of intersection of the segment with the field line passing through point A. Problem 4. Depth of field A lens with a diameter of D = 1 cm and a focal length of F = 2 cm gives on film the sharpest image of an object located at a distance a from the lens. At what distances (indicate $a_{min}$ and $a_{max} )$) are objects located that were depicted quite sharply in the same frame if the grain size of the film (or pixel, if it were a digital camera) is d = 10 µm? Carry out calculations for two special cases: $a_{1} = 12$ m and $a_{2} = 60$ m.
