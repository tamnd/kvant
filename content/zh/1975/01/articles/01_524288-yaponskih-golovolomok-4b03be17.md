封面的最后一页有一个谜题：您需要将所有蓝色图形放在黄色区域上。

让我们弄清楚这个谜题是如何构建的。 As you can see (see 图 on the cover), the yellow field consists of seven regular hexagons.这些人物本身是如何构建的？ Let's see what figures can be folded by connecting sides $n$ of regular triangles. From one, two and three triangles, one figure is formed: a triangle, a rhombus and a trapezoid (图 1). Three figures are made from four triangles (图 2). True, it is possible to add up the fourth, but it is a mirror image of the third, and we do not take it into account.五个三角形组成四个数字（图3）。十二个图形由六个三角形组成。自己画它们。 Twenty-four figures are made from seven triangles. These are the pieces of our puzzle (check it out!).

有些人物有名称：碗、钩、熊等。其中对称的有5个，不对称的有19个。通过翻转一些碎片，你会得到一个新的拼图。 In total, you can get $2^{19}$ puzzles by flipping (why?). Is it possible to lay any of them on the same yellow field?
