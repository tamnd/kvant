万花筒

自 1965 年以来，罗巴切夫斯基空间中的万花筒一直是与群论中的某些问题相关的深入研究的主题。它们在任何维度上的完整描述还远远不够完整。 There is an amazing result (belonging to the author of the article) that at n t 30 there are no kaleidoscopes at all in the n-dimensional Lobachevsky space. Examples of such kaleidoscopes are known only for n d 8. In addition to the already mentioned applications of kaleidoscopes in geometry (regular polyhedra), the theory of functions of a complex variable and group theory, there are no less important applications in number theory, the theory of Lie algebras, algebraic geometry and other branches of mathematics.然而，应该说，“万花筒”这个术语并没有在严肃的数学文献中使用。相反，他们说“由反射生成的离散组”。 In the article we do not have the opportunity to talk about applications of kaleidoscopes (with the exception of the connection between spherical kaleidoscopes and regular polyhedra).然而，对万花筒的研究本身就构成了几何学的辉煌一页。有关万花筒的更完整信息可以在评论文章 [2] 中找到。
