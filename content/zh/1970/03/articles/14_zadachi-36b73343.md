M13。 Prove that if the difference between the largest and smallest $n$ real numbers $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n$ is $d$, and the sum of the models of all $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ pairwise differences of these numbers

$$\sum_{i<j} |a_i - a_j|$$

等于$s$，则

$$(n-1)d \leqslant s \leqslant \frac{n^2}{4}d.$$
