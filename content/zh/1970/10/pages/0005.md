When a substance is cooled, the movement of atoms slows down, the electrons begin to circulate again in their orbits and maintain their movement until absolute zero. But, in addition to the movement of electrons along the orbits of each atom separately, there is also a goal of a series of combined movements in a solid body, which from a modern point of view should be maintained to the lowest temperatures. Thanks to this so-called degeneracy of motion in this temperature range, completely new phenomena can appear that we cannot observe at ordinary temperatures.

One of these interesting phenomena, which has already become widely known, was discovered by Kamerlingh Onnes - the phenomenon of superconductivity. It lies in the fact that at very low temperatures, electric current is able to flow through some conductors without resistance, without forming a body.
