M747. a) The sum of $n$ numbers is zero, the sum of their absolute values is $a$. Prove that the difference between the largest and smallest number is no less than $2a/n$.
b)* The point $O$ is chosen inside the polygon $A_1A_2...A_n$ so that the sum of vectors $\overrightarrow{OA_1} + \overrightarrow{OA_2} + ... + \overrightarrow{OA_n}$ is the zero vector and the sum of their lengths is $d$. Prove that the polygon’s perimeter is no less than $4d/n$.
c)* Can this estimate be improved (for certain $n$)?

V. Prasolov
