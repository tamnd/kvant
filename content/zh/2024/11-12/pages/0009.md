人工智能能做什么？原则上来说，很多。例如：
• 鱼类研究和数据挖掘；
• 对公司规模和国家规模的资金流动进行管理；

• 建立信用风险模型；
• 工业机器人化；
• 科学研究的机器人化；
• 创造新的结构材料；
• 整机医疗诊断；
• 过渡到免费交通；

• 机械艺术（绘画和音乐）；
• 人与机器（智能手机和笔记本电脑）之间便捷通信的能力。

In this impressive list, unfortunately, only two positions have been mastered so far.然而，其中之一是游戏。 According to competent experts, there are no longer games in which a person could defeat a machine. The first game to fall in battle with the machine was chess. In 1997, in his third match with the Deep Blue machine, then world champion Garry Kasparov lost a six-game match with a score of 3.5:2.5. And after this the ethicist could no longer beat the artificial intelligence at chess.
