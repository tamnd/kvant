$$\angle OCO_1 = \angle O_1BO = \angle OQO_2 = \angle O_2RO.$$
这里的第一个和最后一个等式与$BC$和$QR$垂线的相对平分线的对称性非常相似。

$$\angle O_1BO = \angle OQO_2.$$ (*)

$$\angle OQO_2 = \angle OQR - \angle O_2QR = (90^\circ - \angle RCQ) - (90^\circ - \angle RPQ) = \angle RPQ - \angle RCQ.$$

$$\angle RPQ - \angle RCQ = \angle BPC - \angle BQC,$$

$$\angle BQC - \angle RCQ = \angle BPC - \angle RPQ.$$ (*)
