**M1077.** Denote by $p_n(k)$ the number of permutations of an $n$-element set having exactly $k$ fixed points. Prove that

a) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} k \cdot p_n(k) = 0 \cdot p_n(0) + 1 \cdot p_n(1) + \ldots + n \cdot p_n(n) = n!$

b) $\sum_{k=0}^{n} (k-1)^2 p_n(k) = n!$

Remark. A permutation of a finite set $S$ is any one-to-one map $f$ of $S$ onto itself; the number of all permutations of an $n$-element set is $n! = 1 \cdot 2 \cdot \ldots \cdot n$. The element $a$ of the set $S$ is a fixed point of the permutation $f$ if $f(a) = a$.
