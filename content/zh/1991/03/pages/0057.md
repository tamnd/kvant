b) $(a_1^p + \dots + a_n^p)^{\frac{1}{p}} \cdot (b_1^q + \dots + b_n^q)^{\frac{1}{q}} \leq a_1 b_1 + \dots + a_n b_n$ 如果 $q < 0$ 和 $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} = 1$
