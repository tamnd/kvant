令 $SO = H$、$AB = c$、$BO = kc$ ($0 < k < 1$)、$BC = h$ (图 10)。然后$S_0 = \frac{1}{2} hc$，

$$\tilde{S} = \begin{cases} S_1(\varphi), \text{ если } 0 \leq \varphi \leq \varphi_1, \\ S_2(\varphi), \text{ если } \varphi_1 < \varphi \leq \frac{\pi}{2}, \\ S_3(\varphi), \text{ если } \frac{\pi}{2} < \varphi \leq \pi, \end{cases}$$

其中 $S_1(\varphi) = 1/2 hc \cdot \cos \varphi$、$S_2(\varphi) = 1/2 khc \cdot \cos \varphi + 1/2 Hc \cdot \sin \varphi$、$S_3(\varphi) = -1/2 hc \cdot \cos \varphi + 1/2 Hc \cdot \sin \varphi.$

从条件$S_1(\varphi_1) = S_2(\varphi_1)$我们得到tg$\varphi_1 = (1 - k)h/H$。我们使用函数$S_i(\varphi)$。函数$S_1(\varphi)$杀死。由于任意 $S_2'(\varphi)$ 在唯一点 $\varphi = \varphi^* \in (0; \frac{\pi}{2})$ 处消失，因此函数 $S_2(\varphi)$ 在区间 $(\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$（如果 $\varphi^* \in (\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$）上是单调的，或者它在 $\varphi = \varphi^*$ 处具有最大值（如果 $\varphi^* \in (\varphi_1; \frac{\pi}{2})$）。函数 $S_3(\varphi)$ 的最大值为 $\varphi = \varphi^* \in (\frac{\pi}{2}; \pi)$。但$S_3(\varphi^*) = (1/2)c\sqrt{H^2 + h^2} > S_2(\varphi^*) = (1/2)c\sqrt{H^2 + kh^2}$意味着$\tilde{S}_{\max} = S_3(\varphi^*)$。接下来是$\tilde{S}_{\min} = \{ \tilde{S}(\varphi_1), \tilde{S}(\frac{\pi}{2}) \} = \tilde{S}(\varphi_1)$，从$\tilde{S}(\pi/2) = 1/2 Hc = \sqrt{S^2(\varphi^*)} - S_0^2$开始，从问题的数据中我们得到$\tilde{S}_{\min} < \sqrt{S^2(\varphi^*)} - S_0^2$。所以，$hc = 2S_0 = 14; \sqrt{(Hc)^2 + (hc)^2} = 2\tilde{S}_{\max} = 28$，从哪里$Hc = 14\sqrt{3}; hc \times Hc / \sqrt{(Hc)^2 + (hc)^2} \cdot (1 - k)^2 = 2S_{\min} = 8\sqrt{3}$，从哪里$k = 1/4$。因此，$H = \sqrt{AO \cdot BO} = (\sqrt{3}/4)c$，进而$c = 2\sqrt{14}$，$H = \sqrt{21/2}$，所需体积为$V = 1/3 HS_0 = 7/3 \cdot \sqrt{21/2}$。
