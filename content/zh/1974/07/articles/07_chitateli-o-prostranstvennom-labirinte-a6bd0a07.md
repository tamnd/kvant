关于空间迷宫的读者
4日«Kvant»今年第3号。封面上写着“热情的迷宫”。 Many readers have noticed that it is impossible to navigate this labyrinth, so there is an opportunity to keep your friends busy for the whole evening. However, our reader O. M. Kostenok from Moscow suggests solving another problem: how to do another important thing so that you can navigate through the labyrinth? O. M. Kostenok 建议负责获得第二层（在国际象棋术语中的 d4 方格上）。
当然，还有其他选项可以放置另一个答案，之后就可以在迷宫中导航了。只有一个问题：在哪里做这件负责任的事情，让迷宫尽可能地混乱？
