9. 齐奥尔科夫斯基的任务

让我们考虑一下距离引力质量很远的航天器的运动。显然，如果发动机不工作，那么船要么会停下来，要么会匀速直线移动；我们假设他已经完成了，$v_0 = 0$。 Due to conservation laws, after turning on the engine, the amount of motion of the “ship-exhaust gases” system remains unchanged, since no external forces act on this system.

Let us denote by $m$, $v$ the mass of the ship with fuel and its speed at the moment of time $t$, $u$ - the speed of the emitted exhaust gas relative to the ship (a constant value).然后$mv = (m - \Delta m) \times (v + \Delta v) - \Delta m (u - v)$，从$\Delta m = -\frac{\Delta v}{u - \Delta v} m$。让我们找出$m$对$v$的依赖关系。对于小$\Delta v$，考虑到$u$是常数，$u \neq 0$，我们有$u - \Delta v \approx u$，因此

$$\Delta m \approx -\frac{1}{u} m \cdot \Delta v,$$

因此，

$$m = m_0 e^{-\frac{v}{u}}.$$

对 $e$ 取序数并化简，我们得到

$$v = u \ln \frac{m_0}{m}.$$
