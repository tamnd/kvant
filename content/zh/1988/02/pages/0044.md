$$\frac{a}{c} = \frac{2}{7} \left( 2 - \frac{2}{7} \right) = \frac{2}{7} \cdot \frac{12}{7} = \frac{24}{49}.$$

现在取$a=24$、$c=49$或$a=48$、$c=98$...检查：

$$\frac{24}{2} - \frac{49}{7} = \frac{24-49}{2-7};$$

$$\frac{48}{2} - \frac{98}{7} = \frac{49-98}{2-7}.$$

顺便说一句，分母或分子可能是两倍、三倍甚至四倍：

$$\frac{24}{6} - \frac{49}{21} = \frac{24-49}{6-21}.$$
