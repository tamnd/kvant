$2^\circ$。 Let the lattice be such that for any nodes $P$ and $Q$ one can specify some self-alignment of the lattice, transforming $P$ into $Q$ (unlike $1^\circ$, this can be a rotation, and not just a translation).

According to the key lemma, for each point $M$ there is only one node $Q$, for which $M \in D_r(Q)$ is the $r$ node in terms of distance from the point $M$.很明显，不同$Q$的区域$D_r(Q)$仅沿着边界相交。 The imposed conditions show that all regions $D_r(Q)$ are the same - when the lattice is combined, transforming $Q$ into $O$, $D_r(Q)$ goes into $D_r(O)$.

现在让我们解释为什么对于不同的 $r$ $D_r(Q)$ 大小相等。 Let us set the following limit for a given lattice by “density”: $\alpha = \lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{K(N)}{N^2}$, where $K(n)$ is the number of nodes lying in a square of size $N \times N$ with center $O$.让我们证明 $\alpha = \frac{1}{S_r}(S_r - \text{плошадь области } D_r(Q))$ 对于每个

杜高$r$。令$L_r$为点$D_r(O)$距$O$、$N > L_r$的最近距离。那么对于正方形$N \times N$的所有$K(N)$节点$Q$，面积$D_r(Q)$的并集覆盖了正方形$(N - L_r) \times (N - L_r)$并且包含在正方形$(N + L_r) \times (N + L_r)$中，所以

$$\left(1 - \frac{L_r}{N}\right)^2 = \frac{(N - L_r)^2}{N^2} \leqslant \frac{S_r \cdot K(N)}{N^2} \leqslant \frac{(N + L_r)^2}{N^2} = \left(1 + \frac{L_r}{N}\right)^2.$$

在$N \to \infty$时左右两边都尽可能接近1，所以$\lim_{N \to \infty} = \frac{S_r \cdot K(N)}{N^2} = 1$有极限，所以$\alpha = \frac{1}{S_r}$；值$\frac{1}{\alpha}$是“每个节点的平均面积”，特别地，区域$D(O)$的面积$S_1$也等于$\frac{1}{\alpha}$。
