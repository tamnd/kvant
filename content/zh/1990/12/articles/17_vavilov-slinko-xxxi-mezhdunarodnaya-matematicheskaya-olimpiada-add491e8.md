XXXI
国际数学奥林匹克竞赛

物理和数学科学候选人
V·瓦维洛夫，
物理和数学科学候选人
A·斯林科

The next XXXI International Mathematical Olympiad for schoolchildren was held in Beijing from July 7 to July 19, 1990. 308 schoolchildren from 54 countries took part in the Olympiad.今年首次有来自阿尔及利亚、巴林、日本、澳门和朝鲜的队伍参加。

Our country was represented by Malinnikova Evgeniya (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Stoyanovsky Alexaンドr (Moscow, s. sh. No. 57), Perlin Alexa ンドr (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Abramov Georgy (Leningrad, s. sh. No. 239), Soloviev Ivan (p. Chernogolovka, 莫斯科州, 82 号村), Baranovsky Vladimir (鄂木斯克, 115 号村)。 The scientific leaders of the team at the Olympiad were the authors of this article - associate professor of Moscow State University V. V. Vavilov and researcher at the Scientific Research Institute of SI of the USSR Academy of Sciences A. M. Slinko.

与往常一样，奥林匹克竞赛分两轮举行（每轮 3 个问题，每种类型分配 4.5 个小时来解决）。每个问题的解决方案得 7 分。

今年排名前十的球队。总分34-42分的小学生将获得金牌，银牌（23-33分）和铜牌（16-22分）。让我们特别注意卢森堡队的成功，该队只有两名参赛者，赢得了银牌和铜牌。
