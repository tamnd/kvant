$$|y_k - y_{k+1}| = \left| \frac{x_1 + \ldots + x_k}{k} - \frac{x_1 + \ldots + x_{k+1}}{k+1} \right| = \left| \frac{x_1 + \ldots + x_k}{k(k+1)} \right| \leqslant \left| \frac{x_1 - x_2}{k(k+1)} \right| + \left| \frac{x_1 - x_3}{k(k+1)} \right| + \ldots + 1990 \left| x_{1990} - x_{1991} \right| \cdot \frac{1}{1990 \cdot 1991} = \left| x_1 - x_2 \right| \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{1}{1991} \right) + \left| x_2 - x_3 \right| \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{2}{1991} \right) + \ldots + \left| x_{1990} - x_{1991} \right| \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{1990}{1991} \right) \leqslant 1991 \cdot \left( 1 - \frac{1}{1991} \right) = 1990.$$
