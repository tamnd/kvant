If a pencil with hexagonal section is placed on an inclined plane (forming the angle $a$ with the horizontal plane) perpendicularly to the intersection $l$ of these planes, the pencil remains motionless. Placed parallel to $l$, it rolls down. For what angles $\varphi$ between the axis of the pencil and $l$ (see figure Pис. 3) will the pencil remain in equilibrium?

S. S. Krotov

P840. A flat $10 \times 10$ cm box placed horizontally contains 1000 small steel ball-bearings of mass $m = 0.5$ mg each, mixed at random. The box starts to move with velocity $v_0 = 10$ m/s perpendicularly to one of its sides. Assuming the collisions of the ball-bearings with each other and the walls of the box absolutely elastic, determine: a) what impulses will the ball-bearings communicate to each of the walls during the first 10 seconds; b) what impulses will the walls get in the 10 seconds following a sudden braking of the box to a stop.

A. R. Zilberman

P841. A landing craft from a spaceship approaches the surface of a planet with constant velocity, transmitting data on outside pressure to the mother ship. The graph of the pressure $p$ (in conditional units) as a function of time $t$ is plotted on figure Pис. 4. Having landed, the craft measures temperature and gravitational acceleration: $T = 700$ K, $g = 10$ m/s$^2$. Determine the descent velocity of the landing craft, if it is known that the atmosphere of the planet consists of carbon dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$). Determine the temperature at elevation $h = 15$ km above the planet’s surface.

A. I. Buzdin

Решения задач

M811—M815; Ф823—Ф827

Умножим обе части доказываемого неравенства на площадь треугольника $S$ и воспользуемся формулами

$$S = \frac{ah}{2} = \frac{bh}{2} = \frac{ch}{2} = r \frac{a+b+c}{2}.$$

Тогда оно приведется к виду

$$\frac{m_a \cdot a}{2} + \frac{m_b \cdot b}{2} + \frac{m_c \cdot c}{2} \leq S + R \frac{a+b+c}{2}$$

или

$$\frac{(m_a - R)a}{2} + \frac{(m_b - R)b}{2} + \frac{(m_c - R)c}{2} \leq S.$$

Ясно, что величины $m_a - R$ не превозходит расстояния от центра $O$ описанной окружности до стороны $BC$ (см. рис.нок), то есть выcisты треугольника $OBC$. Следовательно, первоое слагаемое в левой части неравенства (*) не больше площади треугольника $OBC$. Аналогично, два других слагаемых не превозходит площадей треугольников $OCA$ и $OAB$ соответственно. Тем самым доказано неравенство (*), а с ним и неравенство задачи.
