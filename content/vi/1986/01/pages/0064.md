$$\tau = \frac{2|v_y|}{g\cos \alpha} = \frac{2v_0\sin \beta}{g\cos \alpha}.$$

$$\Gamma_1 = \frac{f_1}{d_1} = \frac{F}{(F - d_1)}.$$

$$\Gamma_2 = \frac{f_2}{d_2} = \frac{F}{d_2 - F} = \frac{F - d_1}{F}.$$

$$\Gamma = \Gamma_1\Gamma_2 = 1$$
