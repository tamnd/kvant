b) (3) $|x|+|2x+1|-x>1$;

d) (8) $\sqrt{5-x^2}>x-1$;
e) (12) $\sqrt{2x+4}<4$;
f) (1) $\frac{\sqrt{12+x-x^2}}{2x+7} \geq \frac{\sqrt{12+x-x^2}}{x+5}$;
g) (2) $3\sqrt{6+x-x^2}+2>4x$;
h) (1) $\sqrt{2x^2-6x+1}-x+2>0$;
