$$u_0 = 2 = 1 + 1 = 2^{(2^0 - (-1)^0)/3} + 2^{-(2^0 - (-1)^0)/3};$$
$$u_1 = 2 + 1/2 = 2^{(2^1 + 1)/3} + 2^{-(2^1 + 1)/3};$$
