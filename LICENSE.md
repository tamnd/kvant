## What this repository holds

Transcriptions of printed pages, translations of them, structured metadata about the magazine, and worked solutions to its problems. The transcriptions and the translations are derivative works of copyrighted material and are held here for personal study. They carry no licence and are not offered for redistribution.

The metadata under `manifests/`, the tag index under `tags/`, and the generated reports under `reports/` are facts about the magazine rather than the work itself, and may be used freely.

Solutions under `content/solutions/` are original text written for this repository, except where a solution quotes the published one for comparison, in which case the quotation is marked and attributed to the issue it was printed in.

## What this repository does not hold

No scans. No page images. No PDFs. Those stay in the working directory, which is gitignored, and a commit that adds one is a bug.
