# The archive's text against ours

kvant.digital carries the publisher's own text for part of the archive. Where it does, a page needs no model, so the question is how far that text can be trusted, and this is the measurement rather than the opinion.

Up to 12 articles a year, picked by hash so the sample does not move between runs, each compared word by word against the same article assembled from our own reading.

Generated 2026-08-14 by kvant report diff.

## The two numbers

The rate is the share of the archive's own words that our reading of the same pages does not have. That is the number the verdict is taken on, because it is the one that says whether their typing can stand in for a vision pass.

Coverage is the share of our reading their article accounts for, and a low one is not a complaint about them. Assembly cuts at the page and the archive cuts at the piece, so wherever three short items share a page our article is the whole page and theirs is a fifth of it. Coverage is here so that an archive text that stops halfway through a long article shows up instead of scoring a clean rate on the half it did type.
