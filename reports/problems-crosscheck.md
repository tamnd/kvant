# The joined pages against ours

Recovering Задачник «Кванта» off the scans is a join. A problem is cut out of one issue, its solution is cut out of another issue two to four numbers later, and the two are put together on the strength of a number printed in a heading. Nothing in that chain checks itself: a misread digit pairs a statement with somebody else's answer, and the result looks exactly like a problem that worked.

kvant.digital made the same pairing by hand from the same magazine. This is the two put side by side, which is the only outside opinion available on whether the join is right.

Generated 2026-08-18 by kvant problems crosscheck.

179 problems compared, 0 more whose number the site does not index.
