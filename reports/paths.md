# How every page gets read

Three ways to get the text of a page, in order of what they cost. `native` is the text layer of the publisher's own PDF and is free. `publisher` is the transcription kvant.digital already carries for an article and is also free, though it is somebody else's OCR and gets checked against a vision pass on a sample. `vision` is a model reading a scan, and it is the only one that costs anything.

This file is generated by `kvant textguard`. The page by page decision lives in the page manifests next to the scans; what is here is the count and the measurement behind it.

## What the estimates assume

These are assumptions and not measurements. They are here so that the numbers above can be argued with, and they get replaced with what M3 actually measures on one issue.

- One model call per page: 1800 input tokens for the image and the prompt, 1400 output tokens for the transcription.
- $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output, which comes to $0.0022 a page.
- 148 seconds a page on one lane, retries included, across 8 lanes.
- Nothing here counts the second pass on pages that come back illegible, or the translation runs, which are priced separately.
